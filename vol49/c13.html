Chapter 13: 胸の轟 [1287]

胸の轟〔1287〕

§ 1

Takahime had a husband who was unexpectedly Ritsupa, and becoming more and more arrogant, from the following Yokuchō she began to brandish the Giritenjiyōhi Denokami around recklessly once again. Tokiokashinokami went out early in the morning, saying he was going to Sanpai at the Shinden.

Left behind, Takahime placed a Hibachi in front of her, sat properly, and puffed away at some Tabako with a long Kiseru, and then struck a bell with a tinkling sound. Yoru, while trembling with fear and scratching his head, opened the sliding door softly.

Yoru: 'Heh, Gomen-nasaimase.'

He stuck half of his Karada out, already poised to flee.

Takahime: 'Hey, Yoru-kō, what kind of Katsukau is that? What is with your waist, it is unsightly, come in quickly.'

Yoru: 'Heh, Nanibun I used it Yodoho, so it has finally become a waist like this. I truly felt as if the whole Sekaijiu had gathered in Itsushiyo, ehehehe.'

Takahime: 'You were listening to our whispering, were you not?'

Yoru: 'Hai, ehehe, because you said that it was as if the whole Sekaijiu had gathered, I was also anxious, and if the whole Sekaijiu were to come rushing to a place like this, your former husband would surely be mixed in among them. Thinking that a great Ōgenkuwa would start, I stood by Yodoho with a Nejihachimaki on, and now that the Iwato-biraki has been completed safely, congratulations, etsuhehehe, Nanibun it is Yoru's protection, so this Yoru-kō cannot sleep at night...'

Takahime: 'What were the others doing?'

Yoru: 'Hai, the six of them were in the next room, lined up, and were practicing Lamaism in your company. It was quite vigorous.'

Takahime: 'Eeeh, what troublesome fellows they are, come, prepare the Gozen quickly.'

Yoru: 'Hai, I understand. However, shall the Ozen be one, or shall I make it two?'

Takahime: 'Without having to be told such things, why don't you use your head a little?'

Yoru: 'In that case, since there are two of you, shall I make it two?'

Takahime: 'Eeeh, what a dull-witted man you are, two is in fact one; a man who does not understand that if you say one, it means two, is of no use for the true mission.'

Yoru: 'Please tell me clearly. Whether it is two or one...'

Takahime: 'Eeeh, think about it reasonably. You should understand well enough.'

Yoru: 'I see, ya, I understand. Last evening it was two, so I shall make it two. They say that a man who does not accept a served meal is not a man, itsuhihehe.'

Saying this, he stuck his tongue out slightly, thrust his chin out two or three times, and left.

Takahime, while tapping the Hibachi with her Kiseru,

Takahime: 'Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese. May I stay with Toki-sama forever, and may I be able to perform a magnificent service for the Kamikai...'

She was praying. Then, Tokiokashinokami finished his worship at the Shinden and came back smiling,

Tokiokashinokami: 'Ya, Taka-chan, I kept you waiting. You must have been very lonely.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Tokiokashinokami-sama, you never know who is listening. If you call me Taka-chan, my authority will fall to the ground. You must call me Giritenjiyōhi Denokami.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'So you intend to stick with Denokami after all. Indeed, that is strange. Very well, then I shall address you as Denokami-sama from now on.'

Takahime: 'What is it, speaking in such a formal way, it is truly insincere.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'Then, Yama no Kami-sama, what should I call you?'

Takahime: 'I say it is Giritenjiyōhi Denokami.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'Very well, very well, then I shall call you that from now on.'

That it is written in the Norito that he hid his ears means he hid his Goshinriki for a while. The moving of ears is the Shiyōko that one is a reincarnation of a Daishinjin. Tokioka: 'Ahahaha, you were wiggling so much that I was thinking of giving you a Hiyōshi of retaliation. That is why I sulked for a moment. Ahahaha.'

Takahime: 'Stop it now, you are of a good age, it is unsightly.'

Tokioka: 'Etsu-hetsu-hetsu-he.'

To such a place, Kaede opened the sliding door softly.

Kaede: 'Excuse me, Hinodewake-no-Obasama, the meal is ready.'

She said, bringing in the meal tray.

Takahime: 'Ah, thank you for your trouble. Please leave it there. And, there is only one meal tray here. Please bring another one quickly.'

Kaede: 'Um, Obasama, this is all the meal there is. Yoru-sama said that one tray would be enough to bring.'

Takahime: 'Eh, what a thoughtless man. Hey, Kaede-sama, please tell Yoru to come here for a moment.'

Kaede: 'Yes.'

Saying this, she left the place.

Tokioka: 'Hey, Hinodewake-sama, I am taking my leave now. If I am treated with such abuse, I cannot stay even if I wanted to. I am not a wooden statue, and no matter how much of a Mokusuke I am called, food is necessary after all. It is useless to stay in a place where I am not even served breakfast...'

As he stood up indignantly, Takahime grabbed him in a panic and cried out in a shrill voice,

Takahime: 'Hey, Toki-sama, you should not be so short-tempered. Do you remember last night? It is because they are all thoughtless people that such an inconvenience occurred. I will tell them thoroughly, so please calm your mood and eat this meal.'

Tokioka: 'Treating a full-grown man like a monster, and after adding insults about my ears moving and such, what is this? You brought only your own meal tray and ignored me. It is ridiculous; I am not a Toki-sama who will stay in such a place.'

Takahime: 'Do you not understand the heart of this Takahime? ...Hey, hey, Yoru, come here for a moment.'

At this voice, Yoru opened the sliding door softly.

Yoru: 'Yes, do you have some business with me?'

Takahime: 'Why did you not bring two meal trays?'

Yoru: 'Yes, when I heard you softly last night, did you not say, "Shall we make it two? No, no, since this is the first time, let us leave it at one"? Therefore, I did exactly as you said.'
