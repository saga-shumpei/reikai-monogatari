Chapter 18: Poison 酸 [1292]

毒酸〔1292〕

§ 1

Takahime and Mokusuke, the two of them, are indulging in a whispered conversation while pouring sake for each other.

Takahime: 'Mokusuke-sama, a terrible thing has happened. I am filled with such anxiety that I cannot bear it.'

Mokusuke: 'Ha ha ha ha, you are worried because Magahiko has truly gone to the mansion of Uzuhiko, are you not? There is no need to worry about such a thing. There are countless methods and means, are there not?'

Takahime: 'If that is so, does it not mean that your magic, Mokusuke-sama, is not to be relied upon at all? If one thinks of how those three fellows returned so easily, they are all like monkeys, bears, or old tanuki, and the sorcery of Teru and Haru-kou was also nothing but a mere illusion. If we remain like this, will we not be unable to stay in this Giritenjiyō for even five more days? If those three fellows go to the mansion of Iso, they will surely tell everything, every detail. If that happens, it is certain that we will be given an order to vacate. Please, somehow, think of a supreme piece of wisdom and consider how we might remain here, my dear.'

Mokusuke: 'Aha ha ha ha, does even the wisdom of the Hinodewake-sama not work? It seems that no matter how much divine power a god may have, evil deeds are of no avail.'

Takahime: 'Since good and evil are non-dual and right and wrong are one, Giritenjiyō works for good by appearing as evil, so surely the gods will forgive us. As for what those five fellows, Iru, Iku, Saru, Haru, and Teru say, they keep pestering us even now, drunk on sake, saying, "We five are the spirits of strictness. We are the permanent fixtures of the shrine forest who received orders from Tamakuniwake-sama, so we are not people to be swayed by you. If you keep grumbling, please leave." It is truly troublesome. I am worried about what will happen from here on out.'

Mokusuke: 'Since Uzuhiko and his wife hold full authority over this mansion, if it is the order of Uzuhiko, it is a simple matter to banish those five fellows.'

Takahime: 'You have hit upon a good point. Indeed, since Uzuhiko-sama holds full authority, it will be fine if we keep Uzuhiko in the palm of our hand. But what shall we do if Uzuhiko does not listen to what we say?'

Mokusuke: 'Why worry about such a thing? You should use the art of transformation; I will transform into Uzuhiko, and you shall transform into Shizuko.'

Takahime: 'Even if you say that, I wonder if it will go so smoothly, even down to the face and form?'

Mokusuke: 'How could it not? I will transform so that there is not the slightest difference. I will make you transform as well.'

Takahime: 'If there are two Uzuhikos and two Shizukohimes in the same mansion, will that not be the cause of our exposure?'

Mokusuke: 'What of it? We will easily beguile both of them, put poison in their sake and food, quietly finish them off, and then transform the two of us into the bodies of Takahime and Mokusuke, hold a funeral, and afterwards, we two will transform into Uzuhiko and Shizuko. That will be safe, will it not?'

Takahime: 'Mokusuke-sama, I thought you were an honest person, but you come up with such terribly wicked wisdom.'

Mokusuke: 'It is a settled matter. In this world today, those who wear the mask of good to commit evil deeds are called the most respectable people. Now that you have become the wife of Mokusuke, you must become even more evil, or it will not do. Do they not say, "a demon husband and a snake wife"?'

Takahime: 'Even so, is that not too cruel? You speak as if you were a devil.'

Mokusuke: 'Aha ha ha ha, if you are going to do evil, half-measures like yours will not do. You must do evil thoroughly. If it is a half-hearted, mixed-up evil of good and bad, it is far better to stop altogether. Since I, Mokusuke, cannot possibly share the same will as a good person like you, shall we not part ways while we still can? You cannot possibly follow me. Your transformation into evil is insufficient.'

高姫は杢助と二人、酒を汲み交しながら、ひそびそ話に耽つて居る。

高姫『杢助さま大変な事が出来たのよ。私心配でならないわ』

杢助『ハヽヽヽ、ヨルやお寅、魔我彦が本当に珍の館へ行つたのが苦になるのであらう、そんな事は心配はいらないぢやないか。幾何でも方法手段はあるのぢや』

高姫『それだと云つて杢助さまの魔術も一寸も当にならぬぢやありませぬか。三人の奴が甘く帰つて来たと思へば、何奴も此奴も、猿や熊や古狸のやうなものだし、テルやハル公の魔法使もサツパリ幻影だつたし、此儘で居たなら、此義理天上もここ五日と居れぬぢやありませぬか。彼奴等三人がイソの館に往きよつたら、きつと、一伍一什を云ふに違ひない。さうすればキツト立退き命令を喰はされる事は知れた事、何うかして、此処に居坐りたいがお前さま、とつときの智慧を出して考へて下さるまいかな』

杢助『アハヽヽヽ、日の出神様の考へでも往きませぬかな。矢張り何程神力のある神でも悪い事は駄目だと見えるのう』

高姫『善悪不二、正邪一如だから義理天上は悪に見せて善を働くのだから、キツト神様も許して下さるだらう。イル、イク、サール、ハル、テルの五人の奴が云ふのには、「吾々五人は厳の御霊だ。玉国別さまに命令を受けた祠の森の常置品だから、お前達に左右される者ぢやない。グヅグヅ云ふのなら出て往つて呉れ」などと、最前も酒に喰ひ酔つて責めて来るのだから困つたものだ。私はこの先どうなるかと思ふて、心配でなりませぬわ』

杢助『此館は珍彦夫婦が全権をもつて居るのだから、珍彦の命令なら、彼奴等五人を放逐するのは何でもない事だ』

高姫『それはよい所へお気が着きました。成る程珍彦さまが全権を握つて厶るのだから、珍彦さへ此方の薬籠中のものとして置けば大丈夫ですな。併し珍彦が此方の云ふ事を聞かなかつたらどうしませうかなア』

杢助『そんな心配が入るか。変身の術を使つて杢助は珍彦に化け、お前は静子に化けたらよいのだ』

高姫『夫だと云つて、顔形迄がさう甘く往きませうかなア』

杢助『いかいでか、チツトも違はないやうに化けて見せる。お前も化けさせてやる』

高姫『同じ館に二人も珍彦、静子姫があつては露顕のもとぢや厶いませぬか』

杢助『何さ、甘く両人をたらし込んで酒や飯の中に毒を入れ、そつと○○して了ひ、さうして高姫杢助の体に二人を変じ、甘く葬式を営み、後に吾々両人が、珍彦静子と化け変るのだ、さうすれば安心だらう』

高姫『杢助さま、お前は正直の方だと思つて居たが、随分悪い智慧が出ますなア』

杢助『極つた事だよ。今の世の中は善の仮面を被つて悪事をするもの程、立派な人間と云はれるのだ。お前も杢助の女房となつた以上は、も一段改悪せなくては駄目だよ。鬼の夫に蛇の女房と云ふぢやないか』

高姫『それだと云つて余り非道いぢやありませぬか。お前はまるで悪魔のやうな事を云ひますな』

杢助『アハヽヽヽ、悪をやるならばお前のやうな中途半は駄目だ。徹底的に悪をやるのだ。中途半の善悪混交的悪なら、止めた方が余程気が利いて居るよ。此杢助は到底お前のやうな善人とは意志が合はないから、今の中に別れようぢやないか。お前では到底私について来る事は出来ない。改悪が足らぬからなア』

§ 2

Takahime: 'Mokusuke-sama, now that things have come to this, I will follow you anywhere. Please cherish me for a long time to come.'

Mokusuke: 'Very well, then you must do exactly as I say.'

Takahime: 'Yes, I shall not refuse anything, no matter what it is.'

Mokusuke: 'In that case, I will give this to you now; sprinkle this powder into their sake or their Gohan. This is a poison called Dokusan, manufactured from the fruit of a tree that grew on Mount Gunmasan in Indo. Here, I give this to you; lure both of them here skillfully, treat them to a feast, and do the deed.'

Takahime: 'Yes, I understand. I shall certainly do it, but once the two are finished, what should be done about that Kaede?'

Mokusuke: 'That Kaede? You may just leave her be. Uzuhiko will die, transformed into the Nikutai of Mokusuke, and Shizuko will transform into your Nikutai; once you bury the Shitai in the Dochiu, you will be able to settle down as the perfect Uzuhiko and Shizuko. If you do that, it will not matter how many Tachinoki Meirei come from the Iso mansion, will it?'

Takahime: 'Indeed, this is a Meuankisaku; even the Giritenjō no Ikumiya of the Hinodewake God would be impressed. Oh ho ho ho, how convenient these magic arts are.'

Mokusuke: 'Now, hurry and start preparing the feast. If you dawdle, there is a fear of discovery. It is best to be swift with a plot.'

Takahime: 'Ah, how busy I am. I must cook the Gohan, and I must prepare the simmered dishes too. Mokusuke-sama, please help me only with warming the sake. If we finish off Uzuhiko and Shizuko smoothly with this, there will be peace under heaven. You and I shall be enshrined in the shrine palace forever, rivaling the Sangōkyō; let us open the Uranai-kyō, while appearing to be the Sangōkyō on the surface, shall we not?'

Mokusuke: 'Aha ha ha, it seems you have been greatly influenced by me, and have achieved quite a conversion.'

Takahime: 'Shall I have someone go to call the Uzuhiko couple?'

Mokusuke: 'The Uzuhiko couple are the masters of this place, no matter what. Once you have the preparations properly arranged, you must humble your words and go to welcome them with proper courtesy; if they were to say they did not want to come, it would be a disaster.'

Takahime: 'Ah, that is true. Then I shall finish preparing the Gohan and go myself. The time for the fulfillment of my desire has arrived; if this goes smoothly, all will be Daijiyōbu. Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese, Giritenjō Hinodewake no Kami-sama, I pray that this scheme goes smoothly.'

She then clapped her hands four times—pon, pon, pon, pon—and began a silent prayer.

Mokusuke: 'Aha ha ha, I am sure He will listen well.'

Takahime: 'That is right. Is it not fine to rely on evil gods for evil matters?'

Mokusuke: 'That is true. A cake should be left to the cake maker, so please do it so that you are not easily perceived.'

At this moment, there was a small sound of footsteps in the corridor in front of the sliding doors, which then vanished toward the front. Takahime caught the sound of those footsteps and said,

Takahime: 'Ah, Mokusuke-sama, did you not hear footsteps? I wonder if someone has overheard this secret.'

Mokusuke: 'What are you saying, those were just the footsteps of a cat or a Chin dog.'

Takahime: 'But are there not neither Chin dogs nor cats here?'

Mokusuke: 'Since we are in the mountains, there are many wild cats and wild Chin dogs; they must have caught the scent of the feast and come sniffing. There is no need to worry about such things.'

Takahime: 'Even so, I feel somewhat uneasy.'
