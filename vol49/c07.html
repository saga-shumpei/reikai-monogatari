§ 1

Filling my spirit with such noble teachings, as a noble messenger of Kami, I prepare my travel attire here and set forth, pointing toward Haruna. Ah, Kannagara, the grace of Kami is deep, and there is no obstacle on the path I tread. May you allow me to fulfill this mission with a healthy body, Ōgami. Though I am but a frail woman, I am a noble messenger of the Ōkami who allows me to possess absolute, infinite divine power. I shall never defile or stain the name of Kami; for the sake of the Way, for the sake of Yobito, I shall ultimately manifest the will of Kami. My resolve is to save all, leaving none behind, including the Yamata no Orochi and the ugly Kami, through the Way of Kami. Establish me, Kannagara.'

Praying with a sincere heart before the presence of Kami, she sang with a gentle voice, descended Ubusunayama, crossed Arayanogahara, and finally arrived at the vicinity of the log bridge over Fukuyagawa at twilight. This valley river is a high log bridge, with almost a hundred ken to the riverbed. All Sendenshi must cross this single-log bridge. However, though it is called a single-log bridge, it is a natural bridge made by felling a large tree on the edge of the valley and laying it across to the opposite bank, so it was a giant log bridge that was relatively sturdy and could be crossed even on horseback. Hatsuwakahime stood in the center of this log bridge, gazing at the magnificent view of the valley water far below, which she could barely see, flowing with a roar and sending up spray.

Hatsuwakahime: 'The grace of the Ōkami is like the clear valley water, flowing into the vast sea. I now stand in the middle of the log bridge that crosses the dark night into the light. As I gaze into the bottomless valley river, the clear, pure water rages and foams. The water flowing at the bottom of the valley is very clear; how is it for one crossing the sky? Twilight has fallen and the curtain of darkness has been drawn, yet the foam shines white. I have taken leave of the white plum blossoms blooming in Isonoyakata and departed. There is no moon, and even the stars are wrapped in clouds; how dark is this solitary journey of the night. Having crossed such a lonely field path, I encounter a darkness where black and white cannot be distinguished. Nevertheless, my spirit, illuminated by the light of Kami, knows no darkness. Standing alone in the middle of the bridge, I worry about the situation of my father.'

While singing this, she proceeded, feeling her way through the dark Kōji. Even the dauntless Hatsuwakahime, beset by darkness and cold, had no choice but to spread her straw mat by the roadside. Hatsuwakahime, thinking what a strange creature had come, held her breath and pondered. Then, she thought within her heart,

Hatsuwakahime: 'I am a missionary of the world. If I am frightened by an Oni of this level, how can I continue my journey of several thousand ri ahead? I shall try to test my strength, get ahead of them from this side, and face them—nay, I shall intimidate them.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Hey, hey, you Oni called Aka and Kuro, I have been listening to you for some time, and it seems you are terribly hungry. You said you wanted to eat warm Jinniku, but if we are talking about warm flesh here, there should be only me. I am in the prime of my youth at sixteen, and my flesh is plump and very tasty. If you desire it, I shall let you eat it. Come, whether from my hands or my feet, you may eat as you please. I am a missionary who must save all things in the world. My spirit is filled with the holy spirit of God. It appears that you, pitifully, have been possessed by evil spirits, and even your physical bodies have become Oni. Today is the start of my journey as a missionary, and having heard your lamentations for the first time, if I do not save you, my duty will not be fulfilled. Come, do not hesitate; eat my body without leaving even a single hair. If you eat the body of Hatsuwakahime, who is filled with the divinity of God, you Oni, Aka and Kuro, will surely receive God's salvation and become pure and noble humans. Though I am a frail person, if I am eaten by rough men like you, and I use you as my living shrine, there is the extraordinary convenience that Hatsuwakahime's body will become two rough men and serve for the sake of God. Come, you may eat quickly.'

The two, Aka and Kuro, were so shocked by this declaration of Hatsuwakahime that they began to tremble violently.

Aka: 'Oi, Ku, Kuro, this is no good, no good. Sa, as expected of the daughter of Mokusuke-sama, she says something formidable, does she not? My threats have all vanished somewhere. Can you not say something? We cannot even talk, can we?'

They whispered, trembling and trembling.

Kuro: 'Oi Aka, that one is a monster. She is certainly not Hatsuwakahime-sama. How could such a delicate woman say such bold things? That one must surely be a monster. If we dawdle, we will be eaten instead. Come, run, run.'

初稚『ホヽヽヽヽ、やつぱり六に八であらう。そんな肝のチヨロイ事で何うして此初稚姫が試されませう。お父さまもそんな腰抜男を沢山の給料を出してお抱へ遊ばすかと思へばお気の毒だよ。六でもない八助だなア』

六『もしお化様どうぞ勘忍なして下さいませ。そしてお嬢様は此処をお通りになつた筈ですがお前さま喰つたのでせう。喰はれたお嬢さまは仕方がありませぬが、併し吾々二人の命だけはどうぞお助けを願ひます』

初稚『これ六に八、妾は初稚姫に間違ひないぞや。些と確りなさらないかい。お前、睾丸をどうしたのだい』

八『オイ六、やつぱり化州だ。彼奴は睾丸狙ひだよ。俺の睾丸まで狙つてけつかる、此奴は堪らぬぢやないか』

六『睾丸狙ひだつて、俺の金助は余程気が利いとると見えて、どこかへ往つて仕舞つた。貴様は八と云つて八畳敷の大睾丸だから些つと困るだらう』

八『イヤ、俺の睾丸も何処かへ往つて仕舞つたやうだ。吃驚してどこかへ落したのではあるまいかな』

六『ハヽヽヽヽ、馬鹿云へ、睾丸を落す奴があるか、大方転宅したのだらう』

八『何だか腹が膨れたと思ふたら腹中にグレングレンやつて居ると見える。オイ一つこんな時には御主人の云つて居られた惟神霊幸倍坐世を唱へようでないか。さうすればきつと曲津神が消えると云ふ事だよ』

六『そりやよい所へ気がついた。サア一所に惟神だ。カーンナガアラ、タヽマチ、ハヘ、マセ』

八『カンナンガラ、タマチハヘマセ。……何だか自分の声迄怖ろしくなつて来た。アンアンアン』

初稚『惟神霊幸倍坐世惟神霊幸倍坐世、吾家の下僕六、八の二人の御霊に力を与へさせたまへ。惟神霊幸倍坐世惟神霊幸倍坐世』
