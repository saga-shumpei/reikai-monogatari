Chapter 1: 地上天国 [1275]

地上天国〔1275〕

§ 1

The palace of the Miaraka, where the Sumeōkami who created all things in heaven and earth based on the goodness of love and the truth of faith is enshrined, is called the Tengoku on earth. Furthermore, the Seijiyō where the teachings of the Ōkami's mercy and Chie are proclaimed and transmitted is called the Reigoku. Therefore, in the Ōmotoshinyu, the sacred ground of Aya is also named the Takaamahara of the earth.

Tengoku is by no means a world in the void as imagined by Ningen. Since the Jitsugetsuseishin that shine in the Taikū were all created with the Chikiu as their center and foundation, the earth where we, the so-called people, reside must be the Reigoku and Tengoku. Ningen is to develop its physical body on earth, and also to domesticate, cultivate, and develop its spirit. If one investigates the true secret of Takaamahara, it is a matter of course that the innermost first Tengoku, as well as the intermediate Tengoku, the lower Tengoku, and the Reigoku all exist in reality on earth. It is merely that the world where the main body of a person who has escaped the physical form, that is, the spirit, resides is called the Reikai, and the place where Ningen with material forms lives is called the Genkai. Therefore, Ningen contains Takaamahara on one side and encompasses hell on the other. Furthermore, existing between the Reikai and the Genkai, that is, the natural world, the spirit remains in what is called the Chūūkai, being neither good nor evil.

All Ningen are created by the Ōkami in order to ascend to Takaamahara and become spiritual or heavenly celestial beings; those who possess the virtue of goodness from the Ōkami are Ningen, and are also destined to become celestial beings. In short, a celestial being is the most refined and pure spiritual body of Ningen, and Ningen is a kind of organ that exists between the two realms of the heavenly world and hell. As long as Ningen possesses what a celestial being has, in that its inner part is equally an image of the heavenly world and exists in the virtue of love and faith, Ningen is the so-called small Tengoku of Takaamahara. Furthermore, Ningen possesses an outer part that celestial beings do not have. That outer part is a worldly image. As long as a person dwells in the goodness of the Ōkami, making the worldly, that is, the natural outer part, subordinate to the inner part of the heavenly world, and allowing it to be controlled by the heavenly world, the Ōkami descends into the inner part of that Ningen just as He is in Takaamahara. Therefore, the Ōkami is present within the heavenly life of Ningen, and also within the worldly life. Thus, wherever there is divine order, the spirit of the Ōkami is always present. This is because all gods exist in order. Those who go against this divine order can never become celestial beings while alive.

That it is indicated in the Shin-yu of the Kyōso that 'ten ri square is within the palace' refers to the distance in the divine world, meaning that the Miaraka of the Ōkami, which is the innermost first Tengoku where the Ōkami of supreme goodness, beauty, faith, and love resides, is almost as vast as one hundred square ri of the human world, rather than a world of mere thought. Even a shrine's inner or outer sanctuary, which appears to be only one or two tsubo to the eyes of us Ningen, becomes a Tengoku of ten ri square or several hundred or several thousand ri through the extension of the virtue of the divine world, that is, the world of thought. The mention of 'Fukuchi Maizuru surroundings' is a term shown in a way easy for people of the Genkai to understand by borrowing place names near the sacred ground of Aya, and it does not mean that there is any special relationship with the place names of the Genkai. Only within a small palace (as seen from the eyes of Ningen)—that is, even within the palace—those who touch the love and faith of the Ōkami and possess perfect Chie and enlightenment can see it as boundless, holy, beautiful, and vast and high through the extension of thought as mentioned above. When one considers everything based on the things of the natural world, it is natural that such a theory appears to be equivalent to mere fantasy. However, when viewed from the eyes of spiritual things, it is by no means strange or unreasonable. If one can thoroughly investigate what spiritual phenomena are, one can eventually grasp the truth. However, it is inevitable that the circumstances of the Reikai, which cannot be understood by following the laws of the natural world to maintain the physical body and by seeing with the physical eyes, cannot be easily contemplated unless one receives the direct inner flow of the Ōkami.

天地万有一切を愛の善と信の真に基いて、創造し玉ひし皇大神を奉斎したる宮殿の御舎を、地上の天国と云ふ。而して大神の仁慈と智慧の教を宣べ伝ふる聖場を霊国といふ。故に大本神諭にも、綾の聖地を地の高天原と名付けられたのである。

天国とは決して人間の想像する如き、宙空の世界ではない。大空に照り輝く日月星辰も皆地球を中心とし、根拠として創造されたものである以上は、所謂吾人の住居する大地は霊国天国でなければならぬ。人間は其肉体を地上において発育せしめ、且其精霊をも馴化し、薫陶し、発育せしむべきものである。而して高天原の真の密意を究むるならば最奥第一の天国も亦中間天国、下層天国も、霊国もすべて地上に実在する事は勿論である。只形体を脱出したる人の本体即ち精霊の住居する世界を霊界と云ひ、物質的形体を有する人間の住む所を現界といふに過ぎない。故に人間は一方に高天原を蔵すると共に一方に地獄を包有してゐるのである。而して霊界、現界即ち自然界の間に介在して、其精霊は善にもあらず、悪にもあらず、所謂中有界に居を定めてゐるものである。

すべての人間は、高天原に向上して霊的又は天的天人とならむが為に、神の造り玉ひしもので、大神よりする善の徳を具有する者は、即ち人間であつて、又天人なるべきものである。要するに天人とは、人間の至粋至純なる霊身にして、人間とは、天界地獄両方面に介在する一種の機関である。人間の天人と同様に有してゐるものは、其内分の斉しく天界の影像なることと、愛と信の徳に在る限り、人間は所謂高天原の小天国である。而して人間は天人の有せざる外分なるものを持つてゐる。其外分とは世間的影像である。人は神の善徳に住する限り、世間即ち自然的外分をして、天界の内分に隷属せしめ、天界の制役するままならしむる時は、大神は御自身が高天原にいます如くに其人間の内分に臨ませ玉ふ。故に大神は人間が天界的生涯の内にも、世間的生涯の中にも、現在し玉ふのである。故に神的順序ある所には必ず大神の御霊ましまさぬことはない。凡て神は順序にましますからである。此神的順序に逆らふ者は決して生き乍ら天人たることを得ないのである。

教祖の神諭に……十里四方は宮の内……と示されてあるのは、神界に於ける里数にして、至善至美至信至愛の大神のまします、最奥第一の天国たる神の御舎は殆ど想念の世界よりは、人間界の一百方里位に広いといふ意味である。吾々人間の目にて僅かに一坪か二坪位な神社の内陣や外陣も、神界即ち想念界の徳の延長に依つて、十里四方或は数百里数千里の天国となるのである。福知舞鶴外囲ひと云うてあるのは、所謂綾の聖地に接近せる地名を仮つて、現界人に分り易く示されたものであつて、決して現界的地名に特別の関係がある訳ではない。只小さき宮殿（人間の目より見て）の中でも……即ち宮の内でも神の愛と神の信に触れ、智慧証覚の全き者は、右の如く想念の延長に仍つて、際限もなく、聖く麗しく、且広く高く見得るものである。すべて自然界の事物を基礎として考ふる時は斯の如き説は実に空想に等しきものの如く見ゆるは当然である。併し乍ら霊的事物の目より考ふれば、決して不思議でも、不合理でもない。霊的事象の如何なるものなるかを、能く究め得るならば、遂に其真相を掴むことが出来るのである。併し自然界の法則に従つて肉体を保ち、且肉の目を以て見ることを得ざる霊界の消息は到底大神の直接内流を受入るるに非ざれば、容易に思考し得可らざるは、已むを得ない次第である。

§ 2

Therefore, the Mitsui of the Shinkai is such that unless one is a Shinjin of the Reishiyutaijiuteki, it is Tauzen that no matter how one explains it to Ningen of the Chiukon and Gekon, they cannot easily accept it. Only those who have thoroughly mastered what their own Jiko is, based on the Naibun existing within their own Tainai, can understand the Shōsoku of the Reikai; otherwise, no matter what Shiyoseki one may search, no matter whose words of a Chishiya one may hear, and no matter how much one relies on a Tetsutei and Bisai Gakuri, as long as one cannot depart from the Shizenkai, it is impossible to glimpse the Shōsoku of the Reikai. Ningen in the Taiko Ōgonjidai understood that all Nanigoto were Naiteki, and that all Shiyojibutsu of the Shizenkai appeared based on those results. Therefore, they directly received the inward flow of the Ōkami, were able to discern the truth of the universe, attributed everything to the Lord God, and enjoyed their lives in accordance with the will of the Lord God. However, today, having already passed through the Silver, Copper, and Iron Ages, the world has become increasingly external; it has now become a world of a sea of mud in darkness and ignorance, devoid of both good and truth, furthest from disobeying the Lord God, and everyone's Naibun is directed outward, rebelling against the Lord God, and facing toward hell. For this reason, the hell of darkness at one's feet is immediately visible, but because the light shining in the sky is carried on one's back, it is impossible to believe in the teachings of the Lord God. Here, the Sumera Ōmikami, the Creator of heaven and earth, manifested as the Aramitama and the Nigimitama, and in order to turn the eyes of Ningen, who had fixed their gaze only on the underworld and could not perceive the light above their heads at all, toward the light of the Lord God, He proclaimed and transmitted the path of salvation through prophets.

The state in which Ningen, whose Naibun was opened toward hell in this manner, is made to face Takamagahara is what is proclaimed as the overturning of heaven and earth.

In short, to speak without reserve, Takamagahara refers to the Reikai, which is the residence of the Ōkami and the Tenjin; the Reikoku refers to the place where the sendenshi who transmit the teachings of the Lord God gather; and the place where one hears those teachings is called Tengoku or Reikoku. Those who have entered the Tenjin organization of Tengoku make ritual their only business, while the Tenjin of the Reikoku make the transmission of the teachings of the Lord God their sacred duty. Therefore, the place where the teachings of the Lord God are transmitted based on the most supreme and noble wisdom and enlightenment is called the First Reikoku, and the spiritual realm where those who have attained the highest and most marvelous love, goodness, wisdom, and enlightenment gather is called the Highest Tengoku. This is why it is called Genyū-Itchi.

When one possesses Takamagahara within one's heart, one should remember that such a Tenkai should appear not only in the greatest of Ningen's actions, that is, the general ones, but also in the small ones, that is, individual actions. This is the reason why it is stated in the 'Michi no Ōhara' that the body of the Great Spirit is supreme and without outside, and smallest and without inside. Indeed, the reason why Ningen is Ningen lies in the love that one possesses within oneself. It originates from the fact that the love which is the master of nature is one's own personality. This is because the love that each person takes as their master flows into even the most minute parts of their thoughts and actions, arranges them, and induces in all places that which is similar to oneself. In the various Tenkai, love for the Ōkami is taken as the primary love. This is because in Takamagahara, there is no one who is not loved as the Ōkami is loved. Therefore, in Takamagahara, they love and respect the Ōkami as the All in All.

The Ōkami flows into both the general and the individual, arranges and guides them, and by causing the image of the Ōkami Himself to remain upon them, Takamagahara is built wherever the Ōkami goes. Therefore, a Tenjin is a single Tenkai in an extremely small form, and their organization is a Tenkai possessing a larger form than this. And that which unites the various organizations into one forms the largest form of Takamagahara.

大神は全般の上にも、個々の上にも流れ入り玉ひて、之を按配し之を導いて、大神自身の影像を其上に止めさせ玉ふを以て、大神の行きます所には悉く高天原が築かれるのである。故に天人は極めて小さき形式に於ける一個の天界であつて、其団体は之よりも大なる形式を有する天界である。而して諸団体を打つて一丸となせるものは高天原の最大形式をなすものである。

§ 3

The Ōmoto of Kami in the Seichi of Aya is Takaamahara, which possesses a great Keishiki, and each branch office that has thoroughly realized the Aishin of holiness and righteousness to propagate its Oshihe is a Dantai of the Tenkai next to the Seichi; furthermore, it is a matter of course that a Mamehito who has opened the Tengoku within their own inner self is a Takaamahara of a small Keishiki. Therefore, all Dantai in the Reikai are formed into a single Tengoku-dantai in each Kakushiukyō based on the principle of Sauō, where those of the same spirit seek one another, according to the degree of the virtue of Aizen, the light of Shinshin, and the Chie and Shiyōkaku; and the fact that the Chūyūkai and Jigokukai are formed is, like the Tenkai, by no means fixed. However, because the Ōkami regards the Tenkai, Chūyūkai, and Jigokukai as a single person and unifies them as a single unit, no Dantai, however it may be, can escape from the divinity of the Gensei-no-Mitama and Mizuho-no-Mitama, nor is it permitted to take free action apart from this.

When one views the entirety of Takaamahara as unified, it resembles a single person. Therefore, because all Tenjin, raising up their entire being, know that it resembles a single person, they call Takaamahara the Ōkamibito. Those who can realize that the Seichi of Aya is the center of the deepest Tengoku where the Ōkami of the creation of heaven and earth resides eternally are the Mitama who can certainly become inhabitants of the Tengoku after death. Therefore, such heavenly humans regard the safety and prosperity of the Seichi as their own body and are able to dedicate love and faith for the sake of the Kamikai.

Once one has realized that the entirety of Takaamahara is a single unit, the great Ōkamibito, it is because all believers know that they are individuals or parts of the limbs of that Kamibito. Those who do not possess such a correct concept regarding spiritual and heavenly things do not know that these things are arranged and harmonized according to the Keishiki and image of a single person. Therefore, they think that worldly and natural things, which form the outer part of a human, are indeed personality, and that without them, a human would lose the reality of being human. Therefore, if a believer of Kami, who is a part of the Ōkamibito, is caught by such self-love and comes to lead an isolated life, even if it appears that they follow Kami outwardly, their inner self does not love Kami at all, but rebels against Kami, and being a faith for the sake of self-love, they have become a captive of so-called falsehood and evil. Those in such a state of faith can never harmonize with Kami or harmonize with the Tenkai. It is just as if a human of the Chūyūkai were to ascend to the First Tengoku, lose their way in that direction, be unable to see even a single Tenjin, suffer in their heart, be dazzled, and flee to the Jigokukai.

One should know that a human being's status as a human does not consist of a personality made of worldly and material things, but rather in the power to truly know the truth and to aspire to goodness. These spiritual and heavenly things are the very reason for forming a personality. And the superiority or inferiority of a personality depends on the degree of that person's intelligence and will.

When one considers what is shown in the Ōmoto Shin-yu: '...Beneath the lighthouse it is pitch dark; while being drawn to the magnificent land of Takaamahara, there are those who cloud their spirit with the desires of the flesh, and while entering the mountain of treasures, they return injured, barefoot and naked. This is because there is desire and arrogance in the heart. And so on...', it is a great trouble to the Ōkami that there are many hypocrites and the blind and deaf who, while being drawn by the rope of Kami's salvation, have their inner eyes unopened because their degree of hypocrisy is too deep, cannot even spiritually see the direction where the Ōkamibito of brilliant light resides, and attempt to criticize the words and deeds of the Kamibito from their own hearts filled with evil, judging everything externally. Unless all humans realize that they are dark and ignorant, believe and follow the power of Kami who is the supreme good, supreme beauty, supreme benevolence, supreme love, supreme wisdom, and supreme righteousness, and accumulate the virtue of obeying the command and following the command, it is impossible to open the Tenkai within one's own heart. It is natural that those who cannot open the Tengoku within their own bodies cannot lead a peaceful life in either the Genkai or the Yūkai. Therefore, when one looks at the state of those gathering in the same hall in the Genkai, praising Kami, worshiping Kami, and listening to the Oshihe of Kami, it appears that they are lined up in good order within the same Miroku-den, and it is a fact that they are certainly lined up when viewed from the material world. However, when one enters the world of thought and observes carefully, some of their spiritual bodies are in the Reikoku, some are listening or worshiping in a Dantai of the Tengoku, some are occupying seats in the Chūyūkai and listening, and some are completely turning their backs on Kami and facing the Jigokukai. Therefore, depending on the people who listen to this story, it may be heard as a gospel from heaven or the words of Kami's salvation, or it may be heard as a Rakugo from a theater, or felt as a clumsy Naniwabushi; and to the ears of a hypocrite wandering in the Chūyūkai, it is felt as an imprudent story, by no means the words of Kami, but as if the humorous and witty thoughts of Mizuuki Onisaburō were projected and became a story, causing some to feel ridicule and contempt, while others feel it is the unrestrained guardian deity of the scribe, and feel that the spirit of the narrator believes it is the words of Kami and compiled it, while others stop their ears and flee quickly, taking the words as ugly and violent language filled with strange, chaotic, mad, and filthy things. This is the state of people who place themselves in the Reikai, worship Kami from the position of the Dantai each person possesses, and listen to the story. Therefore, although this story is truly a gospel of salvation for people of high spirits, it is most difficult for the ears of the arrogant and inferior humans of the middle path to accept. Furthermore, it permeates well and is easily understood by the ears of innocent children and the elderly who have transcended the material desires of society. This is because children and the elderly are in a state of innocent heart, and are harmonized and in Sauō with the deepest Reikoku and Tengoku.

綾の聖地に於ける神の大本は大なる形式を有する高天原であつて、其教を宣伝する聖く正しき愛信の徹底したる各分所支部は、聖地に次ぐ一個の天界の団体であり又、自己の内分に天国を開きたる信徒は、小なる形式の高天原であることは勿論である。故に霊界に於けるすべての団体は、愛善の徳と信真の光と、智慧証覚の度の如何によつて、同気相求むる相応の理に仍り、各宗教に於ける一個の天国団体が形成され、又中有界地獄界が形成されてゐるのも、天界と同様、決して一定のものではない。され共大神は天界中有界地獄界をして一個人と見做し、之を単元として統一し玉ふ故に如何なる団体と雖も、厳の御霊瑞の御霊の神格の中より脱出することは出来ない、又之を他所にして自由の行動を取ることは許されないのである。

高天原の全体を統一して見る時は、一個人に類するものである。故に諸々の天人は其一切を挙げて、一個の人に類する事を知るが故に彼等は高天原を呼んで、大神人といふのである。綾の聖地を以て天地創造の大神の永久に鎮まります最奥天国の中心と覚り得る者は、死後必ず天国の住民となり得る身魂である。故に斯かる天的人間は聖地の安危と盛否を以て、吾身体と見做し、能く神界の為に、愛と信とを捧ぐるものである。

高天原の全体を一の大神人なる単元と悟りし上は、すべての信者は其神人の個体又は肢体の一部なることを知るが故である。霊的及天的事物に関して、右の如き正当なる観念を有せざる者は、右の事物が一個人の形式と影像とに従つて配列せられ和合せらるることを知らない。故に彼等は思ふやう、人間の外分をなせる世間的、自然的事物即ち是人格にして、人は之なくんば人の人たる実を失ふであらうと。故に大神人の一部分たる神の信者たる者が斯の如き自愛心に捉はれて、孤立的生涯を送るに至らば、外面神に従ふ如く見ゆると雖も、其内分は全く神を愛せず、神に反き、自愛の為の信仰にして、所謂虚偽と悪との捕虜となつたものである。斯の如き信仰の情態に在る者は決して神と和合し、天界と和合することは出来ない。恰も中有界の人間が、第一天国に上つて、其方向に迷ひ、一個の天人をも見ることを得ず、胸を苦め、目を眩して喜んで地獄界へ逃行く様なものである。

人間の人間たるは決して世間的物質的事物より成れる人格にあらずして、其能く真を知り、能く善に志す力量あるに仍ることを知るべきである。此等の霊的及び天的事物は即ち人格をなす所以のものである。而して人格の上下は、其人の智性と意思との如何に仍るものである。

大本神諭に……灯台下は真暗がり、結構な地の高天原に引寄せられ乍ら、肉体の欲に霊を曇らせ、折角宝の山に入り乍ら、裸跣足で怪我を致して帰る者が出来るぞよ。これは心に欲と慢心とがあるからであるぞよ。云々……と示されあるを考ふる時は、折角神の救ひの綱に引かれ乍ら、其偽善の度が余り深きため、心の眼開けず、光明赫灼たる大神人のゐます方向さへも、霊的に見ることを得ず、何事もすべて外部的観察を下し、おのが邪悪に充ちたる心より神人の言説や行為を批判せむとする偽善者や盲聾の多いのには大神も非常に迷惑さるる所である。凡て人間は、暗冥無智なる者なることを悟り、至善至美至仁至愛至智至正なる神の力に信従し、維れ命維れ従ふの善徳を積むに非ざれば、到底吾心内に天界を開き、神の光明を認むることは不可能である。吾身内に天国を啓き得ざる者は到底顕界幽界共に安楽なる生涯を送ることは出来ないのは当然である。故に現界にて同じ殿堂に集まり、神を讃美し、神を拝礼し、神の教を聴聞する、其状態を見れば、同じ五六七殿の内に行儀よく整列して居る様に見えてゐる、又物質界より見れば確実に整列してゐるのは、事実である。併し其想念界に入つて能く観察する時は、其霊身は霊国にあるもあり、又天国の団体にあつて聴聞せるもあり拝礼せるもあり、或は中有界に座を占めて聞き居るもあり、又全く神を背にし地獄に向つてゐるのもある。故に此物語を拝聴する人々に仍つて、或は天来の福音とも聞え、神の救ひの言葉とも聞え、或は寄席の落語とも聞え、或は拙劣な浪花節とも感じ、又中有界に彷徨ひたる偽善者の耳には不謹慎なる物語にして、決して神の言葉にあらず、瑞月王仁の滑稽洒脱の思想が映写して、物語となりしものの如く感じ、冷笑侮蔑の念を起し、之に対する者もあり、或は筆録者の放逸不覊の守護神に感じて、口述者の霊が神の言葉と自ら信じ、編纂せしものの如く感ずる者もあり、或は其言を怪乱狂妄悉皆汚穢に充ちたる醜言暴語となして耳を塞ぎ逸早く逃げ帰るものもある。之は霊界に身をおいて、各人が有する団体の位地より神を拝し、且物語を聴く人の状態である。故に此物語は上魂の人には実に救世の福音なれ共、途中の鼻高や下劣なる人間の耳には最も入り難きものである。又無垢なる小児と社会の物質欲に超越したる老人の耳には能く沁み渡り、且理解され易きものである。こは小児と老人は其心無垢の境涯に在つて、最奥の霊国及天国と和合し相応し居るが故である。
