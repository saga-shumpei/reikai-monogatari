Chapter 12: お客さん [1286]

お客さん〔1286〕

§ 1

At the Genkuwanguchi of the forest of the shrine, Yoru is acting as the Uketsuke as usual. A man of immense stature, tall enough to pierce the clouds and wearing a Fukaamigasa, appears there, and in a deep, rumbling voice says:

Man: 'Setsushiya am a person who has come from the Iso mansion of Mount Ubusuna. Takahime-dono should be here; please convey to her that I wish to see her privately for a moment.'

Yoru: 'Yes, it is not that I cannot convey your message, but I must ask for your Goseimei, for this is the Ikimiyasama of Giritenjiyōhinodenokami, after all...'

Man: 'Indeed, I apologize for the delay. There are circumstances that make it difficult to give my name, so it will suffice if you simply say that a man whose name includes the sound To has come. And if you convey that I am a person holding a Jiueu position at the Iso mansion, Takahime-dono will surely understand.'

Yoru: 'I have heard that you are a Jiueu Yakuninsama of the Iso mansion; I am Yoru, who has Kijiyun from the Brahman Kyō, and I ask for your kind favor in all matters.'

Man: 'Ah, so you are the man called Yoru. I have certainly heard of you from Isokohime-dono. That is Gokurau. You should pay a visit to the Iso mansion sometime.'

Yoru: 'Yes, Arigata-gozaimasu. Although I am serving in this Goyō, I cannot even hold a conversation without knowing the true Honzan, so I desire to go very much, but the Ikimiyasama of Hinodewake says that my spirit is not yet polished, and that I must not make a pilgrimage until she permits it, so I am refraining. I truly wish to polish my spirit quickly and be allowed to tread upon the grounds of the sacred land. It makes me uneasy to be acting as Uketsuke without even knowing the sacred land.'

Man: 'In any case, please convey that a person whose name includes To says that he has a matter he wishes to discuss in secret.'

Yoru says:

Yoru: 'I understand. Please wait here for a while.'

Having said this, he hurries to the living quarters of Takahime, opens the sliding door to the inner room softly, and sees that Takahime is leaning against an armrest, in the midst of being lost in thought.

Yoru: 'Excuse me, Hinode-no-Kami-sama, a large man whose name includes To has arrived from the Iso mansion, saying he wishes to see you; he said he has a matter he wishes to discuss in secret, so I am reporting this to you for a moment.'

Takahime: 'What, you say a large man whose name includes To has come from the Iso mansion?'

Yoru: 'Yes, he is a very large man indeed. And he said he is a person serving in the most high-ranking duty of the sacred land.'

Takahime nods happily and says:

Takahime: 'Ya, Yoru-dono, tell him I shall see him. However, please wait in the next room for a while; if I have him come immediately, I will not be prepared, so when I ring this bell, you shall come out slowly. Until then, please wait in the waiting room, perhaps smoking some tobacco.'

Yoru: 'Yes, I understand. Please ring the bell firmly, for I shall take that sound as the signal to come and welcome him...'

Takahime: 'Ah, I ask that of you. But Yoru, until Hinode-no-Kami permits it, you must not tell anyone that a person named To has arrived.'

Yoru: 'Ehehehe, I am not a Yoru who would speak of any secret, so please rest assured. You are under the protection of Hinode, and I am under the protection of Yoru, so Yoru's pleasure is also quite grand, indeed.'

Takahime: 'Kore-kore, Yoru, you should not say unnecessary things. Now, hurry and wait in the next room. And who did you ask to take over the Uketsuke?'

Yoru: 'Yes, I asked Haru-ko to do it.'

§ 2

Takahime: 'Un, good, good, that is fine; that man is a sensible Ningen, after all. You too, when the person called To arrives, must be sensible.'

Yoru: 'He-he-he-he, Isai shiyōchi itashimashita.'

Scratching his head, he went to the next room and decided to wait for Takahime's ahizu. Takahime stood up smoothly, and while peering out of the windows here and there, as if relieved that not a single person was around, she sighed with relief and spoke to herself,

Takahime: 'No matter what one says, he is a man after all. Because Tōsuke-sama said such Kizuyo things, I had been resenting him just a little, but he must have spoken so coldly because he thought we were in front of Ōzei no hito. Ah, I have done a terrible thing by not knowing a man's heart. Ah, Tōsuke-sama, if I hear of that gentle heart of yours, Mohaya Takahime can die Tokushin with this. Now, I must fix my face, smooth my hair, and Kika my Kimono.'

Suddenly, she applied white powder thickly and with Neni, tied her hair in the Masaoka style, changed into a new Kimono, donned a purple hakama with a flourish, let a bit of the red collar show, and while standing and sitting before the Kiyōdai,

Takahime: 'Ah, this is good, this is good. It is as if the Sangokuichi bridegroom-dono is coming. With this, Takahime's Guwanmau will finally reach Jiyōjiyu. Ah, Tōsuke-sama, Osananajimi are truly wonderful things. My, you have come so well. Is it not a bond that we even had a child together? I truly feel as if flowers have bloomed on a Kareki. You are a manly person from nowhere; as I expected of Takahime's desire, you have advanced even to the position of Fukukyōshiyu, one step Ichidanuhe of Mokusuke-sama, so it is no wonder that Takahime feels anxious. Now, now, it would not do to keep you waiting forever; now that I have applied this much makeup, there is no problem whenever you come. However, I have started to feel somewhat embarrassed. Ho-ho-ho-ho, even though I have aged, I somehow recall the old days, and my face feels as if it has suddenly become hot. Truly, a woman's heart is a gentle thing. If Tōsuke-sama were to see this innocent heart, he would surely be satisfied. I-hi-hi-hi-hi, ah, here, here, Yoru-kō, it is fine now, please go and tell that person called To so.'

Forgetting to ring the bell, she called out in a seductive voice. Yoru, who had been waiting for the bell to ring, heard Takahime's unexpected voice, opened the sliding door, and fearfully poked his head out,

Yoru: 'Hinode-kami-sama, do you have any business with me?'

As he said this and looked up, Takahime had completely changed; she was wearing splendid attire, had applied white powder thickly, and the wrinkles on her cheeks were all smoothed over. Yoru was surprised and said,

Yoru: 'Iya, this is, this is, Hinode-kami-sama, how young you have become. Ya, now I understand. As expected of a Kami-sama, is it not? Heen, I am envious.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Yoru, you should not tease me so much. Come, quickly, go and guide To-sama here.'

Yoru: '(In a theatrical tone) Etsu-he-he-he, certainly... shiyōchi... itashimashita, I shall go immediately.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Yoru, what are you saying? Go quickly. Truly, truly, you are a man who is not sensible.'

Yoru: 'Hinode-kami-sama, well, please try using me; I am quite sensible...'

Saying this, he ran out to the front, faced the large man, and said,

Yoru: 'This is, this is, To-sama, when I spoke to Hinode-kami-sama, she pondered for a short while and did not give an easy reply, but when I, this Yoru, spoke of various things, she said that since you had come all the way here, she must meet you even if only out of duty. She said that no matter what, she is the Giri-tenjō Hinode-kami who values duty, and she gave orders to guide you to the back... Come, please follow me. She is a very beautiful person.'

Man: 'Yes, Arigata, but since I have come on a Naimitsu matter, I wish to keep my Kazuki on as it is; would that be a Sashitsukahe?'

Yoru: 'I am not a Yoru who would be so lacking in wit for such a thing. I am the Yoru-kō who is your Shiyugo. He-tsu-ha-ha-ha!'

Man: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha, then I ask for your Goannai.'

Guided by Yoru, he proceeded with loud footsteps, thumping and stomping, into the Ima of Takahime. Takahime, with her heart pounding—doki, doki—at the sound of the footsteps approaching step by step, was waiting for her Kohibito to enter with the feeling of Hitoikisenshiu. And when the Ōotoko opened the sliding door, it seemed that a sense of bashfulness surged over her all at once, and she sat with her head bowed limply.

Man: 'Gomen-nasai, Takahime-sama, I was Shitsurei the other day; you must surely be in a state of Goritsupuku, I suppose.'

Takahime finally opened her mouth and said,

'Tōsukesama, you have done well to come and visit me. I am truly sorry for having resented you out of the immature heart of an onago... Wait, are you not Yoru? Did you not say you would be witty?'

Yoru: 'Ha-i, Shiyōchi-itashimashita. Please, do enjoy yourselves intimately, with Tō-sama...'

Takahime: 'E-i, you should not say such unnecessary things. Is that not Shitsurei to our Kiyakusama?'

Yoru, holding his head with both Riyōte, bent his waist and left this place, walking away dejectedly.

Takahime: 'Moshi, Tōsukesama, please take off your Kazuki. And since no one will come to this Ima, please Uchikutsuro and I request that you speak with me at your leisure.'

Man: 'I feel somewhat awkward, and remembering things of the past, I feel embarrassed even though I have grown old, atsu-ha-ha-ha-ha.'

Takahime: 'Moshi, Tōsukesama, I too feel embarrassed, you know, just like on the mountain path in Jerusalem, ho-ha-ha-ha.'

The man said, 'Takahime-dono, Gomen-kaburimasu,' and suddenly removed his Kazuki. When she looked, it was not Tōsuke, but the god of the Tokiokishi, Mokusuke.

Takahime: 'Ya, you are the Tokiokishi-kami-sama... My, my, what a bad stomach you have...'

Tokioki: 'Aha-ha, it would be fine if it were Tōsukesama, but a truly unsophisticated man has come, so this must be a great nuisance for you.'

Takahime: 'This is indeed a welcome visit. What is the matter for which you have sought me out?'

Tokioki: 'The truth is, I have taken my leave from the Iso mansion and have come here.'

Takahime: 'How is it that you, a person of such high divine virtue, have come to such a situation?'

Tokioki: 'It is difficult to say such things in front of you, but in truth, due to a dispute over administrative matters with Tōsukesama, I resigned against my will; that is the outward appearance, but in fact, I was thrown out by Tōsukesama.'

Takahime: 'My, my, what a pitiful thing that must be. Tōsukesama was not such a bad person, so why did he become so hard-hearted? The human heart is a thing that cannot be understood.'

Tokioki: 'Takahime-sama, you must feel the same, must you not? He made nothing of the kindness you showed by following him all the way from Jitentō-shima, and he gave you the cold shoulder in front of many people like that, putting you to shame; you must understand well enough. If it were me, being a kind person like you, how could I ever do such a hard-hearted thing?'

Takahime: 'That is true, Tōsukesama is truly a heartless person. He was not such a cold person when he was young, you know.'

Tokioki: 'It is unbearable to have as many women as Tōsukesama does. Even Imokohime-sama and Isokohime-sama of the Iso mansion, while having husbands, cast amorous glances at him, and besides that, he is a man who is diligent with his chopsticks toward all the women of the holy land, regardless of age, so he is picking them all up; it is because you do not know that, that you made such a mistake. As for me, as you know, I am an unsophisticated widower, so not even a single female cat would look my way. Atsu-ha-ha-ha-ha.'
