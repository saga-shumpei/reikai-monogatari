Chapter 15: 彗 Stars [1289]

彗星〔1289〕

§ 1

Otora, Magahiko, and Yoru, the three of them, argued bitterly with Takahime and departed from the forest of the shrine, proceeding toward the mansion of Iso. When they reached the vicinity of Futokorodani, the sun had set completely. Strange to say, a great comet appeared in the southern sky. Yoru looked up at the sky and said,

Yoru: 'Look, Otora-sama, Magahiko-sama, look at that comet. Is it not appearing right above the capital of Haruna? It must surely be a sign of some strange event.'

Otora: 'Indeed, indeed, it is a mysterious comet. I wonder if that is the Halley's Comet, which is said to appear once every eighty years.'

Yoru: 'That must be it; it is surely a portent that some great event will occur in the world.'

Maga: 'What, that is the Takahime star. It is an evil star that clouds everything with its tail, so I do not know what it is called, but I, Magahiko, shall call it the Takahime star.'

Yoru: 'Let us compose a poem about that comet.'

Yoru: 'The gloomy air filling the earth rises to the heavens and condenses, suddenly turning into a comet, radiating evil light and exhaling strange smoke. What kind of evil omen is this, after all? Poisoned by the evil light of the comet, the color of the Shibi Palace has changed drastically. Thinking of this, it must be a sign that a great calamity will soon arrive in the world. That evil star will eventually violate the Shibi Palace. That time will truly be a time requiring vigilance. The downfall of Ōkuninushi is clearly appearing even now within the celestial Shibi Palace.'

Maga: 'Ha ha ha ha, hearing such things makes my heart uneasy. However, if it is the truth, there is nothing to be done.'

Otora: 'Magahiko-sama, please perform a Norinaoshi of Yoru-sama's Kototama; everything is a manifestation of God, after all.'

Magahiko: 'All things in the universe are the manifestation of the Lord God. When the human body receives the Holy Spirit and the divinity of the Lord God, only then can it connect and act in common with all things in existence.'

Yoru: 'Ah, now I feel a little relieved. Since it has become dark, shall we take a rest here?'

Otora: 'That would be fine, for the sun does not set on the road at night.'

So saying, they sat upon a rock by the roadside and rested their breath for a while.

Otora: 'Looking up at the heavenly sky, the comet seems to be a mechanism to sweep away the dust of the world.'

Magahiko: 'Looking up at the sky, the Takahime comet sweeps people away like trash.'

Yoru: 'The comet appearing in the night sky has swept away the songs of the sky-Takahime.'

Otora: 'Comet that appears once in eighty years, show yourself again and protect me. Even in the great sky, the evil is gradually disappearing by the power of Halley's Comet.'

Magahiko: 'Come now, let us three together cross the Kajika Pass and proceed to the mansion of Iso.'

Yoru: 'Climbing the night road, my body is righteous in heaven, bidding farewell to the Hinodewake God.'

Thus they sang to each other, and once again they quickened their pace, climbing the steep slope, and Otora began to sing.

Otora: 'Through the mediation of Chirukowake, I intended to visit the mansion of Iso. Accompanied by Magahiko-sama, when I came to the forest of the shrine, the banners of the Ten Stars were fluttering. High in the Plain of High Heaven, the rafters were tall, and the great palace pillars were thick. There dwells the truly venerable Great God of the Three-Five. The eight-fathom palace was also newly built and lined up, and Yoru-sama was sitting properly at the reception desk, looking very solemn. Otora stepped forward and asked about the situation, realizing that the official of Takahime was there. Though I do not know what kind of person it is, I rejoiced to meet the living palace of the Hinodewake God, whom I have revered as my teacher until now.'

Otora: 'Together with Magahiko-sama, I had an audience with Takahime-sama. At the incomprehensible oracle, Otora was completely dumbfounded. Without a word to answer, we argued for a few words. With all affection and courtesy exhausted, we finally flew out of the mansion. Together with Magahiko-sama, we quickly prepared our travel gear and departed. Following behind, Yoru-sama chased after us on the night road. Here, the party of three, helped by the grace of God, climbed the steep slope that seemed to touch the clouds. Just as we climbed, how strange, a comet shining in the sky. What is it a sign of? Though I do not know good or evil, right or wrong, this is a scene that is not easy.'
