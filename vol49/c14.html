Chapter 14: Great 妨言 [1288]

大妨言〔1288〕

§ 1

In the living room of Takahime, three people—Takahime, Otora, and Magahiko—sit in a triangle, listening intently to Takahime's sermon.

Takahime: 'Magahiko-sama, it is fine for you to visit the Iso Mansion. I shall by no means stop you, but since you would only bring shame upon yourself by going to the Iso Mansion in your current state, you must listen to the sermon of Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami until I, this Takahime, say that you may go without any hindrance.'

Magahiko: 'I am grateful for that, but I cannot linger so idly. No matter how great you may be, Anata, the origin remains the origin, after all. I did not come here with the intention of visiting the shrine forest. Since I have been permitted by Matsuhime-sama to go directly to the Iso Mansion to hear the teachings, even if I am under your care tonight, I shall certainly go to the Iso Mansion tomorrow to undergo training for about a hundred days.'

Takahime: 'Now, Magahiko, are you not being a bit arrogant? No matter how much Matsuhime-sama tells you to go to the Iso Mansion, how can one without Shinriki go there? There is no one who does not know that you were originally a disciple of Takahime. If a person like you, whose training is insufficient, goes to the Iso Mansion, it will not only be your shame, but people will think, "Did Takahime really have such an ignorant person as a disciple?" It will immediately become the shame of this Takahime. Therefore, you may go to the Iso Mansion only after you have trained sufficiently here and received permission from Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami.'

Magahiko: 'In that case, how many days should I stay here under your care?'

Takahime: 'Let me see, it would probably be a hundred days at the earliest, and two hundred days at the latest.'

Magahiko: 'I cannot stay for such a long time. I have taken leave for a hundred days, including the days for the round trip, so if I were to stay in a place like this for a hundred days, would I not be unable to visit the Iso Mansion? That would mean I lied to Matsuhime-sama, so in any case, I shall go to visit with Otora-sama tomorrow.'

Takahime: 'Even if it takes a hundred or two hundred days, how could you possibly go if you do not possess the virtue to ascend to the sacred ground? It is precisely because I hold you dear that I am being careful with you like this. Try to think about it well. No matter how much the soul of a Yotsumado-nin-soku wandering in the Chūyūkai tries to peek into the heavenly realm, it is only dazzling and actually painful; you would be exposed and have to flee back. You should examine your soul's connection well by receiving the Fudesaki of Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami here, and if you have the qualifications to visit, then go. In any case, polishing the soul is the most essential thing.'

Magahiko: 'Takahime-sama, was Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami not me?'

Takahime: 'That is so; for a while, I had you say that to the public, but I shall not let you keep that up forever. The true Hinodenokami is this Takahime. Hen... excuse me.'

Magahiko: 'I have had enough of talk about soul connections, Giritenjiyō, and Hinodenokami. When Matsuhiko-sama appeared at Kohokuzan, everything was completely exposed as a fraud; the one you call Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami is a large black fox, a servant of the Golden-haired Nine-tailed Fox. Are you also happily worshipping that black fox?'

Takahime: 'Now, Magahiko, what an outrageous thing to say. Do not be so foolish as to call Hinodenokami-sama a fox. Magatsu must be dwelling inside your belly. That is what made you see such things. That is why the gods gave you the name Magahiko. If you speak such things, I will not let you go to the Iso Mansion no matter what.'

Magahiko: 'Otora-sama, what shall we do? Takahime-sama is saying such things, you know.'
