§ 1

Takahime, seeing that the three, Otora, Magahiko, and Yoru, had rejected her counsel after exhausting thousands of words and had departed in a forceful manner, felt that this was a grave crisis; unable to contain her agitation, she clawed at her own chest while crying out:

Takahime: 'Oh, Giritenjiyō-sama, what are you doing? At a time like this, why do you not cast the Fudō-Kanashiba to stop them? Hey, hey, Iru, Iku, Saru, Haru, Teru, what are you doing? Why do you not hurry and chase after them? Oh, how frustrating! With five rough men here, why can you not capture them when I am so anxious? Do you not hear the command of Hinodenokami?'

Iru: 'Takahime-sama, why do you not ask Giritenjiyō-sama directly? Did you not say that you always govern the Sanzensekai freely? Is there anything that the Ikimiya of Giritenjiyō, who possesses such Shinriki, cannot bring back? If you do not show us your Goshinriki at a time like this, we cannot truly submit to you from the bottom of our hearts.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Iru, what kind of senseless things are you saying? Hinodenokami-sama is the great Ōkami of Ten, and he is performing the role of supreme administrator. In other words, he is like a Prime Minister; it is the duty of the police to go and capture humans. Where in the world is there such an outrageous thing as a god in the position of a minister performing the duties of the police? That is why I am telling you five to become police and spies and go capture them.'

Iru: 'If your god is a Prime Minister, we are but like governors. Governors cannot perform the duties of spies or police, you see.'

Takahime: 'Oh, what a dull-witted man you are. A god should be able to appear and disappear freely. To be great is to govern all in the universe, and to be small is to hide even within a speck of dust; that is the work of a god. If you cannot do such things, how can you serve the purpose of the shrine forest?'

Iru: 'If the Ōkami can transform and appear or disappear in various sizes, then is it not fine for him to become a policeman or a spy and go capture them?'

Takahime: 'That is the work of Hinodenokami’s Ikimiya when he is alone. Since there are five rough men here, you are a thoughtless person to try and place the burden of labor on me alone. Can you call that the way of the gods? If Hinodenokami were to do everything by himself, there would be no reason to keep five blockheads like you around, would there?'

Iku: 'Hey, Takahime-sama, you said you would keep five blockheads like us around, but, hah, excuse me, I am not receiving orders from you. Is it not you who came here as a freeloader on your own? If you are going to complain so much, we shall have you leave.'

Takahime flew into a rage, her eyes glaring, and she suddenly grabbed Iku by the collar of his garment.

Takahime: 'Hey, Iku, do not think you can insult me because I am a woman; I will not forgive such rude language. I am the Ikimiya of the august Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami, a magnificent missionary of the San-go-kyō, the priestess of the Ikuta forest, and the guardian deity of the jewel of Ryū. Furthermore, I am the wife of Kamimokusuke, the chief administrator and time-keeper of the Iso mansion, and one of the three crows of the San-go-kyō. If you dare to speak rudely once more, just try it; I will twist off your jaw and everything else.'

She twisted his cheek with all her might. Iku cried out:

Iku: 'Ouch, ouch, ouch, ouch! Have mercy, have mercy!'

Takahime: 'Since your cheekbones are so prominent, I will pull them out until there is not even a single tooth of a geta left.'

She pinched him even harder. Saru, unable to watch any longer, grabbed both of her legs from behind; at that moment, Takahime somersaulted and flew out to the entrance, even offering her steamed bun to the heavens, and scrambled to her feet in a flurry.
