Chapter 2: Great Deity 人 [1276]

大神人〔1276〕

§ 1

As stated in the previous section, those Tennin who are registered in the various organizations of Reigoku and Tengoku, as well as those Tennin on Chijiyō—that is, human Seirei who have attained a deep understanding of Kami—being themselves Tennin on Chijiyō, do not concern themselves with the Kaui of the human Nikutai, but rather observe the Ishi that causes that Nikutai to perform its Dōsa. Therefore, they never engage in the foolishness of attempting to judge the Zenaku of the Kaui of humans, regardless of whether they are one's Chiyōjiyō or one's Kaboku. This is because those who have advanced to the Ichi of a Tennin possess the Dōsatsu that their Jinkaku resides in their Ishi, and is never in the Kaui itself. Although their Chisei is also a part of their Jinkaku, it is only regarded as Jinkaku when it acts in Itsuchi with their Ishi. Ishi arises from the Jiyōdau of love, and Chisei originates from the truth of faith. Therefore, a faith devoid of love can never be regarded as Jinkaku. Love is the righteous Ishi that loves Kami first, and then loves one's neighbor. Merely believing in Kami is not enough to touch the love of Kami and attain the happiness of the Reikon; it is impossible. Love unites with love, and wisdom unites with wisdom. Offering pure treasures to Kami with all one's heart, or dedicating material goods, is the manifestation of what is called love. Through that love, Kami always grants humans what is necessary. Humans preserve their lives and continue to elevate their Jinkaku through what is thus granted. Because Kami is formless, or is a gas, or exists as formless or gaseous, Kami never demands material things like the people of the Genkai; it is the height of delusion to attempt to win the favor of Kami by offering money or goods. It is sufficient to simply have faith in Kami, but if that faith cannot be recognized through scientific knowledge, it is equivalent to foam. Therefore, those who preach that one must stand upon scientific principles before believing in Kami are all Yotsumado Ningen, and the majority of them are specters whose inner depths are open toward hell, turning their backs on Kami and fearing the light.

The fact that Tennin of Reigoku and Tengoku perceive the celestial realm as a single form is not something that applies to its entirety. No matter how enlightened the eye of a Tennin may be, it is impossible to measure or know the entirety of Takamagahara. However, it is to the extent that Tennin may look at a group consisting of hundreds or thousands of Tennin from a remote Ichi and perceive it as a single unit forming a human-like form. Therefore, it goes without saying that the Yotsumado Ningen, who are still lost in the Chūyūkai, are far from reaching the virtues, faith, and enlightenment of the Tennin.

As such, even any Tennin is not in a position to fully grasp the entirety of Takamagahara, to be well-versed in the divine plan of Kami, or to be able to see and hear about other various organizations in detail. Thus, there is no reason why Yotsumado Ningen—who are immersed in the self-interest and greed of the natural world, who take self-love and worldly love as the supreme moral law, who wear the mask of a good person, and who have only just recognized the direction of Kami—could ever measure the Ishi of Kami. The reason why the entirety of Tengoku is collectively referred to in the divine realm as the Great Deity-Human (Ōkamibito) is because the form of the celestial realm is governed as a single individual. Therefore, the Takamagahara of the earth is one Great Deity-Human, and the person in charge of the Reikai who represents that Takamagahara, illuminates the virtues of love and goodness and the light of faith and truth, and seeks to grant wisdom and enlightenment to humans lost in darkness, is the Great Deity-Human. It is something that could be called the root of the Deity-Human or the Deity-Human of the root. From the perspective of the principle of the correspondence between the Genkai and the Yūkai, this is by no means a fictional statement. Furthermore, the general believers are the individual entities that make up the heart, lungs, head, waist, and even the extremities of the limbs of this one Great Deity-Human.

The Great Deity governs the celestial realm as such, as a single individual, that is, as a single unit. Therefore, humans are called a microcosm of the universe, a small heaven and earth, and the administrators of the divine plan of heaven and earth. It is well known that the human body, whether viewed as a whole or as an individual, is organized from myriad things. That is, viewed from the whole, there are limbs, respiratory organs, and internal organs; viewed from the individual, there are fibers, nerves, and blood vessels. Thus, although there are limbs within limbs and parts within parts, when it acts as a single individual, it acts as a single unit. Therefore, each believer, as an individual, should strive to make the heart of the Great Deity-Human—who is a single unit—their own heart, to construct Tengoku on earth, and to ensure that not even a shadow of the hellish realm remains. The way the Great Deity governs Takamagahara is also the same as this. Therefore, at the sacred ground of Aya, which is the Takamagahara on earth, the Holy Spirit filled with the divine character of the Great Deity comes to the prophet, demonstrates the virtues of love and goodness based on the divine character of Kami, illuminates the light of faith and truth, grants wisdom and enlightenment, makes the living beings on earth into earthly Tennin, and further makes everything on earth into Tengoku, striving so that when they enter the Reikai, all people may dwell forever in the joy and pleasure of Tengoku. Each missionary and believer has the mission to participate in this great divine work, regarding that unitary Deity-Human as the entirety of one individual, becoming an individual entity, harmonizing from top to bottom, and uniting the wise and the foolish.

霊国天国の天人が天界を見て一個の形式となすのは、其全般に行わたつてのことではない。如何なる証覚の開けた天人の眼界と雖も、高天原の全般を測り知ることは出来ない。されど天人は数百又は数千の天人より成れる団体を遠隔の位地より見て、人間的形式をなせる一団と感ずる事がある位なものである。故に未だ中有界に迷へる八衢人間の分際としては到底、天人の善徳や信真や証覚に及ばないことは無論である。

斯の如く如何なる天人と雖も、高天原の全体を見極め、神の経綸を熟知し、且他の諸団体を詳しく見聞し能はざる位のものであるに、自然界の我利我欲にひたり、自愛と世間愛のみを以て最善の道徳律となし、善人面をさげ、漸く神の方向を認めたる位の八衢人間が到底神の意思の測知し得らるべき道理はないのである。天国の全般を総称して大神人と神界にては称へらるる理由は、天界の形式は凡て一個人として統御さるるからである。故に地の高天原は一個の大神人であり、其高天原を代表して愛善の徳と信真の光を照らし、暗に迷へる人間に智慧と証覚を与へむとする霊界の担当者は、即ち大神人である。神人の大本か大本の神人か……と云ふべき程のものである。之は現幽相応の理より見れば、決して架空の言でもない。又一般の信徒は所謂一個の大神人の体に有する心臓、肺臓、頭部、腰部、其他四肢の末端に至る迄の各個体である。

天界を大神は斯の如く一個人として、即ち単元として之を統御し玉ふのである。故に人間は宇宙の縮図といひ、小天地と云ひ、天地経綸の司宰者と云ふ。人間の身体は、其全分にあつても、其個体に在つても、千態万様の事物より組織されたるは、人の能く知る所である。即ち全分より見れば、肢節あり、気管あり、臓腑あり、個体の上より観れば、繊維あり、神経あり、血管あり、かくて肢体の内に肢体あり、部分の中に部分あれ共、一個人として活動する時は、単元として活動するものである。故に個体たる各信者は一個の単元体たる大神人の心を以て心となし、地上に天国を建設し、地獄界の片影をも留めざらしむる様、努力すべきものである。大神が高天原を統御し玉ふも亦之と同様である。故に地上の高天原たる綾の聖地には、大神の神格にみたされたる聖霊が予言者に来つて、神の神格に仍る愛善の徳を示し、信真の光を照らし、智慧証覚を与へて、地上の蒼生をして地的天人たらしめ、且又地上一切をして天国ならしめ、霊界に入りては、凡ての人を天国の歓喜と悦楽に永住せしめむが為に努力せしめ玉ふたのである。其単元なる神人を一個人の全般と見做し、各宣伝使信者は個体となつて、上下和合し、賢愚一致して此大神業に参加すべき使命を有つてゐるのである。

§ 2

When one can create the form of an Enman Dantai, the whole is as the part, and the part is as the whole; the point of difference between the two exists only in the matter of their Bunriyō. The state of affairs in the Seichi today is such that all things stand individually separated and active, and the whole cannot harmonize with the Buntai; the individual, who is a Buntai, takes their own Shizenteki observation as a Kiten, and according to their own whims, turns against the light and distances themselves from love. The most excellent among them merely wander in the Chiūkai, while the inferior ones only cast Shiuha toward the Dantai of Jigoku. Therefore, these Ningen are the images of demons that defile the Seijiyō of the Ōkami, the Takaamahara of the earth, and as individuals, they are hypocrites. A hypocrite may at times speak of goodness, teach goodness, and perform goodness, but in all things, they prioritize their own love. There are those who appear most profound, like heavenly beings or sages, when speaking to others about the divine nature of the Ōkami, the state of Takaamahara, the virtue of love, the principles of faith, and the future of Takaamahara. Even if there are those who claim that what they speak is the unity of heart, word, and deed, and attempt to demonstrate it in their actions, adorning their conduct to become a model for others, many of them act only so that what they actually think may be known to others or so that they may be praised. These are, even among hypocrites, a fairly superior portion at the present time, and from the perspective of the vulgar eye, they can be seen as believers who truly understand God and possess a pure faith in the unity of heart, word, and deed.

Next, when one observes the heart and nature of those who belong to the most superior portion in the current Seichi of Aya through the spiritual eye—that is, through internal observation—they are not yet well-versed in the tidings of the heavenly realm. Even their self-love and worldly love are not yet thorough; they criticize the existence of heavenly beings with an attitude of half-belief and half-doubt. Or, even if they speak of life after death, the most superior portion merely speaks and teaches as if they know well that which they have not truly accepted into their own hearts, all for the sake of being seen by others as philosophers enlightened in the truth. Furthermore, even if they say extremely splendid things with their mouths, the majority are those who do not move their hands and feet, do not sweat on their brows, and thus do not execute sincerity toward God. Such people, or ritual officials who transmit the teachings of God or serve God, are either Yotsuji-ningen who misunderstand that being busy with worldly affairs or tilling fields and handling filth like fertilizer is what is called defiling God, or they are lazy people who, out of sloth, dislike manual labor and hide behind the beautiful names of propagandists or ritual officials. All of these, before God, do not spiritually recognize even one heavenly being, nor can they feel them physically; eventually, they become hungry ghosts of the Jigoku-dō who use God and Buddha as a means to satisfy their own greed. To preach the teachings of God and serve near to God with such a heart and nature is to be a sinner who completely blasphemes God.

Such Ningen regard the words of God as if they were the efficacy descriptions on patent medicine; they believe in nothing, have no thought of performing virtue outside of themselves, avoid doing good where others cannot see, hide evil before others, and desire that goodness, no matter how small, must always be displayed before others. Therefore, if by any chance they perform a good deed, it is all because of what they do for their own sake. Also, if they perform good for the sake of others, it is nothing more than a desire to be seen by others and the world as a sage or a benevolent person. Everything such a person does is for the sake of self-love. Self-love is what is called the love of Jigoku. There are even hypocrites who come to hear this story at the 567-dono, though it may be against their will. There are those who come to listen reluctantly, thinking, 'I must not be thought of as ignoring the intention of the narrator, who night and day has painstakingly dictated and compiled the Reikai Monogatari, and who wishes to read it aloud every night to explain the tidings of the spiritual world to those who are lost...' Never is such concern necessary. No matter how much one attempts to flow into or transmit to the Mitama of people whose inner depths are closed toward heaven, it is ultimately futile. Therefore, those who truly do not like this story should cease such hypocritical behavior and, based on the love of the Lord, I hope they will freely conduct research appropriate to their own Mitama. I am by no means forcing the listening or reading of this story.

斯くの如き人間は神の言葉を売薬の能書位に心得、何事をも信ぜず、又自己を外にして徳を行ふの念なく、人の見ざる所に於て善をなすことを忌み、悪を人の前に秘し、善は如何なる小さきことと雖も、必ず人の前に現はさむことを願ふ。故に彼等がもし万一善なる行ひをなしたりとせば、それは皆自己の為になす所あるによる。又他人の為に善を行ふことあれば、それは他人及世間より聖人或は仁者と見られむことを願ふに過ぎない。斯の如き人のなすことはすべて自愛の為である。自愛は所謂地獄の愛である。

心ならずも五六七殿に此物語を聞きに来てゐる偽善者も偶にはあるやうだ。それは折角昼夜艱苦して口述編纂した『霊界物語』を毎夜捧読して、霊界の消息を、迷へる人々に説き示さむとする口述者の意思を無視したと思はれてはならないから……といふ位な考へで、厭々聞きに来る人もあるのである。決して左様な御気遣は無用である。何程内底の天に向つて閉塞したる人々の身魂に流入し或は伝達せむとするも、到底駄目である。故にどうしても此物語の気にくはぬ人は、かかる偽善的行為を止めて、所主の愛に仍り、身魂相応の研究を自由にされむことを希望する。決して物語の聴聞や購読を強るものではない。

§ 3

It is said that there are some respectable persons who, upon listening to the Shinyu of the Sutra, feel as if tears might fall, yet upon hearing the Monogatari of the Latitude, find it devoid of any Shinmi, and remark that it is like a Don Quixote-style tale or a Yosekibun atmosphere. This, too, is due to the principle of Mitamasauō, and thus it cannot be helped. However, that the extreme of sorrow is joy, and the extreme of joy is sorrow, is something that even scholars of Shizenkai often proclaim. And sorrow closes Tengoku, while joy opens it. When a Ningen laughs without any deep thought, it is never a time of sorrow; it is a time filled with Omoshiro and Kuwanki. Kami makes Kuwanki Itsushiyun life, and exists within love. When an Akago cries, its Hahaoya rushes to feed it, and seeing the child's Egaho and rejoicing is precisely love. There is no parent who thinks it pleasant to make their own child cry or feel sorrow. The heart of Kami is such that, even for an Itsushiyun, He desires to fill Ningen with Kuwanki and have them enjoy and conduct all Jigefu. If there be any person who feels Fukuwai because this Monogatari lacks Majime and causes laughter, that person is one who has a defect in what is called Seishinjiyō, and is either a madman or a hypocrite. It is an attitude of falsehood and vanity, as Senmatsu of Sendai Hagi said: 'Even if I am hungry, I am not famished.' As long as one does not abandon such thoughts, no matter how much one may worship before Kami, praise Kami, or preach of love, one can never enter Tengoku. Such a person is a fool who strives to force their way into the gate of Jigoku.

All those who have been drawn to the sacred ground of Aya by the grace of Kami, and all believers in various regions who follow this teaching, are all within the divine character of the Ōkami. Yet, as the saying goes, 'it is dark at the foot of the lighthouse,' those who cannot recognize this are unable to maintain the personality of a Tennin (a form similar to Ningen). Just as the saying, 'Visiting Fuji while seeking Fuji,' goes, once one has entered into Mount Fuji, one does not know that it is a spiritual mountain more noble than any other, and it appears as an ordinary mountain. However, when one views it from a distance, one can see its pure form standing tall above the clouds, and its sublimity and greatness that surpasses all other fresh peaks and clear mountains; in the same way, those who are far away from the path and have not entered the teaching, when they remove their colored glasses, can know its outline and survey the whole. Thus, those who have not yet heard a single word of the teaching and have not set foot within its circle are the ones who know its truth. Furthermore, the Ōkami sometimes manifests as a Tennin in Tengoku, and in the Genkai, that is, the Takamagahara of the earth, He manifests as a Kamibito. However, Ningen whose inner parts are blocked in this way, while directly facing the Kamibito and listening to His teaching, regard Him as an ordinary person, or as a respectable person equivalent to themselves, or as someone slightly superior, or even as someone inferior to themselves, and treat Him accordingly. Such Ningen are not merely at a crossroads, but are already facing the great gate of Jigoku with their toes pointed toward it. Those who lack true wisdom and enlightenment have no path other than to sink into Jigoku. Therefore, when such Ningen are viewed through the eyes of a Tennin or a Kamibito, no matter how splendidly their form is adorned, and no matter how good their character and physique may appear, their inner parts are almost entirely lacking the physiognomy of a Ningen. Because they dwell in sin and falsehood, they are consequently in opposition to the wisdom and enlightenment of Kami. Like monsters or hungry ghosts, their ugliness is such that one cannot bear to look at them. In the Kamikai, such Ningen of flesh are not called life, but are referred to as spiritual dead. They are also called Shaba-moja or Gari-gari-moja.

凡て綾の聖地に、神の恵に仍つて引つけられたる人、及此教に信従する各地の信者は、すべて大神の神格の中にあるものである。然るに灯台下暗しとか云つて、之を認め得ざるものは天人（人間と同様の形態）の人格を保つことは出来ないものである。富士へ来て富士を尋ねつ富士詣で……と云ふ様に、富士山の中へ入つて了へば、他に秀れて尊き霊山たることを知らず、普通の山と見ゆるものである。併し遠くへだてて之を望む時は、実に其清き姿は雲表に屹立し、鮮岳清山を圧して立てる其崇高と偉大さを見ることを得る様に、却て遠く道をはなれ、教に入らざりし者が、色眼鏡を外して見る時は、其概要を知り全般を伺ふことが出来る様に、却て未だ一言も教を聞かず、一歩も圏内に足をふみ入れざる人の方が其真相を知る者である。又大神は時によつて一個の天人と天国にては現じ玉ひ、現界即ち地の高天原にては一個の神人と現じ玉ふ。されど斯の如く内分の塞がつた人間は神人に直接面接し且其教を聴き乍ら、之を普通の凡夫とみなし、或は自分に相当の人格者又は少しく秀れたる者となし、或は自分より劣りし者となして、之を遇するものである。かくの如き人間は八衢所か、已に地獄の大門に向つて、爪先を向けてゐるものである。真の智慧と証覚とを欠いた者は、総て地獄に没入するより道はない。故にかかる人間は天人又は神人の目より見る時は、何程形態は立派に飾り立て、何程人品骨格はよく見えても、殆ど其内分は人間の相好が備はつてゐないのである。彼等は罪悪と虚偽とに居るを以て、従つて神の智慧と証覚に反いてゐる。恰も妖怪の如く餓鬼の如く、其醜状目も当てられぬばかりである。斯の如き肉体の人間を称して、神界にては生命といはず、之を霊的死者と称ふるのである。又は娑婆亡者或は我利我利亡者ともいふ。

§ 4

Those who are separated from the virtue of the love of the Great Deity have no life. And when one is separated from the love or the Shinkaku of the Great Deity, one can accomplish nothing. Therefore, it is shown in the Ōmotoshinyu: 'Without the protection and permission of Kami, nothing can be achieved. Even when things have reached ninety-nine percent, they will be overturned. What humans think is good and perform, if it lacks the permission of Kami, all becomes evil. At ninety-nine percent, the palm of the hand will turn over, and it will end in a gasp...' By reverently listening to this, one should be able to understand the Shōsoku of this matter. Humans, through the self-love of the Shizenkai, can to a certain degree act in an Eukuwaiteki and Daseiteki manner, but they can never achieve the beauty of Iushiu. If one looks at the Jitsujiyō of all banks, companies, and other Shiyodantai that are formed today from self-love and worldly love, all of them run counter to their Saishiyo Shiyoki, and within them, Chimimauriyō are Haikukai and jumping about, making them dens of monsters and apparitions, harboring ugly conditions that one cannot even bear to look at. And is it not the case that the hellish path of the strong devouring the weak and the survival of the fittest is being manifested without regret?

Even in the present age, the Kami seen in the hearts of those whose minds are upright is in the form of the people of ancient times, but those who are in a life of self-attained wisdom and sin, having severed the inner flow from the heavenly realm, have extinguished such original evidence. Such blind persons attempt to see the Kami that cannot be seen, and those who have extinguished the evidence through a life of sin are those who never seek Kami. Therefore, even among modern humans who cling to Kami, because there are many who have severed the inner flow from the heavenly realm, they attempt to see the Kami that cannot be seen, and those hellish humans who are only busy with material desires and have extinguished their original evidence do not acknowledge the existence of Kami and greatly dislike Kami. The Shinkaku itself, which first flows into humans from the heavenly realm, is truly this original evidence. This is because the birth of a person is not for the manifest world; its purpose is to make the organization of the heavenly kingdom perfect. Therefore, no one can enter the heavenly realm without the concept of Shinkaku.

Those who do not know what the Shinkaku that forms the organization of the Takamagahara and the heavenly spirit realm is, cannot even ascend to the first gate of the Takamagahara. If a human whose only external aspects are opened in this way were to mistakenly attempt to approach the gate of the heavenly kingdom, they would feel a kind of repulsive force and a strong sense of aversion. This is because their internal aspects, which should receive the heavenly realm, have not yet entered the form of the Takamagahara, and thus all the gates are closed. If one were to forcibly break through these gates and attempt to enter the Takamagahara, their internal aspects would become even more tightly closed, leading to an irremediable state. Among believers, there are those who forcibly approach the Takamagahara on earth, and after serving near Kami and listening closely to the teachings, their internal aspects become even more tightly closed, their minds and bodies become confused, their mental state becomes inferior to what it was before their faith, and furthermore, extraordinary hellish activities appear in their conduct; this is based on this principle. Those who deny the Great Deity and do not believe in the Kami-person who is filled with the Shinkaku of the Great Deity will all fall into such a fate. The life of the heavenly realm within a human refers to a mental state that knows thoroughly that one is living in accordance with the truth of Kami. Kannagara Tamachi Haemase.

斯の如き大神の愛の徳に離れたる者は生命なるものはない。而して大神の愛又は神格に離れた時は、何事もなし能はざるものである。故に大本神諭にも……神の守護と許しがなければ、何事も成就せぬぞよ。九分九厘いつた所でクレンとかへるぞよ。人間がこれ程善はないと思ふて致して居ることが、神の許しなきものは皆悪になるぞよ。九分九厘で手の掌がかへり、アフンと致すぞよ……と示されてあるのを伺ひ奉つても、此間の消息が分るであらう。人間は自然界の自愛に仍つて、或程度までは妖怪的に、惰性的に出来得るものだが、決して有終の美をなすことは出来ない、今日自愛と世間愛より成れる、すべての銀行会社及其他の諸団体の実状を見れば、何れも最初の所期に反し、其内部には、魑魅魍魎の徘徊跳梁して、妖怪変化の巣窟となり、目もあてられぬ醜状を包蔵してゐる。そして強食弱肉優勝劣敗の地獄道が、遺憾なく現実してゐるではないか。

現代に於ても心の直なる者の胸中に見る所の神は、太古の人の形なれ共、自得提の智慧及罪悪の生涯に在つて天界よりの内流を裁断したる者は斯の如き本然の所証を滅却し了せるものである。斯かる盲目者は見る可らざる神を見むとし、又罪悪の生涯にて所証を滅却せし者は、神を決して求めない者である。故に現代の人間は神にすがる者と雖も、すべて天界よりの内流を裁断したる者多き故に、見る可らざる神を見むとし、又物質欲のみに齷齪して、本然の所証を滅却した地獄的人間は、神の存在を認めず、又神を大に嫌ふものである。すべて天界よりして先づ人間に流入する所の神格其者は実に此本来の所証である。何となれば、人の生れたるは、現界の為にあらず、其目的は天国の団体を円満ならしむる為である。故に何人も神格の概念なくしては天界に入ることは出来ないのである。

高天原及天国霊国の団体を成す所の神格の何者たるを知らざる者は、高天原の第一関門にさへも上ることを得ない。かくの如き外分のみ開けたる人間がもし誤つて天国の関門に近付かむとすれば、一種の反抗力と強き嫌悪の情を感ずるものである。そは天界を摂受すべき彼の内分が未だ高天原の形式中に入らざるを以て、すべての関門が閉鎖さるるに仍るからである。もし強て此関門を突破し、高天原に進み入らむとすれば、其内分はますます固く閉ざされて如何ともす可らざるに至るものである。信者の中には無理に地の高天原に近付き来り、神に近く仕へ親しく教を聞いてからますます其内分が固く閉ざされて、心身混惑し、信仰以前に劣りし精神状態となり、且又其行ひの上に非常な地獄的活動の現はるるものがあるのは此理に基くのである。大神を否み、大神の神格に充されたる神人を信ぜざる者は、凡てかくの如き運命に陥るものである。人間の中にある天界の生涯とは即ち神の真に従ひて、棲息せるものなることを知悉せる精神状態をいふのである。惟神霊幸倍坐世。
