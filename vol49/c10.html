Chapter 10: 添書 [1284]

添書〔1284〕

§ 1

Harukuniwake caused the Rānchi Shiyōgun and the Katahiko Shiyōgun of the forest of Ukiki to surrender, and while preaching and instructing them in the Kyōri of Ananai along the way, he advanced as far as the forest of Kurusu. Then, turning to Otora, he said:

Harukuniwake: 'Otora-sama, you were an enthusiastic Kimoiri of the Uranai-kyō, but it is truly a great joy for us that you have thus surrendered to the Ananai-kyō and become a fine Shinjiya. However, how would it be if you were to make a Gosanpai to the mansion of Iso, receive the permission of Ōkamisama, and serve as a fine Sendenshi? It might be far more convenient than remaining a common Shinjiya.'

Otora: 'Yes, thank you very much. I wonder if even an old woman like me could be made into a Sendenshi.'

Harukuniwake: 'Whether you are an old woman or anything else, there is never any Kubetsu of Rauniyaku in your Mitama itself. Since old people are inevitably innocent, you might even become a finer Sendenshi than us. I shall write a letter, so take this, cross the river of Kajika, make a pilgrimage to the mansion of Iso, and after meeting with Lord Yashimano-mikoto, undergo training for about a hundred days; then, become a fine Sendenshi and serve for the sake of the divine realm. That is for the sake of your afterlife more than anything else.'

Otora: 'If even a wicked old woman like me can repent, I wonder if I could perform work even as much as the dirt under your fingernails. If so, I shall follow your instructions and make a pilgrimage once.'

Harukuniwake: 'In that case, I shall write the letter for you now. Please take this and go.'

So saying, he took out his yatate from his waist and wrote something fluently on a sheet of paper. According to the text of the letter:

'From Harukuniwake, I offer this introduction to Lord Yashimano-mikoto. I have now received various trials from the gods along the way, have received vast and boundless divine virtue, and while thanking for the depth of divine grace, I have finally arrived safely at the forest of Kurusu. Furthermore, the vanguard of the Brahman army, Rānchi and Katahiko Shiyōgun, have now completely surrendered to the Ananai-kyō due to the divine virtue of Ōkamisama, so I humbly request that you report this to Ōkamisama. As I am thinking of having these people undergo a little more preparatory education and then, by means of a letter, have them enter the mansion for training and request that they be appointed as Sendenshi as a result of their training, I ask for your kind favor in all matters. Now, the bearer of this letter is a woman called Otora, a former female chivalrous person of the village of Ukiki who held the reins of the Uranai-kyō of Mount Kokita. Harukuniwake, on the way to the expedition, met my foolish younger brother Matsuhiko, who was the secretary to Katahiko Shiyōgun in the forest of the shrine, and that Matsuhiko immediately surrendered to the path of Ananai and went to the headquarters of the Uranai-kyō of Mount Kokita, and it is he who finally caused Tokage-wake, Magahiko, and Otora to surrender under the divine virtue. Therefore, I ask for your kind favor toward the bearer of this letter, Otora-sama. She is somewhat superstitious and has a tendency to go off track, but I believe that if you educate her sufficiently, she will become a suitable Sendenshi. That is all.'

He wrote this and handed it to Otora. Otora, with a look of triumph filling her face and squaring her shoulders, bade farewell to Harukuniwake and his party, and set off alone toward the mansion of Iso.

On the way, she passed by Mount Kokita and, thinking she would at least stop by once to meet her beloved Okiku, and also meet Katsuhime, Magahiko, and others to tell them of the Kyōri she had realized, and to make the sacred ground of Mount Kokita flourish all the more, she returned in high spirits during her pilgrimage. The sacred ground of Mount Kokita still had a fair number of Shinjiya gathered. However, she did not see many faces that Otora recognized. This was because, although they had believed the Hegure Shrine of Mount Kokita and other gods to be the true gods, it was exposed that they were merely nameless evil gods, and because it was ridiculous to be mocked by relatives, friends, and acquaintances, the former Shinjiya did not come around much. Furthermore, since the reform, the former Shinjiya had been filled with dissatisfaction for some reason. This is because there were those who, having believed in the living shrines or the karmic connections of the souls of noble gods and having been proud of their faith, had their faith exposed as nothing but evil gods, and although they were saved from great calamity by small calamity, their hearts had become unhappy for some reason.

途中小北山の傍を通り兎も角一度立寄つて最愛のお菊に巡り合ひ且松姫、魔我彦其他に面会し自分の悟り得た教義を云ひ聞かし、小北山の聖場をして益々栄えしめむと、参拝の途中意気揚々として立帰つた。小北山の聖場は依然として信者が相当に集まつてゐる。然し乍らお寅の見覚えのある顔は余り沢山に見当らなかつた。何故ならば小北山のヘグレ神社、其他の神々を誠の神と信じてゐたが、サツパリ名もなき邪神たりし事を曝露され、親族朋友知己等より嘲笑さるるのが馬鹿らしさに、前の信者はあまり寄り付かなかつたからである。さうして改革以来何とはなしに前の信者は不平に充たされたからである。今迄尊き神の生宮又は霊魂の因縁を信じ得意になつて信仰してゐたのが、何でもない邪神であつた事をスツパぬかれ大難を小難に救はれ乍ら何とはなしに心面白くなくなつた者もあるからである。

§ 2

Otora stepped up to the Uketsuke and saw that Bunsuke was still working with all his might on a painting. Looking closely, it was neither a turnip, nor a daikon radish, nor a Kurokuchinaha. On the side, he had written 'Protection of the Sunrise,' and a sun like crimson was shining on the trunk of an old pine tree. He was painting a quite splendid picture. Otora suddenly called out to him,

Otora: 'Hey, Bunsuke-sama, I hope you are well. You are painting a splendid Kakejiku as always. Have you given up on the Riujinsama already?'

Bunsuke, whose eyes were poor, did not dream that it was Otora, and replied,

Bunsuke: 'Welcome, pilgrim. I do not know who you are, but please pass through to the back. Until now, this Seichi has enshrined the Hegure Jinshiya, the Tanemono Jinshiya, and various other Kamisama, but since the Kyōso Imoriwake-sama and Otora-sama have fled, we have replaced them with the Ōkamisama of Ananai-kyō. Therefore, I must repaint the Kakejiku as well, so with the permission of the Ōkamisama, I am writing this Kakejiku of a pine tree and the sunrise as you see. If you also wish to practice Goshinkau, I will give it to you, so please mount it and enshrine it. They call it the age of the sunrise and the age of the pine; if you enshrine this, your family will be safe, your business will prosper, and even when you become a spirit, it will serve as a passport to heaven.'

Otora: 'Hey, Bunsuke-sama, will you not pull yourself together? A pine and a sunrise are truly splendid, but I am Otora.'

Bunsuke: 'I thought you were someone I remembered hearing. Ah, is it Otora-sama? Well then, thank you for returning; Okiku-sama and everyone else must be overjoyed, to say nothing of myself. I feel a sense of excitement rising within me. In that case, I shall report this to Matsuhime-sama and Magahiko-sama. Please wait a moment.'

Otora: 'No, no, you stay here and attend to the Uketsuke. Rather than having a person with poor eyesight move about, I, this capable Otora, will return to my own living quarters... Okiku should be there as well. If I do that, I will have Okiku notify Matsuhime-sama and Magahiko.'

So saying, she hurried toward her own living quarters. Behind her, Bunsuke tilted his head repeatedly and muttered to himself,

Bunsuke: 'Ah, Otora-sama’s character has truly improved immensely. She was like a completely different person. The way she spoke and everything about her seemed to make light emanate from her body. Even I, who have associated with her for so long, can hardly recognize her; the power of divine virtue is truly a magnificent thing. Now then, I shall go and offer my thanks to the Kamisama for the joy of Otora-sama’s return.'

So muttering to himself, he trudged toward the shrine. Before returning to her own quarters, Otora visited the shrines of Kohitayama one by one, and just as she was about to rest upon returning to her quarters, the rumor that Otora-sama had returned spread before she knew it, and Magahiko and Okiku came running from the Matsuhime mansion in a panic,

Okiku: 'Mother, you left with Matsuhiko-sama for the sake of propagation, so have you returned after only a few days have passed? Did you shrink back and flee again?'

Otora: 'What, shrink back? I have returned having received splendid divine virtue. Okiku, have you been performing your duties well since then?'

Okiku: 'Yes, I have been doing well. Please do not worry about me. And has Mankō-sama been doing well?'

Otora: 'Ho ho ho, so you are concerned about Mankō after all. No, I am relieved to hear of your reliable state of mind.'

Okiku: 'Mother... oh, how unpleasant, you immediately let your mind wander to strange places.'

Otora: 'Even so, you do not say how Gosankō-sama is, or how Aku-sama is, do you?'

Maga: 'Otora-sama, thank you for returning. Since then, the divine work in this Seichi has been developing extremely harmoniously, so please be at ease.'

Otora: 'Magahiko-sama, please guard this Seijiyō so that no Datsusen may occur. I have received a letter of introduction from that distinguished personage, Harukuniwake, who is the Sensei of Matsuhiko-sama, and I intend to go to the mansion of Iso to perform a one-hundred-day ascetic practice and return as a splendid Sendenshi, so please be happy for me.'

Magahiko: 'That is extremely Ketsukō. By all means, please perform your training without any Buchōhafu and return as a splendid Sendenshi. If I were granted permission, I too would like to make a Sanpai once as a commemoration of my Kaishin.'

Otora: 'Why don't you also inquire about the Gotsugafu of Matsuhime-sama, receive leave, and make the Sanpai together with me? As they say, seeing is believing; unless you visit and worship at the Seichi of Ubusuna-yama at least once, you will not be able to practice the Oshihe of Manzoku, nor will you receive the divine virtues.'

Magahiko: 'If you wish for that, it would be Ketsukō, but...'

Okiku: 'Mother, Magahiko-sama, I shall go and inquire with Matsuhime-sama now. Please take your time and wait while drinking tea with Magahiko-sama.'

Saying this, she leaves and hurries up the narrow stone steps of two hundred to the mansion of Matsuhime. Afterwards, Magahiko turns to Otora.

Magahiko: 'Otora-sama, it seems your personality has completely changed. Look at the luster of your face and even your hair has turned black, has it not? Truly, even your voice has changed, so you seem like a different person.'

Otora: 'Thanks to you, I have been rejuvenated by the heat of the love of the Gods. And illuminated by the light of faith, I feel as if light is emanating from my body from nowhere, ho ho ho ho. If I get conceited from being praised again, I will fall into the bottom of the valley, so let us stop at this point.'

Magahiko: 'By the way, Otora-sama, have you obtained the money that Imoriwake-sama ran away with?'

Otora: 'Magahiko-sama, are you still thinking about such things as money? Even hearing about money makes me feel ill. Imoriwake-sama is also a person of a naturally detached disposition, so he gave all that money away to others and is now penniless. And he is living in harmony with Otami.'

Magahiko: 'What, is he living together with Otami? Eh, eh.'

Otora: 'Hey, Magahiko-sama, what is that "eh"? Do you still have feelings of attachment remaining for Otami? If so, you cannot achieve Kaishin. What is that "eh"?'

Magahiko: 'I was about to say that you have done an "eh" (excellent) thing. Eh, the thing called En (fate) is a mysterious thing.'

Otora: 'It is useless to try to deceive me by saying half-hearted things. Otami also seems to have awakened, and now she is only associating with Imoriwake-sama as a friend of the Hon no Oshihe. Otami has also achieved a great deal of Kaishin. I am thinking that once you receive splendid divine virtues, I will act as a mediator and have you two married.'

Magahiko: 'Will you really take care of that for me?'

Otora: 'Why, would I tell a lie? I have been watching the situation of Otami and Imoriwake-sama, and it seems neither has any lingering attachment. Rather, it seems Magahiko-sama is the one who is favored by Otami, so please be happy.'

Magahiko: 'Ehe-tsu-he-ho-ho-ho, are you not mistaken?'

Otora: 'The "eh" from before and this "eh" have a very different tone, don't they? Oho ho ho, what a mercenary man you are.'

Magahiko: 'Otora-sama, have your feelings of attachment toward Imoriwake-sama already been removed? It is somehow suspicious. Judging by your appearance, you seem somehow happy and youthful. This is something that cannot be unless there is something happy.'

Otora: 'Hey, Maga-yan...'

She taps his shoulder two or three times with her open palm.

Otora: 'Do not make a fool of me. It is not such a thing that makes this Otora happy. Once I have awakened to the path of sincerity... how ridiculous... at my age, to think of such foolish things as love or money in my dreams? Do not look down on people so much. This Otora is a little different from such weak-willed women. Hen, to try to judge my heart by comparing it to your own, you are an outrageous man.'

魔我『本当に世話をして下さいますか』

お寅『何、嘘を云ふものか。私はお民と蠑螈別さまの様子を気をつけて考へてゐたが、どちらにも未練がない様だ。却て魔我彦さまの方がお民の気に入つてる様だから、マア喜びなさい』

魔我『エーヘツヘヽヽヽ、違やしませぬかな』

お寅『最前のエーとは同じエーでも大変に調子が違ますな。オホヽヽヽ、何と現銀な男だ事』

魔我『お寅さま、お前さまは蠑螈別さまに対する恋着は、最早、とれたのですか。何うも怪しいものですな。貴女の御様子と云ひ、何だか嬉し相に若々してゐられます。之は何か嬉しい事がなくては叶はぬ事だ』

お寅『これ魔我ヤン……』

と肩を平手で二つ三つ叩き、

お寅『馬鹿にしておくれな。此お寅はそんな事が嬉しいのではありませぬよ。一旦誠の道に目が覚めた上は……阿呆らしい……恋の、金のと、よい年をして、そんな馬鹿げた事が夢にも思へますか。あまり人を見下げて下さいますな。お寅はそんな柔弱な女とはチツと違ひますよ。ヘン、自分の心に引き比べて私の心を忖度しようとは、怪しからぬ男だな』

§ 4

Magahiko: 'My apologies. There is a saying that a sparrow does not forget its nature until it is a hundred, so... I just happened to ask. I pray you will overlook and forgive my rudeness. If you would please change your mood and obtain the permission of Matsuhime-sama, I ask that you take this Magahiko to the Seijiyō of Mount Ubusuna just once.'

Otora: 'Ah, very well. Since one cannot become a Sendenshi if one is like a frog at the bottom of a well who has never seen the world, it is excellent to make a Sanpai to the Goshiyutsugenchi of the Ōkami to broaden your Kenbun.'

As they were speaking, Okiku returned with Matsuhime and Chiyo.

Okiku: 'Mother, Matsuhime-sama has arrived. Come, offer your Goaisatsu.'

Otora looked back, saw Matsuhime, and while smiling with great joy, spoke gently with her hands placed on the ground.

Otora: 'This is indeed Matsuhime-sama, thank you for your daily Goshinmu. I am sure my naughty Okiku has been a great trouble to you. I received a letter from the Sendenshi of Chikokuwake and was on my way to train at the Iso mansion, so I stopped by to offer my Goaisatsu and make a Sanpai to the Kami. I humbly ask for your continued favor regarding Okiku.'

Matsuhime: 'Is that you, Otora-sama? Thank you for stopping by. Please do not worry about Okiku-sama. After you departed, Okiku-sama, Bunsuke-sama, and Magahiko-sama have all been studying very hard. Thanks to that, believers come for Sanpai every day, and the Goshintoku increases day by day, which is truly Ketsukō. And Otora-sama, your face has gained a wonderful luster. Judging by the color of your hair, you look as if you have become twenty years younger even at a glance. Truly, Goshintoku is a grateful thing.'

Otora: 'Yes, well, I am but a water-dipper in a privy, ho ho ho ho.'

Magahiko: 'Ha ha ha ha, if that is the case, it seems you have floated quite a bit in the Forest of Ukiki, Otora-sama.'

Otora: 'Ho ho ho ho, I have found a lover. That is why I have become young like this.'

Magahiko: 'Ha ha ha ha, I thought something was funny, and at last you have spoken your true heart.'

Matsuhime: 'The lover of whom Otora-sama speaks must be the Mizu no Mitama Kami, Susanoo Ōkami-sama. That is truly a fine lover you have chosen. I also consider Susanoo-no-Mikoto-sama to be my only lover, ho ho ho ho.'

Magahiko: 'This is outrageous. It is fine for Otora-sama, but does not Matsuhime-sama have a splendid lover, or rather a husband to whom you pledged your life in two worlds, named Matsuhiko-sama? Is this not a bit unfaithful?'

Matsuhime: 'Firstly, I yearn for the Ōkami, and secondly, I yearn for my husband. That is why I call him my husband of two worlds.'

Magahiko: 'My, I have heard plenty of your self-praise. Magahiko is satisfied with this. However, I have one request; I would like to accompany Otora-sama for a while to visit the Seijiyō of Mount Ubusuna, would you not permit it?'

Matsuhime: 'That is a wish come true; in fact, I had been thinking I wanted to have Magahiko-sama go for training once. However, I refrained because I did not want it to be thought of as a woman's meddling. That is Ketsukō, but Otora-sama, I entrust Magahiko-sama to you, so please take good care of him.'

Otora: 'Yes, yes, once I have taken charge of him, I will not let him do anything reckless. Please be at ease.'

Magahiko: 'Then, Matsuhime-sama, I shall take my leave for a while.'

Matsuhime: 'When you visit the sacred ground, please say that Matsuhime sent her regards.'

Otora: 'Yes, I understand.'

After resting for a while, Otora accompanied Magahiko, offered remote worship at each shrine, finished greeting Bunsuke at the reception and the many believers, crossed the single-log bridge, and headed toward the mountain pass of Kajika, her elderly feet moving quite vigorously as she chanted the sacred text and advanced with great spirit.
