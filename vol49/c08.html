Chapter 8: スマート [1282]

スマート〔1282〕

§ 1

The night wind is cold, and even with the snow falling, it is a lonely grass pillow. Though Hatsuwakahime sleeps peacefully on her bed of dew, Rokukō and Hachikō, the two men enticed by the wind of cowardice, tremble and shake, their teeth chattering as they embrace each other. Praying that the breaking of the night might come even a moment sooner, they feel the pitiful state of their souls, which have been cast into the void with nowhere to rest. The darkness grows ever deeper, and even the stars in Amatsusora are hidden by black clouds, causing their hearts to pound with fear. Just then, a lantern bearing the family crest of Tokiokashi-no-Kami swings to and fro, approaching from the other side of the Itsuponbashi.

As the figure advances quickly toward them, the two men watch and, with a start, stroke their chests. 'This is surely Tokiokashi-no-Kami, who has come all the way from the Iso mansion to save us, his servants. He must have set out to rescue us.' Wishing not to show their Hikefumiren state, the two men suddenly stand up and, gazing at the approaching lantern, speak: 'Are you Mokusuke-dono, our master? We are Rokukō and Hachikō. Following your orders, we came here to test Hime-sama's courage, but though she resembles Hatsuwakahime, she is but a seventeen-year-old novice, and she speaks things that do not suit her character.'

'This woman is surely a monster. She intends to devour Hatsuwakahime-sama and, having transformed into her to deceive us, is sleeping here with Guusū Hachibē in such bold defiance. We beseech you, master, now that you have arrived, please join your strength with ours to strike down the enemy of Hime-sama. It is a most regrettable situation. I, too, was about to become prey to the monster, but through the divine virtue of Ubusuna-yama, you have been sent here to strike down the enemy of Hime-sama and save your servants. We are grateful for your arrival. Please, investigate quickly. Look, look, right there as I said.'

'With a bamboo hat pulled low, she is snoring away. She is a bold and fearless monster,' they say, even their voices trembling. Hearing this, Mokusuke laughs. 'You cowardly Roku and Hachi. In the first place, there is no such thing as a monster in this world. You are quite the cowards. Where is this monster? Guide me there at once.' When he shouts this, the two men reply, 'Yes, yes, right there, she is snoring. Please, you go first. My waist and bones have begun to tremble for no reason, and they will not obey my commands. If my body becomes free, I will listen to anything you say.'

Just as they say this, the figure wearing the hat stands up. In the long pampas grass on one side, Hatsuwakahime turns toward the three men with a gentle air. 'You three rough men, for what purpose do you follow in my footsteps in the middle of the night? Setting aside the two of you, Roku and Hachi, the absurdity of coming here pretending to be Mokusuke! Mokusuke is a man who serves in the role of general affairs at the Iso mansion. With the dignity of a high official, he would be concerned for his child, but he is not so reckless as to forget his divine duties and follow me all the way here. I have no such parent who would act in such an incomprehensible way. You are surely a manifestation of a demon.'

'You have blocked our path and, I suspect, plotted to prevent me from carrying out my divine duties. Leave this place at once. I am Hatsuwakahime-no-Kami. I have no time to deal with you, so I shall depart from here. You three should have a thorough discussion about your wicked plans.' Saying, 'Excuse me for going ahead,' she puts on her straw cape, takes up her staff, and walks away. Mokusuke calls out from behind, and saying, 'Hey, hey,' he follows after Hime, with Roku and Hachi in tow.

Hatsuwakahime chants the Amatsu Norito in a rhythmic tone and proceeds while singing a divine song. As the night begins to brighten, the surroundings become clear, and she sits on a rock on the side of the road to catch her breath, spending a short while lost in thought.

Hatsuwakahime sits on a rock at the mountain pass of Kajika-tōge and thinks:

'The monster that appeared last night is something I cannot comprehend. Both the two men, Roku and Hachi, and my father, Mokusuke—it is all beyond my understanding. When I set out on my journey, my father spoke to me so coldly; if he were to follow me, he should have been able to speak a few kinder words. Is this perhaps a divine arrangement by the Great God to test my heart? In any case, it is something I cannot comprehend.'

『神が表に現はれて 善と悪とを立別ける 悪魔の征途に上り行く 初稚姫の魂を 査べむ為か父となり 或は巨大な獅子となり 妾が首途を遮りて 所存の程を調べしか 但しは誠の曲神か 心に解せぬ事あれど 只何事も一身を 神に任せし上からは 仮令如何なる事あるも 初心を曲げずドシドシと 人にたよらず皇神の 神言のままに真心を 尽して往かむ吾心 仮令曲津は行先に さやりて仇をなすとても 吾には神の守りあり

今又神はスマートを 吾行先の供となし 与へ玉ひし尊さよ 神は吾等と共にあり 吾等は神の子神の宮 いかでか恐れむ敷島の 大和心をふり起し ハルナの都に蟠まる 八岐大蛇を言向けて 神素盞嗚の大神の 誠をあらはし奉り 五六七の御代を詳細に 普く地上に建設し 三五教の神力を 現はし奉る神の道 進みゆくこそ嬉しけれ あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸ひましませよ』

と歌ひ乍ら進み行く。
