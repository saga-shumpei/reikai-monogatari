Chapter 19: Kami 丹 [1293]

神丹〔1293〕

§ 1

Uzuhiko and Shizuko are sitting around a hibachi, lost in conversation.

Shizuko: 'Excuse me, Uzuhiko-sama, we as a family became captives of Brahmanism, were saved from a dangerous situation by Harukuniwake-sama, and on top of being unable to repay such a great debt, we have even been granted the position of palace keepers; is this not far beyond our humble merit?'

Uzuhiko: 'That is true. As you say, we have received the great kindness of heaven and earth, and furthermore, despite knowing nothing of the way of Kami, we have been appointed as masters of this mansion, which is truly an honor beyond our station. However, since we are complete amateurs in the way of Kami, it is a burden too heavy and quite troublesome. They speak of the Giritenjiyō of the Hinode no Kami, and an Ikigamisama has appeared and acts as if he were the master, but no matter what, he is an Ikigamisama, so there is nothing to do but follow his commands. Though we are called masters, it is a name without substance, and things do not go as we wish in the slightest; I wonder if the way of Kami is always like this.'

Shizuko: 'Even so, the six officials still treat even a person like me as a master, so is that not a wonderful thing? It is grateful that they respect a girl like Kaede, who knows nothing, by calling her Ojosama, Ojosama; this is entirely thanks to Kami.'

As they were talking, Kaede gently opened the sliding door and entered.

Kaede: 'Father, Mother, Takahime-sama has come to call for you, saying she wishes to treat you to a feast; will you go?'

Uzuhiko: 'That is her kind intention, and I cannot let it go to waste. Besides, if I refuse her bluntly, I must not hurt her feelings.'

Kaede: 'Do you intend to go as well, Mother?'

Shizuko: 'Since Uzuhiko-sama is going, there is no reason for me, his wife, not to go. I must not be thought of as a strong-willed woman by Giritenjiyō-sama. Since a distinguished person called Mokusuke-sama has arrived, I had been thinking that I should go once to offer my greetings.'

Kaede: 'In that case, Mother, Father, please go. Following that, last night in a dream, Monju Bosatsu appeared to me and gave me a medicine called Kamitan, saying, "Danger is approaching your parents, so if you have them take one pill each, it will be safe," and handed it to me. I thought I bowed to say thank you, and then I awoke from the dream. Even after waking up, these three splendid pills remained on my hand. Let the three of us take one pill each; they say that if we do, there will be no food poisoning or anything of the sort.'

Uzuhiko: 'That is grateful; it is truly a blessing from Kami. At any rate, let us take them and go. Hey, Shizuko, you take one too.'

Kaede: 'Monju Bosatsu said that this medicine will not be effective unless I give it directly from my hand to your mouths. Come, open your mouths.'

Uzuhiko and Shizuko both opened their mouths wide as Kaede commanded. Kaede popped one pill into each of her parents' mouths. Instantly, an indescribable fragrance enveloped the surroundings, their chests felt refreshed, and they felt as if light were emanating from their bodies. Kaede also held them to her forehead and took one herself. The three of them suddenly had beautiful complexions, and their beauty increased all the more. Just then, Takahime entered with a disgusting smile covering her face, gently opened the sliding door, and said:

Takahime: 'Excuse me. Uzuhiko-sama, Shizuko-sama, I have been here for some time, but because the divine work has been so busy, I have not been able to offer my proper greetings, and I am truly sorry for that. Therefore, as Mokusuke says he wishes to offer you a meal from his heart, it is a dish prepared by the hands of Giritenjiyō Hinode no Kami; it may not suit your palates, but would you, as a couple, be so kind as to come? The sake is also warmed and ready.'

Uzuhiko: 'Is that so? I should have been the one to offer my greetings to you first, so it is truly a shameful thing that you are treating us to a feast instead.'

珍彦、静子は火鉢を中に囲み、話に耽つて居る。

静子『もし珍彦さま、吾々親子はバラモン教の擒となり、危い所を治国別様に助けられ、御恩の返しやうもない其上に、こんな結構な宮番迄さして頂き、何とも冥加に余つた事ぢやありませぬか』

珍彦『さうだ。お前の云ふ通り山海の大恩を受け、其上、神様の事も分らないのに、此館の主人を仰せつけられ実に身に余る光栄だ。併し吾々は神のお道には全くの素人だから、余り荷が重過ぎて迷惑だな。日の出の神の義理天上とか云つて、生神様がお出になり、主人顔をして御座るが、何と云つても生神さまだから、維命維従ふより外はない。主人とは云ふものの有名無実で吾々の思ふやうには一寸もなりはしない、神様のお道と云ふものはこんなものかなア』

静子『それでも、六人の役員さまは矢張り私のやうな者でも主人と立てて下さるのだから結構ぢやありませぬか。楓のやうな何も知らぬ娘をお嬢さまお嬢さまと尊敬して下さるのだから有難いものだ、これと云ふのも全く神様のお蔭だわ』

斯く話す所へ楓は襖をそつとあけて入り来り、

楓『お父さまお母さま、今高姫さまが、貴方方に御馳走を上げたいと云ふて呼びに来られたらお出になりますか』

楓『お母さまも往く積りですか』

静子『珍彦さまが往かれるのに、女房の私が往かぬ訳には往きますまい。我の強い女だと義理天上様に思はれてはなりませぬからな。杢助さまと云ふ立派なお方がお出でになつて居るのだから、御挨拶に一度は往かうと思ふて居た所だ』

楓『それならお母さま、お父さま、お出なさいませ。就ては私夜前夢に文珠菩薩がお出になりまして、神丹と云ふ薬を下さいまして、「お前の両親の上に危急が迫つて居るから、これを一粒づつ飲ましておけば大丈夫」と渡して下さいました。有難う厶いますとお辞儀をしたと思へば夢は醒めました。目がさめましてもこんな立派な薬が三粒、手の上に残つて居ました。これを三人が一粒づつ頂きませう、さうすれば食当りも何もないさうですからなあ』

珍彦『それは有難い全く神様のお恵だ。何は兎もあれ頂いて往かう。オイ静子お前も頂きなさい』

楓『このお薬は私の手から口へ直接に上げなくては利かないと文珠菩薩が仰有いました。サア口をお開けなさいませ』

珍彦、静子二人は楓の命ずるままに口をパツと開けた。楓は一粒づつ両親の口へ放り込んだ。忽ち得も云はれぬ香が四辺を包み胸は爽かになり、身体から光が出るやうな心持になつた。楓も亦押頂いて自ら服用した。三人は俄に面色美しく、其美は益々美を加へた。斯かる所へ高姫は満面に嫌らしき笑を湛へながら入り来り、襖をそつと開けて、

高姫『御免なさいませ。珍彦様、静子様、此間から参りまして、余り御神業が忙しいのでとつくり御挨拶も致しませず、誠に済まない事で厶いました。ついては夫杢助が心許りの御飯を差上たいと申しますので、義理天上日の出神が手づから拵へましたる料理、お口に合ひますまいが、御夫婦お揃ひなさつて御出下さるまいか、御酒の燗も出来て居ますから』

珍彦『左様で厶いますかな、私の方から一度御挨拶を致さねばならぬのに、貴方の方から却つて御馳走をして頂くとは誠に済まない事で厶います』

§ 2

Shizuko: 'Ikimiyasama of the Sun-Rising Kami, if that is the case, we, as a married couple, shall gratefully accept your hospitality without reserve.'

Takahime felt inwardly overjoyed, thinking she had succeeded, and intentionally spoke in a seductive voice.

Takahime: 'This is indeed a prompt acceptance; I am satisfied for the sake of the Sun-Rising Kami. Mokusukedono will surely be delighted as well. By deepening our mutual friendship and participating in the divine work, the divine virtues will shine in all four directions, and consequently, the honor of Uzuhiko-sama, the master of this mansion, will resound throughout the world; it is a splendid thing. Now, please follow me.'

Uzuhiko: 'Yes, thank you very much. However, I must put on my hakama, so please return ahead of us for a moment. I shall come immediately.'

Takahime said, 'Please come quickly; I shall be waiting for you,' and left for her own living room.

Afterward, Uzuhiko, Shizuko, and Kaede washed their hands and rinsed their mouths, and prayed, 'Kamususanō no Ōkamisama, please save us from this crisis,' and appeared in Takahime's living room with an indifferent expression.

Uzuhiko: 'Thank you very much for inviting the likes of us, a married couple, despite your noble status. Accepting your kind words, and thinking it would be improper to decline, we have come as a couple.'

Takahime: 'That was a great effort on your part. I have nothing special, but the sake is warmed to just the right temperature. Now, please have a cup.'

Uzuhiko: 'Thank you very much.'

Saying this, he thought, 'A leap into the cauldron of hell; knowing it is poison, I drain the cup... Kamususanō no Mikoto, protect me,' and drank the cup poured by Takahime in one gulp.

Mokusuke: 'Oh, Uzuhiko-sama has been served by the Ikimiyasama of the Sun-Rising Kami. A man and a woman, a good combination. Then, I shall have Shizuko-sama pour for me. Hahaha, men and women are somehow a good arrangement, aren't they?'

Shizuko: 'Yes, thank you,' she said, and held out her cup while her hands trembled.

Mokusuke poured it to the brim.

Shizuko prayed to Kami with all her might, whispering, 'May the efficacy of the Kototama of the Divine Elixir be manifested,' and drank it dry.

Then, being served rice and various simmered dishes by Takahime, the couple filled their stomachs to the full.

However, there was not the slightest change in the bodies of the two.

Mokusuke and Takahime, contrary to their expectations, wore displeased expressions.

Uzuhiko intentionally made up an excuse and said,

Uzuhiko: 'Both of you, I have had so much that my head feels dizzy and my eyes seem to be blurring. And my stomach has started to ache. Please excuse us, and allow us to rest quietly in our own living room.'

Shizuko: 'Ah, I also feel somewhat nauseous. Having had so much sake, please excuse me.'

Takahime: 'Yes, are you feeling unwell? That is a pity; please go to your living room and rest as you please.'

The two said, 'Yes, thank you very much. If that is the case, we shall take our leave,' and while intentionally staggering their feet, they withdrew to their own living room, covered themselves with many futons, and feigned illness.

Watching them go, Takahime stuck out her long tongue and clicked her chin two or three times.

Mokusuke: 'Hahaha, the desire is fulfilled and the time has arrived. Namu Akuma Daimyōjin, protect us and bless us.'

Takahime: 'Ehehehe-hen. Ehehehe-hen. Ohohohoho. Ohohohoho.'
