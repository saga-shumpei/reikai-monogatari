Chapter 19: Jewel Field の Kami 丘 [1887]

玉野の神丘〔1887〕

§ 1

Treading upon the white sand of the forest of Tamano, fragrant with white plum blossoms, beneath the hoofs of their horses, and slipping through the shade of the aged pines, they at last arrive at the sacred place where the Kami Tamanohime-no-Kami dwells in peace.

This hill is called Tamanōka, a level highland stretching one ri from north to south and two ri from east to west, where the silvery sand shines brightly in the sun of Amatsu, and the evergreens encircling the divine garden intertwine their branches in winding trails, making it a sacred place that seems to have gathered the very essence of Shibitenkai.

Akitsuo-no-Kami rides his horse down to the foot of the mountain, and looking up at the summit, sees the five-colored curtains of red, white, purple, yellow, and blue hung all around, in a manner suggesting that some noble Kami has descended from the heavens. Here Akitsuo-no-Kami chants a sacred poem:

'Wishing to give birth to the land, I crossed over upon my horse and came to see, And the sweeping of the ground is pure and fair; Though I know not which Kami has descended from the heavens, It fills my soul with such profound awe. Tamanohime, treating my departure as if it were nothing, Must have some reason for not coming out to greet me; Be that as it may, with reverence and utmost devotion, let me climb this hill, And view it with a cleansed heart.'

Just as he chants this, Ikuyohime-no-Kami, who has hurried her horse and ridden in, leaps lightly from her horse and chants a sacred poem:

'The divine spirit has already arrived quickly to this place, And I made haste to follow right behind you; Could the Lord Great Kami of this sacred place have descended from the heavens, For it fills the air with such solemn majesty. Being one who serves in the divine work of creating the Kami, I was ashamed to find myself falling behind you, Kimi; As if the Lord Kami has descended, even the wind that blows Has a wondrous fragrance upon the hill of white plum blossoms; Come then, go on before me, for I Shall follow in your divine rear and climb the sacred mountain.'

Just as she chants this, Toomiou-no-Kami and his retinue along with the other Kami finally come rushing up, leap from their horses all at once, tie their reins to the branches of the aged pines, and while resting their breath, Toomiou-no-Kami chants a sacred poem:

'Riding afar upon the white horse, at long last Have I tracked down the dwelling place of Kimi; Could some Kami have descended to this sacred place, for Even the wind blowing through the sky is wondrously fragrant. This sacred place, the true mahoraba of the land of Manazuru, Is indeed fitting for the birth of the land; Will you erect the sacred pillar of the land here, And govern the land of Manazuru? The evergreen pine grove flourishing upon this hill, How wondrously thick and prosperous it has grown! With the nesting of thousand-year cranes in every single pine, This pure hill is the dwelling of the Kami; Feeling as though the Lord Kami has descended hither, I have toiled in climbing this pure hill.'

Maruyahiko-no-Kami chants a sacred poem:

'What manner of noble Kami has descended from the heavens, That even my feet have grown hesitant; This sacred hill where the majestic Kami dwells, Though I know not the reason, I gaze upon it in awe; Even the wind that blows gently is Illumining my face with the pure shade of filtered sunlight.'

Just as he chants this, Tamanohime-no-Kami appears with a serene demeanour, holding an ōnusa in her hand, and chants a sacred poem:

'Waiting for Kimi, Tamanohime has grown long in years, And has aged together with the evergreen pines; Serving in the divine work of creating the Kami for so many years, I grew old while waiting in vain for Kimi; Visiting me after crossing the wilderness of myriads of ri, Your sincere heart, Kimi, brings me such joy. How many times have I folded my fingers to count the passing months and days, And now, not in vain is the waiting, as I meet Kimi once more. The Lord Kami has descended from the heavens in solemn majesty, And has graced the inner depths of the sanctuary; Come then, O Akitsuo-no-Kami, ascend the heights, And I shall stand in your presence to serve.'

Akitsuo-no-Kami chants a sacred poem:

'Crossing the vast plains of a thousand leagues openly, Today at last I have come to seek you; When I gaze upon the shade of the aged pines, Mossed over and imbued with the antiquity of the Kami, I am reminded of Kimi. Until the princess dwarf pine grows and prospers like an aged pine, I hold dear Kimi who has made me wait; If it be so, there is no avail in simply making the Kami-birth, Shall we join our hearts and give birth to the land? Ikuyohime, welcome me and let us conceive the precious divine child, In place of Kimi.'

Ikuyohime-no-Kami chants a sacred poem:

'The divine form of Tamano-no-Hime of which fame is heard, With awe do I revere its holiness and purity; Born forth from the essence of the mountain of Manazuru, I served in the work of giving birth to our divine child.'

白梅の薫る玉野の森の白砂を、馬の蹄に踏みなづみながら、老松の蔭を潜りて、漸く玉野比女の神の鎮まり給ふ聖所に着き給ふ。

この丘は、玉野丘と称し、南北一里、東西二里にわたる平坦の高地にして、白銀の砂は、天津日に照りかがよひ、神苑を包める常磐樹は蜿蜒として枝を交へ、紫微天界の粋を集めたるばかり思はるる聖所なりける。

顕津男の神は、山の麓に駒乗り降り給ひ、丘の上をふりさけ見給ふに、紅、白、紫、黄、青の五色の幔幕を張り廻され、何事か尊き神の御降臨ありし様子なり。茲に顕津男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『国土生むと駒に跨り来て見れば 箒目正しく清められあり 何神の天降りますかは知らねども いと尊くぞ思はれにける 玉野比女わが出で立ちをよそにして 出迎へまさぬは訳あるらしも ともかくも謹みいやまひこの丘を 心清めて登り見むかな』

斯く歌ひ給ふ折しも、駒を早めて入り来りし生代比女の神は、ひらりと駒を飛び降り、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『瑞御霊早くも此処に来ませるよ 吾は急ぎて後追ひまつりぬ この聖所主の大神の天降りますか いと厳かに思はるるなり 神生みの神業に仕へし吾にして 岐美に後れむ事をはぢけり 主の神の天降りますにや吹く風も かをり妙なり白梅の丘に いざさらば前に立ちませわれこそは 御後に従ひ御山に登らむ』

斯く歌ひ給ふ折しも、遠見男の神一行其他の神々は、漸く駆けつけ給ひ、一斉に駒を飛び降り、老松の枝に手綱を結びつけ、息を休ませながら、遠見男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『道遠み白駒かけて漸くに 岐美の在所をさぐり来にけり 何神の天降りますにやこの聖所 空吹く風も妙にかをれり 真鶴の国の真秀良場この聖所は 国土生み給ふにふさはしきかも 此処にして国の御柱たて給ひ 真鶴国を治め給ふか この丘に繁れる常磐の松並木 すぐれて太く栄えけるかも 松毎に千歳の鶴の巣ぐひたる この清丘は神の御舎 主の神の天降りましたる心地して 登りなづみぬこの清丘を』

圓屋比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『如何ならむ尊き神の天降りますか わが足さへも縮まりにけり 稜威高き神の鎮まる神の丘を わけは知らねど吾は畏みぬ 吹く風も穏かにしてわが面を 清しく照らす木洩陽のかげ』

斯く歌ひ給ふ折しも、玉野比女の神は大麻を手にしながら、悠然として現れ給ひ、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『岐美待ちて気永くなりし玉野比女 常磐の松と共に老いぬる 神生みの神業に仕ふと永年を 岐美待ちかねて老いにけらしな 幾万里の荒野をわたり訪ひ来ます 岐美の真心嬉しかりける 幾度か指折り数へよき月日 待つ甲斐ありて岐美に逢ふかも 主の神はいと厳かに天降りまし 奥殿深く臨ませ給へり いざさらば顕津男の神登りませ われは御前にたちて仕へむ』

顕津男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『千万里の大野をわたり公許に 今日は漸く訪ね来つるも 苔むして神さびたてる老松の かげをし見れば公の偲ばゆ 姫小松はや老松と栄ゆまで 待たせる公をいとしみ思ふ かくならば神生み為さむ詮もなし 心を合せて国土を生まむか 生代比女吾を迎へて貴の御子 孕ませ給へり公に代りて』

生代比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『音に聞く玉野の比女の御姿の 尊さ清しさ畏みまつる 真鶴の山の精より生れ出でで 吾御子生みの業に仕へし』

§ 2

The Kami Tamanohime chants a Miuta:

'Ah, how lovely is the heart of Ikuyohime! Be at peace, for I too shall celebrate. Having accomplished the divine work, Ikuyohime, I revere the divine words of Kami. From now on, nurturing the child in your womb, together we shall build the divine country, I think.'

The Kami Ikuyohime chants a Miuta:

'How grateful I am for the words of Tamanohime! To the end of ages, I shall not forget them. Being one who has conceived the Kami of Kunitama, I hold your public words in deep gratitude.'

The Kami Akitsuo chants a Miuta:

'How admirable are the words of Tamanohime! I have no words left to speak. In any case, following Tamanohime, I shall ascend and climb up to this pure hill.'

Serving as an attendant to the Kami Tamanohime and appearing here, the Kami Mototsumakoto chants a Miuta:

'I am indeed the Kami Mototsumakoto born of the U-sound! Today, how joyful it is to welcome Kimi. The Mizumitama for which Himegami has waited and waited—how joyful it is today to welcome it! Coming from afar, crossing the wild fields and oceans, I hold Kimi, who has arrived, in high reverence. Kimi, who has reached the sacred place where the Lord Kami has descended, is indeed a valiant Kami! From the very beginning of the opening of the land of Manazuru, there can be no such auspicious precedent. The Lord Kami has descended, and the Mizumitama appears here today—how auspicious it is! Though Tamanohime desires to welcome Kimi, yet unable to leave the side of Ōkami, forgive the sin of delay in coming forth to welcome Kimi who has journeyed from afar! Now, here in place of the Kami Tamanohime, I, the Kami Mototsumakoto, declare the distinction of words!'

The Kami Machiaihiko chants a Miuta:

'At the break of morning, the Great Kami descended, and the Mizumitama has now appeared! In the supreme realm of Aizen's Shibi Heaven, how joyful today, welcoming the Kami! Come now, under the guidance of Tamanohime, ascend to this pure hill!'

The Kami Akitsuo chanted:

'How grateful I am for the welcoming by the three pillar Kami! Your profound heart I rejoice in.'

As he quietly and gently ascended the hill path with its gentle slope, the Kami from Toomiyahiko downward, hearing of the descent of the Lord Kami, were filled with awe, joining their palms at the base of the mountain and chanting Kamigoto while waiting for the time to come.

The Kami Toomiyahiko chants a Miuta:

'Did I think to come and serve the Mizumitama and meet the descent of the Great Kami? This country where the Lord Kami has descended will surely flourish more and more! Even hearing good things in the forest of evergreens standing dense and shutting out the sky, if it be thus, we shall reverently and in awe pray purely to the Lord Kami.'

The Kami Maruyahiko chants a Miuta:

'In this forest where the ancient pines revere the four quarters, I never thought there would be such auspiciousness! On this hill where the Lord Kami has descended, purple clouds are trailing. Stretching out five-colored curtains purely, it seems the Lord Kami has been enshrined. My spirit has no means to cross these curtains, because of the cloudiness of my heart that has not yet cleared away. Since wisdom and enlightenment are still lacking, how sad it is that I have no means to meet the Lord Kami! At the foot of the hill where I worship the achievement of the Lord Kami who descended from the distant heavens.'

The Kami Tatakumi chants a Miuta:

'Though wisdom and enlightenment may be lacking, if the light of sincerity exists, one should be able to ascend! Even if my sincerity is insufficient, the heart that thinks of the divine country is venerable. Nevertheless, without the permission of the Ōkami of the Mizumitama, there is no path to ascend. Tamanohime, together with the Mizumitama and Ikuyohime, uttered living Kototama and returned. Not even a single word addressed to the Kami, how regrettable the fact that they returned! The light of sincerity still lacks the power to ascend this hill, how regrettable!'

The Kami Ureshiho chants a Miuta:

'How joyful I think my happiness in having served as an attendant as far as the foot of this pure hill! Being one whose Kototama cannot yet fully clear, I rejoice in coming to this sacred place. Cleansing my heart in the shade of the ancient pines, shall I see it reflected in this pure spring water? The pure spring waters gushing forth everywhere to wash the souls—such is the achievement of the Kami! Amidst the many basins of the purification pool, the regret of having merely crossed right through them! The spring waters that gushed forth to my right and left as I came are pure waters to wash the soul.'

玉野比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『愛らしき生代の比女の心かな 心安かれ吾も祝はむ 神業を果し給ひし生代比女 神の神言を尊しと思ふ 今よりは御腹の御子を育みて ともに神国を造らむと思ふ』

生代比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『有難し玉野の比女の御言葉 いくよの末まで忘れざるべし 国魂の神を孕みし吾にして 公の言葉を有難く思ふ』

顕津男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『けなげなる玉野の比女の言葉かな 我はいふべき言の葉も無し ともかくも玉野の比女に従ひて この清丘に進み登らむ』

玉野比女の神の御供に仕へまつり、此処に現れ給ふ本津真言の神は、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『吾こそはウ声に生れし本津真言の神よ 今日嬉しくも岐美を迎へし 比女神の待ちに待たせる瑞御霊 迎ふる今日ぞ嬉しかりけり はろばろと荒野をわたり海を越え 来ませる岐美を尊く思ふ 主の神の天降りましける聖所に 着かせる岐美は雄々しき神はも 真鶴の国のひらけし始めより かかる目出度き例はあらじ 主の神は天降りましまし瑞御霊 此処に現れます今日ぞ目出度き 玉野比女は岐美迎へむとおぼせども 大神のみそば離れかねつつ はろばろと岐美の出でまし出迎への 後れし罪を許させ給へ 玉野比女神に代りて今此処に ことわけのぶる本津真言の神よ』

待合比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『朝まけて主の大神は降りまし 瑞の御霊は今現れましぬ 愛善の紫微天界の真秀良場に 今日は嬉しも神々迎へて いざさらば玉野の比女の導きに 登らせ給へこの清丘へ』

顕津男の神は、

『有難し三柱神の出で迎へ 厚き心を我は嬉しむ』

と歌ひ給ひつつ、しづしづと緩勾配の丘道を登らせ給へば、遠見男の神以下の神々は、主の神の御降臨と聞きて畏み、山の登り口に両掌を合せ神言を奏上しながら、時の到るを待たせ給ひける。

遠見男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『思ひきや瑞の御霊に仕へ来て 主の大神の天降りにあふとは 主の神の天降り給ひしこの国は いやますますに栄えますらむ 鬱蒼と天を封じてそそり立つ 常磐樹の森によき事を聞くも かくならば吾等は謹み畏みて 主の大神に清く祈らむ』

圓屋比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『老松の四方をかしこみしこの森に かかる目出度さ思はざりけり 主の神の天降り給ひしこの丘に 紫の雲棚引きにけり 五色の幕を清しく張り廻し 主の大神を斎きたるらし この幕を越ゆる術なきわが御魂 まだ晴れやらぬ心の曇りに 智慧証覚未だ足らねば主の神に まみえむ術の無きが悲しき 久方の天より降りし主の神の 功を拝む丘の麓に』

多々久美の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『智慧証覚よし劣るとも真心の 光しあらばのぼり得べけむ よしやよしわが真心は足らずとも 神国を思ふ心は尊し さりながら瑞の御霊の大神の 御許しなくばのぼる道なし 玉野比女瑞の御霊と生代比女に 生言霊をのべて帰らせり 神々に一言だにもかけまさず 帰り給ひし事のうたてさ 真心の光は未だこの丘に のぼらむ力無きぞうたてき』

宇礼志穂の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『うれしくもこの清丘の麓まで 御供に仕へしわが幸を思ふ 言霊の澄みきりあへぬ吾にして これの聖所に来りしを喜ぶ 老松のかげに心を清めつつ この真清水にうつしてや見む あちこちに魂を洗へと真清水は 湧き出でにける神の功に 幾何の御手洗池のある中を ただによぎりし事のくやしさ わが来る右りと左に湧き出でし 清水は魂を洗ふ真清水』

§ 3

Mihashihiko no Kami chanted a divine poem.

'Illumined by the Kototama of Ureshiho no Kami, I have indeed become ashamed; passing indeed through the pool of pure water where I should advance after purifying my body and washing my soul, mutely the Kami has imparted teachings and caused pure water to gush forth to wash the soul; serving the Mizumitama as an offering, unconsciously my soul-line had grown arrogant.'

Ubudama no Kami chanted a divine poem.

'I regret having merely passed by the edge of Tamanoike, whose limits are unknown for hundreds; I would seek the pool of pure water to the extent that this forest exists, and wash my soul; I have committed an irreparable fault, making light of this Mitarashi; I now repent having thoughtlessly regarded it as water gushing forth in the shade of the forest trees.'

Tamakiharu no Kami chanted a divine poem.

'While seeing the pure water of life, I remained forgetting the way to scoop it up; hurrying merely on my way forward, I have come past the pure water before my eyes; this forest where the Lord Kami has descended is only for the pure Mitama to advance; Lord Ōmikami, forgive my sin of defiling Tamanomori with horse hooves.'

Musubiahase no Kami chanted a divine poem.

'Come now, turning back to the road from whence we originally came, let us halt our horses and walk on foot; I repent my carelessness in advancing without knowing that the Lord Kami descends on this auspicious day.'

『今となりて吾恥づかしくなりにけり 真言いづみの禊忘れて いざさらば神々たちよ駒並めて 元来し道に引返し見む この森の外に抜け出で数多き 泉に御魂洗ひて進まむ』

斯く神々は、馬の蹄に知らず知らず聖所を汚せし事を悔い、一目散に元来し道に引返し、駒を玉野の森の入口遠く繋ぎ置き、各も各も真清水に身を清め心を浄め、天津祝詞を奏上し、再び主の神の天降ります丘を指して、真砂に足を踏みなづみつつ、其翌る日の黄昏るる頃、辛うじて丘の麓に着き給ひける。
