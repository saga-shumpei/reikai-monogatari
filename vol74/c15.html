Chapter 15: 晴天澄 Tide [1883]

晴天澄潮〔1883〕

§ 1

By the Kototama laden with the merciful benevolence of the Kami Akitsuo, the consuming flames of romantic resentment in Ikuyohime no Kami vanished like the foam upon Tamanōmi, reflecting the mirror of the moon upon the tranquil surface of the water, while the curtains of clouds and mist were swept away from wherever they hung, drawing the star-shining brilliance of the azure great sky deep within the lake bed, wondrously restoring a vista akin to the heavenly paradise and pure realm.

Tohomio no Kami gazed upon the cosmic scenery of heaven and earth now unfolded before his eyes, turned toward the expanse of the lake, and chanted this Miuta.

Aha, how brilliant! By the Kototama of the auspicious Mitama, The clouds of heaven and earth and the four quarters have cleared away! Ah, what a terrifying thing is love, what a haunting thing is longing, That turned this heaven and earth into utter darkness! Even the dark clouds that deeply enshrouded heaven and earth Have now cleared brightly away in the words of deep emotion; Realizing the deeply troubled divine heart of the Mizumitama Kami, Even I am drowned in tears of sorrow. While the moon shines bright and clear upon the mirror of Tamanōmi, The waves grow quiet, emitting a gentle fragrance; O Kimisama, who have soothed the deep thoughts Of this lake, clear to the depth of eight thousand fathoms! Far away on the distant shore, there faintly began to appear The sacred groves of Tamano. In the cloudless expanse of the great sky, The spiritual rays of the Kami shimmer in the heart; Compared to the heart of the Kami of Great Love, Even I am raving in my petty love; From this day forth, I will draw tight the reins of my heart, Resolved to walk the path of Great Love.

The heart that speaks of love is tender and pure, Radiating amidst heaven and earth; It is an evening when a gentle breeze, brushing the vast lake's plain, Blows across my face and breathes a sweet fragrance. As far as the eye can see, beneath the moonlight, grass and trees alike Seem to have fallen into a tranquil sleep; While the reeds along the water's edge, buffeted by the wild winds, Lean to one side, glistening in the dewdrops; By the moistening dew of the Mizumitama's grace, This heaven and earth have been cleansed anew. During those moments when the darkness was deep and the lake plain raged, I endured tribulations while serving in your divine retinue; Even the lake winds, howling and raging wildly, Have grown still in the tenderness of Kimisama; That which we should truly trust is the Kami, and what we should fear Is the wicked deviousness of love in this heaven and earth. If it be so, serving with courage as your divine retainer, I shall journey across, guarded by the sacred steed.

Maruyahiko no Kami chanted this Miuta.

Far, far away, having served upon the journey of Kimisama, I have come to realize the state of the world; My heart of love, burning, extinguishing, and burning once more, Has sealed heaven and earth in bitter resentment; Yet the darkness of the sealed heaven and earth has also broken open Into dawn through the living Kototama filled with deep emotion. The dark clouds that enshrouded the great sky without a single gap Seemed terrifying when thought of as the flames of love; All the marvels of the beautiful Ziwei Heavenly Realm Awe me when I think they were born from love; Love-good and love-evil alternatingly passing to and fro, Even as the Ziwei Heavenly Realm solidifies itself; Both heaven and earth, by the sovereign majesty of Maruyahiko no Kami, Shall be governed in harmony, according to the will of the Kami. To govern in harmony the communion between Kami and Kami, nation and nation, Such is my sacred vow. Round and round, the moon in the august sky shines brightly Above and below upon Tamanōmi; Beholding the manner of this evening's shifting transformations, I gaze upon the light of the path before me. Oh, how the Mizumitama hurries to and fro, To solidify this distant divine realm!

Tatakumi no Kami chanted this Miuta.

My power was indeed unequal to the task, during that brief moment When the clouds of love blocked the four quarters; When I reflect upon the awe of serving as a retainer To the Kami who creates the land, my heart cannot find release. While the lake raged and the great serpent manifested itself, I stood watching in profound awe; Standing upon the shore of Tamanōmi, I simply waited For the moment when the waves would subside; Though deeming myself a useless retainer, having no means To scatter the clouds of love, I remained in silence; Witnessing right before my eyes the tribulations endured by the Mizumitama, I grieve over my own utter helplessness. As the dew of grace shone brightly upon the Kototama, The breast of the great serpent must have been softened; Truly terrifying and abominable is the shadow born Within a heart that speaks of love. Even the boundless, limitless great moorland Begins to glow brightly in the light of the moon; The wicked deviousness that enshrouded both the eternal heaven And the earth in black clouds was none other than love.

Having served in the retinue for the birth of the land, I beheld the shadow Of that terrifying thing called love; Quieting my deep heart and remaining in silence, I gaze upon the awesome great serpent of love.

Ureshino no Kami chanted this Miuta.

Feeling as though I have awakened anew within heaven and earth, I gaze up at the moon over the mirror-like lake. The brilliance of the moon shining at the zenith of heaven is clear, And the waters of Tamanōmi shine forth in pure clarity. I have deeply pondered and recalled right before my eyes The shifting conditions of the passing world. Crushing even the austere power of the Kototama, The flames of love flared up high; Those flared-up flames of love turned into clouds, And becoming rain, they enshrouded heaven and earth. When I reflect upon the tribulations of the noble divine work Of the Mizumitama in giving birth to the divine children, reverence wells up. Reverently remembering how arduous is the divine work Of the Kami who gives birth to the Soul of the Land; If we do not solidify the drifting, immature land of the earth, The Ziwei Heavenly Realm surely will not flourish. Even amidst the divine age of love-good, it is disheartening That they so easily harbor resentment, hatred, and strife. I muse upon the tribulations of Kimisama, who opens up The realm of the land overgrown with reeds.

原文（近代文語）

顕津男の神の仁慈の籠れる言霊の御歌に、生代比女の神が恋の恨みも炎も、玉野湖の水泡と消えて、水面には月の鏡を写し、雲霧の幕何れにか取り外されて、大空の蒼にきらめく星影を湖底に描き、天国浄土の光景と回復したるぞ不思議なる。

遠見男の神は、今目前展開したる天地の光景を眺めて湖面に向ひ、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『天晴れ天晴れ瑞の御霊の言霊に 天地四方の雲晴れにけり 恐しきものは恋かも思ひかも この天地を闇となしける 天地を深く包みし闇雲も 情の言葉に晴れ渡りぬる 瑞御霊神の苦しき御心を 悟りて吾は涙に暮るるも 玉野湖の鏡に月は冴えにつつ 波は静に香りこそすれ 八千尋の底まで澄めるこの湖の 深き思ひを和らげし岐美よ 目路遠く彼方の岸にうつろへる 玉野神森見え初めにける 一片の雲さへも無き大空の 心にかがよふ神の霊線 大愛の神の心に比ぶれば 吾は小さき愛に狂へるも 今日よりは心の手綱ひき締めて 大愛の道進まむと思ふ

恋すてふ心は愛し清しもよ 天と地との中に輝く 湖原をなでて吹き来しそよ風の わが面吹きて香る宵なり 見の限り月の下びに草も木も 安き眠りにつきにけらしな 荒風に揉まれて汀の葭葦は 片靡きつつ露に光れる 瑞御霊恵の露の霑ひに この天地は洗はれにける 闇深く湖原荒れしたまゆらを 吾は艱みぬ御供に仕へて 吹きすさび荒れ狂ひたる湖風も 静まりにけり岐美の情に 頼むべきものは神かも恐るべき 邪曲は恋かもこの天地に かくならば勇みて御供仕へつつ 吾渡り行かむ神馬の守りに』

圓屋比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『はろばろと岐美の旅路に仕へ来て 吾は悟りぬ世の状態を 恋心燃えつ消えつつまた燃えつ 天と地とを恨みにとざせり とざしたる天地の闇も情ある 生言霊に明け放れたり 大空を隈なく包みし黒雲は 恋の炎と思へば恐し 美しき紫微天界のことごとは 愛より生みしと思へば畏し 愛善と愛悪交々ゆきかひて 紫微天界は固まり行くも 天も地も圓屋の比古の神の稜威に 丸く治めむ神のまにまに 神と神国と国との交らひを 丸く治めむわが誓ひなり 丸々と御空の月は玉野湖の 上と下とにかがよふこの宵 この宵の移り変りのさま見つつ わが行先の光見つむる

目路遠きこの神国を固めむと 駆け廻ります瑞の御霊はや』

多々久美の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『わが力及ばざりけり恋雲の 四方をふさぎしその束の間を 国土造る神の御供の畏さを 思へば心ゆるされぬかな 湖荒れて大蛇の出でしたまゆらを 吾は畏み見て居たりける 玉野湖の岸辺に立ちて吾はただ 浪凪ぎ渡る時を待ちつつ 不甲斐なき吾と思へど恋雲を 晴らさむ術なく黙し居にけり 瑞御霊艱める態を目前 見つつ術なき吾を悲しむ 言霊に恵の露の輝きて 大蛇の胸は和みたりけむ 恐しく忌はしきものは恋すてふ 心に生まるる影なりにけり 縹渺と限りも知らぬ大野原も 月の光に輝きそめたり 久方の天また地を黒雲に 包みし邪曲は恋なりにけり

国土生みの供に仕へて恐しき 恋てふものの影見たりけり 村肝の心静めて黙しつつ 眺むる恋の大蛇すさまじ』

宇礼志穂の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『天地によみがへりたる心地して 鏡の湖の月仰ぐかな 天心に輝く月のかげ冴えて 玉野湖水は澄み照らひけり 移り行く世の状態をつくづくと わが目前偲びけらしな 言霊の厳の力も揉み消して 恋の炎は燃え立ちにけり 燃え立ちし恋の炎は雲となり 雨となりつつ天地を包めり 瑞御霊貴の神業御子生みの 艱み思へば謹みの湧く 謹みて国魂神を生みまする 神の神業の難きを偲ぶも 地稚く漂へる国土を固めずば 紫微天界は栄えざるらむ 愛善の神代ながらも兎もすれば 恨み憎みて争ふが憂し 葭葦の生ひ茂りたる国原を 拓かす岐美の艱みを思ふ』

§ 2

Mihashihiko no Kami chants a sacred song:

'Even the howling wind of the wilderness and the breeze of the lake, have completely calmed before the words of feeling; it goes without saying that the Kami do so, and all in Tenkai revives through feeling.

There is no Kami in Tenkai who knows not feeling; awe-inspiring are the hardships of the wondrous Mitama.

The clouds that enfolded Ametsuchi clear away entirely, and pure become my soul-threads (tamashihi).

Expanding across the ten thousand leagues of vast space (kuōbaubanri), I pioneer the youthful Kunibara; I contemplate the awe-inspiring achievements of Kimi.

Though the brilliant achievements of the wondrous Mitama, born as the moon-spirit of the great sky (ōzora), shine brightly, even the moon of the great sky is covered by clouds.

In a world like this, I ponder the majestic potency (izu) of the Kototama.

Tenkai, born from the august potency (miizu) of the Kototama, is a land growing ever clearer and utterly free of dust.

A realm free even of sin, defilement, and dust is clouded and raged over by the dark clouds of passionate love.

Were there no such thing as passionate love in Tenkai, the clouds enfolding Ametsuchi would never arise.

Though the Kototama proclaims that Tenkai, filled with the light of Aizen, shall never be defiled, in this Tenkai where good and evil (yoshiashi) cross paths, the rising of clouds and mist is perhaps inevitable (zehi).'

Ubudama no Kami chants a sacred song:

'Far along the road, protecting Kimi, I beheld the state of the world upon the shores of Tamanōmi.

When I ponder the heart of Himegami who proclaimed she would cast a curse for eternity, how sorrowful it was.

Pray reform this crooked deed that leaves behind eternal resentment, within the land where the Kami dwell.

Out of an excess of intense love, the resentful heart of Himegami must have blazed up in flames.

To resent the world and to resent the Kami—this too was but the tangling of the threads of the heart's passionate love.

With no way to untangle this deep-seated tangle of the heart, it has become a love tragically weeping.

Since Kimi serves the divine work of Kami-creation, you must surely cherish Him all the more deeply.

Being the Tenkai of Aizen, every Kami possesses a heart of passionate love.

The deep thoughts of Himegami have finally welled up even from the depths of the lake.

Awe-inspiring things indeed are passionate love and resentment, which sought to shatter even this divine nation.'

Tamakibari no Kami chants a sacred song:

'Striving to fulfill the passionate love of fleeting life, the end of suffering has turned her into a great serpent.

Spurning life as a thread of jewels (tama no o), the maiden's flame of passionate love scorches the heavens.

Through the Kototama imbued with the feeling of the wondrous Mitama, this Ametsuchi has dawned wide open.

Deeply, the night has grown late, and the moonlight too has shifted low into the western sky.

The pale light of the moon tilting into the great sky grows sharp, and the shadow of my steed grows long.

Just as the Lord God commanded, I think with affection of Kimi, who is to give birth to the Kunidamagami.

Had this befallen the body of an ordinary kami, such hardships would never have been encountered out of season.

To the eyes of ordinary kami, the divine work of my Kimi appears wrongly as evil.

More fearsome than the jealousy and envy of ordinary kami is the unfeeling antagonist of passionate love.

Enfolding endless suffering within my breast, Kimi crosses this lake-plain.

Pondering the suffering in the heart of my Kimi, the tears in my eyes drift like a lake.'

Musubiai no Kami chants a sacred song:

'Splendid is Tamanōmi, binding heaven and earth together and housing the reflections of moon and sun.

Standing upon the shores of the lake-plain where both moon and sun shine in pristine clarity, I ponder the world.

Even the sound of insects echoes ever more softly and vividly, while the moon upon the water shines intensely sharp.

When I see the reflection of the moon floating upon the lake, I think upon the heart of the wondrous Mitama.

Though I am a Kami of binding heaven and earth together, what shall I do with this cord of passionate love?

Come what may, by the august light of the living Kototama, let us advance brightly as far as Tamanomori.

The stamping hooves of the spirited steed eagerly paw the ground, urging me onward with such endearing grace.

As I watch the reflection of the moon floating upon the lake, does the steed grow courageous from its hurried pawing?'

Mihasu no Kami chants a sacred song:

'Tsukuyomi in the east and Amatsuhi in the west cross paths while illuminating the lake's surface.

Pushing aside the curtain of clouds in the western sky, the Kami Tsukuyomi advances to the east.

Pushing aside the sky of the dawn clouds, Amatsuhi sinks daily into the western sky.

Precisely because moon and sun, right and left, cross paths, this Tenkai prospers indeed.

Moon and sun, heaven and earth, let us bind them together completely and protect the age of the Kami.'

Makotogentsu no Kami chants a sacred song:

'Having come hither, unwittingly time has slipped away amid the curtain of suffering between affection and passionate love.

High are the heavens and broad is Kunibara; Tsukuyomi distributes the dew of grace without omission.

Come then, let us line up our steeds and offer our escort, however deep this lake-plain may be.

Grasping the reins of our spirited steeds firmly, let us swim across together with our steeds.

Presumptuous though it is, I shall serve at the very vanguard; do you, the hundred Kami, follow on.'

Having thus finished chanting, he springs lightly astride a white horse, applies a single whip, and rides forth across the moonlit surface of the lake, kicking up waves like leaping dragons and serpents. Beginning with Hinodewake no Kami, the hundred Kami follow, tracing the foam-tips of the waves stirred up by Makotogentsu no Kami, and press forward eagerly on horseback.

美波志比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『吹き荒ぶ荒野の風も湖風も ひたをさまりぬ情の言葉に 神々はいふも更なり天界の 総ては情によみがへるなり 天界に情を知らぬ神は無し 瑞の御霊の艱み畏し 天地を包みし雲も晴れ渡り 清しくなりぬわが魂線は 広袤万里稚き国原拓きます 岐美の功の畏さ思ふ 大空の月の御霊と生れませし 瑞の御霊の功光るも 大空の月さへ雲に覆はるる 世に言霊の稜威を思へり 言霊の御稜威に生りし天界は 澄みきらひつつ塵の無き国 罪穢塵さへも無き国原を 曇らせ荒ぶ恋の黒雲 天界に恋すてふことなかりせば 天地を包む雲は起らじ
