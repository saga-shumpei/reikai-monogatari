Chapter 13: 水上の Moon [1881]

水上の月〔1881〕

§ 1

When the white-horse troop of Akitsuo no Kami's entourage arrived at the shores of Lake Tamano just as twilight was about to fall, the light of the full moon crossing the expansive sky gently illuminated the lake surface, where the waves of crepe-like ripples drifted calmly.

The forest of Tamano, situated on the opposite shore of the broad lake waters, bathsed in moonlight like solemnity itself, was depicted both upon the earth and at the bottom of the lake.

Akitsuo no Kami, facing the lake surface, composed a sacred poem with a tranquil heart.

'The evening moon looking up floats upon the waves of Lake Tamano, how serene it is! Drawn densely upon the evening earth, the forest of Tamano is wondrously pure. I now whip my steed and having crossed the great plain afar, have now come hither. The face of the moon, floating upon this mirror-like lake, has grown manifold broader. Without a breath of wind blowing softly across the lake, this heaven and earth remain hushed. The heart of my inmost soul has grown utterly tranquil. In the mirror of the moon-floating lake, the forest of Tamano whither I wend lies far across the waves, making the path over the lake seem distant. Crossing through the wilderness where reeds and rushes thrive, now widely I behold the moon upon the waves. Hearing the voices of insects echoing here and there along the shore, I perceive the purity of my spirit. In these eyes of mine that beheld the dark clouds of Manazuru, how surpassingly tranquil they feel. Resting my steed for a brief while and giving it water to drink, let me mount and cross to the opposite shore. This lake, lacking even boats to traverse its waves, found its strength solely in the backs of noble steeds. Since setting forth from the high palace of Hisakata, I have beheld no lake so tranquil as this. If it were possible, I would fain make this mirror-smooth expanse of the lake a gift for the Lord Ōkami. The distant sky is blue and the lake is blue, the moon floats purely in heaven and earth. Does the blue of the clouds reflect in the blue lake, or does the blue of the lake reflect in the mirror of the moon? Upon the surface of the lake, where the sky is blue and the water is likewise blue, how clear is the shadow of the white bird floating. Shining as it reflects the stars of the crowded heavens, the lake is like a blooming of a thousand flowers. Floating upon the water's depths, the flower of the stars makes the blue of the lake float into the heavens, a pure evening indeed. As far as the eye can see, the sky is blue and the water is blue, with the shadow of the moon-boat flowing through the midst. Beholding the moon, my heart is pure; beholding the lake, my spirit expands ever wider. Is it the form of Tamanohime? The light of the full moon floating upon the blue lake's surface shines together with the moon of the lake waters within my heart, to illuminate the dwelling place of the princess of Tamano. How lovely, is it the spirit of the purple heavenly realm, this moonlight floating upon the lake's surface? Leaving the palace of the lofty-shining mountain, have they come forth to meet one another upon the pure lake? Even my spirit, when crossing the muddy river, has been washed in the blue of the moon-lit lake. High is the heaven and deep is the lake's bottom; I have now profoundly comprehended the majestic divine power of Kami. As the lake's surface grows ever broader and the prospect stretches far away, how distant indeed are the thoughts of my journey.'

Tōmiwake no Kami composed a sacred poem.

'Serving as an offering to the august spirit, how pure it is! With the moon of this very evening, my soul has been washed. Illuminating this boundless heaven and earth, the moonlight tonight floats upon the lake. The moon-boat, shining like a silver jewel, sparkles upon these lake waters. Is the blue and purity of this lake, where moon, sun, and stars alike float, the heart of Kami? How pure indeed is the lake where the moon of the wish-granting jewel shines bright as it floats. The moon upon the evening lake, where not even a small wave stirs, shines as it reflects the palace of jewels. Do the insects among the myriad grasses of the shoreline sing in the purity of the moonlight? Their voices have grown clear. Dew rests upon the back of the white horse upon which I came, and tonight's moonlight sparkles like jewels. Both the moon of the august sky that I look up to and the moon at the bottom of the lake—you are the primal great progenitor Akitsuo no Kami.'

Maruyahiko no Kami composed a sacred poem.

'The moon waxes full, and tonight's shadow is Maruyahiko; the form of Kami floats upon the lake. Reflecting the distant august sky, how clear and bright is the bottom of Lake Tamano! Though the darkness of twilight presses close, it grows transparent and bright in the moon crossing the heavens. Reflecting the color of the blue clouds, the evening moon grows clear and the lake turns blue. The forest of Tamano, faintly outlined upon the distant shore, glistens brilliantly upon the water. The sparkling waves appear as though a sea-fire illuminates the ocean expanse. In the moonlight, one can scarcely discern the mountain of Manazuru soaring high above the clouds. As far as the eye can see, clouds and mist clear away, the sky turns blue, and stars sparkle in a pure evening. Serving as an offering for the creation of the land, how wondrous it is to behold the clear moon tonight! Resembling white in the moonlight, the noble steed I rode upon has cast its shadow down to the very bottom of the water.'

玉野森は広き湖水の彼方の岸に、月光を浴びて森厳そのものの如く、地上と湖底に描かれて居る。

遠見男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『瑞御霊御供に仕へ清しくも 今宵の月に魂を洗へり 果しなきこの天地を照します 月光今宵は湖に浮べり 白銀の玉と輝く月舟の これの湖水にかがやき給ふ 月も日も星も浮ぶなるこの湖の あをく清きは神の心か 如意宝珠玉の月光明らけく 浮べる湖の清くもあるかな 小波も立たぬ夕の湖の月は 玉の宮居を写してさゆるも 汀辺の千草の虫も月光の 清きに鳴くか声冴えにけり 乗りて来し白馬の背に露おきて 玉とかがよふ今宵の月光 仰ぎ見る御空の月も湖の底の 月も太元顕津男の神よ』

圓屋比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『月盈ちて今宵のかげは圓屋比古 神の姿は湖にうかべる 久方の御空うつして玉野湖の 底明らけく澄みきらふかな 黄昏の闇は迫れど天渡る 月に明るく透きとほるなり 青雲の色を写して夕暮の 月澄み湖のあをみたるかも ぼんやりと彼方の岸に描きたる 玉野の森は水に映えたり きらきらと輝く波は不知火の 海原照す如く見ゆめり 雲の上高く聳ゆる真鶴の 山ほの見えぬ月の光に 見の限り雲霧晴れて空蒼み 星きらめきて清しき宵なり 国土生みの御供に仕へて珍しく 冴えたる月を今宵見るかな 乗りて来し駿馬白く月に浮きて 水底までも影を写せり

§ 2

A reliable journey indeed crossing the wilderness, Beholding the clear moon of Tamanokosui.

Somehow my tamashihi has grown at peace, With the purity of this evening's moonlight.

Although it is a Kuni made into Miiki by the Lord Kami, I never thought it could be so beautiful.

Tsukiyomi shining with the utmost of its light, Floating peacefully upon the surface of the waves,

In the Hironohara where there is not a single mountain shadow, A single moon floating upon the lake.

At any rate, following behind Kimi's august footsteps, Within this very evening I shall cross over to Higan.'

Tatakumi no Kami chants an august miuta.

'Amidst the clear heavens and the equally clear lake, Shall I not joyfully chant a song? The voices of insects grow clear by the kohan as the night deepens, How tranquil is the sky of this evening!

The moon shining in the ōzora and the moon reflected In the misoko are both the mizu no mitama.

Did even the hayakoma become captivated by this keshiki? Its neighing voice was pure indeed.

The kamimori of the distant shore to which we cross over Has vanished deep into the misoko.

Now I feel it is a pity to think of breaking this minazuki With the hoofs of the hayakoma,

Yet if it may be, I would wish to entrust this moonlight of the lake To the sky and cross over.

This evening as I cross thekomen where the moon floats, It is as though walking above the clouds of the misora.'

Ureshiho no Kami chants an august miuta.

'This Kuni filled with joy in Ametsuchi Is the mahoraba of the Shibi tenkai,

Serving the mizu-mitama as an offering, I beheld the moon of the lake Shining upon the mahoraba of the tenkai.

The youthful kuniwara of the true crane shall grow lush and flourish In the verdure of the kosui.

This lake reflecting the distant ame Shall house the sun and moon of the Amatsu eternally.

The fish scales playing in this clear misoko Look up at the moon and float to the surface.

Feeling as though both ame and tsuchi have been revived, I gaze upon the evening moon floating on the komen.

Even as evening falls, the kohan shining Brightly down to the depths of the misora is radiant indeed.

It is a magari for a kami to live and lament In the tenkai filled with eternal joy.

High in the sky and deepening at the bottom, the color of the blue sky Floating upon the surface of this lake,

May the kototama of the Lord Kami purely bless And make this lake clear and serene.

Standing beneath the clear, serene moon, I look down upon the moon at the bottom of the lake.

Illuminating the blessings of the path whence the mizu-mitama emerges, The moonlight shines clear upon the lake.

Meeting with countless sorrows, now here Beneath the pure misora I behold the moon.

§ 3

Musubiahase no Kami chant the sacred Miuta.

"One after another the mists arise and broadly / narrow the shining lake by degrees / The moon of the lake bottom is gradually hidden / While the moon alone floats in the sea of the high sky / The moon that should be reflected is enwrapped in mists / And the surface of this lake is dimly darkening / Is this the flames of Ikuyohime's resentment gathering / So that mists are welling up once more? / Well, be it so, even if black clouds enwrap the four quarters / I shall blow them away and see with the living Kototama."

Umashimoto no Kami chant the sacred Miuta.

"Do you not know they are the Kimi who have set out on the journey / Of creating lands and creating kami at the entrustment of the Lord God? / O kami of the lake bottom, listen tranquilly / This lake too is a gift of the Kami / If there be any kami who would make obstruction to the land-creation / We shall blow them away with the water and fire of Kototama / Lining up our horses we shall now cross the lake / Clearing its surface, O wind, grow calm / This wind is not the water and fire of Shinado no Kami / It is the water and fire of the curse of the bender of the lake bottom / Having appeared in the Mahoraba of the Land of Aizen / In this lake water of here, there shall be no bending / It is not a lake that should be made the dwelling place of Magakami / Quickly depart, depart, simply withdraw / Since you are kami who do not even fear the water and fire of Kototama / We think we shall not let you dwell in this Ametsuchi."

Makotoizu no Kami chant the sacred Miuta.

"I indeed am the Kami of the stern severity of Makoto / Clear the lake and let the Kimi pass through / O kami of the lake, without listening to our Kototama / Do you come forth to oppose us, regardless of your life? / In the lake created by the Chihaya-furu Kami / Do not defile and desecrate it, O Mizu-no-Mitama no Kami."

Thus the Kami, each and every one of them, chanting their sacred Miuta, pacified and admonished the kami of the lake and passed the time, yet the flames of love of Ikuyohime no Kami were so strong and fierce that even the light of the living Kototama of the Kami was wrapped and concealed, alas, how deplorable it was.

かく神々は、各も各もに御歌うたひて、湖の神をなだめつ諭しつ時を移し給へども、生代比女の神の恋の炎は強く猛く、神々の生言霊の光さへ、包みかくすぞうたてけれ。
