Chapter 26: 総 Kami 登丘 [1894]

総神登丘〔1894〕

§ 1

Akitsuo no Kami and Tamanohime no Kami, having been astonished by the manifestation of Mototsumakoto no Kami, renewed their astonishment at the descent and manifestation of Chikaramichio no Kami upon the heavenly summit, and crouching in the sacred place of the jeweled palace while feeling ashamed that their spiritual threads were terribly clouded, chanted their miuta.

The miuta of Akitsuo no Kami.

'To solidify Manazurukuni, clear and bright, two pillars of Kami have descended. Have my spirits indeed grown clouded, sitting in completion without knowing the manifestation of the two pillars? How awe-inspiring is the divine work of the heavenly realm, where not even a hair's breadth of gap exists. To solidify this child-like land-plain, did the Lord Ōkami descend. Rather than saying how grateful and unworthy I feel, my words could not come forth. On Tamanōka, where both moon and sun shine purely, my face has truly become embarrassed. Possessing such clouded spirits, I feel hesitant and doubtful about the task of producing the land. By virtue of the Lord Kami's plan, I wish to solidify the land of Manazuru by purifying my spirits. I had not realized that the divine shigumi was so noble until now. From now on, I will retrain my spiritual threads, and advance with courage in producing lands and producing Kami.

Reflecting on the difficulty of the work to solidify this endless land-plain in detail. How awe-inspiring, Mototsumakoto no Kami was none other than the Lord Ōkami. The Kami of the summit descended to the sacred place, and caused our spiritual threads to shine. The distant august sky shines purely. When I look up, how shameful my spirit is! Having abandoned Toomiwo no Kami and climbed to Tamanōka, my spirit is clouded. Now I regret my transgression of leaving the Kami behind at the foot of the hill. From now on, I will purify my heart and purify my body, and guide and bring up the Kami from the foot.'

The miuta of Tamanohime no Kami.

'Accepting the words of Mototsumakoto no Kami, how is it that I left the hundred Kami behind? It cannot be the crime of Mizu-no-Mitama no Kami, it is the august heart of Mototsumakoto no Kami. The two pillars descended from the heavenly high sky, and illuminated this sacred place. Even in broad daylight, the moonlight caught in the ever-green pine was also pure. The sunlight crossing the heavens is pure, and the moonlight is thoroughly clear on this day of all days. Awe-struck by the divine words of the two pillars of Kami, I wish to create the land with the hundred Kami. With Kalavinka singing unceasingly, today is the day that the cranes in the sacred place bless the divine age. Fragrant with white plum blossoms, sent by a gentle breeze, pure is the jeweled palace. White plum blossoms seal the jeweled palace, and scent the divine age in the shade of the pine trees. In the shade of the grasses, the voices of insects chirp and resound, welcoming the spring of Tokoyo.

Born on this hill, the Mizu-no-Mitama shines and creates the land, how joyful.'

Ikuyohime no Kami chants her miuta.

'Ikuyohime, to the end of all ages, like the ever-green pine, swear to protect the world. Offering the heart of the deep-bottomed Tamanō lake to Kimishi, I will protect the child. Though the mountain of Manazuru soars among the clouds, it surely cannot reach the merit of Kimishi. Born from the Kototama born of the Mizu-no-Mitama, I am she born on Mount Manazuru. I am truly the Himekami born in the water and fire of the Kototama of the Mizu-no-Mitama. Having thoroughly produced and completed the child of the Kunitama, I shall become the Kami of Manazuru once again. Treading upon and parting the white sand, riding across upon a white horse, the jeweled forest of Tamano is pure. Treading upon the golden sand of Tamanōka, I have heard the august voice of the noble Kami. Moistened by the deep grace of the Lord Kami, have I conceived the august child of my precious lord? As far as the eye can see, white clouds haze over Manazuru; open up and pioneer the land widely, O Kimishi!

My soul shall settle on Mount Manazuru and protect this divine land forever. Though I am presently of gaseous form, once I give birth to the child, I shall become of spiritual form again and serve. Giving birth to the child, my body changed into a gaseous form, and longing for Kimishi, I have come to serve. My love, even after tens of thousands of years, shall not perish together with heaven and earth. Even a body born in the Aizen Shibi heaven experiences clouding of the heart precisely because of love. Because of love the heart shines, and because of love the heart grows clouded—what a miserable world.'

Machiaihiko no Kami chants his miuta.

§ 2

'How joyous is this day when we purely hear the living Kototama of Kamigami!',

'Even as I sit, I think upon the blessing of hearing the divine voice of the heavenly firmament of Hisakata,',

'Though my wisdom and perception are lacking, how happy I am to have faintly heard the divine words of Amatsukami,',

'Following the male and female deities of the three pillars, I shall eternally guard and serve.'

Even as they thus chanted the sacred song, sealing the firmament, countless numbers of true cranes, boundless as the myriad millions, circled high in the sky above Tamanōka from left to right time and time again, raising their pure voices to celebrate the founding of Kamiyo, while descending to land upon the sacred sanctuary until the garden seemed narrow; and each and every one of them lifted their heads, appearing as if they were adoring Amatsutakamiya.

At the foot of Tamanōka, beginning with Tohomio no Kami, the nine deities including Maruyahiko no Kami repented that the soul-lines of their own were clouded, purified their bodies in the jewel spring, and polishing and clearing the water and fire of Kototama, awaited the time when the auspicious Mitorama would summon them, and each and every one of them chanted sacred songs.

The sacred song of Tohomio no Kami.

'I long to hear the tidings of Kimi who has ascended to the plum-scented hill of Tamano,',

'My soul is clouded and clouded, and the Kototama is murky, so there is no way for me to ascend to the divine hill,',

'Oh, the limits of things to be ashamed of, as I have been cast away at the foot of Tamanōka,',

'Serving as an attendant on a distant journey, now I have met with such a shameful and sorrowful eye,',

'Even upon the horse that has been tethered, my heart has become shamefully clouded, and the Kototama is turbid,',

'Though I plunge my soul into the jewel spring and wash it, I fear the darkness of the light of wisdom,',

'How peaceful is the grove of Tamano where the true crane sings high in the treetops of the ever-verdant pines,',

'If I had the wings of a true crane, I too would ascend to this Tamanōka,',

'To rule over the southern land—how shall I serve the words of command of my Kimi?',

'Though I thought Ikuyoihime no Kami was an evil deity, she has ascended to this divine hill,',

'Puzzlingly, Ikuyoihime no Kami has ascended briskly without even turning back,',

'Compared to the clouded soul of Ikuyoihime, is the turbidity of my soul even deeper?,',

'Amidst the ever-vaster Shibi heavenly realm, I feel ashamed and my heart trembles,',

'Writhing with the high throbbing of a pure breast that thinks upon the world, I wept at midnight and trembled,',

'If there is no way to serve as an attendant in the creation of the land, I shall become the wind blowing across the fields and perish,',

'Though I lament, there is no remedy for my body, as I grow ever more distant from the auspicious Mitorama.'

Maruyahiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Tohomio no Kami, do not lament, for I rejoice in this as a divine trial,',

'You are indeed the one whose body shall become the ruler of the land, as pure, righteous, wide, and like the true crane,',

'You indeed are the ruler of this country realm, summoned by the auspicious Mitorama,',

'Having brilliantly and wondrously brought forth the land of the true crane, you are one who shall eternally reign,',

'Since it is the hill where the Lord God has descended from heaven, you should wait for a little while,',

'Since there is the august presence of the Lord God, I think that I shall straightway ascend to this divine hill.'

Ureshiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Be that as it may, I rejoice in my heart that I have come to the grove of Tamano,',

'I think it a matter of awe to adore the Lord God when my virtue is not yet complete,',

'How happy it is to sleep in this divine grove where both moon and sun shine purely in the firmament,',

'My soul dances with joy in the grove of Tamano, which is filled with the light of joy,',

'This is surely the height of joy—the blessing of having come to this sacred sanctuary of the grove of Tamano.'

Minashihiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Parting from the auspicious Mitorama God, for a single night my heart fluttered, illuminated by the moon,',

'Soon now, upon the hill of Tamano where the Kototama of the Lord Great God shall reach its august bridge,',

'There is no way to ascend a mountain that lacks a bridge; wait for a little while, O hundred deities,',

'How could the divine work of the deities who create the land easily be able to ascend this divine hill?'

Mayutama no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Until the day the august child is born, I, the Mayutama no Kami, shall wait here,',

'The voice of the true crane is heard loud and clear; is it the return of the Lord Great God?,',

'The voice of the Kalavinka singing out upon the treetops of the white plum must be one that celebrates Kamiyo,',

'Is this grove, fragrant with the scent of white plums, the sacred sanctuary where the Lord Great God descends?,',

'With both moon and sun shut out by the dense thicket of pines, the shadows of sunlight are drawn upon the sand,',

'Since my strength is not yet sufficient, the divine will must be that I hide in the shade of the grove and wait.'

Urahiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Though I am unworthy, having reached the foot of Tamanōka, my heart is filled with awe and reverence,',

'Listening to the cries of the myriad birds, I feel the dawn of a new heavenly spring,',

'The cloud that covered my sleeves is now swept away by the pure wind blowing from the divine peak,',

'Waiting for the august command, I shall polish my soul until it shines like a mirror.'

Takamahiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Upon the sacred heights of Tamano, the august light of the sun and moon illuminates the paths of heaven and earth,',

'Though my steps are hesitant, the guidance of the Mitorama draws my spirit ever upward,',

'Cleansing away the dust of the mortal world, I draw near to the sacred precinct with reverent awe,',

'Now is the time when the divine decree shall be fulfilled throughout the vast domains of the universe.'

Hinodewake no Kami chants a sacred song.

'Like the rising sun that breaks the gloom of night, the glory of the Lord God shines over Tamanōka,',

'My heart, once burdened with heavy shadows, leaps with the dawn of divine awakening,',

'In this blessed realm where heaven and earth unite, all souls find their true refuge,',

'Praising the name of the Supreme Deity, I offer my eternal devotion upon this holy mountain.'

Kamehiko: 'Hey, Yotahiko-san! Look at all those cranes flying around up there! It makes my head spin just watching them.'

Yotahiko: 'Quit gawking, Kamehiko-san! This is a sacred place, so wipe that silly grin off your face before the deities strike you down!'

Kamehiko: 'Aw, come on! I was just admiring how high they go. Do you think we'll ever get to climb up to the top like them?'

Yotahiko: 'Not with a clumsy, mud-headed attitude like yours! We've got to clean up our act and polish our Kototama first, just like the others are doing down here.'

Kamehiko: 'Alright, alright, I get it. Pass me some of that spring water so I can wash my ears out. Maybe it'll help me understand these fancy poems everyone's chanting!'

Yotahiko: 'You're hopeless, Kamehiko-san. Absolutely hopeless! But hand me some of that water too while you're at it—my soul could use a good scrub.'

Thus the attendants and deities continued their purifications and chanted their songs, waiting with pure hearts for the wondrous hour of the divine ascent upon the sacred hill of Tamano.

§ 3

Tamakiharu no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Appearing as the Kami of life, tamakiharu, I shall protect the life of the Miko; Having come here, my heart has grown pure, Though I could not climb this Kamioka, This grove is surely a reflection of Shibitenkai, Everything I see shines brilliantly.

Musubiahase no Kami chants a sacred poem.

The futayosa purification ended, I now Shall serve in the divine work of musubiahase; Making Heaven and Earth, Kami and Kami, intimately United, I shall protect Miyo; Even wider, in Tamanomori where the tokihagi flourish, The scent of plums is pure, the garden where Kami dwells is This hill where Tamanohime-no-Kami is enshrined, Its brilliance is strong, dazzling my eyes; Henceforth, to the mikokoro of the Lord Ōkami, Entrusting ourselves, we shall await the time; Since nanigoto is according to the heart of Kami, I shall not raise a single word of kotoa.

Umashimoto no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Descending from the Takaamahara of umashimoto, The virtues of the Lord Ōkami are noble; Wrapped in the spiritual light of the Lord Kami, How bright is Tamanomori even at night! Moonlight shines high and clear in misora, Illuminating warera through the pines; Whenever I look upon the adornment of the shiraume flowers, I am led to yearn for Tamano no Hime.

Makotoizu no Kami chants a sacred poem.

From afar, serving Kimi, I am now Illuminated by the moon of Tamanomori; The kototama that builds the land of manazuru Has purified water and fire in the jewel spring; Upon the hill where we hear that the Lord Kami will descend, The voice of manazuru is heard high; Awe-struck by the living kototama of the Lord Kami, We have come hither also to build the land; The pines of tokihagi are covered in moss, and the heavenly sky In this closed grove, let there be joy.

Thus the Kami chanted their reflections, and just as the time was being awaited, Futomotoakitsuo no Kami, accompanied by Tamanohime-no-Kami, Ikuyohime-no-Kami, Machiaihiko-no-Kami, and numerous other Kami, leisurely descended the hill, paid respects to all the Kami, and once again guided every single one of them back up to the hill, until finally, all the Kami joined forces to engage in the divine work of creating the land. Ah, kanagara tamachihau mase.

『二夜の禊終りて吾は今 結び合せの神業に仕へむ 天と地と神と神とを睦じく 結び合せて神代を守らむ いや広く常磐樹繁る玉野森に 梅の香清し神います苑は 玉野比女神の鎮まるこの丘の 輝き強しわが目まばゆく この上は主の大神の御心に 任せまつりて時を待つべし 何事も神の心のままなれば われ一言も言挙げはせじ』

美味素の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『美味素の高天原より下ります 主の大神の功尊き 主の神の霊の光に包まれて 夜も明るき玉野森はや 月光は御空に高く冴えにつつ 松を透して吾等を照らせり 白梅の花のよそほひ見るにつけ 玉野の比女の偲ばれにける』

真言厳の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『はろばろと岐美に仕へて吾は今 玉野の森の月に照らさる 真鶴の国土を造ると言霊の 水火清めたり玉の泉に 主の神の天降り給ふと聞く丘に 真鶴の声高く聞ゆる 主の神の生言霊を畏みて 此処に来つるも国土造るとて 常磐樹の松苔むして天津空 閉せる森に歓びあれかし』

かく神々は述懐を歌ひ給ひ、時を待たせる折もあれ、太元顕津男の神は、玉野比女の神、生代比女の神、待合比古の神、其他数多の神々を従へて、悠々と丘を下り、諸神に敬意を表し給ひ、再び丘の上に一柱も残らず導き給ひ、いよいよここに国土生みの神業に、諸神力を合せて、従事し給ふ事とはなりぬ。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
