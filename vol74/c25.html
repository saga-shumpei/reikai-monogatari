Chapter 25: 感歎幽 Bright [1893]

感歎幽明〔1893〕

§ 1

Although the Lord Ōkami descended from Amatsutakamiya to Tamanomori, because the wisdom and enlightenment of the kamigami were not yet complete, He unavoidably established the divine strategy of Wakuōdōjin, became the Kami of Mototsumakoto, and continued to assist in the divine work of Kunitsuku. However, Tamanohime and the other kamigami did not know that He was an avatar thereof, and treated Him carelessly as if He were a fellow kami. When His identity was clearly announced by the wise eyes of Chikaramichio, the Kami of Mototsumakoto immediately assented, composed and recited two miuta, and riding upon a shikon cloud high up in the hisakata misora, returned to Amatsutakamiya—how awe-inspiring indeed.

Again, Tamanohime was chosen as one of the Yasohimegami, and though she awaited the arrival of time for long years at Tamanomori, she still lacked the qualifications to give birth to a precious Miko as a Kunitamagami. If her wisdom and enlightenment were not yet complete, she was caused to suspend the divine work of kamiu in a kamunagarateki manner, and thus she was inevitably made to serve in the divine work of kunitsuku.

Next, Akitsuwanomiko, paying deep attention to the various whispers of the hundred kami and hesitating over the words of the Lord Ōkami, passed long years, so it was inevitable that the divine work of kamiu was thereby delayed of its own accord. Because Akitsuwanomiko was the spirit of mizu, his heart of benevolence and mercy was deep, and furthermore, making the harmony of the kamigami his primary aim, he lacked a valiant and bold heart. Therefore, that he ultimately missed the opportune time for the divine work is a matter to be regretted over and over again.

Ikuyohime, who was born as the mitama of Manazuruyama, was a kami who happened to be omitted from the selection of the eighty himegami, but because she was a wise and clever woman excelling in wisdom and enlightenment, she was established in the world as a Kunitsubashira, and since she was fit to give birth to a Miko, the Lord Ōkami tacitly permitted it, and thus the great divine work was accomplished. The proactive actions of Ikuyohime accorded with the divine strategy of kunitsuku and kamiu; thus, that He adopted the divine strategy of taking up great achievements while ignoring small flaws may be perceived as a measure appropriate to the times.

Ah, Akitsuwanomiko and Tamanohime both lacked wise hearts of supreme goodness, supreme beauty, supreme benevolence, and supreme love, and moreover their valiant hearts were weak. Contrary to the fact that they did not know the true nature of the Kami of Mototsumakoto, who manifested Himself as an avatar before their very eyes, Ikuyohime was a clever woman wise enough to dimly perceive that He was indeed that avatar. Therefore, it is naturally to be assented that she bore a precious Miko. Ah, Kamunagara Tamachihauhaseyo.

At this, Akitsuwanomiko and the others distantly worshiped the Kami of Mototsumakoto as He revealed His true body and returned to Amatsutakamiya, and they recited their miuta:

The Lord Kami finished proclaiming the kototama of izu, And riding upon clouds, He returns to heaven. While dyeing the hisakata sky in shikon hues, Does the Lord Ōkami truly return? Now once more, looking back upon my foolish spirit, It is of no avail to regret the delayed divine work. Forgive me the sin of my hesitating heart, O Lord Great Kami who returns to heaven! Advancing, advancing, opening up and pioneering, to go forth and serve, Treating the great highway of the kami with negligence, I pay heed to the hundred kami and regret That I went against the living kototama of the Lord Kami. Though I hold no heart of opposition against the Lord Kami, Through my hesitating heart, the divine work was delayed; Now that it has come to this, I repent my weak heart. Come, let us readjust the horse of valor! When I think of the heart of Tamanohime, I now Desire to vanish, I grow so sorrowful.

The miuta of Tamanohime:

Not knowing Him to be the avatar of the Lord Kami, morn and eve, Did I think of Him as my subordinate kami? With such a foolish heart of mine as this, How could I ever give birth to a Miko and serve? Because my wisdom and enlightenment were not yet sufficient, did the Lord Kami Entrust the task to Ikuyohime? Carrying the Kunitsubashira of Manazuru within her womb, Ikuyohime was truly precious. Waiting for my lord upon this hill, years have passed, And though my tamashii-sen was clouded, Serving morning and evening in the palace of the Lord Kami, How shameful that I knew not the Kami of Mototsumakoto. Even in the softly blowing wind there is the voice of the kami, How embarrassing not to know the kami sitting by one's side. From this day forth, treating Ikuyohime as a kami pillar, May I look up to her in reverence and accomplish kunitsuku!

原文（近代文語）

主の大神は天津高宮より、玉野森に天降りましつれど、神々の智慧証覚未だ全からざれば、止むを得ず和光同塵の神策を立て給ひて、本津真言の神となり、国土造りの神業を助け給ひつつありけるが、玉野比女の神を初め其他の神々も其化身たるを知らず、うかうかとして同僚の神の如くに扱ひたりけるが、力充男の神の賢き目に名乗り明されて、本津真言の神は直に諾ひ給ひ、二首の御歌を詠ませ給ひて、久方の御空高く紫紺の雲に乗りて、天津高宮に帰らせ給ひしこそ畏けれ。

又玉野比女の神は、八十比女神の一柱に選まれ給ひ、玉野森に年永く時の到るを待たせ給へども、未だ国魂神としての貴き御子を生まさむ資格備はらず、智慧証覚全からざりせば、惟神的に神生みの神業を止められ、茲に国土生みの神業に仕ふべく余儀なくされ給ひしなり。

次に太元顕津男の神は、百神等の種々の囁きに深く心を配り、主の大神の御言葉をためらひつつ、永き年月を経給ひければ、神生みの神業の自ら後れさせ給ひしこそ是非なけれ。顕津男の神は瑞の御霊なれば仁慈の心深く、且つ神々の和親を旨として勇猛心を欠き給ひしかば、終に神業の期を逸し給ひしこそ、返す返すも惜むべき事にこそあれ。

真鶴山の御魂と生れます生代比女の神は、八十柱の比女神の選に漏れ給ひし神なれども、智慧証覚に勝れたる細女賢女にいませば、国津柱と世に立てられ、御子生ますべきに叶ひたれば、主の大神は黙許し給ひ、茲に大神業は遂げられたるなり。生代比女の神の積極的行動は、国土生み神生みの神策に叶ひ奉れば、大功を採りて小瑾を顧みざる神策を採り給ひしも、時代相応の処置とこそ窺はるるなり。

嗚呼顕津男の神、玉野比女の神は、何れも至善至美至仁至愛にして賢しき心を欠き給ひ、且つ勇猛心薄かりしかば、主の神の、眼前に化身として現れ給ふ本津真言の神の真相を知り給はざりしに反し、生代比女の神は賢くも其化身なる事を朧気に覚り居給ひし程の細女なりければ、貴の御子を孕ませ給ひしも宜なりと諾かるるなり。嗚呼惟神霊幸倍坐世。

茲に顕津男の神等は、本津真言の神の御本体を現して、天津高宮に帰らせ給ひしを遠く拝ませ給ひて、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『主の神は厳の言霊宣り終へて 雲に乗らせつ天かへりますも 久方の空を紫紺に染めながら 主の大神は帰りますかも おろかなるわが霊線を今更に 悔ゆるも詮なし神業遅れて ためらひし心の罪を許しませ 天に帰らす主の大御神よ 進み進み拓き拓きて仕へ行く 神の大道をおろそかにせるも 百神に心配りて主の神の 生言霊にそむきしを悔ゆ 主の神にそむく心は持たねども ためらひ心に神業遅れし 今となりて弱き心を悔いにけり いざや勇みの駒立直さむ 玉野比女心を思へば我は今 消えたくなりぬ悲しくなりぬ』

玉野比女の神の御歌。

『主の神の化身と知らず朝夕を 吾従神と思ひけるかも 斯くの如わが愚しき心もて 如何で御子生み仕へ得べきや 智慧証覚未だ足らねば主の神は 生代比女神に依さし給ひしか 真鶴の国津柱を孕みます 生代比女神貴くありける この丘に岐美を待ちつつ年経りし わが魂線は曇りてしかも 主の神の宮に朝夕仕へつつ 真言の神を知らざりにけり そよと吹く風にも神声あるものを 側に坐す神知らざる恥づかしさよ 今日よりは生代比女神を神柱と 仰ぎ奉りて国土生みなさばや』

§ 2

Miuta of Ikuyohimegami.

Since you are a public personage, being among the Yasohashira Himegami,

You shall offer the child of my womb.

From today, submitting to Tamanohime,

I shall nurture the child and tend to it day by day.

Becoming a maidservant to the goddess Tamanohime,

I shall nurture the child and serve morning and evening.

I am merely yearning for the Mizumitama,

And have conceived solely in that brief moment.

As it is not through the direct mandate of the Lord Kami,

I am merely a humble maidservant rearing the child.

Tamanohime, together with the Mizumitama Kami,

Open up the land of Manazurukuni.

Treat me, who hold not a speck of jealousy or grudge,

With ease, and forge the realm.

Until the child of my womb grows up,

I shall purify my heart and serve closely.

Miuta of Akitsuo no Kami.

Oh, the Kototama of the steadfast Ikuyohimegami,

Though I choke with tears of gratitude,

Yielding to the pure heart of the Himegami,

Could it be the child was thus conceived?

Now once more, deeply perceiving the purity of Hime's heart,

I am plunged into tears.

That pure, righteous, and bright Tamashihi,

The Lord Ōkami must have cherished.

Though the child is born from public and myself uniting our Iki,

It is truly the Mitama of the Lord Ōkami.

Miuta of Tamanohime no Kami.

At the purity of Ikuyohimegami's heart,

I have surely been made to feel ashamed.

How could my clouded Tamashihi

Ever have managed to conceive a noble child?

Realizing anew the deep Shigumi of the Lord Kami,

I have trembled.

Thinking of everything as the divine work of Kami's mandate,

There are no clouds or mists of resentment.

Hearing that Ikuyohime preceded me in conceiving the child,

How ashamed I am of my jealous heart.

Like the ever-verdant pine whose color never fades,

I wish to serve with an emerald heart.

Even in the breeze whispering through the pine treetops,

There are visits several times a day.

The Tamashihi, having wearied of waiting for eighty years,

Had grown only more and more clouded.

If not out of love for the pure and radiant Makoto,

How could I ever have conceived the child of Makoto?

In vain have I patiently awaited the Mizumitama ki for so long,

Oh, my foolishness in lagging behind in the divine work.

From now on, transforming my heart single-heartedly,

I will follow Kimikami and assist in begetting the realm.

Miuta of Machiaihiko no Kami.

Weeping as I weigh the heart today of Tamanohime,

Who has waited for so many years,

Even though it is not the divine work of begetting a child

While patiently awaiting the Mizumitama,

Now anew I tremble with awe

At the deep divine heart of the Lord Ōkami.

Since the heavenly realm is a divine kingdom of love and goodness,

It could not tolerate even a hair's breadth of defilement.

In a world where good and evil intersect, how could dust

Remain in the divine work of forging the realm?

The pillar of the young land of Manazurukuni

Has been conceived through pure Makoto.

The noble achievements of Ikuyohimegami

The Lord Ōkami must surely have praised.

Amidst the various events of the age of the gods, morning and evening,

Beholding them, how I used to wander in bewilderment.

Not knowing Him to be the incarnation of the Lord Ōkami,

How I treated the Kami of Hontsu Makoto as a subordinate spirit.

Oh, my foolish Tamashihi, to have treated lightly

The incarnation of the Lord Ōkami.

Weeping as I gazed upon the Kami of Makoto

Who had returned high into the distant expanse of the August Sky,

Now I know the achievements of the Kami of Hontsu Makoto.

Day by day, the divine pillar whose radiance had grown ever brighter

As it returned to Amatsu Takamiyagura,

Was something I dwelt with, not knowing it to be an incarnation.

Be that as it may, blessed is the land of Manazurukuni,

For the child of the Lord Ōkami has been conceived therein.

Uniting the Iki of Su, the Mizumitama is here,

And Ikuyohimegami has conceived the child.

Wide and high is the great firmament, illuminating the young plains of Manazurukuni,

Oh, the divine child.

Miuta of Rikimitsuo no Kami.

The heavenly realm, filled with the power of spirit and body,

Was born solely from the Kototama of Su.

I am Rikimitsuo no Kami, born now

By receiving the spirit and body of the Lord Ōkami.

I too am an incarnation

Who descended here together with the Kami of Hontsu Makoto.

I am indeed the deity of the highest peak

Residing in the Shibi heavenly realm; now I am about to return.

From this day when the three deities have manifested,

I have no more use here; now I shall return.

Oh, myriad deities, multiply earnestly and beget

The realm of Manazurukuni and bring forth the divine child.

Chanting these miuta, turning once more into light and illuminating the surroundings, Rikimitsuo no Kami summons purple clouds, and majestically returns toward Amatsu Takamiyagura—how revered.

斯く御歌詠ませ給ひつつ、再び光となり四辺を照らしながら、力充男の神は紫の雲を呼び起し、悠々として天津高宮に向ひ帰らせ給ふぞ尊けれ。
