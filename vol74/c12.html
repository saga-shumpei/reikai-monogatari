Chapter 12: Forest の Distant 望 [1880]

森の遠望〔1880〕

§ 1

As the Kototama of the Su became crystal clear, upon the summit of Manazuruyama, beginning with Akitsuo no Kami, the countless deities stood arrayed.

Far away in the western land, gazing upon that scene where the light of the sun and moon, reflected upon the bright and clear waters of Tamanōmi, appeared like a mirror, and proclaiming it a wondrous land, with the Auspicious Mitama at the head, mounted upon a white horse, majestically descending the gentle slope of the sakamichi, how valiant it is!

The neighing of the steeds, the sound of hooves, and the clattering echo of the bits; combing their hair in the wind, they advanced toward Tamanōmi.

Akitsuo no Kami chanted majestically upon his horse:

The sun and moon shine bright in the heavens, Illuminating every corner of the land of Kunibara. The blowing wind is fragrant as well, And the scent of plum blossoms drifts about. Joyful is our advance to Tamanomori, Leaving Manazuruyama behind us, To open the divine land and give birth to deities, And to make the land of Manazurukuni thrive. Escorted by the countless deities, We proceed across the great plain of Ōnogahara. This land, where reeds grow lush, Is yet youthful in spirit; Blending the water and fire of the Kototama purely, We shall serve in the divine work of Kami.

Looking out, the sun and moon float upon the mirror-like waters of Tamanokosui deep within the great plain. Since the Himegami awaits us at the pure sacred court where ancient trees densely flourish, The cloudiness of Ikuyo hime's heart must have cleared away. The path we tread remains serene under clear skies, And the sound of our steed's hooves is valiant indeed. Even as we advance in accordance with the blessing of Mimashi no Kami, Determined never to go against the words entrusted by the Lord God, This is a journey circling the eighty lands of the realm.

Tōmio no Kami chanted his divine poem:

How pure is the divine escort of the Auspicious Mitama! The mirror of Tamano lies across our path. Is the pure Lake Tamano, where both sun and moon float, The heart of Himegami who awaits her dear Lord? Though the way is far and our steeds urge us on, straight ahead, I feel an impatient longing on this journey that is hard to press forward. Ah, come now, drawing tight the reins of our steed, Let us give it a single whip and gallop forth! The valiant figure of the Auspicious Mitama and her Lord Resembles the majesty of the ever-verdant pine. The mountain of Manazu has grown hazy far behind; How fares the deity Kuninaka no Hiko? May we learn the blessings of the Kototama Upon the mountains of Manazuru, which were once wrapped in dark clouds and afflicted.

Maruyahiko no Kami chanted his divine poem:

Both sun and moon shine brightly and roundly in Maruyahiko's round sky, Illuminating the land of Manazurukuni to its utmost bounds. Though the forest of Tamano toward which we press forward is far, We shall easily reach it by the strength of our steeds. How the pure Lake Tamano, where sun and moon float amidst the waves, Must be the form of my Lord! That lake which floats the shadows of the eternal sun and moon Must surely be the Auspicious Mitama itself. The joy of advancing with steeds aligned Through the land where white plum blossoms shed their sweet fragrance. The peaks of Manazuru grow successively distant in shadow, Until even the range of my vision fails to reach them. Far-off Manazuru mountain grows hazy, and the forest of Tamano Becomes clearly visible to my eyes step by step. Praying that there be blessings on the path where the Auspicious Mitama advances, I worshipped her retreating figure. Valiant indeed is the sound of the trotting hooves of our spirited steeds On this very day of our journey for the birth of the land. Do the heavens and earth celebrate the departure Of this journey for the birth of the land, growing ever more clear and serene?

As the day draws near for Tamano Hime and her Lord to meet face to face, Both my heart and my steed surge with valor. The wind blowing softly across the fields May well caress the hair of the Auspicious Mitama and grow fragrant. The journey of departure today, across the land where reeds luxuriantly grow, is bright and clear. Even the sound of the wind rustling through the tips of the leaves Sounds as though it blesses the departure of my Lord. Even to the limits of the low-hanging, prostrate white clouds stretching as far as the eye can see, The Kototama of the Kami lives on.

スの言霊に澄みきりし 真鶴山の頂上に 顕津男の神はじめとし 百神等は立たせつつ 遥かに遠き西の国 輝き澄める玉野湖の 水にうつらふ日月の 光は鏡の如くなる 其の光景を打ち眺め 美しき国よと宣らしつつ 瑞の御霊を先頭に 白馬に跨がりとうとうと 緩勾配の坂道を 下らせ給ふぞ勇ましき 駒の嘶き蹄の音 轡の響もがちやがちやと 風に頭髪梳り 玉野湖さして進みけり。

見渡せば大野の奥に鏡なす 玉野湖水に月日浮べり こんもりと老樹の森の繁りたる 清庭に行かむ比女神いませば 生代比女心の曇り晴れにけむ わがゆく道は空晴れにつつ わが駒の蹄の音も勇ましく 美波志の神の幸に進むも 主の神の依さしの言葉そむかじと 国の八十国廻る旅なり』

遠見男の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『瑞御霊神の御供の清しさよ 玉野の鏡行手に横ふ 月も日も浮びて清き玉野湖は 岐美を待たせる比女神の心か 道遠み駒ははやれどすくすくに 進まぬ旅をもどかしみ思ふ いざさらば駒の手綱を引きしめて 一鞭あてて駆け出さむかな 瑞御霊岐美の姿の雄々しさは 常磐の松のよそほひなるも 真鶴の山は後にかすみたり 国中比古の神如何ますらむ 黒雲に包まれなやみし真鶴の 山に学びし言霊の幸を』

圓屋比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『月も日も圓屋の比古のまるまると 高く照らせり真鶴国原 進み行く玉野の森は遠けれど 駒の力に安くいたらむ 月も日も波間に浮ぶ玉野湖の 清きは岐美の姿なるかも 久方の月日のかげを浮べたる 湖水は瑞の御霊なるらむ 白梅の薫ゆかしき国原を 駒たて並べ進むたのしさ 真鶴の山はつぎつぎ影遠み わが目路さへもとどかずなりぬ 真鶴山遠くかすみて玉野森 つぎつぎわが目にしるくなりぬる 瑞御霊進ます道に幸あれと 吾は祈りぬ後姿拝みつ 駿馬の蹄の音も勇ましく 進む今日こそ国土生みの旅 国土生みの旅の門出を天地は 寿ぎ給ふか澄みきらひつつ

玉野比女見合ひます日の近づきて 吾も勇みぬ駒も勇みぬ 野辺を吹く風やはらかに瑞御霊 御髪を撫でて薫りゆくかも 葭葦の生い茂るなる国原を 出で立つ今日の旅はさやけし さらさらと葉末に渡る風の音も 岐美の旅立ち寿ぐがに聞ゆ 見の限り墜居向伏す白雲の 果さへ神の言霊生くるも』

多々久美の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『天地にタタの言霊組み合ひて 生り出でにけむ玉野の森は わが駒は勇みに勇みわが魂は 清みに清み心安き今日 瑞御霊旅に立たする今日の日は 天地清しく晴れ渡りぬる 清きあかき正しき神の言霊に 晴れ渡りけむこれの天地は つぎつぎに国土生み神を生ましつつ 荒野を拓かす岐美ぞ畏き 気永くも岐美を待たせし玉野比女は 今日の生日を寿ぎますらむ 吾は今駿馬の背に跨りて 葭葦茂る大野を走るも わが駒は言霊の水火に生れし駒 永久に疲れず進み行くなり』

§ 2

Ureshiho no Kami chants a sacred poem.

'As the forest of Tamano draws ever closer and closer in succession, Joyful am I, my heart leaping high; The evening moon floating purely on Lake Tamano Washes and purifies my soul-line; Serving in the entourage of Kimi day after day, How full of hope is this journey on which I set forth! Though any manner of obstruction may baffle heaven and earth, I have no regrets, I am Ureshiho no Kami; It is the heavenly land of joy, and in Aizen's Purple-Forbidden Heavenly Realm, what is there to lament? My steed shakes its mane and surges forth with courage; Ah, what a valiant figure, how joyous! In the heavenly realm overflowing with joy, I rejoice in the fortune of having been born; Into the land of gods where delight and joy never end, I shall go forth with courage and serve in the divine work; The great sky is purely and deeply azure, the plains of the country Are fragrant with flowers, and the blowing wind is cool; Swift though the legs of the noble steed are, the sweat that breaks out Is swept away by the wind, and purely I go onward. Dense and verdant, deep within the great plains, The view of the forest of Tamano is clear and bright.'

Mihashihiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

'Awe-inspiring is the divine majesty that gave birth to mountains, Like floating oil, from the drifting Manazuru! The mountain of Manazuru has receded and can no longer be seen; Far indeed have we come along the winding mountain trails. Making a mirror of the great plains of the land as far as the eye can see, The shining water—oh, it is Lake Tamano! I now follow the wondrous Mitama, And with a pure heart I proceed onward. Scents drifting mildly upon the wind blowing over the great plains, Today's journeying has indeed been delightful. Eternal heaven is purely and brightly serene and clear, And the land through which we pass has a pure wind. When I hear the voices of insects chirping loudly in the thickets, I praise the departure of Kimi; While Manazuru beats its wings against the sky, Flitting and circling right above our heads as we go. Flocks of white herons amidst the reeds and rushes Rise up in surprise at the sound of my steed's hooves; Valiant are the strides of the steeds marching side by side, Oh, you eleven deities, matching step for matched step! Pure indeed is the heart of today's entourage, Who set forth to create the land and give birth to gods; Holding our innermost hearts righteous and pure, We shall humbly offer true words to Kimi. Crossing how many great rivers and traversing wild moors, Today we behold the shadow of a pure forest; Moon and sun and the light of the stars sparkle and gleam, O land of Manazuru, clearing in utmost purity!'

Tamadama no Kami chants a sacred poem.

'Tamadama no Kami shall assist the divine work of birthing divine children, For as long as the sacred words of Tamadama no Kami shall endure, That divine work of creating the land and giving birth to divine children, Shall Tamadama no Kami reverently uphold and aid. Looking out afar, the plains of the four directions are serene and fair, Beautiful and vast, their limits can never be known; Endlessly vast and of unknown bounds, this youthful land, His virtue is noble, Kimi who causes it to be made! To the utmost limits where the walls of blue clouds stand, as far as White clouds sink down and bow their heads, it is the divine land; Giving birth to the Kunitama deity of the heavenly realm, Following the deity, have I come hither. Divine work is vast, remote, unto the very furthest Ends of the heavenly realm shall I serve in the entourage. When I gaze upon the divine figure of Hatsunoo no Kami From behind, it was gleaming light! Becoming the flower of this youthful land of Manazuru, And becoming light, Kimi goes forth! Far off is the land, and the whisperings of turbulent deities Echoed even to the ends of the fields. Proclaiming the sacred kototama of the wondrous Mitama, May the hundred ugly deities submit! Shall I too, before the august tail-front of the wondrous Mitama, Serve and proclaim the wondrous kototama? My steed tosses its mane high and scatters it wild, Courageously traveling across these great plains! All the hundred deities also encouraging themselves, today On which they chant the kototama miuta is truly auspicious!'

Tamakihari no Kami chants a sacred poem.

'Thinking that I preserve my life as long as the jewel-bound limit and serve In today's entourage, my heart is glad. Preserving eternal life, let us create the land, And guard the divine work by which gods are given birth. Blessed by the life of water and fire of the kototama, The Purple-Forbidden Heavenly Realm shall flourish forever. I think to turn and pacify the deities of Lake Tamano Through the sacred words of Tamakihari no Kami. Clearing away the dark clouds of the mountain of Manazuru, Hime of Ikuyo lies hidden within the lake's waters. The lake's waters where Hime of Ikuyo lies hidden are calm in their waves, Floating the sun and moon like a mirror. Towering and sealing the sky of evergreen trees, The forest of Tamano draws ever closer. Tamanohime shall stand upon the lake shore and wait For the coming forth of the wondrous Mitama. The footsteps of the steeds have suddenly grown swift, Could it be that the divine work has grown urgent?'

魂機張の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

§ 3

Musubiahase no Kami chants a Miuta.

The divine fire and water of the Ametsuchi are united, How joyous is today, serving at the august sleeves! The pure Mitama of Tamanohime and the Mitama of my Kimi Shall be joined together and protected. The Kami lurking deep at the bottom of Tamanomizūmi Shall be made to ascend to the Mikuni and be saved. Today's sky, without even a shred of a Kumokire, Is pure in the wind as we advance over the great plain. With the coming forth of the courageous and wondrous Mitama, Even my Hayakoma grows spirited. The sound of my horse's hooves goes katsu-katsu As today's journey advances to the Sugado. Bathed in the dew of the Kayakusa, when I think of How I slept through a Hitoyo, today is truly joyful. As evening falls, the joy of advancing to the mansion Where the Miyabashira is erected in the forest of Tamano!

Umashimoto no Kami chants a Miuta.

Evening approaches swiftly, and my horse, Hurrying along the way, has started forth with spirit! Even the shadows of the Tsukihi reflected in Tamanomizūmi Are soft in light, perhaps because evening draws near. Softly, the wind of Shinado blows refreshingly Against my face as twilight is about to fall. With evening drawing near, the voices of insects gathering on the Kusaba Can be heard growing all the more distinct! Having been born in the heavenly Mikuni of Aizen, To live forever is a joyful thing indeed.

Shingongen no Kami chants a Miuta from horseback.

Like the long, long tail of the copper pheasant, crossing the field road, It has quickly become twilight, it seems. Close to the range of vision, Tamanomizūmi lies spread horizontally, And the shadow of a white bird floats upon the water's surface. The white bird plays, inverting its pure shadow As it sports on the lake at twilight. In this lake where the sky is blue and the water is also blue, The shadow of the white bird floats unstained. Crossing these broad waters of Tamanomizūmi, Shall we press onward to the forest of Tamano? Though it has become twilight, the moonlight of the august sky Shines ever more brilliantly.

Here, the party of Akitsuo no Kami, having continued their long journey, at length arrive at the shore of Tamanomizūmi at evening, resting their breath and resting their horses, and as they gaze across at the green shadows of the forest on the far shore, they stand there in pure composure.
