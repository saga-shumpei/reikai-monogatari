Chapter 6: Truth Crane Mountain Spirit [1874]

真鶴山霊〔1874〕

§ 1

In the living Kototama of the Lord Kami, the heavenly realm which the Palace of Shibi rules, although it opened wonderfully and minutely, yet reaching to its every nook and corner, the realm of the land was still immature, and like young herbs the ground was soft and yielding, floating like grease upon the water, and because it did not yet form the shape of a land, the Kami, who is manifested as the august Mitama, Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, was born from the water and fire of the sound 'a', and opening the heavenly realm of Shibi, solidifying the land, creating Kami, and striving day and night to open it wonderfully and minutely, the Kami of the four quarters who obstructed the bends of the way having been made to submit, crossed mountains and rivers, overpassed wild paths, and where reeds and rushes grew thick,

He set forth, pointing toward the direction of the immature land of the southwest, the region of Mount Manazuru, but by the devising of the hundred Kamitachi, today he arrived at last upon the mountain ridge of Manazuru. When he proclaimed the living Kototama, the curtain of dense mist that enveloped the four quarters and the upwelling steam gradually grew thin, and the sun and moon of Hisakata grew clear and bright, becoming an age to illuminate the reed plains. Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, leading the hundred Kamitachi to the summit of Mount Manazuru, proclaims the rigorous Kototama.

In that august poem, he says:

'Even the heavenly realm of Hisakata: is the land still immature, and does it float like grease upon the water? Opening the floating land, creating Kami, and fulfilling the divine will of the Lord Ōkami, when I look out, as far as the eye can reach, the mist has risen. The land is such that there are no means to make Kami live, yet by the majesty of the Kototama, I now think to solidify the immature realm of the land. Ma-mo-mu-me-mi, in the utilization of the living Kototama, this realm of the land is about to be born. Although there are lands ever so wide, the land is still immature, and its floating shape is not yet fixed. Mount Manazuru towers above the earth, yet it is soft like mire.

Though flowers are brilliant, they scatter. Who is there whose world shall be eternal? Crossing today the far-off mountains of changing life, dreaming no shallow dreams, nor getting drunk. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, in the divine light of the hundred thousand living Kototama, may the august sky clear up, may the earth of rough metals dry up, may it solidify. May even the hundred trees and plants grow lush and thick, may flowers open, and precious, round fruits grow and ripen, becoming the food of life for the jewel-strings of the Kamitachi.

Coming to the southern limit of the heavenly realm of Shibi, I now desire to produce the land, to create Kami, acting in accordance with the Divine Will, and to shine the divine light of the living Kototama upon heaven and earth and the four quarters; enduring a hundred sorrows, day and night progressing gradually,

To the immature land of Mount Manazuru, leading the hundred Kamitachi courageously, has he come this day? From the very beginning when the heavenly world was opened, it is an unprecedentedly joyous day. The moon in the august sky grows clear, and the stars, like the number of fine sands, shine forth, blooming like flowers, and appearing as fruits, as if celebrating the glory of the heavenly realm. Ah, truly acting in accordance with the Divine Will, the august water and fire of the living Kototama surely create the divine land and produce Kami, being the divine work of the divine majesty of Kami, being the divine work of the divine majesty of Kami.'

When he thus sang and chanted the living Kototama as 'U-ā-ō', the immature land of Mount Manazuru gradually swelled up, puffed up, and solidified; and those that grew forth first were the young pines of evergreen trees, the stalks of white plum blossoms, and bamboo shoots.

Maruyahiko no Kami, unable to restrain his admiration upon seeing this auspicious omen, chants an august poem:

'How glorious, how glorious! By the Kototama of the auspicious Mitama, this heaven and earth have cleared away entirely. The pines of evergreen trees, the white plum blossoms, and bamboo shoots have grown, enveloping Mount Manazuru. When I stand upon Mount Manazuru, as far as the eye can see, the clouds and mist have cleared and begun to shine. Bathing in the dew of the blessings of the moon traversing the heaven, may the hundred trees of this realm of the land flourish. The wide marsh surrounding Mount Manazuru has also dried up, becoming pure white to the bottom. The sites of the marshes gradually swell up, and the hundred trees and plants continue to grow forth. Mount Manazuru, where I stand, grows higher, swelling one after another. Swelling and expanding one after another, the mountain of Manazuru will soon become a land of the realm.'

§ 2

Ubudama no Kami chants a sacred song.

The Mount Manazuru which I myself brought forth is the auspicious Mitama, Solidified indeed in the living Kototama. The solidified Mount Manazuru is Ma-Mo-Mu-Me-Mi, While water and fire congealing swelled high. As commanded by the auspicious Mitama, the Kototama of Ma-Mo-Mu-Me-Mi, Manifestly the Kuni has solidified. In the auspicious Kototama of Ma-Mo-Mu-Me-Mi, Ikuyohime, The form of the divine Mikoto is faintly visible. Come forth, O Goddess Ikuyohime, to the sacred mountain of Manazuru, Where the auspicious Mitama stands manifest. Though the mountain of Manazuru is solidified in Miiki, It is lonely that there is no Goddess to guard it.

From beneath the voice thus chanting, cleaving the earth, there is a Himegami who appears gradually, her Yōshiyoku being Tanrei and like a jewel.

Akitsuo no Kami, beholding this demeanor, unable to contain his joy, chants a sacred song.

Born as the soul of the mountain of Manazuru, How lovely is the form of Ikuyohime! Born in the water and fire of Kototama, O Ikuyohime, Guard this Kunibara eternally.

Ikuyohime no Kami chants a sacred song with an uncertain Kototama.

Parting the mountain of Manazuru, I now, It seems, have met the radiance of Kimi. Since I am she who was born in the water and fire of Kototama, I shall eternally guard the sacred mountain.

Akitsuo no Kami chants.

O the Kototama of the courageous Ikuyohime, I shall faithfully and truly assent to it.

Ikuyohime no Kami chants a sacred song.

How precious is the divine Mikotonori of the auspicious Mitama, I shall never forget it even to the end of myriad generations. O Goddess Ikuyohime, awakened From the Miiki of the divine Mikoto of Ubudama no Kami.

Maruyahiko no Kami chants.

Thou, Ikuyohime, who hast emerged into the wondrous Miiwaki of Kototama, art fair, Born beautiful, gentle, and valiant, Ikuyohime is indeed the mirror of women. By the meritorious deeds of the auspicious Mitama and Ubudama no Kami, Thou, O Himegami, art born with a fair countenance. Even in the vast Purple Tenuity Heavenly Realm, I think, There cannot be so pure a Goddess. The mountain of Manazuru gradually expanding, Becomes the pillar of Kuni, which is most felicitous.

Kuninakahiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

Earlier than all, I came and awaited, Proclaiming the Kototama that I would welcome the auspicious Mitama. How blessed is the emergence of the auspicious Mitama, Whereby this Kunibara has been solidified. Hereupon, serving as Kuninakahiko, I shall eternally guard this sacred sanctuary. Since I am he who became the U sound of the Lord God, I shall eternally solidify this noble Kunibara.

Tatakumi no Kami chants a sacred song.

Clouds and mists, by the Kototama of the auspicious Mitama, Have cleared away completely; how glorious, how glorious! Glorious, glorious, the Kuni has cleared, and the dense mists have cleared away. From this day forth, clearing away, may it be guarded eternally.

Minaboshihiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

I am Minaboshihiko, who became the U sound of the Lord God, From this day forth I shall guard the Floating Bridge of Heaven. Since this Kuni is still youthful, the auspicious Mitama Shall suspend the Floating Bridge and ferry across. Solidifying the earth with Wa-Wo-U-We-I living Kototama, I shall guard the growth of the hundred grasses and trees. Opening up the Kuni where reeds and rushes grow lush, I shall serve the Kami as Minaboshi.

Tamakiharu no Kami chants a sacred song.

Preserving all lives, Tamakiharu no Kami shall serve in all the ages of the world. Distributing and granting the nourishment of Tamakiharu life to all things, I shall open up the divine Kuni. Guarding the lives of the Kami and assisting all Water and fire, I shall serve in the divine age. Serving in the retinue of Akitsuo no Kami, Today I have ascended the mountain of Manazuru. Guarding Tamakiharu life eternally, I shall uphold the divine work of Kimi. If I should become a Kami who guards the thread-like Lives of the hundred deities of Heaven and Earth, By the blessing of the Kototama of the U sound of the Lord God, I am the Kami of life who was born. Hastening to Ubudama no Kami, for the divine work Of creating Kami, I shall serve through all the ages of the world.
