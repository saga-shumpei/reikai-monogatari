Chapter 9: Truth Crane 鳴動 [1877]

真鶴鳴動〔1877〕

§ 1

In the universe of heaven and earth, from the divine majesty of the living Kototama, there is nothing more precious besides. Both the heavenly divine land and the land of the Lord, upon the achievements of the living Kototama, mountains and rivers are born, grasses and trees sprout, and the myriad Kamigami are born forth. Now, the heavenly realm of Shibi is a land of love and goodness. Love and goodness are the sincere spirit and divine form of the Lord God. Love, from the beginning of the heavenly realm, as a divine gift born forth to bring the heart of God to life—yet love accumulates, and at last becomes a wondrous passion, burning the heavens with the flames of the breast, scorching the earth, and raising all manner of calamities—how fearsome! As the spirit of Manazuruyama,

appearing forth, Ikuyohime, the fresh spirit of the divine Mikoto, upon seeing even the majestic divine form of the Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, her flame of passion blazed up. Her face flushing, wagering her life like a cord of jewels, she longed for him; yet dreading lest she act contrary to the divine will of the Lord Ōkami, even as He declared the principles with skillful words, Himegami, sinking deeply into the abyss of disappointment and dejection, while the flame in her breast blazed fiercely, suddenly transformed into the appearance of a fearsome Orochi, like black clouds enveloping heaven and earth, and raising her voice to speak against Tamano no Hime—how terrifying! Beginning with Akitsuo no Kami,

the hundred deities were troubled. Wishing to soothe the heart of Hime, in due measure the fresh spirit escaped from that place, yet the flame in her breast would not subside. Her whole body heating and blazing fiercely, unable to endure the intense distress, she plunged into the lake to extinguish the agony—how touching is the heart of Hime! Ah, Kaminagara Kamigami! God is love, God is benevolence. The ultimate of love and feeling is the thread of passion that cannot be easily untangled, and its tangling and disorder cannot be helped.

Musubiai no Kami chants a divine poem:

'The sincere power that binds together all things in the heavenly realm is indeed none other than passion! Binding together and cherishing one another, uniting water and fire, the divine work of giving birth to life is awe-inspiring. Hikogami longs for Himegami, and Himegami longs for Hikogami—such is the way of the heavenly realm. Joy, sorrow, pleasure, and tumult all well forth from passion. What shall be done? There is no way to stop it; it is the galloping of the steed called passion! I, who am the god of union born in the water and fire of the sound of U, consider it a sorrow. I had no means to soothe the sincere heart of Tamano Hime and Ikuyo no Hime.'

Umusu no Kami has a divine poem chanted:

'In the land of Umukuni, the land of abundance, the land of jewels, is it not the flower of passion that blooms at all times? The end of love and the height of goodness become passion, and appear as sincerity. If there were no such thing as passion in the heavenly realm, this land region would be lonely. Before long, now once again they would rage. Ikuyo no Hime will cleave the lake, and at this time heaven and earth will shake all at once; winds will blow fiercely, and rain will overflow the earth. The land of Umusu, born forth by the Kototama, will once again become a muddy mire. The flame of passion will burn all things, beginning with the vast skies and even upon the earth; the heart of passion will fill heaven and earth, and wrap the heart of God in darkness. Fearsome is passion, and joyous too is the flower that blooms along the path of love.'

Just as this is being chanted, once again a fierce fire blazed up from the foothills, drawing close near the person of Akitsuo no Kami. The fresh spirit, having no other choice, leading the myriad deities,

'Sa sa sū sē shī Za zo zū zē zī Step back, step back, O flames, O smoke! The fresh spirit shall extinguish the flames.

The mountain of Manazura is the sacred mountain of the gods; How should the might of Magatsu no Kami rage? Ikuyohime has yearned solely for me; I find it deplorable that she has become a flame. Along the path of creating the land and giving birth to children, Do not obstruct, O Himegami of passion! I now possess no heart of passion; yet for a while, I shall become your sacrifice. If I can bring forth the god of the land's soul, I shall simply Depart and press forward, a suffering body indeed! Ikuyohime, how shall I deal with your longing for me? If it were but a single time of sharing the pillow—'

Even as he chants thus, the flames that had burned up to his feet suddenly vanished without a trace, a sweet fragrance perfumed all directions, and even the singing voice of the Kalaviṅka could be heard clearly—how wondrous.

Thus, as the fires flickered and the sacred space shifted between the realms of the living and the divine, the resonance of the spirit echoed through the valleys of Manazura, leaving an indelible mark upon the unfolding tapestry of the heavenly creation.

原文（近代文語）

あらはれ出でし生代比女 神の神言の瑞御霊 太元顕津男の神の 雄々しき御姿見るよりも 恋の炎は燃え盛り 面ほてりつつ玉の緒の 命をかけて恋ひ給へば 主の大神の御旨に そむかむ事をおそれまし 言葉たくみに理を 宣らせ給ふを比女神は 忽ち失望落胆の 淵に沈ませ給ひつつ 胸の炎は燃えさかり 天地を包む黒雲と 忽ち変じ恐しき 大蛇の姿と現れて 玉野の比女に仇せむと 言あげ給ふ恐しさ 顕津男の神はじめとし

百神等は悩みまし 比女の心を和めむと よきほどほどに瑞御霊 其の場を逃れ給ひしが 胸の炎はをさまらず 全身熱して燃え盛り 強き悩みに堪へかねて 湖水に飛び込みなやましさ 消し止めむとなし給ふ 比女の心ぞいぢらしき 嗚呼惟神々々 神は愛なり仁なり 愛と情の終極は とくにとかれぬ恋の糸 もつれ乱るぞ是非なけれ。

結比合の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『天界の総てのものを結び合はす 誠の力は恋なりにけり 結び合ひ睦び合ひつつ水火合せ 命を生まむ神業かしこき 比古神は比女神を恋ひ比女神は 比古神恋ふる天界の道 喜びもまた悲しみも楽しみも 騒ぎも恋よりわき出づるなり 如何にせむ止むるよしもなきものは 恋てふ駒のあがきなりけり ウの声の水火に生れし結び合せの 神なるわれはかなしと思へり 玉野比女生代の比女の真心を なだむる由も吾なかりける』

美味素の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『美味国弥栄の国玉の国に ときじくひらく恋の花かも 愛の果て善の極みは恋となり 誠となりてあらはるるなり 天界に恋てふもののなかりせば さびしかるらむこれの国原 やがて今再び荒び給ふらむ 生代の比女は湖水を割りて この時ゆ天地一度に揺ぎ出し 風吹き荒び雨地に溢れむ 言霊に生み出で給ひし美味素の 国も再び泥濘となるらむ 久方の御空をはじめ地の上の 総てを焼かむ恋の炎は 恋心天と地とにふさがりて 神の心を闇に包まむ 恐しきものは恋なり楽しきも 恋路にかをる花なりにけり』

斯く歌はせ給ふ折しも、再び山麓より猛火燃え上り、顕津男の神の身辺近く迫り来ければ、瑞の御霊は止むを得ず、百神等を率ゐまして、

『サーソースーセーシー ザーゾーズーゼージー さがれさがれ炎よ煙よ 瑞の御霊は炎を消さむ。

真鶴の山は尊き神の山 曲津の神の威猛るべきやは 生代比女神はひたすらわれ恋ひつ 炎となりしかあさましとおもふ 国土を生み御子を生まさむ道の辺に さまたげするな恋の比女神 我は今恋心なしさはあれど しばしを汝の犠牲とならむ 国魂の神を生み得ば我はただ 離りて進まむ苦しき身なるよ 生代比女我を恋ふるも如何にせむ ただ一度の手枕なりせば』

斯く歌ひ給ふや、足下まで燃え上りし火焔は忽ち跡なく消えて、芳香四辺に薫じ、迦陵頻伽の啼く音さへ清しく聞え来たるぞ不思議なれ。
