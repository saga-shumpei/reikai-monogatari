Chapter 8: Black Cloud 晴 Bright [1876]

黒雲晴明〔1876〕

§ 1

Here, the flames of grudge of Ikuyohime no Kami, who had deeply enveloped Manazuruyama, became black smoke and troubled the group of Kami; yet perhaps somewhat eased by the thick and affectionate words of Mizumitama, the deeply hanging black clouds cleared away as if wiped clean, the scent of plum blossoms wafted through the four quarters, and the truth of Shibitenkai was revealed, whereupon Tohomio no Kami also, out of an excess of joy, chanted a Miuta.

How pitiful is the heart of Ikuyohime no Kami / Has the fire of her burning breast become black clouds? / Fearsome things are love indeed, and words indeed / The darkness cleared away instantly / That the black clouds of love should block the heavenly world / I had never even thought in a dream / Since it is the Aizen Tenkai, that a heart called loving / Should arise must surely be unavoidable / To be loved and to love in return, I think, conforms / To the way of the Tenkai / By the gentle Kototama of Mizumitama no Kami / The black clouds of love cleared completely away / The heart called loving is precious, and I too / Think of living while loving and being loved.

Mihashihiko no Kami chants:

So that I may fill the hearts of the Futahashira Kami / Would that I might build the Mihashi of Kototama / Becoming the kami of bridge-building, I desire to accomplish this love / 〓__GT_13105__ with pity and in detail / Yet, to disobey the divine decree / Of the Lord Ōkami is also painful / Though it accords not with reason, love is love / There is indeed a principle of the heart that must be heard / The clouds of love flowing outside the bounds of reason / Were beyond even the power of the kami / Born into the raw Kototama of the voice 'U' / For me there is not even the shadow of love / My heart that merely loves is solely / The light of the divine work of Mizumitama.

Kuninakahiko no Kami chants a Miuta:

In the forest of the shore of Tamanoike, eternally / Dwelling, the heart of Himegami is deeply touching / If Tamanoikehimegami also hears of this matter / She too will likely kindle her flames again / Praying for all ages that such an example / Shall not be in the age of kami / Even after praying, this robe of love of no avail / How sad that there is no way to tear it / In the thriving land of Toyoashihara / If there were not love, the age of kami would not flourish / Is it an age of kami where good things are nurtured with perversity, and perverse things with goodness? / Becoming ogres and demons, the fire of the breast / Enveloped Manazuruyama in clouds / Fearsome and yet gentle is what is called love / It is the burning of the fire of the heart.

Umitama no Kami chants a Miuta:

When I think of the painfulness of the heart / Of Mizumitama no Kami, I am truly sad / Standing among Tamanoikehime and Ikuyohime no Kami / One feels a longing for the Kimi who troubles his heart / If only it were possible, I would save the troubles of Kimi / Though I think so, I cannot reach them, for I am a person of ugly face.

Maruyahiko no Kami chants a Miuta once more:

Today I have come to thoroughly realize the difficulty / Of the divine work of creating the land and giving birth to kami / Now I know the sad heart of Akatsuo / Because he stands as the pillar of the country / If it befell the body of an ordinary kami, he would grandly / Enjoy love to his heart's content / The words of the Lord of Kami are heavy; Ikuyohime / The heart of the kami wells up as flames / Having the feeling of standing within burning fire / How pitiful is Mizumitama who suffers / Standing as the Kunitsu pillar of Manazura / Before long she was enveloped in clouds / The clouds of love that shroud the village-liver heart / Did Ikuyohime not clear away? / How will Mizumitama escape this difficult pass / I worry as I watch / There is surely no way for Ikuyohime to extinguish the fire of her burning breast / Neither, it seems, is there for Mizumitama no Kami.

Ureshishi no Kami chants a Miuta:

Thinking how one might untangle the tangled hair of love / Is also for the sake of Mizumitama / If it is granted to Ikuyohime no Kami, it will surely trouble / The heart of Tamanoikehime no Kami / Since it is a love that knows no barrier between upper and lower / The strong-hearted Hitogami will likely win / Though the black clouds of Manazuruyama merely cleared away / The heart of Kimi has still not cleared of its trouble / Now, thinking upon the breast of Mizumitama no Kami / I cannot stop my tears / The path called love, limiting it to the age of kami / I pray that it shall not be left even to the last ages / Even if one prays, love will likely be of no avail / For the Goddess whose threads of the heart are tangled.

Tamakiharifun no Kami chants:

I think there is no way to tear / The robe of love to which life is staked, fleeting as a jeweled cord / The flames of love of the Himegami who grudges not her fleeting life / Have they become ogres? / To think that one would cast away life for the sake of love / Must be the truth of the color of the heart / From the manly yet gentle deportment of Mizumitama / The heart of Himegami has been nurtured / Advancing straight ahead while laying low the path of reason / There was no wicket gate in the darkness of love / At any rate, all you hundred kami, if we are to proclaim the divine words / In the sacred place of the mountain of Manazura, would that we might do so.

To this Miuta, the hundred kami and others purified their hearts and cleansed their bodies, and reverently chanted the divine words facing the direction of Amatsutakamiyaya, repeating the Kototama of seventy-five sounds over and over again, praying for a long time.

原文（近代文語）

ここに真鶴山を深く包みし生代比女の神の、恨みの炎は黒煙となりて、一行の神々を悩めたりしが、瑞の御霊の厚き情の言葉に、稍心安んじたるか、さしもに深き黒雲は、拭ふが如く晴れ渡り、梅ケ香四方に薫じ、紫微天界の真相を表はしければ、遠見男の神も喜びの余り、御歌うたひ給ふ。

『いたましき生代の比女神の心かな 燃ゆる胸の火黒雲となりしか 恐しきものは恋かも言葉かも 闇は忽ち晴れ渡りける 天の世に恋の黒雲ふさぐとは われは夢にも思はざりしよ 愛善の天界なれば恋ふるてふ 心のおこるも是非なかるべし 愛されて愛し返すは天界の 道に叶へるものとし思ふ 瑞御霊神のやさしき言霊に 晴れ渡りける恋の黒雲 愛すてふ心はめぐしわれもまた 愛し愛され住ままく思ふ』

美波志比古の神はうたひ給ふ。

『二柱神の心をみたすべく われ言霊の神橋かけばや はしかけのわれ神となりこの恋を 〓__GT_13105__怜に委曲に遂げさせ度きもの さりながら主の大神の御依さしに そむかせまつるは心苦しも ことわりにあはねど恋は恋として 聞くべき心の理ぞある 理の外を流るる恋雲は 神の力も及ばざりけり ウの声の生言霊に生れたる われには恋のかげだにもなし ただ恋ふるわれの心は瑞御霊 神業の光のみなりにける』

国中比古の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『玉の湖の汀の森にとこしへに います比女神の心いぢらし 玉野比女神もこの事聞かすならば またもや炎をもやし給はむ 神の代にかかる例はあらせじと 万代のためわれは祈るも 祈りても何の甲斐なき恋衣 破らむ術のなきぞ悲しき 栄えゆく豊葦原の国中に 恋なかりせば神代は栄えじ よき事に曲事いつき曲事に よき事いつく神代なるかも 鬼となり魔神となりて胸の火は 真鶴山を雲に包みし 恐しくまた優しきは恋すてふ 心の炎の燃ゆるなりけり』

産玉の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『瑞御霊神の心の苦しさを 思へばわれは悲しかりけり 玉野比女生代比女神の中にたち 心なやます岐美ぞ偲ばゆ ままならば岐美の悩みを救はむと 思へど及ばじ醜面われは』

圓屋比古の神は再び御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『国土造り神を生まさむ神業の 難きをつくづく今日悟りけり 顕津男の悲しき心今ぞ知る 国の柱と立たすが故に 凡神の身にしおはさば大らかに 恋を楽しみ給はむものを 主の神の言葉は重し生代比女 神の心は炎とわき立つ 燃ゆる火の中に立たせる思ひして なやみ給へる瑞御霊あはれ 真鶴の国津柱と立ち給ひ 間もなく雲に包まれ給ひぬ 村肝の心をつつむ恋雲を 生代の比女は晴らしまさずや 如何にしてこの難関を瑞御霊 のがれ給ふと煩ふわれは 生代比女燃ゆる胸の火消す術も なかりけらしな瑞御霊神も』

宇礼志穂の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『如何にして恋の縺毛とかばやと 思ふも瑞の御霊の御為め 生代比女神に叶へば玉野比女の 神の心をなやまし給はむ 上下のへだてを知らぬ恋なれば 心のつよき霊止の勝つらし 真鶴山黒雲ただに晴れぬれど まだ晴れやらぬ岐美の心よ われは今瑞の御霊の御胸を 思ひて涙止めあへずも 恋すてふ道は神代を限りとし 末の世までも残さじと祈る 祈るとも恋は詮術なかるらむ 心の糸の縺れし女神に』

魂機張の神は歌ひ給ふ。

『たまきはる生命をかけし恋衣 破らむ術もあらじと思ふ たまきはる生命惜しまぬ比女神の 恋の炎は鬼となりしか 恋ゆゑに生命捨てむと思ふこそ 心のいろのまことなるらし 瑞御霊雄々しき優しきものごしに 比女神の心いつきたりけむ 理の道をなみしてひた進む 恋の闇には木戸なかりけり ともかくも百神たちよ真鶴の 山の聖所に神言宣らばや』

この御歌に百神等は、心を清め身を浄め、恭しく神言を、天津高宮の方に向つて奏上し、七十五声の言霊を、繰り返し繰り返し、祈り給ふぞ久しけれ。
