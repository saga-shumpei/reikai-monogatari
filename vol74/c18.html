Chapter 18: Jewel Field の Forest [1886]

玉野の森〔1886〕

§ 1

Akitsuo-no-Kami, escorted by the Kamigami of the party, proceeds with his horse into the Sugado of Tamanomori.

Tamanomori, spanning ten Jifuri from east to west and twenty Jifuri from north to south, stands with ancient pines sealing the Ame, its ground covered with Hakushiya, and in the Kubodo here and there, it holds the water of Seisen, being a Kamimori that is pure by nature. The mansion of Tamanohime-no-Kami is built upon a Kodaka hill in the Chiuau of this forest, with Miyabashirafutoshikita, and Chigitakashi reaching toward Takaamahara, where she solemnly enshrines the Shinrei of the Lord Ōkami; and Tamanohime-no-Kami herself, serving as Saishiyu, devotes her entire Magokoro morning and evening, and her service is truly awe-inspiring.

Akitsuo-no-Kami, while stepping Hitoashifutaashi with the hooves of his horse into Tamanomori, stops his horse and chants a Miuta.

Looking out, to the limit of my sight, the Tokiwagi / Is a Sugado where the green of the pines flourishes / How rare it is in this Ame-kai where the Kuni-tsuchi is still young / That pines having passed a thousand years should grow so thick / On the paths I travel, the Tokiwagi / Pines flourish, a beautiful Kunihara / To serve the Kami-waza of creating the Kuni-tsuchi, I have now / Come to this Kamimori, finding it pure / In this forest where thousands of cranes nest / The green dyes the colors of a thousand generations / The ancient pines, embracing the wind blowing on the noji / Are a Sugado singing of the eternal Kamiyo / I knew not that such a pure and vast Kamimori / Existed in this place / The pines are pure, the earth is also pure, the water is pure / Being the Mahoyaba of the Manazuru-no-Kuni / Where does Tamanohime reside, for her figure / Is not even visible, and today feels lonely / Since I heard that Ikuyohime was to conceive a child / Has Tamanohime been hiding herself?

Tōmio-no-Kami chants a Miuta.

I now stop my horse and, seeing / This Kamimori of deep green, find it pure / In the shade of the Tokiwagi pines, whitely / I saw flowers blooming as the Lord Kami / Thinking of them as white plum blossoms born from the Mitama of the Lord Kami / They are truly precious / I know this forest where white plum blossoms bloom out of season / To be the Miya-i of the Lord Ōkami / Come then, I shall stand before you and accompany you / Chanting a Miuta while riding high on my horse.

Having chanted thus, Tōmio-no-Kami stands before the party and proceeds west, ever west, through the pure forest shade of Hakushiya and green pines, with the sound of his horse's hooves ringing bravely.

Enuyahiko-no-Kami chants a Miuta from horseback.

Crossing over the Manazuru-yama and Tamano-ko / I have arrived at the Sugado on this auspicious day / The sound of the horse's hooves treading on the masago is crisp / In the shade beneath the pines / The gentle breeze blowing on my face is also fragrant / With the white plum blossoms decorating between the trees / Where is the mansion where Tamanohime resides? / Will Kimi not come out to welcome us? / Through the forest shade whose end I know not, though I go and go / I feel a restless, endless longing / This Kamimori must have been born / As the Kunitsubashira that secures the Manazuru-no-Kuni.

Tatakumi-no-Kami chants a Miuta.

On the path where the Mizu-no-Mitama advances, there is no shadow / And even within the forest shade, the Amatsu-hi shines / Piercing through the treetops of the Tokiwagi pines / The light of the Amatsu-hi shines abundantly / To the right, left, front, and back, the Tokiwagi / Pine branches are pure and pregnant with the wind / Reflecting the blue of the Ōzora, the treetops / Of the pines in this forest are bluer still / In the sky where the blue of the pines overlaps with the blue of the heavens / White manazuru fly and cross / In the forest shade where white-colored masago is spread / I ride a white horse, feeling pure / How cheerful this Kamimori is, where plum blossoms bloom / Here and there in the forest, hundreds of birds sing / Is it the sound of a koto, or the sound of a flute, or the white birds / Whose singing voices are pure in the shade of Tamanomori / Though I go and go, only pines flourish in this forest / I wish the depth of it were the heart of the Kami.

Ureshio-no-Kami chants a Miuta.

The Hisakata-no-Ame is clear and the earth is purified / A Sugado where the Tokiwagi pines flourish / How auspicious it is that the still young Manazuru-no-Kuni / Should have such flourishing ancient pines / Thinking of the Mizu-no-Mitama who finished the Kami-waza of Kami-umi / And the preciousness of creating the Kuni-tsuchi again / Though I go and go, Tamanohime has not yet appeared / Where might her noble mansion be? / This forest must be the pivot of the Manazuru-no-Kuni / Created by the Lord Ōkami / Though I have come to this Sugado from afar / Following the Mizu-no-Mitama / If Tamanohime has a heart, quickly / Come out and welcome the arrival of Kimi.

原文（近代文語）

顕津男の神は、一行の神々に送られ、玉野の森の聖所に駒を進ませ給ふ。

東西十里、南北二十里に渉る玉野森は、老松天を封じて立ち並び、白砂を以て地上を覆はれ、あなたこなたの窪所には、清泉の水を湛へ、自ら清しき神森なり。玉野比女の神の館は、この森の中央の小高き丘の上に、宮柱太敷立て、高天原に千木多加知りて、主の大神の神霊を厳かに祀り給ひて、玉野比女の神自ら斎主となりて、朝な夕なを真心の限りを尽し、仕へ給ふぞ畏けれ。

顕津男の神は、玉野森に駒の蹄を一足二足踏み入れ乍ら、駒を止め御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『見渡せば目路の限は常磐樹の 松の緑の栄ゆる聖所よ 国土稚きこの天界に珍しも 千歳を経にし松繁るとは わがい行く道の先々常磐樹の 松は繁りて美し国原よ 国土生みの神業仕ふと吾は今 この神森を清しみ来にけり 幾千万の田鶴の巣ぐへるこの森の 緑に千代の色をそめたり 老松は野路吹く風を抱へつつ 神代とこしへを歌ふ聖所よ かくの如清しき広き神森の 此処にあるとは知らざりにけり 松清し地又清し水清し 真鶴国の真秀良場にして 玉野比女いづれに在すか御姿 さへも見えなく今日の淋しさ 生代比女御子孕ますと聞しより 玉野の比女はかくろひ坐ししか』

遠見男の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『吾は今駒を止めて緑濃き この神森を清しみ見るも 常磐樹の松の樹蔭に白々と 匂へる花を主の神と見つ 主の神の御霊ゆ生れし白梅の 花と思へば尊かりけり 時じくに白梅匂ふこの森は 主の大神の宮居とこそ知る いざさらば吾前に立ちて御供せむ 馬上ゆたかに御歌うたひつ』

斯く歌ひ給ひ、遠見男の神は一行の前に立ち、白砂青松の清しき森蔭を、駒の蹄の音勇しく、西へ西へと進ませ給ふ。

圓屋比古の神は、馬上より御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『真鶴山玉野湖のり越えて 今日の吉日に聖所に着けり 真砂踏む駒の蹄のさくさくと 音の清しも松の下蔭 わが面を吹くそよ風も香るなり 木の間を飾る白梅の花に 玉野比女います館はいづらなる 岐美の出でまし迎へまさずや 行けど行けど果しも知らぬ森蔭を 果なき思ひもどかしみける 真鶴の国を堅むる国津柱と 生れ出でにけむこれの神森は』

多々久美の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『瑞御霊進ます道に隈もなく 森かげ乍ら天津日は光る 常磐樹の松の梢を射し通し ゆたかにかがよふ天津日の光 右左前も後も常磐樹の 松ケ枝清しく風を孕めり 大空の蒼をうつしてこの森の 松の梢はますます青し 松の青御空の蒼と重なれる 空に飛び交ふ白き真鶴 白妙の真砂を敷ける森蔭を 吾は清しく白馬に跨る 梅匂ふこの神森のほがらかさ 森のあちこち百千鳥啼く 琴の音かはた笛の音か白鳥の 鳴く音清しき玉野森蔭 行けど行けど松のみ繁るこの森の 深きを神の心ともがな』

宇礼志穂の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『久方の天晴れ地は清まりて 常磐の松のしげる聖所よ まだ稚き真鶴の国かくの如 老いたる松の繁る目出度さ 神生みの神業終へましし瑞御霊 また国土生ます尊さを思ふ 行けど行けどまだ現れまさぬ玉野比女の 貴の館はいづらなるらむ この森は主の大神の造らしし 真鶴国の要なるらむ 吾は今瑞の御霊に従ひて はろばろこれの聖所に来つるも 玉野比女御心あらばいち早く 出で迎へませ岐美のお成りを』

§ 2

Mihashihiko no Kami chants a sacred song.

Achi kochini kiyoki mashimizu tataetaru / kono kamimori wa mizu no mitama ka / minasoko no masago mo kiyoku mienikeri / sumikiraitaru mizu no hikari ni / nigorinaki kono mashimizu wo fushiogami / nushi no ōkami no mikokoro sakatorinu / hikariyami yukikafu yo ni mo kaku no gotoku / kiyoshiki mono no chi ni egakarenu / nushi no kami no efude ni narishi tamanomori / midori no nagame koyonaku kiyoshi

Ubudama no Kami chants a sacred song.

Shirasuna ni matsu no kozue no komorebi wa / mizutama no gotoku utsurohikagayofu / shirasuna wa toshi furu mama ni akaramite / komoRebi shiroku niwa wo egakeru / shirakoma no ashi ni fumiyuku komorebi wo / ware wa osoremi susumi yukunari / amatsuhi no megumi wa matsu no kokage ni mo / kagayaki tamafu to omoeba koshika / kuniuimi no miwaza tsukaefu to odetamashishi / kimi norasu koma no ashi hayaki kamo / mizumitama noraseru koma no ashi hayami / haya misugata wa kakuremashinuru / kuniuimi no miwaza tasukemu ware ni shite / kaku okureshi wa mikami no kokoro ka / hayaku shite yoki koto mo ari osoku shite / yoki koto mo ari kami no manimani / tamanomori no masago wo fumite susumi yuku / koma no ashioto kiyoshiki sono nari / manazuru no kuni tokoshiwa ni hirakamu to / odetamasu kimi no ushirode ōgamoshi

Ōgashikaru kimi ni tsukaete ware wa ima / kore no seisho wo tadori susumu mo

Tamakiharu no Kami chants a sacred song.

Nushi no kami no ikukototama ya sakahite / kono kamimori wa umaremashinikemu / Tamanohime no tokowa ni mamorasu kamizono to / omoeba nani ka tsutsumashikunarinu / amatsuzora ni shikuritsutsu koma no ashi / shizuka ni susumamu kore no seisho wo / shunba mo kore no seisho wo osomite / ashibumitsutsu susumu osorasa / kiyorakeki masago ni nokoru hizumeto wa / mizu no mitama no kayoiji narikeri / ware wa ima koma no hizume wo tadoritsutsu / mizu no mitama no miato ofamu ka / sashikomoru kozue no shigemi tokorodokoro / tsuru no sugomoru kamizono kiyoshiki / kyūten ni takaku kikoyuru manazuru no / koe ni kunihara akewataruramu

Musubiai no Kami chants a sacred song.

Ten no mizuhi to chi no mizuhi to wo musubiawase / umaremashinikemu kore no kamikuni wa / tenchi no nakazora ni aru kokochi shite / tsuru no sugomoru matsukage wo yuku mo / Ikuyoihime no kamigoto mo shirakoma no / se ni kura torite shitagaimaseri / Ikuyoihime umi no waza tsukaemasu to / kore no seisho ni kitarasu ōgashisa

Ikuyoihime no Kami chants a song from upon her horse.

Ten to chi wo musubiawase no kami to masu / thou art one who comprehendest not my heart / my child-bearing self, especially Tamanohime / yearning for her, have come hither indeed / meeting the Kami Tamanohime, my heart / I shall lay bare in the mizuihi of sincerity / in this sacred grove where ever-green trees flourish / I shall dwell eternally as a goddess with thee / aiding the divine work of Tamanohime, forever / I think to guard this sacred grove / since the land of Manazuru is still young, to build the land / I must consult with the Kami and open the age / the divine child born by uniting mizuhi and mizuhi / is truly the pillar of the nation

Thus speaking, Ikuyoihime no Kami whips her horse, takes the lead before the retinue, and gallops away like gathering clouds and mist, so that in the twinkling of an eye even her retreating figure vanishes from sight. Watching the retreating figure of Ikuyoihime no Kami, Musubiai no Kami chants a sacred song.

Hosome yo aa kashikome yo Ikuyoihime no / kami no sugata no sugureru kamo / Ikuyoihime wa imukafu kami yo omokachikami yo / matsusakakete kakedashi tamafu / ware yori mo mae ni tatsubuki kami nagara / imamade ushi ni tsudukasete habeeri / ametsuchi no shijo wo tadashi ima yori wa / kuniuimi no waza ni tsukaefumatsuramu / Ikuyoihime no Kami is pregnant with a noble child / she is proven to be a deity whom I must serve / her retreating figure advancing in the vanguard / when I caught a glimpse of it, it was light / shining among the pines, with all haste / she must have pursued the wake of Kimi

Umashuma no Kami chants a sacred song.

Go though I may, knowing no end of this forest / upon the sands the hooves of the horse are troubled / crisp and clear sounds the hoofbeat of the horse / in the forest shade of troubled sands / hidden in the waters of Lake Tamano and turned into a dragon / Ikuyoihime is the triumphant deity / the mizu mitama, pacified by the achievements / of Ikuyoihime, triumphant deity / has realized the strength of the goddess in this world / the divine merits that only press forward / come now, let us whip the horse across the sandy plain / eagerly pressing forward to seek Kimi

『あちこちに清き真清水湛へたる この神森は瑞の御霊か 水底の真砂も清く見えにけり 澄みきらひたる水の光に 濁りなきこの真清水を伏し拝み 主の大神の御心悟りぬ 光闇行き交ふ世にもかくの如 清しきものの地に描かれぬ 主の神の絵筆になりし玉野森 緑のながめこよなく清し』

産玉の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『白砂に松の梢の樹漏陽は 水玉の如うつろひかがよふ 白砂は年ふるままにあからみて 樹漏陽白く庭を描けり 白駒の脚に踏みゆく樹漏陽を 吾はおそれみ進み行くなり 天津日の恵は松の樹蔭にも 輝き給ふと思へば畏し 国土生みの神業仕ふと出でましし 岐美乗らす駒の脚早きかも 瑞御霊乗らせる駒の脚早み はや御姿はかくれましぬる 国土生みの神業助けむ吾にして かく後れしは御神の心か 速くしてよき事もあり遅くして よき事もあり神のまにまに 玉野森の真砂を踏みて進み行く 駒の脚音清しき園なり 真鶴の国とこしへに拓かむと 出でます岐美の後姿雄々しも

結比合の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『天の水火と地の水火とを結び合せ 生れましにけむこれの神国は 天地の中空にある心地して 鶴の巣籠る松蔭を行くも 生代比女神の神言も白駒の 背に跨りて従ひませり 生代比女神生みの業仕へますと これの聖所に来らす雄々しさ』

§ 3

Thus having finished chanting the poem, applying a single whip and with the sound of hooves also valiant, they urge their horses forward in a headlong rush along the sandy path of the pine shadows. At length, Makotoizu-no-Kami chants an imperial poem.

The precious Kototama of the Kamigami, in minute detail, Even I have heard them in a pure heart; While joyfully proceeding through the shining midst Of the pure ponds filled with clear waters on the right and left, The Kototama of Yasu in the heavens sounding and resounding, Such a sacred place must have thus appeared. Even the land of Aragane which I gave birth to, thinking of it as the Mitama Of the Lord God, is filled with awe; Even the reverberating sound of the horse's hooves, crunching and crunching, Thinking of it as the voice of Kami, is filled with awe. Now for a while we must whip our horses and advance, As the Miaraka of Tamanohime draws near; Meeting with Tamanohime of the Mizumitama, At this opportune tide when we shall converse with her, Whether hurrying is good or not hurrying is good, Kamunagara, We must simply proceed according to the will of Kami.

Thus each and every one of them, richly upon horseback, while chanting their poems, advanced straight toward the wide-built gate of the deity Tamanohime upon a slightly elevated hill in the center of Tamanomori.

原文（近代文語）

斯く歌ひ終り、一鞭あて蹄の音も勇ましく、松蔭の真砂路を一目散に打たせ給ふ。いや果に、真言厳の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『神々の貴の言霊まつぶさに 吾は聞けるも澄める心に 右左清水たたへし清池の 光れる中を嬉しみ行くも 天なるやスの言霊の鳴り鳴りて かかる聖所は現れにけむ わが生める荒金の地も主の神の 御霊と思へば畏くぞある ざくざくと駒の蹄のひびかひも 神の御声と思へば畏し 今暫し駒に鞭うち進むべし 玉野の比女の御舎近めば 瑞御霊玉野の比女に見合ひまし 言問ひ給はむこの潮どきに 急ぐもよし急がずもよし惟神 神のまにまに進むべきのみ』

斯く各も各も馬上ゆたかに、御歌詠ませ乍ら、玉野森の中央なる小高き丘の上に、広く建てられし玉野比女の神の門前さして進み給ひぬ。
