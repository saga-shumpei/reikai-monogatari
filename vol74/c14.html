Chapter 14: Truth 心の曇らひ [1882]

真心の曇らひ〔1882〕

§ 1

In the universe of Mushi and Mushiu, the strongest and most beautiful thing is the activation of love. With the activation of love in the great emptiness, the Kototama of Su is born, and all the gods of Tenchi are born. Therefore, this is the reason why Kami is called love and power. Because there is love, Uchiu is created, and Banbutsu come into existence. Everything in Uchiu is swayed by this love, and creation, construction, destruction, ruin, and confusion all arise from it. Love is to be revered above all and is also to be feared. From love are born the Kototama of suku and sukanu; if the degree of the emotional movement of love is appropriate, the divine work of seiseikuwaiku is completed, and if the degree of the emotional movement of love exceeds the limit, it eventually leads to the destruction of Itsusai.

And in love, there is good, there is evil, there is greatness, and there is pettiness. The love of Kami is loving-goodness, while the love of Seken is loving-evil. The love of Kami is great love, whereas the love of Seken is petty love. To love oneself, to love one's home, to love one's locality, and to love one's country is what is called self-love, and compared to the great love of Kami, there is a difference as vast as that between clouds and mud. Therefore, petty love magnifies the mind of egoism and selfish desire, and eventually, for the sake of self-love, harms others, ruins other homes, contends with other localities, and fights with other countries, ultimately leading both sides to receive the baptism of misery. Moreover, since the love of Kami is great love, it is universal to all things in Uchiu without the slightest partiality. Because the love of Seken disregards others and single-mindedly loves oneself, loves one's home, loves one's locality, and loves one's state, when it sees that a disadvantage has been inflicted by others, it immediately rises in rebellion and contention, leading to the ruin of body, home, and state. A thing to be feared is the degree of the emotional movement of love.

Here, the personal love of Ikutayohime-no-kami piled up to become passion, and as passion grew ever more intense, it turned into resentment, with the flames of anger raging in her breast; the flames that burned her spirit rose up in billows, turning into black smoke that enveloped the heavens, and remaining unable to bear it, she transformed into a spirit and became a great serpent, and seeking to prevent the suffering of fever and heat, she finally hid at the bottom of Lake Tamano, which is indeed a terrifying state of affairs. Since all passion belongs to self-love, having no leisure to regard others, it eventually ruins oneself, injures people, poisons the world, and throws the world into disorder. Therefore, even by using the power of the living Kototama bursting forth from the pure, bright, and righteous august hearts of Akitsunoo-no-kami and other deities, there was no way to extinguish this fierce flame of passion. Even so, it could not resist the bright, pure, true Kototama arising from the mind of great love, and the fact that it finally had no choice but to surrender is because it is the inviolable and solemn divine majesty of Kami.

Here, Ikutayohime-no-kami had her heart calmed for a while by the august poem of the living Kototama filled with emotion by Akitsunoo-no-kami at the sacred ground of Mount Manazuru, but the fire of passionate emotion raged once more, black clouds overspread the heavens and hindered the progress of the August Spirit, and at last, awed by the solemn power of the deity Su, she abandoned Mount Manazuru, turned into a serpent body in the lake waters of Tamano close to the dwelling place where Tamano-hime-no-kami forever calms her spirit, and lurked deep at the bottom of the lake, waiting now impatiently as if it were too late for the August Spirit to come across, making the waters of the vast lake boil over with the fire in her breast, she waited to achieve her stubborn passion—how terrifying it was. The sky was blue and the sea was blue once more, the wind carried the scent of white plum blossoms, waves were calm and the light of the full moon floated clearly, serene and clear like a mirror, and the tranquil evening surface of the lake suddenly saw a gale blow up, heavy rain arrived impetuously, and waves surged so that it became impossible to easily cross—this was truly helpless. The moon that had shone purely and brightly until now suddenly hid behind black clouds, and the steam and smoke that enveloped all sides turned greyish-white, enveloping the vicinity of the group of deities and leading to a state where nothing could be done; this too was the wild fury of the flames of anger ignited from fierce passion. Therefore, one should know that the most approachable is Kami, and the most terrifying is the emotional movement of passion.

原文（近代文語）

無始無終の宇宙間に於て、最も強く美しきものは愛の発動なり。大虚空中に愛の発動ありて始めてスの言霊は生れ、天地の万神は生る。故に神は愛なり力なりと称する所以なり。愛あるが故に宇宙は創造され、万物は発生す。宇宙間一切のものはこの愛に左右され、創造も建設も破壊も滅亡も混乱も生ずるものなり。愛は最も尊むべくかつ恐るべきものとす。愛よりスク、スカヌの言霊は生るるなり、愛の情動にしてその度合よろしければ、生成化育の神業は完成し、愛の情動の度合過ぐれば、遂には一切を破壊するに至る。

而して、愛には善あり、悪あり、大あり、小あり。神の愛は愛善にして、世間一切の愛は愛悪なり。神の愛は大愛にして世間の愛は小愛なり。わが身を愛し、わが家を愛し、わが郷土を愛し、わが国土を愛するは所謂自己愛にして、神の大愛に比して雲泥の相違あり。故に小愛は我情我欲の心を増長せしめ、遂には自己愛のために他人を害し、他家を破り、他郷と争ひ、他の国と戦ひ、遂に彼我共に惨禍の洗礼を受くるに至る。又神の愛は大愛なれば、宇宙一切万有に普遍して毫も依怙の沙汰なし。世間の愛は他を顧みず、只管にわが身を愛し、わが家を愛し、わが郷土を愛し、わが国家を愛するが故に、他よりもし不利益を加へらるると見る時は、忽ち立つて反抗し争闘し、身を破り家を破り国家を破るに至る。恐るべきは愛の情動の度合なり。

茲に生代比女の神の個性的愛は積み重なりて恋となり、恋ますます募りて怨恨となり、胸に瞋恚の炎燃えさかり、其心魂を焼きし炎は濛々として立ち昇り、黒煙となりて天を包み、尚ほ堪へ切れぬままに霊魂化して大蛇となり、炎熱の苦しみを防がむとして、遂には玉野湖底にひそみたるこそ、実に恐しき次第なり。総て恋なるものは自己愛に属するが故に、他を顧みるの暇なく遂にはわが身を破り、人を損ひ世界を毒し天下を乱すに至るものなり。故に顕津男の神其他の神の清き明き正しき御心より迸る生言霊の力をもつてするも、猛烈なこの恋の炎を消しとむるに由なかりける。然りと雖も大愛の心より出でし明き清き真の言霊には反抗する能はず、遂には帰順せざるを得ざるに至るは、厳として犯すべからざる神の御稜威なればなり。

茲に生代比女の神は、真鶴山の聖場に於ける、顕津男の神の情のこもりし生言霊の御歌によりてしばし心を和め給ひしが、再び恋々の情火燃えさかり、黒雲天に漲りて瑞の御霊の進路を妨げ、遂にはスの神の厳かなる威力に畏服して真鶴山を捨て、玉野比女の神の永久に鎮まりたまふ宮居に近き玉野湖水に蛇身となりて湖底深く潜み、瑞の御霊の渡り来ませるを今や遅しと、さしもに広き湖水の水を、胸の火に沸きかへらせつ、恋の意地を達せむと待ちかまへ居たまひしぞ恐ろしき。空蒼く海又青く、風は白梅の香を送り、浪穏かに満月の光清く浮みて鏡の如く澄み切り、落着きたる夕の湖面は忽ち暴風吹き起り、大雨沛然として臻り、浪逆巻きて容易に越ゆべからざるに至らしめたるぞ是非なけれ。今迄清皎々と輝きたる月は忽ち黒雲にかくれ、四辺をつつみし湯気煙は、灰白色となりて、神々の一行の辺りをつつみ、如何ともなす由なきに至らしめたるも、猛烈なる恋より燃え出でたる瞋恚の炎の荒びなりける。故に最も親しむべきは神にして、最も恐るべきは恋の情動なりと知るべし。

§ 2

Aa, Kamunagara chihahe mase.

Akitsuono Kami, suddenly seeing the scenery upon the lake transform completely, with darkness surrounding all sides and an unpleasant air enfolding the surroundings, chanted a sacred miuta to purify this gloomy heaven and earth through the divine majesty of the Ikukototama.

'How pitiful that heaven and earth are blocked at once / these black clouds are the flames of love / by the divine words of the Ōkami, the Lord of great love / do not dream of causing hardship to me who builds the land / how strange are the warped gods of love that becloud / such a beautiful Shibitenkai / truly I am the god serving in the divine work / of producing lands and producing deities, the god of great love / though I think the heart of Ikukohime is dear / there is no reason to violate the divine commission / make heaven and earth bright as quickly as a single moment / realizing the heart of my great love / though the moonlight of the distant sky shines clear / there is no way to shoot through this ugly cloud / Ikukohime, peacefully and tranquil / make her realize the heart of my great love / did I think that every time I produced the Kunitamagami / I would be hindered by the ugly warped gods / in the heaven where even the utmost goodness and beauty knows no end / cast away your narrow heart, O Hohime-gami / while causing your soul to shine in the love of the Lord God / cause your disordered thoughts to be removed.'

As He chanted such a miuta, Ikukohimegami, appearing blankly from the darkness like a phantom, manifested a ferocious countenance before the group, her expression of resentment intensely fierce,

'Resentful is the heart of Kimi, and his kototama / suffering, I have turned into a great serpent / Kimi has caused the burning chest of a pure maiden / to give birth to a great serpent / having now ended up in such a form / I resent Kimi all the more / sinking eternally and firmly at the bottom of the water / I think to avenge the enemy of love / if the goddess and god cross this lake plain / I will become a great serpent and swallow them / how resentful is Kimi for matching with Tamanohime / I shall cause him suffering to the utmost of my strength / however Ikukototama proclaims it, because of love / there can be no way to console me who has grown disordered / my thoughts will become black clouds to close the heavens / and becoming a great serpent, will disturb the land / had I not the heart called love, to this extent / would I not have thought Kimi hateful / because of Kimi I suffer, because of Kimi / I have burned and become a great serpent / with the accumulation of sweetness, the flames of hatred / burning on, I have become a great serpent / does he not know that even the guardian deity of beautiful Mt. Manazuru / has become a great serpent because of Kimi / my thoughts, unto the end of countless kalpas / shall become the demon of love and cast curses / consider that the fearful thing is the path of love / I shall cause hostility to the land created by Kimi / my thoughts congealing and hardening, lurking in mountains, in seas / in rivers and in swamps, I shall suffer.'

Akitsuono Kami chanted a miuta.

'The words of truth with which I earnestly preach and teach / do you not listen, O kimi, do you not assent / born and bred in the solemn Shibitenkai / how poignant that you have become a great serpent / I assent to the sincerity of the heart called love / yet this is a journey of producing deities where my will cannot prevail / it is I who fear lest meeting with an alien woman / should leave eternal sins and defilements in the world / even the severe light of the kototama is enveloped / how hot indeed are the flames of love / washing away everything in the waters of the lake / be revived through my kototama / Aouei, wash the flames of the hot heart / in this pure clear water and cause them to live / though there is nothing I fear in heaven and earth / I have been troubled by the flames of love.'

Ikukohimegami chanted softly.

'Cherishing Kimi as my cousin, I at last / have ended up becoming a god of hatred / too overflowing in my breast is the fondness, and the depth / of the hatred, alas for me who has grown so deep / resenting Kimi who has no path to be resented / I have become the soul of a great serpent / because of Kimi I am revived, because of Kimi / do you not know that my soul-line will not perish / even if my soul should perish, this thought / will be transmitted ever successively and never cease.'

Akitsuono Kami:

'Knowing that I go against the divine word of the Lord God / yet I think to save you, my beloved kimi / determining in my heart that whatever sins I sink into / shall truly recompense the sincerity of Hohime-gami / be at ease in your deep heart, from now on / I will assent to your true heart.'

As He thus chanted, the whole sky suddenly cleared up, and the raging lake plain likewise suddenly calmed down like a mirror, the light of the full moon shining brightly, and not only was the wide lake surface exceptionally clear, but it illuminated the distant, far-reaching plains of the country without leaving a single corner unlit.

原文（近代文語）

嗚呼惟神霊幸倍坐世。

顕津男の神は、忽ち湖上の光景一変して、四辺暗黒となり、不快なる空気の身辺を包みたれば、生言霊の御稜威によりてこの暗憺たる天地を清めむと、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『あさましも天地一度にふさぎたる この黒雲は恋の炎よ 大愛の主の大神の神言もて 国土造る我を艱ますな夢 美しき紫微天界をかくのごと 曇らす恋の曲神怪しも 我こそは国土生み神生みの神業に 仕ふる神ぞ大愛の神 生代比女の心愛しと思へども 神の依さしに反くよしなき 片時もはやく天地を明しませ わが大愛の心さとりて 久方の月の光は冴ゆれども この醜雲を射通す術なき 生代比女心平に安らかに わが大愛の心を悟らせ 思ひきや国魂神を生む度に 醜の曲神にさやらるるとは 至善至美果しも知らぬ天界に 狭き心を捨てよ比女神

主の神の愛に魂を光らしつつ 乱れたる思ひをのぞかせ給へ』

斯く御歌詠ませ給ふや、闇の中より茫然と夢幻の如く現れたる生代比女の神は、獰猛なる面を一行の前に現し、恨みの形相凄じく、

『恨めしき岐美の心よ言霊よ 吾はなやみて大蛇となりぬる 清かりし乙女の胸をこがしたる 岐美は大蛇を生みましにけり 吾は今かかる姿となり果てて ますます岐美を恨みこそすれ 水底に常磐堅磐に沈み居て 恋の仇をば報いむと思ふ 女神男神この湖原を渡りなば 吾は大蛇となりて呑むべし 玉野比女に見合す岐美の恨めしさ 力限りになやましまつらむ 生言霊如何に宣らすも恋故に 乱れし吾をまつらふ術なけむ わが思ひ黒雲となりて天を閉ぢ 大蛇となりて地を乱さむ 恋すてふ心なければかくまでも 岐美を憎しと思はざりけむ 岐美故に吾はなやめり岐美故に 吾は焦れて大蛇となりける

めぐしさの重り合ひて憎しみの 炎燃えつつ大蛇となりける 美しき真鶴山の守り神も 岐美故大蛇となりしを知らずや わが思ひ幾億万劫の末までも 恋の悪魔となりて祟らむ 恐るべきものは恋路と思召せ 岐美がつくりし国土に仇せむを わが思ひ凝りかたまりて山に海に 河又沼に潜みてなやめむ』

顕津男の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『ねもごろにわが説きさとす言の葉を 公は聞かずや諾ひまさずや 厳かなる紫微天界に生れ生でで 大蛇となりし公ぞいぢらし 恋すてふ心の誠は諾へど わが儘ならぬ神生みの旅よ あだし女に見合ひて永久の罪穢 世に残さむを恐るる我なり 言霊の厳の光もつつむなる 恋の炎のあつくもあるかな 何事も湖水の水に流しまして わが言霊によみがへりませよ アオウエイあつき心の炎をば この真清水にあらひて生かせよ 天地に恐るるものは吾なけど 恋の炎に艱まされける』

生代比女の神は微に歌ふ。

『いとこやの岐美をめぐしみ吾遂に 憎の神となり果てにける いとしさの胸にあまりて憎しみの 深くなりぬる吾は悲しも 恨むべき道なき岐美を恨みまつり 吾は大蛇の霊魂となりぬる 岐美故に吾よみがへり岐美故に わが魂線の亡ぶと知らずや わが魂はよし亡ぶともこの思ひ いや次々に伝へて止まじ』

顕津男の神、

『主の神の神言に背くと知りながら いとしの公を助けむと思ふ 如何ならむ罪に沈むも比女神の 誠にむくゆと心定めし 村肝の心やすかれ今よりは なが真心を諾ひまつるも』

斯く歌ひ給ふや一天忽ち晴れ渡り、荒れ狂ふ湖原も俄に鏡の如くをさまりて、満月の光皎々として、さしもに広き湖面は更なり、目路遠き国原を隈なく照らし給ひける。
