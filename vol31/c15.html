Murmuring in a small voice, she twittered.

Kuniyoriwake spoke in a woman's voice,

Kuniyori: 'Erina-sama, how ever shall I cross this single-span bridge? My legs are trembling.'

Yasuhiko: 'Jōsama, do not worry. Erina is right here with you. Once you cross this very log bridge, you will surely meet Akiyama-sama, whom you so deeply yearn for from the bottom of your heart. How could I ever return as I am without meeting Morris-sama, whom I fell in love with at first glance, and pouring out the very depths of my heart? That fine man is dawdling around, and it would be a terrible disaster if he were snatched up by other women, you know.'

Kuniyori: 'Is that so? If that is the case, shall we cross stealthily? Somehow this bridge is rickety and scary.'

Yasuhiko: 'Do not worry. If a wicked person like Kuniyoriwake were to cross, the gods might pull the ropes and drop her down, but since a woman is going in pursuit of the man she loves, even a log bridge will not be dropped. I know for sure that Akiyamawake-sama and Morris-sama will protect us so that we are not dropped, even out of the sincerity of their hearts.'

Kuniyori: 'If I cross this bridge, I can meet Akiyama-sama... I am so happy.'

Yasuhiko: 'That is only to be expected. After all, I am pining this much even for that dark-skinned Morris-sama. It is only natural that you should be infatuated with such a splendid man as Akiyama-sama, ho ho ho ho ho.'

Kuniyori: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, oh how embarrassing! I say, Erina-sama, please make sure not to speak of this any further. Now let us hurry along.'

So saying, Kuniyoriwake's party safely crossed the bridge, hid themselves in the thicket of trees along the valley path, whipped their shank's mare, and galloped off some four or five chō.

'Ah ha ha ha, that went quite smoothly. They evidently still take us for women, don't they? Shall we shave the bark of this tree and leave some kind of mark, both of you?'

Yasuhiko said, 'Yes, certainly,' took out a tantau, shaved a little of the bark of a young zelkova tree, picked up some ubatama berries growing nearby in her hand, and with their juice wrote:

'Beniihime and Erina, both of us, have passed through here. By any chance, should Akiyama-sama and Morris-sama arrive here after us, please do chase after us. And if you would please drop the bridge so that Kuniyoriwake cannot come across, we shall feel even more at ease. ... From the two of us ... to the delicate woman.'

'Ah ha, this is interesting! Shall I make Kuniyoriwake peel tree after peeled tree in advance, leaving marks everywhere, and escort her no matter how far she goes? If we do that, she will surely reform herself before she knows it. Ah ha ha ha.'

Aki and Mori hastily scrambled up from the valley floor to the opposite side of the bridge,

'Oi, Morris, that was definitely the voices of Beniihime and Erina, but yet they crossed away smoothly without a single hitch, isn't that somehow devoid of anything unusual? The cuckoo's voice is heard but its shape is not seen, the Konjin of Aki-Mori has been guarding you from the shadows. When they realize this, even Beniihime and Erina will go "ahun" and reform themselves... Have they guarded us even unto that extent? This is the Great Bridge of the World, and unless you cross this place you cannot meet the true man. Set apart to the west and the east, the gods provide their protection, the gods do not tell lies—thus, mimicking the Oudesaki of Omoto, the Princess surely praises the divine virtue of the Konjin of Aki-Mori and rejoices over it, is it not so? That voice we just heard could not have traveled so far on a woman's feet. Now let us whip our shank's mare and press forward. There can hardly be any risk of mistaking the single path in the valley,

My lord shall not yet go afar, until the tears of my sleeves have dried away.

Isn't there a poem that goes like that? Surely she will not go far away. Come, let's run, run!

Stamping their feet with otchini otchini, they climbed up a narrow mountain path where they could scarcely put their feet. They peeled the bark of a zelkova sapling about three paces around, and something was marked on it. Looking closely, as expected, Kurenaihime and Erina, the two of them, had passed through here... Seeing the wording which meant: 'Please, if you two are still behind, look at this mark and follow after us...' the two leaped up for joy,

'Oh, thank goodness, thank goodness! This is why, no matter where it takes, if we do not carry it through, the barrier of love cannot be crossed, they say. Come, we cannot be dawdling; let's go quickly! It's one final push. If we do so, we are bound to catch up with them for sure!'

And off they darted running again, such a comical sight.

Kuniyoriwake's party, convinced that the two were bound to chase right after them, oiled the compass, poured a flood of water into their throat-whistles, safely passed through the Panama Canal, and ran onward with all their might. Finding a dense, bushy large yamamomo tree, the three of them scrambled up like monkeys, sat cross-legged high in the branches, and were plucking and eating the yamamomo fruit that looked as if moisture were dripping from them. The leaves and branches grew so thickly that it was utterly impossible to see them from below.

Aki and Mori, their throats parched,

'Hey, Morris, coming along at this much of a rapid pace, there's no way we shouldn't have overtaken them. My throat-whistle has already started to sound. I cannot walk even one more shaku. Fortunately, isn't there that delicious-looking yamamomo hanging right over there? Shall we pick some yamamomo here and moisten our throats?'

'That's true, Akiyamawake, it looks exceedingly sweet. Well, let's take a leisurely break and go on. Even the princess may not have known she was resting in the nearby woods when she came this far, perhaps. If not that, since we came all the way with such severe hardship and great speed that we are out of breath, there is no way we wouldn't catch up to her. Well, shall we wait leisurely here? Surely, in the meantime, she is bound to grace us with her presence. It was certainly written just like that by a woman's hand, and that voice of hers when she honored us by crossing the bridge—even now, when I recall it, my mouth waters as if to drool. Ah, she truly is a lovely princess! At any rate, until she passes through here, let's rest beneath this tree. If we climbed up, there would probably be quite sweet ones, but they say that the biggest fools are those who climb trees, those who ride airplanes, and those who walk under eaves on a broad street and hit their heads on shop signs; so well, let's refrain from tree-climbing and make do by picking the delicious-looking yamamomo hanging low down.'

So saying, they plucked the semi-ripened yamamomo from the lower branches, chewed them noisily to moisten their throats, filled their bellies, and waited with outstretched necks, wondering when they would finally appear.

The three up in the tree exchanged glances with one another, pressed their hands over their mouths, signaled to one another not to speak, and impatiently waited for the two to depart.

『さうだなア、秋山別、随分甘さうだ。マアゆつくり一服して往くことにせう。姫さまだつて足を痛め、傍の森の中で休みて御座つたのを知らずに此処まで来たのかも知れないよ。さうでなくては、俺達が息が切れる程始終苦節の大速力大急行でやつて来たのに、追つつかぬ筈がない。マアゆつくりと此処で待つことにせうかい。キツと其間にはお出遊ばすに定まつてゐるワ。確に女の手であの通り書いてあつたなり、橋をお渡り遊ばす時のあの声つたら、今思ひ出しても涎が落つるやうだ。あゝ本当に可愛いお姫様だなア。兎も角ここへお通りになる迄、此木の下で休むことにせう。登つて見たら、ズイ分甘いのがあるだらうけれど、木登りする奴と、飛行機に乗る奴と広い街道を軒下歩いて看板で頭打つ奴程馬鹿はないと云ふから、マア木登りは止めにして、低く垂れ下つた甘さうな山桃をとつて辛抱せうかい』

と云ひ乍ら、低き枝の半熟の山桃をむしり、ムシヤムシヤと喉をしめし、腹をふくらせ、今か今かと首を長うして待つて居る。

三人は樹上にて互に顔を見合せ、口を押へ、物を言ふなとの合図をし乍ら、二人の立ち去るのを、もどかしげに待ち居たりける。
