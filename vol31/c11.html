Chapter 11: 売言買辞 [877]

売言買辞〔877〕

§ 1

Anan had Riyōnin and Nairu lead the way, and coming before Kuniyoriwake and two others who sat down leisurely at the entrance of the cave, suddenly touched his hands to the ground and said,

Anan: 'Well, well, O Sendenshi-dono of Kuniyoriwake, how very kind of you to have come to visit us in such a place as this, and everyone inside the cave feels exceedingly overjoyed and filled with rapturous awe. In regard to this, there may be some blame for past discourtesies, but just as the teachings of Ananai-kyō direct, please overlook and listen anew to everything with Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi, rectify and restate the faults of us all, manifest your boundless heart of mercy and benevolence, and by means of your great heart, we pray to the gods that you will take generous measures in all things, which we reverently beseech the gods to grant. Ah, Kamuna garagara tama sakibae mase.'

Thus Anan bowed to Kuniyoriwake recklessly and completely, and so as to leave him not a single word of complaint, spread barbed wire from the front and the rear—truly a cunning and crafty villain.

Kuniyoriwake: 'Well, well, you who are called Anan-sama, some time ago you made a most splendid struggle on the banks of the log bridge, and truly at your divine strategies and demon-like ploys Kuniyoriwake is struck dumb with wonder. The ultimate secret art of military strategy is, among thirty-six stratagems, to consider flight as the first, they say; and this world does not become governed because people always think of winning and winning. Just like you, to yield victory to a minority enemy, and without the least shame throw your ranks into disorder and flee—at such bravery of yours, we too must follow suit. As they say about winning by losing, rather than a fixed-price shop without bargaining, a shop that boldly shouts out high asking prices and then heavily discounts for the customer prospers much better, so no doubt the Ural religion also discounted very well for people. Therefore your business must be enjoying a prosperous trade with countless customers coming in flocks. Ah no, Kuniyoriwake cannot even approach you. Please give us a nice, clean discount this time too, so that your shop will continue to prosper just as before. If you say you will not discount because your abacus does not balance on your side, there is nothing to be done. I shall become Urashikohime's [Urashiko no Mikoto] husband and defeat you. Even if it is a bit urushi—no, annoying—since that is precisely where one looks anew, listens anew, and restates everything with Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi, ha ha ha ha!'

Anan: 'Yes, yes, even before receiving any orders for business, since I am taking care of you by offering discounts and more discounts, I humbly beg for your continued patronage for ages and ages.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'On this occasion, the reason we have come is that we have an article we would like you to sell us, and we have taken the trouble to come all the way to this store as new patrons. Won't you please let us have it cheap with a discount?'

Anan: 'What on earth is the article you have ordered? Is it an animal, a plant, a utensil, or perhaps fish or shellfish? Depending upon your order, if only we have it, we will gladly give it to you for free. However, you must excuse us for things we do not have.'

Anan: 'Those who have come to purchase from us are neither animals nor plants. It is a suridashi, a kigisu, and a sumika, you see.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'Heh, this is a strange order indeed. Kigisu and the like do not reside in this hall. We have neither striking-fire nor houses to sell, as we are unfortunately out of stock, so please look elsewhere. Heh, thank you as always, and we hope for your continued patronage...'

Anan: 'You say "thank you as always," but didn't I come to place an order for the very first time today?'

Anan bent his lower back deeply and rubbed his hands together, saying,

Anan: 'Heh, this is a custom of trade, and even for a first-time customer, it is arranged so that we say... "for your continued patronage." Please do step right into the back, eat a hearty meal of Kyoto-style ochazuke, and then be on your way back.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'What we ordered a moment ago, this kigisu, means the pheasant fellow of Ananai-kyō. And what we called a suridashi means the Machi fellow. What we called a sumika means the letter S. Even if you keep them locked away inside the cave forever, it won't yield much profit. In these times of new fashions, if they become stale goods their sales will drop, so I think it is to the shop's advantage to sell them while there are buyers, don't you see?'

アナンは切りに腰を屈め、揉手をし乍ら、

': 'As this matter must be heard from the opinion of Oyakata, it cannot become the freedom of the bantō, so please wait a moment. As he tried to go forth unreasonably, Anan spread wide his great hands and said,

': 'Now, now, that is altogether unreasonable; if you wait a while, permission from the Kyōshiyu will be issued, so until then, I will not say too long. I ask you to wait a while.'

Kuniyoriwake said,

': 'Nay, there must be not a moment's delay. Do not get in the way.'

As he stepped forward, Anan once again spread his great hands, and saying 'Wait, wait,' while retreating, blocked the way, and accidentally he himself fell headlong into the pitfall where Kiji and Machi had sunk.

': 'Ha, that I should fall into the pitfall I myself made—truly, what is called divine punishment is a terrifying thing, my! Yet I cannot be off guard for a moment. ...... Hongaihime-san, Erina-san, you must not walk outside the footprints where Kuniyoriwake walked. Because it is a terribly dangerous zone......'

Saying thus, when he peered down into the pitfall from above, both men, Kiji and Machi, at the bottom of the hole seized the now-fallen Anan and said,

': 'Now, Anan, since you too have fallen in here, there is no longer any help for it. It will be well if you petition the general to rescue us as quickly as possible; if you dawdle, we will pull off your raw head. Now it is all right; whatever is dropped from above, it will be fine if you catch it with your body—a good thing has come floating down. If by any chance our bellies become empty, we will eat your flesh; no matter what is said, you are already ours now. Aha-ha-ha-ha!'

As they laughed and looked up at the sky, the very thing that suddenly caught their eye was the propaganda envoy of Kuniyoriwake. Kiji-kō unthinkingly said,

': 'Ha, Envoy of Propaganda-sama, how good of you to come!'

': 'Thinking today is the day and tomorrow is the day, Machi-kō is like the hototogisu of Machikaneyama; today is the auspicious day we have awaited and awaited! Ah, how wondrous, ah, how venerable, ah, how joyous, my! Into the hole Anan has fallen down once more. Ho, Envoy-sama, if only you have come, it is already equal to a thousand men. Please do hang down a ladder. If there is no ladder, if you let down a thick rope, we will cling to it and go up.'

': 'Your long retirement—you must have accomplished spiritual discipline indeed. Truly, I envy Kuniyoriwake, my!'

': 'If one interprets everything in a good spirit, even a pitfall is not felt to be particularly painful. Truly, thanks to the divine grace of the Ōkami, Kiji-kō has also been permitted to polish his soul splendidly.'

': 'If it is that splendid, why do both of you not spend a little longer right there and perform thorough spiritual training?'

': 'Nay, now with this we will be allowed to take a brief rest, and since we shall resume our austere practices anew, please hoist us up at the very earliest moment. However, this Anan-dono has just entered now, so it would be a pity to raise him. At least for four or five years, until he is able to perform practical discipline, let us make him undergo fasting discipline at the bottom of this well....... Now, Anan, that would be splendid, wouldn't it? Pinch your own body and know the pain of others—there is such a saying. We too have long been favored with the care of a splendid, deep, cold pitfall, and this grace we cannot forget, my! What you desire, do that unto others; what you do not desire, do not do unto others—saying thus, we have taken a very great liking to the bottom of this pitfall, so shall we distribute it to Anan-sama as well, without monopolizing the divine virtue?'

'I have truly done an inexcusable thing. Please forget everything up until now as if it were a fleeting dream, and beg Kuniyoriwake-sama to have this Anan pulled up together with you, won't you?'

'Ah, I shall ask that for you. However, pheasant-kun cannot guarantee when he will pull you up. For a human, the instantaneous mind is important. Well, you had better keep your mind calm and settled. Without a great magnitude of mind, immovable as mountains, like taizenjijiyaku, you could hardly fulfill the duties of an executive in the Ural Teaching, ahahahaha.'

While they were thus speaking, Kuniyoriwake searched out a rope ladder and hung it down with a flutter, and pheasant-kun climbed up the rope ladder like a monkey first of all. Next, machi-kun came up. This time Anan eagerly placed his hands on the rope ladder, and just as he had climbed up two or three steps, pheasant-kun abruptly severed the knot of the rope ladder with a snap. Anan fell back down to the bottom of the well with a thud, landing heavily on his buttocks,

'Save me, save me!'

he was crying out. Pheasant-kun from above said,

'It is not that I will not save you, but for that there is one order. Will you comply with that order or not?'

'Yes, yes, I will comply with any orders whatsoever. Earlier too, I promised Kuniyoriwake-sama that I would make an incomparable, smashing clearance sale. Since I will discount them heavily, please make your orders to your heart's content. In exchange, you will pull me up from the well, won't you?'

'Is there such a thing as raising the goods you are discounting? Regarding that, the detail of the order is: where is the whereabouts of S? Confess that clearly. Without doing so, pheasant-kun cannot save you either.'

'S-sama, you ask? As for that, I do not know. Please ask the general of Buur. Since the general keeps it a secret, he does not permit our knowing it.'

Kuniyoriwake, with an urgent tone, said,

'Pheasant-sama, Machi-sama, now let us proceed cautiously toward the inner chambers from here. ... Anan-sama, please practice your training there for a while. We will surely rescue you, but yet, as soon as the whereabouts of S is known we will save you, so until then please bear with it there. If there is anything you need, whatever it may be, please do not hesitate to tell us. Whether it is stone dumplings, sand rice balls, or roundworm somen noodles, according to your order, we will exert ourselves and supply them to you cheaply, ahahahaha.'

Laughing thus, pointing toward the leader's living room, the party of three sons and two daughters, watching their step, proceeded onward.
