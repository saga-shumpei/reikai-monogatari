Chapter 23: 化老爺 [889]

化老爺〔889〕

§ 1

Akiyamawake was blown about by the fierce wind, separated from Morris whom he relied upon as his strength, and having no other choice, alone, heading toward Mount Biyōbuhata in the Biyōbusanmiyaku range as his mark, he let the day end once again, approached the shade where old trees grew thick, laid his body down, and resolved to await the dawning of the night here.

The starlight of the full heaven was brilliantly casting a golden glow, the azure sky somehow felt exhilarating, and just as he was looking up at the heavens and praising the divine virtue, suddenly a great sound like mountains collapsing could be heard coming, the entire sky instantly clouded over, and large raindrops began to fall patter-patter.

Akiyamawake, having no choice, approached the shade of the old trees to avoid the rain, and at that very moment, a strangely white-haired, gigantic monster suddenly appeared, bulging out mirror-like giant eyes, with a high nose, a large mouth, and a copper-like physiognomy, and glared fixedly at Akiyamawake. Akiyamawake pressed his roaring chest, settled the deity in his lower abdomen (saikatanden), and glared back at the monster's face without even blinking. The monster also did not budge an inch, standing upright like a tree grown from the ground, changing its facial color time and again to red, blue, white, black, yellow, and purple, thrusting its long-clawed, furry giant arms straight forward, and now showing the momentum to grab Akiyamawake in one clutch and throw him away, while Akiyamawake within his heart was offering prayers: KuninotachiratSU no Ōkami, Toyokunihime no Ōkami, Kuniōtachi no Ōkami, Hinode no Kami, Konohanahime no Kami, Kan-satyō no Ōkami, protect me and bless me... but the monster opened its great mouth and burst out laughing with a giant voice like thunder.

Atsuha hahaha, missionary of Akiyamawake, listen well! I am indeed the incarnation of Yamata no Orochi who permanently resides in the forests of the Amazon River.

I-i-i-i, no matter how valiant you may be, you hateful fellows who shoot Kototama rays toward our clan and try to desecrate our dwelling of many years,

U-u-u-u, if you carelessly head toward the forest, I shall sever your body into pieces using my divine and wondrous arts. Should you by any chance reform and turn back from here, I shall forgive your sins and spare your life. What is your answer?

Thus it bellowed, while Akiyamawake solidified his unyielding conviction,

E-e-e-e, what a bother, I am the child of the teaching of KuninotachiratSU no Ōkami, the divine administrator of Izuzu, and not a spineless warrior who would use the words of crooked deities like you and come slinking back.

O-o-o-o, even if you put on such a terrifying expression and try to intimidate a missionary of the living deity, it will be utterly useless in the end. Quickly hide your figure.

Yai Akiyamawake, listen well.

Ka-ka-ka-ka, as for the gods, if Kuninotonokotonomikoto is a god, Yamata no Orochi is also a god. In the point of being called a god, where is the difference? This side is the original deity who protects the Ural teachings, and is one who appeared from the spirits of Adam and Eve, famed as the ancestors of the world. So to speak, we are the ancestral deities of you people. By what reason is it that a child stands up against its parent?

Ki-ki-ki-ki, what kind of nonsense is the Kimon no Konjin? If Yamata no Orochi is an evil deity, is not the character for demon (oni) in Kimon also a demon?

Ku-ku-ku-ku, rather than uttering useless things, if you quickly meet your end, will you not receive divine virtue without suffering?

Ke-ke-ke-ke, how could the pattern of the divine realm, where no bearings can be taken, be understood by the status of a human being?

Ko-ko-ko-ko, if the deity has explained things this muchly, even someone as stubborn as you must have understood by now.

Sa-sa-sa-sa, will you neatly change your mind up to now and submit to the deity of the serpent?

Shi-shi-shi-shi, if you remain stubborn, you will no longer have any recourse.

Su-su-su-su, you spineless cowards who make distinctions with the deity as to whether you like it or dislike it, and wander about the world,

Se-se-se-se, without thinking of petty things, discern the truth of heaven and earth as one, and quickly submit to this side,

So-so-so-so, that is the safest path for you.

と呶鳴り立てけるを、秋山別は不退転の信念を固め、

『エヽヽー面倒な、某は国治立大神の教の御子、三五教の神司、汝が如き曲神の言を用ゐて、のめのめ引返す様な腰抜武士ではないぞ。

『ヤイ秋山別、良つく聴け。

シヽヽしぶとう致して居ると最早量見はならぬぞ、

Ta-ta-ta... Calling yourself a Kamuzukasa of the Nishiki-no-miya in the Seichi of Takaamahara, or a Buka of Susanōnomikoto who dwells in the Mansion of Iso,

Chi-chi-chi... Setting up small barriers, you have wandered into a small hole of your own making,

Tsu-tsu-tsu... Praising only the Lord Moon Ōkami,

Te-te-te... Placing the Lord Sun Ōkami who shines in the Ama in second place,

To-to-to... Flashing the Toeu Shinmon, and wandering about the Sekai with a Sendenshizura; what nonsense! Quickly gather under my Sanka and Hōshi in my Shingefu, or else your Shintai shall be like a Fūzen-no-tomoshibi. Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha!'

Thus a loud laugh echoed in the Atari. In the Anya of this Shinzan, Akiyamawake encountered such a Kuwaibutsu; his heart throbbed, and he became so terrified that his hair stood on end. However, determined to the last not to overturn the Shinkau he had once decided upon, he concentrated his breath in his Saikatanden and managed to recite the Kazūta of Ama, 'Hi-fu-mi-yo-itsu-mu-nana-ya-koko-to-momo-chi-yorozu.' As he did so, the face of the Kuwaibutsu gradually softened and began to wear a slight smile. Akiyamawake rejoiced in his heart that his Kototama had been extremely effective, and facing the Kuwaibutsu, he spoke.

Akiyamawake: 'Hey, old Oji-sama, you certainly tried to intimidate us with great force, didn't you? There is not a single person who would be afraid of such things. What I call scary is not such a ghost or anything like that.'

Kuwaibutsu: 'Wa-ha-ha-ha-ha! If that is so, what do you think is the most frightening thing? Am I, this old man, not scary?'

Akiyamawake: 'Hmph, what is there to be afraid of? You come out with such a grand face when night falls, but you are a mere thing that vanishes as soon as the Sun-sama appears; how could one live in this world being afraid of that? Look here, I am the Sendenshi Akiyamawake, who has crossed the Sanzu River, gone to the Searing Heat Hell, explored the very bottom of Hell, and undergone practical training. Do you think I could be so scared of the likes of you?'

Kuwaibutsu: 'Then, what did you mean when you said there is one thing you fear? The only thing I fear is the single character for Sincerity.'

The Kuwaibutsu accidentally let these words slip, and hearing this, Akiyamawake thought,

Akiyamawake: 'Ha! This fellow said he is afraid of Sincerity. He is likely terrified of Kototama. All right, if he starts babbling from now on, I shall fire off a volley of Kototama.'

He decided this within his heart.

Akiyamawake: 'Hey, old man, I've got you now. You said you fear Sincerity most of all. I am about to fire the Kototama of the pure Yamato-damashii of Sincerity, so be prepared.

Na-na-na... No matter what you say, this Kototama comes from my mouth, so you cannot stop it with your power. If you do not repent quickly,

Ni-ni-ni... I, Akiyamawake, shall seal you right here so that you can move neither forward nor backward.'

Kuwaibutsu: 'Nu-nu-nu... Do not babble, do not babble. To call you a brazen thief is an understatement. You fool who tried to steal away the master's daughter,

Ne-ne-ne... How could your twisted Kototama ever be applied against me?

No-no-no... Becoming a vessel for troublesome gods like field tengu, field tanuki, and field foxes, and talking about firing Kototama and such,

Ha-ha-ha... My stomach is twisting! It is just too funny.

Hi-hi-hi... Sniffing around after the Hime like a stray dog,

Fu-fu-fu... You clumsy fool,

He-he-he... You might as well eat a fart,

Ho-ho-ho... You senile rascal,

Ma-ma-ma... As soon as I said I fear Sincerity, you immediately grew conceited and are chuckling in your heart about firing Kototama. For my part, it is so ridiculous that,

Mi-mi-mi... It is unsightly! With that face, talking to a woman about love and such; what nonsense.

Mu-mu-mu... Since ancient times, there has never been an instance where a forced, impossible love was fulfilled.

Me-me-me... In a world of blind recklessness,

Mo-mo-mo... Blindly,

Ya-ya-ya... Even if you think you can push it through,

I-i-i... It shall not go well.

Yu-yu-yu... You shall be dropped into the Yūmeikai, wander into the Searing Heat Hell, be placed upon a chariot of fire,

E-e-e... And meet with acrid, hot agony!'

Because you were thrown into the Season River for the sake of a woman, suffered unbearable kurau, and were cast down to the eighth street of Jigoku, it seems you have had enough of women; what is with that spiritless tsuratsuki of yours?'

Akiyamawake: 'Wait, you kusojiji, soon I shall fire a barrage of bullets of Ikukototama, peerless in shinriki and perfectly seirau, at your mentei, so prepare yourself. This is the Kototama of the taking. The previous ones were leaden, but this time they are silver and gold. It has been known since ancient times that if one fires golden bullets at a monster, the monster shall perish. Now, are you ready?'

Monster: 'Aha, ha, ha, firing your testicles would be amusing. Since you have already renounced women, those testicles are unnecessary. Come, fire as many as you like. I shall receive them. However, you likely have only two. But once you fire those two, your courage will vanish, and your Kototama battle will be finished.'

Akiyamawake: 'You are a fool. It is not that kind of testicle. It is a golden Kototama whose single word shakes heaven and earth, and whose single voice can command the wind, rain, thunder, and lightning.'

While saying this,

'Hifumiyoitsumunayakokonotari yorozuyo'

As he repeated this four or five times, the monster, formidable as it was, gradually reduced in volume, and finally became the figure of a child two or three shaku tall, and in a small voice said,

Monster: 'Akiyamawake, you have attacked me by firing the true Kototama that I fear most. Very well, I have my own plans. I shall part with you for today, but be prepared, for I shall surely take my revenge tomorrow night. Ugh!'

With a groan, it vanished instantly.
