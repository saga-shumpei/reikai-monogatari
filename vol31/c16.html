Chapter 16: 天狂坊 [882]

天狂坊〔882〕

§ 1

Kuniyoriwake and his party formed a three-way entanglement at the apex of the yamamomo tree, holding their breath and praying quietly in their hearts that the two people beneath the tree would quickly leave this place.

As for the three hiding in the tree-tops, it was beyond the knowledge of mere mortals lacking divine nature; Aki and Mori, the two of them, sat down resting their hips against the roots of the tree,

Aki: 'Hey, Mori, isn't this the strangest thing imaginable? It is a fact that we crossed the log bridge singing songs and talking together. Moreover, the written message left on the zelkova tree is neither a dream nor an illusion. If so, they must come by this path no matter what. Even if one says their feet are fast, there is no reason they could have gone so fast with a woman's legs; they must have been snatched away by a tengu or someone similar, I suppose.'

Moris: 'Come now, Akiyamawake, is there any such worry? They will surely come out right here. Even so, isn't this a delightful thing? It would be annoying if Kuniyoriwake were to chase after us, so Lord Aki, Lord Mori, please drop that single-log bridge... didn't she write such a cheeky thing? With just those few words, I was completely convinced and satisfied, you see. However, my love, which had been on the verge of giving up quite a bit, flared up anew, scorching the heavens with flames, plunging me into pitch darkness, blinding me like a swift gale and thunderbolt, deafening my ears, leaving my spirit entranced, and seeming to be enveloped by demonic and romantic winds. Even so, once a bridge like that is dropped, that fellow Kuniyoriwake is bound to sniff out our tracks and come along. To go a step further, shall we wait here for Kuniyoriwake to arrive, frighten him, and send him tumbling back? Regarding that, fortunately, there is this yamamomo tree. If we climb up on top of this, use a tengu's voice, and roar at him, even Kuniyoriwake will surely think better of it and turn back. What a brilliant plan! Come on, it is about time for him to arrive. Let us climb, let us climb.'

So saying, he laid his hand upon the tree trunk and started to climb about a span.

Kuniyoriwake found this thoroughly displeasing. Deciding in his heart to play the tengu himself, he raised a voice like a cracked bell,

Ugh!

and began to rumble,

This is the Great Tengu of Mount Brazil, Tenkyōbō. Although I have made the yamamomo tree my dwelling for tens of thousands of years, if you dare to climb this tree with your defiled human bodies, try climbing it if you can. I will tear you apart from your crotch. Ugh!

The two of them suddenly turned pale in the face,

Aki: 'Hey, this tengu has quite a bit of substance for a tengu of the Forest of the Divine King. I do not know what kind of fate we will meet if we dawdle. Hey, Mori-kō, shouldn't we offer an apology?'

Moris: 'Dear Tengu-sama, it was Aki-kō who said we should climb. I have never had the intention of doing such a rude thing at all. Please forgive me, I beg of you.'

You are an atrocious villain who plotted the moment when Kuniyoriwake, the proselytizer of the Izu-no-Mitama, would cross the single-log bridge, and attempted to drop the bridge; I cannot forgive you.

Moris: 'Yes, yes, I have truly done a terrible thing, but since this was also done by Kuniyoriwake who dropped the bridge of love for Aki-kō, I had no choice and tried to drop it. However, because of that, Kuniyoriwake neither injured himself nor died. I humbly beg you to look upon us again and listen anew with Kanadorahi-Ōdorahi.'

Do you know the whereabouts of the two women?

Moris: 'Yes, they surely crossed that log bridge and ought to have come this way, but where have they sunk? They are still missing, and we are in the midst of searching. If you would graciously inform us of their whereabouts out of your great mercy, I would consider it a truly fortunate and grateful thing.'

The two women you are asking about must be Akaihime and Erina.

『七人の子はなす共、女に心許すなとか云ひますなア。本当に女と云ふ者は一寸髪を結ひ、白粉をつけ、口紅でもすると、鬼の様な洒面が俄に天女の様に見えるのだから、堪りませぬワイ』

『あれは本当の女ぢやないよ』

『さうでせうなア、男でさへも人三化七と云ひますから、何れ四足の容物でせう。お姫さまもあこ迄堕落しちや、モウ駄目ですな。何程新しい女が流行すると云つても余り極端ぢやありませぬか。丸で狐が化けとる様なスタイルをしよつて、吾々の前であのザマは一体何だい』

『どこ迄も分らぬ男だなア。あの御方は旭明神、月日明神と云ふ御二方だよ。吾々の迷惑をお助け下さつた結構な白狐さまだよ』

『あゝそれで安彦も分りました。何だか尻に白い尾のやうなものがブラ下がつてゐましたワ。是からあの二人は何うなるでせうかなア』

『どうせアフンとするのだらう。サア行かう』

と国依別の詞に二人は足を早め、谷路を東南さして進み行く。
