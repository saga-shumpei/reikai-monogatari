Chapter 8: 人獣 [874]

人獣〔874〕

§ 1

Kuniyoriwake no Sendenshi led the Kōkuntai, departed from the castle town of Hiru, saved numerous people from illness through the divine arts of Chinkon and Kototama, and gradually leaving the castle town of Hiru behind, approached the Arashika Pass.

Accompanied by two women of weak step, Kurenaihime and Erina, at the twilight hour they drew near to the forest of Shinō, where the camphor trees grew thick and gloomy, intending to endure the night dew and pass a single night.

As the three sat down at the roots of a camphor tree to rest their breath, from the midst of the darkness a strange voice came echoing. The curtain of the dark was deep; one could not clearly tell whether it was wild dogs sporting together or merely lions snarling at one another.

Not knowing what it might be, wishing to investigate and peering through the starlight, how strange this was! Unthought-of rough men, wrestling and tangling with each other, struggling and fighting for their lives.

Kuniyoriwake nodded and spoke in a fabricated voice: 'I am the great tengu of the sacred mountain, the guardian deity of the forest of Shinō. Why do you come to make a quarrel in this most disrespectful holy place? Whoever you might be, I will grab you by the back of the neck, tear your hakama at the crotch, and hang you from the branch of a camphor tree. Prepare yourselves!'

Hearing this call, the two men were startled and parted in a flash into two. Placing both hands on the ground, one said: 'I am a person serving the mansion of Hiru, called Akiyamawake.' The other said: 'I am called Morris.'

'Regarding my marriage to the woman, the competition became extremely fierce, and the result has come to this. It was truly a lamentable affair. Now, now, mooshi, Tengu-sama, please grant Kurenaihime to me as my wife! If you give this Erina to Morris as his wife, the realm will instantly become peaceful, and domestic harmony will be right before our eyes. I humbly beg your kind favor.'

Hearing this, Morris shook his head and said: 'No, no, mooshi, Tengu-sama, a beauty like Kurenaihime cannot be made to fall in love with such a man! Please bestow her upon me instead. Erina is quite enough for him. I ask you to judge this fairly.'

Praying with a single heart and mind and putting his palms together, the way he begged was indeed comical. Suppressing a laughter that was about to burst out, Kuniyoriwake once more assumed his tengu voice: 'The two women climbing up the Arashika Pass—I shall give Kurenaihime to him who wins the race! To the brave warrior who arrives first at the finishing line, I will give Kurenaihime. As for the loser, I will give him Erina, so bear with it patiently.'

'I, the divine priest Kuniyoriwake, will protect you and cast you down to the bottom of the valley just as you desire! One, two, three, go quickly!' At that battle cry, the two men rushed ahead, tumbling and falling as they descended.

Afterwards, Kuniyoriwake laughed aloud: 'Aha, ha, ha, ha! Aha, ha, ha, ha! Erina and Kurenaihime, you princesses, you have given me truly amusing entertainment tonight.' When he laughed, the princesses were startled and said: 'My heart went thump-thump with fear! How did you fare, you truly scary Tengu-sama? First of all, it is a great blessing that we are safe.'

With the loveliness of maidens, Kuniyoriwake led the two women, departed from this place, finally climbed to the summit of Mount Arashika, slipped stealthily into the neighboring forest, and there spent a single night.

Bathing in the light of the morning sun, they descended the steep slope of the Arashika Pass pointing toward the southwest. Ah, Kaminagara kamigami, may your august spirits be prosperous!

Kuniyoriwake, gradually as the day drew to a close, and while caring for the two weak-stepped women, finally reached the bank of the log bridge at Higure-shi River. Due to the great earthquake, the bridge had completely collapsed and fallen, and the Higure-shi River was flowing with turbid water in a swift current.

Having no other choice, the three spent the night among the cogongrass at the foot of the bridge, peacefully following the path of dreams.

Around what seemed to be the middle of the night, the two women suddenly awoke, trembling at the rustling sound of the cogongrass leaves whispering, and looking closely to observe, two rough men were wandering and pacing round about them.

Speaker: 'Hey, Morris, that tengu finally made fools of us, didn't he? If we hadn't been bewitched by the tengu at that time, by this time we would have easily caught up and made her our own—now, didn't we look like absolute fools?'

'Never mind, that's just an old pigeon. A wild pigeon is sleeping around here, but it seems old and toothless, making that kind of sound.'

Kuniyoriwake had been awake from the very beginning, listening to how the two were carrying on.

『ホーホーホホホー、アホ　アホ　アホ　アホ　アホ、アヽヽホヽヽキヽヽホヽヽヤホヽヽマホヽヽワホヽヽケホヽヽホホホイケキヨ、モーホ、リーホ、スーホ、ホホホーホーケキヨ、ケツキヨ　ケツキヨ　ケツキヨ、ニヤーン、モウ　モウ、ワン　ワン　ウー、ヒン　ヒン　ヒン』

『オイ、モリス、いよいよ怪しからぬ事になつて来たぞ。鳥だと思へば猫の声を出しよる、猫かと思へば牛犬狼虎に馬、オイどうぞ頼みだ、俺を負うて逃げてくれぬかい。腰が立たぬワイ……』

『オヽヽ俺だつて、腰が怪しくなつて来たワイ。足だつて細かう動き出すなり、お前所か、俺の身が持てぬのだイ』

『なんぼ利己主義が発達した世の中だとて、チツとは秋山別にも同情して呉れたら如何だ』

『俺だつてシンパシーの涙に暮れては居るが、斯うなつては如何ともする事が出来ないぢやないか。心臓寺の和尚奴が無茶苦茶に早鐘をつきよつて、何所ぢやないワイ。俺やモウ人の事所ぢやない、四這になつて逃げ出すワイ』

とワナワナする足を、犬の様に四這となり、ガサガサと元来し道へ這ひ出した。秋山別も余りの怖さに、腰の痛いのを打ち忘れ、無理無体にモリスの後に従いて、無暗矢鱈に這ひ出し逃げ出す。

『アハヽヽヽ、オイ秋山別、モリスの両人さま、アラシカ峠の大天狗又の御名は国依別命、紅井姫様のお伴をしてエリナさまと三人、此処に休息して居るから、チツと来たら如何だ。よい取持をしてやらうかなア』

両人は益々驚き、

『ヤアバヽ化者奴、国依別の声色を使ひよつた。コラ大変だ』

と無性矢鱈にガサ　ガサ　ガサ　ガサと四這になつて、力限りに逃げ出して行く。
