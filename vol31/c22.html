Chapter 22: Kami の試 [888]

神の試〔888〕

§ 1

Kuniyoriwake and his party crossed the Season River, ran through the wilderness, crossed mountains, and day and night made their way toward the upper reaches of the Amazon.

Suddenly ahead, they saw a mountain range stretching for several hundreds of ri, neither too high nor too low, resembling a folding screen. This mountain is called Mount Biyōbu, with an elevation of twenty-five thousand shaku and a summit fifty ri in width. When one widely views the southeast from atop this mountain range, the Amazon River flows like the Milky Way, the dense forests resemble clouds, and it is a scenic spot that catches the eye.

As for climbing this mountain, Kuniyoriwake decided that they would divide into right and left, and after reaching the crest, determine their operational plan. He had Akiyamawake and Morris ascend from the south, while he himself, together with Yasuhiko and Munehiko, sang propagation songs from the northern valley, deciding to ascend with a peak protruding like a hat in the center of the Biyōbu mountain range set as the meeting place. To reach the top of this mountain requires, no matter what, a distance taking four or five days on foot.

Akiyamawake and both of Morris progressed from the southern valley toward the target hat-shaped mountain while singing propagation songs. The day gradually hid in the mountains, and the curtain of darkness increasingly and thickly came to wrap around the two people's bodies. As the two had no choice but to spread grass by the side of the slope, lie down, and try to spend the night, a fierce mountain wind suddenly blew up, and the two people's bodies became of such momentum as to be almost blown off into mid-air. Although the two chanted 'Kaminagara Tamashii Sakihaumase' with all their might, the seventy-meter fierce wind force did not easily stop, and finally, Akiyamawake was blown away by the wind and scattered away into the dark night sky, nobody knows where.

Morris fortunately saved himself from this predicament by biting into the root of a rock. As the wind gradually subsided, it became dawn and when he looked around, there was no sign of Akiyamawake. ... He is probably blown away by the gale from before night and has fallen into some valley bottom or other. That wind was a tailwind, so there is no way he would have scattered toward the northwest. He surely scattered toward the southeast. If that is so, from now on, if I advance while loudly singing propagation songs, Akiyamawake will hear my voice and come ... Thinking thus in his heart, while singing propagation songs over and over, he tobbotobo ascended the valley path surrounded by mountain and mountain.

A woman's cry is suddenly heard. Wondering what matter it might be, he quickened his steps, and when he drew near to the place where the voice came from, a woman in the prime of youth had her hands and feet bound, her hair disheveled, and had fallen there. Morris was surprised, quickly untied the bonds of her hands and feet, and with a quiet voice,

Morris: 'Moshimoshi, I know not of what domain's gentlewoman you are, but by what person were you subjected to such a cruel treatment? There must be some deep circumstance to this.'

Woman: 'Hai, thank you very much. When I crossed the Season River and came this way, I was seized by four or five rough men, bound by the hands and feet dodondondondon, and carried all the way here like a dream. And just a moment ago, five men turned and turned, throwing unreasonable demands at me, so due to extreme sadness, I responded to nothing at all. When I did so, the five rough men grew angry, pulled out sharp swords, gathered together all at once, and said they would torture me to death, and just at the fatal moment when I was about to be tortured to death by them, the voice of the auspicious propagation song could be heard, so the ruffians were discouraged by that voice and fled scattering like clouds and mist, leaving not a single one. I know not which direction you are of, but a lone journey for a fragile woman—she can neither go nor return, and it is truly perilous. It will surely be an intrusion, but won't you please let me accompany you?'

Morris: 'That was a most dangerous matter. However, since I must go as far as the upper reaches of the Amazon River by the command of the Kami, it is utterly impossible for me to become travel companions with a woman, so I humbly refuse this matter alone.'

国依別の一行は シーズン河を打渡り 荒野を駆けり山を越え 夜を日についでアマゾンの 上流さして進み行く。

忽ち前方に屏風の如き、余り高からず、低からざる延長数百里に渡る山脈の横たはるを見る。此山は屏風ケ岳と云ひ、海抜二万五千尺、山頂の横巾は五十里に及ぶ。此山脈上より東南を広く観望すれば、アマゾン河は銀河の如くに流れ、鬱蒼たる森林は雲の如く、目に入る景勝の地点なりける。

国依別は此山に登るに就いて、左右に分れ絶頂に達したる上、作戦計画を定むる事とし、秋山別、モリスを南より登らしめ、自分は北の谷から安彦、宗彦と共に宣伝歌を唄ひ、屏風山脈の中央に帽子の如く突出せる峰を出会所と定めて登ることとせり。此山上に達するには如何しても、徒歩にて、四五日を要する丈の距離がある。

秋山別、モリスの両人は南の谷より、宣伝歌を唄ひ乍ら、標的の帽子山を目がけて進み行く。日は漸くにして山に隠れ、暗黒の幕は次第々々に濃厚に二人の身辺を包み来たるにぞ、二人は止むを得ず、坂道の傍に草を布き、横臥し、夜を明かさむとするや、俄に猛烈なる山颪吹き来り、二人の体は殆ど中天に飛ばさるる如き勢となりぬ。二人は『惟神霊幸倍坐世』を一生懸命に称へたれども、七十米の猛烈なる風力は容易に止まず、終に秋山別は風に吹き飛ばされて、暗夜の空を何処ともなく、散り失せにける。

モリスは幸ひ岩の根に喰ひつきて此難を免れける。漸くにして風は歇み、夜明けとなりて四辺を見れば、秋山別の姿無し。……大方夜前の烈風に吹き散らされて、どつかの谷底にでも落ちて居るのだらう、あの風は追風であつたからよもや西北の方へ散つて居る筈はない、キツと東南へ散つたであらう、さうすれば是から宣伝歌を高らかに唄ひ進み行かば、秋山別が吾声を聞きつけて来るだらう……などと心の中に思ひ乍ら、宣伝歌を唄ひ唄ひ山と山とに囲まれた谷道をトボトボと登り行く。

俄に聞ゆる女の叫び声、何事ならむと足を早め、声する方に近より見れば、妙齢の女、手足を縛られ、髪ふり乱し、そこに倒れ居たり。モリスは驚きて、手早く手足の縛を解き、言葉静かに、

『モシモシ、どこの御女中かは存じませぬが、何者に斯様な残酷な目に会はされたのですか。是には何か深い様子のある事で御座いませう』

『ハイ、有難う御座います。妾はシーズン河を渡り、此方へ参ります折り、四五人の荒くれ男に捉へられ、ドンドンドンドンと手足を括られた儘、ここまで担がれて、夢の如く連れて来られました。さうして今の先、妾に向ひ五人の男が交る交る無理難題を吹きかけまするので、妾は余りの悲しさ、何事にも応じませなかつた。さうした所、五人の荒男は腹を立て、鋭利な剣を引抜き、一度により集まつて、妾を嬲殺しにしてくれむと申し、今や彼等に嬲殺しにされようとする刹那、有難き宣伝歌の声が聞えて来ましたので、曲者は其声に辟易して一人も残らず、雲を霞と逃げ散つた所で御座います。あなたは何れの方かは存じませぬが、かよわき女の一人旅、行きもならず、帰りもならず、実に険呑で御座います。誠に御邪魔で御座いませうが、何卒御伴をさして下さいませぬか』

『それは大変に危い事で御座いました。併し乍ら私は神様の御用に依つて、アマゾン河の上流迄参らねばならぬ者、女の方と道伴れになることは、到底出来ませぬから、是計りは平に御断り申します』

§ 2

When he peered into the woman's face, wondrously enough, it was Kurenaihime.

Morisu: 'Oh, anata are you not Kurenaihime-sama? Why, pray tell, were you brought to such a place as this? Come, please leave at once without a moment's delay, and turn back along the road whence you originally came. If you sit here for long, the warumono will come turning back again, and there is no telling what sort of thing he will bring about.'

Kurenaihime: 'Ah, such unfeeling words of yours, Moris-sama-san! Ever since the time when I, serving as your Naijitsukasa, attended upon you at the mansion of Hiru, I beheld your face morning and evening, and before I knew when, my heart grew clouded on the path of love, and day by day my karada grew lean and withered, until I became a person of heavy illness. Thereupon, that hateful man Akiyamawake, morning and evening, drew close to my side, spoke of various strange things, and caused me terrible meiwaku. Out of the weakness of love, thinking too much of you, I felt ashamed and uttered unfeeling things unbefitting my heart, but my heart is by no means like that. Please, Moris-sama, today you and I are just futari; an kikuwai like this will surely not come twice. Now that I lay bare the bottom of my heart, please cherish me even to the second and third lives to come.'

So saying, shedding tears and manifesting the truth upon her face, her pathetic pleading was thus. Moris suffered intense anguish within his heart, but resolvedly speaking,

Morisu: 'My goodness, Hime-sama, you are uttering outrageous things. How could such a wanton matter be done freely without the permission of your elder brother-sama? Please forgive this matter alone flatly.'

Kurenaihime: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, now Moris-sama, do come now, do not utter such things at this late hour. I have peered well into the very depths of your heart. Without uttering such shy pretenses, say yes without a single moment's delay. I am beside myself with anxiety too, wa!'

Morisu: 'In truth, as you surmise, sleeping and waking, though I knew it was a transgression against the Way, upon gazing even once upon the beautiful figure of Hime-sama... Ah, what a lovely woman... so I became obsessed, fell into the sickness of love, and from then on, whatever I ate had no taste, my body gradually grew lean and withered, and I continued in anguish and suffering. But from a certain event, having received the divine admonition, I awoke with a sudden realization, and now, I am different from the Moris of until yesterday, so please forgive this matter alone. Moris bows with hands to the mat and begs you.'

Kurenaihime: 'Oh my, Moris-sama's feigned words! Even should heaven and earth overturn, a woman who has once resolved herself to the point of wasting away, how could there possibly be reason for her to give up so cleanly and neatly? Do not torment me overmuch. They say love is divine, and even if you and I were to become husband and wife, what manner of crime would a husband incur? Come, give me your answer quickly. Or if you truly detest it, say so decisively that you detest it. I too have a certain resolve.'

Sooner said than done, she took out a kaikén, drew it, and as she was about to place it against her throat already, Moris in a panic pressed down her hand, and amidst tears,

Morisu: 'Moshimoshi Ohime-sama, the heart with which you think of me to such an extent is truly undeserving and I feel deeply grateful. That devotion of yours I shall not forget even should I die. However, I of today, now illuminated by the light of Kami, have become a disciple of Kuniyoriwake-sama, and am on my way to wage a Kototama warfare in the forests of the Amazon River, so please understand the situation here and restrain yourself.'

As he rebuked her with a trembling voice that turned to weeping, Kurenaihime shook her head,

Kurenaihime: 'No, no, no matter what you say, I will not rest until a woman's single-minded desire is fulfilled. If that is so, let us become husband and wife after you return, so say just one thing here. Is there such a thing as being unable to do that?'

と云ふより早く、懐剣を取出し、引抜いて、早くも喉にあてむとするを、モリスはあはてて其手を押へ、涙乍らに、

『モシモシお姫様、あなたのそこ迄思ふて下さる御心は実に勿体なく有難う存じます。其御志は仮令死ンでも忘れは致しませぬ。併し乍ら今日の私は、最早神の光りに照されて、国依別様の弟子となり、アマゾン河の森林に言霊戦に参る途中で御座いますれば、何卒ここの所を聞分けて、思ひとまつて下さりませ』

と声を震はせ、泣き声になつて諫むるにぞ、紅井姫は首をふり、

『イエイエ、何と仰有つても、女の一念晴らさねば置きませぬ。そンなら帰つてから夫婦になつてやらうと、ここで一事云つて下さい。それが出来ぬと云ふことが御座いますか』

§ 3

Moris: 'Though I appreciate your kindness, please give up on that matter. I must decline once again.'

Kurenaihime: 'Ah, there is no other way. If that is the case, Moris-dono, I shall depart for Meido.'

As she drew her kuwaiken once more and attempted to press it against her neck, Moris hurriedly held her hand and said,

Moris: 'Again and again, you are a Himegimisama who is difficult to reason with. How could a fool like me, a hyottoko such as Moris, ever become the lover of a noble Himegimisama? I am grateful for your feelings, which I value more than my own life, but please, I beg you to forgive me this one thing. I have already given up on meeting women, so please, do not loosen your resolve. This is my plea to you.'

He pressed his hands together and begged her.

Kurenaihime stood up with a stern air, changed her tone, and said,

Kurenaihime: 'I am the one called Asahimiyōjin, a subordinate of Onitakehiko who guards Taikōzan. There is no longer any room to doubt your shintei. Now, head toward the Amazon River, and show your merit and achievement in the Kototama battle, Atsupa! I shall also lend you my strength and protect you, so no matter what happens, do not fear, do not falter, and do not yield; act for the sake of the Shinkai in accordance with the command of Kuniyoriwake-no-Mikoto. Moris-dono, farewell...'

As soon as these words ended, a white smoke suddenly rose and enveloped the area, and the figure of the beautiful woman who was thought to be Kurenaihime vanished from this place like mist.
