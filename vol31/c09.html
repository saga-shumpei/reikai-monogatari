Chapter 9: 誤 Kami 託 [875]

誤神託〔875〕

§ 1

Akiyamawake and Morris, both men, resting upon the plain of Kayanohara on the southern bank of the river Higura, were suddenly seized with terror at a strange voice heard issuing from the darkness, and becoming like unto crawling creatures fled from that place, retreating about two or three chō, and at last here easing their breasts; from thence they once more climbed up the Arashi-ka Mountain, arrived at the Forest of Shinō, and receiving a divine oracle, came to make inquiry concerning the whereabouts of Kurenaihime. Akiyamawake was to become the kamunushi, and Morris was to become the saniha, and it was arranged that they should respectfully offer up a divine divination around midnight of the following day.

Upon the floor of the aged shrine, placing themselves three or four shaku apart, the kamunushi and the saniha faced each other, and Morris, folding his hands, chanted forth the heavenly number-song with an irregular rhythm and tone.

"A person, two persons, finding them, whether by day or by night, chasing them around everywhere, pressing them down with a spear, knocking them down no matter what may happen, until thinking in one's mind up to nine and deciding it in one's heart up to ten, even a million times, if you do not cause us to form our intentions, the Forest of the Everlasting Shinō will not be left!"

Morris: 'Hold on, Morris, what nonsense are you uttering? Can a spirit descend with such stuff as that? Isn't it too ridiculous for one to strain oneself over? Do it all over again.'

Morris: 'It is a newly invented patented design. Since the procedures for patent design registration are currently underway, just stay quiet and listen. Since it is an era when anything new is in fashion, things like one, two, three, four... which have been practiced since the beginning of creation, have grown too mossy and are not interesting, you see. Since the gods are already tired of hearing the number-songs up to now, this is my single-rin secret of saying something a bit unusual and making them turn this way.'

Akiyamawake: 'You become the kamunushi, and I will act as Akiyamawake the saniha for you. With a saniha like you, my heart is fundamentally not in it, you know. For if the saniha is only good, any magnificent god whatsoever will possess you, you see. However, no matter if it is Morris, crow-spirit possession at Mori-sumika is excused, so you must settle your mind within the lower dantien and enter into the state of selflessness, or it will be no good. First of all, the elimination of all illusions. To make the body and clothing clean. However, since we are traveling, that matter of making the clothing clean shall be excused. Since there is no place to perform water purification upon the mountain, the cleanliness of the body is also taken as unavoidable and is likewise excused. Next, to exhaust the sensations and cut off the thoughts, entering into the state of great death once over. To straighten the posture and sit quietly with closed eyes. Next, as for what the saniha will ask... to have no doubts such as saying what on earth it might be. Not to make needless worries. Not to make worries about the past. To enjoy the instant mind. Then, the most important mental preparation is to have not the slightest attachment toward Kurenaihime. If you do not have just this much mental preparation, a true god will not come to possess you and give a true judgment, so with that intention you must clarify your body and mind, and guard against being disturbed by sensory touches... Well, it is something like this.'

Morris: 'You are saying a terribly difficult thing, aren't you. Can't you say it a bit more plainly?'

Akiyamawake: 'Even I cannot say it plainly, you see. As for what it means at present, I do not understand it either. I have merely unconsciously stuffed into my belly the things that Kaedewake-mikoto always says. However, it is precisely because it is incomprehensible that it is wonderful, you know. Is it not the same with sutras? The wonderfulness lies in the fact that even the priest who is chanting them does not understand at all what they are about, nor do the people who are listening understand them, you see. Compared to the mind after one has come to understand, How wonderful are the days of incomprehensible antiquity! It is something like that. Come, will you not quickly close your eyes and sit quietly?'

Morris: 'Now, you never know what kind of mighty god-sama is going to possess us. Do not be startled, okay.'

Akiyamawake: 'What kind of style is that, stretching out your elbows in a reckless manner, are you not acting ridiculously boastful? You look just like a leaden Tenjin-sama, it is unseemly. Will you not lower your shoulders a bit more and make them into a tasteful Jizō-like shoulder?'

モリスは又喋り出した。

『ヤヽヽ喧しワイ、イヽヽいろいろとらつちもないことを、ユヽヽ言やがつて、エヽヽ縁起の悪い、ヨヽヽヨタリスクを、ラヽヽ乱発し、リヽヽ理窟にも合ぬ事を、ルヽヽ縷々数万言を並べ立て、レヽヽ廉恥心を一寸弁へぬか、ロヽヽ碌でなし奴、ワヽヽ笑はしやがる、ヰヽヽ何時迄も女の尻を、ウヽヽ迂路々々と、うろつき廻り、エヽヽエツパツパを喰はされても、オヽヽお前はまだ目が醒めぬのか、ガヽヽ餓鬼ぢやなア、我利我利亡者の、ギヽヽ義理知らず奴、グヽヽ愚にもつかぬ事を、何時迄もグヅグヅと、ゲヽヽげん糞の悪い、ゴヽヽ御託を並べ、ザヽヽザマが悪いぞ。ジヽヽジつと胸に手を当てて考へて見い、貴様の様なズヽヽづ法螺に誰がエリナだつて、ゼヽヽ膳を据ゑるものかい、ゾヽヽぞぞ髪が立つと云うて逃げ出すぞよ』

§ 4

Akiyamawake also became determined not to be outdone,

Akiyamawake: 'Da da da, shut up, how noisy you are. Di di di, if I sit quietly and listen, zu zu zu, presumptuously and endlessly you keep on chattering away, and now I am completely fed up. Talk as much as you please, de de de, even a snail has a house, yet a homeless fellow, do do do, keeps on venting your venom to the utmost, ba ba ba, there is a limit to making a fool of me. Try spitting out whatever else you have after this, bi bi bi, I might just cast a spirit upon you to bind you so you cannot even tremble with poverty. Bu bu bu, making an unsightly, shachi-faced expression, be be be, fluently putting on the airs of a handsome man, what are you spitting out, bo bo bo, you foolish dregs of a fellow!'

Morris begins chattering again.

Morris: 'Pa pi pu pe po, stopping such evasive and far-fetched sophistry, from now on, with two women, it is a full-effort AIUEO. If you do that, even the other side will not end up saying to me that you are doing NANUNENO towards me. In the very end, it is SASISUSESO. Whenever I think of that fellow's affairs, I do not know why, but it is TATITSUTETO. Rather than doing HAHIFUHEHO to someone's something in the dark and having an elbow-blow administered, and making a fool of oneself with KAKIKUKEKO, you should just turn that nice person into WAWIWEWO, won't you. Come on, even if you ask through spirit-possession or mouth-divination, from the very root they never let us know MAMIMUMEMO things, so practical reality is the quickest path. You might be letting out pale, blue sighs, thinking: surely, trapped inside a cave on Mount Higurashi, someone strong will come out, rescue me quickly, and I wonder if that is Kurenaihime... Come on, it is not like a Tengu's divine oracle, but whoever gains the finishing line in a marathon race is Kurenaihime's husband. To abandon a nice person whom you've completely sweet-hearted to a merciless death is also something a man cannot save face for. It would be good if you happen to have fallen in conveniently, Akiyama-san.'

Akiyamawake: 'If you do that, this Akiyamawake will tightly hug Kurenaihime-sama... Well, well, if I wonder who this might be, it was none other than Kurenai no Kimi, the younger sister of Kaedewake-mikoto-sama of the Mansion of Hiru, it was truly a perilous moment, but my, my, this is splendid. To think that this is thanks to the gods, and first of all the result of Akiyamawake's self-sacrificing activity... smoothly and haughtily driving the Kototama carriage, and then the princess sheds jewel-like tears... If I wonder who it is, you were Akiyamawake after all, even with so many people in the world, the only one who shows me life-or-death sympathy is none other than you, ah, how regrettable. Not knowing you were such a kind man, until now I treated you coldly as if chasing flies off a meal, how regrettable that was. Akiyamawake, please treat me affectionately as before... that is the setup where, conversely, from Kurenaihime-sama's side she rubbers you. Ihi hi hi, ufu hu hu, ehe he he, oho ho ho, oh, how amusing, how amusing, oh, what a happy occasion. Becoming inseparable companions and delightfully and cheerfully passing this life, aren't we. To spend life weeping is one lifetime, and to spend life laughing is also one lifetime. Aha ha ha, Mori-kun, how is that?'

Morris: 'It is fine for you to say whatever you like and rejoice prematurely. Come on, whoever reaches the finishing line the earliest is the husband. No matter what anyone says, because it is the Great Tengu's permission... Hey, if you dawdle, if the sun happens to set around the log bridge, it will be the usual ho ho ho ho. Come on, let's go.'

Tucking up their skirts and leaving the Forest of the Divine King behind, the two of them advance with all their might once more toward the cave of Mount Higurashi.
