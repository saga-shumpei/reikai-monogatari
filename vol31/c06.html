§ 1

Amidst this uproarious commotion, Morrison triumphantly made his way to Kuniyoriwake's living room, and perceiving no one in sight, concluded with absolute certainty that this scoundrel had broken into Kurenaihime's chambers and was acting in a vulgar, ingratiating manner; accompanied by Erina, he strode in with heavy footsteps, forcefully flung open the sliding screen door,

Kuniyoriwake-sama, your wife has come all this long way to visit you. Come now, please enjoy yourself to your heart's content.

Ah, Lady Erina, what has happened to your mother? I have been anxiously thinking of her day by day.

Yes, thank you very much. At long last, my mother passed away in that great earthquake. Now, as you already know, my father is imprisoned on Mount Higurayama, leaving me a helpless, solitary woman; yearning for your guidance and wishing to place myself under your care, I have come seeking you all the way here. Please forgive my discourtesy the other day, do not scold me, and, wretched creature that I am, I beg you to save me.

Morrison said impatiently,

Come now, Erina-sama, is there any need to speak in such a formal, distant manner? You... there, just as you boasted in my presence, why don't you make your debut right here? After all, when I look at the face of my dear husband, I turn all mushy and helpless, so it cannot be helped. Women are weak creatures when it comes to men, ahaha!

Akiyamawake spoke harshly,

The Princess is ill, and right now she has fainted away like this. What careless things are you saying?

Ah, the Princess is ill. Oh, this is terrible. Well now, Morrison will nurse her. She must have been seized by a spasm. And since Kuniyoriwake-sama's wife has come to such a place, it makes matters even worse. However, the Princess will surely resign herself to it in the end. Come, let me take your stomach... A man's strength is a joy in spasms, I want you to hold me until my body is ground to powder... or something like that, heh heh, is what people say. It would be convenient if it were Kuniyoriwake-sama, but unfortunately it cannot be so because of the wife's inspection... Kuniyoriwake-sama, your heart must be quite troubled, isn't it?

Smiling broadly and slyly,

Come now, Princess, it is Morrison, the Director of Internal Affairs. Come, pull yourself together.

Oh, disgusting!

Thus struck in the flank with a sharp elbow blow by the gravely ill patient who had until just a moment ago lost consciousness, and taking a severe hit to her third rib, she cried out 'Ah!' and collapsed on the spot with her eyes rolling back. Kuniyoriwake was astonished and said,

Come now, Princess, isn't that a bit too violent?

Kuniyoriwake-sama, you deceived me. You said you were single, but as you see, a splendid wife has come to visit you, hasn't she? Oh, what a bitter and vexing thing.

She gnashed her teeth and went wild about the room. Akiyamawake firmly grasped Kurenaihime's body and said,

Pardon me, Princess, getting so agitated will harm your health. Please calm your mind. The world is wide. The number of men is certainly not just one or two. There is no need to be so narrow-minded. Since there are one or two men suitable for you right here in this mansion, I will take care of you. Please calm down.

Oh, you are Akiyamawake, are you? Again and again you are so unpleasantly hateful, I am getting the shivers, get away!

So saying, she clenched her fist with her frail hand and struck him square on the bridge of the nose with all her might. Taken by surprise and hit on the bridge of the nose, Akiyamawake saw stars, staggered, staggered, staggered, and collapsed heavily right on top of Morrison's head, and as he crashed down, he sat squarely on Morrison's groin; Morrison in a frenzy grabbed hold of Akiyamawake's testicles with all his might,

As she flashed a hidden tantō and tried to thrust it fiercely into her throat, Kuniyoriwake was startled, leaped forward instantly, and struck the hime's hand; at that moment the tantō dropped with a clatter to the front. Kuniyoriwake quickly picked up the tantō, and shedding hot tears,

Kuniyoriwake: 'Ah, what a troublesome thing has come to pass. This too must be the retribution for having made women weep, ruined houses, and destroyed households countless times in my youth... I am a fellow who has made women weep plenty of times. If you make a death-vow to such a man, you will be cast aside immediately, and you will be made to eat an octopus trap. Please put all thought of such a man out of your mind.'
