Chapter 5: Autumn Deer の叫 [871]

秋鹿の叫〔871〕

§ 1

Kurenaihime was nursed by Kuniyoriwake, her first love whom she had yearned for above her own life, and due to that joy her illness gradually grew light, approaching almost a full recovery.

Kurenaihime was still in the flower of her youth at nineteen, while Kuniyoriwake had already crossed the slope of forty by three or four years.

Yet, illuminated by the divine virtue of the ball of the soul, his vigor increased ever more, his complexion was good, and at a single glance he looked no different from a youth around thirty.

Kurenaihime sent away her maid and, all alone, with a lonely heart, played the ichigenkin, singing of the depths of her heart.

Having completed her song, she laid the ichigenkin aside, peeled the skin of an aubergine, and while moistening her throat with a bite, once more troubled by love, she folded both hands and sighed.

To such a place, Kuniyoriwake, taking advantage of having administered chinkon to many people and having become somewhat free, entered Kurenaihime's living room by way of rest,

Kurenaihime-sama, I certainly heard the sound of the ichigenkin, but you are really skilled, are you not? Will you not please let me listen to it as well?

At these words, Kurenaihime's heart thudded with the thought that perhaps her previous song had been overheard, and instantly dyeing her face in a flash of crimson,

Yes, did you listen to all of my idle pastime with the instrument?

she lowered her eyes bashfully. Kuniyoriwake, casually and unconcernedly,

No, I did not hear it. As I became a little free and thought to inquire after your health, when I came along the long corridor, the sound of the koto could be heard in your living room, so thinking I would dearly love to listen to it just once, when I came as far as that, the sound of the koto had already stopped. What a regrettable thing I have done. Had I come to listen just a breath earlier, it would have been a place where the music of Myōon Bosatsu could have been heard.

§ 2

Kurenaihime, covering her face with her sleeve, smiled bashfully with a 'Ho ho ho ho ho.'

Kurenaihime-sama, I have been under your care for a long time, but tomorrow, I intend to take my leave and return. Therefore, as it will be early in the morning, and I must not awaken you while you are in your slumber, I know not if I shall even see you for a while after this. I think it best to bid you farewell and offer my parting greetings here for tomorrow.

Kurenaihime suddenly changed her countenance, and said,

Eh? What are you saying? Returning tomorrow—is that not really too much? I have thus far...

I have been under your kind care for a long time, but from here, I must cross the land of Haru, go to the land of Uzu, and meet Kotoyoriwakenomikoto. Before that, there are matters in which I must help two or three men, and because my heart is extremely hurried, I must without fail set out tomorrow. I have been under your intimate care for a long time, but the living must die and those who meet must part, and they say meeting is the beginning of parting; please think of this as our connection hitherto, and since I shall pray day by day for your sound and healthy state, I beg of you never to worry about things like illness.

Kurenaihime, at the mere word 'Eh,' almost fell down in shock upon that spot; suddenly her eyes grew dim, her ears beat like a rapid bell, the beating of her heart became fierce, and a state of anxiety appeared. Kuniyoriwake was thinking... Well, a troublesome matter has arisen... and was somewhat worried. Kurenaihime appeared to have become unable to endure it, and with a single utterance of 'Un...' fainted away on the spot. Kuniyoriwake, astonished, immediately took the princess's hand, blew breath from his fingertips, and as a result of various ministrations, the princess finally regained consciousness.

Princess-sama, have you come to your senses? Oh, that is a relief. I too was extremely worried. I know not what worries you may have, but in this world, no matter what, things do not go as human beings think. Nothing goes according to anything other than the heart of the gods. For instance, even in the way of husband and wife, though there be a woman who thinks she wishes to cleave unto him, if there be no permission from the gods she cannot cleave unto him, and there are those who possess a wife they cannot help disliking and spend their whole lives unhappily; moreover, there are those who, having become husband and wife as a couple who liked each other, spent their time extremely happily for a while, when an obstacle entered in the middle, difficulties arose, and they tasted the grief of a shattered mirror. Therefore, if human beings think that things will ultimately never go as they themselves wish, they will be able to accept anything.

Kurenaihime gazed resentfully at Kuniyoriwake's face, and, as if about to say something yet hesitating, caused her upper and lower lips to tremble violently.

Just at the moment when Kuniyoriwake was stroking and patting Kurenaihime's back and comforting her in various ways, Akiyamawake, pulling open the separating fusuma sliding door quietly yet roughly with loud footsteps, poked his head straight in and said,

My, into this place of enjoyment has come a boor who knows no etiquette, and I have not the slightest sense of shame left. However, Kuniyoriwake-sama, with whose permission did you come to the princess's private room? If she were ill, that would be one thing, but nowadays she has already fully recovered, and today when there is no longer any need to ask for your prayers, for what reason have you come to the private room of the princess alone, and upon that, taking her hands, stroking her back—what kind of rude thing are you doing? Impropriety is forbidden in our house; come, come, now that I, Akiyamawake, have witnessed the scene, no matter what excuses you may make, I will not condone it. Depart quickly from this mansion as of today. I, Akiyamawake, general superintendent of the mansion of Hiru, order you by virtue of my official title.

These are words of yours that I cannot understand. Does Kuniyoriwake appear to be a transgressor from your eyes?

Yoshikohime: 'Hey Akiyamawake, it is not proper to speak so bluntly and rudely to a person. When I was suddenly stricken with a severe illness and suffering, he happened to pass by, heard the voice of agony, and came to help me. Please clear your doubts and say your thanks to him.'

Akiyamawake: 'What is this, Honorable Hime, you too have become quite a traveler lately. Trained by Kuniyoriwake-sama, well now, even Akiyamawake must yield to your Kototama, ha-ha, I am quite defeated, well!'

So when I told my elder brother every single detail, he said he would soon throw Akiyamawake out and replace him with someone else, so I should be patient for a while. Akiyamawake: 'Tch, oh, it can't be helped, no matter how much kindness I exhaust, can my heart ever be taken in, oh Benai-hime! Bah, if I'm to be driven out of here, it's all up with me now; as a revenge for unfulfilled love, I'll smash Kuniyoriwake's skull and clear away my grudge!'

As he says this, faster than taking up the brazier nearby, he strikes directly at Kuniyoriwake. Kuniyoriwake nimbly dodges his body,

Kuniyoriwake: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha, dangerous, dangerous, Akiyamawake-sama, you must not take the Hime's words literally. Bad in the mouth and praising in the heart, wanting to hear the flirtations of the shadows... that is the technique, so please be at ease. Akiyamawake: 'Kuniyoriwake-sama, I beg your pardon. This too was a temporary act, so please do not take it amiss. Surely, since it would be terrible if you were struck with an Uun, it might make your stomach angry, but please, there, look straight, listen straight, and declare it straight through Oomikoto.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'I am not such a Kuniyoriwake as to get angry over such a thing.'

Akiyamawake: 'Will you indeed forsake pitiful me and set out tomorrow?'

Kuniyoriwake: 'Yes, having become so dear to each other, it is truly full of regret, but the divine affairs are urgent, so this evening I intend to speak fully of the circumstances to Kaedewake-no-Mikoto and take my leave.'

Benai-hime cries out 'Ah!' and once again collapses right there, falling into a state of unconsciousness.
