Chapter 19: Monster Plain Field [885]

怪原野〔885〕

§ 1

Endless dead grasses, and the silver plumes of eulalia swaying to and fro, beckoning in the wind. Only the two of them traversing the vast moor of Onogahara on a journey of uncertain destination. The loathsome roars of tigers, wolves, lions, and bears felt as though they would tear their bodies asunder. With their hearts fluttering wildly in empty space, they press onward without even a word.

The rocks lying in their path shine upon the surroundings like mirrors of reflection. When they look, the physical bodies of the two are reflected with a pale complexion, wearing triangular spiritual caps. The two gaze into each other's faces and say, 'Akiyamawake, hey, how is this? Up to the point where we fell into the river, drank the water, and suffered in agony, I remember, but this has turned into something strange.' 'What might the place name here be called? The mirror rocks stand in a row right like this; it defies all comprehension,' they tilt their heads and let out a sigh, whereupon Akiyamawake says,

'Oh, this is strange, this is strange! Could it be that we two, before we knew it, were seized by the lethal hands of evil deities and have begun a dreary journey to the nether world? Today's sky defies understanding; there is not a trace of the sun or moon, and not even a single star is visible to the eye. The hellish path of the netherworld realm—drawn in by a woman's heart, have we fallen here? How pitiable! Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Though we are ministers of the Way who proclaim and transmit the teachings of the Kami,

forgetting the precious decrees, while the monkey-like mind and horse-like will run wild, possessed by the deity of love, falling into the abyss of destruction, and coming all this way—how shameful! From now on, we must compose our hearts, chant the heavenly congratulatory words (Amatsu Norito), purify our souls, and return our words before the presence of the deities of Heaven and Earth. Must we not speak our deep sins? Kamunagara, re-proclaim them to the divine spirits of the Kami, and re-hear them, O deities of Yomi! We, Morisu and Akiyamawake, reverently and respectfully pray. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon fills or wanes, even should the Great Earth sink—the divine teachings of Izutsu-no-miya (the teachings of the Kami of the Three-Five Way),

ah, from now on, from now on, no matter what may happen, we must never step astray! Since we understand this from now on, please send us back once more to the original manifest world! Or if even that is impossible, please let us meet even once with that woman we yearned for, Red Well Princess (Kōaihime) or Princess Erina. Meeting her to thoroughly apologize for our sins of discourtesy until now, and unless we receive forgiveness, somehow our hearts accuse us and we cannot bear it! Here is the eighth block of hell, illuminated by the Mirror of Pure Clarity that distinguishes good and evil, right and wrong. Inside my filthy heart, I have become thoroughly disgusted.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, leaving the capital of Hiru behind, with merciless and relentless passion, crossing the mountain pass far away, targeting the propagation emissary of Kuniyakawake as our romantic enemy, and advancing hither—how foolish! When I think of it, when I think of it, how sinful! On the log bridge spanned across the valley, stealthily removing the pillars, tying it with wisteria vines, hiding our bodies in the foliage of the trees, thinking that when our romantic enemy crosses this bridge, the two of us will coordinate our breath, pull it down, and dispatch him on the journey to the nether world—such a scheme came to naught.

Letting the woman we thought of cross easily to the other side, we surged with valor, sought her trail, and pursued. How could it be? The figures of the two beloved women were nowhere to be seen. Directing our hearts to the four quarters and sharpening our gazes, when we approached the shade of a large wild peach tree, suddenly a roaring voice echoed in the sky. The Great Tengu of the mountains of Brazil, Aikyōbō, appeared, making the two of us his target and beginning to tease us—how loathsome! At a juncture when it made one's hair stand on end, the gentle figure of Red Well Princess, unforgettable even in dreams, and the figure of Erina at last appeared here.

Telling of the depths of our hearts and reviling the rustic tengu, parting the grassy fields, and coming to the banks of the Season River, when we looked, the princess's heart underwent a total change. Uttering every harsh word of mockery and cold disdain, without thinking it too hateful, we consoled the woman's heart, and as we grew impatient that we must accomplish this fierce romantic path, Red Well Princess grew angry and leaped into the torrent of the Season River. Her figure vanished, and amid white waves, it became a matter of a past where we could meet no more. Morisu pulled Erina's hand and appeared here.

The two men persistently pleaded with Erina in various ways, but she instantly flicked them back with an elbow-gun. Defenselessly struck, they were cast with a splash into the Season River, and thinking that their precious thread of life of suffering and agony had been extinguished, our own bodies are here, as they were originally, wrapped in spiritual garments. Truly, a wondrous turn of events! Even though one calls it hell, human figures of one or two ought to be somewhere. Truly, a lonely journey this is. If it is a dream, a dream, awaken already! Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may your august spirits be prosperous and peaceful,' singing thus, they depart from here. The mirror rock suddenly turns into a raging fire and blazes up, roaring close to the two of them with fierce sounds, brandishing tongues of crimson, and chasing them to burn them to dust.

Out of fear, the two put strength into the soles of their entire bodies, and without even looking back, dashed away, fleeing without any set path. Lonely indeed is the journey of the nether world. Akiyamawake and Morisu, before they knew it, driven by the tongues of raging fire, fled with all their might from the surrounding scenery—a desolate wilderness most altered compared to the manifest world—and arrived at a certain riverside.
