Chapter 25: 会合 [891]

会合〔891〕

§ 1

Kuniyoriwake, together with Yasuhiko and Munehiko, crossed the dense forests where trees grew thick, traversed valleys, and climbed over several small mountains, until at last they reached the summit of Bōshigadake, known as the highest point of the Biyōbusanmiyaku. There, while waiting for Akiyamawake and Morris to arrive and meet them, they sat at the base of a kaya tree, offering Kamigoto as they waited.

At this moment, the sound of a Sendenka was heard from behind the mountain.

'The Kami appears on the surface, / Dividing the good from the evil. / The One who created this world, / Kuniharutachinōokami, / Together with Toyokunihime, / In the Mizuhokuni of Toyoashihara, / Beginning with the Aohitogusa, / From birds, beasts, fish, and insects, / Even to the single leaf of grass, / Bestowing the life that sustains them, / Granting each its proper place, / Making this world pure and beautiful, / How grateful we are for this growth. / Yet, in this Kamikuni of Brazil, / Which is vast and wide, / With high mountains standing on three sides, / And facing the great ocean / Where rough waves rage in the east, / It is a land where human footprints are rare, / And the evil Magatsu-kami, / Competing to be first, / Have gathered from the Amazon River / And the Shigure-no-mori, / Sharpening their fangs and claws, / Crossing the mountains from time to time, / To appear in Argentina, Peru, / And other lands such as Chile and others, / Performing their evil deeds, / Breaking the safety of the Aohitogusa, / And threatening this world. / To those evil Kami, / I shall direct the pure water and fire of the Sangokyō, / So that there shall be no demons under heaven, / Cutting off the shadows of the ugly serpents and evil Kami, / Through the divine work of the Kami's command, / To purify the Nakatsumikuni of Ashihara, / Leaving it behind, / And crossing the ocean where rough waves roar, / Together with Kuniyoriwake, / Advancing and advancing to the land of Peru, / Crossing over the mountain pass of Peru, / Appearing in the divine capital of the Uzu country, / Where the youngest daughter of the eight maidens of Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami resides, / And pausing our journey for a while. / Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami, / Having crossed the long path of waves, / Leaving the palace of Iso behind, / Appeared in the Uzu country of the precious spirit, / And commanded: / "Without delay, even for a day, / To the forest along the Amazon River, / You, Kotoyoriwake-no-Kami, / Leading two or three companions, / Advance, advance, advance quickly! / To the highest point of the great mountain range, / The green fence standing like a folding screen / From north to south, / To Bōshigadake, / Where the group of the teacher of Kuniyoriwake / Is waiting for you." / Reverencing this Kamigoto, / Passing many months and days, / Crossing mountains and fields, / And rivers, / We have finally arrived here. / Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, / May the spirits be blessed, / Meeting with Kuniyoriwake, / Joining our strength, / Firmly uniting our hearts, / Serving the divine work of the Kami's command, / To save the group of Takayorihime, / Who are lost in the Shigure-no-mori of the Amazon, / Along with Takahime, Tsunehiko, and Yasuhiko, / And to blow away the evil Kami lurking in the forest / Into the mist of Ibuki, / With the divine power of the living Kototama, / Converting the demons into good, / Harmonizing them with words, / And completing the divine work as soon as possible, / To report back to the presence of Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami. / Even if the sun sets or clouds gather, / Even if the moon is full or hidden, / No matter how fierce the evil Kami of the Shigure-no-mori may be, / I shall fight to the end, / Using the words of command of the Great Kami of the Iwa-no-Mitama as my shield. / Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, / May the spirits be blessed!''

The Senden-shi climbing the eastern slope of Bōshigadake was the very group of Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto that had appeared in this song.

Kuniyoriwake heard this Sendenka, and feeling a great joy, as if he had gained an army of a million reinforcements, he straightened his collar and waited.

Yasuhiko said happily, 'Senden-shi-sama, is this not a most refreshing Kototama, and a voice with tremendous strength? I never dreamed that the Senden-shi of the Sangokyō would come to such a high mountain, but I wonder who it is that is coming.'

Kuniyoriwake replied, 'I have not the slightest idea who it is, but it is likely that the Kami is coming. Come, let us behave properly, sit here, and welcome them.'

Yasuhiko said, 'Yes, I understand. However, are Akiyamawake and Morris not very late? I fear they may have been blown away by the gale the day before yesterday and scattered somewhere. I am truly worried. What do you think, Senden-shi-sama?'

帽子ケ岳の東方を登り来る宣伝使は、此歌に現はれたる言依別命の一行にぞありける。

国依別は此宣伝歌を聞いて、大に喜び、百万の援軍を得たる如き心地して、襟を正して待ち居たり。安彦は嬉しげに、

『宣伝使様、今の宣伝歌は何とも知れぬ清涼な言霊で、大変な強味のある音声では御座いませぬか。此様な高山へ三五教の宣伝使が出て来うとは夢にも思ひませぬが、誰がやつて来るのでせうかなア』

『誰だかチツとも分らぬが、大方神様が御越しになるのだらう。サー行儀よくして、ここに坐つて、御迎へしたがよからうぞ』

『ハイ、畏まりました。併乍ら、秋山別、モリスの両人は大変に遅いぢやありませぬか。大方一昨日の烈風に吹きまくられて、どつかへ散つて了つたのぢや御座いますまいかな。実に案じられた者です。宣伝使様は如何御考へなさいますか』

Kuniyoriwake: 'Because those two have not yet achieved sufficient kaishin, I intentionally made them climb the southern valley. They will encounter various trials from the Kamisama to polish their souls and become ritsupa ningen before they arrive here, so you two must also be very firm, or else something shameful will occur.'

Munehiko: 'Even if you tell us to be firm, Munehiko, what more can I do? Have we not remained unmoved and focused our efforts on the Goshinriki, even when hearing the voices of maujiu, facing shifurai, or enduring record-breaking taifū?'

Kuniyoriwake: 'From the eyes of Kuniyoriwake, there are still many places where your resolve is not yet settled. From here, we must go to the Shigure forest, and if you boast that such things were nothing, you will find yourselves unable to endure when you encounter a truly formidable foe. The reason you were able to keep your resolve was because we were there. If you had crossed that slope hitori and kept your resolve, it would be daijiyōbu, but I am concerned about your ability, having only managed to reach this point by standing in the middle of the three of us.'

Just as they were speaking, Morris, pulling Akiyama-wake by the hand, finally climbed up to this place. Upon seeing the party of Kuniyoriwake, they wept with such overwhelming joy for a long time, and finally opening their mouths, they said:

Morris and Akiyama-wake: 'Kuniyoriwake-sama, thank you truly. When you said, "You two must pass through the southern valley," I felt an indescribable loneliness and even resented you somewhat, but oh, we have received truly magnificent divine virtue, and for the first time, I have understood the heart of the Kamisama and your benevolence. Our souls were still clouded, and we were not qualified to participate in the Kototama battle of the Amazon River, but somehow or other, I feel as though we have finally managed to achieve a passing grade. I thank you deeply once again.'

The two of them placed their hands on the ground, lost in tears of joy.

As the sound of the propaganda songs drew closer, Koto-yoriwake-no-Mikoto, leading a small group of companions, advanced toward the side of the large kaya tree where Kuniyoriwake was sitting in meditation.

From this point, the two generals, Koto-yoriwake-no-Mikoto and Kuniyoriwake, made this place their base of operations and finally began the war of Kototama-muke against the evil spirits of the Shigure forest. Due to space limitations in this volume, this story shall be recounted in the next volume.

と話して居る折しも、モリスは秋山別の手を引いて漸く此処に登り来り、国依別の一行を見るや、余りの嬉しさに嗚咽涕泣久しうし、漸くに口を開いて、

『国依別様、誠に有難う御座いました。南の谷間を、汝等両人通つて行け……と仰せられた時は、何とも云へぬ淋みしさを感じ、又幾分か貴方を恨ンで居りましたが、イヤ実に結構なお神徳を頂きまして、始めて、神様や貴方の御仁慈の御心が分りました。吾々両人は未だ身魂に曇が多く、到底アマゾン河の言霊戦に参加する資格は御座りませなかつたが、どうやら、斯うやら、やツと及第点を得られた様な感じが致します。今更めて厚く感謝し奉ります』

と両人は土の上に手をついて、嬉し涙にくれてゐる。

宣伝歌の声は追々近付きしと見る間に、言依別命は先頭に、二三人の伴人と共に、国依別が端坐し待ち居たる榧の大木の側近く進み来る。

是より言依別命、国依別の両将はここを策源地となし、いよいよ時雨の森の魔神に対し、言向戦を開始することとはなりぬ。此物語は紙面の都合に依り、未の巻に口述する事とせり。

惟神霊幸倍坐世。
