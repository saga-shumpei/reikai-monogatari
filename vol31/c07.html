妻の選挙〔873〕

§ 1

Unexpectedly appearing at the mansion of Hiru and saving and assisting the morobito, Kuniyoriwake, spending days he never expected amidst the heartfelt and special hospitality of Kahedenowakeno, was attacked by the foul makaze, fell captive to the hands of Kurenaihime's persistent passion of love, found his advancement and retreat cornered here, and in the midst of suffering and agonizing, Erina from the foot of Mount Arashika came searching; the bustling turmoil also finally brought down its curtain, making Kurenaihime and Erina disciples of the way of the teaching, bidding polite farewells to the master of the mansion, singing a Sendenka, and stepping forth from this place.

The party of one man and two women, their path blocked by the people of the capital of Hiru, healing and helping the sick one by one, unknowingly letting days accumulate, leaving the capital of Hiru behind, and arriving briskly at the mountain foot of the Arashika Pass.

Yoshikohime-sama: 'Now, Princess, as you say this is your first journey, and from here on it is a terribly steep slope, please climb leisurely step by step. Kuniyoriwake, you too climb at an easy pace to accompany her. Since a man's pace naturally tends to become fast as he goes ahead, please let you, my lady who has the weakest legs, climb ahead here.'

Yoshikohime: 'Bringing someone with weak legs along, it must surely be a great trouble for you. You might look down on me as a woman who indulges in your words and throws tantrums, but since I have become like this, I will never entertain any strange thoughts, so please rest assured.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'If you climb straight up this slope to the end, there is a grove of giant camphor trees, and an old dilapidated shrine of Tokoyonokami-ōno-kimi is built there. Please let us climb up to that point and take a rest.'

Erina spoke gently,

Erina: 'Yoshikohime-sama, it is quite a steep slope, but since I am always used to passing through here, I do not find it particularly painful. You must be having a hard time with the slope. I will push your lower back from behind, so please climb leaning back against me.'

Yoshikohime: 'Yes, thank you very much for your kindness. For the present, it seems I can somehow manage to climb, but when it ultimately becomes impossible, I humbly ask for your assistance.'

Erina said brightly,

Erina: 'Yes, I will push you at any time. Pray do not worry at all.'

Thus they climbed on while caring for each other along the path.

The scene changing, at the root of a giant camphor tree where the shrine of Tokoyonokami-ōno-kimi is built, there are two men indulging in whispering talk.

Morisu's companion: 'Hey, Morisu, weren't we made fools of? Now that it has come to this, you and I are both fellows sharing the same pity, so there should be no particular internal strife arising, and since there is no longer any need to attempt fighting in the dark, why don't we somehow clear our resentment and make the two of them our own people? How absurd; just because that fellow Kuniyoriwake came along, the two of us were dismissed with a character of discharge, and now we are almost in the status of stray dogs. There is a saying that even a dog walking runs into a stick. Not that it's a temple fire, but out of desperate spite, why don't we crawl into the head temple of the Uralic teaching and have them use us nicely?'

Morisu: 'That's right. You too had your vital object of desire snatched away by Kuniyoriwake, the enemy of your love, and when that fellow Kuniyoriwake brought the two women out in such a manner with both hands full of flowers, putting on a big face, the surging irritation—how could this be overlooked as a man hanging testicles? Since those three fellows are coming along while propagating the teachings path by path, they will surely need some time. But if they have come as far as this place, how would it be if we suddenly throw Kuniyoriwake down into the bottom of this valley, make the two women yield to our demands freely, declare that this is what the greatest lover in the world is like, and stride along the great highways of the world holding hands with such peerless nice girls and shins? If we don't do at least that much, won't our stomach worms fail to be satisfied? However, there is a saying to settle the later fight beforehand; once we have sweetly achieved our objective and come to the stage of selecting them as our own wives, it would be terrible if an election rivalry or the like were to break out, so why don't we hold a preliminary election right now?'

『大天狗様、どうか、秋山別を能く御戒め下さいまして、紅井姫を思ひ切り、此モリスの女房に首尾よく渡します様にして下さいませ、これが一生の御願で御座います。それが叶はぬ様な事なれば仮令引裂かれても構ひませぬ。此世に生てる甲斐が御座いませぬ。秋山別にはエリナを女房にしてやつて下さいますれば、三つ口に新粉、四つ口に羊羹で、本当に都合の良い縁で御座います』

『どうぞ、私に紅井姫を御授け下さいませ。モリスはエリナで結構で御座います。さうして下さいますれば、天下は太平、無事長久疑なしで御座います』

国依別は可笑しさを怺へて、

『其方の申す紅井姫、エリナの両人はどこに居るか』

秋山別は声を震はせ乍ら、

『ハイ、三五教の馬鹿宣伝使の女殺しの後家倒し、家破りの国依別と云ふ、それはそれは酢でも蒟蒻でもいかぬ悪い奴で御座いますが、其奴が二人の女を、アタ欲どしい、ひつさらへて、ヒルの都の神館を出立いたし、天下の色男はこンなものだい、両手に花とは此事だ、二人の妻に手を引かれ黄金の橋を渡るとは、俺の事だと言はぬ計りに、そこらうろつき乍ら、やがてここへ登つて来るでせう。どうぞ天狗さま、国依別をグツとさらへて楠の上へ連れて上り、股から引裂いてやつて下されませ、これが一生の御願ひで御座います』

§ 4

'Is that Kurenaihime a woman who harbors feelings of love toward you?'

'Yes, well, seeing as she is an innocent maiden, she does not speak out clearly, but I can pretty much gauge her intentions. She is definitely infatuated with me, without a doubt. Even if she snaps back at the slightest touch and shakes her elbow like a Sanbasau dancer, going "Oh, no, no, no," I remember what it was like in my youth. When someone you like pulls even your sleeve, you feel embarrassed, and at first you put on a cold front and push them away, and afterwards you think to yourself, "Oh, I shouldn't have done that..." and regret it—since I have experienced such things myself, there is quite a pulse for me with Akiyamawake.'

'Look here, Tengusama, what Akiyamawake is saying is totally different. He has decided it all by himself, so it is just unbearable, you see. Like an abalone shell's unrequited love, even though this side's lid of a nagamochi chest is open, the other side's is completely closed for good. But if it is Erina, somehow or other, since you, Tengusama, have introduced us, she will become my wife, reluctant though she may be. Please, Tengusama, grant that sacred vote to this Morris.'

'And you are asking me to make Kuniyoriwake vanish from this world?'

'Yes, he is a man who violates the heavenly laws. If he wanders about the realm in a trio like chickens or something, first of all, it defiles the name of the divine teachings. Morris believes it is the most necessary thing both for the sake of the teachings and for the maintenance of order.'

'If that is so, since I, this Tengu, grant you permission, hurry down this slope. I shall make her the wife of whoever catches her first, even by a single step. I, the Great Tengu, shall henceforth grant my protection and guard her so that she gladly becomes the wife of the one who catches her. Since the three of them are climbing up to around the third station of this slope even now, come, hurry along, to the victor go the spoils. If you run a marathon and the one who takes the finish line does not get Kurenaihime, I will give her to you. I will give Princess Erina to the runner-up. One, two, three, off you go...'

'Yes, thank you.'

And the two of them hurried down the slope at top speed, making a rattling sound like rolling stones in the pitch darkness.

'Ha ha ha ha ha! What an amusing entertainment I have been shown by the gods... Tell me, you two, how was that?'

Kurenaihime, timidly,

'Yes, it was quite frightening, and my heart was pounding. Oh, dear, for the Tengusama to appear and sweetly chase them back home—there could be nothing more fortunate than this. Kuniyoriwake-sama, I was worried wondering what had become of you. Did nothing happen at all?'

‘Ho ho ho ho ho! Princess, that was no Tengu at all. Kuniyoriwake-sama used a voice like that and sweetly shook the two of them off, you see. I found it so awfully funny that my navel almost started boiling tea.'

Kurenaihime, in a bewildered voice,

'Oh, is that so? And yet, Erina-sama, were you carrying an earthenware teapot on your belly or something...'

'Ha ha ha ha ha! As expected, you are truly a princess raised in the deep boudoir. Kurenaihime, it is all a lie. It was so awfully funny that I used the Great Tengu's voice to threaten them and shake them off, ha ha ha ha ha!'

'You really are a wicked person. Even if you tell such lies, won't you incur the wrath of the gods?'

'People are not always what they seem. You, who until now thought Kuniyoriwake was honest, must have been quite surprised. For I am Kuniyoriwake, a hardened rogue who cares for neither vinegar, konjac, nor beating sticks, ha ha ha ha ha!'

'You, do you dislike konjac, vinegar, deco-imo potatoes, or stick potatoes? Konjac and yams are my absolute favorites.'

'Ha ha ha ha ha! You are a lovely princess through and through.'

'Ho ho ho ho ho! What a kind person. I too wish I could come to have an innocent heart like the Princess's.'

'If those two fellows should turn back, this Tengu would have to exert himself once more, and since that would be a nuisance, let us leave this place, and a little further on, let us find a suitable spot and decide to rest. Erina-sama, please look after the Princess and take her by the hand so her feet do not slip.'

'Come, Princess, let us go.'

And the three of them climbed steadily onward toward the summit into the dark sky, where stars were visible here and there through the rents in the clouds. True to its high altitude, the night storm rustled through the branches of the surrounding trees, and somehow everything all around felt intensely eerie.
