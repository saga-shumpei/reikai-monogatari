While a match is damp it is shigoku anzen, but once it crawls up from a damp otoshiana and starts frolicking about like this, it is zuibun kennon. Ahahahaha!"

"Your goritsupuku is gomotsuto, but please kamunahohi and ōnahohi, minaho and kikinaho. Būru offers its owabi."

"Ahahahaha, hey, that's a lie, a mistake. Wareware have neither enemies nor mikata in the sanzensekai. Considering only Kamisama as our parent and all things in the world as our brothers, we will never engage in a sibling quarrel that would cause the slightest worry to the Ancestral Kamisama, so Būru-sama, please be at ease. The nice lined up here like hinasama is the famous Benaihime-sama of the Shrine of Hiru. And the other one is Elina-sama, the daughter of Esu-sama, whom you have put in a water dungeon and tormented."

Putting strength into the end of his words, he shouted involuntarily, rolled his eyes, and glared. Būru was startled, shook its body violently, and said,

"Yes, is that so? Oh, how wonderful that you have come. I do not know what the world says of the father either, but since he is being treated with the utmost care, he will surely come here shortly. Please set your mind at ease."

"Indeed so. Even a mere pheasant like me was dropped into an otoshiana, and Esu-sama is carefully pickled inside a pitch-black water dungeon—it's simply astounding. We too, at the bottom of the otoshiana, were carefully served rotten pears and worm-eaten apples as side dishes, and were treated with such exquisite care. Now, Elina-sama, express your gratitude properly to Būru-sama. A cruel Būru-sama like an ogre or a snake... no... it is not, it is not, it is not so, so there you have it, since gratitude comes in many varieties, you see."

Just as they were speaking thus, Yūzu returned to this place with a pale face, walking heavily and sluggishly.
