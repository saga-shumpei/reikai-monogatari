Chapter 17: New しき女 [883]

新しき女〔883〕

§ 1

Wandering astray on the dark path of love, to the bottom of the valleys of Mount Brazil, enchanted by the demon of passion, the two, Morris and Akiyamawake, resting at the base of a huge yamamomo tree among the dense yutsukatsuragi, cried out a sorrowful voice of love: 'Even should the mountains tear apart and the seas dry up, how could I ever forget Kurenaihime? I will search for Elina's tracks to the ends of the earth and never let her go!' So he boldly shouted, when suddenly upon a transformed tree above, engaging a Tengu in a great debate of daimondau, in the very midst of their clash of wits, the beloved one never forgotten even in dreams, miraculously appeared right here, pouring forth countless grievances: 'You are a heartless man! Taking the body of a frail woman, through the fierce and howling wild fields where tigers, wolves, lions, and bears roar and shriek, those who came following and seeking you—until now, where have you been wandering aimlessly? Breaking off ownerless flowers, abandoning us two women, you say you have come to such a place as this! Can there be any such heartless thing?' With the male heart's and Akiyamawake's terrifyingly swift transformations, clinging on with cries of 'Yaida, yaida!', even the whisperings of the young man and woman finally melted together into one: 'If we had known your gentle heart sooner, we would never have had to suffer such hardships. In love there are no distinctions of high and low! Come, come, take my hand!' So saying, leading them onward, parting the mountains of mysterious women and white clouds, such was the fate of the two men who had ended up falling completely captive to love—a truly pitiful state. Ah, praise be to the deities! Receiving the divine grace, with the emotional movement of the body ruling the spirit, Mizuki, rich in experience, and the two of Jōsha Bosatsu, with the waves of the Kanō style running high (Namiko), quietly gazing upon the cedar forest with all their hearts (Hayashi Shizu), reclining in armchairs, without a trace of reserve—even the splashing sound of the Arakawa river whispering, wrapping those around them in the pale smoke of Shikishima—an entourage led by Hinodewake recounted the romance of four men and women at great length, sounding as though it were a story of some new history.

A hundred years, a thousand years, five thousand years ago, in those ancient days of the fountain pen, in the ancient of ancients, in times nearly three hundred thousand years ago, in the divine age of old. To think that the secondary guardian spirits possessing the two were speaking and writing of their physical experiences in the past would be a most enormous mistake. Ah, praise be to the deities! Seeing it anew through the divine mind, interpreting everything with good intentions, please listen to this story. Whether it be a dream, a reality, or truth, or a lie, it is no wonder that one cannot judge. With the minds of people in the world today, supposing that the people of the divine age were all uniformly straight-laced and upright, and that none had their hearts captured or suffered on account of passion, if you read this story from the perspective of such a misconception, it will surely be something you cannot accept. From the past, the present, and even to the future, there is no change in the truth of the divine realm. For a while, with a pure and innocent heart, reading it not by disparaging it merely as a love story of the age of the gods, but reading it with a hushed heart, you can truly comprehend the ultimate truth of suffering, accumulation, cessation, and the path, and it can serve as a light in the darkness of the heart and as salt to purify all sins and impurities. Believing it to be a refreshing tonic, I briefly set it down here. Ah, praise be to the deities! May your august spirits flourish and rejoice.

The two who had become captives of love, accompanied by the mysterious woman, parting the thorns, creeping among the daylilies, startled by swarms of centipedes and giant lizards, stung by bees, bitten by horseflies, tormented by gnats, and exposed to the scorching sun, pressed on aimlessly toward the southeast along the foot of an endless, great mountain range.

Reaching at last the banks of a river, a fairly wide and deep tributary of the Amazon River called the Season River, the four men and women finally arrived.

Akiyamawake-san, how far are you going to take me?

You are the one, where are you going to take me? Wandering and lost on the dark path of love, if it were something whose destination could be known, I would never call it love! With Hime-sama looking back and beckoning me, calling out quickly, 'Come, come quickly,' making a love-beckoning gesture as my joy, afraid of losing sight of her for fear something terrible might happen, I roused my agile and courageous heart, and came following you with my life at stake.

と云ひ乍ら、身を躍らしてザンブと計り、シーズン河の激流に飛び込み、パツと立つ水煙と共に後白波と消えにける。

秋山別は水面を眺め、アフンとして、暫し思案に暮れ居たりしが、

『あゝどうしたら良からうかな。こンな事ならヤツパリ、エリナの方を情婦に持つのだつたに、モリスの奴甘い事をしよつたナ。何時の間にやら、俺達の目を掠め、エリナを伴れて、どこかへ伏艇しよつたと見えるワイ。潜航水雷艇をどこへ伴れて行きよつたかなア。一つ俺もエリナ丸に乗り替へねば、敵艦に向つて夜襲することが出来まい。アーア、掌中の玉を取られたとは此事だ。紅井姫も可哀相に、余り暑い所を無理に歩かしたものだから、陽気のせいで精神逆上し、そこへ数十万年未来のハイカラ女の悪霊が憑依し、君だの、僕だのと、取とめもない事を云ひ、しまひの果にや、シーズン河の投身とお出かけなすつた。どうも気の毒なものだ。あゝ併し俺も斯うして、一人こンな所に溺死よけの石地蔵の様に川を眺めて立つてゐてもつまらないワ』

と呟いてゐる。そこへエリナの手を引いて、モリスはさも嬉し相にニコニコと辿り来り、

『ヤア秋さま、お前はここに居つたのか。紅井姫さまにお前はモウ秋さまだと云つて、エツパツパを喰はされたのだなア、アハヽヽヽ』

『ホヽヽヽヽ』

『エヽ喧しワイ、余りハイカラ女で、到底家庭の主婦として不適任だと思つたから、俺の方から秋山風を吹かして、どうぞ是からお前の様な女は、俺に顔を合して紅井姫だと云つて、エツパツパとやつたら、紅井姫が云ふのには……君それ程僕が不信用なら、僕も君に対し、強つて添うてくれとは云はないよ、アリヨースと云つてあつたら生命を水の中にザンブと計り、シーズン河だ、さすがの俺でもチツとは憐れを催し、同情の涙にシーズンで居るのだよ、あゝゝゝとは云ふものの、如何したら姫が帰つて来るだらうかな、思へば思へばいぢらしいワイの、オンオンオン』

『君は何かい、紅井氏をどうしたと云ふのだい。僕に詳細なる顛末を差支なくば知らして呉れないか。僕大いに期する所があるのだからね』

『ヤア此奴も又伝染しよつたなア。オイ、モリス、用心せいよ。又ドンブリコと計画に取掛られるかも知れないぞ。こンな所で、舟の一艘や二艘沈めたつて、閉塞隊の御用も勤まるものでなし、丸で淵へ塩をほり込むやうな不利益だから、シツカリエリナ君を捉まへてゐ玉へ。僕は経験上、君に注意を与へておくよ』

『あゝ、モリスも薩張り合点の行かぬ事になつて来たワイ』

と拍手し終つて『惟神霊幸倍坐世』を三唱し、何事か切りに暗祈黙祷を久しうして居る。
