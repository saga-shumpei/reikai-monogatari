Chapter 13: 姉妹 Teaching [879]

姉妹教〔879〕

§ 1

Būru sat down cross-legged in the inner room together with Kuniyoriwake and his entourage, receiving words of thanks from Kiji, Machi, Erina, and others that felt both ear-stinging and tickling, while waiting with eager anticipation, saying to himself, Oh, I do hope Yūzu brings Esu here quickly and handles this smoothly...

Just then, Yūzu arrived with a crestfallen face, and Būru immediately gazed at his expression, thinking to himself: Could it be that Esu has somehow died in prison? If by a million-to-one chance such a thing has happened, how shall I smoothly extricate myself from this situation? Even though there are hundreds of subordinates, every single one of them is a spineless coward devoid of any real strength before Kuniyoriwake. My advance and retreat are completely cornered... Deliberating thus to himself alone, Būru put on a distant manner and spoke,

Būru: 'Hey, Yūzu, I wonder if Lord Esu is in good health.'

Yūzu: 'Before you know it, their honors have graced us with their presence. Ah, well, well, Lord Kuniyoriwake, how wonderful that you have come to this humble hut. Somehow, because my thumb is not cooperating well, the pulley simply will not operate smoothly. Nearly all of us have run out of engine oil, to the point where we are ready to raise our voices... And you, what an amazingly extraordinary and nice person you are! From where have you descended? Are you Tanabatakihime-sama, who set afloat a jewel boat on the heavenly river, held the moon's mirror in her bosom, and plied a golden oar as you came down? Or are you perhaps the descent of Konohanahime-sama of Mount Amenokawa, or the reincarnation of Matsutorahime-sama? You are truly a splendid person beyond description, ah.'

Thus he rattled on with endless flattery.

Būru: 'I am not asking about such things. I am asking whether Lord Esu is in good health... that is what I mean.'

Yūzu: 'Ah, you mean that Esu-sama, whom you, the Kyōshu-sama, instigated and cast into the water dungeon. Even if I speak thus, bearing the thousand-fold purification is the proper duty to be borne by one who is a leader of men, so please try not to take it the wrong way. We as a whole advised you that we should not do such a cruel thing, but you proudly shook your head from side to side, rotated your eyeballs at high speed like a gyrocompass, and refused to listen in the least, which is why that great tragedy finally broke out, you see. Just as you say, if we had guarded him faithfully, Esu-sama would have become a person of the spiritual world long ago, but hiding and appearing, this Yūzu has been carrying sweet things... Oops, hold on, Kyōshu-sama, you must not make such a complicated face. To take the sins of all subordinates entirely upon oneself and not utter a single murmur is the breadth of mind becoming a leader. Well, anyway, if you will just guess the rest without me having to say it, Lord Kuniyoriwake, Lady Erina, you must have a general idea of how things stand, right?'

Kuniyoriwake: 'Būru-sama, it appears that Esu-sama has received quite harsh sympathy.'

Būru: 'When you say that, I truly have no way to reply, but the truth of the matter is that Yūzu—'

Yūzu: 'Now, now, Kyōshu-sama, you must not derail like that. If you do, your value as the Kyōshu will be utterly zero. Since things have come to this, it is to our advantage to side with the one whose prospects are bright. Even if something or other is done to me by Būru-sama, I do not mind at all. If I do something or other to win the great favor of Lord Kuniyoriwake, have him make me a splendid propaganda envoy, and things go conveniently, I might even get to be given a certain something as a certain something. That is why, hiding and showing myself, I have cherished my father figure, Esu-sama. Right, Erina-sama, even though I look like this, on the surface I am—on the backside, I am a hard worker truly brimming with tears of beautiful compassion.'

Elina: 'Hēen, Sayō desukai na,' she sneered into the empty air.

Speaker: 'What are you talking about? Will you not go quickly and welcome Esu-sama?'

Speaker: 'Hai, no matter what is said, as expected, Esu-samasama it is. Just as I had foreseen, magnificent indeed, quite admirable. No matter how much I say, he does not budge an inch, nor will he move at all, my. He is quite like a dead horse, wa.'

Elina's complexion suddenly changed,

Elina: 'Eh, has Father passed away?'

Speaker: 'No, no, why, he is jumping about lively and vigorously like a shrimp. To tell you the truth of everything openly, until today I have been treating him cruelly under Būru-sama's orders, but upon hearing that you people had arrived, Leko's commander shook like a grandchild of an earthquake, Būru-būru, and greatly distressed himself (Konniyaku), thought up a sudden piece of theater: to have Esu-sama come out here a step ahead of you people, make him drink plenty of sake or the like, issue a gag order (Kankōrei), and somehow muddy the waters of this place. That was what we attempted, but since your arrival was altogether too early, all our plans crossed wires and in the end they became a painted cake (Guwahei). Even if one thinks to treat him like a child or something by making him drink a little sake to win his good humor and hide the maltreatment up until now, it is settled that such a thing is no use; at a time when one panics, such wisdom simply did not come forth, wa. Truly, the Kyōshiyusama's wisdom is so shallow that the subordinates all together are in a state of confusion, or rather distress (Konniyaku). By no means, by no means is this Yūzu's fault. Yūzu was not the one who first proposed putting Esu in, so I ask you to understand it that way. And Esu-sama knows everything properly; he was well aware that your coming here was by Tōshida or Suinou or whatever. Having been placed in such a splendid august residence, who would ever come out? Even if a thousand or ten thousand years were to pass, he will never make a move, he declared with such an extraordinary stubbornness. Judging by appearances, unless Kyōshiyusama Būru-sama goes personally to Esu-sama's side, bows his head, and offers an apology, there is fundamentally no likelihood that he will grace us with his presence, wa.'

Speaker: 'Būru-sama, what shall we do? If Kuniyobake himself goes directly to the place where Esu-sama is... otherwise, with an old man's obstinacy, he surely will not move...'

Speaker: 'To have that sort of place seen is truly inexcusable. Since I am going now to bring him along, please wait here for a while... Now, Yūzu, you come out too, for if you chatter about useless things again, it will not do...'

Speaker: 'Indeed. For me to be here is surely a most inconvenient matter, as I have known beforehand, wa.'

Speaker: 'Now everyone, let us go. Yūzu-sama, please guide us. Elina-sama, from now on I shall let you meet your beloved father. Please rejoice.'

Speaker: 'Hai, thank you very much. I beg your kind favor in all things.'

Here, with Yūzu in the lead, Būru and the party of Kuniyobake cautiously drew close to the dim underground water dungeon,

Kuniyobake: 'I am one called Kuniyobake of Aiso. Though you are a missionary of the Ural Church, you have nonetheless taken good care of the missionaries of Aiso. Because of that, you have been locked away in such a place, and it must have been a most distressing ordeal for you indeed.'

Esu, shedding tears,

Esu: 'Hai, thank you very much. You have come well. To tell the truth, until yesterday that wretch Yūzu who is right there inflicted cruel treatment upon me, but suddenly his attitude completely changed, and just a moment ago, with fawning flattery, he drew me out, and I came to realize that he was thinking up crafty things like making me the material to write off the vicious wickedness up to now. Therefore, I purposely stood my ground and acted stubborn, declaring that even for a thousand or ten thousand years I would never leave such a splendid august residence. When I did that, taking it to heart, he grew worried, begging and pleading—it was truly a pitiful sight. Who on earth would want to remain in such a place for even half an hour? Please imagine my feelings.'

Even the voice sounded wet with tears. Hearing this, Elina simply burst into tears and fell prostrate on the spot.

Yūzu hurriedly raised her and said,

Yūzu-san: 'Moshimoshi Elina-sama, get a grip on yourself. Since this kind Yūzu will nurse you, it will be all right now.'

Elina: 'Ah, my father's enemy, even your speech is filthy, do not touch my body!'

Saying this, she shoved him away with a thud. At that moment, because the entrance of the cell happened to be open, he suddenly fell headlong with a splash into the puddle inside the cell.

Taking advantage of that opening, Esu quickly darted out of the cell, slammed the entrance door shut from the outside, and threw the lock. Yūzu crawled up from the puddle and said,

Yūzu: 'Moshimoshi, please open it, it is me.'

Esu: 'It is because it is you that I threw you in. Come now, since it is you who hitherto slandered Esu-sama in various ways and plunged her into guilt, from today as atonement you shall dwell in the water prison in Esu-sama's stead. Such a thing as divine punishment is a fearful thing. Have you not been pushed in by Esu-sama's daughter, Elina-sama, right now? This too is by no means what Būru did. Your sins have accumulated, and I have thrown you into the water prison.'

Yūzu: 'Is that not too avaricious? There are also things I left forgotten just outside, and since there are many things I want to say meeting Anan, if I can enter I will enter, so please just let me out for once.'

Esu: 'I will not, I will not. You said without exception your own evil deeds, that the Kyōshiyu should bear the thousand-seat purification decrees, and you uttered Būru's slanders before Kunioriwake-sama and his suite, did you not? Letting such a great villain roam outside is like turning a maulband loose in the fields, you see.'

Yūzu: 'Omaesama, do a little ascetic practice right there. No matter how much you make excuses, it is no good. When this Esu was caught by your subordinates at the entrance of the gate and brought out before Kyōshiyu-sama, do you remember what the Kyōshiyu uttered? In those words... Yūzu, you speak so ill of Esu, but you must think of it a little. The world is mutual sympathy, so even if she lodged a missionary of Anisatsu, you should not speak of such small matters, it would be good to have forgiven her—when he uttered that, tears of gratitude flowed from me as well. Yet if you throw a tantrum, and cause the Kyōshiyu to break such a rule, this Yūzu will lead every single one of numerous believers, enter the Brahman religion, and overthrow this mansion, you threatened, and finally, being unavoidable, the Kyōshiyu also tacitly permitted me to be thrown into such a place. That is why you are the ringleader of evil. If only you thus seclude yourself here forever, the Anisatsu and Ural religions will melt together from the bottom of their hearts, take each other's strengths, make up for each other's weaknesses, become sister religions, and happily participate in the divine work. Come, everyone, it is of no use staying in such a place forever. Shall we go somewhere?'

Būru said,

Būru: 'Please come all the way to my living room. I shall guide you.'

Going ahead, she returns to her own living room. Behind, Yūzu raises his voice and cries,

Yūzu: 'Ooi, ooi! Help me, help me!'

The company, paying no heed to the details, returns to Būru's living room, is given wine, and drinks it deliciously while indulging in idle talk. After a short while, the Kyōshiyu comes from the inner chamber wearing solemn ritual vestments and says,

Kyōshiyu: 'Now, everyone, I am going to the sanctuary from here. Please do come as well, you too.'

Kunioriwake and his suite nod and proceed to the sanctuary, chant the divine words under the guidance of Preceptor Būru, and afterwards return once again to the inner chamber. Būru expresses her repentance from the bottom of her heart, and by the decision of Kunioriwake, Esu is made Kyōshiyu, Elina is appointed to the administration of all internal affairs, Akaihime is for a while treated as an honored guest and celebrated as the flower of Mount Himuresi, and finally establishing the Anisatsu religion, she becomes the wife of Būru, and in mutual cooperation with the divine mansion of Hiru, they expand great influence across both countries of Hiru and Karu, saving and helping all people and making their illustrious names resound.

Yūzu is subjected to a hundred days of penance, and upon being pardoned, serves the gods once more. Anan is also rescued from the bottom of the pitfall, repents, serves the way of the gods purely, and comes to spend her life in peace.

Here, Kunioriwake, after staying for four or five days, accompanied by the two persons Kiji and Machi, bids farewell to Mount Himuresi, crosses mountains, fields, rivers, and marshes, heads toward the Brazil Pass, and proceeds while singing missionary songs. Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sakihamase.

ブールは、

『どうぞ私の居間迄御越し下さいませ。御案内致しませう』

と先に立つて、吾居間へ帰り行く。後にユーズは声をあげ、

『オーイ　オーイ、助けて呉れ　助けて呉れ』

と呼ばはつて居る。一同は委細構はず、ブールの居間に帰り来つて、葡萄酒を与へられ、甘さうに、四方山の話に耽り乍ら、飲ンでゐる。少時らくあつて、教主は奥の間よりいかめしき祭服を着し来り、

『サア御一同様、私は是より神殿へ参ります。どうぞあなた方も御参り下さいませ』

国依別以下は打うなづき乍ら神殿に進み、ブール導師の下に神言を奏上し、終つて再び奥の間に引返し、ブールは心の底より改心の意を表し、国依別の裁決に依つて、エスを教主となし、エリナは内事一切の司に任じ、紅井姫は暫く賓客として、日暮シ山の花と謳はれ、遂に三五教を樹て、ブールの妻となり、ヒルの神館と相提携して、ヒル、カル両国に亘り、大勢力を拡充し、万民を救ひ助け芳名を轟かしける。

ユーズは百日の間の苦行をさされた上、許されて、再び神に仕へ、アナンも亦陥穽の底より救ひ出され、悔い改めて神の道に清く仕へ、一生を安く送る事とはなりぬ。

茲に国依別は四五日逗留の上、キジ、マチの両人を従へ、日暮シ山に別れを告げ、山野河沼を渡り、ブラジル峠を指して、宣伝歌を歌ひ乍ら進み行く。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
