Chapter 10: 噂の Shadow [876]

噂の影〔876〕

§ 1

At the foot of Mount Higura, opening the hall of the teaching, morning and evening neglecting their duties, the Ural teaching of Mount Higura, starting with Anan and Yūzu, hiding from the eyes of the Kyōshiyu Būru, pushing open the storehouse of Budaushiyu, and with their tongues tangled by the rich and sweet stolen sake, the two sit with their knees touching, engrossed in whispering.

Yūzu, with the wine going to his head, says,

Anan, '...' -- Wait, Yūzu: 'Hey, Anan, when we went to Erina's residence at the foot of Mount Arashika, it must have been quite interesting, wasn't it? If there hadn't been an earthquake like that at the time, you would have been having a sweet time of it, wouldn't you? Isn't it a pity thing?'

Anan: 'Well, that certainly was interesting. However, as she is truly the daughter of Esu, when she was really making her debut, even the likes of Anan could not help being a little startled. But as for the recent mood of the Taishiyō, isn't it getting completely out of hand? Is there no ingenious plan to somehow put him in a better mood? No matter what is said, it is because we failed in the attack on the capital of Hiru like the other day, and since we did not capture Kurenaihime alive and bring her back for him to see, his mood is bad, you see.'

Yūzu: 'Don't talk nonsense; this Yūzu knows well that, of all people, the Kyōshiyu does not have such a cheerful mind. Since he is concentrating with all his heart solely on matters of the Kamisama... you are making quite a miscalculation, you know.'

Anan: 'That is what they call being beyond calculation. You too are quite simple-minded. The truth of the matter is that when you were sent to the attack on the capital of Hiru, the very core and objective was that you should make Anan capture Kurenaihime alive and bring her back... that is the main point, you see.'

Yūzu: 'How on earth does the Kyōshiyu know about Kurenaihime of the hall of Hiru? Isn't that what we don't understand? Kurenaihime is a so-called sheltered maiden, and is she not said never to have taken a single step outside the castle walls?'

Anan: 'That is just it... they say that even if they look far, a man and a woman are close, you know. Before we knew it, properly in the sharp eyes of the Kyōshiyu, she was marked long, long ago. Since you people became high-ranking members, not much time has passed yet, so you do not know the true story, but it was five or six years ago. Riding in a boat from below the capital of Hiru, letting the sails catch the favorable wind, on the way back of the Kyōshiyu of Būru hither, in a pleasure boat that drifted smoothly down from above, was there not riding a maiden of fourteen or fifteen, who looked just like a heavenly maiden, whose identity was unknown? At that time, the Kyōshiyu was captivated by that woman, missed his footing at the gunwale, and fell into the river, and there was a terrible danger. Since this Anan was by his side at that time, I know it well. The Kyōshiyu was hauled up from the river by us, and without saying a word of thanks or anything... "Whose person in the world is that beautiful maiden?"... when he asked with a significant air, the moment I said to the Kyōshiyu... "That woman is the daughter of Momoyahiko-no-Mikoto of the divine hall in the capital of Hiru"... he drooped limply at once, and it was truly a state of disappointment and discouragement. Ever since then, whatever the circumstances, no matter how good a match there might be, the Kyōshiyu has brushed them all aside, and living in the prime of his youthful vigor, continuing a bachelor life down to the present day, there is a certain design behind it. Somehow or other, I want to pull that woman in and make her the Okusama of the Kyōshiyu. If we do that, he will always be smiling and his mood will be good, but when he shows such a difficult face as he has lately, we really cannot stand it, wah.'

Yūzu: 'This is the first time Yūzu hears of it. If his mood can be cured by such a thing, I want to somehow continue devotional activities and make it a success. Knowing such a secret, why haven't you told Yūzu, who is peerless in strategy, until now? No matter what, since I am a Yūzu who has sway, if I had known about it long ago, I would have succeeded, you know.'

Yūzu, you are terribly strong only when you are drunk, but once you sober up, you become timid like the traces after a great flood has receded, shriveling up and looking completely deflated, so you can never be relied upon.

Nonsense, when it comes to such matters, I am Yūzu with a sail to the fair wind; I will surely make it a success.

If that is so, why don't you head into the capital of Hiru from now and use some scheme to drag them out?

For me, this Yūzu, all things are in the palm of my hand; today, as a pre-celebration, I will heartily partake of Būru wine, and tomorrow at last I will raise the curtain on our activities, so if things go smoothly I ask for your applause and cheers—ha ha ha ha, how interesting, how interesting!

However Yūzu, you must postpone your trip to the capital of Hiru for a while and there is something you must stay here and do for us.

Postpone it for Yūzu, what sort of reason is that, pray tell?

As you already know, Kiji and Machi, the two sent by the missionary of Kuniyoriwake, have been left thrown into a pit like that forever, with two or three rotting pears and mandarins tossed in every day just to keep them alive, and because we maltreat them and refuse to let them return, Kuniyoriwake by now must be thinking: 'The two I sent to Mount Higurashiyama have not returned yet, what on earth can be happening to them? Surely, with all that zeal of theirs, they could not have been converted to the Ural Cult; they must have been dropped into some pit and are suffering.' And if he should come striding over saying, 'Let us go out on a field expedition... and so forth'—then, not unlike the recent earthquake, a great catastrophe of strange natural phenomena will suddenly erupt in this sacred ground; Anan is worried about that above all else. If Kuniyoriwake should come, we must completely change our attitude from before and devise the best possible method.

That is true. But this time, making use of Yūzu, we shall stoop to the lowest position from our side, treat him as an honored guest, make Kuniyoriwake understand from the bottom of his heart, and if circumstances permit, recommend him as the vice-leader of the Ural Cult; no matter how stubborn that fellow may be, compared to his present status as an ordinary missionary, the difference in position and fame is like mud and heaven, so he will surely rejoice and accept, won't he? Since the Ural Cult is also feeling the autumn wind like these days and is enveloped day by day in an atmosphere of desolation, if we use the tactic of controlling the enemy with the enemy and win such a splendid man over to our side, the Ural Cult will surely regain its strength and become a splendid religious organization once again, or so I think—what do you think?

That is also true. However, will this enterprise progress so smoothly and steadily just as Yūzu says?

Anan-dono, please have no worry whatsoever.

Just as they were talking thus, Haru and Nairu, the gatekeepers, came running up in a flurry, and Haru said:

I have an announcement to make. Just now Kuniyoriwake has come to the mouth of the Iwato, accompanied by two strikingly beautiful women named Akaihime and Erina, and he says he will not leave no matter what until he is made to meet one of the leading figures quickly; how should we handle this, I wonder?

Anan, startled with a start, shook his voice slightly and said:

Wh-what do you say? Ha-has Kuniyoriwake come?

Did you say that both Akaihime and Erina have arrived? Could that perhaps be a case of mistaken identity? Just a moment, for Yūzu is also worried, you know.

Oh, there is absolutely, absolutely no mistake in Haru's words; he is the missionary we saw in the valley of Mikurayama, and there is no mistake about one of them being Erina. As for the one called Akaihime, since I have never met her before, I cannot tell whether it is true or false, but she is a remarkably pretty woman; she has a look in her eyes as if she would smash you to pieces with a single glare even here in this rock-mansion... Right, Nairu, she was really a beauty, wasn't she?

Anan: 'Goriyōninsama, I think there is surely no mistake with the eye of the Nile. We must give some reply, but what shall we do?'

To this inquiry, Anan bowed his head, crossed his arms together with Yūzu, and remained lost in thought.

Meanwhile, both Haru and Nairu quickly departed from this place, appeared at the front entrance, turned toward Kuniyoriwake, and speaking with one voice, declared:

Haru and Nairu: 'Hey, wait a moment! The supreme commanders Anan and Yūzu have said that they also have a plan on this side...'

Kuniyoriwake: 'What? "Wait a moment, we have a plan on our side..."—that is outrageous. Well, if that is how it is with you, we also have a plan on our side... Come, Princess, Lady Erina, follow after me.'

He attempted to step inside the cavern.

Both Haru and Nairu spread their arms wide and said:

Haru and Nairu: 'Now, now, please wait. This is a terribly serious matter. Because the two commanders said, "Wait a moment, we also have a plan on our side," if you force your way in so arbitrarily, I have no way of knowing what kind of dreadful fate will befall me afterwards. Until a reply comes from the depths a little later, please rest there for a while, I beg you.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'Ah, it cannot be helped. Kuniyoriwake will wait here for a while to rest. Go once more to the inner chambers and make them give a reply quickly.'

Haru muttered in a low voice:

Haru: 'Coming here for the very first time, putting on such grand airs, and treating us like mere slaves. Ah, what an unpleasant nuisance...'

Muttering thus, he ran back once more toward the living quarters of Anan and Yūzu.

The two men had been crossing their arms and looking down in despair, but when they raised their faces for a moment, the two who had gone as messengers earlier were gone before they knew it, whereupon Anan changed his countenance and said:

Anan: 'Ah, those two fellows, where on earth have they gone? Could it be that they rushed out before hearing the answer? They say that misfortune comes from below, and if they speak of useless things, the aftermath will become troublesome and things will fall apart. There is even a precedent where painstaking divine strategies and demonic plans came to naught. Ah, what troublesome fellows they are.'

The two muttered this while looking at each other's faces.

To that spot came running hastily both Haru and Nairu, who said:

Haru and Nairu: 'I report to you. They are terribly stubborn fellows and are trying to force their way in, so just as you said—"Hey, wait a moment, because we also have one plan on this side"—when we hurled that at them, that fellow Kuniyoriwake said... What, how presumptuous, if you have a plan on your side, we have a plan on our side too... and he was saying such arrogant things. If you do not exercise caution, now that such a strong fellow has appeared, it will be a terrible crisis.'

Anan: 'Hey, you two, Haru and Nairu, who told you to say such a thing to Kuniyoriwake, and when on earth did we utter such words?'

Haru and Nairu: 'Dear me, Anan-sama, have you already forgotten? You surely... Nairu was listening right here, too, and didn't you say, "Wait a moment, because we also have a scheme on this side"? Even if you say now that you did not say it, such unreasonableness will not pass no matter how high-ranking you may be.'

Anan: 'That was said to you, meaning "wait a moment"...'

Haru and Nairu: 'Indeed so, you said it to me, so naturally I became your proxy and conveyed it to Kuniyoriwake without a single mistake. What on earth is wrong with that?'

Anan: 'Ah, what troublesome fellows they are. Now that things have come to this, there is no help for it... Anan-sama, casting aside our pride, you and Yūzu must use thoroughly polite words before Kuniyoriwake, coax him, make sure not to anger him, and if we proceed smoothly, we shall enthrone him as the vice-prelate of the Ūraru Religion, make Princess Kurenai the wife of the prelate, and settle Erina as my wife; then tightening our bellybands, let us try wielding our tongue-swords smoothly, shall we not? Depending on the use of a single tongue, one becomes either the goddess of peace or the demon of war, so let us employ sufficiently clever diplomatic language, and resort to the method of subduing the enemy through the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases, much like the Misoshiru Teachings—shall we not put this plan into action?'

Yūzu: 'That must be an extremely brilliant plan. However, prior to that, we must inform the prelate of Būru. You have our gratitude, but please undertake that duty. I shall go to the front entrance from here and thoroughly flatter Kuniyoriwake to get on his good side...'

Suddenly changing their language from rough masculine speech to beautiful words, Yūzu proceeded toward the prelate's living quarters, and Anan advanced toward the entrance, each fixing their station.

The two, Haru and Nairu, stood before Anan and muttered:

Haru and Nairu: 'Now, now, what kind of thing lies just ahead? Is it a dark, moonlit night or a softshell turtle? A troublesome matter has arisen.'

Muttering thus, pointing toward the entrance, they dashed forth.

『さうでせう、私に仰有つたから、即ち私があなたの代理となつて、国依別に一言の間違もない様に伝へたのです。それが何処が悪う御座いますか』

『あゝ困つた奴だなア。モウ斯うなつちや仕方がない。……アンナさま、肝玉を放り出して、あなたとユーズの二人が国依別の前に十分に慇懃な詞を使つて、言向和し、怒らさない様にして、甘く行けばウラル教の副教主に祭り上げ、紅井姫は教主の奥方となし、エリナは私の奥方と決定ておいて、腹帯を締めて一つ円滑に舌剣を揮ふて見ようぢやありませぬか。一枚の舌の使ひやうに依つて、平和の女神ともなり、戦争の魔神ともなるのだから、十分に巧妙な辞令を用ゐ、三五教ぢやないが善言美詞の言霊を以て、敵を悦服させると云ふ手段に出ようぢやありませぬか』

『それは至極妙案でせう。併しそれに先立ち、ブールの教主に申上げておかなくてはなりますまい。あなた御苦労だが、其任に当つて下さい。私は是から表口に往て国依別に都合をよく胡麻をすつて来ますから……』

と俄に吾れ、俺の辞を改め、美しい言葉を使ひ乍ら、ユーズは教主の居間へ、アナンは入口へと持場を定めて進み行く。

ハル、ナイルの二人はアナンの前に立ち、

『サアサア一寸先は如何なる事やら、暗か月夜か鼈か、困つた事が出来て来た』

と呟き乍ら、入口指して駆け出しにける。
