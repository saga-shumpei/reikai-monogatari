Chapter 24: Demon 違 [890]

魔違〔890〕

§ 1

As Akiyamawake was spending the night under this tree, the sound of a Sendenka reached his ears from afar. Akiyamawake danced with joy, and when he looked back carefully, he saw Morris alone, holding something like a baton in his hand, waving it as he advanced toward him. Akiyamawake, feeling as if he had met a Kami in Jigoku, waited while dancing with joy. However, when he looked closely at the figure approaching, it was not Morris, but the Bakejiji from the previous night, who had made his body slightly smaller and was using Morris's voice.

Yet, Akiyamawake, deeply believing it was Morris and not doubting it in the least, said as if to leap upon him,

Akiyamawake: 'Hey, Morris, where have you been? I have been waiting for you so long. Last night, as I was sleeping under this tree, a truly disgusting Bakejiji appeared, and even I, as you might expect, had my courage shaken. But when I fired my Kototama at him, the joy I felt when he was startled and ran away, having shrunk in size, was beyond words! Hahaha!'

The man who appeared to be Morris,

Morris-lookalike: 'Is that so? That must have been amusing. Oh, it must have been unpleasant, I suppose. But today, I have brought you plenty of what you fear most, so consider it a return for last night. You had better enjoy it at your leisure.'

As he said this, he scattered something like hemp to the left and right, flicking it repeatedly. The moment hundreds of small, ball-like objects fell, they all exploded at once. As if Momotaro had been born from them, hundreds of Beniihime appeared, surrounding Akiyamawake from all sides,

Beniihime: 'Look here, Akiyamawake-sama, you are a pathetic man. To think you would abandon me and flee to such a place; it is truly hateful.'

Saying this, she slapped his cheek, pulled his ears, and twisted his body all over. Another woman of the same appearance clung to Akiyamawake's legs,

Beniihime: 'You are truly a sinful man. Even though I am so in love with Kuniyoriwake-sama, you, in your unwanted, illicit passion, have ended up with nothing at all. Oh, you are my rival in love, Akiyamawake-sama!'

Saying this, she bit into his fist with a snap. Yet another woman grabbed him like a warrior,

Beniihime: 'Oh, what a pity, what a pity. Because of you, I threw myself into the Season River. I shall not let you go without taking my revenge!'

She grabbed his topknot and pulled him around mercilessly, causing him great pain. Akiyamawake screamed at the top of his lungs,

Akiyamawake: 'It hurts, it hurts, please spare me. I cannot bear being attacked by women like this anymore. My life is fading, please help me!'

He cried out in a weeping voice. The many women grabbed him again like warriors,

Beniihime: 'Did you not say that if it were for a woman, you would need neither your life nor anything else? You are the Beniihime born of your own heart; now, let us take your life. You should be satisfied to die at my hands. Look here, Akiyamawake-sama, one should not be born as a dark-skinned handsome man. Hahaha! Does it hurt? Does it hurt? Even if it hurts a little, you must endure it. Being poked, bitten, and pinched all over your body by cute women must be the greatest honor for a man in the world... Come, come, Beniihime of the first, second, third, fourth, and fifth, let us gather and torment this man. Ah, how interesting, how interesting.'

As they said this, hundreds of women took turns hitting his head, pulling his hair, pinching his nose, scratching his ears, and biting his hands and feet, causing him such agony that Akiyamawake finally could not bear it and collapsed on the spot. The man who appeared to be Morris opened his mouth wide and said,

Morris-lookalike: 'Hahahaha! That is the return for you firing your Kototama at me and tormenting me last night. You said that while you fear nothing in this world, you fear women most of all, so I have taken my revenge using women. Now that it has come to this, your life is likely at an end. Hahaha! It serves you right. Now, let us return.'

秋山別は此樹下に一夜を明かす折しも、遥の方より宣伝歌の声が聞え来るにぞ、秋山別は雀躍し、後ふり返り能く見れば、モリスは只一人何か手に采配様の者を握り、之を打ふり打ふり此方に向つて進み来る。秋山別は地獄で神に会ひし心地にて、雀躍しつつ待ちゐたりしが、近寄つて来るを、能く能く見ればモリスにあらで、夜前の化爺、体を少し小さくして、モリスの声色を使ひながら来れるなりけり。されど秋山別はモリスとのみ深く信じて少しも疑はず、飛びつく様に、

『ヤア、モリスどこへ往つて居つたのだい、随分待ち詫びたよ。夜前も夜前とて、此木の下に寝て居れば、それはそれは厭らしい化爺が出て来よつてナ、流石の俺も荒肝を潰したよ。併し俺の取つときの言霊を発射したのに驚き、小さくなつて逃げよつた時の愉快さと云つたら、有つたものぢやないワ、アハヽヽヽ』

モリスに見えた男、

『そうか、ソリヤ愉快だつたネイ。イヤ気味が悪かつただらうネ。併し今日はお前の一番怖い者をドツサリ持つて来てやつたから、マア昨夜の返礼だ。ゆつくり楽むがよからうよ』

と云ひ乍ら、麻の様な物を左右左にプツプツプツと振りまはす。小さい玉の様な物が、幾百ともなく落つる途端に、何れも一時に爆発し、中から桃太郎が生れた様に、何百とも知れぬ紅井姫が現はれて、秋山別の前後左右に取りつき、

『コレコレまうし秋山別さまお前は情ない人だよ。能うマア私を見捨ててこンな所迄逃げて来やしやつたは本当に憎らしいワ』

と云つて頬辺たをピシヤツと叩き、耳を引かき、そこら中をひねりまはす。又同じ姿の女、秋山別の足にしがみつき、

『お前は本当に罪な人、私が国依別さまに、あれ丈恋して居るのに、好かぬたらしい、横恋慕をして、一も取らず二も取らずにして了つたぢやないか。エヽ恋の敵ぢや、秋山別さま！』

と云ひ乍ら、拳を一口クワツとかぶり取る。又一人の女は武者振りつき、

『エヽ残念々々、お前故に、私はシーズン河へ身を投げたのだよ。敵を討たずにおくものか！』

と髻を掴み、無性矢鱈に引まはす其痛さ。秋山別は声を限りに悲鳴をあげ、

『痛い痛い、怺へてくれ。モウ是丈女攻めに会うてはやりきれないワ。命がなくなる、どうぞ助けてくれ！』

と泣き声になつて呼ばはつて居る。数多の女は又もや武者ぶりつき、

『女にかけたら、命も何にも要らぬと云つたぢやないか。お前の心が生ンだ紅井姫、サア命を貰はう。妾の手にかかつて死ンだら得心でせう。コレ秋山別さま、黒い色男には生れて来ぬものぢやなア。ホヽヽヽヽ痛いか痛いか、チト痛いとても辛抱なされ。可愛い女につつかれたり、囓ぶりつかれたり、体一面抓られるのは、男として天下第一の光栄でせう。……コレコレ甲乙丙丁戊己の紅井姫さま、寄つて集つて此男を苛めてやらうぢやありませぬか。あゝ面白い面白い』

と云ひ乍ら何百人とも知れぬ女が、交る交る頭を叩き、髪をひつたくり、鼻を抓まみ、耳を引つかき、手足にかぢりつく其苦しさ。遂に秋山別は堪りかねて、其場に昏倒したりける。モリスに見えた男、大口あけて、

『アハヽヽヽ、偉相に昨夜は俺に言霊を発射し、苦めよつた其返報がやしぢや。此奴は何にも世の中に恐いものはないが、女が一番恐いと吐しよつたので、女で仇敵討をしてやつたのだ。モウ斯うなれば、命もあろまい。ハヽヽヽヽ、好い気味だナ。サア帰らう』

§ 2

No sooner had he struck with his saihai again than the amata women vanished as if in a dream, and the kuwaibutsu, too, disappeared like smoke before one knew when it had happened. Akiyamawake breathed a sigh of relief, exposed his bakazura, and looked around here and there, but when the voice of the sendenka was heard once more, Akiyamawake was startled and stood up again; looking closely, he saw it was Morris. 'You have appeared again, have you,' he said, his eyes blazing with anger, and holding his breath in his saikatanden, he clasped his morote and took a stance against Morris, but Morris, who had approached the jiyuka, saw this behavior and said,

Morris: 'Hey, so you were here. I thought you had scattered somewhere and was worried about you. Well, it is good that you are safe. However, I met an obake of Kurenaihime on the way and was tested quite severely.'

Upon hearing this, Akiyamawake said,

Akiyamawake: 'Ottodokkoi, you bakemono, I will not fall for that trick.'

Saying this, he took a stance, clasped his morote, faced Morris, groaned with all his might, 'Uun, uun,' and repeated 'Hifumi-yomitsuki' with itsushiyōkenmei. Morris, not understanding what it was all about, said,

Morris: 'Hey Akiyamawake, what is that? Why don't you stop the Kototama for a while? I have something I want to talk to you about.'

Akiyamawake: 'Do not talk nonsense, do not talk nonsense. You say to stop the Kototama, but do you think I will stop it? I will fire it even more intensely.'

Saying this, he repeated 'Hifumi-yomitsuki' once again.

Morris: 'Hey, have you gone mad? Do you not recognize me? I am Morris.'

Akiyamawake: 'Do not make a fool of me. Even if you think you can torment me by bringing out women again, I will not fall for that trick. Kannagara tamachi haemaese, Hifumi-yomitsuki...'

He was sweating profusely and chanting the number song. Morris thought,

Morris: 'This fellow must have been blown away by the gale, lost his wits, and gone insane. If I splash some water on his head, he will surely come to his senses.'

Whispering this in a low voice, he grabbed Akiyamawake's hand tightly, dragged him forcibly to the side of the mountain stream, and with one hand, splashed the valley water incessantly over his head and face, to which Akiyamawake said,

Akiyamawake: 'You beast, what are you doing? After making me face so many women, do you intend to subject me to a water torture as well? Very well, I have my own plans. I will take revenge on you soon. My sworn brother Morris will be here soon, so just you wait, I will have him avenge me.'

Morris: 'Hey Akiyamawake, pull yourself together. I am Morris. Look at my face carefully, there is no mistake that I am Morris...'

As he thrust his black face forward in front of Akiyamawake, Akiyamawake stared intently at Morris's face and said,

Akiyamawake: 'Aha, you have transformed well. You look exactly like Morris. If you have such skill in transforming, how about you repent and become my disciple?'

Morris: 'You do not have to say such things now. We are like brothers. If you insist that I become your disciple, I do not mind doing so until you come to your senses.'

Akiyamawake: 'Do not transform into a white-haired old man like you did last night. And also, if you bring out so many Kurenaihime, I have too many gates to guard and will be in trouble with what to do.'

Morris thought that he was a fellow who said things that did not make sense... He must be a bit delirious, it would not be good to oppose him too much... Thinking this in his heart, he humored him appropriately, and they proceeded to climb toward Mount Bōshi.
