Chapter 22: 比丘 [1430]

比丘〔1430〕

§ 1

While assigning the six guests to Thomas and having them attentively received by a Cupid of Samorinokami, Samorinokami cheerfully waited upon the living room of Setsuteiri.

Setsuteiri was lying on the sofa and being cared for by Hirunahime; since he was somewhat on the mend, his complexion had suddenly improved, and he was smiling.

Samorinokami presented both hands and said, 'Setsuteirisama, it seems your condition has improved; I too, upon beholding your august countenance, have somehow felt my spirits grow buoyant. As your subsequent recuperation is of the utmost importance, please take good care of yourself.'

Setsuteiri: 'Outside the window, the trees in the garden are dancing naturally as the wind sways them. The cool summer breeze plays natural music and comforts my heart. Indeed, the body in illness is a suffering thing, and I had not found this bounty of nature so delightful, but now that my spirits are so improved, every single thing seems exceptionally interesting again.'

Samorinokami: 'Indeed so. The way the wind strikes the garden trees and plays natural music is just like the tone of a clavichord. An allegretto mood floats in the air, does it not.'

Setsuteiri: 'Samorinokami, will you not tell me some rare story?'

Samorinokami: 'Yes, I have no particularly rare tale, but please do not worry about the matters of the princess. It is said that surely by the grace of God she will return before many days. That your illness has progressed to an allegretto state is entirely due to the earnest efforts of Bankobewake-sama, the missionary of Izumokyo. Bankobewake-sama troubled himself greatly on account of our lord's distress and came here saying it was the command of an angel. From that very hour, my illness turned toward recovery.'

Setsuteiri: 'What, do you say that Bankobewake-sama of Izumokyo has come? And did you say the princess will return safely before many days?'

Samorinokami: 'Yes, there is not the slightest mistake in the words of that missionary, so please be at ease. Right now I am asking him to rest in my living room. And the reason I wish to tell Setsuteiri-sama a rare story is none other than this. Though it is shameful to speak of it, twenty-five years ago from now I made a maidservant pregnant, gave birth to a boy, named him Montes, and sent him off to the house of a Sudra. That very son, accompanied by a splendid wife, has come into the castle just now along with the missionary Bankobewake, had a parent-and-child reunion, and has just wept tears of joy with all his might. Oh, I have done such a worthless thing and feel quite ashamed.'

Setsuteiri: 'That was fortunate. Montes must have rejoiced greatly. You too must have been immensely happy. Ah, speaking of that, what has become of the princess whom I sent out as a foster child to the village of Chinu? Is she still safe and alive in this world? As I grow older, my spirit seems to have weakened, and even the matter of the child whom I gave to commoners and severed ties with comes to mind; at least while I have breath in me, I somehow want to meet her once, but if I hear vaguely, the parents of her adoptive home have already left this world, and the whereabouts of the daughter are unknown, they say. Surely she is suffering.'

So saying, he hung his head in dejection. Samorinokami also shed tears as he spoke,

Samorinokami: 'My lord, if by any chance the princess were safe in this world, would you meet her gladly?'

Setsuteiri: 'Though the ties are cut, we are parent and child. I want to meet my daughter somehow. I must meet her and apologize to her. Ah, what a pitiable thing I did.'

Hiruna: 'Samoridono, if we ask the missionary Bankobewake-sama, would the princess's whereabouts not be found? I would like you to ask him for us. Since our lord is also yearning for the princess so greatly, please go ahead and ask him.'

Samorinokami: 'The truth of the matter is an awe-inspiring thing, but she has become the wife of my son Montes, and wearing splendid attire, the husband and wife together have come to worship at Tamunomiya, and they are now resting in my living room together with the missionary.'

刹帝『それは結構だつた。定めてモンテスも喜んだであらうなア。御前も嘸嬉しかつただらう。アアそれに就いても思ひ出すのは、チヌの里へ里子にやつた姫は何うなつたであらう。未だ無事で此世に生きて居るであらうか。年が寄るにつけて気が弱つたと見え、民間に与へて縁を切つた子供の事までが思ひ出され、せめて息ある内に一度何とかして会ひたいものだが、仄に聞けば養家の両親は早くも世を去り、娘の行方は知れぬといふ事だ。定めて難儀をして居るであらう』

と憮然として首垂れる。左守も涙を流し乍ら、

左守『吾君様、姫様がモシヤ此世に御無事で居られましたならば、貴方は快く御会ひなさいますか』

刹帝『久離切つても親子だ。どうかして一度娘に会ひたいものだ。会うて娘に詫をせねばなるまい。アア可哀相な事をしたものだ』

ヒルナ『左守殿、万公別の宣伝使様に御尋ね致したら姫様の所在が分りはせよまいかな。一つ願つて貰ひたいものだな。吾君様も大変に姫様に憬がれてゐられますから、どうか一つ願つて見て下さいなア』

左守『実の所は恐れ多い事で厶りまするが、私の伜モンテスの妻となり、立派な服装をして夫婦仲よく玉の宮へ御参拝になり、宣伝使と共に今私の居間に休んでゐられます』

§ 2

Setsutei: 'What, is the princess inside the castle? And is she married to my son? That is ketsukō, ketsukō; this too must be some innen. I want to meet the princess as soon as possible, even if it is only a moment.'

Samori: 'If it causes no osashitsukahe, I shall sugusama attend you as your otomo.'

Hiruna: 'My kimisama, I shall go and omuka her, so please wait shibaraku. Now, Samori-dono, let us go.'

With that, Princess Hiruna, feeling very isoiso, received Setsutei's permission and proceeded into the guest chamber (kiyakuma). Mankōwake was right in the middle of praying earnestly together with five men and women for the recovery of Setsutei's biyōki and the safe return (kijiyō) of Princess Daiya. Princess Hiruna stood outside the sliding paper door (fusuma) and waited together with Samori until the prayer was completed. Timing the moment, Hiruna swiftly slid open the fusuma, bowed politely with both hands on the floor,

Hiruna: 'Master propagator of the Izumo Sangokyo, you have so kindly helped save my kimisama's illness. Thank you very much. In regard to that, Setsutei-sama says he wishes to meet Omin's person once, so won't everyone please come together to the living room?'

Mankō: 'Ah, you are Princess Hiruna-sama; first of all, I am so glad that you are safe. Oh, we are already receiving your great care. Now, everyone, let us go following behind the princess.'

Urging everyone onward, the six of them in a file followed behind Samori and Hiruna, and proceeded into the king's living room.

Mankō: 'Setsutei-sama, this spring I enjoyed your care for a long time together with the master. I became an adopted son of the village headman in Tamaki Village, and having brought along my wife, we made a pilgrimage to Tama no Miya, whereupon the angels Takayasuhiwako and Takamitsuhiko suddenly descended and kindly informed us of the cause of Setsutei's illness and the whereabouts of the princess, so we came to visit for a moment.'

Setsutei: 'Thank you very much for taking such great trouble.'

Mankō: 'This person is the king's own discarded heir, Omin-san of Chinu Village. She has come on a pilgrimage to Tama no Miya combined with her honeymoon.'

Setsutei: 'Ah, so you were the princess. I am so glad you came back safely. It was my fault that, though born inside the castle, I let you fall into the house of a shuda. Please forgive me. You were called Princess Tamate, were you not?'

Omin: 'Yes, I am Princess Tamate. Father, I am so glad you are safe. I wanted to meet you.'

With that, tears were falling in a torrent from both eyes. Setsutei also raised his body, grasped Princess Tamate's hands, was lost in tears of joy, and for a short while, without a word, they clung to each other and sobbed.

At this juncture, Taruman, Ex, and Haruna, who had finished their worship at Tama no Miya in a hurry and returned, came forth, and placing both hands on the floor,

Taruman: 'I speak to Setsutei-sama. Princess Daiya has been escorted by ascetics and has just now returned safely. Congratulations.'

Setsutei: 'Ah, if happy things pile up, they just keep piling up. Now, quickly show Daiya and the ascetics in here.'

Taruman alone answered 'Yes' and left the spot, and after a short while, accompanied by Princess Daiya and four ascetics, entered looking very isoiso,

Taruman: 'My kimisama, Princess Daiya has returned.'

Setsutei: 'Oh, you are Princess Daiya, I am so glad you came back safely. Where on earth have you been?'

Daiya: 'Yes, wishing to pray for the complete recovery of my father's illness, while I was taking water ablutions at the Kiyotaki Waterfall of Mt. Terukuni, where I am accustomed to living, both Berts and Shell, the former right guardsmen, appeared and spoke unreasonably, and in the end both sides tried to kill me, so just as I was defending and fighting using an oak root as a shield, the sound of a conch horn echoing and startling the mountain spirits grew closer and closer, and at the same time four ascetics appeared, saved me from my peril, and escorted me all the way here. Please express your thanks to them.'

§ 3

Setsutei: 'I know not what mountain ascetics you may be, but you have indeed graciously saved my daughter. Come, pray rest yourselves at your leisure.'

Chidau: 'You may not remember me, but I was formerly Oniharuwake, the general of Brahmanism. These three are Kumehiko, Supāru, and Emishi, but receiving the teachings of Harukuniwake-sama, we awakened to the Bodaishin and became ascetics; changing our names, I am now Chidaukoji, Kumehiko is Daukuwankoji, Supāru is Sodaukoji, and Emishi is Kiudaukoji, and as we went blowing our conch shells to go for ascetic practice at the purification waterfall of Terukuniyama, when we climbed up we beheld the distress of the Hime-sama, straightway scattered the evildoers, and have escorted them safely hither.'

To this full and detailed account, Setsutei and everyone else were startled as though struck dumb, and looking into one another's faces could not utter a word for a while. Setsutei, having met again the two princesses whom he had almost given up as lost to all hope of reunion, with tears of joy floating in his eyes, pressed his palms together, and began to offer up a prayer of thanksgiving to the Great Deity of Aza-no-Oshie. The official Samori-no-Tsukasa, in the joy of meeting his own child, likewise joined his palms and offered words of gratitude. Haruna met her unexpected brother Montes, declared their sibling bond, and rejoiced with inspired courage. Tamatehime met her father, and unexpectedly meeting her younger sister Daiyahime as well, was choked with tears of delight.

Now the four ascetics Chidau, Daukuwan, Sodau, and Kiudaudan became the guardians of the Palace of Jewels at the request of Setsutei, shaved their heads, propagated the teaching of Aza across the four quarters, and going on pilgrimage tours to various regions in turn, devoted their whole lives to the salvation of sentient beings. These four were the pioneers who shaved off their hair and went about propagating the teaching. And since it began from the Palace of Jewels in the land of Biku, in later times holy men who shaved their heads and wore robes to propagate the teaching came to be called bhikshus. Ah, may the spirits ever bless us and keep us in divine peace.

刹帝『何れの修験者か存じませぬが、よくまあ娘を救けて下さいました。サア、何卒御緩りと御休息下さいませ』

治道『拙者は御見忘れになつたか知りませぬが、元はバラモン教のゼネラル鬼春別で厶います。此三人は久米彦、スパール、エミシで厶いますが、治国別様の御教を承り、菩提心を起し修験者となり、私は治道居士、久米彦は道貫居士、スパールは素道居士、エミシは求道居士と名を改め、照国山の清めの滝に修業に参らむと法螺貝を吹き鳴らし、上りて見れば姫様の御遭難、直様悪者を追散らし、此処迄送つて参りました』

と一伍一什の物語に、刹帝利を始め一同はアツと許りに驚き、互に顔を見合せて少時言葉も出なかつた。刹帝利は殆ど会見絶望と諦め居たりし二人の姫に廻り会ひ、嬉し涙を浮べ乍ら、両手を合せて、三五教の大神に感謝の祈願を奏上し始めた。左守の司も吾子に会ひし嬉しさに、同じく合掌し感謝の辞を奉つてゐる。ハルナは思ひも寄らぬ兄のモンテスに会つて兄弟の名乗りを上げ悦び勇む。玉手姫は父に逢ひ、又妹のダイヤ姫に思はず面会して歓喜の涙に咽んでゐる。

偖治道、道貫、素道、求道の四人の修験者は刹帝利の依頼に依つて玉の宮の守護役となり、頭を丸めて三五の教を四方に宣伝し、代る代る各地に巡錫して衆生済度に一生を捧たり。頭髪を剃り落し教を宣伝に廻つたのは、此四人が嚆矢である。而してビクの国の玉の宮から始まつたのだから、後世頭を丸め衣を着て宣伝する聖者を比丘と名づくる事となつたのである。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
