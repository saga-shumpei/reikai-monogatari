Chapter 19: Pure 滝 [1427]

清滝〔1427〕

§ 1

The blazing sun in the Amatsumisora shone brilliantly over the valley of Terukunidake, where a high, grand Daibakufu, the purification waterfall, flowed. Near the edge of it, building a small hut, two men stripped naked morning and evening, praying at the top of their lungs for something.

These two men were the master and servant, Berutsu and Shieru. Having been stripped of the office of Umori by Samorinokami and Taruman, sentenced to one hundred days of house arrest, and harboring a grudge that pierced to their Kotsuzui, they learned the Mahafu of Eugenbau to overthrow Victoria Castle, regain their power, make Berutsu a Setsuteiri, appoint Shieru as Samorinokami, and seize the sovereignty of the country, thus performing water ablutions with undivided attention. On the seventh night, as the two were desperately performing their water ablutions, amidst a deafening roar as if the mountains were collapsing, hoofbeats resounded from the empty sky as someone rode down on a white horse, appearing in the guise of a Buddhist monk clad in crimson robes. This was a demon god known as Yuzawabō, rumored to be a sworn brother of Eugenbau. Yuzawabō faced the two and said,

Yuzawabō: 'You must be Berutsu, who served as the Umorikami of the land of Biku, and his steward Shieru. Your wish shall be granted speedily. Accordingly, you must perform water austerities for one hundred days and one hundred nights, eating only crabs, salamanders, and frogs inhabiting this valley river as your food, and you must consume absolutely nothing else. If you mistakenly take other food, your ascetic practice will come to nothing. Also, during the hundred days of practice, if you are discovered by people, your painstaking practice will be rendered invalid, so you must be ever watchful. Once this severe penance is finished, I shall bestow upon you the art of aerial flight and teach you the law of transforming into myriad forms; doubt it not for a single moment.'

So he spoke solemnly, and shaking the mountains, he turned his horse's head once more and vanished high into the sky. Drowned in tears of gratitude, the two pressed their palms together toward Yuzawabō's receding figure and chanted spells. When they had practiced for about thirty days, whether poisoned by the frogs and salamanders, Berutsu suddenly suffered a stomachache, flailing his limbs and foaming at the mouth. Shieru desperately said,

Shieru: 'Oralahiko-nomikoto, Yuzawabō-sama, please heal my master's illness.'

Thus battered by the waterfall basin, he once again engaged in severe penance with undivided attention. Berutsu grasped at the empty air and writhed in agony. Seeing this state, Shieru plunged into the waterfall basin at the risk of his life and concentrated his prayers. Thereupon, a lovely maiden of eleven or twelve years climbed up briskly, parting the thicket of trees, and suddenly plunged stark naked into the waterfall basin. Thinking that an angel had heard his prayers and come to save him, Shieru desperately prostrated himself before the maiden's figure, drowning in tears of gratitude. Paying no attention to the two men, the maiden plunged into the waterfall basin and prayed with undivided attention,

Maiden: 'Great Ōkunitokotochi no Ōkami, I beseech you, please save my father's illness. Even if the life of my own body should be taken, I do not feel troubled in the least. If my father were to pass away now, I know not what the master and servant, Umorikami Berutsu, might do once again. It is a matter of grave crisis for the land of Biku.'

Thus she uttered the divine words and began to pray. However, blocked by the thundering roar of the waterfall, what the maiden was wishing for did not reach the ears of the two men. Shieru advanced to Berutsu's side, stroked his head, and said,

Shieru: 'Oh, master, please set your mind at ease. When I just now asked Yuzawabō, look, right there, a heavenly maiden has descended from heaven and is standing in the waterfall basin praying for the healing of your illness. It is surely an auspicious sign that your illness will be cured. Please do not worry at all. Namu Yuzawabō Daimyōjin, protect and bless us.'

Thus he prayed, mixed with tears. Strangely enough, upon hearing these words, whether due to a neural reaction or not, Berutsu's condition suddenly improved. Raising his head and looking at the waterfall basin, he saw a lovely maiden surpassing flowers, beaten by the waterfall, exposing her white body, and concentrating with undivided attention. Forgetting even his own pain, Berutsu stood up.

ダイヤ『此照国山は妾兄妹六人が永らく住居してゐた馴染のある所だ。父の御病気を平癒させむが為に、清めの滝へ水垢離をとりに来たのだよ。臣下の身分として主人のする事をゴテゴテいふ権利があるか、控えて居れ。年は若く共、ビクの国刹帝利の娘だ。エエ汚らはしい、一時も早くどつかへ姿を隠せ。執拗帰らぬに於ては線香を立てて燻べてやらうか』

シエール『丸切り青大将が座敷へ這上つた時のやうに言つてゐやがる。こんな女つちよに脅迫されて、此荒男の顔が立つものか、地異天変もここ迄行けば極端だ。地震ゴロゴロ雷ビリビリとやつて来たやうだ。併し乍らどう考へても、こんな美しい女をムザムザ殺すのは勿体ない様だ。オイ、ダイヤさま、物も一つ相談だが、何程お前が王女だといつても、位＠の高いのは実地の時の間に合ふものでない。荒男二人と格闘すれば、到底お前は殺されねばなるまい。蛇と蛙のやうなものだから、茲は一つ思案をし直して、旦那様の奥方となり、ビクの国の女王となつて暮す考へはないか』

ダイヤ『悪逆無道の謀叛人奴、エエ汚らはしい、下りおらう』

ベルツ『何と云つても美しい者だ。そしてこれ丈の胆力があれば、此女を女房にすればどんな事でも出来るだらう。イヤ、ダイヤ姫様、茲は一つお考へ直しを願ひます。左守といふ奴は表面忠義らしく見せて居りますが、彼こそ心中深く野心を包蔵する曲者で厶いますぞ。刹帝利様は左守に誤られ、ビクの国家を棒に振らうとして厶る。危険至極な今日の場合。真の忠臣が現はれて支へなくては、万代不易の王家は続きますまい……大忠は不忠に似たり、大孝は不孝に似たり、大信は偽りに似たり、大善は大悪に似たり……といふ事がありませう。表面大悪人と見做されたる此ベルツ位、王家や国家を憂ひて居る者は厶いませぬぞ。チツと冷静に胸に手を当てて、王家と国家の為にお考へを願ひ度いものです。よく考へて御覧なさい。貴女の父上は左右の奸臣に誤られ、大切な五人の王子迄悉皆殺さうとなさつたぢやありませぬか。何処の国に親が子を愛せない者がありませう。何が宝だと云つても、吾子位宝はない。其宝を殺さうとなさるのだから、決して之はお父上の心から出たのでは厶いませぬ、皆左守やタルマンの入れ知恵で厶りまするぞ。かやうな悪人を重用するは実に危険千万で厶りまする。貴方はお若いので、城内の様子を御存じ厶いますまいが、それはそれはタルマン、キユービツトの両人は天地容れざる大悪人で厶いますよ。何卒此急場を救ふ為に、幸貴方は王家のお血筋、此右守と夫婦になり、国家の大難を未然に防ぐお考へはありませぬか』

§ 4

Daiya: 'Hey, I have no ears to listen to your crooked and deceitful arguments with your endless chatter; it is filthy. Whatever happens to the royal family, or whatever happens to the state, do not concern yourself with it. Everything is the season of Heaven. It is not something for the likes of you, the mere Sanmiao tribes, to interfere with. Much obliged for your unsolicited care, so withdraw and sit down.'

Thus he declared with stern majesty. Belz and Shier said,

'It is no longer any use. Both of you, close in from left and right, and though it is pitiable, let us put them to death.'

So saying, they drew their great swords and slashed in from both sides, but Daiya dodged his body, slipped beneath the blades like a flying bird, and taking an oak tree as a wooden shield, defended himself and fought on.

Just as this was happening, blowing conch shells with loud sounds of buu-buu-buu, four Yamabushi,

'Shiujiyōhikonyaku, Muriyōkuhitsushin, Kuwannonmeuchiriki, Nōgusekenku, Gusokujintsūriki, Kōshūchihōben, Jippōshokukokudo, Musatsufugenshin, Shūjushokakushu, Jigokugichikushō, Shōrōbyōshiku, Ianjiushitsuryōmetsu'

chanting the Kanon Sutra, came climbing up. The two men, Belz and Shier, startled by the appearance of the four, abandoned Daiya, tucked up their robes, and fled toward the mountaintop through the thick growth of thorns, vanishing like clouds and mist. These Yamabushi were none other than the four ascetics: Harumichi, Dōkan, Sodō, and Gudō.

と厳然として言ひ放つた。ベルツ、シエールは、

『衆生被困厄、無量苦逼身、観音妙智力、能救世間苦、具足神通力、広修智方便、十方諸国土、無刹不現身、種々諸悪趣、地獄鬼畜生、生老病死苦、以漸悉令滅』
