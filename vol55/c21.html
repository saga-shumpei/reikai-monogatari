Chapter 21: 嬉涙 [1429]

嬉涙〔1429〕

§ 1

Thomas appeared again in the reception room,

Thomas: 'Yaa, Mankōwake-sama and all of you, now that you are gathered together, please come to the room of Samori. As great worry has also arisen for Samorisama, if you would perhaps let us hear even your stories of past experiences, it will doubtless distract his mind a little. Come, I will show you the way. Please come along.'

Mankō: 'Good, old man Samori bought Mankōwake cheap, didn't he? Saying everyone come together, well, I will go. Now, lead the way, everyone please follow me. For three couples together to pass all the way to the inner chamber of Victoria Castle, with the son of Rishō in the village of Tamaki passing by, is an extraordinary thing. This too is, after all, because of the lingering radiance of Mankōwake.'

So saying, following behind Thomas, they threaded their long corridors and advanced into the living quarters of Samorinokami. Mankō, facing Samori,

Mankō: 'Well, well, Samori no Kyūbitto-dono, I have not seen you for a while. We set out together as three couples on a honeymoon, and on our way back from worshiping at Tamanomiya, thinking it would not do unless we went up to pay our respects once, we somehow slipped past the gatekeeper who was making a fuss and have come right up to here. You have aged quite a bit, haven't you? Has not white hair sprouted in abundance?'

Samori: 'Hahahahaha, you are all very welcome. Now, Mankō-sama, my white hair has been growing since twenty years ago; did you just notice it now? And though I know not where you are serving, you are accompanying a beautiful young lady, but you should consider what befits your station and refrain from displaying such ambition as you have hitherto shown.'

Mankō put his hand on Sugaru's shoulder,

Mankō: 'Hehehehehe. I say, Samorisama. How is it regarding Princess Daiya? My wife is, well, just like this.'

Samori: 'Now, now, Mankō-sama, once again you are lacking in discretion. Is there such a thing as taking your master's young lady and treating her as your wife? Exercise a little discretion.'

Mankō: 'Hen, excuse me, hey Sugaru, tell him from yourself so that Samorisama's doubts may be cleared. Truly, everyone alike buys me cheap and makes a fool of me.'

Sugaru: 'Samorisama, I have the honor of meeting you for the first time. I am called Sugaru, the younger sister of Temusu from the village of Tamaki. I was captured by the Brahman army and was suffering in the rock cave of Ikurayama when Harikunowake-sama's party came out and saved me. Among them, this Mankō-sama is actually called Mankōwake-sama and is the master of Harikunowake-sama, but he intentionally masquerades as a subordinate and says only comical things. I became the wife of Mankōwake's missionary through the matchmaking of Harikunowake-sama, and on my way back from visiting Tamanomiya to give thanks, I have come to pay my respects along with my husband. Please do not abandon us, and henceforth treat us with kind familiarity. And this person is called Shīna-san, and is the husband of this older sister of mine named Sumieru. The other couple is called Āshisu-sama and Otami-san; these two are also a newlywed couple.'

Samori: 'Dear me, I had quite misjudged the situation. Missionary-sama of Mankōwake, you have indeed taken the trouble to visit. However, there is something I earnestly wish to ask of you; will you not listen?'

Mankō: 'You are worried, no doubt, because you do not know the illness of Chatriya-sama and the whereabouts of Daiya-san.'

Samori, astonished,

Samori: 'Yes, just as you surmise. How on earth did you come to know such a thing?'

Mankō: 'No matter what, I am the supreme missionary of Izumo-shinji, Mankōwake. Even matters a thousand ri away are thus neatly understood while sitting here. However, I just heard this briefly in the castle town. Ahahahahaha, to tell the truth, I know it no better than looking at things with a thin piece of paper held to my face.'

と云ひ乍ら、トマスの後に跟いて長い廊下を潜り、左守司の居間に進み入つた。万公は左守に向ひ、

『これはこれは左守のキユービツト殿、暫くお目にかかりませぬ。吾々は三夫婦揃うて新婚旅行と出掛け、玉の宮への参拝の帰り途、一度御挨拶に上らないでは済まないと思ひ、門番がゴテつくのをやつと潜つて此処迄参りました。随分貴方も年が寄りましたねえ。白髪がどつさり生えたぢやありませぬか』

左守『ハハハハハ、皆さま好くお出なさいませ。時に万公さま、拙者の白髪は二十年前から生えて居るのぢや、お前さま今気がついたか。そして何処に奉公して居るか知らないが、綺麗な娘さまのお伴して居るが、身分相応と云ふ事を考へて今迄のやうな野心を出さないやうにしなさい』

万公はスガールの肩に手をかけ、

『ヘヘヘヘヘ。もし左守様。ダイヤ姫様とはどうで厶いますな。私の女房は、マアザツト此通りで厶います』

左守『これこれ万公さま、又してもお前さまは心得の悪い。主人のお嬢様を捉まへて女房扱ひをすると云ふ事がありますか。些と心得なさい。』

万公『ヘン、済みまへんなア、おいスガール、左守様に疑の晴れるやうにお前から言つて呉れ。本当に誰も彼も俺を安く買つて馬鹿にして居るからな』

スガール『左守様、初めてお目に懸ります。私は玉置の村のテームスが妹娘スガールと申すもので厶います。バラモン軍に捉へられ猪倉山の岩窟で苦しんで居ました所を、治国別様一行がお出なさつてお助け下さつたのです。中にもこの万公さまは実は万公別様と申まして治国別様のお師匠さまですが、ワザとに部下と化けて剽軽の事許り云つてお出なさるので厶います。私は治国別様の御媒酌によつて万公別の宣伝使と夫婦になり、玉の宮へお礼に参りましたその帰りがけ、夫と共にお伺ひしました。何卒お見捨てなく今後はお心易く願ひます。そして此方はシーナさまと申し、スミエルと云ふ此姉の夫で厶います。も一組はアーシスさま、お民さまと申しましてこれも新夫婦で厶います』

左守『イヤ、どうも見違ひを致して居りました。万公別の宣伝使さま、よくマアお尋ね下さいました。併し折入つてお願ひ申度い事で厶いますが、聞いては下さいますまいか』

万公『刹帝利様の御病気とダイヤ様の行衛が分らないので御心配なさつて居るのでせうがな』

左守は驚いて、

左守『ハイ、お察しの通りで厶います。どうしてマアそんな事がお分りになりましたか』

万公『何と云つても三五教切つての大宣伝使万公別で厶います。千里先方の事でも斯うして居つてチヤンと分つて居るのですからなア。併し乍ら此事は城下で一寸聞いて来たのですよ。アハハハハ、本当の事云へば薄紙を顔に当てて物を見る位より分りませぬわい』

§ 2

Samori: 'Jiyōdan aside, what is your view on the illness of Mankōwake-sama and Setsuteirisama?'

Mankō: 'Ah, the truth of the matter is that while I was visiting the palace of Tama and in the midst of my prayers, two angels named Takayasuhiko and Takateruhiko descended before unworthy me and said, "Since Setsuteirisama is being afflicted by the malevolent spirits of Bělcz and Sieyr, you must quickly enter Hofus and go to her rescue. Furthermore, since Daiya Hime-sama is being tormented for the sake of the two and her very life is in a perilous place, she will be saved by four shugenja and will soon return, so let her know not to worry..." That is how it is. Therefore, regardless of my discourtesy, I have intruded here accompanied by six persons.'

Samori: 'Indeed, during Tarman's inquiry he was saying just such a thing. There can be no mistake about it. Ah, thank you so much. Would it by any chance be possible for you to perform Chinkon right away, if you would be so kind, for the healing of Setsuteirisama's illness?'

Mankō: 'Even if I, your humble servant, do not go separately to Setsuteirisama's private chambers, the moment Mankōwake entered this castle, overawed by the divine virtue, the two evil spirits vanished like clouds and mist. Before long they will come out here with smiles on their faces. And Daiya Hime-sama, too, will be escorted by the shugenja and will return here, so first of all please calm down and compose yourselves.'

Samori: 'Yes, thank you very much. With that I feel somewhat reassured. By the way, you who are called Āshisu, you somehow resemble my son Haruna in no small way; might I ask you to let me hear the story of your upbringing?'

Āshisu: 'Yes,'

Having said just that, she is already letting tears fall in a steady stream.

Mankō: 'Oh, Āshisu-sama, how faint-hearted you are, what are you crying for? Why do you not announce your name proudly?'

Āshisu: 'Yes, but for some reason I find it hard to speak.'

Mankō: 'Tell me, Samori-sama, is Haruna-sama the only child you have?'

Samori: 'Yes, well, I have only him.'

Mankō: 'To say you have only him, is that not somewhat ambiguous? Did you not steal into your wife's eyes, sneak into the maid's room, do something or other, and cause a belly to swell?'

Samori: 'Yes, to be sure, in my youth there were various improper things. I am too ashamed to speak of it.'

Mankō: 'Tell me, if your unrecognized child were alive and well today, would you gladly meet him? Nay, would you acknowledge him as parent and child? I should like to hear this as a preliminary question.'

Samori: 'My wife having passed away, I have grown old just like this, and having had my single son Haruna take a wife, now I feel somewhat at ease for the moment, but there should have been a son named Montes, who was an older brother to Haruna. In view of society, I gave him some money and sent him off to some countryside, but I hear a rumor that because he was an unfortunate son both parents perished and no one knows where he went. Now when I think of it, I truly did a regrettable thing. Having grown this old and not knowing when I shall make my pilgrimage to Heaven, I yearn for the God, wishing at least to meet that son of mine just once while I am still alive. Might it be somehow possible, by means of your divine power, to look into the whereabouts of my son?'

And sniffling his nasal mucus, he wilts limply.

Mankō: 'Tell me, Samori-sama, your esteemed son Montes-sama has a splendid wife and is living splendidly. And that wife of his is no ordinary person. It is a husband-and-wife pair with a disparity in station, as they say like a paper lantern and a temple bell.'

Samori: 'What, my son is living splendidly? That is a thankful thing indeed. And where is he living?'

Mankō: 'Yes, his present whereabouts is in the country of Biku, inside Victoria Castle, in Samori's chambers, where he has come following an esteemed wife named Otami-no-kata and Mankōwake. There, he is this person here.'

左守『何、伜が立派に暮して居りますか、それは有難い事で厶います。さうして何処に居りますかな』

万公『ハイ只今の所在はビクの国、ビクトリア城内、左守の室内に、お民の方と云ふ奥様と万公別に従ひお出になつて居ります。それ、このお方ですよ』

He points at Āshisu. Gazing intently upon the face of Āshisu, Samori speaks,

Samori: 'Ah, were you indeed my son? Thinking you bore some resemblance to Haruna from nowhere in particular, I have harbored suspicions since a while ago. Well, have you remained safe? And who might this person you call your wife be?'

Āshisu: 'Ah, were you indeed my honorable father? Hoping by any means to have an audience with you just once, sleeping or waking I have never had a moment to forget it. Yet having fallen to the status of a lowly Shiyuda, I had resigned myself that meeting the noble Samorisama would ultimately be impossible.'

So saying, he weeps an ignoble man's cry. Applying his sleeve to both eyes and wiping away tears like a sudden summer shower, Samori is so overcome with joy that he cannot utter a single word, and clinging to the body of Āshisu, he sobs loudly.

Stroking the backs of both with her two hands, Omin speaks,

Omin: 'Father, Dannasama, please compose yourselves by all means. Because even I am becoming sorrowful.'

Samori: 'Ah, were you the wife of my son? You have come well. My, what a pretty woman you are. My son must be exceedingly glad as well. I too am happy......'

Once again filling both eyes with tears, he weeps aloud.

Mankō: 'Ey, how unseemly, pull yourselves together firmly. Even Mankō has begun to feel sad. I say, Samorisama, as whose daughter do you suppose this Ominsama is regarded? Reverently enough, she is the hidden child of Setsuteirisama, Tamatehimesama. She is the princess who was sent down to the house of Takusuke in the village of Chinu, and now she goes by the name of Omin.'

Hearing this, Samori is startled, retreats five or six paces, places both hands on the floor and bows his head to the tatami,

Samori: 'Ha-ha, were you indeed the princess? Not knowing, I have committed a rudeness. Ah, how awe-inspiring. I am worried whether it is not the height of presumption that you have become the wife of our lowly son. Please forgive me by all means.'

Omin: 'Father, what are you saying? When you speak of such things, I feel distressed. By all means, do address me familiarly as "Omin, Omin".'

Mankō: 'Come now, since the declaration of parent and child is finished, tears are forbidden; let us sing with a little song.'

As he thus speaks, Princess Karuna gently pushes open the sliding screen and, bowing politely, speaks,

Karuna: 'Honored guests, you have come well. By all means, I pray you take your leisure and rest. By the way, honored Father, the condition of Setsuteirisama has suddenly improved, and he has become vigorous. "Samori is likely worrying, so go quickly and inform him," is the lord's command; please rejoice.'

Samori: 'What, the illness of Setsuteirisama has been healed? Ah, how thankful, how thankful; that this should be so is entirely the divine protection of the gods of Misokyo. Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sakihaimase, Kannagara Tamashii Sakihaimase.'

Clouded over once more with tears of joy, Karuna promptly departs from this place and hurries to the sitting room of Setsuteiri.
