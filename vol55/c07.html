Mankō: 'Nanibun, since I do not understand the state of the household at all, I committed an unexpected shippai. However, do not worry. I will surely have the rice cooked over again, and we servants will bring out the haizen ourselves.'

Mankō: 'No matter what anyone says, I am the Mankōwake of Ananai-kyō.'

A certain renowned personage in the world, flabbergasted by the living Kototama, even the indomitable General Oniharuwake removed his helmet, cast aside his sword, and becoming completely unarmed, with tears streaming down like red pearls, turning his viscera upside down all the while, sobbing bitterly, surrendered quite easily.

Not pursuing those who fled, what profit could there be, he thought, in striking even once with his fist a coward who apologizes, and here, displaying the true nature of magnanimity and broad-mindedness, he transformed the ugly Magami into Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi, re-examining and rehearing a hundred thousand faults, to save and help them being the Way of Heaven, instantly felt it in his utmost pure and immaculate Mitama, and safely and completely the matter was settled.

While spiritually binding the enemy general, sounding songs of victory, he leaped across valleys, squeezed through gaps in rocks, brushed aside groves of cedars, and across the lush green great plain, accompanied by the mountain cuckoo whose voice is heard but whose form is unseen, he made a triumphant return to the village estate mansion of Tamaki, heading towards the Thames side.

Being such a deity of wisdom and bravery, the peerless beauty Sugāru, who had abhorred Oniharuwake and Kumehiko like centipedes, suddenly fell in love with him, and with lightning speed, at a blindingly rapid pace, cast her gaze into his two eyes, heart-to-heart, their emotions and intentions instantly uniting in harmony, becoming a true husband and wife in the cleared divine realms, and the young master of the Thames mansion

became Mankōwake.

O master of Chikunibake, please, I beg of you, grant leave to your pupil Mankōwake, and cause the match between the two of us to be perfectly and intricately bound, I earnestly beseech you.

faced Victoria Castle, once again manifested divine power, rescued the diamond princess, was blocked by the Minister of Saimori, and was somewhat at a loss and nonplussed.

Yet, who could have foreseen it, the Great Deity of Kan-katsu-kaname, the divine grace of the God of Idomu, at last here the two lovers, in no distant interval between them, are about to hold the ceremony of sharing the bedchamber.

Now, for Mankōwake, this is the most crucial and critical moment.

O master of Chikuniwake, until my wedding is concluded, please, oh please, have patience, pretend to be Mankōwake's disciple, gloss over the eyes of the public, and graciously grant this wish of a lifetime, please, I beg of you.

please, you too, have patience, let Mankōwake save face, and there, each of you in a fitting manner, strike the right chord and accommodate us, I earnestly beseech you.
