So singing, the lead singer and the dancers became intoxicated with the sacred sake of the enshrinement ceremony, floated on joy, formed a circle, danced wildly until the dead of night, and grandly concluded the rites of the festival. From this time forth, the four persons Harukuniwake, Michiharuwake, Matsuhiko, and Tatsuhiko bade farewell to the Thames family, and once again hurried their steps toward Jerusalem. Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sakiyabaese.
