Chapter 9: 三婚 [1417]

三婚〔1417〕

§ 1

Through the prayers of Harukuniwake, Matsuhiko, and Tatsuhiko, the four injured persons were able to make a full recovery after three days. Grateful for the divine virtue of Harukuniwake's party and wishing to celebrate the complete recovery of their daughter, Themes and his wife had everyone from Harukuniwake down to the head clerk and the maidservant take their places at a celebratory feast. Harukuniwake sat in the primary seat, on the upper left side Matsuhiko, Tatsuhiko, and Mankō occupied the seats, while on the right side six members of the Brahman group lined up in a row.

Michiharuwake, Sheena, Sumiel, and Sugaru, the group of those fully recovered from illness, sat in the lower seats facing Harukuniwake, forming an order with Themes and his wife, Azumos, and Arsis, and so a celebratory banquet was opened.

It goes without saying that prior to this, Harukuniwake and the rest of the party offered a prayer of gratitude to the divine presence and conducted a solemn rite. Facing Harukuniwake, Themes bowed with both hands on the floor looking overjoyed,

Themes: 'Harukuniwake-sama, Missionary, I know not how to express my gratitude; I am so overwhelmed that words fail me.'

Harukuniwake: 'By the grace of the Kami, Michiharuwake has been saved, and that you have permitted your respected daughter and head clerk to show their safe faces here today is entirely due to the grace of Ōkunitokotachi no Ōkami, who makes us keep the teachings of Izanagi and Izanami, and the numerous Kami. It is by no means our own power. If you offer thanks, it will result in us appropriating the divine virtue of the Kami to ourselves, which causes us distress. We earnestly ask that you say no such words of thanks.'

Themes: 'Hai, thank you very much, Kami. I thank you deeply for granting your divine virtue to our family, precisely through the bodies of Harukuniwake-sama and his party.'

Berishina: 'To all the teachers of the Izanagi-Izanami teachings, as my husband has just said, I truly cannot express my gratitude. With our two daughters, I am finally at ease. Michiharuwake-sama has also made a full recovery, and there is nothing so happy as this.'

Saying this, she let fall tears of joy one after another.

Sumiel: 'Teachers of the Izanagi-Izanami teachings, captured by evil spirits, you saved me from the jaws of death, and I have no words to express my gratitude. This is entirely the result of the kindness of Harukuniwake-sama and everyone in the party.'

Sugaru: 'Shut up in a dark cavern, prepared for death thinking I could never see the light of this world again, I was saved by the grace of the Kami. I earnestly beg you to stay and rest at your leisure and impart wonderful stories to us. In regards to this, I also gratefully thank all the people, beginning with Kishunwake-sama, for the trouble they have taken. I pray that you will all rest at your leisure, and though there is nothing special, please partake of the sake.'

Mankō: 'Wishing to offer some hospitality, we have done various things, but as you know, since this is a remote mountainous place, there is nothing that will quite suit your palates. I earnestly ask you to partake at your leisure. Replacing my parents-in-law, my elder sister Sumiel, as well as Sugaru, I, Mankōwake, respectfully offer my thanks. Kamunagara Tamachihai Mase, Kamunagara Tamachihai Mase.'

Harukuniwake: 'Is this you, young master? Oh, we have received a most magnificent feast and we thank you very much.'

Themes wore a puzzled expression, looked back at Berishina, and lowering his voice,

Themes: 'Hey, Berishina, did you promise to give this missionary as a husband behind my back? Why didn't you say a single word to me? Even if he is a missionary as wonderful as they come, though he looks like a man made of straw, since I am the master of the house, isn't it rather an overreach of authority to decide this without consulting me, the master?'

Berishina: 'No, I know nothing about it. Until this very moment, I thought that you had decided it.'

§ 2

Thames: 'How strange, Senseisama Harukuniwake, what could be the reason for this?'

Harukuni: 'Alas, I do not know either. I have been wondering when Mankōwake, the Daisendenshi, became an adopted son without even informing us, his disciples.'

Mankō: 'Since it is a bond tied by the Chihaya-furu Kami, it is not a matter for humans to know. Following the teachings of the Kami who brings spiritual happiness, I have become the husband of Princess Sugāru.'

Thames: 'How suspicious, even though you call it the words of the Kami, it happened while we, the parents, knew nothing of it.'

Berishina: 'Everything must be a bond tied by the arrangements of the Kami of Idomu. Nevertheless, Kamutsukasa Harukuniwake, what do you think of this bond?'

Mankō: 'To leave everything to the heart of the Kami is known as the path of being human. Though this is a bond that does not dye my heart, I cannot go against the words of the Kami.'

Matsuhiko: 'This is an interesting and unheard-of bond, a surprise of today with no intermediary.'

Tatsuhiko: 'In this present age, there is no distinction between onokōmina, and bonds are tied freely. How does the Kami of Idomu view the state of the world which has become so disordered?'

Mankō: 'My beautiful teacher is Kamunagara; since he is a Kami, he will surely forgive me.'

Thames: 'Kamutsukasa Harukuniwake, please teach us what to do about this bond.'

Harukuniwake: 'Chihaya-furu, from the distant past of the Kamiyo, the Kami-bashira who opened the path of marriage for onokōmina, the Great Kami Izanagi, dwelt in the Aoki-ga-hara of Oto in Tachibana, Hyūga, Tsukushi. Purifying the defilement of his body, he descended to the Jitentō-jima, opened the path of husband and wife, and gave birth to many Kami of the sea, rivers, mountains, and fields. He opened and governed the Mizuho-no-kuni of the fifteen hundred autumns of Ashihara completely and in detail, and caused hundreds, thousands, and ten thousands to be born and multiply upon this land, finishing the precious divine work. Seeing that joy before my eyes, how auspicious it is that it appears in this mansion which had been sunk in sorrow.'

Harukuniwake: 'Since we know that the path of marriage for onokōmina is determined exactly as the eight million Kami in heaven and the eight million Kami on earth decree, you must never trouble your hearts. Shīna-sama, as a child of the house, has served this house for a long time. O faithful believer who has served with such diligence, morning and evening, giving your heart and breaking your body, if you pray for the prosperity of this house, first of all, you should take Princess Sumieru, who was born as the child of the elder sister, as your wife, and tie the bond of husband and wife that not even water could leak through. Next, Princess Sugāru, who was born after, is the Daisendenshi Mankōwake, the parent of life; if you tie the contract of husband and wife with her, it will surely fulfill the heart of the Kami of heaven and earth.'

Harukuniwake: 'Also, let the child of the house of Āshisu tie the contract of husband and wife forever with the beautiful Otami-no-kata. If you keep the precious teachings of Sango purely morning and evening and serve before the Great Kami, the house of Thames in the village of Tamaki will become high and wealthy, and your children will flourish like the eighty branches of the fifty-kashi and eight-kuwa, flourishing in prosperity. Thames and Berishina, the two pillars, Mankōwake, Shīna-sama, and starting with Kamutsukasa Āshisu, Princess Sumieru, Princess Sugāru, and Otami-no-kata, following the teachings of the Chihaya-furu Kami, you must tie the auspicious bond of husband and wife here.'

Harukuniwake: 'Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Before the precious Kami, I, the Kamutsukasa of Harukuni, who serves in the mansion of the Saien, offer this recommendation with a sincere heart. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the great earth sinks, even if the Indian Ocean dries up, now that this bond has been completely tied in detail, may it flourish forever and ever, unchanging for a thousand and eight thousand generations, as long as the life of the tama-no-o, and may it flourish in firmness and eternity. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Before the Kami, I swear this with a sincere heart.'

Sumieru: 'The plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds, opening all at once, the age of the evergreen pine Kami has returned, and it has become the spring of Tokoyo. Born in the house of Thames in the village of Tamaki, I, Sumieru, to protect the house of my ancestors, have decided that Shīna-sama, who is a child of the house, shall be my husband. If I protect the mansion of my father, the house will become increasingly wealthy and prosperous, and the descendants will flourish more and more. Believing that the house of Kamutsukasa Thames will suddenly open into a heavenly pure land, the fruits of my prayers to the Kami morning and evening have appeared. This evening is truly grateful for the divine mediation of the Kamutsukasa of Harukuni of the Sango-kyō.'
