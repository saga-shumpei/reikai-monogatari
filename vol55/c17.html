Chapter 17: 万巌 [1425]

万巌〔1425〕

§ 1

Temes of the village of Tamaki listened to the teachings of Harukuniwake, discarded his hitherto greed and attachment as if casting away worn-out shoes, opened his vast residence to be shared by the villagers, and furthermore offered the mountain forests, fields, and paddies within the village for common use, thus forming a group to manage a new village. First, intending to construct a shrine for Ōkami, the villagers ventured into the distant and near mountains that had hitherto been Temes's private holdings, cut down trees, sawed planks, devoted their sincere hearts day and night, and after a month finally constructed a temporary palace to enshrine Ōkami. Harukuniwake intended to impart the teachings to the villagers and, moreover, to wait according to the divine decree until this divine lodge was completed. Several hundred men and women of all ages, rejoicing and taking courage, gathered before the shrine and took part in this grand ceremony. Harukuniwake became the chief ritualist and offered a proclamation chant of norito-kuzushi facing the shrine.

Harukuniwake: 'In the high plain of heaven of the distant heavenly august sky, residing in tranquility, the Great Deity Ōkuninotokotachi, beginning with the Great Deity Kami Izanagi and the Great Deity Kami Izanami, the Great Deity of the Rigorous Spirit and the Great Deity of the Mild Spirit, before the august presences of the myriad deities of the heavenly deities and earthly deities, I, Harukuniwake, the divine administrator of Izumo Shinri, with reverence, respect, awe, and dread before the pure, noble, and marvelous august presence, proclaim thus: The Great Deity of the Mild Spirit, who governs the moon realm of the high plain of heaven, and the Great Deity of the Rigorous Spirit, who governs the sun realm, shall blow away the cloudings, impurities, sins, and transgressions of the world of flesh with the winds of the passes of storms, sweep and discard them in the rapids of the swift rivers, and re-establish them in the pure and lovely august reign of Miroku; causing the Great Deity Kami Susanoo to bear a thousand seath of depositions, erecting the lodge of the sacred garden in the sacred place of the Mount of Labor, establishing it as a dwelling place for a thousand generations, revering the august words of the deity, and marvelously and completely opening the marvelous teachings in the distant and near lands—beginning with the hundred administrators, the people of the four quarters, rejoicing and looking up to the august grace of the Imperial Great Deity, gathering close before the deity like swift winds and crashing tides, kneading and forging the heart of the village-liver bestowed by the deity, completely and thoroughly fulfilling the duty of the human body born as the pillars of heaven and earth by wiping away and purifying the hundred sins, impurities, and transgressions, the valor of striving and serving; because the luxuriant reed-plain fifteen-hundred-autumns-fresh-rice-ear land, which the Imperial Great Deity whom it is awe-inspiring even to name possesses, is an august land where the living kototama prospers, an august land which the living kototama aids, an august land where the living kototama lives, the people of the grass living under heaven shall polish their hearts and be cautious in their bodies day and night, chant and raise the marvelous kototama bestowed by the deity, never even temporarily revile people, nor deride, nor envy, nor harbor hatred, living in harmony and affection like brothers and sisters, living long in this present world, enjoying and serving their daily livelihoods, and since they are those who should serve the august work entrusted by the deity, without forgetting the teachings of Izumo Shinri even in a dream, reflecting upon them morning and evening, and receiving completely the blessings of the divine realm, we revere and pray before the great presence of the Imperial Great Deity: as this palace, served with high chigi in the nether bedrock, is extremely broad and extremely pure, forever and ever, may the villagers of Izuno Tamaki revere Temes, the village administrator, as a parent, diligently apply themselves to their respective livelihoods, offer martial exploits before the august presence of Ōkami, may their households live in harmony and affection, rejoice and bustle happily, and flourish and prosper; ah, indeed, according to the divine will, O spirits, prosper and multiply!'

Into such a place, appearing to be the young men of the village, wearing red headbands, beating bells and drums, loading a thousand-drawing rock onto a cart, wishing to offer it before the presence of the deity, while men and women of all ages grasped a great rope and shed sweat drop by drop, their valor as they advanced while singing songs.
