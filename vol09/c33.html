Chapter 33: The Chestnut Steed [426]

第33章　栗毛の駒〔426〕

§ 1

Deeply ashamed before the strong, faithful, true hearts of her husband, daughter, and the multitude, Natsuhime was at a loss for words, and wiping her tears with her crimson sleeve against her blushing face,

"Ah, how wretched is my heart! Kamunaohi created by the Kami, Ōnaohi of broad heart, into the path of the teachings pouring true heart morning and evening, the husband-deity Haruyamahiko, and the lovely children Tsukiyukihana, who entrust their bodies to the great way of the Kami, exhausting their hearts like hemp pillars to offer sincerity in their disposition— in contrast to that bravery, Natsuhime, who was born a mother, finds the sky of her heart like a hydrangea whose colors have faded, such is the shame. The pure and precious divine teachings of the divine words prayed day and night, completely forgotten at the moment of death; indecently in a woman's heart, relentless attachment that cannot be severed, bound by the thread of bonds,

The delusion of heart that cannot be unraveled, like tangled hair beyond description, before the Kami who shines without a shadow in miraculous divine authority— ah, how shameful, how disgraceful! If it serves the Kami, the country, and the people of the world, even if husband and wife are separated in life, and even if I face the grief of seeing the thread of life of my lovely daughter severed, make my soul like a thousand-pull rock, unshakeable for eternity! Do not laugh at the disposition of a weak woman, O multitude! O Haruyamahiko, O my daughters Tsukiyukihana, do not laugh at me as an inept mother! Like a pheasant on a burnt field or a crane in the night, fettered by love for my child, the darkness of the heart that went astray— ah, how shameful, how shameful!

O missionaries of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum, do not laugh at me as a thoroughly despised Natsuhime, and illuminate the soul of my body for thousands of generations!"

Thus, her tears of shame fell all at once like rain pouring down a surging rapid, and pressing her sleeves to her face, she wept in deep dejection.

Wishing to dispel the gloom of this place, the missionary Umegaka-hime-no-Mikoto, whose divine authority also opened forth, spoke in a graceful voice:

"Ah, how gracious, O Natsuhime-sama! Exceeding even that of a birth mother, your deep and noble intention— in what world could it ever be forgotten? Unable to wait for spring, blooming fragrantly, the flower bud Umegaka-hime, the truly warm affection of the divine Haruyamahiko-no-Kami-no-Mikoto, on a winter evening when the winter blast rustled, rescuing and caring for me, in the firm-hearted rock cave, the three sisters were saved, with no lack whatsoever, through the deep hospitality of Natsuhime-no-Mikoto; the blessing of the Kami also, in the human world chilled as the winter blast strikes the pine upon the ridge of Takasago, the pine-heart of the Kami of Miroku, firm as rock and evergreen, Matsuyo-hime; pure of heart, Akizuki-

hime-no-Mikoto's intention; in order to save the delicate elder sister Takeno, a divine heart purer than snow, Miyuki-hime, the goddess of love; in the harmonious gathering of parent and children, wandering beneath the sky of early winter where six-petaled snow flowers fall, by the roadside, not unlike Tachibana-hime, Umegaka-hime, worn out by travel; the blessing of Konohana-hime who dwells upon Mount Tenkyō; Haruyamahiko's deep affection, upon whom rests the living spirit of Haniyashiko and Haniyashihime-no-Mikoto who appeared on Mount Kogane; through fifty-six hundred and seventy million years, endlessly throughout the reign of Miroku, the deep affection of husband and wife, of parent and child— how, oh how could I ever forget it, O Natsuhime-sama of deep and true heart?"

§ 2

"Please do not trouble your heart over me; Following the divine teachings Of the Kami who protects Ananai-kyō, May you preserve your precious life As firm and enduring as rock, May parents and children, husband and wife live harmoniously, And may you continue a life of gratitude day and night. Though I am a frail missionary, In order to repay the benevolence of the mountains and seas, I shall pray for your safety morning and evening. Please remain at ease in your heart."

Thus she spoke in a gentle voice. At this point, the noisy voices of villagers arose before the gate:

"We beg to report that just now in the forest of Ma, an incomparably powerful missionary of Ananai-kyō appeared and began loudly singing propagation songs. We villagers intended to surround him in ten-fold and twenty-fold ranks from front, back, left, and right to capture him alive, but his gaze is sharp, and somehow struck by his imposing power, we cannot approach him. Please, Lord Haruyamahiko no Mikoto, ride forth and capture that missionary!"

When they shouted this from the doorway, the whole company, beginning with Haruyamahiko, involuntarily looked at one another's faces and were lost in thought for a while, but Haruyamahiko stood up, and in a loud voice that could be heard outside,

"To come to the land of Ma, ruled by Takatoriwake, a vassal of Tokoyo Shin'ō, and sing propagation songs of Ananai-kyō—what an insolent missionary! I shall strike him down now. You men, go back ahead and prepare bows and arrows. Hey, retainers, prepare my horse!"

so he shouted. At this voice, the numerous villagers finally stroked their chests in relief and ran at full speed toward the forest of Ma. Haruyamahiko turned toward the company and said,

"Since everything is within my mind, may all of you be at ease and rest at your leisure,"

and leaving these words behind, he mounted his chestnut horse, gathered the reins, and advanced quietly toward the front.

現代日本語訳

どうぞ私に心を配られますことなく、 三五教をお守りになる 神の教えに従って、 玉の緒の命を 堅磐常磐に保たれ、 親子夫婦は睦まじく、 日々に夜々に感謝の暮らしを続けられますように。 私はか弱い宣伝使ではありますが、 山海の御恩に報いるため、 朝な夕なに御無事をお祈り申し上げましょう。 どうぞ心安らかにおいでください。」

と声も優しく述べ立てる。そうしたところへ、門前で騒がしく村人の声が、

「申し上げます、ただ今間の森に無双の力を持つ三五教の宣伝使が現れ、盛んに宣伝歌を歌い始めました。我々村人は前後左右から十重二十重に取り囲んで生け捕りにしようと思いましたが、眼光が鋭く、何となくその威勢に圧倒されて近寄ることができません。どうか春山彦の命様、御出馬なさってかの宣伝使を召し捕ってください」

と門口から叫ぶので、春山彦をはじめ一同は思わず顔を見合わせ、しばらく思案に暮れていたが、春山彦は立ち上がり、表に聞こえる大声で、

「常世神王の家来、鷹取別の治められる間の国へやって来て、三五教の宣伝歌を歌うとは憎らしい宣伝使だ、今から討ち取ろう。者ども、先へ戻って弓矢の用意をいたせ。おい家来ども、馬の用意をせよ」

と叫んだ。大勢の村人はこの声にやっと胸を撫で下ろし、間の森へと一目散に走って行く。春山彦は一同に向かい、

「何事も我々の胸中にございますので、どなた様も御安心の上、ゆっくりとお休みください」

と言い捨て、栗毛の馬にまたがり、手綱を繰り、静々と表を指して進んで行く。

原文（近代文語）

と声も優しく述べ立つる。斯かる処へ、門前騒がしく村人の声、

『申し上げます、只今間の森に強力無双の三五教の宣伝使現はれ、盛んに宣伝歌を歌ひ始めました。吾々村人は前後左右より十重二十重に取り巻いて生捕り呉れむと思へども、眼光鋭く何となく、威勢に打たれて進み寄ることが出来ませぬ。何卒々々春山彦の命様、御出馬あつて彼の宣伝使を召し捕り給へ』

と門口より呼ばはるにぞ、春山彦を始め一同は思はず顔を見合せ、暫時思案に暮れけるが、春山彦は立ち上り、表に聞ゆる大音声にて、

『常世神王の御家来、鷹取別の治し召す、間の国に参来り、三五教の宣伝歌うたふとは心憎き宣伝使、今打ち取らむ。者共先へ帰つて弓矢の用意いたせ、ヤア家来共、駒の用意』

と呼ばはりたり。数多の村人はこの声にやつと胸撫で下し、間の森に一目散に走り行く。春山彦は一同に向ひ、

『何事も、吾々が胸中に御座いますれば、何れも様は御安心の上、ゆつくり休息遊ばされよ』

と言ひ捨て、栗毛の駒に跨り、手綱かいくり、しとしとしとと表を指して進み行く。
