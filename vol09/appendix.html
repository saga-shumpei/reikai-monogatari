Appendix: Second Travelogue Poems of Mount Takakuma Pilgrimage

附録　第三回高熊山参拝紀行歌（二）

§ 1

It was early dawn on the seventeenth day of the third month, the month of Yayoi; wearing the grace of God like a straw hat and chanting sacred hymns individually, composing their appearance with calm composure, the ministers of the divine teachings and the believers who had realized the Way—pupils of the Great Sovereign God springing like an auspicious true soul from the inexhaustible, fresh sacred well—trudged their path heading toward Kameoka. Illuminated by the divine light of the Parent God, the Great Dōjō manifested divine virtue in a remarkably prominent manner. The Nagai household, joined in unity through the power of faith in the path of sincerity, advanced into the sacred ground of the deep mountain dense with reeds; led by Deguchi Zuigetsu of Ōmoto, who set foot carried on the back of Ueshiba, a valiant man of the sixth dan in jujutsu, the party ascended with high spirits. Guided by Yuasa Jinsai, struggling and panting up the steep, chest-thrusting slope, from old men with sunken eyes, faces as dark as if smeared with pure soot, and ladies in white makeup, to truly lovely beauties adorned with the finest Misono white powder—just as the plains of Takamanohara clear away, regardless of being drenched in the joyful rain, standing prominently and high, they aimed for the sacred mountain, pushing vigorously through the foot of the large trees; surveying the four directions while entrusting themselves to the Originating God, the Ancestor of the Land, as they ascended and looked down into the bottom of the valley, though neither melons nor eggplants grew there, the divine blessings overflowed; overcoming the demons was Zentarō Fujii; from a distant land came Kitamura, like double cherry blossoms on a blooming mountain; Yamamoto and the entire party were full of vigor; passing through groves of young pines and cryptomerias, aided hundreds and thousands of times by divine grace, the dwellings, Akayama, the Obata River, and all the way to Anao from the northeast gradually came into view; how auspicious the glistening roof tiles of the northern village were—the scenery of high and low mountains standing side by side, brightly spreading in all directions, is profoundly captivating to behold.

現代日本語訳

時は弥生の三月、十七日の未明から、神の恵みを笠に着て、各自で神歌を吟じながら、身繕いを調えて泰然自若として、神の教えを司る役員や、道を悟った信徒たちが、汲んでも尽きることのない清らかで新しい御井に湧き出る瑞々しい真魂、皇大神の教え子たちが亀岡を目指して辿り行く。親神の御光に皆が照らされて大道場は、神徳が殊に著しく現れている。永井の氏は一家中そろって誠の道に信仰の力を合わせ、茅が生い茂る深山の霊地に、武勇の士である柔術六段の植芝におぶわれて踏み込む大本の出口瑞月をはじめとして、一行はいさみたって登ってゆく。湯浅仁斎の先導により、喘ぎ喘ぎ登る胸突きの険しい坂、目まで窪んだ老人や、墨を塗ったような黒い顔、白い化粧の淑女たち、極上品である御園白粉を塗った実に愛らしい美人に至るまで、高天原の野が晴れ渡るように、喜ばしい雨に濡れた身もいとわずに高く秀で、神山を目指して大木の根元を掻き分けずかずかと進み、四方を見回しながら国の祖なる元つ御神に身を任せつつ、登って谷底を眺めれば、瓜や茄子は生えていないが溢れるばかりの神の恵みがある。魔神を払う藤井善太郎氏、遠き国より来た北村、花咲く山の八重桜、山本をはじめ総勢が元気よく、小松林や杉林を進み、神の助けに百千の人々、民家や赤山、小幡川、艮の方角からの穴太に至るまで次第に現れ、北の村の甍も光るめでたさである。高山や低山が立ち並ぶ景色が四方八方に朗らかに目に映るのは、実に心惹かれることである。

§ 2

Enfolded by bamboo groves, in a rural village where thin, quiet smoke rises from rows of eaves, people work diligently drawing strength from the soil; Tarō, Jirō, and Saburō are peaceful, joyful, and exceedingly vigorous. Though knowing not a single rule of etiquette, the preciousness of their protecting the household—Tōtosa! Their hearts are settled with generosity, and manly is the way the farm-folk, steadfast as rock castles, gather and serve; the sounds of sacred court music are heard in the midst of the fields. In the venerability of this peaceful reign, the morning sun's rays in the eastern sky shine brightly and excel (Ryōshun), and the moon likewise shines all across. The world, lasting for long ages under the heavens, progresses anew day by day. Great ships rowing across islands, crossing the many-layered tide-ways from the Naniwagata of good and bad, open the way even unto the foreign lands of Kara. The long-awaited guardian of the barrier, Shigehide who served as a deity, in the shrine-precinct grounds where plum blossoms are fragrant; the born Baka-Tarō, renowned throughout the world as a mischievous boy, Kishitaihei whose name is praised; the blessings of this peaceful reign enrich all eight directions, the capital and the rice-sheaf villages clearing completely into an eternal field, celebrating and living for thousands and tens of thousands of ages in great fortune (Kōkichi)—is this not the reward for having served the divine realm? While clouds and mist gather in all four directions in this dark world where the Magatsu deities rage, the divine path dawns and comes to aid; the long-held prayer is at last fulfilled, and today we make a pilgrim's thanksgiving visit to the sacred mountain of Takakuma. With minds firm (Katō) and clear, moving forward in abundant joy, Jirōemon accompanied by believers crosses mountains large and small; viewing the sacred land established by the deity, the cherry trees of Yoshino shine particularly auspicious and bright. Outside the villages of Anao and Saijō, with fresh hues of the heart, the enigmatic mountain of three-leaf azaleas rises clearly and sublimely high above the clouds before the deity; accompanying Kikuko along the original origin where the Maiden of Sawada appeared, many people from village to village leave behind the Kameoka Dōjō, advancing steadily, including Yukimura Bunjirō. The profound divine will is Shiraishi; good men and good women fill the paths of the moor coming forward to be first, affirming it as the duty of the true path of the source of the land of Urayasu; from east, west, south, and north, far and near, three hundred and fifty people in all, courageously seeking only good things, without minding the distance of Aonogahara, head toward the forest of the transcendent sacred mountain, as only good people go on pilgrimage.

§ 3

Famed as the pure land of the world, the divine garden of Ame no Manai, the central land of the capital of Urayasu, celebrated and revered as the Great Foundation of the world. Urashima Tarō with the lifespan of a turtle manifests again in this world, becoming the spirit of pure water, and remarkably pours forth all the sincere heart that exists upon the world. Well governing the ponds, marshes, swamps, and grass fields in the primordial age of the gods, and while superintending the estate of the spirit of Japan according to the divine will, the Great Imperial Deities who initiated the path of teachings uniformly, without distinction between near and far, on the third day of the third month when peach blossoms are fragrant, without disdaining myriad hardships, exhaust the sincere heart and rule. Seeking to create souls of goodness at the Wachi River of the golden world, blowing away the village mists from all four directions, teaching and praying, clearing the pampas grass fields to shine on an auspicious day. Entering the river and expelling myriad sins by fire, while extending the beautiful cord of life, mutually admonishing against conduct where the body dominates the spirit, revealing the full stature of the heart, rounding out the mountain of thoughts; the divine blessings grasped at Ōmoto are altogether the utmost limit of the sacred, unknown even in the dreams of worldly people.

現代日本語訳

世界の浄土と聞こえている天の真名井の神の園であり、浦安の都の中心地であって、世の大本として祝いお祀り申し上げる。亀の齢をもつ浦島太郎が再びこの世に現れ、清らかな水の魂となって、真心のありったけを世にいちじるしく注ぎ進めてゆく。池や沢、沼も草原も、原始の神代によく治め、日本の御魂の荘園を神の御心のままに司りながら、教えの道を始められた皇大神はあまねく、近きも遠きも隔てなく、桃の花も香る三月三日に、万の苦難をも厭うことなく、真心を尽くして治められる。黄金世界の和知の川において善の御魂を造ろうと、四方の村霧を吹き払い、教え、願いつつ薄野を切り開いて輝かす吉祥の日。川に入り火によって万の罪を祓い清めて、美しき生命の綱を延ばしながら、体主霊従の行動を互いに戒め合い、心の内をすっかり打ち明けて、山と重なる思いを満ち足らせ、大本で掴んだ神徳は、総じて世の人の夢にすら知られていない尊き極みである。

§ 4

The moon, curved like a sharp sickle, hangs in the mid-sky; raising the jewel to vie in brilliance with the sunlight, the divine person ascending to the high peak of the mountain he governs hears the wondrous voice like Kikutarō. In order to spread widely to the countries of the four directions the teachings of the Three-Five, parting the gathering clouds of the West, the vigorously shining man in the moon bestows dew to moisten the people of the villages and fields.

The falling cascade water splashes refreshingly, and traversing the undulating grassy plains of rock and stone, as well as the shade beneath the groves in the densely wooded mountains, even the throat of the righteous person of deep faith becomes parched before the God of immense divine virtue. The serene and utterly pure spring breeze blows coolly, and the blossoms of Mount Takakuma in the northern village shine brightly.

Even if the foolish deride the single-hearted, sincere teaching of Giichi, the true guardian of Izu, one proclaims it uprightly with the broad heart of Kamunaobi, and listens anew to the divine path. One's inner heart is dyed red like the autumn leaves of Tatsuta; how prosperous must the capital be? Adorning large and small mountains alike to their very corners with blossoms, the spring that has arrived in the divine age is an eternal, pure guardian bracelet. High resounds the majestic authority of the Isuzu waterfall, echoing across the divine land. The threefold spirit of Yashima no Hiko appears in the small pine grove; to govern the world in peace and tranquility, the divine person of superior virtue advances once more with the spirit of the gods.

To snap off a single blossoming, fragrant flower atop the mountain ridge, summoning courage and shedding rivers of sweat, they steadily climb upward. With the innocent heart of an Ichima doll, deeply humble and without double-mindedness, pledging to God and the Way, pure people one after another harbor within their firm, stone-like breasts hearts aligned without distinction, and joyfully, without reluctance or deceit, tread the mountain path in praise of the divine virtues, walking quietly together on the same path like men boldly crossing over to the other shore.

God truly dwells in this world and protects us. Even in an age deemed to be without God, dispelling the gathering clouds in the depths of the heart and proclaiming anew with the divine words of Miyabi, the land and its people alike shall unite as one heart to fortify the stronghold in the auspicious land of God. By the precious teachings of the threefold spirit that appeared in the land of Tanba, pounding and solidifying the foundation of thousands of generations, while all encourage one another, we who abundantly receive the dew of grace are the wondrous children descended from the gods of the Land of the Rising Sun, the true divine pillars that guard the Great Bridge of Takamanohara.

Vigorously traversing even the steep, wooded mountains, led by the threefold spirit, though without small woven sedge hats, the destination of those enduring on the plain of Aonogahara is indeed the village of the gods. Those who assist gather in abundance, spreading wide toward the future, and with all their might upon the stones, the children of God, with joyful hearts, expound and instruct the Great Way of sovereign and subject as smoothly as flowing river water. Within the mortal dwelling of the three-leaved azalea, becoming the awe-inspiring shield of the Great Deity Kunitakehiko, offering all good to the utmost limit of life and soul, scooping and drinking deeply from the depths of the well of Ikui amidst the plain, they follow the path of salvation. Hearing together as companions of the same heart, winding the precious teachings of God up to the very clouds, they exalt the truth of heaven and earth. Just as the river water of the valley flows purely forever, washing the world without limit, the divine progress extends to the countries of the four directions, even to the innermost depths of the three mountains where miscanthus plains spread and deer dwell, opening wide the divine purpose of the transformation of the world.

現代日本語訳

鋭い鎌のような月が空の中ほどにかかり、玉をかざして日光と輝きを競い合いつつ、山を治める高い峰へと登ってゆく神人の珍しい声を菊太郎のように聞き、四方の国々に至るまで三五の教えを広く行き渡らせようと、西洋の群雲をかき分けて隆々と輝く桂男の月の光の露に潤う村や野の人々。

落ちる滝の水は清々と響き、岩石が起伏する草原や、木立の生い茂る山中の森の木陰を伝い歩きながら、信仰篤き正しい人の喉さえも涸れつつ、神徳のいとも大いなる神の御前に至る。清らかで至純な春風も涼しく吹いて、北の村の高熊山の花も光り輝いている。

伊豆の真の守りたる義一の誠一つの御教えを、愚かにも馬鹿にする者がいようとも、神直日の心も広く正しく宣り直し、聞き直す神の道である。腹の中も竜田の紅葉のように染まり、都はどれほど豊かであろうか。大山も小山も隅々に至るまで花を髪飾りに挿頭して、神の世となった春は永久に清らかな守りの腕輪のように、五十鈴の滝の神威高く鳴り響き渡る神の国に、八島の彦の三つの御魂が小松林に現れて、世を平穏に治めようと、道にも優れた神人は、またしても進み行く神の気迫である。

山の尾根の上に咲き香る一輪の花を手折ろうと、勇気を奮い起こし、汗の川を流しながら着々と登ってゆく。市松人形のような素直な初心で、よくへりくだり二心なく、神と道とに誓いを立て、清らかな人々が次々と、固い石のような胸の内を抱き、何の隔てもなく心が一つに合った信徒たちが、神の御徳を称えつつ、めでたくここに山道を嫌がることもなく正直に、雄々しく彼岸へと渡るように、静かに同じ道を歩んでゆく。

神はこの世に真にましまして我らをお守りくださるのである。いかに神なき世のようであっても、心の奥の群雲を宮比の言葉によって正しく宣り直し、御国も人々もことごとく運気の良い神の国において、一つの心となって城を固めよ。丹波の国に現れた三つの御魂の尊い教えにより、千代の礎を突き固め、一同が互いに奮い立つ中で、恵みの露を豊かにいただく我らは、日の本の神の末裔たる尊き御子であり、高天原の大橋を守る誠の神柱である。

険しい木山も健やかに、三つの御魂に誘われて、菅の小笠はなくとも、青野が原を忍び行く身の行き着く所は神の里である。補佐する人々も数多く集まり来て末広がりとなり、君と臣との大道を力の限り神の子らが心いそいそと石の上で、古事記を川の水が流れるように説き諭す。三つ葉ツツジが咲く人の身の宮において、国武彦大神の厳しき御盾となり、肝と命の続く限り善美を尽くし、生井の奥深き水までも汲んで飲み干す原の中で、救いの道をたどってゆく。同じ心の友として聞き、尊き神の御教えを雲の上まで巻き上げ、天地の真理を発揚し、谷の川の水が永久に清らかに流れるように、世界を洗い清めて限りない神の御幸が四方の国々に及び、薄の原や鹿の住む三山の奥の奥にまでも、世界を改造する神界の経綸の道を開き進めてゆく。
