Chapter 25: Moving to a New Cavern [418]

§ 1

The divine majesty shines high on the mountain, From which falls the Kotodaki Waterfall, Spraying into the jewel-like river at its resonance, Along the mountain stream of pure water-sounds, To the right and to the left are the massive rocks, Within the rock cave firm and eternal, The kotodama of a thousand ages is proclaimed and transmitted, The grace of the kami is Orochihiko, The teachings lie deep in the recess of the cavern, How could one in a shallow and lowly human form know this? The divine command of Shigeyamatsumi, And Kumakō, whose heart is bewildered, facing each other, Asking and answering, They thus spent the pitch-black night here, They thus spent the pitch-black night here.

Purifying heaven, earth, kami, and humans through the cadence of seventy-five sounds, the Waterfall of Kotodama fully scattered jewel-like droplets of crystal spray, and the rising mist enveloped the deep ravine.

Shigeyamatsumi and Kumakō sat upright before this rock cave, and as the veil of their hearts was opened by the dialogue of kotodama, the light of the heavenly sun dyed the sky madder-red and began to shine in its glorious ascent. Shigeyamatsumi began a dialogue within his mouth toward the kami of the rock cave. His voice was extremely faint, to such an extent that it did not even reach the ears of Kumakō beside him.

Seeing that Shigeyamatsumi swiftly stood before the rock cave, hid his form in the rising mist, secretly crossed the mountain stream to conceal himself deep inside the western rock cave, took the bowl and bamboo tube beside the basin of the waterfall in his left and right hands, filled the water bowl with water, and concealed himself deep within the rock cave, Kumakō said,

"Hey, Shigeyamatsumi, where are you going? Wait first, I will go together with you."

At this time, the rock cave once again,

"Ū, Ō,"

emitted a roar so tremendous that it seemed as though the mountains would crumble; but after a short while, as the sound ceased, from inside the rock cave came the voice:

"Kumakō, Kumakō, you must not move from this place for a while. How can you carry out the propagation if you lean on Shigeyamatsumi like a staff and rely on him as your strength? In your body, a kami possessing divine power a hundred thousand times greater than Shigeyamatsumi's is guarding you."

"Th-th-that is truly wonderful, but where on earth has he gone?"

From inside the rock cave, Orochihiko said,

"What use is it to ask that? You had better ask the kami of your own heart."

At this juncture, Shikakō, whom he had met yesterday on the grassy plot at the mountain entrance, appeared before this rock cave together with many villagers, bowed his head, clapped his hands, and seeing the figure of Kumakō, said:

"Oh, aren't you Kumakō whom I met yesterday? How did it go? You must have been severely scolded. And what happened to Torakō?"

"Oh, is that Shikakō? That fellow Tora, with no sense of friendship, left me behind in such a place and went off somewhere as he pleased. I thought of following after him, but for some reason I do not understand, suddenly my body became fixed and I cannot move."

"That must be because you have lost your nerve; what a weak fellow you are! Hello, O deity within the rock cave, today I have brought many villagers with me. Please let the villagers hear your fine teachings."

Orochihiko said, "Ah, is that you, Shikakō? You have done well to come forth. Because Torakō came and defiled this rock cave yesterday, I shall now change my lodging to the western rock cave. Led by Kumakō, the whole party leapt across the mountain stream below the waterfall, arrived before the western rock cave, bowed their heads, and spoke.

Kumakō said, "Well now, today Lord Orochihiko has relocated to a new palace—no, to a parlor in this dark rock cave—and I offer my heartiest congratulations. Amid such confusion, you must be extremely busy. If there is anything I can do, please permit me to assist you."

From inside the rock cave came:

"Bū, bū, buru, buru, buru, buru!"

If you reform without objection, well and good; but if you disobey, shall I shoot you dead right here on the spot with a white-feathered arrow?"

「やあこいつ、少しおかしいぞ。東の岩窟の声とは随分違う。竹筒を吹いて、物を言うようなことを言っている。おそらく虎公のやつ、俺を一杯食わせようと思って、熊公と申し合わせて芝居を仕組んだのだろう。よし、よし、よし、これから、この鹿公が、虎穴に入らずんば虎子を得ずだ。一つ命を的に穴の中に探検と出かけようか」

と言いながら、むくっと立ち上がり、鹿公が今まさに岩窟に立ち入ろうとするとき、天地も破れるばかりの大音響、

「ウー、オー」

鹿公は、

「いよーこいつはやっぱり本物だ。どうぞお許しくださいませ」

と拍子抜けしたような声で、またしても岩窟の前に平伏する。

折から吹き来る烈風が梢を渡る音は、滝の響きと調和して心が砕け、魂が消え入るような騒然たる光景を現出した。
