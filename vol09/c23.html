Chapter 23: Mount Takateru [416]

第23章　高照山〔416〕

§ 1

On the lawn at the mountain entrance of Takateruyama, on the border between Hiru and Karu, the two men, Kumakō and Torakō, were left behind; while watching the figures of Matsuyohime's party until their very shadows vanished, Torakō said,

"Ah, the party of the missionaries of Ananaikyō did not permit us to accompany them, but left behind warm words and cheerfully departed from this place. In any case, because we are humans with heavy sins, accompanying them was probably not granted, but ah, what a regrettable thing we have done. If only, three years ago, we had attained our present state of mind, they surely would have granted us permission to accompany them; the more I think of it, the more I resent the sins of this body of mine,"

and letting out his voice, he wept bitterly.

"Torakō! It is by no means, by no means so. Wishing by all means to make us splendid pillars of God, they deliberately abandoned us and departed. It is said that the beast called the lion, three days after giving birth to its cub, kicks it down into the bottom of a valley, and kicks down again and again the cub that climbs up, and does not accept it as its own child unless it has climbed up three times or more. If one is so weak as to perish upon being cast into a valley, one can never survive in this world of devils. Still more, as missionaries who teach and guide devil-like humans, if we were to lean upon our Master like a staff or rely on them, we could not perform our complete divine service; therefore, shedding inwardly the tears that would flow outward, out of the divine heart of Great Mercy and Great Compassion, they left us behind and went away. Humans can never comprehend the deep, deep mind of God. That is why in the missionary song of Ananaikyō, too,

"Kamunaohi, who created this world, Ōnaohi, of broad and spacious heart; In this human world, whatever may happen, Directly re-examine and hear anew,"

it is written. Re-examination is of the utmost importance. Frail and weak humans with dim wisdom like us have no choice but to follow the infinite and absolute, beginningless and endless, true God, and to pray with all our sincere hearts. If we pray, the glory of God will surely shine above our heads,"

and just as he spoke thus amid tears, four or five rough men bearing bows and arrows came down the sloped path, leading dogs with them. One of the men, looking at the faces of Kumakō and Torakō, said,

"Oh, aren't you Kumakō and Torakō, so renowned by reputation throughout this Takasagojima? Even crying children are said to stop weeping upon hearing your names, yet what on earth has happened today? What is the matter with you, sniffling and weeping so uglily...?"

Torakō said, 'Oh, is this Shikakō? As for me, I have completely reformed. Until now, people of the world have called me an evil person, but from now on, I will turn back entirely to a good heart, confess my sins, and, to atone for my past sins, sing the divine propagation songs of the deities, preach the teachings around, and save people. It is so grateful that I was just now weeping tears of joy. You, too, should stop taking lives, listen to the teachings of the Ananai Doctrine, and become a good person. This is the threshold of Torakō’s reformation, the maiden battle of my propagation. If you reform, all the people on this Takasago Island will reform.'

'Huh, what nonsense are you spewing? Spewing such things like a demon chanting the Nembutsu, where does one press you to produce such a sound? With the recent heat, your mind has gone a bit strange. Humph, how foolish; the world is entirely the survival of the fittest. It is a world where big fish swallow small fish, and small fish eat insects, living upon one another. Dog catches cat, cat catches rat, rat takes the retiree’s tea cakes, the tea cakes humor the retiree, the retiree picks lice from his undershirt, and the lice take dandruff from the head; thus the world goes round. Sea fishermen catching fish and mountain hunters taking wild boars are all for the sake of society. If there were no hunters, one could neither use hides nor eat fish. The world is the survival of the fittest; the weak are meat for the strong, which is the law of nature. Instead of saying such effeminate things, why do you not become the Oni-tora of old and make a name for yourself? Is it not a pity to reduce the name you have built up to nothing?'

Kumakō said, 'Ah, good and evil are indeed different things. There is nothing as bitter as good; no, there is nothing as splendid as good. Instead of saying such things, do you not feel like reforming like Torakō and praying to the deities?'

'No, I do pray to a deity. The deity I pray to is entirely different from a deity who gives such weak, spineless, unreliable teachings, like breaking wind in the water. Just now, I have returned from worshiping that deity.'

Torakō asked, 'That deity you went to worship, what kind of deity is it?'

'You, famous as it is, have you not heard of it yet? You are quite slow of hearing. If you go straight about three ri deeper in from here, there is a deep valley of Mount Takateru. A long waterfall falls there, and to the right and left of the waterfall basin are large rock caves. Moreover, a strange voice comes from the eastern cave. People go facing that rock to have anything and everything taught to them. Go and see for yourself once; many people are worshiping there. If a fellow with slight misunderstandings goes, a large tongue of flame comes out from that rock cave, and his body is crackled and scorched. If someone like you who speaks such foolish things goes, you will surely be licked by the tongue of fire emerging from the rock cave and end up charred black.'

Kumakō: "Just what kind of deity is that?"

"What deity it was, well, I have forgotten, but anyway I heard it was Yamata no Orochi or something like that."

Kumakō: "Just what sort of thing does it say?"

"I have forgotten the details, but at any rate it is a deity that says things suited to the times. Well, to put it briefly, it says that human beings should live splendidly even for a single day, eat sweet things granted by heaven, wear beautiful clothes, drink sake, and cheer up. It is completely different from those stingy oracles, like the Mirror Pond of the land of Chiri, like farting from within the water. Well, you fellows, do not be weeping and whimpering, but go there once. It will surely open your eyes."

Torakō: "Shikakō, do you believe in that teaching?"

"How could it be a matter of believing or not believing? Where could there be such a splendid teaching? Farewell."

So saying, the five hunters quickened their pace and went down the slope. The two men, Tora and Kuma, hastened their steps, hurried along the valley path, and finally reached the waterfall of Tamagawa at dusk; looking upon it, a great waterfall resembling an upright koto hung high beyond hundreds of jō, playing exquisite music. In front of the great cavern on the east side, many worshippers were pressing their palms together and uttering various prayers. The two men, with innocent faces, sat upright before the cavern alongside the crowd and pressed their palms together, whereupon the cavern suddenly began to roar with a great sound. All of them pressed their heads flat to the ground and listened to that sound in awe. The roaring gradually ceased, and from the dim depths of the cavern,

"Ahahaha!"

a repulsive laughter came heard.

The two men, Tora and Kuma, looked at each other's faces in dismay. From inside the cavern,

"In a world where evil flourishes, you wretches fight over good and evil! You are utterly fools exposing your shame; unless you do evil, you cannot cross through this world in ease. Eat to your heart's content, drink to your heart's content, display your power to your heart's content; even if you are called devils, exert yourselves to the utmost for your own sake to your heart's content! Iiii—while there is life, there is hope! Rather than following useless teachings and cowering in fear of good, of evil, and of dreadful ends, drink sake with vigor, and prolong your lives, ever vivacious! Uuuu—even if people point fingers behind your back, do not look behind! Pretend not to see, eat sweet things to the full, get so drunk on delicious sake as if floating upon it, smack your lips saying it is sweet, sweet, and do not listen to the teachings of Ananaikyō, which are so annoying, annoying, and stiffen your shoulders! There is a hole in this cavern. There is a hole that roars 'Uuuu!' Ah, what an amusing hole-possessing teaching! Do not listen carelessly. Eeee—without reservation or hesitation, for one's own sake one cannot care about others! If it brings profit, go anywhere, anywhere! If you fear Enma, you cannot exist in this world! Cultivate a mighty heart that would pickle Enma of hell in miso and eat him; live laughing and flourishing, drinking sake! Oooo—unless you dwell in this world with the heart of an ogre or a serpent, this world is one of survival of the fittest, where the strong prey upon the weak! Watch out for one another and seek your own gain! Do not fear, do not lag behind, pass through this world with amusement, and pray to the serpent deity morning and evening! Oooo, amusing, amusing!"

『タヽヽヽ誑け者、叩き潰して食うて了はうか、鼻高神の宣伝使、チヽヽヽちつとばかり改心が出来たと申して、この方様に意見がましい知識の足らぬ大馬鹿者、一寸は胸に手をあてて見よ。ツヽヽヽ捕へ処のない事を吐いて歩く、罪の深い両人共、掴み潰してやらうかい。強さうに言つても、貴様の胸はドキドキして居らうがな、テヽヽヽ天にも地にも俺一人が宣伝使だと言はぬばかりのその面つき、多勢の中で面を剥れてテレクサイことはないか。てるの国から遥々出て来て、扨もさても馬鹿な奴だ。トヽヽヽ虎公のトボケ面、熊公の心の暗い俄改心、迚も迚も衆生済度は六つかしからう』

熊公『ナヽヽ何を吐きよるのだ。タヽヽ他愛もないこと、立板に水を流すやうに、よくも囀る狸の親玉、誰にそんな事教へて貰ひよつたのだ。誑け者。チヽヽ一寸は貴様も考へて見よ。ツヽヽ詰らぬ理窟を並べよつて、テヽヽ手柄さうにトボケきつたことを吐しやがるな』

巌窟の中より、「ウヽヽヽワーワー」と大音響ひびき来る。
