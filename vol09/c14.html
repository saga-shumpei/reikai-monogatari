Chapter 14: The Bottom of the Dark Abyss [407]

第14章　闇の谷底〔407〕

§ 1

The party of Odoyamazumi descended Teruyama Pass toward the east. When they drew near to the village of the land of Teri, the party's feet stopped dead, and they could not advance even a single step by any means.

Uzuyamahiko said, "Ah, my feet have become unable to walk. How is it with all of you?"

All together replied, "No, it is the same with us. What an incomprehensible thing to happen."

Komayamahiko said, "Surely this means there are many wicked fellows ahead, lying in ambush to attack us. Therefore, the deity must have bound our feet and given us an intimation: 'Do not advance rashly; place your hands upon your chests, and carefully consider the consequences.'"

The seven persons of the party remained standing on the road, frozen stiff like stone Jizō statues. From within the dense forest of ancient trees beside them,

"Odoyamazumi, Komayamahiko, Teruhiko!"

resounded a voice like a cracked bell. Along with that voice, the bodies of the three felt as though drawn by something; unconsciously turning toward the direction of the voice, their feet moved forward of their own accord, and finally the figures of the three disappeared from sight.

Afterwards, the four—Uzuyamahiko, Matsu, Take, and Ume—found their feet free without realizing it, sat upright upon the pure grass by the roadside, and

Uzuyamahiko said, "Now, everyone, for frail women who do not yet even know the doctrines of Anaikyō to become missionaries and teach and guide this world where devils run rampant is not something accomplished through ordinary hardship; and furthermore, walking about to propagate lined up in a crowd of seven like this may look showy and splendid at a glance, but that is all a vain flower. The propagation of the true Way must strictly be done one by one. From now on, although I am unworthy, I, Uzuyamahiko, will provide practical instruction to the three sisters; so in the meantime, let us four separate from the three missionaries, reach Ataru from the port of Hara, and then travel around to Tokoyo-no-kuni to receive visual education and propagate in Yomishima. Come, come, please follow me."

With that, he went ahead. The three followed after Uzuyamahiko as if drawn. Uzuyamahiko said quietly,

"Everyone, completely forget about Odoyamazumi, Komayamahiko, and Teruhiko. Unless human beings burn their bridges and encounter hardships where they escape death by a hair's breadth, the path of absolute sincerity will never open. The blooming of the flower of hardship has a long prime; once the general work is accomplished together with us, the three sisters must divide your efforts, scatter separately, and serve in the divine work. Even if you become alone, the deity will protect you, so by relying on masters or siblings as a pillar of strength or leaning on them as a staff, one can by no means fully perform service to the divine realm. Come, let us go."

§ 2

Saying this, they pointed toward the plain and advanced.

Odo-Yamadzumi and the other two, drawn by the strange voice, unknowingly traced the mountain stream upward and strayed several ri deep into the mountains.

Just at that time, the moon of the fifteenth night shone across the eastern sky, yet between the rugged, towering mountains, the deep gorge received not a beam of moonlight; the night only grew deeper and deeper, and loneliness pressed in with every passing moment. The three sat down all at once as though by agreement and sat upright beside the mountain stream. Their bodies stiffened once more so that they could not budge in the slightest, with only their necks remaining free, when suddenly from atop a dense oak tree,

"Uuu,"

a great groaning voice came roaring.

Odo-Yamadzumi said, "Hey, you two, my body cannot move an inch; how is it with you?"

Komayamahiko said, "S-s-strange. There has never been such a bizarre thing."

Teruhiko, raising a voice like thunder, said,

"I am Tsukiteruhiko no Mikoto!"

Komayamahiko said, "What, Tsukiteruhiko? Talk sense; do not perform such farce. You were the 'hiko' attached to the three daughters, but now you are a 'hiko' completely separated from them, are you not? If you stray into such a dark mountain depth and act the fool, Komayama will not permit it. Is this any time for such carefree antics?"

Teruhiko said, "Ahahaha, how utterly absurd! Pompously calling yourselves missionaries of the Ananai Teachings and strutting about shouting in loud voices, you foolish missionaries, how is it now? Thrown into this ravine where pitch blackness lies an inch ahead, are you completely flabbergasted? Has your jaw dropped? You are so astounded that you cannot speak. Your open mouth cannot even close. Ahaha, what pitiful beings! In the manifestation of the original nature of your souls, wash your souls, wash your rears, wash your feet; though the clear teachings of the gods exist, is your way of walking not mistaken?"

"Ah, this fellow has fallen into possession by an evil spirit. Spouting such preposterous nonsense, what a truly, truly pitiful fellow. Hey, Lord Odo-Yamadzumi, do not just sit still yourself; in this situation, perform a splendid spirit discernment and reveal the demon possessing Teruhiko!"

"No, our own bodies have suddenly become disabled, unable to stand on our feet and hips; at such an extremity we were dumbfounded, and our very wits were snatched away. Ah, what a shameful thing!"

Teruhiko said, "Ihihihi, how pitiful. As retribution for having acted arrogant in the far corners of foreign lands, though you regret various things in your final moments, nothing can be done. If such a scene were witnessed by many people of the land, the dignity of the missionaries would completely fall to the ground. That God intended to admonish you, underwent diverse troubles, and lured you to this ravine of the village of Yutsuishi is God's benevolence. A missionary is one who ought to preach and propagate through the realm entirely alone. Yet what is this? Like tourists on a pleasure trip, you cowards who traipse around propagating in a flock of several people, henceforth you must strictly conduct yourselves with care! Do not disobey the word of God! Do you hear? What is your answer?"
