Chapter 15: Dumpling Sophistry [408]

第15章　団子理屈〔408〕

§ 1

Although the moon of the fifteenth night has risen, the ravine between the mountains is pitch darkness where black and white cannot be distinguished; and while the three missionaries sit upright at the bottom of the valley where sky is narrow and ground is cramped, moving only their necks, a sound like the roaring of tigers and wolves echoes from somewhere and is heard.

"Uu-oo!"

Komayamahiko: "Ah, is it a tiger or a wolf? A terrible voice has been heard. Lord Okitsuyamatsumi, shall we recite the divine words or something?"

Okitsuyamatsumi: "Yes, it cannot be helped; let us recite the divine words."

Teruhiko: "Ha, ha, ha! Missionaries who treat God like dried bonito flakes, relying on God only when at your wit's end—what do you mean by 'divine words or something'? Are you still missionaries despite that, merely 'or something' missionaries? In the country of Teru where divine majesty shines, Mara shines brightly while spring mountains are clouded over; hold on, Okitsuyama is clouded over, and Komayamahiko makes rain fall; rain it is, but a rain of tears. Look at that sorrowful expression; without the resolution that, even if your body were torn into eight pieces, it would not matter in the least if it is for the sake of God, can the path of sincerity be opened? You empty-spirited, cunning souls, you might as well divine the heart of God. Missionaries who, encountering even a little hardship and suffering, tuck up your skirts and flee in haste; you moth-like pests who carry the true God with black souls like crows, walking about spouting arbitrary nonsense. Furthermore, while walking sideways like a crab on a crooked path, you are missionaries who merely deceive, saying, 'None is as sincere as I; sincerity alone is the treasure of the world; exhaust your sincerity, become of pure heart.'

"Without knowing the identity of Lord Kakatora, who is as noisy as a crow, you despised him as a frivolous man, sweating, feeling ashamed, scratching your heads, being mouthy, and skilled at empty bluster as messengers of God; that there is a battle between Kara and Japan means the renewal and rebuilding of your souls, casting away your foreign souls of the past, and replacing them with pure crystal Yamato souls. Being merely noisy does not make one a missionary. It is well if you reform; if you do not understand to the bitter end, God will put an end to you at once. No matter how patient a God may be, God will lose patience and yawn."

Komayamahiko: "Ha, ha, ha! It is too much, too much; please forgive us. We will reform from now on, so please do not meddle with our affairs any longer. Having been made to listen to an elusive admonition that is like trying to catch someone running, ah, what a fine embarrassment!"

Teruhiko: "Ki, ki, ki! You are an exceedingly stubborn fellow; does what I say displease you? Blowing bizarre tall tales all around, acting complacent and carefree while singing missionary songs, wandering about the realm half out of whim, you flustered fool; looking dazed, there is a limit to how obtuse you can be. The Golden Deity of the Demon's Gate appears and separates good from evil. Missionaries of mad souls like beasts will be cleanly removed at the turn of the world and cut away from root to leaf. It is natural that you do not like it. You may think these are harsh, unyielding words like those of a fierce spirit, but pay close attention and savor them. With ears like wood-ear mushrooms, you will not be able to catch the true words of God. The anxious heart of God—if you will not consent forever, I shall make you consent and bring you to agreement. Unless you completely cast away your filthy heart, become of the true divine mind, exercise tact in all things, and show attentiveness and consideration, you can never perform an intelligent service. Do not harbor doubts; even if it is not to your liking, these are unvarnished divine words: from now on, exert yourself, change your mood, reform your sluggish mind, cleanly cast away your self-indulgence, devote sincerity to God and your lord, soften your sullen face, make up your mind, temper your bold courage, do not shriek in thought, do not suffer in anguish, purify your mind and cleanse your body; if you obey the true God, the path will spread comfortably. Things will proceed smoothly and precisely, like a well-fitted box. Do not use God as a pretext or shield. Harbor the mindset of standing amid drawn swords and blades, and do not be off your guard. The boundless grace of God; myriad sins and offenses vanish in the divine light, your body and soul are peacefully renewed, and only upon doing so are you for the first time a missionary of Sango-kyō. Well, do you understand?"

照彦「キヒヒ、貴様は余程しぶとい奴だ、こちらの言うことが気に入らないのか。奇怪千万な大言壮語を吹き散らし、いい気になって気楽そうに宣伝歌を歌い、気まぐれ半分に天下を巡るうろたえ者め、間の抜けた顔をして、気が利かないのにも程がある。鬼門の金神が現れて善と悪とを峻別する。鬼畜のような、狂気じみた魂の宣伝使は、世の切り替えにきっぱりと取り払い、根から葉まで切り落としてしまうぞ。貴様が気に入らないのはもっともだ。ひどく気丈な鬼神のような言葉と思うであろうが、よく気をつけて味わってみよ。木耳のような耳では神の誠の言葉は聞き取れまい。気をもんでならない神の胸のうち、いつまでも承知しなければ承知するようにして承知させてみせようぞ。汚い心をさっぱり捨て去り、誠の神心になり、万事に対して機転を利かせ、心配りや気配りができないようでは、気の利いた御用は到底務まらないぞ。疑わしく思わず気に入らなくても、歯に衣を着せぬ神の言葉である、これから気合を入れて気分を改め、無精な心を立て直し、気まま勝手をさらりと捨て去り、神と君主に誠を尽くし、気難しそうな顔を和らげ、心を決めて剛胆さを練り、きゃあきゃあと思わず、きゅうきゅうと苦しまず、心を清め身を浄め、誠の神に従えば気楽に道が広がるぞ。きっちりきっちりと箱を組み合わせたように進むぞ。神を笠に着るなよ。抜刀や刃物の中に立っているような心持ちになって、油断をするな。極まりもない神の恩、万の罪や咎も神の光に消え失せて、身魂は穏やかに改まり、そうした上で初めて三五教の宣伝使だ。どうだ、分かったか」

§ 2

Komayamahiko said, "I-I-I have realized it. I will exert myself, exert myself, and promptly repent from now on. Meeting with a bizarre, extraordinary experience in this eerie ravine and having my nerves racked—what a terribly heedless thing! Believing that I was pleasing the Kami, exerting myself, exerting myself, paying care and heed, and serving up to this point, I have been made to suffer unexpected anxiety. Come, let us quickly settle this and return the way we came. And what is with Okatsuyamazumi-sama? How faint-hearted he is, trembling timidly, is he not? What a weak-spirited man. If we linger long in such a place, not to speak of monkey's urine, I worry about what lies ahead."

Okatsuyamazumi said, "In an embarrassing, eerie manner, I was severely reprimanded. Having been exposed to the very brink, ah, I too am beside myself with anxiety. In any case, we have not been conducting propagation that pleases the Kami."

Teruhiko said, "Tr-tr-trouble-free, dark-hearted propagators of dark clouds!"

Komayamahiko said, "Excuse me, saying such trivial things in the d-d-dark—pl-pl-please stop around here!"

Teruhiko said, "Is it painful? Is your scowling face unsightly? Even if you conceal things as though covering something foul, and no matter how much you try to pilfer, so long as the forty-eight bad habits remain, I will correct them. Blabbering worthless logic from your mouth, endlessly churning out tedious reasoning sluggishly and slovenly, losing your spine at a single error, fretting over the slightest matter, without knowing the detailed facts, spouting contradictory excuses, talking of overturning or not overturning at ninety-nine percent, and claiming to have completely realized or comprehended the one-percent plan—what sort of nonsense is that? The fathomless teachings of the Kami are elusive like grasping clouds in the vast sky; this is the retribution for blowing foolish boasts. Bitterly enduring frustration and regret, repent of your misunderstandings hitherto, manifest the light of the Kami without brooding, illuminate the dark world, and by all means await the time when the plum blossoms of the returning spring bloom. The divine plan reverses completely, the divine way where the flowers of hardship bloom; if you wish to know the details, repent and take on the mind of the Kami. I make this known to you in painstaking detail."

Komayamahiko said, "A t-t-tedious sermon—I u-understand, I understand, it is fine. Never, never again will I make any further misunderstanding. Please stop with this, and make the bodies of the three of us free!"

§ 3

Teruhiko: "Ke-ke-ke, 'splendid, splendid,' what is splendid? It is the divine teaching that does not deviate even by the breadth of a hair. Resolution is foremost. Because one must not defile the Way, the Kami takes care even before there is any sign. Unless you correct your bizarre mind, refrain from being petty, and abide in the divine mind, you will be deceived by the souls of beasts and have even the hairs on your buttocks completely plucked away. Climbing up and down steep mountains with a manner of fault-finding like blowing hair to search for a scar, taking along attendants and bringing women—will the divine teaching spread through such things, you propagators inferior to caterpillars?"

Komayamahiko: "Ke-ke-ke, what strange things this Kami says. Odochimayama-sama, what shall we do? My ears hurt, and it is not pleasant in the least; if I had known I would be subjected to such an ordeal, I would not have come around on propagation; ah, what a painful thing a propagator is! It is because an odd fellow like Teruhiko came along that in such a strange ravine, I have suffered outrageous treatment, as if having my eyebrows read, my nostril hairs plucked, and even the hair of my buttocks pulled out, and have met with a far more wretched fate than being kicked down to the bottom of the valley and injured. Since your head is bald, it might be fine for you to have no hair and no injury, but Komayamahiko has met with an impasse where nothing can be done and is thoroughly troubled!"

Teruhiko: "Ko-ko-ko, Komayamahiko, what are you saying? Clumsily wandering here and there as a party of seven men and women like a beggars' theater troupe, you propagators—the Kami's pouch of patience will burst! Causing the demons of this world to pass on and bringing joy to the Kami, Buddhist rituals, and the people is the mind of the Kami; what use is a ragged propagator of unsettled mind without endurance? § 6

Komayamahiko: "Ah, Lord Odoyama, have your legs stood up? Ah, that is splendid. We do not budge at all yet; our torsos are firmly settled, are they not?"

"Listening to you from earlier, you talk back to the deity at every turn. You have not reformed your heart from the very root. If only you could reform your heart, the deity would make your legs stand like ours. Please stop your quibbling quickly and reform your heart."

Komayamahiko: "Ha, ha, ha! Even if you speak of reforming one's heart, how can one listen to what is said by a guardian spirit who possesses a fellow like Teruhiko? Divine spirits possess people in accordance with right and wrong, wisdom and folly. If one largely takes this physical body as a standard, one should know the rank of the spirit."

Teruhiko: "Now, now, Odoyamatsumi, using the standing of your legs as the occasion, advance immediately toward the land of Karu. Taking a companion is strictly forbidden."

"Obeying your command and having reformed my heart, I shall go immediately. Until now, I had made a terrible mistake in my thinking."

Teruhiko: "Leave this place as quickly as possible, swiftly without a moment's delay."

Komayamahiko: "Ah, Lord Odoyama, that is too selfish. It may be fine for you whose legs have stood up, but even if you abandon those of us whose legs do not stand and go, would that conform to the way of the gods? Abandoning the distress of a friend, where are you going? I have heard that kindness is foremost in the way of the gods. If so, your way must be wrong."

Teruhiko: "There is no need to listen to Komayamahiko's petty quibbling; stand up quickly and go. Shall I give Komayamahiko a thorough grilling until he can reform his heart?"

As Odoyamatsumi was about to slowly leave this place, the bright full moon rose over the mountain peak and shone brilliantly upon the narrow valley. Odoyamatsumi, gaining strength from this moonlight, followed the valley path while singing a propagation song, and went down the road by which he had come.
