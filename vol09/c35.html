Chapter 35: The Autumn Moon [428]

第35章　秋の月〔428〕

§ 1

Mingling green and scarlet, weaving the brocade of the autumn hills and fields, Sayohime's heart-red maple leaves are beaten down by the cold winter blast as if to rot completely away; Takonohime rises to add blossoms to this desolate scene and claps her maple-like hands as she sings so gracefully, while the depths of her heart are truly pitiable.

"Whether the morning sun shines or clouds gather, whether the rain falls or clears away, whether the snow piles up or melts away, how could the person who follows the teachings protected by the true deity ever fear the Way of the Gods, even if earthquakes, thunder, carts of fire, hundreds of disasters, and tens of thousands of raging Magatsukami should attack? Who is Takatoribake? Who is the Tokoyo Shin'o? They are mortal children, children of sin; we are children of God, divine offspring. How could the children of God who obey God have anything to fear? The deep devotion of Haruyamahiko and Natsuhime conforms to the divine mind of the awe-inspiring, exalted deity, and as for the Magatsukami raging upon the ridges near and far,

they have subdued even the Shikome and the Sagume, reporting their return before the deity right before our eyes. Who is it that appears in the Forest of Aida as a missionary of Ananai-kyo, singing the propagation song in a voice so cool? May Matsu, Take, and Umegakahime fragrantly unfold this joyous news and divine authority. What of the ugly cries of the fierce deities who wring out fresh blood? There can be no blade that dares turn against us of strong faith. Onitakehiko, who crushes even demons, where are Asahi and Takakura now? May the white fox of the sun and moon appear and save us from our present distress. May it dispel the disasters that fall upon Haruyamahiko.

Missionary of Ananai-kyo, born as the middle daughter among pine, bamboo, and plum, hear from high in the sky the prayers of Takonohime. Otomehime, who dwells at the bottom of the thousand-fathom sea, must also be listening. Takonohime set sail from the port of Ataru and drifted across the ocean where rough waves raged; just as you saved me, please save in peace the missionaries of the teaching of the Way, the hemp-pillars who have now appeared. Hear this most peacefully. God is with me. I am a noble missionary; the divine spirit protects me. Protect us, Amatsukami; bless us, Kunitsukami.

Save us, Yomitsukami. Dispel the clouds that block the Land of Tokoyo, Shinatohiko! The pure-hearted Akitsukihime no Mikoto, Miyukihime, Tachibanahime no Mikoto of great merit, and the missionary Komayamahiko no Kami no Mikoto, pray, oh pray before the deity; pray, oh pray before the deity."

Such is her tender grace as she sings and dances.

With moist corners of her eyes and crescent-moon eyebrows, her skin white and soft, even her stature resembling the full moon of the ninth month, Akitsukihime stands up; her cool voice is like the bell-ring crickets swarming in the grassy autumn fields, and like the pine crickets awaiting their father's return, she sings and dances auspiciously.

現代日本語訳

緑と紅を混ぜあわせ、錦の織物を織りなしている秋の野山の小夜姫の、心も赤き紅葉が、冷たい冬の木枯らしに朽ち果てよと打ち叩かれる、寂しいこの場の光景に華を添えようと立ち上がり、紅葉のような手を打って歌うのも奥ゆかしい竹野姫は、心の奥が哀れである。

「朝日が照ろうとも曇ろうとも、雨が降ろうとも晴れようとも、雪が積もろうとも消えようとも、誠の神がお守りくださる教えに倣う人の身は、地震や雷火の車、百の災害や千万の曲津神の猛威が襲おうとも、どうして神の道を恐れようか。鷹取別は何者か、常世神王は何者か。彼らは人の子、罪の御子であり、われらは神の子、神の御子である。神に従う神の子に、どうして恐れることがあろうか。春山彦や夏姫の厚き心は、千早振る尊き神の御心にかない申して、遠近の尾根の上に猛る曲津神、

醜女や探女も言向け和して、神の御前に復命を申し上げなさるのは目の当たりである。三五教の宣伝使が間の森に現れて、声も涼しく宣伝歌を歌ってやって来るのは何者か。嬉しい便りを松竹や梅ヶ香姫が香ばしく、開かせたまえ神の威光を。生血を絞る鬼神の醜い叫びも何のその、信仰の強いわれわれに刃向かう刃はあるまい。鬼をも挫く鬼武彦、旭、高倉は今いずこ。月日の白狐が現れて、今の悩みを救ってほしい。春山彦に降りかかるその災厄を祓ってほしい。

三五教の宣伝使、松竹梅と三つ栗の仲娘として生まれた竹野姫の祈り言を、御空高くお聞き届けください。千尋の海の底にいらっしゃる乙米姫もお聞きになっているだろう。アタルの港を船出して、荒波たける海原を漂い来た竹野姫、私を救われたそのように、今現れなさった麻柱の道の教えの宣伝使を救わせたまえ。平らかに、いと安らかにお聞き届けください。神は私と共にいらっしゃる。われは尊き宣伝使、神の身魂がお守りくださる。守りたまえや天津神、恵みたまえや国津神。

救わせたまえ黄泉神。常世の国に塞がれる雲を払えよ科戸彦。心澄みきる秋月姫の貴の命や深雪姫、大いなる功績のある橘姫の妹の命や駒山彦の神の命の宣伝使よ、祈れよ祈れ神の前で、祈れよ祈れ神の前で」

と歌い舞うしおらしさである。

滴るような目じり、三日月の眉、肌も白く柔らかく、身の丈までも長月の十五夜の月に似せている秋月姫は立ち上がり、涼しい声は秋の野の草野に群がり鳴く鈴虫か、父の帰りを待つ松虫の声もめでたく歌い舞う。

§ 2

Nurtured by father and mother, Of deep and maternal blessing; The baby talk of "Hō-hokekyō" From the nightingale that could not wait for spring, Gradually grew familiar, and the sisters Are beside their deeply beloved mother, Natsuhime of abundant benevolence; The august blessing of their father no-Mikoto Is higher than the mountains, and clear; How grateful that the autumn moon illuminating the august sky Protects my body. Praised as moon, snow, and flowers, Renowned throughout the Land of Hazama, In the age ruled by the divine no-Mikoto, The gloriously shining Haruyamahiko, May it last forever like eternal spring, Enduring like the pines upon the ridge; Branches flourish, and leaves grow thick; Upon the flourishing pines, O red-crowned cranes, build your nests in this sacred age.

Though it is a time when the people of the four directions harmonize, Enticed by the eighty Magatsu that come rampaging, Seeking to violate the divine teachings, The vile god Takatoribewake, Using the Ural teachings as a shield, Ah, how detestable the crooked teachings, Clouded the hearts of the people of the world. The bird-ships of heaven danced madly, On earth the Magatsumi roared and raged, And countless disasters Arose all at once in the underworld. Subduing the wicked armies through gentle words, Restoring the world to a sacred divine age, And seeking to save the multitudes of people, Konohana-hime appeared; To quiet the words of men, And to consolidate a world disordered like reaped reeds, While bestowing Her divine care, The divine messengers keep watch in all four directions.

Departing to the eight islands far and near, Even as far as the Land of Koshi, Gathering the wise women of pine, bamboo, and plum, And the fair maidens of moon, snow, and flowers, Firmly pounding the foundation of the divine land. How noble is that achievement! How awe-inspiring is that achievement!

Thus in a clear voice did Akizukihime recount the exalted virtue of the deities, and expressed gratitude for the missionary's hardships.

Suddenly, the neighing of horses, the sound of hooves, and many human voices were heard in front of the gate. Just as Natsuhime stood up and opened the gate, Haruyamahiko, straddling a horse alongside a single missionary, entered unhurriedly. From atop his horse, Haruyamahiko called out:

"Villagers, thank you for your great labor. Now that things have reached this point, all is well; inform the barrier station of Nakayoribewake as quickly as possible and bring a palanquin. As I have matters to investigate a little concerning this missionary, come to meet us again tomorrow evening."

When he shouted this in a loud voice, the many villagers,

"Oh!"

answered, and departed from before the gate like an ebbing tide. Haruyamahiko leapt down lightly and, lowering his voice, said:

"Missionary Teruhiko, please rest comfortably in this unsightly, dilapidated hut. There are many things I wish to show you."

Scarcely had he heard this when Teruhiko leapt down lightly and, guided by the husband and wife, appeared unhurriedly before everyone.

と秋月姫は声も涼やかに神明の高徳を述べ、宣伝使の労苦に感謝した。

たちまち門前に聞こえる馬の嘶き、蹄の音、大勢の人の声。夏姫が立ち上がって門を開けるちょうどその時、春山彦は、一人の宣伝使とともに馬にまたがり、悠々と入って来た。春山彦は馬上から、

「村人たちよ、大儀であった、もはやこうなった上は大丈夫である、一刻も早く中依別の関所に知らせ、駕籠を持って来い。我はこの宣伝使について、少し取り調べる仔細があるから、明日の夕方に再び迎えに来い」

と大音声で呼ばわると、大勢の村人は、

「オー」

と答えて、潮が引くように門前を立ち去った。春山彦はひらりと飛び下り、声を低くして、

「照彦宣伝使様、見苦しいあばら家ですが、ごゆっくりご休息なさってください。あなたにお目にかたいものがたくさんございますので」

と聞く間もなく照彦はひらりと飛び下り、夫婦の案内に従って一同の前に、悠然として現れた。

忽ち門前に聞ゆる馬の嘶き、蹄の音、数多の人声。夏姫は立ち上り門を開く折しも、春山彦は、一人の宣伝使と共に馬に跨り、悠々と入り来たる。春山彦は馬上より、

『村人共、御苦労千万、最早かくなる上は大丈夫、一時も早く中依別の関所に報らせ、駕籠を持ち来れ。われはこの宣伝使に就て、少しく取調ぶる仔細あれば、明日の夕方復び迎ひに来れ』

『オー』

『照彦の宣伝使様、見苦しき荒屋、ゆるゆる御休息下さいませ。貴方にお目にかけたきものが沢山ござれば』

と聞く間もあらず照彦はヒラリと飛び下り、夫婦の案内につれて一同の前に、悠々として現はれにける。
