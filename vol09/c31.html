Chapter 31: The Seven Women [424]

第31章　七人の女〔424〕

§ 1

Without distinction between the inside and outside of the sea, the divine majesty shines across. Separated by the waves of Tokoyo, over the rough waves of the northern and southern continents, like Takasago, lies the divine grove of Hazama-no-kuni. In the spring mountains where flowers bloom and give off fragrance, Haruyamahiko, the head of the village, possesses a flower of the heart that is beautiful, whether of plum, cherry, or peach. Haruhime, with whom the fields and mountains laugh, patterns the young green of the fields. Flourishing and prospering in the skies of the fifth month, the darkness clears away for Natsuhime; the moon shining in the sky of her heart is dazzling in light and serenely clear. Akizukihime's true heart is like weaving a brocade of autumn foliage. Parting the eastern sea and ascending, the form of the moon is also in the western sky.

Upon the summit of the sky-piercing mountain, Miyukihime shines across with the glitter of deep snow. In a cold-hearted and merciless world, like the blooming of spring flowers and the dripping green of summer mountains is the affection of husband and wife. The teachings of the Kami are also like the tachibana, Tachibanahime whose fragrance is ever-present. The true hearts of the five parents and children became known as the heart of the Kami, the soul of Izu that saves the world. Waiting for the reign of Miroku, Matsuyohime seeks to clear the sky of Tokoyo; Takenohime is like bamboo and sasa grass whose heart endures the dew and frost of spring, summer, and autumn, bending like pliant grass buffeted by the wind. Buffeted by the cold wind, with swords of frost and garments of snow, the color of her heart remains eternal.

Anticipating myriad flowers, blooming and fragrant is Umegakahime; her true heart is sweet-scented, and the buds of the flower are lovely. The divine protection is conspicuous; the attendant deities of Onitakehiko, who appeared on Mount Ōeyama. The divine majesty is Takakura; the white fox Asahi shines across the sky, and together with Tsukihi they transform themselves, and their deeds are indeed valiant.

Onitakehiko stood up, sat heavily in the center of the room, and said:

"The moon of the sixteenth night of this month of the Boar, of so pure a Water-junior, has already tilted toward the western mountains; with the crowing of the cock announcing the fourth watch, the yang energy rises in the eastern sky, and the intensely bright Asahigitsune shall ascend high into the sky like Takakura. Without any barrier to check the steed of days and months, since departing from Mount Ōeyama, under the command of the Sun-Rising Kami who illuminates the world without leaving a single corner dark in the east, west, north, and south, I crossed over to the land of Tokoyo, disguised my form in myriad transformations, and served the divine plan. I am a humble white fox deity, and while leading numerous retainers and defending the great path of the Kami, I racked my mind morning and evening to overturn the perverse plots of the prideful Takatoribetsu, protecting Ananai-kyō alongside Asahi, Takakura, and Tsukihi; pray comprehend the heart of Onitakehiko, who smashes even demons. The perverse deeds of Ōkunihiko, cursed by the Yamata-no-Orochi, are uniquely atrocious and villainous; the three deities Takatoribetsu, Tōyamabetsu, and Nakayoribetsu keep watch in all directions to capture the princesses, posting countless ugly hags and spies. In such peril, using strategy, I became Takatoribetsu's vassal, ingratiated myself by falsely naming myself Takeyamahiko, and favorably won the regard of Tokoyo Jin'ō. Being entrusted with today's duty is a widespread omen of Heaven's grace, the true divine plan of aiding good and destroying evil; haha, how joyful, how joyful, and how awe-inspiring it is. That being so, all of you present, by no means let down your guard together. When one desire is fulfilled, another arises; such is the boundless ambition of the evil spirits of body-master-spirit-servant. Sometime again, watching for a gap like a swift horse passing through a chink, they may unexpectedly snatch back the three daughters of Moon, Snow, and Flower. Only through the blessings of Kaminahobi-no-Kami and Ōnahobi-no-Kami, repel the great disasters that befall you with the words of the exalted Kami, and entrust your hearts to the Kami morning and evening. The voice of the cock proclaims the dawn; if time is lost, it will be a grave matter. I shall depart from this place henceforth and proceed to the mansion of Takatoribetsu. Farewell to all."

現代日本語訳

海の内外の区別なく、神の御稜威は照り渡る。常世の波を隔てた、北と南の大陸の、荒れる波も高砂の、間の国の神の森。花が咲き香る春山の、郷の司の春山彦。心に咲く花も美しく、梅か桜か桃の花。野山も笑う春姫の、彩る野辺の若緑。栄えに栄えて五月空、暗闇も晴れていく夏姫の、心の空に照る月は、光まばゆく澄み渡り、秋月姫の真心は、紅葉の錦を織るかのよう。東の海を分け昇る、月の姿も西の空。

空を突く山の頂に、光も深雪がきらきらと、輝きわたる深雪姫。冷酷無惨な世の中に、春の花が咲き夏山の、緑が滴る夫婦の情。神の教えも橘の、いつでも香る橘姫。親子五人の真心は、厳の身魂の世を救う、神の心と知られたことだ。ミロクの御代を待つ松代姫。常世の空を晴らそうと、春夏秋の露や霜を、凌ぐ心の竹や笹のように、風にもまれるなよ草の、ただ撓むばかりの竹野姫。霜の剣や雪の衣、冷たい風にもまれながら、心の色は永久に変わらない。

万の花に先駆けて、咲き香る梅ケ香姫の、真心こそは香ばしく、花の蕾は麗しい。神の守りが顕著で、大江山に現れた、鬼武彦の御従神。神の御稜威も高倉よ、空を照り渡る白狐の旭、月日も共に変身し、その働きは健気である。

鬼武彦は立ち上がり、座敷の中央にどっかりと座って、

「あれほど清らかな癸の、亥の月の今日の十六夜の月は早くも西山に傾いたので、四更を告げる鶏鳴に、東の空は陽気が立ち上り、光の強い旭狐が空高く高倉と昇るであろう。月日の馬を留める関所もなく、大江山を出てから、東へ西へ北へ南へと、世界を隈なく世を照らす日の出の神の御指揮を受け、常世の国へ渡って来て、千変万化に身を変装させ、神の経綸にお仕えした、私は卑しい白狐神であり、数多くの眷属を引き連れて、神の大道を守る際、心のおごった鷹取別の不正な企てを覆そうと、朝な夕なに心を砕き、旭、高倉、月日と共に、三五教を守護した、鬼をも打ち砕く鬼武彦の心を察していただきたい。八岐大蛇に呪われた大国彦の悪行は、比類のない悪逆無道であり、鷹取別や遠山別、中依別の三柱の神は、姫の命を捕らえようと四方八方に目を配り、醜女や探女を数限りなく配置しているその危うさの中で、計略を用いて鷹取別の臣下となり、竹山彦と偽って巧みに取り入り、常世神王の覚えもめでたく、今日の務めをお言いつけいただいたのは、天の恵みのあまねき前兆であり、善を助け悪を滅ぼす、誠の神の経綸である、ははあ、嬉しくもあり、忝いことである。そうではあるが、皆様方、決して油断なさるな。一つ叶えばまた一つと、欲望に際限のない、体主霊従の邪神の魂胆であり、走る馬のようにいつかまた隙を狙って、月・雪・花の三人の娘御を奪い返すことも測り知れない。ただ何事も神直日の神、大直日の神の御恵みによって、降りかかる大難を尊い神の神言によって払い退け、朝な夕なに神へ心を委ねられよ。暁を告げる鶏の声、時機を逃しては一大事である。私はこれよりこの場を立ち去り、鷹取別の館に参ろう。皆様、さらば」

§ 2

Scarcely had it been spoken when the form vanished, and where it went, white smoke it became, turning into a dream and illusion.

At the incomprehensible state of this place, beginning with Haruyamahiko, the seven women, likened even to flowers, were stunned and for a while had no words, but Haruyamahiko stood up, worshipped Heaven, worshipped Earth, and said,

"Ah, how grateful, how precious; has God also beheld the sincerity of parent and child, and husband and wife?"

Thus, with tears, he began very gracefully to sing the song of propagation. The seven women also in unison said,

"God appears upon the surface, Setting apart good and evil. Kaminaobi who created this world, Ōnaobi broad of heart, In all things whatsoever in the human world, Look again straightly, hear again straightly, Proclaim anew the crooked things of the world. Though the morning sun shines or is clouded over, Though the moon waxes or wanes, Even though the great earth should sink, The true God saves the world, The true God saves the world."

The sound of clapping their hands as they sang was enough to shake heaven and earth. Matsushirohime stood up,

"Heaven and earth harmonize with each other; The people of all quarters before the divine wind Bow down in a world where Matsushirohime, The God Miroku appearing, The five august spirits of parent and children, The three august spirits of pine, bamboo, and plum; The moon of the fifteenth night also high in the sky, Shining brightly, the divine teachings of Asabashira Wishing to convey, Leaving Takasagoshima behind, The sky of Tokoyo-no-kuni cold, Approaching the capital of Karu, A messenger of God, an envoy of propagation, Exposed to the cold wind, Approaching the Land of Hazama; Is it rain or tears, the dew of the pine, This dewy body to the divine land Dedicated, at the border of the Land of Hazama, Just as I came, before the fierce power of Takatoribake, Like a little sparrow, A frail woman traveling alone, A slender woman with withered plumage, When he was about to capture and carry away, Scorching the sky and descending, A pillar of fire of deep crimson, Struck by which the fleeing Magatsumi Disappeared, after which, all alone, Laying down this exhausted body, Secretly in my heart the propagation song Just as I sang, by Haruyamahiko no Kami no Mikoto being saved, To the rock cave, firm and everlasting, When I came and saw, the dear Takenohime, growing vigorously, How joyful to be welcomed with a smiling face; While the hearts of the people of the world are completely chilled, Among them auspicious Natsuhime's Benevolence deep day and night; God's blessing ever deeper, God's divine majesty ever higher; Children of the radiant moon, snow, and flowers, The favors of Haruyamahiko and Natsuhime, When could I ever forget? Their hearts, when could I ever forget? Ah, how grateful, of the Asabashira Having established the teaching, the Sumegami's Divine majesty shall flourish for a thousand generations; The great merit shall spread in all four directions."

Thus chanting the song of gratitude, she returned to her original seat.

Outdoors, the sound of the heavenly rock-boat and bird-boat roaring across the firmament pressed upon heaven and earth, and the wintry wind, howling, beat against the storm doors, how desolate it was.
