Chapter 34: Whispers of the Forest [427]

第34章　森林の囁〔427〕

§ 1

While the moon of the early evening twilight shone in the sky, the propagator Teruhiko, who had been walking briskly along, stopped by the Forest of Ma to take shelter from the rain and rest his breath, as a passing shower happened to fall,

"The moon shines and shines, Tokoyo grows clouded; Rain falls upon the Forest of Ma."

Thus he was singing. Four or five young men lurking in the shade of the trees said,

"Hey, another one has appeared, it is a propagator! The fellow who showed up last night was like a ghost, but tonight's fellow is singing with a powerful voice. He is completely beyond our power. Unless the whole village turns out and prevents him from passing here, Lord Takatoriwake-no-kami will scold the entire village, saying, 'You were stationed at the bottleneck, so why did you carelessly let a propagator enter the land of Tokoyo?' and we do not know how Haruyamahiko-no-tsukasa might rebuke us either. That being said, the five or six of us can by no means capture him. Quickly, each of you go make arrangements and summon the villagers here. I will keep watch here."

"All right,"

So saying, the five or six young men parted ways to the east, west, south, and north, and knocked around at the houses under the moonlight. The propagator Teruhiko calmly sat down on a tree stump and said,

"Ah, whenever I see it, the moonlight is pleasant. All the more, the beams of the moon filtering through the trees of the forest are even more pleasant. However, this Land of Ma is the bottleneck leading across into Tokoyo. I cannot remain careless as I have been until now. Paying attention in all directions, I should make a prayer to the gods so as not to fall into the enemy's trap. Oh, yes, indeed,"

While thus muttering to himself, he made the sound of clapping hands echo through the woods, and just as he was reciting divine words in a clear and ringing voice, the lights of countless hundreds of lanterns came flickering, weaving through the vastly wide forest. In an instant, a dense crowd surrounded Teruhiko from a distance, the lantern fires like summer fireflies circling around. Yet, perhaps fearing the imposing demeanor of the propagator, not a single person drew near, and a man among those surging into the shade of the trees on one side said:

Person A: "Hey, this one is quite tough. Unless Haruyamahiko-no-tsukasa comes in person, it will be quite difficult."

Person B: "Do not worry so. He will arrive riding a chestnut horse at any moment, for a proper report has already been made."

Person A: "Is that so? Then it will be fine. I only hope he comes quickly."

Person C: "Lord Haruyamahiko-no-kami has both wisdom and strength, and is a compassionate person, but just last night, having smoothly lured into his house three exceedingly beautiful maidens named Shōchikubai, he reportedly handed them over to Takatoriwake under the pretext of granting blessings. I could not go there in the end, but Hachikō next door was saying so."

"Hear, hear, villagers! This missionary is an evil deity who, with wondrous demonic power, sings a propagation song and binds your bodies with paralysis. When he shouted in a loud voice, the crowd blew out the flame of each lantern and fled into thin air. Frightened out of their wits by these words, the cowards whose legs had given way trembled, three over there and five over here. Haruyamahiko once again shouted,

"Hey, villagers, have all of you fled? If you dawdle, it will be dangerous."

From the shadows of the forest here and there,

"Hello, hello, my legs have given way, I c-c-c-c-c-cannot flee. What shall we do?"

Haruyamahiko, in a low voice,

"Ah, what troublesome fellows. Even if their legs have given way, their ears are still sharp. Ah, I cannot say anything careless,"

while muttering this,

"Hear, missionary of Ananaikyō! Now that this Haruyamahiko has appeared, even if you possess secret arts of infinite variety, you can by no means prevail. Mount my horse at once and come to my residence, for there are particulars to be investigated. While the heavenly moon and sun of hisakata shine, can there be any human being who knows not mercy?"

Thus he began to sing to him. Hearing this song, the crowd of cowards whose legs had given way said,

First person: "Hey, what is that? The Governor Haruyamahiko said... 'After a long time, you possessed monster, do you think people do not know? How pitiful!'"

Second person: "How noble! He is indeed the Governor Haruyamahiko!"

Hearing this song, Teruhiko tilted his head and thought for a while, but then,

"The light of the stars that seemed to vanish in the rising sun has not vanished, but is merely concealed."

Thus he answered, and Haruyamahiko, rejoicing that the missionary had understood his intention,

"You, missionary of Ananaikyō, according to your words just now, it seems you have yielded. Come, mount this horse at once and come to my residence!"

Thus he shouted. The missionary,

"I am the missionary of the realm. Although it is foul to be accompanied to the house of a demon such as you, for the sake of the salvation of all living beings, I shall deign to ride your horse,"

and quicker than he could finish speaking, he nimbly leaped into the saddle, rode abreast with Haruyamahiko, and galloped toward the residence with the sound of hooves resounding high.

原文（近代文語）

『われこそは三五教の宣伝使、戸山津見の神なるぞ。悪逆無道の鷹取別に諂び諛ひ、この世を曇らす悪魔の部下、耳をさらへてわが宣伝歌を聴け』

『ヤアヤア村人達、この宣伝使は不思議の魔力を以て、宣伝歌を歌ひ、汝等が身体を鉄縛りにいたす魔神であるぞ。われこそはウラル山の大神の神力を得て、神変不思議の術を得たれば少しも怖るることなし。汝らは力の弱き臆病者なれば、生命の惜しき奴は早くこの場を立去れ。汝らが遁げ去りし後は華々しき竜虎の争ひ、春山彦が生命を取らるるか、宣伝使を生擒りにして馬に乗せ、縛つてわが家へ連れ帰るか、二つに一つのこの場の境、足手纏ひにならぬうち早く立去れ』

と大音声に呼はれば、群衆は各々提燈の火を吹き消し、雲を霞と遁げて行く。この言葉に驚いて肝を潰し、腰をぬかした弱虫共は、彼方に三人此方に五人と戦いてゐる。春山彦は又もや、

『ヤア村の者ども、残らず遁げ去つたか。グヅグヅいたせば険難だぞ』

彼方此方の森蔭より、

『モーシモーシ腰が抜けました、ニヽヽヽヽヽ遁げられませぬ。どういたしませう』

春山彦は小声で、

『ヤア困つた奴だな。腰は抜けても、耳は利いてゐる。コリヤ、迂闊したことは言はれない』

と呟きながら、

『ヤア三五教の宣伝使、この春山彦が現はれし上は、千変万化の秘術あるとも到底叶ふまじ。速かにわが馬に乗つてわが館に来れ、取調ぶる仔細あり。

久方の天津月日の照る中に 情けを知らぬ人のあるべき』

と歌ひかけた。腰の抜けた弱虫連中はこの歌を聞いて、

甲『オイ、何だ、春山彦の司は……久振りに、つきもののついた化物奴、人は知らぬと思ふか情けない、と仰有つたぞ』

乙『偉いな、流石は春山彦の司だ』

照彦はこの歌を聞いて、暫し頭を傾け考へゐたりしが、

『昇る日に消えしと見えし星影は 消えしにあらずかくれたるなり』

と答へけるに、春山彦は宣伝使のわが意を悟りし事を悦び、

『汝三五教の宣伝使、今の言葉に依れば往生せしと見えたり。サア、早くこの駒に乗つてわが館に来れ』

と呼ばはる。宣伝使は、

『われは天下の宣伝使、汝が如き悪魔の家に伴はれ行くは汚らはしけれど、衆生済度のために汝が馬に乗つて遣はすべし』

と云ふより早くヒラリと跨り、春山彦と轡を列べて、蹄の音高らかに館を指して走り行く。
