第10章　言葉の車〔403〕

§ 1

Satsukihime stood up and sang:

"Looking up, high is the Plain of High Heaven; The Sun Deity who traverses across it, The guardian deity of the precious capital. Tied by the thread of fate, May it be eternal like a coverlet of mandarin ducks, Never changing for all time. Manifesting as the protection of the land, Pure and upright, intertwined in growth; Punishing the Magatsumi of vulgar appearance, And establishing here the land of the gods. The flourishing pines also, on the island base Of Takasago, pure and beautiful; The old man and woman living in clarity. Though the flows of the rapids may change, The two together, like pine needles, Harmonizing their hearts with one another, For a thousand ages, for eight thousand ages, eternally, Possessing the lifespans of the crane and the turtle, Illuminate the grove of Takasago Island; May you govern it as firm and unchanging as bedrock.

Across the ocean where waves and winds are rough, To west and east, north and south, As the ship weaves steadily onward, Even to the ends of the realm of the deep roots, The Heavenly Deity rules in peace. Departing from the land of Haru, Through the sandy plains under the severe blaze of the sun, Treading their way forward go the four companions. The renown of the divine teaching to humble oneself and yield Resounds in all four directions. While healing the afflictions of the body Of the deity Masaka-yamatsumi-no-Kami, At the hot spring where bonds are formed, The wife and husband who have met by chance, With a pledge of a hundred generations, a hundred years, And cementing eight thousand generations in harmony, Ever more eternally, eternally. At Mezurashiyama where hot water springs forth, Beneath the travel skies of deep destiny, Though the world may change like hydrangeas,

The sincere heart that I possess within myself Shall surely not change for thousands of ages. Having the thread of fate tied To the deity Uzuyamahiko-no-Kami, How peacefully settled is this evening today!"

Thus she sang the kotodama song of the forty-five pure sounds.

Komayamahiko said, "Oh, I am deeply impressed. What a celebratory song of the forty-five pure sounds! May you always remain in peace and tranquility."

Mezurashiyamahiko said, "Hey, Master Komayama, you tumble quite easily."

"That is only natural. It is just like rolling a top down from the mountain summit. Ahahaha!"

"Come now, Master Komayama, do roll the top down from the mountain summit. How about letting us hear a kotodama song from you?"

"Spare me from singing; I am unaccustomed to it."

"In such an auspicious place, speaking of excuses or being unaccustomed—we certainly want you to perform a kotodama reciting anew! Since Lady Satsukihime on the host's side sang in kotodama, it is only natural that you, on the guest's side, make a return song in kotodama. Having already sung once, we have fulfilled our term and are exempt. Come, come, quickly, quickly! Roll and roll the top of kotodama from atop the mountain; this is Mezurashiyamahiko's request!"
