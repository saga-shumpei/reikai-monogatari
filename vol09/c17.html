Chapter 17: Resurrection [410]

第17章　甦生〔410〕

§ 1

Komayamahiko was left all alone in the dark ravine, and the navel of his resolution gradually began to firm up. In the darkness, without knowing what deity's voice it was, the Song of the Fifty Sounds came to be heard.

From above the head to the soles of the feet, Always exercising care and mindfulness, Do not live heedlessly, O people of the world. Awakening from the dream of glory and splendor, Let each apply themselves to their occupation. The grace of God throughout heaven and earth Sparkles across and clears across, Illuminating without any dark corners. Upon the crooked paths of base appearance, Do not defile the jewel of your heart. The Great Deity of the Moon of Three and Five, In the eighty islands of the archipelago He governs, Shines through to every corner. The world opens all at once; The white plums of the garden are fragrant. Appearing in Takamanohara, Weaving with a thousand looms, a hundred looms, The Deity of the Moon and Tanabata, With shining robes, soft robes, rough robes, Raising the curtain, the Heavenly Deities

Rescue even unto the bottom of hell. West and east, north and south, Though the pitch-black night be dark, Though the land at the bottom of the root be dark, Deep is the hope of the Divine Way. In the Yayoi sky of spring where flowers bloom, Upon the path trodden by the radiant Sovereign Deities, Gradually and steadily, The parted spirits of the deities who have passed through time, Changing their steps from the path of ruin, Advance upon the path of truth. The paths are two: good and evil, Unchanging from ancient times until now. By the auspicious Great Norito of the deities, Hundreds of sins and transgressions fade away. In the islands of Yamato forever, Ever flourishing are the peach blossoms, The sacred camellias are crimson red, Their branches and leaves grow thick and extend far, Guarding the world for all eternity.

Our divine island of Takasago, Where the Izu-no-Mitama and Mizu-no-Mitama Assent together, is a fair Treasure island entrusted by the deities. Like old pine trees with thick branches and leaves, Auspicious is the reign that is peaceful. Crossing over to this divine land, For base-hearted missionaries To open the Sango Teachings, What a foolish state of affairs it is, What a foolish state of affairs it is.

Komayamahiko performed one hundred days of ascetic practice in this ravine, his soul was purified, and here he became Hayamatsumi-no-kami; with a healthy body and a clear voice, singing propaganda songs, he crossed steep mountains and forded valleys toward the capital of Karu, advancing while battling the heat and enduring hunger.

Led into the deep ravine Of the deep mountains of the land of Chiri, Made to taste Hundreds of thousands of myriads of sufferings, Hearing the various divine admonitions And teachings of Kotodama, the body shines, And the heart also shines in the land of Chiri. The gathering clouds hanging over the spirit Are blown away, and by the moonlight of a summer night, As if washed clean, Oh, the joy of advancing while being illuminated! Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the earth should sink, Washed in the deep ravine Deep in the mountains of Chiri, My soul, for eternity, Throughout a thousand ages and eight thousand ages, shall never be clouded. Even if black clouds shut out all four directions And the world turns into eternal darkness, In the sincere heart of Komayamahiko,

The moonlight that has cleared forever and ever Is dazzlingly radiant, in accordance with the divine will. The road is three thousand three hundred ri; Illuminated by the Moon of Three and Five, The heart shines like a clear mirror. Holding in the right hand The sword of the sincere heart that embraces all and casts none aside, And in the left hand the Great Norito of the deities, Proclaiming it is also the sacred Divine Way. Subduing with words the spirits of body-master-spirit-servant Who walk upon the crooked path, While illuminating the darkness of the eternal world, Both heart and feet toward the land of Karu, Toward the Land of the Eye where souls shine, Treading over the Rocky Mountains, Crossing over to the Island of Yomi Where Shikome and Amanosagume rage, I shall serve the sacred works Of the Great Deity Kami-Izanagi, I shall serve the wondrous works.

Kaminaobi who made this world, Ōnaobi of vast heart, By the grace of the deities of Naobi, The spirit also shines purely through. Noble is the divine plan! Noble is the divine authority!

Thus, without distinction between day and night, while imparting the teachings of God to numerous people of the land, he advanced pointing toward the land of Karu.
