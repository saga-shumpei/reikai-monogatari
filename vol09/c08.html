Chapter 8: Yearnings for Rebirth [401]

第8章　再生の思〔401〕

§ 1

Momogamihiko no Mikoto of the capital of Uzu had set out to the capital of Haru, was pierced by the enemy's sharp spears, and vanished like dew in the desert—hearing this, the three shocked princesses almost fainted with an outburst of tears, clung to the bottom of the boat, and wept in suppressed voices, so Teruhiko, letting out a breath of perplexity, said:

"What an unexpected turn, my three ladies; that your noble father has passed away is by no means certain. In my estimation, we have fallen prey to the wicked scheme of a villain who, wishing for drink money from us, spoke in such a manner to sway our hearts and achieve his purpose. Pray do not worry at all. Until we go to our destination and see for ourselves, the truth cannot be known. If you are misled by street rumors, and at this crucial time pain your delicate feminine hearts, and if some harm should befall your bodies, how could this servant Teruhiko make excuses to your noble father? It would also be inexcusable to your lady mother in the netherworld. Please, my three ladies, keep your spirits firm; surely this Teruhiko will bring you to meet your master."

Matsuyohime, wiping away her tears, said:

"Ah, Teruhiko, you have spoken well. Your kindness penetrates my heart and pleases me, but the traveler's tale did not seem like a lie, and if the worst has happened, what shall I do? Although, having crossed long seas and mountains with my frail younger sister, I have at last arrived here, upon hearing the news of my father's death, how can I endure losing our only parent under heaven and on earth?"

Takenohime said, "Sister, whatever happens is due to fate; if by any chance our father has passed away, the only one we can rely upon is you, sister, so pray be strong. Umegakahime is barely sixteen years of age, and before the flower bud has even opened, that she should meet such misery—what karma this must be!"

"Pardon me, my three ladies, pray do not worry at all; this is the famed land of Chiri. Look over there! Today the Sun is especially radiant, smiling cheerfully with a pleasant face. If your father were no longer in this world, why would the Sun shine upon us with such a lovely countenance? Let us put an end to unnecessary anxiety and look forward to what lies ahead. Since step by step we are approaching the capital of Uzu, come, come, let us go ashore!"

松代姫は涙を拭いながら、

「ああ照彦、よく言ってくれました。お前の親切は身にしみ渡って嬉しいけれど、嘘とは思えない旅人の話、万が一の時には私はどうしましょうか。せっかく長い海山を越え、か弱い妹を連れて、ようやくここに着きは着いたものの、父上の訃報を聞いて、天にも地にも他にはいない一人の親を、私はどうして耐え忍ぶことができるでしょうか」

竹野姫「姉様、どうなっていくのも因縁次第です、万が一にもお父様がお隠れになったとすれば、頼りとするのは姉様だけですから、どうかしっかりしてくださいませ。梅ケ香姫はようやく十六歳、花の蕾が開かないうちから、このような憂き目に遭うとは、なんという因果なことでしょうか」

「もしもしお三方様、決して決してご心配なさいますな、ここは名高い智利の国、あれをご覧くださいませ、今日の太陽様はひときわ美しく、ご機嫌のよい顔をしてにこにこと笑っていらっしゃいます。もしもお父様がこの世にいらっしゃらないようなことであるならば、どうして太陽様があのように麗しいお顔で我々を照らしてくださるでしょうか。不要な取り越し苦労をやめて、先を楽しみに進んで参りましょう。一歩一歩、珍の都へ近づくのですから、さあさあ上陸いたしましょう」

§ 2

At this time, from a corner of the ship, a man of medium height and build with darkish skin appeared before the four of them, saying,

"I am a man with a dreadful name, called Orochihiko. Since I am returning to the land of Uzu from now, I will be a timely traveling companion; I shall accompany you near to the capital, so please be at ease. Hearing just now, it seems you are the daughter of Momogamihiko no Mikoto, whose name is renowned in the capital of Uzu; having now become the splendid deity named Masakayamatsumi no Kami, he is safe and sound. By no means worry; let us go together with me."

Matsuyohime, appearing joyous as if about to leap up, said,

"You have told us such wonderful news. Thank you very much."

Takenohime and Umegakahime suddenly gained a radiant complexion and, in clear voices, said,

"Elder sister, we are so glad!"

"Ah, how glad you must be, how glad you must be! I, your elder sister, am glad too; my heart has become clear and refreshed."

Teruhiko: "Oh, Lord Orochihiko, you are a person of the land of Uzu; well now, you have told us good news. We too are now relieved by this. Somehow my feet feel lighter. Please, I beg of you, do become our traveling companion."

"Yes, very well. I shall guide the way."

Leaving the Mikasamaru behind, the party left the port town of Chiri behind and proceeded eagerly southward.

Orochihiko walked ahead, and sang:

"Here is Takasago, the land of Chiri, A divine island where Tatsuyohime rests And protects; High in the skies, the divine soul Of Tsukiteruhiko no Mikoto Appeared in the Mirror Pond, And with the blessings of the word-souls Proclaimed day and night, profound is the land of Chiri. The divine official, Momogamihiko, Who appeared in the capital of Uzu, Is a herald of auspicious name; Becoming Masakayamatsumi no Kami, He crossed over Uzuyama Pass, And just as he set forth to rescue The capital of Haru, He was surrounded by the servants Of the wicked god Takatoribewake, And the thread of his life, his breath, ceased once; Yet, rescued by Konohanahime, He was revived here once again. Returning to the capital of Uzu, Taking as his wife Satsukihime, A maiden surpassing flowers And a herald of pure heart; Together with the heralds Of the divine orders of Ogotoyamatsumi and Komayamahiko, Along with Uzuyamahiko, The wife and husband pledging a thousand years— Today is the garden of their celebratory banquet. Rejoice, you three Princesses, for a thousand ages and eight thousand ages; Deep is the color of the pine that changes not; Straight as bamboo grows the heart; Fragrant is the scent of the blooming plum; As sun and moon shine in the sky, together with The accompanying deities of Teruhiko, Carried upon the boat of Orochi, Already have you arrived here! Already have you arrived here!"

No sooner had he finished singing than the figure of Orochihiko vanished like smoke, and though they called and shouted, there was no reply whatsoever, only the sound of the pine wind passing through the treetops rustling in their ears.

Carried across the rugged and distant mountain path in an instant, the four masters and servants appeared here near the front gate of Masakayamatsumi no Kami.

と歌い終わったかと思うと、大蛇彦の姿は煙のように消えて、呼んでも叫んでも何の返答もなく、木々の梢を渡る松風の音が、颯々と耳に響くばかりである。
