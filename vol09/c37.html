Chapter 37: Song of Triumph [430]

第37章　凱歌〔430〕

§ 1

Just as the morning sun shines in the sky, Teruhiko, an apostle by divine decree, changed his name to Toyamazumi, and as soon as he reached the mansion of the deeply compassionate Haruyamahiko, without even a moment to catch his breath, he was placed into the palanquin of Nakayoriwake's captors; without flinching or fearing, singing propagating songs and seen off by many people, he went along swinging loosely in the palanquin. Afterward, Teruhiko pushed open the window and laughed loudly with wide-open mouth.

"Wahahahaha, how thoroughly they were deceived! And how valiant was the deed of the white fox of Onitakehiko; ah, how delightful, how delightful! Hear, hear, Komayamahiko, the apostles of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum, and the family of Haruyamahiko, please come over here."

When he called out in a loud voice, Komayamahiko with thumping heart, and Haruyamahiko and his wife and children with hearts bold as one warrior against a thousand, appeared in this place all at once, and Princess Matsuyo said in gentle words:

"Ah, you are Lord Teruhiko; how on earth did you safely escape? Even as we speak, my heart grows hurried. There is no telling whether the henchmen of Takatoriwake might search for your whereabouts and return again. Flee from this place quickly."

"Wahahahaha, what of it, what of it? Even if Takatoriwake possesses courage enough to crush fierce gods, I, with my wondrous and divine arts, can confound millions of crooked spirits through countless transformations, pacify them through divine words, and cause them to submit to the teachings of God within my design, so by no means be anxious. That I have hitherto been Teruhiko, acting as the servant of Momogamihiko-no-Mikoto of Jerusalem and protecting the three of you day and night, was by the will of Princess Konohana who appeared on Mount Tenkyō, acting as the herald for the accomplishment of divine rule, quelling the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka, blowing away the multi-layered shelf clouds obstructing Tokoyo-no-Kuni, being the reincarnation of Tsukiteruhiko-no-Kami who illuminates without shadow. Teruhiko was merely a temporary name; I am now the god Toyamazumi-no-Kami who bears the name of Ten, of the sacred celestial counting song: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. No matter what crooked spirits may come, as long as I remain in this world, do not worry or fret."

Thus he revealed his identity for the first time. Beginning with Haruyamahiko, Pine, Bamboo, and Plum, as well as Snow, Moon, and Flower, Komayamahiko, and Princess Natsu gazed into his face in spite of themselves, unable to utter a single word. Once again, the sounds of men and horses were heard, and wondering suspiciously what it was, they pushed open the window and looked out: illuminating the darkness of twilight and advancing toward them were high-hung lanterns and battle banners, clearly showing the crest of Tōyamawake—hollyhock upon a white background—flickering as they swayed in the wind.

"Ah, valiant, valiant! Teruhiko will hide in the inner garden and observe your divine actions. Come, Hayamatsumi!"

saying this, he quietly opened the back door and departed. Breaking down the gate doors and bursting in, Tōyamawake, leading his retainers, rushed up into the parlor without the slightest reserve or hesitation,

Takatoriwake suspects that they might by some slight chance be substitutes; though there are the testimonies of Teruyamahiko and Takeyamahiko, it is his lordly wish that, just to be sure, your three daughters be taken back to Tokoyo once to determine the truth. There is no reason to reject the lord's command, so I, Tōyamawake, have arrived as his emissary. Hand over the three daughters at once!"

he said sharply and overbearingly, squaring his shoulders and glaring. Haruyamahiko, while startled at heart, showed a look of determination, and said:

"Though they are my three irreplaceable daughters in heaven and earth, since it is none other than the command of Takatoriwake, I have no reason to refuse; I respectfully accept his will and shall hand over the daughters. Wait for a while."

『ホーイ』

『ヤア、そなたは秋月姫、深雪姫、橘姫か、どうして此処へ帰り来りしぞ。警護厳しき駕籠の中、ハテ合点がいかぬ』

と両手を組み、頭を垂れて思案顔。

『ヤア、春山彦殿、千変万化の白狐が働き、最早この上は大丈夫、心を落付けられよ』

と、言はれて驚く春山彦。

『アヽ、有難や、又もや鬼武彦の御身代り』

と、両手を合せ、神前に向つて手を拍ち声も静かに神言を宣る。神の仕組の引合せ、三男七女の水晶の御魂も揃ふ十曜の神紋、一、二、三、四、五、六、七、八、九、十と、天の数歌うたひながら、男女五人の宣伝使、親子五人は一斉に、心いそいそ宣伝歌を歌ふ。

Having finished singing thus, the missionary, accompanied by the three companions Tsuki, Yuki, and Hana, bid farewell to Haruyamahiko and his wife, and, singing missionary songs in a cool, clear voice, advanced toward Mexico.
