Chapter 27: Moonlight upon Plum Blossoms [420]

第27章　月光照梅〔420〕

§ 1

Night followed by day toward the Land of Hiru, The fields where tigers lie, lions and giant serpents, Sent forth by the voices of the Magatsu spirits, Crossing over large rivers and small streams; With worn-out straw coat and sedge hat, Light of attire in the Land of Karu, Like a flower bud, Princess Umegakahime-no-Mikoto is all alone. Though her woman's heart feels lonely, With God as her strength and sincerity as her staff, Her attire of straw sandals and gaiters Is truly the utmost of bravery. The spring days fragrant with the scent of plum blossoms Passed away unawares, and the mountain fields Once dripping with fresh green are now under a winter sky; Buffeted by the winds of the storm, The autumn leaves have completely fallen, Treading the unfamiliar Tokoyo-no-Kuni, Weary from traveling along the snowy road, While the waves of the Pacific Ocean rise high.

Surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean, Takasago Island and Tokoyo-no-Kuni, Land and land, sea and sea, Joined by the Isthmus Land of Hazama; Princess Umegakahime at long last Arrived at the Hazama Forest.

The wintry blast, mingling even with snow, roared like a roaring lion. Shining upon the waves of the Pacific Ocean, the moon of the sixteenth night showed its form on the surface of the sea. Princess Umegakahime, all alone, facing the moonlight that rose parting the waves,

"Ah, today is the moon of the sixteenth night, with an ever-beautiful face whenever I gaze upon it. I, too, am a maiden of the same age of sixteen on a solitary journey; how the world changes and changes! When I reflect upon it, the time was the third day of the third month, the month of Yayoi; the four of us, master and servants, departed from the sacred land of Jerusalem, renowned as the capital of flowers, and on an unfamiliar journey enduring countless hardships, crossed the distant vast ocean, and by the grateful grace of God met my beloved father, and without even time to rejoice in relief over the reunion of parent and child, we sisters became noble missionaries for God and for the people of the world, once again crossing mountain slopes and traversing rough seas, battling all manner of hardships; and here the four of us, master and servants who relied on one another as our strength, parted ways toward east, west, south, and north in accordance with the admonition of Uzuyamahiko-no-Kami, with the sorrow of the four birds and the tears of the hooked fish, beneath the skies of the fifth month that never dried, leaving behind the capital of Uzu, crossing the summer wilderness without tidings, autumn too has unawares drawn completely to a close, and has the beginning of winter already arrived? Though I say it is for God and for the world, how truly lonely it is; with God as my strength and sincerity as my staff, although I have managed to come this far, I cannot take even one step further. This exhausted body, ah, what shall I do?"

Just as she wiped her tears with her sleeve, a few young and old appeared at this place from ahead,

First Person: "Hey, hey, look at the shadow of that Hazama Forest; it has appeared, it has appeared!"

Second Person: "What has appeared?"

Kō: "Did it appear or not—look, it is a spirit, a spirit!"

Otsu: "What is a spirit?"

A spirit it is indeed, but with 'yu' placed above 'rei'—a ghost. Look, the wintry blast is moaning with a howling sound. Presently from around there will come the eerie rumble."

Hei: "What are you trying to frighten us with? There is no ghost or anything of the sort. What is the matter with you fellows, shivering and trembling, with even your voices sounding unnatural?"

Kō: "I am not trembling! Somehow my body is merely making minute movements."

Hei: "At any rate, it seems someone is muttering a monologue. Shall we sneak over and reconnoiter?"

Kō: "You, go on ahead."

Hei: "Aha, so you are afraid. What a faint-hearted fellow; can our detective work be carried out like that? Takatoribake-no-Kami told us that female missionaries of Anai-kyō would cross the Land of Hazama and head to Tokoyo-no-kuni, and ordered that if we found a female missionary, we were to bind her and bring her back, and are we not receiving a handsome allowance to patrol like this day and night? If that fellow over there should turn out to be one of them, we would receive a great bounty in abundance, and parents and children could live a lifetime of leisure. Unless one goes into frightening places, one cannot eat ripe persimmons; if you do not enter the tiger's den, you cannot catch the tiger's cub. Show some courage, and go reconnoiter first."

Kō: "Ah, that is true, but somehow it feels eerie. Well, in that case, shall the three of us hold hands and go together? If it turns out to be a missionary, there will be nothing frightening at all. It is three men against one woman. It will be easier work than smashing an egg with a boulder. However, if it should be the character 'yu' and the character 'rei'—a ghost—what will you do?"

Otsu: "Whether a ghost or whatever, if three of us are together, we will be all right. Let us hold hands firmly and go see."

Thus Kō, Otsu, and Hei appeared in the shadow of the forest where Umegakahime was resting, and:

Kō: "Hey, what kind of being are you? Are you a living being, or an unliving being? A g-g-g-ghost, or a m-m-monster?"

Otsu: "A m-m-m-missionary? If you are a missionary, then to Takatoribake-no-Kami..."

Hei: "Hush, what are you saying? What a fool you are. Excuse me, young lady, I would like to ask you something. You must be a missionary of the noble and gracious Anai-kyō that we believe in. Pray declare your name clearly."

The wintry blast was blowing violently with a whistling sound. The roaring of the waves came thundering in. Umegakahime thrust her snow-white, slender hand forward, and

"Ah, how bitterly I resent this; I crossed the steep mountain slopes, and..."

Ko, Otsu, and Hei: "Ah, this is unbearable! It really is a spirit, a spirit, a thing with 'ghostly' attached before 'spirit'! Run, run!"

So saying, they tucked up their skirts and fled away as fast as they could. Umegakahime despondently muttered to herself:

"The scheme of the evil gods allows not a drop of water to leak through; I hear that Takatoribiko plots to seek out the whereabouts of us sisters and torment us. A frail woman's solitary journey—ah, if only Teruhiko were here, he would surely be of help at such a time. Ah, no, no! 'Do not make your master a crutch, do not rely on men. God is with you'—such is the teaching of Anaikyo. Ah, have I gone astray? How pitiful is a woman's heart! No matter what strong enemies may appear, I am a messenger of God who must subdue and pacify hundreds, thousands, and myriads of Magatsumi through the power of the Kotodama of absolute sincerity. Ah, God, please forgive me."

So saying, she prostrated herself upon the ground, and just as she was reciting a norito of thanksgiving in a low voice toward the moon shining through the trees, Hei, one of the three who had appeared earlier, suddenly showed himself in this place and said:

"Ah, you are an Anaikyo missionary, whom I had heard to be the daughter of Lord Momogamihiko no Mikoto, former Chief of the Angels in Jerusalem. Here the vigilance of the deity Takatoribiko is severe; female spies have been dispatched in all directions to capture you three sisters, and a cordon like a spider's web has been spread. I, too, am in fact one of those officials, but when the three of us observed the situation just now, I realized that you were indeed one of the missionaries; therefore, I intimidated my two colleagues, scattered them away, and came here in secret. My house is truly a squalid, humble shack, but I would be grateful if you would take refuge in my home until the surveillance eases for a time. This country is a place where the teachings of Uralhiko flourish, and it is a dangerous place where even uttering the letter 'A' of Anaikyo will bring severe punishment upon you. I, too, originally believed in Uralism, but aboard the ship in which your party crossed from the Land of Teru to the Port of Ataru, I came to know the precious doctrines of Anaikyo and have secretly believed in them; my wife is also a devout believer in Anaikyo. Lingering in such a place is fearful, and if you are caught by the eyes of detectives again, it will be a grave matter. Would you not please come to my house as soon as possible?"

と大地に平伏し、木の間洩る月に向つて、声低に感謝の祝詞を奏上する折しも、最前現はれし三人の中の一人、丙は突然としてこの場に現はれ、

『ヤア貴女は三五教の宣伝使、昔はヱルサレムの天使長桃上彦命の御娘と承はつて居りました。ここは鷹取別の神の警戒激しく、貴女様三人の御姉妹を召捕るべく四方八方に探女を遣はし、蜘蛛の巣の如き警戒網を張つて居ります。私も実はその役人の一人、今三人連れで様子を窺へば、まさしく宣伝使の一人と悟つた故、二人の同役を威喝して、まき散らして私は忍んで参りました。私の家は実にむさ苦しい荒屋で御座いまするが、暫らく警戒の弛むまで、わが家にお忍び下さいますれば有難う御座います。この国はウラル彦の教の盛んな所で、三五教のアの字を言つても、酷い成敗に遇はねばならぬ危い所でございます。私も元はウラル教を信じて居りましたが、貴女様一行がてるの国からアタルの港へお渡りになるその船の中に於て、三五教の尊き教理を知り、心私かに信仰致して居りますもの、私の妻も熱心なる三五教の信者でございます。かういふ処に長居は恐れ、又もや探偵の眼にとまれば一大事、どうぞ一時も早く、私の家へ御越し下さいませぬか』

§ 4

"Ah, there are no demons in this world; I thank you for your kindness. However, having entrusted everything to the divine will, even if I were dragged before Takatoriwake and tortured to death, how could I, as a missionary, allow myself to be swayed by human affection and spend even three or five days in vain? With God as my strength and sincerity as my staff, I shall chant the propagation hymns to the utmost and go as far as I can. Even if I am captured by you and dragged before Takatoriwake, that too would be by God's design; I thank you for your kindness, but please allow me to decline hiding in secret at your house."

"Nay, those words truly move me deeply; to such clearly reasoned statements, I have no words to reply. However, even the brushing of sleeves between strangers is karma from a past life; this too must be by God's arrangement. Ah, then without doing such a thing as hiding in my house, please do come to my house for just one night, and I would be grateful if you would let my wife hear the precious teachings of Anai-kyō."

"Ah, if so, unworthy though I am, let me convey the teachings of God."

"Thank you for your prompt consent."

So saying, he walked ahead. Once again, from behind, clamorous human voices were heard approaching.

Looking there, the light of a lantern bearing the crest of Takatoriwake was flickering in the shade of the trees, appearing to head swiftly toward them.

「いや、まことに深く感銘を受けたお言葉、道理が明白な仰せには、返す言葉もございません。しかしながら、袖振り合うも他生の縁、これも何かの神様のお引き合わせでございましょう。ああ、それならば私の家へ隠れ忍ぶということはなさらずに、どうか一晩私の家へおいでくださり、妻に尊い三五教の教えを聴かせてやってくださればありがたいです」

と先に立って行く。またしても後ろの方で、騒がしい人声が聞こえてくる。
