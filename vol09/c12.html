Chapter 12: Setting Forth from Kashima [405]

第12章　鹿島立〔405〕

§ 1

Hereupon, Odoyamazumi-no-kami entrusted the future affairs in detail to Masakayamazumi-no-kami,

"Until the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka, please protect the land of Uzu together with Satsukihime,"

he said in parting, and accompanied by Uzuyamahiko and Komayamahiko, as he was about to bid farewell and depart after staying for several days, Matsushirohime held back the sleeve of Odoyamazumi-no-kami,

"Please wait a moment first. We three sisters have basked in the broad and deep blessings of the Kami, met our beloved father, and now have been granted an affectionate mother; since we have no regrets remaining, will you not please employ us in your service? Though a woman, I wish to serve in the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka. Please, in your broad divine heart, reconsider, listen again, and by all means allow us to accompany you..."

Thus she pleaded earnestly, with true sincerity showing upon her face, and Odoyamazumi said,

"That is admirable. However, it is not within our discretion. Once you obtain permission from Masakayamazumi-no-kami, let us travel together."

Takenohime and Umegakahime, both placing their hands upon the floor with voices in unison, said,

"Please, we too wish to accompany you."

Uzuyamahiko said, "Well, until now there were four men and one woman, and even with that, the journey was quite lively. After all, a silly fool like Lord Komayama is mixed in among us. But now, if the three princesses are to come along, the journey will surely be quite lively. There is a saying that when three women gather, they are noisy (kashimashii). Indeed, if that happens, it will not be a Kashimadachi departure, but a noisy departure. However, that intention is admirable, most admirable; well now, from here this Uzuyamahiko shall go and ask your father on your behalf."

Saying this, he returned to the living room of Masakayamazumi-no-kami, laid bare the wish of the three daughters, and stated it in detail; whereupon Masakayamazumi-no-kami was moved by his daughters' courage and said,

"Ah, just when I thought I had met my beloved daughters, must we part already? No, this too is for the divine land and for the divine way. Meeting and parting is the way of the fleeting world; please, Uzuyamahiko-sama, I entrust my daughters to your care,"

he answered, his voice clouded with sorrow, and Uzuyamahiko said,

"Splendid. Without such resolve, serving the Kami could never be accomplished,"

and appearing before the three daughters, he said,

"Rejoice, you three sisters. Truly your father's heart is splendid. Such a parent begets such children, such children have such a parent, such a husband has such a wife, such a wife has such a husband, such a lord has such retainers, and such retainers have such a lord!"

「どうぞわたくしもご一緒させていただきたいのでございますわ」

珍山彦「やあ、今までは男四人と女一人、それでさえもずいぶん道中は賑わったものだ。何といっても駒山さまのようなでれすけが混じっているのだからなあ。ところが今度は三人の姫君がおいでになるとなれば、道中は随分賑わうことであろう。女が三人寄ればかしましいということがある。いやもう、そうなれば鹿島立ちではなくて、かしましい立ちだ。しかしながらその志は感心感心、どれどれ、これからこの珍山彦がお父上にお伺いを立てて来てあげましょう」

と正鹿山津見神の居間に引き返し、三人の娘の願いを打ち明けて細々と申し立てたところ、正鹿山津見神は娘たちの勇気に感銘を受け、

「ああ、せっかく可愛い娘に会えたと思えば、もう別れねばならないのか。いや、これも神国のため、神の道のためだ。会者定離は浮世の常、どうぞ珍山彦さま、娘たちをよろしくお願いいたします」

と声を曇らせながら答えるのを、珍山彦は、

「結構だ。その覚悟がなくては神様の宮仕えは到底勤まらない」

と三人の娘の前に現れて、

「三人のご姉妹、お喜びなさい。実にあなた方のお父上の心は立派なものだ。この親にしてこの子あり、この子にしてこの親あり、この夫にしてこの妻あり、この妻にしてこの夫あり、この主君にしてこの家臣あり、この家臣にしてこの主君ありだ」

§ 2

Komayamahiko said,

"Hey, Guzuyama, what are you grumbling about? Repeating only the same things, your true colors are starting to show again."

Matsuyohime said, "Then we three sisters shall serve and accompany you. We humbly ask for your favor, to everyone present."

Bowing her head, she gave a polite greeting, whereupon her two younger sisters also placed their hands on the floor and,

"Please treat us well,"

they earnestly requested with smiles on their faces.

Uzuyamahiko said, "Well, from now on it is a long journey. We will cross Teruyama Pass, come out to the port of Hara, trudge the long road through Chiri and Hiru, cross over to the land of Karu, and proceed to the land of Me and the land of Tokoyo, so troublesome formalities only make my shoulders stiff. On our journey hereafter, we shall completely remove barriers such as master and disciple, or old and young, male and female, and speak openly to one another about whatever we wish to say, so please put yourselves at ease with that intention."

"Yes, yes, thank you very much,"

the three wept tears of joy.

Masakayamatsumi and Satsukihime came to the gate to see them off, wishing the party a safe journey, and just as they were about to part ways, Teruhiko came running up and said,

"Excuse me, I am the one who followed in pursuit of the three ladies from Erusaremu. If I part from you now at this place, I feel somehow uneasy. Please, by special consideration, even if I cannot accompany the missionaries, I beg to be allowed to accompany the princesses."

As he held back tears looking reproachful, Masakayamatsumi said,

"Ah, Teruhiko, is it? Though I have entrusted my three daughters to the party of missionaries, they are frail girls and three of them at that, so it will surely cause much inconvenience. Teruhiko, follow behind my daughters and take care of them in various ways."

"Oh, do you grant me permission? Thank you very much,"

said Teruhiko with a bright and spirited countenance, and following behind the party, he departed from the Capital of Uzu together with all the missionaries.
