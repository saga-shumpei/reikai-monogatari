Chapter 28: The Two Couples [495]

第二八章　二夫婦〔四九五〕
§ 1

Ame-no-Koyane-no-Mikoto circled the sacred enclosure from the left to meet his bride, raising his voice in a wedding hymn:

"Parting the blue clouds of the vast skies, Manifested on Mount Ryōshu as Takahikogami, A missionary of Ananai-kyō founded by Haniyashiko on Mount Ōgon! To save the people of the four quarters, I crossed the Ashi desert on camelback, Braved wide rivers, trod snowy trails, and endured freezing rain To subdue the raging spirits of Armenia! Yet Uralhiko's inspectors swarmed like locusts, Casting me without a word into the seven rock caverns! While despairing in deep anguish, overtaken by the god of slumber, Plunging into dark subterranean pools and freezing in bitter cold, I was revived by the faint light of sacred Kotodama! Opening my eyes, I beheld Takeno-hime, Bending like a slender bamboo in deep snow. Her prayers stirred my courage; Climbing the sheer rock face, I reached her side just as Ame-no-Mahitotsu-no-Kami smashed through the stone with his heavenly hammer! While the three of us shared our wondrous karmic reunion, Footsteps echoed outside—the rock door opened wide, And there stood Matsuyo-hime, fragrant Umegaka-hime, Ishikoridome, Tokiokashi, Yatsuhiko, and Kamohiko! Now meeting fragrant Umegaka-hime in marital vows— Ah, what a delightful maiden! Ah, what a radiant maiden! Bound together in love for ten thousand generations!"

Umegaka-hime, circling the sacred enclosure from the right, answered in song:

"Youngest of the three peach maidens born of Momogamihiko, I journeyed across Tokoyo, Yomotsu, Mount Ōgon, The Ashi desert, camphor plains, Lake Akashi, and Lake Biwa Alongside my sister Matsuyo-hime! Now meeting Takahikogami around the sacred pillar— Holding hands in pure love, Uniting our breath as husband and wife! May we serve the sacred work of creation, A couple united like flower and moon— May the flower never fade, May the moon remain forever round!"

Singing this repeatedly, their marriage ceremony reached a harmonious conclusion.

現代日本語訳

天之児屋根命は神籬を左より巡り合い、結婚式の歌を歌い始めた。

「青雲を分けて霊鷲山に現れた高彦神、黄金山の埴安彦が開いた三五教の宣伝使として、民を救おうとラクダに乗って砂漠を渡りコーカス山へやって来たが、ウラル彦の目付役に捕まり七つの岩窟へ投げ込まれた。暗き水底で凍えそうになった時、かすかに響く言霊の光に力を得て、雪にたわむ竹のような竹野姫の姿を見て勇み立ち、岩壁を登って姫の前に辿り着いた。折しも天之目一箇神が頭槌で岩戸を破って現れ、三人が巡り合っているところへ岩戸口が開き、松代姫、梅ケ香姫、石凝姥、時公らと再会した。そして今、床しき梅ケ香姫と巡り合って夫婦の契りを結ぶ。あな麗しき好少女（えーをとめ）、あな麗しき好少女！」

梅ケ香姫は右より神籬を巡りながら歌った。「桃上彦の御子、松竹梅の末娘として生まれ、姉の松代姫と共に常世国、黄泉島、黄金山、アシの沙漠、クス野ケ原、明志の湖、琵琶の湖を越えてコーカス山へ進み来た。高彦の神の命（天之児屋根命）と巡り合い、あな美しき好男子（えーをとこ）！　花と月との夫婦連れ、花は幾千代も散らず、月は何時までも円くあれ。」

こうして繰り返し歌い終わり、首尾よく結婚の式を終了した。

§ 2

Next, Ame-no-Mahitotsu-no-Kami placed the three sacred pillars in the center and prepared to conduct the wedding rites with Takeno-hime, the two circling around from left and right.

Ame-no-Mahitotsu-no-Kami sang:

"Born upon Mount Ryōshu, spreading the teachings as Shirakumowake, I met Ishikorigami by the banks of the Eden River, Weaving the warp and weft of divine truth into seamless fabric! Yet while proclaiming the Kotodama, a ferocious assailant stepped from the mob, Thrusting a sharp bamboo pole through my eye to the bone! Rejoicing that I sacrificed one eye for the sake of the Divine Path, I praised the grace of heaven and earth. Rescued by Azumahiko, I became the one-eyed missionary of the Kami! By the command of Kamu-Susanoo, I excavated the steel of the Alps, forging double-edged swords As divine emblems for the land's protection. Now bound by the sacred threads of Kinkatsukane-no-Ōkami, Meeting Takeno-hime in unexpected marital vows— Slender and tall like thriving green bamboo, Discerning and wise, dispelling evil winds! Dew glistening upon her eyes, lips like flowers, brows like crescent moons— Ah, what an exquisite maiden! Ah, what a noble maiden! Forging our body and soul like a precious sword, Uniting our fire and water through hundreds of generations, How joyous it is to serve the holy work of the Kami!"

Takeno-hime, circling from the right, responded in song:

"Middle daughter of the three peach maidens born of Momogamihiko, I set sail from the port of Yoru seeking my parents, Crossing strange lands, captured upon the serpent's boat, Reunited with my father and mother in the wondrous capital! Sent forth to Yomotsu Island, Mount Ōgon, Lake Akashi, and Lake Biwa, I was encircled on the snowy slopes of the Caucasus By the dark-visaged captors of Ushikumo, Imprisoned inside the cave like a caged bird! While waiting for the appointed hour, my sisters, Ishikoridome, And the mighty Toki-sama arrived to open the rock cave! There I met the one-eyed deity Kitateruhiko— His noble, manly valor ten thousand times superior To any monster of the wilderness! Though possessing but one physical eye, He holds countlessly the spiritual eyes of the soul! Brandishing His forged sword of light to sweep away evil weeds— Ah, what a splendid man! Ah, what a magnificent hero! Takeno-hime shall stand at your side without wavering, Devoting all the truth in my heart before you! Flourishing like a grove of bamboo under evergreen pine, Enduring unchanged even unto the reign of Miroku!"

現代日本語訳

天之目一箇神は撞の三柱を中に置き、竹野姫と結婚の式を挙げるべく、二人は左右より巡り合った。

天之目一箇神は歌った。「霊鷲山に生まれ、白雲別と現れて教えを開こうとする途中、エデンの川のほとりで石凝神と巡り合い、教えを錦の機に織り成して三五の教えを宣べ伝えた。その折、暴徒の中から猛悪な神が現れ、竹切れを持って我が眼を骨まで刺し貫いた。片目を失った我が身の幸いを喜び天地を拝したところ、東彦に救われて一眼の宣伝使となった。神素盞嗚大神の委任のままにアルプスの鋼鉄を掘り出し、両刃の剣を鍛えて国の守りの神実と仕え奉った。そして今、金勝要大神の縁の糸に結ばれ、思わぬ妹背の契りを結ぶ。すくすくと背丈の伸びた竹野姫、露滴る眼、花の唇、月の眉。ああエー女（をみな）、エー女！　貴き剣を鍛えるが如く身魂を鍛え磨き上げ、神の依さしの神業に仕える身の楽しさよ。」

竹野姫は右より巡りながら歌った。「桃上彦の御子、松竹梅の中娘として生まれた竹野姫。父の行方を尋ねて旅に出て、都で父母と再会し、黄泉島、黄金山、琵琶の湖を経てコーカス山の雪路で牛雲の捕手に取り囲まれ、岩窟に入れられて籠の鳥となった。そこへ姉妹や石凝姥、力持ちの時さまが現れて救い出され、北光の一眼の神様（天之目一箇神）と巡り合った。その姿は千重も八千重も優れた男振り。たとえ眼は一つでも、人目に触れぬ霊の目を数多く保たれ、鍛えに鍛えた剣太刀で四方の醜草を薙ぎ払う雄々しき姿！　ああエー男子（をのこ）、エー男子！　竹野姫が付き添い、脇目も振らず神業を助けん。夫婦睦び合い、竹の林のいつまでも栄え、ミロクの代までも変わらざれ。」

§ 3

Thus concluded the singing, bringing the wedding ceremonies of the three couples to an auspicious finish. The assembly then transitioned to a joyous banquet, where the attendant deities composed entertaining songs and celebrated the holy marriages.

(March 4, Taisho 11 [1922] • Lunar February 6 • Recorded by Mayu Tanimura)

現代日本語訳

（大正11年3月4日・旧2月6日　谷村真友録）
