Chapter 29: The Grand Finale [496]

第二九章　千秋楽〔四九六〕
§ 1

The sacred rites of the Palace of Utsushikunitama were concluded in peace, the divine spirits duly enshrined, and the wedding ceremonies of the three couples were auspiciously performed. Ishikoridome-no-Kami stood to celebrate this festival and joyous event with a hymn:

"Like the morning sun rising through dawn skies, The dazzling Path of the Kami shines bright! West, north, south—Azumahiko, The faithful missionary of Ishikoridome, Departing from Mount Ōgon, Crossing the Uchi River and passing the plains of camphor And Lake Akashi on his journey, Traversing the deep snowy wastes to Lake Biwa! Arriving at the fortress of the Caucasus Mountains Alongside Umegaka-hime and Tokiokashi, Beholding Ōgetsu-hime indulging in every luxury Of food, clothing, and magnificent palaces! Swarms of Yakukosu, Hitsukosu, and Kusu deities Gathered before the Palace of the Manifest Land, Worshiping the Three Deities in drunken frenzy, Singing wild and discordant drinking songs! At that moment, the missionaries of Matsu, Take, and Ume, Alongside Ame-no-Koyane, Futotama, Moon, Snow, Flower, and Ame-no-Mahitotsu, Stepped before the shrine proclaiming the sacred Kotodama! Uralhime vanished like scattering clouds and mist, Fleeing high across the skies toward Armenia! Here, once again, the shrine courtyard was purified, The rites of the Three Deities solemnly performed, Chanting the great heavenly liturgy! Washing and purifying our hearts with the sacred wine, By the decree of Kamu-Susanoo, The joyous nuptials of Matsu, Take, and Ume were celebrated! Ame-no-Koyane, Futotama, and Ame-no-Mahitotsu— Sublime are the sacred rites that unite husband and wife forever! O Three Holy Couples! May the grace of the Kami be bestowed upon you Higher than the Caucasus Mountains, Deeper than the waters of Biwa and Akashi! May your love never fade through thousands of generations, Enjoying the bliss of the Miroku Age! Parting the clouds and mist of dark night, Spread the divine teachings throughout the Central Land! Just as the Kami sits upon Mount Sumeru to guard this realm, Grasp the reins of your heart's steed without error! May the love of husband and wife remain true forever, Like evergreen pine needles that never part even when dry! Like fragrant plum blossoms blooming before all others, May you walk in beauty, hand in hand with your beloved across the golden bridge! How sublime is the grace of the Kami! How sublime is the grace of the Kami!"

Thus he concluded his song and resumed his seat.

現代日本語訳

顕国玉の宮の祭典は、恙無く神霊が鎮座せられ、次いで男女三組の結婚式が行われた。石凝姥神はこの祭典と慶事を祝すべく立ち上がって歌い始めた。

「東雲の空に昇る朝日の如く眩い神の道。西・北・南・東彦、石凝姥の宣伝使が黄金山を旅立ち、クス野ケ原や明志の湖、雪積もる野辺を踏み分け、言霊清き琵琶の湖を渡り、梅ケ香姫や時置師神と共にコーカス山の砦に来てみれば、大気津姫と現れて衣食住に奢りを極めていた。八王神やヒツコス、クスの神まで集まり、酒に酔い痴れて歌い狂っていた時、松竹梅の宣伝使、天之児屋根、太玉、月・雪・花、目一箇神らが現れて言霊を宣ると、ウラル姫は雲霞のごとく逃げ去った。ここに大宮の庭を清め、三柱の神祭りを仕え奉り、神須佐之男大神の許しのままに三組の妹背の契りが結ばれた。三夫婦の神達よ、神の恵みを山より高く湖より深く授かり、千代も万代も色褪せず、ミロクの御代の楽しさを共に分かち合え。千木高く仕え、海の内外に神の道を輝き渡らせよ。枯れて落ちても離れぬ松葉の二人連れのように、永久に変らぬ夫婦の契りを結び、黄金の橋を渡り合え。神の恵みの尊さよ、神の恵みの尊さよ。」

そう歌い終わって元の座に着いた。

§ 2

Tokikō of Kanatani Village, manifested as Tokiokashi-no-Kami, then leaped to his feet and began to sing a humorous congratulatory song:

"The three maidens of Ananai-kyō—Matsu, Take, and Ume— Passed their youthful spring without a suitor, Reaching the height of midsummer! Though early blooms have fallen, Like late cherry blossoms bursting forth in glorious second bloom, The moment they embraced Ananai-kyō, They secured splendid, handsome husbands! Matsuyo-hime found the husband of her dreams after patient waiting; Umegaka-hime with her spunky heart married Takahiko-no-Kami; Takeno-hime wedded the stalwart Ame-no-Mahitotsu! The three sisters are bathed in the divine rain, Snug under warm quilts, embracing their beloveds hand in hand! From this day forth, pound the celebratory mochi And bear plenty of noble children, Sowing the seeds of the people across heaven and earth Like stars in the sky and sands upon the shore! Yet amid these three happy couples, Look at the faces of Azumahiko, Toki-sama, Yatsuhiko, and Kamohiko— Left behind like poor bachelors, biting their fingers Like envious temple acolytes at the foot of the pass! Looking like disgruntled lizards, watching in pure envy! Truly, it is an auspicious occasion! Toki-sama's heart is clear as a mirror— Though not a Kagami-mochi, I won't easily scorch with jealousy, So fret not over me! Ushi goes with cow, Uma with horse, Yakkō with Yakkō, and Kamokō with Kamokō— Octopus holding on tight, duck dancing in sacred kagura! O Three Wedded Deities, Remain steadfast and inseparable like the stone of Ishikoridome! This is the heartfelt prayer of Tokikō, Yakkō, and Kamokō!"

Concurring his song, he threw open his mouth and burst out:

"At-ha-ha-ha-ha!"

He rolled about laughing, clutching his belly. The entire gathering collapsed in helpless laughter at Tokiokashi-no-Kami's comical gestures and comical face, rocking heaven and earth with their mirth.

鉄谷村の時公（時置師神）は、再び立ち上がってユーモア溢れる祝いの歌を詠み始めた。

そう歌い終わると、大口を開けて「アッハハハハ！」と笑い転げた。一同は時置師神の手つきや身振りの可笑しさに、天地も揺らぐばかりに笑い崩れたのであった。

§ 3

Yakkō protested: "Hey, Tokikō, why are you laughing like that? Don't make fools of people! You said yourself it didn't matter if our songs were clumsy—poor verses are all the more amusing! But your song wasn't all that fine either; it felt like a carrying pole snapping under too heavy a load!"

Tokikō retorted: "Hey now! What do you mean by a snapping pole? Rephrase that with proper Kotodama!"

Yakkō chuckled: "Even so, singing that much broke more than a carrying pole—it broke quite a few of my bones! Aha-ha-ha!"

Tokikō said: "Now it's Kamokō's turn! No doubt you'll utter nothing very clever, but let us hear the honest truth of your heart!"

Kamokō: "All right, all right! I am a man too! I'll put my back into it and sing while catching my breath! If it's good, make sure to cheer 'Bravo!'"

Tokikō laughed: "Very well, very well! But shouting 'Yoshi' doesn't mean adopting you as a son-in-law!"

Kamokō then raised his voice in song:

"Following from Lake Akashi, trudging through the snow, We reached Kuronogahara! Hearing the Peacock Maiden ate humans, we were terrified, Yet meeting her, she had a face like the full moon— Matsuyo-hime, far different from the dreadful rumors! Casting aside the Ural sect, turning over to Ananai-kyō, We crossed Lake Biwa through fierce winds, Led by the four inspector deities to the Caucasus Mountains! There we beheld an unexpected divine festival: Yakukosu, Hitsukosu, and Kusu deities dead drunk, Mud smeared everywhere, Uralhime dressed in mud! Spouting sweet lies before the shrine, Until Matsu, Take, Ume, and Kamohiko chanted the Ananai songs! At once she turned into a demoness and fled amid black clouds! In the aftermath, the sacred rites were held, And the Imperial Kami issued the divine decree: 'Take husbands for the maidens Matsu, Take, and Ume!' Like sweet dumplings stuffed into open mouths, The men swallowed the decree with gusto! Three couples uniting in auspicious cups of san-san-kudo, Dancing and singing with joy! I am but an unpolished monkey with no talent, Yet let me offer my sincere wish before the Three Great Deities: May these three couples flourish in unbroken harmony! And please, grant me a fine bride as well without delay! Live in loving harmony, fruitful like stars in heaven, Raising noble children for the holy service of the Kami! May Matsuyo-hime, Umegaka-hime, and Takeno-hime, Together with Ame-no-Mahitotsu, Futotama, and Ame-no-Koyane, Shine the divine light of the Kami's Path Throughout the oceans and across all realms without end!"

Concluding his hymn, he returned to his seat. Thus the sacred festival of the Caucasus Mountains and the joyous nuptials of the three couples bound to the Divine Spirit of Misu reached their grand and auspicious finale (Senshūraku).

(March 4, Taisho 11 [1922] • Lunar February 6 • Recorded by Masumi Matsumura / Corrected by Onisaburo on February 19, Showa 10 [1935])

八公「おい時公、何を笑うか。人をあまり馬鹿にするなよ。お前は下手でもよいと言っただろう、下手な歌が却って面白いのだ。しかしお前の歌もあまり立派な作ではなかったぞ。」

八公「それでもこれだけ歌うのには棒どころか、ずいぶん骨が折れたのだよ。アハハハ！」

鴨公は歌った。「明志の湖から従いてきて、雪の道をザクザクと黒野ケ原へやって来た。孔雀の姫が人を食うと聞いて恐る恐る会ってみれば、十五夜の満月のようなお顔の松代姫。ウラルの教えを振り捨てて三五教に寝返り、琵琶の湖を渡り、コーカス山へ来てみれば思ひがけなき神祭り。泥まみれの乱痴気騒ぎの中、三五教の宣伝歌を歌えばウラル姫は鬼女と変じて逃げ去った。その後の尊き神祭りで、皇大神より『松竹梅の三柱に婿を貰え』との神勅。男たちは開いた口に牡丹餅を詰めたように一口に飲み込み、三々九度の盃。自分は芸のない猿だが、伊弉諾大神の神生み・国生みの道を開かれたように、三夫婦揃って縁を結んだこの目出度さよ！　どうぞ私にも一日も早く嫁を貰わしてくださんせ。二人仲良く、天の星、浜の真砂のように子宝をたくさん生み落とし、天地の神の御用に立ててくれ。夫婦仲良く、神の教えを海の内外に隈なく輝き渡らせよ！」

そう歌い終わって座に着いた。コーカス山の神祭り、瑞の身魂に因縁ある三柱神の婚姻は、ここに目出度く千秋楽を告げたのであった。
