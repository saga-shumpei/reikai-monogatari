Chapter 22: A Rain of War Arrows [489]

第二二章　征矢の雨〔四八九〕
§ 1

Inside the rock cave, the two missionary maidens, Tokikō, and the six others sat in silence, tilting their heads in deep thought ever since footsteps came to an abrupt stop before the rock door. After a short while, Tokikō spoke:

"Could the fellow standing outside and spying on what is happening inside be a spy from the Ural sect? It could hardly be an Ananai-kyō missionary. What do you think, Lady Matsuyo-hime? How would it be if we threw the door open, and if it is someone from the Ural sect, gently win him over with words?"

Matsuyo-hime replied: "Have no worry. Please open it."

Tokikō said: "Understood."

With that, he pulled open the door of the rock chamber with a rattle. The man who had been leaning against the door tumbled headlong into the cavern as it swung inward. Looking at him, it was none other than the missionary of Ishikoridome.

Tokikō exclaimed: "Why, it is Lord Azumahiko! Forgive my terrible rudeness! What a wondrous place to meet you!"

Ishikoridome picked himself up, brushing off the dust:

Azumahiko: "Ah, Toki-sama! And Lady Umegaka! What an unexpected and wondrous reunion! Who might the other maiden be?"

Umegaka-hime answered: "Ah, Lord Azumahiko, thank you for coming! This is the missionary of my elder sister, Matsuyo-hime. We heard that our sister, Lady Takeno-hime, was imprisoned in a cavern of the Caucasus Mountains by demons, and we are currently on our way to rescue her."

Azumahiko said: "That is most fortunate indeed! We too heard that Uralhiko's faction was holed up in these mountains, and in order to pacify those evil spirits through gentle persuasion, I made my way here all alone, plowing through the deep snow. Meeting you in such a place is surely the guidance of the kami. Tomorrow let us labor splendidly together!

I had two men guide me up to the front of this rock door, but as I stood outside listening intently to the voices drifting out, I heard the missionary songs of Ananai-kyō. Thinking how wondrous that was, I leaned against the door to listen, and the two fellows—apparently spies of the Ural sect—vanished like mist. No doubt they have gone to report our presence to Lady Ōgetsu-hime. Negligence is our greatest enemy. Come, let us all advance together! As the saying goes, 'He who strikes first gains the upper hand.'"

Matsuyo-hime said: "This is the first time I have had the honor of meeting you. Are you the renowned missionary of Ishikoridome? Umegaka-hime spoke so kindly of you. Please grant me your good acquaintance as well."

Yakkō and Kamokō said: "Ah, Lord Ishikoridome, we are Yatsu and Kamo, newly converted believers! Please grant us your good favor!"

Ushikō said: "I am an old, old believer of Ananai-kyō! Please bestow your kindness upon me!"

Yakkō laughed: "Ha-ha-ha! Old you may be, but until just a moment ago your wickedness was showing, and you were a shivering, quivering believer shaking like konjac jelly! Nothing a fellow like that says can be relied upon in the least!"

Tokikō said: "Quiet, you noisy fellow! Children should go to sleep like children!"

Kamokō: "How could we possibly sleep, until we rescue Lady Takeno-hime?"

Tokikō: "That is true enough. But rather than worrying and fretting till your bodies grow weak, take life moment by moment! When it is time to sleep, sleep peacefully; when it is time to work, then work!"

八公・鴨公「やあ、石凝姥様とやら、私は八、鴨という俄信者でございます。何卒よろしくお願いいたします。」

牛公「私は三五教の古い古い信者でございます。何卒よろしく。」

八公「ハハハ、古いは古いだが、今の前まで悪事が現れて、こんにゃくのようにピリピリと震えていた古い信者さまです。こんなお方の仰る事は根っから当てになりませぬ。」

時公「それもそうだ。しかし心配して心を痛めて体を弱らすより、刹那心だ。寝る時はゆっくりと寝て、働く時に働けばよいのだ。」

§ 2

While they were speaking, a great clamor of many voices arose outside the rock cave.

Tokikō: "Aha, they have come! They have come! We cannot fight properly trapped inside a cavern like this! Everyone, step outside! Hey—Ushi, Uma, Shika, Tora! You four must not step out! Who knows when you might turn traitor again!"

Shouting this, Tokikō leaped outside.

Dark shadows swarmed like countless flies clinging to a white wall, closing in moment by moment toward the rock cave with turbulent shouts.

Matsuyo-hime said: "Listen, everyone! Even if tens of thousands of foes arrive, we have the true kami with us, so there is no reason to be alarmed. Shall we all leisurely recite the sacred divine words here?"

Azumahiko said: "Lady Matsuyo-hime, Lady Umegaka-hime, please remain here and support us calmly with your divine prayers. Tokikō and I shall take action outside!"

With these words, Azumahiko opened the rock door and stepped outside, gazing upon the surging horde with calm composure.

Arrows whistled through the air like a sudden deluge of rain, converging upon Azumahiko and Tokikō. Catching the flying arrows in both hands and casting them to the ground, Tokikō roared with a mighty voice:

"Hear me, hear me! I am the unmatched hero across past and present, whose name echoes through heaven and earth—the Great Deity Toki-ikazuchi! Dealing with three or five of you is far too tedious! Whether you are a hundred, a thousand, or tens of millions, bind yourselves together and attack all at once! I shall crush you like lice!"

From among the crowd stepped forward a gigantic man. He jutted his right shoulder upward while dropping his left, stood in a crooked stance, and squinted his eyes:

Man: "Hear me, hear me! I am the chief of the Hitsukosu deities, Bear of Bikko! The fellow who captured three of the Ananai-kyō missionaries under his own command was none other than myself! Scrawny rōnin Toki-ikazuchi, I shall make you eat humble pie! Prepare yourself!"

Azumahiko then raised his voice in song:

"Though the morning sun shine or turn overcast, Though the moon wax full or wane, Though the snow pile high or thaw away— Holed up in the Caucasus Mountains, We shall pacify the crooked deeds of Ōgetsu-hime With gentle words! Though countless thousands of foes draw near, The clear mirror of the Divine Spirit Shines forth and blows away the clouds and mist! Bear of Bikko and all his band, We shall shatter their proud and haughty noses, Chew them to pieces, and wash their bellies By filling them with the divine Path! Even the Yakukosu deities, the Hitsukosu, And the Kusu gods assemble together; The hour has come to open swift and sure The heavenly Rock-Cave Door! The Kami steps forth into the light To divide the good from the evil! Kamunaobi who fashioned this world, And vast-hearted Ōnaobi— Re-examine in righteousness, hear anew, And speak afresh the crooked words! The clouds atop the peaks of the Caucasus Mountains Are blown away by the divine breath! The crooked fortress of the fierce and raging spirits Is shattered by the jewel of Kotodama, By missionaries who pour out their hearts! Matsuyo-hime prays to the kami For the repentance of Ōgetsu-hime; Steadfast in heart is the missionary Of Ishikoridome-no-Mikoto! Umegaka-hime, who waited long for spring, And her elder sister, Lady Takeno-hime— Send her back here at this very moment, And atone for your sins before the Kami of Heaven and Earth! Kitateruhiko and the missionary Of Odottsumiyama-no-Kami— Gather the three divine spirits And return them swiftly! If you return them not, The Kami shall appear openly To renovate the Caucasus Mountains! The missionaries of the age of divine pine, Harmonizing good and evil; Upright of heart is Takeno-hime, Like the rising morning sun is Azumahiko, Smiling in light like the fragrance of plum! When the dew of divine grace falls, Like Toki-sama who pierces the pitch-black night, A missionary who is an incarnation of the Kami, One among the thirty-three aspects, Revealing light alongside Kitateruhiko-no-Mikoto, Illuminating the Caucasus Mountains! The sun shines bright, the moon waxes full; In the sacred teaching of the San-Go moon, Part the clouds of your hearts, Return unto the spirit of the Kami! Stand restored in your original divine spirit! Plum blossoms bursting open all at once! Plum blossoms bursting open all at once! Plum blossoms bursting open all at once!"

『朝日は照るとも曇るとも／月は盈つとも虧くるとも／雪は積むとも解けるとも／コーカス山に立籠もる／大気津比売の曲業を／言向け和しておくづきの／敵は幾万来るとも／神の御霊の増鏡／照らして雲霧吹き払へ／ビツコの熊も諸共に／高い鼻をば打ち砕き／噛んで砕いて神の道／腹に詰め込み洗てやる／八王神やヒツコスや／クスの神まで打ち揃ひ／天の岩戸を速やかに／開く時こそ来りけり／神が表に現はれて／善と悪とを立てわける／此の世を造りし神直日／心も広き大直日／直日に見直し聞き直し／曲の言霊宣り直せ／コーカス山の峰の雲／伊吹き払ふは神の息／勢ひ猛き曲津見の／曲の砦を言霊の／玉の功に打ち砕き／心を砕く宣伝使／大気津比売の改心を／神に祈りて松代姫／心も固き石凝の／姥の命の宣伝使／春待ち兼ねし梅ケ香の／姫の命の姉の君／竹野の姫を今此処に／送り来りて天地の／神に罪をば贖へよ／北光彦や淤縢山の／神の命の宣伝使／三つの御霊を揃へたて／早く返せよ返さねば／神が表に現はれて／コーカス山を立替へる／善と悪との真釣合ふ／松の神代の宣伝使／心直ぐなる竹野姫／朝日は昇る東彦／光に笑める梅ケ香の／恵みの露のかかる時／かかる例も烏羽玉の／闇世を開く時さまの／神の化身の宣伝使／三十三相の其の一つ／光現はれ北光の／彦の命と諸共に／コーカス山を照らすなり／日は照る光る月は盈つ／三五の月の御教に／心の雲を掻き分けて／神の御霊に立ち帰れ／本津御霊に立直せ／一度に開く梅の花／一度に開く梅の花／一度に開く梅の花』

§ 3

Thus ended his song. The sound of arrows falling like rain ceased entirely with the resonance of this sacred Kotodama. All the numerous pursuers crouched down along the snowy mountain path; among them were some choked with tears of gratitude, and some even wept aloud.

Matsuyo-hime and Umegaka-hime appeared on the scene and spoke gently and earnestly to the entire assembly, expounding the divine teachings of Ananai-kyō. Moved deeply by the pure Kotodama of the kami, all the captors reformed their hearts and pledged to devote their full strength to bringing the Yakukosu deities under Lady Ōgetsu-hime into submission.

When a divine person knows the true heart of the kami, purifies the Kotodama, unites body and soul in harmony with the Divine, and fully manifests the Four Spirits of Courage, Wisdom, Love, and Friendship—no matter how ferocious the evil demons or crooked spirits may be, there is none who will not submit to that Kotodama. Therefore, those who serve as missionaries must first polish their own body and spirit, and maintain toward all fellow beings the very same care and devotion they would hold toward themselves. Those who lack this devotion—no matter how much wisdom they brandish or eloquence they display—must understand that they cannot bring salvation into the Kingdom of God.

(March 3, Taisho 11 [1922] • Lunar February 5 • Recorded by Akiko Katō)

現代日本語訳

そう歌い終わった。雨のように降り注いできた矢の音は、この言霊とともにピタリとやみ、大勢の捕手たちはいずれも雪の谷道に蹲り、中には感涙にむせび、声を放って泣く者さえあった。

松代姫と梅ケ香姫はこの場に現れ、一同に向かって三五教の教理を懇ろに説き諭した。数多くの捕手たちは神の清き言霊に打たれ、いずれも心を改め、ついには大気津姫の部下である八王神の帰順に全力を尽くすこととなった。

神の誠の心を知り、言霊を清め、身も魂も神と等しくし、勇・智・愛・親の四魂の活用が完全に成った神人の宣伝には、いかなる悪鬼邪神といえどもその言霊に帰順しないものはないのである。それゆえ、宣伝使たる者はまず己の身魂を研き、すべての神人に対して自分自身に対するのと同様の心掛けを持たねばならない。この心掛けのない宣伝使は、いくら智恵を振るい弁舌を尽くしても、神の御国に救うことはできないものであることを知るべきである。

（大正11年3月3日・旧2月5日　加藤明子録）
