Chapter 9: 虎猫 [9]

虎猫〔9〕

§ 1

Princess Umegaka was attacked from three sides by the violent man and was somewhat in distress, when Tokikō appeared there and punished Katsukō for her, so that she finally stroked her chest in relief and said:

Umegakahime: "Ah, Toki-sama, you have come at a good moment. He is an annoying person, like an octopus with no ears to hear, no matter how much one propagates the teachings."

Tokikō: "Ah, that must be so. For the northern forest is where the most unreasonable fellows in this vicinity live."

Umegakahime: "And what did you do about the cultivation of Kusunogahara?"

Tokikō: "Well, cultivation is cultivation, but the master said... 'I cannot help worrying about the well-being of Princess Umegaka. Since she is the person who saved the life of my precious daughter, I shall summon the people of the entire village and have them do the cultivation. You must follow her path and accompany her until she accomplishes her purpose'... and so, finding it a timely boat to cross on, thinking that rather than remaining a peasant forever, following after you and strolling through unknown lands singing propagation songs would be Toki's own pleasure, I came in high spirits; however, I lost track of everyone and, thinking that you had already crossed this lake and gone over to the other side, I got into a boat and fell fast asleep. But then I was awakened by a noisy clamor like a quarrel, and when I looked, it was your song; just as I was rejoicing at this encounter brought about by the kami, this person called Katsukō was on the verge of acting violently, so I played a little trick on him."

Umegakahime: "Ah, was that so? What do you intend to do with this Katsu-sama now?"

Tokikō: "I have no plan in mind yet. I intend to deal with him somehow."

Katsukō: "Excuse me, Toki-sama. To win by losing is Katsu-sama's way. There is a saying to tighten the helmet strings after victory; you too appear to be a gōriki of the propagators of the Ananai-kyō, but do you know the propagation song that says: 'In all things, let human beings re-examine and rehear through the Naobi spirit, and rectify the errors of the self'?"

Umegakahime: "Ho ho ho ho, my, what quick wit Katsu-sama has."

Tokikō: "Confound it, how troublesome. Counterattacking in reverse, he is a fellow who brazenly takes shelter under the kami's authority."

Katsukō: "That is the teaching of the kami. Truly, the kami are great beings, aren't they? As they say, 'There is no kami who does not partake of sacred sake'; they know well both the sour and the sweet. Since you are a propagator, it may be bad for you to drink alcohol, but surely there is no hindrance to partaking of as much sacred sake as you wish."

梅ケ香姫「ああ、時さま、よいところへ来てくださいました。うるさいお方で、宣伝したところで聞く耳を持たない蛸のような方ですからね」

§ 2

Tokikō: "Hey, hey, Master Katsu, somehow it seems as though you are the missionary, and as though I am completely listening to a sermon."

Katsukō: "That is certainly so; for Ananaikyō says that heaven and earth will be overturned, so heaven becomes earth and earth becomes heaven, heaven and earth turned upside down. You look upon people suffering as heaven without looking back, but do you know the mercy of earth and Kami?"

Princess Umekaga: "Ho-ho-ho-ho, at last Katsu-san too has been defeated by Ananaikyō. From now on, change your name to 'Master Defeat.'"

Katsukō: "Yes, at the will and defeat of the Great Kami."

Tokikō: "Do not joke; in this cold, it is no time for jokes."

Katsukō: "Far from a place of jokes, it is a place of sake. Well, if ordinary sake is no good, why not partake of sacred sake, fix that sour face, ask Princess Umekaga to pour for you, and have a cup? If you do, your sullen face too will bloom all at once like plum blossoms. Hey, Hachi, Kamo, what are you snickering at? This is not the Plain of Kusunogahara."

Hachikō: "Hey Katsukō, there is always someone higher above. I thought you were a strong fellow, but what is this appearance today?"

Katsukō: "What, I am strong; it is merely that the other side is one notch stronger."

Kamokō: "You may be strong, but you are a man strong only in bad loser's pride, so it is hilarious."

Tokikō: "Hey, Katsukō—hold on, Master Katsu—they say you have been acting as a tool of Ural-kyō until now, but were you not playing innocent like a cat inside the ship, only to glare with wide eyes and bare your claws to imitate a tiger-cat once you landed?"

Katsukō: "Tiger or cat, I do not know. However, man is a child of Kami, a shrine of Kami; Lady Konohanasakuyahime has enshrined Herself within the soul of Master Katsu and speaks. Master Katsu is still Master Katsu, and the possessing Kami is Kami. If you think you are worshiping my physical body, your expectation is wrong, but thinking of worshiping Kami, come now, Princess Umekaga, Master Toki, bow down, bow down! It is gracious! It is awe-inspiring! She will hear anything well; will you not offer sacred sake?"

Tokikō: "Do not mock us! There is no such thing as this blossom or that flower! With a snout like a lion dance, it is exceedingly outrageous!"

Katsukō: "Because it was so cold that cold sake was of no use, I put a bit of fire into it and became possessed by Kami."

All: "A-ha-ha-ha-ha!"

§ 3

Umegakahime, "Oho-ho-ho-ho."

Katsukō, "Do not go around saying 'Ahahaha' and 'Ohohoho' and calling me 'fool, fool' so much. All of you ganging up together with jumbled voices shouting 'fool, fool' is not amusing in the least. The other day, when Kitateru-no-Kami, a missionary of Ananaikyō, was giving a difficult sermon in the northern forest, he said that when the end of the world comes and the people of the world reach the final five minutes where nothing can be done, both those who believe in various religions and those who do not will turn to God in their desperation, and the prayers of their respective religions will blend together into one, sounding like 'Namu Amen Hōrendabutsu, Tohokami Emitame, save us, Kannagara Myōmyō,' so that even the deities will be troubled to distinguish what is what, and thus only those whose voices can be heard pleading 'Kamunagara tamachihaemase' amidst the crowd clamoring that they want to be saved will be rescued; it is the very same story. Even if you raise your voices all at once to call me a fool, your tongues do not keep pace together, so you are just laughing with foolish faces. It is truly ridiculous."

Hachikō, "Hey, hey, Katsukō, do not get so angry. Look at that surface of the sea; your friend the albatross is spreading its wings and soaring freely through the air, is it not? That creature is called a fool-bird, but written in characters, it is the venerable old man who trusts in heaven."

Katsukō, "Well, you have changed your tune nicely; it is the venerable old man who trusts in the divine teachings of Mount Tenkyō; though no white beard grows upon it, it is an auspicious sign that it will soon return having achieved a great deed. Hey, Hachikō, Kamokō, how about you too become trusters of heaven, cast aside your former Ural religion as easily as turning your palm, become believers of Ananaikyō, and finally set out for Mount Caucasus to subjugate the demons?"

Hachikō, "Since even you have reformed, it must surely be a good teaching. Would you not make a request to the missionary on our behalf?"

Katsukō, "What is this about requests and petitions? The missionary is a messenger of God, so you should make your request directly to God; isn't that so, Umegakahime-sama? Is what Katsukō says mistaken?"

Umegakahime, "That is right; well, well, how wonderful that you have come to feel that way. God is a gracious being."

Tokikō, "Long live Ananaikyō...!"

Just as they were speaking thus, the ship arrived safely at the port of Takao on the western coast.
