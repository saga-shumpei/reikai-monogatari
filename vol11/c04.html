Chapter 4: The Plum Blossom [471]

第4章　梅の花〔471〕

§ 1

Just as the two, Azumahiko and Tokikō, utterly exhausted, were lost in warm dreams unconscious of before or after, the hurried footsteps of someone were heard approaching from ahead. The two awoke at this noise, and looking through the light of the semicircular moon that happened to be rising, a pack of gigantic lions, numbering countless hundreds, was running past the side where they slept, vanishing like clouds and mist. Tokikō brought his mouth close to Azumahiko's ear,

"Excuse me, Messenger of Propagation, a thousand lions have passed by... Did you know?"

Azumahiko: "Is there any such thing as a thousand lions? Those were a thousand wolves. Because you sang a worthless propagation song and, though told to chant it anew, did not do so, the deities have grown angry and sent lions to search for you. It is about time for them to turn back toward here soon. Quickly, chant the propagation song anew."

Tokikō: "Even if you say such things to frighten me, this Toki-san occasionally encounters the thousand lions in this field, so that intimidation does not work on a dog that has eaten a lion... You chant it anew."

Azumahiko: "No, you mistook them for lions with your sleepy eyes; those were gigantic wolves. By all accounts, a great serpent appeared, so they fled here. Surely the next will be that thick fellow. Be on your guard."

Tokikō: "By 'the thick fellow,' do you mean a great serpent?"

Azumahiko: "Yes, great serpents are things that hide in grasslands like this. Because you talk so much like an ordinary human, that great serpent appeared thinking to swallow you up whole. However, as long as this Lord Takahiko is present, it is fine. Come, be at ease."

Tokikō: "You are indeed a monster, aren't you? Just when I thought you were calling yourself Akumahiko, you say that eagles are hawks, or black kites—you are a person who speaks utter nonsense."

Takahiko: "Well, whatever. Presently, a long and thick fellow will make his entrance with a rushing sound. Shall I let him hear a propagation song?"

Tokikō: "Indeed. Testing the wondrous function of the kotodama of innumerable propagation songs is precisely for a time like this."

At such a place, a beautiful woman suddenly appeared.

Woman: "Oh, are you Lord Toki? Why did you come to such a place?"

Tokikō: "Ha, you have showed yourself! Hey, great serpent, transforming yourself into a pretty beauty to try and deceive me, but you cannot fool me. Hey, who do you take me for? I am an incomparable hero throughout all ages, such that even the evil deity of serpent-snatching who grabs and devours snakes flees and scatters like clouds and mist at a mere snort of my nose. If you do not quickly vanish, shall I seize and devour you?"

現代日本語訳

東彦と時公の二人は疲れ果てて、前後も分からず温かい夢を結んでいるちょうどその時、前方から慌ただしい何者かの足音が聞こえてきた。二人はこの物音に目を覚まし、折から昇る半円の月に透かして見ると、巨大な獅子の群れが何百とも知れず、二人が眠る脇を雲を霞と走り去って行くのであった。時公は東彦の耳に口を寄せ、

「もしもし、宣伝使様、千匹獅子が通りましたよ、……知っていますか。」

東彦「千匹獅子というものがあるか、あれは千匹狼だ。お前がくだらない宣伝歌を歌って宣り直せと言うのに、宣り直さないものだから、神様がお怒りになって、獅子を遣わしてお前を探しておられるのだ。もうじきこちらへ引き返して来る頃だ。早く宣伝歌を宣り直せ。」

時公「そんなことを言って脅したって、この時さんは、時々この野原で千匹獅子に出会うのだから、獅子を食べた犬にその脅しは効きませんよ……あなたこそ宣り直しなさい。」

東彦「いや、お前は眠たい目で獅子と見間違えたのだ、あれは巨大な狼だよ。何でも大きな大蛇が現れたので逃げて来たのだ。きっと次はこの太い奴だ。用心しろ。」

時公「太い奴って、大蛇ですか。」

東彦「そうだ、大蛇というものはこういう草原に隠れているものだ。あまりにお前が人間臭いことを言うものだから、大蛇の奴が一つ呑み込んでやろうと思って現れたのだよ。しかしながらこの高彦様がおられる間は大丈夫だ。まあ安心しろ。」

時公「あなた、やはり化け物だな。悪魔彦などと言っていたかと思えば、また鷲は鷹だとか、トビだとか、とりとめもないことを言う人だ。」

高彦「まあどうでもよい。今に長くて太い奴がざーっと音を立ててお出ましだ。一つ宣伝歌でも聞かせてやろうか。」

時公「そうですねえ。千変万化の言霊の妙用を試すのはこの時です。」

こういう所へ忽然として美しい女が現れた。

女「まあ、お前は時様かい。どうしてこんな所へ来なさったのかい。」

時公「や、出やがったな。やい大蛇、綺麗な美人に化けやがって、俺をごまかそうと思ったってごまかせないぞ。こら、俺を何と心得ている。蛇を掴んで食う蛇掴みの悪神でさえも、俺のふんと吹いた鼻息で、雲を霞と逃げ散るというような、古今無双の豪傑だ。いい加減に姿を隠さないと、掴んで食ってやろうか。」

時公『ヤ、出やがつたなア。ヤイ大蛇、綺麗な別嬪に化けやがつて、俺を誤魔化さうと思つたつて誤魔化せないぞ。コラツ、俺を何と心得て居る。蛇を掴んで喰ふ蛇掴みの悪神でさへも、俺のフンと吹いた鼻息で、雲を霞と逃げ散るといふ様な、古今無双の豪傑だ。良い加減に姿を隠さんと、掴んで喰てやらうか』

§ 2

Takahiko: "Ahahaha!"

Tokikō: "Here, here, Taka-san, what is so funny? It is one against a thousand! From now on is Master Toki's test of skill. Please sing a conveniently suited propagation song. It is cheering, cheering!"

Takahiko: "Ahahahaha!"

Woman: "Hohoho."

Tokikō: "Humph, what are you babbling? Saying 'hohoho' in such cold weather, acting foolish. Even if you imitate a bush warbler, who would fall for that trick? You idiot, do not play dumb. Toki-san knows the time! Those that come out saying 'hohoho' do so when the plum blossoms bloom!"

Woman: "I am Princess Umegakahime."

Tokikō: "What! As if you could be Princess Umegakahime! Princess Umegakahime was supposed to have departed Kuroganedani Village two days ago in a party of three. There is no reason she would be left alone in a place like this. Master Toki has clearly seen through your true identity. See, you are a thick and long fellow, are you not? My clairvoyance never misses. Are you awed, you evil deity?"

Woman: "Hohoho! The way Master Toki strains himself makes my navel boil tea! Hohoho!"

Tokikō: "Ugh, disgusting! Coming out into this field in the dead of night, in demonic form, what are you prating about? Shall I make you drink tobacco tar?"

Takahiko: "Ahahaha!"

Woman: "I am indeed awed by Master Toki's clairvoyant power. As you guessed, I am the thick and long master of this Kusunogahara. I prey upon many lions. Unless I devour about ten cow-like lions in a single bite, it feels as though they do not even touch my teeth. Hohoho!"

Tokikō: "Oh, what a disgusting creature! Here, here, Lord Takahiko, will you not get up too? No matter how much it is said that one dies and is reborn, and dies and is reborn, dying by being swallowed by such a creature is a bit regrettable. Come, please get up, hurry, hurry!"

Takahiko: "Ahahaha, what an amusing creature. Excuse me, Miss Giant Serpent, even if you eat nothing but lions, it cannot be very rare. Here is a piece of warm human flesh, so how about this?"

Woman: "Hohohoho, that is my favorite food above all; I shall partake of it."

Tokikō: "Oh, that is good, that is good! By the serpent's mouth, Takahiko is saying, 'Eat me.' Gulp this fellow down in one go, and with that, we are even."

Woman: "No, no; it is Master Toki who has such a delicious-looking face, such fine quality of flesh, and such a richly dark-skinned, delicious-looking figure. Hohohoho!"

Tokikō said, "Blast it, what a nuisance, open your mouth; if it is a mouth so huge that it could swallow ten lions without touching a tooth, I will think of it as a tunnel and let myself be eaten. In exchange, I will swing and swing and brandish this staff, and when I have entered your belly, I will poke and poke and thrust about your intestines, so prepare yourself for that. Come now, will you not reveal your true form quickly?"

The woman said, "My mouth has heat like fire. Once inside my mouth, it is like throwing thin ice into a fire; both your body, Master Toki, and the iron staff will all melt away."

Tokikō said, "This is inconvenient. Hey, Master Taka, let us draw lots. The one who spoke first, let loose first, laughed first! It is because you said such a trivial thing that she talks about eating me. Come, keep me company. Shall we let her swallow the two of us together?"

Takahiko said, "Ahahaha!"

The woman said, "Hello, Master Toki, it is a lie. I am Umegakahime, who became a substitute for Lady Kiyohime to the cave of Hebitsukami and whom you carried thither."

Tokikō said, "It sounds like a lie, and yet like the truth; but if so, why, what have you done with my master, Master Kuroganehiko, and Ishikoridome the missionary?"

The woman said, "Both of them are right here. Ah, hello, the two of you, please come here."

The voices of two men from within the grass:

"Ahahaha, Ohohoho!"

The moonlight, which had until now been enveloped in thin clouds and had been gloomy, began to shine brightly.

Takahiko said, "Well, you are the deity Ishikoridome; I have met you in an unusual place."

Tokikō said, "What on earth. It is completely as if I were bewitched by an Omon fox."

From this moment, the five were to wait for dawn and pacify the great serpent of Kusunogahara through words.
