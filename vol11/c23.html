Chapter 23: The Deity Ukemochi [490]

第二三章　保食神〔四九〇〕
§ 1

In the battle of Yomotsu Hirasaka, the supreme commander of the Land of Tokoyo, Ōkunihiko-no-Mikoto, along with Ōkunihime-no-Mikoto and all the other deities, were won over by the divine words of the Sun-Rising Deity (Hi-no-De-no-Kami) and repented, entering into holy service for the divine work. Consequently, the crooked spirits—including the Eight-Forked Serpent (Yamata no Orochi), the Golden-Haired Nine-Tailed Fox, evil demons, fiendish hags (shikome), and treacherous sprites (sagume)—fled to their stronghold in the Ural Mountains to exert their tyrannical power. There they possessed Uralhiko, Uralhime, and their subordinates, increasingly corrupting and polluting their souls, causing them to pursue selfish, material-first actions (taishu-reijū) with ever greater vigor.

These crooked spirits established main citadels in three locations: the Ural Mountains, the Caucasus Mountains, and Armenia. In the Caucasus Mountains in particular, they erected numerous magnificent and splendid golden halls and jeweled towers. There, the vassal deities under Uralhiko's command established their bases, plunging into the pleasures of wine ponds and meat forests, indulging in every conceivable luxury, and turning the place into a haven for selfish gluttons who acted as if the entire world belonged to them. Such material-first mortals who indulged in luxury of food, clothing, and shelter were collectively called Ōgetsuhime-no-Mikoto.

The faction of Ōgetsu-hime selected the most scenic spot in the mountains to construct a grand palace. Gathering numerous carpenters, they exerted their full strength day and night to build the sanctuary, finally completing a magnificent shrine to enshrine the divine spirits. Even Uralhiko and his wife, perhaps dreading the gods of heaven and earth, first enshrined as the guardian deities of the land: the Great Deity Kinkatsukane (the spiritual soul of the Great Earth), Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto (the spiritual power of the Great Earth), and Susanoo-no-Mikoto (the spiritual body of the Great Earth).

Numerous Yakukosu deities competed to send out searchers to gather rice, barley, beans, millet, and panic grass, as well as seasonal fruits, rough-furred and soft-furred beasts, broad-finned and narrow-finned fish, offshore and coastal seaweed, sweet greens, and pungent herbs, offering them all before the great shrine. This shrine was called the Palace of Utsushikuni (the Manifest Land). The festival was vigorously celebrated over three days and three nights. Countless Yakukosu, Hitsukosu, and Kusu deities, wishing to express their congratulations, indulged in sake, singing, and dancing wildly like a gathering of madmen.

As soon as the festival began, the Palace of Utsushikuni began to groan with an unspeakably dreadful rumble. Uralhime, convinced that this was a sign of the kami's boundless pleasure, was overjoyed and indulged even deeper in the banquet. More than eight hundred Yakukosu deities, along with countless millions of Hitsukosu and Kusu spirits, burst out singing in ecstatic delirium:

"Come, come, is it not fine? Is it not fine? Even if we get drunk, is it not fine? Even if we weep, is it not fine? Even if we laugh, is it not fine? Even if we get angry, is it not fine? Even if we die, is it not fine? Even if we collapse, is it not fine? Even if the shrine groans, is it not fine? Even if heaven and earth turn upside down, is it not fine? Even if the mountain splits open, is it not fine? Whether it be Ananai-kyō, is it not fine? Whether it be the Ural sect, is it not fine? Whether we win, is it not fine? Whether we lose, is it not fine? Whatever happens, is it not fine? A festival of three days, or four days, or five days— Even ten days, is it not fine? Who cares about others? Is it not fine? As long as oneself is well, is it not fine? The Ural teaching is the finest in all the three thousand worlds— Is it not fine, is it not fine, is it not fine? Yoiyasa no yoitosassa! Drink and make merry, for darkness lies just an inch ahead! After the darkness, out comes the moon! The moon is a sign of good fortune, but also the end of fortune! Even if fortune runs out, is it not fine? Even if all perishes, is it not fine? As long as we strike down The missionaries of Ananai-kyō!"

In utter frenzy, day and night without distinction, countless Yakukosu, Hitsukosu, and Kusu deities were possessed by evil spirits, howling and dashing about. The elegant halls of the Yakukosu deities were trampled by the muddy feet of swarming Hitsukosu dancing in madness: sliding doors fell, paper screens were torn, wooden doors smashed, floors collapsed, mats covered in mud, and garments pulled out at will and worn to shreds. Dressed in fine clothes, they tumbled drunk into the mud of melting snow; food was ravaged, treasures trampled underfoot, and a gigantic drunken riot erupted. Yet because Uralhime and the Yakukosu deities were all under the control of evil spirits, they were in high spirits, joining hands with the Hitsukosu and Kusu deities to dance in frenzy, their faces and clothes smeared thick with mud.

The rumbling of the Palace of Utsushikuni grew more violent with every passing moment. Oblivious to her mud-caked body, Uralhime stepped before the Palace of Utsushikuni to pray.

大気津姫の一隊は、山中で最も風景の佳き地点を選び、荘厳なる宮殿を建設するために多数の大工を集め、昼夜全力を尽くして宮殿の造営に掛かり、ようやく立派な神殿を落成して、いよいよ神霊を鎮祭することとなった。流石のウラル彦夫婦も天地の神明を恐れてか、まず第一に国魂の神として大地の霊魂である金勝要大神をはじめ、大地の霊力である国治立命、および大地の霊体である素盞嗚命の神霊を鎮祭することとなったのである。

数多くの八王神は競って稲、麦、豆、粟、黍をはじめ季節外れの木の実、その他の果物、毛の粗き獣、柔らかな獣、鰭広物（魚類）、鰭狭物、沖津藻菜、辺津藻菜、甘菜、辛菜に至るまで人を派遣して求めさせ、それぞれに大宮の前に供え奉ることとした。この宮を「顕国の宮」と言う。この祭典は三日三夜にわたり力行された。数多くの八王神、ヒツコス、クスの神たちは祝意を表すため酒に溺れ、あるいは歌い、あるいは踊り舞い狂う様は、恰も狂人の集まりのような状態であった。

顕国の宮は祭典が始まると共に、得も言われぬ恐ろしい音響を立てて唸り始めた。ウラル姫はこれを全く神のお喜びであると勇み立ち、酒宴に耽っていた。八百余の八王神をはじめ、幾千万のヒツコス、クスの神は無我夢中になって歌い狂った。

「さあさあ、良いじゃないか、良いじゃないか！　酔っても良いじゃないか、泣いても良いじゃないか、笑っても良いじゃないか、怒っても良いじゃないか、死んでも良いじゃないか、倒れても良いじゃないか！　お宮が唸っても良いじゃないか、天地が覆っても良いじゃないか、山が割れても良いじゃないか！　三五教でもウラル教でも良いじゃないか、勝っても負けても何でも良いじゃないか！　三日のお祭り、四日でも五日でも十日でも良いじゃないか！　他人はどうでも良いじゃないか、自分だけ良ければ良いじゃないか！　ウラルの教えが三千世界で一番良いじゃないか！　ヨイヤサのヨイトサッサ！　飲めよ騒げよ一寸先は闇だ、闇の後には月が出る。月はツキだが運の尽き、尽きても滅んでも倒しても良いじゃないか、三五教の宣伝使め！」

こうして無我夢中になって、昼夜の別なく数多くの八王神、ヒツコスやクスの神らに多くの邪神が乗り移って叫び回る。八王神の綺麗な館も、多数のヒツコスに土足のまま踏みにじられて踊り狂われ、襖は倒れ、障子は破れ、戸は壊れ、床は抜け、敷物は泥まみれになり、着物は勝手気ままに取り出されて着潰され、雪解けの泥の中に着たまま酔って転げ回り、食べ物は食い荒らされ、宝は踏みにじられ、大乱痴気騒ぎが始まった。しかしウラル姫をはじめ八王神らは、いずれも悪魔に精神を操られているため、皆いい気になってヒツコスやクスの神らと共に手をつないで踊り狂い、顔も着物も泥まみれになっていた。顕国の宮は刻一刻と鳴動が激しくなってきた。ウラル姫は泥まみれの体にも気づかず、たちまち顕国の宮の前に進み出た。

Uralhime chanted her prayer before the shrine:

"In the Caucasus Mountains, roof-crossbeams rise high, The great palace pillars firmly planted deep, To serve and worship the shrine of the kami! The divine spirits of this manifest land: The Great Deity Haya-Susanoo, The Great Deity Kuniharutachi, And the Great Deity Kinkatsukane! May these three sacred spirits dwell here forever, Shining the divine majesty across Armenia, The Caucasus Mountains, and the Ural peaks! Radiating the teachings of Uralhiko To all four quarters of heaven and earth, Protect our reign forever and ever! Protect this realm for all eternity! The groaning roar of this Manifest Shrine— Is it the sacred sign that our deeds Have found favor in the hearts of the gods? Day by day the roaring grows louder— An auspicious omen of Uralhime's merit Shining bright throughout heaven and earth! Ah, how grateful we are! How blessed we are! Mount Tenkyō, Mount Chikyō, Mount Ōgon, and Mount Manju— Calm the crooked spirits gathered here, Cause them to submit from the bottom of their hearts To the sacred teachings of the Ural deity, And peacefully protect all under heaven! With utmost earnestness, she knelt and prayed before the shrine. At this moment, hordes of Yakukosu, Hitsukosu, and Kusu deities surged toward the sanctuary like a tidal wave, shouting once again:

"Is it not fine, is it not fine, is it not fine? Who cares about the shrine? Is it not fine? As long as we have sake to drink, is it not fine? Drink, drink, drink! Darkness lies just an inch ahead! Who cares what comes after? Is it not fine? After the darkness, out comes the moon! Even if fortune runs out, is it not fine? Even if the world comes to an end, is it not fine? Is it not fine, is it not fine, is it not fine?"

Thousands of revelers collapsed in drunkenness, covered in mud, leaping, frolicking, and dancing without distinction between high and low.

と一生懸命神前に拝跪して祈つて居る。此の時数多の八王神、ヒツコス、クスの神は神殿に潮の如く集まり来り、又もや、

『ヨイヂヤナイカヨイヂヤナイカヨイヂヤナイカ／お宮はどうでもヨイヂヤナイカ／酒さへ飲んだらヨイヂヤナイカ／飲めよ飲め飲め一寸先や暗よ／後はどうでもヨイヂヤナイカ／暗の後には月が出る／運の尽きでもヨイヂヤナイカ／この世の尽きでもヨイヂヤナイカ／ウラルの姫の泥まぶれ／笑うて見るのもヨイヂヤナイカ／上でも下でもヨイヂヤナイカ／八王でもビツコスでもヨイヂヤナイカ／三五教でもヨイヂヤナイカ／ウラル教捨ててもヨイヂヤナイカ／お宮が唸つてもヨイヂヤナイカ／潰れた所でヨイヂヤナイカ／お酒が一番ヨイヂヤナイカ／ヨイヂヤナイカヨイヂヤナイカヨイヂヤナイカ』

と数千の群衆は口々に酔ひ潰れ、泥にまぶれ、上下の区別なく飛廻り跳狂ひ踊り騒いで居る。

§ 3

Into this chaotic scene calmly stepped the missionaries of Ananai-kyō, led by Matsu, Take, and Ume, alongside Ishikoridome-no-Kami, Amenomahitotsu-no-Kami, Odoyamatsumi-no-Kami, Tokiokashi-no-Kami, Yatsuhiko-no-Kami, and Kamohiko-no-Kami. Raising their voices in unison, they sang:

"The Kami steps forth into the light To divide the good from the evil! Kamunaobi who fashioned this world, And vast-hearted Ōnaobi— In all human affairs beneath the sun, Re-examine in righteousness, hear anew! Gathered upon the Caucasus Mountains, Beginning with Uralhime, All the hundreds of Yakukosu, Hitsukosu, And Kusu deities— Awaken swiftly in the sacred Fire and Water of the Imperial Kami! Reform your wretched spirits! The crooked deeds of Ōgetsu-hime— Re-examine in righteousness, speak anew! The Kami is with us! Now is the hour when the wretched crooked spirits perish! Now is the hour when the eighty fiendish hags perish! The Eight-Forked Serpents, crooked demons, Wretched foxes, and tens of thousands of evil souls— Cast them out before the Imperial Kami! Open your eyes! Awaken from your stupor! Enshrined within the Great Palace of Utsushikuni, The wrath of the Three Deities is before your very eyes, Echoing in rumbles throughout heaven and earth! Sober up from your drunken haze! Awaken your sight! Push open the veil covering your hearts! Unto the sincere heart of the Sun-Rising Deity, Radiant as the morning sun across the sky— Return, return, O all ye people! O Uralhiko! O Uralhime! The Kami, yearning to save you, Pours out immense love and sends us forth! We are the messengers of the Kami, Missionaries of Ananai-kyō! Through the Kotodama of ten thousand sacred songs, Restore the true pillar of your soul! Renovate your spirits without a moment's delay! The renovation and restoration of body and soul! The rectification of material-first living! Bring an end today, once and for all, To the crooked practices of Ōgetsu-hime! Though the heavens shake and the earth trembles, Though mountains spew fire and split asunder, The true Kami shall surely save Your sincere and faithful soul! Repent without a single day's delay! Proclaim the truth without a single day's delay!"

Thus they concluded their song with clear and refreshing resonance. The violent rumbling of the shrine ceased completely with this missionary song. The spirit possessing Uralhime instantly transformed into a demoness, summoning dark clouds, stirring fierce winds, and unleashing torrential rain to plunge the surroundings into pitch-black darkness. Gathering the Yakukosu and Hitsukosu deities, she boarded heavenly rock-boats and bird-boats, fleeing like scattering clouds toward Armenia and the Ural Mountains.

While Matsu, Take, Ume, and the missionaries were offering prayers before the altar to give thanks for the divine favor, five divine beings appeared on the scene: Onitakehiko, Katsuhiko, Akizuki-hime, Miyuki-hime, and Tachibana-hime. All of them were incarnations of white foxes led by Onitakehiko. Even the cunning Golden-Haired Nine-Tailed Fox was no match for the divine might of the white foxes—Onitakehiko, Asahi, Takakura, and Tsukihi—and fled the scene alongside Uralhime.

Thereupon, Ishikoridome-no-Kami, Amenomahitotsu-no-Kami, and Amenokoyane-no-Kami instructed Takakura and the other white foxes to hold in their mouths the ears of rice, barley, beans, millet, and panic grass offered at the Palace of Utsushikuni, and sow them throughout the various regions of the world.

Enshrining the Three Deities—Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto, Kamu-Susanoo-no-Mikoto, and Kinkatsukane—they renamed the Palace of Utsushikuni the Shrine of Ihinari (Inari, "Abundance of Grain"). The reason the palace had rumbled and groaned was that the offerings came from the defiled, material-first Yakukosu deities; the kami, angered, refused to accept them.

The white foxes distributed the ears of the five sacred grains in all four directions, disseminating their seeds throughout the world. Though grains had ripened in various places prior to this, the seeds offered here were of an exceptionally superior kind. Know that even unto the present day, the custom of revering white foxes as the deities of Inari originates from this divine principle.

(March 3, Taisho 11 [1922] • Lunar February 5 • Recorded by Mayu Tanimura)

現代日本語訳

このような騒乱の中へ、神殿を目がけて悠然と姿を現した三五教の宣伝使たち——松代姫、竹野姫、梅ケ香姫をはじめ、石凝姥神、天之目一箇神、淤縢山津見神、時置師神、八彦神、鴨彦神らは声を揃えて歌った。

「神が表に現れて善と悪とを立て分ける。この世を造られた神直日、心の広大な大直日、人の世のすべての事を直日に見直し聞き直せ！　コーカス山に集まったウラル姫をはじめ、百の八王神、ヒツコスやクスの神に至るまで、皇神の御水火（みいき）によって速やかに甦り、醜き身魂を立て替えて、大気津姫の曲業を直日に見直せ、宣り直せ！　神は我らと共にある。邪悪な曲津神が滅びる時、八十の醜女が滅びる時、八岐大蛇や悪鬼、邪悪な狐や千万の曲神の身魂を皇神の御前から追い出し、目を醒ませ、目を開け！　顕国の宮に鎮まり給う三柱の神の怒りは目の当たりに天地に響く唸り声となっているぞ。酔いを醒ませ、目を覚ませ！　胸の帳を押し開けて、空に輝く朝日の如き日の出神の真心に帰れよ、立ち帰れ諸人よ！　ウラル彦よ、ウラル姫よ！　神はそなた達を救おうと千々に心を砕かれて我らを遣わされたのだ。我らは神の御使い、三五教の宣伝使。万歌の言霊によって魂の真柱を立て直し、一時も早く立て替えよ！　身魂の立て替え立て直し、体主霊従の立て直し、大気津姫の行いを今日限りで立て直せ！　天は震い地は揺らぎ、山が火を噴き割れようとも、誠の神は誠ある汝の身魂を必ず救われるであろう。一日も早く改めよ、一日も早く宣り直せ！」

と言葉爽やかに歌い終わった。神殿の激しい鳴動はこの宣伝歌とともにピタリと止んだ。ウラル姫に憑依していた神はたちまち鬼女と変じ、雲を呼び、風を起こし、雨を降らせて辺りを暗闇に包み、八王神やヒツコスを引き連れて天の磐船・鳥船に身を任せ、アーメニヤやウラルの山を指して雲霞のごとく逃げ散っていった。

松代姫、竹野姫、梅ケ香姫をはじめ宣伝使一同が、改めて神殿に祝詞を奏上して神徳に感謝していた折しも、この場に現れた五柱の神があった。見れば鬼武彦、勝彦、秋月姫、深雪姫、橘姫であった。いずれも鬼武彦が率いる白狐の化身である。流石に悪知恵に長けた金毛九尾の悪狐も、白狐の鬼武彦、旭、高倉、月日らの神力には敵わず、ウラル姫と共にこの場を捨てて逃げ去ったのであった。

ここに石凝姥神、天之目一箇神、天之児屋根神は、高倉以下の白狐たちに向かい、顕国の宮に捧げられた稲、麦、豆、黍、粟の穂をくわえさせ、世界各地に播種させた。

国治立命、神素盞嗚命、金勝要の三柱を祭り、顕国の宮を改めて「飯成（いなり）の宮」と称した。宮が鳴動していた理由は、いずれも体主霊従の穢れた八王神の供物であったため、神が怒ってこれをお受けにならなかったからである。

白狐は五穀の穂を四方に配り、世界に五穀の種子を播き広げた。これ以前にも五穀は各地に稔っていたが、今ここに供えられた五穀の種子は極めて優れたものであったからである。現代に至るまで白狐を「稲荷の神」と言うのは、この神理に基づいていると知るべきである。

（大正11年3月3日・旧2月5日　谷村真友録）

と言葉爽やかに歌ひ終つた。神殿の鳴動は此の宣伝歌と共にピタリと止んだ。ウラル姫の神は忽ち鬼女と変じ、雲を呼び、風を起し、雨を降らし四辺を暗に包み、八王神、ヒツコス引連れて、天の磐船、鳥船に其の身を任せ、アーメニヤ、ウラルの山を指して雲を霞と逃げ散りたり。松竹梅を始め宣伝使一同は、改めて神殿に祝詞を奏上し、神徳を感謝する折しも此の場に現はれた五柱の神がある。見れば鬼武彦、勝彦、秋月姫、深雪姫、橘姫であつた。何れも皆鬼武彦が率ゐる白狐の化身である。流石奸智に長けたる金毛九尾の悪狐も、白狐の鬼武彦、旭、高倉、月日の神力には敵はず、ウラル姫と共に此の場を捨てて逃げ去つてしまつたのである。

茲に石凝姥神、天之目一箇神、天之児屋根神は、高倉以下の白狐に向ひ顕国の宮に捧げ奉れる稲、麦、豆、黍、粟の穂を銜へしめ、世界の各地に播種せしめたり。国治立命、神素盞嗚命、金勝要の三柱を祭り、顕国の宮を改めて飯成の宮と称へたり。宮の鳴動したる理由は、何れも体主霊従の穢れたる八王神の供物なれば、神は怒りて之を受けさせ給はざりし為めなり。白狐は五穀の穂を四方に配り、世界に五穀の種子を播布したり。これより以前にも五穀は各地に稔れども、今此処に供へられたる五穀の種子は勝れて良き物なりし故なり。今の世に至るまで白狐を稲荷の神と云ふは此の理に基づくものと知るべし。

（大正一一・三・三　旧二・五　谷村真友録）
