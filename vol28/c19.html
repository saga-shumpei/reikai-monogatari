Chapter 19: High Island 丸 [819]

高島丸〔819〕

§ 1

Wielding as her sole weapon the Kamuzukasa of Ananai-kyō, the lineage of Henjiyōnanshi, and the Ikimiya of the Hinode-no-Kami, Takahime, who maintained her self-will, shedding tears of gratitude upon the impartial and selfless Mikokoro of Kamususanōnōokami, dissolved the Urayai-kyō, and accompanied by Kurohime, Takayamahiko, and Magahiko, whom she relied upon as her arms and legs, returned to the Seichi, surrendered to Ananai-kyō, and was commanded to supervise the Kongaufue no Niyoihotsushiyu and the Murasakiiro Hōgiyoku, whereupon, blinded by the demon of arrogance who looked down upon all people with her nose in the air, she reversed her course once more toward the path of evil,

and reviving her attachments, wishing to search out the whereabouts of the jewels, crossing over the myriad ri of surging waves to the west, east, north, and south, she exhausted her strength in the search for the jewels, forsaking the Seichi; and while she wandered near and far, the Mani-no-Hōju connected with the Amatesumizu-no-Chi of the Ryūgū—namely, Umekohime, the Centipede Princess, Kōryūhime, the Tellus Princess, and Tomohiko—had their divine works occupied by five spirits, wherefore she grew irritated in spirit, returned to the Seichi, vented her anger in various ways, and threw tantrums; and having been commanded with hypocritical grace to supervise the Mani-no-Hōju, she appeared in the Hall of Eight Fathoms, and upon a platform surrounded by the multitude, together with Takayamahiko and Kurohime,

she began the inspection of the jewels; at this time, of the five Mani jewels, excluding the purple jewel, wondrous to say, the remaining four—though thought to be treasures of rare value—were mere dumpling stones of no worth to look upon; wondering when, where, and who had slipped and changed them, Takahime poked out her lips, opened wide her eyes, and while she stood astonished, Kotoyoriwake no Kami no Mikoto, holding the treasure, fled away from the Seichi, and together with Kuniyoriwake, crossed over to Takasagojima; upon hearing this, Takahime grew irritated in spirit, left the Seichi once more, and committing her body to a flat-bottomed boat amidst the waves of the strait where success hung in the balance, together with Tsunehiko and Haruhiko,

she handled the oars and managed the rough waves, crossing and crossing until in the midst of the Great Wa Sea, the Ryū Island floated blue, and she arrived safely at the port of Naha, where, appearing in the hollow of the great zelkova tree which was the dwelling of Old Tsunezuna, she heard that Kotoyoriwake no Kami no Mikoto had obtained the jewel of the Ryūkyū and swiftly crossed over to Takasagojima; upon hearing this, her heart too grew agitated, and committing her body once more to the boat, crossing the mountain-like waves, to cross over to Takasagojima floating in the midst of the Great Wa Sea, and to proceed forward was indeed a daring feat.

Takahime had Tsunehiko and Haruhiko manage the boat, and continuing through the night and day, they finally rowed to a point where the mountains of the land of Tellus faintly entered their eyes like haze. The boat suddenly ran aground on a reef and was splintered to pieces. The sea surface at this time was extremely high in salinity, and even on the open sea when there were no waves, walking upright would submerge one only as far as the calves; the water's resistance was strong, and one could easily cross a ri or two on foot. However, when storms blew and waves rose, they were instantly enveloped by the waves, and there was a danger of losing their lives.

The party of Takahime broke their boat, and having no other choice, tucking up their skirts, they began to wade across the sea aiming for the land of Tellus which had appeared like a haze. Suddenly a wind began to blow. Gradually the waves grew rough. The scorching sun shone down as if to burn everything. Even the bold and fearless Takahime, feeling that she could by no means cross over to Takasagojima in this state, was driven by a sense of anxiety in her heart, and to the limit of her voice she chanted the Amatsunorito and sang the heavenly numbers,

Takahime: 'Kuninotachinotokami-sama, Kamususanōnōokami-sama, Tamateruhiko-sama, Tamateruhime-sama, Kotoyoriwake no Kami-sama, Tokiokishin-sama, Kuniyoriwake-sama, please, save us from this peril! Takahime too, for this very once, humbles her self-will, and will strictly obey and keep your teachings.'

thus praying while even calling upon the names of the officials who had hitherto stood in opposition, her state of mind seemed thoroughly desperate. Tsunehiko and Haruhiko, hearing this prayer of Takahime, suddenly grew faint-hearted, and with crying voices,

'Kamunagara masumitsuki koso,'

chanted faintly like the chirping of mosquitoes.

At that opportune moment, the Takashima Maru, kicking up the waves and advancing, saw the plight of the three drifting upon the waves and in difficulty, and immediately brought the ship close and rescued them. Aboard the Takashima Maru, almost two hundred people were embarked, seeking to cross from the land of Tsukushi and the single island of Ryūgū to the eternal land of Tokoyo. The captain was a giant of a man with prominent bone structure named Tarthur.

Takahime: 'That is why they say humans are troublesome. Must I teach you everything from turnips to rapeseed? The truth of the matter is that Yoriwakemikoto, the religious leader of Ananai-kyō, together with the ruffian Kuniyoriwake, stole a precious jewel and fled to Takashimajima. Therefore, if we leave the supreme treasures of the three thousand worlds in the possession of such a do-hi-kara or ruffian, the world will only fall into disorder, and the age of Miroku will never come forth; hence Takahime, the living shrine of Hinodewake of great mercy and great compassion who saves the Jinmin of the three thousand worlds, employing the arts of divine transformation and mystery, has come all the way from Onokorojima without even riding a boat, leading two retainers, and crossing over the waves—I am the living Kami Takahime. Senchiyō pursed his lips,

'Hey, you called Takahime, treating us like criminals and telling us to reform—is that not terribly rude? The word reform is what a judge ought to say to evil persons and criminals. We are not such defiled tartars that we should be called upon to reform by the likes of you. If you speak with excessive rudeness, I will not put up with it.'

He said this with a slightly angry tone, changed his complexion, and shouted in a loud voice.

Takahime: 'Hey helmsman, do you not like the words of Takahime? Do they make you angry? In the divine admonitions of Kami, there is the saying: do not be angry. To be angry is the heaviest crime when viewed from the Shinkai. You are presently making an angry face, raising a sharp voice, and are a criminal who has violated the crimes of the Shinkai. That is why Takahime told you to reform. Heh... If you can make a reply even to this, try doing it. Even if you threaten me with such a high voice, I am not a living shrine of Hinodewake who will cower, no matter when, no matter when. Heh...'

So saying, she rubbed her nose upward with the back of her hand and sneered.

Senchiyō: 'Hey, hey! You must be a traveling companion of this Takahime, right?'

Both: 'Ha! It is just as you say. No matter what is said, Takahime-sama is distraught, so please do not let it bother you. Even as we two listen from the side, our hearts flutter with anxiety. Nay, we are growing angry. Moreover, we consider your anger to be entirely justified.'

The captain's Tarthīl had listened to the occasional sermons of Kotowake-no-wake no Mikoto and Kuninake-no-wake, had completely become a believer in the Izumo-shinshū faith, had become a senior disciple of Kotowake-no-wake, and had already been granted the title of propagation envoy. 常彦、春彦は驚いて、

両人『モシモシ船長様、此高姫には吾々両人が附添ひ、決して御迷惑になる様な事は致させませぬから、頭を割つて血を出したり、縛り上げる事丈は何卒許して下さいませ』

船長『…………』

高姫パツと怒り、

『盲の垣覗き、猫に小判とはお前のことだ。此生宮は金鉄も同様、指一本触へることは出来ませぬぞ。勿体ない、日の出神様の生宮を、仮令蚤の口程でも傷つけてみよれ。神の御立腹は忽ち、此船は瞬く間に岩に打つかり沈没致し、日の出神に敵対うた者は海の底へ突落され、真心になつて頼んだ人民は、天から抓み上げて、善悪の立別けをハツキリ致して見せるぞや』

船長『ヤア此奴は如何しても駄目だ。……常彦、春彦、お前は今迄先生と仰いで来たのだから、何程船長の命令でも、高姫を縛る訳には行くまい、師弟の情として無理もない。これから此タルチールが直接に荒料理をしてやるから、お前達両人は、下の船室に控へて居れ』

高姫『コレコレ常、春、日の出神の生宮を、チツとの間も、目放し致すことはならぬぞや。此肉体は、尊き神のお役に立てねばならぬ系統の生宮だ。船長に付くか、日の出神に従くか。サア二つに一つの返答を承りませう』

船長は『エー面倒』と強力に任せ、高姫を後手に縛り、両足を括り、太縄を帆柱にかけ、キリキリと絞り出した。高姫は足を空に頭を下にした儘、チクチク帆柱目がけて吊り上げられた。常、春の両人は地団駄ふんで、ワイワイと泣き叫ぶ。

此船に折よくも乗つてゐた言依別、国依別の宣伝使は慌て此場に走り来り、船長に何事か目配せした。船長は驚いた様な顔して、慇懃に腰を屈め、直に高姫を吊りおろした。常彦、春彦の両人は、余りの事に肝を潰し、此場に言依別、国依別の現はれ来りし事に気がつかなかつた。高姫も亦苦しさに両人の現はれて吾れを助けて呉れたる事をチツとも知らなかつた。

船長のタルチールは、言依別命、国依別の時々の説教を聞き、スツカリと三五教の信者となり、言依別の高弟となつて、既に宣伝使の職名を与へられてゐた。それ故タルチールは言依別命を高砂島へ送り届けると共に、自分は宣伝使となつて、高砂島や常世の国を宣伝すべく決心してゐたのである。さうして高姫の事も略、国依別より聞かされてゐた。

言依別、国依別は手早く船長の寝室の間に姿を隠した。船長も亦言依別命に従ひ、おのが寝室に這入つて、三人鼎座し、高姫話に時を遷した。
