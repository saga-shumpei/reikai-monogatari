Chapter 8: 混 Turmoil Battle [808]

混乱戦〔808〕

§ 1

Yale committed acts of violence, was surrounded by a great crowd, found himself in a tight spot, and cleverly ducking between the legs of the people who had risen en masse and were in an uproar, leaving the sacred ground behind, together with two or three acquaintances, crossed Mount Ārisu, concealed himself in the mountains close to Taianjiyō, and watched to see how the situation would unfold.

Meanwhile, in Yahirodono, the leading figures such as Hōrusāsu, Māruēsu, Tērusutan, Hōrensu, and others once again let the flowers of debate bloom. Tērusutan stood up, revealing a look of determination on his face, and spoke to the full assembly.

Tērusutan: 'Gentlemen of the full assembly, shall we not set out for the front even a single moment sooner? If we dawdle, Taianjiyō will be occupied by the faction of Shiyākarutan or Tororensu, and should they rescue King Kārusu by their own hands, all our hopes and pains up to now will completely end in foam. Rather than leaving the regret of untimely chrysanthemums and irises for a later day, it is better to lead the masses at the earliest possible moment, rush upon Taianjiyō in a bamboo-cutting onslaught with flying banners and beating drums, repel the faction of Princess Sērusu, rescue King Kārusu, get ahead of the faction of Shiyākarutan and Tororensu, and seize control of the governance of the main island—this cannot be done at any other time than now. This chance of a thousand years should not be met with hesitation and irresolution, leaving the regret of being swallowed up at a later day. Even though Sandōhiko no Mikoto preaches a doctrine of superficial non-resistance, surely that cannot be his true intention. They say that he who strikes first controls others; if we take the lead in guiding the masses and head toward Taianjiyō, Sandōhiko no Mikoto and Princess Yāchin will certainly give their consent. It is only natural that as the leader of the Misogikyō, he should not openly utter that he is leading a divine army and seizing sovereignty over Taianjiyō. We, being in the position of leaders, must possess the knowledge to hear one and understand ten. How could the leader, whose learning surpasses past and present and whose understanding penetrates East and West, have any reason not to open his arms and accept this heavenly fortune falling before his very eyes? Unworthy though I am, Tērusutan's observation will not be mistaken. Everyone, please grant me your approval.'

The gathering felt a sense of heartening courage, raised cheers, and expressed their agreement.

Here, they looked up to Hōrusāsu as the general, with Māruēsu and Tērusutan as vice-generals, and adding the believers of Lake Candidius, numbering almost over thirty thousand, carrying bamboo spears, they decided without a moment's delay to head toward Taianjiyō.

Sandōhiko no Mikoto was greatly astonished, and used every word to explain and expound to the assembled that this was not his intention, but the multitude, thoroughly impatient, took Sandōhiko's words perversely, and easily not a single person obeyed his command. Sandōhiko had no choice, and being pushed forward by the leaders and others, it was decided that he would go forth to battle. Leaving the brothers Hinodate and Tsukihoko in the two places of Amamine and Tamine, and making Princess Yāchin and Princess Mariyasu guard the sacred ground of Mount Tamamo, he led the masses with a wealth of horses and headed toward Taianjiyō against his will. Ah, what will become of the fate of Sandōhiko no Mikoto?

Yale, who had fled earlier, together with a faction consisting of Maruchiru, Urāru, Kingusu, Tōmasu, Māshiyaru, and others who had slipped into Mount Tamamo as spies for Princess Sērusu, gathered dozens of subordinates and was lying in wait at the northern foot of Mount Ārisu for the arrival of the divine army of Sandōhiko. Tērusutan, standing at the head, saw this and cried out in a loud voice:

Tērusutan: 'Hey, Yale, do you intend to escape from the sacred ground, lead a wretched band of a minority, and ambush our great army? To attempt to resist a great army like clouds and mist with a feeble faction is truly terribly reckless. Rather than that, repent, join our army once more, and let us together achieve outstanding fame and merit.'

He spoke as if giving instruction. Yale greeted this with a smile, stepped briskly up right in front of Tērusutan, firmly grasped his hand, and while letting hot tears fall from both eyes,

エールは乱暴を働き、大勢に取巻かれ、進退谷まつて、捷しこくも満座総立となつて立騒いで居る人々の股をくぐり、聖地を後に、二三の知己と共に、アーリス山を越え、泰安城の間近の山に身を忍ばせ、形勢如何にと窺ひつつあつた。

一同は何となく心勇み、歓声をあげ、賛意を表しける。

茲にホールサースを大将と仰ぎ、マールエース、テールスタンを副将とし、日月潭の信徒を加へて、殆ど三万有余人、竹槍を携へ、愈時を移さず、泰安城に立向ふ事となつた。

真道彦命は大に驚き、一同に向つて吾意にあらざる事を言葉を尽して、説き明せ共、逸り切つたる数多の人々、真道彦の言葉を却つて逆に取り、容易に一人として其命に従ふ者はなかつた。真道彦は止むを得ず、幹部其他に推されて出陣する事となつた。日楯、月鉾の兄弟を天嶺、泰嶺の両所に残し、玉藻山の聖地にはヤーチン姫、マリヤス姫をしてこれを守らしめ、馬上豊に衆を率ゐて、心ならずも泰安城に向ふ事となつた。あゝ真道彦命の運命は如何になるらむか。

先に逃走したるエールは、セールス姫の間者として玉藻山に忍び入り込みたるマルチル、ウラール、キングス、トーマス、マーシヤル等の一味と共に、数十人の部下を集め、アーリス山の北麓に、真道彦の神軍の到るを待伏せて居た。先頭に立つたるテールスタンはこれを見て大音声、

『ヤア、エール、汝は聖地を脱出し、少数の一味の奴原を引率し、吾等が大軍を待討たむとするか。弱小の味方を以て、雲霞の如き大軍に抵抗せむとするは、実に険呑千万であらうぞ。それよりも悔い改めて、再び吾軍に加はり、共に共に抜群の功名を致さうではないか』

と教ゆる様に言つた。エールは笑顔を以てこれを迎へ、つかつかとテールスタンの前に近づき、堅く手を握り、両眼より熱涙を落し乍ら、

Ael: 'Ah, Teresutan, well said indeed. Escaping the sacred grounds from a moment's anger and returning near the capital of Taian, I see that the forces of Shakartan and Torensuto are extremely rampant and not to be despised. If we face them with our small band of allies, not only will it be without any effect, but it will be as if we were leaping into the abyss of destruction of our own accord. Regarding this, too, as the faction of Sārubōsu surrounds the person of King Kārusu and the mansion of Tankei tenfold and twentyfold, standing in strict guard against the attacking forces of Shakartan so that the King shall not be snatched away, the life of the King is fast approaching morning and evening. I imagine that the faction of Princess Sērusu, if King Kārusu is snatched away by Shakartan and others and they enthrone him to promulgate a new administration in Taian Castle, recognizing it as a grave matter for our faction, are guarding with all their might so as not to hand the King over to the attackers. Nevertheless, knowing that the attacking forces are increasingly fierce and can by no means be defended against, the faction of Sārubōsu, fearing future disaster, may well assassinate the King and flee; considering this, not a single moment can be delayed. If you will grant a part of the divine army of Agama to me by any means, I shall be able to safely protect the life of the King. Please forgive this matter by all means.'

Thus exhausting words, he begged earnestly. Teresutan immediately set forth with the divine army under his own command, as Ael requested, to go to the rescue at the King's mansion in Tankei.

At the mansion of Tankei, the subordinates of Sārubōsu surrounded it tenfold and twentyfold and were undertaking the guard of the King. The troop of Torensuto made an aggressive charge upon the mansion of Tankei, attempting to snatch the King away, and clashed with the subordinates of Sārubōsu, fighting with all their might with one victory and one defeat each; but the forces of the attackers increased hourly, and Sārubōsu arrived at a point where there was no longer any escaping with his own body. Deeming it unpropitious for future days if King Kārusu were handed over to the enemy's hands, now hardening his resolve, he pressed the King toward suicide, and at the very moment when, should the King unhappily not consent, Sārubōsu himself would lay hands and assassinate the King, from whence it was neither known, a mass of great firelight came flying, exploded in front of Sārubōsu, and raised a great sound. Sārubōsu suddenly lost consciousness and fell on that spot.

The attackers of Torensuto, taking advantage of their victory, attempted to riotously break into the interior of the mansion already. Just at that moment, the bamboo-spear troop led by Teresutan and Ael appeared at this place raising a war cry like clouds and mist, and suddenly charging recklessly toward the attackers, as a result of panic throughout the whole army starting with Torensuto, they fled in defeat pointing toward Taian Castle.

Sārubōsu finally regained consciousness, and when he looked around, the attackers of Torensuto were without a shadow, and in their stead, shattered in spirit once again by the coming in attack of the Agama army of Teresutan and Ael, he was able to escape this place barely with his life. Teresutan and Ael came out before King Kārusu, reverently applied their hands, knew of the critical danger of the King, and reported that they had come all the way for rescue. The King stood, firmly grasped the hands of the two, and bestowed words of gratitude along with tears.

From this time the two escorted the King, ascended Tankei, made the rock cave of Niitakayama a provisional fortress, Teresutan divided half of one army and headed for the attack on Taian Castle, while the remaining divine army was led by Ael, tightly guarding the personal safety of the King while watching for the opportunity. From this time the two of Teresutan and Ael received the special favor of King Kārusu and came to be visited by an era of triumph.

Meanwhile in Taian Castle, the generals Princess Sērusu, Seurusuchin, and Hōrokēsu commanded the soldiers within the castle, worked brilliantly, and the castle did not fall easily. The attacking force of Shakartan was also naturally a crowd of disorderly people, so they slightly generated disfavor, stirred up internal discord, and it was a critical occasion where they were about to collapse from within.

と言葉を尽して頼み入る。テールスタンは直ちに自己の率ゆる神軍を以てエールの請ふが儘に、淡渓の王が館に応援の為、出で向ふ事となつた。

トロレンスの寄手は勝に乗じて早くも館内に乱入せむとする。時しもあれ、テールスタン、エールの率ゆる竹槍隊は雲霞の如く鬨を作つて此場に現はれ来り、忽ち寄手に向つて遮二無二突込めば、トロレンスを始め全軍狼狽の結果、泰安城を指して敗走して了つた。

サアルボースは漸くにして正気づき、あたりを見れば、トロレンスの寄手は影もなく、それに代つてテールスタン、エールの三五軍の襲来せるに再び肝を潰し、僅に身を以て此場を逃るる事を得た。テールスタン、エールはカールス王の前に出で、恭しく手を仕へて王の危急を知り、遥々救援に向ひし事を奏上した。王は立つて両人が手を固く握り、涙と共に感謝の辞を与へた。

これより二人は王を奏じ、淡渓を溯り新高山の岩窟を仮りの城塞となし、テールスタンは一軍の半を割いて泰安城の攻撃に向ひ、残りの神軍はエールこれを統率し、王の身辺を堅く守り、時機を窺ひつつあつた。これよりテールスタン、エールの二人はカールス王の殊寵を蒙り、得意の時代に見舞はるる事となりぬ。

一方泰安城にてはセールス姫、セウルスチン、ホーロケースの大将株、城内の兵を指揮し、華々しく立働き、容易に落城せず。寄せ手のシヤーカルタンの攻軍も素より烏合の衆なれば、稍厭気を生じ、内訌を起し、内部より瓦解せむとする危急の場合であつた。

§ 3

At this time, Sārubōsu, having fled from Tankei, gathered the scattered allies and arrived at this place to provide reinforcements. Just as the forces of the people's army had slightly recovered and attempted to scale the fortress and make a chaotic intrusion without missing this opportunity, a unit led by Tērusutan rushed forward from the rear, raising a battle cry. The people's army was attacked by the enemy from both inside and out, falling once again into a predicament, and reached a position where retreat was no longer avoidable. At this time, the large army led by Mamichihikonomikoto from the rear, together with Hōrusāsu and Māruēsu, divided their forces into three units and advanced to attack Taianjiyō from three directions. Daunted by this momentum, Sērusu Hime, Sērusutin, Hōrokēsu, Sārubōsu, as well as the attacking people's army, fled like an avalanche without distinguishing between friend and foe.

Here, the Ananahigun led by Tērusutan and the Ananahigun led by Mamichihikonomikoto unexpectedly came into a great collision. As soon as Tērusutan saw that the situation was unfavorable, he retreated with his subordinates to King Kārusu's temporary fortress, and reported the outrageous behavior of Mamichihikonomikoto to him, mixing falsehood with truth. King Kārusu reached the height of anger, crossed his arms and clenched his teeth, determined by any means to destroy Mamichihikonomikoto's faction, while tears of anger streamed down, enduring his deep mortification.

Meanwhile, the divine army of Mamichihikonomikoto leisurely advanced into Taianjiyō. Starting with Mamichihikonomikoto, alongside vice-commanders Hōrusāsu and Māruēsu, and brave generals such as Hōrensu, Yūtopiyāru, Tsūrensu, Shīrinsu, Hāreyāru, Ōitsuku, Hyūzu, Andēya, Nyujieru, and others, they cast off their chiyūrikku, held a banquet of victory, and rewarded the numerous soldiers. In the inner shrine of the castle, they enshrined the Great Deity of the Ananakyō, played music at each shrine as an offering of gratitude for victory, performed dance music, and consoled the divine will, transforming the interior of the castle into something resembling a heavenly paradise.

Mamichihikonomikoto commanded Hōrusāsu to take a letter and be dispatched hurriedly to the sacred place of Tamamoyama to reverently welcome Yāchin Hime and Mariyasu Hime back to Taianjiyō. Also, having managed to locate the whereabouts of King Kārusu, he hurriedly dispatched Māruēsu with a small number of retainers to reverently welcome the king to Taianjiyō.

Although Hōrusāsu successfully welcomed and brought back Yāchin Hime and Mariyasu Hime, for some reason Māruēsu did not return even after ten days had passed, and holding suspicions over the complete absence of news, this time he sent Hōrensu once again to the king's camp to welcome the king. When this too yielded no news after several days, he sent Yūtopiyāru, Tsūrensu, Shīrinsu, and Hāreyāru to welcome the king, but this also resulted in no news whatsoever.

Here, Mamichihikonomikoto, driven by a slight sense of anxiety, this time urged Yāchin Hime and personally went to the king's hideout to reverently welcome him to Taianjiyō, leaving Mariyasu Hime and others to guard the castle, and personally set out to the king's camp situated in the upper reaches of Tankei.
