Chapter 21: 喰へぬ女 [821]

喰へぬ女〔821〕

§ 1

In the captain's cabin of the Tarchīru, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto and Kuniyoriwake, three persons in all, sat in a tripartite arrangement, earnestly debating opinions concerning the divine administration of Shinkai.

Senchiyō: 'Just now, a person calling herself Takahime, a sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, was on thekanbanjiyō speaking of disjointed and rambling matters, but truly, just as the okotoba of the kyōshiyusama say, she is a narrow-minded person of deep attachment. Is there no way we might somehow save her? She bitterly resents and suspects you in every possible way, and she is absolutely certain that both of you have the Mani jewel hidden in your futokoro and have fled to Takasagojima, so she was in such a furious rage, insisting that she must chase after you to the ends of the earth and take it back; it was truly a dreadful fit of excitement. How would it be if you too made a fair confession and set that Takahime at ease?'

Kotoyoriwake: 'The substitution incident of the Mani hotsushiyu is something that emerged entirely from the divine administration of the Ōkami, and as for us, it has been decided that we cannot clearly indicate all of it to Takahime. Moreover, Takahime is one who will never believe what we say. No matter how much of the truth we explain and make her hear, she is thoroughly warped from the bottom of her heart, so she will never truly accept it. She is truly a troublesome person.'

Senchiyō: 'If it will not do with you, how would it be if you made it known through Kuniyoriwake-sama?'

Kotoyoriwake: 'It will utterly come to nothing. Kuniyoriwake has teased Takahime quite a lot, countless times, and moreover has caused failures by letting her know the whereabouts of the jewel, so there is all the more reason she will not listen.'

Senchiyō: 'What on earth has become of that jewel?'

Kotoyori: 'Although I cannot speak of it to anyone, I shall tell you on the condition that you alone keep it a secret from others. The precious jewels of fire, water, and earth of the Nyoi hotsushiyu are carefully concealed in the central region of Jitentōjima, namely Kamurijima and Kutsujima. Thinking that we made off with them, Takahime must have pursued my footsteps and come all the way hither. As you know well, neither of us two possesses even a single jewel, do we?'

Senchiyō: 'As you say, you possess nothing whatsoever. How would it be if, as a further step, you met Takahime directly and showed her that we do not possess any jewel like that, thus and thus?'

Kuniyoriwake: 'That will not do. Even if we do not have it here, she will suspect that we hid it somewhere, and she will pursue her suspicions anywhere and everywhere while threatening us all the more harshly, so I think it is more advantageous to leave her alone until she naturally comes to her senses.'

Kotoyori: 'No matter how much truth we two speak, Takahime alone will not trust us, so I am truly sorry to trouble you, but if you would quietly invite Takahime somewhere and tell her that no such jewel is hidden in Takasagojima, and that she should search Jitentōjima... she might perhaps trust it rather more. Since it is pitiful beyond endurance to have her waste her efforts on a trivial matter... the truth of the matter is that, in accordance with the divine intent of the God Susanoo no Ōkami—who desires to have Takahime search for that jewel, recover from her failures hitherto, and make her one who need not be ashamed as a glorious priestess of the sacred ground—Kotoyoriwake made off with it as if in flight, and I shall become a sacrifice and leave the sacred ground, from here propagating the faith in Takasagojima and Tokoyonokuni, and finally arriving at the sanctuary of the Ubusuna mountain range in the land of Fusa, with the plan to reach the Caucasus mountains. Will you not kindly be so good as to earnestly admonish Takahime through your own words so that she does not waste her efforts?'

Senchiyō: 'Yes, since I have been entrusted by you with the office of sendenshi, I shall try the opening battle of propagation before Takahime-sama. If it should end in failure, must I resign my post as sendenshi?'

Kotoyori: 'Such worry is needless. Since whether it succeeds or fails is according to Kamunagara, please leave success or failure out of account and try negotiating once.'

Senchiyō: 'Very well, then, let us try an uhijin.'

Hereupon the captain invited Takahime into his private room, and decided to give her a caution secretly directed toward Takahime.

The captain set aside his busy jimu, softened his facial expression from the bottom of his heart, and spoke to Takahime with a gentle voice.

Senchiyō: 'Takahime-sama, a little while ago, truly not knowing that you were of such a noble onmi, I committed a rudeness. Once again I offer my owabi.'

Takahime: 'You are the captain of the Takashimamaru, so you ought to have realized at least that much. I actually found it endlessly mysterious why until now you did not understand that this is the living shrine of the Hinodewake. However, clever you, there is no way you should not have understood rarely; in short, an evil god of Brahmanism possessed you and made you say such worthless things. If the Hinode no Kami glares at you with a single glance, you will understand very well. Even the Magatukami, fearing the majesty of the Hinode no Kami, crossed the waves and ended up running away. Your face right now and your face just before are as different as Emma and Jizō. If you from now on listen to the teachings of this Takahime and become a believer of the Anago no Oshie, divine virtues will manifest more and more, you will become a splendid person of character, and from here on, it is not impossible that you might even become the king of Takashimagoto. If one is to live out the same lifetime, rather than becoming a senchō and ending up as a hikakagemono, even though there is a little mental toil, by becoming the king of that vast Takashimagoto and making your name resound through ten thousand generations, how wonderful it would be—you probably do not understand.'

Senchiyō: 'Yes, thank you, as you surmise, I am a believer in Brahmanism.'

Feigning ignorance on purpose, he absolutely concealed the fact that he had been given the official title of propagation envoy by Kotoyoriwake no Mikoto.

Takahime: 'Brahmanism is no good. What is the use of sticking your head into such a heretical teaching? Though you were born as a splendid man surpassing the common run, to enter such a teaching is somewhat out of balance. Quickly enter the Anago no Oshie. You will surely be able to achieve success in the world.'

Senchiyō: 'I do not even dream of becoming a king or the like. If a captain exerts his utmost efforts as a captain and completely fulfills his own mission, there is no joy surpassing this; moreover, if one remains trapped in pretentious names like the Anago no Oshie or Brahmanism, true truth cannot be understood. Though separated into rain, hail, snow, and ice, when they fall they are the same valley water... or as they say, the great ocean does not reject slender streams; the radiance of truth shines brilliantly regardless of such distinctions or pretentious names. While you are fiercely displaying sectarian spirit, trapped in good, evil, Anago, or Brahman, you are rather narrowing that teaching, hiding its light, and making yourself an insect within the lion's body. I had heard that the Anago no Oshie is the great bright light of the grand unification of all teachings. Nevertheless, though you are among the propagation envoys of the Anago no Oshie who shine across the world, even being a soul from the single-grain lineage and the living shrine of the Hinode no Kami, to be driven by a narrow sectarian spirit without even studying other teachings and to try to reject them in a single word—is that not somewhat reckless? Just as the proverbs say, the hunter chasing a wild boar does not see the mountain... the frog in the well knows not the great ocean... coming to Fuji, searching for Fuji, making a pilgrimage to Fuji... if you become too obsessed with a single differentiated thing, you will not be able to grasp the true essence. What do you think of this? However, I do not yet say that I have thoroughly investigated all the teachings of Brahmanism. As a semi-finished believer, to say such things to you, a distinguished propagation envoy, may perhaps be a contradiction, like preaching the sutras to Shakyamuni, or a bald-headed urchin attending kindergarten acting as a teacher toward the vice-principal of a university. Please kindly instruct me on any inappropriate points and correct them, I beg of you.'

Speaking with utmost fluency, he wielded eloquence coached by Kotoyoriwake, stepping low from beneath, striving to reform Takahime's heart.

Takahime: 'My, what a remarkably fluent speaker you are! Exactly like Ananai-kyō, there are ultra-modern folks who say things just like you. Since such ultra-modern folks have even become leaders, they try to fathom the profound and subtle divine arrangement of Shinkai using intellect, learning, and sophistry; and because of that, like a fine-cord loincloth, they slip this way and slip that way, never achieving a single thing—just as it appears in the divine revelation of Henjiyōnanshi, you are bound to end up full of failures, aren't you? If you too stop talking such petty sophistry, then truly you are a genuine believer. With a proud and arrogant spirit that chatters criticisms of good and evil or offers opinions to the living shrine of Hinodewake, true authenticity cannot be understood, you know. Just as the mitama of a world hardened by intellect, learning, sophistry, and lies are reflected in the Kotoyoriwake of Henjiyōniyoshi, you too, as a human being, are indeed a splendid person, but viewed from the side of the gods, you speak of matters quite like a babe. How could the true state of Shinkai be understood by human comprehension? Unless you become like a newborn infant with an innocent heart, just as the gods command, the one-rin divine arrangement of Ananai-kyō can never be understood at all, you know.'

Captain: 'Indeed, to Kotoyoriwake-sama... um... oops, whoops, is a person called Kotoyoriwake-sama someone who speaks sophistry like me? You must be quite troubled for the sake of the Way, aren't you?'

Takahime: 'That is so indeed; Kotoyoriwake is famously fond of new things, ultra-modern, and in the very end went so far as to pilfer even the audacious Mani Jewel, and by now without a doubt is plotting some rebellion or other on Takasago Island. Therefore, if Kotoyoriwake's selfish desires were to be established even a tiny bit, the world would turn into dark clouds and once more the Heavenly Rock Door would have to be closed; hence, Hinodewake must act and meddle in everything Kotoyoriwake does, from one to ten, from a hundred to a thousand, to obstruct him, or else the people of the world will not be saved. Ah truly, truly, there is nothing so anxiety-inducing as divine service in Shinkai!'

Captain: 'You said you are the living shrine of Hinodewake, so sitting right where you are, like the God of Sofuto, you must know everything happening beneath the heavens, mustn't you?'

Takahime: 'If it concerns the three thousand worlds, just ask me anything at all. From the fundamental cause and destiny of the beginning of ancient worlds, to matters of the world yet beyond the future, I shall let you know as clearly as if reflected in a mirror.'

Captain: 'That being the case, with your clairvoyant power, you surely must know where Kotoyoriwake no Mikoto and Kuniyoriwake are right now, don't you?'

Takahime: 'Hmph, how foolish of you to speak thus. Ask something more plausible. Kotoyoriwake is right now in the capital of Teru, together with Kuniyoriwake, plotting some audacious rebellion, decorating four jewels, and starting a mountain child venture.'

Captain: 'Ah, is that so indeed? Truly, the one called Lord Hinodewake must be a magnificent person, isn't he? If so, please take me along to the capital of Teru from here on. Then, let me meet Kotoyoriwake no Mikoto and offer my opinions using your teachings. There is no mistake that they are in the capital of Teru, right?'

Takahime: 'There are no two words in a god's speech. Just for today and right now, they are in the capital of Teru, but by the time this ship arrives yonder, they will walk away yonder too, so they won't be in the capital of Teru. Since we walk as much as they walk, no matter where you say they are right now, you cannot meet them.'

Captain: 'If so, by the divine power of the gods, wouldn't it be possible to protect Kotoyoriwake-sama so that he does not leave the capital of Teru?'

Takahime: 'Such a thing is like a fart's ochiya to me, but since Kotoyoriwake is a high-collar fellow whom I detest, my spirit somehow finds it hard to feel him and I feel terribly sick; so please think of Kotoyoriwake and Kuniyoriwake as an exception. Ah, please don't talk about this anymore, my stomach is starting to feel sick. Otsuho hon hon hon'

Senchiyō: 'Ah, I understand now. You mean that when it comes to matters concerning the kyōshiyu of Kotoyoriwake, your mitama becomes clouded because you feel sick, and you find everything difficult to owaka. The truth is that just a little while ago, Kotoyoriwake-sama and Kuniyoriwake-sama unexpectedly boarded my ship, and as they otsushiya, actually they had taken four ritsupa jewels of mani out of the seichi like this, but because that fellow called Takahime was persistently chasing after them, even if they went to Takasagojima she would probably chase them again, so they hurriedly transferred from my ship to another boat and hikikahe back to Onokorojima to hide them in Oshima and Meshima. They are certainly hidden soko without a doubt. If you too intend to search for those jewels, it is dame even if you go to Takasagojima. They were truly beautiful, magnificent hōgiyoku of blue, red, white, and yellow that made one's throat thirsty.'

Takahime: 'Eee, what are you otsushiya? Did you meet Kotoyoriwake? And did he really have the jewels?'

Senchiyō: 'They were truly ritsupa things. Since they were actually riding on this ship, by imagoro they are probably far away navigating near Taiwan Island.'

Takahime tilted her head for a while, lost in thought, but suddenly shook her body violently,

Takahime: 'Senchiyō-san, how much did you get from Kotoyoriwake? Is it this much?'

and showed five fingers.

Senchiyō: 'There is no need to receive hush money separately from Kotoyoriwake-sama; I merely informed you out of kindness about what I actually witnessed. If I were to receive anything, it should be from Takahime-sama.'

Takahime gazed intently at Senchiyō's face, floating a nasty smile,

Takahime: 'My, how thoroughly the wicked god's schemes are carried out from beginning to end! Making it look as though Kotoyoriwake returned to Onokorojima, he must have changed to another boat and crossed over to Takasagojima; somehow it is vividly visible to Takahime's clairvoyance. ... Hey, senchiyō-san, stop teasing me halfway. If it were someone else that might be one thing, but can the living shrine of the Hinodewake be so easily fooled? If you otsushiya such clever things, people will make a fool of you, ho ho ho ho ho'

and burying her head between her shoulders, jerking her chin, lowering both hands vertically, spreading out all ten fingers with a snap, and shaking her hips back and forth, she laughed.

Senchiyō: 'Takahime-sama, please take your time returning to your seat and rest. Later on again, we shall leisurely listen to your talk.'

Takahime stuck out her tongue, rolled her eyes wide,

Takahime: 'Hāi'

and with that, she trotted in a pitter-patter run out of the captain's cabin.

高姫『その位なことは、屁の御茶でもありませぬが、言依別は妾の嫌ひなドハイカラで厶いますから、どうも妾の霊が感じにくいので気分が悪うてなりませぬから、言依別や国依別に対しては例外と思うて下さいませ。あゝモウ此事は言つて下さいますな、胸が悪うなつて来ました。オツホヽヽヽ』

船長『あゝそれで分りました。言依別の教主に関する事は御気分が悪くなつて、身魂がお曇り遊ばし、何事も御分り憎いと仰有るのでせう。実の所は此少し前、私の船に図らずも、言依別さま、国依別さまが乗つて下さいまして、仰有るのには、実は此通り立派な麻邇の玉を四つ迄聖地から持出して来たが、どうも高姫と云ふ奴、執念深く附け狙ふので、高砂島へ往つても又追かけて来るだらうから、ヤツパリ自転倒島の冠島沓島へ隠しておかうと、慌だしく私の船から他の船へ乗替へ自転倒島へ引返されましたよ。キツと其処に隠してあるに違ありませぬぜ。お前さまも其玉を探す積りならば、高砂島へ御出でになつても駄目ですよ。それはそれは美しい、青赤白黄の四つの立派な、喉のかわく様な宝玉でした』

高姫『エヽ何と仰有る。言依別にお会ひになりましたか。そして本当に玉を持つてゐましたか』

船長『それはそれは立派な物でしたよ。現に此船に乗つてゐられたのですもの、モウ今頃は余程遠く台湾島附近を航海して居られるでせう』

高姫暫く首をかたげ、思案にくれてゐたが、俄に体をビリビリと振はし、

高姫『船長さま、あなた言依別に幾ら貰ひましたか。コン丈ですか』

と五本の指を出して見せる。

船長『言依別さまに別に口止め料を貰ふ必要もなし、只実地目撃した丈の事を、お前さまに親切上御知らせした迄の事だ。貰うのなら高姫さまから貰ふべきものだよ』

高姫は船長の顔を穴のあく程眺め、いやらしき笑を浮かべ乍ら、

『何とマア悪神の仕組は、どこから何処まで、能う行届いたものだなア。言依別が自転倒島へ帰つたと見せかけ、外の船に乗替へ、キツと高砂島に渡つたに相違ない、どうも高姫の天眼通には彷彿として見えてゐる。……コレ船頭さま、イヽ加減になぶつておきなさい。外の者ならいざ知らず、日の出神の生宮がさう易々とチヨロまかされるものですかいな。そんなアザとい事を仰有ると、人が馬鹿に致しますで、ホヽヽヽヽ』

と首を肩の中に埋めて、頤をしやくり乍ら、両手を垂直に下げ、十本の指をパツと開いて腰を前後に揺り乍ら笑うて見せた。

船長『高姫さま、マアゆるりと貴女の御席へ帰つて休息して下さい。又後程ゆるゆると御話を承りませう』

高姫は舌を巻出し、目をキヨロツと剥いて、

『ハーイ』

と云つた限り、チヨコチヨコ走りに船長室を出でて行く。
