Chapter 15: 願望成就 [815]

願望成就〔815〕

§ 1

Hitate, wishing to express congratulation, followed the example of Princess Mariyasu and began to sing and dance of himself.

From the remote ages of the divine age, Chosen by the august Kami, In the central ground of Takasagojima, Soaring into the clouds of Nihitaka, In the sacred ground to the southeast of the mountain, Before the lake of Jitsugetsutan, Stands the pure Tamamoyama. Here, Mamichihikonomikoto, the distant divine ancestor, Resides through every generation as determined by the Kami, And while teaching and guiding without exception All the common people of the four quarters in the august teachings of the precious Kami, The era of the father's rule arrived. The ugly teachings of Brahmanism Crossed over to this divine island, Imbuing the minds of the people With the behavior of body-dominant and spirit-subordinate, Clouding human hearts day and night, Causing mountains and rivers to echo and grasses and trees to wither.

People in the four quarters wept and cried out, With not a moment's cessation of strife, Until it ended as an alley of Asura. The father's rule, unable to bear the sight Of the state of the present world, With all his heart and all his body, To the people of the world the path of truth, Morning and evening, minutely Did he admonish, yet what could he do? The power of time without mercy Caused wicked men to prosper and spread, While goodness fell into ever-greater decay, And the majestic virtue of the Kami was not fully manifested. Now early on, the sincerity he exerted for the august nation Turned into a grudge, Doubted by King Karl, Moaning in a dark dungeon above a cave Where coming and going had ceased, Lamenting the fleeting floating world.

In the sadness of living thus, We two brothers, Like the rising of the rich prosperity of the morning sun Upon a world dark as a nubatama duck's feather, Illuminating and purifying it to rescue the populace From the suffering of pitch and charcoal, To rinse away the false charges against our father And exhaust our filial piety, Embracing the divine decree of the land-spirit Kami, Princess Ryūsei, Very solemnly received, Crossed over Mount Aris, Following the ridges of Mount Suan, A long journey whose treading we were not accustomed to, Riding across the waves of a thousand ri, Tsukihoko my younger brother and Princess Yuriko, These three safely arrived In the southern islands of Ryukyu, Advanced into the capital of Sawara, And met with Teruhikoo-ō, who combines the roles of supreme priest of Izumo-shinshu and king, And Princess Teruko, Instructed very minutely in the divine strategy for the fulfillment of divine government, Crossed over the Otani River, Advancing and advancing into the cave of Kōyōsan, Where the hermit priest of the eternal kusunoki Bestowed upon them the jewel and the mirror, Their five-fold spirits of two men and three women, With courageous hearts and light bodies, Once again handling their ship, Reached this divine island, And gradually returned here at last. Ah, wondrous Kami-nagara, Blessed by the favor of the august spirits, Taking us brothers as the beginning, Princess Yuriko, Princess Yachiyo, And Princess Terushiro who illuminates this clouding world, Manifesting miraculous achievements,

The joy of returning To the sacred site where the father's august spirit had peacefully resided for eternity, To what could it be compared? There is nothing. Ah, how precious is the grace of the Kami! How awe-inspiring is the virtue of the Kami! More and more, taking the great august protection of the Ōkami as our strength, Facing towards Taian Castle, Holding the jewel and the mirror in our hands, Throwing open the battle of Kototama, Directing our speech to make all evil Kami submit, Starting with the precious life of King Karl and Princess Yachin, And saving the life of our beloved father—the time has come! When I think of it, joyful is the grace of the Kami; When I look up to it, high is the virtue of the Kami. Ah, wondrous Kami-nagara, Let the blessing of the august spirits be with us!

So singing to the end, he took his seat. Tsukihoko again stood up and of himself began to sing and dance.

Although they call it the fortunes of the times, Our father was captured and imprisoned, And heading towards Tamamoyama, the sacred site of Izumo-shinshu, Numerous intermediaries of the Kami Who had been designated as our dwellings for a thousand generations, Exposing the raw fabric of their hearts upon the father's disaster, Rebelled against the Kami and fled back, Exhausting every limit of flattery and sycophancy, And returned as evil Kami scheming for their own profit and glory. Though the moon waxes or wanes, Even though the Great Earth should sink, This single body of ours whose life is singular, We shall dedicate it for the sake of the Kami, Thus with mere words brilliantly Had the hundred birds been chirping, Only to drop their dung from behind,

In wicked deeds with innocent faces, irrepressible tears of bitter regret, Morning and evening, prostrating themselves Before the precious august presence of the land-spirit Kami, Just as we were praying, Princess Ryūsei Very solemnly appeared, Turning towards the two of us, Proclaiming: 'From now on, you shall cross over To the southern islands of Ryukyu, Meet with King Teruhiko in the capital of Sawara As quickly as even a single day, Receive the divine strategy of supreme rule and great peace, And return with it in detail,' In the joy of her august decree, Our elder brother Hitate and Princess Yuriko, Lost in the darkness, secretly departed from the sacred site, With the light disguise of sedge hats and raincoats, ploddingly, following the mountain path, reaching the pass Of Aris, at the roadside

Sitting down on a rock, resting my breath at that moment, unexpectedly Tērin-hime, tracking me down with the persistent fixation of a snake targeting a frog, made a solitary journey of extreme boldness. Grumbling and complaining, lining up a million grievances, just as I was at my wit's end, the precious command and device of Mariyas-hime, who appeared of a sudden, made me swing my Kongauzue and beat my own Shintai mercilessly. Even Tsukihoko, which is not made of wood or stone, grew violently agitated in his belly, and though tears of regret fell like rain, what could be done? Since this body of mine currently embraces a grave matter, there is no path other than to endure it—just as I had thus resigned myself,

Tērin-hime suddenly bit down with all her might on the arm of Mariyas-hime, and watching for the opening in the struggle, we three quickly hid ourselves, slipping away into the shadows. Crossing mountain ridges and traversing valleys, tasting various mixed bitters, we finally reached Kiru Port. Seeking a ship upon the waves, we arrived safely in Riukiujima, and as commanded by the Hime of Tatsuyo, we met with the revered deities Teruhikofūfu and Tsunekusu. Receiving the five treasures, at last we have returned here. Ah, Kanagara-kamigami, with the spirits bestowing their blessings, the joy of today's living soul is like the blooming and fragrant state of parched beans yielding fresh flowers. The grace of Tianjin Ōkami, Kunitsu Ōkami, and Kunitama no Kami—the happiness that they still do not abandon us brothers! Then, from here on, uniting the five-fold Mitsutama, water and fire, and advancing to the capital of Tai'an, while releasing the Kototama arrows of Ibuki, turning and directing the swaging demonic gods, constructing the age of Isuzu of the five, six, and seven, which Kuritaritsu Ōkami has caused to be arranged, as a steadfast and unchanging age of pine trees for thousands and myriads of generations, is well within our plans. 'Hearing that a man's heart and the autumn sky, along with a cat's pupils, are prone to change, no matter how steadfast your honorable words may be, my heart cannot feel at ease. If we are souls destined to be united, it is the same now or in the future. As the worldly proverb goes, "If something is delicious, eat it first." With whom are you feeling constrained that you utter such things? I cannot hear you, Tsukihoko-sama! Saying such comforting words to me, you obtained a precious jewel, and because of that, your heart has changed. Rather than having a rustic Tērin-hime as a wife for one's whole life and generation, when we reach Tai'an Castle, you intend to look ahead and choose to take whichever good wives you please—peaches, violets, cherry blossoms—at your own free will and whim. My heart simply cannot accept your words. If we are to be united anyway, let it be now while you embrace grave matters; as long as I am not an insensitive woman, I will not say things that do not make sense. Before the presence of Mariyas-hime, declare clearly that we are husband and wife for two and three worlds! Your older brother, Hidate-sama, leading Yuriko-hime by the hand, are you not living together harmoniously? You have treated me like a nuisance and gone off alone to the island of Riukiujima, so that within our relationship as husband and wife there is nothing at all save a single white jewel. Your older brother, Hidate-sama,

husband and wife together, devoted themselves to the divine work, and thanks to that, the marital affair of the jewel and the mirror—you must understand this even from that! If at that time you had taken me along with you on official business, I too would certainly have joined in the precious divine work and returned with a rare sacred mirror. If this time you go to Tai'an Castle for the Kototama battle, in the same manner as the Hidate couple, why not complete our marriage, unite the water and fire of husband and wife, and stand forth for the divine service? If, even when I say this much and explain it to you, you still refuse to listen, then it cannot be helped even if your present words are called lies. Let us quickly receive your reply!'

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, O Kunitamagami, Tatsuyohime, Now and here, I beseech thee, I beseech thee, Let us bind the covenant of husband and wife, With all the sincere heart of Tērinhime, I offer up this prayer, Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, May thy divine spirits be ever blessed.'

Thus she finished singing. Tsukihoko concealed his perplexed expression and said,

'Ah, Lady Tērinhime, your heart is well understood. I, too, must henceforth engage in the activities of a thousand riders and a single horse. If I have a wife, my mind will be drawn away by it, and I will not be able to act as I wish, so please wait until after my triumphant return. I will surely fulfill my promise....... Lady Mariyasu, if I do so, how would it be?'

Mariyasuhime: 'Heh...'

That was all she said, and she remained silent with her head bowed.

Tērinhime: 'To say that having a wife distracts one's mind and prevents full service—that is a strange thing for you to say indeed. If having a wife prevents one from working, would not all the men of the world remain unmarried without exception? Even Lord Hinodewake himself, alongside his wife, splendidly performed the divine service, did he not? Using some evasive tactics like a gourd-catfish, delaying the day as a temporary escape, your intention is surely to make me into an old cherry blossom. No matter what you say, I will yearn for your footsteps and head toward the battlefield. If I go with you and prove to be even a slight hindrance, I, too, am a woman of humble standing, and I shall cleanly slash my throat and die before your eyes.'

Tsukihoko: 'Since I do not want to make you do such a thing, I am telling you to wait until after the triumphant return.'

Tērinhime: 'Do you look down upon me so much as to treat me like a worthless woman? Even I can splendidly perform the likes of what Yurikohime does and show it to you. ... Now, now, Lady Mariyasu, did you not say so to me at Mount Ārisu? Please hurry and arrange our marriage. Hesitating at this juncture must mean you intend in the end to harmonize your affections with Tsukihoko-sama.'

Thus she turned her spear toward an unexpected quarter and began to throw atantrum. Mariyasuhime, looking utterly at her wits' end and resolved, spoke:

Mariyasuhime: 'Tsukihoko-sama, please hold the marriage ceremony right here. Should you by any chance refuse to listen, in order to clear Lady Tērinhime's doubts, I shall commit suicide right here and perish.'

Promptly drawing out her pocket dagger with a sharp flash, she manifested her resolute mind, her lips trembling violently, her face turned pale, and she displayed an extraordinary atmosphere.

Tsukihoko: 'Very well, I shall obey your will. ... Lady Tērinhime, then from today we are publicly a married couple, and I ask for your kind care for long years to come.'

Tērinhime rejoiced as if about to take flight,

'Lady Mariyasu, thank you... Tsukihoko-sama, you are a splendid man, truly the seed of Lord Shinadohiko, my observation was not mistaken.'

She rejoiced like one mad, rushed straight up before the divine altar, and offered up a prayer of thanksgiving.

Happily, through the matchmaking of Mariyasuhime, the marriage of Tsukihoko and Tērinhime was performed. From this time forth, with Mariyasuhime as the leader, beginning with the sun and moon husband and wife, the two sons and five daughters—Yachiyohime and Teruyohime—made ready their preparations, and set out to march upon Taian Castle in order to rescue King Kārusu, Princess Yāchin, Mikoto Shinadohiko, and the others.

テーリン姫『それ程妾をヤクザの女とお見下げ遊ばして御座るのですか。妾だつてユリコ姫様のなさる事位は立派に勤めてお目にかけます。……コレコレ、マリヤス姫様、貴女は、アーリス山で妾に仰有つたぢや御座いませぬか。どうぞ早く結婚の取持をして下さいませ。此期に及んで躊躇なさるのはヤツパリ末になつて月鉾さまと情意投合なさるお考へでせう』

と妙な所へ鉾を向けて駄々を捏ね出した。マリヤス姫は是非なく思ひ切つた様に、

マリヤス姫『月鉾さま、是非此場で結婚の式を挙げて下さい。万一お聞き下さらねば、妾はテーリン姫様の疑惑を解く為、ここで自刃して相果てまする』

と早くも懐剣をスラリと引抜き決心の心を現はし、唇をビリビリ震はせ、顔色青ざめて、唯事ならぬ気配を示してゐる。

月鉾『ハイ、貴女の御意に従ひます。……テーリン姫様、そんなら今日から天下晴れての夫婦仲、どうぞ末長う御世話を頼みます』

テーリン姫飛び立つ許りに打喜び、

『マリヤス姫様、有難う、……月鉾さま、お前は立派な男、流石は真道彦様の御胤、私の観察は違はなかつた』

と狂気の如く喜び、直に神前に駆上つて、感謝の祝詞を奏上するのであつた。

マリヤス姫の媒酌にて芽出度く、月鉾、テーリン姫の結婚は行はれた。これより、マリヤス姫を首領とし、日、月夫婦を始め、八千代姫、照代姫の二男五女は、愈準備を整へ、泰安城へカールス王、ヤーチン姫、真道彦命其他を救ふべく出陣する事となつた。
