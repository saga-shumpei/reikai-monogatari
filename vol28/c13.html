Chapter 13: 唖の対面 [813]

唖の対面〔813〕

§ 1

In the ancient days of the Gods of Chihaya, serving in the rigorous capital of Jerusalem, Kunibito, a priest of Kami, was born as the spiritual descendant.

With a heart as firm as Tsunekusu, drawing from the pure and radiant streams, two royal children received the twin islands of Riukiu, north and south, receiving them mutually to protect this floating island eternally.

Among them, especially Teruhiko, in that distant age of Kami, the archangel of the noble capital, descendant of Hiromunehiko, taking to wife Teruko hime, and serving simultaneously as the priest of Kami and king of the Yaheyamatau islands facing the south, opened the sacred capital in the land of Sawara, a highland plateau where even the winds are pure, and cherished the people of the four quarters like children.

Revered as Kami, the world was governed in profound peace, resembling truly the age of Kami.

Encircling the castle of Sawara, in the water-pales where clear springs drift, numerous black carps and red carps gleamed, flashing their golden scales briskly in the morning sun, sparkling and swimming happily in a spectacle resembling the golden sea that once surrounded the holy land.

High upon the castle head, the golden ten-sun divine crest shone brilliantly, and the divine majesty resounded across the four quarters, while the voices of small birds somehow chanted of a tranquil spring.

Truly, the divine awe manifested the achievements of Teruhiko and Teruko hime high in the august sky.

In the peaceful island of spheres, without trouble, the people befriended one another, free from strife, free from illness, and free from crop failure, while the countrymen indulged in dreams of peace, joy, and harmony, and the glossy voices of song, dance, and music echoed throughout the entire country without a corner left untouched.

To such a place, from the holy land of Tamamo no Yama in Taiwan, Hinodate, Tsukihoko, and Yurikohime, three divine priests for the first time, cutting through the waves of the sphere island, rowed into the port of El.

Spurring the horses of their hearts across the tree-grown highlands, knowing no sorrows with their running steeds, they arrived safely at the capital, crossed the long bridge, and slowly from the eastern gate, walked upon the road where crape myrtles and daily flowers bloomed in full glory.

§ 2

Sannin: 'Yes, we understand completely. And around what day will the King be returning?'

Yachiyohime and Teruyohime, deeming their initial greeting finished, answered not a single word, and sliding their feet smoothly across the tatami mats, they pushed open the sliding screens once more, closed them with a swift motion, and concealed their figures in the adjoining room. The men and women of the assembled company all remained completely silent, sitting properly in seiza upon their round cushions, thereby expressing absolute restraint during the King's absence.

The three of them also had no choice; remaining utterly silent and without breaking their posture, they sat straight upon their round cushions day and night without distinction. Enduring with steadfast patience the agonizing pain as if their knees and heels were being torn away, sweating profusely while minding the presence of everyone around them, they could not even wipe away the sweat from their faces,

'Ah, what a severe training we have been made to undergo. If it were going to be like this, we should have just gone off and played somewhere else for another two or three days...'

thus they thought within their hearts, enduring the intense suffering.

The previously mentioned Yachiyohime and Teruyohime had the three court ladies carry in the dining trays, and solemnly placed them before the three persons in complete silence. When they looked, the boiled rice, soup dishes, clear soups, and seasoned vineled dishes were entirely piled high with nothing but round dumplings, stones, and sand. The three court ladies quietly and silently withdrew. Teruyohime and Yachiyohime, dressed in full formal attire, each opened a silver fan and, maintaining absolute silence as if performing Mibu Kyōgen, perhaps to entertain the three guests, gracefully and with perfectly matched hand-clapping and foot-stamping, danced for a short while while dripping with sweat. Their hand gestures, foot movements, hip swaying, and posture reached the height of comicality, and it seemed as though they would burst into laughter; yet in this seat of the vow of silence, bursting into laughter was impossible, and the agony of forcibly enduring the hilarity was extreme. Due to the intense heat, their throats grew parched. Their stomachs grew empty. Yet they could not very well eat stones, and could only gaze resentfully at the dining trays. After dancing for a short while, Teruyohime and Yachiyohime concealed their figures in the next room.

Men and women entered and left one after another, performing amusing pantomimes, as if striving to make the company's jaws drop and cause them to laugh helplessly. However, all of them wore thoroughly grim faces that seemed to say, 'What on earth is funny about such things?', concealing their amusement.

Thus at last three days and three nights passed. Just as the three were paying heed to a sudden and bustling sound of footsteps, wondering what could be the matter, the party that came back, guarded by numerous retainers, was none other than King Teruhiko and Princess Teruko.

Advancing straight into the inner palace, they likewise offered a light, silent bow of recognition to the three, and husband and wife, making their eyes speak to one another, swiftly concealed their figures in the annex.

The men and women who served in King Teruhiko's retinue likewise offered a light, silent bow to the assembly, and sat down properly in correct order in that place. A short while later, from the annex, the voices of King Teruhiko and Princess Teruko chanting the Amatsu Norito came echoing forth. Following this voice, the company chanted the Amatsu Norito and intoned the heavenly counting songs in a tone as though they had been waiting eagerly. However, Hinidate, Tsukihoko, and Yurikohime, remaining as before in absolute silence, only folded their hands and continued their silent inward prayers and supplications.

After a short while, King Teruhiko and Princess Teruko, having the aforementioned beautiful maidens Yachiyohime and Teruyohime carry three-legged offering stands, appeared sternly before the three persons, offered a light bow without saying a word, and exchanged a glance with Teruyohime and Yachiyohime, whereupon the two women presented the offering stands before the three. When they looked, there were three sealed letters. One was addressed to Hinidate, and the other two bore the names of Tsukihoko and Yurikohime. The three received them upon their heads in absolute silence, immediately broke the seals to open them, and upon looking, various divine revelations were set forth. The three displayed expressions of joy, and in absolute silence showed their gratitude. King Teruhiko and Princess Teruko leisurely concealed their figures in the annex once again.

At this, Hinidate's party exchanged bows with the assembly, hurriedly departed from the mansion, passed through the front gate, and ran out beyond the castle walls. From behind, Yachiyohime and Teruyohime came running in breathless pursuit. At last, at the southern gate of the capital of Sawara, the two caught up, and here the two men and three women decided to proceed pointing toward Mount Kōyō, where Sennin Tsunekusu was secluded.

八千代姫、照代姫は一旦挨拶を了りし事とて一言も答へず、サラサラと畳に足を辷らせ乍ら再び襖を押あけ、スツと閉ぢて一間に姿を隠した。並ゐる一同の男女は何れも無言の儘、行儀よく円座の上に端坐して、王の不在中絶対的謹慎を表して居る。

三人も已むを得ず、無言の儘、膝もくづさず、昼夜の区別なく円座の上に安座し、膝も踵もむしれる如き痛さをジツと怺へ、汗をブルブルかき乍ら、一同の手前を憚り、顔をも得拭はず、

『ヤアえらい修業をさせられたものだ。こんな事なら、モウ二三日どつかで遊んで来たらよかつたに……』

と心の内に思ひ乍ら、苦しさを怺へて居る。

以前の八千代姫、照代姫は三人の侍女に膳部を運ばせ、恭しく三人の前に無言の儘つき出した。見れば飯も平も汁も生酢も一切団子石や砂許りが盛つてある。三人の侍女はしづしづとして、無言の儘立去つた。照代姫、八千代姫は盛装をこらし、二人各銀扇を開いて無言の儘、壬生狂言の様に三人の馳走の心持か、品よく、手拍子足拍子を揃へ、一時許り汗をたらして踊つて見せた。其手つき足つき、尻の振りやう、腰の具合、実に滑稽を極め、吹き出す様に思はれたが、無言の行の此席には吹き出す事も出来ず、可笑しさを無理に怺へて居る苦しさ。厳しき暑さに喉は渇いて来る。腹は空いて来る。され共石を食らふ訳にもゆかず、恨めし相に膳部を眺めてゐるのみであつた。照代姫、八千代姫は一時許り踊つた末、次の間に姿を隠した。

交る交る男女入り来りて、面白き物真似を演じ、一同の腮をとき、笑ひ倒さむと努むるものの如くであつた。されど何れも、そんな事が何が可笑しい……と云つた様な渋り切つた顔をして、可笑しさを隠して居る。

斯くして漸く三日三夜を過ぎた。俄に騒々しき人の足音、何事ならむと三人は心を配る折、数多の従臣に守られて、帰り来りしは照彦王、照子姫の一行であつた。

矢庭に奥殿に進み入り、これ亦無言の儘、三人に軽く目礼し、夫婦は目と目に物言はせ乍ら、別館に足早く姿を隠した。

照彦王の御供に仕へし男女も同じく無言の儘、一同に軽く目礼し、其場に行儀よく列を正して端坐して居る。稍少時あつて別館より、照彦王、照子姫の奏上する天津祝詞の声響き来る。一同は此声に連て、待兼てゐた様な調子で、天津祝詞を奏上し天の数歌を唱へ上げた。されど日楯、月鉾、ユリコ姫は依然として無言の儘、合掌して暗祈黙祷を続くるのみであつた。

暫くあつて照彦王、照子姫は、以前の美人八千代姫、照代姫に三宝を持たせ乍ら、三人の前に厳然として現はれ、軽く目礼して物をも言はず、照代姫、八千代姫に目配せすれば、二人は三宝を三人の前に差出した。見れば三通の封書である。一通は日楯に、又他の二通は月鉾、ユリコ姫の宛名が記してあつた。三人は無言の儘押戴き、直に封を押切つて開き見れば、種々の神示が示されてある。三人は喜びの色を現はし、無言の儘感謝の意を示した。照彦王、照子姫は悠々として又もや別館に姿を隠した。

茲に日楯の一行は一同に目礼し乍ら急ぎ館を立出で、表門を潜り、城外に走り出た。後より八千代姫、照代姫は息もせきせき追つかけ来る。漸くサワラの都の南の門にて、二人は追ひ付き、爰に二男三女は常楠仙人の立籠る向陽山を指して進み行く事となつた。
