§ 1

Once routed by the divine forces of Mamichihiko of Ananai-kyō and lurking in various places to watch for an opportunity to rise again, the faction consisting of Shyakartan, Tororensu, Princess Sērusu, Saarbōsu, Hōrokēsu, Seurusutin, Tārusu, Tsūrensu, Nandārusu, and Biyasēru formed a Triple Alliance army, took advantage of the gloom of night to attack Taianjiyō with a great war cry, and fired stone mortars; thus, even the staunch and impregnable fortress crumbled away within a single night, and the supreme figures including King Kārusu, Tēresutan, Hōrusāsu, Hōrensu, Yūtopiyāru, Tsūrensu, Shīrinsu, Ēru, Hāreyāru, Ōitsuku, Hiyūzu, Andēyāru, Nyūjiēru, Hāru, Kanton, Maruchiru, Urāru, Kingusu, Tōmasu, Māshiyāru, Princess Yārusu, and Princess Kānēru were all taken prisoner, bound without exception in the dark dungeons of the rock cavern castle on the upper reaches of the Tankei where King Kārusu had once established his camp, and were left to suffer the cruel and unremitting tortures of day and night.

The recaptured Taianjiyō hailed Princess Sērusu as queen, Shyakartan and Tororensu became the gods of the Left and Right Protectors, Saarbōsu, Hōrokēsu, and Seurusutin each assumed important posts, and once again grasping the political power of the lands north of Niitakayama, riding on the momentum to occupy the sacred sites of Ananai-kyō such as Jitetsu-tan and the palaces dedicated to the deities of the nation's soul, and aiming to establish the power of Brahmanism, they made Seurusutin the general, with Saarbōsu and Hōrokēsu as vice-generals, leading tens of thousands of soldiers, crossing Mount Ārisu with flags flying and drums beating, and now surged close to the expanse of the Lake of Tamamo.

Here from the sacred mountain of Tamamo, Princess Mariyasu was made the general of the divine forces, and Hinodate, Tsukinohoko, Princess Yuriko, Princess Tērin, Princess Teruyo, and Princess Yachiyo, together with a mere handful of attendants, advanced to face this grand army and commence a Kototama battle. The enemy's vanguard and a detachment of the Shingun met on the shores of Lake Tamamo, and the numerous soldiers led by Seurusutin, despising the enemy for their small number, aligned the tips of their bamboo spears toward Princess Mariyasu's small troop and rushed forward with the fierce momentum of a lion springing upon its prey, a truly terrifying sight. Princess Mariyasu immediately issued orders to Princess Yuriko, causing her to take out the Mirror of the Great Serpent and shine it upon the rushing, ferocious soldiers. The mirror taken out by Princess Yuriko instantly emitted an intense radiance, and not a single soldier of the enemy army was left unscathed; their eyes were dazzled, their hearts trembled, and without even exchanging a single blow, they collapsed in a heap like falling dominoes right on the spot.

Seurusutin, dazzled by the majestic virtue of the mirror, had his life narrowly saved by his subordinates and fled away to somewhere unknown. Seeing this unsightly defeat of their general Seurusutin, the left and right vice-generals Saarbōsu and Hōrokēsu fell into disorder and retreated at top speed, each trying to be first, heading back toward Taianjiyō.

The ferocious soldiers who had advanced here as the vanguard numbered nearly five or six thousand. Losing their eyes to the last man, falling and groaning until there was scarcely any room left to move, trying to escape by groping with hands and feet only to tumble down ravines, colliding with one another to reach the utmost confusion, their tragic state was truly beyond bearing to look upon. Princess Mariyasu immediately sought to bring them to allegiance through Kototama, and with a clear voice began to sing a propaganda poem.

When the deities manifest in the outward realm, Distinguishing clearly between good and evil, The deity who created this world is Ōnaobi, With a heart as vast as Great Naobi. Whatever may happen in the world of men, Look upon it straight with Naobi, listen to it straight. Confess and rectify the errors of your body, Whether the morning sun shines or is clouded, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even though the great earth should sink, The military forces of Princess Sērusu, No matter how fierce their momentum, No matter by how many tens of thousands they attack, Guarding the divine teachings of the true God, The divine army of Ananai, Completely beats back the approaching enemy. Beginning with Seurusutin, Who has lost the eyes of his heart, Led by Princess Sērusu—

After a while, every single one of the blinded enemy generals and soldiers opened their eyes, knelt toward Princess Mariyas and the other divine armies, and, shedding tears of gratitude like waterfalls, expressed their submission, ultimately joining the Ananai forces to serve in the Kototama war at Taian Castle.

Princess Mariyas, Hinodewake, and Tsukihokowake, while rejoicing and marveling anew at the great power of the Kototama, suddenly gained thousands of allies, unfurled the divine banner of Jūyō in the autumn wind, and with banners and drums in grand array, set forth to march upon Taian Castle.

Led by the divine generals of two sons and five daughters, and drawing thousands of submissive followers in their train, as they drew close to Taian Castle, Saelbōsu and Hōrokēsu deployed double-lateral formations to resist the attack. At this time, Princess Yachiyo shone the Great Serpent's mirror toward the right-wing army of Saelbōsu, while Princess Teruyo pointed the Great Serpent's mirror toward the left-wing army of Hōrokēsu and made it shine upon them; once again, not a single person was left untouched, all becoming blind, and even Hōrokēsu and Saelbōsu were struck with dazzled vision and prostrated themselves on the spot, every last one of them expressing submission.

However, Princess Mariyas paid no heed to the prostration of both armies, and proceeded directly to the front gate. Accompanied by Hinodewake and Tsukihokowake within the castle, she advanced deep inside, and presented red and white jewels toward Princess Sērusu and the other retainers. When she shone these upon them, Princess Sērusu instantly reverted to a golden-furred, nine-tailed evil fox, and all the other retainers likewise became evil demons and evil foxes, raising clouds and vanishing like smoke into mid-air, no one knew where.

From this time forth, Princess Mariyas remained within the castle, and together with Princess Teruyo, Princess Teruyo, Princess Yuriko, and Princess Tērin, along with the soldiers who had surrendered at the shores of Lake Tamamo, she took charge of garrison duties. She dispatched Hinodewake and Tsukihokowake to the rock-cave prison in the upper reaches of Dankei, ordering them on an expedition to rescue King Kārusu, Princess Yāchin, and Michimichihiko no Mikoto.

The brothers of Sun and Moon led four or five hundred of the surrendered soldiers, advanced toward the rock-cave prison, subjugated dozens of guards one after another through the virtue of the jewels, and entering the prison themselves, rescued King Kārusu above all others. King Kārusu's beard had grown long and disheveled, his complexion was pale, his body was skin and bone, and he appeared precisely like a dead person, presenting a pitiful sight to behold. Hinodewake and Tsukihokowake, upon witnessing this state, could not bear their feelings of sympathy. The resentment they had held until then—seeing him as the hateful King Kārusu who had previously imprisoned their father—vanished goodness knows where, and they were simply overcome with tears of compassion.

King Kārusu was rescued by the two children of Michimichihiko no Mikoto, and being even more touched by the deeply compassionate hearts of the brothers, he firmly grasped their hands, shedding tears and expressing his gratitude as time slipped away. As she finished chanting with a clear and refreshing voice, the tens of thousands of armies who until then had been groaning in blindness upon Daichi all opened their eyes simultaneously, joined their hands before the four, and choking with tears of gratitude—wondering if the heavenly deities had descended—surrendered from the very depths of their hearts.

Hinodewake addressed them all, meticulously preaching and expounding the teachings of the path of Ananahi, causing each to first return to their respective homes, and together with King Kārsu and the others, he calmly and leisurely proceeded to enter the innermost chambers of Taian Castle.
