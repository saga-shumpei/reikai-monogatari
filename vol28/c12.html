In the sacred precinct of Mount Tamamo, from the remote ages of the divine era, as the lineal descendants of the Hiko of Mamichi who pioneered the Ananai-kyō, Mamichihiko—renowned in the world—in order to rescue the sudden crisis of Taianjiyō, gathered together the administrators and believers of the Ananahi deities, organized a divine army, appeared at Taianjiyō, and repulsed the encroaching enemies; but at last, suffering a bizarre disaster, suspected by King Carl, and thrown into a dark prison, having no way to escape and only weeping together with Yoshikohime, suffering amid an unspeakable false charge, they groan in the darkness and spend bitter days and months.

Wishing to rescue that miserable state, with a sincere heart thinking of her father, Hinitate and Tsukihoko, both persons leaving the sacred ground behind along with Yurikohime, disguising their bodies in the light attire of sedge hats and raincoats, stealing away in the night, hurriedly reached the summit of Mount Aris, and while bathing in the moonlight, sitting upon a rock on one side, just as they were resting their breath, from the sacred ground of Mount Tairin, Tsukihoko, the administrator of the deities, pursuing them by searching for their tracks, thought to untie the tangled threads of Teerinhime's relentless love and make her return to the sacred ground by exhausting all words; yet Teerinhime, clouded by love, the more he explained, the more entangled she became; just at that moment, from beneath the shadows of the trees,

When everyone looked around in astonishment at the human shadow that appeared unexpectedly, by the appearance of the precious Mikoto of the unexpected Mariyashime, at last escaping from the sudden peril, Hinitate, Tsukihoko, and Yurikohime, the three administrators, gradually feeling as if they had revived from the dead, crossed over the peaks of Mount Aris, traversed the mountain range of Suan, day and night arriving gradually at the village of Teruna, and just as they were proceeding with a single flickering fire in the valley as their target, their throats parched and their bellies starved, their endurance exhausted, the three rested beneath a certain tree's shade, and while listening intently to the faint human voices leaking through, suddenly the valley wind blew,

When it seemed that something touched the heads of the three people, realizing it was fruit with the fragrant aroma of eggplants, rejoicing to the extent of leaping up, the three instantly stripped off every single eggplant without leaving one, filled their bellies, lay down, and before they knew it, fell asleep. Just in the nick of time, right in the middle of the sacred festival of Brahman, four or five natives, wishing to offer eggplants before the presence of the deity, with imposing official garments and solemnly shaking gohei, came advancing;

Originally, the three were right in the middle of navigating a night boat on Shirakawa; the natives, instantly losing their wits because every single eggplant had been stolen by someone without a single one left, illuminated them with a light and gazed upon them, and seeing snoring sounds like thunder, astonished, they immediately reported this circumstance to the chief of the Teruna village; James, upon hearing this, rushed over without losing a moment, confronted the three men and women, flared his chestnut eyes, and raged haughtily: 'The people of the Teruna village protect with their lives the eggplants offered to the deity; you outlaws who took and ate them, I shall take the lives of you three and offer them as a sacrifice to the deity!'—when he scolded and reviled thus, Yurikohime placed her hands upon the ground before the chief and pleaded:

'Fatigued to the extreme by a long journey, our throats parched and our bellies starved, not knowing they were such precious fruits, we took and ate them; please look upon this deep sin with a broad heart and forgive us.' When she begged, the chief looked up at Yurikohime's face with a smiling countenance and glared, shouting: 'If you accept my demand and become my wife, I shall forgive your sin; as for the two men, no matter what, I must take their lives and offer them as precious sacrifices before the deity, so what shall I do about the anger of the deity? Prepare yourselves!' Yurikohime exhausted various words and devoted her body to persuasively argue, until the chief gradually softened his heart, and ordering various painful ascetic practices to the two men, at last this matter came to a settlement;

Accompanying the three men and women to the side of the great altar, giving beautiful garments to Yurikohime, and giving strict orders to the two men saying, 'Pass through the raging fire,' Hinitate and Tsukihoko both chanted the Amatsu Norito, singing the heavenly numbers in a low, sobbing voice, and while softly chanting, they passed through the blazing fire many times with utmost ease, returning right before the great altar without even a burn. Just as the people lined up were astonished at their divine power, looking at each other's faces and biting their tongues in speechlessness, a great blaze of light, from nowhere, suddenly descended upon this place.

Hereupon the three transmitted the teachings of the thirty-five Kami in minute detail, beginning with James and extending to the villagers, and immediately solidified the pillars of the thirty-five teachings.

Escorted by scores of villagers, starting with the chieftain, they proceeded day and night and finally arrived at the beach of Kīrun situated at the northern edge of Takasago.

Ah, Almighty Kami, may Your divine spirits be ever prosperous and peaceful.

The waves of Tokoyo, too, as the placenta of Takasago Island, formed by the Kami as Taiwan Island—with its abundant tree fruits, pure waters, and thriving birds, beasts, insects, and fish—was renowned as an earthly paradise created by heaven.

Leaving the sacred ground of Mount Tamamo in the central region of Taiwan Island behind, while tasting hardships and sufferings, guided safely through the long journey by the chieftain of the village of Teruna, they arrived safely at the port of Kīru, hired a boat, and rowed forth at the mercy of the waves.

In the north wind blowing at that moment, mountain-like waves mercilessly crashed against the boat of the three.

Yuriko no Hime stood before the helmsman, calming the waves and heading towards the Yaeyama Islands of Ryūkyū as the Kami willed.

Gradually the three arrived safely here at the port of Eru, tied their boat to the shore, and headed towards the capital of Sawara, which was famed as the eternal dwelling place of resounding Teruhiko.

The three finally arrived at the capital of Sawara. This was a new metropolis built in the center of an endless plain; a forest of white poplars enveloped its four sides, and groves of bashō were dotted here and there. The people of the country generally subsisted on bashō fruits, strawberries, apples, eggplants, persimmons, and the like, or sustained their lives with supplementary foods such as mountain yams and freshwater fish.

The capital of Sawara was surrounded on all four sides by a vast moat. Its width was almost about one chō. Sturdy bridges were thrown across the east, west, south, and north, and slightly to the north, the towering peak of Sawara soared into the clouds, being hailed as the sacred ground of the Four Divine Guardians. Within the castle, hundreds of dwellings stood in rows, and as a metropolis from three hundred thousand years ago, it was acclaimed as the greatest. The castle of Sawara occupied a vast area almost at its center, with stone mansions towering high above the old trees. Within the castle, there were fields, rivers, and swamps, constructed in such a way that nothing was lacking.

The three crossed the bridge from the eastern gate and advanced inside the gate. Yellow, red, white, purple, and dark blue flowers blossomed in wild profusion on tree branches and the tips of grasses. Moreover, on both sides of the road grew dense thickets of crape myrtles and hidorihana, and the white sands shone in the sunlight, making it a land of pleasant atmosphere that surpassed by many folds the Sun-Moon Lake of Taiwan Island.

Feeling somehow shy and abashed, and chanting the Sendenka in low voices, the three finally made their way before the castle gate designated by Teruhiko as his eternal dwelling place. 一人の男『私はセルと申して、照彦王の御側近く仕ふる者で御座います。二三日以前より、照子姫様に高砂島の竜世姫命様御神懸り遊ばし、あなた方御三人様がここへ御越しになるから、出迎ひに出よとのお告で御座いました。それ故今日はお越しの日と早朝よりいろいろと、あなた方の歓迎の用意を致し、照彦様、照子姫様、奥にお待で御座います。サア御案内致しますから、早くお這入り下さいませ』

三人は一度に頭を下げ、

『有難う』

と言ひ乍らセルの後に従ひ、奥深く進み入る。門番は漸く目を醒し、

甲『オイ、ベース、チヤール、起きぬか起きぬか、大変な事が出来て来たぞ。お側役のセル様が沢山の御近侍の方々と共に三人のお客さまをお迎へ遊ばして、奥へお這入りになつた。貴様達はなまくらを構へて、寝真似をし、糟に酔うた様な事をぬかしてをつたが、たつた今ドテライお目玉だぞ。サア早く何とか、言訳を拵へて、セル様へお断りに行かねば、足袋屋の看板だ。サツパリ足あがりになつて了ふぞよ』

エル『バカを言ふな、俺達が寝真似をして居つたのを知つてゐた以上は、貴様もチヨボチヨボだ。俺が足があがる位なら、貴様は頭だから、キツト首が飛ぶぞよ。貴様は俺達の組頭だからお詫に往つて来るがよからう。俺や、こんな門番なんか、何時足があがつても構やしないのだ。常世城の門番を見い、失敗して王様のお側附になつたぢやないか。何時迄も門番を厳重に勤めて居つたら、彼奴は門番に適当な奴だと、セルの大将に見込まれたが最後、金槌の川流れ、一生頭の上る事はないぞよ。それより日中にグウグウと寝てる方が、何時栄達の途が開けるか知れないぞ。大体こんな智者学者を門番にさしておくのが見当違ひだ。マア見て居れ、明日になつたら、貴様達は門番に不適当だから、奥勤めに使つてやらうとの御命令が下るに違ない。余り取越苦労をするものでない。マア刹那心を楽むのだなア。待てば海路の風が吹くとやら、何事も運は天にありだ。そんな事に心配するよりも、酒でも呑み、一生懸命に騒ぎ、ステテコでも踊つて、今度のお客様のお慰みに供したら、照彦王様も面白い奴だと云つて、……苦しうない、門番近く参れ……とか何とか仰有つて、お手づから盃を下され、結構な御褒美に預るやらも知れたものだない。サアサア、酒だ酒だ、酒なくて何のおのれが門番かなだ。アハヽヽヽヽ』

と他愛もなき無駄事を囀り乍ら、バナナで作つた強烈い酒を、五人の門番が胡坐座になつてガブリガブリと呑み始め、ヘベレケになつて、妙な手つきをし乍ら、踊りつ舞ひつ、奥殿指して転げ込んだ。
