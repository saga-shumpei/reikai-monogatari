§ 1

Tsunehiko and Haruhiko were startled, untied Takahime's bindings, and raised her up.

Tsunehiko: 'Sensei, it was nothing of the sort, but it was a terribly dangerous affair. Please, from now on inside the ship, even if there is something you wish to say, endure it without speaking out. Both of us earnestly beg you.'

Takahime: 'You fellows are called cowards just for that reason. As if the divine living shrine of the Hinodewake-sama—who acts as the right arm of Kuniharutachinomikoto, making the three thousand worlds as round as dango, as flat as mochi, triangular, or square, and playing with them freely—would ever tuck her tail between her legs and cower just for the likes of the boatmen of the humble Takashimamaru! Hmph, do not misjudge me so excessively. You fellows are just like showing gold coins to a cat. What do you take this living shrine of the Hinodewake to be, which shines with a brilliant light far superior to gold coins or diamonds? Will you not pull yourselves together properly? As long as the gods are protecting us, even if a hundred or a thousand boatmen came together, I am not a Takahime who would let them touch even a single finger of mine, you know.'

Haruhiko: 'Even so, Milady, were you not tied up with your hands behind your back by Taltil and hoisted upside down from the mast?'

Takahime: 'My, oh my, how troubled those with clouded spirits are, because their way of receiving divine virtue is so clumsy! The living shrine of the Hinodewake thought she would like to take a quick look at the state of Takashimajima, and so she put Taltil to divine service, having him hoist her up to the top of the mast without using her hands or troubling her feet, in an elevator-style fashion. Everyone must interpret things directly and correctly through Kaminaohi and Ōnaohi, with good intentions, without exception. That is the flawless, magnificent plan of the Hinodewake-sama; that is why I tell you until my ears are covered in barnacles to always polish your spirits and reform your hearts. That fellow Taltil finally got frightened by the divine light of the Hinodewake and went and hid somewhere like a mouse.'

Tsunehiko: 'I was so amazed that I could hardly see straight and I do not know the clear details, but anyway, two splendid-looking persons appeared, and just as I thought they said something to the boatman, the boatman hurriedly slackened the rope, lowered you by hanging, and hid somewhere.'

Takahime: 'There, you see, that is why Takahime is called the living shrine of the gods. When the boatman fellow hoisted me up to the top of the mast, the divine light of the Hinodewake was so intense that his eyes were about to be dazzled, and even the passengers were about to grow dizzy, so two representatives came forward, negotiated with the boatman, and for that reason he was inevitably forced to lower me. That is what is called the divine might of the gods, where things are arranged conveniently. How could such a flawless plan ever be understood by the likes of you?'

Tsunehiko: 'Hey Haruhiko, what an obstinate sensei she is! Today of all days, I am truly exasperated. If people think we are disciples of such a person, there is simply no way we can arrive safely at Takashimajima. Once we have gone up to the continent, you may frolic, blow your own horn, or do whatever you please as Takahime-sama, but to put on such a stubborn front of not wanting to lose an argument in a place like this, to talk back insolently, and to be hated by the captain would be terrible. Come now, why don't we ask Takahime-sama right here to cut the ties of master and disciple with us, and become total strangers? It is not that we want to sever ties from the bottom of our hearts. We just want you to cut them outwardly, on this ship alone.'

Haruhiko: 'That is an extremely brilliant idea. Let us ask Takahime-sama right away.'

Tsunehiko: 'This is the petition of Haruhiko and myself. Will you not break the relationship of master and disciple with us for a while? That too is only outwardly; once we arrive safely at Takashimajima, please treat us as your attendants just as before. If we do not do this, it is too perilous to endure...'

Takahime: 'Do you say you want to cut ties? Even if you didn't say it from your end, Takahime was troubled by having you useless cowards become dead weight and getting in the way. I thought of cutting ties since you are annoying even for a moment, but since once abandoned by this Takahime, you cannot even manage to be beggars, being such good-for-nothing cowards, I have helped you until now thinking it pitiful. If you say from your end that you want leave, this is like setting sails to a fair wind. Cutting ties for all eternity and for the last 10,000 kalpas, do not ever be in a place that offends Takahime's eyes a second time. You trash scoundrels, ah, blind men and a person with open eyes—how well said that is. The ones who truly know Takahime's real power in this wide world are only Hinodewake-samas. There is nothing as annoying as having jinmin as opponents. Come on, both of you Tsune and Haru, won't you go off somewhere quickly? Looking reluctant, not acting like men, what are you fussing about for, eh!'

Puffing up her cheeks in a huff, swinging her body to the corners, she stamped her feet strongly two or three times on the deck, and spun around two or three times to show them.

Tsunehiko: 'Moshimoshi Takahime-sama, I cannot bear it if you get so angry. We are only asking to be given leave outwardly for just two or three days... Right, Haru, you are the same, aren't you.'

Haruhiko: 'That's right, abandoned by Takahime-sama, what will become of us? Please, Takahime-sama, without losing your temper so quickly, interpret what we say with good intentions.'

Takahime: 'Even if we cut ties for just a single day, from that moment on we are complete strangers. Cutting ties for three or five days is a bother. I will cut ties all the way to the end of the Miroku world, for all eternity and the last 10,000 kalpas, as well. When maggots like you are swarming, the whole place feels crawling and sickening. Ah, my illness feels cleared up now. Once ties are cut, we are neither master nor disciple. Please hide your figures quickly, wherever it may be. Even looking at you is disgusting, it makes my stomach sick.'

Tsunehiko: 'Takahime-sama, do not utter things like hide your figures in a single moment, or you are disgusting, or my stomach is sick, as if an Aoki-daishō had crawled right into the parlor.'

Takahime: 'It is a settled matter, you are a true foolish general. If you dawdle, I will set up incense and smoke you out. Ah, how troublesome, come the day before yesterday, come the day before yesterday!'

So saying, from her toothless mouth, she muttered and spat saliva recklessly toward the two of them.

Haruhiko: 'Saying come the day before yesterday like a spider or something, isn't that going a bit too far?'

Takahime: 'You are a spider. You are a palanquin bearer in dark clouds who cannot see an inch ahead. Until now, thanks to Takahime, you have acted arrogant doing the auspicious work of the gods, but once ties are cut by Takahime as of today, you have no choice but to become a palanquin bearer, do you? No matter how much you flatten yourself like a spider to apologize or suffer, it's no use. Ottsuhohoho!'

Tsunehiko flushed slightly in the face,

Tsunehiko: 'Takahime-sama, we are by no means your disciples. We are persons ordered as fine propaganda emissaries from the leader of Kototamawakenomikoto, so to speak, on equal footing with you. However, observing the order of seniority, respecting you as our senior and master purely out of obligation, while treating us as disciples, how could there be any question of cutting ties or not cutting ties? If you talk on sluggishly, I shall break off relations from my side... Right, Haru, aren't you the same?'

Haruhiko: 'Hearing it put that way, it is so. Moshi Takahime-sama, I am truly sorry, but starting right now, we shall cut ties with you for all eternity and the last 10,000 kalpas, until the end of the Miroku world—no, we break off relations! Since you are disgusting... and smell like a fool, please hide your figures somewhere quickly. If you stay sullen, I'll light incense. Ahahaha!'
