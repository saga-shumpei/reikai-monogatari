Chapter 3: Jewel 藻 Mountain [803]

玉藻山〔803〕

§ 1

Mamichihikonomikoto had been residing in this island since the age of Kuniharutachinōokami, and his descendants, all succeeding to the name of Mamichihiko, established a sacred place to the north of Nihitakayama, expanded the Way of Ananahi throughout the entire island, and were nurturing the root of Mikunidamashihi. However, ever since a faction of Brahmanism drifted to this island, Arkus, the king who was a descendant of Hanamorihikonomikoto, abandoned the teachings of Ananahi and caused the people to submit to Brahmanism, so that the inhabitants, without distinction of high and low, all submitted to the teachings of Brahman. Yet, the power of King Arkus and the religious authority of Brahman were exercised only to the north of Nihitakayama, and their influence did not reach even in the slightest to the south of Nihitakayama.

Mamichihiko crossed far beyond Nihitakayama, established his dwelling in Jitsugetsutan, a highland region corresponding to the southeast, and was civilizing the lands of the southeast, south, and west. However, the Sarbous brothers, who were the prime ministers of King Arkus, attempted to occupy this location as well to build a second kingdom, and from time to time led troops and pressed on toward the sacred ground of Mount Tamamo. Nevertheless, they could not easily cross southward over the great lake where Ryūsekihime permanently resides.

At one time, Holokess led numerous Brahman believers, disguised himself in the pilgrimage of the Sanpō (Ananahi) teachings, rushed like clouds and mist into the sacred place of Mount Tamamo, watched for an opening to capture Mamichihikonomikoto alive, and attempted to occupy the entire island at a single stroke. Mamichihikonomikoto foresaw the malicious and cunning plan of Holokess, gathered numerous believers, made it his resolve to confront him by means of Kototama warfare, newly set up an altar at the summit of Mount Tamamo, and was emitting Kototama rays toward the advancing enemy. Even so, Holokess of Brahmanism did not yield in the least, and with the vigor of a roaring lion, brandished the deadly weapons each had concealed, raised war cries, and came slashing in to destroy them at a single stroke.

Mamichihiko had two robust sons in the prime of their youth, whose devotion to the gods was steadfast, named Hinotate and Tsukihoko. The father Mamichihiko chanted the Kototama toward Holokess, whereupon Holokess grew enraged, pierced Mamichihiko's chest with a long sword, struck him down upon this spot, raised a song of triumph, and his momentum was such that even heaven and earth seemed to tremble. From the body of Mamichihiko, who had been pierced and fallen upon that spot, white smoke suddenly rose in billowing clouds, transformed into a beautiful goddess, and concealed her form beyond the clouds.

The brothers Hinotate and Tsukihoko lamented the disappearance of their father Mamichihiko, and as a result of their painstaking efforts to somehow destroy the clan of Holokess, avenge their father, and re-establish the teachings of the Sanpō (Ananahi) teachings, they secretly rowed to the Dragon Island floating in the midst of the lake by night, and offered up their prayers. At this time, the sacred place of Mount Tamamo had already become a place occupied by Holokess. Mamichihiko's subordinates were scattered in all directions, and not even their shadows remained.

The Dragon Island was a sacred island with dense trees, floating in the center of the lake, with a circumference of almost nearly one ri. The two climbed up, aiming for the summit of the island mountain. Here, tall giant rocks stood side by side like walls, and in the center opened a rocky cave just large enough for a person to enter. The brothers involuntarily directed their footsteps toward that cavern. Into the summer sky, which burned like blazing heat, a wind indescribably cool and fragrant blew incessantly from within the pit. The two felt a sudden urge to explore the interior of this cave, and unknowingly proceeded about four or five chō into the depths.

Suddenly, a intense beam of light shone from somewhere. Looking back, it appeared to be the end of the cavern, where circular natural holes had been pierced on both sides, from which the sunlight was shining directly. Looking around, something like a staircase was naturally carved. The two brothers, Hinotate and Tsukihoko, climbed up these stairs, and when they looked far out ahead, the lake of Tamamo filled with azure waves, small island shadows floating here and there, and numerous waterbirds spreading their white wings flying back and forth and left and right, were truly beautiful, and the two were captivated by this scenery. When they cast their eyes far to the southeast, the sacred place of Mount Tamamo remained just as before, but since the invasion of Holokess, it had become a place where Brahmanism relied, and feeling somehow resentful in their hearts, they sank slightly into thoughts of the past and present.

Hitate: 'O younger brother, such a sacred place has been trampled by the enemy, our father has gone missing, and we brothers have nowhere to lay our heads. Though we have barely managed to escape and come hither to this Isle of the Dragon, we have not yet reached a place of safety. If things go awry, the scoundrels of the Brahmanism might pursue our footsteps even to this island—who can truly say otherwise? What if we brothers now make our prayer here to the Great Deity of the Ananai-kyō, and stake our fortune upon a single moment? If we look down, there are sheer cliffs and precipices a thousand fathoms high; consecrating our prayer to the Kami, let us cast ourselves into this blue abyss and try the measure of life and death. Should the two of us by any chance preserve our lives, the Ananai-kyō will once more restore its power as of old, and we shall be able to drive the faction of Brahmanism back to the north of Niitakayama. Tsukihoko, what think you?'

Displaying a look of determination, he thus spoke to him.

Tsukihoko: 'As you, my elder brother, have commanded, let us henceforth offer our prayer to the deities of heaven and earth, leap from this cliff into the lake, and behold the divine will.'

Expressing his agreement, the two recited the Amatsu Norito, and repeated and chanted the Heavenly Counting Song several times as a farewell to this world. From the shade of the dense trees nearby,

'The Kami appear in the front, And cleanly divide good from evil. The Kami who created this world, Kamunaozahi, And Oonaozahi of broad heart as well. Now whatever may happen in the world of men, Look straight upon it, hear it straight anew, Recite and set right your past transgressions. Propagators of the Ananai-kyō, Kototamawake and Kuniorawake, The officials of the Kami are present here. Shinadahiko, who transmits The teachings of the Great Deity Kuninotokotachi, Has been driven from the sacred ground by the enemies so brittle, Leaving Mt. Tamamo behind, And fled away like clouds and mist. The brothers who remained behind, Parted from the father whom they relied upon as their strength, Forsaken by the child of the teaching, A drifting small boat on the shores of helplessness, Weeping and sobbing they left the sacred ground,

And hither to the rough waves of the Isle of the Dragon, While drenching in the rain of tears, They sought and came to this cavern, Gazing out upon the surface of Lake Tamamo, With thoughts of boundless sorrow, Thinking now to determine their life and death, Oh, the pity of their troubled hearts! Hitate and Tsukihoko, both of you, Do not by any means afflict your hearts. Bearing the divine majesty of the precious gems Of the Ryū and Kyū upon our very bodies, Propagators of the Ananai-kyō, To you two we shall restore Mt. Tamamo Back to its original ancient days, And make the enemies of Brahmanism Yield to the truth and harmonize with it, Bestowing upon you rare and miraculous divine treasures. Ah, Kannagara kamugamumaseyo, May your august spirits be blessed and peaceful.'

So chanting, two propagators appeared and came to this place, stood erect before the brothers, and bowed slightly.

The brothers were struck with amazement as if in a dream, lowered their heads in deep reverence for a short while, without making any reply whatsoever. When the two at length raised their faces, oh, how strange it was—where had the shadows of the two propagators vanished? There only resounded the sound of the wind blowing across the mountain ridge, rustling far and wide.

From this time onward, the courage of the two brothers increased a hundredfold beyond their usual wont. Singing the Heavenly Counting Song, they scoured every corner of the islands floating upon the lake without missing a spot, searching for the whereabouts of the two propagators; yet to whatsoever place they had gone, even their shadows could no longer be seen. Nevertheless, the two were filled somehow with courage, and determined to commence their kototama warfare once more directed toward Mt. Tamamo. Sinking and floating in the lake water, they performed seven days and seven nights of ritual purification, and devoted their utmost strength to the practice of kototama.

Princess Mariyasu, a posthumous child of King Arcus who had long served as a lady-in-waiting to Princess Sales, slipped out of the mansion of Saruboth, crossed Niitakayama to the southeast day and night, wandered around the shores of Lake Tamamo, and had found refuge in the sacred precincts of Mt. Tamamo. However, due to this recent raid by Horokese, Mikotohiko no Mikoto had gone missing, his numerous subordinates had scattered in all directions, and the two, Hitate and Tsukihoko, had likewise gone missing. Just when they were driven to a dead end in their progress, she was captured by Horokese, subjected to merciless tortures, and finally imprisoned in a room with strict guards assigned to watch her.

Horokese, acting in concert with his elder brother Saruboth, sought to seize sovereign power over this entire island. With spirits soaring to the heavens, he established a Brahmanic sacred ground on Mt. Tamamo, and behaved as if the place belonged to him alone. Furthermore, he kept Princess Mariyasu imprisoned, and would occasionally go to her living quarters to force harsh negotiations upon her.

§ 3

Changing the subject, Mariyasu-hime, while drowning in tears of sorrow, murmured to herself and sang of the sadness in her heart.

'The flow of water and the destiny of man, how the world changes as it turns. When I seek the source of the Tohotsumioya, it is the Jerusalem of Takaamahara. Born as the child of King Arks, the descendant of the beautiful Mikoto, whom my ancestor served as an angel of Hanamorihiko, I am a child of fate, a hidden heir enduring this Ukiyo. Born as the sister of King Karls, who rules over Takasagojima, I became a lady-in-waiting to Sales-hime, the daughter of the foul-hearted Saarbose. While I was doing my utmost to uncover their ugly schemes, fearing the Mikoto of my father, I was driven away by the heartless behavior of Sales-hime, and now, in my wretched solitude,'

'A traveler with no known destination, saved by the compassion of Kami. Saved at this mansion, known by reputation as Tamamoyama, the Reijiyō of Ananai-kyō opened by Mamichihikonokami, I was spending my days in joy. But clouds gather over the moon, and storms fall upon the flowers; blown by the winds of this Ukiyo, today I am a prisoner in sorrow. Ah, what shall I do? I can only weep. As the tears dry in the evening twilight, where is the Kami of Tsukihoko whom I long for? Together with my parents and siblings, scattered by the midnight storm, I am on a journey with no known path. Even if you are somewhere, the true heart of Mariyasu-hime, though separated by thousands of miles of mountains, fields, seas, and rivers, what of it? I will seek you out and go to your side.'

'Yet, though I say so, how heartless it is. Captured by the foul-hearted, ugly Kami, Horokēsu, and imprisoned in a dark room, I spend my days in misery. Wringing my sleeves with tears morning and evening, I seek the whereabouts of the one I love. If only in a dream, I wish to meet the Tsukihoko-kami I adore. Alas, the prayers I offered before the presence of Konohana-hime were in vain. The unwanted love of Horokēsu, coming night after night to my dark prison cell to plead with me, the words he speaks are so loathsome. Though I wish to vanish from existence, what can I do, being a body that is not a Kami? There is no way to escape.'

'The one I love does not come, and the ugly Magatsu-kami, whom I loathe like a viper, is persistent, stalking me morning and evening. O, priest of the Brahman religion, if I had wings, I would fly over the window of this prison and soar to the side of my beloved Lord. Ah, how frustrating, how painful it is,' she whispered, pleading through her tears.

At that moment, the mansion suddenly became noisy, and many people were running about in confusion. Seeing that the situation was extraordinary, Mariyasu-hime thought, 'Has Mamichihiko-mikoto led many allies to head into battle? Or have the two, Hidate and Tsukihoko, led a great divine army and appeared here? For some reason, my heart has become brave. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, may your spirits be blessed.'

She unconsciously pressed her palms together in prayer. Just then, Horokēsu burst open the door of the secret room with a violent force, his expression terrifying.

Horokēsu: 'Hey, Mariyasu-hime, an emergency has occurred. Come, follow me!'

He tried to drag her away by force to escape the scene, but in his panic, Mariyasu-hime stood firm.

Mariyasu-hime: 'As a priest of the Brahman religion who leads many subordinates, what is the meaning of this panic? First, calm yourself. Once I have heard the situation, I may or may not follow you.'

She deliberately feigned composure to stall for time. Horokēsu replied,

Horokēsu: 'There is no time to lose!'

Without allowing her to speak, he tucked her under his arm and attempted to rush out of the secret room. Just then, Hidate and Tsukihoko, perhaps moved by the virtue of the jewels of Ryu and Kyu, appeared on the scene, radiating intense five-colored light from their bodies.

Hidate and Tsukihoko: 'Hey, Horokēsu, wait a moment!'

Horokēsu stumbled and fell, leaving Mariyasu-hime behind, and together with his many subordinates, he vanished into the night, disappearing into the clouds and mist, no one knew where.
