Chapter 17: Sincerity の告白 [817]

誠の告白〔817〕

§ 1

Continuing from there, Tsukihoko also rescued from within the dungeon Māruēsu, Tērusutan, and others who had once become executives at the sacred site of Ananai-kyō, raised the banner of revolt once more, worked for King Kārusu, had been captured by the faction of Princess Sērusu, and along with King Kārusu, had been cast into the dungeon of the rock cave, returned directing towards Taianjiyō, and in the great hall of Yaata, held a ceremony of mutual meeting.

Upon the central august seat appeared King Kārusu, displaying his emaciated physique, with Princess Yāchin and Princess Mariyasu standing side by side near him, and next in order stood Mamichihikonomikoto, Hitate, Tsukihoko, Yachiyohime, and Teruyohime arranged in proper sequence, gathered like glittering stars, mutually offering norito, and concluding by erecting himorogi, strictly performing the grand festival for the Land-Spirit deities beginning with Ōkunitachirutsu-no-Mikoto, then moving on to the feast of naorai, mingling together without separation, superiors and inferiors harmonizing and indulging in reminiscences of the past, furthermore praising and thanking the wartime exploits of Princess Mariyasu and the followers, and while playing the ichigenkin, each stood up, praised the gods, chanted sacred songs, and danced wildly.

While moving his crane-like emaciated body, King Kārusu sang and danced by himself. His song:

Leaving the truth of Ananai established by the infinitely merciful Ōkami as secondary / Superstitiously believing that the teaching of body-master and spirit-subordinate was supreme / Upholding pure selfishness to the end / Misled by attendants / What foolishness to have bought the resentment of the people of the land without knowing it! / Ah, kannagara kamukamu / The divine spirits blessing us / The astonishing and earth-shaking joy / Of being born into this world once again on a day I never expected / At what time in the world could it ever be forgotten? / Hitate and Tsukihoko, both persons / Without holding a grudge against King Kārusu who tormented their father / And exhausted every limit of evil / The pure hearts of the two brothers / Until the end of time / Until the end of the reign of 567 / Handed down from generation to generation among descendants / Never forgetting this august grace / Princess Yāchin and Princess Mariyasu / Tormented by the demons of their hearts / What foolishness to have doubted single-mindedly up to this very moment / Longing for them from the bottom of my heart / Princess Yāchin and Princess Mariyasu / The unreasonable demands and sufferings / That I have until now inflicted upon you / Please look back straightly and listen back straightly / And forgive my sins and faults!

Ah, kannagara kamukamu / May the august spirits bless us / The hundred sins of the ugly officials / Who unknowingly and unconsciously performed crooked deeds in obedience to me / Please look back straightly and listen back straightly / And grant forgiveness, Heavenly Gods / And Earthly Gods, the eight million deities / Before the august presence of the Land-Spirit deities / Relevantly and reverently I pray / Ah, kannagara kamukamu / May the august spirits bless us!
