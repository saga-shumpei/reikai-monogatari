天下泰平〔817〕

§ 1

Mariyasuhime stands up, singing and dancing of her own accord.

A descendant of the god Hanamorihikonokami, Born as the hidden child of King Ākusu, My very Mitama, Obeying the Naimei of my father's Mikoto, Disguising myself as a jijiyo, Following Sērusuhime, Spying morning and evening upon The actions of Sārubōsu, Hōrokēsu, And other magami, Folding the keiriyaku of the akunin One by one within my breast, While waiting for the time to come, That faction of Sērusuhime, Rejecting Yāchinhime, Became the consort of King Kārusu, And just at the juncture when she sought To grasp the sovereignty of Taianjiyō In her own hands and enforce its bausei, I, unable to bear it, severely punished The faction of Sērusuhime,

Stepping out of Taianjiyō, To the seijiyō of Jitsugetsutan Where Mamichi no Hiko has appeared, Serving the kami in stealth, As every morning and evening I gazed upon The valiant figure of Tsukihoko, The dark distress of love suddenly afflicted me; Though approaching on every occasion And pleading the depth of my heart, Tsukihoko of firm shinjin, Taking the reason of heaven and earth as his shield, Did not easily grant my path of love; Though I resented Tsukihoko's merciless heart, When I prostrated myself before the presence of the kami And quietly inquired the divine will, I realized: I am the hidden wife of King Ākusu, My physical body born from sin, And to force the pledge of brother and sister Upon the precious and sacred office of Tsukihoko, Who is equal to the kami—what manner of thing is this, Repenting that I had erred, Here I finally relinquished it, And when I apologized for that sin To the kami of heaven and earth, Suddenly the moon of Shinnyo shone in my heart, And the darkness of the path of love cleared away. From then on, with all my heart, Devoting my mind and devoting my body, I served before the divine altar of the sacred ground of Tairin, but, When I heard that Mamichi no Hiko, the divine officiant, Was bound within the prison upon the cave And made to suffer a myriad torments, My heart burned with impatience, And even as I prayed morning and evening Before the presence of the kami of the Land's Soul To rescue him, Tsukihoko,

The god Hidate, Yurikohime, Teruhiko no Ō-sama who served King Teruhiko, Terushiro no Hime, and Yachiyohime, Bringing the five precious divine treasures, Returned to the seijiyō, Enshrining them morning and evening, At the juncture when they were sharpening their hearts, The faction of Sērusuhime, Gathering together the evil kami Shiyākarutan, Tororensu, Sārubōsu, and Hōrokēsu, Threw Taianjiyō into ruin, Exercising violent might, Throwing every single one of the officiants Who had served the king of Kārusu Into the prison upon the cave, Exhausting all bounds of outrageous and lawless tyranny, And further advancing, seeking to trample The seijiyō of Mt. Tamamo, Beginning with Sērusuchin,

Making Sārubōsu and Hōrokēsu their generals, The force coming to attack truly Looked difficult to underestimate; I, leading a small divine host, Departed from the seijiyō, At the juncture of arriving at the lake shore of Tamamo, Encountered the numerous hosts led by Sērusuchin; Faced by the wild boar warriors Coming to attack with aligned spears With the momentum of a lion's fierce rush, When I took out the mirror of the great serpent And illuminated the enemy, Their eyes were blinded by the fierce light of the mirror's face, Their souls trembled, and in an instant They fell like falling dominoes. When I unfailingly proclaimed The Kototama received from the kami, Needless to say the eyes of the heart, Even the physical eyes of the enemy forces Who had fallen until now Were suddenly opened as before, The joy of at last realizing And awakening from the bottom of their hearts To the precious divine grace of the three-five And making allegiance! From then on, riding over the peak of Ārisu, I advanced to attack the riding ground of the castle of Taian, Facing the swarming tens of thousands of military forces, The enemy was abashed by the light Of the precious mirrors of Terushiro no Hime and Yachiyohime, Blinded in their eyes, struck down, And amidst their suffering and writhing, I advanced deep into the interior of the castle, Confronted Sērusuhime, And when I illuminated her by shooting the jewel of the great serpent, She reverted to the form of an evil fox, And together with her numerous retainers,

Revealing her strange true nature, Vanished like clouds and mist. Here I rested my breath, And while guarding Taianjiyō Together with Terushiro no Hime, Yachiyohime, Tērinshime, Yurikohime, And other precious divine beings, Wishing to rescue the imprisoned people Beginning with King Kārusu, Mamichi no Hiko, Yachiyohime, And including Māruēsu, Hōrensu, Tērusutan, and others, When I dispatched the two brothers Sun and Moon To the prison upon the cave, The two parties auspiciously fulfilled their mission, And how valiant was their triumphant return Raising songs of victory! Ah, wondrous are the kami, With their august Mitama flourishing ever more!

Hearing this proclamation, Mamichihikonomikoto was greatly astonished, and

Mamichihikonomikoto: 'King Kārusu, despite your kind words, since our ancestors have exclusively served in the divine government since before the retirement of Kuniharutachinomikoto, making it our heavenly duty not to meddle in the politics of the phenomenal world, no matter what circumstances may arise, I am not one to become the sovereign of both government and religion and advance to the position of a king. We humbly beg you to cancel this matter alone. Anata are the descendant of Hanamorihikonomikoto, and since you possess the mission from your ancestors to surely govern this island country, please ascend to your former throne as quickly as possible, take Princess Yāchin as your principal consort, and reign over the world. Though unworthy, I, Mamichihikonomikoto, along with my son, will support your governance from the shadows as pillars of hemp, and we shall protect you so that you may rule the world in everlasting peace. In connection with this, there is something I wish to submit to the King. If you appoint Māruēsu, who is firm in faith and combines benevolence with directness, as prime minister, make Hōrusāsu vice prime minister, pardon numerous criminals, and heavily employ Shiyākarutan and Tororensu as well, the world will become all the more peaceful. This is the reason why Mamichihiko, putting his utmost sincerity into it, submits this for the sake of the country.'

Thus he confessed that he had not the slightest political ambition. King Kārusu was deeply moved by the pure and uncorrupted spirit of Mamichihikonomikoto, revered Mamichihikonomikoto as his spiritual guide, became himself a believer in Izumo Shinshū, set an example for the entire island, and the world came to be permanently governed as a wondrous divine nation under the protection of Izumo Shinshū, peaceful and serene like the famed pines of Takasago. Then, the King's suspicions regarding Princess Yāchin were completely cleared, and he now welcomed her to become his principal consort, appointed Princess Mariyasu as the head of the attendants, caused Teresutan, Hōrensu, and others to repent, employed them heavily as before, the world was governed in perpetuity, and all the people revelled in the joy of patting their bellies and stamping the ground.

Thereupon King Kārusu dispatched Māruēsu and Hōrusāsu to the capital of Sawara in Ryūkyū, laden with various rare tributes, to express their gratitude and allegiance before King Teruhiko.

King Teruhiko, touched by the sincerity of King Kārusu, crossed over to Taiwan for the first time together with Māruēsu and Hōrusāsu, was welcomed at Taian Castle, stayed there for a while, received the hospitality of mountains, fields, rivers, and seas, and furthermore worshipped at the sacred grounds of Mount Tamamo where Mamichihikonomikoto resided in tranquility, firmly grasped hands there, and Ryūkyū and Taiwan joined hands in partnership to serve in the divine work.

Through the mediation of Mamichihiko and King Teruhiko, Māruēsu took Princess Yachiyo as his bride, Hōrusāsu took Princess Teruyo as his bride, and the husband and wife lived harmoniously, serving Taian Castle, and their descendants prospered for a long time.

Eventually, between King Kārusu and Princess Yāchin, one boy and one girl, Yachihiko and Princess Yachi, were born. Also, between King Teruhiko and Princess Teruko, one boy and one girl, Terukurohiko and Princess Terukuro, were born. Through the match-making of Mamichihikonomikoto, King Teruhiko's eldest son Terukurohiko was married to Princess Yachi, and King Kārusu's eldest son Yachihiko was married to King Teruhiko's daughter Princess Terukuro, thereby newly establishing kinship ties.

Princess Mariyasu became the wife of Fuerusu, the younger brother of King Ākusu and eldest son of Ērisu, was praised as the flower of Taian Castle, bore the trust of high and low alike, assisted Princess Yāchin in her governance so that their descendants prospered enduringly, and the fact that Taiwan Island, which had piled confusion upon confusion until now, was completely transformed into a heavenly pure land, must be said to be largely due to the efforts of Fuerusu and Princess Mariyasu. Ah, Almighty Gods, let us be blessed and prosper in peace.

と宣示したるを、真道彦命は大に驚き、

『カールス王様、折角の御言葉なれ共、吾々は国治立尊の御退隠以前より、吾祖先は専ら神政に仕へ、現界の政治に容喙せざるを以て天職となし来りたるものなれば、如何なる事情あり共、吾は政教両面の主権者となり、王者の位地に進むべき者に非ず。何卒此事許りは御取消を願ひ奉ります。貴王は花森彦命の御裔、必ず此島国を治め給ふべき、祖先よりの使命おはしませば、一刻も早く元の王位に就き、ヤーチン姫を正妃として天下に君臨し玉へ。及ばず乍ら、真道彦命父子の者君が政事を蔭より麻柱奉り、天下泰平に世を治め玉ふべく守護し奉らむ。就ては王に進言したき事あり。信心堅固にして、仁慈と剛直とを兼ね備へたるマールエースを以て宰相となし、ホールサースをして副宰相となし、数多の罪人を赦し、シヤーカルタンやトロレンスをも重く用ゐ玉へば、天下は益々太平ならむ。これ真道彦が赤誠を籠めて、国家の為に進言する所以であります』

と毫も政治的野心なき事を告白した。カールス王は真道彦命の清廉潔白なる精神に感じ入り、真道彦命を導師と仰ぎ、自ら三五教の信者となり、全島に範を示し、天下は永久に三五教掩護の下に、枝もならさぬ高砂の芽出度き神国と治まることとなつた。さうしてヤーチン姫に対する王の疑は全く晴れ、茲に姫を容れて正妃となし、マリヤス姫を侍女の頭と定め、テールスタン、ホーレンス其他の人々をも悔い改めしめ、元の如く重用して、世は永久に治まり、万民鼓腹撃壌の楽みに酔うた。

茲にカールス王は琉球のサワラの都へ、マールエース、ホールサースを遣はし、いろいろの珍らしき貢物を齎せ、照彦王の前に感謝帰順の意を表することとなつた。

照彦王はカールス王の誠意に感じ、マールエース、ホールサースと共に始めて台湾島に渡り、泰安城に迎へられ、暫く此処に留まり、山野河海の饗応を受け、且つ真道彦命の鎮まり居ます玉藻山の聖場に参拝し、茲に固く手を握り、琉球、台湾相提携して、神業に奉仕する事となつた。

真道彦、照彦王の媒介に依りて、マールエースは八千代姫を娶り、ホールサースは照代姫を娶り、夫婦相睦びて泰安城に仕へ、其子孫は永く繁栄した。

末に至りてカールス王とヤーチン姫の間に八千彦、八千姫の一男一女が生れた。又照彦王と照子姫の間にも、照国彦、照国姫の一男一女が生れた。真道彦命の媒酌に依つて、照彦王の長子照国彦に八千姫を娶はせ、又カールス王の長子八千彦に照彦王の娘照国姫を娶はせ、茲に改めて親族関係を結ぶ事となつた。

マリヤス姫はアークス王の弟エーリスの長子フエールスの妻となり、泰安城の花と謳はれて、上下の信望を担ひ、ヤーチン姫の政事を輔けて子孫永く繁栄し、今迄混乱に混乱を重ねたる台湾島も全く天国浄土と化するに至つたのは、フエールス、マリヤス姫の力大に与つて功ありと言ふべしである。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
