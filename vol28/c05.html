Chapter 5: 難有 Delusion 惑 [805]

難有迷惑〔805〕

§ 1

The two kyōshiyu, Hitate and Tsukihoko, surrounded by numerous toritsugi shinto and others, and lighting numerous taimatsu, formed a chiyōda formation along the lakeshore, and went back enen toward the seichi of Tamamoyama. The kuwakuō of the taimatsu was reflected upon the komen, just as if hiriyō were swimming suichiu, making it a saukuwan view incomparable to anything else.

Mamichihikonomikoto followed the procession from behind the taimatsu, together with Yachinhime, Yurikohime, and Kierusutan, and arrived back at the seichi. However, because it was yonaka, and because they came from the saigo, not a single person noticed them.

The two, Hitate and Tsukihoko, advanced into the newly kenzau shinden, and were praying itsushinfuranly, saying, 'May the whereabouts of our father Mamichihikonomikoto be known even one day sooner.'

Mamichihikonomikoto, having pushed his way through the crowd and appeared in a leisurely manner, first of all turned toward the sacred altar, clapped his hands, and began to offer a norito. The two brothers, both by his appearance and his voice, and furthermore thinking that not a single person in Aonisokoro was one to come out before them and offer a norito, wondered in their hearts whether it was truly a divine manifestation or simply the return of their father, and waited for the norito to come to an end.

Mamichihikonomikoto finished his worship, bowed to everyone, grasped the hands of the brothers, and shedding tears, said,

'I am your father, who has long escaped this seichi. Not only have you well and safely lived to see this day, but that you have been able to once again restore the sacred site is entirely because the Great Deity of Aonisokoro has witnessed the sincerity of your faith and generously protected you. Ah, how thankful, how awe-inspiring!'

Choked with falling tears, and overwhelmed with joy, he collapsed flat upon that very spot.

The numerous toritsugi and shinto who heard this praised the divine virtue in unison, thanked the divine grace, and out of joy and leaping jubilation, without even knowing that night was turning into day, spent three days and three nights upon the banquet of naorai, which proved to be the grandest banquet since the establishment of the Tamamo seichi.

Mamichihikonomikoto had a beautiful mansion built for the two brothers Hitate and Tsukihoko, and there he came to nourish his aged body. Yet Mamichihikonomikoto, unbefitting his age, was full of divine virtue and vigorous in both spirit and flesh, with a youthful energy that surpassed even the robust.

The lake water of Tamamo was fifteen ri east-to-west and eight ri north-to-south, a considerably large body of water amidst the mountains. The sacred ground of Tamamo was situated almost at its center, and at the eastern edge there was a scenic mountainous area of small elevation and dense trees called Tenrei. There he caused Hitate to keep guard, newly built a shinden, and caused him to spread the path of the unity of government and religion. Then he made Yurikohime the miyuji, secured the eastern side of the seichi, while Mamichihikonomikoto stayed at the sacred ground of Tamamo, nourishing his old age while reverently attending upon Yachinhime, and serving in the divine work.

At the western edge of Tamamoko there was a sacred mountain called Tairei. There Tsukihoko was stationed, and he was causing Mariyasuhime to serve as kamitsukasa while attending upon him. The region east of Tamamoyama was called the seichi of Jittan, and the region to the west was praised as the seichi of Getsutan, and like the planet Oreon, the three seats stood side by side, serving in the divine work of Aonisokoro, and their awe-inspiring majesty thundering across the entire island of Taiwan, extending their power even as far as the capital of Taian at the foot of Niitakayama.

Tsukihoko, who served as the guardian deity of Tairei, continued a life of celibacy by the command of the deity. Mariyasuhime, without knowing when it began, felt a feeling of romantic yearning arise toward Tsukihoko, and unable to restrain herself in the slightest, neglected her divine work day and night without distinction, her heart being captured by Tsukihoko, and whenever an opportunity arose she would draw close to him and express the full measure of her thoughts in various ways. However, Tsukihoko was firm in his faith, well-kept the command of the deity, and furthermore knowing that Mariyasuhime was of noble standing as the younger sister of King Kārusu dwelling in the capital of Taian, he could neither harshly admonish her nor banish her, and paid her the utmost respect from the bottom of his heart. The romance of Mariyasuhime became increasingly fierce, until finally even the humblest toritsugi shinto firmly believed that a warm relationship had been forged between Tsukihoko and Mariyasuhime. Tsukihoko, caught between the divine command and the hime, spent his days suffering in anxiety day and night. Meanwhile, Hitate became husband and wife with Yurikohime, living harmoniously and participating in the divine work.

真道彦命は松明の後より、ヤーチン姫、ユリコ姫、キールスタンと共に一行に従ひ、聖地に帰り着いた。されど、夜中の事と云ひ、最後より来りし事とて、気の付く者は一人もなかりけり。

日楯、月鉾の二人は新に建造されたる神殿に進み入り、『父真道彦命の一日も早く行方の分りますよう』……と一心不乱に祈念をし居たり。

そこへ衆人を掻き分け、悠々として現はれ出でたる真道彦命は、先づ第一に神前に向つて拍手し祝詞を奏上し始めた。二人の兄弟は其姿と云ひ、声と云ひ、且つ……吾が前に出でて祝詞を奏上する者は、三五教に一人もなし、正しく神の顕現か、但は吾父の帰りませしにあらずや……と心中に且つ疑ひ、且つ歓び、祝詞の終るを待つて居た。

真道彦命は拝礼を了り、一同に目礼をなし、兄弟の手を握り、涙を流し乍ら、

『吾れは久しく此聖地を逃れ居たる汝が父なるぞ。よくマア無事に生き永らへしのみならず、再び聖場を復興し得たるは、全く汝等が信仰の真心を、三五教の大神御照覧遊ばし、厚く守らせ玉ふものならむ。あゝ有難や、辱なや』

と落涙に咽び、嬉しさ余つて、其場にハタと打倒れけり。

これを聞きたる数多の取次、信徒等は一斉に神徳を讃美し、神恩を感謝し、欣喜雀躍の余り、夜の明くるも知らずに、直会の宴に、日三日、夜三夜を費やしけるが、玉藻の聖地開設以来の大盛宴なりける。

真道彦命は日楯、月鉾二人の兄弟に、美はしき館を作られ、そこに老の身を養ふこととなりぬ。されど真道彦は年齢に似合はず、神徳、霊肉共に充実して若々しく、元気も亦壮者を凌ぐ許りなり。

玉藻の湖水は東西十五里、南北八里、山中にては可なり大なる湖水なり。玉藻山の霊地は殆ど其中心に位＠し、東の端に天嶺といふ小高き樹木密生せる景勝の山地があつた。そこに日楯をして守らしめ、神殿を新に造り、政教一致の道を布かしめた。さうしてユリコ姫を宮司とし、聖地の東方を固めしめ、真道彦命は玉藻山の霊場に在つて、老後を養ひつつヤーチン姫を奉じ、神業に奉仕して居た。

玉藻湖の西端には泰嶺と云ふ霊山があつた。そこには月鉾を配置し、マリヤス姫を神司として奉仕せしめつつありき。玉藻山以東を日潭の聖地と称し、以西を月潭の霊地と称へ、オレオン星の如く三座相並びて、三五教の神業に奉仕し、其稜威は台湾全島に轟き渡り、新高山の山麓なる泰安の都にまで、其勢力は轟いて居た。

泰嶺の鎮守として使へたる月鉾は神の命により独身生活を続け居たり。マリヤス姫は何時とはなしに月鉾に対し恋慕の念起り、矢も楯もたまらず、神業を閑却して昼夜の区別なく、月鉾に対し心を奪はれ、隙ある毎に寄り添ひて、種々と思ひの丈を述べ立つるのであつた。されど月鉾は信心堅固にして、神の命をよく守り、且つマリヤス姫は泰安の都にましますカールス王の妹たる尊き身の上なる事を知り居たれば、手厳しく戒むる事も得せず、又放逐する事も得ずして、心の限り尊敬を払つて居た。マリヤス姫の恋路は益々猛烈となり、遂には取次信徒等の端に至る迄、月鉾とマリヤス姫の間に温かき関係の結ばれある事を固く信じいたりけり。月鉾は神命と姫との板挟みとなつて、日夜苦慮しつつ其日を送り居たり。又日楯はユリコ姫と共に夫婦となり睦まじく神業に参加し居たり。

§ 2

Though he was old, Mamichihikonomikoto, whose vital spirit was still exuberant, had been predeceased by his wife, and continuing a life of solitude, he spent his remaining years in this sacred ground, while Yātinhime, having had her perilous life saved, fell in love with Mamichihikonomikoto unaware of when it began, and as a result of day and night anguish, her face became haggard, her body grew bony, and at last she took to a bed of severe illness.

The attendant Yurikohime was in the sacred ground of Tenrei, had become the wife of Hitate, and was already in the state of pregnancy. Therefore, she could not even nurse the severe illness of Yātinhime. Keelsutān devoted his whole strength faithfully to nursing the hime without distinction between day and night, yet the hime's illness grew heavier day by day.

Mamichihikonomikoto offered prayers at the shinsen morning and evening to save the hime's great illness, yet not even a little of its kauken appeared, and while breaking his heart to somehow restore the precious hime of Eerisu to her original body, he visited the sickbed. Keelsutān, greatly rejoiced at the coming of Mamichihikonomikoto, without even making proper greetings, flustered and rushed to the bedside of Yātinhime, brought his mouth close to her ear,

Keelsutān: 'Mamichihikonomikoto-sama, whom you have loved day after day, has come here today.'

At this voice whispered, the hime sat up abruptly, as if overjoyed, and facing Mamichihikonomikoto,

Yātinhime: 'Mamichihikonomikoto-sama, welcome and thank you so much for kindly visiting me. Now, even if I change my country at this very limit, there will be nothing left behind. Please, after my death, if you happen to think of me even as a dream, call my name if only for a single word. This is the wish of my whole life.'

Having finished speaking bashfully, she buried her face in the pillow. Mamichihikonomikoto, in a somewhat perplexed posture, hesitated for a moment, but resolved that toward this woman who so yearned for him, at her final hour, he should not treat her too heartlessly; if she were indeed a person destined to die shortly, it would be best to speak gentle words and let her leave this world with composure... and composing himself sternly,

Mamichihikonomikoto: 'Yātinhime-dono, your precious heart, even Mamichihikonomikoto who is not wood or stone, understands with satisfaction. Please forgive my ruthlessness toward you until now.'

He spoke decisively. Yātinhime was somehow cheered by these words, forgot her sickly body, sat up, rubbed her knees closer, gazed intently at the face of the mikoto, and while letting tears of gratitude fall patter-patter,

Yātinhime: 'Mamichihikonomikoto-sama, whom I have loved and yearned for day by day, if so, you from today and this very moment are the husband of Yātinhime; surely this is no joke, is it?'

When she pressed him thus,

Mamichihiko: 'Oh, how absurd! I too am a person who serves the gods, so I shall never tell a lie.'

Yātinhime: 'If that is so... you are my husband. Now that things have come to this, illness is of no account whatsoever.'

Her withered body suddenly regained spirit, the color of her face even became faintly red, and she immediately walked to the edge of the well, purified her body, changed her garments by herself, finished her grooming, and reappeared before Mamichihikonomikoto,

Yātinhime: 'Ah, my husband-sama, please come over to my private room. There are matters of detail I wish to speak to you about in various ways.'

She pulled his hand by force and plunged her figure into her private room.

Yātinhime accompanied Mamichihikonomikoto into her private room, sealed the surroundings airtight, the two sat down formally, and lowering her voice,

Yātinhime: 'Kārusu-sama, how have the circumstances of the capital of Taiyasu become? How has Sērusu-hime fared? Please let it leak out without hiding or concealing anything.'

Mamichihiko: 'This is yet another strange thing to hear. I am one who has lived in this sacred ground of Tamamo for generations of ancestors, opening the Izumo teaching, and the thought that I am King Kārusu of the capital of Taiyasu, so awe-inspiring, is a thing I never even dreamed of. Because of your long illness, you have brought an abnormality to your spirit, and perhaps you saw me as King Kārusu. I am by no means of such a noble status. Please examine me carefully and calmly.'

§ 3

Saying thus, she thrust her face right up close before the eyes of Yātinhime and displayed it. Yātinhime looked and saw this, yet smiled with a grin,

Yātinhime: 'Even though you are in the condition of travelling in disguise, there is no need for you to conceal it so. When I was thrown into Tankei and my life was in peril, you are doubtless none other than that benefactor of my life, Prince Kārusu, who went ahead and saved me. Now that matters have come to this, there is no need to keep it hidden any longer. I pray you, unbend yourself and speak the honest truth. No matter how much you may play at hiding it, from anywhere to everywhere, your august figure does not differ by a single hair's breadth of width—how could I ever think you are someone else?'

Mamichi: 'This is somewhat terribly vexing; do pray consider the matter well. Prince Kārusu has not yet reached thirty years of age, whereas I am an old dotard who has already set foot upon the slope of fifty. No matter how well we may be said to resemble each other, an old man and a robust youth—the color of our skin and the tone of our voices could never, ever be the same. Even though our figures may indeed closely resemble one another, there is an earthly distance and a difference of clouds and mud between us in the matter of nobility and lowliness. I beg you to calm your honorable heart and be so good as to pass a true and false judgment.'

Yātinhime: 'Your words are guarded to the very last; believing the proverb that women let out great matters through jealousy, it vexes me deeply that you, your honorable heart, should attempt to wrap up and conceal a secret that is already perfectly clear to the end. If you are going to conceal it to the end, then there is truly no help for it. If you are Mamichihiko no Mikoto, then Mamichihiko no Mikoto let it be. There is no mistaking you for the King. I beg of you, right here, to graciously hold the wedding ceremony anew.'

Mamichi: 'Ah, how troubling this is. I wonder how the Princess's doubts might be cleared away. Although they say it is a case of likeness between strangers, how awe-inspiring! To resemble Prince Kārusu so well... what an honor for Mamichihiko—or rather, what a vexation. Ah, how ever shall this difficult pass be successfully crossed?'

So saying, he hung his head and sighed.

Yātinhime: 'No matter how much you play at concealing it, there is no mistaking you for Prince Kārusu. Fearing those two villains Sārubōsu and Hōrokēsu, you hid your person on the banks of Tankei, changed your name to Mamichihiko no Mikoto, and engaged in the cowardly and unmanly behavior of deceiving this world. A marital bond permitted by your august father—even though we have not yet shared a pillow for a single night, it is a marital relationship permitted by father and parent—whom have you any cause to hesitate before? It is entirely natural that you should not be fond of Princess Sērusu, who in former times served in Jerusalem, who is the august remote descendant of the angel Hanamorihiko no Mikoto, and who was born from the wicked wombs of Takakuniwake and Tamatehime, having Sārubōsu and Hōrokēsu for a father and an uncle. Yet what on earth are you suffering from that you stole away from the capital of Taian and hid your person in such a place as that?... That aside, you are likewise the very person who saved my life; thanks to your grace in letting me leave the capital, as proof of our unexhausted bond, I have been saved by you whom I love and have come to this village—this too is the guidance of the God of Binding. From this day forth, joining our hearts as husband and wife, leading the believers of Anisankyō, and waiting for the right moment to make a sudden assault upon the capital of Taian, do you not have the intention to graciously inherit your father's work? If I but hear that much, even though I return to the hidden world just as I am, I am prepared to take your heroic heart as my strength and assist the divine work from the unseen realm. Whether in life or in death, the true heart of Yātinhime will never leave your august side; I pray you, do not think of it as in vain.'

Mamichi: 'I have two sons named Hidate and Tsukihoko. My wife has already departed this world, and now, just as you see, she has become a mortuary tablet and is enshrined in this divine hall; thus, no matter what, taking a wife is something I cannot do. However, thinking it heartless to reduce your honorable intentions to nothing, if I may be granted only the communion of husband and wife, I would ask you to let me participate in the divine work pretending to be husband and wife. Should any emergency arise concerning the august person of Prince Kārusu, without a moment's delay we shall lead many valiant warriors, ride into the capital of Taian, and humbly come to your rescue, and we are prepared to accomplish your desires. Please be at ease concerning this matter alone. I am by no means Prince Kārusu. I am Mamichihiko in all truth and genuineness.'

ヤーチン『何程御隠し遊ばしても、カールス王様に間違はありませぬ。あなたはサアルボースやホーロケース両人の悪者に恐れて、淡渓の畔に身を隠し、真道彦命と名を変へて、此世を偽る卑怯未練の御振舞、御父上の許し玉ひし夫婦の仲、未だ一夜も枕を交さね共、親と親との許し玉ひし夫婦の間柄、誰に遠慮が御座いませう。あなたは昔はエルサレムに仕へ玉ひし、天使花森彦命の御末孫、高国別や玉手姫の悪神の腹より生れ出でたる、サアルボースや、ホーロケースを父や叔父に持つ、セールス姫が御気に入らぬのは御尤もで御座います。さり乍ら何を苦んで、泰安の都を脱け出で、あの様な処に身を隠し遊ばしたので御座いますか……それはさうと、妾の生命を助け下さつたのもヤツパリあなた様、都を出でさせ玉ひし其御蔭、尽きぬ縁の証にて、恋しきあなたに助けられ、此里に参りましたのも、結ぶの神の引合せ、これより夫婦心を合せ、三五教の信徒を引つれ、時を見て泰安の都に攻め寄せ、父の業を御継ぎ遊ばす御所存は御座いませぬか。それさへ承はらば、妾は此儘帰幽致す共、あなたの雄々しき心を力として、幽界より御神業を御助け申す覚悟で御座います。生ても死しても、決してあなたの御側を離れぬヤーチン姫の真心、どうぞ仇に思召し下さいますな』

真道『私には日楯、月鉾と云ふ二人の息子が御座います。私の妻は既に此世を去り、今は此通り位牌となつて、此神殿に御祭りして御座いますれば、どうしても妻を持つことは、私として出来ませぬ。併し乍らあなたの御志を無にするも情なく存じますれば、夫婦の交はりのみは御許しを頂きまして、夫婦気取で御神業に参加させて下さいませ。カールス王様の御身の上に就て、変事あらば時を移さず、吾々は数多の強者を引具し、泰安の都に乗込んで御助け申し、貴女の望みを達し参らす覚悟で御座います。此事計りは御安心遊ばしませ。決して私はカールス王では御座いませぬ。正真正銘の真道彦で御座います』

§ 4

Yāchin: 'Ah, how vexing!'

So saying, she abruptly clings to Mamichihiko's right arm, weeping and pleading amidst tears, and writhes her body.

At this moment, Keelstan nonchalantly pushes open the sliding partition screen and enters, is startled upon seeing this state, and rushes out of this place headlong without even uttering a word. From this time onward, between Mamichihiko no Mikoto and Yāchin-hime, a pact of emotional accord was considered to have been concluded, and it came to be secretly transmitted among the general populace of Ananai-kyō. Yet Mamichihiko no Mikoto, pondering the future, did not oppose the Hime even by a single finger.

Thereafter, the Hime gradually recovered from her illness, became a beauty of exquisite appearance and countenance just as before, served morning and evening at the grand shrine of the sacred ground, and the majestic virtue of Kami shone and spread all the more broadly across the four quarters. Mamichihiko no Mikoto respected Yāchin-hime to the utmost, treated her like a master, and devoted utmost sincerity to her; whereupon Yāchin-hime also gradually realized that Mamichihiko no Mikoto was not King Karl, furthermore marveled at the steadfastness of the Mikoto's faith, opened their hearts to one another to participate in the divine work, and awaited the arrival of the time. Kamunagara Tamashii Sakihamase.

ヤーチン『エーもどかしや』

と言ひ乍ら、矢庭に真道彦の利腕にしがみつき、涙と共に泣き口説き、身をもだえ居る。

此時何気なく、隔の襖をおし開き、入り来るキールスタンは此態を見て驚き、物をも言はず一目散に此場を駆出したり。これより真道彦命とヤーチン姫の間には、情意投合の契約が結ばれたるものとして、窃に三五教の一般に伝へらるる事となりぬ。されど真道彦命は将来を慮り、姫に対して一指だにも支へざりける。

姫は其後病気追々快復して元の如く容色端麗なる美人となり、聖地の大神殿に朝夕奉仕して、神の威徳は益々四方に輝き亘りぬ。真道彦命は飽く迄もヤーチン姫を尊敬し、主人の如く待遇して、至誠を尽したるに、ヤーチン姫も漸くにして、真道彦命のカールス王に非ざりしことを悟り、且つ命の信仰の堅実なるに感歎し、互に胸襟を開きて神業に参加し、時の到るを待ちつつありける。惟神霊幸倍坐世。
