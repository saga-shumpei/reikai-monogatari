§ 1

Prompted by Haukoku's report, Saalbōse and Hōrokēsu appeared in this place with heavy footsteps, turning toward Seresuhime,

Saalbōse: 'Just now, according to Haukoku's report, I hear that you went to visit Yātinhime in her biyōki, suffered indescribable giyakutai, and sustained indelible bujiyoku. Is this truly so? Even Saal must prepare himself. Tell me the tenmatsu without concealing or hiding anything. Was the kega not great? How is your kibun?'

Even this sasuga akunin, drawn by the love of his child, with a pounding chest, questioned her in a flurry. Seresuhime, looking as though in great distress, at last lifted her face,

Seresuhime: 'Ochichiuhesama, Ojiuhesama, it is zannen...... Please, I beg you, slay this kataki.'

Saalbōse, shaking his head vertically,

Saalbōse: 'Ah, do not shinpai. You are my only daughter, irreplaceable in ten or on earth; even if one were to hold the whole of Taiwantau in one's hand, it could hardly be exchanged for your life. That hateful Yātinhime who struck my precious daughter, I shall make her realize it soon enough!'

Yielding to his love for his child, he believed without a single word of doubt the exaggerated defense of Seresuhime, whom he loved so dearly that she would not hurt even if crawled into his eye.

Seresesuhime: 'Yet even with that, what is most zannen is Mariyasuhime. Though she has always served me as a maid, she raised objections to every single matter, and today of all days, rather than showing pity for her master who suffered unreasonable violence and shame from Yātinhime, she instead returned with a triumphant and joyous air. To Haukoku's inquiry as well, she answered not a single word; in other words, she acts as though heaven is chastising the wickedness and lawlessness of me and my parent, though she says it not with her lips, it is vividly painted upon her countenance. Therefore, though I know that as a woman's body such violence is wrong, I transferred the persecution and shame I suffered from Yātinhime onto her and showed it to Haukoku. Pray do not scold me for committing violent outrages as a woman and being a hard-hearted daughter—the excess of my zannen curdled, and I struck Mariyasuhime.'

So crying out as if to shriek, she collapsed in tears, shaking her body and writhing. Hōrokēsu smiled a grin,

Hōrokēsu: 'Ah, I have been waiting for this. If there were no such thing as this, our plot would not be accomplished....... Seresuhime, well indeed that you were struck and returned. However much it was on account of illness, Yātinhime's violent outrages mean her qualifications as the consort of King Kārusu are utterly extinguished by this. Ah, Seresuhime, the giyakutai and bujiyoku you received today are rather your own fortune; how well indeed you were tormented....... Now Mariyasuhime, you, serving as Seresuhime's maid close by her person day and night, why did you not resist Yātinhime? For what purpose are you a maid? The divine punishment for your disloyalty and injustice has instantly come round before your very eyes; in this state, heaven has borrowed Seresuhime's hand to chastise you. You must not harbor resentment. Think of it as the whip of grace, and thankfully touch both hands to the ground before Seresuhime to apologize.'

Mariyasuhime, at length wiping away her face dripping with droplets of blood, abruptly raised her body, glared sharply at the four of them,

Mariyasuhime: 'I am she who became the maid of your vile daughter Seresuhime in order to monitor the day-and-night plots of you and your child, having received the command of the former King Ākusu, and crept into your house. I am the posthumous child of King Ākusu, Mariyasuhime...... So to speak, standing in a sibling relationship with King Kārusu, even though I served as a maid to such vile parents and child, it was because of deep circumstances. Heaven will never forever pardon the wickedness and lawlessness of you and your child. From this time forward, I shall go before the elder brother of King Kārusu, report your plots one by one, and announce them to the general public of the realm. That being so, if you from this moment repent and return to the path of goodness, I might let it pass as it is. What is your reply?'

Thus she began to reprove them with fierce intensity. Saarbōse and the other three men were dumbfounded by Princess Mariyas's words, hesitating for a moment as to what they should do, but having plotted such a conspiracy up to this point, how could they abandon their original intent? Glancing at one another with shared determination, each drew his long sword and slashed at Princess Mariyas from all directions—front, back, left, and right.

Princess Mariyas slipped through the blades above, below, left, and right, gripped Saarbōse firmly by the back of his collar, and hurled him into the decorative pond in the garden.

Mariyas: 'What insolence!'

Catching hold of Taals, who lunged at her, she once more seized him by the collar and cast him from the high hall down into the fountain below. The remaining two men, terrified by this spectacle, fled from the scene.

Princess Mariyas composedly stood before the mirror, arranged her hair, changed her garments, and singing the heavenly number-song, vanished somewhere, leaving behind her jewel-like form. Ah, what could have become of Princess Mariyas's whereabouts?

King Kāls, upon receiving the report that Princess Yāchin—whom he held constantly in his thoughts—was gravely ill and in critical condition, fell into a state of utter despair and disillusionment. Determined to throw himself into the deep ravines of Mount Niitaka to escape the agony of unrequited love, he secretly stole out of his mansion by night, reached the edge of the valley, and wandered hither and thither in search of a place to die.

Suddenly, a mass of firelight appeared in the distance across the valley, and from amidst it walked a beauty of peerless countenance, smiling gently as she traversed the mid-air. Drawing near to King Kāls with tears streaming down her cheeks, she extended her slender hand and grasped the hand of King Kāls. Instantly, King Kāls was thrown into a trance, nearly forgetting that he himself was in this very place. When he looked closely, her features, bone structure, and even the subtle nuances of her speech were identical to those of Princess Yāchin, whom he could never forget even in his dreams.

The woman, speaking in a quiet voice,

'My dearest King Kāls whom I love most,'

said she, her lovely eyes brimming with sorrowful tears. Instantly, King Kāls's heart grew radiant, and all his hitherto grief-stricken emotions vanished completely.

Kāls: 'Are you not Princess Yāchin? Ever since I heard that you had fallen gravely ill and that your life hung by a thread, I thought of casting myself into this mountain stream as a result of my heartbreak, awaiting your arrival in the netherworld. Yet how is it that your appearance has suddenly changed, and furthermore, that your body is so robust?'

he questioned her insistently with a look of suspicion. Princess Yāchin nodded with a gentle smile, took King Kāls's hand, and turned back toward the mansion.

As they approached the front of the mansion and looked, the figure of the princess who had accompanied him until now vanished like smoke, the moon shone brightly in the mid-heaven, and the cries of the evening cicadas could be heard noisily here and there. King Kāls suddenly came to his senses,

'How strange this is! When I was about to cast myself into the mountain stream in despair over my lost love, Princess Yāchin came and accompanied me all the way back here—was that a dream? Be that as it may, I shall enter the mansion to rest, and upon that foundation, firmly steel my resolve,'

he muttered to himself, stepping stealthily into the inner chambers.

The Princess Yāchin who had just appeared was actually a transformation of a malevolent golden fox spirit employed by Princess Seels. It was a witchcraft stratagem devised to halt King Kāls's sudden death and achieve her own objective.

King Kāls sat upright in the inner room, folding his hands, and having seen Princess Yāchin ride upon a cloud to cross the valley, or vanish like smoke in front of his own mansion, he became somewhat unnerved. Driven by feelings of suspicion, he spent the entire night wide awake without sleeping a wink.

As Princess Yāchin had completely recovered from her critical illness through the divine virtue of the Izusan-no-mitama, she accompanied Princess Yuriko and Kieldstan to visit King Kāls's mansion. At the outer gate, the gatekeeper Hall stood in solemn attendance.

Kieldstan: 'Ho there, Hall! Princess Yāchin has just arrived at the castle. Pray inform Lord Terens instantly without a moment's delay, and report this to His Majesty King Kāls before his presence!'

Hall: 'Very well, I understand. Please wait here at the gate for a moment.'

He proceeded deep into the interior with quick steps and reported the details to Terensu.

Terensu immediately attended before King Karusu,

Terensu: 'Yachinhime-sama has arrived at the castle just now.'

King Karusu: 'What, has Yachinhime come? Did she not come riding upon a cloud? Does she not vanish again at the entrance of the mansion? Go and investigate thoroughly.'

Terensu: 'This is a strange thing to hear. Yachinhime-sama is no eukuwaihenge; there is no reason for her to ride upon clouds or vanish like smoke. She has arrived riding in a magnificent palanquin, accompanied by Kiirusutan and Yurikohime-sama. Shall I show her in at once?'

King Karusu tilted his head for a while, and while letting out a sigh, said,

King Karusu: 'Be that as it may, show her to my presence.'

Terensu answered, 'Hai,' and went to welcome them at the gate himself, guiding the three before the King.

Yachinhime bowed respectfully with both hands and said,

Yachinhime: 'I am Yachinhime. Having been ill for a long time, I have caused you much worry, but thanks to your grace, I have fully recovered as you see. Please, set your mind at ease.'

King Karusu remained silent for some time, unable to take his eyes off Yachinhime's figure, lost in thought. It was the same suspicious feeling as when Yachinhime appeared the night before, identical in every detail. He remained silent and deep in thought, wondering if this was not another eukuwaihenge from the previous night.

Just then, the Prime Minister gods Saarubōsu and Hōrokēsu, with Sērusuhime in the lead and followed by many attendants, advanced toward the inner chamber of King Karusu's mansion without any reserve or hesitation. Seeing this situation, they turned a cold smile toward King Karusu and said,

Saarubōsu: 'Greetings, King Karusu-dono. We are delighted to see you in such good health as always. As you are aware, Yachinhime has gone mad, her body has withered away, and she is now a hideous sight of nothing but bones, with no flesh left to lose; it has become impossible for her to serve as the consort of King Karusu of Taiwan Island. We truly do not know whether to call this regrettable or a supreme disappointment, and we are at a loss for words in our sorrow. As you cannot govern the myriad affairs of state for even a day without a consort, I humbly offer my only daughter, Sērusuhime, as your consort. Though she is unworthy, I ask you for the sake of the nation to accept her and keep her for a long time to come.'

Thus, he pressured the King to make Sērusuhime his consort.

King Karusu, with a look as if wandering in a dense fog, gave no response and remained silent, comparing the faces of Yachinhime and Sērusuhime.

Kiirusutan knitted his brows in suspicion and said,

Kiirusutan: 'Saarubōsu-dono, in your words just now, you spoke as if Yachinhime-sama were unable to serve as Queen due to her illness, withered body, and hideous appearance. However, as you can see, Yachinhime-sama has already fully recovered from her illness, and she is in such good health and full of spirit. On what grounds do you say such things?'

Saarubōsu laughed with a grin and said,

Saarubōsu: 'The Yachinhime sitting here now is not the real one; she is entirely a fake, an eukuwaihenge. She is a reincarnation of a demon who rides on clouds, vanishes her body into white smoke, uses all sorts of magic, sneaks into the King's mansion, exhausts every art of sycophancy to steal the King's heart, and seeks to disturb the nation. Though I am unworthy, I have attained the heavenly eye through the divine power of the Great God of Brahmanism, so distinguishing the truth from the falsehood of Yachinhime is a trivial matter for me. Ah, indeed, indeed, it is truly amazing how many blind gods have gathered in this mansion. Hahaha!'

『只今此処に安坐し居るヤーチン姫は本者にあらず、これ全く妖怪変化の偽者で御座る。雲に乗り、或は身を白烟と消し、種々雑多の摩術を使ひ、王の館に忍び込み、巧言令色の限りを尽し、王の心胆を奪ひ、国内を攪乱せむとする悪魔の再来で厶る。某は不肖なれ共、バラモン教の大神の神力に依りて天眼通を得たれば、ヤーチン姫の真偽を分別する位は朝飯前の事で厶る。あゝサテモ　サテモ、当館内には盲神許りの……よくも集まつたもので厶るワイ。アツハヽヽヽ』

§ 4

Shaking his shoulders, he glared sharply at Keelsutan.

Keelsutan: 'What evidence do you have to utter such utterly outrageous and audacious words, Prime Minister?'

Saarubōsu: 'This is not a matter for blind gods like you to concern yourselves with. As for the wise King Kārusu, he surely knows the truth of it well enough; there is no need for us to offer any explanation. ...With all due respect, King Kārusu, what are your thoughts on this?'

King Kārusu sits in silence, crossing his arms, lowering his head, and lost in deep thought.

Yāchin-hime: 'Hey, Prime Minister, upon what evidence do you dare call me a specter and a shapeshifter? Explain yourself in detail. Depending on your answer, I, Yāchin-hime, will show no mercy.'

King Kārusu's uncle Eerisu raises his voice slightly, his face burning with fierce and terrifying anger,

'Ha! Saarubōsu, how dare you call my daughter Yāchin-hime a specter and a shapeshifter? There must be some deep plot behind this. State the circumstances in full detail.'

Hōrokēsu stands up and says,

'I address myself to Lord Eerisu. Your esteemed daughter, Lady Yāchin-hime, fell gravely ill some time ago; her body wasted away until she became a shadow of her former self, with no hope whatsoever of a full recovery. King Kārusu and all of us alike were consumed by unbearable grief and anxiety, desperately praying to the Brahmin gods day and night that she might be healed at the earliest possible moment. Then, the evil gods of the Sanzenkyō suddenly came and devoured the princess's flesh, taking her place and impersonating Yāchin-hime, appearing here in this grand and haughty manner. Even to the eyes of you, her own father, the truth has not yet become clear; she is a wicked deity who has disguised herself so thoroughly that she is by no means a simple or trivial creature whose true form can be easily exposed. I trust that the truth of this matter is already well known to His Majesty King Kārusu. Since she is, so to speak, the mortal enemy of your daughter Yāchin-hime, I humbly request that you execute her with your own hands right here and now. Should you harbor the slightest doubt, unworthy though I am, I, Hōrokēsu, shall chastise her and show her to you right in this very place.'

Yāchin-hime: 'Now see here, Hōrokēsu, what on earth are you talking about? Have you lost your mind? Look closely and examine my face.'

Hōrokēsu: 'King Kārusu, what are Your Majesty's thoughts?'

King Kārusu: 'This Yāchin-hime is utterly incomprehensible. She is the very same mysterious woman who rode upon the clouds last night, appeared before me, and vanished once more right in front of the mansion, without a single hair's breadth of difference. I have no other choice but to see her precisely as a specter and a shapeshifter.'

Hearing the king's words, both Saarubōsu and Hōrokēsu suddenly breathe heavily with intense agitation,

Saarubōsu: 'A monarch's word has no second tongue. You, Yāchin-hime, are definitively a specter and a shapeshifter. In accordance with the laws of this land, we shall place Yāchin-hime in a wicker basket, cast her down into the deep ravines of Mount Niitaka, and eradicate the very root of this calamity. ...Well now, Lord Eerisu, do you still harbor doubts even after this?'

He glared fiercely at him. Eerisu tilts his head in silence for a moment, but soon turns toward the king and asks, gasping for breath,

'King Kārusu, do you positively conclude that Yāchin-hime is without a doubt a specter and a shapeshifter?'

King Kārusu shakes his head from side to side and replies,

'No, no, I do not definitively conclude that she is a specter and a shapeshifter. I merely find it strange that she bears not the slightest particle of difference in appearance and all else from the mysterious woman I encountered last night. Whether she is truly a specter and a shapeshifter, or whether she is Yāchin-hime, remains yet unproven.'

Turning to the brothers Saarubōsu and Hōrokēsu, Eerisu says,

'Lord Prime Minister, judging by the king's words just now, has he not yet acknowledged her as a definite specter and a shapeshifter? And yet, to rashly treat Yāchin-hime as a shapeshifter and cast her away into a mountain torrent is an act of utter ruthlessness. I beg of you to give this matter one more thorough deliberation.'

原文（近代文語）

と肩を揺り、パツとキールスタンを睨め付けた。

キールスタン『益々以て不届き至極の宰相の言葉、何を証拠に左様の事を仰せらるるか』

サアルボース『汝等如き盲神の関知する所ではない。賢明なるカールス王に於ては、よくも其真偽を御存じの筈、吾々が弁明するの必要は御座らぬ。……恐れ乍らカールス王様、如何思召しまするや』

カールス王は黙然として腕をこまねき、俯むいて思案に暮れて居る。

ヤーチン姫『アイヤ宰相殿、妾を妖怪変化とは、何を証拠に仰せらるるか。詳細に弁明なされよ。返答次第に依つては、ヤーチン姫容赦は致しませぬぞ』

カールス王の叔父エーリスは稍言葉を荒らげ憤怒の面色物凄く、

『ヤア、サアルボース、汝は無礼千万にも吾娘ヤーチン姫を妖怪変化と申すは何故ぞ。これには深き企みのある事ならむ。詳さに事情を申述べよ』

ホーロケースは立あがりて、

『エーリス殿に申上げます。貴方の御娘、ヤーチン姫様は先つ頃重病に罹らせられ、身体は痩衰へ、見る影もなき御姿と御成り遊ばされ、到底御全快の見込みもなく、カールス王様を始め、吾々一同憂苦の情に堪へず、如何にもして一刻も早く御全快遊ばす様と、バラモン神に祈願を籠め居りました所、三五教の邪神忽ち来つて姫の肉体を喰ひ、己代つてヤーチン姫と成りすまし、斯の如く堂々として、此処に姿を現はして居りまする。現在御父上なる貴方の御目にさへも、其真偽が判明せないまで、よく化込んだ枉神、到底一通りや二通りでは、正体を現はす様なチヨロコイ奴では御座らぬ。此真偽はカールス王様の既に御承知の事と存じまする。言はば貴方の御娘ヤーチン姫の仇敵で御座いますれば、此場で御手討に遊ばされたう存じます。万一御疑ひとあらば、憚り乍ら此ホーロケースが此場に於て退治し御目にかけませう』

ヤーチン姫『コレコレ、ホーロケース、そなたは何を言ふのだ。気が違うたか。トツクリと妾が顔を調べて見よ』

ホーロケース『カールス王様、貴方の御考へは如何で御座いまする』

カールス王『如何にも合点の往かぬヤーチン姫、昨夜雲に乗り、吾前に現はれ、再び館の前にて消え失せたる不思議の女に寸分違はぬ此女。吾は正しく妖怪変化と見るより外に手段はない』

サアルボース、ホーロケースの両人は王の言葉を聞くより、俄に鼻息荒く、

サアルボース『王者の言葉に二言なし。汝ヤーチン姫、妖怪変化にきはまつたり。此国の掟に従ひ、ヤーチン姫を籠に乗せ、新高山の淡渓に投げ棄て、災の根を絶たむ。……如何にエーリス殿、これでも猶御疑ひあるや』

と睨めつけた。エーリスは暫く首を傾けて居たが、頓て王に向ひ、

『カールス王よ、ヤーチン姫を妖怪変化に相違なしと断定さるるや』

と息を喘ませ、問ひつめた。カールス王は首を左右に振り、

『否々吾は決して妖怪変化と断定はせない。只訝かしき昨夜出会ひたる女に、容貌其他寸毫の差なきを不思議と思ふのみ。果して妖怪変化なりや、ヤーチン姫なりや、これは未だ判明せず』

エーリスはサアルボース兄弟に向ひ、

『宰相殿、今の王の御言葉に依れば、未だ的確なる妖怪変化と認め玉はざるに非ざるか。然るに軽々しくヤーチン姫を妖怪変化として、渓流に棄つるは没義道で御座らう。今一息御熟考を願ひませう』

§ 5

Hōrokēsu: 'Be silent.'

Hōrokēsu: 'Utilizing your position as the uncle of King Kārusu, your conduct in attempting to block our loyal counsel—no matter how blinded you are by parental affection—in making an eukuwaihenge the consort of King Kārusu and attempting to throw the kokuka into disorder is the height of disloyalty and unrighteousness; hold your peace.'

Sārubōsu: 'Once it has been officially declared from the mouth of the monarch that she is an eukuwaihenge, it must not be retracted again.'

Sārubōsu: 'Moreover, as for being a dark monarch who hesitates over the truth or falsehood regarding the eukuwaihenge currently present before our eyes, you have zero qualifications as a monarch.'

Sārubōsu: 'Promptly abdicate, or else accept our words, cast the eukuwaihenge who has transformed into Yachinhime into Tankei, and if you receive Sērusuhime and grace her as your consort, that we shall see the auspicious sign of the upper and lower in accord and peace throughout the realm is clearer than beholding fire; I await your reply.'

With no way out from this inescapable wedge driven home, King Kārusu also had no words in return, became depressed and turned pale, and concealed his figure in the inner chambers together with two or three retainers.

The two men, Sārubōsu and Hōrokēsu, commanded numerous retainers, bound Yachinhime with her hands tied behind her back, placed her in a crude hanging basket, and—even though her father Eerisu to begin with, as well as Kīrusutan and Yurikohime, tried to stop them—pushing and brushing them aside, they marched onward toward Tankei while chanting a song of triumph.

ホーロケースは言下に、

『お黙りなさい。カールス王の叔父たるの地位を利用して、吾等が忠言を遮らむとする貴神の振舞、如何に親子の愛情に眼眩めばとて、妖怪変化を以てカールス王の妃となし、国家を紊乱せむとするは不忠不義の至りで御座らう。御控へめされ』

サアルボースは又もや立上つて、

『一旦王者の口より妖怪変化ならむと宣示されたる以上は、再撤回す可からず。且又現在目前に居る妖怪に対し、真偽に迷ふが如き暗君なれば、王としての資格は絶無なり。速かに退位さるるか、さなくば吾言葉を容れ、ヤーチン姫と変じたる妖怪を淡渓に捨て、セールス姫を容れて妃となし玉はば、上下一致、天下泰平の祥兆を見む事火を睹るより明かならむ、返答承はらむ』

のつぴきさせぬ釘鎹にカールス王も返す言葉なく、うつうつとして顔色青ざめ、二三の従臣と共に奥の間に姿を隠した。サアルボース、ホーロケースの二人は数多の従臣に命じ、ヤーチン姫を高手小手に縛め、粗末な吊籠に入れ、父のエーリス始め、キールスタン、ユリコ姫の止むるのも聞かばこそ、突きのけ撥ねのけ、凱歌を奏しつつ淡渓指して進み行く。
