Chapter 6: Mountain 下り [1175]

山下り〔1175〕

§ 1

Harukuniwake, Tamakuniwake, and their party are gathered before a sunny shrine, listening to Yoru's account.

Harukuniwake: 'Yoru-sama, it seems General Ranch is setting up a camp at Ukigahara with the intention of fighting a war of attrition. Why on earth are they not advancing in full force to cross Kajikatauge?'

'Not at all, General Ranch has fallen into a predicament where his advance and retreat are both deadlocked, you see. As long as you honorable ones remain here like this, there is no way to break through this pass. Therefore, having no choice, he set up camp at Ukigahara and dispatched a hurried messenger to the capital of Haruna with this report: "The enemy's army of several hundred thousand has rushed upon us; to advance blindly is to labor without merit. For a while we shall pitch camp at Ukigahara and plan to make the enemy surrender naturally." Yet, seeing that there is no prospect of ultimate victory, General Oniharuwake, accompanied by General Kumehiko, claims it is to offer explanations to Great Lord ōkuninushi, but in truth they are to cross the land of Fusa and advance toward the Golden Mountain of Jerusalem.'

'What? Advance to the Golden Mountain, you say? That is news to me indeed. Tamakuniwake-sama, we cannot simply remain here like this. Our mission is to guide and reconcile the Great Lord ōkuninushi of the land of Haruna, but since Terukuniwake and Golden Princess have already gone forth as the vanguard, you must hold this Kajikatauge and block the enemy's assault. We shall break through the enemy's encirclement and head to the rescue of Jerusalem. Now, Ikozakohime-sama, what do you think?'

'Indeed so, this is by no means an ordinary problem, let us ask the God for guidance. You must decide according to that divine oracle.'

'Very well, you have thought of a splendid place. Then, please become the priestess from now on. I shall perform the shinji (divine divination).'

'Since I have stopped being a medium for a while, I am not sure how it will go, but anyhow let us ask the Gods.'

So saying, Ikozakohime spreads a straw raincoat upon the earth, sits squarely upon it, closes her eyes, joins both hands, and lets her spirit wander into the cosmos. After a while, there appears to be a divine spirit response, and Ikozakohime's face begins to shine ever more radiantly. Harukuniwake and the others all start with a gasp, bow their heads low, and are filled with awe. Ikozakohime breaks the silence and says,

'I am the spirit of Kuniteruhime. You, the divine officials Harukuniwake and Tamakuniwake, seem to be hesitating regarding your course of action concerning the fact that Oniharuwake, a Brahmin, is leading an army to advance toward the Golden Mountain. Now, embodying the divine heart of the Great God Kami-Susanoo, I shall proclaim and transmit everything unto you. From here on, Harukuniwake, together with Mankō, Harukō, Gosankō, Matsukō, and Ryūkō, shall break through General Ranch's camp, cross Persia, and advance to the Golden Mountain. Oniharuwake's army has not yet gone far; if we pursue them now, it is hard to tell whether we might perhaps intercept and stop them midway. Furthermore, Tamakuniwake shall build the dwelling of the Land's Ancestor Great God and Toyokunihime-no-Mikoto right here, establish the gardens of teachings side by side, and guard Kajikatauge, which must serve as the throat of the Saien no Yakata (Mansion of the Purified Garden). Come, starting tomorrow, fell the trees of the forest, level the earth, and engage in the construction. Since carpenters, plasterers, assistants, stonemasons, and others are already on their way hither from the Saien no Yakata, Tamakuniwake shall remain here and nurse your eyes to health. Moreover, Ikozakohime, together with Imokozahime, shall remain and tend to her husband until his eye disease is completely cured—such is the divine proclamation of the Great God. I have nothing more to proclaim or transmit to you with this, farewell.'

Leaving these words behind, the divine spirit returns. Matsu, Ryū, and the others, who had surrendered from the Brahmin teachings, along with Iru, Iku, Sāru, Yoru, Teru, and Haru, are struck by the majestic and awe-inspiring divine possession of Ikozakohime, straighten their collars, hold their breath, and cower in fear.

'According to the divine oracle just given, we must somehow intercept and stop Oniharuwake's army. We shall set out without a moment's delay from now on. Pray, do you act according to the divine oracle, take on the role of the commandant here, and perform the duty of guarding the throat, even though you will be busy with the construction for a while. First of all, smashing General Ranch's camp to smithereens and displaying divine power is our joy, and we go forth with high spirits. Tamakuniwake-sama, do take good care of yourself. Ikozakohime-sama, Imokozahime-sama, and all the rest, farewell then.'

'He who formed and solidified this world, The true Kami has descended to earth, Sweeping away and purifying with His breath The cloud and mist blocking the four quarters, Distinguishing and establishing zenshinjiyashin, Reaching down to aohitogusa, birds and beasts, And even down to the grasses and trees, How noble is His saving grace! I am a joyful sendenshi, Obeying the imperial edict of Kamususanōnōokami, Advancing forward upon the seito of magakami, Going up and down the steep slope of Kajikatauge, Chasing away and scattering the numerous attacking enemies, Encountering in an unexpected place My younger brother for whom I have pined day by day, My yūki increased a hundredfold compared to usual, At last rushing forward here against the attacking forces of the Brahmans, The time has truly come now For Ananai-kyō to manifest Its unique daishintoku! The party of the Sendenshi, peerless throughout the past and present, and of Harukuniwake, praised as the flowers of Tenchi, shall make their fine name resound for banse. Suddenly a fall right before your eyes. In whatever you do or perform, stumbling, you will likely not be able to reach the capital of victory. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara.
