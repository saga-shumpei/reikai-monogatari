Chapter 4: 滝の下 [1173]

滝の下〔1173〕

§ 1

The moonlight shining in the early winter sky hangs over the waterfall resembling a folding screen falling through the valley of the Kajikagaha, scattering spray like jewels, and in that spray the moon dwells one by one, looking as though stars are flying, and this place is about three chō downstream from the shrine's forest. Drowning out the sound of the waterfall, there were three men laughing and making merry in loud voices.

Hey, Iku-nisaru, isn't tonight a strange night? Matsukō-sama met the elder brother, surrendered to Ananai-kyō from the very root of roots, and got caught up together with me, but yet when you think about it, it is a dangerous thing. Even if you say Ananai-kyō has strong divine power, Tamakuniwake and Harukuniwake's party totals ten people or less. Having their advance blocked by the numerous forces led by General Ranchu, and staying retreated near the shrine's forest forever like a rat in a bag, provisions won't last long, and if they change their plan this time and come climbing up under General Ranchu's command as a comeback, that truly will be terrible. We surely will be hoisted onto the spear's tip, being told we surrendered to the enemy. If we surrender to Ananai-kyō, we will be glared at by the Brahmanism. If we go toward Brahmanism, we will probably be attacked by Ananai-kyō; we can't go anywhere, we can't run away anywhere, we've fallen into a grand dilemma. What are you fellows thinking?

Inside this belly of Iku-sama, wondrous plans and eccentric strategies are stored away in abundance, so you shouldn't be so pessimistic. If you surely surrender to Ananai-kyō, a path to life will open up. This Kajika Pass is the place where the victory or defeat of friend and foe is decided, but nevertheless, since this throat has been gripped by Ananai-kyō, even if General Ranchu climbs up leading a million soldiers, he cannot fight all at once like that, and being defeated from the narrow mouth like falling dominoes is only natural. Therefore, in order to preserve the safety of the body and the soundness of the spirit, I smoothly surrendered to Ananai-kyō. Are you still hesitating? You are a fellow lacking in faith. You're like a bath lid, needed when it's there, but something you enter when you don't need it.

Even so, a person must think of the future as well, now that it has come to this, even if one flurries and is dismayed, it is after the feast and there's nothing to be done about it.

At any rate, since he is a propagandist of such broad and magnanimous tolerance who tells us, without any suspicion, to go and play around over there, surely he has a conviction. I don't know how much more reassuring it is to leave things to the gods without saying such foolish things anymore. Look at this waterfall water, isn't it truly beautiful? To wash and purify the stains of the heart with this pure water shining pure white, to be illuminated by the moonlight, to roam in the realm of nature, and for three close friends to be able to be like this even for half an hour is entirely the precious grace of the gods. Ah, how thankful, how thankful. If it were Brahmanism, how would they ever let those who have surrendered now take free actions? Even seeing this, can you not tell the greatness or smallness of the teachings? Above all, trying to govern the world by means of blades is truly ten thousand times dangerous. As for me, I've grown sick of even hearing the 'ba' of Brahman. Anything with a 'ba' attached has nothing decent. Old hags, hags, monsters, gambling, banters, baris, and idiots—things like that, nothing but filthy things. Since only fellows with flaws are gathered together, as for me too, ever since I entered Brahmanism, I was greatly repudiated by worldly fellows and friends. Now, fellows other than Brahmanism say things like there is a curse from the god of Saru, and they won't associate with me anymore.

It's not that people stopped associating with you after you entered Brahmanism; you are a drunkard, a gambler, a hag-bothering idiot, so you were ostracized by worldly fellows, had nowhere to go, and entered Brahmanism, right? Anyway, fellows who enter Brahmanism are all at a dead end. Since you reach a dead end and become useless, you enter it while knowing it's pointless, right?

Iku: 'Well, to say the least, that is not entirely without a grain of truth. But still, look at Iru, for instance; he is hated by the world like a centipede, has no one to turn to, and has sunk into Brahmanism, so it would be better for you not to criticize people with too loud a voice. Iru: 'Even I still have a use in this world. I am an indispensable article. That is why I am named Iru. Iru for a bow, iru for a bath, and iru for the very liver for people's sake. Iru blisters on the soles of the feet... such a precious older brother am I. Iku: 'You speak so grandly, but do you even have a wife? In fact, I have never even heard a whisper of such a thing from the very beginning.'

Iru: 'I say iru because a wife is needed at home. They say a bride iru and a bridegroom iru, so they are indispensable within a single household. However, I am still young, so I have roughly two dozen candidates for a wife, but since the god called Kinkatsukane has not yet given a decision, I am awaiting it.'

Iku: 'If you are awaiting it, you must be receiving two-thirds of your salary. How about distributing a little bit to Saru as well?'

Iru: 'Since it is Lord Kinkatsukane after all, he possesses plenty of gold. As for me, it is a nuisance to receive it little by little all at once, so I have deposited it in the warehouse of heaven to be received as a lump sum. If you want it, you might as well work of your own accord and take it with all your might; I will let you take as much as iru.'

As they were thus talking, two masked men appeared slowly from the shade of the trees, leaning on hand-spears as canes. The one with the black hood let out a thunderous shout of 'Hey!', and when the three involuntarily turned their gazes toward the voice, two big men were standing there.

Black Hood: 'Hey, I do not know whose fellows you are, but while Lord Iru is enjoying a pleasant night out, what is the meaning of shouting "Hey"? Who on earth are you? You are most likely inspectors of the Omoto religion. I am nothing less than a henchman of Lord Daijaiten. We are the three venerable ones—Iru, Iku, and Saru—guarding Kiyoharu Mountain. Now, we are going to tie both of you up and take you before General Ranchi, so prepare yourselves.'

Alice: 'Listening to the three of you talking in the shade of the trees just now, you are already rebels who have surrendered to the Omoto religion. To make such excuses and conversely call us the catchpolls of Omoto—you are indeed fellows well-versed in worldly wisdom. Now, if matters have come to this, we will no longer show leniency. We are General Ranchi's inspectors, Alice and Sam. My martial prowess is known throughout the world, is it not? Heroes capable of matching a thousand riders—that is what Alice and Sam are. Now, prepare yourselves.'

Saru: 'Ha ha ha ha! Talk about boasting! You fellows Alice and Sam, if you keep blabbering and dawdling, shall I fire off a living kototama? Since things have already come to this, there is no need to hide it; we three are the three crows of the Omoto propaganda envoy, Jinodewake. If you keep dawdling, we will not show you our hand.'

Iku: 'What a sudden commotion you have raised. And are the three of you the only ones here? Without someone backing you up, there is no way you could speak such strong words from your mouths. Now, explain those circumstances clearly.'

Iru: 'Alice is our great backer. Since Iru is willing to give us reinforcements no matter how many, we are all right. We are not lords commanded by a weak general like you. Now, if you can capture us brilliantly, just try capturing us. The moment I blew one signaling whistle, millions of lions would roar and appear upon this scene, biting and crushing weak warriors like you into fine dust, and dyeing the mountain stream crimson. Now, if you can even lay a single finger on us three, just try doing it.'
