§ 1

『戦陣にあつてラツパのみとはこいつは面白い、ラツパの一声で三軍を自由自在に動かすのだからなア、武道の達人が葡萄酒を呑むのは合つたり叶つたりだ』

と云ひながら、ハルがキルクを抜いた酒瓶を一ダースばかりつづけざまに呑み干して仕舞つた。忽ちヨルは足を失ひヨロヨロとしながら二人の前にドスンと倒れ、

『あゝ、そこらがなンとはなしにポーとして来た。これだからポーとワインと云ふのだなア、何と酒と云ふものは怪体な代物だナ、俺はもう軍人が嫌になつた。オイ、テル、ハル、このヨルさま等がヨルに紛れて此場をテル、そしてハルバルと、斎苑の館へ帰順と参らうぢやないか、エーン何だか此頃は俺も実の処はバラモン教がいやになつた。三五教の三人や四人の宣伝使に言霊を打ち出され人馬諸共逃げ散るとは実に情なくなつて来た。これを思へば、実に三五教の神様は天のミロク様、バラモン教の神様は大蛇の乾児様位に違ひないよ、こンな事をして居ると終には地獄の釜炙ぢや。テル、ハル貴様も同意見だらう』

§ 4

Yoru: 'That is something I cannot state for sure, for people's minds are not to be known, so I cannot say a careless thing. Yoru-sama, you are intending to capture the two of us and drag us before General Katahiko to make a display of your achievement, but because your body has fallen out of your control from partaking in mean sake, you are saying such things to win our favor. We are not Teru-sama and Haru-sama who would be hoodwinked by such a thing.'

Teru: 'If you suspect me so, it cannot be helped. However, I am certainly—though I am drunk, just as they say a drinker's true nature never changes—not telling a lie. If I wanted to capture you two, it would be the easiest thing; if I merely blew the signal whistle I carry inside my bosom, dozens of people would come out to this very spot.'

Haru: 'When you put it that way, you have blown your true feelings after all, haven't you? Good, good, Yoru is also indeed a warrior of our Teru faction. With this, the three of us are gathered. Heaven, Earth, and Man; Sun, Earth, and Moon; Spirit, Power, and Body—the gods of the three-fold unity. The origin of the three-person world, splendid, splendid! Could there be such splendour anywhere else in the world? What do you say, to celebrate the formation of the triangular alliance, shall we set the levee-cutting into motion?'

Yoru: 'Wait a moment for that. If we make a racket in a place like this and get discovered, it will be terrible. Hey, while it is still now, let's load all the sake here onto our backs, and under the cover of night, head over to the grove of the shrine. If we dawdle, it will be terrible.'

Teru: 'Judging from Teru's eyes, Yoru-sama, you, with those feet of yours, can you walk that mountain path? It is a dangerous thing, you know.'

Yoru: 'Would it not matter even if I cannot move? As long as the feet of the two of you are sound, it will be fine to put me in a mountain palanquin and carry me along. Fortunately, there are four or five mountain palanquin here, so you will do something or other with one of these and put me on it, won't you?'

Haru: 'How sweet are the things you say! Yet, escaping is limited to this very night. There is no help for it, hey, Teru-sama, let us carry Yoru-sama and climb up the mountain path of the night, shall we not? If we go to Kajika Pass, it is already a safe zone, you see.'

Here the three men steal away a single palanquin, place Yoru upon it, and while both Teru and Haru murmur amusing and comical songs in low voices, they stealthily escape from the encampment of Ukigahara, and proceed heading toward the grove of the shrine at Kajika Pass. The moon breaks through the curtain of dark clouds, looking down and smiling cheerfully upon the heads of the three men.
