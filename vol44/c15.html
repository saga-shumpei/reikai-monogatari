§ 1

Harukuniwake and his party left the forest of Yamaguchi behind, quickened their pace, and advanced about twenty ri to the south. Twenty ri, however, is an extremely short distance. When one speaks of a ri, it is equivalent to about two hundred ken in our country, which amounts precisely to a little over three chō.

Harukuniwake descended to a narrow stream by the roadside to quench his thirst, gazed at the clouds drifting across the sky, and rested his breath for a while. About twenty ken away, amid the rice paddies, a dense forest stood majestically, occupying the vast wilderness and looking as if it owned the place. From within that forest, a clamorous woman's voice was heard. Mankō caught it early on and spoke.

Mankō: 'Hey, Sensei, a strange and bizarre melodrama seems to be playing out in that forest. What do you say? Shall we spend the night in that forest as a side trip for melodrama sightseeing? A forest hotel is quite a refined thing, too. Since it was a forest hotel yesterday and will be the same again tonight, it will probably be very convenient to jot down in our diary. Listen to that, just listen! A woman's voice like monkeys being attacked—there must be some secret lurking behind this. At any rate, shall we go and make an on-site exploration?'

Harukuniwake spoke.

Harukuniwake: 'I would like to go a bit further, but after hearing that voice, as a propaganda emissary, I cannot simply overlook it and pass by. Since it is a terribly overgrown forest, let us slip in quietly and see what is going on.'

Gusankō spoke.

Gusankō: 'Hey Mankō, it is that spooky ghost business again. Don't lose your nerve.'

Mankō: 'Nonsense, as if I would be scared of a ghost in broad daylight and give up! It doesn't matter whether it is a ghost or a thief, does it? Most likely, some thief has dragged a traveler in, stripped off their clothing, and is forcibly pinning down a reluctant woman while holding a Nenbutsu lecture.'

Gusankō: 'What do you mean by a Nenbutsu lecture? Aren't you saying something strange? Hahaha, since a ghost is appearing, they must be chanting the Nenbutsu so it can attain Buddhahood. If that were the case, wouldn't you hear the voices of Buddhist hymns from the very beginning? It is a piercing, high-pitched screeching sound. At first hearing, it sounds somewhat like kitsune-sama, and somewhat like monkeys, yet it also sounds like a woman. It is some kind of uncanny creature. Gusankō thinks there is ample value in investigating it, doesn't he, Sensei?'

Harukuniwake: 'Hmm, at any rate, let us go and see. However, since I am imposing a gag order, until the command is given, no matter what happens, you are not allowed to make a sound.'

Mankō: 'Understood. Can't we even whisper, Mankō is a bit troubled by that.'

Harukuniwake: 'Hmm, of course.'

Mankō: 'Mankōwake imposes a gag order on Gusankō and Ryūkō. You must strictly keep your silence.'

Gusankō: 'Hahaha, you are immediately peddling it secondhand. Even if you retail things like that, we have no intention of buying. Although a price control decree has been issued, small merchants ignore it, greedily amass exorbitant profits, and act wildly and aggressively, making it quite a headache for statesmen and for this Gusankō-sama as well, ahahaha.'

Harukuniwake spoke.

Harukuniwake: 'Now let us go. Silence.'

With this strict command, he parted the grassy field and advanced.

When they looked, within the densely wooded forest, five or six rough men had captured a beauty, closing in upon her from all four directions and beginning to beat her. Harukuniwake calmly watched this scene from the leafy shade of the trees. Mankō's heart leaped, his mouth flew open, he spread both hands and crouched halfway down, taking a posture as if he were about to leap out at any moment, swallowing his saliva with a sense of frustration, and steeling himself to beat down the assailants one by one and rescue the tormented woman the very moment Harukuniwake gave the order. Gusankō and Ryūkō also watched anxiously and with a sense of impatience. Harukuniwake and Matsuhiko watched with a detached, smiling expression, looking quite amused.
