§ 1

With the sound of the conch shell blown with all his might by Kiudaukoji, Beru, Heru, and Kerina vanished from this place like smoke. The figure of Kiudaukoji also disappeared before one knew it, and the faint sound of the conch could be heard in the distance. Takahime turned toward the two, Sharu and Rokuzau,

Takahime: 'Hey, you two, Sharu and Rokuzau, isn't Takahime's divine power truly magnificent? Because you were too headstrong, Giritenjiyōsama lost his patience and gave just a single glance, and at that very moment, that conch blower, both Beru and Heru, and the high-collar woman as well, all vanished into thin air like smoke without a trace... such is the tragic state of affairs. Seeing this, repent. And why has Roku-san stayed silent like a mute or something, and where on earth have you been hiding until now?'

Rokuzau: 'Heh, the conch shell was so terrifying that I hid away inside the privy for a short while. Quite a formidable fellow came along, indeed. Even so, as for that fellow Beru, it is truly gratifying that he vanished like smoke as divine punishment for tossing me into the Lion River. I am profoundly grateful, thinking that this too is entirely the result of Takahime's divine power.'

Takahime: 'That is why I say that if you simply rely upon the Kami, the Kami will avenge you. You must completely reform your past mischief and do whatever this Ikunomiya tells you to do.'

Rokuzau: 'Yes, I will do anything, but somehow I am starting to feel unwell. Unless you treat me to a drink, I cannot summon up my energy, wah.'

Takahime: 'Eh-eh, you are getting too big for your boots, you are hopeless precisely because of that. Even though I have made your enemies vanish just like that and am letting you hear wonderful teachings, what an impudent thing to say about wanting me to let you drink!'

Rokuzau: 'No, I did not say to let me drink sake. I said I wanted you to let me swallow the deba knife you are holding down into my belly. Is there truly no way I could be given just one mouthful?'

Takahime: 'That will never do. What do you intend to do carrying a deadly weapon like a deba knife? Do you intend to become a deba-kame or something?'

Rokuzau: 'No, I am not a deba-kame, I am thinking of becoming a deba-roku. With this, I intend to cut the sliding paper doors of the bathhouse into squares and peep at the activities of the bath attendants and maids. If you put bath attendants and maids together, you get six, don't you? When Roku-san enters into that, six times six is thirty-six, so it should become the full moon of the fifteenth night, right? You were softly chanting just a little while ago, too... As if there could be a half-moon on the fifteenth night, hiding in the clouds, half of it is here... so I heard. Since a half-moon on the fifteenth night is not very auspicious, I shall henceforth become deba-roku, turn into the moon of three and five, ascend to the First Spirit Realm, and report your present state to the Great Deity of the Moon. How is that, a brilliant idea, isn't it?'

Takahime: 'Brilliant ideas or manifest and hidden, there is no such thing; your spirit is the darkness of darkness. That is why people call you a dark-hontann. Since you are a good-for-nothing, even your name is Rokuzau. You are by no means the sort of creature that can go to the land of the moon. You are a commodity for the crossroads of the six paths. Hee-hee-hee-hee.'

Rokuzau: 'Hey, Taka, who do you think you are treating me as, eh-en. Up until now, that conch-blowing teacher came along, so I too was somewhat taken aback and kept quiet, but now that it has come to this, it is settled. There is no one to fear, an old hag or two is within my absolute freedom. Come, will you hand over all your money, or else, lay out all your clothes here and apologize, which is it? As for your spear, to tell the truth, I broke it to pieces. If you keep grumbling, you won't have your life!'

Takahime: 'Hey, Sharu, aren't you my retainer? What on earth are you dawdling for? Come, I will lend you this deba knife, so, not to be outdone, stand up to this fellow and press him hard. To help the good and punish the wicked is the way of the Kami. If such fellows swagger about in this world, there is no telling how much harm will be unleashed upon the world. Come, grasp this firmly and press upon him with overwhelming force!'

と言ひ乍ら、顔を真青になし、又もや次の山を転けつ輾びつ駆け上る。シャルも是非なく黒い褌をたらし乍ら、高姫の足型を尋ねて、息も苦しげに跟いて行く。
