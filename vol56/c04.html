Chapter 4: 盗 Song [1434]

盗歌〔1434〕

§ 1

Between Takaamahara and the Root Country lies the intermediate realm of Chiūkai. A hundred angels descended, gathering at the mansion of Ibukidonushi. Teaching the virtue of Aizen and shining the light of Shinshin, they sought to save all spirits into Tenkai. Awe-struck by the great divine will of the Ōkami, they exhausted words and attuned their breaths, admonishing them thus: yet the spirits who, while in Gense, had stained and broken their mitama with the sins of various evils and falsehoods, could not endure the kototama proclaimed by the pure angels. Their heads throbbing, their chests burning, feeling as though needles were being thrust into their ears, in extreme agony and of their own accord,

freely they drove themselves away toward the Root Country and the Nether-Bottom Country. Ah, Kamunagara! The Ōkami, who has appeared as the shiyusai of the Uchiu with infinite jinjimugen compassion—even setting aside the spirits of human beings, down to birds, beasts, insects, and fish—desired to rescue all endowed with spirit entirely into Tengoku, and to cause the enman of kakudantai to be accomplished; yet what is to be done? Spirits accustomed to evil loathe and reject goodness and truth, and of their own accord descend bravely onto the demonic path of evil and falsehood. How pitiful are those ugly mitama! The Sumeru Ōkami, looking upon this with pity, sent down into Gense the angels of the stern mitama, the joyful mitama, and the divine markers,

proclaiming and explaining fully and minutely every wondrous aspect of the spirit world, and showing it to them; yet, captured by the selfish desires of Gense, they closed their eyes and sealed their ears, turned their backs on the divine light, and fled scatteringly in all directions. How pitiful are those ugly mitama! The God appears at the forefront, explaining and admonishing in minute detail the retributions of good and will, shattering His heart a thousand times over in the desire to forgive sins and grant salvation—like a cuckoo with the thought of coughing up blood, His voice hoarse through eight thousand and eight times, standing at the ferry landing of the Ryūgūkan! How awe-inspiring it is. Ah, Kamunagara! May your mitama be ever prosperous and multiplied.

The red and white guards inspected the census registers of Heru, Shiaru, and Princess Kerina, proclaimed to the three that each of them still possessed several decades of remaining lifespan in the Gense, and commanded them to leave this place as quickly as possible and proceed toward the east. Wondering whether it was a dream or reality, the Gense or the Yūkai, completely unable to make sense of it and groping in the dark as the guards instructed, the three turned their heels and walked onward, stumbling toward the east.

Kerina: 'Leaving the shadow of the light—where even the thin smoke is faint and dying—behind me, seeking my beloved husband, pulling the thatched pivot, and coming forward as far as the banks of the Elshina River—at that very moment, the moonlight of the slanting peak looks down upon my figure and trembles, deeming it the impermanence of all things. Ah, how nostalgic, how nostalgic! Following after the footsteps of my beloved person, crossing wildly over the desolate plains, wetting my body with dew, and wringing out my tears—how sorrowful it is! If I draw near to lodge under the shadow of flowers even though getting wet, the waning moon leaves the night behind, and if I look up, the Milky Way is high. Although the voices of wild geese sound in the sky, there is no way to hear or learn tidings of the person I seek, hiccup!'

Ah, what shall I do? Swallowing the hatred of a thousand autumns, wandering far and near, heading toward the valley streams of the Elshina River and throwing myself in, becoming tranquility and bliss through extinction—before I could even think this, rescued by two men, Heru and Shiaru, and once again facing the rough winds of the floating world, at the moment when my heart was breaking, the thief chieftain Lord Beru blows an uncalled-for amorous passion. Setting Heru and Shiaru both aside, each striving to take Kerina as his wife—how futile and heartbreaking! When I was startled and climbed up into the treetop of the forest pines to escape the danger,

suddenly the three dark shadows—were they fallen into the deep abyss of the Elshina River, how pitiful—just as I peered down, I missed my footing, and I thought Kerina too had fallen together into the deep abyss. Before I even knew when it had happened, wandering into a farming path where grass and flowers flourished—how mysterious! Thinking upon it, is this place the spirit world? Meeting once again with my lord of the back whom I had searched for in vain, Kamihiko-sama, and hearing the tale of bygone days—how my heart is startled! My beloved person is my older brother's enemy. By what kind of working of karma did we come to be bound in such a mysterious thread of fate? This too is the karmic destiny of Gense revolving and revolving upon my own body...'

ヘル『ウン、承はらう、どうで碌な歌ぢやあるまいが、併し乞食の門付けを聞くと思つてお耳を借してやらう、古汚い虱のわくやうな歌なら御免だぞ』

六『どうせ虱のわく着物は古いに定つて居るわ、黴の生へた頭から捻り出した歌でなくては虱に対する名歌が出来るものでない、野暮の事を云ふな。サアこれから地獄の虱だ。

地獄

捻る楽潰す極楽火は浄土 水に入るこそ地獄なりけり

餓鬼

脱ぎ捨てて竿にかけたる古布子 餓鬼の如くに痩虱かな

畜生

人を喰ふ事より外はいざ虱 生畜生の果と云ふべき

修羅

血交りに殺し捨てたる虱こそ さながら修羅の衢なりけり

人間

帷布の縫目に宿る虱こそ 人と同じく立ちてゆくなり

天上

五月雨や竜の鱗にわく虱 つれてもろ共天に登れり かくれ住む肌の守の虱こそ 生きた観音菩薩なりけり』

シャル『アハハハハ。十八世紀のお茶坊主が吐いた歌ぢやないか、貴様の作つたのぢやあるまい』

六造『誰が作つても同じ事ぢや、現在俺の口から出たのぢやないか、他人のものなら他人の口から出る、俺の作つた証拠には俺の声をもつて俺の口から貴様に伝へてやつたぢやないか、ゴテゴテ云ふない』

シャル『他人の歌を盗む奴は、八衢の関所で調べられたら矢張咎られて咎人になるぞよ、歌を盗む奴を盗歌人といふのだぞ、ウフフフフ』

斯かる所へ蓑笠を被つた五十余の一人の婆アが、金剛杖をつき、何か小声に歌ひながら、トボトボと進み来る。四人は其姿の何処ともなく変つて居るのに不審を抱き、つくづくと眺めて居た。婆は何か急用でもあるやうに頻りに足許を急いで居る。四人は何と思ふたか道端の背丈の延びた雑草の中に身を隠した。
