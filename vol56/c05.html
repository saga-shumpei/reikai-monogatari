Chapter 5: 鷹魅 [1435]

鷹魅〔1435〕

§ 1

The primeval ancestor who created this world, the deity Miroku, masquerading as the sun deity possessing the physical shrine of Takahime, clung fiercely with diamond-indestructible wish-granting jewels and a hundred other divine treasures to the four quarters of the land. Crossing the waves of ten thousand ri of ocean, she roamed in turmoil, the result of which was that, through the seamless and compassionate mediation of the divine spirit of the great deity Kamususanō from the depths of her heart, she felt deep repentance, returned to live in the true path, and for a while appeared in the sacred ground to transmit the teachings. However, upon hearing that Tōsuke of the village of Ahaji was an old acquaintance, her monkey-like mind and horse-like will, going mad once again, surged with unstoppable pulsation.

Leaving the forest of Ikuta behind, she crossed over long expanses of sea and mountain, and with an eager heart hurried to the sacred place of Isoyakata on Mount Ubusuna to meet Tōsuke. Wrapping herself in the clouds of romance, seeking to renew old acquaintance by making a smooth show of things, her very core became shrouded in darkness. Tōsuke, firm in his faith, gave not a single glance to Takahime, who was maddened by love, and coldly and unceremoniously rebuffed her. Her heart clouded, Takahime became increasingly desperate, and once again her original wicked spiritual body had a relapse. Exposing her face to the storms of Mount Kajika, she cared nothing for shame or honor.

Stopping by the grove of the shrine built by Tamakuniwake, and posing there as the master of the teachings, just as she was looking down disdainfully upon Tatsuhiko, the master of the estate, she was manipulated by Yōgenbō—noted even among the villainous factions as the right arm of the eight-forked serpent lurking in Mount Daigumo. Mistaking him for Mokusuke, the general affairs manager and time-keeper of the estate of Isoya, she skillfully coaxed him, and raising a banner, sought to hole up in the sacred ground to spite Hinodewake's provocation and settle her scores of love. Just as she was plotting this, Hatsuyahime appeared. Unable to endure her kaleidoscopic activities, Yōgenbō, along with Takahime, sneaked through the forest.

Slipped away swiftly, and advanced like a married couple into the temple of Mount Konishi. Illuminated by the divine light, dropping the jewel of her boundary, Yōgenbō and Mokusuke, who fled together with the priestess Takahime, appeared in the forest of Ukiki where the Brahman army was stationed, and while performing all manner of magic, tormented the people. Just at that moment, startled by the voice of Hatsuyahime—a divine priestess equal to a heavenly maiden, renowned even in Miroku Teachings—Yōgenbō raised black clouds and, holding Takahime under his arm, fled through the empty air, just as in the midst of the vast plateau of Dekatan,

He dropped the priestess Takahime, and fled away like clouds and mist. Takahime fell headlong from the sky, fell unconscious, and having barely crossed the barrier of the separation of soul and body, came journeying far to the barrier of Yachimata, only to find her path blocked by fierce red and white guards. She became one whose soul is polished and purified while dwelling for three years in the world of Chūu. Even so, Takahime's spirit registered her name in hell, fearing, trembling at, and abhorring the spiritual light of Takamanohara. Even today, a full year later, she wanders aimlessly about the Chūu realm, confronting a hundred straying and bewildered spirits,

Continuing her propaganda full of deropments as if she were still in the physical world—how foolish indeed! Catching sight of the four individuals—Kerina's hime hiding in the valley of Erishina, along with the Brahman soldiers Hel and Shar, and Rokuzō—concealing themselves among the grass by the roadside, she raised her withered voice and shouted out: 'I am the loyal heavenly envoy of the sun deity, the vanguard of the deity Miroku, the living shrine of the priestess Takahime! I am paying attention to you four! Quickly leap out of the grass and step forth before my living shrine!' How comical indeed was her style as she called out incessantly, asking 'Why and wherefore?' Ah, truly divine are the kami; souls that have wandered to the very end can find no power in the kami, nor any means to save them.

Takahime, facing the four men and women hiding in the tall grass by the roadside, with a sharp voice and the tips of her words lifted sharply, her mouth corners sending spiva flying, thrusting her Atlas-like face forward with sudden jerks, twitching her chin two or three times, shaking her shoulders, and making beckoning-cat-like gestures while clawing at the empty air two or three times,

Takahime: 'Hey there, whoever you may be, this wilderness is the jurisdiction of this Takahime. Why have you come silently to such a place? Come on, come over this way. I will tell you a marvelous story. Oh dear, how gloomy. Will you not come out quickly? No matter how long you stay cooped up in the grass like crickets or grasshoppers, nothing will be settled.'

高姫『エー、そりや何を云ふのだ。此婆の話を聞いてやらうも、糞もあつたものかい。底津磐根の弥勒様の生宮だ。何と云つても助けにや措かぬ、さア来なされ来なされ。これ、其処な若いお女中、お前は一寸見た所で仲々気が利いて居る。事と品とによつたら妾の脇立に使つてやらうまいものでもない。何せよ、曇りきつた霊が直に天国に行くと云ふのは余り気が良すぎる。中途で墜落る様な事をしてはならず、苦労の花が咲く世の中だから……天国紫微宮から人間の姿となつて降つて来たのだ。そして苦労の手本を見せて皆に改心させる役だぞえ。お前も出て来て苦労をしなさい』

ケリナ『ハイ、有難う厶います。実の所は八衢の関所迄参りました所、まだ生命が現世に残つて居るから帰れ、と仰有つたから帰つて来たのです。最早此処は現界で厶いますか』

高姫『きまつた事だよ。此処は現界も現界、大現界だ。現幽神三界の救ひ主だから先づ現界の人間から助けてやるのだよ』

ヘル『あああ、何が何だか訳が分らなくなつて来た。然しさう聞くと現界の様にもあるし、も一つ心の底に疑念も残つて居る。こんな道端に立つて居た所が仕方が無い。先づお婆アの後に跟いて何でも可いから探らして貰ふ事にしようかい。のう二男一女の御連中』

高姫『探らして貰ふなんて、そりや何を云ふのだい。神の教は正真一方だ。水晶の様につきぬけて居るのだぞえ。スパイか何ぞの様に探るなんて、心の穢い事を云ふのぢやありませぬわい。さアさア来なさい』

と羽ばたきし乍ら欣々と東を指して小径を歩み出した。四人は兎も角、婆さまの館に行つて休息せむと重い足を引摺り乍ら跟いて行く。

谷川の辺に萱で葺いた二間作りの小かな家が建つて居た。これが高姫の中有界に於ける住家である。ヒヨヒヨした板の一枚橋を危く渡り乍ら漸くにして四人は高姫の館にやつと着いた。
