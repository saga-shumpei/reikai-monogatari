Chapter 6: High 圧 [1436]

高圧〔1436〕

§ 1

Guided by Takahime, the four men and women crossed the single-span bridge of Hosotanigaha and were led to a small house built in the two-bay style.

The spirit of Takahime already had its registry in Jigoku, and she was equipped with the natural qualifications to descend straightway into Jigoku.

However, the Lord Ōkami of infinite benevolence and compassion commanded her to undergo the training of Bulgario for three years in order somehow to save that spirit.

All inner states of a spirit promptly manifest themselves in the outer state.

Generally speaking, the outer state refers to the physical body, movements, facial expressions, language, and the like.

The inner state consists of thoughts and emotions of good and love.

Spirits possessing registry in the Jigoku realm are strongest in arrogant egoism, holding feelings of contempt toward others and unconsciously manifesting this to the exterior.

Toward those who do not respect them, they promptly manifest intimidation, or show expressions of hatred and vengeful countenance.

Therefore, those who utter even a single word not conforming to their intent are immediately subjected to various labels such as vanity, evil, or falsehood, and it is the disposition of those registered in the Jigoku realm to attempt to beat them down.

Human beings in the mundane world are likewise the same, in accordance with the principle of the unity of the manifest and the hidden.

Regardless of whether it is the mundane world or the spiritual world, those in Jigoku are all immersed in worldly love, self-originating evils, and various falsehoods.

Consequently, unless one's mind is similar to their own mind, staying together with those whose minds do not harmonize is truly painful, to the extent that one cannot even breathe freely.

However, evil ones—that is, those in Jigoku—perform evil deeds with evil hearts, and moreover attempt to express or explain all truth through evil.

Therefore, their explanations are filled everywhere with contradictions, clashes, incoherence, and fragmentation, which, seen from the eyes of righteous humans and spirits, are extremely inappropriate.

When such evil spirits spontaneously fall and go into the Jigoku realm, numerous evil spirits residing there gather upon them and attempt to inflict harsh and ferocious punishments.

That state of affairs is roughly analogous to the legal organization in the mundane world.

All those who punish evil must be evil persons.

Those devoid of vices such as falsehood, deceit, ferocity, and harshness can by no means punish evil persons.

However, the point wherein the mundane world and the ghostly world differ is that, whereas in the mundane world major evils frequently go undiscovered, or good people are mistaken for evil and receive punishment in great abundance, in the Jigoku realm evil itself spontaneously goes and falls, so there is not the slightest imbalance, precisely as if weighed on a scale.

And the inner states of ferocity and harshness also manifest upon the outer state, namely the facial expression.

Therefore, evil demons and evil spirits who have fallen into Jigoku all retain facial expressions corresponding to their inner states, manifesting the appearance of lifeless corpses, giving truly unpleasant impressions at a single glance with warts, moles, large boils, and the like.

Yet this is an aspect seen by piercing through their inner states from the eyes of heavenly beings destined to reach heaven, and among the evil spirits of Jigoku mutually, they do not look particularly ugly at all.

The reason is that they all believe falsehood to be truth and feel evil to be good.

When at times the brilliant light of the Lord Ōkami shines from the heavens to illuminate the Jigoku realm, they promptly expose bizarre figures and strange postures, manifesting a countenance like that of specters, and are themselves astonished at the frightfulness of their appearance.

However, when the brilliant light descends from the heavenly realm, the hazy Jigoku increases its degree of pitch-black darkness layer by layer.

The virtue of love and goodness and the brilliant light of true faith become ever darker in Jigoku, which is filled with evil and falsehood.

Therefore, no matter what divine majesty, virtuous goodness, or brilliant light of true faith there may be, when viewed by a human registered in Jigoku, it appears darker than the world wherein they reside, truth is felt as falsehood, and the virtue of love and goodness comes to be felt as hatred.

Consequently, it is rather to be called natural, in accordance with the principle corresponding to their inner states, that modern people who have for the most part fallen into the Jigoku realm look upon the brilliant light of Ōmoto and instead take it for pitch-black darkness, regard the teachings of supreme goodness and supreme beauty as doctrines of supreme ugliness and supreme evil, or slander them as heretical teachings.

And the inner states of ferocity and harshness also manifest upon the outer state, namely the facial expression.

高姫に導かれて四人の男女は、細谷川の一本橋を渡り、二間造りの小さき家に導かれた。高姫の精霊は既に地獄に籍を置き、直ちに地獄に下るべき自然の資格が備はつてゐる。併し乍ら仁慈無限の大神は如何にもして其精霊を救ひやらむと三年の間、ブルガリオの修行を命じ給ふたのである。総て精霊の内分は忽ち外分に現はれるものである。外分とは概して言へば身体、動作、面貌、言語等を指すのである。内分とは善愛の想念や情動である。

地獄界に籍を有する精霊は最も尊大自我の心強く、他に対して軽侮の念を持し之を外部に不知不識の間に現はすものである。自分を尊敬せざるものに対しては忽ち威喝を現はし、又は憎悪の相好や復讐的の相好を現はすものである。

故に一言たりとも其意に合はざる事を言ふ者は、忽ち慢心だとか悪だとか虚偽だとか、いろいろの名称を附して、之を叩きつけむとするのが地獄界に籍を置くものの情態である。

現界に於ける人間も亦、顕幽一致の道理に依つて同様である。現界、霊界を問はず地獄にあるものは、全て世間愛と自己よりする、諸の悪と諸の虚偽に浸つてゐるが故に、其心と自己の心と相似たるものとでなければ、心の相応せないものと一緒に居る事は実に苦しく、呼吸も自由に出来ない位である。併し乍ら悪即ち地獄に於ける者は悪心を以て悪を行ひ、又悪を以て総ての真理を表明したり、説明せむとするものである。故に其説明には矛盾撞着支離滅裂の箇所ばかりで、正しき人間や精霊の眼から見れば、実に不都合極まるものである。斯かる悪霊が地獄界に自ら進んで堕ちゆく時は、其処に居る数多の悪霊は、彼等の上に集まり来り、峻酷獰猛なる責罰を加へむとするものである。其有様は現界に於ける法律組織と略類似して居る。総て悪を罰するものは悪人でなければならぬ。虚偽、譎詐、獰猛、峻酷等の悪徳無きものは到底悪人を罰することは出来得ないのである。併し乍ら現界と幽界と異なる点は現界にては大悪が発見されなかつたり、又善人が悪と誤解されて責罰を受くる事が沢山にあるに反し、地獄界に於ては、悪其物が自ら進んで堕ち行くのであるから、恰も衡にかけた如く、少しの不平衡も無いものである。

而して獰猛と峻酷の内分も亦外分即ち相好の上に現はるるものである。故に地獄に墜ちて居る邪鬼及邪霊は何れも其内分相応の面貌を保ち生気無き死屍の相を現じ、疣や痣、大なる腫物等一見して実に不快な感じを与ふる者である。然し之は天国に到るべき天人の目より其内分を透して見たる形相であつて、地獄の邪霊相互の間にては決して余り醜しく見えない者である。何故なれば彼等は皆虚偽を以て真と信じ、悪を以て善と感じて居るからである。時あつて天上より大神の光明、地獄界を照す時は、彼等は忽ち珍姿怪態を曝露し、恰も妖怪の如き相好を現はし、自ら其姿の恐ろしきに驚くものである。併し乍ら天界より光明下り来る時は、朦朧たる地獄は層一層暗黒の度を増すものである。愛善の徳と信真の光明は悪と虚偽とに充されたる地獄では益々暗黒となるものである。故に如何なる神の稜威も善徳も、信真の光明も、地獄に籍を置きたる人間より見たる時は、自分の住する世界よりは暗黒に見え、真理は虚偽と感じ、愛善の徳は憎悪と感ずるに至るものである。故に大部分地獄界に堕落せる現代人が、大本の光明を見て却て之を暗黒となし、至善至美の教を以て至醜至悪の教理となし、或は邪教と誹るに至るは、其人の内分相応の理に依つて寧ろ当然と謂ふ可きものである。

§ 2

Although Takahime was released into Chiūkai and given a period of time to accumulate the self-cultivation of a Seirei, she could not easily escape from the realm of Jigoku. Mistaking falsehood for Shinri and believing evil to be good, she continued the activity of the wheels, frantically trying to expand the path of goodness. As they say that like gathers with like, unless things are similar to her own inner state, it is simply impossible to harmonize in Reikai. In Genkai, because one can wrap up a fraction of one's inner state through various honeyed words and smooth speeches, or through falsehoods, there were at least some people who listened to Takahime's teachings. Having now come to Reikai, however, unless they were similar to herself, she could no longer spend her life together with anyone. Nevertheless, Takahime thought solely that she was still in Genkai, and treated the words of the guards of Yachimata with a half-believing, half-doubting attitude. As months and years passed, almost a full year since coming to Reikai, she ceased to take the guards' words to heart in the slightest. Growing increasingly worse, she firmly believed that her teachings were supreme goodness, that her actions conformed to the gods, and that she herself was the living shrine of Girijōten Hizunokami, and the divine pillar of the Great Miroku God of Sokotsuiwane who universally controls heaven and earth. Thus, there was no doing anything with her. At any rate, Takahime led four men and women into her own room, sat herself haughtily in the seat of honor, and began to expound her disjointed and incoherent teachings in a detailed manner.

Takahime: 'Everyone, you have truly followed the teachings of Hizunokami and arrived here. You are personages with a profound karmic connection. Even if you searched the entire world until your iron sandals wore out, you would never find such wonderful teachings anywhere else! Therefore, be joyful. This Takahime is the soul of an angel in the First Spirit Realm of Takamanohara, and the living shrine of the Great Deity of the root of roots. Therefore, if she props up heaven, heaven is propped up; if she props up earth, earth is propped up. She is the central pillar gripping everywhere as one. To compare it to a fan, she is the rivet, you see. To compare it to a clock, she is like the winding crown. If a fan has no rivet, it falls apart into pieces. If a clock has no winding crown, you cannot even wind it, can you? That is why this Takahime is the Wish-granting Jewel, unparalleled in the world of the root of roots, so listen well. Since you have committed theft, become Brahman soldiers, and done all sorts of evil, you are items that ought to be cast straight into Jigoku; but if you listen well to what the living shrine of this Takahime says and put it into practice, I will save you even to the wondrous, wondrous First Heaven, you see.'

Thus she blew her grand, endless falsehoods without stopping. However, Takahime herself never once thought that her own words were grand falsehoods. She firmly believed them to be the genuine, faultless, and sincere words of God, down to the very last fraction of a percentage.

Heru: 'Hey, Takahime-san, if you are such a great personage, why do you not ascend to heaven and protect the nether world? To build a palace in a remote mountain like this and catch ordinary humans like us one or two at a time to give sermons—is that not a bit thoughtless for a deity? Although there are countless Seirei in the whole world of whom not even millions are known, for the living shrine of the Great Deity herself to do such a thing—it simply does not make sense to me, wah. In short, is this not just Takahime-san's tall tale?'

Takahime suddenly transformed into a Jigoku-like state, displaying expressions of contempt, intimidation, and hatred. Seething and swelling up with anger, even her words began to manifest the phase of Jigoku.

Takahime: 'Hey, you, what an absurd thing to say! Truly, you are a worthless creature no better than an insect. To contemptuously look down upon the living shrine of the deity, of all sacrileges, is quite out of the question! This living shrine will not forgive such a narrow-minded attitude. I shall cast you straight into Jigoku, so be prepared for that!'

Displaying the color of intense fury in her ferocious countenance, she gnashed her teeth furiously, glaring with enraged eyes.

と獰猛なる形相に憤怒の色を現はし、歯をキリキリと噛みしめて、眼を怒らし睨めつけて居る。

§ 3

Heru looked at Takahime's countenance and gave a start, yet firmly settling his chest and thrusting out his elbows, he deliberately thrust his body forward to conceal the pounding of his heart,

Heru: 'Ahahahahaha, you have vomited forth much, Takahime! What is the meaning of that demonic face? The Kamisama of infinite benevolence will not make such a difficult expression just because someone spoke of something that was not to Your liking a little bit. Is not Kami love, goodness, and truth? By doing such things as intimidating, holding in contempt, or hating people even for a moment, how can You be called a righteous Kami? Step back!'

thus he shouted in a loud voice.

Takahime was angered like raging fire, her countenance becoming yet more ferocious, and glaring with a look of extreme bitterness,

Takahime: 'Hey, you Brahman thief, what are you talking about? How could a true Ikigami be understood by a blind and deaf person like you? Because you are building up a hell called evil within your heart, the complete and beautiful visage of this Hinodenokami appears terrifying to you, and words of supreme good and beautiful language sound to you like harsh words and violent speech. When the hierarchy of Mitama differs, evil appears as good, and good appears as evil.'

thus, not knowing that she herself was falling into hell through her own evil and falsehood, she wildly and indiscriminately hurled accusations of evil at others. When both human beings and spirits become this blind and foolish, no power of any Kami can save them.

Heru thrust his neck straight out in front of Takahime, feeling as though he had deployed a strategy of an army with its back to the river, clenched his fists tightly,

Heru: 'Say one more thing, whatever it may be. The moment this iron fist touches your crown, I will smash you into fine dust!'

showing such an attitude. Perhaps even the formidable Takahime was intimidated by that threatening demeanor, and for her part, she ceased to treat him as an adversary any further. Heru found himself with nowhere to direct his raised fist, and awkwardly lowered it back to its original position.

Takahime laughed with a sneer, and with a most arrogant expression, looked down upon the three people behind,

Takahime: 'Hey, Rokukō, Sharu, Kerina, no matter what is said, this cannot be done unless it is a matter of the fate and innate nature of the Mitama, so those into whose ears my words do not enter are bound to go to hell no matter what. Everyone, what do you say? Would you not like to listen to what this Ikigama says and ascend to heaven?'

Kerina: 'Yes, thank you very much. A sinful human being like myself must surely go to the hell appropriate for me on account of the sins I have created. No matter how much Your Ladyship says you will save me up into heaven, going to a place unsuited to my Mitama would be agonizing beyond endurance. I wish to live on in this world just as I am now for all time.'

Takahime: 'Well, how incomprehensible you are! When Kami is offering blessings and thrusting them forth, is there such a thing as refusing to receive them? As the proverb says... If one takes not what Heaven bestows, disaster visits one's body instead... does it not? Why do you decline the divine virtue thrust forth by this Ikigama?'

Kerina: 'Yes, thank you for your kindness, but if it is divine virtue given by Kamisama, it will never be taken away unless I myself return it. However, divine virtue given by human beings - since one never knows when it will be taken back - it would be to the advantage of both sides not to receive it from the beginning.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Kerina, what an incomprehensible thing you are saying! As I said from the very beginning, is this not the Ikigama of the Great Miroku of the Bottommost Bedrock? To think of this Ikigama as a human being is a complete mistake from the start. That is why I say you are lacking in repentance. The reason you came to my mansion is also that you were drawn here by the affinity of your Mitama from the ancient, ancient, fundamental ages of the primordial age of the gods. Since your remote ancestor was a Mitama of the Ten Evil Paths who made the Taishōgun-sama suffer, that sin has been transmitted to the descendants, and now, following the renewal and rebuilding of the world, a great sweeping clean is about to begin, and the Mitamas of the evil lineage are to be burned and destroyed so that they are not left between Heaven and Earth; therefore, to obey obediently while this Ikigama speaks is your virtue.'

高姫『ドークズの身魂といふものは上げも下しもならぬものだなア。人間の分際として根本の因縁が解るものかいなア。それだから此高姫が身魂調べをして各自に因縁性来を表はし、因縁だけの御用を仰せつけるのだ。先祖からの因縁性来が解らぬやうな事で、何うして底津岩根の大神様の生宮の御用が勤まりますか。神の申す間に柔順に聞いて置きなさらぬと後で後悔を致しても、其処になりたらモウ神は知りませぬぞや。マア悠りと胸に手を当てて雪隠へでも入つて考へて来なさい。アーア一人の氏子を誠の道に導かうと思へば、並や大抵の事ぢやない。乃木大将が旅順口を十万の兵士を以て落したよりも難いものだ。針の穴へ駱駝を通すよりも難い。これでは神も骨が折れるワイ。盲聾に何程結構な事を噛んで含めるやうに言ひ聞かしてやつても、豚に真珠、猫に小判のやうなものだ。憐れみ玉へ助け玉へ、底津岩根の大弥勒様』

と掌を合し一生懸命にケリナ姫の改心を祈つてゐる。シャル、六造の二人は此問答をポカンと口を開けた儘延び上つて立膝し乍ら聞いてゐる。暫くは土佐犬の噛み合ひのやうな光景で沈黙の幕が下りた。其処へ銅羅声を張り上げて門の戸をブチ割れる程叩くものがある。
