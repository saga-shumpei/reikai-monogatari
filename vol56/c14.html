§ 1

When Heru looked closely at Beru's form in the moonlight, Beru's eyes were dazed. Fortunately, noticing a small puddle nearby in which the moon was shining, Heru took water into his mouth and made Beru drink it repeatedly on his face and mouth. Gradually recovering his breath and looking around restlessly in all directions,

Beru: 'Ahhh, where on earth is this? I have been to a dreadful place.'

He peered into Heru's face with a bewildered look. Raising his voice,

Heru: 'Hey, Beru, pull yourself together! Just now, Naisu choked your throat and made your eyes go blind. I attended to you in various ways and saved you. Come on, won't you stand up quickly? Who knows when the pursuers might come. Though it's night, this is a thoroughfare. Come on, pull yourself together, pull yourself together!'

He patted Beru repeatedly on the back.

Beru: 'Ah, after all it was a dream? I had gone to a dreadful place. The agony of many red and blue demons chasing me from all four quarters while holding iron bars—ah, I'm glad it was a dream. But what has become of that Naisu? We let him escape, I suppose.'

Heru: 'What? Seeing that you couldn't bear watching as your throat was choked and you were thrashing about, when I struck him from behind with a broken cudgel with a thwack, he tumbled down quite easily. Just look at him, isn't he collapsed right over there?'

Before Beru could see the flash of the diamond,

Beru: 'Die!'

Uttering just that, he extended his gibbon-like arm, tore off the head ornament, and thrust it instantly into his bosom.

Heru: 'Eh, if you're going to eat poison, go down to the plate. Since you've committed the capital crime of murder, stealing jewels is the same thing after all. If it's going to be the same sin, shall we plunder even this and live a thick and short life?'

Setting his vicious resolve, he searched Debisu's corpse and stripped off every single shining thing without leaving a trace.

Beru: 'Ahahahahaha, what a fragile thing he is. But isn't he an extremely extravagant fellow, attaching such a multitude of jewels to his body? Since everyone living the aristocratic life today is like this, it is no wonder that the people below suffer. Shall we take these jewels to some town, sell them off, and as atonement, help the paupers of the world to the utmost? If we do that, even if we kill one person, we are saving the multitude, so far from a great crime, we might even receive a reward from Heaven!'

Heru: 'Even if receiving a reward from Heaven is utterly unhoped-for, at the very least we might be able to have our sins made lighter. Anyway, hand over all the jewels you are holding to me. If I let you hold them, you'll awaken your selfish desire again and probably won't use them for the relief of the paupers. If you let me hold them, I will make good use of these treasures so that your sins become lighter and my sins become lighter too, you see.'

Beru: 'Stop talking foolishness! It is dangerous to leave them in the hands of a human like you who trembles even when the wind blows. If you keep them in the bosom of a man like me who is daring and fearless, no demon whatsoever can aim at them. Come, hand them over cleanly this way!'

Heru: 'Stop talking foolishness! If I hadn't rescued you, you wouldn't be able to stay alive in this world. Quit such clinging attachments and hand them all over to me!'

Beru: 'You say such plausible things, and you must intend to snatch the jewels from me and pocket them all by yourself. The fellows nowadays gather money by blubbering about charitable societies or orphanages, and they are all fellows who fatten their own bosoms. For a laborer who has once degenerated into a thief, there is no way charity could ever be a dream-like reality! If you let such a hypocrite hold treasures, he will misuse the treasures of the world, so hand them over cleanly to me!'

Heru: 'What are you spitting out?! Aren't you a thief yourself? If it's dangerous to hand them to me, it's equally dangerous to hand them to you. Come on, won't you produce them quickly?'

Beru: 'Yosh, interesting! I'll show you I can take them brilliantly.'

With that, both stamped their thighs, spat upon their hands, and in the manner of a street sumo bout, intertwined fourfold, rolling over and about while clinching and locked in struggle. Scratching each other's eyes with hard nails, scraping noses, and with hands, feet, and faces all becoming like masses of blood, they began to fierce-fight, both of them becoming limp and in a half-dead state, and collapsed with a thud right on the spot.
