§ 1

In the mansion of Allstin, the son Wux, along with Ekis and Hermann, the two of them, sat cross-legged and were deeply engrossed in a whispered conversation.

Wux: 'You two shouldn't keep coming to ask me for money over and over again like this, don't you think? As you already know, I am a dependent living under my father's roof, so money doesn't just flow freely for me. Once that bald old man dies off successfully, the wealth of this house will be entirely at my disposal, so I will do whatever it takes... but don't say things like that and wait for a while. If you do that, Okuniwake and his wife will have their duties stripped from them due to the crime of losing the jewel, and they will end up being severely punished. If things go that way, and I report to Ōkuronushi-sama that I have discovered this jewel, it is undoubtedly settled that Davis will be made Okuniwake's successor by adoption. Then, as a reward for my discovering the jewel, I will become his son-in-law. Since success is already dangling right there before us, do not make such a fuss and wait for a while. In return, I will firmly establish you as executives, and I will hand over whatever amount of money you need, so come on. If my father finds out about this, I will be cast out of the house, and I will end up losing everything down to the last single thing. If that happens, you two will be in trouble as well, won't you?'

Ekis and Hermann were bad friends of Wux, who constantly urged him into all sorts of evil deeds, making him steal his father's money and waste it on food and drink. Wux was originally a foolish son with a somewhat missing screw somewhere in his head. Even so, under the pretext of being the steward's son, he was greatly flattered by the company of very young men, and getting carried away, he would steal his father's money and waste it on food and drink together with his bad friends. His father, Allstin, had lost his wife, and taking his only son Wux as his mainstay, loved him so much that it wouldn't hurt even if he put him in his eye. Because of that, Wux grew increasingly arrogant and ended up completely out of hand. Furthermore, Wux was infatuated with Davis-hime, the daughter of Okuniwake, and day and night he would speak of Davis, Davis, as if it were his pet phrase. However, Davis-hime, the one who mattered most, loathed the foolish son Wux like a centipede, and every time he narrowed his eyes and approached her, she would sternly elbow him and put him to shame. Nevertheless, Wux's love only grew more intense; the more she hated him, the more endearing she became, and hoping somehow to achieve his goal, he consulted the two men, Ekis and Hermann. The cunning Ekis sneered without a moment's hesitation and said,

Ekis: 'If you intend to make Davis-hime your wife, it is the easiest thing in the art. If you secretly steal away the Nyoi Hōju and hide it away, it is certain that Okuniwake, his wife, and all their family members will be dismissed from office on the grounds of negligent supervision, and furthermore, they are destined to be subjected to punishment. First of all, if you hide that jewel and make them worry, Okuniwake and his wife will eventually run out of all their plans, and they are bound to say things like, "If there is anyone who has searched for and brought back that lost Nyoi Hōju, I will give them Davis-hime," or "I will make him my son-in-law." First of all, hiding that jewel is the best course of action.'

Thus did Ekis and Hermann plant the idea in his mind. Thereupon Wux, the blockhead, stole secretly into the inner palace at night, and working together with Ekis and Hermann, stole the jewel, dug beneath the floorboards, and hid it where no one knew. And as a temporary hush-money for the moment, he handed over a hundred ryo to each of them. However, the two of them, Ekis and Hermann, quickly used it all up on food and drink, and taking advantage of his vulnerability time and time again, kept coming to Wux's place to beg for money. Every single time, Wux also devised various ways to hand it over. Yet, since he had already stolen and handed over his father's money to the very limit where none was left, he had no more money to give no matter how much was demanded. Therefore, Wux kept refusing them, saying that since he no longer possessed even a single mon... they must wait a while, and once his wish was fulfilled presently, he would give them money in any amount whatsoever... But Ekis... convinced that in the house of this steward gold and silver must be glaring with wide-open eyes, and that if he only threatened him, this foolish son would certainly produce whatever amount was asked... hardened his wicked resolve and raised his voice sharply,

ワツクス『ヘエ、ほんの酒の上で訳もない喧嘩をおつ初めまして誠に申訳が厶いませぬ』

オールス『さうではあるまい。最前から門口ですつかり立聞をした。貴様ら三人は如意宝珠を盗んだ大罪人だ。仮令吾子と雖も許す事は出来ぬ。サア三人とも手を後へ廻せ』

ワツクス『お父さま、誠に済まぬ事を致しました。併し乍らもう今日限り心を改めますから、何卒内証にして下さい』

オールス『馬鹿を云ふな、誠の道に親疎の区別はない。オールスチンの悴に貴様のやうな大悪人が出来たかと思へば、神様に対し、先祖に対し、申訳がない、どうして俺の顔が立つか。グヅグヅ云はずに罪に伏するが好い。これやエキス、ヘルマンの両人、元を云へばお前達が悴に知恵をかつたのだから、お前等の罪が最も重い、併し乍ら悴も悪いのだから免れる訳にはゆかぬ。三人共覚悟してバラモンのお経でも唱へたがよからう』

§ 4

Tears are pooling in his Riyōgan.

Ekisu is bitsukuri and says,

Ekisu: 'If it is Ōrusuchin-sama, it was truly a regrettable matter, but since your son Wax is also involved in this, dōzo overlook it. Please, dōzo forgive the matter of thrusting us to the authorities. In exchange, I will sugusama return the jewel to you.'

Ōrusu: 'Returning the jewel is mochiron. However, the crime once taken cannot be forgiven by any means. My, my, you have done a troublesome thing. If this is left as is, the master's house will be danzetsu, and accordingly this karei, on account of the crime of kantokufuyukitodoki, might be subjected to any kind of genbatsu. We must tsukida you kisamara sannin and protect the shiyuke and my house. Ōrusuchin is not such a man as to make a mistake in a daiji, drawn by his love for his son at kayō a time.'

Thus he is chiding them in a kowadaka voice.

The three of them became like a flattened gumo, grinding their heads against the tatami, and were tadatada wabii-ing and nothing more.

Ōrusuchin immediately prostrates himself before the shinzen and prays itsiyōkenmei, 'Forgive the crime of my child.'

However, there is no kufū by which these three, who once committed a daizai, can be saved by any means.

If he forgives their crime simply because they are his own son, he would spark the beginning of the disorder of public morals, receive the reproach of unfairness, and crush the true path; ah, what shall he do, he is shedding tears like a waterfall.

The two of them look into each other's eyes, and from behind they loop a thin rope around his neck, pull him down, pile on top of each other, and try to strangle him to death.

Ōrusuchin, with the limit of his strength, tries to defend and fight back, and escape, but his strength is insufficient, and he has no choice other than to leave it to whatever they do.

Wax: 'Oi, Ekisu, Herrmann, do not do such painful things to my old man, will he not end up dying? Rolling him about is all very well, but are you going to take his life as well?'

Ekisu: 'That is a settled thing. If we do not take this fellow's life, our lives will be gone. Your life will be gone as well, you know. What are you playing the fool for? Oi, Herrmann, I am going to rip apart the old dotard, so you go ahead and beat down Wax.'

Herrmann: 'Yoshikita.'

So saying, he leaps upon Wax.

Here begins a mutual slaughter of two pairs, and strange sounds of jitan, batan echo as far as the outside of the door.

Catching wind of this sound, the one who hurriedly flew in was Eru, the servant of Oguniwake.

The moment Ekisu and Herrmann see Eru's face, they flee at full speed out the back door, leaving like clouds and mist over the mountains.

And Eru heard to the very end every bit of the circumstances and origin of the quarrel from before.

Ōrusuchin at last manages to rise and is stroking the pain in his neck.

Wax leaps into the storehouse, fastens the lock from the inside, and is trembling.

Eru, at full speed, dashes back to the mansion to report this state of affairs, cutting through the sky.

エキス『定つた事だい。此奴の命を取らねば俺達の命が無くなるのだ。貴様の命もなくなるのだぞ。何を呆けて居るのだ。オイ、ヘルマン俺は老耄をバラして了うから、貴様はワツクスをやつつけて了へ』

ヘルマン『よし来た』

とワツクスに喰ひつく。茲に二組の殺し合ひが初まり、ジタン、バタンと怪しき物音が戸外まで聞えて居る。此物音を聞きつけ慌ただしく飛び込んで来たのは、小国別の僕エルであつた。エキス、ヘルマンはエルの顔を見るより一目散に裏口から雲を霞と山越に逃げて仕舞つた。そしてエルは最前からの喧嘩の顛末や由来を残らず聞いて仕舞つた。オールスチンは漸くにして起き上り首筋の痛みを撫でて居る。ワツクスは、庫の中へ飛び込み、中より錠を卸して慄つて居る。エルは一目散にこの有様を報告せむと、宙を切つて館へ馳帰り行く。
