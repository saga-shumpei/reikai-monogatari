Chapter 1: Kami 慮 [1431]

神慮〔1431〕

§ 1

Modern people think that / In the realm of the root / From the beginning / A single demon king actually exists, / Governing numerous hells, / And feared for controlling / The sins of the falling spirits; / That the demon king was once / A heavenly being of radiance, / Yet for the crime of rebellion / Was cast down into hell / Together with his clansmen— / Such a belief has from ancient times / Been deeply carved into people's hearts, / And none awake to the true state. / Demon king, Satan, and Lucifer— / To state it briefly, are hell itself. / Especially that which is called the demon king / Is hell located in the background, / And those dwelling herein are called fierce fiends, / Their wickedness the most extreme. / Moreover, that hell situated in the foreground / Is what is called Satan. / Compared to the demon king, / Satan is not so exceedingly wicked, / Therefore this is called a fierce spirit. / Furthermore, the meaning of being called Lucifer / Is a song belonging to Babel, / And their territory has expanded / Even to the heavenly realms of hisakata. / Therefore, that a single demon king exists / And sits ruling over hell— / Such a distinction does not exist / For the spirits dwelling in both the heavenly and hellish realms, / But all these originate / From the human spirits; / From the beginning of the creation of heaven and earth / Down to modern society, / As many hundreds of millions of human souls / Exist in the phenomenal world, / Every time they have opposed / The divine status of the Great August Kami, / Each has accumulated deeds / That are their own personal demons, / Becoming wicked ghosts / And bringing forth hell— / Realizing this, they should constantly / Purify their souls / And strive to ascend / To the land where the Kami dwells. / Ah, Kannagara kamugamuni, / May the august spirits be blessed and prosper.

Filled with the virtues of love and goodness, / Dwelling in faith and truth, / The true Kami turns the back of / Benevolent and glorious august face / Upon people filled with sin and falsehood, / Rejects them, / Causes them to plunge into hell, / Grows angry against evil, / And punishes and harms them— / Such things have been transmitted / By the religious ministers of various sects and schools. / This discourse / Is the speech of foolish scholars / Who have misunderstood the great august mind of the Ōkami. / The Kami never turns the back of the face / Upon any sinner whatsoever, nor rejects them, / Nor grows angry and casts spirits into the realm of hell. / If one asks why this is so, / It is because goodness and love / Are the rare physical body of the Lord Kami. / The substance of goodness never inflicts harm; / For love and benevolence, / There is no reason for anyone to reject. / By any chance, if the Kami were to turn away, / Reject, and grow angry at sinners, / It would run counter to benevolence and love, / Return to that original nature, / And contradict the divine status itself. / Therefore, how does the Kami approach human spirits everywhere? / There is never a time / When the Kami does not manifest / Through goodness, benevolence, and love. / The Kami of 567 / Only thinks of goodness for human beings, / Deeply loves them, / And bestows benevolence. / Ah, Kannagara kamugamuni, / May the august spirits be blessed and prosper.

All things flowing from the Kami to humans / Are solely the goodness of love, / And the light of faith and truth; / Things coming from the land of the root / Are nothing but wickedness and lawlessness. / The true Kami makes it His work / To cause human beings / To depart from evil / And return to the path of goodness. / Contrary to this, the realm of hell / Is single-mindedly and frantically anxious / To tempt human beings into evil. / Nevertheless, if human beings / Were not intervening between the two realms of heaven and hell, / Humans would have no thoughts whatsoever, / No significance, no freedom, no choice, / And their bodies and souls would perish. / That humans possess two aspects of good and evil / Is the bestowal of the Kami / Who balances right and wrong. / If the Kami were to turn His face away from human spirits, / Evil deeds would be done as the heart pleases, / And the reason for being human would perish. / The radiance flowing forth / From the Kami toward humans / Is solely and exclusively the virtue of goodness. / Yet both wicked human beings / And souls of peerless goodness / All bathe in that divine virtue. / The point of slight difference is that / The true Kami acts to cause evil people / To depart from evil and saves them, / While for souls of peerless goodness, / The Kami causes them to accumulate / Goodness that is ever more perfect and complete. / Such differences as the above / Are daringly brought about / From the hearts of human beings themselves. / For all humans are vessels received / From heaven or hell, / Because they reside in the intermediate realm.

○

現代人はおもえらく 根底の国には最初より 一個の魔王厳在し 諸多の地獄を統轄し 堕ち来る精霊の罪悪を 制配なすと恐れられ 魔王は嘗て光明の 天人なりしも叛逆の 罪に問はれて衆族と 共に地獄に堕されし ものとの信仰昔より 深く心に刻まれて 真相覚れるものも無し 魔王もサタンもルシファーも 約言すれば地獄なり 殊に魔王と称ふるは 背後に位置せる地獄にて 此処に住めるを兇鬼と云ひ 兇悪最も甚だし 又前面に位せる

地獄をサタンと称ふなり サタンは魔王に比ぶれば さまで兇悪ならざれば これをば兇霊と称ふなり 又ルシファーと云ふ意味は バベルに属する曲にして 彼等の領土は久方の 天界までも拡がれり 故に一個の魔王ありて 地獄を統治し坐さざるは 地獄天界両界に 住める精霊に別ち無く 皆これ人の精霊より するものなるや明けし 天地創造の始めより 現代社会に至るまで 幾億万の人霊が 現実界に在る時に 皇大神の神格に 反抗したる度に比して

各自に一己の悪魔なる 業を積み積み邪鬼となり 地獄を造り出せし由 悟りて常に霊魂を 浄めて神の坐す国へ 昇り行く可く努むべし ああ惟神々々 御霊幸はへ坐しませよ。

○

愛と善との徳に充ち 信と真とに住みたまふ 真の神は罪悪と 虚偽に充ちたる人々に 仁慈と光栄の御面を 背けて之を排斥し 地獄に墜落させたまひ 邪悪に対して怒りまし 之をば罰し害なふと 各宗各派の教役者が 伝へ来りしものぞかし この言説は大神の 大御心を誤解せし 痴呆学者の言葉なり 神は如何なる罪人にも 面を背け排斥し 怒りて精霊を地獄界へ 決して堕すものならず その故如何と尋ぬれば 善と愛とは主の神の

珍の身体なればなり 善の自体は害悪を 決して加ふるものならず 愛と仁とは何人も 排斥すべき理由なし 万一神が罪人に 背き斥け怒りまさば 仁慈と愛に背反し その本性に戻りまし 神格自体に反く可し それ故神は何処までも 人の精霊に接しますや 善と仁慈と愛により 臨ませ玉はぬことは無し 五六七の神は人のため 善を思念し克く愛し 仁慈を施し玉ふのみ ああ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましませよ。

○

神より人に流れ来る 凡てのものは愛の善 信と真との光のみ 根底の国より来るものは 悪逆無道ばかりなり まことの神は人間を 悪より離れて善道に 立帰らせむと為し玉ふ 之に反して地獄界は 人をば悪に誘はむと 一心不乱に焦慮せり さは去りながら人間は 天界地獄両界の 間に介在なさざれば 人は何等の想念も 意義も自由も撰択も あらず身魂も亡ぶべし 人に善悪二方面 あるは正邪を平衡する 神の賜なればなり

神若し人の精霊に 面を背けたまひなば 悪事を心の儘になし 人たる所以は滅ぶべし 神より人に向ひまし 流れ来れる光明は 唯々善の徳のみぞ 然るに悪しき人間も 善良無比の身魂にも 皆その神徳に浴すなり 少しく相違の点あるは 真の神は悪人に 対して悪を離れしめ 救ひやらむと為したまひ 善良無比の身魂には 益々円満具足なる 善をば積ませたまふなり 以上の如き差異あるは 人間自身の心より 之をば敢て為すものぞ

凡ての人は天界や 地獄の所受の器にて 中有界に居ればなり。

§ 2

The people of Sekai receive the current of Tenkai and perform good, and by Jigoku they perform evil. Therefore, in the Ōmotoshinyu, it is shown that all jibutsu are actions performed by the seirei of Reikai; this is the yuen. Nevertheless, because people believe that all their waza proceed entirely from their own bodies, the evil they have done is all made their jiu, and is deeply kochiyaku within their shinchiu. Therefore, people take evil and kiyogi to be from jishin, and there is no yurai relating to kami. The evil and kiyogi contained within the mitama of a ningen are the jigoku within that person's heart. Both what is called jigoku and what is called evil are entirely the same dōitsu thing.

Because people are themselves the genin of all shiyoaku which they haū, even if they fall into jigoku and suffer, it is the shidai of going there of their own accord. By no means is the true Ōkami one who causes people to fall into jigoku, makes them suffer, and shiyobatsu them. How is this? You must realize that if ningen do not desire or love evil, the Lord Ōkami will cause them datsuri from jigoku, guide them to Tenkai, and will never cast people down into jigoku. Ah, kamunagara kamunagara, mitama sakisahemashimaseyo.

原文（近代文語）

○

世界の人は天界の 流れを受けて善を為し 地獄によりて悪を為す 故に大本神諭には 凡ての事物は霊界の 皆精霊の為す業と 示させ玉ふ所以なり されども人はその行為を 残らず己れの身よりすと 信ずる故にその為せる 悪は皆その自有となし 心中深く膠着せり それ故人は自身より 悪と虚偽との因となる 神の関する由来なし 人の身魂に包有せる 悪と虚偽とはその人の 心の中の地獄なり 地獄と云ふも悪と云ふも 皆同一の事ぞかし

人は自ら包有せる 諸悪の原因なる故に 地獄に墜ちて苦しむも 自ら赴く次第なり 決して真の大神は 地獄に堕し苦しめて 処罰し給ふものならじ 如何となれば人間が 悪を欲せず愛せずば 主の大神は地獄より 脱離せしめて天界へ 導き玉ひ人をして 地獄に投げやり給ふこと 決してなきを悟るべし ああ惟神々々 御霊幸へましませよ。
