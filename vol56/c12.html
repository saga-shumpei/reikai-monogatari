Chapter 12: 照 Gate 颪 [1442]

照門颪〔1442〕

§ 1

Kiudaukoji, accompanying Kerinahime, pointed toward the mansion of Okuniwake on Mount Terumon and broke into a song as they went along their way.

Kiudau: 'Though they say that change and impermanence are the ways of the world, truly mysterious is the ultimate end of human existence! Manifesting in the moon-realm of Sangokuichi, close to the capital of Haruna, dwelling deep within Mount Daiunzan, believing day and night in the teachings of the Brahman religion, and chosen for appointment as a religious instructor of Brahmanism; displaying achievements one after another, favored by the gracious discovery of Ōkuronushi, and at last bestowed with the honorable office of colonel; intending to slaughter the mansion of the sacred garden where Kamususanō has manifested, receiving the command of Ōkuronushi, and subordinate to the two generals Oniharuwake and Kumehiko,

with banners flying proudly, they left the moon-realm behind and pressed forward. Traversing myriad miles of mountains and fields with courageous hoofbeats, they advanced as far as the Forest of Floating Wood. When they were advancing with General Katahiko and General Kumehiko, having aligned their military ranks, as they approached Mount Kajika, they were shattered and routed by the Kototama of the ruling governor of Sankan, retreated to the encampment of Floating Wood, and once again dividing their entire army into two here, crossed the wide stream of the lion, constructed a temporary encampment at the skirts of Mount Victor, and threatened Victoria Castle. Terrified by the friendly military forces, they fled together with the general in a complete rout of the main army.

Though stepping out into the field of battle, trembling with fear even at the plumes of eulalia grass, and shivering across the vast moorland, they finally crossed Shimeji Slope. Descending at last the cliff-like slope, they constructed a permanent encampment within the impregnable mountain stronghold of Ikura, waiting for the time to come. Meanwhile, hearing once again the propaganda song, the general was easily persuaded to cast off his helmet by the party of Okuniwake—known far and wide as a divine general of the Sankan teachings—and abandoned his military post in Brahmanism, how pitiful that he suddenly transformed into a puritan of the path of the Sankan teachings! Let us too make our advances and retreats

the same, he thought, and stopping his office of colonel, changed his name to the ascetic Kiudau, washing his spirit in the clear waterfall of the land of Biku, firmly erecting the pillars of the shrine at the lower bedrock of Victor, and visiting every single day before the divine presence of the Great Deity who eternally abides and reigns in peace. Receiving the permission of the three men—Jidō, Michinuki, and Sodaukoji—they traversed the land of Fusa, making the light of God their staff through the wild fields of fierce beasts and poisonous snakes, bathing in the dew of the night road, until coming to the foot of the Elsina River.

At the moment they arrived, the moon in the vast sky gradually dimmed, and suddenly the voices of birds were heard, along with the rushing sound of the river rapids. As they descended, guided by these sounds, four figures were floating in the deep pool. Thinking they must not miss them, he stripped off his garment, held the conch shell in his mouth, plunged in with a splash, and rescued the four men and women onto the empty sand, blowing a mighty conch shell right into their ears. The divine grace immediately blew breath back into three of them, excepting only Shaaru. Breathing a sigh of relief at last, and fully exhorting the three men and women in the teachings of the imperial deities until they reached the foot of the Great Rock Valley, they rested their breath.

While transmitting the secret arts of the cross and the efficacy of the number-chants, the wicked and lawless villain Beru, realizing quickly that there was gold in my bosom, harbored evil thoughts and attacked to seize it. Heru and Beru, targeting my bosom from the very beginning, gradually and earnestly acted out a clumsy quarrel. The official Beru quickly escaped from this spot and went away. Nevertheless, the villain Heru, letting his heart be clouded by lust and greed, pursued our footsteps and advanced, parting the grassy fields, hoping to send away Kerinahime.

When the day had turned to twilight, he drew near to the shade of a poplar to rest his breath, and lo, a black shadow came once more! This truly must be the villain Beru, who had consulted with Heru and come to target my bosom, so I realized. Though I explained and admonished them in various miscellaneous ways about the true path, what could be done? The spirits of the two men, who had fallen headlong into the realm of hell, sought to accomplish their evil plots to the very end, and suddenly Brandishing their clubs, they struck down upon my crown. How could this be endured? Suddenly falling unconscious with a groan, as I wandered along the dream-road, the angel who rules over the divine realm of Takamanohara descended like a dove, and how grateful we are that you saved our two lives from peril! Ah, Amattsukami, Amattsukami! We two who have received the grace of God before our very eyes, no matter what sufferings may come—

Just as they took the original path again and were about to return, Rokuzau, intoxicated with wine, blocked their way once more, babbling on with all sorts of nonsense.

Just as they thought they could bear it no longer, an old hag came running swiftly from the opposite direction.

The party of five, feeling suspicious, hid themselves in the grassy area by the roadside, and just at that moment,

The hag stopped abruptly, beckoning with strange gestures, and said smoothly, 'This is the true palace of Miroku Kami, the heavenly and earthly foundation and the great pillar of the bedrock at the bottom. From now on, I shall teach all of you the truth of the heavenly paradise and the Pure Land, so follow me.'

She preached so earnestly. Thinking they should go and see whatever happened, they followed behind the hag, crossing a river and trailing along to the humble thatched hut of a mountain peak.

Hearing that this was the dwelling place of Takahime-sama, the standard-bearer of the Uranai teachings, was a shock to their hearts.

Feigning an unconcerned look, they were observing the situation when suddenly the sound of a conch shell was heard.

As they saw Kudō Koji, a shugenja of the Asechi teachings, appear to save all of us from our peril,

Suddenly the sound of water was heard, and the voices of small birds entered their ears refreshingly; even as they looked,

Breathing life once more, and being rescued up again,

They had finally returned this far.

'Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara! May the gracious Kami protect us along the path, covering every corner, and let us quickly return to our motherly home where we dwell, Kamunagara. We pray before the presence of Kami.'

So chanting, following behind Kudō Koji, tracing the night road, was Kerinahime.

Suddenly, the growls of fierce beasts were heard, echoing from front, back, left, and right all at once, shaking the mountain ravines. Kudō Koji chanted the heavenly numbers song aloud,

True observation, pure observation, vast and great wisdom observation, compassionate observation and loving-kindness observation, ever prayed for, ever looked up to. The stainless, pure light, the sun of wisdom breaks all darkness, able to subdue disastrous winds and fires, universally illuminating the secular world. The body of compassion is like a peal of thunder; the mind of loving-kindness is a wondrous, great cloud. It pours down the sweet dew of the Dharma rain, extinguishing the flames of affliction. In disputes and litigation, in the fears of the military camp, keep in mind the power of Kannon; all enemies will be scattered away. Wondrous voice, Kanzeon, Brahma voice, sea-tide voice, surpassing those worldly sounds; therefore you must constantly keep her in mind.

In thought after thought, raise no doubt. Kanzeon, the pure and holy one, in sufferings, afflictions, death, and disasters, can act as a refuge. Endowed with all merits, with compassionate eyes she views all living beings; the ocean of accumulated blessings is boundless. Therefore you should bow in reverence.

Chanting thus, matching it stride for stride, he blew the conch shell with all his might. The sound of the conch shell is that which dispels the evil spirits of the mountains and fields. Kerinahime, trembling at the voices of the fierce beasts, bit onto the waist of Kudō Koji, and with a crying voice followed Koji while reciting the sutras. After a while, the intense growls of the fierce beasts stopped dead. The clouds that had sealed the sky suddenly scattered, and as if washed clean, the summer moon began to shine low in mid-heaven. From this time on, Kerinahime's feeling of reverence, faith, and love toward Kudō Koji deepened all the more, causing waves to strike her heart from time to time and burning her chest.

と歌ひつつ求道居士の後に従つて、夜道を辿るのは、ケリナ姫であつた。

忽ち聞ゆる猛獣の唸り声、前後左右より一斉に山彦を轟かして聞え来る。求道居士は天の数歌を歌ひ上げ、

『真観清浄観 広大智慧観 悲観及慈観 常願常瞻仰 無垢清浄光 慧日破諸闇 能伏災風火 普明照世間 悲体戒雷震 慈意妙大雲 澍甘露法雨 滅除煩悩炎 諍訟経官処 怖畏軍陣中 念彼観音力 衆怨悉退散 妙音観世音 梵音海潮音 勝彼世間音 是故須常念

念々勿生疑 観世音浄聖 於苦悩死厄 能為作依怙 具一切功徳 慈眼視衆生 福聚海無量 是故応頂礼』

と念じ乍ら、負ず劣らず、力一杯法螺貝を吹き立てた。法螺貝の声は山野の邪気を払ふものである。ケリナ姫は猛獣の声に戦慄し、求道居士の腰に喰ひつき、泣き声になつて居士に従ひ経文を誦唱して居る。暫くにしてさしも激しき猛獣の唸り声はピタリと止まつた。天を封じて居た雲は俄に散つて夏の月は洗ひ出した様に、中天低く輝き始めた。是よりケリナ姫は何となく求道居士を尊信愛慕するの念益々深くなり、ハートに折々波を打たせ胸を焦がすに至りたり。
