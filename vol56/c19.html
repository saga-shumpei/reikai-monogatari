Chapter 19: 痴漢 [1449]

痴漢〔1449〕

§ 1

The master of the mansion, Okuniwake, lies stretched out upon the sofa, suffering and drawing breath with the utmost difficulty.

Two nursing attendants forgot sleep and food, giving themselves over entirely to his care without a single distraction.

Okunihime entered, accompanied by Austin, Michihiko, and Wax, and spoke:

Hime: 'Dannasama, please rejoice.

Thanks to Michihiko-sama, the sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, the divine treasure of the Niyoihotsushiyu has returned.

Please look upon this.'

So saying, she untied the wrapping and held it out before his eyes.

Okunihime gazed upon the jewel with illness-fatigued and weakened eyes, smiled faintly, brought her palms together, and was choked with tears of gratitude.

And saying only a single word of thanks, she was unable to utter any subsequent speech.

This was because, atop the extremity of his weakness, he was overcome by an excess of joy.

Michihiko drew close to the side of the patient and spoke:

Michihiko: 'Now that the Goshinpau has returned in this manner, once more by the grace of Kamisama, surely Kerinahime-sama too will return within a short while.

Please set your mind at ease.'

When he comforted him with gentle words, Okunihime joined her palms together, and upon hearing the testimony that her daughter would return shortly, she revived slightly in spirit and spoke:

Okuni: 'Is my daughter returning?

That is truly arigata.

After all, I must set out on a journey this time.

At least by that time, I had thought to restore the lost Niyoihotsushiyu to its origin and to gaze upon my daughter's face even for a single glance in my lifetime before leaving this world, but having grown so weak as this, my life will surely not continue for even three more days.

If possible, I wish to be drawn toward her even a moment sooner.'

Michihiko spoke:

Michihiko: 'She will return before long.

Kamisama spoke thus close to my ear.

However, since it must not become a hindrance to your illness, we shall withdraw.'

Okuni: 'Please rest at your complete leisure.'

So he greeted him with a faint voice.

Austin, the steward, drew close to the side of the patient and spoke:

Austin: 'Dannasama, please pull yourself together.

Moreover, it was my son Wax who stole and concealed the jewel of the Niyoihotsushiyu.

I have caused you grave worry and have no words of apology.

When I had resolved to cut open this wrinkled belly to offer my apologies, I was stopped by Okusama, and though I prolonged a worthless life for a short while, if by any chance your lifespan comes to an end and you make your change of realm, I will surely accompany you.

Please ensure that the bond of master and servant is not severed anywhere, no matter what.'

Okuniwake nodded slightly.

Michihiko took Wax by the hand and returned to his own living quarters.

Entrusting the care of Okuniwake to the two nursing attendants and Austin, Okunihime came once more to Michihiko's quarters, wore an anxious look, and spoke:

Hime: 'Michihiko-sama, I have caused you nothing but worry and have no words of apology, but will the master truly not recover?'

Michihiko: 'I am sorry to say, but it will surely be in vain.

However, even if the physical body passes away, the spirit will remain lively and youthful, performing great activities for the sake of Kamisama in the spiritual realm, so do not worry.

For a person, resignation is of the utmost importance.'

Hime: 'Yes, arigata.

I am already prepared.

However, there is yet another matter of concern, and might I trouble you to listen for a brief moment?'

Michihiko: 'I know not what it may be, but speak of it for a moment.'

Hime: 'The truth of the matter is that she who is called my daughter, Princess Davis, for the space of twenty-one days ending today, to the waterfall of Umbrac where even by day people scarcely go, in order that the whereabouts of the jewel might be made known, that her father's illness might be healed, and lastly that her younger sister's whereabouts might be ascertained, taking upon herself her delicate female form has gone to and returned from a two-ri road every single night, and always returns at the break of dawn, but today, for whatever reason, she has not yet returned.

Could she perchance have fallen into the waterfall basin and cast away her life?

Or else, could she have been slain by a fierce beast?

A sudden agitation grips my breast and I am beside myself with anxiety.'

Michi: 'Do not worry at all. In less than half an hour, your two daughters will be gathered together, sent back safely by a single shūgenjiya, and will return without mishap. There is no mistake about it.'

Hime: 'Is that so? If my two daughters return, I shall have no worries left. Oh, Namu Daiji Daijizaiten-sama, please, I beg of you to let me see the faces of my two daughters even a moment earlier, while my husband's life still remains.'

So saying, she wept and poured forth her prayers.

Michi: 'Here now, Wakkusu-sama, you have committed a most atrocious and evil deed, but since this was done by your secondary guardian deity, it has been decided that Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi shall look upon it with Minaho and Kikinaho, and that the matter will be settled quietly behind closed doors, so you must understand this from now on.'

Wakkusu: 'Yes, I am most grateful. Truly I have committed a fault without excuse. If you will now save me from my crime, considering my life as already gone, I shall perform any manner of service and most certainly repay this great grace. Tell me, Okusama, will you truly pardon me?'

Hime: 'Though you are an unpardonable criminal, thanks to the arrangements of Michihiko-sama, I shall let the matter be settled in secret. From now on, you would do well to understand this clearly. To cause anxiety to a single aged parent, truly you are an unfilial person. Not only your parent, but you have also troubled and distressed us, husband and wife, and even our daughters, so henceforth you must surely restrain yourself.'

Wakkusu: 'Yes, I am most grateful. From now on, I shall serve you, treating you as my parent with all my heart.'

Hime: 'Here now, Wakkusu, do you not have a parent? You ought to serve me as your master. To speak of serving me as a parent, is that not somewhat comical?'

Wakkusu: 'In terms of righteousness, you are my master. However, in terms of affection, I thought of you as a parent and thus spoke somewhat improperly. But since you have pardoned me, won't you please make me your child? The truth of the matter is that Ekisu and Heruman both stole it, but I used various painstaking efforts to make them confess the whereabouts of the jewel, and worked for the sake of the household. Wishing to save the two persons, I told my father that I had stolen it, but the reality is that Heruman and Ekisu both stole it. Concealing that from my father, I gave them money, made them drink sake, and made them confess, and in that manner obtained the Nyoi Hōju. You may not remember, but did you not proclaim throughout the entire household that whoever found and brought the jewel of the Nyoi Hōju would be made the adopted husband of Princess Debisu? That being so, I believe I have the qualifications to be adopted as you commanded.'

Hime: 'As for that, just as you say, it was said that whoever found and brought the jewel of the Nyoi Hōju would be made an adopted son. However, you are an only child with a single parent, a person who must inherit the household of the steward, so that cannot be done. You cannot treat the house of your ancestors lightly, after all.'

Wakkusu: 'Nay, such worry is unnecessary. If I become the adopted son, three or five children may be born between Princess Debisu and myself, so wouldn't it be fine to take one of them and let him inherit my house?'

Hime: 'I say, Michihiko-sama, he is saying such things, but what would be best for us to do?'

Michihiko stared intently at Wakkusu's face, keeping his mouth closed in the shape of the kana character 'he'. With a fearful expression and a voice trembling slightly,

Wakkusu: 'I say, Senden-shi-sama, I beg you to intercede so that you will make me an adopted son just as promised, since I am the discoverer of the jewel.'

Michi: 'Here now, Wakkusu, are you trying to blind us, or do you intend to deceive a husband and wife? The words you just spoke are all falsehoods, aren't they? You concealed the household treasures, distressed the husband and wife, and plotted to become the husband of Princess Debisu out of sheer desperation, did you not? Michihiko is not a person to be fooled by such things.'

Wakkusu: 'Good heavens, absolutely not! It troubles me greatly to be so misunderstood. To treat this loyal vassal, who took such pains to secure the treasure for the sake of the family, as a wicked person makes no sense to me from the very root. I beg you to reconsider once more.'

Michi: 'Silence. If you say such things, I shall show no mercy. Shall I bind you hand and foot, place you in a wicker cage, and send you off to the capital of Haruna? Moreover, no matter how much you are infatuated with Princess Debisu, what do you intend to do if the princess herself dislikes you? Do you think you would find even a loveless marriage pleasing? Rather than resembling the family steward, are you not uttering things whose meaning cannot be understood?'

Wakkusu: 'You intend to intimidate us, obstruct our romance, and later sneak in yourself as an adopted son, is that your design? Such a thing, this Wakkusu has read down to the very bottom of his belly, clear as day.'

Michi: 'Well, this is astonishing, immensely troublesome, what a rude thing you are saying. We are missionaries of Misogaku, our bodies must advance to a certain place upon receiving an important message, the thought of taking women along never even crossed our minds. Is it not rather rude to measure our hearts with your heart?'

Wakkusu: 'Those who are called missionaries often say such things. With your mouths you speak splendidly as if you detest women, but once you turn to the shadows, because by origin you are mere human beings, it is no good at all. If you want Princess Debisu, say clearly that you want her.'

Hime: 'Hey, Wakkusu, what a rude thing you are saying. There is no doubt that you are the thief of the jewel. Is your own parent not testifying to it at present?'

Wakkusu became desperate, tucked up his skirts, left the place, and dashed out through the front gate at full speed. Princess Oguni clapped her hands, invited Eru, and ordered him to pursue Wakkusu. Eru, a flustered man, did not even hear to the end and said, 'Yes, understood,' and once again bounded out of this place, making the ground rumble as he rushed out Dondon-don through the outer gate, heading straight to where the road turned at a right angle, poking his head forward and running sideways, the moment he ran, a large ox was tied to a persimmon tree at the roadside which was not even very wide. With a thump, he gave a headbutt to the ox's rear. The ox was startled, and at the moment it kicked with a pon, Eru fell down flat with an un. The ox shook its rump two or three times, stepped heavily right on the edge of Eru's testicles, and with force twisted them with a guutsu. Eru raised shrieks of kyyaku-kyyaku. Travelers who happened to pass by and people from the neighborhood gathered in swarms, helped Eru, carried him into a nearby house, and when they asked about the circumstances, Eru, while frowning with his face, spoke thus:

Eru: 'Everyone, the treasure of the Nyoi-hōju has been obtained. And when I asked about the circumstances, as a reward for Wakkusu finding the whereabouts of the jewel, it is that he is to become the husband of Princess Debisu. Furthermore, Lord Oguniwake-sama is critically ill, and we do not know at what moment he will draw his last breath. Most likely by now he may have expired, this is terrible. Oh everyone, make haste even for a moment, each of you spread the word through the town and go into the castle to offer condolences.'

Thus, even though he had not yet died, he spread the rumor beforehand, assuming that he had died with good arrangement. The old and young who heard this became postmen of quick delivery running back and forth, one after another, and went about proclaiming:

'The jewel of the Nyoi-hōju has been obtained. And Oguniwake has changed his fief, and Wakkusu has been decided as the husband of Princess Debisu.'

Thus, without leaving a single house, quite politely they went about proclaiming it.

The town at the foot of Mount Terumon suddenly began to bustle with noise, people changing their clothes and going to the mansion to offer condolences came and went without stopping, and it came to be as if a great disturbance had suddenly broken out. Eru had the edge of his testicle torn off by the ox's hoof, his fever rose more and more throughout his body, and he kept muttering only delirium saying, 'I am dead, I am dead.'

Some old and young wept upon suddenly hearing of the death of Oguniwake, others shouted in surprise that the fool of a son Wakkusu was to become the husband of Princess Debisu, and others rejoiced that the jewel of the Nyoi-hōju had returned, so that the shrine town at the foot of Mount Terumon was filled entirely with this rumor. Impatient men already erected banners and began to go around the crossroads shouting, 'Mourning the demise of the divine official Oguniwake,' or 'The re-appearance of the Nyoi-hōju,' or 'Celebrating the marriage of Princess Debisu with Wakkusu,' shouting wassho wassho.

To such a place came the cool voice of propaganda songs heard from the edge of town. This voice was none other than the seeker of the Way returning while helping Princess Debisu and Princess Kerina.

姫『これ、ワツクス、何と云ふ失礼な事を申すのだ。玉盗人はお前に違ひない。現在お前の親が証明して居るのぢやないか』

ワツクスは自棄糞になり、尻をクレツと捲つて此場を後に、一目散に表門を潜つて駆け出した。小国姫は手を拍つてエルを招きワツクスの後を追跡せよと命じた。狼狽者のエルは皆まで聞かず、『ハイ、承知しました』と又もや此処を飛び出し地響きさせ乍らドンドンドンと門外へ駆け出し、道の鍵の手になつた所を、頭を先につき出し体を横にして走る途端に、あまり広くもない道端の柿の木に大牛が繋いであつた。其牛の尻にドンと、頭突をかました。牛は驚いてポンと蹴つた拍子にエルはウンと許り倒れた。牛は二つ三つ尻を振つて再びエルの睾丸の端をグツと踏み、力を入れてグーツと捻た。エルはキヤツキヤツと悲鳴を挙げてゐる。通りかかつた旅人や近所の家からドヤドヤと集まつて来てエルを助け、傍の或家に担ぎ込み、様子を聞けばエルは顔を顰め乍ら、

エル『皆さま、如意宝珠のお宝が手に入りました。そして様子を聞けばワツクスが玉の所在を探した御褒美に、デビス姫さまの婿になると云ふ事ですよ。それから小国別様は御危篤で何時息を引きとられるか分りませぬ。大方今頃は絶命れたかも知れませぬ、大変で厶います。何皆さま、一時も早う各自に町内を触れまはり城内に悔みに行つて下さい』

とまだ死んでも居ないのに、手まはしよく死んだものと仮定して吹聴した。之を聞いた老若男女は次から次へと、尻はし折り駄賃とらずの郵便配達となつて、

『如意宝珠の玉が手に入つた。そして小国別が国替へをなさつて、ワツクスがデビス姫様の婿にきまつた』

と一軒も残らず、御丁寧に布令まはつた。

テルモン山の麓の町は俄にガヤガヤと騒ぎ出し、衣裳を着替へて館へ悔みに行くもの引きもきらず、俄に大騒動が起つた様になつて来た。エルは睾丸の端を牛の爪にむしりとられ、益々体中に熱が高まつて『死んだ死んだ』と囈言ばかり囀つて居る。

俄に小国別の訃を聞いて泣く老若男女もあれば、馬鹿息子のワツクスがデビス姫の婿になるげなと驚いて触れる奴もあり、如意宝珠の玉が帰つたと喜ぶものもあり、テルモン山の麓の宮町は此噂で持ちきりとなつた。気の早い男は早くも幟を立て「神司小国別の御他界を弔ふ」とか、「如意宝珠再出現」とか、「デビス姫ワツクスとの御結婚を祝す」とか云ふ長い幟を立てて、ワツシヨ　ワツシヨと辻々を廻り初めた。

かかる所へ宣伝歌の声涼しく町外れの方から聞えて来た。此声は求道居士がデビス姫、ケリナ姫を助けて帰り来るにぞありける。
