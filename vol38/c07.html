Chapter 7: Fire 事蚊 [1044]

火事蚊〔1044〕

§ 1

It is said that when men are prosperous they overcome Heaven, and when Heaven is settled it controls men; although Kiraku abandoned his own life and family to devote his full power to the service of the Saniha, because the practitioners—some twenty or more people who lacked common sense from the beginning—had become active, there was no way to settle the situation. Having no discernment of right and wrong, nor of reason and unreason, and being composed entirely of former believers of the Konkuōkyō, they were a coagulation of superstition and blind faith, so they would not listen to what the Saniha said at all. Moreover, Kamugakari is a strange thing; if a believer of the Konkuōkyō practices, the god of the Konkuōkyō possesses them. All of them call themselves Konjin. If a believer of the Tenrikyō practices, they appear by announcing the names of the ten pillars of gods. If a believer of the Myōreikyōkuwai practices, they appear by announcing the names of the gods enshrined in the Myōreikyōkuwai. Besides these, various spirits appear by announcing the names of the gods or Buddhas who are the main enshrined deities of each sect. Since the practitioners at the Kamitani training ground were all believers of the Konkuōkyō, they all announced the names of Konjin, such as the Ushibito Konjin, the Tatsumi Konjin, the Tenchi Konjin, the Tsuchido Konjin, and the Ishi Konjin. There were also many secondary guardian deities who appeared under the names of the Princess of the Dragon Palace or other dragon gods.

Most of the people who come to practice at Ōmoto today have received secondary or higher education, so such derailed and low-level spirits do not possess them as they did at that time. However, if one speaks of the Kamugakari at the beginning of Ōmoto, that is, around the thirty-second year of Meiji, it was truly chaotic and extreme, a state just like that of an insane asylum. Furthermore, due to the schemes of evil spirits, the person acting as Saniha was often placed in a dangerous position, and it was not something that could be fully described by pen or mouth. Although these are things that people who do not know the circumstances of the spiritual world at all would find impossible to believe even if they read this story, facts must be presented as facts, for they will not serve as a reference for future researchers of this path; therefore, I have decided to dictate it as it was, without wrapping or hiding anything, and without reserve or hesitation toward anyone.

The time was the thirty-second year of Meiji, when the colors of autumn were gradually deepening. In the great hall of the Kinmeikai, led by the usual Fukushima, Murakami, Shikata Shunzo, Shiomi, and Kuroda, there was an incessant assault from the evil spirit world day and night. Not only did they not listen to the various divine instructions of the Founder or the Saniha, Kiraku, but they even avoided the Founder and Kiraku. Fukushima and his like formed a separate faction, occupied the back room of the front hall, and continued strange Kamugakari together with the three practitioners, Shikata, Shiomi, and Kuroda.

Fukushima: 'Father, it has been a long time since I last saw you.'

Fukushima: 'Ah, was it you, my child? I wanted to see you... I wanted to see you... Ah, are you my wife?'

Fukushima: 'Is it my husband? When Lord Ushitora no Konjin descended into the world, I was also cast down to the same place, and parents, children, and siblings were scattered far and wide. Now that the time has come, thanks to Lord Ushitora no Konjin, I have been permitted to meet my husband, parents, children, and siblings after such a long time. Oh, how grateful, how precious, oh-i, oh-i, oh-i, an-an-an.'

They were performing a scene of lamentation. Unable to bear the excessive madness, the Founder, who was usually very patient, had no choice but to sweep out the Kamugakari of Fukushima with a broom.

Founder: 'You must be a spirit protecting the Konkuōkyō. I have known from the very beginning that you are borrowing the physical body of Fukushima to disturb this Ōmoto. Now that it has come to this, I cannot permit it, so leave at once.'

Having been scolded severely, Fukushima, whose body was half-possessed, became greatly enraged.

Fukushima: 'What kind of Founder are you, not being able to distinguish Fukushima Toranosuke, who is possessed by this true Lord Ushitora no Ōkonjin? You have swept out the living shrine of Lord Ushitora no Konjin with a broom, how precious. Also, since Ueda is possessed by a piece of junk god like Komatsubayashi, it is troublesome that you cannot distinguish this magnificent Lord God. What is the purpose of a Saniha? There is a limit even to saying you do not understand. Come, come, all you possessed ones, from now on, I will show you whether the divine power of the Ushitora no Ōkonjin, born in the Year of the Ox, is stronger, or if the divine power of Deguchi and Ueda is stronger, by distinguishing the white from the black. Follow this side and come to Kamitani. If Toranosuke loses, I will follow you, but if this side wins, I will make Deguchi Nao and Ueda follow the true Lord Ushitora no Konjin and use them as servants. Today is the critical moment of a splendid battle. All you possessed ones, go to Kamitani as soon as possible. Because Deguchi and Ueda will not repent, the god will burn Deguchi's house to ashes for the sake of their awakening and repentance. After that, the whole town will be the same. Oh, it is truly a pitiful thing. It is no easy matter for a person to build even one house, and the god cannot help but feel sorry for them. This is also because Deguchi Nao is headstrong and Ueda will not repent.'

With a voice that echoed in all directions, he roared and ranted. No matter how much Kiraku explained the difference between true and false Kamugakari to Fukushima, it was to no avail; declaring, 'I am the true Ushitora no Konjin, what could the Saniha of Uheda possibly know?' he puffed out his shoulders, stuck out his elbows, and in a haughty manner, led the Yakuin and the entire group of Kamugakari, running with the speed of Idaten for over one Ichiri, and headed straight for the Shiugiyōba in Uhedani.

Leaving the Kyōso, Kiraku, and Sumiko at the Hiromahe, all the Yakuin and Kamugakari, firmly believing the false words of the Jiyashin who had possessed Fukushima, went off to Uhedani. Following the command of the Kyōso, Kiraku went to the training hall at the house of a man named Shōhō Isaemon in Uhedani after two or three hours had passed, accompanied only by Nakamura Kikuko, the wife of Nakamura Takezō. There, he found that the Yakuin, the Kamugakari, and the villagers, regardless of age or gender, had all followed Fukushima and climbed up the high Fudōsan, leaving only Kuroda Kiyoko and Nozaki Atsusaburō behind to look after the training hall. Kuroda was possessed by the spirit of a wicked fox, and without even noticing that Kiraku had arrived, she was babbling away to herself about something. Nozaki was sitting beside her with both hands on the floor, bowing meekly like a stone guardian dog. As soon as he saw Kiraku's face, Nozaki was startled and whispered into Kuroda Kiyoko's ear, whereupon Kuroda immediately fell backward.

Kuroda: 'Has Uheda come? Listen well. I am none other than the awe-inspiring Lord Susanoo-no-Mikoto. Because you cannot repent, I shall, though it pains me, burn the Ayabe Kinmeikai to ashes. Why have you come here? Why do you not return to Ayabe at once and attend to the firefighting? This is no time to be dawdling; is this not the moment of one in a thousand?'

She babbled on without end. Kiraku suddenly spoke.

Kiraku: 'Hey, wild fox, what are you spewing? As if such a thing could ever happen. Hey, wild fox, reveal your true form!'

As he clasped his hands behind his back and exerted spiritual power with a shout of 'Un!', Kiyoko immediately dropped to all fours.

'Kon-kon!'

Crying out thus, she dashed off toward the mountain behind the house at full speed. Nozaki was astonished and chased after her, and when he finally caught her in a valley about three chō away and brought her back, Kiyoko appeared to have regained her senses.

Kuroda: 'Ah, Uheda-sensei, I have done a truly terrible thing. From now on, I will no longer listen to Fukushima-daisensei. I was too arrogant, and so the fox of Fudōsan possessed me. Ah, how shameful and regrettable.'

She hid her face with her sleeve. Kiraku replied.

Kiraku: 'Do you think I would be deceived by such a thing? Do not tell lies; that is a mere excuse to escape the moment. If I glare with the eyes of a Saniha, there can be no mistake. You old fox of the four-legged shrine!'

As he glared at her, she once again cried, 'Kon-kon!' and fled at full speed toward Fudōsan.

A short while later, the aforementioned Yūsuke-jiisan came running before Kiraku.

Yūsuke: 'Uheda-sensei, I hear you have scolded the Kamugakari-san once again. Just now, Lord Susanoo-no-Mikoto possessed Kuroda-san, and she has gone up the mountain, very angry. The burning of the great Hiromahe by divine punishment is, in short, because of your stubbornness, Sensei. Even though I have been doing my utmost, apologizing and praying to Ushitora no Konjin-sama and the Kamugakari-sama to turn the great calamity into a small one, you, Sensei, call the wonderful Kamugakari-san, whom the three great Ōkami-sama have deigned to possess, a wild fox. Because of this, the Ōkami-sama are beyond furious and say they will not forgive you this time. Sensei, it is the request of my life, so please apologize to the god of Kuroda-san right now. It would be unbearable if the great Hiromahe of Ayabe and the whole town were to suffer a great calamity...'
