Chapter 21: 凄い権幕 [1058]

凄い権幕〔1058〕

§ 1

Since the outbreak of the Nichiro Sensō in Meiji Sanjifushichinen, the group including Shikata Heizau, Nakamura Takezau, Murakami Fusanosuke, Kinoshita Keitarau, Tanaka Zenkichi, Honda Sakujirau, Kojima Torakichi, Yasuda Shiyōjirau, Shikata Yohei, and Shihomi Jun suddenly became aggressive; while Kiraku was confined to a six-mat detached room, hiding away to investigate the Kojiki and writing down news of the Reikai, Nakamura Takezau arrived with a swaggering gait accompanied by two or three officers. And facing Kiraku,

Nakamura: 'President-san, how do you feel? Has not the great ambition begun? If you do not reform quickly, the world will be completely ruined within this year. Does it not appear in the Ofudesaki that another war will start beginning with Russia? Hmph, do you still say the Ofudesaki is wrong? Even though the Ofudesaki says to make spiritual studies three parts and the Ofudesaki seven parts, you do not read the Ofudesaki at all, so you probably understand nothing even though the war has started with Russia. Do you know what will happen from now on? Apologize quickly to the Kyōsu-sama.'

He spoke in a domineering manner, as if giving a lecture. It was exactly the third day after the Imperial Proclamation of War had been issued. Thereupon, Kiraku brought out a book titled as his own essay and showed it to Nakamura,

Kiraku: 'I have known such things for a long time. Look at this. It is clearly written in my own hand in January of Meiji Sanjūgonen that the Nichiro Sensō would break out from February of Meiji Sanjifushichinen.'

When he spread it open and thrust it forward to show him, Nakamura made a strange face,

Nakamura: 'No matter how much you write with such square characters and a foreign soul, such things do not pass for anything here. The Ofudesaki of the Kyōsu, who has no learning and knows nothing but possesses only divine power, is what is precious.'

He said this derisively, as if rubbing a nose with a stick. Thereupon Kiraku said,

Kiraku: 'Did you not say in Meiji Sanjūsannen that the Nichiro Sensō would break out this year, and in the thirty-fourth year, and the thirty-fifth year, every year saying that the Nichiro Sensō would break out this year, the reconstruction would begin, and things beyond imagination would occur? Even such a prophecy will eventually hit the mark.'

Then Nakamura became overbearing,

Nakamura: 'I did not say it myself. It is because it appeared in the Ofudesaki of the most gracious Ushitora no Konjin Henshō Nanshi Deguchi no Kami-sama... that the reconstruction begins this year, there will be a war with Russia... that is why I said so. In short, President-san, are you dismissing what the Kyōsu-han said and the words of God? It is because your reformation was delayed that the Divine Plan has been delayed. If the President-san had been able to reform in Meiji Sanjūsannen, God would have performed the reconstruction in the thirty-third year; if the reformation had been done in the thirty-fourth year, it was already decided three thousand years ago in the Divine Plan to perform the reconstruction in the thirty-fourth year. While you yourself have delayed your reformation, caused trouble for God, postponed the Divine Plan, and made the people of the world suffer, do you dare to say that God told a lie? If you say such things, we cannot have you stay in Ayabe. No matter what is said, since the war between Russia and Japan has begun, Japan will surely go to the point where it seems ninety-nine percent impossible to win. When it reaches that point, from the Ōmoto of Ayabe, the soul of Ushitora no Konjin Henshō Nanshi shall appear as the Great Deguchi no Kami, strike the finishing blow, overturn the three thousand worlds, rule the world in Great Peace, and thereafter make it the world of Miroku, the world of the Pine; therefore, if you do not reform quickly, you will be a hindrance to the Divine Plan. Those who hinder the service of the reconstruction and restoration of the three thousand worlds shall have their deeds written down for ten thousand kalpas and all eternity as a warning, and their souls shall be cast down to the Root-Country and the Bottom-Country... so God has shown in the Ofudesaki. If the President-san's reformation is delayed by one day, the people of the world will suffer for one extra day; it is written in the Ofudesaki that because your soul is of an extremely evil karmic nature, reformation in all things is the first priority.'

と威丈高になつて、説諭するやうな気分で喋り立た。丁度宣戦の詔勅が下つた三日目である。そこで喜楽は自分の随筆と題した一冊の書物を出して、中村に示し、

He continued to press the attack, stringing together one digression after another. The Chairman held back his amusement, and

Kiraku: 'If there were such a person who could save the people of the Sanzensekai one day earlier just because he himself had undergone Kaishin one day earlier—whether for good or for evil—that would be fine. But what possible relation does it have to the world if one of us individuals undergoes something? Do not say such incomprehensible things. It is because you say such things that Ayabe’s Ōmoto is reviled by the world as a den of Kichigahi or a gathering of Meishinka, and is cast aside. I am in trouble if you do not think a little, am I not?'

When he said this, Nakamura pursed his lips and

Nakamura: 'Be silent, Kaichō-san. No matter how well you disguise yourself, it is useless. Are you so concerned about being spoken ill of by the Sekai? What a small-minded Sensei you are. That is why the Kami say that the Henjiyōniyoshi is the opposition. Since the whole world has become clouded, a dark world where one must walk carrying a lantern in broad daylight, is it truly the way of a teaching to be praised by the people of the world? It is a mechanism where you are thoroughly reviled and then thoroughly improved. Because you are too obsessed with square characters and foreign teachings, your spirit has become completely twisted, and you cannot understand the Ofudesaki. Listen to the Ofudesaki for a moment.'

He shouted this, and began to read the seven or eight volumes of the Ofudesaki that he had respectfully brought on a sanbō.

Kiraku began to have a headache and felt so unwell that he could not help it. Thereupon,

Kiraku: 'If it is that Ofudesaki, I have heard it many times, so you do not need to let me hear it. I know everything.'

No sooner had he said this than

Nakamura: 'Hey, Komatsubayashi, is the Ofudesaki painful? Now, I will drive you out with an Ofudesaki attack. Come, quickly, Komatsubayashi, listen to this Ofudesaki and leave the body of the Kaichō-san at once. And after that, the Kon no Konjin-sama of the spirit of the Henjiyōniyoshi shall settle there. The body of the Kaichō-san is not a body for a four-legged creature like you to enter. Hey, will you not leave?'

He shouted. Murakami and Shikata Heizō from the side said,

Murakami: 'Hey, Komatsubayashi, why are you dawdling? Will you not quickly jump out of the Kaichō’s body and settle into Naitō of Sonobe? It is the end of the year for the evil spirit.'

They attacked from three sides. Shikata Heizō pursed his lips and

Shikata: 'Hey, Komatsubayashi-san, would it not be better if you also underwent Kaishin to a reasonable degree? Because your Kaishin is not accomplished, the Kyōso-sama is suffering hardships that are unbearable. The officials and believers are worried every day, and the people of the world are suffering greatly, are they not? Now, quickly return to the Inari of Suruga. This is not a place where low-lifes like Inari gather. This is the Takamagahara of Ryūmonkan, where only spirits of crystal-clear sincerity gather to perform their duties.'

Groaning, they clasped their hands and performed chinkon from three sides. Since there was nothing else to be done, the Chairman said,

Kiraku: 'If that is the case, there is no help for it. Komatsubayashi will leave as of today. And I shall have the Kon no Konjin-sama enter in his place and perform the duties.'

When he said this, Takezō said,

Nakamura: 'Hey, Heizō-san, be careful. You might be deceived again just like in Sonobe. A creature called an evil spirit is stubborn to the very end, so if you are careless, you will be made a fool of. Komatsubayashi has not really undergone Kaishin, has he? Is he not smiling in a haughty manner? Hey, Komatsubayashi, even if you spout such sweet words and think you can use the Kaichō’s body, I, Nakamura, will not permit it. Now, show me some proof. Unless you clearly demonstrate that you have finally left the Kaichō’s body and apologize to the Kyōso, I will not forgive you no matter what. Now that it has come to this, even if it takes three days or ten days, I will not stop until I have thrown you out of the Kaichō’s body.'

He stomped his feet and let out a roar of defiance, but the more he tried to persuade them with a thousand words, the more they opposed him, until the situation became utterly unmanageable. Just then, Hisako Fukushima arrived from Yagi, offered her greetings to Kyōso-sama, and having finished, hurried over to Kiraku.

Hisako: 'My, Heizau-san, the Fudesaki is truly something to be feared, is it not? Has war not finally broken out with Rokoku, and has the Kuwaichiyō-san still not undergone Gokaishin?'

Nakamura: 'Is that you, Fukushima-han? I am glad you have come. The Fudesaki of Kami-sama is indeed something to be feared. Even though such a Gotaimau has begun, Komatsubayashi still persists and will not leave the body of the Kuwaichiyō-san, so all the Yakuin are gathered now to offer Setsuyu, but he is quite stubborn and will not listen to us. Please, you too, try to say something to him.'

He was attempting to enlist the support of the eloquent Fukushima. Thinking it would be terrible to be caught by such a shrill woman, he put on a face as if he were going to the lavatory, slipped out the back door, and fled at full speed to the house of Kazō Ōtsuki in Nishimachi.

Seeing Kiraku running away, the two, Kazō Ōtsuki and Okome-san, laughed.

Ōtsuki: 'Kuwaichiyō-san, has another quarrel started?'

Kiraku: 'Hisako from Yagi just arrived, and she is so noisy that I ran away.'

Upon hearing this, Kazō Ōtsuki said,

Ōtsuki: 'Haha, is it that same Komatsubayashi-san again? Well, stay here and rest at your leisure until Hisako returns to Yagi. That old woman of Shingū is also quite something; talking about Tatekae and Tatenaoshi, that is the very thing I find disagreeable. Because the old woman spouts that Kazō Ōtsuki is the spirit of Shuten Dōji of Ōeyama, everyone treats me like an ogre, and whether it makes me angry or not, if it were any other old woman, I, this Kazō, would not stand for it, but since she is the mother of Okome and Denkichi, I am being patient. Truly, it is a gathering of nothing but fools. More than that, cook some beef here and eat it. Since Hisako, Heizau, or Nakamura will surely come looking for you, it would be amusing to make them suffer with the smell of beef.'

As he happened to be running a beef shop, he brought about three hundred me of high-quality meat from the store and began to simmer and eat it in the detached room at the back. Just then, Nakamura arrived.

Nakamura: 'Ōtsuki-san, by any chance, is the Kuwaichiyō-san here?'

He was asking from the direction of the back door. Since Kazō was a little hard of hearing, he did not understand at first, but Okome-san replied,

Okome: 'Is that you, Nakamura-han? Well, come in and eat some beef. I am just now recommending beef to the Kuwaichiyō and having him eat it. It is far more sensible than eating oak-nut dumplings or porridge made from potato leaves. Since Onisaburō-han has come to the home of a couple whose spirits have the connection of the Shuten Dōji of Ōeyama and the serpent, we are eating beef in accordance with our spirits, so why don't you join the ranks of the ogres too?'

She was teasing him. Nakamura, holding his nose and grimacing, entered and said,

Nakamura: 'Excuse me, Ōtsuki-san. You have taken the eldest daughter of Kyōso-han as your wife and have been blessed with fine children, yet is it acceptable to encourage the Kuwaichiyō-san to do such a thing? Feeding him four-legged animals—is that not going too far?'

He shouted with dissatisfaction. Laughing, Kazō said,

Ōtsuki: 'In this world, it is wise to eat sweet things even for a day and do as one pleases. You too should undergo Gokaishin, eat some beef, build up your strength, engage in the antique business to make money, wear fine clothes, and eat sweet things. No matter how much you talk about virtue, if only one of you becomes a saint, there is no one who will take you seriously. The Kuwaichiyō-san is, as expected, someone who understands well. Where is there anyone who would say such foolish things as not being able to eat the meat of a four-legged animal in this day and age? You are quite the fool.'
