Chapter 17: Journey 装 [1054]

旅装〔1054〕

§ 1

On the first day of the eighth month of the thirty-third year of Meiji, Kiraku received a telegram at his hometown of Anao regarding his brother-in-law being in critical condition, and he hurried back to his birthplace. As expected, his brother-in-law, Nishida Motokichi, was in a grave state due to a serious illness. Upon immediately offering prayers to the Shinkai and declaring that he would make a full recovery, he returned to Ayabe on the third day, two days later. Nishida Motonori, a propagator of Ōmoto, learned of the sanctity of Kami for the first time on this occasion and entered into the faith. There is a truly interesting story behind the motive for this, but I shall reserve it for another manuscript.

Now, upon returning to Ayabe, I found all of Kiraku's belongings wrapped in straw ropes and old newspapers, thrown out at the entrance. Thinking it strange, I called Shikata Haruzō and asked who had done such a thing, whereupon he changed his expression and fled into the inner room. Thinking it even more mysterious, I called Shikata Yūsuke and asked for the details, which were as follows.

Yūsuke: 'During your absence, Sensei, there was a meeting of the board members. At that time, Shikata Haruzō-san was the initiator, saying that a Sensei like Ueda-san, whose intentions are incomprehensible, should be driven away as soon as possible. He claimed that Shikata Haruzō has learned everything that Ueda-san knows, including the divine eye and the divine ear, so taking advantage of your return to your hometown, they decided to send your belongings to Anao as quickly as possible, and Shikata Heizō-san was to go as the representative of the board members to deliver the notice. If your return had been one day later, it would have been a pity for the expectations of all the board members.'

He was scratching his head and smiling bitterly as he said this. Thereupon,

Kiraku: 'Is the Kyōso-sama aware of this matter?'

I asked, and,

Yūsuke: 'No, no, we are to report it to Kyōso-sama only after we have sent you away first. If we had told Kyōso-sama beforehand, he would surely have stopped us. Since all the board members are troubled by having a Sensei like that Gontarō-san stay for so long, and because there is a saying that one should be quick to do good, everyone has been gathering at the house of Murakami Shinnosuke in Ida since yesterday.'

Such was the situation. Truly, one cannot be too careful or let one's guard down. Meanwhile, Shikata Haruzō, having had his conspiracy exposed, thought he must devise a remedial measure, so he ran to the Murakami residence in Ida himself to report. The opposition was so astonished that they had no means to act, so they finally went to the home of Shikata Heizō in Takasu, Yamaga-mura, to plead for an apology. Through the mediation of Mr. Heizō, both sides were settled peacefully for the time being, but the thought of driving Ueda out at the first opportunity never left them. Since they were all people who had become teachers, board members, or believers of the Konkō Church, they were possessed by the guardian deities of the Konkō religion and sought to exclude Ueda, making their physical bodies truly pitiable.

The results of Kiraku's activities, into which he poured his wholehearted sincerity for the sake of this path, forgetting both sleep and food, were highly successful and led to prosperity day by day. As the Kyōso also decided that it was a divine decree to have Ueda take charge of the administration, the aforementioned Mr. Adachi could not contain his resentment. While remaining in Kyoto, he instigated his former subordinates to attempt exclusion in every way, making the ambitious Shikata Haruzō his standard-bearer, and thus repeated the recent tyranny. At a time when the Kyōso, whose heart was troubled by watching such grave incidents, was becoming somewhat desperate, he summoned Ueda through the messenger Shikata Yūsuke. Thinking that this was an opportunity not to be missed to clear the doubts piled up in his heart, he went directly to the Hiro-mae, spoke to the Kyōso about the situation in his hometown, and they rejoiced together that his brother-in-law's illness was heading toward recovery. However, Nakamura Takezō came out from an inner room bearing a divine revelation, and facing Kiraku with great solemnity, he said,

Nakamura: 'This time, Kyōso-dono is setting out on a pilgrimage in this cold weather in obedience to a divine command, and we have no words to express our feelings regarding the hardships you will endure in your elderly state. This is entirely because Ueda-san's repentance has not been achieved. Please listen respectfully to the divine writing. For a person who must serve for the sake of Kami and the nation to return to his hometown just because of an illness is truly to make light of Kami. Can the life of one or half a person be a substitute for this important divine task? This writing is the divine decree for Kyōso-sama to set out on his pilgrimage this time; those who have not repented cannot accompany the Kyōso. It says to take Ueda along, but this is not a place for a person like you to go. No matter how much it is a divine decree, as a board member, I will not let you accompany him this time, even at the cost of my life, for the sake of the path. Instead, I will accompany him, though I am inadequate. What say you, Ueda-san? Answer right now before the Kyōso. Write a contract saying that you will repent thoroughly and please let me accompany him.'

原文（近代文語）

明治三十三年八月一日、喜楽は郷里なる穴太より義弟危篤の電報に接し、急ぎ故郷へ帰つた。案の如く大病で義弟なる西田元吉は重態である。早速神界へ祈願の上、全快すべきことを告げ、其翌々三日綾部へ帰つた。大本の布教者西田元教は此時始めて神の尊き事を知つて信仰に入つた。其動機には実に面白い次第あれど稿を改めて口述することとする。

さて綾部へ帰つて見ると、門口に喜楽の荷物一切が荒縄や古新聞で包んで、放り出してある。不思議に思つて四方春三を呼んで、誰が斯んな事をしたのかと尋ねると、彼は顔色を変へて奥の間に逃げ込んだ。益々不思議だと思つて四方祐助を呼んで委細を聞くと斯うである。

祐助『先生の御不在中に役員会議がありました。其時に四方春三サンが発起人で、あんな上田サンの様な訳の分らぬ先生は、一日も早く追ひ返すがよい。天眼通も天耳通も何もかも、皆上田サンの知つて居る事は、四方春三が皆覚えたから、今故郷へ帰つて居られるのを幸ひ、一時も早く荷物を穴太へ送つて、断りに四方平蔵サンが役員総代で行かれる処でありました。あなたの御帰りが一日遅かつたら、皆の役員サンの思惑が立つのに惜い事ぢや』

と頭を掻いて苦笑ひをして居る。そこで、

喜楽『此事は教祖さまは御承知か』

と尋ねると、

祐助『イエイエあんたを先へ送つて了ふた上で教祖様に申上げるのです。前に教祖様に申上げたら、キツと止められるは必定ぢや。あんな権太郎先生に永らく居つて貰つては、皆の役員が困るから、善は急げといふ事があると昨日から皆の者が位田の村上新之助サンの家で集会してます』

との事である。誠に油断も隙もあつたものでない。又一方の四方春三は陰謀露顕に及んだので、何とか善後策を講ぜねばなるまいと、位田の村上宅へ自ら走つて報告した。反対者は驚愕の余り施すべき手段がないので、とうとう山家村鷹栖の四方平蔵方へ、謝罪して貰ひたいと歎願に出かけた。平蔵氏の取持で双方共に一先づ無事に治まるは治まつたが、機会さへあらば、上田を追出してやらうといふ考へは少しも放れなかつたのである。何れも皆金光教会の教師や役員や信者になつて居つた人々計りだから、金光教の守護神が憑つて、上田を排斥せむとするので其肉体は実に気の毒なものである。

喜楽が斯道の為に満腔の熱誠をこめ、寝食を忘れて活動せる結果は大に功を奏し、日に月に隆盛に赴き、教祖も是非神勅なれば上田をして事務を総理せしめむとされたので、例の足立氏は憤怨措く所を知らず、身は京都に在り乍ら、従来の部下を使嗾して百方排斥を試み、野心満々たりし四方春三を旗頭となし、今回の横暴を繰返したるなるに、斯る重大事件を傍観し居られし教祖の心事面白からずと、稍捨鉢気分に成り居れる際、四方祐助の使を以て、教祖は上田を招かれたれば、心中に積み重なれる疑団を晴らすには好機逸す可からずと、直に広前に参じ教祖に故郷の様子などをお話し、互に義弟の病気の快方に向へることを喜び合つて居たが、中村竹蔵は奥の一間より御神諭を奉じて出で来り、さもおごそかに喜楽に向ひ、

中村『今回教祖殿は此寒空に、何国へか神命を奉じて御修行に御出ましになり、御老体の身として御苦労遊ばすこと、吾々は何とも申様がありませぬ。是も全く上田サンの改心が出来ぬからであります。謹んでお筆先を拝読きなされ。神様や御国の為に尽さなならぬ人が、病人位で郷里へ帰るなんて、実に神様を軽しめて居られるのぢや。人の一人や半分死んだつて、大切の御用に代へられますか、此筆先は今度教祖さまが御修行に御出ましなさる御神勅でありますぞ、改心の出来ぬ者は教祖の御伴叶ひませぬ。上田を伴れて行くとありますが、あなたのやうなお方のお出でになるべき所ぢやない。何程神勅でも、役員として御道の為に、拙者が今回の御供は、生命に代へてもさせませぬ。其代りに拙者が及ばず乍ら御供仕る。上田サン如何で御座る。只今教祖の前で御返答なされ。トコトン改心するから、御供をさして下さいと契約書を御書きなさい』

§ 2

Nakamura-san, having something on his mind, was foaming at the mouth and hurling abuse at Ueda. Kiraku, feigning ignorance, turned his head to gaze out at the garden, whereupon the Kyōso turned to Nakamura-san and said,

Kyōso: 'You think the Goshinyu refers to Ueda-sama, but you are mistaken, Nakamura-san. Place your hand upon your heart for a moment, reflect upon the conduct of everyone since the other day, and take care not to commit a Torichiga...'

Having inserted this soft needle of a remark, Nakamura retreated from before the Kyōso looking discomfited. After returning the Goshinmon to its original place, he remained silent, his face puffed up in displeasure, and with heavy, resounding footsteps and a rough tread upon the tatami, he withdrew to his own Ima.

The Kyōso rose from his seat and personally handed the Fudesaki, which had been placed upon the Sanpau before the Shinzen, to Kiraku. When he received it with reverence and read it on the spot, the Goshinmon contained the following passage:

'This time, it is a place that ordinary Ningen cannot go. It is a terrifying place, a magnificent place where the true Kami resides. For the Kaishin of all, I shall take Ueda Kaichō, Deguchi Sumiko, and Shikata Haruzō along with Me.'

Upon reading this, he immediately launched a stern protest to the Kyōso. The reason was the inclusion of Shikata Haruzō in the entourage. He was a man who could be called the mastermind behind the movement to oust Ueda since the summer of that year, a treacherous person who had even attempted to do away with Ueda on the steep slope between Nishihara and Uetani. Despite this, we, in our magnanimity, had forgiven him by looking anew, listening anew, and rectifying our hearts through the Kami-naohi and Ōnaohi, yet here he was again, acting as the driving force behind a major campaign of exclusion while I was away in my hometown. However, I could not comprehend how the Ushitora no Konjin-sama, who sees through the good and evil, right and wrong of the world, could possibly include him in the party; the Ushitora no Konjin-sama must be a rather careless Kami. To travel with a person like him is akin to walking with a wolf in sheep's clothing. If the Kami says to take him along knowing this, it must mean that He intends to exclude Ueda. Since the outward appearance would be made to look good, while the underlying divine intent would be to prevent Ueda from accompanying the party, I would like to be excused from this journey... he pressed the Kyōso with a sense of indignation. Upon this, the Kyōso replied,

Kyōso: 'No, no, that is by no means the intention. Since the preparations for the journey are already complete as you see, you must by all means accompany us this time.'

He showed him the straw raincoat, hat, staff, and sandals that were already prepared, so he finally became convinced and agreed to join the party. That day was the seventh of August. The Kyōso then turned to Ueda again and said,

Kyōso: 'Kaichō-san, please open the back door for a moment and look. There is something terrifying, so please observe it well. The Kami says it is for the sake of everyone's warning.'

Feeling somewhat uneasy, he invited Shikata Yūsuke and Shikata Haruzō, who were nearby, and opened the shoji of the back door, but there was nothing terrifying to be seen as the Kyōso had described. As the three of them were looking around in confusion, they saw only an earthworm crawling under the persimmon tree by the back door. After a short while, a toad jumped out with great vigor and swallowed the earthworm in an instant. Following that, a fairly thick black snake appeared and swallowed the toad in one gulp. However, thinking that such a thing was hardly terrifying, the three of them watched intently, when suddenly O-naga, a female cat that Ueda usually doted on, ran up and bit the snake to death. Just as they saw this, a large black cat appeared from nowhere and tried to bite O-naga to death. O-naga, startled, immediately fled up the persimmon tree. The black cat followed in pursuit and climbed the tree as well. Ueda, desperate to save O-naga, climbed the tree after them, but by the time he reached the first branch, O-naga had been bitten by the black cat, let out a sad cry, and fell from the high branch to the ground, where she lay stretched out, wailing. Ueda tried his best to shake the black cat off to avenge O-naga, but it would not fall, so he had no choice but to climb down. When he looked, his new white yukata was covered in cat excrement. For the first time, the three of them were struck with such terror that their skin crawled, realizing, 'Ah, there is always something above the one above; this is truly a terrifying matter.' At that moment, the Kyōso appeared before the three of them with a smile and said,

Kyōso: 'Now you should understand everything.'

Although they did not take the Kyōso's words to heart too deeply at the time, the reason for them became clear later on.

原文（近代文語）

云々と中村氏は胸に一物ある事とて、口角泡を飛ばして上田に毒付いて居る。喜楽は聞かぬ顔して、横を向いて庭の面を眺めて居ると、教祖は中村氏に向ひ、

教祖『御神諭は上田さまの事ぢやと思ふたら違ひますぜ、中村サン　チと胸に手をおいて、先日からの皆サンの行ひを考へて、取違ひを成さらぬやうに……』

と一言柔かな針を入れられて、中村は首尾悪さうに教祖の前を下がり、御神諭を元の所へ納めて了つた限、物も言はず面ふくらしつつ、足音高く畳ざわり荒々しく、自分の居間へ下つて了つた。

教祖は自ら座を立ち、神前の三宝の上に置かれたお筆先を手づから喜楽に渡された。恭しく押戴いて直に其場で拝読すると、御神文の中に、

『今度は普通の人間では行かれぬ処ぢや。実地の神の住居いたして居る、結構な所の怖い処である。皆の改心の為に上田海潮、出口澄子、四方春三を連れ参るぞよ』

と記されてあるので、早速教祖に向つて厳しく談判を吹きかけた。其理由は四方春三の御供に加はつて居ることである。彼は当年の夏頃より上田排斥の主謀者とも云ふべき人物で、西原と上谷の間の峻坂にて上田を○○せむとなしたる如き侫人である。それでも寛仁大度の吾々は、神直日大直日に見直し聞直し宣直して赦して置いたにも係はらず、又々今回吾帰郷中に大排斥運動の原動力となつて駆廻つて居る。然るに世界の善悪正邪を透見し玉ふ艮の金神様が彼をお供に加へられるとは如何しても合点が出来ぬ、艮金神さまは良い加減な神さまだ。彼の如き者と同行するは恰も送り狼と道づれになるやうなものだ。それを知つて同行させると神から言はれるのは、要するに上田を排斥されたのであらう。表面は体裁を良くし、裏面には上田を同行させない御神意であらうから、今度の御伴は御免蒙りたい……と稍憤怒の情を以て教祖に肉迫した。さうすると教祖は、

教祖『イエイエ決して其様な主意ではありませぬ。最早此通り旅立の用意も出来て居りますから、今度は是非同行して貰はねばなりませぬ』

と蓑笠に杖草履など準備の出来上つたのを、見せられたので、漸く得心して御供することにした。其日は八月の七日であつた。教祖は尚も上田に向ひ、

教祖『海潮さん、一寸裏口を開けて御覧なさい、恐ろしい事がありますからよく見ておいて下されや。皆戒めの為ぢやと神様が仰有りますぜ』

との言である。何となく気味が悪いので側に居た四方祐助と四方春三とを誘うて、裏口の障子を開放して見たが、教祖の言はれたやうな恐ろしいものは一つも見当らぬ。何の事か合点が行かぬので、三人がそこらをキヨロキヨロ見廻して居ると、裏口の柿の木の下に蚯蚓が一筋這うて居る斗り、暫時経つと一疋の殿蛙が勢よく飛んで来たかと思ふと、矢庭に其蚯蚓を呑んで了うた。其後へ又黒い可なり太い蛇が出て来て、其蛙を一呑みにして了うた。併し別に是位の事が何恐ろしいものかと思つて三人が熟視してゐると、平素上田が寵愛して居るお長といふ雌猫が走つて来て其蛇を噛み殺して了うたと見る間に、何処から来たか、黒色の大猫がお長を噛殺さむとする。お長は驚いて直に柿の木へ逃げ登つた。黒猫も亦続いてお長の跡を追うて柿の木へ登つた。上田はお長を助けたさに柿の木へ続いて攀登つたが、一の枝まで上つた頃、お長は黒猫に噛まれて、悲しい声を出して、高い枝の上から地上に墜落しふンのびて鳴いてゐる。上田はお長の仇敵と一生懸命になつて黒猫をゆり落さうとしたが、何うしても落ちぬので、止むを得ず下へおりて見ると、上田の新しい浴衣の白いのが、猫の糞まぶれになつて居た。三人は始めて……あゝ上に上のあるものぢや、如何にも恐ろしい事ぢや……と肌に粟粒を生ずる程に驚いた。其時教祖はニコニコし乍ら三人の前に現はれ、

教祖『これで何事も分りませう』

と言はれたが、其時には余り深く教祖の御言葉も心にとめなかつたけれど、後に至つて其理由が判明したのである。
