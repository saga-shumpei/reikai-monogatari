Chapter 28: Tamashii [378]

第28章　玉詩異〔378〕

§ 1

The party tied their camels in a forest of thick old trees at the entrance to the capital of Haru and rested. Odo-yamazumi sat in a circle with everyone and was discussing the operational plan.

"This place is the domain of Daijizaiten, now Tokoyo-shin'o, governed by Takatoriwake, so we must be extremely cautious. The faction of Daijizaiten possesses elite weapons, holds power, and has knowledge. In addition, they have prepared countless Heavenly Rock-boats and Bird-boats, practicing a government of body-over-spirit and the law of the jungle that has annexation as its sole principle. We must punish this wickedness and atrocity. Furthermore, as for our weapon, we possess only a single jewel. Since we are to pacify them by words with that jewel, it takes immense effort. Above all, there is no way to win this battle other than patience. Propagators, can you fulfill this grave mission?"

Kakatoko said,

"Of course; we have neither weapons nor bombs, only this single jewel received from heaven,"

and clenching his fist, he thrust it out before everyone, squaring his shoulders,

"I am a propagator of the realm; though I have no three-foot autumn-water blade at my waist, with this fist harder than iron, I will strike, strike, and beat down the approaching enemies one after another, throwing them into utter confusion!"

"Hey, hey, can you really do such a violent thing? We who impart the teachings of Miroku cannot bear any weapons at all. There is only the jewel."

"That jewel is this!"

so saying, he rounded his fist and thrust it out abruptly to show it.

Takahiko: "What a fool! That is a clenched ball of a fist. It is the wrong jewel."

Kakatoko: "In that case, I am Kakatoko who possesses two balls. Which one shall I use? Compared to the ones you have, they are considerably larger and more magnificent. They get in the way when riding a camel and galloping. They are a great hindrance when walking as well; shall I lend one to you? It is indeed a magnificent testicle."

"Is this any time for jokes? This is a critical moment of one facing a thousand horsemen. It means you must confront the enemy with your soul."

"Could one serve as a propagator without knowing that much? I was merely teasing you. Even facing enemy territory, I showed you the courage of Lord Kakatoko, who is calm and composed with plenty of room to spare. Takahiko, look at this, hanging down loosely. They say a fellow without guts shrinks up when facing a formidable foe, but both of your balls must be resting around the heavenly rock-cave of the lower abdomen. Nay, they must have flown up in panic."

§ 2

Satsuki-hime,

"Ho-ho-ho, how vigorous Kagatora-sama is; my bowels are twisted with laughter,"

holding her belly, laughed in muffled laughter.

"Now, now, Hime-gozen, how unbecoming; how can a mere young woman laugh at what a missionary says? It is unwomanly; act a little more like a woman."

"Odo-yamazumi-sama, we cannot get the point at all from the talk of Kaga-sama or Taka-sama. Please show Komayamahiko the grand policy."

Odo-yamazumi stood and sang a poem:

"The missionary general possesses a voice like thunder; Advancing the divine troops across ten thousand leagues of desert sands. How could one know that, facing the enemy at the base of their castle, The Great Way overcomes pride and casts aside empty fame?"

"What a difficult poem! Missionary-sama, shall I, Kagatora, perform a spirit-discernment for you once? You say such strange things; it makes no sense at all, just like a monkey talking in its sleep."

"No, Komayamahiko understands it."

"If you understand it, tell me; it is but the false oracle of a clumsy spirit-discerner. At any rate, no good will come of it. If Kagatora-sama were made the commander, all plans would fit together perfectly like a well-made box, but because Odo-yamazumi has an ego, nothing goes well at all. Komayamahiko, you too speak of things like the sleep-talk of dogs and monkeys, saying you know this or do not know that; as if the likes of you could understand! I will not have you teach me anymore. There is no point in listening to things that are completely off track."

As they were conferring thus, a camel troop of several dozen fierce warriors, carrying long swords and clad in armor, appeared,

"Ho there! Are not those of you in that party, who have tied your camels in that forest and are resting, missionaries of the Ananai Teachings? Manfully state your names and become rust upon our spears!"

so they shouted.

"Aha, you have appeared! Now is the time to test the prowess of my arms, in which I daily take pride. Ah, Lady Satsuki-hime, behold the martial valor of this Kagatora! Hey, you three cowards, do not lose your nerve when you see my warrior spirit!"

Takahiko turned to Kagatora and said,

"Have you forgotten the doctrine of the Ananai Teachings?"

"In this moment of life and death, who cares for the Ananai Teachings? Meeting occasions and adapting to changes is precisely divine strategy and marvelous cunning. It is beyond the knowledge of fools and petty men like you. Do not get in my way!"

With that, rolling up his dark-red sleeves, he leaped into the crowd of several dozen men, stood firm like a Deva king, and shouted in a loud voice:

"Tracing back my origin, I am Tsuneteruhiko, the surviving child of the Divine King Banko! Having now falsely assumed the name of Kagatora, I am an unprecedented, matchless hero and champion who has descended to the capital of Haru! If I swing this iron fist but once, hundreds of thousands of enemies will crumble all at once like an avalanche—crash, crash, crash! If I stamp my foot to resound but once, the capital of Haru will all at once go crash, crash, crash, smashed completely to pieces! The second coming of Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, the Golden Deity of the Demon Gate; if I stomp my feet firmly upon the lotus pedestal, three or four, or a hundred or two hundred lands of Haru will instantly tremble and shake into the ocean—if you can truly be my match, come and try!"
