Chapter 38: The Nuptial Rites [388]

第38章　華燭の典〔388〕

§ 1

The entire company recited the divine words before the altar of Kunitama, finished chanting the hymns, and was resting. Masakayamazumi pushed open the sliding door, came inside, and said:

"The meal is ready. Please come and eat. Having been away for such a long time, and having no wife, there are many shortcomings and inconveniences, but nonetheless—"

Having offered this greeting, he departed from the room.

Uzumahiko said, "Everyone, from what we just heard, Lord Masakayamazumi has no wife. As the protector of an entire land concurrently serving as a missionary, his person is exceedingly busy, so is it not pitiful that he lacks that essential person, a wife? I, Uzumahiko, should like to arrange a wife for him; what do you say?"

Komayamahiko leaned forward and said,

"That is splendid. Do you have a suitable candidate in mind?"

"How could I not? Indeed I have one. The person we wish to arrange for him is Lady Satsukihime, the female missionary. Come now, Lady Satsuki, upon the rock at the foot of Mount Uzu Pass, you said that Lord Masakayamazumi was a truly manly person with a magnificent countenance, did you not? Surely you have no objection."

Satsukihime silently hid her face in her sleeve. Komayamahiko said rapidly:

"That will not do. Is that not the wrong person? Weren't you infatuated with her to that extent? I was thinking of arranging her to be your wife. There is no need for such hesitation. Instead of posing riddles as if tickling from afar, why don't you come out manly and decisively, saying, 'Lady Satsukihime, please become Uzumahiko's wife'? Speaking as if something were caught in your back teeth, you are an utterly shameless man who feigns ignorance."

"Konohana-sama has reasons not to take a wife. Forgive me on that point alone. Because I make jokes so often, you take them seriously and suspect me without cause, which is vexing. That said, I am not saying I dislike this lovely Satsukihime. I love her, love her, love her dearly, yet there is a karma that forbids me from taking a wife."

"Hey, Kakatora! Hold on, Uzumahiko, shall we hear of that karma?"

"If it were the kind of karma I could tell you, why would I hide it? Forgive me for this alone. For I still have grave duties ahead of me."

Odoyamatsumi said,

"Well, Lord Uzuma, we simply cannot make sense of you. You are entirely like a flood dragon soaring through the sky, shifting in myriad ways and impossible to grasp. I shall say nothing more. Yielding to your opinion, I should like to recommend Lady Satsukihime as the wife of the master of this house."

§ 2

Uzuyamahiko said,

"If you also agree, please try negotiating with Masakayamazumi-sama."

Odoyamazumi said, "Very well," and standing up from that place, went into another room.

Satsukihime blushed and looked down. Komayamahiko said,

"Now, now, Satsuki-sama, this is the most critical matter in a woman's lifetime; if you do nothing but look down, we cannot know the matter. Will it be Uzuyama-sama, or will it be Masakayamazumi-sama? Answer whether right or left. If you have an opinion, we have no barriers between us, so please state it clearly. If by some chance neither of these two persons pleases you, it is not as though there are no other candidates. Shall I arrange someone whose name begins with the syllable 'Ko'?"

Uzuyamahiko looked at Komayamahiko's face and said,

"Ufufufufu."

Satsukihime finally raised her face and said,

"Yes, yes, so long as Masakayamazumi-sama has no objection."

Uzuyamahiko clapped his hands and said,

"It has come, it has come; our wish is fulfilled, the time has arrived. Ah, as expected of Lady Satsukihime, splendid, splendid, you have good discernment. That is indeed worthy of a missionary of the world. Since a day chosen on impulse is auspicious, let us hold the wedding ceremony today."

Komayamahiko said,

"Hey, hey, Uzuyamahiko, even if one party agrees, we do not know what the other party will say; it might be an unrequited love like an abalone, yet what a panicked fellow you are."

Uzuyamahiko said, "No, it is all right. It is dried bonito to a cat, fried tofu with mouse to a fox; that he will bite with ready consent is a guaranteed watermelon, bright red all the way to the core. Now, now, Lady Satsukihime, until now you have been an acknowledged, full-fledged woman, and men and women had equal rights, so to speak, equality of the sexes. However, once you are married from today onward, you must follow your husband. Observing the heavenly law of the husband leading and the wife following, serving your master well, and managing the household is your duty. Even if there is equality of the sexes, there is no equality between husband and wife, so do not forget that; show the model of a wise wife and good mother, and manifest the light of the Ananaikyō to the world. Becoming husband and wife in this wide world is also a very deep karma, brought together by the Kami, so you must never indulge your wilfulness. As I spoke to you at Uzuyama Pass, please cherish this bridegroom, stay on the lotus pedestal forever, and make sure not to repeat the folly of Iwaihime. I beseech you."

§ 3

Satsukihime, shedding tears profusely, said:

"Yes, in every respect, not to mention down to the era of my grandchildren, I will never forget your kindness even until the world of Miroku. I will surely and firmly abide by your teachings. Please be at ease."

"Well now, Uzuyama, you are a strange man. Truly an incomprehensible man. Giving away such a beautiful woman to another—what an eccentric fellow you are. But still, it is admirable. If it were this Komayama, my spirit would never be polished to such a degree."

Just as they were speaking thus, Odotsumitsumi, accompanied by Masakayamatsumi, appeared at this place and, bowing politely, said:

"Everyone, in addition to having received your various kindnesses, thank you for giving us such splendid help this time. There is no longer any way to repay your favor."

Thus he expressed his gratitude.

Uzuyamahiko said:

"Ah, splendid, splendid; with that, we too can set forth to propagate with peace of mind. Please remain on good terms as husband and wife for a long time, and govern this divine land eternally. In the event of an emergency, please serve the divine realm together as husband and wife."

Unlike his usual cheerful self, his voice grew clouded, and he shed tears of joy.

Odotsumitsumi said:

"Well, now that the matter has been settled in this way, good deeds should not be delayed. Shall we set about preparing for the wedding before the deities without delay?"

Hereupon, everyone, together with their retainers and followers, held a grand wedding ceremony. As all moved to the naorai banquet, clapping their hands and singing songs, at the very moment when their spirits rose with excitement, Kunihiko, the chief clerk, opened the sliding door and said:

"I report to the master. Just now, the three young ladies, Matsuyohime, Takenohime, and Umekahime, have arrived from the holy ground of Jerusalem, accompanied by a single attendant, saying, 'Is our father's dwelling here?' How should we handle this?"

Masakayamatsumi, being surprised, said:

"Ah, joyful events are indeed piling up."

Everyone clapped their hands, shouting, "Urō, urō!"

Postscript

Masakayamatsumi is Momogamihiko no Mikoto, who was the archangel of the holy ground of Jerusalem. He usurped the theocracy of his elder brothers, Hiromunehiko no Mikoto and Yukinarihiko no Mikoto, exhausted all extremes of the body-master and spirit-subordinate state, and on this account the earthly Takamanohara fell into the extreme of chaos and entanglement; his wife died of illness, and he himself, due to a temporary failure, was banished on account of Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime; leaving behind his three daughters, he left the familiar capital, and just as he was proceeding toward Hitotsushima, he encountered a violent storm, his boat immediately capsized, he was rescued by a turtle belonging to Kotohirawake and reached the Ryūgū Castle; just as he had ended up becoming a gatekeeper, he was saved by the God of the Rising Sun and became the guardian of this capital of Uzu.

His three daughters perceived this matter in a divine dream and came all the way here in search of him. The three peach fruits with which Izanagi no Mikoto waited for and struck down the army of Yomi on the slope of Yomotsuhirasaka indeed represented the activities of the three daughters of Momogamihiko no Mikoto.

と感謝の意を漏した。

珍山彦は、

『あゝ結構々々、それで安心して吾々も宣伝に参ります。どうぞ幾久しく夫婦仲好くして此の神国を永遠に治めて下さい。一朝事ある時は、夫婦諸共神界の御用に立つて下さい』

と日ごろ快活な男に似ず、声を曇らして嬉し涙を零し居たり。

淤縢山津見は、

『ヤア、斯く話が纏まつた上は、善事は急げだ。早く神前結婚の用意にかかりませうか』

茲に一同は家の子郎党と共に、盛大なる結婚の式を挙げける。一同は直会の宴にうつり、各手を拍ち歌を歌ひ、感興湧くが如き折しも、番頭の国彦は襖を開いて、

『御主人様に申上げます。只今ヱルサレムの聖地から松代姫、竹野姫、梅香姫の三人の御嬢様が、「御父様の住家は此処か」と云つて、一人の供を伴れて御出でになりました。如何が取計らひませうか』

正鹿山津見は驚きながら、

『あゝ嬉しいことが重なるものだな』

一同手を拍つて、ウローウロー。

附言

正鹿山津見は、聖地ヱルサレムの天使長であつた桃上彦命である。兄広宗彦命、行成彦命の神政を奪ひ、体主霊従の限りを尽し、地の高天原は為に混乱紛糾の極に陥り、その妻は病死し、自分は常世彦、常世姫のために、或一時の失敗より追放され、三人の娘を後に残して住み慣れし都を後に、一つ島に進む折しも、暴風に逢ひ船は忽ち顛覆し、琴平別の亀に救はれ竜宮城にいたり、門番となり果てし折しも、日の出神に救はれ、この珍の都の守護職となれるなり。

この事を三人の娘は、神夢に感じて遥々此処に尋ね来たり。黄泉比良坂の坂の上に於て、黄泉軍を待ち討ち給ひし伊弉諾命の三個の桃の実は、即ち桃上彦命の三人の娘の活動を示されたるなり。
