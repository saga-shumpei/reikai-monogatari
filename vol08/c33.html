Chapter 33: The Heavenly Brows [383]

第33章　天上眉毛〔383〕

§ 1

Under the summer sky as if blazing with scorching heat, Leaving behind the capital of Haru, Which was praised as the capital of flowers, The party of Odoyamazumi Has at last arrived here, Accompanied by Momogamihiko; Noble children of the Heavenly Deities, Seeking to save the people of the world, Singing the propagation song with clear, cool voices, Keeping pace together as they descend the pass Of Mount Uzu, what bravery!

The sun gradually sank to the west, and large shadows cast themselves onto the valley path between the mountains.

Komayamahiko said:

"Well, it has become quite cool. What is indispensable, yet troublesome to have, is the light and heat of the sun. With the sun hiding in the mountain shade like this, it has become as cool as night. Such refreshing coolness cannot be tasted without traveling."

Kakatoko pouted his lips and said:

"What are you saying, you cursed fellow? What do you mean by 'indispensable, yet troublesome to have'? Take it back, take it back! The Sun Deity is indispensable, and we cannot do without Him; that He shines His hot light and heat upon our heads is the deep grace of God. And will you not restate that casting cool shade upon us and blowing clear wind upon us is owing to God's pure and cool blessing that protects us?"

"Well, I have made a blunder. O Sun Deity, as Kakatoko has just said, I, Komayamahiko, restate my words."

"There, see that?"

"Even if I look at the sky, isn't the Sun Deity hidden in the mountains?"

"Do not play dumb!"

Odoyamazumi said:

"Well, well, have you started again? How amusing."

Satsukihime looked down,

"Ho-ho-ho-ho-ho,"

and laughed faintly.

Kakatoko said:

"Satsukihime of the May sky opens her flower-like lips with an exquisite voice, 'Ho-ho-ho-ho-ho,' like a cuckoo whose voice is heard and whose form is seen. A blue flag flower of noble figure the more one looks; in the dark night where black and white cannot be distinguished, accompanied by the wondrously precious Satsukihime. After all, the world is best with women. When only men are walking, things somehow become rough and angular before one knows it, and the wheels just do not seem to turn smoothly."

Komayamahiko said, "Kakatoko, with your dog-legged waist, how dare you say that without women things become angular or rough! As if Satsukihime, however she may be, would cast amorous glances at sweltering fellows like you; who on earth would send you glances of love? Becoming conceited, flattered, and snorting with pride as if to say you are the world's greatest handsome man—what is all that arrogance about?"

"The breath of the steed has grown quite rough; what a weak fellow. Are you suffering? If it is so painful, there is a convenient rock right there. Why not take a break there? Walking with missionary disciples weak in feet and weak in hips makes them a hindrance, which troubles me. Well, you had better take a rest. Excuse me, Lord Okitsuyama, Lord Masakayama, why do you not take a break as well if you like? Lady Satsukihime, your legs are nimble, unbefitting a mere woman. Shall the nimble-legged among us excuse ourselves and go ahead a step?"

"Aha, you speak clever words. I have read your mind. Lady Satsuki, you rest too. Let Kakatora go ahead alone, lose his way, fall to the bottom of the ravine, and find bliss in extinction. Go on ahead; Komayamahiko will at least pick up your bones out of traveling courtesy!"

"Aha, since you speak of such troublesome and ominous things, shall I also practice patience and rest my nimble feet?"

"At last you have blurted out your true feelings. Ahahaha!"

The party stretched their legs on the flat rock and rested for a while. The sun appeared to have completely sunk below the horizon, and the surroundings gradually grew dark.

Masakayamatsumi, with an anxious face, said,

"There is still a considerable distance from here to the capital of Chi. Ahead of us, we must cross yet another large mountain, but since it is a steep mountain path overgrown with briars through which none but wild boars have ever passed, shall we leisurely spend the night here? The mountain ahead is called Mount Amagumo, and it is far higher than this Chizan Pass. Moreover, these days huge serpents and venomous snakes lie across the path, so traveling by night is dangerous. It is even called the Great Serpent Pass."

"What! Is it the Great Serpent Pass? If a giant serpent appears, that is amusing. It is a test of one's daily skill and courage. Hearing that, Kakatora's arms swell and throb. Ah, splendid, I can no longer contain myself. Hey, all you missionary disciples, shall we not let the giant serpent hear a propagation hymn? By the virtue of the propagation hymn, the giant serpent will become deified and turn into a great beauty."

Komayamahiko, dumbfounded, said,

"You speak of beauties again. You had better go all by yourself."

"Were you frightened, did you lose your wits, were you startled out of your senses? What is with that complexion? Turning completely pale like a rat snake, you fool general!"

"Hey, hey, Kagatora, how can you call someone a foolish general? Retract and restate your words before Komayama."

"I will certainly restate them. Until now I was riding a camel, but this time I have changed my mount to the back of a giant serpent."

"You blow a great boast. If you were to see the real thing, it would be Kagatora, conversely, who would turn tail and run."

Masakayamazumi said quietly,

"What with the dark night and the steep mountains and sheer cliffs, the sun does not set on a night road. Well, let us take our time."

Odoshiyamazumi, following upon his words, said,

"Until we worship the new sun again, shall we chant the norito here and observe the vigil for the sun?"

All together: "That would be well."

Thus, sitting upright upon the crag, they chanted the Amatsu-norito, sang the propagation hymns through once, made pillows of their arms upon the bed of rock, covered themselves with a blue quilt adorned with star crests, and wandered into the land of dreams. A semicircular moon rose, grazing the eastern sky. The figures of the five became clearly visible as if held in one's hand.

Kagatora awakened and said,

"Well, well, each and every one of them has fallen sound asleep. Humans are truly innocent creatures. Shall I inspect them one by one to see whose face looks the most innocent? First of all, let me examine the head of Odoshiyamazumi. Well, I have thought him a bad fellow from long ago, and indeed he wears a somewhat villainous face. This mouth is rather hateful. The way he opens his huge mouth and drools is simply unbearable to look at. Fortunately, there are mulberries that I plucked when coming down the pass. Shall I color his face with these?"

Saying this monologue, he smeared the purple juice around the mouth and the eyes. Peering at it in the moonlight, he said,

"Well, amusing, amusing! When tomorrow morning comes, he will surely be greatly startled. Let me write 'Bound for Hell' on this fellow,"

and he marked it on the cheek.

"Well, is this Masakayamazumi's face? This fellow has a good face, comparatively unbecoming of a villain. If I color it, it might not suit him, but it will not do to leave a one-sided grudge, so shall I write something on him?"

and he painted the nose purple.

"Now, next is young Koma of the Yagamashiki family. What shall I write on this fellow's forehead? I know: 'A man who is ever jealous out of desire for Satsukihime.' Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, this is good. Now, next is Satsukihime's turn. A face like a flower, eyebrows like the moon; she has no flaw anywhere. Still, for the sake of company, I must do something. Oh, that is it: the high eyebrows of a bride's hood!"

『ヤア、何れも此れも、よく臥せり居つたものだナア。人間も罪の無いものだワイ。何奴の顔が一番罪のない顔をしてゐるか、一々点検をしてやらうかい。まづ第一に淤縢山津見の首実検に及ぶとしようか。ヤア、此奴は昔から悪い奴だと思つたが、ホンニ一寸悪さうな顔をしてをるワイ。この口許が一寸憎らしい。大きな口を開けよつて、涎を出して居る所の態と云つたら、見られたものぢや無いワイ。幸ひ峠を下る時にむしつてきた桑の実がある。此奴で一つ顔を彩つてやらうかナア』

と独語を云ひながら、口の辺り目の周囲に紫の汁を塗つけた。月影にすかして見て、

『ヤア、面白い面白い、明日の朝になつたら随分吃驚することだらう。此奴は「地獄行き」と書いて置いてやれ』

と頬辺に印を入れる。

『ヤア、正鹿山津見の顔か。此奴は割とは悪人に似合はぬ好い顔だな。彩ると却つて似合はぬかも知れないが、片怨みがあるといかぬから、何なと書いてやらうか』

と鼻を紫に塗つて了つた。

『サア、これから矢釜敷家の駒公だ。此奴の額に何と書いてやらうかナ。分つた「五月姫欲しさに、よう妬く男」と、ハヽヽヽヽ是で好い。サアこれから五月姫の番だ、花の顔月の眉、何処にも欠点がないワイ。それでも御附合に何とかせなくてはなるまい。オーさうだ、角隠しの天上眉毛だ』

§ 4

Saying thus, he drew a circle on the forehead dab by dab.

"Well now, this is splendid. She has become even more of a beauty. Her charm has somehow increased, and dignity has been added. Ah, is it finished with this? Since it is no fun if only I remain unblemished, shall I do one to myself too? Yes, that is right. Shall I write 'the world's most handsome man' and leave it? Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, amusing, amusing; hold on, face-black, face-black; it has ended up looking like an exhibition hall of the Five Hundred Arhats. Wa-ha-ha-ha!"

Komayamahiko woke up and said,

"Who is it that disturbs sound sleep? Man, you see, should live in the moment. When you sleep, sleep soundly, and when you work, work. Were you dreaming as if you were mad, holding a wedding with Satsukihime or something? What is it, laughing in the middle of the night—go to sleep at once."

"Yes, yes, I will sleep, I will sleep; you too had better sleep without raising your heads. Ah, he has gone to sleep quickly. He is a fellow as if he had seen a monster shark. Wa-ha-ha-ha, everyone is sleeping soundly, with faces that seem both innocent and guilty. I, this Kakatora, shall join in. Shall I put on a badger's feigned sleep, I wonder?"

Saying thus, he rolled over and lay down, resting his head on his elbow.

True heart— Sleeping upon the rocky face, A warm dream of mulberries. (Yumi)
