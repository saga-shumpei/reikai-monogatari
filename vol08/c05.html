Chapter 5: The Three Spirits at the World's Origin [355]

第5章　三人世の元〔355〕

§ 1

Hinode-no-Kami listened to this idle chatter, and fell silently into thought for a short while, but suddenly turned toward Kiyohiko, and with solemn words said:

"Kiyohiko, I shall henceforth postpone my departure for the capital of Chirī, and intend first to explore the Dragon Palace in order to rescue Tsuranagi-no-Kami. Because I will protect your person, proceed without anxiety to the capital of Chirī and propagate Ananai-kyō. On the island of Takasago, Ryūsehime-no-Kami and Tsukiteruhiko-no-Kami are protecting it, so go forth courageously. Also, Sarusehiko and Komayamahiko, reform your past hearts and follow the teachings of the deities. People of the ship, I bid you farewell from this moment."

Swifter than the utterance of these words, he leaped and plunged into the sea, whereupon Kiyohiko and all the people fell into utter confusion and panic,

"Ah, he has cast himself into the sea! He has cast himself into the sea!"

they cried out, one after another. Kiyohiko stood at the ship's prow, and with all his might,

"Hinode-no-Kami-sama! Hinode-no-Kami-sama!"

he wailed aloud; yet suddenly on the distant surface of the sea, a human figure appeared. Looking closely, Hinode-no-Kami was riding on the back of an enormous turtle, advancing calmly and pointing toward the distance. Kiyohiko turned to Sarusehiko and Komayamahiko, and said:

"That person is Hinode-no-Kami. Did you hear his words just now? Did he not say, 'I am going to the Dragon Palace from now on, so do not worry; I will protect Kiyohiko'? Kiyohiko, possessed by the divine soul of Hinode-no-Kami, is different from who he was before. From now on, revere me as Hinode-no-Kami. I will show you any divine virtue whatsoever."

Sarusehiko: "Humph! A god with sparks flying from his eyes, a fellow of a god with farts bursting from his buttocks—making fools out of people, it makes my buttocks dumbfounded!"

Komayamahiko: "O missionary who expels farts from his backside and claims to do everything, shall we have you let us hear that so-called propaganda song of yours?"

Kiyohiko: "Hinode-no-Kami rode upon a turtle and went to the Dragon Palace. Therefore, I am the one who will open up that vast capital of Takasago. If you too are going to the island of Takasago from now on, you shall not land without my permission!"

Komayamahiko: "Suddenly making such a fuss like a lead Tenjin figure—shall I splash a bit of seawater on you to dampen you down?"

Sarusehiko: "Hey, hey! Do not utter such violent words; he is a splendid missionary. Still, what if we also disguise ourselves as missionaries of Ananai-kyō and propagate on the island of Takasago?"

Komayamahiko: "That should be interesting! Hey there, Hinode-no-Kami-sama, heave-ho, heave-ho! Hello there, Hinode Missionary-sama, please make me your disciple!"

日の出神はこの雑談を聞き、黙然として、ややしばらく思いに沈んでいたが、たちまち清彦に向かい、言葉厳かに、

「清彦、吾はこれよりチリーの都に出張することを見合わせ、面那芸の神を救うため一まず竜宮を探険しようと思う。吾は汝の身辺を守護するから、心配なくチリーの都に至って三五教を宣伝せよ。高砂の島には竜世姫神、月照彦神が守護してくださるから勇んで行け。また猿世彦、駒山彦も、これまでの心を改め神の教えに従え。船の諸人よ。吾はこれでお別れを申し上げる」

と言うより早く身を躍らせて、海中へ飛び込まれると、清彦をはじめ諸人は狼狽し、

「ああ、身投げだ、身投げだ」

と口々に叫ぶ。清彦は船首に立ち、声を限りに、

「日の出神様、日の出神様」

と号泣していたが、はるかの海面に忽然として人影が現れた。よくよく見ると日の出神は、巨大な亀の背に乗り、悠々として、あちらを指して進んで行く。清彦は、猿世彦、駒山彦に向かい、

「あの方は日の出神だぞ。今のお言葉を聞いたか。『俺はこれから竜宮へ行って来るからお前たちは心配するな、清彦を守ってやろう』と仰ったであろうがな。日の出神の御魂の憑依った清彦は今までとは違うぞ。これから俺を日の出神と崇め奉れよ。どんな御神徳でもお目にかけてやる」

猿世彦「フム、目から火の出る神の、臀から屁の出る神めが、人を盲目にして、尻が呆れるわい」

駒山彦「尻から屁の出る、何でもできる宣伝使様、宣伝歌とやらを聞かせてもらおうかい」

清彦「日の出神は、亀に乗って竜宮へ行かれた。そこで、あの広い高砂の都を俺が開くのだ。貴様もこれから高砂の島へ行くのなら、俺の許しがなくては上陸することはまかりならぬぞ」

駒山彦「にわかに、鉛の天神様みたいにはしゃいで、少し海の水でもぶっかけて湿らせてやろうか」

猿世彦「これこれ、そんな暴言を吐くな、立派な宣伝使様だ。しかし俺たちも三五教の、一つ宣伝使に化けて、高砂の島を宣伝したらどうだろう」

駒山彦「面白かろう。おい日の出神さま、どっこいどっこい。もしもし日の出宣伝使様、私をあなたの弟子にしてくだされいな」

駒山彦『面白からう、オイ日の出神さま、ドツコイドツコイ。モシモシ日の出宣伝使様、わたしを貴所の弟子にして下されいな』

§ 2

Kiyohiko: "If you repent, I shall forgive you."

Saruyohiko: "Hmph, you speak so grandly. Hearing you say 'repentance' makes me utterly amazed."

Kiyohiko triumphantly began to sing a propagation hymn.

"The Kami appears on the surface, Separating good from evil; Kamunaohi, who created this world, Ōnaohi, whose spirit is vast; Simply in all things of the human world, Look again and listen again with a straight spirit, Confess anew the errors of oneself."

Komayamahiko: "That is a splendid song. Shall I try one? ... A turtle appears on the surface, carrying Hinode-no-Kami away..."

Saruyohiko: "Hey, that is wrong... 'A turtle appears on the surface, carrying Hinode-no-Kami away...' How could there be such nonsense? It is supposed to say, 'The Kami appears on the surface.'"

Komayamahiko: "The wife appears on the surface, abandoning Saruyo to follow the deer. Simply in all things of the human world, surrender yourself to the vile woman of a wife, and change rides from the ship to the turtle."

Saruyohiko: "Fool! How can you become a propagator with that? Are your ears wood ear mushrooms, or knotholes?"

Komayamahiko: "Saruyo's weeping voice of wood ear mushrooms, Kakazaemon the deer steals, Kakazaemon the deer steals, the deer exchanges rides with Okame..."

Saruyohiko: "If you say such things again, there will be wind, and waves will rise."

Komayamahiko: "It is all right. Hinode-no-Kami is here."

Saruyohiko: "What good is such a Hinode-no-Kami? He is an overnight Hinode-no-Kami. Well, if the previous one was Hinode-no-Kami, this fellow—hold on, this gentleman is at best a Higure-no-Kami. And you are a Yowa-no-Kami."

Thus they chattered idly. At length, the ship arrived at the port of the land of Chiri. The three rushed out of the ship at top speed and advanced steadily deep into the interior.

Kiyohiko: "You two cannot accompany Hinode-no-Kami. If I walk through Takasago Island, a land teeming with beautiful women, accompanied by fellows with faces like gourds and sake bottles like you, even I cannot help looking foolish; so let us three part ways here and go to propagate as each pleases."

Saruyohiko: "Hey, Kiyohiko, is that not going too far? You have been taken care of at Mount Kijō where I lived until now, so you surely must know some gratitude. Why will you not take us along? Fortunately, the people of Takasago know nothing of our origins; so if Kiyohiko becomes the Hinode-no-Kami of high renown throughout the world, this one becomes Ara-no-Kami, and Komayamahiko becomes Ame-no-Kami, and we display divine power by making Takasago Island fair-weathered, or creating gales, or bringing rain, and terrify them out of their wits, they may well be impressed. That is right, three heads are better than one; the three of us are the Three Origins of the World. Praised as splendid, splendid, how about playing the bat in a birdless village?"

Kiyohiko: "No bats for me; that fellow only comes out at dusk. I am not a Kami of dusk. I am Hinode-no-Kami, so let us enter deep into the mountains and talk things over leisurely tonight."

Saying this, Kiyohiko quickened his pace toward a forest of dense trees. Muttering complaints, the two followed after Kiyohiko. The sun sank into the western mountains, and as they were enclosed in a curtain of darkness so thick that one could not tell even if one's nose were pinched, Kiyohiko slipped into the dark, abandoned the two, and concealed himself, disappearing no one knew where.

清彦「貴様ら二人は日の出神のお供は叶わないぞ。貴様のような、瓢箪や徳利のような顔をした奴を美人の群がり集まる地である高砂島で連れ歩くと、俺までが馬鹿に見えて仕方がないから、ここで三人は別れて、思い思いに宣伝に行こうか」

清彦「蝙蝠は御免だ、あいつは日暮ればかりに出る奴だ。俺は日暮れのカミではない。日の出神なのだからな、まあ山奥にでも入って、今晩はゆっくり相談でもしようか」

と言いながら樹木が鬱蒼とした森林を目がけて、清彦は足を速めた。二人はぶつぶつ小言を言いながら、清彦の後を追う。日は西山に沈み、鼻をつままれても分からないような闇の帳に閉ざされたが、清彦は闇に紛れて二人を置き去りにし、どこへともなく姿を隠した。
