Chapter 3: The Accord of Spirit and Form [353]

第3章　身魂相応〔353〕

§ 1

Saruyohiko and Komayamahiko both at once let go of their hands that had seized Kiyohiko, and Saruyohiko said,

"Kiyohiko, you are indeed an evangelist. You chatter smoothly and glibly about off-track matters. Even if you are mistaken, when your tongue turns that well, any holes get plugged up. You are a self-proclaimed, aptly named evangelist of Ananaikyō whose boasts pass through. How grandly you have blown your conch horn of tall tales! Shall we have you let us hear it to while away the boredom?"

Kiyohiko said, "To say 'Shall we have you let us hear it' when asking an evangelist is insolent; an orator of rushing torrents, an eloquent speaker like Pūrṇa is none other than myself. Purify your ears and listen attentively to the sacred teaching of Kiyohiko, whose body and soul are pure."

Saruyohiko and Komayamahiko said, "What an imposing manner! You are the evangelist who is said to expound and admonish all things in the universe. Surely you know everything."

Kiyohiko said, "Of course. If it concerns the three thousand worlds, ask anything. I do not say that I shall grant you a detailed solution, though."

Saruyohiko said, "Speaking of the three thousand worlds, I remembered. In Ananaikyō, there are teachings such as 'The plum blossoms open across the three thousand worlds all at once, open, scatter, and bear fruit,' or 'Though the voice of the cuckoo is heard, its form is not seen.' What on earth does that mean... hold on... what does it mean? Let me humbly receive your instruction."

Komayamahiko said, "If you speak so politely, you will come out on the losing end."

Saruyohiko said, "Be quiet; I am having him speak for free."

Komayamahiko said, "You are probably asking because you intend to imitate others, in true Saruyohiko fashion, somewhere else again."

Saruyohiko said, "Excuse me, Evangelist Kiyohiko, please let us hear that story about the three thousand worlds from before."

Kiyohiko said, "Ahem, ohon, ahan."

Saruyohiko said, "Is it the fifty sounds again?"

Kiyohiko said, "It is a habit of mine; well, listen closely. As for 'the plum blossoms open across the three thousand worlds all at once,' in today's world the rice-eating bugs have greatly multiplied, and on top of that, there are only idlers, so rice is lacking. On the other hand, there are also humans who have never seen the face of rice throughout the year, eating grass and trees. Therefore, God tells everyone to share joy and sorrow alike, and commands the entire world to eat rice gruel. Even then, one must not eat five bowls or eight bowls at one time. One must not eat more than three bowls at a time. Therefore, it is said: 'Make it three bowls of gruel at a time.'"

Saruyohiko said, "Indeed, that too is interesting, but what about 'the plum blossoms that open'?"

§ 2

Kiyohiko said, "Opening a large mouth, heaping gruel into a Gorohachi bowl, when a high-nosed one like you sips the gruel, the nose ends up buried in it. Thus it is 'the nose of opening and burying.' As for 'opening and scattering to bear fruit,' even when sipping thin gruel in which the reflection of a running mouse in the ceiling can be seen, one sips a little of the fruit of the rice grain. Therefore, opening a large mouth and sipping a little of the fruit is what it means."

Saruyohiko said, "He makes a fool of people. Kiyohiko, will you not preach seriously, or shall I strike you again?"

Kiyohiko said, "Even if I explain such profound and boundless plans of the divine realm to you, your ears are only shallow; therefore, once genuine matters can enter your ears, I shall tell you. Since this Anaikyō is a teaching that can be received according to the capacity of one's soul, preaching the teachings of sages to a three-year-old child for the first time is like preaching to a stone Jizō. Much less, to loaches or dried earthworms, how could I speak of genuine truths? Polish your souls quickly; once you have polished them, I will give you a sermon suited to your souls."

Komayamahiko said, "Do not mock us. Yet, since this is a long journey upon the waves, if we regard it as listening to banter, we can endure it. Tell us more."

Kiyohiko said, "Shall I give you an exposition within the scope of your understanding?"

Saruyohiko said, "What on earth is the meaning of 'Though the voice of the cuckoo is heard, its form cannot be seen'?"

Kiyohiko said, "That is something attached to your body morning and evening. When you eat gruel, the feces become soft, and when you go to the privy, they make a plop-plop sound. Then, following after, the delusion of the sweet potato gruel comes out with a whoosh. Thus the feces go plop-plop, and the fart goes whoosh. Because feces become fertilizer and take effect, the fertilizer is heard. The shape of the fart that emerged with a whoosh cannot be seen, can it? Therefore, the deity says that the form of the fart that emerged with a whoosh cannot be seen."

Saruyohiko shouted loudly, "Fool!"

The passengers all burst into laughter at once.

At this time, from a corner of the boat, a great divine being, gentle in countenance, filled with a spirit of broad benevolence and magnanimity, of profound thought and superior wisdom and virtue, appearing to possess both literary and martial arts, arose and began to sing a propagation song.

"Leaving behind the island of Tsukushi Of Arabia with rough waves and wind, Advancing toward the capital of Chiri Of Takasago with the divine majesty, The broad ocean of wide blessings, The ocean of deep majesty, Manipulated by the threads of the plan Of the fathomless Heavenly Deities, Upon the waves where the heart has calmed, Leaving behind the mountain of Kijō, From the familiar village, Saruyohiko, And Komayamahiko of impatient heart, Drifting here, toward the missionary of Kiyohiko Carrying the divine command, Closing in from right and left, Clenching their turban-shell fists, Striking painfully, The sincere heart of Kiyohiko, Whose soul is pure, enduring and bearing it, To the divine heart of the Great Heavenly Deity, May it be found pleasing, of the Heavenly Sun, Firm and everlasting in the land of Chiri, Though wearing the brocade of rags, The sky of the heart is wrapped In the twill brocade of radiant beauty, Atoning for sins deeper Than the fathomless sea of thousand fathoms, Now a noble missionary Going forth to unfold the Anaikyō, I am a missionary of the Rising Sun Deity, Said to illuminate the dark night, Unexpectedly, here is the boat of teaching, With united hearts sharing a common destiny, Raising the sail on the boat of salvation, Upon the ocean of raging waves And the Eternal Land wrapped in black clouds, Pushing open the Heavenly Rock Door, To construct and solidify The divine land of the Rising Sun Deity, a missionary, To construct and solidify, a missionary."

Thus there was a divine being who sang out refreshingly. Kiyohiko was astonished by this voice, and joining his palms, bowed in veneration before the majestic figure of the Rising Sun Deity, choking with fallen tears.
