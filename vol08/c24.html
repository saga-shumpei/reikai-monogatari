Chapter 24: Blind Spirit Discernment [374]

第24章　盲目審神〔374〕

§ 1

In a spacious chamber of Kurayamazumi's inner palace, preparations for the evening meal were made, and once the polite greetings between host and guests were concluded, each took their place at the dinner table.

The evening meal thus having come to an end, Odoyamazumi, together with the two, faced the sacred altar and chanted the Amatsu Norito.

Kurayamazumi politely thanked the company for their labors, and furthermore,

"There is a matter about which I earnestly wish to inquire of the propagation envoys. I beg for your guidance,"

so he said.

"I do not know what the matter might be, but let us inquire of the deities."

"The propagation envoys who explain all things in the universe, from the vast universe down to the inside of a louse's belly—"

"Now, now, Kakatora, be quiet."

"I had heard that Izanami-no-Mikoto, who dwelt in Takamanohara, had departed for the land of Yomi. Yet recently, I heard from Takatoriwake, the master builder of the land of Haru, that Izanami-no-Mikoto has appeared on Mount Rocky in Tokoyo. If there are two Izanami-no-Mikoto, which one would be real? At a loss as to our course of action, and wishing somehow to discern the truth, we were praying day and night. However, in last night's dream, there was a revelation: 'Tomorrow, propagation envoys of Ananaikyō will come to this land, so send Satsukihime to meet them.' Therefore, today I had my daughter mingle with the crowd and enter the Forest of Kunitama at the edge of the town to await the arrival of the propagation envoys; that she met the propagation envoys of Ananaikyō just as the dream revealed, I deeply believe to be the unshakeable divine revelation. Concerning this matter, I beg for your guidance."

"Well, we certainly accompanied Izanami-no-Mikoto from the Ryūgū Castle, but we parted ways mid-journey. Izanami-no-Mikoto must be accompanied by the splendid living deity named Hinode-no-Kami and by Omonagitsukasa. Since she stated that she was now going to Mount Rocky, that must be the true Izanami-no-Mikoto."

Kakatora's body suddenly began to vibrate, and at last he spoke:

"Oh, oh, Odoyamazumi! What you say is mistaken, mistaken! Izanami-no-Mikoto is—te-te—after all, it cannot be spoken, cannot be spoken! The one who appeared on Mount Rocky is the transformed spirit of Ōkunihime, wife of the God-King of Tokoyo!"

"What manner of Magatsukami are you? We actually accompanied Izanami-no-Ōkami and parted on the sea. Her words at that time were that she would from then on shut herself away on Mount Rocky. You must be an evil deity seeking to deceive me!"

Kagatora, swinging his hands up, shook his head from side to side and said,

"Wrong, wrong, completely wrong. Izanami-no-Mikoto of Mount Rocky is Ōkunihime. Perform the saniwa examination more firmly. Which god do you think I am? Blind saniwa, open your spiritual eyes a little more and ascertain my true identity."

"No matter how skillfully you state it, you cannot hoodwink the eyes of this saniwa. Other matters aside, as for Izanami-no-Ōkami, I have firmly witnessed it. Speak no falsehoods; depart, depart!"

"I shall by no means depart. Until your spiritual eyes are opened."

Odotsumatsumi clasped both hands with all his might, attempting to apply a spiritual binding. Kagatora opened his mouth wide and said,

"Uwahaha—insolent, you are actually trying it. Uwahaha—it is so amusing that my belly skin is twisted. Uwahaha—!"

"Kuramatsumi-sama, this spirit possession cannot be relied upon. A terrible Ōmagatsu possesses him. As you see, he laughs rolling about, an audacious evil spirit that mocks us. You need not believe what such a fellow says. We are living witnesses; Izanami-no-Ōkami is certainly on Mount Rocky of this Tokoyo-no-Kuni."

Once again opening his mouth wide, Kagatora said,

"Ahaha, it is no good, it is no good, Odotsumatsumi, you blind saniwa! However much you apply spiritual binding, uuu, I do not budge, I do not budge. Is it painful, or have you grown weary? Ee, putting on the face of a great saniwa, what an unsightly state—can you not discern my true identity? It is hilarious, it is hilarious, uwahaha. Kaka, what a pitiful thing. Kiki, straining and straining, covered in muddy sweat, clasping both hands and groaning to apply a spiritual binding, what an unsightly state. Kuku, you have not had enough training. If a saniwa suffers over such a thing, how can you serve as an evangelist? Keke, outrageous fellow, you cannot make any guess; even if your menacing manner alone is strong, you cannot match a god. Koko, do you still insist on having your own way despite this? Are you not in trouble? Sasa, babbling on about being a saniwa and such—you are a groping saniwa whose spiritual eyes do not work at all. Shishi, say you do not know when you do not know, you audacious fellow. Opposing what the god says, saying this god is an evil god and cannot be relied upon, what kind of delirious talk is that? Susu, unless you are a saniwa attentive from corner to corner, the matters of the spiritual realm cannot be understood clearly. Sese, putting on the face of an evangelist, a blind saniwa examining me is utterly laughable. Soso, can the people of the world be guided with such a thing?"

"P-p-please, I understand now. Forbear, but are you not mistaken in your thought? Actually, as I accompanied Izanami-no-Ōkami and heard directly from her own mouth that she was going to Mount Rocky, this thing alone I cannot possibly accept as true."

Postscript

The divine intention of Izanami-no-Mikoto in giving birth to the god of fire and going to Yomotsu-kuni was to resort to a diversionary divine measure to seal within Yomotsu-kuni the Magatsu-kami who came raging and estranging themselves toward Ashihara-no-Mizuho-no-Kuni, preventing them from appearing upon the earth. From then on, as the Yomotsu-kami changed their abode to the Ryūgū of the sea and signs appeared that they would once again disturb Ashihara-no-Mizuho-no-Kuni, Izanami-no-Ōkami arrived at the Ryūgū of the sea once more, where she carried out a diversionary divine plan. Making Otomehime-no-Mikoto her substitute, she sealed the Yomotsu-kami within the Ryūgū, and being welcomed by Hinode-no-Kami, she declared that she would shut herself up in Mount Rocky, but secretly returned with Hinode-no-Kami and Omonagitsukasa to Mount Tenkyō, where Izanagi-no-Ōkami resided. Yet neither the gods nor the people of the world knew anything of this watertight divine plan, even in a dream. The Izanami-no-Mikoto who appeared on Mount Rocky was in reality Ōkunihime, the wife of Tokoyo-shin'ō, possessed by the evil spirit of the Golden Fox and usurping the divine name; and Tokoyo-shin'ō Ōkunihiko, possessed by the evil spirit of the Eight-Forked Serpent, falsely assumed the title of Hinode-no-Kami outwardly while advancing various strategic plans, ultimately bringing about the Battle of Yomotsu-hirasaka. Therefore, the ancient event in which Izanami-no-Mikoto stood facing him and pronounced the severance of ties at Yomotsu-hirasaka was not Izanami-no-Ōkami, the true guardian deity of the lunar realm, but an incarnation of Ōkunihime.

伊弉冊命の火の神を生みまして、黄泉国に至りましたるその御神慮は、黄泉国より葦原の瑞穂の国に向つて、荒び疎び来る曲津神達を黄泉国に封じて、地上に現はれ来らざるやう牽制的の御神策に出でさせられたるなり。それより黄泉神は海の竜宮に居所を変じ、再び葦原の瑞穂の国を攪乱せむとする形勢見えしより、又もや海の竜宮に伊弉冊大神は到らせたまひ、茲に牽制的経綸を行はせ給ひつつありける。乙米姫命を身代りとなして黄泉神を竜宮に封じ置き、自らは日の出神に迎へられて、ロッキー山に立籠るべく言挙げしたまひ、窃に日の出神、面那芸司とともに伊弉諾の大神の在ます天教山に帰りたまひぬ。されど世の神々も人々も、この水も漏らさぬ御経綸を夢にも知るものは無かりける。ロッキー山に現はれたる伊弉冊命はその実常世神王の妻大国姫に金狐の悪霊憑依して、神名を騙り、常世神王大国彦には八岐の大蛇の悪霊憑依し、表面は、日の出神と偽称しつつ、種々の作戦計画を進め、遂に黄泉比良坂の戦ひを起したるなり。故に黄泉比良坂に於て伊弉冊命の向ひ立たして事戸を渡したまうたる故事は、真の月界の守り神なる伊弉冊大神にあらず大国姫の化身なりしなり。
