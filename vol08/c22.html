Chapter 22: Princess Satsuki [372]

第22章　五月姫〔372〕

§ 1

On this day, in order to celebrate the Kunitama of the land of Haru, a great crowd came out onto the wide lawn, erected a himorogi, offered various things, drank heavily of the sake of the naorai feast, lit torches as night fell, and just as the feast had concluded and they were about to disperse, Gagatora came running at full speed and had begun to sing the propagation song. Gagatora, having been beaten by Kenkatora and Kenkayoshi, was just holding back his temper and enduring it when Ochi-Yamatsumi and Takahiko appeared, so he breathed a sigh of relief, sang the propagation song once again, after which Takahiko gave a speech on repentance, and then Ochi-Yamatsumi, in a refreshing voice, was singing the propagation song rhythmically.

From among the crowd, a beautiful woman like a celestial maiden appeared, bowed her head before the propagators, and

"Propagators, thank you very much indeed. I am Satsukihime, daughter of Kura-Yamatsumi, the chieftain of this region; if you would please employ me as your attendant with your great heart of great benevolence and great compassion, I shall be most grateful."

Thus she entreated them bashfully. Seeing the appearance of Satsukihime, the chieftain's daughter, appearing in this place and greeting the propagators politely, the crowd was greatly astonished and said to one another,

Person A: "What an important thing a propagator is! Satsukihime, the daughter of Kura-Yamatsumi who governs the eastern half of the land of Haru, bows her head to propagators who look like beggars and says, 'Please take me along as an attendant'; how I too would like to become a propagator, have such a beauty bow to me, and have her open her petal-like lips and beg, 'Please take me with you wherever you go!'"

Person B: "You lecherous fellow!"

And straightaway struck Person A across the face with a slap.

Person A: "Do not be jealous; even if you are jealous, nothing sweet will come of it. With a toad-like, worn-straw-sandal face like yours, who would ever say they want to follow you, even if you became a propagator? It would only be, 'I will poke you with a bamboo spear, poke you with dung stuck on the tip of a broken stake'—that is all it would be, ahaha!"

Gagatora turned toward Satsukihime and said,

"Ahem! Whatever one may call precious in this world, there is nothing as precious as performing the sacred role of saving all the people under heaven and polishing their vital spirits into crystal. Come now, follow us; Gagatora permits it. Excuse me, master—no, ugly—hold on, Propagator Ochi-Yamatsumi, my capability is just as you see. The kerria flowers of the bay are in full bloom!"

群衆の中から天女のような美人が現れ、宣伝使の前に頭を下げ、

「宣伝使様、誠にありがとうございました。私はこの地方の酋長である闇山津見の娘、五月姫と申す者、なにとぞ私を大慈大悲の大御心をもってお供にお使いくださいますればありがたく存じます」

と恥ずかしそうに頼み込む。群衆は酋長の娘の五月姫がこの場に現れ、宣伝使に丁寧に挨拶している様子を見て大いに驚き、口々に、

甲「なんと宣伝使というものは偉いものだな。巴留の国の東半分をお治めになる闇山津見のお娘の五月姫様が、あの通り乞食のような宣伝使に頭を下げて『何卒お供に連れて行ってください』とおっしゃるのだから、どうにか俺も一つ宣伝使になって、あんな美人に頭を下げさせたり、『私をどこまでも連れて行ってください』なんて、花の唇をパッと開いて頼ませたいものだ」

乙「この助平野郎」

と即座に甲の横面をピシャリと殴りつける。

甲「妬くなよ、妬いたっていいことなどありはしないぞ。貴様のようなヒキガエルの草鞋面に、誰が宣伝使になったからといって付いて行きたいなんて言う者がいるものか。『突いて行きます竹槍で、欠け杭の先に糞でも付けて突いて行きます』くらいのものだよ、アハハハハ」

蚊々虎は五月姫に向かい、

「エヘン、世の中に何が尊いと言ったところで、天下の万民を救って肝心の霊魂を水晶に磨き上げる聖なる役目をするほど、尊いものはありません。さあさあ、付いて来なさい、蚊々虎が許す。もしもしご主人、ではない、醜、どっこい淤縢山津見の宣伝使様、拙者の腕前はこの通り。お浦山吹の花が咲き盛りですよ」

§ 2

Satsukihime said, "No, no, I do not wish to be taken along by a person like you. No matter how noble a missionary you may be, with such a dark face it is too unsightly to walk outside, ho, ho, ho, ho, ho."

"Even though the color of my face is dark, the color of my heart is red. A red heart is the heart of God. Become of the heart of God, O people; man is a child of God, you are a child of man, and if you follow Kakatora, the missionary who acts in the stead of God, you will be safe. Splendid flowers shall bloom."

"Your nose is truly a magnificent nose like a peony. Your wife must surely be delighted. Affinities are strange things, as they speak of fortunate and strange affinities; for some reason, my feelings cannot take a liking to your face. By all means, I wish to accompany that gentleman over there."

Takahiko pressed his nose with his right forefinger, thrust his face out abruptly, and jerked his chin as if to say, 'Me?'

"Hey, barrier keeper, valley tumbler, half-dead survivor, wild bear, do not be conceited. She is a goddess who gives the cold shoulder even to me, Kakatora, the most handsome man in the world. Who on earth would ever follow a mug like yours?"

Takahiko said, "Excuse me, Satsukihime, of whom on earth do you speak? Do not just keep looking down as if embarrassed, but say it clearly. It must be me, Takahiko, is it not?"

Satsukihime shook her head from side to side and said,

"No, that is not so; that is not so."

"Then who is it?"

"The other gentleman."

"Making a fool of me! Hey, Shikome, Odo, Ghost, Shrine Destroyer, Gatekeeper of Ryūgū, there certainly are eccentric people in this world. To say that you like such a persimmon-tanned paper face! Hey, Missionary Shikome, treat us, treat us! Truly, to make me lose face before so many people, I, Kakatora, will stop doing missionary work together with you. When I had gone to great pains to preach to such a beauty, you came out casually from behind and sang a song in a hopeless voice, so my role was completely stolen away. Hey, Takahiko, let the two of us quickly leave this place."

"Kakatora, it will not go that way. Because we shall accompany this missionary wherever he goes."

"Ah, I see! Since this beauty follows behind the missionary, you speak plausible words with the intention of accompanying Satsukihime. And in the meantime, you hope to receive a whiff of even a foul fart from her—what a truly repulsive fellow you are! Whenever it comes to women, you narrow your eyes; what on earth is that shameful manner of yours?"

§ 3

"What is that look on your face? Hey, wipe your drool. You look unsightly."

Odoyamatsumi folded his hands in silence and let out a sigh. Satsukihime, as if having made up her mind, said,

"Excuse me, three honorable missionaries, my dwelling is indeed a small, humble hut, but it will suffice for you to stay. My father, Kurayamatsumi, is also extremely grateful for the propagation of the teachings of Ananai. Please follow me and come."

Saying so, she took the lead and began to walk.

Odoyamatsumi opened his mouth for the first time, saying,

"At any rate, let us meet Kurayamatsumi and have him listen to the doctrines of Ananai. In that case, we will accept your hospitality tonight."

"Ah, such prompt consent; my parents will surely be delighted. Here, attendants, bring the palanquin here."

"Aye!"

they answered, carrying out a palanquin from the darkness into the light.

"Please, honorable missionaries, ride in this."

"We are those who propagate the teachings throughout the world; hardship and tribulation are our heavenly calling. It is undeserved; we can by no means ride in a palanquin. If we must ride in a palanquin, we flatly decline."

"Well, if there is no rider, Kakatora will gladly take it."

Satsukihime said,

"It is not your palanquin."

Kakatora stuck out his tongue a little, saying,

"'She says it is not your palanquin!'"

While making a gesture of being elbowed away, Kakatora reluctantly followed behind Odoyamatsumi.

The palanquin vanished into nowhere while remaining empty. Satsukihime took the lead and, accompanied by the three missionaries, returned to the mansion of Kurayamatsumi.
