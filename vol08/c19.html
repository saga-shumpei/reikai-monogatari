§ 1

The missionary of Odoyamazumi turned to Arakuma, who was prostrate upon the earth, and

strictly commanded him: "Lord Takahiko, though you were heretofore a fearless warrior, the reason you have now fallen into such a cowardly and lingering spirit is, I surmise, that evil deities and malicious demons have possessed your body, weakening the soul bestowed by heaven and degrading you into a coward. In all things, when evil is in a person's heart, they fear matters, but when sincerity is in their heart, they fear nothing. From this moment, I shall replace your soul. Close your eyes and sit quietly here for a while."

At this strict command, Arakuma complied meekly and obediently, clasping both hands according to the instruction, and sat quietly with closed eyes upon the road.

The missionary clasped both hands, and having finished reciting the sacred auspicious words of one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand, he kept his hands clasped and emitted spiritual light from the tips of his index fingers, illuminating Arakuma's entire body. Instantly, Arakuma's body began to shake and he raged wildly to the front, back, left, and right; at the very instant he collapsed upon the earth with a cry of "Kya!", the evil spirits that had possessed him up to that moment departed from his body as though wiped away. As the missionary shouted out "Forgiven!", Arakuma was restored to his original state; his heart became filled with vigorous spirit, and even his facial complexion suddenly turned bright.

Arakuma sprang up, stomped heavily upon the ground, and

shouted vigorously, striking his arm: "As for myself, tracing back to the origin, I was the right hand of Shikobunowake, chancellor of the Great Self-Existent Heaven. From a momentary failure, my mind and soul were disheartened, and as a result of manifold anxieties I lost my composure and was possessed by the Eight-Forked Serpent; I was distressed even by the sound of the wind and the clamor of the rain, trembled in fear at the heads of eulalia grass, and the soul I received from the deity was almost completely ruined. While I was utterly weakened, by what divine arrangement did it happen that, in this remote mountain where human traces are rare, I met again with the missionary of Shikobunowake whom I once served, was saved from peril, had the malicious demons and evil deities that had infested my body purged away, and my heart has cleared, shining brightly and pure like the moon in the vast sky! Now that matters have come to this, against enemy armies of several millions or innumerable twisted deities, brandishing the clear mirror and drawing the sword of sincerity, cutting through in every direction, by the power of the word-soul that resounds throughout heaven and earth, I shall subdue Takatorihiko who coils in the capital of Haru and establish great merit! Ah, how joyous, how joyous, how delightful!"

Thus he struck his arm and uttered a triumphant shout.

The missionary, with a smile across his entire face, said,

"Ah, how valiant, how valiant! Lord Takahiko, let us now head toward the capital of Haru; please guide the way."

『コラコラ法螺を吹くにも程がある。黙らぬかい。言はして置けば調子に乗つて……ここを何と心得をる。数万の強敵を前に扣へて置いて、ソンナ気楽なことを言うて居る所で無いぞ』

『ヤー、ヤツパリ淤縢山津見ぢやなあ、数万の敵にオドオドして、向ふは真暗がり、暗墨の如うに、一寸先は真黒黒助だ。エヘン豪さうに口ばつかり、取越苦労はするな過越苦労は禁物ぢやのと、口先で立派なことを仰有るが、この蚊々虎さまはかう見えても刹那心、たとへ半時先に嬲殺しに逢はされやうが、ソンナ事は神様の御心に任して居るのだ。モシ宣伝使さま、さうぢや有りますまいかな。釈迦に説法か、負うた子に教へられて浅瀬を渡ると言ふのか、いやもうトンとこの辺が合点の虫が、承知しませぬワイ。まさかの時になつて来ると、宣伝使さまの覚悟も誠に怪しい頼り無いものだワイ』

と、目を剥き舌を少し出して、宣伝使の顔をチヨツと見上げる。

宣伝使は顔を少しくそむけながら、

『さうだなア。さう言へば、マアソンナものかい』

『ソンナものかいも有つたものかい。甲斐性無し奴が、ちと改心したか、エーン』

『蚊々虎、無礼で有らうぞよ』

斯かる所へ以前の荒熊は、呼吸を喘ませながら、坂道を上り来たりぬ。

蚊々虎は頓狂な声で、

『ヤー帰つたか様子は何んと、仔細は如何に、細に、言上仕れ』

『また貴様出しやばるな』

『出しや張るツて、刹那心ですよ。気が何だか急くから急いで問うたのですよ。決して取越苦労ではありませぬよ』

荒熊が、

『申し上げます、不思議なことには何時の間にか人影も無くなつて居ります。之には何か深い計略の有る事と思ひますが、軽々しく進む訳には行きますまい。一つこれは考へものですな』

『ナーニ刹那心だ。行く所まで行かな分るものかい。進め進め』

と蚊々虎は、先に立つて進み行く。

後に六人は路傍の岩に腰打ち掛け、何かヒソヒソと頭を鳩めて囁きゐたり。

蚊々虎は只一人、ドンドン腕を振りながら一目散に坂道を下り行く。
