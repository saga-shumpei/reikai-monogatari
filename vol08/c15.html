Chapter 15: Brazil Pass [365]

第15章　ブラジル峠〔365〕

§ 1

Parting the waves of the ocean Over which spring haze spreads, The apostle of Hinode-no-Kami rises; To purge the wicked crooked spirits, Shikokuniwake's matter-over-spirit Transforms into spirit-over-matter; A living soul purified through ablution, Renamed Odoyamazumi Of the heartfelt tachibana, Accompanying Kakatorahiko, The officer of servitude and the hipbone, Carrying the teachings, he approaches Mount Brazil at the border of Baru.

Although it was spring, it was a region of intense heat directly beneath the equator; while wrapping tree leaves all over his body to withstand the heat, he had the bent-waisted Kakatorahiko carry the luggage and climbed up Brazil Pass.

"Excuse me, please let me rest for a moment. My sweat pours like a waterfall, and my clothes and everything have become soaking wet as if caught in an evening shower. If there is water somewhere, I would like to drink a cup."

"Pull yourself together, Kakatora; what is this, when we consider that we tasted long hardships and tribulations at the bottom of the sea guarding the Golden Gate, can we complain of heat and suffering? There was not enough air, wherever we looked there was only water, and we could hardly breathe properly. However steep the slope may be, even if sweat pours forth, does not a cool breeze arrive from time to time? After sweating thoroughly, exhausting your legs, and reaching the point where you cannot advance even a single step, that is when you rest. The relief of that moment is when you truly understand the flavor of ease. The joy of being brought ashore from Ryūgū, that painful place where one could hardly breathe, is not even one ten-thousandth of that hardship, even if one's legs turn to sticks. You say such weak things because you have not suffered enough. Follow me."

"Is that not far too merciless? Since I have never been to Ryūgū, I do not know whether your words are a lie or the truth, but I understand that it is painful underwater. Yet, if one were truly underwater, would not one's breath be cut off before three or five minutes passed? Moreover, you stayed in Ryūgū for a long time, so considering that, I must take your words with a discount. If I am forced to walk this mountain path for even half an hour without resting, all the fluids of my body will turn entirely to sweat and pour out, and I will become a mummy inside such a hot mountain. Rather than saying such cruel things, have you not reformed your heart as well? There ought to be at least a little mercy."

§ 2

...he said, shedding tears.

"Hey, Kagatora, what is the matter with you? Are you not a man? How could you lose heart and shed tears over such a thing?"

"I am by no means crying."

"Then who is crying?"

"Yes, yes, I am a splendid, fully fledged man. If one is indeed a man, no matter what hardships and suffering one encounters, one does not flinch in the least. It is the guest attached to me who is crying."

"What do you mean by a 'guest'? Is it your secondary guardian spirit? What a crybaby it is. Since you are called Kagatora, a guardian spirit of mosquitoes must be possessing you. It is the retribution for having done nothing but evil deeds like sucking people's fresh blood until now. What has happened to your waist? Has it not bent completely into the shape of a dogleg? It is atonement for your past sins. Pay no mind to your secondary guardian spirit, summon the courage of your primary guardian spirit, and follow me."

"Under the name of Shikobunowake, you have done quite a lot of evil things up until now, have you not? Under your orders, I thought, 'This is bad; if I do such a thing, surely no good retribution will come,' but because you scolded me overbearingly from above, up until now I have done things against my own heart, like a fox borrowing a tiger's power. So to speak, you are the ringleader of evil. I was merely used like a machine."

"Humph! Either way, you are either a flea used, an unused louse, a mosquito that sucks people's fresh blood, a tiger, a wolf, a bear, or a wild dog."

"Excuse me, is that not going too far? Tiger and wolf refer to you! Having been saved by Hinode-no-Kami and given a splendid name such as Odotsumatsumi, you act so important, but you are an Odotsumatsumi who intimidates people. That is too much; reflect a little on the past as well. You should hardly talk so big. Since only you and I are here and no one is listening nearby, I will speak without reserve: if anyone is truly Shikonomagatsu, it is you!"

"In Ananai-kyō, dwelling on past hardships and worrying about the future are strictly forbidden. Because it is a teaching that re-examines, re-hears, and proclaims afresh everything in the spirit of Kaminaobi and Ōnaobi, you had better stop such foolish things as counting the age of a dead child. You had better not say a single word about things that have already passed."

"Well, well, how skillfully you speak! Did you not say that it was painful serving as a gatekeeper at Ryūgū? Is that not dwelling on past hardships?"

§ 3

"What a fellow you are for arguing! Wash away everything up to now clean into the river. And then, with not a speck of dark cloud in your heart, attain a spirit of pure and untainted clarity, and perform the service of God."

"Might not your base metal show through again? No matter how splendid a golden jewel it may be, if it is a gilded jewel like the one Takekuma possesses, it is said that it soon peels off. For if the base material is stone, no matter how much gold is applied, rubbing it two or three times causes the true nature to appear."

"Speak no nonsense, my soul is crystal to the core. Originally it is a splendid parted spirit. There is a saying that the pheasant would not be shot if it did not cry. Stop talking about the base material already."

"Hmph, you speak cleverly. The mouth is indeed a convenient thing."

"Oh, we have already reached the mountain summit. Hey, Kakatora, while we were talking, before we knew it, we arrived at the top of the mountain. Whining that it was bitter, bitter, and that you could not walk another step, you lost heart in an unmanly way; whether you are a deputy governor or whatever, your dried, tearful face was an unsightly sight. Now that we have come this far, a cool wind blows upon us, making us forget all our past hardships. If your face having turned black could also make you forget the hardships and become a white face, that would be convenient, but as for this, after all, the base material is iron, so it cannot become gold. Well, even if your face is black, there is no need to worry. It is perfect for sneaking about doing this and that in the darkness you are always so fond of. It is like a crow flying in the dark, so there is no one who can spot you. It is truly worth the trouble of all your hardships."

"Since it is mosquitoes that come out in the darkness after all, what you say may be true. It may also be mistaken. However, your name was promoted just now and you said it is Okutsuyamatsumi, but what a black name that is! Speaking of darkness like a crow flying on a fearful, trembling, dark night, you cannot say much about others. The god Hinode-no-Kami is indeed great. He bestows a name suitable to that. Bestowing the name Okutsuyamatsumi upon Shikotsuwake, who intimidates people, turns into dark clouds, and lines up incomprehensible, unclear, ink-like quibbles—how witty that is, ahahaha!"

"Hey, Kakatora, what are you saying to your master? That must be insolence."

"Hmph, do you remember that Hinode-no-Kami sang that the past is the past and the present is the present? The past is the past, the present is the present, and after that I have forgotten what followed. Ahem."
