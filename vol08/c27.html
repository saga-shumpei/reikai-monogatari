Chapter 27: The Great Desert [377]

第27章　沙漠〔377〕

§ 1

The blue sky was without a single speck of cloud, and the heavenly sun was shining at high noon.

Here, the four missionaries loaded numerous provisions onto several tens of camels, lightly mounted the backs of the camels, and parting reluctantly with Kurayamazumi and his wife, were about to cross the great desert and proceed toward the capital of Haru, when Satsukihime came out to the gate as if reluctant to part,

"Everlasting and unchanging as the solid rock In this world, poling a boat across the riverbed of Heaven In the far-reaching firmament, Descending to the river of Eden, The Southern Heavenly King, who abides In the village of Ken'on, rich in blessings, Manifested as the Sun-Rising God, Causing the divine teachings to shine Into all the four quarters of the world without a single dark corner; Proceeding to the Dragon Capital, The palace at the bottom of the thousand-fathom sea, He is the missionary, child of the teachings, Of the living God who rescued The divine beings suffering in the shallows of distress, Master Odotsumidukasa, Together with the other three missionaries; Summer sky, so hard to part with, Overcast like the darkness of the fifth month, Satsukihime, heart-stricken with sorrow, Is like a cuckoo crying as if spitting blood.

Having inherited the divine soul With the same aspiration to save the world, Though I am of woman's body, Why should I not become A pure minister who proclaims the words of God? The sinister gloom gathered in my heart, Which longs to dispel the darkness of the eternal realm, Please clear it away, Odotsumitsumi, Missionary, minister of the teachings! You to the capital, and I left behind, What joy could I possibly take? In a mortal life that knows not tomorrow, Should I merely spend empty months and days? Even if I vanish like dew on the wilderness, Even if I am buried in the dust of the desert, Thinking of the people of the world, my innermost heart Grows dark like the gloom of the fifth month; Quickly dispel it, O missionaries!"

Singing in a gentle voice, she parted reluctantly. Hearing this song, Kurayamazumi perceived what was in Satsukihime's heart; bringing out a new camel, he gave it to Satsukihime, and permitted her to enlighten the realm as a missionary together with Odotsumitsumi's company. Satsukihime felt as though she were soaring into the heavens, and here, the five missionaries, men and women, rode abreast, and came to cross the vastly wide great desert of Haru.

Here, the five missionaries, seen off with shouts of 'Uro, uro' by Kurayamazumi and many people of the land, set out in high spirits from Kurayamazumi's mansion, proceeding while singing missionary hymns. When they finally reached the great desert, a fierce wind raged from ahead, kicking up clouds of sand, so that they could not even face forward.

Bungotora raised his loud voice:

"Blow, wind, blow! Blow, whirlwind! Fly, sand, fly! Fly even up to heaven! Fall, rain, fall! Fall as much as you will! Even if the desert turns into a sea, Even if heaven descends and becomes earth, And earth ascends and becomes heaven, Whatever great calamity may come, With these Kotodama of Bungotora, Given by God, I will blow them scattered, Mowing down and clearing them away, Submitting the land of Haru Beneath the divine virtue! Before the divine power of Lord Bungotora, Any god and all people, Tigers, wolves, lions, and bears, Just like salted greens, Shrink, wither, and bowing obsequiously, Apologize right before our eyes! Blow, wind, blow! Blow as much as you like! Fly, sand, fly! Fly as much as you like! I am a god who flinches not at such things!"

と声をしとやかに歌って、別れを惜しむ。闇山津見はこの歌を聞いて五月姫の胸中を察し、新たに駱駝を引き出して来て五月姫に与え、淤縢山津見の一行とともに宣伝使として天下を教化することを許した。五月姫は天へも昇るような心持ちがし、ここに男女五人の宣伝使は轡を並べて、あれほどまでに広い巴留の大砂漠を横断することとなった。

ここに五人の宣伝使は、闇山津見をはじめ多くの国の人々に「ウロー、ウロー」という声に見送られ、意気揚々として闇山津見の館を後にし、宣伝歌を歌いながら進んで行く。いよいよ大砂漠にさしかかったところ、前方からは激しい風が吹き荒れて砂煙を上げ、まともに顔を向けることもできないほどであった。

蚊々虎は大音声を張り上げて、

『風よ吹け吹け、旋風よ吹け。 砂よ飛べ飛べ、天まで飛べ。 雨も降れ降れ、いくらでも降れ。 たとえ砂漠が海となり、 天が下りて地となり、 地が上がって天となる いかなる大難が来ようとも、 神から授かった蚊々虎の この言霊で吹き散らし、 薙ぎ払って、巴留の国を 従わせ伏させよう、神の徳で。 蚊々虎様の神力に、 いかなる神も諸人も、 虎や狼、獅子や熊も、 青菜に塩の如く、 縮んでしおれて、ペコペコと 謝り入るさまは目の当たりである。 風も吹け吹け、いくらでも吹け。 砂も飛べ飛べ、いくらでも飛べ。 そんなことにはびくともしない神である』

§ 2

"It is only natural that they are no match for us—we are the missionaries who transmit the teachings of the true Kami: Lord Tsukasa of Odoyamatsumi, the brave-hearted Komayamahiko, Takahiko with the nose of a tengu, Satsukihime resembling a heavenly maiden, and the completely fearless, adamantine-strong Lord Kagatora is here! Advance, advance, come, let us advance!"

Thus spouting whatever came to his mouth and blowing big brags, he straddled his camel and vigorously pressed forward defying the wind. At last, the wind stopped completely. Once again, the summer sun began to shine brilliantly.

Komayamahiko said, "Hey, hey, Kagatora, missionary, that was magnificent. Before those big brags of yours, even the wind-kami and all else would shrink away. How you did blow!"

Kagatora replied, "I blew because he was blowing. Since he blew indiscriminately and engulfed us in sand and smoke, I turned toward the wind-kami and blew, blew big brags, baffling both the wind-kami and all of you together!"

Takahiko said, "Hahaha, as always, you are a man skilled at bluffing!"

"Do not be so noisy; wait and see what lies ahead. Do not be surprised later; do you know what kind of deeds will be done?"

"Ahem, you are living only in the moment. Who can know the future? It would be best for you to make an effort and keep blowing your horn while you can. Just because the lone woman missionary, Satsukihime, is here amid this crowd of men, you suddenly gained energy and sang a boastful song, proud of your voice; yet while the wind-kami might be overwhelmed, Lady Satsukihime will not be swayed by such things in the least."

"Do not talk foolishly. Hey, hey, look at this boundless desert. Do you know how many days of riding it will take to reach the capital of Haru?"

"I know no such thing. Are you not possessed by the kami? If you understand all things in the universe, such a matter ought to be as clear to you as if reflected in a mirror."

Riding their camels, now singing missionary songs and now indulging in idle talk, the party finally arrived at the capital of Haru.
