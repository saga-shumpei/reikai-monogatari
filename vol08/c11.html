Chapter 11: The Dragon Palace of the Ocean [361]

第11章　海の竜宮〔361〕

§ 1

The branches of plants and trees on the foot-dragging mountain grow thick, And their leaves grow hazy in the spring breeze. Upon the waves of vast expanse stretching a thousand leagues, Emerging, an eight-fathom turtle bears Him on its back, Straddling it as He receives the spring sun, Parting the waves of a thousand fathoms, Sinking down, down to the bottom. Born as the child of Izanagi no Kami no Mikoto whose divine majesty shines, The reincarnation of Ōmichiwake no Mikoto, Hinode-no-Kami, at last, Seeks to explore the depth of the Divine Plan Of the palace of Ōwatatsumi no Kami, The Great God of fathomless depth, And how valiant it is that He advances thus.

Before the gate, the two deities, Masakayamatsumi no Kami and Odoshiyamatsumi no Kami, concentrated their breaths of A and Un like guardian Niō statues, stark naked, showing flexing muscles across their entire bodies, proudly standing guard. Odoshiyamatsumi no Kami stepped forward first and said:

"This is the entrance to the Dragon Palace. It is the dwelling place of the awe-inspiring Great God Ōwatatsumi; under the permission of which deity have you arrived here? You had better return swiftly to Hon-tsu-kuni, but if you disobey, I shall grant you a taste of this fist."

Even before he finished speaking, as he struck at Hinode-no-Kami, the eight-fathom great turtle, the incarnation of Kotohirawake, stood between the two deities and transformed into a boulder requiring a thousand men to pull. At that moment, a noisy sound was heard coming from within the gate. Hinode-no-Kami raised His loud voice and sang a song:

"By the word of the Heavenly Deities, To illuminate the darkness of the Eternal World, With a light heart in straw raincoat and hat, A missionary traveling around the world. In the heavenly sky, the vast ocean, And the divine land of Toyoashihara, In this world where the great blessings Spread without exception to every corner, This castle of the Dragon Palace alone, Because the divine protection is ever so deep, Is sometimes left out from the testing. Coming forth according to the mandate Of Ame-no-Mihashira no Ōkami, Where the morning sun shines and the evening sun glows, The missionary of the Divine Land of the Sun, Hinode-no-Kami appears, And to rescue Omonagi no Kami no Mikoto, Who has wandered here in confusion, Riding the turtle of Kotohirawake, I have appeared and arrived here. The ocean palace at the bottom of a thousand fathoms, Pushing open its rocky door, To explore the divine work Of the renowned Otomehime no Mikoto— Such is the purpose of this departure. Swiftly, without a moment's delay, Open this golden gate! I am Hinode-no-Kami. O Odoshiyamatsumi and Masakayamatsumi no Mikoto, Gatekeepers, Knowing nothing of the profound Divine Plan, How foolish are your bodies and minds! How foolish are your bodies and minds!"

When He sang this aloud in a resounding voice, the two deities were astonished by this song, and bowing low while trembling violently, expressed their apologies. Odoshiyamatsumi dashed into the gate at full speed, proceeded to the inner palace, and reported something. Masakayamatsumi went ahead of Hinode-no-Kami and ushered Him into a separate hall. As increasingly tumultuous noises were heard from one part of the castle, Hinode-no-Kami, deeming it no ordinary matter, listened intently and casually looked at the face of Masakayamatsumi,

"Ah, are you not Momogamihiko? Why do you guard the golden gate in such a place? In any case, what is that clamorous noise?"

When he pressed him with rapid words, Masakayamazumi said:

"Just as you have surmised, in the holy land of Jerusalem, I served for a time as the Archangel, but through my own failure, I brought trouble upon the Earth-Ancestor Kunihitotachi-no-Okami, was expelled in divine banishment by the myriad kamibito, and just when I was about to fall into the Ne-no-kuni and Soko-no-kuni, I was saved by the deeply compassionate Takateruhime-no-Kami; now I am of humble station serving as gatekeeper of Ryugu Castle, and meeting you in such a place as this fills me with shame indeed; Momogamihiko, who once prospered at the Ryugu on land, is subject to the worldly law of mutability, swept away by the rough waves of the world through my own unwitting error; in this ocean, so changed from yesterday, in the depths of a thousand fathoms of water, please perceive the day-and-night hardships and tribulations of this gatekeeper of the Ryugu of the sea."

Thus, his voice choked, he prostrated himself weeping on that spot.

As though unable to bear the feeling of sympathy, Hinode-no-Kami looked downward for a while and even shed tears of sorrow, but continuing his words, said:

"I sympathize with your present circumstance. There is something I must ask urgently. What is that noisy din? Speak of it in detail."

"As a mere gatekeeper, it is difficult for me to disclose the secret of the Ryugu sea. I can only leave it to your conjecture."

Thus he politely turned him away. The voices of agonizing cries grew increasingly fierce, feeling just like a scene of carnage.

Hinode-no-Kami sprang to his feet, and said:

"Momogamihiko, guide me to the inner palace."

While saying this, he started to stride forward rapidly. Momogamihiko, in a panic, said:

"Ah, please, wait a moment. Th-th-th-this is terrible! If you go to such a place, there is no telling what severe punishment you might receive from Otomehime. In the first place, I too would have to be thrown into that place where the terrifying voice comes from. First, please wait; I shall go and make inquiry first."

So saying, taking the lead, he walked briskly toward the inner palace and disappeared. Hinode-no-Kami was left alone, dumbfounded, looking around restlessly on all sides.
