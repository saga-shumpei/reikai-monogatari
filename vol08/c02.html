Chapter 2: The Fifty Divine Resonances [352]

第2章　五十韻〔352〕

§ 1

Hinode-no-kami, listening intently with interest to the idle talk as a compass to discern the trends of the world's morals and human hearts, crouched in a small corner of the ship, pretending not to notice and letting the words flow by. The self-proclaimed missionary Kiyohiko began earnestly to sing the propaganda song of the Ananai teachings.

Komayamahiko and Sarusehiko devoted all their efforts to disrupting him by singing the propaganda song of Uraruhiko. Yet, for some reason, the people aboard the ship sympathized with Kiyohiko of the Ananai teachings, continually asking for his sermon without ceasing.

Brimming with immense pride, Kiyohiko began a lecture full of contradictions and digressions, and proudly said,

"Every single thing in the world is illuminated upon the mirror of this person's heart. From the foundation of the universe at the largest, down to the inside of a louse's belly at the smallest, it is thoroughly transparent. Kiyohiko, the missionary of the Ananai teachings, is none other than myself. No matter what is asked, there is nothing I do not know. I am a missionary of the Ananai teachings without a single flaw, a so-called faultless missionary with no holes."

Thus he blew a great boast.

Person A: "Are you the reincarnation of Ono no Komachi? You said you have no holes, but how do you relieve yourself of feces and urine?"

Kiyohiko: "Those are not holes; they are pipes and cavities. There may be pipes and cavities, but there are no holes."

Sarusehiko: "Do not blow such empty tall talk. What on earth are your ears, nose, and mouth? Even so, do you have no holes? Indeed, because you are a missionary who confuses musk with the smell of farts, confounds the taste of sake with muddy water, and jumbles together the screams of ogres with the music of heavenly beings, your holes are blocked, and that must be why you are of the Ananai teachings. Ihi-hi-hi-hi!"

Kiyohiko: "Shut up and listen to my propaganda, you sake-drinking cultists. Those fellows of the Daichu teachings founded by Uraruhiko are Komayamahiko, who troubles people by spouting incoherent sermons as if always drunk on sake, and Sarusehiko, who mimics others well and mocks like a monkey laughing at another's backside."

The two, Sarusehiko and Komayamahiko, raged like a blazing fire and sprang at Kiyohiko, whereupon Kiyohiko,

"How cheeky!"

saying this, clenched his fists and struck the heads of the two with a blow. The two firmly gripped his left and right hands, and said,

"Hey, Kiyohiko, they say the Ananai teachings instruct to re-examine with Naobi and listen afresh. Even if I strike you hard, if you are truly a believer of the Ananai teachings, you must re-examine it!"

saying this,

"You cowardly rogue!"

and again clenching their fists, they struck him with a thump.

Sarusehiko: "Hey, Kiyohiko, the Ananai teachings say to listen afresh. Even if called an idiot, listen afresh. You coward, you hole-hunter!"

Sarusehiko: "Now, if you want to spout, try spouting; I will treat you to another fist."
