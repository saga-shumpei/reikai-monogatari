Chapter 10: The Sacred Syllabary Exemplar [360]

第10章　仮名手本〔360〕

§ 1

The groaning sound of the Mirror Pond gradually subsided. This time, in a solemn and heavy voice, great words were heard coming one after another from the bottom of the pond.

"Saruyohiko, makeshift missionary who mimics monkeys: coming over to the country of Chiri, where the light of the Kami is most pure, and professing to be a missionary of the Ananai Teachings which does not suit your nature—how boldly you spouted such words! Your prayer is truly magnificent indeed. Do not brag grandly from now on; if you pretend to know what you do not know, you will suffer painful experiences like this and have to expose your shame; there is no need to speak any reasoning. You had better preach with humility, saying merely, 'I am a fool; other than praying to the Kami, I do not know even the letter 'I' of the Iroha.' A little learning is the source of great injury. Even granting that you do not know, you are too dreadful; listen to what I say, if only a little, for your future study. It is the Way of the Kami opened by the forty-eight letters of the Iroha."

Saruyohiko said, "Excuse me, O Kami at the bottom of the pond, I cannot stand up on my hips. Please let my hips stand up."

From the bottom of the pond:

"'I'—Will you not pray, will you not pray? Prayer is the foundation of life. If you desire life that lives on through myriad kalpas and all eternity, pray always, pray.

'Ro'—Rather than concocting worthless and foolish logic, conduct yourself with discretion and become a mirror to others.

'Ha'—Nothing is as splendid as swift repentance. Humans born naked must become like newborn infants in heart.

'Ni'—Makeshift faith will not serve in time. Teach them to practice faith at ordinary times.

'Ho'—Your preaching is like carving a Buddha without giving it eyes; repent from the very roots and attain the true heart of the Kami.

'He'—Clumsy, lengthy discourses are strictly forbidden. Do not speak quibbles; do not falter midway; do not lose heart.

'To'—Carry sincerity through to the very end. The protection of the Kami knows no barrier of near or far; govern people with virtue.

'Chi'—Do not rely on intellect and learning. What becomes strength is only the power of the Kami and faith. Do not be led astray by immediate desires; do not eat the flesh of beasts.

'Ri'—Do not run after logic; do not be blinded by selfish greed. Do not lose your soul solely to your own advancement in the world and stray from the path of sincerity.

'Nu'—Do not steal; do not blunder; walk the Way of the Kami with a spirit as though standing amidst drawn blades; do not think of the glory of forestalling others.

'Ru'—Even in an unoccupied house, the Kami is present. Do not bring forth an evil heart thinking it to be empty."

"O—The fearful thing is thine own mind. By the single disposition of thy mind, demons, giant serpents, and wolves alike shall come forth. Become not cowardly; take heed with one another and make thy way through this world.

Wa—Put thine own self behind and place others first. Evil deeds must not be done even to the extent of a speck of dust. Cease thy selfishness, act not out of self-interest, and do not commit evil to be mocked."

Sarusehiko: "Wa—I understand. I understand. When I hear the words of Thy Noble Divinity, somehow,

Ka—I have become sorrowful. Pray forgive me; I cannot bear it, I cannot bear it.

Yo—I have understood well. Pray cease now; I shall not perform covetous things. Whatever concerns the world, I shall do anything.

Ta—Help me, I beseech thee. Whosoever met with such terrors could not endure it, whether standing or sitting.

Re—Even if I am continuously made to hear difficult preachings like farting from within the water,

So—I cannot comprehend it. Pray speak softly into my ear in a small voice; if thou raisest a voice like a cracked bell, or makest a sound like blowing through a bamboo pipe, I cannot understand in the least.

Tsu—Tedious, tedious; whether thou art the deity Tsukiteruhiko or whatever, if thou hast already spoken that much, what thou hast to say ought to be exhausted.

Ne—From root to leaf I cannot understand at all. Pray reveal thy form."

From the bottom of the pond:

"Na—What sayest thou? Utter no whining complaints; a weak propagator such as thee, unless thou undergoest a little more hardship,

Ra—The Way shall not open with ease.

Mu—Deemest thou it unreasonable? The deity speaketh no unreasonable things.

U—Listen not heedlessly; become of the beautiful divine mind, and become a propagator who unfoldeth the divine teachings."

Sarusehiko: "Wi—Thy endless preaching is all very well, but why dost thou not cease already? It is so painful that it severely pierceth to the bone, and

No—I feel as if I were vomiting blood from this throat."

From the bottom of the pond:

"Inextricable clamp and nail,

O—Resign thyself. In moderation,

Ku—After hardship, there is joy.

Ya—The reason I reprimand thee clamorously is that I hold thee dear.

Ma—Hearken well unto the words of the true deity; there are no double words in the words of the deity. If thou mishear them now, thou shalt never hear them for myriad ages. Through the dark minds of mortals, the divine plan of the true deity...

Ke: Your aim cannot be taken; the Way of God, which does not deviate by a hair's breadth, must not be defiled.

Fu: Ponder deeply, polish your soul, and stand in divine service!"

Saruyohiko said, "Ko: With this, it is already quite enough. What an occasion today is—grateful, painful, seeming splendid yet not splendid, seeming joyful yet not joyful..."

From within the pond:

"E: Do you still not understand?

Te: Are you not a missionary who conveys the teachings of the God of heaven and earth?

A: Saruyohiko, who have committed evil until now, mend your heart from now on,

Sa: Thoroughly cleanse your body and soul, transform yourself into a newborn infant entirely clean,

Ki: And assist and guide the people of the world with a pure, upright, and straight heart of sincerity.

Yu: Never forget what God says. Always brace your heart and keep your spirit taut like a drawn bow;

Me: Because your body and soul are deep in sinful obstacles, I must make you labor,

We: Do not speak arrogantly, for the Enma of this world shall appear and break your high nose.

Hi: Pray to God both day and night.

Mo: Polish your body and soul until God says that this is already enough. Once you have caught the eye of God, I will grant you any divine virtue.

Se: Do not hold a narrow heart; become a broad and warm divine mind and guide the people of the world.

Su: Do not forget, even while awake, what the divine soul of Tsukiteruhiko-no-Kami of the clear open sky says, Saruyohiko; I am originally Ōyashimahiko no Mikoto, the Archangel of the Ryūgū Castle. You too have acted quite haughtily, but from now on reform your heart entirely, become the governor of this country, become Governor Sayorihiko, and devote yourself to the world. This Takasago Island is under the divine protection of Ryūsehime-no-Kami, a separated spirit of Kin-katsu-kane-no-Ōkami; this Mirror Pond is the back gate leading to the Land of the Bottom, so if there is anything you do not understand, come to ask again!"

With a groan of "U—", the voice abruptly ceased. Sayorihiko and all the rest were able to stand on their feet for the first time. The entire company, rejoicing and encouraged, chanted the divine words toward the Mirror Pond.
