Chapter 18: The Barrier Keeper of Paru [368]

第18章　巴留の関守〔368〕

§ 1

Odoyamatsumi, who had leapt down into the thousand-fathom ravine clamorous with the roaring spray of the rushing torrent, helped up Arakuma who was suffering from serious wounds, carried him upon his back, and at last came running up to this place; he set Arakuma down upon the earth, chanted divine words, performed soul-pacification, and blew breath toward the wound on his head, whereupon, wondrously, Arakuma's injuries healed as if wiped clean, his pain completely ceased, and he was restored to his original body. Arakuma placed both hands upon the earth, thanked him for his great benevolence with tears, and apologized for his rudeness.

"Hey, Master Ara, hold on, Master Black Bear, the divine virtue of Ananai-kyō is like this. Clear out your earholes and listen closely. Ahem, Lord Kakatora's—"

While saying this and pressing his nose with the tip of his finger,

"The moment I snorted from these big nostrils, you opened your mouth wide like a newt drunk on mud, grasped at the empty air shouting 'Ah, ah!' and fell backward head over heels, tumbling with a crash into this deep, deep valley bottom and splitting your head open, and while soaked in the valley stream like a river frog, you were spouting such nonsense as: 'Ouch, ouch, ouch! Oh dear, what an evil day this is; of all places, to be hurled into such a deep, deep valley bottom and die here; afterward, my wife will surely regret it so much. Though I do not begrudge this dying body, yet the sky of today changes from yesterday, and though it is said to be an uncertain world, even so, even so, it is too much; can misfortunes pile up like this! so my wife will surely weep,' and right then, Heaven does not kill a person, so the teacher of us of Ananai-kyō, Shikotsuwake—whoops, Lord Odoyamatsumi—calmly appeared, and saved you with a divine heart of embracing all and abandoning none, of great mercy and great compassion. How grateful, how august! Come, repent; pride is the root of great injury. The God of Ananai-kyō says that if you are conceited, you will surely fall into the bottom of a valley, meet with a terrible experience, and be brought to your knees. I have shown you the true reality of that. There is nothing as splendid as repentance. Ahem!"

"Hey, hey, Kakatora, won't you be silent? What big lies you blow, having only heard scraps of the God's teachings; you are a hopeless fellow. Be silent and listen to what I say."

と言いつつ指の先で鼻を押さえながら、

「この大きな鼻の穴からふんと息吹をやったが最後、貴様はイモリが泥に酔ったように大きな口を開けて、ああーと虚空を掴んで仰向けにひっくり返ったが最後、この深い深い谷底へすってんどうとひっくり返って頭を打ち割り、『痛たたた、痛たたた、ああ今日は何という悪日か、場所もあろうにこんな深い深い谷底へ投げ出され、ここで死ぬのか、後で妻はさぞかし悔やむであろう。死ぬこの身は厭わないが、昨日に変わる今日の空、定めのない世とはいいながら、それにしてもあまりにひどい、不運が重なる時は重なるものか、と言って妻が泣くであろう』などとくだらないことを、カジカのように谷水に漬かって吐き散らかしたそこへ、天道は人を殺さず、三五教の俺たちの先生様の醜国別、おっとどっこい淤縢山津見様が悠然として現れなさって、摂取不捨、大慈大悲の大御心を持ってお助けくださったのだ。なんと有難いことか、もったいないことか、ええい改心をせよ、慢心は大怪我のもとだぞ。慢心するとその通り、谷底に落ちてひどい目に遭ってあふんと参らねばならぬぞと、三五教の神様は仰せなのだ。その実際の手本をこちらが見せてやったのだぞ。改心ほど結構なものはないぞ。えへん」

「これ、これ蚊々虎、黙らないか。なんという法螺を吹くのだ、神様の教えを聞きかじって、仕方のない奴だ。黙って俺の言うことを聞いておれ」

激潭飛沫囂々と音騒がしき千仭の谷間に、身を躍らして飛び入り、重傷に悩む荒熊を助け起して吾背に負ひ、漸く此処に駆上つて来た淤縢山津見は、荒熊を大地に下して神言を奏上し鎮魂を施し、頭部の傷所に向つて息を吹きかけたるに、不思議や荒熊の負傷は拭ふが如く癒え、苦痛も全く止まりて元の身体に復したり。荒熊は大地に両手をつき高恩を涙と共に感謝し、且つ無礼を陳謝したりける。

『オイ荒さま、ドツコイ黒坊の熊さま、三五教の御神徳とはコンナものだい。耳の穴を浚つてとつくりと聞かう。エヘン、蚊々虎様の』

と云ひつつ指の先で鼻を押さへながら、

『この大きな鼻の穴からフンと伊吹をやつたが最後、貴様は蠑〓__34696__が泥に酔つたやうに大きな口を開けよつて、アヽアーと虚空を掴んで仰向けに顛覆返つたが最後、この深い深い谷底へスツテンドウと顛覆返つて頭を打ち割つて、「アイタツタツタ、コイタツタツタ、アーア今日は如何なる悪日かと、処もあらうにコンナ深い深い谷底へ取つて放られ、此処で死ぬのか、後で女房は嘸やさぞ、悔むであらう。死ぬるこの身は厭はねど、昨日に変る今日の空、定め無き世と云ひながら、さてもさてもあまりだわ、不運が重なれば重なるものか、と云つて女房が泣くであらう」などと下らぬ事を、河鹿のやうに、谷水に漬つて吐きよつた其処へ、天道は人を殺さず、三五教の俺らの先生様の醜国別オツトドツコイ淤縢山津見様が悠然として現はれたまひ、摂取不捨、大慈大悲の大御心をもつてお助け遊ばしたのだよ。何と有難いか、勿体ないか、エーン改心を致せ、慢心は大怪我の基だぞよ。慢心するとその通り、谷底に落ちて酷い目に遇つてアフンと致さねばならぬぞよと、三五教の神様は仰有るのだ。その実地正真を此方がして見せてやつたのだぞ。改心ほど結構なものは無いぞよ。エヘン』

『コラ、コラ蚊々虎、黙らぬか。何といふ法螺を吹く、神様の教を聞きかじりよつて、仕方のない奴だ。黙つて俺の云ふ事を聴いて居れ』

§ 2

"Hmph, even if you did not put me to shame in front of the crowd, you might at least have let me save a little face."

So he muttered in a low voice. Arakuma looked up steadily at the propagator's face,

"Well, well, were you not Lord Shikokuniwake?"

"Oh, are you not Takahiko, you who speak thus? Well, well, we have met in a strange place. Why on earth did you come to such a place as this? At the time of the Tokoyo Conference you heckled with considerable vigor; will you not tell me the reason why it has come to this?"

"Yes, yes, I shall tell you everything without concealment, but, you were Lord Shikokuniwake, prime minister to Daijizaiten-sama, and it was said that you had departed for a distant land called the hidden realm, so how on earth have you come here; could you perhaps be a g-ghost?"

"I am neither a ghost nor anything of the sort."

Explaining that the matter was in truth such and such, he recounted the past events in detail, and listened intently to the story of Takahiko's personal history. Takahiko, with tears welling up in both eyes,

"When you served Daijizaiten as prime minister, I was granted a considerably respectable post through your protection, and used to walk with swagger; but after you passed into the hidden realm, it became the world of Takatoribake, and being slandered by evil persons, I incurred the displeasure of Tokoyo Shin'ō-sama, was driven out of the land of Tokoyo, my wife, children, and kin were scattered, and having no harbor of refuge anywhere, like an abandoned boat upon the strand, I was finally washed ashore in the land of Haru, landed in this country under cover of night, became a laborer, mingled among the working folk, and fortunate in possessing a little strength, I am now merely a head of five men, serving as a barrier-guard of this land of Haru, spending joyless days and months. In this land of Haru the power of Tokoyo Shin'ō is not to be trifled with, and now Izanami-no-Mikoto-sama has appeared from somewhere and settled upon Mount Rocky, so that the power of Tokoyo Shin'ō flourishes more and more; this land of Haru is the domain of Takatoribake, where an exceedingly strict system has been established, such that not a single person from other lands can enter this country. If by any chance you should proceed beyond here, I will be unable to fulfill my duty as barrier-guard, will be dragged before Takatoribake, and must undergo judgment. If at that time Takatoribake, who knows my face, should glare at me saying, 'Aha, are you not Takahiko?', I must again be driven out of this country and suffer bitter hardship. Having had my life spared with great pains, to tell you now to return back without having repaid that favor would be the way of returning evil for good; yet if I let you proceed, I must meet the plight I have just spoken of, so if you are to go on, please sever the head of this barrier-guard Arakuma before you go."

So speaking, he shed tears like a waterfall and prostrated himself upon the earth, weeping. Kakatora burst out laughing,

"Bwahaha, what a weak fellow you are! What of it, that trifling Takatoribake—is that fellow so terrifying? Just as I blew you away with my snort, I will blow Takatoribake away as well! Hey, do not worry; follow Kakatora, and I will make you the king of the land of Haru! Amusing, amusing!"

"Hey, Kakatora, you talk too much. The guardian deities of this land are listening all around here."

The summer wind that chanced to blow conveyed the whispers of this place and carried them off to the capital of Haru.

と滝のような涙を流して大地に泣き伏した。蚊々虎は笑い出し、

「おい蚊々虎、貴様は口が過ぎる。この国の守護神が、その辺り一面で聞いているぞ」

折から吹き寄せる夏の風が、この場の囁きを乗せて巴留の都へと送り届けていく。
