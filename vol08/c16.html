Chapter 16: Spiritual Binding [366]

第16章　霊縛〔366〕

§ 1

The party, gazing at the surrounding scenery from the summit of Brazil Pass, indulged in idle talk. A cool wind blew violently, growing increasingly fierce until the surrounding trees were on the verge of toppling over. Kagatora clung tightly to the roots of a nearby tree and said,

"Excuse me, Lord Propagator, what shall we do? I shall be scattered, I shall be scattered!"

"That is why you are called a mosquito. Just because a wind like this has blown, what do you mean by clinging to the roots of a tree and crying, 'I shall be scattered, I shall be scattered'? Speaking as though you were being poured sake, you coward! When we go to the land of Baru from now on, winds like this will blow continuously every day. We must cross the great desert riding on camels' backs, but with your weakness, you might be blown right off the camel's back like a mosquito. Ah, a journey is best taken alone. Having such a burden with me pulls me back by the hind-hair, so that I can neither advance nor do anything; what a nuisance it is!"

"Excuse me, Lord Propagator, do not speak so grandly. Even if you say you are pulled back by the hind-hair, you do not have a single hair on your head. Look at my head; there is plenty of magnificent hair like a palm tree—ahem, ahem!"

"Yours is not hair of the head. That is just fur. Hair grows upon the heads of true human beings, while beasts grow fur on their heads. The top of my head is true hair. That is, hair is a mirror shrunk down. It shines well, does it not?"

"With a head upon which even a mosquito would slip and fall if it landed, how could there be anything of a deity about it? It is not a god, but a biting god when a mosquito lands! Ahahaha!"

"What are you saying? I am, with all due reverence, the Great Deity Kashiraterasu-no-Ōkami. Surely you know that the divine body of the Great Deity Kashiraterasu-no-Ōkami is the Yata-no-Kagami."

"Hmph, do not speak such naive nonsense. As expected of the Lord Propagator! The chief retainer of Daijizaiten, doing nothing but evil deeds, driven off to the Land of Ne and the Land of Soko, finally selling his homeland, wandering around the entire world—a plausible beggar full of sour grapes. It sounds grand when you are called Lord Propagator, but you are something like a boss of beggars. How could there be any Great Deity Kashiraterasu-no-Ōkami? You are Kunidokoro-tachinoki-no-Mikoto, the Deity of Eviction from the Realm!"

"I hereby dismiss you. Go home from now. No matter what you say, I will not take you along."

(Gidayū style)

"Do you mean to say that no matter what, you will not take me along? That is far too heartless and cruel. The more I reflect upon it, could there be anyone in this world as ill-fated as I? To think of the joy when I became a retainer to that deity of renowned exploits, Shikocunibake, who was the servant of Daijizaiten manifested in the Land of Tokoyo, and who could shoot down even flying birds from the sky; whether it should have been or should not have been, I defiled the Sanctuary of Jerusalem where the awe-inspiring deities of Heaven and Earth were enshrined, and by that divine punishment my hips ached, bending completely into the shape of the letter Ku, so that I was nicknamed Lord Kagatora and now crouch low; yet if one traces my origin, I am of the lineage of a noble deity, Tokoteruhiko, born as the secret child of Tokoyohime, the child of the deity Wakasakurahime. Had times been otherwise, would I have become an attendant to this incomprehensible Odotsuyamazumi, forced to bear heavy baggage, staggering up Mount Brazil with sweat and tears, and before I can even catch my breath, being told that it is enough and to go home now? How truly cold and merciless! If there be deities in this world who distinguish between good and evil, Odotsuyamazumi is Shikocunibake, a propagator of body-over-spirit, a fellow knowing neither duty nor compassion; evil was indeed evil after all. Even Kagatora, the tabby cat pretending to be a tiger, is dumbfounded, and under these present circumstances where I can neither weep nor refrain from weeping, how shall I vent my resentment? Now adopting the solemn-sounding name of Odotsuyamazumi, his essential soul is none other than Shikocunibake of Shiko-no-Magatsu, whose true nature has been exposed, making this a pitiful state of affairs. Yet far more pitiful still is to abandon Kagatora alone in the depths of these mountains with aching legs—what truly astonishing, evil soul! As long as the cord of my life continues, I will cling to this fellow's heels, expose all his past faults, and hinder him without fail! Ya, ton-tsun-ten-chin-chin-chin!"

"Hey, hey, Kagatora, do not say foolish things. Are you in your right mind, or do you truly think so from your heart?"

"If it were not my true nature, what else would it be?"

So saying, he waved his hands, twisted his mouth, and began to dance with comical gestures.

"Ha, ha, ha, you are a carefree fellow. Even if you perform a kyōgen play in such a place, there is no one to watch or listen. To whom do you intend to show it?"

"You are a missionary of the realm; do you not realize that this many guardian deities are listening without any gap? I am not letting you hear it. Do you not know my scheme to treat the guardian deities around here to your shame, cause spirit possession at every destination, and strip bare your flaws? Look, look, there is a guardian deity there too; look, look, over there too is a guardian deity; many four-legged spirits are also listening with amusement. Can you not see them, can you not see them? What a pity, is it not a pity?"

At this time, shouts numbering no one knew how many hundreds of thousands overwhelmed the surroundings, resounding 'Uwaan' like the buzzing of mosquitoes. After a little while, as the voices of hundreds of thousands of people, 'Uwahahaha' resounded from all around, with only voices being heard. Okitsuyamazumi clasped both hands, changed the color of his face, sat cross-legged on the earth, and became like one lost in thought.

Kagatora's complexion suddenly became like fire, and while keeping both hands clasped, he leaped around forward, backward, left, and right,

"Ku-ku-ku, Kuni, Kuni, Kuni, Te-te-teru, teru, Hi-hi-me-me, Kuniteruhime,"

so he began to speak.

Okitsuyamazumi at once straightened his posture, clasped both hands, and undertook the saniwa spirit discrimination.

"One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand,"

and along with the divine words chanted, Kagatora stamped upon the earth with a roar and began to dance.

"Thou Kuniteruhime, what deity art thou?"

"I am Kuniteruhime, the reincarnation of Kitsunehime, who shut herself up in Mount Kijōzan and, together with Miyamahiko, obeyed the orders of Tokoyohime no Mikoto and toiled day and night to occupy the Earthly Takamanohara. You are Shikotsuwake, who has now become the noble Okitsuyamazumi-no-tsukasa, a senior disciple of Hinode-no-Kami, and a splendid missionary. I repented my past errors, was discovered by the deity Konohanahime-no-Kami, and together with Uraruhiko, who founds the divine capital in the plain of Armenia, underwent rooting and reform of heart, and now have become a noble and true deity, who opens Anaikyō in the plain of Armenia, establishes divine government, and spreads the teachings of Haniyashiko no Mikoto to the realm. You are about to depart from here for the Country of Haru to propagate the teachings, but postpone it for a while, turn back, cross this sea, and return to the capital of Armenia. In the Country of Haru, the plan of the divine realm has changed and Hinode-no-Kami Himself is personally departing; never doubt it. There is no mistake that I am Kuniteruhime."

Odoyamazumi gathered strength throughout his whole body, recited the divine words, and shouting "Un!" emitted spiritual light toward the spirit-possessed Kagatora, whereupon Kagatora fell prostrate upon the earth, writhing in agony and blowing foam from his mouth.

"You must be an evil deity. You who, fearing that we should reach the land of Haru, use the physical body of this Kagatora to deceive the propagandist of the realm—you instigator of Magatsu, there shall be no forgiveness. Confess!"

"How utterly insolent toward Kuniteruhime, the august messenger of the God of the Rising Sun! I will show no mercy!"

"What talk of showing mercy or not! It is I who shall grant no mercy!"

As he said this, he cried "Un!" once more, drew a circle in the air with his right index finger, and cast a spiritual binding, whereupon:

"O-ouch, it hurts, it hurts! Forgive me, forgive me! I-I will confess. I am Y-Yachimatahiko, a follower of Yamata-no-Orochi. This land of Haru is our hiding place, and having you come here now is a grave crisis for our comrades, so I feigned that Kuniteruhime had reformed, scheming to drive you back from this island. Bound like this, I can do nothing. Now, from here on, our clan will flee toward the Rocky Mountains, so please release the spiritual binding upon my body. I b-beg of you, I beg of you!"

"If you depart from the land of Haru and go out beyond the sea, I will spare you. Under no circumstances may you go to the Rocky Mountains. Well, do you agree?"

The spirit-possessed Kagatora nodded his head vertically again and again in silence. When Odoyamazumi called out "Un!" and released the spiritual binding, Kagatora's body instantly recovered completely as before, and wiping away the flowing sweat, he said,

"Ah, what an awful ordeal it was! Something, I know not what, hung onto my body, and I had a terrible time. Now, now, Master Propagandist, shall we be on our way soon? Nothing good can come of staying in such a place."

With these words, Kagatora, having returned to his senses, took the lead and went down Mount Brazil to the west.
