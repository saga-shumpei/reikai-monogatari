Chapter 29: Harayamatsumi [379]

第29章　原山祇〔379〕

§ 1

The five missionaries, heading toward the castle town of Haru while singing the propaganda song, advanced briskly. Kagatora, standing at the head and rolling his eyes, kept watch in all directions before and behind, to the left and right; knowing not when the enemy might attack, he clenched his fists ready to strike with his iron fist if any approached, squared his shoulders, and advanced with a strange gait. In the castle town, people gathered here and there in groups of three, five, or ten, looking upon the attire of these missionaries and making various rumors.

Person A: "Hey, they say the missionary who came the other day met with a terrible fate at the hands of Lord Takatoribake and ended up buried in the desert, but doesn't the fellow who has arrived now look far stronger? Surely he has come for revenge. Another amusing disturbance is about to begin. Look at those glaring eyeballs. If stared down by such eyes, no matter how powerful Lord Takatoribake may be, he would shrink back in fear."

Person B: "What, look at that waist of his; it is completely bent crooked. Strutting proudly down the main road swinging his arms and walking like a Hachiōjin, a fellow like him will easily be plucked up and tossed out just like his bent waist."

Person A: "Hey, what is that? There is a stunning beauty. What carefree missionaries! To bring along a wife and come singing into an enemy castle town like this—unless one had immense courage, it is not a feat that could be done."

Person A: "Just now, the camel corps from inside the castle set out with tremendous vigor, but when they returned, did they not flee back with pale faces saying a fireball had appeared? That fellow must be quite formidable."

Catching this voice in his ears, Kagatora made a triumphant face,

"Hey, you humans there, what did you just say? You said a fireball appeared, didn't you?"

The crowd: "Yes, yes, we said it."

"Do you understand where that fireball came from? Awesomely enough, this of the missionary of Ananai-kyō shone forth!"

And he pointed at his own eyes with his finger.

Takahiko: "Hey, hey, will you not go straight ahead without wasting time?"

"What, spouting off as if I were a cow or a horse; whatever, it matters not! Follow behind Master Kagatora. Creeping timidly into the center of the five, Takahiko, what kind of appearance is that? The one standing at the front line is none other than Master Kagatora. Sing, sing!"

"God appears out of the background to the front, Dividing and distinguishing good from evil. Haughty and arrogant, looking down upon the people of the world As inferiors, a demon-faced roof tile, Uncaring of cold or heat, The long-nosed Takatoriwake Appeared in the capital of Haru, Squeezing out raw blood, sucking and drinking The sweat and fat of the populace. God appears out of the background to the front, Dividing and distinguishing good from evil, And those of sincerity He goes to save into the heavenly realm of Hisakata. The land of Haru is like hell: Though spring comes, no flowers bloom; Though autumn comes, no fruit ripens; To save the populace Who shiver and tremble In the cold of winter, the propagators have come. O people of the capital of Haru,

Awaken your eyes to the teachings of God. Whether the morning sun shines or is clouded over, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the earth should sink, The Ananai-kyō, protected by the true God, Saves the world. The renowned propagator of Otoyamatsumi no Mikoto; Illuminated by the two eyes Of the shining Kagatora, Tokoyo no Magatsumi flees away. In the skies of the fifth month, where black and white cannot be told apart, Hinode-no-Kami appears, Satsukihime illuminating the world. Obstructed on all sides, the spirit's Horse of the heart, Yamahiko no Tsukasa. In the great desert so vast, Straddling the back of a camel, The propagator of Takahiko of high divine virtue, Who teaches the Way.

O people of the capital of Haru, Wash your eyes, awaken your eyes, Wash your eyes and awaken your eyes. In all things of the human world, Re-examine and re-hear with a straight spirit. Propagators of the true God, Without flinching, without fearing, steadily Appear before us. Quickly listen to the way of salvation, Quickly board the ship of salvation, Do not miss boarding the ship of God."

Thus singing, they headed toward the castle gate of Takatoriwake without the slightest fear. At this moment, rumbling through the sky, countless thousands of Heavenly Rock Boats and Bird Boats concealed themselves high in the northern sky.

Otoyamatsumi, unperturbed, said:

"Ahahaha, even the formidable Takatoriwake was terrified by the mighty power of the kotodama, cowered at the propagation song, and fled. This is what it means to win without staining a blade with blood. However, carelessness is a great enemy; let everyone be vigilant."

"Well, Propagator, it is entirely due to the kotodama of Kagatora. Just now, a vast army like clouds and mist seemed to be blown away by our breath, and yet again they cowered at our propagation song and fled. Truly, I do not know why this Kagatora possesses so much divine power. Even to myself, there is nothing to do but marvel, is there not?"

"Hey, hey, even if you try to monopolize the credit all for yourself, I will not let you. It is all the divine protection of Hinode-no-Kami. You bragged that your eyes shone, but look at that. Is there not a large fireball appearing above the turret of the castle?"

When all turned their eyes to the turret, just as Takahiko had said, a gigantic fireball, gleaming brilliantly and blazing brightly, was swaying in the air, showing a five-colored radiance. Involuntarily crying out, everyone prostrated themselves on the ground, clapped their hands, and recited the Amatsu Norito. Thereupon, Otoyamatsumi named Takahiko as Harayamatsumi, the guardian deity of this land, and dispatched an express messenger to the presence of Konohanahime on Mount Tenkyō to submit a petition for approval.
