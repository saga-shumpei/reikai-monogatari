Chapter 26: In Praise and Exaltation [376]

第26章　讃嘆〔376〕

§ 1

Inside the gate, the cries of camels and the chatter of the crowd rose moment by moment. Kagatora abruptly raised himself up, appeared at the entrance, and

"Hey, hey! Is that noise friend or foe? What is the truth of it?"

he shouted repeatedly.

Takahiko burst out laughing, and said,

"Hey, hey, do not panic! Why do you not manifest the mind of the eternal now?"

"Even if you speak of the mind of the eternal now, when matters have reached this state, it is utterly beyond help! Mind of the eternal now or not, this Master Kaga finds it painful!"

"Hahaha! What a weak fellow you are, blowing only big boasts. Can you not summon courage like yesterday's?"

"Why should I not summon it? Just watch! From now on, toward the advancing enemy, Kagatora will swing this iron fist freely, striking and striking until they are beaten down, and raising a victory shout in an instant. The craft is my own—say whatever you like after seeing the finish!"

No sooner had he said this than he bolted outside the gate at full speed. Takahiko, watching him from behind, said,

"Hey! Wait, wait! Ah, he has gone. What a hasty fellow! Is he a summer insect flying into the fire? Excuse me, Lord Otsunoyamatsumi, how shall we manage this?"

"Well, there is no need to hurry. It would be best to drink tea leisurely and calm our minds."

"As I surmise, could it not be that the troops under Takatoriwake have marched upon us to destroy our entire party, Lord Otsunoyama?"

"Well, Otsunoyamatsumi knows nothing about it."

At this moment, Kagatora, rushing in through the entrance at full speed, said,

"Hey, Otsunoyamatsumi! All of you, brace yourselves! The enemy has advanced close at hand! Beginning with Komayamahiko, rather than foolishly trying to escape and becoming prisoners of the enemy, exposing yourselves to the disgrace of death, cleanly commit seppuku, seppuku on this very spot! Come now, cut your bellies, cut them!"

"Hahaha! Hey, do not act out a farce! Did you hear those voices? Are they not saying 'Uro, uro'? The people around here regard our party as deities and have come to celebrate! Be it the cries of camels or the cries of joy, how could those spiritual words contain any hostility? You are truly a panicky fellow! That is precisely why it is said that unless you settle your soul in the lower dantian, the Heavenly Rock Cave, you will panic and be driven into a frantic dance in times of emergency! What is this wretched sight? Dancing frantically like this—know some shame! Lady Satsukihime is slightly opening the sliding screen and laughing at you!"

"Hey, are you putting on a farce again? Satsukihime is watching."

From the entrance, two or three voices were heard saying:

"Pardon us, pardon us. Please let us see Lord Kurayamatsumi."

The doorkeeper,

"Yes,"

he answered, and immediately ran into the inner room and conveyed this message; whereupon Kurayamatsumi appeared before all the propagators together with Satsukihime, saying,

"I was discourteous last night. Please rest comfortably today. Furthermore, the people of the land of Haru have brought various gifts, saying they wish to reward the labors of the propagators. Three representatives are coming here now, so please be so kind as to speak with them."

"We are deeply grateful for your repeated kindness. We shall meet them at any time."

Just as they were exchanging these greetings, three men appeared in this place as representatives, bowing politely toward everyone and saying:

"We speak to all the esteemed propagators. Into this region, many troops of Takatoribake entered, committing robberies, mistreating women and children, and engaging in every sort of violence and outrage; until yesterday, the people of this land trembled with fear, unable to attend to their work, saying that anything might break out, and in their anxiety began a festival for the Kunimitama deity; whereupon, unexpectedly, as soon as the propagator's propagation song was heard, both Takatoribake's troops and the whispers of evil deities instantly concealed their forms and lowered their voices into silence. They have likely crossed this great desert and fled back to the western land. In order to repay even one-ten-thousandth of that great favor by which you saved us, the people, from the misery of extreme distress, we have come as representatives of the people wishing to present several tens of camels here. We would consider it a great blessing if you would accept them."
