Chapter 30: Chinzan Pass [380]

第30章　珍山峠〔380〕

§ 1

Takahiko became the Guardian of the western part of the land of Haru, enshrined the divine spirit of Kunitama Tatsuyohime-no-kami, and succeeded Takatoriwake. The party stayed here for several days, taught the propagation songs to the people of the land, and, while regretting their parting, sang the propagation song once again, advancing toward the land of Uzu. Continuing night and day, the four propagators finally reached the foot of Uzu Pass at the border between Haru and Uzu. Sitting on the grass, the four cooled their sweat in the refreshing breeze that blew at that moment, and indulged in various small talk.

In the treetops all around, large brown cicadas were chirping "meen-meen" in sleepy voices. As Komayamahiko scooped the pure water of a slender valley stream into his hands and drank, he said,

"Ah, there is nothing as sweet as water. A nondrinker knows nothing of the sweetness of water that sobers one up."

Gagatora said, "Hey Koma, how can there be any sobering up when you have not even drunk sake? The day is so long that you must be exhausted and dreaming. Though they speak of this fleeting world as a dream, you really are a troublesome Komayamahiko. Ahahaha!"

"Hey, Cicada Boss, do not act so proud just because you think you have many underlings."

"Who do you mean by Cicada Boss? Surely you do not mean me."

"I know not whom I mean, but a cicada is a creature that stops crying when a person approaches, flees swiftly to the neighboring tree, and unfailingly sprays urine at that moment. You must be the kind of fellow who buys whatever he wants, uses it plenty, says he is sick of it, and urinates without paying the price. The one who prattles noisily is a cicada. However, shall I excuse such things now; if you get disheartened and sulk on this mountain slope, it will be a nuisance to the party."

"Setting out from the capital of Haru on a particularly hot summer day, trampling over roots of rock and wood, spurring the steed of my heart, I have come this far, but having such a fellow as a companion has made me weary. Even the Great Kami is ruthless. That troublesome fellow Komayamahiko, tumbling down from the crest of Uzu Pass like a horse..."

"Hold on, Gagatora, do not speak of inauspicious things. Do you not know that Odoyamatsumi-no-kami is present?"

"What do I care about Mount Odo or anything else? What troubles me is Uzu Pass. Let me drink some water to summon my strength and cross it."

So saying, he scooped up the valley water, took a sip, and said,

"Hey, this has a strange taste! And is it not as hot as bathwater? In any case, a hot spring must be welling up upstream. It is not a particularly rushed journey, so let us follow this valley stream and explore up to the place where the hot water springs forth."

高彦は巴留の国の西部の守護職となり、国魂竜世姫神の神霊を奉斎し、鷹取別の後を継ぐことになった。一行は数日間ここに滞在して国の人々に宣伝歌を教え、名残を惜しみつつ再び宣伝歌を歌って、珍の国を指して進んで行く。夜を日に継いで四人の宣伝使は、ようやく巴留と珍との国境、珍の峠の山麓に着いた。四人は芝生の上に腰掛け、折から吹いてくる涼風に汗を払いながら、四方山の話に耽った。

あたりの木々の梢では油蝉が、ミーンミーンと眠たそうな声で鳴いている。駒山彦は細い谷川の清らかな水を手ですくって飲みながら、

「ああ、水ほど甘いものはない。酔い醒めの水の甘さは下戸には分からないぞ」

蚊々虎「おい駒、酒も飲まずに酔い醒めもへちまもあるものか。あまりに日が長いので疲れ果てて夢でも見ているのだな。夢の浮世とは言うものの、まったくもって困った駒山彦だ。アハハハハ」

「蝉の親方とは誰のことだ。まさか俺のことではあるまいな」

「ことさら暑い夏の日に、巴留の都を出発して、岩の根や木の根を踏みしだき、心の駒に鞭打ってここまで来たのは来たものの、こんなやつと道連れになるのは俺も飽きがきた。大神さまも無慈悲だ。困った駒山彦のやつ、珍山峠の頂上から、駒のように転げ落ちて……」

「これこれ蚊々虎、縁起の悪いことを言うな。淤縢山津見さまがおいでになるのを知らないのか」

「おど山も何もあったものか。俺の困るのは珍山峠だ。ひとつ水でも飲んで元気を出して越えてやろう」

と言いながら、谷水をすくって一口飲み、

「よう、こいつは妙な味がするぞ。それに湯のように熱いではないか。どうあってもこの川上に温泉が湧いているに違いない。あまり急ぐ旅でもないし、ひとつこの谷川を伝って湯の湧いている所まで探検しようではないか」

と云ひながら、谷水を掬うて一口飲み、

『ヨー、此奴は妙な味がするぞ。さうして湯のやうに熱いじやないか。ナンデも此の水上に温泉が湧いて居るに違ひ無いわ。余り急く旅でも無し、一つ此の谷川を伝うて湯の湧いて居る所まで探検しようぢやないか』

§ 2

Odoyamazumi, looking puzzled:

"Is that so? Is it warm? How strange."

"It is very warm and good-tasting water. It is ideal for soothing the fatigue of travel. Shall we go and see?"

"Very well."

And so, leaping across the mountain stream to the right and crossing it to the left, climbing several tens of cho, the group finally came out into a place where the valley was wide. From far away, a propagation song echoing through the valley became audible.

Kakatora: "Oh, a propagation song. Who could have come to such a place?"

Komayamahiko: "Do not talk foolishly; who would come to such a place and casually sing a propagation song when no one else is around? It must surely be a Tengu."

"What! A Tengu? That is interesting. Shall Kakatora test his strength against the Tengu?"

"Hey now, you are always so aggressive about everything. Leaping out first into whatever dangerous place, you will end up making a blunder."

"Have I ever made a blunder even once? Withholding the spear before a formidable enemy, rolling up the banners, and making a planned retreat is not the true aspiration of a real man."

"Boasting again. Now, now, negligence is a great enemy. Quietly consider the situation as you go, and decide what to do based on that."

"It is troublesome that you are always like that. It is timid regressive thinking. 'The nail that sticks out gets hammered down.' 'Leave the bee alone and it will not sting.' You are a faint-hearted missionary of peace-at-any-price. I cannot stand around doing something as tedious as scouting. From here on, I will go a step ahead to scout and fight at the same time. If I win, I will call for you, so come out. If I lose, I will keep silent. If a coward like you follows along, you will only be a hindrance, and I will not even be able to fight properly."

Saying this, he sped up at full speed, leaped lightly over the boulders of the mountain stream like a monkey, and hid himself from view.

The three missionaries leisurely climbed up in pursuit of him. Suddenly, straight ahead of them,

"Ahoy! Ahoy!"

Kakatora's shrill voice calling out came echoing back.

Komayamahiko: "Ah, that is Kakatora's voice. He is probably swaggering all by himself again. What an amusing fellow he can be."

Odoyamazumi: "He is a comical fellow and relatively bold, which is why I keep him with me, but he is a man perfectly suited for dispelling the gloom of travel. Hahahaha!"

Satsukihime: "He is truly an amusing person. Traveling around on propagation with him, one is constantly laughing and feels as if it were spring. Hohohoho!"

Komayamahiko: "You are quite devoted to him. Lady O-Urayamabuki, Koma cannot bear it either."

Satsukihime,

"Hohoho,"

concealed her face in her sleeve while laughing. Once again,

"Ahoy! Ahoy!"

such a voice came echoing. The group instinctively quickened their pace and hurried toward the voice.
