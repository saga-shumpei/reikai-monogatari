Chapter 25: The Chariot of Fire [375]

第25章　火の車〔375〕

§ 1

The exposition of the Ananaikyō teachings by Odoyamazumi and his party at the mansion of Kurayamazumi went increasingly into the subtlest and finest details, and finally reached the crowing of the cock.

Kurayamazumi turned to everyone and said,

"I became so engrossed in the noble discourse that it is already the fifth watch of the night. You must all be fatigued; please rest for a while,"

and preparing sleeping quarters in a separate room, he withdrew into an inner chamber.

"I am extremely exhausted. What do you say, shall we take a rest? In truth, after crossing Burajiru Pass, Babatora's legs have become like sticks."

"That is why I say to leave big talk for later. You were full of great vigor, saying that thousands of miles would not affect you, or that even if tens of thousands of enemies pressed in you would knock them flat, yet you whine quite weakly now. Your fleeting whims are truly convenient."

"Hey, Takahiko, unless one rests a little, it does no good for the body. When it is time to sleep, one sleeps. When it is time to play, one plays. When it is time to act, is it not fine to act with the furious momentum of a lion?"

"The clouds have changed considerably. It looks like it might rain tomorrow. If it rains, shall we rest at our leisure again? Yesterday was a great campaign following the missionary. Crossing the great desert was also a heroic endeavor. By the way, how was the divine possession last night? It was quite a useless affair, Lord Babatora."

"What nonsense, how could a novice saniwa possibly understand the gods? From now on, I will be both the spirit medium and the saniwa. Excuse me, Lord Missionary, from today I am in the role of saniwa. Sit over there for once. If you dislike sleeping, shall I act as saniwa and reveal your guardian deity? Just because an evil spirit possessed this priest at Burajiru Pass, do you think evil spirits will keep possessing me forever? Odoyamazumi's saniwa discrimination fails to cast off preconceived notions, so he performs utterly mediocre spirit-judging. As one would expect, because he keeps thinking of past matters, he cannot understand the truth."

"Then shall I judge you through saniwa anew?"

"How can a mere mortal presume to judge a god?"

"Naturally, for it would be disgraceful if your disguise were exposed."

"If my dignity is crushed in front of Satsukihime by being called an evil deity or unreliable, I have no desire to be judged by a saniwa."

At this moment, a propagation song was heard outside the gate. The three strained their ears and listened. The propagation song drew gradually closer. Led by two women, a missionary appeared in this place. He rolled up his veil and greeted the party.

§ 2

"I am a missionary of Ananaikyō. Hearing that a missionary of the same Ananaikyō appeared in the land of Haru, I have come in all haste. I am one who conducts missionary work in the land of Teru."

Kagatora stared closely at the missionary's face, saying,

"Why, aren't you Ko-Ko-Komayamahiko? When you attacked Kōhakuzan together with us, you were supposed to have died easily from being hit by a bomb, so how did you come here? Haha, last night we performed divine possession, so you must have come from the Netherworld hoping to be saved. No wonder your face is pale and dark. Hey, ghost of Komayamahiko, I shall now conduct spirit discernment upon you."

"Oh, are you Kagatora? How fortunate that you have been safe. Unable to forget you, I came from the Netherworld to receive you. Come, come, let us go together. Lord Enma is waiting. Because you did nothing but terrible things, messengers came from Enma's tribunal to fetch you. At the entrance, many red demons and blue demons have arrived and are waiting. Since I know your face, I came to serve as the inquest inspector. Come, come, hurry, hurry."

"Komayamahiko, please wait. Excuse me, Lord Missionary, Lord Ototsuyamatsumi, this fellow must be an evil spirit. Please perform spirit discernment. A troublesome fellow has arrived."

"There is no need for spirit discernment. With this spiritual eye, a single glance makes it clearly understood. Indeed, you are a wicked fellow. Hereafter, receiving a rebuke from Lord Enma and undergoing spiritual discipline, you had better conduct missionary work in the Netherworld. The physical world and the spiritual world are the same thing. The only difference is merely that life departs; you had better leave at once. Ah, one truly cannot commit evil deeds."

"Excuse me, Sir Messenger of Hell, Komayamahiko, as you also know, the ringleader far worse than I is right here. This fellow arrogantly calls himself Ototsuyamatsumi now, but originally was called Shikotsuwake and, inconceivable as it is, was the very ringleader who destroyed the shrine of the Three Great Ōkami. I was merely ordered about at his beck and call. Lord Enma must listen for a moment. Please judge well the gravity and magnitude of the sins. Leaving such an evil scoundrel in this world while taking an honest person like Master Kagatora to the Netherworld is far too cruel."

Takahiko said, 'Ah, Lord Kagatora, you must think of the present moment. Do not worry about how things will turn out ahead. It is time to pay your dues. You should go briskly like a man. And by the way, Lord Odoyamazumi as well... The only missionary left behind will be—ahem—Lord Takahiko himself. From now on, Satsukihime and I will walk about together on our mission.'

'Do not mock me! To me, that is the delusion of an asura. Excuse me, messenger of Komayamahiko, this fellow named Takahiko is someone who has done nothing but evil in this land of Haru under the name of Arakuma until now. You must know him too. In the past, he did a great deal of evil together with us. You might as well take all three of us with you.'

'No, it cannot be so. This time I will escort only one person back. Only a single carriage has arrived.'

'Well, how stylish! Has a carriage come to fetch someone going to hell? What a fine carriage might it be?'

'It is a splendid, magnificent carriage of fire.'

'What, a carriage of fire? I want none of that! In that case, shall we draw lots?'

'Ahahaha, how foolish! It is a lie. Kagatora, he is neither a ghost nor anything of the sort; you have boasted arrogantly until now that you would become a saniwa or achieve a splendid divine possession, yet do you not recognize the spiritual robe of Komayamahiko? If he were someone from the spirit world, it would be triangular. Do you not recognize that spiritual robe of his, radiating harmonious five-colored light?'

'Indeed, indeed; I was so flustered that I did not notice the spiritual robe.'

'Can you really see spiritual robes? Your own spiritual robe is beginning to turn triangular. A fellow whose robe turns triangular is proof that his departure for the netherworld is near. Ahahaha!'

Kagatora put his hands to his head and groped all over his body, searching to see if his spiritual robe would touch his hands. Komayamahiko turned to everyone and said,

'By a wondrous connection, while aboard a ship crossing from the land of Tsukushi to the land of Chiri, I encountered Hinode-no-Kami and received wonderful teachings; thereafter, I turned away from my evil heart, crossed over to this Takasago Island with an old friend, divided the land of Chiri into north and south with Sarusehiko, and engaged in mission work. However, according to rumors carried by the wind, the missionaries of Anankyō have crossed the Brazil Pass, and in the capital of Haru there is an evil deity named Takatoriwake who is devising various plans to annihilate the missionaries of Anankyō; therefore, wishing to lend our assistance as well, I have come. I would be most grateful if you would kindly permit me to accompany you.'

'Splendid, splendid! Kagatora grants permission for you to accompany us.'

'I did not ask Lord Kagatora. I made my request to Lord Odoyamazumi.'

'If I grant permission, it is the same thing. Isn't that so, Lord Odoyamazumi?'

At that moment, the voices of an unknown number of hundreds of people were heard from outside the gate.
