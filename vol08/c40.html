Chapter 40: Exposition of Kotodama II [390]

第40章　言霊解二〔390〕

§ 1

"The name of the deity who came into being upon the head of the slain Kagutsuchi-no-Kami is Masakayamatsumi-no-Kami; next, the name of the deity who came into being upon his chest is Odoyamatsumi-no-Kami; next, the name of the deity who came into being upon his belly is Okuyamatsumi-no-Kami; next, the name of the deity who came into being upon his genitals is Kurayamatsumi-no-Kami; next, the name of the deity who came into being upon his left hand is Shigiyamatsumi-no-Kami; next, the name of the deity who came into being upon his right hand is Hayamatsumi-no-Kami; next, the name of the deity who came into being upon his left foot is Harayamatsumi-no-Kami; and the name of the deity who came into being upon his right foot is Toyamatsumi-no-Kami."

The deity who came into being upon the head of the slain fire deity is not a good deity. That is to say, Masakayamatsumi-no-Kami signifies an evil deity residing in a strong and exalted position. As for the sound "ya", in terms of kotodama, the A-column represents the voice of heaven, the Ya-column represents the voice of humanity, and the Wa-column represents the voice of earth; that is, "ya" is the voice of humanity, the foolish opinions of the masses among the general races of the world, and "matsumi" means the accumulation of demons; therefore, "yamatsumi" means that demons inhabit the realm of speech, which constitutes the arising of Masakayamatsumi-no-Kami. Coming into being upon the head means saying that the upper level is unnecessary, and, like today's democracy, it means the arising of ideas such as the abolition of classes based on the doctrine that humanity equally receives the blessings of heaven.

Next, regarding the "name of the deity who came into being upon his chest, Odoyamatsumi-no-Kami," the chest, if compared to the human body, is the region of the heart, the lungs, and the breasts, and, speaking of politicians, ministers, officials appointed directly by the Emperor, and officials appointed by imperial decree correspond to the chest. That is, the thoughts of these people are written here. Because various ideological wars arise from below, causing them heartache and making them tremble with fear, it means that they exert pressure and threats through the power of the military and the police.

Next, what is called the "name of the deity who came into being upon his belly, Okuyamatsumi-no-Kami" signifies the backbone of the nation, that is, the middle-class society, and if compared to the human body, corresponds to the navel.

"O" means heart or mind, and "ku" means uniting or suffering; thus, the middle class stands in the center and is suffering, wondering what ought to be done. That is, in terms of kotodama, it carries the meaning of wishing to manage skillfully by adopting the middle course, proceeding neither with conservatism nor with new ideologies.

Next, regarding the "name of the deity who came into being upon his genitals, Kurayamatsumi-no-Kami," the hoto represents the people engaged in agriculture, and if compared to the human body, corresponds to the private parts, the place that gives birth to children. That is, it means farming households. It means that these peasants do not know what kind of thoughts currently exist or what their meaning is, and can turn in any direction depending on their leaders. An unprincipled ideology that sides with whichever direction entirely according to the trends of the times constitutes Kurayamatsumi-no-Kami.

「殺されなさった迦具土神の御頭にお成りになった神の御名は正鹿山津見神、次に御胸にお成りになった神の御名は淤縢山津見神、次に御腹にお成りになった神の御名は奥山津見神、次に御陰にお成りになった神の御名は闇山津見神、次に左の御手にお成りになった神の御名は志芸山津見神、次に右の御手にお成りになった神の御名は羽山津見神、次に左の御足にお成りになった神の御名は原山津見神、右の御足にお成りになった神の御名は戸山津見神である」

殺された火の神の頭にお成りになった神は良い神ではない。すなわち正鹿山津見神は強く尊い位置にある悪い神という意味であります。「ヤ」ということは、言霊上、ア行は天の声、ヤ行は人の声、ワ行は地の声、すなわち「ヤ」は人の声、世界一般の人種の衆口愚論であり、「マツミ」は魔積みでありますから、「ヤマツミ」ということは言論界に悪魔が住むという意味で、これが正鹿山津見神の起こることになります。頭にお成りになるとは、すなわち上の方は要らないと言って、今日のデモクラシーのように、人類は平等に天の恵みを受けるという説で、階級撤廃などという思想が起こるということであります。

次に「御胸にお成りになった神の御名、淤縢山津見神」の胸というのは、人間の身体に例えれば、心臓や肺臓や乳のあたりで、政治家で言えば、大臣とか、親任官とか、勅任官などが胸であります。すなわちこれらの人々の思想が書いてあるのであります。下から種々様々な思想上の戦争が起こって、それに胸を痛めて、おどおどしているから軍隊や警察の力で圧迫威嚇するという意味になります。

次に「御腹にお成りになった神の名、奥山津見神」というのは、国民の中堅すなわち中流社会ということで、人体に例えれば臍に当たるのであります。

「オ」は心、「ク」は組むとか、苦しむとかいうことで、中流階級は中央に立って、どうしたらよかろうかと言って、苦しんでいるのであります。すなわち保守主義でも行かず、新しい主義でも行かず、その中庸を取って、うまくやりたいという言霊上の意味になるのであります。

次に「御陰にお成りになった神の名、闇山津見神」というのは、「ほと」は農業に従事する民で、人体に例えれば陰部に当たり、子を産み出す所であります。すなわち農家ということになります。この百姓は現在どのような思想があって、その意味が何であるか分からず、指導者によってどのようにもなることを意味しているのであります。全く時の勢いによってどちらにもつく無定見な思想が闇山津見神ということになります。

§ 2

Next, concerning "the name of the deity who came into being from the left hand, Shigiyamatsumi-no-Kami," this left hand means the upper hand, that is to say, politicians, and the right hand corresponds to business. All politicians serve as the deity's left hand and businesspersons as the right hand; thus they do work with the right hand and govern with the left hand. In Shigiyamatsumi-no-Kami, "shi" means water, and "gi" has the meaning of the overlap of deity and country. This means that politicians, without realizing spiritual civilization, place greater importance on material matters than on spiritual education. Today, anti-Japanese thought has arisen everywhere; and while this problem of thought must be suppressed through thought, when pressure is applied in trade and the other side is well prepared in armaments, this side—if it be Japan—builds an Eight-Eight Fleet or expands the army, and this time when thoughts of defending the country in such a way are rampant is called Shigiyamatsumi-no-Kami.

Next, "the name of the deity who came into being from the right hand, Hayamatsumi-no-Kami," refers to the lower peasants, laborers, and businesspersons. That is to say, they think only of how they can make money, such as by hoarding rice to profit because rice prices will rise or commodity prices will surge, even though public sentiment might worsen if a war breaks out, or by stocking a large quantity of goods to make a fortune. This is indeed the disordered selfishness of the lower people's sincere heart.

"Ha" means to open, but when it continues as "Hayamatsumi," it carries the meaning of brooding over a desire to make a profit if some upheaval occurs; that is to say, a soul that is tainted by truly improper selfishness and awaits some sort of disturbance—thinking to stock up on lumber now because lumber will rise in price if there is a great fire, or to buy up a large amount of rice now to make a profit if a famine comes, the hundred grains fail to ripen, and there is a poor harvest—is called Hayamatsumi-no-Kami.

Next, "the name of the deity who came into being from the left foot, Harayamatsumi-no-Kami; the name of the deity who came into being from the right foot, Toyamatsumi-no-Kami" refers to people who hold the idea of expanding overseas: if one goes overseas, one will study foreign thought; foreign countries are truly civilized nations, while our country is uncivilized. The theories of all those scholars who yearn for Western civilization, desiring to befriend foreign nations and conveniently resolve international disputes amidst amicable conversations, constitute Harayamatsumi-no-Kami of the left foot.

§ 3

"Toya" means to open to the outside, and this Toyamadsumi no Kami signifies things such as emigration and working abroad; it refers to sending emigrants to foreign countries, foreign countries on their part excluding immigrants, and various great problems erupting, exactly resembling today's world. Although it is not known what this emigration indicated in the age of the gods, a kotodama-based prophecy is indicated in this manner. That is to say, while our compatriots under distant skies endure regret and bitter vexation due to anti-Japanese agitation, undergoing unspeakable hardships, people complain that the citizenry is indifferent or ask what the government is doing, bringing forth opposition and discontent; that very state is called Toyamadsumi no Kami.

"Thereupon, wishing to see his younger sister Izanami no Mikoto, he pursued her to the Land of Yomi. Then, when she came out through the palace entrance to meet him, Izanagi no Mikoto spoke, saying, 'My lovely wife, the country that I and you were creating is not yet completed, so please return.' Then Izanami no Mikoto replied, saying, 'How lamentable! Because you did not come quickly, I have eaten of the hearth of Yomi. Nevertheless, because my lovely husband has entered and arrived, which is awe-inspiring, I wish to return. For a while, I will confer with the deities of Yomi. Do not look upon me.' Having spoken thus, she went back into the palace, and the interval was exceedingly long, so he could wait no longer. Therefore, breaking off one of the large teeth of the sacred comb stuck in his left hair, he lit a single fire and entered to look; maggots were swarming and boiling, and on her head sat Ōikazuchi, on her breast sat Honoikazuchi, on her belly sat Kuroikazuchi, on her private parts sat Sakuikazuchi, on her left hand sat Wakaikazuchi, on her right hand sat Tsuchiikazuchi, on her left foot sat Naruikazuchi, and on her right foot sat Fusuikazuchi; altogether eight thunder deities had come into being."

These sacred words signify that this came to pass because the protection of the Ōkunitama, which is the soul on the earth, was poor, and that due to the dreadful poison of the civilization of fire, that is, material civilization, the world came almost to the brink of ruin in this manner.

Izanagi no Mikoto is spirit, and Izanami no Mikoto is body. This world cannot exist by spirit alone; that is, spirit and flesh must be unified. Our Japan is a country that must enliven the national souls of the world and safely nurture all peoples of the world through the teaching of the primacy of spirit over body. Stemming from the great august heart of supreme benevolence and supreme love that morally cherishes all of the entire earth through the blessings of spiritual civilization, the Great Deity of the Sun requested an interview with Izanami no Mikoto and descended from afar in order to complete the earth.

現代日本語訳

「トヤ」というのは外に開くということであり、この戸山津見神は、移民とか、出稼ぎとかいう事であり、外国に移民を送るとか、外国は外国で移民排斥とか、さまざまな大問題が勃発する事であって、ちょうど今日の世の中に実によく似ているのであります。この移民ということは、神代ではどういう事を示されたものかは分かりませんが、このように言霊的予言が示されているのであります。すなわち我らの同胞が遠国の空の下で、排日のために悔しさと無念に耐え、言うに言われぬ苦労をしているのに国民が冷淡であるとか、政府は何をしているのかと言って、反対や不平を持ち出す、その状態を戸山津見神というのであります。

『ここにその妹伊弉冊命に会いたいと欲して、黄泉国に追い赴かれた。そこで御殿の戸から出迎えられた時に、伊弉諾命が語り仰せられるには、「愛しい我が妻よ、私とお前とで作った国はまだ作り終えていないので、戻っておいで」と仰せられた。そこで伊弉冊命がお答え申し上げるには、「無念なことです。早くおいでにならなかったので、私は黄泉国の竈の物を食べてしまいました。けれども愛しい我が夫がおいでになったことは畏れ多いので戻りたいと思います。しばらく黄泉神と談判しましょう。私を見ないでください」と、このように申してその殿内に戻り入られた間がはなはだ長くて、待ちかねられた。それゆえ、左の御美髪に刺していた湯津津間櫛の男柱を一本折り取って、一つの火を点して入って御覧になった時に、蛆が集まり沸き返り、御頭には大雷がおり、御胸には火雷がおり、御腹には黒雷がおり、御陰部には拆雷がおり、左の御手には若雷がおり、右の御手には土雷がおり、左の御足には鳴雷がおり、右の御足には伏雷がおり、合わせて八つの雷神が成り居られた』

この御言葉は、地球上の霊魂である大国魂の守護が悪いために、このようになったのであり、火の文明すなわち物質文明の惨毒のために、このように世界がほとんど滅亡に瀕したのであります。

伊弉諾命は霊であり、伊弉冊命は体であります。この世の中は霊だけでもいけず、すなわち霊肉一致でなければならないのであります。我が日本は霊主体従の教えをもって、世界の国魂を生かし、世界万民を安育していかねばならない国であります。世界を道義的に精神文明の徳沢をもって、全地球のすべてを愛撫するという至仁至愛の大御心から、日の大神が地球を完成させなさるために、伊弉冊命に会見を申し込み、遥々と降りて来られたということであります。

トヤといふのは外に開くといふことで、この戸山津見神は、移民とか、出稼とかいふ事で、外国に移民を送るとか、外国は外国で移民排斥とか、種々の大問題が勃発する事で、丁度今日の世の中によく似て居るのであります。この移民といふことは、神代では何ういふ事を示されたものか判りませぬが、斯ういふ風に言霊的予言が示されて居るのであります。即ち吾が同胞が遠国の空で、排日のために悔し残念を耐へて、言ふに言はれぬ苦労をして居るのに国民が冷淡であるとか、政府は何をして居るかというて、反対やら、不平やらを持出す、其の状態を戸山津見神といふのであります。

『是に其妹伊弉冊命を相見まく欲して、黄泉国に追往でましき。爾ち殿騰戸より出向へます時に、伊弉諾命語詔ひたまはく、愛くしき我那邇妹命、吾汝と作れりし国未だ作り竟へずあれば、還りまさねとのりたまひき、爾に伊弉冊命の答曰したまはく、悔しきかも、速く来まさずて吾は黄泉戸喫しつ。然れども愛くしき、我那勢命入来ませる事恐ければ還りなむを。且く黄泉神と相論はむ。我をな視たまひそ。如此白して其殿内に還り入りませる間甚久しくて待ちかねたまひき。故、左の御美髪に刺させる湯津津間櫛の男柱一箇取闕きて、一火燭して入見ます時に、蛆集り蘯きて、御頭には大雷居り、御胸には火雷居り、御腹には黒雷居り、御陰には拆雷居り、左の御手には若雷居り、右の御手には土雷居り、左の御足には鳴雷居り、右の御足には伏雷居り併せて八の雷神成り居りき』

この御言葉は地球上の霊魂なる大国魂の守護が悪いから、斯うなつたのであり、火の文明即ち物質文明の惨毒の為に斯の如く世界が殆ど滅亡に瀕したのであります。

伊弉諾命は霊で、伊弉冊命は体であります。この世の中は霊ばかりでもいけない、即ち霊肉一致でなければならぬのであります。我日本は霊主体従の教を以て、世界の国魂を生かし、世界万民を安育させて行かねばならぬ国であります。世界を道義的に精神文明の徳沢を以て、全地球一切を愛撫すると曰ふ至仁至愛の大御心から、日の大神が地球を完成し玉ふ為に、伊弉冊命に会見を申込み、遥々と御降りになつた事であります。

§ 4

Regarding the passage, "Desiring to see His younger sister Izanami no Mikoto, He went in pursuit to the land of Yomi," this land of Yomi does not refer to the state after death, but the condition of the entire world today is the land of Yomi. Thereupon, the true God descended from heaven and slightly opened the raising-door of the rock-cave to see. When He did so, in every country of the world, the doors were closed. These doors refer to cliques; such things as family cliques, political party cliques, capital cliques, academic cliques, and religious cliques are the doors.

Opening that door, Izanagi no Mikoto spoke; His words, "My beloved," are, in short, words spoken in love toward all things—the National Spirit of the earth, the general populace of the world, and all of creation. That is, together with the spiritual lineage and the physical lineage, He sought to create a beautiful world, but on account of the god of fire—namely, thermal-power civilization—the world turned into the land of Yomi. Therefore He told them to return once again to the origin. This instruction to return to the Great Origin means that according to the divine teachings, the gods reform, the National Spirit reforms, and the people reform, and by uniting high and low, they shall build a complete land. That is, it amounts to asking the evil guardian deities upon the earth to reform.

Thereupon, Izanami no Mikoto answered and spoke: "Regrettable it is that You did not come quickly; I have eaten of the furnace of Yomi." This means: "I have done a regrettable thing. I have eaten of the furnace of Yomi. If You had warned me a little earlier, all things upon the earth would not have corrupted to this extent, but at the present day, it is indeed a completely clouded, completely turbid, completely rotten world, and there is no way to deal with it. It is a state where one can neither advance nor retreat, neither raise nor lower, and cannot move one way or another."

That is, since God, myself, and humans are all equally defiled, for the true God to come forth from heaven and say, 'I will mend the earth that is about to perish, and I will establish a complete heaven,' is truly an awe-inspiring, noble, and gracious divine word; therefore, if the National Spirit—that is, the divine humans of the world in general—reforms, this is what is called 'I shall return.' However, She replied, 'I will consult briefly with the gods of Yomi, so I beg You to wait until then.' These gods of Yomi refer to the sovereigns and presidents of each country governed by body-master-spirit-servant principles, who rule over the modern dark world; and 'to consult together' means, 'Let us consult about this matter for the time being, expound the truth to the multitude, and listen to their opinions.'

Next, the passage, "He waited exceedingly long and grew impatient," means that this discussion could not be settled in a short while; with various arguments—such as materialism being good, the gold and silver standard being good, or self-sufficiency in natural produce—it could not be resolved in two or three years. God has been persistently saying since the twenty-fifth year of Meiji, 'It is now, be swift,' urging them to reform quickly and being in haste, yet it carries the meaning that the discussions among the gods of Yomi do not easily come to an agreement.

原文（近代文語）

『其の妹伊弉冊命を相見まく欲して黄泉国に追往でましき』といふ、この黄泉国は死後のことをいうたのでなくして、今日の全世界の状態が黄泉国であります。そこで天から、本当の神様が下つて来て岩戸の騰戸をば少し開いて見られたのであります。さうすると世界各国、戸が閉つてゐる。この戸といふことは閥の事でありまして、門閥だとか、政党閥だとか、資本閥だとか、学閥だとか、宗教閥などいふものが戸であります。

その戸を開けて、伊弉諾命が曰れますには、『我が愛くしき』と云ふ事は、要するに地球の国魂も世界一般の人民も、森羅万象一切のものを皆愛し玉ひての御言葉であります。すなはち霊系と体系と相俟つて、美はしい世界を作らむとしたが、火の神いはゆる火力文明のために、世界は黄泉国と化つたのである。それで今一度元に還れと曰はれたのであります。この太元に還れといふことは、神の教に従つて神が改心し、国魂が改心し、人民が改心して、上下一致し以て完全なる国を作らむとの意味であります。即ち地球上の悪の守護神に、改心してくれといふことになります。

そこで伊弉冊命は答て曰るるには、『悔しきかも速く来まさずして、吾は黄泉戸喫しつ』とあります。これは残念なことを致しました。吾は黄泉戸喫した。モウ少し早く御注意下さらば、茲まで地球上の一切は腐敗せなかつたで在らうに、今日となつては実に曇り切り、濁り切り、腐り切りた世の中で手のつけやうもない。往きも戻りも、上げも下しも、二進も三進も行かぬ状態であるといふ意味であります。

即ち神も、吾も、人も、共に皆汚されて居ることでありますから、天から誠の神が御出下さいまして、地球が破滅せむとするのを直してやらう、完全なる天国を建設してやらう、と曰れますのは、誠に恐れ多い、尊い、忝ない神の御言葉でありますから、私は国魂即ち世界一般の神人が改心すれば、と曰ふ事を『還りなむ』と申すのである。しかし一寸黄泉神と相談して見ますから、それまで御待ちを願ひますと答へられたのであります。この黄泉神といふのは、現代の暗黒世界を支配して居る各体主霊従国の主権者や大統領といふことでありまして、相論うといふことは、一応この事を相談して見ませう、多勢に理を説いて聞かせて、その意見を聴いて見ませうといふ事であります。

次に『甚久しく待ちかねたまひき』といふのは、この議論が一寸や、そつとの間に纏まらずに、やれ物質主義がよいとか、金銀為本がよいとか、天産自給だとか、いろいろの議論があつて、二年や三年で尽き果てぬのであります。神様は今ぢや早ぢやというて早く改心せよと、明治二十五年から言ひ続けに言はれて御急ぎになつて居るが、黄泉神の議論は中々纏まらぬといふ如うな意味であります。
