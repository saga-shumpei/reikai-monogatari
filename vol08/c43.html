Chapter 43: Exposition of Kotodama V [393]

第43章　言霊解五〔393〕

§ 1

"'At last, his younger sister Izanami-no-Mikoto came in pursuit herself.'"

The statement that, whereas until now they had advanced to the attack with an army of one thousand five hundred of Yomi, at the last the entire world united and came attacking against the land of the Sun Deity, signifies that Izanami-no-Mikoto came in pursuit herself. This is the final great mountain pass of the world. That is, it is the one great watershed of the rise and fall of the realm, deciding the victory or defeat between the divine army and the demonic army.

"'Thereupon, blocking the Pass of Yomotsu Hirasaka with the rock requiring a thousand men to pull, she said that she would strangle to death one thousand persons in a single day.'"

The rock requiring a thousand men to pull signifies a rock of tremendous weight that cannot be moved in the slightest without the strength of ten million people. The rock requiring a thousand men to pull carries the meaning of blood, the sun, the nation, adamantine strength, and multitude; it refers to a host of brave warriors endowed with great adamantine power, in whom flows the blood of sincere devotion to their sovereign and nation, and means soldiers of matchless loyalty and valor who serve as the nation's shield. Furthermore, the august virtues of the divine spirits that protect the nation and the national defense forces are likewise all the rock requiring a thousand men to pull, carrying the meaning of military power that repels or prevents the invading demonic army.

The phrase "placed between them and exchanging Kotodo" means that there is utterly no hope of harmony or concord between a nation and people of spirit-dominant-body-subordinate and a nation and people of body-dominant-spirit-subordinate. Because their national souls are inherently and fundamentally different, the divine decree to separate the spirit-dominant-body-subordinate nation from the body-dominant-spirit-subordinate nation—the divine land proceeding according to its way, and foreign lands (the land of Yomi) proceeding according to ways befitting their own national souls—is what is meant by exchanging Kotodo.

Because a government solely devoted to goodness and the military art of the divine army are fundamentally different from the martial laws of body-dominant-spirit-subordinate nations, this means that everything was strictly separated here, distinguishing the way of maintaining the world in a spirit-dominant-body-subordinate nation from the way of governing the world in a body-dominant-spirit-subordinate nation. In short, it is a divine decree that undesirable elements of the Yomi army must not enter into the soil of the divine land. Just as the tenth human sovereign, Emperor Sujin, based on his divine intent to await the opportune moment for the development of the imperial fortunes, softened his light and identified with the dust, causing foreign civilizational artifacts to be imported into our nation, one cannot forever adhere stubbornly to the system of softening the light and identifying with the dust, and thus the state of affairs has arrived at the present age wherein one must resolutely exchange Kotodo. Koto carries the meaning of words and discourses, and to carries the function of closing off. In short, the meaning that Japan determines the sacred teachings of the Imperial Ancestral Great Deity to be the essential path for governing the nation and pacifying the people, and does not import any of the unbefitting discourses of body-dominant-spirit-subordinate nations, is precisely what is meant by bestowing Kotodo, and if one compares this to the relationship between husband and wife, it means handing over a letter of divorce and severing all ties. Continuing indefinitely to adopt a policy of softening the light and identifying with the dust is something that we must also reconsider in our conduct of life today. Severing relations as quickly as possible with evil thoughts and deities of poverty is beneficial both for the family and for one's own person. Our nation today should also awaken as soon as possible, sever relations with thoughts, discourses, philosophies, and religions unbefitting our land, and exchange the so-called Kotodo.

§ 2

"Izanami-no-Mikoto proclaimed: 'My beloved husband-lord, if you do thus, I shall strangle and kill one thousand heads of the people of your land in a single day,' so she spoke."

In the declaration of Yomotsu-Ōkami, it means: "If you thus reject the religious doctrines of Yomotsu-kuni through the divine proclamations of the country of the divine husband-brother whom we love and adore without ceasing, I too have an idea. By the stratagems of the Yomotsu army, in a single day—that is, at a single stroke—I shall dismiss the chief officials standing above the people of your country; that is, I shall show you that I can strip them of their offices."

If there is a guardian deity who seeks to unify the world of thought according to the Great Way of Kannagara—that is, the sacred testament of the Imperial Ancestors—they are immediately treated as an outdated antique and not only not appointed to the head of officials, but have their heads severed at once; therefore, only an egoism that seeks to avoid propagating and practicing Izanagi-no-Mikoto—that is, the Great Way unique to Japan—develops.

This is the present age, in which people have become nothing but spineless cowards who willingly submit to the beck and call of the Yomotsu army and the souls of body-master-spirit-subordinate. When we seek to propagate and practice the divine teachings of Izanagi-no-Mikoto—that is, the holy instructions of the heavenly deities and ancestors—throughout the realm, we are oppressed by the guardian deities of the national souls of body-master-spirit-subordinate belonging to the Yomotsu army; and that the pawns of the Yomotsu deities spread obstructions in every direction in order to separate one thousand sincere believers daily from Ōmoto carries the same meaning.

Even though knowing well that the teachings of the deities of Japan are splendid, and fully understanding that the emergence of Ōmoto is greatly needed to save the modern age, they shrink from public opinion and fear being called old-fashioned thinkers, and ultimately being dismissed from their current positions and socially murdered and buried; thus there are only guardian deities who consider it a safe policy to slander and oppress Ōmoto—which engages in social service of heavenly-piercing sincerity—just as ordinary society does, and once someone openly professes to be a believer of Ōmoto, that sincere person, whether soldier, educator, or company employee, is immediately dismissed and discharged from office, which corresponds to the saying, 'She spoke, saying, "I shall strangle and kill one thousand people in a single day."'

"Thereupon Izanagi-no-Mikoto declared: 'My beloved wife-sister-lord, if you do so, I shall surely erect one thousand five hundred birthing huts in a single day.' Therefore, without fail, one thousand people die in a single day, and without fail, one thousand five hundred people are born in a single day."

§ 3

Hereupon, Izanagi no Mikoto said, "O my beloved Nanimome no Mikoto, if you throw the Land of the Sun into confusion through ideological problems, and furthermore, now unite to attack our land by military force, I too have a great resolution. I shall manifest the Great Way of Kannagara, and thereby erect one thousand five hundred birthing huts in a single day." The sacred ground that fosters people with the spirit of birth, people with the hearts of newborn infants, as mentioned in the Divine Revelations, is what is called the birthing hut.

"Chi" is blood, absolute sincerity; it has the meaning of spirit being superior to matter; it is the virtue of the father, milk, and salt.

"I" is to unite and accumulate; it is to establish oneself firmly and be immovable.

"Ho" is to appear above; it is the luminous distinction of the sun, shining intensely inward, and the heart of Heaven.

"U" is to unite together; it is true adamantine strength, and the work of parents.

"Bu" is to flourish and prosper; it is the binding place of the world, and mutual care between parents and children.

"Ya" is the inherent Great Father; it is returning to Heaven, and the form of the divine administration.

When these six kotodama of Chi, I, Ho, U, Bu, and Ya are settled, the souls of divine lineage and karmic affinity gather together, practice absolute sincerity, manifest the true quality of spirit being superior to matter, maintain the rank of father of heaven and mother of earth, provide the milk of benevolence to all people, purify all defilements like the salt of the great sea, give fine taste to all things to prevent decay, form a united body of capable individuals, stabilize the direction and course of their conduct, thereby remain unmoved in heart by false doctrines and greed, appear transcendent above the mundane world, cause the august mind of the Great Deity to shine intensely throughout the universe, clearly teach and awaken to the luminous distinction of the sun—that is, the heavenly mission of the Divine Land of the Sun—accumulate supreme, true, and adamantine strength, perform the work of parents of the world, unite the upper and lower classes through the single true Way, add binding power day by day, ultimately unify and unite the world, gather all people in longing as parents, look up to the divine authority of the National Ancestor Ōkunitokotachi no Kami, the inherent Great Father, govern the world by returning to endowed spirituality, and thus develop and salvage the three thousand worlds; such is the significance of the sacred place. In the Divine Revelations of Oomoto, it is stated: "Because Ayabe is the place for the reconstruction and renewal of the three thousand worlds, it is the splendid Takamanohara of the earth where, by the command of the Great Deity of the Sun, the peoples of the world shall be governed with the single sincerity of the Great Deity of Heaven"; this too is the meaning of the so-called one thousand five hundred birthing huts, the central organ of the great divine administration of the divine realm that gives birth to and rears the pure and good souls of newborn infants. "Ame no Masuhito" also carries the meaning of persons of supreme sincerity who work for the benefit of the realm and the nation. All true believers of our Oomoto must together become Ame no Masuhito. Furthermore, unless they take actions as Ame no Masuhito throughout all of Japan, develop and advance the nation, and set an example for the nations of the Underworld, they cannot fulfill their heavenly calling as the divine people of the Divine Land. Today, the fact that corrupt citizens corrupted by socialism and radicalism have become dependents of the Yomotsu army, sending disturbing threatening letters to high officials, and that similar threatening letters arrive for the executives of Oomoto, is the meaning of having stated that she would kill one thousand people. イフヤザカの五言霊を約言する時は善悪正邪の分水嶺であります。男神の伊弉諾命と女神の伊弉冊命と、互ひに自分の住し、かつ占有する国土を発展せしめむと、強く思ひ合ひて争ひ賜ふ所は同じく平等にして何の差別もなく、只々施政の方針に大なる正反対の意見あるのみ。然れど女神黄泉神の御経綸は惟神の大道に背反せるが故に、終に海外の某々の如く悉く大動乱大破裂の惨状を露出したのは、近来事実の確証する所であります。

男神の神国は、日進月歩至極鋭敏にして、終に世界の大強国の仲間入りを為したり。されど忽ち昇り忽ち降るの虞れあり。黄泉国の二の舞を演ぜざる様、注意を要する次第であります。ヤは日本にして、何処までも徳を積み輝きを重ねつつ、外面を覆ひ、以て克く隠忍し、天下の大徳を保ちて天下に臨むと雖も黄泉国の八雷神や、千五百の妖軍は何の容赦も荒々しく、焼也、天也、の活動を成し、裏面の天地を生み成しつつあり。故に世界各国は殆ど騒乱の極みに達し正義仁道は地を払ひ、スに事の在りし暴国なり。茲に仁義の神の国の一切の善事瑞祥発生して、仁慈大神の神世に復し治め、暗黒界を光り輝かせ、妖軍に悩まされ滅亡せむとする、国土人民に対しては身命を投げだして救助し治国平天下の神鍵を握る可き、治乱興亡の大境界線を画せる、現代も亦これ出雲の国の伊賦夜坂と謂ふべきものであります。（完）
