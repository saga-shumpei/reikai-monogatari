Chapter 19: 復活 [1544]

復活〔1544〕

§ 1

Kiiu Sōjiyō: I respectfully offer this at the Mitamaya. Before the Mitama of the Tohotsumioya, the ancestors of yoyo, the Ukara, and the Yakara of the house of [Name], I humbly speak: today, because [Name] of this house has returned to the Kakuriyo, I report this fact, and having placed and offered various things before your presence, I humbly pray that you will listen to this state of offering and grant your consent, and with awe and trembling, I speak.

Bochi Jichinsai: O Ōmotosumeōmikami, whose name is to be spoken with awe, before the Ubusunanōokami who dwells in Ushihagi, separating this place, I, the Itsukinushi [Name], respectfully and reverently speak: regarding this place as the Okutsukidokoro of [Name], that we have newly cleared the Arakusa and Kariso, and are burying and settling it into the deep bedrock, I pray that you will listen peacefully and calmly, that there be no harm from birds, beasts, or insects, that you will protect and bless this land for eternity, and I pray that you will listen to the state of this petition, and with awe and trembling, I speak.

Shōkonsai Norito: Alas, O [Name] no Mikoto, that you should now withdraw your body in this manner, we did not even think of it when hanging the yū-tare, and we only relied on the thought that you would dwell forever and firmly like the evergreen tree. In the hearts of the Ukara and Yakara, if there were a way to bring you back, we would wish to keep you, and if there were a way to make you live, we would wish to exchange our own bodies for yours; yet, since it is a matter of a preordained contract with the Kakuriyo, and a matter that cannot be left to human power, there is nothing left but to perform the funeral rites beautifully and console your Mitama. As we prepare the Reiji of your Mikoto, respectfully and reverently invite you, and perform the rites of purification, we pray that you will quickly move here, partake of the offerings of food, sake, and various things, listen peacefully, and dwell forever as the guardian of the house and the protector of your descendants, and hidden in the shadow of the vast and deep grace of Ōmoto Sumēōmikami, may you find a wider place, advance to a higher rank, and dwell in peace.

Shigō Kokubun: Alas, before the Mitama of [Official Rank and Secular Name], I respectfully and reverently speak: since you have withdrawn your body on this day of this month, which was the limit of your life in this world, and have entered the ranks of the gods of the Kakuriyo, I pray that you will listen peacefully and calmly, and grant your consent, that from now on we shall invoke your name as [Posthumous Name] no Mikoto.

Reikon Anteishi: O [Name] no Mikoto, Mitama of the late [Name]biko, listen clearly to what I now proclaim. That a living person should die is a custom of the physical body and an unavoidable matter; since one has entered the Kakuriyo, there is no way to see that form as a person of this world, nor any way to hear that voice, and though it is in the true heart of man to grieve and sorrow, this parting on the path that one must eventually travel is a matter that cannot be stopped or calmed by human power. Furthermore, while the physical body returns to the earth upon withdrawal, the Reikon does not vanish for eternity and becomes a god; therefore, there is nothing to do now but to deliberate and act for the sake of the Mitama and to pray single-mindedly to the Ōkami. Moreover, the life and death of a person cannot be left to the hearts of parents, nor can they be stopped by the power of wife or child; since it is all a matter of a contract with the Kakuriyo, as long as one is a person of this world, one must pray for the favor of the Sumēōmikami, perform the duties of the Great Way that one must fulfill as a person, and upon death, serve by obeying the laws of the Kakuriyo. Build a firm heart like a thick pillar of true wood, fix your thoughts like a single strand of bark-rope, and no matter how others may praise or blame you, do not be concerned; only fear and revere that which the Ōkami observes, and even if you encounter hardship, do not be confused. If you raise your noble heart more and more, resolving not to let the Reikon bestowed upon you become clouded, and not to neglect the work entrusted to you, if you establish your integrity purely and firmly, the people of this world will respect you, and the Ōkami will love you. Alas, the heart of man is like the moving clouds, and like the dayflower that easily fades; just as one is blinded by the color and fragrance when seeing the beauty of flowers and autumn leaves, and just as one tilts one's ear to the sound when hearing the chirping of a thousand birds, it is a pitiful thing that one is deceived by the fleeting pleasures of this world and does not think of the eternal grace of the Kakuriyo. Nevertheless, in the deep and great heart of the Ōkami, He looks past such errors, dispels and removes such sins and defilements, and just as He saves and revives a person who has fallen into a deep abyss, and just as He moors a boat drifting without a shore on the great ocean, He helps and saves; therefore, one should look up to and revere the fact that one should observe His great heart, follow His teachings, and hide in the shadow of His favor in both death and life. O [Name] no Mikoto, because you always practiced the way of the gods, performed your duties with diligence, served the country with a pure and bright heart, and blessed people widely and deeply, even though you have now returned to the Kakuriyo, you must be dwelling there in peace and joy, having found your place. Yet, if there should be any sins or defilements that you may have committed, I pray that you will be purified and cleansed, and I pray that you will become a pure Mitama; if we pray single-mindedly to the Ōkami, the Ōkami will surely protect and bless you, and the Ōkami will surely measure you widely and deeply. Thinking well of this and understanding this well, do not let your heart be confused by the unsatisfying parting of this world, do not lose the original heart of the Way of the Gods, and just as your Ukara and Yakara pray, I pray that you will obey the Ōkami single-mindedly and dwell in peace and joy for eternity, and I pray that you will grant your consent to this.

霊魂安定詞

天晴礼○○命、故○○毘古の神霊也、今告申事を、美らに聞食せ。生る人の死る事は、現身の慣と得免れぬ事にして、幽冥に入りては、現世の人として其形を見るべき術なく、其言葉を聞くべき由も無きが故に、憂ひ悲まむは人の真心には在れども、遂に往く此一道の別は、人の力以て留め安敞ず、又現身古曾身退りては土に帰れ霊魂は常久に消る事無く、神と為る物にし有れば、今は唯神霊の御為に、善き事を議り為て、只管に大神に乞祈奉るべき事になも在る。抑も人の生死は父母の心にも任せ得ず、妻子の力にも留め敢ず、総て幽冥の契有る事にし在れば、現身の人と有ては、皇神等の恩頼を乞祈奉りて、人と在るべき大道の任々恪み勤めて、死りては幽冥の掟に服ろひ仕へ奉らむと、真木柱太き心を築固め、栲縄の一筋に思ひ定めて、人は如何に毀誉むとも拘ふ事なく、唯神の御照覧し給ふ所を愧畏み、辛苦に罹る事有るとも思ひ惑ふ事なく、愈々益々利心を起して賦け給へる霊魂は曇らせじ、依し給へる業は怠らじと、清く堅く節操を立てば現世の人も尊み、大神も愛はしみ給ふ事なるを、阿波礼人の心は浮雲の動き易く、月草の移ろひ易き物にし在れば、花紅葉の美はしきを見ては、其色香に目暗れ、百千鳥の囀るを聞きては、其声に耳を傾くる事の如く、波加奈久限り在る現世の楽に惑ひて幽冥の遠永き御恵を思はず在る類こそ憐むべき事なりけれ。然は有れども大神の厚き大御心には其過を見直し給ひ、其罪を攘ひ給ひ除き給ひて、深き淵に落ぬる人を救ひ活さしめ給ふ事の如く、大海に依る辺無く漂ふ船を繋ぎ留むる事の如く、助け給ひ、救ひ給ふ事にし有れば、其大御心を窺奉り、其教に随ひ奉りて死も生も恩頼の蔭に隠るる事の状を仰ぎ尊み奉るべき事になも有る。天晴礼汝命は常に神習ひ恪しみ勤め給ひ清き明き心に人と有る道の任に忠に直しく、御国に仕奉り、広く厚く人をも恵みて坐しければ、今幽冥に復り坐しても安く楽しく、其所を得て鎮まり坐すらめど、若し由久利奈久も過犯しけむ罪穢有らむには祓ひ給ひ清め給ひて、清々しき霊魂と成幸へ給へと斎ひ鎮めて只管に大神に乞祈奉らば必ず大神は守らひ恵まひ給はむ、必大神は広く厚く量り給はむ、此を善く思ひ、此を善く覚り給ひて、現世の飽かぬ別れに御心を惑ひ給ふ事無く、惟神の本つ心を失はず親族の乞祈む事の如く、唯一筋に大神に服ひ給ひて遠永に安く楽く鎮り給へと白す事を相諾ひ給へと白す。

§ 2

Hatsusau Norito (Funeral Liturgy): Ahare, O O O Nomikoto, before the coffin of the late O O O O Biko, I speak with reverence and awe: the world of the living is truly a place of helplessness. Until yesterday, we spoke together and deliberated together, yet today you have become one who is no longer in this world, and there is no way to commune. Shikahaaredo, your Mitama shall never fade for Tokoshihe, but shall enter the ranks of the Kami, flourish in Iyasakae, and as you are to be the Mamorigami of your Uminoko, although your Nagamikoto's Mitama is enshrined and pacified within this Yanuchi, because there is the separation of Kakuriyo and no way to speak or converse, and because it is not the way of the Utsusomi to remain as such, we have set this day as the day of Mihafuri. We have prepared the coffin of Uchito, wide and thick, pure and firm, and having served it as a Miaraka of purity, we have added various items you cherished and Medetama, and having carried the Mikoshi, we have caused you to depart from the Miyakata. We have built and solidified a stone wall at the base of the rock at the Mihafuri-sho, surrounded it with a sacred fence, and set it as your dwelling for a thousand generations. That your Nagamikoto's name may not be cast away or lost, but serve as a memorial for ten thousand generations, we have carved it into the hard stone, making it the face of the Okutsuki. We have deliberated and arranged that your descendants, one after another, shall visit throughout the long ages of spring and autumn to perform the rites, and because we are about to transfer you there, the relatives, the family, and all who were always close to you—not only those in the nearby villages within the reed fences, but even those in distant borders that seem like the clouds—have heard the sound of the wind and hastened to come. Hearing the news, the village birds have gathered and competed to arrive, saying they shall not be late for this parting in the Utsusomi world, and shall not miss this final accompaniment of the day. Each and every one, serving with reverence and awe, holds the banners in a straight line, and the attendants holding brooms who stand in rows sweep and purify the long path, intending to protect and escort you to the very limit. We offer these food offerings as the funeral rites for the deceased, and we pray that you will partake of these satisfying offerings, pass through the eighty corners of the path without any hindrance, and proceed safely, peacefully, and calmly to your rest.

Maisosai (Burial Rite): Shusai Masa-jun O and others, O O no Nomikoto, we have enshrined and pacified your Nagamikoto's Mitama within the house, and now we intend to bury this coffin, which contains your remains, deep within the Okutsuki. Hearing that your descendants and family will come to worship at this, your dwelling for a thousand generations from this day forward, we pray with awe that the stone walls shall not move, and that you shall remain in peace and tranquility.

Kasai-shiki Haraedo Norito (House Rite Purification Liturgy): Before the great presence of the four pillars of the Great Kami of the Haraedo, who are to be spoken of with awe, I speak with reverence and awe: today, having performed the funeral rites for the Shusai O and the O O master, and having finished them early, I pray that you will purify and cleanse the inside and outside of this house, as well as all the relatives, the nine clans, and all the people gathered at the funeral site, and grant that they may become pure and fortunate. I pray that you will grant this request, and I speak with reverence and awe.

Toka oyobi Yokajusannichi-sai mata Nensai Sojo (Tenth Day, Fortieth Day, or Annual Rite Presentation): Before the great presence of the San-go Sumeru O-kami, who are to be spoken of with awe, I speak with reverence and awe: today is the day to perform the rites for the late O O no Nomikoto, for the O O year (or day) of the deceased. Hearing the state of our intent to perform these rites, I pray that you will grant your consent, and that you will bless the soul of the O O master, causing it to flourish higher and wider, that we may receive your love and mercy for the distant eternity, and that you may allow us to perform these rites beautifully, and I speak with reverence and awe.

Gojunichi Goshi Sojo (Fiftieth Day Joint Enshrinement Presentation): Before the great presence of the San-go Sumeru O-kami, who are to be spoken of with awe, I speak with reverence and awe: today is the day to perform the fiftieth-day rites for the late O O master. Hearing the state of our intent to join and pacify the Mitama, I pray that you will grant your consent, and that you will bless the soul of O O, causing it to flourish higher and wider, that we may receive your love and mercy for the distant eternity, and that you may allow us to perform these rites beautifully, and I speak with reverence and awe.

Gojunichi Goshi Saimon (Fiftieth Day Joint Enshrinement Liturgy): How awe-inspiring. Shusai O and others invite the Mitama of O O Nomikoto and speak: since you, Nagamikoto, departed from the Utsushiyo, yesterday and today have passed, and as the flowing months and days have quickly reached the fiftieth day, on this auspicious day, we have enshrined you to be moved to this spirit hall to dwell together with the ancestors of generations past. We offer various items such as food and sake on the table in place of the altar and conclude our Kototama. Hearing this state of our service, we pray that you will dwell in peace as a Shizumiya for the eternity of heaven and earth. We pray that the head of the house and all within the house will have no strange hearts, perform no evil deeds, and not allow their own directions to be wayward. May the affection between parent and child be deep, the intimacy of wife and child be profound, and may they work together with one heart, not staining the name of the ancestors. May the descendants of the living, one after another, cause the family gate to rise higher and higher like the mountain pines, and may they flourish like the branches of the Igasayakuhabie, and we pray that you will allow us to perform the rites beautifully, and with our necks stretched out like the Shimatsu-dori, we speak with reverence and awe.

Saibun for the Tōkasai and Hiyakunichisai: Before the Mitama of the late Biko, we, the Shiusai, humbly speak: O Nagamikoto, though we relied upon you as a great ship, trusting that you would dwell in Kakiha and Tokiha for a hundred or a thousand years, as is the way of the Ukara and Yakara and all those near to you, alas, the Utsusemi are helpless. Since you departed for the Kakuriyo on the [ ] day of the [ ] month of the past year, marking the limit of your Utsushiyo, it is but the Kotowari of nature to feel deep sorrow, longing, and yearning. Yet, as there is a bond of the Kakuriyo from the beginning, we have consulted and determined to conduct the Mihafurinonori beautifully and to perform the Mimatsuri without omission. Though we have served with the limit of our hearts and the Kihami of our strength, our longing grows ever more intense, stirred by all we see and hear, and as days pass, today has arrived as the day of the [ ]-day Mimatsuri. Reflecting calmly, while you, Nagamikoto, were in the Utsushiyo, you served with uprightness and correctness in your duties, and were loyal and diligent for the sake of the nation and the people. We pray that the Ōkami will praise and cherish you, that you will advance into the ranks of the high gods with ever-increasing joy, and that you will attain the ultimate happiness of the seasons. We pray that you will look upon and hear our efforts to govern your Mitama well, and that you will dwell in peace and tranquility, protecting and blessing your descendants and your house. We humbly pray that you will partake of the offerings of food and sake presented at this Mimatsuri, peacefully and tranquilly.

十日祭及百日祭迄の祭文

修斎○等○○命、故○○毘古の神霊の御前に白さく、汝命伊、親族家族は更なり親しき諸人に至る迄百年千年も巌なす堅磐に常磐に坐さねと、大船の思頼みて有しを、空蝉は術無き者にかも、去にし○年○月○日を現世の限と為て、幽冥に帰き給ひぬれば、現身の習と甚も惜しく、甚も懐しく思ひ、慕ふは道理には有れども、素より幽世の契有る事にし有れば、御葬儀をだに美はしく仕奉り、後の御祭をも足はぬ事なく為さむと相議り相定めて、心の限力の至極仕奉りつつも、見る物につけ聞物に依りて左有し、右有しと偲草のみ弥繁くて、日を経る間に今日は早くも○日の御祭の日に成ぬ。倩に思へば汝命伊、現世に坐しし間は、人と有道の任に直く正しく、国の為にも人の為にも忠実に恪しく坐しつれば、大神も褒め給ひ、愛しみ給はむ事を、嬉しみ悦び弥益々に高き神の列に進み給ひ、春秋の歓楽をも極め給ふべく、大神に乞祈奉り、善く神霊を治め奉らむとする状を見行し、聞食て平穏に鎮り坐して、子孫の八十続家をも身をも守幸ひ給ひ、今日の御祭に供へ奉る礼代の御饌御酒種々の物を平けく、安らけく聞食せと白す。

一年祭以上の年祭文

此の霊殿に斎奉令坐奉る修斎○等○○命、故○○毘古の神霊の御前に白さく、汝命伊、去し○年○月○日に、現世を去り坐して今は幽冥の神の列に鎮坐が故に、此家の守神と常も尊び敬ひ仕奉るを、今日は早くも○年の御祭仕奉るべき日にも廻り来ぬれば、御祭の式も既事竟へぬ。故此御前をも持由麻波利拝み仕奉らくを御心も平穏に聞食て弥遠永に代々の祖等と御心を睦び、御力を合せ給ひて、子孫の遠き世の守、家の鎮と坐す御徳を現はし給へ、親族、家族和び睦び浦安く転楽しく令在給へと、御饌御酒を始め、海川山野の種々の美味物を、百取の机に置足はして恐み恐みも白す。

臨時祖霊拝詞

此の神霊殿を伊都の真屋と斎ひ鎮むる、何々の家の遠津御祖、世々の祖等親族家族の神霊の前に白さく。汝命等の清く明き直き正しき心を以て、大神の広き厚き恩頼を信なひ奉りて、奇き妙なる神業を悟り奉り、諸々の人等を救ひ給ひ助け給ひ、子孫の八十続守幸給ひ各も各も神を敬ひ、君を尊び親を幸ひ、夫婦兄弟睦まじく己が向々有らしめず、力を戮せ、心を一び家の業ひ緩ぶ事無く、弥遠に弥長に守奉らむ事を嬉しみ、今日の吉日の良辰に御祭仕奉りて、種々の美味物捧げ拝み仕へ奉らくを、相諾ひ給へと、畏み畏みも白す。

建碑除幕式祝詞

此の奥都城を、千代の住家と鎮り坐す○○命、故○○毘古の御霊の御前に、斎主○○畏みも白さく、今回新に太く高く厳き石碑を建て設つるに依り、今日の生日の足日に（石碑の被幕取放ち）其由告奉らくを、平けく安らけく聞食て、弥遠永に鎮まり坐して、何々が家を、石碑の弥堅らに、石垣の動く事無く、揺ぐ事無く、子孫の八十続五十橿八桑枝の如く茂久栄に栄えしめ給へ、夜の守日の守りに守り幸へ給へと御饌御酒を始め、種々の味物を百取の机に置足はして、畏み畏みも白す。
