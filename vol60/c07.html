Chapter 7: 方便 [1532]

方便〔1532〕

§ 1

Before the newly constructed shrine on Mount Adumos, Akisu and Karu were ordered to take the role of wardens, and Bachiru, together with Tamakuniwake and his party, decided to return to the mansion with joy and courage. The villagers of Suma left the old men and young girls at the sacred site, saw off the party of Tamakuniwake, and followed Bachiru to the mansion.

Originally, all the mountains, fields, and rice paddies of the village of Suma had been swallowed up by the wealth of Bachiru, and the villagers were all resigned to the circumstances of tenant farmers. However, as days passed, months advanced, and stars shifted, voices of discontent and dissatisfaction began to arise here and there, and socialists and communists began to appear. Among them, there were even extreme mammonists, and tenant farmers appeared who buried their meager assets in the depths of the earth and exhausted themselves in stinginess. Yet, this time, with the completion of the construction of Mount Adumos, all assets were to be released and distributed evenly among the villagers, and the villagers, all rejoicing, came to praise the couple Bachiru to the skies as the builders of the republic. Suddenly, the village of Suma was free of voices of resentment and jealousy, and with each face filled with a warm glow, it became a peaceful dwelling for optimists.

Princess Saberu gathered more than a dozen representatives of the villagers at her feet, took out all the account books, handed them over to them willingly, and pledged to serve the Great God of Mount Adumos forever with her husband. Here again, a grand celebration was held by the villagers, and they toasted to the long life of the couple.

Now, the party of Tamakuniwake was invited into the living room of Bachiru, where they enjoyed themselves to the fullest, and while talking to each other about the precious teachings of the God, they spent the day happily.

Chiruteru: 'I have a request for you, Tamakuniwake-sama. That I have awakened the Bodhi mind, cast aside all worldly defilements, and become a happy person who follows the Great Way of Kannagara is entirely due to your grace, my teacher. Therefore, I would like to hear your profound and subtle teachings, and since I wish to share my joy with all living beings and serve a part of the divine work, I would like you to teach me the method of propagation of the San-Iku-Kyo style.'

Tamakuniwake: 'That is truly a wonderful intention, and I, Tamakuniwake, cannot contain my feelings of joy. If that is the case, I shall tell you a little about the method of propagation directly received from our Great God.'

To receive the divine grace in one's body, And to save and help the people of the world, To open the teachings in all directions, If you wish to be a divine minister Of supreme benevolence and supreme love, At any time, Keep your heart calm and peaceful, While filling yourself with the feeling of joy, Face the living beings, And preach the profound and subtle Way. In a pure and immaculate sacred place, Build a seat and purify your body, Exclude dust and dirt, Wear clothes unstained by filth, Purify both your heart and body, And only then take your seat on the throne, Following the inquiries of the people, Preach the Way in the simplest terms. Whether they be monks, nuns, believers, Or kings, nobles, or various others, Without fearing their presence, with a sincere heart, Exhaust yourself and explain the subtle meaning, Softening your facial expression and tone of voice, Examine the spirits of the people well, Expand upon karmic connections and metaphors, And preach and teach the principles of heaven and earth. People are children of God, palaces of God, There is not a single person who dislikes The Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases. If among the audience, There is someone who attacks your preaching, Or criticizes you, Reflect deeply upon yourself. That the Kototama which does not accord with God, From the defilement of the crookedness of the heart, Has been uttered unknowingly and unconsciously, If you can surely realize this, Even if there are hundreds of thousands of enemies, With just a single word of good speech, They will be moved and suddenly become Powerful allies of God.

Even if you boast of having tens of thousands of my subordinates As your allies, With just a single word of evil speech, They will be moved and suddenly become Enemies of resentment, Like turning over one's palm. Reflect on this ultimate truth, And never make a mistake. In all things in this world, Everything should be rectified into good deeds, Well maintain the good of love, Well realize the truth of faith, And only after that, if you preach the true Way to the people of the world, No matter what kind of heretical and crooked person they may be, They will never oppose you. Sincerity alone saves the world, The teachings of God are before your eyes, Manifesting as a great miracle, Everything in heaven and earth is created By the water and fire of the Kototama.'
