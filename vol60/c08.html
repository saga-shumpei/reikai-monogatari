Chapter 8: 土蜘蛛 [1533]

土蜘蛛〔1533〕

§ 1

Tamakuniwake and his party left the mansion of Bachiru and, approaching the site of the old shrine at the foot of Mount Adsumosu once again, found that it was just as Shiyōjiyōhime had said; when they cleared away about five sun of surface soil, a rectangular stone lid appeared.

Tamakuniwake first offered a prayer for the removal of the stone lid.

‘In this precious sacred site of Sumēru, where the palace pillars were built thick and firm to endure for eternity, at the ancient site where we served the shrine of Ōkunihiko, the ancestor of the teachings of the Brahman who dwells in eternal peace, I now, by the wish of Shiyōjiyōhime, purify my heart and cleanse my body, and chanting the precious Kototama, open the Tamatebako that has been sealed since three thousand years ago, receiving the grace of Kami. I humbly pray that those who serve in this Kamuwaza, being pure, upright, and sincere in heart, and having returned to the spirit of Kami, may open this Ihato with great ease, without any blame or punishment, regardless of what Kami may dwell within, as this is a precious treasure. Furthermore, grant us leave to steal into this Gankutsu and swiftly search out the secrets that have remained since the age of the gods. Before the presence of Kannagara Sumeru Ōkami, I humbly and reverently pray. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ah, Kannagara Reikō Haisase, Kannagara Reikō Haisase.’

Having chanted this precious prayer, he used the sacred spade and hoe to push aside the earth, inserted a washed and purified iron bar into the gap in the rock, and at last succeeded in removing the wide and thick stone lid. Black smoke rose up in thick clouds, and for a time it was a dismal scene where one could not even distinguish things close at hand. Just then, the wind of Shinato blew, scattering the black smoke until it vanished, and the entrance to the Ihato, including the stairs, became clearly visible.

Tamakuniwake: 'How auspicious this morning is, as we begin to open the secrets that have existed since the age of the gods.'

Bachiru: 'How auspicious this day is, as the Ihato that has sealed the Nine-Headed Dragon for all eternity is finally opened.'

Saberuhime: 'This is the day we shall meet the Takushaka Dragon King, of whom we have heard stories passed down since the age of the gods.'

Itahiko: 'I intend to explore the depths of this Gankutsu now; pray, grant me leave.'

Tamakuniwake: 'The inclination of Itahiko, who seeks to be first in all things, has truly manifested.'

Itahiko: 'To always be the first to advance ahead of others is the very taste of my being.'

Sanzenhiko: 'The nature of Itahiko has manifested, and he seeks to advance into a dangerous hole.'

Itahiko: 'Exploring a Gankutsu such as this should not be difficult; it is a mediocre task, no more than a pre-breakfast affair.'

Tamakuniwake appointed Itahiko as the chief supervisor, had him take Wax and Elle as companions, and left the rest of the party waiting at the entrance of the Gankutsu. He hung a bell at the end of a long rope at the entrance of the hole, instructing them that if an emergency arose, they should pull the rope so that reinforcements would come, and then, brushing away spider webs one after another, he descended the stairs with a heavy tread to search for the key to the secret of several thousand years.

Strangely, the long and deep tunnel was brilliant with phosphorescent light, and it was so bright that they did not even have to complain about the difficulty of seeing their footing.

The three of them, while singing the song of the heavenly numbers, explored the Gankutsu, which was said to be the place where the Takushaka Dragon King was imprisoned, sometimes descending, sometimes ascending, and turning left and right as their feet led them. Suddenly, there was a bright spot. When they approached and looked, a mysterious, round, brownish object about three shaku in diameter was lying in the middle of the tunnel, radiating a dim light. When they strained their ears to listen, they heard a mysterious buzzing sound. For a moment, the three stood there, dumbfounded, gazing at this strange object. Suddenly, a sound like porridge boiling, bub-bub-bub, was heard loudly.

Elle: 'Hey Wax, this thing must be a monster. How about we give it a good whack with this staff?'

Wax: 'Wait, wait. We don't know what might come out. If we carelessly make an enemy of it, that would be a disaster.'

Itahiko: 'Hahaha, it looks just like the thing Elle-sama was trampled by a cow. It seems to be steaming, too. Perhaps this is what Tano Kiuhei dropped, his eight-mat-sized sheet.'
