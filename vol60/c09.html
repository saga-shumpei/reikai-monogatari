Chapter 9: Night Light Jewel [1534]

夜光玉〔1534〕

§ 1

Eru had his Kimodama taken by the Kuwaibutsu, turned pale, and, lured by Okubiyōkaze, began to tremble bit by bit.

Wakkusu: 'Hey, Eru, pull yourself together. How can you possibly carry out this Tanken when a full-grown man with Kintama is frightened by a mere Kumo Bakemono? Are we not supposed to show Batsugun Tegara this time to atone for the Arayuru Akugofu we have committed until now? You are truly a Koshinuke.'

Eru: 'You should not scold me like that. If I were the man I was until now, I would show more Yūki, but since the Kintama I should have hanging are gone, things do not go according to Chiumondoho. You would be in trouble if you did not show me some Dōjiyō for that.'

Wakkusu: 'What is that Manuke look on your face? Thinking that your small Ganmen was covered in Menekichi, Mikaikonchi, and Kuōbuchi, it turns out you were a useless Shiromono after all. Itahiko-sama, if we take a fellow like this deeper from here on, it will be a Meiwaku to us, so how about we send him back from here?'

Itahiko: 'That would be fine too. Now, Eru, you are exempted from here. You had better return at once.'

Eru: 'Yes, thank you. In that case, would you please send me back to the entrance?'

Itahiko: 'That is a bit difficult.'

Wakkusu: 'Hey, Eru, pull yourself together. Everything depends on one's state of mind. Now, it would be better for you to return alone. You will be fine with this one Kongō-zue.'

Eru: 'If that is the case, I have no choice; I shall follow in the middle of the three of us.'

Wakkusu: 'Ha, you have finally turned into a coward. In that case, Itahiko-sama, I, Wakkusu, who am strong against both evil and good, shall take the lead; this is interesting.'

With that, while stomping his feet, he advanced into the phosphorescent rocky tunnel, probing step by step. From the direction ahead, two or three glowing orbs came floating toward them at a height of about three feet above the ground. When they looked closely, a disgusting face was clearly visible within those pale blue orbs. Eru hunched his back, pressed his face against Wakkusu's back, and followed with trembling legs. The pale blue fireballs exploded all at once about one or two ken in front of Wakkusu with a tremendous sound. Eru screamed and fell down while still clutching Wakkusu's shoulder. Wakkusu had no choice but to fall down on the spot with a thud.

Itahiko: 'Hey, Wakkusu-sama, Eru-sama, get up, get up, the enemy has met with the disaster of being pulverized and has vanished. It is safe now. It seems they perished easily in fear of the divine radiance of the Lord God, ha ha ha.'

Wakkusu: 'I am not a Wakkusu to be surprised by such a thing, but because Eru grabbed my neck and fell, even a brave warrior like me met with the disaster of falling together. Hey, Eru, pull yourself together.'

Eru: 'Yes, I will pull myself together now. Brother, please pull yourself together too. As long as you and Itahiko-sama are strong, it will be fine.'

Wakkusu: 'No matter what you say, this is a demonic cavern that has been sealed for thousands of years, so I am prepared for various strange and bizarre incidents to break out. Now let us go; since we are messengers of pardon to the Takushaka Ryūō, there is no reason for the demons to torment us so recklessly. It is also Eru's fault that his hand was bitten by the Kuwaibutsu; a bee that is not teased will not sting. Now, let us cheer up and sing a propaganda song to boost our spirits. I will sing, so follow along with the chorus. It is a demonic cavern where, for some reason, there is no feeling of comfort anywhere.'

Though the morning sun may shine or cloud over, Though the moon may wax or wane, Though the spiders of the cavern may transform, What is there to fear? As the messengers of the three-five God, Beginning with Itahiko-tsukasa, We are the three pillars of Wakkusu and Eru. In the midst of the three thousand worlds, At the deep roots of Mount Adsumos, To pardon and save the Ryūō Who was sealed away, We have come to act As the messengers of the noble God. Even if any kind of Kuwaibutsu May lurk like clouds and mist, Bathed in the power of God, My body that advances is diamond-indestructible, Like the wish-fulfilling jewel. It does not drown in water, it does not burn in fire, It does not rust, it does not rot, it does not cloud.

Itahiko and his party descend the tunnel again with a thumping sound. There, a sound like thunder can be heard. Strange to say, as they descend the flat tunnel for about one chō, they find a fairly wide river where water as cold as ice flows, as if emerging from the rocks and being absorbed back into them. The three crossed the stream and reached the other side. Here, countless rocks of various sizes and shapes stand, shining brilliantly. It is a mystery that light shines from the gaps in the rocks from nowhere. As the three looked around in wonder, they noticed a night-light jewel shining atop a pillar of stalactite that must have been a jō high. Itahiko recited the Amatsu-norito here and inquired about the divine will. According to the divine revelation, this jewel is the Night-Light Jewel, a treasure of the Dragon King Takshaka. However, it was said that when this jewel was left in his possession, he would again run rampant between heaven and earth, inducing natural disasters of wind, water, and fire, so the god Tsukiteruhiko took it away, enshrined it here, and sealed the Dragon King deep at the bottom of the cavern. Furthermore, the divine revelation was that Itahiko himself should take this jewel back and hand it to Tamakuniwake. Itahiko was greatly overjoyed, and after much ingenuity, he obtained the jewel and respectfully tucked it into his bosom. Once again, chanting the heavenly number songs, he proceeded endlessly toward the underground cavern. The light of the jewel stored in his bosom illuminated the underground cavern, and he passed through rugged, narrow, and cramped rock holes until he reached the lowest cavern. Here, a rock lid was applied, and the Dragon King Takshaka, that is, the Nine-Headed Dragon, was firmly sealed within.

Itahiko stood still and proclaimed the divine revelation.
