Chapter 15: Liturgy [1540]

祝詞〔1540〕

§ 1

Norito: In the presence of the august and sacred Ōmotōomikami, whose name is to be revered, I humbly, respectfully, and with awe, speak. The land of Mizuho, with its thousand-five-hundred autumns of abundant rice ears, has been established since the time of the beginning of heaven and earth, with Kuninotokotachinomikoto reigning in Kakiha and Tokiha. As the original land, the Urayasukuni, which the Lord has loved and protected, and which has been opened up in such detail and with such grace, the Sumeōkami has released the heavenly Ihakura, parted the eight-fold clouds of heaven into a thousand divisions, and descended; since that time, there has been no movement and no change. The hearts of men are straight and true, and the mountains and rivers are pure and clear. For the Inadane that the Utsushiki Aohitogusa must eat to live, the Ōmikami, by His divine word, appointed the rulers of the heavenly villages. The rice planted in the narrow and long fields of heaven was bestowed upon us, as the Ōmikami commanded that it be tasted as the distant divine offerings of the heavenly food, the red-eared rice. The Toyoashihara-no-nushinokami took the five-hundred-fold sacred plow to cultivate the high and low fields; the Mizuwakenokami caused the water to be spread, and the Haniyasukami enriched the true clay. The Ōtoshinokami warded off the locusts. The autumn's long-eared rice, with its heavy heads, has ripened in prosperity. The heat and cold of summer and winter are mild, and there are few evil plagues. In every cape of the islands one looks upon, in every nook of the shores one scrapes to see, the heavy waves of Tokoyo come rolling in, and all the people who live and dwell there, whether high or low, old or young, rejoice and hold them in reverence. The eighty islands of the land where the Tōtsu-mikami reigns are vast; the walls of heaven stand at their limits, and the boundaries of the land are set. The narrow lands are made wide, and the steep lands are made level. To the limits where the blue clouds drift, and to the ends where the white clouds descend and hover, the great plains are trodden by the roots of trees and the rocks. As far as the horse's hoof can reach, the loads are bound firmly, and the long roads continue without interruption. In the blue sea, the oars and rudders are never dried, and the prows of the ships reach as far as they may. With true rudders aligned and ships floating side by side, all the treasures of various kinds, which make the ears marvel and the eyes shine, are brought from near and far lands. Like the river boats that come and go, they are brought slowly and steadily, and are piled up like mountains on the hundred-fold tables. These are offered to the Sumeōkami and distributed throughout the great land. In the arts of learning and in all the laws, they have opened and added to their prosperity. The lands in the four quarters under heaven are the divine land, the Urayasukuni. The country is rich and prosperous, and both the capital and the countryside rejoice and bustle in abundance. When there are enemies who, having no way and possessing dark hearts, shoot and turn against us, all the people, raising the sincerity of the Kototama transmitted by our ancestral gods, shall rouse themselves. With the stern and heroic spirit, and the stern rebuke, they shall rise up, saying: 'If I go to the sea, I shall be a corpse in the water; if I go to the mountain, I shall be a corpse in the grass; I shall die by the side of the Ōkami, I shall not die in ease.' Without looking back, they shall advance and advance, pressing and pressing. At every mountain ridge, they shall strike down the foe; at every river rapid, they shall drive them away, bringing them to submission and harmony. That we are at peace and in joy is entirely due to the vast and thick divine grace of our Ōkami. With the knowledge of the sacred, and the reverence for the divine, like the snail that crawls around and the fish that looks up and gasps, we offer our worship. The Ōkami, clearing the surface of the great mirror of truth, looks upon us. Giving His divine consent, He makes the divine land a prosperous and blessed reign, established in Kakiha and Tokiha. Those who serve in the government offices, without neglecting the root or the branch, the inner or the outer, holding the middle path of the sacred spear, shall perform the government of Aizen-Shin-Ma. Following the single path of the ink-line struck by the Hida people, and following the laws of the teachings of the three-five, they shall spread and guide the good and true path, ever further and ever wider. Both men and women, old and young, together, shall not be caught like the bird on the birdlime by the evil words of Oyozure. They shall yearn for and believe in this path, not harboring evil hearts, nor doing crooked things. If they should commit a fault, the Kannohinomi and Ōnohinomi shall see it right and hear it corrected. The rice seeds that the common people plant in the narrow and long fields of heaven, having descended and gathered the water bubbles on their arms and the mud on their thighs, shall not be damaged by the frequent rains or the overflowing river rapids. They shall not be uprooted or rotted by the raging winds. They shall not be eaten and ruined by poisonous insects. The name of the land they hold shall be made to ripen in abundant Mizuho. Proclaiming themselves as the long-lived people of the world, and living for a thousand and ten thousand years, if there be those who wish to build great merits for the sake of the world and for the sake of others, and who suffer from illness, may they be healed and saved instantly, with the divine power of the spirit of the Kaminagara, and with the manifestation of clear signs, even in a single day or a moment. May they not slacken in their own hands or in their family business, but work and work without idleness. May their descendants flourish like the eighty-fold branches of the fifty-oak tree, and may You protect and bless us in the watch of the night and the watch of the day, I humbly, respectfully, and with awe, speak.

原文（近代文語）

祝詞

掛巻も畏き大本大御神の宇豆の大前に、慎み敬ひ恐み恐みも白さく。大地の千五百秋の瑞穂の国は。天地初発之時より、国之常立尊の堅磐に常磐に鎮り居坐して。国の本国浦安国と愛給ひ守賜ひて、〓__GT_13105__怜に委曲に開給ひし国にし有れば。皇大神の天の石位放ち、天の八重雲を伊都の千別に千別て、天降り給ひてより動く事無く変る事無く。人の心は直く正しく、山川は清く潔けく。顕見蒼生の食て活くべき稲種は、大御神の大御言以て天の邑君を定給ひ。天の狭田長田に植ゑしめ玉ひし稲を、天津御饌の遠御饌と赤丹の穂に聞食詔給ひ授け賜ひて。大地主神は五百津斎鋤を採りて、高田窪田を耕し賜ひ、水分神は水を撒かしめ、埴安神は真埴を肥やし。大歳の神は蝗を攘ひ。秋の足穂の八束穂の、重穂に成幸給ひ。夏冬の暑さ寒さも和らかに、悪しき疫少く。打見る嶋の崎々、掻見る磯の隈々。常世乃波の重浪依来て、生と生き住と住む人の悉。高きも卑きも老も若きも嬉しみ尊み。遠津御神の敷坐す島の八十島は。天の壁立極み、国の退立限り。狭き国は広く峻しき国は平けく。青雲の靉く極み、白雲の墜居向伏限り。大野原は磐根木根履佐久美。馬の爪の至り留る限り。荷緒結堅めて、長道間無く立続き。青海原は棹舵乾さず、船の舳の至らむ限り。真舵繁貫き浮並べて、遠近の国の悉、耳驚き眼輝く種々の珍の宝を。霜黒葛来るや来るや川舟の毛曾呂毛曾呂に持渡り来て、百取の机に横山の如く置足はして。皇大神に献り。大御国中に敷施こし。学の術に総の法に、弥益も開け添はりて。天の下四方の国は、神の御国浦安国と。国富栄えて都も鄙も恵良々々に歓ぎ賑はひ。邂逅に道無く黒き心以て、射向ひ奉る敵在る時は。万民挙り、御祖神の伝へ賜へる、言霊の真心を振起し。厳の雄健び踏健び。厳の嘖譲を起して、海往かば水潜屍山往かば草生屍大神の辺にこそ死なめ閑には死なじ。顧は為じと、弥進みに進み、弥逼に逼り。山の尾ごとに追伏せ河の瀬ごとに追攘ひて言向け和し、心安く心楽しきは、専我大神の広き厚き大御恵と、斎知り厳知り細螺の伊這回り噞喁ふ魚の打仰ぎ、敬礼奉らくを、真澄の大御鏡の面を押霽して見行し。相諾ひ玉ひて神の御国を。堅磐に常磐に茂御代の足御代に成幸賜ひ。官々に仕へ奉る人等は本末内外を過たず、茂鉾の中執持ちて愛善信真の政事をなさしめ給ひ。飛騨人が打つ墨縄の唯一道に守るべき。三五の教の憲の随々。善き道の正しき道を、弥遠に弥広に弘め導かしめ給ひ。男も女も老も若きも。相共に於与豆礼の妖言に黐鳥の罹る事無く。斯道を慕ひ信ひ、悪しき心を持たしめず、曲れる事を為さしめず。過ちて犯さむ事は、神直日大直日に見正し聞治し坐し。百姓の天の狭田長田に降りて手肱に水泡掻垂り。向股に泥掻寄せて、取作らむ水田種子は。霖雨降頻き河の瀬溢れて浸し損ふ事無く。暴き風吹荒びて、根掘倒れ朽損ふ事なく。毒しき虫の生出て喰荒しめむ事無く。負持てる国の名の、茂瑞穂に稔らしめ給ひ。世の長人世の遠人と名告らひつつ。千歳万歳生存へて。世の為人の為太じき功績を樹てむと欲ふ人々の病煩はむ事あらば、一日片時も疾く速けく。惟神の霊法に威の御魂を寄賜ひ。著き験を効はして忽ち癒えしめ救助けて。己が手手家の業務緩ぶ事なく怠る事無く。弥勤めに勤め締て。子孫の八十連五十橿八桑枝の如く。茂木栄に栄えしめ給ひ、夜の守日の守りに守幸へ賜へと。恐み恐みも白す。

§ 2

I speak with Kotowa. In the morning, I purify the sins and defilements of my body, and I offer my worship. May You listen high and far, like the ears of the Fuchikoma of Ame. Do not let the Oshihetsukasa have their own ways for the sake of their Oshi, but let them build their respective Isao. Especially, I pray that You protect and bless the Yanuchi, and all the Mamehito of Ananai-kyō, and their bodies and homes. With Kagojimono kneeling and Ujimono stretching their Unane, I pray with awe and trembling.

Ananai-kyō Honbu Taisai Norito: In the presence of the Uzu of the awe-inspiring Ōmotosumeōmikami, who resides and is enshrined in this Miaraka, I, the Ihahinushi (so-and-so), speak with reverence and awe. Although there are many Yasokahi, I have chosen today as the day of life and sufficiency. With awe and gratitude for the teachings of the Imperial Way of Ananai, and wishing to repay even a thousandth of a part, I perform the annual spring (autumn) Great Festival. I hang cotton on the five-hundred-branched sacred trees of the deep mountains, fill high-footed vessels with offerings of food, fill the jars to the brim with sake, stack the mirror-mochi in many layers, and fill vessels with water and salt. I pile high the products of mountain and field, such as sweet and bitter greens and various fruits, and the products of sea and river, from wide-finned and narrow-finned fish to deep-sea and shore seaweeds, upon the hundred-fold offering tables. I pray that You listen peacefully and tranquilly, and grant Your consent. May the life of the Emperor be as long as the long-armed ones, and may the Heavenly Sun-Lineage flourish forever with the heaven and earth. May the teachings of the Ōmotosumeōmikami, which are taught directly and correctly to people, be free from discrepancies in the words of the speakers and free from misunderstandings in the ears of the listeners. May the believers of the Imperial Way of Ananai, both old and young, believe only in the pure, bright, and sincere path, without being led astray or entering into strange and base evil paths. May they respect the gods, honor the sovereign, and conduct themselves correctly and beautifully as parents, spouses, siblings, and friends. May they not have their own ways, but work diligently and ever more diligently at their family trades. May they flourish and prosper like the fifty-oak and eight-mulberry branches, down to the eighty generations of their descendants. If there be any illness or disease, I pray that You grant them the divine spirit of Izu according to the divine law, and let them receive Your grace as quickly as a moment. I pray that You listen to this petition for protection by day and by night, and grant Your consent. With Kagojimono kneeling and Ujimono stretching their Unane, I pray with awe and trembling.

Honbu Ōkami Tsukinamisai Norito: In the presence of the Uzu of the awe-inspiring Ōmotosumeōmikami, who is enshrined and resides in this Kamidoko, I, the Ihahinushi (so-and-so), speak with reverence and awe. Today, I perform the monthly festival. I fill high-footed vessels with food, fill the jars to the brim with sake, stack the mirror-mochi in many layers, and pile high the products of mountain and field, such as sweet and bitter greens and various fruits, and the products of sea and river, from wide-finned and narrow-finned fish to deep-sea and shore seaweeds, and water and salt, like a mountain. I pray that You listen peacefully and tranquilly to these words, and grant Your consent. May the reign of the Emperor, who rules the Great Eight-Island Land as a living god, be as long as the long-armed ones, and may it flourish forever with the heaven and earth. May the teachings of the Ōmotosumeōmikami be free from discrepancies in the words of the speakers and free from misunderstandings in the ears of the listeners. May the believers of the Imperial Way of Ananai, each and every one, believe only in the pure, bright, and sincere path, without being led astray or entering into strange and base evil paths. May they respect the gods, honor the sovereign, and conduct themselves correctly and beautifully as parents, spouses, siblings, and friends. May they not have their own ways, but work diligently and ever more diligently at their family trades. May they flourish and prosper like the fifty-oak and eight-mulberry branches, down to the eighty generations of their descendants. I pray that You protect them by day and by night. With Kagojimono kneeling and Ujimono stretching their Unane, I pray with awe and trembling.

Kakke Kamidoko Senzasai Norito: In the presence of the awe-inspiring Ōmotosumeōmikami, I (so-and-so), having purified and cleansed myself, speak with awe and trembling. Although there are many Yasokahi, I have chosen today as the day of life and sufficiency. I have moved the deity to this newly prepared Kamidoko and intend to perform the festival. I pray that You peacefully and tranquilly accept the sake, food, and various products of sea, river, and mountain. May any sins or defilements committed by the head of the household and all others be seen and heard correctly by the gods of rectification, and may the heart of the Great God be at peace and settled. I pray with awe and trembling that You protect and bless them forever.

原文（近代文語）

辞別けて白さく。朝に異に身の罪穢を祓ひ清めて、拝み仕奉らくを。天の斑駒の耳弥高に聞上賜ひて。宣教師が教の為に己が向々有らしめず、其程々の功績を建てしめ賜ひ。殊に是の家内を始めて、三五教の教信徒諸々が。家をも身をも護り恵まひ幸賜へと。鹿児自物膝折伏せ、宇自物頸根突抜来て、恐み恐みも祈願奉らくと白す。

三五教本部大祭祝詞

此の神殿に坐せ奉り斎奉る、掛巻くも綾に畏き、三五皇大御神の宇豆の大前に、斎主（某）慎み敬ひ畏み畏みも白さく、八十日日は有れども、今日を生日の足日と選み定めて、皇道三五の御教を恐み辱み、千々の一重も報い奉らむと、毎年の例の任々、春（秋）の大御祭仕へ奉らむとして、奥山の五百枝真栄木に木棉取垂で、御食は高杯に盛足らはして、御酒は甕の戸高知り甕の腹満て並べて、餅の鏡は八十比良加に積み重ね、御水堅塩は窪手に盛満たして山野の物は甘菜辛菜種々の果実、海川の物は鰭の広物、鰭の狭物、奥津藻菜、辺津藻菜に至る迄、百取の机代に盛高成して称言竟へ奉らくを、平けく安らけく聞食し相諾ひ給ひて、天皇の大御寿を手長の大御寿と堅磐に常磐に、天津日継は天地の共弥遠永に栄え座さしめ給ひ、直く正しく人に憑りて教へ給ひ諭し給へる、三五皇大御神の御教は、説く人の説きの違ひ無く、聞く人の聞きの誤あらしめず、皇道三五の信徒等は老も若も、清き明き誠の道をのみ信ひて、異しき卑しき邪道に惑ふ事なく入る事なく、神を敬ひ君を尊み、親子夫婦兄弟朋友の行ひ正しく美はしく、己が向々有らしめず、家の業弥締りに締り弥勤めに勤めて、子孫の八十続に至る迄五十橿八桑枝の如く茂久栄に立栄えしめ給ひ、邂逅に病疾らはむ事あらば、一日片時も疾く速けく神随の神法に伊豆の神霊を蒙らしめ給ひ、恩頼を仰がしめ給ひ、夜の守日の守に守護幸へ給へと祈願白す事の状を聞し食し相諾ひ給へと、鹿児自物膝折伏せ鵜自物頸根突抜きて畏み畏みも白す。

本部大神月次祭祝詞

此の神床に斎き奉り座せ奉る、掛巻も綾に恐き三五皇大御神の宇豆の大前に、斎主（某）慎み敬ひも白さく。今日はしも月毎の御祭仕へ奉るとして、御食は高杯に盛足らはし、御酒は甕の戸高知り甕の腹満て並べて、餅の鏡は八十比良加に積み重ね、山野の物は甘菜辛菜種々の果実、海川の物は鰭の広物鰭の狭物、奥津藻菜辺津藻菜、御水堅塩に至る迄、横山の如く置き足らはして、称言竟へ奉らくを平けく安らけく聞食して、現御神と大八洲国知食す天皇の大御代を、手長の大御代と堅磐に常磐に、天地の共弥遠永に栄え座さしめ給ひ、三五皇大御神の御教は、説く人の説の違なく、聴く人の聴の誤あらしめず、皇道三五の信徒等は各も各も清き明き誠の道をのみ信ひて、異しき卑しき邪道に惑ふ事なく入る事なく、神を敬ひ君を尊み、親子夫婦兄弟朋友の行ひ正しく美はしく、己が向々あらしめず、家の業弥締りに締り弥勤めに勤めて、子孫の八十続に至る迄、五十橿八桑枝の如く茂久栄に立栄えしめ給ひ、夜の守日の守に守り幸へ給へと、祈願白す事の状を聞こし食相諾ひ給へと、鹿児自物膝折伏せ鵜自物頸根突抜きて、恐み恐みも白す。

各家神床遷座祭祝詞

掛巻も恐き三五皇大御神の大前に（某）由麻波利清麻波里、恐み恐みも白さく。八十日日は有れども今日を生日の足日と選み定めて、此回新に設備へる神殿に遷し奉り座せ奉り、御祭仕へ奉らむとす。故御酒御饌海川山野種々の物を、平らかに安らかに聞食して、家長を始め諸人が過犯しけむ罪穢有らむをば、神直日大直日に見直し聞直しまして、大神の御心も安く隠に鎮り坐して、弥遠永に守り幸へ給へと畏み畏みも白す。
