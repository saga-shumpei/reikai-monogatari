Chapter 17: 崇詞 [1542]

崇詞〔1542〕

§ 1

Soreishiya Asayufu Nitsupai Norito

In the presence of the great and sacred Ōmotosumeōmikami, who is enshrined and dwells in this Mitamaya, I humbly, reverently, and with awe speak: though it is a matter of great awe to utter, I humbly and gratefully praise and conclude the words of prayer, that the Mitama of the Aohitogusa (the distant ancestors and successive generations of ancestors of the Nanigashike), whom the Ōkami have always cherished and loved through their deep and lofty Miizu, may be enshrined and calmed in this Mitamaya, and that they may enjoy the Mitamanofuyu according to the Ōmichi of Kamunagara.

Specifically, I speak before the Mitama of the various spirits (the distant ancestors and successive generations of ancestors of the Nanike) who are enshrined and dwell in this Mitamaya: as humans are granted the Amatsumitama through the wondrous Musubi of the Sumemioya, and are created by the two Ōkami, Izanagi and Izanami, and are nurtured within the great light of Amaterasuōmikami, if they serve and strive purely and correctly according to the laws of the heart and the conduct of the body in the manifest world, they shall return to the divine court of the Amatsukuni, and there receive the divine favor of the Amatsukami; therefore, I pray that you may value and respect the divine principle of serving for long and eternal ages, and that you may peacefully and calmly partake of the offerings (the daily food) presented to you; and I pray that the various people who worship and serve at this court of teachings may be free from strange hearts and evil deeds, free from illness, free from troubles, and that the family business may not slacken or be neglected, and that you may cause them to flourish in ever-increasing prosperity, and protect and bless them in the watches of the night and the watches of the day, I speak with awe and trembling.

Sorei Senza Sai

Before the Mitama of the distant ancestors and successive generations of ancestors of the Naninani family, I humbly and reverently speak: I have purified this shrine, and as we move and enshrine you here from this day forth, I pray that you will peacefully and calmly hear and consent to this, and that you will protect and bless the family line and the eighty generations of descendants, causing them to flourish ever more; with this, I offer various divine food offerings and speak with awe and trembling.

Ichinen Sai Saibun

Before the Mitama of the late [Name] no Mikoto, who is enshrined in this Mitamaya, I speak: For you, O Mikoto, it has already been one year since you departed from this manifest world, on this very day of this month last year. Therefore, without a moment of forgetting, we yearn for you; seeing the beautiful colors of flowers and autumn leaves, we think of the past; hearing the chirping of the hundred birds, we long for those days; and having yearned for and remembered you in various ways, we have served you as the guardian deity of this house, day and night, without ceasing; and today, because the relatives, family, and various people have gathered together to perform the memorial rites widely and generously, we offer the ritual offerings and various divine food offerings upon the hundred-fold tables like a mountain, and as we conclude our words of praise, I pray that you will peacefully and calmly hear them, and that you will join your hearts and combine your powers with the ancestors of successive generations for ever and ever, manifesting your Miizu as the guardian of the distant descendants and the protector of this house, causing the family to stand high and flourish, and causing the family and relatives to be harmonious and peaceful, and to live in comfort and joy, I speak with awe and trembling.

Specifically, before the Mitama of the distant ancestors and successive generations of ancestors of the Naninani family, I speak: Today, as we perform the one-year memorial service for the Mitama of [Name] no Mikoto, I pray that you will peacefully and calmly taste and accept the delicious food offerings presented, I speak with awe and trembling.

Itsujukakan Shinrei Haishi

O Mitama of the late [Name] no Mikoto, for your sake, O Mikoto, we have exhausted our sincerity in praying to the Ōkami to deliberate and act for your benefit; we pray that you will praise what is good, and for any transgressions, we pray that you will forgive them and find your place, and we pray that you will attain the ultimate joy; hearing this, I pray that you will rely solely upon the Ōkami, and without being confused, without wavering, and with peace and tranquility, you may be calmed.

Kasai Norito

With awe, I speak before the Mitama of [Name] no Mikoto: You, O Mikoto, have layered years of a hundred and a thousand, and have borne the name of a long-lived person in this world; while you lived, not only your family but also many people always thought of you with admiration; however, as is the way of humans in the manifest world (being unable to endure illness), you have left the manifest world, hidden yourself in the Yūmei, and ascended to the Amatsukuni; and as the rites of the divine funeral have already been completed according to the rules of Kamunagara, we have purified the inside and outside of the sacred hall, offered various things, and perform this memorial service; I pray that you will peacefully and calmly hear this, and hidden in the shade of the wide and deep blessings of the Ōkami, you may dwell in peace and joy, and as the guardian deity of this house for the eighty generations of descendants in the distant future, you may remain enshrined, and I pray that you will consent to our serving the rites of the seasons without ceasing and without slackening, I speak with awe and trembling.

祖霊社朝夕日拝祝詞

是の祖霊殿に斎き奉り鎮まり坐す三五皇大御神の宇豆の大前に慎み敬ひ畏み畏み白さく、言巻も畏けれども、大神等の深き高き御徳を蒙りて、常も撫で給ひ愛給へる青人草等（何某家遠祖代々祖等）の神霊諸々を、此祖霊殿に斎ひ鎮めて惟神なる大道の随々恩頼を幸ひ給ふ事を、嬉み忝み畏みも称へ言竟奉らくと白す。

言別て此の霊舎に斎き奉り坐せ奉る諸々（何家遠祖代々祖等）の神霊の御前に白さく、人は皇御祖の奇に妙なる造化に依りて天津御魂を賜はり、伊邪那岐、伊邪那美二柱の大神の生成し給ひて、天照大御神の大御光の中に養育はるる者なれば、顕世の心の律法、身の行を惟神清く正しく務め励みせば、天津御国の神の廷に帰り坐して其程々に天津神の御愛顧を受て、永く久しく仕へ奉るべき神理を尊み、重みつつ、供へ奉る幣帛を（毎日の御饌を）平らけく安らけく聞食て、是の教の御廷に拝み仕奉る諸人等は、異き心悪き行ひ無く、病しき事なく、煩はしき事なく、家の業緩ぶ事なく怠る事なく、弥栄に栄しめ給ひ、夜の守り日の守りに守り幸ひ給へと、畏み畏みも白す。

祖霊遷座祭

何々家の遠津祖、世々の祖等の御霊の御前に慎み敬ひも白さく、此の御宮を祓清めて、今日より遷し奉り坐せ奉る事を、平らけく安らけく聞食相諾ひ給ひて、弥益々に家門高く子孫の八十続をも守幸へ給へと、種々の神饌物を捧げ奉りて、恐み恐みも白す。

一年祭祭文

此の霊殿に斎ひ奉り坐せ奉る、○○命故○○毘古の神霊の御前に白さく。汝命や現世を身退坐つるは、昨年の此月の今日と早くも一年廻れる御祭の日に成ぬ。故常も忘るる間なく、慕ひつつ花紅葉の美麗き色を見ては昔を思ひ、百鳥の囀る声を聞ては其世を恋ひ、種々に恋しみ偲び奉りて、此の家の守神と持斎、御前には夜となく昼となく仕奉中にも、今日は親族家族諸人等、弥集へに集へて広く厚く祭祀治め奉るが故に、礼代の幣帛と種々の神饌物を百取の机に横山の如く供へ奉りて、称言竟奉らくを、平けく、安らけく聞食て、弥遠永に世々の祖等と御心を一び御力を合せ給ひて、子孫の遠き世の守り、家の鎮と坐す御徳を現はし給ひ、家門高く立栄しめ給ひ家族親族和び睦び、浦安く転楽しく在しめ給へと畏み畏みも白す。

辞別て何々の家の遠津御祖、世々の祖等の御前に白さく、今日はしも○○毘古の神霊の一年の御祭仕奉るとして、供へ奉る美味物を相甞に聞食相諾ひ給へと、恐み恐みも白す。

五十日間新霊拝詞

○○命、故○○の神霊や、汝命の御為には、善き事議り為さむと真心を尽して大神に乞祈奉り、善き事は褒め給ひ、過あらむには宥め給ひて其所を得しめ給ひ、其楽を極めしめ給へと祈白す事を聞食て、只管に大神を憑頼坐して。惑はず多由多はず平穏に鎮まり給へと白す。

家祭祝詞

畏きや、○○命の御前に白さく、汝命は百年千年の齢を重ねて、世の長人の名を負ひ坐む事をし、家族は更なり、諸人も常に多能母志美思ひつつ在経し間に、現身の人の慣と（病には得堪給ずて）現し世を離て幽冥に隠り坐し天津御国に昇り坐しぬれば惟神の御掟の任々事議りて、神葬の礼も既功竟ぬれば、瑞の殿の内外も清らかに祓ひ清めて種々の物供へ奉りて、御祭仕奉る状を平けく安らけく聞食て、大神の広き厚き御恵の蔭に隠ひ、浦安く、浦楽しく坐して、子孫の八十続き遠き世に此の家の守護神と鎮り坐して、時々の祭の礼をも、絶る事なく、懈る事なく、仕奉らむ事を相諾ひ給へと、恐み恐みも白す。

§ 2

Shōkon Harahinokotoba (Soul-Summoning Purification Prayer)

Kakemaku mo kashikoki Harahidoyohashira no Ōkamitachi no Ōmahe ni tsutsushimi uyamahite mōsakaku. Because Nanigashi, who dwelt in this village, departed from Utsushiyo on this day of this month, for the sake of his Mitama, we have Kotowake to Ōmotosumeōkami and Kohinomi to the Ubusuna no Ōkami, and have prepared the Mitamashiro, intending to settle and revere him in this house for far and long. Therefore, not to mention the Miketsumono offered, we pray that You shall purify and cleanse any sins or defilements that the Matsuritsukasa, as well as the Ukara, Yakara, and Morohitora may have committed, and we humbly and fearfully pray that You shall make them Sugasuga and Nashisakiha.

Mihafuri Harahinokotoba (Funeral Purification Prayer)

Kakemaku mo kashikoki Harahidoyohashira no Ōkamitachi no Uzu no Ōmahe ni, Harahinushi, Nanigashi, tsutsushimi uyamahite mōsakaku. Regarding the funeral rites for Nanigashi, who departed from Utsushiyo on the past day of this month, not to mention the Miketsumono offered, we humbly and fearfully pray that You shall purify and cleanse any sins or defilements that the officiating Matsuritsukasa, as well as the Ukara, Yakara, and Morohitora who have gathered, may have committed, and that You shall ensure that the Yukumichi is free from the Magakami and their evil deeds, and that the Mihafuri ceremony is conducted in a Sugasuga manner.

Itsujū-nichikan oyobi Toshimatsuri Okutsuki Norito (Prayer for the Fifty-Day and Annual Memorial Rites at the Tomb)

Before the Okutsuki of the late [Name] no Mikoto, [Name] no Hiko (child), we speak: as today has come around to the day when the annual memorial rite should be performed, the Ukara, Yakara, and Morohitora have gathered together, and we humbly and fearfully pray that You shall listen peacefully and calmly to the state of our offering various delicious foods and worshipping, and that You shall protect and bless our descendants, our house, and our bodies for far and long.

Yūka Fukusai Sōjōkotoba (Prayer for the Restoration Rite of the Spirit House)

Kakemaku mo kashikoki Ōmotosumeōkami no Ōmahe ni tsutsushimi uyamahite mōsakaku. Because the Mitama of Nanigashi (name of the spirit) has no divine lineage to perform the rites, this time, in accordance with the teachings of Kannagara, we have purified and reformed it, intending to settle it in the Yūka and have it serve in the divine affairs of the Yūmei that the Ōkami governs, and we intend to perform the rite on this auspicious day of the living day and the sufficient day. Therefore, we offer sake, offerings, and various things from the sea, rivers, mountains, and fields, and we humbly and fearfully pray that You shall listen peacefully and calmly to our Kohinomi, and grant that we may receive Your divine favor widely and thickly for far and long, and thus we conclude our words of praise.

Fukusai Gōshi Norito (Prayer for the Restoration and Joint Enshrinement Rite)

Before the Mitama of Nanigashi, who is enshrined and revered here in this Moroki, we speak with reverence and awe. Because you, the Mikoto, were left out of the previous rites, we intend to perform the rite of joint enshrinement on this auspicious day and hour at the spirit shrine where the ancestors of the [Name] family have long been settled, and we offer sake, offerings, and various other things before you, and we pray that you shall listen with a calm heart. Furthermore, it goes without saying that while people live in this world, after they die, their Mitama are loved and blessed by the wide and thick heart of the Ōmotosumeōkami, who allows them to enter the ranks of the gods and obtain joy and pleasure; having carefully observed and understood this, we now pray for the divine love of the Ōkami for the sake of your spirits just as we do now, and we have purified and reformed them according to the divine national style of Kannagara. We pray that you, the Mikoto, will also listen with joy and pleasure, peacefully and calmly, and from this day forward, serve the Ōkami with all your heart, advance to high divine ranks, obtain even wider and more expansive places, join your powers and unite your hearts with the ancestors of the [Name] family, and appear before your descendants for far and long through the spring and autumn, and we humbly and fearfully pray that you shall listen to our Kohinomi to have the rites performed beautifully, and that your hearts shall be settled in peace.

Seoritsuhime no Ōkami

Hayakitsuhime no Ōkami

Ibukidonushi no Ōkami

Hayasasurahime no Ōkami

All the Harahidoyohashira no Ōkamitachi,

Ascend and dwell upon the Mottsu-goza.

Sorei Taisai Norito (Prayer for the Grand Ancestral Festival)

Before the divine spirits who are settled and revered in this spirit shrine, we speak with reverence and awe. Although there are many days, we have chosen and determined today as the living day and the sufficient day to perform the Grand Spring (Autumn) Festival. Before you, we have cut and brought the five-hundred-branched sacred trees from the deep mountains, added the flowers of the season, filled the high-footed trays with offerings, piled up the mirror-shaped rice cakes on eighty earthenware vessels, filled the sake jars to the brim, and placed various delicious foods from the sea, rivers, mountains, and fields, as well as water and salt, like a mountain. We have gathered the Ukara, Yakara, and Morohitora as much as possible, and while the sacred court resounds with joy and laughter, and we clap our hands, we conclude our words of praise. Since you, the Mikoto, have finished your work in this world and have entered the ranks of the gods of the Yūmei, the Ōkami loves and caresses you with His divine heart, allowing you to advance to higher and higher ranks and obtain wider and more expansive places. While you may have had things you felt unsatisfied or regretful about while you lived in the world of the living, now all things are fulfilled and prepared according to your heart, and we pray that you shall love and cherish the fact that we consider this a peaceful and enjoyable state. We pray that you shall listen to the state of all the descendants, Ukara, and Yakara, who are working and striving to spread the achievements you began and passed down, one after another, and who do not let the name of their ancestors, whose Kototama were pure, fall into disrepute. We pray that you shall allow your pure name and high achievements to stand in this world and be passed down to the next, and that you shall guide the Mitama who will enter the Yūmei later, so that they may obtain joy and pleasure with you, the Mikoto, and protect and bless them. We pray that you shall listen to this Kohinomi with a harmonious heart, and that you shall allow the descendants to continue for eighty generations, and to perform the annual festival of this day beautifully, and with the sincere hearts of the Ukara, Yakara, and all the Ujiko, we conclude our words of praise.

§ 3

祖霊社大祭斎主祝詞

掛巻も畏き三五皇大御神の宇豆の大前に斎主某畏み畏みも白さく。

大神等の奇霊に玄妙なる天津御量りを以て、天地を〓__GT_54356__造堅め、万物を造化成し給ひし中にも、天下の大公民はしも、三五皇大御神の天津御霊を皇産霊に産霊成し給へるまにまに、諾冊二柱の御祖神の生み成し給ひ、日の大御神の養育ひ給へる物にして、元より清き明き神魂を給はりて、生れ出でたる事著ければ、惟神直き正しき道に神習ひ恪まむ人は、大御神等の広き厚き仁恩以て弥栄えに栄え往くべく守り給はむ事は、唯斯世のみに限らず、必ず後の世までも慈み育み守り給はむ事を、尊み辱みつつ八十日日は有れど、今日を生日の足日と斎ひ定めて、毎年の例のまにまに、大御祭仕へ奉るとして、奥山の五百枝真栄木に木綿取り垂て、御饌、御酒、海川山野の種々の美味物、御水堅塩に至るまで、百取の机代に置き高成して仕へ奉らくを、神慮も和かに聞食して皇大神の統治す大八洲国は堅磐に常磐に動く事なく揺ぐ事なく敷き坐す御祭政治は春の花の清く美しく、秋の果実の宇麻良に安らかに行はしめ給ひ、地の上に成出でむ天の益人、弥益々に繁殖て直き正しき大和心の真心を、一つ心に治めさせ給ひ、是の神殿に斎ひ鎮め奉る遠津御祖の神霊等、及世々の祖等諸々の御魂等は、弥広に広所を得しめ給ひ、高き位＠に進ましめ給ひ、参来集へる子孫の弥次々、男女の別なく、老も若も心正しく身健かに、命長く君臣、師弟、父子、夫婦の道を始めて、人の行ふべき業は遺る隅なく励み勤めて、生涯は神の教に違ふ事なく、世の人をも賛け導き、各も各も罷らむ後は、高天原に復命白さむまにまに、其行の分々に、永き世の幸福を授け給はむ縁由をも、説き明さしめ給ひ、太き雄々しき功績を立てしめ給ひ、顕世も幽冥も、弥遠永に守り恵み幸へ給へと各も各も玉串を持ち捧げ、乞祈奉る状を聞食せと畏み畏みも白す。

復祭鎮祭祝詞

此の日茂呂木に斎ひ鎮むる何某家遠津御祖世々の祖等の神霊の御前に、神裔何某に代りて、何某慎みて白さく、皇大神の御手風の万古に復し給へる太じき御典のまにまに今此月何日の朝日の豊阪登りを（夕日の降知を）（夜昼を）吉時と此の神祠に、汝命等の神霊を安置奉り斎ひ奉り、是の小床に鎮め奉りて真榊木差しはやし、木綿取り垂でて、礼代の幣帛と奠る豊御饌の大御饌、味し御酒の大御酒を、高杯平甕に満て並べて、海の物、野の物、山の物、種々の果実を取添へて仕へ奉らくを、御心穏に聞食し給ひて、家族親族は邪悪の道に惑ふ事なく、諸々過つ事なく、攘ひ給ひて、清き赤き直き正しき真心に、誘ひ導き給ひ、家の業をも弥奨めに奨め給ひ、子孫の八十連五十橿八桑枝の如く立栄えしめ給ひて息長く御祭美しく仕奉らしめ給へと、乞祈白す事をも、平かに安らかに聞召して夜の守り日の守りに守り、堅磐に常磐に恵み幸へ給へと畏み畏みも白す。

鎮祭日より年祭の祝詞（年月日不明の霊）

是の日茂呂木に斎ひ奉り移し奉る何某の命（等）の御前に斎主何某慎み敬ひも白さく、汝命（等）の御祭日詳かならねば、惟神の皇国風に改め奉りし日を、吉日の良辰と斎ひ定めて、其が神霊（等）を慰め仕へ奉らむとして、種々の御饌物を備へ奉りて、乞祈奉る状を相諾ひ給ひて、皇神等の任け給ひ依さし給はむ程々の御位に進み給ひて、枉津神の群に入り給はず、此の何々家が代々の栄を此上なき幸と心安く、心楽しく、時々の御祭を、御心も閑に享け聞食し給へと畏み畏みも白す。

Norito for the Monthly Festival of the Reishiya: Before the august presence of the awe-inspiring Ōmotosumeōmikami, I, the Ihahinushi Nanigashi, do humbly and reverently speak: Although there are many days, we have chosen and determined this day to be the Ikuhi and Taruhi, the auspicious day, and in accordance with the custom of every month, we perform the Tsukinamisai. Before the divine presence, we have piled up mirror-shaped rice cakes, and as for the things dwelling in the Ōunabara, we have piled up the broad-finned and narrow-finned creatures, the Okitsumoha and Hetsumoha, and as for the things growing in the Noyama, we have piled up the Amana and Karana, and various Konomi, and even down to the Mimohi and hard salt, piling them high like a mountain range. We humbly and reverently acknowledge that we have been living while looking up to and honoring the broad and thick divine virtues of the Ōkami. We humbly and reverently pray that the long life of the Emperor may be as the long life of the long-limbed, and like the Yutsu-Iwamura, may the reign be made a flourishing and prosperous reign, firm and eternal. We pray that You hear our words as we petition for the glory of the distant ancestors, the ancestors of generations, and the divine spirits of all our relatives. May the teachings of Ōmoto remain unchanged with heaven and earth, without moving or tilting with the sun and moon, and may they bloom and flourish like the rising morning sun. We pray that the believers of Ōmoto may not err in the root or branch, inside or out, and that they may not fail or deviate from the deep and high compassion of the Ōkami, but spread and lead it, and receive the divine favor. We humbly and reverently pray that You hear these words and grant them.

Furthermore, before the presence of the distant ancestors, the ancestors of generations, and the divine spirits of all our relatives and families, I speak: Today, we perform the Tsukinamisai and offer Mikemono. While receiving Your divine favor daily, we pray that You look upon and listen to us regarding any various sins or impurities we may have committed unintentionally. May You not stain the names of our parents, and may our descendants flourish and prosper more and more, standing as high as the mountain pines. We pray that You protect and bless us in the night and in the day, and holding the Isukashihoko, I humbly and reverently speak.

Ritual Text for the Monthly Festival of the New Shrine: Before the presence of the awe-inspiring Ōmotosumeōmikami, who is enshrined and sits in this divine hall, I humbly and reverently speak: In accordance with the custom of every month, on this Ikuhi and Taruhi, we perform the festival. The offerings we present, the Mikemono, are piled high on high-legged trays, and the sake is filled in jars to the brim of the jar-mouths and lined up. As for the things dwelling in the Ōunabara, the broad-finned and narrow-finned creatures, and as for the things growing in the mountains and fields, the Amana, Karana, and various Konomi, and the Okitsumoha, Hetsumoha, and even down to the Mimohi and hard salt, we have placed them on a hundred tables, piling them high like a mountain range. We pray that You hear these words of praise calmly and peacefully. May the spirits of the families enshrined in this Reiden be blessed and prosper more and more, may they advance to higher and higher ranks, and may they obtain wider and wider places. May You protect and bless the homes and bodies of the relatives and families who come to worship. May our descendants, even unto the eighty generations, flourish and prosper like the Isukashi-Yakuwa branches, and we pray that You hear our words that they may serve the festival without forgetting and without falling, and we humbly and reverently speak while holding the neck-root of the cormorant.

Furthermore, before the presence of the divine spirits of the various families who are enshrined and sit in this Reiden, I speak: Today, we perform the Tsukinamisai, and we have purified the divine presence and offer various abundant foods. We pray that You partake of them together, receive the broad and thick divine grace of the Ōkami, obtain wider and wider places, and sit in eternal peace and tranquility. May You enjoy the joys of spring and autumn to the fullest, and we pray that You serve the festival without ceasing.

霊社月次祭祝詞

掛巻も畏き三五皇大御神の宇豆の大前に斎主何某畏み畏みも白さく、八十日日は有れども、今日を生日の足日の良辰と選み定めて、毎月の例のまにまに月次の御祭典執行ひ仕へ奉らむとして御前には餅の鏡を積み重ね、大海原に住むものは鰭の広物鰭の狭物、奥津藻菜、辺津藻菜、野山に生ふる物は甘菜辛菜、及種々の果実、御水堅塩に至るまで横山の如く盛り高成して、平素に大神等の広き厚き威徳を仰ぎ奉り、尊み辱けなみつつ在り経るを畏み奉りて、言巻も畏けれど天皇命の大寿命を手長の大寿命と湯津石村の如く、堅磐に常磐に茂し御代の足御代と成し幸ひ給ひて、遠津御祖、世々の祖等、親族諸々の神霊等の御栄を乞祈白す状を聞食して、三五の御教は天地と共に変りなく、月日と共に動き傾く事なく、朝日の豊栄登りに咲み栄えしめ給ひ、三五信徒等は本末内外を過たず、大神等の深き高き慈愛を過つ事なく、違ふ事なく広め導かしめ給ひ、恩頼を蒙らしめ給へと、乞祈白す事を聞食し、相諾ひ給へと恐み恐みも白す。

辞別けて遠津御祖、世々の祖等、親族家族諸々の神霊等の御前に白さく、今日はしも月次の御祭仕へ奉り、御饌物供へ奉りて、日毎に恩頼を蒙りつつ、不意なくも過ち犯しけむ種々の罪穢在らむをば、見直し聞き直し坐して、親の名汚さず、子孫の弥次々、山松の弥高々に立栄えしめ給ひ、夜の守り日の守りに守り幸へ給へと、五十橿鉾の中取持ちて恐み恐みも白す。

新祭殿月次祭祭文

此の神殿に斎き奉り坐せ奉る、掛巻も畏き三五皇大御神の御前に恐み恐みも白さく、月毎の例のまにまに今日の生日の足日に、御祭仕へ奉るとして、奉る幣帛は御饌は高杯に盛足らはし、御酒は甕の戸高知り甕の腹満て並べて、大海原に住める物は鰭の広物鰭の狭物、山野に生る物は甘菜辛菜種々の果実、奥津藻菜、辺津藻菜、御水堅塩に至る迄、百取の机に横山の如く置足らはして称言竟へ奉らくを、平けく安らけく聞食して、此の霊殿に鎮ります家々の霊等をば、弥益に御霊幸ひ給ひて、弥高に高き位＠に進ましめ給ひ弥広に広所を得しめ給ひ、参出来む親族家族が家をも身をも守幸へ給ひ、子孫の八十続に至る迄五十橿八桑枝の如く茂久栄に立栄えしめ給ひ、忘るる事なく、堕る事なく、御祭仕へ奉らしめ給へと白す事を聞食し相諾ひ給へと鵜自物頸根突抜きて恐み恐みも白す。

辞別けて此の霊殿に鎮まり坐す諸々の家の御霊等の御前に白さく。今日はしも月次の御祭仕へ奉るとして、御前をも持斎き種々の多米津物を備へ奉らくを、相甞に聞食して、大神等の広き厚き大御恵を蒙り給ひて、弥広に広所を得しめ給ひ、永遠に安く穏に鎮まり坐して、春秋の歓び楽しみをも極め給ひて、断る事無く御祭仕へ奉らしめ給へと白す。
