Chapter 1: アリナの滝 [823]

アリナの滝〔823〕

§ 1

In the ancient Kamiyo of long ago, when the Chihaya-furu gods reigned, in the mountains of Seiun that span across the three lands of Chiyaina, Chibetsuto, and Tsuki, the Yatsuōgami resided. The heart of Kami was clear and serene, and Kamuzumihiko and Yatsugashira, along with the Kamuzukasa Azumanohiko, entrusted the Kogane-no-tama to Tamamorihiko to be kept within the golden palace, guarding the Uzutakara that creates the Kamiyo. Being targeted by the Hiko of Uraru, the situation finally became perilous, and so, starting with Tamamorihiko, Azumanohiko, whose morning sun shines bright, placed the jewel into a Mikoshi and brought it to the foot of Ōgonsanka, where Haniyasuhiko, Mitsubahiko, and Haniyasuhime were, and for a time, they sent it to their Onmoto.

While they kept it in the treasure storehouse, waiting for the time to arrive, the Kogane-no-tama suddenly began to roar, separating into the jewel and the cauldron of Riumon. Because there were many Fushigi occurrences, Haniyasuhiko inquired of the Shinchiyoku and buried it once again in the mountain of Okebuse, watching over it day and night with a heart as deep as a village. Just then, Mukade-hime, a Kamuzukasa of the Brahman, devised various schemes and finally stole the precious Uzutakara. While she had it stored in a rocky cave on Mikunigadake, the party of Koto-yoriwake and Tamayoriwake, who were Kamuzukasa of the Sango, sniffed out the location of the jewel.

Once again, the jewel was enshrined in the capital of Ayanishiki, which had been built with high Chigi at the foot of the mountain of Okebuse. While Koto-yoriwake, the Great Teacher of the Sango, was transmitting the teachings of Kami far and near, entrusting the safekeeping of the jewel to Kurohime, who served as the wife of Takayamahiko, Takahime, who called herself the living palace of the Hinodewake, and the others began to show a heart and spirit that were extremely suspicious. Koto-yoriwake went before the presence of Kami and performed the Shinto prayer, and when he humbly inquired of the precious life of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, who pass through the hands of the Lord Kunitokotachi, the Lord God spoke most solemnly: 'The hearts of both Takahime and Kurohime are not settled; they may once again swallow the jewel, restore the Uranai-kyo, and disturb this world. Koto-yoriwake, you must take out the Kogane-no-tama, the jewel of purple color, the Nyoi-hōju that is indestructible as diamond, and hide it in secret.'

Koto-yoriwake resolved his intent, and without Kurohime and Takahime knowing that he had taken the sacred treasure—which they relied upon and guarded day and night as the lifeline of their existence—in accordance with the divine words of Kami, he took it out before they knew it and stored it deep within the Nishiki-no-miya. Kurohime, unaware of this, visited the root of the pine tree night after night to stand guard over the jewel. Feeling something amiss about the place where it was hidden, Kurohime quietly pushed open the Karahitsu, and when she peered inside, she was astonished to find the Karahitsu empty, the jewel of golden light having vanished in vain.

She intended to cast away her life by leaping with a splash into a small pond at the foot of the peak of Shio, where thin ice was spread, but her servants, Teristan and Karinsu, who had been lurking nearby, heard the sound of the water. Saying, 'Oh, this is a disaster,' and risking their own lives for the sake of the jewel's thread, they did not fear the freezing cold sky, but jumped into the pond, dived to the bottom, and rescued Kurohime-tsukasa, bringing her back to the mansion at last.

While Kurohime, having had her life saved, was being showered with unreasonable demands, the matter reached the ears of Takahime-tsukasa. She confronted the five, including Kurohime, the Kamuzukasa Ryunokuniwake and Taka-yorihime, as well as Teristan and Karinsu, and gave a most severe command: 'Search everywhere for the location of the Kogane-no-tama, bring it back to the Nishiki-no-miya, and do not return to the sacred land until you have fulfilled that responsibility.' With tears swallowed, the five of them set out.

Takayamahiko and Kurohime drifted to the Ryūgū on a single island in the vast ocean to search for the location of the jewel. Following their departure, the Kamuzukasa Taka-yorihime, accompanied by the divine life of Ryunokuniwake and the three, Teristan and Karinsu, arrived safely at the port of Teru in Takasago and proceeded, pointing toward the south. Ah, Omoikane-no-kami, may the spirits be blessed.

That Kurohime secretly accompanied Takayamahiko and left the sacred land behind to search for the location of the Kogane-no-tama on the single island of Ryūgū is as already stated in the Nyoi-hōju (Tori-no-maki).

Meanwhile, Teristan and Kārinsu, resolved to search for the whereabouts of the jewel in Tsukushijima of Afurika, departed from the Seichi, but thinking deeply on the way, they reflected that for one or two persons to set out for the island of Tsukushi, which was thousands of ri in extent, was a matter even less reliable than catching clouds; thus, suddenly changing their minds, they followed in the rear of Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake, and the four of them, leaving their fate to Ten, wandered over the Kaijiyō toward Nanbei (Takasagojima) for hundreds of days to search for the whereabouts of the jewel, exploring every nook and cranny of countless large and small Shimajima; at length they anchored safely at the port of Teru, and from that time the party of four turned their course south, taking up their nest in the cave of the Mirror Pond in the upper reaches of the Alina Waterfall, in the mountain depths of Takotori Village, the old site where Saruyohiko of old had become Sayorihikonokami and founded Ananai-kyō; Takayorihime hid herself deep within the cave and concealed her form, while Tatsukuniwake built a hermitage outside the cave, claiming day and night to receive the divine oracle of the Mirror Pond, and laid plans to search for the whereabouts of the jewel without stirring from the spot.

Their method was to cause Teristan and Kārinsu to assume the guise of pilgrims and circulate through the land of Teru, the land of Chin, the land of Hiru, and the land of Haru, ... and this time, Gesshorihiko no Kami appeared in the cave of the Mirror Pond in the land of Teru, declaring: "Whoever presents a jewel of any kind whatsoever to the Mirror Pond shall be granted wealth and honor, protected in longevity, and saved even from the calamities of theft, wind, water, fire, and swords. Whoever they may be, those who possess a jewel should bring it as quickly as possible to the Mirror Pond of the Alina Waterfall, thereby opening the foundation of complete bliss and everlasting posterity, and eventually they shall be bestowed with the divine virtue of seizing the supremacy of the world. Especially, a golden-colored jewel is that which pleases the Okami most..." With such words, the two divided the directions of east, west, south, and north, and went about propagating the message.

The people of Takasagojima, who were relatively simple and rustic, took the propaganda of Te and Kā literally, and items that seemed to be jewels were brought by people racing ahead of one another, crossing distant mountains and slopes, and coming all the way to the Mirror Pond in the upper reaches of the Alina Waterfall; those who came to assemble and receive divine virtue were so numerous that their heels touched. Jewels, numbering more than hundreds, were gathered in less than a year. However, not a single one was a strange stone jewel or a round dumpling stone jewel, and not even one golden jewel sought after by Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake was gathered.

At that time, a man named Āru from the land of Hiru took out a golden-colored jewel that had been treasured as a divine treasure from generation to generation by his ancestors, respectfully placed it in a willow box, constructed a beautiful portable shrine, caused the villagers to carry it while marching with tens of banners flying, blowing conch shell trumpets, beating gongs, and playing transverse and vertical flutes, meandering through the long journey, intending to present it to Gesshorihiko no Kami of the Alina Waterfall; thus continuing his long journey day and night, he finally arrived safely at Takotori Village, stopped there for a while, performed seven days and seven nights of ritual purification, donned ritual garments anew, and was to present it to the Mirror Pond.

Teristan and Kārinsu had finished propagating throughout the noteworthy spots of Takasagojima once over, finally returned to the Alina Waterfall, and together with Tatsukuniwake and Takayorihime, they waited while counting on their fingers for the golden jewels to gather.

Moreover, Takayorihime hid her body deep within the recesses of the cave, placed a bamboo tube to her mouth, and became a living god conveying divine messages with a hollow, deadpan voice, while Tatsukuniwake performed the duties of a shinjakusha inquiring into divine oracles, skillfully deceiving the countrymen and devoting all her strength to the collection of jewels.

Since not a single person seemed likely to come since the morning today, Takayorihime also let down her guard and gathered with Tatsukuniwake, Teristan, and Kārinsu in the hermitage beside the Mirror Pond, holding an informal chat meeting.

Takayorihime: 'Ever since I grew older and left the Seichi, coming all the way to such a distant land of Teru, hiding my body inside a cramped cave, being bitten by insects, having my shins pinched by crabs, and enduring all sorts of hardships, I have had to forcefully pucker my toothless mouth, blow through such a heavy bamboo tube, and perform acts like a common quack just to collect jewels; when I think that I must do all this, even though it is for the sake of the divine realm and the world, I have grown quite miserable. Although jewels have piled up like mountains, not a single golden jewel has come forth; there are only worthless stones that look like dumpling stones with hair growing on them, and not even a single desired precious jewel has gathered... Ah, it seems that no golden jewels have come to this island after all. Since I cannot endure such a cramped pretense forever, someone else should take my place, and I want to go outside and work. If I tried propagating once more, perhaps they would gather. Since human beings have thick skins of greed, that precious treasure where the golden ascending dragon and descending dragon appear is not something easily let go of even if one possesses it. To deal with that, we must change our method of propaganda and make them bring it out. Among them, quite a few rare jewels have been gathered, but compared to the treasures of the fulfillment of divine government, they are like mud and clouds in difference. Ah, there is nothing more wretched than serving as a palace attendant!'

竜国別『お母アさま、お歎きは御尤もなれど、これ丈玉の多い高砂島、初まりは団子石の様な玉計り集まつて居つたが、段々と数は減つて来た代りに、一日々々立派な玉が此通り集まつて来ることを思へば、モウ暫く此処で御辛抱下さいませ。私が中へ這入つて、あなたは外で審神者の役をして貰ふのは易いことですが、何程竹筒を通して物を言つても、竜国別の声は熱心な信者が能く聞分けるであらうし、又今迄姿を見せた事のない、年寄りのお前さまが審神者となり、此竜国別の姿が見えなくなつたら、それこそ疑の種を播き、九仭の功を一簣に虧く様な事が出来ても詰りませぬから、モウ暫くの所、何程御窮屈でも御辛抱下さいませ。大蛇や猛獣の猛び狂ふ此山国や、大沙漠を渡る事を思へば、何程窮屈でも、穴の中で涼しい目をして、辛抱して下さる方が何程能いか分りませぬ』

鷹依『アヽそんなら、お前の言ふ通り、モウ暫く辛抱致して見ようかなア』

竜国『どうぞ御苦労ですが、暫く、さうして居つて下さいませ。キツト前途有望だと信じますから……。オイ、テーリスタン、お前も永々と御苦労だつたが、随分宣伝に骨が折れただらうなア。……カーリンス、お前も中々の骨折だつた。お前の往つた方も、テーの行つた方も、余程よく宣伝が行き渡つたと見えて随分、珍の国や、ヒルの国、カルの国あたりから、種々の玉を供へに来たよ。まだ一人も出て来ぬのは、ハルの国だ。ヒヨツとしたらハルの国にあるかも知れない。併しあの国はブラジル山と云ふ大きな山があり、アマゾン河と云ふ広大な流れがあつたり、大沙漠もあるから、何程熱心な者だとて、一寸此処までワザワザ玉を納めに来るものはなからう、モウ一寸辛抱しても来なかつたら、ハルの国へ宿替へして、モウ一芝居打たうぢやないいか』

テー『さうですな、随分山の如く玉が集つて来ましたが、世の中には欲呆けや、迷信家が沢山あると見えますわい。アハヽヽヽ』

カー『身魂相応の玉を持つて来ると見えて、随分ヤクザ玉ばかり集つたものだ。高姫玉や黒姫玉、高山玉に杓子のお玉、狸の睾丸、瓢六玉、団子玉などは沢山集まつて来たが、肝腎の黄金の玉はまだ根つからお出で遊ばさぬ。何程毎日、あゝ惟神御玉幸はひましませ……とか、玉ちはひませ……とか云つて拝んでも、根つから神様は肝腎の御性念玉を集めては下さらず、わしも肝玉がひしげる様な恐い目にあうたり、睾丸が縮み上がる様な苦労をして、随分頭の脳味噌を絞つて見たが、タマで目的の黄金玉は集まり来らず、玉々黄色い色がして居ると思へば、土玉で、少しひねくつてをると砕けて了ふ様なフヌケ玉許り、これ丈苦労艱難しても玉の悪い奴許りより集つて来ぬかと思へば、わしも癇癪玉が破裂しさうだ。本当に遠い山坂や谷川を駆けめぐり、こんな張合のない事では、たまらぬぢやありませぬか。なア竜国別さま』

竜国『さう気投げをせずに、モウちつと辛抱して呉れ。チツとは結構なことが出て来るよ。黄金の玉を手に入れるが最後、俺達は聖地へ帰り、高姫の頑固者に頭を下げさせ、アツと云はして、天晴三五教の柱石となり、巾を利かして大神業に参加するのを楽みに、モウ暫く忍耐して、モウ一働き働いて呉れ』

§ 4

Kā: 'Patience is the mother of happiness, Takayorihime is the mother of Tatsukuniwake, and the Mizu no Mitama is the mother of the world. ... Separated from the mother, an infant has crossed far mountain paths through great hardships to search and come all the way here, yet you say you cannot hear us and brush us off—our tears preceding our voice of resentment, chinchirichin ya!'

Tē: 'Korya korya, is this a time for such a lighthearted place? You must firmly and properly turn your wisdom about, and do yet one more activity; this is the crucial and vital climax of our resolve.'

Just as this was being spoken, there suddenly echoed the sounds of vertical flutes, transverse flutes, conch shell trumpets, and the striking of keiban gongs heard incessantly, while flags bearing the words 'Offering of the Golden Orb' appeared and disappeared among the trees, fluttering as they were blown by the valley wind while ascending.

Takayorihime, still grasping the bamboo tube in her right hand, hurriedly concealed her figure within the cave. Tatsukuniwake straightened his dignified bearing and sat formally in seiza before the Mirror Pond, clapping his hands together and reciting the Amatsu Norito. Tē and Kā both prostrated themselves with proper decorum behind Tatsukuniwake and were worshiping the Mirror Pond with all their heart.

カー『忍耐は幸福の母、鷹依姫は竜国別の母、瑞の御霊は世界の母だ。……母に別れて幼子が、遠き山路を打渉り、艱難して此処まで尋ね来たものを、聞えませぬと取り付いて、涙先立つ恨み声、チンチリチンぢや』

テー『コリヤコリヤ、そんな気楽な事所かい。チツと確りと智慧をめぐらし、モウ一活動やらねばならぬ、肝腎要の性念場だぞ』

斯く云ふ折しも、俄に聞ゆる縦笛、横笛、法螺貝、磬盤を叩く音頻りに聞え、『黄金の玉献上』と云ふ旗幾十となく木の間に見えつ隠れつ、翩翻として谷風に吹かれ乍ら登つて来る。

鷹依姫は竹筒を右手に握つたまま、慌だしく岩窟内に姿を隠した。竜国別は威儀を正して鏡の池の前に端坐し、両手を合せて天津祝詞を奏上してゐる。テー、カー両人は行儀よく竜国別の後に平伏して一生懸命に鏡の池を拝み居る。
