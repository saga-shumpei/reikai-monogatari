At Takahime's sincere confession, one of the passengers saw his previous feeling of anger vanish who knows where, and this time he had completely become a sympathizer toward Takahime. Person C turned toward Takahime and spoke,

C: 'I am a person called Job from Karin Island (currently Falkland). Around this time last year, boarding this Karinmaru, on the way going back and forth to the port of Zem, I witnessed the drowning of the parent and retainer like I talked about before, and from then on I somehow felt pity, and when I looked into it well, after hearing that you were a pitiful person who was driven out by Takahime-sama of Onokorojima and came all the way up to here, I thought to myself that the moment I found Takahime I would not leave her neck unsevered, and as if she were even the enemy of my parents, putting strength into my arms I could not endure the feeling of anger; but now having met Takahime-sama in person, hearing and seeing are vastly different, and even this Job has been filled with admiration for your innocent and pure mind. As for stories, if one does not hear both sides and listens only to one side, they cannot be understood. Please, Takahime-sama, by a wondrous bond we have met inside this ship. Taking this opportunity, will you not make me your disciple? I have both parents, and I also have an older brother and a younger brother, but happily being a dependent living in my household, no matter where I go, if it is for the sake of the Lord God I will say nothing against my parents and siblings. Please let me accompany you anywhere at all. Also, if you are troubled for travel funds, travel funds for two or three years are precisely what I am carrying here with me, so I earnestly ask you to let me accompany you.'

Takahime: 'That is truly a splendid intention, but since you still have attachment within yourself, you surely will not be able to endure it. Also, if there is a bond, let us receive help at that time. However, please become a believer of the Way of Izumaru.'

Job: 'I am naturally a believer of Izumaru-kyō, you see. I do not tread formal paths such as separate churches, doctrines, or scriptures, but true religion is by no means born from churches or rituals and the like. Please, to propagate such a splendid Way to the world, if one is born as the same human being and spends a lifetime, I desire to save the peoples of the world, be appreciated, and pass this world. Please, though it may not please you, make me your disciple.'

Takahime: 'Just looking at your appearance, you seem to be a person who has pursued quite a modern learning. Since you appear to be quite a clever person, you surely will not be pleased by the words of an uneducated and illiterate person of the old days like me...'

Job: 'The true Way is not understood by learning or wisdom. It was for your sincerity of the unity of word, mind, and deed that I was truly impressed. The world nowadays is full of people whose mouths, minds, and deeds are completely opposed; wishing to find a person of sincerity and devote myself for the sake of the world, sleeping and waking I have been offering prayers to the Lord God. Today of all days, having actually witnessed you whom I had firmly believed to be a great villain, possessing the sincerity of the unity of word, mind, and deed, an attachment-less heart, and a strong faith, I cannot contain my delight. If I cannot become your disciple, please take me along even if only as a baggage bearer. Since I know the coast from this Land of Utsu to the Land of Paru (present-day Brazil) in detail, I earnestly beg you to let me accompany you as a guide along the way.'

Takahime: 'By my single opinion alone I cannot decide. Listening to the opinions of Tsunehiko and Haruhiko who are accompanying me here, upon that I shall give you an answer. ...Now Tsunehiko, Haruhiko, just as you have also heard now, what do you think of what this person is saying? Please speak your opinions without reservation right here and now.'_para_7

Takayorihime's party meeting with disaster, if one inquires deeply into the origin thereof, is the result of Takahime's selfishness and stubborn pride, hearing which our hearts ached, and while we wondered what would become of things, Takahime, whose mitama is pure, suddenly transforming herself, honestly confesses her past errors and makes a resolute sacrifice of herself, at whose manliness Yobu-sama's usual anger melts like ice, and with a completely changed, good-humored face, deeply moved by the action of unspoken deeds, she begs to become Takahime's disciple, ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, the mitama of Kami blessing her, Takahime-sama's heart today is clear and bright like the morning sun, shining and radiant, how manly it is! Thus finishing singing, he sat down. Starting with Takahime, Yobu and other people in the senchiu were utterly lost in admiration at the fair and selfless attitude with which Tsunehiko, having Takahime present before him right then and there, had spoken frankly of the course of events.
