Chapter 8: High 姫慴伏 [830]

高姫慴伏〔830〕

§ 1

Takahime and her party finally arrived at the waterfall of Arina.

Four or five persons resembling believers were kneeling stark naked before the waterfall basin, praying with all their might about something.

However, it was impossible to catch the words amidst the roaring sound of the waterfall.

Takahime and her party purified their bodies, and from there climbed up the right side of the waterfall, and it was precisely at dawn when they finally reached the Mirror Pond.

Flocks of crows were flying back and forth in front, behind, to the left, and to the right, crying cleanly, 'Kaa, kaa.'

By the way, crows crying in the morning with the clearest voice, uttering 'Kaa, kaa,' are called magpies, and they are slightly smaller than ordinary crows.

Those that cry in the evening are called true crows.

Compared to magpies, evening crows are considerably larger in physique, possess something vulgar no matter where one looks, and their cry is turbid, sounding like 'Gaa, gaa.'

Moreover, the magpie bridge mentioned in the Hyakunin Isshu poem—'When I see the whiteness of the frost laid upon the bridge crossed by magpies, the night has indeed grown old'—refers to the stairs of the Daikiyokuden.

The kototama of 'ka' has the meaning of shining and illuminating, and 'sasagi' has the significance of happiness.

It means the Gyōkō Bridge upon which the Imperial Heavenly Succession Sovereigns ascend and descend.

Also, to call an imperial tomb a misasagi has the meaning that water is auspicious, and the physical body from which the divine spirit has departed is precisely water.

Therefore, the place where the sovereign passes away and makes an imperial progress is called an imperial tomb.

The 'ka' of the magpie is the significance of fire, and the 'mi' of the imperial tomb is the significance of water.

The crow that just cried was indeed the voice of a magpie.

Near the Mirror Pond, the divine spirit of Sayorihiko no Mikoto had established a new shrine and was enshrined there.

Then, on the front left side of the cavern, facing the Mirror Pond, the hermitage where Ryūkokuwake and others had lived remained there.

There, the two persons Kuni and Tama concurrently took charge of guarding the Mirror Pond and engaged in service at the Sayorihiko Shrine.

Various jade stones, agates, and other jewels presented from various directions were piled up like mountains.

A little higher up, the familiar suspended-bridge palace was newly built, the fragrance of wood wafting and drifting around.

Facing the Mirror Pond and clapping her hands, Takahime already set her eyes upon the various jewels piled high, and gazing intently with hawk-like eyes to see if wish-granting jewels or mani-jewels were among them.

The Mirror Pond suddenly broke its long silence and began to rumble,

'Buku-buku-buku, un-un-un.'

The divine officials Kuni and Tama changed their countenances and ran off to the suspended-bridge palace to make a report before the divine official Kunitamayoriwake.

§ 7

Takahime: 'There is a proper law of Shinkai that says thou must not be angry, you know. If a god is one who gets angry here, that is truly an Akugami.'

Tsunehiko: 'If that is so, are you really an Akugami?'

Takahime lifted her eyes and puffed out her face,

Takahime: 'Hey, Tsunehiko, what a thing to say! Where am I an Akugami? I will not put up with this anymore!'

And she grabbed him firmly by the lapel.

Tsunehiko: 'That is precisely why I say you are an Akugami. Do you not get angry right away?'

Takahime: 'Where did I get angry? Did I not merely move my body a bit suddenly and raise my voice? Even from your eyes, does this look like I got angry?'

Haruhiko: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Haruhiko, what is so funny? Pray keep yourself in hand a little.'

Haruhiko: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha, ihi-hi-hi, ufu-hu-hu, ehe-he-he, oho-ho-ho, you got angry, you got angry! How interesting, how interesting! What a terrifying bout of goritsupuku.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Haruhiko, will you not pull yourself together? The yomaigoto of the soft-shelled turtle god at the bottom of the pond has almost infected you.'

Haruhiko: 'Ta-ta-ta-ta-ta, Takahime-sama's yomaigoto has infected us just a ti-ti-tiny bit, ufu-hu-hu.'

Just as this was happening, Kuni and Tama, both of them, guarding Kunitamayoriwake the Kamuzukasa, appeared upon this place.

Kunitamayoriwake: 'I do not know from where you people come, but if you are going to worship at this Mirror Pond, unless you first report to the Kakehashigoten, the Kamisama at the bottom of the pond will be terribly angered, and since that would be troublesome, please bear it in mind a little.'

Takahime: 'You just said you are a Kamuzukasa of some place called the Kakehashigoten, but is the god at the bottom of this pond really that terrifying? This fellow, you see, struts about looking erasau and calling himself Tsukiteruhikonokami, but he is just an aeons-old soft-shelled turtle ghost at the bottom of the pond. You should stop being superstitious to a reasonable degree. From here on, I shall go to the Kakehashigoten and preach the principles of heaven and earth to you from their ancient fundamentals. Behold this! Shall I now hurl a single stone here into the bottom of the pond? Surely the soft-shelled turtle will float right up.'

Kunitamayoriwake: 'How can you speak such violent nonsense, you who are a missionary? Is that not a bit rude?'

Takahime: 'Well, proof is better than argument. Behold.'

So saying, she gripped in her right hand a jewel-shaped stone piled high, and threw it with a splash. Instantly, from the bottom of the pond came a fierce growling sound, the earth began to shake as if in a great earthquake, Takahime's face turned pale blue, and trembling violently, as if resorting to divine help in a hopeless hour, she pressed her hands together with all her might and collapsed flat onto the spot. Starting with Kunitamayoriwake, the attendants Kuni and Tama, along with Tsunehiko and Haruhiko, remained utterly unconcerned, and with the feeling of listening to music, joyfully pressed their hands together in gratitude. Takahime trembled more and more, clenched her teeth, oozed bright red blood from between her teeth, and shook with terror.
