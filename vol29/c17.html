Chapter 17: 途上の邂逅 [839]

途上の邂逅〔839〕

§ 1

Takahime's party safely arrived at the port of Zem, the waves of the sea being calm and the divine virtue manifest. As this place was a landing area, the houses were quite dense. Men and women of all ages, young and old, seeing the missionary figures of Takahime's party, watched them go by with a look of novelty while whispering to one another.

First Man: 'It was just about this time last year. One old woman missionary and three male missionaries, looking utterly exhausted and forlorn, chanted some sort of missionary song and passed through here, but aren't they coming around again this year? However, isn't that old woman looking a little younger? It seems that if you chant missionary songs with all your might, even elderly people will naturally become young.'

Second Man: 'Don't talk nonsense. The missionary who passed by last year was an old woman named Takayorihime of Ananai-kyō. One was her son, and the other two were said to be that old woman's servants. Having fallen into the sea from the Karin-maru, the whereabouts of all four, starting with the old woman, became unknown, and a massive search was conducted, but before anyone knew it, all four calmly floated up to the port of Zem riding on the back of a huge turtle, making everyone drop their jaws in astonishment; compared to that old woman-sama, she falls far short. She is probably nothing more than that old woman's disciple. Still, isn't it a strange thing? It was precisely on this day last year. It is truly a mystery that one old woman and three male missionaries should pass along this street without a single bit of difference in either day or hour.'

First Man: 'Is that what they call the providence of God? According to what a man named Te or Ka, who was accompanying the old woman, said, poor thing, after growing old she was driven out of Jitōtō and came out to a place like this, undergoing untold hardships and suffering. And that person who drove her out, called Takahime—what a hateful old woman she sounds like. Even for someone else's affair, it makes me angry. Could she possibly be that very Takahime?'

Second Man: 'That might well be. I had heard that Takahime was about ten years younger than Takayorihime, so it could just possibly be her-sama, you know.'

Thus they were chattering in loud voices, as if wanting to be overheard. Hearing this, Takahime stepped briskly up to the side of the two men,

Takahime: 'Pardon me, but if I hear your conversation just now, you mentioned that an old woman-sama called Takayorihime of Ananai-kyō passed through here; is that true?'

Second Man: 'True indeed; there is not a single person on the main island who would utter such nonsense like Takahime of Ananai-kyō, or tell lies when things become inconvenient.'

Takahime: 'Oh, is that so? Thank you very much. And where did that party of Takayorihime-sama go? If you know, would you please inform me?'

Second Man: 'If rumor is to be believed, it is said that after performing ascetic practices for a while at the waterfall of Mount Tenshō, she came out to the port of Chin by crossing the mountain, and, tracing the Amazon River upstream, went to a place called Tamanomori, which is located in the mountains of Molband.'

Takahime: 'Oh, is that so? I am sorry to have disturbed you. ... Now everyone, let us go.'

First Man: 'Hey, hey, old woman-sama, wait a moment. Aren't you Takahime, the mean-spirited old woman who drove away Takayorihime?'

Second Man: 'Hey, isn't there no need to ask about such a thing? Doesn't the face plainly bear the stamp of Takahime of Ananai-kyō, who has such a wicked disposition? You really are a man of very dull perception.'

First Man: 'Just as you say. Even a brainless fellow like me understands at a single glance that you are Takahime. ... Listen here, Takahime, wait a moment! There are matters I have to declare to you.'

Takahime: 'As you have perceived, I am undoubtedly Takahime. And furthermore, you seem exceedingly well-informed about the affairs of Takayorihime-sama; what kind of relationship do you actually have with her?'

§ 2

Kō: 'What do you mean, whether she exists or not? Takayorihime-sama is the parent of our very lives. If that honored person had not come to the waterfall of Tenshiyōzan last year, the two of us, rather than seeing the light of this world at this time of day, would be reduced to white bones in some place. To maltreat such a benefactor of our lives, to make unreasonable demands, and to banish her to a place like this—Takahime is none other than the mortal enemy of the parent of our lives. Now, Takahime, since matters have come to this, it is the exhaustion of heavenly fortune. Let us set you off on your journey to Hades. Prepare yourself.'

Saying this, he took out a glittering object from his bosom, and attempted to lunge at her from both sides. Takahime nimbly dodged her body. Yobu spread his great hands wide in the very middle of the two men,

Yobu: 'Now, wait, wait. There is a deep reason for this. I too, until now, as soon as I found this Takahime, had been stalking her with the intent that I could not let her go without pulling out her raw head, but after hearing the circumstances, now I too have become a disciple of Takahime-sama. Now, wait. Aren't you Māru and Bōru of Kārin Island?'

Māru: 'Speaking of which, are you Yobu-sama? It has been a long time.'

Yobu: 'Well now, you two, have you managed to repent?'

The two of them, Māru and Bōru,

'Yes.'

Changing their attitude abruptly,

Both: 'Last year we truly did a terrible thing. Meeting you in a place like this is probably because divine retribution has come around.'

Yobu: 'Well, as for things that happened in the past, it goes without saying. Since there are two persons present, beginning with Takahime-sama, I shall say nothing at all. In exchange, from now on I will explain and let you hear of Takahime-sama's karmic affinity, so listen well. I shall completely cross out your sins up until now...'

Māru: 'Yes, thank you very much.'

Bōru: 'We have repented, and we would like you to let us hear the story.'

Yobu: 'Earlier you said that Takayorihime-sama is the parent of your life, but what sort of reason is that again? Let me hear the circumstances of it.'

Māru: 'The absolute truth of the matter is that on Kārin Island we were called ruffians and reviled as villains, with not a single person willing to be our companion, and because it was not very interesting, we slipped into your house in the middle of the night, looted three hundred ryo in gold, and just as we were attempting to make a successful escape, you happened to encounter us at the gateway as you were returning from outside... "Hey, aren't you the two men, Māru and Bōru?"—the terror we felt at the moment those words were spoken! Believing that we would surely be subjected to the punishment of strangulation on the morrow in accordance with the island's rules, we stole a small boat, drifted in the darkness, hurried day after day, and somehow or other were blown by the wind as far as the port of Zemu... "Ah, with this we are at ease for a moment." However, somehow it felt as though pursuers were coming after us from behind, and being too terrified to bear it, we thought, "Let us beat ourselves under the waterfall of Tenshiyōzan, perform a single period of ascetic practice, and have the gods forgive our sins." From then on, every single night, we soaked in the waterfall and performed our austerities. Then, before we knew it, a moluband came fluttering down, holding its long tail straight up, stark naked, and came right before the two of us who were being beaten by the waterfall, shaking its buttocks brisly and taking up a posture to strike us with the sword at the tip of its tail. Thinking that this was unbearable, we earnestly invoked the gods of Heaven and Earth, but far from easily retreating, it wagged its tail all the more, and just as the bodies of the two of us were about to be cut in two by the sword of its tail, a propaganda song suddenly began to be heard. Along with hearing that voice, the moluband wretch gradually drew in its tail and shortened it. The voice of the propaganda song grew higher and higher. The monster of a moluband drooped its tail, dropped its head, and fluttering down, hid its form toward the nearby forest. The ones who arrived there were a party of propagation emissaries, beginning with Takayorihime-sama of Hinode no Izumo, followed by Ryūkokubake, Tē, and Kā. If Takayorihime-sama had not come down there at that time, we would no longer be persons of this world. Therefore, we revered Takayorihime-sama as the parent of our lives, immediately entered the faith, and became her disciples. We had the relationship between Takahime and Takayorihime-sama explained to us completely by the two men named Tē and Kā, and feeling so exasperated that we could no longer bear it, we realized through the divine guidance that Takahime would surely land at the port of Zemu today. Today, when my life was saved, is our anniversary; slaying the enemy of the parent of our lives is today—so we had prepared our edged tools, sharpened them, and were waiting. Then, just as the divine guidance had it, the Takahime party appeared here, so no matter what, in order to repay the profound grace of the parent of our lives, we must take Takahime's life and slay the mortal enemy... Please, Yobu-sama, allow me to fulfill my purpose.'

§ 3

Yobu: 'It was probably Takayorihime who saved your life, wasn't it? But who do you think it was that made that Takayorihime come out to Takasagojima? Isn't it Takahime-sama who is sitting right here? If so, except for the difference between direct and indirect, isn't it the same as Takahime-sama saving your life?'

Māru: 'When you put it that way, that's true.'

Bōru: 'Indeed, just as Yobu-sama says, if Takahime-sama hadn't said unreasonable things and driven out Takayorihime-sama, there wouldn't have been any worry of her coming to a place like this, and moreover, we wouldn't have been in a position to be saved either. When you think about it that way, it's not really possible to think too badly of Takahime-sama, is it?'

Yobu: 'Since everything comes about from the heart of Kamisama, for humans to grab onto just one part with their own thinking and talk about good and bad, or enemy and ally, is the very first mistake. Simply put, humans have no choice other than to leave everything to the will of Kamisama. You had better apologize for that sin of trying to commit rudeness toward Takahime-sama.'

Māru: 'Well, well, Takahime-sama, we truly made a terrible misunderstanding; please do not think of us as hateful wretches, but with Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi, look upon us anew and listen to us anew, and please forgive us. Now, through the advice of Yobu-sama, we have completely reformed. You are truly our life's benefactor, surpassing even Takayorihime-sama.'

Bōru: 'Takahime-sama, please grant us your forgiveness. ...Yobu-sama, please be so kind as to intercede well on our behalf.'

Takahime: 'Ah, thank you everyone. To think that you would care so much for Takayorihime-sama. More than anything, I am gladdened by those beautiful hearts of yours. As you heard, I too was a woman of considerable selfishness and stubbornness. And toward Takayorihime-sama and others, I acted in truly cruel ways, but today I have already completely reformed, and with a single sincere heart I am doing the work of Kamisama, so please treat me well from now on, and let us help one another.'

Māru: 'Thank you very much. With this, I too am finally relieved.'

Bōru: 'Takahime-sama, Yobu-sama, everyone, please do not abandon a foolish person like me, and I beg for your guidance from now on.'

Tsunehiko and Haruhiko both feel deeply moved as if for the first time by this strange gathering of fate, and with blank expressions on their faces, they watch this scene without blinking an eye.
