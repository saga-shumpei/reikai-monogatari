§ 4

Takahime-sama's unwavering confession evoked great wonder, and even Job-sama was astonished, becoming a disciple of Takahime-sama and entering the faith—how auspicious this is! Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara! The grace of Kami is before our very eyes; there is no greater joy than this. The heartfelt repentance of Takahime-sama and the path ahead for Job-sama, who has entered the faith, may they traverse it ever more soundly, and may they illuminate the world with the divine virtue of Kami; counting upon our fingers, we await and yearn for that day. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara! Mitama, may you be blessed and prosperous!"

At the end, Takahime sang a song of joy regarding her repentance and entrance into the faith.

"Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara! By the grace of the precious Kami, the darkness of Tokoyo has cleared away entirely, and the moon of Shinniyo shines in the sky of the murakimo heart. My mitama, which had been instantly snared by the crafty deity of Kinmaukiubi, has at last awakened from its dream; the shadow of the magatsu-gami is gone, and the light of the Izunome spirit bestowed by Kami shines brilliantly, causing the anguish of the heart to vanish away. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even should the Great Earth sink into the depths, once repentance has been made above all else, as long as this mitama remains in this world, I swear to Heaven and Earth that I shall never change! If this repentance of Takahime had been delayed by even a single day,

In this very ship, my life would have been taken by Job-sama. At the tip of the pen of Hensei Danshi, it is written that above all things, repentance is the most splendid thing of all. Once you repent, from that very day there are neither enemies nor hardships; it is written over and over again to repent quickly. Ah, repentance! Repentance! By the words of Konohanahime-sama, the true flavor of repentance, which I understood for the first time, is just like this. Job-sama of Karin Island, who had targeted me as an enemy, has completely transformed, looking up to Takahime as a master and entering the faith; if we seek the cause by which this place has safely settled down, it is none other than my own repentance.

Repentance and entrance into the faith came together, raining down only good things. There is no joy greater than this! Even so, the party of four who fell into the ocean—when I think of them, how pitiful they are! At the very least, let us comfort their spirits, enshrining them morning and evening, and serving them with the utmost care. Takayorihime and everyone, look upon me with a generous heart and forgive my sins! Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara! Revering before the presence of Kami, for today's joy to last eternally, I offer my gratitude and pray for the blessed afterlife of the director of teachings, Takayorihime, and the three others. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara! Mitama, may you be blessed and prosperous!"

Having finished singing thus, she smiled gently and took her seat. The ship sped across the sea for three days and three nights, and at last arrived safely at the port of Zemu. The party of four, consisting of Takahime and her companions, landed here, and with high spirits, advancing deep into the mountains while singing propaganda songs to perform purification at the great waterfall of Mount Tenshō, which was separated from the town of Zemu by some two or three ri. Ah, Kamunagara Mutama Sachiwahimaseyo.
