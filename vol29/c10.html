§ 4

Takahime: 'To-to-to-do not say such absurd things! To say it is the land of Kuniharutachinomikoto—there is a limit to even such conceit. Conceit is the source of a terrible injury, you know.'

Re-re-re-without knowing either etiquette or manners,

So-so-so-so impetuously coming into another's mansion, without even greeting properly,

Tsu-tsu-tsu-while eating up admonitions for Tsuteruhiko no Mikoto,

Na-na-na-though you know nothing at all, you blow tall tales about knowing every single thing in the three thousand worlds or not knowing it,

Mu-mu-mu-putting on a serious face, even when people listen,

U-u-u-only lining up selfish things that make one groan with fed-up fatigue,

I-i-i-spouting only ill-natured things without cease,

Ku-ku-ku-no matter how much you are told to reveal the whereabouts of the jewel to Kuni-sama, it's no use! Such a foolish thing,

Ma-ma-ma-with a state of mind of sheer sincerity, even jewels that are hard to get can be obtained, but if you step off the path of true sincerity, situations will arise where you cannot grasp even a jewel right before your eyes,

Ke-ke-ke-even by the width of a hair's breadth, if you displease this Kuni-sama's honorable mood, even though you might wish to present a splendid jewel, you will pull it back midway.

Fu-fu-fu-as long as you puff out your cheeks and swagger about, Takahime-sama, it's no use either.

Ko-ko-ko-if you are a person who cannot understand even after having such reason explained and beaten into you, please just trot right back home.

E-e-e-as the station of branch deities or minor deities, trying to carry away the sacred treasures enshrined in this palace is what you call not knowing your own place.

Te-te-te-rather than plotting things that are utterly out of the question,

A-a-a-make a clean break of it,

Sa-sa-sa-please be off in a hurry.

Ki-ki-ki-my mood has turned foul. Aha-ha-ha-ha, hi-hi-hi-hi, ufu-hu-hu-hu, ehe-he-he-he, oho-ho-ho-ho!'
