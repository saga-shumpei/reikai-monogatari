Chapter 21: 仙 Holy 郷 [1677]

仙聖郷〔1677〕

§ 1

Sumanā: 'Flowers are crimson and leaves are green, circling round the ridges of the brocade mountains, the paradise of Chiugokuichi, Senseikiyō is eternally a land tasting the joys of Tengoku and Jiyōdo; yet the bloodlines of ugly Magami like Adarunohiko and Ebahime have stealthily watched from unknown times, and men's hearts day by day and month by month grow wild and desolate—how sad indeed! The subordinates, ruffians of the thieves who have built their nests in the mountain stronghold of Torakumayama, made a sudden raid, most ruthlessly slaying my beloved father and mother, robbing me of the lives of my dear husband and brothers and their fortune, and returning with it; in my sorrow where I am left utterly alone behind,

while I was mourning and praying for the repose of the souls of my parent, husband, and brothers, there appeared that crooked one of Tēra, whose business is to unearth people's evil deeds and make false accusations, speaking glibly morning and evening—such ear-polluting, delirious ravings. Unable to bear listening to them, Sumanā grew weary of this floating world, and determined in her heart to ascend to Tengoku, yearning after the footsteps of my departed parents and my husband, composing an estate of leaving, departing from my home two days ago, and applying myself to sutra-chanting at Hakukotsudau; just as I was fixing my resolve for ascension here at last,

Mikahiko, the missionary of Saniwakyō, peerless in divine power, appeared and mercifully and minutely expounded to Sumanā concerning the unreasonableness of her wandering heart, and preached the teachings of the Kami; thus my breast suddenly cleared up, the sun and moon of Shinnyo shone bright, and oh, the joy of being reborn as a complete child of the Kami! Following Mikahiko-tsukasa, when I came to my home and looked around, there was a most noisy clamor of people's voices; wondering what the state of affairs might be, entering from the back door into a room and spying upon them, the wicked and lawless Tēra-sama, going mad with the intent to occupy my family's property, was contending with the people of the youth corps such as Tākku and Intā—how deeply lamentable! If I, now safely preserved in life, were to return,

pray make Tēra-sama's anxiety entirely unnecessary. I offer my gratitude for the goodwill with which the arresting official Kinguresu and a hundred other people have traveled far to come. When the Kami appear in the manifest world to set apart good and evil, He is Kamunaobi who created this world, and Ōnaobi whose heart is broad; simply, in human life for any matter, look upon it anew and listen to it anew with Naobi, and proclaim and rectify the errors of the world. Ever since I heard the teachings of the Kami, I will never, never hate Tēra's scheme. Promptly depart from this place and show the purity of your person. The youth president and other people of pure spirit,

pray linger a little while afterwards. To express my personal feelings, I shall serve a cup of consolation and a divine offering for which you have bestowed your minds for my sake; pray wait a while. Since I have returned together with Mikahiko-tsukasa, no matter how great the number of arresting officers may be, and however much they press upon my home, or however much Tēra may make a fuss, they shall not be counted as things at all. Ah, how grateful, how grateful! The true Kami dwells in heaven and earth, protecting my home and richly blessing my very body. Ah, Omiakami-akami, may your august spirits prosper in bliss! Whether the morning sun shines or is clouded, whether the moon fills or wanes,

even though the Great Earth sinks, truth alone saves the world. Conduct like that of Tēra, which strays from the path of truth, how could the Kami ever forgive? Reflect upon it, Tēra-sama! Sumanā-hime offers her loyal warning with all her heart. Ah, Omiakami-akami, grant us the shelter of your august spirits!'

So chanting, following behind Mikahiko, she appeared quietly from the inner chamber. Tēra, who had turned timid, cried out, 'Oya, this is unbearable!' tumbled in a panic from the threshold into the garden, struck his shins, went down on all fours, and fled into the darkness of the back door. Kinguresu and the others were none of them true arresting officers; Tēra had purposely disguised the thieves of Torakumayama and staged this farce. And the robbers who had broken in two weeks before and brought the household almost to the verge of total extinction were also men of Kinguresu's subordinates. Kinguresu, overwhelmed by Sumanā-hime's Kototama and Mikahiko's divine power, suddenly trembled in all five limbs, sat down heavily on the spot with a thud, his mouth hanging wide open in astonishment, and trembled. The dozen small thieves who had been disguised as arresting officers likewise lost their footing on the spot, trembling and shaking.

§ 2

Michihiko took his seat with solemn dignity in the center of the zashiki, turning toward the Seinentai and the Dorobaugumi, and chanted the Sendenka in place of a greeting.

Michihiko: 'Man is the child of God, the palace of God, If he be one equal unto God, They say he governs heaven and earth, An honorable messenger of God. Caught up in petty desires, This vast world by his own hand, To the point of having no place to put his body, Narrowing it down—how lamentable it is! In the boundless universe, Pure and vast in the world, The joy of the Anyō Jiyōdo, That it might be obtained, in the ubusuna At the mansion of the iso do they dwell, Cleansing the filth of this world, The divine pillars of the pristine mitama, Kamususanō no Ōkami, Dispatching the divine offices to the countries of the four directions, To save and succor in peace The people who have become captives of the Magakami, How venerable are His decrees! I am Michihiko, the Sendenshi, Receiving the power of God in my body, Traversing the land of Fusa, Coming here at last and seeing, In the Senseikiyō famed in story, The paradise of Takaamahara as well, Enveloped by strange clouds and fog, Reduced to the darkness of eternal night, Akki Rasetsu in all directions Have run riot and rampaged. Beholding this tragic state, With the body of a messenger of God, How could I possibly overlook it? The teachings I proclaim are the holy and august imperial words of the Sumeragi no Kami. Purify your hearts and lend your ears, Hear them with reverence and awe! The wind god who preys upon weakness, The widows of the drifting shores, To the vacant home of Yoshikohime, The Taylor who forced his way in, Truly a pitiful captive of the crooked form, Reduced to an ugly prisoner, Without realizing his heavy crimes, How wretched to have committed them! Kingress and all others, Ye people who call yourselves catchpolls, You are no true officers of the law, Setting up a mountain stronghold In some mountain and threatening travelers, You are remembered as great thieves. If truly you were in the office of catchpolls, By the Kototama of the delicate princess, How could you be struck down and fall? Those who have weakness in their heart Even a single word of the Kototama Are those who strictly fear it. Though you are fellows hard to forgive, We too are fellow children of God, As brethren, without punishing you, Causing you to submit to the true way, To save and deliver you— This is the aspiration of my pure heart. Straighten your hearts and purify your souls, Expose the sins and transgressions you have committed hitherto Before the great presence of Ōsumeragi no Ōkami, Without wrapping them up, without hiding them, Warning yourselves for the future and unto the good path, All of you together, return and stand. Both I and thou are children of God, However clouded the soul may be, If you polish and raise it, just like the original It must become a crystal soul. Ah, Kannagara kamugami-gami, Vowing to God we proclaim and transmit it: Though the morning sun shines brightly, though it is clouded, Though the moon waxes, though it wanes, Even if the great earth sinks, The power of truth saves the world. There is no power superior to truth, Though one grasp the sovereignty of this world, If one lacks the path of truth, This is like a lamp before the wind. Reflect upon this, all people! Michihiko of the divine teachings, Toward everyone, of the divine mind's sincerity Proclaims and transmits it. Ah, Kannagara kamugami-gami, May your mitama be blessed and prosper!'

Thus he repeatedly and reiteratively preached and admonished everyone with the Sendenka. Kingress let tears flow in torrents, fell prostrate on the ground, and turned toward Michihiko,

King: 'Before the presence of Michihiko Tsukasa, the Sendenshi of the Mi-soshiru-gami, I lay bare and confess all my crimes and evils. Now, from this time forth, following your teachings, I shall return to the good path, so please forgive my sins up to now. An evil person like me surely does not exist anywhere else in the world. The truth of the matter is that we secluded ourselves in the mountain stronghold of Torakumayama, plundered the villages for ten ri around, and tormented travelers; whereupon Sell and Haar, who had been soldiers of the Brahman religion, brought along a large number of subordinates, climbed up Torakumayama, and together with twenty of our subordinates we were bound hand and foot, compelled of necessity to surrender, became their adopted sons, and are those who came out working toward this direction. And that man called Taylor of this village has always maintained secret communication with our gang, and was a person who acted as a guide for house-breakers and treasury-cracks; but two weeks ago, plotting to slaughter the household in this very house and usurp this property, upon the suggestion of that Taylor, we broke in with drawn swords, and attempted to completely annihilate the family, when divine punishment swiftly came in return, failing to strike down only one person, Yoshikohime-sama, and because of that our conspiracy was instantly brought to light, and smitten by divine punishment where we could not even move our bodies, we cannot bear the feeling of repentance. Please, oh please, even though we are fellows hard to forgive you, just as the words of the Sendenka just now say, look upon us again, hear us again, and proclaim us again with the great Tadani of vast heart, and I beg that you save at least our lives. If you say you will not forgive us no matter what, since there is nothing else we can do, please take my life, save the lives of my dozen or so subordinates, and after you have transmitted the true teachings to them, I pray that you save them into the path of God.''

Manifesting sincerity upon their faces, they apologize from the bottoms of their hearts.

Michihiko: 'Speaking from the perspective of the human world, you are unforgivable, heinous criminals. According to the laws of today, as perpetrators of robbery and murder, you would be headless corpses; yet because Kamisama dwells in great mercy and great compassion, if only you reform, He will surely forgive you. However, this Michihiko possesses neither the right to judge people nor the right to forgive them. Consequently, I also lack the authority to torment you. Please return to the path of truth, serve Kamisama morning and evening, and return to becoming true human beings who are the Miko of Kami. That is the hope of Michihiko, who is a Miko of Kami. The people there who are the subordinates of Kingres, if what Setsushiya says sinks into your hearts, you would do well to offer your apologies to Kamisama right here and now. Furthermore, I have heard that the people of the Seinenkuwai in this village revere the teachings of the Ural religion, but I ask you to walk the path of truth so as not to misunderstand the Mikokoro of Kamisama. If the teachings of the Ural religion are truly spreading, this Senseikiyō must remain as it was in ancient times, an Anrakukiyō of Tenka, peaceful and safe. Yet the sudden outbreak of such a tragic event makes this Michihiko foolishly think that there may be points in it that run counter to the Mikokoro of Kami. And since Sumanahime-sama lives in loneliness, separated from her parents, brothers, and husband, I ask the young people to protect her kindly, both overtly and covertly. I also request that you exhaust your filial devotion to the spirits of your parents and ancestors to the utmost, and guard her so that she may steadfastly maintain her chastity toward her deceased husband.'

Turk, choking with tears of gratitude:

Turk: 'Ah, truly the sincere Kami exists, / Having destroyed the ugly Magatsuhi. / Morning and evening guarding well the sacred words, / I shall proceed to serve upon the Great Way of Kami.'

Inter: 'I too shall engrave the words of the Kyōji / Upon my heart and never forget them.'

Michihiko: 'Even deep within the mountains of Michinoku, if there be the protection of the Sumeragi no Kami, dwell in peace. / Rebuilding this ancient Senseikiyō, / Smiling and flourishing, exist for eternity.'

Sumanahime: 'Aided by Michihiko, the official of Kami, / Today I am happy, smiling and flourishing. / The shadow of the attacking ugly Magatsuhi is gone, / And this day of its destruction is indeed most joyous.'

To such a place, the elders of the village, each carrying weapons, come running in a bustling manner from both the back and front gates. Moreover, the young people who had gone to search for Sumanahime have not yet returned, even a single one, due to the relatively long distance. When they looked at a man suffering and bubbling away inside the water-gate jar at the back door, it was Tailor, who had mistakenly fallen in while fleeing. Suddenly the great earth shook violently, and as soon as the mountains on all four sides began to roar thunderously, a deafening explosion echoed as if heaven and earth were splitting apart, and Tailor, who had fallen into the water-gate jar, was bounced up by the tremor, leaped two or three ken into the air, was violently dashed down upon the earth, and is foaming at the mouth in agony. This sound was the echo of the Torakumayama volcano erupting all at once.'

スマナー姫『三千彦の神の司に助けられ 今日は嬉しくゑみ栄えける。 おそひ来る醜の曲津の影もなく 亡びし今日ぞいとど嬉しき』

斯かる所へ村の中老、各自に得物を携へ、裏口表口より慌しく走り来る。又スマナー姫を捜しに行つた青年は、比較的遠路の為、一人も未だ帰つて来ない。裏口の水門壺の中にアブアブして苦しんでゐる男を見れば、逃げしなに過つて落込んだテーラであつた。俄に大地はビリビリと震動し、四辺の山岳は轟々と唸り出したとみるまに、轟前たる爆音、天地もわるる許りに響き来り、水門壺におちて居た、テーラは其震動にはね飛ばされて、二三間飛上り、どんと大地に投げ付けられ、苦しげに泡をふいてゐる。此音響は虎熊山の火山が一時に爆発した響であつた。
