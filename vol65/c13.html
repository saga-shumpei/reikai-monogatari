A pine tree, whose trunk is said to be thick enough for two men to clasp around, stands solitary like an open moon umbrella beside the narrow path bordering the mountains on one side and the fields on the other. In the shade of that pine tree, four or five men were huddled together, whispering closely and indulging in a secret conversation.

First Man: 'Hey, we've plundered farmhouses all around there, stealing wine and various appetizers, but if we don't hurry back, we might not make it in time for tonight's wedding. Otherwise, we're bound to receive another scolding from the boss.'

Second Man: 'No matter how much we hurry, considering this distance, there is simply no way we'll make it by tonight. Might as well, since we have both the wine and the appetizers right here, let's have a drink right under this pine tree.'

First Man: 'If we do something like that, that really would be a disaster. We'd be immediately excommunicated, you know. Excommunication alone might not be so bad, but just imagine if we went elsewhere and blabbed—they'd bind our hands and feet tightly and toss us straight into a volcanic crater. A precious life would be utterly ruined. You always talk foolishly when you drink wine, so you're hopeless.'

As their bodies grew robust, they gradually became emboldened, and once again they vigorously expounded upon their supremacy-of-the-thief doctrine. Pyō, Tei, and Bo, all three of them, also blabbered on like flowing river water about the benefits of thievery, giving Haaru a hard time. The rumbling of Mt. Torakuma, shrouded in clouds, suddenly became fierce, and the earth began to tremble violently. Even the four of them, brave as they were, turned pale and bit down upon the grass. With the deafening roar of a single explosion, Mt. Torakuma underwent a massive eruption, black smoke filled the skies, and lava scattered in all directions like rain, falling all the way to this spot several leagues away.

Trembling with fear, everyone suddenly changed their hearts and began to offer prayers of repentance. By the grace of the Kami, the colossal lava that had rained down subsided without injuring a single person. From this time on, everyone realized the preciousness of repentance, and following the missionary of Itahiko, they decided to head toward the holy land of Jerusalem under the guise of a pilgrimage.
