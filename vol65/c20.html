§ 1

Michihiko and Sunarahime, taking advantage of the dusk's darkness, quietly returned to their home and considered the situation from the back door.

Tela gathered twenty or thirty young men together and made a speech with a proud expression:

Tela: 'Hey, you fellows, I must make an announcement to the Youth Corps. As you can see, we have met with the misfortune of total family extinction, and Sunarahime alone was left behind, but what has become of her? Her whereabouts have now become unknown. When things come to this, the estate here must legally be inherited by our relatives. Well, this is quite a troublesome thing that has come about. As for me, being naturally free from greed and indifferent to worldly things, I never even dreamed of desiring a relative's property, but since this too is an event that has fallen and welled up from heaven, I have decided to patiently inherit the estate. Therefore, everyone in the Youth Corps, please do me the favor of sympathizing with me. Truly, what a troublesome thing has arrived, hehehe! The reason I had you come today is to celebrate... oops, instead of a celebration, the whole household has been wiped out, so in order to comfort the souls of the deceased, I want you to drink your fill. It is not property that I prepared, but I will let you drink plenty of sake, so drink heartily.'

From among the youths, a man stepped forward in front of Tela,

Taku: 'Hey, Tela, what on earth are you saying about the total extinction of the family? Isn't there a widow named Sunarahime left right here? As long as there is a widow, things will not go as you please. No matter how much you say you will do as you please, the Youth Corps will not agree to it. Could it be that you have received some kind of request letter for property management from Sunarahime-sama? Surely, to a scoundrel like you, no matter how deranged Sunarahime-sama might have been, there is no way she would ever make such a request.'

Tela: 'Hey, Taku, what are you saying? Sunarahime has already left a suicide note and dashed out of the house, writing that she is going to commit suicide. By now, she must have thrown herself into some mountain stream and died, no doubt. If so, it is naturally my property. Starting today, the master here is me. No matter how much the villagers complain, since I am a branch of the relatives that can never be cut off even if you cut it, it cannot be helped.'

Taku: 'If so, won't you let us see that suicide note? Come on, read it before everyone.'

Tela briefly pulled out the suicide note from his pocket, waving it around in front of the crowd,

Tela: 'Look, it is just like this.'

flipping a corner of it just a bit,

Tela: 'Hey, everyone, this handwriting must be Sunarahime's without a doubt. How about it? If anyone thinks it is different, please say it is different.'

Taku: 'Indeed, it is truly a lovely trail of brushstrokes. In this village, there should be no one else besides Sunarahime-sama who can write this much.'

Tela: 'Well, didn't you see? Your doubts are cleared now, ehehehe. Fortune comes to those who wait while sleeping. Today onward, the family's heirlooms, mountain forests, and fields are all the property of this Tela-sama. Ah, somehow becoming a person of wealth all of a sudden makes my shoulders feel heavy. Oh, I've even begun to feel as if my body has grown bigger. What a troublesome matter it is, anyway. Ihehehe.'

Taku: 'Hey, I want you to read that suicide note once before everyone. Because we don't know what is written on it.'

Tela: 'Won't you read it for me for a moment? Since my eyes are a bit bad, with it being dusk and the letters being far too fine, it is hard to see.'

Taku: 'Hmph, what a clever thing to say. Despite being night-blind, didn't you read a newspaper upside down in front of people some time ago? ...When someone from the side said to you, "Tela-sama, isn't that newspaper upside down?", you made a show of bad losers... You even went so far as to say that you were showing it to them, so there is no reason such beautiful characters could be readable by you. Come on, let's open the treasure box. Depending on this phrasing, whether you inherit the property or it becomes village common property will be according to Sunarahime-sama's last will.'

捕手の頭キングレスは威丈高になり、

『オイ、当家は一人も残らず不慮の炎難にかかつて滅亡し、憂愁の空気が漂うてるにも係はらず、其方は不都合千万にも当家の財産を横領せむと押寄せて来たのか、返答次第に依つては容赦は致さぬが何うだ。当家は一人も残らず滅亡したのだから、親類関係のテーラが遺産相続するのは法律の許す所だ。不都合千万な、マゴマゴしてると皆捕縛するぞ』

ターク『モシ、キングレスさまとやら、万々一、未亡人のスマナーさまが生残つて居つたら何うなりますか。それでも遺産をテーラが相続せねばならぬのですか。左様な法律は未だ聞いた事は厶いませぬが…』

キング『きまつた事だ。もし未亡人が居るとすれば、却てテーラが財産横領を企てた事になり、大罪人となる所だ。併し乍ら今軒下に隠れて聞いて居れば、夫の後を逐うて自殺をすると申し、書置まで残しておいたぢやないか』

ターク『成程、夫れには間違厶いませぬが、もしも死なずに居つたら、矢張、テーラの物にはなりますまいな』

キング『無論の事だ』

テーラ『アハヽヽヽ、何と云つても運が向いて来たのだから……オイ、ターク、インター、駄目だよ。神妙に捕縛されるか。但しは茲であやまるなら、俺も村のよしみで許して貰うてやる。何うだ。返答を早く致せ』

此間に五六人の青年は中老をかり集めると云つて出たのは、其実白骨堂のあたりにまだスマナーが生きてうろついて居りはせぬか、それさへ居ればテーラの鼻をあかしてやるのに好都合だと、目ひき袖ひき捜索に出たのであつた。かかる所へ幽かな女の歌ふ声が聞えて来た。
