Chapter 7: 反鱗 [1663]

反鱗〔1663〕

§ 1

As the Sannin ascended the Kifuhan, a sound of groaning, 'un-un', was heard from within the Mitsurin. Itahiko, startled, parted the grass and entered the forest to find a man lying stretched out, gasping for breath, still wrapped in a Hautai. Immediately, he opened the mouth of his Suitō and gave him water to drink, chanted the Kazūta of Ama, and provided him with compassionate Kaihau. The fallen man gradually regained his Shiyōki, looked around the Atari restlessly, and was startled to see Sendenshi Itahiko before him; he attempted to flee at once, but as the strength in his Teashi had not yet undergone Kuwaifuku, he merely stomped his feet in frustration like an Ishigame.

Ita: 'Ya, traveler, have you regained your senses? First of all, Ketsukō-Eitau. You seem to have sustained a Taihen Kega; I suppose you were likely attacked by the Dorobau of Torakumayama.'

Man: 'Yes, thank you. I am a person from this vicinity, but while on my way to visit a Shinseki on a Chiyotsuto sudden errand, I encountered an evil man named Sēru, the boss of the Dorobau; he took all my money, split my head open, and I was in a state of unconsciousness. Oh, how you have saved me. I shall never forget this favor.'

Emu stepped forward and gazed intently at his face.

Emu: 'Ya, are you not the vice-boss of the Dorobau of Torakumayama? It is no use telling such lies. If I may, Sendenshi-sama, this man is called Hāru, and he is a very, very wicked fellow. He is the one who gagged the young Hime-sama you are searching for and had her carried into a rocky cavern by numerous henchmen; do not let your guard down.'

Hāru: 'Oi, Emu, ...no, I do not know who this fellow is, but it is troubling to be so mistaken. I am no Dorobau or anything of the sort; I am a farmer from this neighborhood. I will not have you saying such reckless things.'

Emu smiled with a grin.

Emu: 'He-he-he-he, you speak well. Even if you say such things, here stand two former Dorobau who were once your henchmen and are now true human beings. Confess like a man.'

Ita: 'Oi, Emu and Tatsu, there is no mistake that this fellow is a Dorobau, is there?'

Emu: 'Yes, yes, he is a first-rate Dorobau. This fellow is called Second Lieutenant Hāru of the Brahman Army, an officer who made a name for himself as a handsome man; but after General Kishun-betsu-sama disbanded the army, he had no choice but to start a Dorobau business with Captain Sēru, and he is a most wicked man who holds great influence in the caverns of Torakumayama as a leader of a hundred men, so you must not let your guard down.'

Ita: 'What you say must be true. Your conversion has also been proven by this. From now on I will look after you, so be at ease.'

Emu: 'Oh, thank you. I humbly request your patronage Eitau-Eitau.'

Tatsu: 'I shall study hard and make things cheaper than at other shops. I humbly request your patronage for a long time to come.'

Ita: 'Ha-ha-ha. What interesting men. Ya, I like you greatly. From now on I shall keep you busy with errands, so you should study hard.'

Both: 'Fu-fu-fu-fu.'

Ita: 'Oi, as these two are now testifying, there is no doubt you are a Dorobau leader. If you do not confess the truth, it will not be to your benefit.'

Hāru: 'No, I am filled with awe; please forgive my manifold sins. It is true that I took the two Hime-sama back and threw them into prison. However, there is a great boss who employs me, so the sin is not mine alone. Please, I beg you to investigate him.'

Ita: 'To pin your own sins upon your boss is an act of extreme insolence. Even if it was something the boss did, do you not have the sincerity to take the responsibility upon yourself? Surely even among the Dorobau circles, a code of morality must be practiced.'

伊太『オイ、エム、タツの両人、此奴は泥棒に間違ひないなア』

伊太『お前の云ふ事は本当だらう。お前の改心もそれで証明された。これから可愛がつてやるから安心せい』

伊太『ハヽヽ。面白い男だな。ヤ大に気に入つた。是から精出して御用を仰せつけてやるから、充分勉強するがいいぞ』

両人『フヽヽヽ』

伊太『オイ、お前は今此両人が証明して居るが、泥棒頭に相違あるまい。有体に白状せないと、お前のためにならないぞ』

ハール『いや、恐れ入りまして厶います、何卒重々の罪をお赦し下さいませ、二人の姫様を連れ帰り、牢獄に打ち込みましたのは私に相違厶いませぬ。併し私を使ふ大親分が厶いますから、私許りの罪では厶いませぬ。どうぞ彼をお調べ下さいませ』

伊太『自分の罪を親分に塗り付けるとは不届き千万の奴だ。たとへ親分がやつた事でも何故私がやりましたと引受けるだけの赤心が無いか、泥棒仲間にも道徳律が行はれて居るだらう』

§ 2

Hāru: 'Yes, that is certainly so, but no matter what is said, the Oyabun is the Oyabun. Finally, that wretch of an Oyabun, out of resentment from a Kiyōsō of love, tried to subject me to a Yamiuchi, and so I have met with such a fate and have actually just run away. If the Oyabun has his Hanrin, I have my Hanrin too. That is why it is so Ahau-like that a Giseiteki Seishin simply will not arise, will it?'

Ita: 'What has become of those Futari women?'

Hāru: 'Yes, surely... since Taishiyō Sēru is infatuated with them, he will not do anything so Teara. Well, as far as their Migara are concerned, they are Daijiyōbu, so please be at Goanshin. And please forgive my sins. I too, as of Kefu, shall Haigefu my life as a Dorobau.'

Ita: 'That is Kanshin. If that is so, from now on, will you not follow me and go to the Ihaya Ichido more? It would be convenient for Nanika Bengi.'

Hāru: 'Yes, oh—my desire to accompany you is Yamayama, but as you see, my head aches, and a Kifusei Okubiyōen has suddenly made a Totsupatsu, so to my Ikan, I am unable to make the Deki of coming. Please forgive me this time.'

Ita: 'You are a Hontau-ly troublesome fellow. Since I have just performed Chinkon on you, the pain should have stopped by now. Without saying such spineless things—since I am not saying I will boil and eat you or roast and eat you—why not guide me as proof of your submission?'

Hāru: 'If that is the case, it cannot be helped. Following your words, I shall guide you to the Hime-sama's room. By the way, is that person a member of your family, or perhaps your brother?'

Ita: 'The older one is the wife of my friend. The younger one is my wife. I suppose they have been quite a burden to you.'

Hāru: 'Ya! Hearing that, I have no face to show them, so please, I beg you, forgive me.'

Emu: 'If I may, Propagator-sama, this Hāru fell in love with the younger one, and after wooing and wooing her to the limit, he was given the cold shoulder; then, exploding in a fit of passion, he threw her into a dark, dark rock cave and has been mistreating her. That is why he just confessed he has no face to show. Right there, please give him a taste of something truly painful. It is for the sake of the future.'

Ita: 'What a helpless man you are. However, you say you have reformed, but aren't you quite ill-natured? To report the faults of the person you looked up to as your superior until now to me—you are a Hontau-ly fellow who does not understand duty and humanity.'

Emu: 'Can one enter a band of Dorobau while knowing duty and humanity? It is the height of the survival of the fittest and the victory of the superior over the inferior; how could there be such leeway? If one were concerned with such things, one's own self would perish. Even Takeo Arishima went to what he called the height of love and finally destroyed himself, did he not? Takeo Arishima emphasized that "Love is Best" and said that love is not something that should continue forever. And he said that even if it is a married couple, if someone more beloved than the spouse appears, it is the truth to part and lean toward the one with deeper love. Therefore, this Taishiyō no longer has any prospects; I thought you were far more splendid, a savior who would rescue us, and so I have discarded my former Oyabun like a worn-out sandal. Is that bad of me?'

Ita: 'Ahahaha, how cold-hearted. With that, you have not yet reached the point of reformation. I shall have to squeeze the fat out of you once.'

Hāru: 'Please, overlook it now. My speaking ill of Sēru was, in short, because he is a rival in love; my heart felt so bad that it just spilled out of my mouth. Henceforth, I shall be careful. If that is so, following your command, I shall reorganize our ranks and gallantly take the lead. Now, Emu and Tatsu, you two follow behind the Propagator.'

Ita: 'Yaa, you three should all go ahead. I have no eyes in the back of my head, after all. Haha!'

Emu: 'It is like traveling with a "sending-off wolf." It is dangerous for you to go first. If you stumble and fall, we don't know when he might bite into you.'

Hāru: 'Here now, Emu. Do not say unnecessary things.'

Scolding her thus, he takes the lead and climbs up with quick steps.
