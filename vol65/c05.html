§ 1

Itahiko: 'I parted from my master and lord, And briskly climbed Mount Halsey. At that very moment, My soul was tested by the Gokeshin of Konohanahime. Here the flowers of Kuwaigo have bloomed, And while my Mitama is perfumed with Haukau, I proceed aimlessly through the fields smelling of lotus blossoms. Through the valleys of mountain upon mountain, I make the Miizu of the Kami my staff And my strength, as I gradually Arrive at the shores of Lake Halsey. Looking out, I see Mount Torakuma Soaring high into the Unpeu. From within the midst of the clouds covering it, The crater, famous for its sound, Is so fierce it scorches the heavens. Where is my master and lord now? How fares Burawāda-hime? She must be suffering on her path, her feet aching, Enduring hardships and tribulations. In the Moon Country where Magami rage, If she has been captured by Warumono, Is she not perhaps meeting with such agony That she knows not what to do with herself? Whether it be due to my heart I know not, But somehow my chest is in turmoil, And an air of Fuan has assailed me. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara! By the Goitoku of Sumeōkami, May the Hitoritabi of this Kayowa woman Be led in great peace and tranquility To the noble capital of Jerusalem, Where the Kami resides. As for me, being a man, I shall proceed without fearing in the least Any Nayami or Magakami. Yet the Mitama of the two, Hime of Devis and Burawāda, weigh on my mind. My legs, weary of travel, falter in their step. One who has become a minister of the Kami Must indeed taste the feeling of a breaking heart Time and time again. "Truly, it is a weary human world," I grumble thus morning and evening. May you look upon the faults of Minister Itahiko with Naohi, Hear them with Naohi, and forgive them while speaking with Naohi. In the forest lands at the foot of Mount Torakuma, Where the clouds and mists are deep, To say nothing of fierce beasts and poisonous snakes, I hear that thieves of filthy heart Frequently appear and disappear. Protect my uncertain journey, O Mizu-mitama of the three and five pillars of Kami, O Great Kami Susanoo, The Great Ancestor of the origin of this world, O Great Lord Kunitokotachi. I offer my prayers before your noble presence. Whether the morning sun shines or is clouded, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the great earth should sink, The power of Makoto saves the world. The power of Makoto saves the world. A single Evangelist of Makoto, Receiving the teachings of the Kami, Should find no reason for Magakami to obstruct The path upon which he proceeds; Yet I wonder why, of late, The well-being of the Hime weighs on my mind. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara! Grant me the grace of the Mitama. O Kaminaho-bi who created this world, O Ōnaho-bi of vast heart, Look upon me with Naohi, speak with Naohi, And as I rouse my courage, I proceed lonely up the mountain slopes Where the Magami of Torakuma rage.'

The one climbing briskly up the small path in the dense forest while singing thus was Minister Itahiko.

By the side of the road, once again, two men were engrossed in a whispered conversation.

Emu: 'Hey, Tatsu, why don't you just give up this trance business?'

Tatsu: 'Hmph, what are you talking about? You are in a trance yourself, aren't you? A tofu maker makes and sells tofu, a liquor dealer makes and sells liquor, and a thief's business is to aim for other people's pockets to fatten his own. In this world, one must work hard at one's own business. Is there any other business as good as this, requiring no taxes and no capital?'

Emu: 'It sounds like a lucrative business when you first hear it, but even if there is income once or twice a month, most of it is taken by the boss. Is it not a futile thing to live a narrow life in this wide world, trembling with fear and barely having enough to eat? We were originally Brahman soldiers, so we thought thievery was interesting and thought nothing of killing people. However, I encountered the former boss of the Sale of Mount Torakuma, General Oniharunawake-sama, who has taken the form of a monk and goes about blowing a conch shell; I heard a wonderful story from him and converted. However, my partner, a fellow named Tāru, insisted on persisting in evil to the end, so I parted ways with him and came this far. Then, arriving here, I found you. Since I have been saved by the Kami, I want to make you a good person too, so listen to my advice with some sincerity. I am certainly not telling you anything bad.'

§ 2

Tatsu: 'Yeah, hearing you say that, it is so. It is not that I like being a Dorobau. I had plenty of Zaisan inherited from my parents, but I started a Shinki Hatsumei Shiyōbai and met with a total Shitsupai; unable to stay in my Kunitokoro, I became a Kojiki and came here. It was then that the Oyabun of the cells picked me up, and I finally became able to wet the area under my nose, one way or another. Compared to my life until now—walking through Sanchiyōmachi and Gochiyōmachi to receive a single Ichimon, being despised as a Kojiki-Kojiki, being kicked when sleeping under people's eaves, and being driven away after spending an Ichiya in a Tsujidau—I thought this was much more clever, so I became a Dorobau. However, if there were some good Shiyōbai, I would not want to do such a thing, but I suppose this too is an Innen.'

Emu: 'You say you did Shiyōbai, but what kind of Shiyōbai did you do and meet with Shitsupai?'

Tatsu: 'Yeah, well, listen to this. Since I was young, I have been fond of Hatsumei and have obtained twelve or thirteen Senbai Tokukiyo. However, while the Nōshiyōmushiyō granted me the Senbai Tokukiyo, when it came to the stage of Urida, not a single one moved, which troubled me. Because of that, I completely squandered the Zaisan inherited from my parents.'

Emu: 'What kind of things did you invent?'

Tatsu: 'Eh, just as Watt invented Jiyōki by looking at the Yuge of a Tetsubin, or Newton advocated the theory of Earth's gravity by seeing a Ringo fall, I too cannot make an Hatsumei without some motive. One time, I was cleaning a lamp chimney. When you rub the blackened part of that chimney up and down with a chimney cleaner, the dust is entirely removed. That pitch-black thing becomes the original transparent chimney, right?'

Emu: 'Yeah, indeed, it becomes quite beautiful. And then what happened?'

Tatsu: 'And then, you see, as a result of twisting my head and thinking for a hundred days and a hundred nights, I invented the Human Body Cleaning Device.'

Emu: 'Indeed, that sounds interesting. Have you ever researched medicine or the like?'

Tatsu: 'What, medicine is useless. None of the doctors nowadays can cure a real illness. Illness is never something that is healed by swallowing medicine. An illness that can be healed will heal even if left alone. Rather than that, I made the Human Body Cleaning Device so that illness would not occur. That is, I extended the machine called a brush for cleaning lamp chimneys to about eight feet, made it look like an upward-crawling insect, and when I lined them up on the ground, something like a giant centipede was completed. I put that on Urida as a Human Body Cleaner. Since people often ruin their stomachs and accumulate filth when they drink too much sake or eat too much rice, my idea was to pass it from the mouth to the anus like cleaning a lamp, and rub it vigorously up and down to completely remove the grime inside the belly. However, because a human's mouth and anus are too narrow and the belly is thick, the mouth and anus can be cleaned, but the essential cleaning of the belly is impossible. Therefore, everyone criticized it without even using it and would not buy it. I made about ten thousand of them intending to sell them, but it was no use.'

Emu: 'Hahaha, that is quite a story. What did you do after that?'

Tatsu: 'After that, you see, this time I invented the Urida of natural things that do not require much capital. That was, truly, an idea of extraordinary Shinki.'

Emu: 'Won't you let me hear that extraordinary idea?'

Tatsu: 'This is a great secret. If you swear not to let it out, I will tell you. Actually, I collect them when the cold is about twenty-seven or twenty-eight degrees Fahrenheit. I invented a substitute for glass, for which there is high demand nowadays from barbers to merchants and farmers. I break the thin ice of about one or two Bu thickness covering the surface of ponds, pack it into empty oil crates, and sell it for use in mirrors and shoji screens. In summer and such, if you fit this ice glass into the shoji, the wind naturally hits the ice and the living room becomes cool even in the height of summer. Since the raw material is free, I thought there was no easier way to make money, so despite the cold, I floated a small boat in the pond, cut it out, returned home to store it secretly, named it the Shinki Hatsumei "Cooling Glass: Summer Unknown," paid a lot of advertisement fees to the big newspapers in the city and country, and opened for business. But the people of the world mocked me and not a single person gave an order. Wondering why, I opened the storehouse to investigate, and found the inside of the warehouse was soaking wet with water. While thinking perhaps a rat had dribbled urine, I checked the boxes where I had stored the ice glass, and every single sheet had melted away without exception. So, I am still in the middle of researching a method to prevent ice from melting. If only I succeed in this, I can live as a fine gentleman without having to work as a ridiculous Dorobau, you see.'

タツ『兎も角、兄貴に任しておくわ。ヤア、何だか宣伝歌の声が聞えて来るぢやないか、何と恐ろしい声だのう』

エム『ハイ、エベスだとか、ブラブラ婆アだとか云ふ事ですが、仲々どうしてどうして婆ア処ですか、水の垂るやうな別嬪でした。今はセール親方の居間の近くの牢獄に打ち込んで厶います』

伊太『コリヤ、その方は泥棒だな』

伊太『馬鹿申せ、真人間が岩窟の中に姫が隠してある事が、どうして分らうか。大方貴様は乾児だらう』

エム『ハイ、今日迄は泥棒の乾児で厶いましたが、実の所は鬼春別将軍様が比丘となつて、ここをお通り遊ばし、結構なお道を教へて下さつたので、漸く改心しまして、仲間の目を忍び、ここ迄逃げて来ました処、ここに又一人の小泥棒が休んで居ましたので、早く改心したらどうだ、と今も今とて意見をして居つた所で厶います』

§ 4

Ita: 'Have you also finally returned to your good heart? Is there no mistake in that?'

The two persons, while trembling, spoke in unison:

Riyōnin: 'Yes, there is no mistake.'

Ita: 'Then guide me to that Gankutsu. I must rescue the two Hime.'

Emu: 'As there are a great many Dorobau, it is dangerous for you alone; how about you stop? In fact, Oniharuwake-sama went there with six Kobun, saying he would make the Oyabun reform. Surely the whole itsuken will soon be settled and they will return in buji.'

Ita: 'What, you say that Shiyōgunsama in Bikusugata has gone there? Then all the more reason. Now that I have heard this, I cannot let it pass. Come, provide the annai.'

Emu: 'Yes, if you command me to provide the annai, it is not that I will not do it, but since I have already reformed from being a Dorobau, if I am found by those fellows, my inochi will be gone. I beg of you, please forgive me from this alone.'

Ita: 'What, there is no need for worry. I have this luminous jewel received from the divinely miraculous Ubananda Ryūō. With this, there is no need to fear even a million enemies. Come, provide the annai.'

At these words, while the two were seized by feelings of anxiety, out of fear of Itahiko, they dejectedly took the lead like sheep to the slaughterhouse and advanced toward the Gankutsu.
