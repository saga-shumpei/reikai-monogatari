Chapter 24: 危母 Jewel [1680]

危母玉〔1680〕

§ 1

When Tamakuniwake and Masumihiko arrived at the western shore of the Lake of Surajah, following the solemn admonitions of Hatsuwakahime, they parted ways with Michihiko, Itahiko, Chidaukoji, and others who had accompanied them, and wishing to press on swiftly to the sacred land, day and night they disregarded the fatigue of travel, quickened their steps, and at last arrived at the village of Sankau, which was close to Jerusalem.

Here the Jordan River, flowing down from Mount Zion, rushed past with a roaring sound of water.

As they hurried along the narrow path on its northern shore, suddenly black clouds covered the sky as if ink had been poured across it, and an indescribable, gloomy atmosphere drifted in.

Then the surroundings grew completely silent, not a single breeze blew, and for no apparent reason it became oppressively hot, with sticky sweat oozing from every part of their bodies.

They became as breathless as if they had been exposed to poison gas, and collapsing like falling timber in the shade of the trees by the riverside, they sat down, pressed their faces against the roots of the grass, inhaled the vital energy welling up from beneath the earth, and sought to recover their health.

This was because Mount Torakuma in the east, tens of ri away, had erupted, and fanned by the easterly wind at that moment, ash and smoke containing poison had gathered toward the lowland of the valley.

With their breath coming in gasps, the two men offered up the heavenly number-chants in low voices.

Masumihiko: 'Moshi-sensei, right on the verge of arriving at the sacred land of Jerusalem with just one more push, the heavens suddenly darkened, and my breathing has become so painful that I can no longer endure it; might there be something displeasing to the Lord God in this?'

Masumihiko: 'If we were to come this far and unfortunately perish, it would be a regret lasting a thousand ages.'

Masumihiko: 'What do you think about this?'

Tamakuniwake: 'Uun, it certainly is strange; I cannot make sense of it either.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Yet when I thought I heard a mysterious sound echoing toward the eastern sky around noon today, the heavens darkened from then on, and even the surface of the earth became enveloped in such an atmosphere.'

Tamakuniwake: 'It is likely that some volcano has erupted somewhere... or so one would think.'

Tamakuniwake: 'In any case, this atmosphere is mixed with something like fine ash.'

Tamakuniwake: 'We have no choice but to commune with the soil here for a little while, pray to the Lord God, and wait for our bodies to recover.'

Tamakuniwake: 'I too feel somehow distressed, and my four limbs and five bodies seem to have gone all shaky.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Ah, May the Almighty's Spiritual Blessing Abound!'

So saying, they pressed their palms together in prayer.

Just as things were thus, a faint, distant barking of a dog, 'wan wan wan wan,' was heard.

Upon hearing this sound, both of them felt as if they had awakened from a dream, and their spirits somehow became clear and bright.

Masumihiko: 'Ah, could that voice possibly be Smart? It certainly sounds familiar.'

Masumihiko: 'And as that voice entered my ears, my spirits suddenly brightened, and the circulation of my blood seems to have improved.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Un, indeed, my vigor seems to have recovered as well upon hearing that voice.'

Tamakuniwake: 'There is no doubt it is Smart.'

Tamakuniwake: 'If so, it appears that Hatsuwakahime-sama is also coming nearby.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Ah, what a joyous thing.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Yet if the Princess were to find the two of us collapsing in such a place, it would be a terrible disgrace, so let us muster our energy, chant the propaganda songs, and walk along step by step.'

Masumihiko: 'If that is so, I do not know whether I can walk or not, but let us try walking slowly.'

Leaning upon their staffs as strength, the two stood up and attempted to walk, but the joints of their knees were weak and felt as if they were laughing, so that no matter what they did, they could not move their feet.

Just as things were thus, running from the east as if shooting an arrow, was Smart.

Smart repeatedly shook its head and tail, showing a joyful expression, and with all its might barked loudly, 'Wan wan.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Ah, you are Smart-sama, how good of you to come.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Surely Hatsuwakahime-sama must be together with you as well.'

Addressing Smart as though speaking to a human being, Smart bit at the hem of Tamakuniwake's garment and pulled at it persistently.

Tamakuni: 'I wonder if some disaster is about to befall us. Since Smart-sama is a divine messenger, come Masumihiko, let us follow behind and go wherever it may lead.'

Masumi: 'Yes, let us do so.'

So saying, Smart released the hem he had been holding in his mouth, wagged his tail, and began to ascend, pointing toward the high peak of Sankao on the northern bank of the Jordan River. Reaching a spot where they had climbed some seven or eight chō, noticeably gigantic olive trees and other miscellaneous trees formed a grove around the second station of the mountain. When they went to look, a small, old-fashioned shrine was built there.

Tamakuni: 'I wonder if Smart-sama means for us to worship here and go on. There must be some reason for this as well.'

The two naturally knelt and offered the Amatsunorito with broken breaths, in fragments, from beneath painful gasps. From far behind the shrine came a gentle woman's voice.

Having finished chanting, she revealed her form before the two. At this time, Hatsuwakahime had been praying in the forest two or three chō deeper than this shrine for the subjugation of the Manasuin Dragon King, but knowing that the miraculous efficacy was difficult to manifest easily, and wishing above all to save the lives of the two, she had imbued herself with divine power, stripped naked, and doused herself in the waterfall of Sankao. Then, hearing Smart's voice and learning that the two had safely reached this shrine, she had changed her clothes from the foot of the waterfall and appeared here while singing.

Wiping away tears that welled up naturally, with a muffled voice, Tamakuniwake spoke:

Tamakuni: 'Hatsuwakahime-sama, we two have not yet enough divine virtue, and having approached almost to a spot barely a moment from the sacred ground, just as we passed this riverside our physical condition suddenly grew ill, we lost the freedom of our limbs, and unworthily collapsed. Fearing in secret that we might have committed some offense against the gods and incurred their reprimand, the two of us were troubled in anxiety and offer our apologies; when we heard the voice of this Smart-sama whom you sent to us, our strength suddenly recovered and we were led up to this place. Whenever I behold your figure, out of joy and longing, tears spill forth naturally. Could your inviting us here be for some unusual official business?'

Hatsuwaki: 'Tamakuniwake-sama, Masumihiko-sama, you have safely come this far. Just as I sang a moment ago, the Manasuin Dragon King has occupied the Garden of Gethsemane, and since he intends to trample the Garden of Eden and the Mountain of Gold, I had Smart go ahead and provided various protections so the Dragon King would not occupy it. Realizing that you were passing through this highway, and fearing lest peril befall your persons, I was waiting here. Presently, the Manasuin Dragon King will leave Mount Torakuma and advance, shaking mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees, in order to occupy the Garden of Gethsemane now that the time has truly arrived; but since there is utterly no place to put his body in the Garden of Gethsemane, he will ascend this river and go to Mount Sion. That being the case, since he possesses a gigantic dragon body, it was a foregone conclusion that if he saw your figures, he would make short work of you with the sword at the tip of his tail, so I made arrangements for you to take refuge up to this place. The suffocating air lingering in the lowland is proof that the Dragon King will soon be ascending. Because evil qi drifts for some ten ri ahead of wherever the Dragon King's head turns... soon, making a loud sound, his dragon body will ascend, no doubt.'

Seeing this figure, Tamakuniwake and Masumihiko suddenly had their bodies cower and knelt down on the spot.
