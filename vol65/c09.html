Chapter 9: 劇流 [1665]

劇流〔1665〕

§ 1

Selle was in his ima, feeling that the time for the fulfillment of his desire had finally arrived, and in the mood of a celebratory prelude, he tilted the wine that his kobun had stolen from here and there inside, became drunkenly intoxicated, and that night fell asleep oblivious to zengo.

Yale, thinking to bind Selle during this present time and strike him down into the raugoku, crept up to look, but the cautious Selle had firmly lowered the jiyōmahe and was sleeping all hitori, so there was nothing he could do, and that night he spent waiting out the dawn before the raugoku of Chidaukoji.

Prior to this, Yale turned toward Chidaukoji, and speaking in a kogoe:

Yale: 'I say, Chidaukoji-sama, when I went bringing your tegami, a tall man was standing in front of the door of the raugoku, so thinking this was a shima, when I looked and considered very well, it was the boss Selle. Without sleeping a wink through the night, it looks as though he was uttering some kind of guchi in front of a woman and kudoing. Truly, he is a troubled fellow.'

Chidau: 'Ahahahaha, that soitsu is interesting. And did the tegami reach the mukafu successfully?'

Yale: 'Yes, I certainly threw it in. However, those futari of women were saying strange things. Because the oyabun said to go over there at itsutoki, I left the spot, and when I listened to the state of affairs in the darkness, the futari of princesses, Bravāda and Davis, were holding extreme grudges against you.'

Chidau: 'Ah, is that so, is that so. For I too have tormented them quite a bit.'

Yale: 'Eh, is that true? You too are quite a villain... This time they were petitioning, begging the great general Selle to let futari come together and torture you to death, you know. They were saying that if they put the futari who were put separately into the raugoku into one place, tied Chidau-sama up and shoved him in there, and tortured him to death, they would listen to what Selle says. What on earth should be done, I wonder?'

Chidau: 'My, that is interesting. As expected of Princess Bravāda and Princess Davis. Oh satisfaction, satisfaction, ahahahaha, it is delightfully unbearable.'

Yale: 'I say, Chidau-sama, you are acting somewhat strangely.'

Chidau: 'Un, I am acting strangely.'

Bat: 'I say, I say Chidau-sama, are you not uttering strange things? If you are tortured to death, what do you intend to do with us?'

As they spoke thus, Yaku came slinking up.

Yaku: 'Ya, Yale, were you here? If you speak with too loud a voice, it will be terrible if the petty thieves outside hear.'

Yale: 'Chidau-sama is uttering things that I simply cannot comprehend, so I find it wondrous beyond endurance.'

Yaku: 'Ahahahaha, he is Chidau-sama possessed of both wisdom and valor. There must be some good clever plan. Well, do not worry. You guys should plan leisurely. I am going to make the rounds, so while I am gone, you had better consult together thoroughly.'

So saying, he went out on patrol.

Chidaukoji lowered his voice and turned toward Bat, Kārkus, Bēsu, and Yale the jailer,

Chidau: 'There is a deep reason why the futari of princesses are saying they wish to torture this Chidaukoji to death. Tonight is late, but in any case it is tomorrow night's work. Bat will become my substitute, and both Kārkus and Bēsu will transform into the futari of women, use women's voices, and taking advantage of the darkness, enter the raugoku to perform a single play. And they will bring this Chidau and the futari of princesses outside. Then, using Kototama, it is a plan to bring Selle first and the other fellows into allegiance all at once. It seems the futari of princesses are about to execute that plan. How about it, Bat, are you able to use my voice?'

Bat: 'As for that, it is not that I cannot use it, but I cannot bear being tortured to death, you see.'

バット『そりや使へぬ事は厶いませぬが、私が嬲殺にされちや堪りませぬな』

Chidau: 'That is the play. It is fine as long as you just cry kyakya. Do you think we will really poke or slash each other? And the two women's parts are played by Kāku and Bēsu, but can you do women's kowairo? You should be able to use it a little.'

Kāku: 'That is interesting. I have liked shibai since I was young, so I can do a woman's kowairo for an old woman, a girl, an old furukakaa, or anything.'

Chidau: 'Then please become Debisu. Debisu is twenty-two or three, so please keep that in mind. Anyway, since it is dark, it is fine just to raise your voice.'

Kāku: (Kowairo) "You must be Chidaukoji, who holds heavy grudges. How dare you, how dare you heap bujiyoku upon me. Realize it now—..." How is this?

Chidau: 'Ahahahahaha, excellent, excellent, then that is shiiuitsu. Now with this, the hime Debisu is done. Hey, Bēsu, you are going to be Burawāda. You are still sixteen. Can you use a girl's voice?'

Bēsu: 'I certainly can. Shall I try it? ... (Kowairo) "Are you not Chidaukoji? How well, how well you have tormented me. Ten will not forgive a zainin to the end. Now become the rust of the swords of us riyōnin. Grudges pile up, the mountain of Torakuma." Hehehehe. How is this?'

Chidau: 'Ahahahahaha, jiyōtō, jiyōtō. First, with this, the hime Burawāda is done. Hey, Batto, you are to use my kowairo. How is it, won't you try one here?'

Batto: (Kowairo) "This is kokoroe, hime Debisu, hime Burawāda, or whatever you are, setsushiya has never added bujiyoku toward you. There are takusan dōmeiijin in the world. You are misunderstanding because it is dark. Still, meiwakusenban, no matter if it is dark, you should be able to tell by the color of the voice. I am in trouble if you misunderstand me..." Well, Lord Chidau, how is it like this?

Chidau: 'Well, it is acceptable. However, since the two hime take up their kuwaiken and stab firmly, at that time you must make a painful-sounding voice.'

Batto: 'Yes, since I have done Kanpei's harakiri, making a painful-sounding voice is nothing at all. Please be at ease.'

Chidau: 'Hey, with Kanpei there is no composure. Try doing it around the place of En'ya Hangan.'

Batto: 'Yes, I shall try it... "Rikiya, Rikiya, has Yuranosuke not come yet? ... Hahaa, he has not yet appeared... Eh, chiehe, e, it cannot be helped..." Making up his mind, sooner than drawing his kuwaiken, he stabs it firmly into his left flank... "Aa, it is regrettable not to meet Yuranosuke... Yuranosuke, just now, appear... Ya, I have waited for you. Come near, come near..." Hahahahaah.'

Chidau: 'Hey, at that part, do not say come near, come near, but say Chidau, Chidau. However, if it turns into a shibai, the enemy will figure it out, so it has to be Chidaukoji after all.'

Batto: 'Is there any oversight in such a matter? Please be at ease.'

Ēru: 'Ah, this fellow is interesting, ahahahahaha. Taking advantage of being a prison guard, I will successfully slip them through. I can hardly wait for tomorrow night.'

Yaku enters hurriedly,

Yaku: 'Hey hey, if you raise your voice so loudly, it will be exposed. The shibai you were doing just now was heard about a chō away. Fortunately, no one was outside, so it is fine, but if someone heard you, it would be a disaster.'

Chidau: 'Hum, are you Yaku? Good work, Yaku-me, it was hard. Pay full attention and take care of us, Yaku. In return, once your yaku is finished, as yakusoku, I will make you a splendid propagandist.'

Yaku: 'Hai, arigato.'

Even as they spoke thus, footsteps resounded loudly, and a rauban came over. Chidaukoji, wishing to gloss over this place, began to chatter things that were neither kiyōmon nor gāthā.

Chidau: 'Miroku shin miroku. Suiginmuka. Bunshinsenhiyakuoku. Agimushin. Chiyōteishiraiya. Eyes engender a sankaku, the head steepens the gogaku. Good is not yet necessarily good. Evil is not yet necessarily evil. When Hotei's head opens, it is waiwai, it is kotsukotsu, it is tōtō. Lightness is like a gaumau, heaviness is like a kiusan. Nentoku and straightway cast it away, dotoku and straightway use it. He raises the hotsusu and says, still this is tosotsudatentei. How is one to bauro just like the time izen to Miroku's not yet being born? He strikes the bed and says, collecting usei to return to the old trees. Letting autumn colors reach the wu-tong tree. Inscribing upon the images of the fifth and sixth patriarchs, he says, 'Hensatsusu this zuda. Though mountains grind, this hate is unground. My eaves are heavy now, and for your sake, I plant many pines.' For over thirty years at Seigan, what was attained at the place of Bukan, when taken out and shown to people, has not a drop of leakage. For the subsequent thirty years, it is medicine for dotting the eyes.'

The night was quiet and deep as it grew late, and only the sound of a faint wind occasionally rattling the rain shutters was heard in broken snatches.
