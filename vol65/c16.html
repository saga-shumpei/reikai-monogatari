Chapter 16: Mud 足坊 [1672]

泥足坊〔1672〕

§ 1

As Michihiko of the Divine Oshihe arrived safely on the western shore of Lake Sudaru, just at that opportune moment, Hatsuwakahime appeared, and sternly admonishing him that the propagation of Ananai-kyō must not be done in company, she left him with no choice. Princess Debisu, whom she had brought along, parted her sleeves with tears, and alone walked hastily on, anxiously pondering the lady's fate. At the juncture when he had climbed about halfway up the mountain pass of Mount Harusei, by the side of the road there was a woman lying fallen and weeping sadly. Wondering who on earth she might be, he drew near and looked closely, and lo, how could this be? Her age was the blooming spring of sixteen, and she was recognized as the beloved Buravāda-hime of Inspector Itahiko.

Console her, aid her, and nurse her, melted by deep affection, in her breast the flames stood blazing, and in the resulting anguish their hearts were the dark of love alike. Just at the time when he was holding her and weeping beneath the moon, Princess Debisu suddenly appeared and explained away the wandering of their hearts. The two awoke from the dream of love and felt repentance for their soiled hearts. When they apologized, it was not Debisu, but the divine incarnation of Konohanahime. The two met the trial of the noble Kami deep within their breasts. Leaving the pitiful maiden behind, he climbed right hastily up the mountain path where human footprints were sparse. Swept by the gale blowing across the peaks of Mount Harusei, his skirts were fluttering.

With tottering steps he climbed to the summit, and when he looked, three unfamiliar men were lurking in the tree shadows of the hazy night, talking of something in loud voices. Michihiko thought within his heart: these are entirely mountain bandits who lie in wait for passing people, and for the sake of stealing treasures, they must be holding a consultation on wicked matters. He sat down in the tree shadows nearby and listened to how things were.

Kō: 'Hey, wasn't that terribly frightening? That fellow who just passed by, what on earth could he be? No matter how much I mustered my courage to call out and stop him, the other party was such a huge man that terror seized me, my body naturally trembled, and I could not do a thing. When one meets such a fellow, even Mud-sama is utterly useless, isn't he?'

Otsu: 'Well, that fellow is undoubtedly a Dēdarabotchi! To think of the terror when he let out a voice like a great conch shell and passed by making the surrounding mountains and valleys echo—it was as if one's body were shrinking. The testicles move away somewhere. The head priest of the Heart Hospital drags his luggage and flees. The alarm bell of the Lung Hospital appeals for emergency. The peasants of Five Viscera County and Six Bowels Village shoulder their hoes and run away. Truly, the gentleman's country of the microcosm was a chaotic uproar of earthly anomalies and heavenly changes. As a result, even my face has turned pale.'

Hei: 'Ha ha ha ha, what a weak fellow. As if there could be such a small Dēdarabotchi! Speaking of a Dēdarabotchi, also called Daidōbō or Doroasubō, he is a giant who would kick down Mount Sumeru with his foot and attempt to bury the Indian ocean. He differs slightly in selection from our Daidōrōbō, but nevertheless, the fellow who just passed by is surely a bhikkhu. Your eyes were probably so startled that the black balls of your eyes moved away, leaving only the whites, and by the action of the optic nerve, he looked that big. Even if you say he is big, he is about eight feet or so. Surely he must be a bhikkhu of military background. As the saying goes, "doubt produces phantoms"; because you kept thinking it was scary, scary, you produced such an illusion. If you do not pull yourself together a bit, this trade will be no good.'

Otsu: 'Indeed, he might be a bhikkhu. His whole body was very bhikkhu-like. It is traditionally established that, called the double scare of Harusei Pass, no matter how saintly and gentlemanly a thief may be, he is bound to be startled once or twice at this pass. The propagation emissary of Ananai-kyō went chanting, "If you do not steady your torso and tighten your bellyband, the surprise box will open! Before long you will be startled and get dizzy! Hum, many people stretching out will be made!" Unless we get used to a bit of startling, we cannot operate with twelve-fold activity when it really counts. No matter what, you two are novices, so it cannot be helped.'

甲『オイ、随分恐ろしかつたぢやないか。今通つた奴は、一体ありや何だらうな。何程勇気を出して呼びとめようと思つても、あまり先方が大きな男だものだから、怖気がついて、自然に身体が慄ひ、どうする事も出来なかつたのだ。あんな奴に出会ふと泥さまもサツパリ駄目だのう』

乙『うん、彼奴ア何でもデーダラボッチに違ひないぞ。大きな法螺貝の様な声を出しやがつて、四辺の山や谷を響かして通りやがつた時の怖さと云つたら、体が縮まる様だつた。睾丸は、何処かへ転宅する。心臓院の庵主さまは荷物を引担げて遁走する。肺臓院の半鐘は急を訴へる。五臓郡六腑村の百姓は鍬を担げて逃げ出す。本当に小宇宙の君子国は、地異天変の乱痴気騒ぎだつたよ。その結果、俺の顔まで真青になつて了つたよ』

丙『ハヽヽヽヽ、弱い奴だな。あんな小さいデーダラボッチがあるかい。デーダラボッチと云へば大道坊とも泥足坊とも別称して、スメール山を足で蹴り倒し、印度の海を埋めようとするやうな大道者だ。俺達の大道路妨とはチツとは選を異にしてゐるが、然し乍ら今通つた奴は屹度比丘に違ひないぞ。貴様の目はあんまり、びつくりして目の黒玉が転宅して、白目許りになり、視神経の作用で、さう大きく見えたのだらう。大きいと云つても八尺位のものだ。キツト彼れは軍人上りの比丘に違ひないわ。疑心暗鬼を生ずと云つて、恐い恐いと思つてるから、そんな幻映を生じたのだ。チツとしつかりせぬと此商売は駄目だぞ』

乙『成程比丘かも知れぬ。体中が比丘々々しよつた。ハルセイ峠の二度ビツクリと云つて、如何に聖人君子の泥棒さまでも、此峠丈けは一度や二度はビツクリする事はあると、昔から定評があるのだ。三五教の宣伝使が「胴を据ゑ、腹帯をしめて居らぬと、ビツクリ箱が開くぞよ、今にビツクリして目まひが来るぞよ。フン延びる人民沢山出来るぞよ」と謡ひもつて通りよつた。チとビツクリに慣れて置かぬと、吾々の商売は真逆の時に、十二分の活動が出来ぬからのう。何と云つても貴様等二人は新米だから仕方がないわ』

§ 2

Kō: 'Hey, anyway, isn't there work at all tonight? Just as we thought a nice-looking fellow had come, he passed by shouting in a voice that nearly startled our liver out, saying he is Itahiko the Sendenshi. This time, thinking we would do a job since the footsteps of four or five people were heard, when we lay in wait in the shade of a tree with twisted headbands, a priest like a Dēdarabotchi passes by. Isn't this really bad luck? Novices like us would totally be done for if we encountered a fellow like that. If someone young, weak-footed, or even a woman happened to pass by, it would be convenient, though, ah.'

Otsu: 'That is so. Our business deals with humans. Humans are not just men. There are old men and old women. Sometimes there might be young women. If you rashly reach out from a small opening, you will fail, so wait here until a good bird comes.'

Kō: 'Painful for the waiting self is the boat off the offing, Truly there is no way to send this yearning, Chi-tsu-tsu-shyan, shyan, shyan, Ah-ha-ha-ha-ha-ha.'

Hei: 'Idiot, what are you spacing out for? Isn't it Senkiitsuki?'

Kō: 'What, I am just singing a song or two to temper my liver and make it big. An eiyū has leisurely days, and like the great sky, he is relaxed and possesses composure.'

Otsu: 'Hmph, well you do say so. Such a foolish song coming from a mouth like an anglerfish, it's a wonder you could sing it. Your way of talking is just like an otter. When you eat rice, you pucker your mouth like a fox, your nose twists, and your whole face throbs just like a horse's, you see. And you move only your head wildly, and from your forehead up it vibrates vertically, which is such a bizarre physiognomy that even a thief's threatening words are completely useless. Instead of being startled, the other side just laughs at you; not only when eating rice, but even if you say a single word your whole face throbs vertically and horizontally, so you are such a rare article that you are totally useless. Rather than that, you should quit being a thief, become a showman in the back streets of the capital of Haruna, and perhaps you might draw popularity with facial stunts. Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha.'

Kō: 'Hey, doing an inventory of people's faces up on a mountain like this, don't make too much of a fool of me. Even so, as a thief-sama, depending on the opponent, my glare carries weight. Well, don't blame my past, but watch my future activities. Saying, "Were such people once such persons?", I'll show you a big enterprise that will make you guys surprised.'

Otsu: 'Hmph, after seeing your handiwork, I'll let you tell me about those commissioned deeds.'

Mikotohiko had been listening to the conversation of the three men from the shadow of a tree, finding it amusing.

Mikotohiko (Soliloquy): 'However, Burawūdahime is surely coming up here from behind. She will certainly be stripped naked by these three fellows and may suffer outrage, so I shall wait, hiding in this shade of a tree, until Burawūda-sama safely passes through here. If the situation looks urgent, I can just scare them away by saying he is a Dēdarabotchi. Yeah, that's right, that's right.'

So nodding to himself, he held his breath and stood by.

Just at that moment, the slender, gentle propaganda song of a woman could be heard. Straining their ears, the gang of thieves silently watched the situation.
