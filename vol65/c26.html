Chapter 26: 七福 Kami [1682]

七福神〔1682〕

§ 1

Flanked on either side by Michihiko and Yasuhiko, Hinodewake no Mikoto led the party of Hatsuwakahime, letting hundreds of five-colored banners flutter in the wind as they advanced into the blooming Garden of Gethsemane. To honor the achievement of Tamakuniwake's party in bringing and presenting the three jewels of the Dragon King, a welcome banquet was held especially by the command of Haniyasuhikonomikoto. In the Garden of Gethsemane, various crops, music, and theatrical performances were being actively held. Meanwhile, from Mount Caucasus, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto had led many temple officials and arrived at the sacred place early, two or three days before.

Tamakuniwake and Masumihiko had been warned by Hatsuwakahime along the way, 'The sacred place is a terrifying place masked as a fine one,' and though they had kept their muscles tense, they were thunderstruck by this extravagant welcome and stood dumbfounded as if in a dream. Seeing and hearing only things beyond their expectations, they did not even know what to say and proceeded silently behind Hatsuwakahime. The god Hinodewake pointed to a makeshift building,

Hinodewake: 'Now everyone, to console your hardships, various entertainments have been arranged by the divine will. From now on in this building, a play called the Treasure Ship of the Seven Gods of Fortune will begin, so please relax and watch at your leisure.'

Tamakuniwake, though finding it contrary to his expectations,

Tamakuniwake: 'No, why, how could we ever be so at ease? Until we safely hand over this sacred jewel entrusted to Masumihiko to the god, this is no place for plays. Please forgive me earnestly for this alone. It would be terrible if we grew careless and capsized at ninety-nine percent, you see.'

He remained on guard and stiffened his body to the utmost.

Hinodewake: 'Please, please do not worry at all. Just as you see, a divine play called the Treasure Ship of Treasures is being held to celebrate your arrival. Since you also clasped the treasure, crossed the Jordan River by boat, and entered this sacred place, you are the protagonists of the treasure ship.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Yes. Masumihiko, what do you think? Somehow, the Arch-Instructor's words are a bit hard for me to comprehend, you know.'

Masumihiko: 'Teacher, perhaps this is being played with by the gods. In any case, let us make our apologies and hurry to hand over this jewel to the god Haniyasuhikonomikoto, shall we not? Without doing so, I have no heart to watch a play.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'There is no need for worry at all. Please step inside and see. No, no, you yourselves must become the actors. Presently Harumichi-koji, Itahiko, Mitsuhiko, Princess Davis, and Bravada-sama are appearing, so we plan for you to become the Seven Gods of Fortune. Harumichi-koji-sama is Hotei, Tamakuniwake-sama is Jurojin, Masumihiko-sama is Bishamonten, Itahiko-sama is Daikokuten, Mitsuhiko-sama is Ebisu, and then, Princess Davis-sama is Benzaiten; thus your respective roles are fixed precisely. Now please enter the green room. The likes of us are going to watch. Since you are the ones who will actually play the actors, please perform this thinking of it as divine work as well.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Well now, I cannot comprehend it in the least. If it is an order, I do not mind becoming a sudden actor, but I cannot understand the lines at all, you see.'

Hinodewake: 'You do not need any lines. At that time the god will possess you and borrow your mouth to speak, so you only need to consent.'

Masumihiko: 'Say, teacher, or rather Jurojin-sama, it is the god's command; shall we become thousand-ryo actors?'

Tamakuniwake: 'No matter what anyone says, if it is the god's command, there is no way we can turn our backs on it. Let us undertake it. And Mitsuhiko and Itahiko, are they already appearing over here? I should think that they would somehow be two or three days later than us, you know.'

Before they knew it, Chidaukoji, Michihiko, Itahiko, Princess Davis, Princess Bravāda, and other people had gathered, and all had assumed the forms of the Shichifukujin. At last, here the celebratory divine play of the treasure ship of the Shichifukujin was performed. Numerous kami-officials and believers had gathered within this wide building to the extent that there was no room left to stand, watching with delight and praising their marvelous techniques to the utmost.
