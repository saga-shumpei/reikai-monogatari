Chapter 4: 不聞銃 [1660]

不聞銃〔1660〕

§ 1

Torakumayama was erupting fiercely day and night without distinction between Chiuya. And from time to time it began to rumble, and the shaking of the earth occurred three or four times a day. It was the custom of the Riyōnin Sērud and Hāru to order their Kobun to sweet-talk Tabibito, entice them into this Ihaya, strip them Matsupadaka, sever their Shishigotai, and throw them into this Funkuwakō to be burned up. When Sērud woke up at dawn, his drunkenness had already worn off. However, remembering that he had collided with Hāru Yūbe and Hāru had been brought to a state of Kiushiitsushiyō, thinking it would be Taihen if he were to be restored to Sosei and that he should finish him off right now, he took up his Nukimi himself and went before the dim Raugoku, but Hāru's figure had vanished without trace or shadow. In reality, Hāru, listening to Sērud's drunk Hitorigoto, had thought, 'Koitsu is Taihen. If I remain in such a place, I know not Itsu I may lose my own Inochi,' and bandaging his head with Hautai and leaning on a staff, he took advantage of the Yain to escape this Ihaya.

Sērud stepped forward all alone before the Raugoku of the Futari women, softening his expression and speaking in a cat-purring voice, opened the door of the Raugoku himself, and unbefitting his beard-shaggy face,

Sērud: 'Un, so you were the gentlewoman Debisuhime-sama; you must indeed have suffered great hardship. Anyway, that fellow Hāru committed such cruel acts by kidnapping you innocent ones, and I too pitied you and racked my brains day and night wanting to save you somehow, but since he is a formidable warrior of a hundred ranks in Judo, though I am the general, the actual power was held by Hāru, so it could not be helped. Yūbe I unexpectedly avenged your enemy, so to speak I am the parent of your Inochi; well, are you glad? The woman named Burawāda is also pitiful, but that fellow seemed to be speaking sweet things with Hāru, so peradventure they may have shared mutual affection. At any rate, she has a pale, melon-seed face... so leaving her for later, I shall save you whom I love the most first. Well, are you glad? You must be truly glad.'

Debisu: 'I am grateful for your kindness, but I do not feel very glad. Somehow your face has become frightful to me.'

Sērud: 'That is a misunderstanding on your part. Such a fellow like Hāru, who has a gentle face where one knows not whether he is woman or man, is the kind to murder people or be a great thief. A fellow with a dark, beard-shaggy face like mine has rather a beautiful heart. Come, without saying such things, since I am the parent of your Inochi, you had better come out at once.'

Debisu: 'I dislike leaving this place. I shall become the Ihayahime-kami eternally for absolute eternity and become the master of Torakumayama, so please do not say such senseless things. Rather than that, I shall be satisfied if you take my Inochi even a moment sooner. Far better to die as I am than to be pulled out from such a place and become your toy.'

Sērud: 'This is indeed what one calls a bad intention; as long as there is Inochi, there is hope. Without saying such incomprehensible things, why not listen to what I say? You might be able to see plenty of interesting and glad things again.'

From the adjacent room, Burawāda said in a slender voice,

Burawāda: 'Elder sister, Debisuhime-sama, you must not come out. Rather than becoming prey for a lion, let us go freely and unhindered from here to heaven.'

Debisu: 'Ah, Burawāda-sama, is that your thought as well? Then let us both resolve to settle in this Ihaya eternally. I shall become Ihayahime, so since you are young of age, please become Konohanasakuyahime. And I shall protect the lifespan of the world's people, and you please become the Kami who protects the peace of the world. That is my heart's desire.'

Bura: 'Let us do so. You must never come out.'

Sale: 'Oh, what a strict alliance you have made. Hey, you two, I will let you out together, so do you not feel like meeting each other once in good friendship?'

Bura: 'I also wish to see my elder sister's face once, so although it is disagreeable, I shall grant your wish and come out for you.'

Sale: 'Eh, what a helpless Himegoze.'

Saying this, he wrenched open the doors of both prisons with a clattering sound. The two were so joyful they could nearly fly, and leaving the prison, they embraced each other, weeping tears of joy.

Bura: 'Sister, I have longed to see you.'

Davis: 'Bura-sama, I have longed to see your face.'

Thus they clung to one another.

Sale, seeing this, laughed loudly on purpose.

Sale: 'Ahahaha, first of all, how auspicious, how auspicious. It is as if the Ame-no-Ihato has opened. Ah, how delightful! Ah, how refreshing! Oke! Has the Megamisama Athena appeared again in the world by the Shirikumenaha of Sale? The Antan heavens and earth have now cleared throughout the Rikugafu, becoming a scene of Kuōmiyō-Henzeu in the Jitsupau-Sekai. In a manner of speaking, this Sale is just like the Kami Tajikarao of Ame. Now, the two Himegamisama, please come to my living room. From now, the three of us, man and women, shall boisterously perform a sacred Kagura.'

The two, while exchanging glances... thinking they would make this fellow drink sake and manipulate him, proceeded to Sale's living room. Sale showed a look of great triumph across his whole face.

Sale: 'Ah, truly, one man and two women is an inconvenient arrangement. I wonder if it might be possible to have one Hime-sama wait in a separate room?'

Bura: 'Sister, how unpleasant, is it not? You and I are fratino bound together by Kami. From now, shall the two of us not form a Dōmei and launch a pincer attack on Sale-sama? I wonder if the preparations for the cannonballs are complete.'

Davis: 'There is also the new-model Mitsuhiko cannon, and I also possess the extremely sturdy elbow-gun Kikazu-jū, hohoho.'

Bura: 'Even I have prepared plenty of the latest Itahiko cannons and Eppappa guns, and for the love of Boss Sale, I have prepared many Kikazu-jū, so it is quite safe. Say, Mr. Boss of the thieves, are the war preparations also complete on your side?'

Sale: 'How could they not be prepared? In everything in this world, it is the teaching of Kami to harmonize through words. I am a former captain of Brahmanism who subdues all the people of the world by replacing swords with plows and hoes, substituting cannons with Kototama, and changing the sound of bombs into music; therefore, I shall return the title of thief to the Lady General. I have the shamisen, the king of music, tucked in my fuemūro; when I pluck it a little, it goes chin-chin-chin and the flavor becomes good. It produces a very fine tone. After all, it is the highest quality red sandalwood neck covered with a face of gold.'

Davis: 'That must be wrong. Yours must make a tsun-tsun sound like a Jōruri shamisen. After all, it is a specially made thick-neck, is it not? Hohoho.'

Sale: 'Ahaha, if that is so, shall I have you listen to the sound of the thick-neck?'

Bura: 'Sister, we do not wish to hear either a thick-neck or a thin-neck, do we?'

Sale: 'Then shall I make it the beat of a drum? If you dislike that, how about the shakuhachi?'

Davis: 'Ohoho. How disgusting. Look at that slovenly face. Say, say, Boss-sama, your drool is flowing. Oh, how unsightly. You are like a cow.'

Sale: 'Eh, now, joking aside, I have a direct negotiation with you. You will surely listen to me, will you not?'

Davis: 'Since those are your words, of course I shall listen; that is why I have ears.'

Sale: 'No, that is most gratifying. Be sure to listen to me. There is no mistake, is there?'

Sale, with a Fukigen face:

Sale: 'You women, do you make Chōrō of me? Did you not just say you would listen to anything?'

Davis: 'Did I not listen exactly as per our Yakusoku? It is because I listened that I am giving this Ōtafu. Whether we Saiyō your Kotoba or not is the Jiiu of us Riyōnin. To harbor Renbo for a Tenniyo-like Bijin—is that not a bit beyond your station? Please have a Gosaudan with your Gomensau. Oh, what a scary face... It is a countenance that looks as if a single stone of rice would cost a hundred gold pieces.'

Sale: 'Ugh, you useless wretches! At last the cord of my bag of patience has snapped. As an act of revenge for unrequited love, I shall cast you back into the dungeon and torment you to death! Ho there, my henchmen! Seize these two women and cast them into the dungeon!'

He shouted with such a frenzy of rage that the interior of the prison seemed to split asunder. From beneath the sound of his voice, seven or eight henchmen came rushing in, and instantly taking the two by their hands and feet, they carried them off with great effort and threw them into the former dungeon. Afterward, Sale heaved a sigh.

'Ah, love alone is something that cannot be won by violence, nor by the power of money, nor by threats, nor by absolute authority. Nevertheless, once a man has spoken, if I leave things as they are, I shall somehow feel ashamed in my own heart. Even if I must subject them to the torture of water or the torture of fire, if I do not make them submit to my will, it will affect my authority as an Oyabun. Being in a position to command many followers, if I cannot even have my way with two frail women, then I am already finished. All right, the best thing is to subject them to the torture of starvation. Even a lion, a tiger, or a wolf will listen to what a human says if only they are tormented by food. This must be limited to the torture of starvation.'

Thus he spoke to himself. It was there that Tar, one of his henchmen, returned in great haste.

Tar: 'Master, master! I have just found a good bird.'

Sale: 'What? You say you found a good bird—is it a Bijin or a Kanemochi? Which is it?'

Tar: 'Yes, I have conspired with both Yaku and Ale and have successfully lured in the party of Jidō-koji. What shall we do?'

Sale: 'Jidō-koji? Is that not that General Kishunwake? If it is that man, he surely must possess money. Since he is a brave general endowed with both wisdom and courage, we must not be careless. At any rate, deceive him skillfully and cast him into the dungeon.'

Tar: 'Yes, I understand. However, there are five in the party, and four of them are Dorobō who have reformed. Even Jidō-koji is saying bold things, claiming he will make the cavern Oyabun, Captain Sale, and all the rest of the fellows reform through the Path of Sincerity or the Law of Adultery; so please, come here for a moment.'

Sale: 'No, no matter Nanihodo of a Dorobō no Oyabun I am, I cannot personally throw in a General whom I once looked up to as my master. Let all the henchmen gather and throw all five of those fellows in. Then, snatch away their belongings one by one and finish them off sweetly. Do you hear? Be sure not to blunder!'

セール『イヤ、おれは何程盗賊の親分でも、一旦主人と仰いだ将軍を、手づから放り込む事は出来ぬ。乾児が全部集まつて、五人の奴を皆ブチ込んで了へ。そして弗々と持物を引たくり、甘く片付けて了うのだ。可いか、キツとぬかるでないぞ』

§ 4

Tāru: 'Yes, I have understood. Then shall I throw all five of them into the Daiichi Raugoku?'

Sēru: 'Hmm, the Daiichi would be good. You must not give them even a single cup of water. And where are the two, Yaku and Ēru?'

Tāru: 'Yes, they are standing on both sides of Chidaukoji.'

Sēru: 'Ah, is that so? They have done a good thing. It is their first Tegara. I must praise them. However, what has become of Emu, who went together with you?'

Tāru: 'That Emu, you say? That aitsu suddenly fell into the way of good; having been preached to about things like Daugi and Shinri by Chidaukoji in the Shinrin at the foot of the mountain, he shed tears, wagged his tail, ducked his head, and vanished somewhere into Mushō. He is truly a Fugahi fellow. Since that aitsu has not yet attained mastery in Dorobōgaku, he finally lost his grace. There was no way to help him, so I let him go.'

Sēru: 'What, that is Taihen! If a fellow like Emu escapes this Dantai and goes around blabbing everywhere, there is no telling when the Torite will appear. Why did you not bring Emu back? You have done a Baka thing.'

Tāru: 'Even so, you see, there were Sannin on this side, and on the Mukau side were five Gauketsu; thinking that if I dealt with the likes of Emu, I would ruin the essential jewels, I did not pursue him.'

Sēru: 'It cannot be helped. I shall not blame the past, but from now on you had better be mindful. Now, quickly throw those five fellows in.'

Tāru swiftly left this place and skillfully threw the four including Chidaukoji into the Daiichi Raugoku. Chidaukoji, as if anticipating something in his heart, very cheerfully led the four and entered the Raugoku without any resistance. The two, Yaku and Ēru, had already by today reformed their thief-hearts and become allies of Chidaukoji. However, among the thief gang including Sēru, Tāru, and others, there was not a single person who knew this. Therefore, that Yaku and Ēru were ordered to be the jailers of the five was an extraordinary convenience for Chidaukoji.
