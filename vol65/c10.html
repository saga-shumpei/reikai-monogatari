Chapter 10: 赤酒の Voice [1666]

赤酒の声〔1666〕

§ 1

That night cleared up brightly.

Inside the dimly lit prison, Bravada read the letter from Chidaukoji.

The text was written as follows:

Item: I am Chidaukoji, whom you know well.

Hearing that my former subordinates, Sale and Haar, along with numerous followers, were acting as mountain bandits in the cavern of Torakumayama, I took upon myself the responsibility before the gods of Tenchi and deliberately became a prisoner, now cast into the first prison.

I have learned that you two ladies have also been captured in this mountain stronghold and are suffering day and night from Sale's outrageous romantic advances.

Accordingly, as I also have various plans in mind, for these next day or two, please feign indifference with words and await the time, guarding your chastity to the very end.

Since I will surely rescue you, you are ladies of resource in all things, and though I imagine it cannot be so, should you happen to act rashly, it will ruin Chidaukoji's painstaking efforts.

Therefore, for these next day or two, please adopt a flexible attitude according to the circumstances.

From Chidaukoji

To both ladies

It was written thus.

Both women looked at the same letter, smiled bitterly, and rejoiced that the time for their rescue had arrived.

Then, eagerly hoping to realize somehow the elaborate performance they had spoken of last night, they waited with the yearning of a thousand autumns, but that night passed without a single sound, and at last the morning broke.

Bravada spoke in a soft voice:

Bravada: 'Even the perpetual dark night will soon clear away, As the shadow of the saving Kami becomes visible.'
