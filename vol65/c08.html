Chidaukoji, together with the four men Beru, Batto, Kākusu, and Bēsu, was cast into a dim stone raugoku, and by the strict genmei of Sēru, they were cut off from inshiyokubutsu. However, the rauban Yaku and Yēru, who had surrendered to Chidaukoji in their hearts, labored diligently in various ways to supply food and drink for the gonin, and afforded them every possible bengi. Their oyabun Sēru trusted Yaku and Yēru deeply and never once came to visit the raugoku. Moreover, feeling a certain sense of shame and dread toward Chidaukoji, whom he had revered as his daishiyōgun, he did not wish to meet him. Leaving Chidaukoji's itsutai entirely in the hands of the riyōnin, he dispatched his other kobun in all directions, pouring his zenriyoku into the collection of zaibutsu, while on itsupau, he was completely absorbed in the sole desire to somehow make the two bijin his own.

The riyōnin Yaku and Yēru secretly performed the role of postal carriers between Chidaukoji, Buravūda, and Hime Debisu. Inside the raugoku, with Chidaukoji in the center, the four members of the reformed group whispered together about various matters.

Beru: 'Hey, haven't we all started to feel just a little bit uneasy? We're no better than caged birds with our freedom bound, and since the oyabun holds the power of life and death over us, no matter how much hōryoku Chidaukoji-sama possesses, unless he smashes this raugoku with the power of sutras, it's a dangerous situation, isn't it? Chidaukoji-sama is completely unconcerned, saying it's a rest for body and soul, and is sleeping so soundly as if he'd checked into a hot spring, but we just can't feel that way at all. Don't you guys have some scheme to break out of here?'

Batto: 'Don't worry about it. If we say the word, Yaku and Yēru are acting as rauban, so they'll open the door anytime. The reason he isn't opening it now is because he has a deep plan in mind. After all, with this many people gathered together, if we do something clumsy, we'll end up catching neither the horse nor the fly. Chidaukoji-sama is counting on that and sleeping in peace. Even if we were locked up here for three or four days, we could endure it without eating a single piece of a bread. Since everyone here is from a military background and all are capable, it wouldn't do any good for just four or five of us to riot.'

§ 4

Debisu: 'Hontau ni nee, watashi mo riuin ga sagarimashita wa.'

Yēru is puzzled and tilts his head as he walks back through the dark tunnel.

Sēru: 'Ahahaha, hey you two, you also suffered some persecution from Chidaukoji, didn't you? Good, since I will take your revenge, you had better set your minds at ease. In return, you will listen to what I say, won't you?'

Bura: 'Anesama, what shall we do? Truly, unless we wipe out our grudge here once and for all, won't a woman's stubborn pride fail to stand?'

Debisu: 'That is so indeed. I would be so happy if you could somehow have that fellow thrown in here and make the two of us torment him to death from both sides. If you do that, I would gladly comply with the oyabun-sama's orders, though.'

Bura: 'If that is your thought, Anesama, I am in agreement too. He is the fellow who killed my husband. I hate him, I hate him so much I cannot bear it, and if you would have us take that revenge through the oyakata-sama's sympathy, I think I would listen to whatever the oyakata says to repay the favor.'

Sēru: 'Utsufunfun. Hey woman, your wish has been granted. In return, you will listen to whatever I say, won't you?'

Both women at once:

'Hei, hei, of course we will listen! How could we possibly not listen to what you say... Since we have two fine ears, after all...'

They followed behind in low voices. Sēru even changed his voice and said,

Sēru: 'Un, un, yoshi, yoshi. Because it is your cute wish, I will grant you anything. You had better take your fill of revenge as you like.'

Both women at once:

'Thank you very much. We beg of you even a moment sooner.'

Sēru:

'Yoshiyoshi, from now on I will order the rauban to drag Chidau here, so you had better do as you like with him.'

So saying, he casts those words aside and returns to his own living room.

Sēru returns to his own living room, and smiling broadly, he mutters to himself,

Sēru: 'Yea, with this my desire is also fulfilled. Delicious, delicious, as expected, women are no good unless you give them some sort of stimulus. I haven't searched his pockets yet, but that Chidau is no doubt holding a lot of maru. Moreover, since fellows with a grudge against him have come, it is convenient for me too. Anyway, she is a beauty resembling both the moon and flowers. Ehehuhu. Flowers in both hands refers to me. The saying that one is led by the hand of two wives and crosses a bridge of gold now seems about to be realized. Utsufunfun.'

Taking advantage of the absence of anyone around, he is lost in solitary laughter.
