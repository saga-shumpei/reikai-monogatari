Chapter 23: 義侠 [1679]

義侠〔1679〕

§ 1

The villagers of Senseikiyō had hitherto seen all the landed property of the tochi become the possession of the Bātara family, and each of them had become a hisan kosakunin, leading a truly hisan kurashi while dwelling in Senseikiyō. However, from a gūzen dekigoto, they were given as much sanrin and tahata as they could cultivate, and kiki they enjoyed their work, while also respecting the widow Sumanā like a kami. Three men, Ko, Otsu, and Hei, went out into the sanrin to gather firewood, sat down under the kokage, and indulged in zatsudan.

Ko: 'Oi,yotahiko-san, doesn't the sekai change indeed as it changes? Although we are jiumin of Senseikiyō envied by the sekai, from the generation of our sof we fell into the kiyōgū of wretched kosakunin, delivering daibubun of the rice we labored to grow to the Bātara family, not a single hitotsubu of the kanjin rice entering our mouths, and we have connected our romei by using the mugirui of the urasaku as hanmai; yet from kotoshi, the fact that we are able to receive rice is entirely thanks to Kami. Because of this we can probably lead a kurashi befitting Senseikiyō, truly a happy thing, ah. The wicked people of Tēra have completely reformed recently, and even thieves have come to till the tahata like that, so the world has truly changed, ah.'

Otsu: 'No matter what is said, it was because there were those who monopolized the gifts of Heaven that we suffered. If things come to this, no grievances will arise in the village, and the way of loving Kami and loving people will probably be perfectly practiced. No matter how much one is told to have faith, in a state where there is not even the rice to eat today, faith cannot be practiced, neither can one help people no matter how troubled they are, and one is inclined to knock down and take even what people possess, but now that things have become equal in this way, evil deeds and evil thoughts will probably be severed. Tēra, that fellow, was the most hated person in the whole village, but as the saying goes that good and evil are not two, because that fellow plotted such evil deeds, we have turned out so splendidly. Evil people cannot be hated either. To say that evil changes into good and good changes into evil is probably what they spoke of mostly like this.'

Hei: 'Tēra, that fellow, has tormented us terribly until now, but that fellow too will probably make atonement for all his sins with this. He did a rather clever thing. Dragging the thieves of Torakumayama, making them do such rough work, and further trying to make thieves into officials to usurp the property is a feat we could not possibly have imagined. Thieves do not seem to be evil people from the beginning, and are they not working diligently from morning till night just like that? If we too had a little more capability, we might have entered their group, you see.'

Ko: 'A weak-spirited man like you is roughly about on the level of a beggar, ah. If it were me, I would surely have become something like a boss of thieves, if another year or two had passed just as things were, perhaps. The truth of the matter is that I had been thinking about it since two or three years ago, but I could not go so far as to consult about such a thing carelessly, and even with just a single thief I felt helpless, and weeping I had lived up until now. Nevertheless, Tēra, that fellow, before the teacher of Misoshikō, saying, "Please forgive me as I will become the servant of the villagers and devote myself," but recently isn't his bearing a bit rough? Twitching his nose, he brags, saying, "The fact that you people have become this fortunate is also thanks to me." Since he acts as a bantō and tries to press down upon our heads, it is infuriating. It might be better to summon the youth corps and demote Tēra back to a mere bantō so that he does not grow arrogant.'

Otsu: 'Don't talk foolishly; in a world where things like the equality of the four classes or the movement for equity are flourishingly conducted, one should not be bound by grotesque ideas forever. Tēra is like a benefactor to us. The fact that the Bātara family met with such a plight is also some kind of karmic affinity, the retribution for wringing the fat and blood of us people and living in extreme luxury. The resentment of three hundred people of Senseikiyō coagulated and such a tragic event suddenly broke out. Yoshikohime-sama too was originally born from the womb of a commoner, and was picked up by the Bātara family to become the mistress, so she took the side of us commoners. A thing called a human being is one in whom sympathy does not arise unless one has undergone hardships oneself. Among those who talk about universal love or sympathy, there is not a single person who preserves a heart of universal love or sympathy. They are nothing but fake gentlemen and fake sages who cover up the world with the masks of universal love and sympathy. That bikuni-sama is truly our savior-sama.'

原文（近代文語）

仙聖郷の村人は、今迄土地の財産は全部バータラ家のものとなり、何れも悲惨な小作人となり、仙聖郷に住み乍ら、実に悲惨の生活を送つて居た。所が偶然の出来事より山林田畑を作れるだけ与へられて、嬉々として其業を楽しみ、又未亡人のスマナーを神の如くに敬つて居た。甲乙丙三人の男は山林に薪をからむと出で往き、木蔭に腰打ち卸し、雑談に耽つて居た。

甲『オイ、世も変れば変るものぢやないか。俺達は世界から羨まれる仙聖郷の住民であり乍ら、祖父の代からみじめな小作人の境遇に陥り、働いて作つた米の大部分はバータラ家に納め、肝腎の米は一粒も口に入らず、裏作の麦類を飯米として露命を繋いで来たが、今年から、お米を頂く事が出来るやうになつたのも、全く神様のおかげだよ。是でこそ仙聖郷相当の生活が出来る事だらう、本当に嬉しい事だなア。テーラの悪人も此頃すつかり改心するし、泥棒迄があのやうに田畑を耕すやうになつたのだから、世の中も変つたものだなア』

乙『何と云ふても天与の産物を独占する者があつた為め、吾々は苦しんで来たのだ。斯うなつたら村に苦情も起らず、愛神愛人の道も完全に行はるるであらう。何程信心せよと云ふても、今日食ふ飯も無いやうの事では信心も出来ず、人が何程困つとつても助ける事も出来ず、人の持つて居るものでも、叩き落して取りたいやうに思ふものだが、かうして平等になつた以上は、悪事悪念は断たれるであらう。テーラの奴、村中の憎まれ者だつたが、善悪不二と云ふて、あの奴が彼様悪事を企みよつたものだから、吾々はこんな結構に成つたのだ。悪人だつて憎めぬよ。悪が変じて善となり、善が変じて悪となると云ふのは、大方こんな事を云ふたのだらうよ』

丙『テーラの奴、随分俺達を今迄苦しめよつたが、彼奴もこれで一切の罪亡ぼしになるであらう。仲々気が利いた事をやつた。虎熊山の泥棒を引ぱつて来て、あんな荒い仕事をやり、又泥棒を役人に仕立てて、財産を横領しようとしたのは、吾々の到底考へ付かない芸当だ。泥棒だつて元からの悪人ではないと見えて、あの通り朝から晩迄神妙に働いて居るぢやないか、俺達も、も些し甲斐性があつたら、彼れ達の仲間に入つて居たかも知れないからなア』

甲『お前のやうな気の弱い男はまア乞食位のものだなア。俺だつたら屹度泥棒の親分位に、あのままでモウ一二年経つたなら成るかも分らなかつたよ。実の所は二三年前から考へて居たが、そんな事をウツカリ相談する訳にも行かず、一人の泥棒も心細いなり、泣き泣き今迄暮れて来たのだ。併し乍らテーラの奴、三五教の先生の前で、「村人の僕となつて尽しますから許して下さい」といつて置き乍ら、此頃少し見幕が荒いぢやないか。鼻をピコづかせ乍ら「お前達がこんな結構になつたのも俺のお蔭だ」と云ふて威張りやがる。番太のくせに俺達の頭をおさへようとするのだから業腹だ。一つ青年隊を召集して、テーラを元の通り番太にこき卸した方が慢心せいでよいかも知れないよ』

乙『馬鹿云ふな、四民平等とか、衡平運動とかが、盛に行はれて居る世の中に、いつ迄も怪体な思想に因へられて居るものぢやない。テーラは吾々の恩人見たやうなものだ。バータラ家があんな目に遇つたのも、何かの因縁だらう、吾々人民の膏血を絞り、贅沢三昧に暮して来た報いだ。仙聖郷三百人の恨が凝結してあんな惨事が突発したのだ。スマナー姫様も、元は貧民の腹から生れ、バータラ家に拾ひあげられて奥様になられたのだから、吾々貧民の味方をして下さつたのだ。人間と云ふものは自分が難儀をして来ねば同情の起るものではない。博愛だとか、同情だとか云つて居る者に、一人も博愛心や同情心を保つて居るものはない。世の中を博愛と、同情の仮面を被りて胡麻かす贋君子、贋聖人ばかりだ。あの比丘尼様こそは本当に吾々の救世主様だ』

§ 2

As they thus conversed, the one who came reeling up in a state of utter Hebereke drunkenness was Taylor, who had once repented out of fear.

Taylor rolled his round eyes wildly, shrugged his shoulders, and with Kuchigitana foul language,

Taylor: 'Hey, who on earth is that Gaki staying over there? Behold, the Savior of Senseikiyō, Lord Taylor, is passing through! Will you not quickly come here and perform Dogeza? Is it not thanks to this person that you folks received a share of your relatives' property and became Jinushi for the first time in Nanbiyakunen? If I were to make a single Guwanba effort, all of your relatives' things would become mine, but precisely because I am the Savior, I arranged matters so that they would become yours. You ungrateful Onshi wretches, always treating me like a Banta and failing to offer proper Aisatsu, aren't you?'

A: 'Look here, Taylor, what are you saying? Did you not join forces with the thieves of Torakumayama in your Futodo audacity, and attempt to drag us members of the Seinentai away on Sauzeuzai charges? The reason we became Jinushi is that the Onkei of our ancestors through Daidai generations has come to bear fruit. We are not indebted to you. If you do not reflect a little, the entire village will come to resent you. If there is anything to be feared in this world, there is nothing so terrifying as resentment and public hatred. Unless you reflect and resolve not to drink sake, the entire village will gather together and have you beaten out of the place, you incorrigible wretch.'

Taylor: 'Wh-What are you babbling about? Is there anyone in this village who would dare lay even a finger on me? If you keep rambling, shall I report you with trembling fear?'

A: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha! The place where you file reports must be the cavern of the Torakumayama thieves. Do you not know that, unhappily for you, Torakumayama has more than half exploded, and both your bosses and your friends have been reduced to smithereens? If the entire village were to submit a joint petition to the authorities regarding your misdeeds, there is no telling what kind of fate would befall you. Up until now, since we had no property, whatever we said would not be taken up by the officials, but now, as citizens who pay taxes and who have also acquired the right to vote, the officials will certainly take up even minor injustices for us. No matter how much you swagger, one person is no match for a multitude, so you would do well to withdraw quietly. Your espionage is already finished. Furthermore, put an end to such detestable business.'

Taylor: 'If you keep babbling like that, I will report your slanders to the boss of Kingres and have you tied up tightly!'

A: 'Well now, are you still dreaming of the past? Kingres is no longer a man who would deal with the likes of you. Nowadays, he is a kind friend of ours. When we met just the other day, he spoke of you and expressed deep regret. If you talk nonsense again, let us know. He said that for the sake of discipline, he would tie your feet and hang you upside down from the branch of a Sala tree. He remarked that if we did that, you might reform just a little.'

Taylor: 'Wh-What are you blathering about? I am not a fellow to be startled by such things. If you keep rambling, shall I smash in the bases of your hats one by one?'

Hardly had he spoken when he clenched his fist like a Tsubu (sea snail), cut through the wind with a buzzing sound, and went wild in an attempt to beat them down. The three of them used tree trunks as shields, dodging to the right and left while defending their bodies. Taylor, carried away by his momentum and mad with anger, pursued and chased the three around. A large man who suddenly appeared straightway seized the back of Taylor's neck firmly from behind, and with a voice like thunder,

Man: 'Look here, are you going to riot once again? I will no longer overlook your conduct.'

Taylor shrank in terror at this voice,

Taylor: 'Ah, I-I am truly sorry. Just, just, just because I ate and drank myself into a drunken stupor, I derailed. Lord Kingres, please spare me, for from this very limit I will refrain from sake and will no longer commit violence; therefore, please forgive me.'

斯く話す所へ、泥酔に酔ふてやつて来たのは、一たん恐さに改心したテーラであつた。テーラは丸い目をギヨロツと剥き出し、肩を聳やかし乍ら、口汚く、

テーラ『オイ、其処に居る餓鬼はダダ誰人だい。仙聖郷の救世主テーラ様のお通りだぞ。早く茲へ来て土下座を致さぬか。貴様達は此方様のお蔭によつて親類の財産を分けて貰ひ、何百年振りに地主となつたのぢやないか。俺が一つ頑張らうものなら、親類の此方の、皆物になるのだが、そこは救世主様だけあつて、お前たちの物になるやうに取計らつてやつたのだ。恩知らず奴、いつも俺を番太扱ひにしやがつて、碌に挨拶もせぬぢやないか』

甲『これや、テーラ、貴様は何を云ふのだ。不届き千万にも虎熊山の泥棒と腹を合せ、俺たち青年隊を騒擾罪で引張らうとしたでは無いか。吾々が地主となつたのは、先祖代々の恩恵が報うて来たのだ。恩に着せない。些と心得ないと村中が貴様を恨んで居るぞ。世の中に何が恐ろしいと言つても、恨と人気程恐ろしいものはないぞ。些と心得て酒を喰はぬやうにせねば、村中寄つて集つて、叩き払ひにせられて仕舞ふぞ。不心得者奴が』

テーラ『ナヽ何を吐しやがるんだい。此村に俺に指一本でもさへる奴があるかい。グヅグヅ吐すと、恐れ乍らと訴へてやらうか』

甲『ハヽヽヽ貴様の訴へる所は、虎熊山の泥棒の岩窟であらう。お気の毒乍ら虎熊山は半以上爆発し、貴様の親分も友達も、木端微塵になつて居るのを知らぬのか。貴様の悪行を村中が連判で上へ届けたならば、それこそ貴様は、どんな運命になるか分らぬのだ。今迄は吾々も財産が無いので、何を云つても官が取り上げて呉れなんだが、もはや、租税を納める公民となり、選挙権も獲得したのだから、官だつて屹度少々の無理だつて取り上げて呉れるのだ。何程貴様が威張つても、大勢と一人とは叶はぬから、好い加減に引込んだらよからう。貴様の偵羅もモウ駄目だ。そしてそんな憎まれる商売は止めて仕舞へ』

テーラ『グヅグヅ吐すと、キングレスの親分に貴様の悪口を報告して、フン縛つてやるぞ』

甲『こら、まだ昔の夢を見て居るのか。キングレスはもはや貴様の様な者を相手にする男ぢやないぞ。もう此頃は吾等の親切なる友達だ。此間も遇つたら貴様の事を云つて大変悔んで居た。今度グヅグヅ云つたら知らして呉れ。懲戒の為めに足を縛つて沙羅双樹の枝に俯向に吊るしてやらう。さうしたら些とは改心をするだらうと云つて居たぞ』

テーラ『ナヽ何を吐きやがるんだい。そんなことに驚く哥兄ぢやないぞ。グヅグヅ吐すと片ツ端から笠の台を張り飛ばしてやらうか』

と云ふより早く蠑螺の如き拳骨を固め、ブウブウと風を切つて撲りつけむと暴れ出した。三人は木の幹を盾に取り、右へ左へと避け乍ら、身を守つて居る。テーラは勢に乗じ怒り狂ひ、三人を逐ひかけ廻す。俄に現はれた大の男、矢庭に後からテーラの首筋をグツと握り、雷のやうな声にて、

男『これや、又しても貴様は暴れるのか。もう了見はせぬぞ』

テーラは此声に縮み上り、

テーラ『ハイ、マヽ誠に済みませぬ。チヨツ、チヨツ、チヨツと酒に喰ひ酔つたものだから脱線を致しました。キングレス様、どうぞ是限り酒も慎み、乱暴も致しませぬから、何卒許して下さい』

§ 3

Kinguresu: 'No, I cannot forgive you. No matter what you say, you are no good. Unless I drill a hole in your head, hang you upside down from a tree, and let out a little blood, your mind will probably not be set right. Now, you three people, since I have appeared, everything is all right. Come, you all, beat this fellow to your heart's content.'

The three men joyfully run to this spot.

A: 'Kinguresu-sama, you have come just in time. Since there is no particular effect in beating such a man, I do not think of torturing him, but please warn him fully so that he will not commit violence in the future.'

Kinguresu,

King: 'Yes, I understand. Look here, Tēra, if you commit violence hereafter, this is what happens.'

Before he could finish speaking, grabbing him like a cat, relying on his mighty strength, he picked him up and hurled him into the rice paddy four or five ken away. Tēra, crossing his legs and groaning, crawled on all fours across the rice field and fled like clouds and mist. From then on, Tēra became as docile as a cat, and Kinguresu was revered by the villagers as a man of mighty strength, took on the duty of subduing evil men, and was pampered in this Senseikiyō, living out his life in safety. Ah, Kamunagara Chihahe Mase.

キングレス『イヤ、許す事は出来ぬ。幾ら云つても貴様は駄目だ。頭に穴をあけ、逆さに木に吊り上げ、ちと血を出してやらぬ事には性念が入るまい。これこれ三人の方、もう私が現はれた以上は大丈夫です。サア貴方方思ふ存分、此奴を撲つてやつて下さい』

三人は喜んで此場に走り来る。

甲『キングレス様、よう来て下さいました。別にこんな男を撲つた所で、何の効もありませぬから、苦しめてやらうとは思ひませぬが、どうぞ将来乱暴をせないやうに充分誡めてやつて下さいませ』

キングレスは、

キング『ハイ、承知致しました。これやテーラ、此後乱暴を致すと此通りだぞ』

と云ふより早く猫を掴む様に、強力に任せ抓み上げ、四五間向ふの田圃の中へ放りつけた。テーラは足を打ちチガチガし乍ら、四這になつて田の中を横ぎり、雲を霞と逃げて行く。是よりテーラは猫の如くにおとなしくなり、又キングレスは里人から強力と崇められ、悪人征服の役目となり、此仙聖郷に持て囃されて一生を無事に送つた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
