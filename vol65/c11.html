Chapter 11: Great 笑裡 [1667]

大笑裡〔1667〕

§ 1

Yaku and Eru, both of them, thrust Bat, Kakusu, and Besu into the raugoku where Debisu-hime was confined, and quietly led Chidaukoji, Debisu-hime, and Burawada-hime out of the raugoku, letting them rest in a kenrau itsushitsu out of hitome, blessed by the anya, by giving them tea and bread, and advanced into the ima of Seru,

Yaku: 'Oyabunsama, exactly as gomeirei, I have thrown Burawada and Chidaukoji into Debisu's raugoku. And since I have bound Chidaukoji so tightly that he cannot make a single miugo, you may rest anshin now.'

Seru: 'Ho, Yaku and Eru, you have done well. In that case, I think I shall go and see for myself from now on.'

Yaku: 'Ah, dannasama, as the futari bijin otsushiya, they wish you and only four or five other uhayaku to attend. And because they would hate to have their faces seen, they ask that you do not light the tomoshibi, but instead kenbun—no, chiyōmon.'

Eru: 'Come, dannasama, we beg you to come quickly.'

Seru: 'Very well, very well, in that case it would be best to have only the kogashira retsuseki. Let us listen to the yōsu outside the raugoku.'

Following the gonin kogashira, Eru and Yaku together formed a row of eight outside the raugoku, listening to the yōsu inside. Using the voice of Debisu-hime, Kakusu said,

Kakusu: 'You must be Chidaukoji, whom I hate so deeply and who is hateful beyond compare. Unable to escape tennmei, you have been dragged into this raugoku and are to suffer vengeance at the hands of a delicate woman—what a pitiful fellow you are indeed. You had better resign yourself to this as an affinity from a previous life.'

Bat, using the voice of Chidaukoji, said,

Bat: 'Ha ha ha ha ha! What do you take me for, you presumptuous and self-important fool? No matter how tightly you bind me with fine cords, how much time and trouble would it take to snap ropes as frail as these? Do not do foolish things only to regret them later. I am none other than Kishunwake no Chūjō, who earned the renowned title of hero and warrior in the Brahmin army. I, who once commanded an army of three thousand with the tip of a three-inch tongue, am now a bikku of the Sanjuskyō and a shugenja with a single straw raincoat and a bamboo hat; yet the skill of my arms, honed in days of old, still remains. If you dare to touch me with even a single finger, come on, try touching me!'

Kakusu: 'Ho ho ho ho ho! What use is your retrospective whining at this stage, when there is no longer any escape... This is Debisu's long-standing grudge, come, receive a single slash of the sword. Now, Burawada-sama, are you not also seeking vengeance on your husband? Come, let us hack off both his ears indiscriminately from both sides.'

Besu, using the disguised voice of Burawada, said,

Besu: 'Without waiting for the instructions of Aneue-sama, how could I let this grudge go unavenged? In this situation, which is like breaking an egg with a boulder, this is also entirely the result of the sympathy shown by oyabunsama Seru. Come, Chidaukoji of Kishunwake, even though you are a brave general who once commanded three armies, now that things have come to this you can no longer even make a miugo. Prepare yourself for this blade imbued with all the malice in a delicate woman's arms. Come, Aneue-sama, let us do it on the count of one, two, three. You take the left ear, and I shall take the right ear... Ho ho ho, what a pleasing thing this is, one, two, three!'

Bat: 'Agh, it hurts! Aren't my ears being torn off? Isn't blood flowing? Ugh, ah, I will not say it hurts! I am a man too, ugh, ogh, ogh.'

Kakusu: 'It must hurt. I cut you so that it would hurt. It is only natural that blood should flow. But oh, what a good feeling this is!'

Besu: 'Hey there, Kishunwake, this blade of Burawada's grudge—must it not hurt at least a little? You must have realized it by now.'

Bat: 'Well, truly, it is not without pain, hee hee hee hee!'

She uttered the last word like thunder. Sale, believing the two women completely, listened to their words in silence. Bell: 'Sister, shall we finish him off with one blow now?'

Kirkus: 'Let us do so. Now, Chidaukoji, I shall stab your Gontō. Where is your throat?'

Kirkus: 'You stubborn one... taste the sharpness of Hime Davis's arm.'

'Kyaa!' she cried, and then there was a sound of flapping, and she did not utter another word.

Kirkus: 'Ho ho ho, the grudge of days past, the revenge for my sister's husband, have you realized it now, Namu Tonshō Bodai Chidaukoji?'

Base: 'Husband's revenge, you must have realized it now. Namu Brahman Taishaku Jizaiten, I gratefully thank you for your divine virtue.'

Bat: 'Ya, how hateful. This Chidaukoji has made an unexpected blunder, bound hand and foot by a petty thief, unable to move, tormented by three women, and now a corpse no longer in this world. My grudge grows at Mount Torakumayama. Not only you, but the great thief of this cavern, starting with Sale and the other wretches, I shall pull off your raw heads and take my revenge. How regrettable, ku, how vexing.'

A cold chill started from the base of Serle's neck, turning his face pale blue, and trembling in the darkness,

Serle: 'Ko-ko-ko-kora Chidaukoji-to-yara, it would be best for you to make peace with your fate. No matter what you say, since it has come to this, you have no further ties to this world, so prepare yourself and attain enlightenment piously. Namu Tonshiyō Bodai Chidaukoji...'

Kirk: 'If I may say so, Serle-sama, thanks to you I have fulfilled my heartfelt wish. Please light the lamp so it is brighter than day.'

Trembling, Serle said,

Serle: 'A-a, splendid, splendid. Hey, everyone, prepare the lamps.'

Answering 'Hai,' both Yaku and Yeru brought and lit the candles. Serle and the five minor leaders had lost the strength in their waists from shock and were cowering flat on the ground, trembling. The candlelight was lit countless times. If one looked closely, Chidaukoji, Davis-hime, and Brawda were standing before Serle with smiles on their faces. From inside the prison, Kirk, as before, speaking in the voice of Davis-hime,

Kirk: 'Please, Dannasama, open it for me. I have fulfilled my heartfelt wish.'

Base: 'Please, Dannasama, open this door quickly. The ghost is frightening.'

Davis: 'If I may say so, Serle-sama, I am the real Davis-hime, you know. The one inside the prison is my double, and the secret is a man named Kirk.'

Serle: 'Na-na, what? How on earth did you get out?'

Davis: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, you are quite a dunce, aren't you?'

Chidaukoji: 'I am Kishunwake Shōgun Chidaukoji. That which you saw as being killed inside the prison is your henchman, Bat. Hey, Bat, that is enough. Ya, Yeru, open the doorway.'

Answering 'Hai,' Yeru suddenly flung the doorway wide open. The three of them came out through the entrance window, smiling. Serle and the five minor leaders had lost the strength in their waists from shock, their mouths hanging wide open, and their faces pale blue.

As such, the voice of the propaganda song echoing through the darkness,

'When the gods appear in the world, They clearly separate good from evil. The deity who created this world is Kamunaohi, And with a heart vast and wide, Ōnaohi. Now whatever happens in the world of men, Look straight upon it, see it straight, hear it straight, Correct the errors of crooked ways. So chanting, they arrived at the spot. Serle, startled for the third time, pressed his head to the ground, shedding tears and trembling. From this time on, Serle and the other thieves all realized the terrifying nature of evil deeds, repented from the bottom of their hearts, surrendered to Izanagi, swore never to commit evil deeds again, and together chanted the august name of the Great Deity Izanagi, expressing their remorse and reform. Here, Chidaukoji and Itahiko once again parted ways with Brawda and Davis-hime, and each set forth toward Jerusalem as their hearts desired. Serle set fire to the cavern of Torikumayama, repented together with his numerous henchmen, left this place, and each returned to their respective hometowns. Haar, forgiven by Itahiko, came to follow him all the way to Jerusalem. Ah, Kamunagara kamusabimashimase yo.

『神が表に現はれて 善と悪とを立分ける 此世を作りし神直日 心も広き大直日 唯何事も人の世は 直日に見直せ聞直せ 曲の過ち宣り直せ 朝日は照るとも曇るとも 月は盈つとも虧るとも 仮令大地は沈むとも 誠の力は世を救ふ 三五教の宣伝使 神の教の伊太彦が 此岩窟に囚はれし デビスの姫やブラワ゛ーダ 二人の珍の御使を 救はむためにハールをば 先頭に立ててスタスタと 登りて茲に現はれぬ 鬼春別の治道居士

定めて無事におはすらむ これの岩窟の頭分 セールは今や何処に居る 一時も早く出迎へて 吾言霊の神力に 心を直し霊清め 誠の道に帰れかし 吾に夜光の御玉あり 神は吾等を守りつつ 虎熊山の征伐に 早くも向はせたまひけり あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸倍ましませよ』

と謡ひ乍ら此場にやつて来る。三度吃驚のセールは、地上に頭をくつつけ涙をたらして慄ひ居る。是よりセールを初め、其他の盗人共は何れも悪事の恐るべきを悟り、心の底より悔改め、三五教に帰順し、向後は決して悪事をなさざる事を誓ひ、打ち揃ひ三五の大神の御名を唱へ、改悛の意を表はした。茲に治道居士及び伊太彦は又もやブラワ゛ーダ、デビス姫と袂を分ち、思ひ思ひにエルサレムを指して進む事とした。セールは虎熊山の岩窟に火を放ち、数多の乾児と共に悔い改めて此場を立ち出で、各自に自が郷里に帰る事となつた。ハールは伊太彦に許されて、エルサレム迄従ひ往く事となつた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
