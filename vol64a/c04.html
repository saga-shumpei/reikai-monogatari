Chapter 4: The Visiting Guest [1633]

訪問客〔1633〕

§ 1

Since Burabāsa had lost sight of Mariya, he had no choice but to return to the Catholic monastery alone; the surroundings were silent and still, and the only things reaching his ears were his own weary, labored breathing and his own footsteps.

Fortunately, as the front gate had been left open, he returned to the guest room on the second floor that had been assigned to him, lay down upon the sofa, and without knowing what was happening around him, plunged into a dreamlike world.

Awakened from his dream by the tolling of the monastery's bronze bell, he sat up abruptly to find that the surroundings were completely bright, and the clock striking eight in the morning was echoing from downstairs.

Counting the chimes of the clock on his fingers, Burabāsa muttered to himself:

Burabāsa: 'Ah, it is already eight o'clock. I certainly did fall into a deep sleep. Even so, I wonder if Mariya-sama did not come to this hotel last night. She was a nervous and sentimental woman, but as is often the case with women who experience Kamugakari, perhaps she suddenly spoke of the will of Kami and, having had a change of heart, returned to the American Colony. She was not a very pleasant woman, but I am truly grateful for her fervent faith and kind attitude.'

He then entered the washroom to attend to his needs and returned once more to his own room.

Upon looking, he saw that two servings of a meal were laid out on the dining table, yet there was no sign of a boy.

Seeing this state of affairs, Burabāsa thought:

Burabāsa: 'The boy is nowhere to be seen, but considering that two servings of a meal have been brought to my room, it may be that Mariya-sama was sleeping in the other room as well. How strange this is.'

He kept shaking his head repeatedly.

It was a young and handsome boy who entered slowly.

Burabāsa: 'Boy-sama, where is the woman who was my companion last night?'

Boy: 'Yes, I brought the meals for two, thinking that you had rested in this room together with her last night. She is not anywhere else.'

Burabāsa: 'How strange, this does not make sense. However, be that as it may, I shall finish my breakfast.'

He sat at the table and finished his morning meal in about half an hour as if shoveling it down. The boy, having no choice, cleared away Mariya's meal while grumbling to himself, accepted a suitable tip from Burabāsa's hand, and happily hid his figure in the next room.

Burabāsa leaned back in his chair, looked down from the second-floor window at the streets of Jerusalem with a longing heart, and was filled with infinite thoughts.

It was a dignified and amiable-looking old gentleman, who appeared to be a Jew, who knocked on the door, quietly calling out, 'Excuse me.'

Burabāsa: 'I do not know who you are, but please, come in.'

He stood up himself and pleasantly opened the door to welcome him into his room.

The old gentleman grasped Burabāsa's hand as if very satisfied, gazed intently at his face, and was already shedding tears from both eyes.

Burabāsa: 'Who are you, sir? I have somehow begun to feel a sense of nostalgia.'

Old Gentleman: 'Yes, I am Subatsufuōdo, a lean wanderer who serves as a deacon at the American Colony. Last night, Mariya-sama was very rude, and because she felt she could not face you again, she was very worried and asked if I would go to apologize, as she knew you were staying in room number nine on the second floor of this monastery. Therefore, I wished to have the honor of seeing you, sir, to offer an apology for her rudeness, and I have troubled you by coming early in the morning.'

Burabāsa: 'Ah, so you are Subatsufuōdo-sama, who manages the Colony together with Mariya-sama? Thank you so much for coming to find me. Now, please, come this way.'

原文（近代文語）

ブラバーサは、マリヤの姿を見失ひしより止むを得ず、只一人にてカトリックの僧院に帰つて見れば、四辺は寂として静まりかへり、只耳に入るものは自分の行歩に疲れた苦しげな鼻息と、その足音のみなりき。幸ひ表の門が開け放しになつて居たので、与へられた二階の居間に帰り、ソフアの上に横たはりて前後も知らず夢幻の国へと突進したりける。

ガンガンと響く僧院の梵鐘の声に夢を破られ、ツト身を起して見れば四辺はカラリと明け放れ、午前八時の時計が階下に響いて居た。ブラバーサは時計の音を指を折つて数へつつ、

『アヽもう八時だ。克くもマア寝込んだものだ。それにしても昨夜のマリヤさまは此ホテルには来て居ないだらうか。何処とはなしに神経質な感傷的な婦女だつたが、帰神の婦女によく在る習ひ、俄に神の命とか言つて心機一転してアメリカンコロニーへ還つて了つたのだらうか。余り気持の良い婦女では無かつたが、その熱烈な信念と親切な態度には実に感謝の至りだ』

と独語つつ洗面所に入り用を足して再び自分の居間に帰り来たり。

見れば食卓の上には二人前の膳部が並んで居て、ボーイらしき者も居ない。ブラバーサは此態を見て、

『ボーイは其処等に見当らないが、二人前の膳部が吾居間に運ばれて在ることを思へば、どうやらマリヤさまも外の居間に寝て居たのかも知れない。ハテ不思議だなア』

と首を頻りに振つて居る。

そこへ徐々として這入つて来たのは年の若い美しいボーイであつた。ブラバーサは、

『ボーイさま、夜前の相客たる一人の婦人は何処に居られますかな』

『ハイ、昨夜は貴下と御一緒に此の室で御休みになつた事だと思つてお二人の膳部を運んで来たので御座います。別に外には居られませぬ』

『ハテナ、合点の行かぬ事だ。併し何は兎もあれ朝飯を済まさむ』

と食卓に就いて、半時ばかりの間に掻き込む様にして朝の食事を済ませて了つた。ボーイは是非なくマリヤの膳部をブツブツ小言を云ひながら片付けて了ひ、ブラバーサの手から応分のポチを受取り、嬉々として次の室に姿を隠した。

ブラバーサは椅子に依りかかつて、二階の窓からエルサレムの市街を心床しげに瞰下し無限の情想を漲らし居たり。

そこへ『御免下さい』と静に声をかけて扉をたたいたのは、猶太人らしき品格の高い人好きのしさうな老紳士なりける。

『何れの方かは存じませぬが、先づ御這入下さいませ』

と自ら立つて快く扉を開いて吾室へと迎へ入れる。

老紳士はさも満足気にブラバーサの手を握つて、その顔を熟々ながめ、早くも両眼から涙さへ流し居たり。

『貴師は何れの方で御座いますか。何となく懐かしくなつて参りました』

『ハイ、私はアメリカンコロニーの執事でスバツフオードと申す瘠浪人で御座います。昨夜はマリヤさまが、大変な失礼をしたので再び御顔を拝する訳には行かないから、私に一度この僧院の二階の第九番に御逗留だから謝罪に行つて下さるまいかと大変に心配して居られますので、私はその御無礼の御詫を兼ねて尊い貴師に拝顔の栄を得たいと存じ、朝早くから御邪魔を致しました』

『アヽ貴師がマリヤ様と御一緒にコロニーを司宰遊ばすスバツフオード様で御座いましたか。良くマア御尋ね下さいました。サア何うか此方へ』

He offers a chair.

Raushinshi: 'Hai, arigata.'

He sits on the offered chair and begins to smoke a cigarette with a strong aroma.

Raushinshi: 'Mariya-sama kindly guided me through the Seichi, so I received great Bengi. I should have come to offer my Orei, but as I suddenly Miushina her Osugata Yazen, I have been Shitsurei. However, I heard that she has returned to the colony, and with that, my heart has finally Ochitsu.'

Raushinshi: 'Nanibun, Mariya-sama is a Reikanshiya, so she begins Datsusenteki Kaudō from time to time, and afterwards, she worries about it herself Itsu. This kind of thing did not start today. I am always used as her Benkai and Wabiyaku. Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

Raushinshi: 'Did Mariya-sama Reishi something on the Tochiu?'

Raushinshi: 'According to her story, a most intense radiance was emitted from your brow, and being struck by your divine power, she could no longer accompany you. She said that she was unintentionally chased by fear, abandoned you, and fled back. I thought this must surely be the possession of an evil spirit, so I performed Saniwa, and as I suspected, the evil spirit of Yamada-oroshi was possessing her, and it greatly fears and hates that you have come to the Seichi. After the evil spirit departs, Mariya-sama is a splendid person, but she was pained, saying she felt too sorry for you, and it was her request that I go to offer an apology.'

Raushinshi: 'Haa, such worry is absolutely unnecessary, so please tell her that.'

Raushinshi: 'Hai, if I convey those words, Mariya-sama will also be greatly delighted. Last night, she left the colony to guide you, also according to a Shinji. Would you, Seishi-sama, please bend your steps to the colony once?'

Raushinshi: 'Hai, thank you very much. I would certainly like to receive your favor. By the way, Subatsufo-do-sama, the Jews, who are the Israelite people, have finally regained their homeland after enduring three thousand years of hardship, have they not? The power of the times is truly a fearful thing.'

Raushinshi: 'Hai, arigata. We are still the Israelite people, but we have finally managed to establish our own public nation, however small it may be. The three great powers of the world were all trying to obtain this Palestine with desperate force, and in the end, they even started the world war you know of. That it has returned to us, the wandering people, is nothing but a blessing from Heaven. In short, I believe that God has allowed us to have a nation as a preparation for the second coming of the Messiah.'

Raushinshi: 'Since it is the center of the Earth, the land of Zion, it is no wonder that great powers like Germany, England, and America would want it.'

Raushinshi: 'The Baghdad Railway built by Germany, the African Railway made by England, and the great railway via Siberia that America is building all have this Palestine as their goal, but since it has come to this, these great railways have also ended up being used for us, the Israelite people. It is a clear fact that if only this railway is utilized, the Jewish people can unify the world. However, many of today's Jews are troubled because they have strong material desires and have forgotten the essential God. With human wisdom and power, one can succeed in almost everything up to ninety-nine percent, but the final touch can never be done without the power of God. Therefore, we are waiting for the second coming of the Messiah, who is the manifestation of the Lord God. When Palestine was given into the hands of the Israelites, who were called the chosen people of God in the past, it was a beautiful place with good scenery, sung by poets as a holy place where saffron scented and olives smelled, a land of Canaan said to flow with milk and honey. Today, that shadow is gone and it has become desolate, but that Palestine has returned to the hands of the Jews, and the beautiful scenery of the Mount of Olives of the past is gradually appearing. God, who sits in Heaven, in order to entrust Palestine to the Jews whom He chose prior to the second coming of the Messiah, gave them this beautiful mission from several thousand years ago and made them suffer for forty years, three million people, to prepare them with the qualifications of the chosen people. That He made three million people taste ascetic practices for forty years, where there was no water to drink and no food to eat, where parents died and children died, and it continued for many generations, is considered to be a divine plan to cultivate the national character of the Israelite Empire.'

Speaker: 'Is it not the case that the Yudayajin have been haiseki by other Taminzoku and have tasted shiyujiyu of konnan because they killed Christ? If so, when a powerful Yudayajin appears and unifies the Sekai, is there not a possibility that they will adopt a fukukiuteki taido toward the Jinmin of all Ikyōkoku?'

Speaker: 'Among many Dōhau, there may be some who hold such thoughts, but since the Israelites are a relatively zenriyō Minzoku, even if they might ichiji resort to kuwageki kaudō, they possess a deep heart to follow Kami; therefore, we judge from the perspective of their Kokuminsei that once they understand the true Messiah, they will kitsuto follow His Mei, and we are waiting for the Sairin of the Messiah. Furthermore, even if the Yudayajin unify the Sekai and construct the Zion Teikoku, they do not even dream of becoming the emperor themselves. We merely believe in the prophecies of the Bible, and are convinced that the Messiah shall descend from the eastern sky riding upon the clouds, and that the Emperor whom we must serve for eternity shall appear from the Hinode no Shima. It is because the Israelite Minzoku have endured thousands of years of hardship and persecution under this faith.'

Speaker: 'I believe that both that Messiah and the Emperor are already prepared in Takasago-jima, and have been preserved for the sake of today's world since thousands of years ago. I feel that the age of the gods of benevolence, where one heaven, one earth, and one sovereign shall rule, is already drawing near. However, I think that before then, a great mountain pass will inevitably appear in the world.'

Speaker: 'Indeed, we are of the same opinion as you, sir. That the Kami of heaven will finally appear on earth to distinguish and reconstruct good and evil, right and wrong, is what the sacred words indicate. We must polish our mitama as soon as possible to become of one heart with Kami and begin preparations for the end of the world. And I do not doubt your theory in the slightest that the Messiah and the Emperor will appear from Takasago-jima. Now, I apologize for keeping you occupied for so long. How about it, would you be willing to trouble yourself to visit the American Colony once?'

Speaker: 'Yes, thank you very much. In that case, I shall follow your words and accompany you.'

Having clarified this intention to the director of the monastery, he followed in the footsteps of the old gentleman and proceeded toward the colony.
