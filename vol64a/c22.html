Chapter 22: The Demonic Conduct [1651]

妖行〔1651〕

§ 1

Yamoriwake looked at Takehiko's face and said happily,

Yamoriwake: 'Ah, Takehiko-sama, you have come at last. Are you still practicing the worship of Hinodegami as before?'

Takehiko: 'Yes, I am practicing the worship of Hinodegami even more fervently. However, there are various kinds of Hinodegami, and since I have recently discovered the true Hinodegami, I have opened what is called the Ōmichikuwai and am doing propaganda with pamphlets.'

Yamoriwake: 'Indeed, that must be a suitable method adapted to the current Jidai. Does it sell a little?'

Takehiko: 'Yes, I am printing about one thousand copies of every Maigau at Chimokushiya, and they fly away as if they had wings. It seems that the Fudesaki of Otora-sama has quite a lot of Kauriyoku. Hahaha!'

Yamoriwake: 'I would like you to let me use one as well. In truth, I have come to dislike such old-fashioned Sendenhafu in front of Otora-sama. With a blind and reckless method that cannot see what is right in front of one's eyes, one only labors without effect and falls behind the Jisei, which is not worth it.'

Otora puffed up her face, became agitated, and said in a hurried voice,

Otora: 'Look here, Take-sama, that Yokota-harimono fellow, even if he thinks he will eat up Yamoriwake-sama, I, this Otora, will never let him go. You, sir, should work hard and sell your bread or whatever you like. Now, Yamoriwake-sama, you must not make a Torichigahi. Warugami are coming in the lineage of Henseijoshi, trying to make you lose your divine grace by disguising themselves in various ways. If you dawdle, you will be stripped even of the hair on your buttocks. It is troublesome that you are so fickle.'

Yamoriwake: 'Now, Otora-sama, what an unenlightened thing to say. Are you not also an Ikemiya of Hinodegami? Do they not say that the world flows like the sun, moves like the moon, and shifts like the stars? Is it not the teaching of heaven and earth that all things move according to the will of heaven? It is troublesome if you try to bind even the free will of Yamoriwake.'

Yamoriwake: 'I thought that since Otora-sama could no longer have me, you might have bent your will a little, but as expected, you have not moved at all. Even water will breed mosquito larvae if it remains stagnant in one place for too long.'

Otora: 'You Yokota-harimono Take-kō, why are you spitting out complaints toward Hinodegami? Is this something for the common people to know? This is no place for a baker to come. If you say things that are not in the magazine, it will interfere with the divine work, so leave at once.'

Takehiko: 'I came because I had business with Yamoriwake-sama. I had received a letter from Shanghai and was waiting for this moment. Do not mind me. It is enough for me to just meet Yamoriwake-sama.'

Otora: 'Now, Yamoriwake-sama, are you thinking of going to that Yokota-harimono Take-kō? Now, Ohana-sama, will you not do something to help? That fellow Take-kō is trying to eat him up, is he not?'

Ohana: 'Yokota, hold on there, Takehiko-sama, it would be best if you listened a little to what Hinodegami-sama says; Ohana will not know if you regret it later.'

Takehiko: 'Hmph, do not mind me. Since I am a Yokota-harimono who serves as a forerunner for the side-role, there is nothing that would please Otora-sama. And even if I became a godchild of Otora-sama, there is no future prospect, so it is not worth it. More than that, Yamoriwake-sama, since you have been traveling the world for a long time, please tell me some rare stories. I want to use them as material for my pamphlets.'

Yamoriwake: 'Yes, there is certainly something I must have you hear.'

Just as he was about to say this, Otora placed her hand over Yamoriwake's mouth and said,

Otora: 'This is improper; you must not speak so bluntly to such a fellow. A secret is a secret to the end. Now, Take-kō-sama, is it not too greedy of you to try to use Yamoriwake-sama as material for your bread? The very air becomes polluted when you are here. What do you think this place is? This is the sacred ground of Kohokuzan, where the true, pure crystal spirit resides. It is not a place for a four-legged spirit to come.'

原文（近代文語）

守宮別は、竹彦の顔を見て嬉しさうに、

『ヤア竹彦さま、よう来て下さいました。相変らず日の出神崇拝をやつて居られますかな』

『ハイ、日の出神の崇拝は層一層熱烈にやつて居ます。併し日の出神にもいろいろありましてねえ、私は此頃真の日の出神を発見しましたので大道会と云ふのを開きパンフレツト宣伝をやつて居ます』

『成程そいつは今日の時代に適した適当のやり方でせう。些と売れますかな』

『ハイ、地黙社で毎号一千部ばかり印刷して居ますが、羽根が生えて飛んで行きますよ。お寅さまのお筆先とは余程効力があるやうですわ。アハヽヽヽ』

『どうか私も一つ使つて頂き度いものですなア。実の所はこんな古めかしい宣伝法はお寅さまの前だが嫌になつたのですよ。目先の見えぬ盲滅法のやり方では労して効なく時勢におくれるばかりで約りませぬもの』

お寅は面膨らし、かつかになつて声せわしく、

『これ竹さま、横田はりもの奴、守宮別さまを喰へて往のうと思つても、いつかないつかな此お寅が離しませぬぞや。お前さまは精出して勝手にパンなと売りなさい。これ守宮別さま、取違してはいけませぬよ。悪神が、お蔭を落させやうと思つて、いろいろと化けて変性女子の系統が来て居るのですよ。グヅグヅして居ると、尻の毛迄ぬかれて了ひますよ。お前さまはそんなに移り気だから困るのだ』

『これお寅さま、何と云ふ開けない事を云ふのだい。お前さまも日の出神の生宮ぢやないか。世の中は日流れ月行き星移ると云ふぢやないか、天地惟神に移つて往くのが天地の教ぢやないか。守宮別の自由意志迄束縛して貰つちや困りますよ』

『お寅さまも守宮別さまがをられなくなつたので、些とは我が折れただらうと思つて居つたのに、矢張り、ちつとも動いて居りませぬなア。水でも余り一所に停滞して居るとぼうふらがわきますよ』

『こりや横田はり者の竹公、何をつべこべと日の出神に向つて小言を吐くのだ。人民の知つた事かい。パン屋がこんな所へ来る所ぢやない。雑誌も無い事を云ふと御神業の邪魔になるからトツトと去んで下さい』

『私は守宮別さまに用があつて来たのだ。上海から手紙を貰つたので今か今かと待つて居たのだ。構うて下さるな。私は守宮別さまに会ひさへすればよいのだ』

『これ守宮別さま、お前さまは横田はり者の竹公の所へ行く気か。これお花さま、お前も何とかして加勢をせぬかいな。竹公の奴、喰へて行かうとしよるだないか』

『横田、おつとどつこい竹彦さま、ちつと日の出神さまの仰有る事も聞いて置きなさるがよからうぞえ、後で後悔してもお花は知りませぬからなア』

『ヘン、構うて下さいますな。どうせ横役の先走りを勤めて居る横田はり者だから、お寅さまの気に入りさうな事はありませぬわい。又お寅さまの乾児になつた所で末の見込が無いから約りませぬでなア。それよりも守宮別さま、貴方は永らく世界漫遊をして居られたのだから、何か珍しい話を聞かして下さい。パンフレツトの材料にしたいのですからなア』

『ハイ、是非聞いて貰はなければならない事が御座いますよ』

と云はうとするのを、お寅は守宮別の口に手を当て、

『これはしたり、さうズケズケとこんな奴に喋るぢやありませぬぞ。秘密はどこ迄も秘密です。これ竹公さま、守宮別さまをパンの材料にせうとは余り虫がよ過ぎるぢやありませぬか。お前さまが此処に居ると空気迄が汚れる。此処を何と思ふて御座る。誠生粋の水晶身魂の居る小北山の霊地で御座るぞ。四足身魂の来る所では御座いませぬぞや』

§ 2

Speaker: 'Ahahaha, Koitsu is Omoshiro. If I drink a cup of sake and watch this Kuwatsugeki, it would be sweet. By the way, Takehiko-sama, when I saw the Shinbun in Shiyanhai, it said that the Sairin of Christ is approaching in Jerusalem, Asia Minor. They say a Kiuseishiyu will appear from Hinodejima, and they are making a Taihen fuss about it. If you were to publish such things in a pamphlet, they would surely sell well.'

Speaker: 'Ya, that is a good thing to have heard. Please write a detailed Genkau, as I will pay at least for your Sakate.'

Speaker: 'Shiyōchi. I shall write it and Yūsō it within Nisannichijiu.'

Otora, pricking up her Kikimimi, said:

Speaker: 'What, a Kiuseishiyu is descending to the Seichi? And the Hinode-no-kami is appearing? And from which country did they say it would appear?'

Speaker: 'According to an article in the London Times that I saw in Shiyanhai, it says that Okebuseyama of Hinodejima is the Eastern Palace, and Jerusalem in Palestine is the Western Palace. And they are making a Taihen fuss, saying that the Kiuseishiyu will make his Gokaurin at that Western Palace. It seems that the world of Miroku is also Yohodo Setsukin.'

Speaker: 'And is the name of that Kiuseishiyu known?'

Speaker: 'It is known. As his forerunner, Burabasa has already gone from the Seichi long ago. Before long, Uzunbarachiyanda-sama will likely appear as the Kiuseishiyu.'

Speaker: 'Yaa, that is Taihen. If such a person were to become the Kiuseishiyu, the world would be in darkness. The Kanjinkaname Kiuseishiyu is none other than the great Miroku-sama, the Hinode-no-kami, from the Sokotsuihane. Ee, what a dull-witted person you are. If you have gone as far as Shiyanhai, you are already one step away. Why did you not make a speech saying that the Kiuseishiyu has appeared at Kohokuzan, one step ahead? You only drink sake and dote on women, which is why things end up like this. Aa, important matters should not be left to others after all. Saa, from now on, the living palace of the Hinode-no-kami shall appear as the Kiuseishiyu. Ohana-sama, Magohiko-sama, Moriyawake-sama, saa, follow me. Let us board a ship from Bakan, pass through Chosen and Shina, cross Siberia, and arrive quickly. If we dawdle, we will be subjected to such treatment as to be stunned by the Yokoyaku again. Ee, how vexing it is.'

Speaker: 'Please take me with you as well.'

Speaker: 'It would be excellent if you would accompany us.'

Speaker: 'Now, Moriyawake-sama, what will happen if you take such a person with us? We absolutely cannot have her as a companion.'

Speaker: 'Hahaha, I am sorry, but since our brothers are already there, you do not need to take her along. Shall we accompany the Mizu-no-mitama and enter grandly? Otora-sama, go to Jerusalem and bring back red shame. If I am to bet that far, it is thoroughly Omoshiro. It is comical and Omoshiro. Ufuhuhu.'

Otora, becoming like a Yasha, said:

Speaker: 'Ee, you meddling Magotake-yatsu, you are filthy.'

Saying this, she made a gesture of sweeping with a palm-leaf broom and spitting repeatedly. Takehiko, his mouth agape at the excessive situation, walked with haste toward the Kohokuzan station to visit the Seichi, wearing a deep sedge hat while chuckling. Later, Otora sold off unnecessary clothing, tools, and even a cast-iron fire pot inscribed as the Great Gate of the World, received some travel expenses from a certain Takayama who served in the logistics department, and having finally prepared the funds, the four of them—Otora, Ohana, Moriyawake, and Magohiko—hurried toward the Kohokuzan station.

お寅は聞耳たてて、

『何でも上海で見た、ロンドンタイムスの記事によると、日出島の桶伏山が東のお宮、パレスチナのエルサレムが西の宮だと云ふ事です。そして其西の宮に救世主が御降臨になると云つて大変騒いで居ます。ミロクの世も余程接近したと見えますわい』

『そして其救世主の名は分つて居るのかい』

『分つて居ります。その先走りとして聖地からブラバーサが疾うの昔に往つて居るさうです。やがてウヅンバラチヤンダーさまが救世主として現はれるのでせう』

『ヤアそれは大変だ。アンナ者が救世主にならうものなら世界は闇だ。肝腎要の救世主は底津岩根の大ミロク様日の出神より無いのだ。エヽ気が利かぬ人だなア。上海迄行つて居るのなら、モウ一歩だ。一歩先に行つて救世主は、小北山に現はれて居るとなぜ演説をして来なかつたのかい。酒ばかり喰つて女に呆けて居るからこんな事になるのだ。アヽ大事の事は矢張り人任せではいけない。サア是から、日の出神の生宮が救世主として現はれませう。お花さま、曲彦さま、守宮別さま、サア私に従いて来なさい。馬関から船に乗り朝鮮、支那を通りシベリヤを横断して早く参りませう。グヅグヅして居ると又横役にアフンとする様な目に遭はされますよ。エヽ気の揉める事だわい』

『どうか私も連れて往つて下さい』

『御同道を願へば結構ですな』

『これ守宮別さま、コンナ奴を連れていつてどうなりますか。絶対にお伴はなりませぬ』

『ハヽヽヽ、済まんけれど吾々の兄弟分がチヤンと先に行つて居るのだから、別につれて往つて貰はなくてもよろしい。瑞の御霊のお供して堂々と乗り込みませうかい。お寅さま、エルサレム迄行つて赤恥を掻いて来なさい。我もそこ迄張ると徹底して面白い。滑稽味があつて面白い。ウフヽヽヽ』

お寅は夜叉の如くになり、

『エヽ横田はり者の曲竹奴、汚らはしいわい』

と云ひ乍ら棕櫚箒で掃き出しプツプツと唾を吐きかける真似をする。竹彦は余りの事に開いた口もすぼまらず、クツクツ笑ひ乍ら深編笠を被つて聖地に参るべく小北山の停車場へと歩を急いだ。後にお寅は不用の衣類や道具や、世界の大門と誌した鋳物の大火鉢迄売飛ばし、兵站部を勤めて居る高山某から若干の旅費を受取り、漸く旅費を調へてお寅、お花、守宮別、曲彦の四人は小北山の停車場へと急ぎける。
