Chapter 19: The Misguided Rite [1648]

祭誤〔1648〕

§ 1

Along the ridge of Takashiroyama, there was a Datsusōkyō of the Rūtobahā, which had cleared the pine forest of Kogitayama and built many small shrines and churches. The master of this place was called Torashima Hisanosuke, and his wife was called Torashima Torako. Although she was a woman with a naturally strong Jigashin, she took advantage of the lineage of Henjiyōnanshi as a Kikuwa, established an irregular Kyōdan called the Yurari-kyō, enshrined Yurari-hikonomikoto, and was actively taking a rebellious Taido against the Kyōshiyu of the Rūtobahā, Uzunbarachiyandā. Furthermore, she called herself the Ōmiroku of Sokotsuihane and the Hinode-no-kami, and from morning until night, she would emit a Shiwagaregoe and dye the Atari air gray with her murky Kototama. Among the believers who gathered here, there were quite a few eccentric characters, and the one who gained the most trust from Torako was an elderly woman who was not very fair-skinned, short, and stout, known as Naniwae no Ayame no Ohana, who was good and evil alike. And the one whom Torako trusted most was a man who had been a naval officer and could chatter well in foreign languages, called Miya-wake. While calling herself the living shrine of Hinode-no-kami, Torako was a formidable person who, together with this Miya-wake, would leave their home, wear the sash of the Magatsu-tama army, tour the entire Hinode-jima from east to west and north to south to preach, and even conduct missionary work on warships, thereby selling the name of the Yanchā-bā-sama. Miya-wake, in collusion with Hinode-no-kami, struggled to make the Henjiyōnanshi Uzunbarachiyandā a social outcast by any means and replace her with themselves; as a result of his efforts, Miya-wake started opposition movements in all directions, and in the end, he was a man of such fortitude that he even used the beast of 666 to deprive Uzunbarachiyandā of the freedom of her physical body.

Having successfully overwhelmed the person who had been a thorn in her side, and thinking there was no longer anything in the world to fear, she conspired with the group including Ayame no Ohana, Miya-wake, Magatsuhiko, Kidoguchi, and Omatsu, built a worship hall on Kogitayama, and prayed fervently for her desires to be fulfilled as soon as possible. Then, intending to invade the Chi no Takamagahara and seize all religious authority, she cajoled the old officials of the sacred ground, but just as she was ninety-nine percent of the way there, Uzunbarachiyandā returned, so she was struck with terror and fled back to Kogitayama in a state of disarray, while Miya-wake escaped overseas, and later the three, Torakohime, Ohana, and Magatsuhiko, put their heads together and began planning their second strategy. First, they wrote the Fudesaki of Hinode-no-kami to entice the believers of the Rūtobahā, and in order to cause the downfall of the power of the Henjiyōnanshi, they had a certain Takayama of Naniwa-no-sato donate military funds, toured the shrines and temples of the entire Hinode-jima, called it a soul-searching, sought out oral traditions, attached various karmic connections, and created Fudesaki to deceive a small number of believers in a plausible manner.

Today, fifty or sixty believers had gathered for the spring grand festival. The ceremony ended safely, and the believers returned to their respective homes. Afterward, Magatsuhiko, Torako, Ayame no Ohana, Hisanosuke, Takayamahiko, and others put their heads together and engaged in deep consultation. Magatsuhiko opened his mouth first:

Magatsuhiko: 'Everyone, thanks to the divine virtue, the spring grand festival has ended safely, and although the believers did not gather to the point where there was no room to stand in the vast sacred ground, we were blessed with divine virtue without it being too crowded. I am grateful and thankful for this, as it is the result of the activities of the Hinode-no-kami-sama, who preaches so enthusiastically every day, and the Ryūgū no Otohime-sama. Now, as you know, the marriage of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, which we had been planning, has finally been performed due to the Mizu no Rei possessed by an evil god that disturbs this world, and despite all our hard work and suffering, it is truly not a happy occasion. You always said that you would never let the Henjiyōnanshi lay even a finger on this marriage, that this Hinode-no-kami would permit it and allow the marriage to take place splendidly, and that you would restore the teachings of the Rūtobahā, but what on earth has happened?'

高城山の峰つづき、小北山の松林を切り開いて沢山な小宮やチヤーチを建てたルートバハーの脱走教があつた。ここの主人を虎嶋久之助と云ひ、女房は虎嶋寅子と云ふ。生れつき自我心の強い女であつたが変性男子の系統と云ふのを奇貨としてユラリ教と云ふ変則的なる教団をたてユラリ彦命を祀つて、盛んにルートバハーの教主ウヅンバラチヤンダーに反抗的態度をとつてゐる。そして自分は底津岩根の大弥勒、日の出神と自称し、朝から晩まで皺枯声を出して濁つた言霊で四辺の空気を灰色に染て居る。ここへ集る信徒の中には随分色々な変り者があつて、中にも最も寅子の信任を得たのは、善も悪きも難波江の菖蒲のお花と云ふ、あまり色の白くない背の低い横太い年増婆アさまである。そして寅子の最も信任してゐるのは守宮別と云ふ海軍の士官上りの外国語をよく囀る男であつた。寅子は日の出神の生宮と自称し乍ら此守宮別と共に宅を外にして曲霊軍の襷を掛け、日の出島の東西南北を隈なく巡教し、軍艦布教までやつてヤンチヤ婆アさまの名を売つた、したたか者である。守宮別は日の出神と腹を合せ如何にしても変性女子のウヅンバラチヤンダーを社会の廃物となし、自分等がとつて代らむと苦心の結果、守宮別は四方八方に反対運動を開始し、終には六六六の獣を使つてウヅンバラチヤンダーの肉体の自由まで奪つた剛の者である。

目の上の瘤として居た人物を、うまく圧倒した上は、もはや天下に恐るべきものなしと、菖蒲のお花を筆頭に守宮別、曲彦、木戸口、お松等の連中と謀り小北山に拝殿を建て、一時も早く願望成就致しますやうと祈願をこらして居た。さうして地の高天原へ乗込んで一切の教権を握らむと聖地の古い役員をたらし込み、九分九厘と云ふ所へウヅンバラチヤンダーが帰つて来たので、肝をつぶしホウボウの態にて再び小北山へ逃げ帰り守宮別は海外に逃げ出し、後に寅子姫、お花、曲彦の三人は首を鳩めて第二の策戦計画にとりかかつた。先づ第一に日の出神の筆先を書いてルートバハーの信者を籠絡し、変性女子の勢力を失墜せむものと難波の里の高山某に軍用金を寄附させ、日出島全体の神社仏閣を巡回し、身魂調べと称し、口碑伝説を探つていろいろの因縁をつけ、筆先を作つて誠しやかに少数の信徒を誤魔かして居る。

今日は春季大祭の為五六十人の信徒が集つて来た。祭典は無事に済んで信者は各家に帰つた。あとには曲彦、寅子、菖蒲のお花、久之助、高山彦等が首を鳩めて協議を凝して居る。曲彦は先づ第一に口を開いて、

『皆さま、お神徳によりまして春季大祭も無事終了致し、さしもに広き霊場も立錐の余地なき迄に信者が集まらず、却て、込みあはずお神徳を頂きました。之も日頃熱心に御布教して下さる日の出神様を初め竜宮の乙姫様の御活動の結果と有難く感謝致します。就いては御存じの通り、吾々がかねて計画してゐた玉照彦、玉照姫の御結婚もたうとう此世を乱す悪神の憑つた瑞の霊の為に挙行されて了ひ、本当に苦辛した甲斐もなく誠にお目出度う御座いませぬわい。貴方はいつもいつも此結婚は変性女子には指一本さえさせぬ、此日の出神が許して天晴れ結婚をさし、ルートバハーの教を立直すと仰有いましたが、一体どうなつたので御座います』

§ 2

Torako: 'That is because the arrangements of the Shinkai have been altered. Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime have finally been infected by the Akurei of the Henjiyōniyoshi, and their Suishiyōmitama are on the verge of becoming Doromitama. Now begins our Shiyōnenba. You must not dally. Since Tamateruhiko-sama and Tamateruhime-sama, in whom we had placed our expectations, are no longer of use, the Ikimiya of this Hinodewake must perform one more Hitohatara, or the Jiyōjiyu of the Shinsei will be tautei impossible. The Kamisama say in the Ofude that there are reserves in abundance, and that the Kanjin matters have been entrusted to the Hitsupō, do they not? Who do you think that Hitsupō refers to? The Mitama of Kinkatsukanenokami is also utterly Dame, and without the Hinodewake, the Tatekahetatenaho of this Sanzensekai will surely be impossible. The Ofude states that the Ushitora no Konjin, Hitsujisaru no Konjin, Kinkatsukanenokami, and Hinodewake shall gather their four souls to perform the service, does it not? The Mitama of the Ushitora no Konjin has already ascended to heaven, the Ikimiya of the Hitsujisaru no Konjin has been ruined by the Akurei, and Kinkatsukanenokami is a god with a strong ego who has been manipulated by the officials and has grown arrogant; far from the Jiyōjiyu of the Shinsei, he cannot even maintain the Lūtobahā. If three out of the four souls are of no use, the Ofude says that at the ninety-nine percent mark, the one-rin Shigumi will turn everything over, does it not? That is why this Hinodewake will turn the palm of the hand at the one-rin point. Is that clear? Do not make any mistakes in your understanding.'

Speaker: 'If the spirit of the Henjiyōniyoshi is so clouded, why do you seek them out and ask them to come for every grand festival? Is that not a bit contradictory?'

Torako: 'Ah, you are a person who does not understand. By having the Henjiyōniyoshi visit, the believers of the Lūtobahā are made to think, "After all, the shrine of Kohokuzan must have a deep connection. Even though it is spoken of so poorly, the Henjiyōniyoshi goes to bow her head there, so it must be a great Kamisama." ...I am performing the one-rin Shigumi. The Shigumi of the Kamisama cannot be understood by humans. It is the divine strategy of the Hinodewake to employ the god-like schemes and devilish tactics of the divine plan.'

Speaker: 'Oh, so I see your Machiavellian ways, your methods that are relentless when it comes to evil. Hmph, it is not even interesting.'

Torako: 'Hmph, leave it be. Do you see me as evil? The Shigumi of the Kamisama appear as evil while performing good. I am the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake, who has gone to the very bottom of the ten billion worlds to investigate the fundamental connections from the ancient past. No matter how wise you may be, you are just a former soldier, are you not? Can a soldier understand the affairs of the Shinkai? While the women were away, you were to build the worship hall and the office, and while you claimed you would provide any amount of money, when the time came, you made a fuss and did not provide a single yen, did you not? Do you have the qualifications to complain about such things? Just stay out of it.'

Speaker: 'Oh, there is no way to stand against the authority of the Hinodewake-sama. As a person who cannot see even an inch ahead, I cannot find any reason to talk back, so let us wait for the right time.'

Torako: 'Ah, that is good, that is good. If we do not do everything as the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake says, the Shigumi of the Shinkai will be delayed, and that will not do. Now, Hisunosuke-sama, if you are my husband, should you not be a bit more firm? What are you doing, Shōbu-sama? How can you remain silent while letting Magohiko say such things?'

Speaker: 'I, too, have been offering my opinions to Magohiko-sama at every opportunity, but as he is a young man, he will tautei not listen to what an old woman says. However, Torakohime-sama, I have heard something strange, and if it is true, we cannot just dally like this.'

寅子は、

『ソレハ神界の都合によつてお仕組を変へたのだよ。玉照彦、玉照姫もたうとう変性女子の悪霊に感染して了ひ、水晶魂が泥魂になりかけました。さあ之から吾々の正念場だ。グヅグヅしてゐては駄目ですよ。もはや期待してゐた玉照彦様、玉照姫様も駄目だから此日の出神の生宮が、もう一働きやらねば到底神政成就は出来ませぬぞや。神様は控えは何程でもあるぞよ、肝腎の事は系統にさしてあるぞよとお筆に出してゐられませうがな。その系統は誰の事だと思ひますか。金勝要神の身魂もサツパリ駄目だし、日の出神が居らなくては、もう此三千世界の立替立直しは出来ますまい。艮金神、坤金神、金勝要神、日の出神、四魂揃ふて御用を致さすぞよ、とお筆に出てゐるでせう。艮金神の御魂はもはや御昇天遊ばし、坤金神の生宮は悪霊にワヤにされて了ひ、金勝要神は我の強い神で役員達に祭り込まれて慢心致し、到底神政成就どころか、ルートバハーの維持も出来ませぬ。四魂の中、三魂迄役に立たねば、九分九厘の処で一厘の仕組でクレンと覆すとお筆に出てゐるでせう。それだから此の日の出神が一厘のところで掌をかへすのですよ。宜しいかな。取違ひを致しなさるなや』

『それほど変性女子の霊が曇つとるのなら、何故大祭毎に頼みさがして、変性女子に来て貰ふのですか。チツと矛盾ぢやありませぬか』

『エー、分らぬ人ぢやな。変性女子さへ詣らしておけばルートバハーの信者が「ヤツパリ小北山の神殿は因縁があるに違ひない。あれだけ悪く云はれても変性女子が頭を下げに行くから、矢張偉い神様だ」と思はせる……一厘の仕組をしてるのだよ。神の仕組は人間に分りませぬよ。神謀鬼策の仕組を遊ばすのが日の出神の御神策だよ』

『ヤア、それで貴女の権謀術数、悪にかけたら抜目のない、やり方が分りましたよ。ヘン糞面白くもない』

とあとは小声で呟く。

『ヘン、措いて下され、私が悪に見えますかな。神様のお仕組は悪に見えて善を遊ばすのだよ。何もかも昔からの根本の因縁を十万億土のドン底まで行つて調べて来た日の出神の生宮、何程お前さまは賢うても軍人上りぢやないか。軍人が神界の事が分りますかい。お前さまは早く女子の留守の中に拝殿を建て、事務所を建て、そして費用は何ぼでも出すと云ひ乍ら愈となれば、スツタモンダと云つて一円の金も出さぬぢやないか、そんな事でゴテゴテ云ふ資格がありますかい。スツ込んで居りなさい』

『ヤア、どうも日の出神様の御威勢には楯つく事は出来ませぬわい。何と云つても一寸先の見えぬ人民ですから、何と口答へする訳にも行かず、マア時節を待ちませう』

『あ、それが宜いそれが宜い、何事も日の出神の生宮の申す通りに致さねば神界の仕組がおくれて仕方がない。これ久之助さま、お前さまも私のハズバンドなら、少しシツカリしなさらぬかい。菖蒲さまも何して御座る。曲彦にアンナ事を云はして黙つてる事がありますかいな』

『私も間がな隙がな、曲彦さまに御意見を申して居りますが、何と云つても若い人だから到底婆の云ふ事は聞いて下さいませぬ。然し乍ら寅子姫さま、私は一つ妙な事を聞きましたが、それが本当とすれば、かうしてグヅグヅしてゐる訳にも行きますまい』

Speaker: 'What kind of strange matter are you talking about? If it is no trouble, please tell me. I have my own thoughts on the matter as well.'

Speaker: 'If that is the case, I shall tell you. In the divine brush of the Henjiyōnanshi, it is written that shrines shall be built in the west and the east, and that Kami shall move into them to provide Shiyugo. Where on earth do you think these eastern and western shrines are?'

Speaker: 'Otsohohohoho, Ohana-sama, what are you playing dumb for? The eastern shrine is none other than the Shinden of Kogitayama, is it not? The beginning of Ningen and the beginning of Gokoku is this very Kogitayama. And as for the western shrine, is it not the Seichi of Okefuseyama? The Shinden of Okefuseyama was smashed to pieces as you saw, but the eastern shrine is in the full Kiyokujitsushiyōten, and there is not a single person who can lift a finger to stop it, is there? Looking at this, can you not tell whether there is Shintoku or not? The Shinjiya of Rootbahar are Baka, so they are deceived by the Henjiyōniyoshi, and they gather at the site of the destroyed shrine, and with pitiful voices, they are crying and making Hoezura every single day, saying:',

For the shrine that is destroyed,

For the Idai that has passed away,

We prostrate ourselves upon the earth and weep,

Ah, Kamunagara, Chihahe Mase.

Speaker: 'Looking at that, you should be able to tell whether Kamisama is present or not. Kami says that it takes time to understand the path of good, but although this Kogitayama is currently mocked by the Shinjiya of Rootbahar, before long a Konjikisanran Shinden will be built, and it will reach the point where they will be told to go eat the dirt of Okefuseyama. That is why I am telling you all to stay firm. Itsuhihohohoho.'

Speaker: 'Torako-sama, is it not a bit off the mark to say that shrines are to be built in the west and the east? I believe the divine Shinden of Okefuseyama is the eastern shrine. The divine plan of Kamisama cannot be such a small thing.'

Speaker: 'Hohohoho, you know that the direction where the Nichirin-sama appears is the east, and the direction where He hides is the west, do you not? Do you think Kogitayama is the west? You are a person who does not understand either. You were also living in seclusion at the foot of Okefuseyama, so you have been possessed by the evil spirits of women and are slowly starting to go senile. Utsufuhohohoho.'

Seeing Moriyawake-sama in Western clothing return in a busy manner to such a place, everyone said happily:

Speaker: 'Ya, Moriyawake-sama, thank you for your hard work. How was the plan in the foreign land? Surely the plan of the Hinode Kami must have reached there as well.'

Speaker: 'In any case, please bring me a cup of sake. While I partake of the Omiki, I, Moriyawake, shall tell you the story at my leisure.'

斯かる所へ洋服姿の守宮別は忙がしげに帰り来たるを見て一同は嬉しさうに、

『ヤア、守宮別さま、御苦労で御座いました。外国のお仕組はどうで御座りましたな。定めし日の出神様のお仕組も行渡つて居るでせうな』
