Chapter 27: The Turning Anew [1656]

再転〔1656〕

§ 1

A gentleman who visited the Sauan of Burabasa in the valley of Mount Zion with loud footsteps was Dr. Subar, a professor at the University of Chicago. The doctor pushed aside the brushwood door and entered, saying, 'Gomen-nasai-mase.' Burabasa came out to welcome him with a look of joy and ushered him into the back of the small Zashiki.

Burabasa: 'Ah, are you the doctor whom I met on Mount Kanransanjiyō? It is very kind of you to visit such a humble Sauan.'

Dr. Subar: 'Hai, I have come because there is something I would like to ask you for a moment. In truth, I am a professor at the University of Chicago, but this time I have been chosen as a member of the committee for the Kensetsu of Zion University, and I have been here for about two months for the sake of supervision. Furthermore, I am a man who has traveled around Hinodewajima from corner to corner two or three times, visited various Jinjiya and Butsukaku, and earned the name of a doctor of Ofuda. You said you came from the Seichi of Okebuseyama, and I have also once visited the Honzan of Rutobaha, met the leader directly, and was taken care of at the Kototama-kaku. Since I heard you are a missionary of Rutobaha, I felt a sense of nostalgia and had been thinking I would like to visit you once, but because I am busy, I have not had the opportunity. However, on the evening of two or three days ago, a man called Moriyawake came from Hinodewajima accompanied by three men and women, and he said things like they were the savior of the Hinode-gami or the great Miroku-sama, and he spoke ill of you, calling you a fake missionary. I defended you in various ways, but since he would not listen to anything I said, I parted ways with him without engaging further, but what kind of person is he exactly? I have been wanting to ask you about it, and since today is fortunately Sunday, I have taken the liberty of disturbing you.'

Burabasa: 'Hate-na, has a man called Moriyawake been here? Is he not together with an old woman of about fifty called Otora?'

Dr. Subar: 'I remember him mentioning Otora-sama, Ohana-sama, and Magarihiko. And Moriyawake-sama insisted firmly and strongly that this old woman is the second coming of Christ and the reincarnation of Miroku. Those people are quite strange, are they not?'

Burabasa: 'They must have sniffed out that I came here and came to interfere. They are a tenacious group to the very end. It is truly troublesome.'

Dr. Subar: 'You must be very troubled. However, you spoke earlier about having come regarding the second coming of Christ, but I have traveled around countries of the world, and saviors have appeared in India, China, Japan, Russia, and also in South America and Mexico. I think that unless the saviors gather at some point somewhere and begin to hold a national assembly, the true savior cannot be known as a human being. What do you think?'

Burabasa: 'In any case, the saviors of the world will gather in one place, and among them, the person who is most impartial, selfless, and rich in benevolence will likely be chosen as the true savior. Since it is said that a savior will emerge from each of the twelve streams of Israel, I believe that the great savior will appear from among them.'

Dr. Subar: 'Indeed, that is an impartial view. And where do you think the place of the descent will be?'

Burabasa: 'Naturally, I believe it is Jerusalem, and that is why I have come all the way here.'

Dr. Subar: 'Indeed, according to the prophecies of the Bible, it would be Jerusalem, but one never knows where the savior will descend. I do not think it is limited to Jerusalem at all. Perhaps he may appear in Hinodewajima.'

§ 2

'I am not sure about that.'

The three who came walking briskly to the place where they were talking were Otora, Ohana, and Magahiko.

'Ah, it seems you have found a hideout. This too is surely the guidance of the Hinode-no-Kami. Pardon me, you are Burabasa-sama, are you not? Are you aware that the Kiuseishiyu of the Hinode-no-Kami has descended upon Kanranzan since two or three days ago? Oh, and is that Ketau-sama there not the Hakase who was chirping away with Yamoriwake on Kanranzanjiyō the other day? My, what a remarkable thing this Innen is. I never imagined we would meet in a place like this. Now, Ketau-sama, have you been deceived by this Burabasa and come here again? You had better be a little careful.'

Subaru, looking annoyed, used the Kotoba of Hinodejima and said:

'Oh, you are that famous Otora-baba of Kogitayama who goes around disrupting the Oshihe of Rutobaha. And one is Magahiko, and then there is that tough one called Ohana, I presume. If you do not settle down properly, there will be no one left in this Seichi to deal with you.'

'My, what a Hakase you are indeed. You have become able to use the forty-eight characters of the Iroha. Until the other day, you were chirping like a four-legged beast or a bird, but I suppose thanks to meeting the Hinode-no-Kami for a moment, you have become able to use the Kotoba of a true human. Now, Ohana-sama, Maga-yan, you can see the divine power of the Hinode-no-Kami now, can you not?'

'After all, he is the very person who fled in the night, so he is quite something.'

'Now Maga, what kind of thing are you saying?'

'Even so, facts are facts, so there is nothing to be done. If I may, Burabasa-sama, please make me your disciple. In truth, Otora-sama lost her money, and the three of us are penniless, so we are at a complete dead end.'

Burabasa smiled slyly and said:

'Even the Hinode-no-Kami seems to be in trouble if he has no money. My own pockets are empty, so I cannot lend you any money, but stay here for a while, eat something simple, and wait until the money arrives. If you send a telegram, it will arrive in less than twenty days.'

'Oh, we have troubled you greatly. We shall meet again properly next Sunday and have a talk. We have business today and are in a bit of a hurry, so please excuse us.'

'I am sorry that I could not offer any hospitality after you have come all this way. Since it would be a shame to make you walk again, I shall visit you myself.'

The Hakase said:

'If that is the case, let us meet another day. Everyone, please take your time.'

And with that, he walked away briskly from the spot.

The three, Otora, Ohana, and Magahiko, felt their hearts grow empty and lonely in a foreign land, and having no other acquaintances, they resolved to seek help at the residence of Burabasa, whom they otherwise hated. They acted deliberately submissive, following the words of Burabasa, and by saying yes to everything and playing the hypocrite, they made it appear as if they had surrendered.

Around sunset the following day, Yamoriwake came to visit, having stuffed two hundred and fifty yen into his pocket.

'Excuse me. Is this the house of Burabasa-sama?'

'Yes, I do not know who you are, but please come in.'

'Is that voice not Yamoriwake-sama? Come, come, hurry and come in.'

'Ah, excuse me, Otora-sama, you are terrible. You are truly a wicked person against whom one must be on guard. Hey, Magahiko, Ohana-sama, is it not a lack of friendship to leave a person behind?'

Yotahiko: 'If that is the case, how can I pay the twenty-four thousand ryō? Did you sneak out in the night again? It is amazing that you were able to get out.'

Kamehiko: 'Thanks to you leaving your saifu behind, I have settled the kanjiyō completely and returned. Here, there are about two hundred and fifty ryō left, so I will return them to you.'

Yotahiko: 'How did you settle the kanjiyō for that money?'

Kamehiko: 'Since a dozen costs no more than six dollars, I paid for everything including your share, and it only cost twenty-five dollars.'

Yotahiko: 'Ah, was that so? I wonder why that fellow said something so machiga. Well, with two hundred and fifty ryō, I will be daijiyōbu for a shibaraku. If I just send a denpau to my kunimoto, they will send me more. Hmph, imamade I have been listening to the gomuri and gomotsuto of Burabāsa-sama, which made my ears ache, but who would listen to that anymore? Hey, Burabāsa-sama, you said the Kiuseishiyu is the Mitama of Mizu, but how could a fellow like that ever be a Kiuseishiyu? The kiu in Kiuseishiyu, which torments the world, must be the kiu in hinkiu, right? Ohohoho.'

He began to raise his meter slowly.

Burabāsa-sama: 'Do as you please. The jijitsu will shiyōmei it.'

Yotahiko: 'Come, you sannin of the gorenchiu, let us leave this place and go to the seichi quickly. And since there is no one in the town of Jerusalem to fear anymore, I shall now climb Kanranzan and become the Kiuseishiyu, so you sannin must walk around the machijiu and proclaim it. Farewell, the jijitsu will shiyōmei it eventually. Right, Burabāsa-sama?'

He walked away, swinging his large buttocks and flapping about.
