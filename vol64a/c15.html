Chapter 15: The Grand Sumo [1644]

大相撲〔1644〕

§ 1

An elderly gentleman came to visit the living room of Burabasa, who was staying at a Catholic Sōin hotel. This was Bahā'u'llāh, a missionary of the Bahá'í faith. He was led by a boy to Burabasa's living room, and

Bahā'u'llāh: 'Gomen-nasaimase.'

saying this, he offered a slight bow. Burabasa pulled up a chair with his own hands and

Burabasa: 'Ah, were you Bahā'u'llāh-sama, whom I met on the train? I had intended to visit you once, but as I am not yet accustomed to the place, I have been visiting here and there. Thank you for coming to visit me.'

as he greeted him, Bahā'u'llāh placed the table between them, and they sat facing each other from both sides.

Bahā'u'llāh: 'Yes, I too had been thinking of visiting you once, but somehow, what with one thing and another, it has finally become today. How are your impressions of the Seichi since you arrived?'

Burabasa: 'Yes, what I see and what I hear are different from Hinodewake, so I was bewildered. It seems I have finally come to understand the Chiri and have grown accustomed to the air, so I have begun to settle down a little.'

Bahā'u'llāh: 'Indeed, I feel the same. I came here all the way from the land of Tokoyo, but oh, everything I see and hear is strange; when I think that the Savior might descend to such a place, I am struck by a sense of wonder. When I was in my country, I spoke of the Seichi, Jerusalem, Jerusalem, and longed for it day and night, but the ruins of the ancient and desolate holy city are nothing but seeds of tears. My expectation that golden flowers would be blooming and fragrant has been completely betrayed. Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

Burabasa: 'They say that cities are made by men and the countryside by God, and surely the Lord would not descend unless it were in such a rustic place. There is no reason for the Savior to descend to a place where people are gathered, defiling both spirit and flesh in the streets of red dust ten thousand jō high.'

Bahā'u'llāh: 'Indeed, if you put it that way, it may be so. A few years ago, a wisp of black smoke that appeared on the Balkan Peninsula spread throughout all of Europe with the force of a prairie fire, wrapping the earth of the whole world in the clouds of war; because of this, the hearts of men have worsened ever since, and have they not reached a state where they can neither advance nor retreat? Even if the Savior were to descend to such a place, there would likely be no place for Him to set a single foot. We must polish our hearts and polish our souls, one person more than another, to become of the heart of God and await the descent of the Savior. It has truly become a world of eternal darkness.'

Burabasa: 'In the teachings of John, the founder of the sect of Rūtobahā, it was said from thirty years ago that the great war of the three thousand worlds would begin, and at last, the great world war occurred. And the founder John ascended to heaven on the morning the peace treaty was concluded, which ended the 1,567 days of the duration of the previous world war—that is, on the morning of the third day of the tenth month of the seventh year of Taishō (old calendar) in terms of the Jitentō Island. Since then, the state of the world has been truly miserable, such that one cannot even bear to open one's eyes.'

Bahā'u'llāh: 'If one examines the total area of the belligerent nations regarding the previous war, it amounted to 43,402,762 square miles, which is more than 75.8 percent of the world's area, and the number of personnel who participated in that war reached no less than 1,611,920,000, which was an unprecedented great war equivalent to 92.5 percent of the world's population. It showed the great power of autumn frost and fierce sun, causing all the grasses and trees under heaven to wither in a single night. Only the evergreens stand towering, and apart from that, there are only a few kinds of red, yellow, purple, and blue flowers competing for brilliance, boasting for a short while of the beauty of the end of the world. Ah, has the fearsome great world war been eradicated by this? Looking at the ends of the earth after the great war, can we not see that there is no symbol of peace to be found? Are not small wars being fought everywhere, exposing the miserable state of hungry ghosts, beasts, and asuras without reservation? Is the war of Armageddon not referring to the previous war? The Bible teaches that when the war of Armageddon is over, the end of the world draws near; things have become quite ominous. Cold winds blow when it is hot, crops do not develop as expected, volcanoes erupt everywhere, and there is the suffering of earthquakes and floods, robbery, murder, and various unpleasant movements; there is no way for humans to do anything about this world. At this point, there is no path left but to look forward to the descent of the Savior, is there?'

§ 2

Speaker: 'I am convinced that the Kiuseishiyu will surely make his Gokaurin and govern the Sekai in Buji and Taihei. However, before that, a great Ōtauge will surely emerge. I believe the Sensō of Armageddon will break out in the Kongo. Since there are currently two great powers in the Sekai, Koshitantan and watching each other, the Genjiyō will surely not be settled as it is. The Tatekahe and Tatenaho of the world will not begin until the strength of the Ningen of Konnichi is exhausted, their Hanabashira is broken, and there is no Yochi left for any remedy. I believe it is the Keirin of the Kamisama that the Kiuseishiyu will make his Kaurin when it becomes Kubukurin and Senkiitsuki.'

Speaker: 'Indeed, I am in Godōkan. And what are you referring to when you speak of your two great powers?'

Speaker: 'I believe you are generally aware that two great powers are currently competing in secret upon this earth. On one side, there is a powerful nation that exerts a formidable new force, attempts to leap and act throughout the Sekai, behaves in an arrogant and lawless manner, expands through unnatural and artificial violence, and makes the law of the jungle its sole national policy; on the other side, there is a nation that broke the dream of national isolation and exclusion, appeared suddenly on the stage of the Sekai, stood shoulder to shoulder with the great powers, and leaped again to become one of the great powers of the Sekai. All the people of the Sekai look upon these two great powers with eyes of wonder and are in a state of bewilderment. Their development is an unprecedented fact in history, yet that development is claimed to be extremely fair and just. One is like a pyramid, an extremely spectacular but lifeless structure, while the other is like a tall tree, full of life; although it cannot match the pyramid in terms of mere spectacle, it is truly undergoing a living growth. And these two great powers are, one a small isolated island in the Far East, and the other a vast continent in the Far West. One is the oldest nation in the present day, and the other is the newest among the great powers; one is a kingdom since its founding, and the other is a republic since its founding; one boasts an imperial line of unbroken succession, and the other boasts a sovereignty that changes every four years; one is based on the majesty of the Tenson, and the other is based on the civil rights of the masses. And as for their national character, one values righteousness while the other values profit; one calls itself a divine nation while claiming to be a great power, and the other calls itself a republic while claiming to be a Christian nation; one makes the vertical relationship between parent and child the basis of the family, and the other makes the horizontal relationship between husband and wife the basis of the family; one makes the relationship of male superiority and female inferiority the basis of human ethics, and the other makes the relationship of female superiority and male inferiority the basis of human ethics; one uses the sun as its national emblem, and the other uses stars as its national emblem. Therefore, is it not natural that they are incompatible in their national conditions and missions?'

Speaker: 'Indeed, what you have just said is truly a declaration that has grasped the era. You have shown the Genjiyō where one side stands as the land of the sunrise and the other as the land of Tokoyo, confronting each other in the Sekai. As the proverb says, when two heroes fight, the situation cannot remain whole, so it must be unified by one side or the other. It has truly become a troubled world. Whether it be politics, economy, thought, or religion, there is surely no time like Konnichi when the world is so deadlocked. There is no doubt that this distress must burst somewhere.'

Speaker: 'That is so. Since the great champions of the East and West are now presenting the strange spectacle of facing each other on the great sumo ring of the Sekai, tightening their loincloths, flexing their arms, and making their muscles leap, it is clearer than seeing fire that the curtain will sooner or later rise on the battle of the tigers. Armageddon, that is, the final Sensō of the Sekai, has become unavoidable. Therefore, in order to let the earth dwell in peace in the paradise of the heavenly kingdom, the Ōkamisama is causing the spirits of John and Christ to descend to the world, and is having the gospel of the heavenly kingdom spread to all people. Nevertheless, the humanity of the earth, accustomed to the world of eternal darkness, has not a single person who can understand the true intent of this Ōkamisama, and only the faithful servants of God are exhausting their sincerity and waiting while praying to God; truly, the Sekai is in a miserable state. Is not the divine work of the Kamisama to perform the Tatekahe and Tatenaho of the three thousand worlds filled with such evil truly a grand plan?'

§ 3

Speaker: 'Since all humanity in this Sekai are precious Miko born from the same Miiki of Kamisama, we humans are all Kiyōdai. However, in the current Jiyōtai, no matter how much we religious figures may rush, it will be Dame. Unless a great Kiuseishiyu appears to organize it, a Sekai like tangled hemp can hardly be brought to Shiushifu. Yet, regarding these Nidaiseiriyoku, which one do you think will eventually unify the Tenka? Is it the Tokoyo no Kuni, or is it Hinodejima? I would like to hear your thoughts.'

Speaker: 'It is Tautei impossible for a person of Ningen status to understand the Gokeirin of Kamisama, but what I have received as a blessing through the teachings of Lūtobahā is that the race which should eternally govern the future state will by no means be the people of Tokoyo no Kuni. The people of Sanbikyō, who had been a stateless people for two thousand six hundred years, have recovered the Palestine granted by Kamisama through the great war of the past, and are now in the vigor of the rising sun. And their power of faith, patience, and spiritual perception are things that can never be compared to anything else in the Sekai. Through Shintō, I am convinced that there is a great power lurking even deeper than the Nidaiseiriyoku I mentioned earlier, which will unify the final Sekai. The Jews have seven mysteries; they are as follows: First, that they have lost their own country while bearing the unbroken imperial line of the ages; Second, that despite two thousand six hundred years having passed since the loss of their country, they still recognize themselves as the chosen people of Kamisama even today; Third, that they have restored their lost country of two thousand six hundred years and built a state in Palestine, however small it may be; Fourth, that even though they have forgotten their own language and have reached the point where those who speak the national language are called great scholars, they have not forgotten their country nor bent their faith; Fifth, that they never assimilate with other nations under any circumstances; Sixth, that while holding the status of a stateless people, they are constantly planning the unification of the Sekai; Seventh, that the entire Sekai today is unfolding freely according to the will of the Jews in terms of politics, economy, and academics.'

Speaker: 'Indeed, that is truly astonishing. It is truly great, worthy of being called the chosen people of Kamisama. And then, what is the other power in the depths?'

Speaker: 'That is the seven mysteries of Hinodejima. First, that they bear the unbroken imperial line of the ages and stand consistently with righteousness, never having been invaded by others, and the state is heading toward increasing prosperity; Second, that while calling themselves the land of Kamisama, there are few who call themselves the chosen people of Kamisama or the people of Kamisama; Third, that although they have a history of the restoration of imperial rule, they have never once had to rebuild the nation; Fourth, that although they have evolved their national language, they have never made it a dead language, and therefore have never had to revive the national language; Fifth, that although they appear to be a people difficult to assimilate, in reality, they are easily assimilated into the customs of any country, and they embrace and return to any ideology or religion, and there are people who are prone to forgetting their own motherland; Sixth, that while Tokoyo no Kuni chooses any means for the sake of Sekai unification, Hinodejima always seeks to soar through justice and public morality, that is, Kannagara; Seventh, that while the Sekai is planning to isolate Hinodejima by gathering together, Hinodejima does not yet have a Sekai-wide plan, which is where it differs in nature from the Jews. Speaker: 'You said just now that you came riding on clouds, but is that not an airplane?'

Speaker: 'No, what I call clouds is an ancient word from Jitentōtō for a boat. Since clouds and boats are related, it is written in the Bible that coming on a boat is coming on a cloud.'

Speaker: 'I see, I have understood that the Kiuseishiyu will descend riding on a cloud. Well, thank you very much. Sorry to have disturbed you... Let us meet again. Please visit me when you have a moment of leisure. I have built a modest hall on the banks of the Jordan River where our believers are gathered...'

Speaker: 'Yes, thank you very much. I will visit you in the near future. If so, let us part here.'
