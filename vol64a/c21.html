Chapter 21: The Wandering Path [1650]

遍路〔1650〕

§ 1

Otora and Ohana, the two of them, opened the window of the longhouse standing by the ditch and gathered their foreheads together, whispering something with troubled faces, when a man wearing an amigasa hat pulled deep over his eyes came visiting and began to sing a Sendenka in a cool voice.

Hearing this song, Otora, with a very pleased look on her face, said:

Otora: 'Here, Henro-sama, you do not look like a beggar but truly like a person with a polished spirit; please come up and have a smoke. Now, now, Ohana-sama, did you hear the song just now? It said, "The capable Takahime hides her claws," did it not? My guardian deity is called Takahime, and she contributed greatly to the achievement of the divine government in the past. So, the fact that I am hiding my claws and acting gently now has its reasons and connections. You must not mistake me. Even just now, as the Henro-sama said, it said that my name would be known to the world even a hundred or a thousand years from now, did it not? You are questioning me, the living shrine Otora of the Hinodewake who is the reincarnation of this Takahime, as if you were picking at the corners of a tiered food box with a toothpick; is your judgment not a bit off? In a previous life, were you not called Kurohime-sama, and did we not gather together again and engage in this activity of achieving divine government due to the connection of having worked so much for me?'

Ohana: 'I have heard such things until my ears have grown calluses without having to ask you to repeat them now. It did not say "capable Takahime," it said "the capable hawk hides its claws." Say, Henro-sama, it is not Takahime, is it?'

Otora: 'What does it matter if the rank is a little different? Since he sang of the true nature of my spirit, it is fine to think it is Takahime.'

Ohana: 'In the song just now, you said "if you marry, make your husband your heaven," but are you not failing to keep your chastity toward your husband, Ten? Did you not fly around everywhere all year round with Mamoriyakewake-sama, without listening to how much Hisanosuke-sama was clamoring for you to stop? You are only taking the parts that are convenient for yourself.'

Otora: 'How can you lump the divine service of the spirit world and the human world together? If I were just a woman of a household, that would be one thing, but this Otora is a living shrine serving the Hinodewake-sama of the reconstruction and renewal of the three thousand worlds. Can you lump a pillar of God and a human being together? Even though you have been listening to the teachings of the Hinodewake-sama for so long, how can you say such incomprehensible things?'

此歌を聞いて、お寅は大変に御機嫌な顔をしながら、

『これ、遍路さま、お前さまは乞食にも似ずホントに身魂の研けた人と見えますわい、ちやつと上つて一服して下さい。コレコレお花さま、今の歌を聞きましたか、「能ある高姫爪かくす」といつたでせう。私の守護神は高姫と云つて昔の神政成就には随分尽したものですからなア。今私がかう爪を隠して柔和しくして居るのは曰く因縁があることですよ。見違ひをしては困りますよ。今もお遍路さまの云ふやうに百年千年の後迄も世間に名をば知られると云つたでせう。お前はこの高姫の生れ変りの日の出の神の生宮お寅にむかつて、爪揚子の先で重箱の隅をほじくるやうに詰問をしなさるが、ちと了見が違ひはしませぬかい。お前さまも前世には、黒姫さまと云つて随分妾の為に活動した因縁によつて、又一緒に集つてかうして神政成就の活動をして居るのぢやありませぬか』

お花『ソンナ事は今更改めて聞かずとも耳が蛸になる程聞かして貰つて居ますワ。能ある高姫と云ふたのではなく能ある鷹は爪かくすと云つたのですよ。ねえ、遍路さま高姫ぢやありますまい』

『ちつと位違つたつて構はぬぢやありませぬか。的きり妾の身魂の性来を歌つて下さつたのだから、高姫だと思つて居ればよいのですよ』

『今のお歌に嫁がば夫を天とせよと仰有いましたが、貴女は、夫に対してテンと貞操を尽して居ないぢやありませぬか。久之助さまがあれ丈矢釜しくお止めなさるのも聞かず、守宮別さまと年が年中そこら中を飛び廻つて居られたではありませぬか。貴女は自分に都合のよい所ばかりとつてゐらつしやるのですなア』

『ヘン神界の御用と人間界と一所にして貰ふてたまりますかい。一家の婦人としてなれば兎に角、このお寅は三千世界の立替立直しの日の出神様の御用をする生宮ですよ。神の柱と人間と一所にしてたまりますか。お前さまも永らく日の出さまの教を聞きながら、何と云ふ分らぬ事を仰有るのです』

§ 2

Ohana: 'Hai, hai, since your words cannot even reach the ears of Anata, I shall remain in Chinmoku.'

Otora: 'From now on, do not say anything more about Ohana. Even if you say she is the Ikimiya of the Otohime of Riugū, compared to the Ikimiya of Hinode-no-kami, the difference is as great as that between a Shiyujin and a slave. Moshi-moshi, Henro-sama, please Hai and have an Itsupuku.'

The man standing at the gate said, 'Hai, arigata,' took off his Fukaamigasa, and walked straight in; when they looked closely, it was a face they recognized from somewhere.

Takehiko: 'Gomen-nasaimase, I have not seen you for a while, but I am Takehiko.'

Otora: 'Naruhodo, is that you, Take-sama? If so, Naze did you not Nanori before entering? I thought I had not seen your face for about an Ichinen, so Itsutai Doko have you been?'

Takehiko: 'Hai, I have not been anywhere in particular. Because the things you Anatagatara Otsushiya change so much, I broke my Shinkau, and now I am in the land of Naniha, opening something called the Ōmichikuwai, publishing pamphlets, and acting as a Betsudōtai for the Root-baha. I saw in the newspaper a little while ago that Morimiyawake-sama had returned from abroad, so I thought he might be here and came to scout for a moment. Has he not appeared yet?'

Otora: 'Take-sama, has it not been quite a long time? Why do you not listen to the teachings of Hinode-no-kami as well? Morimiyawake-sama is also here.'

Otora hurriedly covered Ohana's mouth with her hand.

Otora: 'Kore, Ohana-sama, what kind of foolish things are you saying? Morimiyawake-sama has not come out yet, has he? You must have seen a dream. They do say it is a dream-Morimiyawake, after all.'

Ohana: 'In that case, shall we leave it as a dream?'

Takehiko: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha, then the Great Master has returned after all. That is interesting; I think I shall become a member of the Ōmichikuwai and have him write a pamphlet for me.'

Otora: 'Kore, kore, Take-sama, that will not do. Do you think you can have Morimiyawake-sama of the Crystal Mitama talk about such material pamphlets? Morimiyawake-sama likes sake more than bread. You are a pamphlet that has abandoned the teachings of Hinode-no-kami and lost your blessings. Please repent a little.'

Takehiko: 'Otora-sama, what you are saying makes no sense at all. A pamphlet means a small booklet. I am publishing thin magazines to spread the teachings of the Hensei-joshi to the world.'

Otora: 'E-e, what kind of foolish thing are you doing, having no sense at all? Are you trying to make this already clouded three-thousand-world even more clouded? If you do not repent a little, will the suffering of the people of the world not be prolonged? This is why they say that if you listen to the teachings of the Hensei-joshi, nothing good will come of it. Ah, there is not a single sincere person among any of them. Hinode-sama must have a hard time as well. How I pity the heart of the Great Miroku-sama of the Sokotsu-iwane.'

She wiped her tears with a bean-patterned hand towel.

Just then, Morimiyawake woke from his drunkenness, and while letting out a large yawn, 'A-a-a-a-a,' he came out slowly. Takehiko said in a voice as if he were pouncing on him,

Takehiko: 'Yo, is that Morimiyawake-sama? Yaa, I have met you at a convenient time. Kore, Otora-sama, you cannot tell lies. As you can see, the transformation appears right after, does it not?'

Ohana said, looking pleased,

Ohana: 'Fu-fu-fu-fu.'

Otora looked stern and said,

Otora: 'E-e, is that so? Ah, you are noisy.'

She was jutting out her chin.
