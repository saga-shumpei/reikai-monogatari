Chapter 6: The City of Idols [1635]

偶像都〔1635〕

§ 1

Burabāsa and Mariya once again began their Jiyunran of the Shigai of Jerusalem, and passed through the temple gate to see the Seisebarukuru Jiin, which is the most Jiueu monument in the Shinai.

Mariya: 'Seishisama, it is said that this temple is the Bashiyo where the Lord Christ was subjected to Takukei, and that it is built upon the ground of Golgotha; however, when considering it according to the Seishiyo, Golgotha ought to exist outside the Shigai. If the current Jiyōheki has been expanded from what it was in the Tauji, it might become a Mondai, but if it is in the Dōitsu Bashiyo, I believe we must recognize the rocky hill outside the Damascus Gate, which looks like a Zugaikotsujiyō and is currently a Bochi, as the ground of Golgotha.'

Burabāsa: 'As for us Ningen, the Shingi can by no means be known. It is a matter that the Daiseishiyu will determine upon His descent. By the way, I would like to hear the history of this Jiin.'

Mariya: 'If I am to speak of the history of this temple, as a result of excavations ordered by Emperor Constantine, the cave where the Lord Christ was buried was discovered; therefore, the Emperor's mother, Saint Helena-sama, made a pilgrimage to Jerusalem and discovered the cross of Christ here, and thus recognized this land as the sacred site of Golgotha, and it is said that the Jiin was first established here in the year 336. It is said that it was burned down by the Persians in the year 614, and was immediately rebuilt. I have heard that even after that, it has been destroyed, rebuilt, and repaired many times over, leading to the present day. Would you not like to view the interior of the temple? I shall guide you.'

Burabāsa: 'Yes, thank you.'

He followed behind Mariya and proceeded deep into the temple.

When they entered the Jiin, they found a structure like a labyrinth that was quite complex, and in addition, it was dim and dark where the rays of the sun did not sufficiently penetrate, giving it a somewhat lonely feeling. It became necessary for each to carry a candle in their hand. The air inside the temple was heavy and damp, and it was not a very pleasant place. Because the paving stones were all wet with moisture, if one were careless, it was frequent that one's feet would slip and one would be about to fall. For Burabāsa, who was accustomed to the bright life of Takasagoshima, where he thought that temples were peaceful and clean, he was struck by an unexpected feeling, and he was wrapped in a cloud of anxiety as if danger were pricking his body.

Whether it came from the Jewish quarter outside or originated from within, a strange and unpleasant odor assailed them. And the interior was filled with every site of historical significance related to the Takukei of Christ, giving a somewhat sorrowful and lonely feeling. It became a mood like when a spirit crosses the crossroads and reaches the entrance to hell.

Mariya looked back at Burabāsa and opened her mouth for the first time, and with a sorrowful air, said:

Mariya: 'Seishisama, this rectangular stone is said to be the one upon which the Lord Christ's body was placed by Nicodemus so that he could wrap it in a cloth with oil. This is the tomb of Joseph, and this is the tomb of Arimathea, and the one a little further ahead is the tomb of Nicodemus. And that place over there is the sacred place where it is said the Lord Christ appeared before His mother after He was resurrected.'

She pointed out one by one, with great care, the spear that pierced Christ, the prison into which Christ was thrown, and the place where the soldiers divided Christ's garments.

Burabāsa: 'Having viewed the relics of the Savior who sacrificed Himself for the sake of all people under heaven, I have received divine virtue that I cannot express in words. It is a constant truth from ancient times to the present that in any age, good people are persecuted by the Ningen of this vulgar and evil world. The Daiseishiyu of Rūto Bahā also, though His physical body is preserved, has been thrown into a prison in spirit, pierced by bayonets, bathed in all manner of social insults and mockery, and treated like a great villain; I truly wish that the people of the world would come to recognize the true Savior as soon as possible. The tomb of the Daiseishiyu of Tsuruku has been violently destroyed by the authorities, the altar has been demolished, and many believers have been unable to bear the oppression and have scattered in all directions; now, only those who have dedicated their lives to the pure faith are continuing their fervent belief for the sake of all people under heaven with a spirit of martyrdom, relying on the holy couple Uzunbara Chiyandā as their sole strength. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.'

『ハイ有難う』

とマリヤの後より寺内へ深く進み入る。

寺院内へ這入つて見ると、迷宮の様な構造で随分複雑して居て、加ふるに太陽の光線が十分徹らない薄暗闇で、何んとなく寂しい感じがする。それぞれ手に蝋燭を携帯せなければ成らなくなつて居る。寺内の空気は重くしめり勝で余り気分の良い所ではない。敷石は全部湿気で濡れて居るため、ウカウカして居ると脚下が辷つて転倒せむとすること屡である。平和にして清潔なるものは寺院だと思つて居た高砂島の明るい生活に馴れたブラバーサの心に取つては意外の感じに襲はれ、危険がチクチクと身に迫る様に何となく不安の雲に包まれにける。

外のユダヤ人街から来るのか、内部から発したのか知らぬが、一種異様の厭な臭気が襲つて来る。そして内部は凡てキリストの磔刑に関するあらゆる由緒ある場所に由つて充されて居て、何となく物悲しい寂しい感じを与へる。精霊が八衢を越えて地獄の入口に達した時の様な気分になつて来る。

マリヤはブラバーサを顧みながら初めて口を開き、さも愁た気に、

『聖師様、この長方形の石はニコデモがキリスト様の体に油の布を以て捲くために御身体をのせたと伝へられるもので御座います。これがヨセフの墓で此方がアリマヂエの墓で、その少し向ふにあるのはニコデモの墓で御座いますよ。そして彼所がキリストの復活された後母の眼に現はれたまふたといふ聖所ですよ』

キリストを刺した鎗、キリストを投入した牢獄、兵卒がキリストの衣をわかつた場所なぞ一々叮嚀に指し示すのであつた。

『天下万民のために犠牲とお成り下さつた救世主の御遺跡を拝観いたしまして、何とも言ひ得ない程私は御神徳を頂きました。何時の世にも善人は俗悪世界の人間から迫害されると云ふ事は古今一徹ですな。ルート・バハーの大聖主も肉体こそ保存されて在りますが、精神的に牢獄に投げ込まれ銃剣にて突き刺され、あらゆる社会の侮辱と嘲罵とを浴びせられ、且つ大悪人の如く扱はれて居られますが、何うか一日も早く天晴れ世界の人類が真の救世主を認める様になつて欲しいもので御座いますよ。ツルク大聖主の墓は官憲の手に暴破れ聖壇は破壊され数多の聖教徒は圧迫に堪へ兼ねて四方に離散し、今は純信な神に生命を捧げたものばかりが殉教的精神を以てウヅンバラ・チヤンダー聖主夫妻を唯一の力と頼んで、天下万民のために熱烈なる信仰を続けて居るのです。アヽ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

§ 2

Mariya, while drowned in tears, guided them to the exact spot where the Seishi had stood and the Jifujika of Christ was erected, and to the place where the Seibo Mariya received the Nakigara of Christ taken down from the Jifujika. At each of these spots, a chapel had been established, named in accordance with its significance.

Mariya: 'This is the tomb of Adam, and it is called the most sacred and mysterious place. It is handed down that as soon as the holy Onchi of Christ seeped into his head from the fissure in the rock, this Genjin Adam was instantly restored to life.'

She was smiling with a somewhat mysterious air.

Burabasa, filled with deep emotion, did not utter a single word, but followed behind Mariya while his heart palpitated with excitement. At the eastern end of the temple stands the chapel of Saint Helena. It is said that the northern Shindan is dedicated to the one repentant Nusubito who was nailed to the Jifujika alongside Christ. The main Shindan is said to be dedicated to the Kuōgō Saint Helena. Thirteen steps down into the basement from its side, the chapel of the discovery of the Jifujika is built. It is said that here, Saint Helena discovered the three Jifujika through a revelation in a dream.

Mariya: 'Saint Helena-sama discovered the three Jifujika through a revelation in a dream and built a chapel here, but since it was not known which of the three discovered was the Jifujika upon which Christ was hung, she had them touched to a severely ill person one by one to test them; it is said that one of them healed the sick person, and so it has been preserved as that of Christ. There is also the pillar to which Christ was bound, but it is hidden deep behind the wall of the Shindan, so it cannot be easily worshipped. However, there is a round hole in that wall, and deeply devout worshippers insert a pestle-like rod placed there into the hole, touch the pillar, and kiss the rod. Now, I shall guide you to the tomb of Christ-sama.'

Mariya stood at the lead and advanced deeper and deeper inside.

They arrived in front of the tomb of Christ, which was built of independent rectangular marble in the center of the temple. Both of them performed a respectful act of worship for a while, struck by the thought of longing for the Savior, and found themselves shedding tears unintentionally. It is placed inside a circular structure consisting of eighteen pillars, illuminated by numerous antique-style lamps. A temple guardian was there, and he said,

Guardian: 'You have come to worship well. Please offer a donation to the tomb of Christ-sama. It is for the sake of your afterlife and for your happiness in this world.'

He was demanding money with shrewd words.

Burabasa thought to himself,

Burabasa: 'Ah, how pitiful it is for the holy Christ-sama. Are you to be used as a means of livelihood for lowly guardians? The world is truly in the degenerate age of the end of the law.'

Sighing, he searched his pocket and offered a small amount of money in front of the tomb. The guardian picked up the money on the spot like a starving tiger and hid it in his pocket.

Mariya: 'The various chapels, tombs, Shindan, and other parts within this temple, and even the lamps illuminating the holy tomb, have their ownership determined respectively among the Greek Orthodox, Roman Catholic, and other Armenian sects. This is not only the case for this temple, but also for the places of religious significance scattered inside and outside of Jerusalem. Is this not truly a cynical irony? And this temple was the objective of the famous Crusades. The cry "Remember the Holy Sepulchre" was the shout that echoed through the towns and villages of the European nations at the time of the departure of the Second Crusade.'

『良くこそ御参拝に成りました。どうかキリスト様の御墓へ御賽銭をお上げ成さいませ。後生のため現世の幸福のためで御座います』

と抜目なき言葉でお賽銭を強要して居る。

ブラバーサは心の内にて、

と歎息しながら懐中を探つて少しばかりの賽銭を墓の前に捧げた。番僧は餓虎の如く其場で賽銭を拾ひ上げ、懐中へ隠して了つた。

『この寺院内の各種のチヤペルや墓や、神壇や其他寺内の各部分、又は聖き墓を照して居るランプに至るまで、ギリシヤ・オルソドツクス及びローマ・カトリックや其他アルメニヤ派の間にそれぞれ所有がきまつて居るのです。それは此お寺ばかりでは無く、エルサレムの内外に散在して居る宗教上の由緒ある場所に付いても同様です。実に皮肉なアイロニーぢやありませぬか。そしてこのお寺が彼の有名な十字軍の戦争の目的物であつたのです。「聖墓を記憶せよ」との声は、第二回十字軍の出征に際して欧羅巴諸国の町々や村落を通じての叫びだつたので御座います』

Speaker: 'The era when the countries of Europe raised even Crusades in their yearning for the Holy Sepulchre seems to have been one of very fervent faith, but today, faith has already fallen into decay and only materialistic notions have become prevalent, so it seems that such sacred sites and holy relics are no longer much regarded by the people of the world. It is shown in the teachings of Lute Baha that even God cannot prevail against the times, but I wish that the Holy Christ would return as soon as possible to restore the Holy Land to its ancient prosperity, to bathe all the people of the world in the bliss of the Pure Land of Anyō, and to make them realize the grace of Christ.'

Speaker: 'That is indeed so. The holy order to which I belong is waiting only for the return of Christ the Messiah. I have come to long for the day when the Messiah, who was reborn in that place called Takasago-jima, will return to this land.'

From this point, the two left the temple gate and began to walk through the city streets. They passed through a narrow, oriental street where butcher shops, vegetable stalls, and other general stores selling items appropriate to the land stood with their eaves pressed tightly together, and emerged onto what is called the Via Dolorosa.

Speaker: 'Seishi-sama, this is called the Via Dolorosa, and it is the historic site said to be the path that Christ walked from Pilate's palace to the land of Golgotha, that is, to the present Holy Sepulchre. And on this road, fourteen stations have been designated. Now, I shall guide you through them one by one.'

She stood in front and moved forward.

Barabasa nodded, saying, 'Indeed, indeed,' and followed along, savoring the experience with deep interest.

Speaker: 'This is the sad place where Christ was sentenced to crucifixion. The next is the place where He was made to bear the cross. Please, look and pay your respects to this chapel on the east side. The scene at that time is clearly depicted in relief.'

She walked on steadily while speaking.

Speaker: 'This is the place where Christ met His Mother, and it is a spot where fervent believers stop and do not move. Do you see that arch there, the "Ecce Homo"? That is the place where, after being interrogated by Pilate, Christ was brought out before the crowd of Jews and suffered various persecutions and mockery.'

Walking slowly with a tearful air, she looked back and stared at Barabasa's face.

Speaker: 'Jesus went out wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe. Pilate said to them, "Behold, this is the Son of Man," a fact recorded in the Gospel of Matthew; it is said that this is the spot where Christ fell for the second time. And this is the spot where it is said that He spoke to those who followed Him. It is clearly carved into this chapel.'

She guided him with great care and kindness.

○

The Holy Land of Jerusalem, adorned with the idols of Christianity, pained Barabasa's heart even more than in the case of the heathens; this was because the monks of later ages had fabricated each of the locations and histories recorded in the Bible, aiming at the purses of pilgrims. At a glance, one might take it for a simple expression of faith, and it is not impossible for us to look upon it again, listen to it again, and proclaim it again through the Kami Naobi and Ōnaobi, but he feared that it would be felt as something unpleasant to the eyes of the general public lacking faith or to the so-called Christians. Is it not also a lamentable proof that later monks and believers, being empty of internal knowledge, are seeking signs in the external world? Ah, he sighed deeply within his heart once more, wondering if it is not because of the loneliness in the hearts of believers who cannot recognize His own figure within the Gospel, the only legacy left to later ages, and cannot draw from it the spiritual spring, that they have created such idols to serve as at least some measure of consolation.

と叮嚀親切に案内したりける。

○

キリスト教の偶像を以て飾られたる聖地エルサレムは、異教徒の場合よりも勝つてブラバーサの心を痛めしめたのは、後世の僧侶輩が聖書に録されたる一々の場所や由緒なぞを捏造して、巡礼者の財布をねらつて居ることである。一寸見ると単純なる信仰の発露だらうと、神直日大直日に見直し聞直し宣り直すことも吾々に採つては出来得ない事も無いが、一般の信仰なき民衆やデモ基督教徒の眼には却つて不快に感ずるものたる事を恐れたのである。又後世の僧侶や信者がその内部的知識に空なるがため、外部に徴を求めむとして居る事の嘆ずべき一つの証拠では有るまいか。アヽ後世まで唯一の遺宝たる福音書の中に彼れ自身の姿を認め、それから霊泉を汲み得ることの出来ない信徒等の心の淋しさより、斯様な偶像を作り出してせめてもの慰安の料にして居るのでは有るまいか、なぞと又もや心の内にて長大嘆息をして居る。

§ 4

Mariya: 'Seishisama, you seem to be sighing deeply as you look upon these many idolatrous things; it appears that in every age, Lord Christ has never been correctly believed in or understood. Whether in the time when Lord Christ was persecuted or in this day, He is misunderstood by the world. And in the ages after He came to be worshipped throughout the world, it is not the true Lord Christ who is revered, but rather idols that humans have arbitrarily fashioned in His likeness; instead of believing in Christianity itself, they have mistaken the beautiful fruits flowing from it for the thing itself, and in the end, Christianity has lost its essential spirit, and instead of being the teaching of the Kingdom of God, it has fallen into being a means to bring about earthly happiness, albeit in a good sense. Therefore, I too grieve over the contradiction that this holy place is filled and adorned only with idols, and that the true Lord Christ cannot be recognized.'

Lamenting thus, Mariya continues to walk through the city.

原文（近代文語）

『聖師様、沢山の偶像的事物を御覧になつて非常に嘆息されて居る様で御座いますが、何時の世にも聖キリスト様は正しくは信仰され、又理解されなかつた様で御座います。キリスト様が迫害されなさつた当時と、今日とを問はず、世間から誤解されて居られます。そして普く世界から崇敬され玉ふ様になつた後の世は真のキリスト様では無くて人間が勝手にキリスト様に似せて作つた偶像を崇め、キリスト教そのものを信ずる代りに、それから流れ出づる美しい果実のみを夫と誤認して了ひ、終にキリスト教は肝心の精神を失ひ神の国の教である代りにそれは良き意味に於てではありますが、地上の幸福をもたらす手段と堕落して了つたので御座います。夫れゆゑ妾も此の聖地が偶像のみにて充たされ飾られ、真のキリスト様を認識し得ない事の矛盾を悲しむので御座います』

と悔やみながらマリヤは猶も市中を歩み続ける。
