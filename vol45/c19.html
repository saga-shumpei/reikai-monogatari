Chapter 19: 吹 Snow [1209]

吹雪〔1209〕

§ 1

Wielding the horns of jealousy, while dyeing her face brilliant red, stroking down her thundering chest, and leaving Magahiko behind in the living room, stylish and quick-witted Otora-sama advanced straight toward the kitchen.

When she visited Otami's living room, Otami had begun eagerly turning the pages of the scriptures.

Otora called out from the outside, 'Hey, hey, listen here, Otami-sama, what are you doing at such an hour of the night? After people have all fallen fast asleep, you must be writing some kind of letter or another; what a careless woman you are.'

When she thus called out so abruptly, Otami was startled, folded the scriptures in two, and placed them beside her, asking, 'Are you Otora-sama of the inner chambers? What on earth has brought you to visit on purpose in the middle of this night? I cannot make any sense of it and find it strange.'

Rolling her eyes wide, Otami in the height of her youth, Otora retorted, 'Otamisama in the prime of her youth, with so many suitors, it must surely be a matter of constant heart-stirring. The reason you have come here is that your devotion to Kami is merely for outward show, and you surely have other desires in your heart. Possessing such a beautiful body as yours, this is no time to be hesitating and dawdling. Hearing from the Ōkamisama that you must be quickly made to take a husband, rubbing my sleepy eyes, I have come all this way on purpose.'

Your mitama is without a doubt Tamanorihime, the inner consort of Hinodenokami. By the karmic connection between Kami and Kami, you must become husband and wife.

Quickly celebrate your wedding ceremony with Magahiko-sama, who dwells in the raw palace of Hinodenokami. If you disobey this divine oracle, it will be a grave crisis for your very body, and by the wrath of Kami, you will instantly plunge down into the nethermost realm of the root. No matter how much you may pine and long for Imoriwake-sama, it is like a flower on a high peak or the moon's reflection on the water; there is no way you could ever grasp it. Without harboring any such wild ambitions, horses go with horses and cows with cows, so celebrate the bridal union in good spirits with Magahiko-sama; how do you answer, what is your reply?'

When she berated her so sternly, Otami was thunderstruck, smitten in her breast as if a bolt of lightning had flashed across a clear sky, and for a while she was utterly speechless.

Otami: 'Otora-sama, just now you spoke as if I were doing something with Imoriwake-sama, but that is a terrible trouble to me, and you said I should become husband and wife with Magahiko-sama—is that meant seriously?'

Otora: 'If it were not serious, who on earth would have come out of her way when she is this sleepy? It was revealed through the divine oracle that your mitama is Tamanorihime-sama, and that is why, if we do not make you husband and wife with Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami even for a single moment, the divine administration of the divine realm will be delayed, so I came to urge you.'

お民『藪から棒のやうなお言葉、早速に御返事が出来ませぬ。どうか一週間程熟考の猶予を与へて下さいませ。さうすれば否とか、応とか御返事致しますから』

お寅『さてもさても歯切れのせぬお方ぢやな、なぜ神様の仰有る事を素直に聞かないのだ。十万億土に落されても構はないのですか』

お民『どうなつても仕方が無いぢやありませぬか。何程神様の御命令だと言つても自分の気に合はない夫をもつ事は一生の不愉快ですから、何程神様だつてそんな無理は仰有いますまい。私は女としての人間を作つて、その上で夫をもつ考へですよ。人形の家になつては困りますからな。ホヽヽヽヽ』

お寅『随分悪思想に染つたものだねえ。それだから今時の女は仕方が無いと云ふのだ、そんな剛情を張るものぢやありませぬ。「剛強必ず死して仁義王たり」と云ふ事を知つて居ますか、女と云ふものは仁義の心が肝腎だよ、剛強なは男に欠くべからざる特質だ。剛強にして仁義を保つのが男の中の男だよ、女に剛強の必要はない、サア早く返答して下さい。返答のないのは矢張り蠑螈別さまに野心があるのだらう』

お民『エヽ何と仰有つて下さつても、女の一生の一大事ですから、一週間熟考の余地を与へて貰はなくては御返事は出来ませぬ』

§ 2

Otora, wearing a look of deep displeasure, slammed the amado shut with a rattling crash, stepping firmly with hitoashihitoashi to stamp his shiko, kicking and scattering the koishi of the garden in all directions,

Otora: 'You stubborn-headed, vile ama-tsu-chiyo wretch, do you want Imoriwake that badly?'

cursing in this kuchigitana manner as he walked away. Left behind, Omin muttered to herself in hitorigoto,

Omin: 'Ah, how wretched it is! To be born an ichininmahe woman only to have a low-stumped, hunchbacked, heutanzura, penniless man as a husband—even Otorasama is going too far. No matter whose meirei it is from the gods, how could I ever kike such a thing? I may be a bit of a fool myself, but I still intend to hold my head high in jifuninnami. If I were to become the wife of such a miserable Magahiko, I wonder how much wiser it would be to bite my tongue and die right now, though I cannot quite tell. Why on earth is such a man serving as a fukukyōshiyu? The Kamisama of koko must truly have a great fondness for mischief. Up to nine parts out of ten, the only reason I made sankei to this mountain is because of those tender words spoken to me by Imoriwake-sama: "Wait for a while; once I get my hands even on the ten thousand ryō of gold that Otora is hiding, I will take you away to a fine place, and there the two of us shall live happily together." I have never forgotten those kind words, not even in my dreams. And yet, for Otorasama to take Magahiko-sama as her husband—how thoroughly, how utterly she has looked down upon this Omin! Otorasama must have suspected that Imoriwake-sama and I were too close, and came over with all that harsh talk just to test my feelings. Oh, how ridiculous! As for being the mihama of Gyokusokuhime, I am not an Omin who would be fooled by such tricks. Ah, I am sick of it all. If the moon would only come out somehow, I wonder if I should escape from koko right away? Tonight is the twenty-first day's moon, so it will not be long before it rises. Ah, yes, exactly. The most prudent tsumori is to pack up my belongings now and make my escape. Still, unless I say a word to Imoriwake-sama beforehand, there is no help for it if he complains of grievance later. Ah, what on earth should I do? Most likely he is drunk and asleep by this hour. Ah, what should I do?'

muttering thus to herself, she was torn in her mind and lost as to what course to take.

Without delay, Otora went and banged on the doors of Matsuhiko and Matsuhime's residence, midnight notwithstanding.

Otora: 'If you please, Jōgihime-sama, won't you wake up for a moment?'

Jōgihime replied from within,

Matsuhime: 'Hai, I am still awake, so please, do come right in. Isn't it rather late?'

Otora: 'Will it cause you any trouble if I enter?'

Matsuhime: 'Come, come, please make yourself at home.'

speaking thus, she slid open the monoguchi with a rattle, took Otora by the hand to lead her quietly into the tatami room, stepped down into the garden once more to shut the door of the monoguchi tightly against the night wind, placed an earth-kneaded hibachi before them, and the two sat facing each other there. In the next room, the snoorous breathing of Matsuhiko and Ochiyo could already be heard.

Matsuhime: 'Otorasama, in visiting me at this dead of night, has some urgent matter arisen?'

Otora: 'Hai, the truth of the matter concerns Magahiko. Up until now, he firmly believed himself to be the living shrine of Giritenjō-Hinodewake and has served diligently as a fukukyōshu, but now that he has learned that you have a master, he has given up all hope completely. Because of that, if we do not find a wife for Magahiko as well, he will go to ruin. Therefore, worrying over this, I went out of my way to force my way into the place where Omin, a believer hailing from Kimokake Village, is staying, and said to her, "Won't you become Magahiko's wife?" But she raised a great fuss and simply refuses to agree to it. She says she wants a week to think it over thoroughly and give her answer then, which makes me so angry I cannot help it. If we dawdle, there is no telling where she might elope with Imoriwake-sama. Therefore, on one hand to prevent Imoriwake's romance, and on the other to set Magahiko's mind at ease—killing two birds with one stone—how would it be? Would it not be possible for you to use your divine virtue and authority to persuade Omin over to our side?'

お寅『もし上義姫様、一寸起きて下さいますまいか』

上義姫は中より、

Matsuhime: 'Is that so? If things actually go that way, it is most convenient. However, since the great Ōkamisama of Matsudaihi has fortunately graced us with His presence, I shall first inquire thoroughly before speaking of it. As there is probably nothing that must be said tonight, we can leisurely negotiate about it tomorrow.'

Otora: 'No, no, such dilatory ways will not do. The truth is, I wish to have this settled one way or another within tonight.'

As they are thus talking, Okiku and Magahiko come over,

Okiku: 'Pardon me, is Mother here?'

From within the door, Otora says,

Otora: 'That voice is Okiku, is it not? I have told you repeatedly that a young girl should not walk alone at night, yet you are a child who will not listen.'

Okiku: 'Mother, I came together with Maga-yan, that bent-waist Maga-yan.'

Otora: 'Maga-yan, did I not ask you to tend to the watch-dog duties for Imoriwake-sama? What have you come to a place like this for? I am worried, having gone to Ootami's room and come to Matsuhime-sama's chamber at this late hour, thinking to arrange a match for you. Come, hurry home and be the watch-dog for Imoriwake-sama; as long as you are there, even if Ootami comes, it will be all right.'

Maga: 'If that is so, Otora-sama, I shall go and perform my watch-dog duties from now on. But I am leaving Okiku-sama by your side, you see.'

Otora: 'There is no help for it. In that case, Okiku, come on in.'

Okiku: 'Auntie, pardon me.'

So saying, she opens the door herself, closes it again, and sits down demurely beside Matsuhime and Otora. Unable to bear her curiosity about the conversation between Matsuhime and Otora, Magahiko presses his ear against the outside of the wall and eavesdrops on their talk. The withered-tree wind brings scattered snow, which flutters and brushes against Magahiko's cheeks as it passes.
