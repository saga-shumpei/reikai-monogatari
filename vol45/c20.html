Chapter 20: 蛙行列 [1210]

蛙行列〔1210〕

§ 1

Imoriwake, oblivious to everything around him, was dead drunk and snoring like thunder, when Omin gently slid open the back door and crept in, trembling all over.

Omin: 'Sensei, something terrible has happened. I cannot stay here any longer. I am escaping from here tonight, so I came to give you a quick word. Please do not think ill of me.'

Imoriwake suddenly woke from his drunken stupor, sat up, and rubbing his eyes said,

Imoriwake: 'Aren't you Omin? What do you mean by something terrible? If we dawdle and Otora finds us, it will be disastrous for both you and me, so tell me your business quickly and get out of here.'

Omin: 'What I mean by terrible is none other than this: just a moment ago, Otora-sama came out to my living room and insisted that I become Magahiko's wife. Oh, how disgusting, as if I would ever become his wife, even if I died! The only reason I came here is because I have a promise with you, and I look forward to catching glimpses of your face during your service; that is why I came to tell you this, and since I will be heading back ahead of you now, if you have any kindness in you, please chase after me later.'

Imoriwake: 'That is terrible! You go on ahead as far as Noguchi Forest and wait for me there. Taking advantage of the fact that I am now going to Otora's Matsuhimeyakata while she is away, and having more or less figured out where Otora keeps her hidden money, I will wrap nine thousand ryo of gold around my waist and catch up with you later.'

As he urged her on, Omin smiled sweetly and said,

Omin: 'Imoriwake-sama, you must promise! In that case, I will go on ahead for a moment...'

So saying, fearing a long stay and wanting to leave before the moon came out, she hurried down the slope panting. Imoriwake immediately changed his clothes, prepared his straw raincoat and hat, tightly fastened nine thousand ryo of gold inside his garment, put on his leggings and straw sandals, firmly grasped his iron-tipped staff, and just as he was about to leap out the doorway, he frantically struck his forehead against a pillar, uttered a single groan, and collapsed on the spot.

That such a thing was happening was more than a mere mortal could know, while Otora was absorbed in earnest efforts to obstruct the secret agreement between Imoriwake and Omin, sitting knee to knee with Matsuhime and indulging in whispers.

Otora: 'Hey, Okiku, even though they say it is time to sleep, a child should not be staying up until the middle of the night. This child is already seventeen and never listens to a single word her parents say. I am truly at my wits' end.'

Okiku: 'Mother, somehow my eyes are wide awake, and I cannot sleep a wink. Tonight is the twenty-first night, so the moon will soon cast a sickle-like light upon the earth and make its way up Kita-yama, so it would be much better to go moon-viewing.'

Otora: 'Don't talk such foolishness. Outside, the winter wind is howling and it is snowing. What is fun about moon-viewing on a night like this? And what will you do if you catch the flu from the wind?'

Okiku: 'Mother, it is snowing—is that not all the more splendid? In the sky is the moon, down below is the snow, and right there emerges the chrysanthemum flower in bud, so is it not like gazing at the moon, snow, and flowers all at once? Such a fine opportunity rarely ever comes.'

Otora: 'What a saucy child. Won't you hurry up and put her to bed? Now, Matsuhime-sama, as you can see, she is such a tomboy, and I am truly having a hard time handling her.'

Matsuhime: 'Indeed. Why are young girls nowadays so mischievous? Even Ochiyo says nothing but cheeky things and talks back to us with twisted logic, leaving us with no recourse.'

お寅『コレお菊、モウお休みと云ふのに、夜更まで子供が起きてゐるものぢやないよ。此子はマア十七にもなつて一寸も親の言ふ事を聞かない子だ。本当に困つ了わ』

お寅『馬鹿な事お言ひでないよ。外は凩が吹いて吹雪がして居るよ。こんな夜さに月見したつて何が面白いか。又風に当つてインフルエンザにでも罹つたら何うするのだい』

お寅『コマシヤクレた子だな。早く休ましてお貰ひなさらぬか。モシ松姫さま、此通りお転婆娘で、本当に親も手こずつてゐますのよ』

松姫『さうですね、今時の女の子はどうして、これ程ヤン茶になるのでせう。お千代だつてコマシヤクレた事ばかり云つて、私達に逆理屈をこね、仕方がありませぬわ』

Otora: 'That is so, indeed; if such a child were to be given a modern education, she would undoubtedly become completely unsuited to her parents' expectations. I intend to stop her schooling at the ordinary level; if I were to enter her into a school like Kauchikuji Girls' School, she would trouble her parents by uttering such trivial sophistries as the equality of men and women or the expansion of women's rights.'

Matsuhime: 'Does that Kauchikuji have a girls' school? If so, I suppose the daughters of priests and the like enroll in it.'

Otora: 'My word, not a single daughter of a priest enrolls there! They are all just the daughters of Bishiya and Shiyuda, and every single day they are taught nothing but worthless things like ball-playing, marathon running, tennis, and dancing.'

Ho ho ho ho ho! That surely cannot be Kauchikuji Girls' School; it must be an upper girls' school. You must have read the character for "higher" (kau) by splitting it into "bamboo" (take) and "temple" (tera).

Otora: 'By the way, Matsuhime-sama, what shall we do about Magahiko's marriage problem? I am most eager to settle it within this very evening, come what may.'

Matsuhime: 'Now then, in any case, I shall go directly to meet Omin-san myself, listen carefully to her honorable opinions, and exert myself so that the matter may be settled as harmoniously as possible.'

Otora: 'That would be most helpful; I beg of you to succeed through your eloquence and divine virtue.'

Matsuhime: 'In that case, I shall now proceed to Omin-san's sitting room.'

So saying, she descends the narrow two-hundred-step staircase. Otora and Okiku follow right behind Matsuhime. Matsuhime walks straight toward the room adjacent to the kitchen, which is Omin's bedroom, making her way through the drifting snow. When Otora returns to Imoriwake's sitting room, lo and behold, he is collapsed right there in his travel attire.

Otora: 'Goodness me, what on earth is the meaning of this? Hey, Imori-sama, why are you lying flat out here in such solemn attire? Oh my, where on earth did Magahiko go? Since you were charged with being the watchman, it seems you were wandering about at night while the snow was falling. What a troublesome man you are; hey, Okiku, bring some water.'

Okiku: 'When you say bring water, if we do not go all the way down to draw it, there will not be a single drop to be had. Rather than that, just pinch his nose; if you do that, he will surely regain consciousness.'

Otora, thinking this a capital idea, grips Imoriwake's nose firmly with her right hand as usual, and strikes his back three or four times with her left. Imoriwake gasps back to consciousness,

Imoriwake: 'Omin, oh how you have saved me! At the very end, while running, I tripped and it was a terrible ordeal. I was just on the verge of making a netherworld journey in utter collapse. That woman Otora came chasing after me…'

Otora fiercely grabs him by the collar,

'You rascal Imoriwake, what on earth are you talking about? What is this you say about Omin?'

Startled by this voice, Imoriwake opens his eyes wide to see Otora glaring at him with teeth clenched and trembling violently, wearing a face like Emma having licked a darasuke pill, while she tightly grasps his collar.

Imoriwake: 'What? I just had a dream. It, it, it, it, it is nothing at all; let go of me, it hurts, it hurts!'

Otora: 'Where on earth do you intend to go dressed in such extravagant leg guards?'

Imoriwake: 'What? I just thought I would like to meet Matsuhime-sama.'

Otora: 'To go to Matsuhime-sama's place wearing travel attire… What on earth is the meaning of this? Is it not rather suspicious?'

Imoriwake: 'What? I just intend to go as far as the grand hall to offer my thanks.'

Otora: 'Is there any need to go today, of all days, while this snow is falling? Do not make such a fool of me, treating me like a blind person…'

Imoriwake: 'What? Because the snow is falling, I thought snow would pack into the teeth of my geta and make me trip, so I put on straw sandals instead.'

お寅は合点だと蠑螈別の鼻を例の如くグツと右の手で捻ぢ、左の手で背を三つ四つ喰はした。蠑螈別はハツと気がつき、

蠑螈『お民、ヨウ助けて呉れた。到頭走る最中、蹴つまづいて、ひどい事だつた。もうスツテの事で幽冥旅行をやるところだつた。お寅の奴追ひかけて来やがつて………』

お寅はグツと胸倉を握り、

Otora: 'Is there any such thing as putting on heavy travel gear just to go to a grand hall that is no more than five or six ken away? Moreover, wearing straw raincoats and hats with such elaborate politeness—what in the world is this? The place you are going to must be outside, right?'

Imori: 'Yeah, it's outside. I am just going to pay my respects near the sacred tree at the base of the pine tree.'

Otora: 'If you make too much of a fool out of me, I'll twist your nose off!'

Imori: 'No, just spare my nose.'

Otora: 'If so, shall I pinch you up instead?'

Imori: 'Nooo, pinching me in this cold weather is something I'd rather avoid. Otora, please forgive me already. I am not going anywhere else anymore.'

Otora: 'Hey, you Hiyōrokudama, what do you take this Otora for? Even if she is called Ushitora, the Shiranamionna of the village of Ukiki, she is an elder sister whom nobody fails to know! Do you think you can pull the wool over this elder sister's eyes? There is no way a scoundrel's power could ever deceive them! Come on, confess cleanly and frankly; if you do not confess, affection will overflow into a hundredfold hatred, and I'll twist and crush you to pieces!'

Imori: 'Actually—ugh, the truth is... well, the truth is just the truth. No matter what you tell me to say, while you are grabbing me by the collar, my breath is so tight I can't even speak, can I? Let go, let go!'

Otora: 'If so, I'll let go, so confess frankly. You intend to elope with Omin tonight, don't you? Omin is waiting around the forest of Noguchi, right?'

As she spoke, she suddenly let go of the hand with which she had been gripping his collar. The moment Imoriwake stood up straight, the oval gold coins in his pocket dropped with a rattling sound. Seeing this, Otora's anger reached the peak of her heart, and she became like one mad,

Otora: 'You thieving rascal, you intended to scoop up the money and elope with Omin, didn't you! Wait, just wait, I'll make you rue this before long!'

Flustered, she tripped over the brazier, and her overexcited body suddenly fell flat on its face with a thud, banged her forehead hard against the corner of a pillar, cried out 'Ouch!', and crouched down right on the spot. Imoriwake quickly picked up the oval gold coins, reattached them to his waist, took his Kongo-staff in hand,

Imori: 'Otora, I am going away on a brief training journey, so I leave the house in your care. They say youth and age are uncertain, and meeting is the beginning of parting; if there is a bond between us, let us meet again in the future. This is a keepsake from me to you, Otora-sama, on the occasion of our parting.'

So saying, he tapped her head repeatedly with his Kongo-staff, said 'Adieu!', and dashed away like clouds and mist. Otora, whose senses had been dulled by hitting her forehead against the pillar, regained consciousness the moment her head was tapped, raised her face, and looked outside to see Imoriwake running about a cho down the slope, illuminated by the sharp sickle-like moon rising just then. Otora went frantic,

Otora: 'You wretch Imoriwake, making a fool of this Otora!'

Raising a voice of anger, with her skirts flying, she chased after him over the snowy path, tumbling and stumbling.

Now Matsuhiko, starting with Matsuhime and consulting with Mankou, Gosankou, Aku, Taku, Teku, and others, performed purification at Kobakayama, pacified one by one the deities of heaven and earth numbering eight million who protect the Gokuritsutei no Ōkami and the Misankyō, expounded the truth to Matsuhime, Ochiyo, Okiku, and the receptionist Bunsuke and others, had this sacred place kept pure and righteous, and then Matsuhiko, Mankou, Gosankou, Aku, Taku, Teku, and their party left Kobakayama behind and quickened their pace toward the forest of Ukiki.

Incidentally, Magahiko, hearing that Omin had fled away from this mansion, determined that he would find her no matter where she went, tucked up his skirts just like that, and chased after Otora while maintaining a distance of about three cho, dashing away with a pit-a-pat toward the forest of Noguchi as his mark.
