§ 1

After Magahiko's Giritenjiyōhi no Ide no Kami no Ikimiya, guided by Ochiyo, went to Jiyōgihime's mansion, Matsuhiko's party, along with Granny Otora-sama and Okiku, a total of eight people, were drinking tea, stuffing themselves with fruits, and indulging in tales of the road.

Bunsukejii-sama, the receptionist, came over there at a slow, shuffling pace,

Bunsuke: 'If I may, Otora-sama, someone from the hiromahe is asking you to come over, making a terrible fuss. It's a bit awkward in the middle of looking after the guests, but please go there for a moment. I will keep them company until then.'

Otora: 'Another mad believer must have started rampaging again. Oh well, it cannot be helped; I shall go and calm them down for a bit. Matsudaisama, excuse me for a moment. Ochitakihiko will keep you company in my place.'

Matsuhiko: 'Thank you for your trouble, please take your time and go, for I am resting comfortably here.'

Mankō: 'Say, Gosankō, Imoriwake-sama, as I surmise, has drunk himself into a stupor and is sleeping in the inner room. There is no mistake about it. And he had a lover's quarrel with that Granny Otora-sama. It is definitely settled as that.'

Gosan: 'Didn't he keep calling out Takahime, Takahime? I wonder if he has some connection with Takahime-sama of the Izanagi-kyō.'

Mankō: 'I have no idea at all, but isn't Takahime-sama, saying they are old acquaintances, devoting herself wholeheartedly to Hinodewake no Mikoto and coming all the way from Jitentōtō to the mansion of Saien with her eyes narrowed in anger? The Takahime whom Imoriwake-sama here speaks of must be a different person with the same name.'

Gosan: 'I wonder if that is so. Since there are many people in the world with the same name, it might be so. Still, women with the name Taka seem to have a great many admirers, don't they?'

Bunsuke: 'Everyone, you just said that Takahime-sama is staying at the mansion of Saien, is that true?'

Mankō: 'Yes, it is true. I vaguely heard that she was someone who founded something like the Uranai-kyō. She is an awfully noisy propagandist.'

Bunsuke: 'Well, if that is so, she might be the very Takahime whom the teacher of Imoriwake is searching for.'

Gosan: 'It seems that this Takahime had a disciple called Kurohime. And Kurohime supposedly had a long-headed husband named Takayamahiko.'

Bunsuke: 'Hearing that, there is no doubt she is the Takahime whom Imoriwake-sama is searching for. Is she staying at the mansion of Saien now?'

Gosan: 'Yes, she is staying there. Did Takahime-sama also have some deep spiritual bond with the sect's leader here?'

Bunsuke: 'She certainly did, she certainly did. Because they are spiritual husband and wife, no matter what, unless Takahime-sama comes, Imoriwake-sama's conduct will not be corrected. Making Imoriwake-sama reform is Takahime-sama's duty, as she is the Ikimiya of Giritenjiyō.'

Gosan: 'Hey, isn't Magahiko-sama the Giritenjiyō no Ide no Kami? If there are two such figures, wouldn't it become impossible to tell which is true and which is false?'

Bunsuke: 'The truth of the matter is, until the whereabouts of Takahime are known and she is welcomed here, Magahiko-sama acts as a substitute because Giritenjiyō-sama is indispensable even for a single day. Magahiko-sama's true spirit is Michinarinari-sama, you see.'

Mankō: 'What a flexible deity indeed.'

Imoriwake, dead drunk in the next room, enduring the pain of the head beaten by Otora, no sooner let the talk of Takahime enter his ears than his drunkenness suddenly vanished, pressed his ear against the sliding door, and listened so as not to miss a single word.

Granny Otora-sama came briskly marching back there,

Otora: 'Everyone, I have kept you waiting terribly long.'

Bunsuke: 'Now, Otora-sama, you must not get angry. According to what these people are saying, that Takahime-sama whom Imoriwake-sama talks about when he is drunk has come to the mansion of Saien.'

Otora: 'Ah, you can well say it is unseemly! Do not make too much a fool of me. Even this Otora is not a fool, for I possess some measure of spirit and awareness.'

Otora: 'Ah, what are you saying? You are going to throw away this hateful self of mine and go to the place of Takahime, who is like a nightwalker, are you? If so, then go. As a parting gift, like this—'

Saying thus, using all her tremendous strength, she twisted his nose upward. Imoriwake grew dizzy with his vision blurring, and fell flatly to the spot with a heavy thud.

Startled by this sound, the five persons Mankō, Gosankō, Aku, Teku, and Taku rushed helter-skelter into the room,

Gosan: 'Now, now, Grandmother, what kind of rough business are you doing even while dwelling in the path of the Gods?'

Otora: 'Ho ho ho ho! A dog that has eaten a wild boar must have something different about it, I suppose. Come, go yonder. Imoriwake-sama has this sort of illness off and on. From now on, I will infuse him with life and revive him, so if too many of you crowd around noisily, his spirit will wander in the intermediate state and fail to return to its original scabbard, which would be troublesome.'

Mankō: 'Shall we leave this place to Otora-sama and go to the next room to have some tea?'

Everyone: 'Yeah, let us do that then.'
