Chapter 10: 棚卸志 [1200]

棚卸志〔1200〕

§ 1

Defeated in love, Magahiko, swaying his bent waist hither and thither with a waddling piyo-piyo motion, striking his forehead with his right hand, and turning the palm of his left hand upward like a beggar receiving alms, with a posture both comical and absurd, felt anger and foolishness. Mocked by little Ochiyo, though he thought to himself, 'Damn it,' dealing with a mere child-like woman was not worth his time. If he were laughed at for lacking adult dignity, the manhood of Magahiko-tsukasa would lose its foil completely. To endure is safe and long-lasting; to rage invites self-destruction, as Imoriwake-sama said. Am I not a man as well? Let me tighten my haraobi firmly and do my best.

Nay, wait a moment, wait a moment. This is the boundary line between good and evil. The worm of his belly stirs, and wildly incites him, 'Resent! Resent!' This arbitration is quite difficult; even Granny Otora's chivalry cannot settle it easily. Ah, there is no help for it, there is no help for it. Shall I proceed right through the middle between good and evil? Oh, yes, exactly. If I do so, the worm will surely be somewhat contented. Whispering thus in a low voice, kicking aside the tatami mats, catching his foot in a palm-frond broom—ah, how annoying, obstructing my path. This broom, which sweeps away love, lies there unasked. Pardon me, oh broom, oh broom; I have my own resolve.

Matsuhime surely cannot be the only woman in the world. Among the old and young, men and women gathered in this sanctuary, there might be a nice one who catches my eye. Whispering 'Let us inspect the heads,' he briskly hurried up toward the platform, out of breath, gave a composed, affected cough, gulped down a cup of hot water, tightly gripped a white folding fan in one hand, struck the desk two or three times, gazed at the assembly of believers, and at the moment his eyes flashed, a gentle figure of barely sixteen or seventeen, looking somewhat beautiful, caught his attention, and fixing his gaze upon the woman, he tilted his head and stared fixedly.

Her style was like that of a summer snake eyeing a frog. At this bizarre appearance, the company looked up, thinking it made no sense, and involuntarily their gazes focused all at once upon Magahiko, pressing upon him and illuminating his face. Yet for some reason, the former nice one hid her face and crouched down, provoking him with her refusal to turn her gaze toward Magahiko. Once more he gave a cough of a-hem, gulped down a cup of hot water, and striking the desk pat-pat with his fan like a storyteller, he said, 'Welcome indeed, everyone, to this pilgrimage! You people of deep karmic ties with the God of the Urayai Teachings, listen with reverence to this sermon by Magahiko.'

Gathered in the same hall, Magahiko, who is permitted to preach the sacred teachings of the gods, and you all who listen, are brought together by the benevolent deities; since there is a deep bond, living in the same age and dwelling upon the same earth, if there were not karmic ties, you might not even hear a single word of the sacred teachings throughout your entire life, and how many days would pass away unreckoned? Considering this, you must have gathered here together through the divine soul's karmic affinity with me. Even taking shelter from the rain under the same tree or drawing water from the same river is said to be a deep bond. In this sacred place where the Lord God of Great Compassion and Great Mercy...

gathers, experiencing truly warm grace, just like chicks crying piyo-piyo-piyo, nestled beneath the wings of their parent, rising courageously with a shared destiny to grow and flourish—do not forget this gratitude of yours, everyone! Fulfill the righteous path of faith and love, and the Lord God will surely love and save our spirits. The path of husband and wife is just the same; without karmic affinity, no matter what, you cannot create the living palace of the gods, nor accomplish the sacred divine work. This Magahiko is also a bachelor and has no wife as yet, but now at last the time has come, and a believer who is to become my bride has appeared here as well.

Whether willing or unwilling, if it is a bond ordained by the Lord God, we can never oppose it. Everyone, understand that well! The person upon whom the white-feathered conquering arrow of Magahiko's choice has now fallen must obey the divine will without yes or no, strictly guarding faith and love, and acting in awe of the divine command, taking heed toward all present. The person who is to become my wife has somehow appeared in this place, turning her face away in shyness, lost in thought. Now, now, you there! If you keep the path of the gods, what need is there for hesitation? There is nothing to be ashamed of whatsoever.

To become the wife of Magahikotsukasa, the vice-instructor of the Urayanai teaching, and to serve alongside your husband in the righteous work of inviting countless believers up into the heavenly paradise to save and help them, is a glory second to none under heaven; resolution is of the first importance for a person, and if there should be any such thing as being deceived by people of the world and rashly casting aside the bond tied by Kami, that person must weep throughout the span of a single lifetime in a condition of widowerhood, widowhood, and solitude as the punishment of Kami; comprehending and sorting out this logic here, to joyfully accept whatever Magahikotsukasa says, no matter who you might be, is what is called filial piety toward Kamisama; ah, Kamunagara kamunagara.

Swearing by Kami, Magahiko transmits the path of truth; I shall step down from the platform from now on, and the next teacher is Otorasama; listen attentively to the precious talk and receive a heap of divine virtue, all you people—such things coming out of the mouth without restraint; to achieve the ambitions of love, dragging out Kami like a bonito and preaching away is truly shameless.

And in the inner room with Imoriwake, engaging in a lover's quarrel that even dogs would not eat; playing lovey-dovey to her heart's content, pinching arms, twisting noses; collapsing with a thud and making the eyes spin; while performing an unprecedented, great sensational event and a chaotic uproar, putting on an unconcerned face and feigning ignorance, putting on garments and straightening the collar, holding a Shinto fan in the right hand, making the purple hakama rustle and rustle as she advances grandly toward the broad hall with an offensive face, is the number-one enthusiastic follower of Urayanai, chosen as the official of internal affairs, the ascent to the platform of old hag Oto.

Oto stands leisurely upon the platform, gazes around at the crowd below from corner to corner, gives a single cough of clearance, pours water from a tin bottle, grabs a cup with her left hand, swallows a gulp with a guzzle, clears her throat once more with an ehem, and Oto opens her mouth and says: Everyone, you have indeed visited well, and Kamisama will surely rejoice and bestow a heap of divine virtue upon you; the founder-sensei Imoriwake was to ascend the platform, but because the divine world affairs are extremely busy and the arrival of countless believers is bustling, with the sake reception busy, turning this way and that way without even a moment to spare; though that is so, for the preaching day established by Kamisama to be absent is also how things go, so though it is a hardship for you, Oto, please replace me and kindly preach the precious divine words to the entire gathering in detail—such was the divine decree.

Having no way to refuse, even though this old hag knows herself to be unskillful, whatever is said is by a divine pillar, the command of Imoriwake; receiving it and stepping up onto the platform right now is how things have come to be; in the first place, to believe in the divine path is not some small matter like trying to live safely and uneventfully for a mere hundred, two hundred, or three hundred years of human life; it is for the sake of going to that heavenly country where the gods dwell in the heavenly realm without night or winter, living on through countless kalpas, where heavenly beings eternally enjoy freedom from aging and death, and live flourishingly; after leaving this world, directly saved and guided, the progression of five winds and ten rains being good, the wind playing infinite music, the grasses and trees of mountains and fields enjoying a natural dance—a paradise where they live in joy; saved into that heavenly country, transcending time and space for thousands and tens of thousands of generations eternally, and receiving happiness without knowing any limits, that is the very faith for which we believe; the founder of Urayanai is an angel sent by the gods of the spiritual country of Takamanohara; the leaves of words of this angel are the exact same thing as the direct words of the true Kami who created this world; do not ever doubt them; taking the body of a person without wisdom, it is impossible to judge the precious leaves of words of Kami; even if Imoriwake-sama were to tell you to climb a mountain upside down, and speak of unreasonable things, you must never rebel against it; simply, in all things, faith is the foremost rescue boat; we humble human beings drifting in the mud of this world, no matter what is said, are helped by the saving hands of Kamisama, and to safely cross the dreamlike floating world where one cannot see an inch ahead is the duty of the believers of the Urayanai kami; pray, everyone, this old hag's—

The sliding doors are knocked over, the sake cups are trampled into dust. That a wild, drunken revelry breaks out is but the mischief of sake and Rinki. To say nothing of Imoriwake, even Magahiko-sama and Otora-sama, though they speak with such grand eloquence, since their words and deeds do not match, how could they possess any authority? Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. To the Magabito who appear in the world, proclaiming goodness on the surface while secretly committing evil, deceiving this world, may the Ametsuchi strike down with a Daitetsutsui and chastise them. Before the Onmahe of the God of grace, I humbly revere and pray.

Whether the Asahi shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, whether the stars fall from the Ten, the Oshi of God is everything. You must never abandon it. Even if the conduct of the Kyōso goes against the heart of God, or if he be a vessel of Magatsu, there is no change in the original Kamisama of this world. Do not rely on people who have manifested in this world; though invisible to the Nikugan, revere, love, and believe in the Lord God who surely exists, and without flagging or yielding, encourage your faith.

Praying that you may be encouraged, I, a maiden, disregarding my own youth, convey this to you all. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. May your spirits be blessed.

Ochiyo, feeling her courage multiplied a hundredfold by the joy of meeting her parents, and with a clever, resolute spirit, boldly and unreservedly laid bare all her daily observations. She descended the platform with composure, offered a light bow to the assembly, and walked slowly toward the mansion of Yoshikohime.

There were not a few believers who heard for the first time from the lips of the maiden Ochiyo the ugly reality of the Kyōso Imoriwake. All of them, with expressions contrary to their expectations, buzzed with noise, and the discussion comparing the characters of Imoriwake, Otora-baba-sama, Magahiko, and Ochiyo blossomed. From among the crowd, a red-faced man of forty stood up abruptly:

Kumakō: 'Everyone, I am called Kumakō the drunkard, and I was a believer in the Uraru sect. You may or may not know the Uraru sect, but it is a stylish teaching that says, "Drink and revel, for the future is dark..." I drank away mountains, drank away the fields and paddies inherited from my ancestors, drank away my house, drank away my storehouse, and stored everything in the tank of my stomach so that no regrets would remain. Because I drank so much, my wife took the children and returned to her parents' home, and I, Kumakō the drunkard, in the loneliness of being single, was invited by someone and joined the Uranai sect. The Uranai sect is a teaching of truly good conduct, where there is peace under heaven, unity between high and low, no strife and no resentment. I heard that although the Kyōso-sama likes sake, he does not endure it to drink it himself; even if he puts it into his own mouth, it is the Kamisama who drinks it, and he himself does not drink a single drop. But to my surprise, if I listen to what Ochiyo-sama said just now, he is dead drunk from morning till night, using the Kamisama as an excuse. Children are honest, so there is rarely any mistake. Ah, how ridiculous! I am angry to think I have been deceived until now. I am not believing in a human being, so one might say it is fine as long as I believe in the Kamisama, but is it not truly outrageous that the Kyōso and other executive officers, who are the intermediaries of God, are squeezing the lifeblood of the people from morning till night, drinking and getting drunk? Even with this, do you still believe in this teaching? If you take as a model a teacher who cannot put into practice what he says with his mouth, no matter how grand, your own conduct will inevitably become bad. You all must have precious sons and daughters, but if they are taught such things, the harm will reach not only your own families but become a poison to the whole world.'
