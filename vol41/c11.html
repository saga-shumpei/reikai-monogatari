Chapter 11: 麓の邂逅 [1115]

麓の邂逅〔1115〕

§ 1

Riūn: 'In the Gankutsu of Takateruyama, led by wolves, I encountered people I did not expect, and while receiving an unexpected Shimei, I, Riūn, a Sendenshi of the noble Mikoto, Ōgonhime, who adorns the Akino, along with Kiyoteruhimemikoto, whose heart is pure, we mother and daughter danced with Kōdori, and as we followed the narrow Tanima, we sang the Miuta of Kami together while walking through the Ōnohara where the Akikaze rages, and as we advanced bravely and came toward the pass of Teruyama, we saw the shadows of three people, Nanniyo, upon a rock by the roadside, whispering something, and watching the figures of the mother and daughter, their eyes shining with Kiyōi, they turned toward Ōgonhime and said, "Excuse me, honorable travelers."

Riūn: 'Where are you heading? We are a party of three returning to the capital of Iruna. Please, I beg you, let us accompany you. As I gaze upon you, I see you are a noble Sendenshi, perhaps of the Ananai-kyō. I, too, am a Miko of Kami who serves the same path of Ananahi. Though I, Riūn, am a person of impure heart and this is my fallen form, please let me serve as your companion, having been purified and enlightened by Nahohi and Minaho. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase! Having received the salvation of Kami, I am a Shugenja who has finished traveling through the moon-country of over seven thousand nations, assisted by the one-eyed Kami of Hokkō in the pure age of Kami; I am by no means a suspicious person.'

Riūn: 'Please let me serve as your companion. This Hime-tsukasa here is the niece of the head of the office of the Left Guardian, who is well-known in the capital of Iruna; she is the Kami-tsukasa, Yasudarahime. One of the men is a servant of matchless loyalty who has followed us from the land of the Hime of Teruman and escorted us this far. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase! Blessed by the grace of Kami, I wish to calm the disturbances rising in the capital of Iruna and save the life of King Seran, but I, Riūn, lack the divine power. How can Yasudarahime fulfill this great task? Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō, have pity on our humble hearts.'

Riūn: 'Save us, Kannagara. We offer our sincerity before the presence of Kami and pray.'

Thus Riūn tried to negotiate with the party of Ōgonhime through a song. Ōgonhime immediately replied to this with a song.

Ōgonhime: 'The great Ōkami who created heaven and earth, and the Kami-tsukasa of the Ananai-kyō, protected by Toyokunihime, I am Ōgonhime-mikoto, and this woman is my daughter, the Sendenshi Kiyoteruhime. When we were led by wolves to the Gankutsu of Takateruyama and climbed up, what did we see? The pillar of Kami of the Ananai-kyō, starting with the Hokkō-kami, and Takano-hime, acting as the master of the Gankutsu while calmly using many wolves, bestowing the dew of the noble grace of Kami even upon birds, beasts, insects, and fish; how noble is their state of tranquility. In the words of Hokkō-kami:'

Ōgonhime: '"You, Ōgonhime-mikoto, will surely meet Riūn on the way, who is accompanying Yasudarahime and advancing toward the capital of Iruna. You shall tell Yasudarahime and her party my words completely and in detail, and strictly command them to proceed immediately to the Gankutsu of Takateruyama." Are you truly Riūn? I remember you as the Kami-tsukasa of Yasudarahime, Tetsukiri. Now, change your path from here and advance toward the deep mountains of Takateru where wolves gather. We, mother and daughter, will quickly cross the Teruyama pass and advance into the capital of Iruna.'

Ōgonhime: 'We shall save and assist the clan of King Seran, and guide them to the wolf Gankutsu of Takateru, and protect your person well. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase! Do not doubt even in your dreams the words of Ōgonhime, who has received the divine words of Kami. Since humans are the true Miko of Kami, given a crystal soul, revered as pillars of Kami, and entrusted by the will of Kami as Sendenshi of the noble path, how could they speak lies and falsehoods while possessing such pure souls? Rise from your seat and advance quickly. With the words of Hokkō-kami, I turn to you three and respectfully urge you.'

Ōgonhime: 'Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase! May your spirits be blessed.'

When she finished singing, Yasudarahime replied to this and sang.

Yasudarahime: 'Ah, how grateful, how grateful I am. Are you Ōgonhime, the Sendenshi who transmits the teachings of the famous Ananai? Though I did not know that you were the noble Mikoto, Kiyoteruhime, the famous young woman Kami-tsukasa, I have been truly rude, and I have no words to apologize. Just now, by your words, you command that we, the party of three, should not go to the capital of Iruna, but proceed as quickly as possible to the Gankutsu in the depths of the deep mountains of Takateru. I shall never forget your kindness in conveying the words of Hokkō-kami. I, Yasudarahime, on whom misfortune piles up...'

竜雲『高照山の岩窟に 狼どもに誘はれ 思はぬ人に巡り会ひ 思はぬ使命を受けながら 秋野を飾る黄金姫の 貴の命の宣伝使 心も清照姫命 母娘は勇み雀躍し 細き谷間を辿りつつ 秋風荒ぶ大野原 神の御歌を歌ひ合ひ 勇み進んで照山の 峠をさして来て見れば 道の傍の岩の上に 男女三人の人の影 何かヒソヒソ囁きつ 母娘の姿を打ちまもり 驚異の眼を光らせて 黄金姫に打向ひ もしもし旅のお方様

と竜雲は歌を以て黄金姫一行に掛合つて見た。黄金姫は直に歌を以てこれに答へた。

『天と地とを造らしし 国治立の大神や 豊国姫の守ります 三五教の神司 妾は黄金姫命 一人の女は吾娘 清照姫の宣伝使 高照山の岩窟に 狼等に伴はれ 登りて見ればこは如何に 三五教の神柱 北光神を始めとし 竹野の姫は悠然と 数多の狼使ひつつ 岩窟の主人となりすまし 禽獣虫魚に至るまで 尊き神の御恵の 露を施し給ひつつ 鎮まりいます尊さよ 北光神の御言葉に

汝黄金姫命 必ず途中に竜雲が ヤスダラ姫を伴ひて イルナの都に進み入る それの途中に会ふならむ 汝は吾の言の葉を ヤスダラ姫の一行に 完全に詳細に物語り 高照山の岩窟に 直様進み来るべく 諭せと厳しく宣べ給ふ 汝は正しく竜雲か ヤスダラ姫の神司 テツキリそれと覚えたり さあ今よりは道を変へ 狼群がる高照の 深山をさして進むべし 吾等母娘は逸早く 照山峠を乗り越えて 入那の都へ進み入り

と歌ひ終れば、ヤスダラ姫はこれに答へて歌ふ。

§ 2

I am the child of the sorrowful Kami,

In the Mikokoro of Daiji and Daihi,

Save us, Kamunagara.

Before the presence of the guides of the path,

Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime,

I pray with all my Magokoro.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama be blessed and prosperous.

So she sang, expressing her gratitude. Ōgonhime began to sing with a clear voice.

In the Misora, the moon is Kiyoteruhime,

Like the number of twinkling stars,

As numerous as the Masago of the Gafuga,

The Aohitogusa are suffering,

To save and assist them, and lead them,

To the Misono of Tengoku, the Sendenshi,

Of the Ōkami of Ananai-kyō,

Revering the Mikoto, traveling from afar,

Crossing the Mikuni of the moon,

Heading toward the capital of Haruna,

Bearing a noble Shimei,

The two Kami-tsukasa, mother and daughter,

The Kshatriya of the capital of Iruna,

To save the noble Seran-ō from peril,

Revering the Mikoto of the Hokkō Kami,

As we proceed eagerly on our way,

To meet the party of Yasudarahime here,

Is truly the guidance of the Kami.

Follow the words of my mother without delay,

And proceed to the mountain of Takateru.

I shall follow behind, protecting the life of the ruler of Seran-ō,

And we shall surely meet again,

To celebrate our safety.

In the wilderness where the Majin rage,

While you hesitate,

If the capture-men of the ugly ones who serve the guardian of the right should come,

I would have to engage in a struggle that is not to my heart's desire.

While there is yet no trouble, proceed with all haste.

Before the noble presence of the Kami,

I swear my Magokoro and proclaim it.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama be blessed and prosperous.

When she finished singing, Ryūun answered their song and sang again.

Ah, how grateful, how grateful,

We who follow the noble teachings of the Kami,

The Kami-tsukasa of Ananai-kyō,

How could we refuse your command?

Obeying your words, from now on,

I shall whip the horse of my heart,

And parting the mountain path where wolves and tigers howl,

Eagerly, our party of three,

Shall climb to the rock cave of Mount Takateru.

Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime,

Your noble commands, protect our path,

And let us proceed to the rock cave of Mount Takateru without loss or trouble.

Even if some Magatsu should appear,

And trouble you,

We shall strike out with the Ikikototama forged in Ananai-kyō,

And subdue all the approaching enemies,

Sending them to the shrine where the Hokkō Kami dwells, Kamunagara.

Look back upon the heart of the Kami,

And set your heart at ease.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama of the Kami be blessed,

And the two Kami-tsukasa, mother and daughter,

Ascend to the capital of Iruna,

Subduing all the evil Mitama with the Ikikototama,

Protect the life of Seran-ō,

I pray with a pure Magokoro before the presence of the Ōkami.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama be blessed and prosperous.

The leader sang again.

Kami-tsukasa of Ōgonhime,

Sendenshi of Kiyoteruhime,

Your appearance and form are as beautiful as snow or flowers,

Your noble command of pure heart,

The declaration of the Hokkō Kami,

How noble it is that you have proclaimed it to us three without omission.

We, the party of three,

Relying on your command as our strength,

Shall bravely proceed to the rock cave of Mount Takateru,

And receiving the blessings of the Kami,

I shall protect the life of Yasudarahime.

Even if heaven and earth should change,

As long as the servant of the leader exists,

You need not worry about the life of Yasudarahime.

Two Tsukasa, farewell then,

I shall serve you both from here,

And head toward Mount Takateru.

May your command purely,

Tread over the Teruyama pass,

And reach the capital of Iruna,

And ringing out the Kototama of rigor,

Protect the surroundings of the King.

Before the presence of the Kami of heaven and earth,

Reverently and respectfully, I pray for the success of the two pillars,

The Kami-tsukasa.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama be blessed,

And may I, the leader, appear before you again,

And pray for the day we celebrate great success,

As firm and eternal as the pine, bamboo, and plum.

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama be blessed and prosperous.

Here, the five sang their respective thoughts, and while lamenting the parting, the three climbed toward Mount Takateru, and the two climbed the Teruyama pass, blown by the wild winds of the field.

ここに五人は各述懐を歌ひ、別れを惜しみながら三人は高照山へ、二人は照山峠を野嵐に吹かれながらエチエチと登り行く。
