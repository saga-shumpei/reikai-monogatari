Chapter 8: 無理往生 [1112]

無理往生〔1112〕

§ 1

In the south of the Dekatan Kaugen in Chiuauindo, there was a considerable great power in Indo called the land of Teruman, with a population nearing one hundred thousand. In the capital of Teruman, there was a man named Shiyaru of the Bishiyazoku, known for his great wealth. He chose the most scenic location in the east of the capital for his residence, surrounding the grounds with a deep and wide moat extending over one ichiri in all four directions, and displayed a luxury that surpassed even that of an Ōjiya, relying on his wealth. He used his financial power to purchase numerous beautiful women and make them his concubines, while treating his legal wife, Yasudahime-sama, with extreme coldness.

Yasudahime-sama was born of the Setsuteiri, a cousin to King Seran of the land of Iruna, and as stated in the previous section, she had been the betrothed of King Seran. Yasudahime-sama was given a fairly beautiful house within the vast residence of Shiyaru, where she spent her unhappy days with two maids. There was a young, handsome, and clever man named Riida who handled all matters and managed the mansion of Yasudahime-sama. Yasudahime-sama loved this Riida above all else, and from time to time, she would have him play the koto and the like to console her melancholy of the day. Once, Yasudahime-sama shut herself in a room and, while playing a single-stringed koto, sang of her inner thoughts.

The flow of water and the end of man's path / Though they say this world is uncertain / Born into this floating world of dreams / I am of the noble Setsuteiri / Born as the cousin of King Seran / From my tender, youthful days / Betrothed by our parents / My future destination is the land of Iruna / To serve as the queen / Of King Seran who governs the land / Now I am but a frail Yasudahime-sama / Separated by distant mountains and fields / Living as the wife of Shiyaru / A Bishiya of the land of Teruman / Morning and evening, I live / Lamenting this fleeting world / When I gaze at the clouds passing through the sky / They flow ever westward / When I see the birds crossing the heavens / They too proceed to the west.

If the clouds passing through the sky and the flying birds / Knew of human sorrow / Please visit the presence of King Seran / Who dwells in the land of Iruna / And convey my painful thoughts / Completely and in detail / Thinking of the state of the holy King / Whom I love morning and evening / I spend my nights and days / Yasudahime-sama, whose heart aches / When I reflect upon it, is there anyone in the world / As ill-fated as I am? / Born into the noble house / Of the Setsuteiri in the land of the moon / While holding a position of high honor / Now I am the wife of a lowly Bishiya / Do neither gods nor Buddhas exist / In this world? Ah, how sad / Living long in this heartless world / While burning with the flames in my breast.

The suffering for which there is no way to extinguish / My husband Shiyaru, morning and evening / Relying on the power of his wealth / Gathers up daughters of the Bishiya and children of the Shuda / Hunting them from here and there / Treating women as his own possessions / While dallying with them, he forgets / The noble path of the brother and sister / And has never once / Shown affection toward me / I keep the Brahman teachings / O Lord God Taikokuhiko / Of the Bonten, Taishaku, and Jizaiten / Have pity on the circumstances / Of Yasudahime-sama, and for a moment / Let me meet, if only for a glimpse / My beloved King Seran / Even if my body should decay / The soul I received from the gods / Will continue to visit the side of the King.

I shall protect your person morning and evening / Even if the land of Teruman is vast / Even if the sacred fence of Shiyaru's house / Is built higher than the mountains / Even if the moat water surrounding the mansion / Is as deep and wide as it may be / How could my heart, which yearns for the King / Not reach him? / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara / May your spirits be blessed / May a person with a pure soul come / To save me, and under the cover of night / Send me to the land of Iruna where my father dwells / O Jizaiten / Before the noble gods / With a heart that is never at peace / Yasudahime-sama prays / With all her sincerity / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara / Before the Great God / Who created this world / I humbly and reverently pray.

Thus she sang elegantly. To such a place, having finished cleaning the inside and outside of the mansion and having performed ritual purification, the one who came to visit the princess's room, having changed into his formal attire, was the loyal servant Riida. The princess stopped playing the koto and smiled brightly.

ヤスダラ姫は夫シヤールの不甲斐なき態度にグツと腹を立て、声を震はせながらテーナ姫に向ひ、

『其方は右守の司の妻テーナ姫ではないか。汝が夫カールチンは卑しき首陀の生れ、吾父クーリンス殿より引き立てられ、今は右守の司の地位に迄進み、漸く世に認めらるる様になつたのは誰のお蔭だと思うて居るか。汝は吾幼少の時の子守役を勤めて居つた卑しき婢女、今は右守の司の妻となりたればとて、妾に対し無礼の雑言、最早聞棄てはなりませぬぞ』

と睨めつくればテーナ姫は平然として打笑ひ、

『オホヽヽヽ、愈以てヤスダラ姫は狂気致したと見ゆる。妾の夫はイルナの国にて其名も高き刹帝利の生れ、妾は又貴勝族の生れで厶る。ヤスダラ姫が父クーリンスこそ卑しき首陀の生れ、吾夫カールチンの引立によりて今日の地位を得ながら、其大恩を忘れ、反対に妾の夫を卑しき首陀族と申すは何たる暴言、こりや屹度正気ではあるまい。早く牢獄に打ち込めよ』

§ 4

Carried away by the momentum, he spouted Hantaiteki Netsuron and was anxious to imprison Yasudarahime through Muri Ōjiyō. It was with deep consideration that Karuchin sent Tenahime as a messenger to the mansion of Shiyāru to imprison Yasudarahime by finding fault with her. At the time when Karuchin, under the Kōen of Ōkuronushi, was attempting to achieve his Yashin by Hōchiku of King Sēran and Kūrinsu, he feared that the presence of Yasudarahime would become a Daibaugai in the Gojitsu, and thus it was his Sakuriyaku to find fault and keep the Hime in Iuhei. Furthermore, because the Kaujiyō between his daughter Samarīhime and King Sēran was not very Omoshiro, he suspected that King Sēran was Reigū toward his daughter because Yasudarahime was still in Seizon in this world and might eventually communicate her feelings to King Sēran; thus, Tenahime, as a messenger of her husband, came Harubaru to the mansion of Shiyāru to perform an Atamaosa in a Kenpunteki manner. Due to the Keiriyaku of the foul-hearted Karuchin and Tena, Yasudarahime ended up being imprisoned in a Kenrau Raugoku built within the Teinai of Shiyāru, a Fubin and Zankoku fate.

On a night of fierce wind, rain, thunder, and lightning, Rīdā, who served Yasudarahime well, broke down the Kenrau Raugoku, rescued Yasudarahime, escaped the mansion of Shiyāru under the cover of darkness, and began preparations to flee back toward the country of Inna, the mansion of her father, traveling day and night.

原文（近代文語）

と勢に任して反対的の捏論をまくしたて、無理往生にヤスダラ姫を幽閉せしめむと焦慮つてゐる。カールチンがテーナ姫をシヤールの館に、ヤスダラ姫に難癖をつけて幽閉せしむべく、使者に遣はしたのは深き考へがあつての事である。カールチンは大黒主の後援の下にセーラン王、クーリンスを放逐し其野心を達せむとする折、ヤスダラ姫のありては後日の大妨害となる事を慮り、難癖をつけて姫を幽閉し置かむとの策略である。又一つには娘のサマリー姫とセーラン王との交情あまり面白からざるは、ヤスダラ姫此世に生存して何時とはなしにセーラン王に思ひを通じるを以て、セーラン王の斯くも吾娘を冷遇するならむとの僻みより、犬糞的にシヤールの館へ頭抑へにテーナ姫が夫の使者として遥々やつて来たのである。ヤスダラ姫は心汚きカールチン、テーナの計略によつて、シヤールの邸内に堅牢なる牢獄を造り其中に不愍にも残酷にも幽閉さるる身となつて了つた。

風雨雷電の烈しき夜、ヤスダラ姫によく仕へたるリーダーは堅牢なる牢獄を打ち破り、ヤスダラ姫を救ひ出し暗に紛れてシヤールの館を脱出し、夜を日についで入那の国、父の館をさして逃げ帰る準備にかかれり。
