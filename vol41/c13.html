Chapter 13: Night の駒 [1117]

夜の駒〔1117〕

§ 1

In the inner chamber of King Seran's mansion in the capital of Iruna, the six people—the King himself, Ōgonhime, Kiyoteruhime, Thames, Rebu, and Karu—are seated in order of rank, and a whispered conversation has begun.

King: 'Ōgonhime-sama, despite the late hour and the distance, you have kindly come. With this, I too feel a sense of relief. You are the wife of Onikumawake-dono, Mukadehime-sama, and Koitohime-sama, are you not?'

'Yes, though it is shameful to admit, drawn by the threads of fate, I have finally parted from my husband and become a Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō for the sake of the Kamisama. The world does not always go as one wishes.'

'That is indeed so. I, too, have fallen into a very tragic situation regarding the path of marriage. I wonder if, by the Mimegumi of the Kamisama someday, it will be possible for me to be united with one whose Mitama matches my own as I desire. You, Anatasama, will surely be able to become a proper husband and wife with Onikumawake-dono once again and serve in the Shinkai. I, however, likely have no such hope.'

'There is nothing more wonderful than parents and spouses serving in the Shinkai together, but as you know, my husband is a pillar of Brahmanism, while I and my daughter are Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō. No matter how much one says the Kamisama are one, it is a difficult relationship.'

'Please, do not worry at all. Onikumawake-dono will surely agree with your teachings. My own situation today has fallen into a state truly beyond words. My fiancée, Yasudarahime, was driven away by treacherous retainers, and I have been forced to take a wife who does not suit my heart; I have not lived a single day in happiness. Furthermore, treacherous subjects and sycophants are rampant, and my own situation has fallen into a state of critical danger. Therefore, I wished to welcome you and have you save me from this crisis; following a dream revelation from the Kamisama of the Northern Light, I have been searching for your arrival here for several days. How glad I am that you have come. From now on, I intend to conduct myself according to your guidance, so I humbly ask for your favor in all matters.'

'Do you believe in the teachings of Ananai?'

'Yes, it is not that I believe in any particular way, but I believe that Daijizaiten-sama is the creator of the world, and Kuninotokotachi-no-Ōkami-sama is also the creator of the world; though their names differ, they are fundamentally the same Kamisama.'

'Kuninotokotachi-no-Ōkami-sama is the true ancestor of this world, while Banko-shin-ō and Jizaiten-sama are Kamisama possessed by the evil spirits of great serpents, evil foxes, and evil demons that originated from the Mitama of the human ancestor Amatsuhiko and Hōjōhime. Since they are those who have brought their ancestors into the human realm, there is a tremendous difference. There must be a distinction between a Kamisama who manifested from the Divine and a Kamisama who manifested from humanity.'

'Ah, is that so? I have heard that Kuninotokotachi-no-Ōkami-sama, the presiding deity of the Ananai-kyō, as well as Banko-shin-ō and Daijizaiten-sama, are the same Kamisama and that only their names differ. I had firmly believed that, but if I hear it that way, I must reconsider. I have something I would like you, mother and daughter, to see, so please come to my place of seclusion. It is a sacred room that not even my wife or the Sayo-no-Tsukasa has ever entered. Thames, stay here for a while with Rebu and Karu.'

Thames bows his head and says, 'Yes, I understand.'

The King accompanies the mother and daughter and proceeds to the room of seclusion. Upon arriving, one finds two fairly large rooms side by side. There, a magnificent altar was set up, and various interesting antiques were displayed.

The King rolls up the blinds of the tokonoma, claps his hands, and begins to recite the Norito. The mother and daughter also lower their heads and recite the Norito in low voices. When they finish and look closely at the altar, a hanging scroll is displayed in the center of the tokonoma, and sacred sake, offerings, and sacred water are neatly arranged. This was a secret room where the King always performed purification to inquire after the divine will.

§ 2

When one looked closely at the Shingau on the Kakemono, it was inscribed in large Daiji characters at the Shiyōmen as 'Tenichishinō Kuniharutachinomikoto,' and directly Mashita, it was written as 'Kyōshiyu Kamususanōnomikoto.' On both sides of the Chiuau, it was inscribed in the King's own Jikihitsu as 'Bankoshinō Shihonagahikonomikoto' and 'Tokoyoshinō Ōkunihikonomikoto.' Ōgonhime and her daughter fixed their eyes upon this scroll, struck by an indescribable sense of Saukuwai and astonishment, and gazed at the Shingau in a state of Bauzen.

Ōgonhime: 'My Shinkau is as you see here. Do you understand?'

The other: 'I have received an unexpected Goshintoku. With this, the castle of Iruna is also Daijiyōbu; pray, be at Goanshin. However, even the Gosenzosama of this world are difficult to oppose when it comes to Tokiyojisetsu, and since you were once driven as far as the foundation of the country by the faction of Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime, you must never be careless. If your Shinkau were to be understood by the side of Ōkuninushi, it would be a disaster, so it is best not to announce it for a while yet.'

Ōgonhime: 'Yes, I have not even let the officials of the Left Guard peek into this room. Since there is no one who knows, it is Daijiyōbu.'

The other: 'Even if they do not peek into this room, if your Shinkau is as it is, it will eventually appear in your voice and on your skin, and you will be found out. For one can never truly conceal the color of the heart.'

Ōgonhime: 'Since you have come here, there should be no need for much worry. Please take a look at this for a moment.'

She leads the two to the next room. Looking there, there was a small Tokonoma, and two portraits were hung. Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime could not help but be surprised with a gasp. One was a portrait of her husband Onikumawake, whom she kept in her heart daily, and the other was a portrait of Kamususanōnomikoto. Kiyoteruhime, without thinking, said:

Kiyoteruhime: 'Ah, this is Father, the great Ōkami!'

As she was about to say this, Ōgonhime placed her hand over her mouth and said:

Ōgonhime: 'Now, now, Kiyoteruhime-dono, what are you saying? This is surely a portrait of Ōkuninushi-sama and Jizaiten-sama. If you raise your voice like that, it will be a disaster if it reaches the ears of demons.'

The other: 'It is a portrait that bears a great resemblance to your father. Ho ho ho.'

The other: 'Until now, I have governed this country by believing in the Brahman gods, but one night in a dream, the two deities Kamususanōnomikoto and Onikumawake-no-mikoto appeared, and after bestowing upon me various precious teachings, I have followed the divine command, devoted myself to the Shinkau alone, and waited for the time to arrive. Recalling the figures that appeared in my dream, I have taken up the brush myself and have been serving them in secret. Onikumawake-sama serves as an attendant to Kamususanōnomikoto in the divine realm. Surely, his physical body will also enter the path of the Three-Five. I feel that only the matter of time remains.'

Ōgonhime and her daughter are struck with emotion, unable to speak, and are overcome with tears of joy. King Seran said:

King Seran: 'The blessings of the gods of heaven and earth, seen before my eyes, / How precious is this day of worship. / Submitting to the honor of the god Susanō, / You are the divine official Onikumawake who guards the teachings. / The life of the god Onikumawake shall for a while longer, / Remain hidden in the capital of Haruna. / When the time comes, he shall soon appear in the open, / And become the official of the Three-Five teachings. / Ah, how joyful, Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, / This evening when I have met the divine official.'

Ōgonhime: 'Coming here, I saw in the King's living room, / The figure of my dear husband. / I thought he was a child of the Brahman teachings, / But what a mysterious evening this is.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Because the light of the Chihayaburu gods is strong, / It illuminates the heart of my father's life. / My father has already become, / A child of the teachings of the god Kuniharutachi. / King Seran-no-mikoto, for a little while longer, / Please wait for the time, according to the will of the gods. / This evening, the heart of Kiyoteruhime, the official of the teachings, / Has cleared of all accumulated thoughts.'

Ōgonhime: 'The life of the god of the Northern Light is hidden, / Proceed quickly to Mount Takateru. / Though people of the world fear and gossip about Mount Takateru, / It is a truly magnificent place. / Leading a pack of thousands of wolves, / The god of the Northern Light awaits the King. / Now then, Thames, Reave, and Cal, you three, / Follow behind and depart quickly.'

Ō: 'Following the Mikoto of Ōgonhime-Kami, I shall soon depart for Takateruyama. After I have gone, do you, Nagamikoto, remain here for a while and guard the mansion.'

Kiyoteru: 'Illuminated by the light of the Izu of Ōkami, may your path be clear and safe. My daughter and I shall unite our hearts and devote ourselves to guarding the castle of Iruna for the time being.'

Ō: 'I shall entrust to you the Ikutamazusa bestowed by the command of Onikumawake-Kami. Open and read it with care, and see the color of the Magokoro of the Kami's command, Onikumawake.'

Saying this, he respectfully handed to Ōgonhime the secret letter sent to the King by Onikumawake. Ōgonhime quickly broke the seal, unfolded it, and read the contents, which were as follows.

'From Onikumawake to King Seran: First, this Tenchi is a Shinkoku created by Tenichishinō-Ōkuniharutachinomikoto, and is by no means a Tenchi created by the Kami such as Ōkunihiko and Shihonagahiko. Furthermore, although Ōkuronushi holds the power of arms as the supreme leader of Brahmanism and wears the mask of a great religious leader, Heaven will not tolerate such falsehoods forever; surely there will come a time when he returns to the original truth and follows the Kyō of San-go. Regarding this, according to the providence of Kami-Susanoo-Ōkami, my wife and children shall visit the King's mansion in the near future, so you must disclose all matters and make the best efforts for the sake of the nation. As for the capital of Haruna, the majority of the army has now departed on an expedition, and the defense is at its lowest. However, a petition to depose the King from the right guard who serves the King has come to Ōkuronushi, and as Ōkuronushi is about to send several thousand knights shortly, the Iruna castle is truly like a candle in the wind; therefore, you, noble King, should consult on a contingency plan with my wife and children and temporarily take refuge somewhere. Onikumawake is remaining in the capital of Haruna, striving morning and evening to make Ōkuronushi repent and to save his Mitama in the heavenly pure land. I pray you to await the day of meeting with my wife and children, and without a moment's delay, take care to hide yourself in a safe zone. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese.'

It was written thus. Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, seeing the handwriting of Onikumawake for the first time in a long while, felt as if they had met their husband and father directly; their hearts were encouraged, and while shedding tears of gratitude, they spoke.

Ōgonhime: 'Ah, now I understand everything. The command from the Kami of Hokkō to lure you to the rock cave of the wolf as soon as possible is also clarified by the contents of this letter. Ah, how thorough and attentive the Kami is!'

Kiyoteru: 'I feel as if I have met my father directly. Thank you, Kami.'

Joining their hands, they offered words of gratitude toward the sacred image of Kami-Susanoo-no-Mikoto.

Ōgonhime: 'Now that it has come to this, please set out for Takateruyama as soon as possible before the night breaks. There are many things I wish to say, but now there is no such leisure. Come, quickly, quickly.'

As she urged him, the King replied,

'If that is so, I leave everything to you.'

He stood up first and appeared in the living room where Thames and the others were waiting. The King appeared in the front living room with the mother and daughter, turned to Thames, and said,

'Thames, I am sorry for the trouble, but please prepare the horses quickly. I am departing for Takateruyama, accompanied by Rebu and Karu.'

'Yes, I understand. However, according to the orders of Ōgonhime-sama, I have already prepared the horses properly. I shall accompany you whenever you wish.'

'Ah, that is grateful. In that case, Ōgonhime-sama, Kiyoteruhime-sama, I leave the rest to you.'

Ōgonhime: 'Though you depart and the time may grow long, I shall guard the mansion and wait. Proceed in peace, for the Kami awaits in the rock cave of Takateru mountain. The Kami of Hokkō, the teacher of the San-go, shall await your arrival.'

Ō: 'Now then, Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, I must part with you, dear to the command of the Kami.'

Singing this, he hurriedly went outside, led the horses out from the back gate, followed the dark path, and the party of four rode off, racing through the clouds and mist toward the rock cave of Takateruyama.

『鬼熊別よりセーラン王に密書を送る。

一、これの天地は天一神王大国治立尊の造り給ひし神国にして、決して大国彦、塩長彦の神等の創造せし天地にあらず。又大黒主はバラモン教の大棟梁として兵馬の権を握り、大教主の仮面を被り居らるれども、天は何時迄も斯かる虚偽を許し給はず、必ずや本然の誠に立ち返り、三五教を信従する時あるべし。それに付いては神素盞嗚大神の御摂理に依り、吾妻子近々の中に王が館に訪問すべければ、一切万事を打明け、国家の為に最善の努力をなさるべし。ハルナの都は今や軍隊の大部分は遠征の途に上り、守り最も少なくなり居れり。然るに王に仕ふる右守より王を廃立せむとの願書、大黒主の許に来り、大黒主は数千の騎士を近々差向くる事となりをれば、イルナ城は実に風前の灯火なるを以て、貴王は吾妻子と共に善後策を講じ、一時何れへか避難さるべし。鬼熊別はハルナの都に止まつて、大黒主を悔ひ改めしめ、其身魂をして天国浄土に救はむと朝夕努めつつあり。吾妻子に面会の日を期し、一刻の猶予もなく、安全地帯へ一時身をかくさるべく呉々も注意致します。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

と記されてあつた。黄金姫、清照姫は久しぶりに鬼熊別の肉筆の手紙を見て、夫に直接会ひし如く、父に面会せし如く、心勇み、感涙を落しながら、

黄金『あゝ之にて何もかも分りました。北光の神様が一時も早く貴方を狼の岩窟へ誘ひ来れとの御命令も、此手紙の文面にて氷解しました。あゝ、何と神様はどこまでも注意周到なお方だなア』

清照『お父様に直接お目にかかつた様な心持が致します。神様、有難う厶います』

と両手を合せ、神素盞嗚尊の聖像に向つて、感謝の詞を捧げた。

黄金『サアかうなる上は、一刻も早く高照山へ夜の明けない中にお越し遊ばせ。申上げたき事は山々あれど、今はさういふ余裕も厶いませぬ。サア早く早く』

とせき立つれば王は、

『左様ならば、万事宜しく願ひます』

と先に立ち、テームス等が控へてゐる居間に姿を現はした。王は母娘と共に表の居間に立現はれ、テームスに打ち向ひ、

『テームス、御苦労だが、早く駒の用意をしてくれ。これから高照山へレーブ、カルを伴ひ、出発致すから』

『ハイ承知致しました。併し乍ら黄金姫様の御命令に依り、馬の用意はチヤンと整へておきました。何時なりともお供を致しませう』

『あゝそれは有難い。それなら、黄金姫様、清照姫様、あとを宜しく御願ひ致します』

黄金『君ゆきて如何にけながくなるとても われは館を守りて待たむ。 うら安く進み出でませ高照の 山の岩窟に神は待たせり。 三五の教司の北光の神は 君のいでまし待たせ給はむ』

王『いざさらば黄金姫や清照姫 神の命よ惜しく別れむ』

と歌ひながら、慌しく表に出で、裏門口より駒引き出し、暗の道を辿りて、高照山の岩窟指して一行四人は雲を霞と駆り行く。
