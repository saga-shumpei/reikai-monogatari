§ 1

In the mansion of Umori in the capital of Iruna, Karlchin, Tenahime, and Yufutesu are gathered, putting their heads together to discuss some matter.

Karlchin: 'Yufutesu, is the whereabouts of Yasudarahime, the wife of Shiyar of the country of Teruman, still unknown, eh?'

Yufutesu: 'Yes, it is not yet clearly known. When I had Serisuhime look into the situation, it was reported that after Tenahime-sama went to the country of Teruman and had Shiyar of Bishiya imprison Yasudarahime-sama, about ten days later, taking advantage of a dark night of storms, a man named Leader who served the princess broke down the prison and escaped to parts unknown. I have also heard rumors that there might have been some deep relationship between the princess and that servant. Serisuhime-sama is also deeply regretting her sister's misconduct. Last night, acting under the orders of Dannasama, I went to the summit of Teruyamatauge, where five men from Shiyar's house in the country of Teruman came riding on horseback and asked, "Yasudarahime, have you not met Leader?" However, thinking that they might be spies for King Seran, I feigned ignorance and did not engage with them, so the five knights went down Teruyamatauge toward the north. I listened to the opinion of Serisuhime and, believing that Yasudarahime would surely return to the mansion of her father, the official of Samori, I searched thoroughly, but no woman came. I caught up with the five knights, returned to the capital with them on horseback, let the knights stay for the night, and ordered them to search the places they suspected.'

Karlchin: 'To send back those who have come from a far country without consulting me is a bit presumptuous, is it not? Why did you not let me see them even for a moment, eh?'

Yufutesu: 'That is a regrettable matter, but I was anxious that Samori might find out, so I intentionally sent them back. There is no doubt that Yasudarahime will cross Teruyamatauge and return. If we dally and let the subordinates of Samori capture Yasudarahime, it would be a disaster. It seems that even Samori is generally aware that Yasudarahime is returning this way, so we must never let our guard down. Also, the five knights who came last night are all people who know the style of Yasudarahime well, so I thought it was just right and sent them out to the foot of Teruyamatauge.'

Karlchin: 'That was truly a good idea. However, your beloved Serisuhime is the younger sister of Yasudarahime, so I hope there will be no untoward incidents. You cannot be careless with what you say.'

Yufutesu: 'What are you saying? Even though they are sisters, their hearts are as different as black and white; Serisuhime is by no means a villain who would favor her sister or her parents and sacrifice her love for me. She is an extremely good person for my sake.'

Karlchin: 'By the command of the Lord God Ōkuninushi, Dannasama has ascended to the rank of Kshatriya, and it is the divine will to have King Seran retire and live in peace; this is entirely due to the virtue of my daughter, Samarihime, who is the consort, that King Seran-sama is safe. Since Samarihime also seems to be deeply in love with the King, we must ensure they remain together for a long time. If Yasudarahime were to return there, the King's heart might change again, and I do not know what reckless things Samarihime might do as a result of her love being broken; it is truly a worrying matter. We must block Yasudarahime's entry into the castle as soon as possible, capture her, imprison her in a place unknown to others, and prevent her from approaching the King. Yufutesu, do you understand?'
