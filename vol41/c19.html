Chapter 19: 当て飲み [1123]

当て飲み〔1123〕

§ 1

A shōshiya chiyashitsu stands within a sensui that incorporates a portion of the seiriu of the Iruna River. This is the mansion of Umori, and since the sauchō of this kesa, the kanburen of Karlchin, Tenahime, Mammoth, Samoahime, and Yūfutesu have been drinking together in celebration of the guwanmau jiyōjiyu, cultivating their kōzen spirit. Karlchin, having been overwhelmed by the drinking of Tenahime and Samoahime, has begun to act like an ōjiya, boldly and recklessly spewing the mud from his belly.

Karlchin, using his thick tongue with difficulty while grimacing, spoke:

Karlchin: '(Yohidore kuchō) Hey, Obaa-sama... no, I mean the betsupin of old, Tenahime, your chiriyaku is something great, is it not? Since shinchikisaku like that of Kuebikonokami or Omohikanenokami wells up from your seikatanden, eh? This Kami, though his feet do not walk, is a Kami who knows all things under heaven; the sairai of Kushimitamachiyorihiko-no-Mikoto is you, eh. Before long, I shall annex the land of Inya and the land of Teruman, construct a great kingdom, and change your name from Tenahime to Tenahime-kisaki. How about it, Obaa-sama, you must be happy, right? Eh.'

Tenahime: 'It is not that such things are impossible. However, if that were to happen, I might actually become sad. We are able to live together as a harmonious couple in our current state, so you cannot possibly know how wonderful that is. If you were to imitate the Lord Ōkuninushi-no-Kami, cast away your wife of humble beginnings, and install a beauty like Yasudahime as your successor, it would be exactly like having one's fried tofu snatched away by a kite. You have a bad nature, my husband, so I cannot help but worry.'

Karlchin: 'What are you spouting? I, who follow the path of Brahmanism and walk the straight line of virtue, would not do such an immoral thing; would that not be unfair to you? No, not just to you, but it would not sit well with my own heart either, so you may rest assured that such a thing will never happen, eh. Just when the sake has begun to circulate so well, if you say such things to worry about the future, you spoil the mood, do you not? Eh.'

Tenahime: 'Hearing that, I have felt a little relieved. You are not the type to do such a thing, are you? When we first met, what did you say? Surely you have not forgotten.'

Karlchin: 'Now, now, what are you saying? If you talk about such things, Yūfutesu, Mammoth, and Samoahime will get worked up and jealous, so how about we wrap up our old romance here? Eh.'

Tenahime: 'Recalling the honey-like love of our youth from time to time is not such a bad thing, you know. There is nothing as pleasant as occasionally remembering the romance of one's youth for human enjoyment. If one forgets that, there is no taste or anything left to life.'

Thus, Tenahime, in the momentum of being drunk, tries to chatter about their old love without regard for those around her.

Yūfutesu: 'My, my, Danna-sama, you and Okusama had interesting times as well. Please let me hear about it once. I have a woman I love named Sērisuhime, so it would be very convenient for my research if you would let me hear it. Ah, one widower among two couples; Sērisuhime is not very clever either. If she would just show her face for a brief moment, the standing of Yūfutesu would be elevated, but since we are not yet a public couple, it cannot be helped. I shall look forward to it in the future, eh.'

Karlchin: 'Now, now, Yūfutesu, what is this "eh"? You have occupied my trademark; who gave you permission to steal that "eh", eh?'

Yūfutesu: 'It is not that I stole it. Since you, Danna-sama, drop so many "eh"s, I merely picked one up. I wish to unite Sērisuhime and "eh" as soon as possible, eh.'

§ 2

Ohohohoho, what a Kakeahi of 'een-een' this is. Would it not be better to show a little restraint? Does it not say that one should wait for the Tsukihi? 'Een' is a matter of the Tēna.

No matter what you say, scorched by the Jiyōkuwa of love, flames burn within my breast with an 'een-een'. While you people are here, happily laughing as a Fūfu and engaging in Ichiya, you do not think of the Mikonshiya Yūfutesu as pitiful or Kaaisau, and you remain with an attitude that says, 'Your 'een' talk and such are Warekansezu.' Therefore, I am almost becoming an 'een'-world-Shiyugi... though I shall not.

Be that as it may, is this not the most Medeta thing? Manmosu wishes to see the day of Seikō as soon as possible.

Is the day of Seikō not already in sight? Did the Ōsama Sēran not say that he would grant the rank to Umori-sama? There shall never be a Nigon in the words of an Ōjiya. This is all because, as expected, Dannasama has a daughter of such fine Kiriyō and Kenmei. Ah, truly, one should be grateful for having such a daughter. Yūfutesu also wishes to marry Sērisu-hime soon, beget a beautiful Keikoku daughter, and enjoy his Raugo, ahn.

Hey, hey, Yūfutesu, if you let out an 'ahn' like that, your marriage proposal will end up as an 'ahn', ahn.

Yūfutesu: 'Hey, Manmosu, are you trying to interrupt? If you are going to interrupt, then go ahead. I have my own Ryōken as well.'

This country is famous for its Chacha. Shall I serve you some Tencha or Sencha? Chachya, yātoko-se, mamachītoko-se, being cleverly deceived by Sērisu-hime-sama, and in the end, receiving a cold shoulder, all my usual thoughts are in a mess, and to send amorous glances to such a shyan with a face like a lizard is Chanchyara ridiculous. In the end, chattya, this marriage proposal will be crushed to pieces in a mess. This Manmosu knows that clearly with his heavenly eye, een.

Samoa: 'Ohohohoho, what an interesting day this is, my.'

Hey, you lot, today is the celebration for Umori, so you may chatter about whatever you like, but in another month or two, I shall be a Kshatriya-sama, so I will not be able to be this carefree. You understand that much, do you not, een?

I understand that, this Yūfutesu does. However, since you are also thinking of things that are not very good, I wonder if it will be settled one way or another, ahn.

Hey, hey, what do you mean by 'not very good'? Is that not a bit rude, een?

I said you were a person of Kawayoku-ten-tan, with not even a bit of greed. Everything in this world, if one tries to grasp it, is something that cannot be grasped. Dannasama is flawless in all things and is a person who is not good, which is why the Ōsama spoke of such splendid things as he did yesterday. Thinking of this, one must wait for the proper season. (Dodoitsu) 'Though they speak of the power of the times and seasons, a person who is not good becomes the Ōsama.' Ah, yoitose, yoitose. Hey, Manmosu, will you not sing one song as a pre-celebration? You are just drinking away like the child of a giant snake, what kind of Taido is that? Why do not you sing a 'kokekko' or something, ahn?

Manmosu, looking solemn, said:

When it is time to drink, I drink; when it is time to play, I play. And thus, when there is a time to sing, I sing. Even I, in my youth, was a hero and a man of great talent who traveled through a thousand armies and ten thousand horses... no, that is not it, I am a person who reads the biographies of such heroes and men of great talent to cultivate a little bit of influence... that is the kind of person I am, een. It is not something that the likes of a swallow or sparrow such as you can dare to fathom. (Shigin) 'The moon shines brightly in the middle of the sky, and Manmosu is leisurely immersed in his wine cup. In the cup-washing bowl reflecting the moonlight, the beauty of the age is by my side.' How is that? It is good, is it not? My poetry is, after all, specially ordered, een.

§ 3

Kisama's Shika sounds just like the voice of a Sensei calling his Yotsuashi Niyōbau in the Momijibayashi, crying 'Kairō, Kairō.' Oh, that reminds me of that 'Kairō'; I too wish to soon bind a vow of Kairaudōketsu with Seichan. It makes me feel ill to hear such a sloppy Shika as yours. The 'shi' of Shika must be the 'shi' of Shinin, must it not? Why don't you sing a song with more Seimei, ahn?

Mammoth, while coughing once, said:

The character for the 'shi' in Shika is written with the radical for 'word' and the character for 'temple,' is it not? The place where Shinin are laid to rest is a temple.

Yūfutesu: 'Hen, what a clever thing to say; a temple, is that not a Bakabakashi thing to say, you Mammoth fellow?'

As he was thus spinning his yarn, a man came walking up briskly, presented a letter, and said:

Dannasama, a messenger in haste from the capital of Haruna has brought this letter.

As he presented it reverently, Kāruchin opened his Suigan wide, took the letter quickly, cut the seal, and cast his eyes upon the Bunmen:

Eh, what? Difficult Moji are written here; it is a Bunmen that seems to move quite a bit. The Moji are active like a Giyōretsu of Sōmen, in two or three lines. This appears to be the handwriting of Ōkuni-nushi-sama; the brush of a living god is indeed different. It is becoming more and more active, indeed.

He flickered his eyes and shook his hands, but try as he might, he could not read it.

Oi, Tēna-hime, could you read this for me? It is such a living script, with the ink dripping and the characters running like a dragon, that it seems it might run away somewhere, ehn?

Tēna-hime: 'Ho ho ho ho, very well, I shall read it for you.'

She took the letter and said:

Eh... regarding your request, I intended to dispatch two thousand cavalry soldiers, but a riot has broken out in the neighboring land of Seina, and I have had Aruman lead the troops to suppress it, so I cannot comply with your petition. However, as it is difficult to gauge when the disturbance will be quelled, I shall dispatch five hundred cavalry to your location in haste, so you should be prepared for all contingencies. To Kāruchin, Guardian of the Right, a proclamation from Ōkuni-nushi...

Kāruchin: 'Good, good, I understand now. Still, to dispatch five hundred cavalry despite the fact that a disturbance has broken out in the neighboring land, they have trusted us well indeed; it is truly grateful. However, since it has been said from the mouth of King Sēran, there should be no need for battle. Yet, there may be some fellow with evil wisdom who might change his mind. If we have five hundred brave warriors in preparation for such a time, everything will go conveniently. Well, I suppose I shall accept it with reverence. Oi, Yūfutesu, you meet the messenger of Ōkuni-nushi-sama and offer our thanks properly. It is my true intention to meet him directly, but it would be terrible if the messenger saw me thus drunk and at ease. I must not lower the trust of Ōkuni-nushi-sama, you see.'

Having sobered up slightly, he became a little more serious and proclaimed.

Yūfutesu: 'I understand the details perfectly. There is no one else suitable to receive the messenger but this Yūfutesu, if I may say so. If that is the case, I shall receive him as a Minister Plenipotentiary. No, the dignity of a Minister Plenipotentiary is not enough with just one of us. Mammoth, accompany me, ahn?'

Mammoth: 'Eh, don't you make a fool of me. Who would go under you, Kisama? This Mammoth is a body that must make his way in the world from here on. If I am seen by the messenger... and thought to be a subordinate of Yūfutesu... it would be a great disadvantage for my future, so looking at it from the calculation of profit and loss, well, I think I shall pass, ehn?'

Karuchin: 'Ah, I am drunk, I am drunk. How can I possibly have an audience with the envoy in such a state of a drunkard? Daijizaiten Ōkunihikonomikoto, protect me and bless me, ge-utsuppu, gara-gara gara-gara. Because of this sudden mission, the worms in my belly have started their own Seiketsuhafu, and even the sake I drank has started to Giyakuriu. Ah, how painful it is. Karuchin: 'Very well, very well, I delegate full authority to you, so go and handle it well.'

Yūfutesu: 'If so, I shall now demonstrate my Tokui Guwaikauteki Shiyuwan.'

Having said this, he ran out to the front with a clatter. Yūfutesu was not drunk and collapsed like the other Yonin. This was because his Daiichi Mokuteki was to observe all the Kaudō of the Karuchin Fūfu according to the Chiyūi of Serisu Hime.

Yūfutesu went out to the front and, while deliberately walking with a Chidoriashi, (in a drunken tone): 'The Shishiya of the Ōkuninushi no Kami-sama of the land of Haruna, do-do-do-where the he-he-he-hell is he? I am Yūfutesu-sama, the Tokumei... Zenken Kōshi. Why do you not come out here... before me quickly, ahn?'

The gatekeeper Kēru saw this behavior and ran over, saying: 'Excuse me, Okarō-sama, the Shishiya from the land of Haruna handed over that letter and said he was going on a mission to the land of Karumata, and without even listening to me saying "Please wait a moment," he whipped his horse and rode off at full speed. It is useless to chase after him with such unsteady feet.'

Yūfutesu: 'Wa-wa-what? It is completely a festival after the fact, and I cannot hold it or put it down. However, this must also be some convenience of the Kami-sama.'
