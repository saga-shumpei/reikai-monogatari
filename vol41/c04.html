Chapter 4: 右守館 [1108]

右守館〔1108〕

§ 1

Umori no Tsukasa Karuchin, while drinking and exchanging cups with his wife Tena, becomes tipsy as the night deepens, and they plot the treacherous scheme to exile King Seran.

Tena: 'Dannasama, surely this time Ōkuronushi-sama will understand, will he not? I have repeatedly informed him, mixing truth and falsehood, that King Seran is of the faction of Onikumawake, whom Ōkuronushi-sama detests above all else.'

Karuchin: 'This time, the moment for the fulfillment of our ambition has arrived. Once our desire is finally achieved, we couple shall become the Kshatriya of Iruna, so we must live long. Until now, we have sacrificed Samarihime and raised her to be Queen, but it seems the King has sensed our plot, and he treats Samarihime so harshly that she has come fleeing back to us in tears. If we dally, our evil scheme will be exposed. As they say, he who strikes first controls the other; since I have already sent a Hayōmazukahi to the capital of Haruna as soon as the princess returned, our messenger must surely have arrived first. Even if King Seran sends a messenger now, it is already too late; how agile my methods are! Hahaha!'

Tena: 'Since you are always quick to see the opportunity, Dannasama, I do not know how happy I am to be wed to a husband like you. At times, is it not poor Samarihime who is to be pitied? I told her thoroughly and made her the Queen of King Seran, but now it seems she has forgotten her parents' intentions and has developed an attachment to the King. We have truly committed a sin. Although she has returned like that, as I think of it, she is troubled because she yearns for the King even in her sleep. Therefore, even though she is the daughter I bore, I cannot speak of this strategy in front of her now; if by any chance she were to hear of it, she might inform the King, you see.'

Karuchin: 'If someone acts with such lack of heart and defies their parents, is it not fine to simply execute them? We couple, who harbor such great ambitions, must be prepared to sacrifice one or two children, must we not?'

Tena: 'Is that not too greedy? No matter how much we couple may rise in the world, if there is no child to inherit the essential legacy, it will amount to nothing. We cannot live for a thousand or ten thousand years, and is it not because we love our child that we are worrying like this?'

Karuchin: 'That may be so, but does the proverb not say that while there is a thicket to abandon a child, there is no thicket to abandon oneself? When it comes to the crunch, it is the way of the world—nay, human nature—to abandon one's child to save one's own life. I, too, would dearly love to achieve my goal and have my child succeed me, but if our conspiracy were to be exposed for the sake of that child, and we couple were to have our lives taken, would that not be a disaster?'

Tena: 'Do you have such cold thoughts toward our own child? I would be satisfied if only our child could become someone great, no matter what happens to my own life.'

Karuchin: 'You are a fool; that is why they say mothers are soft. Even if you call her your child, once she is born and separated from your body, is she not a stranger? As proof, even if our child were suffering from a serious illness, you do not feel the slightest pain or itching in your own body, do you?'

Tena: 'How merciless you are to the very end. When my daughter became seriously ill, my own body felt distressed so that I could not sleep, and I even thought that if it were possible, I would want to suffer in her place.'

Karuchin: 'Even I felt it in my body when I saw my daughter suffering, but compared to her pain, it was only about one-twentieth of the agony. Still, since it is painful for me to suffer, if the secret is to be revealed, we must save our own lives even if it means executing our daughter. It is not wrong for a parent to execute a child who does not listen to what they say. Even if it were a total stranger, if they leaked our secrets or broke the rules, would we not cut their head off, slicing through them like cutting a radish? Even the person who is cut has parents, siblings, and children, so the suffering is the same. If we were to talk like that, we could never live properly in this world. Protecting oneself is the first priority.'

§ 2

Speaker: 'If we proceed with that Hitsupafu, would you kill me if the time came when you had to take my life to save your own?'

Speaker: 'That is a settled matter. Is it not an honor for a wife to act as a substitute and be killed to save her husband's life? Since she will be praised as a model of a virtuous woman until Kōsei, one cannot imagine how much more glorious it is for the wife who is killed.'

Speaker: 'You have become terribly Reikoku since you went to worship at the capital of Haruna. I wonder if perhaps the Yamata no Orochi has Hiyōi upon you.'

Speaker: 'It is said that what those above do, those below follow; the water from the Kahakami will surely flow down to the Kahashimo. Since I have reached the point of being favored by Ōkuronushi-sama, I do not even consider a slight sacrifice, for great Taikō does not look back at Saikin, and it is no more to me than a Harukaze blowing against my face.'

Speaker: 'If that is so, then if there were another Ritsupa woman, you would drive her away just as Ōkuronushi-sama drove away Onikumohime-sama, would you not?'

Speaker: 'Hey, do not Tsuikiu me that far, for it becomes Mizukusa.'

Speaker: 'Hmph, you certainly Otsushiya. You, who hold the principle that even parent and child, who are said to be inseparable as parent and child for one life and husband and wife for two, may be killed for self-preservation, would surely find it as easy as eating breakfast to divorce a wife who can be replaced at any time. Truly, if your thoughts have deteriorated to this extent, there is nothing more to be said.'

Speaker: 'Hey, you think this is about someone else, but it is your own affair. Are you not the villain who constantly consulted with me about deposing King Sēran? You are the ringleader, are you not? Because you always complained like a habit that it was pathetic for me to be the right-hand man and a subordinate to Kūrinsu, I, who was never a good person to begin with, ended up being infected by you and started this scheme, which is neither good nor, for myself, evil.'

Speaker: 'Ho ho ho, you certainly Otsushiya such transparent things. As expected of one favored by Ōkuronushi-sama, you Otsushiya such grand things.'

Speaker: 'Let us bring this husband and wife quarrel to a reasonable end. It would be Taihen if it reached the ears of Samarihime.'

Speaker: 'What, what does it matter if it reaches her? Your cruel spirit is such that if things go wrong, you would kill your only daughter and make me follow in the footsteps of Onikumohime-sama, so when I think of that, it is absurd, and I cannot perform such a dangerous feat. Samarihime is not your child alone; she is a daughter born from my own pain. If you Otsushiya such Mizukusa things, I will not stand for it.'

As they were speaking, Samarihime appeared, looking frightened with her eyes swollen.

Speaker: 'Father, Mother, would it not be better if you went to sleep now?'

Kāruchin was startled.

Speaker: 'You are Samarihime, why do you come out to a place like this at such an hour? Why do you not go to your bedroom and sleep? You must have been eavesdropping on our conversation.'

Speaker: 'Yes, I have heard every detail of the situation. Please, do as you wish with me. I will resign myself to the thought that I have a demon for a parent...'

Speaker: 'Hey daughter, what are you saying? What is this about a demon parent?'

Speaker: 'Ho ho ho, although this Samarihime has returned to the mansion of Kāruchin after arguing with the King, in reality, she is the Queen of the King, Samarihime. Even though you call yourself a parent, you are a subject. If you say such insolent things, I will not forgive you. Now, let us do as we please.'

She pressed her body against him and threw herself before Kāruchin.

Speaker: 'Very well, now that the conspiracy has been revealed, you are not one whom I should let live. What does it matter if there is a master-servant relationship or not? Now, prepare yourself.'

§ 3

He stood up, and as he attempted to grasp his sword and draw it, Tena gripped his hand firmly.

Tena: 'Hey, Karuchin-dono, you must not do such a reckless thing.'

Karuchin: 'Now that it has come to this, there is no other way but to kill Samari-hime and prevent the conspiracy from being exposed. Come, prepare yourself.'

As he reached for the hilt again, Tena held him back from behind with all her might, and at the top of her voice, she cried out.

Tena: 'Samari-hime-dono, please flee quickly!'

Samari-hime remained calm and did not budge an inch.

Samari-hime: 'Ho-ho-ho, Karuchin-dono, you have become quite senile, have you not? Do you think that by taking the life of me alone, this conspiracy will not be revealed? Now that it has reached the ears of the Ōsama, it is already too late. No matter how strong the Goisei of Ōkuronushi-sama may be, he cannot send reinforcements so quickly from the capital of Haruna, which is separated by several hundred ri. Furthermore, since the Ōsama is followed by many retainers of incomparable Chiusei, your rushing will be to no avail. I shall take the head of Karuchin from here, present it to the Ōsama as a Miyage, clear my name, and be cherished as before; therefore, both of you, sit down there—it is the command of a Hime. I, Samari-hime, the consort of the King of Inna, strictly command it.'

Tena: 'Hey, hey, Hime-sama, how can you be so heartless? To raise a blade against the parents who gave you life and nurtured you—is that not a deed unbecoming of a human?'

Samari-hime: 'Since it is the result of my parents' upbringing, it cannot be helped. You said just now that for the sake of one's own body, one would even take the life of one's child. You, who have taught the lawless doctrine of kin devouring kin, it cannot be helped. Come, prepare yourself.'

Karuchin: 'No, Hime-sama, please wait a moment. I was merely teasing my wife while under the influence of sake. It is absolutely, absolutely unthinkable. Even though you are my own child, how could I ever aim a treacherous blade at you, who have become the consort of the King?'

Samari-hime: 'Have you not already aimed an invisible blade at the Ōsama? To harbor such grand ambitions and attempt to usurp the throne is a wicked act against humanity; do you not fear Daijizaiten-sama? You have made me a sacrifice for your conspiracy, have you not? There can be nothing more cruel than this. My daily concerns for the Ōsama are not merely one or two. Because I feel pity for the Ōsama, I have gradually come to sympathize with him, and now I have come to love him. Yet, the Ōsama pours his heart into Yasudara-hime-sama, the daughter of Sayomori-sama, whether he is sleeping or waking, and treats me with extreme coldness; this is all because the hearts of my parents are not good, and thus, for some reason, I do not please the heart of the Ōsama. I pray that you will repent as soon as possible. If not, I, Samari-hime, shall execute you both; prepare yourselves.'

As she drew her dagger smoothly, Karuchin became desperate.

Karuchin: 'What, you insolent, unfilial daughter!'

Saying this, he quickly snatched the dagger away, bound her hands behind her back, and locked the Hime in the basement.

The Hime gritted her teeth in frustration, and while cursing Karuchin's wickedness at the top of her voice, she prayed with all her heart: 'Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko-no-Mikoto, protect me, bless me...'

Karuchin sighed with relief.

Karuchin: 'Ah, now I can be at ease. The world does not always go as one wishes. This is a case of being able to give birth to the body, but not the soul. Hey, Tena, though she is a daughter born from your womb, she is a remarkably righteous and splendid person. What she says is truly in accordance with reason. However, now that it has come to this, there is nothing that can be done. It is pitiful, but there is no other way but to keep her locked in the dungeon for a while. For there is a fear that the conspiracy might be exposed...'
