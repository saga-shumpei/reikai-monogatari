Chapter 10: 狼の Rock Cave [1114]

狼の岩窟〔1114〕

§ 1

At a distance of four or five ri from the capital of Iruna lies a high mountain range called Takateruyama. To reach the capital of Iruna, one must by all means cross this mountain. In the days of the great Daikōzui of old, Takateruhimenomikoto descended from heaven and saved the Kunibito at the summit of this high mountain; it is for this reason that it is still called Takateruyama today. This mountain pass is known as Teruyamatauge. One hundred thousand years ago, a great Daijishin occurred in the world, and what is now Indo, which was once a vast Kaugenchi, subsided significantly. The capital of Haruna has now become Bonbei, but the present-day Bonbei corresponds exactly to the summit of Taiunzan in that era. The capital of Haruna sank deep into the Kaitei. Therefore, it goes without saying that from the perspective of modern Chirigaku and Chishitsugaku, there are many points in this story that differ greatly.

Two or three ri to the right of Teruyamatauge lies what is called the Rock Cave of the Wolves. This was a wolf cave where truly fearsome packs of wolves roamed and strode about as if the heavens and earth were their own, allowing not a single human to set foot in that place. Koganehime and Kiyoteruhime were lured by a pack of wolves from just before the forest of Iruna and came to enter this Rock Cave of the Wolves. (Wolves is another name for a cannibalistic race.)

Although it was said to be the fearsome lair of wolves one hears of in rumors, as the mother and daughter sang their propaganda songs and were led by the wolves deep into the mountains, the wolf army, numbering in the countless millions, formed lines along the narrow mountain path, letting out cries of joy as they waited to welcome the two as they approached. The mother and daughter, while keeping a watchful eye on their surroundings, finally advanced into the great rock cave where the Wolf King resided.

The interior of the rock cave was very spacious and beautiful, with gold, silver, agate, giant clam, and lapis lazuli shining in various places. Its beauty felt as if they had stepped into a palace of heaven. As the mother and daughter, contrary to their expectations, were led by the wolves deep into the interior, they found an old man of strange appearance with white hair sitting upright with a beautiful Himegami, whispering something to the wolves. The wolves seemed as if they could well understand human speech. When the mother and daughter, filled with wonder, drew near to the old man, they discovered to their surprise that it was the propaganda envoys of the Sangokyō, the husband and wife of the Amenomehitotsukami.

Koganehime was startled and, letting out a cry of astonishment, said: 'Oh, are you not the god of the northern light? It is a rare place to meet you. Why on earth have you and your wife shut yourselves away in such a wolf cave?'

The old man replied: 'Having received the command from Kami Susanoo-no-Mikoto that, as you have attained the divine power of the miraculous, there is no longer any need for you to save the human world, and that since the human world is sufficiently served by other propaganda envoys, you shall from now on enter the dwellings of wild beasts, save the spirits of those pitiful creatures, elevate them, and bestow upon them the dew of grace so that they may change their lives and be reborn as humans—I have humbly accepted this, and at last, I have become a king of wild beasts, just like Takayorihime and Ryūkokuwake-sama. Aha, ha, ha, ha.'

Koganehime replied: 'How truly the benevolence of the great Kami extends even to birds and beasts; this is what is meant by that. As we, mother and daughter, were about to cross the forest of Iruna to proceed to the capital, several dozen wolves appeared, bit the sleeves of our garments, and pulled us forcibly; thinking it must be the will of the Kami, we were led by the wolves to this place and have come to visit the rock cave. Oho, ho, ho, ho.'

Takeno said: 'Were you the divine official Koganehime, whose name is renowned in the Sangokyō? And is the young one Kiyoteruhime-sama? You have my gratitude for your hard work for the sake of the world and the Way.'

Koganehime replied: 'Yes, thank you. Were you Takenohime-sama, who served with the peach fruit at Yomotsuhirasaka? I have heard of your great name and have prayed day and night to see your face, but here I have obtained an audience in an unexpected place. As we are mother and daughter whose spirits are clouded, please, I beg you to scold us.'

'It is just like us mother and daughter. So saying, she let her voice grow dim, shed a tear, and looked down.

'God Kitateru, please protect Kiyoteruhime as well. I will surely fulfill the mission. Takahime-sama, I humbly ask for your favor.'

'What a gallant figure Kiyoteruhime-sama has; please be careful so that you do not encounter the troubles of men. After all, you have had such an experience yourself once before.'

Kiyoteruhime blushed slightly and looked down in a modest manner. The God Kitateru, wishing to celebrate the departure of the mother and daughter, began to sing a Sendenshi-ka in a clear and resonant voice:

'High shines the divine majesty, Built deep in the mountain depths, In the rock cave of the wolves, The Sendenshi who opens the Oshihe, Kitateruhiko and Takahime, Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, Their noble lives' departure, Celebrating with a pure Sendenshi-ka, Reverently and respectfully I proclaim, Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, May the spirits be blessed, Heading toward the capital of Iruna, The colors of the autumn fields are deep, For the noble mission of Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, May the infinite benevolence of the Ōkami, Grant them the great divine power, Let the Kenzoku protect them, To the mansion of King Sēran, How valiant to send them forth.'

竹野姫は又黄金姫母娘の首途を祝し、宣伝歌を歌ふ。

『鬼熊別が妻司と 現はれ給ひし蜈蚣姫 心の暗の戸押し開き 真如の月の御光に 照らされ給ひ三五の 神の司と進みまし 名さへ目出度き黄金姫の 貴の命と言あげし 黄竜姫と現はれて 竜宮島に時めきし 小糸の姫も今は早 三五教の神司 清照姫となり給ふ 神の恵の幸はひて 曲は忽ち善となり 曇は晴れて大空の 青きが如く　すくすくと 心勇ませ給ひつつ 神素盞嗚大神の 御言畏み秋の空

と歌ひ了り、神殿の神酒を下げ来りて、母娘に戴かせ、首途を送る。黄金姫は簡単に三十一文字を以て答礼に代ふ。

『北光の神の命や竹野姫 その宣言を固く守らむ。 世を救ふ心のたけの清ければ 世に恐るべき曲はあるらめ。 いざさらばこれより進み入那国 セーラン王を守り助けむ』

清照『二柱神の御言を畏みて 母娘は心勇みて行かむ。 狼の御供の司に守られて 入那の国に進む嬉しさ。 北光の神の命よいざさらば 待たせ給へよ帰り来る日を。 竹野姫神の命に物申す 汝が背の君をよく守りませ』

竹野姫は之に答へて、

『大神の御稜威も空に高照の イルナに進む人ぞ尊き。 北光の神の司は生神よ 今日は岩窟に明日は入那に』

黄金『いと清き神の司の御教に われは進みて都に上らむ。 いざさらば二柱ともまめやかに 神の大道に仕へ給はれ』

かく歌ひ、別れを告げて再び身づくろひをなし、黄金姫、清照姫は狼に送られ、急坂を勇み進んで下り、山口に出で、再び元来し道に引返し、照山峠を越えて入那の都に進むこととはなりぬ。
