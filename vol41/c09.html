Chapter 9: Lotus の River 辺 [1113]

蓮の川辺〔1113〕

§ 1

In the land of the moon where the sky shines brightly, in the capital of the country of Teruman, where the morning sun also shines clearly, lived Yasudarahime, a Setsuteiri and cousin to King Seran, who had descended as the wife of a wealthy man of the Bishiyazoku Shiyar. As she lamented her own misfortune morning and evening, passing sad days and months, an unexpected event occurred. Tena, the wife of Karuchin, a man of the ugly officials who served as the Umori-no-tsukasa of the Iruna country, approached the mansion of Shiyar from afar. Acting as a messenger for the Umori-no-tsukasa, she cleared the surroundings and advanced with a dignified air, which was truly detestable. Upon hearing that a messenger had arrived, Shiyar was startled and, in his own living room, treated the messenger with polite hospitality, offering tea and sweets.

After entertaining the lady Tena, he ordered the Yakatamori of Yasudarahime, Leader, and others to cleanse the inside and outside of the mansion. Thinking the time was right, Lady Tena entered while being guided, and facing Yasudarahime, she spat out unreasonable and violent language. Yet, fearing that she might offend the heart of Lady Tena, she was a sycophant who wagged her tail to the fullest, a coward. She threw Yasudarahime, the wife of the second generation, into a solid and incomparable prison, and feeling relieved, she thought she had finally escaped the crisis; such petty behavior is hateful. Leader, the servant who had devoted his sincere heart to Yasudarahime, took advantage of a night with heavy rain and wind, swung his sharp axe, and broke through the prison without difficulty.

Carrying the princess on his back, he slipped through the heavily guarded mansion, vanished into the darkness, and walked along the night road crossing the wilderness. Advancing north and north, they fled back to the banks of the Lotus River, the border of Iruna, the homeland that Yasudarahime longed for. Just as they were about to escape, Karuchin and his subordinates, countless in number, were waiting on both banks of the Lotus River, having prepared themselves to intercept the two as they fled. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, may the blessings of the gods be upon them.

May the two, man and woman, be returned safely to their home country as soon as possible, as Mizuki prays in the sky of her heart, and as I humbly pray before the Lord Brahman in her stead. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, or the Magatsu-no-kami should rage, may the gods open the path ahead without a shadow, protecting the master while the loyal servants continue their way. Kannagara, becoming one with the heart of the gods, while worrying about the path ahead, I now begin to speak of it. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, may the divine spirits be blessed.

Yasudarahime and Leader, as a result of a thousand hardships, finally escaped the tiger's mouth and reached the bank of the Lotus River. It was the end of autumn, and the slightly waning, clear, and bright moon shone upon the heads of the master and servant without fail, protecting their journey warmly. The two arrived safely at the riverside, and while gazing at the moon, which had finally tilted to the west, Yasudarahime said:

Yasudarahime: 'Illumined by the light of the moon traversing the heavens, it seems we have arrived at the banks of the Lotus River.'

Leader: 'The moon is good and the sky is clear in the land of the moon; I, who have followed Yasudarahime, feel my heart finally clearing up, and the moon of the Iruna country shines brightly.'

Yasudarahime: 'Protected by the god of my servant, I have easily returned to my homeland of Iruna, and I am happy. How might King Seran, the god, be gazing at tonight's moon? I yearn for his face in this cold night, thinking of the moon I once saw in Iruna. The moon that shines upon the body of Leader, who has accompanied me, and protects us, is noble.'

Leader: 'The moon in the sky I look up to is clear, but the mansion where Tena lives is muddy. Even the moonlight shining across the great sky is covered by the ugly village clouds, which is detestable. May this journey be protected easily by the grace of the god Ōkunihiko.'

Yasudarahime: 'Having snuck out of the mansion of my husband in Teruman, the moonlight finally shines here. The darkness of my heart clears at once at this Lotus River; there are no demons to hinder me as I cross this floating world. Nevertheless, there are wind and rain in the heavens, and it is not certain that there will be no calamity for men. Be careful, Leader, my servant; if this river is the barrier of the Iruna country.'

Leader: 'With reverence, I shall pour my heart into the front and rear, guarding and serving thee.'

Yasudara: 'The land where my Lord and my father dwell draws near, the land I have yearned for morning and evening. How frustrating it is that my feet cannot advance as quickly as my heart desires.'

While singing thus, the master and servant look up at the sky and rest their breath. Suddenly, from the grass on the riverbank, several dozen black shadows appear, and in the blink of an eye, they surround the pair from front, back, left, and right, maintaining a distance of four or five ken without drawing closer, forming a human wall and glaring at them. Leader raises his voice:

Leader: 'I am Leader, a master of martial arts who serves as the Samori-no-Tsukasa and attends to Yasudara-hime-sama, the Omusume of Kurinsu-sama. I know not whose command you follow, but it is a grave discourtesy to obstruct our path. Open the way at once, perform dogeza, and offer your apologies to the Hime-sama. If you hesitate, I shall show you something to behold. Come, make haste; those who value their lives would do well to do as I say.'

From among the crowd, a large man who appears to be the leader suddenly blocks Leader's path, opens his mouth wide, and laughs aloud:

Haruman: 'Hahahaha, your grand words just now are laughable. I am Haruman, peerless in strength, known in the land of Iruna as a master of martial arts. By the order of Karuchin-sama, I have gathered men at this checkpoint to capture you both alive, and I have been waiting impatiently for your arrival. Do not dally, and surrender to your bonds at once.'

Leader: 'Hahahaha, what nonsense you spew, you empty-headed Haruman. I, Leader, shall show you a bitter sight.'

No sooner had he spoken than he swung his iron fists and charged toward Haruman. Haruman stepped back a pace or two, took a firm stance, and commanded:

Haruman: 'Hey, you men, I am occupied with Leader alone, so capture Yasudara-hime in the meantime!'

At his command, the several dozen men answered with a roar and rushed headlong toward the lone Yasudara-hime. Yasudara-hime immediately untied her sash, crossed her sleeves in a cross-pattern, and tightened her headband—the very picture of a brave female warrior. Though she fought off the small-fry warriors who approached, throwing them one after another into the grass or the riverbed, she became exhausted by the enemies who replaced one another, and she collapsed to the ground with a thud. Just as several large men seized the opportunity to pile upon her, pinning her down with all their might and twisting her arms to bind her with rope, a song of propaganda echoed from behind, shaking the four directions:

'The Kami has appeared in the world, / To distinguish between right and wrong, truth and falsehood. / I, who served the Kami of Ural, / Am a noble missionary. / Crossing over to Ceylon Island, / I was a Kamutsukasa of the Brahman religion. / I banished King Sagaren, / And having obtained Kerisu-hime, / I entered the Kamiji Castle in high spirits. / When I became a Kamutsukasa of the Kami, / A missionary of the Sangyō, / The Kamutsukasa of the Heavenly One-Eye, / Appeared and uttered the most pure, / Noble Kototama. / The Kamiji Castle was instantly / Licked by tongues of crimson lotus. / The Yamata-no-Orochi that had long possessed my body / Was startled, / And fled like clouds and mist; / The dreams that had bewildered me until now have awakened.

'Returning to a heart of sincerity, / I advanced with courage / And became a noble believer of the Sangyō. / Moved from the bottom of my heart / By the infinitely merciful ways / Of the Tsukasa of the Sangyō, / I left the capital of Kamiji behind, / And crossed over to the land of the Moon. / Without leaving a corner of the seven thousand and more lands, / I am the one who advances / Singing the propaganda songs of the Sangyō. / Though there may be various distinctions / Between the Brahman religion, the Ural religion, / And the Sangyō, / The original Great Ancestor who created and solidified this world / Is but one pillar, / The Kami who is our Father. / The people under heaven / Are all, without a single exception, / Noble children of the Imperial Kami, / Manifested in His breath.'

と、包まず隠さず己が素性を打ち明け、落涙しながら其事実を物語る殊勝さに、ヤスダラ姫は感に打たれ、

『貴方が音に名高き竜雲様で厶いましたか。ようまあ其処迄御改心が出来ました。実に御立派な御人格とおなり遊ばしましたなア』

『お褒めに預かつては畏れ入ります。私も神様のお蔭によつていろいろと苦労を与へられ、身魂を研いて頂きました。これも全く三五教のお蔭で厶います』

『三五教はそれだけ感化力が厶りますか、実に結構な教で厶いますなア。私も一度其教が聞かして頂きたいもので厶います』

『貴女さへ聞きたいお心におなりなさつたならば、神様はキツト聞かして下さるでせう。先日も三五教の宣伝使、照国別様が数多の人の前で、結構な話をして居られた。其教を聞いた人間は貴賤老幼の嫌ひなく残らず帰順して仕舞ひました。誠一つを立て抜く無抵抗主義の三五の教へは、其伝播力も強く、恰も燎原の火の如き勢で厶います』

ヤスダラ姫は、

『あゝ左様で厶いますか』

と云つたきり、さし俯いて両手を合せ「国治立の神、吾身の将来を守らせたまへ」と小声になつて祈つて居る。リーダーは膝頭の負傷を撫で擦りながら漸くにして立ち上り、竜雲に向ひ叮嚀に頭を下げ、

『今承はれば貴方は有名な竜雲様で厶いましたか、思はぬ所でお目に懸りました。これも何彼の因縁で厶いませう。よくまあ姫様の御危難をお救ひ下さいました。私は下僕のリーダーで厶います。何分宜敷此後の御指導を願ひます』

§ 4

Riūn also bowed his head and spoke in a polite manner,

Riūn: 'Yes, I am humbled by your words. As they say, even the brushing of sleeves is a bond from a previous life; I, a sinful Riūn, pray that we may help one another. And where might you be traveling to?'

Speaker: 'Yes, the Hime-sama is returning from the mansion of Shiyāru in the land of Teruman to the capital of Iruna, to the house of Kūrinsu-sama, and I have come to accompany her. However, here by this river, the subordinates of Kāruchin, the Kamutsukasa of the Umori, attempted to capture the Hime-sama, and I cannot help but be truly worried that some Daihenji must be occurring in the capital.'

Riūn: 'I, too, have heard faintly that a terrible Antō has arisen between the two, the Samori and the Umori. The man called Kāruchin is truly a Shiremono of Kannei and Jiyachi, a Kamutsukasa who is ill-regarded by the Kunibito; in contrast, the popularity of the Samori is so great that it is enviable. It is likely that because of such things, the Umori has stirred up Shitsutoshin, and a Monchiyaku is taking place. From here on, though my abilities may be limited, I, Riūn, shall escort the Hime-sama to the capital of Iruna, so please have Goanshin.'

Speaker: 'Yes, thank you. Shall I say I have met a Buddha in hell, or that I have met a saving god in the land of the roots? There is nothing more joyful than this. Being a weak woman on a journey, I humbly place myself in your care.'

Riūn: 'Then I shall protect your person, following behind and going before you. Come, let us go.'

No sooner had he said this than the figure of Riūn vanished into the thicket of grass. The two, master and servant, crossed the lotus river while singing a propaganda song, and feeling a sense of courage welling up within them, they proceeded toward the north with light footsteps.

『ハイ、テルマン国のシヤールの館からイルナの都、クーリンス様のお宅へ指して姫様がお帰り遊ばすので、私はお供に参つたので厶います。然るに此川辺に於て、右守の司のカールチンが部下共、姫様を此処にて捕へむと致しましたについては、何か都に大変事が起つて居るので厶まいせうと実に心配でなりませぬ』

『左守、右守お二方の間に大変な暗闘が出来て居ると云ふ事は、此竜雲も薄々聞き及んで居ります。カールチンと云ふ男は実に奸侫邪智の痴者で、国人の受けの悪い神司、それに引きかへ左守の人気のよい事、羨ましい位で厶います。そんな事から右守が嫉妬心を起し、悶着が起つて居るのでせう。これから及ばずながら竜雲が姫様をイルナの都迄お送り致しますから御安心なさいませ』

『ハイ有難う、地獄で仏に会うたと申さうか、根底の国で救ひの神に会うたと申さうか、こんな嬉しい事は厶りませぬ。何分かよわき女の旅、宜敷お願ひ致します』

『然らば私が後になり前になり御身辺を保護して参ります、サア往きませう』

と云ふかと見れば、竜雲の姿は忽ち草の茂みに隠れて仕舞つた。主従二人は宣伝歌を歌ひながら蓮の川を横ぎり、何となく勇気加はり、足許もいと軽げに北へ北へと進み往く。
