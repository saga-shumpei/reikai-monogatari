Chapter 20: 誘惑 [1124]

誘惑〔1124〕

§ 1

Serisuhime was playing the Ichigenkin in her Ima at Iruna Castle.

'Kuniharutachinōokami, who created heaven and earth, is the true parent of the hundred Kami and all Hitobito, and bestows His Jinji and Mugen Shintoku upon all. In Iruna Castle, day by day, Yamataorochi, Shikogitsune, and Magaoni run rampant. The Umoritsukasa Karuchin, born of the Shiyuda lineage, has climbed the ladder of success, and now, in his pride and arrogance, he covets the Mikurai of King Seran, which is a wretched thing. At this time, when Iruna Castle has become like a Fūzen no Tomoshibi, the saving Kami shall appear to Tatenaho the tilting castle and protect the person of King Seran. When the time comes for Him to bring down the Tetsutsui upon the heads of the Magami, ah, how Omoshiro, how Omoshiro it shall be. I, who was born as the younger sister of Yasudarahime, am the Futobashira of Iruna Castle. Knowing it to be a Hidau act, I manipulate Eufutesu, who follows the Magami, with my Kotoba, and I have probed into the plots of the Shikogami without missing a single detail. For the sake of the Kami, for the sake of the sovereign, and for the sake of the Yobito, how my heart aches with suffering. Nevertheless, the Kami of heaven and earth shall gaze upon our sincerity with pure eyes and will surely forgive us. Though I am not unaware of the pity in the heart of the deceived Eufutesu, this is a small matter before a great one. I cannot disobey the imperial command of King Seran, and so I swallow my tears and manipulate the rough man, what a painful task it is. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. The Kami shall appear before us to distinguish between the good Kami and the evil Kami. The Kaminaohi who created this world, and the Ōnaohi whose heart is vast; in all things in the world of men, let us look again and listen again with Naohi, and repent of our own transgressions. May the noble teachings of the Kami and the heart of Serisuhime be pitied, and may the person of King Seran be protected quickly, Kannagara. Before the Ōkami who created this world, I bow and pray; before Him, I revere and pray.'

Having finished the song, she joined her hands and prayed in a quiet voice, 'Kuniharutachinōokami, protect us and grant us happiness.' Just then, Eufutesu entered, treading softly.

Seeing Eufutesu enter in a fluster, Serisuhime spoke with urgency:

Serisuhime: 'Ya, you are the dear Eufutesu-dono, is there something unusual?'

Eufutesu: 'Hai, I have something I must report immediately, so I have groomed myself before the Umoritsukasa. The plot has been finalized for the Kami Daikokunushi to dispatch an army of five hundred cavalry, join forces with the Umoritsukasa, and force King Seran to retire. We must make some preparations.'

Serisuhime: 'When will that army arrive here? You must know, I suppose.'

Eufutesu: 'It will not be too long. A messenger going to Ōashiwake, who was dispatched to the land of Karumata, threw a letter from Daikokunushi-sama into the mansion of the Umoritsukasa on his way. The Umoritsukasa was somewhat relieved and was even saying that since the army was no longer necessary, he might decline the offer; but then he received the letter promising to send five hundred reinforcements, and suddenly his arrogance has grown. Therefore, I came to report to you without a moment's delay.'

Serisuhime remained calm, not the least bit flustered, and with a smile on her face, she said:

Serisuhime: 'That is becoming more and more Omoshiro. No matter how it turns out, is it not fine as long as your marriage and mine can be arranged conveniently? Ohohohoho.'

Eufutesu: 'That may be so, but if King Seran is forced out, it will not be very convenient for you either. Consequently, I will not be able to wield much influence, you see.'

Serisuhime: 'In any case, I will go and speak to Koganehime-sama, so please wait here.'

She stood up quickly and proceeded into the Ima of Koganehime, where she recounted the entire story of Eufutesu's report. There, Koganehime, Kiyoteruhime, and Serisuhime sat in a triad and whispered to discuss their countermeasures.

Speaker: 'It seems the army of Ōkuronushi is approaching sooner than expected; do you have any thoughts on how to stop them, Kiyoteruhime?'

As he spoke, he peered into the face of Kiyoteruhime. Kiyoteruhime, while smiling, replied:

Speaker: 'Mother-sama, that is a trivial matter. Shall I stop those five hundred cavalrymen myself?'

Speaker: 'That would be truly excellent, but how do you intend to stop them all by yourself?'

Speaker: 'At any rate, please call Umori here. Then, Umori and I will enter a room alone to hold a secret discussion, and I shall skillfully make Umori stop them.'

Ōgonhime nodded and said:

Speaker: 'Ho ho ho ho, Kiyosama, if you apply your beauty and eloquence, there should be no problem at all. Please, do it firmly.'

Speaker: 'To scatter an army of five hundred, leaving not a single one behind, with just a three-inch tongue—that would also be delightful, o ho ho.'

Sērisuhime, looking pleased, said:

Speaker: 'In that case, shall I now order Yūfutesu to summon Umori to this castle?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Please, do so quickly; attend to the procedure.'

Speaker: 'At times like this, a tomboy is also necessary. Kiyosama, you have certainly gained plenty of experience in such matters, o ho ho.'

Speaker: 'Mother-sama, please do not tease me. Even if it is autumn, this is too much.'

Speaker: 'Sērisuhime-sama, I beg you, please do it quickly.'

Sērisuhime answered 'Ai' and left the scene, approached the ear of Yūfutesu, who had been waiting in her living room, and whispered something. Yūfutesu, with a look of having understood everything, left Sērisuhime's living room, went outside, and while making the earth resound with his heavy footsteps, he hitched up his skirts, passed through the castle's riding ground where the winter wind blew through the treetops without reserve, and dashed toward the mansion of Umori with the speed of Idaten, as if shooting an arrow.
