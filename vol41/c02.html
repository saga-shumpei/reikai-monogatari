Chapter 2: 入那 Castle [1106]

入那城〔1106〕

§ 1

The palace of King Seran in the land of Iruna is surrounded by vast swamps to the east, west, and south, with only the northern side connecting to the wilderness. In this country, the King's palace is built upon a location chosen for its excellent scenery and superior defensive position.

King Seran ascended to the Shinden dedicated to Bonjizaiten from early in the morning to concentrate on his Kiguwan, and upon finishing, he returned to his Ima, sat down heavily, folded his hands, and lost in thought, he spoke to himself:

Seran: 'Ah, the world does not go as one wishes. Though Yasudarahime, the daughter of the loyal and peerless Samori-no-tsukasa Kurinsu, had been my betrothed since childhood by the command of my father the King, the influence of the Umori-no-tsukasa Karuchin, who fawns upon the god Ōkuronushi, increases day by day. He treats me as if I were non-existent, has driven Yasudarahime away to be the Niyōbau of Bishiya Shiyaru of the land of Teruman, and has forced me to take his own daughter, Samarihime—whom I despise most of all—as my queen. Truly, while it is said that the lower may offend the upper, this is the height of insolence. Ah, I wonder how Yasudarahime is faring now. I wish to meet her once, to pour out the depths of my heart that I have held since childhood, and to talk with her at leisure; yet, as I am of the Kshatriya royal lineage and Yasudarahime has been reduced to the status of a Bishiya's Niyōbau, a meeting in this world will surely never be granted. To be a king of a nation, yet to be separated in life from my betrothed and beloved wife, who is the most important person in my life, and to be forced to spend such painful days—what kind of karmic connection is this? Ah, Yasudarahime, please understand my heart.'

Unable to bear the feelings of longing, he found himself choking back tears. At this moment, the sliding door was pulled open with a soft sound, and Samarihime entered with the rustling of silk, her expression slightly changed.

Samarihime: 'King, your ill humor these days is not just a one-time or two-time occurrence. I cannot endure seeing your displeased face day and night, so I wish to be granted leave starting today.'

She spoke with a trembling voice, as if accusing him with deep meaning. The King was startled and, while gazing intently at Samarihime's face, replied:

Seran: 'Your words are incomprehensible; have I offended you in some way?'

Samarihime: 'No, no, it is by no means anything that would offend me. No matter what is said, my father, the wicked and lawless Karuchin, drove away Yasudarahime-sama, the daughter of the loyal and peerless Samori-no-tsukasa and your betrothed, and installed me—whom you do not favor—as your queen; therefore, it is no wonder you are displeased day and night. By this day, I can no longer endure it either. There is nothing as unfortunate as a couple without love or mutual understanding, so no matter what you say, I shall take my leave today and return to my father's mansion.'

Seran: 'Now, Samarihime, it is troublesome for you to say such things at this late hour. Could you not put yourself in my position for a moment?'

Samarihime: 'Yes, it is only natural that I, whom you dislike, should cause you worry and pain by serving at your side. It is truly heart-wrenching for a person of low status like myself to have stolen the position of Yasudarahime-sama and to be placed in such a station. Our union is like a paper lantern paired with a temple bell, or a moon with a soft-shelled turtle; if you would let us part while there is still no mutual grievance, I do not know how happy I would be. I heard your Hitorigoto just now, and you said it was regrettable that my father Karuchin drove away Yasudarahime-sama, the daughter of the loyal and peerless Samori-no-tsukasa, and installed the unwanted Samarihime as queen. No matter how you try to hide it, it is already useless. I shall now return to my father's house, have him inquire of Ōkuronushi-sama, and upon that, have my future course decided; so please, consider this the end of our bond.'

As she was about to stand up, the King, in a flurry, grabbed the sleeve of Hime and held her back.

King: 'Do not be so short-tempered. You are trying to trouble me, are you not?'

Hime: 'No, no, far from troubling you, I am merely anxious that you should be at ease. If that is all, pray excuse me.'

With these words, she shook off the King's hand, and with a look of intense anger, she turned and fled at full speed toward her father's mansion.

After Hime had dashed off as if in a frenzy, King Seran stood alone, silent and head bowed, lost in deep thought as to what would become of his own fate. Just then, Kurinsu, the Minister of the Left, entered quietly. Kurinsu was the younger brother of King Seran's father, King Badara, and thus a member of the Setsuteirizoku, serving as the King's uncle.

Kurinsu: 'Your Majesty, good morning. Just now, as I was entering the castle, I heard rumors among the household staff that you had some sort of dispute with Samari-hime-sama. It seems she changed her expression, ignored the attempts of many retainers to stop her, kicked them aside, and went straight back to the mansion of Karuchin. In any case, I have dispatched seven or eight retainers to the mansion of Karuchin to handle the situation and bring Hime back, but what on earth did you say to her? Karuchin, the Minister of the Right, is a great favorite of Lord Ōkuninushi, and he acts with such rising power that he seems to have neither the King nor the Minister of the Left in his eyes. If you offend Samari-hime now and invite the wrath of Karuchin, your own safety will surely be in jeopardy. A truly troublesome situation has arisen.'

King: 'There is no choice but to accept everything as the will of Heaven. I do not desire a position of prominence. I would not care if I were a Shudra or anything else. If a husband and wife can live in this world with their hearts in harmony, there is no greater joy for me than that.'

Kurinsu: 'Your Majesty, what are you saying? How can this country of Inuna be governed with such a mindset? If you do not keep your spirits strong, we, the Ministers of the Left, cannot perform our duties. Does the Minister of the Left not exist solely because of the King?'

King: 'Now that it has come to this, there is nothing to be done. Once Samari-hime has returned, Karuchin will surely seize this moment to carry out his long-standing conspiracy. With the help of Lord Ōkuninushi, he will eventually usurp my position and seize control of the country of Inuna. I have resigned myself to the fact that whatever happens is my fate.'

Kurinsu: 'While it is true that the recent insolent behavior of the Minister of the Right, Karuchin, is inexcusable, if one traces the root of the matter, it began when you offered your opinion regarding the retirement of Onikumohime-sama, which led to you being branded as a confidant of Onikumawake. In a world where villains prevail, sycophants and flatterers grow in power day by day and walk rampant, while the sincere and upright are oppressed. Therefore, you must consider the circumstances and become more socially adept. Though I know it goes against my heart, I offer this advice to you, who are so stubbornly upright, for the sake of our house.'

King: 'Even if I am glared at by Lord Ōkuninushi, even if my country is taken and my throne stripped away, and even if my life is taken, I will never side with evil. Rather than submitting to the whims of the lawless and wicked Lord Ōkuninushi, I would prefer to agree with the principles of Onikumawake-sama and be destroyed. Ah, say no more of such things to me.'

Kurinsu: 'If you leave things as they are, a terrible situation will occur.'

King: 'In my dream last night, a white-haired, strange divine being who called himself the God of Hokkohiko appeared and admonished me, saying: "Your own life, and indeed the nation of Inuna, are on the verge of a critical crisis. There is one way to save it. It is none other than this: Koganehime and Kiyoteruhime, the wife and daughter of the divine minister Onikumawake, have now become peerless evangelists of the divine power of the Sangokyo. As they are on their way to the capital of Haruna to begin a Kototama war, they must pass through this country of Inuna. If you have your subordinates search for them before Karuchin's men can capture them, and secretly invite them back to this mansion, then no matter how strong the power of Karuchin or Samari-hime may be, they will never be able to oppose them. Now that Lord Ōkuninushi has sent the two generals, Oniharawake and Ōashiwake, to lead a large army into battle, his power today is not what it was before. You must quickly select loyal subordinates, protect the path of the mother and daughter, and ask them to stop at this royal castle." Kurinsu, whether it was a dream or reality, I cannot say for certain, but I believe this must be the truth. What do you think?'

Kurinsu: 'Did you also see that dream, Ōsama? My, what a strange thing it is. I also saw that dream clearly last night, so I have come to report the nature of the dream. This must surely be a divine revelation from the Kamisama. If so, without losing a moment, I shall select loyal subordinates, claim that we are capturing the mother and daughter on the surface, and bring them here.'

Ōsama: 'In that case, Kurinsu-dono, I entrust the preparations to you without a moment's delay.'

Kurinsu: 'Yes.'

Having answered thus, Kurinsu respectfully took his leave and headed straight for his home at full speed.

The King sat alone in silence, folding his hands, feeling as though he had touched a glimmer of light.

Ōsama: 'If the dream of last night is realized, I too shall be able to escape this suffering. If things go well, I might even be able to be with Yasudarahime-sama again.'

Thinking such uncertain thoughts, he was lost in deep contemplation. Just then, Yufutesu, known as the chief retainer of Karuchin, arrived with heavy footsteps. With the arrogance of a fox borrowing the tiger's might, acting as if the King were beneath his notice, he pushed open the sliding door without announcement.

Yufutesu: 'Ōsama, I have come just now as a messenger for Karuchin-sama. It is said that you have abused Samarihime-sama and committed violence unbecoming of a king. Since my master Karuchin-sama is outwardly your retainer, and also the father of Samarihime-sama, he is the same as a father to you, Ōsama. Saying that as a parent he cannot help but admonish the impropriety of a child, no matter how much of a king he may be, he has sent a swift messenger to the Daikokunushi-sama of the capital of Haruna. Go to the annex and remain in confinement until there is a decree.'

He spoke in an overbearing manner, as if striking the King. His rudeness was beyond description. The King flared with anger.

Ōsama: 'Thou, a mere subordinate, art incredibly rude to me! I have no ears to hear such things. Yufutesu, tell thy master Karuchin that as of today, I dismiss him along with Samarihime-sama. Tell him to leave the mansion of the Minister of the Right without a moment's delay and go wherever he pleases!'

As the King shouted and glared at him, Yufutesu shrank back at the King's unexpected ferocity. Scratching his head, he hung his tail like a thin dog encountering a wolf, and looking diminished, he left in a dejected manner.

Ōsama: 'Ha ha ha ha! Yufutesu, the slave who serves the villain of the Minister of the Right, met with my shout and lost his spirit. Where did his initial momentum go? The way he slinks away—truly, evil is weak when it comes to the crunch. Ha ha ha ha!'

He laughed aloud, unaware of his surroundings. Then, Serisuhime, the younger sister of Yasudarahime, entered with quick steps.

Serisuhime: 'Ōsama, I, Serisuhime, am truly delighted to see you in such good health today. I have come forth without regard for my status as a woman, following the orders of my father, Kurinsu. Karuchin is planning to depose the King, believing this is the time to fulfill his long-held ambition, and has sent a swift messenger to the capital of Haruna. My father, Kurinsu, must make preparations against this, and as he is in the midst of ordering the chief retainer Teemusu to search for the whereabouts of Koganehime and her daughter, he could not come himself, so I, an unworthy woman, have come in his stead. Also, if my father were to visit the castle many times, it would only increase the suspicions of the kin of the Minister of the Right, so considering the future, he sent me as his proxy.'

Ōsama: 'Ah, is that so? If the matter is understood, a woman is fine. Tell me, Serisuhime, did you not meet Yufutesu just now?'

Serisuhime: 'Yes, I met him in the corridor just now. He returned in a very dejected state. That man is truly a person I do not like. Every single day he sends love letters to me and says such disgusting things. It is truly a troublesome matter.'

Ōsama: 'Ho, that is convenient. Here, Serisuhime, come closer, come closer.'

When he beckoned, Serisuhime answered 'Yes' and moved close to the King's side. When the King whispered something into the princess's ear, Serisuhime smiled, nodded, and left the room.

『王様、只今カールチン様のお使で参りましたが、貴方様はサマリー姫様を虐待遊ばし、王者の身としてあるまじき乱暴をお働きなさつたさうで厶りますな。吾主人カールチン様は表向き貴方様の御家来なり、又サマリー姫様の父親なれば、王様にとつてはお父様も同然で厶りませう。親として子の不埒を、何程王者なりとて戒められずには居れないと云つて、ハルナの都の大黒主様の御許に早馬使をお立てになりました。何分のお沙汰あるまで別館に行つて御謹慎をなさりませ』

と横柄面に打ちつけるやうに云ふ。その無礼さ加減、言語に絶した振舞である。王はカツと怒り、

『汝、臣下の分際として余に向つて無礼千万な、左様な事は聞く耳もたぬ。ユーフテス、汝が主人カールチンに対して余は今日限りサマリー姫と共に暇を遣はす、一時も早く右守の司の館を立出で、何処へなりと勝手に行けと申伝へよ』

と声荒らげてグツと睨めつけ叱りつくれば、ユーフテスは案に相違の王の権幕に縮み上り、頭をガシガシ掻きつつ、狼に出会うた痩犬の様に尾を垂れ、影まで薄くなつてシヨビ　シヨビとして帰つて行く。

『アハヽヽヽヽ、右守の司の悪人に仕ふるユーフテス奴、余が一喝に遇うて悄気返り、初めの勢何処へやら、スゴスゴ帰り行く其有様、ほんに悪といふものはマサカの時になれば弱いものだな、アハヽヽヽヽ』

と思はず知らず高笑ひして居る。そこへスタスタと足早に這入つて来たのはヤスダラ姫の妹セーリス姫なり。

『王様、今日は御壮健なお顔を拝し、セーリス姫誠に恐悦に存じます。就きましては早速ながら、父クーリンスの命により女の身をも顧みず罷り出でました。カールチンは年来の野心を成就するは今此時と、ハルナの都へ早馬使を立て王様の廃立を図つて居りまする。就いては吾父クーリンスはそれに対する準備も致さねばなりませず、家老のテームスに命じ黄金姫母娘の所在を探すべく準備の最中なれば、父が参る暇が厶りませぬので不束なる女の妾が参つたので厶ります。又父が幾度も登城致しますれば右守の身内の奴等に益々疑はれ事面倒となりますれば、向後を慮り妾を代理として参らせたので厶ります』

『あゝさうか。事さへ分れば女でも結構だ。時にセーリス姫、其方はユーフテスに今会はなかつたか』

『ハイ、只今お廊下で会ひました。大変な悄気方で帰つて参りました。あの男は実に好かない人物で厶ります。毎日日々妾の許へ艶書を送り、それはそれは嫌らしい事を云つて参ります。本当に困つた事で厶ります』

『ホー、そりや都合のいい事だ。これセーリス姫、近う近う』

と手招きすれば、セーリス姫は「はい」と答へて王の側近くににじり寄る。王は姫の耳に口寄せ何事か囁けば、セーリス姫はニツコと笑つて打頷き此場を立つて帰り行く。
