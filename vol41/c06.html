誤解〔1110〕

§ 1

In the inner chamber of Themus, the Karaushiyoku of Kurinsu who serves as the Samori of King Seran, four people—Rebu, Karu, and the wife, Berisu-hime—have formed a circle and begun a private conversation. Themus distanced Berisu-hime and finally began the serious discussion. The cautious Themus had intentionally sent away even his most trusted wife, fearing that the secret might leak elsewhere. Berisu-hime, having no choice regarding her husband's words, stood up and went to her own living room. Afterward, the three men huddled their heads together and became absorbed in a secret conversation.

'The truth is that wicked people are overrunning the palace of King Seran; Karuchin, the Umori, has ingratiated himself with the Daitōriyō Ōkuronushi, and they are plotting to exclude not only our master, the Samori Kurinsu-sama, but even the King himself. Since that would be a disaster, I have thought about how to overcome this crisis and punish the wicked, but no particularly good method or plan has emerged. Then, thinking that this was something beyond our own capabilities and that there was no other way but to ask the gods, Kurinsu-sama secluded himself in the shrine of Bonten-ō-sama for twenty-one days to beg for a divine oracle. To my surprise, the instruction was: "The propagators of the San-go-kyō, Kogane-hime and Kiyoteru-hime, will soon pass through the capital of Iruna, so beg the two of them to resolve this for you." Even when the Samori asked repeatedly, unable to grasp the meaning, the divine oracle remained unchanged. Therefore, the Samori-sama secretly summoned me, Themus, and I led seven or eight subordinates to the checkpoint to wait for the two of them on their way, intending to bring them back to the castle to have them resolve this. While we were hiding in a cave in the ground and watching, I heard your conversation along the way. Thinking the time had come, I crawled out of the cave and tried to hear the situation of the two of you. You said you would speak in front of the King, but if you do that, the enemy will surely find out, so could you please let us know where they are?'

Karu: 'Yes, actually, we accompanied them as far as the forest of Iruna, but suddenly a pack of wolves appeared and carried the two of them off somewhere, so we do not know the real details.'

'That is troublesome. If that is the case, I should not have gone to the trouble of coming all the way here.'

Rebu: 'No, please do not worry. Karu is a newcomer and knows nothing. I know everything from beginning to end. The truth is that the two of them use wolves as their kenzoku. Since they always help the two of them in times of crisis, there is no way the two of them would ever be killed by their own kenzoku. The gods foresaw that you were waiting in the cave with seven or eight subordinates and sent the wolves to hide them in another direction.'

'If that is so, has this Themus been seen as an enemy by the two of them? If that is the case, even if we knew their whereabouts, they would not easily grant our request. Oh, what a difficult situation.'

Rebu: 'No, no, that is absolutely not the case. Since more than half of the eight people you brought along are subordinates of Karuchin, they intentionally kept their distance. I, Rebu, sensed that, which is why I intentionally said such vague and incomprehensible things. Surely, within a day or two, they will come leading many wolves to subdue the wicked. Since that person possesses divine power, she knows where Rebu and Karu are, so she will certainly appear. She is not the kind of cold-hearted master who would abandon such important retainers and go off on her own.'

Shāru: 'Do not say stupid things; the moon has not even risen yet, has it? You must have been neglecting your duty as a Monban and dreaming. Why are you not awake until the Shinrei rings? You are useless because you always have a habit of slacking off. Tomorrow, I shall report this to the Goshiyujinsama and dismiss you.'

Pi: 'Iie, I was talking about the moon from yesterday. Please, I beg you to overlook it tonight.'

Emu: 'In that case, I shall refrain from reporting it to the Danna-sama today. Be very careful. There is still about a Han-toki until the Shinrei rings, so you must work diligently until then. If you are sleepy, bring out your eyes. I shall even smear some chili powder on them for you.'

Pi: 'Me, messō na, how could I endure such a thing? My eyes would swell up.'

Emu: 'Oi, Shāru, how about a visit of chili powder for you? You look quite sleepy, do you not?'

Shāru: 'Iya, there is nothing particularly sleepy about me. My eyes are different from the soft eyes of Pi. They are hard, very hard eyes. It is just that occasionally my upper and lower eyelids hold a meeting or get married, that is all.'

Emu: 'Saa, that meeting is what is not allowed. Keep your eyes wide open like a chestnut, do you hear? Are you listening, Aan?'

Emu gave a haughty 'Un' and, swinging his staff, hid his figure in the darkness.

After a while, the last quarter moon parted the eastern sky and began to rise, casting a shadow like a sharp sickle upon the ground.

At the gate, a woman's voice was heard: 'Moshi-moshi, Monban-sama, I am sorry for being so late, but there is a small matter, and I have come to see Tēmusu-dono, so please let me pass.'

Pi: 'Oi, oi, Shāru, a woman has come at this hour. This one is not to be trifled with. It is a fox or a raccoon dog in disguise. After eighteen o'clock at sunset, a woman should not be walking about. Besides, for her to make such a gentle voice at this hour, whoever knocks at this gate is surely a suspicious character. It is best to pretend we do not know.'

From outside the gate, a knocking sound was heard, 'Excuse me, Monban-sama, please open up quickly.'

Monban: 'Oi, oi, they have come, they have come. Look at the way that gate is being knocked. A fox has transformed itself and is striking the gate door with its tail, you know, Shiaru.'

Shiaru: 'Even so, that is surely a Ningen. Who knows what kind of secret Goyō or what kind of person has arrived. Why not open it and see? If you see that it is something suspicious, it would be fine to strike it with this club and make it reveal its Shiyōtai, would it not? If it happens to be a fox, it would be extremely Uma if we roasted its meat and used it as a side dish for our sake.'

Monban: 'In that case, shall I open it? Shiaru, do not let go of your club either. I will also strike it if I see that it is suspicious.'

He grasped a club in one hand and opened the gate with the other. As if she had been waiting for this moment, the woman leaped in from the narrow opening as though she were tumbling. The woman's white face and the color of her beautiful garments shone in the moon that was rising at that moment, appearing exactly like a Tenniyo. The Futari, thinking that this Koitsu was definitely a Bakemono, attacked from both sides with their clubs, but the woman nimbly dodged them, swept their legs, and threw the Futari onto the Daichi with a thud. She turned around with a Heizen expression and said, 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, how Abuna.'

Saying this, she walked on, aiming for the depths. The Futari, having been thrown by the woman's Gauriki, were crushed in spirit and said with trembling voices,

Monban: 'Oi, Shiaru-yo.'

Shiaru: 'Oi, Pii...-yo.'

Monban: 'It is Satsupari, Shiaru.'

Shiaru: 'Un, it is Satsupari. This is why I say I do not like being a Monban. Surely tomorrow I will be Menshiyoku. A Monban has no Menshiyoku once it comes to this, so it cannot be helped even if I am Menshiyoku. Ah, it seems I struck my Daitaikotsu terribly, and I cannot move even a little bit. Pii, how about you?'

Pii: 'Since I was also thrown onto the Daichi, it is pretty much the same for me.'

Just as they were talking, the sound of a ringing bell echoed all around.

Monban: 'Yaa, yaa, thank goodness, from now on it is my world for a while.'

The Futari crawled on all fours into the guardroom and went to sleep while enduring the pain in their legs and loins.

Berisu-hime had been ordered by her husband to leave the place of consultation, and she muttered in her heart, 'What a cold-hearted husband. Even if he says the secret might leak, where is there a secret that cannot be told to the wife one lives with for a lifetime? Surely he is hiding something from me and keeping a beautiful woman somewhere. Otherwise, there is no reason he could not have his wife by his side. Reebu and Karu are surely acting as go-betweens, and in the end, they might even make this Berisu be cast out and repeat the same fate as Daikoku-nushi-sama. Ee, what a bad feeling. Men are creatures that cannot be let off guard. Now that it has come to this, I have become tired of the world.' While muttering this, she could not sleep and came wandering, staggering to the entrance. At the entrance, a beautiful woman of marriageable age was illuminated by the moon, and she said in a thin, cool voice,

Woman: 'Excuse me, I have urgent business with Teemusu-sama, so please announce me for a moment.'

Berisu-hime was offended and said,

Berisu-hime: 'I do not know what kind of demonic woman you are, but where is there anyone who comes calling for a man's name in the middle of the night? Teemusu has a fine wife named Berisu. There is no need for you people to call my husband's name; please go home at once.'

Woman: 'Were you Berisu-hime-sama? I wish you happiness in your good health. Is Teemusu-sama at home?'

Berisu-hime: 'Hai, I cannot answer immediately whether he is here or not. You must have had a long relationship with Teemusu and have been waiting for me to die, but they say that a hated child thrives in the world... Look at this body, which is so lively that it seems it will live for a thousand or ten thousand years; it probably is not very auspicious for you in your position that I am so healthy.'
