急告〔1109〕

§ 1

It was at the Genkuwanguchi of the palace of King Seran that Umori no Karuchin met Mammoth, his right-hand man, and Samoahime.

Samoahime: 'Oh, Mammoth-sama, you have arrived at the Gotojiyō quite early today. Since I have grasped some solid Shiyōko regarding Yufutesu, whom you always say stands in the way of your Goshiyutsuse, would you please come quietly to my Ima? It is too crowded with Hitome here, and it would be Taihen if we were overheard.'

Mammoth lowered his voice and said,

Mammoth: 'What, are you saying you have found some Ketsuten in Yufutesu? Very well, let us go then.'

Samoahime: 'Please, tread softly and come to my room.'

She stood up and walked ahead, keeping a distance of about Shigoken.

Mammoth followed behind the princess, looking at her Ushirosugata with a Nanikuwanu face, and thought,

Mammoth: 'What a fine woman she is. She is clever in every way. Her Kiriyō, the way she walks—she is a woman with no Ketsuten anywhere. I must quickly form an Omowaku, win the Kuwanshin of Samoahime, and hold our Ketsukon ceremony as soon as possible. Since the princess herself is revealing such Tokubetsu Himitsu to me, I have secured my position. There cannot be another person as happy as I am in this land of Iruna. She is slightly in love with me, and I am deeply in love with her, so it is irresistible, ehehehe.'

Laughing and whispering to himself, he sneaked into the room of Samoahime. Samoahime lit some tobacco in a long pipe, and while wiping the mouthpiece with the sleeve of her kimono, she narrowed her eyes like willow leaves and said,

Samoahime: 'Now, Mammoth-sama, have a puff.'

She held it out. Mammoth also forced his chestnut eyes to narrow, purred like a cat, and acting like a handsome man, he took the pipe offered by Samoahime quietly, tilted his body, and blew smoke in rings. Samoahime lowered her voice and said,

Samoahime: 'Listen, Mammoth-sama, something Taihen has been discovered. It is surely the seed of your Goshiyutsuse.'

Mammoth also lowered his voice and said,

Mammoth: 'Samoahime-sama, what is it? Please tell me quickly.'

Samoahime stood up abruptly, opened the door slightly, stuck her face out, and looked around in all directions. As if relieved that there was fortunately no one there, she slammed the door shut, locked it firmly from the inside, sat quietly in front of Mammoth, gripped his left hand tightly, shook it back and forth two or three times, and said,

Samoahime: 'Listen, Mammoth-sama, be firm. This is your gateway to success. Yufutesu-sama has been sneaking into the room of Serisuhime, the daughter of Kurinsu, in secret, and has been leaking all of Karuchin-sama's schemes. It seems that the two of them have already concluded a love pact just like you and I have. And it appears he intends to reveal every Himitsu to King Seran-sama.'

Mammoth: 'Is that the truth? If it is true, it is as if a great stroke of luck has come to you and me.'

Samoahime: 'If I may, Mammoth-sama.'

She brought her mouth to his ear and whispered something for a while. Mammoth nodded repeatedly and said,

Mammoth: 'Samoahime-dono, please be very careful. I shall now go to the palace of Karuchin-sama to report this, explain Yufutesu's treason in detail, and have him suppressed. If we do that, we will become the chief retainers of Karuchin-sama, and I will be able to spend happy and splendid days with you. No matter how high the prestige of King Seran-sama may be, he is of the lineage of Onikumawake-sama, so there is no need to fear him. Karuchin-sama is an Umori, but he is a favorite of the Lord Ōkuninushi-sama, so he is a person of great consequence. After all, it is the way of a clever person to side with the one who has the better flag.'

Mammoth, who was ever alert to evil, left the Jiyōnai with Itsushiyōkenmei and rushed in haste to the mansion of Kārchin. Mammoth snuck in through the Uraguchi of Kārchin's mansion, proceeded to the inner chambers, and prostrated himself before the married couple, Kārchin and Tēna-hime.

Mammoth: 'Dannasama, Okusama, a Taihen matter has occurred. Please, Goyōjin.'

Kārchin was startled by these Kotoba and sat up on his knees.

Kārchin: 'What, what is this Taihen matter? Tell me the Isai quickly.'

He urged him on. Mammoth wiped away his sweat.

Mammoth: 'Yes, it concerns Yūfutesu, whom you hold in Goshinnin. You trust him as one who has no equal, but please be Yōjin, for he is a Kanjiya for King Sēran. He has even gone so far as to have an affair with Sērisu-hime, the daughter of Kūrinsu, and is reporting Itsusai of your Himitsu to King Sēran and Kūrinsu. If you do not Goseibai him while there is still time, an Ichidaiji that concerns your very Oinochi may occur at any Itsu. I had instructed Sāmoa-hime to observe his Yōsu, and since we have obtained certain proof, I humbly believe that it is for the sake of your house and for your own sake that you should summon him and Goseibai him even now.'

Kārchin: 'What, is Yūfutesu taking such treacherous actions? That is outrageous. I cannot leave this as it is.'

Tēna-hime: 'Wait, Dannasama, Yūfutesu is truly a man loyal to us, so he would surely not do such a thing. One must not immediately believe what others say. We cannot judge right or wrong without first investigating. Now Mammoth, you seem very flustered, but is this after a thorough investigation? Or have you merely heard rumors from others?'

Mammoth: 'Tēna-hime-sama, I am also one who has received the favor of Dannasama. Why would I report something without proof and cause the married couple such distress? I have proof that is genuine and beyond any doubt. Please, without losing any time, capture Yūfutesu and remove this root of evil from the house. No matter how much administrative work he can handle, I could do the same things that man does. If we delay, it will be an Ichidaiji; please, make your decision quickly.'

Kārchin was half-believing and half-doubting Mammoth's words. The reason was that Yūfutesu was the man he trusted most, and because he was well aware that a rivalry for status had implicitly arisen between the two, he wondered if Mammoth might be fabricating such things to bring about Yūfutesu's downfall.

Kārchin: 'Mammoth, is there not a single hair's breadth of error in what you say?'

Mammoth: 'I would never, ever speak a lie or falsehood. If we dally, it will be an Ichidaiji for the master, so I rushed out of the Jiyōnai without even taking my belongings to come and report this secretly.'

Kārchin and Tēna, the two of them, folded their arms and, unable to judge the truth or falsehood of Mammoth's report, remained silent and lost in thought for a while. It was then that Yūfutesu arrived, as if nothing were amiss. Yūfutesu, sensing that the atmosphere in the room was unusual and feeling a little suspicious of Mammoth's presence there,

Yūfutesu: 'Dannasama, Okusama, are you well? Oh, you are Mammoth. To request an audience directly with Dannasama without our permission is incomprehensible. Has some urgent matter occurred?'

He asked, pressing him with a little force in his voice. Mammoth was caught off guard and struggled to find an immediate reply.

Mammoth: 'Yes, well, I am overjoyed to see that you are also in good health. Dannasama is also well, so, well, how auspicious, how auspicious.'

He mixed up his honorifics and was flustered.

Yūfutesu: 'Mammoth's behavior is quite incomprehensible; he must have come to report secretly to Dannasama about my actions. This is no place for a subordinate like you to be; withdraw at once.'

Karchin: 'Whatever the case may be, since there is a shisai that I must inquire of Eufetes, Mammoth, you should go down to the ima for a while and wait for my meirei.'

At this stern hitokoe, Mammoth had no words to reply, and leaving his heart behind with a sense of temochibusata, he returned toward his ima.

Wrapped in a cloud of hanshinhangi, Karchin spoke with a stern kotoba,

Karchin: 'Eufetes, there is one thing I wish to ask you. For what naniyō did you go to the ima of Seris-hime? State the wake to me without wrapping it or hiding it.'

He asked with a harsh goki. Eufetes remained heizen and replied,

Eufetes: 'In truth, I had finished my tojiyō and was hurrying to appear before your gozen to maoshia the ichibushijifu regarding that very matter. Did that fellow Mammoth not say something to you?'

Karchin: 'Indeed.'

In truth, Seris-hime has fallen zotsukon in love with my manly qualities, and the oka of her sending me enshiyo time and again is beyond words. I believe it would also be very tsugafu for your shinyōjiyō regarding the kokumin.'

Tena: 'Is that truly shinjitsu? If that is shinjitsu, then I too must think about this. There is no need to stir up waves on heichi, after all.'

Tena: 'Indeed, that is so. Since we will be appointed as the samori no tsukasa as long as Dannasama occupies the position of setsuteiri, we have searched this far with all our might. Tomorrow, I shall go to the castle again and explain this thoroughly to Serisuhime, and through his mouth, which has the trust of the King, I shall urge Ōsama to make a decision as soon as possible.'

Thus, Yufutesu had already fallen into the trap of Serisuhime and had successfully deceived the Karuchin fūfu. Consequently, it was decided that Samarihime would be brought out from the prison, made to apologize to her parents, dressed in full attire, and sent to Inna Castle in a palanquin. Through the arrangements of Yufutesu, Manmosu was immediately thrown into prison.
