§ 1

Viewed from the outside, the cave appears quite vast, and a tunnel two ken wide is bored high into it. Following the three of them, I proceed for about two or three chō, feeling amazed at the grand cavern.

Takako: 'This is my dwelling. Please, do not stand on ceremony and come on up.'

Takahime: 'Yes, thank you. My, what beautiful blue tatami mats are spread out, and such magnificent wall hangings are displayed. How could such a magnificent and well-appointed place be created in this inconvenient land? Persistence is a fearsome thing. It must have taken a great deal of time to bore through such hard rock and construct a mansion like this.'

'Yes, my father began the excavation little by little from the time I was born. It is exactly fifty-six billion years this year, ho ho ho ho.'

'What are you saying? This cave, which you began digging when you were born, being fifty-six billion years old—does that not make the calculation a bit off? Is your age not still that of a flower bud of seventeen or eighteen years?'

'No, no, I am not such a young woman. Although I am not a theater actor, a woman can transform herself into anything, you see. I fill the wrinkles on my face with plenty of white powder, apply hair oil over that thick like wax, then apply white powder again like a white wall, add a touch of rouge, and paint my white hair with black liquid, thus making myself look young like this. This is the Dragon Palace of the land, and I am the Princess of the Dragon Palace. And my husband, Hinodewake, is resting in the back, snoring loudly. It seems he always borrows your physical body, which must be quite a trouble for you. I am the Princess of the Dragon Palace who frequently borrows the body of your servant, Kurohime-sama. The people of the world think that the Princess of the Dragon Palace always looks young, but it is because I am disguised like this, you see. You are also a vessel for a great monster, so depending on how you dress yourself up, you can look young too. Ho ho ho ho.'

'Are you not saying things that do not make sense? I am a bit confused, you know.'

'That is to be expected. This is the center point of the dark world called the Land of Five-Mile Dreams, a strange place named the Kurohime Palace of Takahime Village.'

'You lured me into a place like this and spoke of giving me the jewel; you must have some sort of scheme in mind.'

'Yes, of course, it is with a great purpose in mind.'

'What is that purpose? Let me hear it. Depending on your answer, I, Takahime, have a mind of my own. I am not a Takahime who would be transformed by some monster and remain meek!'

She shouted in a loud voice. Both Otsuki and Oasa pursed their small mouths,

'Ho ho ho ho...'

and laughed in mockery. The figure of Takako-hime suddenly changed into that of an old woman with white hair, thrusting forward a face like wrinkled astringent paper, with sharp fangs protruding about an inch from her lower lip,

'Ihi hi, what an old hag who cannot let go of her attachments, even after all this time.

Ufu hu hu, searching for the jewel, wandering aimlessly all over the world,

Ehe he he, why don't you give up already? Thinking you have repented, you hear the voice of the jewel and immediately become arrogant, you troublesome old hag Takahime,

Oho ho ho, what a fearsome, sore-losing, sharp-tongued old hag,

Ka ka ka ka, shall I bite and eat you? Nay, if I do not bite, swallow, and spit you out, you will not truly repent. No matter what is said, since you are the successor to the spirit of Tokoyohime, it is no wonder you are so tenacious.'

Takahime: 'Did you not say you would show me the wish-granting jewel? Even if you try to deceive me with that old hag's face, I shall neither be deceived nor frightened. Now, the curtain rises on Takahime's cave extermination. ...Hey, Tsunehiko, Yobu, why are you two trembling so timidly? In this situation where one must face a thousand, be a little more alert.'

Tsunehiko: 'Takahime-sama, what are you talking about all by yourself in this grassy field?'

'You are quite a foolish laborer, aren't you? Can you not see this magnificent cave with your own eyes?'

Yobu: 'Takahime-sama, a monster like a mole-band is glaring at us from the shade of the forest over there. Please, pull yourself together.'

'It is you who must pull yourself together, or we shall be in trouble. What is to become of us if you say such foolish things at a time of Senkiitsuki? For what purpose did I bring you all this way? I, Takahime, never imagined you would become such a burden at a time like this. ...Hey, you monster hag who calls yourself Takakohime, why do you not quickly bring out the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu? If you deceive the Hinodewake, I shall cast you into Hachimanjigoku and subject you to the punishment of Mizuzemehizeme. Do you know who the Savior of the Sanzensekai is?'

Takako: 'Ho, ho, ho, if you wish to see the jewel, search the small island near Onokorojima. The Mani treasure is hidden on Oshima and Meshima. Quickly meet with Takayorihime, fulfill your duty regarding the Mani jewel with distinction, repent thoroughly, serve Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime-sama from the bottom of your heart, and cease your ego and arrogance.'

'Be quiet. I do not need your opinions; I have them right here in my own heart, Takahime. Do not say such stupid things as that it is hidden on Oshima and Meshima. Everyone, as if they had conspired together, says with one voice that the Mani jewel is hidden on Oshima and Meshima. I am tired of hearing such old lines. Now, as promised, if you do not have the jewel, I will let you go, so you had better let me meet Takayorihime. If you keep babbling on, I, Takahime, will take action of Senkiitsuki.'

Takakohime, remaining in the form of an ugly old hag with white hair, said:

'Takahime-sama, please come this way. I shall let you meet the four persons: Takayorihime, Ryukokubake, Teristan, and Karinsu.'

She stood in the lead, walking around the right and left of the cave, and led them to the dim depths.

Takako: 'Now, Takahime-sama, all four of them are here. Please speak with them at your leisure.'

Looking inside, within a partition as transparent as glass, Takayorihime and the others were surrounded by rabbits and crocodiles, drinking fruit wine, singing, dancing, and playing happily. Upon seeing this, Takahime, not knowing that there was a glass partition, said:

'Hey, Takayorihime-sama, what is the meaning of this? At this time of Senkiitsuki, how can you be so carefree, entering the cave of such a demon, drinking wine with rabbits and crocodiles, and dancing wildly? I followed your trail, thinking I would help you in your distress, so I risked danger to come this far. If you are living so comfortably, ah, how ridiculous, I should not have come all this way.'

As she lunged forward, trying to drag out Takayorihime and Ryukokubake, she struck her face against the glass and cried out:

'Ouch, ouch, ouch! There is someone inside who is exactly the same as me, Takahime. Hey, you monster!'

As she raised her arm, the one on the other side raised its arm as well. When she widened her eyes, the one on the other side widened its eyes. Takahime had seen a mirror for the first time.

'Hey, hey, Tsunehiko, Yobu-sama, look at this! There is a monster that looks exactly like me. You must not let your guard down for even a second. It is manifesting my figure exactly like a mirage. It is a monster that cannot be trusted. Come here for a second.'

The two replied, 'Yes,' and drew close to Takahime's side. Once again, the same figures of Tsunehiko and Yobu were standing there. Takahime compared the two and let out another eccentric cry.

Three white foxes crawled out before her eyes, slinking away toward the forest at full speed, crying 'kon-kon kai-kai.' Takahime realized for the first time what was happening, and when she looked around, she understood that she was standing covered in mud in the Shikushiku Plain. From this, Takahime was suddenly enlightened, wiped away all attachment to the jewel from the depths of her heart, finally brought the evil spirits of the Shigure Forest to submission through Kototama, and happily set off on her journey home, heading toward Jitentō Island.
