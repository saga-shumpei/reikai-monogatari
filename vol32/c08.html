Chapter 8: 三人娘 [899]

三人娘〔899〕

§ 1

Takahime, seeing the beauty from before approaching leisurely with two attendants, gazed at them happily from about a hundred ken away, her face beaming with a joy as if she had eaten a rice cake in a dream. The somewhat foolish and comical nature of her style made Haruhiko feel as if he might burst out laughing, but he held it in, pointed at the women, and said,

Haruhiko: 'Look there... I thought there was only one, but three demonic women are coming this way, flapping their ears, are they not? Takahime-sama, can you really trust them?'

Takahime, having heard they were jewels, felt her heart cloud over once again.

Takahime: 'Now, Haruhiko-sama, you should not say such rude things. Look closely and see if those ears, which are so conspicuous, are actually moving or not. The reason they look like they are moving is because your own eyeballs are moving, making the ears on the other side appear to move.'

Haruhiko: 'If my eyes were moving, would not everyone's ears and bodies be moving along with them? You must be careful, open your eyes, and look, look, look. If you end up with your mouth hanging open in a daze and cannot close it, no matter how much you regret it later, it will be too late, and your precious high nose will be flattened. No matter how much I offer my opinion now, it will likely fall on deaf ears. Since you think everything I say is just nonsense, it is only natural that I get no response no matter what I say. However, do not let people point fingers at you later, saying that you did not listen to the advice of your thumb and little finger, and that you were made a fool of in the Forest of Shigure. You cannot trade your back for your belly. So that you do not end up biting your own navel later, clear your mind, steel your stomach, and think carefully. Even if you complain later, I will not listen; it is a case of eat-shit Kannon for you.'

Takahime: 'If I listen to you in silence, you crawl all over me like a flea or a louse; what kind of sophistry are you spouting? It is disgusting, I tell you.'

Haruhiko: 'Ah, my stomach is Haruhiko. It is not urine, but there seems to be someone somewhere who, despite not even being able to do a little bit, speaks so loftily and will soon be left in a daze. Ah, I wonder if that usual illness has struck again.'

Haruhiko: 'Quiet!'

Just as he stopped her with that command, the three women had already approached, and while bowing politely, they said,

Takakohime: 'Takahime-sama, we have kept you waiting for a long time. I am one called Takakohime, whose name is very similar to yours, and these attendants are called Otsuki and Oasa. Please keep us in mind and we hope for a long-lasting relationship.'

Haruhiko whispered in a low voice,

Haruhiko: 'There they are! Do not be deceived!'

He whispered this while keeping time with a beat. Takahime glared at Haruhiko, then suddenly softened her expression and turned to Takakohime,

Takahime: 'My, my, this is the first time I have heard your name. Oh, what a truly gentle and magnificent appearance you have!'

Takakohime: 'Oh no, no, why, to be praised so much makes me feel embarrassed. I am but a mountain cuckoo raised in the mountains, chirping in broken phrases, a naive girl who knows nothing, so please, I beg for your kind guidance.'

Haruhiko said from the side,

Haruhiko: 'No matter what you say, Takahime-sama has the experience of a thousand seas, a thousand mountains, and a thousand rivers, so you will be fine, ha-ha-ha-ha. The young lady who tries to trick a Takahime-sama who has passed through three thousand kalpas must be a terribly great, strange, and unfathomable person. Watch out, watch out, put spit on your eyebrows, take care of the hair on your backside, and shall we follow after Takahime-sama?'

Takahime narrowed her eyes, pursed her lips, and while spitting from her toothless mouth, said,

Takahime: 'Now, Haruhiko-sama, is it right to say such rude things to a lady so bluntly? My, Takakohime-sama, since such an animal is following me, I am truly troubled. Please, I beg you not to take offense.'

春彦小声で、

『それやつて来たぞ！　だまされな！』

と拍子をつけて、小声で囁いてゐる。高姫は春彦をグツと睨めつけながら、俄に顔色を和げ、高子姫に向つて、

『これはこれは、始めて御名を承はりました。マアマア実に御優しい御立派な御姿ですこと！』

春彦はそばより、

『何と言つても、海千山千河千の経験を経た高姫さまですから、大丈夫ですよ、アハヽヽヽ。其三千年の劫を経た高姫さまをチヨロまかす娘さまは、ドテライ偉い変つた変な見当の取れぬ仕方のない御方で御座いませう。オツトドツコイ眉毛に唾をつけ、おけつの毛に気をつけて、高姫さまの後から従いて参りませうかい』

高姫目を怒らし、口を尖らし、歯のぬけた口から唾を吐き出しながら、

『コレ春彦さま、さうズケズケと淑女に向つて、失礼な事を云ふことがありますかい。モウシ高子姫さま、斯様な動物がついて居りますので、寔に妾も頭を悩めます。どうぞお気を悪くせぬやうにして下さいませ』

Haruhiko: 'Yes, yes, this is truly Shitsureisenban. Please, Minaho to the Kangitsune, Norinaho to the Ōgitsune, and even if the Ōzora is Takakura, if you clear away the clouds that hide the Tsukihi, our souls will rise with the vigor of the morning sun... Hey, you there, Takahime-sama, watch out, do not be deceived. In the castle of Tokoyo, the Hatsupiyakuhachijifuyahashira of the Yatsuō Kami and the Yatsugashira, the noble Okata, are gathered, and the great failure of the Dorotanbo is already being examined. Hey, hey, Takakurainari-sama, Tsukihi, Asahimiyōjin-sama, even if you have transformed, this Haruhiko will not accept it. Are you not intimidated... The evidence that cannot be hidden is your two ears. A white Shitsupo is hanging down from your rear. To think you would come to such a deep Shinrin to deceive us... (Jiyōruri Monku) That is an unreasonable greed; are you trying to lure the heart of Takahime-sama, who is dazed by greed, by appearing as Takako-hime? To offer the wish-fulfilling jewel, the Kongōfukai, which is to say, if you speak of a jewel, you mean the eyeballs, to Takahime-sama who has the habit of rolling her eyes around—you have gone too far in playing the fool. This Haruhiko has the heavenly eye; one glare and I have examined you, and there is no mistake. Takakurainari-sama of the white fox, Asahimiyōjin of the Tsukihi-Asahi, show your Shitsupo quickly!'

Takahime: 'Hey, hey, are you saying something rude again? Takakurainari-sama and Asahimiyōjin of the Tsukihi-Asahi should not be here on this Takasagojima. They reside in Ōeyama in the land of Tokoyo, and since they are the ones who cross over to Jitentōtō, with the land of Ma as the boundary, you are mistaken to such a degree. Be silent!'

Haruhiko: 'In that case, shall I be silent and watch your performance... Yobu-sama, Tsune-sama, what do you think? Please let me hear your negative or positive opinion for a moment.'

Tsunehiko: 'There is neither negative nor positive. Do not go on and on.'

Yobu: 'No matter what you say, it is something I cannot accept.'

Takako: 'Please doubt whatever you wish. The facts will prove it more than anything else.'

Takahime: 'If that is the case, I shall accompany you... Hey, hey, Haruhiko, Tsunehiko, Yobu-sama, be quiet and follow me. You must not say anything. Even if the people talk on and on, they cannot understand the divine plan. You do not know how much wiser you look when you keep silent.'

Saying this with an irritated voice to admonish them, she followed behind the three, cooling the soles of her feet in the water-logged Shikishiku-hara,

'Ah, I feel good; I have reached the water for the first time in a while. This is all due to the divine plan of the Kami that lets no water leak, the divine virtue of the Mizu no Mitama. It is a waste to step on it with my feet, but I cannot go on without passing through here, so please, Kami, forgive me.'

She held her shoulders square, shook her hips with a wiggle, followed behind the three, and proceeded forward with high spirits.

Here, a very wide river was flowing with a roar, sending up spray. The three daughters tucked up their skirts and, aiming for the high stone heads like jumping rabbits, crossed to the other side in an instant. Takahime, not wanting to be left behind, tucked up her skirts as well,

'Hey, hey, everyone, be careful. These stones are very slippery.'

Just as she turned back, the weight of the stone Jizō she carried on her back caused her to slip of her own accord and fall into the torrent, floating and sinking, struggling with her feet in the air, and she was swept away as if shot by an arrow.

'This is a disaster!'

Haruhiko immediately stripped naked,

'Hey, Tsunehiko, Yobu, take my clothes!'

So saying, he plunged into the swirling waves, swam through the rapid current, caught up with the floating and sinking Takahime, and finally rescued her onto a rock. Fortunately, Takahime had not swallowed much water and had not lost consciousness.

Haruhiko: 'Takahime-sama, that was a dangerous affair. Is that not why I said it was suspicious and tried to stop you? From now on, you must listen to what we have to say.'

Takahime: 'A living Kami would not be weakened by getting stuck in a mountain stream like this, but nevertheless, the heart of Haruhiko, who was concerned as a Jinmin and came to help me, is truly beautiful. For the Kami rejoices in the sincerity of humans.'

Haruhiko: 'Takahime-sama, there may be times when even Haruhiko can be of service.'

Takahime: 'As the proverb says, even a broken stick becomes fertilizer if left in the field for three years, and even a rotten rope has its uses; you have received the true and genuine blessing of the Ofudesaki of the Kamisama, which says that such a person would perform such a feat. Come, offer your thanks to the Kamisama. You are a very fortunate person to be allowed to perform such an excellent task. From now on, you must listen to everything Takahime says without disobeying a single word. If you do not, Takahime will have to be the sacrifice in your place again, so please understand that. There is no reason for a young woman like that to slip on stones that one can cross without a second thought. This was entirely the Kamisama causing me to slip and you to jump in, to perform your purification in a Kannagara manner for the atonement of your sins, so you must never think that you helped Takahime.'

The three of them looked at each other’s faces at once, their eyes wide and their mouths pursed, dumbfounded by Takahime’s words.

Takakohime, Otsuki, and Oasa, the three daughters, stood on the opposite bank, extending their thin white hands and beckoning, saying, 'Come quickly.' Takahime, putting strength into the tips of her toes step by step and crouching low, crossed over to the opposite bank with a fearful look, and while heaving a deep sigh... Kannagara Tamachi Haese... she repeated it two or three times, and together with the party that had crossed over, she followed behind the three women, twitching her nose in triumph and following them with a joyful heart.
