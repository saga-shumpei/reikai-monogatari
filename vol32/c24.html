Chapter 24: 冷氷 [915]

冷氷〔915〕

§ 1

Takahime left the room of Kuniyoriwake, and seeking support to achieve this objective, she visited the room of Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake.

Takahime: 'Excuse me... Takayorihime-sama, Tatsukuniwake-sama, what a wonderful parent-child relationship you have; you are truly happy to have such a filial son. When I, a lonely person like myself, see you two living together in such harmony, I truly begin to feel envious. It is truly a beautiful thing to see a parent and child in such perfect harmony... By the way, I have come because there is something I must consult with you both about urgently. Please, allow me to intrude for a little while.'

Takayorihime: 'Oh, you have come at a good time. Since my son Tatsukuniwake is so filial to me, I think it is entirely thanks to the Kamisama, and I give thanks morning and evening. When he was young, he was quite a delinquent and made his parents cry many times, but with the medicine of age, he has become such a filial son. There is no parent who cries for not having a child, but there are many parents in the world who cry because they have children. Thanks to the Kamisama, I live my days with spirit because I have a child.'

Takahime: 'That is more wonderful than anything. However, you two, something has suddenly come up that I must have you listen to.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Takahime-sama, that must be about the happy news of Kuniyoriwake-sama. It is truly wonderful. I have always admired him as a slightly unusual and great man, but as they say, the sandalwood is fragrant from the sprout, and a snake swallows a person even when it is only an inch long; the true nature of one's spirit cannot be denied. I have been associating with him as a close friend, treating each other as equals, but from now on, I will have to change my attitude. Truly, one never knows how a person will succeed... Takahime-sama, you must be here for some sympathetic consultation on what kind of gift you should send to celebrate Kuniyoriwake-sama's marriage... right, Mother?'

Takayorihime: 'Since there is Takahime-sama, who sees through everything from ears to nose and eyes to mouth, there is no oversight in what she says. You should leave it to Takahime-sama's opinion.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Yes.'

Takahime: 'Now, Tatsukuniwake-sama, just as your mother said, you must follow my opinion in everything.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Yes, I will follow. What should I celebrate with?'

Takahime: 'Oh, how tiresome, are you talking about celebrations? The rise and fall of the nation is at a critical moment, and there is a great upheaval of natural disasters; why are you so carefree and composed? Just think about it carefully; if a womanizer who ruins houses, a house-destroyer like Kuniyoriwake, were to become a husband to the youngest princess of the pure Crystal Yamato-damashii, it would be like mixing sand into white rice. It simply cannot be allowed to happen. I have come to consult with you because it is a great matter for the nation, and we must find a way to cool down this marriage proposal.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'A consultation to cool it down? It is so hot that there is no sudden way to do so. After all, he is inflamed to over a hundred degrees. However, if we do not cool it, there may be a risk of rot. It is a trouble for you, but please run to the Ryumon Ice House and buy about a hundred kan of ice.'

Takahime: 'Now, Tatsukuni-dono, this is not a matter of ice. Because you are only thinking about ice in this heat, everything I say sounds like ice to you.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'If you do not break it into small pieces and explain it in detail, I cannot melt the ice.'

Takahime: 'Now, now, you, even though I am speaking so earnestly, are you trying to cool me down from the start?'

§ 2

Takahime-sama, did you not just say that it is cold? That is why I am following your opinion and am in the middle of mocking it. Shall I offer you a Kototama of cold ridicule and abuse? I have been hanging it down into the well since this morning...

Eh, what a man who does not understand the meaning. Who is talking about a watermelon!

Now, now, Tatsukuniwake... it is a waste, you must not talk back to Takahime-sama with such complaints... Now Takahime-sama, young people are truly, even when heard from the side, saying things that make one feel chilly, as if one were standing beside ice. Please listen with composure.

Takahime-sama, have you forgotten the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases? Is there such an inhumane thing as exposing the flaws of the past or conducting an opposition movement just when Kuniyoriwake-sama is on the verge of success? I am absolutely excused from such consultations.

Even for me, it is truly painful to expose the flaws of Kuniyoriwake, and it feels as if I am swallowing red-hot iron, but because I cannot replace the many Jinmin with one person, I am a person of karma who must say unpleasant things against my will... Now Takayorihime-sama, please fathom the heart of Takahime.

That is quite reasonable. However, according to the wishes of the Lord God Susanoo-Ōkami, and what Kotoiyorawake-kami-sama and Matsuwakahiko-no-kamitsukasa have decided, we have neither the right nor the occasion to say anything about it. Since whatever happens must be the arrangement of the Gods, I believe there is no other way for us but to simply offer our congratulations.

Eh, both parent and child, you are both people who do not understand. At this rate, no matter how much the lineage of the transformed male... oops, wait, I should not have said that... no matter how much Takahime becomes a shachihoko and strives, can this muddy stream not be dammed? Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese. Please, God, quickly distinguish between good and evil, and I pray earnestly that the muddy soul of Kuniyoriwake does not defile the magnificent soul of the precious child of the Lord God Susanoo-Ōkami, and that You protect and bless him in the protection of the night and the protection of the day.

Takahime-sama, now that it has come to this, it is useless no matter how much you writhe in impatience. I too have been made to hear too many bad things about Kuniyoriwake-sama, and my stomach has become Tatsukuniwake. Come, come, please make this place Tatsukuniwake as soon as possible. From here, I must go to Kuniyoriwake-sama's place to offer my congratulations... Mother, shall we be rude to Takahime-sama, and go together as parent and child to the pre-celebration of this auspicious marriage?

Do as you please, whatever happens. I will be careful for a moment so that you do not regret it later.

Far from regretting it later, this marriage talk has already been made public in advance. Hahaha.
