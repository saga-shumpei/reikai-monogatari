Chapter 11: 人の裘 [902]

人の裘〔902〕

§ 1

The great forest extending along the southern bank of the Amazon River was a place where wild beasts and poisonous snakes openly exercised their violent power, yet for that very reason, one did not need to exert too much effort for its conquest. By merely manifesting a superficial divine power, one could cause lions, wolves, and other wild beasts to submit.

Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake became the rulers of the Capital of Rabbits and remained there for a time. However, lions, bears, tigers, wolves, great snakes, vultures, jackals, and other beasts frequently gathered in groups to surround and attack the Capital of Rabbits, and just as they were struggling greatly with its defense, a mysterious divine light from the summit of Bōshigatake shot and illuminated the heads of the wild beasts in their moment of critical peril and survival. Eventually, even the formidable wild beasts and great snakes humbled themselves, dispatched eagle messengers to Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake to beg for submission, and the southern forest of Shigure-no-mori came entirely under the jurisdiction of Queen Takayorihime.

In contrast, the northern forest was filled with beasts that were treacherous and prone to shape-shifting into demons and monsters, making their actions impossible to fathom. A party of four, led by Takahime—who was prone to relapsing into attachments and whose heart was easily swayed—set out to assist Takayorihime. However, Onibuhiko of Ōeyama foresaw that the northern forest could not be traversed in one or two attempts. He planned to send the kin of the white foxes Takakura, Tsukihi, and Asahi to first remove Takahime's attachments from their roots, force her to humble herself, and ensure she completed the exploration of the forest as a perfect and flawless servant of God. Yet, when Takahime heard of the jewel, the clouds of attachment suddenly covered the sky of her heart; how pathetic it is to encounter such a divine trial.

Takahime is left dumbfounded in the reeds of the muddy field, exposing a face that looks as if she has just awakened from a dream. Tsunehiko and Job stand silently, looking as if their souls had been extracted by a soft-shelled turtle, their faces drained of all expression, staring at Takahime's body without blinking from head to toe.

Haruhiko had regained his freedom of movement before anyone noticed.

Haruhiko: 'Takahime-sama, how was what I said? Was I wrong?'

Takahime: 'It is not that you are entirely wrong, but if you ask me if you are wrong, well, it is somewhat like that. If you do not repent a little, even I will have to suffer such an experience.'

Haruhiko: 'Hahaha, how thoroughly strong you are! It is getting to the point where you will not be able to show your face to the world. It is written completely in the teachings of the San-go-kyō that if one boasts that there is no one like oneself and becomes arrogant, one will have their eyebrows plucked, their tail hairs pulled out until not a single one remains, and be left dumbfounded; and once it comes to that, no matter how much you rely on God, your prayers will not be heard... and that you will slink away and hide your form.'

Takahime: 'Now, now, Haruhiko, to whom are you saying that? Those are things only those who know them well understand. Takahime is fully aware of such things a hundred or a thousand times over, so please do not say anything more. Ugh, rather than staying by the side of such a man and being teased, I shall set out to some tree and engage in deep contemplation.'

Saying this, she hikes up her skirts with all her might and dashes deep into the forest.

Tsunehiko, not wanting to lose sight of Takahime, also hikes up his skirts and follows after her.

Haruhiko and Job, having lost sight of the two, spoke.

Haruhiko: 'We shall meet somewhere again. We must quickly search for and rescue Takayorihime's party and return to Jitentōtō Island.'

Haruhiko took the lead and deliberately walked in the opposite direction to where Takahime had run. After pushing through the forest for about an hour, heading toward the northwest, he found a stone Jizō about three shaku tall, covered in pitch-black moss, with its features so worn that its eyes, nose, and mouth were barely distinguishable. It stood lonely in a horizontal line, with its ears chipped, hands broken, and half of its head smashed.

『アツハヽヽヽ、口計り立派な事を云つて居つても、まさかの時になつたら尻込みを致す、誠のない代物計りだなア。それでは三五教も駄目だよ』

『喧しう云ふな。春彦の精神が石地蔵のお化けに分つて堪らうかい。俺は高姫さまの様に有言不実行ではないのだ。不言実行だ。どんな事をやるか見て居つて呉れい。モールバンドであらうがエルバンドであらうが、誠と云ふ一つの武器で言向け和し、見ン事二人の生命を助けて見ようぞ。サア、ヨブさま、春彦に従いてお出でなさい』

とあわてて、高姫の走つた方へ行かうとする。石地蔵は、

『アツハヽヽヽ、たうとう俺の言に励まされて、直日の霊に省みよつたなア。人に言うて貰うてからの改心は駄目だよ。心の底から発根と改心した誠でないと役には立たぬぞよ。今にアフンと致して腮が外れるやうな事がない様に気をつけたがよいぞよ。石地蔵が気を付けておくぞよ。此方はアキグヒの艮の神、それに、良き獣の使はし女を沢山抱へて居る狼又アークマ大明神と云ふ立派な御方だ。ドレ、是から石地蔵に化けて居つても本当の活動は出来ない。うしろから、お前の腕前を、実地見分と出かけよう。口と心と行ひの揃ふやうな誠を見せて貰はうかい』

『エヽ喧しい、化州、俺の御手際を見てから、何なと吐け。サア、ヨブさま行かう』

と尻ひつからげ、以前の谷川を兎の如くポイポイポイと身軽く打渡り、転けつ輾びつ、

『オーイオイ、高姫さまはどこぢやアどこぢやア、モールバンドのお宿はどこぢや、春彦さまの御見舞だ、俺がこれ程ヨブのに、何故春彦ともヨブさまとも返答をせぬのか。高姫、お前は聾になつたのか。オーイ、オイ』

と声を限りに叫び乍ら、ドンドンドンと地響きさせつつ、草原を無性矢鱈に大木の茂みを指して走り行く。
