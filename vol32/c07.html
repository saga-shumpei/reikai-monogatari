Chapter 7: 試金 Jewel [898]

試金玉〔898〕

§ 1

The Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu, the Murasakiiro Hōgiyoku, and the Kogane tama were lost, and Takahime, in her agitation, grew angry at Takayorihime, Kurohime, and the bystanders, venting her fury upon them all. Seeking the whereabouts of the jewels, she crossed the vast oceans, yet though she searched everywhere, not even a shadow could be found, and she wandered upon the waves. Returning once more to the sacred land, she and Kurohime examined the Mani Hotsushiyu in the Yahiro palace, but this too was contrary to her expectations, and she puffed out her cheeks and foamed at the mouth. Convinced that the Kyōshiyu-slave of Kotoyoriwake had stolen and hidden the three treasures and the Mani tama, and had abandoned the sacred land to cross over to the island of Takasago, her mind became so obsessed that her Shineniba grew wild.

Accompanied by Haruhiko and Tsunehiko, she crossed over to Takasagojima. Setting out from the island nation of Teru, she stopped at the Mirror Pond, trespassed into the Kakyō Palace, and rattled off nothing but insolent talk. Having received a warning from the Sumeru Ōkami, she leaped out of the mansion, crossed over Mount Arina, and reached the great plains of Argentina. There, she encountered Hinodehime, who was an incarnation of Konohana-hime, and finally repenting, she crossed the wilderness and rivers, traveling day and night to reach the port of Aru. From there, she entrusted herself to a ship, landed at the port of Zemu, crossed over Mount Tenshō, and arrived at the port of Chin.

Determined to save the party of Takayorihime, she whipped her horse of sincerity and pressed onward. When she came to the Amazon River, the semi-turbid waters were flowing with great force, a wide river beyond the reach of the eye. As the waves surged, the Mōrubando raised its strange head, raging as if it were the master of all. The usually composed Takahime was astonished, but she reached the north bank of the Amazon River with her life barely intact. Offering the Amatsu Norito and singing the Sendenka, she advanced deep into the forest. The story of the distant Age of the Gods is now finally being told here.

Ah, Utsushimi-kami-kami, may the spirits be blessed.

Takahime, together with Tsunehiko, Haruhiko, and Yobu, finally arrived safely at the northern forest of the Shigure Forest, deep within the great Amazon jungle. This is a boundless, vast forest, nearly three hundred ri from east to west and four hundred ri from north to south.

Deeply believing that the party of Takayorihime was lost in this forest, and wishing to rescue them as soon as possible, Takahime pushed through the dense trees, turning right and left, parting the grass, and crawling through bamboo thickets that nearly swallowed her whole. Occasionally threatened by wild beasts and chased by venomous snakes, she managed to emerge into a slightly wider, sparsely wooded wilderness.

As it was the greatest forest in the world, nameless giant trees roared and raged, containing the wind, and strange, beautiful fruits were ripening here and there. The party, while bathing in the scorching sunlight in this sparsely wooded field, indulged in sunbathing with a sense of curiosity.

At this moment, a woman of indescribable beauty appeared from ahead, advancing toward the party of Takahime with a leisurely air, which caused them to wonder. The four looked at each other, and thinking it might be the appearance of a specter or a demon, they tightened their sashes, steeled their nerves, and waited for the woman to approach.

The woman came before Takahime with a gentle voice, bowed, and with a slight blush on her face, said:

Speaker: 'Excuse me, may I ask, are you not the Senden-shi of the San-go-kyō, the jewel-seeking party of Takahime-sama? If you are in need of jewels, the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu, the Murasakiiro tama, the Kogane tama, and the Mani Hotsushiyu have been stored in my mansion in great numbers since the distant Age of the Gods as treasures for the fulfillment of divine government. If you would be so kind as to examine them and accept them, I would consider it a truly grateful happiness.'

Takahime, who had once let go of her attachment to the jewels, heard these words and, as if her chronic illness had relapsed, rounded her eyes and tensed her face strangely, saying:

Speaker: 'What are you saying? I have understood that there is only one Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu in the world, but do you truly possess so many at your home? Is that not, for the most part, a fake jewel? If one speaks of the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu, there should only be one in the world...'

With eyes wide in Hanshinhangi, and filled with the Yashin to obtain this jewel and return, she asked back with an Isoiso heart. The Bijin Uchiwara and said,

Takahime: 'Ho ho ho ho, Takahime-sama, are you doubting what I say? Proof is better than argument; if you come to my house, you will understand. To think that there is only Itsuko of the Niyoi Hotsushiyu is, if I may say so with Shitsurei, like the proverb of the frog in the well that knows not the Taikai. Come to my house for a Chiyotsuto and see for yourself.'

Takahime: 'What a strange thing you say. And besides, is it not a Gatsuten that you live in this Shinrin where no human has ever come before? Are you not the Keshin of some Nanigamisama? This is not a Bashiyo where a Ningen should live.'

Bijin: 'Ho ho ho ho, where is the Dauri that a Ningen cannot live in this Shinrin? In fact, is not the party of Takayorihime-sama, the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō, living here?'

Takahime: 'Ah, that Takayorihime; my primary purpose is to search for the whereabouts of the party of Ryūkokuwake. I have already abandoned the idea of the jewel. However, if that precious jewel is at your house and you tell me to receive it, I shall not refuse. In that case, I will go. Please guide me to your house.'

Bijin: 'Please wait a Chiyotsuto. Since my dwelling is just over there, it would be Shitsurei if you came suddenly, as it is quite untidy. Please rest here leisurely. I will come to welcome you in the meantime. Also, if you are searching for the whereabouts of the party of Takayorihime-sama, I know of it, so I will guide you slowly. Even if you search this vast forest for five or ten years without a destination, there is no Dauri that you would find them...'

Takahime: 'Your words are kind in every way; the things that the gods do are truly flawless... Meeting you is also a divine arrangement by the gods.

Though you set out in the faint light, do not feel lonely,

For there is one prepared to be your strength.

It is written in the Ofude. Not even a hair's breadth of the gods' words can be wrong. Since it is said that if this were wrong, the gods would not exist in this world, there is nothing more secure than this. Oho ho ho.'

Bijin: 'I must excuse myself for a while. I shall return to welcome you later...'

Saying this, she disappeared into the surrounding Shinrin as if vanishing with quick steps.

Takahime watched her go with a smiling face,

Takahime: 'Hey, Tsune-sama, Haru-sama, Yobu-sama, the ones we call gods must have tremendous power, must they not? You would never have dreamed that we would meet such a beautiful woman in the midst of this vast Shinrin, stretching endlessly to the east, west, south, and north. Even I never thought such a thing would happen. Therefore, there is nothing as excellent as Kaishin. When I was in Jitentōtō, I was obsessed with a single jewel of Kongōfukai, thinking that without this treasure, the Shinsei-jōju could not be achieved, and that if I, Takahime, did not use it, the Shinsei-jōju would be a failure, and I racked my brains in many ways; but the gods are grateful. To think that after making me suffer enough hardship and difficulty, they would unexpectedly grant me the Niyoi Hotsushiyu in a place like this—how grateful I am! That is why they say to polish your soul and perform Kaishin. Hey, Tsune-sama, Haru-sama, Yobu-sama, you will receive excellent divine virtue and be able to perform a service that will leave your names for ten thousand generations. This is also entirely thanks to the Hinodewake... oops, wait, I was not supposed to say Hinodewake... God, I spoke carelessly, please forgive me... It is only because you followed me, Takahime, that you can perform such a service. No matter what I say, the lineage of the Hensei-danshi... oops, wait, I was not supposed to say this either, but I let it slip out of sheer joy. Ho ho ho ho.'

ヨブ『違ふか違はぬか、そんなこと如何して分りませう』

§ 4

Job: 'I am confessing honestly, as I have said. I cannot claim to understand what is fukakai, yet I cannot say that I do not understand it at all, can I? I have given my answer standing in the middle between hitei and kōtei. If I have offended you, I pray you, matsupira gomen.'

Takahime: 'Eh, what a hopeless goninsoku you are... Now, now, Tsunehiko, you have a face that looks quite rikau. Your thoughts should not be machiga; what do you think?'

Tsunehiko: 'No matter what anyone says, I believe what Takahime-sama says is the truth. However, unless what that girl said holds true in jitsuchi, I, being a gudon person, cannot give a certain gohentafu.'

Takahime: 'Well, well, what a gathering of people who cannot understand. If there were but one person in the sekai who could discern this matter, things would be ritsupa jiyōjiyu, but because their mitama are so clouded, the God truly finds it difficult... so the Ōkami-sama has said. When I think of it, when I think of it, the heart of the Ōkami-sama is dear to me... That aside, why does that girl-sama not come out sooner? Perhaps she needs time to clean such a large room?'

Haruhiko: 'If you were to look with your divine eye, Takahime-sama, you should be able to see what that girl is doing.'

Takahime: 'At this time of senki-ikki, when we are at the point of deciding whether or not the treasure of the Shinsei-jiyōjiyu can be obtained, do not speak such petty logic. That is why it is shown that a large crowd is not needed, and that if many are present, interference will occur and things will not be jiyōjiyu.'

Haruhiko: 'In that case, shall I take my leave and return home now?'

Takahime: 'The essential goyō can be fulfilled if there is one person, so do as you please.'

Job: 'According to your words just now, you say that one person is sufficient; in that case, shall I also take my leave with Haruhiko-san? I would not want to be a nuisance.'

Takahime: 'Hey, you are impatient; what are you saying? I am not telling you to go home, Job-sama. Even regarding Haru-sama, I did not say that he is a nuisance. I only told you the words of God for your reference. In short, I only spoke of the divine indication that the true path is better served by one person than by many, so it troubles me if you take it the wrong way now. In this case of senki-ikki, no matter how much you desire the Nyoi-hōju, one person can hold no more than about three. And if there are four people, can we not obtain three times four, twelve jewels? Ah, if it comes to this, I should have brought even more garakuta-ningen with me; the world does not go as one wishes.'

Haruhiko: 'Ahahaha, how eloquent you are. In any case, I shall offer my apologies after witnessing your skill.'

Tsunehiko pouted his lips and said, 'Hey, Haruhiko, what kind of rudeness are you speaking to the lineage of the Henjō-danshi? This is the ikimiya of the Hinodewake-no-kami.'

Haruhiko: 'Yes, I am humbled. However, I beg you all to ensure there is no impropriety.'

Takahime: 'Ohohohoho, what deep suspicion there is.'

Just as they were speaking, the girl from before, adorned in beautiful attire and followed by two attendants, approached them leisurely.
