§ 1

Following behind the three maidens, Takahime walks with a swaying gait as if she has something in mind, following along with a jaunty air as if Harukoma were spirited. Tsunehiko and Yobu follow with a gait that suggests they have nothing better to do, feeling their way step by step through the thick, overgrown grass.

Haruhiko, while serving as their attendant, feels something inexplicably amusing from the bottom of his heart, and sings:

Deceived by the demonic woman / Who appeared in the forest of Shigure / The greedy Kumatakahime-sama / Has had a recurrence of her chronic illness / 'Since I have hidden countless / Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu / In my dwelling, come out and see / If there is one that pleases you / I shall give you as many as you wish' / Being told such sweet tales / As if rice cakes were growing on thorns / Her heart is in the clouds / Enveloped by the clouds of Gayoku / One inch ahead is pitch darkness / She does not know the sun is rising / Nor does the form of Tsukihi catch her eye / Takahime of Takakura-gura / Having been thoroughly swindled / Will surely be left crying again / Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara

Why does Takahime's heart / Waver to such an extent? / With such enthusiasm, just like that / She stands spiritedly, crying 'A jewel, a jewel!' / And is led into a fox's den / 'Hey, hey, listen, Takahime-sama / This is the Niyoi Hotsushiyu' / The Tamatebako she revealed / When opened, oh, what is this? / Thinking it was the Niyoi Hotsushiyu / It is a tanuki's Kintama, eight mats wide / 'Oh, I am shocked, I am shocked' / 'This is unbearable,' she says, tucking up her skirts / And dashes off at full speed / To fall with a splash / Into a bottomless valley stream / Blowing bubbles as much as the foam on the water / She will surely be left stunned / Seeing that, Haruhiko / Cannot help but feel pity for her.

Though I gave her so much advice / Takahime, whose soul was completely stolen / Once she set her heart on the jewel / While spitting foam from the corners of her mouth, says / 'The divine plan is not something / For the people to interfere with / The divine plan is known to the gods / People like you / Must not stick your oars / Into the divine plan' / Telling me to shut up and stay away / She proceeds, trying to grasp a jewel / That does not even exist / Like a monkey trying to catch / The reflection of the moon in the water / Drowning in the water, trembling / Blowing bubbles and lashing out at everyone / Though it is not the wind / That blows fiercely on the two hundred and tenth day / We think of the trouble for the buckwheat.

We who are watching from the side / Do not think the millet / That Takahime-sama blows is good / Without knowing of the fox's seven transformations, the tanuki, and the bean-tanuki / And the eight transformations, she gains the jewel / And on top of that, in the dark clouds / Of her miserable joy at being told / The whereabouts of Takayohime / Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over / Whether the moon waxes or wanes / Even if the earth should sink / Is the jewel of Niyoi Hotsushiyu / Really on Takasagojima? / The great leader of Kototama, / Hatsuwakahime and Tamanohime / Have hidden it secretly / On a remote island / In the souls of the two / So that no one knows its location / Though she says so, in her self-deception

That it is hidden on some island / That much is certain, Mokusuke of Nishiki-no-miya / Was telling me / It is poor Takahime who is to be pitied / Please, give it up to a reasonable extent / And cast away your attachment to the jewel / Because you are attached to the jewel / And cloud your heart / You must suffer such hardships / Whether you are the living shrine of the Hinode god / Or a soul of the lineage, I do not know / But I have lost my patience / Just when I think you have repented, again / You return to your arrogance / Repeating the cycle of repentance and arrogance / Even if you are a god, I feel pity for you / It is a great task to polish / Such a soul back to its original state / If I were a god / I would have abandoned it long ago / You are truly, truly patient / The noble will of the gods / I am truly moved / The three who are going ahead / Including Takakura Inari / And the deities of Tsukihi and Asahi / Without knowing that the gods have revealed their forms / And are testing Takahime-sama's repentance / She follows them, which is pathetic / Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara / May the spirits be blessed / And may Takahime-sama's attachment / Be cleared as soon as possible / I am also truly troubled / If I am to accompany such a person / I will never be able to return to the island of self-deception / The party of Takayohime...

On the southern bank of the Amazon River, / Served by the King of Rabbits, / Guarding the sacred Reichi, / Surely waiting for the arrival of Takahime-sama. / Please grant her Kaishin quickly, / Not for the sake of Takahime alone, / But for Tsunehiko, Haruhiko, and Job, / I pray to Kami with all my heart. / Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, / May the Mitama be blessed.'

Singing this, he follows behind, Iyaiya.

Takakohime parts the Kusamichi, and in this Shinrin, a Daiganseki of about Jitsuchiyōshimen stands like a Yōkuwan, and on the surface of the rock, Shirakoke grows in Tokoromandara. In the Kubodo of the rock, Jiyumoku, though small, are aged, and grow here and there like Ueki with Omoshiro Edaburi. Below that, a square hole about Ichijiyō high and Hatsushiyaku wide is carved, and the entrance is closed with a sturdy rock door. Takakohime turns to Takahime,

Takakohime: 'This is my dwelling. Please, everyone, enter without hesitation. I shall serve you tea, so please rest at your leisure. If you would also grant an audience to Takayorihime-sama, I would be truly grateful.'

Takahime is stunned by the magnificent entrance of the rock cave and, biting her tongue in surprise, says,

Takahime: 'My, my, what an unexpectedly magnificent residence. When was this rock cave built? I never dreamed that such a splendid mansion would stand within such a Shinrin.'

Takakohime: 'Well, in this area, the infestation of giant snakes and evil beasts is severe, and at night, I could never sleep peacefully unless I were in a place like this, so I took advantage of the natural rock mountain and dug it out; it has only just been finished recently. To think that you are the first guest to enter, I am truly honored.'

Haruhiko: 'Hey, Takahime-sama, if you do not stay sharp, you will be made to suffer a fate from which you cannot escape. You must never enter. Even though this looks like a magnificent rock cave, it is actually the mud field of Shikushiku-hara. Will you not be a little more careful?'

Takakohime: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Haru! Are you not acting strange? You must be possessed by Magatsuhi, thinking only of obstructing our excellent work.'

Haruhiko: 'Ouch, ouch, ouch, my legs have suddenly become stiff. It seems I am starting to turn into a fossil. Hey, Takahime-sama, please perform Chinkon. I cannot bear to become a stone statue in a place like this.'

Takahime: 'See there. Because you do nothing but obstruct the divine work, divine punishment has struck you instantly, and you have been frozen just like that. Well, stay there for a while and serve as a gatekeeper. This Takahime, along with Tsunehiko and Job, will be guided to the inner chamber to view the magnificent jewel, and depending on the circumstances, I intend to receive it, so until then, it would be best for you to stand guard there. If you interfere again, you will offend the master of the house, and it would be troublesome if we could not even view the jewel we have gone to such lengths to see... Ah, what a clever person the one called Kami is. Actually, I thought it would be terrible to bring you into this mansion, so Kami has conveniently arranged things. As it is written in the Ofudesaki, if you become arrogant, you will not be able to move even an inch. Please have a little Kaishin. Well then, Haru-sama, thank you for your trouble...'

Saying this, she leaves him behind and, led by the hand by Takakohime, attempts to slip into the rock cave. Haruhiko cries out at the top of his lungs,

Haruhiko: 'Takahime-sama, stay sharp, you are mistaken. Hey, Tsunehiko, Job, both of you, listen to what I say and stop Takahime-sama. You will have to face a terrible ordeal. Even though I look like this, my Tennantsū is working...'

Takahime: 'Hey, noisy one, you cannot even move your body, yet you claim to have Tennantsū... Come, Tsunehiko, Job, let us go.'

Led by the hands of the three women, she advances deep inside. Behind, Haruhiko watches the backs of the three in a daze,

Haruhiko: 'Well, what a bunch of blind fools. Takahime-sama is also completely hopeless at this rate.'
