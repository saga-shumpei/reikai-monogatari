Chapter 14: Mountain 上の祝 [905]

山上の祝〔905〕

§ 1

In the days of Kamiyo, in Takama, the Moon Kami of the divine Mitama appeared as the Miroku Kami. In the Himitsukiyō of Takasagojima, which drifts in the Ōunabara, and in the land of Brazil, where the great Daishinrin stands tall to the north and south of the famous Amazon River, the noble Seichi was formed in the center of the vast Kuōbōsenri. The Mitama of the Moon descended from heaven, and this Seichi was bestowed upon the tribe of rabbits, designated as their Chiyo Sumika. As they bowed down with all their hearts morning and evening to worship the Tsukiōkami, and while they were reverently serving, at the time of the Tokoyokuwaigi, when the decree for Bubitetsukuwai was established, the beasts whose wings had been torn off—

Leaving the land of Tokoyo, crossed over to the land of Brazil, and raided this forest. They oppressed and slaughtered the righteous tribe of rabbits to use as prey. With the Baugiyaku increasing day by day, when the righteous rabbits were at the point of Kubukurin, falling into the fangs of the enemy and on the verge of their race becoming extinct, leaving the Seichi of Aya, the San-go Kami-tsukasa Takayorihime and the party of Ryūkokuwake appeared. Riding in a boat of Menashikatama, they ascended the Amazon River, where the great torrents were flooding and raging violently, and reached the southern shore.

Upon arriving here, the party was welcomed by the King of the Rabbits. Having finally reached the Seichi where the Moon Kami was enshrined, the tigers, wolves, lions, bears, great serpents, vultures, and all other birds, beasts, insects, and fish, through the Kototama of the divine grace, were brought to submission and harmony. Now, the Shigure-no-mori is in the midst of enjoying the spring of the heavenly kingdom. Following behind Takayorihime, the San-go Kami-tsukasa Takahime, Tsunehiko, and Haruhiko, who had come searching from afar, submitted to the divine breath. Here, the twelve finally stood out upon the Amazon River.

When they proclaimed the noble Kototama bestowed by the Tianjin-ōkami, the Maulband, the Elband, and all other monsters were completely subdued by the divine grace.

Purifying their spirits, they raised clouds and danced up to the heavens, becoming messengers of the noble Kami. It is truly noble that they serve by regulating the wind and rain. Teristan, Karlins, and Ryūkokuwake, together with the Kami-tsukasa of the pure heart, Akiyama-wake, and with the pure-hearted Yasuhiko and the calm-hearted Munehiko, followed the guidance of the one who firmly guards the teachings, and proceeded to the Byōbu-yama. Relying upon the spiritual light of the Bōshi-ga-take, which towers high in the sky of the endless mountain range, as their staff and strength, and embraced by the divine grace, they crossed mountains and rivers, traversed valleys, and climbed the steep slopes.

Here, the twelve living Mitama settled upon Bōshi-ga-take. Having reached the two who had been giving light and assistance to the divine army of Shigure-no-mori, the Great Teacher Kotoyoriwake and the Kami-tsukasa Kuniyoriwake, they proceeded toward the capital of the Uzu-no-kuni while expressing gratitude for the vast and boundless divine grace. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. May the Mitama be blessed.

The party of twelve brought the demons of the Amazon River to submission and harmony, clearly admonished each of them regarding the destination of their souls, and opened the path of salvation. Illuminated by the spiritual light of the Ryū and the Kyū, they marched with high spirits, singing the propaganda song while trekking through mountains, rivers, and valleys. Finally, they stopped at Bōshi-ga-take, performed various divine strategies, and returned to the presence of the Great Teacher Kotoyoriwake-no-mikoto and the Kami-tsukasa Kuniyoriwake-no-mikoto, who were engaged in supporting the divine army. Celebrating each other's safety, praising their success, and shedding tears of gratitude, they opened their hearts to one another. Rejoicing with courage, they enshrined the divine spirit of the Kunitama-kami on the summit of Bōshi-ga-take, offered prayers of gratitude, and celebrated their triumphant return. They gathered the fruits of the surrounding trees, praised their delicious taste, and thus the great banquet on the mountain was held.

However, the squad of Masazumihiko, Karl, Ishiguma, and Harukō, who had headed toward the forest north of Shigure-no-mori, had no news, and even after waiting for one day, and then two days, there was no sign of them returning.

Here, the Great Teacher Kotoyoriwake-no-mikoto prayed deeply to the Kunitama-kami, and all performed Kagura and sang the Kototama song, earnestly praying with the twelve Mitama united that the party of Masazumihiko would return safely to this place. The four members of the party searched high and low through the great forest for the whereabouts of the party of Takahime, but the great forest of several hundred ri, known by reputation, was not easily discovered. Sinking into the abyss of near despair, the four members were drawn by the divine will to the side of the Headless Stone Jizō, where Haruhiko and Job had previously rested for a while. From the Stone Jizō, they were explained in detail that Takahime, Takayorihime, and the other ten had brought the demons of the Amazon River to submission and harmony, and were now on their way back in triumph toward Bōshi-ga-take. Rejoicing with courage, they headed for Bōshi-ga-take, and finally reached the summit at dusk, three days late. Celebrating the safety of Kotoyoriwake-no-mikoto and the others, the party now became eighteen in number. They descended the mountain range of Byōbu-ga-take with great liveliness, crossed the long wilderness, climbed over the Brazil Pass, and while exposing their whole bodies to the scorching sun, they finally made their triumphant return to the mansion of Suekohime in the capital of Uzu.

聖地にやうやう辿りつき 虎狼や獅子に熊 大蛇禿鷲其外の 禽獣虫魚に至る迄 神の恵の言霊に 言向け和し今は早 時雨の森は天国の 春を楽む真最中 鷹依姫の後を追ひ はるばる探ね来りたる 三五教の神司 高姫、常彦、春彦が 神の伊吹に服従ひて 茲にいよいよ十二人 アマゾン河に立出でて 天津御神の賜ひてし 貴の言霊宣りつれば モールバンドやエルバンド 其他の怪獣悉く 神の恵に悦服し

霊を清め天上に 雲を起して舞ひ上り 尊き神の御使と なりて風雨の調節に 仕へ奉るぞ尊けれ テーリスタンやカーリンス 竜国別を始めとし 心の空も安彦や 胸凪ぎ渡る宗彦が 清き心の秋山別の 神の司と諸共に 教を固くまモリスの 案内につれて屏風山 果てしも知らぬ山脈の 空に秀でていと高き 帽子ケ岳の霊光を 杖や力と頼みつつ 神の恵に抱かれて 山河渡り谷を越え 嶮しき坂をよぢ登り

ここに十二の生身魂 帽子ケ岳にをさまりて 時雨の森の神軍に 光を与へ助けたる 言依別の大教主 国依別の神司 二人が前に辿りつき 宏大無辺の神恩を 感謝しながらウヅの国 都を指して進み行く あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ。

十二人の一行はアマゾン河の魔神を言向け和し、各自に霊魂の行末を明かに諭し、且つ救ひの道を開き、琉と球との霊光に照らされ、意気揚々として宣伝歌を歌ひながら、山川渓谷を跋渉し、やうやくにして、帽子ケ岳に止り、種々の神策を行ひ、神軍応援に従事しゐたる教主言依別命、国依別命の前に帰り来り互に其無事を祝し、成功をほめ、感謝の涙を流しつつ互に打解け、喜び勇んで帽子ケ岳の頂上に、国魂神の神霊を祀り、感謝の祝詞を奏上し、凱旋の祝を兼ね、あたりの木の実を採収し来りて各其美味をほめ、ここに山上の大宴会は開かれにける。

然るに、時雨の森の北の森林に向ひたる正純彦、カール、石熊、春公の一隊は何の消息もなく、一日待てども二日待てども、帰り来るべき様子さへなかりける。

ここに言依別命が国魂神を厚く念じ、一同神楽を奏し、言霊歌をうたひて、正純彦一行が、無事此処に帰り来るべき事を十二の身魂を合せて、熱心に祈願をこめつつありぬ。

一行四人は大森林を右に左に駆巡り、高姫一行の在処を捜し求むれども、音に聞えし数百里の大森林、容易に発見すべくもあらず、殆ど絶望の淵に沈み、一行四人は双手を組んで、以前春彦、ヨブが暫し休息したる頭欠け石地蔵の傍に惟神的に引寄せられ、石地蔵より、高姫、鷹依姫以下十人、アマゾン河の魔神を言向け和し、今や帽子ケ岳に向つて凱旋の途中なることを詳細に解き諭され、喜び勇んで、帽子ケ岳さして、三日遅れた夕暮に漸く山上に辿りつき、言依別命以下の無事を祝し、ここに一行十八人となり、賑々しく屏風ケ岳の山脈を降りて長き原野をわたり、ブラジル峠を乗越え、暑熱の太陽に全身をさらしながら、漸くにしてウヅの都の末子姫が館に凱旋する事となりたり。
