Chapter 15: 万歳楽 [906]

万歳楽〔906〕

§ 1

Matsuyohime of the Miroku Kamiyo, Takenohime of the upright heart, and Umegakahime who opens all at once, appeared as the noble Mikoto and Onchichi, and Momogamihiko, the Kamutsukasa who opened the Kamuyakata in the Takasago Uzu capital and guided the Yobito, together with Satsuki-hime, received the divine Mikoto and set out for the Yomotsu islands; the Masakayamazumikami, whose father-child and fūfu bonds established such batsugun merit that their glory shone in yomo, entrusted the Uzu mansion to Kunihiko, the Oshihe no Kamutsukasa, and traveled far across the Unabara, now once again residing in the noble sacred land of Jerusalem.

He opened the Oshihe of the San-go-kyo completely and in detail. Matsuwakahiko, born as the eldest son of the pure-hearted Kunihiko, revering his father's words, remained in the Uzu mansion and was transmitting the true path of the Sumeru-Ōkami to yomo, when he accompanied Sutehime, the youngest daughter of the eight maidens who was born as the noble child of Kami-Susanoo-Ōkami, and crossing the eight hundred paths of the tide, they descended shining brightly. The Oshihe of Kami flourished far and near, and the people of the yomo countries, moved by the divine grace and the noble governance of Sutehime, submitted with joy, rejoicing and delighting in one another, as they established the Matsu Kamiyo.

At that very time, the great Oshihe-nushi of the San-go Kami-no-Oshihe, the Kamutsukasa Kotoyoriwake, departed from the Nishiki-no-Miya and, observing the divine imperial decree of the noble Mikoto of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, arrived here. Starting with Sutehime, Matsuwakahiko and the other Kamutsukasa rejoiced, revering the Oshihe-nushi Kotoyoriwake as a living Kami, and just as the flowers of the Oshihe were blooming and flourishing in all directions, the Kamutsukasa who had set out to guide and harmonize the Kami in the forests of the Amazon River, beginning with Kuniyoriwake, Takayorihime, and Takahime, sought to assist in the Kototama battle.

The Kamutsukasa Masazumihiko, leading four attendants—Karl, Ishikuma, and Haruko—traversed mountains and rivers day and night, crossed several ranges, and headed toward Mount Bōshi, which stands towering, pressing against the clouds in the center of the great mountain range, to meet with Kuniyoriwake. They emitted the precious spiritual light of the jewels of Ryu and Kyu, and from Mount Bōshi, they shone upon and shot at the divine army fighting against not only the fierce beasts and great serpents lurking in the Shigure forest, but also the Mallband, Ellband, and numerous other Magatsukami, harmonizing them through Kototama and achieving a great victory, standing even above the clouds in the nobility of that Kototama.

Takahime and her party, along with many other Kamutsukasa, surged with courage and, aiming for Mount Bōshi, finally arrived here. Gazing at the scenery where the Amazon River flows like a belt, they recited the Futo-norito-goto of Kototama, and together with the Kami-people of Ryu and Kyu, Kotoyoriwake and Kuniyoriwake, and sixteen other Kamutsukasa, they returned to the Uzu mansion in the great city of Argentina, protected by Sutehime, playing songs of triumph with ease; the bravery of their appearance was such that Sutehime, Matsuwakahiko, Sutehime, and many other Kamutsukasa...

Accompanied by many people of the Uzu capital, they went out to welcome them, celebrating their safe return and ushering them into the Uzu mansion. Such an example of the opening of Argentina is truly noble. Kuninotokotachi-Ōkami, who created and solidified this world, Toyokunihime-Ōmikami, Kaneyasutome-Ōmikami, Hinodewake, and Konohana-no-Kami-no-Mikoto also surged with courage, rejoicing that the Miroku Kamiyo was opening before their very eyes, and sent one hundred righteous Kamutsukasa to this sacred land to rejoice and celebrate the triumphant return of the divine army; the voices of acclamation echoed through heaven and earth, and in Takasago...

It was as if the very island would shake. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. May the spirits be blessed, and may the great activities of Kotoyoriwake and Kuniyoriwake on Takasago Island, and the story of the noble and pure Kami-Susanoo-Ōkami appearing here from afar to perform the Shikum of the Kamiyo, be rolled out perfectly, in detail, and minutely, without delay, by the Kototama-guruma. Kannagara. Before the presence of the Kami, Mizuki prays with reverence and awe.

Sutehime, celebrating the triumphant return of the party of Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto, spreads a golden fan and dances while singing herself.

Sutehime: 'The world is long-lasting and ever more noble; departing from the high Takama-no-hara, to illuminate the people of Takasago Island with the noble light of the Kami of infinite benevolence, the Kamutsukasa Kotoyoriwake, together with Kuniyoriwake, boarded the Menashi-katama ship and crossed the sea route to the port of Asahi-mo-teru. Between the borders of Hiru and Teru, in the valleys of the three mountains, while saving the people suffering from near-death with the noble divine Oshihe, both in spirit and body, they parted from Kuniyoriwake and traveled here and there, saving many people, in the wilderness where tigers, wolves, bears, and lions rage and go mad.'

§ 2

Having crossed over and gradually arriving at the capital of Uzu, there were times when the teachings of the Kami, Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, were expounded in detail. The Mizumitama that cleanses this world, by the Mikoto of the Chichiōkami, borrowed the mouth of the Suteko-hime to proclaim the Futonorito. Reverently receiving this, the Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto departed from the Kamuyakata, and with Masazumihiko, the Oshihetsukasa, as the leader, accompanied by the four attendants Karl, Ishikuma, and Harukō, they proceeded to the great forests spreading north and south of the Amazon River, subduing and harmonizing the raging wild beasts, the Maulband, and the Elband.

Revering the noble Miizu of the Mizu-no-Mitama, the Moon Kami enshrined in the capital of Uto, from the summit of Mount Bōshi, the spiritual lights of Ryū and Kyū shine upon each other, governing the hundred spirits eternally. Oh, the joy of returning here now, raising a song of triumph! Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may the Mitama be blessed, and may the capital of Uzu be governed eternally like the evergreen Tokiwa-no-Matsuyohime, the pure and upright Takenohime, and Umegakahime, all at once opening their Miizu, appearing as the great Ōkamuzumi of the peach fruit, and just as they achieved great merit in the battle of Yomi, beginning with the youngest Hime,

May the great achievements of the noble Mikoto, such as Kotoyoriwake and Kuniyoriwake, shine clearly like the sun and moon, eternally with heaven and earth. O Amatsukami and Kunitsukami, the eight million Kami, and Ryūyohime, the Kunitama-no-Kami, before the presence of Gesshōhiko, the youngest Hime humbly prays and beseeches that the three-five teachings of the Kami may never wither nor scatter, but like the flowers and fruits of the Tokijiku-no-Kagu-no-Konomi, may they flourish in fragrance forever. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, before the presence of the Sumerōkami, I pray with a pure Kototama.

Having finished singing, they bowed to one another and advanced into the inner sanctuary where the Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami is enshrined.
