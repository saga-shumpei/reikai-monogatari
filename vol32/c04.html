Chapter 4: 鰐の Kotodama [895]

鰐の言霊〔895〕

§ 1

The head of the crocodile appeared before the four, and with a look of great joy, bowed its head to express its gratitude and sang:

'If one looks up, the grace of the God of Heaven and Earth is before one's eyes. Rowing a boat upon the Heavenly River, the Divine Child who descended to the mouth of the Amazon River, the crocodile clan rejoices, and offering up our red hearts, we have appeared and come here to celebrate and serve. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. The precious grace of God has descended upon us. Our restless souls, like the blooming of flowers, flourish as if they are fragrant. O Takayorihime-sama, O Tatsukuniwake-sama, you have descended from afar to this place, and the Rabbit-kimi, whom we serve as our King, has settled here.

Is it the grace of the God of Heaven and Earth that you have appeared in this sacred ground? In joy beyond compare, even the grasses and trees of the Aogakiyama that surround this lake seem to have revived, looking beautiful and vibrant. We are the Usagizoku, the noble children of the Moon Ōkami. We revere and respect you, and morning and evening, we exhaust our hearts and bodies to defend and protect against the disasters brought by the Maulband, Elband, tigers, wolves, lions, and bears throughout the long years. Yet, although we receive the divine water and fire of God, the sins of the past do not fade away. Truly, this is a miserable situation, no matter how much we exhaust our hearts.

Even the act of illuminating the Kototama of the divine water and fire is a sorrow, drifting like white waves, and there is no reward for our days and months. We make this lakeside our dwelling, and we only barely protect this sacred ground of ten square ri. Yet, while we dwell by this lakeside, no matter how fierce the beasts may be, they shall not easily violate it. Ah, but alas, alas. The spirits born as fierce beasts and poisonous snakes are pitiful; they are children of God, yet they are not. Repeating sins through the survival of the fittest, and day by day, month by month, continuing the law of the jungle, they barely maintain their lives—how pitiful. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara.

Blessed by the divine spirit, may the living things under heaven love and help one another, without strife or ferocity. May they receive the grace of God equally, polish their souls, cast off their lowly shells, and may their souls return once more to the presence of God in Heaven. May You make them into Gods who can freely use the Kototama, which is filled with authority, as the leaders of all things. Representing the entire crocodile clan before the Divine Minister Takayorihime-sama and Tatsukuniwake-sama, may the light of the grace of the purely polished Kototama save all living things of Heaven and Earth. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara.

Before the Divine Minister, we manifest the fullness of our sincerity, and with reverence and respect, we humbly pray.'

Having finished singing, the crocodile shook its long, massive body, which was encased in a sturdy shell, from side to side to express its satisfaction, and together with many other crocodiles, it danced to and fro, showing its welcome. Tatsukuniwake stood up and, facing the lovely group of rabbits and crocodiles, sang with great joy:

'The Divine Minister, born having inherited the water and fire of Heaven and Earth, the missionary of the Anabana-kyo, having borrowed the body of Takayorihime and built a palace of flesh, the Divine Minister Tatsukuniwake is now here. Before the palace of the Moon Ōkami, which shines across the vast sky, while worshipping the noble divine virtue, I proclaim the majestic Kototama to the companions of rabbits and crocodiles, and declare the extent of my heart. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara.

Leaving behind the brocade palace that towers in the Aya-no-Takama, which is known as the majestic sacred ground having received the grace of God, and seeking the location of the golden jewel, I crossed to the island of Takasago. While my heart was restless and I was making ugly schemes, I was awakened by the voice of God once more, and I became the original soul of beauty. The grace of God is without limit; not to mention the common people, even to the birds, beasts, fish, insects, and the very leaves of the grasses and trees, the dew of grace is bestowed. Realizing the impartial and selfless heart of God, we who have appeared here now are perhaps those who, without knowing it, have been manipulated by the threads of the Great God's plan and have come forward. The four of us, who have been ordained as the true messengers of God, are now becoming your parents.'

と唄ひ終り、堅牢なる甲を以て包まれたる長大なる身体を左右に揺りながら、満足の意を表し、数多の鰐と共に前後左右に舞ひ踊り、歓迎の意を示したり。竜国別は立上り、兎と鰐の愛らしき群に向つて、さも嬉しげに歌をうたふ。

§ 2

Becoming brothers, the Ametsuchi's Kami's Mimegumi, spreading thoroughly to the ends of the Shigure forest, and as a Rakuen where the Mimegumi rain pours down, shall be protected in Kakihatokiha. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. O tribe of rabbits and crocodiles, born of the divine Mitama of Kami, you must never lament. Now that we four Shinjin have appeared here, not to mention Molband, any fierce Kedamono shall be pacified by the Kototama born of the divine Miiki of Kami's Mimegumi, and we shall make this a forest of Heiwa forever, without enemies or strife. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara.

O you, the flock born of the divine Miiki of Kami, starting with us, even if the Ametsuchi should change, do not forget the noble and deep Mimegumi for even a single Hitohi; prostrate yourselves before the Mimahe of Kami morning and evening, and praise His Shintoku. Kami is with us; Kami protects your bodies. How could any Magamono, even if it be any kind, fear the Ikimono under the Ame that are opened by the Mimegumi of Kami? Purify your hearts, cleanse your bodies, and devote your Magokoro to the will of Kami, all of you. Ryokokubake now proclaims and transmits this to your flock. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. May your Mitama be blessed.

Telistan sings an interesting song to add excitement.

Following the teachings of Takayorihime and Ryokokubake, together with Karlins, we advanced through the wilderness of a thousand ri, parting the grass, and arrived safely at the port of Alu. While we were crossing the great ocean by ship, suddenly a fierce storm arose, the waves raged, and Takayorihime fell headlong into the sea with a splash, and her figure was seen no more. Ryokokubake, deep in filial piety, forgetting his own self, leaped into the sea and lost his form. Tel and Kar, the two, were astonished, and thinking that the hundredth year had come and it was no longer possible, they resolved to commit junshi, and when they jumped in at the count of one, two, three,

While being swallowed by the white waves, thinking that we were exploring the Dragon Palace sea, it was a miscalculation; we were carried on the back of a turtle and drifted to the port of Zemu. Facing Mount Tensho, we met Molband, and by the divine power of my Kototama, 'Ottodotsukoi'—no, that was wrong—by the Kototama of Takayorihime, we pacified the rough men, saved the lives of the two, crossed the valley of Mount Tensho, and were sent by a cool breeze to the wilderness of unknown ends, arriving safely at the port of Chin. We built a ship and proceeded toward the mouth of the Amazon.

Molband and Elband, heard of in rumors, here and there, while raising their strange heads, looked at the faces of our party. I do not know what they feared, but they hid their figures in the rapids of the river where the water force was strong. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. Did they fear the divine power of the teaching master and flee, having received the Mimegumi of Kami? Be that as it may, when we climbed up the bank of the Amazon river and came into this forest, how joyful it was to see the King of Rabbits, aged through countless thousands of years, leading many of his kin, coming to welcome our party with courtesy. That such a holy sacred ground should exist in the center of a forest of a thousand ri,

I could not have realized even in a dream, but who could have expected it? The Moon Kami, shining in the sky that transmits the Ame, has enshrined the palace of mere form in this sacred ground, surrounded by clear water. How elegant it is that the tribe of the King of Rabbits offers their Magokoro and prays morning and evening.

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. For humans, who are born as the palace of Kami, is it not even more necessary to protect the way of Kami purely, to be grateful for the deep Mimegumi of the Ametsuchi, and to fulfill the heart of the Sumer-Ōkami? Now that we have appeared here as the three-five divine officials, even though they are called beasts, if one seeks their origin,

Are they not all the same children of Kami? How could we not save them? O flock of rabbits and flock of crocodiles, you must never trouble your hearts. Now that the party of Takayorihime has appeared, any disaster of evil Kami shall be swept away and purified like dew vanishing in the morning sun, and we shall open and protect this as a flower garden moistened by the dew of the Mimegumi of Kami, and the roots of calamity shall be severed. Kamunagara. Rejoice in the divine majesty of Kami and protect this sacred ground well. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. May your Mitama be blessed.

テーリスタンは面白き歌をうたつて興を添ふ。

とうたひ終り、社の前に端坐し、神恩を感謝し、且つ一時も早く此森林の災を除き、再び綾の聖地に帰し給へと祈願なしける。惟神霊幸倍坐世。
