§ 2

Suekohime: 'Born as the youngest of the Yatariotome, manipulated by the threads of Shigumi, I crossed over to Takasagojima. Since hearing that you are the savior of Geniunikai at the Uzu-kan in Argentina, and that you are opening the way of Kami, I departed from the mansion of Iso. Riding the bird-like Ihakusufune, I have gradually arrived here to see. Seeing the Kamutsukasa of Kotoyoriwake, Kuniyoriwake, Takahime, Takayorihime, and Tatsukuniwake standing in line, and seeing how you bring the remnants of Yamataorochi hiding in the Amazon River and all the fierce beasts to submit and return to my Ananahi Ōji, my heart is stirred with courage at the sight of such bravery. I have appeared here to offer words of Kamuho to you, who are the righteous children of Kami.'

Suekohime: 'Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Guard well the Ōji of Kami, and serve purely in the divine government of the Miroku age of Kami, becoming the pillars of heaven and earth. Kami is with you. Man is the child of Kami and the shrine of Kami. Do not lose your way in small desires and defile the palace of treasures. May the sky of your heart be clear and serene, and may the sun and moon of Shinnyo shine brilliantly forever, illuminating the darkness of the lower world; establish your heavenly mission as children of Kami firmly and eternally. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. I swear to Kami and proclaim it; I swear to Kami and teach it. Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, no matter what troubles you may encounter, do not defile or lose the sincerity you have received from Kami. This is the Kototama of the path of Makoto that I, Susanoo, teach you with all my heart. It is the divine word that saves the world. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. May your spirits be blessed.'

Having finished singing, she sits in the formal position.
