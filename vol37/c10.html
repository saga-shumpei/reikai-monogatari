Having been tricked squarely into grasping cow dung on the western slope of Ashinoyamatauge, I felt utterly absurd and unbearable, turning slightly desperate, and for two or three days I slept in the morning and slept in the evening, suspending the chanting of Amatsunorito and the spiritual training of Chinkonkishin.

When I did so, on the evening of the third day, once again that familiar swirling from the Saikatanden soared up to my throat,

There stood a manure pit used for storing fertilizer to be applied to the fields, and a strange stench pierced his nostrils.

A lump from within his abdomen swirled and again pressed hard against his throat,

Matsuoka: 'Hey, physical body, strip completely naked, crawl into this manure pit, and perform the washing of your mitama!'

Kiraku: 'Hey, Matsuoka-sama, if I crawl into a place like this, won't I become even dirtier?

Giving this command while manipulating Kiraku's physical body freely as he pleased, he crossed the Iōdani swiftly, followed the edge of the large pond, guided him to the inner valley of the Tatsuta Shrine in Kameoka which was the tutelary shrine of his birthplace, and ordered him suigiyō. And he strictly commanded his physical body to visit this waterfall every night for the space of one week. From then on, Kiraku came to visit the Yada Waterfall every single night, crossing lonely mountain paths, the edges of ponds, and graveyards.

On the seventh day since he began visiting the Yada Waterfall, when it came time for this evening to be the completion of the practice, a feeling of terror somehow welled up in the depths of his heart. Slinging the large spear that had been hanging in the inner room, he set out from his home around the twelfth hour, and when he had advanced as far as the outskirts of the Anō village, the spear that he was holding began to move of its own accord by the momentum of his heart, and a ring-ring humming sound issued from it. Although he could not see clearly because it was night, the tip of the spear could not help feeling as though it were naturally bending and raising its neck like a snake. Having thought he was holding a black, aged spear, before he knew it, he came to feel as though he were holding a thick snake, so he threw it down abruptly into the midst of the barley field and started to advance toward the direction of Kurakiyo. After throwing away this spear, his feeling of terror grew considerably lighter.

As he advanced gradually and came close to the large pond of Iōdani, it looked to him as if a man with a tall back thought to be some two or three jo, of a proportionate thickness, and with a red, round face—said to be situated within a mountain basin with a circumference of as much as one ri—was dipping up to his waist in the deep pond water and walking with a splash-splash sound facing toward his own direction. His hair stood on end, and his heart beat with heightened palpitations. Immersed in single-minded devotion, reciting 'Imakami Kamunagara Tamachihau Mase', he ran eastward and still eastward along the edge of the pond. What became of this monster, he did not know. Ahead of him, a blue fire appeared in a place where it was not usually lit. Unable to advance and unable to retreat, when he stood on the way for a while to ponder, his body began to shiver with an eerie dread; he grew more and more frightened, and could not help feeling as though he were being attacked by disgusting monsters from every direction. Ah, he thought, how good it would be if Matsuoka-san would possess me at a time like this,

Matsuokatengu-sama, Matsuoka-sama,

Well, for a while I must sit quietly here and make a fair judgment... so thinking, he sat down upon the turf by the side of the road, straightened his posture, and crossed his hands. 夜はホノボノと明けて来た。そこらの様子が何となく昼らしくなつたので俄に元気を出し、細谷川を伝うて、一週間歩き馴れた谷路を登つて行く。併し実際は夜が明けてゐるのではなかつたと見え、再びそこらが薄暗くなつて来た。空を包んでゐた雲がうすらぎ、東の空から月が昇つたのが薄雲を通して光つたからであつた。二三町許り行つた所に、五十五六の骨と皮とになつた、痩た可なり背の高い婆アが、一方の手を前に出したり後へ引いたり、切りに樵夫が前挽をひくやうな事をやつてゐる。……ハテ怪体な奴が出やがつた。夜が明けたと思へば暗くなつて来る。そこへ川に臨んで婆アが妙な手つきをして体を揺つて居る。此奴ア、ヒヨツとしたら稲荷山の峰つづきだから、奴狐がだましてゐるのかも知れぬ。心よわくては駄目だ……と俄に空元気を出し、婆アの近くによつて、一生懸命の声で、

『コラツ！』

と呶鳴つて見た。婆アは此声に驚いて、折角発動してゐた手をピタリと止め、腰を屈めて、

婆『ハーイ、どなたか知りませぬが、何か御無礼な事を致しましたかな。妾は樽幸の稲荷さまに信心して居りまして、御台さまから神うつりの伝授を受け、今日で三年許り毎晩此処へ修業に来て居ります。おかげで右の手丈此通り御手うつりが出来出しました。モウ三年すれば又左の手に御手うつりがあり、それから胴うつり、頭にうつり、御口が切れるのが、マアマアザツと之から十年の修業で御座います。お前さまは此頃評判の高い、穴太の天狗さまぢや御座いませぬか』

喜楽『お婆サン、そんな年寄りがこれから十年も修行して居つたら、口の切れるのと死ぬのと一時になるぢやないか。モツと早う口の切れるやうにして上げようか。私が修業さしたら、一週間にはキツと口を切つて上げる』

婆『ハヽヽヽヽさうかが易く神様が憑つたり、口が切れるやうな事なら、此婆もこんな永い修行は致しませぬワイナ。早う口の切れるやうな神は碌なものぢやありませぬ。どうで狐か狸でせう』

§ 4

While Jibun was possessed by a Mamedanuki, the absurdity of running down Kori all the while was just like a nightsoil carrier who did not know the stench of his own dung, thought Jibun; and as Jibun tried to slip away from this spot, the old woman again moved her right hand as a woodcutter handles a saw, shook her hips with a rhythmic creak-creak, thrust her stationary hand straight forward, and—

Old Woman: 'Hey you, Tengu-dono of Anao, anyhow I am asking a favor of you, but first I will consult the Inari-sama of Tarukau before I rely on you. The other day, though the Odai of Nishimachi was a disciple of the Inari-sama of Tarukau, when she switched her allegiance over to the Inari-sama of Amarube, she died as a punishment for it. There was a funeral yesterday. Since it is terrifying to displease the Kami, I will ask a favor of you after prudently consulting the Inari-sama of Tarukau.'

Kiraku: 'The Inari-sama of Tarukau is bound to object flatly. This side is a Tengu, and that side is a black fox, you see.'

Old Woman: 'Now, now, what a sacrilegious thing you are saying. That Kami is Shiyōichii Tenko Mitsurugidaimiyōjin-sama. The supreme guardian deity of the entire mountain, who protects the First Peak. It is sacrilegious to dismiss him as merely a black fox and reduce him to a mere tanuki; retribution will strike you. If one were to receive favors from such a personage, there is no knowing what kind of things might happen. From this very moment, you too, please forget about me, and I will forget about you too. For if the strange cords of karma become tangled, it brings mutual trouble. Rokkon shōjō, rokkon shōjō, Namu Myōhō Renge Kyō......'

Thus she began to chant with all her might. When Kiraku abandoned this place and went about two cho upstream to the eastward-facing waterfall, whereas the waterfall was usually not very thick and fell only about one jo, today, by some quirk of affairs, when viewed from five or six ken this side, a pure white figure was standing there. Peering through the hazy moonlight as he drew near to the front of the waterfall basin, a woman of twenty-five or six, wearing white robes with her hair disheveled, was standing under the waterfall. Recognizing her as being possessed by a spirit, Kiraku—

Kiraku: 'What Kami might you be? Please let me hear your name.'

He addressed her thus. The white-robed woman standing under the waterfall kept her hands clasped together, raised them high above her head, stretched her back, arched herself slightly backward, and—

Woman: 'Rikimatsu Daimiyōjin......'

She shouted in a shrill voice.

Kiraku: 'Where is the guardian deity whose name is Rikimatsu Daimiyōjin?'

Woman: 'I am Rikimatsu Daimiyōjin, a divine retainer of Okumura Daimiyōjin on Inariyama. If you have faith in this side, I will grant you escape from all illness and calamity. You must be the Tengu of Anao. Hearing that today is the completion of your one week of ascetic training, this side lured out Oshiharu of this physical body and waited in order to let her meet you. You must have been quite frightened along the way, hadn't you?'

Kiraku: 'I understand, please be so good as to withdraw.'

Woman: 'Even if you did not tell me to withdraw, since this Rikimatsu Daimiyōjin knows your mind well, I am withdrawing. Un, un......'

Having said just that much, the spiritual possession known as Oshiharu of Hatago-machi in Kameoka returned to her senses completely.

Meanwhile, the night cleared up brightly. The two of them talked about various matters concerning the Kami, and at Oshiharu's request, they made a detour to Hatago-machi, met her husband named Fudekichi, promised to help one another for the sake of the Way, and returned to Anao.
