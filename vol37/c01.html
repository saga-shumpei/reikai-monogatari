富士山〔1013〕

As seen above, our Mt. Fuji has through the ages been praised by various poets for its lofty grandeur, surpassing all others in the Japanese land, hailed as the most renowned mountain in Japan, becoming the sacred ground where the myriad deities of heavenly and earthly gods gather, and is revered especially as the august divine spirit of Konohanahimenumikoto. It has been praised as the Mt. Fuji of the three countries, becoming the foremost renowned mountain among the three countries of Japan, China, and India. Nevertheless, that so-called Mt. Fuji has a tremendous difference in both height and breadth compared to the Mt. Fuji of over tens of thousands of years ago. The present Mt. Fuji is merely what remains of the Takachiho peak as referred to in the Imperial Classics. When it was formerly called Mt. Tenkyo and also Mt. Tenkyo, its skirts drooped long to the west in modern Shiga and Fukui prefectures, to the north in Toyama and Niigata prefectures, to the east in Tochigi, Ibaraki, and Chiba prefectures, and to the south, its foothills extended straight away one hundred and forty or fifty ri southward from the various prefectures of Kanagawa, Shizuoka, Aichi, and Mie. Due to a great earthquake, the southern parts collapsed and now form a part of the Pacific Ocean.

以上の如く我富士山は古来各種の歌人に依つて其崇高雄大にして、日本国土に冠絶し、日本一の名高山と称され、天神地祇八百万の神の集り玉ふ聖場となり、特に木花咲耶姫命の御神霊と崇敬されて居る。三国一の富士の山と称へ、日本、唐土、天竺の三ケ国に於ける第一位の名山となつて居た。併し乍ら其富士山と云ふは、十数万年以前の富士山とは其高さに於て、又広さに於ても、非常な相違がある。現在の富士山は皇典に所謂高千穂の峰が僅に残つてゐるのである。昔天教山と云ひ、又天橋山と云つた頃は、西は現代の滋賀県、福井県に長く其裾を垂れ、北は富山県、新潟県、東は栃木、茨城、千葉、南は神奈川、静岡、愛知、三重の諸県より、ズツと南方百四五十里も裾野が曳いて居た。大地震の為に南方は陥落し、今や太平洋の一部となつて居る。

§ 2

This point was called Takaamahara, and the Shinjin who dwelt in that land were called the Takaamahara race or the Tenson race. The present Fujisan remains from the eighth station upward of the ancient Fuji zone. The Fuji zone, with a circumference of almost thirteen hundred ri, was collectively called Aokigahara and was the largest plateau in the world, blessed with the regular succession of Gofūjifū, where the Gofūjifū was abundant, fruits ripened, and it was truly called the paradise of the world. Because of this, there were no evils of the Seizonkiyōsō, and they enjoyed the divine grace as the chosen people of Kami.

Lake Biwa in Ōmi was formed from its fissure when the Fuji zone collapsed. And the ancient Fujisan zone maintained a height like that of the summit of modern Fuji around the third and fourth stations, with clouds surrounding it. Therefore, the Tenson race lived in peace in the zone above the fourth station. Viewed from other countries, they lived almost above the clouds. The ancient saying that the Imperial Grandchild Ninigi no Mikoto parted the eightfold clouds of heaven in a thousand ways at Itsuno-chiwake and descended to the Central Land of Reed Plains means precisely that he descended from this highest Fuji zone in the world to the countries of the lowlands. It goes without saying that he did not descend from the world of the sun or the world of Venus.

When the divine jewel extension of Utsukuni was built and the bridges of salvation made of gold, silver, and copper were spanned, the highest golden bridge stood equal in height to the summit of Fujisan. Also, the Himalaya Mountains are said today to be the highest mountains in the world, but in those days, called Chikyōzan or Ginkiyōzan, compared to the height of ancient Fuji, they did not even reach one-half. Although modern Fujisan is thirteen thousand feet high, ancient Fuji had a height of sixty thousand feet. The so-called Mount Sumeru of the Buddhists also referred to this Chikyōzan.

The likes of modern Shimizu Bay and a part of Enshū-nada were large lakes spread widely across the eighth station of Fujisan, and were called the lake of Chikushi. Also, the lake of Suwa in Shinano in the same Fuji zone was called the lake of Susano. In the lake of Chikushi, numerous golden dragons dwelt, serving the Ōkami and protecting wind, rain, thunder, and lightning. Also, in the lake of Jewel, numerous white dragons dwelt, protecting the climate of the Luxuriant Land of Reed Plains (the entire world) so that it would be favorable. And the divine spirit of Susanoo no Mikoto protected this, occupying a position around the second station of the Fuji zone. Due to the great earthquake of antiquity, this zone subsided more at the center point, while the surroundings maintained a relatively smaller degree of subsidence. Therefore, except for the present Fuji, which is the peak of Takachiho, the degree of sea level came to maintain almost an average, unlike modern times. Modern Yamashiro, Tanba, and the like, if anything, rose slightly due to the influence of the earth's inclination.

Tanba is originally written as field of rice, and it is the place where Amaterasu Ōmikami created the rice seeds that the Aohitogusa should eat and live by. Therefore, Toyoukehime no Kami, who is said to protect the Gofoku, was enshrined at Manai in Hinuma, Tanba Village, Tanba County, Tanba Province, and in the reign of Emperor Yūryaku, was transferred to Yamada in Ise Province. When selecting the Yuki-da and Suki-da at the enthronement ceremony, it is based on the divine age destiny where the region west of present-day Lake Biwa produced the Gofoku. The Kototama called Yuki is the spirit-reversal of Azukuni, and the Kototama called Suki is the spirit-reversal of Shiroshimesu Kuni. Seeing this, one can understand that there is a deep destiny in the province of Tanba from the divine age.

Moreover, Armenia and the Caucasus Mountains in Asia Minor, Jerusalem, Mesopotamia and Persia, and a part of India protruded high above the clouds like the Fuji zone. India too was called Tenjiku and was the highest point in that region, but simultaneously with the collapse of Fujisan, this region also collapsed as it is now. The word Armenia means the meaning of heaven or the meaning of Takaamahara. The meaning of Jerusalem in the divine age has the meaning of the capital of Uzu, the heavenly paradise. Here Kuninotokotachi no Mikoto first appeared, and when surrounded by the enemy forces of Ooyashimahiko no Mikoto, Kuninotokotachi no Mikoto exerted divine power from above the lotus pedestal, causing the Tenpōzan where the demons were based to collapse and simultaneously manifesting Chikyōzan, exactly as stated in Volume One of Reikai Monogatari. Also, Jerusalem is not the modern Jerusalem. It was Eruserumu, corresponding to the south of Armenia. And it goes without saying that the Jordan River is also different from the modern Jordan River. The location of the Dead Sea was the present Persian Gulf flowing down, sandwiching the east and west of Mesopotamia.

原文（近代文語）

此地点を高天原と称され、其土地に住める神人を、高天原人種又は天孫民族と称へられた。現在の富士山は古来の富士地帯の八合目以上が残つて居るのである。周囲殆ど一千三百里の富士地帯は青木ケ原と総称し、世界最大の高地であつて、五風十雨の順序よく、五穀豊穣し、果実稔り、真に世界の楽土と称へられて居た。其為め、生存競争の弊害もなく、神の選民として天与の恩恵を楽みつつあつたのである。

近江の琵琶湖は富士地帯の陥落せし時、其亀裂より生じたものである。そして古代の富士山地帯は殆ど三合目四辺に現代の富士の頂上の如き高さを保ち雲が取巻いて居た。故に天孫民族は四合目以上の地帯に安住して居た。外の国々より見れば、殆ど雲を隔てて其上に住居して居たのである。皇孫瓊々杵命が天の八重雲を伊都の千別に千別て葦原の中津国に天降り玉ひきといふ古言は、即ち此世界最高の富士地帯より、低地の国々へ降つて来られた事を云ふのである。決して太陽の世界とか、金星の世界から御降りになつたのでない事は勿論である。

顕国の御玉延長して金銀銅の救の橋の架けられし時も、最高の金橋は富士山上に高さを等しうしてゐた。又ヒマラヤ山は今日では世界最高の山と謂はれてゐるが、其時代は地教山と言ひ又銀橋山とも云つて、古代の富士の高さに比ぶれば、二分の一にも及ばなかつたのである。現代の富士山は一万三千尺なれ共、古代の富士は六万尺も高さがあつたのである。仏者の所謂須弥仙山も此天教山を指したものである。

現代の清水湾及遠州灘の一部の如きは、富士山の八合目に広く展開せる大湖水であつて、筑紫の湖と称へられてゐた。又同じ富士山地帯の信州諏訪の湖は須佐の湖と云つたのである。筑紫の湖には金竜数多棲息して、大神に仕へ、風雨雷電を守護してゐた。又玉の湖には白竜数多棲息して、葦原の瑞穂国（全世界）の気候を順調ならしむべく守護してゐたのである。そして素盞嗚尊の神霊がこれを保護し玉ひ、富士地帯の二合目あたりに位地を占めてゐた。太古の大地震に依つて、此地帯は中心点程多く陥没し、周囲は比較的陥没の度が少かつた。其為現代の如く、高千穂の峰たる現富士を除く外、海抜の程度が殆ど平均を保つ事になつたのである。現代の山城、丹波などは、どちらかと云へば地球の傾斜の影響に依つて少しく上つた位である。

丹波は元田場と書き、天照大御神が青人草の食いて活くべき稲種を作り玉うた所である。故に五穀を守ると云ふ豊受姫神は、丹波国丹波郡丹波村比沼の真名井に鎮座ましまし、雄略天皇の御代に至りて、伊勢国山田に御遷宮になつたのである。御即位式の時、由紀田、主基田をお選みになるのも、現今の琵琶湖以西が五穀を作られた神代の因縁に基くからである。由紀といふ言霊は安国の霊反しであり、主基といふ言霊は知ろし召す国の霊反しである。之を見ても丹波の国には神代より深き因縁のある事が分るのである。

又小亜細亜のアーメニヤ及びコーカス山、エルサレム、メソポタミヤ及びペルシヤ、印度の一部は、富士地帯の如く高く雲上に突出してゐた。印度の如きも天竺と称へられて、其地方での最高地点であつたが、富士山の陥没と同時に、此地も亦今の如く陥落したのである。アーメニヤといふ事は天の意味又は高天原の意味である。エルサレムといふ神代の意義はウズの都、天国楽土の意味がある。茲に国祖国治立尊は始めて出現され、大八洲彦命の敵軍に囲まれ玉ひし時、国治立尊が蓮華台上より神力を発揮して、悪魔の拠れる天保山を陥落せしむると同時に天教山を現出せしめ玉うたことは、霊界物語第一巻に述ぶる通りである。又エルサレムは現今のエルサレムではない。アーメニヤの南方に当るヱルセルムであつた。そしてヨルダン河も、現今のヨルダン河とは違つてゐることは勿論である。死海の位置もメソポタミヤの東西を挟んで流れ落つる現今の波斯湾がそれであつた。

§ 3

Moreover, the Mediterranean Sea of the present day uses the ancient name in this Monogatari and is called the Sea of Seto. This Sea of Seto extended as far as the vicinity of Armenia. However, because of an earthquake disaster, the eastern part of the Sea of Seto has become dry land. Therefore, since this Monogatari is dictated based on the geography of the earth's very beginning, I would like you to understand in advance and read with the awareness that, when viewed from the standpoint of today's chirigaku, the positions and names have changed tremendously.

At the very beginning of Shiyashinkuwatsuyaku, the reason why I described this Fujisan (the ancient Tenkyōzan) is because, at the very beginning of Zuigetsu'snyūdō, his reikon was guided by the angel of Fuji, Matsuokashin, and he was shown this ancient state of affairs; and due to the karmic relation whereby his physical body was protected in the rock cave of Mount Takakuma, I think it is in proper order to orally dictate the general outline of Fujisan at the beginning of the Monogatari.

Shiyashinkuwatsuyaku is intended to orally dictate the general outline of how Zuigetsu, in May of the 31st year of Meiji, once again received an imperial divine decree to undertake a two-week period of ascetic training at Mount Takakuma, had things reflected upon his spiritual eyes, and explored the three realms of the visible, the hidden, and the divine across the past, present, and future, thereby witnessing the sacred activities of the gods.

Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sachiwa Mase.

原文（近代文語）

又現今の地中海は此物語に於て、古代の名を用ゐ、瀬戸の海と称へられてゐる。此瀬戸の海はアーメニヤの附近迄展開してゐた。併し乍らこれも震災の為に瀬戸の海の東部は陸地となつて了つたのである。故に此物語は地球最初の地理に依つて口述するものであるから、今日の地理学の上から見れば、非常に位置又は名義が変つてゐることを予め承知して読んで貰ひたいのである。

『舎身活躍』の最初に当りて、此富士山（太古の天教山）を述べたのは瑞月が入道の最初、富士の天使松岡神に霊魂を導かれ、此太古の状況を見せて貰ひ、其肉体は高熊山の岩窟に守られて居つた因縁に依つて、物語の始めに、富士山の大略を口述するのが順序だと思ふからである。

『舎身活躍』は瑞月が明治卅一年の五月、再び高熊山に神勅を奉じて二週間の修業を試み、霊眼に映じさせて頂きし事や、過、現、未の現幽神の三界を探険して、神々の御活動を目撃したる大略を口述する考へである。

あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
