Chapter 7: 五万円 [1019]

五万円〔1019〕

§ 1

Having borrowed the back room of his friend Saitō Uichi's house, Mizuki finally set about undertaking the shiugetsu spiritual discipline of Iusai. The practitioners consisted of Uichi's aunt Shizuko, his younger sister Takako (thirteen years old), Tada Koto, Ihamori Tokuko, Uheda Kaukichi, and two or three others; morning and evening they bathed in the small river flowing past the eaves, and practiced strictly for three hundred and twenty minutes a day, divided into three sessions of forty minutes each in the morning, another three sessions in the afternoon, and two sessions at night, making a total of eight sessions. Then Mizuki, wearing a haori and hakama, and holding a provisional natural flute picked up by the Obatagawa River, served in the role of a saniwa with the utmost solemnity.

Since it was his first time acting as a saniwa, he did not understand the situation at all. The very moment Tada Koto took the seat of the kannushi, she began waving her clasped hands back and forth and to the left and right, and a woman thought to weigh as much as twenty kan started leaping up to the extent that the floor of the old house threatened to collapse. The doors, shoji, and fusuma all became utterly rattled. After about a week, perhaps because she leaped up and down so violently, the floor sank by two or three inches, and the shoji and fusuma began to fall over by themselves with a clatter. Uichi was only twenty-two or three at the time, and complaints arose from his parents, who issued an order demanding that the training ground be relocated somewhere else.

As things were proceeding thus, Tada Koto began to open her mouth.

Sishi-shi, Shirataki, Shirataki, Shirataki.

So she began to utter. For the saniwa, it was his very first utterance, and taken aback, with a mixture of shock and the joy of the speech finally breaking forth, he unknowingly became puffed up with pride, thinking that the Iusai spiritual discipline was truly promising for the future. Tada's kannushi grew more and more violent in her manifestation day by day, and came to use her words fluently. Believing that a great god had come because the saniwa at that time produced a loud voice, manifested vigorously, and leaped about wildly, he even came to feel impatient when he saw a genuine and gentle kamigakari.

Following Tada Koto, Saitō Shizuko's facial expression suddenly turned ferocious, she made her clasped hands tremble recklessly and she too began to rage and leap up and down with thuds. One was a large woman, and the other was a chinko-san less than four shaku tall who was said to have no offers of marriage even at the age of thirty. Both of them started leaping up simultaneously with resounding thuds, yielding to neither. Starting with Shizuko's elder sister, Motoichi who had come as an adopted son had been Uichi's parents, but because Shizuko had become possessed by a spirit, they suddenly became enthusiastic and applied to have the cancellation of the relocation of the training ground withdrawn.

Tada's kannushi slowly opened her great mouth, and tomoe-shaped red patterns formed in the whites of her eyes, and she declared,

Which is greater, Shizugozen, Yoshitsune and Benkei, or Katō Kiyomasa? This person is Tomoe Gozen, the wife of Wada Yoshimori! As proof thereof, behold this person's eyeballs!

And she pointed to her eyes. When the novice saniwa looked closely at those eyeballs, a unmistakable single tomoe was dyeing both eyes in deep crimson. ...Could she not possibly be Tomoe Gozen after all...? While he was pondering thus, the kannushi slapped the saniwa's cheeks with a snap and shouted,

Foolish saniwa, blind saniwa, do you not understand this person's true identity? This person is none other than Shirataki Daimyōjin, who respectfully protects the third waterfall among the retinue of the Kanpei Taisha Inari Daimyōjin! Come now, henceforth this Shirataki shall act as saniwa for you. Sit you down in the seat of the kannushi!

Shizuko manifested once more and shouted,

I am the Myōken-san protector, the Heavenly Emperor's Fox Tsunetomi Daimyōjin! If you make a mistake in recognition, I shall not forgive you as saniwa as of today! Un-un, bata-bata, dosun, dosun!

And a small old woman leaped up. Thinking of it now, it is the height of belly-aching laughter, but for the saniwa at that time, it was a matter of life or death. There was neither room to laugh nor time to be angry, nor leisure to investigate. The state of manifestation encountered for the very first time, the divine oracle, and the conviction that if even ordinary human beings underwent spiritual discipline, regardless of old or young, male or female, miraculous powers could be attained—that alone was firmly established; but absolutely nothing else entered his ears, nor did he think of anything else. With single-hearted devotion, he simply continued the three weeks of spiritual discipline.

About a week later, the spiritual trainees simultaneously broke their silence, and from the mouths of the young maidens, pursing their lips, they began to speak while moving their clasped hands briskly up and down.

The training hall had strictly forbidden all outsiders from approaching, but owing to the excessively loud echo of the Hatsudō and the voice of the Kamunushi, the neighbors caught wind of it, and spreading the word from one to another, day and night the surroundings of the house became a mountain of people.

Tada Kotoha said... it is the command of Shirataki Daimiyōjin... and declaring thus, leading away every single trainee, attired in Biyakui with scarlet hakama, marching boldly in broad daylight down a road of over an ichiri,

Retinue, come along and accompany me!

Saying this while chanting a somewhat unintelligible song and keeping time with their feet, while the other trainees joined in with that song, striking stones and tiles, and crying out 'tentsu-tentsu, tsuntsuku-tsun,' they ended up going to the house of Tada Kame in Nakamura.

The Saniha thought... Oh, this is terrible; I must somehow calm them down... and tried to pursue them from behind, but for some reason, his own body had become as heavy as if pressed down by hundreds of kuwan of stone, and he could not move even a single bit.

With no other choice, Uichi became the acting Saniha and sat by his side; while he himself was made to clasp his hands in Kamunagara, and as he closed his eyes, two or three round lumps from deep within his belly rolled and pushed up to his throat, with an indescribable bitterness.

After being subjected to an experience akin to losing one's breath for thirty or forty minutes,

I am the angel Matsokawa, who guided this physical body to Mount Takakuma and led its soul to Mount Fuji. Come now, I shall appoint you as the true Saniha. The divine messengers who save all the people under heaven must undergo a considerable amount of training, or it will be no good. Now then, do not defy a single thing that I, this person, am about to say!

Thus it began to speak out of his own mouth.

Crawling on the ground, Uichi spoke:

Uichi: 'Reverently I speak to Matsuesan. I am performing my training together with everyone else, but not even once have my hands trembled, nor have the gods descended upon me to speak through me like the others; what sort of reason is there for this? If only the gods will descend upon me, I will perform any kind of training whatsoever, so please teach me.'

So he pleaded.

Then once again from the mouth of the Kamunushi,

Matsuwaka: 'Since you are generally a man of poor spirit, the gods cannot descend upon you. Even if you trained for three or five years, you would be no good, so it would be far better for you to become the Saniha instead.'

Uichi: 'How could someone who cannot even become a Kamunushi ever manage to be a Saniha?'

Matsuwaka: 'If the god says to do it, you will surely be able to. Since the god will use your physical body to do it, you need not worry in the least.'

Uichi: 'If that is so, I shall perform your bidding. As I am an unworthy Saniha, I humbly ask for your kind favor.'

Matsuwaka: 'Very well, I leave the physical body of this Kamunushi to your supervision, so you had best be quite watchful. You never know at what hour of the night he might bolt out, so it is best that you remain on guard.'

Uichi: 'Yes, thank you very much. Once the training of everyone on this occasion is finished, what shall we do thereafter?'

Matsuwaka: 'Since the gods will give roughly fifty thousand yen in gold, we shall purchase a certain location here in Anō, build a grand divine garden, construct a sanctuary, erect headquarters for Shinto, and spread the teachings. Whatever happens, we must faithfully obey the commands of the gods one by one, so you would do well to understand it thus.'

Uichi was the son of a textile merchant living to the east of a person named Saitō Genji. His father had squandered almost all of his ancestral property on financial speculation, and now even his residence was precariously afloat. It had reached such a dangerous state that no one knew when his house and estate might fly off to parts unknown or simply wash away. 元市『ハイ、有難う御座います。併し乍ら此通り門一杯人が聞いて居りますから、どうぞ低い声で仰しやつて下さいませ。私も折入つて御願が御座いますから……』

大霜小さい声で、

大霜『ヨシ分つた、何ンなつと聞いてやらう。大方五万円の金を相場に勝たしてくれいと申すのだらう』

元市『ハイ、御察しの通り五万円の金の必要が起りました。何とマアあなた様は、私の心を御存じで御座います。疑もなき天狗様、これから家内中が打揃うて、村の奴が何と申さう共信仰を致しますから、どうぞ米の上り下がりをハツキリ知らして下さりませ』

大霜『ヨシ、俺は生前に於て坂井伝三郎といふ堂島の相場師であつた。相場の為に財産をなくした位だから、神界に於ても相場に詳しいので、其方面の守護を致して居る神だ。つまり言はば専門家だ、此方の負た丈の金は其方に勝たしてやつても、別に社会の科にもなるまい。五万円などとそんな吝嗇臭い事申すな。八十万円勝たしてやらう、どうぢや嬉しいか？』

§ 4

Motoichi: 'Yes, I am very happy.'

Ōshimo: 'What do you intend to do if you get your hands on that eight hundred thousand yen?'

Motoichi: 'Well, needless to say, I will buy up the entire Sogabemura, purchase a forest for about fifty thousand yen to make it a park for the Tengu-sama, and as for the remaining seven hundred and fifty thousand yen, well, please let me think about it for a moment.'

Ōshimo: 'You intend to become a person of wealth with seven hundred and fifty thousand yen and throw your weight around, don't you? You are likely thinking even now that once you get that money, you will build a magnificent house, keep a mistress, and live in luxury and splendor. If you become too extravagant, you will indulge in wine and women, which is not good for your hygiene; your body will weaken and you will have a short life. Also, if you keep a mistress while having a wife, it is only natural that your household will always be in conflict. It might even be that your current poverty is actually happier. If that happens, the blessing of God will turn into a curse, so perhaps I should limit it to fifty thousand yen.'

Motoichi: 'No, no, that is impossible. I have heard that there are no two words in the speech of God. Since you are a god, you may have no need for money, but as long as one has a physical body, money is necessary. Out of the seven hundred and fifty thousand yen, I shall take one hundred thousand yen for myself, and the remaining six hundred and fifty thousand yen I will deposit in the post office, donate to charity, and use for the sake of society.'

Ōshimo: 'That is also fine, but do you not have the intention to use it for the path of God?'

Motoichi: 'Yes, as for the side of God, the fifty thousand yen is already settled just as promised.'

Ōshimo: 'Hahaha, if that is the case, then that may be fine, but how will you prepare the base capital to play the market?'

Motoichi: 'Yes, as you know, I have become completely destitute, and there is no one left who will lend me even one yen, so I would like to rely on the help of God to lend me this capital.'

Ōshimo: 'Very well. I know the place where gold coins are buried, so I shall show it to you. You must not tell anyone. However, since this money is buried deep in the mountains, even if you do not go, I will use the body of this priest to have it dug up, so you had better wait for two or three days.'

Motoichi: 'Yes, thank you. I do not know how much is hidden, but it is dangerous on the way if I am alone. It would be terrible if I were to encounter thieves, so please, let me at least accompany you.'

Ōshimo: 'No, no. There are about one million yen worth of gold coins hidden there, but if I let you know the location, you will again bring out an evil spirit and fall into the state of body-dominant and spirit-subordinate, so I will first have this body dig up only ten thousand yen as capital. You must absolutely not follow me.'

Motoichi: 'In that case, I will have my son Uichi accompany you, so please allow just that.'

Ōshimo: 'No, that will not do either. God will use the body of this priest at His own discretion to dig it out. I will hand it over depending on your repentance.'

Motoichi: 'Yes, I understand. Please rest assured. I have no intention of becoming arrogant at all. I am repenting from the very bottom of my heart.'

Kiraku was listening to everything said from within his own belly, and hearing Motoichi's words as well, he could not help but find it ridiculous... 'Oh, there is no such money,' he thought to himself, unable to help it.

Ōshimo: 'God is now taking His leave. Revere this body as a great master and treat it with care. Dosusu, un.'

With that, he jumped up, and the possessing spirit immediately left the body.

Motoichi: 'Ah, Great Master, thank you for your trouble. Please take care of yourself. I have received a very magnificent divine virtue.'

Kiraku: 'I was listening too, and although Ōshimo-san said such sweet things, I cannot help but worry that it might be a lie.'

Motoichi shook his head from side to side.

Motoichi: 'Great Master, you should not say such disrespectful things. Tengu-san is a person who is ten percent honest, so there is no fear of him telling a lie. Ah, with this, my luck has begun to turn.'

He was smiling.

That day, feeling a strange urge to return home, he decided to return to his house for the first time in a long while.
