Chapter 25: Phantom 魅来 [1037]

妖魅来〔1037〕

§ 1

Walking from Shinomura, on the way back, I took the opportunity to drop by the house of Toranosuke Fukushima in Yagi.

However, the master of the house, Mr. Toranosuke, was away in Ayabe for spiritual training, and his wife, Hisako-san, and children were there, so Shikata-san recounted in detail everything from the state of affairs in Ayabe to the circumstances of Fukushima-san's divine possession.

However, since Hisako was a believer in Konkuōkyō, she was half-doubting about talks of Reigaku, brushed everything off at the tip of her nose, and took it in a way that did not sit well with her, making it uninteresting.

The two made haste to leave this house, crossed the Ōhashi Bridge in Yagi, and visited the residence of Takehiro Tsuchida in a place called Osabe, where, as they were mutually discussing topics such as the way of the gods, an urgent telegram arrived from Kyoto.

When Mr. Tsuchida promptly opened it wondering what could be the matter, it was a telegram saying that his cousin, a man named Magosaburō Nambu living in Kyoto, was critically ill and he should come immediately.

Since Mr. Tsuchida did not have a very affluent life, he did not even have the travel expenses to go to Kyoto.

Greatly troubled, he turned toward Kiraku and spoke thus,

Tsuchida: 'The telegram just now is about my cousin Nambu, who until now has served as a missionary for the Konkuō Church, but he is a man of unsettled character; from Kyoto up to the Owari, Enshū, and Suruga areas, he has opened as many as thirteen Konkuō preaching places, only to fail each time by getting involved with women, and then moving to other lands to open churches, where he was likewise driven out for becoming involved with women, and finally, being excommunicated by Mr. Sesshū Sugita of the Konkuō Church, at present he is dependent upon his younger sister's home. From two or three years ago, he contracted pulmonary tuberculosis and has been lingering aimlessly. Since divine punishment must have finally fallen upon him, I believe that a full recovery is surely hopeless, but if possible, I wish to receive the divine aid of the Lord God once more. Is it possible for you to save him once again through your prayer?'

He asked anxiously.

Kiraku felt pity and immediately inquired of the divine realm.

According to that divine oracle, the day one week from now would be the great turning point for this patient, and ninety-nine percent he would surely not be saved...

At that, Mr. Tsuchida...

Tsuchida: 'If you will save Nambu's life, I will persuade him myself and make him your disciple, and have him vow to exert himself for the sake of our Way.'

So he said.

Kiraku laughed and said,

Kiraku: 'It would be troublesome if he were to repeat his past conduct from the days of being a Konkuō Church missionary, wouldn't it? However, for these three years, I will pray to the gods to have them prolong his life. After observing his conduct for three years, the gods will surely prolong his span of life once more. Write this in a letter and let Nambu-san know. If you do so, there is no need to spend money on travel expenses to go to Kyoto.'

Mr. Tsuchida was overjoyed, wrote a letter in minute detail, and gave up on going to Kyoto.

As expected, on the seventh day Nambu's breath stopped once, but after a little while he drew breath again, and from then on he headed day by day toward recovery.

When Mr. Tsuchida went to Kyoto upon Nambu's full recovery and met with him,

Tsuchida: 'Your recovery from this severe illness is entirely the blessing of the divine virtue of the Ushitora no Konjin who appeared in Ayabe, and the reward of the fervent prayers of a man named Ueda.'

Thus speaking, he talked in detail about what he had promised Kiraku and the experimental accounts of divine possession training in Ayabe.

However, Nambu said,

Nambu: 'It is not necessarily the divine virtue of the Ushitora no Konjin of Ayabe. Through the protection of the Kami of Heaven and Earth whom I ordinarily believe in, and the Konkuō Founder, my severe illness was saved by utilizing the god of Ayabe and the man named Ueda. Therefore, ninety-nine percent of this gratitude belongs after all to Konkuō-sama.'

So saying, he went directly to pay a formal visit of thanks to the Shimabara Konkuō Church in Kyoto, and toward the direction of Ayabe he did not even join his hands properly.

§ 2

From then on, saying, 'Having hitherto received the appointment as a Fukyōshi of Konkuōkyō yet daring to commit various wicked acts, and because of my sins having fallen under the wrath of Kamisama such that my life was already in imminent peril, I received blessings through the profound mercy of the Lord God of Tenchikane and the Goitoku of the Kyōso of Konkuō,' his mother, younger sister, and he himself visited the Kyōkuwaishiyo in Shimabara by turns morning and evening. However, one or two months later, this time his abdomen began to ache violently, falling into a critical condition day by day, to the extent that not only daily visits but even movement within the room became impossible. Thereupon, his mother and younger sister desperately performed Hiyakudo at the Konkuwai, but not the slightest Reiken appeared. When carried to the Daigaku hospital and examined by a doctor, it was declared to be a very severe case of appendicitis requiring an immediate surgical incision, but because the patient's physical weakness was extreme, his life could not be guaranteed. Therefore, having no other choice, they gave up having the operation performed, brought him back to their own home, left him to the course of nature, and had no means left other than to await the arrival of his final hour.

Falling increasingly into a critical state, nothing more could be done, and his life approached morning or evening. Once again, a telegram was sent to his cousin, Mr. Tsuchida: 'The illness is critical, come at once.' Mr. Tsuchida, staying at the usual lodging of Gyōbu, perused this, sent a reply reading 'Face toward Ayabe and clap your hands,' and did not go up to Tokyo. Mr. Nanbu's mother and younger sister in Kyoto, seeing that telegram, thought 'A god-pleading in times of extremity; if only his life is saved, any Kamisama will do,' and facing toward Ayabe, prayed with single-minded devotion: 'Ushitora no Konjin-sama, please forgive our past misunderstandings and discourtesies. If you will save Sonzaburō's life just once more, we will offer both his body and spirit, and humbly perform the service of Ushitora no Konjin-sama.'

Miraculously, an immediate response occurred; a large horsefly, which seemed to be about an inch in size and regardless of the bitter cold, came flying from nowhere making a buzzing sound, and after circling three times above the patient's head, a sound like even rocks shattering issued from Mr. Nanbu's abdomen, and foul matter amounting to some two or three shō was discharged from the anus. At the same time, the severe abdominal pain ceased, and day by day he headed toward recovery. This was the motive by which Mr. Nanbu abandoned Konkuōkyō and entered the Ōmoto faith in Ayabe.

When the two returned to Ayabe, they found that evil spirits had made a raid upon the training hall in Kamidani, and Shinto priests such as Toranosuke Fukushima, Fusanosuke Murakami, Atsusaburō Nozaki, and one or two others had fallen into great chaos, running about raving and uttering nothing but absurd things. Murakami ran about the neighboring villages day and night without distinction, spreading various matters, and possessed by evil spirits, ran about raving in his desperate attempt to ruin the name of Ōmoto. Toranosuke Fukushima, with a loud voice echoing throughout the village of Kamidani, spoke thus:

Fukushima: 'Toranosuke, born in the Year of the Ox, is the sole Toranosuke in Fukushima. Therefore, this one is truly the genuine Great Ushitora no Konjin. Ueda was born in the Year of the Sheep, and Nao Deguchi was born in the Year of the Monkey. At last, the two combined make Hitsujisaru no Konjin. They are two as one. No one can possibly match this Toranosuke. Come, all you people, completely repent of your past misunderstandings, and if you apologize to this one, I will forgive your past sins. Deguchi and Ueda are the Konjin of the Ura-kimon; the true Ushitora no Konjin was not Nao Deguchi. Those who do not understand this will be severely chastised and thrown down to the bottom of the valley. From now on, Kamisama will use Toranosuke Fukushima to carry out the Tatekae and Tatenaoshi of the three thousand worlds, making gods, Buddhist affairs, people, and even hungry ghosts and tiny insects thrive with vigor. If this is wrong, Kamisama does not exist in this world. The character for "Great" is upside down. Before long, heaven and earth will be completely turned upside down. Be prepared. Before long, birds will rise from under your feet. Ushitora no Konjin has hitherto been reviled as an evil god and a cursing god, but was truly a magnificent god. Kamisama will appear in the open, clearly separating good from evil, make the people of the world repent, and bring about the Age of the Pine Gods. Kamisama never speaks falsehoods. If you doubt, it will become a spiritual offense to Kamisama. Even when Ueda returns from now on, you must not become his opponent. The true Ushitora no Konjin is taking heed.'

Becoming entirely naked, the eumi possessed them, shouting and raving incessantly, merely imitating the fudesaki of the kyōso, which became quite unmanageable.

Kiraku immediately poured his prayers into the shinkai and performed chinkon.

Although this temporarily quelled the riot of the jiyashin, a multitude of other eumi kindred possessed other gamugakari and lent support to the kami of Fukushima.

At last, all the gamugakari unisonously shouted,

Shikata: 'All of you! If you do not pull yourselves together, you will be deceived by the magatsu of Uheda and made to suffer terribly.

There is no doubt that the true Ushitora no Konjin is Daisensei of Fukushima.'

Hearing this, Fukushima was once again seized by the jiyashin, raising and lowering his thick black eyebrows, rolling his eyes, swinging his arms up, jumping and leaping, turning up his buttocks and dancing around, making a sound as though mats were splitting and floors were collapsing, echoing with a boom, boom, boom, and he began to re-enact a prodigious ōsawa, so that country folk, finding it a curiosity, came from every direction day after day carrying lunch boxes to watch.

Even though Kiraku devoted all his strength to suppression, since the eumi had possessed the majority of the more than twenty-odd spirit-possessed individuals while he was absent, it was by no means easily quieted; suppress this side and that side rose up, resembling precisely the situation where kappa-ya were drying kappa on the racecourse when a sudden gale blew up, causing the kappa to dance into the air, and unable to hold them down all at once, the old man flurriedly tumbled into the moat.

And day by day the wild behavior grew more violent.

At length the parents and brothers of the practitioners grew angry and came forward,

Shikata: 'Since you have driven my family's precious son mad, I will not let this pass; you have made my younger sister fox-possessed... you tried to make my child into a miko... you must have attached a tanuki to him, and I will report you to the authorities,'

and so forth, attacking all at once.

Shikata Tōtarō, possessing a fair amount of common sense among them, cared in both overt and hidden ways, and faithfully assisted the saniwa; thus Kiraku also gained immense strength, brushing aside all thousand difficulties and myriad sufferings, disregarding all simultaneous resistance and interference, and fully wielding the authority of the saniwa until he finally succeeded in bringing the jiyashin to allegiance.

On the other hand, Adachi Masanobu and others, who had been Konko teachers, completely changed their mindset, concluding that this was the very opportunity not to be missed to ruin the Konmei-kai; they secretly gathered numerous believers within the home of a former believer named Tanaka Shinnosuke, emphasizing the poor results of the spiritual arts of chinkon kishin, and plotted in secret to expel Kiraku.

The believers of the Konmei-kai, who had been on the verge of solidifying, wandered about in a thick fog, lost as to their future course, and fell into a state of utter disunion.

Believing this to be a clever stroke, ambitious men such as Nakamura Takezō and Shikata Shunzō ran about everywhere, spreading worthless rumors to the utmost of their power and capacity that Kiraku's gamugakari was harmful and useless, that he was a fox-user, or a sorcerer.

In the end, they even began to speak ill of the kyōso.

The state of affairs at that time was precisely in the style of the hiding in the Heavenly Rock Cave of the Kojiki, where all ten thousand spirits arise.

Fortunately, through the zeal and sincerity of Shikata Heizō, Shikata Tōtarō, and others, the fierce resistance against Kiraku temporarily gained a slight lull.

And the one who raised the banner of rebellion first of all was Fukushima Toranosuke.

Originally, Fukushima enjoyed a reputation for honesty, being a man of impeccable character.

A creature known as an eumi is a thoroughly tricky fellow; it rarely possesses a human being who is regarded by the world as a bad person or as dishonest.

This is because it knows that even if it were to possess such a person, since the individual lacks credibility, the world would not believe it.

Therefore, evil spirits invariably choose to dwell in virtuous human beings, so unless a practitioner of gamugakari possesses considerable fortitude and working wisdom, he will invite unexpected failure.

Pests are apt to swarm upon a tree that bears good fruit.

Even with a single chrysanthemum, insects tend to swarm all the more on one that blooms large and beautiful; thus, to think that an evil kami will not attach to someone simply because he is honest and good-natured is a colossal misconception.

Ah, Kamuna garu nayorashi base.

『吾家の大事な伜を気違にしたから承知せない、吾妹を狐つきにしよつた……おれの子を巫子に仕立よとしよつた……狸をつけたのだろ、其筋に告訴してやる』

などと一斉にせめかくる。四方藤太郎は其中でも稍常識を持つてゐたから、陰に陽に気を配り、忠実に審神者の手伝ひをしてくれたので、喜楽も非常に力を得、千難万苦を排して一斉の反抗も妨害も頓着なく、あく迄審神者の職権をふりまはして漸く邪神を帰順せしむることを得た。

一方では金光教師たりし足立正信氏等は心機一転して、金明会を破滅せしむるは此好機を措いて他にある可らずとなし、数多の信徒をひそかに、以前の田中新之助といふ信者の内に集めて、鎮魂帰神の霊術の不成績なることを強調し、且つ喜楽を放逐すべく密議をこらしてゐた。折角固まりかけてゐた金明会の信徒は五里霧中に彷徨し、去就に迷ひ、四分五裂の状態になつて来た。えたり賢しと、中村竹造、四方春三の野心家等が、諸方へかけまはつて喜楽の神懸は有害にして無益だとか、狐使だとか、魔法師だとか力限り根限り下らぬことをふれ歩く。遂には教祖のことまで悪口するやうになつて来た。其時の有様は全く万妖悉く起るてふ古事記の天の岩戸がくれ式であつた。

幸にして四方平蔵、同藤太郎等の熱心と誠実なる調停で、一時は喜楽に対する猛烈な反抗も稍小康を得ることとなつた。そしてイの一番に叛旗をかかげたのは福島寅之助氏であつた。元来福島は正直の評判をとつてゐる、人間としては申分のない心掛のよい人である。妖魅といふ奴は中々食へぬ奴で、世界から…彼は悪人ぢや、不正直だと見なされてゐるやうな人間にはメツタに憑るものでない。たとへ憑つて見た所で其人物に信用がなければ、世人が信用せないことを知つてゐるからである。そこで悪魔は必ず善良なる人間を選んで憑りたがるものであるから、神懸の修行する者は余程胆力のある智慧の働く人でないと、とんだ失敗を招くものである。良き実を結ぶ木には害虫がわき易いものである。菊一本にても、大きい美しい花の咲くものには虫が却てよけいにわくやうなもので、正直だから善人だから、悪神がつく筈がないと思ふのは、大変な考へ違である。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
