Chapter 7: 妻生 [1774]

妻生〔1774〕

§ 1

The eaves are tilted and the roof is broken; the Kayabuki roof is so dilapidated that the Susudake bones are exposed to the point where butterflies, Tonbo, bees, sparrows, and even rain fall through from above. The Amado are eaten away by the wind as if they were tucked up at the hem in a Shichibusanbu style, and the Shiyōji are pitch black, hanging Yōraku from every muntin, dancing in Jiiu every time the wind blows. The damp tatami mats are completely torn on the surface, and only the reddish floorboards barely maintain their Meimiyaku, looking so Miguru that one's hair stands on end even when stepping inside. Furthermore, an indescribable and Iyō Shiuki strikes the nose. Yet, to the eyes of Takahime and Kyūbā, this Bauoku appears like a Kindengiyokurō, and it is strange that the Iyō Shiuki is perceived as Jiyakau, felt through the Jiyōdō of their Saunen. They feel the taste of Ushiguso like Botamochi, and the smell of Umaguso like Ohagi, and they are so Manzoku that they cannot help but click their throats and slurp it up. With a rattan vine handle attached to a chipped and soot-covered Dobin, hung from a soot-covered hook dangling from the roof, the Futari are Kiki and playfully fooling around in a Taai manner while warming tea by burning Ushiguso. The correspondence of the Mitama is truly a mysterious thing.

This Takahime-sama, to the eyes of Kyūbā, looked like an old woman at first glance, but before he knew it, she began to look like a beauty such as Princess Chigusa, the queen of the Tolman Kingdom. Also, to the eyes of Takahime, the face of Kyūbā, with his bulging eyes like a Karasu-tengu and his pointed, ugly mouth, began to look like the dignified Mokusuke, the Tokiokishi, whom she could not help but admire. Takahime, pursing her mouth which was wrinkled like a mouse's whiskers, shook her body with a 'shona shona' motion.

Takahime: 'Look here, Mokusuke-san, no, Takamiyahiko-dono, you have transformed quite remarkably. When we met at that crossroads, I thought you were just an ordinary pilgrim, but now that I look at your face closely, you are undoubtedly Takamiyahiko-sama. Why, I am Takamiyahime, you know. What is this? Please stop that distant, stranger-like behavior. No matter how much you have succeeded and become great, you are still my husband.'

Kyūbā: 'Have you changed your name to Takamiyahime? I thought your name was Princess Chigusa, if I recall correctly.'

Takahime: 'Oh, Mokuchan, how disingenuous you are. Did we not promise each other at that palace and change my name to Takamiyahime? You also changed your name to Takamiyahiko at that time, did you not?'

Kyūbā: 'Strange, you must be Princess Chigusa, no matter what. You are saying such odd things. However, the name does not matter. If our hearts are perfectly in tune, that is enough.'

With this, the two exchanged their roles, and began to flirt in a Kiki manner.

Takahime: 'Tell me, my dear, where have you been walking since you left me? You cannot imagine how much I have been searching for you.'

Kyūbā: 'I established a base in the Tolman Kingdom on the Dekatan Plateau, and ever since I saw you, you have been flickering before my eyes, and I have been burning with the desire to meet you. Just as I was agonizing over how to see you, you had become the queen of the Tolman King, leaving me with no way to approach you. Through every possible means, I was finally able to get close to you and clear away the long darkness of my love. Come, let us now unite our hearts, take possession of the Tolman Kingdom, and trample over the Moon Kingdom of over seven thousand nations. To save the modern age, which can be called the pinnacle of scientific civilization, mere sermons, speeches, and prayers are unlikely to be effective. One must stand in the position of a king, grasp the military in one hand, and govern the hearts of the people with a sword in one hand and the Koran in the other; otherwise, both religion and politics are lies.'

Takahime: 'Indeed, there is likely no other divine being in the world with such wisdom and courage as you. Ah, the three years I spent suffering in this mountain hut were solely because I wanted to see you; I wonder if the time has finally come.'

While exchanging such incoherent and selfish responses, they lowered the curtain of Yami and fell asleep while embracing each other. Tonbo, who had been standing outside, could not bear his jealousy; he picked up pebbles and threw them one after another through the tear in the door with a thudding sound. The pebbles broke the belly of the hanging Dobin, and urine dripped down onto the feet of the two who were sleeping. Takahime, startled and jumping up, said with a trembling voice,

Takahime: 'Hey, what are you doing while the savior of Tenka is sowing seeds? Who are you? State your name!'

She shouted. Tonbo replied from outside,

Tonbo: 'Wahahahaha! It was I, Tonbo-sama, who threw the stones. Hey, Baba, just you wait, I will smash this house to pieces soon. I have my own Shiyōnen, too.'

He said, and once again, with the danger of grabbing pebbles in both hands like rain, he threw them in. The stones hit the Todana and Mizuya, and with a clattering, pattering, and banging, even the Setomono were smashed to pieces. Takahime and Kiubā, the two of them, could not bear the danger; they pulled open the Omotedo and shouted 'Hey!', and in the momentum of their shouting, Tonbo, with his skin-and-bones body, baring his buttocks, ran away with a thumping sound. Takahime and Kiubā caught up to him, grabbed him by the Sotsukubi to punish him, and while still Matsupadaka, they chased after Tonbo, breathless, turning into blue fireballs.

Tonbo arrived at the gate of the Yachimata Sekishiyo, and in his haste, he bumped hard into the black gate, and while saying 'Ah!', he collapsed on the spot. Kiubā and Takahime, the two of them, raised their shrill voices, shouting 'Hoi-hoi, hoi-hoi-hoi' with an extraordinary volume, and when they saw the figure of Tonbo collapsed there, they felt a sense of triumph.

Takahime: 'Hohohohoho! Hey, Moku-chan, is it not true that divine punishment is a terrifying thing, my dear? Are you not the blockhead who threw stones out of jealousy because you saw me and my dear performing the sacred dance of sowing the seeds of the Aohitogusa, which has been passed down since the age of the gods? Hey, blockhead, pull yourself together! Have you been struck with awe by the divine power of Ikumiya-sama?'

Tonbo finally regained consciousness and said,

Tonbo: 'Are you not Ikumiya-sama? It is not right to come as far as the front of this government office and expose people's shame. Please, at least spare me the slander. I am still a person with a long future, and I must stand up in the world again and work. If my bad reputation reaches the ears of the officials, I will never be able to hold my head high no matter where I go, you see.'

Takahime: 'Hen, hey, what are you blathering about? Is it not your own fault? If I were to let someone like you hold your head high in this world, would the world not become covered in dark clouds? That is why I said it in a loud voice so that the officials could hear it. My, what a pathetic face you have.'

Tonbo: 'Hey, Baba, I am already at the end of my rope, so say whatever slander you like. In return, I will shout out your secrets at the top of my lungs so that they reach the ears of the officials.'

Kiubā: 'Hey, hey, Tonbo or whatever your name is, do not say such reckless things. For a three-penny rascal like you, it should not matter much whether you are spoken of well or ill. However, for a savior like us, even if it is a lie, if you speak ill of us, it will not only hinder the work of world salvation, but the sin will immediately return to you and you will fall into the hanging hell.'

Tonbo: 'Hen, leave me alone. Were you not performing an outrageous seed-sowing ritual with this Baba by the hearth? That ugly scene... excuse me, Mr. Official, these two are great criminals who have violated the heavenly laws. Please, in accordance with the rules, throw them into hell. They are hungry ghosts and beasts trying to spread the teachings of evil gods to Tenka, calling them things like the Uranai sect or the Suko sect. I will be the witness. Please, investigate these two strictly.'

He bellows at the top of his lungs.

Kiyū: 'Hey, you there, you so-called Tonbo, the sin of slandering the Kyōshiyu or Ikimiya is light, but the sin of slandering the path of the Oshihe shall not be forgiven for Mangomatsudai. Do you know how heavy the sin of Bauhofu is?'

Tonbo: 'Hmph, do not speak so haughtily. I have never committed the sin of Bauhofu anywhere. You two are the very ones who have been doing evil things in Haubau. Hey, Yakuninsama, I have brought two Daizainin here. If you do not come quickly, they will Tonbau. Quickly, quickly!'

He is shouting thus. The two Shiyuei, one red and one white, suspicious of this voice, open the gate to the Saiu and look out to see this Teisai.

Red: 'Hey, hey, today is a Kōkiubi. Why are you making such a Yakama noise? If you have a complaint, come back Asu; I shall listen to it then.'

Tonbo: 'Hey, I am speaking to you, Yakuninsama. While you are looking right now at such Daiakunin who disturb the Tenka, can you really fulfill your duties as a Yakunin by saying you will not investigate because it is a Kōkiubi? You must be receiving your Getsukifu even for Nichieu. Please investigate, even if it takes only a moment.'

Red: 'Oh, are you not the lieutenant of the Brahmins? Have you not yet finished your training and are you still lost at the Yachimata? You are a troublesome fellow.'

Tonbo: 'Hey, Yakuninsama, you say such funny things. Does a Yachimata exist in the Genzai world as if it were the underworld?'

Red: 'This is the Yachimata of the underworld. You once became a subordinate of General Oniharubake, and after the army was disbanded, you became a thief and wandered in all directions, were killed by a certain warrior, and have come here as a spirit. Are you still not aware of that?'

Tonbo: 'Hmph, do not mock me so much. Please be a little serious. I am not a madman. Do the dead speak like this? One would be dead only when one cannot see, cannot hear, cannot speak, and cannot move one's hands and feet. Hmph, you are mocking me. It is pointless to be the side dish for the sake of a fellow who has turned his face bright red from drinking such sake. I shall go home today. In return, watch out Asu; I will report everything about today to your superior. Then you will instantly become a signboard for a tabi shop, and your wife and children will turn into mummies, ha-ha-ha-ha-ha!'

Leaving these parting words behind, he grabs a stone from the roadside and throws it at Kiyūbā and Takahime, and while floating his long, thin skeleton—which looks like the shadow of a setting sun—in the air, he flees toward the north.

Red: 'Hey, is that not Takahime there? You were asked by the Tokiokishi Mokusuke-sama and left to wander this Yachimata for three years, but you still have several decades of life remaining in the Genzai world. The life of the spirit world cannot be permitted for you. The body you inhabit is the body of Princess Chigusa, the consort of King Toruman. Go, leave at once. And Kiyūbā, you are a villain without equal under heaven, but your lifespan still remains in the book of life and death. Return to the Genzai world as quickly as possible. If you dawdle, your physical body will not be in time.'

The moment the two felt a shock and came to their senses, they were inside Toruman Castle, collapsed in a room of Princess Chigusa with the lock undone. The noisy sounds of men and horses, the cries of battle, and the sounds of cannons and rifles could be heard as if they were right at hand. From this point on, the words and deeds of Princess Chigusa changed completely, and she began to take actions full of derailments once again. The spirit of Takahime, which had been at the Yachimata, had obtained the body it was meant to inhabit and was revived.

赤『ヤ、そこに居るのは高姫ぢやないか。お前は時置師の杢助さまに頼まれ、三年間この八衢に放養して置いたが、未だ数十年の寿命が現界に残つて居る。到底霊界の生活は許されない。お前の宿る肉体はトルマン王の妃千草姫の肉体だ。サ一時も早く立ち去れ。又キユーバー、汝は天下無比の悪党であるが、まだ生死簿には寿命がのこつて居る。一時も早く現界へ立ち帰れ。グヅグヅ致して居ると肉体が間に合はなくなるぞ』

と厳しく言ひ渡した。二人はハツと思ふ途端に気がつけばトルマン城内、千草姫の一室に錠前を卸して倒れて居た。どことも無く騒々しい人馬の物音、矢叫びの声、大砲小銃の音手に取る如く聞え来る。是より千草姫の言行は一変し、又もや脱線だらけの行動を取る事となつた。八衢に居た高姫の精霊は己が納まるべき肉体を得て甦つたのである。
