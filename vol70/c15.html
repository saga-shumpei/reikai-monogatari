Chapter 15: 地位転変 [1782]

地位転変〔1782〕

§ 1

Chigusahime entered the King's Ima, flapping her arms as if performing a Shimahi, exposing an Ōheizura, and spoke in a Sauchiyō manner:

Chigusahime: 'Listen well and hear the Takusen of the Kokuō of the Toruman country, the Ōdono Garden, the Kiuseishiyu of the Sanzensekai, the Futobashira of the Sokotsuihane no Ōmiroku, and the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake Tennin of the Daiichireigoku. Though this Nikutai is Chigusahime, the spirit is the Makotokitsusui Suishiyōdama of Hinodewake, who has appeared as the Sukuhinushi of this world. From your perspective, you must think this Ikimiya is a Kichiga. There is no Machiga in the true Kami.'

King Garden, seeing this attitude of Chigusahime, knitted his brows in Fushin and thought, 'Ah, a troublesome matter has occurred. At last, the Ōhi has gone mad. However, to oppose her while she is agitated will only worsen the illness, so I shall listen to what she says in silence for a while...'

King: 'Indeed, you must be the Ikimiya of Hinodewake. Tell me, what is your business?'

Chigusahime: 'This is an outrageous word from you; how disrespectful. To say "tell me" to Hinodewake... what insolence. Your head is too high; sit down. I shall explain the innen of the Sanzensekai to you.'

King: 'Yes.'

As he reluctantly left his chair and sat down, Chigusahime smiled and said,

Chigusahime: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, as expected of the King of the Toruman country, you have observed this Hinodewake well. I shall bestow this upon you as a reward. Receive it with gratitude.'

While saying this, she placed her left foot heavily upon his shining bald head, which resembled that of a Kshatriya, and while groaning twice, 'Uun, Uun,' she lowered her left foot and then placed her right foot upon his head in the same manner, groaning twice again... laughing, 'Ho ho ho ho ho,' she stood upright in the Tokonoma with composure.

Chigusahime: 'Listen well, King Garden. Prince Siddhartha, the son of King Suddhodana of Ceylon, ascended Mount Dantoku and Mount Gridhrakuta, attained Buddhahood after five years of ascetic practice, returned to his country, and made his father, King Suddhodana, pay homage to the Buddha's feet. You, who have received the divine feet of Hinodewake—the Futobashira of the Sokotsuihane no Ōmiroku and the Tennin of the Daiichireigoku—on both your feet, are the most fortunate person in the Sanzensekai; be grateful and give thanks. There is no Machiga in Hinodewake.'

King Garden had thought it was something suspicious at first, but after having Chigusahime's feet placed upon his head, his state of mind changed completely, and he came to believe firmly that she was a living Kami. Now that it had come to this, the organization within the castle lost its center, and it became impossible to manage.

Chigusahime: 'Ōdono Garden, although this Nikutai of Chigusahime was permitted by the Kamikai to be your wife until today, now that the time of heaven has arrived and I have appeared as the Kiuseishiyu of the Sanzensekai, I am no longer your wife. Therefore, you shall become a disciple of the Nikumiya of Hinodewake from now on, swear absolute obedience, and serve without violating anything, will you not?'

King: 'Yes, without you even having to ask, I shall accept any command.'

Chigusahime: 'Oho ho ho ho, satisfied, satisfied. The Kami's mechanism of turning the heavens and earth, where the top becomes the bottom and the bottom becomes the top; the husband of until now becomes the disciple of the wife, and the wife of until now uses her husband as a disciple—this is the divine plan. Since it has come to this, King Garden, you shall uphold the divine decree of Hinodewake, search for the great monk Kiubā, who is the vice-pillar of the Kiuseishiyu of the Sanzensekai, as quickly as possible, and bring him back to this castle. If you violate this, divine punishment will arrive immediately.'

King: 'Yes, I understand the details, but as for that Kiubā, because he broke out of prison and fled, I have sent people out in secret and am searching for him, but I have yet to receive any good news. I humbly ask for a little more time.'

ガーデン王は始めの間は何だか怪しいと思つてゐたが、千草姫の足を頭にのせられてから、ガラリと心機一転し、全くの活神と固く固く信ずる様になつた。サア斯うなつては、最早城内の整理は中心を失ひ、手のつけやうもなくなつて了つた。

千草『ガーデン王殿、此千草姫の肉体は、今日迄は汝が妃として、神界より許しありしも、いよいよ天の時節到来し、三千世界の救世主と現はれたれば、最早汝の妃ではない程に、汝は之より日の出神の肉宮が弟子となり、絶対服従を誓つて、何事にも違背せず尽すであらうなア』

王『ハイ、委細承知致しましたが、彼れキユーバーは如何なりしか、破獄逃走致しました故、内々人を派し、捜索致して居りまするが、未だ何の吉報も得ませぬ。少時の御猶予を願ひ奉りまする』

Chigusahime: 'If your words are without error, then in all likelihood, it is Junk who has hidden her.'

Ō: 'No, no, absolutely not; such a reason does not exist. He consulted with me in secret, wishing to save Kyūbā as quickly as possible. I immediately granted Junk's request and sent men to the prison to investigate, but Kyūbā had already been abducted by someone and her whereabouts were unknown.'

Chigusahime: 'Ah, that must be it, that must be it; yes, I understand, I understand. There is no mistake that the ringleaders are the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō, Terukuniwake and Terukō. Bind them at once and throw them without delay into the prison where Kyūbā was confined. This is by no means the word of the physical Chigusahime. It is the Shinchiyoku of the Ōmiroku of Sokotsuihane.'

Ō: 'I am humbled by the Goshinchiyoku, but no matter what is said, I cannot in good conscience throw Terukuniwake, the Sendenshi who saved the nation from crisis, into prison without any crime. I beg your pardon for this one thing.'

Chigusahime: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, what foolish things are you saying? If you are glared at once by the eyes of the living God who sees through the three thousand worlds at once, there is never any mistake. If you stubbornly refuse my Shinchiyoku, I shall take your life on the spot; are you prepared for that? I shall hear your answer.'

Ō: 'No, please wait a moment. In that case, I shall follow the Goshinchiyoku and use whatever means necessary to bind the divine officials Terukuniwake and Terukō and throw them into prison for you to see.'

Chigusahime: 'U, good, good, with that, the God is satisfied. The Tolman Castle shall be prosperous, and not only the Moon Kingdom of over seven thousand nations, but every single country upon this earth shall be under your rule. As the father of a mere thirty thousand people, is it not a pity to spend your whole life in vain? How is it, do you understand?'

Ō: 'Yes, I have understood every detail.'

Chigusahime: 'Ya, satisfied, satisfied. Next, there is something I must command you. You shall give Prince Chūin and Princess Chinrei a single hat and a single straw raincoat each, provide them with a staff, and have them depart from this castle as travelers as soon as possible.'

Ō: 'As you command, but I am old, and if the Prince is not here, I lose the central figure of the nation; furthermore, Princess Chinrei is somewhat sickly, so I beg for your grace to reconsider this one matter.'

Chigusahime: 'Foolish, King Garden. Now that the living palace of the Hinode-no-Kami has appeared as the Ōmiroku of Sokotsuihane, I intend to roll the three thousand worlds into one and place them under your rule. You are already of advanced age, and it is necessary for the successor, the Prince, to see and hear the world widely. Does the proverb not say to send a beloved child on a journey? Are you drowning in parental love and trying to destroy the happiness of your own precious child, you spineless old man?'

Ō: 'No, I understand. I shall follow the Shinchiyoku and send the Prince on a journey for his training, but as a parent, I cannot bear to make my sickly younger sister endure the hardships of travel. I beg you to grant me grace for this one thing.'

Chigusahime: 'Well now, what an uncomprehending old man you are. Did you not swear absolute obedience to God? As soon as Princess Chinrei leaves this gate, her illness will instantly vanish, and she will have a body as healthy as gold and iron. There is no mistake in the word of God. What is your answer?'

Ō: 'If that is the case, I shall follow the Goshinchiyoku and convey this to both of them.'

Chigusahime: 'King Garden, splendid, splendid; through your repentance, the divine government will soon be achieved, and the world of Miroku will appear.'

Ō: 'Yes, thank you very much.'

Chigusahime: 'There is one more thing I must command you. You will show absolute obedience to this as well, will you not?'

Ō: 'Yes.'

Chigusahime: 'You have entrusted the pivot of politics to Junk, but how could a rustic like him manage the politics of the Tolman Kingdom created by God? He is the explosive bomb of our nation. He is the spirit of the Yamata-no-Orochi. Drive him out of this castle as soon as possible.'

King: 'Since this person is essential, I humbly request that you grant a stay of execution.'

Chigusahime: 'I shall grant a stay of three days; by then, you must instruct him thoroughly and drive him out of the castle. However, if you inquire into the whereabouts of Shiyōnin Kyūbā while on that junk, and succeed in bringing him to the castle, I shall have no objection to entrusting a portion of the state affairs to you as a reward. Now, Setsuteiridono, you have worked hard. Retire to the living room and rest. Ensure that everything the Kami has commanded you from the beginning is executed without fail by tomorrow.'

While saying this, she again spread her arms wide in the shape of the character for 'one,' leaned back, stepped down from the alcove, and returned leisurely toward her bedroom.

King Garden had evil spirits injected into him from the tips of his toes by Chigusahime, and his state of mind suddenly underwent a complete transformation, leaving him almost in a state of possession by an evil Kami. The evil nine-tailed golden fox had successfully occupied Toruman Castle.

The Crown Prince and the Princess, having no way to refuse the strict orders of their parents, claimed they were setting out on a journey, prepared tens of thousands in gold, and dressed themselves as pilgrims. As the sun was setting, they headed toward the dwelling of Rēru and Māku, and stood at the gate, shaking a bell with a ringing sound.

Rēru and Māku had been guarding the rock cave together during the day, and at this very moment, the four men and women were in the middle of sharing a meal at the table.

The Crown Prince stood at the gate, shaking his bell and calling out, 'May we request entry? May we request entry?' whereupon Māku peered outside through a gap in the door.

Māku: 'Oh, a pilgrim couple. I do not know what your business is, but there is nothing for you here in such a slum, so please be off at once. There is no way we can put you up in such a cramped house at this hour, so we must decline.'

Crown Prince: 'Nay, I am by no means a suspicious person. As I am an acquaintance of Rēru-dono and Māku-dono, I pray you, open this door.'

Rēru: 'Oh, that spy fellow has come in disguise; this is a disaster. I must hide the Princess. Now, Princess-sama, I am sorry, but please step inside this cupboard for a moment.'

Teira: 'Ho ho ho, there is no need to be so flustered. It seems some sudden change has occurred within the castle, and Taishi-sama has come in disguise. That voice is undoubtedly Taishi-sama.'

While saying this, Teira slid open the dilapidated door with a rattling sound.

Teira: 'Oh, Taishi-sama, you have come at last.'

The Crown Prince merely replied, 'Hmm,' and entered the interior together with Chinrei.
