Chapter 10: 二 Teaching 聯合 [1777]

二教聯合〔1777〕

§ 1

Chigusahime was indignant at the treatment of her beloved Kyūbā, who had been excluded from the ranks of the ritual officials at the Harima Forest Saiten, while Terukuniwake had been appointed as the Saishiyu. Feigning illness, she shut herself deep within the Jiyōnai, and despite the Sanpai of Setsuteiri and Taishi Chiuin, she remained in her Ima, sulking and refusing to leave her bed.

Meanwhile, Kyūbā, intending to observe the Saiten casually, followed Setsuteiri to Harima Forest, only to find Terukuniwake acting as the Saishiyu, clad in Shinchō Seisau. The general Gunshifu whispered among themselves: 'What a Ritsupa Sendenshi he is; there could be no Sendenshi of such high Shintoku even within the Uraru Kyō. They say it is that Sendenshi who saved us from the recent Kokunan. That is why he is serving as the Saishiyu, standing ahead of the Ōsama at this festival. Is he not truly a magnificent Ikigami? And as for that Kyūbā of Sukobutsuen, he has been acting so haughty and arrogant until now, but look at his state today. Just look at that pathetic appearance. He is not even allowed to join the ranks of the ritual officials, nor is he permitted to offer the Tamagushi, is he?'

These whispers reached the sensitive eardrums of Kyūbā, who, in a fit of rage, suddenly lost his composure. He trampled upon the solemn ritual and knocked the crown from the head of the Saishiyu. Through the decisive judgment of Taishi Chiuin, he was immediately bound and cast into prison.

Chigusahime, who knew nothing of these events, waited anxiously, feeling as if a single moment were a thousand years, longing for Kyūbā to return as soon as possible. Meanwhile, Gāden Ō returned to the room next to the throne, chanting 'Banzai' three times along with Terukuniwake, Terukō, the brave general Jiyanku, Chinrei, Harisu, and others. They discussed the various circumstances of the day's Saiten, and while in the presence of Setsuteiri, they spoke with great interest about how Kyūbā had disturbed the sacred grounds and had been promptly bound and thrown into prison, all while a Naorai banquet was being held.

The suspicious Chigusahime, who had been hiding in the room next to the throne and listening intently, became frantic upon hearing that Kyūbā had committed violence and been thrown into prison. She burned with the fire of resentment, plotting to somehow remove Terukuniwake, and if possible, to find fault with him and cast him into prison to avenge Kyūbā. Setsuteiri, in high spirits, thanked Terukuniwake for the gift of rebirth and even praised him as the savior of the Toruman nation. The King, holding a cup in his hand, began to sing a celebratory song.

Toruman-koku is the land of God, From the distant ages of the gods, The divine spirit of the Uraru-no-hiko, Has been enshrined and worshipped to rule the world, How precious is the history that has come down to us. Yet, in this world, Yamata-no-orochi, ugly foxes, And the spirits of rampaging demons, Are gradually raising their heads. Brahmanism, the Suko sect, And a hundred other evil religions, Have invaded our divine land, And are tearing apart the souls of our people, While the sincere heart of reverence for gods, ancestors, and patriotism, Has withered away. The people think only of self-interest and greed, Caring only for the treasures of the surface, Sharpening the fangs and claws of jackals and wolves, How sad it is to see them scavenging. The rich grow ever wealthier and more prosperous, The poor grow ever more destitute, The voices of resentment fill the nation, Communism, Anarchism, Socialism, And other evil ideologies from outside the country, Have invaded, and the hearts of men, Have become like those of wild beasts. Taking advantage of such a situation, The evil religion called the Sukobutsuen sect, A branch of Brahmanism opened by Daikokushu, Has spread like a wildfire, and there is no help for it. Kyūbā of the Sukobutsuen sect, Received the command of Daikokushu, And established a base within our nation, Working day and night to dominate our officials and people. He wielded violent power, But still unsatisfied, he conspired with Ōashiwake of the Brahmans, To seize the Toruman nation, And to trample upon the seven thousand nations of the Moon Kingdom one by one, How hateful his ambition to achieve such a goal. He led the army of Ōashiwake, To threaten the royal family, And in this unexpected war, He caused the people to suffer and struggle, And with such brazenness, he snuck into our castle, Only to be swept up by the eloquence of Chigusahime, And in a sudden turn of events, he changed his course to become an ally of the castle, What a truly mysterious magician he is, He is a wretched, wretched, wretched fellow.

千草姫は、最愛のキユーバーがハリマの森の祭典に於て、祭官の列外に立たせられ、照国別をして斎主となした其処置に憤慨し、病と称して城内深く閉ぢ籠り、刹帝利、チウイン太子の参拝あるにも拘らず、吾居間にだだをこねて寝込んで仕舞つた。一方キユーバーは何気なく祭典を見むとて刹帝利に随ひ、ハリマの森に来て見れば、新調の盛装を身に纒ふて、照国別が斎主をやつて居る。一般の群集は、

『何と立派な宣伝使だ、ウラル教の中にもあんな神徳の高い宣伝使は無からう。今度の国難を救ふて下さつたのもあの宣伝使ださうだ。それだから、今度のお祭に王様の先に立つて斎主を勤めて居られるのだ。ほんとに偉い生神ぢやないか。又スコブツエンのキユーバーなんて、今迄偉さうに威張つて居やがつたが、今日の態つたら無いぢやないか。あの見窄らしい態を見い。祭官の端にも加へて貰はず、玉串の献上もさしてよう貰ひやがらぬぢやないか』

などと囁く声が耳敏きキユーバーの皷膜に響いたので、キユーバーは立腹の余り俄に逆上し、荘重なる儀式を蹂躙し、斎主の冠を擲きおとしたのである。それがチウイン太子の英断によつて、彼は即座にふん縛られ、忽ち牢獄に繋がれて了つた。こんな事とは夢にも知らぬ千草姫は、キユーバーの一刻も早く帰れかしと、一時千秋の思ひをして待ち焦れて居た。そこへガーデン王、照国別、照公、勇将ジヤンク、チンレイ、ハリス其他の面々と共に万歳を三唱し乍ら、玉座の次の間に帰つて来た。さうして今日の祭典の状況につき種々と談じ合ひ、特にキユーバーが聖場を乱し、忽ち縛につき牢獄に投ぜられた事なぞ迄、刹帝利の前にて興味を湧しつつ話し乍ら、直会の宴が開かれて居た。疑ひ深き千草姫は玉座の次の間に身を潜め、耳を澄まして聞いて居たが、キユーバーが乱暴を働き牢獄に投ぜられたと聞くより気が気でならず、どうとかして照国別を排除し、出来得べくんば彼に難癖をつけ牢獄に投じ、キユーバーの仇を打たむと瞋恚の炎を焦して居る。刹帝利は上機嫌で、照国別に再生の恩を謝し、トルマン国の救世主と迄称揚した。王は先づ盃を手にし乍ら祝歌を謡ふ。

『トルマン国は神の国 遠き神代の昔より ウラルの彦の神霊を 斎き祭りて世を治め 来りし事の尊さよ さはさり乍ら世の中に 八岐大蛇や醜狐 荒ぶる鬼の身魂等が 漸く首をもたげつつ バラモン教やスコ教や 其他百の邪宗教 吾神国に襲ひ来て 国人達の魂を 支離滅裂に乱しつつ 敬神尊祖愛国の 誠心は消え果てて 唯国人は我利我欲 形の上の宝のみ 豺狼の爪牙を磨きつつ あさり居るこそ悲しけれ

富者は益々富み栄え 貧者は益々窮乏し 怨嗟の声は国内に 漲り果ててコンミュニズム アナアキズムやソシヤリズム その他所在悪思想 国の外より襲ひ来て 人の心はまちまちに 野獣の如くなりにけり かかる所へつけ込んで 大黒主の開きたる バラモン教の別派なる スコブツエン宗と云ふ邪教 燎原を焼く火の如く 蔓延したるぞ是非なけれ スコブツエン宗のキユーバーは 大黒主の命を受け 吾国内に根拠をば 定めて日に夜に活躍し 吾官民を睥睨し

暴威を揮ひ居たりしが 尚ほ飽き足らずバラモンの 大足別と結託し トルマン国を手に入れて 七千余国の月の国 片つ端から蹂躙し 野望を達成せむものと 企らみ居たる憎らしさ 大足別の軍隊を 率ゐて王家を威喝なし 思ひもかけぬ此度の 軍を開き国民を 苦め難ませヅウヅウしくも 吾城内に忍び入り 千草の姫の弁舌に 捲き込まれては急激に 進路を転じ城内の 味方とかはりし早業は 実に不思議の手品師だ へぐれのへぐれのへぐれむしや

§ 2

He is the Mitama of Hegure Jinjiya. He appeared within the Jiyōnai, and though he feigns loyalty on the Heumen, he is a fellow who can by no means be let off guard. For that reason, in this Saiten, the Sendenshi of Ananai, Terukuniwake, who drove him away and helped our country, has kindly requested to serve as the Saishiyu. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. The Goshinriki of the God of Ural protects the Ananai-kyō. Protected by the high Miizu of Ōkuniharutachiōmikami and the Ōkami of Kamususanō, the Futahashira of Samori and Umori shall be the guardian gods of the country, forever and ever, for Chiyo and Yachiyo, and for Tokoshihe. How noble it is that they shall dwell.

The Tsukasa of Kyūbā, upon seeing this, became painfully enraged. With extreme violence, he climbed up to the altar, instantly knocked down the crown of the master, and continued to spit out evil words and violent language, which was truly hateful. By the command of Prince Chiuin, the Tsukasa of Jiyanku appeared, bound the wicked monk, and threw him into prison for the time being. Until he repents of his past wrongs from the very bottom of his heart, I shall keep him locked away. Ah, how valiant, how valiant. From today, the Toruman country shall observe both the Ananai-kyō and the Ural-kyō, and by serving the Ōkami of the great foundation of the world, we shall lead the people. How reliable it is. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. How grateful is the virtue of God, and how noble is the grace of God.

Terukuniwake chants in a calm voice: 'The Sendenshi of Ananai, Terukuniwake, also in the past, wishing to propagate the teachings by upholding the doctrines of the God of Ural, was given the name Umehiko and crossed over to Ryūgū-shū. My three years of hardship were in vain, and having no choice, I entrusted my body to a boat without a shelf with five others. While crossing the vast ocean, when I arrived at the sea of Perushiya, I was troubled by storms and raging waves. In that moment of life and death, I was saved from my precarious life by the Kototama of Hinodewake, the Sendenshi of Ananai. Serving the teachings of the God of Ananai here, I was granted the name Terukuniwake.

Leaving behind the sacred site of the Saienkan of Ubusunayama, I have propagated the teachings, and now, my joining the imperial army to save the Toruman country from its crisis is also a pure connection drawn by the Ōkami who protects the Ural-kyō. If one looks from the human world, the way of Ural and the teachings of Ananai may appear as two, but the true God who created the world is one pillar; both are the same way of God. Therefore, just because I am in the teachings of the God of Ananai, there is no reason I cannot participate in the Saiten of the Ural shrine. The Kototama of the Tsukasa of Kyūbā is a delusion of the world, extreme in its narrow-mindedness.

Now, from here on, I shall make all teachings one, propagate the virtue of the God of Aizen throughout the world and the countries in the four directions, and serve the divine work of the fulfillment of the divine government; this is my resolve. Please grant this, O Kshatriya. Before Prince Chiuin, I reveal all of my sincerity and offer my vow. I pray that the Toruman country may be forever, that this royal family may become the parents of the people for ten thousand generations, and that this country created by God may be protected to flourish eternally and firmly. I pray that you may open it.'

Prince Chiuin chants again: 'This divine country shall flourish and prosper forever without limit, like the stars in the heavenly sky, and like the countless grains of sand on the beach. Day and night, the people shall multiply, and barley, beans, and millet shall ripen well. The Kiyokawa river, flowing purely through the center of the Toruman country, shall have many fish multiplying, and in the fields and mountains, birds and beasts shall dwell in great numbers, making the wealth of the country the pride of the world. How auspicious it is. However, the God of Ōkuninushi, having taken the Toruman country, the land of abundant Mizuho, and intending to create a second base, has brought in the Sukobutsuen sect to disturb our country. He has plotted a great conspiracy and dispatched the wicked monk Kyūbā.

That he has opened the beginning of this disturbance is a grave matter. The evil deeds of that Kyūbā are a great sin that even heaven and earth cannot forgive. However, since we are in the teachings of God, I thought to release him after confirming his repentance, and to entrust him once again as a messenger of God. But his wicked act of deceiving my mother and stealing her heart cannot be forgiven. I consulted with the Sendenshi of Ananai, Terukuniwake, and the Terukuni-tsukasa, asking how we should dispose of his body. When I prayed to the Ōkami, he appeared in a dream and commanded me to bind Kyūbā tomorrow without fail.'

へぐれ神社の身霊だろ 彼は城内に現はれて 表面忠義をよそほへど 仲々油断のならぬ奴 それゆへ此度の祭典に 彼をば退け吾国を 助け給ひし三五の 照国別の宣伝使 御苦労乍ら斎主をば 願ひ奉りし次第なり あゝ惟神々々 ウラルの神の御神力 三五教を守ります 大国治立大御神 神素盞嗚の大神の 高き御稜威に守られて 左守右守の二柱 いよいよ国の守り神 千代に八千代に永久に 納まりますぞ尊けれ

照国別は言葉静に謡ふ。

『三五教の宣伝使 照国別も其昔 ウラルの神の御教を 奉じて教を伝へむと 名も梅彦と賜はりて 竜宮洲に打渡り 三年の辛苦も水の泡 止むを得ずして六人が 棚なし船に身を任せ 大海原を渡りつつ ペルシヤの海に来て見れば 暴風怒濤に悩まされ 九死一生の其場合 三五教の宣伝使 日の出の別の言霊に 危き命を助けられ いよいよ茲に三五の 神の教に仕へつつ 神の御言を蒙りて 照国別と名を賜ひ

チウイン太子は又謡ふ。

『此神国は永久に 栄え栄えて限りなく 天津御空の星の如 浜の真砂の数知れず 日に夜に国民繁殖し 穏麦豆粟よく実り トルマン国の中心を 清く流るる清川は 魚類多く繁殖し 野にも山にも鳥獣 数多住ひて国の富 世界に冠たる目出度さよ 然るに大黒主の神 瑞穂の秋の豊の国 トルマン国を奪ひ取り 第二の根拠を作らむと スコブツエン宗を持ち込みて 吾国内を乱さむと 大陰謀を企らみて 妖僧キユーバーを派遣なし

Terukuniwake: 'Since it has come to this, to anger the heart of Hahauhe would be clearer than seeing fire. Yet, when I think of the Kokuka according to the decree of Kami, my position cannot be forgiven. Please consent, Chichigimi. I, a Sendenshi of Terukuniwake, hereby offer my Chinshiya for the reason of binding Kyūbā. Ananai-kyō and Ural-kyō, here the Riyōkyō shall unite. Not to mention Toruman-koku, to the Kokumin of the Shichisenyokoku of the Moon, without omission or failure, I shall teach and transmit the precious Mioshihe of Kami, and serve the Daishingefu of the Shinkoku achievement as quickly as Ichinichi. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara.'

Terukuniwake: 'Before the Mimahe of Kami, I reveal my Magokoro and offer this vow. With my Magokoro contained, I offer this prayer.'

Just as he sang this, with the force of a Yashiya, splashing red across her entire face, and appearing with Chimanako, the one who sat down in a Funzen manner to the right of the Setsuteiri was the Ōhi Chigusahime. The entire assembly was wrapped in a strange and peculiar atmosphere due to the sudden appearance of Chigusahime.

斯く歌ふ折しも、夜叉の如き勢、満面朱を濺ぎ、血眼になつて現はれ来り、刹帝利の右に憤然と座を占めたのは王妃千草姫であつた。一座は千草姫の突然の出現によつて一種異様な空気に包まれて了つた。
