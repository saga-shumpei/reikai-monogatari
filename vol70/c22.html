Chapter 22: 優秀 Beauty [1789]

優秀美〔1789〕

§ 1

Kyōmusōkan Junk shut himself in a hitoma, troubled by the unusual conduct of Chigusahime, and the disappearance of Taishi Chuin, Ōjiyo, Teira, and Harisu also became a source of worry for Junk. Without knowing when, he fell into a doze. Just then, a strange Shinjin of beautiful yōshiyoku appeared from nowhere,

Junk, Junk

and shook his shoulders. When Junk was startled and opened his eyes, the same Shinjin he had seen in his dream was sitting solemnly on a chair before his eyes, smiling, and said,

Shinjin: 'I am an angel of Kototamawake, a Tennin of the Daiichireigoku. I truly admire the fact that you are worrying about the genjiyō of the Toruman country and are troubled in your shintan. Your shisei has reached Ten, and I have descended to assist in your shingefu as an angel; do not doubt it at all.'

Junk: 'Yes, as a person of gudon, I am at a loss in my shintai and sunk in iushiu, so I am truly grateful for the arrival of a noble god. I humbly pray that you will grant me your protection.'

Angel: 'The members including Taishi Chuin, whom you are worried about, as well as Ōjiyo Chinrei, Teira, and Harisu, have been kept in a certain place for a while under the protection of God, but since the time for the decisive reform of the religious administration has arrived, I shall let you meet them soon. You must remain the Kyōmusōkan of the Toruman country to the end, assist Taishi Chuin, and carry out correct religious education. Also, as the Left Guardian and Right Guardian for Taishi Chuin have already been decided, you must follow the Taishi's opinions. Make Shunkō, a disciple of Terukuniwake, the husband of Ōjiyo Chinrei, enshrine him together with the Sango no Ōkami and the God of Uraru in the temple of the Harima forest, make Shunkō the Kannushi, and have him serve forever with Chinrei.'

Junk: 'Yes, thank you for guiding me in everything. God... I ask you with fear, but it seems that Kyōō Garden has an abnormality in his spirit, his words and deeds have suddenly changed, and this old retainer is truly having difficulty; will Kyōō return to his original sanity?'

Angel: 'He is possessed by an evil spirit that is a part of the Yamata no Orochi and his spirit is in confusion, so let him rest in a quiet place for a while. Also, Chigusahime has already left this world, and the spirit of Takahime has possessed her remains, calling herself the living shrine of Hinodewake, performing ugly behavior, and acting violently; this is entirely the work of the spirit of the evil nine-tailed golden fox, so be very careful.'

Junk: 'Yes, I understand. If it is not Chigusahime-sama, this old retainer has much to think about. I humbly pray for your protection so that there will be no mistakes.'

Angel: 'Be especially careful of Chigusahime, who is the return of Takahime. Farewell.'

No sooner had he said this than he suddenly made his divine form disappear. Junk thanked the divine grace and sang a hymn.

The sun has set and there is no road, The snow lies deep on the fields and mountains, There is no way to walk as it piles up. The sun god who illuminates the head of the traveler, Who is troubled on his path, Clears away the darkness and melts the piled snow, And has opened the great road for me to go. Ah, how grateful, how grateful. My sincere heart devoted to the country, God must have accepted it. On this evening when I was at my wit's end, Solemnly appearing by my pillow, You spoke your words. Those words are not a dream, An appearance of the true God. High is the Mount Sumeru I look up to, Are you Konohanahime-mikoto, Who protects us, Or are you the god Kototamawake?

I do not know who you are, But you are a majestic Mizu-no-mitama. How happy I am that you have moistened my dried spirit, And filled my body with the blood of purity, And saved me. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara, Before the Lord Ōkami, I humbly thank you, I rejoice and thank you.

§ 2

Just as they were singing, Taishi Chiwin returned in high spirits, accompanied by Terukuniwake, Terukō, Rēru, Māku, Teira, Harisu, and the princesses Chinrei, Teruman, and Harukō.

Jiyanku, upon seeing the Taishi, clung to him and, squeezing out his voice through tears, said:

Jiyanku: 'Ah, Taishisama, how wonderful that you have returned.'

Having said this, he was so overcome with emotion that he could not utter another word.

The Taishi, thinking of how this old retainer had stood alone, guarding the lonely castle amidst the demons, felt tears welling up in his eyes; choking back tears of joy, he wiped his eyes with a handkerchief and remained looking down for a while, unable to speak.

Terukuniwake: 'Jiyankusama, please rejoice. The Taishisama is, as you see, in good health. And as a preparation for the great reform of the religious administration, he has already appointed the chief ministers and has returned, so I hope that you will revere the Taishisama as the new Kyōō, request the retirement of the Kyōōsama, drive out the evil spirit of Chigusahime, and devote yourself to the religious administration.'

Jiyanku: 'Yes, thank you for everything. Even just now, a noble angel appeared at my bedside and gave me various instructions regarding the foundation of the religious teachings. I humbly ask for your continued guidance.'

Terukuni: 'I believe that the issuance of the Decree of Benevolence with such great resolution this time is truly a splendid thing, even from the perspective of renewing the hearts of the people. In connection with this, I hope that you will quickly release the wicked monk Kyūbā, whom the Kyōōsama Chiwin has kept confined in the rock cave of Araigadake.'

Jiya: 'Indeed, in truth, I did not know the whereabouts of Kyūbā, so I have taken no action, but now that his location is known, I shall release him immediately.'

Terukuni: 'With this, the dust of the nation has been swept away, and the succession of the Kyōōsama Chiwin has been happily settled. Now, let us perform the divine judgment of Chigusahime. Jiyankudono, everyone, please accompany me to the living quarters of Chigusahime.'

The group nodded and, following behind Terukuniwake, crept forward with hushed footsteps toward the living quarters of Chigusahime.

Inside the room, a woman's voice was heard:

Voice: 'O Queen, please, at least spare me this.'

Chigusahime: 'No matter what you say, did you not swear absolute obedience? I must cut off your breasts with these red-hot tongs, offer them to the Great God, and have you reveal the whereabouts of Kyūbā. To think that one who is called Mokuren would be so cowardly and cling to her life at the very end; what a pitiful face you have. No matter how much you run or flee, once I have locked you in this room, you shall not be saved. Now, close your eyes in resignation. If you are to become a sacrifice to the gods, that should be an honor for you as well.'

Moku: 'I will obey any order you give, but please, let me see my daughter Teira just once. After that, I will perform any task you require.'

Chigusa: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho. You say such things, intending to escape from this place, spread slander about Chigusahime, and attempt to diminish my divine virtue. I am not such a Chigusahime as to be taken in by such schemes.'

With a murderous aura on her gentle face, she heated the metal fire tongs until they turned white, and just as she was about to apply them to the breasts of the wailing Mokuren, Terukuniwake broke down the door from the outside with all his might.

With a single shout of 'Uun!', he glared at her, and Chigusahime, flustered and panicked, revealed a fox's tail that had split into eight from around her rear, swinging it wildly as she fled through the skylight and vanished into nowhere.

Incidentally, Teruman, by the discovery of the new Kyōō, was appointed as the head of the guardian monks, and the people sang the praises of the new Kyōō's divine government, leading the Toruman nation, which had been in complete disarray until then, to manifest a small heaven on earth. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemase.
