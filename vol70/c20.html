§ 1

In the Rusutaku of Rail and Mark in Shigure Town, the four, Taishi Chiuin, Chinrei, Teira, and Harisu, are quietly bowing their heads and holding a secret meeting.

Teira: 'If I may, Taishisama, have Rail-sama and Mark-sama not finally failed and been captured? When I went to draw water, I was startled to see an extra edition dropped there.'

Chiu: 'What, they failed? Oh, this is troublesome. I cannot just sit here. I must go to where Junk is and think of some way to rescue the two.'

Teira: 'Please stop, it is dangerous. It is Chigusahime possessed by the evil fox of Kinmaukiubi, so who knows what kind of nandai she will impose to throw Taishisama into raugoku again and take your inochi.'

Chiu: 'Even if you say that, I cannot just leave the shishi of the kokuka to be migoro. I do not care what happens to me from here on. I cannot bear to zashi the saunan of the two. I intend to exhaust every shiyudan to try and save them. Please, you three, try not to go outside and wait for me.'

Chinrei: 'Anisama, please stop such reckless actions with your precious omi. If by any chance danger approaches your omi and you end up losing your inochi, the Toruman nation will truly fall into eternal darkness. In this world where the hearts of the people are so restless and the jackals, wolves, tigers, and dragons are running wild, no matter how great the authority of Taishisama may be, there is nothing you can do. The current heavy-ranking monks think of nothing but their own greed. They are all wicked people who have been deceived by the monster Chigusahime and have had golden bits placed in their mouths, so even for Taishisama, they will show no mercy for the sake of their own advancement. They are even trying to move Junk-sama, the Superintendent of Religious Affairs. You must not go to such a dangerous place. Please, at least stop this.'

She clings to the sleeve of the Taishi and weeps.

Chiu: 'Oh, this is troublesome. In that case, shall I leave it to your opinion and wait for a while?'

As he says this, two or three men come stomping in, and while peeking at the doorway, one says,

Man: 'Hey, it seems someone is here. Hey, who are you? It seems the leaders Rail and Mark were unfortunately captured, but who are you?'

Chiu: 'Oh, are you brothers? Well, come in. Actually, I also heard that the boss was captured, and I came here with the members of the Kōjōkai to think about the situation, but since none of the others had come, I was acting as the commander of the Rusutaku. Who are you?'

Man: 'Me? I am one of the three, Tar, Har, and Kēsu. Seeing the extra edition, I thought this was terrible, and thinking that someone must have come to pay a visit, I rushed over without delay, but who exactly are you?'

Chiu: 'Me? I am the cherished younger brother of Rail's older brother. I had heard your names from before, but because I was entrusted with a special mission, I had hidden it from you. Now that it has come to this, I cannot remain silent, but I suppose you are doing some kind of movement?'

Tar: 'Well, actually, just as we saw the extra edition, while the comrades were gathering in the Forest of Kurusu and consulting on how to get the boss back, hundreds of monk-guards appeared and surrounded us ten and twenty layers deep, so we could do nothing, and the comrades were scattered in all directions. Truly, for our movement, the imprisonment of the boss is like a bee stinging a crying face. It is like a lotus root for a thin child. There is simply no way to handle it at the moment. Hey, Anisama, do you not have any good ideas?'

Chiu: 'Wait, wait, there is no use in panicking like that. Now that the heads of the acting monks have become extremely sharp, we cannot even say a single thing in a loud voice. There is no other way but to leave it to the course of events for a while and wait for the right time to take them back. Well, take it easy.'

That night, they spent the time talking in low voices, piling things up and knocking them down, until they fell asleep. The next morning, from early on, the three women were busy cooking and sweeping the area, when two women with infants strapped to their backs, dressed in proper attire, stood at the gate.

Woman: 'Excuse me, I would like to ask a question, is this the house of Rēru and Māku?'

Tei: 'If that is the case, you said you are Masako-sama and Karuko-sama, are you not the okusama of Rēru-sama and Māku-sama?'

Masako: 'Hai, I am the nyōbō of Rēru. This person here is the okusama of Māku-sama. My husband does such strange things, calling it an improvement movement or something, that even at my place, the bansō are keeping watch. It is truly a shinsetsu to be troubled by this.'

Chiu: 'Ya, are you truly the okusama? Now, please, do sit over here. There is no need to worry so much. They will likely be pardoned and return before long.'

No matter how much I offer my opinion, he brushes it off with a single word, saying it is not a matter for a woman to know, and he will not listen to a single word his nyōbō says, and so we have finally met with such a disaster. Our relatives and brothers also speak sternly, urging us to sever our ties and come home, but a bond that cannot be cut has formed between the two of us, and thinking it pitiful to leave my dear child in the hands of a stepmother, I have endured until today with tears in my eyes, but I can endure no more. Therefore, I have come here thinking I would like to have the ties severed once and for all.'

Masako: 'That may be true in one sense, but living with such a husband, I cannot live with peace of mind in this world. Are you also a companion of Rēru?'

Rēru: 'In any case, this place is likely inconvenient. Let us go to the Kushiyakuniken Goten, ahahaha.'

Laughing as they returned, the six Danjiyo gathered their heads together here and opened a conference for the Shinnaikiyokusoshiki. Even the voices of the sparrows today sounded somehow valiant, heard as Chiyochiyo.
