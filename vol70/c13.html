Chapter 13: 喃悶題 [1780]

喃悶題〔1780〕

§ 1

Chigusahime has summoned Mokuren, the wife of Samorinokami, her daughter Teirahime, and Harisu, the daughter of Umori, to be near her Shitsuka, and is exhausting every effort in her Kuwantai to make them Yakurōchiu.

The Sannin, Mokuren, Teira, and Harisu, were fearfully surrounding a table in the Goten of Chigusahime, receiving the heartfelt Chisō of Chigusahime.

Chigusahime turned to the Ichidō and said,

Chigusahime: 'Now, Mokuren-sama, because you are the Okusama of Samori-sama who gave his Ichimei for the sake of the Kokuka, even though you are a woman, to the Toruman nation you are a Chiuseki of the state, a person who must be treated as more Taisetsu than anyone else; therefore, I ask you to continue to exert your Jifubun of strength for the sake of the nation together with me from now on.'

Mokuren: 'Hai, these are words of gratitude from Hime-sama, but as you can see, I am already of Raurei and am of no use, so I am ashamed.'

Chigusahime: 'Now, now, why do you say such weak-spirited things again? Are you not the Mibaujin of Samorinokami, who was the Daiichininshiya in the Toruman nation? Since your husband has met his Uchijini, you must fulfill the reality of a Kenboriyōsai and act even more than your husband did. From now on, this Chigusahime will grant you infinite divine virtue, so please work with all your might for Chigusahime. That, in short, will also be for the sake of the King and for the general good of the Toruman nation.'

Mokuren: 'Hai, thank you very much. An old woman like me may be of no use, but if it is for the service of Hime-sama, I have no reason to refuse. If you command me in any way, I shall certainly serve with all the strength I possess.'

Chigusahime: 'Nay, satisfied, satisfied; that is the Mokuren-dono, wife of Samori, that I know. This Chigusahime has changed somewhat from the Chigusahime of until now, so please keep that in mind. This Chigusahime is by no means insane. From this day forward, I am the Savior of the three thousand worlds, the spiritual body of the great Miroku of the bottom-most bedrock, the heavenly being of the First Spirit Kingdom, and the living shrine of the Hinodewake. Until today, I have been involved in internal affairs as the Queen of King Garden of the Toruman nation, but I can no longer do such small things. As the spiritual body of the great Miroku, the root of the root of heaven, now that I have appeared on this earth, I am the activity of the Hinodewake who will perform the Tatekae-Tateraoshi of the three thousand worlds, not to mention the seven thousand and more nations of the Moon Kingdom. If you do not also brace yourself considerably, the great ambition of this world, the Tatekae-Tateraoshi, will be delayed.'

At these unexpected words of Chigusahime, Mokuren, Teira, and Harisu were all stunned, looked at each other, clicked their tongues, and stared in amazement.

Chigusahime: 'Now, Harisu, were you not just clicking your tongue? Is what I say so funny? Why do you not listen seriously to what the God says?'

Harisu: 'Hai, I am truly filled with awe. Although I had thought until this very moment that you were only the Queen, I was bewildered and lost in doubt as to the truth or falsehood of what you said, regarding your spiritual body as the great Miroku-sama, which is too vast for our small hearts to comprehend.'

Chigusahime: 'Otso-ho-ho-ho, that is surely how it would be. If one compares the Savior of the three thousand worlds with the daughter of the Umorinokami of the Toruman nation, there is a greater distance than between an elephant and a germ, so it is no wonder you do not understand. However, what do you think of this Chigusahime? You surely do not think I am a madman, do you?'

Harisu: 'How could I possibly view such a precious Hime-sama as a madman?'

Chigusahime: 'Then, how do you consider this fleshly shrine of mine?'

Being pressed with questions in rapid succession,

Harisu: 'Hai, how could a mere germ like me possibly understand the affairs of the Queen, who possesses such high divine virtue, and the matters of a God as vast as the universe? I have no Kotoba other than to say that I am truly grateful and filled with awe.'

Chigusahime: 'Indeed, indeed, that is so. As you say, since it is not a matter that can be understood by the status of a human, once you have believed this Chigusahime to be the Futobashira of Miroku and the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake, you will surely swear absolute obedience in all things, will you not?'

Hari: 'Yes, I swear absolute obedience. If it is the word of Ikimiyasama, even if you were to command me to climb a mountain upside down, I would climb it to show you.'

Chigusahime: 'Ho ho ho ho, as expected of the bereaved child of Umori, you are truly admirable. Since this Ikimiya is now appearing as the Savior of the Sanzensekai, I shall make you a splendid and unparalleled person in the Sanzensekai, and I shall entrust you with a task that will leave your name for Mangomatsudai...'

Hari: 'Yes, thank you very much. I humbly ask for your kind favor in all matters.'

Chigusahime: 'Mm, good, good. The Futobashira of the Great Miroku has certainly understood. Next, I would like to hear the opinion of Teira-dono, the daughter of Samori, as to how she regards this Ikimiya.'

Teira: 'Yes, no matter how I think about it, I cannot think of you as anything other than the Queen of the Garden King. You have spoken of the Hinodewake and the Miroku of the Sokotsuiwane, but as I have never heard of such things until today, I am lost in my heart as to the judgment of truth or falsehood.'

Chigusahime: 'To be lost in the judgment of truth or falsehood regarding the Kami, which is fearful even for the status of a human, what insolent words are these? For a human who cannot even see one step ahead to judge the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake, who sees through the Sanzensekai at a single glance, is an act of evil beyond the pale. With such thoughtless judgment, I shall not allow you to be called the daughter of Samori.'

Teira: 'Yes, I am humbled. I was so surprised by the suddenness of it all that I inadvertently spoke rudely. I beg you to reconsider and listen to me with your broad heart.'

Chigusahime: 'Mm, if you understand so obediently, then that is fine. It is natural that you do not understand the true form of this Ikimiya. No matter what is said, for the sake of saving the Sanzensekai, I have come through various and sundry hegure-hegure, and because of that, in the world of Kami, I am called the Hegure-no-Hegure-no-Hegure warrior, the Great Kami of the Hegure Shrine.'

Teira: 'Yes, thank you very much.'

Chigusahime: 'What are you thanking me for? Have you understood my words? Do not think that because I am a Queen, you can just say "Yes, yes" and "Certainly, certainly" to everything, and that it is enough to just obey on the surface; such sly thinking is useless. I am a living Kami who can see through to the very bottom of the hearts of the people.'

Teira: 'That is so. I shall never doubt you. I will only serve your purpose according to the heart of the Kami.'

Chigusahime: 'Indeed, as expected of the bereaved child of Samori, you understand things well. You will surely not disobey this Chigusahime, who is the meat-palace, even by a single word, will you? You will swear absolute obedience, will you not?'

Teira: 'Yes, I shall swear absolute obedience to whatever my master commands.'

Chigusahime: 'Hey, hey, what are you saying? I am pained by such a greeting to this Ikimiya as if I were a human. Why do you not say it is the command of Ikimiyasama, instead of calling it the order of a master, which is outrageous?'

Teira: 'Yes, I spoke rudely. If it is the command of Ikimiyasama, I will not shrink from any task. Even if it be in the midst of fire or at the bottom of the water, I will gladly accept your command.'

Chigusahime: 'Ho ho ho ho, well, well, how happy I am, the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake is satisfied. With Harihime who walks up mountains upside down when commanded, and Teira-dono who says she will gladly leap into fire or the bottom of the water, now that such a suicide squad has been formed, this Ikimiya of the Hinodewake is as firm as a great rock. In regard to this, what is the opinion of Mokuren-dono? I would like to hear it clearly.'

Moku: 'Without needing to be told, I swear absolute obedience.'

Chigusa: 'With absolute obedience, isn't your answer a bit dull? One would expect a more precise response, such as passing through fire, diving to the bottom of the water, or climbing a mountain upside down.'

Moku: 'Yes, if that is the case, if it is your Gomeirei, I shall become a sacrifice to Kami and offer my warm Chishiho.'

Chigusa: 'Hmm, good, good, you are indeed excellent. As expected of the Mibaujin of the Umorinokami, Chigusahime—no, the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake Kami—I am deeply impressed. Now, quickly, since the matter is settled, I, this Ikimiya, shall now issue my Goyō to both Teira and Harisu.'

Teira and Harisu both lowered their heads at once,

Teira and Harisu: 'Whatever the Gomeirei may be, we shall humbly accept it.'

Chigusa: 'Ho, ho, ho, as expected of wise women. Then I shall issue the Goyō immediately. As you have heard, Teira-dono, please go and search for the whereabouts of the Meisō Kyūbā-dono of the Sukobutsuen sect.'

Teira: 'Where should I go to find him? Please, Kamisama, give me your instructions.'

Chigusa: 'Is there any reason why one would know the direction when going out to search? It is precisely because I do not know where to go that I am telling you to go on a search.'

Teira: 'Yes, I understand. Then, I shall go immediately to inquire about his whereabouts. Please, I ask that you kindly obtain the consent of both the King and the Crown Prince as well.'

Chigusa: 'Hey, Teira, what kind of senseless thing are you saying? The King is merely the sovereign of the Toruman country, the father of three hundred thousand people. The Crown Prince is his successor, a mere dependent with no authority at this moment. How do you perceive the words of the Ikimiya of the three thousand worlds, who saves the spirits of five billion six hundred seventy million people?'

Teira: 'Yes, I humbly apologize.'

And leaving this place in a hurry,

Teira: 'Everyone, if that is the case, farewell.'

She left these words as a greeting and attempted to depart. Her mother Mokuren said,

Mokuren: 'Hey, Teira-hime, even though it is the Gomeirei of the Ikimiya-sama, since you are the daughter of the Sashimori who serves King Gāden, it would be best to greet the King once before you go to search for Kyūbā-dono. As your mother, I shall give you one word of caution.'

Chigusa: 'Hey, hey, Mokuren, what kind of senseless thing are you saying? Did Teira not say that she would render absolute obedience to what this Ikimiya says?'

Moku: 'Yes, I have said something truly inexcusable. Teira, quickly, come, go now.'

While saying this, she did not speak the words, but with her eyes she conveyed, 'You must speak to the King first.'

Teira understood the heart of her mother Mokuren, and with an indifferent air, said,

Teira: 'If that is the case, I shall now go on the search. Ikimiya-sama, please be at ease.'

And with that, she left the place quickly.

Chigusa: 'Otso, ho, ho, ho, my, as expected of the great Teira-dono. Hey, Mokuren, rejoice. The one who has made the Goyō of the Hinodewake Kami, the great pillar of Miroku at the bottom of the rock, her first priority is your daughter Teira. Now, quickly, offer your thanks to Kamisama.'

Mokuren, having no choice, put her palms together and prayed silently toward the heavens.

Chigusa: 'Hey, hey, this is far too incomprehensible, isn't it? Mokuren, where are you bowing to? What is the use of bowing to the empty sky? The Miroku Kami who dwells in heaven has now descended to earth and is present right here, is he not? When I say to bow to Kami, I mean to bow to this Ikimiya. Truly, you are an incomprehensible creature.'

Mokuren thought in her heart, 'Eh, this mad woman, what is she spewing? How ridiculous,' but such is the sorrow of a subordinate, she did not show it on her face and said,

Mokuren: 'Yes, was that so? I am such a foolish person, to think that I did not realize a magnificent Kamisama was appearing right before my eyes; what a fool I am, I am disgusted with myself. Then, if you will excuse me, Ikimiya-sama.'

千草『コレコレ、あまり訳が分らなさすぎるぢやないか。モクレン其方は、どこを拝んで居るのぢや。空虚なる大空を拝んで何になる。天にまします大みろくの神は今や地上に降臨し、此処に御座るぢやないか。神を拝めと申すのは此生宮を拝めと云ふのだよ。ても扨も、訳の分らぬ代物だなア』

モクレンは心の中にて、「エーこの狂人女郎、何を吐しやがる。馬鹿らしい」とは思へども、そこが主従の悲しさ、色にも出さず、

『ハイ、左様で御座りましたか。何分愚鈍の妾、現在目の前に結構な神様が御出現遊ばして御座るのに気がつかないとは、何と云ふ馬鹿だらうかと、吾乍ら呆れはてて御座ります。然らば御免下さいませ、生宮様』

§ 4

With that, she performed Sanpaikiuhai and Hakushiyu.

Chigusahime became increasingly Tokui, and while exposing her haughty face, she said, 'Ya, Zenzai, Zenzai. You are the very first person to recognize this Ikimiya as Kami; you must never, ever change your Shinkau. Now, since it has been decided thus, I shall dismiss you, Mokuren-dono. You may return to your home at your Zuii and take Kiusoku. I have a special Orii and Tokubetsu Goyō to attend to toward Haris; come to my Ima. I shall entrust you with the Mirokujiyōjiyu Goyō, which is unparalleled in the Ketsukō, Ketsukō Sanzensekai.'

Haris: 'Hai, I shall follow your command and attend.'

She was then accompanied to the Ima of Chigusahime. Chigusahime shut the door tightly, closed the windows on all Shihau7, and lowering her voice, said, 'Here, Haris-dono, listen well, for this Ikimiya is about to entrust you with a special, Daidaidai Himitsu Goyō.'

Haris: 'Hai, I shall listen with reverence. Whatever the Goyō may be, if it is within my power.'

Chigusa: 'Ya, Haris-dono, it is nothing other than this. I hear that you are the greatest Bibau in the Turman country. If you use that Bibau as a shield to steal the heart of Taishi Chiwin and cause him to fall into the abyss of love, I shall make you the consort of Taishi Chiwin and make you the flower of this Turman castle. How is it? Are you happy? Surely you can have no Fusoku with that.'

Haris: 'I am overwhelmed, not knowing what to say. Such an order—how could I, a mere subject, dare to speak of such a bold matter to the Taishi-sama, especially as a woman? In the first place, there is a great gap in our social standing. Furthermore, I am the only daughter of the Uemori-shi, and I must inherit the Uemori house. I humbly beg your forgiveness for this one thing alone.'

Chigusa: 'Here, here, Haris, such hesitation is not needed at all. You are the only one in heaven and on earth of the Uemori bloodline; indeed, you must inherit the house. That being the case, is this not a perfect, tailor-made story for you? If you successfully lure Chiwin into love, I shall let you have him as your husband. How happy you must be.'

Haris: 'To bestow the Taishi-sama, who is the successor of the Turman country, upon the house of Uemori is equivalent to the overturning of heaven and earth; King Garden would never permit it. Moreover, there is no reason for the Taishi-sama to abandon his position of high importance and become an adopted son in a subject's house. Even if the bloodline of the Uemori house were to end with my generation, it cannot be exchanged for the royal house. I humbly beg your forgiveness for this matter alone.'

Chigusa: 'Here, here, Haris-dono, did you not say that if it were the command of this Ikimiya, you would even climb a mountain upside down? With the roots of your tongue not yet dry, this change of heart as if you had turned your palm—I, Chigusahime, the Ikimiya, will not accept such a thing.'

Haris: 'Hai, there is no help for it. But once I have, by my power, caused the Taishi-sama to fall in love, what will you do about the successor to the royal house? That is what I cannot help but worry about.'

Chigusa: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, indeed, that is reasonable, very reasonable. As a human heart, your sincerity is truly beyond reproach. However, now that the great pillar of Miroku of the Soko-tsu-iwane, who freely governs the Sanzensekai, has appeared, the plan is to make those who are spiritual parents and children the successors. You need not worry about such things at all. Because your spirit and Taishi Chiwin are a couple in spirit, and because the Ikimiya of Hinodewake has investigated, investigated, and thoroughly investigated this in the Kamikai, I am saying this to you; therefore, please exert all your power. Success is surely without doubt.'

Haris was a female warrior who, together with the Taishi, led a great army and troubled the enemy forces. She admired the Taishi's appearance and courage from the bottom of her heart. However, she had not the slightest ambition to have him as a husband or to become his wife. Unable to refuse the words of Chigusahime, and wishing to escape from this situation for the time being, she played with words that were not in her heart, met the intentions of Chigusahime for a while, and showed a happy face. Chigusahime, in a state of satisfaction, said,

Chigusa: 'Ya, Haris-dono, splendid, splendid, I shall pray for your success. Now that you have agreed to this, the Goyō is finished for today. From here, return home, adorn yourself with all the finery you have, spare no rouge, powder, or oil, and prepare to act without any oversight.'

Haris said, 'Hai, thank you,' and after bowing politely, she left the place in a hurry.
