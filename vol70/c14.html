Chapter 14: 賓民 Cavern [1781]

賓民窟〔1781〕

§ 1

Receiving the command of Chigusahime and intending to head out for the sōsaku of Kyūbā, Teirahime thought to meet with the Ōsama by including the intentions of her mother Mokuren, but because the keikai of Chigusahime was strict and she could not tautei approach, she visited the mansion of Taishi Chiuin.

Teira: 'Gomen nasaimase. Taishisama, it is Teira.'

The Taishi was leaning against his desk, busily dokushiyō-ing the kiyōten of Ananahi, but because he heard the voice of Teira at the kadoguchi, he immediately went out to welcome her at the kadoguchi, and looking very happy, said:

Chiuin: 'Yaa, Jiyoshiyōgun Teira-dono, well, well, come this way... You have come well. I was just thinking from saizen that I wanted to meet you. Let us talk at leisure konnichi.'

Teira: 'Hai, arigata gozaimasu. Since a kifuyō has arisen, I have come to consult with you chiyotsuto. I will take the liberty of intruding.'

And entering the room and facing the Taishi, she said:

Teira: 'Tokini Taishisama, konnichi I was invited by the Ōhisama together with my mother and received takusan of treats, but the state of the Ōhisama changed suddenly, and she said strange things like "I am the savior of the three thousand worlds" or "I am the living shrine of the Hinodewake," and on top of that, she gave me a strict command to "go and search for the whereabouts of that yōsō Kyūbā," and being unable to refuse, I have come to consult with you in secret. Please, I would like to hear your opinion.'

Taishi: 'Hate, this is troublesome. Perhaps Mother has gone mad. I had thought her state had been strange since the other day. For some reason, Mother cherishes that akusō greatly. However, if one such as him were to be brought into the castle, it would further disturb Mother's heart, and who knows what kind of evil wisdom he might pour into her. Therefore, since I have kept Kyūbā confined in a certain place by my own arrangement, it would be best to stop the sōsaku.'

Teira: 'Hai, arigata gozaimasu. I too had been thinking that something was strange. However, I cannot remain in the house of the Samoshi. I do not know when the Ōhisama might send someone to investigate my actions.'

Taishi: 'Indeed, that is also troublesome. If your figure is not seen, Mother, who does not know, will likely be relieved thinking that you have gone on the sōsaku for Kyūbā.'

Teira: 'Where should I hide, if you please?'

Taishi: 'No, do not worry. I will write a letter now, so take this and go to the person addressed and be taken care of. It is only for a while.'

Teira: 'Hai, I shall follow your command and do so.'

The Taishi wrote something smoothly on a scroll, sealed three hundred yen inside, and said:

Taishi: 'Saa, take this and go. I am sure he will take care of you.'

Teira, seeing the address on the letter, was surprised to the point of nearly collapsing.

Teira: 'Moshi Taishisama, is the man called Rēru not the ringleader of the Kōjō Undō?'

Taishi: 'Indeed, that is so. He is also the savior of the Toruman country.'

Teira: 'Does Taishisama also have a relationship with this man called Rēru?'

She asked as if in wonder.

Taishi: 'It is not exactly what you would call a relationship, but I have met him once. At that time, I saw through to the bottom of his heart. He will surely treat you with care. Since three hundred yen is sealed inside this letter, give this to Rēru as your living expenses.'

Teira: 'Hai, arigata gozaimasu. In any case, I shall go.'

Just as she was greeting him, from the kadoguchi:

Teira: 'It is Harisu, the daughter of the Migimori. I wish to see the Taishisama.'

A woman's gentle voice was heard. The Taishi immediately stood up and opened the door of the entrance himself, and said:

Taishi: 'Yaa, Harisu-dono, you have come well. Jiyoshiyōgun Teira is here with me now. Saa, Jiyoshiyōgun Harisu, please enter.'

He invited them in casually, and the three of them sat facing each other in a circle.

Harris: 'Taishisama, I am sorry to have interrupted your studies. Oh, are you Teirahime-sama? I apologize for my rudeness earlier.'

Teira: 'No, not at all. Have you also come here regarding the orders of Ōhisama?'

Harris: 'Yes, that is correct. I am actually quite troubled, as she has commanded me to do something quite difficult.'

Taishi: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, ha. I suppose General Harris has also been entrusted with a search party for the whereabouts of Shiyōnin Kyūbā.'

Harris: 'No, no, not at all. The search party mission was given to Teirahime-sama. I have been entrusted with a much, much more difficult task.'

Taishi: 'And what kind of task is that, pray tell? If there is no objection, I would like to hear it.'

Harris: 'Yes, I have been ordered to capture you, Taishisama, alive.'

Taishi: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, ha. As expected of General Harris, you have been assigned a task worthy of your station. If you were to attack with your beauty, even the Chiuin Haudai might be smashed to pieces in the blink of an eye.'

Harris: 'Ōhisama said to me, "Spare no rouge, powder, or oil; dress yourself in your finest, and without fail, lure the Taishi into the abyss of love. If you succeed, I shall make you the Taishihime." I am truly overwhelmed by such a gracious command.'

Teira: 'What a stylish mother you have, is she not? Harris-sama, I am envious of you.'

Harris: 'Please, Teirahime-sama, since I am the one going on this search, would you not trade places with me? This mission is simply not suited for someone like me. Furthermore, such a great ambition as capturing the Taishi alive—how could I ever do it, considering the royal family and the Usumorike?'

Taishi: 'This is no ordinary matter. My mother must be possessed by some evil spirit, plotting to throw Tolman Castle into chaos. No, Harris-dono, I shall write a letter, so please go into hiding for a while at the place of the person addressed. I will handle my mother's side of things conveniently later. It seems to me that danger is closing in upon both of you generals.'

While saying this, he smoothly wrote something on a scroll, enclosed a three-hundred-yen banknote within it, and said, 'Now, Harris-dono, please go to this person's place and stay in hiding for a while.'

As he handed over the letter, Harris bowed her head in surprise and received it with both hands. When she looked at the addressee, it was written as Mark-dono. Harris was so astonished she nearly gasped, and while staring intently at the Taishi's face, she said,

Harris: 'Taishisama, are you not joking? The man called Mark is the ringleader of the Shudakōjō movement and a person of interest to the Rama headquarters. How could I possibly hide at the place of such a man?'

Taishi: 'No, you need not worry. He is by no means a bad person. He is a man who will become a key figure in the future of our country, Tolman. If you go with this letter, he will surely take care of you.'

Harris: 'Teirahime-sama, what shall I do? Is the Taishisama not being a bit too much? To be exiled to a place like that is far too harsh.'

Teira: 'Even I am to be entrusted to the house of Rēru-sama, of the famous Kōjōkai. There must be some deep consideration on the part of the Taishisama regarding this. In any case, shall we not go and see?'

With that, the two of them quickly bid farewell to the Taishi,

'Taishisama, we shall be going for a while,'

and taking advantage of the twilight, they went out through the back alleys, heading toward the residences of Rēru and Mark.

Both Rēru and Mark, driven by the struggles of daily life, were living together in a small, cramped room in the back streets. They had just finished their duties as guards of the cavern at the foot of Araigadake and had returned after locking it securely. Just as the two were sighing with relief and about to pick up their chopsticks for dinner, a gentle woman's voice was heard at the gate.

'Gomen nasaimase. Is this the house of Rēru-sama and Māku-sama?'

Upon hearing this voice in the dim light, Rēru and Māku were startled.

Rēru: 'Oi, Māku, do you not hear a charming and refined woman's voice at the doorway? And she seems to be saying, "Is this the house of Rēru-sama and Māku-sama?" What on earth could this be?'

Māku: 'Hen, do not talk like a fool. Who would come looking for us in a place like this, and at the edge of dusk? It is likely a fox or a tanuki in disguise.'

Rēru: 'No, no, it is certainly a woman's voice.'

Just as they were saying this, the gentle voice of the woman spoke again,

'Rēru-sama, Māku-sama, is this your house?'

Māku: 'It is indeed a woman's voice.'

Saying this, he stood up quickly, slid open the broken door that had become a diamond shape, and saw two beauties dressed in elaborate attire, smiling at them.

Woman: 'I have come to your house for a certain reason, seeking your care. I humbly ask for your kindness. Seeing that there is no wife or children here, please, I beg you to take care of us.'

Māku: 'I do not know what noble ladies you are, but you must not joke like this. I am the master of a slum, holding my empty stomach, weeping in hunger and shivering in the cold; how could I possibly have the capacity to take care of others? It is surely a case of mistaken identity. Please return home. Oi, Rēru, this has become a serious matter, has it not? Noble ladies, and two of them at that, dressed in fine clothes, have come seeking you and me, but this is surely something I cannot accept. They are likely not simple folk, perhaps related to the Kyūbā.'

He whispered this in a low voice.

Rēru: 'Indeed, that may be so. It will not do to carelessly engage with them. It is said that the fellow called Kyūbā of the Sukobutsuen sect has deceived many noble ladies. Perhaps those ladies have sniffed out that we two are acting as prison guards and have come seeking us. If we do such a thing, we will have no excuse to offer to Taishi-sama. Refuse them and send them away.'

Māku: 'I am sorry to trouble you, but the two ladies, Rēru and Māku are not at this house. Please be on your way at once. A woman who would visit such a broken-down house cannot be human. I recognize you as foxes or tanuki in disguise, so please, leave at once.'

No sooner had he said this than he slammed the broken door shut and braced it with a prop.

Māku: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, this single broken door is worth more than an iron gate, ha, ha, ha, ha, ha.'

He laughed in a loud voice.

Tei: 'If you two gentlemen would please read this letter. Then your suspicions will surely be cleared.'

Saying this, she threw both letters through the gap in the door. The two men picked them both up, and when they held them to the dim lamp to look, one was addressed to Rēru-dono from Chiuin Taishi, and the other to Māku-dono from Chiuin Taishi. When they hurriedly broke the seals and looked, there was no mistake; they were truly letters from Chiuin Taishi. Furthermore, inside the letters, three hundred yen each was enclosed as living expenses for the women, sent to their impoverished household. The two men hurriedly pushed open the door,

Rēru: 'Ya, this, this, I have been most rude. It is a filthy place, but please, do come in. Oi, Māku, will you not sweep the place with a hand broom? We have rare guests. These are the young ladies of the Hidari-mori family and the young ladies of the Migi-mori family.'

Saying this, the two of them swept the blackened tatami mats and the garden with all their might.

Tei: 'Please, do not trouble yourselves for us. From today, I shall do the cleaning. I will also cook the rice. It looks unsightly for you gentlemen to do it.'

テイ『どうぞお構ひ下さいますな。今日から私がお掃除も致します。御飯も炊きます。男さまがなさいますと見つともなう御座います』

§ 4

Re: 'Oh, what a waste. How could I let you cook or clean for me? However, though you have come all this way, I have no bedding or tableware. Please wait a while. I shall send Mark to buy them. Hey, Mark, take this money I received and go buy two sets of silk bedding. And make sure to bring back two sets of high-quality tableware.'

Mark: 'Understood.'

As he was about to dash out, Teira held Mark's sleeve with her slender, soft hand.

Teira: 'Excuse me, Mark-sama, if I may be so bold, if you were to bring silk bedding and fine tableware into such a dwelling, you would immediately arouse the suspicion of the authorities and cause yourselves trouble. We two wish to live the same life as you. Please purchase the worst, most chipped tableware you can find. And for the bedding, please buy the worst kind, the kind of which one would say there is nothing worse. If you do not, and you of the Kōjiyōkuwai are suspected by the authorities, we would not be able to remain under your care for long.'

Mark: 'Hey, Reiru, what should we do? No matter what, we cannot possibly make such noble ladies wear torn futons, can we?'

Reiru: 'What, it does not matter. These princesses who have lived the life of the Jiyōgiyōkaikifu until now—would it not be fine to let them taste the life of our Hinminkutsu for a little while? If you are so hesitant, how will we ever be able to carry out our purpose?'

Teira: 'Ho ho ho, Reiru-sama, I am truly fond of your words. Don't you agree, Harisu-sama?'

Harisu: 'That is so. Truly, the life of the Hinminkutsu must be a pleasant one, is it not?'

Mark: 'Now, Princesses, what are you saying, that the life of the Hinminkutsu is pleasant? Just try it for two or three days, and you will find yourselves crying and wishing to run away back home.'

Harisu: 'No matter how difficult it may be, I do not mind. If I think that I am living together with two such fine people who are to become the pillars of the nation, I will endure any hardship.'

Reiru: 'Ah, they have appeared. Well now, what is this? From this very moment, the retreat of the Binbōgami and the arrival of the Fukugami—it is just like a dream.'

The four of them all at once: 'Ha ha ha ha ha, ho ho ho ho ho.' The three-day-old moon hid behind the western mountains, the curtain of darkness wrapped around the surroundings, and the chirping voices of the old women in the neighborhood gradually faded away.
