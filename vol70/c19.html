Chapter 19: Plum Flower 団 [1786]

梅花団〔1786〕

§ 1

Chigusahime returned to the castle in high spirits, entered the living quarters of King Garden, and spoke.

Chigusahime: 'The living shrine of Hinodewake has details to convey to King Garden. Listen well.'

King Garden: 'Yes, I am delighted that you have returned safely. I shall respectfully listen to the purpose of your mission.'

Chigusahime: 'The Tennin of the Daiichireigoku, Hinodewake, has deliberated for a long time with Hikonomikoto of Ural, Bankoshinō, and others regarding the treatment of the junk. As a result, it has been decided that his life shall be ended within this week to remove the poison of the Kyōkoku. Until then, you must never, under any circumstances, lay violent hands upon him. I have arranged for the divine mechanism to cause his natural extinction...'

King Garden: 'Yes, having received that divine decree, I am greatly relieved. In that case, when will you summon the Kyūbā who is to be employed as a high minister to take charge of the state affairs of the Kyōkoku in the divine realm?'

Chigusahime: 'No, there is no need for concern. When I, in the divine realm, used my kin to investigate that Kyūbā thoroughly, they reported back that he broke out of this castle's prison, was pursued by wolves while fleeing, and finally met a tragic end, his entire body being devoured by the pack. Having no choice, I consulted with Bankoshinō, who decreed that by sending the spirit of a Tennin from the Daiichitenkoku, Hinodewake-dono would accompany that spirit back to the castle to govern the religious administration. Thus, he would be revered by all the people of the world as the ruler of the three thousand worlds, not to mention the Toruman country. Upon hearing this decree, this Hinodewake, with a courageous heart, was on his way back under the protection of the attendant monks when he transformed into a mere man and attempted to approach my palanquin. The attendant monks, blind as if they had no spiritual eyes, mistook him for someone who would harm the palanquin and show disrespect, and they immediately captured him. Truly, there is no one more troublesome than such ignorant, worldly people. Ohohohoho.'

King Garden: 'What has become of the man in whom that spirit resides?'

Chigusahime: 'He is waiting at the entrance accompanied by attendant monks, so you must go and welcome him with the utmost respect. He is a great divine man of both the manifest and hidden realms, who shall become the right arm of the savior of the three thousand worlds, Hinodewake, and the inspector general of religious affairs to assist your administration. Go, welcome him quickly.'

The King bowed his head, saying 'Ha-ha,' and ran out to the entrance himself. The guard monks were startled and were offering their deepest respects. King Garden knelt before the captive, trembling with fear.

King Garden: 'This is, this is, the spirit of the Tennin from the Daiichitenkoku, my ignorant subordinates have shown you such great disrespect. Please, I beg of you, come up this way.'

Baikō remained silent, smiling. The escorting monks were surprised, quickly untied the ropes, apologized for their rudeness while turning pale, and shrank away like mice chased by a cat, slinking off.

Baikō: 'I am one who has received the command of Bankoshinō from the Heavenly Kingdom and appeared suddenly in human form to save the Toruman country. Since the living shrine of Hinodewake, the savior of the three thousand worlds, the great pillar of Miroku, and the Tennin of the Daiichireigoku are supposed to have descended to this house, please guide me to his living quarters.'

He spoke with composure. King Garden had believed ninety-nine percent that Chigusahime was the Tennin of the Daiichireigoku, but hearing the words of this divine man, his conviction grew even stronger. Bowing repeatedly, he stood ahead of Baikō and guided him, pointing toward the living quarters of Chigusahime. Seeing the figure of Baikō, Chigusahime became even more delighted.

Chigusahime: 'Oh, this is, this is, Takamiyahiko-dono, you have come to assist my divine work. In the divine realm, I am Hinodewake, also known as Takamiyahime. Surely you have not forgotten, have you?'

と応揚にいふ。ガーデン王は千草姫が、第一霊国の天人といふ事は、九分九厘迄信じてゐたが、今此神人の言葉を聞いて、一層確信を強め、ペコペコし乍ら、梅公の先に立ち、千草姫の居間を指して案内する。千草姫は梅公の姿を見て、益々悦に入り、

『ヤ、これはこれは、高宮彦殿、能くマア妾が神業を助けむと、お越し下さいました。妾は神界に於ては、日の出神、又の名は高宮姫で御座います。ヨモヤお忘れは御座いますまいなア』

Ume: 'Indeed, I am none other than Takamiyahiko. However, I have since ascended to Tengoku, rejuvenated myself through Senjiyutsu, and have now returned as this Zetsusei Bidanshi to Saido the Shiyujiyō. Ikimiya-dono of Hinodewake, you may now be at ease.'

Chigusa: 'Indeed, with this, the Kiso of the Toruman nation is established, and it is a Shiyōchō for the Kyōōke, truly worthy of Keiga. Garden-Ōdono, as there is a matter of Shinkai Himitsu, please leave this place until I issue an order.'

Ō: 'Yes, I obey. If you have any Goyō, please summon me at any time. If so, Takamiyahiko-sama, Hinodewake-sama, please take your rest in comfort.'

Saying this, he returns to his living quarters with alacrity. Ume-kō suddenly breaks his posture, sits with legs spread wide, and says,

Ume: 'Hey, you, Chigusa-kun, or rather, Takahime-kun, you have disguised yourself quite well. How about it? Shall we perform another play after such a long time? I am Mokusuke, the Tokiokishi.'

Chigusa: 'Indeed, so you were Moku-chan. My, what a splendid appearance. You have changed so much that I thought you were someone else.'

Ume: 'What a remarkable skill. Even after making such a firm promise to me, you intend to devour any handsome man who passes by; truly, men are but a fair mask. With that beauty and those seductive words, any man of strong character would be captivated without resistance. Fortunately, I am the real Tokiokishi, so it was fine, but if you were to devour men so easily, it would not do.'

Chigusa: 'Ho ho ho, we had made such a firm promise, after all... It is you who found a beautiful woman, abandoned me, and must have been wandering about somewhere. You are truly cruel.'

Ume: 'Do not talk nonsense. I have been searching for your whereabouts for almost three years, suffering here and there. When you were offering prayers at the temple in the forest of Harima, I caught a glimpse of your back and thought, "How similar, how similar, you are the spitting image of Taka-chan." When I went around to the front to peek into the palanquin, I was caught by the guard monk. Your spirit seems to be that of a queen everywhere; it is a grand thing.'

Chigusa: 'That is certainly true; am I not a Ten-nin of the First Spirit Realm? After all, a spirit that is to be above must remain above, no matter what.'

Ume: 'By the way, what are you thinking of doing from here on? Would you like to escape from here with me and build another Kuruwa Castle to live together in harmony? That is what I want to hear.'

Chigusa: 'Well, I would do anything if it is your word, but with such a splendid Toruman Castle at hand, is there any need to build a separate Kuruwa Castle? How would it be if you and I use this place as a base, establish the headquarters of the Uranai religion, and appear as the saviors of the three thousand worlds?'

Ume: 'Ah, that would also be fine. In that case, let us have a private consultation from now on.'

Chigusa: 'Please, let us do so.'

Ume: 'This room is somewhat cramped and unbearable. Do you not have a bedchamber? Please guide me there; I would like to have a long-awaited pillow talk.'

Chigusa: 'Ah, let us do so. Ho ho, I feel somewhat embarrassed.'

Saying this, she lowers her eyes, leads Ume-kō by the hand to her own living quarters, closes the door tightly so it cannot be opened from the outside, spreads out the silk bedding, and the two lie down side by side.

Ume: 'Hey Taka-chan, it has been a long time.'

Chigusa: 'It has truly been a long time.'

Ume: 'You were quite beautiful back when you were projecting your image before the Unuborekagami, but compared to that time, have you not become even more beautiful?'

Chigusa: 'I do not know why, but I feel as if I have become thirty years younger. Look at this, the luster of my skin is like the radiance of platinum. But Mokuchan, have you not also become very beautiful? Is it not as if light is emerging from a body that looks just like a diamond?'

Ume: 'I wonder if that is so. We are spirits of the Tennin from the Daiichitengoku, but as we are about to commence the Daishingefu, we must first perform the Jinkeirei to gain the trust of the world toward you and me. Why not take the plunge and release the prisoners from the Raugoku?'

Chigusa: 'Yes, I cannot refuse the words of my beloved. I shall follow your command and order King Garden to perform the Jinkeirei. However, I cannot forgive the special Jiudaihannin. They watch our surroundings, and I do not know when they might cause us harm.'

Ume: 'Ha ha ha, Takachan, you are a woman after all. Do not say such weak-hearted things. To have not a single Shiujin in the Raugoku is what is meant by benevolence. If you listen to the rumors in the castle town, things have become quite serious. They say it is outrageous for Chigusa-hime to throw two propagandists of the San-go-kyō into the Raugoku. They say they will carry out a coup d'état now, take Chigusa's head, and clear the resentment of the masses... it is truly terrible, bad popularity. That is why I say you should order King Garden to perform this act of benevolence and clear the suspicions of the masses. There is no better method than this.'

Chigusa: 'Indeed, that might be a good policy. By the way, Mokuchan, there is something I would like to ask of you. That fellow called Junk is quite a tough character; he is a stubborn man who even refuses the divine command of the living shrine of Hinodewake, so I am a little troubled. Is there not some way to get rid of him?'

Ume: 'Ha ha ha, what is a Junk who would fly if blown? However, if you banish him now, your popularity will worsen instead, and it will hinder your great ambition, so leave him as he is. Once this Mokusuke raises his head, I have the intention to kick the likes of Junk down to the bottom of the valley.'

Chigusa: 'Ho ho ho, I suppose that is so. I have long known of your power. There is no hero in this wide world who surpasses you.'

Ume: 'That is right. Now, you had better call the King out as soon as possible and arrange for the Jinkeirei to be performed.'

Chigusa: 'Mokuchan, is there any need to be so hurried? Have we not, as a man and woman who have longed for each other for so long, finally met? Come, please hold me and sleep peacefully tonight. Since I saw your face, Mokuchan, all my courage has vanished, and I have become like a child.'

Ume: 'Popularity is boiling over, and we are on the verge of a coup d'état; I cannot afford even a moment of delay. Now that this Kami has appeared, the Turman Castle is Daibanjiyaku; pray, be Goanshin.'

Ōdono: 'Hai, Arigata gozaimasu.'

Chigusa: 'Thereupon, there is a Shisai that Hinode Kami wishes to convey to you. Listen well.'

Ōdono: 'Hai,' he replies, bowing his head.

Chigusa: 'The reason Hinode Kami himself has appeared in the Ima of the Kyōō is not a trivial matter. First of all, as a celebration of the Kaishi of the Miroku Shinsei, you are to issue a decree of mercy and release the prisoners in the jail without delay, leaving not a single one behind. This is truly the divine command of the living palace of Hinode Kami, the savior of the three thousand worlds, the spiritual body of the great Miroku of the deep rock roots, the celestial being of the first spiritual kingdom.'

Garden Ōdono answered 'Hai,' and immediately called for the junk, releasing every single prisoner from the jail. While Chigusa-hime was smiling and returning to her own Ima, Ume-kō was snoring loudly, sleeping soundly without a care.

Chigusa: 'Hey, Moku-chan, won't you wake up? What is this, after showing me your face, you are truly a cold person. If you are Moku-chan, please open your eyes.'

No matter how much she shook or called him, he snored like a shark and did not wake up at all. Chigusa-hime had no choice but to watch Ume-kō's sleeping figure for a while.

Chigusa: 'Ho ho ho, what a noble face. The texture of his face is as white as freshly pounded mochi, his eyes are cool, his nose is straight, his mouth is neither too large nor too small, and with the condition of his beard, the hair on his head, the smoothness of his skin, and the luster of his nails, he is the most beautiful man in the universe, without a single flaw. What a lucky person this Taka-chan is to have such a beautiful man as her husband. Ho ho ho, without knowing it, about a gō of drool has spilled onto his knees. To eyes that have been looking at a dull-headed person like Garden Ōdono or a mallet-headed person like Kyūbā-sama, he looks exceptionally magnificent. It feels like eating special yōkan after having eaten rotten pickles with a bad taste, ho ho ho. Still, what a lovely, dignified, and manly appearance, truly like a Kami.'

She strokes his face, tries to open his eyes, and plays with him for amusement. Just then, because the prisoners outside the castle had been released, the sound of the prisoners shouting 'Banzai, banzai' in unison echoes through the window glass.

Chigusa: 'Ho ho ho, it seems that thanks to Moku-chan's invention, the prisoners in the jail have been released and are shouting banzai. The prisoners may be shouting banzai, but this Taka-chan is also shouting ten thousand banzai for having met an unparalleled hero, a great man, and a beautiful man, o ho ho ho.'

Ume: 'Ah, ah.'

Yawning, he stretches out both hands and says,

Ume: 'Ya, Taka-chan, were you there?'

Chigusa: 'Hai, I was indeed. How are you? No matter how much I shook you or called you, you would not wake up.'

Ume: 'Oi, vessel of the golden-furred nine-tailed fox, corpse of Chigusa-hime, reincarnation of Takahime, listen well. I am neither Moku-suke the Tokiokashi nor anything of the sort. I am an angel of Kototama-wake, a celestial being of the first spiritual kingdom who protects the San-go-kyō.'

No sooner had he said this than he turned into a great ball of light, piercing Chigusa-hime's eyes, and vanished through the gap in the window without a sound. The voices of the men and women, young and old, shouting banzai continued to be heard without interruption. Chigusa-hime fainted for a while from the sheer shock.
