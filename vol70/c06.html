Chapter 6: Ogre 遊婆 [1773]

鬼遊婆〔1773〕

§ 1

At the hour of twilight, the color of the sky is as if covered by a pot from above, and there is an indescribable stench of blood, with a wind blowing with a whistling sound as if it were tearing the bowels. Two or three emaciated crows are shivering, holding their hunger together with the treetops of a persimmon tree that has shed its hagoromo.

On the ground, withered grass grows in patches, revealing the red kiji here and there. Indescribable, disgusting insects surround the withered grass on all sides, and as soon as they smell the scent of a person, they gather all at once, lying in wait to suck the bodies of humans. Appearing there was Takahime of the Uranai sect, who had been detained in the Chiūkai for three years and ordered to undergo training.

While accompanying one new maujiya, Takahime, intending to work in the Genkai as before, was buffeted by the nowake, wearing tattered clothes like the Gojifusantsugi, and was lying in wait with her new disciple Tonbo to pull in passersby.

Tonbo: 'Excuse me, Ikumiya-sama, shall we not return now that it is enough? This road is somehow so lonely, so lonely, that not even a single dog passes by, is it not? No matter how long you wait like a spider spinning a web for a cicada to land, it will be useless if the cicada does not come.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Tonbo, what a faint-hearted thing you are saying. Even if no humans pass by, if this Ikumiya is stationed here, many spirits will pass by, touch the divine virtue of the great Miroku Ikumiya, be illuminated by the light, and all one hundred out of one hundred will receive blessings and ascend to heaven. That is why, even if physical humans do not come, it is fine as long as spirits come. To your vulgar eyes, it may look as if not a single human is coming, but to the eyes of this Ikumiya, about eighty thousand have come since this morning. It is busy, truly busy. Because your body is clouded, it is no wonder that you cannot see even one of those maujiya. However, the eighty thousand maujiya who have passed by since this morning were looking at your face with envy.'

Tonbo: 'Why would people as unfortunate as me pass by looking at me with envy? I cannot understand it at all.'

Takahime: 'That is why you are called blind. Even if you are sitting on a golden pedestal, if you cannot see, you feel as if you are being thrust into the mud. While being allowed to serve the direct mission of the savior of the three thousand worlds, the great Miroku of the bottom-most rock roots, the Hinodewake, what a wasteful way of thinking you have. Where else is there someone who is allowed to perform such a magnificent mission as you? That is why eighty thousand spirits passed by looking at you with envy.'

Tonbo: 'Hmph, that is strange.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Tonbo, do not say such absurd things. What do you mean by "Hmph, that is strange"? You are making a fool of this Ikumiya-sama, are you not? If you serve the mission of Ikumiya with such a mindset, divine punishment will strike you instantly, and you will have to turn a somersault with your head down and your feet up. Be careful.'

Tonbo: 'I have been finding it so painful and ridiculous lately that I was actually thinking of watching for a gap in Ikumiya-sama's attention and successfully escaping, but if eighty thousand people who envy me pass by in a single day, it is the same wherever I go. I shall serve you for a while longer by your side, Ikumiya-sama.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Tonbo, what are you saying? To say you will serve for a while, you cursed fellow, if you are such junk with that kind of mindset, I will dismiss you from today. Come, go home quickly. Even if you are not here, since my physical body is a woman, all cooking and chores are easy for me. You are a useless thing, a big tree of wood, a foolish fellow who is just a waste of space. From now on, I will change your name from Tonbo to Tonma-yaro. That should suit your nature.'

トンボ『もし生宮様、もういい加減に帰らうぢやありませぬか。何だか此街道は淋しくて淋しくて犬の子一匹通らぬぢやありませぬか。何時まで蜘蛛が巣をかけて蝉がとまるのを待つやうにして居つても、蝉が来なくちや駄目でせう』

高姫『これ、トンボ、お前は何と云ふ気の弱い事を云ふのだい。仮令人間が通らなくても、此生宮が此処に出張して居れば、沢山の霊が通つて大弥勒の生宮の御神徳に触れ、御光に照され、百人が百人乍ら、お蔭を頂いて天国へ上るのだぞえ。それだから肉体人が来なくても、霊界人が来さへすればいいのだ。お前の俗眼では一人も人間が来ないやうに見えるだらうが、此生宮の目には、今朝から八万人許り来たのだよ。それはそれは忙しい事だよ。お前も肉体が曇つてゐるので、あれ丈けの亡者が一人も目につかぬのは無理もない。然し乍ら今朝から通つた八万人の亡者が、お前の顔を見て羨ましさうにしてゐたよ』

ト『何故又私のやうな不幸者を羨ましさうにして通るのでせうか。サツパリ合点が行きませぬがな』

高『それだからお前は盲と云ふのだ。目が見えぬと黄金の台に坐つて居つても、泥の中に突込まれて居るやうな気がするものだよ。結構な結構な三千世界の救世主、底津岩根の大弥勒、日の出神の直々の御用をさして頂き乍ら、何と云ふ勿体ないお前は量見だえ。お前のやうな結構な御用をさして貰ふものは、何処にあるものか。それだから八万人の精霊が羨ましさうにして通つたのだよ』

ト『ヘーン、妙ですな』

高『これ、トンボ、トンボーもない事を云ひなさるな。「ヘーン、妙ですな」とは何だい。この生宮さまをお前さまは馬鹿にして居るのだな。そんな量見で生宮の御用をして居ると神罰が立所に当つて、頭を下にし、足を上にしてトンボ返りをせねばならぬぞや。チツト心得なされ』

ト『私やこの間から、余り辛いのと馬鹿らしいので実の所は生宮様の隙を窺ひ、うまくトンボ（遁亡）しようかと考へて居りましたが、私を羨むやうな人物が八万人も一日に通るかと思へば、何処に行つても同じ事だ。生宮様のお側にマアしばらく御用をさして頂きませう』

高『これ、トンボ、そら何を云ふぢやいな。暫く御用をさして頂くとは罰当り奴、そんな量見で居るやうなガラクタなら、今日から暇をやる。サア、トツトと帰つておくれ。お前が居らなくても肉体は女だから炊事万端お手のものだよ。無用の長物、ウドの大木、体見倒しの頓馬野郎だな。之からトンボと云ふ名を改名して、トンマ野郎と云ふてやらう。それがお前の性に合うてるだろ』

Taka: 'Na, what are you saying? How wasteful, you will be punished for that heart. Ne, you do not do any work worth mentioning all year round, No, you just stand around like a scarecrow even when doing field work, Ra, you sing songs that make one's head ache with a raspy voice, and Ri, to say things like "do not call me a fool" as if you were clever, it makes me sick. Ru, did you not cry and beg me to use you because you were wandering and had nowhere to go? Re, to forget to say thanks and only complain, you are truly a great sinner. You are confined in a prison by the warning of the gods. However, while this Ikimiya takes care of your physical body, your soul is trapped in a great prison, saying "I want to eat, I want to eat, I want to play, I want to play." Fu, tsu, fu.'

§ 4

The Chijiyō is cold, and even the Kusaki are all withered and drooping in this world; yet the Sukobutsuen sect alone, as if ascending to the Asahi of Ten, prospers day by day, Hinichi by Hinichi. Utsufu-fu-fu-fu, utsufu-fu-fu-fu.

He came as far as the Yotsutsuji, blowing such an Ōbora. Upon seeing the figure of Kyūbā, Takahime spoke in a shrill voice:

Takahime: 'Hey there, Henro-sama, please wait a moment. Even at a glance, you seem to be a fine man who understands things. I am the savior of the Sanzensekai, the Futobashira of the great Miroku, the Tennin of the Daiichireigoku, and the Ikimiyasama of the Hinodewake. Come, come, please come to my mansion for a moment. I shall let you hear some wonderful, truly wonderful stories.'

Kyūbā: 'What, you say you are the savior? Fu-fu-fu-fūn, how strange.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Henro-sama, what is this "fu-fu-fu-fūn"? Strange... this is the beginning of the world, the Mirokushiyutsugen, the Jiyuritsu of the Kamiyo, the beginning of the world at the end of the world.'

Kyūbā: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha, what an Omoshiro old woman you are. Fortunately, it is dusk, and there is no lodging around here; perhaps I shall have you put me up.'

Takahime: 'Come, come, please stay. I will tell you wonderful, truly wonderful stories, ho-ho-ho-ho-ho. That fellow Tombo has finally buried himself in the grass. There is no need to care what happens to a fellow like that. He only spits out Rikutsu toward Ikimiyasama. How strange the world is. Just when I thought one fellow had become disgusted and run away, the Kamisama has properly prepared a replacement for me. This Henro might just become the right arm of Ikimiya. Ho-ho-ho-ho-ho.'

Tombo had been hiding himself in the grass from the beginning, observing the behavior of Takahime, but... if such a fellow comes, I am finished. However, I do not know what kind of consultation that fellow Takahime is having after pulling such a man in. At any rate, tonight I must listen to the conversation between the two from outside the mansion... Having made this decision, he followed the two as they returned to the dilapidated house at the foot of the rocky mountain, hiding his footsteps in the darkness.
