Chapter 8: Great 勝 [1775]

大勝〔1775〕

§ 1

Taishi Chiuin of the Tolman nation appointed Ōjiyo Chinrei and the Jiyoshiyōgun of Haris as Betsushiyō, looked up to the renowned Buyū Junk as the commander of the Daiichigun, and placed the Ananai-kyō Sendenshi Terukuniwake and Terukōtsukasa as the rear guard. Beating shiyōko and with hatasashimono fluttering in a niginigi manner, they led two thousand five hundred cavalry, singing gunka as they returned day and night to annihilate the taigun of Ōdaruwake that was besieging his kiyojiyō. The sankasaumoku bowed before their ifū, and there was nothing, even down to the kinjiuchiugiyo, that did not praise their itoku. Taishi Chiuin sang a shingunka with great spirit from horseback.

Taishi Chiuin: 'The Tolman nation, from ancient times, was created by the noble Kami as a Tengoku upon the Chijiyō. The ancestors of our royal house took the hearts of the people as their own, and by teaching the laws of Kami to all, they ruled the world in great peace and tranquility; how noble was their reign. Since the middle ages, the evil teachings of the Brahman have mingled in, and patriotism fades day by day like spring ice. My father, Garden, has also been swayed by the winds of the times, and it has become an age where the teachings of the Kami of Ural are held in contempt. Politics fall into increasing disorder, and the cries of the people are clamorous, heard like the plovers. Ah, what shall we do? To revive the weary hearts of the people into a brave and pure spirit, and to restore our nation to the peace and tranquility of the ancient age of Kami, I pray to Kami with all my sincerity. A branch of the Brahman religion, the Skobutsuen sect, has crossed over, and it is hateful that they have planted evil teachings to drive the souls of our people into madness. The foul-hearted Kyūbā, holding up the power of Ōkuninushi as a great signboard, presses upon our royal house. While punishing him, we shall break the army of the Brahman Ōdaruwake with the itoku of Kami. Until we raise the song of victory and govern our home castle in safety and peace, my heart shall not waver even if I die. Take courage, take courage, and rise up, my three thousand soldiers. I have the help of Kami. The messenger of the noble Kami Susanoo, who shines in the Saienkan of Ubusunayama, the Sendenshi Terukuniwake, together with Terukōtsukasa, shall assist our army. When they unleash the invincible Kototama, the enemy army shall scatter like leaves in the wind, and their defeat is before our eyes. Advance, advance, now advance, until Ōdaruwake perishes and the evil monk Kyūbā falls.'

With voices clear and cool, harmonizing with the sound of shiyōko and conch shells, they formed a crane-wing formation and pressed in upon the vast, boundless plains, moving like a dragnet toward the center of Tolman Castle.

While serving as the rear guard, Terukuniwake led several hundred soldiers, formed a separate unit, and approached while singing a shingunka.

Terukuniwake: 'As a Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, I, Terukuniwake-tsukasa, find no joy in facing a war where men take each other's lives. Nevertheless, now that I see the dire straits of the Tolman nation, as one who serves the path of the Great Kami, I cannot simply pass by. In this heart-wrenching situation, I have no choice but to join the imperial army and serve in the rear guard as we advance. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, Kami is with us. We are the children of Kami, the palace of Kami. From the start, I have no heart to stain my blade with blood or to slay the enemy. Only Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, the dew-jewels of Kami's grace, loaded into the cannon of a pure heart, shall be aimed at the enemy, and I shall only wield the whip of benevolence. Advance, advance, my soldiers, Tolman Castle draws near. King Garden and the Samosu-tsukasa must be pouring their whole hearts into the defense, waiting for our army to arrive. The vanguard of Taishi Chiuin, all following the command of Kami, move freely and at will like the fingers of one's hands, and it is clear that they will easily drive the enemy out of the country. Do not be surprised by anything. Take courage, take courage, all take courage, the capital of victory draws near. Advance, advance, now advance. Until the army of Kami under Ōdaruwake raises the white flag, shows all the sincerity they possess, submits from the bottom of their hearts to the correct teachings of Kami, and repents their past wrongs, do not retreat a single step. This is the vanguard of the fulfillment of the Kingdom of Kami. Although the Brahman Ōkuninushi plots to seize the Moon Country of over seven thousand nations, as long as our army of Kami exists, how could he touch even a single finger? Ah, how brave, how brave. Even if the blowing wind is fierce, even if the Tolman River is deep, since we have the protection of Kami, we shall not fail by even a single horseman, and we shall successfully strike the rear of the enemy army in safety and peace. Advance, advance, now advance, the figures of the enemy have come into view.'

Itsuseishiyageki is before my eyes, / Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, / We protect the Ananai-kyō, / The Ōmikami of Kuniharutachi, / Before the Mimahe of the Ōkami, / Kamususanō, / Terukuniwaketsukasa, / With awe and reverence, prays.

While singing thus, he encouraged the Shisotsu, kept his mind on the zengo, and advanced bit by bit. Ōdaruwake, seeing this state from the Monomidai, was greatly surprised.

Ōdaruwake: 'That must surely be reinforcements. Now that it has come to this, it is most distressing to delay the time for the sake of one person, Kyūbā, and fall into the enemy's jiyutsuchiu. I shall take the Honjiyō as quickly as possible, and if reinforcements arrive, I will use the Jiyōkuwaku as a shield and slaughter every last one of them. Now is the time to attack.'

He suddenly gave a Genrei to his subordinates, and with their Tsutsusaki aligned in unison, they surged toward Toruman Castle like a tide.

Suddenly, the sound of war cries and the roar of cannons and rifles were heard. King Gāden, who had been lying in wait, and Samamoritsukasa commanded five hundred castle soldiers and fought with all their might. Samamori, with frost upon his head, rushed out of the castle gate and charged into the enemy's ranks with three hundred soldiers. As a result of his bitter struggle, his martial fortune ran out; he fell from his horse and, at the age of seventy, departed on a journey from which he would not return. Ōdaruwake seized the victory and pressed upon the front gate. Just as the castle was about to fall, Chigusahime and Kyūbā leaped out to the front gate, brandishing their naginata. Upon seeing Ōdaruwake, Kyūbā raised his voice.

Kyūbā: 'Ōdaruwake, wait a moment. Kyūbā-tsukasa is here. If there is support from Chigusahime, do not be in such a hurry. The Honjiyō is already in my hands.'

As he shouted this from horseback, Ōdaruwake dodged, backed against the castle gate, and fought to defend himself against the attacking reinforcements. From within the castle, hundreds of King Gāden's soldiers began firing in unison with their cannons. Ōdaruwake was beset by enemies from all sides, abandoned his horse, threw away his weapons, and fled in a desperate scramble for his life. Due to this battle, eighteen men of the Baramon army died, while only two deaths occurred within the castle. Prince Chuin, seeing the enemy flee so easily, was eager to pursue the enemy soldiers at this moment and slaughter every last one of them, but upon the advice of Terukuniwake, he ceased. They played songs of triumph and, in a dignified manner, returned in victory to Toruman Castle. King Gāden had climbed the Monomiyagura and was commanding the strong men within the castle, but he was emboldened by the enemy's miserable rout and the valiant activities of Prince Chuin. He opened his war fan and, while singing himself atop the tower, danced in a celebratory mood of triumph.

King Gāden: 'The ugly black clouds that shrouded the land of Toruman have now cleared, / The Heavenly Sun Succession is high in the sky, / How joyous it is to see it shine, / When I look out far over the earth, / That there is not a single shadow of an enemy / Around the capital is a cause for joy, / This must surely be because the Imperial Gods / Are protecting the land, / And have helped us, / Now from this time on, let us revere the gods of heaven and earth, / Become a model for the people, / Build the ancient age of the gods of Urayasu, / And while threatening the heart of Ōkuninushi, / Once again, / Facing our divine land, / To renounce hostile acts, / May the Ural teachings protect us, / Before the Mimahe of the Ōkami / Who opened them, I pray.'

Just as he was doing this, it appeared that Ōdaruwake had set fire to the city streets along his way. In the twilight sky, flames rose in various places until the heavens were splashed with crimson, and cries of misery could be heard from near and far. At this moment, Terukuniwake happened to be within the gate. Upon seeing this, he rushed straight up to the Monomiyagura and, together with Lord Teru, recited the Heavenly Number Song and the Amatsu Norito. The fires that had broken out in all directions were instantly extinguished as if water had been poured upon them. At the sound of the joyful voices heard once again, King Gāden, the Prince, the Princess, and Harisu all clapped their hands in delight. The King gave orders to his subordinates to bury the remains of Samamori and Migimoritsukasa in the royal tomb with special honors, to build a shrine as the guardian deity of the nation, and to hold services for them forever. Furthermore, based on the petition of Prince Chuin, having heard of the benevolent support of Terukuniwake and Terukuni-tsukasa, and the divine virtue of the Ōkami, he was moved to such gratitude that he vowed to enshrine the Ōkami of the Three-Five. Once the dust of battle had settled, the King first offered thanks to the Ōkami, ordered Prince Chuin, Jiyanku, and other senior retainers to handle the aftermath, and returned to Chigusahime's chambers to rest. Upon seeing Chigusahime and Kyūbā facing each other with smiles, exchanging cups of celebration, the King flew into a rage.

『不義者見つけた、そこ動くな』

と手槍を以て立向へば千草姫は王の手に取付き、

『王様、少時お待ち下さいませ。此神柱は決して国に仇する悪人では御座いませぬ。大足別に脅迫され、心にあらぬ詐りを申し立て、此城内に忍び込み、妾に事情を打明し、救ひを求めて居る者で御座います。今此キユーバーをして、神主となし大神に国家安泰の祈願をし、凱旋の御礼を申上げ、直会の神酒を頂かせて居つた処で御座います。必ず必ず誤解のなき様御願ひ申し上げます』

と落涙し乍ら言葉さかしく弁解する。ガーデン王も忠実なる姫の言葉を疑ふに由なく、其儘差許す事となり、己が居間へと帰りゆく。高姫の霊と憑り変つた千草姫はキユーバーに向ひ、

『コレ、キユーバーさま、貴方は本当に危ない事で御座いましたよ。妾も王様のお出になつた時は何うなる事やらと、大変に心をもみました』

キユーバーは慄ひ乍ら、

『全くだ、お前の為に大切な命が助かつたのだ。併し乍らどうだらう、大足別将軍は脆くも敗走した様子だし、遠からず私は当城を追出さるるに違ひない。さうなれば恋しいお前と添ふ事が出来ぬ。何とかして夜陰に乗じ、此城内を脱け出す心はないか』

千草『ホヽヽヽヽ、キユーバー様の気の弱い事、そんな御心配がいりませうか。王様は私の美貌にゾツコン惚込んでゐられますよ。貴方は何処までも救世主と名乗つて、神さまいぢりをしてゐて下さいませ。何程王様が御立腹遊ばさうが、重臣が何と申さうが、千草姫此世にあらむ限りは、貴方様に指一本さえさせませぬ。暫くは両人共猫をかぶり、時期の至るを待つて此王城を奪ひ、七千余国の覇者とならうでは御座いませぬか』

キユ『成る程、其奴ア面白からう。そんなら姫の仰せに任せ、此城内に永久に止まる事としよう』

千草『ハ、さうなさいませ』

斯く話す時しもチウイン太子は軍功を誇り顔に、王女チンレイ及右守の娘ハリスと共にドアを開いて入来り、

『母上様、御無事で御目出度たう御座います。おかげを以て敵軍を撃退致しました。どうかお喜び下さいませ』

千草『ヤ、其方は太子、天晴れお手柄お手柄。其方こそトルマン国の柱石、ガーデン王の嗣子として恥しからぬ偉丈夫だ。サア草臥ただらう、ゆつくり休んで下さい。其方はチンレイ、ハリス、能くマア女の身を以て凛々しい武者振、母も感じ入りました』

太子は妖僧キユーバーを見て目を丸くし乍ら、

『母上様、此処にゐる坊主はスコブツエン宗の邪教を開き、大足別の軍勢を導いたる悪僧では御座いませぬか。かかる魔者を何故御居間に侍らせ、優待遊ばすのですか。チウイン、其意を得ませぬ』

千草『如何にも此方はキユーバー様に違ひない。併し乍ら、大足別が手先となり当城へ談判にお越になつたのも、止むを得ぬ事情あつての事、此母がとつくとキユーバー様の心底を調べ、大足別の秘密を探り、キユーバー様の応援によりて無事に敵を撃退する事を得たのだ。此母が保証するから、必ず必ず疑うてはなりませぬぞ。チンレイもハリスも必ず誤解しちやなりませぬ。母が証拠だから……』

ハリス『ハイ、畏れ入りまして御座います。太子様、王女様は申すに及ばず、妾の如き孱弱き女の身として戦陣に立ち、勝利を得たのも神様の御蔭、キユーバー様の御尽力の致すところで御座いませう。併し乍ら吾父右守は如何なりまして御座りまするか』

千草『右守殿は国家の為犠牲者となつて国替遊ばしたよ。キツト神様に導かれ、天国にお出になつてゐるだらう。必ず必ず心配致されな。千草姫が其女の身は引うけて世話を致すから……』

ハリスは『ハイ』と言つたきり、父の死を聞いて驚愕し、其場に気絶して了つた。チウイン太子は水よ薬よと種々手を尽し、漸くにして息ふき返さしめ、吾居間をさしてチンレイと共に伴れ帰り行く。
