Chapter 17: Spring の Light [1784]

春の光〔1784〕

§ 1

Chigusahime, unable to know the whereabouts of Kiūbā whom she could not forget even in her dreams, felt her Seishin grow increasingly confused; she claimed that the Kamuzukasa of Terukuniwake and Terukō were divine Shinchiyoku, and using the King's Meirei as a pretext, she forcibly threw them into Raugoku to vent her Munen, and gave Gokusotsu Genmei to inflict cruel treatment upon them. Furthermore, she banished Taishi Chiwin and Chinrei from the castle under the pretense of Shiugiyō, gave Genmei to Teira to seek the whereabouts of Kiūbā, caused Harisu to commit errors through Kauji, and planned to annihilate the lineage of the old Kiushin, thus truly becoming an Akugiyakubudau Maō.

Chigusahime's Seirei was suddenly occupied by the Kinmaukiubi no Akuko, while King Gāden had his heart and soul occupied by a fragment of the Yamata no Orochi; he submitted to the commands of Chigusahime, and set his hands in all directions to rescue Kiūbā, pouring his full strength into the search. However, even the Nikumiya of the Hinodewake could not perceive that Taishi Chiwin had confined him in the rocky cave of Araikegoku. Chigusahime, in order to pray for the earliest possible return of Kiūbā to the forest of Harima, began to make pilgrimages twice a day, morning and evening, riding in a palanquin with strict vigilance, followed by many retainers.

Māku and Rēru were earnestly persuaded by Taishi Chiwin that Chigusahime's physical body had already passed away and had been replaced by the evil spirit of the Kinmaukiubi no Akuko; furthermore, learning from a newspaper extra that Terukuniwake and Terukō had been thrown into Raugoku, were suffering from cruel treatment day and night, and were in danger of their lives, they could no longer stand still. They whispered to each other, and resolved to lie in wait during Chigusahime's pilgrimage and kill her with stones.

The two men, filled with joy and courage, thinking that now was the time to sacrifice their lives for the nation, hid stones in their clothes and waited impatiently for Chigusahime's palanquin to pass. Chigusahime successfully finished her pilgrimage and was returning, guarded by many temple guards and foot soldiers, when two assassins leaped out from the corner of an alley and immediately threw two stones at the palanquin. For some reason, their aim was off and the stones did not hit, and the palanquin continued on its way back to the castle with composure.

The two men were immediately captured on the spot by the temple guards, and after a preliminary investigation at the Komonzan, they were thrown into a Raugoku outside the castle as major criminals. The Raugoku, which was reasonably spacious, was full to capacity, and they were thrown into a cell meant for two. In this cell, Terukuniwake and Terukō were being held with their hands still bound. Rēru looked at Terukuniwake and said,

Rēru: 'Are you not the messenger of Terukuniwake-sama? Why have you been put in a place like this?' Terukuniwake: 'I do not recall having done anything particularly wrong, but they said it was the Genmei of the King, and we were both bound tightly hand and foot and thrown in here since last night. I believe that once the suspicion is cleared and I am proven innocent, I will be able to leave this prison in the near future.' Rēru: 'Well, that is an outrageous thing to do. I have heard from Taishi-sama that you are the person of the greatest merit who supported the recent war, so that wicked woman Chigusahime must have done something truly outrageous.' Terukuniwake: 'What, are you someone connected to Taishi Chiwin?' Rēru: 'Yes, in fact, the Taishi and the Princess, as well as the young ladies of the Left and Right Guards, are hiding in my house. Recently, the Kinmaukiubi no Akuko has possessed Chigusahime, and she is trying to drive away meritorious retainers and inflict cruel treatment upon them, so the popularity of the castle town is boiling like a cauldron, and no one knows when a great disturbance will break out. Taishi Chiwin-sama is very worried about this, and is currently gathering representatives of the masses to carry out a reform of the religious government. You will surely be able to leave this prison safely before long.'

『もし師の君様、私は春公で御座います。葵の沼に於てお姿を見失ひ、このトルマン城下へ参りました処、何の手掛りもなく、師の君一行の所在が分りませぬので、看守を出願し、やつと十日以前に合格し、かかる卑しき獄卒を勤めて居ります。どうか御心配下さいますな。時期を考へて屹度お助け致します。王様からの厳命で、「湯も水も与へな、食料は日に一回に致せ、彼等が自然に餓死する迄捨ておけ」との小ラマへの達しが参つたさうで御座います。併し乍ら私が当番に当つて居ります以上、決して御心配下さいますな』

照国『ヤ、お前は春公だつたか、これも全く神様が、かかる事の出で来るべきを前知したまひ、お前を牢番にするやう取計らつて下さつたのだらう。春公覚られないやうに気をつけよ』

春『ハイ、心得まして御座います。どうか御一同様御安心下さいませ』

ラマが巡視に来たと見え、靴音がギウギウと高く聞えて来る。春公は、

『オイ、未決囚共、静に致さねば厳しき懲戒を加へるぞ』

と云ひ乍ら、ラマに最敬礼をやつて居る。高壁の外には新聞号外の配達のリンが耳騒がしく響いて居る。デカタン高原の名物、大暴風は牢獄の桁をギクギク揺つて通る。
