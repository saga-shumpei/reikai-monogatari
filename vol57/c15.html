Chapter 15: 糸瓜 [1465]

糸瓜〔1465〕

§ 1

In the night storm of Mount Terumon, lashed by the dark-dyed faces, two men come walking briskly. At the entrance of the rock cave of the dove, they stand for a while, holding their breath, and peer at the state of things inside.

Princess Kerina, who has been shut away, sings of her inner thoughts in a voice like the ringing of bells.

Ekisu and Heruman, with hearts pounding, draw their bodies close to the iron gate at the entrance, not knowing that their love is unrequited, and it is amusing that they have come to visit.

Princess Kerina, like a Buddha who knows not that there is a person outside the cave, laments her own misfortune.

Her singing voice, which seems to be damp, is it the sound of insects gathering in the autumn fields, or is it the sound of horse bells?

In the sorrowful sound that seems to be confused, she begins to speak, and it is pitiful.

The two men, while keeping their voices low,

Ekisu: 'Oi, Heruman, since that fellow Wax has fled to the depths of Mount Terumon, cursed by the speech of the Akusuikuwai, it is a stroke of luck. Why don't you and I search for her together, win the favor of the Princess, and become the winners of love? There will never be another opportunity like this.'

Heruman: 'Un, that is true. To snatch the six hundred ryo of gold, and to marry the beauty who is sung of as the flower of Mount Terumon and live happily and joyfully, is that not also a fine thing? However, this wisdom borrowed from others might not be enough. If I am carelessly struck with an elbow, I will not be able to recover. We must squeeze out our wisdom and do this smoothly. It will be a disaster if we get too close and it reaches the ears of Princess Kerina-sama. Shall we move four or five ken away from here and consult at our leisure?'

Ekisu: 'Un, that is the superior judgment.'

Saying this, they sit down heavily in the weeds four or five ken away,

Ekisu: 'Oi, I will settle the quarrel for later, but when we have obtained her smoothly, which one will you take?'

Heruman: 'Un, I intend to receive Princess Debisu.'

Ekisu: 'Hen, look at your face in the mirror. If you become the husband of Princess Debisu, you will be the heir of Oguniwake-sama. How could a bumpkin like you manage such a thing?'

Heruman: 'Maa, either one is fine. It is not interesting to count the skin of a raccoon dog before you have caught it. Rather than that, should we not let her sister Naisu choose? That is the most fair thing to do.'

Ekisu: 'That might also be interesting, but if we let the other party choose, it is certain that both sisters will send autumn waves to me. At that time, I will look at the situation and perhaps give you the leftovers.'

Heruman: 'Don't say stupid things. Do not speak so conceitedly while having a long, thin face like a loofah with eyes and a nose attached.'

Ekisu: 'If I am a loofah, you are a pumpkin.'

Heruman: 'What do you mean by pumpkin or loofah? Maa, just watch and see what this pumpkin will do. As the song says, This year is the year of the pumpkin. I have never heard of a year of the loofah since the creation of the world, ehehehe.'

Ekisu: 'Koriya, you stupid pumpkin, what kind of nonsense are you spitting out? Maa, in any case, I am older, so you should follow the etiquette of respecting your elder and leave it to me. It is useless to quarrel when we do not even know the intentions of the other party yet.'

Heruman: 'Un, that is right. It is not interesting to stand out and quarrel in a place like this. First, let us trick Princess Kerina and leave it to her will. Now, let us stand in front of the cave and sing a song to make Princess Kerina think of us.'

The two men, hiding their footsteps, creep up to the side of the cave,

Ekisu: 'You, who were kidnapped by evil men and shut in the rock cave, are dear to me.'

Heruman: 'O Great Imperial God who illuminates the heavens, please come out of the rock cave as soon as possible.'

Ekisu: 'The manifestation of the life of Tajikarao-no-Kami shall open the rock door for Princess Kerina.'

Heruman: 'How clear, how interesting it is to worship your noble figure, I am truly happy.'

Ekisu: 'The figure of Kerinahime-sama, who dwelt in the mansion of the God of Terumon, is graceful.'

Heruman: 'This lady is a Tenkamusau beauty; how could she ever be swayed by Ekisu?'

Ekisu: 'How arrogant the ugly official Heruman is, to come yearning for Kerinahime-sama.'

Heruman: 'Hey Ekisu, your mouth is running too far; who stole the Niyoihotsushiyu?'

Ekisu: 'The one who stole the Niyoihotsushiyu is Wakkusu; it is stored in the mansion for the sake of Ekisu.'

Heruman: 'Do not talk nonsense; is the jewel not the achievement of the Michihiko official of Ananahi?'

Ekisu: 'Is there anyone else in the Sekai who would be such a Baka as to say such things in a place like this?'

Heruman: 'This is a disaster; I have carelessly forgotten about the front of the Gankutsu where Kerinahime-sama is hidden.'

Ekisu: 'That is why you are called a dull-witted man with a Kabochiyazura, with neither rhyme nor Hechima.'

Heruman: 'You Hechimayarau, what are you saying with that pale face, clutching your head that looks like a mortar?'

Ekisu: 'If it has come to this, what care I for Giri or Hechima; come on, let us have a Shiyōbu of Chikarakura.'

Heruman: 'In a world where Genron is valued, Chiyokusetsukaudō is the Kotsuchiyō of Yaban.'

Ekisu: 'Do not talk nonsense; the Saigo Shiyōri is Jitsuriyoku; I shall take Kerina and show you how Migoto it is.'

Heruman: 'You Hechimayarau, no matter how much you are in love with the Hime, if the Mukafu does not listen, you will only look like a Baka.'

Ekisu: 'Beyond this, I must crack your pumpkin head; a man cannot stand if he does not vent his frustration.'

Heruman: 'Hey, do you know the strength of this Hechima and pumpkin?'

Ekisu: 'It is precisely because I know it that I am fighting you.'

Finally, it turns into a great brawl, and leaving the matter of Kerinahime-sama aside, the tall man and the short, stout man grapple with each other, screaming 'un-un' and 'kya-kya', pulling each other's hair and grabbing testicles, fighting with all their might. Kerinahime-sama, hearing the dialogue between the two, forgets that she is in the painful Gankutsu, and before she knows it, she laughs 'ho-ho-ho-ho'. Kerinahime-sama sings quietly.

Kerinahime-sama: 'In the Gankutsu deep in the mountains of Terumon, far from human villages, snatched away by heartless men, even in my body lamenting its misfortune, the time for my heart to be comforted has not come. The pair of men, skilled in evil, stole the treasures of the God's mansion, and together with the devil Wakkusu, they torment my parents and plot terrifying schemes. These Baka men, their eyes dazzled by my beauty, block the front of the Gankutsu, and in their shallowness, they have exposed the black curtains of their hearts to each other. Even if one calls this world blind and deaf, can there be such Baka in the world, who expose their own plots before me, leaving nothing behind? The voices of the filthy love and the monsters of Hechima and Kabochiyazura are pitiful. The world's proverb that there is no medicine for a Baka is truly before my eyes, and how amusing it is to behold. Whether the sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth sinks, and even if my life is lost, I have determined in my heart that the Kyūdō-sama of Tenkamusau divine power shall be my husband for two lives. No matter how handsome a man may be, in my eyes they are but ogres, and even to look back at them is disgusting. The two Baka slaves, Ekisu and Heruman, have begun to fight over each other's lives, and how amusing it is to see them suffer. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara.'

Kerinahime-sama: 'May the divine spirit bless me, and may Kyūdō-sama save me as soon as possible. I pray with awe before the Great Kami, asking that he protect the life of the Kyōshi.'

Outside, Ekisu and Heruman are covered in blood, their faces scratched and torn, fighting for their lives with their last breaths. It was Michihiko of the Ananahi-kyō who arrived there, bringing Sumāto with him. Sumāto approached the entrance of the Gankutsu, sniffing sharply with his keen sense of smell, and as if he had confirmed that Kerinahime-sama was there, he wagged his tail incessantly and began to growl 'woo-woo'. Michihiko called out from the entrance of the Gankutsu,

Michihiko: 'I am Michihiko, a Kami-tsukasa of the Ananahi-kyō. I have been taken care of at the mansion since the other day and have been searching for your whereabouts, but I have finally determined that you are hidden here, and I have come to welcome you. Please wait a moment. I will open the door of this entrance now.'

Kerinahime remained silent for a while and was lost in thought.

Kerinahime: 'How could the himegami inside the tightly shut gankutsu yield to a person whom she knows not? Even a moment ago, two strange men came and tried to entice me. Though my body were to rot inside the gankutsu, I shall never yield my body to hateful men.'

Michihiko: 'This is astonishing, the onkotoba of Kerinahime. In the words of a divine messenger, there is no falsehood. Calm your murakimo heart and come forth; I have come just as the kami wills.'

Kerinahime: 'Obeying the words of a man who possesses mercy, I would leave the gankutsu; open it quickly.'

Faster than one could take up the rock fragments of atari in his hand, Michihiko snapped the jiyōmahe and at last opened it with a snap.

Kerinahime: 'Oh, you are called Michihiko-sama; how very well you have come to save me. This gōn I shall never forget.'

Michihiko: 'Come, let us return quickly. Your goriyōshin are waiting and longing for you. Furthermore, we must rescue your anesama, and we must also rescue Kiudausama, so come out quickly.'

Kerinahime: 'Yes, I am arigata, but my feet are trembling and do not stand at all, so I am troubled.'

Michihiko: 'Ah, that must be so. Being shut up in such a place, your legs must have grown weak. Come, please be piggybacked upon my back. The dog here is named Smart, and is a giken who has helped me many times. As long as this one is here, it is daijiyōbu.'

He thrusts out his back. Kerinahime felt as secure as if boarding an ōbune, and obediently allowed herself to be carried on Michihiko's back, at last leaving the bitter gankutsu. The light of the stars shone across the whole heavens as if scattering kinshiya and ginshiya.

Kerinahime: 'Moshi Michihiko-sama, two villains have fallen here; would it be well to let them escape notice like this?'

Michihiko: 'Indeed, I was too taken with your side and had forgotten the disposal of the villains. Come hime-sama, please get down for a moment. Let us shut them up in your former dwelling at last, ahahahahaha.'

Kerinahime: 'Ohohohohoho, Michihiko-sama, they are thoroughly bad fellows, so please throw them in and keep them safely until they reform.'

Saying 'It is well,' Michihiko grasped their feet in both hands using his mighty strength, dragging them over the grass. Like dragging even a cat, he tossed them in with a poof, closed the door of the entrance with a click, and carefully propped up a brace.

Michihiko: 'Hey, great thieves of the unperishable jewel of kongō, Ekis and Herman, both of you would do well to enjoy a comfortable retirement here for a while. If you manage to reform, it is not as though I might not let you out again. Since I am not an evil-drunken monster, I save the weak and punish those who are strong in evil. You should consider that turning out this way is entirely rust emerging from one's own body, and resign yourselves to it.'

Ekis: 'Moshimoshi Michihiko-sama, please by all means save the two of us. In exchange, we shall capture Wax and bring him in and put him here, for the truth of the matter is that we stole at the command of Wax. The principal culprit is Wax.'

Michihiko: 'Ahahahahaha, well it is pitiable, but please take a little rest. Come hime-sama, let us return.'

Carrying her on his back and guided by Smart, he proceeds toward the gankutsu where Debisuhime was shut up. The two beat upon the door of the entrance wildly and without measure,

Both: 'Save us, save us! Are there no kami or hotoke in this world? Ah, what a regret, what a pity!'

and complain of nothing but self-serving things. From deep within the gankutsu came a disgusting, hair-raising laughter: 'Kera kera kera.'

三千彦『オイ金剛不壊の玉の大盗人、エキス、ヘルマンの両人暫く此処に楽隠居でもして居るがよからう。改心が出来たら又出してやらうまいものでもない。俺は悪酔怪員でないから、弱き女を助け、悪に強き奴を懲らしてやるのだ。斯うなるのも皆身から出た錆だと思うて観念したがよからう』

エキス『モシモシ三千彦様、何卒吾等二人を助けて下さいませ。其代りワックスを此処へ捉まへて来て入れるやうに致しますから、実の所はワックスの命令によつて盗んだので厶います。張本人はワックスで厶います』

三千彦『アハハハハハ、マア気の毒だけれど些と御休息なさいませ。サア姫様帰りませう』

と背に負ひ、スマートに道案内されて、デビス姫の閉ぢ籠められた岩窟を指して進み行く。二人は入口の戸を無性矢鱈に叩き、

両人『助けて呉れエ助けて呉れエ、此世に神や仏は無いものか。エエ残念や口惜しやなア』

と身勝手な事許り愚痴つて居る。岩窟の奥の方から『ケラ　ケラ　ケラ』と嫌らしい、身の毛のよだつやうな笑ひ声が聞えて来た。
