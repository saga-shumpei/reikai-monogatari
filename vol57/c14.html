Chapter 14: 人畜 [1464]

人畜〔1464〕

§ 1

In the rock cave of the camphor tree at the foot of Mount Terumon, Debisu-hime, the eldest daughter of the Kamuyakata, had been confined under the guise of being a fox-spirit monster. The men and women of all ages in Miyamachi, for the most part, did not know she was the true princess; they all believed her to be a transformation of a monster, and fearing her, no one dared to approach. Wax, under the cover of night, would secretly light a lamp and visit the princess in the cave, feigning kindness. The princess sat in the darkness of the cave, singing of her inner thoughts.

Debisu-hime: 'The world has turned into eternal darkness, the light of the sun and moon has faded, and demons run rampant in all four directions. They harm the good and aid the evil, devising every possible means, and it is lamentable that they now target the Kamuyakata of Mount Terumon. Wax, the son of the steward, flaunts goodness on the surface, but while speaking sweet words, he is a terrifying villain like a bee with a sting in its tail. He has long desired me, approaching with a lecherous grin, and his style is truly repulsive. Though I am a woman and destined to have a husband, I would at least wish to revere a manly hero as my lord, to protect my father's house well, to comfort my mother's heart, and for the sake of the gods and the world, to uphold sincerity alone. Praised as a mirror of this world, I prayed morning and evening before the gods, wishing to fulfill my duty as a child of the gods, born to sprinkle the dew of grace upon the people. Yet, what demon's interference could it be? The sacred ground opened by Daikokunushi has, in no time, become a den of evil gods. Wax, the son of the steward, who should have served my house with all his might, has become a slave to lust. Every day and every night, he plots evil, stole the wish-granting jewel left by Daikokunushi, brings disaster upon my house, torments my father and mother, and schemes to make me his wife by force. How hateful his behavior is! My father, suffering in his heart, has fallen into a grave illness, and his life hangs in the balance. Tears of grief fall incessantly in the Kamuyakata, and there is no clearing of the skies. Oh, have pity on the hearts of the suffering parent and child! Deep in the terrifying mountains, alone, I went night after night to perform water ablutions. At the end of the three-times-seven days, I encountered a most terrifying thief, and just as my life was about to be cut short, the divine official of the Sangokyo, Kyudo-koji, came accompanied by his younger sister Kerina. They saved my life, and with hearts of infinite mercy, they even saved Bell and Hell—how heroic they were! I followed the Koji, walking steadily along the dew-covered field paths toward the Pine Forest. When we rested for a moment, Wax, with gongs and drums, making a great din and raising banners, arrived. He incited many townspeople, slandered me as a fox incarnation, and after binding my hands and feet tightly, threw me into this rock cave—how terrifying it is!'

Debisu-hime: 'Where is the wish-granting jewel now? I wonder if my father's life still continues; I wish for a letter from a wild goose, but there is no way to inquire. When I think of what has become of Kyudo-koji and his sister, my heart feels as if it will burst. How can I heal this suffering? There is no way, and I have thought many times of biting my tongue to die. Yet, wait a while. As long as my aged parents are in this world, if I were to lose my life out of impatience, the sin of unfilial piety would pile up, and I fear for the future. I draw the bow of my heart once more. My thoughts, like the arrow-bamboo, shall not fail to reach their mark, for I protect the Sangokyo.'

Debisu-hime: 'O Great God of Kunihachitachi, O Great God of Toyokunushi, protect our helpless lives. Kannagara. I pray with reverence before your presence. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, the divine teachings of the Sango, which are sincerity alone, shall save the world. The words of my master, who taught me to rely on the saving God, are not forgotten even now. Oh, how I miss you, Kyudo-koji. Stay well, and you too, my sister. And in connection with that, Hell-shi, bathe your body in the dew of the Great God's grace as soon as possible, and be freed from the suffering within the rock cave. Kannagara.'

Praying to the Kami, The plum blossoms of the Sanzensekai Though there are instances of them opening all at once, To what should they be compared, the plum and the cherry, How is it that we, sisters, Are like unopened buds of flowers? Will not someone quickly open the ihato, Some samurai of deep emotion, Come seeking and open them? Though in the sky of the heart the jitsugetsu Shine across, what shall be done with this utsusomi body That cannot have its way? Separated by a wall of steel, How wretched it is not to see even the light Of the utsushiyo, thoroughly darkened! Kamunahohi who created this world, Ōnahohi of broad heart, In the human world, simply whatever happens, Minaho, kikinaho in accordance with Nahohi, Proclaim and rectify the misfortunes of the body.

I cling to the wide sleeves Of the Ōkami who grants salvation. Though I do not grudge the inochi of me, a humble woman, Upon the persons of my father and mother, Save the treasure of the hotsushiyu of niyoi, Ōmikami! Ah, Tsudoe Kami-Kami, May your august spirit be blessed and prosper!

Wakkusu listened thoroughly to this song across the iron gate, folded his hands, heaved a sigh, and spoke to himself,

Wakkusu: 'Ah, judging from the manner of the present song, Princess Debisu seems to resent me considerably. This will not be won over by ordinary means. I must somehow skillfully frame words to win the princess's favor and make her think she has no resentment toward Wakkusu. Alas, what a troubling matter this is. ...Well, well, I shall press this fellow Ekisu hard. If I do so, Princess Debisu will surely clear her doubts and trust me. By any chance, if that fellow Mitsuhiko were to discover that she is hidden in such a place and attempt to rescue her, that indeed would be a tremendous disaster. No matter what, before the night is through, I must deal with this skillfully, or else I will have to suffer the regret of Rokushō Jukiku. That being said, no good wisdom suddenly arises; what a troublesome thing.'

So muttering to himself, he had been pondering for about half an hour. Wakkusu appeared to have struck upon some good plan, and with a nasty smile floating on his face, taking advantage of the moon sinking behind the western mountains, he advanced and drew close to the entrance,

Wakkusu: 'Hey, hey, whatever personage might you be, my august lord, who are confined in this cavern? According to rumors on the road and talk in the streets, you are said to be the incarnation of the evil fox of San-Go-Kyō, the Takakura Inari. Believing that you, a precious princess, were swallowed up by the Takakura Inari, who was mistaken for the great villain Ekisu, and that you are skillfully transformed into that appearance, I have closed you up within this cavern, fearfully desiring to take revenge for the princess. This is not at all something arising from the heart of Wakkusu; yet, my sincere feeling to avenge the princess's enemies could not brook being held back by spear or shield, and so, thinking you to be the incarnation of a white fox, I mistakenly confined you. In last night's dream revelation of the Brahman Ōkami, the princess who has entered this cavern is said to be the true princess, and though my heart raced like bamboo arrows to set forth immediately and offer my rescue, the villains Ekisu and Hermann were watching my surroundings. Thus, regrettably waiting for nightfall, I have now come for the purpose of welcoming you. Princess, the jeweled orb of the nioyo hotsushiyu was also stolen by both Ekisu and Hermann, but having seen through this with my insightful eye, I have immediately returned it to Okuniwake-sama, so please rest easy about this matter. Also, since your august father is still in good health, please do not afflict your mind with anxiety. Both your parents have praised the loyalty of this Wakkusu, and now, having fixed upon you as the wife of Princess Debisu and having appointed you as the successor of the Kamidate, from this very day forth you are my wife. To spare neither body nor life for my wife, this Wakkusu—please rest easy. Moreover, since your younger sister Princess Kerina is also safe, please rejoice. This is entirely the result of Wakkusu's utmost efforts; please do not doubt me at all. I, Wakkusu, have now come secretly, eluding human eyes, to welcome you.'

Thus he shouted in a loud voice. From within, Princess Debisu spoke in a faint voice,

Princess Debisu: 'Who is it that visits me, a humble woman, in this terrifying deep mountain despite the night? Are you a fox, a raccoon dog, or a hobgoblin? Quickly depart from here. Such raving talk I have no ears to listen to. Pah, how filthy!'

Wakkusu: 'Oh dear, Davis Hime-sama, I am neither a Kori nor an Eukuwai. I am Wakkusu, the son of a Karei, in Shiyōshinshiyōmei. You cannot imagine how much Kurau I have endured to save you. Please, I ask for your Gosuiriyō.'

Davis Hime: 'Whether you are a Ningen or a Kuwaibutsu, I do not know, but you are a Furugitsune or a Furudanuki wearing the face of a man. Nay, you are a Daieukuwai. You are an Akugiyakubudau Jiyaki and Rasetsu; it is filthy even to hear your Kotoba. Even if you were the true Wakkusu, I have no Shiyozon to exchange words with you. To come sneaking into this Gankutsu in the Yachiu, carrying such a Hiretsu Konjiyō, you are a Haraguro Kusemono. Mohaya, I have become the Jiyōu of this Gankutsu, saved by Amata angels, and I am living in peace without any lack of food. I would never accept help from a dog-like beast such as you, even if I were to die. Rather than harboring such impossible desires, tuck your tail and leave at once, you human-beast slave!'

With these words, she scolded him severely. Wakkusu, enraged, struck the iron gate with a stone, banging it with all his might to intimidate her, and said:

Wakkusu: 'Listen, Hime-sama, even if you say such things, there is no food inside that Gankutsu, and there is no reason for angels to descend into a notorious Makutsu. Instead of putting on a brave front, settle your mind and listen to the love of this Wakkusu. If there is a heart for fish, there is a heart for water; there is no one other than this Wakkusu who can protect the Terumon mountain Shinkan and the safety of your parents. Do not say such thoughtless things, and say yes. If you persist and refuse, I have my own Kakugo. If my love is not fulfilled, I shall take revenge; are you not afraid that I might torture you to death?'

Davis Hime: 'Ugh, how filthy, you villain! I shall not forgive your crime of being rude to your master; prepare yourself!'

As she said this, she picked up a piece of rock and threw it at Wakkusu through the iron lattice window. Wakkusu, furious like a raging fire, brandished a bamboo spear and thrust it through the fence, aiming at Davis Hime, wishing to pierce her bones. Separated by the narrow Gankutsu, Davis Hime had no room to dodge. At the very instant she was about to be stabbed to death, a fierce dog came flying through the air from nowhere, and while growling 'Woo-woo, Uwah-uwah,' it bit into Wakkusu's sword arm. Wakkusu, with a cry of 'Ah!', collapsed weakly on the spot. The fierce dog Sumāto looked back repeatedly, scolding him with 'Woo-woo-woo,' and then hid its form somewhere.

After a while, Wakkusu regained consciousness, wiped the blood from his arm, bound it with grass from the surroundings, and crawled away, slinking back to his own mansion. From the thickets of trees nearby, the voice of an owl cried, 'Aho-aho, Gorottokahese, Yararetana, Bakabaka.' Suddenly, from the branches of a mountain persimmon tree, the sound of cicadas cried, 'Zama, Zama, Zama, Miin, Miin, Miin, Miin,' and the sound of crickets chirped, 'Kera, Kera, Kera.'

デビス姫『エー、汚らはしや、悪党者、主人に向つて無礼の罪、赦しは致さぬぞ、覚悟しや』

と云ひ乍ら岩片を拾ふより早く鉄の格子窓の間よりワックスに向つて投げつけた。ワックスは烈火の如く憤り竹槍を扱いて垣越しに骨も通れよと、デビス姫目蒐けて突き出した。狭き岩窟に隔てられ、デビス姫は身を避くる余地が無い。あわや突殺されむとする一刹那、何処ともなく宙を飛んで駆け来る猛犬、『ウーウー、ウワッウワッ』と威喝し乍らワックスの利腕に噛り付いた。ワックスはアツと云うた儘、其場に脆くも倒れた。猛犬スマートは後振り返り振り返り『ウーウーウー』と叱りつけ乍ら、何処ともなく姿を隠した。

暫らくあつてワックスは気がつき、腕の血を拭ひ四辺の草にて括り乍ら這々の態にて一先づ吾館へスゴスゴと帰り行く。四辺の木の茂みから梟の声『アホーアホー、ゴロツトカヘセ、ヤラレタナー、バカバカ』と啼いて居る。俄に山柿の枝から蝉の声『ザマ　ザマ　ザマ、ミーン　ミーン　ミーン　ミーン』、蟋蟀の声『ケラ　ケラ　ケラ』。
