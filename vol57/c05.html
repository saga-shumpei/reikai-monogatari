§ 1

In the reception room of the mansion, three men—El, Oaks, and Burma—placed a desk in the center and sat cross-legged, having pleasant dreams while competing with one another in telling ephemeral stories of worldly success.

El: 'Hey, Oaks, despite being just a mere gatekeeper, you barged right through the reception checkpoint and forced your way into the inner chambers, but it looks like you took quite a severe scolding from the mistress, seeing your face all swollen up like that. I honestly thought you were an owl monster.'

Oaks: 'Hah! Gatekeeper this, gatekeeper that—stop talking down to me in that grand manner. You balls-crushing general, I am a bit different from the gatekeeper Oaks of up until now. From now on, I am going to become the steward of this mansion, take up residence in the inner quarters, and perform my duties as Oaks, so you must obey my instructions in everything. Right, Burma? You know all about it, don't you?'

Furu: 'That's right. Well, it is probably no more than a dream-like stewardship or something. If you go about blabbing things like "steward" in a place like this, you might have all your plans reduced to a castle in the air right when it matters most. Those who succeed keep their mouths shut. As they say, the silent person catches the night-shining jewel, so you shouldn't go rattling on about everything. Silence is the highest virtue.'

Oaks: 'Don't talk nonsense. I have already obtained testimony directly from the mistress. No matter what anyone says, we will make Wax-sama the master of this mansion, have him joyfully consummate his marriage with Princess Davis, and with this Oaks becoming the steward and my younger sister Princess Kerina soon returning, I will become Princess Kerina's husband, renew church and state affairs, restore public discipline, and bring about the age of Yao and Shun. We don't need useless old fogies, panicked cowards, or balls-crushing receptionists like the ones we've had until now. Heh-heh-heh-heh!'

El: 'Itsu-hi-hi-hi-hi, ouch-ouch-ouch-ouch! You are spouting such utter nonsense that it tickles me so much it echoes in my balls, and my balls hurt so bad I can't even laugh properly! You've had the days grow long, so you must have been dreaming up something like that. Instead of all that, why don't you promptly and faithfully attend to your duties as gatekeeper? A sudden illness of our master could happen at any moment, so this is no time for you to be dozing off.'

Oaks: 'Hey, El, didn't you go around spreading the word that the master had already passed away? What a thoroughly panicked coward you are.'

El: 'It was a settled matter. We have to handle things smoothly, or the accounts won't square, don't you think? Even if he weren't a sick man, it's only natural for an old person to die first. Since we never know when the master might actually kick the bucket, I issued a notice just to wake up the townspeople and ran a preliminary rehearsal. How could a mere gatekeeper possibly understand the mind of a hero? Ahem, ihim, ouch-ouch-ouch-ouch! Confound it, my balls keep talking back to me and I can't help it at all.'

Oaks: 'Hey, El, be serious for a moment and listen to me. Truly, starting today, I am the steward. And Wax-sama is the heir of our house. Then this Burma will sit at the reception desk, and you will be granted a leave of absence to rest until your balls heal. Once I become steward, I will rarely assign anyone below the rank of receptionist, so you had better sit quietly and keep in line.'

Oaks: 'Good grief! Even if I die, I will not quit the reception desk. No matter how much you may have become steward, my position at reception has been ordered by the Great Lord of the Capital of Haruna, so you surely cannot move my position at all. Rather than saying such things, you would do well to faithfully attend to your duties as gatekeeper.'

Oaks: 'Fine then, I'll show you how to get dismissed from reception. Though you are a receptionist, you rushed out beyond the gate and went around spreading the word that the master—who cannot possibly die—had passed away, thereby creating a commotion throughout the town. If I were to report this to the Great Lord, dismissal would come as fast as dusk, and your very head would go flying off your neck. How is that? Even so, does it not trouble you at all?'

Because the house steward's role is higher in rank, he will endure without taking a princess. That way there will be no unfairness, right?'

Thus, before they knew it, they became engrossed in their talk and were chattering in loud voices. From earlier, Wakkusu had pressed his ear against the wall, concealed his body, and listened to the talk of the three, but since his anger swelled beyond endurance, he quietly entered the large toilet, brought a filthy thing in a long-handled ladle, appeared before the three while concealing his own face, scattered it with a splash over their faces, and at the very moment of darting away, caught his foot on a tear in the tatami mat and fell down flat on his face. At the moment he fell, he struck his high nose against the threshold separating the rooms, held his breath, and suffered without stirring an inch.

The three were suddenly splashed with smelly stuff all over their faces, and while pressing their faces with handkerchiefs, at the very moment they ran to go and wash toward the kitchen, they tripped over Wakkusu's body and fell down with a thud. One after another, all four became covered in dung, rolled over upside down, and were groaning with groans.

At this sound, Okunohime ran to this place and when she looked, an indescribable foul fragrance pierced her nose in full force. When the hime drew near pinching her nose and looked, there was one long-handled ladle covered in dung and four men covered in dung, who had fallen right there.

三人は不意に臭い物を顔一面にかけられ、顔をハンカチーフにて抑へ乍ら、炊事場の方へ洗ひに行かうと走つた途端に、ワックスの体に躓きバタリと倒れた。次から次から四人が糞まぶれになつて引つくり覆り、ウンウン唸いて居る。此物音に小国姫は此場に走り来り見れば、何とも云へぬ臭い香がプンプンと鼻をつく。姫は鼻を抓み乍ら近寄り見れば、糞まぶれの長柄杓が一本と、四人の男が糞まぶれになつて、其処へ倒れ居たり。

§ 4

Okunihime: 'A man who set his gut-kuso stumbled on his feet and tasted kuso.

A kuso-yatsuko whose old crone's body was also smeared with kuso, I am utterly disgusted with such a smelly fellow.

By the tenbatsu for plotting a monokusa scheme, the man must have collapsed with spasms.

Has the foul-hearted monban of Ōkusu suffered a great drenching of ōkuso today?

A bakamono whose kintama was stepped on by an ox, and now here again is doused in kuso and howling.

Whether it is wax or kuso, I know not, but whichever it may be, what a smelly fellow he is.'

Wax: 'Three kuso-yatsuko gathered together in the midst thereof, smeared with kuso at the kuso-baba's house.'

Okunihime: 'Wax, do you remain forgetting not the kotoba that you spoke to me, calling me a kuso-baba.

Revolving various smelly shian, the present tenbatsu is that you have tasted kuso.'

Ōkusu: 'To one who becomes the karei of Telmon's mansion, what are the kurau of kuso to him?'

Okunihime: 'Various smelly fellows gather together and scatter kuso in this mansion.

From this time, I shall report it to the Great God of Haruna's capital, the kamubashira Ōkuronushi.

As for all things, the kamuzukasa Michihiko has already admonished your schemes.

Bakamono who take advantage of the troubles of a person's house, revolve kusojian, and throw away your own bodies.'

Ōkusu: 'Michihiko is a mahafutsukahi of Ananai-kyō; I shall report in detail to Ōkuronushi.

If Ōkuronushi hears this arisama, it will be the end of Okunihime's life.'

At that moment, Ekisu and Heruman hastily came running up, pinching their noses against the punpung-smelling shiuki,

Ekisu: 'Moshi okusama, is this not Wax and the rest of the gang?

Did you douse them with kuso, strike them down, and try to take their lives fearing the exposure of your conspiracy to these four persons?

This is outrageous.

Now that it has come to this, even though he is our master, we will show no mercy.

Come, Wax-sama, take heart.

From here I will send a swift horse messenger to the capital of Haruna and avenge you.'

So saying, tucking up their skirts, Ekisu and Heruman dashed out toward the front gate like clouds and mist.

The four managed to get up, finished washing their bodies, and entering a single room, they put aside their quarrel for a while and held a secret consultation to once again fulfill their wicked ambitions.

Okunihime, worrying about her husband's illness, hurriedly left the place and concealed her body in the inner room.
