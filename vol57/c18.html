Chapter 18: 翼琴 [1468]

翼琴〔1468〕

§ 1

Okuniwake's severe illness seemed to be encouraged by the safe return of the two sisters, increasing in vigor moment by moment, and had now come to a condition no longer requiring anxiety. The joy of Okuniwake's family, beginning with Okunihime, was beyond comparison. Moreover, since Hel's injuries were relatively light, thanks to the meticulous nursing and care of Princess Kelina, he headed toward recovery day by day, and reached the point where, led by Princess Kelina, he could walk in the rear garden.

Now Princess Devis entered the detached room together with Michihiko, meditated upon the Ōkami, finished praying for the safety of the mansion, and intimately indulged in talk of all directions. Devis took out the clavichord (yokukin) after a long time, and in order to reward Michihiko for his labors, while raising a subtle and delicate voice and skillfully playing the cords with her fingertips, she entrusted the depth of her heart to the strings and began to recite.

Devisheime: 'Of the Imperial Gods of the Heavenly august country, Pure, noble, and lovely, Exalted beyond all compare, Opening the precious divine teachings for the lands in all directions, Among heaven and earth, The living creatures and common people, not to mention further, Down to birds, beasts, insects, And even the single leaves of grass, Wishing to bestow the dew of grace, Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, Toyokunihime no Ōkami, Awing the precious divine words of Haniyasuhiko and Haniyasuhime, Manifesting at Jerusalem, the city of gods, At the foot of Mount Golden, Opening the teachings and making the eighty islands and eighty countries of the lands in all directions Into a golden world, To transfer the heavenly paradise upon the earth, The Minutama, the great god Susanoo no Ōkami, While rousing majestic bravery and forceful stamping, And stirring up majestic censure and rebukes, Sending forth the Mukatsugami, Eight-Forked Yamata no Orochi, ugly foxes, And warped demons who disturb this world, To illuminate the entire world widely with the mind of boundless benevolence and compassion, On mornings of snow or evenings of frost, Combing his hair amidst the storms, And exposing his august body to the rains, Continuing the long journey of wandering hither and thither, Beginning with Mount Golden, Descending to the mansion of Saien, the Caucasus Mountains, universal sins of the world, Bearing the thousand seats of atonement, Righteous in heart and exceedingly pure, Sending gentle and numerous divine officials To use them in all directions, The Kanagara.

For the sake of the gods, for the sake of the Way, Offering my humble body, Together with the official Michihiko, To the teachings of Brahmanism whose souls were extracted by the Yamata no Orochi, Offering sincere devotion to the lord official of the pillar of Brahmanism, Daikokunushi, And saving him, Removing without exception the divisions of the ways of Brahmanism and the Three-Five teachings, together Combining our strengths with the august teachings of our ancestral gods who created this world, Binding our hearts together as one liver of the village, Making the clouded present world into the world of Bravasa, Before the gods of heaven and earth, We shall establish merits and recompense the grace of mountains and seas.

Michihiko-sama, official of the Way where the spirits of the divine teachings prosper and bless, Pouring the Mizutama into the burning flame Within my small breast, Let me rest as early as possible! However vast the world may be, Gazing up and calling you my lord husband, There is no warrior whom I shall serve other than you, Yet, official Michihiko, Since you are wise in knowledge, In the fore-fronts of islands we look upon, in the corners of rocky shores we gaze upon without missing, You might take a young-grass wife, Since I am a woman, There is no man other than you, There is no husband outside of you, Under the fluttering of the patterned fence, upon the steaming bedding, Embracing your youthful budding breast like rustling light snow, Intertwining our white arms, Striking and caressing, true jade hands and jade hands, Wrap around and around, for a hundred lengths, Lie down to sleep and partake of the rich divine sake! My dear lord, my beloved man, When the sun hides in the green mountains, The night of crow-feather darkness will surely come forth! 原文（近代文語）

小国別の重病は、二人の姉妹の無事に帰り来りしに力づきしものと見え、時々刻々に元気を増し、最早憂慮を要せざる容態となつて来た。小国姫初め家族一同の喜びは譬ふるに物なき程であつた。又求道居士、ヘルの負傷も比較的浅かりしため、ケリナ姫の注意周到なる看護によつて日に日に快方に向ひ、ケリナ姫に手を引かれ、裏の庭園を散歩し得る迄に到つた。

扨てデビス姫は三千彦と共に離れの間に入つて、大神を念じ、館の無事を祈願し終り、睦じく四方八方の話に耽つた。デビスは久し振りにクラヴィコード（翼琴）を取り出し、三千彦の労を犒はむため、微妙なる声音を張り上げて指先巧にコードを弾じながら、心の丈を糸に托して述べ初めた。

デビス姫『久方の天津御国の皇神の 清き尊き麗しき 外に例もあら尊 珍の御教を四方の国 開かせ給ひて天地の 中に生とし生ける者 蒼生は云ふも更 鳥獣や虫けらや 草の片葉に至る迄 恵の露を与へむと 国治立の大御神 豊国姫の大御神 埴安彦や埴安姫の 珍の御言を畏みて 神の都のエルサレム 黄金山下に現まして 教を開き四方の国 島の八十島八十国を 黄金世界に作り上げ 天国浄土を地の上に

§ 2

Having finished singing, she set the clavichord aside, and while blushing, she bashfully offered the jade cup to Michihiko. Michihiko, feeling somewhat troubled by the kindness, with his heart pounding, silently took the cup offered by Hime, received the Daigomi poured to the brim, held it up, drank it down in one gulp with gratitude, and having steeled his resolve, sang a Kaheshiuta in a clear voice.

Michihiko: 'Looking up at the high, eternal / Amatsusora, descending / To save and protect the various things / Beneath the Ame, the Ubusuna / Kami, in the Kamuyakata, eternally / Residing, the Mizumitama / Kamususanō no Ōkami / Who governs the Oshihe no Yakata / By the Mikoto of the Lord of Yashima / As the teacher, Tamakuniwake / Accompanied by Masumi no Hiko and Itahiko / We worship the Medeta Kamuyakata / While on a Tabimakura / In light Minari and Idetachi / Climbing and descending the steep slopes of Kajika Pass / Encountering various hardships, at last / Crossing the wilderness where wolves howl / The four of us together, intending to advance to the capital of Haruna.'

Michihiko: 'Just as we were coming, attacked / By the ugly officials of the Brahman / The four teacher and disciples were scattered / Separated, not knowing where each had gone / Where is my teacher now / How are my companions of the Oshihe / The summer sky weighs on my heart / The mountains and fields laugh, the fresh green / Buds blowing, even more joyfully / The branches fluttering in the wind / Performing a dance, bravely / Laughing and flourishing, yet Michihiko's / Heart is dark as the fifth month / Hiding his form in the green leaves / The cuckoo singing is also unusual / Eight thousand and eight times, his voice hoarse / Seeking a way, weeping and sobbing / Continuing the lonely journey / Trudging through Aonogahara / Driven by the fierce wind.'

Michihiko: 'Saved by the smart incarnation of Kami / From a dangerous life / Crossing the Anburakku River / Just as we were advancing / Toward the sacred ground of Mount Terumon / I happened to meet the figure of Oguni-hime / By the side of the road / As requested, I stole deep into the Kamuyakata / And proclaimed the Oshihe of the three-five / The eight-fold black clouds wrapping this mansion / Were blown away by the breath of the Kami / Of the Shinatobe / The sun and moon in the sky, Terumon / Became a Medeta mansion / Just then, the Magatsukami / Raged and went mad, terribly / Bearing a grudge against this mansion / It is wretched to see it rampaging / Due to my own lack of vigilance, Michihiko.'

Michihiko: 'Caught by the Magatsukami / With no mercy, bound / With rough ropes, to escape / Both way and means were thrown / Into the Arakawa, and the dew-like life / Of the Tama no O was lost / Just as I was wandering / At the crossroads of the Kamireikai / The grace of Kami was very deep / Sending the fierce dog Smart / To welcome and return my soul to the Genze / Call, call, Kannagara, Kami, Kami / How precious is the grace of Kami / Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over / Whether the moon waxes or wanes / Whether the stars fall from the sky / Whether the great ocean dries up / Would I, Michihiko, who dedicated my life to the path / Regret this dew-like life?'

Michihiko: 'Seeing the hardships of the mansion / Unable to abandon it, as Michihiko's / Heart-horse advances / Risking my life, I sought / The rock cave in the depths of the deep mountains of Terumon / Hiding from human eyes / I rescued the two Hime / Whose hearts and forms are beautiful / And finally returned here / The illness of my father, which weighed on my heart / Has also gradually healed / The Kyūdō-koji and the Hel-tsukasa / The souls of the two are also at peace / Returned to the original state, bravely / Guided by Kerina-hime / Wandering through the flower-blooming Kami-en / With a joyful heart / Rejoicing in the dew of grace / I have become one who enjoys it / Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami.'

Michihiko: 'How precious is the grace of Kami / Nevertheless, Debisu-hime / The heart of your life / Though it reaches my sincere heart / I am one who bears the great mission / Of the precious Ōkami / In the midst of that Kami-waza / How could Kami permit / An unexpected marriage / Please give up on this matter alone / And let me fulfill the mission / That I bear, Kannagara / Swearing to Kami, before you / I clarify my sincerity and proclaim it / Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami / May your spirit be blessed.'

Having finished singing, he had explained in great detail that although he was truly grateful for the kind intentions of Debisu-hime, he could not accept them because he was in the midst of serving the Kami-waza with his teacher, bearing the mission of Kami. Ah, how will the love affair between these two be resolved?

原文（近代文語）

と歌ひ終り、クラヴィコードを傍に直し、顔を赤らめ乍ら、いと恥づかしげに三千彦に向つて玉の盃をさし出した。三千彦は聊か有難迷惑の体にて、胸を轟かせながら黙つて姫のさし出す盃を取り醍醐味を浪々と注がれて押し頂き、感謝と共にグツと飲み乾し、決心の臍を固めて声も涼しく返歌を歌ふ。

三千彦『仰げば高し久方の 天津空より下りまし 天の下なる諸々を 救ひ守らす産土の 神の館に永久に 鎮まり居ます瑞霊 神素盞嗚の大神の 教の館を統べたまふ 八島の主の御言もて 玉国別の師の君と 真純の彦や伊太彦を 伴ひ目出度神館 伏し拝みつつ旅枕 軽き身装の扮装に 河鹿峠の急坂を 登りつ下りつ種々の 難みに遇ひて漸くに 虎狼の吠猛る 荒野を渉り四人連れ ハルナの都に進まむと

と歌ひ終り、デビス姫の御親切なる御志は、実に感謝に堪へないが、神の使命を帯び、吾師の君と共に神業に仕ふる中途なれば折角のお志なれどお断わり致すとの意味を、いと細やかに述べ終つたのである。アア此両人の恋愛関係は如何にして落着するであらうか。
