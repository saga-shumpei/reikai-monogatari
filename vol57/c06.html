Chapter 6: 強印 [1456]

強印〔1456〕

§ 1

About seventeen or eighteen chō deep in the valley from the mansion of Mount Terumon, there is a deep cavern called the Serpent's Cavern. Inside it, Michihiko had been forcefully pushed and confined, and two guards were keeping strict watch over him by the orders of Wax.

Kō: 'Hey, no matter what, the sorcerer who was shoved in here seems to have done an outrageous thing. They say he's an outrageous villain who stole the jewel of the wish-granting niyoihotsushiyu, slandered that it was hidden by Lord Wax, tried to become the husband of Princess Davis, and intended to occupy this mansion, but since he's a sorcerer, who knows when he might break this iron gate and come out? If he comes out, I don't know what kind of treatment we'll be subjected to. No matter how much daily pay we receive, I'd rather be excused from such a perilous business.'

Otsu: 'What, don't worry. A sorcerer's spells might work to a certain extent, but now his tricks have run out, he's been caught by everyone, and he's been shoved into a place like this, so he's already safe. There's little chance of him coming out. If we stand guard like this for ten or twenty days, he'll starve and die; if so, that's safe. We only need to receive our daily wages, you see.'

Kō: 'However, if this fellow dies and turns into a vengeful ghost, that really would be a catastrophe. Wouldn't there be some way to decline this assignment?'

Otsu: 'How could there be such a way? Since we always ask Wax-danna for favors in times of trouble and are helped by him, we are returning his favor at a time like this. Thanks to Lord Wax, at a time when the whole town of Miya was facing hardship, the wish-granting niyoihotsushiyu jewel that this fellow stole was discovered, and even our lives were saved, so we must engage in a battle of endurance with him until this fellow drops dead. Don't worry too much. If you worry, the hair on your head will turn white.'

While they were thus talking, a propaganda song that seemed to split the head was heard coming from the forest far above.

Michihiko: 'The gods appear in the outer world, To clearly separate good and evil. The propaganda emissary of Izumimare, Michihiko-tsukasa appears. The steward's son Wax, Who was bewitched by Sanjūbō, Hid the sacred treasures of the divine mansion in secret, Yet was seen through by Michihiko-tsukasa, And since his own person became endangered, He spread rumors throughout the town of Miya in the exact opposite way, Claiming that Michihiko was the one who hid the wish-granting niyoihotsushiyu, How hateful that he deceived people who knew nothing at all! However much Wax may wield his crafty wisdom And deceive the world for a time, The Lord of this world who created heaven and earth Has manifested Himself. How could the true God Forever tolerate the rampancy of crookedness? I am Michihiko-kamitsukasa, Thrust into the depths of the cavern, At a moment when I was lost in thought for a while, The angel sent by the Great Deity Tsukushiteruhiko Appeared instantly, Swung and brandished the hammer of true steel, Bore a hole into this cavern, And easily saved me. The divine transformation of Smart sent by Hatsuwakihime Now clings closely to my person, And from the time she grants her protection, Even if Wax leads tens of thousands of soldiers And comes to attack, How could I possibly fear? Kannagara. With the kototama of the breath of the gods, Blowing them away without leaving a single soul, The sacred virtue of love and good Shall shine upon this earth, While illuminating the light of faith and truth Between heaven and earth, The path of the gods demands that we sweep away The misfortune of this mansion! Oh, how marvelous, how marvelous! The power of the gods is manifested Right before our eyes in the divine mansion! You two guards, If you repent and come before me To confess your sins, I shall pray to the gods to save you from the bitterness of the nethermost land, And guide you to the heavenly kingdom Where you shall taste and spend eternal and boundless joy, Kannagara. Swearing to the gods, I proclaim and transmit this.'

Singing thus, leading fierce dogs, he leisurely descended from the upper side of the cavern. Upon seeing this figure, the two guards pressed their heads to the earth, stuck their rear ends up, and unable to utter a single word, trembled while expressing their intent of apology.

The sun gradually sank into the western mountains, and the surroundings grew darker by the moment.

Guided by the two, Michihiko secretly escaped through a side path and returned toward the mansion.

§ 2

Waxes, Oaks, Burma, and El, these four washed their bodies with water, entered the meeting room, and whispering together, without realizing the day turning into dusk from daytime, were carrying on the arrangements for the ambitious faction.

Smart twitched his nose at the suspicious odor inside the room, and while groaning 'Oo-oo' in a low voice, let Michihiko know that four wicked men were absorbed in a secret conference.

Michihiko, leaving the two guards bound by spiritual binding as they were, slipped through the back door and proceeded quietly into Okunihime's living room.

Okunihime was drowning in tears of grief, letting out deep sighs as she wondered what would become of her from now on.

Okunihime: 'How shall I get through today's troubles, Ah, Michihiko the Tsukasa, how he is yearned for. Michihiko's path-guiding Tsukasa Must be the messenger of the true gods of Ananahi. Though I have male servants and attendants in abundance, They are all of impure hearts. People who love only themselves gather together, And there is none who thinks of their master. Weeping them dry, the seeds of tears have run out; Save us, O gods of Ananahi. Meeting with whatever manner of sorrow, it is the sacred mansion; For the sake of protecting it, I will not begrudge my own body. The precious treasure of the Niyoihotsushiyu has returned, Yet what has become of my child treasure? My husband's illness grows ever more heavy, And soon the Kotoito threads are about to snap. People suffering from grief in the world, Though I hear they exist, how could they compare to me? Some sort of past sins coming around, Sending days of such suffering. Wait a while, for the grace of the gods is deep, And soon Michihiko will return. Serving as the teacher of Ananahi, The lord of pure truth is a brave warrior.'

Thus she expressed her innermost feelings with a sorrowful air.

Just then, Michihiko came back on stealthy feet.

Michihiko: 'Mistress, Mistress.'

He called out in a low voice.

Hearing this voice faintly, Okunihime wondered if it was a dream and was startled, yet when she peered through the dim light of the lantern, it was unmistakably Michihiko the Tsukasa.

Okunihime: 'Ah, you are Michihiko-sama, oh, my, you have returned to us. Where have you been staying?'

Michihiko: 'Yes, there is a long story to this. However, since these two people are listening, I will leave spiritual bonds placed on them for a while.'

So saying, he applied spiritual binding to their ears and mouths, made them lurk in the next room, and had Smart stand guard.

Smart kept his eyes even on the slightest movement of the two guards, and when the two tried to move even a hair's breadth, he glared fiercely, and trembling with fear at the threat of biting them down, he kept watch in the next room.

Michihiko: 'Now then, with this it is safe. However, how is the master?'

Okunihime: 'Yes, thanks to your grace, he still lingers on. He says he wants to meet his daughter even for a moment before he dies, but since the daughter's whereabouts are still unknown, even now I was just remembering you and weeping.'

Michihiko: 'No matter what, both young ladies should have been sent back by the Shugenja and already returned to this mansion. In this, there must be some scheming by the wicked scoundrels. Right now in that meeting room, the four fellows led by Waxes are holding a secret consultation, and if they realize I have returned to this mansion, there is no telling what kind of things they might do. I earnestly wish to be guided to a living room where no one can come. There I shall speak with you in detail.'

Okunihime: 'It is a bit cramped, but above my husband's sickroom there is a dark living room. No one is able to go up there, so I beg you to come up there, and I wish to hear of various matters.'

Michihiko: 'That is convenient. Come, let us go quickly. We never know when the wicked ones might come.'

So saying, guided by Okunihime, he lit a faint flame in a dark room on the second floor, hid his body, and indulged in secret talk.

Michihiko: 'The truth of the matter is, as I surmise, I think the two young ladies are hidden in the cave of Mount Terumon. That fellow called Waxes fell in love with Princess Davis, and having been given the cold shoulder, without learning his lesson, tried to the very end to fulfill his desire of love, hid the Niyoihotsushiyu to trouble the mansion, and schemed to force his way in as a forced son-in-law, when I arrived; fearing the exposure of his conspiracy, on the contrary he went about spreading rumors that I am a magician, insisted that it was also I who hid the Niyoihotsushiyu, and the townspeople knowing nothing took his words for truth, moreover trumpeted that the two lords sent by the Shugenja were monsters, hid them in a cave, and after making the princess yield by force, intended to step in grandly after the master's passing—such must be his evil plot. However, since the young lady is a resolute and strong woman, you need have no anxiety that she will ever fall into his poisoned hands. Furthermore, since she will never surrender her body to them nor have her chastity broken, please be at ease. Nevertheless, we cannot do anything right this moment. After the minds of the people in the town have calmed down, upon the occasion when Waxes's conspiracy is revealed, I will show my face and rescue the princess. We must remain settled for the next two or three days. Also, regarding the master's illness, even if there are fluctuations, these four or five days nothing will happen, so please be at ease.'

Okunihime: 'I am somewhat relieved to hear that. I wonder if our daughter is safe. How overjoyed my husband will be to hear of this! Taking this as a parting gift for the netherworld, I shall cheerfully return to the spirit realm. Ah, Kamunagara mi-tamahiko chihahe mase. And when luck is bad, misfortunes tend to pile up; our steward Allstin has been gravely injured and is in such a critical condition that he might die even before my husband. Is there no way we can save him?'

Michihiko: 'He will hardly be saved. He has broken as many as two ribs.'

Okunihime: 'Oh, how distressing this is. This too must be some karmic connection. Since my son behaves so much like a villain, could it be that the child's sins are visiting retribution upon the parent?'

Michihiko: 'By no means, by no means; there is no reason whatsoever that a child's sins should requite a parent. Since the Kamisama exist in impartiality and selflessness, they can never act to punish or afflict people. Much less would there be any unreasonable circumstance where they would visit the sins of a child upon an innocent person. Truly, beyond this there is no other course than to pray for the peace of Allstin's soul in the afterlife. And besides praying that you may change your province as quickly as possible and be relieved of the suffering of your illness, there is no other path.'

While they were whispering thus, Waxes, Oaks, Bilma, and El, all four of them guzzling liquor recklessly, came stomping noisily into the sickroom.

Waxes: 'Well, well, honored master Okuniwake-sama, how is your illness? I felt it would not do unless I came to inquire after you, but as my own father has sustained a severe injury, and he is my only parent whom I cannot simply abandon, I have been nursing him without closing my eyes at night, putting filial piety first. That being said, not to visit my precious master felt like the height of disloyalty, and I have been beside myself with worry; even while staying at home, my heart has remained connected to my master's condition. Ah, if I wish to be loyal I cannot be filial, and if I wish to be filial I cannot be loyal; somehow the world does not go as one desires. Please forgive me for my discourtesy. If beforehand you will utter just a single word asking Waxes to look after your two daughters kindly, I shall exert myself with the vigor of a raging lion and present the princesses to your sight. Since I have brought a proxy-written copy of your last will and testament here, would you not press your thumbprint upon it for a moment? It is not merely for the sake of Waxes alone; needless to say, it is for the sake of the master and everyone in the town, and furthermore for the sake of the entire nation of Telmon.'

Okuniwake barely raised himself up upon the sofa, his sunken eyes staring wide, and in a feeble voice said,

Okuniwake: 'Are you Waxes? You seem to be saying something or other, but perhaps because of my illness, my ears are ringing and I cannot hear a thing. My wife should be somewhere around there, so if you have matters to discuss, please talk things over thoroughly with my wife. My body is already weakened and even my ears can no longer hear.'

Thus Okuniwake intentionally feigned deafness to cast off the annoyance and refused to engage with him.

と故意と小国別は煩さを排除せむと耳に事寄せて取り合はぬ。
