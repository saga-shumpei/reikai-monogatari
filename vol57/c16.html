Chapter 16: 犬労 [1466]

犬労〔1466〕

§ 1

Carrying Kerinahime on his back up the midriff of Mount Terumon, Michihiko guided her smartly while treading heavily along the overgrown and difficult paths, and finally arrived before the cave where Debisuhime had been imprisoned.

Hearing Wax's account, Debisuhime vaguely realized that the wish-granting jewel had returned, that her father was still alive, and that her younger sister was safe; thus easing her breast, her mind relaxed slightly, and leaning limply against the rock, she fell asleep. At last, Michihiko arrived at the entrance of the cave.

Michihiko: 'If you please, Debisuhime, I am Michihiko, the Kamuzukasa of Ananai-kyō.'

Kerinahime: 'Elder Sister, it is Kerina.'

Though the two of them called out her name by turns, there was not the slightest response. Wondering if her elder sister had already been snatched away by some mysterious being, Kerinahime felt utterly beside herself.

Kerinahime: 'Michihiko-sama, what shall we do? It appears that Aneuhesama has been carried off by someone. Is it not strange that there is no answer no matter how much we call?'

Michihiko: 'Do not worry in the least. The sound of snoring can be heard. She is surely fast asleep.'

Smart vibrated the surrounding air and shouted, 'Uwatsu uwatsu.' Startled by this voice, Debisuhime broke from her dream, looked toward the window, and thinking that it sounded somewhat like a human voice, she crept close to the door. Peeking through the gap, because it was a starlit night, her form was not clearly discernible, but she closely resembled her younger sister's style.

Debisuhime: 'The color of the flowers has faded, oh the figure of the princess, how agonizing it is that you have grown emaciated. How yearning is the dark form of Inuhiko who saved my very life.'

Rejoicing at this voice, Kerina spoke.

Kerinahime: 'Saved by the compassionate hands of Michihiko, we have come seeking to rescue thee.'

Michihiko: 'Thou who art known as the august child of Kamidate, come forth in peace, O Debisuhime. Now then, let us shatter this iron gate and save thee, in accordance with the will of the Kami.'

Debisuhime: 'Such joy is in a maiden's heart, as I look up in reverence to Michihiko, the Kamuzukasa.'

Using his mighty strength, Michihiko snapped the lock, entered the cave, took Debisuhime by the hand, embraced her, and rescued her. Seeing this, Kerina clung to Debisuhime.

Kerinahime: 'Aneuhesama.'

Having said just that, she could not utter another word, choked with sorrow and joy. Debisuhime, sharing the same nostalgic emotion, hugged her younger sister's body, shed hot tears, could not even bring forth words, and wept and sobbed with tears of joy.

Michihiko: 'Two of you, lingering in such a place is to be feared. We must rescue Gudō Kōshi and Eru without losing a single moment. Come now, let us go.'

Debisuhime: 'Yes, thank you very much for your kindness. Alas, I was imprisoned in such a place and could neither stand nor sit, so I cannot walk. What should I do?'

Michihiko: 'Ah, indeed, I understand your feelings. With your permission, both of you, please burden yourselves upon my back. Come what may, I shall deliver you as far as the mansion. Setting out once more, I shall have Smart guide me and come to rescue Kōshi.'

Debisuhime: 'Since this is an emergency, I shall humbly accept your kind words and ask just that. I am truly sorry to trouble you.'

Michihiko: 'You need not worry at all. Now then.'

So saying, he crouched slightly and thrust out his back. The two remained piggybacked on Michihiko's back, and quietly descended the starlit mountain path to return to the Kamidate. Smart guarded the front and rear while guiding them through places devoid of human shadows, and before dawn, they arrived at the mansion with much effort.

At the entrance of the mansion, Eru was still snoring loudly while on night duty. Taking advantage of the receptionist being asleep, and knowing his way around the inside of the house, Michihiko proceeded straight toward Oguniwake's sickroom with the two daughters still on his back. Oguniwake was now at the point of drawing his last dying breath. Ogunihime, feeling that this was the end, sat closely by her husband's side, hanging her head and sinking into sorrow. Therefore, she did not notice that Michihiko had returned. With a quiet voice, Michihiko—

Michihiko: 'Okusama, I have returned bringing the young lady with me. Please set your mind at ease.'

Hearing these words, Okunihime suddenly turned this way. When she looked, Michihiko was standing with two daughters carried upon his back. Whether it was a dream, a waking reality, or an illusion, Okunihime was so overwhelmed with joy that she could not even speak a word, and standing open-mouthed with fixed and staring eyes, she stood stark still like a stone statue. Michihiko gently cared for the two daughters and softly lowered them into the living room. The two maidens were so utterly fatigued in body as if made of cotton that they had become unable to walk by themselves.

Debisuhime: 'Father, Mother, we have at last been saved this way and returned. We are truly sorry for having caused you such great worry.'

Kerinahime: 'Goriyōshinsama, we were led astray by the Akuma and left home, and having caused you worries in various ways, I have no words to express my apologies. Please forgive us.'

So saying, they apologized amidst tears.

Okunihime: 'Ah, I thought it was a dream, but was it not a dream? Michihiko-san, arigatō gozaimasu. Kerinahime-sama, the Kyūdōkoji who rescued Debisuhime-sama has been shut up inside a rock cave by villains, so I am going from now to rescue him. And please keep it a secret that the hime has returned until I come back. I earnestly ask that you move to another room and keep it hidden.'

With these words, he dashed out from the back door together with Smart.

Eru at the reception desk sensed that the atmosphere in the inner rooms was somehow noisy, so she abruptly woke up, and crawling on all fours while keeping her footsteps stealthy, she had been listening to the scene of the parent and children meeting. Now that Michihiko had dashed out, she too sprang out from the back door barefoot, and keeping out of sight went pat-pat-pat following behind him. Michihiko followed closely after Smart with all his might, hurrying along the road to rescue the Kyūdōkoji. When he came to within about a chō this side of the rock cave, he saw a crowd of rough men shouting back and forth about something.

Michihiko hid his body in the grass and pondered, wishing to observe the situation for a while. Eru diverted her path, parted the grass, and ran up close before the large crowd,

Eru: 'Now that fellow called the magic user of Hinodewake, Michihiko, of the Omotokyo, has brought back the two hime and has now come again to rescue the Kyūdōkoji, and he is hiding in the grassy field right over there.'

Sumāto instantly bristled its fur, dashed about in all directions, and by biting their legs, knocked them down one after another like falling dominoes. Michihiko: 'Ahahahahaha.'

As he laughed aloud and approached the cavern, several dozen men were binding Kiudaukoji and Heru with ropes and beating them with sticks.

Kiudaukoji and Heru were losing consciousness from the severity of their injuries, and they sat there as if dazed, merely shaking their heads. Michihiko raised his voice.
