Chapter 2: 煽動 [1452]

煽動〔1452〕

§ 1

In the inner chamber of the divine hall on Mount Telmon, the illness of Okuniwake grew increasingly grave, and his life approached morning and evening. Inside the hall, people bustled wildly upside down; Okunihime, Michihiko, and the steward Aursustin, together with two nurses, remained constantly at the sickbed, worrying over the fate of the dying man while their hearts leapt. Michihiko, deeming it no longer of any avail, prayed toward the Kami that He might save him unto Tengoku. Okuniwake arrived at a mental state where the manifest and the hidden could no longer be discriminated. Okuniwake gazed up at the sky with a joyful countenance,

Okuniwake: 'Ah, you are Lord Chandra Devaputra [Guwatsutenshi], you are Lord Sūrya [Nitsutenshi], oh my... I am coming right now. Yet, I pray you grant me a brief respite until I meet my two daughters once more. Ah, what a Manohiñjyasavāra [Gakuon] that is. I would love to let my wife hear that voice as well. I say, Okunihime, can you hear that Manohiñjyasavāra [Gakuon]? Can you set your eyes upon those beautiful angels? Hello, dear Angel-sama, I beg of you for a short postponement. For this is the parting of this world.'

He pressed his palms together incessantly as he spoke.

Okunihime: 'Look, Dannasama, pull yourself together. You are surely experiencing such hallucinations because of your illness. Can you not hear even the voice of the Manohiñjiyagandharva [Gakuon]? And the figures of the angels are certainly not visible at all. Pull yourself together. Before long, our two sisters will return.'

Okunihiko, seeing that his wife's voice failed to enter his ears, continued his words all the more,

Okuniwake: 'My, what beautiful flowers! Oh, Angel-sama, is this a dahlia flower? Eh, peach blossoms? How on earth could such large peach blossoms bloom again? What did you say? The peach blossoms of the peach forest in the supreme Tengoku—hey, how truly beautiful they are! Am I going there? Oh, thank you very much.'

Okunihime: 'Look, Teacher Michihiko-sama, what shall we do? Surely my master's life is hopeless, is it not? I would earnestly wish to have you somehow sustain his life until at least Davis and Kelina return.'

Michihiko: 'Rejoice. This is by no means any hallucination. You may not perceive it with your eyes, but just as you see, Lord Chandra Devaputra and Lord Sūrya have become angels and are appearing to save him in Tengoku. Since I shall offer a prayer right now, let us pray for a postponement of his journey to Tengoku until the reunion of parent and child is completed.'

So saying, Michihiko clapped his hands, chanted the heavenly number songs, and poured forth his prayers. Both angels of the Moon and the Sun accepted Michihiko's plea, cast sweet fragrances about all four sides, and returned once for all to the heavenly realms amidst subtle music. Okuniwake, who had almost returned to the hidden world, regained his sanity once more, quietly opened his eyes, and looked around at the surroundings,

Okuniwake: 'Ah, wife, were you there? You were being led away to a splendid place by Michihiko-sama, ah, and by tremendously beautiful angels. Have the daughters not returned yet?'

Okunihime: 'Yes, they have not returned yet, but before long, by the grace of the Kami, they will show their safe faces. Please be at ease.'

Even while worrying about the two sisters herself, she spoke purposely as if she were at ease. Okuniwake fell peacefully asleep while yearning incessantly, saying, 'I should love to meet them.' At this time, the shouting voices of men and women, young and old, began to be heard echoing around the perimeter of the hall. Since Okunihime's hands could not be spared from nursing her husband, she remained beside the sofa along with the nurse. Aursustin appeared at the entranceway alongside Michihiko, and when he looked, wild men wearing red headbands and red sashes, completely drunk and staggering, pressed forward like an avalanche, knocked Aursustin down onto the archery ground, trampled heavily right over him, gathered around Michihiko, took him by the hands and feet, raised a victory song, and carried him away together with shouts of 'Washo washo' toward the deep recesses of Mount Telmon, accompanied by dozens of rough men. Aursustins's son Wax straddled a donkey, commanding the crowd while waving a baton. Michihiko, the object of their aim, was finally snatched away. Wax first entered the entranceway with a sense of relief, only to see his father Aursustin lying fallen and unconscious. He applied a blindfold to both eyes upon the archery ground, sprinkled water, made him drink a restorative, and finally brought him back to life. Tying cloth over both eyes was a preparation to prevent his father from recognizing Wax's appearance. It was because even Wax, villain that he was, could not help but rescue his father when he witnessed his crisis. Wax ordered his wicked friend Alex and Hellman to abduct Michihiko deep into the mountains and shut him away inside a cold cavern.
