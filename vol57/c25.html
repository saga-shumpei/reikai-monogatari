Chapter 25: 天 Voice [1475]

天声〔1475〕

§ 1

Nicholas's captain, together with Hanna, Maris, and four others, bound Okunihime and Heru at Takate-Kote, and when they came to the riding ground in front of the gate, they saw that the soldiers who had been set to guard were all sharing the same fate, sleeping soundly while standing upright.

The figures of Michihiko and the other three who had been tied to the great stake were nowhere to be seen, and the two who had just been bound had also, before anyone knew it, turned into nothing but ropes.

Nicholas, unable to contain his suspicion, folded his hands, hung his head, and was lost in thought.

Suddenly, a vulture flew down from the sky with a flap and pecked at the top of his military cap with a sharp tapping sound.

Screaming in surprise, Nicholas crouched down on the Shibahara field, holding his head and trembling.

The vulture flew over the heads of the fifty sleeping soldiers, pecking at them one by one, from the first to the fourth, and so on, all around.

Then, it grabbed the last one firmly, spread its wings in mid-air, and flew about with a proud look. The soldiers, suddenly attacked by evil spirits, drew their swords and charged recklessly at Nicholas and the six other officers. Nicholas, enduring the pain in his head, directed Hanna, Maris, and the other four, and fought back against the soldiers. In an instant, more than a dozen were killed or wounded, staining the grass crimson. At this moment, music resounded in the air, and a solemn song of propaganda was heard.

Takamitsuhiko: 'By the words of the Heavenly Great God, the divine hall of the Brahman religion and the sacred site of Mount Terumon, for the sake of saving them, the Great God Susanoo has sent Michihiko, the precious minister of the three-five, here, accepting the plea of the Brahman god. The captain of the Brahman army, knowing nothing of that divine will, led dozens of soldiers, marched out to this hall, and without even offering a prayer before the presence of the God, suddenly broke into the inner chamber, bound every one of the divine pillars of the true path, including the wife of the master of the divine hall, and committed such heartless acts that the God of Heaven and Earth, whose mercy is infinite, became enraged. Sending the two divine messengers, Asahi and Takakura, He awakened the army of the triumphant Nicholas, and through the teachings of the noble God, He set out to guide them to harmony through Kototama. No matter how brave you, Nicholas, may be, how can you match the power of God? Repent and reflect. Though the teachings and names of the Three-Five Religion and the Brahman religion may differ, do you not know that if you trace their source, they are the precious descendants of the Great God Ōkuninushi-Tachitate? Ah, Kannagara. Oh, you warriors who are color-blind and utterly dark to the teachings of God, return to sincerity as soon as possible, and understand the Great Way of Heaven and Earth. Kannagara. God is with you. Man is the child of God, the palace of God. If the soul of your heart is pure, no matter what evil may attack you, how could you ever be afraid? Entrust yourself to your own strength; the propagandists you have bound and the five other true men are warriors of sincerity alone. Having repented and fulfilled the will of the Great God, they are noble, great pillars of God; how could they be bound by the ropes of you evil spirits and be daunted? Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if heaven and earth should overturn, if you exhaust sincerity alone, the three worlds of the manifest, the hidden, and the divine will become as you wish. Wake up, wake up quickly. I am the god Takamitsuhiko, who has received the divine mission from the Heaven of the Heavenly Imperial Sky to save you, a group of evil spirits, into the path of sincerity, and I have come down by parting the eight-fold clouds of heaven. The God Naohi who created this world, the Great Naohi whose heart is vast, in the world of men, simply look again and speak again with Naohi in all things. Repent and realize the sins of your own body, and look again and listen again to all the faults of men with Naohi. That is the true heart of God. There is no cloud in the heart of God. There is no obstruction in the path of sincerity. Sincerity is the treasure of Heaven and Earth. Originally, this divine hall was one that worshipped the Great God of the Brahman religion, but as long as the wish-fulfilling jewel that descended from the Heavenly Kingdom exists, it is a sacred site of the spirit of Mizu. Ōkuninushi, fearing the divine virtue of the spiritual treasure, fled, crossed over a thousand leagues of mountains and fields, and now has finally settled in the city of Haruna in the Moon Kingdom. Manipulated by the eight-headed serpent that dwells forever in the rocky cave of Mount Ōkumo, you have begun a propaganda full of derailments, taking lies as truth and believing evil to be good, which is truly lamentable. You, Captain Nicholas, listen to the words of my angel.'

Not missing even a single word, / folding them deep within your heart, / reflecting deeply and understanding well, / the precious Mikokoro of Kami, / as the Ananahi, serve Kamunagara. / According to the will of Kami, I decree and leave this: / From now on, I shall ascend and fly up / to the mansion of the Moon Kami / who resides, firmly establishing the great pillar of the palace, / in the Reigoku of Takaamahara of Hisakata; / I shall make Fukuishi of this Arisama / in complete and minute detail, / and pray that you, all the children of Kami, / may be granted forgiveness for your sins; / well then, from the bottom of your hearts reforming, / for the Ontame of Kami and for the sake of the world, / devote yourselves by offering your Magokoro. / Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara / / Thus transmitting and proclaiming in accordance with the will of Kami,'

Having finished chanting thus, Kami vanished into the empty air somewhere as a faint smoke. The wounds of the soldier, who until now had been dozing, had his flesh pecked by a vulture without resentment, and had been suffering in agony, were instantly healed, his drowsiness suddenly left him, he felt a refreshing Seishin, and somehow even his Kahoiro had become Ikiiki. Nicholas, unable to comprehend, led Hanna, Maris, and four other officers, advanced once again into the inner room of the mansion, and lo and behold, starting with Yoshikohime and Mitsuhiko, the people who had been tied up were happily clapping their hands in the midst of a feast. Nicholas was enlightened with a sudden shift of heart, felt deeply moved by the vastness of divine virtue, shed tears, and apologized to Mitsuhiko for the crime of his rudeness. At this time, outside the mansion, a clamorous war cry that seemed to shake even the mountains was heard. Everyone, wondering what could be the matter, pricked up their ears and peeked at the situation for a while. Smart's voice, piercing the ears, echoed through the surrounding echoes like 'Uwah Uwah'.

と歌ひ終り、淡き煙となつて何処ともなく中空に消え玉うた。今まで居眠つて居て禿鷲に片身怨みなく額をコツかれ、苦痛に悩んで居た兵士の傷は忽ち癒え、眠気も頓に覚め、精神爽快を覚え、何んとなく顔色まで生々して来た。ニコラスは合点行かず、ハンナ、マリス外四人の士官を引率れ、再び館の奥の間に進み入り見れば豈図らむや、小国姫、三千彦を初め縛り上げた人々は嬉しげに手を拍つて酒宴の最中であつた。ニコラスは翻然として悟り、神徳の広大なるに感じ、涙を流して三千彦に無礼の罪を謝した。此時館の外にはワイワイと山岳も揺ぐばかりの喊声が聞えて来た。一同は何事ならむと耳を欹て暫し様子を窺つて居る。スマートの声は耳を劈く様に『ウワッウワッ』と四辺の木霊を響かして居る。
