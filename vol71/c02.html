Chapter 2: 生臭坊 [1791]

生臭坊〔1791〕

§ 1

Stationed upon the mountain of Aura, the Tenrai Yuiitsu Kiuseishiyu, proclaiming himself the Keshin of Tentei, the Onnatauzoku Yorikohime, conspiring with the two of Shīgō, and distributing three thousand Zokuto in all directions, plotted a Daiinbō to occupy the moon-country of Shichisenyokoku.

Just as they were planning this, they were raided by Umekō-sama, whose name is renowned in the Ananai-kyō, and having their Tokihakakiha Ihayado broken through, they surrendered.

Yorikohime and Shīgō submitted to the path of truth, and in exchange for pledging to dedicate their remaining lives to the Tenkakōkiyō, they feigned submission for a time.

The Maisubauzu Genshin, having crossed the lake of Haru, became enamored with Dariya, the daughter of a medicine merchant known in the world as a wealthy man of the port of Suga, and seduced her with clever words.

Upon hearing that Shiyakanna, the official of the left guard, was hiding in the deep mountains of the Taniguku valley with many subordinates, he thought to raise a banner once again.

With a haughty air, he took Dariya by the hand, and after spending the night, he slurred his tongue with the sake of the banquet.

While he was fast asleep, Dariya-hime disappeared with the thief Barugī, which was a ridiculous turn of events.

Genshinbō, who had deceived others as the Keshin of Tentei, could not bear it, and borrowing two hundred of Shiyakanna's subordinates, he took the clever Koburai with him to search for the Hime.

They trudged along the valley path, and as the sun set completely at the foot of Tateiwa, they fell into a dark pit.

Spending a lonely night, then following the wisteria vines, they finally escaped the jaws of death, and while following a winding path where the grass grew thick, they wandered like dogs and cats in heat sniffing the hips of a female, a pitiful sight.

The door of darkness was lifted, the eastern sky was stained with madder, and the dew on the grass leaves glittered.

Shining with the brilliance of seven treasures, the two walked in the midst of it, treading and breaking the roots of rocks and trees while calling 'Dariya, Dariya' in a single-minded pursuit, sweating profusely, and climbing the bitter slope without complaint, their hearts ascending ahead.

Tenshinbō: 'Oi, we have walked quite a distance now, so why don't we rest for a while in this place with a good view? The scenery overlooking the mountain ranges in all directions from this summit is as if one were wandering through a dreamland.'

Koburai: 'It truly is like a dream; even last night, when I fell into a deep pit while dreaming of Dariya-sama, I truly felt as if I were not alive, and I thought to myself, if only this were a dream. At that time, I was truly worried about what would happen, just a little bit.'

Tenshinbō: 'The architects of dreams are all human, so life would be lonely without dreams. The rainbow of life is a dream, and humans are at their peak while playing in this dreamland. The appearance of Dariya-hime, like a silver wind blowing through the young leaves of spring, is a refreshing sight, is it not? While you are longing for that charming figure, that is the flower of life, the architecture of dreams, the rainbow of life.'

Koburai: 'Indeed, if that is the case, should we understand everything in this world as a fleeting dream?'

Tenshinbō: 'That is correct, they do call it a floating world of dreams, but what one cannot forget even in a dream is, after all, Dariya-hime. Even in a dream, I wish to meet, a thousand ryo of gold and Dariya-hime. Ha ha ha ha ha.'

Koburai: 'We who are listening to such a full serving of love talk on a mountain that is open in all directions are truly honored, or rather, not at all. I will not say it is disgusting or anything, but surely the gods of the mountain and others must be drooling while looking at your noble figure.'

Tenshinbō: 'Ufufu, gathering the happiness of the world into one body, I am the Keshin of Tentei, the Tenrai Kiuseishiyu, and the other name of Genshinbō is Tenshinbō-sama, so my class is just a little different from you, a member of the thieves' guild.'

"Moshi moshi bald head, O bald head, / In all the Sekai there is nothing / Whose light is as faint as yours. / Why are you so dark?

○

What is it that you say, O Denki-san? / Well then, let us match our light against yours. / When the Hi rises upon the Koyama over there, / Which of us shall shine more brightly?

○

Oh dear, dawn has broken! / Pikapika pikapika, pīkapika. / How exceedingly dark you are, O Denki-san! / What has become of your Jiman from a moment ago?"

Tenshinbau, hearing this song, became somewhat Shiyogegimi, and while deliberately shaking his Shiyakujiyō harshly, making a gachan-gachan sound:

Tenshinbau: 'Hey, Gatarau, say what you just said Ichido more! Depending on the Baahi, I shall not be accepting of it! You Gakidaishiyō brat!'

Kodomo: 'Ahahaha! Hey, Bauzu, this village, you see, has been the Seniuchi of Ananai-kyō since ancient times! A Bakemono of such Ketsutai appearance cannot enter even a Chiyotsuto! If you do not hide your figure somewhere quickly, I shall set up Senkau for you!'

Tenshinbau: 'Tch! You spout talk as if an Aodaishiyō had Hai into the Zashiki! Even a Kodomo is not a thing about which one can exercise Yudan. Kodomo, hey, Bauzu, did a beautiful woman not pass by here?'

Kodomo: 'She passed by! Leading one servant, striking each other's backs, pinching each other's cheeks, flirting together, she must have passed by here just a moment ago! You called me Bauzu just now, but I am no Bauzu! You yourself are the Bauzu, are you not? I am the son of the Shōya of Kamiya Village, a divine child called a child of the gods! If it be matters of the Sekai, ask me anything! I shall inform you of anything and everything as plainly as pointing to my palm! You must be the degraded remnant of Genshinbau, who shut himself up in Mount Ōra and played the grand fraudster! You were infatuated with Dariya-hime of Suga Port, deceitfully led her into the cavern of Mount Taniguku, had your face turned into a paper notebook while sleeping, were left foaming at the mouth when she fled together with a lizard-faced man, were duped by an artificial Bakemono beside the Standing Rock last night, fell into a deep pitfall, scraped your shins, reached the mountain top weeping and crying, grandly began the lecture on the seven herbs, and from there came pursuing Dariya all the way here, did you not? How is it, am I wrong?'

Tenshinbau: 'N-n-no, indeed, just as you say, just as I hear, just as the crow crows in the forest, the receipt is as stated above, it is just as you say! Ahahaha!'

Kami no Ko: 'Hey, Baldie-chan, it would be better if you called it quits already! A person like Dariya-hime does not suit your nature! Repent while there is yet time and become my rear-wiper! If you do so, there may yet be a chance of salvation! If you continue your evil deeds forever, you shall be dropped into the roasting scum of the kettles of the eighty thousand hells!'

Tenshinbau: 'Hey Kodomo, is that Dariya-hime not hidden in your house?'

Kami no Ko: 'Yeah, she is hidden! She is certainly sheltered!'

Tenshinbau: 'Then where is she hidden? Could you tell me just a Chiyotsuto?'

Kami no Ko: 'Do not speak nonsense! Is there any fool who reveals what he has hidden? Once hidden, it is proper to keep it hidden to the very end!'

Koburai: 'Moshi moshi Tenshinbau-sama, this child is truly a child just like a god, is he not? Why do you not surrender already? How about giving up on Dariya-sama? An evil appearance has manifested upon your forehead, you know; now is the time to repent! I have followed you all the way here, but regardless of whether you repent or not, I shall now take my leave of you right here and have myself used as a rear-wiper by this child who is like a god!'

Kami no Ko: 'The child of the gods is a pure being serving God; / Who would let a thief wipe his rear?'

Koburai: 'This is a mistake, I have spoken an impolite thing, / Without even recognizing my status as a thief.'

Tenshinbau: 'Though you speak cleverly like a child of the gods, / You can by no means be a match for Tenshinbau! / Rather than that, the whereabouts of Dariya-hime / Let me know quickly, for I shall give you money.'

Kami no Ko: 'He is an incarnation of the Rear-Eating Kannon! / Tell no lies, O crooked Genshin!'

So saying, with a sound like an elephant farting pusu, he turned into white smoke. The accompanying children also disappeared without a trace or shadow.
