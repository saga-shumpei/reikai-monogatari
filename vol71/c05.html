Chapter 5: 転盗 [1794]

転盗〔1794〕

§ 1

Tamakiyowake and his wife, together with their child, Tamako, began the great cleaning of the Shinden before the break of dawn, offering Kumotsu from the mountains and fields and reciting the Norito.

With the Ōmikoto of the Mototsumioya Ōkami in the sacred Seijiyō of Takaamahara, the Amatsukahi and Yahoyorozu Kamutsudo gather and assemble; in the eastern capital, the land of Hiizu, renowned for its name, in the Yahirodono of the Aya sacred ground surrounded by the clear and pure Kokumo River, and reaching to the western land, the capital of Jerusalem, renowned for its name in the land of Palestine, where the Miyabashira are firmly erected upon the summit of Mount Olive, the two pillars of strictness and auspiciousness, the Kamuzukahi who follow them, travel through heaven and earth morning and evening; in the sacred ground of Mount Ubusuna, Kamususanō-no-Mikoto lets his footprints remain in Tokihakakiha, and not to mention the Aohitogusa of the four directions, even down to the grasses, trees, and insect tribes, we worship the noble and pure Ōmikokoro that lets the dew of grace fall, and we serve by purifying the spirit of strictness at this Kamudoko morning and evening, and we pray with awe and reverence that you may hear and accept this with peace and tranquility, and that you may let the three-five Kamubashira, Tamakiyowake, who has been repelled by the evil gods of the Brahmins, become the light of the world again, become salt, and become flowers, and serve the Ōmikami-waza of the Amaterasu Ōmikami; we pray with awe and reverence that you may protect this household from all evil, sin, and defilement, and that you may let us work diligently in our daily tasks, without slackening or neglecting, and let us be a mirror for the village of Kamitani, and let us dwell in Tokihakakiha. May the three-five Ōkami protect us and bless us, and may the spirit of Kannagara be prosperous.

Having finished the Norito, the four members of the family walked out into the garden to breathe the fresh air.

Balgy, watching for the breathing of Dariyahime, slipped out from the back door, snuck into a house somewhere, and recklessly rushed out, thinking he would steal a large amount of gold to surprise Dariyahime and win her favor.

Dariyahime, awakened by the sound of Tamakiyowake's Norito, hurriedly used the water for purification, recited a simple Norito at the Shinden, returned to her room, and was playing the clavichord when Tamakiyowake and his wife quietly pushed open the sliding doors and entered.

Tamakiyowake: 'Dariyahime-sama, good morning.'

Tamako: 'You are so diligent from early in the morning; your skill is truly sharp and refined.'

Dariyahime placed the clavichord on the floor, retreated a foot or two, and bowed respectfully with both hands on the floor.

Dariyahime: 'Oh, oh, Master, Mistress, good morning. I am truly sorry for the great trouble I have caused you.'

Tamako: 'Hime-sama, what are you saying? This is the house of Kami, and serving you is a service to Kami. I am troubled if you thank me; please do not stand on ceremony and stay as long as you like.'

Dariyahime: 'Yes, thank you very much. I will take you at your word and stay comfortably.'

Tamakiyowake: 'However, Dariyahime-sama, do you know where the person called Balgy, who came with you, has gone?'

Dariyahime: 'Yes, I thought he went straight to sleep after listening to my clavichord last night.'

Tamakiyowake: 'Well, do you not know, Hime-sama? He has not been seen since this morning.'

Dariyahime: 'Oh, is that so?'

She remained calm.

Tamakiyowake: 'Hime-sama, I would like to ask you something; I assume you know the background of that man, do you not?'

Dariyahime: 'He is called Balgy, and he was a sub-leader in the thieves' cavern on Mount Taniguku, but I was enticed by an evil monk named Genshinbō who came last night, and I was imprisoned in the thieves' cavern; thinking I wanted to escape somehow, I took advantage of the evil monk being drunk on sake, and I used my charms to trick that Balgy, and successfully escaped the tiger's mouth. Since there is still a long way to go before returning home, I thought I would have him escort me by saying this or that, even though I knew he was a bad person, so he is traveling with me. In truth, I wanted to confess the actual situation to you, but Balgy would not leave my side no matter what I said, so I never had the opportunity to tell you. You must surely despise me for bringing such a bad man with me, but since that is the situation, I humbly ask for your kindness.'

Tamakiyo: 'Indeed, I thought you were a strange couple from the moment you visited my house. How, pray tell, could a lady like you and a thief-faced scoundrel like him travel as a married couple? It is entirely... like a wooden horse neighing and a barren woman bearing a child... that is what my wife and I were whispering about. Ah, hearing that, I am relieved. That Balgie will surely not return here anymore; I shall have my wife and me escort you to your home, so please be at ease.'

Dari: 'Why would that Balgie not return here? If you would kindly escort me, I would be relieved not to have to travel with such a dangerous person, but did he do something wrong again?'

Tamakiyo: 'Yes, late last night, he broke into the house of Mokubee in the village wearing a mask and hood, tied up the family, and as he was running away after stealing all their money, he fell into a deep well at the gate. As he was making a great commotion, the night watchman discovered him, pulled him out of the well, and it seems he is now tied up at Mokubee’s house. Just a moment ago, the night watchman came to report this to me.'

Dariya, looking surprised, said:

Dari: 'Oh, what a wicked man he is. It must be that divine punishment has immediately repaid him, causing him to fall into the deep well by his own hand. However, even though he is a thief, he is the man who escorted me from the Taniguku Valley over these mountain slopes to this place, so I cannot simply abandon him. Would you please let me see him for a moment? I wish to preach the path of truth to him. Or, if it is the law of the village to punish him, then I have no objections.'

Tamakiyo: 'This village consists of thirty-three households, and all of them are believers of the Sangokyō, so we do not judge humans. It is our custom to preach the path of truth and then escort them to the outskirts of the village to banish them. Fortunately, if you, Hime-sama, would give him a reprimand, he will surely be satisfied. In that case, I shall bring him here.'

Dari: 'Yes, if I may trouble you, I would be grateful if you could do so.'

Tamakiyo: 'Then I shall instruct the night watchman now and have him dragged here. Please wait for a while.'

Saying this, he leaves with quick steps.

Tamako-hime also follows her husband, offering a light bow, and returns to her living room. Dariya-hime, who remains behind, feels pity for him despite him being a villain, and while playing the clavichord, she prays to God, hoping to reform his heart somehow.

'Within the heaven and the earth, Though there are many living things, Created in the image of God, Man is called the child of spirit and the palace of spirit. If one searches the path of human life, Like the flowing water of a river rapid, Changing from morning to evening, Floating, sinking, stumbling, falling, and rising again, Crossing the waves of human life. Whether good or evil, all alike, Every person is a child of God. Though there are many deeds to be done, One who steals the treasures of others, And spends his days doing so, Is a person who is not a person, Perhaps a beast wearing a human skin. Even Balgie, by birth, Cannot be a thief. Assailed by the waves of this floating world, It must be that he entered the group, Of thieves, which is loathsome even to hear of. He, too, knows human affection. That he yearned for me and escorted me, Over the mountain slopes to this place, Though he calls it love, it is because there is, A glimmer of a sincere heart. If we had reached the port of Suga, He swore to reform his evil heart, And become a man of truth. Before the roots of that tongue have dried, Ah, how wretched, the child of man, Sneaking into a house to steal gold, What is this heart that steals? Is it that, in his excess of love for me, He stole the treasure of gold, To buy my favor? Alas, what a wretched heart. Great God of Sangokyō, Let the light shine through, Into his heart, even for a moment.'

May he quickly enter the ranks of the Mabito, stand to the use of this world while alive, and upon death, ascend to the land where the Lord Kami dwells in Tokotoha, and may he be granted to spend the Toha of Inochi joyfully, Kamunagara. In place of the boy of Balgy, I, Dariya-hime, offer this prayer with all my Magokoro. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, grant us your Mitamanofuyu, grant us your Mitamanofuyu.

Just then, the voices of the Murabito were heard clamoring as they brought Balgy along and arrived at the Monguchi.

Balgy, crouching within the Uekomi of the garden, felt he had no face to show Dariya-hime, and with his head bowed low, he wept in Rakurui.

Dariya-hime put on her Nihageta, walked briskly to his side, and while lightly striking his head two or three times with her Sensu, she raised her voice through her tears:

Dariya-hime: 'Hey, Balgy-sama, the things you told me about having undergone Kaishin were all a complete lie, were they not? What a shameful thing you have done. With so many trades to pursue in this world, to sneak into a person's house under the cover of Yain, to commit such Akugiyaku and Budau as to tie people up and spout threats, and to steal money earned through sweat and toil—you are truly a Tsurayogo for any Danshi. What kind of Akuma could have possessed you? I regret even having been your traveling companion for three days. However, there is something I must tell you as well. While I am a Puritan of the Sangokyo, I have been deceiving you by saying things I did not mean, trying to find a way to escape from that cave. I have never been in love with you. If you were to probe the depths of my heart, you would find that I detest you beyond measure. Nevertheless, it was my sin to think I would use you to my advantage until I returned to the village of Suga, and for this, I offer my apologies again and again. That you have come to this village and suffered such disgrace is also because of me; I am the one at fault. Please, from this moment on, change your heart and become a true human being. And if you should ever come toward the village of Suga, please do visit my home. This village is full of believers of the Sangokyo and kind-hearted people, and since they say they will forgive your sins, come, leave here quickly. You must never, ever do evil things on the road again. This is but a small amount, but please use it for your travel expenses.'

Saying this, she took out a single Koban that had been sewn into her collar, thrust it into Balgy's pocket, and saying 'Farewell,' she returned to the room she had been given, sobbing as she went. The Murabito gathered in a crowd around Balgy, and while striking the earth with green bamboo poles, they shouted:

Murabito: 'Come, stand up, and get out!'

They drove him from behind and sent him off toward the outskirts of the village.

Tamakiwake-fufu sighed in relief, and entering Dariya-hime's room once more, they said:

Tamakiwake: 'Dariya-sama, the noble resolve you showed—the two of us, listening from the side, were moved to tears from the bottom of our hearts. Surely, Balgy will also undergo Kaishin because of that admonition.'

Dariya: 'Yes, I am truly ashamed. That Balgy-sama ended up in such a state is, if one traces the origin, my fault. I am sorry for causing trouble for your household. I feel as though I would like to crawl into a hole if one existed.'

Tamako: 'What are you saying, Dariya-sama? In your position, it was unavoidable that you had to deceive Balgy depending on the time and circumstances; everything is the work of the Kami. However, that fellow called Tenshinbo might be waiting on the way and do who knows what, so it would be safe if you stayed for two or three days before returning. When you do leave, I will have two or three sturdy Murabito accompany you, so please be at ease.'

Dariya: 'Thank you very much for everything you have done for me. I leave everything in your hands.'

玉清別夫婦はヤツと胸を撫で下し、再びダリヤ姫の居間に入り来り、

玉清『ダリヤ様、貴女の見上げたお志、側に聞いてゐた吾々二人は心の底から泣かされましたよ。あの御訓戒によつてバルギーも改心するで御座いませう』

ダリ『ハイ誠に赤面の至りで御座います。バルギーさまが、あのやうなザマになつたのも、もとを訊せば私が悪いので御座います。お館に迷惑を掛けて相済みませぬ。穴でもあればもぐり込み度いやうな気分が致します』

玉子『何仰有います、ダリヤ様、貴女の立場としては、時と場合によつて、バルギーを騙しなさるのも止むを得ませぬ、何事も皆神様のなさる業で御座います。然し乍ら天真坊と云ふ奴、途中に待ち受け、どんな事をするかも知れませぬから、二三日逗留なさつてお帰りなさつたら安全で御座いませう。その時は、屈強な村人を二三人つけて送らせますから御安心下さい』

ダリ『何から何まで、お世話になりまして誠に有難う御座います。何分宜しくお願ひ申します』
