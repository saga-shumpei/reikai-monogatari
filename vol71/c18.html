Chapter 18: 金妻 [1807]

金妻〔1807〕

§ 1

At the foot of Dainichizan, in the forest, there stands an old, dilapidated shrine dedicated to Dainichiniyorai. Inside the shrine, Genshinbau, who has a face like a toad that failed to croak, and Chigusa no Takahime, a beauty with a noble appearance like a maiden of Ama, are lying down without reserve, whispering to each other while resting their chins on their hands.

Genshinbau: 'Hey, wife.'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'I dislike that, being called a wife.'

Genshinbau: 'Then I shall make you my spouse. Hey, spouse.'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'Is not the word spouse quite dull? Please call me by a more noble name.'

Genshinbau: 'Then shall I make you my saikun, or if you dislike that, shall I make you my gonaigi?'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'I absolutely refuse to be treated as a wife with terms like saikun or naigi.'

Genshinbau: 'That goes against our promise; did you not say you would become my kaka?'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'I did say that, but that was at that time and in that situation, so I said it out of necessity. I never promised to be your wife for a lifetime. Would it not be an honor if I were to be your wife for even half an hour?'

Genshinbau: 'That is unreliable; will you not become my wife for a lifetime?'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'It is not that I cannot, but your heart is what it is. How could I entrust my life to such a distant person?'

Genshinbau: 'We have only just met today, so how could you know whether I am distant or not? That must be your own groundless suspicion.'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'Even so, you are truly distant. You possess a great deal of gold, yet you will not entrust it to me, your wife. A wife must manage all the household accounts, must she not? How can I manage the accounts without money? Try thinking about it well.'

Genshinbau: 'That is true, but we are still traveling in the open air like this, and it would be a pity to make you, my wife, carry such heavy things. Once we have a home, I will entrust all expenditures to you, so please, just be at ease for now.'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'You are doubting me to the very end. I am a human being, too; I am not such a weak woman that I would collapse on the way just because I am holding some money. Now, hand it over to me cleanly. Am I not the one who saved your very life? Even if we are not husband and wife, am I not your benefactor who saved your life?'

Genshinbau: 'That is true, and I remember well that I am indebted to you. However, I cannot be so trusting when we have not even shared a pillow for a single night.'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'What a base thing to say. Are you that strongly attached to money?'

Genshinbau: 'I have no particular attachment to money, but since money is a voucher for the exchange of goods, it is something that must be cherished next only to the Lord God. If I offer a hundred ryo of gold coins, I can buy any beauty to be my wife. With this much money, even if I were to live as a high-interest lender in some capital, I could live in comfort for the rest of my life.'

Chigusa no Takahime: 'Hmph, do not make a fool of me. If I am seen as one with a prostitute, even the heavenly beings of the First Spirit Realm would be troubled. If you are such a person who does not understand that, then I shall take my leave with this. Who would endure being called a wife or a kaka by such a hyottoko-faced fellow? Farewell, please consider this the end of our connection.'

With that, she stands up abruptly and attempts to leave. Genshinbau hurriedly grabs Chigusa no Takahime by the waist.

Genshinbau: 'What soft skin you have. Hey, do not be so short-tempered. If there is fish-heart, there is water-heart; I am a human with blood flowing through me, not wood or stone. In that case, I will hand over one-third to you, so can you not be patient with that for a while? One-third is roughly ten thousand ryo, so is it not too greedy to try to take it all from the start?'

Chigusahime stuck out her tongue playfully and said, 'Genshin-sama, do not misjudge me. I have not followed you because I am in love with your money. Oh, how filthy. Money is such a cold and distant thing; they say this world is a place where money is the enemy, so now that I understand your heart to that extent, I have no need for money. If you keep it, you can take it out when I need it, so I could never carry such a heavy thing.'

Genshin: 'Indeed, I understand your sincerity well. If that is your heart, I may entrust it all to you. Come, it is heavy, I am sorry, but let me attach it to your waist.'

Chigusahime: 'I hate that, such a heavy thing... It is something for a man to carry. A woman could never travel with such a burden.'

Chigusahime intended to have Genshinbau carry the heavy items until a certain point, and at that place, she planned to tighten her grip on his kintama and rob him. Genshinbau, blinded by love, did not know of the scheme deep in Chigusahime's heart; he became like a boiled octopus, pulling his low nose, pointed mouth, and squinting eyes together, and even changing the tone of his voice, he said,

Genshin: 'As expected of Chigusahime. Great, great, I am truly impressed. Now that it is settled like this, you are my niyōbau forever, are you not?'

Chigusahime: 'That is so, but it is yabo to say such things at this late hour. Have we not been settled as husband and wife from the beginning?'

Genshin: 'Even so, I am troubled when you say things like you are only a temporary wife, or when you say you were not asked to be a wife for life. If it is for life, say clearly that it is for life; I would be troubled if you were only my wife while there is money.'

Chigusahime: 'Now, Genshin-sama, do not say such geretsu things. You speak of money again and again, but money is something for ningen to possess. My bibau and tenshiyoku must surely lie elsewhere. How could a savior who is the only one in tenka, or a beauty, be obtained through money? Try thinking about it carefully. If I wanted money, I am the queen of the Tolman country; I could bring as much as I want. Because you were a leader of thieves, you only thought about taking people's money, and because you only think about whether your wife will take your money, that is what I find regrettable. Unless you improve your personality a little more, you will not reach the place called the supporting pillar of the Great Miroku.'

Genshin: 'No, I am truly humbled. From now on, I leave everything to you. Yes, I entrust it all to my wife. However, there is no time for the buds to sprout if the two of us keep whispering in a place like this. Shall we not dash out to Sugano-sato or somewhere, purchase a splendid house, and plan the great work of unifying tenka with that as our base?'

Chigusahime: 'Ho, ho, ho, for a small man, you have a surprisingly bold spirit. That is the first thing I like about you. Now, from here on, you shall become a herald and go around the neighborhood. I shall become a savior, build a shrine deep in the mountains of this Ōhinoyama, and wait there, so you must gather the foolish men and women one after another.'

Genshin: 'Ah, that is one strategy, but most people in this neighborhood know my face. If by any chance I am recognized as the mountain priest of Mount Aura, our hard-won plan will end in a painted cake, so shall we not go to Sugano-sato without saying such things? In any case, I cannot go on in this appearance; I must order proper priestly robes and wear them, or people will not trust me.'

Chigusahime: 'Then, in any case, I shall follow the command of my husband and go to Sugano-sato.'

From this point on, Genshinbau and Chigusa no Takahime departed from the forest of Ōhino and proceeded toward the port of Suga, intending to plot a great conspiracy. Genshinbau first sang:

千『エヽ頭の悪い、女房の讃美歌ぢやありませぬよ。下つ津岩根の大ミロクさまの讃美歌を歌つて下さいと云ふのですがな』

玄『ウンウンそりや分つて居る。よしよしそんなら慎んで歌はして頂きませう。オイ併し乍らスガの里迄はもう十五六里あるから到底足が続かない。この向に入江村と云ふ所がある。其所はハルの海がズツと入り込むで居る処で、大変景色も佳い。其所の宿で今晩は宿つたら如何だらうかなア』

千『里程は其所迄幾何程ありませうかな』

玄『三里半計りある。そこ迄行つておけば明日は船で楽に行けるからなア』

千『成程そりやよい事を思ひ付いて下さつた。さア、之から入江の里迄急ぎませう』

と両人は足に撚をかけ、一生懸命に駆け出したり。
