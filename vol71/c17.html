§ 1

Chigusahime and the two, Genshinbau, successfully ensnared Koburai and Kōro with their scheme, burying them alive in a pit; the two, barely escaping with their lives, crawled out of the hole, and while catching their breath covered in mud,

Koburai: 'Hey, Kōro, we have been put through a terrible ordeal, haven't we, by that Tanukibauzu and that Kitsunemerau. We have truly been made into fools, haven't we?'

Kōro: 'Truly, I am so filled with hatred I cannot bear it. Yet, that woman is a cute one, no matter where you look. Even if we were buried alive and died, it would have been our original fate. What is hateful is that Genshinbau. Shall we not pursue him wherever he goes, pull off his head, and take our revenge?'

Koburai: 'Yes, yes, that is true. Chigusahime, who saved us, would never kill us. Genshinbau must have thought that if he were told of his past evils in front of Chigusahime, his reputation would suffer, and that if he only did away with us, he could do anything he pleased; that is why he committed such an atrocious and heartless act. Come, let us pursue him, pull off his head, and make him suffer such shame before Chigusahime that my anger will not be appeased otherwise. Even Chigusahime could not possibly be truly in love with such a Hyottoko-faced man. Surely, once he has swindled all the money from her bosom, she will either drip green snot all over him from above, or he will be squeezed by his testicles until he is utterly exhausted; that is the best he can expect, ufu-fu-fu-fu.'

Kōro: 'In any case, it is useless to hold a council here, is it not? Come, let us set out to pursue that fellow and take our revenge.'

Koburai: 'Even if you say let us pursue him, we do not know which way he fled, do we?'

Kōro: 'What, let us stand this piece of wood in the middle of the road and see in which direction it falls. It is certain that he is in that direction.'

Koburai: 'Well, that may be so.'

Saying this, they picked up the piece of wood and stood it straight up before letting it go. The piece of wood fell with a thud toward the south. From this point, the two ran south, ever south, along the moonlit road.

Futari: 'The Kami appears in the world / To distinguish between good and evil / The Kami who created this world, Kamunaobi / With a heart as vast as Ōnaobi / In all things in the world of men / See straight and hear straight / Correct the errors of the world / Though we have heard the teachings of the three-five / Of the Kami who teach us thus / There is one in the world / Who cannot see straight, correct, or hear straight / That is the thief-turned-Genshinbau / Who barricaded himself in the mountains of Aura / Planning mountain schemes and failing / Wandering about the area again / Tormenting people and causing women / To suffer, that wicked monk / Saved from a life in danger / Even the money we dropped / Was dug up for us, and for that grace / He repaid us with evil, a Magatsukami / I know not where he has vanished / But I shall search him out even if I must part the grass / I will not be satisfied until I clear this grudge / If the Kami exists in this world / Surely He shall distinguish good from evil / And punish and admonish / The evil Kami, Genshinbau / Though we say this, we / Do not have the leisure to wait / For the time when the long-suffering Kami / Shall bestow His divine punishment / We must pull off the head of Genshin / And take our revenge as soon as possible / Or a man's pride cannot stand / Ah, how hateful, how hateful / As our irreconcilable enemy / The two decided from this point / To run about in all directions.'

Futari: 'To search out his whereabouts / And not leave him until we take his life / Ah, how hateful, how hateful / The thief-turned-Genshinbau / I will not leave him until I take his life / Ah, how hateful, how hateful / Whether it takes a moment or five minutes / I will grind his bones and head into powder / My heart will not be satisfied until I devour him / Ah, my anger rises, my anger rises / If I do not clear this grudge / My heart will not rest even in death / Have mercy, Jizaiten / Before the presence of Ōkunihiko / I pray with all my heart / Whether the sun shines or clouds over / Whether the moon waxes or wanes / Even if the earth should sink / I will not leave him until I take my revenge / The atrocious and heartless Genshinbau.'

The text ends here.

No matter where he may hide in the ends of the earth, by the power of the Kami and our own Netsushinriki, we shall search him out. We shall snatch away the Ōgon he possesses and bring it all here. Once we have achieved our initial Mokuteki and established ourselves as men, there is no way we shall leave it at that. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. May the Mitama be Sachihahe.

Saying this, the procession of frogs ran south, ever south, along a narrow path that seemed to exist only in form, without looking toward the other side. Far ahead, a clump of trees appeared in the mountain shade. The two sat down heavily on the grass.

Kooro: 'Hey, Aniki, if we keep running without any Atedo, we will just end up hungry and our legs will be tired. Can we not do something about this? Let us try to think about it here.'

Ko: 'Yeah, well, my compass has stopped working too. Even if we run through these vast Tanbo without knowing the Arika of our enemy, it is like trying to grasp at clouds. When I think about it, is it not ridiculous? We are running south, ever south, but if those fellows have run north, ever north, the harder we try, the further away we get. This is something we must think about.'

Kooro: 'Even so, when I used the staff divination, it fell to the south. We have no choice but to run south. Ah, when it comes to this, I resent the dog. If I were a dog, I could sniff out the path he took, but with this human nose, it is absolutely useless.'

As the two talked like this, they grew limp and weak, and even felt sleepy, until at last, in the middle of the wilderness, they became guests of the Shirakawa night boat, unaware of what was before or behind them.

Suddenly, a divine person in white robes appeared there. The divine person spoke quietly:

Divine Person: 'Are you not the two, Koburai and Kooro?'

Both at once: 'Yes, that is so. Judging by your appearance, we take you for a noble lady from somewhere, but where have you come from?'

Divine Person: 'I am Toyotawake-no-Mikoto, who leaves traces on Mount Ryōju. Because of the sins you have committed in this world to this day, you have received various divine punishments, were buried even underground by the evil ones Genshinbō and Chikusahime, and though you escaped death by a hair's breadth, you have forgotten the preciousness of divine virtue. You are merely obsessed with resenting him, and furthermore, you are plotting to snatch away the gold coins in his bosom, are you not?'

Ko: 'Yes, as you say, we are humbled.'

Divine Person: 'You two, if you do not listen to my teachings and repent now, you shall fall into the Avīci Hell. How about it? Do you not have the intention to end your resentment toward Genshinbō and return to the original sincerity?'

Ko: 'No, even I am not an evil person by nature, but because the cutting of my umbilical cord was done poorly, I could not live a life like other people. Before I knew it, I became desperate, threw myself into a gang of thieves, and have committed every evil act possible. Born as a human, I am deeply ashamed in my conscience to act like a jackal or a wolf, but in order to preserve this physical body, I have had no choice but to plot and commit various wicked deeds. I am prepared to be cast into the depths of hell for the sins I have committed until now. Since I will fall into hell anyway even if I change my heart now, I have made up my mind that if I am to do evil, I want to carry out evil deeds thoroughly.'

Divine Person: 'No matter what kind of evil person one may be, through repentance, evil can instantly vanish, and one can turn toward the path of good. As long as one is on this earth with a human body, one cannot perform absolute good. Therefore, if you entrust everything to the Kami, believe in the Kami, love the Kami, and strive in faith day and night, you will surely be able to lead a life of peace and comfort both in this life and the next.'

Ko: 'Yes, thank you. What kind of Kami should I believe in? I have believed in the Brahman Kami until now, but I have never once been given peace or happiness.'

Shinjin: 'Every Kami is originally a Gobunrei of Tentei, and though there is no Kaugeshiyōretsu in their Shintoku, the world of Konnichi has already seen the end of the age of Bankoshinnō and the passing of the age of the Brahman Jizaiten; now, it has changed into the Miyo of the Miroku Ōkami.
