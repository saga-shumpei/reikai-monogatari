Chapter 1: 追劇 [1790]

追劇〔1790〕

§ 1

Rich in the blessings of Kami, the Tenonkiyō that opens the Kototama. The great Itefu tree towering at its Chiyōjiyō brushes the Ten, holding aloft a fan of Kogane. This Seijiyō is the Manjiyuen, the Zuishiyōkaku that shall remain unchanged even until the end of the Miyo of Miroku. The Shihau are a Yamabiyōbu of brocade, and the loom of Aya, woven with warp and woof, is the Hikita. Not to mention our Hinomoto, even to the ends of the Daichi, the Kuōshōden is illuminated by the Shinkuō. On the Kikuzuki Kamiyōka, when the completion of this was finally announced, the Eugaichi, which overlooks the Heigen of Minamikuhada at a glance, was the place where, in the old days of Tenshiyō Ninen, Mitsuhide of the Toki clan, who served the Lord of Oda, reminisced about the traces of his great achievements.

In the inner room of the Shoimeikan, Matsumura-san, who is associated with a thousand years, received the words of Kami, wishing to illuminate this dark world with the Zuigetsu of the light of San-go. As always, he lay on his side, entrusting his body to the ship of his bedding, and while floating on the waves of the tatami, he crossed the Pacific Ocean and climbed over the seas of India. Tenshinbō, who had been possessed by Akki-rasetsu, had cleverly deceived and lured out Dariya-hime, who served in the hidden retreat of the Left-Guard official in the Moon Country of over seven thousand nations, which was known as an ancient civilization, and who was at the port of Suga.

The story of the comical and tragic situation in which he exposed his ugly figure in the deep mountains of the Taniguku valley is recorded in detail at the beginning of the volume of the Child (Dog) of Senzan-bansui (mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees). Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagamise, may the spirits be blessed.

Although he was a rare corrupt monk and a villain of cunning and evil wisdom, he had a vacant-looking face. His head was shaved in a way that looked serious, but by nature, he had thin hair, so he was the owner of a shiny, convenient head that could have done without the meddling of a razor. The tip of his nose was strangely pointed, his eyes were slightly upturned, and two front teeth protruded from his thick lips. No matter how much he tried to make a small, puckered mouth, these two front teeth remained outside the atmosphere, in a state of extraterritoriality. Baring teeth that looked like the teeth of a saw, covered in tartar like boiled pond snails, he searched for Dariya-hime with bloodshot eyes. Breathing heavily and spitting foam, he deployed his many small thieves in all directions, while he himself chose the mountain path where it seemed Dariya-hime had fled. Bringing along Koburai, who seemed a bit sharper than the other thieves, he arrived at the foot of the famous Tateiwa in these mountains, in a manner like a hound sniffing out the tracks of a wild boar.

Sometimes startled by poisonous insects and having his liver chilled by wild beasts, as the sun began to set, he muttered that his legs had become like sticks. Exhausted, he dropped his thin buttocks with protruding ischial bones onto the grass by the roadside.

Tenshinbō: 'Hey, Koburai, how about it? Shall we take a break for a moment? If I don't oil the transportation equipment, it seems it will become inoperable. I have been walking this steep slope day and night, so my knees have filed a protest, and I have no choice but to order a rest. Hey, can you scout around here for a while? Even if that Dariya is fast on her feet, she is a woman, so she won't go too far.' Koburai: 'Indeed, that may be so. However, we have already passed the hour of the sunset, so the cornea-in of my eyes has blown the trumpet for bedtime. Since I am not paid for night work, shall I also take a rest with you, incarnation-sama? No matter what, you are just a human. In this case, where a saint who is supposed to be an incarnation of the Heavenly Emperor is exhausted and collapses on the road, how could the compass work? Please let me rest properly without saying such things. No matter how long you search for one woman in such a vast mountain field, you will not find her so easily. If we rest here like this, Dariya-sama might pass by later with Balky, flirting. If that happens, it is the same as obtaining the desired treasure without moving a finger.'

Ten: 'Eh, you are a fellow who whimpers despite being a thief. Eh, however, as they say that for all things in human life, the fortune of the old man at the frontier is like the tail of an ox, one cannot tell what will turn out to be convenient. Ten: 'Whether it is a horse or an ox, does it matter? When I said the tail of an ox, I meant that I am a man of knowledge.'

Kobu: 'Indeed, as one would expect of an incarnation of the Tentei, it is a mystery that you know everything so well.'

Ten: 'It is a settled matter. If it concerns the Sanzensekai, from the beginning of Uchiukaibiyaku down to the smallest Mijin, I am a famous monk of knowledge who knows everything thoroughly and without omission, as if reflected in a mirror, Otsuhon.'

Kobu: 'Etsuhehehe, if you are such an ox-tail Sensei who understands everything so well, is it not a bit of a contradiction that you must borrow two hundred of Shiyakanna Tōmoku's Buka to search for the whereabouts of the great Dariya-hime?'

Ten: 'Do not talk like a fool. Love is a strange and peculiar thing; oh, hold on, they say love is a tricky thing, do they not? There is a story that even Kume no Sennin fell from the sky upon seeing a woman's white shin. No matter how much I am an incarnation of the Tentei, once I am infatuated with a woman, it is a natural principle that my surroundings become dark and completely pitch black.'

Kobu: 'Heen, is that so? That is strange, you say such bizarre things. It is mysterious beyond measure, strange and astonishing, unique in all ages, and unparalleled; it is a situation where stones might flow and leaves might sink, where heaven might become earth and earth might become heaven. A woman, when one hears this, is truly a terrifying thing. In that case, Tenshinbau-sama, Dariya-hime, who possesses the capacity to make you blind, must be a very great person. I have heard the idiom that women are the weaker sex, but does it not make one want to say that women are the strongest sex?'

Ten: 'Unlike the women who are scattered about like pebbles and are less than a comma, she is, after all, the incarnation of Tanabatahime who floated a boat of jewels on the Amanogawara and descended from heaven; so that is only natural.'

Kobu: 'Indeed, then shall we not pray to Tanabata-sama and have her clearly reveal the whereabouts of Dariya-hime? I remember you saying that you are an incarnation of the Tentei and are a married couple with Tanabata-hime, but no matter how you look at it, it seems a bit illogical that an incarnation of the Tentei would not know the whereabouts of his own empress.'

Ten: 'It is a settled matter. Since it is a rule that Tanabata-hime and I, Hikoboshi, have not been able to meet more than once a year since ancient times, it is no wonder that I do not know. Because I am saying that I want to meet and enjoy her company every single day, it is a matter of saying that there is a bit of unreasonableness on my part as well. However, once I have set my mind on something, I must see it through, or a man's pride will not stand; nay, it is a matter concerning the dignity of Tenshinbau.'

Kobu: 'Indeed, truly, most reasonable, eh, in the ten-thousandth chance, what do you intend to do if Dariya-hime gives you the cold shoulder?'

Ten: 'Hen, do not talk like a fool. How could such a thing happen? Dariya is deeply in love with me.'

Kobu: 'Utsufufu, if she is so in love with you, why did she consider the time you were sleeping, scribble on your face, and run away?'

Ten: 'That is where your interpretation is wrong. Dariya had walked such a long mountain path that she was extremely exhausted. Because I made her drink sake recklessly, she fell into a deep sleep, and her eyes were clouded, causing her to mistake me for someone else. That fellow Barugii is a villain who is good for nothing, so while Dariya and I were asleep, he secretly scribbled on my face so that it would not look like my face at first glance, and in the meantime, he shook Dariya awake, used my voice, and lured her away under the cover of the night. By this morning, when Dariya could see people's faces clearly, she must have looked at Barugii's face and been quite surprised and astonished that it was not what she expected. As for Dariya, there is no way she could have even a Mijin of a heart to abandon me; surely Barugii disguised himself as me and took advantage of her sleepy eyes to deceive her. No matter what, once the women of the world have seen my face, they never forget it. Much less a woman to whom I have spoken even a single sweet word; no matter how much she tries to brush me off like a bee, she cannot escape me, Ehehehe.'

From the Kōkaku, a sticky, thread-like substance was trailing down, which he was reeling in with the back of his hand.

The sun sank behind the Seizan, and darkness fell from the Ten as if it had shattered. The gloom expanded in all directions as if a rubber ball were being inflated. The voices of the Hototogisu could be heard from here and there in a competitive manner. The two, having no other choice, leaned their bodies against the hollow of the Tateiha and the curtain of snoring had already fallen.

The four or five Shōtoruren who had been serving as subordinates to Shiyakanna, having also been entrusted with the search for Hime Dariya, had come pushing through the dense forests here and there, covered in spider webs. However, with the roadside grass grown to their height and not a single star visible in the deep shade of the trees, they were at a loss for what to do and lay down to rest their heads together there. Though it was dark and unclear, there was a sound as if Zafusui were being cooked, bubbling away.

Kō: 'If it happens to be, I wonder if Tenshinbō-sama is sleeping nearby, snoring away. If not, perhaps the old Hototogisu has lost his teeth and is singing in that manner.'
