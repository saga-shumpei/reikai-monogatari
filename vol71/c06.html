達引〔1795〕

§ 1

The Eusau, driven mad by love, Tenshinbau, sought the whereabouts of Dariya to the ends of the earth. Accompanied by Koburai, he traveled the night road, chanting the Fumonpon, and while performing the Majinahi against Dokuda, he walked briskly until he struck the base of a mountain. Having lost his way, he stood in a Tachiōjiyō, unable to proceed, and waited until dawn. Just then, from the opposite direction, a man came running, gasping for breath, and stumbling over a Koishi, he fell flat before the two.

Genshinbau, suspicious, looked closely and wondered how this could be. Seeing the face of a thief he recognized from inside the Ihaya, he seized him Yaniha, raised his fist high, and shouted, 'Hey, hey, Kisama, you are surely a follower of Barugī, and you must be the one who let Hime Dariya escape! If you do not confess quickly, your Inochi will be like the morning dew, vanishing instantly without a trace. Are you Shiyōchi, or how is it? Confess clearly!' When he said this, the man raised his face and cried, 'Aitatatatsuta, aitatatsuta! You are the Nadaka Tenshinbau, a Kamisama who sees through every corner of the Sanzensekai at a glance. I was asked for your sake to search for the whereabouts of Dariya. It is unbearable to be Michiga. Even if I do not speak of the Shingi, if you are a Kamisama, you should know.'

'Please forgive me. I will never tell a lie before a god.' When he said this, Genshinbau nodded, softened his expression, and lowered his voice, 'Then, did you go and search for Dariya for me? Hey, hey, Koburai, is there no mistake? Check it for me.' When he said this, Koburai shook his head up and down and said, 'This fellow is a subordinate of Barugī, but he was asked by Shiyakan-sama to search for Dariya and Barugī, and he is a man who went out to the front. It is certainly no lie. You may rest assured.' When he said this, Genshinbau nodded, 'Indeed, as you say, this fellow's words must be true.'

'Have you found the whereabouts of Dariya? Have you searched for the state of Barugī? Can you not inform me quickly? In this situation, I am beside myself with anxiety.' When he said this, the thief shook his head and said, 'I am a man called Kōro. Wishing to achieve the fame of the first spear, I rushed out without taking anything, climbed over the Kamiya village, and when I climbed to the top of the Haru mountain pass, I heard in the talk of travelers that Hime Dariya and a pair resembling Barugī were hiding in the house of a god in Kamiya village. I heard this with sharp ears. Therefore, I have returned here briskly.'

'Surely the two must be hiding in the village of Kamiya. Aitatatatsuta, aitatatatsuta! I have scraped my shin, and look, the blood is dripping down like this. Please help me quickly. If you abandon me who met with such a fate for your sake, you will instantly incur the punishment of the gods. Hime Dariya will not be obtained, and you will eventually roll down to the bottom of the valley, and you will surely see a terrible sight. Ah, it is unbearable, it is unbearable, it is unbearable! It is as if my eyeballs are popping out. Aitatatatsuta, aitatatatsuta!'

Genshinbau: 'Ei, you useless fly of a fellow, is there anyone who cries over such a small wound? You have been making a living as a thief until now, haven't you? Hey, Koburai, it is because you tried to rush out taking the words of such a useless child seriously that you met with this fate. Ee, there is no help for it now that it has come to this. It is a single path that one must pass through anyway. If they are in Kamiya village, they will eventually appear here. You are truly a fellow whose meaning I cannot understand.'

Koburai: 'Do as you please. You, who put up a big signboard as a savior sent from heaven and an incarnation of the Heavenly Emperor, to say you do not even know the whereabouts of Hime Dariya is to be a peddling monk who deceives this world. I thought you had more of the divine eye, but the more I associate with you, the more your gold leaf peels off, and on top of that, you are an unbearable, foul monk. I will stop this now. Come, give me the daily wage you owe me so far. Hey, Kōro, you too, pull yourself together and stand up. Shall we snatch the money from this fellow's pocket? Being a thief is my specialty, after all.'

Kōro: 'That is true, I have lost all affection for this monk, thinking he was a more clever one. He is a filthy monk who knows neither mercy nor compassion. Even though I am suffering here with my shin broken like this, he will not even offer a single word of scripture, and he is a defiant, wicked monk who speaks nothing but abuse. Kōro: 'By all means, brother, I leave it to you. I beg you to intercede for me before leader Genshinbau.'

Genshinbau: 'Hahaha, that sounds interesting. However, for the time being, I must wear a mask and act as an incarnation of the Heavenly Emperor, or I cannot get any work done. Since she is the daughter of a wealthy man at the port of Suga, I will cleverly snatch her up, and once I make her say yes, I will instantly become the master of a wealthy house. Once that happens, I will promote you two to be my first and second head clerks. If I wear the label of such a wealthy man and act as a thief, I will rarely leave any tracks behind.'

Koburai: 'Indeed, your words are most reasonable. That is exactly how it is, a brilliant idea, a brilliant idea.'

Genshinbau swung both his arms and struck his chest alternately with his clenched fists, and began to roar.

It is as if I have already taken the world.
