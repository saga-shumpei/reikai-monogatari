Shiyakanna, Koburai, and Kōro, the three of them, advanced while singing a marching song, determined not to lose sight of Genshinbau's figure.

/ Hey, hey, hey, hey Genshinbau / Will you not pull yourself together quickly?'

As they trampled over his chest and head without distinction, with a squelching sound, Genshinbau gasped and regained his breath.

Genshinbau: 'Ouch, ouch, ouch, ouch! You stepped on my head. You have surely broken two or three of my ribs, Koburai. Put them back as they were and return them. It is a precious treasure I received from my parents, to be used for a lifetime. Ouch, ouch, ouch, ouch! Hey, hey, Shiyakanna, Samori-dono, please take revenge for me. I am in such pain that I cannot even describe it. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. My eyeballs are about to pop out.'

He was saying such things, tapping his chin despite his body being unable to move.

Koburai: 'Ha ha ha ha, hey Genshin-sama, why don't you get up? What is this state, spewing poison when you cannot even move your body? Come on, get up, get up. Or don't get up, that is fine too; we three are leaving you behind. Do not make a fool of me by talking about revenge or whatever.'

Genshinbau: 'Hey, Koburai, do not be so angry. Listen to what I have to say calmly. When I say take revenge for me, I mean to smash this standing stone here. It is only because this thing existed that I met with such a fate.'

Koburai: 'I see, this is a strange stone. Yo-yo, yo-yo, yo-yo, eyes have appeared. There is the nose, the mouth, and even ears have grown. This thing is a transforming stone.'

The standing stone suddenly changed into the figure of a white-haired person.

The figure: 'Gya ha ha ha, hey you senile fools, do you know who you are dealing with? I am an old hag of a demon called Okon of Sayaguzome, famous even in the land of the moon.'

As she said this, the moment she made a clicking sound in her throat, she blew out fire, and with a tongue of crimson lotus, she licked Genshinbau's bald head clean. In his heat and agony, Genshinbau thrashed his limbs about, and letting out a voice like the buzzing of a mosquito, he cried:

Genshin: 'Oi, Shakanna, help me, help me!'

Even that voice gradually grew thinner and thinner. Shakanna, unable to watch any longer, sang the heavenly number song with all his might, 'One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand,' and as he finished, the figure of Sayoguzome no Okon swayed in the air like cigarette smoke and vanished.

Strangely, Genshinbau regained the freedom of his body and once again stood at the head of the party, and without learning his lesson, he proceeded with his head forward and his rear behind, bracing his thin shins like the kicking legs of a locust. The three of them were once again left two or three chō behind Genshinbau in an instant.

Koburai: 'Excuse me, Shakanna-sama, it seems Genshinbau is a man of great sin. That creature called Okon must have been married to Genshinbau once. Even if it were not Genshinbau, I would be repulsed by that face myself.'

Shakanna: 'Well, whether they were married, or whether she is Okon or Mikon, I do not know, but she had a truly difficult countenance. As long as such yōkai appear, it must surely be the eighth ward of hell. Let us proceed slowly.'

Koburai: 'Does it not feel lonely all around here? I feel as if I am wearing a pot over my head. Kōro, how about you?'

Kōro: 'I feel lonely too. However, it would be fine if Okon or Ohan or whoever would pop out now and then to kill the boredom. Though I do feel a little sorry for Genshinbau.'

Shiya: 'Ha ha ha ha, as I thought, even if you speak with such great airs, it seems your heart is as weak as a radish. Come, I shall take the lead, so you all follow me.'

While saying this, Shiyakanna stood before the party and approached the Akamon with a leisurely air. As a rule of the Meifu, two Shiyuei, one white and one red, were standing guard with a stern presence.

Aka: 'Hey, hey, who are you?'

Shiyakanna: 'Yes, I am the one called Shiyakanna, the official of the Samori of Tarahan Castle.'

Aka, while flipping through a long, narrow ledger, said:

Aka: 'Indeed, your Meisū has expired. I would love to send you to Tengoku immediately, but it may be that you must undergo some Shiugefu at the Yachimata. Everything depends upon the Gosaidan of Ibukidonushi. Now, pass through.'

As he tapped Shiyakanna on the backside, Shiyakanna flew into the Monnai as if floating, like a leaf scattered by the wind. Aka turned toward Genshinbau and said:

Aka: 'Hey, you are that wicked monk called Genshinbau, aren't you? You are undoubtedly a candidate for Jigoku, however, your Meisū still remains. Those who still have their names registered in the Genkai are not under the jurisdiction of this place. Now, get out and go back at once!'

Genshinbau: 'Indeed, I am quite a wicked monk. However, I have never succeeded in anything. Since all my acts are merely attempted crimes, please overlook them.'

Aka: 'Eh, do not go on with your excuses! If I tell you to go back, will you not go?'

As he glared with eyes like two-sen copper coins, Genshinbau trembled, shook, and cowered.

Aka: 'Hey, you two there, you are also fellows who make a living as thieves, are you not? You are called Koburai and Kōro, right?'

Koburai: 'It is as you have surmised.'

Kōro: 'It is exactly as you say.'

Aka: 'Your sins are quite heavy as well, but your names still remain in the Genkai. Now, return at once without a moment's delay!'

With that, he slammed the Akamon shut and, together with the white Shiyuei, hid his form inside the gate. The three had no choice but to trudge back along the path they had come for about seven or eight chō, when a gentle woman's voice reached their ears, sounding as if it were coming through a telephone. The three of them all came to their senses at once, and when they looked around, they found themselves rescued by several people downstream of the Tarahan River, surrounded by a large crowd of onlookers, and being cared for by a beautiful woman. This woman was the enchantress Chigusa no Takahime, who had escaped from the land of Toruman.
