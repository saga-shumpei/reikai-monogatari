Chapter 9: 生 Kotodama [774]

生言霊〔774〕

§ 1

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto stood up and, opening a golden fan, danced and chanted of his own accord.

'The great deity Kuniharutachinōokami, who forged and solidified this world, and Toyokunihime's wakemitama, who united water and fire to eternally protect the realm—the angel of Kototamawake, who is said to aid, bless, and give life; unable to bear the fury of the ugly fiends, dwelling hidden in the nethermost land, and appearing as Sukunahikona, how valiant was the protection of the universe in the land of Tokoyo! The wakemitama of Kototamawake, by the mikoto of Sumeōkami, appeared once again in this world, becoming Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, the kamuzukasa of Ananai-kyō, to transmit the divine teachings of the deities of heaven and earth according to the will of Kami, and went to hide in Mount Yotsuo.

By the mikoto of Kunitakehiko, he serves in the palace of brocade. Together with Tamateruhiko and Princess Tamateru's mikoto, he applies his liver-piercing heart morning and evening, devoting his body to lay the foundation of the Miroku divine administration, burying forever the adamantine wish-fulfilling jewel, the golden sphere, and the precious divine treasures of purple, just as Kami wills, while waiting for the time when the plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds shall all open at once. Just at that moment, the great deity Susanoo's noble spirit, the grand deity of the golden island's secret realm, the Lake Suwa where the golden waves glitter, and the Mani jewel eternally protected by Tamayorihime, have finally appeared here.

The achievements of the fivefold mikoto shine ever more brightly. The jeweled path of three and five, and the mikoto's teaching of the thirty-five moon, have completely and minutely pushed open the heavenly rock cave for the second time, dispelling the darkness of Tokoyo, and reaching down to the eight million deities of heaven, the eight million deities of earth, the myriad common people, grasses and trees, beasts and insects, down to the very limit of life; saving every single one without omission or exception, establishing the true age of Kami where high and low rejoice and mingle together in harmony. The precious foundation has now been fixed. Ah, kannagara kamukamu, may your august spirits be blessed and prosper.

○

Not a single word of the great divine decree proclaimed by Kami Susanooōokami would he let slip; pricking up his ears, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto of the auspicious mikoto single-mindedly made today the boundary, and to save the people of the world into peace, he bore the thousand offerings upon his back, emulating the divine heart of the infinite benevolent great deity, resolving to serve faithfully—the auspicious august spirit, the great deity Susanooōokami, before Kunitakehiko, reverently and with deep awe, devoting his sincere heart to swear: though the morning sun may shine or be clouded, though the moon may wax or wane, even though the great earth should sink, the Kototama sworn to Sumeōkami shall remain forever unchanged until the age of Miroku. Ah, kannagara kamukamu, may your august spirits be blessed and prosper.'

Having thus chanted his firm resolution to the end, he returned to his seat in dejection. How indeed will the future sacrificial activities of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto develop?

Ever since Kami Susanooōokami had retired into the innermost chamber of Akiyama-kan, not a single person knew his whereabouts or what had become of him.

Kunitakehiko no Mikoto vanished into white smoke upon that spot, and was destined to await the dawn of the fulfillment of divine administration deep within the mountains of Mount Yotsuo.

Hereupon Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, together with Umekohime, Isokohime, and all the others, placed the divine treasures into a beautiful portable shrine from the river mouth of the port of Yura, settled them within a boat inlaid with gold and silver, filled the white-silk sails of gold and silver with the autumn wind, and voyaged bravely up the Yura River back to the sacred ground, chanting, dancing, and playing various kinds of music within the boat. Ah, kannagara tama sakihahe masemase.

This is the end of the text.

五六七の世迄も変らまじ あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましませよ』

と自ら固き決心を歌ひ了つて悄然として座に帰つた。今後の言依別命の犠牲的活動は果して如何に発展するであらうか。

神素盞嗚大神は秋山館の奥の間に隠れ給ひしより、何れへ出でませしか、その消息を知るものは一人もなかつた。

国武彦命はその場に白煙となつて消え給ひ、四尾の山の奥深く神政成就の暁を待たせ給ふ事になつた。

茲に言依別命は梅子姫、五十子姫その他の一同と共に、神宝を由良の港の川口より美はしき神輿の中に納め、金銀を以て鏤めたる御船に安置し、金銀の真帆に秋風を孕ませ、由良川を遡りて聖地に勇ましく、船中歌ひ舞ひ、いろいろの音楽を奏しながら帰り給ふ事となつた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
