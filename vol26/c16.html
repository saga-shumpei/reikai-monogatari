§ 2

Driven by the evil spirits of Fukushiyu, / There are some who occasionally stray / From the unprecedented Kamuwaza. / Believers of the same Oshihe, / Drawing upon the heart of Kami, / Warning one another and helping each other, / Before exposing the shameful spectacle / Of having no remedy whatsoever / At Afun Station, where the train of Manshin / Has reached a dead end, / Zuigetsu, solidified in the Magokoro / Of thinking of the Yobito, / Here states a single word. / Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara, / May your Mitama be blessed and prosperous! / Whether the Asahi shines or clouds over, / Whether the moon waxes or wanes, / Even if Daichi sinks, / The power of sincerity saves the world. / Chie gakushiya who know not sincerity,

If they look upon this Monogatari, / There are those who harbor feelings of keibu, / There are those who laugh at it as a datsusen bunshiyō. / Some dismiss it from the very beginning / As a semi-frantic mischievous prank / Listing vulgar colloquialisms. / Cold ridicule and abusive language are, / From the very beginning, something Zuigetsu knows a hundred times over; / Yet, fearing the sacred words of Kami, / To correct straightway, re-examine, and re-proclaim / The errors of misunderstanding the true nature of the Sangyōkyō / Through the commentary of learning and knowledge, / And to make known the true path of the divine realm, / Heedless of abusive language and fervent mockery, / As long as the wheel of the mouth turns, / As long as the brush of the writer continues, / Like an unwinding ball of thread, / He records it at great length.

原文（近代文語）

副守の悪霊に駆使されて 空前絶後の神業に 外れる人も偶にある 同じ教の信徒は 神の心を汲み取りて 互に気を付け助け合ひ 慢心鉄道の終点に 行詰りたるアフン駅 何のエキ無き醜態を 暴露させない其間に 世人を思ふ真心の 凝り固まりし瑞月が ここに一言述べておく あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましませよ 朝日は照るとも曇るとも 月は盈つとも虧くるとも 仮令大地は沈むとも 誠の力は世を救ふ 誠を知らぬ智恵学者
