Chapter 6: Great Deity 宣 [771]

大神宣〔771〕

§ 1

Susanōnomikoto stood up with majestic dignity, and in a solemn tone of voice, caused it to be sung.

Throughout the land of Toyoashihara, Yamataorochi, Shikogitsune, and the swarming hordes of Magaoni soil the mountain ridges and river rapids with foul demonic winds; seeing this tragic state where the common folk beneath the heavens are tormented and afflicted, not enduring to look upon it, the Mizumitama appeared as Kamususanō. Dispatching eighty fierce deities of Kamuzukasa, together with the eight maidens and noble children, to the four quarters to proclaim and spread the divine teachings of Sango, and to save mountains, rivers, grasses, trees, birds, beasts, and even the insect tribes through the pure water and fire of Kototama, leaving behind the sanctuary of Mount Ubusana, at a time when he wandered the central lands of the great islands of Yashu, amidst the upheaval at the very heart of the land of Yamato, in the sacred realm of Aya no Takama, appearing as the foundation of this world, Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, hiding his identity in the world as Kunitakehiko, reverend is he as he dwells in concealment. To save this world, the severe august spirit, standing side by side with the auspicious august spirit, to bestow eternal peace and life upon all the grasses and trees of the four quarters under heaven, opening the teachings of Sango to the gods of heaven and earth, while worrying and caring morning and night, the three august jewel divine treasures shall eternally repose in Takamanohara, and once again the Mani jewel that shall appear, shining brilliantly with the five august jewels, with the clear shadows of the three-five moon, appearing as the divine decrees of Haniyashihiko and Haniyashihime, the divine august spirits now here at last more clearly became enshrined before the noble august command of Tamateruhiko and the noble august command of Tamateruhime. Appearing as the auspicious august spirit, the deity of Kamuzukasa of the Sango teachings, keeping the woven brocade plan of the imperial deities hiding deep within the heart, hiding in the world, sinking and floating, until the coming of the pine age of the gods, establish the deep, profound, and subtle divine strategy upon the eternal and unchanging bedrock. My own spirit, Kamususanō, shall guide the Yamataorochi winding through the land of Yashu, and until the time comes when he shall reach the august presence of the Ōkami who shines in heaven to report and serve, hiding his body like a salamander or an earthworm, creeping beneath the fallen leaves, awaiting the blossoming spring, while completely and minutely accomplishing the noble scheme of the pine age. O Kunitakehiko no Ōkami, let your august command too, for a little while, like the cuckoo deep in the remote mountains, hide your form, endure the sorrows of many long years, and quietly await the divine administration of the pine age that shall soon arrive. These five Mani jewels appearing from Ryūgūjō shall repose eternally in the sacred realm of Aya, and the lotus pedestal fragrant upon the mountain of Okebushi, bound with heaven, fire, water, and earth, the highly fragrant plum blossoms, the living spirit of Konohanahime, revealing her form in thirty-three phases, to save the people of the world comprehensively, the tears she sheds are the Wachi River, flowing and flowing to the sea of Yura, hoisting the sails upon the ship of salvation, fulfilling sincerity as a single island. The true heart of Akiyamahiko, and the pure heart of the sacrifice of Kotowakebe, shall be the foundation of the divine age of Miroku, established by the deity as the severe august spirit, truly the limit of reverence. Ah, wondrous and divine are the deities, may the august spirits prosper and bless us, while the severe august spirit of Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami for a little while deeper in the recesses of Mount Shio, appearing as Kunitakehiko, hiding his form among the single leaves of grass, placing the prince and princess deities who were born in the brocade palace at the front, making the divine command of Kotowakebe the head, serving in the work of the deep, profound, and subtle divine realm. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the great earth should sink, this scheme of severity and auspiciousness, for a thousand and eight thousand ages eternally, shall never change; it is the truth unchanging from the beginning of heaven and earth. It is the truth unchanging from the beginning of heaven and earth. Shinmidakahi who created this world, Oomidakahi with a heart so vast, simply regarding everything as Shinmidakahi, as Oomidakahi, to save all the deities and men of heaven and earth, my sacred suffering is the very same heart and mind as the august heart of Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami; deeply do I revere and comprehend it, deeply do I offer my thanks."

Thus he finished singing, bestowed a smile upon all present, and caused his form to disappear into the inner chamber.

Kunitakehiko no Mikoto watched the retreating figure of Susanōnomikoto, joined his hands to express his gratitude, and finally standing before the assembly, raised his voice, which was tinged with a slightly sorrowful tone, and sang.

§ 2

The realm under the heavens, the land and its country, forged and solidified in the cascading waters of sweat and tears, purifying the ancestral origin of the Toyoashihara realm: the rigorous spirit of Kuniharutachi, his august majesty and exalted noble palace appearing in Takaamahara, proclaiming and transmitting the guiding ways for the myriad deities and human multitudes, and extensively spreading the divine rituals in minute detail as the primordial ancestor. From the twisted and warped mitama of Adarunohiko and Ebahime, there were born the crooked mitama, Yamataorochi, Shikogitsune, Shikome, Sagume, and Magaoni, enveloped in ominous clouds, until even the most beautiful land became a realm overflowing and drifting with filthy, muddy waters.

Tokoyonohiko and Tokoyohime, possessed by the crooked spirits of the vile, bearing upon their bodies the sins and defilements of the fifteen million deities, leaping forth from the crater of Mount Tenkyōzan guarded by Konohanahime, plunging down to the very depths of the coarse-metal earth, wandering and searching without a single shadow of omission through the nethermost rootlands, exhausting their hearts and bodies for the sake of the myriad viscera, solidifying and creating anew, reappearing at the volcanic crater of Tenkyōzan, changing their name to Nodate nohiko, racing across the provinces as if beckoning, and appearing as the divine spirit of Toyokurohime named Nodate nohime, making Mount Himalaya their base, and while concealing their bodies in the four quarters of the land, uniting the fire and water of husband and wife.

Inspecting the world without a single corner left unexamined, to solidify the foundation of the coming age of the pine trees, concealing their body in the four-tailed mountain at the edge of Mount Obuse, famed as the patterned Takaama where the purifying mizunomitama that washes this world resides, in order to serve the grand divine work of the five mitama, departing from the heavenly stone seat by the divine word of Hinode no Ōkami, descending to the lower bedrock, assuming the guise of Kunitakehiko, and appearing as the attendant deity of the divine deity Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami. The pure thought of a sincere heart that contemplates this world is not in vain, but a diamond-like, indestructible wish-fulfilling jewel that shines through the visible and invisible worlds.

Golden beads and purple noble treasures gathered swiftly in Jitentōjima, and to rule and protect the three thousand worlds, the foundation thereof became ever firmer, transforming into Kuninotokotachi. Again, the joyous five sacred jewels, hidden deeply in the bottom of the lake of Suwa—a secret homeland upon a single island in a dragon palace drifting on the waves—hailed as the five mitama, the Mani jewels of dazzling blue, red, white, yellow, and purple light, entrusted by the divine messengers Umikohime, Kōryūhime, Mukadenohime, Tomohiko, and Princess Teles to a golden-winged Yatagarasu, making the heavenly expanse glow brilliantly.

Parting the cloud paths, near the sacred land of patterned Jitentōjima—a divine island where pines grow lush—the grand shrine of Akiyamahiko towers at the river mouth of the deeply blessed Yura Sea; the color of his heart is patterned brocade, and Momohanahime illuminates the sky. In the garden tip where husband and wife, fire and water, share a mutual life and pine needles flourish, a ten-man group bearing the ten-fold crest returns and descends quietly. Ah, how noble is that august figure! At last, here the five sacred jewels and Kunitakehiko are forever concealed, continuing to protect this world; the Mani jewel sent by Tamayorihime has fallen into our hands. Ah, Omoikane-like deities, the gods! Times must be awaited.

There is nothing so noble as time. Even the true ancestral deities who created and solidified this world, if they do not obtain the time, fall into the world, concealing their bodies in the shadows of grass blades along the bitter, deep Tamba road, tormented in heart and injured in body even by the winds crossing the ridges on morning rains or snowy eves, having nothing to do but weep that they cannot exert their own strength for the sake of heaven and earth. Cuckoos whose breasts are torn asunder spitting blood in eight-thousand-eight-fold cries, while waiting for the time to come—what manner of blessed day is today! The posture of the divine age, the autumn garden of the ninth day of the ninth month of Kan-e. The august sky is high, the wind is clear, people's hearts are cool and refreshed, and my feelings are as clear as a cloudless Japanese sky.

The harmonious union of water and fire, illuminating the realm of Yashima, the auspicious sign solidifying the origin of the thirty-five lunar divine teachings, from the beginning when this world opened up, is still a fresh jewel of my heart. Ah, awe-inspiring deities, the gods! Suppressing ourselves and filled with awe before the august presence of Hinokami, who is enshrined in the youthful palace of the heavenly expanse, we offer distant thanksgiving to the branch spirit of Kuniharutachi, the hidden deity Kunitakehiko. Bearing upon our bodies the thousand-fold seat of purification, rejoicing in and revering the infinite, compassionate heart of Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami, who has appeared as the bright mitama of the living deity saving this world, we remain as the divine administrators of Kototama-yoriwake.

§ 3

Carrying once again the ritual offerings of a thousand seats for the divine work of this path ahead, together with that very Mitama, the three Mihashira assemble as a triad of Mitama. For the divine work to wash and purify the utterly turbid Utsushiyo, we humbly serve, and for the salvation of the hundred Kami of Ametsuchi and the Hitogusa, breaking our Magokoro into a thousand pieces like Tsukushigata, our deep thoughts are like the dragon's sea. Secretly, secretly serving the divine work, purifying our hearts and cleansing our bodies, we await and pass our days counting on our fingers for the divine administration of the Miroku Kami in the age of Matsu. Deign to hear fully and in every detail the divine hearts of us three Mihashira, you who have appeared unchanging and eternal in the Wakamiya of Amatsumisora.

Before the August Presence of the Hinode no Ōkami, we respectfully pray again and beseech you. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may your august Mitama bless and preserve us.'

So singing to an end, with a slight bow to all present, the august figure instantly became white smoke and vanished from that place. Everyone was startled with a gasp, immediately clapped their hands, chanted the Amatsu Norito, and recalling the loftiness of the august divine will, they choked with tears of emotion.
