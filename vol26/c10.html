Chapter 10: Ship Song [775]

船歌〔775〕

§ 1

From the river mouth of the port of Yura, the Mikoshi is placed upon a newly built Mifune, and while guarding the Mani Hotsushiyu, with courageous sounds of Ongaku, swelling the Maho in the Kitakaze, they proceed leisurely up the deep Kahase toward the Seichi.

Standing straight at the bow of the boat, Kuniyoriwake, wearing the Sendenfuku, rolling up the Himenpu, began to chant with a clear voice and wondrous meter. His song:

Plum blossoms of Sanzensekai / Opening all at once when the Tokitsukaze blows / Parting the Yahekumo of Ame / The rays of the Amatsu-hi / That were deeply shrouded / Gradually growing bright and clear / And the light of the moon so Kaukau / Becoming an age that illuminates Gekai / The Mizumitama washing this world / The Shintoku shining, the Izunomitama / The divine planning of the Sutora and Hatara / Now gathered here in the northern sky / In the port of Yura, famed far and wide / The divine mandate's mansion / Of Akiyamahiko, the minister of the children of men / Ensconced in the innermost room / The Yanagibako containing the jewels / The Lord God Susanoo / And the great deity Kunitakehiko / Revering their divine words, Kuniyoriwake / The divine minister of the word-decree / Fifty children of eight maidens, Isokohime / The princess Umeko, purely guarding the jewels / As the Kominami come dashing and gathering at the river mouth / They go up swaying, swaying / The breeze of Kadokaze also refreshing / Blowing away the dust and dirt / The shadow of the crooked Mitsutama lost / The venerable divine age draws near / The river of Yura, whose flow is pure / Proceeding upward by divine grace / Ah, Ame-no-Musubi-no-Kami, may the spirits prosper and abide / The sincere flowers planned by the venerable deities / Begin to emit their fragrance / To the unprecedented divine work / Kuniyoriwake, who has served / As the missionary of the divine will / With what could I compare it, lacking all means / Rejoicing, taking courage, he goes forth to transmit it.

When I think upon it, in the distant past, the land of manifestation of Mesopotamia / Entrusting my body to Brahmanism / Though I became a teacher of the doctrine / Since the Lord God Susanoo ascended to Takamanohara / And the Ama-no-Iwato was closed / And the world became eternal darkness / In the sadness of a tender infant separated from parents / And separated from brothers while alive / My crooked and warped spirit / Exhausting every manner of crookedness / Seeking my parents as a husband and wife pair / Jitentoshiba near and far / Pillow of travel in sorrow for three years / Climbing over steep mountains / The palpitations of my chest also at Utsuyama / A pair advancing to the river's edge / To my father whom I have sought day by day / Unknowingly and unconsciously meeting by chance / The divine minister of the forest of Takeshi / Matsutakahiko is my father / Oh, the joy when I realized it / Not only that, but my older brother / Ama-no-Mura in unexpected wise / In the joy of meeting by chance / At the very time my heart was thundering / My wife, the Mikoto, as brother and sister / Living together without knowing or realizing it / The terror of the sin of my own body / Praying to gods to grant forgiveness / To wash away sins and pollutions / Ascending to the Seichi of Aya / The divine minister of the word-decree / Standing forth before your honored presence / The venerable divine teachings / Extensively and earnestly taught / Upon my sins being forgiven / Truly the venerable teachings of Mitama / Missionary of the divine doctrine of Mitsu and Go / Given the name Kuniyoriwake / Together with Tamajiwake and Ryukokubake as intimate friends / Leaving behind the venerable Seichi / Visiting the Tsukinomiya-den of Kameyama / Whose very name is auspicious / Under the moonlit grove at midnight / Teased by Tamajiwake / What a foolishness that made my blood run cold / Visiting the majestic Izu cavern of Takakuma / Where the divine might also resides / Receiving the divine virtue yet further / Crossing over the Sakai pass / Letting my feet go to Hokidani / A petty thief blocking the way / Directing my Kototama to him / As I proceed, the footfalls of a thousand wolves / Frightening even to hear, traversing the mountain path / Concealing my form in the green mountains where tree leaves grow thick / I proceed / To my friend Tamajiwake / Meeting auspiciously at the mansion of Mokisuke in Yudanigatani / The missionary whose heart is also emboldened / At the water's edge of the lake of Tsuda / Parting sleeves, tracing the peaks of Rokko / Arriving at last all together / At the cavern of the Mikoto of Takayori / Rescuing Takahime and Kurohime / Subjugating the Mikoto of Takayori / To the true path of Mitsu and Go / Advancing thence, at the divine mansion in the forest of Ikuta of Mokisuke / Appointed to the post of caretaker / At the time when Akihiko and Komahiko together / Were opening the teachings / Beginning with Takahime, who called herself the sun god whose heart was stolen away / Coming to visit at the twilight / Along with Takayamahiko and Kurohime, to the divine ministers / In the innermost room, stealthily staying / Takahime and Komahiko at the doorway / A dispute across the door / The voice of the old crone and the voice of the maiden / Manipulating words skillfully / The comicalness of Takahime also being temporarily led astray / At length pulling open the front door / And Komahiko, the teacher of the doctrine / Having disputed for a while / Noticing the sound of the smoking pipe / In the innermost room where someone was smoking tobacco / Takahime suddenly / Pushes open the sliding door and steps inside

原文（近代文語）

由良の港の川口より新調の御船に神輿を乗せ、麻邇の宝珠を守護しながら、音楽の音も勇ましく、北風に真帆を孕ませ、悠々として深き川瀬を聖地を指して上り行く。

船の先にスツクと立上り、宣伝服を着したる国依別は、被面布を巻上げながら、声も涼しく節面白く歌ひ出した。其歌、

『三千世界の梅の花 一度に開く時津風 天の八重雲掻き分けて 深く包みし天津日の 影もやうやう冴え渡り 月の光も皎々と 下界を照らす世となりぬ 此世を洗ふ瑞御霊 神徳輝く厳御霊 経と緯との御経綸 茲に揃うて北の空 由良の港に名も高き 人子の司秋山彦の 神の命の御館 奥の一間に安置して 玉を納めし柳筥 神素盞嗚大神や 国武彦大神の 御言畏み言依別の 瑞の命の神司

八人乙女の五十子姫 梅子の姫のいと清く 玉を守りて小波の 打ち寄せ来る川口を ゆらりゆらりと上り来る 科戸の風も爽かに 吹き払ひたる塵芥 曲の身魂の影失せて 尊き神世は近づきぬ 流れも清き由良の川 神の恵に上り行く あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましまして 尊き神の仕組みたる 誠の花は匂ひ初め 空前絶後の神業に 仕へ奉りし国依別の 神の命の宣伝使 何に譬へむ術もなく 喜び勇み伝へ行く

思へば遠き其昔 メソポタミヤの顕恩郷 バラモン教に身を任せ 教司となりぬれど 神素盞嗚大神が 高天原に上りまし 天の岩戸は閉されて 世は常闇となりしより 年端も行かぬ幼児の 親には別れ兄弟に 生き別れたる悲しさに 奸け曲りし吾身魂 曲の限りを尽しつつ 親を探ねて夫婦連 自転倒島を遠近と 憂を三年の旅枕 嶮しき山を乗り越えて 胸の動悸も宇都山の 川辺に進む二人連 日頃探ぬる吾父に

知らず識らずに廻り会ひ 武志の森の神司 松鷹彦は吾父と 覚りし時の嬉しさよ それのみならず吾兄の 天の真浦にゆくりなく 廻り会うたる嬉しさに 胸轟かす折もあれ 妻の命は兄妹と 知らず識らずに暮したる 吾身の罪の恐ろしさ 神に祈りて許々多久の 罪や穢を払はむと 綾の聖地に参上り 言依別の神司 貴の御前に立出でて 尊き神の御教を いと懇に教へられ 罪赦されし其上に 実にも尊き三五の

神の教の宣伝使 国依別と名を賜ひ 玉治別や竜国別の 親しき友と諸共に 尊き聖地を後にして 名さへ目出度き亀山の 月宮殿に参詣で 月照る夜半の森の下 玉治別にからかはれ 肝を冷せし愚さよ 神の御稜威も高熊の 厳の岩窟に参拝し 神徳更に蒙りて 堺峠を打渉り 足に任せて法貴谷 道にさやれる小盗人 わが言霊に言向けて 進み行く折千匹の 聞くも恐ろし狼が 山路を渉る足音に

木の葉茂れる青山に 姿を隠し進み行く 玉治別の吾友に 湯谷ケ谷なる杢助が 館に目出度く廻り会ひ 心も勇む宣伝使 津田の湖水の水際に 袂を別ち六甲の 峰を伝ひて鷹依の 姫の命の岩窟に 一同漸く廻り会ひ 高姫さまや黒姫を 救ひ出して鷹依の 姫の命を三五の 誠の道に服はせ それより進んで杢助が 生田の森の神館 留守居の役を命ぜられ 秋彦、駒彦諸共に 教を開く折柄に

玉に心を奪られたる 日の出神と自称せる 高姫さまを始めとし 高山彦や黒姫の 神の司に夕間暮 来訪されて奥の間に 秋彦諸共忍び居る 高姫、駒彦門口の 戸を隔てたる押問答 婆さまの声や娘声 言葉巧に操れば 高姫さまも一時は 迷はされたる可笑しさよ 漸う表戸引開けて 教司の駒彦と 暫時争ひ居たりしが 奥の一間に煙草吸ふ 煙管の音に心付き 高姫さまは忽ちに 襖押開け進み入る

§ 2

Saying Namusanpō, throwing himself backward, / Rolling over like a four-legged creature possessed, / Though not actually possessed, like a great tengu, / The high-nosed one of Futatabiyama, / His quick-witted cleverness turned to his ruin; / Mistaken for a true tengu / By Takahime-sama and Kurohime, / Demanding to know where the jewel is, / He lunges to attack the Mikoto / Of Kuniyoriwake, whom he never knew even in dreams, / With no way to escape, / While saying it is all right even if it is a lie, / The hiding place of the three jewels / Is in the Lake of Biwa in the province of Afumi, / Floating upon the waves, Chikubushima, / Beneath the shrine, sealed with a triangular / Stone lid, they are hidden away; / Quickly, even for a single moment, / If we do not hurry and go, says Kotoyoriwake's

Messengers of the Mizu no Mikoto / Might dig them up once more, / Urging them on, faster and faster, / All three to Chikubushima, / To the same place, yet separately, / If orders are given, the three of them / Run across the void, rushing away; / Kuniyoriwake and Akihiko, / Feeling as though they had escaped the jaws of death, / Gazing back at the mansion in the forest of Ikuta, / Walking resolutely away, / As fast as the steed of their heart gallops, / Whipping their legs like a Hamanaka post-horse, / Crossing mountain and field, traversing rivers, / Night following day, to Ayanosato, / Coming to the Palace of Nishiki and looking, / Kotoyoriwake and Mokusuke, / Beginning with the officials of the shrines, / All the sacred officials / Are returning to the port of Yura.

Welcoming the Mani jewel, / Just as they are setting forth, / Rejoicing and bold, accompanying Akihiko, / Coming here and looking, / The unexpected Mizu-Mitama, / The Great Deity Kami-Susanoo-no-Ōkami, / With a stern yet smiling countenance, / Was waiting for us; / Ah, Kamugarakari, kami kami, / By the grace of divine favor, / Now entrusting our bodies to this boat, / The joy of returning to the sacred land! / Yet even so, Takahime, / Takayamahiko, and Kurohime, / Their three souls must surely be, / Beyond expectation, drained of strength, / As if even the hair on their rear ends had been plucked / By the tortuous deity Kuniyoriwake, / Puffing up their faces and blowing bubbles, / They must be furiously angry.

The three jewels cannot be obtained, / The divine work of the five gems / Is once more performed by humans, / Troubling their hearts, no doubt; / When at last we return to the sacred land, / Kuniyoriwake, by Takahime, / Will surely have his lapel seized and a scolding / Given to him to hear, no doubt; / Ah, Kamugarakari, kami kami, / May the Mitama bless and prosper us, / Takahime-sama's rising anger, / If it be possible, peacefully / May you soothe, O Great Deity / Who protects the path of three and five, / Before the august presence of Tamateruhiko and Tamateru- / hime no Mikoto, / Anticipating the future, the Shinto officials / Of Kuniyoriwake offer their prayers; / Ah, the joy of reaching the riverbank / Of Kawamori, where the divine virtue now rests.

Driven by the waves, in the middle of the river, / Higher than Takahime, / Even the anger-rock, barely / Crossing it, the joyous Yakumogawa, / Where the meeting waters rush swiftly, / A cool breeze too, Fukuchiyama, / Joyful tidings, Shirasegawa, / Kicking up the waves, ascending upstream; / Ah, Kamugarakari, kami kami, / May the Mitama bless and prosper us!'

Thus the song was ended.

Kotoyoriwake no Mikoto and all the others burst out laughing at this song, holding their bellies.

As if towing a boat through clear waters with eighty ropes, the vessel makes its smooth way, its full sails swelling in the favorable wind.

Looking up to gaze at the sky, the autumn air is high and clear, clouds of blue, red, white, yellow, and purple suddenly enfold the all-encompassing heavens, an indescribable fragrance permeates all around, subtle music is heard, and heavenly beings and maidens dance wildly in mid-air, blessing and sending off this boat; all are drifted into a state of exhilarating freshness, joy wells up and fulfillment overflows, and with courage running high, it seemed as though even the long journey's boat route would reach the sacred land in the blink of an eye.

原文（近代文語）

南無三宝と仰向けに グレンと覆つて四足の 憑依せし如装ひつ 憑依もせない大天狗 再度山の鼻高と 早速の頓智が仇となり 高姫さまや黒姫に 真の天狗と誤られ 玉の在処を知らせよと 夢にも知らぬ国依別の 命に向つて攻めかくる 遁るる由もなきままに 嘘言でもよいかと言ひ乍ら 三つの玉の隠し場所 近江の国は琵琶の湖 波に漂ふ竹生島 社の下に三角の 石の蓋して隠し在り 一時も早く片時も 急いで行かねば言依別の

瑞の命の使等が 又も掘出す虞あり 早く早くとせき立てて 三人共に竹生島 同じ所に別々に 指図をすれば三人が 宙を駆つて走り行く 国依別や秋彦は 虎口を逃れた心地して 生田の森の館をば 後に眺めてスタスタと 心の駒の逸るまに 足に鞭打つ膝栗毛 山野を渉り川を越え 夜を日に次で綾の里 錦の宮に来て見れば 言依別や杢助の 神の司を始めとし 聖地の役員一同は 由良の港に帰ります

三つの宝珠は手に入らず 五つの玉の神業は 又もや人に勤められ 心のもめる事だらう どうせ聖地へ帰りなば 国依別は高姫に 胸倉とられ一叱言 聞かして貰ふ事だらう あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましまして 高姫さまの腹立ちを なる事ならば穏かに 和め給へよと三五の 道を守らす大御神 玉照彦や玉照の 姫の命の御前に 先を案じて国依別の 神の司が願ぎ奉る はや神徳を河守の 川辺に着いた嬉しさよ

波にせかれて川中に 高姫ならぬいと高く 腹立岩も漸うに 越えて嬉しき八雲川 落ち合ふ水に颯々と 涼しき風も福知山 嬉しき便りを白瀬川 波を蹴立てて上り行く あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましませよ』

と歌ひ終つた。

言依別命を始め一同は此歌に吹出し腹を抱へて笑ひこけた。

船は清流を八十綱もて索かるる如く、順風に真帆を膨らせスルリスルリと辷り行く。

仰いで空を眺むれば天高く風澄み渡る秋の色、忽ち青、赤、白、黄、紫の雲満天を包み、得も言はれぬ芳香四辺に薫じ、微妙の音楽聞え、天人天女の中空に舞ひ狂ひて此船を祝しつつ送り給ふ如き爽快の気分に一同漂はされ、歓湧き興満ち、勇気凛々として流石長途の船路も瞬く間に聖地に帰り着く如く思はれた。
