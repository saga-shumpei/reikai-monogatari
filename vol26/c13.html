It is suspicious that they draw near.

It is commonly transmitted that the lake water of Biha in the province of Afumi has this name because its shape resembles the musical instrument, the biwa. However, upon Chikubujima, which floats within this lake, when the Lord God Kamususanōnōokami caused His worn Totsuka no Tsurugi to be sworn upon in the heavenly river of Yasukaha, as a signifier of the august spirit of purity, the three Goddesses renowned for their gentleness and chastity appeared, caused their august spirit to dwell in this very place, and from the time when dragon gods occasionally came to console the august hearts of the himegami and played the biwa, it came to be called the Lake of Biwa. Moreover, Kototama scholars also call it the heavenly Manai. Although present-day Chikubujima is at the extreme north of the lake, in this era, almost in the center of the lake, three islets—Pine Island, Bamboo Island, and Plum Island—floated together, forming a mitsudomoe, and floated their magnificent figures upon the azure waves. On Pine Island, Tagirihimegami was enshrined; on Bamboo Island, Ichikishimahimegami dwelt; and on Plum Island, Tagitsuhimegami dwelt, and each island, maintaining a position of about one hundred ken, was arranged orderly. Takahime first pointed to the shrine where Ichikishimahime of Bamboo Island was enshrined and rowed up to it.

Kurohime and Takayamahiko also, unarranged yet by the sorrow of the dark night, brought their boats to the same Bamboo Island, and gathered their heads together under the floor of the same shrine for the sake of searching for the jewel.

As the noble child of Kamususanōnōokami, Eikohime who was born, and Kamehiko who celebrates for myriad ages, morning and evening pray— that the severe divine works of the Lord God Kamususanōnōokami be accomplished in detail— chanting the heavenly congratulatory words, the heavenly numbering song cleanly: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten tallies, a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand— putting the heart of the pit of the stomach into it, at the time of praying, a boat is moored beyond the shore, and a black shadow comes patter-patter; without minding it at all, right in the midst of prayer, before the god, suddenly appearing and bowing low, chanding and offering the heavenly congratulatory words; though it is not distinctly known in the gloom, is that hoarse voice Takahime?

Entangled by the heart of attachment, searching for the jewel not knowing even the place, seeing it as too miserable to behold, Eikohime quietly starts up and leaves this place, and making the light of the stars her strength, with quiet steps to her own mansion, dividing the dark road, of the island's shadow she returns to the pure mansion. Later, Kamehiko all alone, with lowered voice and the august door opening softly and entering inside, if he peers to see how the state of things is, Takahime, who is no god, knowing not that there is a person inside the shrine, like a Buddha without knowledge, single-heartedly, grateful for safe arrival, the sound of hand-clapping also damply, "Grant the wish-granting jewel indestructible as diamond, the golden jewel or the purple rare treasure to Takahime's two hands!" with a trembling voice she is praying.

After a while, Takahime, like a rat, crawls close under the floor of the rare shrine, in the darkness where neither black nor white is told, while chanting divine names in a low voice, how amusing it is that she is searching about. Again, the approaching footsteps clapping hands before the shrine, while speaking of the secrets of the heart, dark prayer and silent supplication for a little while, with a eager heart, of the august shrine secretly peering about the four sides, like a mole under the floor she hid her figure once more. Although there is no light of the moon, illumined by the light of the stars, single long head, dividing the darkness, comes advancing.

Is it the shadow of the setting sun or a bamboo pole? That great man, the Migoshi Nyūdō, clapping hands before the shrine once more, the voice of gratitude also inside the mouth, having finished praying about something minutely, suddenly under the floor of the shrine hall hid his long head. Ah, wondrous gods, wondrous gods, the mitsudomoe of straying souls, upon the divine island where even the true mechanism floats open upon the white waves, with rough waves striking against the breast, standing the nails on the heart's demon, scraping about recklessly and at random, sweating profusely, the three persons from time to time colliding their heads, producing sparks like a flash, a flash to illuminate the darkness of the surroundings, yet the darkness of the heart does not clear away, with faces toward each other like unkazu, thoughts that break the heart.

Takahime thinks in her heart: "As Kuniyowake said, that high-collar of Gendaiwake sending two messengers, dug out the divine treasure of vital importance and buried it somewhere again quite cleverly, to make the noses of Hatsuwakahime and Tamanohime, the dear two, bleed? The achievements set up with much pains— is it a mischief to turn them into nothing? Hateful and bad is the ugly Mikoto of Gendaiwake, the vulgar high-collar. Hatsuwakahime and Tamanohime, when I think of it, when I think of it, how pitied!
