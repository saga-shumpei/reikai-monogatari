Passed into the jeweled hands of the kamuzukasa, returning through the three gates, just at the time when she went out to the beach, riding upon the eight-ata crow with golden wings illuminating the sky, splitting the clouds and mists of eternal heaven, descending leisurely to the garden of the noble mikoto, Akiyamahiko, the human-child kamuzukasa of renown at the port of Yura, the outer enclosure of the sacred ground of Aya. Ah, awe-inspiring Kami, Kami! Higher and higher rise the divine mighty forces, moistened by the dew of blessing, Tamanohime serving in the divine work of the mizu no mitama, bearing honor beyond my body, once more of the Ryugujo,

Reverently worshipping even the Mani jewel, the joy of serving in a part of the Izanagi-Izanami Shinkō! Entrusted to Brahmanism, appearing on Mount Oe, Onikumohiko the vice-leader, appearing as the spouse of Onikumobetsu, constructing the stronghold of Onigajō with sternness, transmitting the divine teaching far and near, the heroine also purified by the water of the teaching of the great deity of Ananai-kyō, now a precious Sendenshi, the light of the princess of Mukadehime shining universally to the four quarters. The red jewel, red as the heart, its light shining and flourishing more and more, and the divine mighty forces shining through the countries of the four quarters, illuminating thoroughly without a corner.

Teaching and guiding without leaving a single corner in the lands of all four directions

The teachings of the divine instruction of Ananai-kyō,

To the utmost of even a one-ten-thousandth part

Of the precious divine plan of the heavenly deities,

Pray exert yourselves, O Heavenly Deities,

Earthly Deities, and the Eight Hundred Myriad Deities,

And especially the most revered ruling deities of the divine teachings of Ananai-kyō,

Before the august presences of the Great Deity Kuninotokotachi

And Toyokunihime,

Kiusuke of the village of Akashi,

With a pure heart, offers up this prayer,

Ah, how truly wondrous are the Kami,

May the mitama flourish and prosper.'

Thus he finished chanting and returned to his original seat.
