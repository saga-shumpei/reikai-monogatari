Chapter 8: Tiger の嘯 [773]

虎の嘯〔773〕

§ 1

Mokusuke stood up, spread open a silver fan, and began to sing and dance of his own accord.

Descending unto Tenkyōzan, Of Kamuizanaginōokami, Born as the mitama of the miso, Tokiokashi who repels the magami, At the foot of the Altai mountains, As Kanehiko, the minister of hitogo, Disguising his form as Tokikō, The servant of Kami, As a sendenshi of Ishikoridome's Izu no Mikoto, Seeking to open Ananai-kyō, Crossing over the desolate moorlands, Advancing and advancing, the dark clouds Enveloping the deep mountains of the Caucasus, Blown away and swept clean, Close beside the side of the august mizu-no-mitama, Serving faithfully was this mitama of mine, Receiving secretly the august mikoto of Kamususanōnōokami, Crossing over to Onokorojima, Even the hue of the heart like Akanami's,

At the foot of the mountain pass of Yumidani, Disguising his form as a kikori, Taking to wife his spouse Osugi, Blessed by the auspicious miiki of Kami, The august birth of Hatsuwakahime, Raised affectionately like a butterfly or flower, When she reached the age of five in the autumn sky, His wife's mikoto departed from this world, At the juncture when father and daughter, left behind, Would bring this world to its limits, Visiting guided by the light of a lamp, Is the sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, The divine official Tamajiwake, Now at this time Mokusuke, Leapt forth into the Great Path of Kami, A pillar of Kami establishing the teachings Of the Alps upon Mount Takaharu, Directing Takayorihime, Proceeding to the sacred land of Aya, At the foot of Saigoyama,

Constructing a hall for the teachings, Close beside the forest of Ikuta, As the living mitama of Wakahimegimi, Reverently enshrining and promulgating the august teachings Far and near, Ever since hearing the teachings of Kami, Setting forth from Saigoyama, Ascending to the sacred land of Aya, The Way of Kami fulfilled by father and daughter, Ah, kannagara kamukamu, May the mitama flourish and abide, Even the tender Hatsuwaka, The princess's mikoto is Tamanohime, Together with Tamajiwake, Crossing over the ten thousand ri of surging waves, Crossing over to Ryūgūjima, Permanently settled as Tamayorihime, Visiting in worship the dragon's palace dwelling of Suwa's lake, Bestowed with the Mani Jewel,

Presented to the princess Umeko, Mounted upon a golden crow, With a loud sound across the clouds of Kumoi, Crossing back to the port of Yura, Into the garden of Akiyamahiko, The minister of hitogo, Of the noble mikoto, How wondrous that a party of ten persons Arrived safely descending, Ever since hearing that the party of princess Umeko, Bearing the Mani Jewel, Would return and arrive, My heart's steed surged with eagerness, Following Kotoyoriwake, Coming hither at last to behold, The unexpected auspicious mitama, Kamususanōnōokami, Kunitakehiko Ōmikami, How joyous to behold their holy countenances appearing with exceptional brilliance,

Not only that, but morning and evening, Constantly keeping in mind and praying for her safety, My own daughter, Hatsuwakahime especially, How joyous to see her august face beaming And encouragingly vibrant, Ah, kannagara kamukamu, Those three august jewels abiding eternally According to the will of Kami, Upon the foundation where they constructed The accomplishment of divine rule, Furthermore, the Mani Jewel of Ryūgūjō, Which should be called the mitama of ara-mitama, Gathering once more in the sacred land, Radiating its light, upon that very fact, Manifesting before our very eyes The divine workings expanding ever wider And unchanging for all eternity, Kami appearing in the forefront, Standing apart good and evil, Kamunaohi who created this world,

With a heart as vast as Ōnaohi, Simply whatever may happen in the mortal world, Look upon it straightly, listen to it straightly, Correctly re-proclaim the errors of the body, Drinking deep of the profound heart Of the Way of Kami of Ananai-kyō, While extracting the jewel of the heart From the innermost depths of the three august jewels, Searching for jewels while wearying one's suffering body, Just like the light of Amatsuhikari Shining brilliantly in the autumn sky, Flourishing and advancing directly before our eyes, Infinite in benevolence and compassion, the auspicious mitama, Kamususanōnōokami, Kunitakehiko Ōkami, Before their august presences Mokusuke prostrated himself, Reverently and respectfully praying, Ah, kannagara kamukamu, May the mitama flourish and abide,"

Thus concluding his song, he returned to his former seat.

原文（近代文語）

杢助は立ち上り銀扇を拡げて自ら歌ひ自ら舞うて見せた。

『天教山に天降ります 神伊弉諾大神の 御裳の身魂と生れたる 魔神を攘ふ時置師 アルタイ嶺の山麓に 人子の司鉄彦が 僕の神の時公と 姿を窶し石凝姥の 伊都の命の宣伝使 三五教を開かむと 荒野ケ原を打ち渉り 進み進みてコーカスの 深山を包む黒雲を 伊吹払ひに吹き払ひ 瑞の御霊の側近く 仕へまつりし吾身魂 神素盞嗚大神の 御言密かに蒙りて 自転倒島に打ち渡り 心の色も丹波の

教の館を築き上げ 生田の森の側近く 稚姫君の生身魂 斎きまつりて御教を 遠き近きに開きつつ 神の教を聞きしより 再度山を立ち出でて 綾の聖地に参上り 父娘が尽す神の道 あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はへましまして 年端も行かぬ初稚の 姫の命は玉能姫 玉治別と諸共に 万里の波濤を乗り越えて 竜宮島に打ち渡り 玉依姫の常久に 鎮まりいます諏訪の湖 竜の宮居に参詣で 麻邇の宝珠を賜はりて

梅子の姫に献り 黄金の烏に乗せられて 雲井の空を音高く 渡りて帰る由良港 人子の司秋山彦の 貴の命の庭園に 一行十人恙なく 降り来ませる尊さよ 梅子の姫の一行が 麻邇の宝珠を携へて 帰り来ますと聞きしより 心の駒は勇み立ち 言依別に従ひて 此処に漸く来て見れば 思ひ掛けなき瑞御霊 神素盞嗚大御神 国武彦大御神 聖顔殊に麗しく 現はれ給ふ嬉しさよ それのみならず朝夕に

と歌ひ終つて旧の座についた。
