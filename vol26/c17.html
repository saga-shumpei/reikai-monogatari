Chapter 17: 帰り路 [782]

帰り路〔782〕

§ 1

Driven by obsessive desire to find the whereabouts of the jewel stubbornly, with heart burning over the mountain tengu's raving, Takahime took Kurohime and Takayamahiko along, thought variously beneath the floorboards of the old shrine on Chikubujima, removed the triangular stones, dug and dug only to find no jewel, making her eyes pointed like triangles in vain labor, shaking her shoulders into squares, pushing aside the curtain of darkness, finally giving herself up to despair right there, committing her body to the roofless small boat, crossing the kosui of Biha, arriving safely at the beach of Ōtsu, together with Takayamahiko and Kurohime as soul-draining nanniyo,

Leading Aru and Eiru, in the journey's sky with drained strength, on the persimmon tree standing by the robau, with a sour face the muragarasu, looking down upon Takahime's entourage from above, caw aloud as ahoo-ahoo, while Takahime, though finding it cheeky, turned her head back to mutter to herself, ah how foolish how foolish, even the crow's slave laughs at me, this too is surely the reward for Kurohime being too clever and leaving a blank, a great retainer is a desirable thing, a star is a star but a shooting star, in whose sky or dark clouds will it derail I cannot tell, crossing many high mountains and low mountains, the old slope with dangerous footings.

With no strength at all, the pear tree's heart-darkness of lost affection, the prince's gloom beneath the shrine, the shame of passing with things brought to light, what appears ahead could it be Kameyama, amidst the forests soaring into the clouds, glitteringly the crest of the jūyō, that surely is none other than the Tsukigūden, Umeteruhiko-shi must be waiting there with a serious face, if she stops by for a moment and Takahime appears as the Hinode-kami, she will let them hear the teachings of the hensei danshi of the three-thousand-world divine plan and make them repent, she cannot simply ignore and pass by lost ghosts wandering the road, this too is a part of the divine work, what does Kurohime-sama think?

Takayamahiko's Fukurokuju-sama, if you have any opinions right here now, reserve and hesitation are not needed, without wrapping or hiding them smoothly, please spit out your opinions, saying this Kurohime nods and says, truly reasonable indeed reasonable indeed, to Takahime-sama's words I shall flatter and blindly obey, Takayamahiko's husband, you too come along together, three people at the origin of the world is splendid, Aru and Eisu play the role of attendants, this is not a matter of mihon numbers, since ancient times it is heard that when three gather together the wisdom of Monju comes out, in spite of the spite of being made fools of beneath Bententsama's floorboards, pretending to be Monju Bosatsu, the three of us including Fugen and Seishi,

Taking up the single rin of the divine governance straight before the upper brow, blindly obeying Kotowakebetsu, sending Umeteruhiko, let us act as Sarutahiko-kami guiding them back to the sacred place, ah, omikamisama, may your august spirits prosper and be blessed, making the earth resound with a thumping beat against their knee-caps with saké bottles, walking with a swagger as if the great road were narrow, aiming for the divine mansion of Umeteruhiko on the edge of Tsukigūden, how comical to advance thus.

Standing at the gate of Umeteruhiko's divine mansion, Takahime says, hey open this place, the august advent of the Hinode-kami, the fleshly palace possessed by the Otohime of the Ryūgū Sea, is Umeteruhiko at home, not knowing that such a splendid divine personage has come to visit, to be staying in the back is pitiful, even his celestial eye has lately become clouded it seems, entangled by obsessive desire, the self-righteous believer in 'good for oneself,' as punishment for blindly obeying Kotowakebetsu with yes-yes, has the heavenly eye you took such pains to learn become zero, how pitiful,

To push open the rock-door of the village-liver chest at the earliest possible moment, welcoming the Hinode-kami, will you not listen to the divine plan where the flower of sincerity blooms in an unprecedented divine realm, truly the karmic ties of mihon cannot be disputed after all, Umeteruhiko's wife is also just like that, thoroughly gathered blind fools, grandfather and grandmother alike, shouting with hoarse voices asking whether they will open quickly or not, inquiring with all their might, the gatekeeper with a sullen face appears ungraciously, asking who on earth is knocking at the gate of this master's absent house, go away at once,

Hearing this, Takahime raises her voice and asks, are you the gatekeeper of this house, where has Umeteruhiko gone?

§ 2

As the Shinkai grows day by day critical, In such a time of the myriad horsemen's charge, Seeing the world's distress as someone else's affair, As if a husband and wife carefreely Went out on a foliage-viewing excursion, A parent's heart is unknown to the child, The heart of the Kami is unknown to man. If that were merely of an ordinary person, it might pass, Yet being in the position of a sendenshi, To fly about leaving the mansion vacant, This too is surely Kotoyoriwake's Ugly Mikoto's ultra-high-collar ways, Or perhaps the projection of a deep influence? If they are absent, they are absent and it cannot be helped, Quickly open this gate for me, Inspect the mansion once, Investigate whether it be good or evil, And make a report to the Kami, or else I cannot do my duty. If I dawdle and delay, the day will decline, Since it is not the dusk-god after all.

It would be best to open quickly, Open, open, he urges impatiently. From within, the gatekeeper's shrill voice replies: Whose fellows you may be, I know not, Yet to demand by force that this gate be opened, Is the act of a foolish woman lacking all courtesy. Umeteruhiko and his wife Are not the sort of people you speak of. Kotoyoriwake-sama, With the Mani jewel of the Dragon Palace island, Splendid, splendid five-colored Treasures having been safely deposited, To join all together both for the welcoming and celebration, Such truly auspicious news having arrived, To serve in the divine work of the myriad horsemen Is precisely at this time, And so they have bravely gone forth. Concerning that, Takahime too, Kurohime-sama, and Takayamahiko,

The long-lived Fukurokuju divine ministers, Their souls extracted by the three jewels, Like fools they wandered about, and to the Lake Biwa waters of Ōmi, To Chikubushima, To grasp a jewel that has neither shadow nor form, They went—and after that, The vital and essential divine work Was performed at the sacred land of Aya, Everything has thereby been settled, And who will be left dumbfounded but Takahime And Kurohime-sama, so they say. Whoever you may be, I know not, Yet during your long journey, If you happen to meet Takahime, Tell her to abandon this incomprehensible jewel-search, To give up all hope from the bottom of her heart, And quickly return to the sacred land, So the gatekeeper of Umeteruhiko Has left this message for you to convey. Ah, Kanagara-kamigami!

I am like the unfulfilled autumn sky, The rice is scorching, my mood is like autumn leaves. Come, come, let me hurry to the inner chambers, And while Umeteruhiko is away, Let us leisurely partake of a feast. Ah, Kanagara-kamigami! If it cannot be fulfilled, then turn back, With this, excuse me, and the gatekeeper Joyfully conceals himself away in the inner rooms.

Hearing the words of Umeteruhiko's gatekeeper, Takahime feels her chest leap As if struck by lightning and rolling thunder, Her heart surging with violent waves. Like a dog that has eaten a boar, Takahime, Feigning an indifferent air, presses against her chest, Her words growing extremely gentle, And floating an unpalatable, disgusting smile As she completely transforms into a fawning cat: Truly, the floating world goes not as one wishes! As expected, the Dragon Palace's august treasure, Its proper time having arrived, like brocade of Aya, Was deposited in Takamanohara, Awaiting the realization of divine rule. Regarding that, the Dragon Palace's Otohime-sama's physical shrine, Must steady herself firmly here and now. The Mani jewel bound with heaven, fire, water, and earth Is the august treasure of the Dragon Palace's

Otohime-sama. Hatsuwakahime and Tamanohime, Kuniyoriwake rejoices And stirs up a tumult from top to bottom, And she who braves it on is none other than Kurohime, Because it is the grace of her soul. Since the Hinodegami is a Kami, From here on you must bare one shoulder And set yourself to the task. The vital and essential crucial moment: As Kurohime of Misoshirugi Deposited within the physical shrine, Tamayorihime who performed her spiritual training, That noble Mikoto is none other than myself! That I have dwelt for so long upon the single isle of the Dragon Palace Was for no other reason. Foreshadowing today's joyous event, My soul became active And bestowed the five jewels, To speak so sweetly is the thing to do now!

The vital and essential crucial moment, A divine work unprecedented and never to be repeated. You must by no means let it slip. Takayama-sama is of the same mind, Smoothing his square shoulders, Narrowing his round eyeballs, Jizo-face concealing his sharpness, Play that part gracefully as you should. This Takahime too, it is now or never, If it cannot be done by this hand, then by that hand, I will swiftly carry out my clever tricks. This is entirely Asahiko's, The Hinodegami's divine working. Just let everything be Naobi of the Kami, With a heart wide and Oonaobi, Look afresh and pronounce afresh through Naobi, Erasing all past faults, Openly and fairly for the sake of the Kami, For the sake of the people of the world, let a few flaws Be successfully buried away.

When the unprecedented and never-to-be-repeated divine work Is finally completed, Then indeed will one be a true divine pillar, Plum blossoms fragrant in all four directions. If we look afresh and pronounce afresh in this manner, All the failures tasted up to now Will vanish like bubbles on the waters of Lake Biwa, And become like white clouds over Mount Ibuki, To drift away and vanish in a mist of smoke. With but a single twist of one's mind, One can always live in ease. To live laughing is also a lifetime, To live regretting is likewise a lifetime. Though someone's achievement be usurped, And people call you a crafty wretch, Such things cannot be cared about in our present posture— If you win you are good, if you lose you are bad. Once you win and tighten the cords of your helmet, Afterwards all under heaven is at peace. Ah, truly, truly, how interesting!
