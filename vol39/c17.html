Chapter 17: テームス峠 [1082]

テームス峠〔1082〕

§ 1

Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, mother and daughter, disguised themselves in Jiyunreisugata, and while striking the ground with their Kongauzue, they proceeded toward Ukikigahara, singing Sendenka as they headed southwest through the mist-covered Nobe.

There was a fairly high mountain blocking the path. There was no way forward without crossing this mountain. The sun had already set behind the mountain's edge, and the Shimen were enveloped in Ankoku. The name of this mountain is Mount Thames. It is a fairly large pass, with a three-Sanri ascent and a three-Sanri descent, serving as the Kiyōiki between the land of Fusa and the land of the Moon.

The Futari, mother and daughter, were resting their breath, sitting on a rock by the Michibata at the Sanroku.

Two Umakata appeared there, leading Kanba and saying 'Hai, hai,' and spoke:

Umakata: 'Excuse me, travelers, this Mount Thames is a Iumei Shiyunpan in Afghanistan. It is Tautei that a woman's feet could cross it. I am one who is now crossing this pass to return to the land of the Moon; please, ride these horses. Since I am on my way back, I will charge you half the usual Chingin.'

Ōgonhime: 'Setsukaku as that is, we have sturdy feet, so we shall refrain from troubling the horses.'

Umakata: 'Hey, do not make a fool of me. Saying you have feet is stating the obvious; is there any person without feet who travels? If you do not want to, then fine. You act as if you will not ride, but I am the one who will not let you ride.'

Ōgonhime: 'Ohoho, Umakata-sama, you are not a real Umakata, are you? The proof that the horses you are leading are wild horses you caught on the spot is that they have no bits, their hooves are grown quite long, and your speech is not the speech of an Umakata. You must be some sort of creature who served as an attendant to a Brahmanist missionary. You intend to do something evil to us, mother and daughter, by making us ride the horses, and then when we reach a steep slope, striking the horses' legs to make them fall and doing something to us, do you not? It is written clearly on your face. I am not an old woman who would swallow such lies. Shall I toss you into the bottom of the valley just as I did at Kajika Pass? You are one of the five, the man who luckily escaped, are you not? I recognize your face from somewhere, so it is useless to try to deceive me.'

Umakata: 'Alas, now that it has been seen through to that extent, there is nothing I can do. In truth, I am a creature called Rebu who was among the five at that time, and not a very good one at that. The terror I felt when you manifested great divine power and threw three of my colleagues into the bottom of the valley! If we do not somehow make you, mother and daughter, cease to exist, our schemes will never succeed. Also, since two of our poor friends have made the journey to the underworld, we must avenge our friends. Thinking that you would surely cross this pass, I caught wild horses, and together with one friend I met on the road, I ran here at full speed and waited. However, now that you have seen through my scheme, I cannot lift a hand or foot. In that case, please refrain from riding the horses. Even if I were to take travelers who are not on their guard, my scheme would not succeed.'

He is speaking while looking fearful and ready to flee.

Ōgonhime: 'Hey, you called Rebu, are you not a subordinate of Onikumawake-sama? Or are you working on a temporary basis?'

Rebu: 'Yes, I have been receiving a fine allowance for about three years, but since the whereabouts of Onikumawake-sama's wife, Mukadehime, and Koitohime-sama are unknown, Onikumawake-sama has now attained a splendid status, stands shoulder to shoulder with Daikokunushi-sama, and enjoys far greater trust in the world than Daikokunushi-sama. Therefore, those who follow Onikumawake-sama increase day by day, and I, too, am one who has received his favor; so we four, including Hamu, formed a party to search for the whereabouts of the wife and daughter. However, no matter how much we search, we have divided the work to search this wide world and the Jitentōtō, but their whereabouts are still unknown. I heard a rumor that they had joined the San-Go-Kyo, and since there was talk that the missionaries of the San-Go-Kyo were gathered at the Saienkan on Mount Ubusuna, on the way here after crossing Kajika Pass, I encountered you. I thought that even if you were not Mukadehime or Koitohime, if I brought back an old woman and a daughter who were just right, and presented you to Onikumawake-sama, he might say, "This is a case of mistaken identity, but that is understandable, as one cannot tell from a portrait," and if I could receive words of praise for having gone to such lengths, it would prove that I had not been receiving an allowance for three years for nothing, so I performed a little act, disregarding my rudeness. That is how the situation stands, so I am by no means a thief or anything of the sort. I merely had a sly thought to sacrifice you out of duty for my allowance. However, I am but a minor official; the mastermind behind this is one called Hamu. I cannot endure being glared at by you, mother and daughter, so if this is the San-Go-Kyo, please save me by declaring it to be the divine Naohi and Ōnaohi, and by re-declaring that this too is the convenience of the Gods. I repent from the bottom of my heart and apologize with my hands joined like this. Hey, Teku, will you not join me in apologizing? It seems your anger is severe, and your mood will not easily improve, will it?'

Ōgonhime: 'Is what you say without a Sunbun of Machigahi?'

Rebu: 'He-he, how could I possibly tell a lie?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Hey, you called Rebu, is Onikumawake-sama truly in such Gosauken health? Is it not the case that he has taken a Kirei Okusama?'

Rebu: 'How could that be, how could that be? He is a Hinkauhausei, Jihi-deep person, so highly regarded throughout the Haruna capital and the entire Baramon country as a Seijinkunshi.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Is Ōkuronushi-sama in Sauken health?'

Rebu: 'He-he, he is Sauken indeed, but saying his previous Okusama had grown old, he built a tiny house to make her Inkiyo, installed a young Niyōbau like a Tennin in her place, keeps Takusan concubines, and from morning till night engages in Shiyuchinikurin Ranchiki-sawagi; everyone frowns upon it, but because he is an Eiyū Gauketsu who commands a Takusan Guntai, and is a descendant of Bontenō, no one dares to offer him Goiken no matter what he does; compared to Onikumawake-sama, in terms of both his Shinyō and his conduct, it is a difference of heaven and earth, black and white. Because Ōkuronushi-sama's Shinyō for Onikumawake-sama has grown so high, he has developed a bit of suspicion, and for one reason or another, he finds fault with everything my master does, makes unreasonable demands, and applies various kinds of pressure, but my master, who has great patience, no matter what Ōkuronushi-sama says, follows him with a calm face, being submissive and obedient. Even I, as his servant, cannot help but feel pity for him.'

He lowered his head and shed tears, and in those tears, the truth was revealed.

Ōgonhime: 'Hearing that, I am relieved. In truth, I am Onikumawake-sama's Niyōbau, Mukadehime.'

Rebu was astonished by these words and prostrated himself on the ground,

Rebu: 'Oh, oh, were you the Okusama? Not knowing this, I have been guilty of grave rudeness; please, I beg you to forgive me. In that case, is this young lady Koitohime-sama?'

He then wept tears of joy.

Kiyoteruhime: 'I am Onikumawake-sama's daughter, Koitohime. At the age of fifteen, I ran away from home due to a clouding of my heart, causing my parents worry. You must have heard of my history, must you not?'

Rebu: 'Yes, I do not know the details, but as it comes up in conversation among my colleagues from time to time, I have heard of it vaguely. Hearing this, there is no doubt that you are the Okusama and the young lady. Please, rest assured, ride this horse, and let me escort you to the Haruna capital. If I do so, I will have fulfilled my loyalty to my master; I beg you.'

Ōgonhime: 'Ah, how deep and watertight are the arrangements of the Lord God; they are things that a mere mortal cannot fathom. However, that my husband Onikumawake-sama has become such a splendid person, I am truly happy. Since he has become a Kami-tsukasa of such deep patience, if I surely preach the teachings of the Ananaikyo, he will likely become a follower of the Ananaikyo. Ah, how grateful, how grateful... Hey, Kiyoteruhime, it is now all right, please be at ease.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother, I am truly happy.'

Rebu: 'Excuse me, Okusama, since it is only before me, it is fine to say such things, but from now on, it would be best if you did not speak of the Ananaikyo anymore; it will cause trouble for Onikumawake-sama. Even without that, ever since the rumor that you, Okusama, and the young lady have become missionaries of the Ananaikyo reached the ears of Ōkuronushi-sama, tremendous pressure has been added upon my master. My master is a Seijinkunshi and does not hold even a bit of military power; once he is glared at by Ōkuronushi, it means his ruin, and therefore, he is enduring everything while leaving it to the Lord God, but if it is heard by Ōkuronushi that my master has listened to the story of the Ananaikyo, he will be destroyed. Please, for the sake of my master and both of you, it would be best if you did not speak of it from today on.'

Ōgonhime thought within her heart: 'What is this? Who is Ōkuronushi? No matter how much he wields the power of the military, it is a simple matter to subdue him through the breath of the Kototama of Ananahi, which possesses infinite benevolence. However, it would be pitiful to cause this fellow anxiety by saying such things...' and having made up her mind,

Ōgonhime: 'Just as you say, since it would cause trouble for the Dannasama, I shall no longer speak of the matters of Ananai-kyō. Now, Kiyoteruhime, you too must make sure not to speak of this any further.'

Rebu: 'Ah, hearing that has put me at ease. However, there is one great concern. At the entrance to the capital, Ōkuronushi’s army is on guard, inspecting every single person and conducting a test of faith; therefore, if you do not refrain from saying anything that sounds like the teachings of Ananai-kyō at that time, we must not fail at the very last moment...'

Ōgonhime: 'Ah, very well, very well, please be at ease. Since I was originally a missionary of Brahmanism, I shall handle that matter with perfect tact...'

Rebu: 'It is truly a regrettable thing to ask, but until you meet the Dannasama, you must not say things such as that you were Mukadehime or Koitohime. The Dannasama, for his part, yearns for you and wishes to summon you secretly, and has sent us to the lands of the four directions to search for your whereabouts. On the other hand, regarding Ōkuronushi... he has heard that the wife and daughter of Onikumawake-sama have become missionaries of Ananai-kyō, and thinks they will surely return someday. If Onikumawake were to submit to Ananai-kyō at that time, Brahmanism would be overthrown from its very foundations. It would be a terrible thing to let Onikumawake have a reunion between parent and child; thus, the General’s cunning plan is to capture and take your lives before that happens, and to keep it a secret from Onikumawake-sama. In this manner, a secret struggle is constantly being waged between Ōkuronushi-sama and Onikumawake-sama, so it is by no means an easy task to pass through the gates of the capital. Though it is very difficult for me to say, there is no other way but to bind you, mother and daughter, as criminals, tie you to the back of a horse, and send you through the gates to the mansion.'

Ōgonhime smiled broadly and thought in her heart: 'What, is there anything to be so surprised about? The gods are surely protecting us, do not worry about such things...' and she almost let it out of her mouth, but suddenly swallowed her words and, acting worried on purpose,

Ōgonhime: 'I am grateful for your kindness in looking out for us in every detail, Ōgonhime thanks you.'

Rebu: 'Yes, I am humbled by those words. If that is the case, let us sleep here tonight. In truth, this Tēmusu Pass is dangerous. Since a faction of Ōkuronushi has established a barrier and is inspecting travelers, it is all the more dangerous at night; let us wait for the break of dawn, change our appearance, and then cross this pass.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother-sama, if it is such a dangerous place, would it not be more interesting at night than during the day? Even if tens of thousands of Ōkuronushi’s subordinates come pressing upon us, if I were to throw every single one of them into the bottom of the valley and chastise them with the divine power of Kototama and my natural bravery, would it not be an interesting way to wake ourselves up? Hearing such things, how could I spend the night in a place like this? My flesh is dancing and my arms are throbbing so much I can hardly bear it.'

Ōgonhime: 'Now, now, Kiyoteruhime, do not lose sight of the great for the small. If we were to deal with mere child-soldiers and it became an obstacle to our entry into the capital of Haruna, that would be a grave matter indeed. Therefore, as Rebu says, let us go submissively until we reach the capital. Moreover, a missionary of Ananai-kyō, which practices non-resistance, must not do such things. Though you seem wise, you are still young... ah, it is a problem; in times like this, an old person is indeed necessary. Ohohohoho!'

Kiyoteruhime: 'I shall leave everything to the gods and to you, Mother-sama.'

Ōgonhime: 'Ah, that is good, that is good. The gods will surely be satisfied with those words of yours. Well then, Rebu, let us spend the night here.'

Rebu: 'Yes, please do so. I too am now at ease.'

Thus the four, master and servants, spread their straw capes upon the rocks and prepared to spend the night. The howling of wolves heard in the surroundings came eerily along with the sound of the cold wintry wind.

レーブ『アヽそれを聞いて此奴も安心致しました。併し乍らここに一つの大心配が厶います。都の入口に大黒主の軍隊が警護し、一々人物検めを致し、信仰の試験をして居りますから、其時にどうぞ三五教の教じみたことは一つも言はないやうにして下さらぬと、九分九厘で失敗してはなりませぬから……』

黄金姫『あゝヨシヨシ、安心しておくれ、私も元はバラモン教の宣伝使だから、そこは如才なうやつてのけるから………』

レーブ『誠に済まないことで厶いますが、旦那様にお会ひなさる迄、蜈蚣姫であつたとか、小糸姫であつたとか云ふやうなことを仰有つてはなりませぬぞや。旦那様は旦那様で、あなた方を恋慕うて私かに呼び寄せたいと思召し、吾々を四方の国にお遣はしになり、御行方を探させてゐられますなり、又一方の大黒主の方では……鬼熊別様の奥様娘子が三五教の宣伝使になつてゐるさうだから、何時かは帰つて来るだらう、其時に鬼熊別が三五教に帰順しようものなら、バラモン教は根底から覆つて了ふ。鬼熊別に親子の対面致させては大変だ、それ以前に取つ捉まへて命を取り、鬼熊別様にソツと内証で居らうといふ大将のズルイお考へ、かういふ具合で、大黒主様と鬼熊別様とは始終暗闘が続けられて居りますから、中々都の関門をくぐることは容易なことでは厶いませぬ。甚だ申にくいこと乍ら、あなた様母娘を科人として縛り上げ、馬の背に括りつけて関門をくぐりお館まで送るより手段はないので厶います』

黄金姫はニタリと笑ひ、心の中にて………ナニそれ程驚くことがあるものか、吾々にはキツと神様が守護して厶る、そんなことは心配すな……と口まで出さうとしたが、俄に呑み込み、ワザと心配さうに、

『何から何まで気をつけて呉れるお前の親切、黄金姫も有難う思ふぞや』

レーブ『ハイ、勿体ない其お言葉、左様ならば今夜はここで寝むことに致しませう。実は此テームス峠は剣呑で厶います。大黒主の一派の奴が関所を構へて往来の人を査べて居りますから、夜分は尚更剣呑なれば、夜の明けるのを待ち、姿を変へて此峠を越えることと致しませう』

清照姫『お母アさま、昼よりもそんな危険な処なら、夜分の方が面白いぢやありませぬか。大黒主の部下が仮令何万押寄せ来る共、言霊の神力や、生れつきの吾武勇にて、一人も残らず谷底へ投げやり、懲しめてやつたら、眠気さましになつて面白いぢやありませぬか。そんなことを聞くと、如何してこんな所に、夜明かしが出来ませう。どうも肉が躍つて腕が鳴り堪へられなくなつて来ました』

黄金姫『コレコレ清照姫、大事の前の小事だ。小童武者に相手になり、ハルナの都へ入城の妨害になつては、それこそ大変だから、レーブの云ふ通り、都へ着く迄は柔順しうして行きませう。まして無抵抗主義の三五教の宣伝使がそんな事をしてはなりませぬぞや、賢いやうでもまだ年が若いから、……アヽ困りますワイ、かうなると老人も矢張必要だなア。オホヽヽヽヽ』

清照姫『何事も神様とお母ア様にお任せ致しませう』

黄金姫『アヽそれが良い　それが良い。神様も嘸お前の其お言を御満足に思召すであらう。そんならレーブ、今夜はここで夜を明かすことにしよう』

レーブ『ハイ、さうなされませ。私もこれで安心を致しました』

と主従四人は岩の上に蓑を布き、一夜を明すこととなつた。四辺に聞ゆる狼の唸り声、凩の声と共に物凄く聞え来たる。
