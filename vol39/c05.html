Chapter 5: 人の心 [1070]

人の心〔1070〕

§ 1

Rēbu and Tāru, the two of them, were struck with terror as their three dōyaku were easily thrown into the tanizoko by the two women, and they fled for about jitsuchiyō, from where they finally descended to the tanigaha, leaping over rocks and crossing the asase to save the three, and at last managed to climb up about gorokuchiyō.

Rēbu: 'Oi, Tāru, what a terrible person has appeared. To think that Hamu the taishiyō would take them for mere women and kodomo, and suffer such an unexpected fukaku... that bear... I thought she was a tengu of a woman.'

Tāru: 'She is a jiyunrei with quite a bold kimodama, isn't she? Judging by the way she speaks and her bujiyutsu tanren, she is no ordinary woman. I do not know if she is a tengu, but since she said she was a believer of the Ananai-kyō, we must not treat her lightly even if we encounter women from now on. However, I wonder if the three of them are safe. That is the only thing I am worried about, and I cannot help it.'

Rēbu: 'It is not as if they would fall straight into the tanigaha and hit their heads the moment they fell. Since the trees are so thick that they block the valley, some of them must have been caught by trees on the way, and it is unlikely that all three of them fell into the tanigaha and died. However, Hamu the taishiyō must have surely met with shinbatsu. It is because his conduct has always been bad. If he is still breathing, we must not save him. Shall we save Iiru and Yosefu first, and leave that fellow for last?'

Tāru: 'That is true, that would be fine. But that woman was quite a wonderful person. It would be a great honor for a man to have such a nice woman as his wife.'

Rēbu: 'This is no time to be saying such cheerful things. Come, we must quickly find the whereabouts of the three and do something. If we dawdle and spend the day in a tanizoko like this, it will be a disaster. We will end up as food for lions, wolves, or large snakes. Come, let us go.'

Taking the lead, they climbed up along the tanigaha while thinking of various ways to proceed.

The two finally reached the tanizoko where the three had been thrown, and when they looked around, they saw them lying half-buried in the manago between the rocks, strangely enough.

Rēbu: 'What a strange way to be thrown. Even with so many rocks, all three of them were conveniently thrown into the manago that looks like true brocade and are sleeping peacefully; the one who threw them is quite clever, but the ones who were thrown are also quite clever.'

Tāru: 'Oi, let us talk about that later; we must quickly give them water and revive them, otherwise it will be no use at all. However, as we agreed, shall we decide not to save Hamu? It would be better to throw him into the river now and give him a water burial so that no trouble remains.'

Rēbu: 'First, I will attend to Yosefu, so you attend to Iiru. The procedure is to return the soul that has left the body far away back to the original body, Yosefu. Tāru, you must easily return the soul that has once left Iiru-sama back to his body. If we leave Hamu to drift in the tanigaha, he will surely croak, and a large fish will come out and eat Hamu headfirst. Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

While saying this, the two grabbed Yosefu and Iiru and performed artificial respiration. After about an hour, they finally began to blow air out with a wheeze and started to groan.

Rēbu: 'Come, success. The two are safe now. Look at that fellow Hamu. This guy is not even worth saving for last; because his daily conduct is bad and he is hated, no one will help him at a time like this. Even the gods will turn a blind eye to him. Humans must live so as not to be hated. A person cannot stand alone. It is a world where we help each other and get by. They say there are no demons in this floating world, but since this Hamu is hated even by us, his colleagues, this fellow is a true human-demon. It would be interesting if a demon went to the underworld and was tormented by demons. Ufu-fu-fu.'

§ 2

Hamu began to groan, 'Un, un, un.'

Rebu: 'Hey, this Koitsu will revive on his own even if we leave him alone. They say that a hated child thrives in the world... he is a fellow with strong Akūn. Throw him in now, throw him in. If we do not, there will be no Jisetsu for us to get ahead.'

Tāru: 'If we keep dealing with such a person, what we have painstakingly done with Jinkō Kokifu will end up being in Chiuto and Dame.'

Rebu: 'That is also true. The two of us cannot stop this work now, and if we leave him as he is, Hamu will gradually revive, and I have come to want another companion. This is why they say humans are animals of Kiyōdō Seikuwatsu.'

Hamu groaned loudly, 'Un, un, un,' and said,

Hamu: 'Re, re, re, Rebu, Tā, tā, tā, Tāru, there will be a punishment for uttering such Burei things. Ufu, fu, fu, fu.'

Rebu: 'Hey, this is Taihen. He has revived. Oi, Tāru, if we do not settle this quickly, something that cannot be Torikahe will happen.'

Tāru: 'Un.'

Hamu: 'Hey, hey, if you loosen your hands, the Inochi of you two will not be saved. I have heard every Ichibushijifu of your conversation from the beginning. Shall I, the hated child Hamu, thrive once more? Rebu, Tāru, you Kowatsupa, think that Hamu-sama will perform a Suisau on you. Ihi, hi.'

Rebu and Tāru were Bitsukuri and said,

Rebu and Tāru: 'Hey, this is Taihen.'

With that, they abandoned the two on the spot and fled, following the mountain stream one after another. Hamu sat up with a start and said,

Hamu: 'Atsu, ha, ha, ha, the heart of Ningen is something one cannot understand. Those two, Rebu and Tāru, thought I was dead, mocked me with the utmost language, and consulted on how not to save me, but the Tenbatsu is a fearful thing. In any case, I must save both Iru and Yosefu.'

While saying this, he scooped up the valley water, held it in his mouth, and tried his best to care for them. However, it did not seem easy for the two to regain their breath.

At that moment, a propaganda song echoed through the valley's reverberations:

As this voice entered his ears, Hamu, who had been attending to the two people at the bottom of the valley, suddenly lost his Ganshiyoku.

Hamu: 'Ya, this is Taihen. The kin of the woman from before have come to provide Ōen. I cannot be Guzuguzu. The lives of those two are in danger, but my own life is what matters most.'

Saying this, he leaped over the rocks of the valley stream and crossed the shallows, moving like a monkey. From the valley path on the mountainside, Terukō suddenly caught sight of this figure.

Terukō: 'Moshi, Sendenshi-sama, a strange fellow is running at the bottom of this valley right now. Please, look at that.'

Pointing, he spoke. Terukuniwake replied,

Terukuniwake: 'What, a person at the bottom of this valley?'

Saying this, he looked down intently and could clearly see a man running like a monkey, with blood dripping from his face.

Terukō: 'Moshi, is it not possible that some great tragedy is being enacted at the bottom of this valley? Given the suspicious behavior of that man, shall we not go down to the bottom of the valley and investigate?'

Terukuniwake: 'Un, perhaps I shall investigate.'

Kunikō: 'Oi, Terukō, how do you intend to descend this sheer cliff? If it were three or five ken, one might land safely even by gliding through the air, but in such a deep valley bottom, is there anything we can do?'

Terukō: 'Un, that is true. However, since that man went as far as that place, there must be a path somewhere. First, I shall leave it to Sendenshi-sama; if you command me to investigate, I shall investigate, and if you tell me to stop, I shall stop. As long as we follow the orders of Sendenshi-sama, we shall have no faults.'

Umekō: 'Moshi, Sendenshi-sama, it is too deep a valley bottom to be certain, but it appears as though two people have been killed. In all likelihood, the fellow who just fled killed them and ran away.'

He pointed to the bottom of the valley, gasping for breath. Terukuniwake gazed at the bottom of the valley and said,

Terukuniwake: 'It is indeed suspicious. I wonder if I should try to find out the situation somehow.'
