Chapter 16: 親子対面 [1081]

親子対面〔1081〕

§ 1

Semu and Shiamu, upon hearing that the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō had come to inquire, followed the orders of Pōro and stood at the entrance of the Gankutsu, rubbing their hands together and bowing their heads and waists repeatedly like rice-hulling grasshoppers.

Semu: 'Ah, this is, this is, Sendenshi-sama, you have come to visit us most welcome. For you to have traveled such a long distance, I have no words to express my gratitude. Unfortunately, my master is away; he has followed the Gomeirei of Daikyōshiyusama and has departed for the Dekatan Kaugen. During his absence, he gave strict orders that no one, whoever they may be, is to be allowed in here. I am sorry, but please, do return home. Say, Shiamu, what I am saying is surely not a lie, is it? Since I am bowing my head and waist until I become a Semushi, begging you, I would be most grateful if you would use your Gariyō as a Sendenshi and return. Heh, heh, heh, I am certainly not saying this with any Akui, and the old couple of the Ananai-kyō are definitely not locked inside this Gankutsu, so even if you search, it will be a Torau. I beg you, please, make a Gotaikiyaku at once.'

Terukuniwake: 'No, today, I cannot return just like this. There should be a couple named Kashiyahiko and Kashiyahime who were captured and brought here from Ameniya.'

Semu: 'Please, do not say such absurd things. Where would such curious people come from, all the way from Ameniya to such a deep mountain? That must be some kind of mistake.'

Ayame: 'No matter what you say, I must meet my parents. If you stand in my way, it will only be to your own detriment.'

Semu: 'Hey, this is a tough negotiation. I cannot handle this alone. Hey, Shiamu, go inside and report this to Pōro-sama quickly. And do that thing with that thing.'

Terukuniwake: 'I cannot wait even for a moment. I am going through, so show me the way.'

Semu: 'Please wait a moment. I must ask for the opinion of the absent division commander Pōro, otherwise it will be a crime of trespassing into the Gankutsu.'

Terukuniwake: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha, what a flustered way to act. At this rate, it is surely nothing good. I am worried about the fate of my parents. Come, Ayame-dono, let us proceed inside.'

Semu: 'Ah, hey, wait a moment, the couple is perfectly healthy and safe. We are definitely not abusing them.'

Terukuniwake: 'Aha, ha, ha, ha, I suppose so. It seems you are protecting them inside a Gankutsu so well-guarded that not even a gnat could pass through. Well, I shall thank you for your kindness later.'

The scene changes, and in a room in the back, the liquor that had kept them dead drunk until now suddenly wore off. With pale faces, they opened the door of the Gankutsu, and it was in the midst of a great chaotic uproar, with some dragging the couple out and others running around clutching rice bowls. Shiamu came flying there, gasping for breath,

Shiamu: 'Ta, ta, ta, this is terrible, terrible! Hey, General Pōro, Rail-sama, what should we do? Please lend me your wisdom.'

He kept patting the ground with both hands and struggling.

Pōro: 'What is it, with that hurried way of yours? What has happened?'

Shiamu: 'There is no way to describe it, the son has come. That girl, no matter what, is insisting on entering.'

Pōro: 'Whether she stands to enter or crawls to enter, I do not care about such things, but what do you mean by son and daughter?'

Shiamu: 'The son and daughter of the old couple we had hidden in the back have arrived. They say it is for revenge, and they have led a force of hundreds of thousands and are standing at the front to confront us.'

Pōro: 'How could a force of hundreds of thousands come through this narrow path?'

Shiyam: 'I do not know what it is, but a great number of soldiers in white robes have come from the mid-air. Hey, General, if you dawdle, the subjugation of this Gankutsu will begin. Please make some preparations.'

Pōro: 'Well, it cannot be helped. I shall first stand at the Omoteguchi and try to negotiate.'

Saying this, while whispering something to Rēru, he rushed out to the Omoteguchi, bowed politely, and said:

Pōro: 'I am Pōro, a humble servant who looks after this Gankutsu. I pray you take note of me and grant me your Gohiiki in the future. Now, please, although it is a mere Bauoku as you can see, do not be Goenriyo, and step right in.'

Terukuniwake: 'Are the couple, Kashiyahiko and Kashiyahime, present within this Taugankutsunai?'

Pōro: 'Yes, they are present. They are in excellent Gokigen and are waiting for your arrival in a state of Kinkijiyakuyaku. Now, please enter quickly and I pray for your Gotaimen.'

Terukuniwake: 'Whatever you say, this is an unfamiliar Gankutsu, and it is hard to tell into what trap of schemes one might fall. I am sorry to trouble you, but bring that couple here to guide us. We shall have our Gotaimen here.'

Pōro: 'That is reasonable, but at this time, our General Ōashiwake-sama is away at the Dekatan Plateau, having been summoned by the Daikyōshu from the capital of Haruna, leading tens of thousands of soldiers. Since the remaining people are only a little over twenty, and they have been drinking sake from morning till night and are helplessly dead drunk, we are certainly not plotting anything. Please, do come in.'

Ayame: 'Hey, Brother-sama, you must not advance carelessly. You there, the guard or whatever you are, you had better bring my parents here quickly. If you dawdle, it will not be for your own good.'

Pōro scratched his head and said:

Pōro: 'Well, your words are reasonable, but the couple has had a recurrence of their chronic illness; they say their kakke has flared up and they have headaches in their feet, so they cannot move at all. Also, the lady has her post-natal blood path or tail path acting up, and she is just groaning and writhing, unable to move her body due to her infirmity. I pray you will advance and grant them a meeting.'

Terukuniwake: 'If that is the case, it cannot be helped. Lead the way.'

Pōro: 'Now, please come this way.'

Saying this, he stood in the lead. The four in the party followed behind, and while keeping their attention on their surroundings, they advanced deeper and deeper. Suddenly, the floorboards of the mechanical device flipped over with a clatter, and the four fell all at once into a dark pit. Pōro, looking composed, locked the trapdoor, placed two or three heavy stones on it, and breathed a sigh of relief, saying:

Pōro: 'Hey, everyone, it is safe now. Set your minds at ease. That woman is Ayame, the one whom Ōashiwake-sama was enamored with. And the other is her brother, Terukuniwake, a famous heroic propagandist of the San-go-kyō. Once one is caught in his Kototama, there is an unparalleled divine power that makes one's hands and feet go completely numb. However, if we drop them into the depths of the abyss like this, this Gankutsu will be safe and peaceful from now on. Shall we hold a second party to celebrate?'

He shouted with a grin. Rēru, Shiyam, and Hāru gathered around joyfully.

Rēru: 'As expected of Pōro-sama, you are fully qualified to be the acting division commander. Yay, bravo, bravo! Now that it has come to this, what is there to fear? Let us drink and drink until we fall, and shall we partake of the sacred sake until we turn into snakes or tigers?'

With that, they brought out more sake bottles from the cellar and, singing with all their might, began a victory banquet for having escaped disaster and evil.

此声を聞くより老人夫婦は牢獄を飛び出で、樫谷彦は菖蒲に樫谷姫は照国別に抱つき、嬉し涙にかきくれ、暫しは無言の幕をつづけて、熱き涙を滝の如くに流すのみなり。

これよりポーロ、レールを初め一同の罪を赦し、照国別は両親を初め、妹菖蒲を国公に守らせ、タール、イール、ハム、ヨセフも前後を守つて、アーメニヤの故郷へ帰らしめ、自分は大神の使命を果すべく、照公、梅公及岩彦を伴ひ、岩窟を後にフサの国をさして、宣伝歌を歌ひつつ、勇み進んで出でて行く。惟神霊幸倍坐世。
