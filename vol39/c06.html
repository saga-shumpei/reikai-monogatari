Chapter 6: Phantom Mist [1071]

妖霧〔1071〕

§ 1

In a dense forest beside the mountain path stands an old, dilapidated shrine. Behind it, two men were whispering to each other.

Taaru: 'Hey, Reebu, have you ever experienced a day as terrifying as today? Truly, there could be no day more bizarre. A beautiful Queen-sama, who looked like a rose, a pansy, or a peony, descended from Kajikatauge accompanied by an old woman. The moment I caught a glimpse of her, my soul flew into the heavens. Even if she were an enemy, I would not have cared; I wanted to be touched by those beautiful hands just as the three fellows were. However, after meeting such a fate, I cannot help but wonder, what kind of Goddess-sama could that be? Ever since then, the figure of that Goddess has been flickering before my eyes, and I simply cannot help myself. I have come to feel a strange, indescribable sensation that is almost frightening.'

Reebu: 'You are quite the hopeless fool. Can you fulfill an important mission with an attitude like that? If that was the very Reco we were looking for, what do you intend to do?'

Taaru: 'I will not know until the time comes. In any case, since there are only useless fellows loitering about, there is no means to be taken. But even if those three fellows lost their lives, I think it is an honor, and they will likely attain Jiyōbutsu. If I could go to Tengoku in one leap from such a high place, I do not know how much more wonderful it would be if I were cast away by that Goddess and sent to Tengoku. After all, there is nothing interesting about staying in this world.'

Reebu: 'If you want to die that much, why did you not let Hamu kill you when he was chasing us? You are still clinging to your life, are you not?'

Taaru: 'Do not say such stupid things. You were running away, so I respected the duty of a friend and ran along with you. No matter how wonderful Tengoku may be, I cannot bear to be killed by a fellow like Hamu. If I am to die, having only one life to lose, I would be content to die at the hands of a Queen-sama like the Goddess Athena. But to be killed by Hamu, that demon-faced man whom everyone detests like a centipede—even a death-loving fellow like me would politely decline that. Ah, I have started to want to see the Goddess's face once more.'

Reebu: 'What about the old woman's face? If that Queen-sama were to say she would become your wife, the old woman would surely come along as a gift, but what would you do then?'

Taaru: 'Even an old woman is a woman. Since she gave birth to such a daughter, she must have been a great beauty in her youth. If I think of her as a beauty of the past, I suppose I would not feel too bad about it. Ufufufufu.'

Reebu: 'Quiet now. If Hamu hears our voices and comes here, it will be a disaster. He will surely take my life this time, as we have both said too much. He must be furious, so let us lower the curtain of silence for a while and hide under the floor of the shrine like a submarine.'

At this moment, Hamu arrived, breathing heavily with loud footsteps. He sat down on a stone in front of the shrine and began to mutter to himself.

Hamu: 'Ah, what a bizarre day this is. A beauty like a celestial maiden appeared, manifested infinite power, and tossed us three into the bottom of the valley as if a cat were holding a frog, then simply walked away. I thought I had spun in the air seven or eight times before I was dropped onto the sand, which felt like cotton wool. I lost consciousness for a while, but when I finally came to and tried to stand up, I could not move at all, perhaps because of some injury to my hip bone. While I was waiting for it to heal naturally, those heartless wretches Reebu and Taaru came along, spouting nonsense about giving me a water burial or saving the other two. I was so furious at the fellows for spouting such outrageous things that I forgot the pain in my hip and tried to rise, but the two of them fled like clouds and mist. They must have gone quite far by now. I wanted to revive Iiru and Yosefu while they were still warm, so I was tending to them when someone began singing a propaganda song in a voice that seemed to pierce my stomach. This must surely be related to the mother and daughter from earlier. Fearing that it would be a disaster if I lingered, I managed to crawl here, but my hip began to ache again and I cannot take a single step. Oh, how happy I would be if I could relax, but my mouth is the only thing that works while my body will not move at all. Ah, what should I do? If that propagandist from earlier comes, I will be thrown into the valley again, and this time it will be the end of my life. Ah, Great God of Brahmanism, I did this for the sake of the Path, so even if there was some impropriety, please look upon me with a broad heart and help me stand on these legs quickly. I beg of you.'

With a tearful voice, he began to pray. Rebu and Taru, the two of them, heard this monologue entirely from under the floor of the shrine, stuck out their tongues at each other with a grin, and nodded in agreement.

Suddenly, a thick, gray mist rose from the bottom of the Kajikagaha valley, wrapping the surroundings completely. Soon, not even a step ahead could be seen. Taking this as a stroke of luck, the two crawled out from under the shrine floor.

It appeared that Hamu's pain had become increasingly intense, for he began to groan, and finally, he writhed in agony, crying out, 'Ah, it hurts, it hurts.' Hamu did not even dream that the two were there. He was merely trembling, terrified only that the party of the Sendenshi might be chasing after him.

Rebu, assuming the voice of an old crone, said, 'Why are you, an eight-foot man, whining and groveling in such a cowardly and lingering manner before this shrine? I am Ōgonhime, whom you threw into the valley bottom at Kajikatauge. I thought you would be traveling through the Jifumanokudo by now, but have you wandered into a place like this again? You surely cannot be an Iurei. Since leaving Hamu half-dead is a pity, I must finish him off completely. There is a sharp rock here. Now, Kiyoteruhime, shall we two smash his Tokuri? Since red blood will likely come out instead of sake, it should be quite sweet if we drink that instead of sake; I have not sucked human blood for a long time, and this is a very fine prey. It is quite painful to be disguised as Ōgonhime like this. Ah, the Lord God provides such excellent food and drink. Since there is quite a lot of plump meat around the buttocks, it would taste very good if cooked as sukiyaki, but since we are on the road, it would taste better to gouge it out piece by piece with this sword and eat it raw. Otsuhohohoho.'

Taru, in the voice of a young girl, said, 'Mother, I am truly hungry, and this demon daughter has been troubled as well. This must surely be what the Daikokunushi of Onikumohiko-sama has provided for us. For three years now, we have been searching for the whereabouts of Mukadehime-sama and Koitohime-sama, only to be swindled out of our expenses without doing any remarkable work, so this must be our punishment, and Onikumohiko-sama has finally provided this for us mother and daughter after such a long time. They are such round, disgusting eyeballs; shall we gouge them out from those eyes first? Hohohoho. What a sweet scent it has; even a demon cannot endure it if there are no such occasions from time to time. Itsukihihihi.'

Wrapped in the mist, hearing only the voices, Hamu thought, 'So the mother and daughter from before were indeed demons after all; this is unbearable...' and tried to flee, but his waist ached, his legs were withered, and he could not move an inch. Finally, Hamu cried out, 'Excuse me, demon mother and daughter, please, just for today, spare my precious life. I am a retainer of Onikumohiko-sama. If you eat someone like me, it will instead become your sin, and I fear you will face reprimand from Onikumohiko-sama. It is impossible for allies to eat allies; please forgive my rudeness for today and grant me my life alone.'

Taru: 'Hohohoho, look at that hypocritical speech of Hamu. Now, demon hag, shall we cover our ears to everything and eat him? There is no way Onikumohiko-sama's eyes could reach a place like this; as long as we eat him up completely and keep a face like we have just eaten a manju in the privy, we will not be found out easily. Fortunately, we are in the mountains, and there is no demon watching; such an opportunity will not come again. Ah, I cannot stand it anymore, I cannot stand it; it is such an indescribably sweet human scent. Now, demon hag, if we dawdle, it will be terrible if the Sendenshi of the San-iku-kyo arrive.'

Ham panicked and said,

Ham: 'Excuse me, Onibaba-sama and Onimusume-sama, that is far too dōyoku. Where in the world does one hear of allies killing their own allies? Please save me out of the friendship we share as fellow followers of Brahmanism.'

Tār: 'Hotsuhahaha, Onibaba-sama, please listen to that. He speaks such selfish nonsense; do they not say that even among allies there are enemies? This fellow named Ham is an enemy within our own ranks, so there is no need for any yōshiya; when this is understood, we shall surely receive a gohōbi, and there is no kizukahi of receiving a kogoto from Onikumohiko-sama. There are fellows named Rēbu and Tār who are his dōrui, and saizen, as the two of us, Baba-sama and I, went far down into the tanisoko seeking to save the inochi of the sannin—Īru, Yosefu, and the other—whom we had thrown into the tanisoko, even his own buka were saying that while they were on the same side, this Ham alone was an akunin whom it would be better not to save, a hated child who thrives in the world, an utterly hopeless fellow; therefore, even if we devour him, metta a punishment will fall upon us, isn't that so, Rēbu... Whoopsie-daisy, Onibaba-sama.'

Rēbu: 'Hey, won't you speak with some kokoroe? If the fellow Ham realizes the truth, our setsukaku kiyōgen will turn to bubbles of water, will it not?'

Tār: 'Naw, what does it matter even if he realizes it? Ham cannot keep his ashikoshi, so even if it weren't an Onibaba or an Onimusume, one could just smash that itsushiyōdokuri, squeeze out his namachi, and eat his ketsuniku. Come, come, the sooner the better; if we shilly-shally, it will be a terrible thing if we are spotted by a propagation envoy of the Ananai-kyō.'

Ham: 'Excuse me, Onibaba-sama and Onimusume-sama, it is useless even if you disguise yourselves as such Rēbu or Tār. I am not such a good person as to be deceived by such a thing, nor am I an akunin. Please, kindly overlook me just for today; if you are to overpower someone whose sune and koshi cannot stand like this, even a three-year-old child could do it. Taking advantage of weakness to do such a thing will lower your koken, Onibaba-sama; Rēbu and Tār, who are far more stubborn articles, just fled ahead moments ago, and unlike me, those fellows have good flesh and plenty of blood. Please consume those fellows today, and forgive me because today is the death anniversary of my parents. I know not how much my father and mother in the meido will grieve. An-an-an, on-on-on!'

He broke down and began to weep like a wolf.

Rēbu: 'Are those fellows called Rēbu and Tār better persons or worse akunin than you? Let us hear that.'

Ham: 'Yes, yes, I shall certainly tell you. It is merely because I am faithful to my duties and use my buka strictly that I am regarded as an akunin. And because my position is high, people harbor suspicion and spread this or that bad rumor about me, so I am by no means such an akunin as the world claims. If you are Onisama, you surely understand the bottom of my heart well. In these times, there are no decent fellows among those who walk around with the faces of good people. The likes of Rēbu and Tār are fellows who fully possess the bad aspects of modern thought without regret, so they cannot have gone far yet. They are bound to be wandering about nearby, so please take a single bite and gulp those fellows down; by doing so, the duty of Onisama will be fulfilled, and a fragment of evil will be removed from this world, whereas a withered good person with a weak waist like me is useless. Please, if it can be done, chase after Rēbu and Tār.'

Rēbu: 'This Onibaba dislikes an akunin because their bones are tough; she was searching because she wanted to eat a good person like you. If one intends to capture and eat humans, there are as many in the world as the grains of sand on the beach, but because there are no good persons who taste delicious when eaten, the mother and child Onisama are wandering around here and there clutching their hungry bellies like this. Hearing that you are a good person, we cannot help but eat you. Hey, Onimusume, what an auspicious day today is!'

ハム『モシモシ鬼婆アさま、鬼娘さま、そんなレーブやタールに化けたつて駄目です。私はそんな事に騙されるやうな善人では厶いませぬ、悪人でも厶いませぬワイ。どうぞ今日丈は気よう見のがして下さいな、これ丈脛腰の立たぬやうな者を自由にするのなら、三つ子でも致しますぞや。弱味につけ込んでそんな事をなさると、鬼婆アさま沽券が下ります、モツト負惜みの強い代物の、レーブ、タールが今此先逃げましたから、彼奴は私と違つて肉付もよし血も沢山厶います。どうぞ今日は彼奴をきこしめし、私は親の命日だから許して下さい。冥土に御座る父母がどれ丈歎く事か知れませぬ。アンアンアン　オンオンオン』

と狼のやうに泣き出した。

レーブ『其レーブ、タールといふ奴は、貴様より善人か悪人か、それを聞かして呉れ』

ハム『ハイハイ聞かせます共、私は只職務忠実に部下を厳しく使ひますものだから、悪人にしられて居るのです。そして地位が高いものですから、猜疑心を起して、何とかかんとか悪評を立てられてるので、決して世間に言うてるやうな悪人ぢや厶いませぬ。あなたも鬼さまなら、よく私の腹の底が分りませう。善人面をして歩いてる奴にロクな奴ア、今の時節にや厶いませぬ。レーブ、タールの如きは、実に現代思潮の悪方面を遺憾なく具備した奴ですから、まだ遠くも行きますまい。此先あたりにマゴついてるに違ないから、彼奴を一カブリ　カブつてやつて下さい、然すりや鬼さまのお役目もつとまり、此世の中から悪の断片が取除かれるといふもの、私のやうな腰抜の萎びた善人は駄目ですよ。どうぞなる事ならば、レーブ、タールを追つかけて下さい』

レーブ『此の鬼婆アは悪人は骨がこわいから嫌だ、お前のやうな善人が喰ひたくて捜してゐたのだよ。人間を取つて食はうと思へば世界に浜の真砂ほどあるが、食て味のよい善人がないから、かうして母子の鬼がひもじい腹を抱へてそこら中をウロついてゐるのだ。善人と聞けばどうしても喰はずに居られぬ。コレ鬼娘今日は何といふ吉日だらう』

§ 4

Tāru: 'It is truly as Onibaba-sama says; there is nothing more delightful than this. In a world where good people are scarce, finding a good person like Hamu is like searching a rubbish heap and picking up a diamond. If I do not eat this, what should I eat?'

Hamu: 'Excuse me, but you have misunderstood me. Hamu is a Daiakunin of Tenkaitsupin. If you speak of true Zennin, they are Tāru and Rēbu. When you threw the three of us—Hamu, Īru, and Yosefu—into the Tanisoko earlier, the three of us were already on the verge of Kotoki, yet you risked Kiken to cross that Tanigaha and were Daizennin who kindly tried to save the three of us; therefore, the taste of your blood must surely be good, and the Guahi must be excellent. If Zennin taste good, it must be those two. Eating someone like me would be like chewing on sand, so please, do not look at such junk and hurry to chase after those Zennin of Tenkaitsupin. If you dawdle, they will Chinbotsu somewhere.'

Rēbu: 'This Onibaba changes her mood Tokidoki, and her state of mind changes Setsuna-Setsuna. Earlier, I thought I wanted to eat a Zennin, but since there is not much to chew on, I want to try eating you, who are a Daiakunin of Tenkaitsupin. Come, you had better prepare yourself; perhaps you should recite a Nenbutsu. Otsuho-ho-ho-ho, utsu-fu-fu-fu, it is pitiful for Hamu, who cannot even stand on her Ashikoshi and is only Tatsushiya with her mouth. With the mist covering the Shihau like this, and the Ohikari of Nichirin-sama gone, it is the golden age for demons. I would love to show you the Ohikari of Nichirin-sama one last time as a memento of this world, but then I could not do my work. Come, Tāru, ottodokkoi, you demon girl, shall we eat her after all?'

As they said this, the gray mist cleared away with a sudden mountain gale, and the figures of the three became clearly visible.

Rēbu: 'Atsuha-ha-ha-ha, the phantom has finally appeared. Hey, Hamu, you are quite the coward, are you not? Come, try chasing after the two of them. As a coward, you probably cannot chase them recklessly.'

Hamu: 'What is this, making me worry for nothing? Just you wait, I will take my revenge on you soon.'

With relief and anger combined, Hamu forgot the pain in her waist and the trouble in her legs, and stood up straight. The two were terrified, thinking, 'This is unbearable,' and ran off into the narrow valley path, fleeing for their lives in every direction.
