Chapter 4: River Deer Crossing [1069]

河鹿越〔1069〕

§ 1

With the autumn sky calm and serene as far as the eye could see, the mountains on all sides were woven into a brocade by Sahohime, creating golden fields and hills. Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime, the two of them, dressed in the diligent attire of pilgrims, climbed the steep slope of Kajikatauge, tapping the ground with their two staves as they ascended with an 'echi-echi' sound.

Ōgonhime is the name newly bestowed by Kamususanōnomikoto upon Mukadehime. Kiyoteruhime, on the other hand, is Ōriyōhime. The two sat upon a rock to rest their breath, gazing at the torrent flowing through the valley of the patchy, bald mountain where jagged edges appeared here and there, and comforting their traveler's hearts by watching the Hankiu flying about in various places.

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother-sama, what a beautiful view this is. This is the famous place where Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, while riding a horse and being blown by a violent wind, fell into this valley along with his attendants Tamahiko, Kusuhiko, and Itsukihiko, and while he was unconscious, he explored the heaven of five hundred thousand years in the future. It is said that when spring and summer come, it is a famous spot for river deer, and their voices are so fine that they overcome the sound of the valley water and can be heard even up here on the mountain. Since this is a journey where we need not rush, shall we rest our breath here for a while?'

Ōgonhime: 'No matter what you say, it is said that spies from the Brahman and Ural religions are appearing in large numbers near the mansion of Saien, so we must not be too careless. If we linger, it will be a disaster if the sun sets before we reach our planned destination, so let us proceed bit by bit. No matter how slow one's feet are, if one keeps to the roots, one will arrive quickly; no matter how much one rushes, if the rest is long, the journey will not progress. Come, let us go.'

So saying, she took the lead. Kiyoteruhime, having no reason to refuse her mother's words, used her staff for support, climbing up and down the steep slopes and crossing the valley many times, hurrying toward the capital of the land of Fusa.

On one side was a rugged, bald mountain, and on the other, a valley stream with a rushing torrent; sitting on a rock by a narrow path that wound around the waist of the mountain like a headband, four or five men were indulging in idle chatter while looking at the scenery. These five were subordinates of Onikumawake: Ham (Hanroku), Iru (Izo), Yosefu (Yoshiji), Rebu (Reiji), and Taru (Taro).

Ham: 'We have been searching this area for three years now, but no matter what anyone says, the world is wide, and there is no way we could know the whereabouts of Mukadehime, is there? No matter how many wanted posters we have, the figure from ten years ago must surely be quite different from how she looks now. Also, if we had met her even once, we would have some memory of it, but with only such descriptions as having a slightly square face, large eyes, or being of average height, we cannot possibly find a lead. In any case, whenever we see an old woman, let us grab her by the collar and inspect her face from now on. If we could only discover Mukadehime-sama, that would truly be something...'

Iru: 'Isn't it said that Mukadehime-sama has sunk into the San-go-kyo? It might be the fastest way if we were to disguise ourselves and infiltrate the sacred sites of the San-go-kyo to think it over.'

Ham: 'No matter how fast a way it is, how could we easily enter the midst of the enemy? The San-go-kyo has a method called the Heavenly Eye, which allows them to foresee our actions, so we cannot approach carelessly. Since this is near the Saien-kan, the propagandists of the San-go-kyo pass by in relatively large numbers. If we wait for the right time, Mukadehime-sama might pass by. First, let us not be flustered or hurried; isn't it safe enough to just receive our pay and pass the days like this?'

Iru: 'Even if you say that, having spent three years without finding a single clue and only receiving pay makes me feel somewhat guilty. In the end, we might be called incompetent and face the misery of being dismissed. If that happens, it will not be just our own hardship. Our wives and children will immediately be cast out onto the streets, and that is what I fear most. Even though Onikumawake-sama, who is next to the Great Master Daikokushu, has that much power, the whereabouts of his essential wife and daughter are unknown, and because he is such a firm-minded person, he does not do such disgraceful things as abandoning his precious wife like Daikokushu, taking beautiful women as his main wife, or keeping many concubines and playing with them in secret; so, in truth, it is pitiful even to hear about it. Since we are believers and are receiving a generous allowance from Onikumawake-sama, we must find them quickly and help them to be reconciled as husband and wife so that they may participate in the divine work.'

Hamu: 'Onikumawake-sama is a Kamisama of strong Shinkau, so he leaves everything entirely to Kamunagara, and does not worry even a little about things like his Saishi. He is like a Kiuseishiyu who shows us a true Tehon, entering into the Zanmai of Shinkau, restraining his Hinkau day and night, and saying that it is enough to leave everything to the Kamisama; but lately, he is in great Gomeiwaku due to the Kengi cast upon him by the Taishiyō Ōkuronushi-sama, and it is said that he is often Ketsukin from his Goshiyutsushi to the castle of Haruna.'

Iru: 'I cannot say it in a loud voice, but I feel much better having Onikumawake-sama, our Shiyujin, as our Kyōshiyu than looking up to a Hauratsufuki person like Ōkuronushi as our Kyōshiyu. The Ninki of the two is a great Saui. I wonder why Ōkuronushi, who is so unpopular, is allowed to wield such Haburi.'

Hamu: 'No matter what you say, it is a world overwhelmed by power, so it cannot be helped. It seems that even in the main castle of the Brahmanism that transmits the teaching of Makoto alone, one cannot win against the thing called power. The fact that Onikumawake-sama's wife and his only daughter, Koitohime-sama, have surrendered to the Sangokyō has reached the ears of the Taishiyō Ōkuronushi, and since then, Ōkuronushi has kept a suspicious eye on Onikumawake, saying that he must find the whereabouts of his Saishi and make them submit to Brahmanism from the bottom of their hearts. If not, he insists that his heart is divided, and he is making unreasonable demands, which is pitiful; it is truly a terrible Gomeiwaku for our Taishiyō-sama as well. It must be an unspeakable hardship for him.'

Rebu: 'Hey, I see a human shadow over there, don't I? It seems to be approaching this way. Let us be silent for a while and hide in this forest. That fellow might be one of the Sangokyō.'

Taru: 'Judging by that style, it looks like a pilgrim, but it seems to be a woman.'

Hamu: 'If that is a woman, or even if it is an old hag, I do not care who it is; let us tie them up and take them back to the country of India to show that we are not just idling away and receiving our pay. We have no excuse if we do not do that.'

Iru: 'Even if you bring back a fake after traveling all this way, will it not be immediately obvious once Onikumawake-sama sees them with his own eyes? You will only receive a scolding, being told, "You are a very stupid fellow." Bringing back such a fake is just a waste of effort and fatigue; it is safer not to deal with things you do not understand.'

Hamu: 'Naturally, we are people of low status and do not know the faces of Mukadehime-sama or Koitohime-sama, so if we find an old hag or a girl and bring them back saying we thought this must be them, even if it is a mistake, the kind and sincere Onikumawake-sama might say, "I see, that is likely; it is no wonder you mistook them. Since this is such a simple portrait... and since they must have aged greatly and their appearances must have changed," and he might even praise us instead, and we might be entrusted with this role for a long time. In any case, if we do not show our enthusiasm, our role will not be fulfilled. No, it is because we cannot fulfill our responsibility.'

Rebu: 'Hey, hey, it has approached that spot. Come, hide, hide.'

Saying this, they hid their figures in the thicket of bushes by the roadside.

The two pilgrims, parent and child, not knowing that the five were lurking there, stopped as they gazed at the valley stream with its beautiful scenery.

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother-sama, I heard that Kajika Pass is the most superb view in the world, but the flow of the brave valley stream and the colors of the mountains like brocade are truly beautiful, especially in autumn; it is a golden color just like Mother-sama's name, and I feel as if I am traveling through heaven. The mansion of Saien is also quite magnificent, but compared to this scenery, it cannot even come close.'

Ōgonhime: 'It is a truly beautiful Keshiki. In spring, flowers bloom, birds sing, and Aome sprouts; everything is fresh and the view is at its best, but the autumn view is also Kakubetsu. However, as I look at this autumn brocade, a cold wind blows again, and every tree, save for the Tokihagi, becomes like a withered Kareki that has shed its Hagoromo; thus, Jinsei is truly Haka. I, too, have grown old, and like a tree that has shed its Hagoromo, I feel Kokorosabi for no reason. You are still like the bud of a plum blossom; I wish to see you married to a good husband soon so that I may have Anshin, but it seems there is no permission from the Kamisama yet. Once I fulfill this Shimei, I want to see you married to a good husband, build a happy Katei, and I, too, wish to meet my husband again and live together on the same path as a Fūfu. What an Inguwa person I am. Although I have a husband in the Genzai, because our Shinkau differs, even though I know his Arika, I cannot go and make my Nano. When I was young, I thought nothing of it, but now that I have grown old, I find myself thinking of my husband.'

She speaks with a clouded voice and teary eyes. Kiyoteruhime says,

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother-sama, please do not have Goshinpai. I am still young, and there is no need to be so hurried to have a husband. However, through the Kuōdaimuhen Omegumi of the Kamisama, I am sure that you and Goriyōshinsama will meet again and come to serve on the same San-go path.'

Just as she says this, five men appear, shaking the trees, and stand in the way on the narrow mountain path.

Hamu: 'Listening to you just now, you seem to be people walking to open the path of God, but where are you from? And what are your names?'

He asks with a haughty air, arms akimbo, trying to intimidate them.

Ōgonhime: 'I am... you were listening in hiding earlier, were you not? I am Ōgonhime, who is here to restore this world to a golden world. There must be some reason for you to ask my name with such a fierce demeanor.'

Hamu: 'Is that so? If you call yourself Ōgonhime, you must be that person from the holy land of Jerusalem, the Haniyasuhime who was opening the San-go-kyō under the golden mountain. Hey, everyone, as I said before, we have no souvenir for our leader, so let us tie this woman up, carry her all the way to the sea of Fusa, and take her back to our mansion.'

All: 'Very well, but should we not probe a little more? It would be a disaster if she were a spy.'

They hesitate for a moment. Ōgonhime says,

Ōgonhime: 'Ho ho ho, are you not mountain bandits? You must have a cave somewhere around here. To be born as humans and yet make a living by threatening travelers is truly a pitiful thing. I am a believer of the San-go-kyō, so I will tell you a story; listen well right there.'

Hamu: 'Hey, everyone, this one is quite formidable; it is clear from her words just now that she is not a spy. Come, attack! One, two, three!'

He gives the order. Kiyoteruhime, still wearing her hat, says,

Kiyoteruhime: 'Hey, you senile old men, if you underestimate us just because we are women and try to meddle, you will be made to suffer. We are only two women passing through this dangerous mountain path, so we would not be here if we did not have the skill; if you wish to be our opponents, you had better be prepared.'

Hamu: 'How rude! What do you mean by calling us thieves? You are the insolent one; you must be the boss of the thieves. No matter how much of a boss you are, it will not work. We are five men in our prime, and you are an old hag and a young girl; instead of making such excuses, why not do as we say? If you do not make a fuss, we will not bind you, and we will take you to a good place. What is your answer?'

She glares at them. Ōgonhime, remaining Taizenjijiyaku, says:

Ōgonhime: 'Otsuhohohoho, what Negoto are you spouting, swinging your arms like crane flies? Ikaruga is laughing at you. As she urges her daughter and attempts to pass by, Ham issues a Meirei:

Ham: 'Now, attack!'

As both of them rush toward the two women with Mushiyabu, Ōgonhime says, 'What a Mendau,' and effortlessly throws both Ham and Eel into the Tanizoko. Joseph has his neck gripped by the Hosōde of Kiyoteruhime-sama, and he too is sent toward the Aofuchi below, falling with a splash after rotating three or four times in the Kūchiu.

Watching this, both Reeb and Taaru run off at Ichimokusan, reaching the Tanigaha where the three were thrown. Though they are impatient to save the three, the Daiganpeki stands like a vertical board, and they cannot even approach it. They flee about ten chō downstream, finally manage to descend to the Tanigaha by grabbing onto vines and the like, and travel along the current, searching for the Aofuchi where the three had fallen.

Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime-sama, unconcerned with the details, descend the mountain pass toward the southeast in a hurry, singing the propaganda song.
