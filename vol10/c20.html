Chapter 20: The Returning Host [450]

第20章　還軍〔450〕

§ 1

The imperial envoy Sakakuniwake, who rejected good, encouraged evil, and opposed the way of heaven and earth, mistook the counterfeit of Kasatoriwake—who, like a wild fox borrowing the tiger's terror, strutted triumphantly and threw his weight around under his master's shelter—for the true Divine King, cast him into a barred prisoner's cage, raised a shout of victory, leisurely mounted his steed, and taking along numerous troops, set out on the way back to his castle.

Takahiko, a gatekeeper of Tokoyo Castle, said:

"Hey, Kurahiko, wasn't Tokoyo Shin'ō-sama forced into a barred prisoner's cage for criminals and taken away to Rocky Castle? A terrible thing has come to pass. Now that it has come to this, even as we stand guard at the gate, we cannot be at ease. Guarding the gate in the master's absence makes one feel somewhat faint in presence, and it isn't worth boasting about."

"That does not matter at all. Drink and make merry, for an inch ahead is darkness; yet as they say that after darkness the moon appears, the dark of the eternal night too will surely clear up in time. Come now, we must firmly guard this bolt. Well, think about it carefully: inside this castle, all the prominent men have marched off to Yomishima, and there is truly a total dearth of talent. Hey, let me discuss one thing with you: from this moment on, Kurahiko shall quit being a gatekeeper and become Tokoyo Shin'ō. And then, you shall become Takatoriwake."

"Do not make a fool of me. You shall be the retainer,"

they were whispering. Once again, someone knocked hard and fiercely on the gate door.

Takahiko said, "Hey, hey, they've come again, they've come. If we are not careful this time, they might take us away. You had better become Tokoyo Shin'ō as you wished, get tied up tightly, and be taken away."

Kurahiko said, "Oh, I've already quit being Tokoyo Shin'ō!"

Outside the gate, the sounds of men and horses could be heard dreadfully loud.

"Hear, hear! I am Takeyamahiko, a retainer of Tokoyo Shin'ō. Open this gate immediately!"

Kurahiko said, "Hey, Takeyamahiko, who I thought had marched off to Yomishima, has returned. This must surely be a defeated army."

Takahiko said, "Well, whatever it is, shall we open it quickly?"

And so the two stood up, removed the bolt, and opened the gate to the left and right. Leading a vast army like clouds and mist, Takeyamahiko entered with majestic dignity, advancing deeper and deeper inside.

Just as Tokoyo Shin'ō and his wife were gasping in deep distress and lost in contemplation, they heard that Takeyamahiko had returned, found it baffling, appeared in the main hall together with four or five attendant retainers, and granted Takeyamahiko an audience. Takeyamahiko vigorously took his seat before the Divine King.

常世城の門番の高彦は、

「おい倉彦、常世神王様は罪人の乗る唐丸駕籠に乗せられて、ロッキー城へ召し連れて行かれたではないか。大変なことが起こってきたものだね。こうなると我々も門番をしていても気が気でないね。主人の留守の門番も、何だか影が薄いような気がして威張り甲斐がないではないか」

「そんなことはどうでもよいわ。飲めよ騒げよ一寸先は闇だ、闇の後には月が出る、と言うからには、常夜の闇もいつしか晴れることがあるよ。まあまあこの閂を我々はしっかりと守ることだ。まあよく考えてみよ、この城内には、偉い奴は皆黄泉島へ出陣してしまって、本当に人物払底だ。おい、一つ相談だが、これから倉彦は、ただ今限り門番を廃業して常世神王になるのだな。そうしてお前が鷹取別になれ」

「馬鹿にするな。貴様が家来だ」

と囁いている。またしても門の戸を手厳しく打ち叩く。

高彦「おいおい、また来たぞ来たぞ。今度は気をつけないと我々を連れて行くかも知れないぞ。貴様は望み通り常世神王になって縛り上げられて連れて行かれるとよいわ」

倉彦「やあ、常世神王はもう廃業だ」

門外には人馬の物音が物凄く聞こえている。

「やや、我は常世神王の従臣、竹山彦であるぞ。この門を速やかに開けよ」

倉彦「おい、黄泉島へ出陣したと思った竹山彦が帰って来おったぞ。こりゃきっと敗軍だな」

高彦「まあ何でもよい。早く開けようか」

と二人は立って閂を外し、左右に門を開いた。竹山彦は雲霞のような大軍を率いて、威風堂々と入って来て、奥へ奥へと進み入る。

常世神王夫婦は、青息吐息で思案に暮れている折しも、竹山彦が帰って来たと聞いて納得がいかず、四、五人の侍臣と共に本殿に現れて来て、竹山彦に拝謁を許した。竹山彦は威勢よく神王の前に座を占めた。

§ 2

Tokoyo Shin'ō said, "Are you not Takeyamahiko? Did you not march out to Yomotsujima? Yet your returning midway, I cannot comprehend its reason; surely there must be some deep circumstance in this."

Takeyamahiko said, "Your doubt is entirely reasonable, but there are many wicked people in Rocky Castle, and having detected midway that those intending to ensnare Tokoyo Shin'ō-sama have appeared, I deemed it an extraordinary and grave matter, and returned leading every single soldier of Tokoyo Castle. Soon, the other commanders below will also return, leading their respective subordinates. Now that it has come to this, let us firmly defend Tokoyo Castle, take advantage of the weakened defense of Rocky Castle, advance in an assault all at once, take Hinode-no-Kami captive, and annihilate the calamity of the Shin'ō. Ah, how delightful, how delightful!"

"Ah, as expected of Takeyamahiko, you have noticed a fine point."

Just at that moment, it became noisy before the gate once again, and the sounds of arrow-cries and battle-cries were heard distinctly as if grasped in the hand. These were the shouts of the brave commanders and fierce soldiers of Tokoyo Castle, having returned to the castle without a single exception.

From this moment, Tokoyo Shin'ō, gaining strength from the return of his commanders and soldiers, came to advance against Rocky Castle. In Rocky Castle, upon hearing of this situation, they were greatly astonished, and detached a portion of the soldiers heading toward Yomotsujima, made them hasten back to the castle, and exerted all efforts in defense; because of that, the greater part of the military force for Yomotsujima came to be reduced.

常世神王「お前は竹山彦ではないか、黄泉島に出陣したのではなかったか。それなのに中途で帰ってきたのはその意味が分からぬ、これには深い仔細があるのだろう」

竹山彦「御不審はごもっともですが、ロッキー城には悪人が多く、常世神王様を陥害しようとする者が現れたことを中途にて探知し、容易ならざる一大事と、常世城の軍卒を残らず引き連れて帰ってまいりました。やがて以下の諸将も各自の部下を引き連れて帰って来るはずです。こうなった上は、我々は常世城を固く守り、ロッキー城の守りが少なくなったのを好機として、一挙に攻め寄せて、日の出神を捕虜にし、神王の災いを殲滅いたしましょう。ああ、面白い、面白い」

「おお、さすがは竹山彦、よいところに気がついた」

ちょうどその時、門前がまたしても騒がしくなり、矢叫びの声、鬨の声が手に取るように聞こえてくる。これは常世城の勇将猛卒が一人も残らず帰城した叫び声であった。

これより常世神王は、将卒が帰ったことに力を得て、ロッキー城に攻め寄せることとなった。ロッキー城においては、この様子を聞いて大いに驚き、黄泉島に向かう軍卒の一部を割いて急ぎ帰城させ、防御に全力を尽くしたので、そのために黄泉島の兵力は、その大半を削がれるに至った。
