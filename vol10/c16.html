Chapter 16: The Opening of the Barred Gate [446]

第16章　固門開〔446〕

§ 1

Dividing the land of Tokoyo into east and west, high above the ridges of the Rocky Mountains, fluttering through the thick and thin clouds, hundreds and thousands of hollyhock banners on white ground fluttered and waved in the mountain storm, as the missionary of Odoyamatsumi gave the name Katayamahiko to the firm-hearted Katatora, who had completely shown his submission at the summit of Shira Mountain Pass; and in order to trample Rocky Castle and overturn the plots of the Shikome and Sagume from their very foundations, he advanced under a disguise.

This was the front gate of Rocky Castle. As brave generals such as Miyamabake and Takeshimahiko had marched out with fierce soldiers like lions and tigers to the battle of Yomishima, the garrison inside the castle had become extremely thin. For that reason, the vigilance was made all the stricter, and even during the day the front gate was not easily opened, being strictly guarded by Kamahiko and Kasahiko together with several gatekeepers. Katayamahiko raised a loud voice and cried:

"Hey, gatekeepers, open this gate! By the order of Tokoyo Shin'ō, having taken captive in the Karigane Peninsula of the Land of Me Odoyamatsumi and four missionaries, I have brought them back with me!"

When he so called out, Kasahiko, arrogant under the full authority of his master, assumed a haughty attitude and shouted:

"Oh, is that voice not that of Katatorahiko, the superior officer of Tokoyo Castle? You are one who marched to the Land of Me as a brave general of Tokoyo Castle; why did you not return to Tokoyo Castle, but have come to this main castle? Narrate the details minutely. Depending on the circumstances, this gate shall never be opened!"

Katayamahiko shouted in a loud voice:

"For a lowly gatekeeper, to speak such insolent abuse toward Katatorahiko, an attendant deity of Tokoyo Shin'ō! Do not quibble, but open this gate immediately! If you refuse, this is a matter of life and death, admitting not a moment's delay! With these arms of Katatorahiko, harder than iron, I shall smash it open and enter!"

Kasahiko: "Hey, Kamahiko, what shall we do? What tremendous force he has! In such a case, we gatekeepers cannot judge. There is no telling what scolding we might receive from Izanami no Ōkami. Kamahiko, won't you go into the interior and receive permission to open the gate?"

Kamahiko: "What does it matter? Just open it for him."

"Because if Katatorahiko has turned traitor and become a spy for the enemy, it would be a terrible matter."

"What does it matter? Opening it is the only way."

"Come, Lord Odoyama, at last the gate has opened. Well, Kasahiko, good work. You are a fellow who always acts arrogant under your master's authority, and it seems your habit cannot be cured after all."

Kasahiko: "Yes, yes, for the arrival of such a fine, splendid, and valiant general as yourself, my heart is full of the desire to let you pass, but no matter what I said, this fellow Kamahiko stubbornly resisted, so we took time and I am terribly sorry. For trifling gatekeepers like us to resist you, who command the three armies, even by a single word or half a phrase, is just like a praying mantis brandishing its axes against an imperial carriage; since it is utterly impossible, I told him to open the gate quickly, yet that fellow Kamahiko summoned the courage of a mantis and would not easily open it. Truly, he is a fellow beyond comprehension."

Kamahiko: "What are you saying, you cowardly scoundrel? Were you not the one who refused? Even though I was saying I did not care at all and that we should open it, you interfered, saying you feared the reprimand of Hinode-no-kami or that the eyes of the Ōkami would glare, and what is this pathetic sight now? The moment someone slightly strong appears, you wag your tail like a dog, tell obvious lies, and try to smear your own blunder onto me, your comrade—an utterly insolent rogue! As an example for the future, take this iron fist of Kama!"

No sooner had he said this than he slapped Kasahiko across the cheek, whereupon Kasahiko bared his shoulders and cried,

"Hey, Kama, do not make a fool of me! You are the one who lowers your tail whenever you see someone strong and babbles insincere flatteries; fine, remember this! This Kasahiko will pull off the support of your hat!"

So saying, he leapt at his neck. The two grappled with each other, struggling up and down. Katayamahiko of the Katatora clenched his fists in both hands and, squaring his shoulders and elbows, strode slowly and heavily toward the middle gate. Odoyamatsumi looked back repeatedly at their fight, and having opened the middle gate, both of them advanced inside.

Once again, someone was pounding on the front gate as if to break it down. At this time, four or five gatekeepers said,

"Hey, hey, Kama, Kasa, what shall we do? Shall we open it, or not open it? This is no time to be fighting! As many as two such strong fellows have passed through to the interior. We are terribly worried about what will become of us. Besides, someone is pounding at the gate with tremendous force once again, as if to break it. This is no time for quarreling! Stop it at once, will you not?"

Kasahiko, "Gate or dung, as if I care! Do whatever you please. I will not stop until I have wrenched off Kamahiko's neck!"

Kamahiko, "Hey, Kasa, why not stop this fight and postpone it until tomorrow? There is a saying that though brothers quarrel within the walls, they ward off insult from without. The sound of pounding on the gate grew increasingly fierce, and the four or five gatekeepers, trembling violently, said,

The gate was broken open by a man of matchless strength, and the bolt cracked and split with a loud sound. The four or five gatekeepers lost the use of their legs at this noise and sat trembling on the bare ground. A single man, pulling open the gate and entering, leisurely appeared on this scene together with three maidens who eclipsed the flowers, and looking at this state of affairs, said,

"Hey, what are you doing? That is upon the bare ground."

"There is no need for that. Stand up quickly and guide the way."

鎌彦『オイ執拗いぞ、いい加減に洒落て置け。そんな時ぢやなからう。大奥は今大騒動が始まつてゐる。さうして門には獅子とも虎とも狼ともわからぬやうな強い奴が、大勢の武士の出陣した後を狙つて攻めて来て居るのだ。前門には虎、後門には狼を受けて居る危急存亡のこの場合、喧嘩どころの騒ぎぢやなからう』

『ナンでもよいワイ。俺の奴さまになるか』

門は強力無双の男に押破られ、閂はめきめきめきと音して裂けた。四五の門番はこの物音に腰を抜かし、大地に坐つたまま慄へてゐる。門ひき開けて入り来る一人の男、花を欺く三人の娘と共に悠々としてこの場に現はれ、この体を見て、

『オイ、その方は何を致して居るか。其処は地の上だ』

一同『ハイ、畏まつて御迎へを致して居ります』

『それには及ばぬ。早く立つて案内いたせ』

§ 4

"Yes, in any case, the gatekeeper chiefs, Kasakō and Kamakō, have begun grappling with each other and will not let go, so we are at a loss. Unable to stand or sit, we have no choice but to settle our waists and torsos and remain calm and composed."

"Are you not trembling? Stand up quickly and guide us."

"If you insist that we stand, it is not that we will not stand. Please negotiate with Kasakō and Kamakō."

"What a strange fellow. Hey, gatekeepers called Kasa or Kama, what are you fighting over?"

Kasahiko: "Well, look who it is; if it isn't that fellow Teruhiko, who was brought to Tokoyo Castle in a wicker cage last winter! Hey, Kamakō, a terrible fellow has arrived. The quarrel is over for now. Shall we postpone it until tomorrow? You too are a fellow blessed with life. Shall we capture this fellow Teruhiko alive and send him to Tokoyo Castle?"

So saying, he released his neck.

Teruhiko, accompanied by three beautiful women, advanced leisurely toward the inner gate without minding anything. Seeing this situation, Kama and Kasa said,

"Hey, you fellows, he is heading for the inner gate! Seize him by the hair of his neck and pull him back!"

All: "We would like to pull him back very much, but our legs will not support us. Kasakō, Kamakō, if you have enough energy to quarrel, join together, sweep his legs from under him, knock him over, tie him up, and send him to Tokoyo Castle."

Kasahiko: "Hey, Kamakō, you were on the verge of losing your head. Considering yourself already dead, take a chance, chase after him quickly, and leap at him to catch him, won't you?"

Kamakō: "I feel somewhat unwell; shall I have a doctor examine me, and if the doctor utters that it is fine to go, go leap at him?"

"Is this the time to say such things? Do not play the fool! I will egg you on, so go, go! Even a dog, if egged on well, will leap at a wild boar five or ten times the size of its own body. If not egged on well, a dog is weak."

"Do not make a fool of me, daring to liken a person to a dog!"

"Didn't you always spout, as if a habitual phrase, that you would crush your bones and shatter your body for the God of Sunrise, or not spare the toil of a dog or a horse? Now is the time to render the toil of a dog or a horse. You stray cur, who only chatters noisily with your mouth and tucks your tail between your legs to withdraw at the crucial moment, go quickly! Sick him, sick him!"

Hearing the refreshing propagation song of men and women coming from inside the inner gate, all the gatekeepers grimaced, covered their ears with their hands, clung to the ground, and shrank back.

How is the state of the inner palace? It is precarious.
