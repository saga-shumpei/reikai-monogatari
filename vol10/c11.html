Chapter 11: Foxfire [441]

第11章　狐火〔441〕

§ 1

Departing the town of Kawata, at the rocky roots of the forest of Tokiwa, five deities of resolute heart, beginning with Uzuyamahiko, the propagator Odoyamatsumi of the roaring waves and blowing wind, Matsuyohime awaiting the reign of Miroku, Umegakahime, and Takenohime—here these five briskly crossed the high plains of Anau, by the Kotodo River where words are exchanged at the eastern foot of Mount Shira Pass, seeking to wash away the painful affliction of the Magatsukami dyed in gushing blood in the village of Yutsuishi, with hearts minded like wild geese, setting out from the beach of Karigane Bay toward the east over the waves, their advance is truly valiant.

On the Karigane Peninsula, which extended like a belt from north to south, the five propagators landed. The shadows of the propagators fell long upon the ground toward the east, like slender bamboo. Even the long spring day began to sink beyond Karigane Bay. Those walking upright and those lying flat as they advanced, a party of ten bearing the ten-star crest, bid a reluctant farewell with the setting of the sun.

Parting the mist-wrapped waves, the moon of the fourteenth night began to shine in the eastern sky. In the hazy moonlight of the spring night, neither fully shining nor completely overcast, the faint figures of five appeared once again with their pillows to the west. Kakako said:

"O Odoyama-sama! Idling along like this from the forest of Tokiwa, circling the foot of Mount Shira Pass, barely crossing the renowned Kotodo River, making temporary pillows on a bed of grass, and crossing the inlet of Karigane in this sunken floating world as a party of ten—ah, as the day darkened upon the world, we parted from the intimate five, yet no sooner had we felt lonely than we gained company of another five. Just as in the divine song, the world cannot be governed unless it is ten. Crossing far, far over seas and mountains, somehow or other we have arrived at this place in a state of sheer exhaustion. Odoyamatsumi the propagator of rough waves and wild heart, Matsuyohime of the wind blowing through pines—Miroku-sama has risen. Come, come, from now on, unless we thoroughly dispel the darkness of Tokoyo in the Land of Tokoyo where Kotodamahime resides, and cause Tokoyo-shin'o to repent, our mission will not be fulfilled. Even in the pitch-black night, the Mount Shira range appears in the moonlight; come, come, let us go now."

Odoyamatsumi said, "Excuse me, Uzuyamahiko-sama, we were supposed to have traveled as a party of five until now. Yet you said a party of ten. Do you always blow such preposterous boasts and throw us into confusion?"

Uzuyamahiko said, "Thanks to the Sun Deity, we are a party of ten; without the favor of the deities, we are merely five men and women. Just when we thought we had lost the favor of the Sun Deity, this time thanks to Miroku-sama, we are back to our original ten. People may call this a heartless floating world, yet it is a world where even an octopus can be caught. I hear that you too, at the bottom of a deep mountain valley, had an octopus caught by Teruhiko-no-kami."

"Do not ask me of that tale. Let us cross over this Shira Mountain Pass to the other side as quickly as possible and proceed to the land of Tokoyo. In truth, I had planned to cross the Anau Plateau and come out at the rear of Tokoyo Castle from the direction of Texas, but somehow my feet turned suddenly to the east; that I miraculously met the three princesses in the town of Kawata and then met you once more in the Forest of Tokiwa must be some manner of direction from the spirit world."

Just as he was speaking, the sounds of horses and men, numbering who knew how many hundreds, came echoing from ahead. Thinking it was yet another enemy attack, the five missionaries tightened their bellybands, immediately clasped their hands toward the moonlight, recited the Divine Words, and sang the propagation song in unison.

From among the crowd that gradually drew nearer, a figure who appeared to be a chieftain showed himself, saying:

"Hold! The five persons there must be the missionaries of the Ananaikyō. Beyond all doubt, they are the three women, Matsu, Take, and Ume. What utterly insolent wretches, who fled Tokoyo Castle under the cover of night, and have come again to this Karigane Peninsula to sing their propagation songs! By order of the Divine King of Tokoyo, I, Katatora, whose physical strength is harder than iron, have come to capture you. Well, will you surrender tamely to the bonds? If you say no, shall I run you through with the tip of this spear? What is your answer?"

Thus shouting, he advanced his horse and pressed close upon them.

The party gave no reply and remained standing in silence, whereupon the battle cries that suddenly arose on all sides gradually drew nearer to them. In the sky, several tens of heavenly bird-ships covered the heavens, raging wildly, and a campaign of intimidation was begun. Katatora said:

"Ha! Are you terrified by our great might, unable to utter even half a word, and trembling and quaking? If I, Katatora, give the signal at any moment, your five bodies will be blown to smithereens by the bombs dropped from the bird-ships in the sky. Rather than be blasted into dust and perish, as the saying goes that an inch prolonged may reach a fathom, you surely hold your lives dear even for the space of a single breath. Come, follow me without babbling on and on. Even if you chirp nonsense, it is utterly troublesome to me; I will strike your jaws, beat you down, and devour you whole. Do you not know the morning-sun-ascending might of Katatora of the land of Tokoyo?"

Thus he shouted, swaggering in mere bravado. Chizuhiko burst out laughing and said:

"Bwahahaha! Hey, Katatora, keep on prating, keep on blabbering! You wretches who are mere tails of the Eight-Forked Serpent nesting in the Rocky Mountains and Tokoyo Castle, just whom do you take us for? The world-renowned Lord Kakatotora of the Ananaikyō is none other than myself. All of you wretches, do not be terrified upon hearing this name. No matter how many bombs you drop from above, why should that be frightening? Give your signal as quickly as possible and have them drop the bombs! We are a company of five possessing Izunomitama, endowed with mysterious, miraculous divine power. Your side is nothing but a disorderly mob. Bombs dropped onto a crowded troop squirming about will be just the thing for us. This is truly what it means to destroy the enemy with the enemy's own weapons. Come, I myself will give the signal that you intended to use! You utter fool, binding yourself in your own ropes and opening the way to your own destruction!"

Saying so, Kagatora took out flint from his bosom, transferred the spark to the tinder, gathered dry leaves, and kindled fires in three places, whereupon Katatora said:

"Ah, this is terrible! How did they learn our signal? We shall be destroyed by our own weapons! Intolerable, intolerable! Hey, hey, all of you, extinguish that fire as quickly as possible!"

As the crowd went toward the bonfires to put them out, the three women—Matsu, Take, and Ume—stood tall beside the fires in the three places, handled the approaching fellows with ease, and threw them one by one into Karigane Bay.

Suddenly a roaring sound was heard, and as bombs burst over the heads of the concentrated troops, countless were wounded and killed while frothing at the mouth, some fled for their lives, and groaning voices of those collapsed on the spot were heard here and there. Uzuyamahiko said in a loud voice:

"Hear, hear, subordinates of Katatora, have you reformed? Terrified out of your wits, losing your legs, your noses broken, mouths torn open, eyeballs popping out, ears torn off, arms broken, legs torn away—you are truly most pitiable. If you reform right here and now, well and good; if you refuse, shall we make the signal once more?"

From among the crowd, squeezing out weeping voices:

"Lord Kagatora, three princesses, I shall reform. Please, help me."

"Those who have reformed, I will forgive their sins on the spot. Nothing in the world is as splendid as reforming. Come, everyone, follow us and sing the Propagating Song!"

All: "The Magatsu-no-kami hiding in the Tokoyo-no-kuni And the Rocky Mountains, Targeted by the Yamata-no-orochi To throw the Divine Land into disorder, The followers of Takatoriwake who is haughty, Whose hideous evil spirits lost their legs And had their noses crushed; Ye subordinates of Katatora, Who strut arrogantly, listen well! Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the Tokoyo-no-kuni should sink, Even if the fortresses of Magatsu are destroyed, Ananaikyō saves the world. Mouths torn open, noses bent, Eyeballs popping out, ears torn off, Arms broken, and legs pulled away, Your present state is just like A child's toy doll box thrown open. The time to reform is now!

The occasion to reform is this very one! With the sun and moon in the sky, Listen closely to the kotodama uttered by Kagatora; Purify your mouths, wash your eyes, Humble your proud noses, and chant at once The divine words praising The Kami of heaven and earth, all of you! Embark, embark, embark! All of you, embark upon the Ship of Salvation of the Sumegami! The one who appeared on the Rocky Mountains, Claiming to be Hinode-no-kami or Izanami-no-kami, Is none other than the incarnation Of the giant serpent and golden fox that throw the world into disorder! Awaken now, awaken! Blowing away the gathering clouds hanging over your hearts With my kotodama, Purifying and saving: this is the Divine Way. Though nations called nations are many, Though gods called gods are many, The Tokoyo-no-kuni shall endure eternally!"

The chest of the Kami cannot leave things in darkness; The evil plots of the Magakami of Mount Rocky Cannot be left as they are, Such is the divine heart that does not abandon them. Kamunaohi who created this world, And Ōnaohi of vast heart; In all things, in this human world, Look upon them anew with an upright spirit and speak anew. So he sang and danced.

Katatora and his subordinates, without realizing it, were swept up into the spirit-word chariot of Bunbuntora, and while knowing it was about themselves, they unconsciously sang, danced, and danced madly. Their eyes, noses, mouths, ears, hands, and feet were all saved by the divine grace, returned to their original, perfect physical bodies, and left not even a trace of injury, which was truly wondrous.

From this moment, Katatora was moved by the song of Uzuyamahiko, realized the truth completely, and, serving as a guide, advanced toward Mount Rocky. Later, Katatora received the divine name Katayamatsumi-no-Kami and came to perform great activities for the divine realm.
