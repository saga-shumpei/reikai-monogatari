Chapter 13: The Crab General [443]

第13章　蟹の将軍〔443〕

§ 1

As a guide for Odoyamadzumi-no-kami, Katatora climbed mountain paths, crossed valleys, and passed through byways to arrive at the foot of Mount Rocky, where a great host of demonic troops had prepared their arms and were in the very midst of departing for battle. Odoyamadzumi dispatched Katatora on reconnaissance toward the fortress of Mount Rocky, and just as Katatora was about to enter the castle gate, he ran right into Kanihiko.

Kanihiko said, "Oh, Katatora! Did you not march forth leading a vast army to capture the missionary in the Karigane Peninsula of the Land of Me? Since then, I had heard no news whatsoever and wondered what had become of you, but for you to appear here all alone must mean there are circumstances. Not returning to Tokoyo Castle, just what happened to the army you led?"

Katatora replied, "What could have happened? In battle, suffering few losses among one's allies is best. Rather than harming many warriors who know nothing, there is nothing better than luring a mere three or five missionaries to Tokoyo Castle by stratagem, so I brought forth my treasured wisdom. Just watch my work."

"An upstart gatekeeper like you should not brag so grandly!"

"We are both upstart gatekeepers. But gathering so many warriors so noisily, what does the God of Hinode intend to do?"

"It is troubling that you say such foolish things. Do you still not know? You are quite dim-witted. Upon the vital Yomi Island, which can be likened to the very eyes, nose, and throat of the Land of Tokoyo, Izanagi-no-kami has appeared from Mount Tenkyō, and to purify this ugly, filthy Land of Yomi and advance even into the Land of Tokoyo, he has led many divine generals possessing wisdom, benevolence, and courage, and has launched an assault toward Yomotsuhirasaka. If that happens, it would be as if the Land of Tokoyo had lost half its jaw, and its ruin would be right before our eyes; therefore, Izanami-no-Ōkami and the supreme commander, the God of Hinode, have gathered numerous warriors here, and are on the very verge of joining with the forces of Tokoyo Castle to march upon Yomotsuhirasaka. If you do not quickly lead your troops to take part in the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka, you will miss a once-in-a-thousand-years opportunity. Do not dally and leave regrets for posterity. In this moment of unmatched gravity, if you are to win distinction, now is the time!"

"A marital quarrel between kami is indeed an exaggerated affair. Why should a great host go to a marital quarrel that even a dog would not eat, as it is utterly absurd? Suppose one goes to battle and loses one's life. Tens of thousands of warriors, one and all, would have to part from their beloved wives and children. That so many must become widows merely by being used in a single marital quarrel—what an incomprehensible world."

"You are quite a simpleton. You ought to have had a faint inkling of the secrets of Mount Rocky and Tokoyo Castle, should you not? If you do not know, I shall tell you. The one who professes to be Izanami-no-Mikoto is, in truth, Ōkunihime-no-Mikoto. And the one professing to be the Hinode-no-Kami is her husband deity, Ōkunihiko-no-Mikoto. It will not do for Katatora not to understand even that."

"I hear this for the first time. Your story makes less and less sense to me. Who, then, is the Divine King of Tokoyo? Master Crab, surely you know."

"The Divine King of Tokoyo is Hirokuniwake. Claiming that he had died once, he deceived the general populace of the land of Tokoyo, and as a result of consultations with Ōkunihiko-no-Kami, Hirokuniwake has become the Divine King of Tokoyo. There are profound circumstances behind this. Kanihiko, having grasped the key to that secret, has deliberately acted as the gatekeeper until now at the private request of the Divine King of Tokoyo."

"Who, then, are you originally?"

"What a fool; do you still not understand? I have deliberately twisted my body to walk sideways and painted various juices on my face to disguise myself, but trace my origins and I am Takeshimahiko-no-Mikoto, who was a retainer in the Holy Land of Jerusalem. From now on, we shall stand at the forefront and head toward Yomotsu Hirasaka. However, the military secret cannot be leaked; say nothing of this to others. Nevertheless, the Great Deity Izanami of Mount Rocky is an utter counterfeit. It feels good for us to be employed by the real article, even if an enemy, but when I think that it is mere gilt hiding the base metal, I somehow feel as though I cannot put my full strength into it."

"You, who is going as commander this time?"

"It is a settled matter; it is this one,"

he said, pressing his own nose to show.

"A weak commander indeed. This next battle is utterly worthless. It cannot be helped, as the commander is so foolish after all."

"Do not speak foolishly. It is good for a commander to be a fool. If one has too much cleverness and acts fidgety, there is a danger of mistaking the greater situation; that is why this dim-witted Takeshimahiko is charged with the duty of commanding the entire army. Even so, I am the general of the three armies. Do not make too much of a fool of me. But Katatora, where have you placed the five missionaries? Izanami-no-Mikoto... hold on, Ōkunihime-no-Mikoto commanded that there is no chance of winning this battle without the three peach fruits of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum. If the three are in your hands, present them quickly, achieve distinguished renown, and be praised as a person of great merit."

"All right, I shall show you now."

"There is no need to show me; show it quickly to the fake Ōkami Izanami. Hey, Naruikazuchi, Wakaikazuchi, come quickly!"

Thus, mounting his horse and blowing his conch horn vigorously, he advanced while spraying crab-like froth from the corners of his mouth with buzzing sounds.

Listening to this unfeigned tale of Kanihiko with a pounding heart, Katatora reported everything to Odotsumitsumi, whereupon Odotsumitsumi let out a deep breath,

"Ah, is that so? That it cannot be doubted is divine possession. To tell the truth, it is shameful that I doubted Kakatora's divine possession until now. Saniwa cannot easily be performed by blind ones such as ourselves. Nevertheless, when thinking of this, one cannot help but admire the wondrous, miraculous power of Uduyamahiko. For now, let us hide ourselves for a while and observe the situation."

Thus, the two hid themselves in the dense forest and awaited the right moment. After Kanihiko, Takeshimahiko, led a unit and set out for battle in an imposing manner that cleared the surroundings, the sound of conch horns and the reverberation of drums arose once more; wondering what this might be and gazing through the trees, Katatora said in an eccentric voice,

"Look, a second unit is marching out as well. I wonder who the general of the second unit is."

Odotsumitsumi said, "It is troublesome, but could you approach near the enemy, investigate the situation, and report back?"

"I obey."

No sooner had he spoken than Katatora, like a monkey moving along tree branches, crept closer to the enemy front. Miyamawake stood at the head of the battle array, swinging his commander's baton,

"Advance, advance!"

commanding thus. The vice-commanders on the left and right were the fierce generals Tsuchiikazuchi and Fusuikazuchi. Kunitamahime, Taitohime, and Kitsukihime, disguised as the three of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum who surpass flowers in beauty, rode upon horses as the Peach Fruit Unit, shining brightly as their splendid garments caught the sun while advancing. The numerous troops marched out with synchronized footsteps and various weapons in an ostentatious display. Katatora immediately turned back and reported the full details to Odotsumitsumi.

"Well, thank you for your trouble. Are those all the soldiers of the Rocky Mountains?"

"No, it is merely a fraction. Depending on necessity, they may send out even more."

"Indeed, the scheme of these cunning deities is not to be underestimated; we too must devise a plan."

At that moment, the voice of the propagation song echoed in the mountain valleys, and the two involuntarily strained their ears to listen to that voice. Presently, the southeast wind blew violently and noisy sounds arose, and the propagation song was engulfed by the sound of the wind.

と、樹木茂れる森林の中に両人は姿を隠し時を待ちゐる。蟹彦の竹島彦が一隊を引率し、威風凛々として四辺を払ひ出陣した後に、又もや法螺貝の音、太鼓の響、ハテ訝かしやと木の間を透して打眺め、固虎は頓狂な声にて、

『ヤア、また第二隊が出て行き居るぞ。第二隊の大将は誰だか知らむ』

淤縢山津見『御苦労だが、敵近く寄つて様子を査べ報告して呉れないか』

『畏まりました』

といふより早く固虎は、猿が梢を伝ふが如く、しのびしのび敵前近く進み行く。美山別は陣頭に立ち采配を打揮ひながら、

『進め進め』

と号令してゐる。左右の副将は土雷、伏雷の猛将である。花を欺く松、竹、梅の三人に扮したる国玉姫、田糸姫、杵築姫は馬上に跨りながら、桃の実隊として美々しき衣裳を太陽に照されながら、ピカリピカリと進んで来る。数多の軍勢は足音を揃へて、種々の武器を携へ繰出す仰々しさ。固虎は直様引返し、淤縢山津見に詳細の顛末を報告したり。

『ヤア、御苦労ご苦労、ロッキー山の軍人はあれでしまひか』

『ナニ、ほんの一部分です。必要に応じて未だ未だ出すかも知れませぬ』

『ウン、油断のならぬ醜神の仕組、吾々も一つ考へねばならぬワイ』

このとき木霊に響く宣伝歌の声、二人は思はず其の声に聞耳澄ました。忽ち東南の風吹き荒んで音騒がしく、宣伝歌は風の音に包まれにける。
