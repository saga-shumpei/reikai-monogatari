Chapter 24: Reconciliation through Peaceful Words [454]

第24章　言向和〔454〕

§ 1

Dividing good and evil, At the great pass of the distant age of the gods, In the battle of the island of Yomi, Miyamawake, thoroughly weakened, And the subordinates of Kunitamahime, Tormented by the allied army Of Hinode-no-kami where the morning sun shines, Reduced to a wretched plight By the precious power of the kotodama roaring through heaven and earth, Just as they were writhing in agony, Making the sky blocked by black clouds resound, The camphor-wood boat soared down, Descending moment by moment toward the earth; To falsely claim Ōkunihime to be Kamuisanami-no-Ōkami, Deceiving both friend and foe, And thereby accomplish the wicked deed long plotted, How wretched and shameful it is.

The evil deities of shikome and ama-no-sagu-me, whose souls were extracted, spirits crushed, and loins paralyzed by the kotodama of the divine army, writhed in agony like carp intoxicated by mud or shōjō drunk on poisoned sake, their bones and muscles turned soft as konjac. Thereupon, hearing of the departure for the front of Kamuisanami-no-Kami, the commander-in-chief of Tokoyo-no-Kuni, they rallied their spirits once again; starting with the eight kinds of thunder gods, the demonic army of hundreds of thousands and millions raised a great war cry all at once, advancing up toward the slope of Yomotsuhirasaka like splitting bamboo.

With cries of "Uro, uro" so loud that heaven and earth shook, black clouds in the heavenly sky grew ever thicker, thunderclaps roared, the earth trembled, and tidal waves surged even to the midst of the mountain; amid this cruel spectacle of unknown depths—be it the land of Yomi or the land of Ne—Miyamawake and Kunitamahime,

"The rise and fall of Tokoyo-no-Kuni depends on this single action,"

encouraging the subordinate demonic forces,

"Advance! Advance!"

gave the command, whereupon the reckless demonic army, with the shikome and ama-no-sagu-me at their head, straying into the darkness of their minds, fought with all their might.

The reverberation of bombs, the sound of artillery, and the whistling of passing arrows, uniting with the roar of the violent gale, grew increasingly terrible.

At this time, Hinode-no-kami stood atop the slope of Hirasaka, facing the tens of thousands of demonic troops surging up, and said:

"Kamuisanagi-no-Ōkami and Kamuisanami-no-Ōkami, grant Thy protection. Confound the Yomotsu deities coming estranged and wild from Tokoyo-no-Kuni and Ōkunihime's Izanami-no-Mikoto, sweep away the crookedness of their hearts, and illuminate their evil hearts with the voice of the auspicious words of the Great Imperial Deities. O deities of one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand! Now is the time to illuminate heaven and earth with the single torch of Hinode-no-kami. Grant Thy permission!"

No sooner had he spoken than his figure vanished, turning into a gigantic fireball, which roared above the heads of the demonic forces and flew about in all directions. Many of the demonic troops, dazzled by the divine light, suffered headaches from the roaring of the torch, their ears went numb, and their bodies instantly stiffened like fossils; thus tens of thousands of standing statues appeared, numerous as the sands of the earth.

現代日本語訳

善と悪とを峻別する、 遠き神代の大峠である、 黄泉の島の戦いにおいて 弱りきってしまった美山別や、 国玉姫の部下たちは、 朝日が輝く日の出神の 味方の軍勢に苦しめられ、 天地に轟く言霊の 貴い力によってほうほうの体となり、 もだえ苦しんでいる折から、 黒雲が塞ぐ大空を 轟かせながら舞い降りる 磐樟船が刻々と 地上に向かって降りて来る。 大国姫を神伊邪那美大神であると 敵や味方を偽って、 平素から企んでいた邪悪な業を 成し遂げようとすることは浅ましいことである。

神軍の言霊に魂を抜かれ、胆を挫かれ、腰を抜かした醜女や探女の悪神らは、泥に酔った鮒の如く、毒酒に酔った猩々の如く、骨も筋も蒟蒻のように悶え苦しむそこへ、常世の国の総大将である神伊邪那美神のご出陣と聞いて、再び元気を盛り返し、八種の雷神をはじめとして、百千万の魔軍は一度にどっと鬨の声をあげて、黄泉比良坂を目指して破竹の勢いで攻め登る。

「ウロー、ウロー」という叫び声は天地も震撼するばかりで、天津御空は黒雲がますます濃厚となり、雷霆が鳴り轟き、大地は震動し、津波は山の中央まで押し寄せてきて、黄泉の国か、根の国か、底の知れない無残な光景に、美山別と国玉姫は、

「常世の国の興亡はこの一挙にある」

と、部下の魔軍を励まして、

「進め、進め」

と指図すると、命知らずの魔軍は、醜女や探女を先頭に、心の闇に迷いながら、力の限り戦った。

爆弾の響き、砲の音、矢の行き交う音は、暴風の声と相まってますます凄まじくなってきた。

この時、日の出神は比良坂の坂の上に立って、攻め登ってくる数万の魔軍に向かい、

「神伊邪那岐大神、神伊邪那美大神、お守りください。常世の国からうとみ荒んでやって来る黄泉神、大国姫の伊邪那美命に一泡吹かせ、心の曲がりを払い去り、皇大神の神嘉言の声によって邪悪な心を照らさせ給え。一、二、三、四、五、六、七、八、九、十、百、千、万の神々よ、日の出神の一つの炬を天地に照らすのは今この時である。お許しください」

と言うより早く、姿は消えて巨大な大火球と変わり、魔軍の頭上に向かって唸りを上げて、前後左右に飛び回ったので、あまたの魔軍は神の光に照らされて目がくらみ、炬の唸りに頭が痛み、耳が痺れ、身体はたちまち強直して化石のようになり、幾万の立像が大地の砂の数の如くに現れた。

原文（近代文語）

善と悪とを立別る 遠き神代の大峠 黄泉の島の戦ひに 弱りきつたる美山別 国玉姫の部下たちは 朝日輝く日の出神の 味方の軍に艱まされ 天地に轟く言霊の 貴の力に這々の体 悶え苦しむ折からに 黒雲塞がる大空を 轟かしつつ舞ひ降る 磐樟船の刻々に 地上に向つて降り来る 大国姫を神伊邪那美大神と 敵や味方を偽りて 日頃企みし枉業を 遂げむとするぞ浅ましき。

此の時日の出神は比良坂の坂の上に立ちて、攻め登り来る数万の魔軍に向ひ、

『神伊邪那岐大神、神伊邪那美大神、守らせ給へ。常世の国より疎び荒び来る黄泉神、大国姫の伊邪那美命に一泡吹かせ、心の曲を払ひ去り、皇大神の神嘉言の声に邪の心を照させ給へ。一二三四五六七八九十百千万の神等よ、日の出神の一つ炬を、天地に照すは今この時ぞ。許させ給へ』

と云ふより早く、姿は消えて巨大なる大火球と変じ、魔軍の頭上に向つて唸りを立て、前後左右に飛び廻るにぞ、数多の魔軍は、神光に照されて眼眩み、炬の唸りに頭痛み、耳痺れ、身体忽ち強直して化石の如く、幾万の立像は大地の砂の数の如くに現はれける。

§ 2

Masakayamatsumi raised his clear voice,

"The deity appears on the surface, Dividing good and evil; Kamunaohi, who created this world, And Ōnaohi of vast heart. In all things of the human world, Look again and hear again with direct correctness; Declare and right the errors of yourself. The island of Yomi is the great pass Separating the paths of good and evil; Where the deities confer across, It is the world's great pass Fixing the path of sincerity for thousands of ages. Even demons, giant serpents, and Magatsumi Are mollified through words at Kototoi-iwa. The Chibiki-no-iwa rock blocking above this slope Is the eightfold fence dividing the divine world From the crooked and evil world. The eightfold fence of Izumo, enclosing the spouse, In the land of the deities where an eightfold fence is made; Truly, claiming to be Izanami-no-Ōkami,

The crooked deity who comes deceiving, Ōkunihime, Kunihime! Thy destiny is to pacify through words All the Yomotsushikome of the hidden world; Protect the myriad souls Who abandon the present world and return. Go forth unto the land of Yomi, And pacifying the thousands and myriad souls That fall each day, Guard well the iron gate of Yomi Lest they come to the visible world. By the virtue of generation and development of Kami-Izanagi-no-Ōkami, Manifesting as Hinode-no-Kami, Breed and multiply fifteen hundred mortal grasses And the myriad peoples each day, Like the stars in the great sky, And open the divine nation. May the two pillars, Nagi and Nami, Harmonize their breathing with each other, And giving birth to the eighty lands, eighty islands, The green human grasses, and all living things, Erect the divine world firmly and eternally, the land of Tokoyo. Forsaking the Rocky Mountains, Calm thy heart, and appear As the deity of Yomi in the hidden realm, Appear as the deity of Yomi!"

Moved perhaps by this song, Ōkunihime appeared before the Chibiki-no-iwa rock,

"I, the divine administrator of Tokoyo, Falsely claiming to be Kami-Izanami-no-Ōkami, Deceived myriad deities and men, Doing crooked deeds day and night, Clouding and troubling my heart, And spent wrongful days; Yet illuminated by the pure heart Of Hinode-no-Kami and the various deities, Whose divine majesty shines brightly, A single torch has shone within my breast. In the sky of my heart, where it shines across, The beautiful, true sun and moon have appeared. Ah, Kami-Izanami-no-Ōkami, who solidified heaven and earth! I will conceal myself in Yomi, And guiding all the ugly, crooked spirits, the base souls, The shikome and the sagume, Unto the divine teachings, I shall have their spirits washed and purified,

Causing them to be born again alive As pillars of the deities in the visible world. While dwelling in the beautiful divine world, The base deities who plot crooked deeds I shall receive by a thousand a day, Take them to the bottommost land, And inflicting upon them a hundred torments, I shall cleanse their filthy souls. Ah, Sovereign Deities, Sovereign Deities! In the land of Yomi of eternal darkness, Shine upon the darkness, and let the sun and moon Shine without leaving any corner, Even unto the bottomless land of roots And unto the depths below! Where the morning sun shines And the evening sun radiates, O Hinode-no-Kami of the single torch, farewell! O myriad deities, farewell!"

Singing thus, she manifested the sincerity of her repentance, and awesomely vowed to become Yomotsu-no-Ōkami and govern the affairs of the hidden realm.

Hereupon, by the divine word of Izanagi-no-Kami, Hinode-no-Kami and the other deities, commanders, and troops, without staining their blades with blood, mollified the army of Yomi through the power of kotodama, and triumphantly returned toward Mount Tenkyō under divine protection.

Many Magatsukami repented: some returned to the good path while still alive, some repented as spirits, and not a few deities fell into the bottommost land, received the admonition of Yomotsu-no-Ōkami, suffered for long months and years, reformed their hearts, purified their spirits, and were born into the visible realm to participate in divine work—such was the divine word.

と歌って改心の誠の心を表し、黄泉の大神となって幽界の政事を治めることを誓われたことは畏れ多いことである。

ここに伊邪那岐神の神の言葉によって、日の出神その他の諸神および将卒は、刃を血塗ることなく、言霊の威力によって黄泉の軍勢を言向け和らげ、神の守護のもとに天教山に向かって凱旋した。

数多くの曲津神は悔い改めて、生きたまま善き道に立ち返る者もあり、霊魂となって悔い改める者もあり、あるいは根底の国に落ちて行って黄泉大神の戒めを受け、長年月のあいだ苦しんで、その心を改め霊魂を清め、現界に向かって生まれ来て、神業に参加する神々も少なくない、という神の言葉であった。
