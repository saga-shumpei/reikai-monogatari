Chapter 34: The Sacred Coffer [464]

第34章　唐櫃〔464〕

§ 1

In a world where the Magatsu rage, Such an instance has occurred: Building a nest in the mountain fortress of Altai, Be it ogre or giant serpent, the evil deity Hebitsukami Catches and devours twelve snakes a day; Though spring, summer, and autumn are well, In the winter nights when snow falls incessantly, The forms of the snakes also hide, Leaving no means to stave off hunger; The evil deity at last appeared In villages, towns, and hamlets near and far, Devouring one wife or young maiden of the world each day; Not spared from the sorrow of women decreasing day by day, The white-feathered arrow has fallen upon Kiyohime, The only daughter of Tetsuhiko, the head of the village of Tetsutani.

Within the gate sunken in grief, on a dark night when the blowing wind was damp, the resolute propagandist Ishikoridome, having knocked on the gate door and entered, appeared at the entrance accompanied by five natives and guided by the gatekeeper Tokikō; upon seeing this, Tokikō hurriedly rushed into the inner room,

"I beg to report, I beg to report. The propagandist of the Ananai Teachings has arrived."

The master Tetsuhiko sat before the Chinese chest with his wife Tetsu-hime and Kiyohime, sunken in grief as though the gatekeeper's words did not reach his ears.

In the adjoining room, the sorrowful whispering voices of the villagers could be heard. Tokikō said,

"Excuse me, the propagandist of the Ananai Teachings has arrived."

Tetsuhiko: "What! A propagandist of the Ananai Teachings—that is dreadful. Shut the gate tightly; they must not take a single step inside!"

Tokikō,

"Yes,"

said no more than that, scratching his head vigorously and shrinking back.

"Go quickly and shut the gate, will you not? If by any chance the propagandist of the Ananai Teachings should step even a single pace into my estate, there is no telling what impossible demands might again be thrust upon us by the god Hebitsukami, retainer of Uralhiko. Shut the gate at once!"

"J-Just now, r-right here, the propagandist forced his way in by shoving me down, kicking me down, knocking me aside, and moreover striking me with his fists. Ah, he is a violent fellow, and even with my peerless, great strength, there was nothing I could do. Since good things are sure to follow bad, please reconsider and listen anew."

"Ho, you are with the Ananai Teachings, are you? When did you hear such teachings?"

"Yes, I just heard them at the gate entrance."

At this moment, at the entrance,

"The deity appears on the surface To distinguish good from evil; O Hebitsukami, serving as a subordinate To the evil god Uralhiko Who builds his nest in the Ural Mountains, Now that the propagandist Of the Ananai Teachings has appeared, This is the fateful hour with no escape; By the kotodama of divine teachings, Crushing the head of the evil god Who devours people, clutching it firmly, Subduing and pacifying it by words, Blowing cleanly away the gathering clouds And distresses that block the village of Tetsutani, It shall be governed as the peaceful, divine land Of the peaceful realm; Ah, Tetsuhiko, O Tetsu-hime, O Kiyohime pure in body and spirit, Do not fret or worry; The Kaminaobi who created this world..."

「申し上げます、申し上げます。三五教の宣伝使がお越しになりました」

主人の鉄彦は、妻の鉄姫、清姫とともに唐櫃の前に座り、門番の言葉も耳に入らない様子で憂いに沈んでいる。

隣の部屋には、村人たちが口々に囁き合う声が悲しげに聞こえている。時公は、

「もしもし、三五教の宣伝使がお見えになりました」

「早く行って門を閉めないか。万が一にも、三五教の宣伝使をわが屋敷へ一歩たりとも踏み込ませたならば、またしてもウラル彦の眷属である蛇掴の神にどんな難題を吹きかけられるかも測りがたい。早く門を閉ざせ」

「い、今、こ、ここへ宣伝使が無理やりに私を突き倒し、蹴り倒し、撥ね飛ばし、その上拳骨を食らわせて入って来ました。いやもう乱暴なやつで、力の強い剛力無双の私でも、どうすることもできません。悪いことの後にはきっと良いことが来ますから、どうぞ考え直し、聞き直してくださいませ」

「ほう、貴様は三五教だな、いつそんな教えを聞いたのか」

「はい、今、門口で聞きました」

この時、玄関において、

「神が表に現れて 善と悪とを立て分ける ウラルの山に巣を構える ウラル彦という曲神の 部下に仕える蛇掴よ 三五教の宣伝使が 現れ来た上からは もはや逃れられぬ観念の時だ 神の教えの言霊によって 人を奪い食らう曲神の 頭を打ち砕いて鷲掴みにし 言向け和らげて鉄谷の 里をふさぐ群雲や 悩みを清く吹き払い 浦安国の心安らかな 神の御国へと治めるであろう ああ、鉄彦よ、鉄姫よ 身魂も清らかな清姫よ 心配し思い煩うことなかれ この世を造られた神直日」

§ 2

The great Ōnaohi with a spacious heart; In the human world, whatever may be, Looking afresh and hearing afresh in righteousness, Correcting by proclamation the perversions of the world; The divine way of Ananai-kyō. Though the fury of ogres, serpents, and wolves Be ever so fierce, Subdued and harmonized By the spirit of speech through the divine majesty, The innermost heart clears, like the winter sky, As the moon shines across the night of the full moon, With a clear sky for Kiyohime, Within this night, without mishap, Her life shall be saved, and driving and scattering The crooked spirits to the ridges of the Altai Mountains, One must purify in the shallows of the Uchi River; One must purify in the shallows of the Uchi River; Dispel your sorrows, dispel them quickly, Rejoice and take heart together!

Though the morning sun shines or is clouded, Though the moon waxes or wanes, Even though heaven and earth should overturn, Ananai-kyō will save the world; Ah, how grateful is the way of the Kami! Ah, how grateful is the way of the Kami!"

Such was the clear voice of the propagation song. The villagers who had gathered in another room, beginning with Tetsuhiko and his child, hearing this song, completely changed from before with complexions as if revived, unconsciously clapped their hands, and stood up, dancing wildly while shouting, "Uro, uro!"

At this time, there was again a woman who passed through the gate and entered, singing a propagation song in a gentle voice. Stopping at the entrance,

"Following a single light that weaves through the darkness, When I come here and look, The iron gate of the chieftain of Tetsudani Village Is opened smoothly; Within the house enveloped in sorrow, Wondering what the situation might be, Tilting my head and straining my ears, It is dark, dark as the darkness of pitch-black night, To which are compared Tetsuhiko, His wife Tetsuhime, and Kiyokohime; This night, when she becomes the vile prey Of the crooked spirits of the Altai Mountains, My feelings are the same womanly heart, And guessing the maiden's heart, I, who open the teachings of Ananai That save the people of the world, Will serve here tonight The teachings of the Kami of Mikaheru; The heart that thinks thus is deeply determined, Deeper than the valleys of the Altai,

Even deeper than the Uchi River Has this propagation messenger resolved her heart; Tonight, carry me to the cave door, Go, go quickly! The Kami appears openly And separates good and evil; Now is this time, this is the time! If the time is lost, it will be a grave matter; Quickly open this gate! Quickly open this gate!"

Ishikoridome-no-Kami, hearing the unexpected voice of the propagation messenger, wondered and stepped out to the entrance to look, and it was Umegakahime, who had achieved great distinction in the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka. The two unconsciously looked at each other's faces, and mutually,

"Oh!"

saying only this, they stood silently for a while, astonished at this unexpected encounter.
