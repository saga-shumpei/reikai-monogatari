Chapter 14: The Murmur of Wind in the Pines [444]

第14章　松風の音〔444〕

§ 1

At the sound of the propagation hymn approaching on the wind, looking ahead, Uzuyamahiko, along with the three—Matsu, Take, and Ume—from whom they had parted on the mountaintop, came advancing leisurely in this direction.

Odoyamazumi said, "Oh, we have met in a strange place. Something terrible has happened."

Uzuyamahiko said, "What do you mean by 'something terrible'?"

"Just a little while ago, you must have heard the sound of the conch and the beating of the drums."

"Ah, that? That is the enemy marching toward Yomotsuhirasaka. Interesting things have begun. Our walking about propagating in this manner was preparation to take the field in the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka. Ah, it has become interesting!"

"Uzuyama-sama, this is no time to call it interesting. Matsu, Take, and Ume must serve the purpose of the peach fruits as soon as possible. Until now I thought to act before the enemy occupied Yomotsuhirasaka, but because of the enemy army's too sudden departure for the front, the opportunity has been lost. Ah, what should be done?"

"How could there be any question of what to do? The three propagators, Matsu, Take, and Ume, already crossed over to Yomishima last year."

"How could there be such a ridiculous thing? Here in reality are the three propagators, Matsu, Take, and Ume, are they not?"

"Look at this mountain. There surely is no rule that only a single pine grows in the world. The same is true for bamboo and plum. These three are actually apparitions."

As soon as the words ended, the figures of the three maidens turned into smoke and vanished.

"My, Uzuyama-sama, you are quite strange, are you not?"

"I may be strange, but did you only just notice it? You have quite a dim-witted discernment."

"How foul-mouthed you are! Yet you say 'renew, renew,' so it is a strange thing indeed. It is just as if I have been bewitched by a fox."

At this time, the tremendous sound of men and horses was heard once again. Looking closely, the two men, Takatoriwake, whose nose was flattened, and Teruyamahiko, were straddling swift steeds while their martial sleeves glittered in the sunlight, waving their batons and commanding, 'Advance, advance!'

This time it was a considerably large force; among the commanders, Ōikazuchi, Kuroikazuchi, Honoikazuchi, and Sakuikazuchi each led their own units, their banners bearing the crest of the hollyhock on a white ground fluttering in the spring wind, presenting a valiant sight as they marched grandly with banners and drums.

Odoyamazumi said, "Having sent forth an army of such size, is the camp behind them empty, I wonder?"

淤縢山津見「おや、妙な所で出逢いました。大変なことになりましたよ」

珍山彦「大変とは何ですか」

「つい先ほど、法螺貝の声や鼓の音が聞こえたでしょう」

「ああ、あれですか。あれは敵が黄泉比良坂に進軍しているのですよ。面白いことが始まってきました。我々がこのように宣伝に歩いたのも、黄泉比良坂の戦いに出陣するための準備でした。いやあ、面白くなってきましたね」

「珍山さま、面白いどころではありませんよ。一刻も早く、松、竹、梅は桃の実の御用に立たねばなりません。敵に黄泉比良坂を占領されないうちにと今まで思っていましたが、あまりに急な敵軍の出陣で時機を失してしまいました。ああ、どうしたらよいでしょうか」

「どうもこうもあるものですか。松、竹、梅の三人の宣伝使は、去年のうちに黄泉島に渡っておられますよ」

「そんな馬鹿なことがありますか。ここに現に三人、松、竹、梅の宣伝使がおられるではないですか」

「この山をご覧なさい。松は世界に一本しか生えないという決まりはないはずです。竹も梅もその通りです。この三人は実は化物ですよ」

言葉が終わるとともに、三人の娘の姿は煙となって消え失せてしまった。

「おやまあ、珍山さま、あなたはずいぶんと変わっていますね」

「変わっているでしょうが、今気がつきましたか。ずいぶんと鈍い鑑識眼ですね」

「あなたの口の悪いこと。それでも宣り直し宣り直しとおっしゃるのだから、妙なものです。まるで狐に化かされたようだ」

この時、またしても人馬の物音がすさまじく聞こえて来る。見ると、鼻のつぶれた鷹取別と照山彦の両人は、軍服の袖に日光をきらきらと浴びながら駿馬にまたがり、采配を振って進め進めと指図している。

今度はかなりの大部隊で、武将には、大雷、黒雷、火雷、拆雷が各自部隊を率い、白地に葵の紋所の旗を春風になびかせながら、旗や太鼓も堂々と進み行く勇ましい光景である。

淤縢山津見「あれだけの軍勢を繰り出してしまったら、背後の陣営は空っぽでしょうか」

淤縢山津見『あれだけの軍勢を繰り出して了つたら、後の陣営は空虚でせうか』

§ 2

Uduyamahiko: "In form it is empty. Instead, countless hundreds, thousands, and tens of millions of evil deities—shikome and sagume—fill the inside and outside of the castle. In the end, that demonic army will attack and advance upon Hirasaka."

No sooner had he spoken than the figure of Uduyamahiko again turned into smoke and vanished.

Odoyamadzumi: "Ah, what is this, a strange thing indeed. Hey, hey, Lord Katatora, will you not also turn into smoke and vanish?"

Katatora: "There are only such exceedingly noble deities that I feel ashamed and wish I could disappear, yet by no means can I vanish."

"Ah, it cannot be helped. Shall I now disguise myself and enter the castle of the Rocky Mountains to scout the situation? Going to Yomotsu-hirasaka now would be too late. Oh, Lord Katatora, taking advantage of the fact that you have been a retainer of Tokoyo Castle until now, will you not take me and guide me into the castle?"

"That is an easy thing, yet there is hardly anyone who does not know the name of Odoyamadzumi. Moreover, Ōkunihime no Mikoto knows your original identity and your face. It is perilous to enter thoughtlessly."

"Is that so? However, by dallying too much, I have arrived too late for the divine work of Yomotsu-hirasaka. That is why I must establish contact with the main camp of the Great Deity. Merely because I took independent action, has my plan become a day late and a dollar short? Ah, how regrettable, how vexing! How can I recover from this failure?"

While choking with tears of mortification, he clapped his hands, recited the divine words, and powerlessly began to sing the propagation song, at which very moment a missionary suddenly appeared. Odoyamadzumi said,

"Oh, you are Teruhiko and Lord Toyamadzumi. Ah, what a regrettable thing we both have done. It is vexing to have arrived too late to participate in the once-in-a-thousand-years battle of Hirasaka. Already the evil deities of the Rocky Mountains have departed for Yomishima in great numbers. What should we do?"

Teruhiko: "Nay, there is no need to worry particularly. According to the divine plan of the spirit world, there is a need to detain you here. Fortunately, taking Lord Katatora as your guide, it is necessary to advance deep into the Rocky Mountains and scout the state of the false deity of Izanami. Arriving late is what is called the watertight scheme of the deities. Setting forth to battle is not the only divine work. Come, now let you two proceed to Rocky Castle. We shall slip into Tokoyo Castle and investigate all their plans. Farewell."

No sooner had he spoken than the figures vanished into white smoke, and only the sound of the wind blowing through the pines was heard.

Ah, in what sort of divine work will these three participate?

と悔し涙に咽びながら、両手を打って神言を奏上し、力なく宣伝歌を歌い始めるときしも、忽然として現れ出た宣伝使があった。淤縢山津見は、

『おお、貴方は照彦、戸山津見様、ええお互いに残念なことをいたしましたなあ。千載一遇の比良坂の戦いに参加し遅れたのは口惜しい。もはやロッキー山の魔神らは大挙して黄泉島へ出陣してしまった。どうしたらよかろうか』

照彦『いや、別に心配はいりません。神界の御経綸によって、貴方をここに留め置く必要があるのですよ。幸い、固虎さまを案内者として、ロッキー山深く進み入り、伊弉冊の贋神の様子を探る必要がある。遅れたのはいわゆる水も漏らさぬ神の仕組みだ。戦に出陣することのみが神業ではない。さあ、これからご両人はロッキー城にお進みください。我々は常世城に忍び入り、一切の計画を調査いたします。さようなら』

と言うかと見れば姿は消えて白煙となり、松を吹く風の音のみが聞こえる。

ああ、この三人は如何なる神業に参加するのであろうか。
