Chapter 23: The Loving Mercy of God [453]

第23章　神の慈愛〔453〕

§ 1

Just as Ōkunihime no Mikoto, together with Taketorawake, had her wits taken away by the strange spectacle of this place and stood in a daze without a word, Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko, who called himself the Sun-Rising Deity, appeared at this place together with four or five attendants, and said,

"Ah, a terrible thing has occurred at Rocky Castle. Tokoyo Shin'ō of Tokoyo Castle has plotted rebellion leading a multitude of troops, and though on our side we have had Otoyamatsumi and Katatorahiko confront them, ultimate victory is uncertain. You, Ōkunihime, secretly cross over to Yomishima from now on, march out calling yourself Izanami no Mikoto, inspire the morale of our allies, win a great victory, and drive away the divine army. If you do so, no matter how fiercely the forces of Hirokuniwake attack, they will fear your martial majesty and immediately surrender. To shut ourselves up in the main castle and be quietly destroyed by Hirokuniwake is not a wise plan. I shall remain in the main castle from now on and ambush the approaching enemy. You must head for Yomishima as quickly as possible."

Ōkunihime said, "I have understood the particulars. However, in this castle where strange occurrences are many, please exercise the utmost caution."

Leaving these words behind, she boarded the Heavenly Rock-Boat and, roaring through the sky, hurried onward toward Yomishima together with four or five follower soldiers.

At this time, it grew noisy outside the gate once again, and Otoyamatsumi rushed in flustered together with Katayamahiko, saying,

"We report to the Sun-Rising Deity. At Rocky Castle, swords have already broken and arrows are exhausted, and it has finally fallen under enemy occupation."

The Sun-Rising Deity said, "Bah, what spineless wretches! I shall now advance against the army of Hirokuniwake and decide victory or defeat. Otoyamatsumi, Katatora, follow me!"

Saying this, he mounted a swift horse, led a small number of soldiers with an imposing air, left the Rocky Mountain Castle behind, and galloped toward Rocky Castle.

Reaching Rocky Castle and looking, the front gate was wide open, and there was not a single enemy soldier, nor even a shadow of an ally. While suspecting something strange, the false Sun-Rising Deity led his officers and soldiers, advancing deep inside while paying attention in all directions. As he looked, the voices of foxes rose from all directions:

"Foxes and phantoms, strange and bizarre!"

All was silent, and there was not even a human shadow.

The Sun-Rising Deity said, "Those cries just now make no sense. Aiya, Otoyamatsumi, Katatorahiko, search without leaving a single corner unchecked!"

Otoyamatsumi said, "Oho, I am none other than a missionary of the Ananai Teachings! That I claimed to be your ally until now was in order to chastise your treacherous villainy. Now, since it has come to this, will you surrender honorably?"

"Ei!"

Katayamahiko: "You are the ringleader of evil demons and rakshasas who falsely claimed the name of Hinode-no-kami, holed yourself up in Rocky Castle, and sought to destroy the plan of the divine realm from its foundation. Now that it has come to this, whether you hide or flee, your strength will no longer avail. Prepare yourself!"

Hinode-no-kami: "Ah, exceedingly regrettable! Ōkunihime has marched toward Yomishima, and my subordinate brave generals and fierce soldiers have either set out for battle or fled, so that now I am all alone with no means left. Come, if you will strike me down, strike; if you will kill me, kill!"

Odotsumatsumi: "Hold, false Hinode-no-kami, listen well. The deities of heaven and earth take love as their heart. What we humans can by no means control are air, water, and death. Dying and living are both the divine will. What efficacy could there be in vainly taking a life such as yours? Even if your fleshly body were to die, it is plain to see that your spirit would once again become an evil demon, rampage throughout the realm, and commit wickedness. I am not one who considers taking your life sufficient. I merely desire to cause the evil spirit entrenched within your soul to repent, or to depart, and to achieve the reality of reforming errors and turning to good. Sango-kyō is not, as you claim, something that conquers people with weapons or plunders other lands. Wash your ears well and listen to the teachings of the God of supreme benevolence and love."

"Oh, I have no ears to hear your clever words. Now that it has come to this, our fortune has run out; taste the thoroughly forged Tsumukari sword!"

No sooner had he spoken than he drew his great sword cleanly, and the fury with which he slashed at Odotsumatsumi and Katayamahiko was tremendous, just as though the Asura King were running amok. Odotsumatsumi and Katayamahiko ducked beneath the blade and fled at full speed toward the front gate.

Hinode-no-kami: "Ah, cowardly and contemptible fellows! Why do you not contest fairly and squarely?"

Katayamahiko: "Ah, it is regrettable, Lord Odotsumatsumi! However precious the teaching of Sango-kyō may be, to roll up our banners, sheathe our weapons, and flee this place while suffering such an insult will surely be regarded as cowardly. Manifesting the dignity and virtue of the sword in times of crisis ought to be permitted by God."

Odotsumatsumi: "No, no; with the heart of the God of supreme benevolence and love, I flee this place. If the dragon and the tiger fight together, neither's strength can remain unscathed. Whether I strike him down or am struck down, he too is a child of God, and I too am a child of God; for children of God to injure one another is inexcusable before the Parent God. For a while I shall avoid his sharp blade, and bide my time once more to bring him to repentance."

"Ah, the Ananaikyō is truly a teaching most difficult to practice!"

Thus he stamped his feet in mortification and chagrin. Hinode-no-Kami had been following behind, appearing and disappearing; hearing this conversation, he was greatly astonished and involuntarily,

"Wah!"

cried out and fell prostrate weeping.

Katayamahiko: "Look, somehow there is the sound of weeping in the dark."

Odotsumitsumi: "Indeed, it is a strange weeping, a very familiar voice. Look, who is it that cries out weeping in the darkness?"

From within the dark,

"I am Ōkunihiko, who falsely assumed the name of Hinode-no-Kami. Just now, hearing your words brimming with benevolence, I was choked with tears of gratitude and wept unawares. I promptly and completely repent of my past wickedness. Please grant me forgiveness."

Odotsumitsumi: "Oh, well satisfied, well satisfied! If it is thus, there is neither foe nor friend, we are entirely brothers. It is out of the desire to save my brothers that I have become an evangelist and endured hardships. As you know, I too was formerly the treasonous and atrocious Shikotsuwake, who was bathed in the rain of divine benevolence, repented, and became an evangelist. Matters having come to this, I shall proceed with you from here to Tokoyo Castle, and cause the Tokoyo Divine King Hirokuniwake to submit to the divine teachings."

Katayamahiko: "Ah, as expected of Lord Odotsumitsumi, it is truly admirable. I have received a fine practical lesson. Come, come, Hinode-no-Kami... no, Lord Ōkunihiko, let us now head toward Tokoyo Castle."

Odotsumitsumi skilled in intimidation, the purely unbending Katayamahiko, and Hinode-no-Kami from whose eyes sparks flew—faced with an unexpected hardship, Ōkunihiko now completely repented, the steed of his heart was roused, and all three together mounted swift dapple-gray horses; heedless of the swarming foes in Tokoyo Castle amid the darkness of Tokoyo where evil spirits raged, taking God as their strength and faith as their staff, transcending the boundary between life and death, stirring up courage against the multitude of enemies, with the gallant sound of horse hooves, urging them forward with the whip shouting 'hai-yō, hai-yō', they advanced.

Takeyamahiko and the other generals, upon seeing this sight, contrary to expectations that they would resist, spoke from atop their horses:

"Ah, Ōkunihiko no Mikoto, urō, urō, auspicious, most auspicious! Enter into the inner palace as swiftly as possible!"

Contrary to anticipation was their manner of greeting; Ōkunihiko, seized with bewilderment, proceeded with Odotsumitsumi and Katayamahiko deep into the interior, riding steadily upon their horses. What had seemed until now a vast army like clouds and mists vanished like an illusion without a trace, and in the inner palace, clear and resounding music echoed, an invigorating feeling drawing near their bodies. All sat upright in the inner chamber, recited the Amatsu Norito, and chanted the propagation hymns.

From this moment, upon both Rocky Castle and Tokoyo Castle, the divine flags of the Ten Luminaries fluttered, voices praising the divine virtues resounded through heaven and earth, and it is auspicious that the Land of Tokoyo was temporarily transformed into a heavenly paradise.

『エヽ三五教は誠に以て行り難い教であるワイ』

と地団駄踏んで口惜しがる。日の出神は見え隠れに後をつけ来り、この話を聞いて大いに驚き、思はず、

『ワツ』

とばかり泣き伏しにける。

固山彦『ヤア、なんだか暗がりに泣声が致しますよ』

淤縢山津見『さうだなア、何だか妙な泣声だ、よく似た声だ。ヤア、暗中に泣き叫ぶは何人なるぞ』

暗中より、

『私は日の出神と名を偽つた大国彦であります。只今貴方の仁慈に富める御言葉を聞いて、感涙に咽び思はず泣きました。私は今迄の悪を翻然として悔い改めます。どうぞ御赦し下さいませ』

淤縢山津見『ホー、満足々々、斯くならば敵も味方もない、全く兄弟だ。兄弟を助けたさに、吾は宣伝使となつて苦労を致して居るのだ。貴方の知らるる如く、吾も旧は大逆無道の醜国別、神の仁慈の雨に浴し、悔い改めて宣伝使となりし者、かくなる上は貴下と共に是より常世城に進み、常世神王広国別を神の教に帰順せしめむ』

固山彦『ヤア、流石は淤縢山津見さま、本当に感心だ。実地の良い教訓を受けました。サアサア日の出神……ではない大国彦殿、これより常世城に向ひませう』

嚇し上手の淤縢山津見、固い一方の固山彦、目から火の出る日の出神の、意外な憂目に大国彦、今は全く悔い改めて、心の駒も勇み立ち、三人一同に連銭葦毛の駿馬に跨り、魔神の猛る常世の暗の常世城、群がる敵を物ともせず、神を力に信仰を杖に、生死の境を超越し、勇気を鼓して敵の群衆に向つて、馬の蹄の音勇ましく、ハイヨーハイヨと鞭を加へて進み行く。

竹山彦その他の部将は、この光景を見て、抵抗するかと思ひの外、馬上より、

『ヤア、大国彦命、ウローウロー、目出度しめでたし、一時も早く奥殿に入らせられよ』

と案に相違の挨拶ぶり、大国彦は怪訝の念に駆られながら、淤縢山津見、固山彦と共に、馬上ゆたかに奥へ奥へと進み行く。今まで雲霞の如き大軍と見えしは、夢幻と消え失せて跡形もなく、奥殿には嚠喨たる音楽響き渡り爽快身に迫る。一同は奥の間に端坐し、天津祝詞を奏上し宣伝歌を唱ふ。

是よりロッキー城も常世の城も、十曜の神旗翻へり、神徳を讃美する声天地に響き、常世国は一時天国楽園と化したるぞ目出度けれ。
