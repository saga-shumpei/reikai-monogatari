§ 1

"Next, the names of the deities who came into being when he washed at the bottom of the water are Sokotsuwatatsumi-no-Kami, and next, Sokozutsunoo-no-Mikoto; the names of the deities who came into being when he washed in the middle are Nakatsuwatatsumi-no-Kami, and next, Nakazutsunoo-no-Mikoto; the names of the deities who came into being when he washed on the surface of the water are Uhatsuwatatsumi-no-Kami, and next, Uhazutsunoo-no-Mikoto; these three deities of Watatsumi are the gods whom the Azumi-no-Muraji and others hold and revere as ancestral deities, therefore the Azumi-no-Muraji and others are the descendants of Utsushihikanasaku-no-Mikoto, the child of that Watatsumi-no-Kami. Those three deities, Sokozutsunoo-no-Mikoto, Nakazutsunoo-no-Mikoto, and Uhazutsunoo-no-Mikoto, are the three great deities of Suminoe."

When interpreting the kotodama of minasoko (water bottom) one by one:

Mi is the complete fulfillment of physical form. It is fullness. It is pure nature. It is the great origin of the Way. It is the physical spirit jewel.

Na is the comprehensive governance of all things. It is the entirety of hydrogen. It is Omoikane-no-Kami. It is knowing the hidden through the visible. It is reaching everywhere thoroughly.

So is the ocean of the mind. It is adamantine void. It is the navel. It is encompassing Su. It is boundless clarity.

Ko is the essence of heavenly sincerity. It is total summarization. It is becoming the true source of all things. It is the parental element. It is yielding and accepting defeat.

In short, the water bottom refers to the bottom of the sea, the bottom of a river, the bottom of a pond, and the like, being a place where water gathers. Water nourishes and raises all things, accomplishes the work of generation, and furthermore mingles with all impurities without the slightest aversion, and that which washes clean the defilements of all things and thereby makes them pure is water alone. Moreover, water flows toward the low, gathers in hollows, conforms to square and round vessels, and thereby functions for welfare and utility, being the most important sacred vessel in the universe. When it encounters the heat of fire, it evaporates, ascends to heaven, becomes clouds and rain, and nurtures all upon the earth. Praising the divine spirit possessing such functions, we refer to it as the Spirit of Water.

Mi has its physical form completely fulfilled, without a single gap, filling every place, remaining in its pure nature without contending at all, forming the great origin of generation and development, nurturing the human body, and preserving the characteristic of becoming a jewel; Na governs all things, detects the invisible divine realm through the visible, becomes Omoikane-no-Kami to open and govern the world, and completely reaches the high, middle, and low alike; So becomes the ocean of spirit, manifests divine wisdom and wondrous ability, keeps the lower abdomen well-composed without being moved by affairs, is boundlessly clear without a shred of ambition, does not engage in selfish actions, and manifests the dignity of Su; Ko reveals the truth of heavenly sincerity, naturally preserves the status of a parent, becomes the true source of all living beings, and possesses the divine power and spiritual ability to unite the entire earth, yet softens its light and shares the dust, awaiting the arrival of the time like a horned dragon ascending to heaven, diligently concealing its spiritual ability when it is not the time, yielding and taking a lower place even before the inferior, the foolish, and the weak, and the low place where such a great true person lives in seclusion, not minding in the slightest, is what is called the water bottom. Ah, deeper than the sea and higher than the mountains, this is an era of longing for the Spirit of Water to appear as soon as possible, to wash and cleanse the heaven and earth of ignorance and perpetual darkness, stretching our necks like cranes while waiting for the emergence of Heaven and the Pure Land of Ultimate Bliss, and seeking the auspicious omen of the turtle finding its place and surfacing from the water bottom.

§ 2

Kotodama Explanation of Watatsumi-no-Kami.

"Wa" is a ring and the form of a tube. It is the origin of patterns. It is parent and child. It is the kotodama by which one begins to know the world. It is the origin of things and the origin of human beings. It is the kotodama that draws together and binds. It is the kotodama that preserves the world in order. It is the kotodama of the child's generation succeeding to the position of the parent.

"Ta" is the power of contrast. Such relations as the east contrasting with the west, the south with the north, heaven with earth, and life with death are what is called the power of contrast.

"Tsu" is great adamantine power. It continues strongly; true reality and suchness, this is called "Tsu." Furthermore, it is the power of corresponding response and the power of pairs; it is the great source of creation; it is the ultimate of equilibrium; spiritual and divine, radiant without intermission, it is the power of great creation, the great source of timely manifestation, and the ultimate of speed.

"Mi" is water, it is the body, and it carries the meaning of being filled to the full; it is the path of the Great Way of Kannagara.

When considering through these four kotodama, it becomes clear that this is a deity possessing the activities of the courageous Great Deity of Miroku, who is equipped with dignity and virtue, fully endowed with infinite divine power, perfectly complete, sweeping away the wicked and evil spirits of the world, and cleansing all existing sins, evils, and defilements.

Tsutsunoo-no-Mikoto.

The kotodama of Tsutsunoo is a divine name signifying that it possesses great adamantine power, thereby judges right and wrong, truth and falsehood, manifests the fundamental nature of water, washes and purifies all evil deeds, and restores souls to the state of Japanese soul where spirit leads and body follows. That "Kami" and "Mikoto" are distinguished and recorded here across the bottom, middle, and upper is something of great significance. The classics refer to spirit as "Kami" and body as "Mikoto." "Kami" means the subtle body, the hidden body—that is, kami—while "Mikoto" means bodily difference or physical distinction—that is, an extraordinary physical body. Those who studied ancient learning in later times were ignorant and unenlightened; not knowing the ancient meanings, they conflated "Kami" and "Mikoto" and designated both the invisible and the visible worlds by the same name, which is why, no matter how long they study, the true meaning of the classics is never understood. Furthermore, "bottom" points to the lowest spirit realm and society, "middle" points to the middle spirit realm and society, and "upper" points to the upper spirit realm and society. Therefore, Watatsumi-no-Kami divides into the three stages of bottom, middle, and upper to carry out the great reform of the divine realm, while Tsutsunoo-no-Mikoto likewise divides into three stages to carry out the great reform of the present society; this is the divine work. In the Omoto Shin'yu it is written: "I shall effect the demolition and reconstruction of the divine world and the human world," and it is also indicated: "Because the season has arrived to effect the rebuilding and reconstruction of the souls of deities, Buddhists, Confucianists, the populace, and the like, Ushitora no Konjin Okunitokotachi-no-Mikoto appears as the deity of Deguchi and, precisely according to the command of the Great Deity of the Three Heavenly Manifestations, performs the great laundering and great cleaning, making it into the splendid age of the Pine, the world of Miroku, bringing together the souls of the three ranks of upper, middle, and lower, and turning the three thousand worlds into the realm of the gods"; this is none other than the activity of these three deities and Mikoto.
