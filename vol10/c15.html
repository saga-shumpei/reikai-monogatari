Chapter 15: The Parting of the Word-Souls [445]

第15章　言霊別〔445〕

§ 1

In the primordial world where the divine Ancestor Kuniharu-tachi no Mikoto appeared, the wind was clear and pure, the water was clear, the sky was blue, the sun and the moon were without cloud, the stars shone beautifully across the whole sky, the mountains were green, the divine humans were all filled with harmony, peace, and joy, in the mountains and fields various tree fruits and vine berries ripened abundantly, the human grass plucked and ate them freely, there were neither rich nor poor, neither aging nor illness, and they knew not death; the winds and rains followed in orderly sequence, and it was truly a world of a golden age, a heavenly paradise. However, the evil thoughts of Amataruhiko and Enaba-hime, which prioritized body over spirit, congealed into evil serpents, and into evil demons and evil foxes, and their spirits walked rampant upon the earth; hereupon the air of monstrous evil filled the land, a disparity between rich and poor arose, the strong oppressed the weak, the struggle for existence became fierce, and the earth not only finally turned into a scene of carnage, but many of the divine humans became infected with that evil air, devoted themselves to selfishness, and at last came to attempt the destruction of the divine rule of the most benevolent and loving Great Deity. The evil air of the divine humans on earth finally manifested celestial anomalies and earthly cataclysms in the world, brought about a great flood, and the earthly world was once turned into an ocean of mud, leaving only the peaks of a few high mountains, becoming a disastrous sight too terrible to behold.

At this time, Takamimusubi no Kami, Kamimusubi no Kami, and Ōkuniharutachi no Kami made use of the divine power of Utsushikunitama, manifested the Floating Bridge of Heaven to admonish the divine humans on earth, rescued every single one of them with the rope of God, caused the two deities Izanagi and Izanami to descend to Mount Tenkyō, which was the Takamanohara of the earth, had them repair and solidify the drifting land that resembled a jellyfish using the Heavenly Jeweled Spear, and sought to give birth to countries, islands, and deities, and once again establish a golden world upon the earth. Nevertheless, after many months and years had passed once more, the earthly world turned into a dark world rampant with evil demons, evil serpents, evil foxes, and other demonic spirits, giving rise to a society where the superior win and the inferior lose, where the weak are meat for the strong, and manifesting an age in which various deities ran wild, including Ōyamakui, Nozuchi, Kayanohime, Ame no Sazuchi, Kuni no Sazuchi, Ame no Sagiri, Kuni no Sagiri, Ame no Kurado, Kuni no Kurado, Ōtomohiko, Ōtomohime, Tori no Iwagusufune (also known as Ame no Torifune), Ōgetsuhime, Hi no Yakihatsuo (also known as Hi no Kagahiko and Hi no Kagutsuchi), Kanayamahiko, and Kanayamahime.

The earthly world, newly established once through the great upheaval of heaven and earth, again became an age where evil deities ran rampant; since the various good deities returned to heaven, or concealed themselves underground, or entered the invisible realm to exercise their unseen protection, the lineage of Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime appeared once again as Uraruhiko and Uraruhime, held sway centering on the Ural Mountains, and falsely called themselves Banko Shinnō; meanwhile, the faction of Ōkunihiko and Ōkunihime, having their souls deceived and bewitched by evil deities, entrenched themselves in the Rocky Mountains, styled themselves Tokoyo Shinnō, and at last falsely arrogated the titles of Izanami no Mikoto and the Hinode Deity, coming to harbor the ambition of usurping the divine government of the realm for themselves.

ひとたび天地の大変動によって新たに建てられた地上の世界は、またしても邪神の荒ぶる世となり、諸々の善神は天に帰り、あるいは地中に潜み、幽界に入って、陰の守護をなさることとなったため、再び常世彦・常世姫の系統はウラル彦・ウラル姫として出現し、ウラル山を中心として割拠し、自ら盤古神王と偽称し、大国彦・大国姫の一派は邪神のためにその魂を誑惑され、ロッキー山に立て籠もって、自ら常世神王と称し、遂には伊弉冊命、日の出神と僭称して、天下の神政を私物化しようとする野望を抱くに至った。

§ 2

Here, Izanami-no-Mikoto, unable to bear seeing this disastrous state, personally went forth to Yomotsu-kuni, the root source of evil deities, to bring the evil deities to submission; and even if She could not bring them to submission, in order to conduct a diversionary movement so that they would not come raging and becoming estranged in the earthly world, She went forth to Yomi-no-kuni, then appeared at the Ryūgū Castle in the sea, and enacted various divine strategies; but having entrusted all hidden administration to Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto and Wakasakurashime-no-Mikoto, and having entrusted the Ryūgū in the sea to Otomehime-no-Mikoto, She openly declared that She would proceed to the Rocky Mountains, secretly returned to Mount Tenkyō, and, once again hiding Herself on Mount Chikyō, was engaged in the divine task of repair and consolidation.

The divine beings and humans of heaven and earth did not know this meticulous divine plan and firmly believed that Izanami-no-Mikoto had descended to Yomotsu-kuni; but upon hearing the divine oracle that Izanami-no-Mikoto would appear in the Rocky Mountains, Ōkunihiko—who was originally Daijizaiten and later became Tokoyo Shin’ō—thought it a golden opportunity, plotted with Ōkunihime and other subordinates, and attempted to occupy Yomishima to seize the earthly authority; therefore, the Great Deity finally came to initiate the unprecedented divine-demonic battle of Yomotsu Hirasaka.

This battle was the place where the victory or defeat of all divine beings and humans of good, evil, right, and wrong was decided, and this is what is called the Great Pass of the world.

○

What should be added regarding this story is that the location of Tokoyo Castle prior to the world in which the two deities Izanagi and Izanami repaired and solidified the drifting land resembling a jellyfish, gave birth to lands, gave birth to islands, gave birth to deities, and bestowed life upon all things, and its location thereafter, have changed considerably. Also, one must consider that there were some changes regarding Mount Kijō and other strategic divine lands, and that there were considerable alterations in the shapes of lands, the shapes of islands, rivers, lakes, mountain landscapes, and the like. Since there would be no end if each and every detail were explained in full, there are many parts omitted in this story to avoid complexity. Moreover, when deities from before the Heavenly Floating Bridge who appeared in Volumes 1 and 2 appear in the second world becoming younger than at that time, or when divine beings and humans who have once passed away manifest their previous forms in the divine realm and are active, it always appears contradictory if judged from the standpoint of common sense. Furthermore, that it sounds like a chaotic, disorderly, and incoherent story is rather natural. However, this story is a so-called tale of the spirit world that takes the spirits of all divine beings and humans as primary and sets aside their physical bodies, and because it is based on the principle of spirit-primary and body-subordinate, such detailed points as in which age this divine being passed away, after how many years they appeared with a physical body, what activities they carried out, whether they did good or evil, or in which deity's body they dwelled and were born, have been mostly omitted because there would be no end to them.

§ 3

All divine humans upon the earth engage in divine works by coming and going between spirit and flesh, flesh and spirit, light and darkness, life and death, and the visible and invisible worlds; hence, divine humans of remote antiquity appear in the middle ages, appear again in the present age, and appear in the future, growing ever younger, and eternally and infinitely maintain the life of the spirit—that is, the true guardian deity, that is, our true essence; therefore, if one does not read this narrative with that thought in mind, it is only natural that numerous doubts and contradictions will arise.

There are instances where mountains, plains, rivers, and seas stretching thousands of ri are traversed in one or two months, or traversed within a single day. I also wish for it to be understood that this narrative of myriad transformations and unpredictable fluctuations is entirely a materialization and concretization of spiritual activities consistent through the visible and invisible worlds, which transcend the time and space of the spiritual world. Furthermore, there are various strange stories, such as the summer sun rising at the North Pole or snowfall being seen directly beneath the equator. Even to the narrator, these are incomprehensible when considered from present-day knowledge. However, the tropics cannot eternally and infinitely remain the tropics, nor the frigid zones remain the frigid zones, remaining constant and immutable forever. As long as this universe is not a dead object, it shifts in climate and in position, and since this occurs over tens of billions of years, I believe it cannot be entirely denied. Therefore, whether the reader affirms or denies this book, the narrator feels nothing about it. Infinitely vast with nothing outside it, infinitely minute with nothing inside it, as if having no specific location, yet as if being nowhere absent, without light or dark, without large or small, without broad or narrow, without far or near, transcending time and space regardless of past, present, or future, escaping the human realm, and standing at the center of the great universe, I have dictated this narrative of the divine and spiritual worlds. Yet the narrator by no means relied upon his own conjectures or powers of deduction. It is the story exactly as seen and heard during the ascetic practice of Yūsai, and in short, it is an arrangement of spiritual dreams from seven days and seven nights. However, I confess that I myself do not doubt it in the least, nor do I think of it as an uncertain narrative.

原文（近代文語）

総て地上の神人は、霊より肉へ、肉より霊へと、明暗生死、現幽を往来して神業に従事するものであるから、太古の神人が中古に現はれ、また現代に現はれ、未来に現はれ、若がへり若がへりして、永遠に霊即ち本守護神、即ち吾本体の生命を無限に持続するものなるが故に、その考へを頭脳に置いて此物語を読まねば、幾多の疑惑や矛盾が湧いて来るのは当然である。

数千里の山野河海を一ケ月或は二ケ月に跋渉したり、又は一日の間に跋渉する事がある。千変万化、明滅不測の物語も、総て霊界の時間空間を超越したる現幽一貫の霊的活動を物質化、具体化して述べたものである事をも承知して貰ひたい。また北極に夏の太陽が出たり、赤道直下に降雪を見たり、種々の奇怪な物語がある。口述者に於いても、今日の知識より考へて不可解である。されど永遠無窮に熱帯は熱帯、寒帯は寒帯の侭、何時までも一定不変たる事を得ない。此宇宙は死物ではない限り、気候に於て位置に於て変動するも、幾十億万年の間の事であるから、強ち否定する訳にも行くまいと思ふ。故に読者の本書を肯定するも、否定するも、口述者に於ては何の感じもしないのである。至大無外、至小無内、若無所在、若無不所在、無明暗、無大小、無広狭、無遠近、過去と現在、未来とを問はず時間空間を超越し、人界を脱出し、大宇宙の中心に立つて、神霊界の物語を口述したものである。されど口述者は、決して自己の臆測や推考力によつたものでない。幽斎修業の際、見聞したる其侭の物語であつて、要するに七日七夜の霊夢を並べたものである。併しながら私としては些しも疑うて居るのではない。また不確実の物語とも思うて居ない事を告白して置きます。
