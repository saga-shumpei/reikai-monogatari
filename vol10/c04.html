Chapter 4: The Ogre-Snout Dumpling [434]

第4章　鬼鼻団子〔434〕

§ 1

With skin smooth as snow, flesh soft as floss-silk, eyes moist as dripping dew, three maidens possessing somewhere a dignity amidst their gentleness—were they the reincarnation of the Heavenly Maidens, or perhaps the cherry blossoms of the third month, the crouching-dragon pine or the snow-covered bamboo, the scent of plums where warblers sing—these sisters who formed the collar of spring, the propagators of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum, illuminated by the dazzling light of silver candles around them, added still more to their loveliness. Tokoyo-shin'ō was in exceedingly good humor, and seating the three maidens to his left and right, with a full smile on his face,

"The more I look at you, the more graceful sisters you appear; I am deeply impressed by your courage in advancing so splendidly to propagate the Teachings of Anaite in the Land of Tokoyo, where the Ural religion flourishes most. Nevertheless, the Land of Tokoyo takes the teachings of the Ural religion as its national policy. All the people joyfully submit to it, praising and revering its divine virtues. If, however, the Teachings of Anaite, whose doctrine and spirit are completely contrary, were to be spread in this land, the hearts of the people would immediately turn away, breaking the spirit of national unity and causing strife throughout the realm, as is clearer than seeing fire; therefore, the Land of Tokoyo strictly forbids the propagation of the Teachings of Anaite. Yet, despite being of frail womanly stature, to enter our land so boldly and sing propagation songs makes you great criminals who open the way to turmoil throughout the realm, and that you sisters should be subjected to severe punishment is what the law dictates; however, in appreciation of the merit of you three sisters in saving us from peril, I forgive your sins up to this point, entrust all matters within the palace to you, and shall have you attend by my side and devote yourselves to all domestic affairs."

When he thus gave this strict order, Matsushiro-hime smiled pleasantly, turned to Tokoyo-shin'ō, and bashfully opened her flowery lips:

"Truly gracious are your words; that you should condescend to allow us sisters, awkward and unaccustomed to worldly affairs, to remain within the palace, is an honor to our persons like finding a light in the dark or like a blind turtle encountering a floating log, and we respectfully accept."

Thus she spoke without hesitation.

"Ah, Lady Propagators of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum, though you were great criminals whom heaven and earth could not pardon, from this very day and moment you are jewels in the palm of Tokoyo-shin'ō, ladies of the court far superior even to imperial consorts and attendants. Henceforth, we shall look up to your ladyships' commands. As I, Takeyamahiko, am exceedingly rough, please do not hesitate to scold me properly."

Thus he expressed his respect. Takatoriwake, snorting through his nose, said:

"Ah, auspicious, auspicious, I offer my congratulations! You three court ladies, no matter how much you have risen in status, you must not become proud. Whatever you say, the prime minister to Tokoyo-shin'ō is this Takatoriwake; no matter how much power you may gain, you cannot exclude this Takatoriwake."

"Ahahahaha! Well, I, Takeyamahiko, who until now was the retainer of Lord Takatoriwake, have from this day forth, by the word of Tokoyo-shinnō, become his direct retainer, and am no longer your subordinate. You should consider yourself our colleague. As I say this, I feel as though this nose in the middle of my face has somehow swelled and grown high. In contrast, your nose was struck by a fireball, and the usual 'Takatoriwake of the prominent nose' is now utterly pitiful, crushed flat like a persimmon calyx and stuck across both cheeks. From now on, become 'Hikitoriwake of the low nose' and mend your former arrogant and insolent ways. Truly, what an insufferable face that is! Wahahahaha!"

Tokoyo-shinnō, with a friendly expression,

addressed them: "There is something I wish to ask of Matsushirohime. That you ladies, in your frail female bodies, have come to propagate teachings in this land of Tokoyo must surely be due to some profound reason. Speak without concealing anything. Now that matters have come to this, there are no divisions between us, so state the facts without reservation."

Thus he inquired. Matsushirohime spoke in a light and airy tone:

"Yes; the reason that we three sisters have endured hardships and trials to come to the land of Tokoyo is for nothing else. We heard that the august guardian deities of Anaikyō, Kamun-Izanami no Mikoto and Hinode-no-Kami, had manifested on Mount Rocky, and so we came following in their footsteps. As the saying goes, when in a village, follow the village; henceforth, I shall abandon Anaikyō and convert to the Ural religion that Tokoyo-shinnō reveres. However, are not Izanami no Mikoto and Hinode-no-Kami likewise establishing Anaikyō?"

"No, Izanami-no-Ōkami and Hinode-no-Kami have firmly set up the pillars of their palace on Mount Rocky and founded the Ural religion!"

So argued Tōyamawake with a smug look, yet his contention was exceedingly suspicious. At this moment, the gatekeeper Kanihiko appeared timidly before them,

saying: "I report to the minister Takatoriwake. Just now, Miyamawake no Mikoto, along with Kunitamahime, has arrived at the castle as an envoy from Mount Rocky and is resting in the detached palace. How shall we proceed?"

"I shall now enter the bedchamber and rest. Takatoriwake, ascertain clearly the intent of the divine messengers from Mount Rocky and report it before me. Come, court ladies Matsu, Take, and Ume!"

"Serves you right, you Katatora fellow, trembling and shaking! Curse others, and you dig two graves. Even your two nostrils have been ruined. As for the unsightliness of Takatoriwake's nose, it is a sight too wretched to behold. Hey, shaking Tora, you come along too!"

As they said this, they led the way, waving their hands, with a peculiar and comical manner of walking. In particular, Kanihiko bent his hips, swinging his buttocks vigorously with every step as he went. Miyamawake and Kunitamahime leisurely advanced into the inner palace and took their proper seats.

Miyamawake: "Oh, Prime Minister Deity of Tokoyo Castle, Takatoriwake, are you the one?"

Takeyamahiko said respectfully,

"Well, well, Lord Imperial Envoy, welcome indeed. Until now he was Takatoriwake, but from today on, having had the height of his nose taken away, he has changed his name to Hikitori-Hechahiko."

While making snorting sounds through his nose, Takatoriwake tried to say something, but his tone was distorted and incomprehensible, which was pitiful.

"In any case, receive firmly the divine decree of Izanami-no-Ōkami. We hear that the three missionaries of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum who crossed over to the land of Tokoyo are hiding in the house of Haruyamahiko, the intermediate chieftain. The strict command is that you must swiftly dispatch captors, take those three alive, and send them to the Rocky Mountains as soon as possible."

Thus he solemnly delivered the command. At Miyamawake's words, Kanihiko said,

"Excuse me, Lord Imperial Envoy Miyamawake, those three of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum are already in the personal chamber of the Divine King of Tokoyo..."

Tōyamawake said: "Hush, Kanihiko, hold your needless tongue! What does a mere gatekeeper know? This is no place for the likes of you to speak; withdraw and stay back! ...Well, well, Lord Imperial Envoy, as you can see, Takatoriwake lacks clear speech, so I, Tōyamawake the second-in-command, shall receive it in his stead. I have fully understood the purport of the imperial envoy's message. We shall take the three maidens alive as soon as possible and deliver them."

"Your prompt consent brings great satisfaction. The Great Deity will surely also be pleased. Well then, I shall hasten back to the Rocky Mountains. Convey the full details to the Divine King of Tokoyo."

Tossing out these words, leading numerous retainers and comfortably swaying on horseback, he departed toward the outside of the gate together with Kunitamahime. Chasing after Miyamawake, Kanihiko cried,

"Excuse me, Lord Imperial Envoy, please do not be deceived by Tōyamawake's preposterous words! Truly, this Kanihiko knows everything and anything!"

Though he shouted in a hoarse voice, drowned out by the sound of hooves, Miyamawake paid no heed, quickened his pace, and swiftly vanished into the distance on his way back.
