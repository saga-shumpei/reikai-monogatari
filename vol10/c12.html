Chapter 12: Surveying the Vale from the Mount [442]

第12章　山上瞰下〔442〕

§ 1

Guided by Katatora, while being blown by the spring breeze across the Shira mountain range, they finally reached the summit of the pass. To the east, the vast and boundless ocean unfolded without limit, and the Rocky Mountain towering to the west was enveloped from its mid-slope to its peak in gathering clouds, sometimes thick and sometimes thin.

The party of six rested their breath on the turf where green grass was sprouting at the pass, refreshing their eyes with the surrounding scenery.

Uzuyamahiko said, "Ho, Otoyama-sama, do you still believe that Izanami no Mikoto and Hinode-no-Kami are indeed present on the Rocky Mountain?"

Otoyama-tsumi replied, "Of course. How could I not believe it? Indeed, when I accompanied them from Ryūgū Castle and parted on the sea, I received the words directly from their own mouths that they were going to the Rocky Mountain to conceal themselves."

"Indeed, that is natural enough, but do you still by no means believe what I said in my divine possession?"

"It is not that I do not believe it, but for the present my state of mind is, as it were, like wandering in a deep fog."

"Even if the divine plan of God is generally fixed and unchanging, it also has both an inside and an outside. Just as there is an inner realm beyond the innermost, and a depth so profound that there is no bottom beneath the bottom, great errors will arise if one does not carefully discern it. That is why it is shown in the divine poem that a missionary of the Way of the Gods must look again, hear again, and proclaim anew."

"Ah, the truth or falsehood, correctness or incorrectness of that is a matter of time. We wish to overcome all difficulties as soon as possible, break through the enemy's strict vigilance, climb the Rocky Mountain, and seek out their whereabouts."

"I am sorry to say this, but the inside of your heart is just like that Rocky Mountain. It is the same as being half shrouded in clouds and half revealing the natural surface of the spring hills and fields. Unless you clear away the clouds of your heart, the true divine plan of God will not become clear. You will understand it best if you ask this Katatora."

Katatora said, "No, I cannot state anything for certain either, but according to what Tokoyo Shin'ō says, the Great Kami Izanami and Hinode-no-Kami are on the Rocky Mountain; not to mention Tokoyo Castle, the common people also think so and firmly believe it. If anything, we are also among those who believe it."

淤縢山津見「当然のことです。これをどうして信じないでいられましょうか。現に竜宮城からお供をして海上で別れた時、これからロッキー山に行って身を隠す、とご自身の口からお言葉を承ったのですから」

「はあ、その真偽、当否は時の問題です。我々は一日も早く万難を排して敵の厳しい警戒を突破し、ロッキー山に登ってその消息を探ってみたいと思うのです」

「こう言うと済まないが、あなたの心の中はちょうど、あのロッキー山のようですよ。半分は雲に包まれ、半分は春の野山の素地を現しているのと同じことだ。心の雲を晴らさなければ、真実の神の経綸ははっきりしない。この固虎に聞いたら一番よく分かるでしょう」

固虎「いえ、私も確かなことは申し上げられませんが、常世神王のお言葉によれば、伊邪那美の大神様、日の出神様は、ロッキー山におられるとのこと、常世城は言うに及ばず、一般の人民もそう思って固く信じております。我々も、どちらかと言えば、信じている方の仲間ですよ」

§ 2

Uzuyamahiko said, "Speaking of Lord Odoyama, and speaking of Lord Katatora, these are truly vague and ambiguous thoughts. Is your spirit not uneasy? I cannot help but find it wondrous how, on such an unreliable ground, your conviction can endure."

Odoyamatsumi said, "Whatever may be said, with the thoughts of us foolish human beings, the divine work of the vast and boundless God cannot be clearly understood. Rather, I think it is natural that we cannot understand. I consider it presumptuous for our status as human beings to judge or criticize the divine work of God. Only with a mind fixed on each moment, one will not understand until one goes as far as one can go."

"If Izanami-no-Kami and Hinode-no-Kami were frauds, what would you do at that time?"

"At that time, for the first time, the clouds and mists of the heart will clear, and the sun and moon of true reality will shine across the sea of the heart. All things are simply according to the will of the gods."

"Will you not become angry?"

"Just as the propagation song of the Anankyo teaches, at that very time I am prepared to re-look, re-hear, and re-declare in accordance with the spirit of Naohi."

"Oh, with such a thought, you too will receive a passing grade as a propagator. The manner of your faith has changed greatly. It is said that the power of faith can move even a mountain, but do you possess sufficient power of faith to draw that mountain before yourself?"

"Such a thing is after all a spiritual matter, and it could not be done realistically. Can you do it?"

"Of course I can; not only in the spiritual realm, but I will realistically draw the mountain before me and show you. You also, please follow behind me. If one turns the hand it becomes rain, and if one overturns the hand it becomes cloud—in this world of absolute freedom, in the exceedingly rare event that it does not move by my kotodama, it must be because the god of the mountain has some circumstances and is busy, so it is merely a matter of me walking over there and drawing it before my eyes."

"I thought it would be roughly something like that. In that case, we too could draw even the sea."

"Oh, Lord Katatora, until this very moment you were a follower of the evil deities and tried greatly to torment us, yet how suddenly you have changed, as if turning over your palm."

Katatora said, "If one turns the hand it becomes rain, and if one overturns the hand it becomes cloud."

Uzuyamahiko said, "Hey, hey, do not imitate me. If it is evil, then evil; if it is good, then good—why not carry it through to the very end? To think that you were wrong and suddenly reverse your spirit like a swallow turning in flight does not suit you, Lord Katatora, who are usually so resolute, does it?"

§ 3

"These are incomprehensible words from the missionaries; when one mistakenly believes evil to be good, dashing forward to the end is the true merit of a man. When one realizes that it was bad, is it not the true man who immediately looks anew, hears anew, and declares anew?"

"O deity of shifting opinions and altering arguments, declaring anew is fine. It is like leaving the horned Ushigumowake and the sharp-beaked Takatoriwake, exchanging an ox for a horse, and riding anew."

Otoyamatsumi, "Ahaha haha, interesting, interesting!"

Mezurahiko, "Lord Katatora, if you go to Mount Rocky again, might you not turn right around? Because you are the originator of shifting opinions and altering arguments, one cannot help but worry."

Katatora, "Please look into Katatora's iron belly, harder than rock. I am not something to be so mocked. You say exceedingly rude things, but regarding that, what I still cannot understand is the mystery of Tokoyo Castle last winter; missionaries bearing the same names as the three missionaries present here were captured from the Middle Country and entered Tokoyo Castle, and while they were in the great favor of Tokoyo-shin'o, before anyone knew it, they turned like smoke and vanished. At that time I was serving as a gatekeeper, and a missionary of Sango-kyo named Teruhiko was also captured, and this too, oh wonder, turned into smoke and vanished, manifesting various wonders and subjecting Tokoyo-shin'o, Takatoriwake, and the others to terrible plights at will; and whether by the punishment of that time I was demoted from gatekeeper to a senior official of Tokoyo Castle or promoted, heavens, it is something I can no longer understand. Is this what is called a labyrinth? The gatekeeper suddenly ascended to heaven and wielded influence, and when I thought to make an achievement by leading many retainers and surrounding you on Karigane Peninsula, that state of affairs occurred; how could I not reform?"

"A reform born of fright, fear, and the desire to spare one's life is not a true reform; it is a monstrous mind. Katatora, declare anew."

At this time, war cries were heard all at once from the direction of Mount Rocky. The party of six,

"Yo, it is a critical moment. Everyone, let each of us prepare our minds from now on."

Thus thinking, each parted ways toward the mountain summit, and finally came to take independent action against Mount Rocky. Ah, what will be the outcome of this? It is uneasy, yet encouraging.
