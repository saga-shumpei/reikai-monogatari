Appendix: Second Travelogue Poems of Mount Takakuma Pilgrimage

附録　第三回高熊山参拝紀行歌（三）

§ 1

God appears openly, purifying both the fields of Suzuki and the mountain depths in this joyous reign of the pine; the divine heart deems it endearing (Kawai). Hearing one and comprehending ten, transformed into true preservation (Ampo), holding (Tometarō) the teachings of God deep within the heart, how dependable it is to worship morning and evening; that person's safety, a mountain of springs (Izumiyama), the husband rejoicing in the soul's resolve, the Founder's departure to the west (Shussai), the island of God. God's divine journey also crosses the broad ocean (Wada-no-hara), with rough waves surging as the boat rows far and wide past the tidal flats of Ojima and Mejima; at the pure climbing path on the rocky shore of the divine mountain floating upon the waves, youths and maidens of steadfast heart prostrate themselves before the shrine, having braved perils to at last arrive on pilgrimage, good believers upholding the teachings of Ōmoto. Blessings are abundant (Ōno Tokumatsu); Ogata Tarōsaku acts manfully; sincere red hearts are like the autumn foliage of autumn mountains (Akiyama Yoshiyuki); in the month of chrysanthemums, searching for the dwelling place of the newly unearthed golden jewel, sinking gradually toward the west, parting the dense grasses, the crescent moon shines in the sky, illuminating the darkness of the eternal world. Truly awe-inspiring is the divine majesty of God!

§ 2

Both the West and Yamato alike are covered in the sky by ugly clustered clouds, and like fledgling birds separated from their parents, having no way to ascend to the high heavens, in the shallows of the swift river plunging down precipitously like a waterfall, beautifully washing their bodies and souls, the missioners of Yagyū by the grace of the Great God, and the flowers on the lush trees in Obara, fragrantly and purely bloom in the soundness of Yoshii; for the sake of the way of the same teaching of the Great Origin, in the country of rice plants relying on paddy fields, knowing neither how to advance nor how to retreat, in the manor of Saeki where the command of the Heavenly Sun-Succession's Sun Child is revered with awe, just as Hieda no Are narrated the national history, so that Zuigetsu, clear as the sky, may transmit the noble texts of the divine age, proclaiming the divine works of the Plain of High Heaven simply and joyfully, narrating without any hesitation just as the Arakawa River flows, recounting the divine history of the land of Uraho, while gathering the power of the soul, with a brave spirit that melts iron and gold, standing beyond worldly good and ill fortune, reforming the believers who ponder the precious teachings of the gods shallowly and babble idly, bringing them into unity under a single truth. To the people who noisily clamor like a marketplace, firmly expounding and guiding them to the true righteousness, the ark of divine salvation transcends all things, being the preeminent and precious sole teaching. In the Ōmoto of the true god, young and old alike possess a firm heart like a vault of iron and gold; in the world-renowned island country, cranes nest in the thousand-year pine trees, the winds of blessing bring overflowing good fortune, joy is heaped upon joy, and the people of both inside and outside the country take heart; even the plains of the realm once tangled like bamboo grass now look up to the towering divine authority, celebrating the thousand-year longevity of the Lord.

§ 3

The sixteen petals of the chrysanthemum flower emit a charming fragrance above the jewel pond; righteous and eminent is the teaching, the august grace of Ōmoto Kamino. Never heard of as ordinary is the conspicuous and boundless divine protection; prosperous and auspicious is the village of Ueki, where, with exquisite intricacy, the Pine Island floats on the pond of the Dragon Palace. The glorious virtue of the deities excels in the world; born into the lineage of Fujiwara who administer sacred rites, these are the written characters inscribed in service to the Great Deity of the Creation of Heaven and Earth. Lest this clumsy diversion should deviate from the profound assistance of the Sovereign Deities, the divine age that illuminates the grass and mountaintops in all four directions draws near, casting the radiant light of the realization of divine governance upon the islands inside and outside the sea. Grandly rising, the figure of the morning sun is pure, scattering the gathering clouds in mid-sky to all directions, revealing anew the goodness of the landscape. The view too bears the auspicious spring hues of Yoshida, embodying the tranquil state of an era governed in peace. Gazing closely to the left, blossoms bloom in full across the fields of Sogabe, the flowers of the trees fragrantly scenting the myriad vast green plains. Beaming brightly in the heavenly sunlight, households are peaceful and exalted, filled with the laughter of rejoicing people. This is the land of Ōyashima, where the unstoppable divine army governs the way, where the waves of the Kinryū Sea surge white. How pleasant is the plain of Inada; the countless small sparrows fluttering about chirp "chiu chiu" as if serving in loyalty. The clappers sound over the rice fields of Furuse, the golden waves of rice lie calm, the fields and river shallows are free from harm, and the divine law that prospers in tranquil peace resounds with august majesty across Mount Takakuma.

§ 4

The age of the gods turns round, celebrating the thousand years of Kitazawa in Great Japan. The blessing of the God of True Metal is valiant; without distinction between high and low, noble and base, looking up to the light of Mishima as Sahei, beneath the moon of pines awaiting the clearing of the eternal night, the clustered clouds disperse into the clear sky, and in the divine garden of the Ryūgū Pavilion, planted so densely as to leave no room, the flowers of the lawn are now in full bloom. High and radiant in the pond's Naka-no-Oshima, the rock god enshrined, the great gods of rain and wind leave their figures pure and cool above the well of jewels, shining upon and protecting the brows of believers who trust Higorō; how joyful within the heart to receive the divine virtue of the god of food provision created by Ōmitagara! The produce ripens abundantly and cheerfully, offered before the Great Kami like a sprawling mountain; the inexhaustible divine rope of Eiji, the mulberry bow drawn and never returned, the feeling of Suminoe in Takamanohara, especially Princess Yoshiko, the rice beautifully ripens one after another in the abundant land of the honorable realm; the fields and mountains are ever more blessed, and delightful is the plain of Suzuki where the divine work of the Creator God unfolds. The shining and brilliant divine assistance is the drop of the blessings of the sun and moon crossing the heavenly sky; children of the path of the same teaching gaze at the clearing clustered clouds of the west, looking up at the reign ruled by the divine virtue of the Sovereign Great Kami; the high mountain of Fuji stands upright in the distant sky of clouds, valiant, robust, and broad of root, sweeping away the rising clouds and mist, towering with fragrant and fine scenery. Relying on the path of sincerity, the mother of Kamigorō who pursues pure goodness—before the Kami, splendid to hear, the paddy fields are waves of a bumper crop of rice, the wind blows gently across, and the edges of the fields and forests are exceedingly pure. Gathering together in the reign of Lord Saburō, in the flower capital and in Shibuya, devoting their hearts to the single way of martial spirit and benevolence, moving forward; how fine is the bliss of Ōmitagara! Promptly standing on their own feet toward the woven high heaven, the believers who had hesitated to advance now harmonize their hearts with one another in the field; the fellowship of the pure teaching is a delight without equal. In the shrine precinct where pure water springs forth, the Spirit of Mizu prospers gracefully; believing in the divine teaching of the peach and Sakurai, the hearts of people are the spring of a thousand ages fragrant above the well of jewels; awakening to the way and protecting the six realms of the gods beneath the shade of the everlasting evergreens, love extends not only to neighbors, but endlessly across the seas within and without, to countless nations large and small, the narrative unfolding the divine authority of the gods of Takamanohara. The origin of the teachings is firmly established; illuminating and aiding the foundation of the great bridge that crosses to the other shore, the water and trees of the Isuzu River are fair, divine governance is peacefully established, in the Central Land of Reed Plains, the shelves of the sacred storehouses are settled, the land of Urayasu develops, and joyful it is to recount the eternal history of divine governance; before the Great Kami who appears upon Mount Takakuma, I offer my thanksgiving.

神代が巡り北沢の千歳を祝う大日本、真金の神のお恵みは雄々しく、貴賤上下の区別なく、三島の光を佐平と仰ぎ、常夜の晴れを待つ松の月のもと、群雲が散りて真澄の空となり、竜宮館の神苑に所狭しと植え込まれた芝生の花も今盛りである。高く輝く池の中野、男島に斎き祀った岩の神、雨と風の大御神が玉の井の上に御姿を清く涼しく留め給い、日五郎を信ずる信徒の額を照らして守りつつ、大御田柄が造られた保食神の御神徳を戴く心の中の嬉しさよ。作柄も豊かに朗らかに実りて神の大前に横山のように献じられ、尽きることのない英二四の神の綱、引かれて戻さぬ桑の弓、高天原に住之江の心地も殊に淑子姫、稲が次々に美しく実る御国の豊の国、野山も幸いがますます増し、造化の神の御神業が開けるのも楽しい鈴木原。照り輝く御神助は、天津御空を渡るあたりの月と日の恵みの滴である。同じ教えの道の子が晴れゆく西の村雲を打ち眺め、皇大神の神徳によって治まる御代を仰ぎつつ、藤の高嶺が遥かなる雲居の空に端然として勇壮健やかに、根も広く立ち上る雲霧を払いながら、景色も芳しく聳え立つのである。誠の道に依りすがる善一筋の神五郎の母、聞くもめでたき神の御前、田は満作の稲の波が立ち、風がおだやかに吹き渡り、田の辺りも林も大そう清らかである。寄り集まりて三郎君の御代、花の都も渋谷も尚武慈愛の一筋に心を傾けて前へと進み行く。大御田柄の幸いはいかに良いことか、綾の高天へといそいそと足に任せて立ちながら、進みかねていた信徒が互いに心を合わせる田の中、清き教えの交わりはこれに越すもののない楽しみである。清水が湧き出る宮垣内、瑞の御霊のゆかしくも栄えて桃も桜井の神の教えを信じた人の心は、玉の井の上にも匂い立つ千代の春、道を悟りて神の天地を守る常磐の木下陰、愛は隣人のみならず海の内外の限りなく大小無数の国々に、高天原の神々の御稜威を押し開く物語。教えの根本の立ち位置も良く、彼岸へと渡す大橋の根本を明らかに助け導き、五十鈴川の水も木も清らかに神政が善く治まりて、豊葦原の中津国、御倉の棚も定まりて浦安の国も発達し、天壌無窮の神政史を語るのも嬉しく、高熊の山に現れ出で給う大神の御前に感謝し奉る。

§ 5

The trees standing in a row in the spacious courtyard of the shrine of Obata; the water of the brook flowing to the east of Nakajō is exceedingly pure, and for those who draw it, body and mind are cleansed well, and happiness shall surely be filled anew day after day. Mizushiro, who received high blessings abundantly, climbed the hill of Takakuma and opened the Great Fundamental Way; Takeo, who learned this tale distinctly at Chūwa and Ōnakajō, underwent spiritual cultivation on this sacred peak in order to spread it in fine detail across the land of Yashima. The enclosing trees and grasses grow thickly in profusion, full of bustling vitality. At the village shrine where stalwart men keen on justice cling before the altar, the sound of the Shintō prayers is dear to the heart, and that they shall attain the realm of safety and peace is truly certain. The origin of weaving the shizuhata fabric of man and woman lies in the lake of Shiga; pure is the base of the Kogane-bashi bridge, and desiring to widely deliver the divine salvation to the public, the Imperial God of Kagura-ga-oka opens the great way of single-minded goodness ever higher upon Mount Takakuma—how noble and venerable it is.
