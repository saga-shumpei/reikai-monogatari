Chapter 19: The Substitute [449]

第19章　替玉〔449〕

§ 1

The light of the heavenly sun shone purely and widely, and the full moon shone in the firmament without leaving a single shadow; yet in the inner palace shrouded in the darkness of eternal night with its dim, wicked schemes, the roaring voices of eighty evil spirits leaked forth from who knew where. As if warned by a premonition, his heart felt heavy and his chest ached; Tokoyo Shin'ō Hirokuniwake, together with Hirokunihime, having seen off the brave generals and fierce troops dispatched to the island of Yomi, was driven by a deep sense of anxiety.

Tokoyo Shin'ō said, 'Ah, Hirokunihime, the heroes whom I usually rely on as my right and left hands—Teruyamahiko, Nakayoriwake, and Takatoriwake—have all departed for the front, and somehow I feel completely enervated. The loneliness within the castle is just as if a paper lantern had been kicked in; should a strong enemy launch a sudden attack on the off chance, Tokoyo Castle would be like a rat trapped in a bag. If such a suspicious occurrence as happened last year should arise once more, we could do nothing about it. If only we had left at least Takeyamahiko behind.'

Hirokunihime said, 'Somehow I too cannot bear this anxiety. Last night as well, I had a strange dream and was exceedingly worried. Nevertheless, this is said to be a floating world of dreams; whatever happens, one way or another, unless we entrust all our fortunes to Heaven, we can do nothing. Somehow every day my conscience pricks me, and I feel as though the God of Heaven were shouting at me, so that I am always timid and my heart finds no peace.'

'Do not utter such weak words. When one god abandons you, another picks you up. If good things come, bad things will also come. If the battle on the island of Yomi turns out well and ends in our victory, this vast world will be entirely at the disposal of Izanami no Mikoto. If that happens, we will be Great Divine Kings not merely of the land of Tokoyo, but of the earthly realm as well.'

While the husband and wife were thus whispering with their heads close together, worrying about their future yet holding on to hope, the maidservant Kiyohime appeared in this place, fearfully placed both hands on the floor, and said,

'Just now, as an envoy of Hinode-no-Kami from Rocky Castle, Sakakuniwake arrived leading many attendants, stating with tremendous fierceness that he has particulars to deliver to Tokoyo Shin'ō. How shall we handle this?'

Tokoyo Shin'ō said, 'Ho, that must be an order commanding us to go forth to battle as well. Hirokunihime, you must act as my deputy and defend this Tokoyo Castle. If it is an order, it cannot be helped.'

Hirokunihime said, '.......'

Kiyohime said, 'What answer shall I give?'

§ 2

Tokoyo-shin'ō: "Call Kasatoriwake."

"Yes,"

answered Kiyohime, and departed from this place. Kasatoriwake crept through the bushes of the garden trees, rushed in hurriedly, knelt in bow before the shoe-removing stone, and said,

"Kasatoriwake has now arrived before you."

Tokoyo-shin'ō: "Ho, Kasatoriwake, is it? I have a command for you. Go and deal with Sakakuniwake, the high envoy from Rocky Castle."

"We lesser deities possess no right to speak directly toward the high envoy."

"Nay, today I appoint you Chancellor. You are the deputy of Takatoriwake."

"Eh, allow me to inquire for a moment. Is it only for today? Or do you grant me the office of Takatoriwake forever? If it is temporary, I must flatly decline. Being a magistrate for an hour or a commissioner for a day would leave me utterly insecure, without the courage to negotiate in earnest."

At this moment, Sakakuniwake called out in a loud voice:

"Hear, hear! I, Sakakuniwake, the high envoy of the Hinode Deity, have come to this main castle on a matter of investigation. Tokoyo-shin'ō, appear at this place without a moment's delay!"

Kasatoriwake: "Listen, listen, he does not say in the least that he wishes to meet Kasatoriwake. The high envoy is shouting for Tokoyo-shin'ō."

Tokoyo-shin'ō: "You are the one who goes out as the deputy."

"Ah, so I am the deputy of Tokoyo-shin'ō? What a great thing! Shall I say a snail has ascended to heaven, or that a dung beetle in the privy has made its way up, grown wings, and perched on the king's head? It is just like Kanihiko becoming a general. Truly, Tokoyo Castle is enveloped in the darkness of eternal night. That is why all who labor hard gain nothing but fatigue, and all honest men of ability are cast down. For someone like me, who struts beneath the prestige of superiors and does nothing but blunder in secret, such splendid fortune comes along. Well then, now I shall become Tokoyo-shin'ō himself, look down upon Sakakuniwake, and shout at him. And it will be amusing to give him a shock, showing him, 'Well, see what I can do!'"

Tokoyo-shin'ō: "Hey, Kasatoriwake, what are you muttering about? Go at once!"

Kasatoriwake: "There is no question of going or not going. By your absolute decree, I am the deputy of Tokoyo-shin'ō; now that you have made use of a deputy, you should have no further business, so please hold your tongue... Hear, hear, Sakakuniwake or whoever you are, the high envoy from Rocky Castle! I, Tokoyo-shin'ō—listen, Lord Tokoyo-shin'ō, I shall just leave out the word 'deputy' for now, so be prepared for that."

§ 3

Tokoyo-shin'ō, together with Hirokuni-hime, silently entered the separate hall and considered the situation.

Kasatoriwake: "The deputy of Tokoyo-shin'ō... no, Kasatoriwake... wait, hold on, Hirokuniwake is here! I grant Sakakuniwake an audience. Draw near, draw near!"

Sakakuniwake calmly appeared in this place and, bowing with hands clasped, walked directly up toward the seat of Tokoyo-shin'ō.

Kasatoriwake: "Hold, imperial envoy! That is my seat. Step back!"

Sakakuniwake: "Tokoyo-shin'ō, you insolent knave who disguised yourself as the three men, Matsu, Take, and Ume, by means of magic and deceived your superiors! Surrender Tokoyo Castle this very day, at this very moment, and make haste to arrange matters to ride in this palanquin and come to Rocky Castle!"

"This is outrageous!"

Before he had even finished speaking, four or five retainers swiftly bound Kasatoriwake's arms tightly behind his back.

Kasatoriwake: "I am Kasatoriwake! How I hate these ropes! How I regret taking off my hat! I am not Tokoyo-shin'ō. I am a vassal's vassal's vassal. I merely took on the role of the Divine King temporarily for a moment. Rather than binding me, bind the true Tokoyo-shin'ō!"

Sakakuniwake: "No matter how skillfully you try to deceive me, by virtue of my discernment, once I have laid eyes upon you, you are unmistakably Hirokuniwake, Tokoyo-shin'ō! Hearken, retainers, there is no need to listen to his words. Thrust him into the palanquin at once!"

"Aye!"

they answered, and forcibly shoved him into the palanquin;

Sakakuniwake said, "Now, since matters have come to this, all is well. The surrender of Tokoyo Castle can be handled later; let us return to the main castle as swiftly as possible!"

and mounting his horse, he led his numerous retainers, riding back in high spirits toward Rocky Castle.
