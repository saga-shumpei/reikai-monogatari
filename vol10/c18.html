Chapter 18: The Riding Grounds of Tokoyo [448]

第18章　常世馬場〔448〕

§ 1

Tokoyo Castle shone upon by the spring sun, the castle keep where mists trail, Mount Rocky and Rocky Castle, the three mallows of the Castle of Tokoyo, and Jizaiten of the Sun-Rising God; without knowing in the slightest that they had fallen under the suspicion of Ōkunihiko, and unaware of the white flags fluttering flappingly in the wind, the gatekeepers lie upon the turf of the wide riding ground, indulging in idle chatter. Takahiko said:

"Hey Kurahiko, was it last winter? Propagandists like celestial maidens named Matsu, Take, and Ume arrived, and Lord Tokoyo Shin'ō was pleased as punch; in the end he grew excited, making an executive of a gatekeeper fellow who had blundered, and 'promoting' a splendid official who had worked with all his might to gatekeeper; a monster named Teruhiko appeared and rampaged about; outrageous beauties called Tsuki, Yuki, and Hana arrived, and this vast Castle of Tokoyo was filled day after day with the vernal cheer of the third month wherein a hundred flowers bloomed in profusion, so that we too cleansed our ears with the resonance of stringed and wind music; but in this world of abrupt changes, while thinking we were inside the castle, Lord Shin'ō, and even we ourselves, found ourselves in this riding ground, exposed to the night dew, looking foolish. Just as the cries of foxes were heard here and there, yelping 'kon-kon' and 'kwai-kwai', Teruhiko, who had arrived riding in a palanquin, and the six maidens vanished into smoke, and strange things indeed took place. That fellow Kanihiko, skilled at walking sideways, pompously took the name of Takeshimahiko and departed for the front to the Island of Yomi leading numerous troops; the world undergoes a complete turning indeed. If such things happen again, even a gatekeeper cannot be taken lightly. It is as they say: if one waits, favorable winds for the voyage will come. Since all from Mount Rocky and Tokoyo Castle have departed for the front, and there is a total dearth of talented persons left behind, Takahiko will surely be chosen and might very well become Takatoriwake."

Kurahiko said, "Are you dreaming because the day is so long? It might be amusing if Takahiko became Takatoriwake, had his nose crushed by the monster's fireball, and became Hanabishyagehiko."

"What, what does it matter if one's nose gets crushed? Takatoriwake is still Takatoriwake. Comporting himself with majesty as the general of the entire army and sweeping aside all around him, his splendid figure departing for the Island of Yomi leading numerous troops was truly the height of envy, even in the eyes of this Takahiko."

"Greedy bear Takahiko, do you know the saying about tearing one's crotch?"

"You blind, dimwitted, blunt-witted fellow, what nonsense are you spouting? A person's worldly success lies in luck. How could someone like you understand my luck?"

"Is there anyone who does not know your un? Just the other day, saying it was troublesome to go to the privy, did you not drop a tremendous counterclockwise coil shaped like an oil-lamp wick-trimmer at the foot of the bridge? A multitude of greenbottles came out, swarming until it grew black with their buzzing. You are no Takatoriwake; you are a shit-fellow of a fly-swarming Hiko."

"What a troublesome fellow you are. Luck is no such thing."

"Then what is it?"

"When one speaks of luck, it means rising in the world even above the clouds. Besides, whatever one's wish may be, once Master Tokoyo-shin'ō, the commander here, consents, even that sideways-crawling, shit-sagging-loined Kanihiko became a superior. How about it, do you understand now, the karma of luck?"

"Ah, is that so; if one says 'un,' one can rise in the world. Then I shall make you the superior, the attendant flanker of Tokoyo-shin'ō: un, un, un!"

"What is this, letting out a voice as if you had entered the privy and straddled to strain? What good is such an 'un'?"

Just as they were indulging in such idle chatter, there were those who came riding briskly toward them, straddling scores of horses.

"Hey, some envoy or other has come again from Castle Rocky. We cannot stay like this; get inside quickly and shut the gate."

So the two tumbled inside the gate in confusion, rattled the bolt into place,

Takahiko: "Now, this is the opening of luck. Once they say to open this gate, we shall say 'un' and open it."

Kurahiko: "Hey, hey, there is no value in opening it so easily. If you wish to rise in the world like Kanihiko, try holding out with all your might."

Just then, Sakakuniwake appeared before the gate, leading a great many subordinates,

"I am Sakakuniwake, envoy of the maiden deity Izanami-no-Ōkami of the Rocky Mountain mansion and of Commander Hinode-no-Kami of Castle Rocky. I have urgent business with Tokoyo-shin'ō; open this gate without a moment's delay!"

The two: "Well now, they have arrived, they have arrived; at last it is the opening of luck. This is why they say to endure being a hard gatekeeper. Even a dog walking about hits upon a stick."

Thus they rejoiced without understanding the circumstances. From outside the gate, in a loud voice:

"Hey, why do you not open this gate? Are the gatekeepers asleep?"

Takahiko: "Ho, envoy of Rocky Mountain or whatever you may be, how could these gatekeepers, charged with an important duty, be fellows who sleep in broad daylight? No matter how noble an envoy you may be, the opening and shutting of this gate is the gatekeepers' right. Do not shout over our heads; it will do you no good."

Kurahiko: "Hey, can't you produce more of a raspy voice? With such a stupid voice like blowing into a bamboo tube, there will be no scraps for us, you bottomless fool."

From outside the gate,

"Open quickly! The hour draws near!"

Thus they banged repeatedly. The two said,

"Hey, at any rate, we shall decide after opening it,"

and unfastened the bar, flinging open the front gate to the left and right. Sakakuniwake rode through the gate on horseback,

"Ho! Everyone, guard the iron gates of Tokoyo Castle to the east, west, south, and north! Do not overlook a single person!"

Leaving these words behind, he marched steadily toward the middle gate. Takahiko and Kurahiko chased after him, clinging desperately to the horse's tail.

Takahiko: "Excuse me, Lord Sakakuniwake, you cannot pass through the middle gate without permission."

Sakakuniwake: "Utterly insolent toward the imperial envoy! Stand back!"

Horse: "Neigh, snort, snort!"

Kurahiko: "Hey, you make a fool of us, making us smell foul gas! Eating three shō of waste beans a day and wearing two pairs of sandals bought for sixteen mon, you fart at Lord Kurahiko and try to make a pun on loud-sounding Takahiko! Excuse me, Lord Envoy, when placing an order, advance payment is required. If you tell us to open the middle gate, we also have an order of our own."

While this was happening, the middle gate smoothly swung open. Sakakuniwake attempted to pass through the middle gate still mounted on his horse. Takahiko and Kurahiko raised frantic voices:

"Hey, do not enter this gate without permission! Dismount from your horse and carriage! Get down!"

Though they shouted at him, Sakakuniwake, together with several retainers, paid no heed whatsoever and advanced deeper and deeper inside.

Takahiko: "In the end, with stubborn persistence, he has gone entirely inside."

Kurahiko: "The authority of the gatekeepers is truly a hollow thing. Just as you say, Kurahiko may become Teruyamahiko, and you may become Takatoriwake. Well, do not make such a worried face. Look, look! Because we did not keep the gate shut, three palanquins are entering without our permission. They might be the monsters of pine, bamboo, and plum, just like last winter."

"This is the path leading to promotion. It was like that last year as well, was it not? Three women came, and next the strong Teruhiko arrived and ran amok, and after that, three beautiful women entered again, didn't they? Thanks to the turmoil at that time, that fellow Kanihiko has now become a splendid general of the three armies. Splendid, splendid!"

Saying this, he shut the gate with a slam, dropped the bar, and said,

"Come, come, now begins the next act. Until another strong fellow breaks through and enters, you must not open it!"

At this moment, from out of nowhere came a voice like a broken bell:

"Takahiko of the long tengu nose, Kurahiko blind in the heart's eye, your fortune will not open at this rate! Uwahahahaha!"

The two spontaneously turned toward the voice and were struck with amazement.

倉彦『ヤア、馬鹿にしやがる、臭い屁を嗅がしよつて、日に三升のくづ豆喰ひ、十六文で二足の履穿きよつて、この倉彦さまに屁をくらはし、音高彦さまとは洒落てけつかる。モシモシ御上使、物を註文する時には前金が要りますぜ。あなたが中門を開けと仰有るなら、此方にも註文がある』

かくする内、中門はサラリと開いた。逆国別は乗馬のまま中門を潜らむとする。高彦、倉彦は頓狂な声を出して、

『ヤア、この門みだりに入るべからず。下馬下乗だツ、下れツ』

と呶鳴りつけるを、逆国別は数人の家来と共に、委細かまはず奥へ奥へと進み入る。

高彦『たうとう我慢の強い、這入つて仕舞ひよつた』

倉彦『門番の権威もよい加減なものだなア。貴様の云ふ通り、倉彦が照山彦で、貴様が鷹取別になるかも知れないぞ。まア、そんな心配らしい顔をすな。ヨウヨウ、門を閉め置かないものだから、吾々に無断で駕籠が三つも這入つて来よる。また昨年の冬のやうに、松、竹、梅の化物かも知れないぞ』

『これが出世の導きだ。昨年もさうだつたらう。三人の女が来て、次に強い照彦がやつて来て暴れよつて、その後へまた三人の綺麗な女が這入つて来ただろう。その時の騒動のお蔭で、蟹彦の奴、今は立派な三軍の将となりよつたのだ。うまいうまい』

と云ひながら門をぴしやりと閉め、閂をおろし、

『サアサア、これから次の幕だ。また強い奴が破つて這入つて来るまで、開けてはならないぞ』

この時何処ともなく、破鐘のやうな声がして、

『天狗の鼻高彦、心の目倉彦、今に運が開かぬぞよ。ウワハヽヽヽ』

二人は思はず声する方に向つて仰天したり。
