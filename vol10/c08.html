Chapter 8: The Inscrutability of Good and Evil [438]

第8章　善悪不可解〔438〕

§ 1

Tōyamawake, a treasured retainer of Takatoriwake who intimidated doves, sparrows, bulbuls, and spinners, having served as an envoy to the Land of Hazama and traversed three hundred ri each way through mountains, rivers, and wilderness, at last triumphantly mounted his steed from the mansion of the haughty Yamahiko with the three persons—Tsuki, Yuki, and Hana—crossed the Takano River swelling with muddy currents, appeared before the gate,

and was calling out, 'Tōyamawake has returned to the castle! Gatekeepers, open this gate quickly!'

Kanihiko said, 'Ah, how noisy! From the inner quarters they say, "Come here a moment," and from outside the gate, "Open up!" This is why it is troublesome to be a popular man. My sleeve is pulled from that side, and pulled from this side, and when I go home my wife grumbles at me; what a nuisance. Ah, it is a hard thing to be an attractive man. Hey, Akaguma, I order you to act as gatekeeper; I am going to the inner quarters to rest.'

Akaguma replied, 'Do not jest. From this very moment, I, Akaguma, am the distinguished official named Nakayoriwake. Hey, Kanihiko, I order you to be the gatekeeper.'

'What nonsense are you spouting?'

Saying this, both men slid open the inner gate with a rattle and stepped into the inner hall.

Takatoriwake said in a nasal voice:

'Furafura, honohoa, honpakagirino, hanhirakanaika, hanahiho, hakafumajyanaika, hagareorō.'

Kanihiko said, 'Hear, hear, Nakayoriwake gives an order. Snub-nosed Takatoriwake, serve as the gatekeeper. Hey, Teruyamahiko, you likewise are promoted to gatekeeper. Be grateful.'

Teruyamahiko said, 'Hey, Kanihiko, Akaguma, have you lost your minds? Have you run mad? Pull yourselves together.'

Kanihiko answered, 'Wahahaha, I have not run mad. Nor have I become senile. Obey what I say without a moment's delay, become gatekeepers, and firmly guard the front gate. Nay, Lord Takeyamahiko, from this day forth I am of the same rank as you; I look forward to our pleasant acquaintance hereafter.'

Takeyamahiko replied, 'These are indeed gracious words; I look forward to your favor.'

Takatoriwake, twitching his dazed face, said:

'Well now, this is utterly incomprehensible. Heaven turns into earth, earth becomes heaven, mountains become seas, seas become mountains, mulberry fields turn into lakes, and graveyards turn into playhouses. How completely incomprehensible this is!'

Takeyamahiko said, 'Hey, Takatoriwake, Teruyamahiko, what are you dawdling about? Will you not open the front gate quickly? Nakayoriwake, why do you not leave this place?'

「遠山別が帰城した。門番は早くこの門を開放せよ」

と叫んでいる。

蟹彦「ええい、やかましいわい。奥からは『ちょっと来てくれ』、門の外からも『開けてくれ』、これだから人気者になるのも困るわ。あちらからも袖を引かれ、こちらからも袖を引かれ、家に帰れば妻に愚痴を言われて困ったことだ。ああ、色男も辛いものだなあ。おい赤熊、その方に門番を申し付ける。自分は奥へ行って休息する」

赤熊「冗談を言うな。この赤熊は今日ただいまより中依別と申す高位の役人様だ。やあ蟹彦、その方に門番を申し付ける」

「何をぬかすのだ」

と言いながら、二人は中門をガラリと開けて奥殿へと進み入る。

鷹取別が鼻声で、

「フラフラ、ホノホハ、ホンパカギリノ、ハンヒラカナイカ、ハニヒホ、ハカフマヂヤナイカ、ハガレオロー」

蟹彦「やや、中依別が申し付ける。鼻ぺちゃの鷹取別は門番を務めよ。よう照山彦、その方も同様に門番に昇級させる。ありがたく思え」

照山彦「おい蟹彦、赤熊、その方たちは気が狂ったのか。血迷ったか。しっかりしろ」

蟹彦「わっはっはっはっは、血迷いもしない。呆けてもいない。自分の言うことを一刻も早く聞き入れ、門番となって表門を堅く守れ。いやなに、竹山彦殿、今日からは貴殿と同僚である。今後は親しくお付き合い願いたい」

竹山彦「これはこれは恐れ入ったご挨拶、何分よろしくお願いいたします」

鷹取別は呆けた顔をしゃくり上げながら、

「はてさて納得のいかぬことだわい。天が変じて地となり、地が天となり、山は海となり、海は山となり、桑畑が変化して湖水となり、墓場が変化して劇場となる。なんと合点のいかぬことでござるわい」

竹山彦「やあ鷹取別、照山彦、何をぐずぐずしているのか。早く表門を開けないか。中依別はなぜこの場を立ち去らないのか」

竹山彦『ヤア鷹取別、照山彦、何をグヅグヅ致して居るか、早く表門を開けぬか。中依別は何故この場を立ち去らぬか』

§ 2

Opening the front gate with a rattle before anyone knew it, Tōyamawake entered vigorously, appearing in this place while leading the three maidens,

"Well, I am the one who went on an envoy to the land of Hazama, successfully accomplished the official mission, achieved brilliant distinction and meritorious deeds, and have returned to the castle."

Kanihiko: "Well, is that you, Tōyamawake? Good work."

"What? You are Kanihiko. Even while being merely a gatekeeper, you not only dare enter the inner palace, but you also speak with such coarse language and insolence toward me as though treating a vassal; what do you think you are doing?"

"Well, I do not consider it anything at all, nor like a crab. Guide the three of Moon, Snow, and Flower to this place without a moment's delay."

"What? How strange, things have become incomprehensible. Well, Takatoriwake, what happened to your nose? Teruyamahiko, what happened to your head?"

"Ei, to hell with head and face! Bring out the princesses to this place at once! In any case, I shall conduct an inspection of the three maidens."

Saying this, he opened slightly the palanquin placed at the entrance, peered inside, and spoke in an astounded voice,

"Hey, Akaguma, I can say neither this nor that. I am completely dumbfounded."

Akaguma: "Is it Teruhiko again?"

Kanihiko: "Talking of shining or not shining, no, it is already a tremendous shining! Akitsukihime shining across the sky, Miyukihime so pure white as to be dazzling, Tachibanahime blooming and fragrant regardless of the four seasons; I too was startled and astounded almost to the point of being paralyzed."

"Hey, Kanihiko, they are merely three ordinary women; why are you trembling so fearfully?"

The three maidens appeared in this place calmly and composedly,

"Hey, Tōyamawake or whatever your name is, your welcome was appreciated. As a reward for that, from this very day and moment you are dismissed from the executive post of Tokoyo Castle and appointed a gatekeeper. Go down to the gatekeeper's quarters without a moment's delay."

At this time, from the inner palace, Tokoyo-shin'ō, along with the three court ladies Matsu, Take, and Ume, came and appeared in this place,

Tokoyo-shin'ō: "Well, Tōyamawake, well done, well done."

"Yes, please humbly forgive my having been so delayed. Finally, the three of Matsu, Take, and Ume—ah, no, no—the three maidens of Moon, Snow, and Flower, I have brought here. Please inspect them thoroughly."

From nowhere in particular, unknown as to whose deity's voice it was, from mid-air:

"Wah-ha-ha-ha-ha, oh-ho-ho-ho-ho! Tokoyo-shin'ō and everyone present, watch your feet!"

As the voice cried out thus, Tokoyo-shin'ō was startled by this voice and looked around; he had collapsed flat on the riding grounds of Tokoyo Castle, and all the other officials were also wriggling, covered in mud. Once again, there was a voice in mid-air:

"Yaa, kon-kon-chikichin, konchikichin, is it truly so from the very root? Konchikichin, kon-kon-chikichin, konchikichin!"

どこからともなく、どの神の声とも知れず、中空より、
