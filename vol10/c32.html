The country that drifted like a jellyfish having been finely Solidified and formed, Izanagi-no-Sume-ōkami Returned to the Mototsu-mikura Of the Country of the Sun; Kamu-Izanami-no-Ōkami Returned to the Mikuni of the Moon; Appointing Haya-Susanoo-no-Ōkami As the Tsukasa-gami of the vast ocean, Having Konohana-hime, the spirit of Idunome-no-Kami, And the Hinode-no-Kami protect the visible world, the hidden world, and the divine world, Heaven and earth were well governed, The sun and moon shone purely across, the wind was refreshing, The order of rains was moderate, Flourishing, it became the reign of Miroku; The eight million heavenly deities, The eight million earthly deities, The multitude of people, and the tens of millions of Plants, trees, and beasts, Moistened by the dew of blessing,

Their voices rejoicing and cheering Resounded throughout Takamanohara, It had become an auspicious divine age. In the war against the Yomotsu-ikusa, The eighty Magatsu vanished, And Kaminahobi, who made this world, And the broad-minded Ōnahobi, Correcting what was seen and heard according to the Nahobi, Being harmonious and close to one another, Under heaven there was neither strife Nor disease, nor aging, nor death, Yet while it was governed, in a mere moment, Like a steed passing through a gap, here and there, The Magatsumi of the raging deities, The evil deeds of the Yamata-no-Orochi, the hideous ogres, And the hideous foxes Arising, they stirred up and disturbed The Chiyahafuru Mikuni of the gods, And the hearts of the people of the world gradually Leaned toward the wrong direction,

Falling into turmoil and clamor, which is grave. Illumined by the true light Of the deeply benevolent Sume-kami, The Jizaiten of the Tokoyo-no-kuni, The venerable Ōkunihiko-no-Mikoto and Ōkunihime-no-Mikoto, Re-erected the true pillar of the soul, And remaining in the Yomi-no-kuni And Tokoyo-no-kuni according to their duties, Although they protect the deities and humans of the four quarters, Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime, And Uralhiko, descendant of the gods, And Uralhime, without learning their lesson, Falsely claiming to be Banko-shin'ō, Established a capital in the field of Armenia At the foot of the Ural Mountains, And drawing together the Sano-me, Shikome, and various Eighty Magatsu, Again disturbing this world, which cannot be helped.

The missionary of Shinonome-wake who illuminates the darkness, Higashihiko, transformed into Ishikoridome-no-Kami and advanced into the wilderness at the foot of the Altai Mountains. Here flowed a considerably large river. This was called the Uchi River. Among those who crossed this river, almost ninety-nine out of a hundred lost their lives, so it was alternatively called the River of Death or the River of Demons. Seeking to attempt missionary work in Armenia, Ishikoridome-no-Kami crossed the Altai Mountains, traversed the wilderness of Kusu, crossed Lake Akashi and the Biwa Sea, passed through the southern foot of the Caucasus Mountains, and hastened the journey to go to Armenia.

Ishikoridome-no-Kami finally arrived at the bank of this River of Demons. There was neither a bridge nor a boat. In addition, muddy currents were surging. By chance, enormous timbers came floating down one after another from upstream and lay across the river, naturally forming a floating bridge. At this time, four or five men stood by the riverside, gazing at this scene and absorbed in conversation.

A: "In this river muddy water flows continuously, and if anyone tries to cross to the other side, each and every one loses their life in the middle of the river; yet today, rare timbers have come floating down in great numbers, and a natural bridge has formed—what do you think? Three years ago, when that bridge was formed, we took advantage of the good fruits on this side and barely managed to cross over, but then the bridge was washed away, making it impossible to return, and we had resigned ourselves to the thought that we would never in our lifetimes return to our homes across the river; yet today, for some reason, a bridge has been spanned. Taking advantage of this opportunity, shall we not return?"

B: "Now wait, we must think it over. Life is precious, and we have only one. It would not do to become the sea-drift of this demonic river."

C: "What, who cares? My beloved wife and brothers are waiting with anxiety, so leaving fortune to heaven, shall I try crossing?"

D: "They say that upstream an evil deity, a retainer of Uralhiko, resides, and this demonic god is guarding the river so that the so-called missionaries of Anaikyō cannot cross it. We are neither of the Ural religion nor of Anaikyō. Various deities appear and fight from both sides, so our nuisance knows no bounds."

A: "Oh, that mention of Anaikyō reminds me: the deity called Uralhiko said that if a missionary of Anaikyō comes, we should seize them and bring them before the fortress of Mount Altai; if we do so, he will build a bridge across this river and grant us many rewards. So if a missionary of Anaikyō should come to such a place, let us capture them and make an achievement of it."

B: "If such a convenient thing were to happen it would be fine, but for Pindola-types like us, it is completely out of the question. Even Tanabata-sama has a tryst once a year, yet separated by the river for three years like this, looking at each other's faces across the river for a long time, we are unfortunate beings suffering such frustrating hardships; so we might perhaps meet by the time of our grandchildren's generation."

As they were talking together thus, Ishikoridome-no-Kami came walking nonchalantly forward, singing a propagation song of Anaikyō. The whole group pricked up their ears at this voice and tilted their heads,

A: "Oh, speak of the devil and his shadow appears, or slander rather than summon—this is just that. It seems to be the song of a missionary of Anaikyō. Hey, everyone, shall we hide our bodies completely in the sand of this riverbank, stick only our necks out, and consider the situation?"

The whole company hid completely from their necks down into the light, soft sand that was like ash, and lying prostrate, they listened to the song of propagation. Ishikoridome-no-kami casually stepped forward to this riverbank, and looking upon the surface of the river, saw that numerous logs had fallen horizontally, forming a natural bridge.

"Ho, the grace of the deities is truly a splendid thing. In truth, this Uchi River is called the river of death or the river of demons, and cannot possibly be crossed. I had heard that not more than one person in a hundred crosses this river successfully, but today, what fortunate circumstance this is! This, too, is entirely the protection of the deities of the Anahikyo. Ah, when I think of this, I feel as though the light of the future shines forth brilliantly. At any rate, in order to give thanks for the vast and boundless divine favor, let me recite the divine word once here, boldly sing the song of propagation, and then cross over."

Thus soliloquizing, she began to recite the divine word.

The sun was sinking toward the western mountains, casting its light upon the river water. With the beginning of the voice chanting the norito, there was the strangeness of groans being heard from the nearby riverbank.

When Ishikoridome-no-kami casually looked in the direction of the voice, four or five black, round objects were groaning with murmurs for some reason.

"Ho, this is the doing of the evil deities under the command of Uraruhiko. Most likely demons have disguised themselves. It looks somewhat like a watermelon patch... black, round objects have started groaning with murmurs. Well then, shall I ascertain their true identity?"

So saying, she directed her steps toward the soft sandy plain where knees would sink, and grabbing the black, round lump, she saw that it was the head of a native. Looking at it, it rolled its eyes, opened its mouth, and said:

"Ah, ah, are you an envoy of propagation of the Anahikyo? This river is called the river of demons, and all who cross it lose their lives. There is no knowing how much the people of this land suffer because of the existence of the Anahikyo. From now on, we will gather together, seize you, and take you to the fortress of the evil deities on Mount Altai, so be prepared. Even though it looks as if a bridge has been built like this, this bridge is a monster. We too wish to return to the opposite bank, but we cannot cross without a gift. Hey, everyone, come out and seize this fellow! She has grabbed the hair of my head and will not let go, so there is nothing I can do!"

At this voice, the heads of the four men suddenly rose up from the sand and surrounded Ishikoridome from front and rear, left and right.

§ 4

All in unison, "Ah, long-awaited missionary of Ananaikyō, come now, submissively put your hands behind your back!"

"What on earth are you? Are you humans who dwell in the sand? You are fellows like ochiyobo bugs or bembeko bugs. Do not act like such fools. I am a missionary of Ananaikyō. I shall cross this river, advance to Armenia, subdue the evil deity Uralhiko, and save you from your distress. Do not worry."

All in unison, "Worry not, what nonsense! We are ones who dive freely through the sand. If a fellow is weak, we intend to seize him, present him to the god Uralhiko, and receive a reward; but if by any chance you are an unmanageable mighty person, we will dive and hide in the sand, so you will not be able to do anything to us!"

"What? You are fellows like moles, fire rats, or earthworms. Diving through sand—that is interesting. Won't you show me that trick once for the amusement of my journey? Submissively dive into the sand. Do not botch it."

"Do not speak flippantly! You are the one who should submissively put your hands behind your back; do not make a blunder and regret it later!"

So saying, they grabbed sand and hurled it with all their might aiming at both eyes of Ishikoridome no Kami. While closing her eyes, Ishikoridome no Kami inadvertently let go of one man. The five stood up all at once, and

"Now that it has come to this, we are quite safe! Will you not quickly do as we say?"

Ishikoridome, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand."

"Oh, this is unbearable! My head aches, my eyes spin, dive, dive!"

As they ran while concealing their entire bodies and causing the sand to swell like moles, it looked like waves. Ishikoridome grasped sand in both hands, formed dumplings, and blew her breath upon them, whereupon they instantly condensed and became balls of stone. When she hurled those balls pop-pop aiming at the waves of sand, the natives, unable to endure it, abruptly revealed their sand-covered bodies, pressed their palms together, and

"P-p-p-pardon, pardon!"

Thus prostrating upon the sand, they apologized profusely.

"Hey, moles, I will forgive you, so come forth before me. What are you timidly fearing? There is nothing fearful at all."

"Yes, truly, c-c-can we be spared?"

"Even in the slightest, one who is a missionary of Ananaikyō speaks no falsehood at all. Submissively gather before me. I shall tell you something good."
