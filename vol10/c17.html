Chapter 17: Disheveled Tresses [447]

第17章　乱れ髪〔447〕

§ 1

Without any sign of hesitation, Katayamahiko accompanied Odoyamadzumi and entered deep into the inner palace. At this time, Sakakuniwake appeared at the entrance and said,

"Ho, Lord Katatorahiko, I had heard that you, by order of the Tokoyo Divine King, led an army and departed for the front in the land of 'Me.' Most of our allies have marched to Yomotsujima, and now both Castle Rocky and Tokoyo Castle are extremely weakly defended. Yet, instead of returning to Tokoyo Castle, you have come here; there must be some particular reason. Please relate it in detail."

Katayamahiko said, "You are Sakakuniwake; there is a deep reason for this. In any case, report that Katatorahiko of Tokoyo Castle has captured alive and brought back Odoyamadzumi, the missionary of the Ananai teachings."

Sakakuniwake said,

"Please wait for a moment. I shall inform the God of Hinode and receive his instructions,"

and turned on his heels to enter the interior. Without being guided, the two kicked off their shoes and straw sandals at the entrance, entered a room, and were resting their breath, when the God of Hinode, accompanied by four or five attendants, solemnly appeared in this place and said,

"Ho, Katatorahiko, on what business have you come?"

"Because there are deep circumstances to this, please dismiss other people for a while."

"Everyone, withdraw from this place, return to the living quarters, and take rest."

"Yes,"

replied everyone, and they departed from this place.

The God of Hinode said, "Nay, are you not the missionary of the Ananai teachings?"

Odoyamadzumi said, "Indeed. I am Shikokuniwake, who formerly served you; now, feigning to be a missionary of the Ananai teachings, I am one spying on the enemy's condition. However resourceful and versatile you, Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko, may be, there must be things you foresee from afar. Having deliberately entered the Ananai teachings to repay former favors and having returned after detecting everything and all matters, do not doubt me by any means, and speak with an open heart. Although you call yourself the God of Hinode, the fact that your true identity is Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko no Mikoto of peerless divine power leaves not an iota of room for doubt. Furthermore, that she who is called the Great Deity Izanami is your consort, Ōkunihime, is completely clear. Such being the case, without concealing anything, please tell all the plans in detail."

"As you have deduced, I am Daijizaiten. You too must have been astonished at my divine schemes and ghostly strategies."

"We are people who go so far as to disguise ourselves as missionaries of the Ananai teachings and undergo hardships; your plans are almost fully understood by us. Just now, this Katatorahiko, obeying the command of the Tokoyo Divine King Hirokuniwake, led numerous troops and advanced to the land of 'Me' to capture me, but he at last perceived what was in my heart, finally felt relieved, disclosed everything, and guided me to the main castle—a true hero and outstanding figure, whose deed is truly admirable. Please bestow words of high praise upon him."

"Nay, I am deeply impressed by the sincere devotion of both of you. However, what has become of the missionaries Matsu, Take, and Ume, and Kagatora, whom you brought with you?"

At these words, both of them choked with hesitation, and said,

"Those four have matters of consideration, and we have hidden them away in a certain place. We shall show them to your eyes later."

"I wish to meet them as soon as possible. Report their whereabouts to me right here and now. I will dispatch suitable persons to welcome them back to the main castle. As long as the whereabouts of the four missionaries remain unknown, there is ample room to suspect you. Make known their whereabouts quickly."

Katayamahiko: "We beg for a reprieve of four or five days."

"If, as you say, it is truly so that you have been secretly active for our sake until now, there is no reason why their whereabouts should be unknown. That you cannot reply must be an evil plot whereby you feign submission and, uniting your hearts, seek to overthrow the lightly defended Rocky Castle. Depending on your answer, I can scarcely show leniency. Come, tell me quickly."

Thus questioned severely with a somewhat raised voice, having had Kagatora and the three maidens vanish into smoke in the mountains, not knowing their whereabouts, suffering over their reply, changing color, and fretting in their hearts, 'Now we have failed,' just at that moment, Teruhiko, who came forward opening the middle gate, suddenly transformed into the appearance of Kagatora and entered, accompanied by the three—Tsuki, Yuki, and Hana—and,

"I am Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko's, and now Hinode-no-kami's, former retainer Kagatora. Disguised as Tsuki, Yuki, and Hana, propagating the Ananai teachings and leading astray the realm, I have arrested the female missionaries Matsu, Take, and Ume, and have brought them here to this place. As quickly as possible, Hinode-no-kami, pray conduct the inspection!"

so he cried out.

Hinode-no-kami, as well as Katayamahiko and Otoyama-tsumi, looked at one another's faces with expressions as if struck by a bolt from the blue, holding back in silence. Teruhiko, accompanied by the three maidens, boldly made his appearance in this place, and said,

"Ah, well, well, Lord Ōkunihiko, I am your former retainer Kagatora. At last, having sought and found the three missionaries, I have come here. The two who have appeared here, Katatorahiko and Otoyama-tsumi, ought also to know this matter well. I humbly beg for your detailed examination."

No sooner had he spoken than he removed the veils of the three maidens, whereupon everyone involuntarily,

"Ah!"

「そうだ、人は見かけによらないものだ。醜国別が淤縢山津見となって三五教の裏をかき、広国別が常世神王となってこちらの裏をかき、天教山に通謀するのも同じ道理だ。敵の中にも味方があり、味方の中にも敵があるとはこのことだなあ」

「私を信じてくださいますか」

固山彦「我々が日の出神であったなら、簡単には信じないな。ハハハハハ」

淤縢山津見「固虎さま、あまり口が過ぎますよ。あなた、そんな顔をしていて、心の底では天教山の三五教に通謀しているのでしょう。アハハハハ」

日出神「何だか訳が分からなくなってきた。狐につままれたようだなあ」
