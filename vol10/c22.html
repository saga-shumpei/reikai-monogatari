Chapter 22: Murky Prodigies and Apparitions [452]

第22章　混々怪々〔452〕

§ 1

While the mountain winds of the Rocky Mountain blew fiercely with hideous demonic winds, Ōkunihime-no-Kami was waiting impatiently for the victory of her allies in the battle at Yomotsujima, and for the arrival of Miyamawake and Kunitamahime. Just at that time, a Heavenly Rock-Boat, roaring across the vast sky, descended in circles here and there. Ōikazuchi and Honoikazuchi, resounding loudly, appeared at the castle gate of the Rocky Mountain, ordered the gatekeeper from outside the gate to open the iron gates to the left and right, and, breathless and rushed, advanced straight toward the inner palace.

Taketorawake, a senior vassal of the Rocky Mountain, stepped forward, and no sooner had he seen the two generals, Ōikazuchi and Honoikazuchi, than he said:

"Oh, unexpected is the return of you two gods to the castle! How stands the victory or defeat of our allies in the battle of Yomotsujima? Speak quickly!"

Ōikazuchi: "That we have hurried back midway is for no other matter. In the battle of Yomotsujima, our allies have suffered an almost total defeat; if left as it is, the enemy general Hinode-no-Kami, leading numerous divine forces, let alone Yomotsujima, will invade the Eternal Land, occupy the Rocky Mountain, and cast us down into the Nethermost Land in the twinkling of an eye. You must quickly report this matter to the Great Goddess."

Honoikazuchi: "If time is lost, it will be a grave matter; not even the blink of an eye can be delayed. Hurry, hurry!"

—thus he urged.

Taketorawake made no reply, but pushed open the partition sliding doors just as he was, and rushed hastily toward the inner palace. Left behind, the two waited impatiently, dumbfounded, craning their necks for Ōkunihime to appear; but at this moment, a somewhat clamorous sound was heard in front of the gate. As the two listened intently to the noise, the three beauties—Kunitamahime, Kizukihime, and Taitohime—calmly entered the scene, leading numerous goddesses. Ōikazuchi involuntarily called out to them:

"Hey! Despite being in the very midst of battle, at a moment of life and death, have you three abandoned the battlefield and returned like cowards? Surely there must be some deep reason for this; tell it in full detail!"

Kunitamahime: "Ah-ha-ha-ha, o-ho-ho-ho!"

Kizukihime: "U-fu-fu-fu, e-he-he-he!"

Taitohime: "I-hi-hi-hi, ho-ho-ho-ho!"

At the lighthearted laughter of the three, who raised disagreeable voices in unison as if giving no thought to the defeat of the army, Ōkunihime-no-Mikoto and Taketorawake hastily emerged, and:

Ōkunihime: "Hold! Are you not Ōikazuchi and Honoikazuchi? In this battle that decides the fate of the realm, while friend and foe are exerting all their strength and locked in fierce combat, that you return in the very midst of it is incomprehensible, and utterly suspicious. Furthermore, what is the meaning of that laughter from Kunitamahime and the other two?"

When she, blushing slightly, questioned him, Ōikazuchi opened his great mouth wide and said:

"Ohohoho, what a humble greeting! Why on earth would Ōikazuchi, who seizes and devours demons, giant serpents, and wolves, come back meekly in shame? There is no reason to come back timidly with bent waist and curled tail while leading a great host of soldiers. Humbly speaking, this Ōikazuchi is the transformed avatar of Onitakehiko, the messenger of Hinode-no-kami. You cannot know my true identity. How comical is your face, completely flustered! Ōkunihime-no-mikoto too, setting aside the awe-inspiring Izanami-no-kami and falsely claiming to be Izanami-no-ōkami, is the ringleader of Ōmagatsu who deceives this world; shall I leave you as you are? Hey, stop such foolish tricks at once. Presumptuously, you are from the home of the evil deity of the Rocky Mountains. Opposing the unshakeable Hinode-no-kami is a plot far beyond your station; henceforth obey the teachings of Anaikyō and do not devise such terrifying schemes. What! Putting on the face of an Otāfuku, putting on airs as the false deity of Ōkunihime and calling yourself Izanami-no-mikoto—even my buttocks are flabbergasted! If you fall into Ne-no-kuni Soko-no-kuni and meet terrifying torments, no matter how tomboyish you may be, you are merely a weak-willed woman; chased around with demons' iron clubs and axes, you will have nowhere to flee. Rather than shamelessly suffering humiliation in Ne-no-soko-no-kuni, repent in your heart right now, abandon your sinister plans, and obey Oyagami-sama. If you do so, your sins will gradually be forgiven. Taking me for Ōikazuchi was a huge mistake; it is the myriad transformational activities of Onitakehiko! Ahahaha!"

Honoikazuchi: "Hohohoho, with a dazed face, shedding tears in drops—what kind of wretched sight is that? Cast away all your evil schemes up to now cleanly, as if swept away with a broom. You, who easily succeeded in disguising yourself as Izanami-no-kami and smirked, thinking everything was fine—no matter how much you are infatuated with and boast of your own strength, or spout arrogant big talk, the Yomishima you desire will never fall into your hands; it is like a loincloth made of thin cord. Missing here and missing there, no rice cake will fall from the shelf; it would be best if you repented from the very root; holding that big Hotei-belly, whatever you scheme will only end in sheer contradiction, like the cuckoo bird. Vomiting blood through eight thousand and eight cries, writhing in agony, far from receiving praise, you will become the empty shell of a conch horn, and shameful things will happen such that you will have to dig a hole and crawl into it, shedding tears in drops, abandoned by the deities of heaven and earth, and an irreversible disaster will occur. If you are to repent, now is the time. Hohohoho, calling me Honoikazuchi is an outright lie; I am Homusubi-no-kami. Take a good look at my face! Ahhahahaha!"
