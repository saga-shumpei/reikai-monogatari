Chapter 28: Exposition of the Word-Soul: Part Two [458]

第28章　言霊解二〔458〕

§ 1

"The name of the deity who came into being from the skirt He next cast off is Tokiokashi-no-Kami."

Regarding the kotodama of the skirt (mimo), mo signifies the lower. It means the education of the common people, and signifies social language.

Tokiokashi-no-Kami signifies the leader of novels, theater, song and dance, performing arts, popular songs, and the like. This also means that these are to be fundamentally reformed, and thus it means that He casts off the deity who became the skirt.

"The name of the deity who came into being from the upper garment He next cast off is Wazurahinoushi-no-Kami."

The kotodama of the upper garment (migoromo) means the house of the body. The human fleshly body is the dwelling place and the upper garment of the soul. Because the five kinds of medical arts—medicine, bathing, prevention, disposal, and avoidance—do not conform to the medical law of the Imperial Country, have no efficacy in curing disease, and rather become harmful poisons, it means that He abolishes contemporary medical methods, and this is why it is said that He casts off the upper garment. Wazurahinoushi-no-Kami signifies the deity who heals sickness and affliction. Having seen through that all medical and pharmaceutical arts are contrary to the divine laws of the Imperial Country, He cast off His upper garment in order to fundamentally reform them. It is the same as the present situation where both modern Western medicine and Chinese herbal medicine must be abolished and the medicine unique to the Divine Land must be adopted.

"The name of the deity who came into being from the trousers He next cast off is Chimata-no-Kami."

The kotodama of the trousers (hakama) signifies running and driving about; in short, hakama refers to organs of transportation and communication. Today's trains have become dangerous cars and demon cars, and electric trains, automobiles, steamboats, airships, mail, telegraph, telephone, and the like greatly need to be improved. In short, there is a need for fundamental reform of conventional transportation and communication facilities; therefore, that modern methods must once undergo a great change is what is meant by casting off the trousers.

Chimata-no-Kami means the deity of railways, sea routes, and roads; chimata refers to the state in which organs of transportation and communication extend in all directions. As for Japan, present railways, roads, mail, telegraph, and so forth have come to require massive improvement. It is the same as the present reality, wherein these must be expanded to provide convenience for the people.

"The name of the deity who came into being from the crown He next cast off is Akiguhi-no-Ushi-no-Kami."

The kotodama of the above means the Three Ducal Ministers, court nobles, court officials, Shinto priests, and the like. In terms of today's world, it signifies the peerage, Shinto priests, Ministers of State, high-ranking officials, and the like. That these too are resolutely to be improved is what is meant by casting off the crown. Is the present age not indeed a time that requires a great reformation?

「次に投げ棄てる御裳から成り出でられた神の御名は、時置師神である。」

御裳の言霊は、モは下である。平民教育の意味であり、社交的な言葉の意味である。

時置師神は、小説や演劇や歌舞や芸技や俗歌などの頭株ということである。これも根本的に革正されるということで、御裳に成る神を投げ棄てなさるということであります。

「次に投げ棄てる御衣から成り出でられた神の御名は、和豆良比能宇斯神である。」

御衣の言霊は、身の家ということである。人の肉体は霊魂の住居であり御衣であります。薬・浴・防・棄・避の五種の医術も、皇国の医法に適さず、病気治療の効き目がなく、かえって害毒となることによって、現代の医法を廃止なさるということで、御衣を投げ棄てなさるというのである。ワヅラヒノウシ神とは、病み煩いを癒やす神というのである。すべての医術・薬法が、皇国の神法に背反していることを見破って、根本的に革正なさるために、御衣を投げ棄てなさったのであります。現代の西洋医学も漢方医学も、これを廃止して神国固有の医学を採用しなければならなくなってきているのと同じことであります。

「次に投げ棄てる御袴から成り出でられた神の御名は道俣神である。」

御袴の言霊は、走り駆け巡るということで、要するに交通機関や通信機関を指してハカマと言うのである。今日の汽車は、危車となり鬼車となり、電車、自動車、汽船、飛行船、郵便、電信、電話なども大いに改良すべき必要がある。要するに従来の交通や通信機関に対して根本的革正の必要があり、ゆえにいったん現代の方法を大きく変更すべきことを、御袴を投げ棄てると言うのであります。

道俣神とは、鉄道や航路や道路の神ということである。交通と通信機関が四通八達している状況を指して道俣と言うのである。日本において言えば、現今の鉄道や道路や郵便や電信なども、大々的に改良しなければならなくなっている。これを拡張し、それによって国民の便宜を図らねばならない今日の現状であるのと同じことであります。

「次に投げ棄てる御冠から成り出でられた神の御名は飽咋之宇斯神である。」

右の言霊は、三公や、公卿や、殿上人や、神官などを言う意味である。今日の世で言えば、華族や、神官や、国務大臣や、高等官などを言う意味である。これも断固として改善するということが、御冠を投げ棄てるということである。現代は実に一大改革を必要とする時期ではないでしょうか。

§ 2

"Next, the names of the deities brought forth from the bracelet cast off from His left hand are Okizakaru-no-Kami, next Okitsunagisabiko-no-Kami, and next Okitsukaibera-no-Kami."

Speaking of the left hand, the left represents the superior rank and the government officials. Okizakaru-no-Kami represents the army. Okitsunagisabiko-no-Kami represents the navy. Okitsukaibera-no-Kami represents the weapons of the army and navy. Because conventional military laws and tactics are utterly useless, He casts away the bracelet of His left hand in order to apply grand-scale improvements, rely upon the military methods of the divine army, and carry out actions to realize the true nature of the Land of Abundant Fine Spears.

"Next, the names of the deities brought forth from the bracelet cast off from His right hand are Hezakaru-no-Kami, next Hetsunagisabiko-no-Kami, and next Hetsukaibera-no-Kami."

The right represents the lower position, the common people, and the earth. Hezakaru-no-Kami represents agriculture. Hetsunagisabiko-no-Kami represents industry and commerce. Hetsukaibera-no-Kami represents the machinery and implements to be used in agriculture, industry, and commerce. Since these also require a great reform, casting away the deities brought forth from the bracelet of His right hand signifies reforming the previous policies.

"Regarding the aforementioned, the twelve deities, from Kunado-no-Kami down to before Hetsukaibera-no-Kami, are deities born from His stripping off and casting away the articles worn upon His person."

These twelve deities represent the great divine work wherein, because the land had utterly turned into an unsightly and polluted country like the Land of Yomi, a grand-scale reform was executed through the great and resolute decision of the Great Deity, thereby opening the beginning of the Great Universal Purification.

"Thereupon, He declared, 'The upper rapids are swift, the lower rapids are weak,' and when He dived down into the middle rapids and washed for the first time, the names of the deities brought forth were Yasomagatsuhi-no-Kami, next Ōmagatsuhi-no-Kami; these two deities were brought forth on account of the impurities when He went to that polluted, overgrown land."

The upper rapids denote the so-called upper class of today, and the lower rapids denote the lower class. That the upper class, relying upon power and wealth, does not readily amend the unsightly conduct of putting the physical body above the spirit, but rather resists the divine revelations to the utmost, is what is meant by 'the upper rapids are swift.' The lower class has neither power nor wealth, and no matter how much they might wish to carry out the divine revelations, they suffer from their daily livelihood and fear the oppression of power, possessing no strength to carry out even a single divine work of reform. Therefore, it is said that 'the lower rapids are weak.' Thereupon, the Great Deity dives down into the middle rapids, which is the middle class, and accomplishes the divine work of the Great World Purification and Great Reform. Because those of the middle class are not destitute of their daily clothing and food, and possess reasonable scholarship and understanding along with the capability to become the backbone of the nation, the Deities took up dwelling in the body and soul of true persons of the middle class and commenced this one great divine work.

§ 3

The deities who appeared when the Ōkami cleansed and exorcised all the foulness and defilements of the universe were the two deities, Yasomagatsuhi-no-Kami and next Ōmagatsuhi-no-Kami. Man is a microcosm of the universe. Because both the world and the human body are made in the identical mold, it was not spoken of here as the universe, but was demonstrated by being compared to the person of Izanagi-no-Ōkami. Therefore, Zuigetsu also considers it convenient to explain this briefly from the standpoint of the human person.

Yasomagatsu-no-Kami is a demon that exists outside our bodies, obstructs our path, takes large-scale opposing actions, and thereby seeks to profit itself. In fact, those like hack writers who dare to level various slanders and calumnies against Ōmoto and seek to gain excessive profits by publishing books are the so-called Yasomagatsu-no-Kami. When speaking of this from the standpoint of the nation, it corresponds to anti-Japanese boycotts, boycotts of Japanese goods, or the assault of enemy armies and navies. The deity who supervises, controls, and chastises these Yasomagatsu-no-Kami is called Yasomagatsuhi-no-Kami. Depending on whether the character 'hi' is added or not, the position shifts, just like that between a police officer and a criminal. Ōmagatsu-no-Kami is an evil spirit and depraved soul that infiltrates into our body and soul to cause us to commit wicked deeds and shameful conduct. Drowning in lust, turning wild with alcohol, and committing evil deeds and misconduct are all the doing of Ōmagatsu-no-Kami.

When comparing this to the nation, it refers to the fact that unpatriotic persons who plot conspiracies such as dangerous thought, anti-nationalism, the overthrow of the government, and civil war are latent, and that treacherous and atrocious figures with human faces and beasts' hearts, who seek to change politics into something resembling the body-primary and spirit-secondary state, are residing there. It is Ōmagatsuhi-no-Kami who subdues, chastises, and warns them.

Righteous: Yasomagatsuhi-no-Kami, Ōmagatsuhi-no-Kami

Evil: Yasomagatsu-no-Kami, Ōmagatsu-no-Kami

大神が宇宙一切の醜穢を祓除し玉うた時に出現せる神は、八十禍津日神、つぎに大禍津日神の二神であります。人は宇宙の縮図である。世界も人体も皆同一の型に出来て居るのであるから茲に宇宙と云はず、伊邪那岐大神の一身上に譬へて示されたのである。故に瑞月亦之を人身上より略解するを以て便利と思ふのであります。

八十禍津神は、吾人の身外に在りて吾人の進路を妨げ且つ大々的反対行動を取り、以て自己を利せむとするの悪魔である。現に大本に対して種々の中傷讒誣を敢へてし、且つ書物を発行して奇利を占めむとする三文蚊士の如きは、所謂八十禍津神であります。之を国家の上から言ふ時は、排日とか排貨とか敵国陸海軍の襲来とかに当るのである。この八十禍津神を監督し、制御し、懲戒し玉ふ神を八十禍津日神といふのであります。日の字が加はると加はらざるとに依つて、警官と罪人との様に位置が替るのであります。大禍津神は吾人の身魂内に潜入して、悪事醜行を為さしめむとする悪霊邪魂である。色に沈溺し、酒に荒み、不善非行を為すは皆大禍津神の所為であります。

之を国家の上に譬へる時は、危険思想、反国家主義、政府顛覆、内乱等の陰謀を為す非国民の潜在し、且つ体主霊従同様の政治に改めむとする、悪逆無道の人面獣心的人物の居住して居る事である。之を討伐し懲戒し警告するのは大禍津日神であります。
