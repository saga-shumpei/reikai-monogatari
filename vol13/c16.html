Chapter 16: Sporting with Jewels [542]

第16章　玉遊〔542〕

§ 1

Along the newly brightened path, the three men hastened their steps. Directly ahead, objects resembling red and white rubber balls were floating and spinning up and down, left and right. Spotting them with sharp eyes, Otohiko cried:

Otohiko: 'Hey, Komahiko, here is more sport! Look ahead: the demon gods of the cavern are playing billiards!'

Kamehiko: 'Nay, 'tis a baseball match! Wondrous indeed is this demon cavern; they play a clever game! Next we may perchance behold a dance performance as an extra entertainment! The atmosphere of civilization appeareth to have permeated not only mountain valleys, but even the subterranean depths of this cavern!'

Otohiko: 'Why not sit down a while to watch the spectacle, convene an Extraordinary Council of Officers, and decide whether we too should join the demons' ball game or not?'

Kamehiko: 'An Officers' Council of three is a slapdash affair! At all events, let us watch at our leisure! Behold how they multiply! Balls appear in boundless swarms: ten become twenty, twenty become forty, forty become eighty, eighty become a hundred and sixty, multiplying beyond number, wheeling in every direction!'

Otohiko: 'Hey, art thou practicing arithmetic in such a place as this?'

Kamehiko: 'Since I must submit an audit report to the Board of Audit, I am exerting my utmost best that nothing be omitted! A Board of Audit inspector leadeth a hard life!'

Otohiko: 'That is the concern of petty mortals hundreds of thousands of years hence! We are heroes and champions of the Age of the Gods! Cease borrowing future troubles and engage in present action!'

Kamehiko: 'Master Kame, who may well be called a treasure-house of wisdom, seeth ten thousand years into the future, and visions reflect unceasingly upon my clairvoyant sight! Such mystical matters are beyond your comprehension; yet to enlighten primitive folk of childish intellect is also one of my appointed duties!'

Komahiko: 'Because thou canst never wash away the stain of the Ural teachings, thou art ever eager to display materialistic knowledge! In the Age of the Gods, calculation is wholly unnecessary! The Deity beholdeth the formless, hearkeneth unto the voiceless, and counteth without calculation! Banish such worldly conceits!'

Kamehiko: 'Having inadvertently raised an unviable motion, I have become the focus of universal censure! Alas, it cannot be helped: I withdraw the bill!'

Otohiko: 'Withdrawal? We stand upon a tabula rasa! Such a foolish motion is pigeonholed forthwith!'

Kamehiko: 'Yet how wilt thou manage the Upper House? Hee-hee-hee!'

音彦『ヤア駒彦、又面白いぞ。先を見よ、魔窟の魔神が玉突をやつてるわ』

亀彦『オー、あれや野球戦だ、流石は魔窟だな、味な事をやりよるわ。此次には又ダンスの余興が見られるかも知れないぞ。文明の空気は山の谷々はおろか、斯様な地底の巌窟内迄もゆき亘つてゐるのだね』

音彦『マア一寸ここらで腰を下して見物し乍ら臨時将校会議を開いて、その結果吾々も魔神の打球会に参加するかせないかを決定したら如何だ』

亀彦『三人では将校会議も良い加滅なものだな。何は兎もあれ、ゆつくりと見物する事に仕様かい。ヤアヤア殖えるわ殖えるわ、沢山な毬が現はれた、十が二十になり、二十が四十になり、四十が八十になり、八十が百六十になり、百六十が三百二十になり……』

音彦『コラコラ、貴様はコンナ処で算術の稽古でもするのか』

亀彦『ヤア会計検査院へ決算報告をする必要があるから、遺漏ない様に十分のベストを尽して居るのだ。会計検査官も骨の折れたものだ』

音彦『それは数十万年後の豆人間のする事だ。吾々は神代の英雄豪傑だ。ソンナ取越苦労はやめて現実的活動をやらなくてはならぬでは無いか』

亀彦『智識の宝庫とも言ふべき亀サンは、万年の先まで前途を達観してるから、天眼通に映じて仕方がない。コンナ神秘的な事は貴公等には諒解出来まいが、然し原始的で頭脳の発達せない宣伝使には無理もないワイ』

駒彦『貴様は如何してもウラル教の垢が脱けないから直に物質的の智識を出したがるのだ。神代には計算等は必要が無い。神は無形に見、無声に聞き、無算に数へ給ふものだ。ソンナ時代に適合はぬ前後のそろばん様な、迂遠な事は没にした方が面倒臭くなくて良からう』

亀彦『フト不成立の問題を提出して非難の焦点となつて仕舞つた。ヤア仕方が無い。本案は撤回します』

音彦『撤回も何もあるものか、吾々は白紙主義だ。ソンナ愚案は忽ち握り潰しだ』

亀彦『それでも上院は如何する積りだ。ヒヒヽヽヽ』

§ 2

Rising to their feet once more, the three resolved to charge straight into the midst of the countless floating spheres that spun in all directions, and Otohiko, taking the lead, shouted:

Otohiko: 'Villains! What meaneth all these balls when there is not a single player? Launching a mass movement in such a subterranean depth—desist forthwith; 'tis seditious and improper!'

Kamehiko: 'Lo, these are exceedingly massive and heavy for baseballs! They are spheres of solid stone! If we should collide with them heedlessly, 'twould be a dire disaster! Hearken, ye myriad goblin balls! Compete against the Kototama of Master Kame! Come forth from behind the screens and disclose your true form!'

Straightway upon the most colossal of the spheres, eyes, nose, and mouth manifested themselves, and it laughed aloud:

'Ha-ha-ha-ha! Art thou thunderstruck (tamageta)? Canst thou not bear it (tamarinu)?'

Kamehiko: 'Aha, true to the fashion of the Cavern of Demons! 'Tis poor sport with thee alone; let every sphere among ye display eyes, nose, and mouth at once!'

'If that be thy bidding, we shall manifest millions of faces before thine eyes!'

Kamehiko: 'Thou art like the phosphorescence of a firefly, fragmenting into endless sparks! Who under heaven art thou? We are apostles of the Anai teachings upon an august exploration: art thou adorning the hall with jewels to welcome us, or dost thou surrender in terror before our divine majesty?'

The colossal sphere chuckled unceasingly: 'U-fu-fu-fu, i-hi-hi-hi!'

Kamehiko: 'What an uncanny fellow! In the midst of travel, such diversion is pleasant for a time, but when ye swarm in such legions, 'tis unbearably vexatious! What is your purpose? In the name of the Deity I shall cross-examine ye; disclose the truth!'

'We desire nothing of worldly goods! Solely from yearning for the jewel (tama) of universal suffrage do the spirits of us all assemble in one band to wage our campaign!'

Otohiko: 'Thou art of communist persuasion! Marshalling so pompous a host, what meaneth this spectacle? Why dost thou not appoint representatives to parley with us?'

Colossal Sphere: 'Because ye are utterly blind and deaf! If three or five delegates spoke, they would never catch your notice! Wherefore we were constrained to form an immense multitude, raising high-pitched shrieks that we might arrest your eyes and ears!'

音彦『ヤイ、選手も居らぬのに玉ばかり何だ。コンナ処で民衆運動を開始しよつて良い加減に廃めないか、不穏当だぞ』

亀彦『ヤア此奴、中々野球にしては大きいなり、重たい玉だ。此奴は石玉だ。うつかり衝突でも仕様ものなら大変だ。ヤイ数多い化け玉、この亀サンの言霊と競争だ。如何だ、屏息するか』

玉の中の最巨大なるもの忽ち目、鼻、口、現はれて、

§ 3

Kamehiko: 'What! Though tens of thousands of tiny voices faint as mosquitoes' whines be gathered together, can they ever penetrate our ears? In the first place, our eardrums are utterly numbed; shout with a louder, thunderous roar!'

Colossal Sphere: ''Tis said that great eloquence entereth not the vulgar ear! Doth not the voice of heaven reach thy hearing?'

Kamehiko: 'Whether it be the voice of a marten or a weasel, I know not! Yet rather than raising such a clamor, take heed lest the weasel let fly its desperate stench! Sniffing out foul matter like dung-flies and buzzing about, since ye are mere flies, ye will never amount to a straw!'

With furious eyes and bristling brows, breathing fierce wrath, the colossal sphere thrust forth hands and feet and clenched its fists, shouting:

'Comrades! Onward to direct action!'

And from all four quarters they rained blows upon Kamehiko. Smote heavily upon the cranium by a fist knotty as a turban-shell, Kamehiko cried 'Ouch!'—and in that very instant, his dream vanished into waking.

Kamehiko: 'Lo, I was nodding and slumbering in such a place! Hey, Master Oto, Master Koma, white roots have grown beneath our rumps! Let us arise without delay and resume our resolute advance!'
