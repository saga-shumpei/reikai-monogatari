Chapter 8: The Cavern of Demons [534]

第8章　醜の窟〔534〕

§ 1

The night had at last broken into dawn; though the rays of the heavenly sun remained veiled, heaven and earth grew faintly luminous.

Takahiko: 'Hey, everyone! Dawn has broken! Open your eyes and rise!'

Iwahiko: 'What, is it dawn already? Last night my backside was nearly bitten off by a demon; peaceful slumber was out of the question!'

Otohiko: 'What are you babbling about? That was trifling as tea brewed from wind! More importantly, my belly is rumbling with hunger! Does anyone have any breakfast provisions?'

Umehiko: 'When departing from the port of Taru, I purchased some hard biscuits and dried meat. Let us divide and share them among all of us.'

Takahiko: 'Ah, splendid! Each of us must prepare his stomach!'

Kamehiko: 'How grateful, how blessed! Nothing is more agonizing than marching on an empty stomach.'

Komahiko: 'Come, let us share and partake together!'

The companions sat upon the grass, dividing the biscuits and dried meat among themselves to fill their bellies, and recovered their vigor somewhat.

Takahiko: 'Now that our stomachs are bolstered, let us set forth toward the Cavern of Demons that is said to lie ahead!'

§ 2

Iwahiko: 'Aye, that monster? Bah! Such a creature is as trifling as tea brewed from wind!'

Takahiko: 'Talking big again! Last night your hips gave way and you were practically worshipping him, yet come morning, you put on bold airs—what an astonishing fellow!'

Iwahiko: 'These are no bold airs! When one regulates the breathing of Shinto Kototama, any evil spirit will disperse!'

Otohiko: 'Fine words indeed! Did you not bare your backside and slap it crying, "Even my rear is dumbfounded"?'

All burst into hearty laughter.

Iwahiko: 'Nonetheless, dancing in the shadows beneath the veranda feels like wasted effort. What say you, Umehiko, Otohiko?'

Umehiko: 'Well, whether anyone is watching or not, our mission is to pacify evil spirits through Kototama in accordance with the Anai teachings.'

Kamehiko: 'Indeed, our mental attitude is entirely different from the days when we served Lord Uralhiko of Armenia.'

Komahiko: 'Just so! Sharing a single lotus, we have only to advance straight along the Divine Way!'

Takahiko: 'Hey, everyone! Look yonder atop that Cavern of Demons: see what stands there! That is surely none other than the emissary Hinodewake who vanished last night! He seems to have executed a stratagem to seal the demons before our arrival so they cannot flee! Come, all is well now! Hasten, hasten! Hey, Iwahiko, you have thoroughly given up the Ural faith by now, have you not?'

§ 3

Iwahiko: 'Given up or not given up? Never in my wildest dreams have I ever cared for the Ural faith!'

Takahiko: 'Ahahaha! What a shameless turncoat! Well, no matter! Double-time march!'

Counting 'One, two, three!', they dashed toward the Cavern of Demons. Hinodewake stood upon the rocks, singing promotional hymns with a clear, refreshing voice.

The radiant presence and sacred chanting of Hinodewake-no-Mikoto resonated magnificently through the crisp morning air.

Iwahiko: 'Oho! Your Excellency is the emissary Hinodewake! Forgive our great discourtesy in parting ahead of you last night!'

Hinodewake: 'That was splendid. Yet your hips gave way a little, did they not?'

Iwahiko: 'Hold on, this is strange! Could it be that the monster from last night is posing as Hinodewake and executing another stratagem here? What an incomprehensible puzzle! Hey, monster, do not make fools of us! You are masquerading as Hinodewake-no-Mikoto, but I will not fall into that trap! Proof that you are a monster: you knew that my hips gave way last night! The real Hinodewake abandoned us without so much as a "Pardon my leaving first," vanishing into thin air! Although Hinodewake is an enigmatic figure, he does not transform like you! You fellow-monster from last night: last night was late so I indulged you with sleepy eyes, but today I am deadly serious! No matter how much you squirm, your hundred years are up—surrender your helmet honorably, what is your reply?'

Hinodewake: 'Kyah-hahaha!'

Iwahiko sat down firmly with a thud:

Iwahiko: ''"Kya-kya, kyah-haha"—what gibberish is that? Laughing with such incomprehensible, derailed sounds—what sort of laughing is "Kyah-hahaha, kyufufu"? Proof of a beast in disguise: you are using contracted sounds and nasal tones!'

Hinodewake: 'Ahahaha! You ask what Hinodewake speaks (tokasu), but you yourself are he whose hips gave way (nukasu)!'

Iwahiko: 'Do not speak, do not speak! If left alone, who knows what nonsense you will spout! Men, stay on guard! This fellow too is a bizarre freak!'

Takahiko: 'Ahahaha! Hey, Iwahiko, you are a hopeless simpleton! Does the noble Lord Hinodewake appear to you as a monster?'
