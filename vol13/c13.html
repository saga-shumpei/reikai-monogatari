Chapter 13: The Celestial Vessel Jōten-maru [539]

第13章　上天丸〔539〕

§ 1

'The Deity hath appeared openly in the world / To sever good from evil asunder! / Entering into the Cavern of Demons / Come the missionaries of the Anai teachings: / Takahiko of haughty and fierce breath, / And seemingly rigid yet pliable / Iwahiko, Kamehiko, and Komahiko! / Wandering into this gloomy cavern / From the famously renowned plain of Furu, / Falling into pitfalls a thousand fathoms deep, / Astonished twice over in pitiful plight! / Ah, how grievous, how calamitous! / Henceforth all is utter, pitch-black dark: / Limitless myriads upon myriads / Of monstrous Magatsu spirits assemble! / Your fragile lives, like flickering candles / Confronting the raging hurricane's blast, / Shall have your spirits tormented and racked / In manifold, agonizing tribulations, / Treading the road to the Land of Roots / Whence no traveler ever returneth!'

'Pitiful indeed is your doom! / I am the guardian deity of this cavern; / Having compassion upon your company, / That I might deliver your lives from destruction, / I appear in this hour to warn ye! / In the hour when warning is given, / If ye hearken not, whatever betideth / Is no concern of mine! / With a heart of mercy I declare it: / Rather than falling prostrate in terror, / Paralyzed and crying "Alas, all is lost!", / Turn your heels without a moment's delay / And retreat upon the road whence ye came! / U-u-u, Ru-ru-ru, Sa-sa-sa, I-i-i!'

Having so spoken, the uncanny voices and monstrous sounds abruptly ceased.

Umehiko: 'Hey, brethren! Let us take counsel together in this place! To advance heedlessly, carried away by momentum, is no mark of a wise man!'

Iwahiko: 'What faint-hearted whine is this? With what aim art thou uttering such cowardly words? Art thou terrified of a mere voice? If terrified of a voice, how couldst thou survive in a world where thunderclasps roar?'

Umehiko: ''Tis not that I fear a voice! I say rather that we ought to investigate the source of that voice and devise our strategy accordingly!'

Takahiko: 'What mattereth it? 'Tis the lair of demons in any case! Without such minor diversion, an assault hath no zest whatever!'

Umehiko: 'Though thou talkest of assault, this tunnel is exceedingly pitch-black!'

Iwahiko: 'What if it be dark? 'Tis the so-called leap in the dark! In a cavern where a hundred goblins walk in darkness, we have only to become fearsome demons ourselves and wage counter-warfare—that will suffice!'

Faintly in the far distance, a round aperture bearing a glimmer of light began to show itself.

Iwahiko: 'Look, look! The way ahead is visible! At all events, let us advance toward that bright aperture!'

The rocks above their heads began to roar with a ferocious din, groaning 'U-u-u!' It was a colossal roar that threatened to burst their eardrums. Clapping their fingers into their ears, they dashed headlong toward the circular light. When at last they reached the spot, behold, an immense hall stretched beyond the reach of human eye, and high above them clouds were drifting in the sky! All four quarters were encircled by precipitous walls of rock thousands of cubits high, while smooth avenues radiated in all eight directions.

と云つた限り異声怪音はピタリと止まりける。

梅彦『オイ皆の連中、一寸ここで相談をして見ようか。勢に任して軽々しく進むは智者の為す可き所では無いぞ』

岩彦『エーナアンだ。弱音を吹きよつて一体貴様は何を目標に、ソンナ卑怯な事を云ふのだ。声が怖ろしいのか、声が恐くつて雷の鳴る世の中に生きて居れるか』

梅彦『声が恐いのぢやないが、その声の主をよく探究して、その上に策戦計画をやらうかと云ふのだ』

鷹彦『何かまふものか。何れ悪魔の巣窟だ。これ位の余興がなくては進撃するのも張合がないワ』

梅彦『進撃すると云つたつて此の隧道は馬鹿に闇いぢやないか』

岩彦『闇くても構ふものか。いはゆる暗中飛躍だ。百鬼暗行の岩窟だもの、此方も厄鬼となつて対抗運動をやれば、夫れで好いのだ』

幽か向方に稍光の有る円き穴が見え初めた。

岩彦『オイオイ、モウ先が見えた。兎も角あの明い穴を目標に進むことにしよう』

頭上の岩石は猛烈なる音響を立ててウヽヽヽと唸り始めた。鼓膜が破裂しさうな巨音である。一同は耳に指を当て乍ら一目散に円き光を目標に進み行く。漸う此処に着いて見れば、眼の届かぬばかりの広場がある。さうして上面は雲が見えてゐる。四辺は幾千丈とも知れぬ岩壁を以て囲まれありて、坦々たる大道は四方八方に通じゐたり。

かすかに向こうの方に、やや光のある丸い穴が見え始めた。

岩彦『おいおい、もう先が見えた。ともかくあの明るい穴を目標に進む事にしよう』

頭上の岩石は猛烈なる音響を立ててウーーーと唸り始めた。鼓膜が破裂しそうな轟音である。一同は耳に指を当てながら一目散に丸い光を目標に進み行く。漸くここに着いて見れば、目の届かぬばかりの広場がある。そうして上空には雲が見えている。四方は幾千丈とも知れぬ岩壁をもって囲まれており、坦々たる大道が四方八方に通じていた。

§ 2

Takahiko: 'Ho, this place is like unto the Ame-no-Yachimata (Eight Heavenly Crossroads)! Take your deliberate counsel together, and press forward into the true cavern of demons! As for me, I beg your leave to depart!'

Quicker than words could say, he spread both wings wide to left and right, beat them with vigor, soared into the mid-heavens, and escaped from the cavern.

Iwahiko: 'Alas, forsaken by Hinode-wake, deserted by the enchantress, and with Takahiko having taken his flight home—now, when the way ahead is vast and gloomy, we five are left behind! We must needs proceed with the utmost vigilance! Were there but one clever fellow among us, 'twere well; but with none left save mere rubbish, there is no way to contrive a single plan!'

Otohiko: 'Insolent Iwahiko, utter not such disrespect! What meanest thou by mere dregs? In the remnants there is great blessing! Matters having come to this pass, God is our only reliance! Come, chant the Amatsu-Norito!'

So saying, Otohiko sat erect upon the subterranean floor and solemnly recited the sacred prayer. The remaining four followed, fervently chanting in unison. Presently, thundering from the heavens above, the Heavenly Bird-Ship (Ame-no-Torifune) descended before the five kneeling men with a stupendous roar. From within appeared Hinode-wake no Mikoto, Takahiko, and the maiden of before.

Hinode-wake: 'Well done, my brethren; ye have labored faithfully! Your exploration of the cavern is hereby brought to its conclusion! Since mortals could never scale these precipitous walls of stone, I have come to receive you! Come, embark without delay!'

Iwahiko: 'Wondrous, wondrous! How deep is our gratitude for your gracious mindfulness in all things! Verily, he who is a supreme commander of men must needs be even thus!'

Takahiko: 'Hey, Master Iwa, how glibly thy polite compliments flow! Though these eight crossroads appear thus, each of them cometh to a dead end after a short space; wherefore, harbor no lingering attachment, but board this vessel swiftly!'

Iwahiko: 'Who could ever harbor attachment to such an eerie cavern? To board the Heavenly Bird-Ship together with a goddess radiant as a celestial maiden and journey unto heaven is a blessing exceeding profound! Up, brethren, let us embark forthwith!'

With these words, the five men leaped lightly aboard.

And once more, with a colossal roar that shook the cosmos, the Bird-Ship vanished into the boundless vault of heaven.
