§ 1

The three men resumed their advance. From an unseen quarter, the delicate strains of a biwa lute began to echo.

Otohiko: 'Aha! Being upon an exploration of a goblin cavern, we are treated to myriad entertainments! This is our very perquisite! What wondrously subtle music! In short, judging therefrom, deliberating thereon...!'

Komahiko: 'Why displayest thou such repetitive phrases? This place is no exposition!'

Otohiko: 'To expound without flowery prefaces seemeth a lack of reverence! Beyond all doubt, this is plucked by that somewhat sensitive, charming beauty who emerged from the white smoke within the luminous orb! Not that I am like the cruel knight seeking the Lady Kogō; yet whether it be peak-storm or pine-breeze or the lute of the beloved, while Koma and Kame sit and listen, 'tis the poignant cadence of Sōfuren! She who cast a swift glance upon the handsome countenance of Master Oto, smiled gently, rolled her cool, deep eyes like a gyrocompass beneath crescent brows, shot forth electric waves, and charmed Master Oto—'tis she without doubt! The sound of this lute hath a distinct tone (oto); the character for sound is the very "Oto" of Master Oto! We ought to hearken diligently without losing a single note!'

Komahiko: 'What nonsense babblest thou? Self-conceit hath its bounds! Bearing a face like a long eggplant draped in a hammock, how canst thou speak of beauties? A missionary need only fulfill a missionary's duty!'

Kamehiko: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha! A self-styled handsome youth! Venting such feverish bluster, 'tis but the unrequited love of an abalone on the rock! Like the lid of an oblong chest: though this side be open, the other side openeth not!'

Otohiko: 'Thou oughtest to remain submerged in silence! If thou bobbest heedlessly to the surface, a drifting mine will blow thee to pieces!'

Kamehiko: 'Aha! Like Mount Sakurajima, his pouch of wrath hath burst! "Towering indignation, approach not!"—such is the fierce fury of Master Oto! Mention but a woman, and he turneth crimson, breathing fierce wrath and sour humor! Ha-ha!'

The strains of the lute grew ever clearer and more piercing. Exchanging banter, the three paused at a three-forked junction, resting their breath and listening with keen interest. Suddenly, around the corner dashed a rough fellow, collided violently with Otohiko, and tumbled backward flat upon his rump, crying:

'Villains! Whosoever ye be, trespassing into this Cavern of Demons without leave, standing there like road-gods and knocking me flying! Ye blind fools, can ye not pay the slightest heed?'

Otohiko: 'I know not who thou art, but colliding with my chest is the height of insolence! Why dost thou not apologize forthwith?'

'What! Knocking a man down, and then babbling of apologies? Whom dost thou take me for? I am Master Yajihiko, the guardian deity of the Cavern of Demons! Hey, Yotahiko! What art thou doing? Strike this fellow down! Howsoever the world alter, where in the law books is it written that the victim must apologize unto the assailant?'

Otohiko: 'Ha-ha-ha! This fellow swaggereth boldly! Possessed of unbending stubbornness, he doth not collapse in terror even before an Anai missionary!'

Yajihiko: 'Utter no such ill-omened words! Who would collapse in sudden death, thou blockhead?'

Yotahiko: 'All standards of good and evil have become utterly confounded! The shover gaineth and the shoved loseth! Yaji, this is the trend of the times! Even deities cannot resist the season; 'twere safer to swallow our tears in silence, lest resisting the times cause us to be branded as reactionary!'

Yajihiko: 'Parleying with one wrapped in the splendid protective coloring of a missionary will profit nothing! Rather let us contrive some means to reap a fat profit! If we regard being knocked down as having tripped of ourselves, all grievance will vanish naturally! Hark ye, Sir Missionary, let us wash away all that hath passed down the river! Yet I have a wondrous enterprise to propose!'

Otohiko: 'A swift resolution indeed! Befitting a guardian deity of the Cavern of Demons, thou art remarkably flexible! What manner of enterprise is it? Pray disclose it!'

Yajihiko: ''Tis a matter of fabulous profit! As though rice-cakes grew upon trees, or gold coins hung clattering in boundless profusion from melon vines, and thou didst seize them all at a sweep! To leave so magnificent a treasure lying neglected is foolish indeed. We too would fain receive a share; but having found no man capable of retrieving it, we have been waiting! A valiant hero like thee will surely succeed! As for us, being heaven-born ugliness, howsoever we direct rapid-fire electric waves, whether the receiver yonder be defective or our transmitter be imperfect, we gain not the slightest response! But thou art bold of front, and possessest somewhat of a human countenance; wherefore the peerless Princess Garyō will surely leap upon thee purring like a cat shown dried bonito!'

Otohiko: 'Who under heaven is this Princess Garyō?'

Yajihiko: 'If we could divine her true nature, we should not have labored in vain all this while! Yet 'tis reported that she is the daughter of a great and noble deity of Jerusalem!'

Otohiko: 'And where doth the princess dwell?'

Yajihiko: 'If I reveal that, the seed of our profit is lost! First let us secure our commission! Until we gain our profit, I cannot disclose the secret!'

Kamehiko: 'Look at that fellow's face, narrowing his eyes greedily! This is no hour to be entangled with such a knave, is it, Koma?'

Komahiko: 'Aye, verily! Otohiko would never pass muster! Let us advance without more delay! At all events, we have only to follow the sound of the biwa lute! If we go thither, Princess Garyō will surely cast amorous glances upon us! An electric current seemeth already to course through my soul!'

Yajihiko: 'Hark ye, sir! Before so clumsy and threadbare a face as thine, long as a sponge-gourd, even an eel would lose all patience and slither away in disgust! Ha-ha-ha!'

Komahiko: 'Pay no heed! The foremost spear and first renown shall be mine!'

So saying, swinging his arms boldly, stamping his feet like a sumo wrestler toward the sound, he pressed forward.

Yajihiko: 'Well met, gentlemen! This is indeed none other than the secret retreat of Princess Garyō! 'Tis an exceedingly grotesque and wondrous cavern of enchantments; wherefore take heed that ye fall into no disgrace!'

Otohiko: 'A magnificent portal, wholly unsuited to so gloomy a cave! Daughter of Jerusalem or whomsoever she be, let us explore her dwelling!'
