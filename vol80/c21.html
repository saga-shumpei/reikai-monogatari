Chapter 21: 青 Tree ケ Plain [2025]

青木ケ原〔2025〕

§ 1

In the sacred ground of Takamitsuyama, the highest peak of the central mountain range that divides the land of Yoshihara into east and west, purple auspicious clouds are always trailing, the wind is pure, and the vegetation, unlike that of the highlands, thrives by bathing in the divine virtue; it is the most excellent and wonderful sacred place from which one can overlook the shape of the land in all four directions. This location is called Aokigahara, and the eight million Kami gather here to serve in the administration.

Asagirihime-no-Kami strolls through the divine garden of Aokigahara and chants a poem.

Hisakata no takahi no miya o tachiidete / Koko ni mitose o suginikeru kana. / Akitsuo-no-Kami no idemashi osokereba / Ware itazura ni toshihen to suru. / Yoshihara o subemamoru beki kimi nakuba / Araburu kami o ikani osamemu. / Yosan no kuni no chūshin ni tateru kaenzan wa / Honō to tomo ni kieusenikeru. / Miwataseba kaen no yama no ato shiroku / Mizuumi to narishika nami kagayoe-ri. / Yosan no kuni ni ware tsukawaseshi futahashira / Imada kaerazu kokoromotonashi. / Nushi no Kami no megumi no sachi no fukakereba / Kō o tatete yagate kaeramu. / Meji no kagiri yoshikusa shikusa shigeriau / Kore no kunihara ikani hirakamu. / Kunitsukami wa yamayama no suso ni sumai tsutsu / Hirano wa yoshi to shikusa shigerau. / Kono hiroki shikusa haeru no o hiraki / Gokoku nado uwehirometaki.

Just as she is strolling through the garden while chanting thus, Kokorohime-no-Kami, holding a child in her arms and singing a lullaby, advances quietly toward this direction in the shade of the trees in the garden.

Kokorohime-no-Kami sings:

Bōya wa yoi ko jiya nenne shina / Bōya no omori wa doko e ita / Yama o koete no o koete / Kawa o watarite tabi ni deta / Tabi no yukusaki ya izuko zoya / Mizukamiyama no seijiyō e / Mizukamiyama no furusato no / Sato no miyage ni nani morota / Denden daiko ni shō no fue / Nennen nennen nenneshina.

She sways her body from side to side, with her mind entirely on the upbringing of Tatsuhiko. Seeing this, Asagirihime-no-Kami says:

Kokorohime-no-Kami, your sincerity is gentle, even in the way you nurture the lord Tatsuhiko. Since this child was born from the womb of a dragon, he is a wise child and a beautiful child. This child possesses a nature that may become good or evil depending on how he is raised. Nurture him morning and evening without letting him leave your side, and let him shine as the ruler of the land.

Kokorohime-no-Kami sings:

Arigatashi mihishiro-kami no mikotoba / Ware tsutsushimite tsukae matsuramu. / Asakarao, Kunikao-no-Kami no torifune wa / Ikaga narishika kikamahoshikere. / Nishi no sora tōku me o miwataseba / Kuroki hitotsu no kage no ukaberu. / Kasukanaru kumo no kurokage wa futahashira no / Norite kaerasu torifune narazu ya.

Asagirihime-no-Kami, while looking far away at the western sky, says:

The faint shadow is approaching one after another; it must surely be the heavenly bird-boat. The two pillars are coming back with high spirits, having pacified the calamities of the land of Yosan.

Just as she chants this, the bird-boat carrying the two pillars lands in the plaza of Aokigahara like a dove, with rapid speed.

The many Kami who serve in this sacred place are filled with joy and dancing at the safe return of the two Kami, and the sound of them shouting 'Ooh, ooh' is such a roar that it seems as if it would split Takamitsuyama.

Asagirihime-no-Kami advances close to the side of the two Kami and says:

Having flown through the distant sky and returned, I think of the achievements of you two pillars.

Asakarao-no-Kami first makes a deep bow before Asagirihime-no-Kami and sings:

Himegami no kototama osore zannen ni / Ima fukumyō mōshikeru kana. / Arehateshi kunigata mitsutsu odorokinu / Yoshikusa shikusa shigeru Yosankoku. / Kaenzan chichū ni fukaku kanbotsu shi / Hi no mizuumi wa nariidenikeri. / Shikigami no kazuta tsudohishi Yosan no kuni no / Tenpenchii ni arimari hajimenu. / Sari nagara kusamura ni sumu oniorochi / Mizuhonki-tō no magatsu wa sakashimo. / Yoshihara no kuni no hikari no kaenzan / Mizuumi to narishi yu hi no tane naki kuni. / Ikaga nishite kono kunihara ni hi no tane o / Motome emu kamo satorase tamae.

葭原の国土を東西に画したる中央山脈の最高峯高光山の聖場には、常に紫の瑞雲棚引き、風清く、植物も高地に似ず、神徳に浴して繁茂し、四方の国形を瞰下し得る最勝最妙の霊地なり。この地点を青木ケ原と称し、八百万の神等ここに集りて政に仕ふ。

朝霧比女の神は青木ケ原の神苑を逍遥しながら御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『久方の高日の宮を立ち出でて ここに三年を過ぎにけるかな。 顕津男の神の出でましおそければ 吾いたづらに年経むとする。 葭原を統べ守るべき君なくば あらぶる神をいかに治めむ。 予讃の国の中心に立てる火炎山は 焔と共に消え失せにける。 見渡せば火炎の山の跡白く 湖となりしか波かがよへり。 予讃の国に吾遣はせし二柱 いまだ帰らず心もとなし。 主の神の恵の幸の深ければ 功を立ててやがて帰らむ。 目路の限り葭草醜草茂り合ふ これの国原如何に開かむ。 国津神は山々の裾に住まひつつ 平野は葭と醜草茂らふ。 この広き醜草生へる野を開き 五穀など植ゑひろめたき』

斯く歌ひつつ苑内を逍遥し給ふ折もあれ、庭の樹蔭に小児を抱きて子守唄を歌ひながら、子心比女の神は此方に向つて静に進み来る。

子心比女の神は歌ふ。

と身体を左右にふり、竜彦の養育に余念なかりける。朝霧比女の神はこの体を見て、

『子心比女神の真心やさしけれ 竜彦のきみを育みますも。 この御子は竜の御腹ゆ生れませば 賢しき御子よ美しき御子よ。 この御子は育てによりてよくもなり 悪しくもなるべき性をもつなり。 朝夕に肌身放さず育みて 国の司と照させ給へ』

子心比女の神は歌ふ。

『ありがたし御樋代神の御言葉 吾謹みて仕へ奉らむ。 朝空男、国生男の神鳥船は いかがなりしか聞かまほしけれ。 西の空とほく眼を見渡せば くろき一つの影の浮べる。 かすかなる雲の黒影は二柱の 乗りて帰らす鳥船ならずや』

朝霧比女の神は、遠く西空をふりさけ見ながら、

『かすかなる影は次々近み来ぬ 正しく天の鳥船なるべし。 予讃の国土の禍ひ鎮めて二柱 復命すと勇み来るも』

斯く歌ふ折しもあれ、急速力を以て二柱の乗れる鳥船は、青木ケ原の広場に鳩の如くに着陸せり。

この聖地に仕ふる数多の神々は、二神の無事帰りしを欣喜雀躍し、ウオーウオーと叫ぶ声、高光山も割るるばかりのどよめきなりける。

朝霧比女の神は二神の側近く進ませ給ひ、

『久方の空を翔りて帰りてし 汝二柱の功績を思ふ』

朝空男の神は、先づ朝霧比女の神の御前に最敬礼をほどこし歌ふ。

『比女神の神言畏み漸くに 今復命白しけるかな。 あれはてし国形見つつ驚きぬ 葭草醜草生ふる予讃国。 火炎山地中に深く陥没し 火の湖は生り出でにけり。 醜神の数多集ひし予讃の国の 天変地異に新まり初めぬ。 さりながら叢に棲む鬼大蛇 水奔鬼等の曲津はさかしも。 葭原の国土の光りの火炎山 湖となりしゆ火の種なき国。 如何にしてこの国原に火の種を 求め得むかも悟らせ給へ』

§ 2

Asagirihime-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'Wait for the time but a little while, for the seed of fire shall be granted by Amatsumikami.'

Kuniumio-no-Kami sings:

'Revering the command of my Lord, I boarded the Torifune and came to Shira the Kunigata. Crossing the clouds for a hundred thousand ri, I descended peacefully upon Oshino-ga-oka in the land of Yosa. I communed with the son of the Ihane of Minakamiyama, where the Inochi of the Seirei had been taken. Conversing with the sons of Ihane-ga-ne, Fuyuo and Akio, I realized the ferocity of the Akuma. I think that the Magatsu lurking here and there in the Kuni of Yoshihara will not be subdued unless they are burned. There is nothing that can equal the power of the Mihi of the Lord God to burn and destroy the Magakami. It is said that the Maujiu and Dokujiya were guarding it, fearing that the seed of fire would be stolen. The seed of fire has vanished into the Kanbotsu of the Kuwaen mountain, and not even its shadow can be seen.'

Asagirihime-no-Kami chants:

'Praising the Lord who has achieved merit while layering journeys in the Kumomakura of the Misora. The Kuni is young, the earth is still soft, and the stabilization of the Kuni of Yoshihara is no simple matter. There is no power greater than the Mihi to burn away the Yoshigusa and the Suihonsau. I have prayed for a hundred days and a hundred nights as to how to obtain the power of the Mihi. The God of the light who has performed the Misogi of a hundred days shall soon return here. It is a joyous thing that today, when the hundred days have been fulfilled, you have returned in triumph. The great Oomiteru-no-Kami shall also return soon, for the hundred days of Misogi are fulfilled today.'

Just as she was chanting thus, having completed the Misogi ritual and filling his whole body with the power of Kami, Oomiteru-no-Kami finally returned to the sacred ground of Aokigahara, parting the clouds of the ravine. Appearing reverently before the four pillars of Kami, Oomiteru-no-Kami sings:

'Having received the Kototama of the Mihishiro-no-Kami, I have finished the hundred days of Misogi and returned. I performed Misogi in the clear water of the mountain stream and realized the shifting world. It is the divine will to purify the land of Yoshihara through the power of water and fire. Grant me leave for a little while, for I shall go to welcome the Mihishiro-no-Kami. I have faintly realized that Asakahime, who floats upon the sea of ten thousand ri, possesses the Mihi. I shall welcome my Lord at the port of Matsuura and seek to borrow the power of the Mihi.'

Asagirihime-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'I am happy to hear that Asakahime of the eight maidens, the Mihishiro-no-Kami, is setting forth. If so, Oomiteru-no-Kami, take the lead, and let Asasorao-no-Kami and Kuniumio-no-Kami follow and depart.'

Oomiteru-no-Kami sings:

'Following the Kototama of Asagirihime-no-Kami, I shall go to welcome Asakahime. Having finished praising the Kototama of the Ihane before the great presence, I shall proceed directly. Through the Misogi of a hundred days, my soul has become transparent like a mirror. The joy of having the two pillars, Asasorao-no-Kami and Kuniumio-no-Kami, added to me.'

Asasorao-no-Kami sings:

'Mihishiro-no-Kami, send forth the Torifune to welcome Asakahime. Though the port of Matsuura is far away, I shall board the Torifune and proceed.'

Kuniumio-no-Kami sings:

'It is courageous to think that I shall descend to the port of Matsuura, following the two pillars of Kami. The vigor of soaring through the distant Misora makes me forget that I am a Kami of the earth. I remember crossing the sky of the land of Yosa; I shall descend peacefully to the port of Matsuura.'

Asagirihime-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'If so, you three pillars, hurry and board the Torifune to proceed and welcome the Kami.'

As a departure to welcome Asakahime-no-Kami, Asagirihime-no-Kami, leading the four presiding Kami and many other Kami, performed ablutions and purification before the great Lord God, who had established the palace pillars thick and strong and was enshrined in the center of Aokigahara. Offering various tributes and becoming the chief priest herself, she began the prayer for the safety of the air.

Asagirihime-no-Kami, clapping her hands four times, says:

'Before the great Lord God, who has established the palace pillars thick and strong upon the lower Ihane of this Takamitsuyama, which is fearful even to mention, and who reigns high, I, the chief priest Asagirihime-no-Kami, humbly and reverently pray. Having received the Kototama of the Lord God and being appointed as the Mihishiro-no-Kami, three years have already passed since I descended to Takamitsuyama, parting the eight-fold clouds of the Amatsumisora into a thousand parts. I, the Mihishiro-no-Kami, have sent the days and months without achieving any notable merit, praying for the peace of the Kuni while crouching in heaven and treading the earth. Yet, the Kuni is still young, the earth is soft, and there is no way to open the Kuni of Yoshihara, which is left to the rampant behavior of the Magatsumi. Seeing the seed of Mihi, born of the water and fire of the Lord God, reflected in the true mirror of the heart of Oomiteru-no-Kami, and hearing faintly that Asakahime-no-Kami is crossing to this Kuni while holding the precious seed of fire, I am filled with joy. Because I intend to send Oomiteru-no-Kami, Asasorao-no-Kami, and Kuniumio-no-Kami to the side of Asakahime-no-Kami to welcome her, I have finished building the Torifune of heaven firmly. Please hear my prayer of this day as I send the three pillars aboard. May the party of Asakahime-no-Kami, who shines at the port of Matsuura for long years, be guided safely to the sacred place of Takamitsuyama without any calamity, even as they travel through the strange clouds of the sky. I pray this, bowing my knees and prostrating myself, fearing and trembling. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, may there be light in the living Kototama, and may there be power in my Kototama.'

§ 3

Thus the Saiten was concluded in Buji, and the Mihashira no Kami purified their bodies here, boarded the Torifune, and set out to travel through the air, parting the Yahekumo of Izu to head toward the port of Matsūra.

Mihishirogami chanted a Miuta:

How joyous is this Ikuhi for which I have waited long, / Thinking to welcome Asakahime. / Among those Yahashira no Mihishirogami, / Especially valiant is Asakahime. / If the Mikoto of Asakahime-gami shall emerge, / This Kuni of Yoshihara shall be at peace. / The efficacy of praying morning and evening to the Lord God / Manifests on this auspicious day. / Now then, depart safely upon the Kumoji, / I shall continue to pray before your Mimahe.

Ōmiterashi no Kami chanted:

Revering the Mikoto of Asagirihime, I shall depart, / With Ikukototama, the Kumoji shall be peaceful. / Crossing over the valleys of clouds and the rivers of clouds, / Protect us as we advance to the port. / Crossing over the peaks of clouds in the vast sky, / We shall go traversing the Amanokawahara. / With eagles, hawks, and the myriad birds beneath our eyes, / How valiant is the sky as we cross over.

Asasorao no Kami chanted:

Through the morning sky where the Amatsuhi shines across, / We who advance are like the Hōō. / Since the wings of the Torifune are built firmly, / I think we shall advance with peace of mind. / Since it is a cloud sprung from the sacred fire of the Lord God, / The journey through the sky shall also be peaceful.

Kunioo no Kami chanted:

I too shall follow the two Kami, / And advance, parting the Amatsumitsora. / Even if there are many pockets, this ship / Is ever firm, so we shall advance with ease. / We go while gazing at the clouds of the Misora, / Above the endless Ōno. / Now then, we shall cross over, / While bowing and worshipping the sacred ground of Takamitsuyama.

Asagirihime no Kami chanted a Miuta:

The journey of the Mihashira no Kami upon the Kumoji, / I shall now send off from this Kiyoniwa. / O Mihashira no Kami, travel in peace, / Thinking of it as the divine work entrusted by the Kami.

Ōmiterashi no Kami chanted:

Now then, from the sacred ground of Aokigahara, / We shall rise and advance to Matsūra-minato.

Having finished chanting thus, as the Mihashira entrusted their bodies to the Ama no Torifune and ascended high into the air, the hundred Kami who serve in this Shin-en raised a war cry of 'Wao-wao' and sent off this valiant departure.

原文（近代文語）

斯くて祭典は無事終了し、三柱の神はここに身を清め鳥船に乗じて、伊頭の八重雲をかき分けて松浦の港に向ひ航空することとなりぬ。

御樋代の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『待ちわびし今日の生日の目出度さよ 朝香の比女を迎ふと思へば。 八柱の御樋代神のその中に 殊に雄々しき朝香比女かも。 朝香比女神の神言の出でまさば この葭原の国土は安けむ。 主の神に朝夕を祈りたる 験かがよふ今日は目出度き。 いざさらば雲路安けく出でませよ 吾は御前に祈りつづけむ』

大御照の神は歌ふ。

『朝霧比女の神言畏み出でゆかむ 生言霊に雲路安けむ。 雲の谷雲の川をば横ぎりて 港に進まむ守らせ給へ。 大空の雲の峰をば打ち渡り 天の河原渡らひ行かむ。 鷲も鷹も百鳥千鳥も目の下に ながめて渡る空の雄々しさ』

朝空男の神は歌ふ。

『天津日の輝き渡る朝空を 進む吾等は鳳凰なるよ。 鳥船の翼堅らに造りあれば 心安けく進まむと思ふ。 主の神の御火より湧ける雲なれば 空の旅路も安けかるべし』

国生男の神は歌ふ。

『吾も亦二柱神に従ひて 天津御空をかき分け進まむ。 ポケツトは数多ありともこの船は いや堅ければ安く進まむ。 はてしなき大野の上を限りなき 御空の雲を見つつ行くなり。 いざさらば高光山の聖場を 伏し拝みつつ渡りゆくべし』

朝霧比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『三柱の神の雲路の旅行きを 今や送らむこの清庭に。 三柱の神よ安けく渡りませ 神の依さしの神業と思ひて』

大御照の神は歌ふ。

『いざさらば青木ケ原の聖場を 立ちて進まむ松浦港へ』

斯く歌ひ終り、三柱は天の鳥船に身を托して空中高く昇らせ給ふや、これの神苑に仕へ侍る百神等は、ウオーウオーの鯨波を造りて、勇ましきこの首途を送りける。
