Chapter 14: 報哭婆 [2018]

報哭婆〔2018〕

§ 1

On the summit of Kuwaenzan, ferocious beasts such as tigers, bears, shishi, wolves, leopards, and large Orochi dwell around the crater, guarding it day and night, lest the Hidane be stolen by anyone. Should this Hidane be snatched away and released upon the vast plains of Yoshihara, it would be an Ichidaiji; fearing that the ferocious beasts and Dokujiya would be instantly burned to death and meet the misery of total annihilation, the kings of the ferocious beasts and Dokujiya, after consultation, selected guards to keep a tight watch around the Funkuwakō. Akio planned to seize this Hidane, set fire to the Sansongenya, and burn down the entire nest of Akuma in the Yoshihara all at once. However, the Suihonki of the Akiribaba, who serves as the vanguard for the ferocious beasts and Dokujiya, tries to prevent this with all her might, yet there is a constant fear of Akio climbing the mountain; in order to hinder him by any means, she exhausted various kinds of sorcery and kept him wrapped in a curtain of darkness for a while.

Around the crater on the mountain, while the kings of the ferocious beasts were trembling at the Kototama of fire and water echoing from the foot of the mountain with their heads bowed, and while they were in the midst of a consultation on how to prevent the enemy who would take the fire, those who came climbing up with heavy breath, walking steadily, were the two demons, Warai-baba and Akiri-baba.

Tora-ō, upon seeing the two demons, called out in a flustered manner,

Tora-ō: 'Who is it that makes the Kototama echo at the foot of the mountain? Tell me in detail, you two old hags.'

Kuma-no-ō:

Kuma-no-ō: 'Why do you hesitate, you two? Convey the truth to us quickly, without a moment's delay.'

Warai-baba:

Warai-baba: 'Ahahahaha, ihihihihi. Five detestable gaki have appeared and are attempting to take the fire of this mountain. I have been blocking them until now with all the strength my soul possesses. My power is now exhausted; I pray you, grant your power to me.'

Akiri-baba sings:

Akiri-baba: 'Ihihihihi, those meddlesome gaki have appeared and are about to climb the mountain of fire. I, too, have blocked them with all my might, but the enemy possesses the weapon of Kototama. Thinking thus, I believe there is no other way but to borrow your power.'

Tora-ō sings:

Tora-ō: 'Because you engage in petty tricks, you must have been easily defeated by the enemy. The weapon of Kototama is not something to be feared; exhaust your sorcery and face them in battle. If your demonic heart does not falter, how could the sword of Kototama be something to fear?'

Ōkami-no-ō sings:

Ōkami-no-ō: 'Hearing the weakness of Warai-baba and Akiri-baba, the wolf is utterly astonished. Since I have given you the curtain of darkness, use it to blind his eyes and destroy him.'

Warai-baba:

Warai-baba: 'Ahahahaha, Warai-baba has fought with all her might to the very root. Though I confused him and dropped him into pits, he did not falter before the sword of Kototama. Even the schemes of the renowned Warai-baba have now been completely broken. Beyond this, there is no path for me to live other than to borrow your power.'

Ōkami-no-ō:

Ōkami-no-ō: 'You two useless old hags, from today, the wolf dismisses you. To think that you, while possessing the curtain of the eternal night of darkness, would lose heart and worry—what spineless creatures you are.'

Shishi-ō sings:

Shishi-ō: 'Lord Wolf, wait a moment. Realizing the depth of the old hags' magic words, if this is the case, let us combine our demonic powers at once and destroy the enemy. Come, Bear; follow me, Tiger and Wolf. We shall descend the mountain and face the enemy. Even if the light of the sword of Kototama is sharp, we shall bite them to death with our fangs.'

Thus, the Akuma on the mountain are engaged in deep consultation. Akio and his party, who had awakened as if from a dream in the shade of the trees at the foot of the mountain, while gazing at the scene of the mountain top erupting, said,

Akio: 'Ah, I was deceived by Akiri-baba and was dreaming in the shade of the trees. No matter what hardships I may encounter, I shall not falter, for I must take the fire on the mountain ridge. They say the ferocious beasts and Dokujiya are guarding it, fearing that the Hidane will be taken. In any case, I shall cast aside my life and, with dignity, push forward to the fortress of Magatsu. If I have but one Hidane, I shall burn down the dwellings of the Akuma lurking in the mountains and fields.'

火炎山の頂上に、虎、熊、獅子、狼、豹、大蛇等の猛獣が、火口の周囲に棲息し、何者にも火種を盗まれざるやうと、日夜固く守つてゐる。若し此火種を奪はれ、葭原の大原野に放たれることあらば、それこそ一大事、猛獣毒蛇は忽ち焼き殺され、全滅の憂目にあはむことを恐れ、猛獣毒蛇の王は協議の上、当番を選びて噴火口の周囲を固く守り居たりける。秋男は此火種を奪ひ取り、山村原野に放火して、一斉に葭原全帯の悪魔の巣窟を焼き尽さむと計画したりける。然るに猛獣毒蛇どもの前衛を務むる譏り婆の水奔鬼は、力限りにこれを阻止すれども、動もすれば秋男が登山するの恐れあり、如何にもしてこれを妨げむと、種々様々の魔術をつくし、暫時の間を闇の幕に包みおきたるなり。山上の火口の周囲には、猛獣の王首を鳩めて山麓より響き来る言霊の水火に戦きながら、如何にもして火取の敵を防がむやと、協議の真最中のところへ、すたすたと息をはづませ登り来りしは、笑ひ婆ア、譏り婆アの二鬼である。

虎王は二鬼を見るより慌しく声をかけ、

『山裾に言霊ひびくは何者ぞ つぶさにかたれ二つの婆ども』

熊の王は、

『汝等は何をためらふか一刻も 早くまことを吾等に伝へよ』

笑ひ婆は、

『アハハハハ、イヒヒヒヒ

いけすかぬ餓鬼ども五つあらはれて この山の火を取らむとするも。 たましひのあらむ限りの力もて 吾は今までふせぎゐたりき。 わが力最早つきなむ願はくば 君の力を吾にあたへよ』

虎王は歌ふ。

『その方は小刀細工いたす故に もろくも敵にくじかれにけむ。 言霊の武器おそるるに足らざらむ 魔術をつくして向ひ戦へ。 魔心のひるまずあれば言霊の 剣もいかで恐るべきかは』

狼の王は歌ふ。

『笑ひ婆ア譏り婆アの気の弱さ ききて狼あきれ果てたり。 闇の幕汝に与へあるからは 彼がまなこをくらませ亡ぼせ』

獅子王は歌ふ。

『狼の君よしばらく待てよかし 婆アの魔言のふかきをさとりて。 斯くならばわれ等一度に魔力を あはせて敵を亡ぼさむかな。 熊も来よ虎狼も従へよ 山を降りて敵に向はむ。 言霊の剣の光するどくも われ等は牙もて咬み殺すべし』

斯く山上の悪魔等は協議を凝らしてゐる。麓の樹蔭に夢よりさめたる如き秋男一行は、山頂の噴火するさまを眺めながら、

『ああ吾は譏り婆アにはかられて 樹かげに夢をみてゐたりけむ。 如何ならむ艱みにあふもひるむまじ 山の尾の上の火をとらざれば。 火の種をとられむことをおそれみて 猛獣毒蛇は守りゐると言ふ。 ともかくも捨身となりて堂々と 曲津の砦に押し寄せゆかむ。 火の種の一つありせば山に野に ひそむ悪魔の棲処を焼かむ』

§ 2

The pine tree sings:

'Alas, by the schemes of the mocking hag, I, a Masurao, have been deceived. If it be so, I shall now prepare myself and go forth to repel the ugly plots of the Onibaba. Beside me, as I fret for the sake of the nation, the innocent Kikiyō blooms in peace. When I look up and gaze at Amatsusora, the Amatsu-hi shines within the thin clouds.'

The bamboo tree sings:

'We must ascend while brushing aside the obstructions of the laughing hag and the mocking hag. How hateful, the Oninochi of Lord Fuyuo has been taken; we must surely set out to conquer the hag. This hag must be an ugly, crooked thing serving as the vanguard for the Magatsukamira.'

The plum tree sings:

'Though the Ōzora is somewhat cloudy, the Chigusa by the roadside are adorned with flowers and fragrant. The Itsuten has suddenly clouded over and a heavy rain has begun to fall; is this a plot of the crooked ones?'

Just as they sang thus, the Maujiuren on the mountain sought to block the ascent of the party of Akio, calling forth clouds, raising winds, bringing down heavy rain, and wielding thunder, until suddenly the Tenchi became dark and gloomy, manifesting the Shiyuradau.

The plum tree, gazing at this Kuōkei, says:

'The rain and wind are heavy, likely the wild sport of the Maujiu and Dokuhebi dwelling at the summit. The thunder roars high and the wind rages; there is not even a means to climb the mountain. If it be so, I shall wait until the power of the crooked ones wanes before I climb the summit of fire.'

Akio sings:

'Again, how cunning the crooked gods are, raising black clouds and making it rain. I shall wait quietly in the shade of the trees until the crooked gods have finished creating their ugly materials.'

Thunder roared and lightning flashed, the mountain wind blew fiercely, and heavy rain fell incessantly; though they suffered greatly as their leather garments became soaked through even in the shelter beneath the trees, the five Masurao did not flinch in the slightest, and they waited for the time to pass by chanting the heavenly number songs. Through the lightning that stitched the darkness of the Tenchi, the shadow of the Onibaba appeared flickeringly, looking especially repulsive. From high in the branches of the trees, a strange voice was heard once more.

Onibaba: 'Gyahahahaha, it is the Onibaba who has appeared, borrowing the power of Lord Lion King. This laughing hag is different from before; since I have appeared here having borrowed the powers of Lord Lion King, Lord Bear King, Lord Tiger King, and Lord Wolf King, you should surely flinch at your so-called Kototama. Now, depending on your reply from here on, I shall crush your bones, strip your flesh, squeeze out your blood, and offer it as a meal for Lord Lion King. Oh, how amusing, how brave, ihihihihi, ufufufufu, ihihihihi, ohohohoho, you cowards, you are trembling to hear my words, how pathetic you are. Gyahahahaha, I am the mocking hag you fear. Today is the end of your luck; your lives shall be taken by the power of Lord Lion King. Even if you struggle, you will no longer be able to prevail. Now, try to move if you can. I have cast the spell of the mysterious and miraculous Kanashibari upon you, so you will surely not be able to move a muscle. Oh, how pleasant this is, gyafu-fufufu, hiuuu-doro-doro-doro, I am the ghost of the mocking hag, the water-running demon. Is it not repulsive? No, is it not terrifying? Ufufufufu.'

At the words of the Onibaba, who repeated the same things over and over again, Akio steeled his courage, worshipped the Tenchi once more, and chanted the living Kototama; then, the thunder and lightning, which had been so violent, ceased all at once, and the raging of the mountain wind and the falling rain also stopped abruptly. The Tenchi became clear and bright, there was not a single speck of cloud or mist in the sky, the ground appeared as if brocade mats had been spread out in rows, the sun and moon shone brilliantly, and it was a wonder that the scene of the Tenchi had returned to its original state.

『又してもこざかしきかな曲神は 黒雲おこし雨を降らすも。 曲神の醜の材料つくるまで 心静かに樹かげに待たむ』

雷鳴轟き稲妻ひらめき、山風強く吹き荒び、大雨沛然として降りしきり、樹下の宿りも雨洩りの為に、皮衣もびしよ濡れとなり、大いに苦しみたれど、五人の大丈夫は少しもひるまず、天の数歌を奏上して時の過ぐるを待ち居たり。天地の闇を縫うてひらめく稲妻の間より、鬼婆の影ちらりちらりと現はるるさま、一入いやらし。樹の枝高く怪しき声又もや聞え来る。

『ギヤハハハハ、獅子王様の力を借り、あらはれ来りし鬼婆ぞや。この笑ひ婆は以前と事変り、獅子王、熊王、虎王、狼王様方々の御力を拝借致してこれに現はれしものなれば、最早、汝等の言霊とやらにひるむべき。さあ、これよりは汝等の返答次第にて、骨を砕き、肉を削ぎ、血をしぼり、獅子王様のお食事に奉らむ。てもさても面白や勇ましや、イヒヒヒヒ、ウフフフフ、イヒヒヒヒ、オホホホホ臆病者、この方の言葉を聞いて胴ぶるひ致してゐるが、さてもさてもいぢらしい者だワイ。ギヤハハハハ、此方は汝が恐るる譏り婆ぞや。今日こそは汝等が運の尽き、獅子王様の力に依つて生命を奪はるべし。じたばたしても、もう敵ふまい。さあ動くなら動いてみよ。神変不思議の金縛りの術にかけおきたれば最早びくとも動けまい。さてもさても心地よやな、ギヤフフフフ、ヒウーードロドロドロ、この方は水奔鬼の譏り婆アの幽霊ぞや。いやらしくはないか、いや、おそろしくはないかウフフフフ』

と、幾度となく同じことのみ繰返す鬼婆の言葉に、秋男は胆力を据ゑ、再び天地を拝し、生言霊を奏上するや、さしも激しかりし雷鳴電光一時に止まり、山風の荒びも、降る雨も、ぴたりと止まりて、天地清明、空に一点の雲霧もなく、地上は錦の莚を敷き並べたる如く、日月輝き渡り、再び元の天地の光景にかへりたるこそ不思議なれ。
