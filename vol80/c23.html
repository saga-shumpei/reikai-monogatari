Chapter 23: Field Fire の壮観 [2027]

野火の壮観〔2027〕

§ 1

The Seijiyō of Takamitsuyama suddenly shone and overflowed with the Kaurin of the Mihishirogami Asakahime, while Zuiun drifted over the Misono of Aokigahara, and the spirit of Shinsei floated in the Shihen.

Asagirihime-no-Kami invited the party of Asakahime-no-Kami to the Yahirodono, extended her utmost hospitality, piled up the ripe apricot fruits of Takamitsuyama, which were the Meibutsu and seemed as if dew would drip from them, like a mountain, offered Bishiyu made from Boke, and here, with Ōmiterashi-no-Kami and the Jiyūshin and a hundred Kamigami gathered, a Daienkuwai was held.

Asagirihime-no-Kami chanted a Miuta.

Asakahime-no-Kami replied to this,

Ōmiterashi-no-Kami chanted,

Hatsutohiko-no-Kami chanted,

Asakarao-no-Kami chanted,

Kiritsuhiko-no-Kami chanted,

Kunioo-no-Kami chanted,

Tatsuyohime-no-Kami chanted,

Kokorohime-no-Kami chanted,

Asakahime-no-Kami chanted a Miuta again,

Asakahime-no-Kami chanted:

'Upon the arrival of the eight Yahashira Mihishirogami, this Misono has been revived. Crossing the Namiji from afar and parting the clouds, Asakahime has arrived. Kamigami who serve in the mansion of Takamitsu, praise the divine majesty of Asakahime. Among the eight Yahashira Mihishirogami, you are the most excellent, Asakahime-no-Kami. In the Kototama of Asakahime-no-Kami, I speak; partake of the delicious fruits and sacred wine. This wine is made from Boke, and these apricots are the pride of Takamitsuyama. In this mountain where the mountain is high and the clear water is scarce, the most precious things are the fruits.'

'I shall gratefully and joyfully partake of these delicious things prepared with such various considerations. Having traveled over the grass-pillow, I have met with this beautiful banquet today. I shall receive the taste of the pure hearts of the Kamigami with joy. I am happy to face this banquet in this Seijiyō that overlooks the lands of the four directions. Thanks to the merit of the bird-boat, I have come easily over the long road and the steep mountains.'

'I am Ōmiterashi, who serves the Mihishirogami and proclaims the Kototama morning and evening. While I hold the name of Ōmiterashi-no-Kami, I am ashamed of the darkness of my heart. Through the repeated Misogi of a hundred days, the light of my heart has finally begun to shine.'

'Did I ever think that I would meet such a banquet on the high ridge of Takamitsuyama? Drunk on the wine prepared with the hearts of the Kamigami, my body has turned red. My body and my spirit are purely revived; this sacred wine and food are the gifts of the Kami. Partake of the rich wine as if it were the ears of red grain, and be encouraged, Asakahime-no-Kami.'

'The light of Asakahime, whom I welcomed after parting the clouds from afar, is noble. The Kami of light has descended to the Misono; from now on, the darkness of the Ashihara will surely clear. That the four pillars of attendant Kamigami have descended to this Misono and are partaking of the sacred wine—there can be no day as auspicious as today, welcoming the Mihishirogami.'

'Over the lands of the four directions that I overlook, it has shone with the brilliance of the Amatsubi, reflecting like brocade. Whether it is the reeds or the water-running grass, I know not, but the field seen from above the ridge is like brocade. In any case, it is the limit of auspiciousness, for the two Mihishirogami are present. Satiated with the fragrant sacred wine and food, I am now revived in both body and spirit.'

'I never even dreamed that there would be such an auspicious precedent in such an age. The Kamigami whom I welcomed after parting the clouds are now sitting face to face with me in this pure hall. From today, the land of Ashihara will open up without a corner left behind, thanks to the merit of the Mihishirogami. Kamigami, partake of the sacred wine and eat the sacred food, until your faces glow and your bellies are full.'

'I, a goddess, have also served in the retinue and climbed to the ridge of Takamitsuyama. Guided to the Seijiyō of Takamitsuyama, I have melted into the noble view. Looking out over the endless, distant wilderness, I think of the greatness of the power of the Kami.'

'Though I am one who holds a divine child in my arms, forgive me, Kokorohime-no-Kami. This child is the beautiful child of the Kunitsukami of the mountain above the water, and I have raised him. I, too, am a goddess who is happy to serve on the narrow mat of this auspicious celebration today.'

'To the deep hospitality of Asagirihime-no-Kami, I have no words of gratitude. I shall offer this as a treasure of the land of Ashihara: this flint that preserves the seed of fire. If this one treasure exists, the land of Ashihara will be purified and open forever.'

高光山の聖場は、御樋代神朝香比女の神の降臨に俄かに輝き漲り、青木ケ原の神苑は瑞雲棚引き、新生の気四辺に漂ふ。

朝霧比女の神は、八尋殿に朝香比女の神一行を招じ、心の限り歓待を尽し、高光山の名物たる、露も滴らむばかりの熟れたる杏の実を山の如く積み、木瓜もて造りたる美酒を献り、ここに大御照の神以下の重臣はじめ百の神々集りて、大宴会は開かれにける。

朝霧比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『八柱の御樋代神の出でましに これの神苑は蘇りたり。 はろばろと波路を渡り雲を分けて 朝香の比女は出でましにけり。 高光の館に仕ふる神々も 朝香の比女の御稜威讃へよ。 八柱の御樋代神の中にして 勝れ給へる朝香比女神よ。 朝香比女の神の神言に物白す 美味果物御酒を召しませ。 此酒は木瓜にて造りこの杏は 高光山の誉なりけり。 山高く清水とぼしき此山に 尊きものは果物なりけり』

朝香比女の神はこれに答へて、

大御照の神は歌ふ。

朝空男の神は歌ふ。

『はろばろと雲路を分けて迎へてし 朝香の比女の光り尊し。 御光の神は神苑に天降りましぬ 今より葭原の闇は晴れなむ。 四柱の御供の神の此苑に 天降りいまして御酒召しますも。 今日の如めでたき吉き日なかるらむ 御樋代神を迎へ奉りて』

起立比古の神は歌ふ。

国生男の神は歌ふ。

子心比女の神は歌ふ。

『懐に御子を抱ける吾なれど 許させ給へ子心比女の神を。 此御子は水上の山の国津神の 美し御子なりわれ育くみつ。 めでたかる今日の祝ひの狭蓆に 仕へて楽し女神の吾も』

再び朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『朝霧比女の厚き心のもてなしに ゐやひの言葉吾なかりける。 葭原の国土の宝とまゐらせむ 火種を保つ此燧石を。 此宝一つありせば葭原の 国土清まりて永久に開けむ』

§ 2

Asagirihime-no-Kami, while performing the Kōdo dance, filled with smiles upon her face, chanted a Miuta:

'How grateful I am for the treasure of the Kuni and the flint,

Do you bestow it upon me, Asakahime-no-Kami?

To obtain the fire-seed of Kuwaenzan,

I have long broken my heart in pieces.

The demon Orochi, guarding the crater of Kuwaen,

Would not let the Kuni take the fire-seed.

Kuwaenzan collapsed and became a lake,

A lonely Kuni where the fire-seed was lost.

The flint bestowed by Asakahime-no-Kami,

Is the eternal treasure of this Yoshihara.'

Thereupon, Asagirihime-no-Kami, in her joy at being blessed with the flint, commanded Ōmiterashi-no-Kami, and leading the various Kamitachi, boarded the Torifune of Ama, descended to the Chijiyō with the flint, and caused fire to be set to the Yoshihara by riding the wind; as the Senpū blew at that moment, the fire spread in the Yomo and Hatsupau, and the wilderness of Maujiu, Dokujiya, water-plants, and reeds instantly became a sea of fire, and the spectacle was beyond compare.

Asagirihime-no-Kami watched this scene from the high palace on Mount Takamitsu and chanted a Miuta:

'By the flint of the blessing of Asakahime-no-Kami,

Our Kuni-hara is being renewed.

Seeing the redness of the smoke of the field fire,

Spreading flamingly in the Yomo, is joyful.

The crooked demons, the ugly Orochi, and the ugly weeds,

Are perishing by the power of the true fire.

From today, by the power of the true fire, I shall make the Kuni of Yoshihara,

A beautiful sacred place.'

Asakahime-no-Kami saw this scene and sang joyfully:

'The reeds of the Yoshihara, which have grown old over the years,

Are being burned away so easily.

As I watch the smoke rising in thick clouds,

How joyful it is that the calamities of the Kuni are vanishing.'

Having sung thus, and watching the scene of the field fire spreading, the Kamitachi raised cheers of 'Woe, woe.'

Leaving many attendant Kamitachi there, Ōmiterashi-no-Kami boarded the Torifune again and returned to this place, and reported in detail to the Lord God regarding the glorious achievement of the true fire.

'When I set the true fire to the Yoshihara, the wind rose,

And before my eyes, the ugly weeds burned away.

The crooked demons that lurked among the ugly weeds,

And the beasts and Orochi were tormented by the heat.

If it be so, the fierce beasts, the crooked demons,

And the Orochi will no longer dwell, and it shall be opened in peace.

I sent the Kamitachi to the Yomo, made torches,

And caused them to set the true fire.

While I watched, the field of ugly weeds burned out,

And as far as the eye could see, it became a field of ash.'

Asagirihime-no-Kami chanted a Miuta:

'How grateful I am for the achievement of the true fire, the Kuni of Yoshihara,

Shall live and prosper anew.

This flint, as a treasure of the Kuni, shall be enshrined forever,

In the palace of the Lord Ōkami.

The Kuni of Doa and the Kuni-hara of Yosan, from today,

Shall no longer retain the shadows of the crooked Kami.'

Thereupon, the banquet on the mountain ended, and having expressed deep gratitude to the party of Asakahime-no-Kami, she had Asasorao-no-Kami and Kunioo-no-Kami steer the Torifune to the port of Matsuura, and sent off the party of Ohi-shiro-no-Kami in peace.

Asakahime-no-Kami parted the clouds, and after a half-day's journey, arrived safely at the port of Matsuura, and chanted a Miuta:

'Carried on a rare ship, across the distant cloud-path,

I have arrived here in peace.

Convey my words to Asagirihime of Ohi-shiro,

That I have arrived safely.'

'I shall convey the words of the Kami of Ohi-shiro,

To Asagirihime-no-Kami in detail.'

With these words, Asasorao-no-Kami left Kunioo-no-Kami behind, boarded the Torifune, and returned high into the mid-sky.

Here, Asagirihime-no-Kami, wishing to repay the kindness of Asakahime-no-Kami, sent Kunioo-no-Kami, who had merit in building the Torifune, to serve as an attendant.

From this time, the party of six Kamitachi, together with their horses, floated upon the O-fune and set out toward the Kuni of the west.

原文（近代文語）

朝霧比女の神は雀踊りしながら満面に笑みを湛へ、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『ありがたし国土の宝と燧石 吾に賜ふか朝香比女の神。 火炎山火種を得むと村肝の 心を長く砕き来にけり。 鬼大蛇火炎の火口を守りつつ 国に火種を取らせざりけり。 火炎山陥没なして湖となり 火種の失せし淋しき国なりき。 朝香比女の神の賜ひし燧石は 此葭原の永久の宝ぞ』

茲に朝霧比女の神は、燧石を恵まれたる嬉しさに、大御照の神に命じ、諸々の神等を従へ、天の鳥船に搭乗させ、燧石をもちて地上に降らしめ、風に乗じて葭原に火を放たしめ給ひければ、折から吹き来る旋風に、火は四方八方に燃え拡がり、猛獣毒蛇、水奔草、葭草などの原野は忽ち火の海となり、其壮観譬ふるに物なかりけり。

朝霧比女の神は、高光山の高殿より此光景をみそなはし、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『朝香比女神の恵の燧石に わが国原はあらたまりゆく。 炎々と四方に拡ごる野火の煙の 赤きを見れば楽しかりけり。 曲鬼も醜の大蛇も醜草も 真火の力に亡び行くかも。 今日よりは真火の力に葭原の 国土を美しき聖所となさむ』

朝香比女の神は此光景を見、嬉しげに歌ひ給ふ。

『年を経て老い茂りたる葭原の 葭はもろくも焼かれけるかな。 濛々と立ちたつ煙見てあれば 国土の禍消ゆる楽しさ』

斯く歌ひ、互に野火の燃え拡がる光景を見て、神々はウオーウオーと歓声をあげ給ひける。

所へ数多の従神を残し置きて、大御照の神は、再び鳥船に乗り此場に帰らせ給ひ、真火のいさをしのいやちこなる事を〓__GT_13105__怜に委曲に奏上し給ふ。

『葭原に真火を放てば風立ちて 見る見る醜草焼け失せにけり。 醜草の中に潜みし曲鬼も 獣大蛇も暑さに悶えし。 かくならば猛き獣も曲鬼も 大蛇も棲まず安く開けむ。 神々を四方に遣はし松明を つくりて真火を放たしめしはや。 見るうちに醜草原は焼け尽きて 目路の限りは灰の野となりぬ』

朝霧比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『ありがたし真火のいさをに葭原の 国土新らしく生きて栄えむ。 この燧石国の宝と永久に 主の大神の御殿に祀らむ。 土阿の国土も予讃の国原も今日よりは 曲神のかげを留めざるべし』

茲に山上の宴会は終了し、朝香比女の神の一行に厚き感謝の辞を述べ、松浦の港まで朝空男の神、国生男の神をして鳥船を操らせ、御樋代神の一行を安く送りける。

朝香比女の神は、雲路を分けて半日のコースを経て、安々と松浦の港に着き給ひ、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『珍らしき船に乗せられ雲路はるか 渡りて安く此処に来つるも。 御樋代の朝霧比女にわが言葉 伝へ給へよ安く着きぬと』 『御樋代の神の御言葉まつぶさに 朝霧比女の神に伝へむ』

と朝空男の神は、国生男の神を後に残し、鳥船に乗り中空高く帰りける。

ここに朝霧比女の神は、朝香比女の神の好意に報いむとして、鳥船造りに功ある国生男の神を御供に仕ふべく遣はし給ひたるなり。

これより一行六神、駒諸共御舟に浮びて、西方の国土さして出で給ひける。
