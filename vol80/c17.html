Chapter 17: 水 Fire 垣 [2021]

水火垣〔2021〕

§ 1

Due to the eruption of Kuwaenzan, the land for a hundred ri in the vicinity became entirely a lake, and the lake water boiled like hot water; while most of the wild beasts, venomous snakes, and poisonous insects such as the ijichi met with the calamity of annihilation, the reptilian tribes with the strongest shells and hardest scales gathered on the shores of the lake, burrowing into the Suihonsau and Yoshigusa at the water's edge, and their poisonous harm became all the more severe, which was truly lamentable.

Oshigaoka, where the two gods Asazorao and Kuniumio descended, escaped the disaster of collapse, but because the lake water had expanded to within about one ri, the monstrosity of the countless Magatsu approaching Oshigaoka was beyond description.

Fuyuo, Kumakō, Torakō, and the spirits of the mountains, rivers, and seas exerted all their strength to fight, determined not to let them set even one foot into their dwelling at Oshigaoka, but alas, being spirits, they had no way to stop the onslaught of demons possessing physical forms, and they were in the depths of distress. Here, gaining strength from the divine power of the two pillars of gods who had descended riding the Heavenly Bird-boat, they approached the presence with trepidation, though somewhat calmed:

Spirits: 'Since the lake of fire appeared, the crooked gods have lost their places and seek to gather here. Even while the two pillars of gods have descended, the Magatsu are surging upon this place. Though we defend with all our might, how can our power as spirits suffice? Please save us.'

Upon hearing this, the two gods stood up, supporting their bodies against the trunk of the evergreen tree on Oshigaoka, and

The god Asazorao sang:

I who have descended to govern the land of Yoshiwara and Yosan, be at peace. Even if crooked demons and great serpents surge forward, I shall drive them away with the living Kototama.

The god Kuniumio sang:

Morning and evening, uniting our power with the gods, we shall peacefully protect Oshigaoka.

Fuyuo sang:

We are grateful for the divine proclamation of the two pillars of gods; having heard it, we are revived.

The god Asazorao sang:

Though you are spirits, I shall proclaim; support the living Kototama.

Fuyuo sang:

Receiving the divine proclamation upon our necks, we shall proclaim the living Kototama with all our might.

Just at that moment, voices of agony, cries of war, and shouts arose all at once; wild beasts, venomous snakes, and Suihon-ki gathered at the foot of Oshigaoka as the most peaceful habitat, some roaring and raging, others writhing, and the surroundings of Oshigaoka were noisy with the piercing cries of the Suihon-ki, and though they tried to ascend all at once, it was amusing to see them unable to ascend, hindered by the Kototama of the two gods. The two gods and the spirits, including Fuyuo, took turns proclaiming the living Kototama.

The god Asazorao sang with a clear and resonant voice:

I shall clearly proclaim the Kototama that appeared in the water and fire of the Lord God as the pacification of the land. Even if the heaven and earth change their places, I shall simply pacify them with the noble Kototama. Even if tens of thousands of crooked gods attack, I shall cut them away with the sword of Kototama. By the blessing of the beautiful and stern Kototama, I shall turn the crooked words of this land. The crater that burned with flames has suddenly vanished and become a lake. Even if tens of thousands of demon serpents come to attack, should I, a heavenly god, fear them? The Kuwaenzan has suddenly ended up as a lake, thanks to the blessing of the living Kototama. Both trees and grasses have sunk deep to the bottom of the lake of fire, due to the wildness of the crooked. Since I am the one who descended to create the land, are demons and serpents of any account? This defiled land shall also become clear, blessed by the water and fire of the Kototama.

The end of the evil-hearted crooked demons is clearly visible in the waves of the lake. The sky has clouded over with the water and fire of the Magatsu, to the point where even the light of the clear moon cannot be seen. I am a heavenly god who crossed the white clouds of the sky and descended; I shall not fear the crooked. Even if there are many demons and serpents pressing in, there is a shining jewel at Oshigaoka. The Suihon-ki of the mocking hags and laughing hags will now have no power to resist. The crater of the high mountain has suddenly sunk to the bottom of the lake, becoming waves of boiling water. Can they stand against the living Kototama bestowed by the great Lord God who shakes the heavens? I shall illuminate the land of Yoshiwara, where even the moon and sun are hidden, and govern it in peace. Do the crooked not know the power by which both heaven and earth clear up, thanks to the merit of my Kototama? I descended holding the mirror of Kototama to illuminate this land of eternal darkness.

火炎山の爆発により、附近百里の地は全く湖水となり、湖水は熱湯の如く煮えくり返り、猛獣、毒蛇、イヂチ等の毒虫も大半殲滅の厄に遇ひけるが、中にも最も甲羅の強く、鱗の堅き爬虫族は、湖水の岸辺に集り来り、汀辺の水奔草や葭草の中にもぐり込み、一層其の害毒甚しくなりゆくこそ歎てけれ。

朝空男、国生男二神が天降りたる忍ケ丘は、陥落の難は免れたれども、約一里附近まで湖水の展開せるより、あらゆる曲津は忍ケ丘に向つて、幾百千とも限りなく上り来る物凄さ、名状すべからず。

冬男、熊公、虎公、山、川、海の精霊は、忍ケ丘のわが住処には一歩も踏み入れさせじと全力を尽し戦へども、悲しきかな精霊の身の上なれば、形体を持てる悪魔の襲来を喰ひ止むる由もなく、苦心を極め居たりける。ここに、天の鳥船に乗りて天降りましたる二柱の神の神力に力を得て、稍落着きながら御前に恐る恐る進み寄り、

『火の湖の現れしより曲神は 処失ひ集ひ来むとす。 二柱神の天降りし間もあらず 曲津は此処に押し寄せ来る。 力限り防げど精霊わが力 如何で及ばむ救はせ給へ』

これを聞くより二神は立ち上り、忍ケ丘の常磐樹の幹に御身を支へながら、

朝空男の神は歌ふ。

『葭原の予讃の国原治むべく 天降りしわれよ心安かれ。 如何ならむ曲鬼大蛇押しよすも われはやらはむ生言霊に』

国生男の神は歌ふ。

『朝夕に神と力を一つにし 忍ケ丘を安く守らむ』

冬男は歌ふ。

『有難し二柱神の御宣示 聞きてわれらは蘇りぬる』

朝空男の神は歌ふ。

『汝れ等は精霊なれどわが宣らむ 生言霊を補ひまつれ』

冬男は歌ふ。

『御宣示頸に受けて力限り われ等は宣らむ生言霊を』

かかる折しも、阿鼻叫喚の声、鬨の声、一時にドツと起り、猛獣、毒蛇、水奔鬼は最も平安なる棲処として忍ケ丘の麓に集り来り、咆哮怒号するあり、のたうちまはるあり、忍ケ丘のまはりは水奔鬼の矢叫の声かしましく、一斉に上らむとせしも、二神等の生言霊に妨げられて上りあぐみたるぞ面白き。二神及び冬男以下の精霊は、交る交る生言霊を宣る。

朝空男の神は音吐朗々として歌ふ。

『主の神の御水火に現れにし言霊を 国の鎮めと清けく宣らむ。 アオウエイ天地処を変ふるとも ただに鎮めむ貴の言霊に。 幾万の曲神襲ひ来るとも 斬りて放らむ言霊剣に。 麗しき厳の言霊幸はひて この国原の曲言向けむ。 炎々と燃えたちし火口は忽ちに 消えて湖水となりにけらしな。 鬼大蛇たとへ幾万寄せ来とも 恐るべきかは天津神われは。 火炎山忽ち湖となり果てぬ 生言霊の幸はひによりて。 木も草も火の湖の底深く 沈みけるかな曲の荒びに。 国土生むと天降り来りしわれなれば 鬼も大蛇も物の数かは。 汚れたるこの国原も言霊の 水火幸はひて澄み渡るべし。

心悪しき曲鬼どもの身の果ては ありあり見えぬ湖水の波に。 冴え渡る月の光も見えぬまで 御空曇りぬ曲津の水火に。 白雲の空を渡りて天降りてし われ天津神よ曲等恐れじ。 迫り来る鬼や大蛇は多くとも 忍ケ丘には光る玉あり。 譏り婆笑ひ婆アの水奔鬼も 今は手向ふ力無からむ。 高山の火口は忽ち湖の 底に沈みて湧きたつ湯の波。 千早振る主の大神の賜ひてし 生言霊に刃向ひ得むや。 月も日もかくれて見えぬ葭原の 国土を照らして安く治めむ。 天も地もわが言霊の功績に 晴れゆく力を曲は知らずや。 常闇のこの国原を伊照らすと 言霊鏡持ちて天降りし』

§ 2

Kuniumio-no-Kami sings:

'As I look out over the waves, the demon Orochi, / How wretched it appears in its drowning state. / Tossed by the waves of the boiling Kosui, / The Orochi writhes, spitting blood. / The Nubatama darkness has descended, as the breath / Exhaled by the Magatsumi grows ever heavier. / Though I have earnestly proclaimed the Ikukototama, / It seems it does not enter the ears of the crooked. / In the explosion of the Kuwaenzan, where fields and mountains lie, / The Kusaki have withered, trembling in fear. / Is this the state of a world that is Haka, / Where all that is crooked seeks to perish? / The Hikiyama have sunk into the lake, and the Kuwaenzan / Floats upon the water with its summit narrowed. / The falling rain must have been fierce, for the lake / Has submerged both the Hikiyama and the Takayama. / Even the Magatsumi, in the Kahari of the Kuwaenzan, / Trembles in fear, its body having perished. / The evening view of the lake, seen dimly through the mist, / Is truly desolate.

Is it the end of the Magakami's Inochi, or a cry of victory / That echoes from beneath this hill? / I shall purify with the blessing of the Ikukototama / The Ōnohara where Shikogusa grow as far as the eye can see. / The Kahari of the Ametsuchi, such as has never been seen since ancient times, / Must be a warning from the Kami. / This Kunihara, so pitiful that one cannot bear to open one's eyes and look, / Is a warning from the Kami. / Mōmō black clouds hang low, wrapping the Nozora of the Yoshihara, / And the Tsukihi cannot be seen. / In the depths of the Yachihiro Kosui, the Magatsumi / Likely lurks once more to commit disaster. / No matter how fiercely the Magatsumi Orochi may rage, / I shall subdue and harmonize it with the divine authority of the Kami. / On the surface of the lake, which has boiled like hot water, / Steam is rising up. / Without knowing the distinction between near and far, / How fearful is the warning of the Kami of disaster. / There is nothing that equals the noble merit of the Kototama / As a true power to save the world.

I am the one who serves the Mihishiro-no-Kami, / The master of the Ikukototama. / I descended quickly to Shinobugaoka / And beheld the state of the Yoshihara-no-Kuni. / Intending to give birth to a beautiful divine land, / I have descended, bearing the words of the Kami. / To the extent of my power, I shall not leave it uncreated, / This divine land that rejoices and thrives in such splendor. / Even in the Yoshihara-no-Kuni where the Orochi dwells, if I am there, / It shall be very peaceful; be brave and stand firm.'

Fuyu-no-O sings:

'On this hill where the two pillars of Kami have descended, / I shall be resurrected and prevent the crooked. / The Kototama of the two pillars of Kami has blessed me, / And the power of my soul has been added. / Since it has come to this, I, a spirit, shall also be brave / And advance toward the fortress of the demons. / I feel as if I have been born as a person of this world, / As the radiance of my spiritual body begins to shine. / For long years I was a demon of Shinobugaoka, / Waiting in vain for the descent of Kimi. / Even I, of the wretched spirit, from this day forth / Shall serve behind the Kami. / While preserving the life of my resurrected existence, / It is a joy to become a flower of the Yomi-no-yo. / My wavering soul has also settled, / And my unmoving heart stands brave. / Even if the demon Orochi and the ugly demon-hag come to attack, / I shall no longer fear, for I am a Kami. / The sound of the arrow-cry echoes at the foot of the mountain, / As both the demons and the Orochi seek to climb up.

Since I have built the Kototama Mizuhigaki high, / No manner of crookedness shall be able to climb it. / Embraced by the noble grace of the Kami, / I shall pass the time peacefully at Shinobugaoka. / Shall I fear even the lake of fire where the mist rises? / No matter how the crooked may lurk. / Even if the water-running demons, the Mōryō, and the crooked spirits gather in countless numbers, / Why should I fear them?'

Kumakō sings:

'Who would have thought that the two pillars of Kami would appear / And cause the disaster of the land to be removed? / Though I am now a demon of Shinobugaoka, / My original soul has become a Kami. / My soul is pure from the beginning, Kannagara, / For it is an Inochi received from the Kami. / Chased by the water-running demons, for long days / I hid at Shimizugaoka. / It is sad when I think of Shimizugaoka, / Where I hid lonely with Torakō. / My lord, too, was deceived by the laughing hag / And perished at Shimizugaoka.'

Asazora-no-O-no-Kami sings:

'As I listen to your various stories, / I perceive the strength of the ferocity of the crooked. / The land of Yoshihara shall be without even the shadow of the Magatsu, / For I am a Kami who shall purify and clear it.'

Torakō sings:

'I am grateful for the words of the noble Kami; / I shall not forget them, no matter how many ages pass.'

The mountain sings:

Mountain: 'Though I am not an Onibaba, I am a true Miko of the Kami, living in Kamunagara. I traveled and stopped at Shinobugaoka, where my Inochi was taken by the laughing crone. The souls of many have had their precious Inochi stolen by the Onibaba. By the Miizu of the Futahashiragami, may the shadows of the Suihonki be extinguished from the Chijiyō.'

Kuniumio no Kami sings:

Kuniumio no Kami: 'I shall purify and clear the vast, boundless Kunihara without a corner left, and destroy all evil. Be that as it may, I shall build a palace on this hill and govern the Kuni.'

The river sings:

River: 'The light of the Kami of Ametsuchi has appeared, and the darkness of the Yoshihara has begun to clear. I, too, have followed the same Unmei and become an Otome who turned into an Oni. From Kefu, I shall illuminate my clouded heart and continue to proclaim the Ikukototama.'

The sea sings:

Sea: 'Receiving the blessings of the Umiyama, I now dwell peacefully at Shinobugaoka.'

Fuyuo sings:

Fuyuo: 'May the Futahashiragami illuminate the boundless Kuni of Yoshihara without a corner left. Though I am powerless, I shall follow in your Mishirihe and serve in the Kamuwaza. I pray only that you use the bears, tigers, mountains, rivers, and seas for the Kamuwaza.'

Asazorao no Kami sings:

Asazorao no Kami: 'I accept thy prayer, and together with Kuniumio, I shall remain here for a Shibashi and govern.'

Having sung thus, they waited for the day when the lake of fire would return to Heion. At the foot of Shinobugaoka, Sūman of Maujiu, venomous snakes, and Suihonki, having lost their place of escape, were crowded together, moving to and fro in confusion.
