Chapter 20: 復命 [2024]

復命〔2024〕

§ 1

The small island floating in the center of the lake of fire is called Akiojima. Due to the collapse of Kuwaenzan, boiling water gushed forth, instantly forming a lake one hundred ri in all directions, and this is the island that remains floating slightly above the water at its summit. Here, due to the Tenpenchii, although all manner of beasts, poisonous snakes, and Suihonki had largely perished, the stubbornly persistent and strong-willed Warai-baba, Soshiri-baba, Okoribaba, and Nakibaba had barely managed to cling to this island, making it their sole habitat and plotting to commit all manner of atrocities. However, the spirit of Akio, who had leapt into the crater and become white bones, remained on this island, serving as the guardian of the island together with the pine, bamboo, plum, and cherry trees. The Suihonki, fearing that as long as Akio remained they had no way to indulge in their violent behavior, were engaged in a decisive battle day and night without interruption, trying by any means to destroy Akio. Akio, while hiding his body in the crater of the dormant Kuwaenzan, relied on this place as a fortress of iron walls and frequently launched Kototama. The Suihonki exhausted every means to pollute and destroy the spirits of Akio's party with their foul Kototama. As the highest point of Akiojima was but one hundred ken from the water's surface, the Suihonki, led by Warai-baba, surrounded the perimeter of the crater and incessantly spewed forth turbid Kototama day and night without distinction.

Warai-baba peered into the wall of the crater, opened her mouth wide, and with eyes upturned, said:

Warai-baba: 'Ahahahaha, ihihihihi, ufufufu, ehehehehe. Ohonohoho, you Akio brats, Leave this island base quickly. Gyahahahaha, this island base is the summit Of Kuwaenzan, so it is my habitat. If you do not leave soon, this old hag Will gather demons and attack you to death. Return quickly while the footing is still bright, I will not let you live on this floating island. I know not if they are your followers, but the pine, bamboo, Plum and cherry are worthless brats. This is not an island for brats to live on Who have fallen into my traps and lost their lives. If you are going to stay stubbornly forever, try staying, I will spew fire again and kill you. Though this island sleeps quietly now, It is a terrifying island that will soon spew fire. To think you can live here With the power of your weak spirits is shallow.'

Akio sang indignantly:

Akio: 'Do you not know that the lake becoming Kuwaenzan Is the sign of the living Kototama I proclaimed? The time has come to destroy you Who have taken the lives of many Kunitsukami. If you value the life of your soul's thread, Leave this island as quickly as you can. O hateful hag who tries to defile The island base by spewing forth foul breath.'

Soshiri-baba, thinking Warai-baba's Kototama battle was too lenient, stood up abruptly and, leaning against the wall of the old crater, said:

Soshiri-baba: 'Gyahahahaha, you spineless Akio brat, If you value your life, leave this place. If you will not listen, I will make you listen, With all the power of Soshiri-baba. In any case, this island base is our Eternal habitat, so leave, leave quickly. If you do not leave the island no matter what, I will gather Many Suihonki and torment you. Demons, demons, gather and come, press upon them Until you take the lives of these brats.'

As she sang thus, a strange sound roared from the entire island as if the embankment of a great river had collapsed, and it became a scene that threatened to deafen the ears.

To this place, the Heavenly Bird-Boat came roaring high through the sky and landed gracefully on the flat sandy ground of Akiojima. Appearing from within were the god of Asasorao, who serves the Mihishiro-kami, the god of Kunioo, and beginning with Akio's younger brother Fuyuo who had entered the spirit world, Kuma-kō, Tora-kō, the spirits of the mountains, rivers, and seas, as well as Haruo and Natsuo who had departed from the sacred ground of Suijiyō in search of their younger brother's whereabouts, along with Mizuoto, Seoto, and many other followers. Seeing this, the leaders of the Suihonki gathered at the water's edge, determined to fight with all their might, and began a demonstration while raising war cries.

原文（近代文語）

火の湖の中央に浮びたる小さき小島を秋男島といふ。火炎山の陥落により、熱湯吹き出で、忽ち百里四方の湖となり、其頂の僅に水上に浮べる島なりける。茲に天変地異の為め、あらゆる猛獣毒蛇も、水奔鬼も、大略滅亡したれども、流石に執着深き意地強き笑ひ婆、譏り婆、瘧婆、泣婆は辛うじて此島に取付き、此処を唯一の棲処とし、あらゆる暴虐を振舞はむとたくらみ居たりけるが、噴火口に飛入りて白骨となり居りし秋男の霊は此島に止り、松、竹、梅、桜と共に島の司となり居たりしが、水奔鬼の面々は、秋男のある限り其暴状を逞うするに由なきを恐れ、如何にもして秋男を亡ぼさむものと、昼夜間断なく決戦をつとめて居る。秋男は休息したる火炎山の火口に身を潜めながら、此処を堅城鉄壁と頼み、頻に言霊を打出しける。水奔鬼はいやらしき言霊を以て秋男一行の精霊を濁し亡ぼさむと、あらゆる手段を尽しける。秋男島は水面より最頂上と雖も百間ばかりなりければ、水奔鬼は笑ひ婆を先頭に、火口の周囲を取巻き、昼夜の区別なく、連続的に濁れる言霊を吐出すこそ嘆てけれ。

笑ひ婆は火口の壁内を覗き、大口を開けて眼を釣上げながら、

『アハハハハ、イヒヒヒヒ、ウフフフフ、エヘヘヘヘ

オホホホホおのれ秋男の餓鬼どもよ 早く立去れ此島ケ根を。 ギヤハハハハ此島ケ根は火炎山の 頂なれば吾棲処なり。 何時までも立退かざれば此婆が 鬼を集めて攻め殺すべし。 足許の明るい間に早帰れ 此浮島には住まはせぬぞや。 其方の供か知らねど松、竹や 梅と桜はつまらぬ餓鬼ぞや。 この方の罠に陥り身亡せたる 餓鬼の住むべき島でないぞや。 何時までもしぶとう居るなら居つてみよ 又火を吐きて殺してやるぞや。 此島は今は静に眠れども 今に火を出す恐ろしき島よ。 其方の弱きみたまの力もて 住めると思ふは浅はかなるぞや』

秋男は憤然として歌ふ。

『火炎山湖となりしも吾宣れる 生言霊のしるしと知らずや。 国津神の数多の生命を奪ひたる 汝を亡ぼす時は来にけり。 玉の緒のみたまの生命惜しければ 少しも早く島を立ち去れ。 汚れたる息を吐き出し島ケ根を 穢さむとする憎らしき婆』

譏り婆は笑ひ婆の言霊戦を手緩しと思ひしか、すつくと立ち、旧火口の壁に寄添ひながら、

『ギヤハハハハ腰抜野郎の秋男の餓鬼よ 生命惜しくば此場を立去れ。 聞かざれば聞くやうにして聞かすぞや 譏り婆アの力限りに。 兎も角も此島ケ根は吾々の 永遠の棲処ぞ早く去れ去れ。 どうしても島を去らねば水奔鬼 数多集めて悩まし呉れむ。 鬼よ鬼よ集まれ来れ此餓鬼が 生命取るまで詰め寄せ来れ』

斯く歌ふや島全体より、大河の堤防の崩れたる如く怪音轟き来り、耳を聾せむばかりの光景とはなりぬ。

斯かる処へ天の鳥船御空を高く轟かせながら、秋男島の平坦なる砂地に悠々と舞ひ降り、中より現れしは御樋代神に仕へたる朝空男の神、国生男の神を始めとし、精霊界に入れる秋男が弟冬男及び熊公、虎公、山、川、海の精霊一行及び水上山の聖場より弟の所在を探ねて出発したる春男、夏男を始め、水音、瀬音、其他数多の従者にてありければ、水奔鬼の司も此処ぞ一生懸命と、死力を尽して戦ふべく汀に集まり、鬨の声を揚げつつ示威運動を試みて居る。

§ 2

Akio, upon seeing this, was filled with such joy that he knew not where to place his spiritual body. He immediately descended from the crater, leading four attendants, and thanked the two deities for the hardships of their long journey. Furthermore, in the joy of meeting his younger brother, the spirit, as well as his two elder brothers and the attendant deities, he forgot himself and danced wildly while singing.

Akio: 'Oh, the attendants of the noble Mihishirogami, the two deities have descended from heaven. Though I am now in the Seireikai, I live by beholding the appearance of the Kami. My soul lives and flourishes, beholding the light of the two deities' descent from heaven.'

The Kami Asazorao sings:

Asazorao: 'To save the land of Yoshihara from calamity, we have crossed over to Takamitsuyama. Riding the Torifune, we soared through the great sky and first descended upon Oshigaoka.'

The Kami Kuniumio sings:

Kuniumio: 'Descending to Oshigaoka, I saw that your younger brother Fuyuo had become a spirit and was dwelling there. Alas, Fuyuo has cast away his life through the schemes of the laughing hag, Suihonki. It is pitiful that you, too, have lost your life through the tricks of the laughing hag and the mocking hag.'

Akio sings:

Akio: 'How grateful I am for the appearance of the two deities; this Shimanegane shall surely flourish in peace.'

Fuyuo sings:

Fuyuo: 'It is sorrowful to think that my beloved elder brother has also entered the world of spirits. Yet, it is a blessing of the Kami to meet my brother here on this Shimanegane. It is a joy to see the figures of my elder brothers, Haruo and Natsuo, before my eyes now.'

Mizune sings:

Mizune: 'Following in your footsteps to this island, it is a joy to meet the young prince who is no more. Though you are a lord no longer of this world, it is a joy to see you shining in the Seireikai.'

Seone sings:

Seone: 'Having received the words of the lord of Iwagane, I have searched and come to meet you. It is a joy to meet you on the shore of this island through the deep grace of the Kami.'

Haruo sings:

Haruo: 'Coming here because my father's words were heavy, I see that my younger brother has already become a demon. It is a sorrowful thing to think that my brother has had his life taken by Suihonki.'

Natsuo sings:

Natsuo: 'Though I have met my younger brother of the mortal world, I feel a certain loneliness. Yet, hearing that the life of the soul lives forever, I have comforted my heart. It is sad to think that if our father were to hear of this state, he would surely grieve. Saved by the heavenly Torifune of the Amatsukami, I have crossed over to meet you.'

Akio sings:

Akio: 'How grateful I am to hear the kind heart of my elder brother; I am revived. The life of the Yomi world is long; pray tell my father that I dwell in peace. O two deities, hear my prayer; I shall conquer the four hags. Save me, who am powerless, and conquer the demon hags for the sake of the Kamikuni.'

Thereupon, the two deities climbed to the summit and stood there. As they sang the heavenly number-songs with clear voices, the laughing, malaria, mocking, and weeping hag-officials—Suihonki's forces—were struck by the Kototama, received wounds over their entire bodies, and fled the lake castle with their lives barely intact. In the end, their strength exhausted, they fell into the boiling lake water and were utterly annihilated, which was a joyous event.

The two Amatsukami appeared on the island and repelled the Magatsuhi with the living Kototama. Although the two, Fuyuo and Akio, had become spirits, their souls remained alive through the power of the Kami. Meeting their two elder brothers of the mortal world, they felt as if they had been revived. The Torifune of the Mihishirogami carried the party high into the sky and sent them off. Fuyuo and the others were sent to Oshigaoka, while Haruo and Natsuo returned to Mizukami. Akio and the others, together with the four souls, became the masters of Akio Island. Flapping the wings of the Torifune, the two deities returned to report to the Mihishirogami. Crossing the sea of the sky, the two reported everything in detail to the Mihishirogami here. Although the Magatsuhi of Yoshihara had largely perished, the calamity of the Suihon-grass did not cease. Fierce beasts, great serpents, and poisonous insects spread, and the land of Yoshihara was not yet created.

Postscript

Haruo, Natsuo, as well as Mizune, Seone, and other attendants, did not lose a single life. Through the help of the Kami, they returned safely to Mizukami and reported the circumstances of the two younger brothers' fates to the Kami. When they told their father, Iwagane, every detail, Iwagane also shed tears at the depth of the divine grace and became absorbed in offering prayers of gratitude, shutting himself away before the Kami morning and evening.

Next, Akio named the floating island in the lake, along with the pine, bamboo, plum, and cherry trees, 'Akio Island.' He established his eternal home there and, as a Kami of the lake, came to protect the passing ships and fishermen forever. Furthermore, Fuyuo settled permanently in Oshigaoka with Kumakō, Torakō, and the spirits of the mountains, rivers, and seas. He came to be respected by the people of the land forever as a Kami who protects the prosperity of the Yoshihara land from the spirit world and destroys demons. Anakashiko.

秋男は之を見るより歓天喜地、霊の身の置き処も知らず、忽ち火口より四人の従者を引連れ降り来り、二柱の前に遠来の苦労を謝し、且弟の精霊や二人の兄及び従神等に面会したる嬉しさに、吾を忘れて踊り狂ひつつ歌ふ。

附言

春男、夏男並に水音、瀬音其他の従者等は、一人も生命を落すものなく、無事神の助けにより、水上山に復命し、二人の弟の身の成行等を審さに神前に報告し、父の巌ケ根にも一伍一什を物語りければ、巌ケ根も神恩の深きに落涙し、朝夕神前に差籠りて感謝祝詞の奏上に熱中したりける。

次に秋男は松、竹、梅、桜と共に湖中の浮島を秋男島と命名け、此処に永遠の住家を営み、湖中の神として、往来の船や漁夫等を永遠に守る事とはなりぬ。又冬男は忍ケ丘に熊公、虎公及び山、川、海の精霊と共に永久に鎮まり、霊界より葭原国の栄えを守り、悪魔を亡ぼす神として永遠に国人より尊敬さるる事となりにける。あなかしこ。
