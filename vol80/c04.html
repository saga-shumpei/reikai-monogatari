Chapter 4: 姉妹婆 [2008]

姉妹婆〔2008〕

§ 1

Standing outside the dilapidated house of the Laughing Crone of Shinobigaoka, the three Seirei—Fuyuo, Kumakō, and Torakō—judging the time to be right, stepped forward. Showing a hint of surprise, and in the agony of being unable to move, she laughed faintly, 'Ahahahaha, ihihihihi,' glaring with her eyes as she looked up at the faces of the Sannansanjiyo.

Fuyuo, seeing this state, spoke:

Fuyuo: 'As retribution for the suffering you caused me in days past, what manner of thing is this state of yours? Do you, Laughing Crone, now realize that the warning of the Ametsuchi Kami is revealed? The Kamigami have borrowed the arms of Kuma and Tora to pull out your two hands. Though your arms appear to be attached to your body, their joints have been pulled out. Though I feel it is pitiful, there is no help for it; I cannot leave the Inochi of the Laughing Crone's Tama intact. How pathetic is the end of the crone who has stolen the many Inochi of the Kunitsukami. After you have cast away the Inochi of a Seirei, I think there is no place for you to go. How pathetic is the end of the Tama of the Onibaba who offered poisoned tea on this hill. You persistently followed us even to Shimizugaoka, only to be tormented by Kuma and Tora. If it has come to this, Crone, you should repent and receive the help of the Kami directly.'

While groaning, the crone squeezed out her hoarse voice:

Onibaba: 'That I offered poisoned tea to you who came wandering, was because I thought it would take your Inochi. I gave you the poisoned broth to help you from the agony and suffering of the Utsushiyo. Forgetting to be grateful, what kind of heart is it that makes you resent this crone? This crone offered the poison to increase the Ujiko of Shinobigaoka. It was a sincere heart to take away the limited Inochi of the Utsushiyo and grant them the Toha Inochi. Shinobigaoka is a place consisting only of those who were saved by having their Inochi taken for my sake.'

Fuyuo, indignant, spoke:

Fuyuo: 'This Onibaba, who killed those of us with the duty to create the land, must be a Mōryō. There is no reason for me to offer a reply before you; I am one whose Utsushiyo Inochi has been stolen. I have become a Seirei, and I am now one who has no face to return to my hometown. From here on, I shall destroy the Tama of the crone and become the master of Shinobigaoka. Give up and perish quickly, Onibaba; the Inochi of your Tama is now mine.'

Kumakō spoke:

Kumakō: 'It was my Tama that became wretched after falling into the trap of the Onibaba's schemes. Just the two of us waited for the time at Shimizugaoka, wondering when we would clear this grudge. If it has come to this, no longer shall my strength be insufficient; I shall cut down this Onibaba and cast her away. Joy and delight well up at once, and today I shall end the Inochi of the crone.'

While lying down, the crone spoke from beneath her painful breath:

Onibaba: 'Do not spout insolence, Seirei of Kumakō, you fool who fell for my schemes. You wretches who think the Inochi of a Seirei dies, you are fools who know not the Yūmei. No matter how the Inochi of this crone is pressed, I shall return the grudge without flinching or faltering. Though the physical body may die, the Seirei shall not perish for thousands of generations. I shall live on forever and torment your Inochi in a thousand changes and ten thousand transformations. I am not a crone who will flinch at being struck by the likes of you; I am merely resting for a short while.'

Torakō spoke:

Torakō: 'What a deep-seated grudge the crone has; even now, she only spouts insolence. It is hateful how the crone continues to curse even until her death, while having both her arms pulled out. I shall try to stop her breath with one blow, while smashing the skull of this crone. The time has come to clear my grudge; realize this, Crone, this morning of today.'

The crone stuck out her long tongue, and while sweating coldly, she remained stubborn:

Onibaba: 'Torakō, do not talk nonsense; this crone is a warrior who cannot be destroyed even if you cut me. Even if tens of thousands of people come to attack, do you not know the Laughing Crone who does not flinch? No matter what difficulties I encounter, I shall laugh it off with 'Ahahahaha, ihihihihi.' I laugh even when facing a crisis; if one laughs, the Inochi will never perish for Toha. How pathetic are those who do not know how to laugh; they are always angry or crying. You three maidens took advantage of my weakness and have slowly begun to reveal your true nature. The Tama of this crone shall not perish; I shall live forever and return the grudge to you maidens. Let the mountains, rivers, and seas remember well; I shall soon repay the grudge of today.'

忍ケ丘の笑ひ婆が破れ家の外に立ちて、様子をうかがひ居たる冬男、熊公、虎公の三つの精霊は、時こそよしと進み寄れば、やや驚きの色を見せながら、身動きならぬ苦しさに、アハハハハ、イヒヒヒヒとかすかに笑ひ、目を怒らせ、三男三女の顔を見上げて居る。

冬男はこの体を見て、

『さきの日に吾苦しめし報いにて このありさまは何事なるかも。 笑ひ婆思ひ知りしや天地の 神のいましめ今あらはれぬ。 神々は熊と虎との腕をかり 汝が両手を引き抜かせたり。 両腕は体につける如見ゆるとも その関節は抜けてありけり。 いぢらしと思へど詮なし笑ひ婆の 霊魂の生命を断たねばおかじ。 国津神の数多の生命奪ひたる 婆アの最後のあはれなるかな。 精霊の生命すてたる其の後は 行くべき所あらじと思ふ。 この丘に毒茶を進めし鬼婆の みたまの果てぞあはれなるかな。 しつこくも清水ケ丘まで追ひ来り 熊と虎とにいためられける。 斯くならば婆よ心をあらためて 神の助けを直にうくべし』

婆は呻吟きながら、しわがれ声をしぼりて、

『迷ひ来し汝に毒茶を進めしも 生命奪ふと思へばなりけり。 現世のもだえ苦しみ助けむと 吾は毒湯を与へたるなり。 感謝することを忘れてこの婆を 恨むは何の心ぞやそも。 この婆は忍ケ丘の氏子をば 殖すが為に毒を進めし。 かぎりある現の生命を抜きとりて 永久の生命を与ふる真心。 わが為に生命うばはれ救はれし 者ばかりなる忍ケ丘ぞや』

冬男は憤然として、

『国土つくる務めある身を殺したる この鬼婆は魍魎なるらむ。 御前にかへりごとせむ由もなし 現の生命奪はれし吾。 精霊となりて故郷にかへるべき かむばせもなきわが身なりけり。 この上は婆アのみたまを亡ぼして 忍ケ丘の司とならむ。 あきらめて早く亡びよ鬼婆よ 汝がみたまの生命はわがもの』

熊公は、

『鬼婆のたくみの罠におちいりて われは果敢なくなりしみたまぞ。 この恨いつか晴らすと只二人 清水ケ丘に時を待ちしよ。 斯くならば最早力も及ぶまじ この鬼婆を斬りて放らな。 嬉しさと楽しさ一度に湧き出でて 婆アの生命を今日は断つなり』

婆アは寝ながら苦しき息の下より、

『へらず口たたくな熊公の精霊よ 吾のたくみにかかりし馬鹿者。 精霊の生命死すると思ふ奴 幽冥知らぬたぶれなりけり。 この婆の生命は如何に迫るとも ひるまずたゆまず仇をかへさむ。 肉体は死すことあるも精霊は 幾千代までも亡びざるなり。 どこまでも生きながらへて汝が生命 千変万化に悩ましくれなむ。 貴様等に討たれてひるむ婆ならず しばしの間をやすむのみなる』

虎公は、

『執念の深き婆かも今となりて へらず口のみたたき居るなり。 両腕を引き抜かれながら知死期まで ののしる婆アの心にくきも。 一打ちに息とめてみむこの婆の 頭骸骨をば打ちくだきつつ。 わが恨み晴らさむときは来りけり 思ひしれ婆ア今日の朝を』

婆アは長い舌をベロリと出し、冷汗をかきながら尚もしぶとく、

『虎公よ馬鹿をほざくなこの婆は 斬つても斬れぬ亡びぬつはものぞ。 よしやよし幾万人の攻め来とも ひるまぬ笑ひの婆アを知らずや。 如何ならむ悩みにあふもアハハハハ イヒヒヒヒヒと笑ひ過さむ。 難局に処しても吾は笑ふなり 笑へば生命は永久に亡びず。 笑ふこと知らぬ輩のあはれさよ いつも怒りつ泣きつ居るなり。 三人の乙女は弱味をつけこみて そろそろ生地をあらはしにけり。 この婆のみたまは亡びず何時までも 生きて乙女に仇を返さむ。 山も川も海もおぼえて居れよかし 今に報いむ今日の恨みを』

§ 2

Yama raised her eyebrows slightly and her voice trembled:

Yama: 'For the old woman who took the life of the flower bud, I shall repay the many grievances. Until this day, I have served the old woman, watching for an opening with a smiling face. How careless the old woman was, to pass the time with an easy heart, unaware of my true feelings. When I think of my mother in my Furusato, who must be grieving day and night, I feel nothing but hatred. I shall destroy the precious life of this old woman and clear away the calamities of the world.'

Baba had a terrifying look of anger on her face:

Baba: 'Ahahaha, how pathetic, Otome Yama. You are a fool who does not know your own place. Do you think you can preserve your spiritual life without my permission, you foolish wretch? I am the wife of Enma; I have built my mansion here and I shall cut off your life. Do not despise me as an old woman of Miguru appearance; I am the wife of the Daiōsama.'

Kawa sings:

Kawa: 'I know not if you are the wife of the Daiō, but you are an old woman who does only evil things. As long as this old woman remains in the Iumeikai, the Seireitachi shall not be able to ascend. I shall not mind meeting with any kind of suffering; I will not stop until I have taken the life of the old woman. By pouring poison into the rapids of the river, this old woman has committed the Magawaza of taking human lives.'

Baba:

Baba: 'You Otome, what are you saying without knowing the truth? You are a blind person who does not know the light of the old woman.'

Umi:

Umi: 'If it has come to this, no matter how you struggle, it will be of no avail; I shall repay the long-standing grievances against the old woman. Can a flower-like Otome remain silent after having her precious life stolen?'

Baba:

Baba: 'Blather whatever you like as you please; if I do not permit it, you shall have no place to live.'

While saying this, she groaned, sweating profusely like a waterfall, 'Ume, ume.' Here, Fuyuo turned to Otome:

Fuyuo: 'Are you not a lowly Otome, you who offered me Dokuchiya the other day? I thought you were a Teshita of the Onibaba, but today you have become an enemy of Baba.'

Yama replied to this:

Yama: 'I could not disobey the strict Oshihe of the Onibaba, so I offered the tea of Suihonsau. I felt sorry, but it could not be helped; please forgive my Magawaza.'

Kawa sings:

Kawa: 'I thought that you, being a true Masurao, would make yourself a Seirei and lend your strength. Because you are a Masurao, I thought you offered the poison with the intention of conquering this old woman. I believed that if you, a Masurao, became a Seirei, it would be my strength as well. Since you are a Seirei, I shall rely on you as a power, for I too am a Seirei.'

Yama sings:

Yama: 'In any case, let the six male and female Seirei combine our powers to conquer the old woman. Onibaba, prepare yourself calmly; your luck has finally run out.'

Just as the laughing Baba seemed to be in a desperate situation, a middle-aged Baba with salt-and-pepper hair hanging down her back quietly opened the front door and entered. The middle-aged Baba bowed silently to the six men and women with a graceful manner of speech:

Middle-aged Baba: 'I heard that the master of this house, Warai-san, has suffered a severe injury. I am one called "Kishiri" from this village, and I have come as a representative of the villagers. Seeing you, I thank the kind three, Yama, Kawa, and Umi, for your generous care. It also seems that the other three men have come to visit, and I have no words to express my gratitude. Now, let me take a look at her condition for a moment.'

While saying this, she lifted the laughing Baba up, hoisted her onto her back, and with a 'Hohohohoho,' she looked back, laughed, and ran straight out toward the front. She rode the clouds to the sky, and fled toward the distant southern sky, vanishing like mist. This Baba was the younger sister of the laughing Baba, and a demon who delighted in constantly mocking people.

Since the laughing Baba was saved by her sister, escaped the crisis, and disappeared, the six had no way to pursue her. They looked at each other's faces and were left in a daze for a while.

笑ひ婆アは絶体絶命と見えける折しも、表戸を静かに開きて入り来る胡麻塩の髪を後に垂らしたる中婆アありけり。中婆アは言葉淑やかに六人の男女に黙礼しながら、

『此家の主笑ひさんは、きついお怪我をなさつたと聞きました。私はこの村の「譏り」と言ふ者でありますが、里人の代理として参りました。見れば山、川、海の優しき三人様の厚き御介抱を御礼申します。また外の男の三人様まで御見舞ひにお越し下さいましたやうですが、何とも御礼の申しやうもございませぬ。どれ、私も一寸御容態を見させていただきませう』

と言ひつつ、笑ひ婆アを抱き起し、すつくと背に負ひ、ホホホホホと後を振りむき、笑ひながら驀地に表をさして駈け出し、冲天の雲に乗り、遠き南の空に向つて雲を霞と逃げ去りぬ。この婆アは笑ひ婆の妹にして、間断なく人を譏り楽しみとせる悪魔なりける。

笑ひ婆アは妹に助けられ急場を遁がれ、行方をくらましたるより、六人は後を追はむ術もなく、互ひに顔を見合はして、暫しが程は呆然たりける。
