Chapter 10: 五乙女 [2014]

五乙女〔2014〕

§ 1

Itsukau stopped by a small, humble house in Morikage, and seeing five maidens with smiles filling their faces, they welcomed the party in and urged them to rest their travel-weariness in this dilapidated house. The names of these maidens are Akikaze, Nowaki, Yufugiri, Asagiri, and Akisame.

Akikaze: 'Though it is autumn, sweat has broken out from the weariness of travel; pray, rest yourselves in this dilapidated house. The sound of the pines and the rustling of the bush clover in the wind, how they echo in this chilly autumn. In this mansion where the autumn wind blows through, pray, wipe away your sweat for a while.'

Akio replied to this:

Akio: 'Though it is autumn and cool, I have grown sweaty; shall I rest here in this Morikage? Having rested for a hitotoki in this mansion, I think to nourish my strength. I pray you, permit me but a hitotoki of rest in this mansion purely.'

While saying this, he led the party, and as they passed through the brushwood door and entered the interior, it was a change from the dilapidated house seen from the front; there were beautiful, spacious living rooms, lined up without number, and he said:

Akio: 'Who would have thought that in this dilapidated house there would be such beautiful, spacious living rooms? Having rested for a while in this mansion, I shall advance to the mountain of blazing fire.'

Matsu sang:

Matsu: 'Dividing the grass and following the slope, my feet have felt a slight weariness. Resting my breath in this house, I shall advance while calling forth my soul to life. What a mysterious mansion this is; the number of living rooms is contrary to the expectation from the front. I am anxious, wondering if perhaps I have fallen into the schemes of the hag of slander.'

Take sang:

Take: 'If this is the mansion of the hag of slander, it is fortunate; it is fortunate if it is in the midst of the day. If the hag of slander lurks in this house, I shall fight to the limit of my life. That five maidens stand at the front of this house is also one mystery. It does not seem like a mansion where a hag lurks; do such gentle maidens live here?'

Ume sang while tilting her head:

Ume: 'Have I entered the trap of an Akugami? Somehow my soul does not feel at ease. If I knew this to be the dwelling of the Yasomagatsu-Kami, I would fight with all my might. Is the Akugami pretending to be a gentle maiden and watching for our lives? Everything in this house is mysterious; there are no windows, yet it is bright below.'

Sakura sang:

Sakura: 'If I doubt, there will be no end to it; I do not think this house is the dwelling of the Magatsu. Maidens appeared at the front of the dilapidated house and welcomed me with smiles filling their faces. There is no Magatsu to hinder this journey, which proceeds by the shining words of the Imperial Kami.'

Just as they were singing thus, the four maidens, led by Akikaze, came in, and while drawing tea into trays, they held them high above their eyes and offered the tea of their hearts to the guests resting in the dilapidated house.

Akikaze: 'This tea is a sweet medicine that flourishes on the high fields of the mountain of the spring. For that reason, people everywhere call it spring tea and drink it morning and evening with pleasure. Please drink this tea; the weariness of your long journey should be eased at once.'

Akio, while feeling suspicious, said:

Akio: 'The fragrance of this tea is somehow suspicious; I shall wait for a while before I sip it.'

Akikaze, while changing her expression slightly, said:

Akikaze: 'You speak of mysterious things; this tea is the tea of the spring, and it is the life of man.'

Akio replied:

Akio: 'Somehow, because I am stingy with my life, I do not wish to drink tea that I do not know.'

The maiden called Nowaki said in a cool voice:

Nowaki: 'Does the guest doubt our sincere hearts and refuse to accept our pure and gentle hearts? How could there be poison in this tea, which we have made by purifying and refining it morning and evening?'

Matsu sang:

Matsu: 'It is not that I do not accept the pure hearts of the maidens, but pray, wait for a while. I do not like hot tea as it burns my tongue; I shall drink it after waiting for it to cool.'

Yufugiri, while stretching up from behind, said:

Yufugiri: 'Do you doubt the pure hearts of the maidens and refuse to accept our sincerity? It is regrettable if you think this is tea of water-running grass and hesitate. We built this mansion at the foot of the autumn mountain where bush clover and bellflowers bloom, and waited for you. I am one of the five maidens serving the Mihishiro-Kami, and I am no mysterious thing.'

§ 2

Take sings:

Take: 'Though I know not if thou art a Kami-Otome of the Mihishiro, there is a strange mark upon thy face. From time to time, the ears of the maiden move; I truly think thee a Keshin of a fox or raccoon dog. The color of the tea has changed little by little, and it has become like ink. This tea must be a poisonous brew made of Suihonsau that steals one's Inochi.'

Asagiri sings:

Asagiri: 'If it be so, there is no help for it now; though we appear as maidens, we are Magatsukami.'

Akisame sings:

Akisame: 'Since you have seen through us, guest, there is no help for it; prepare yourself. Though it appears to be an Abaraya, it is a Gankutsu of Toha; there is no path of escape now.'

Ume sings in a harsh voice:

Ume: 'Because I knew of your Takumi, I came to break this Gankutsu. You are a wicked creature who disguises yourself as an Otomego and plots according to the commands of an Onibaba.'

Sakura, while angry, says:

Sakura: 'Koriya, you Magatsu, if you are going to be like that, there is no choice. I shall unleash my Kototama to the fullest.'

Akio sings:

Akio: 'Knowing that this was a Gankutsu of Magatsu, I was lured and entered here on purpose. All of you who appear as Otomego are slaves waiting to steal the Inochi of others with Suihonsau. You were made into Oni after being forced to drink Suihonsau by the Kikuri-baba. Listen well to my Kototama with a calmed heart; do you not hold a grudge against the Kikuri-baba?'

Akikaze, with a slightly clouded face, says:

Akikaze: 'The guest's words are correct; I too was deceived by the Kikuri-baba. This area is the territory of the Kikuri-baba, and we are those who are ordered about by her. In the bitterness of having our Inochi of the Tama-no-o stolen, we became Oni who torment people. The four Otome who are here have all followed the same fate as I. The Kikuri-baba is wounded and resting in the inner room; please destroy her. If you defeat the Kikuri-baba, we shall also add our strength to yours. Though she is a powerful Onibaba, she has been groaning since last night, saying she is unwell.'

Matsu sings:

Matsu: 'Struck by the living Kototama of my Lord, the Baba must be suffering from her wounds. It is interesting; now that I have heard such secrets, we shall add our strength to the Otome. To hear such an interesting thing, that the Onibaba is groaning in this mansion. If so, with all our might and at the top of our voices, we shall attack and torment her with living Kototama.'

Here, the five members of Akio's party and the five Otome exchanged firm handshakes, surrounded the living room where the Kikuri-baba was lurking from all four sides, and let out a great voice that seemed to shatter even heaven and earth:

'One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, one hundred, one thousand, eighty million Kami, destroy the Suihonsau-Oni known as the Kikuri-baba who lurks in this mansion, without leaving a trace, by my Kototama. Himorogi! May there be power in my Kototama, may there be light in my Kototama. A-O-U-E-I, Ka-Ko-Ku-Ke-Ki.'

As they recited the Kototama of seventy-five sounds one after another, even the formidable Suihonsau-Oni could not endure it; she raged wildly, running back and forth in the narrow room, raised a scream, and rose up once again. With a desperate, terrifying expression, she flew at Akio, but without a word, he clenched his fists and struck the Baba's face repeatedly, defeating her. Unable to endure the pain, the Baba suddenly pushed open the window door,

And suddenly flew out of the Gankutsu. While exhaling strange clouds and vapors, she headed toward the vast sky as if turning clouds into mist, and while raining down blood-smoke, she fled away into the white clouds. Ah, how fearful is the power of the Kototama of Himorogi! The Suihonsau-Oni, the Kikuri-baba, thus left the spirits of the five Otome in the Gankutsu of ugliness and fled to move on to her second strategy; it is truly terrifying.

The five Otome were filled with smiles, felt relieved, and while shouting 'Uo-uo,' they knew not where to place their dancing hands and feet.

Akikaze sings:

Akikaze: 'I am the daughter of the Kunitsukami born in Izumigaoka. The four Otome are also children of the same village, and we were deceived by this Onibaba. Having been made to drink the tea of Suihonsau, we became Suihonsau-Oni. We offered this tea to the guest and urged him to join forces with us, did we not?'

§ 3

Thinking back, it was the beginning of spring. We, the five maidens, departed from the village of Izumi, and as we were proceeding to make a pilgrimage to Takamitsuyama, we were overcome by the fatigue of the journey and thirst in our throats. Just as we were suffering, we saw a small house in the shade of the forest. When we, the five maidens, approached and looked, an old woman with white hair was living there. She beckoned to us, saying, 'First of all, do partake of this astringent tea,' and in our joy, we rested for a while. Not knowing it was tea of Suihonsau, we drank it all at once. Suddenly, our heads began to ache, our hands, feet, and bodies swelled up, and seeing our state where we could not move, the old woman laughed with a grin.

Old Woman: 'You have fallen into my trap. The life, the jewel-string of you maidens, shall end this very day. Take the lives of all the Kunitsukamira of Yoshihara, and make them into true demons of Kakuriyo. If you disobey my words, I shall raise a whip of thorns, tear your entire bodies apart, and make you suffer bitter fates.' Terrified by her threatening words, we did as she taught us and spent sad days. Akio-no-kimi is a person of the manifest world, so the power of his Kototama is strong. Since we are beings in the spirit world, our Kototama should have power, yet the light of Kototama does not emerge, and we suffer, only weeping in our hearts.

O Lord, we pray before the presence of Akio-no-kami, who is the child of the Iwagane shining on the mountain above the waters, to save us. We, the five maidens, have been content to serve as the tools of the demon hag, and having waited and waited for the arrival of a strong spirit, the time to clear our grudge has come. Ah, how reliable, how pleasant! We planned to make your entire party suffer at the sacred ground of Tsukimigaoka, but the Soshiri-baba was struck by the living Kototama in reverse, and barely escaping with her life, she is now groaning in a single room. Thinking this moment is our chance, we, the five maidens, plotted together, thinking we would take our revenge.

From the beginning, there was no way for the strength of maidens to resist her. It was to such a place that you, your party, whose bodies are of the manifest world, arrived, and in our joy, we intended to offer you the water of Suihonsau, knowing it was poison. Please do not be angry, for we think of you as our strength. We believe that if you appear in Kakuriyo with us and strike and punish the demon hag, you will remove the calamities of the spirit world. Please forgive us, Akio-no-kami. Before you and the gods in your retinue, we reveal our sincerity and offer our apologies. The other maidens, too, were like abandoned boats with no island to cling to, but in the joy of this auspicious day, they have been revived. How noble!

We offer our heartfelt gratitude. From here on, you shall proceed to the mountain of flames while singing the heavenly number-song of Kototama. What the Soshiri-baba fears and avoids above all is Kototama. I shall follow behind, and while watching over your departure from the shadows, I shall offer the strength of my arm. Please proceed.'

Saying this, the five maidens turned into white smoke and vanished. When they looked closely, the party was sitting and crouching in the midst of the overgrown weeds in the shade of the forest. The ruined house and the rocky cave had left no trace, and there was only the chirping of small birds and the sound of insects crying.

Akio sings:

'Since I saw this mysterious dream, I have realized the suffering state of the demon hag. The ruined house and the rocky cave have completely vanished, and the sound of the wind blowing over the field is clear.'
