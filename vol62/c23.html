Chapter 23: Kami 導 [1598]

神導〔1598〕

§ 1

Ah, I believe in the Creator of Tenchi, the true Mioya Ōkunitokotachinōokami, who is Zenchi and Zennō. Guided by the Seirei, the two pillars of the Izunomitama and the Mizunomitama, which appeared from His holy and beautiful Ōmitama, descended to the Aya-no-Takaamahara, and having endured all the Kurushimi of Utsushiyo, the Izunomitama hid in the Okutsuki, ascended to Amatsukuni together with the Mitama of Wakahimegimi, and has shown mercy and grace to all upon the earth; and the Mizunomitama, bearing the Chikura-no-Okido, descended to Yomi, was oppressed for one hundred and twenty-six days, revived again, ascended to the Aya-no-Takaamahara, and I believe in the Kamubashira of the Mizunomitama, who serves as the Mitsukahi of Kami to convey the Ōmimegumi of the Sumeōmikami, who is Mugen, Zetsutai, Mushi, and Mushiu, and to purify the Mitama of the living and the dead. Furthermore, I firmly believe that the Seirei of the Ōkami descends into the fleshly temples of the Henseinanshi and Henseinyojo, who are filled with the spirits, and grants boundless joy, glory, and life to the place of teaching and to the believers. Kannagara Tamachi Haese.

原文（近代文語）

あゝ吾は天地の造り主、全智全能の誠の御祖神大国常立之大神を信ず。その聖き美はしき大御霊より現はれ給ふ厳の御霊、瑞の御霊の二柱、聖霊に導かれて綾の高天原に降らせ給ひ、現世のあらゆる苦患を受け、厳の御霊は奥津城に隠れ給ひ、稚姫君の御霊と共に天津国に上りまし、地の上の総てを憐み恵ませ給ひ、又瑞の御霊は千座の置戸を負ひて黄泉に下り、百二十日あまり六日の間虐げられ、再び甦りて綾の高天原に上り、無限絶対無始無終の皇大御神の大御恵を伝へ、又生ける人と死れる人の霊を清めむが為めに、神の御使として勤み給ふ瑞の御霊の神柱を信ず。又吾は大神の聖霊に充たされたる精霊の変性男子変性女子の肉の宮に下り、教の場と信徒の為に限りなき歓喜と栄光と生命を与へ給ふ事を固く信ず。　惟神霊幸倍坐世。
