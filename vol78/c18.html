Chapter 18: Kami 嘉言 [1974]

神嘉言〔1974〕

§ 1

The dark vapors of evil and falsehood having accumulated so densely that black clouds blocked Ametsuchi and the island of Gross was brought to the very verge of Baukoku, through the descent of Asakahime no Kami, who was one of the eight Yahashiramihishirogami descending from the Miya of Takachiho as the time of Ama arrived, Ametsuchi was purified and not a single speck of Kazechiri remained within Kokunai; thus, the island country of Gross was renamed Ashiharashinkoku, and the Kunitsukami were promoted and commanded to administer the divine work closely beside the divine pillar of Ashiharahime no Kami. Hereupon, the newly revived land of Ashihara, because the Magatsukami of Guronosu and Gorosu had fled and vanished before the light of Ikukototama and the power of true fire, had its Miya moved to Shinogaoka, which is the center of the land, and an eight-fathom hall was urgently constructed so that she might preside over the Kunitsukami. The Jūyō divine banners fluttered and billowed in the spring breeze, the light of sun and moon shone with exceptional beauty into Ama and illuminated the earth, and from all four quarters and eight directions the Kunitsukami as well as all living creatures of fur, feather, insect, and fin gathered at the sacred site of Shinogaoka to celebrate and revere the establishment of the new land.

Hereupon, Ashiharahime no Kami, leading the Amatsukami and others who had been newly entrusted with divine missions, enshrined the Lord God, erecting thick palatial pillars at Shinogaoka, performed ritual purification before the sacred presence, and solemnly chanted the initial divine words of the country. Those divine words were thus declared:

'With trembling awe and reverence even before the dread and majestic lower rock-roots of Shinogaoka, where thick great palatial pillars are erected and chigi rise high into Takamanohara, before the grand presence of the Lord God who abides as the pacification of the land, I, Ashiharahime no Kami, serving as the Kunitsukami pillar of the land of Ashihara, with trembling awe and deep reverence humbly declare: whereas this divine land was formerly still immature in its soil, its national form unsettled, and the foul and evil water and fire of Magatsumi blocked Ametsuchi, with mists and clouds deep and the light of the celestial sun failing to reach the earth, so that all trees and grasses as well as the five grains failed to ripen abundantly, endangering the living souls of the Kunitsukami and causing this immature land to wither and perish in its second leaf; yearning by any means whatsoever to create and consolidate this blessed land of divine entrustment, morning and evening I broke my heart, wrung out my lifeblood, gazed up at Ama and bowed down to the earth, lamenting—at such a time, Asakahime no Kami, who had pure palatial pillars erected in the precious sacred sanctuary of Mount Takachiho, unexpectedly descended to this immature land, and with the light of Ikukototama and the power of true fire burned up completely the dwelling places of the foul Magatsumi. Now that wind and dust have entirely subsided and the shadows of Magatsumi have been scattered and caused to vanish like morning and evening mists blown away by morning and evening breezes, I wish to move the Miya of Sakuragaoka, which was biased toward the east of the land, to this sacred site which is the true heartland of the country, enshrine the divine spirit of the Lord God eternally, fix the national banner and move the government office, and devote myself with a pure heart and concentrated water and fire of true words to the divine work of generating the land and generating kami. Therefore, taking the descent of Asakahime no Kami as our occasion, on this auspicious day and fortunate hour we hold the grand festival of the birth of the new land, summoning the divine spirits of the eight million Amatsukami and others, and offering various delicious foods growing in the mountains and fields of this land, arranged abundantly upon tables of a hundred legs, praying with trembling awe and reverence that You may graciously hear, accept, and consent, and that through ages and eightfold ages, without movement and without change, You may cause this new land to flourish and prosper lushly like an evergreen Iwashika-yasawagi, guarding and blessing it in the guardianship of the night and the guardianship of the sun—thus humbly and fearfully I pray and declare.

Splendid, splendid, the glorious and prosperous land of abundant reeds, The divine realm has truly been born! Erecting thick palatial pillars for the Lord God, How joyous is this day of our service! Even the island roots whose tangled clouds once blocked Ametsuchi Have cleared away completely, and sun and moon shine brightly. The long months and years of twenty springs enveloped it all, And at last the black clouds of the foul ones have cleared away. Selecting the Kunitsukami endowed with light, We entrust them as guardian kami of the land. May the grace of the Lord God eternally Cause the new land of Ashihara to shine! Amatsukami and Kunitsukami, each and every one, Conform strictly to the trust placed in you! The rock door of eternal Ama has now opened, The darkness of Tokoyo is likewise dawning away. Moving the Miya of Sakuragaoka anew to this ground, We shall govern and rule this new land; Shinogaoka, situated in the very center of the land of Ashihara, Is truly fitting for the Great Office!'

Renaming Shinogaoka to Tokiwagaoka, I shall open the divine service of Miyo, Transmitting the flint bestowed by Kami for Yorozuyo, We shall establish it as the seal of Hitsugi, All Kunitsukamira and Ikimono alike, Shall be revived on this auspicious day of Kefu, The voice of joy echoes through Ametsuchi, As if trembling, the Kuni of Ashihara has shaken, For the achievement of establishing the immovable foundation of Kuni for Yorozuyo, Ametsuchi has quaked in joy, While Ametsuchi trembled with the voice of joy, The foundation of the immovable Kuni has solidified, By the merit of the divine words of Asakahime, The Ashihara Kuni is born anew and tenderly.'

Asakahime no Kami chants a song of congratulation.

'The foundation of the Ashihara Kuni having solidified, The sun and moon of Misora shine bright and clear, The Ame is pure and the Chi is likewise immaculate, May this newly born Kuni flourish unto Toha, Far and wide traversing the arano and crossing the seas, How we meet upon this auspicious day of Kuni's consolidation, Seeing afresh and hearing afresh past faults directly as Nahohi, Even sinful Kami have been forgiven, Even Amatsukami having forgiven, here becoming Kunitsukami, They have revived unto Toha, Selecting the pure Mitama of Kunitsukami, The awesomeness of Kami who caused them to descend, Celebrating the birth of the new Kuni, They offered the living Kototama.'

Just as she thus causes the song to be chanted, from the heavenly Misora, waving the ten-lights divine banner, followed by numerous attendant Kami, riding upon purple clouds and descending to this place, was the majestic figure of the messenger Kami of the Lord God, Toshinaderu no Kami.

Asakahime no Kami, astonished at this spectacle, while clasping her hands in reverent worship, chants a song.

'Toshinaderu no Kami, awesome even to name, has descended on this auspicious day of Kefu, By the merit of Toshinaderu no Kami, pillowing on grass, My journey as I go is peaceful indeed, Even this island where Magatsumi raged wildly, has been purified by the merit of the Lord, Along the paths of my journey proceeding through the fiercely raging Kuni of Magatsumi, Pray protect the borders of my way, Without the help of Toshinaderu no Kami, My setting forth would surely lack light.'

Toshinaderu no Kami descends before the Grand Shrine, and clapping his hands reverently,

'To the Lord God dwelling in this new shrine, I shall speak a divine decree, Dividing the distant cloud-paths, the divine proclamation, Reverently have I come hither, I pray that for a thousand and eight thousand generations, you shall protect the Ashihara Kuni and make it flourish, Having watched over the departure of Asakahime no Kami, Auspicously today has it been manifested, Celebrating the birth of the new Kuni through the divine words of Ashiharahime no Kami, Though the Chi is tender and the Kuni is tender, Toshinaderu no Kami shall eternally protect it without fail, Open this tender Kuni with a peaceful heart, I shall add my strength and protect it.'

Ashiharahime no Kami, in wild joy, chants a song.

'Did I think that Toshinaderu no Kami, the noble Kami, would descend to this new Kuni? If it were I, devoid of strength, protect me day and night, Toshinaderu no Kami, The cloud and mist of Ametsuchi cleared away, and anew by the divine majesty of Kami was the Kuni born, Reverencing the two pillars, Asakahime and Toshinaderu no Kami, I shall serve them eternally, O two pillars of Kami, stay your Mitama forever in this new Kuni, Myriads of living things of Kunitsukami, Reviving upon this auspicious day of Kefu.'

Nozuchihiko no Kami chants a celebratory song.

'The Kuni of Ashihara born in the light of the Kami, awe-inspiring even to name, Though the Kuni of Ashihara is tender, living by the grace of the Lord Kami, it shall flourish more and more, By the merit of Asakahime and Toshinaderu no Kami, The dark clouds of the ugly ones have cleared away, Transferring the shrine of Sakuragaoka to this place, Today, when the divine Kuni is made known, is truly auspicious, All the Kunitsukami having gathered here, Rejoice and celebrate the foundation of the Kuni, Chosen by my Lord, from this day of Kefu I shall serve as a heavenly divine Kami, Blowing away the sins and defilements of the Kuni of Kokotaku, I shall serve for a thousand and eight thousand generations, From this day forth, this endless new Kuni Shall be opened together with the Kunitsukami, Purifying the water and fire of Ametsuchi, from this day forth, We shall open the land of the living Kuni and the Miyo of Kami, The clouds and mist of many years now clear away here, To serve my Lord closely as well.'

§ 3

Kami Takahiko deigns to chant a celebratory song.

'Purifying the heart and cleansing the body deep within our breasts, Reverently and respectfully shall we serve in the divine land. Chanting aloud the living Kototama morning and evening, I shall pray for the prosperity of the land. Without neglecting the divine waza of Kamunagara misogi, We shall purify and clear the water and fire of Ametsuchi. According to the awe-inspiring Mikotonori of Kami Ashiharahime, Behold this Sugado that has become Tokiha Hill. Morning and evening in the Tokiha palace of Tokiha Hill, Will I chant the living Kototama and serve. Because Tenkai is a land of love and goodness, How could we ever neglect the living Kototama? Under the protection of Kami Asakahime and Kami Unarizu, May the age of Kami Ashiharahime be peaceful. Thou, the Kamuzukasa born of the Miiki of the Lord God, Kami Ashiharahime is the very light. The ugly clouds that shrouded the light of Kami Ashiharahime Have cleared away, bringing such joy to kefu! O Ashihara, ever enduring through eternity and age upon age, Prosper ever more, according to the will of Kami.'

Kami Teruhiko deigns to chant an imperial song.

'Through the august achievements of the three great Kami, The land of Ashihara has now been born. The island of Gurosu has vanished without a trace, and the land Of Ashihara has been newly born. To the goddess of the new Kunitsu pillar, Shall I render service and offer true words. While living in the fields and ruling over the Kunitsu Kami, How I pined in longing for this auspicious day! To be looked upon by the august eyes of Kami Ashiharahime, And to serve in the rank of Amatsuhiko, When the hearts of the Kunitsu Kami grew turbid and in the vast sky Ugly black clouds rose up to block the way, If Amatsuhiko and the Kunitsu Kami, without division, Grow close to one another and open up the land, From the hearts of Amatsuhiko and the Kunitsu Kami Black clouds welled up into the sky, yet— When Amatsuhiko and the Kunitsu Kami illuminate their innermost hearts, Then shall the land prosper.'

Kami Kiyohiko deigns to chant an imperial song.

'We celebrate the prosperity for a thousand ages Of the newly born new land. All the Kunitsu Kami, inspired with courage, Rejoice in the birth of the land. This august achievement is equal to the morning sun Rising and making the peak of Mount Takasu shine. In this Mahoraba of the new land where both moon and sun shine clear, We proclaim the divine Kamigoto. The heart of joy congeals to become a song, And turning into Kototama, it resounds forth.'

Kami Haruhiko deigns to chant a celebratory song.

'In the distant sky where moon and sun also shine, I, Haruhiko, Shall celebrate the new land. The prosperity of the land newly born By the divine august might shall surely endure forever. Together with Ametsuchi, this endless land Of Ashihara has had its foundation set upon this very kefu! Just like the luxuriant branches of the yusuriha tree, ever succeeding one another, Shall the Kunimitama of the land of Ashihara rule. Before long, now, the Akitsuo no Kami will descend from heaven And bestow upon us the Kunimitama.'

At this, the Amatsukami, while chanting congratulatory addresses on the new revival of the land of Ashihara and praising the great divine work of moving the palace of Sakuragaoka to the central area of Shinobugaoka, each offered their felicitations and prayed for the future prospects of the new land. Moreover, although the celebratory songs of the five deities newly appointed to the offices of the Kunitsu Kami and of the Kunitsu Kami themselves were countless in number, since they are excessively long, they have been omitted here.

Just as the dictation of this present chapter was beginning,

An extra edition arrived announcing that His Imperial Highness the Crown Prince had been born, which revived the souls of our people and filled them with joy, a truly awe-inspiring event.

晴比古の神は祝歌をうたひ給ふ。

『久方の空に月日も晴比古の 吾は祝はむ新しき国土を 新しく神の御稜威に生れたる 国土の栄は久しかるらむ 天地と共に果なき葦原の 国土の礎定めし今日かも 樛の木のいやつぎつぎに葦原の 国土の国魂知しめすらむ やがて今顕津男の神天降りまして 国魂神を授けたまはむ』

茲に天津神々は葦原の国土の新に蘇りたる祝辞や、桜ケ丘の宮居を忍ケ丘の中心地に移し給ひし大神業を謳歌しながら各自言祝ぎたまひ、新国土の前途を祈らせ給ひける。又新に国津神の司に任命されたる五柱の神及び国津神等の祝ぎ歌は数限りなくあれども、余り長ければ茲に省略しおく事とせり。

本章を口述し初むる折しも

皇太子殿下御誕生遊ばさる

との号外来り、我国民の魂を蘇らせ歓喜せしめたるぞ畏けれ。
