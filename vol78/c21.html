Chapter 21: Monster 体の Island [1977]

怪体の島〔1977〕

§ 1

Asakahime no Kami, having been seen off by the party of Ashiharahime no Kami as far as the shores of Tokiha, while cherishing mutual regrets at parting, took advantage of a favorable wind over the vast ocean where the morning sun shone, and proceeded to advance their boat further and further to the south; at that very moment, from the summit of Mount Takashi standing loftily in the distance of the clear, wide-spreading sky, pitch-black smoke billowed forth like a volcanic eruption, reaching up to Ame, swelling and expanding little by little, and rushing onward to press toward the boundless ocean in a truly terrifying manner. The dark clouds assumed the forms of the dragon-serpent deities Guronosu and Gorosu, appearing as if they were advancing right at the forefront toward the ocean.

Asakahime no Kami looked up at this spectacle and chanted a sacred waka:

Black clouds welling up and rising on the ridge of Mount Takashi / Seem to spread high across the vast sky / The dark clouds standing over Mount Takashi / Are perhaps the wicked intentions of the ugly deity seeking to bear malice against me

The ugly deity attempts to paint over with dark clouds / The blue of the clear, boundless sky / Come what may, even though they envelop the boundless ocean / I shall blow them away with my Kototama

The crooked deities, with their dwelling places burned, / Lurk in Mount Takashi and rage forth as clouds / Yet my boat, enveloped in the dark clouds of the demon gods, / Has indeed made its forward path no longer visible

Being I who was born from the sacred water and fire of the Lord God / How could I ever quail before the raging of Magatsuhi?

Though Magatsuhi rage and rage, and countless / Calamities come, I shall never quail

Hatsuhikohide no Kami, seeing this spectacle, chanted a sacred waka:

Guronosu and Gorosu with all the strength they possess / Exhaust themselves to obstruct our sacred boat / Painting over the blue ocean of the wide sky / Enveloping the sea as far as the ten-thousand-ri distance in clouds

Come what may, no matter how the dark clouds envelop us / Splitting the ocean waves, we shall advance ever onward / Amidst the foul water and fire of the crooked deity, the sea wind / Blows in turmoil and rocks our sacred boat

Just as this waka was chanted, a whirlwind suddenly arose upon the sea, the clouds drew a great circle and danced frantically back and forth, left and right, and the Iwanohikobune was tossed about by rough waves, falling into a plight where no forward or backward movement was possible. Asakahime no Kami, smiling composedly, watched this spectacle calmly, dwelling as if she possessed a deep and calculated plan in her heart.

Tachihikohide no Kami, gazing at the stormy ocean where an inch ahead could not be discerned, chanted a sacred waka with stern dignity:

The crooked deity exercises all the violence at his disposal / To overturn our sacred boat / Blow, wind; raise, waves; arise, clouds / How could I fear, being I who am a Kami?

Delightfully, amidst the windy ocean, our sacred boat / Floats and sinks, rises and descends / The rough waves shake their manes and fiercely / Come biting at the sides of our sacred boat

Entrusted to the Lord God and Asakahime / This body of mine is simply left to Ame

Thinking that Guronosu and Gorosu will also exhaust the seeds of their strength / And soon perish, fills me with sadness

To govern the land of Ashihara eternally / How could I stop without punishing these Magatsumi?

Tachiyosehime no Kami chanted a sacred waka:

On the dark ocean of the jet-black night, with rough waves / Rising up, today's journey is indeed valiant

Letting Magatsumi rage to the utmost of their fury / I think I shall delightfully watch them

Neither the roaring of the fierce wind nor the sound of the high waves / Is anything to fear, being I who am a Kami

Guronosu and Gorosu as well, right here, unto the limit of the jewel-string / Of life, appear to be raging

Let them rage, frenzy, and exhaust themselves as much as they will / And only after that shall I proclaim the Kototama

Amehalehime no Kami chanted a sacred waka:

Being I who clear away the skies of the distant heavens / I shall directly sweep away the dark clouds of the upper air

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten / A hundred thousand, eight million / O Kami, gather together / And sweep away the foul dark clouds / That have obstructed today's sacred boat

Ah, Kannagara Kamigami / May there be life, may there be light / In the Kototama that we chant

Asakahime no Kami chanted a sacred waka once more:

Is this something to watch with delight, the fury of the ugly ones? / Each time we pass across the boundless ocean / The crooked deities too will soon grow weary; if they thus / Vomit forth dark clouds, their strength will surely be spent

Raising dark clouds and causing fierce winds to blow / They attempt to threaten my journey

What calamity could such ugliness pose? / After waiting a while, I shall punish them

Thus the Kamigami chanted their Miuta respectively, while their boat was tossed about by the raging storms and violent waves of the dark sea; yet, with minds calm and composed, showing not the slightest fear, they gazed around for a while and remained at peace.

To such a place came echoing the roaring sound of "U-u-u" like a hundred thunders crashing all at once; suddenly the fierce wind relaxed its spear-tip, the waves grew calm and subsided, and the black clouds of the evil deity that had enveloped all sides gradually thinned and scattered in every direction, until at last not a trace was left, the waves grew peaceful, and the Amatsuhi shone brightly in a serene universe—how wondrous it was.

Asakahime no Kami chanted this Miuta:

"By the Kototama that I quietly prayed, The deity of Unarizu has now revealed himself. Overcome by the Kototama of the deity of Unarizu, The treacherous schemes of Magatsu have vanished away. He who raised the wild winds and stirred the raging waves, How pitiful that Magatsu shall perish! Protected by the thick guardianship of the Lord God and by the merit of the deity of Unarizu, Magatsu has fled away. The black clouds gradually thinned, So that even their shadow was no longer seen. Illuminating the myriad waves, the Sun Deity in the high heavens Protects the boat upon which we travel. Though the crooked deity appears mighty in strength, How fragile he is before the deity of true words! When I think that the divine words of Ashirahime no Kami Might be assailed by Magatsu, my heart is filled with sorrow. Nevertheless, as long as the deity of Unarizu is the life-breath of my soul, Our land shall surely be at peace. Over the vast ocean floats the warmth of spring, And the fish scales leaping upon the water's surface gleam brightly."

Hatsuhikohide no Kami, with a gentle smile, chanted this Miuta:

"By the majestic guardianship of the Kamigami, Magatsu Has perished, a sight pitiful to behold. Deep in the valleys of Mount Takasu in Ashihara, How amusing is the mischief of the lurking crooked ones! In form, the black clouds were precisely like the running figures Of Guronosu and Gorosu. Thus the high heavens have cleared and the waves have grown calm; Oh, the joy and peace of our lordly vessel! The island shadows floating afar on the water's surface Cannot be clearly seen, yet they seem to be a rocky mountain. A wondrously calm rock-camphor boat indeed, Through the divine majesty of the Lord God and the deity of Unarizu!"

Okiratehiko no Kami chanted this Miuta:

"An intriguing act of Magatsu's theatrical play Have we beheld upon today's wave-tossed journey! Wondering what the outcome would be for a moment, My inmost heart was sorely troubled. Though he kept uttering fierce words, Deep within his heart he was trembling with fear. Had it not been for the Kototama of the deity of Unarizu, The black clouds would still have lingered obstructing our way. The august vessel upon which our lord rides high and low Has calmed the tossing waves of Magatsu! Since it is so, it is now peaceful; with resolute steps We shall press on straightway to the western land of Magatsu."

Tachisehime no Kami chanted this Miuta:

"Asakahime no Kami was not dismayed, Her smiling demeanor was truly valiant and brave. Oh, the mischief of Magatsu who wrapped the ocean in darkness And toyed with the august vessel amidst the waves! No matter how heavy the calamities of Magatsu may press upon us, By the divine majesty we shall press ever onward. Lurking in Mount Takasu, the crooked ones Guronosu and Gorosu Became black clouds and afflicted the august vessel."

Ameharehime no Kami chanted this Miuta:

"The distant heavens have cleared, and both moon and sun Float upon the water's surface as the sea emits a gentle mist. Borne by the blowing spring breeze, the august vessel Advances grandly across the myriad miles of ocean. Serving Asakahime no Kami, I beheld various Mischiefs of Magatsu as wondrous spectacles. In the dark sea, the fierce wind and raging waves were like a spirited steed That feared not, but neighed quietly as it stood. Now I deeply realize the blessing of the unmindful steed Upon the sea of raging waves; Time and again, over the perilous waves where the vessel tossed, The unmindful steed neighed in joyful delight. In contrast to the hardship of running the ocean path, Perhaps the steed rejoiced in the august vessel. At last the shadow of the island has drawn near; The evil deity seems destined to lurk there forever."

The boat gradually drew near to a gigantic rocky island lying across the maritime route. Upon closer inspection, countless great serpents of various reddish-black hues thrust forth their heads from the rocks, writhing their long and mighty bodies, opening their wide mouths and spitting tongues of flame, showing with all their might their intention to attack the august vessel; seeing this state of affairs, Asakahime no Kami smiled peacefully in her heart and chanted this Miuta:

Even here, the hideous Orochi of Ihashima, which nests in the Magami, seeks to obstruct me. Yet, however much the Shikogami may rage and clamor, should I, who am Kami, feel fear? Subduing or punishing the Magakami, I proceed according to the will of Kami. From atop the Senjiyō crag, the Orochi opens its Ōguchi wide and breathes forth fire. The flames that the Senhiyaku Orochi spew forth at once are blue, not of true Mahi. The Magatsumi that would obstruct the path I, Iyu, take, I shall cast away with Ikukototama. Avoiding the true fire on the Ihayama where not a single blade of Kusaki grows, the Magatsumi lurks, making this its Sendo. Nevertheless, by the Miisao of the Ikukototama, I shall make this Ihayama an island of fire.

I shall burn the Orochi lurking on the Ihashima that towers in the midst of the sea and proceed. May the Ihashima, by the radiance of the Kototama of Ka, become fire and flame without remainder.

As He sang thus, the Ihashima, which stood about three ri in circumference and rose high from the depths of the Senjiyō seabed into the sky, was suddenly enveloped in flames on all sides; the seawater boiled like hot water, and steam and smoke enveloped the surroundings in a spectacle beyond compare. The Orochi, some burned, some wounded, and others barely escaping with their lives, spewed forth clouds and, taking advantage of this, fled toward the sky above the mountain of Takasu. The Ihashima was burned to its very roots in an instant, and the seawater boiled like hot water, with steam rising up. Seeing this sight, the Kami Hatsuhikohiko could not contain his emotion and sang:

原文（近代文語）

『此処も亦魔神の巣ぐふ巌島の 醜の大蛇はさやらむとすも 醜神は如何にさやぐも猛ぶとも 神なる吾は恐るべきやも 曲神を言向けまたは罰めつつ 神のまにまに進む吾なり 千丈の巌の上より長々と 大口開けて大蛇は火を吹けり 千百の大蛇が一度に吐き出す 炎は青く真火にはあらず 吾伊行く道にさやらむ曲津見は 生言霊に放りて行かむ 草木一つ無き巌山に真火を避けて ここを先途と曲津見潜める さりながら生言霊の御功に この巌山を火の島とせむ。

いろはにほへとちりぬるを わかよたれそつねならむ うゐのおくやまけふこえて あさきゆめみしゑひもせす きやうの浪路の旅立ちに さやらむ曲津は悉く 生言霊の御功に 伊吹き払ひて追ひそけつ 神の依さしの神業に 仕へ奉らむ惟神 神の御前に生言霊を 捧げて祈り奉る もしも曲神吾言霊に 従はずしてさやりなば この巌島は悉く 真火を起して灼熱の 炎に残らず焼き尽し 曲津の在処を絶すべし カコクケキ カの言霊に真火出でて

さしもに堅き巌山も 火の海とならむ惟神 曲津見心を改めて 弥永久に此島の 守りの神となれよかし ああ惟神々々 生言霊に光あれ 吾言霊に命あれよ。

海中に聳り立ちたる巌島に 潜む大蛇を焼きて進まむ 巌島はカの言霊の輝きに 残らず火となれ炎となれよ』

斯く歌ひ給ふや、千尋の海底より御空に高く屹立したる周囲約三里の巌島も、忽ち一面に火焔に包まれ、海水は熱湯の如く煮えくり返り、湯気と煙は四辺を包みて其壮観譬ふるに物なく、大蛇は或は焼かれ或は傷き或は命からがら雲を吐き出し、辛うじて是に乗じ、鷹巣の山の空を指して逃げ行きにける。巌島は瞬く間に根底より焼き尽され、海水は熱湯の如く沸騰し湯気立ち昇りぬ。此光景を見て初頭比古の神は感激に堪へず、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『今更に吾驚きぬ御樋代神の 生言霊に島は滅びぬ 八千尋の底の巌根も火となりて 焼滅しにけり公の御稜威に いつまでもグロノス、ゴロスの執拗さ 思へば吾は憐れを催す 永遠に動かぬ巌根も吾公の 生言霊にかなはざりしよ 巌ケ根は残らず火となり湯気となり 煙となりし時の見事さ 面白き公の神業伏し拝み この天地の不思議を悟りぬ 吾公の功績見れば初頭比古 吾は小さき神にぞありける 曲津見は巌の山と変じつつ 公の海路の旅にさやりし 心地よく曲の化身の巌島は 生言霊に滅びぬるかな カコクケキ生言霊の幸はひに 堅磐常磐の巌も焼けたり』

起立比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『尊さに吾は言葉も口籠り あきれ慄くばかりなりけり 今更に生言霊の尊さを 思ひけるかな海路の旅に 炎々と巌島焼ゆる状見つつ 今更の如驚き止まずも 巌の上に千万の大蛇垂れかかり 大口開けて炎を吐きける 斯の如き曲津の猛びも平然と 公は微笑み御覧しける 曲津見は四度破れて同輩を 数多失ひ逃げ去りにける いや広に神の御稜威の幸はひて この天地は拓け行くらむ』

立世比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『今更に朝香の比女の吾公の いみじき功を驚きにけり 斯の如光と功の吾公に 仕へて進まむ思へば嬉しき 心弱き女神の吾も魂太り いみじき力を賜はりにける』

天晴比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『跡形もなく滅びたる曲津見の 化身の巌の跡に湯気立つ 曲津見は万里の海原に浪を立て 雲を起して雄猛びにける 曲津見の雄猛び煙となりにけり 御樋代神の生言霊に 浪路はるけき万里の海原安々と 公に仕へて渡らふ楽しさ カコクケキ生言霊の御光は 千引の巌ケ根さへも焼きたり 巌島の燃ゆる火影はあかあかと 海底までも輝きしはや』

斯く各自御歌詠ませつつ、順風に乗り舟の舳を東南に向け進ませ給ふ。
