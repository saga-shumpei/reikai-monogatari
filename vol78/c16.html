Chapter 16: 天降地上 [1972]

天降地上〔1972〕

§ 1

As the divine train of Ashiharahime no Kami was seeing off the divine train of Asakahime no Kami, and the eternal spring day had grown dusk at the foot of the hills of Shinobugaoka, they here appointed a lodging of a single night of dew, and each and every one of them climbed upon the hill where the evergreen trees grew, and spreading out neat tatami mats woven by Kunitsukami with reeds and rushes in a rustling, rustling manner, melting away into the clarity of the moon in the open sky, each chanted the living Kototama, or else composed sacred miuta while awaiting the arrival of dawn.

When gazing upon one side of the heavens, in the navy-blue sky without a single fragment of a cloud, the waxing moon illuminated the lower world, while close to the lower right of the moon boat a Venus adhered and shone brilliantly across, and as they gazed upon Saturn shining faintly in a place about three suns to the upper right of the moon boat, considering it an extraordinary phenomenon of heaven occurring once in three thousand years and a rare event, the gods each looked up at the open sky, and perceiving that the time for the reform of the land of Ashihara had arrived, they chanted miuta.

The miuta of Ashiharahime no Kami.

Illuminating the sea of the clear-bright open sky, The divine boat of the moon hangs quietly. If you look well, directly beneath the moon, sparklingly, A Venus of strong light hangs. In the upper right of the moon boat, faintly shining, How lonely is the light of the shining Saturn! To say that there is such a strange portent hanging over the heavenly realm Must be the sign that Ashihara is moving. The Venus of faint light is an Amatsukami, And Venus is none other than a Kunitsukami. The light of Saturn standing above is faint in its light, Obscured as it is by the light of the moon. The light of Venus shining below is extremely strong, Sustaining the light of the divine boat of the moon, The time is near to set right the hearts of the Amatsukami Who descended to the land of the Land of Ashihara! O divine words of Asakahime no Kami, explain in words The state of the moon and the stars this evening.

Asakahime no Kami chants through a miuta.

The Kototama of Amatsukami are muddy waters, water and fire are muddy, It was Saturn whose light had faded. Emerging from the midst of the Kunitsukami, Oh, the light of Venus that guards the world! The pure light of the moon boat is none other than The light of the august spirit of Ashiharahime no Kami. All you Amatsukami who are here, take heed! Revering the gods morning and evening, The Amatsukami do not recognize the gods, while the Kunitsukami Revere and serve the gods of the true word. Since the fierce gods reside in light, The souls that conform to the gods gleam brightly. If you turn your backs upon the gods and lack the path of faith, The light of the divine souls will fade one after another.

Ashiharahime no Kami chants a miuta.

How grateful is the divine proclamation of the God of Light! "Just as you say," I nodded in assent. To have suffered for twenty years under the gods of Magatsu Was the retribution for having strayed from the gods. All you Amatsukami who serve in the palace of the Lord, Purify your hearts and polish your souls! Even when the Lord God shows wondrous portents In the heavenly skies and gives warning,

Maimahiko no Kami, astonished, chants a miuta.

Without knowing it, my heart grew arrogant, and I neglected The path of serving the Lord God. At the teaching of the divine words of Asakahime no Kami, My soul's threads trembled. Thinking upon the sufferings of the Kunitsukami, morning and evening, I shall pray and serve the Great Deity of the Lord. The august spirit of Ashiharahime no Kami shines Like the light of the moon traversing the open sky. Alas, that I passed her by without knowing That she was Ashiharahime no Kami who shines thus! While holding the soul of Saturn whose light is faint, I climbed above the light of the moon. Could this be the retribution for disturbing the path of the noble and the humble? Until now, the raging of Magatsu Was because I, as the guardian of the land of Ashihara, Despised the keeper of the Mihishiro? O God of Mihishiro, forgive me! From this day forth, I shall serve you holding the true word.

The God of Mihishiro replies with a miuta.

You indeed were the very name destined To serve before the Great Presence holding the true word! Your pure august spirit was made cloudy by Magatsuhi, And you remained dimmed like Saturn. At any rate, until the light of Saturn shines forth, Descend to earth and devote yourself to the world! Venus is the august spirit of Nozutsumihiko Whom the Kunitsukami have revered. From this day forth, Nozutsumihiko shall be added To the ranks of the Amatsukami and made an official. Maimahiko and all the other gods, without exception, Descend to earth and purify your souls! From this day forth, change your name, Nozutsumihiko, And serve as the God of Nozutsu!

御樋代神は御歌もて答へ給ふ。

§ 2

Nuzuchihiko replies.

Nuzuchihiko: 'How can I, a lowly Kunitsukami, ever presume to serve in the sanctuary of the Kuni? Even though I somehow maintain the light of Kinsei, I have no means to serve with a single Hitohashira.'

The Kami Ashiharahime responds to this, chanting a miuta.

Ashiharahime: 'O Nuzuchi Kami of the Amatsukami, revere my proclaimed Kototama and serve in the Kuni. Selecting the pure and righteous mitama of the Kunitsukami, we shall entrust them with the Amatsumiwaza. Searching out every corner of the Kuni of Ashihara, I shall select and employ the pure mitama.'

Moved by the great and decisive judgment of Ashiharahime, the Kami Asakahime chants a miuta.

Asakahime: 'How valiant is the divine word of Ashiharahime, who has utterly renewed Heaven and Earth. If there were no light, the Ashihara enveloped in dark clouds would be a world of impenetrable darkness. That myriad disasters arise, traversing Tokoyo, is simply because clouded kami have risen up. May you purify the Dosei mitama of the kamigami shining above Mihishirogami. Though they do not shine like Kinsei right away, if they do not weary, they will surely become a mitate. That auspicious signs appeared in the distant sky is the divine will to make our Kuni live.'

Astonished, the Kami Nariyamahiko chants a miuta.

Nariyamahiko: 'Having served twenty years in the sanctuary of Sakuragaoka, my mitama line has muddied the world. Now, as things are, how ashamed I feel. In the august sky the true nature of my mitama has appeared. Fearing the words of the kami of Mihishirogami, from today onward I shall descend to earth. Joining the ranks of the Kunitsukami, I shall take up the hoe and cultivate the fields to live. Even the dark clouds surging in the Kuni of Ashihara are terrifying when I think they are for our sake. Deep indeed is the grace of the kami of Heaven and Earth, for they have forgiven my transgression.'

The Kami Reiseihiko chants a miuta.

Reiseihiko: 'Have I too ended up as a Dosei mitama, thus disturbing this land? From this time forth, descending to the earth fitting the nature of Dosei, I shall clear the fields. How could a lowly mitama line possessing earthly nature ever shine in the august sky? Until now, growing arrogant in heart with the thought that I am an Amatsukami of true words, the years have passed. The clouding of my mitama appeared as Dosei and fell to the earth of Oshinogaoka. If the words of the kami of Mihishirogami are the divine water and fire of the Lord God, there is no reason to turn away. Lift up the shining mitama of the Kunitsukami and guard the Kuni of Toyoashihara. To serve in attendance is also awe-inspiring, and as I think upon it, my body trembles unceasingly. Am I not one who has descended this very night from the distant Amatsukami sky to the earth of arakane? Unwittingly my mitama has grown clouded, and the position of the Amatsukami has shifted to the earth.'

The Kami Eishuruhime chants a miuta.

Eishuruhime: 'Serving morning and evening as Eishuruhime, I have defiled the mitama of Mihishirogami. Without realizing the precious august presence of the Lord God, I served without reverence—terrifying is my sin. Clouds are rising up over Mount Takasu, and in this tender Kuni of Ashihara the winds are raging. The pure mitama line of Nuzuchihiko Kami has become the master of Mihishirogami. From today onward, may the divine light of Nuzuchi Kami shine without a single shadow over the Kuni of Ashihara. To save the land that has grown utterly clouded and thoroughly disordered is the achievement of Nuzuchi Kami.'

The Kami Yayoeihime chants a miuta.

Yayoeihime: 'My service in Sakuragaoka at the end of the eastern Kuni has come to its close. Though I lament that, presumptuous goddess though I was, I served near the sanctuary, now as it is, what is there to grieve? If it be the reward for the clouding of my heart deep within, morning and evening I grew slack in my great norito before the august presence of the kami, and thus it has come to this. Fearing the sin of Ashiharahime, who became the divine water and fire of the Lord God, looking down upon me—thinking in my foolish heart that the Kuni of Ashihara would never be governed without me, how arrogant I was. How awe-inspiring is the fact that the Kami of Aizen has forgiven my sin until this very day. The words I proclaim as awe-inspiring falter on my lips, and the fire in my breast burns fiercely. This night alone, casting aside my position as an Amatsukami, descending to the fields, I shall clear the land.'

The Kami Asakahime chants yet another miuta.

Asakahime: 'The hour of Heaven and the hour of Earth have arrived, and the light of the Kuni of Ashihara has truly appeared. As a symbol of the Kuni of Ashihara, from today onward flutter the banner of the su-jewel. Array the su-jewel, make them ten complete parts, and establish them as the symbol of the Kuni of true words.'

Thus, as the night grew deep, the four pillars of Kami, urging their swift horses with the whip, gathered and came hither, and under the declaration of Ashiharahime no Kami, they reverently joined the ranks of the Amatsukami.

The night deepened in solemn stillness, the voices of countless birds echoed briskly near the dawn, and the breeze blowing across the spring fields wafted the fragrant scent of plum blossoms, while cranes sang of auspicious signs in the marshlands, and magpies heralded the dawn upon the treetops of ever-verdant pines, as if celebrating the birth of a new day.

Ah, how awe-inspiring are the divine blessings of Kannagara.
