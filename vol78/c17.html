Chapter 17: 天任地命 [1973]

天任地命〔1973〕

§ 1

Herein, Ashiharahime no Kami, who was born as the guardian deity of the land of Ashihara, perceived through her sharp intellect and from the wondrous phenomena of the moon and stars appearing in the celestial spheres that the time of three thousand years for Ametsuchi had arrived, and exerting her great valor, she caused all the Amatsukamitachi down to a single pillar to descend to the earth, and selecting the Kunitsukami whose mitama were pure and hidden in the earth, she caused them to occupy the positions of the Amatsukamitachi, and to supervise all the administration of the land; at this great decisive action, Asakahime no Kami was deeply moved, and facing the various deities, she chanted a proclamation miuta.

When Ametsuchi opened, unprecedented How grand is the movement of this day! Ametsuchi feels as if moving all at once. The rising and descending of the rulers of the land— Clearing the rough metal earth, the sunlight Has risen into the heavenly august sky. The stars crossing the heavens, from the heights of the august sky, Descend and hide in the earth at midnight. The divine words and decisive action of Ashiharahime no Kami Will surely be rejoiced in even by the Lord Ōkami. The clouding and defilement of twenty years, from today onward, Shall clear without a shadow, and the moon and sun shall shine. Because the clouding of water and fire among the gods is strong, Dark clouds rise in the heavenly august sky; From today, when the new land is born, The land of Ashihara shall flourish abundantly.

Ashiharahime no Kami chanted the miuta.

Upon Shinobugaoka where the deity of august light appears, The rulers of the land have been appointed! Making the Kunitsukami into Amatsukamitachi, and the Amatsukamitachi Into Kunitsukami, we shall give birth to the youthful land. To guide and harmonize the Kunitsukami, upon this hill We shall pray to the gods and celebrate a thousand ages.

Having thus finished proclaiming, she commanded all the Kunitsukami, caused the altar of the Lord Ōkami to be built in the sacred precinct of Shinobugaoka, and with reverent voices of norito, she grandly held the festival ceremony of the divine appointment for the Amatsukamitachi, Kunitsukami, and the ten pillar deities.

Nozuchihiko no Kami, stepping forward before Ashiharahime no Kami in awe and trembling, replied with a uta.

Descending to heaven as the guardian of the land of Ashihara, How awe-inspiring is the august presence of the Mihishiro no Kami! I, a humble Kunitsukami, having been chosen, Shall from today serve close to your august side. Both heaven and earth were blocked by foul black clouds, And this land plain has now dawned brightly. Exhausting the utmost of my sincere heart, the Lord Kami Morning and evening I shall worship and serve in public. Crawling out from the rough metal earth, into the distant August sky I ascend, and how awed I am! Though humble, if it is your august word, with caution I shall humbly serve as a pillar of the land. Together with the four pillars of the Kunitsukami, We shall purify the vicinity of the august presence and serve.

Takahiko no Kami chanted the miuta.

The kototama of the mandate of the Mihishiro no Kami, Humbly revering them, from today I shall serve. Even the distress of the Amatsukamitachi and Kunitsukami I shall sweep away through the power of the shingon. I have long been in the fields and am unfamiliar With the divine tasks daily practiced by the Amatsukamitachi. I simply with a sincere heart, morning and evening, Shall exert myself for the sake of the hundred deities. The land of Ashihara is youthful, and for a short while Still the fierce violence of Magatsumi is strong. From today, with a pure and bright heart, I shall offer my mitama to the public and the land.

Teruhiko no Kami chanted the miuta.

Could I have ever thought to enter the positions of the heavenly august gods And serve the public in closeness? Startled by the swift horse's messenger, I have hastened in awe to the august presence! The land of Ashihara, which the Lord Kami was born to give life to, We shall keep alive and protect for eternity. To the unprecedented decisive action of the Mihishiro no Kami, I have rushed hither in reverence. Though I know not the tasks practiced by the Amatsukamitachi, I shall humbly serve just as the divine appointment commands. Morning and evening, practicing the divine rituals of misogi, I shall exhaust myself for the sake of this youthful land. Both moon and sun shining purely upon the divine courtyard, Teruhiko No Kami shall establish achievements for eternity. Was not Nozuchi no Kami the one who grew old in years at Shinobugaoka in this age, And lamented the world? I too, going and coming to Shinobugaoka, Have received the teachings of Nozuchi no Kami. Seeking the Way to govern The plain of the country which was utterly clouded and disordered—

The teachings of Nozuchihiko no Kami were indeed the teachings Of the shingon of the gods of Ametsuchi. It was Nozuchi no Kami who, morning and evening, prayed to the gods That the water and fire of the Kunitsukami might be at peace. Following the teachings of Nozuchihiko no Kami, I have revered the gods through long years. Now that it has come to today, I seem to have realized The awe of Nozuchi no Kami, though I am a foolish body. Though awed, before the august presence of the Mihishiro no Kami, I shall serve and peacefully bring life to the divine country.

原文（近代文語）

茲に葦原の国土の守り神と生れませる葦原比女の神は、天体に現はれし月星の奇現象に三千年の天地の時到れることを、鋭敏なる頭脳より証覚し給ひ、大勇猛心を発揮して、天津神等を一柱も残さず地に降し、また地に潜みたる神魂の清き国津神を抜擢して、天津神の位置につらね、国土の政治一切を統括せしめ給ふ大英断に、朝香比女の神は感激し給ひ、諸神に向つて宣示的御歌を詠ませ給ふ。その御歌、

『天地の開けし時ゆためしなき 今日の動きの大いなるかも 天地も一度に動く心地かな 国土の司の昇り降りは 荒金の地を拓きて御日光は 天津御空に昇りましける 天渡る星は御空の高きより 降りて地にひそむ夜半なり 葦原比女神の神言の英断を 主の大神も嘉しますらむ 二十年の曇り汚れも今日よりは 隈なく晴れて月日は照らむ 神々の水火の曇りの強ければ 天津御空に黒雲立つも 新しき国土の生れし今日よりは 葦原の国土はゆたに栄えむ』

葦原比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御光の神の現れます忍ケ丘に 国土の司を定めけるかな 国津神を天津神とし天津神を 国津神とし稚国土生まむ 国津神を言向け和すと此丘に 神を祈りて千代を祝はむ』

かく宣り終へ給ひて、国津神もろもろに命じ、忍ケ丘の聖所に主の大神の斎壇を造らせ、祝詞の声も恭しく、天津神、国津神、十柱神の神任式の祭典を盛大に行はせ給ひける。

野槌比古の神は葦原比女の神の御前に、恐る畏る進み出で、歌もて答へ給ふ。

『葦原の国土の守りと天降ります 御樋代神の御前畏し 卑しかる吾国津神選まれて 今日より仕へむ御側近くを 天も地も醜の黒雲ふさがりし この国原は明け放れたり 真心の限りをつくし主の神を 朝夕斎きて公に仕へむ 荒金の地をはひ出で久方の 御空に昇るわれ畏しも 卑しけれど御言葉なりせば慎みて 仕へ奉らむ国土の柱と 四柱の国津神たちもろともに 御前を近く清めて仕へむ』

高比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御樋代の神の依さしの言霊を 畏み奉り今日より仕へむ 天津神国津神等のなやみをも 真言の力に払ひ奉らむ われは野に久しくありて天津神の 日々に務むる神業に疎し われはただ真心もちて朝夕に 百の神たちの為につくさむ 葦原の国土は稚しもはしばしは まだ曲津見の雄猛び強し 今日よりは清けき明き心もて 公と国土とに神魂捧げむ』

照比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『思ひきや天津御神の位置に入りて 公に親しく仕へ奉るとは 駿馬の使にわれは驚きて 急ぎ御前にかしこみ来るも 主の神の生ませ給ひし葦原の 国土を生かして永久に守らむ 御樋代神の例もあらぬ英断に 吾は畏み馳せまゐりけり 天津神の務むるわざは知らねども 神任のままに仕へ奉らむ 朝夕に禊の神事を修めつつ 此稚国土の為に尽さむ 月も日も神庭に清く照比古の 神は功を永久に立てむ これの世を忍ケ丘に年さびて 世を歎きましし野槌の神はや 吾もまた忍ケ丘に往来して 野槌の神の教うけをり 曇りはて乱れはてたる国原を 治めむとして道を求ぎける

野槌比古神の教は天地の 神の真言の教なりけり 国津神の水火安かれと朝夕に 神を祈りし野槌の神なり 野槌比古神の教に従ひて 永き年月を神斎きけり 今日となりて野槌の神の畏さを 悟りけらしな愚なる身も 畏けれど御樋代神の御前に 仕へて神国を安く生かさむ』

§ 2

Kiyohiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Summoned by the divine Makoto of the Mihishiro, / here have I come to Shinobugaoka. / May I too guard the Mioshihe of the Nuzuchi no Kami, / morning and evening, unceasingly. / The Hisakata skies have grown clouded, the Iki fouled, / and it seems it has become a painful Miyo. / The time of Ten has come at last, and through the power of the Mihikari no Kami, / the Miyo has cleared. / O Ashiharahime no Kami, cease your sorrow, / and forgive the sins of the Kunitsukamira. / The impure Iki of the Kunitsukami has solidified, / reaching even unto the Amatsumikami. / Those Amatsumikami who descended as the guardians of the Aragane land, / how deeply I cherish them. / Though I am not in the Kura that I myself desired, / I have no reason to turn away from the words of the Kō. / Rectifying the paths of Kimi and Omi, / let us soothe the hearts of the Ametsuchi no Kami. / From Kefu, I shall follow the Nuzuchi no Kami, / and devote myself to this Wakakuni.

Until the evening when the temporary Inochi ends, / I shall devote myself to the Mikuni. / Making the true path of Kimi, Omi, and Tami brilliantly clear, / I shall offer my sincere heart before your presence.

Haruhiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Though I knew beforehand that such a day existed, / how filled with awe I am on this auspicious day of Kefu. / Living and living in the purple-clouded heavenly realm of Aizen, / I shall help and protect all things. / Down to birds, beasts, fish, insects, and crawling things, / I shall steep them in the grace of Kami and protect them. / Appointed as the guardian of the new land, / the trembling of my spirit-thread does not cease. / Bathing my body in the water and fire powers of the Lord Kami, / I shall exert my strength in the land of Ashihara. / Reverence the majestic authority of the Mihishiro Kami, / and I shall pioneer and advance into every nook and cranny of the land of Ashihara. / I think to raise lofty achievements / that pierce the clouds of the Takanesuzuyama. / Magatsumi shall conceal their shadows, / now that I hear the joy of Shinobugaoka today. / The Mihikari no Kami appears, and Ashiharahime no Kami / causes her light to shine—what a wondrous Miyo it is. / Long years I lamented the suffering of the land, / and my troubled dream-thoughts have completely cleared away.

Year after year I have spent hating the Magatsumi / that defile the precious island brought forth by the Lord Kami. / The time of the Hisakata heavens has drawn close at hand, / and how joyful is this evening of my appearance in the world. / Without strength and without light, I simply / shall accompany the Nuzuchi no Kami and serve. / Today I look up to the Mihishiro Kami, who appears in the divine water and fire of the Lord Kami, / as my Kō. / Shine on, O guardian deity of the land of Ashihara, / until all eternity.

Thus, each one of them raised their voices in Kototama concerning their greetings and aspirations for the divine appointment ceremony, before the presence of Ashiharahime no Kami, the great central pillar of the land, filling their faces with divine vigor—a truly awe-inspiring sight.

Hereupon, Mihishiro no Kami brings the divine appointment ceremony of the Amatsukami to a felicitous conclusion, and next, wishing to determine the official posts of the Kunitsukami, chants a declarative sacred poem.

O Kami Maihiko, become the guardian of the western land / and rule over the Kami. / Go forth with majesty, knowing that many Kunitsukami / dwell in the western borderlands of Ashihara.

Maihiko no Kami responds with deep emotion through a sacred poem.

Revering the grace of the Mihishiro Kami with awe, / I shall advance courageously to pioneer the western land. / From Kefu onward, as the guardian of the Kunitsukami in the western land, / I shall serve Kimi.

Ashiharahime no Kami turns toward Naruyamahiko no Kami and chants a declarative sacred poem.

O Kami Naruyamahiko, advance into the southern plains / and serve as the head of the land. / Comfort and protect the Kunitsukami dwelling in the southern land, / morning and evening, without fail.

Naruyamahiko no Kami chants a sacred poem with fear and trembling.

You do not cast aside even me, whose divine spirit is defiled; / at your words, Kimi, my tears have fallen. / Though I am a person devoid of strength, / I shall serve morning and evening, resolving never to go against your Mikokoro.

Ashiharahime no Kami turns toward Ikuehiko no Kami and chants a declarative sacred poem.

O Kami Ikuehiko, proceed forward as the guardian / of the eastern plains. / Since it is the land lying east of Mount Takanesu, / go forth with mindfulness against the fury of Magatsu.

Ikuehiko no Kami offers a sacred poem with gratitude.

You have forgiven even my sins and defilements, myriad though they are, / and have appointed me as the lord of the eastern land. / Entrusted with the precious task called lordship, / I wring my sleeves in profound humility.

Ashiharahime no Kami turns toward Sakaehirume no Kami and bestows upon her a declarative sacred poem.

茲に御樋代の神は、天津神の神任式を目出度く終らせ給ひ、ついで国津神の任所を定め給はむとして、宣示的御歌を詠ませ給ふ。

成山比古の神は畏る畏る御歌詠ませ給ふ。

葦原比女の神は霊生比古の神に向ひ、宣示的御歌詠ませ給ふ。

霊生比古の神は感謝しながら御歌奉る。

葦原比女の神は栄春比女の神に向ひて、宣示的御歌を与へ給ふ。

§ 3

Sakaharuhime: 'Become thou the ruler of the lands to the north from hence, and flourish unto eternity; the northern lands are yet young, and hence not many Kunitsukami dwell therein, and Magatsumi are many; proceed with caution against the Magatsumi, O Sakaharuhime, and I shall guard thy merit.'

The deity Sakaharuhime, weeping and sobbing with gratitude for the divine grace, offered a sacred poem.

I myself am a defiled and weak deity, yet thou, in thy merciful Kimi, dost not cast me aside; well, well, even though the life of my soul should be lost, how could I fail to repay the grace of thy Kimi; I shall open up the northern lands, verdant and detailed like the flowers of Yayohi in spring; come now, let us advance into the divine realm of the north; faithfully abide, O deity of the Mihishiro.

The deity Ashiharahime turned toward the deity Yasakahime and bestowed a sacred poem upon her.

Yasakahime, succeeding the deity Nuzuchi, protect the Kunitsukami of Shinogaoka; Shinogaoka is the Mahoraba of the land of Ashihara, the hill where the deity of light descended from heaven.

The deity Yasakahime, unable to contain her joy, answered with a poem.

Enfolded within the profound heart of my Kimi, I suppressed the tears of Shinogaoka; I think upon my fortune in becoming the ruler of this holy place, where the deity of heavenly light descended; from today on, with all the limit of my heart and all the limit of my body, I shall strive to repay the trust of thy Kimi.

Here, under the witness of the deities of the party of Asakahime no Kami, the resolute divine appointment ceremony of Ashiharahime no Kami safely came to its conclusion, with Amatsukami being appointed as Kunitsukami and Kunitsukami being appointed as Amatsukami, and truly, the new life of the land of Ashihara began to shine brilliantly, which is a matter of awe.
