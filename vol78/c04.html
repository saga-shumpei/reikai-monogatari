Chapter 4: 焼 Field の行進 [1960]

焼野の行進〔1960〕

§ 1

The eastern sky gradually dawns, and the cries of seagulls flying to and fro over the sea surface echo more gently from here and there; the island roots of Gurosu brighten sharply, the mountain of Takashi soars resolutely on the eastern side of the island, and the heavenly sun rises majestically while parting the crimson curtains, causing the terrifying spectacle of last night to vanish without a trace, while the cries of true cranes and magpies, growing ever more piercing, seem as if welcoming the retinue of Asakahimeno Kami.

Asakahimeno Kami quietly draws her august boat close to the beach where the Chibikiiro boulders are arrayed, leaves the august boat together with her horses, and causes her retinue to emerge onto the land; Ubugamihiko no Kami firmly binds the august boat to one side of the beach, and Okitatsuhiko no Kami, ascending to the land together with two other female deities, while looking upon the wilderness where daylilies and reeds grow thickly and wildly until there is no path, Ubugamihiko no Kami chants an august poem.

Even upon this desolate island, do daylilies grow? Boundless are the fields where they thickly bloom. Obstructing the reed-filled paths and growing lushly, It is fitting that crooked fiends lurk in this island root. To the very place where horses' hooves find not even a gap, Oh, reed plain growing so thickly! Though I feel it an awe-inspiring honor before my lord, ever onward Shall I have the horses tread and part the way?

Asakahimeno Kami straddles her horse and chants an august poem.

Could every feature of this sweeping island be wrapped In ugly weeds, where the dwelling place of crooked fiends lies? The crooked deities have lurked within this grassy plain, Surely raising a hundred disasters. A rough country plain where clouds rise up and mist wells forth as far as the eye can see, And even the wind blows chillingly! Ashihara Hime no Kami has already crossed over Seeking to pioneer this land. How awaited is the time when we shall advance and speak In the sacred words of the sanctuary of Ashihara Himenokami! This island where Guronosu and Gorosu lurk Sounds even the cries of passing birds as sorrowful. True cranes align their wings and cross the ridge Of Mount Takashi, drawing near. Do countless true cranes also dwell upon this island, As the sound of their wings draws nigh?

Okitatsuhiko no Kami chants an august poem.

Serving the deity of the August Mitashiro, today once more It is auspicious to meet the Mitashiro deity. Shall we not release true fire upon the growth of grass, thickly sprouting as far as the eye can reach, And burn the crooked fiends? It is joyful to think of burning and destroying Every single vision of crooked fiends that exists on this island. The light of the true fire that awakens the eyes of the crooked deities Is surely a treasure unlike any other in the world.

Tachisehime no Kami chants an august poem.

Though they be crooked deities, originally they were deities born From the water and fire of the Lord God. This true fire, spoken of by the deity of Ebinaruide, Is surely a sword to purify the crooked fiends. By the living Kototama of Himenokami, upon the island of Gurosu, The crooked deities have presently hidden their shadows. The wind rustles through the grassy fields Of the vast and measureless island of Gurosu.

Ameharehime no Kami chants an august poem.

In contrast to last night, which was covered by black clouds, The august sky clears and the sunlight is pure. The crooked visions, startled by the light of the heavenly sun, Must have hidden themselves in the shadow of the grass leaves. Though we proclaim the Kototama in various ways and admonish them, The ears of the crooked fiends were merely tree-ear mushrooms! If it must be so, in order to pioneer this living island, The fury of the true fire is surely a necessity. At the foot of Mount Takashi, piercing the clouds, The Mitashiro deity must surely dwell. I hear that the pure palace where the deity of the Mitashiro dwells Is surrounded by broad streams. Even if we release fire upon these moorlands, the palace Of the Mitashiro deity will surely be free from harm.

Asakahimeno Kami chants an august poem once again.

The wind of Shinotobe has arisen; come now, Release the true fire upon this grassy plain!

Revering the divine words of my lord, come now, I, Ubugamihiko, shall release the true fire!

Responding thus with august poems, Ubugamihiko no Kami respectfully receives the flintstones from the august hand of Asakahimeno Kami, and striking the flintstones with a stone like rough metal while chanting divine words with clicks and clacks, the true fire scatters about and instantly transfers to the dead grass at the roots of the daylilies that have piled up in their thick growth for how many years. At that very moment, fanned by the fierce wind blowing from the sea surface, it spreads before one's eyes in all four directions, and the tongues of crimson lotus flames lick around without regard to surroundings, as if demanding that trees, grasses, and living creatures alike leave no trace behind.

原文（近代文語）

東の空は漸く東雲めて、海面を飛交ふ鴎の声は彼方此方よりものやさしく響き来り、グロスの島ケ根はカラリと明けて鷹巣の山は屹然と島の東方に聳えたち、天津日は悠然として紅の幕を別けながら昇らせ給ひ、昨夜の物凄き光景はあとなく消え失せ、真鶴の声、鵲の声、冴えに冴えつつ、朝香比女の神の一行を迎へまつるものの如し。

朝香比女の神は御舟を千引巖の碁列せる浜辺に静々と寄せ給ひ、駒諸共に御舟を出でて陸地に一行出でさせ給ひ、初頭比古の神は御舟を浜辺の片方にかたく結びつけ、起立比古の神外二柱の女神と共に陸に上らせ給ひつつ、萱草、葦の莽々と道のなきまで生ひ茂りたる原野を御覧しつつ初頭比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『荒れ果てし島にもあるか萱草の 生ひ茂りたる野は限りなし よしあしの道を塞ぎて茂りたる 島根は曲津の潜むも宜なり 駒の脚いるる隙さへなきまでに 生ひ茂りたるよしあし原よ わが公に畏れ多けれどいや先に 駒をうたせて道別けせむかな』

朝香比女の神は馬上に跨り、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『見はるかす島のことごと醜草に 包まれけるかも曲津の棲処は 曲神はこの草原に潜みゐつ 百の災起すなるらむ 見の限り雲立ち昇り霧湧きて 風さへ冷ゆるあらき国原よ この国土を拓かむとして葦原比女 神は早くも渡らせ給へる 葦原比女神の神言のみあらかに 進み語らむ時の待たるる グロノスやゴロスの潜むこの島は 鳥の鳴く音も悲しげに聞ゆ 真鶴は翼揃へて鷹巣山の 尾根をよぎりつ近づき来るも この島も真鶴数多棲みけるか 翼の音の近づき来るも』

起立比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御樋代の神に仕へて今日も亦 御樋代神に会ふぞ目出度き 目路の限り生ひ茂りたる草の生に 真火を放ちて曲津を焼かばや この島にありとしあらゆる曲津見を 焼き滅すと思へば楽しき 曲神の眼を醒す真火の光りは 又と世になき宝なるかも』

立世比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『曲神といへどももとは主の神の 水火より出でし神なりにけり 鋭敏鳴出の神のたまひしこの真火は 曲津を清むる剣なるかも 比女神の生言霊にグロスの島の 曲神はいつかかげをかくしぬ ひろびろと限りも知らぬグロス島の 雑草の野に風さやぐなり』

天晴比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『黒雲の覆ひし昨夜に引替へて 御空晴れつつ日光清しも 曲津見は天津日の光に驚きて 草葉のかげに身をひそめけむ いろいろに言霊宣りてさとせども 曲津の耳は木耳なりしよ かくならばこの生島を拓く為に 真火の荒びも是非なかるらむ 雲をぬく鷹巣の山の山麓に 御樋代神はおはしますらむ 御樋代の神のまします清宮居は 広き流れにかこまると聞く この野辺に火を放つとも御樋代の 神の宮居は恙無からむ』

朝香比女の神は再び御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『科戸辺の風は出でたりいざさらば 真火を放てよこの草の野に』 『吾公の神言畏みいざさらば 真火を放たむ初頭比古われは』

かく御歌もて応へ給ひつつ初頭比古の神は、朝香比女の神の御手よりうやうやしく燧石を受取り、荒金の如き石もて燧石を、神言を奏上しつつカチリカチリと打ち出で給へば、真火は辺りに飛散し忽ち幾年ともなく積れる萱草の茂れる根もとの枯草に真火は移りける。折しもあれ、海面よりはげしく吹き来る風に吹きまくられ、見る見る四方八方にひろごり、紅蓮の舌は四辺かまはず、木も草も生物もあとを絶てよとばかり舐めまはりける。

§ 2

Over leagues upon leagues, the great field of Daigenya turned black as one looked, and here and there the burnt-out forms of Tatsugami, Orochi, and fierce beasts lay exposed to the sun, retaining a merciless spectacle; thus Mihishirogami commanded the four pillars of Kami to bury their remains in the earth respectively, spending numerous months and days, which was indeed awe-inspiring.

Asakahime no Kami chanted a miuta:

A pitiful, ugly Magami has perished, We have buried its remains; Guronosu and Gorosu's Maga tsukasara Have not yet perished and have fled away. Magami pointing toward the sky of Mount Takashi, Raised clouds and fled away; A great field thus burned and purified, Is good for transferring Kunitamagami. Transferring and planting Kunitamagami in this soil, I think to pioneer the island of Gurosu. This island, where reeds and rushes grow densely in clusters, Its soil seems to have grown fertile of itself; The dwelling places of Magakami have all been burned, Come, from now on we shall pioneer the land of the Kami.

Hatsuhikohiko no Kami chanted a miuta:

How majestic is the form of Mihishirogami, Shining like blazing fire; Since our kimi is a Kami of light, Even the darkness of Tokoyo clears away; Birds of a hundred and a thousand species flying in the sky are also startled, To what ends of the place have they hidden their forms? That white smoke rises at the end of the range of vision is surely The sign of wild fires burning; However swiftly the feet of the wind may run, The true fire advancing while burning will leave them behind; Only the surface is burned to the end, yet the roots of the grass Are still burning and smoke rises up.

Tachihikohiko no Kami chanted a miuta:

Serving in attendance like today, There has never been a day so majestic and joyful; As I gaze upon the force of the blazing wild fire, I think upon the achievement of the power of kimi; I realized it as a thing more precious than anything else, This flint-stone which kimi possesses; Even the islands of Wanli are with the flint-stone bestowed by kimi, And the dwelling places where Magami lurk shall perish; Coming here, I, Tachihikohiko, have realized The achievement of the power of the true fire; Field-sides of scores of li have burned away in the twinkling of an eye, By the power of the wind and the achievement of the true fire.

Tachiyomehime no Kami chanted a miuta:

The roots of the island of Gurosu enveloped by black clouds Also grow clear while the moon and sun shine brilliantly; With the light of the daytime moon growing clear in the great sky, Our deportment is watched and smiled upon; The steps of our horses have become broad; A hundred grasses and a thousand grasses having been burned and cleared away, The thick trunks of pine and camphor trees standing luxuriantly in Daigenya Remain verdant; The heart of the ever-verdant tree that yields not even to fire Resembles the chastity of Asakahime.

Amehimehime no Kami chanted a miuta:

The ugly dwelling places of Magakami Have all been swept away by the power of the true fire; Strong is the wind of the tide blowing from the sea, And the wilderness is purified as one looks; From today, however much Magamigami may rage, They must not be feared, owing to the achievement of the true fire; Guronosu and Gorosu's Maga, who blew fire and threatened us, Whither have they gone? If we do not punish the Magamigami of Guronosu and Gorosu, This land-plain cannot be peaceful; From now on we shall bravely advance and seek out The august dwelling place of Ashiharahime no Kami; Come now, we shall stand before the august presence and serve; Amehimehime no Kami chanted:

The great field of unknown bounds, By the power of the true fire completely Burned and cleared away—how delightful! Helped by the wind of Shinato, The true fire suddenly in all four directions Swelled and expanded, and roaring, Spitting tongues of flame, Burns up all things; When I gaze upon the majesty of that force Crested upon a horse's back, It becomes like an ocean of fire. Ah, oh, Almighty Kami! Serving before the august front of Mihishirogami, We advance into the burned field; Even the hoofs of our horses clacking, We advance into the unknown bounds. This young land's young field, Still smoke rises here and there, Trailing like haze. The ever-verdant pines and camphor trees At the headlands far and near, Adorned with green treetops, As if congratulating the auspicious omen of the island of Gurosu, Appeared. Clouds welling up on Mount Takashi, How marvelous that the hundreds of trees on the peak Shine blue and green! Mihishirogami and the descending Ashiharahime no Kami's minister, Accompanied by five pillars of Kami, Surrounding a broad stream at the foot of Mount Takashi, Will joyfully welcome and await The going forth of Asakahime; Though the steps of the horse be swift, Far away to the end of this plateau, To the foot of Mount Takashi, To advance will not be easy. If this steed had great Wings, to soar through the great sky Like a hawk And advance—what shall we do? Directly through the burnt field We shall patiently advance. Ah, oh, Almighty Kami! Be fortunate in the august journey of kimi, Let there be light upon the august journey of kimi.

Thus having them chant, the five pillars of Kami, combing their august hair in the wind blowing across Daigenyahara, advanced high-spiritedly, pointing toward the sanctuary of Ashiharagaoka.

原文（近代文語）

幾千里に亘る大原野は、見る見る黒焦げとなりて彼方此方に竜神、大蛇、猛獣等の焼け亡びたる姿、天日に曝され、無残の光景をとどめけるにぞ、御樋代神は四柱の神に命じて各自その遺骸を土中に埋めさせ給ひつつ、数多の月日を費し給ひけるぞ畏けれ。

朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『あはれなる醜の魔神は亡びたり その遺骸をわれ葬りつ グロノスやゴロスの曲津の司等は 未だ滅びず逃げ失せにける 曲津見は鷹巣の山の空指して 雲を起して逃げ去りしはや かくの如焼き浄めたる大野原は 国魂神を移すによろしも 国魂の神をこの土に移し植ゑて グロスの島を拓かむと思ふ よしあしの群がり生ひしこの島は 土自ら肥えにけらしな 曲神の棲処はことごと焼かれたり いざこれよりは神国をひらかむ』

初頭比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御樋代の神の姿の雄々しさよ 燃ゆる火の如輝きましつつ わが公は光の神にましませば 常世の闇も晴れ渡るなり 御空飛ぶ百鳥千鳥も驚きて いづくの果てか姿かくしぬ 目路の果てに白煙たつはまさしくや 野火の燃えたつしるしなるらむ 風のあし如何に速けく走るとも 燃えつつ進む真火はおくれむ 上べのみは燃え尽せども草の根は 未だ燃えつつ煙たちたつ』

起立比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御供に仕へまつりて今日の如 雄々しき楽しき日はあらざりき 燃えさかる野火の勢ながめつつ 公の力の功をおもふ 何よりも尊きものと悟りけり 公が持たせるこれの燧石は 万里の島も公の賜ひし燧石にて 魔神の潜む棲処は絶えむ ここに来て真火の力の功績を さとりけるかな起立比古われは 数十里の野辺はみるみる焼け失せぬ 風の力と真火の功に』

立世比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『黒雲の包みしグロスの島ケ根も 晴れ渡りつつ月日かがよふ 昼月の光冴えにつつ大空に 吾等が振舞ひを見つつ笑ませり わが駒の脚下広くなりにけり 百草千草焼きはらはれて 大野原にすくすくたてる太幹の 松と楠とは蒼く残れり 火にさへもひるまぬ常磐樹の心こそ 朝香の比女の操に似たるも』

天晴比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『曲神の醜の棲処は悉く 真火の力に払はれにけり 海ゆ吹く潮の風の強くして 見る見る荒野は浄まりしはや 今日よりは如何に曲津見荒ぶとも 恐れざるべし真火の功に 火を吹きて吾等をおどせしグロノスや ゴロスの曲津はいづらへ行きけむ グロノスとゴロスの曲津見罰めずば この国原は安からざるべし 葦原比女神のみあらかを今よりは 勇み進みて探ねゆくべし いざさらば御前に立ちて仕ふべし 天晴比女の神はうたひつ

かく歌はせつつ、大野ケ原を五柱の神は吹き来る風に御髪を梳りつつ意気揚々と、葦原ケ丘の聖所を指して進ませ給ひける。
