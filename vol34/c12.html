§ 1

Fusako and Yoshiko, left behind halfway up Takayamatauge, taking advantage of their legs having recovered, pursued the footsteps of Kurohimetsukasa, and aiming to proceed to the Hinomiyako, they finally crawled up to the Zetsuchiyō. Descending the sheer, wall-like slope, the two men, Fusako and Yoshiko, keeping time with the rhythm of their feet, tangled among rock roots and tree roots.

Fusako: 'Untokodotskoi Yoshikō-san! Be careful, it is dangerous! It is a slope built like a wall. Stone carts are rolling around with a look as if waiting for people. It is a road of the slope where you cannot let your guard down. Ottotsudotskoi, my foot slips! Aita tata tata, I stumbled! The tips of my toes begin to ache. Yoshikō, be careful, hold on tight!'

Fusako: 'How on earth could Kurohime-sama descend such a steep slope as this, dotskoishiyo? Truly, it is a dangerous slope! Speaking of which, where on earth is Magokō dawdling? My heart is heavy in the traveling sky. The sky suddenly becomes overcast, with a record-breaking storm blowing rocks away and knocking down trees. In such truly dreadful scenery, shrinking in fear, is Kurohime not rolling around somewhere, dotskoidotskoidotskoishiyo? Untokodotskoidotsukoidotskoi, garagaragara! Look at that, Yoshiko, didn't you fall over? The tips of your toes, dotskoishiyo! Step by step, being careful, while avoiding sharp stones, the kneecaps are laughing kyokyoku.'

Fusako: 'While applying moxa strictly, if we do not descend this steep slope, we cannot go to Hinomiyako. By this time, Kurohime-sama is surely grabbing the back of the neck of Takayamahiko, dotskoishiyo, right in the midst of a noisy commotion. Even the newlywed bride of Aiko no Hime's heart must surely be in turmoil. Even though I say it is someone else's affair, I am worried, dotskoishiyo! It weighs on my heart and I cannot bear it. Just as Takayamahiko is growing youthful and rejoicing at receiving a pretty wife, dotskoishiyo, an old crone with a dark complexion and full of wrinkles might come along and feign ignorance, saying she is the true wife, dotskoishiyo. Truly, she will be troubled.'

Fusako: 'Nevertheless, the sound of singing the Izuho no Mitama propaganda song of earlier, Tamajiwake no dotskoishiyo, has vanished without a trace, disappearing into the clouds like mist. How on earth did the God Priest of Tamajiwake descend this steep slope so quickly, dotskoishiyo? Surely the mountain tengu will come to collect the toll. Aitata, dotskoishiyo, I fell over again! Kurohime, desiring and longing to meet him, wrapped in the darkness of the paths of love, climbs up and down the mountain paths where even birds do not pass, dotskoishiyo, and goes trailing after his back. Such fierce infatuation leaves one speechless and unable to say a word. Dotskoi, dotskoi, dotskoishiyo!'

Fusako: 'Oh, ahead there is a human shadow. That fellow looks suspicious. From now on, tightening my bellyband, pretending to be a grandson of a tengu, if that fellow is a thief making a living, dotskoishiyo, I will roar at him from the top of my head and seize his daring spirit once and for all! Ah, Kamunarigami-gami, may your august spirits be prosperous, please let us descend the steep slope of Takayamatauge swiftly and without trouble or difficulty, Pure World Princess (Jun'yosehime). Before the august presence of the Kunitama God, I reverently and respectfully pray. Ottotsudotskoi, I slip again! Why on earth are so many small stones rolling around? In these days of civilization and enlightenment, roads are cleared everywhere, no matter where you go.'

Fusako: 'Even the steepest mountain paths are excavated so that automobiles and rickshaws can pass with a gradient of three to four sun. Good grief, this is yet another savage country! Aitata, aitata, I stumbled! The tip of my straw sandal has snapped. Must I walk even the most painful stony roads barefoot? A troublesome matter has arisen. If I had known it would come to this, it would have been good if I had prepared straw sandals, dotskoishiyo, dotskoidotskoidotskoishiyo, and brought them along. Kurohime-sama, dotskoishiyo, showing consideration for us elders, even prepared figs and somehow saved the starvation of the two of us, which Kurohime-sama is carrying on her back.'

Chanting thus, they come descending. Four men sitting and idling away on the mildly sloping mountain path, seeing the two figures,

First man: 'Hey travelers, pause and take a breather for a moment. Surely due to the fierce storm earlier, the valley path has become ruined, turning into a dreadful stony trail, so you must be quite fatigued. Oh, you are barefoot, aren't you? That must be unbearable; how about it, though I know not if they will fit your feet, I will present you with a pair of my waraji, so put these on. Descending all the way to the foot of the mountain along such steep slopes with jagged stones will cause your compass to break down completely, you see.'

Fusakō: 'Oh, thank you very much. How very kindly you have spoken to us. Ah, in that case, how much should I pay to receive them?'

First man: 'I am no waraji seller, do not underestimate me so. Even so, I am a man known by name to some extent as Torakō of Takenō village. Seeing your plight, I felt it was a pity, and therefore I am offering them to you.'

Fusakō: 'Even so, to receive them for free from a total stranger, my heart somehow remains unsettled. Please do not hesitate and take payment for them.'

Torakō: 'Do not speak of such distant, cold things as strangers. I am a believer of Hinomoto-no-Oshie, but according to the teachings of Kami, beneath the heaven there is no such thing as a stranger; everyone, all living creatures, leaving aside human beings even down to birds, beasts, insects, fish, grasses, and trees, are the precious children of Kami, and furthermore, the entire universe without exception is composed of brothers and sisters, so He taught. That is why I consider you my true brother, so put them on without hesitation.'

Fusakō: 'Ah, thank you very much. The teachings of Kami are truly magnificent. Thinking that the power of influence has reached even to such a barbarian land as this, the missionaries are not to be underestimated either. Even with Kurohime-sama, we teased her in every possible way, no matter what happened, but what a truly wasteful and regrettable thing we have done. Let us meet Kurohime-sama even a single moment sooner and offer our apologies, won't we, Yoshikō?'

Yoshikō, turning toward Torakō and bowing politely,

'Thank you very much for kindly bestowing your grace upon us, who are total strangers. We shall never forget this favor. This Fusakō was also in trouble with his waraji worn through, and receiving your favor, I feel indeed as though I have come back to life.'

saying so, moved by Torakō's kindness, he expresses his thanks with tears welling in his eyes.

Torakō: 'Ah, do not speak of such tearful things, aren't we brothers together? When I told you to put on these waraji and go, you replied from your end... Good, let's put them on! You too are quite a clever fellow, that's what makes you my brotherly equal. From today on, I shall accompany you, so follow along. When we go to the Land of Fire, I'll even treat you to a cup of sake... speak out with such vigor! When you whine and talk about things being gracious or wasteful even though you are a man, it rubs me the wrong way and I cannot help it!'
