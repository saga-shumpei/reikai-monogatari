Chapter 17: 向 Day 峠 [958]

向日峠〔958〕

§ 1

At the foot of Mukafutauge, beneath a forest of dense, sky-obscuring shiyōjiyu trees, dozens of rough men have bound two women tightly with rough ropes, shouting curses at them about something. The man who appears to be their leader among them is called Orochi no Sankō, a reckless ruffian in this neighborhood. Furthermore, Kanekō and Yosakō are two subordinate villains who rely on Sankō as their right hands. Sankō straddles a giant rock beneath the forest, coldly looking down upon the two women.

Kanekō: 'Hey woman, now that things have come to this, no matter how much you struggle, it will do you no good. Come now, will you nod your head affirmatively here? If you keep spitting out sophistry with endless wrangling, I will no longer tolerate it. This Kanekō will take the place of the boss and beat you to death; is that still fine with you?'

Woman: 'Ah, how filthy! Even if I am beaten to death by Sankō, a woman's chastity will not be compromised anywhere. Rather than that, kill me quickly with a single blow.'

Yosaku: 'Now now, Oai-sama, think about it well. Life is the seed of all things. Instead of saying such things, it is for your own future's sake that you should give a favorable and pleasant reply. Speaking of Orochi no Sankō, whose fierce renown is hidden nowhere in the Land of Fire, he is a high-spirited highwayman of whom even lions, wolves, and tigers hear the name, tuck their tails in, and shrink away as they flee. No matter how much you, Oai-sama, remain faithful to Torakō-sama, what use is a weak-kneed coward like that who is infatuated with Misoshikō? Place your hand upon your chest for a moment and consider the advantages and disadvantages. If you become Sankō's wife, you will truly be a magnificent person. We too will revere you as our elder sister and listen to your commands in all matters. This is the decisive moment for reflection. Since you are agitated right now, you surely cannot judge between good and evil, but put your hand firmly on your chest and think.'

Oai: 'No, no, no matter what you may say, once I have made a pledge to Torakō-sama, how could such a thing ever be done? Even if I were killed and it were said that I broke my chastity, it would bring disgrace upon my ancestors, and down to successive generations of descendants, I would have to expose my shame. Among worldly people, I would be reviled as an unchaste woman and forced to bear shame. By this day, my determination cannot be moved by any authority or monetary power whatsoever. Please, without saying such things, kill me.'

Yosaku: 'Well now, what a bad disposition. That Torakō whom you hold so dear was taken by someone called Tamakō of Tatehi Village to fetch figs, or muttered incomprehensible things about a crystal ball clouding over and Kurohime being thus and so, and went up to the very summit of Takayamahiko Pass. Scenting this, fifty or sixty subordinates of Boss Sankō chased after him, saying they would take Torakō's life, so by now, unfortunately, he is likely making his journey through Hades. No matter how much you, Oai-sama, say that the vital thing is O-O, isn't it hopeless to have a husband who has no life? For people, resignation is important. Men are certainly not limited to Torakō alone. It is because it will be for your own worldly success that I am advising you like this.'

Oai: 'What! Sankō's minions went to kill that Torakō-sama? Is that really true, oh? Ah, how regrettable, how mortifying! Even with a woman's delicate arms, would I fail to take vengeance? Hey Sankō, you had better come to know a woman's single-minded resolve.'

While she writhes her body, bound hand and foot in a tight tangle so that she can do nothing at all, she bites her teeth in bitter mortification, lets down tears of resentment drop by drop, and glares fiercely at Sankō's face.

While Sankō laughs coldly,

Sankō: 'Ah-ha-ha-ha! How touching indeed! Hey Oai, listen well. That evening some time ago, when I met you and asked if you would like to become this person's wife, what did you say... You uttered words like: "An unworthy person such as myself—do you think of me to such an extent? It is the ultimate blessing for a woman. However, I have parents, so please wait until I have consulted with them thoroughly and give you my reply..." Did you not spit out those very words? At that time, it would have been exceedingly easy to force you without taking no for an answer, but out of respect for your character, and even though I knew it would cause the man who first made the proposal to lose face, I patiently waited. Yet, even after a year and then two years, making various excuses, you put me off and finally went as a bride to the place of that fellow Torakō. A hateful article you are! If it has come to this, I too am a man. It must be already three years since you went off to that fellow Torakō. Ever since I became infatuated with you, it is already five years this year, and what do you suppose is the reason I am still living a bachelor's life? How would it be if you guessed my feelings even a little bit? Being stubbornly obstinate cannot possibly be a woman's only talent.'

Ai: 'Ew, how disgusting! Who on earth would ever want a woman to be a partner to a wretched scoundrel like Sankō, who is like a giant serpent? Detested everywhere like a centipede and with no woman willing to become his wife, he lives a bachelor's life against his will, yet he has the sheer audacity to say such brazen things! Even if this body were killed and these flesh and bones were thrown to the crows, do you think I would ever let a repulsive man like Sankō touch me with the tip of a single finger? You might as well give up already, bite your tongue, and die. Ugh, even the wind blowing from your direction carries a scent that makes me feel sick!''

With a desperate, reckless, and fatalistic spirit, she spits out these words without holding anything back. Sankō's anger rises so high it pierces the heavens; he climbs down from the rock, stands squarely in front of Ai, clenches his turban-shell-like fist tightly, thrusts it right before her eyes, and while rotating it three or four times round and round vertically and horizontally,

Sankō: 'Hey Ai, what do you think this is? It's solid bone all the way to the core. Once I offer this iron-hard fern-frond of an ogre's fist to your very crown as a greeting, you'll be instantly plunged into nirvana—a journey to the underworld! Why don't you make up your mind, accept your fate, and give me a good answer? I am not entirely made of wood and stone, you know; I have warm blood and tears as well. Depending on how you act from your side, I can be an unfathomably kind man. Without showing that stubbornness of yours, just try listening to what I have to say for a moment. Immediately your expression will melt, and... as the worldly proverb goes, people are not always what they seem. You'll think, "How could this Sankō, whom I imagined to be such a terrifying and hateful man, be such a wonderfully kind person? Compared to Torakō, his bearing is somehow much finer, his kindness deeper, and he has real spirit. Why on earth did I hate such a splendid man like a packhorse overturning its load? Sankō-sama, I am truly sorry; please be endlessly and eternally fond of me..." —saying this, drowning in tears of joy, and becoming so anxious that you can't bear for me to step even one foot outside: that is as plain as seeing fire! Now Ai, why don't you put your hand over your heart and think about it?'

Thus, before she knows it, he has gradually softened his terrifying facial expression.

Ai: 'Hothothot! My, what a spineless coward he is! Narrowing his round eyes like willow leaves and even drooling—how unseemly! Would anyone, whether cat or weasel, ever offer their life in true devotion to such a hopeless weakling? Come on, kill me quickly! Going to the Pure Land hand-in-hand on the mountain path of Shide and the Sanzu River with Torakō who is in Hades, while mocking your pathetic foolishness, just hurry up and kill me, won't you!''

Sankō: 'Well now, she really knows how to play the fool, doesn't she? Where on earth did she find anything appealing about such a miserly fellow like Torakō? Some things just defy all understanding.'

Ai: 'Hothothot! My, what a magnificent act of playing the fool! No matter how much you play the fool, affinities are strange, and for the life of me I simply cannot take a liking to you, bug that you are! However, in this wide world, as the saying goes, even nettles have their lovers; since there are bugs that gladly bite into bitter tobacco, instead of casting lingering, pathetic sheep's eyes forever at a woman who despises you—since you have so many orphans under your wing—why don't you go find a blind one, a lame one, or a crook-nosed one and make her your wife? Ohohoto, I pity you...''

Sankō: 'Hey Ai, if I just sit here listening quietly, aren't your harsh words going a bit too far? Aren't you a believer of the non-resistance doctrine of Izumo-shinji, which teaches the use of the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases? Does uttering such outrageous abuse not violate the Heavenly Law?''

Ai: 'Henh! A violation of the Heavenly Law is enough to make me dumbfounded! A maggot like the serpent Sankō is the very ringleader of violations against the Heavenly Law! Ah, how revolting; please step away over there. I'm about to throw up.''

Kanekō: 'Hey you woman, if we treat you softly you get full of yourself, what kind of preposterous bluff are you spewing. If you want to be killed that much, there is no reason not to kill you. However, to ruthlessly kill such a nice piece of work is also a waste. How about rethinking things a bit, resigning yourself to become a sacrifice, and saying yes. Don't harbor such far-fetched hopes as going to the netherworld to meet Torakō.'

Oai: 'Hey Kanō, this is no place for you to step forward, so stay back and sit tight. If you don't like staying back, you'd better bite your tongue and die. Since you fellows are in this world, rice just gets more expensive.'

Kanekō: 'Ah well, utterly hopeless. Still, if I think that such a classy dame has deigned to fling even harsh words at me, I am honored too, ha ha ha ha.'

Oai: 'O ho ho ho, it seems you still hate to take my life after all. What a spineless man you are. No matter how much you threaten or cajole, withered and shrunken though I am, as the wife of a chivalrous man, how could my husband's face stand if I were to quail at something like this? Even so, if I return home, I am a mistress who is served by numerous subordinates and respected as big sister. Being barked at by a scrawny dog like you and getting jittery—I could never become the wife of a chivalrous man, oh ho ho ho. Look at that pale face of Kanekō's.'

Kanekō: 'What a stubborn big sister you are. Tied up like this so you can't even budge, right in front of an enemy who holds the power of life and death, to spew this much bluff shows guts of steel. Big sister, I am impressed too. It is no wonder Torakō fell in love with you. I will become your subordinate starting today too, wa.'

Yosō: 'Hey Kanekō, what are you saying, you. Isn't this before the boss? If you spew such things, shall I kill you along with her?'

Kanekō: 'Hmph, what am I spewing, I wish you'd kill me soon. If I could commit a double suicide with such a beautiful dame, it would be a great honor. Hurry up and beat the life out of us, but in exchange I have one favor to ask. Bury us facing each other in the same hole. That is my only request.'

Oai: 'Ho tsu ho ho, how disgusting. Who would ever bear to be buried with the likes of you? I don't know how angry my husband Torakō would be if I were to be haunted by you even after going to the netherworld. You go ahead and get killed of your own accord. It has nothing to do with me at all...'

Kanekō: 'Ehr, what a foul-mouthed woman. You never truly know someone until you live with them, just as you never know a horse until you ride it. Right now you despise this Kanekō like a centipede, but once you go to the netherworld and start making the journey to the underworld together, it will dawn on you. People are not what they seem; if you make a journey to the netherworld with a man like this, you'll end up clinging to him and refusing to leave, even if it goes down to the bottom of hell's cauldron... or so you will say.'

Oai: 'O tsu ho ho, even if you hawk Sankō's secondhand words, they won't sell. Ehr, how filthy, get away from you. What a foul-smelling man.'

Sankō: 'Hey Yosō, since things have come to this, there's nothing more to be done. Oai would probably feel lonely making the journey to the netherworld all by herself, so hack up her younger sister Umé as well. And while you're at it, make an example of the traitor Kanekō as well, and turn him into a blood sacrifice for future deterrence. If we don't do that, Sankō's face won't stand. It's a pity, but since it's come to this, there's no pulling back, ah what a regrettable fellow.'

Yosō and his men abruptly drew fine cords from their bosoms, slipped them around Kanekō's neck from behind, and dragged him two or three ken. With the rope caught under his chin, Kanekō struggled with his hands and feet and suffered. Swarming forward, the mass of subordinates bound Kanekō's body tight as tightly wrapped bamboo.

Umé, Oai's step-sister who had turned fifteen this year, had covered her eyes from the very beginning, and pretending not to care, while stealing moments from the many watchful eyes, she completely untied her own ropes and feigned as if she were still bound. Sankō and all the rest of the fellows were so preoccupied with Oai's side that they failed to notice Umé doing such a thing before anyone knew it.

The sun was gradually drawing toward dusk. Sankō sat down on the rock from before, looking down coldly upon the three of them as he ordered,

Sankō: 'Now strike them down, finish them off!'

Yosō and the multitude of subordinates rushed toward the three with a clatter, striking, kicking, and bludgeoning them, and instantly a bloody battleground appeared. As this was happening, echoing through the echoes of the forest, a propaganda song could be heard.

兼公『あゝあ、サツパリ駄目だ。乍併、こんなシヤンに、仮令悪口でも詞をかけて貰うたと思へば、俺も光栄だ、アハヽヽヽ』

お愛『オホヽヽヽお前はヤツパリ私の生命を取るのが惜しいと見える。甲斐性のない男だなア。何程おどしても慊しても、痩てもこけても、侠客の妻、こんな事で屁古たれて、如何して夫の顔が立つものか。これでも内へ帰れば、沢山の乾児に、かしづかれ、姉貴々々と敬はれる姐御さまだ。お前の様な痩犬に吠えつかれて、ビクつくやうな事で、侠客の女房にはなれませぬぞや、オホヽヽヽ。あの兼公の青い顔わいのう』

兼公『何と剛情な姐貴だなア。これ丈身動きもならぬ様に縛められ、活殺自在の権を握られた敵の前で、これ丈の劫託を並べるとは、太え度胸だ。姐貴、俺も感心した。虎公が惚れたのも無理ではあるまい。俺も今日から姐貴の乾児になるワ』

与三『オイ兼公、ソリヤ貴様、何を云ふのだ。親分の前ぢやないか。そんなこと吐すと、貴様も一緒に殺んでやらうか』

兼公『ヘン、何を吐すのだい、早く殺んで欲しいワイ。こんな美しいシヤンと一緒に心中するのなら、大光栄だ。早う俺達を叩き殺して了へ、其代りに一つ頼んでおくことがある。同じ穴に向ひ合せにして埋けてくれ。それ丈が俺の頼みだ』

お愛『ホツホヽヽ、好かんたらしい。誰がお前等と一緒に埋けられて堪りますかい。冥途へ往つて迄、つきまとはれては、夫の虎公にどんなに怒られるか知れませぬわいな。お前は勝手に殺されなされ。私にチツとも関係はありませぬから……』

兼公『エヽ口の悪い女だなア。人には添うて見い、馬には乗つて見いだ。今お前が此兼公をゲヂゲヂの様に嫌つてゐるが、冥途へ行つて死出の道伴れをするやうになつてから思ひ当るだらう。人は見かけによらぬものだ、こんな男と冥途の旅をするのなら、仮令地獄の釜のドン底まで……と云つて、くつついて離れない様になりますぞや』

お愛『オツホヽヽ、三公の受売をしても、流行りませぬぞや。エヽ汚らはしい、其方へ行つて下さい。気持の悪い匂のする男だなア』

三公『オイ与三、モウ斯うなつちや仕方がない。お愛も一人で冥途の旅は淋しからうから、妹のお梅も一緒にバラしてやれ。序に兼公の裏返り者も、以後の見せしめに血祭りにして了へ。そうなくちや三公の顔が立たねえ。可哀相なものだが、こうなつちや、引くに引かれぬ場合だ、アヽ惜い者だなア』

与三公は矢庭に懐から細紐を取出し、兼公の背後より首に引つかけ、二三間引摺つた。兼公は顋をかけられたまま、手足をもがきつつ苦んでゐる。寄つてかかつて大勢の乾児は、兼公の体をがんじ搦みに巻いて了つた。

今年十五才になつた、お愛の義妹のお梅は最前から目を塞ぎ、素知らぬ顔をして、大勢の目を盗み乍ら、自分の綱をスツカリほどき、依然として縛られた様な風を装うてゐた。三公始め一同の奴は、お愛の方に気を取られて、お梅が何時とはなしに斯んなことをしてゐるのに気がつかなかつたのである。

日は漸く暮れかけた。三公は以前の岩の上に腰打かけ、三人を冷やかに見下し乍ら、

三公『ソラ討て、やつつけろ！』

と下知してゐる。与三公始め大勢の乾児は三人を目がけてバタバタと駆より、打つ、蹴る、擲る、忽ち修羅場が現出した。斯かる処へ森の谺を響かして、宣伝歌が聞えて来た。
