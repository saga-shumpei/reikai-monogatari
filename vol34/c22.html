Chapter 22: 蛙の口 [963]

蛙の口〔963〕

§ 1

In the mansion of Sankō the Great Serpent of the village of Yakata, a somewhat tumultuous voice is heard. Regarding the incident of the previous night, Sankō was holding a banquet of appreciation for his sworn brothers and underlings.

The many underlings, intoxicated on delicious sake for the first time in a long while, began chattering noisily, paying no attention to their surroundings.

First underling: 'Hey, you fellows, isn't this sweet sake simply exquisite? I haven't drunk such sweet sake as this for exactly two months today. I'd be glad if we could have things like this from time to time.'

Second underling: 'Listen here, Toku, you called such watery swill sweet sake, you must have a terribly vulgar palate. If I had to drink stuff like this, I don't know how much more refreshing it would be to drink muddy water.'

Toku, his voice turning to a whine,

Tokukō: 'Listen here, Takakō, what an outrageous thing you are saying! If it's such bad sake, why did you gulp it down like that?'

Takakō: 'The boss here was doing such unreasonable things that it got on my nerves and I couldn't help it, so I became reckless and drank a cup of it out of spite. If you don't drink sake like this, you never know when Oi's ghost might pop out, and it gives me the creeps. That's why I'm enduring and drinking sake that isn't even tasty and that I hate... well, it's not that I hate it.'

Tokukō: 'Don't say such outrageous things. Where could you ever find such magnificent sake? You just spat out that a ghost would appear, but since when do living humans become ghosts and stay that way?'

Takakō: 'Even if you say that, didn't you just kill Oi in the flesh? And didn't you tie up two men and bury them alive? No matter how strong a man is, once he is buried in the earth and loaded down with heavy stones like that, he cannot help but pass away. I am certain that tonight or tomorrow, they will come to pluck off your head, so you had better be on your guard.'

Tokukō: 'Don't talk nonsense. Down there, there is a floor and there is a lid. Oi's wretch is definitely not dead. That creature is so cunning she was just playing dead. The boss properly calculated her breathing and made it look as if she died. As for the other two fellows, not a single one of them has a scratch either; even that scoundrel Kanekō was just bound up. Gathering those three people in one place was for the purpose of preserving each other's warmth. And a thin hole has been made right above their heads so that their breath can pass through. Yosabō-kōan has grasped all of that and handled it without a flaw. Since you are just a low-ranking laborer, you wouldn't understand that far.'

Takakō: 'Even so, to have so many heavy stones loaded on top of them like that, wouldn't their bodies and everything else be crushed to pieces?'

Tokukō: 'You are a fool. Is there any possibility that the boss would kill Oi so easily when he is so deeply infatuated with her? There is a deep strategy behind this. Underneath that pile of rocks, the three of them are by no means buried. That upper part has been successfully left open.'

Takakō: 'Why on earth did you do such a strange thing?'

Tokukō: 'You fools, you could never understand the divine plans and demonic strategies of the boss. If you can keep a secret, I will tell you. Well then, will you not speak of this to others?'

Takakō: 'I will never, ever tell a soul, so please tell me the reason.'

Tokukō: 'Oh, every last one of you has eaten and drunk yourselves into a stupor and fallen asleep. Even elder brother Yosabō has gotten roaring drunk. Well then, I will tell you. In short, the reason is this. This Tokukō here has come from a distant province and has not yet shown his face to Torakō or Oi. Taking advantage of that, the boss asked a favor of me. From now on, dressed as a traveler with a bewildered look on my face, I will go to yesterday's scene of the brawl, dig up the earth with a hoe, drag out the three wretches, first untie Oi's bindings, and with a sympathetic air give her water to drink, pull out medicine from my bosom and give it to her... 'I know not what honorable place you are from, but it was a perilous affair. Well, I am a traveler, and hearing that there was a great brawl here last night, I stopped by along my way to search, and as you know, such large stones are piled up here. Seeing that the earth is fresh, it seems that someone was killed in last night's brawl and buried. Ah, how pitiful, thinking I must somehow save them, behold, I piled up these large stones here and parted the earth, and seeing your three honorable victims of disaster, I thought I must surely save one, and so I gave you my treasured medicine and let you drink water. And lo, you have come back to life like this, is there ever such a happy thing... ' and I will lay it on thick. Then Oi's wretch, with gentle eyes... 'I am the wife of a man named Torakō, but I was made to suffer such a fate by a great boss named Sankō of the Great Serpent, and was about to die buried in the earth, but thanks to you my precious life has been saved and there is no greater joy than this, O traveler, savior of my life, please cherish me forever. No matter how great Torakō may claim to be, that he doesn't even know in his dreams that his wife is suffering such terrible hardships is too heartless a man. Please, honorable traveler, be my strength...' -- ha! -- or something like that she'll spit out. Ah, once it comes to that, it's in the bag. Then I will say... 'Now now, my good lady, your gratitude is quite humbling. We are all in this world together...' and lay it on her. Then Oi's wretch will fall head over heels in love with my handsome face and bold spirit, so that if I tell her to turn right she will say 'Yes' and turn right, if I tell her to turn left she will say 'Yes' and turn left, if I tell her to die she will say 'Yes' and die, and if I tell her to massage my shoulders she will massage my shoulders; she'll be utterly swooning. Ho ho ho ho!'

Kanekō: 'Hey, all of you, aren't you going to wake up in good time? Roku-kō has just come back, so we must clear the room.'

At this voice, the company sluggishly raise their heads, and staggering about, dash out into the rear rice fields, sobering up from their drunkenness in the wind.

徳公『八釜しう云ふない。俺だけはトク別待遇をやつて呉れ。斯んな役に行くのは俺ら一寸気が進まないのではないけれどな、本当の事を言へば哥兄、お前がトク派される処だつたが、生憎トク（禿）頭病の様な頭をして居るから、お愛の奴によく顔を知られて居るなり、又其様な土瓶章魚禿ではお愛だつて釣れはしないし、如何しても此徳公ぢやなくちや勤まらないのだから、トク別大切にするのだよ』

与三『エヽ俺の頭の批評までしやがつて仕方の無え奴だ。貴様は如何やら秘密を此高公に打あけた様だ。よもやそんな事あ致しちや居るまいなア』

徳公『高が知れた高公位に言つた処で、ナニそれが邪魔になりますけエ。高を括つて云つたのだからそう声高にケンケン云つて下さるな。相互に迷惑だからエヽガラガラガラ、エ、酒の奴、あと戻りをしやがる。怪しからぬ奴だ、ウンウンウン』

与三『いい加減に準備をして行かないと遅くなるぞ』

徳公『八釜しう云ふない。死んだつて何だい。お愛が俺の女房になると云ふではなし、折角骨を折つて成功さした処が鹿猪つきて猟狗煮らると云ふ様な目に会ふかも知れないからな。まあ酒の飲まれる時に飲んだ方が余程利口だなア。自分の思ふ様にするのが人間と生れた身の一生の徳利だ。オツハツヽヽヽ』

斯く管を巻く処へ門前俄に騒がしき人の足音……其処へ勘公がやつて来て、

勘公『オイ皆の奴、宜い加減に起きぬか。今六公が帰つて来たから席をあけねばなるまい』

此声に一同はムクムクと頭を上げ、ヒヨロヒヨロし乍ら裏の田圃へ駆出し、風に酒の酔を醒まして居る。
