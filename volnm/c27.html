Chapter 27: Whirlpools of Mukden (Hōten no Uzu) [27]

奉天の渦〔27〕

§ 1

Ever since Hideo departed from the sacred ground of Aya, cherishing his great ambition, Haruko Katō, who had been gazing at the skies of Manchuria and Mongolia while harboring feelings of longing day and night, had been struggling to procure funds for the expedition. About three weeks after Hideo's departure, Haruko received a secret letter from him. In it, he had written an order to the effect that by the third day of the third month of the old calendar, she should come to the place where he was staying, accompanied by four or five people among those who thought of him, such as Yokō Yukiyoshi, Nishimura Teruo, Kokubu Giichi, Fujita Taketoshi, Satō Kunio, and others. Haruko felt as if she were ascending to heaven, and in high spirits, she conveyed this message to the aforementioned people. Among them, Satō remained in the mainland for business, Nishimura said he would wait until the party arrived in Urga, Yokō headed to Mukden in advance because he had other business, and both Kokubu and Fujita immediately began to settle their related affairs. Furthermore, Hirose Yoshikuni, who had decided to join this party by all means upon Haruko's notification, overcame all difficulties to cross into Manchuria, and decided to wait for the opportune moment by seeking employment in Dairen or Mukden depending on the circumstances.

While Haruko was also rushing preparations for departure, overcoming the troubles that were swarming in, Yokō, who had gone to Manchuria earlier, returned and conveyed the message that, 'According to the words of Karakuniwake, Hideo-sensei has already entered Mongolia, so the rest of the group should come after arriving in Urga.'

Since this was contrary to her expectations, Haruko immediately conveyed this to Kokubu and Fujita, but as the two had already completed their preparations and could do nothing about it at this late stage, they had no choice but to consult the Second Master of Ōmoto regarding the matter. Finally, the three of them, Kokubu, Fujita, and Haruko, decided to stake their lives and follow in the footsteps of Hideo's party.

On April 16, a telegram arrived from Karakuniwake in Mukden, instructing them to bring the costume of Seiwōbo. Taking this task upon themselves as well, the three departed for Mukden on April 18. From Moji, Ōtani Kyōhei, who happened to be on his way to inspect Manchuria and Korea, joined them, making the party four in total. Their hearts were already racing across the vast plains of Mongolia, and finding the steamship and train rides agonizingly slow, they arrived at Mukden Station on the evening of the 20th.

As they had telegraphed in advance, Hagiwara, Nishijima, and the wife of Karakuniwake, who was in Mukden at the time, met the party. Fearing strict surveillance by the authorities, they were guided to a house called Nakano in Inaba-chō while being wary of their surroundings.

On the following day, the 21st, the party went to the Suiya Shōkai and met with Karakuniwake, whose name was Ō Tenkai. However, Ō had a displeased look on his face and said to Fujita,

Karakuniwake: 'Did you all come here intending to enter the interior?'

Fujita: 'That is correct, of course.'

Karakuniwake: 'That is correct... you say... don't joke around. Do you think you can enter the interior so easily? It is natural that anyone would want to go to the side of Sensei; it is not just you. But to come here saying, "I want to go too, and I want to go too," without even knowing the complex relationship with Zhang Zuolin—do you think it is something I can endure? My hardships are not ordinary.'

With this preamble, he spoke at length about the circumstances since Hideo's arrival in Mukden, and stated that he absolutely refused to allow any Japanese to enter Mongolia at this time, which was a very unexpected remark.

Haruko: 'We are by no means trying to go to Sensei's side on our own accord. We have come by Sensei's order. You should know this as well...'

With that, she presented the personal letter that had come from Hideo and the request for the three to enter Mongolia from the Second Master.

Karakuniwake: 'Ah, is that so? I did not know that at all. In that case, it is as if I have taken on three more great teachers. It is quite a heavy responsibility. You have no idea what kind of hardships I have gone through regarding Sensei's entry into Mongolia.'

He spent the night talking about his hardships in facilitating the entry into Mongolia, and after the night had deepened, the party withdrew to their lodging. However, in the second meeting that followed, Karakuniwake's attitude changed drastically.

原文（近代文語）

日出雄が大志を懐いて綾の聖地を出発して以来、満蒙の空を眺めては、日夜憧憬の思ひを抱きつつ、軍資金の調達に苦労してゐた加藤明子は、日出雄出発後三週間を経た頃、日出雄よりの密書を受取つた。それには旧三月三日迄に横尾敬義、西村輝雄、国分義一、藤田武寿、佐藤六合雄其他之れを思ふ人々の中、四五名同道して吾滞在の場所迄来れとの命令が認めてあつた。加藤は天にも昇る心地し喜び勇んで右の人々に其旨を伝へた。此中佐藤は用務の為め、内地に残留し、西村は大庫倫へ一行到着迄待つと云ひ、横尾は他に要件があるので先んじて奉天へ向ひ、国分、藤田の両人は早速関係事業の整理に取掛つた。尚加藤の通告に依り是非此一行に加はらむと決心した広瀬義邦は、万難を排して渡満し、場合に依つては大連若しくは奉天に職を求めて時機の至るを待つ事とした。

加藤も蝟集し来る故障を凌いで出発準備を急いでゐる折柄、先に渡満した横尾が帰来し、

『日出雄先生は既に入蒙せしこと、唐国別の言に依れば後の連中は大庫倫到着後、来る様に』

との事などを伝へた。加藤は予期に反したので、取敢へず国分、藤田に其旨を通ずると、二人は既に準備全くなり、今更如何することも出来ぬといふ始末なので、已むなく其処置を大本二代教主に謀り、遂に国分、藤田、加藤の三人は、一命を賭して日出雄一行の跡を逐ふ事に決定したのである。

四月十六日奉天なる唐国別より西王母の服装を携行せよとの来電があつたので、其の用務をも兼ね、右三人は四月十八日奉天に向つて出発し、門司よりは偶々満韓視察の途次にありし大谷恭平が加はつて一行四人となり、心は既に蒙古の大原野に馳せ、汽船や汽車も間ドロキ心地で二十日夕奉天駅に着した。予て打電してあつたので、萩原、西島並に折柄在奉中の唐国別夫人等が一行を出迎へ、其筋の警戒厳なればとて、四辺を憚り乍ら稲葉町の中野といふ宅に案内された。翌二十一日一行は水也商会に趣き、王天海なる唐国別と会見したところが、王は不機嫌な面色で、藤田に向ひ、

唐国別『君等は一体奥地へ入る積りで来られたのですか』

藤田『左様です、勿論』

唐国別『左様です……なんて……冗談ぢやないよ。君等はさう容易々々と奥地へ這入れると思はれるのか。そりや誰だつて先生の側へ行きたいのは当然だよ、君等だけぢやない。しかし張作霖との複雑な関係を知りもしないで、ヤレ吾もソレ私もとやつて来られて耐るものか、僕の苦心は並大抵ぢやないよ』

と前置して日出雄来奉以後の事情を縷々と弁じ、此際日本人の入蒙することは絶対に断ると云ふ、甚だ意外な言葉であつた。

加藤『妾達は決して自分勝手に先生のお側へ行かうと云ふのではありませぬ。先生の御命令で参りましたのです。貴方も御存じの筈ですが……』

とて日出雄より来た親展書と、二代教主よりの三人の入蒙依頼書を差出した。唐国別は、

『あゝさうですか、私は些つとも知らなかつた。さうすると又新に三人の大先生を引受けた様なものだ。中々の大任だ。先生の入蒙に就ては、どんな苦心をしたか分りやしない』

とてこれから入蒙苦心談に夜を更かし、更闌けてから一行は宿に引取つた。然るに其後第二回の会見に於ては唐国別の態度激変し、

Karakuniwake: 'I do not know the current Gozaishiyo of Sensei. Furthermore, even if it were the words of Daisensei or Nidaisama, I cannot accept anything that goes against my own judgment.'

He thus resolutely refused the three men entry into Mongolia. The three were surprised, stunned, and indignant at his Baujiyakubujin Genji, but as there was no Sensube, they had no choice but to withdraw dejectedly.

On April 26th, having received a report from Karakuniwake that Hideo had returned from the Okuchi of Ōkura bringing news of Sensei, the three, hoping for even an Ichiru of hope, hurried to visit the Tenpo of Karakuniwake to meet with Ōkura. Ōkura opened his mouth with a friendly air and spoke.

Ōkura: 'Sensei is very energetic, so please be at ease. However, entering Mongolia at present is quite difficult, but if it is the word of Sensei to come, I shall overcome all difficulties and send you to the Okuchi. Also, even without the word of Sensei, if you insist on entering Mongolia, I cannot simply abandon you as a matter of brotherhood.'

Kato and the other two were somewhat encouraged by these words of Ōkura; they felt the joy of having the previous story—where the wife of Karakuniwake had said, 'A certain ronin says that those who insist on entering Mongolia will be killed on the way'—betrayed, and the joy of hearing from Ōkura's own mouth the words, 'If it is the word of Sensei, I will overcome all difficulties,' which only a believer could hear. Just as they were thinking that they must not miss this opportunity, Karakuniwake reproached them.

Karakuniwake: 'It is improper to still insist on entering Mongolia without following me, who am here in this land and well-versed in all circumstances.'

Kokubu, who had kept his mouth shut and had not interjected a single word until now, opened his mouth for the first time at this moment.

Kokubu: 'If this letter from Sensei had come through your hands, we might follow your words, but since this is not the case, we would like to ask you to verify it with Sensei once.'

When he said this, Karakuniwake was in a state of extreme agitation, but the following day, through Fujita who had visited him, he gave the three men a notification to the following effect.

Karakuniwake: 'I absolutely refuse the entry of the three into Mongolia. I have sent word by letter to Sensei saying... I will turn back those who come to serve... and I have conveyed to Ro Senkai... do not obey even if it is the word of Sensei if it interferes with the actions of the army... and furthermore, I have ordered the messenger... in the unlikely event that there is a reply from Sensei that differs from my own judgment, crush it on the way... If you still take free action and attempt to enter Mongolia, we will not be responsible for the dangers on the way.'

After a thorough discussion upon receiving this notification, the three decided that since there seemed to be an implication of a ban on women joining the army in the tone of Karakuniwake, Kato would give up for the time being, and they would arrange for only the two, Kokubu and Fujita, to enter Mongolia. Kato visited Ōkura and told him this, and Ōkura, sympathizing, said, 'At any rate, until we can ask for Sensei's instructions, why don't you go sightseeing in the region?' Leaving an Ichiru of hope in these words, the three toured Fushun, Dalian, and Port Arthur from April 29th to May 2nd. During this time, Hagiwara set out on the path to enter Mongolia carrying a camera.

On the day the three returned to Hōten, Ō Keigi visited them, containing the intentions of Karakuniwake, and reproached them.

Ō Keigi: 'Why did you visit Ōkura without permission from Karakuniwake, and is it not imprudent to walk about to places like Port Arthur and Dalian?'

The three had believed Ō Keigi to be a sympathizer, so they replied.

The three: 'We received a final ultimatum from Karakuniwake, so we had no choice but to rely on Ōkura-san, and we went sightseeing based on the recommendation of Ōkura-san.'

Ō Keigi: 'Do you not think that the words of Karakuniwake are a trial?'

Kato: 'I did not think so.'

Ō Keigi: 'Do you not recognize the words of Karakuniwake as the divine possession of Zuirei?'

『先生の現在の御在所は自分には分りませぬ。また仮令大先生、二代様のお言葉でも、自分の考へに反した事は聞き容れる訳には行きませぬ』

とて断乎として三人に入蒙を拒絶した。三人は其傍若無人の言辞に驚き呆れ、且憤慨したが、扨何と詮術もないので、スゴスゴと引取る外はなかつた。

四月二十六日に至り、大倉奥地より日出雄の消息を齎らし帰れりとの報告を唐国別より受けたので、一縷の望みもやと、三人は急いで唐国別の店舗を訪れ大倉に面会した。大倉は愛想よく口を開いて語る。

『先生は非常に御元気ですから御安心なさい。併し現在入蒙は余程困難ですが、先生より来いとのお言葉なれば、万難を排して奥地へお送り申しませう。又先生のお言葉なく共、強ひて入蒙せられると云ふのなら、同胞の誼として捨ておく訳にも行きませぬでなア』

加藤等三人は大倉の此言葉に稍心勇み、先に唐国別夫人が『強ひて入蒙する者は途中で殺つて了ふと某浪人が言つてますよ』との話の裏切られた嬉しさと、『先生のお言葉なら万難を排して云々』といふ信者ならでは聴く事の出来ぬ言葉を大倉の口から発せられた嬉しさに、此の機を外してはと思ふ矢先、唐国別は、

『当地に居て一切の事情に精通してゐる吾輩の言に従はず、まだ入蒙を主張するのは不都合だ』

と詰る。今迄口を噤んで一言も挟まなかつた国分は此時初めて口を開き、

『此先生からの御手紙が貴方の手を経て来たのなら貴方のお言葉に従ひもしませうが、之れはさうぢやないのですから、一応先生に御照会願ひたいものですな』

と言へば唐国別は頗る昂奮の態度であつたが、翌日自分を訪問した藤田を介し、左の如き意味の通告を三人に与へたのである。

『三人の入蒙は絶対に拒絶する。自分から手紙で先生の方へ……来奉者は追ひ返しますから御承知ありたし……と申遣はし、盧占魁には……軍の行動の邪魔になる事は先生の言と雖も聴従するな……と伝へ、尚使者に対しては……万一先生から自分の考へと違つた御返辞のある場合には途中で握り潰せ……と命じて置いた。それでも尚自由行動を取り入蒙せられるなら、途中の危険に対して吾々は責任を負はない』

此の通告を受けた三人は熟議の結果、唐国別の口吻に女子の従軍禁制の旨もあつた様だから、此際加藤は断念し、国分、藤田の二人だけ入蒙を取計つて貰ふ事にしようと一決し、加藤は大倉を訪問して之を語つた所が大倉は同情して『兎に角先生の御指図を仰ぐ迄、地方見物でもなさいませ』との事に一縷の望みを残し、四月廿九日から五月二日まで、三人は撫順、大連、旅順などを巡覧した。此間に萩原は写真機を携帯して入蒙の途に就いたのである。

三人が再び奉天へ帰来した日、王敬義は唐国別の旨を含んで来訪し、

『唐国別に無断で何故大倉を訪問したか、それから旅順、大連などと出歩くのは不謹慎ぢやないですか』

と詰る。三人は王敬義を同情者と信じて居たので、

『唐国別より最後の通牒を受けましたので已むを得ず大倉さんに縋つたのです、そして大倉さんのお勧めに依つて見物に行つて参りました』

と答ふれば、

王敬義『唐国別の言は一つの試練とは考へないですか』

加藤『さう思ひませぬでした』

王敬義『唐国別の言は瑞霊の神懸と認めませぬか』

Katō replied, 'Yes, I do not think so,' and described the progress up to this day in detail, which finally caused Ōkeigi to ease his mind and arrange for various conveniences. At this moment, Kokubu said with a look of indignation, 'What is this nonsense about Karakuniwake's words being the Kamugakari of Zuirei? The value of Ōkeigi has reached zero at this point; it is not a faith worth discussing together. If Katō-san had said... "I approve"... at that time, I had intended to break off all relations with him from now on.'

He was full of such high spirits. While waiting for news from the interior, a message arrived from Hideo stating, 'Since it is difficult for women to enter Mongolia at this time, you must travel back and forth between Nitsupōkan and perform the duties of communication.' There was no message whatsoever regarding Kokubu and Fujita, and it became clear that Ōkura's sympathy was merely a temporary comfort. Fujita said, 'What of it? I do not care. In that case, I will prepare an airplane,' and returned to the country alone.

After that, Nadahiko came from the interior as a messenger and conveyed various news, but it consisted mainly of his own tales of hardship and complaints, lacking any clear point. It seemed to contain only the message, 'Take free action for a while and wait for the opportunity.' Therefore, both Katō and Kokubu finally realized that the time had not yet arrived, and on May 8th, they made up their minds and set out on the journey home.

On the way, Kokubu smiled and said to Katō, 'Fujita-kun's plan to enter Mongolia by airplane may be fine, but in this world, gold bullets are the only thing that matters. If you only have money, even the demon mask of a rōnin will immediately turn into the smiling face of Ebisu. If you do that, outwitting the gatekeeper god and having him guide you is a task as easy as eating breakfast.'

After returning, the three took different courses of action. Later, Katō, through the introduction of Yonekura Noriharu, studied under Midorikawa Teiji, also known as Ryū Burin, who had once made a name for himself in the fields of Manchuria and Mongolia. He began practicing horsemanship and, intending to fulfill his plan to enter Mongolia with Midorikawa as his guide, was in the midst of preparations to summon Yuasa Kiyotaka, Tanimae Kiyoko, Matsumura Kiyoka, Higashio Teruko, and others, when he received news of the incident in Tsūryō.

The reason Karakuniwake and the others refused the entry of Katō and his party into Mongolia was that, besides having heard from Hideo beforehand that their entry into Mongolia was to be after their arrival in Ōkurin, they had received no orders whatsoever.

加藤は『ハイ、さうは思ひませぬ』とて今日迄の経過を精しく述べたので、王敬義も漸く心解けて、種々の便宜を計らふ事となつた。此時国分は憤然色をなして言ふ、

『唐国別の言を瑞霊の神懸とは何のこつた。王敬義の価値も茲に至つては零だね、共に語るに足る信仰ぢやないね。若しあの時加藤さんが……承認します……とでも云はうものなら、今後断然事を共にせない積りだつた』

と意気軒昂たるものがあつた。隠して奥地よりの消息を待つ中に、日出雄より『此際女子の入蒙は困難なれば、日奉間を往復して連絡の用務を勤めよ』との伝達あり、国分、藤田に関しては何等の伝言なく、大倉の同情は全く一時の気安めであつた事判明し、藤田は『ナアニ、構ふものか、それでは飛行機を用意して来る』とて単身帰国して了つた。其後へ名田彦が使者として奥地より来奉し、種々消息を伝へたが、主として自身の苦心談や、愚痴のみにて要領を得ず、只僅に『暫く自由行動を採つて時機を待て』との伝言が含まれてゐるらしく思はれたので、加藤、国分の両人も遂に時機の到来せざるを察し、五月八日意を決して帰国の途に就いたのである。途々国分は微笑しながら、

『藤田君の飛行機入蒙計劃もよからうが、今の世の中は黄金の弾丸に限るよ、金さへあれば浪人の鬼面も直ぐ恵比須顔に変るよ。さうすりや門番神を出しぬいて、道案内させる位は朝飯前の仕事だ』

と加藤を顧みて笑つた。帰来後三人は三様の活動方針を取つたが、其後加藤は米倉範治の紹介で嘗て満蒙の野に驍名を轟かせた劉武林事緑川貞司に師事し、馬術の稽古をはじめ、緑川を案内者として入蒙の意を果すべく湯浅清高、谷前清子、松村清香、東尾輝子等を招集すべく準備中、通遼の異変に接したのである。

唐国別等が加藤等の入蒙を拒んだのは、加藤等の入蒙は大庫倫着の後と、予て日出雄から聴いてゐた外、何等の命令を受けなかつたからであつた。
