Chapter 37: Oomoto Ten'onkyō (Ōmoto Ten'onkyō) [37]

大本天恩郷〔37〕

§ 1

The atmosphere of flurried confusion that had stagnated during the Odaharahiyōgi regarding the Zaiseimondai and the Hoshiyakumondai of Hideo was swept away entirely upon Hideo's Hoshiyaku and Kiriyō, and the Seichi was resurrected as a Tenchiseimei, just like the aftermath of a storm.

Jirai, the Shintenshiyugi Hideo, without yielding to Fusai or Sehiyō, moved the Zuishiyōkuwaihonbu from Kameoka to Ayabe and entrusted the Sōran of Shiyomu to Uchimaru; he himself led Masumiwake and others, established his Konkiyo at Manjiyuen, and as he had Sengen at the time of his Nifumō Shiyutsupatsu, he commenced the realization of the Divine Plan to illuminate and break the darkness of all worlds, not merely as Hideo of the Sango Seidan, but as Minamoto no Hideo of the world.

First of all, Manjiyuen was named Ten'onkyō, and it was decided that the Kōshōden, the residence of Hideo, would be newly constructed. Manjiyuen, which had long felt desolate, suddenly overflowed with vitality, and numerous believers from various places gathered in competition, saying, 'I too, I too,' to serve in the construction of the Kōshōden; the foundation stones of the Kameyama Castle ruins were dug up one by one, and in the strange fate of being useful for the divine work as if made to order, the brave shouts of 'Iyashaka, Iyashaka' shook the surrounding air in such prosperity that not only the people of Kameoka Town opened their eyes in wonder, but also people came and went incessantly to listen to Hideo's peerless management of world affairs and the absolute nature of his teachings, some seeking instruction and others looking up to him as the leader of business management; furthermore, with the new establishment of the European Language Printing Office and the relocation of the Overseas Propaganda Department, the Miroku Tower of Mount Gesshō increased its brilliance day by day, and it was not only the believers who waited with craned necks for the day of the construction of the Gekkyūden. Ah, Kannagara Tamachihaemase.

原文（近代文語）

財政問題や日出雄の保釈問題に関する、小田原評議で低迷せし周章狼狽の空気は、日出雄の保釈帰綾と共に一掃され、全くの嵐の跡の夫れの如く、天地清明の聖地と復活したのである。

爾来進展主義の日出雄は、負債や世評に屈することなく、瑞祥会本部を亀岡より綾部に移して諸務の総攬を宇知麿に一任し、自らは真澄別其他を率ゐて、万寿苑に根拠を定め、入蒙出発の際宣言せし如く、単に三五聖団の日出雄としてでなく、世界の源日出雄として、万界の暗を照破すべき、神界経綸の実現に着手したのである。

先づ以て万寿苑は天恩郷と命名され、日出雄の居館たる光照殿が新築される事となつた。久しく寂寥を感じて居た万寿苑は頓に活気横溢し、数多の信者は各地より、吾れも吾れもと先を争つて参集し、光照殿造営に奉仕する事となり、亀山城趾の基礎石は夫れ夫れ掘り起されて、誂へた如く神業に役立つ奇縁に、弥永久世弥永の掛声も勇ましく、四辺の空気を震撼せしむる盛況に、驚嘆の眼を睜るは亀岡町の人々のみならず、日出雄が天下無敵の経綸振と其説示の絶対なるに、今更の如く耳目を欹て、或は教を請ふべく、或は事業経営の主宰と仰ぐべく、往来する人々引きも切らず、更に欧文印刷所の新設、海外宣伝部の移転などで、月照山の弥勒塔日に日に其光輝を増す瑞祥に、月宮殿造営の日をも鶴首して待つのは、敢て信徒のみではないのである。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
