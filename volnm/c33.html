Chapter 33: Disarmament (Busō Kaijo) [33]

武装解除〔33〕

§ 1

As soon as Hideo and his party, having been startled from their sleep by the Denreiki of Lu, arrived at Lu's Shiyukueiminka, Lu, with a look of Fukigen on his face, vented his anger severely upon Sakamoto and Pairinku, then immediately boarded Hideo's Kyōshiya, and finally, putting on an Egaho, gave the signal for Shinpatsu.

Ino had, during the Zenya, taken his Mōkojin Jiyūsotsu and fled toward the familiar Paintara. As this region was already within a distance of seventy or eighty Shiri from Paintara, there were many settlers from Shina, and the Kauchi was open, with the cultivation of vast fields of sorghum in full swing.

Passing through this and approaching the mountainous area, they noticed in the valley to their left that sixty or seventy cavalrymen of the Shina army were advancing in parallel with them. Seeing this, Lu Zhankui jumped down from the Kyōshiya, mounted his horse, and chased after the leading unit.

By the time Hideo's Kyōshiya had advanced near the top of the hill, Lu was in the midst of holding a secret consultation with Liu Shengshan and other key generals.

Before long, the entire unit turned to the right and galloped down the hill, deciding to camp in the houses of the village, and Lu's headquarters was established in the annex within the grounds of Hideo's temporary residence.

A short while later, Lu, accompanied by Inoue Kenkichi as an interpreter, requested an audience with Hideo and Masumiwake, asking for others to be cleared from the room.

Lu: 'I cannot settle my mind unless I entrust the aftermath to Zhang Yan-san and go to Fengtian once to negotiate. It would be fine to go to Paintara first, call for Sasaki, and hear the situation in Fengtian, but please ask for a Shinchoku for me.'

Hideo: 'Masumiwake-sama, the Shinchoku is as it was before, so tell him that.'

Masumiwake: 'Yes, I understand. ...Lu-sama, there are no two words from God; like carrying firewood and leaping into a fire... that is the fate of your journey to Paintara.'

Lu: 'That cannot be. The cavalry that advanced parallel to us earlier are subordinates of the Tongliao Brigade. If there were such a tendency, they would have fired at the Great Master's Kyōshiya at that time.'

Masumiwake: 'Lu-san, I think your way of thinking is mistaken.'

Just as Masumiwake was about to say something more, Lu's adjutant brought a sealed letter, and Lu immediately opened and read it. This letter was a reply to an inquiry Lu had sent via an express messenger to the Chief of Staff of Lieutenant General Kan, who had departed for the Paintara area at this time, and it stated that he should not come to the Paintara area unless he had undergone disarmament. Upon seeing this, Lu said,

Lu: 'If the worst should happen, please pray that a storm or a great flood occurs in Paintara.'

He said this and rushed out in a flurry. Hideo had no choice but to sit in the lotus position in the garden with Masumiwake and offer prayers to God. At this time, the Shiji felt the same to both Hideo and Masumiwake: that strange events would continue from after six o'clock that afternoon, but that floods and the like were not things to be caused recklessly, and that all would be according to the hearts of the people and the timing of the moment. After Hideo and his party left the prison in Paintara, Paintara was struck by two great floods, presenting a miserable sight. If these floods had been earlier, Hideo and his party would surely have been thrown into the vortex. Ah, truly, the providence of God has not even a hair's breadth of gap.

At such a moment, an attendant came from Zhang Yan-san, who was camped five or six Cho to the west, to fetch Masumiwake, saying that an attendant was suffering from severe stomach pain. Masumiwake went immediately and performed Chin-kon, and the sick man recovered at once and led his horse out into the field. At that time, Zhang Yan-san's adjutant, Wang Zengxiang, invited Masumiwake into a room and, while offering him a meal, said,

Wang Zengxiang: 'Thank you for everything. Are you also going to Fengtian with the commander?'

Masumiwake: 'No, although I do not know what the route will be, I intend to go to Da-Kulun in the end.'

盧の伝令騎に夢を驚かされた日出雄一行が盧の宿営民家に到着するや、盧は不機嫌な面色で坂本や白凌閣に散々に当り散らし乍ら、直ちに日出雄の轎車に同乗し、漸く笑顔を作つて進発の合図をした。猪野は前夜の中に蒙古人の従卒を引きつれ、勝手知つたる白音太拉に向つて脱走したのであつた。此地方は最早白音太拉へ七八十支里の距離なれば、支那よりの移住者も多く、耕地開け、広大なる高粱畑の耕作最中である。其中を通り過ぎて山地に掛る頃、左手の谷間に当り、六七十騎の支那兵が並行して進むのが目に付いた。之を見るや盧占魁は轎車より飛び下り、馬に跨つて先頭部隊を追つかけた。日出雄の轎車が丘陵の頂上近く進んだ頃は、盧が劉陞山其他重なる部将と密議を凝らしてゐる最中であつた。

やがて全部隊は右方に向つて丘を駆け下り、村落の民家にそれぞれ宿営することとなり、盧の司令部は日出雄の仮寓所構内の別館と定められた。稍暫くして盧は井上兼吉を通訳として伴ひ、日出雄及真澄別に人払ひの上面会を請ふた。

盧『私は何うしても張彦三に跡を任せて、一度奉天へ行つて談判せねば虫が治まりませぬ。尤も白音太拉まで行つて、佐々木を呼び寄せ、奉天の模様を一応聞いた上でも構ひませぬが、一つ神勅を伺つて下さいませ』

日出雄『真澄別さま、神勅は先般の通りだから、さう言ふてな』

真澄別『ハイ、承知いたしました。……盧さま、神に二言なしで、薪を抱いて火に飛び込むが如し……と言ふのが貴下の白音太拉行の運命ですよ』

盧『そんな筈はありませぬ。最前吾々と並行して進んだ騎兵は通遼旅団の部下です。若しそんな傾向があるのなら、あの時に大先生の轎車に向つて発砲する筈です』

真澄別『盧さん、貴下のお考へは間違つてる様に思ひます』

と真澄別が何事か語らむとする時、盧の副官は厳封せる手紙を齎らしたので、盧は直ちに開封して読み下した。此手紙は、盧が急使を以て此時白音太拉方面に出発して居た闞中将の参謀長へ何事か照会した返書で、それには武装解除の上でなくては白音太拉方面へ来て下さるなとの意味が認めてあつた。盧は之を見るより、

『万一の事が有りましたら、白音太拉に暴風雨か大洪水が起る様に御祈願を願ひます』

と云ひ棄て慌ただしく駆け出した。日出雄は止むを得ず、真澄別と共に庭前に趺座し、神に祈願を凝らした。此時に神示は日出雄、真澄別共に同一様に感じ、当日午後六時以後より異変打ち続くべし、されど洪水などは妄りに起すべきものに非ず、皆それぞれの人心、時機に応ず……との旨であつた。日出雄等が白音太拉の獄舎を立出でて後、白音太拉は二回迄大洪水に見舞はれ惨憺たる光景を呈して了つた。若し此洪水が早かりせば、日出雄等も其渦中に投ぜられたに違ひない。吁実に神の摂理は毛筋の横幅程も隙がない。

斯かる折柄五六丁西方に陣取つて居た張彦三の許から、従卒が激しい腹痛を起してるからとて真澄別を迎へに来た。真澄別は早速赴いて鎮魂を施し、病人は直ちに平癒し馬を曳いて野外に出た。其時張彦三の副官王増祥は真澄別を一室に招き、食膳をすすめながら、

王増祥『いろいろ有難う厶いました。あなたも司令と同道に奉天へお越しになるのですか』

真澄別『イヽエ、併し道筋は何うなるか分らないが、結局大庫倫へ行く積りです』

§ 2

Ōzōshiyō: 'If that is the case, please use them wherever you wish. In fact, I have about one thousand subordinates in Suien, all of whom are hot-blooded young men, so they will certainly be of service to you. Please, even though we must part for a while now, I beg you not to cut off contact.'

Masumiwake wrote something on his business card and handed it to him. Ōzōshiyō watched Masumiwake go until his shadow was no longer visible, as if he were reluctant to part.

Meanwhile, at Hideo's temporary headquarters, several units of Chinese government soldiers had already entered, and both sides were relaxing and chatting together. The men, who had been craving tobacco, received gifts of it from the Chinese soldiers, and small chimneys stood in rows here and there. Hideo called for Masumiwake to welcome him.

Hideo: 'Lu came a while ago, and it has been settled that I and Inoue will accompany him tonight to go to Bailintala under the guidance of the government soldiers who have arrived. It seems you are to ride in a sedan chair tomorrow and travel along with everyone else. It is said that the government soldiers will escort you as well.'

Hideo distributed and had them carry the money he had kept for emergencies. The scene after Hideo departed that night, accompanied by Lu and riding on the white dragon horse, was truly miserable. Rumors bred rumors, an atmosphere of anxiety filled each camp, and Liu Shengshan's troops vanished before anyone knew it; some cried and complained, others were heard trying to stop those attempting to desert, and some disguised themselves to slip out of the camp—such a state continued until dawn. However, Hakuryōkaku, who had been directly under Hideo, said he would follow even to Japan, and Onchōkō said he wanted to return to Fengtian because he had a heart condition, so both decided to accompany Masumiwake's party. Ōsanjō and Kōkokuhō felt helpless and lamented the parting. After consulting with the others, Masumiwake gave them each an allowance, and waiting for the dawn, Masumiwake, Hagiwara, and Sakamoto boarded a sedan chair, while Moritaka mounted Masumiwake's horse, the White Silver Dragon, and they set out for Bailintala. Moritaka yielded the horse to Hagiwara and climbed into the sedan chair, shaking awake Masumiwake, who was just then enjoying himself in the land of Huaxu.

Moritaka: 'Look there, those are a massive number of soldiers.'

Masumiwake: 'Is that so? Have we already arrived at Bailintala?'

Moritaka: 'What are you saying? We have been chased back to where we started. It is because that horse-adjutant fellow is a fool; he took the lead and cut right into the middle of the opposing army.'

Masumiwake: 'Why did you get off the horse, you?'

Moritaka: 'The soldiers, they must have had their eyes on these long boots of mine; they kept pulling at my legs, so I had no choice but to dismount. They have even brought out artillery... there is definitely at least one full division.'

Sakamoto: 'That fellow Ino did a clever thing, didn't he? Onchōkō fled at full speed in the opposite direction a while ago, but I wonder what will happen.'

Moritaka: 'It was before we were surrounded, so he is safe. Even we would be, if only the teacher were not here...'

Hagiwara: 'What do you think, Masumiwake-san? If it comes to this, it will be a handover at the consulate, won't it?'

Masumiwake: 'In the end, that is likely what will happen.'

The group was urged by the Chinese soldiers to get out of the vehicle. Then, many soldiers gathered around, confiscating anything of value they could get their hands on, and pointing at Masumiwake and Moritaka, saying:

'These are Koreans.'

In the midst of the confusion of various actions, Lu Zhankui, who had sent Hideo to Bailintala, returned escorted by soldiers, and here an agreement regarding disarmament was discussed with Lieutenant General Kan. Meanwhile, a sergeant who claimed to be a Christian believer returned to Masumiwake a bag of silver coins he had once confiscated and requested a receipt. On the other hand, Hagiwara, having had his wristwatch stolen, complained incessantly:

'What is this? The Chinese soldiers are all thieves. They told me to show it to them, so when I showed them my wristwatch, they took it off and ran away with it.'

As a result of the long discussion, all the weapons of Lu's army were loaded onto several large carts brought by the soldiers; Masumiwake, Moritaka, and Sakamoto rode in the sedan chair, Hagiwara mounted the White Silver Dragon, those who had lost their horses hitched rides on oxen and horses, and they proceeded toward Bailintala, guarded in front and behind by Kan's brigade. On the way, at a cavalry regiment, they were treated to tea, and as they followed the path through the crowds of people in the streets of Bailintala, they saw the safe faces of Hideo and Inoue inside a carriage, which put everyone's minds at ease as they were led into the barracks. The barracks were in a state of confusion with geishas arriving and feasts being brought in; while preparations for the welcome banquet for Lu Zhankui and his men were underway, they were guided by a major of the Chinese brigade to the Hongbin Hotel, which was Hideo's lodging.

王増祥『それならば何処迄もお使ひ下さいませ。実は綏遠に私の部下が血気盛りの青年のみで一千人程居りますから、大丈夫お役に立てます。どうぞ、これで暫くお別れ致しましても、連絡を断たない様御願ひ致します』

真澄別は名刺に何事か記して之を渡した。王増祥は名残り惜しげに真澄別の影の見えなくなる迄見送つて居た。

一方日出雄の仮本営には、既に支那官兵の幾部が入込み来り、双方に打解けて談笑してゐる。煙草に焦がれてゐた連中は、支那官兵から煙草の寄贈を受けて、彼方にも此方にも小さな煙突が立並んだ。日出雄は真澄別を呼び迎へ、

日出雄『先程盧が来て、俺と井上とを同伴して今夜の中に、今来てる官兵の案内で白音太拉へ行くことに話が纏つた。貴方は明日轎車に乗つて皆と同道に行くのださうな。矢張り官兵が護衛して行くと云ふこつちや』

と万一の用意にと残してあつた金子を日出雄はそれぞれ分与携帯せしめた。此夜日出雄が盧に伴はれて白金竜に跨り出立した跡の光景は、実に惨憺たるものであつた。噂は噂を生み、不安の空気は各宿営に漲り、劉陞山の部隊は何時の間にか影を没し、或は泣声を出して愚痴をこぼす者、脱営を企つる者を引止むる声、或は変装して宿営を脱する者など、斯かる状態は夜の明くる迄継続した。併し日出雄に直属して居た白凌閣は日本迄も従つて行くと言ひ、温長興は心臓を損ねてゐるから奉天迄帰りたいと言ひ、共に真澄別一行に随行する事となつた。王瓚璋、康国宝は心細がつて別れを惜しむ。真澄別は他の人々と協議の上、それぞれ手当を与へ夜の明くるを待つて真澄別、萩原、坂本は轎車に乗り、守高は真澄別の乗馬白銀竜に跨り、白音太拉に向ふ事とした。守高は馬を萩原に譲つて轎車に乗り込み、今正に華胥の国に遊楽中の真澄別を揺り起し、

『あれ見給へ、大変な兵隊だよ』

真澄別『さうか、モウ白音太拉に着いたのか』

守高『何を言ふのだ、元の場所へ追ひ返されたのだ、馬副官の奴馬鹿だから、先頭に立つて向方の軍隊の正中へ割込んだからだよ』

真澄別『君は何うして馬をやめたのだ』

守高『兵隊の奴、此長靴に目をつけたのか、足を引張つて仕様がないから下馬りたのだ、砲兵まで引出してるが……どうしても一個師団は十分居る』

坂本『猪野の奴、巧い事をしましたなア、温長興は反対の方向へ全速力で、先刻逃げ出したが、何うでせうなア』

守高『包囲される前だつたから、大丈夫だ。吾々だつて先生さへ居られなけりやなア……』

萩原『どうです真澄別さん、斯うなりや領事館渡しでせう』

真澄別『結局さうなるだらう』

一同、支那官兵に促されて下車した。すると数多の官兵が集ひ来り、目星しい物を片つ端から没収するやら、真澄別と守高を指して、

『これは韓国人だ』

と評するやら、思ひ思ひの行動に混雑の最中、日出雄を白音太拉へ送つた盧占魁は官兵に送られて帰り来り、茲に闞中将との間に武装解除に関する協約が議せられた。其間に基督教信者と称する軍曹は、一旦没収した銀貨包を真澄別に返し領収書を請求する、一方には萩原が腕時計を奪られたとて、

『何だ、支那兵は皆泥棒だ、見せろと言ふから、腕時計を見せてやつたら、外して持逃げしやがつた』

と小言たらたらである。

長時間の協議の結果、盧軍全部の武器は官兵持参の大車数台に積みこまれ、真澄別、守高、坂本は轎車に乗り、萩原は白銀竜に跨り、馬を失ひたる者は牛馬に便乗し、闞旅団に前後を護られつつ白音太拉に進み行くこととなつた。途中騎兵聯隊に於て、茶湯の饗応を受け、白音太拉の市街人垣の中を辿り行くと、日出雄と井上との無事な顔が馬車の中に見えたのに一同心を安んじ乍ら、兵営内に導かれて行く。兵営には芸者が繰込む、御馳走が運ばれるといふ混雑で、盧占魁以下の歓迎宴準備の最中を、支那旅団の少佐に案内せられて、日出雄の宿所なる鴻賓旅館に向つたのである。
