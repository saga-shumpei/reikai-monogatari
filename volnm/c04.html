Chapter 4: Shadow of the Faint Lamp (Bitō no Kage) [4]

微燈の影〔4〕

§ 1

The fourth day of the second month of the thirteenth year of Taishiyō corresponds to the Setsubunsai, one of the annual events of Ōmoto, and the following fifth day is a Kichishin corresponding to the New Year's Day of the Kinoene of the Kiureki. However, according to the Chiugokureki, the fourth day of the second month is the New Year's Day, which seems more Seitau. Furthermore, the Kinoene of this year was a rare Higara that is said to circulate only once in one hundred and twenty thousand years according to the Chiugokureki. It is customary to hold the Guwanchōsai from the early morning of the fifth day of the second month, which is the New Year's Day of the old calendar, to worship the Tenchi and the four directions, and to express gratitude for the Jintoku of the Seitenshi. Moreover, Kinoene is phonetically associated with 'Kōshi' (renewal), and it is said to be a year in which all things are made new. On this day, the officials and believers who had gathered from various countries and regions finished the Guwanchōsai and surrounded Hideo, whose person was almost entirely accessible to the people, until late at night to listen to the discourse on the divine administration of the Shinkai. As the fifth day of the first month passed, the believers gradually returned to their countries, and the Kyōshu-dono, which had been so spacious, became filled with a sense of tranquility, like the aftermath of a flood. Around eight o'clock in the afternoon, in a room in the inner quarters of the Kyōshu-dono where the light of a lamp was dim, Hideo was whispering about the strategy for overseas propagation, surrounding a desk with a Kintairan, together with Masumiwake, Takamitsuhiko, and Karakuniwake. The bright crescent moon had set behind the peak of Mount Shio, and a chilly wind was blowing through the Shin-en where the bare trees stood in rows. Karakuniwake had once served in the Navy, and his rank was that of a Colonel. While warming his hands over a ceramic hibachi, Hideo said:

Hideo: 'Masumiwake-san, I would like you to tell me how the situation in Korea with the Futenkyō and the results of your trip to Beijing turned out.'

Masumiwake: 'Yes. Following your command, I accompanied Tadaowake and was first welcomed by Kim Seung-min, Jeon Byeong-deok, and two or three other Futenkyō believers who travel to Korea as divine envoys. We took the Korean railway to Daejeon Station, where I had a thorough discussion regarding propagation and missionary work with Mr. Kim Jeong-gon, an executive of the Futenkyō. Guided by Mr. Kim and Mr. Jeon, we arrived at the headquarters of the same religion in Jeongeup and had a pleasant, all-night conversation with the leader, Mr. Cha Yun-hong. Mr. Cha is a magnificent divine pillar, just as I had heard, and since the motives and doctrines of his religion have merely placed their stage of activity in Korea and are entirely serving the divine administration of Lord Ōkunitokotachi-no-Ōkami, I believe you are already aware of the doctrines and circumstances, so there is no need for me to report them again now. However, it seems that due to the circumstances of the Shinkai, or perhaps what one might call a karmic relationship, it is stipulated in the divine revelations of that religion that it is in the year of Kinoene that he should stand on the surface and act socially. At the same time, it is said that he cannot proceed toward the completion of the divine work without meeting Hideo-sensei and hearing your opinion. Due to misunderstandings by the authorities and slander by worldly people, they were regarded as a conspiracy group for Korean independence, and their sacred ground was raided by numerous police officers, causing them great trouble. For that reason, Mr. Cha is currently in a state of having retreated deep into the mountains, hiding from the world and advancing the divine administration of the Shinkai, so as not to show his face to the many believers until the time arrives. Under these circumstances, for the time being, both Ōmoto and Futen are merely names in their respective places of appearance, and after all, they are the teachings of the same God, so a mutual understanding was fully reached, and we returned to the country having formed a spiritual and internal alliance. Also, in Beijing, I mainly associated with university professors and other thinkers, and when we exchanged opinions and explained the doctrines of Ōmoto in detail, they were very impressed and we parted ways promising to meet again. As for the Five Great Religious Institutes, the Gozensha, and other religious organizations, Takamitsuhiko-sama is in charge of negotiations, so I did not touch them. I did, however, examine the Imperial Palace in Beijing and the Wanshoushan Lama Temple with interest to serve as a reference for the future, and they were truly magnificent. Also, Tadaowake, after consulting with leader Cha, decided to remain as an executive of the Futenkyō, changing his name to Kim In-taek, and for the time being, he will master the Korean language under the guidance of Mr. Kim and will teach Esperanto in exchange. I apologize for the delay in mentioning this, but although it is the original intention from the perspective of the divine administration for the leader of the Futenkyō to visit Japan, due to the circumstances mentioned above, I am afraid to ask, but would you please request Hideo-sensei to make arrangements to visit them once? They are waiting with crane-like anticipation, saying that they cannot be permitted to act openly unless you do so.'

Hideo: 'Since I have not met Count Sōheishiyunhaku, the leader of Jitenkyō, since we collaborated in the summer of Taishiyō 6, I have long thought that if I could find an opportunity, I would like to meet him once to discuss our future religious activities in depth. と云ふ半折の書を表装して商会の床の間にかけておいた所、河南督軍趙倜の軍事顧問をつとめてゐた岡崎鉄首と云ふ日本人がやつて来て、その掛物に目をつけ、大変に喜び、こんな大きな事を書く人はよほど変つてゐる。この歌が大変気に入つた。今私は張作霖の内意で裕東印刷所を開設しその技師長となつて勤めてゐるが、印刷所の開設された動機は、実に注意深い酢にも蒟蒻にも行かない張作霖の大秘密に属するもので、やがて雪解けともなれば奉直戦が再び起る形勢だから、その時に必要なる軍票を三千万円ばかり印刷するためです。然し吾々は奉直戦などは如何でもいいです。日本の国威が上り満蒙に流浪してゐる不逞鮮人を安気に生活させてやりさへすればいいのです。犬養先生や頭山先生、内田先生、末永節その他の名士連と謀り肇国会と云ふ高麗国の建設を図り、末永は東京方面での事務をとり、私は満州に渡つて内密にその準備をやつてゐるのです。此際蒙古の大広野を開拓し日本の大植民地を作つたら国家の為になるだらうと思ひます。こんな大事業は吾々凡夫が何程よつても到底着手することも出来ない。そして最も人心を収攪するものは宗教より外ないと考へ、内地の既成宗教家の頭を説いたが、何奴も此奴も口先ばかりで実行する勇気のない糞坊主や偽キリストばかりだ。大谷光瑞でも内地では豪僧のやうに云はれてゐるが、南洋へ行つては失敗し、印度からは追払はれ、本願寺を追出され、船を作つて天津と上海の間を往復してゐるが、それも在留日本人の一部に信用を保つて居る丈で、評判程の事もない憐れ至極の状態です。どうです唐国別さま、貴下は大本信徒の一人でもあり幹部の方でもあるやうですから、私の意図を日出雄聖師に会つて相談しては呉れませぬか等と、いろいろの面白い計画を話しました。さて聖師様、兎も角も今度渡支されたら水也商会に立寄つて鉄首に会つて貰へますまいか』

日出雄『ヤア兎も角も鉄首は面白い事を云ふ男だ。自分も実は紅卍字会へ行くとはホンのつけたりだ。広袤千里に連なる蒙古の大原野に一大王国を建設し度いと思つてゐるのだ。乍然表面は紅卍字会、普天教行としておかう』

唐国別『鉄首も今のお言葉を聞いたら喜ぶでせう。蒙古の大原野に新王国を建設するについては、今から十年以前に七万の精兵をつれて内外蒙古に進出し、庫倫をついて一時驍名を馳せた盧占魁と云ふ大英雄が一度聖師に会見し、意見が合ふたら天下のために大活動をやつて貰ひ度いものだと渇望してゐます。大神の御経綸の一端を実行するには実に絶好の機会と思ひますが、聖師のお考へは如何でせうかな』

日出雄『盧占魁は蒙古の英雄、馬賊の大巨頭と云ふ事は世間周知の事実だ。乍然大本の教主輔、しかも無抵抗主義を標榜して万有愛の実行を天下に示さむとする自分としては、馬賊の大巨頭と提携するのは考へ物だと思ふ。小胆なる大本信者の誤解を受け、教団の破壊者と睨まれるかも知れない。なるべくはそんな危険な方法を採らずに精神方面のみでやつて見ようと思ふ。第一馬賊と云ふ名が大変面白くない感じを与へるぢやないか』
