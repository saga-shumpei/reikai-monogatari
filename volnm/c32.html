Chapter 32: Bullets Flying Like Rain (Dangan Uhī) [32]

弾丸雨飛〔32〕

§ 1

Passing through the valley and facing the vast grassy field, at the foot of the mountain, a group of troops including Moritaka, Hagihara, Sakamoto, Pairinku, and other Chinese officers serving Hideo, are waiting for the rear guard while showing signs of anxiety, all surrounding a single kyōshiya.

And despite the sound of gunfire echoing loudly through the ravines, the sound of snoring like thunder can be heard from inside the kyōshiya.

At that moment, Inogunichiyō, the chief military surgeon, comes flying over with a changed complexion, dismounts in a flurry, peers into the kyōshiya, and shouts:

Inogunichiyō: 'Sensei, it is a crisis.'

Hideo, who slowly raises his body, says:

Hideo: 'Ah, I can indeed hear the sound of gunfire.'

Inogunichiyō: 'Sensei, it seems that the soldiers of Kailu have come to intercept the army of Lu. They have begun firing from the mountain on the left. I warned Masumewake-san that it would be dangerous if he did not flee quickly, but Masumewake-san seems to have realized that bullets fly whether one goes ahead or stays in the rear, and he is watching the scene as if it were a spectacle; that will not do, sir.'

Hideo: 'Ah, is that so? They will catch up soon enough.'

Saying this, he lies back down as if he, too, had realized something. At this time, Masumewake, followed by Inoue Kanekichi, proceeds calmly amidst the flying bullets, and says:

Masumewake: 'Inoue-san, who exactly are the fellows sniping at us?'

Inoue: 'They must be the soldiers of the local Wangfu. Judging by the fact that the bullets are passing overhead, it might be a signal saying that they would be troubled if we did not leave this jurisdiction quickly as it causes them inconvenience. Aha-ha-ha.'

Masumewake: 'However, the fact that both the Lama temple and the Wangfu were completely empty yesterday is suspicious. Perhaps an order has been circulated to suppress Lu as a horse bandit.'

Inoue: 'I doubt it... but... oh, look at that. That fellow Inogunichiyō has abandoned the medical supplies and run away. Aha-ha-ha.'

Just at that moment, a bullet fell with a sharp ping between Masumewake and Inoue. Inoue, with a calm face, says:

Inoue: 'Oh, you bastards, you have started aiming at us.'

Saying this, he walks along with Masumewake, keeping their horses side by side, heading toward Hideo's kyōshiya in the mood of a spectator.

Lu Zhankui gallops back and forth, raising his voice and shouting:

Lu Zhankui: 'Do not return fire, do not return fire.'

By the time the entire unit advanced through Aonogahara, the sound of gunfire could no longer be heard, but even so, two men had been wounded. The boldness of Hideo, who slept through the gunfire, and Masumewake, who watched the flying bullets with indifference, became the object of admiration for Lu and all the officers and soldiers. From this point on, they decided to advance with even stricter security, but on the 15th, when the soldiers of the Wangfu attempted to start an ambush in a mountain pass again, Lu Zhankui whipped his horse alone, rushed forward holding Mauser pistols in both hands, and Liu Shengshan's unit also opened fire, so the soldiers of the Wangfu, leaving behind their banners and some horse gear, and being well-acquainted with the terrain, quickly hid themselves somewhere. The forced march continued as usual toward the south or southeast. Some fell behind naturally as their horses died, while others caught wild horses to ride, and some in Liu's unit even requisitioned and rode a dozen camels.

On June 16th (the 15th day of the fifth month of the lunar calendar), they sat down near some houses standing here and there on a plateau where green grass grew sparsely and ate breakfast. Since the previous day, neither men nor horses had been given a moment of rest due to a lack of food, and they had hurried to this place in search of houses. They requisitioned and replenished parched rice and chickens from the houses, and everyone's energy gradually recovered. They always used washbasins and iron pots for cooking utensils, and the only seasoning was salt, which was sometimes available and sometimes not. There was a place that looked like a field near here, and when they happened to find green onions or greens there, everyone scrambled to be the first, tearing them off, rubbing the soil off with their hands, and devouring them. After the meal, Hideo slept inside the kyōshiya, and the other attendants surrounded him and were greedily enjoying a nap on the ground when, in the far rear, the sound of gunfire was heard intensely for a time. Lu Zhankui immediately rushed out with two or three attendants, and after about an hour, he returned looking wilted and spoke through his tears.

§ 2

Some dozens of the Ōfu soldiers, with deep-seated malice, lay in wait in the Minka along the Endau and sniped at Mandahan of the Saikōhaubutai. Mandahan ordered his Buka to advance, but the moment he approached them Hitori, his horse was struck down by a Tama. By a second Danguwan, Mandahan was shot through the Futomomo and fell flat to the ground. Even so, he said to his Buka, 'Mahera, advance, advance, do not worry, it is Daijiyōbu.' While he was saying this, six or seven Ōfu soldiers ran up close to Mandahan, leveled their Jiyūsaki, and fired into him, and Mandahan met his tragic Saigo. The Fukushiyō of Mandahan looked back in surprise and instantly Uchikoro two or three of them, but he too was shot through the chest and fell. It is said that in the meantime, the Ōfu soldiers hid themselves. 'Zhang Yan-san is my left arm, and Mandahan is my right arm. Today, my right arm has been taken from me; I ask for your sympathy.'

So saying, tears flowed like a river down his cheeks. All who heard him wept in sympathy.

Speaker: 'What a precious hero to have killed.'

Speaker: 'That is why we should have finished off that Ōfu in the first place.'

They muttered one after another, gnashing their teeth and wringing their hands. Just then, the Fukushiyō of Mandahan, who had received a penetrating gunshot wound in his chest, was brought in. Army surgeon Ino applied bandages, Masumiwake-sama performed Chinkon to stop the pain, and he was placed in an empty carriage loaded with provisions, and the entire march began. At this time, Lu turned to Hinodeo and said, 'Great Teacher, will there be a war within the next two or three days? Please ask the Gods.' Hinodeo ordered Masumiwake-sama to request a Shinchoku. The Shinchoku was to the effect that, 'There will be no war within two or three days, provided that we do not initiate any hostilities.' Then Masumiwake-sama asked Lu, 'The situation seems quite different from the beginning; where do you intend to settle?'

Speaker: 'It seems there is some misunderstanding on the part of Zhang Zuolin, so I would like you, Teachers, to stay in a safe zone called Qinggou, and in the meantime, I will go to Fengtian and resolve everything favorably.'

He replied, jumped nimbly into Hinodeo's carriage, and began to wield his long whip.

On the seventeenth, having rested their bodies in camp and continued their journey, the sound of gunfire was heard again, but this was the next Ōfu soldiers seeing them off to their border, and the sound of both sides exchanging salutes.

On the following eighteenth (the seventeenth day of the fifth month of the lunar calendar), while advancing, they encountered a large swamp that appeared to be two or three ri in circumference. The water did not cover the entire area, but large and small ponds were formed here and there. They decided to cross by choosing the parts overgrown with swamp grass that lay between them, but as it was a place where fine, ash-like sand was slightly damp, the horses' walking was extremely difficult. Just then, Hinodeo's carriage, with Lu Zhan-kui himself acting as the driver, had skillfully advanced to just past the center by choosing a shortcut, but for some reason, the carriage sank deep into the earth. No matter how much he whipped the horses, they would not move.

Speaker: 'Oh, this is trouble.'

Even when they sent support horses, the footing was poor, and they could not pull the carriage out. Finally, they dismantled the entire carriage to move it to a safe zone, and it took considerable time until it was reassembled. Hinodeo, Lu Zhan-kui, and others mounted the horses of Shiroganeryū and others who had come to support them, and finally managed to reach Zhang Yan-san's unit, which was waiting on the hill on the opposite shore. Moritaka, while cleaning his mud-covered shoes, muttered, 'This is a hint of the divine work this time. It must mean that we should dismantle it once and rebuild it from scratch.'

Speaker: 'Well, that is about the size of it.'

Speaker: 'By the way, Lu-san, there has just been a Shinchoku saying that for you to head toward Bailintala to go to Fengtian is like carrying firewood and jumping into a fire.'

Aha-ha-ha.'

Hideo: 'Escape, you say? Where would we go?'

Ino: 'I know the geography from here toward the Shiraontaira region well. As Shiraontaira may be dangerous due to mobilization preparations, I will take responsibility and guide you as far as Senkatō.'

Inoue: 'That is right. If Ino-kun takes responsibility as far as there, I will take responsibility for the route from Harubin using the East China Railway to enter Kōanrei.'

Ino: 'Teacher, if you do that and let me go to Kōanrei to study, I will accompany you forever. In truth, I have both parents and a wife in my country, so I value my life.'

Masumiwake: 'That is a very excellent plan, but if you are careless, you will create enemies among your allies.'

Hideo: 'That is right; in any case, it would be best to consult with Lu Zhankui, obtain his consent, and then proceed.'

Ino immediately went to fetch Lu Zhankui and told him in detail about the mobilization preparations of the Shiraontaira army and his own proposal. Lu, with an expression that seemed to say, 'Ino, that fool, is saying unnecessary things,' said,

Lu: 'Subjugate us? How could such a thing happen? And going to Harubin is, on the contrary, dangerous. Rather than that, I will accompany you, so please wait at the Etsuraizan in Eikō, and I will settle matters with Zhang Zuolin and come to see you immediately. And since I have a close friend in Shanghai, I will reorganize my forces there, and I ask that you stay there until I come to welcome you. In any case, I will build a great Lama temple in Shanghai for the sake of the Teacher, so please do not worry at all.'

With that, he returned to a chamber of the Lama temple which had been designated as the temporary headquarters.

On the afternoon of the day he bade farewell to the Lama temple and headed toward Shiraontaira on June 19th (the 18th day of the fifth month of the lunar calendar), they proceeded across a vast grassy plain where not even the shadow of a mountain could be seen as far as the eye could reach. As they advanced, the wind blowing from the direction of Shiraontaira became increasingly strong, to the point where some had their hats blown away, others had their strings snapped and their sedge hats dancing in the sky, and one felt the danger of remaining upright on horseback.

真澄別『それは非常に結構な計画だが、うつかりすると味方に敵が出来るよ』

日出雄『さうだ、兎に角盧占魁に相談して同意を得その上にするがよからう』

猪野は直ちに盧占魁を迎へ来り、白音太拉軍の出動準備の事や、自分の提案を逐一話した。盧は、『猪野奴、余計なことを云ふ』といふ顔付にて、

盧『吾々を討伐なんて、そんな事があるものですか。そして哈爾賓行なんて、却つて危険です。それよりも私がお供を致しますから営口の悦来棧で御待ち願ひ、私は張作霖と話を纏めて直ぐ伺ひます。そして上海に私の親友が居りますから、其処で私が陣容を立直し、根拠地を定めてお迎へに参ります迄、御滞在を願ひます。何れ上海へも先生の為に大喇嘛廟を建てて差上げますから、決して御心配なさいますな』

とて臨時司令部と定められた喇嘛廟の一坊に帰り行く。

六月十九日（陰暦五月十八日）喇嘛廟に暇を告げて白音太拉に向つた日の午後は、茫々として見渡す限り山の影も見えない大草野原を進むのであつた。進むにつれて白音太拉方面より吹来る風は益々強くなり、帽子を飛ばす者、紐を切つて笠の空中に舞ひ上る者、姿勢正しく馬上に居る事の危険を感ずる程である。

§ 4

As the Taiyō, which had emerged from the grass, began to sink into the grass once more, the wind added to its strength ever more. Masumiwake looked back at Ino and said,

Masumiwake: 'A wind of Imishinchiyō is blowing, is it not?'

When he said this, Ino replied,

Ino: 'I feel the same. It must be a Gokeikoku from the Kamisama telling us not to go to Paintara.'

While answering thus, they both galloped in pursuit of Hideo's Kyōshiya. Though they rode on and on, the Kusano never ended, and around the time of the Yohiyami, they arrived at a Mindan of five or six houses lined up in the middle of the Saugenchiu, where Masumiwake called out to Hideo.

Masumiwake: 'Sensei, Hagihara-san's horse seems to be suffering, and he appears to be quite delayed, so shall we not wait for him for a while?'

Hideo: 'Is that so? Then we must wait for him.'

Onchiyōkiyō: 'But Commander Lu has already gone quite far ahead on horseback today. If we do not advance quickly, we will lose our way.'

Sakamoto: 'Hey, On, do not say such unnecessary things. If Sensei says to wait, will you not be silent and wait?'

The moon of the Jifuhachinichi was covered by clouds, leaking only a faint light onto the Kusano. With the arrival of Hagihara, Hideo's Kyōshiya began to move.

'The other unit must have advanced in this direction, surely.'

Just as they thought they had advanced for Sūjitsupunkan, they came out beside the private houses.

'What is this? Have we been bewitched by a fox? We have returned to the back again.'

It was only natural that Sakamoto shouted this, for this unit had circled a part of the Kusano and returned to the original private houses. The escort soldiers were taken aback, and in order to contact the other unit, they fired into the sky, whereupon the sound of a signal gunshot was heard from ahead. Relying on the sound of the gunfire, they began to advance, but before they knew it, they had returned to the side of the original private houses. Despite the fact that there were many paths in the Kusano, they could not advance. And the wind had already diminished in its force.

Masumiwake: 'Sensei, since the white fox is being this careful, shall we not stop advancing into the danger zone?'

Hideo: 'Even if we stop, there is nothing we can do, is there?'

Masumiwake: 'Haha, the convenience of the Ōkami is another matter entirely. In that case, let us at any rate stop here for a while.'

While bathed in dew, they followed their own dream paths with Hideo's Kyōshiya at the center. This unit was awakened by a messenger from Lu Zhankui just as the Kusano was beginning to brighten at dawn.
