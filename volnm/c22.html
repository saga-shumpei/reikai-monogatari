Chapter 22: The Field of Mukyoku Shūgahara (Mukyoku Shūgahara) [22]

木局収ケ原〔22〕

§ 1

Oni (Hideo), Matsumura (Masumiwake), Yano (Karakuniwake), Ueshiba (Moritaka), Nada Otokichi (Nadahiko), Sasaki (Sakaki), Ōishi (Ōkura).

After the organization of the army, Hideo, accompanied by the guard commander Heukiyoshin and over a dozen soldiers, held rabbit hunts in the northern hills and fields, at times gathered wild leeks and garlic, and spent his days in Sōron pleasantly, engaging in 'tenmurichiebuna' while facing the vast wilderness of withered grass. Okazaki Tetsushu, Hagiwara Toshiaki, and Ino Toshio, the three of them, were escorted by several dozen soldiers and, riding on horseback, broke through the encirclement of bandits and government troops on the way to arrive safely at Hideo's location. Nadahiko, following the orders of Hideo and Lu, took several guards with him and returned to the Shuiye Chamber of Commerce in Fengtian as a military envoy.

As mentioned before, the Mongolian houses were extremely filthy, with bedbugs running rampant; furthermore, because they had not used hot water for many days, lice had developed in their clothing. The party spent their days fighting bedbugs and lice, and to prevent bedbugs, they ate the foul-smelling garlic at every meal while grimacing. Hideo also became accustomed to eating garlic, and eventually came to enjoy eating raw leeks and vegetables without concern. Wen Changxing became the head of the kitchen for Hideo's party, Wang Zanzhang became the head of the grooms, Wang Shengming became the head of the attendants, Moritaka became the head of the personal guards, and Nadahiko became a personal attendant, all serving in every task regarding Hideo's person, while Masumiwake came to exercise all powers of attorney. Furthermore, Hagiwara Toshiaki served as the photographer, Sakamoto Kōichi as a personal attendant, and in addition, Li Lianzhang and twenty soldiers were in charge of direct protection. Coming out to Mongolia, which is said to have sixteen times the area of the country from which all their systems were so cramped, and running about freely and at will while dealing with many soldiers and livestock, was a pleasure and a sense of ease that he had not experienced once in the fifty-four years since he was born. Hideo rejoiced, thinking that for a man of courage, it was truly exhilarating to play interestingly with foreigners in the sky of an unknown foreign land, rather than being born into this world and receiving strong oppression in a narrow country.

At the headquarters on Mount Sōron, the leaders of the cavalry troops would arrive daily with two or three hundred of their subordinates, and with the brave sound of trumpets, they participated, greatly boosting the military spirit. Commander-in-Chief Lu came to Hideo's residence on May 1st, and with sincerity appearing on his face, requested orders regarding the army's movements:

Lu: 'If we intend to advance to Da Kulun, scouts have revealed that seven thousand Red Army troops are stationed at a certain point in the Xing'an Mountains, two hundred li away from here, and their vigilance is quite strict. Therefore, following your command, to advance straight to Da Kulun as it is would only result in losing soldiers and consuming ammunition. Moreover, it is not easy for our tens of thousands of participating troops from the three regions of Rehe, Chahar, and Suiyuan to arrive, as the road is long. Therefore, I do not know if this goes against your intentions, but based on military experience, I believe it is best to advance into the special regions of Rehe, Chahar, and Suiyuan, spend the winter in this region this year, complete our military preparations, invite and soothe the various kings, and wait for next spring to advance to Da Kulun and begin negotiations with the Red Army. If peace talks do not succeed, we will have no choice but to resort to war. Since about ten thousand Red troops are stationed in Da Kulun and martial law has been declared, it will not be easy to achieve our goal with a small army. It is a certain fact that at least one hundred thousand soldiers will gather under your command by next spring, so after that, we shall enter Da Kulun, establish a base here, and taking advantage of the momentum, unite with Xinjiang and bring the Red Army in Siberia to submission. To the very end, for the sake of love for humanity and for your sake, I will dedicate my life, and I am determined to carry through the original intention of a Mongolian man, believing that whatever happens hereafter is the will of God. Once this goal is achieved, not to mention the four hundred-odd provinces of China, the Three Eastern Provinces will surely obey your command.'

Hideo had left his homeland far behind for the land of deep Mongolia, a place unknown to him, and was engaged in the greatest divine work since the creation of heaven and earth, dealing with Chinese and Mongolians with whom he could not even communicate in language—how, indeed, must his pride have been? From the various kings, cavalry leaders, and living Buddhas, gifts of pigs, wild sheep, and roasted rice were sent to Hideo and Commander-in-Chief Lu as expressions of sympathy. Hideo, together with Lu, spent his days in a life of gratitude. Hideo, who upheld the teaching of universal love, had to eat pork, mutton, and chicken meat whether he liked it or not after coming to such a country. Masumiwake suffered from 'nūru', 'chiyakana', and 'machina' (facial swellings), Moritaka's cold since leaving the country had not yet healed, Sakamoto also had symptoms of a cold and his whole body ached, and Hideo was being attacked by bedbugs. One day, the Mongolian secretary-general came and spoke in Chinese as follows:

王仁（日出雄）、松村（真澄別）、矢野（唐国別）、植芝（守高）、名田音吉（名田彦）、佐々木（榊）、大石（大倉）

日出雄は軍の編成後、護衛長馮巨臣以下十数名の兵卒を伴ひ、北方の野山に兎狩を催し、或時は野生の韮や蒜を採集し、枯草の芒々たる原野に向かつてテンムリチエブナをなし、愉快に索倫の日を送つて居た。岡崎鉄首、萩原敏明、猪野敏夫の三名は、数十人の兵士に送られ馬に跨りて、途中馬賊や官兵の囲を衝いて無事日出雄の許に着いた。名田彦は日出雄及盧の命に依つて、数名の衛兵を従へ軍使として奉天の水也商会へ引き返した。

蒙古の家屋は前述の通り極めて不潔で、南京虫の横行甚だしく、加之に幾十日も湯を使はない為めに衣服には虱発生し、一行は日々南京虫と虱退治に日を費し、南京虫の予防の為めにとて、いやな香のする蒜を顔をしかめて食事毎に喰つて居た。日出雄も蒜を喰ひ慣れて遂には、葱や野菜の生を平気で嗜食するやうになつた。温長興は日出雄一行の炊事長となり、王瓚璋は馬夫長となり、王盛明は随従長、守高は近侍長、名田彦は近侍となつて日出雄の身辺の凡ての用務に仕へ、真澄別は一切の代理権を行使する事となつた。又萩原敏明は写真係、坂本広一は近侍、外に李連長以下二十名の兵士が直接保護の任に当つて居た。総ての制度がせせこましかつた国から十六倍の面積を有すると云ふ蒙古へ出て来て、沢山の兵士や畜類を相手に自由自在に勝手な事をして飛び廻るのは、生れて以来五十四年間未だ嘗て経験した事のない愉快さ呑気さだ。大丈夫たるもの現世に生れて狭い国で強い圧迫を受けて居るよりも、勝手気儘に知らぬ外国の空で、外国人と面白く遊ぶのは実に壮快だと日出雄は喜んでゐた。

索倫山の本営には馬隊の頭目が日々二百人三百人と部下を率ゐて、喇叭の声も勇ましく参加し軍気大に振つた。盧総司令は五月一日日出雄の館に出で来り、

『大庫倫に進出せむとすれば、此処より二百支里を隔てたる興安嶺の或地点に赤軍七千人駐屯し、警戒なかなか厳重なる事が斥候に依つて判明致しました。それ故、貴下の命に従つて、此儘大庫倫へ直進するは、兵を損じ弾薬を消費するばかりで、且つ熱、察、綏三区域の我が数万の参加軍の到達するは、道遠くして容易でない。それ故、貴下の意に背くかは知りませぬが、軍事の経験上、熱、察、綏の特別区域に進出し、本年はこの区域に於て冬籠りをなし、完全な兵備を整へ諸王を招撫し、来春を待つて大庫倫に進み赤軍と交渉を開始し、若し和議成らざれば止むを得ず開戦の挙に出づるを可とすべく、大庫倫には約一万の赤兵駐屯し、戒厳令を布き居れば、小数の軍隊にては容易に目的を達すべからず、来春ならば些なくとも十万の兵が麾下に集まるは確なる事実でありますから、其上にて大庫倫入を為し、茲に根拠を定め、勢に乗じて新彊を合せ、西比利亜の赤軍を帰順させ、飽迄も人類愛の為め、貴下の為め、一身を捧げ、此上如何になり行くとも神の思召と信じ蒙古男子の初志を貫徹さす考へです。此目的を達した暁は支那四百余州は言ふに及ばず、東三省も必ず貴下の命に服するでせう』

と誠意を面に現はして軍の行動につき命令を乞うた。

日出雄は遠く故国を去つて不知案内の奥蒙の地、しかも言語不通の支蒙人を相手に開闢以来の大神業に従事する──彼の得意は果して如何であつただらう。各地の王や馬隊の頭目、活仏などよりは見舞として、豚や野羊、炒米などを日出雄及び盧総司令に送つて来る。日出雄は盧と共に日々感謝の生活を送つて居た。万有愛護の教を立てて居る日出雄も、かかる国へ来ては否でも応でも豚や羊、鶏肉などを喰はねばならなかつた。真澄別はヌール、チヤカンナ、マチナ（顔面の吹腫物）に苦み、守高は出国以来の風邪未だ癒えず、坂本も亦風邪の気味にて全身痛み、日出雄は南京虫に攻められて居た。一日、蒙秘書長来り、支那語を以て左の如く談じた。

Speaker: 'Yesterday arrived, two hundred rifles and horses complete, today sent two fat pigs and two fat sheep, within five days another troop of six hundred rifles and horses complete will come. The commander is very happy, but is troubled by the lack of funds and suffers from the inability to borrow; he asks how to manage when the troops all arrive without funds, and what is the real way to do it.'

Speaker: 'Do not worry, Excellency. If you do not encounter any difficulties in the current situation, you will surely be able to pay the funds once Chiyōsakurin arrives. Once the troops are gathered, the funds will not be a problem.'

In short, the Shiyushi of this is that once the Heibi is prepared, Chiyōsakurin will send a Sautau amount of Gunshikin and Buki, but until then, there is no Shikata, and he means for you to Shinbau for a while. And so, today too, Sankahei carrying Buki arrived and sent pigs and sheep. He also said that in Itsuka, a Batai of Rokupiyaku would arrive Koko.

Although leaving the Kuōdaimuhen and fertile Genya to be overgrown with Zatsusau, they dislike Takokujin entering and fear them, and if they see a Guwaikokujin, they immediately shoot them to death with guns. The Chiyoroman Jinshiyu, who are neither Mōkojin nor Shinajin, nor are they Roshiajin, had Katsukiyo in this land until Jifunen Izen, but now they have retreated to the forests in the north, several hundred Shiri away. Rabbits and pheasants are particularly numerous in this area, and large trees such as poplars, willows, and elms, which seem to take several people to embrace, grow thick in the mountains and fields. The clear stream of the Tōji River, containing soda, flows slowly, and it is a rare treasure house where the blessings of nature are left infinitely abandoned. Hinodewake wondered why the authorities and politicians of Japan did not turn their eyes to this land.

When Hinodewake was playing with the Mōkojin, saying things like 'Onkusu, arutena, unnurutē' (fart smell), Ro Senkai came bringing rare sweets and fruits sent from Tōnan, saying he was paying his respects. And according to Ro's story, it had been six hundred and sixty-six years since Genghis Khan raised an army in the Mōko wilderness, and seeing that the three head characters matched, he was excited, saying that this year was finally the year of three-six.

In the spring field, Korigīhoara (wild Adonis) here and there, Borungachichiku (purple flowers) have begun to bloom.

Although it was the beginning of May, in the deep interior of Mōko, wild Adonis bloomed in white and purple, as beautiful as if a flower mat had been spread out. Hinodewake whipped his horse through this flower mat in all directions, commanding many Shinamōhei to attempt a picnic. The 'ken-ken' cries of pheasants could be heard from among the withered grass here and there, and three mountain rabbits jumped out from there. The Mōkohei immediately aimed their Mauser rifles, and with 'pon-pon-pon', they fired three shots in succession, hitting and capturing the three rabbits right in the head. All Mōkojin make bows from poplar branches, make arrows from thin ones, tie stones to the tips of the arrows, and use rough hemp rope as strings; they rarely miss when shooting birds flying in the sky. Even with firearms that seem to have become waste in Shina or Roshia, it is truly mysterious that they hit the mark every time the Mōkojin use them. They are equipped with such natural shooting skills that one almost thinks they are gods.

One day, while Hinodewake was wearing Lama robes and doing 'pasapāna' (vomiting feces) far in front of the headquarters, seven or eight 'Shīgō', the famous Mōko fierce dogs, gathered, trying to eat the 'pasa'. This Shīgō is a hybrid of a Mōko dog and a wolf, and is a heaven-sent noble beast in Mōko, a pastoral country, said to be extremely strong and capable of biting any fierce beast to death. When Hinodewake pulled up his pohora and was straining with 'un-un', a Shīgō came along. When a Chiyoroma (large-eyed rodent) living in a hole in the grassland smelled the 'pasa' and stuck its head out of the hole, the Shīgō, with its sharp sense of smell, immediately dug the hole with its front paws from all directions and captured the Chiyoroma in the blink of an eye. Its agility is truly worthy of astonishment.

Then, a great wind, famous in Mōko, began to blow. Because Hagiwara and Sakamoto had gone for a long ride on horseback and set fire to the wilderness, the fire was fanned by the wind and approached Hinodewake's surroundings with the force of a thousand miles, with black smoke billowing. It was impossible to escape from the vast withered grass. The Shīgō barked vigorously. Hinodewake recalled the story of how, when Yamato Takeru no Mikoto was conquering the Eastern Barbarians, he was caught in the enemy's strategy at Yaizu in Suruga and surrounded by fire, took out a fire-starter to set a counter-fire, and mowed down the grass with the sacred sword of Murakumo to achieve a great victory. He immediately drew his sword, mowed the grass around him, took a match from his pocket to set a counter-fire, and then chanted the Kototama of the Heavenly Numbers. Mysteriously, the wind suddenly changed to the south, and both Hinodewake and the soldiers escaped the danger of being burned to death. It is truly dangerous to set fire to the Mōko fields and play.

『昨天到来、二百槍馬完全今日送給肥猪両口肥羊二隻、不出五日又来隊伍六百槍馬完全的。司令近非常歓喜所愁的款項無有的又苦於告貸将来隊伍均到来的無款的怎麼必実在投法子。

閣下不愁不遇現状況難一点的事作到張作霖必能付給款項俟将隊伍招斉款就不困難了』

要するにその主旨は、兵備が整つた上は張作霖より相当の軍資金及び武器を送つて来るが、それ迄は仕方がない、暫く辛抱してくれと云ふ意味である。さうして今日も武器を携帯して参加兵が二百名豚や羊を送つて来た。又五日の後には六百の馬隊が此処に到着するとも云ふた。

広大無辺の肥えた原野を雑草の生ふるに任せ乍ら、而も他国人の入り来るを嫌ひ、怖れて外国人と見れば直に銃を以て打ち殺すと云ふ、蒙古人ともつかず支那人ともつかぬ又露西亜人でもないチヨロマン人種が、十年以前迄此地に割拠して居たが、今は数百支里の北方の森林に退却して居る。兎や雉などは此方面は特に多く、幾抱えもあるやうな楊、柳、楡の大木は山野に繁茂し、洮児川の曹達を含んだ清流はゆるやかに流れ、天然の恩恵は無限に遺棄されて居る稀有の宝庫である。日出雄は日本の当局や政治家が、何故此地に目をつけないのであらうかと怪しんだ。オンクス、アルテナ、ウンヌルテー（放屁臭）など云つて、蒙古人を相手に日出雄が戯れて居ると、盧占魁は御機嫌伺ひだと云つて、洮南から送つて来た珍らしい菓子や果物などを持つて来た。さうして盧の話によれば、成吉思汗が蒙古の原野に兵を挙げてから六百六十六年となり、頭字の三つ揃うたのを見れば、愈々本年は三六の年だと言つて勇んで居た。

春の野にコルギーホアラ（野生福寿草）あちこちとボルンガチチク（紫色の花）咲き出でにけり

五月の上旬であり乍ら、蒙古の奥地では野生の福寿草が白や紫に咲き誇つて、殆ど花莚を敷き詰めた如く美しい。日出雄は此花莚の中に馬を縦横無尽に鞭ちながら、数多の支蒙兵を指揮して野遊を試みた。ケンケンと雉子の声が彼方此方の枯草の中から聞えて来る、其処へ三匹の山兎が飛び出した。蒙古兵は直ちにモーゼル銃を擬し、ポンポンポンと三発続け打ちに三匹の兎を頭許り撃つて捕獲した。総て蒙古人は楊の枝で弓を拵へ、細いので矢を造り、矢の先に石を縛付けて荒き麻の縄を弦となし、空立つ鳥を撃つに滅多に外れた事がない。支那や露西亜で廃物になつたやうな銃器でも、蒙古人が使ふと一々命中するのは実に不思議な程である。殆んど神様ではないかと思ふ程、天性的射術の技能が備はつてゐる。一日、日出雄が喇嘛服を着けた儘司令部の遥か前方で、パサパーナ（吐糞）をやつて居ると、シーゴーと云ふ蒙古名物の猛犬がパサを食はむとして七八頭も集つて来た。このシーゴーは蒙古犬と狼との混血児で、非常に強く如何なる猛獣と雖も噛み殺すと云ふ牧畜国の蒙古にあつては天与の貴獣である。日出雄がポホラを捲つてウンウンと気張つてゐると、シーゴーがやつて来た。草原の穴の中に住んでゐた大眼子（チヨロマ）がパサの臭を嗅いで穴から首をつき出した処、嗅覚の鋭いシーゴーが直様四方八方から其穴を前足で掘り、見る見るチヨロマを捕獲して了つた。其の敏捷こい事は実に驚歎に価する。

すると折柄蒙古名物の大風が吹いて来た。萩原や坂本が馬に遠乗して原野に火を放つたので、火は風に煽られ一瀉千里の勢で黒煙濛々と日出雄の身辺迄迫つて来た。茫々たる枯草の中到底逃れる事は出来ぬ。シーゴーは盛んに吠え立てる。日出雄は昔日本武尊が東夷征伐の時駿河の焼津で賊軍の計略にかかり火に包まれ、燧を取り出して向へ火をつけ、且叢雲の神剣にて草を薙ぎ払ひ大勝利を得られた故事を思ひ出し、直に佩刀を抜き放ち身辺の草を薙ぎ、懐中よりマツチを取出して向ひ火をつけ、さうして天の数歌を奏上した。不思議にも風は俄かに南方に変じ、日出雄も兵士も焼死の難を免かれた。蒙古の野に火を放つて遊ぶのは実に剣呑である。
