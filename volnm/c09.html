Chapter 9: The Commander's Official Residence (Shirei Kōkan) [9]

司令公館〔9〕

§ 1

In the official residence of the Mōko hero, General Rochiujiyō, three men—Minamoto no Hideo, Moritaka, and the Shinago interpreter Ōgenki—remained holed up day and night, while the cadre of Rochiujiyō's adjutants, Onchiyōkō, Kazenei, Shinsen, and Rojiutei, performed their duties as hosts for Hideo, exerting themselves with sincere loyalty at the command of Rochiujiyō. Masumiwake and other Manshiurauninren, using the Mizuyashiyōkuwai and other locations as their bases of operation, engaged in various consultations and ran about seeking the understanding of Chiyōsakurin. Hideo, through an interpreter, preached the way of God to the Chinese military officers who visited daily, and saved those suffering from incurable diseases. The cautious Rojiunkai had a deep inclination toward superstition; he would perform amateurish divination three times a day by placing six one-rin coins in his palm, and since he decided his actions based on the auspicious or inauspicious results, he could not avoid being slow in all matters. Even if the Japanese comrades had gathered their heads together and decided upon a course of action, if the divination was unfavorable, Rojiunkai would deny and reject it without a second thought. Everyone felt greatly troubled by this. There was no path left but for the final resolution to be determined by Hideo.

Hideo had his Chinese attendants and the Japanese side each examine Chinese clothing and wear it, and he even changed their names to Chinese-style ones. Hideo changed his name to Ōbunshō, Masumiwake to Ōbunshin, Moritaka to Ōmoritaka, Natahiko to Chōtetsu, Okazaki to Kōseikun, Ōkura to Sekidairyō, Hagiwara to Ōbinmei, Karakuniwake to Ōtenkai, and Sasaki to Ōshōki, and they prepared to carry out their entry into Mōko, fully disguised as Chinese. When Hideo had his Chinese clothing tailored, Rojiunkai secretly instructed the owner of the clothing shop to call a famous Chinese physiognomist to examine whether Hideo possessed the qualifications of the savior found in ancient legends. As a result of having him meticulously examine everything from Hideo's skeletal structure, appearance, and the shapes of his eyes, mouth, nose, and ears, to the circumference of his chest, the length of his limbs, and even the joints of his fingers and his fingerprints, the physiognomist declared that he possessed the so-called thirty-three marks and was a savior sent from heaven. Upon hearing this, Rojiunkai shed tears of joy and came to revere Hideo as the true pillar for the construction of the Mōko Kingdom. Despite Rojiunkai examining Hideo's body under such caution, Hideo went to the headquarters at Mount Soron and did not realize such things until Rojiunkai confessed it himself. The physiognomist particularly looked at the four-heaven pattern in Hideo's palm and the flow patterns on his fingertips, and delivered the following judgment.

Palm Four-Heaven Pattern = Qian Wei Tian (The Creative)

Great is the Creative, the origin of all things, which indeed governs the heavens. The clouds spread and the rain flows; the various things take their forms. The great brightness ends and begins; the six positions are completed in time. At the right time, one rides the six dragons to govern the heavens. The way of the Creative changes and transforms, each rectifying its nature and destiny. It preserves the great harmony, which is firm and correct. It brings forth the myriad things, and all nations are at peace.

Fingerprint All-Flow = Kun Wei Di (The Receptive)

Perfect is the Receptive, the origin of all things, which indeed follows and supports the heavens. The Receptive is thick and carries all things; its virtue matches the boundless. It contains and expands, bright and great; the myriad things are brought to completion. The mare is a creature of the earth; it travels the earth without bounds. Gentle, compliant, firm, and correct, the superior man has a direction to go. If he goes forward, he loses his way; if he follows, he gains the constant. In the southwest, he gains clarity; in the northeast, he loses clarity. To have friends in the southwest brings joy; to have friends in the northeast brings loss. The rest is auspicious, and the response of the earth is boundless.

Rojiunkai was further astonished and delighted to see the stigmata of the nails on the cross of Christ that had appeared in Hideo's palm, as well as the moles on his back that formed the shape of the Orion constellation. He showed these details to the elders of the Gelaohui, such as Yōseigyō, and to the Mōko kings, the Beile, the Beizi, and the Zhenguo Gong, as well as to bandit leaders such as Chiyōsansan, Chiyōkeirin, Sūshūmei, Kazeinkō, Ryūshōsan, Daieiji, and Kabōkei, and to the officers under Chiyōsakurin. He was convinced that Hideo was a savior sent from heaven, and that if they were to be active in Inner and Outer Mōko with this savior at their head, success would be undoubted. Therefore, even after entering Mōko, Hideo received extraordinary respect and trust from all people, both high and low.

○

It is necessary here to state a little about the relationship between Hideo, Rojiunkai, and Chiyōsakurin. Chiyōsakurin was deeply worried about the insufficiency of his own military strength due to the recent Fengtian-Zhili War. He knew that if the Fengtian-Zhili War were to break out again, the ill-prepared Three Eastern Provinces would immediately fall into the fate of defeat, and it would be impossible for him to reign over the Three Eastern Provinces as the King of Manchuria. Therefore, Chiyōsakurin secretly thought of somehow extending his influence into Inner and Outer Mōko to pressure and threaten Beijing from the rear, thereby preventing the outbreak of the Fengtian-Zhili War. Thus, he had the ambition to use Rojiunkai to advance into Inner and Outer Mōko, and if possible, to take Inner and Outer Mōko completely into his own hands. Even though Rojiunkai was now cooperating with Hideo to build the Ōmoto Kingdom in Mōko, Chiyōsakurin, who had risen from being a bandit and had little religious sentiment, did not pay much respect to Hideo. He only intended to use him for his own purposes by skillfully going through Rojiunkai. Furthermore, the cunning Chiyōsakurin told Rojiunkai to procure weapons with his own money, attempting to obtain Inner and Outer Mōko without dirtying his own hands. Rojiunkai was also inwardly very indignant at Chiyōsakurin's methods, and he shifted the respect and devotion he had felt for Chiyōsakurin toward Hideo, who had supported him, pouring his devotion into him instead. Rojiunkai then swore to Hideo that he would obey his commands in all things. Within the Fengtian jurisdiction, they used the name 'Northwest Autonomous Army' as their banner, and once they went to Mount Soron and the camp was established, they planned to rename it the 'Inner and Outer Mōko Relief Army' and advance to Soron with the plan of carrying out a great undertaking with Hideo as the commander-in-chief.

原文（近代文語）

蒙古の英雄盧中将の公館には源日出雄、守高、支那語の通訳王元祺の三名が日夜立籠り、盧の副官温長興、何全英、秦宣、盧重廷の幹部連が日出雄の接待役として、盧の命に依つて懇切なる忠勤振りを発揮してゐた。真澄別や其他の満州浪人連は、水也商会其他を策源地として種々の協議をこらし、張作霖の諒解を求むる事に奔走してゐた。日出雄は日々訪ね来る支那の軍人に対し、通訳を介して神の道を説き、難病に苦しめる人等を救ふてゐた。注意深い盧占魁は稍迷信に深い傾きがあり、日々三回計り一厘銭を六枚掌にのせて素人流の易占をやつて、其吉凶によりて自分の行動を決定するのだから、何事にも緩慢たるを免れなかつた。日本側の同志が首を鳩めて定めておいた事でも、易占が面白くないと彼盧占魁は、一も二もなく否認して採用しない。之には一同も大変に迷惑を感じてゐた。最後の解決は日出雄の断定によるより道はなかつた。

日出雄は支那人の近侍者や、日本側に一々支那服を調べて之を着用せしめ、姓名も支那風に変へてしまつた。日出雄は王文祥、真澄別は王文真、守高は王守高、名田彦は趙徹、岡崎は侯成勲、大倉は石大良、萩原は王敏明、唐国別は王天海、佐々木は王昌輝等と改名し、支那人に化け済まして蒙古入を決行する準備に取掛つた。盧占魁は日出雄が支那服を誂へた時、ソツと被服商の主人に云ひ含め、支那にて有名なる観相学者を呼んで来て古来伝説にある救世主の資格の有無を調べむため、日出雄の骨格や容貌や、目、口、鼻、耳等の形から胸のまはり、手足の長短等から、指の節々、指紋等に至る迄を仔細に調べさせた結果、所謂三十三相を具備した天来の救世主だと云つた観相家の説に、随喜の涙をこぼし、愈々蒙古王国建設の真柱と仰ぐに至つたのである。かかる注意の下に盧占魁が日出雄の身体を調べてゐるに拘らず、日出雄は索倫山の本営に行つて盧占魁が自白するまで、そんな事とは気が付かなかつたのである。観相者は特に日出雄の掌中の四天紋と指頭の皆流紋を見て左の如き断定を下した。

掌中四天紋＝乾為天

大哉乾元万物資始乃統天雲行雨施品物流形大明終始六位時成時、時乗六竜以御天乾道変化各正性命保合太和乃利貞出庶物万国咸寧。

指紋皆流＝坤為地

至哉坤元万物資生乃順承天、坤厚載物徳合旡彊、含弘光大品物亨牡馬地類行地旡彊柔順利貞君子攸行、光迷失道、後順得常西南得明乃与類行東北喪明乃終有慶安貞之吉応地旡彊。

盧占魁は更に日出雄の掌中に現はれたるキリストが十字架上に於ける釘の聖痕や、背に印せるオリオン星座の形をなせる黒子等を見て非常に驚喜した。そして此次第を哥老会の耆宿揚成業や蒙古王の貝勒、貝子鎮国公を初め、張彦三、張桂林、鄒秀明、何全孝、劉陞三、大英子児、賈孟卿等の馬賊の頭目や、張作霖部下の将校連にも之を示し、天来の救世主だ、此救世主を頭に戴いて内外蒙古に活躍すれば成功疑ひなしと、確信してゐたのである。それ故日出雄は蒙古に入つても凡ての上下の人々より、非常な尊敬と信用とを受けたのである。

○

ここに日出雄と盧占魁と張作霖との関係について少し述べる必要がある。張作霖は最近の奉直戦によつて、自分の兵力の足らない事を非常に憂慮してゐた。万々一再び奉直戦が始まらうものなら、軍備の整はない東三省は忽ち敗北の運命に陥り、満州王として東三省に君臨する事の不可能なるを知つてゐた。そこで張作霖は何とかして、自分の勢力を内外蒙古に張つて北京を背面から圧迫し、脅威し、奉直戦の勃発を防がうと内々思つてゐたのである。そこで彼は盧占魁を利用して、内外蒙古に進出せしめ、アワよくば内外蒙古を完全に吾手に入れて見たいと思ふ野心を持つてゐた。今回盧が日出雄と提携して蒙古に大本王国を建設するについても、宗教心の尠い馬賊上りの張作霖は日出雄に対しては、あまり尊敬を払はなかつた。只うまく盧を介して自分の目的のために利用しようと思つたのみである。さうして狡猾な張作霖は盧占魁自身の金にて武器を調達せよと云つて、自分は手ぬらさずに内外蒙古を手に入れようとしたのである。盧占魁も張作霖の遣り方に対しては、内心非常に憤慨し、応援をしてくれた日出雄に対し、張作霖に対する尊敬と帰依とを移して傾注する事となつた。そして日出雄に向つて盧は一切万事その指揮に服従する事を誓つたのである。奉天管内に於ては西北自治軍と云ふ名称を旗印となし、愈索倫山に行つて陣営が整つた上は、内外蒙古救援軍と改称し、日出雄を総大将として大経綸を行ふ計画を以て、索倫に進出する事を企てたのである。

§ 2

While staying at the Kōkuwan in Lu, Hideo composed several hundred more poems. A portion of them:

The road to Akitsushima is far,

Planning the great work of turning the heavens,

With only a few like-minded comrades,

Bearing the teachings of the Lord God upon our heads.

○

One hundred thousand soldiers under my command,

All are men prepared for death,

Their resolve and integrity are firm and strong,

Advancing by obeying the divine command.

○

For the sake of the nation and for the sake of the people, I now set out on a journey of ten thousand leagues across the seas.

Having crossed over to a land where the language is not understood, I have become a mute, as if I were not born with the gift of speech.

The moonlight I saw at the sacred site is particularly clear when viewed in the sky of Hōten.

The moon of Hōten, shining high and clear in the vast sky, is especially bright.

As the night deepens, the moon alone dwells in the vast sky, lodging in the frost as it shines clearly.

Gazing at the clear mirror of the moon, I stand still for a while, reflecting my heart within it.

My friend, while being on Onokorojima, must also be looking up at the same sky of Zuigetsu.

When I look at the moon, my heart becomes more and more courageous, for there is nothing that compares to its pure form.

I think of the full moon shining in the eastern sky as the light of my future path.

Looking up at the vast plain of heaven, I see the same moon hanging there as in Nitsupon.

The moon shining high in the vast eastern sky must be the mirror of my soul.

In the clarity of the moon tonight, I hear the whispering of my soul.

Shall I see the figure of the person I love in the bright and pure moonlight of this evening?

I look up at the mirror of the moon, wishing for the figure of the person I love to be reflected there.

I look at the dark spots within the moon, wondering if the figure of the person I think of is reflected there.
