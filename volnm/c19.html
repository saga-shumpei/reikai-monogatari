Chapter 19: The Temporary Headquarters (Kari Shireibu) [19]

仮司令部〔19〕

§ 1

The dwelling in Mongolia, where cold winds raged and dust rose in clouds, was truly miserable. Having not bathed for twenty days since leaving Taunan, faces were like soot, and nostrils and ears were filled with fine dust. Not only Hideo and his party, but the faces of the Mongolians were filthy and hideous, as they do not bathe throughout the year. Mongolians wash their bodies with water once when they are born, and never wash their bodies with hot water for the rest of their lives. In summer, they might jump into a river once or twice to escape the heat, but they never attempt to wash the grime from their bodies. The way Mongolian women walk is with their arms swinging wide, and when seen from a distance, one cannot distinguish between men and women. One only knows they are women because rings hang from their ears and jewels shine on their heads. Once, when Hideo went outdoors and was looking over the wilderness with a telescope, a black-skinned Mongolian woman of about fifty, wearing a polo, approached from about two cho away. As Hideo was intently peering to see if it were a man, a woman, an old person, or perhaps a beautiful young woman, the woman, unaware that Hideo was watching her through a telescope, suddenly lifted her hem to reveal her private parts, nonchalantly performed her business in the middle of the road, shook her hips two or three times, wiped her parts with the hem of her garment, and walked on in front of Hideo with a calm expression. While Hideo was watching through the telescope, Masumiwake came to his side and requested, 'Please lend me that telescope for a moment.' Hideo laughed and replied, 'No, this is the crucial moment. I am witnessing the private parts of a Mongolian woman; such an opportunity will not come again, so please excuse me.' By the time he said this, the act was finished. Later, Hideo and Matsumura fell onto the grass, laughing aloud.

The Zhenguo Gong of the Gongyefu presented a large slaughtered pig to the great Japanese teacher, and they received the meat. Hideo and his party thanked him for his kindness and immediately boiled it for their meal. However, it appeared that the pigs of the Gongyefu fed on the excrement of syphilis patients, making the pigs themselves syphilitic. Hideo was poisoned by it, and except for his face, itchy warts like blisters broke out all over his body, causing him to suffer as he scratched them day and night. Masumiwake, the second teacher, was also poisoned by the pork, and warts broke out all over his face. In contrast to Hideo, however, he was not affected anywhere below the neck. Because it was so itchy, he scratched it frantically with his nails, which was unbearable; his face immediately swelled up, and clear fluid began to drip like rain. Even though Hideo tried to heal him through chinkon, Masumiwake would scratch his face unconsciously while sleeping, causing his face to become increasingly torn and inflamed, until he looked like a botamochi. Nevertheless, Masumiwake said that everything was the will of God, and he did not mind it, leaving it to nature. It was fortunate that after a month, his face finally returned to its original, clean state. At one point, Lu Zhankui and the Japanese side were very worried, saying that it would never return to its original state.

In the sky above the Gongyefu, hundreds of cranes danced and played, flying across the snowy wilderness and crying out with strange voices. Hideo remarked that such a sight could surely not be seen in Japan, and he celebrated the good fortune of their future. In this area, willow trees, spindle trees, and apricot trees grew in abundance on the mountains and fields. Sparrows chirped, chickens sang long and clear in their voices, and cows mooed, horses neighed, cats meowed, and dogs barked, making sounds just as they do in Japan. Natahiko heard this and laughed, saying, 'Birds and beasts are remarkable things. The Mongolian fellows do not know a single word of Japanese, but the birds and beasts are using Japanese.'

原文（近代文語）

寒風吹き荒ぶ黄塵万丈の蒙古の住居は、実に惨澹たるものであつた。洮南以来二十日間も湯に入らないので、顔は鍋墨の如く、鼻の穴や耳の穴は細かい土埃に埋まつて居る。日出雄一行のみならず、蒙古人の顔は年が年中入浴しないのだから、実に垢まみれになつて醜しい。蒙古人は生れた時一度水で身体を洗つたきり、終身湯水で体を洗ふと云ふ事はない。夏になると暑さ凌ぎに河中に唯の一回位飛び込むこともあるが、決して体の垢を落さうとはしないのである。蒙古の婦人の歩行する様は何れも外輪に大手を振つて歩き、遠方から見てゐると男女の区別が判らない。唯耳に環が下つて居るのと頭に宝石が光つてゐるので、其女たるを知るのみである。或時日出雄が屋外に出て望遠鏡をもつて曠原を望んでゐると、二町許り向ふから五十許りの色の黒い、ポロを下げた蒙古婦人が日出雄の方に向つて進んで来た。日出雄は男か女か老人か、但しは妙齢の美人かと一生懸命にのぞいてゐると、其女は日出雄の望遠鏡でもつて覗いてゐるとは少しも気がつかず、忽ち裾を捲つてウツトコを現し、無造作に道の中央でシエスアンテイナをやりプイプイと二三度尻を振つて、着衣の裾でウツトコをこすり、平然として日出雄の前にやつて来た。日出雄が望遠鏡で覗いてゐる時真澄別が傍へやつて来て、『一寸其望遠鏡を私に貸して下さい』と頼む、日出雄は笑ひ乍ら『ナニ今が肝心要の正念場だ。蒙古婦人のウツトコを実見してゐるのだ、こんな機会は又とないから先づ御免蒙りませう』と云つてゐる間にシエスアンテイナの行事は済んで了つた。後で日出雄と松村は大声を上げて芝草の上に倒れて笑つた。

公爺府の鎮国公から日本の大先生にと云つて、大きな豚を一頭割つて其肉を贈つて来た。日出雄一行は其厚意を謝し、直様之を煮て食膳に上した。所が公爺府の豚は梅毒患者のひつた糞を食つてゐるので豚までが梅毒性になつてゐると見え、日出雄は其毒に当てられて面部を除くの外身体一面に泡のやうな痒疣が発生し、夜も昼もガシガシとかいて苦しんだ。二先生の真澄別も亦豚の毒にあてられ顔一面に疣が発生した。其かはりに日出雄とは反対に首から下はどつこも犯されなかつた。あまり痒いのでガシガシと爪で掻きむしつたから堪らない、忽ち顔面脹れあがり、澄みきつた液汁がボトボトと雨の如くに落ちるやうになつた。さうして日出雄が鎮魂して癒さうとすれども、真澄別は寝てゐる間に知らず識らず顔を掻くので、益々顔が破れただれ、牡丹餅のやうな面相になつて了つた。されど真澄別は何事も神の御心だといつて意に介せず自然に任してゐた。漸く一ケ月の後に元の如く綺麗な顔になつたのは幸である。一時は到底元のものにならないだらうといつて、盧占魁初め日本人側も非常に心配したのである。

公爺府の天空には数百の鶴の群が前後左右に舞ひ遊び、雪の原野を飛び交ひ妙な声を出して鳴き渡つてゐる。到底内地ではこんな事は見られないだらうと云つて、日出雄は其前途の幸運を祝した。此辺は楊柳の木や錦木及び杏の木などが、山や野に沢山生えてゐる。雀はチユンチユンと鳴き、鶏は澄み切つた声でコケコツコーと長く謳ひ、牛はモウモウ、馬はヒンヒン、猫はニヤンニヤン、犬はワンワン、日本並みに声を放つて鳴いてゐる。名田彦はこれを聞いて『鳥、獣は偉いものだ、蒙古の奴は日本語を些とも知らないが、鳥、獣は日本語を使つてゐる』と云つて笑つた。

§ 2

While staying at Konefu, Hideo began the composition of the Kiokubenhafuwamōsakukajiten and created several thousand Waka mixed with the Mōko language. Furthermore, he composed several hundred Jiyutsukuwaika. A portion of these is introduced below.

○

The Mōko people, whose appearance resembles that of the Nichijin, are robust and pleasant to dwell among.

The Mōko people have no choice but to spend their days in winter seclusion.

I feel as if I have met a friend of ten years, and I mingle purely with the Mōko people.

I have spent another day today encountering the filth of cattle, horses, dogs, pigs, and donkeys.

When I look out over the mountains in all directions at the morning departure, each is dressed in thin white silk of snow.

Again today, the cold powder snow flutters down, licking my face as it passes.

When they see a person, three, four, or five gather around, the Mōko dogs barking incessantly.

Hearing the voice of the barking Mōko dogs, I peer out the window at dawn to see cavalry soldiers approaching.

In the dense hut at Konefu, I slept alone, spreading out a torn Rahanteru.

Seeing the Suiha (earrings) worn by the Mōko women, I am reminded of the ancient days of the country of India.

The sun shines across the Mōko road, and the snow on the white mountains and fields has begun to melt.

○

Kōichi Sakamoto came from Hōten, riding in a carriage and carrying a letter. This was to report on the progress of all plans. Sakamoto was a devout believer of the Nichiren sect. He is a man who once entered the priesthood and attempted to propagate the Nichiren sect in Manshū. Sakamoto recognized with his spiritual eye that golden light was radiating from Hideo's body in the dark night, and knowing Hideo's divinity, he suddenly became an Ōmoto believer. Because he knew all the actions of Sasaki, Ōkura, and others, he was treated as a nuisance and was ordered to enter Mōko alone through the mouth of Karakuniwake. Subsequently, Kanekichi Inoue, who had long been active in the Shina, Manshū, and Shiberia regions for certain projects, came from Hōten. This man is a courageous fellow who, at the command of Ro Senkai, risked danger to convey secret instructions to the leaders of the Ka-rō-kai, such as Yō Seigyō, in the Suien and Chōkakō regions. He participated in the Shina revolutionary wars and the war of Bafusafu, making his name famous, and has many acquaintances among the horse bandits of Manshū and Mōko. He came to work as the treasurer in Ro Senkai's temporary headquarters.

From the old In-kun, four fish called Tōrabō, about two shaku in length, caught in the Tōjiga, were sent. With Natahiko's skillful cooking method, everyone smacked their lips and enjoyed them. As it was the first catch of the year, it was presented to Hideo first of all.

On April 13th, a man named Ka Mōkei, a leader of a horse troop, visited Hideo. Although he has two thousand subordinates, he stole away to this place alone to join Ro Senkai's righteous army. He is a man of new ideas, and his logic is extremely clear. He is a tall, robust man, twenty-nine years of age.

On April 15th, Chō Sakurin's adjutant, Chō Kasen, came and visited Hideo's residence, accompanied by Ro Senkai. He is a Shina person who graduated from Waseda University in Tōkyō in the 38th year of Meiji, and because he is good at Japanese, the conversation was very interesting. The former Mōko king Mantakan also visited, and they exchanged pleasant stories about Mōko independence before the two returned to headquarters. When Hideo departed Hōten by automobile, Mr. Yō Kyohō, who had taken care of him in many ways, seen him off, and protected him, came to visit. As they were exchanging various interesting stories, it was seen that dozens of oxcarts and horse-drawn carriages loaded with food, bedding, and the like were coming, guarded by dozens of mounted soldiers in the front and back. Furthermore, there was a report that the ten-shot Mauser rifles and machine guns would finally depart from Tōnan today. Hideo rejoiced, saying that it was truly not a human feat that he had reached the beginning of the formation of an army within only about two months since crossing into Manshū. Day and night, there are mountain fires in the mountains in all directions, and the sight of the clouds burning is truly spectacular. Every night, Hideo goes outdoors, spreads willow branches on the ground, lies down, and while leading with a loud voice, gazes at the bright red moon of Mōko.

日出雄は公爺府滞在中、記憶便法和蒙作歌字典の著作に着手し、数千の蒙古語交りの和歌を作つた。さうして其外に数百の述懐歌を詠んだ。左に其一部を紹介する。

○

奉天から坂本広一が轎車に乗り手紙を持つてやつて来た。それは総ての計画の進行を報告の為である。坂本は熱心な日蓮宗の信者であつた。一時は僧籍に入り満州に日蓮宗の宣伝を企てた男である。坂本は暗夜に日出雄の身体から黄金色の光が放射してゐたのを霊眼で認めて、日出雄の神格を知り、俄に大本信者となつた。彼は佐々木や大倉等の総ての行動を熟知してゐるので邪魔者扱ひをされ、唐国別の口を通じて唯一人入蒙を命ぜられたのである。次で永らく支那、満州、西比利亜方面に或事業の為め活躍して居た井上兼吉が奉天からやつて来た。此男は盧占魁の命によつて、危険を冒し、綏遠や張家口方面に哥老会の楊成業其他馬賊の頭目に密旨を伝へに行つた剛胆な男である。支那の革命戦争等にも加はり、巴布札布の戦争にも参加して其名を轟かし、満州や蒙古の馬賊の頭目に沢山の知己を持つてゐる。彼は盧占魁の仮司令部に入つて金銭出納係を勤める事となつた。

老印君から洮児河で捕獲した、トーラボーと云ふ長さ二尺許りの魚を四尾送つて来た。名田彦の巧妙な料理法で一同は舌鼓を打ち賞玩した。今年に入つて初めての魚獲だから、先づ第一に日出雄に進呈したのであつた。

四月十三日馬隊の頭目賈孟卿と云ふ男が日出雄を訪うた。彼は二千人の部下を有してゐるが、盧占魁の義軍に参加すべく、単身此地迄忍んでやつて来たのである。彼は新しい思想の男で、其論旨も極めて明晰である。背の高い逞しい男で、年齢二十九歳である。

四月十五日張作霖の副官、張華宣がやつて来て盧占魁を伴ひ日出雄の住居を訪うた。彼は支那人で明治三十八年東京の早稲田大学を卒業した男で日本語を良くするので、大変話が面白かつた。元の蒙古王曼陀汗も訪ねて来て蒙古独立の人気の良い話を交換して二人司令部へかへつていつた。日出雄が奉天を自動車で出発の際、何くれと世話をして見送り守つて呉れた楊巨芳氏が来訪し、種々面白き話を交換して居ると、前方原野に当つて牛車、馬車数十台に食糧、寝具等を積み込み、数十人の騎馬の兵卒が前後を守つて来るのが見えた。さうして弥々本日十連発のモーゼル銃や機関銃が洮南を発すると云ふ報告があつた。日出雄が渡満以来僅か二ケ月許りにして軍の編成の端緒を開くに至つたのも全く人間業ではないと云つて喜んだ。日夜四方の山々に山火事があり、雲の焼けてゐる光景は実に壮観である。日出雄は毎夜戸外に出で楊の枝を地上に敷き、横臥して大きな声で音頭を取りながら、蒙古の真赤な月を眺めてゐる。

§ 3

Rauinkun, together with the Shina officer Bahōkiyo, visited Hideo's residence and offered a changed greeting: 'The other day, due to a misunderstanding, I committed an act of Fuyukitodoki. Since I am also going to Sōron to follow the Sōshirei from now on, I ask for your kind favor in all matters. I have come to apologize for my rudeness until now.' Hideo rejoiced, saying that it was truly a good omen that he was accompanied by the Konefu Saikaushiyōkuwan, the Kefuri, and Rauinkun for the Uhijin of the Shingun, and he offered words of gratitude to the Shinmei.

After the Sōshirei Ro arrived at the Konefu, a Kōrei was issued every evening. This was a password to recognize friend and foe in the Anya, and it was for the purpose of military reconnaissance and vigilance.

On the twentieth of April, by divine decree, Hideo and Masumiwake were given the following Mōko names.
