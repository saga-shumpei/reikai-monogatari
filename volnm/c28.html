Chapter 28: The March Commences (Kōgun Kaishi) [28]

行軍開始〔28〕

§ 1

Prior to this, Mitsui and Sasaki had sent a secret messenger from Taunan, reporting that 'Small Shōbazoku gangs claiming the name of Lu are rampant in the vicinity of Taunan, and as a result of the suffering of both officials and the people, unless Lu leaves the Tōsanshiyō region, Chiyōsakurin intends to dispatch a Daidaiteki Taubatsugun.' However, Commander Lu replied nonchalantly,

Lu: 'That is a Keiriyaku I had previously promised to Chiyōtaishi (an honorific for Chiyōsakurin); it is a brilliant Itsukiyoriyōtoku plan to drive the Bazoku within Tōsanshiyō toward Sōron under the pretext of Taubatsu, and for me to gather them all and make them my Buka.'

He spoke thus. However, the ammunition and weapons that were supposed to be transported from the rear did not arrive, and there was a situation where no news had reached him from Sasaki, Ōkura, Yang Chongshan, and others who had returned to Fengtian for necessary business, so perhaps feeling somewhat anxious, Lu led several of his trusted Buka on horseback on June 2nd, visited the temporary palace of Hinodewake at Kamikokyokushi, and a secret consultation was held there. It was the custom that secret matters or particularly urgent issues were always resolved through written communication between Masumiwake, who received the will of Hinodewake, and Lu Zhankui, so naturally, the issue was discussed between the two by written communication on this day as well, but the author has reason to infer that the main points were roughly the following exchange.

Masumiwake: 'Is there any point in staying stationed here forever? Chiyōsakurin will never support you as much as you think. Rather than that, why not formulate a plan for independent development and first advance to the sacred ground of Kōanrei, where it is said that Minamoto no Yoshitsune first unfurled the imperial banner? You said there were seven thousand Red Army troops in Kōanrei, but when I look with my spiritual eye, there seem to be four or five hundred horses, but fewer than two hundred people. If you just provide me with an interpreter, I will go ahead alone and show you that I can reach a splendid compromise.'

Lu: 'Alas, I am truly sorry for the delay. I was waiting because I thought I might be able to manage, as I am short on rifles, but I will make up my mind now. However, that region of Kōanrei has no food, and if this many people move in, we will immediately be in trouble for supplies. Rather than that, I will head in the direction of the large units that will join us from the Suiyuan and Chahar regions to join them quickly, make our supplies sufficiently abundant, and then head to Outer Mongolia. In any case, I will make arrangements to depart within two or three days...'

At such a moment, Adjutant Ma, who had been left in charge at Shimokokyokushi, changed his expression, came flying on his horse at full speed, and reported something in a flustered manner. Upon hearing this, Lu stood up with a look of determination on his face, requested Hinodewake and his party to prepare for departure, and immediately hurried back toward the headquarters.

At a point about thirty Chinese miles southeast of the headquarters, the great leader of the Manchurian Bazoku, Daieishiji, was stationed with some sixty of his own troops as a rearguard. On this day, just as Daieishiji had gone to the headquarters and was absent, while his Buka were relaxing and indulging in dreams of a nap, about three hundred government soldiers from Taunan suddenly attacked. Although Daieishiji's Buka were few in number, since they were all elite soldiers, each capable of matching a thousand, they immediately took up their guns while still naked and fought back, not letting the hundreds of government soldiers approach even a step, while simultaneously reporting the emergency to the headquarters. At the headquarters, the war and anti-war factions were in conflict and the discussion could not be easily settled, so Daieishiji rode his horse alone back to his own camp, gathered his Buka, and withdrew to the headquarters with composure. Perhaps the government soldiers were so struck by his agility and boldness that they did not dare to pursue him.

General Okazaki, who was waiting in a room at the headquarters, was angered by the spinelessness of the staff officers and said,

Okazaki: 'Staying with such people makes me worry for the safety of Sensei.'

原文（近代文語）

これより曩、洮南より三井及び佐々木が密使を遣はし『洮南附近盧の名を騙る小馬賊の横行甚しく、官民共に困苦の結果、張作霖よりも此際盧が東三省圏外に出でざる限り、大々的に討伐軍を差向くべし』と報じて来たが、盧司令は事もなげに、

『それは自分が予て張大師（張作霖の敬称）と約束した計略で、東三省内の馬賊を討伐の名に於て索倫へ向け追ひ放ち、自分は之を全部糾合して部下となすべき、一挙両得の妙案なのだ』

と云つてゐた。然るに爾後引続き後方より輸送せらるべき筈の弾薬武器は来らず、又所要のため帰奉した佐々木、大倉、楊崇山等より何等の消息も到達しないといふ情況なので、盧も稍不安を感じたのか、六月二日腹心の部下数騎を率ゐて、上木局子なる日出雄の仮殿を訪づれ、茲に密議は凝らされた。秘密事項又は特に緊要なる問題は、何時も日出雄の意を受けたる真澄別と盧占魁と筆談にて解決するのが例であつたから、無論此日も筆談に依つて両者間に問題が議せられたのであるが、其要点は凡そ左の如き問答であつたと、著者は推断すべき理由を有つてゐる。

真澄別『何時迄も、此処に駐屯して居た所で仕方がないぢやありませぬか。張作霖は貴方の思ふてる程、貴方を決して後援しませぬよ。それよりも独立開発の計を立て、先づ源義経が初めて王旗を飜したと伝へられてる興安嶺の聖地迄進軍したら何うですか、興安嶺には七千の赤軍が居ると貴方は謂はれましたが、霊眼で見ると、乗馬は四五百頭ある様だけれど、人は二百足らずですよ。通訳官さへ付けて呉れれば、私一人先発して立派に妥協して見せますがなア』

盧『イヤ、誠に遅延して済みませぬ。長銃が不足だものですから、あれでもと思ふて待つて居ましたが、モウ決心致します。併し興安嶺のあの地帯は食料がなく、これ丈の人数が繰込んでは忽ち物資に困ります。それよりも先づ綏遠、察哈爾地方より当方へ参加する大部隊に早く合する様、其方向に向ひ、充分物資を豊富にして、それから外蒙へ向ひませう。兎に角二三日の内には出発する様に取計らひますから……』

斯る折柄、下木局子に留守居をして居た馬副官は顔色を変へ、全速力で馬を飛ばしてやつて来て、何事か慌しく報告した。これを聴くと盧は決心の色を面に浮べて立上り、日出雄一行に出発の準備を請ひおき、直ちに司令部指して急ぎ帰つた。

司令部の東南方約三十支里の地点に殿として、満州馬賊の大頭目大英子児が手兵の一部六十余騎を率ゐて駐屯して居たが、此日恰も大英子児は司令部へ出頭し不在中部下の者共は、寛いで昼寝の夢を貪つてゐる最中、洮南の官兵約三百余騎が突然襲撃したのである。大英子児の部下は少数なりと雖も、皆一騎当千の粒揃ひの事なれば、直ちに裸の儘銃を取つて応戦し、数百の官兵を一歩も寄せつけず、一方急を司令部に報じた。司令部にては戦非戦両派対立して議容易に纏らずといふ有様なので、大英子児は単身馬を飛ばして、自分の屯営に立帰り、部下を引纏め悠々として司令部迄引揚げた。其敏活さ、豪胆さに官兵は肝を奪はれてか、敢て追撃もしなかつたのである。

此時司令部の一室に控へて居た岡崎将軍は参謀連の不甲斐なきを怒り、

『こんな連中と一緒に居ては先生の御身が案ぜられる』

§ 2

He gathered the Hiyōrau that was close at hand, requisitioned several Giushiya, loaded them, and hurried toward the Karidono of Kamimokukiyokushi. Just as he was passing by, Commander Lu returned to the Shireibu; whether he believed the words of the Sanbō Yōsuitei that 'the Tōbatsutai has come to attack Taainzuru,' or whether he understood that fighting against the troops of the Tōsanshiyō would endanger his own position, he issued orders to move toward the Seihoku while discussions were in full swing. He decided to entrust the disposal of the Hiyōrau, which could not be loaded due to a shortage of wheels, to the Mokukiyokushiyo of Kokuriukō, and set out toward the Seihoku.

By the time the curtain of night had fallen completely, the surroundings of Hideo's Karidono at Kamimokukiyokushi were surrounded on all sides by the soldiers who had retreated from Shimomokukiyokushi. With the neighing of horses, the howling of dogs, and the flames of the bonfires, the heaven and earth of Kamimokukiyokushi, which had been silent until now, were suddenly filled with a murderous atmosphere.

Inside the Karidono, Lu and several other executives continued to explain the actions regarding their future course to Hideo, Masumiwake, and Okazaki until late into the night, but in the end, the Japanese side, being unfamiliar with the geography, decided to leave the route entirely to Lu. Just then, a single gunshot and a cry of agony were heard in a corner of the darkness. A pitiful soldier had been shot to death on the spot for resisting his superior officer.

In the midst of this, it became three o'clock in the morning on the following day, June 3rd. The unit of Regiment Commander Zou became the vanguard, Lu himself took on the duty of guarding Hideo, Mandakan took the rear, Taainzuru patrolled the entire force, and Zhang Yansan supervised the Giushiya unit; each was assigned their role, and they began their march toward the sacred ground of the Seihoku Khingan Mountains. There was no established road, and they simply advanced by sewing through the vast plateau dotted with trees, using the flow of the Taoer River as a guide. When they found a large boxwood tree on the way, Sakamoto pointed at it from his horse and sighed, 'Second Sensei, if we had a few boxwood trees like this, we could make many Chikuzen biwa,' and the fact that countless such trees were standing there was particularly rare for the Japanese group.

Around dusk that day, they decided to follow the dream of camping in the open, judging the forest shadows on the riverbank of the upper reaches of the Taoer to be the campsite for Hideo's party.

Mandakan explained, 'Once we leave Kamimokukiyokushi, we will not see any human dwellings, let alone livestock, for some time.' To prevent attacks from wolves and other wild beasts, the red smoke of the bonfires rising here and there illuminated the forest so brightly that it seemed as if it would scorch the heavens. Around midnight, Taainzuru secretly visited Hideo, and through Okazaki, he swore, 'I will protect you all to the very end.' He repeatedly accepted the dried squid offered by Hideo's own hand, and while showing his dissatisfaction on his face by saying, 'If only our languages could communicate...', he left with a smile. However, he was not pleased with Lu's handling of the situation at Shimomokukiyokushi the previous day, and having a secret intention, he escaped that night and disappeared somewhere along with his subordinates. As it turned out, he is now said to have established a base in the deep mountains of Rehe, organized a Red Hat Army with three thousand elite soldiers, and is holding a grand position, saying he will fight a memorial battle for Hideo, waiting for the return of Hideo and Masumiwake. At the same time as Taainzuru's withdrawal, the brave warriors who did not consider the non-war theory to be honorable disappeared one after another, so when they departed the next morning, the count was five hundred cavalrymen and over three hundred who were unable to secure horses and were riding on the Giushiya.

原文（近代文語）

とて手近にあつた兵糧を取纏め、牛車数台を徴発して之を積載し、急ぎ上木局子の仮殿に向つた。之と入れ違ひに、盧司令は司令部に帰つて来たが、彼は参謀揚萃廷の『討伐隊は大英子児を撃ちに来たものだ』との言を信じたものか、或は東三省の兵と戦ふのは自分で自分の立場を危くするものと解したか、議論百出の間に西北へ向つて移動の命令を下し、車輪不足の為積載出来ぬ兵糧などは、黒竜江の木局署へ処分を委託し、西北指して行動を起す事とした。

○

夜の帳がスツポリと卸された頃、上木局子なる日出雄の仮殿の周囲は、下木局子を徹退した軍兵を以て幾重にも取巻かれ、馬の嘶、犬の遠吠、篝火の焔、今迄静寂なりし上木局子の天地は俄かに殺気が漲つた。

仮殿内にては盧以下数名の幹部が日出雄、真澄別、岡崎に向ひ前途に関する行動に就き説明を重ねつつ夜を更かしてゐたが、結局地理不案内なる日本人側は進路を盧に一任する事となつた。折柄暗の一遇に銃声一発と、断末魔の声が聞えた。哀れなる一兵卒は上官に反抗せるの故を以て、即座に銃殺されたのであつた。

兎角する内翌六月三日午前三時となつた。鄒団長の部隊、先鋒となり、盧は自ら日出雄護衛の任に当り、曼陀汗は殿り、大英子児は全隊の見廻り、張彦三は牛車隊の監督など、夫れ夫れ役割を定め、西北興安嶺の聖地を指して行軍を開始する事となつた。道路とて別に定まつたものはなく、唯樹木点綴せる大高原を洮児の流を標準に縫うて進むのである。途中黄楊の大木があると、坂本は馬上より指して、

『二先生、こんな黄楊一本あれば、築前琵琶が幾つも出来ますなア』

と歎声を漏らす程のが数知れず樹立してゐるのは、特に日本人連中には珍らしかつた。

此日暮近き頃、洮児の上流、河畔の森影を日出雄一行の陣営と見計らひ、露営の夢を辿る事とした。

『モウ上木局子を離れては当分人家は素より家畜も見られない』

と曼陀汗が説明する。狼其他猛獣の襲来を防ぐ為とて、所々に揚る焚火の紅煙は天を焦さむ許り森を真赤に照して居た。此夜半頃大英子児は窃かに日出雄を訪問し、岡崎を介して『私はどこ迄も貴方方を御保護申上ます』と誓ひ、日出雄の手づから与ふる鯣を再三推し戴き『言語さへ通ずれば……』てふ物足らぬ心を面に現はしつつも、ニコニコとして辞し去つたが、彼は前日下木局子に於ける盧の処置を快しとせず、窃かに期する所あつて、此夜脱出し、部下諸共何れへか姿を消して了つた。果せるかな彼は今日熱河の奥地に本拠を構へ、已に三千の精兵を引具して紅帽軍を組織し、日出雄の弔合戦をするのだ……と堂々の陣を張り、日出雄、真澄別の再渡来を待つてゐるさうである。大英子児脱退と同時に、予て非戦論を潔しとせざる勇士は続々として姿を隠して了つたので、翌朝出立の際は、騎馬兵五百騎、馬整はずして牛車に便乗せるもの三百有余と算せられた。
