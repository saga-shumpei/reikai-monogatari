Chapter 30: Ominous Signs in the Cavern (Gankutsu no Kichō) [30]

岩窟の奇兆〔30〕

§ 1

During the two or three months corresponding to the summer season, the nights in the remote regions of Mongolia are extremely short, just as they are in the Siberian territories. No sooner does the remnant of the sunset fade in the northwestern sky than the eastern horizon begins to glow with the dawn. Even on moonless nights, one can travel safely along dangerous mountain paths from past two o'clock in the morning. The entire army, which had taken shelter from what Chiyōkensan called the divine punishment of rain under the shadow of the rocks, finally felt a sense of relief as the rain subsided, and at approximately two-thirty in the morning on the seventh day of the sixth month (the sixth day of the fifth month by the lunar calendar), the order to depart was conveyed to the entire force.

Traveling by horseback is one thing, but with ox-carts and mules (a hybrid of horse and donkey, possessing the greatest pulling power) hitched in pairs or threes, and sedan carriages hitched in threes or fours, all heavily laden and pushing recklessly through difficult, rocky, and uneven terrain—whether mountain or river—those who hitched a ride could hardly set their minds at ease. One must endure striking one's head, striking one's elbows, and at times even pay the price of falling off... As the warning of Ōmoto goes, 'One cannot cross the Golden Bridge with baggage,' one cannot help but feel that the path of life is indeed best traveled lightly.

On this day, they crossed the rocky mountains and gradually advanced through the plateau region, weaving between the mountains. The sky gradually cleared, and the brilliant sun shone down upon the entire army, still dressed in their winter uniforms. Yet, no matter how far they traveled, although there was pasture, not a single drop of standing water could be found. Their carried rations were already running low. Naturally, no human dwellings were to be found, and the sound of sighs saying 'Ah' could be heard from somewhere or other. Some spurred their horses in search of water, while others waited for the food wagons, and the troop eventually became scattered in groups of three or five. At this time, seven men—Masumiwake, Moritaka, Sakamoto, Hakuryōkaku, Onchōkō, Ōsanjō, and Kōkokuhō—were riding abreast at the side of Hideo.

Sakamoto, unable to bear it any longer, said:

Sakamoto: 'Sensei, it seems everyone else has already gone ahead, but since the sedan carriage carrying your luggage and food supplies is still lagging behind, why don't we take a rest somewhere around here? Both men and horses will be completely exhausted at this rate.'

Hideo: 'That is true. Well then, there seems to be quite a bit of pasture here, so let us take a break.'

Sakamoto: 'Sensei, I could endure a little longer, but I feel sorry for Fuji-chan.'

Fuji was the name of Sakamoto's horse; indeed, when traveling in regions where transportation facilities are inadequate, a horse becomes one's only friend, and the horse also yearns for its rider. It seems that if only this affection could be maintained between humans, quarrels would never even occur in one's dreams. Hideo's horse was named Hakukinryū, Masumiwake's horse was Hakuginryū, and Moritaka's horse was Kongō, all of which were white horses. The horses were unsaddled and released into the pasture, while the men, sharing the cigarettes remaining in their pockets, lay down on the green grass, blowing purple smoke toward the heavens, and waited for the sedan carriage while engaging in their usual exchange of sharp-tongued banter. However, the sedan carriage did not catch up, perhaps due to some trouble. When they asked the soldiers who came along late, they said, 'It is still quite a distance behind.' Hideo said, 'Nonsense, there is no reason why humans cannot eat what cows and horses eat,' and began pulling up the grass there and eating it, saying how delicious it was. The people accompanying him also began to munch away, saying, 'Indeed, that is true.'

Sakamoto: 'However, Ro Senkai is an outrageous fellow. Without giving any answer to Sensei, he put on the airs of a commander and went ahead in the sedan carriage. Even though he said, "I will personally attend to the guard, so please do not go to the side of others..."'

Moritaka: 'There is also talk that a power struggle is occurring between Ryū Shōsan and Ro Senkai, with Sensei at the center of it, you know.'

夏期に相当する二三ケ月の間は、蒙古奥地は西比利亜方面と同じく夜が非常に短い。西北の空に夕焼の名残が消えたかと思ふと、間もなく早や東天紅を潮すると云つた調子である。月なき夜でも午前二時過ぎる頃から、危険な山路でも安全に旅行が出来るのである。張彦三の所謂神譴の雨を岩影に避けた全軍も、其中雨が小歇みになつたのでヤツと胸を撫で下し、六月七日（陰暦五月六日）午前二時半頃全軍に出発命令が伝はつた。騎馬にての旅行は兎も角、二頭或は三頭立の牛車や騾馬と（馬と驢馬との混血にて牽引力最も強き種類）三四頭立の轎車が、山と云はず川と云はず岩石崎嶇たる難路を、相当の重量を積んで無茶苦茶に進み行くのだから、便乗した人は中々安き心もなかつた。頭を打ち、肱を打ち、時には転落の犠牲も払はねばならぬと云ふのだから……荷物があつては黄金の大橋は渡れんぞよ……といふ大本の警告の如く、人生の行路はヤハリ身軽に限るてふ感を禁ずるを得ない。此日岩山を乗り切つて次第次第に高原地帯を、山と山との間を縫ふて進んでゆく。空は漸く晴れて赫々たる太陽は冬服その儘の全軍を照しつける。而も行けども行けども牧草はあつても、一滴の溜水も見付からない。携帯の食糧は已に残り少くなつてゐる。無論人家は見付からず『アーア』と云ふ歎息の声が何処からともなく聞えて来る。水を探ねて馬を急がす者、食糧車を待ち合す者、隊は遂に三々五々となつた。此時日出雄の側には真澄別、守高、坂本、白凌閣、温長興、王瓚璋、康国宝の七人が轡を列ねて居た。坂本は堪へかねて、

坂本『先生皆先へ行つて了つた様ですけれども、先生のお荷物や食糧品を積んだ轎車はまだ遅れてますから、どつかそこらで一服したらどうでせう。人も馬もこれではヘトヘトになつて了ひますよ』

日出雄『さうだね、では此処は可なり牧草もある様だから一休みしやう』

坂本『先生、私は今少し位辛抱も致しませうが、富士ちやんが可愛相です』

富士と云ふのは坂本の乗馬の名で、実際交通機関不備の地方を旅行すると馬が唯一の友であり、馬亦騎乗者を慕ひ、人間同士に此情愛が保てさへすれば、喧嘩など夢にも起らないであらうと思はれる位だ。而して日出雄の馬は白金竜、真澄別の馬は白銀竜、守高の馬は金剛と命名され皆白馬であつた。馬は鞍を外されて牧草の間に放たれ、人はポケツトに残つた煙草を譲り合ひつつ青草の上に寝ころび、紫の煙りを天に向つて吹き出し乍ら、相変らず減らず口の叩合をして轎車を待つてゐる。併し轎車は何等か故障の起つたものか、中々追ひついて来ない。遅れ来る兵士に訊いても『まだまだ大分後方だ』と云ふ。日出雄は『ナアニ牛や馬の喰ふ物が人間に喰へない筈はない』とて、其処等の草を引抜いては美味い美味いと喰べ初める。附添ふ人々も『なる程そらさうだ』とムシヤリムシヤリとやり出した。

坂本『併し盧占魁は怪しからぬ奴ですな、先生に何の答もなしで自分が大将面をして轎車に乗つて先へ行つて了ひよつた。自分が護衛を直接に申し上げるから、外の者の側へ御越しにならぬ様になんて云つておき乍ら……』

守高『何でも劉陞三と盧占魁との間に、先生を中心として勢力争ひが起つてるといふ評判もあるがね』

Sakamoto: 'If that is the case, is it not all the more reason that you should not leave the side of Sensei?'

Masumiwake: 'Well, setting that aside, in any case, there may be a place with a little more water, so let us proceed a little further. The kyōshiya will be coming along in the meantime as well.'

Hideo: 'That would be best.'

He became the person on horseback once again, and continued the journey while singing sendenka and detarameuta. The sun shone down more and more, and the wataire hadagi grew increasingly hot. As it was a region with little rain and dry kūki, he did not sweat much, but his thirst was intense. For some reason, on this day of all days, he could not even find a puddle of horse shōben, and having no other shiyudan to take, he let the horse decide the yukute as they advanced, eventually reaching the foot of an ihayama that looked as if it were composed of piled-up kigan. It was already past gogo goji. In a part of the aonohara at the foot surrounding the ihayama, there was a place where the tochi had collapsed by about itsuken, and perhaps due to the thawing of the underground layer, jet-black water was welling up and spilling over. When he brought the horse close to the bank to look, the horse, delighted, vied to be the first to drink greedily. Then, for some reason, Hideo shouted loudly, 'I shall not move from here anymore,' and as soon as he thought that, his figure was no longer visible, and the horse was grazing on grass with an indifferent expression. Sakamoto immediately dismounted and wandered about searching, and soon ran over to Masumiwake, saying,

Sakamoto: 'Sensei is in a gankutsu on the side of that mountain, sitting in silent meditation with his eyes closed. What should we do?'

Masumiwake went to the gankutsu immediately with Moritaka, and upon looking, saw that Hideo had become possessed by a spirit. Masumiwake understood his intent, and said,

Masumi: 'Moritaka-san, the current route seems different from what we had in mind, and there are quite a few suspicious points, so shall we not make this place our base for a while?'

Moritaka: 'Indeed, I agree as well; this place resembles the gankutsu of Takakumayama, and this cannot be an ordinary matter.'

The party decided to camp here for the time being, and sent someone to chase after Lu Zhankui to have Wang Zanzhang report this matter. The two kyōshiya carrying Hideo's luggage, namely the garments of Seiwōbo, the senden-shi robes, other personal effects, as well as the remaining provisions, arrived here about an hour and a half late. These two kyōshiya had been supervised by Yamada Bunjirō, who had loaded military supplies and the like from Taonan to come to Solun, and had accompanied them, fearing the danger of the return journey. Materials were taken out from the kyōshiya and preparations for camping were begun; meanwhile, the jet-black water that had quenched the horses' thirst was purified for drinking by using alum. They gathered branches of withered trees to boil it, and it was Sakamoto who made a fuss, saying, 'When I put tea in it, it turned into black ink, so this must be a mineral spring rich in iron.'

As the sun gradually set in the west and stars began to shine in the sky, Zhang Yansan's unit arrived as the rearguard, heard the circumstances from Masumiwake, and said,

'Then I shall take Lu's place and provide protection for you. I still have some rice and beef on my side; as long as Sensei does not move, I shall also remain by your side forever to provide protection.'

He then ordered his subordinates to begin cooking, and settled down completely. Before long, Hideo also emerged from the gankutsu, and a lively open-air dining hall was opened.

At this time, Wang Zanzhang came galloping back, reporting that Lu and the entire unit were camping about fifty zhili ahead, that there were four or five houses there but a lack of drinking water, and that although Lu had hurried in search of water, he would come to meet them as soon as the unit was reorganized. Masumiwake further wrote something on the back of a business card for Okazaki and Hagiwara, and sent Wen Changxing as a messenger to head toward the forward encampment, urging his horse on, and thus everyone went to sleep.

坂本『それなら尚更先生のお側を離れなきや可いぢやありませぬか』

真澄別『マアそれはそれとして兎に角、も少し位水のある場所がないとも限らぬから、モウ一息進みませう。其間轎車も参りませうから』

日出雄『それが宜からう』

と再び鞍上の人となり、宣伝歌やら出鱈目歌を唄ひ乍ら行を続けた。日は益々照り渡り綿入の肌着は愈々熱して来る。雨少なく空気が乾燥してゐる地方だから余り汗は出ないが、喉の渇く事夥しい。何うしたものか此日に限つて水らしい物は馬の小便の溜すら見付からぬ、さりとて他に取るべき手段もない、行路を馬に任せつつ進むうち、奇岩を折り重ねた如うな岩山の麓に達した。時既に午後五時を過ぐる頃であつた。岩山を取り巻く麓の青野原の一部に、土地の一間許り陥落した場所があり、地下層解氷の為か真黒い水が湧きこぼれてゐる。馬を其畔に近付けて見ると、馬は喜び先を争うてガブガブと呑み出した。すると如何にしけん日出雄は『俺はモウ此処から動かぬのだ』と大喝したかと思へば、もう其姿は見えず、其馬は素知らぬ面で草を食むでゐる。坂本は早速下馬してウロウロと捜し廻り、軈て走せ来つて真澄別に向ひ、

坂本『先生は彼の山の腹に岩窟がありますが、其中に瞑目静坐してゐられます。何うしたら可いでせう』

真澄別は守高と共に直ちに岩窟に到り見れば、日出雄は神懸となつてゐる。真澄別はその意を悟り、

真澄『守高さん、今の進路は吾々の想うて居るのと違ふ様だし、大分怪しい点もあるから、暫く此処を根城とする事にしようぢやないか』

守高『さうだ、僕も賛成だ、此処は高熊山の岩窟に能く似てもゐるし、尋常事ぢやなからう』

一行は此処に当分宿営の決心を定め、王瓚璋をして此事を報告せしむべく盧占魁の後を追はしめた。日出雄の荷物即ち西王母の服、宣伝師服その他手廻り品並に食糧の残品を積んだ二台の轎車は、約一時間半遅れて此処に到着した。此二台の轎車は山田文次郎が便乗監督し、洮南より軍需品等を積載して索倫に来り、其儘帰途の危険を慮つて随伴したのである。轎車より材料を取出して野営の準備に着手される、一方、馬の渇を医やした真黒の水は、明礬を利用して飲料用に浄化せられる。枯木の枝を集めて之を沸かす、『茶を入れたら黒インキになつたから、こら鉄鉱泉ですよ』と騒ぎ立てるのは坂本である。

日は漸く西に臼搗き空に星の輝き初むる頃、張彦三の部隊が殿りとして到着し来り、真澄別より事情を聴取り、

『それでは私が盧に代つて御保護申上げます、私の方には未だ米も牛肉も幾らかあります、先生がお動きにならねば、私も何時迄もお側に止まつて御保護いたします』

とて部下に命じて炊き出しを開始し、スツカリ腰を据ゑて了つた。やがて日出雄も岩窟より出で来り、賑やかな野天食堂が開かれた。

此時王瓚璋馳せ帰り、盧以下全部隊は約五十支里前方に屯営し居り、其処には人家四五軒あれど飲料水の不足なる事や、盧は水を索めて急いだのであるが部隊整理次第直ぐ迎ひに来る事など報告した。真澄別は更に岡崎と萩原に対し何事か名刺の裏に認め、温長興を使として前方の駐屯所に向ひ馬を急がしめ、茲に一同寝に就くこととなつた。
