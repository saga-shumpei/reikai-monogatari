Chapter 15: 愚恋 [1401]

愚恋〔1401〕

§ 1

Under the clear yet unsettled sky of late autumn, a man came trudging along, his lower garments whipped by the cold wind.

This is the Yachimata barrier station of Bulgario.

As usual, two guards, one clad in white and the other in red, stood sternly at the gate.

A certain man casually attempted to pass through this gate.

The red guard stopped him by calling out,

Red: 'Traveller, wait a moment!'

The man stopped in his tracks and said,

Man: 'Yes, do you have some business with me?'

Red: 'Who are you? And state your name.'

Man: 'Yes, I am an automobile driver and my name is Rokusuke.'

Red: 'You must be the paramour of the famous countess, Ashikahakamako.'

Rokusuke: 'That is so, but what of it? There is surely no reason for me to be blamed by you gentlemen.'

Red: 'What do you take this place to be?'

Rokusuke: 'It is neither the phenomenal world nor the spiritual world; I feel as though I have died, and yet as though I have not died—in short, I am wandering about in utter bewilderment. However, since I heard a strange song along this way, I think I perhaps have not died after all.'

Red: 'What kind of song did you hear? Recite it here.'

Rokusuke: 'Yes, I beg you not to laugh. I do not remember it distinctly, but according to that song, it seems somehow that I have died. And I have a feeling that my beloved Kamako remains behind.'

Red: 'I am not asking about your beloved; I told you to let me hear the song.'

Rokusuke: 'Yes, it goes roughly as follows to begin with:

Kamako whose life is claimed,

And Rokusuke, the blissfully ignorant,

Who let him die a dog's death, whose sin is that?

A foolish parent, a foolish child, and a foolish husband

Such was the song.'

Red: 'It must be "a foolish parent, a foolish child, and a foolish Rokusuke," mustn't it? If such a thing really happened, you are a fellow who would throw away his life and do good-for-nothing things.'

Rokusuke: 'Since I am Rokusuke anyway, I would hardly do anything worthwhile. But there is yet another striking piece.

Even if you need not die, you die,

While I, who must die, cannot die.

Ah, the floating world is never as one wishes.

How might this suffering escape me?

Lord Rokusuke surely, doubtless

Upon the lotus flower pedestal,

Dividing the half-seat as I go,

Will surely be waiting for me,

While a hateful husband tends to me.

Such a song could be heard all along the way. When Rokusuke and Kamako are mentioned, I take it to mean myself for the most part.'

Red: 'Do you feel no responsibility at all for doing such a thing to your master's daughter?'

Rokusuke: 'I do not consider it a bad thing at all. It was upon mutual consent, furthermore, passionate emotional waves were sent from the woman's side, and she poured showers of love upon me like the infant Shakyamuni, so much so that it was not even a matter of... hehehehehe, we simply became a happily joined pair. However, since contemporary times are relatively full of fools,... complaining that it is outrageous for an automobile driver to form a love affair with a countess... they raise a jealous fuss over nothing. Therefore, seeking a second world all the more, with the two of us getting along well to live an ideal life, we committed a double suicide, but somehow Kamako seems to have remained behind. Say, about what time will Kamako arrive here? I am sorry to trouble you, but would you mind flipping through the register of the dead for a moment?'

Red: 'Do you not feel ashamed before the world?'

Rokusuke: 'I do not feel ashamed at all; I consider that there is no greater honor than this for a man. Just think about it carefully. On the one hand, there are oblivious fellows, arrogant and insolent wretches, who brandish artificial ranks such as nobility, lineage, and wealth, and throw their weight around. On the other hand, there are foolish and deluded humans who desperately respect a high society akin to thieves, never receiving a single coin of assistance, and—like rice-pounding sparrows—bow their heads, bend their waists, wag their tails, and go to all lengths with servile flattery. To such people, this may well serve as a kind of stimulus, a waking agent, and an exhilarating tonic. We are victims who have demonstrated the specimen that male-female relationships can never be swayed by influential gates, lineage, property, status, or old morals, and so the people of the world... must be praising this Rokusuke as a man among men, the archetype of a real man, the defender of love is best, and a warning against the nobility. Through Rokusuke's actions this time, surely the phantoms of society have also opened their eyes a little. Nobles, commoners, and drivers are the same human beings. Our thoughts and concepts can surely never be different. Therefore, I believe I have become a light in a dark society.'

§ 2

Aka: 'My, what a grand and fierce demeanor! It seems education in the mortal world has truly become democratized, doesn't it?'

Rokusuke: 'I have become a sacrifice for the sake of the men and women of the world suffering from romantic love. If she were a daughter of the plebeian class, a little bit of freedom would be allowed, but when it comes to a daughter of the upper class, oh, it is truly, truly a tragic thing. I am a great philanthropist who has carved open the path of heaven hitherto closed for the sake of the upper-class daughters.'

Aka: 'Anyway, leaving aside your sophistry, that action of yours attempting a double suicide with your master's daughter is something I cannot forgive. First, I am sorry, but you must go to the hell of the path of lust.'

Rokusuke: 'To be driven into an unavoidable predicament and to bring about even a lover's suicide pact was executed because I could no longer endure the so-called coercion and tyrannical oppression of society, and therefore I would like you to understand that much, you know.'

Aka: 'At any rate, it would be best to state your circumstances at the judgment court of Lord Ibukidozu. Now, pass through to the inner chambers, you disturber of social morality.'

As he said this, with a smack he slapped Rokusuke's backside and shoved him inside the gate. Rokusuke came to a sudden halt inside the gate, rolling his eyes about, lowering his voice, and speaking:

Rokusuke: 'My, my, no matter where one goes, how many dense fools there are! Even the guards of the crossroads are consumed with envy and jealousy over our romance, and are making a fuss. Heh, shall I expound fluent and fair arguments at the judgment court from now on, and completely clear the air of the judgment court?'

Saying this, he held one shoulder high, dropped the other shoulder, tucked his hands into his pockets, and advanced at a leisurely, slouching pace.

A woman with white powder thickly applied to her face, tall in stature, looking to be about twenty-four or five with a slight raw edge to her, came walking timidly. Aka, speaking to himself,

Aka: 'Ha-ha, this fellow must be that amoroso of Rokusuke who just went in. She seems to be a spirit whose physical body is still in the manifest world, lacking vigor from who knows where.'

murmured to himself, waiting for her to approach. The moment the woman tried to step over the threshold of the open gate, the white guard spread his arms wide and said,

Shiro: 'Pardon me, young lady, please wait a moment. You are not someone who should come here.'

Woman: 'I am one who has come following the footsteps of my amant. Please do not say such mean things, and let me pass through here.'

Aka: 'Hey there, woman, what is your name?'

Woman: 'Yes, I am called Kamako.'

Aka: 'Oom, if that is the case, you are the daughter of Count Akagawa Fuhenshō, aren't you?'

Woman: 'Yes, just as you surmise, I am a countess.'

Aka: 'Though born into a noble family, you did not consider the proprieties of the world, nor did you reflect upon the family name of your ancestors, but had illicit relations with your servant Rokusuke, an unprincipled woman who disordered morality.'

Kamako: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, in such an open world as this, how old-fashioned your mindset is! Please remove the cork from your head and infuse some new air into it.'

Aka: 'Hey, you outrageous, audacious, and hardened rogue. You, while having a husband, indulged in the pleasures of wrongdoing, disordered the home, and are a great criminal who stained the honor of upper-class society.'

Kamako: 'Heh, is my illicit intercourse with Rokusuke-sama such a great crime? Just look at the world today. Every single woman who is called a lady buys actors or arranges lovers, and that is why things called evening parties are created. Since women are the flowers of high society, butterflies will surely alight upon flowers. If a woman in today's world is like one who cannot manage to have even a single lover, she can by no means be called a lady. Open your eyes wide and observe the backside of society well. Someone like me is merely the appearance of a single grain among the sands of the Ganges. If such things are crimes, then today's society is entirely a society of crime. I have appeared as a sacrifice in order to fundamentally reform the oppressive social system centered on men and to create a society of pitiful, oppressed women. Just look at the daily newspapers. Do they not report adultery cases or double suicide pacts usually three or five times, and sometimes even around ten times when there are many? The worldly events that appear in newspapers are limited to a mere fraction of the whole. Thinking from that, who knows how many sorrowful adulteries and love-suicide pacts are taking place without appearing in the newspapers! Why do such pitiful events occur so frequently? Who must bear the responsibility for this problem, and if responsibility must be borne, is it men or is it women? Needless to say, society as a whole must be the responsible party.'

§ 3

Aka: 'If that is so, are you saying that society must also bear the blame for your recent scandalous affair? What a selfish argument that is!'

Kamako: 'Precisely so, think about it carefully. The present social structure is entirely aristocratic and property-oriented, not to mention male-oriented, where the strong always win. Especially regarding the relations between men and women, today's system is thoroughly advantageous to men in every respect. Yet, no privileges whatsoever are granted to women. It is a social system of the utmost unfairness, and there is nothing more disadvantageous or miserable for women. We struggle to understand the reason why such unfairness must be established between men and women. Therefore, once a problem of divorce arises between a husband and wife due to certain circumstances, the man is invariably placed in an advantageous position, while the woman is put in the opposite position, resulting in her suffering in silence. Even if a woman has legitimate justice on her side, the modern laws, which are framed from the man's perspective and solely to his advantage, refuse to heed her rightful claims in the least. Based on the notion that women are subordinate to men through and through, even in such matters, judgments are handed down with the man placed above. Can this truly be said to be right? We women are crying out that both morality and law must be applied equally to men and women. From a woman's standpoint, we cannot help but cry out thus. However, since such discrimination was arbitrarily established between men and women in the first place, it must be called an unreasonable and lacking fairness. Therefore, women possess rights as women. I do not think that women should hold back so much or debase themselves. If we push forward with thoughts equal to those of men as human beings through and through, would that not be fine? Therein lies the life of a woman, her freedom, and her happiness; that is precisely what a woman should inherently possess. Not only in male-female relations, but since today's system is governed by the evil laws of the weak being meat for the strong and the survival of the fittest, we merely utilized our birth into the Kanto family as good fortune to shatter outdated morals and forms, and displayed our instincts as independent women with the resolve to become the light of a new society.'

Aka: 'There is simply no responding to such a greeting. However, with such thoughts, the order of society will be thrown into disorder, so we must still uphold the law of male supremacy. No matter how much you speak of equal rights for men and women, once you become husband and wife and form a household, you must follow the rule of the husband leading and the wife following, and only then do you enter the realm of male supremacy, the so-called inequality of husband and wife. To steal glances behind the eyes of an inconvenient and outrageous husband, commit adultery with an employee, and yet excuse it as awakening society or becoming the light of a new society—is that not an outrageous rationalization? If the world were to become as you say, first of all the household would be thrown into disorder, adultery would be openly practiced in broad daylight, and there would be no end to jealousy and strife.'

Kamako: 'If a man and woman who love each other become husband and wife, it would be entirely safe no matter how emancipated they are left and even if there were no laws. But in marriages like those of today—oppressive marriages, marriages of property, marriages of lineage, and marriages determined by persons other than the individuals themselves—is it not truly impossible to maintain mutual chastity as husband and wife? It is because a marriage without love is forced upon them that such problems finally arise, unable to be suppressed. That is why I assert that society must bear this responsibility.'

鎌子『さうですとも、よく考へて御覧なさいませ。現在の社会組織といふものは、すべてが貴族本位、資産家本位は申すに及ばず、男子本位で強い者勝で厶いませう。特に男女の関係に付いては、今日の制度は何もかも男に取つては有利な事柄計りです。そして女に対しては何等の特権も与へられて居りませぬ。実に不公平至極な社会制度で、女に取つて之程不利益な悲惨な事はありませぬ。なぜ斯うした不公平を、男と女の間に設けておかねばならないのか、其理由を知るに私達は苦む者です。ですから一度夫婦間に或事情から離婚問題が持上つたが最後、何時も男は有利の位地に立ち、女は其反対の立場におかれて、泣寝入の体ですよ。女は自分に正当の理があつても、男の立場になつて、而も男にのみ有利に定められた現代の法律では、少しも女の正当な申し出でを聞入れてくれませぬ。どこ迄も女は男に従属したものだといふ観念の下に、かうした問題に対しても、男の方を上にして断定を下す事になつてますが、果して之が正しいと云はれませうか。道徳でも法律でも、男女平等に行はなければならないと、吾々女性は絶叫してゐるのです。女の立場からすれば、どうしてもさう叫ばずには居られないでせう。併し元々男と女の間に、さうした差別が勝手に設けられたのですから、云はば無理非道な公平を欠いだものと言はねばなりませぬ。だから女は女としての権利があります。其権利を女の方から、そんなに遠慮したり、自分自らを卑下したりするには当らないと思ひます。どこ迄も一個の人間として、男と同等の考へで押し進んでゆけば、それで可い事ぢやありませぬか。そこに女としての生命があり、自由があり、幸福があるので、それこそ女としての本来の持つ可きものなのです。男女関係計りでなく、今日の制度は弱肉強食、優勝劣敗の悪制度が行はれて居りますから、吾々はカウントの家に生れたのを幸ひ、誤れる古き道徳や形式を打破して、新しい社会の光明となる考へで、女一人としての本能を発揮した計りです』

鎌子『相愛の男女が夫婦となつたのならば、決して何程解放的にしておかうが、法律がなからうが大丈夫ですが、今日の如き圧迫結婚、財産結婚、門閥結婚、本人以外の者の定めた結婚には、真に夫婦としての互の貞操を保持する事が出来ぬぢやありませぬか。愛のない結婚を強るが為に、遂に抑へ切れなくなつて、かやうな問題が起るのですよ。それだから此責任を社会が負はねばならないと、私は主張致します』

Aka: 'However, since you still have life remaining in the Genkai, I will refrain from pressing you too severely today. But once you return to the Genkai, you must lay your hand upon your chest, examine your own erroneous shisau, and faithfully devote your teisetsu to your present husband. Aka raised his saihai and with a shout of 'Fool!', just as he brought it down aiming at Kamako's head, Kamako's spirit instantly vanished and returned to its original physical body.
