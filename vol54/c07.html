Chapter 7: 婚談 [1393]

婚談〔1393〕

§ 1

King Setsuteiri Victoria lay upon a feather bed, resting while having Hirunahime massage his feet. Being of advanced years, and with joyful and terrifying matters coming upon him all at once, his body was utterly exhausted and in a condition resembling that of a semi-invalid, and because his body ached everywhere he was taking repose. Furthermore, the fact that the heir Āru had lately been restless and absent from the castle, stealing away from the eyes of his shinka alone to the suburbs, and after returning after nightfall would gulp down schnapps, and upon becoming intoxicated would raise his voice in great shouts and give the kinji yakunin a hard time, had become the primary cause of wounding Setsuteiri's heart.

To such a place, Samori no Cubit arranged his costume and begged for haietsu. Hearing that it was Samori's inquiry, Setsuteiri immediately granted it. Samori advanced close to the side of the feather bed, placed both hands on the floor, and...

Samori: 'I make my report.'

Setsutei: 'Samori, what is the matter? Your appearance is unusual; has some strange henji broken out once again?'

Samori: 'Yes, if I should tell Ōsama, you would no doubt be immensely astonished, but Āru-sama has gone so far as to bring into Hofus a person called Hanna, the daughter of a lowly serf, and is making a fuss, declaring: "No matter what, if it is not this woman, I will not marry. And should my father by any chance refuse to grant this, I will escape the castle, become a mountain hunter, and lead a pastoral life." Therefore, this old man has also been greatly worried. How on earth should we handle this?'

Setsuteiri, startled by Samori's unexpected report, stepped down from the bed, seated himself properly in front of the hibachi, and...

Setsutei: 'Alas, the House of Victoria is now at its end. When it comes to pass that the vital eldest son wishes to take a serf's daughter as his wife, the aristocracy has truly reached the end of the road. What on earth is best to do?'

Folding his arms, he assumed a pensive posture, while Hirunahime placed her hands on the floor from the side and...

Hiruna: 'Setsuteiri-sama, there is no need for you to be so astonished. No matter how Āru-sama has taken the daughter of a serf to wife, as long as the household is peacefully governed and the country is tranquil, is that not fine? Did not my own station as a chamberlain get promoted, and through your august discernment, did I not get promoted to Arch Duchess? If you are going to worry about Āru-sama's marriage problem, first of all, must you not banish me from Setsuteiri-sama's presence?'

Setsutei: 'Hmm, that is true. Having shown an example from the parent, one cannot very well blame his own child. Nay, however things may go is karma. Samori, make it just as Āru says. And for the time being, must we not consult with the missionary of Hinodewake?'

Samori, contrary to his expectations, stroked his chest in relief and...

Samori: 'The truth of the matter is that when I went to inquire of Hinodewake-sama, his words were that it would be best to arrange this marriage if possible.'

Setsutei: 'If they are the words of the missionary, it should be all right. Good deeds should be done swiftly; convey this reason to my son at the very earliest moment.'

Samori: 'Truly, these are the gracious commands of my lord, and Āru-sama will no doubt regard them with satisfaction. With this, his suburban strolls will likely cease as well. If that is so, I shall henceforth go on an errand. In any case, I beg your kind favor, my august lord and Hirunahime-sama.'

With a joyful countenance, he withdrew from that place.

In Āru's living room, together with Hanna, various conversations had begun.

Hanna: 'I beg of you, Āru-sama, no matter what you may say, since there are many stubborn and narrow-minded old retainers led by your august parents, this matter will surely come to naught. Please do not speak of such things and grant me my leave. And I pray that you, as the heir to King Victoria, will reign over the country of Biku, welcome a fitting mistress, and spend your days in peace.'

原文（近代文語）

刹帝利ビクトリア王はフェザーベッドの上に横たはり、ヒルナ姫に足を揉ませ休んでゐた。何分老年の上に嬉しい事や、恐ろしい事等が一度に出て来たので体がグツタリと弱り半病人の如き有様で、どこともなく体が痛むので休養してゐた。そして世継のアールが此頃ソハソハとして城内に居らず、臣下の目を忍んで一人郊外に出で、日が暮れてから帰つて来てはシュナップスを呷り、酔うては大声を張り上げ近侍の役人共を手古摺らせる等の事が刹帝利の心を痛めた大原因となつてゐる。

かかる処へ左守のキユービツトは衣紋を繕ひ拝謁を乞うた。刹帝利は左守の伺ひと聞いて直ちに之を許した。左守はフェザーベッドの側近く進み寄り、両手をついて、

左守『申上げます』

刹帝『左守、何事だ。常に変つて其方の様子、何か又変事が突発したのではないか』

左守『ハイ、王様に申上げたら、嘸お驚き遊ばすで厶りませうが、アール様は人もあらうに卑しきサーフの娘ハンナとやら云ふ者をホーフスに引入れ、「何うしても此女でなければ結婚はしない。そして万一父が之をお聞届けなくば、城内を脱出し山猟師となつて田園生活を送る」と駄々を捏られますので、此老人も大変に心配を致しました。如何取計らつたら宜しう厶りませうかな』

刹帝利は左守の意外の注進に驚いて、ベツドを下り火鉢の前に端座し乍ら、

刹帝『嗚呼、ビクトリア王家も最早終末だ。肝腎の長子がサーフの娘を女房に持ちたいと云ふ様になつては、最早貴族も末路だ。如何したら宜からうかなア』

と双手を組んで思案の態、ヒルナ姫は側より手をついて、

ヒルナ『刹帝利様、さうお驚きには及びますまい。如何にアール様が耕奴の娘をお娶りになつた所で、家庭が円満に治まり、国家が太平に治まれば宜いぢや厶りませぬか。妾だつて腰元が抜摺され、尊き貴方の御見出しによつてアーチ・ダッチェスに抜摺されたぢやありませぬか。アールさまの結婚問題を御心配遊ばすならば、先づ刹帝利様から妾を放逐遊ばさねばなりますまい』

刹帝『うん、さうだな。親から手本を見せておいて吾子を責むる訳にも行くまい。いや如何なり行くも因縁だ。左守、アールの申す通りにしてやつて呉れ。さうして一応、治国別の宣伝使に御相談をせなくてはなるまいぞや』

左守は案に相違しヤツと胸を撫で下ろし、

左守『実の所は治国別様へお伺ひをして参りました所、治国別様のお言葉では、なるべく此結婚は整へたがよい、との事で厶ります』

刹帝『宣伝使のお言葉とあれば大丈夫だらう。善は急げだ、一事も早く伜に此由を伝へて呉れ』

左守『実に有難き君の仰せ、嘸アール様も御満足に思召すで厶りませう。之で郊外散歩もお止まりになるでせう。左様ならば之からお使に行つて参ります。何分宜しう、吾君様、ヒルナ姫様、お願ひ申します』

と欣々として此場を下がり行く。

アールの居間にはハンナと二人、いろいろの話が初まつてゐた。

ハンナ『もし、アール様、貴方は何と仰有つて下さいましても御両親様を始め頑迷固陋な老臣共が沢山ゐられますれば、屹度此話は駄目で厶りませう。何卒そんな事を仰有らずにお暇を下さいませ。そして貴方はビクトリア王の世継としてビク一国に君臨し相当の奥様を迎へて安楽に世をお送りなさる様お願ひ致します』

§ 2

Aru: 'Ah, as I said just a moment ago, I have lived in the mountains for a long time and have grown accustomed to a life of freedom, so such a cramped aristocratic life is ultimately unbearable. If by any chance I cannot be wedded to you, I intend to slip away from here, enter the mountains, and lead a life of simplicity. Please, do not say such disheartening things and listen to what I say. I beg of you...'

Hanna: 'It would be inexcusable if a serf's daughter like me were said to have corrupted one who is to become the king of a nation. If possible, please make me your lowly maid and, no matter what, forgive this marriage proposal above all else. However, since I shall serve by your side for the rest of my life...'

Aru: 'I have grown to hate the hypocritical society that makes such distinctions as Kshatriyas, Jiyōgiyō, Vaishyas, and Shudras. Therefore, no matter what, I want to try marrying you, a daughter of the pure and unpretentious serfs. And I want to try shattering the unnatural class system created by humans, practicing a government of upper and lower unity and the equality of the four classes. If I cannot do that, I have no hope for survival in the present age.'

Hanna: 'If you go as far as to say that, I shall gladly yield to your words. However, is it your intention to carry this out even if it means going against your parents? If you do that, you surely will not be able to remain within this castle any longer. There is no way you could be permitted to make someone like me your wife.'

Aru: 'There, exactly. I am prepared for that too. Come, let us slip out quietly from the back door from now, enter the mountains, and enjoy a life of simplicity with you. On Mount Terukuni there is an old nest where I lived for a long time. If we go there, no matter what happens, we can make a living...'

Hanna: 'If that is so, it cannot be helped. I shall accompany you.'

Aru: 'Ah, prompt consent, I am well pleased. Come, let us quickly make preparations for our journey.'

And the two of us were wholeheartedly beginning preparations to slip out of the castle. Just then Samori appeared and came before them,

Samori: 'Pardon me. Aru-sama, I have come because I wish to briefly convey your father's words to you. Or are you putting on your travel leggings and heading out for a stroll in the outskirts? Is this not a terribly grand preparation for a mere walk in the outskirts?'

Aru: 'Samori-dono, the truth of the matter is that if I stay here, various women are recommended to me, and it is painful to have a wife who is not to my liking, so I had the intention of slipping away somewhere. Please, I beg of you, overlook it.'

Samori: 'Nay, that will not do. Right in the midst of this marriage issue having arisen, you must not leave this place. For the sake of the state and the royal family, you must succeed to the line of the Kshatriyas no matter what.'

Aru: 'Well, is it not because I am dissatisfied with that marriage issue that I am trying to slip away? Even if I am not here, there are still four younger brothers. If not among those brothers, they should just select an outstanding person from among the common people to succeed to the line of the Kshatriyas, should they not? Surely there is no divine command that I myself must succeed to the line. Rather than that, I intend to stay here with this woman, enter the mountains, and enjoy a simple life.'

Samori: 'Wakanadanna-sama, do not worry yourself. As a result of my running about east and west, through the kind offices of Harukuniwake-sama and Princess Hiruna, it has finally been decided that the Kshatriya-sama's heart has been moved so that you may become husband and wife with Hanna-sama, and I have come to report this to you.'

Aru: 'Hm, is that so? It is quite enlightened of him, unlike my stubborn father. This too must be the power of the times, no doubt. However, there is something about which you too must negotiate a little for me. It is none other than this: can we be allowed to take each other by the hand and freely take a walk outside the castle as husband and wife? If you say that is not allowed either, I am prepared from now on to bolt from this place and continue a pastoral life.'

原文（近代文語）

アール『エー、最前も云ふ通り、私は永らくの間山住居をし、放縦な生活に慣れて来たのだから、斯様な窮屈な貴族生活は到底堪へきれない。万一其方と添ふ事が出来なければ私はここを脱け出して山林に入り簡易生活を送る考へだ。何卒そんな心細い事云はずに俺の云ふ事を聞いて呉れ。頼みだから……』

ハンナ『私の様な耕奴の娘が一国の王様になるお方を堕落させたと云はれては申訳が厶りませぬ。出来る事ならばお小間使になりとお使ひ下さいまして此縁談だけは何処迄もお許し下さいませ。然し乍ら終身貴方のお側で御用をさして頂きますから……』

アール『私は刹帝利だの、浄行だの、毘舎、首陀等と、そんな区別をつける虚偽な社会が嫌になつたのだ。それで純朴のサーフの娘のお前と如何しても結婚をして見たいのだ。そして人間の作つた不自然な階級制度を打破し、上下一致、四民平等の政事をして見たいのだ。それが出来なければ私は現代に生存の希望はない』

ハンナ『そこ迄仰有つて下さるのならば私はお言葉に甘へて従ひませう。然し乍ら御両親に背きなさつて迄決行なさる考へですか。さうすればもはや此城内へ止まる事は出来ますまい。私の様な者を貴方の妻にお許し下さる筈はありませぬ』

アール『そら、さうだ。私も其覚悟はしてゐる。さア、之からソツと裏門から脱け出し、山林に入つてお前と簡易生活を楽しまうぢやないか。照国山には私が長く住まつてゐた古巣がある。そこへ行けばどうなりかうなり生活が出来るから……』

ハンナ『そんなら仕方厶りませぬ。お伴を致しませう』

アール『ヤ、早速の承知、満足に思ふ。さア早く旅の用意をしよう』

と二人は一生懸命に城内を脱け出す用意に取りかかつてゐた。そこへ左守は現はれ来り、

左守『御免なさいませ。アール様、一寸貴方にお父さまのお言葉をお伝へ申し度いと思ひ参りました。又足装束を遊ばして郊外散歩にでもお出ましになるのですか。郊外散歩にしては大変なお準備ぢやありませぬか』

アール『左守殿、実の所は私はここにゐると色々の女を勧められ気に合はぬ女房を持つのが辛いから、何処かへ脱け出す心算で居つたのだ。何卒頼みだから見逃して呉れ』

左守『いや、それはなりませぬ。今結婚問題の持上つた最中、そして貴方はここを出られてはなりませぬぞ。国家のため、王家のため、何処迄も刹帝利の後を継いで下さらねばならぬのです』

アール『さアその結婚問題が気に入らないので、脱け出さうと云ふのぢやないか。私がゐなくてもまだ四人の弟がある。その弟でいかなければ、国民の中から立派な人間を選り出して刹帝利の後を継がせばよいぢやないか。何も自分が後を継がねばならぬ神の命令でもあるまい。それよりも私はここに居る此女と山林に入り簡易生活を楽しむつもりだ』

左守『若旦那様、御心配遊ばしますな。此左守が東奔西走の結果、治国別様やヒルナ姫のお骨折によつて到頭刹帝利様のお心を動かし、ハンナ様と御夫婦とおなり遊ばす様にお話がきまりましたので御報告に参つたのです』

アール『うん、さうか、それは頑固な父に似ずよくまア開けたものだな。ヤツパリ之も時節の力だらう。併し乍らお前も一寸談判をして貰はねばならぬ事がある。それは外でもない、自由自在に夫婦が手に手をとつて城外の散歩をさして貰へるか、それもならぬと云ふのなら俺はこれから、此処を飛び出し田園生活を続ける覚悟だから』

§ 3

Samori: 'You need not worry about such things. It is daijiyōbu. I shall certainly arrange matters so that you may act jiiujizai.'

Āru: 'If I do not secure my father's testimony, I shall be troubled if he interferes clumsily later on.'

Samori: 'By no means, by no means is such goshinpai necessary. Come quickly, your chichiuhe is waiting. I ask you both to come to the ima. I have already sent a messenger to Harukuniwake-sama's quarters as well, so he will be arriving shortly.'

Āru: 'Ah, if that is so, shall I grant an audience? Now, Hanna, will you come following after me?'

Hanna: 'Yes, I shall accompany you wherever you go. Though I am of humble shiyōgen, fearful and unworthy as it may be, since I have become fuzoku to you, I shall follow you anywhere like a shadow.'

Samorinokami softened his ganshiyoku as though greatly relieved, and walking ahead of the futari, he invited them into the ima of the setsuteiri.

原文（近代文語）

左守『そんな事は御心配なさいますな。大丈夫で厶ります。屹度私が取もつて自由自在に御行動の出来る様に致しませう』

アール『父の証言を得て置かなくては、又後からゴテゴテ干渉されると困るからな』

左守『決して決して左様な御心配は御無用です。さア、早く、お父上が待つて居られます。お二人共お居間へお越しを願ひます。治国別様の方へも使ひを立てておきましたから、直お越しになるでせう』

アール『アアそんならお目にかからうかな。これハンナ、お前は私について来るかな』

ハンナ『ハイ、如何なる処へもお伴致します。卑しき妾の身、畏れ多う厶りますが、貴方に附属致した以上は、影法師の如く何処迄も跟いて参りませう』

左守司はヤツと安心したものの如く顔色を和げ、二人の先に立つて刹帝利の居間に誘ひ行く。
