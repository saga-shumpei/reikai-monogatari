Chapter 10: 万 Turtle 柱 [1396]

万亀柱〔1396〕

§ 1

Karunahime sings again.

Karuna: 'Though the morning sun shines or clouds obscure it, though the moon waxes or wanes, even if the great earth were to sink, truth alone saves the world. The sacred teachings of the true God, sent forth to the four quarters as a missionary, from the sacred land of the birth-soil mountain, following behind rare attendants, descending to proclaim the pure teachings of heaven and earth, arranging the sacred marriage of the young lord—oh, how grateful it is! The breathing together of heaven and earth, the people who are born, being the so-called children of God and palaces of God, living to become the gods of this world, dying to become the gods of national protection, the people growing and flourishing in the fruitful reed-plain of the land of ears of rice, protecting them warmly and exhausting their sincere hearts according to the guidance of the imperial gods—oh, how grateful it is that you taught us this! We husband and wife, respectfully, for the sake of God, for the sake of the country, and to protect the common people, purifying our bodies morning and evening before the precious divine presence, guard the realm beneath heaven peacefully and most securely—we humbly pray. The Kototama thus uttered is not in vain. The marriage of the young lord is successfully arranged at last, and the prosperity of the imperial land is like pine flowers whose green color grows ever deeper, flourishing and flourishing forever without end, a joy whose limits are completely unknown—this is entirely the divine blessing.

With awe and gratitude, ah, Kannagara Kamugami, may your august spirits be blessed and prosperous!'

Matsuhiko sings again.

Matsuhiko: 'This place is the renowned land of Biku, surrounded by strong walls and iron ramparts, a citadel overlooking the four quarters, casting light like the moon and sun shining high in the august sky, causing the rain of blessings to fall, and warmly protecting the people of the land—the renowned Kshatriya, the house of King Victoria, eternal for ten thousand ages, may it be long enduring forever together with heaven and earth, we pray before the presence of God. Once enveloped in black clouds, even this renowned famous castle at last appeared perilous, but by the divine protection of the precious God, the ugly crooked deities perished and vanished, and now how felicitous is this reign, leaving no trace of dust! In this felicity, adding yet another joy, the marriage of the Lord of Earl has been brought about so brightly, and the sound of the jade-stringed koto is clear, spreading far with bravery. This boundless joy—keeping it deep in our hearts so as not to let it go outside—Matsuhiko Tsukasa, as Kannagara, lifting up prayers to God, serves and celebrates before the august presence of the lord. When the temple of Victoria is built up, oh Lord of Earl and Lady Hannah, taking each other by the hand morning and evening, never failing to worship, may the people of the four quarters beneath heaven, without illness, rejoice in your governance and serve you accordingly.

Pray to the master lord of Harukuniwake! I, following him, in close proximity, leaving behind the beloved capital, for the sake of God and for the sake of the Way, must advance toward the sacred land. Ah, Kannagara Kamugami, may your august spirits be blessed and prosperous!'

So he finished singing and took his seat. Tatsuhiko sings again.

Tatsuhiko: 'Following my master lord, leaving the forest of the shrine, crossing the forest dark even at day in the mountain pass where the crooked deity rages, viewing the forest in the field and the small northern mountain behind us step by step, and coming to the forest of floating wood—if we look, there was the deep and unexpected plot and trap of the crooked deity, falling down with the master lord into the bottomless abyss, the spirit suddenly leaving the body and wandering in the spiritual world, and by the august direction of God, making a tour of the spiritual and heavenly realms, and descending once more, being the god of the crossroads, Ibukido-nushi, having been taught various precious ways, and living again in this world.

Appearing as the general of Brahmanism, Katahiko, sending forth the Lord of Launch, making manifest the divine power of Ananai-kyo, we, together with our master lord, crossed over the wilderness, traversed across the Lion River, and when we advanced as far as the foot of Mount Victor, at the moment when the battle cry was suddenly heard, thinking this could not be ordinary and advancing closely, when we looked well, it was the very height of a simultaneous shooting by the Brahman Oni-Harukawake and Kumehiko. Though thinking how it might be as us, who are the divine officials of Ananai, even if we were to let it pass by unheeded, together with our master lord, if we strike forth the Kototama of rigor—'

§ 2

How could we have foreseen that it was not the army led by the Brahmans, but the rebellion of the Umori no Tsukasa; and how hateful it felt when we realized it! We disciples, all at once, with aligned hearts and synchronized mouths, straightforwardly and clearly recited the Futonorito, and continuously unleashed the Kototama. Then, the great army, like clouds and mist, fled away like clouds turning to haze. To such a place came Hirunahime, the female warrior and princess of Karna, protected by the Kami, stepping firmly as she straddled a chestnut horse, binding the rebel Umori and bringing him back. There was no one who did not praise and extol such an act of loyal bravery and righteous zeal as a model unto the latest generations.

We disciples were led before the presence of Setsuteirisama, and receiving various and sundry treatments, we humbly expressed our gratitude for their profound kindness. We thoroughly and minutely transmitted the teachings of Ananai-kyō, and upon the summit of Mount Viktor, we shattered the rocks, dug into the earth, pounded it firmly into the upper bedrock, tamped it solidly into the lower bedrock, and raised the great pillar of the shrine. Now, it has finally reached ninety percent completion, which is a great joy. Prior to this, Setsuteiri, who is the august child of Queen Victoria, received divine revelations concerning the whereabouts of the five princes and one princess, and advanced into the valley of Mount Terukuni to welcome and escort them.

Prince Aru, who was born as the eldest son and is a kind-hearted soul, is holding his auspicious wedding ceremony today. Needless to say, the hundred officials were invited, and we disciples as well were summoned to take our seats at the auspicious banquet. Oh, the joy of being granted the great sacred sake of the Kami's grace! Originally, Tatsuhiko, a heavy drinker, loves sake above all else; yet, as one who serves the path of the Ananai teachings, first and foremost, I must refrain from heavy drinking. Nevertheless, today, for some reason, joys upon joys overlapped, and I piled cup upon cup of circulating sake, unwittingly and unknowingly getting dead drunk. I must have been terribly rude.

Simply viewing everything as a true direct manifestation and great direct manifestation of the Kami, I humbly beg your forgiveness, Setsuteiri, and you, the hundred officials. Before your august presence, I reverently offer my apologies. Ah, Kannagara kamugami kamugami, may your august spirits prosper and bless us! »

Tatsuhiko, following in his master's footsteps and dead drunk, dances and chants with staggering steps.

Tatsuhiko (in the style of a Noh chant): « Ah, how auspicious, how auspicious, how very auspicious! Though the morning sun shines or is clouded, this marriage is free from ill; for ten thousand autumns and myriads of years it shall be celebrated and concluded, and thereafter, even if heaven and earth should change, what is there to fear before the grace of the Kami! The might of the ruler of the country of Biku is such that even the birds flying through the sky loosen their wings and fall to the earth, the trees and plants feel it and play natural music, the flow of the river beats a hand-drum, and how we shall celebrate your reign of many ages! Furthermore, starting with my master who serves the Sumeōkami of Ananai, among the three companions, there is one known as a man of note—I, Tatsuhiko of the turtle's age, who celebrate this myriad of ages, rise up boldly to transmit this joy through all eternity, and waking my heavy body that can no longer manage its hands and feet due to being dead drunk, I celebrate and revere you to the limit of my life! The crane preserves the age of a thousand years, and the turtle continues the lifespan of ten thousand years; the lives of the crane and the turtle are trifling compared to the end of countless myriads of years—may the august country of Biku flourish forever, and may the royal house of Victoria flourish without knowing any limit, as long as heaven and earth endure! Concerning this as well, Prince Aru, and your newly welcomed consort, Princess Hanna, may you be deeply affectionate and harmonious to the very end, blending the breaths of Yin and Yang, and showing a pure model of younger sister and elder brother beneath the sun! The sun shines brightly, the moon glows, the stars shine forever in the open sky without losing their radiance, deep beneath the vast ocean of the realm of the moon, may you plight your troth, O Kannagara, before the august presence of the Kami with reverence, I, Tatsuhiko of the turtle's age, celebrate and revere this longevity of today through thousands of ages to come! »

So saying, unlike a drunken man, he solemnly concluded his celebration and returned to his original seat. Besides this, although there were many celebratory songs by numerous officials, they were too lengthy and are therefore omitted.

From the time Prince Aru took Hanna, the ideal wife, as his bride, the governance of the country flourished day by day, and the citizens submitted with joy. The citizens in general offered faith morning and evening to Kuninotachitotsu no Mikoto, Hinotsukami, Tsukinotsukami, and Kamisusanoo no Mikoto enshrined in the shrine of Mount Viktor, and each enjoyed their occupation, leading to the manifestation of the sacred era of Miroku upon the earth. Ah, Kannagara tamashii sakihai masaseyo.

と泥酔者に似ず、いと真面目に祝ひ納め元の座に着きぬ。此外、数多の司人の祝の歌はあれど、あまり管々しければ省略するなり。

アールの王子は理想の妻、ハンナを娶りしより、国の政治日に月に開けて国民悦服し、ビクトル山の神殿に祭りたる国治立尊、日の大神、月の大神、神素盞嗚大神を朝な夕なに国民一般が信仰をなし、各其業を楽しみ、ミロクの聖代を地上に現出する事となりぬ。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
