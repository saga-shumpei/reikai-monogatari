Chapter 17: Fire 救団 [1403]

火救団〔1403〕

§ 1

A woman came trudging to the Sekishiyo of Yachimata. The Shiyuei turned to the woman,

Aka: 'This is the Sekishiyo of Yachimata. I have something to check, so I would like you to wait.'

The woman was startled, and while making a polite Jigi, she said,

Woman: 'Yes, is there something you require of me?'

Aka: 'Where are you from? Let me hear your Seimei.'

Woman: 'Yes, I am Sumieru, daughter of Teemusu from Tamakimura in the land of Fusa.'

The Shiyuei said, 'Land of Fusa, land of Fusa,' and while saying this, he turned the pages of a long ledger with Neni,

Aka: 'You are still a Mikonshiya, are you not?'

Sumieru: 'Yes, that is Sayō. I am troubled because my connections for marriage have been Tohoen.'

Aka: 'Have there not been many offers of Endan until now? Naze did you not listen to the words of your Riyōshin and take in a Yōshi sooner?'

Sumieru: 'Although many Ritsupa Yōshi have offered to marry me, as you are Gozon, I am an Otafuku, so there is no one who loves me from their heart and comes to be a Yōshi. Since all of them make the offer of a Yōshi with the Mokuteki of inheriting my father's Zaisan, I cannot bear to be made such a Gisei. Once a man becomes a Yōshi, he is the master. While my parents are alive, it is one thing, but once my parents pass away or move, they will gradually shed the cat's skin they have been wearing and reveal their true nature, keeping beautiful women as mistresses, or casting aside their legal wife to make a mistress their wife; there are so many such wicked men in this world that I cannot carelessly accept a Yōshi.'

Aka: 'In reality, you are in love with the clerk Shiina, are you not? That is why you say such things and reject all the Ritsupa Yōshi who come along, I suppose.'

Sumieru: 'As you have guessed, he is the clerk I have placed in my house, but in love, there is no distinction of rank. Also, if I make the clerk my Yōshi, I do not know how much better it would be, as he would not be arrogant like the husbands of the world; I think it is very convenient for the sake of the house and for my own sake, and though I do not know what my parents think, I have decided on that. No matter if he is called a clerk, there is no change in his personality. I believe that all men and women should combine based on their personality as individuals. One should not treat the other as a slave, nor view them as an object, nor worship them like a god. Unless it is a union of equal personality and personality on both sides, it is neither true love nor marriage. The marriage of today, which is like the subordination of women to men, is truly extremely irrational, and regarding its sexual relationship, it must be said that it is a highly inhuman and illogical sexual relationship when viewed from a human who has already awakened as an individual, almost like master and servant, customer and product, or mare and stallion. A marital relationship where the wife can say that she is favored by her husband or is loved by him, in response to words from the husband about cherishing, taking care of, or being kind to his wife, is never a genuine marriage, no matter what kind of affection may exist there. It is no different from the affection a master has for his pet dog, or what a capitalist calls warm-heartedness toward wage labor, and it is not a true moral relationship between person and person. A husband who tries to force only gentleness and chastity upon a woman is forcing what is called slave morality upon the opposite sex. We wish to absolutely exclude such social conventions and evil practices. The reason we have come to see the sad fact that many women today have come to possess temperaments common to pet dogs and pet cats, such as fawning, acting spoiled, or flirting, is simply because people until now have had no understanding of love marriage. First of all, I wish to remove the trivial barriers of master and clerk and live in sacred love. Therefore, no matter how Ritsupa a man may be, or even if he is a wise man or a scholar, I cannot entrust my life to a stubborn person who does not understand this logic. Only with the thought of love supremacy can one give an accurate spiritual, logical, and rational foundation to monogamy. Any other theory of monogamy is nothing more than hypocrisy, or merely a matter of convenience, convention, utilitarian opportunism, or formalism. A couple whose ideals do not match will inevitably cause disturbances between them, and they will eventually have to adopt polygamy like the Mormons. Also, on the woman's side, they will inevitably end up in a situation where they must unwillingly practice polyandry, like in Lamaism, regardless of the surface appearance; therefore, I cannot consent to this marriage problem, no matter how much it is the words of my parents. That is why Shiina-sama, the clerk, and I are at our wits' end. There is no one as unhappy as a daughter who has stubborn and unspiritual parents.'

Aka: 'Once again, a Renaishinseironshiya has come and given a strange kind of echo to our Zunau. However, what this woman says is also the most excellent thought for a human of Konnichi.'

As he said this, a very beautiful woman, somewhat younger, came walking along sluggishly. As soon as Sumieru saw this woman, she looked happy and said,

Sumieru: 'Oh, are you not my younger sister Sugaru?'

Sugaru: 'Yes, was it you, elder sister? I am glad to have met you in such a place. I thought I had been taken into the Ihaya of Inokurayama and dropped into a dark Otoshiana, but I found myself pulled out to a place like this. Surely you must have met with the same fate as I, elder sister.'

Sumieru: 'Listen, sister, this is not Utsushiyo; it seems to be an Anbai like Reikai. The two of us sisters must have been thrown into a deep hole, lost our Inochi, and our Reikon have come here.'

Sugaru: 'That cannot be so. My Kibun is this clear, after all. It is neither a dream nor have I died. Please do not say such things. It makes me feel lonely.'

Aka: 'Sugaru, was it? You appear to be the sister of Sumieru, but this is the Yachimata of Reikai, so it is not yet a place for you two to come. You must return to Utsushiyo from here and work for a while longer. However, since the Karada of you both have been thrown into a deep, dark Otoshiana, it will not be Yōi to rescue you. Yet, strangely, you are listed as alive in the Seishibo, so the Lord God will likely find a way to return you to Utsushiyo.'

Sugaru: 'Is that so? If that is the case, then this really was Reikai after all. If I was captured by the subordinates of the general called Kiharuwake and Kumehiko, and thrown into a deep hole, then did we sisters leave Utsushiyo at that very time?'

At this point, Michiharuwake and Shiina came advancing toward them, talking about something along the way and singing a propaganda song in faint voices. Their figures appeared dimly through the trees by the roadside.

Michiharuwake: 'The Lord God appears in the world, To distinguish between good and evil. This world is the land where the Lord God dwells, And the people of the world, Are all without exception, Under the blessings of the noble Lord God, There is none who is left out. Leaving the hall of the Saien, In the land of the moon where the evil gods rage, Coiling upon Mount Daikun, To speak to the ugly gods, And to sweep away the dust of this world, Following Kuniharuwake, Even as we advanced, Left behind in the forest of the shrine, Serving in the sacred hall of Mizu, Having fulfilled that divine work, The propaganda envoys advance once more. Leaving the forest of Ukiki behind, At the foot of Shimeji Pass, Just as we arrived, the Magatsumi, Took position on Inokurayama, And tormented the people all around. Hearing that the Thames of Tamaki Village, Had his two daughters abducted, Moved by a sense of chivalry that could not be abandoned, Disguising my body in military uniform, Together with Shiina, I stood against the Ihaya, Where the evil gods gathered, And was thrown into the dark hole, Of the evil gods' schemes. I thought I had fainted, But before I knew it, drifting, To a vast field with no end, Without knowing, I wandered. I think, is this not the Yachimata highway, Of Reikai? The air all around is somehow, Different from the world of Utsushiyo. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara, May the spirits be blessed, Without separating Utsushiyo and Reikai, Save the Reikon of us, Who are stained with sin, And raise us to heaven, Kannagara. Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, Toyokunihime no Ōkami, Before the great presence of the sacred spirits, I humbly and reverently pray. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara, May the spirits be blessed.'

The two finally arrived in front of the gate of the barrier. When they looked closely, they saw that Sumieru and Sugaru, whom they had been trying to rescue, were talking to the guards in red and white, so Michiharuwake, looking puzzled, said,

Michiharu: 'I am Michiharuwake, a propaganda envoy of the San-kyō. Here is Shiina-dono, the manager for Thames-dono of Tamaki Village. Are these two ladies not Sumieru-sama and Sugaru-sama?'

Aka: 'Sayō it is. Since they have just arrived here as Seirei, I am currently urging them to return.'

Michiharu: 'Ah, that was a Goyakukai. What faintly comes to my mind is that I thought to rescue these Goriyōnin, snuck into the Gankutsu of Inokurayama with a Kikei, failed, and thinking I had been discovered by the enemy and thrown into a deep, Ankoku hole, I found that both of them were here in such a place. If that is the case, are we not also, after all, beings of the Genkai?'

Aka: 'No, there is no need for Goshinpai. You four are not yet people who should come here. You will likely return to the Genkai through some kind of Tetsuzu. By the way, Sheena, since Sumieru-sama is here, will you not offer your Goaisatsu?'

Sheena: 'Yes, I am so embarrassed that no Kotoba will come out.'

Sheena bowed her head.

Aka: 'Sheena-sama, Sumieru-sama was expressing a Daidaiteki Kien toward you. Once you return to the Genkai, please strive for social service or Seirei service with intimacy.'

Sheena: 'Having once had my physical body taken, how could I possibly return to the Genkai?'

Aka: 'Those who must come here must come, no matter how much they say they dislike it. And those who have a lifespan in the Genkai cannot come, no matter how much they wish to. Eventually, a splendid Seirei of the propagandists will come and take you all back to the Genkai.'

The four were struck with a sense of surprise and stared at the faces of the two guards. From the east, the voices of three or four people were heard calling, 'Oi, oi.' As the four turned their bodies toward the source of the voices, a path of light roared through the low sky, advancing with a rumbling sound, and descended slowly before the four. The fire group instantly transformed into four divine beings. Michiharu-wake thought, 'How mysterious,' and when he peered closely at their faces, he saw it was the party of Chikuni-wake, whom he had longed for. While Michiharu-wake was overcome with tears of joy,

Michiharu: 'Ah, Sensei-sama, Matsuhiko, Tatsuhiko, Mankō-dono, thank you for coming.'

He wiped his tears with his sleeves and wept with joy. Before they knew it, a universal light shone from all directions. Illuminated by this light, the figures of the eight vanished like smoke. Consequently, the checkpoint of Yachimata and the figures of the red and white guards also became invisible.
