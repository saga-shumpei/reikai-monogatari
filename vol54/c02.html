日出前〔1388〕

§ 1

By the command of Harukuniwake, To seek the children of King Victoria In the mountain valleys of Terukunidake And bring them home, Tatsuhiko departed the mansion, And Matsuhiko, bearing joyful news, Accompanied by Mankōtsukasa. Across the fields and mountains of Victoria, Renowned in rumor, Crossing valleys, Startled by the cries of monkeys, Treading upon the roots of rocks and trees, Though their footing was perilous and slipping, They grasped the branches of the trees And followed a pathless way. In the midst of the mountains where thorns grew thick, At the cave where Earl, Isu, and four others lay hidden, At last, panting, they arrived.

The two, Oakes and Daiya, were basking in the sun at the entrance of the cave, drying animal skins and doing laundry, waiting for them to dry. As soon as they saw the figures of the three men, they opened their eyes in surprise, took their mountain knives in hand, and assumed a defensive stance, wondering what manner of person was attacking. Looking closely, they saw that each man held aloft a hand-flag inscribed with the words, 'Welcoming the Children of the Kshatriya.' Oakes folded his arms and was lost in thought for a while, but in his heart he thought:

Oakes: 'That hand-flag says they have come to welcome us, but perhaps my father and his stubborn attendants have searched for our whereabouts and, feigning a welcome, have come to capture us. I cannot carelessly reveal my identity.'

Having made up his mind, he winked at his younger sister, Daiya. Daiya quickly understood Oakes's intention and, with an air of unconcern, toyed with the water of the small valley stream, playing intentionally with an innocent manner. Approaching them were the three messengers; Matsuhiko placed his hands on the ground and spoke.

Matsuhiko: 'I would like to ask you something. Are you not the children of the Kshatriya-sama of Victoria Castle?'

Oakes intentionally feigned ignorance.

Oakes: 'I am a mountain man who has lived in these mountains since long ago. I am no such noble person. Not a single person of that kind has ever been seen in this valley, so I would have you search elsewhere.'

Matsuhiko: 'No, no matter what you say, there is no mistake that you are the children. Come, please return to the castle with me. I will certainly do nothing that would be bad for you.'

Daiya drew near and spoke.

Daiya: 'I do not know where you are from, but we are a mountain man and a mountain woman living here as a couple. We are by no means such persons, so please search elsewhere.'

Matsuhiko: 'No matter what you say, in our eyes, there is no doubt that you are the children of the Kshatriya-sama. Without saying such things, I wish for you to return to the castle as we request.'

Daiya: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, what an incomprehensible person you are. A woodcutter's daughter has fallen from grace, taken a village man by the hand, fled to such a place, and become a mountain man and woman to taste love; to call me the daughter of the Kshatriya-sama is so awe-inspiring that I might be struck by divine punishment. If I were the daughter of such a noble person, why would I come to a place like this, and who would live such an inconvenient mountain life? Please think about it carefully.'

Matsuhiko: 'You say just now that you eloped here with a man, but although your body is relatively large, you still look to be only ten or eleven years old. Even if you say such things, this Matsuhiko will not be deceived. The Kshatriya-sama has greatly regretted his actions and yearns for his six children... saying, "Ah, at that time I was deluded by an evil deity. As the years pass, how happy I would be if those six children were with me..." Since he laments morning and night, we newcomers have received the King's command and come to welcome you. No matter how you hide it, there is no mistake. And where have the other four siblings gone? I would like you to please let us know that.'

と心を定め、妹のダイヤに目配せした。ダイヤはオークスの意思を早くも悟り、さあらぬ態にて細谷川の水をいぢり、あどけない態にて、ワザとに遊んでゐた。そこへ近付いたのは迎ひの三人、松彦は両手をついて、

オークス『俺は此山に昔から住居をしてをる山男だ。そんな尊い者ではない。此谷には左様な方は一人もみえたことはないから、外を尋ねて貰ひたいものだなア』

ダイヤは側へ寄つて来て、

ダイヤ『どこの方か知りませぬが、妾はここに二人夫婦暮しをしてゐる山男山女で厶います。決して左様な者ぢやありませぬから、外をお尋ね下さいませ』

ダイヤ『ホホホホ何とマア分らぬ人だこと、木樵の娘が堕落して、村の男と手に手を取つて、斯様な処へ逃げ来り、山男山女となつて、恋を味はつてゐるのに、畏れ多くも刹帝利様の娘だなどとは、勿体ない罰が当りますぞや。妾が左様な尊い方の娘なれば、どうしてこんな所へ出て来ませうか、誰がこんな不便な山住居を致しませうか。よく考へて御覧なさいませ』

Ōkusu: 'Never, never, no matter what you say, I am no such person. Please search elsewhere.'

Just as he was saying this, the four elder brothers came down the steep Tanimichi, carrying a wild boar on their shoulders. Being excessively preoccupied with their Ashimoto, they did not notice that three men had come to this place and were talking with their Kiyōdai. Lowering the boar before the Iriguchi and wiping away their sweat continuously, the four were astonished upon seeing the Sannin of men bowing down upon the Chijiyō.

Āru turned toward Ōkusu:

Āru: 'Hey, who are the Sannin of men who have come Koko?'

Ōkusu: 'They say they have come from the Setsuteirisama of Victoria Castle to welcome us. They insist... saying that you must undoubtedly be his Goshijiyo, so they have come to welcome us. Oyakata, what shall we do?'

Āru: 'There is nothing to deliberate; should we not simply carry out our Kitei?'

Ōkusu: 'As for that, please wait Chiyotsuto.'

Āru: 'What, if we hesitate, there is a danger it will become Hatsukaku; finish off Koitsura Sannin!'

No sooner had he spoken than he gave a Mekuba to Ichidō of the brothers. Ichidō, holding their Itsukiyari, thrust at the Sannin without uttering a word. Ōkusu and Daiya both stepped in between Sauhau,

Ōkusu and Daiya: 'Elder brothers, please wait... elder brothers, wait a moment.'

Far from listening to the Seishi of Riyōnin, the four elder brothers said,

Four brothers: 'Bah! If you get in the way, we will make a sacrifice of you too!'

So saying, they surrounded the Sannin in a confusion. The Sannin took the trunk of a great tree as a shield and recited the Amatsu Norito with all their might, whereupon the four who had been raging furiously felt their bodies grow numb and fell heavily on their backsides right on the spot. And they were doing nothing but shaking their heads.

Āru: 'You must be intending to feign that you are messengers to welcome us six to the Setsuteiri, tempt us back smoothly to the castle, and deprive us of our lives. We are not such ones as to take such a thing for truth and be cleverly caught in a trap. Well, you had better return quickly.'

Thus, though unable to move his limbs, as might be expected of the offspring of a Setsuteiri, he was stout-hearted. Matsuhiko expended endless words, detailing minutely the genuine heart of the Setsuteiri and the condition of the Hōfusu.

Āru: 'If there be no mistake in that, we Kiyōdai shall open a Rāto Koko, and as a result thereof give you our answer. We beg you to grant us a short delay.'

Matsuhiko: 'Ah, your prompt consent is appreciated; after opening the Rāto as quickly as possible, please return with Wareware to the Hōfusu.'

Āru: 'If so, I wish you to withdraw from Koko for a short time; we shall never flee nor hide.'

Matsuhiko said, 'Very well,' and withdrew about forty or fifty ken to the nearby mountainside, watching the state of the six. The six lowered their voices and held a consultation council.

Āru: 'Hey, younger brothers, what do you think? Those Sannin said they were father's retainers, but considering the appearance of their bodies, they seem like Monku. Saying such things, fearing that we Kiyōdai are Koko by father's command, could they not be thinking of deceiving us smoothly, taking us back, and throwing us into prison? We must think about this very carefully.'

Īsu: 'Those are by no means Monku. They must be Gurenajiā who have come receiving father's command. Otherwise, to a place where six of us boarist warriors are present, a mere Sannin or so could not possibly come!'

Ōkusu: 'Elder brother's thought is reasonable for a moment, but in a dream I had last night, I saw that three Propagandesta of a certain noble God would welcome us back to the Hōfusu and treat us with great care. Thinking it was unreliable because it was a dream, I actually remained without speaking of it. Whereupon, Monku similar to those seen in the dream have come. It is surely without mistake. Without saying such things, shall we not follow the Sannin and return? Since father has reached old age and his mind must have weakened considerably, no reckless thing will occur.'

と言つてる所へ四人の兄は猪を担いで、きつい谷路を下つて来た。余り足元に気を取られて居つたので、三人が此処に来て弟妹と話をしてるのに気がつかなかつた。入口の前に猪を下ろし、汗を拭ひ拭ひ、三人の男が地上に平伏してるのを見て、四人は驚いた。

オークス『ビクトリア城の刹帝利様から、お迎へに来たのだ。貴方は其御子女に違ひないからお迎ひに来たのだ……と云つて聞かないのですよ。親方何う致しませうかね』

アール『どうも斯うもない、吾々の規定通り実行すれば可いぢやないか』

オークス『それは一寸待つて頂きたう厶います』

アール『ナニ、グヅグヅしてゐると発覚する虞がある、此奴等三人をやつつけて了へ』

と言ふより早く一同の兄弟に目配せした。一同は猪突槍を持つて、物をも言はず三人に突いてかかる。オークス、ダイヤの両人は双方の中に割つて入り、

アール『其方は吾々六人を刹帝利の迎へと佯り、甘く城内につれ帰り、生命を奪はむとの企みであらう。左様な事を真に受けて、うまうまと計略に乗る様な吾々でない。サ早く帰つたがよからう』

と手足も動かぬくせに流石は刹帝利の胤丈あつて、気丈夫なものである。松彦は、千言万語を費して、刹帝利の真心や或はホーフス（宮中）の様子を細々と述べ立てた。四人は漸くにしてヤツと安心した。

アール『それに間違なくば、吾々兄妹は茲でラートを開き、其結果御返事を致しませう。少時く御猶予を願ふ』

アール『然らば暫くここを遠ざかつて貰ひたい、決して逃げも隠れも致しませぬ』

松彦は『宜しい』と四五十間許り傍の山腹に退き、六人の様子を監視してゐた。六人は声を秘めて、相談会を開いた。

アール『オイ、弟、お前達はどう思ふか。あの三人は父の家来だと云つたが、どうも体の様子を考へてみると、モンク（修道師）の様だ。あんな事を云つて、父の命を受け吾々の兄妹の此処にゐる事を恐れて、甘くゴマかし、連れ帰り、牢獄へブチ込む考へではあるまいか、ここは余程考へねばなるまいぞ』

イース『あれは決してモンクではありますまい。父の命を受けてやつて来たグレナジアーでせう。さうでなければ、吾々猪武者が六人居る所へ、只の三人位で来られるものぢやありませぬワ』

オークス『兄上様のお考へも一応尤も乍ら、私が夜前夢を見ましたのには、或尊い神様のプロパガンデストが三人吾々をホーフスへ迎へ帰り、大切にしてくれる事をみましたが、夢の事だから当にならないと思うて、実の所はお話もせずに居つたのです。そした所が夢にみたと同様のモンクがやつて来ました。キツと間違ひありますまい。そんな事仰有らずに三人に従つて帰らうぢやありませぬか、父も老年に及び、余程気も弱つて居りませうから、滅多な事は厶いますまい』

Aasu: 'Since Chichiuhe is so obstinate and superstitious, there is no reason for us to feel completely at ease. Well, Uerusu, what do you think?'

Uerusu: 'Yes, in my opinion, I cannot think he is an ordinary Ningen. Nor do I think he came by father's Meirei. I have heard from old times that a monster named Eugenbau prowls about these parts, so might not Soitsu have come in disguise? I wonder if we might be thrust into a Dorota, thinking it to be a home. Look here, if we are careless, there is no telling what kind of plight we may suffer.'

Aasu: 'Oh, he is surely no monster. At any rate, as the saying goes, stay away from danger; so we shall offer as much Teikau as possible here, and if we absolutely must go, we will have no Shiyō but to change our Sumika.'

Erinan: 'I speak to Aniuhe. No matter how I think of it, I believe those Sannin are neither Eukuwai nor Akunin, but, as Ōkusu said, ones whom father sent to welcome us as a Ketsukuwa of his Kaishin. Without indulging in Torikoshikurau, how about following them for now? If we find them suspicious, wouldn't it be fine to take Shiyochi at that time?'

Daiya: 'My Gonin brothers, I truly think that person is genuine. Wouldn't it be better for us to return at once?'

Āru made up his mind and said:

Āru: 'Well, they say you cannot eat a Jiyukushi unless you go to a fearful place; let us entrust our fate to Ten and return. However, since the so-called Hirunahime, that hyena in petticoats, is waiting, we must be exceedingly careful as we return. Come, let us make up our minds and return.'

Thus, their counsel was finally settled, and they beckoned to the Sannin. The Sannin joyfully ran up before the six persons, and said:

Matsuhiko: 'It appears it has finally been decided that you will return. Wareware also feel immense joy. Now, let us return. As you have surmised, I am a propagandist of Anai-kyō. To tell the truth, just when Castle Victoria was on the verge of falling due to Berutsu of the Right Guard, my master, Propagation Envoy Kunihirawake, appeared leading us, rescued the castle, and peace was temporarily restored inside the castle. Kasatriya-sama also deeply regretted having tried to take your lives and lamented with sighs; so my master discerned that you were here, spoke to Kasatriya-sama, and dispatched us. Please do not worry by any means. Now, let us return.'

At these words, the six brothers and sisters were relieved at last, and following behind Matsuhiko, Tatsuhiko, and Bankō, the traveling companions of eight men and one woman crossed mountains and traversed valleys, and at last, at the hour of 'vor Sonnenaufgang', arrived back quietly at the mansion of Kunihirawake.

此言葉に六人の兄妹はやつと安心し、松彦、竜彦、万公の後に従ひ、八男一女の道連れは山を越え、谷を渡り、漸くにしてフオール・ゾンネン・アウフ・ガンダの時刻に治国別の館にソツと帰り来たりける。
