Chapter 8: 祝莚 [1394]

祝莚〔1394〕

§ 1

In the guest hall of Victoria Castle, Setsuteiri, beginning with Princess Hiruna, the entourage of Harukuniwake, together with Naijitsukasa's Taruman, Samori, Umori, beginning with Haruna, Princess Karuna, and numerous other officials, aligned in order, and the wedding ceremony was performed.

Without anyone knowing who spoke of it, this matter spread throughout the castle town, and wherever people gathered, they spoke of it as a record-breaking wedding, so that the flowers of conversation blossomed even to the backs of the tenement houses.

Thus, it was expected by the entire citizenry as a symbol of the great political reform.

Here, through the mediation of Harukuniwake, the Shinto wedding ceremony was also safely concluded.

Thereupon, Setsuteiri and Princess Hiruna offered their profound thanks to Harukuniwake and returned to their own parlor.

Afterwards, a celebratory banquet was held for the new couple and all present.

Harukuniwake sings a congratulatory song.

Harukuniwake: 'From the age of the gods of old, Izanagi's Sumeōkami, together with Izanami no Kami, in Takamagahara, circled the heavenly pillar, united the path of husband and wife, gave birth completely and in detail even to the gods of mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees, and created this world peacefully and beautifully—how heroic that divine art! Following those divine techniques, in the inner chamber of Victoria Castle, Lord Āru, awakened to the times, abolished the barriers between high and low, was born in the house of a serf, and performed the ceremony of sharing the nuptial quilt with Princess Hanna. That this was granted to conform entirely to the precious divine will of Heaven and Earth—to make a distinction between the noble and the humble, and to make distinctions in treatment applied to humanity who are the children of gods, saying that such is created, is the perverse work of one who knows not the heart of the imperial gods. The time of the return of spring having arrived, precisely as the great heart of the Supreme God of supreme benevolence and supreme love, opening the path of husband and wife, and showing that paradigm to the people of the land—how precious! Such being the case, the citizenry shall make the King their true parent, revere him as lord, and as master, and offering true sincerity from the depths of their hearts, shall serve him. The gods appearing outwardly, separating good and evil, creating this world, Kamuna-bi, the heart also vast, Ōna-bi, merely whatever happens in the world of man, seeing truly, hearing truly, speaking truly, and correcting the errors of the world, before the presence of Sumeōkami, abolishing the formal distinctions hitherto divergent from the Way, making the hearts of high and low as one, that the citizenry for the sake of the August Land may unite their strength, make their hearts as one, and rejoicing everlastingly in the vicinity of the Sovereign, protect and serve Him, Kamunagara! The gods will doubtless consent to this ceremony, and forever protect the path of husband and wife, and cause the land of Biku to flourish and prosper in abundance. Ah, Kamunagara, kami, kami, offering sincere hearts before the presence of the gods, we revere and celebrate.'

Samori no Tsukasa opens a golden fan, dances of himself, and chants of himself.

Samori (Yōkyoku): 'Ah, how wondrous, how precious! Hanging even in the mouth, awe-inspiring are Heaven and Earth, by the divine decree of Sumeōkami, residing in tranquility in the land of Biku, Setsuteiri, Victoria King's very first prince, Prince Āru, born in the house of a serf, a heroically-minded woman of talent, of mandarin duck quilts forever cherishing and intimate, opening the path of husband and wife—how precious is this ceremony! Even though born in the house of a Shūdra, taking to wife a truly bright and wise woman, the young lord, from that very time when Heaven and Earth opened, an unprecedented and rare prince, a wise prince residing therein, cutting off the separation between upper and lower, pitying the lower citizenry, this is the beginning of opening a beautiful government—Samori no Tsukasa beginning, Umori no Tsukasa needless to say, down to the hundred officials, celebrating today's auspicious ceremony with upturned eyes, the voices of applause also bustling, that joy echoing and resounding to Heaven and Earth, piercing the clouds of the great sky, and to the plain of Wada, echoing deeply to the bottom of the waters, throughout the inner and outer parts of the country in all four directions without a nook, there must be none who do not look up with reverence at this new pledge between husband and wife. Truly, of the auspicious reign of the lord, to a thousand generations and ten thousand generations without limit, cranes dancing playfully in the august sky, turtles floating upon the august pond, celebrating the many reigns of the sovereign, to serve him is auspicious. Though the morning sun shine, though it cloud over, though the moon wax, though it wane, though Heaven become Earth, though Earth become Heaven, how could the sincerity of the sovereign ever change through thousands of generations? Truly serving the precious thirty-five divine teachings, even the august sky is pure, Harukuniwake, the rare propaganda envoy, standing between the two, just as in the ancient days of the gods, newly beginning the path of marriage—how precious! The divine majesty also like the Takasago pine branches at the ridge's end, matching white hair, however far the piling deep snow may melt not, the path of husband and wife, ah, Kamunagara, kami, kami, rejoicing and taking courage in the divine grace, we pray and revere.'

原文（近代文語）

ビクトリア城の客殿には刹帝利、ヒルナ姫を始め治国別の一行、及び内事司のタルマン、左守、右守を始めハルナ、カルナ姫、並びに数多の役員が列を正し、結婚式が行はれた。此事誰云ふとなく城下に拡がり、寄ると触るとレコード破りの結婚だと云つて、話の花が長屋の裏迄咲いてゐた。さうして政治大改革の象徴だと国民一同に期待されたのである。ここに治国別の媒介にて神前結婚の式も恙なく相済んだ。

それから刹帝利、ヒルナ姫は治国別に厚く礼を述べ吾居間に帰つた。後に新夫婦を始め一同の祝宴が開かれた。

治国別は祝歌を歌ふ。

治国別『神代の昔伊邪那岐の 皇大神は伊邪那美の 神と諸共高天原にて 天の御柱巡り会ひ 妹背の道を結びまし 山川草木の神までも 完全に委曲に生み玉ひ 此世を安く美はしく 造り給ひし雄々しさよ その神術に習ひまし ビクトリア城の奥の間で 時代に目覚めたアールさま 上下の障壁撤回し 耕奴の家に生れます ハンナの姫と合衾の 式を挙げさせ玉ひしは 之ぞ全く天地の 尊き神の御心に かなひ奉りし吉例ぞ 尊き卑しき差別をば

神の御子たる人草に つけて待遇に差別をば 作ると云ふは皇神の 心を知らぬ曲業ぞ 一陽来復時臻り 至仁至愛の大神の 大御心のそのままに 妹背の道を開きまし 国人等に其範を 示させ玉ふ尊さよ かくなる上は国民は 王をば誠の親となし 主と崇め師となして 心の底より真心を 捧げて仕へまつるべし 神が表に現はれて 善と悪とを立別ける 此世を造りし神直日 心も広き大直日 只何事も人の世は

直日に見直し聞直し 世の過ちは宣り直す 皇大神の御前に 今まで道に違ひたる 形式差別を撤回し 上下心を一にし 御国のために国民が 力を協せ心をば 一になして君の辺を 弥永久に楽しみて 守り仕へむ惟神 神は嘸々此式を 諾ひまして永久に 妹背の道を守りまし ビクの国をば弥栄に 栄え賑せ玉ふべし ああ惟神々々 神の御前に誠心を 捧げて祝ひ奉る』

左守司は金扇を開き自ら踊り自ら謡ふ。

左守（謡曲）『ああ有難や尊やな、掛巻も綾に畏き天地の、皇大神の神勅もて、ビクの国に鎮まり居ます、刹帝利、ビクトリア王の、初めての御子と在れませる、王子アールの君に、耕奴の家に生れ玉ひし、心雄々しき才女と、鴛鵞の衾の永久に、睦み親しみ妹と背の、道を開き玉ひたる、これの御式の尊さよ。仮令首陀の家に生れたりとも、誠に明かき賢女を、娶らせ玉ふ若君は、天地開けし其時より、例もあらぬ珍の御子、賢しき御子に在しまして、上と下との隔を絶ち、下国民を憐みまし、美はしき政を開かせ玉ふ、端緒ぞと左守の司を始めとし、右守司は云ふも更、百の司に至るまで、今日の芽出度き御式をば、仰ぎ喜び拍手の声も賑しく、その喜びは天地に、響き渡りて大空の、雲をつきぬき和田の原、水底深く響き渡り、四方八方の国の内外隈もなく、此新しき妹と背の御契を、仰がぬものぞなかるべし。実にも芽出度き君が代の、千代万代も極みなく、鶴は御空に舞ひ遊び、亀は御池に浮びつつ、君が幾代を祝ぎて、仕へまつるぞ芽出度けれ。朝日は照るとも曇るとも、月は盈つとも虧くるとも、天は地となり地は天となるとも、君が誠は幾千代も、変らせ玉ふ事ぞあるべき。実にも尊き三五の、神の教に仕へます、御空も清く治国別の、珍の宣伝使、二人の仲に立たせ玉ひ、神代の例そのままに、婚嫁の道を新しく、始め玉ひし尊さよ、神の御稜威も高砂の、尾上の松の友白髪、積もる深雪の何処迄も、溶けずにあれや妹と背の道、ああ惟神々々、恩頼を喜び勇み願ぎ奉る』

§ 2

Having finished chanting, he took his seat. Umorinokami chants again.

Umori: 'As the heavenly Ototanabata trails her string of precious jewel-beads, ah, what precious beads, flashing twofold across the valley, even to the divine august form of the god Ajisuki Takahikone shall they be compared! The wondrous child, Prince R, embraced by divine grace, now dwells here with his ideal wife, taking Princess Hanna in marriage. Tonight, auspiciously, may they consummate their union completely and thoroughly, and with the winds and waves of the four seas at peace, laying the august foundation of a sovereign's reign even where branches stir not, may they walk the path of matrimony. Substituting for the gods of heaven and earth, may they govern our people in peace—even as the heavenly pillar and the earthly pillar standing side by side in this mansion, clearing away serenely, how reverend it is! I, Umorinokami the Kamuzukasa, though yet a newcomer, know this to be a matter of truth for the sake of the sovereign and the country; even should I cast away my life, I shall serve before the young lord and before Princess Hanna. Ah, you two divine spouse and consort, regarding the Umarisukasa and all the hundred officials as your true household slaves, reveal and command whatever matters may arise without reservation! As rulers and subjects live in harmony, the auspicious signs manifest in tonight's sky; the moonlight is clear, and even the stars today gleam and sparkle brighter than ever, blessing and guarding the future of the heir to the throne. Without fierce winds or malicious rains, the five grains ripen abundantly, peace prevails throughout the land, and the realm is accomplished. Revering and worshiping the heavenly and earthly deities, serving well the lord who is father and mother, pitying the lower folk, and spreading a beautiful and pure government, O sage sovereign,' thus chanted, 'protect the land of Biku granted by the gods unto all eternity.' Swearing to the gods, Umorinokami respectfully and devoutly prays before the two young lords. Though the morning sun shine or grow cloudy, though the moon wax or wane, though the stars fall from the sky, though the earth tremble and the mountains rend, and though there be an age when the seas dry up—towards our sovereign, could I ever harbor a two-hearted mind? To the utmost of my heart and the limits of my body, offering my body as a sacrifice, for the sake of the sovereign and the world, worshiping and serving the pure and reverend deity of Misuma, revering the sovereign's august words, I shall approach the lower myriad people. Do you two young lords rest in peace. Umorinokami, dedicating his sincere heart before the Great August Ancestral Deity of Heaven and Earth, celebrates today's joyous occasion, ah, Kannagara Kamugami, may your august spirits bless and prosper us!'

Taruman chants again.

Taruman: 'At the foot of Mount Victor, raising high the great palace pillars, worshiping the Great August Ancestral Deity, the Kshatriya of the land of Biku who dwells as the king of the country, serving the benevolent sovereign, I, Taruman of the Naigijikasa, from the bottom of my heart rejoice, take courage, and celebrate today's joyous event. Accompanying the wondrous children of the Kamuzukasa of the Misuma Teachings, the Propaganda Envoy Kunigiribwake, Matsuhiko, Tatsuhiko, and Mankou, from the very time they descended from heaven, the ugly clouds and mists clogging this inner castle were blown away without a trace, and ten thousand hardships vanished like the spring wind of the Kamado gate, leaving the grasses and trees of the fields growing lush and green together, while the mountain edges on all sides smiled, oh, how auspicious! At such a time, the Kshatriya, dwelling as the heir to the throne, Prince R and the other five imperial children, unharmed and resting in peace by the grace of the great deity, while our lord and officials have not even time to rejoice and make merry, today once again the auspicious ceremony of union is performed, building the foundation of a thousand ages—how precious are these auspicious signs! In the sovereign's reign where the plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds come all at once, and the pine greenery of the ever-verdant tree blooms and sends forth its fragrance luxuriantly, even the voices of the cranes nesting amidst thick branches are exceedingly brave as they call out for a thousand ages, while sparrows and skylarks together, as if celebrating this fresh joyous occasion, sang with valiant voices. Ah, Kannagara Kamugami, the grace of the gods is right before our eyes; the heart of our sovereign, which until now has suffered, melts like ice in the spring sun, becoming just like cherry blossoms blooming all at once. The august forms of the spouse and consort, who may be compared to flowers and butterflies, are awe-inspiring to behold—are they heavenly beings who pushed aside the manifold cloud-paths of the great sky and descended, or the descent of heavenly maidens? It is an auspicious sight to behold; with rejoicing courage, I offer my heartfelt celebrations before the august presence. Though the morning sun shine or grow cloudy, though the moon wax or wane, though the great earth sink, the power of sincerity saves the world; upholding sincerity alone, to the utmost bounds of the land of Biku, high and low uniting their hearts, may you guard us, Kannagara. Respectfully and devoutly I pray before the august presence of the young lord, ah, Kannagara Kamugami, may your august spirits bless and prosper us!'

Having finished chanting, he took his seat.

原文（近代文語）

と謡ひ終つて座についた。右守司は又謡ふ。

右守（謡曲）『天なるや乙棚機のうながせる、玉の御統瓊御統瓊に、あな玉はや、みたにふたわたらす、あぢしき高彦根の神の、その御神姿にも比ぶべき、珍の御子なるアールの君、神の恵みに抱かれて、ここに理想の妻と在れませる、ハンナの姫を娶らせ玉ひ、今宵芽出度く合衾を、完全に委曲に挙げさせ玉ひ、四海波風静にて、枝も鳴らさぬ君の代の、その礎と畏くも、婚嫁の道を行はせ玉ひ、天地の神に代らせ玉ひて、吾国民を心安く、治め玉はむ天の御柱、国の御柱とこれの館に並ばして、すみきり玉ふぞ尊けれ。吾は右守の神司、まだ新参の身なれども、君の御為国の為、誠の事と知るなれば、仮令生命は捨つるとも、仕へまつらむ若君の御前、ハンナの姫の御前に、ああ二柱の妹と背の君よ、左守司を始めとし、その外百の司等を、誠の家の奴と思召され、如何なる事も打明けて、吩ひ咐け玉へ宣らせ給へ、上下睦ぶ君が代の、瑞祥示す今宵の空、月の光もさやかにて、星さへ今日は何時もより、光りも強くきらめき渡り、世継の君の行末を、祝ぎ守らせ玉ふなり、荒き風もなく悪き雨もなく、五穀は豊に実のり、天下太平国土成就、天神地祇を崇め祀り、父と母との君によく仕へまし、下国民を憐れみて、美はしき清き政を、布かせ玉へ聖の君と謡はれて、神の賜ひしビクの国を、弥永久に守らせ玉へ、神に誓ひて右守の司、若君二柱の御前に、慎み敬ひ願ぎ奉る。朝日は照るとも曇るとも、月は盈つとも虧くるとも、星は空より墜つるとも、地は震ひ山は裂け、海はあせなむ世ありとも、君に対して二心、吾あらめやも、心の限り身の限り、身を犠牲に奉り、君の御為世の為に、清き尊き三五の、神を拝み仕へまつり、君の御言を畏みて、下万民に臨みまつらむ、二柱の若君心安くましませよ。右守の司が天地の、皇大神の御前に、誠心捧げ今日の慶事を、寿ぎ奉る、ああ惟神々々、御霊幸はひましませよ』

タルマンは又謡ふ。

『ビクトル山の山麓に 大宮柱太知りて 皇大神を奉りつつ 国の王と在れませる ビクの御国の刹帝利 仁慈の君に仕へたる 内事司のタルマンが 今日の慶事を心より 喜び勇み祝ぎ奉る 三五教の神司 治国別の宣伝使 松彦竜彦万公の 珍の御子をば伴ひて 天降りましたるその時ゆ 此城内に塞がれる 醜の雲霧あともなく 吹き払はれて千万の 艱みは科戸の春風に 散り行くあとは青々と 野辺の草木は茂り合ひ

四方の山辺はニコニコと 笑ひ初めたる芽出度さよ かかる時しも刹帝利 世継の君と在れませる アールの御子を始めとし その外五人の御子等は 恙もあらず大神の 恵みに安く帰りまし 吾君始め司等 喜び歓ぐ間もあらず 又もや今日は合衾の 芽出度き式を挙げられて 千代の礎を築きます その瑞祥ぞ有難き 三千世界の梅の花 一度に来る常磐木の 松の緑もシンシンと 花咲き匂ふ君が御代 枝も茂りて鬱蒼と 巣ぐへる鶴の声さへも

いと勇ましく千代と呼ぶ 雀雲雀も諸共に 今度の慶事を祝ふ如 声勇ましく歌ひけり ああ惟神々々 神の恵は目のあたり 今迄悩ませ玉ひたる 君の心は春の日の 氷と解けて桜花 一度に咲き出す如くなり 花と蝶とに譬ふべき 妹背の君の御姿 仰ぐも畏し大空の 八重の雲路を掻き別けて 下り玉ひし天人か 天津乙女の降臨か 見るも芽出度き御姿 喜び勇み御前に 真心こめて祝ぎ奉る 朝日は照るとも曇るとも

と歌ひ終つて座に着きにける。
