鬼の恋〔1375〕

§ 1

Oniharuwake straightened his posture with solemnity, gripped the hilt of his sword with his left hand, and stood in a style like a rising newt.

Oniharuwake: 'Kumehiko-dono, bringing a woman into the camp is something that cannot be permitted from the standpoint of military law. Why do you bring such a beauty into the camp, forget your military duties, and perform such mad behavior?'

Kumehiko: 'Yes, if you are going to blame me for violating military law and bringing in a woman, then there is no help for it. I shall take responsibility and resign from this moment on.'

Oniharuwake was startled in his heart, thinking it would be a serious matter if General Kumehiko were to resign now, yet he remained composed and said,

Oniharuwake: 'If it is your wish to resign, Kiden, I shall allow your resignation; however, I cannot permit it today. Drive this woman out as soon as possible.'

Kumehiko: 'If I am to drive this woman out, I shall take my leave from this moment on.'

Princess Karuna, determining in her heart to scheme that this was the moment to cause conflict between the two and to disrupt the Brahman army from its foundations, said,

Princess Karuna: 'Oh my, oh my, the valiant, energetic, dignified, and manly, most beloved, and renowned hero and great man, General Oniharuwake-sama, whose influence is like the sun and moon, I meet you for the first time. I do not know if it is improper under military law for a woman to enter the camp, but I did not visit the camp of my own volition. As I was coming with a friend to worship at the forest of the God-King of Mount Victor, I encountered your subordinates, was kicked down on the roadside, and was left dazed. There are truly some ruthless warriors in this world. Then, the officers of Emishi and Marta passed by and kindly helped me and sent me here. I shall never forget that kindness. This must surely be due to the General's daily training and excellent leadership. I am grateful that I was saved entirely by the reflected glory of General Oniharuwake. If you would kindly use your merciful heart to let me rest in this camp, I will massage your shoulders and cook your meals. Even in a camp where military law is strict, it must be inconvenient for a General who commands three armies to be without a woman. If I were a common soldier, that would be one thing, but for a person of such noble status, even if it is in the military, a life of celibacy is a truly regrettable thing.'

Oniharuwake took Princess Karuna's flattering words at face value, suddenly changed his attitude, and his Yama-like face instantly transformed into a Jizo-like face. Then, speaking gently to Karuna, he said,

Oniharuwake: 'Indeed, as you say. General Kumehiko says he will resign, and if so, I feel it is most inconvenient to command this army all by myself. Judging by your appearance, you seem to have received a higher education, so there is no reason why a woman could not serve as a General. From this moment on, I shall have you succeed General Kumehiko and appoint you as a female General. There is a precedent where Izanagi-no-Ōkami used female Generals of Pine, Bamboo, and Plum in the battle of Yomotsuhirasaka and achieved a great victory. I do not know if it violates military law to keep you as a wife, but standing as a General and devoting yourself to military secrets is not a violation of military law.'

He spun out this self-serving logic and began to harbor ambitions to make Princess Karuna his own.

Princess Karuna: 'Yes, thank you very much. I liked that General Kumehiko-sama was in the military register as a General, so in truth, I had entered into a temporary engagement, but I have completely lost interest in a weak-willed, modern man who would resign his position as a General for the sake of a woman in a one-in-a-thousand situation. Please, General Oniharuwake-sama, I am a pitiful woman, so please, please cherish me. In return, I will do my absolute best and intend not to bring shame upon the mission.'

Kumehiko, in a flurry, acted as if to cover Karunahime's mouth.

Kumehiko: 'No, no, Karunadono, I shall never resign. Set your mind at ease. It is only because General Oniharuwake interferes so much with my renai that I became heated and spoke of resigning. Is there any bakamono who would so easily throw away a chii built up to this point? Think about it well. Come now, be at anshin; though I may look this way, I am chinyū. Hahahaha!'

Karunahime: 'Ah, is that so? Hearing that, I am relieved. If that is the case, let us yoyaku to become fūfu. Tell me, General Kumehiko-sama.'

Oniharuwake, with a sullen face, began to get angry again.

Oniharuwake: 'No matter how much General Kumehiko may wish to remain in his gunshiyoku, as long as I, the sōshireikuwan, do not shiyōdaku, it is dame. Once a bushi has offered his resignation from his own lips, he surely cannot withdraw it; otherwise, he cannot be called a man. Spilled water does not return to the tray, and one cannot swallow spit once it is spat out. Therefore, I, Oniharuwake, shall decisively relieve Kumehiko of his post and appoint Karunahime as vice-general. Kumehiko-dono, throw down your sword, change out of your military uniform, and retreat from this place at once.'

Kumehiko: 'This is unreasonable. For what fault do you intend to strip me of my generalship, which was appointed by Daikoku-nushi? That is rather sen-etsu, is it not? To such violent orders, Kumehiko will absolutely not submit.'

He protested in a sharp voice.

Oniharuwake: 'No matter what you say, the order of a superior is the order of Daikoku-nushi. Do not dally, and retreat. Karunadono, what do you think? My authority is such that even a general like Kumehiko can be swayed by a single word of mine. Wahahahaha!'

Karunahime: 'Indeed, you are truly a desirable general-sama. If it were you, I would even give my life. You are a possessor of absolute, infinite power, truly endowed with dignified majesty.'

Kumehiko, seeing that the situation was becoming increasingly unfavorable, became desperate and glared at Oniharuwake. He placed his hand on the hilt of his sword, determined to strike him down with a single blow and, if possible, become the commander-in-chief himself, waiting for an opening. Oniharuwake, sensing that Kumehiko's state was not normal, did not let his guard down; he placed his hand on his hilt, ready to strike if approached, and they stood still like the Nio at a temple gate, holding their breath in mutual tension. Seeing this state, Karuna said:

Karunahime: 'Hohoho, what dignified figures you both cut. Whichever I look at, it is like choosing between an iris and an iris, or taking the first or the second. Flower and flower, moon and moon, you are both such splendid men. Ah, if only I had two bodies, I would follow General Oniharuwake with one and serve General Kumehiko with the other. This floating world is not as one wishes.'

Oniharuwake and Kumehiko were both deeply captivated by Karuna's beauty. Unable to gauge Karuna's spirit, they remained standing like Nio, only their eyes darting about. Just then, with a clamor, Oniharuwake's adjutant, Suparu, arrived carrying a beautiful woman.

Suparu: 'Excuse me, Oniharuwake-sama. I obtained this beauty in the camp. Thinking she might be of use to you, I have brought her here. The battle within the castle is a great victory for our side, and there is no longer any cause for concern. Please examine this woman thoroughly and dispose of her as you see fit.'

He spoke with great courtesy. Oniharuwake was startled by this voice, and when he looked at the woman's face, she was a beauty several degrees superior to Karunahime. She possessed a natural nobility, moist black eyes, and a charm capable of tormenting any man. Oniharuwake completely forgot his dispute with Kumehiko.

久米彦は軍刀をヒラリと抜いて、矢庭に鬼春別に斬りつけた。鬼春別はヒラリと体をかはし傍の軍刀取るより早く又もやスラリと引抜き、カチヤカチヤと刃を合せ火花を散らし、数十合に及んだ。されど何れも手利きと手利き、竜虎の争ひ、何時果つべしとも見えなかつた。此物音に驚いて、カルナ姫はヒルナ姫の身の上を気づかひ、慌ただしく飛んで来た。ヒルナはカルナの顔を見るより、目を以て合図をし、……キツと仲に這入るな……といふ意味を牒した、そして二人の美人はワザと怖相に室の隅に机をかぶつて慄うてゐる、そして……何方か一人が……早くやられたら都合が好いがと、心の中に念じてゐた。

斯かる所へ、スパール、エミシの両人は帰り来り、此態を見て驚き、二人は中にわつて入り、

スパール『モシ鬼春別将軍様、少時お待ち下さいませ』

エミシ『久米彦将軍様　暫く暫く』

と大手を拡げてつつ立つた。これ幸と両人は剣を鞘にをさめ、ハアハアと息を凝らし乍ら、俄作りの椅子に腰をおろした。

ヒルナ姫、カルナ姫はヤツと安心したものの如く、ワザと不安な面をし乍ら、ハアハアハアと息を喘ませ、胸を撫で下ろし居たりける。
