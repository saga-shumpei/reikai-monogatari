Chapter 10: 女丈夫 [1373]

女丈夫〔1373〕

§ 1

Startled by Kanto's Haukoku, the assembly lowered the curtain of silence for a while. As the proverb says, even if brothers fight on the mast, they defend against insults from outside; and there is no child who fights within the house when the father is dead... how true these words are. Hearing that the crisis of the nation was imminent, even the usually stubborn Umori forgot his previous Sōron in a flash, and began to fret only about how to defend against the foreign enemy. Umori opened his mouth in a hurry,

Umori: 'Setsuteirisama, the crisis of the nation is upon us. To stir up internal strife at such a time is most Furiekisenban. For the sake of our Kimisama and the country, I, Umori, shall bend all my arguments and leave everything to our Kimisama, Hirunahime-sama, and Samoridono. Dōzo, I pray for your Otorihaka.'

At this sudden change in his Aisatsu, Victoria-ō finally raised her face,

Setsuteiri: 'Thy Magokoro has just been revealed. A person's true heart is not known until the moment of crisis. Come, from now on, Samori, Umori, and Taruman, prepare for defense in unity.'

Hirunahime: 'Samoridono, what say you? Will you not lead the three armies, join forces with Umoridono, and head out to defend?'

Samori: 'Hai, I have understood the details. Then, from this moment, Umoridono, let us divide the army into two, and I shall take charge of one unit.'

Umori: 'This is outrageous. How could you, who have no experience in military science, do such a thing? Leave this defense to me. Without moving a single soldier, I shall settle this through negotiations between the barrels and the tripods.'

Into such a scene, Emu entered in a flurry as the second messenger. Emu prostrated himself before the assembly, and while wiping away his sweat, said,

Emu: 'I report to you. The enemy is an overwhelming army; under the command of the Brahmin brave generals, Oniharubake and Kumehiko, they are rushing in with several thousand cavalry. They have already destroyed the front gate, burned the camp, and set fire to the civilian houses. If we delay, it will be a great disaster; we must prepare for defense as soon as possible. I shall stake my life and fight to the limit, casting away my life for the sake of our Kimisama. You must prepare quickly.'

Before he had finished speaking, he ran out with the speed of Idaten and vanished into nowhere.

Samori: 'Now that it has come to this, I do not wish to go against your will and continue our Naifun. If so, I shall protect the person of our Kimisama, so you should lead the three armies from here and fight brilliantly; this must be the time to show the martial arts you have trained in daily. Come, quickly, prepare yourselves.'

Though he urged him, Umori-shi remained composed and showed no sign of moving.

Hirunahime: 'Umoridono, in this national crisis, will you not start the defense as soon as possible?'

Umori: 'I cannot believe these are the words of Hirunahime-sama. The enemy is an overwhelming army, and the number of victory and defeat is already decided. Rather than exposing the corpses of brave warriors on the battlefield, it is the secret of military strategy to leave it to the enemy's trampling for a while and demonstrate extreme non-resistance to make the enemy go "Afun." The divine schemes and devilish plans I have stored in my heart will be unleashed in an instant; for now, just wait. Haste makes waste; one cannot handle a nation without the magnanimity to have leisure days like a hero. Ahahahaha.'

He remained excessively calm, as if he had something in mind. In truth, Umori was a coward at heart and had already lost his nerve. However, in front of Hirunahime and the other dignitaries, he could not admit that he was so frightened that his legs had given way; yet, if he handed the army to Samori, the power of military command would never return to his hands. If he were to go out and show his bravery, his legs were already weak. Furthermore, there was no prospect of victory. A cunning thought flashed through his mind: rather than fighting recklessly and suffering defeat, which would ruin his reputation, if he did not draw his sword, the outcome might not be known, and something might work out somehow... Hirunahime had a weakness in her heart, so she could not scold Umori-shi severely, and she reached the depths of agony and distress. Setsuteiri was anxious.

カントの報告に打驚いて、一同は暫く沈黙の幕を下ろした。諺にも兄弟檣にせめぐ共、外其侮りを防ぐとかや、父死して家にせめぐ子なし、……とは宜なるかな。国家の危急存亡目睫の間に迫れるを聞いて、流石の右守も今迄の争論をケロリと忘れ、兎も角外敵を防がむとのみに焦慮し出した。右守は慌てて口を開き、

右守『刹帝利様、国家の危急、目睫に迫りました。斯様な時に内紛を醸すのは最も不利益千万で厶います。此右守は君の為、国の為、一切の主張を曲げて、吾君様、ヒルナ姫様、左守殿に一任致します、何卒よきに御取計らひを願ひませう』

と打つて変つた挨拶に、ビクトリア王は漸く顔をあげ、

刹帝利『汝の赤心は只今現はれた。人は愈の時にならねば本心の分らぬものだ。サア是から左守、右守、タルマン、一致の上防ぎの用意を致されよ』

ヒルナ姫『左守殿、如何で厶る。其方は三軍を率ゐ、右守殿と力を協せ、防戦にお向ひなさらぬか』

左守『ハイ、委細承知仕りました。然らば之より、右守殿、軍隊を二手に分ち、其一班を拙者が預りませう』

右守『これは怪しからぬ、軍学に経験なき其方、左様な事が如何して出来ませうか。此防戦は拙者にお任せ下され。一兵も動かさずして、樽爼折衝の間に解決をつけてみせませう』

かかる所へ第二の使者として、慌ただしく入り来るはエムであつた。エムは一同の前に平伏し、汗を拭ひ乍ら、

『御注進申上げます、敵は目に余る大軍、バラモンの勇将、鬼春別、久米彦両将軍指揮の下に数千騎を以て押寄せ来り、忽ち表門を破壊し、陣営を焼払い、民家に火を放ちました、時遅れては一大事、一時も早く防戦の用意あつて然るべし。いで某は、命を的にあらむ限りの奪戦を致し、君の為に一命を捨て申さむ。早く御用意あつて然るべし』

と言ふより早く、韋駄天走りに駆け出だし、何処ともなく消え失せたり。

左守『只今となつて、拙者は貴殿の意思に反き、内紛を続くる事を好み申さぬ。然らば吾君の御身辺の保護を仕るべければ、貴殿は之より三軍を率ゐ、華々しく戦ひめされ、日頃鍛へし武術の手並、現はし玉ふは此時ならむ。サ早く早く御用意あれ』

とすすむれど、右守司は泰然として動きさうにもない。

ヒルナ姫『右守殿、国家危急の場合、一時も早く防戦におかかりなさらぬか』

右守『これはこれはヒルナ姫様のお言葉とも覚えませぬ。敵は目にあまる大軍、勝敗の数は既に決してをりまするぞ。あたら勇士の屍を戦場に曝すよりも、暫く敵の蹂躙に任し、極端に無抵抗主義を発揮して、敵をしてアフンと致さすが兵法の奥義で厶る。右守が胸中に貯へたる神算鬼謀を発揮するは瞬く内、まづまづお待たせあれ。急いては事を仕損ずる、英雄閑日月あり程の度量がなくては国家を処理する事は出来ますまいぞ。アハハハハ』

とクソ落着きに落着き、何か心に期する所あるものの如くなりき。其実右守は実際の卑怯者で早くも腰を抜かしてゐたのである。併し乍らヒルナ姫及其他並ゐる歴々の手前、驚いて腰が抜けたといふ訳にも行かず、さりとて軍隊を左守に渡せば、再び兵馬の権は吾手に還つて来ない。出でて武勇を現はさむとすれば、已に腰が抜けてゐる。又勝算の見込がない。なまじいに戦つて敗北をなし、自分の沽券を堕すよりも、太刀を抜かざれば、其勝劣が分らないであらう、何とかならうから……といふズルイ考へが咄嗟に起つた。ヒルナ姫は心に弱点があるので、右守司に対して厳しく叱咤する事が出来ず、実に煩悶苦悩の極に達した。刹帝利は心焦ち、

Speaker: 'Aiya, Umoridono, stand up quickly! Is it not for such a time of need that you have been training your army day after day? Is this not the moment for you to manifest your martial prowess? Come, quickly, quickly!'

They urged him on. Samori also drew near and said,

Speaker: 'Umoridono, come forth quickly. If you are in disagreement, I shall take charge of the army and set out for the defense. Let me hear your reply at once.'

Pressed by both sides, Umori did not answer a word; he remained slumped, his hips having given way, shaking his head from side to side.

Karunahime drew close to his side and said,

Speaker: 'Brother, this is the very moment to console the heart of our Lord and to manifest your loyalty. Given the bows and arrows you have kept adorned, if you do not act at such a time as this, it will be a disgrace to your martial house.'

Umori: 'Eeh, the meddling of a shallow woman! Do not bother me; Umori has his own plan as Umori. How should a sparrow know the ambition of a great roc? Get out of my sight!'

So saying, he turned his volcanic crater toward his younger sister.

Karunahime: 'Eeh, pathetic brother! To think you have boasted until today, calling yourself the Umori-tsukasa. To think there could be such a cowardly and irresolute brother—Karunahime is mortified. Very well, from this moment, I, Karuna, shall command the three armies and head to the front. Farewell, brother.'

Before she could finish speaking, she attempted to stand and leave, but Umori gripped Karuna's hand tightly, his eyes blazing with rage, and said,

Speaker: 'Hey, sister! What is the meaning of a woman attempting to head to the battlefield? Is this not an act of overstepping your authority?'

Karunahime: 'Eeh, at a time like this, what talk is there of overstepping authority or iron fists? From the top, starting with Taruman, down to the last common soldier, we must unite our strength and hearts to protect the Royal House and the nation. Now, now, let go of me!'

Though she struggled, Karunahime's slender arm, held by his great strength, would not easily break free. Since it was her left hand that was being held, Karunahime used her right hand to draw her dagger from its sheath and stabbed Umori in the upper arm. Startled by the sudden spray of blood and the pain, he released his grip. Karunahime said,

Speaker: 'Harunadono, come, let us go. Prepare for the defense with me. My Lord, Hirunahime-sama, may you be safe.'

So saying, she dashed off at full speed.

Kshatriya: 'You, Umori-tsukasa, of extreme insolence! To think that at such a time, you would be so cowardly and irresolute as to make no preparations for defense. You are a traitor, unfilial and wicked. I shall strike you down with a single stroke of my blade. Prepare yourself!'

He drew his long sword with a flourish and was about to strike when Hirunahime clung to the King's arm and said,

Speaker: 'My Lord, please wait a moment. It is I who am at fault; I shall confess every sin here. If you must strike down Umori, I beg you to take my life before his. And there is something I must tell you before I die. This Umori, while wearing a mask of loyalty on the surface and commanding many troops, has been working behind the scenes to incite the people, stir up riots in various regions, and wait for the situation to become unmanageable. He harbors the ambition to force you, my King, into retirement and take your place, to become the Kshatriya himself. I have been acting in the shadows and in the light, trying to make him repent of this ambition, and in order to save the Royal House, I engaged in an unrighteous relationship with him. This, too, was entirely out of a single-minded devotion to the Royal House and from the shallow heart of a woman; I have walked a path a woman should not tread. For this sin of infidelity, which deserves ten thousand deaths, I beg you, please take my life first, and then deal with Umori as you see fit.'

Upon hearing this, Kshatriya's hair stood on end with rage. Thinking he would strike down Hirunahime with a single blow, he instead threw his sword onto the floor, sat down heavily, folded his arms, and shedding tears, he spoke.

Setsuteiri: 'Hirunahime, I am pleased by your Kokorozuka. Your Seichiu, which led you to walk a path that a woman should not tread in order to protect the Ōke, is truly beyond Kantan. However, now that I have heard your Jihaku, I can no longer keep you as my consort. Though it is Kaaisau, I must sever our bond as Fūfu. Nevertheless, continue to serve the Ōke as you have done Izen; I am truly grateful for your Magokoro.'

He stroked Hirunahime's back to comfort her. Moved by the King's Aijiyō, Hirunahime could not remain standing or sitting, and faster than one could draw a Kuwaiken, she attempted to thrust it into her own throat; Taruman, being Mezato, saw this, gripped the Hime's hand tightly, and with tears said,

Taruman: 'Himesama, now that you have received the permission of my Lord, this is no place to commit Jisatsu in a time of Kokuka Kikifu. Since you have made such a Kakugo, why not preserve your Mei for one more Hitoiki for the sake of the Ōke, rush into the enemy's Jinchiu, and even if it is but Hitori, torment the enemy and die a brave Uchijini? If you do so, the death-flower of Himesama will bloom, and as a brave female warrior, your fragrant name will be passed down for a thousand years; please, for the moment, refrain from this.'

He remonstrated with her through his tears. The Hime nodded and said,

Hirunahime: 'Ah, indeed, just as you say, I shall rush into the Jinchiu for the sake of the King and cast away my life. If I were to end my life here now, it would be no different from a dog's death, and it is regrettable to have my ugly name spread to future generations as a woman of unrighteousness and infidelity. Ah, what a good point you have brought to my attention.'

She regained her composure, suddenly prepared her armor, tightened her headband with a dignified air, tucked her naginata under her arm, and headed toward the gate, rushing out with quick steps all alone; her bravery was such. The King watched her back as she departed, pressed his hands together, and prayed, 'May the Banko Shin-ō protect her,' while also praying that the Hime would achieve glorious feats of arms and return in a magnificent triumphal procession. As for Samamori-tsukasa, being of old age, he served close to the King by the King's command. Taruman said,

Taruman: 'I, too, shall head to the battlefield from here, strike a blow, and chill the livers of the enemy; my Lord, farewell.'

Leaving these words behind, he prepared his armor and ran off toward the front at full speed.
