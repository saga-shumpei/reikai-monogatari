Chapter 9: 蛙の腸 [1372]

蛙の腸〔1372〕

§ 1

In the Okuden of King Bikutoria, seven persons formed a proper line: beginning with the King himself, Hirunahime, Taruman who served as minister of Naiji and Sendenshi, Kyūbitto of the Left Guard, Berutsu of the Right Guard, Haruna, and Karunahime. Having recognized the dawn of Waboku through the Ketsukon between the Riyōke of the Left Guard and the Right Guard, they were invited by the King to express their Shiyukui, and thus took their seats at this Isū Shiyuen. First, Samorinokami bowed once to the King, and offered polite Aisatsu in due order.

Left Guard: 'Starting with our Kimisama, through the deep Gojinji of Hirunahime-sama, you have given Karunahime, the sister of Umoridono whose Meisei is renowned, to my foolish son in marriage. Truly, it is a Mujiyō Kuōei not only for the Left Guard to speak of, but also for my son. Notwithstanding this, today you have also opened a Konnichi Seidai Enkuwai and expended your heart for our sake; that Gojinji we shall not forget for Shiusei. Above this, even though we cast away our lives, we intend to devote our sincere hearts for the sake of our sovereign and country, and repay one ten-thousandth of your grace.'

Kshatriya: 'Indeed, just as you say, this time it was truly a strange destiny. This too is entirely the thought of Banko Shin'ō-sama, and proof that God has not yet abandoned the land of Biku and the Bikutoria family; this side too is truly satisfied. Until Konnichi, the Left Guard family and the Right Guard family were more than dogs and monkeys, constantly continuing their secret feuds. Concerning this, this side was extremely troubled in head. Now that things have come to this, if you proceed along the single path of civil and military affairs, harmonizing hearts and uniting strength, superior and inferior being in accord, we shall be able to protect the nation, bring peace to the people, and invite the divine government of Miroku.'

Thus he returned the Aisatsu with great joy. The Left Guard bowed down deeply with a gasp, drowned in tears of gladness. The Right Guard became overbearing and said:

Right Guard: 'Just now, my Lord said, "Proceed along the single path of civil and military affairs," but the Left Guard family is a house of literature, and the Right Guard family is a house of martial arts; at their foundation, their duties differ, and they are inherently family lineages like ice and charcoal that cannot be reconciled. Nevertheless, in private social intercourse, we are relatives who cannot be severed, so increasing our degree of intimacy beyond the past, the Riyōke will achieve harmony. In the first place, military force is a necessary organ to protect the nation; a nation without military preparation is like a bird without wings, and its existence as a state can by no means be expected. Therefore, military force is necessary in times of emergency, while literature guides the people in peaceful times and is necessary in governing the world, which wise Kshatriya surely knows well. If one confuses the duties of the two houses of civil and military affairs when facing internal and diplomatic matters, rather a spirit of savagery will fill the world and bring about disturbance to the nation.'

Hirunahime: 'Although the opinion of Umoridono is reasonable for a moment, at a time like Konnichi when internal anxieties and external threats frequently arrive, that the civil and military houses join forces to protect the nation and bring peace to the people is a conduct suited to the times. Umoridono, we ask for your deep consideration.'

Right Guard: 'Oh dear, Hirunahime-sama, if by any chance the Left Guard family should grasp the authority of martial arts, being a person without experience in military science, the discipline of the military forces would not be properly maintained, and would it not rather sow the seeds of internal rebellion? A carpenter builds a house, a plasterer plasters a wall, and an umbrella maker makes umbrellas; all possess their special characteristics according to their respective occupations. A plasterer does not know how to build a house, and a carpenter does not know how to plaster a wall; in the same way, a civil official does not understand martial arts, and much less could the authority to command the three armies be suddenly acquired. On the contrary, no matter how much the Right Guard of the martial family directs its mind toward the field of literature, a complete result can by no means be obtained. Only when the two paths of civil and military stand side by side can the safety of the nation be preserved.'

Kshatriya: 'Hold on, Umoridono, such anxiety is unnecessary. From now on, as Kshatriya, I intend to receive anew the authority over military forces that my ancestors entrusted to your ancestors, and have the Left Guard and Right Guard take charge of the two paths of civil and military affairs. Surely there will be no disobedience?'

Umori: 'Whether our ancestors were entrusted with it, or whether they established this nation by installing Setsuteirisama, there is no record, and as it is a matter of the distant past, I do not understand it in the slightest. I was born into the warrior house of Umori, and I am one who has had the authority of military command transferred to me by my father; I recognize not even a whit of reason to return it to our sovereign, with all due respect.'

He said this with a flushed face and labored breath, and without even glancing at Setsuteiri, he spoke out in a boorish and insolent manner. Even the likes of Hirunahime and Taruman were taken aback, and they stared at the faces of both Samori and Umori. Taruman, as the minister of both propaganda and internal affairs, held a special position that placed him above both Samori and Umori. He opened his mouth for the first time.

Taruman: 'The words of Victoria Ōsama, the sovereign of the land of Biku, are what is known as the divine oracle of Kami. Until this day, the Umori house has usurped the authority of military command, troubled the Victoria house above, and squeezed the lifeblood of the people below; because of this, there is no end to the disturbances within the nation, and the momentum for revolution drifts throughout the country. If you do not heed the command of our sovereign now and return the authority of military command, the Umori house, having become the source of the people's resentment, will immediately face the tragic fate of annihilation, and it is clearer than seeing fire that this will eventually extend harm to the royal house. Umori-dono, I advise you to place your hand upon your heart, consider the trends of the times, and use your own brilliant intellect to adopt the best course of action. This is by no means a private statement of Taruman; it is the transmission of the oracle of Banko Shin-ō Shionagahiko-no-Mikoto.'

He declared this with firm resolve.

Umori glared sharply at Taruman.

Umori: 'I am not an Umori who would lend an ear to the words of one who has a name but no real power. I perceive that you have been won over by the Samori office, or perhaps you have conspired together to usurp the authority of military command from Umori, and eventually, by using the power of the military, you intend to coerce the people, wait for the time to destroy the Victoria house, and harbor the ambition that you and Samori will take their place; this is something that I, the discerning Umori, have foreseen. You scoundrel, attempting to invite disaster upon Setsuteirisama, get out!'

He roared back at him. Upon hearing this, the Samori official stood up indignantly.

Samori: 'This is an incomprehensible statement from Umori. Upon what evidence do you say such unreasonable things? If you have evidence, I would like to hear it.'

Umori: 'Ahahaha, to be as brazen as a villain is just like you. As for evidence, ask your own heart.

If one asks, one might answer that there are no demons,

But how would one answer if the heart were to ask?

This is the state of the hearts of the Samori official and the likes of Taruman. No matter how much you hide it, it is revealed in your countenances and your language.'

Samori: 'This is outrageous. Umori, you yourself cannot have any excuse if you are told that you harbor ambition. For to disobey the sovereign's command and to treat the authority of military command as one's own is something that completely threatens the royal house. If you, Umori, have even a shred of sincere heart that thinks of the royal house and thinks of the nation, why do you not return it to Victoria Ōsama, who is the sovereign of the nation?'

Umori: 'Do not concern yourself with that. The royal house is but an ornament of the land of Biku, and it is an unavoidable fact that all real power is held by the Umori house. No matter the royal house, and no matter the Samori house, while your status may be higher than that of the Umori house, it is natural that the real power of the nation lies in the hands of those who command the military. If I, Umori, had a shred of ambition, it would be a task easier than eating breakfast to command my military, destroy the royal house with all due respect, crush the Samori house, and become Setsuteiri myself. Is it not proof that I have no ambition that I, who possess such real power and authority, am content with a position lower than an old dotard like you and am serving faithfully? If one were to let a stubborn and foolish prime minister like Samori hold the authority of military command, it would be the same as giving a torch to a madman; it is extremely dangerous. Rice cakes to the rice cake maker, umbrellas to the umbrella maker, and clogs to the clog maker. Rather than harboring unattainable ambitions, it would be better for you to protect the position appropriate for the Samori house and devote yourself to the sovereign and the country.'

Hirunahime: 'Umori-dono, Samorinokami has absolutely no such Yashin whatsoever. Please interpret everything with Zeni, and let both houses work in Wachiukefudō for the sake of the Kunkoku; in this time of Kikifu for the Kokuka, please cease these trivial disputes and exert your strength together for the nation. Furthermore, given your Shinmitsu relationship as relatives, if Haruna and Karuna-himedono were to hear of the dispute between you Goriyōnin, it would surely be a painful matter. As you are a Kenmei Umori-dono, I wish for you to consider this with a Heisei mind.'

Umori: 'Haruna and Karuna are those who have married while brandishing Renaishijiyōshiyugi. Are these not, so to speak, Shitekikuwankei? Gunshiyoku is what is called the public institution of the Tenka; is it not a great crime against the laws of heaven and earth to confuse public office with the Shitekikuwankei of a husband and wife? Though this Umori may be foolish, I am not a man who does not understand such principles. Relatives are relatives, the nation is the nation, and duty is duty; they are clearly demarcated and have their own laws. As I perceive it, this has emerged from a scheme by Samorinokami and Taruman to plunder the power of arms from Umori out of their own Yashin, and it is clearly visible. Hime-sama, please do not worry. This Umori is not a rebel as Samori and Taruman fear. Since I shall protect the royal house and defend the nation with the army I have tamed, I hope that you will quickly withdraw today's proposal, in accordance with the words of Kshatriya-sama.'

With such an immovable, rock-like heart, even the Kshatriya had no room to intervene. Umori's younger sister Karuna turned to Umori,

Karuna: 'Oni-sama, though you were born into a family of martial arts, do you not always say that soldiers are weapons of destruction and carnage, and despise them like snakes and scorpions? You have always said that a cultural life will never be achieved until military preparations are abolished. If you dislike the army that much, why not follow the words of Samori-dono and return it to Kshatriya-sama immediately?'

Umori: 'This is no place for a young girl to interfere; be silent. The army is necessary until my ideals appear. It is my intention to swear to the total abolition of armaments once an ideal world has manifested. What do you know, you half-baked, naive haikara? To be infatuated with Haruna's beauty, to forget your own family, and to stand against your brother is extremely improper; from today, I sever our ties as brother and sister. Be prepared for that.'

Karuna-hime: 'Do as you please. I cannot continue to rely on Oni-sama forever. My husband's house is now what is important to me.'

Umori: 'Yoshitsu, well said; I have been waiting for those words. Since the Samori and Umori houses are no longer relatives from today, I cannot bear to have my purpose—no, no—the purpose of our nation's development hindered for your sake alone. I dismiss you once and for all!'

He shouted. Haruna stood up in a panic,

Haruna: 'Oni-sama, what Karuna-hime said may have offended you, but whatever she said was merely the words of a woman, and thinking of the relationship between brother and sister, she must have spoken with such willfulness. Since we have reconciled both houses by the will of Kshatriya-sama and Hirunahime-sama, and are at this banquet to which we were graciously invited by the King as a celebration, to speak such things is to be disloyal to the sovereign. I beg you to reconsider, listen again, and think about this calmly.'

Umori: 'Shut up, you greenhorn; this is not something for a small-fry warrior like you to understand.'

As he said this, he glanced at Hirunahime's face for a moment. Hirunahime looked down, tears falling from her eyes, and she was gritting her teeth. Just then, Kanto, the chief of the guards at the Lion River, entered in a hurry.

Kanto: 'I report, a terrible thing has happened.'

Kshatriya: 'Kanto, what do you mean by terrible? Report the details.'

Stroking his chest, Kanto raised his breathless voice,

Kanto: 'Just now, from beyond the Lion River, hundreds of banners marked with the triple hollyhock crest have been raised, and thousands of knights, in a courageous single-ladder formation, are attacking with the force of a raging tiger, with banners flying and drums beating. Not knowing what to do, I ran here as fast as I could to report this to you.'

Upon hearing this, everyone changed color, and for a while, they folded their hands and fell into silence.

といつかな動かぬ磐石心、流石の刹帝利も手を下すべき余地がなかつた。右守の妹カルナは右守に向ひ、

『兄上様、貴方は何時も武術の家に生れながら、兵は凶器だとか、殺伐だとか言つて、蛇蝎の如く忌み嫌つて居られるではありませぬか。文化生活といふものは、武備撤廃迄行かねば到底駄目だといつも仰有つたでせう。それ程御嫌ひな軍隊なら、左守殿の仰せに従ひ、刹帝利様に速に奉還なさつたら如何です』

右守『女童の容喙する所でない、スツ込み居らう。某の理想が出現する迄は軍隊の必要がある。理想世界が現はれた以上は、軍備全廃を誓つて致す拙者の考へだ。汝の如き半可通のナマ・ハイカラが何を知つてゐるか。ハルナの美貌に迷ひ、生家を忘れ、兄に楯つくとは不都合千万、今日より兄妹の縁を切る。さう覚悟を致せ』

カルナ姫『それは貴方の御勝手になさいませ。何時迄も兄上の世話になる訳には行きませぬ。妾は最早夫の家が大切で厶います』

右守『ヨシツ、よう言つた、其言葉を待つてゐたのだ。今日より左守右守両家は親戚でない程に、汝一人の為に吾目的……否々……我国の国力発展の目的を妨害するには忍びない。キツパリ暇をつかはすツ』

と呶鳴りつけた。ハルナは慌てて立上り、

ハルナ『お兄様、カルナ姫の申した事、お気に障ませうが、何を云つても女の申した事、又兄妹の間柄だと思つて、斯様な気儘な事を申したので厶いませう。折角刹帝利様、ヒルナ姫様の思召に仍つて、両家和合致し、其祝宴として、勿体なくも王様よりお招きに与つた此宴席に於て、左様な事を仰せらるるとは、君に対して不忠と申すもの、何卒見直し聞直し、冷静にお考へを願ひたう厶います』

右守『黙れツ青瓢箪、汝の如き木端武者の知る所ではない』

と云ひ乍ら、ヒルナ姫の顔を一寸覗いて見た。ヒルナ姫は差俯き、両眼よりハラハラと涙を落し、歯をくひしばつてゐる。かかる処へ慌ただしく入り来るは、ライオン河の関守の長カントであつた。

カント『ハイ申上げます、タタ大変な事が出来致しました』

刹帝利『カント、大変とは何事だ、詳細に言上せよ』

カントは胸を撫で乍ら、息苦しき声を張り上げて、

カント『只今ライオン河の彼方より、三葉葵のしるされたる旗、数百旒を押立て、数千のナイトは単梯陣の姿勇ましく、暴虎の勢を以て、旗鼓堂々攻めよせ来りまする。如何致せば宜しきや、心も心ならず一目散に走せ参じ、御注進に参りました』

と聞くより一同は面色を変へ、暫し双手を組んで各沈黙に入る。
