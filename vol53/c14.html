Chapter 14: 女の力 [1377]

女の力〔1377〕

§ 1

General Kumehiko, though lazy and indifferent, brings Princess Karuna into his office, serves wine, and they pour for each other, comforting themselves from the weariness of military life. Since there is no shadow of a woman in the camp, soldiers will smack their lips at any woman, no matter how plain she may be, and cherish her as their sole consolation. Kumehiko compares her to Princess Hiruna and thinks that this Karuna is somehow inferior, feeling as though he had lost out to Oniharuwake, and had even drawn his sword and caused a great uproar over the struggle for the woman. However, having returned to his office and sitting face to face, sharing their opinions with each other, he finds that—perhaps due to his partiality—she does not look inferior to Princess Hiruna in any way; nay, she even possesses a grace and dignity, and he begins to feel she is superior in some indefinable way. Kumehiko, now in a good mood, stares intently at Karuna's gentle face until he could pierce it with his gaze, and is completely captivated.

Karuna Hime: 'If I may say, Shiyōgunsama, that I have come to serve by your side through such a mysterious fate must surely be the matchmaking of the Gods.'

Kumehiko: 'Hmm, that is true. Even I, in my wildest dreams, never imagined I would have such a relationship with a lovely lass like you. You are truly a goddess of peace, my sole consoler. Nay, my sole savior. What enlivens and illuminates the heart of a brave man, and makes a hero all the more heroic, is, after all, the power of a woman.'

Karuna Hime: 'Whatever one may say, women are weak-spirited creatures. We are, after all, meant to be subservient to men. No matter how much one may spout theories about the sanctity of love, a woman must ultimately yield a step to the power of a man. However, even if one says that a woman should submit to a man, it is a matter of degree; since it is a lifelong misfortune to be with a man whose ideals do not match one's own, modern women are all hoping to be with a man whose will perfectly aligns with their own.'

Kumehiko: 'Indeed, as you say. Unless a man's divine wisdom harmonizes with a woman's divine love, and a woman's sacred love harmonizes with a man's sacred wisdom, they cannot be called a true married couple.'

Karuna Hime: 'That is so. There is nothing in this world as pleasant as a couple whose wills are in accord. By the way, Shiyōgunsama, do you like war?'

Kumehiko: 'Nay, I do not like such bloody things as war from the bottom of my heart.'

Karuna Hime: 'Then I must ask, Shiyōgunsama, why did you become a soldier when it is not in your heart? That point is something I cannot understand at all.'

Kumehiko: 'Nay, in truth, I am a propaganda general of Brahmanism, one who preaches the teachings of God's benevolence. I have set out on this campaign against my will, by the order of Lord Ōkuninushi. Truly, being a soldier is a trivial thing.'

Karuna Hime: 'You said just now that you were a propagandist, did you not?'

Kumehiko: 'Hmm, that is correct.'

Karuna Hime: 'Then you must consider it your sole divine vocation to help people, do you not?'

Kumehiko: 'That is certainly true. Even waging war in this manner is by no means to cause suffering to the people, but to construct the Heavenly Pure Land upon the earth.'

Karuna Hime: 'And yet, the army you lead has burned down civilian homes, slaughtered people, destroyed even Victoria Castle, and taken the King captive. Far from constructing the world of Miroku, from my shallow perspective, you appear only as a destroyer.'

Kumehiko: 'Hahaha, it is destruction for the sake of construction. It is not destruction for the sake of destruction. Unless you think deeply about that, you cannot understand the heart of a hero.'

Karuna Hime: 'If your words are indeed true, then once Victoria Castle has been destroyed, you will surely rebuild it, will you not?'

§ 2

Kumehiko: 'That is reasonable; it is my intention to attempt reconstruction immediately, to save the Kokumin from the suffering of Totan, and to bring about an era of Shichitaihei.'

Karunahime: 'In that case, you have taken the Setsuteiri and Jiyūshin of Victoria Castle as Horiyo, but now that the fighting has subsided, you will surely release them, will you not?'

Kumehiko: 'That goes without saying. However, if I leave the Setsuteiri and other Jiyūshin alive, I do not know when they might attempt a Fukushiusen again, so I am afraid I must send the King into Entau or throw them into Raugoku for Matsudai; this too is for the sake of Tenkabanmin.'

Karunahime acted as if she were startled, fell backward with a thud, and lay still. Kumehiko, surprised, lifted her up, sprinkled water on her face, and bringing his mouth to her ear, called out, 'Oi! Oi!' Karunahime feigned as if her breath had stopped on Waza, and after a while, she opened her eyes and looked around the Atari, saying,

Karunahime: 'Ah, I have had a terrible dream. You are General Kumehiko-sama; I am so glad you are safe. I truly had a frightening dream.'

Kumehiko felt uneasy at these words and, speaking urgently to Karuna,

Kumehiko: 'Ah, Karunahime, you had fainted. Well, well, that is fine, but what kind of frightening dream did you have? Won't you tell me?'

Karunahime: 'Yes, I would like to tell you, but since it is a dream, I must not offend you, so I shall not speak of this much.'

Kumehiko: 'Now, Karunahime, do not be like that. It does not matter what it is, so try telling me.'

Karunahime: 'You will surely not take offense, as it is but a dream, will you?'

Kumehiko: 'Eh, why would I be so foolish as to take offense at a dream? Tell me quickly.'

Karunahime: 'In that case, I shall tell you; a considerable amount of time must have passed since I fainted, must it not?'

Kumehiko: 'What? You fainted just now, so I immediately sprinkled water on you to revive you. It has only been about two or three minutes.'

Karunahime: 'That cannot be the case; I feel as though at least five or six hours have passed.'

Kumehiko: 'That is because, while you were unconscious, your spirit must have gone to the Reikai. Since the Reikai is a world of thought, you must have felt it was five or six hours due to the effect of extension. In reality, it was only two or three minutes. Come, tell me quickly.'

Karunahime: 'I was walking alone, stumbling through a wilderness of weeds, I know not where. Then there was a checkpoint called the Yatsumado of Heaven, where a guard with a white face and a guard with a red face were glaring with stern eyes. Strangely, Oniharuwake-sama and you, my lord, arrived in stern military uniforms, and when you attempted to pass through the gate of Yatsumado, the red guard called out, "Wait a moment." Then both generals stopped and said with great vigor, "I am the leader of the Brahman army, General Oniharuwake, and General Kumehiko." While this was happening, many underworld officials with the heads of oxen and horses appeared, bound you both with ropes, and conducted an inquiry into your sins one by one. As I trembled and listened nearby, the interrogation began with you first. You have indeed toyed with women quite a bit. Then, your march to the mansion of the Saien, your sending troops to capture King Victoria and causing him suffering, your binding and tormenting of many Jiyūshin, and your burning of civilian homes and killing of people were all investigated. You bowed your head to the earth and apologized for each one, saying, "That is so." The underworld officials with terrifying faces used whips covered in knots to strike your head, face, and buttocks without mercy, and you were crying out in agony. You were subjected to such unspeakable, cruel treatment. Then you were placed on a scale, and when it was decided that you were to go to hell, I felt truly sorry for you when I saw your disappointed face. Then the white guard intervened and said, "Although this man has committed sins until now, his physical body is still in the Genkai, so he cannot be sent to hell yet. Wait until his life span ends and he comes to the Reikai." Thereupon, you apologized profusely to the underworld officials. And when you said, "I shall help King Victoria, release the other Jiyūshin, restore the Setsuteiri-sama to the throne, and serve as a loyal subject to King Victoria," the underworld official immediately softened his face and said, "If you truly repent, I shall forgive your descent into hell when you come next time and send you to the heaven where flowers bloom and bear fruit; but if you break this promise, I shall cast you into the hell of swords." That was a very stern pronouncement. As I trembled, feeling as though I could not bear to exist, I heard your voice from far, far away, and then I awoke. It was indeed a dream. Is it not a mysterious dream?'

Kumehiko: 'What a strange thing you say. It seems my spirit had wandered off to the crossroads before I knew it. No, perhaps that is the truth; it is a troubling matter.'

Karunahime: 'Please do not trouble yourself over it, as it was but a dream. Yet, if such a thing were true, it would be a sad fate for my beloved husband.'

She pressed her sleeve to her eyes, bowed her head, and began to weep. Kumehiko folded his arms, let out a deep sigh, and fell into deep contemplation. Karuna, rejoicing in her heart that she had succeeded, feigned an expression of concern.

Karunahime: 'If I may, Shiyōgunsama, has your complexion not turned terribly pale? It is exactly as the face I saw in my dream. I am truly sorry for having troubled your spirits by speaking of such a useless dream. Please forgive me.'

She began to weep once more.

Kumehiko: 'No, I too must think on this. Your dream is surely a true omen. It seems my subordinates have been carried away by their momentum and have acted with too much violence. However, the sins of the subordinates are the responsibility of the Shiyōgun. The Shiyōgun must bear the guilt. It is a troubling matter.'

Karuna, intending to continue drawing him in, said:

Karunahime: 'If I may, Shiyōgunsama, you are the commander of the mighty three armies; there is no need for you to worry over such a dream. Is it not unavoidable for a Shiyōgun, in the line of duty, to burn villages, kill people, and slaughter cities? If you worry over such things, you will not be able to fulfill your role as a Shiyōgun.'

Kumehiko: 'You have become suddenly bold since having that dream, have you not? It is strange. Hearing your words, I have suddenly become afraid of the future. I must think this over, but Kiharuwake-shiyōgun stands above me. No matter how much I, Kumehiko, may wish to return to goodness and assist the Kshatriya, if my superior shakes his head, it is ultimately futile. Ah, I am caught in a dilemma from which I cannot retreat. How shall I ever resolve this?'

He sank into thought once more.

Karunahime: 'Anata, is there any need for you to worry so? If you only set your resolve, such a thing should be nothing. Since Kiharuwake-sama has my master as his wife, if I speak to Hiruna-sama, and Hiruna-sama speaks to the Shiyōgun-sama, this problem will likely be resolved quickly. There can be no other way, can there?'

She tilted her head deliberately, appearing worried.

Kumehiko: 'As expected of Karunahime. You have noticed a good point. Then, I shall leave this matter entirely to you. However, I am currently in a state where my will is estranged from Kiharuwake-shiyōgun, so he may not easily listen, even with the remonstrance of Hiruna-sama. Ah, a worrying situation has arisen.'

Karuna looked at Kumehiko's face and smiled, looking somewhat happy.

Karunahime: 'Ah, your face has suddenly brightened. What a fine face it is; it seems the light has manifested in your spirit after all. I have heard it said that the human face is an index of the heart—that an evil heart produces an evil countenance, and a good heart produces a beautiful one. Through the change in your face just now, I have accurately recognized the words of the sages. Ah, you are becoming more and more beautiful. Oh, why have I been blessed with such a noble and beautiful husband? Banko-shinō-sama, Daijizaiten-sama, I thank you. I pray that you will assist us, husband and wife, in Takamagahara where you reside, both in spirit and body, and I humbly ask that we may live together in harmony with Kumehiko-sama, both in this world and the future.'

She prayed with feigned sincerity. Shiyōgun Kumehiko was completely swept away by Karunahime's beauty and eloquence; he had reached a point where, if it were anything Karunahime said, he would listen and obey gladly, without considering profit or loss, and without distinguishing between right and wrong. Truly, the magic of a woman is a fearful thing. Even a great, rugged Brahmin general was defeated without a single struggle upon encountering a single glance from a beautiful woman. Ah, men must keep their hearts guarded and be wary of women. Women are commonly called demons; they can overturn a fortress with just the dimple of a cheek, and with willow-like brows and bell-like eyes, they can swallow fields, blow away storehouses, take the lives of men, and easily shatter the desperate efforts of thousands of soldiers, including a Shiyōgun of such imposing dignity. Young men of the world, and beloved followers of Ōmoto, I hope that by reading this story, you will reflect deeply and take care not to let your body and soul be led astray by false love, thereby ruining your entire life. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.

久米彦『お前は夢を見てから俄に鼻息が荒くなつたぢやないか、妙だなア。俺はお前の話を聞いて俄に未来が怖ろしくなつた。これや一つ考へねばなるまい、併し乍ら吾頭の上には鬼春別将軍が控へて居る。何程久米彦が善に立ちかへり、刹帝利を助けむと致しても、上官が首を横に振つたが最後、到底駄目だ。ああ引くに引かれぬ板挟みとなつた。どうしたら此解決がつくだらうかなア』

と又もや思案に沈む。

カルナ姫『貴方さう御心配には及ばぬぢや厶いませぬか、御決心さへ定まればその位の事は何でも厶いますまい。鬼春別様は妾の主人を妻に持つて居られますから、妾よりヒルナ様に申上げ、ヒルナ様より将軍様に申上げるようにすれば、比較的この問題は解決が早いでせう。それより外方法は厶いますまいなア』

と心配さうに故意と首を傾ける。

久米彦『遉はカルナ姫だ。よい所に気がついた。そんならこの問題は其方に一任する事にしようかなア。併し乍ら拙者は鬼春別将軍と何処ともなしに意志が疎隔して居る最中だから、何程ヒルナ様の諫言と雖も容易に聞くまい。ああ心配な事が出来て来たものだなア』

カルナは久米彦の顔を見て、稍嬉し気に打笑ひ、

カルナ姫『アア貴方のお顔は俄に輝いて来ました。何とまアよいお顔だこと、やつぱり貴方の霊に光が顕れて来たので厶いますなア。人間の顔は心の索引だと云ひますから、心に悪心あれば悪相を生じ、善心あれば、善美の相を現ずるものだと聞きましたが、今貴方のお顔の変相によつて、的確に聖哲の言葉を認識致しました。ああ益々麗しきお顔になられますよ。ああどうして妾はかかる尊い美しい夫に添うたのだらうか、盤古神王様、大自在天様、有難う存じます。何卒妾等夫婦を貴神の鎮まります高天原に、霊肉共にお助け下さいまして、現世も未来も、久米彦様と睦じく暮せますやう偏にお願ひ申上げます』

と誠しやかに祈願する。久米彦将軍はすつかりカルナ姫の容色と弁舌に巻込まれ、最早何事もカルナ姫の言とあれば、利害得失を考へず、正邪の区別も弁へず、喜んで聴従するやうになつて来た。実に女の魔力と云ふものは怖るべきものである。武骨一片のバラモンの名将軍も、美人の一瞥に会つては実に一耐りもなく参つて仕舞うたのである。ああ男子たるものは心を潜めて、女に注意せなくてはならぬものである。女は俗に魔物と云ふ、金城鉄壁をただ片頬の靨に覆へし、柳の眉、鈴の眼に田畑を呑み、家倉を跳ね飛ばし、男の命を取り、さしもに威儀堂々たる将軍を初め、数千の軍隊の必死の努力も、容易にメチヤメチヤに壊すものである。世の青年諸氏よ、敬愛なる大本の信徒よ、此物語を読んでよく顧み、虚偽的恋愛に身心を蘯かし、一生を誤る事なきやう注意されむ事を望む次第である。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
