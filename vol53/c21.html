Chapter 21: 軍議 [1384]

軍議〔1384〕

§ 1

Setsuteiri, together with Taruman, the Kyūbitsuto of Samori, and the newly appointed Umori named Ekusu, are gathered with Haruna in the King's Ima, putting their heads together and indulging in various Okusokudan regarding the retreat of the Brahman army.

Samori: 'Well, I am sure you would understand if you were to ask Taruman-dono for the Shinchiyoku, but it is truly beyond my Gaten how both generals, who had built such a Ritsupa Jinei and were Iatsu-ing Victoria Castle, could suddenly lead their entire Zengun and retreat.'

Taruman: 'As I do not possess Kamugakari, I cannot say for certain, but I suspect that the Chiuyūgiretsu Hiruna-hime-sama and Karuna-hime-sama, wishing to ensure the Zetsutaiteki Anzen of the Bi-koku, have cleverly deceived both generals and caused them to Tachisa.'

Setsuteiri: 'That is likely the case. Since those two women are truly the Chiuseki of the nation, they may have saved the state by sacrificing their Itsushin. Ah, they are truly heroic women; they are remarkable.'

Samori: 'Indeed, Samori feels ashamed before the Geseichū of Hiruna-hime-sama. However, Setsuteiri-sama, as you have reached such an advanced age and oversee the administration of all state affairs, it must be very inconvenient for you to be without a consort to serve as your helpmate. Since Hiruna-hime-sama has made such a resolution, it is unlikely that she will return. Therefore, you must select a Queen, or you, the King, will surely find it inconvenient.'

Setsuteiri: 'No, no, I am not thinking of such a thing at all. Even if it is somewhat inconvenient, how could I take a second wife in light of Hiruna-hime's chastity? I must at least try to understand her heart a little.'

Samori: 'Your words are most reasonable, but no matter what is said, now that you have regained the authority over military affairs and are the sovereign of the nation, it will surely be difficult to oversee the national government perfectly while you are single. I truly wish you would reconsider.'

Samori is persistently urging this because he wishes for his son Haruna to take a wife, and he thinks that as a subject, it would be presumptuous to arrange a marriage for his son before Setsuteiri-sama has chosen a Queen. However, Setsuteiri, sensing the heart of Hiruna-hime, would not consent no matter what was said. Taruman, guessing the heart of Samori, said:

Taruman: 'My Lord is of advanced age, and with many retainers serving him and many ladies-in-waiting present, it is inevitable that we must leave matters to his sacred judgment. However, Haruna-dono is still young, and we must assume that Karuna-hime will no longer return. If that is the case, must you not choose a suitable match and have him marry, lest the lineage of the Samori family be severed? We must request this of my Lord and see that something is done.'

Samori, hearing the kind words of Taruman, is secretly choked with tears of gratitude. Haruna steps forward and says:

Haruna: 'Taruman-dono, please do not worry at all. Even Setsuteiri-sama, with his noble person, says he intends to live a single life. Since he intends to live alone despite his advanced age, it is not the least bit painful for a young man like myself to remain single. Furthermore, when I think of the sacrificial actions of Karuna-hime, how could I take a second wife? My love is truly sacred. Even if I never meet Karuna again, I shall not marry for the rest of my life.'

Taruman: 'Your resolve is truly admirable, and I am deeply impressed. Ah, however, you must pass down descendants for the sake of the Samori family; you cannot bear children if you remain single. I must ask you to bend your will and consent to this.'

§ 2

Haruna: 'No matter what you may say, I beg you to forgive me in this matter alone. Is it not true that Setsuteirisama also has no heir? Much less should there be any need to grieve over the fact that the Samorike has no heir. Is it not true that all things come to pass solely through the Mikokoro of the Kamisama? There is no Dauri that the Samorike must be the descendants of Haruna, and in fact, is there not the precedent of Ekusu-dono newly becoming the Umori?'

Taruman tilted his head repeatedly, deeply moved, and had no words to reply.

In this world, there are many men who, having parted from their Saiai wife, insist that they will live a life of celibacy, claiming they will never take another wife because it would be unfair to her, no matter what anyone says. Some write poems of Tsuitau, some write books of Tsuikuwai and distribute them to their Chikiiujin, or declare their life of celibacy to the Tenka; yet, these men easily forget their own Sengen, and in the case of the slow ones, it takes two or three months, while the fast ones catch a second candidate in about three days. This is the Sekirara psychological state of human beings. However, Setsuteiri and Haruna are not ordinary, worldly hypocrites; they truly pitied the heart of their spouse and continued a life of celibacy for the sake of a wife who had no hope of ever returning.

While they were speaking thus, Eru, the jailer, came rushing in. Eru, with a worried look, pressed his head to the tatami.

Eru: 'I must report that the important prisoner Shieru has broken out of the jail and escaped before we knew it. I am truly guilty of negligence in my duties, and I have no excuse.'

He was weeping. Ekusu, the Umori, said:

Ekusu: 'What, has Shieru escaped? That is a disaster. Samori-dono, what should we do?'

Samori: 'Well, this is a troublesome matter. However, there is no use in regretting it now. His escape is like releasing a tiger into the field; he will surely conspire with Berutsu and plot some Nanigoto of rebellion. Therefore, it will be necessary to search for his whereabouts.'

Setsuteiri: 'Send men out quickly, find his location, and make arrangements to capture and bring him back. Umori-dono, I rely on you to be thorough in all things.'

The Umori said, 'I understand the details,' and left the place. He ordered Kanto, who served as the head of the Kawamori, and Eru to disguise themselves and infiltrate the village of Kiiru, where Berutsu was said to be hiding.

Changing the subject, Berutsu had holed up in the deep mountains of Kiiru, surrounded by mountains on three sides and backed by a great river on the fourth. He gathered his hereditary retainers, trained in martial arts, and waited for the time. Upon hearing the report that the Brahman army had retreated without a single man left, he thought, 'The time for the fulfillment of my desire has come; if not now, when shall I achieve my purpose?' He led nearly one thousand cavalry, conscripted farmers along the way, and news reached Kanto that he was approaching with a force of three thousand, inch by inch. The shock of Setsuteiri and Samori was beyond measure. As usual, they held a secret meeting and deliberated intensely on how to prepare for the attack of the rebel army. However, there were only eight hundred or so warriors who had served the Umori for generations, and they were merely kept in the barracks as a great organ of national defense. If they heard that Berutsu was pressing in, it was hard to tell when they might raise the banner of rebellion and counterattack the King; the heartache was immense. Setsuteiri shed tears profusely.

Setsuteiri: 'Ah, one difficulty passes and another comes. Why is it that these worries never cease?'

He sank into sorrow. Neither Taruman nor the Samori-shi could come up with a good plan; both were in a state of despair.

Haruna stood up with dignity.

Haruna: 'Please, I beg you, do not worry. The eight hundred soldiers in the castle are all men of unparalleled loyalty, so there is no fear of them turning against us. As this Haruna is not yet known to the soldiers, I shall disguise myself in various ways, become a beggar, and go around the barracks night after night to listen to their talk; there is not one among them who refuses to give his life for the King. And since they hate the evil deeds of Berutsu intensely, I believe that even if they are hereditary retainers, they will never commit such unrighteous acts in terms of the great cause of justice. If you entrust this army to me, I shall surely defeat the enemy and see to it that they never harbor such ambitions again. And I shall capture both the villains, Kitsu and Berutsu, as well as Shieru, and present them to you. Regarding this, I have a concrete plan.'

Samori: 'Listen, my son, if you say such things and by any chance suffer a defeat, how shall I offer an excuse to our Kimisama? You are young, so you are optimistic, but while that fellow called Belts is a coward, Shieru is a master of Gunriyaku; it is as if Belts only shines because of Shieru. This is not something for a young and inexperienced Jiyakuhai like you to understand. Though I am not capable, and despite my old age, I, your father Kyūbitto, shall command the entire army with Umoridono for the sake of our Kimisama and our country, standing at the Yaomote to fight and struggle fiercely. As for me, I am of an old age with little Yomei left, and my Inochi is not worth holding onto, but you are a man with a long future ahead of you. You must serve by the side of our Kimisama, keep yourself in a position of Anzen, and after I am gone, you must strive with Jifubun Chiukin for the sake of our Kimisama and our country. Our Kimisama, I humbly beg you to entrust this defense to Samori and Umori.'

Ksatiriya: 'The words of Samori truly strike at the heart of the matter. In that case, I entrust the command of the entire army to Samori and Umori.'

Samori: 'Yes, I am humbled by your gracious command and am grateful. I shall stake my Inochi and fight to the very end to protect the royal house and the nation.'

Umori: 'Though I am not capable, I shall follow the command of Samori-shi, and regarding my Inochi as light as a feather, I shall fight and struggle fiercely, so please be assured.'

Taruman: 'Samori-dono, Umori-dono, throwing away one's Inochi is what a commoner does. Both generals must keep yourselves in a safe zone and command the entire army until the end. You must refrain from reckless actions and be prepared to survive until the very last man, otherwise this battle will be in vain.'

Samori: 'Indeed, as Taruman-dono says, I understand everything.'

Umori: 'I shall never go against what Taruman-dono says, please be assured.'

Haruna: 'Now that my father has become the commander-in-chief of the entire army, I earnestly request that you allow me to serve as your chief of staff.'

Samori: 'No, no, as I said before, you must never go to a dangerous place. Your role is to serve our King faithfully by his side and protect his person.'

He is deeply troubled in his heart by parental affection, trying not to let his child go to the battlefield and be killed.

Haruna: 'If it is under my father's command, this battle will be a zero-loss victory. Regarding this, we have a profound plan, so please, Ksatiriya-sama, will you not leave it to me? I will surely show you my merit.'

Ksatiriya: 'What is this? I do not understand your words. What is this plan? Speak it before me.'

Haruna: 'Pardon me, but I request that you clear the room of everyone. If what I say does not meet with your approval, you need not adopt it, and I shall hold no grudge.'

Ksatiriya: 'Even the words of a young person may have something worth taking. In that case, I shall listen... Ah, no, everyone, it would be best if you stepped away from your seats for a while.'

At this single word from the King, Taruman and the others, including Samori and Umori, reluctantly moved away from their seats. Haruna stepped close to the King and lowered his voice.

Haruna: 'In truth, last night I visited the forest of the God-King, and while I was praying for the peace of the nation with all my heart, I thought it was the God-King Pangu, but it was Lord Susanoo who appeared and said: ...You are a loyal subject who thinks of the nation; you are truly the pillar of the country of Biku. Now Belts has raised a banner of rebellion, leading an army of one thousand cavalry, and with many farmers following him, he will surely press upon us with no less than three thousand men before many days pass. Ah, at that time, do not offer any resistance; lock the castle tightly and remain in siege. If you do so, not one of your eight hundred allies will turn against you. If you go outside the castle to fight, traitors will appear, and it will be to the disadvantage of your side. In any case, with the intention of a siege, if you secure the entrances on all four sides, a mysterious reinforcement will appear and scatter the enemy. Furthermore, Princess Hiruna and Princess Karuna will return, and from behind the enemy, they will release a surprise force, and you will be able to make the rebel army surrender without a single one remaining... thus he showed me clearly. I beg you, even if it be a dream, these are by no means false words. I firmly believe that our wise Kimisama will surely accept my suggestion.'

Ksatiriya: 'Indeed, there is reason in your words. It is something that human power cannot possibly achieve; the strategy shown by Lord Susanoo is truly a perfect method of warfare. In that case, I entrust all temporary authority over soldiers and horses to you.'

Haruna shed tears of joy and said,

Haruna: 'Thank you for listening to the words of a young person. I shall surely save the nation from this great crisis through the divine power. Please be assured. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese.'

He clasped his hands. The King looked as if he were truly relying on him, smiling, and his complexion suddenly began to brighten.

左守『イヤイヤ其方は最前も申した通り、決して危険な所へ行つてはならない。王様のお側に忠実に仕へ、御身辺を守るが其方の役目だ』

と親の情で吾子を戦場に向け討死させまいと頻りに心を悩ましてゐる。

ハルナ『父上の御指揮なれば、今度の戦ひは零敗で厶います。これに就いては吾々に深遠なる計画が厶いますから、何卒、刹帝利様、拙者にお任し下さいませぬか、キツと手柄を現はしてお目にかけます』

刹帝利『ハテ心得ぬ汝が言葉、其計画とは如何なる事か、余が前に言つてみよ』

ハルナ『恐れ乍ら、すべてのお人払を願います。拙者の申し上ぐる事が若し御不承知なれば御採用下されずとも、お恨みは致しませぬ』

刹帝利『若輩の言にも亦取るべき事があらう、然らば聞いて遣はす……ア、イヤ、一同の者、暫く席を遠ざかつたがよからう』

と鶴の一声に、タルマン始め左守、右守は不性不精に席を遠ざかつた。ハルナは王の側近く進み、声を潜めて、

ハルナ『実の所は昨夜神王の森に参拝を致し、真心を籠て国家の安泰を祈る折しも、盤古神王と思ひきや、神素盞嗚尊現はれ玉ひ、仰せらるるやう、……其方は国家を思ふ忠良の臣だ、実にビクの国の柱だ。今やベルツは反旗を掲げ、一千騎の軍隊を引率れ、数多の農民共を従へて、無慮三千人、日ならず押寄せ来るであらう、あ、其時は決して手向ひを致すでない、城内を固く鎖し籠城を致せよ。さすれば八百の味方は一人も裏返る者はない。もしも城外へ出でて戦はむか、裏切りするものが現はれて、味方の不利益であるぞよ。兎も角も籠城の心持にて、四方の入口を固め居れば不思議な援軍が現はれて敵を追ひ散らすであらう。又ヒルナ姫、カルナ姫は帰り来つて、敵の背後より、奇兵を放ち、叛軍をして、一人も残らず降伏せしむることが出来るであらう……とアリアリとお示しになりました。何卒、夢とは云へ、決して虚妄の言では厶いませぬ。賢明な吾君は必ずや、吾進言を御嘉納下さる事と固く信じて居りまする』

刹帝利『いかにも、汝の言葉には一理ある。到底人間の力では及ぶものでない、素盞嗚尊様のお示しになつた戦略は、実に完全な戦法だ。然らば全部、汝に臨時兵馬の権を委任する』

ハルナは嬉し涙をハラハラとながし乍ら、

ハルナ『若年者の言葉、御聞き届け下さいまして、有難う厶います。キツと御神力に仍りて、国家の大難を救はして頂きませう。御安心下さいませ。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

と合掌した。王はさも頼もしげに、ニコニコとして面色とみに輝き出したり。
