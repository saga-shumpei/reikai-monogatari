Chapter 17: 奉還状 [1380]

奉還状〔1380〕

§ 1

Setsuteiri, Samori, Umori, and the others, having observed that the generals Oniharuwake and Kumehiko, along with their four adjutants and subordinates, had drunk themselves into a stupor and fallen asleep without knowing what was happening around them, finally opened their mouths and began to whisperingly hold a consultation regarding a plan for the aftermath.

Taruman: 'Setsuteirisama and everyone else, we have truly obtained an unexpectedly favorable result. This is, needless to say, entirely due to the divine protection of Bankoshinnōsama, and I believe it is what has brought us to this point through the desperate activities of the virtuous and heroic Hirunahime-sama and Karunahime-sama. Truly, there is nothing more grateful than this.'

Setsuteiri: 'For the work of the two ladies which I have deeply felt, you all have also broken your bones considerably for the sake of the royal house and the nation. I am truly filled with gratitude.'

Taruman: 'I would like to ask Setsuteirisama one thing, but although you dismissed Hirunahime, now that such meritorious deeds have been revealed, you will surely restore her as your queen, will you not?'

Setsuteiri: 'There is likely no other woman in the world as virtuous and heroic as she. Since this side was also saved from a national crisis by her, even if there were some minor faults, it was work done for the sake of the nation, so there is no reason to blame her. Regarding this matter, I leave it entirely to you.'

Taruman: 'Thank you for your immediate consent. Hirunahime-sama will surely be very satisfied as well.'

Samori: 'Whether speaking of Hirunahime-sama or Karunahime, they are truly magnificent. Although the army led by Umori-dono was also considerable, perhaps it is said that under a weak general there are weak soldiers, for not a single one was of any use. Although Karunahime is said to be the younger sister of Umori-dono, she is truly a magnificent heroine. Haruna, you must also be suffering from your wounds, but having such a wife, you must find some consolation, do you not?'

Haruna: 'Yes.'

Having said that, she remained with her face flushed and looking down.

Samori: 'Now that such harmony has been achieved, General Oniharuwake will surely not attempt to command even the army of Biku Castle. The Brahman army will likely form a separate camp as a Brahman army. If so, at this juncture, I believe it would be best for Umori-dono to return the authority over the military forces to Setsuteirisama completely; what do you think, Umori-dono? While you claimed that your army was to prevent internal troubles and external threats, when the nation reached a crisis, you showed a weak spirit and caused this castle to fall into the misery of a crushing defeat; that is entirely your responsibility. If you have even a shred of sincerity, at this time, apologize for your crimes and return the authority over the military forces to the King completely.'

Umori, with a very unpleasant look on his face,

Umori: 'This is an incomprehensible remark from Samori-dono. Since my family is of a lineage that holds the authority over military forces, did not Karunahime, born from that lineage, perform military exploits in my stead? Although it is said that Karunahime was sent to Samori's house, she is still one born into the Umori house; now that Karunahime, born into the Umori house, has achieved such meritorious deeds, it can never be said that the Umori house lacks the real power of military force. Is it not the same thing whether one moves a thousand armies and ten thousand horses to gain victory, or whether one achieves the objective completely through a single woman? Furthermore, Hirunahime is a daughter of my relatives, and she also carries the lineage of the Umori house; considering this, the nation of Biku will likely not be preserved unless Umori holds the authority over the military forces to the very end. Samori-dono, you have become a bit senile due to your advanced age.'

Samori: 'Your hateful heart, which attempts to achieve your ambitions to the end by wielding evil wisdom and sophistry—if you do not repent to a reasonable degree, divine punishment will arrive immediately. You are a heinous criminal who has taken the Queen into your fold, caused her to walk a path that is not the way, and finally attempted to achieve a lawless conspiracy; if this were a different age, you are a person who would be unforgivable even if executed by reverse crucifixion, but no matter what is said, because you were the person in actual power holding the authority over the military forces, Setsuteirisama has swallowed his tears and endured you until today. Even this Samori has done the same, and the fact that Hirunahime-sama has also suffered in various ways out of a single-minded concern for the nation is clear. If you are also one born into the Umori house, why do you not manfully commit seppuku to apologize for your crimes before the King, or return the authority over the military forces, descend to the status of a commoner, and apologize for your crimes?'

§ 2

Umoridono thought for a shibashi, but while nodding to himself with a disagreeable metsuki, he suddenly straightened himself into the geza and placed his riyōte on the floor.

Umori: 'Hahaa, Setsuteirisama, and all you other rekirekisama, from this konnichi and tadaima, I shall follow your commands, repent my zenpi, and return the authority of heiba. I pray you, please ōketo it. And if you will forgive our sins, it is my intention to descend to the minkan, become a shiyuda, and spend my yosei in denenseikuwatsu.'

Setsuteiri looked around to his saiu,

Setsuteiri: 'Taruman, Samoridono, do you nanjira have any izon regarding what Umori has just said?'

Taruman and Samori bowed their heads with a start,

Samori: 'We are the ones who have been troubled in our hearts nichiya, wishing for this very thing to come to pass; how could we possibly have any izon?'

Setsuteiri: 'Un, then I shall grant Umori's request. Umori, be arigata for this.'

Umori: 'Hai, I shall engrave your gojinji upon my heart and remain arigata for it.'

Samori: 'Yaa, Umoridono, atsupare, atsupare! A bushi must be like that. In that case, write out the hōkuwan here. And we shall have you press your thumbprint upon it.'

Umori was startled and perplexed by these words. 'If I write the hōkuwan, my position will be ruined. I have fallen into a troublesome predicament,' he thought, yet being the old and cunning Umori, he feigned ignorance and said,

Umori: 'I speak with fear and trembling before Setsuteirisama. I am a descendant of the Umori house, a bushi; once I have stated that I shall return my authority, I shall never change my mind. A bushi does not speak two words. Nanitozo, please buy my character. I wish to return it through my word alone.'

Samori spoke sternly and sharply,

Samori: 'Umoridono, you ask us to acknowledge your character, but do you think you have any character? Place your hand upon your chest and think well. How can you say such a brazen thing?'

Umori: 'If you do not consent, then it cannot be helped. Even though it is the word of a bushi, it seems that my offer to return my authority does not please Setsuteirisama and the rekirekisama. In that case, there is no other choice; I shall continue to succeed to my ancestors' house, remain as Umori, and hold the authority of heiba.'

Taruman: 'Umoridono, you have spoken before the King; you shall never be allowed to withdraw your words. If you are going to say such lawless things, then it cannot be helped. I, too, have my own thoughts.'

With that, he notched a kaburaya onto the bow he held at his side, drew it back like a full moon, and aimed the tip of the arrow at Umori's face. Even the seasoned Umori was daunted by this; his complexion changed instantly, and as his lips trembled, he said,

Umori: 'No, I do not insist on asserting my own opinion. Ah, in that case, I shall return it.'

Taruman continued to hold his bow at full draw, holding his breath in the state of a-un. If the thumb resting on the string were to move even an inch, Umori's life would instantly become like a candle in the wind—nay, he would fall into total annihilation.

Samori: 'In that case, Umoridono, come, quickly, since there are writing materials and an inkstone here, write out the hōkuwan.'

Umori gnashed his teeth,

Umori: 'Ah, there is no other way.'

Muttering in a low voice, he turned to the desk, dipped his brush, and facing the paper, his hands trembling violently, he wrote out the hōkuwan and handed it to Samori. When Samori examined the contents, he read as follows:

1. Regarding myself, as the heir to the Umori house, I have held the authority of heiba and been entrusted with the protection of the state. Until this konnichi, without any impropriety, I have kept the Kingdom of Biku and the Royal House in the peace of Mount Tai. As the heir to the Umori house, I hereby take pride in offering to Setsuteirisama the return of my military office.

2. On the occasion of this recent attack by the Brahmin army, the military merit of Princess Karna, born into the Umori house, is a source of pride, as it verifies that the Umori house, being a lineage holding the authority of heiba, possesses the transmission of brave and vigorous blood.

Date: Umori, Berutsu

It was written thus. Samori curled his lip and read it aloud again before the King. The King remained silent, not uttering a single word, and sat with his mouth tightly shut. Taruman, while nocking an arrow to his bow, said:

Taruman: 'Umoridono, according to this document, one cannot help but acknowledge that your lingering attachment to the authority over military forces is manifested twelvefold. Reform your arrogant and insolent language, and rewrite it at once, manfully and immediately, to return it. Such a document of return is no better than waste paper.'

Samori: 'Umoridono, as Taruman says, come, honestly, manfully, and clearly, acknowledge the document of return.'

Umori: 'Well, for that, I ask for a moment's grace, and after deep reflection and contemplation, I shall acknowledge and submit it.'

Taruman: 'Umoridono, you intend to use glib talk to gloss over the moment, escape this place, and plot your wild ambitions once again, do you not? It is clearly manifested upon your countenance.'

Having had his heart pierced through by these words, with no way to escape and suppressing the demon of attachment, he steeled his resolve for the situation at hand, where he could neither retreat nor advance, and began to write the document again.

Regarding the Document of Return of Military Authority

1. I wish to return to the presence of the current Setsuteiri, Victoria-ō, all of the authority over military forces that the ancestors of the Umorike were entrusted with by the Victoria family until today, and I humbly and earnestly request that you accept it through your special consideration.

Date: Umori, Berutsu

He wrote this and handed it to Samori. Samori read it aloud again before the King.

Setsuteiri: 'Hmm, very well, I accept it immediately. Hand over the duties to Samori-shi as soon as possible. However, for the sake of caution, you had better press your thumbprint on this.'

Umori: 'It is a place where I should press my thumbprint, but in yesterday's disturbance, my arm was wounded by that fellow Karuna, and I feel pain even to the tips of my fingers, so I cannot possibly press my thumbprint. I ask for your grace until I am fully recovered.'

Samori: 'Is the wound of Umori not on your right hand? Thumbprints are limited to the left hand. Come, I want you to press it quickly.'

He thrusts it forward. Taruman, with the arrow still nocked to his bow, glares at Umori's face. To prepare for future excuses, Umori stealthily plucked a hair from his right temple, applied it to his finger, dipped it in ink, and pressed his thumbprint. This was to deceive the fingerprint. Samori, seeing this sharply, said:

Samori: 'Umoridono, this thumbprint is incorrect. I want you to press it again.'

Umori: 'That is an incomprehensible remark, Samori-dono. I have but one left thumb. If this does not please you, Samori-dono, please press it on my behalf, as you are my proxy.'

Samori: 'Your words, Umori, are more and more outrageous. You intend to change the fingerprint with a hair and create a pretext for a future dispute, do you not? Even though I am old, I am not so blind as to not understand such things. Come, press your seal manfully and quickly.'

Shedding tears of mortification and finding himself at an impasse, Umori reluctantly pressed his real thumbprint this time.

Samori: 'Ah, splendid, splendid. Setsuteiri-sama, with this, the procedure is complete. Congratulations. Ah, Umoridono, you too are to be congratulated.'

Umori: 'Yes, from the bottom of my heart... congratulations.'

He gave an indistinct reply. Umori turned to the Setsuteiri and said:

Umori: 'Now that you have auspiciously permitted the return, I shall return to my mansion, remain in confinement for a while, and await the orders of my lord. I humbly ask for your favor in all matters.'

Saying this, he cast a sidelong glance at Taruman, Samori, and the others, and walked out, deliberately stomping his feet loudly along the corridor.

年月日　　　右守、ベルツ

と記し、左守の手に渡した。左守は又之を王の前に朗読した。

刹帝利『ウン、ヨシ、直様聞届る。一時も早く左守司に引つぎを致せよ。併し乍ら之に念の為に、拇印を押しておくがよい』

右守『拇印を押すべき処なれど、昨日の騒動にカルナの奴に腕を傷つけられ、指の先迄痛みを感じ到底拇印は出来ませぬ。全快する迄御猶予を願ひまする』

左守『右守の創は右の手では厶らぬか、拇印は左の手に限りますぞ。サ、早く押して貰ひたい』

と前へ突き出す。タルマンは弓に矢を番へたまま、右守の面体を睨みつけてゐる。右守は後日の言ひ掛りを拵へん為、ソツと右の鬢の毛をむしり、指に当て、墨をつけて拇印を押した。これは指紋を誤魔かさむが為である。左守は目敏く之を見て、

左守『右守殿、此拇印は間違つて厶る。マ一度押し直して貰ひたい』

右守『これは心得ぬ左守殿の言葉、拙者の左の拇指は一本より厶らぬ。之がお気に入らなくば、左守殿、拙者の代理に其方が立派に押しておいて下され』

左守『益々以て不埒千万な右守の言葉、髪の毛を以て指紋を変じ、後日の言ひがかりを拵へむとの、伏線で厶らうがな。左様な事の、老眼と雖も、分らぬ拙者では厶らぬ。サ、早く男らしく捺印なされ』

右守は無念の涙を零し乍ら、進退惟れ谷まつて、厭々乍らも、今度は本当に拇印を捺した。

左守『ヤ、天晴々々、刹帝利様、之にて手続きは済みまして厶います。お目出度う御座います。ヤ、右守殿、其方も目出度いなア』

右守『ハイ、根つから……お目出度う厶います』

と歯切れせぬ答弁をやつてゐる。右守は刹帝利に向ひ、

右守『目出度く奉還を御許可下さいました上は、拙者は館に帰り、暫く謹慎を致し、君の御命令を御待ち致します。何分宜しく御願ひ致します』

と言ひ乍ら、タルマン、左守其他の面々を尻目にかけ乍ら、ドシドシと廊下をワザと鳴らして出でて行く。
