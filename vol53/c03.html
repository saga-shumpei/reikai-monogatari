Chapter 3: 軟文学 [1366]

軟文学〔1366〕

§ 1

The Seido of the Ōkoku of Biku was such that the Samorinokami served as the Shihanyaku to the King, and was responsible for handling all essential Jimu within the Kokunai. The Umorinokami, meanwhile, held the authority over the Gunba and was charged with the duty of striving for the Chinatsu of Naikōguwaiteki. Kyūbitto, the Samorinokami, was engaged in a Mitsudan with Ekusu, the Karei.

Samori: 'Ekusu, the Kokujiyō of Konnichi is worsening day by day, the voices of Ensa among the Kokumin fill the Shihau, Dōran are breaking out in Kakushiyo, and Bauto are taking advantage of the chaos to burn Minka, while Gautau roam in broad daylight, cutting down people and dishonoring women; has the world not fallen into disorder like tangled hemp? Even the King Victoria, in his advanced age, worries day and night, and asks me for various ways to achieve Chinatsu, but no matter what I say, because I do not hold the authority over the military, I cannot suppress it even for a moment, even with force. I must find a way to transfer the official authority of the Umorinokami to the Samori, or there will be no other way. Is there not some clever plan?'

Ekusu: 'No matter what one says, the Umorinokami is treacherous and cunning; he has ingratiated himself with Hiruna-hime-sama and wields power as he pleases, leaving the Sateru-sama and the Samorinokami-sama as little more than nominal figures, which is truly regrettable. Furthermore, the Umorinokami harbors ambition and tends to incite Dōran within the Kokunai, seeking to make the disturbances ever greater. If he had moved the army earlier to begin pacification, things would not have come to this, but because the Umorinokami has his own hidden agenda, he watches these disturbances from the sidelines, arguing that using the army against the people is not the true meaning of politics and that provoking the hearts of the people is extremely dangerous, while secretly inciting riots to force your resignation and reveal his ambition to take your place. I am breaking my heart day and night, fearing that if we do not make preparations while there is still time, an irreparable disaster will occur. Moreover, though it is very difficult to say, your wise son, who is to succeed you as Samorinokami, speaks of aesthetic life and soft literature, spouting strange arguments and giving not a single thought to the affairs of the state, which is a truly troublesome matter.'

Samori: 'Indeed, even in the eyes of a parent, I think he is a troublesome fellow. I wish to somehow gently reform him and make him devote himself to loyal service for the sake of the King. However, I have heard rumors that my son Haruna is exchanging love letters with Karuna, the younger sister of the Umorinokami; if that is truly the case, I must find a way to bring about this marriage and prevent disaster before it happens. If we are to settle the disturbances within the Kokunai, we must first prevent internal strife within the castle and remain united, for it would be truly troublesome to fall into the traps of the Umorinokami.'

Ekusu: 'That is a most reasonable opinion. If there is such a connection between Haruna-sama and Karuna-hime, shall we not have Haruna-sama come here, hear his opinions, and devise some plan?'

Samori: 'That is also one method. Ekusu, could you meet with my son for a moment and sound out his opinions?'

Ekusu: 'Yes, I understand. I shall immediately request an audience with Haruna-sama, hear his opinions, and report back to you in detail.'

He left the room of the Samorinokami and visited the living room of Haruna. Haruna was leaning against his desk with all his might, his face slightly pale, as he pored over and read 'Matrimonial Institutions.' There, the sturdy, unrefined, and fiercely loyal Ekusu thrust his old head in, his style stiff and square as if wearing starched, crackling clothes, and quietly slid open the fusuma.

原文（近代文語）

ビク王国の制度は、左守司は王の師範役となり、国内一切の枢要なる事務を取扱ふこととなつてゐた。そして右守司は軍馬の権を握り、内寇外敵の鎮圧に努むる職掌であつた。左守司のキユービツトは、家令のヱクスと共に密談を凝らしてゐる。

左守『ヱクス、どうも今日の国情は日に月に悪化し、国民怨嗟の声は四方に充ち、各所に動乱起り、暴徒は其隙に乗じて民家を焼き放ち、白昼強盗往来し、人を斬り、婦女を辱め、天下は麻の如く紊れて来たではないか。ビクトリア王様も御老齢の身を以て、日夜宸慮を悩ませ玉ひ、余に向つて種々と鎮圧の道をお尋ね遊ばすけれ共、何を云うても斯かる時には兵馬の権を握つてゐない為に、強圧的に一時なり共鎮圧することが出来ない。何とかして右守司の職権を左守に移さなくては仕方がない。何とか妙案があるまいかな』

ヱクス『何と申しましても、右守司、奸侫邪智にして、ヒルナ姫様に取入り、権を恣に致して居りますれば、刹帝利様も、左守司様も、殆ど有名無実の有様、実に残念で厶います。加ふるに右守司、野心を包蔵し、国内の動乱を煽動し、紛擾をして益々大ならしめむとするの傾向が厶いまする。モ少し早く軍隊を動かし、鎮撫にかかつたならば、斯様な事にはならないのですが、右守司は胸に一物ある事とて、此紛擾を傍観し、軍隊を以て民に向ふは、政治の本義ではない、民心を怒らしむるは危険至極だと主張し、蔭から暴動を煽動し、自発的に貴方の退位を余儀なくせしめ、自ら取つて代らむとの野心が仄見えて居ります。何とか今の内に用意を致さねば、取返しのつかぬ大事が起るだらうと、私も昼夜心胆を砕いて居ります。加ふるに、甚申上げ難い事乍ら、左守司の跡をお継ぎ遊ばすべき御賢息様は、耽美生活だとか、軟文学だとか云つて、荐に妙な議論をまくし立て、国家の事などはチツトも念頭において厶らぬのだから、困つた事で厶います』

左守『如何にも、親の目にも、彼奴は困つた奴だと思つてゐるのだ。何とか彼を甘く改心させ、王様の為に舎身的の忠勤を励むやうにさせたいものだなア。併し仄かに聞けば伜のハルナは右守の妹、カルナに対しラブ・レタースを取交してゐるとやら聞いたが、それが果して真なら、何とかして此結婚を成立させ、災を未発に防ぐ手段を廻らさねばならぬ。国内の紛擾を治めむとすれば、先づ城内の暗闘を防ぎ、一致団結しておかねば右守司の術中に陥るやうな事があつては実に困るからなア』

ヱクス『如何にも御尤もな御説、ハルナ様とカルナ姫との間に、左様な消息があるとすれば、一つハルナ様に此処に来て貰つて、御意見を承はつた上、何とか工夫を致さうだありませぬか』

左守『それも一つの方法だ。ヱクス、お前一寸伜に会うて、意見を叩いて来てくれまいかな』

ヱクス『ハイ畏まりました。直様ハルナ様に御面会を願ひ、御意見を承はつた上、詳細なる復命を致しませう』

と左守の室を後にしてハルナの居間を訪れた。ハルナは一生懸命に机に凭れて、少し青白い顔をし乍ら、マトリモーニアル・インスティチューシャンズを繙き、読み耽つてゐた。そこへ頑強な無粋な忠義一途のヱクスが、古い頭をニユツと突出して、糊つけ物のバチバチを着たやうな四角張つたスタイルで、ソツと襖を引あけ、

Ekusu: 'Hai, Gomen kudasaimase. Ekusu de gozaimasu.'

Haruna seemed not to hear this voice, fixing his eyes intently upon the history of the marriage system, tilting his head and pondering over things like getting married or the physiology of love. Ekusu let out a tonkiyō voice,

Ekusu: 'Mōshi, Haruna-sama.'

Startled by the voice calling him, Haruna hurriedly tucked the history of the marriage system into the desk drawer, put on a soshi-nu kao, and placed both hands neatly upon his knees,

Haruna: 'Ya, you are Ekusu, aren't you? You came suddenly to the place where I was studying, so I was taken aback.'

Ekusu: 'You must have been reading nanpa bungaku again, I suppose.'

Haruna, looking startled, shook his head from side to side,

Haruna: 'Aaiyaiya, nanpa no bungaku is not something a seinen should read; I am reading kauha bungaku.'

Ekusu: 'Even so, anata, is there not a book on matrimonial institutions sitting here and there on your desk?'

Haruna: 'Un, that? That is a history of the marriage system, so while it is not necessary for a kikonshiya like you, it cannot be judged as entirely fuhitsueu for us. However, since one must read it once for the sake of kenkiuhikakujiyō between nanpa and kauha, you see.'

Ekusu: 'Moshi, Haruna-sama, I am very fond of nanbungaku. While you were fuzaichiu, I haishiyaku-ed them here and there and took a peek, and they are quite interesting, you know.'

Haruna: 'Did you read our reference books without permission? That is outrageous, is it not?'

Ekusu scratched his head and said,

Ekusu: 'Heh, I am truly sorry. Because it was so interesting, when I showed it secretly to your father, I thought I would get a scolding, thinking he would say, "Such nanbungaku is filthy, throw it into the toilet bowl..." but contrary to my expectations, as expected of Haruna-sama's father, hehehehehe, he is an open-minded person. "Has my son reached this point of thoroughness? As expected of my son. If it is this, it is fine to let him succeed the Samori family..." he said, with unexpected joy, praising it to the skies. Oh, even this stubborn old man was struck with a feeling of surprise, and ever since then, I have completely kaiaku—no, kairyō—myself into nanpa, and this old head of mine has become just a little bit newer. Thanks to this book, I have completely attained the mood of a vita nuova, and my heart has become youthful, I know not where, ahahahaha.'

Such is his rōkai-sa, trying to align his spirit with Haruna's. Haruna, not knowing Ekusu's inner thoughts, was greatly pleased,

Haruna: 'Indeed, it seems Father has also awakened to the times. Iya, arigatai, arigatai. Originally, the Samori family is a house that handles the authority of ruthless military horses, but it is a house of literature, so it is natural for Father to become like that. You will not complain about my love theory as you have until now, will you?'

Ekusu: 'Hai, there is no need to even say it. Even if my head goes bald, my spirit is still seventeen or eighteen; I am in complete sympathy with your principles. Ahahahaha.'

Haruna: 'Since Father has understood human nature to that extent, I wonder if he will surely agree with my principles. Because today's education method involves love letters mixed into ordinary correspondence within letter writers, he must understand the truth that love is best. In today's world where coeducation is practiced, he must have awakened from the delusions of being caught in old morals, such as the distinction between husband and wife, men and women not sharing seats, and other kyōtōgo, or arranged marriages, forced marriages, loveless marriages, and marriages decided by others than oneself, must he not?'

原文（近代文語）

ヱクス『ハイ、御免下さいませ。ヱクスで厶います』

ハルナは此声が耳に這入らぬとみえて、一生懸命に結婚制度史の上に目を注ぎ、ゲツティング・マリドだとか、フヰジオロヂー・オブ・ラブなどと首をかたげて考へて居る。ヱクスは頓狂な声を出して、

ヱクス『モーシ、ハルナ様』

と呼はる声にハツと気がつき、慌てて結婚制度史を机の引出しにしまひこみ、素知らぬ顔をして、膝の上に両手をキチンとおき、

ハルナ『ヤ、お前はヱクスだないか、僕が勉強してる所へ突然やつて来たものだから、面くらつて了つたよ』

ヱクス『又軟派文学でも耽読してゐられましたのでせう』

ハルナはハツとし乍ら、首を左右に振り、

ハルナ『アアイヤイヤ、軟派の文学などは青年の読むべきものでない、俺は硬派文学を耽読してゐるのだ』

ヱクス『それでも、貴方、机の上にマトリモーニアル・インスティチューシャンズがチヨコチヨコおいてあるだありませぬか』

ハルナ『ウンあれか、あれは結婚制度史だから、お前のやうな既婚者は必要はないが、吾々には強ち不必要と断ずることは出来ない。併し乍ら少し許り軟派でも硬派を研究比較上、一度は読んでおかなくちやならないからなア』

ヱクス『もし、ハルナ様、私は軟文学が大好物で厶いますよ。貴方の不在中にも、チヨコチヨコ拝借しまして、覗き読みをさして頂きましたが、随分面白いものですな』

ハルナ『吾々の参考書を無断で、お前は読んだのか、怪しからぬだないか』

ヱクスは頭を掻き乍ら、

ヱクス『ヘー、誠に済みませぬ、余り面白いものですから、お父上に、ソツとお見せ申しました所、此様な軟文学は汚らはしい、雪隠壺へでも放り込んで了へ……とお目玉を頂くかと思ひの外、流石はハルナ様のお父さま丈あつて、ヘヘヘヘヘ、開けたお方ですよ。内の伜もここ迄徹底したか、流石は私の息子だ。これならば左守の後を継がしても大丈夫だ……と以ての外のお喜び、口を極めて御讃嘆、イヤもう此頑固爺も意外の感に打たれ、それから後といふものは、スツカリ軟派に改悪……否改良致しまして、此古い頭もチツと許り新しくなりました。此書籍のお蔭で全くヰータ・ヌーバの気分になり、どこともなしに心が若やいで来ましたがな、アハハハハ』

とうまくハルナの精神にバツを合さうとしてゐる其老獪さ。ハルナはヱクスの心中を知らず、大に喜んで、

ハルナ『成程父上様も、時代に目覚め遊ばしたと見えるなア、イヤ有難い有難い。元より左守家は殺伐な軍馬の権を扱ふ家だない、文学の家だから、お父さまがさうなられるのも当然だ。お前も今迄の様に拙者の恋愛論に就て、此上ゴテゴテ苦情は云はないだらうなア』

ヱクス『ハイ、仰せ迄も厶いませぬ、頭は禿げても、気はヤツパリ十七八、貴方の御主義に全部共鳴して居ります。アハハハハ』

ハルナ『父上様はそこ迄人間味がお分りになつた以上は、僕の主義にキツト賛成して下さるだらうかな。レター・ライタの中に普通一般の往復文の中にラブ・レターズが混入してゐる今日の教育法だから、ラブ・イズ・ベストの真理は分つてゐるだらうなア。コーエデュケーシヤンの行はれてゐる今日、古い道徳に捉はれて、夫婦別あり、男女席を同うせずなどと、旧套語をふり廻したり、門閥結婚、強圧結婚、無情結婚、自分以外の者が定める結婚などの迷夢は醒まされたであらうなア』

Ekusu: 'Do not worry yourself at all. Your father is a Jeunesse Intellectuelle, you see. If you keep looking around restlessly, he will become far more modern than you, I assure you.'

Haruna: 'If that is the case, then your policy is that you will never interfere with my getting married. If I am forced into what you have been calling an amazing marriage, a Bunmeijinshi like me will suffer psychic trauma and, before I know it, become hysterical. For a parent today, the path of a wise parent is to leave everything to the free will of their child, you know.'

Ekusu: 'You truly possess a sharp Zunau. Like father, like son; oh my, even this stubborn Ekusu is impressed. In that regard, is the person you intend to make your ideal wife already decided?'

Haruna: 'It is not exactly decided, nor is it undecided; I am currently in the midst of Jiyukukauchiu. I had been thinking that if there were some good Kikuwai, I would like to consult with you, but since the Shisaujiyō Kiyori between you and me has been so extreme until now, I could not bring myself to say it, and I have continued to suffer from Hanmonkunau until this very day.'

Ekusu: 'Hahaha, is such Goshinpai necessary? Just as a daughter would confide in her Uba, I am the Karei of the Samorike, so in the unlikely event that your father passes away, I shall be your direct Gokerai; I would like you to speak of anything without any Fukuzau. To not confide the secrets of your heart to me, your Karei, is truly a distant attitude; Ekusu feels hurt by this.'

He deliberately wipes away fake tears with his sleeve. Haruna becomes full of himself,

Haruna: 'Hey, if that is the case, I will tell you. In truth, I have an agreement for getting married to Karuna-hime, the younger sister of Migimoritsukasa.'

Ekusu: 'What, what did you say? You say that a secret promise has been concluded with Karuna-sama... Heee... You are quite the capable person yourself. This Ekusu is deeply impressed from the bottom of his heart. Yes, by all means, go ahead; I, Ekusu, your Karei, support you with both hands.'

Haruna: 'Even if you support me, if I do not ask for the will of my father, who is the most important person, I cannot yet feel at ease. Think about it carefully. The secret struggle between the Migimori and Samori families is becoming more intense with every passing moment, you know.'

Ekusu: 'You are saying something that does not suit you. Is the secret struggle between the two families not just a secret struggle? Is there any reason to sacrifice even the problem of marriage, which is of vital importance to one's life, for that sake? No, that is a different problem. I guarantee that your father will surely agree regarding this matter. Once your resolve is set, I, Ekusu, will take the labor of mediation, however inadequate I may be, as soon as possible. Please be at ease.'

Haruna looks as if he is very happy, with a smile that he cannot contain spreading across his cheeks, and he looks down shyly. Ekusu, nodding to himself, thinking he has succeeded,

Ekusu: 'Haruna-sama, since good deeds should be done quickly, let us go and speak to your father immediately and negotiate with the other party.'

He stands up from this place with a lively air and proceeds to report every detail to the living room of Samoritsukasa.

Left behind, Haruna feels as if he is ascending to heaven,

Haruna: 'Ah, has the time come? My father is quite open-minded. Banko Shin-ō-sama, I pray that you protect this love so that it may be perfectly fulfilled, and bless it. Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase, Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase.'

He clasps his hands and prays for the realization of his marriage. From the ceiling, the sound of mice running while squeaking 'kuu kuu kuu, chiu chiu chiu, chee chee, dodo dodo, bata bata bata' can be heard.

ヱクス『エツ、何と仰せられます、あのカルナ様と情約締結が整うたと仰有るのですか……ヘーエ……何と貴方も辣腕家ですな。此ヱクスもゾツコンから感服致しました。ヤ、大におやりなさいませ、双手をあげて家令のヱクス賛成致します』

ハルナはさも嬉しげに、包みきれぬやうな笑を頬に泛べて、恥かしげに俯いた。ヱクスはしてやつたりと、心中に頷き乍ら、

とイソイソとして、此場を立出で左守司の居間に一伍一什を報告すべく進み行く。

後にハルナは天にも上る心地して、

ハルナ『あああ、時節が来たかなア、よく開けた父上だ。盤古神王様、何卒此恋が完全に成就致します様に、守らせ玉へ、幸ひ玉へ、惟神霊幸倍ませ惟神霊幸倍ませ』

と合掌し、結婚の成立を祈願した。天井から鼠がクウクウクウ　チウチウチウ　チーチー　ドドドドド、バタバタバタと鳴き乍ら走る声が聞えて来る。
