§ 1

King Victoria was invited to the banquet of Waboku, and the two generals Oniharuwake and Kumehiko, along with Suparu, Emishi, Shiyamu, and Maruta, took their seats in order of precedence as the guest side in the Jiyōza.

On the other side, Setsuteiri, the Samori, the Umori, as well as Taruman, Haruna, Ekusu, Shiēru, and others lined up, and the Shiyukuen for the restoration of peace began.

King Victoria bowed respectfully before the two generals Oniharuwake and Kumehiko.

Setsuteiri: 'Riyōshiyōgunsama, for you to have saved our clan this time through your Gojinji and Oboshimeshi, I have no words to express my Orei.'

He stood in an awkward position, as if grateful for having his life spared after his house was burned by thieves and his family killed, yet he offered his greetings while swallowing his resentment and feigning joy.

Oniharuwake became Itakedaka, sat on a folding stool with an air of nonchalance, and spoke.

Oniharuwake: 'Ah, no, Setsuteiri-dono, I am humbled by your words. As I am a rough soldier, I am greatly troubled by stiff etiquette. I shall reveal my true nature as a wild warrior and, without regard for your noble residence, sit on this folding stool and commit this rudeness. I hope you will not take it ill, Setsuteiri-dono, and will forgive me.'

Setsuteiri: 'Ha, what are you saying? It is the value of a soldier to be simple. In this world where empty rituals and formalities are in vogue, a naked and unadorned soldier like you is truly reliable. Come, please, have a drink.'

He offered a cup. Oniharuwake thrust out his thick, hairy hand, held the cup forward, and as it was filled to the brim by Setsuteiri's hand, he drank it down in one gulp.

Oniharuwake: 'Ah, that is a fine sake; it has a wonderful flavor that permeates the five viscera and six entrails. Setsuteiri-dono, please accept a cup from me.'

He thrust it out casually with a gulp. Setsuteiri, fearing that it would be a disaster to offend such a wolf in sheep's clothing, accepted the cup with an air of satisfaction, bowed two or three times, and spoke.

Setsuteiri: 'This, this is the cup of a renowned general; I humbly accept it.'

Oniharuwake: 'Ya, there is no need for reserve. Please drink plenty; it is not as if the sake will hurt my purse, and you may eat as much of the feast as you like. Ah, if you receive a cup from a victorious warrior, perhaps you too will become a hero by following his example. Hahahaha.'

He laughed with the boisterous laughter of a hero. The woman sitting fearfully by Oniharuwake's side had a different appearance, but to Setsuteiri's eyes, he could not help but think she looked like Hirunahime. However... there are many women who look alike in this world... he thought, dismissing it lightly as a matter of his failing eyesight. And he did not realize at all that the reason Setsuteiri and his people were saved this time was due to the desperate efforts of both Hirunahime and Karunahime. Also, Haruna... the beautiful woman by General Kumehiko's side, though her appearance was different, looked exactly like his beloved wife Karuna. And seeing her cast glances toward him from time to time, he wondered if it might indeed be Karuna, and if this unexpected and joyful liberation might be due to Karuna's mediation... thinking this, he stole glances at the woman's face... the more he looked, the more she resembled her... and as he thought this, he looked at another woman and could see no one but Hirunahime. Not only Haruna, but the hearts of the Samori, the Umori, and all the others held the same suspicion. General Kumehiko became Itakedaka and spoke.

Kumehiko: 'Oi, Karunahime, you are my beloved wife. How about you sing a song with your nightingale-like voice in a place like this? It is extremely bleak in the camp with only men. Your arrival here is a divine arrangement, the one and only wish-fulfilling jewel that brings my heart to life. My, what a beautiful creature you are.'
