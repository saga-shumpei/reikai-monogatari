Chapter 15: 婆と婆 [727]

婆と婆〔727〕

§ 1

Takahime, together with Kuwanshiu, steered the new ship presented by Tamanohime, and at last arrived at Shōdodaira, the largest giant island of the Setonaikai. Mooring the boat at the Isobata, she stayed on this island for a while, and in order to search out the whereabouts of the treasure thoroughly even into the valleys of the mountain, she climbed up to the Chiufuku of Kunishiroyama. Here there was a massive grotto, which from ancient times had been called a demon cave where monsters lurked.

Kuwanshiu: 'Takahime-sama, there is a splendid grotto here. That wretch of a Tamanohime, if by any chance she might have hidden it in such a place. The somabito said that it is a deep grotto where no human has ever gone in and out, so shall we explore it once?'

Takahime: 'Wait and let me think for a while. Since it is Tamanohime who is cautious to the end, the moment both of us enter this grotto, if that wretch of a comrade whom I set to watch should block up the hole and pack us in like pickles, that truly would be a terrible affair. Let us first investigate this neighborhood four or five chō square before doing anything.'

Kuwanshiu: 'Such worry should be unnecessary. If so, please wait here, you. I will go and explore alone...'

Before saying this, bending her body low into the grotto, she crept in slowly as if crawling. Afterwards, Takahime folded her arms, held a folding stool, sank into deep thought, and spoke to herself.

Takahime: 'Ah, whatever I do and whatever I make ends up clashing like a crossbill's beak—that perhaps is the reward for going against the divine will of the Great Freedom Heaven, the Great Kuninowake no Mikoto. Once becoming a subordinate of Oigumohiko and praising the doctrines of Brahmanism, yet once again I grew to yearn for the Changing Male of the Hinomoto teachings, and naming it the Urayanai teachings, I concocted a middle teaching, before I knew it I ceased even to chant the name of the Great Freedom Heaven, believed in Kunichiritsunime once more, made a re-turn and went backward to Susanoo no Mikoto's Hinomoto teachings, and now again I am made to suffer miserably for the sake of the executives of the Hinomoto teachings, and how on earth is the Hinodewake no Kami faring? Lately Takahime's spirit is also strange, and the Hinodewake no Kami has also somehow become unreliable. A Hinodewake no Kami for whom the whereabouts of such a glowing jewel is unknown is truly of no practical use. If by any chance nowadays he might be suffering from eye disease, could it not be so? Ah, already enveloped in the clouds of anger, even an inch ahead can no longer be seen. Where should I search to find the whereabouts of this jewel? Might it not be that the earnest Kuromahime has already gotten her hands on it? Takahime's present condition is said to wander completely lost in a dense fog or dreaming before the rocks. Ah-ah, I have grown sick of the gods. From time to time from within my belly they say and let me hear various things, but looking back and gazing behind, not a single divine oracle has ever hit the mark, and to the spirit residing in my own body, somehow favoritism always attaches itself, so even though I myself thought it somewhat suspicious, right up until this very moment I have persevered as the living shrine of the Hinodewake no Kami and carried it through, but even when encountering a shipwreck at the Nada of Akashi, and again having my ship snatched away at Iejima and meeting with miserable hardships akin to exile on an island, to have become the vessel of a blind god who lets me know nothing at all is simply piling shame upon shame day by day, ah, what should I do? To turn back to the sacred place now and offer apologies to the preceptor of Kotobunowake and to Mokusuke is also foolish, and since there is no help for it, if you eat poison, lick even the plate. Now that it has come to this, after all I am not the living shrine of the Hinodewake no Kami... To say such a thing after having put on such airs hitherto is not something I am obliged to utter... Let me confer with my guardian spirit and do things after that. ... Hey, hey, guardian spirit in my belly, stir yourself a little and answer my questions. This time I will not listen to your usual nonsense. Disclose only the sincerity of the net price. Depending on your answer, even Takahime will not listen to what you say from today onward, so come, will you not stir quickly, will you not open your mouth?'

§ 2

Clenching her fist, she was striking herself with all her might around the pit of her stomach. For some reason, today of all days, the possessing entity calling itself the God of Sunrise did not let out even a single squeak, but was shrinking near the lower part of her abdomen and clinging there like a small ball. Takahime gripped the sphere fiercely from above with all her strength,

Takahime: 'Now then, what do you say, will you give me an answer or not? Until now, you have often made a plaything of the body of a fine henjiyōnanshi lineage. Shall I crush you to pieces?'

Putting so much force into it that the skin of her stomach felt as if it were tearing, she attempted to crush the firm, hardened lump by grinding it. From inside her belly,

'Ah, ouch, ouch. I will confess. Please forgive me. I am the minion of the nine-tailed golden fox, the spirit of Kotsunehime who once tormented Ōyashimahikonomikoto and the other gods at the palace in Jerusalem. A branch spirit of that Kotsunehime doubted, and your physical body was formed and born into this world. And since I am a branch of the same mitama, I cannot possibly possess anyone other than you.'

Takahime: 'You have confessed well. I pretty much thought it was something like that. However, since we share the same mitama karmic connection, I cannot go along with separating as you say, and in truth I do not want to part with you either. Still, talking such weak-kneed nonsense about being the soul of Kotsunehime—what kind of pathetic sounds are you blowing? Once you appeared from the beginning pretending to be the God of Sunrise, why not pass yourself off as the God of Sunrise through and through? I despise such faint-hearted guardian deities. Now then, from here on I will give you strict training, so you must not use this physical body as freely as you have up until now. Takahime is going to use you this time, so keep that in mind.'

Spirit of Kotsunehime: 'If the physical body uses the spirit, will that not result in the body predominating over the spirit?'

Takahime: 'Eh? Once again, do even you start arguing? People in this world all put on a fine-looking face on the surface and spit out that the spirit predominates over the body, but what uncomprehending fools they are. How could such a foolish thing ever be carried out in a world of matter? Body predominates over spirit—that is the truth of heaven and earth. Up until now I have followed your spirit and guarded the principle of spirit predominating over body, and has not a single decent thing come of it? The world belongs exclusively to body predominating over spirit. A false lifestyle is something this Takahime refuses to adopt; from now on, I am resolved to pull myself together, stand bare and overtly before the world, and champion the principle of body predominating over spirit, so you should understand that as well.'

Spirit of Kotsunehime: 'Ah, it cannot be helped. No matter what I say, there is no way you would listen to me at the crest of the flood, so for a little while I will drop the curtain of silence. Ah-ha-ha-ha-ha.'

She was carrying on a soliloquy by way of self-questioning and answering.

There was a propagandist who had climbed up here covered in filth. Seeing the middle-aged woman hanging her head before the cave and monologuing,

Propagandist: 'Ha-ha, this fellow is crazy. She talks to herself and answers herself. I had heard since long ago that there is an illness where one naturally speaks things from inside one's belly, and this must surely be what they talked about regarding such a person. As a divine possession, the tone is a little off. However, illness or whatever it may be is fine, as long as something—anything—speaks from inside the belly, then truth or falsehood aside, it is convenient for passing it off as divine possession, but... For someone like me who, no matter how long I practice or perform Chinkon, not a single sound of "un" or "sun" comes out from inside my belly, I am truly troubled. Over there I tried imitating divine possession and failed, over here I tried imitating it and failed, and finally at the chieftain's residence in Sumoto, at the very moment when I was within a hair's breadth of being exposed by his wife and my liver was frightened out of me, the master who was supposed to be dead came back, completely ruining my face, and the suffering and terror when I slipped out of the toilet like a caged rat—thanks to Lord Daijitaiten I finally arrived safely at the seashore, found a single boat, forcibly rowed out into the open sea, was blown away by a violent gale, and came all the way to this island. Yet when I crept through the toilet, my propagandist robe seemed to have served as a toilet rag, for a bizarre stench still remains. Ah, this is a troublesome matter. Perhaps I should reform once more, strip this old woman naked, and exchange clothes with her for her stinking ones.'

§ 3

Before long, without knowing when it began, the whispering grew louder and reached the ears of Takahime.

Takahime understood and perceived most of the Sendenshi's soliloquy,

Takahime: 'Otsuhononono, you dung-smeared Sendenshi, to think of harbouring such an outrageous ambition as swapping the privy's rag with the divine robes of the living shrine of Hinodewake! I have already seen straight through to the very bottom of your belly, so...'

Sendenshi: 'Hey, I do not know what kind of old crone you are, but if you have seen through my inner thoughts from such an early stage, it cannot be helped. I will drop it. My clothes have become thickly coated with golden-hued excrement, turning them into garments of filth. Even if I were to strip you of yours, they are still garments of filth. ...Hey, old lady, won't you wash my clothes for me at some valley stream around here?'

Sendenshi: 'What a nuisance to be mistaken for a laundry hag by the Sendenshi of the living shrine of Hinodewake. How could there be a laundry hag in such a high place without even water? Go off toward the banks of a valley stream and find one. Old hags are fixtures of rivers. This old hag is the foundational laundry hag of the Yamato spirit who washes the mitama of the people of the world. ...Heng, putting on a Sendenshi face, what are you playing dumb about? What a thoroughly foolish rascal you are.'

With that, yielding to her lingering vexation, she pointed her halberd toward the Sendenshi.

Sendenshi: 'My, what a sharp-tongued old hag you are. Aren't you that same loud-mouthed old lady Takahime who is establishing the Urai religion right about now?'

Takahime: 'That very Takahime is this physical body. How do you know my name?'

Sendenshi: 'I am the man who was called Kiji at that time. When you came out to the knees of Momogamihiko and listened to the Brahman teaching, I was listening too. When I think of those times, I have aged considerably... Now I have been granted the name Tomohiko, a Sendenshi of the Brahman religion, and I am going around propagating it all over Jitentōshima.'

Takahime: 'Ah, is that so. I wonder if you could really propagate anything with a mug like that.'

Tomohiko: 'Having worn myself out for the myriad forms of propaganda and acted with the momentum of a lion tearing through prey, I ended up plunging straight into the dust of dung, and it was almost the end of me. Ahahahaha.'

He laughed heartily.

Takahime: 'If you have hardened your dung-bravery to that extent and plunged right into the privy, then from here on it is an uphill climb. Privy insects born of dung will eventually crawl up, become jade flies free to fly through the air, and land on the heads of even the most important people, scattering dung about. Why don't you show some dung-spunk from now on and work together with me? Though when I say show spunk, I do not mean going to the privy and exposing your backside!'

Tomohiko: 'You really are a sharp-tongued dung-crone, aren't you. But still, are you continuing to establish an intermediate religion called the Urai religion or something?'

Sendenshi: 'Even the Brahman religion was a lily-livered teaching that fled home when chased about by the forces of the Three-Five religion from the stronghold of Momogamihiko at Mount Ōe. I tried turning back from the Urai religion to the original Three-Five religion due to a slight convenience, but this fellow is also a constipation religion. Since it is a teaching with no openings whatsoever, it is full of dead ends everywhere, and besides, clueless fellows occupy the executive positions, so even if you think to act, there is no way to move your hands or feet. Still, among the Brahman, Uraru, Urai, and Three-Five teachings, the one with the most power by far is still the Way of Three-Five. On this island, there are three supreme treasures of the universe, and if we can just track down their hiding place, the Three-Five religion will become entirely our own to do with as we please, and we will splendidly be able to accomplish the divine government. Thinking to search out the whereabouts of the jade, I came to this island bringing one retainer along with me, but depending on the circumstances, I will make you a retainer too, so tell me the path you have taken up to now. First of all, I want to hear about the karma of your clothes. I heard that you escaped from the privy, but whose privy was that?'
