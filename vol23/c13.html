Chapter 13: 捨 Small Boat [725]

捨小舟〔725〕

§ 1

The dense fog that had enveloped Takahime's party dissipated after a while, and the surroundings grew bright as before. Wondering what had become of Tamanohime's whereabouts, Takahime and her followers searched around frantically with bloodshot eyes, but without a trace of any kind, they finally arrived in a group all the way to the boat landing. When they looked, neither the boat Tamanohime had ridden in nor Takahime's boat was there. Stamping her feet in vexation, Takahime lamented,

Takahime: 'Ah, how vexing and regrettable! Could that wretch Oseto, taking advantage of the thick fog, have dug out the three treasures, boarded a boat, and fled back home? Even so, what is regrettable is that she seems to have somehow carried away the boat as well. It is just as if we were subjected to banishment to a remote island. ...Now, now, Tōsuke-sama, this is primarily because you lack presence of mind. The boatman ought to have stayed attached to the boat, but forgetting your official duty you came climbing up the mountain like a sendenshi, and that is why we have encountered such a plight. Come now, what are you going to do about it?'

Tōsuke: 'As if there were any such thing as what are you going to do about it! Having had my precious tools of trade stolen away, I have neither hands nor feet to do anything. There is no way for me to return either, and primarily because you started a rebellion and came coming out to a place like this, divine punishment has struck. Come, what will you do about my boat?'

Takahime: 'Oh my, to think you could say such a thing! While bringing along a precious customer, what are you doing having the boat stolen? For the most part, you must have plotted with Oseto's wretch to match minds and shut up Hinodewake in such a place. There is simply no relaxing one's vigilance even for a moment.'

Tōsuke burst into great anger, and

Tōsuke: 'If I speak softly thinking you are a woman, you make unreasonable and impossible demands! This Tōsuke is no villain like you. I am a boatman of established renown whose honesty is absolute. You have smeared mud on a man's face, haven't you. Prepare yourself now to face my decisive resolve.'

Jerking her chin, Takahime said,

Takahime: 'Ohohoho! No matter how strong your strength may be, we are five and you are one—it is utterly hopeless. Rather than that, how about making a clean, neat confession?'

Tōsuke: 'When you say confess, can something I know nothing about be confessed? Do not make too much a fool of me!'

Tōsuke spoke thus.

Takahime: 'Ohohoho! Oh, what a feigned innocence that is. Thinking you do not know, you chatter away with your excuses; others may not know, but it is far too presumptuous an insect to try and hoodwink Hinodewake, whose divine power is so high that it sees through to the very bottom of a human heart. How much money did you receive from Tamanohime? You played a fine trick, didn't you.'

Driven to the utmost bounds of exasperation, Tōsuke could not even speak, his lips trembling violently, fists clenched, drowning in tears of chagrin. Takahime said,

Takahime: 'Is that not so, you can have no excuse. No matter how skillfully you make excuses, you cannot use Kototama before the gods, can you. You too must be regrettable to have your mask stripped off before a multitude, but that is reaping what you sow. In short, it is equivalent to tightening your own neck with the rope you wove yourself—ah, truly, truly a pitiful thing. You must have understood that evil schemes ultimately cannot come to fruition, surely. When we suffered shipwreck on Awaji Island, timing the moment, you came sneaking along to deceive this Takahime, and the thought of managing it sweetly has also turned into soap bubbles; it was instantly seen through by the divine power of Hinodewake, and what is that attitude of yours? Despite being a big man, whining and weeping a pitiful sight. Since you are in the same category as Tamanohime after all, you ought to know the hiding place of the jewel as well. How about it, you—Tamanohime must have brought the jewel back with her, didn't she.'

With his mouth twitching convulsively,

Tōsuke: 'Shi-Shi-I know nothing at all! Do-Do-Do not make a fool of me!'

He finally shouted out in an odd, hysterical voice.

Takahime: 'Shi-Shi-It cannot be that you do not know. Shi-Shi-How obstinate you are. You said do not make a fool of me, but since you started off trying to make a fool of Hinodewake and ended up looking foolish yourself, there is nothing that can be done about it.'

Kanshū: 'Pardon me, Takahime-sama, without the vital boat, can we not do anything at all? Leaving such talk for next after next, as an urgent issue must we not begin from searching for the boat, otherwise how can we feel at ease?'

Takahime: 'Ohohohoho, you are young and therefore you worry, but as long as Tamanohime's cohort Tōsuke is around, he will surely send someone else, put on an innocent face, and bring the boat. At that time, Tōsuke you must swiftly leap into that boat, and with a single bound flee back with this strategy; when the boat comes next time, you must by no means let Tōsuke go. Since if this fellow boards, we too will return together, you four fellows keep watch over this fellow. And when the boat comes, one of you from among here shall come to welcome me. Until then, you must neither let go nor fail to seize both the boat and the boatman!'

Saying this flatly, she headed toward the mountain and climbed up with swift steps. Behind her, the five men occupied seats at the shore, gazing out at the wide sea surface with bewildered faces. Tōsuke appeared to have finally softened in spirit, and began to speak little by little.

Tōsuke: 'Oi, you fellows, do you think of me as an evil person such as Takahime said? I have had my vital trade tools stolen away, and on top of that, an unexpected difficult problem has been cast upon me; such an unprofitable thing has never happened. Truly, one never knows when a calamity will come, oh my.'

Kansshū: 'I do not particularly think of you as an evil person, but our general Takahime is completely crazy once she starts talking like that, so we could not casually talk back and kept silent out of patience; however, judging from your appearance and your complexion, I think you are not at all the sort of man who is in league with Tamanohime.'

Tōsuke: 'Ah, well said. Because of that, I too am a little relieved. What kind of impressions do you all hold? Please speak without hiding anything.'

The three all at once:

'Just as Kansshū said, somehow we cannot think that you are bad. Truly it is a great disaster, and we offer our sympathies. No matter what, our general is like that, so we are troubled. It is hateful that when Tamanohime fled back she drifted the boat away somewhere, but even so, if you were to be abandoned by Takahime now, you would lose face immediately, ah.'

Tōsuke: 'Everyone, you don't need such worries. I am a person from Awaji Island, but even if you three or five people were to play and eat for many days, my house would hardly be ruined. Though I am doing this because I like being a boatman, I am the master of a first-rate wealthy household on Awaji Island. For certain reasons I am acting as a boatman, but I am a little different from ordinary fare-collecting boatmen. I will take charge of your fortunes, so do not worry.'

Tsurukimi: 'That is grateful, but is it true?'

Tōsuke: 'If it were not true, what would it be? Tōsuke, who has taken the name of an honest man from of old, is none other than myself. How could a man tell a lie, even tentatively?'

Tsurukimi: 'Hearing it said like that, perhaps it is true.'

While they were thus talking, due to a shift in the wind, Tōsuke's owned boat, which had once been drifted out to the offing, caught their eyes as it gradually drew closer toward this direction. Tōsuke clapped his hands,

Tōsuke: 'Ah, how joyous! The boat that had been drifting thanks to the wind seems somehow about to drift right over here. Everyone, rejoice!'

The four stood up and, while gazing at the sea surface, watched the boat approaching as it was blown by the wind, and unintentionally clapping their hands, shouted 'Urō urō!'.

Tōsuke: 'It is already ours. Shunkun's exile to the island also seems to have finally received a pardon ship. Come, we must return no matter what. If we stay here for a long time, there is no telling whether we will be enveloped in dense fog and suffer divine punishment just like before... Now, Kansshū-sama, please go quickly and call Takahime-sama, for we shall make preparations for the boat.'

Kansshū: 'Oi Tsurukimi, Kiyokimi, Takekimi, firmly grasp the boat and take care of Tōsuke-sama. I will hurry and call the general.'

Tōsuke: 'Ahahaha, I will hardly flee and return either. Be at ease and search this mountain and come back. I will be waiting... But when I return to my home...'

Kuwanshiu went straight up toward the forest in high spirits to make a report to Takahime.

The boat gradually approached the water's edge.

Clapping his hands, Tōsuke cried out,

Tōsuke: 'Ah, Funagamisama, thank you very much. Now, now, all three of you, get in, get in.'

Tsurukō: 'Takahime-sama and Kuwanshiu are not yet in sight, so please wait a little for them.'

Tōsuke: 'I would wait, but I shall go home and wait there. Now, get in, get in.'

Tsurukō: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha! Those two are banished to the island after all. Ah, well, that is fine too. Somehow or other, the Sun Deity is attached to them, so nothing too extraordinary will happen. Well, it will be good for them to calmly and steadily perform their spiritual training.'

So saying, the four of them nimbly leaped into the boat and began to row out, making the oars creak.

Takahime, who was deeply suspicious, had been hiding herself in the nearby forest since a moment ago, eavesdropping on the conversation of them all, but crying out that this was a terrible crisis, she ran to the shore along with Kuwanshiu, and said,

Takahime: 'Hey, hey, Tōsuke-sama, where are you going? What do you intend to do with me?'

Tōsuke: 'I am going nowhere. I am returning all the way to Sumoto in Awaji.'

Takahime: 'Look, that is different from our promise, is it not? Please bring the boat over here for a moment. Since I too must ride and return, if you do such a thing, I will not pay your wages up to now.'

Tōsuke: 'I am a little different from a Tōsuke who makes a living by taking wages. Though I look like this, I am the wealthiest person on Awaji Island. Since I am acting as a boatman out of a hobby, as for wages, I flatly decline them right from my end. If you want money, I will give you as much as you like from here. Well, the two of you, take your time and do your training on this island.'

And once again he began to row the oars.

Takahime, at the top of her lungs,

Takahime: 'Hey, hey, is there such an unreasonable thing? Divine punishment will strike you.'

Tōsuke: 'The ones upon whom divine punishment has fallen are the two of you. Since your minds are not very wholesome, I am leaving you behind for the sake of your training, so be grateful. ... Hey, hey, Tsurukō, Kiyokō, Takekō, you were helped onto my boat by me, so in every single action you are to do as I say.'

The three of them in unison,

'We understand, we humbly ask for your guidance in all things.'

Kuwanshiu: 'Oyez, oyez, Tōsuke-sama, that is going too far, please bring the boat over here for once.'

Tōsuke stuck out his tongue playfully, and the three of them also looked at each other and similarly stuck out their tongues.

Tōsuke: 'I appreciate the offer, but since there is a great deal of luggage today, perhaps I must decline. On top of this, if humans laden with many sins were to board, it would capsize and become a great nuisance, you see.'

The three of them all at once repeated Tōsuke's exact words.

Tōsuke rowed the oars without a care, singing a boat song with a cool voice as he gradually receded into the distance toward the island.

The two of them, Takahime and Kuwanshiu, stamped their feet at the shore and called out, 'Oyez, oyez.'

Takahime: 'Hey, hey, Kuwanshiu, you are quite a fool. Although I told you so much as if chewing and swallowing it for you, you listen to what people say with your rear end, so divine punishment has fallen and you are made to encounter such a plight.
