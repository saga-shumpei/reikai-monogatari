Chapter 9: High 姫騒 [721]

高姫騒〔721〕

§ 1

A certain beauty entered, passing through the gate of Wakahiko. The two gatekeepers, Akikō and Shimekō, seeing this figure,

Akikō: 'Mosimosi, I know not from what place you are, but for what business do you come? Pray let me, the gatekeeper, first hear the purpose of your business.'

Woman: 'I have some slight matter... At any rate, I desire to meet the master.'

Shimekō: 'It is not allowed to let pass a woman whose name is even unknown.'

Woman: 'Are you not the gatekeeper of this place? With such a state of things that you know not what sort of person I am, can you perform the duties of a gatekeeper?'

Chastising them thus, she advanced briskly deep into the interior.

Shimekō: 'Ah, utterly hopeless. A woman is one who pushes through with a strong rear. Yet that woman was, from nowhere in particular, a woman of high refinement, but whatever can she be, ah.'

Akikō: 'If by any chance it might be the master's mistress, you know.'

So saying, he showed his little finger.

Shimekō: 'Restricting ourselves to the master of this house, how could such a thing be? There is a distinguished mistress named Tamanohime-sama, but now at the foot of Futatabisan in the forest of Ikuta, she has built a hall of the Misoshiru-kyō and is zealously active, so it is said. The husband and wife, far apart across the provinces, faithfully perform the divine work, and it is a tremendous reputation, so how could such a thing be?'

Akikō: 'Even so, they say there is such a thing as being beyond one's calculation. If by any chance it might not be that Tamanohime-sama has graced us with her presence?'

Shimekō: 'Speak nonsense, how could Tamanohime-sama deign to arrive alone? At the very least, one or two attendants ought surely to be following her.'

Akihiko: 'That is... there is such a thing as traveling incognito. Surely the master grew to yearn for her, and she set out upon an imperial incognito journey, no doubt.'

Thus the gatekeepers were beside themselves with the rumor of the beauty.

The beauty advanced deeply into the interior, stood at the entrance porch, and lowering her voice,

Woman: 'Is Wakahiko-sama at home?'

She inquired. Kyūsuke, the entrance keeper, ran out at this voice,

Kyūsuke: 'Yes, the master Wakahiko is now in the inner rooms. Who might you be? Pray let me hear your name.'

Woman: 'There are circumstances by which I cannot well tell my name. As soon as you meet me you will understand, so pray be so good as to convey that "a woman has come alone to visit."'

Kyūsuke: 'If I take you in without ascertaining your full name, I shall be terribly scolded, so pray tell me your name; otherwise I absolutely cannot make the introduction.'

Woman: 'If that be so, I shall pass through of my own accord to see him.'

Kyūsuke: 'This is an outrageous thing to say. This place is my barrier station, and to pass so recklessly is not permitted.'

Woman: 'If that be so, pray introduce me.'

Kyūsuke: 'To look at you, you appear to be a person of considerable character, but do you not understand what I say? The entrance keeper must protect his official responsibility, so however much I might wish to let you pass, one whose name is unknown I know not if she be a phantom or whatever. Regrettable though it is, pray return home.'

The beauty raised her voice slightly,

Woman: 'Now, Kyūsuke, you have not yet ascended to the sacred grounds nor have you ever come to the forest of Ikuta, so it is no wonder you do not understand, but if you hold the post of entrance keeper even without proclaiming your name, you ought mostly to understand. Whatever you may say, I am going to pass, so do not get in my way.'

With a tone of voice that somewhere possessed a strong point.

Kyūsuke tilted his head,

Kyūsuke: 'What, are you not the mistress? Ah, no, no, you cannot be the mistress. The precious Tamanohime-sama performs wondrous divine works, and though she is called a wife, her status has become vastly higher, and it is said that even the master of this house cannot easily approach her side. Such a distinguished personage has no reason to deign to arrive lightly and alone. Ah, this fellow is surely a demonic thing... Now, now, woman, passing through is absolutely not permitted.'

§ 2

Saying thus, he shouted in a loud voice.

Wakahiko, wondering what had happened at Kiusuke's loud voice, rose from his seat and appeared at this place, looking at the beauty's figure in startling surprise,

Ah, you are Tama...

starting to say thus, he suddenly shut his mouth, sat upright again,

I do not know what maid you may be, but please pass through to the inner chambers.

Woman: 'Yes, thank you very much. To come and disturb you amidst your busy divine duties must be truly troublesome for you. Since it is so, following your words, I shall be let through to the inner chambers.'

Wakahiko: 'Come, follow me and make your honorable entry. Hey Kiusuke, you stay here and keep firm watch at the entrance; you must not come a single step into the inner parts.'

Saying this as he left, the two figures concealed themselves in the inner room. Kiusuke, seeing them off, tilted his head slightly to the left, bit his tongue diagonally, and with a strange look in his eyes and an uncomprehending expression, gazed up at the ceiling.

Wakahiko quietly occupied a seat in the inner room together with the woman,

Wakahiko: 'Are you not Tamanohime-dono? While serving in important divine work, why have you come here by yourself without any guide? Once I have sworn to the Gods, the thought of meeting you in this mansion is out of the question.'

Tamanohime: 'Your words are most reasonable, but I have come with a deep circumstance regarding this. As you know well, having been ordered important divine work by Kototorisawake-sama, next I became the master of the mansion in the forest of Ikuta, but in connection with that, people serving under Takahime-sama are saying, "The three divine treasures must surely have been agreed upon between you and me and concealed in this mansion, so even if we have to take Wakahiko's life, we must make him confess the whereabouts of those divine treasures," and they are plotting a terrible conspiracy; therefore, I too, hearing this, could not keep my heart at ease, and thinking that it would not fulfill the responsibility of being your wife if I caused trouble to you who know nothing about it, I braved a long journey all alone to come and report it to you.'

Wakahiko: 'Is that so? Thank you very much for your kindness. However, I am one who leaves everything to the Gods; even if Takahime comes with whatever schemes, I am prepared to push through them by the power of the Gods. Please be at ease, and after resting, please return as early as possible. Should this matter leak to others, it would be a trouble to us both, as people might say, "Wakahiko, whom I thought to be an upright person, is still secretly meeting with his wife hidden from the public eye," so until the teacher grants permission, I absolutely cannot meet you. In exchange, I am guarding the Way as a single man no matter where, so please be at ease.'

Tamanohime: 'Such worries are unnecessary in your case alone. Since we are a pair whose hearts are mutually trusted, we will never, ever give rise to such a sordid heart. As you probably know, before long Takahime-sama or her subordinates will appear bringing food, so please do not eat it under any circumstances. This one thing I ask of you especially.'

Wakahiko: 'Yes, thank you very much; your kindness in paying attention to everything from beginning to end, I will never forget.'

Tamanohime, listening to Wakahiko's words with delight, made a smile, and tears of joy welled in her eyes.

To such a place, the voices of dispute coming toward the entrance gradually grew louder.

When the two listened closely, wondering what matter it could be, as Takahime's shrill voice,

Tamanohime must have come here.

Kiusuke's voice: 'No, no, absolutely, absolutely no woman-like person has come at all. According to the master's command, women are forbidden in this mansion for the time being.'

Tamanohime's voice: 'No matter what you say to conceal it, I heard it properly from the gatekeeper. A single woman must have crept in here, and both high and low joining hearts, dragging in a helpless woman, showing all of her body-primary spirit-subordinate existence, while the surface looks pious—this must be Wakahiko's plot. That fellow is called Aohiko, a man I raised myself. Hey, is there any passing or not passing? To speak of it, the master has come to the disciple's mansion. Do not interfere.'
