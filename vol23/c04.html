Chapter 4: Long High 説 [716]

長高説〔716〕

§ 1

Mokusuke secretly returned to the Seichi together with Magahiko and Takehiko as a pair, closed the front door (Omotedo), and without going out (Guwaishiyutsu) for a while, was observing the state (Yōsu) of the Seichi. The three Sendenshi, Tamaharuwake, Wakahiko, and Kuniyoriwake, also secretly returned to the Seichi, deeply slipped into the residence of Kuniyoriwake, and were scouting the conspiracy of Takahime's faction. Takahime, whose body was not that of a Kami, knew nothing of this even in her dreams, and thinking that now was the very time to do some house-cleaning while the ogre was away, she secretly circulated a notice to the residences of the Yakuin and Shinto of the Seichi, spread the word that an emergency (Kinkifu) incident had suddenly (Totsupatsu) occurred, and gathered them in the Yahirodono of the Nishiki Palace.

On this day, the wind was fierce, a sudden rain (Kifū) was as if overturning a tub, and even thunder (Kaminari) used its rolling tongue across the east, west, south, and north of the heavens, setting off a ruckus. Unyielding to such violent wind, rain, thunder, and lightning, upon hearing of the emergency incident, grandpas and grandmas, and everyone and anyone, those whose legs and loins could stand, gathered closely until there was not an inch of room left in the full hall. At this time, Takahime, solemn in her eboshi and kariginu, proceeded to the shrine, served as the chief ritualist with the air of saying to the kyōshu of Kotoyoriwake, 'You can even eat my backside,' completed the festival as usual, exposed a mandala mask like Atlas upon the high seat, faced the whole assembly, and on the dais, shaking her shoulders with the triumphant air of a demon's head slashed off with a spatula, and twitching her jaw up and down,

Takahime: 'Minasama, despite today being such a splendid day for weather, you have all gathered together without exception, and I am sure the living shrine of Hinode no Kami is also well satisfied. Some among you grand multitudes seem to have been worried, thinking that the kyōshu of Kotoyoriwake has been somewhat indisposed and secluded since the other day, and also that our sōmu-dono Mokusuke has gone off somewhere, so that this headquarters of the Izusan religion is like a headless human being, completely stuck and unable to move; but the divine virtue of Hinode no Kami is a wondrous thing. To say that, even though the kyōshu does not attend work, and Mokusuke and other executive Sendenshi are not present, everyone without exception has gathered together just like this by virtue of divine power, is surely proof that Heaven's Way has not yet abandoned this Takahime. Whether it be the summons of sōmu Mokusuke or the summons of kyōshu Kotoyoriwake, ever since the construction of this Yahirodono, there has never been a gathering so packed that not an inch of room was left. That is why the Kami are saying: Let us have a trial of strength between whether divine power is stronger or scholarly power is stronger, and see which prevails. Proof is better than argument, and seeing the reality with your own eyes and reforming yourselves is the absolute best way. Now, as for what we call an emergency incident, it is nothing other than this: setting aside ourselves, the living shrine of Hinode no Kami, and moreover the lineage of bodies of a hensei-danshi, along with our brilliant executive comrades, the high-collar kyōshu has secretly ordered important divine work to the likes of Tamanohime, a mere upstart of known origin from Oshio, and Mokusuke's daughter Ohatsu, and to pull a fast one on us and the distinguished elders of the executive ranks—no matter how much it is called divine work, is this not behavior that insults all of us? The executive officers go without saying, and all of you gathered here are surely none other than enthusiastic believers of the Izusan religion; while coming here having abandoned your positions and properties solely desiring to perform the service of the Kami's plan, could there possibly be such a thing as being trampled upon by Mokusuke's faction of people whose motives are unclear, and standing by blankly with your fingers in your mouths, doing nothing? Looking around like this, there are quite a few splendid men present, but do you gentlemen carry your testicles? Is this not truly beyond measure mortifying?'

Kamehiko sprang up abruptly from the midst of the full assembly,

Kamehiko: 'I have a question for Takahime-sama. Whatever may be the case, the divine governance of the divine realm is surely not a matter for us humans to meddle in. No matter how much you proclaim yourself to be the living shrine of Hinode no Kami, it is written nowhere in any fudehaki that the Kami demand obedience to Takahime-sama's orders. I would like you to drop the calling of yourself as Hinode no Kami. You are surely the vessel of a demon god who throws the Seichi and the divine governance of the divine realm into confusion and subversion!'

§ 2

Takahime stomped her feet in a fit of rage from the elevated seat, glaring with bulging eyes,

Takahime: 'If I should wonder who it might be, you are none other than Kamehiko, the gatekeeper of Akiyamahiko. As for what this Takahime, who brings about the great opening of the gates of the world, has to say, it is not a matter for the gatekeeper of a mere single house to interfere with. Get on back inside and sit down.'

She attempted to cut him off flatly with a single breath. Kamehiko, becoming defiant, appeared upon the dais where Takahime stood and surveyed the assembly,

Kamehiko: 'Everyone, as Her Ladyship Takahime has just stated, I have served as the gatekeeper for Akiyamahiko, but however that may be, did not Her Ladyship Takahime herself just confess that she too is a gatekeeper of the great gates? As one in the station of a gatekeeper, how could the affairs of the inner chambers possibly be understood by you? Regarding that as well, I am one who served as the gatekeeper at the mansion of Akiyamahiko—that is, the hidden retreat of Kamususanōnōokami and Kunitakehikonomikoto. As for the fact that at that time someone stole the keys to Kamurijima and Kutsujima without responding and swallowed the jewel, even if I do not mention it now, I suppose that all of you are already well aware of it. How could words of such Ikumiya-sama, in whom scheming and stratagems reach every limit, be believed seriously and honestly? I ask everyone to reflect upon it calmly and well.'

From amidst the assembly, voices such as 'Quite so, quite so,' 'Agreed, agreed,' 'Hear, hear,' and 'No, no' arose mingling together.

Takahime, like blazing fire,

Takahime: 'The person who just said "No, no," please step out before this Hinodewake. I shall consider you a man of our faction. Come, make your way here quickly.'

Kamehiko: 'There is likely not even a single person. The person who said "No, no" regarding my argument must have mistaken the meaning of agreement and uttered it. Everyone, it is a rude way of speaking, but since there are elderly people and children among us as well, I will explain briefly. If one says "Hear, hear," it means that they agree with my argument; if one says "No, no," it means that they do not agree. How is it? Let us have you declare it once more. And those who are in disagreement, please say "No, no."'

From the four corners of the place there were only voices of 'Hear, hear.' With particular emphasis, some added in a loud voice, 'No, no, but to Takahime's argument, it is no, no.' While foaming at the corners of her mouth, Takahime,

Takahime: 'Everyone, now that it has come to this, is it not too much to say you do not understand? Having your precious treasures hidden, how can you remain so unfazed? First of all, the ultra-modern leader of Kototama was fooled by a fellow like Mokusuke, ostracized Takahime of the lineage Ikumiya, and thus hid away that treasure somewhere. While you meet with such an experience as having been fed a poisoned octopus pot, is it not the height of thoughtlessness to remain unconcerned? No matter how great the leader of Kototama may be, or how strong Mokusuke's power may be, can the affairs of the divine realm be understood through learning and wisdom? Can you give clear answers as to what kind of fundamental connections from the past your great ancestors were engaged in, by what kind of karmic connection you were born into this world, what the true activities of Hinodewake are like, and what the true identity of the Princess of the Dragon Palace is? From now on, beginning with the Brocade Palace, this Eight-Fathom Hall shall be under the general supervision of this Takahime, inadequate though she is. I cannot fathom the feelings of those who offer joyful reverence and adoration to a leader who chooses women like Tamanohime and Hatsuwakahime to make them perform vital duties. Clean out the holes in your ears just a little bit, everyone, listen to the oracle of Hinodewake, open your living eyes, and examine well the actual deeds. Setting aside the living shrine of Hinodewake, who has grasped the fundamental essence, what can a physical body possessed by a branch-spirit understand? This place is... it is no one else's affair; it is a grave problem concerning the personal status of every single one of you, nay, a grave problem of the nation.'

Kamehiko: 'Those who have any objections regarding Her Ladyship Takahime's words just now, please rise.'

Moreover, I was deeply impressed that they held not the slightest grudge against the two men. Saying that this is entirely because they have embodied the great spirit of Mizu no Mitama, even Mokusuke was choked with tears of gratitude, and from there the three hurried along the road to go and see the mansion of Wakahiko, where they found that both Magahiko and Takehiko were fabricating some wicked falsehoods, instigating Wakahiko, and attempting to plot a great conspiracy. However, even these villains have been touched by a true heart, are reforming in this manner, and are showing their sincerity by exposing their own crimes one by one before everyone. Can you still say that the teachings of Kotoyoriwake-sama are evil? Are the teachings of Takahime-sama truly complete?'

Having been thus pinned down, Takahime was utterly at a loss for words, and with an intensity as if kicking away the dais, tilting her shoulders and shaking her head from side to side, she walked back quickly to her own mansion.

At such a place, the kyōshiyu of Kotoyoriwake appeared with a gentle smile and delivered a speech. Tamaharuwake, Kuniyoriwake, and Wakahiko appeared calmly at this place, bowed to everyone, faced the shinden to recite the Amatsu Norito, and after finishing, everyone dispersed. What kind of actions will Takahime take henceforth?
