Chapter 2: 副守囁 [714]

副守囁〔714〕

§ 1

Having washed away both sins and impurities in the waterfall of Nachi, Wakahiko, feeling refreshed in heart and mind, built a mansion at the foot of Mount Fudarakusan to spread the teachings of Ananai-kyō far and wide, and was diligently propagating the teachings in all directions.

Knocking upon the gate,

'Please open, please open!'

Thus called two missionaries visiting the place.

The gatekeepers, Akikō and Shimekō, were awakened from their sleep by this voice, and stretching widely with a great yawn,

Shimekō: 'Hey Akikō, there is someone outside the gate paying a visit. Won't you wake up quickly and open it for them?'

Akikō: 'Even though the night has scarcely broken, is there any need to open this gate? Since the hour is still a little early, it would be best to take just one more nap.'

The sound of knocking upon the gate grew ever more urgent.

Shimekō, keeping his bedding draped over himself,

'Hey, hey, opening it is Akikō's task. Won't you wake up quickly?'

Akikō: 'If you speak with such annoying insistence, kisama, open it yourself.'

'I am tasked with shutting it, just as my name implies. If we dawdle and drag our feet, we'll receive another slap on the eyeball from the great general Wakahiko. Eh, what a hopeless fellow you are.'

So saying, still in his nightwear, with a sullen look upon his face, wearing a geta on one foot and a zōri on the other, and dragging his three-shaku sash behind him, he rattled the gate wide open.

The two men bowed politely,

'Is the missionary of Wakahiko at home?'

Shimekō: 'Do you think I can understand such high-flown, difficult talk? Are you asking whether he is in or whether he is not? And you there, what sort of missionary are you supposed to be anyway?'

'Why, I am Magahiko, and the other one is called Takehiko; we are missionaries of Ananai-kyō. We have come all this way by the command of the Lord God, so please guide us inside.'

'Whether things are bent or straight, guiding us inside or staying outside the gate—not a single word of what you are saying makes any sense to me at all. Don't use such outlandish jargon, and speak so that we can understand.'

Magahiko: 'Ha ha ha ha ha, what an incomprehensible gatekeeper to have encountered! Since a fellow like this is serving as a gatekeeper, one can pretty much tell what Wakahiko's own proficiency is like, too.'

Shimekō: 'Wait a moment. Just now you called the master of this house simply Wakahiko, didn't you? Why didn't you say Wakahiko-sama? A fellow who says such outrageous things will not be allowed to pass through this gate. You must be the foster child of the Centipede Princess of Mount Maganyagatake. Isn't it true that you've come here disguised as missionaries of Ananai-kyō? ...Hey Akikō, wake up and get over here. Some dreadful scoundrels have come calling.'

Startled by this voice, Akikō appeared on the scene clad still in his nightwear,

'Could this dreadful scoundrel be the fellow you mean? What on earth is the matter?'

'It's because this person here had the audacity to call the master of this place plain Wakahiko, which is why my stomach is churning with disgust, Akikō.'

'That certainly is enough to turn one's stomach. Hey, I don't know where you fellows hail from, but be off with you today.'

Magahiko: 'Aren't you, to put it mildly, merely Wakahiko's gatekeeper? Do you possess any right whatsoever to let us through or turn us away, when we have come by the command of the Lord God? Guide us inside at once.'

Thus he shouted in a tone of voice tinged with some anger.

As the two men scratched their heads,

Akikō: 'Well now, from here on we gatekeepers must use water to wash our hands and mouths, change our clothes, and eat our morning meal before we leisurely guide you inside, so until then you'd better just wait right there.'

Takehiko: 'Magahiko-sama, even if they call it large, Wakahiko's estate is only so big after all. Come, let us go.'

So saying, he took the lead and entered the inner grounds.

Wakahiko was wearing a cool-looking thin robe, completely absorbed in cleaning the edge of the garden, neatly drawing the patterns of his broom across the sand.

Magahiko: 'Ah, that must be Wakahiko's missionary of sorts. From early morning onwards, the great general is performing the duties of a gardener just like that, while the gatekeeper fellows were snoring away and snoozing. Even at the head temple of the Uranai teachings in Kitayama Village, it was just like this. Gatekeepers do nothing but put on airs and refuse to work. Say, Takehiko, when you were serving as a gatekeeper for the Uranai teachings in the land of Persia, it was just the same back then, wasn't it?'

Takehiko: 'You really shouldn't bring up things like that at this late hour. Besides, the Uranai teachings vanished ages ago, and nowadays we are proud missionaries of Ananai-kyō. If you start dragging up my former life as a gatekeeper in a place like this, it lowers our dignity and social standing. If you're going to keep on talking about such bygone matters, shall I open up the discussion right here and now about that sudden incident in the ravine of Aoyama Pass?'

Magahiko: 'Sheets.'

Takehiko: 'What do you mean by sheets? Treating people like four-legged beasts and saying shee-shee, a dead fellow will come along saying how resentful I am, oh dear. This is the way of the gods that celebrates good fortune. You had better be careful not to utter four and nine.'

So whispering while standing and watching Wakahiko's cleaning, the two of them are at it. Entering their ears, Wakahiko holds a broom in his hand and gazes at the figures of both men,

Wakahiko: 'Ah, you are Magahiko-sama and Takehiko-sama, you have come to visit us so early in the morning. Please do go on through to the inner rooms. I shall gladly listen to your talk about things since our parting.'

Magahiko, with a solemn demeanor,

Magahiko: 'I have come all this way as the messenger of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime-sama.'

Takehiko: 'So to speak, you are the messenger of the gods, and you had better listen with reverence.'

So he poses with arrogance. Wakahiko bends his waist,

Wakahiko: 'Be that as it may, please pass through to the inner chambers.'

And he leads the way. The two are invited to the detached guest room, receive the hospitality of tea and hot water, and for a while relax to indulge in all sorts of chatter. Wakahiko goes out to the front and, together with his subordinate officials and believers, performs morning worship at the shrine, finishes a sermon, and eats breakfast. The maid prepares the meal trays, carries them before the two in the detached guest room, and urges them to eat breakfast. Wakahiko finishes his morning meal, adjusts his attire, and appears before the two in the detached guest room,

Wakahiko: 'Well, well, my two lords, I have kept you waiting a long time. Having come all this way, I am truly sorry to have no particular feast to offer.'

Magahiko: 'The teachings of Izunome-kyō have the rule of one soup and one vegetable. Despite that, oh my, I have partaken of a luxurious feast. In the sacred land, even Tamateruhiko-sama has certainly never seen such a feast. However, thinking it would not do to let your kind intention go to waste, I have received it gladly. Haha-ha-ha-ha!'

Wakahiko: 'We too are propagandists of Izunome-kyō, and we faithfully keep the rule of one soup and one vegetable. However, since the gods have arrived today, we offered the feast to the gods. It was not served up to you, Magahiko-sama, or to Takehiko-sama. You said that you ate what was offered to the gods because it would be a pity, but did you eat even the portion belonging to the gods?'

Having been dealt a counterblow, the two are struck dumb with astonishment and roll their round eyes.

Magahiko: 'Our coming today is by the command of the Great Deity, and we have been dispatched by the two divine administrators, Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, to convey the divine will. Do you know of the grave situation in the sacred land?'

Wakahiko: 'Isn't the sacred land safe and peaceful, with the divine work flourishing?'

Magahiko: 'Well now, it seems you do not understand because you have been away from the sacred land for a long time. That fellow Mokusuke whom you know has completely forced his way into the sacred land, takes the words of the maiden Wakahime as a shield, exerts extreme tyranny, causes everyone's hearts to turn away, and a great upheaval is about to occur right now. Because of that, Takahime-sama as well is extremely worried.'

Wakahiko: 'If that is so, let me hear first of all whether you have come following the wishes of Takahime-sama, or if you have come following the wishes of the pontiff, Kotoshirowake-no-Kyōshu.'

Takehiko: 'Isn't such a thing all the same?'

Just as he is about to say this, Magahiko stops him in a panic,

Magahiko: 'Hey, hey, Takehiko-sama, will you not keep your promise? This is not the place for you to speak; aren't you a mere follower, so to speak?'

Takehiko: 'Whether I am a follower or whatever I may be, on the surface I am still an illustrious propagandist of the same rank as Magahiko. Pray do not speak with such airs. How is the summit of Aoyama Pass?'

So saying, he peers into his face.

Magahiko: 'If the sun hides in Aoyama, the night of black arrows will come forth. The smiles of the morning sun come flourishing, holding with bare hands and embracing the white arms like fine snow and the young heaving breasts, intertwining them like true gems and precious jade beads, making the long thighs full and abundant, and offering the rich sacred sake. Haha-ha-ha-ha!'

Magahiko: 'More than that, there is still, even still, a matter of paramount importance. The original culprit who hid the Nyoi Hozuru, the purple jewel, and the golden jewel is Kotoyoriwake-mikoto. Magahiko: '.........'

Takehiko: 'This Takehiko knows not. However, the Fukushiyugojin knows well.'

Magahiko suddenly crosses his hands, lets out a loud grunt toward Takehiko, and roars,

Magahiko: 'You subordinate guardian spirit, get out of the way!-'

Takehiko: 'Oo-oo-oo! If you grow careless, you will be thrust down to the bottom of the valley. Once dropped to the bottom of the valley, Kami shall save you and make you perform the true Goyō. Since this world is Kami's domain of freedom, human clever plans shall not be achieved. From the frog's mouth, I shall make it confess by its own hands and strip off the skin of its face!'

Magahiko: 'Go down, go down, get on down. You must be a Notengu.'

Takehiko: 'Whether a Notengu or whatever, it matters not. At the bottom of the valley, even a Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō who cannot be pushed or shoved will likewise be pushed down, fall to the bottom of the valley, and utter an "Afun!". Presently the top shall become the bottom and the bottom shall become the top. Kami shall appear in the open and clearly separate good and evil!'

Magahiko: 'Agh, what a noisy Notengu. Will you not go down when I tell you to go down?'

Takehiko: 'Oo-oo-oo! Wakahiko-dono, you had better take heed. You must not yield to the temptation of evil. No matter how sweet things they come to say, unless you have consulted Kami, you must not listen to them! Maga-maga-maga!'

Magahiko: 'Moshiboshi, Wakahiko-sama, a troublesome Jiyashin has possessed him, hasn't he?'

Wakahiko: 'Nay, it can hardly be a Jiyashin. For the most part, what this guardian spirit says seems close to the truth. The Sendenshi of Kuniyoriwake and Tamajiwake, might they not have been thrust down to the bottom of the valley by Magatakeruhiko?'

Takehiko: 'Oo-oo-oo! As expected of the Sendenshi of Wakahiko. Your clairvoyance is splendid, splendid!'

Magahiko turns pale in the face, gradually begins to back away, and tries to leave this place.

Wakahiko: 'Now, Magahiko-sama, please take your time. Under heaven there are neither enemies nor allies, you see. Since Kami will judge good and evil, no matter what happens, we shall be well off if we leave everything to Kannagara. Come, please partake of some tea.'

So saying, he pours tea and holds it out. Magahiko's whole body begins to tremble violently.

At this moment, a maidservant named Okō runs up in a flurry,

Okō: 'Just now three guests have arrived. What shall we do?'

Wakahiko: 'Show them into the inner room in the front.'

Magahiko: 'Moshiboshi, what manner of persons are those three?'

Okō: 'They seem to be Sendenshi-sama, somehow. There is one very large person mixed among them.'

The color of Magahiko's face changes in a flash. Takehiko shakes his body with a shudder, becomes spirit-possessed once again, and roars,

Takehiko: 'Here they come, here they come! To the valley, to the valley! To the jewel, to the jewel! Kuni-kuni-kuni moku-moku-moku!'

Wakahiko speaks,

Wakahiko: 'I say, Okō, go and invite four or five men here.'

Answering 'Yes,' Okō points toward the front and conceals her figure, and after a while returns accompanied by five big men: Ko, Otsu, Hei, Tei, and Bo.

Wakahiko: 'Ho, five men, I have a little business with the guests in the front, so I am leaving these two guests in your care to protect them dearly so that they do not get away. Keep watch at the front and back entrances so that no demons may break in and guard them well. It would be somewhat inconvenient if they were to escape, you see.'

Ko: 'Yes, everything is well within my breast. Please have no worries.'

Wakahiko: 'I rely on you in every way. Now, Magahiko-sama, Takehiko-sama, I will go and meet the guests in the front for a moment. Please take your time and enjoy some tea.'

Giving a glance to the five men, he calmly leaves the spot and conceals his figure toward the front house.
