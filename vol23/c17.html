Chapter 17: 黄 Dragon 姫 [729]

黄竜姫〔729〕

§ 1

Following behind Takahime, Tomohiko came to Mukadehime's room, and suddenly pressing his forehead to the floor,

Tomohiko: 'Oh, oh, Mukadehime-sama, after a long time I behold your healthy and honorable countenance, and I consider it a matter of extreme joy and gratitude for Tomohiko's body. I humbly and earnestly entreat you to view and listen to everything correctly with Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi, and to forgive my numerous heavy sins and transgressions up until now.'

Mukadehime: 'Ohohoho, well Tomohiko, thanks to your having spent a long time in the world, your eloquence has truly become quite clever, hasn't it. You are an audacious man who coaxed my only daughter Koitohime in Kenonkiyō and came running out, but where on earth have you hidden Koitohime? Since he is the beloved man of my cute daughter, as a parent I would naturally want to have them live together as much as possible, but even though he is the adopted son of Onikumawake, the sub-leader of the Brahmin teaching, he is far too shabby, so my husband would not consent no matter what, and as a mother I only wanted to make my daughter's love come true and spoke about various things, but she would not listen at all, and meanwhile you are a bold man who seduced my precious, precious daughter, of whom there is none other in heaven or on earth, and made off with her whereabouts. What on earth did you do with that daughter? Confess truthfully. I too have a thought. Depending on how you come out, it is not as though I will not let you live together. If you do that, you too will be the splendid successor of Onikumawake.'

Tomohiko: 'Yes, I shall engrave your words upon my heart and give thanks. However, Koitohime-sama laid out various rejections, saying that the tip of my nose is red, my mouth is ill-formed, or that I have protruding teeth, and though we lived together for about a year, on a certain night she left a suicide note and departed from my side. Therefore I think I want to search for her and meet her once more, and there is not even a fleeting moment when I do not think of Koitohime-sama. I have indeed done a regrettable thing.'

Mukadehime: 'If that is so, then you were abandoned by my daughter, weren't you. Ah, as expected she is my daughter. Your face is one that makes one want to vomit even at a single glance. I couldn't help wondering how on earth that beautiful girl fell in love with you. You must have taken advantage of the child's young heart, lined up various threatening words, and brought her along by force.'

Tomohiko: 'No, no, by no means; from the beginning, wherever she went she clung to me like a shadow, to the extent that she was so annoying I could not bear it. I by no means fell in love with her from my side. In accordance with Koitohime-sama's earnest desire, I too unavoidably complied. When I tried refusing her once, Koitohime-sama said, "You are my first love. And if I am given such a cold shoulder so mercilessly, there is no longer any worth in living in this world with such shame. If you make my wish come true, fine; if not, I will commit suicide right here and now. My path of love is not an ordinary, general love. To the end it is the nine-sun-five-bu style," and as she produced something shining and was about to thrust it into her own throat, I leaped forward and cried, "Hey, do not be rash!" and wrested away the nine-sun-five-bu dagger, and before we knew it we kissed each other. That became the tie and at last we became inseparable companions.'

Mukadehime: 'It is out of the question that Koitohime alone would lose her head and dote on a man like you. Thinking there is no one to talk back, you must be telling such lies. Come now, tell me the true thing.'

Tomohiko: 'The literal truth is an embarrassing story, but as I said earlier, she gave me the cold shoulder, left behind a single suicide note as a memento, and fled like clouds and mist. I am resolved that she must have returned to your side no matter what, but please, without hiding it, just for once, let me meet her.'

原文（近代文語）

高姫の後に従いて蜈蚣姫の居間にやつて来た友彦は、忽ち頭を床にすりつけ乍ら、

友彦『コレはコレは蜈蚣姫様、久し振りで御壮健なる御尊顔を拝し、友彦身に取つて恐悦至極に存じ奉りまする。唯何事も神直日、大直日に見直し、聞き直し下さいまして、今迄の重々の罪科を御赦し下さいます様、懇願仕りまする』

蜈蚣姫『オホヽヽヽ、コレ友彦、お前も長らく世間をして来た徳に依つて、随分弁舌は巧になつたものだなア。お前は顕恩郷に於て、我が一人の娘小糸姫をチヨロマカし、飛び出て了つた不届きな男だが、一体小糸姫は何処へ隠してあるのだ。可愛い娘の恋男だから、成る可く親として添はしてやりたいは山々なれど、苟くもバラモン教の副棟梁鬼熊別の養子としては、余り御粗末だから我が夫は何うしても御承諾遊ばさず、妾は母親の事とて娘の恋を叶へさしてやりたいばつかりに、いろいろと申し上げたが中々御聞き遊ばさず、其間にお前は、天にも地にも一人より無い我が大事な大事な娘を誘拐して、行方を晦ました太い男だ。其娘を一体何うして呉れたのだ。サ有体に白状しなさい。妾にも一つ考へがある。お前の出様に依つては又添はしてやらぬものでもない。さうすればお前も立派な鬼熊別の御世継だ』

友彦『ハイ、貴女の御言葉は肝に銘じて感謝致しまする。併し乍ら小糸姫様は私の鼻の先が赤いとか、口が不恰好だとか、出歯だとかいろいろと愛想づかしを並べられ、一年ばかり添うて居りましたが、或夜のこと遺書を残して私の側を離れて了ひました。それ故私はモー一度探して会ひたいと思ひ、片時の間も小糸姫様の事を思はぬ暇はありませぬ。誠に済まぬ事を致しました』

蜈蚣姫『さうするとお前は娘に愛想を尽かされたのだな。アーア矢張り私の娘だ。一目見ても反吐の出るやうなお前の面つき。何うして彼の綺麗な娘がお前に恋慕したのだらうと、不思議でならなかつた。お前は娘の幼心につけ込み、いろいろと威嚇文句を並べて、無理に伴れ出したのだらう』

友彦『イエイエ滅相も無い、初の間は何処へ往くにも、影の如くに付き纏ひ、煩さくて怺らなかつた位です。決して私の方から恋慕したのぢや御座いませぬ。小糸姫さまの切なる御願ひに依つて、私も已むを得ず応じました。一度刎ねて見ましたところ、小糸姫様は「妾はお前が初恋ぢや。それに斯う無下に肱鉄砲を喰はされては、最早此世に恥かしくて生て居る甲斐が無い。妾の思ひを叶へさして呉れればよし、さうでなければ今此処で自害をする。妾の恋路は普通一般の恋では無い。何処迄も九寸五分式だ」と光つたものを出して、吾れと吾咽喉を突き立て給はむとする時、私は跳びついて「ヤア、逸まり給ふな」と九寸五分を奪ひ取り、思はず互にキツスを致しました。それが縁となつて到頭わり無き仲となつたので御座います』

蜈蚣姫『小糸姫に限つてお前のやうな男に、さう血道を分けて呆ける筈が無い。片相手が居らぬと思つて、そんな嘘を云ふのだらう。サアサ、本当の事を言つて下され』

友彦『実の事は恥しい話ですが、最前も申した通り、私に肱鉄を喰はし、たつた一枚の遺書をかたみに雲を霞と遁げて了はれたので御座います。私は何うしても貴女の御側へ帰つて居られるものと思ひ詰て居りますが、何うぞ隠さずに一度で宜しい、会はして下さいませ』

§ 2

Mukadehime: 'Moshi Takahime-sama, what a troubled fellow he is indeed. How quite audacious of him to be able to say such a thing, oh my.'

Takahime: 'Korekore Tomohiko-sama, your Tachi is quite bad. Now then, do you have that Kakioki or something right now?'

Tomohiko: 'He-y, that single Ichimai of Kakioki is the lifeline of my Inochi. Using this as a Shiyōko, I intend to Megu and meet you once to warm our Kiukau, and Manichi if it fails, to speak all the grievances I have and settle our Munen, and I am holding onto it carefully as an Omamo-sama.'

Mukadehime: 'What, do you hold what the daughter wrote? Show it to me here for a moment...'s

Tomohiko: 'It is not that I will not show it, but can you Anata read it? Something like a Futefu code is written, and tent I cannot read it. Even when I showed it to Nisannin of Tomodachi, they said they had never seen such Moji, and Tarehitori is the one who reads it; I have no idea what is what. Is it written that I will throw myself into Fuchikaha and die, or else is it written to go Doko and wait so come, I simply cannot Uketo it.'

Mukadehime: 'At any rate, lend it to me for a Chiyotsuto.'

While saying so, she Uketo it from the hand of Tomohiko, and while smoothing out the wrinkles of the crumpled paper with her Riyōte,

Mukadehime: 'Ha-ha-ah, since this is written in Spartan script, it will not be understood unless one is quite a scholar. Ee...'s

One, driven by the passion of a moment, without even the leisure to reflect upon good and evil, beauty and ugliness, I went against the imperial will of my parents, fell into the temptation of your thousand words and ten thousand phrases, and though we have shared a pillow for almost a year as of today, compared to when we first met, your crude and base raw nature has appeared; for me, it has become hard to bear as a woman, and though I am sorry, I give this single piece of paper to you as a memento. From now on, do not ever consult with your face, and do not raise too much rebellious spirit. You must not grieve after being put to shame like me and being given the cold shoulder. Crossing over to an island in Australia where many blackamoors live, I will become the queen of the blackamoors and live in glory and splendor. No matter how black your skin is, compared to your shirt-front, I do not know how much more charming it is; therefore, resigning yourself to our past ties, even when seeing this writing, you must not show the foolish intent of chasing after me and coming out. Manmanichi if you should do something like chasing after me, I will order my numerous henchmen and beat you to death, so you had better be prepared for that. If you think your Inochi is important above all else, you had better resign yourself, thinking that you were just having a good dream for today only. Thinking that I was born as the daughter of Imoriwake, the sub-leader of Brahmanism, and was defiled by a foolish man like you, how regrettable and shameful it is, how on earth could I show my face to my parents or to the people of the world? Ah, writing this letter has even made my stomach sick. Since it is too wasteful to write with water, I will grind India ink with the juice of your urine, and leave this single brushstroke written here.

From the brightest and wisest lady in the world, Koitihime-sama

To the jilted man, Tomohiko-dono

Thus it was inscribed. Mukadehime seeing this couldn't help but open her mouth wide,

'Oho-ho-ho-ho, kore Tomohiko, you too are quite a good blockhead, oh. Shall I read it out to you again now? No, no, it would be a pity to put you to shame either. You had better not read it. Since it will also become a shame for the daughter... Moshi Takahime-sama, he is a thoroughly absent-minded fellow, wah. And so, the whereabouts of the beloved daughter has become known. Come now, while searching for treasures, let us pursue the daughter's footsteps. Takahime-sama, will you not guard this mansion?'

Takahime: 'Do not speak of such absurd things. Until we find the three treasures and dedicate them to Daijizaiten-sama, my body cannot stay in such a place at ease. No matter where, I must go with you to become a sister and accomplish our purpose.'

原文（近代文語）

蜈蚣姫『モシ高姫さま、なんと困つた男ぢやありませぬか。よう図々しい、あんな事が云へたものですなア』

高姫『コレコレ友彦さま、お前は余程性質が悪いぞえ。さうして其の遺書とかは今持つて居りますか』

友彦『ヘーイ、其の一枚の遺書が私の生命の綱だ。之を証拠に一度邂逅り会つて旧交を温め、万一叶はずば恨の有り丈を言つて、無念晴しをするつもりで、大切に御守りさまとして持つて居ります』

蜈蚣姫『なに、娘の書いたものをお前は持つて居るか。それを一つ此処へ見せて下さい……』

友彦『見せないことはありませぬが、貴女読めますかな。何だか符牒のやうな文字が書いてあつて、テント私には読めませぬ。二三人の友達にも見せましたけれど、こんな文字は見たことが無いと云つて、誰一人読むものは無し、何が何だか解りませぬ。淵川へ身を投げて死ぬと書いてあるのか、或は又何処かへ行つて待つて居るから来いと云ふのか、薩張り私には受取れませぬ』

蜈蚣姫『兎も角妾に一寸お貸し』

と云ひ乍ら、友彦の手より之を受取り、ももくちやだらけの紙を両手で皺を伸ばし乍ら、

蜈蚣姫『ハヽア、これはスパルタ文字で記してあるから、余程の学者で無いと解らぬワイ。エー……』

一、妾事一時の情熱に駆られ、善悪美醜を省みる遑も無く、両親の御意に背き、お前様の千言万語を尽しての誘惑に陥り、今日で殆ど一年ばかり枕を共に致しましたが初めに会うた時とは打つて変つて野卑と下劣の生地現はれ、妾としては女として鼻持ち相成り難く、気の毒乍ら此の紙一枚を記念として、お前に与へる。今後は決してお前の面と相談し、余り謀叛気を出してはなりませぬぞ。又妾のやうに恥を掻かされ、肱鉄砲を喰はされて悲しまねばなりませぬ。妾は是より黒ん坊の沢山居る、オースタラリヤの一つ島へ渡り、黒ん坊の女王となつて栄耀栄華に暮しまする。何程色が黒くてもお前様のシヤツ面に比ぶれば幾ら優しか知れませぬから、今迄の縁と諦めて、此の書を見ても決して跡を追うて出て来るなどの不了見を出してはなりませぬぞ。万々一後を追うて来るやうな事を致したならば、数多の乾児に命じ、お前を嬲殺しに致すから、其の覚悟をしたがよい。必ず生命が大事と思はば、今日限り一場の良い夢を見て居つたと思つて、諦めたがよからう。苟くもバラモン教の副棟梁鬼熊別の娘と生れ、お前のやうな馬鹿男に汚されたかと思へば、残念で、恥しくて、父母にも世界の人にも、何うして顔が会はされやう、アヽ此の文を書くのも胸が悪くなつて来た。水で書くのは余り勿体ないから、お前の小便の汁で墨をすつて、茲に一筆書き遺し置きます。

天下の賢明なる淑女　小糸姫様より

振られ男の友彦殿

と記しありぬ。蜈蚣姫は之を見て思はず大口を開け、

『オホヽヽヽ、コレ友彦、お前も余程よい抜作だなア。今改めて読んで聞かしてやらうか。イヤイヤ恥を掻かすも気の毒ぢや。読まぬがよからう。娘の恥にもなることだから……。モシ高姫様、随分間抜けた男ですワ。さうして恋しい娘の行方は分りました。サアこれからお宝を探しがてら、娘の後を追うて参りませう。高姫さま、貴女は此の館を守つて下さいませぬか』

高姫『滅相もないこと仰有いますな。三つの宝を探し出し、大自在天様に献上する迄は、妾の身体は安閑と斯様な処に居ることは出来ませぬ。何処までも貴女とシスターとなつて目的を成就する迄は参らねばなりませぬ』

§ 3

Mukadehime: 'Ah, that is so indeed. Then let us cooperate and take action together. Ah, there is no joy greater than finally knowing the whereabouts of my daughter. It is said that the Ōriyōhime of Riugūjima, of whom we have heard rumors, is the highly renowned queen of Australia. So that queen was my daughter all along! How joyful, how joyful; thanks to Takahime-sama, we have finally found where my daughter is. This is all due to the divine guidance of Ōkamisama; I thank You from the bottom of my heart.'

Clasping her hands, she worshiped with tears.

Tomohiko: 'Now, wait a moment, I was not brought along by Takahime-sama. When I came of my own accord as far as the entrance of this cave, Takahime-sama happened to be here. Today is even the very first time I have met Takahime-sama. If you are going to offer your gratitude to Takahime-sama, why do you not offer your gratitude to me?'

Mukadehime: 'Leave off, you brazen-faced wretch! The only reason I worried so much, and the reason my darling daughter went off to an unfamiliar foreign land and suffered hardships, is fundamentally because you are to blame. Where do you press to make a sound like that? You truly are an incomprehensible fellow. It is no wonder that the formidable Koitohime, who is destined to become the queen of a nation, gave you the cold shoulder. As if any woman would cast sheep's eyes at a beggar-woman's kind of man like you! Keep your conceit within reason. What kind of attitude is that, displaying a thief's face? Now that the whereabouts of my daughter are known, I have no use for you. Come, be off with you quickly.'

Tomohiko: 'The gods take compassion as their heart. Have you forgotten the teaching of looking anew and listening anew through Kaminohaochi and Ōnaochi?'

Mukadehime: 'Ah, it cannot be helped, that is so as well. Since the gods will forgive any sin as long as one is capable of repentance, I too shall forgive you in accordance with Kannagara.'

Tomohiko: 'Thank you for your prompt pardon. Please, let me too accompany you as your attendant and take me along to the island of Australia.'

Mukadehime: 'That is absolutely out of the question. Your parents would be troubled if your illness were to relapse, so I will allow you to accompany us halfway, but I absolutely cannot take you to that island.'

Tomohiko: 'Now look here, Takahime-sama, even the rustling of sleeves is a bond of past lives. Madam, please put in a good word for me there... Hey Kanshū, won't you ask the Great One on my behalf and get me pardoned?'

Takahime: 'I am not a body made of wood and stone, so I know well both the sour and the sweet. But now is the rising tide of water. Just wait a while. When the time is right, I will put in a good word for you somehow.'

Kanshū: 'Just as Takahime-sama says, you must be patient for a while.'

Mukadehime: 'No matter what anyone says, once I have spoken, I will never back down.'

Kanshū: 'Hey Tomohiko, it is no use when a low-pressure system attacks this severely. If you grumble and fuss, there is no telling what wind, rain, thunder, lightning, or cataclysmic upheaval might break out. So stop borrowing trouble and follow along with a carpe diem mindset.'

Tomohiko: 'Ah, my old wounds are throbbing now and it is so painful!'

蜈蚣姫『アヽそれもさうだ。そんなら相提携して活動を致しませう。アヽ娘の所在が分つてこんな愉快なことは無い。噂に聞いて居る竜宮島の黄竜姫といふのが、オースタラリヤの声望高き女王と云ふことだ。そんなら彼の女王は我が娘であつたか。嬉しや嬉しや、高姫さまの御かげで、到頭娘の所在が分りました。これと云ふのも大神様の御引合せ、有り難う御座います』

と手を合し涙と共に拝み居る。

友彦『モシモシ、高姫さまに伴れられて来たのではありませぬ。私は勝手に此の岩窟の入口迄参りますと、高姫さまが来て居られたのです。高姫様に逢うたのも今日が初めてだ。高姫さまに御礼を仰有るなら、何故私に御礼を仰有らぬか』

蜈蚣姫『措きなされ、面の皮の厚い、妾が是れ丈け心配をしたのも、可愛い娘が知らぬ他国へ行つて苦労をして居るのも、元を糺せばみんなお前が悪い故だ。何処を押へたら、そんな音が出るのだ。本当に訳の分らぬ奴だ、一国の女王にもなると云ふ、流石の小糸姫が肱鉄を喰はしたのも無理もない。乞食婆だとてお前のやうな男に、秋波を送る奴があるものか。自惚も好い加減にしなされ。泥棒面をさらして何の態だ。娘の行方が分つた以上はお前に用は無い。サア、トツトと帰つて貰ひませう』
