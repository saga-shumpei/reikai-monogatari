Chapter 16: 蜈蚣の涙 [728]

蜈蚣の涙〔728〕

§ 1

To spread the teachings of Brahmanism / Upon Onokorojima, / With a heart of burning haste / At Onikumawake's castle, / Becoming the wife of Onikumawake, / Serving morning and evening, / The Hime of Mukade, with a single heart, / Deeply vowing in her mind / To achieve the restoration of Brahmanism, / Shutting herself up in Mount Mikonokadake, / Distressing her heart a thousandfold, / Snatching away the sacred treasure of Ananai-kyō, / The orb of kogane, / Stowing it deep within the kyōtei, / Wriggling her proud nose, / When she was about to set her hand / To a second plan, / Discovered by Otama-no-kata, / The propaganda envoy of the heavenly Maura, / And even the mountain stronghold of Mount Mikonokadake, / Which she had boasted was impregnable,

Smashed to minute pieces, / With regretful tears and nowhere to send them, / While burning with the flames of shini, / With a heart firm even in her old age, / To carry out her scheme in Mount Makanyadake, / Starting with smart balls, / Calling together amata kyōto, / She barricaded herself in Mount Takatoriyama. / To overturn the divine strategy / Of Takahirohime, / The propaganda envoy of Ananai-kyō, / Crushing her heart and body morning and evening, / The fruits of her efforts scattered in the wild wind, / The desires of a fleeting dream vanished fragilely, / Gnashing her teeth and persistently / Darting out from here once more, / While her clouded heart also viewed Akashigata, / Gazing leftward at Kamishima, / Where she prayed morning and evening, and Ieshima,

Throwing away both body and soul in a small boat, / Crossing the waves of the Seto of this world, / Drifting ashore at the water's edge of Ōshima, Shōshima, and Shōodajima, / Hoping to search out / The whereabouts of the three treasures, / Making the mountain of Kunishiro her aim, / The number of heads in her party was forty-eight. / Taking up camp in the cavern of the ugliness, / Distributing her subordinates in all directions, / Searching and seeking everywhere / Along the ridges of the mountain and the shallows of the river, / Repairing this cavern for a short while / As a temporary dwelling, / Just as she awaited the time, / The divine plan of Takahime also slid over the white waves / And landed, / Coming forth here once more. / To Mukadehime in this mysterious bond, / Growing horns in her heart,

Where had the stubbornness and obstinacy of her usual self gone, / With her shoulders and elbows flared in anger? / Melting away and rejoicing, / Finding that Takahime was a companion on the path of Brahma, / Upon hearing this, her expression changed completely, / And in a totally transformed reception, / Having played her part, Takahime / Turned around and stuck out her tongue, / And with an innocent face, / Proceeded to enter the inner room—how laughable it was.

Takahime: 'It has been a long, long time. When you were thriving on Mount Makanyadake, I too built a hermitage on Mount Takatoriyama, and thinking that I must search out the wish-granting jewel, which is most necessary for Brahmanism, driving the foolishly honest believers of Ananai-kyō, and wishing to obtain it as early as possible and dedicate it to the Great-Freedom Lord, my heart was troubled day and night, and I was thinking that somehow I wanted to have the opportunity to meet you; but no matter what was said, separated by the barrier of human eyes, it did not go as I wished, and I have lived until this very day enduring the desire to meet and see you. Shall we call it the circulation of heavenly fortune? Today, to be able to obtain a glance in such a safe zone at you whom I adore daily—this must be because the Great-Freedom Lord recognized the sincerity of Takahime and gave us this joy of such a happy reunion. I am so overjoyed that I do not know what to talk about first.'

Mukadehime: 'Whether times and seasons change, becoming Brahmanism today and Ananai-kyō tomorrow, as long as there is the true heart to think of the Great-Freedom Lord at the very bottom of the heart, names and professional pseudonyms created by humans are the very least of things. The Great Deity will surely peer into our hearts as if they were placed before a mirror and let us accomplish our purpose. "Even if separated as snow, ice, rain, or hail, once fallen they are the waters of the same valley stream," as the saying goes. Without the readiness to face occasions and respond to changes, being round and rolling, shifting in a thousand ways and changing in ten thousand, and acting with absolute freedom, one can never possibly participate in divine work. In the homeland of Mesopotamia, distinguished priests are lined up like the constellations, but all of them are of the headlong-rush doctrine, stubborn and incorrigible, unsuited to the times, and completely inflexible; not a single person acts with free and easy flexibility like you. Ah, while Brahmanism has splendid teachings, I was worried day and night that there were no figures to utilize them. However, when I thought that such a shrewd propaganda envoy as you was hiding within Brahmanism, tears of joy overflowed, too precious to waste. Since the gods always prepare human instruments of grand strategy and use them, do not worry in the least. When the time comes, the gods will draw close the destined mind and spirit to make them perform divine service and successfully achieve the divine government, so one never knows who or what kind of person is hidden where. The teaching of the gods that there are friends even among enemies and enemies even among friends is undeniable. Now, above all this, let us worry no more. Dear Takahime-sama, from now on let us melt our hearts together, become sisters, and participate in divine work, will we not?'

蜈蚣姫『時世時節で、今日はバラモン教となり、明日は三五教と変ずるとも、心のドン底に大自在天様を思ふ真心さへあれば、人間の作つた名称雅号は末の末です。大神様はキツとお互の心を鏡にかけた如く御洞察遊ばして、目的を遂げさせて下さるでせう「雪氷、雨や霰と隔つとも、落つれば同じ谷川の水」とやら、機に臨み変に応じ円転滑脱、千変万化、自由自在の活動をなすだけの用意がなければ到底神業に参加する事は出来ませぬ。メソポタミヤの本国には綺羅星の如く立派な神司は並んで居りますが、何れも猪突主義の頑愚度し難き、時勢に合ない融通の利かぬ者計りで、お前さまのやうな豁達自在の活動をする人は一人もありませぬので、あゝバラモン教も立派な教理はありながら、之を活用する人物がないと明け暮れ心配して居りました。然るに貴女のやうな抜目のない宣伝使が、バラモン教の中に隠れて居たかと思へば、勿体なくて嬉し涙が零れます。神様は何時も経綸の人間を拵へて神が使うて居るから、必ず心配致すな、サアと云ふ所になりたら、因縁の身魂を神が引き寄せて御用を勤めさせて、立派に神政成就をさして見せる程に、何処に何んな者が隠してあるか分りは致さぬぞよ。敵の中にも味方あり味方の中にも敵があると仰有つた神様の御教示は争はれぬもの、もう此上は何事も心配致しませぬ。何卒高姫さま、是からは打ち解けて姉妹となり、神業に参加しようではありませぬか』

Takahime: 'Nanibun futsutsuka na watakushi, yukitodakanu koto bakari de gozaimasu kara, nanitozo anata no imouto da to omoute, nani ni tsukete mo goshidau o onegai shimasu.'

Mukadehime: 'Tagai ni ki no tsukanu koto wa shiraseaute, iyoiyo senkiitsuki no kuwatsudō o tashi, otto no omei o kuwaifuku katasaneba, niyōbau no yaku ga sumimasen kara naa. Takahime-sama, anata no otto Miyamasama ni taishi maoshiwake ga arimasumai.'

Takahime: 'Watakushi no otto Miyamawake wa gozonji no tōri ningiyō no yō na otoko de, watakushi ga migi e muke to ueba "hai" to ōte migi o muki, hidari to ueba hidari o mukuto iu, hontau ni otonashii ketsukō na hito desu kara, watakushi ga guwanmō jiyōjiyu, tegara o arawashite miseta tokoro de, amari yorokobi mo itashimasumai. Mukadehime: 'There is simply no comparison. Compared to the cavern of Mikunigatake, this is roughly only about a third of the size. I too was driven out of my splendid Onigashima, and have fallen step by step until I had to enter such a cramped place. When I think of it, it is so mortifying that I cannot bear it. Even so, night and morning I pray to Kamisama to somehow accomplish this purpose, and I pray without forgetting it even in my dreams—praying that the Ontaishiyō Gofūfu remain Gokenzen and carry out this purpose Akumade, and that my husband faithfully serves in Shingefu safely. From now on, you and I must join forces and carry out Shiyoshi Kuwantetsu Akumade.'

Takahime: 'That is indeed so. I kindly ask for your Goshidau in every way. However, unlike this splendid cavern, how few people there are today.'

Mukadehime: 'Yes, because they are holding a festival in the sanctuary hall today, no one is here. As you can hear, the sound of music is audible just like that. That is the voice of the festival.'

Takahime: 'I too would love to visit and pay my respects even once.'

Mukadehime: 'Dōzo, please take your time and pay your respects later. If you enter midway, everyone's concentration will be scattered, and the festival cannot be performed completely—by the way, Takahime-sama, who on earth are the two men you brought with you?'

Takahime: 'I became so absorbed in our conversation that I forgot to tell you, but one is a man named Tomohiko, a missionary of Brahmanism, and the other is my servant named Kanshū, a man whom one cannot tell whether he is a fool or wise, or whether he is righteous or evil.'

Mukadehime: 'What do you say? Tomohiko of Brahmanism has come? That is indeed a strange matchmaking by Kamisama—his appearance has changed so much that I mistook him. Ah, now that I hear it, there seemed to be a red spot on the tip of his nose. That fellow is the man who hoodwinked my only daughter and disappeared hand in hand to parts unknown; that he should come to such a place after going around in circles is also strange. If I ask him, I am sure to learn of my daughter's whereabouts.—Now, now, Takahime-sama, please keep this a secret. Since I will ask him directly in a roundabout way,'

saying this, with ripples pounding in her heart, she was all flustered. Takahime left this place, appeared in the next room, turned to the two men,

Takahime: 'Tomohiko-san and Kanshū, you must have been bored. Mukadehime-sama asks you to pass a little further inside, so please go through. Since this place is also the archery ground of Brahmanism... Tomohiko-san, it is as though you have returned to your parents' home. Rejoice, for after a long time you can meet Mukadehime-samay.'

Tomohiko: 'What are you saying? This is Mukadehime-sama's mansion? That must be a mistake. There are many names alike in the world. Surely she could not be Mukadehime-sama, the wife of the vice-chief Onikumabetsu, who used to reside at the main headquarters.'

Takahime: 'That is so, that is so. There may be some awkward things, but you have no choice except to endure. Even if you think of escaping, you cannot escape. Since you are also the husband of her beloved daughter, she will not treat you too cruelly. Rest assured and accompany me.'

Tomohiko's complexion suddenly turns pale, and trembling with fear, he follows behind Takahime and advances into the inner room.

蜈蚣姫『とても比べものにはなりませぬ。三国ケ岳の岩窟に比ぶればまアざつと三分の一位なものです。妾も立派な鬼ケ城を追はれ、だんだんとこんな狭い所へ入らねばならないやうに落ちて仕舞ひました。思へば思へば残念で耐りませぬ。それでも何とかしてこの目的を遂げたいと朝夕神様を祈り、何卒御大将御夫婦が御健全で此目的を飽迄も遂行遊ばすやうに、又我夫の無事に神業に奉仕するやうにと、夢寐にも忘れずに祈願致して居ります。是からは貴女と二人で腹を合せ、飽迄も初志を貫徹せねばなりませぬ』

高姫『左様で御座います。何分に宜敷く御指導を願ます。併し乍ら此立派な岩窟に似ず今日はお人が少い事で御座いますな』

蜈蚣姫『ハイ、今日は神前の間で祭典を致して居りますので、誰も此処には居りませぬ。あの通り音楽の声が聞えて居るでせう。あれが祭典の声です』

高姫『妾も一度参拝させて頂き度いもので御座います』

蜈蚣姫『何卒後で緩りと参拝して下さいませ。中途に入りますと皆の者の気が散り、完全にお祭が出来ませぬから、……時に高姫さま、貴女のお連れになつた二人の男は、ありや一体何者で御座いますか』

高姫『ついお話に身が入つて貴女に申上げる事を忘れて居ましたが、彼はバラモン教の宣伝使の友彦と云ふ男、も一人は妾の召使の貫州と云う阿呆とも賢いとも、正とも邪とも見当の付かない男で御座います』

蜈蚣姫『何と仰せられます。バラモン教の友彦が来たとは、それは又妙な神様のお引合せ、……余り姿が変つて居るので見違へて居つた。アヽさう聞けば鼻の先に赤い所があつたやうだ。彼奴は私の一人娘をチヨロまかし、手に手を取つて何処ともなく姿を隠した男、廻り廻つてこんな所へ来るとは是又不思議、あれに尋ねたら定めて娘の消息が分るであらう。……コレコレ高姫さま、此事は秘密にして置いて下さい。妾が直接に遠廻しに聞いて見ますから』

と心臓に波を打たせながら、そはそはとして居る。高姫は此場を立つて次の間に現はれ両人に向ひ、

高姫『友彦さまに貫州、退屈だつたらう。蜈蚣姫さまが一寸奥へ通つて呉れと仰有るから通つて下さい。此処もバラモン教の射場だから……、友彦さま、お前は親の家へ戻つたやうなものだ。久し振りで蜈蚣姫様に御面会が出来るから喜びなさい』

友彦『何と仰有る。此処が蜈蚣姫様のお館ですか、そりや違ひませう。世界に同じ名は沢山御座います。まさか本山に居られた、副棟梁の鬼熊別の奥さまの蜈蚣姫さまではありますまい』

高姫『さうだとも　さうだとも、チツとは不首尾な事があらうが辛抱しなくては仕方がない。逃やうと云つたつて逃げられはせぬワ。お前も可愛い娘の婿だから、さう酷くも当らつしやるまい。安心して妾に伴いて御座れ』

友彦は顔色忽ち蒼白となり、恐る恐る高姫の後について奥の間に進み往く。
