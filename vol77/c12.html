§ 2

The divine breath of the Kami of Mihishiro is tranquil.

What manner of dreams are they causing to be woven?

Dark clouds of Tokijiku surge and rise up,

To attack the Valley where Demons Dwell on the morrow.

When I gaze upon the sleeping figure of Wamorihiko no Kami,

His mouth is fastened into the shape of the character for he.

Tamayamahiko no Kami uses a tree root as a pillow,

And lies sleeping with his right flank downward.

If one sleeps with the right belly downward in the shape of the character for sa,

It is heard that the life of existence is long.

Upon the face of Wakaharuhiko no Kami as he sleeps,

A diseased leaf of camphor has fallen.

The snoring of Mochimunehiko no Kami was high,

Like the roaring of thunder.

Nahomichihiko no Kami, stretching his hands and feet wide in the shape of the character for dai,

And spreading them ever wider, utters sleep-talk.

Yamatohime no Kami lies with her belly bowed to the earth,

Bearing the light of the moon upon her back.

Chikahihime no Kami opens and closes her twin eyes from time to time,

As she sleeps on.

Yumusubi no Himegami sits upright on the great earth,

With her left and right hands clasped, she slumbers.

Masamichihiko no Kami from time to time blows forth a thick breath,

Startling me.

O Masamichihiko no Kami, who from time to time blows forth a thick breath and moves the region of your mouth munchingly,

I, Kumokawahiko, have been entrusted with the night-watch,

And guard the breathing of the various Kami.

If the Magatumine should come attacking, Kumokawahiko,

Will slash and cast them away with the living Kototama.

The sky of the eastern dawn has grown faintly bright.

Once more the heavenly sun shall rise.

Tsukuyomi no Kami, concealing himself upon the ridge of Mount Shirakamagatake,

Has greeted the eastern dawn.'

Thus Kumokawahiko no Kami was charged with the duty of night watch, and without closing an eye for a moment, faithfully served until the break of night.
