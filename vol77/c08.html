Chapter 8: 征 Demon の出陣 [1940]

征魔の出陣〔1940〕

§ 1

By the descent of Takarahime no Kami and the ten pillars of Kami, the island chain of Banri was pacified; yet in the valleys of Mount Hakubagatake, unseasonable dark clouds rose, ever and anon blocking the heavens, concealing the light of the sun and moon, and raising violent storms to devastate the land time and time again. Desiring first to conquer this Magatsumi, Takarahime no Kami led the ten pillars of Kami, departed from the hills of Banri, and set forth toward the deep valley where the dragon god of Mount Hakubagatake dwelt, chanting a sacred miuta:

Through the august miizu of the Lord God, cleanly pacified Is the island chain of Banri, yet do malignant magatsu lurk therein? The dark clouds rising up within the valleys of Mount Hakubagatake Are the turbulent water and fire of Magatsumi, shrouding the heavens. We shall destroy the magatsu that raise tempests from time to time And afflict the grasses and trees of the island of Banri. Attended by the ten pillars of Kami, from now onward Shall I advance toward the stronghold of crookedness. The island of Banri, which the Lord God hath entrusted unto us, Shall be restored and revived as a land of peaceful hearts.

Wamorihiko no Kami, desiring to serve in the august retinue, chanted a sacred miuta:

Awe-inspiring are the divine words of Himegami; verily, I Shall guard the august van of the ten pillars of Kami. Those magatsu lurking deep in the valleys, are they dragon gods, Or are they great serpents spitting unseasonable venom? Spitting unseasonable venom and bringing disasters Unto this island, such Kami shall I chastise and rebuke.

Yamazumihiko no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

Within this endless divine land, lurking and residing, I shall attack and destroy the haunts of the evil gods. While concealing the sun and moon of the eternal august sky, O ugly crooked gods who let evil vapors surge forth! Upon this island where the August Mihirogi no Kami descended, I cannot comprehend how crooked gods could lurk. Yet morning and evening, spitting forth venom, They raise dark clouds in the skies; And the dark clouds block the heavens and crawl upon the earth, Defiling this Toyo-asoritsu-kuni morning and night.

Wakaharuhiko no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

The blossoms of all flowers, afflicted by the venom Of Magatsumi, are withering and drooping as they bloom. Even the tree fruits of the island, bearing unseasonably, Are eaten by insects and fall without ripening. Though I have heard this island is a land of eternal spring, In the wild rage of Magatsumi it feels like the heart of winter. Mingling amidst the dark clouds, red-crowned Cranes are flying astray in the august sky.

Hosomuhiko no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

Piteous it is how the myriad frogs that have long inhabited this island Hide their shadows day by day. The mice and frogs that plow the soil of Araigane Are perishing and vanishing in the water and fire of the crooked gods. That boars and dogs, as days and months pile up, grow fewer in number Is likewise because of Magatsumi. Removing the harm of Magatsumi, upon the island of Banri, I desire to save the myriad frogs. Following the August Mihirogi no Kami, toward the valleys of Mount Hakubagatake Shall I guide and reason with the great serpent.

Naomichihiko no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

There is no crooked god who can oppose the righteous path Of heaven and earth decreed by the Lord God. Flourishing the sword of the true words of Kototama, I shall smite and scatter the ugly Magatsumi. O heaven and earth brought into being by the power of Kototama! There is no magatsu that does not fear the living Kototama. Raising my eyes to look from the valleys of Mount Hakubagatake, Layers of dark clouds pierce straight up to the heavens.

Yamatohikohime no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

Following the true words of the August Mihirogi no Kami, I shall advance into the valleys of Mount Hakubagatake. Though I indeed am a goddess, with the light Of the sword of Kototama shall I illuminate the magatsu. Concealing the sun and moon of the eternal heavens and raging wildly, Those ugly magatsu must be hewn down and cast away. The joy of lining up our horses valiantly with the ten pillars of Kami And advancing into the valleys of Hakuba! Dispelling the darkness of eternity and beholding the sun of heaven All day long, would that we might make this an age of the gods! When evening comes, with the moon shining brightly in the vast sky, Would that we might open up an age of the gods where they shine brilliantly.

Chigaihime no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

I shall head toward the stronghold of the ugly crooked gods Lurking in the thousand gorges and eight hundred gorges of Mount Hakubagatake. How joyful it is for the grand procession of seven gods and four goddesses To advance into the valleys of Hakuba! Crooked gods who dare obstruct the path where I go Shall I hew down and cast away without a moment's hesitation. Even the sound of insects singing in the thickets beside the road As we walk the mountain path is sorrowful. O myriad birds, insects, and beasts, be at peace! Before long, the shadows of Magatsumi shall perish. So long as there are kami who have descended from the lofty august palace of heaven, So long shall the land be a peaceful realm; Smiting and sweeping away the crooked gods with the living Kototama, We shall purify and clear it to pioneer the land.

Yuyuhime no Kami chanted a sacred miuta:

Mountains and fields alike are filled with the divine spirit; The magatsu lurking in this Toyo-asoritsu-kuni Turn into dark clouds, into mists, And into raging tempests, running wild in fury. The crops grown and nurtured upon the island of Banri Grow ill-favored in the water and fire of Magatsumi. Reasoning with the magatsu lurking deep in the valleys, We shall forge and solidify it as Urayasukuni. May the kami, and all living things in forest and nature, Thrive and prosper within Urayasukuni.

原文（近代文語）

田族比女の神及び十柱の神の降臨に依りて万里の島ケ根は治まりつれども、未だ白馬ケ岳の谷間には、非時黒雲立ち昇り折々天に塞がり、日月の光を隠し暴風雨を起して国土を荒すこと再三再四なりければ、先づ此の曲津見を征服せむと、田族比女の神は十柱の神を率ゐて万里の丘を立出で、白馬ケ岳の竜神のすめる深谷を指して出で立たさむとして、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『主の神の御稜威に清く治まりし 万里ケ島根に曲津の潜めるか 白馬ケ岳谷間に立ちたつ黒雲は 曲津見の水火か天を包める 雨風を折々起して万里の島の 木草を艱むる曲津滅さむ 十柱の神を従へ今よりは 曲の砦に吾進むべし 主の神の依さし給ひし万里の島を 心安国とよみがへらせむ』

輪守比古の神は御供に仕へむとして御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『比女神の神言畏し吾こそは 十柱神の御尾前守らむ 谷深く潜める曲津は竜神か 大蛇か非時毒気吐くかも 毒気非時吐きてこの島に 災を為す神を譴責めむ』

霊山比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『果しなき此神国に潜み居る 魔神の在処を攻め滅さむ 久方の御空の月日を隠しつつ 邪気漲らす醜の曲神よ 御樋代の神の天降りし此島に 曲神潜むとは吾心得ず さりながら朝な夕なに毒気を 吐きつつ空に黒雲起すも 黒雲は天に塞がり地を這ひて この食国を朝夕汚すも』

若春比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『八千草の花も曲津見の毒気に 艱まされつつ咲き萎むなり 非時に実りし島の木の実さへ 虫に食はれて実らず落ちつつ 此島は常春の国と聞きつれど 曲津の荒びに冬心地すも 黒雲の中に紛れて丹頂の 鶴は御空に迷ひ飛ぶなり』

保宗比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『此島に久しく棲みし百蛙の 日に日に影を潜むる憐れさ 荒金の土を耕す鼠蛙は 曲神の水火に滅び失せつつ 猪も犬も月日重ねて其数の 少くなりしも曲津見の為なり 曲津見の害を除きて万里の島の 百の蛙を助けたく思ふ 御樋代の神に従ひ白馬ケ岳の 谷間の大蛇を言向けてみむ』

直道比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『主の神の定め給ひし天地の 正しき道に刃向ふ曲津なし 言霊の真言の剣振り翳し 斬りはふりてむ醜の曲津見を 言霊の功に生りし天地よ 生言霊を恐れぬ曲津は無き 仰ぎ見れば白馬ケ岳の谷間より 天に冲する八重の黒雲』

山跡比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御樋代の神の真言に従ひて 白馬ケ岳の谷間に進まむ 吾はしも女神なれども言霊の 剣の光に曲津を照らさむ 久方の月日を隠して荒び狂ふ 醜の曲津を斬り放るべし 十柱の神と雄々しく駒並べて 白馬の谷に進む楽しさ 常世行く暗を晴らして天日を 終日仰ぐ神世となさばや 夕されば月朗かに大空に 輝き給ふ神世とひらかむ』

千貝比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『白馬ケ岳千峡八百峡に潜みたる 醜の曲神の砦に向はむ 七男四女の神の一行堂々と 進むも楽し白馬の谷間に 吾伊行く道にさやらむ曲神は 躊躇もなく斬りて放らむ 山路行く道の傍への草叢に 鳴く虫の音も悲しかりける 百鳥よ虫よ獣よ安くあれ 今に曲津見の影滅ぶべし 久方の天津高宮ゆ降りたる 神ある限り安き国原 曲神を生言霊に斬り払ひ 清め澄まして国土を拓かむ』

湯結比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『山も野も神の霊気に充たさるる 此食国に潜む曲津はも 曲津見は黒雲となり霧となり 嵐となりて荒び狂ふも 万里の島に作り育つる穀物も 曲津の水火に生ひ立ち悪し 谷深く潜める曲津を言向けて 心安国土と造り固めむ 神々も生きとし生ける森羅万象も 心安国土に栄えしむべし』

§ 2

Masamichihiko no Kami chants a miuta:

None oppose the path of the true makoto, Of the kototama brought forth by the kami of ametsuchi. Though eighty magatsumi lurk in deep valleys, I shall cut them down and cast them away with the ikukototama. Born in the blessing of the kototama, I now set forth upon the kototamaikusa. When there is even a slight cloud upon my soul, Should not the ikukototama fail to shine as well? Performing misogi in the pure shimizu of the valley stream, I shall advance into the kototamaikusa. Without fearing the light of the mihishirogami, How bold are the lurking magatsu demons! Becoming clouds, becoming amekaze, becoming mist, Hateful are the magatsumi who obscure miyo. The source of Madegaha is distant, connected to the clouds Rising in peaks upon Mount Hakuba. Though I go and go, Mount Hakuba remains far; I shall speed the ashinami of my horse and advance.

Kumokahahiko no Kami chants a miuta:

The ten tohashira of kami serving the mihishirogami Are the guardians of the kototamaikusa. As the ten tohashira of kami align their bits, There is no magatsu to obstruct our advancing path. Cutting down the ugly commanders of the magatsumi with the sword of kototama, We shall illuminate miyo. Illuminated by the awe-inspiring light of the mihishirogami, How joyful it is to ascend upon the kitame against the magatsu.

Thus, as the kamigami are about to set forth upon the expedition against the magatsumi, chanting miuta, onomōnomo mount their horses, with Wamorihiko no Kami in the sentō, Tamayamahiko no Kami, Wakaharuhiko no Kami, Yasumunehiko no Kami, and Naomichihiko no Kami serving as the vanguard, with Tazokuhime no Kami in the center, and the other five pillars of kami guarding the rear, they advance valiantly toward the demon-infested valley of Mount Hakuba, where dark clouds rise up.

原文（近代文語）

正道比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『天地の神の生みてし言霊の 真言の道に逆らふものなし 谷深く八十の曲津見潜むとも 生言霊に斬り放りてむ 言霊の幸に生れし吾にして 言霊戦の門出なすも わが魂に少しの曇りある時は 生言霊も光らざるべきを 谷川の清き清水に禊して 吾は進まむ言霊戦に 御樋代の神の光を恐れずに 潜める曲津は強ものなる 雲となり雨風となり霧となりて 神世を曇らす曲津見憎しも 万里河の源遠し白馬ケ岳の 峰に湧き立つ雲に続けば 行き行けど白馬ケ岳は遥かなり 駒の脚並速めて進まむ』

雲川比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御樋代の神に仕へし十柱の 神は言霊戦の司よ 十柱の神の轡を並べつつ 進まむ道にさやる曲津なし 曲津見の醜の司を言霊の 剣に斬りて神世を照らさむ 御樋代神の稜威の光に照らされて 曲津の征途に上る楽しさ』

斯く神々は曲津見の征途に上らむとして、御歌詠ませつつ各自駒に跨り、輪守比古の神を先頭に霊山比古の神、若春比古の神、保宗比古の神、直道比古の神を前供として、田族比女の神を正中に其他の五柱の神、御後辺を守りつつ、黒雲立ち昇る白馬ケ岳の魔棲ケ谷を目指して進ませ給ふぞ雄々しけれ。
