Chapter 24: 会者定離 [1956]

会者定離〔1956〕

§ 1

The clouds and fog that had veiled and blocked the heaven and earth of Marikagashima cleared away completely, and the sun and moon cast their pure light upon the earth, giving the feeling of the resuscitation of all things. Hereupon, the country name was newly proclaimed as the Divine Land of Banri, and all foundations were firmly established for myriad ages. Every living thing was gathered together in the sacred precinct of Marikagashima, and a banquet of celebration was held. After seven days and seven nights, all living things returned respectively to their places of eternal abode, and like a calm after a storm, the palace of the Mihishirogami returned to profound silence.

In such a place, as the twilight sky corresponding to the rear of Mount Hakubakagatake rising to the west became filled with a strange kind of light, Kami Takarahime stood upon the high hall, viewed this state of affairs, and deeming it to be the descent of the precious Mihishirogami, immediately had Wamorihiko no Kami and Wakaharuhiko no Kami whip their horses through the moonlit dusk in order to respectfully welcome the Mihishirogami.

Hereupon, the two deities, in accordance with the divine command, hurried away to the beach of Orayu no Hama lying to the west of Mount Hakubakagatake, where they encountered five deities of the Heavenly Deities resting in the ever-verdant forest. Exchanging polite words and guiding the deities toward the sacred precinct of Marikagashima, by around dusk of the following day, they at last respectfully reported the completion of their mission. Wamorihiko no Kami respectfully presented to the grand presence of Kami Takarahime that he had safely returned, guiding the procession of the eight venerable Mihishirogami.

Wamorihiko no Kami: 'Reverencing your august divine command, we two Hurriedly arrived at the beach of Orayu. When we arrived at the shores of Orayu, within the shade of the forest, Kami Asakahime was resting in peace. Kneeling down before Your August Presence with awe and trembling, I respectfully proclaimed and transmitted your august true words. Mihishirogami Asakahime nodded in assent, And following the deities, she has come hither.'

Kami Takarahime replied to this and chanted an imperial poem:

Takarahime: 'From the distant high mountains of scenic peaks you have descended, How well you have come to welcome the Mihishirogami. By all means guide them to this high hall, I too will descend the steps to welcome them.'

Hereupon, the two deities Wamorihiko no Kami and Wakaharuhiko no Kami cried out 'Oh!' in awe and reverence, and before the procession of Kami Asakahime and her retinue who were waiting in the courtyard, with courteous words:

Wamorihiko no Kami: 'Now then, Asakahime, the Mihishirogami, Please proceed to this high hall. Four deities, follow on behind, And I too shall follow in your august rear.'

Kami Asakahime offered a slight bow and quietly proceeded toward the high hall. Hereupon, Kami Takarahime descended the steps of the high hall and respectfully awaited the procession of Kami Asakahime, but as the august figure of the hime drew near before her eyes:

Takarahime: 'How awe-inspiring is the descent of the Mihishirogami, In whose sacred presence I now respectfully welcome them. Now then, I shall guide you to this high hall, Ascend, ye five deities.'

Hereupon, Kami Asakahime chanted a sacred poem:

Asakahime: 'The Mihishirogami of Takarahime, famed by hearsay, Are none other than you, and I hold you dear.'

Having thus finished chanting, serenely following behind Kami Takarahime, Kami Asakahime ascended toward the high hall. Kami Asakahime chanted a sacred poem:

Asakahime: 'Eighty pillars of Mihishirogami in various places, I had heard were present, and today I have met them. Thinking upon the arduous divine labors of the Mihishirogami who pacify the immature land, The country is not yet settled, and to pioneer the raging plains of Magatsuhi Is indeed a bitter task. Enduring various sufferings, you pioneered Marikagashima, And I think upon your august achievements. I am now about to proceed to the lands of the west, And have stopped by along the way. Hearing beforehand that in this island eighty hime deities reside, I turned aside to behold them. At your august figure, so earnestly present, My joy is truly more than I can bear. Maintaining eternal life and growing ever youthful, Pray bring forth the deities of the land's soul.'

Kami Takarahime could not restrain her deep emotion, and responded with a sacred poem:

Takarahime: 'How wondrous, how reverend are the gentle words Of Kami Asakahime, by which I am revived. The eight deities and the venerable hime in her own person Visiting me—how awe-inspiring is this day. Waiting and longing for the advent of Akitsuno no Kami, Now at last the years have grown aged. In this high hall of lovely prospects, in peace Shedding your light, pray abide here.'

万里ケ島の天地を塞ぎたる邪神の潜みし雲霧はくまなく晴れて、日月は清く光を地上に投げ万物蘇生の思ひして、茲に新しく国名を万里の神国と称へ、総ての基礎を万世に固め給ひ、生きとし生けるものを悉く万里ケ島の聖所に集めて、寿ぎの宴を開き給ひしが、七日七夜の後、総ての生きとし生けるものは各自常住の地に帰り、水を打ちたる如く、御樋代神の御舎は静寂に帰したり。

かかる所に西に聳ゆる白馬ケ岳の背後にあたれる夕暮の空は、一種異様の光に満ちぬれば、田族比女の神は高殿に立ちて、この様を覧はし、尊き御樋代神の降臨なりとして、直に輪守比古の神、若春比古の神をして御樋代神を迎へ奉るべく、黄昏の月下を鞭うたせ給ひける。

茲に二柱の神は神言のまにまに、白馬ケ岳の西に当る御来矢の浜辺に馳けつけ給へば、常磐の森に憩はせ給ふ五柱の天津神等に出会ひまし、恭しく言葉を交し、万里ケ丘の聖所に神々を導きつつ、翌日の黄昏頃やうやくに復命申し給ひける。輪守比古の神は八柱の尊き御樋代神一行を導き、無事に帰りたることを田族比女の神の大前に奏上し給ひぬ。

『わが公の神言畏み二柱は 御来矢の浜に急ぎ着きけり 御来矢の浜辺に着けば森蔭に 朝香比女の神休らひ給ひぬ 恐る恐る吾御前に跪きて 公の真言を宣り伝へける 御樋代神朝香の比女は頷きて 諸神を従へ此処に来ませり』

田族比女の神はこれに答へて御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『久方の高地秀山より降りましし 御樋代神をよくも迎へ来しよ 兎も角もこれの高殿に導けよ 吾も階段を下りて迎へむ』

茲に輪守比古の神、若春比古の神の二柱は「オー」と一声畏まりつつ、御庭に待たせ給へる朝香比女の神一行の前に言葉も恭しく、

『いざさらば御樋代神の朝香比女 進ませ給へこれの高殿へ 四柱の神も後よりつづきませ 吾も御後に従ひまつらむ』

朝香比女の神は軽く目礼しながら、静々と高殿さして進みたまふ。茲に田族比女の神は高殿の階段を降りて恭しく朝香比女の神の一行を待たせ給ひけるが、比女の御姿目前に迫りけるより、

『あらたふと御樋代神の天降りましし 尊さに吾は迎へ奉るも いざさらばこの高殿に案内せむ のぼらせ給へ五柱の神』

茲に朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『音に聞く田族の比女の御樋代は 汝なりしかも愛しと思ふ』

斯く歌ひ終り、悠然として田族比女の神の後より、朝香比女の神は高殿さしてのぼらせ給ひける。朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『所々に御樋代神は八十柱 いますと聞きしを今日会ひにけり 地稚き国土を固むる御樋代神の 苦しき神業を思ひやらるる 国土は未だ定まらずして曲津見の 猛る国原拓くは苦しき 諸々の艱みに堪へて万里ケ島を 拓き給ひし公の功を思ふ 吾は今西方の国土に進まむと その道すがらを立ち寄りしはや この島に八十比女神のましますと かねて聞きしゆ立ち寄りて見し まめやかに在せる公の御姿に 吾は嬉しさ堪へやらぬかも 永久の命保ちて若々しく 国魂神を生ませ給はれ』

田族比女の神は感激に堪へず、御歌もて答へ給ふ。

『ありがたし尊し朝香比女の神の 優しき言葉に蘇りける 八柱神尊き比女の御自ら 吾を訪はせし今日の畏さ 顕津男の神の出でまし待ちまちて 今はやうやく年さびにけり 眺めよきこの高殿に安らかに 光放ちて在しましませ』

§ 2

Asakahime no Kami chants a miuta.

'I too share the same feelings, O Mihishiro, Seeking and coming to meet my dear lord; My dear lord shall fight in the western region Against a hundred hosts of Magatsu; Though it is a covenant of Hitoiki, if it be the divine mission of the Lord Kami, I think it hard to forget.'

Takarahime no Kami chants a miuta.

'At the words of beloved Asakahime, I could not help but shed tears; How pitiful is the Goddess who tastes The sorrow of yearning for her dear lord.'

Here the two Mihishirogami, like friends of a hundred years, melted into each other from the depths of their hearts, and while drowning in tears of sympathy, unawares they piled up the days. Takarahime no Kami took out numerous diamonds which she had brought back as the spoils of the war of the Kamigami in the demon-haunting valley of Shirahamagadake, and presented them to Asakahime no Kami; praising and extolling them as truly rare things, Asakahime no Kami accepted them with a glad heart just as Takarahime no Kami presented them. The gemstones presented by Takarahime no Kami were the most brilliantly dazzling and greatest diamonds, being rare and precious things.

Asakahime no Kami, as a token of gratitude, took out a flint from her bosom, struck fire, gathered dead grass all around, and caused the fire to burn; as it was the first time that the ten Kamigami, beginning with Takarahime no Kami, had seen the burning of true fire, all of them uttered voices of admiration, and Asakahime no Kami presented this flint from her own hands to Takarahime no Kami as a return gift for the gemstones.

Takarahime no Kami chants a miuta.

'Ah, how bright, warm, and blazing the fire burns, True fire is indeed a Kami that illuminates the dark night! As long as the true fire has its efficacy in this land, The Magatsu no Kami must not rage; Burning away the mountain fields where the crooked kami lurk, It has truly become a fine true fire for purifying; The flint bestowed by Asakahime no Kami Is the precious treasure of the divine land of Banri; Until this very day, in my foolishness, I did not realize that striking stone brings forth true fire; Since this treasure has been bestowed upon me, I shall burn and destroy all the crookedness of the land of Banri.'

Asakahime no Kami chants a miuta.

'Since it is a flint bestowed by the Kami of sharp and ringing emergence, To present it publicly as the pacification of the land— If there be but this one flint, even the immature land Shall suddenly and firmly flourish; All grains and every other food, If roasted with true fire, will taste all the better; Even pure spring water will become hot water by the power of true fire, And shall become an offering to be dedicated to the Kami.'

Takarahime no Kami chants yet another miuta.

'The gemstones presented to the Goddess Asakahime Have light but neither heat nor burn; Presenting light without life to the public, And receiving light that possesses life!'

Here the two Kamigami, having finished the presentation and exchange of the diamond and the flint, once more relaxed and indulged in pleasant conversation.

Hatsuhikohiko no Kami chants a miuta.

'Crossing over the crests of the waves to the divine land of Banri, Seeking and coming, protecting my lord, I have beheld a rare and glowing jewel, So soon after arriving at this new land.'

Kiritsuhikohiko no Kami chants a miuta.

'How rare it is to find a sanctuary Amidst the towering mountains like this! This divine land where Mihishirogami and Mihishirogami have met Shall flourish for eternity.'

Tachiyosehime no Kami chants a miuta.

'Following in the train of my lord to the land of Banri, How I have beheld and worshipped the night-shining jewel! Though the night-shining jewel is beautiful, it has no life; It cannot compare to the true word of the true fire of the flint.'

Amaharehime no Kami chants a miuta.

'Awe-inspiring is my lord who bestowed The flint that drove away the various crooked ones! Though there are countless precious treasures, truly there is no treasure Surpassing the flint from which true fire comes forth.'

Wamorihikohiko no Kami chants a miuta.

'The flint bestowed by the Goddess Asakahime Is indeed the living treasure of this new land! No matter how brilliantly the light of the gemstone shines, It was a treasure possessed by evil kami.'

Ryuzanhikohiko no Kami chants a miuta.

'How awe-inspiring it is that I, who directly hear the words Of the eight Himegami who descended from heaven, Am a Hime so valiant, crossing seas and mountains over many myriads of ri Upon encountering the Kami of Hinokatsuotsu!'

朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『吾もまた同じ思ひの御樋代よ 背の岐美に会ふと求ぎて来れり 背の岐美は西方あたり曲津見の 百の軍と戦ひ給はむ 一水火の契なれども主の神の 依さしなりせば忘れ難く思ふ』

『愛くしき朝香の比女の言の葉に 吾はおもはず涙しにけり 背の岐美を恋ふる心の苦しさを 味はひ給ふ女神いとしも』

茲に二柱の御樋代神は百年の知己の如く、互に心の底より解け合ひ、同情の涙にくれ給ひつつ思はず知らず日を重ね給ひける。田族比女の神は、白馬ケ岳の魔棲ケ谷にて神々の戦利品として持ち帰りたる数多のダイヤモンドを取出し、朝香比女の神に奉りければ、実に珍しき物よと賞め讃へながら、田族比女の神の奉るままに、こころよく受け取らせ給ひぬ。田族比女の神の奉りたる宝石は、最も光り眩く、最も大いなるダイヤモンドにして稀なる珍しき物なりける。

朝香比女の神は其の謝礼として、懐中より燧石を取出し、火を切り出で四辺の枯芝を集めて火を燃し給ひければ、田族比女の神を始めとし十柱の神々は初めて真火の燃ゆるを見給ひしこととて、何れも感嘆の声を放ち給ひけるが、朝香比女の神は宝石の謝礼として手づからのこの燧石を田族比女の神に贈り給ひける。

茲に二柱の神はダイヤモンド、燧石の贈答終り、再び寛ぎて歓談に耽けらせ給ふ。

初頭比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

起立比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

立世比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『わが公に従ひ奉りて万里の国土に 夜光の玉を拝みけるかも 夜光の玉は美しかれども命なし 燧石の真火の真言にしかざり』

天晴比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『諸々の曲津をやらひし燧石を 贈らせ給ひしわが公畏し 貴宝数多あれども真火出づる 燧石にまさる宝なきかな』

輪守比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『朝香比女神の賜ひし燧石こそ この新国土の生ける宝よ 宝石の光は如何に輝くも 邪神の持ちし宝なりける』

霊山比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『畏しや天降りましたる八柱の 比女神の言葉直に聞く吾は 顕津男の神に出会ふと数万里の 海山渡らす比女ぞ雄々しき』

§ 3

Wakaharuhiko-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'At last the clouds and mists clear over the vast Kuni,

Two Futahashira of Mihishirogami have descended from heaven.

My Lord is noble, yet the merit of the eight Hime,

Is even higher still.'

Mochimunehiko-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'How rare it is that the Mihishirogami, two in number,

Have descended to this Mikuni.

I think the heart of Asakahime-no-Kami,

Who comes forth to the Kuni of Nishikata, is truly Ō.'

Nahomichihiko-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'Longing and yearning for the one pillar of the sacred Mitama,

The Mihishirogamitachi feel no jealousy.

Because they are the Mihishiro, entrusted by the Kamunagara Kami,

They remain pure and refreshing.'

Yamatohime-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'The Miomote of the two Futahashirakami,

Shine like the Tsukihi.

Even to behold them is dazzling, for the appearance of the Mihishiro,

The Kami, shines with great intensity.'

Chikahihime-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'Longing from afar for the sacred Mitama,

The heart of Asakahime-no-Kami is truly Ō.

Breathing through the clouds and mists, the Lord,

Who came treading upon the crests of the waves of the Unabara.'

Yumusubihime-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'Illuminated by the red hearts,

Of the peerlessly Ō Mihishirogamitachi.

The day of return draws near, and I feel sorrow,

Thinking of parting from such beautiful Kami.'

Masamichihiko-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'I shall revere the flint bestowed by the Mihishirogami upon this Mikuni,

As a treasure for a thousand generations.

If we possess such noble living power,

There is nothing to fear in the vast Mikuni.'

Kumokawahiko-no-Kami chants a Miuta:

'The Kuni is born anew, and the new,

True fire shines by the grace of the Kami.

Purifying my body and purifying my heart, I think,

I shall serve the spirit of the flint forever.'

Thus, as the Kami each chanted their Miuta, they celebrated the future of the Kuni, rejoicing in the visit of Asakahime-no-Kami and the gift of the flint as a national treasure; however, Asakahime-no-Kami of the Mihishirogami could not remain in this Kuni for long, and having taken the four previous Kami as her attendants and bid farewell to the various Kami, she boarded the Iwakusu-bune from the beach of Koraiya and proceeded quietly, ever so quietly, toward the southeastern sky across the vast Unabara.
