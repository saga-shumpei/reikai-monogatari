Chapter 7: 万里平定 [1939]

万里平定〔1939〕

§ 1

The Lord Ōkami, who is peacefully established in Amatsutakamiya, soundlessly and unceasingly ringing forth the Kototama of the seventy-five sounds, in order to solidify the world of the muddy sea, first of all, at the beginning, gave birth to the three great mountains of Tsukushigadake, the peak of Takachiho, and Takateruyama, and thereafter caused countless islands to come into existence in the myriad-ri sea, having planned to bring forth and nourish all living things.

At the very beginning, the Lord brought forth Banri no Shima, which is the center of the myriad-ri sea. This island has an area of about eight thousand square ri, with Shirumagadake in the west and Uzugomine in the east, and the clear river flowing through its center is called the Banri River.

The Lord first sounded forth the Kototama of 'tsu' on this island to give birth to rats, and sounded forth the Kototama of 'ku' to bring forth frogs. Even though they were rats, because it was an era when the land was still immature and the realm was not yet regulated, they were larger than today's oxen, and even though they were frogs, their physical bodies were larger than modern human beings. All other animals, beginning with Shinjin, should be understood in accordance with this. Frogs emerged on this island in countless numbers, unnumbered like hundreds of millions, and rats, too, increasing their numbers day by day, dwelt over the entirety of this land where the soil was most fertile, engaged in the cultivation of all grains and continuing their lives. The rats plowed the fields, and the frogs held plows and hoes to pioneer the fields, producing grains and enjoying their lives.

As the ruler of this island, the Lord Ōkami descended a red-crowned crane whose head bore a red mark impressed with the shape of the sun. This crane flew across the august sky from Amatsutakamiya, came flying to the root of Banri no Shima, nested in a single evergreen pine tree towering in luxuriant foliage upon a small elevated hill by the side of the Banri River, and it came to pass that it gave birth to numerous children and presided as the lord of this island.

Nevertheless, without distinction between morning and evening, the roots of this island were blocked by clouds and fogs, reaching a state where one could scarcely distinguish a foot ahead; and even though the sun and moon shone brightly across the sky, due to the clouds in mid-air and the deep mists burning on the ground, the yang breath was cold, and the growth of all things was likewise not sufficient. The spirit of gloom gradually condensed, and various Magatsumi were generated; in the ravines of Shirumagadake dwelt numerous vicious dragons with sword-like skin, and giant serpents likewise lurked in the ravines here and there, vomiting out untimely poisonous smoke. Since that poisonous smoke became clouds and mists, the clan of frogs dwelling on Banri no Shima had their lives threatened and at last came to reduce their numbers. The evil qi gradually coagulated, becoming countless eagles and wildcats that rampaged about, with the eagles making prey of the frogs and the wildcats making prey of the rats, and the tragic scene of raging madness was too painful to behold. However, among the clans of frogs and rats, only those tribes who prayed to the Lord Ōkami morning and evening and served Him to the utmost of their sincere hearts were able, by the grace of the God, to preserve the existence of their tribe ever so slightly, and while trembling with fear, engaged in the cultivation of the four seasons.

The Lord Ōkami surveyed this tragic scene from afar, and in order to control fierce animals such as dragons, serpents, and eagles, brought forth and caused to be born upon this island oxen, horses, hawks, tigers, wolves, lions, and others, thereby chastising the tyranny of the wicked dragons, giant serpents, and eagles. However, as the years passed, fierce beasts such as tigers, wolves, lions, and bears finally sought to destroy the dragon-serpent clan and even attempted to exterminate the eagle clan. But since eagles possess large wings, the land animals found it difficult to do anything against them, and there was no other method than to use hawks to prevent harm from the empty air. Nevertheless, no matter how valiant a hawk might be, against an eagle possessing wings more than ten times larger and great strength, there was simply no way to keep it in check.

Since tigers, wolves, lions, bears, and eagles were carnivorous animals, they eventually sought to make prey of animals such as cats, oxen, horses, sheep, and monkeys, continuing the tragic drama of struggle day and night without a moment's intermission, to the point where things could no longer be patched up. Therefore, the Lord Ōkami descended great herds of boars and dogs here, causing them to confront and control these fierce beasts. Consequently, the clans of oxen and horses gained a brief respite; the oxen avoided danger at Uzugomine, and the horses avoided danger at Shirumagadake, until the surroundings of both mountains came to be filled with horses and oxen. If one viewed this from afar, Shirumagadake, where the white horses gathered, looked as though it were enveloped in snow, and Uzugomine, where the black oxen gathered, looked precisely like a mountain of black ink.

天津高宮に鎮まりいます主の大神は、七十五声の言霊を間断なく鳴り出で給ひて、泥海の世界を固むべく、先づ初めに当りて、筑紫ケ岳、高地秀の峰、高照山の三大高山を生み給ひて後万里の海に無数の島々をなり出で給ひて、総ての生物を生ませ養ひ給ふべく経綸されたり。

其の最初に当りて万里の海の中心なる万里の島を生り出で給ひぬ。此島は面積約八千方里にして、西に白馬ケ岳あり、東に牛頭ケ峰あり、其の中心を流るる清川を万里の河と言ふ。

大神は先づ此島にツの言霊を鳴り出でて鼠を生みたまひ、クの言霊を鳴り出でたまひて蛙を生み出でたまひける。鼠と雖も未だ地稚く、国土調はざりし時代なれば、今日の牛よりも大きく、蛙と雖も現代の人間よりは体躯長大なりしなり。其他神人をはじめ、総ての動物は之に準じて知るべきなり。蛙は此島に幾百千万とも限りなく発生し、鼠又日に日に其数を増しつつ土地最も肥えたる此島の全部に棲みて、総ての穀物の耕作に従事し生活を続け居たり。鼠は田を鋤き、蛙は鋤鍬をもちて田畑を拓き、穀物を作りて生活を楽しみ居たりける。

此の島の司として主の大神は、頭に太陽の形を印したる赤き紋を頂ける丹頂の鶴を一番下し給ひける。此の鶴は天津高宮より御空を翔りて万里の島ケ根に飛び来り、万里河の傍なる小高き丘の上に、鬱蒼として聳え立ちたる一本の常磐の松に巣籠り、数多の子を生み育て此島の主として臨む事とはなりぬ。

さりながら、朝夕の区別なく此の島ケ根は雲霧塞がり、殆ど咫尺を弁ぜざるに至り、天に日月輝きわたると雖も、中空の雲と地上に燃え立つ深霧の為めに陽気寒く、万物の発育も亦充分ならざりにける。陰鬱の気は次第々々に凝結して、種々の曲津見発生し、白馬ケ岳の谷間には太刀膚の悪竜数多棲み、大蛇又彼方此方の谷間に潜みて非時毒煙を吐きければ、其の毒煙は雲となり霧となりて、万里ケ島に住める蛙の一族は生活を脅かされ遂には其の数を減ずるに至りたり。邪気は次第に凝りて、数限りなき鷲となり山猫となりて狂ひ廻り、鷲は蛙を餌食とし、山猫は鼠を餌食となして猛り狂ふ惨状は、目も当てられぬばかりなりける。然れども、蛙と鼠の一族の中にて、朝夕を主の大神に祈り真心の限りを尽して仕へまつれる種族のみは、神の恵によりて、僅に其の種族の存在を保ち、戦々兢々としながらも春夏秋冬の耕作に従事し居たりける。

主の大神は此の惨状を遥かに覧はして、竜蛇、鷲等の獰猛なる動物を制御すべく、牛、馬、鷹、虎、狼、獅子等を此島に産み生はせたまひ、悪竜、大蛇、鷲の暴虐を懲らしめたまひけるが、年を経るに従ひて、虎、狼、獅子、熊などの猛獣は遂に竜蛇の種族を亡ぼし、鷲の種族をも殲滅せむとしたりけるが、鷲は大なる翼の持主なれば、地上の動物の如何ともなし難く、鷹をもつて空中よりの害を防ぐより方法なかりける。然りながら如何に勇猛なる鷹と雖も、十数倍の翼を保ち大力を有する鷲に対しては如何とも制裁の道なかりける。

虎、狼、獅子、熊、鷲等は肉食動物なれば、遂には猫、牛、馬、羊、猿等の動物を餌食となさむとし、昼夜間断なく争闘の惨劇を続け、収拾すべからざるに至りたれば、主の大神は茲に大なる猪の群と犬の群とを下して、これ等の猛獣を制すべく当らせ給ひたるにぞ、牛、馬の族は稍小康を得て牛は牛頭ケ峰に難をさけ、馬は白馬ケ岳に難を避けて両山の周囲は馬と牛とにて充満するに至りける。遠方より之を眺むれば、白馬の集ひたる白馬ケ岳は雪に包まれたるが如く、黒き牛の集まれる牛頭ケ峰は恰も墨の山の如く見えにける。

§ 2

Hereupon the Lord Ōkami, desiring somehow to establish this beautiful island of Banri as an eternal paradise, commanded the deity Takarahime, who possessed the mightiest shinriki among the yasohashira mihishirogami, accompanied by ten pillar deities—Wakaharuhiko of the kototama of u, Mochimunehiko born from the kototama of e, Nahomichihiko born from the kototama of i, Yamatohime born from the kototama of ya, Chikahihime born from the kototama of yo, Yumusubihime born from the kototama of yu, Masamichihiko born from the kototama of ma, Wamorihiko born from the kototama of wa, Kumokahahiko born from the kototama of pu, and Tamayamahiko born from the kototama of wo—to descend and protect the island of Banri.

Through the gokaurin of these kamigami, the tigers, wolves, lions, bears, and eagles that had churned and utterly disrupted the tenchi of the island of Banri fled away, escaping narrowly from island to island with their lives in their hands, so that for a shibashi a shōkau was attained; the frogs and mice engaged in cultivation as before, and came to enjoy their livelihood. The dogs guarded the dwellings of the frogs, the boars protected the safety of the mice, and at dusk they entered the mountains to eat tree leaves, each enjoying their respective tasks.

Hereupon the red-crowned cranes at last maintained a peaceful life in the evergreen trees, and employed monkeys to protect the peace of Banridegashima. Nevertheless, not knowing the grace of Takarahime no Kami and the other ten follower deities sent down from heaven by the Lord Ōkami, they instead regarded them as intruders who had come to plunder our land, and day and night did not take their eyes off them with jealous hatred.

Birds are the spirit of su and monkeys are the spirit of zu. The monkeys, acting as the subordinates of the cranes, constantly increased their power, and a spirit of arrogance arose until they eventually lived in the trees like the cranes, and came to strive to defile even their seats. Moreover, toward the frogs their tyranny knew no bounds and they oppressed them arbitrarily, so that the island of Banri, which had gradually attained a shōkau, suddenly turned into an alley of turbulence; the voices of tens of thousands of frogs crying out echoed through the tenchi, and their miserable condition had nothing to be compared with.

Hereupon the dogs and boars, who were charged with the protection of the frogs, came flocking from the mountains and fields, became allies of the frogs and others, and challenged the groups of monkeys to battle, until at last those groups of large monkeys came to be almost bitten to death. The evil birds and ferocious beasts, such as eagles, lions, and bears, who had spied out and learned of this miserable condition, deemed this an opportunity not to be lost, and approaching all at once from the sky, the earth, and the sea, caused all the animals on this island to be annihilated. Nevertheless, revering the divine words of the Lord Ōkami, Takarahime no Kami and the other kamigami protected the clan of cranes and the clan of horses, cattle, and sheep, who were determined as the masters of the island of Banri, so that these animals fortunately escaped the cruel hands of the evil birds and ferocious beasts, how awe-inspiring it is.

Revering the divine words of the Lord God, Takarahime no Kami descended from heaven to the island of Banri, Accompanied by ten pillar deities, Takarahime Kami descended into heaven turning into light, Clearing away the clouds and mist in all directions, evil birds And ferocious beasts also perished and vanished, Takarahime who appeared as the deity Haniyasuno Mihishirogami is truly the savior, Since the mihishiro of Takarahime no Kami descended from heaven, The roots of the island of Banri have been revived, Takarahime descended to this island of Banri, And morning and evening chanted the divine words, The ten pillar deities also following behind, Practiced misogi and chanted the divine words, Leaving horses and cattle, dogs and boars in the world, They caused them to protect this living island, The red-crowned cranes upon the pine branches of tokiwa, Celebrating a thousand ages, the age of gods appeared, Eagles and bears, tigers and wolves, and the lion king, Drew not near to this living island.

此の神々の御降臨によりて、万里の島の天地を掻き廻し、乱し尽したる虎、狼、獅子、熊、鷲の輩は、命辛々島より島に渡りて逃げ失せたれば、暫時の間は小康を得て、蛙と鼠は元の如く耕作に従事し、其の生活を楽しむに至りぬ。犬は蛙の家居を守り、猪は鼠の安全を守り、夕ざれば山に入りて木の葉を喰ひ、各も各もの業を楽しみ居たりける。

茲に弥々丹頂の鶴は常磐樹に平安の生を保ち、猿をつかひて万里ケ島の平安を守り居たりける。さりながら主の大神より天降し給へる田族比女の神其の他十柱の従神等の恩徳を知らず、却つて我国土を掠奪すべく来りしものとなし、昼夜嫉視の眼を放たざりける。

鳥はスの霊にして猿はズの霊なり。猿は鶴の幕下として常に其勢力を増大し、驕慢の気起り遂には鶴の如く樹の上に棲み、其の座席迄も汚さむと勤むるにいたりたり。又蛙に対して暴虐限りなく擅に虐げければ、やうやく小康を得たる万里の島は忽擾乱の巷と化し、数万の蛙の鳴き叫ぶ声は天地に響き、其の惨状譬ふるに物なかりける。
