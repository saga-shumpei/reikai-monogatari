Chapter 3: 狭 Field の食国 [1935]

狭野の食国〔1935〕

§ 1

The seven pillars of the Amatsukami and Kunitsukami leaned against the Ihakusubune, passing the time with tales of marvels from every direction, and as the dawn approached, the chirping voices of the numerous Momochidori dwelling in this ocean of mist gradually echoed across the expanse, while the occasional cry of a great eagle caused the ears of the Kamigami to prick up.

At this, Asakahime-no-Kami chanted a miuta:

Floating upon the waves of the sea of mist, I stand / The sky of the Shinonome is growing faintly bright / The voices of the Momochidori singing grow clear, and the waves / Of the sea of mist have at last turned to dawn / From today, when Ameharehime-no-Kami is manifested / The light of the Amatsuhiko will shine ever more brightly / Growing faintly near to the dawn / Suddenly my soul has begun to shine.

Ubugamihiko-no-Kami chanted a miuta:

While the sky of the Shinonome grows faintly bright, the mist of the sea of mist / The mist has gradually grown thin in succession / By the light of Asakahime-no-Kami, the mist of the sea of mist / The mist grows thin and vanishes into the misora / The eastern sky has grown faintly bright / The Amatsuhiko-no-Kami that will soon ascend / Floating upon the dawn boat, my soul / Reviving, has grown clear and bright / The islands of the sea of mist where the Magakami eternally lurk / Shall we not make them submit to words? / In the august presence of the pure Asakahime / Serving, I shall achieve exploits / I, Ubugamihiko, manifested in the Ikukototama of the sound of A / Was the power itself / Manifesting the boundless power of iki / I shall smash and shatter the stronghold of Maga.

Okitatsuhiko-no-Kami chanted a miuta:

The eastern sky has grown bright, come now, therefore / I, Okitatsu-no-Kami, shall proclaim the kamigoto / The heavenly realm is a land born in the Ikukototama / Morning and evening I shall proclaim the kamigoto / Even for a single day, the day one forgets the water and fire of the kamigoto / Is one when the Magatsumi comes attacking / Unceasingly proclaiming the Ikukototama continually / I shall advance toward the stronghold of the ugly ones / The ocean where mist rises densely / I, Okitatsu-no-Kami, will brightly advance / Parting the cloud-mist of the eastern sky, the Amatsuhiko / Has ascended, illuminating the limits of the great earth.

Tenchuhiko-no-Kami chanted a miuta:

Rejoicing in the kototama of the sound of Sa of the Lord God / I was born as Tenchuhiko / In the kototama of the kamigoto of Asakahime-no-Kami / There can be none, I think, who do not obey / To supplement the kototama of the kamigoto of Asakahime-no-Kami / I descended from heaven / The Amatsuhiko is illuminated by the Ikukototama / It illuminates as far as heaven and earth exist / Even the young land where cloud-mist strays unceasingly / From today shall be formed by the light of the Amatsuhiko.

Ameharehime-no-Kami chanted a miuta:

Gradually the kototama of the various Kamigami / Rejoicing in iki, it has cleared up / This sea, wrapped tenfold and twentyfold / The mist having vanished, it has cleared up / O Great Deity of the Sun who makes my iki rejoice and helps me, shining vastly across the great sky / If it be thus, no matter what kind of Maga lurks / I shall banish it with a breath into the Ikukototama / The cloud-mist that wrapped every direction having cleared away / As far as one can see, it is a field of light / The islands floating in the endless ocean of mist / Every single one has come into view in my eyes / Further ahead, upon the island of demons where ants are said to dwell / I think to row my boat close and land.

Tachisehime-no-Kami chanted a miuta:

I, Tachisehime, guarding Asakahime-no-Kami / Shall release iki into the Ikukototama / My boat, gradually and gradually, toward the island of demons / Has pointed and advanced, perhaps because the shore is far / Though there are neither oars nor rudders, that it proceeds / By the living kototama is indeed a wonder / A northwest wind blowing up, my boat / Proceeds pointing toward the island of demons / A swarm of ants, greater in shape than horses / Gathered upon the floating island of demons, I hear.

Sanozuhiko chants:

The island of demons where great ants are said to dwell / Is an island, I hear, not easily approached / Yet since the deities of power are present / Today shall we peacefully land upon the island?

Even while the Kamigami thus chanted their miutata without a moment's pause, crossing waves of several tens of ri, their august boat arrived close to the island of demons.

Here Asakahime-no-Kami, gazing at the island of demons close at hand, stopped the boat and rested for a while, and while observing the state of the island, chanted a miuta:

Blackly swarming across the entire island / The moving shadows, could they be ants? / The swarm of ants greater than the horse upon which I ride / Truly must be the incarnations of Magatsumi / If we land upon this island, suddenly tens of thousands of / Ants will attack, filled with astonishment.

原文（近代文語）

天津神国津神七柱は、磐楠舟に身を寄せ四方八方の珍しき話に時をうつし給ひつつ、東雲近くなりければ、この霧の海原に数多棲める百千鳥の囀る声漸くひびき渡り、時々大いなる鷲の声は、神々の耳を欹たしめたりける。

ここに朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『霧の海の波にうかびて吾立てる 東雲の空はほの明りせり 百千鳥啼く声さえて霧の海の 波は漸くしののめにけり 天晴比女神の現れます今日よりは 天津日の光清く照りまさむ ほのぼのと明け方近くなり行きて 俄に吾魂かがやき初めたり』

初頭比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『東雲の空ほの明りつつ霧の海の 霧はつぎつぎうすらぎにけり 朝香比女神の光に霧の海の 霧は御空にうすらぎ消ゆるも 東の空ほのぼのと明らみぬ やがて昇らむ天津日の神は 明け方の舟に浮びて吾魂は よみがへりつつ澄みきらひたり 曲津神の永久にひそめる霧の海の 島ことごとく言向けやはさむ いさぎよき朝香の比女の御尾前に 仕へてわれは功を立てなむ アの声の生言霊に現はれし 初頭比古吾は力なりけり 限りなき水火の力を現はして 曲津の砦を砕き破らむ』

起立比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『東の空明らみぬいざさらば 起立の神神言宣らむ 天界は生言霊に生りし国土よ 朝な夕なを神言宣らむ 一日だも神言の水火忘れたる 日は曲津見の襲ひ来るも 非時に生言霊を宣りつづけ 醜の砦に向ひて進まむ もうもうと霧立ちのぼる海原を 明し進まむ起立の神吾は 東の雲霧わけて天津日は 大地の限り照らして昇れり』

天中比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『主の神のサ声の言霊幸ひて 天中比古と吾は生れし 朝香比女神の神言の言霊に 服従はぬものはあらじと思ふ 朝香比女神の神言の言霊を 補ひ奉ると吾は天降りつ 天津日は生言霊に照らされて 天地のあらむ限りを照らせり 非時に雲霧迷ふ稚国土も 今日より成らむ天津日の光に』

天晴比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『やうやくに諸神等の言霊の 水火幸ひて天晴れにけり この海を十重に二十重に包みたる 霧うせにつつ天晴れにけり わが水火は幸ひ助けて大空に 天照り渡らす日の大御神よ 斯くならば如何なる曲津の潜むとも 伊吹きに払はむ生言霊に 四方八方を包みし雲霧晴れにつつ 見渡す限り光の野辺なり 果てしなき霧の海原に浮びたる 島ことごとく目にうつりけり いや先きに蟻の棲むとふ魔の島に 舟漕ぎよせて上らむとぞ思ふ』

立世比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『朝香比女神を守りて立世比女吾は 生言霊に水火を放たむ 吾舟は次第々々に魔の島を 指して進めり岸とほみかも 艪も楫もなけれど生ける言霊に 進み行くこそ不思議なるかも 西北の風吹き起りわが舟は 魔の島さして進み行くなり 駒よりも形大いなる蟻の群 魔の浮き島に集へりと聞く』

狭野彦は歌ふ。

『大いなる蟻の棲むてふ魔の島は 容易に近づき得ざる島と聞く さり乍ら力の神々ましませば 今日は安らに島に上らむか』

斯く神々は御歌詠ませ給ふ間もあらず、数十里の波を渡りて御舟は魔の島近く着きにける。

ここに朝香比女の神は魔の島を間近に眺めながら、舟を止めて暫しを休らひ、島の様子を窺ひ給ひつつ御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『黒々と島一面に群がりて 動ける影は蟻にやあるらむ 吾乗れる駒より大なる蟻の群 正しく曲津見の化身なるらむ この島に上れば忽ち数十万の 蟻は襲はむ驚きにつつ』

§ 2

Asakahime no Kami, from upon the boat, with fresh and pure Miiki,

Asakahime no Kami: 'The Magatsugami, turning into ants, gather and crowd close together / Sink this demonic island down into the sea / O ants, O ants, hide your forms and vanish away / I shall presently sink this island'

When She thus chanted, the Magatsumi were startled and in an uproar, rushing ahead and scrambling behind to tear around the island mountain, making a sound as if a million-strong army were attacking all at once, and their flustered state was vividly reflected in the eyes.

Now this demonic island was one that lurked beneath the earth of the Yasomagatsumi, with only its head floating upon the water, and the numerous ants were all lice swarming upon the head of the Magatsumi.

Though Asakahime no Kami proclaimed the Kototama, 'Sink, O island,' for the time it had no effect whatsoever, the Magatsugami grew ever more frantic, the island gradually floated high up, the colossal body of the Magatsumi floated upon the water, its face with eyes, nose, and mouth irregularly attached rose high pressing against the clouds, everything from the knees down of its legs was in the midst of the waves, and its magnitude was beyond description. The laughing voice from its mouth with irregular teeth alignment sounded as if a hundred thunderclaps were roaring all at once.

That voice:

Magatsugami: 'Gaa--hahahaha, Gia--hahahaha, Giyuu--fffff, Gee--hehehehe, Giyoo--hohohoho, pompous though Asakahime no Kami may be, she is no true Kami, but a false Kami deceiving Kamikuni. Wait, presently this god of the Yasomagatsumi shall manifest divine power, and trample you all down into the mud with my feet, leaving not a single pillar. Oh, how comforting it is. Gia--hahahaha, Giyuu--fffff, Giyoo--hohohoho, how utterly pitiful you are!'

So saying, the saliva sprayed from its gigantic mouth in all directions like a waterfall, scattering in wild disorder in every direction, a spectacle indescribable by any means. Should even a single drop of saliva issuing from this mouth touch one's body, it would cause the entire body to become fixed and neither hands nor feet would be able to move, this being the utmost limit of the secret arts of the Magatsugami's sorcery. Asakahime no Kami, without the slightest fear,

Asakahime no Kami: 'Fierce and boastful is the god of the Magatsumi / I shall destroy you with my living soul / The unsightly Miiki that you blow forth becomes clouds / And turning into mists, has clouded this world

Ichini-san-shi, go-roku-shichi-hachi-ku-jū / O god of the Magatsumi / Become a boulder, become a boulder just as you are / Let your hands and feet move no more, let your mouth speak no more / The body of the Magatsugami, become a standing boulder / Let your mouth and nose become a cavern / Let your two eyeballs become ponds / Ants and lice, one and all / Become soil, become soil, and from that soil / Let trees and grasses sprout, let flowers bloom / And let the trees of Kagu bear eternal fruit'

When She proclaimed the Kototama, the gigantic body of the Yasomagatsumi solidified right there in the midst of the sea, and was solidified into a gigantic boulder island.

Hatsuhikohiko no Kami, out of excessive astonishment, chanted a sacred poem.

Hatsuhikohiko no Kami: 'Through the efficacy of the living Kototama of Hikohime no Kami / The Magatsugami has finally become a boulder / What appeared as ants upon the island were lice / Growing upon the head of the Magatsumi / Shut in by the living Kototama of Hikohime no Kami / The Magatsugami has finally become a boulder / From this day forth, upon this boulder island, I think to have the Kunitsukami / Dwell eternally and pioneer it / That Kamikuni is a land formed / From the divine water and fire of the living Kototama, I now realize / Because Hikohime no Kami is the Mihishiro no Kami / She has accomplished any and all divine works / That I proclaimed I would assist the divine work of Hikohime no Kami / Ah, how embarrassing is my living Kototama / From this day forth, with awe and reverence, I think to serve / While reverently upholding the divine work of Hikohime no Kami / I, who was born from the Kototama of the A-sound of the Lord God / Am truly astonished at the achievement of Hiko no Kami'

Kitachihikohiko no Kami chanted a sacred poem.

Kitachihikohiko no Kami: 'Awe-inspiring even to speak of is Asakahime no Kami / I am truly astonished at Her achievement today / Clearing away the clouds and mists in all directions, She has made the Magatsugami / Of the demonic island into an eternal boulder / Solidifying marshes and solidifying islands, Hikohime no Kami / Has brought forth a new land / The demonic island, swelling by degrees / Before our very eyes, trees and grasses sprouted up / As an eternal dwelling place for the Kunitsukami, Hikohime no Kami / Has solidified the island of the Magatsugami / From this day forth, as a land that nurtures / The food of the Kunitsukami, it shall surely prosper / How shameful at the loathsome voice of the Magatsugami / I had been trembling for a time / Though I am Kitachi no Kami, I trembled so violently / That even the living Kototama would not issue forth / Since She is a Kami chosen as the Mihishiro no Kami by the Lord God / It is only natural that it should be thus'

原文（近代文語）

朝香比女の神は舟の上より、御水火さわやかに、

『曲津神の蟻と変りて寄り集ふ この魔の島よ海に沈まへ 蟻よ蟻よ姿をひそめて消え失せよ 吾この島を今に沈めむ』

斯く歌ひ給へば、曲津見は驚き騒ぎ、前後左右に先を争ひ島山を駈け廻る様、百万の大軍一度に襲ひ来りし如く響きを立て、狼狽のさまありありと目に映りける。

さてこの魔の島は八十曲津見の地中に潜み、ただ頭のみを水上に浮かせゐたるものにして、数多の蟻はいづれも曲津見の頭にわける虱なりける。

朝香比女の神は『島よ沈め』と宣らせし言霊も、一時何の功もなく、曲津神はますます狂ひ立ち、島は次第に高く浮き上りて曲津見の巨体は水上に浮び、目鼻口の不規律に附着せる顔は雲を圧して高く、足の膝頭より中は海中にあり、其の大いさ形容すべからず。カラカラと不規律なる歯並の口より笑ふ声は百雷の一時に轟くかと思はれにける。その声、

『ガアーーハハハハ　ギアーーハハハハ　ギユーーフフフフ　ゲエーーヘヘヘヘ　ギヨーーホホホホ　ものものしや朝香比女の神とは真の神にあらず、天界を偽る贋神ならむ。待て、今に此八十曲津見の神が神力を現はし、一柱も残らず吾泥足に踏み躙りくれむ。てもさても心地よやな。ギアーーハハハハ、ギユーーフフフフ、ギヨーーホホホホ、さてもさてもいぢらしいものだわい』

と言ひつつ巨大なる口より四方八方に吹き散らす唾は滝の如、四方八方に散り乱るるさま、何ものを以ても言ひ現はし得ざる光景なりき。万一この口より出づる唾の一滴だも身に触るる時は、全身固着して、手も足も動く能はざるに至る、曲津神の魔術の奥の手をつくしたるものなりける。朝香比女の神は少しも恐れず、

『曲津見の神の雄猛びものものし わが生霊に滅ぼしくれむ 汝が吹く醜のみ水火は雲となり 霧となりつつこの世を濁せり

一二三四、五六七八九十 曲津見の神よ 巌となれなれこのまま巌と 手足も動くな口も利くな 曲津神の体は立巌となれ その口鼻は洞穴となれ 二つの目玉は池となれ 蟻も虱もことごとく 土となれなれその土ゆ 草木は萌えよ花は咲け 香具の木の実は非時結べ』

と言霊宣らせ給へば、八十曲津見の巨体は其儘海中に固まり、巨大なる巌島と固められける。

初頭比古の神は驚きの余り、御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『比女神の生言霊の功績に 曲津神は遂に巌となりける 島の上に蟻と見えしは曲津見の 頭に生ふる虱なりける 比女神の生言霊に閉ぢられて 曲津神は遂に巌となりける 今日よりはこれの巌島に国津神 永久に住ませて拓かせむと思ふ 天界は生言霊の御水火より 成りし国土とは今悟りけり 比女神は御樋代神にましませば 如何なる神業も果し給へり 比女神の神業助くと吾宣りし 生言霊の恥づかしきかな 今日よりは畏れ慎み比女神の 神業謹み仕へむと思ふ 主の神のア声の言霊に生れし吾も 比女の功に驚きしはや』

起立比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『掛巻も畏し朝香比女神の 今日の功におどろきにけり 雲霧を四方に払ひて魔の島の 曲津神を永久に巌となせり 沼をかため島をかためて比女神は 新しき国土を生ませたまひぬ 魔の島は次第々々にふくれつつ 見る見る草木は生ひ立ちにけり 国津神の永久の住処と比女神は 曲津神の島を固め給ひぬ 今日よりは国津神等の食物を 育つる国土と栄えますらむ いやらしき曲津神の声に恥づかしも われは一時ふるひ居たりし 起立の吾神ながら震ひ立ちて 生言霊も出でざりしはや 主の神ゆ御樋代神と選まれし 神にしませばかくもありなむ』

§ 3

Tatsuyohimegami chants a miuta.

Thus near the exalted Kami I think upon the joy of serving Him; O Himegami, Tatsuyohimegami, approve my magokoro And let me serve Thee for tokoshihe. Now I am the megami who joyfully awaited To serve Asakahime, is it not so?

Amenakahikogami chants a miuta.

Unto this island formed hard by the ikukototama of Himegami Shall we not draw our boat nigh? Those that seemed like great ants, were they lice Parted upon the head of Magakami? If it be thus, once going up onto the island, Would that I might sow the seeds of ikukototama! O Asakahimegami, pray grant me leave, I think to sow the seeds of kusaki.

Asakahimegami replies with a miuta.

Unto the mikoto of Amenakahikogami, entrusting all Of this island, I shall cause the kuni to be opened.

This island was a considerably large island, exceeding one hundred ri in circumference. Here Amenakahikogami received in transfer the kunitsukami Sanuhiko, and while bringing forth various kusaki and gokoku through the ikukototama, at last he brought forth the osukuni of Sanu, and remained enshrined there in tokoshihe.

立世比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『かくの如尊き神の側近く 仕ふる吾の幸を思へり 比女神よ立世比女神の真心を 嘉して永久に仕はせ給へ 吾はいま朝香の比女に仕へむと 楽しみ待ちし女神なるはや』

天中比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『比女神の生言霊に固まりし この島ケ根に舟寄せむかな 大いなる蟻と見えしは曲津神の 頭にわける虱なりしか 斯くならば一度島に上り行きて 生言霊の種を蒔かばや 朝香比女の神よ許させ給へかし 草木の種を吾蒔かむと思ふ』

朝香比女の神は御歌もて答へ給ふ。

『天中比古神の神言にこの島の 総てをまかせて国土拓かせむ』

この島は周囲百里に余る相当に広き島なりける。ここに天中比古の神は国津神狭野彦を譲り受け、諸々の草木五穀を生言霊に生み出でましつつ、遂に狭野の食国を生み出で給ひ、永久に鎮まり給ひける。
