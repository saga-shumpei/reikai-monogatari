Chapter 13: 五男三女 Kami [1945]

五男三女神〔1945〕

§ 1

While the righteous deities of Tenkai, who served in the creation of the Uchiu, the Tenchikaibiyaku, and the grand divine work (Ōmiwaza), breathe in the most refined, pure, fresh water and fire (iki), maintaining their life unto Eienmukiu, and manifesting infinite Rikitoku, the evil deities born from turbidity, defilement, and cloudiness constantly breathe in turbid air to maintain their lives, committing all manner of hideous acts, and possessing the very nature of taking delight night and day; thus, wherever evil deities exist, malevolent qi inevitably fills the space, dark clouds (kokūn) billow forth, inflicting grave harm upon the growth of Shinrabanshiyō, and striving with all their might to resist and obstruct the Lord God's divine work of consolidating and repairing the realm (Osameru Katameru). Yet, because the evil deities themselves perceive their own actions as the most righteous and proper, they are never inclined to mend their evil deeds or follow the virtuous path of generation and fostering (Seisei Kaiiku).

Here, even the myriad-league island chain (Banri no Shimagene), born from the clear, stainless, and most refined and pure water and fire of Kototama of the Lord Great Deity, eventually generated evil qi, transforming into terrifying sword-scale dragons or malevolent giant serpents that untimely raised clouds and mist, spewed malevolent qi, rendered heaven and earth turbid, blocked the light of the sun, moon, and stars, hindered the growth of all things, threatened and shortened the lives of deities, men, birds, and beasts, deeming their task accomplished and letting slip smiles of contentment—truly detestable, obstinate entities that could never be completely saved through the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases. Therefore, at the very beginning of the Shibi Tenkai when the earth was yet soft and the land unsettled, since it was no easy task to pacify and harmonize through the weapon of the living Kototama, the deities endured unspeakable hardships for the sake of the world countless eras hence, dedicating all their capabilities and exerting themselves to fundamentally eradicate the spirits of local wicked deities and malevolent qi.

Indeed, that we are able to peacefully maintain our lives in this clear and bright heaven and earth, behold the order of the four seasons, observe the charming scenery of various mountains and waters upon this earth, and worship the sun and moon of life—all these are the bounty of the self-sacrificing activities of the primeval deities; their profound grace is deeper than the ocean and higher than the mountain of Sumeru, and we should know that it is ultimately impossible to fully express and praise it with our human speech. When we perceive, even in the slightest, the divine work of the creation of the universe and the bitter toils of the deities of Tenchikaibiyaku, we should choke with tears and weep at the greatness of this supreme grace, and no matter what adverse position we may find ourselves in today, uttering even a single word of grievance or committing the lawless crime of belittling the divine decree should not even happen in our dreams.

Considering that our earth—the culmination of the Shibi Tenkai, which is the direct lineage of the Lord Great Deity and was consolidated and repaired in the primeval age with the total expenditure of utmost effort, and particularly the Imperial Land of the Central Reed Plains (Toyoashihara no Nakatsukunigami), unparalleled in dignity—has its imperial lineage flowing directly from the Lord Great Deity, bestowing infinite blessings upon Shinrabanshiyō unto Eienmukiu, we must know that we possess the heavenly vocation and mandate to unremittingly devote and offer our sincere loyalty to the deities, reverence to the sovereign, and patriotism day and night, ever more fully manifesting the inherent spirits bestowed by the gods such as loyalty, filial piety, benevolence, righteousness, and fraternal love.

Here, Tanazukihime no Kami, who descended from heaven as the Mihashiro-no-Kami of the myriad-league island, striving by every means and exerting every possible divine power and strategy to pioneer this Shimagene into a heavenly pure land of purity and stainlessness, supreme joy and delight, supreme goodness and beauty, and supreme clarity and holiness, exactly as willed by the Lord Great Deity; yet because the evil qi of the warped deities remained congealed and hardened in the innermost recesses of Mandani Chidani on the southern side of Mt. Hakubakedake, from time to time raising clouds and mist that enshrouded the heaven and earth of Banri-no-Shima to the point where one could not distinguish an inch ahead, thus obstructing the growth of animals and plants, the Mihashiro-no-Kami had no choice but to manifest a great fierce and courageous heart, resolving to thoroughly sweep away the demons of Mt. Hakubakedake; accompanied by ten attendant deities, trampling across a thousand leagues of wilderness upon the backs of their steeds, they managed to arrive at the sacred sanctuary of Izumi no Mori—a spring forest where giant camphor trees grew lush and thick, out of reach of even the eye's gaze—where they rested peacefully for the night under the moon, and awaiting the break of day, finally set forth to sweep away the evil spirits of Masumegadani, fixing their battle stations, with the Mihashiro-no-Kami herself establishing Izumi no Mori as the main headquarters for tactical purposes, having Rumoruhiko no Kami and Wakaharuhiko no Kami guard her side, and commanding the five male deities and three female deities—Reizanbiko no Kami, Hosumuhiko no Kami, Naodokohiko no Kami, Masadokohiko no Kami, Kumokawabiko no Kami, Yamatonohime no Kami, Chigaihime no Kami, and Yuihime no Kami—to serve as the vanguard.

宇宙の創造、天地開闢と大神業に奉仕したまふ天界の正神は、至粋至純なる清鮮の水火を呼吸して、其の生命を永遠無窮に保持し、無限の力徳を発揮し給ふに反し、濁りと汚れと曇りより発生したる邪神は、常に混濁の空気を呼吸して其の生命を保持し、あらゆる醜悪なる行為をなして、日夜を楽しむの霊性を持つものなれば、邪神のある処必ず邪気充満し、黒雲漲りて森羅万象の発育に大害を与へ、主の神が修理固成の神業に極力反抗し妨害せむとするものなり。併しながら邪神自身としては、最も真正にして至当の所行と感じ居るが故に、極力其の悪業を改め、生成化育の善道に従はむとはせざるなり。

茲に主の大神の清澄無垢にして、至粋至純なる言霊の水火より生り出でたる万里の島ケ根も遂には邪気発生して、さも恐ろしき太刀膚の竜となり、或は猛悪なる大蛇と化して非時に雲霧を起し、邪気を吐き、天地を混濁せしめ、日月星辰の光を遮り、万物の発育を妨げ、神人禽獣等の生命を脅かし且つ短縮せしめて能事了れりとなし、会心の笑を漏らし居るこそ忌々しく、到底善言美詞の言霊をもつて完全に済度し得べからざる難物なり。故に未だ地稚く国土定まらざりし紫微天界の頭初に当りては、生言霊の武器をもつて言向け和す事容易ならざれば、神々は数億万年後の世界の為に、所在悪神邪気の霊を根本的に絶滅せしめむと千辛万苦に堪へ、其為に全能力を傾注し、活動し給ひたるなり。

実に吾人がこの清明なる天地に安らかに生を保ち得るも、四季の順序調へるこれの地上に諸々の山水の明媚なる風光を観賞し、生命の日月を拝し得るも、皆太初の神々等の舎身的御活動の賜にして、その厚恩は海よりも深く又スメールの山よりも高く、到底吾人の言語をもつて言ひ現はし称へ了る事は不可能と知るべし。吾人にして宇宙創造の神業と天地開闢の神々の御苦心を幾分にても察知し奉る時は、この鴻恩の大なるに嗚咽感泣し、現代に処して如何なる不遇の地位にあるとも、唯一言の怨み言をのべ又は神命を軽んずる無道の罪を犯す事、夢寐にもあらざるべきなり。

主の大神の直系にして且つ太初に特に全力を注ぎて修理固成したまへる紫微天界の終結たる我地球、特に豊葦原の中津神国尊厳無比にして、其皇統は主の大神より流れ出で、永遠無窮に森羅万象に対し無限の恩恵を賜ふ事を思へば、吾人は敬神尊皇報国の至誠を昼夜間断なく尽しまつり捧げまつりて、忠孝、仁義、友愛等の神授固有の精神を弥益々に発揮せざるべからざるの天職天命ある事を知るべし。

茲に万里の島の御樋代神として天降り給ひし田族比女の神は、如何にもして、これの島ケ根を主の大御神の依さしのままに清浄無垢、至喜至楽、至善至美、至清至潔なる天国浄土に拓かむとして、あらむ限りの神力と神策を発揮し給ひけれども、未だ白馬ケ岳の南側にあたる万谷千谷の奥処には曲神の邪気凝り固まりて、時々雲霧を起し、万里ケ島の天地を咫尺弁ぜざるまでに包みて、動植物の発育を妨害したりければ、御樋代神は止むを得ず、大勇猛心を発揮し、白馬ケ岳の悪魔を徹底的に掃蕩せむと思召し、十柱の従神を従へ、千里の荒野を駒の背に踏み破りつつ、辛うじて楠の大木の生ひ繁りたる目路も届かぬ限りの泉の森の聖所につかせ給ひ、月下に一夜を安息し、夜の明くるを待ちて弥々、魔棲ケ谷の悪霊を掃蕩すべく、部署を定めて出で立たせ給ひ、御身自らは泉の森を策戦上より本営と定め、輪守比古の神、若春比古の神を御側に守らせおき、霊山比古の神、保宗比古の神、直道比古の神、正道比古の神、雲川比古の神、山跡比女の神、千貝比女の神、湯結比女の神の五男神三女神をして先陣を勤めしめ給ひける。

§ 2

Painting the eastern sky in crimson, / the Amatsu-Hi no Kami has ascended. / The white dews resting on the leaves of trees, too, / have revived, releasing seven-colored light. / It feels as though the heavenly cave of Hisakata / is opening up in the rising morning sun. / Come now, Kamitachi, advance into the valley called Masekegani, where the ugly Magakami lurks. / Wamorihiko and Wakaharuhiko, the Futahashira, / stay here, hurrying alongside Me. / Because the wicked schemes of the Magakami are deep, / I shall remain in the forest of the spring to guard it. / The Itsuogami and Mihashiramegami shall swiftly advance, taking the water and fire of the Lord Kami as their mitate. / The Magakami are gathering a myriad host of forces, / fortifying themselves tenfold and twentyfold. / Even for a brief moment, without letting the Futo-norito of the living Kototama cease, advance calmly.

Tamayamahiko no Kami chants an answering miuta.

Chosen as Mihishirogami, how grateful I am, / standing today at the vanguard. / No matter how deep the schemes of the Magakami may be, / how could I ever fear them for the sake of the Kami? / With the morning spring where cloud and mist are cleared away without a trace, / going forth is my joy. / The morning breeze gently crosses over the treetops, / and this morning the water and fire are clearer than ever. / Purifying my body in the clear waters of the spring's forest, / there is no crookedness to oppose the rising Kamitamahiko. / Like the spring snow melting under the Amatsu-Hi, / the stronghold of the crooked must perish and disappear. / Come now, Mihishirogami, you Futahashira, / protect the military forces I lead.

Almost before he has finished speaking, Tamayamahiko no Kami spurs his steed alone, and gallops forth, heading straight toward the direction of Masekegani in the center of the southern wilderness.

Hosumuhiko no Kami, now about to set out on an expedition, chants a miuta.

Tamayamahiko no Kami has galloped out to the vanguard, / how could I lag behind on the marching route of Magatsu? / Following the divine words of Tazukuhime no Okami, / let us advance to Masekegani. / This day where the vast sky is cleared without a trace, / how my heart's steed eagerly exults. / Clearing away the water and fire of the dark clouds spewed day and night by the Magakami, / I shall purify the land. / When I think of driving away the flocks of countless evil deities / into the water and fire of the Kototama, how valiant I feel. / Blown away are the clouds and mists obstructing the myriad islands, / would that we might create the land of Iyaharu. / As my steed strikes the ground with its hooves, / does my steed too exult to trample upon the ugly deities? / From the very beginning when this island first opened, / how joyful is this battle to rebuke the crooked. / Setting up traps of a myriad schemes, / Magatsu probably waits on the path I walk. / Without letting anything stray from the divine will of the Lord Ōkami, / I shall proceed with caution.

Though they be Magakami, since I think of them fundamentally as the water and fire of the Lord Kami, / there is no reason to feel hatred. / If they submit to the righteous path, even the ugly deities / I shall save, according to the will of the Kami.

Chanting this miuta as he goes,

Tazukuhime no Okami, the two great Okami, come now, / protect my path ahead.

So saying as he leaves, he gives his horse's back a single whip and makes his way straight south through the center of Ōnogahara.

Michimichihiko no Kami, upon setting out, chants a miuta.

I speak to the Mihishirogami and Tazukuhime no Kami, / revering the divine words, I shall set forth on the marching route. / Looking up, from the ravines of Masekegani, / black clouds well up and spread once more. / Do the Magakami intend to plunge heaven and earth / into eternal darkness once again? How hateful. / The ugly clouds and mists that attempt to harm / the living beings that have barely revived. / Shining the light of soul-threads and solidifying the water and fire / of the Kototama, I shall drive away Magatsu. / The sunlight that illuminated heaven and earth without a trace / is now enveloped by black clouds. / In order to illuminate all existence forever / with the light of the Amatsu-Hi, I advance. / Splitting apart the righteous path of heaven and earth, / should Magatsu be allowed to obstruct my advance?

The Magakami have opened up crooked paths here and there / in an attempt to throw the age of the Kami into chaos. / Breaking through the crooked paths with a kick, / I shall advance upon the straight path of the single-minded living Kototama.

Tamayamahiko no Kami took his stand at the vanguard, / and already his noble figure has vanished from sight. / Not wishing to fall behind on the marching route of Magatsu myself, / I too shall spur my steed and advance in haste.

Chanting thus, he gave his steed a whip and galloped off headlong.

Masadomichihiko no Kami, upon setting out, chants a miuta.

The three Kami have already departed, / I too shall advance onto the marching route of Magatsu. / One after another the sky has grown cloudy, and the black clouds of Magatsu / have hidden the light of the Amatsu-Hi. / Even mingling with rain, the clouds have enveloped the vast sky, / wrapping around it time and time again. / Surging upon the original dwelling place of the Magakami, / I shall cut the roots of the welling clouds. / No matter how fiercely the rainstorms and black clouds rage, / I shall not fear, for I possess the weapon of the Kototama. / No matter how mighty the force of the Magakami may be, / it cannot surpass the light of the righteous path. / Enduring with patience, stepping through and parting the righteous path, / I shall advance until Magatsu perishes. / Come now, Mihishirogami, you Futahashira, / illuminate us in this sacred place.

Although Mount Shirouma is lofty, Although Magasugatani is deep, Though the foul Magatsu gods gather In countless numbers, with the pure water and fire Of the Kototama we shall drive them away, Slicing and casting them aside, blowing and purifying The clouds and mists of heaven and earth Across the myriad-ri islands, So that all Shinrabanshiyō May receive the dew of the grace of Tsukihi, Preserving life for a thousand years, eight thousand years, And unto eternity, flourishing ever more, That the five grains may ripen and fruits Grow well without the harm of insects, While the myriad flowers of the spring mountains and fields Compete in brilliance and bloom in fragrance, And the songs of myriad birds and the voices of insects Ring clear, refreshing, and serene, That the roots of the myriad-ri islands may become An eternal heavenly pure land, And according to the mighty will of Him who shall govern, O, this valiant setting forth today!
