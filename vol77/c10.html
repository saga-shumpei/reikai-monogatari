Chapter 10: 樹下の Rain 宿 [1942]

樹下の雨宿〔1942〕

§ 1

Yamatohime no Kami, straddling the back of her horse, chants a miuta.

Looking up, at the peak of Mount Shiroumadake / Purple clouds are lying horizontal / Gathering in the deep valley to the south / Are the rising dark clouds the water and fire of Maga? / All things living and breathing in this island / To destroy and shatter them, the water and fire of the ugly kami / Those dark clouds swarming about the borders of Masegadani / The time to blow them away is drawing near / I now, serving Mihishirogami / Advance with valor into the stronghold of Magami / The distant misora is blocked by dark clouds / And fog shrouds the land of Aragane / The clouds in the southern valley of Masegadani, seen through the curtain of fog, are wondrous indeed / On this day when Mihishirogami sets forth on a journey / The welling fog has also grown somewhat thin / Blow, O wind of Shinadobe, blow / Blowing away the fog obstructing the road where you go / The sounds of insects crying, lurking at the roots of the grass / Are all the more sorrowful in this fog-enveloped noji / The white dew resting on the leaves of bamboo grass, chillingly / Comes attacking even to the back of my mount / Setting forth from the high takamiya of distant Ama / Far, far away have I come to this place / Purifying this island lovingly and in detail / I will open it up as Ikukunihara / Had Mihishirogami not descended from Ama / The island-root of Banri would perish eternally / My journey to illuminate with living Kototama the island of Banri / Where the Magakami are rampaging fiercely / Following the divine words of Tanabatakenohimeno Kami / How joyous to ascend the punitive expedition path of Maga

Chikaihime no Kami chants a miuta.

Leading our mounts, ascending upon the punitive expedition path of Maga / May there be good fortune for this day of life / Looking up, the dark clouds that wrapped the sky / Have also grown somewhat thin with your departure / Pushing open the curtain of thinning clouds / The light of Amatsuhi is faintly visible / One by one, the clouds of the misora also scatter away / And the Kami of Amatsuhi illuminates us / To bring the Magatsumi under control, rebuke them, and cut them down / O Kami of the Sun, protect this day's departure / The dark clouds wrapping the island of Banri row upon row / Their wondrous water and fire afflict all things / We, born in the water and fire of Kototama / Have suffered when the water and fire grew turbid / Blow away the wondrous water and fire of the Magakami / By the power of the wind of Shinadobe / Upon the peak of Uzugamine soaring in the eastern sky / Sunlight shines faintly at the summit / Between Uzugamine and Shiroumadake we travel / May there be eternal good fortune upon our journey / The hills of Banri, the clear forests where old trees thrive / Recede into the distance and are shrouded in fog / The great rivers and small rivers of the island of Banri, all of them / Are made turbid by the water and fire of Magami / If there is a pure spring, I will perform misogi / And advance into Masegadani / As long as my life, like a cord of jewels, shall last, to my lord / I resolve to serve and live / Crossing mountains and seas one and all, following behind my lord / I have come to this place / Surely the chihayafuru Kami will also protect us / For the fortune of Kototama on the journey we undertake / Protected by the divine water and fire of the Kami who makes spirits prosper / We will bring the fierce Magatsumi to submission and appease them / The fierce dragon-kami with sword-skin edges, the ugly great serpent / It is Masegadani where they swarm and dwell, they say / Though I am one without power, relying on the power of the ten pillars / Of Kami, I go advancing forward

Yuihimenohime no Kami chants a miuta.

Obeying Mihishirogami, the ten pillars / Of Kototama-kami ascend upon the punitive expedition path / The Magakami who have ravaged and destroyed the island of Banri / Are lurking within Masegadani, they say / Purifying the water and fire of Kototama, to the Masegadani / Of Shiroumadake I am advancing / The clouds of the distant misora, one after another / Dispersing away, the sunlight has shone forth / The clouds and fog that deeply wrapped all eight directions / Also clearing away wider and wider still / The unpleasant cold wind has also subsided / And when my skin grows warm, water and fire are fulfilled / In the great sky, drawing a circle, a falcon / Is dancing while celebrating today's departure / True cranes, aligning their wings, upon the sky where I go / Are dancing and playing high in the heavens / The voices of all birds have grown courageous / Ever since the clouds of the misora were blown away / It feels as though both grass and trees have been revived / The dew on the tips of the leaves shines in the sun / Since this journey is one of living Kototama upon which you go / I think it is right that the misora clears up / Since this island is heard to be the island of Tanabata / The mountains of Shirouma must be filled with the seven treasures / What appeared to be snow on Shiroumayama was the shadow / Of white horses gathering close together / The white horses are swords of the fangs of fierce beasts / Surely they escaped through the grace of Kami / This island where herds of cows and horses dwell / Is the island of Tanabata, where crops will grow / The ugly dragon-kami and Maga-serpents of Masegadani / I will bring to submission and harmonize, making them a living land / Together with Mihishirogami and the ten pillars / Combining our powers, we will purify the land / Deep in the valley, black smoke rises upward / Masegadani must indeed be rugged and steep

§ 2

Masamichihiko no Kami chants a Miuta:

'How valiant, lining up our steeds upon the path of the Magatsumi expedition, Our journey today! Following the Mihishirogami, we shall advance the army of Kototama into the valley of Makuragaya. Heaven and Earth have manifested as Ikukototama; O land of Kototama, what is there to fear? Treading the righteous path of Tenchi, Nothing obstructs our Makoto. The Kumokiri wrapping the foot of Hakubayama Gradually cleared, and light filled the air. The light of Amatsuhi striking straight, Mount Hakubagaku Has now newly revived. To illuminate the path we shall go, Amatsuhi Shines high in the vast Ōzora. The light of Hiruzuki, pale and faint, Hisakata, Passes dimly through the Misora. Clouds and mist have cleared from mountain and field, without a shadow, and To the very limits of our Meji, all has revived.'

Kumokahahiko no Kami chants a Miuta:

'Mihishirogami has come forth, To protect the expedition against Magatsu? By the wind of Shinado and the Miiki, Black clouds blocking the sky Scatter away without a trace, Deep within the blue Misora, The light of the Kami Amatsuhi and Tsukiyomi Shines brilliantly and clear. Illuminating this island-base without a shadow, Shining upon the dew of a hundred grasses, The earth of Arakane revives everywhere, Instantly manifesting the scenery of Eternal Spring. The flowers of the hundred grasses, which had wilted until now, Competing in fragrance, White, red, yellow, and purple Flowers begin to bloom across the earth without a shadow, Kannagara. Ah, is this Heaven, is this Paradise? Upon this beautiful island-base, All obstructing Magatsu Shall be pacified, reasoned with, and cut away With the sword of Ikukototama, To remove the calamities of Tenchi; The coming forth of Mihishirogami Is praised by the Kami of Tenchi, Who blow away the clouds and mist of all directions, Illuminating the light of sun and moon upon the earth Without a single shadow. Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami! Rejoicing in the achievements of Ikukototama, Courageously shall we advance, our journey's road. Without mishap along the corners of the path, Free from the interference of the evil demons, Crossing without exception the eighty roaring waterfalls Gathered through a thousand valleys and eight hundred gorges, Swiftly advancing into Maguragaya, Where the dragon deities lurk eternally to work mischief, How joyful to press forward! Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami! Let life be in Ikukototama, Let blessings be upon our Kototama.'

Thus the party of eleven deities proceeds toward the lair of the dragon deities in Maguragaya, on the southern foot of Mount Hakubagaku. Encountering a rather wide forest dense with large camphor trees blocking their path, they rest for a while in this forest, each performing the sacred ritual of purification in a rare, pure spring welling up beneath the trees, spending the night lodging here, preparing for the morrow, reciting the Amatsu Norito, and nurturing boundless and absolute spiritual vigor—how awe-inspiring.

かくして一行十一柱の神々は、白馬ケ岳の南麓、魔棲ケ谷の竜神の巣窟指して進ませ給ふ。行手に当りて楠の大樹の茂れる稍広き森の横はれるを見給ひ、暫しこの森に息を休めて樹下に湧き出づる珍しき清泉に禊の神事を各自に修し給ひつつ一夜を此処に宿らせ、明日の準備と天津祝詞を奏上し、無限絶対的の英気を養はせ給ふぞ畏けれ。
