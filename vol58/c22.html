Chapter 22: 獣婚 [1497]

獣婚〔1497〕

§ 1

With Tamakuniwake in the lead, Bāchiru passed through the front gate of his beloved home for the first time in three years. The scenery all around, contrary to his absence, remained extremely vivid. The care of the garden was particularly thorough, and peonies, peonies, irises, dayflowers, and beyond them, cockscombs and others, bloomed in competition for beauty here and there in the wide garden, as if unaware of the master's absence. Sparrows and swallows began to chirp in particularly high voices, as if celebrating the master's return. With a look of immense emotion, Bāchiru followed Tamakuniwake from the front entrance and proceeded deep into the inner room.

Since he had returned after a long time, he thought his wife Sāberu would surely have come to welcome him with joy, even from four or five chō down the road. Wondering why she had not even come to the entrance to greet him, and fearing she might be suffering from some serious illness, he proceeded with the propagandist into his living room. There, Sāberu-hime was sitting cross-legged with dignity on the alcove, her hands neatly clasped, waiting with a gentle smile.

Seeing Bāchiru, she leaped down from the alcove and, raising a strange voice crying 'Kya-kya!', spoke.

Sāberu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, well, well, my dear husband, you have been very late, have you not? I came a step ahead, had the servants prepare things, and have been waiting for you. You, too, have had a difficult time on that remote island with me for three years. Now that you have returned here, everything will be convenient, and I pray that you will bind yourself to me in a marriage that lasts until we are old and gray. The propagandist-sama kindly suggested that he might bring my physical body back, but I thought that, being the body of a beast, it could never serve by your side, so I cast my body into the sea, changed my nature, and possessed the body of your wife. I am the Shōjō-fujin whom you loved. Please use me as your second wife.'

Bāchiru: 'Well, this is something I cannot comprehend. Tell me, Sensei-sama, has my wife gone mad? Is she not saying something monstrous?'

Tamakuni: 'No, she has certainly not gone mad. The Shōjō-hime-sama, whose spirit is pure, clean, and innocent, has become a spirit out of longing for you and has taken residence in your wife's body. This, too, is a matter of karmic connection, so please live together in harmony.'

Bāchiru: 'I feel as if there is a monster here. It is a disgusting thing. And what has become of my wife's soul?'

Tamakuni: 'She is with your wife, the two of them together. In short, it is one body with two spirits, so you should accept this as a karmic connection and live together in harmony. There must be some deep karmic connection entangled with this house.'

Bāchiru: 'Hmm...'

Sāberu: 'My husband was a Shōjō who guarded the forest of the Heavenly King on Mount Adsumosu, but your father, Bākusu-sama, trapped my husband and took his life. Therefore, as my spirit had nowhere to go, I settled into the body of Bākusu-sama's son, that is, this husband, Bāchiru-sama. In other words, Bāchiru-sama's spirit is my husband. I led my kin, fled the forest of Mount Adsumosu, loaded my kin onto a boat tied to the shore, and finally crossed to the Island of Shōjō to wait for my husband to come. Therefore, because my spirit communicated with my husband's spirit, Bāchiru-sama came to love looking at the sea, and my husband's spirit caused your fishing boat to be wrecked and drift to my island. It is by no means that we have been husband and wife only for the past three years.'

Bāchiru: 'Well, if that is the case, then I have been living with two people after all. What a thing I cannot comprehend. It seems I have become a living shrine for Shōjō-hiko before I knew it. Well, well, this is truly incomprehensible.'

Itahiko: 'I have now realized that in all things, / A human should serve according to the will of the Kami.'

Princess Sabel: 'This is, this is my husband, I am so happy to see you. You have returned safely, how wonderful. I finished the funeral rites in form only, prayed for my husband's happiness in the next world at the forest of the Heavenly King in the morning, and visited the grave on the mountainside of Mount Adumos in the evening, spending sad days until today. Therefore, I sent two servants to the beach to wait for your return. It seems that a spirit called Shiyōjiyōhime is dwelling in my Nikutai, but having heard the song of Shiyōjiyōhime from earlier, I have already made up my mind. Please, let us live together in harmony. I beg of you.'
