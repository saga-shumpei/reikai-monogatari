Chapter 13: 兎耳 [1488]

兎耳〔1488〕

§ 1

Yakkosu, seeking to justify himself and to retaliate for the grudge he held against Daru for exposing his inner thoughts, grabbed hold of him with the same air as a housemaid clutching the ears of a pot. Keeping time with the rhythm of a song, he began to shout, jerking Daru's head and blowing his foul breath into his face without a shred of restraint or consideration.

Yakkosu: 'A-eh, pardon me for a moment, if you please. If I, Yakkosu-tsukasa, were to chant the words of the sutra, following my submission to the great Daishintoku of the Kamisama of Ananai-kyō, then: Chiyakapoko, chiyakapoko, pokopokopoko.'

Daru: 'Hey, hey, it hurts when you pull my ears like that, won't you let go?'

Yakkosu: 'Eh, no, no, no, do you think I shall let go of these ears? With you foaming at the mouth like a crab, your eyes popping out a little, and while listening to you insult the teachings of the Kamisama of Zengenbiji, I must punish the mouth of that Akugenbaugo, Daru, or else my manhood shall not stand. Compared to my own heart, you, who are a Shiyōshinshiyōmei and Shiyōjiki, have captured Yakkosu and slandered me before the eyes of the Ōzei-sama; what is the meaning of this? I shall never forgive you. Hey, hey, you with your gaunt, bony appearance, looking like a battered sedge hat, you have become nothing but skin and bones, yet you have the nerve to wag your chin. Your ears are Usagimimi, and now that I have gripped them, I shall not let go, no matter what. How is that? If you would only apologize before everyone, saying that you have spoken nothing but truly inexcusable things, then it would be fine. But if you continue to persist without Shiyōkori, then I shall not let it pass. Come now, how is that, you gaunt one? What is the matter with your eyes? They are like signboards for a guide that are not set up, so one cannot tell where they are. Your eyes are sunken deep, and to top it off, the whites are seven or eight parts visible; do you really think this is the face of a human? Are you a great monster or a fool? You are an incomprehensible fool. What is the matter with your eyebrows? They are tattered, ragged eyebrows, looking as if caterpillars have settled upon them. The bridge of your nose is like a person sitting cross-legged, and your unkempt nostrils are attached like spectacles. Your mouth is like a crocodile's, your teeth are protruding, and furthermore, your front teeth are chipped. This face with the short chin is a beastly face beyond description. With such a mouth, what kind of sincerity could you possibly speak? It is too much for you to not know your own place. Hey, hey, shall I shake you once more? Even if you say it hurts, my ears do not hurt at all. Ahahahaha-tsuha, ahahahaha. I am so astonished I cannot speak. Ihihihi-tsuhi, ihihihi. In the middle of your distorted, gloomy face, your crooked nose is sitting there like a folding stool. Ufuhufu-tsufu, Usagimimi. If I gave you to a donkey as a souvenir, it would surely be satisfied. Ehehehe-tsuhe, ehehehe. You spout such arrogant nonsense with that square mouth of yours, sticking out your thick lips, looking like a Malay person who has come to collect taxes—a strange mouth indeed. Ohohoho-tsuho, ohohoho. You are trembling in fear, you gaunt, skin-and-bones creature. Where did I press you to make you say such things? You are a Magatsukami who rejoices in casting misfortune upon others. If you keep babbling such reckless things, I will not let you off like this. I, too, am a child of the Kami. Even if I was temporarily wrapped in clouds and became a captive of the devil, how long do you think I will remain so? There is a limit to how much you can make a fool of me. Try wagging your chin once more; I will not forgive you as things stand. Chiyakapoko, chiyakapoko.'

Daru: 'Hey, won't you let go in moderation? My ears are going to be torn off. It is understandable that you are angry because I hit the mark regarding your essential scheme, but this is a good time for you to repent. This hurts! What are you doing? Won't you let go?'

Yakkosu: 'I will not let go, I will not let go, I will not let go! This Yakkosu will not have peace in his heart until he has explained the causal connections to you, even to the point of tearing your ears off. Hey, hey, how is that, you Daru? You must recant and say that all the abusive language you have spoken until now was nothing but inexcusable lies. Clear the suspicions regarding Tamakuniwake and the other officials of the Kami, no matter what. I will not let go of these ears. What is this, you are shedding tears? You are a cowardly fellow.'

§ 2

Watching from the side, Meto could not endure it, and again from behind, he gripped both of Yakkosu's ears firmly.

Meto: 'Hey, hey, you Warumono Yakkosu, do not commit such Ranbau acts. The teaching of the ancient Seijiya is that you should pinch your own body to know the pain of others. I will also grip your ears, you Kisama, and make you realize it. Hey, hey, does it hurt? It must hurt. Until you apologize and release the Mimitabu of Daru, I will not let go of these ears. Does it hurt? Does it hurt? How is this? Your ears are the ears of a Roba, and both your Uchimimi and Sotomimi are covered in hair like a beast, with a Kibun that has an unpleasant Tezaha. While holding a Gomairiatama, you grind Goma with the tip of your mouth, and though you speak sweet words as an Ichijinoga, the Kamisama knows even to the bottom of your heart.'

Yakkosu, having his ears gripped with all his might from behind by Meto and his head pulled back, shed tears, yet remained Shifunenbuka and did not release his grip on Daru's ears. Mikane, Haru attempted to grab both of his ears as well. Itahiko shouted in a loud voice,

Itahiko: 'Hey, hey, Yakkosu, what kind of Ranbau act are you doing? You can talk without grabbing ears like that, can you not? Come, will you not release them quickly?'

Yakkosu, with Fushiyōbushiyō, said 'Hai' and released Daru's ears.

Daru: 'Ahahahaha, look at that, it must hurt. Oi, Meto, pull them firmly for me. Since this Koitsu's ears are quite like metal rods, even if you tear off a piece, there are no nerves passing through them, so there should be no worry of him feeling pain.'

Meto: 'Very well, it is your command. I will not let go no matter what. Now that it has come to this, it is the same as having one's hand pinched by a crab. It is the same as being bitten by a soft-shelled turtle. I will not let go until they are torn off.'

Yakkosu: 'Aitata, hey Meto, I have already let go, so you release me too.'

Meto: 'I am pulling them so that they hurt. Since the order from the Senden-shi has not yet come down, I will pull them with my Gariboshi hands until the order arrives.'

He said this while pulling. Each time, like a crucian carp intoxicated by mud, Yakkosu opened and closed his mouth with its misaligned teeth.

Itahiko: 'Oi, Meto, that is enough, let him go. This fellow is not just ignorant of his own body, he is ignorant of ears, so he aims directly at people's ears. Not just ears, he even aims for testicles. Everyone should be careful.'

Meto: 'Then shall I let him go? It seems his ears are quite like those of Itahiko-sama, ahahahaha.'

Itahiko: 'The sight is painful to watch from the side; you should forgive him after reconsidering and listening again.'

Meto: 'Yakkosu, having his ears pulled and face twisted, Ah, saved by Itahiko. I shall protect the compassion of Itahiko-sama, And release the ears with reluctance.'

Having finished singing, he let go of the ears with a snap.

Yakkosu's ears appeared to have been deeply pierced by Meto's hard nails, and while dripping blood drop by drop, he glared at Meto's profile with a look of regret.

Itahiko: 'Daru-sama, whose ears were grabbed by Yakkosu, Perhaps his eyes, mouth, and nose are twisted too. Making a sour face with tears falling, How pitiful is Daru's face near his cheeks. Pulled by both ears from behind, Meto makes his nose and face turn away.'

Meto: 'Just as I thought to send this man on Meto's journey, Itahiko's voice came. Refused by the single word of Itahiko, With regret, I released the ears.'

Yakkosu: 'Even if I grab a person's ears, it does not hurt, Yet Yakkosu's ears are painful. Are they ears without nerves, for with all my might, Though I pulled Daru, it did not hurt. My ears have warm blood flowing through them, Oh, the pain of the ears when pulled.'

The ear quarrel finally ended. Daru and Yakkosu both fell silent with sullen faces. The boatman Iiru began to sing in a fluent voice exposed to the wind of the vast lake.

Iiru: 'The quarrel between Daru and Yakkosu, The distortion of rabbit ears and ears. The waves come surging in, Resonating against the eardrums of the ears. The plot of Yakkosu, fearful even to hear, It is amusing that Daru-sama exposes it. Pulling each other's ears saying it hurts, it hurts, Suffering on the waves. Hearing complaints that make the ears ache, And again having ears pulled until they hurt. The placement of eyes, nose, mouth, ears, and eyebrows, How comical they are when lined up. The face is a monkey's, the heart is a demon's, Before long, a crow will take you as a bridegroom. As we slide along the long sea route, There are pitfalls even inside the boat. Causing others to fall for one's own desires, An evil plot of warp and woof. One hundred ri, two hundred ri is not far, But the land of the Gods is not near. To the west is Terukuni, to the east is Kikoku, To the north is Terumon, to the south is Izumi, In the middle drifts the lake of Kiyo.'

Singing this, he parts the hot, surging waves.
