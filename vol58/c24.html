Chapter 24: 礼 Festival [1499]

礼祭〔1499〕

§ 1

Michihiko, Yoshikohime, and the two couples, Birchil and Sabel, set about preparing for the Saiten, and without relying on any Genan or Gejiyo, they were absorbed in the Chōri of the Shinsenmono.

Michihiko: 'Excuse me, Birchil-sama, as you may have guessed, I am but a clumsy Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, and this is my first time preparing Shinsen for the Ōkamisama of Brahmanism. I feel as though something is stuck in my Okuba, a rather strange Kibun. Hahaha!'

Birchil: 'I had been careless, but since we are indeed those who have been helped by the Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō and who are grateful for the Goshintoku of the Ōkamisama of Ananai-kyō, we must by all means make the Saiten of the Kamisama of Ananahi our Daiichi priority. What do you think?'

Michihiko: 'That is so. Since the Kamisama are originally of one Hitokabu, when one considers the history of the Kamiyo, it is the same in either case. Ananai-kyō is the Kamisama who was Oshiko by Kuniharutachi no Ōkamisama and various other Kamisama, and if they were humans, they would be like enemies with Daijizaiten-sama. However, the heart of the Kamisama is broad and unlike the human heart, and without being concerned about such things in the slightest, they wish to assist Daijizaiten-sama and are sending us to bring the Brahman religion into harmony through Kototama. However, since I cannot make a decision on my own, I shall go and ask my teacher for a moment.'

Birchil: 'Yes, I would be grateful for that. While you are at it, I would also like to have the spirits of my ancestors enshrined through Ananai-kyō; would you mind asking if that is also acceptable?'

Michihiko replied, 'I understand,' and left the place to go to the living room of Tamakurowake. He informed Tamakurowake of the request that Birchil wished to withdraw from Brahmanism, enter Ananai-kyō, and have the Ōkamisama of Ananahi enshrined, as well as the desire to have the ancestral rites performed through Ananai-kyō, and asked for guidance from Tamakurowake, starting first with the ancestral rites.

Michihiko: 'Sensei, when a human leaves this world and goes to the Reikai, the spirit of a truly virtuous person immediately ascends to heaven to join the heavenly beings and live a life in heaven. If the connection with the Genkai is severed, it would seem that descendants in the Genkai have no need to perform rites for their ancestors, but is it still necessary to perform ancestral rites? In our view, it feels like a truly useless and meaningless act.'

Tamakurowake: 'No matter how much one has gone to heaven and the connection with the living on earth is severed, the good of love and the truth of faith penetrate heaven and earth and are never delayed in the slightest. There is no reason why a correct and pure Saiten, filled with the love, goodness, and true faith that descendants perform out of filial piety, would not reach them. Even in heaven, there is still a need for clothing, food, and shelter. Offerings and Saiten made from the sincere heart of descendants cause those in the Reikai to rejoice, and furthermore, they protect the happiness of those descendants.'

Michihiko: 'I have heard the teaching that spirits in the Chūyūkai stay for no longer than thirty years at most. Since that spirit has been reborn in the Genkai as a human, it would seem that ancestral rites are unnecessary, but is it still necessary to perform ancestral rites in such a case?'

Tamakurowake: 'According to the divine law of Ken-yū-itchi, even if that spirit has been reborn in the Genkai as a human and is no longer in the Reikai, it is still the duty of the descendant toward the ancestor to perform the Saiten properly. The spirit of a person whose memorial rites have been performed with devotion will receive the offerings with joy, regardless of the distinction between the Reikai and the Genkai. A living person who, while born into this world, still has memorial rites performed strictly for them, will taste great happiness in their daily life as well. Therefore, ancestral rites should be performed solemnly by descendants, not just for thirty years, but for ancestors of a thousand or ten thousand years if possible. Ancestral spirits who have fallen into hell can, through the good virtue of the descendants' rites, immediately ascend to the Chūyūkai and eventually reach heaven. Furthermore, heavenly beings whose descendants perform rites with devotion can live an extremely peaceful life even in heaven, and the ripples of that heavenly being's joy are naturally transmitted to the descendants, protecting the prosperity of the descendants. This is because the good of love and the truth of faith flow into the divinity of the heavenly being and the humanity of the descendant, and are never severed.'

愈祭典の準備にとりかかるべく三千彦、デビス姫、バーチル、サーベルの二夫婦は下男にも下女にも構はさず、せつせと神饌物の調理に熱中して居る。

三千『もし、バーチルさま、私はお察しの通り三五教のヘボ宣伝使ですが、バラモンの大神様の神饌を拵へるのは今が初めてで厶いますよ。何だか奥歯に物が、こまつた様な気分が致しますわ。ハハハハハ』

バーチル『うつかりして居ましたが如何にも吾々は三五教の宣伝使に助けられ、又三五教の大神様の御神徳を感謝してる者で厶いますから、どうしても三五の神様の祭典を第一に致さねばなりませぬ。如何でせうか』

三千『さうですな。神様はもとは一株ですから、どちらにしても同じ様なものの神代からの歴史を考へて見ますと、三五教は国治立の大神様、其外諸々の神様から押籠められた方の神様で、大自在天様とは、人間同士なら敵同志の様な者ですが、然し神様のお心は人間の心と違つて寛大なもので、少しも左様な事に御頓着なく、大自在天様をお助け遊ばさうと思つて、バラモン教を言向和す為に吾々をお遣はしになるのですからね。然し私では到底決断がつきませぬから、一寸之からお師匠様に伺つて参ります』

バーチル『はい、それは有難う厶います。序に先祖様の霊も三五教で祭つて頂き度う厶いますが、之も差支がないか伺つて来て下さいませぬか』

三千彦は『承知致しました』と此場を立つて玉国別の居間に打通り、バーチルがバラモン教を脱退し、三五教に入信し、三五の大神を祭つて貰ひ度い事、並に祖霊祭を三五教にて営み度い事等の願を告げ、玉国別に対し先づ第一に祖霊祭に就いて教示を乞ふた。

三千『先生、人間は現世を去つて霊界へ行つた時は、極善者の霊身は直ちに天国に上りて天人と相伍し天国の生活を営み、現界との連絡が切れるとすれば、現界にある子孫は父祖の霊祭などをする必要は無いものの様に思はれますが、それでも祖霊祭を為なくてはならないのでせうか。吾々の考へでは真に無益な無意義なことの様に感じられますがなア』

玉国『何程天国へ往つて地上現人との連絡が断たれたと言つても、愛の善と信の真とは天地に貫通して少しも遅滞せないものである。子孫が孝のためにする愛善と信真の籠もつた正しき清き祭典が届かないと云ふ道理は決して無い。天国にあつても矢張り衣食住の必要がある。子孫の真心よりする供物や祭典は、霊界にあるものをして歓喜せしめ、且つその子孫の幸福を守らしむるものである』

三千『中有界にある精霊は何程遅くても三十年以上居ないといふ教を聞きましたが、その精霊が現世に再生して人間と生れた以上は、祖霊祭の必要は無いやうですが、斯ういふ場合でも矢張り祖霊祭の必要があるのですか』

玉国『顕幽一致の神律に由つて、例へその精霊が現界に再生して人間となり霊界に居らなくても、矢張り祭典は立派に執行するのが祖先に対する子孫の勤めである。祭祀を厚くされた人の霊は霊界現界の区別なく、その供物を歓喜して受けるものである。現世に生れて居ながら猶且つ依然として霊祭を厳重に行ふて貰ふて居る現人は日々の生活上においても、大変な幸福を味はふことになるのである。故に祖霊の祭祀は三十年どころか、相成るべくは千年も万年の祖霊も、子孫たるものは厳粛に勤むべきものである。地獄に落ちた祖霊などは子孫の祭祀の善徳に由つて、忽ち中有界に昇り進んで天国に上ることを得るものである。又子孫が祭祀を厚くして呉れる天人は、天国に於ても極めて安逸な生涯を送り得られ、その天人が歓喜の余波は必ず子孫に自然に伝はり子孫の繁栄を守るものである。何んとなれば愛の善と信の真は天人の神格と現人（子孫）の人格とに内流して何処迄も断絶せないからである』

§ 2

Michi: 'Those who have worshipped their ancestral spirits through the rituals of the Ural religion or the Baramonkyō have likely gone to the Tengoku of their respective masters. When one changes the ritual to that of the Ananai-kyō, what will become of those ancestral spirits?'

Tamakuni: 'The Seirei of humans, or those who have become Tennin, reside in the Reikai and constantly desire only to attain higher levels of Chie, Shiyōkaku, and Zenshin. Therefore, they rejoice greatly when their descendants in the Genkai believe in a religion that is most thoroughly penetrated by Zenshin, and perform Saishi in accordance with its Oshihe. Even though a Tennin was originally a human who has ascended, as long as they are the ancestors of humans, even if they dwell in peace in Tengoku, their emotional bonds of love and truth remain connected through internal flow. Thus, for descendants to enter a religion with the most excellent Shiyōkaku and to worship and console their own spirits through the Gishiki of that sect is the best salvation and a source of joy, even for Tennin, Seirei, or spirits who have fallen into hell. Since the Tennin in Tengoku also desire the advancement of Zenshin, even if a person currently on earth believes in a religion they deem best, and furthermore, stops the religion their ancestors held and enters the Ananai-kyō, it does not cause any trouble for the ancestral spirits. Moreover, because the ancestral spirits are advancing toward the light, they will never be lost. On the contrary, the ancestral spirits rejoice in this, and can elevate their status while in Tengoku. Therefore, we living beings must advance our faith in the religion we recognize as the best for the sake of filial piety toward our ancestors, give satisfaction to the spirits of our ancestors through that Oshihe, and must strictly observe our duties as descendants. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese.'

Michi: 'Yes, thank you very much. The master of this house will also be relieved by that. Now, I have one more question; what should we do about the Baramon god?'

Tamakuni: 'Since even in the sacred ground of the forest of the shrine, the three great Ōkami and the Great Daijizaiten-sama are enshrined, is there any need to exclude them? You have received the divine virtues of the Baramon god in this house until now, so you surely cannot do anything so heartless.'

Michi: 'I hear that a shrine for the Baramon Daijizaiten-sama is built in the sacred land of Mount Adumos, but at this time, how would it be if I were to instruct the master to build a shrine like the one in the forest of the shrine, and enshrine Daijizaiten-sama on the side in that manner?'

Tamakuni: 'Please go and call the master once. Since building a shrine is not something to be done lightly, I intend to hear his opinion first.'

Michi: 'Yes, I understand. I will go and call him immediately.'

She returned to the original kitchen for divine offerings, conveyed the instructions of Tamakuni-wake regarding the ancestral spirit festival, and furthermore... 'The teacher wishes to ask you something regarding the enshrining of the divine spirit, so please come for a moment'... she said, inviting Bachiru, and returned to the living room where Tamakuni-wake and the others were.

Tamakuni: 'Ah, Bachiru-sama, what are your thoughts on what to do with the shrine of the Tennō-sama in the forest of Adumos?'

Bachiru: 'Yes, it is a shrine that has been worshipped for generations of ancestors, and my Seirei served it as a retainer, so I wonder if it is right to change the object of worship immediately just because I have suddenly entered the Ananai-kyō. Regarding this, I have been wanting to ask you at your leisure. What is your opinion, sensei?'

Tamakuni: 'My thought is to build two new shrines in the forest of Mount Adumos, enshrining the great Ōkami of Ananai in one and the current Tennō-sama in the other, and then prepare several dozen ships to go and welcome the small monkeys remaining on Shōjō-ga-shima, and while at it, I would like to rescue the three members of the Baramon group and bring them back. I believe that is the best method for the gods and also for your guardian deity.'

三千『ウラル教や波羅門教の儀式に由つて祖霊を祭つたものは、各自その所主の天国へ行つて居るでせう。夫れを三五教に改式した時はその祖霊は何うなるものでせうか』

三千『はい、有難う厶いました。当家の主人も、それで安心致しませう。それから、も一つお尋ねが厶いますが、バラモンの神様を如何いたしたら宜しいでせう』

玉国『一度主人を呼んで来て呉れ。宮を建てるとなると、さう軽々しくは行かぬから一応意見を聞いて見る積りだ』

三千『はい、承知致しました。直様呼んで参ります』

玉国『あ、バーチルさま、貴方はアヅモスの森の天王様のお宮を、如何なさるお考へで厶いますか』

バーチル『はい、先祖代々お祀りして来たお宮様なり、又私の精霊が眷族として仕へて居つたのですから、今俄に三五教に這入つたと云つて直に祀り変へる事は如何かと考へます。これに就いては貴方様にゆるゆるお尋ね致し度いと思つてゐました。先生のお考へは如何で厶いませうか』

玉国『私の考へとしてはアヅモス山の森林に新にお宮を二棟建造し、一方は三五の大神様、一方は今の天王様を奉斎し、さうして猩々ケ島に残つて居る小猿を、数十艘の船を用意して迎へ来り、序にバラモン組の三人も助けて帰る様にし度いもので厶います。それが神様に対しても、貴方の守護神に対しても最善の方法だと考へます』

Bachiru: 'Arigata gozaimasu. In truth, my wife and I have been whispering to each other that we have wished for this since saizen. Since those Kozaru are all children of Shiyōjiyōhime, it is tauzen that I wish to hikiyo them close to myself. I feel a strange kibun, as if I have become a parent to a Shiyōjiyō myself. If you would be so kind as to do so, there would be no greater joy than this.'

Tamakuni: 'Once your ketsushin is firm, we shall proceed with the jiyunbi immediately. However, since today is only for a saiten of gratitude to the Ōkamisama, it would be best to worship the Ananahi Ōkami and the Brahman Ōkami side by side, and have everyone down to the genan and gejiyo perform sanpai.'

Bachiru: 'Yes, arigata gozaimasu for your goshinsetsu kizu regarding everything from start to finish.'

Bachiru: 'I heard that the parent of man emerged from the monkey, / And now I have become a parent to a monkey. / From the distant Kamiyo of long ago, / I am bound by an unbroken fate.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Having served long in the forest of the Heavenly King, / By that divine virtue, you borrow a human dwelling. / Even if the physical body is born as a Shiyōjiyō, / The soul is pure, a messenger of God.'

Bachiru: 'Arigata, the words of the master who has spoken truly, / My wife must also be encouraged by them. / Even if the world laughs at us as man-apes, / We shall become gods who remove all sins.'

Thus he sang, and hurriedly returned to the shinsen-sho, where he made all preparations and successfully conducted the festival of gratitude. At the request of the master, Tamakuniwake became the chief officiant, recited the Amatsu Norito, and upon concluding, offered a song of gratitude.

Tamakuniwake: 'In the forest of Adumos, where the morning sun pierces and the evening sun shines, / In the forest of Tokiwa-Kakiwa, / Where the Great Ōkami of Ōkunihiko dwells forever and ever, / Serving as a precious messenger, / The spirit of Shiyōjiyōhiko, / The master of the mansion where he possesses, / I, Tamakuniwake, the divine officiant replacing the official Bachiru, / Before the precious presence of the Ananahi-kyō Great Ōkami, / And the Great Ōkami of the Brahman religion, / Humbly, we all, not to mention Bachiru and Antī, / Having endured three years of sorrow, / Have finally returned here, / By the grace of the Imperial Ōkami, / Wishing to repay even a single layer of the thousand layers of the great divine blessings, / We have placed various delicacies of mountain, sea, river, and field, / Upon the eight-legged tables, / Arranged until the space is narrow, / With sacred sake filled in jars and jugs, / We offer water, hard salt, and the great feast, / May you hear this in full and in detail, / And protect the people of this mansion, / The divine officiants of Ananahi-kyō, / And the people of the village of Suma, / Prostrating before the great presence, / We serve with awe and reverence, / Kannagara Tamachi Haema-se.'

と歌ひ終り、感謝祭も無事に終了した。玉国別一行は美はしき閑静な離れ座敷を与へられ、海上の疲労を癒やすべく、師弟五人は足を伸ばして休養する事となつた。
