Chapter 16: Holy Song [1491]

聖歌〔1491〕

§ 1

With sails hoisted on Hatsuwakamaru, drifting along with the tide, the party of Tamakuniwake gazed at the shadows of islands appearing in their path, harboring vague hopes in their hearts, and advanced upon the waves, blown by the lake breeze.

The moon gradually ascended to the mid-heaven, and the cool air pressed upon their bodies by degrees; feeling an indescribable sensation, they stood up from the mat-roofed cabin without thinking and, while looking up at the distant, hazy island shadows from the gunwale, spoke to themselves.

Tamakuniwake: 'I wonder what kind of people dwell on the floating islands visible beyond this boundless expanse of the lake, or if they are perhaps uninhabited islands. Are they fragments of Shiyōjiyōshima? If there be any who have drifted ashore after being swallowed by the waves and having their ship broken, reenacting the second act of the tragedy of Bachiru-sama, and who now live in a separate world detached from this floating world, having taken a Shiyōjiyō princess as a wife, then to meet them would be a sad parting once more. It is painful to save them, yet if I do not, I shall not be at peace before Kami. Be that as it may, I shall advance amidst the waves and explore the reality. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may the spirits be blessed, and may this ship bestowed by Kami carry us safely across the sea route. Before the Great Kami Kuninotokotachi, the Great Kami Toyokuninushi, and the Mizu-no-Mitama who resides in the palace of Saien.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Beginning with Tamakuniwake, the propagandist of the San-Ikkyō, I pray that the majesty of Kami, the three thousand Hiko, and the hundred other divine officials may safely advance across the distant sea route. The divine official Hatsuwakahime-hime, having crossed these rough waves and riding upon the fierce dog Smart, has set forth upon the waves; how valiant is her appearance. If she is resting in the island shadows yonder, it would be a joy indeed. The heart of Hatsuwakahime-hime, who swiftly abandoned the ship to ride the dog and depart, must surely hold a mission of great importance, though I know not what it is. In the path ahead of us all, will yet another evil appear, and what kind of enmity will it bear? I know not the Kiyo-no-Umi. Whatever may happen, leaving all to the will of Kami, we shall recite the Amatsu-Norito, chant the heavenly number-songs, and all together in one voice, praise the name of Kami. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may the spirits be blessed.'

Having finished singing, they recited the Norito reverently as was the custom together with the others, and after finishing the number-songs, they began to chant the propaganda song with a fascinating melody.

Tamakuniwake: 'The Divine Ancestor who created the foundation of earth, water, fire, wind, and void, the Great Kami Kuninotokotachi, resides outside the universe and has perfectly and minutely created the heavens, earth, sun, moon, and stars. Bestowing His stern blessings upon all things, from the human race, birds, and beasts, down to the insects and the dew on a single leaf of grass, how grateful we are that He protects this beautiful world. First and foremost, the Great Kami Taka-mi-musubi, who appears as the Sun Kami, and the Great Kami Kami-musubi, who appears as the Moon Kami, take charge of the work of water and fire, arranging all things in heaven and earth, causing each to find its place, and bestowing infinite joy.'

Tamakuniwake: 'How noble it is that He builds the sacred era of Miroku in detail. First, He created man, making Amatsu-no-hiko and Hōjōhime the ancestors of the human race, and sent them down to the Garden of Eden; having created many children of man in the image of Kami, there came a time when He sought to open this world. However, due to the evil hearts of Amatsu-no-hiko and Hōjōhime, who disobeyed the heart of the Imperial Great Kami, the air of heaven and earth became filled with evil, and the Yamata-no-Orochi, the ugly fox, and the crooked demons were born, further disturbing this world. The Great Kami Kuninotokotachi, who exists as the stern spirit of the Great Kami Taka-mi-musubi,'

Tamakuniwake: 'descended from heaven far away by the command of the Heavenly Great Kami, and in order to save the three worlds, He troubled His heart in various ways, establishing ten million laws and creating the Kami to govern all things on this earth peacefully and tranquilly. The Great Kami Toyokunihime, who exists as the Mizu-no-Mitama of the Great Kami Kami-musubi, assisted in the divine work of the stern spirit, watching over and protecting all mountains, fields, seas, and rivers, near and far, while the souls of Tokoyo-no-hiko and Tokoyo-hime, possessed by the Yamata-no-Orochi, and the spirits of the ugly demons of the gold-furred nine-tailed crooked demons...'

§ 2

Being kakimida, they obstructed the Daishingefu of the Ōkami, and finally, the magawaza of the evil ones gathering and speaking ill of the laws of the Kami, the Futahashira of Izutsu and Mizuho, who were in Amatsumikuni, to the Mototsumioya Ōkami, caused the Sumeōkami to hold back hot tears, unable to do otherwise. Until the Miyo of Miroku comes, the Kuniharutachi Ōkami was confined to the Newa island, which floats long in the northeast of the Chijiyō Sekai, in the Onokoro, while waiting for the Jisetsu. The Toyokunihime Ōkami, in the Mizuhokuni of Mesopotamia, limited the borders for a while, until the Miyo of Miroku comes.

Because of the sadness of being told solemnly to wait for the Jisetsu, the Futahashira of Izutsu and Mizuho swallowed their tears and, with purity, hid themselves in their respective Kakurega. However, when the Hachisuba that blooms at once opened and it became a fragrant Miyo, the Kamususanō Ōkami, while carrying the Chikura no Okido, hid his form in the mansion of Saien or the mountains of Caucasus. He taught the doctrine thoroughly to the eighty islands and eighty countries, and while raising and nurturing many Senden-shi to teach the people, to pacify the Magakami, and to purify the heaven and earth to restore the original Kamikuni, he dispatched them here and there.

How grateful it is to have the world, which had become completely clouded, illuminated. Among the many messengers of the Kami who exist, the most excellent and powerful in divine virtue, the Kami-bashira, is Hatsuwakahime alone. How awe-inspiring it is that she reveals the divine power of mysterious miracles, crossing the wilderness, mountains, rivers, and seas where the Majin rage, together with the fierce dog Smart. Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth itself sinks, the power of Makoto saves the world. We, who have been taught the doctrine of the path of Makoto, while enduring all hardships for the sake of the Sumeōkami, must devote ourselves to the path and to the world.

Ah, how grateful, how grateful are the Senden-shi who must devote themselves. Since leaving the mansion of Saien, while encountering various trials of the grace of the Ōkami, each time they are given divine power most refreshingly, and having revealed their merits in various places, now here, while floating on the lake of purification, it is joyful to row the divine boat of the doctrine of the Ōkami of infinite benevolence and advance. Masuzumi no Hiko, Mitsuhiko, Princess Devis, and Itahiko, come, from now on, tighten your loincloths at the Shimotsu-iwane, and fasten them firmly at the Uwatsu-iwane, for the Brahmanism and Uralism are coiled in the mansion of the Kami.

Facing the Magatsu of the ugly, with the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases, and with the sword of endurance, you must advance without sparing your own bodies. The Kami is with us; humans are the children of the Kami, the palaces of the Kami. Should any enemy attack, why should there be any fear? The Yamata no Orochi and the ugly Kami that are coiled in the capital of Haruna, until we pacify them with the Kototama bestowed by the Kami and return, raising a shout of victory before the Sumeōkami of the Saien mansion, this journey must not allow the heart to slacken. Please protect us, Kannagara. Before the Sumeōkami, I, representing the group of Tamakuniwake, humbly and respectfully pray.

Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara, may the spirits be blessed.

Eel the boatman, while manipulating the oar, began to sing again.

Eel: '(Trumpet Song) The wind blows gently across. On the lake of purification, a hundred birds flutter here and there. The heavenly pure land is right before my eyes. Is that which I see in the distance the island of the Shōjō? If it is the Shōjō island, I shall go no further. That island shadow floating on the waves is the island of Fuku, the wind that waits for me. (Ryukyu Song) On the island of Fuku, there is fresh water. Not only fresh water, but there is a cave. It is the one island feared because they say a great serpent lives inside the cave. Whether I like it or not, this current carries the boat to the island of Fuku. If the great serpent should come out, I rely on the Kototama of Izutsu. If I strike out with the Kototama of Izutsu, both the demon and the great serpent will be barefoot and helpless. I was born in the village of Suma in Izumi, and I have been on a boat since I was young.'

So singing, he is manipulating the oar with all his might.
