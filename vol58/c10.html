Chapter 10: 報 Grace [1485]

報恩〔1485〕

§ 1

The ship on which Tamakuniwake and his party boarded was provisionally named Hatsuwakamaru.

The reason for this was that this sturdy ship had been granted to them in the event of an emergency for Hatsuwakahime.

With white sails hoisted, it flew southward at high speed across the moonlit surface of the lake.

Ahead, the situation revealed seven or eight ships forming a single-ladder formation, bearing down upon the Hatsuwakamaru.

Seeing this, Yakkosu spoke immediately,

Yakkosu: 'Excuse me, everyone, those seven or eight ships lined up ahead are pirate ships that appear and disappear in this lake to plunder ships traveling north and south. It will be a serious matter if we are caught, so we must devise some plan. I intend to turn the bow slightly toward the southwest, so please be prepared for that. If we head southwest, the pirate ships cannot easily pursue us because of the scattered hidden reefs. Although I serve the Brahman army as a chief inspector, I am also a great pirate boss. I am the only one who knows the conditions of these sea routes. Whenever I encounter a strong enemy, I always take this route to escape. The ships that pursue me invariably suffer shipwreck or sink in that vicinity. May I proceed in that manner?'

Tamakuni: 'I leave everything to you, as you know the ways of the sea route. Now, make those preparations quickly.'

Yakkosu: 'Yes, it is time to repay your kindness. In that case, I shall steer the ship from here on.'

With that, he gripped the oar, made the eight sailors row with all their might, and the ship set off as if shooting an arrow. The pirate ships turned their bows with all their might and pursued the Hatsuwakamaru.

Yakkosu encouraged the sailors with all his might as they rowed. When the ship finally reached the danger zone, two or three of the pirate ships had already approached to the point where their gunwales were rubbing against each other. They then threw their anchors onto the Hatsuwakamaru. The Hatsuwakamaru was finally caught. While they were hesitating, the eight ships formed a wall around the Hatsuwakamaru, and each of them drew their bows to the full moon, attempting to intimidate them. Yakkosu shouted in a loud voice,

Yakkosu: 'Hey, are you not pirate ships? Who do you think I am? I am Yakkosu, the pirate chieftain. I am currently under the orders of Daikokunushi to capture the missionaries of the Sangokyō and am in the middle of sending them to the checkpoint at the port of Kiyo. If you interfere, I will show no mercy.'

The pirate leader, Debu, who was commanding the eight ships, stood at the bow and said,

Debu: 'Ah, was it you, Boss? I offer my deepest apologies. Not knowing it was your official ship, I thought a good prize had appeared and led the whole fleet to chase you this far. I have truly done a terrible thing. I beg you to forgive me.'

Yakkosu: 'You had better understand this from now on. The fact that you can conduct your evil business on this lake in peace is all thanks to the grace of me, Yakkosu, serving as the chief inspector of the Brahman army. If I were to shake my head once, I could immediately capture you all with thousands of soldiers, and since I know where you all live, I could have your wives and children arrested and subjected to heavy punishment. More than that, you should head north from here, as seventy or eighty missionaries of the Sangokyō are coming, and you should proceed to capture them. If you do that well, I will have the checkpoint officials grant you whatever reward you desire. Now go. The ones behind you are all cowards. I have already tied up five or six of the strongest ones and brought them here. If you dawdle, you might lose sight of their shadows. You had better turn your ships around quickly and head due north.'

Debu: 'Yes, I understand. I ask for your continued favor. Now, everyone, turn the bows to the north and row out at high speed.'

He gave the order. Instantly, the eight ships turned their bows to the north and, with the sound of oars rowing with all their might, moved away with the speed of a flying bird.

玉国別一行の搭乗した船は仮に初稚丸と命名された。その理由は初稚姫に危急の場合この堅牢なる船を与へられたからである。月照る湖面を白帆をかかげ南へ南へと急速力にて翔つて行く。前方に当り七八艘の船が単梯陣を張つて初稚丸目蒐けて押し寄せ来る形勢が見えて居た。ヤッコスは之を見るより早く、

ヤッコス『もし、御一同様、あの前方に並んでゐます七八艘の船は此湖に陰顕出没して南北往来の船を掠める賊船で厶います。捉まつては一大事ですから何とか工夫をせなくてはなりますまい。私はこれから舳を少しく西南に向けやうと思ひますから其覚悟で居て下さい。西南へ向ひますれば暗礁点綴して容易に賊船は追つ駆ける事は出来ませぬ。然し私も表面バラモン軍に仕へ目付頭を致して居りますが海賊の大親分です。船路の様子を知つてるのは私許りです。もしも強敵に出会つた時は、何時も此航路をとり逃げます。追駆けて来た船は必ずその辺で難船し、或は沈没するものです。左様さして頂いても宜しいか』

玉国『船路の勝手を知つてるお前に何事も一任する。さア早くその用意をして呉れ』

ヤッコス『はい、御恩の報じ時で厶います。然らばこれから私が船を操ります』

と櫓を握り八人の水夫に一生懸命に櫂を漕がせ矢を射る如く走り出した。賊船は一生懸命に舳を転じ初稚丸の後を追ふて追駆け来る。

ヤッコスは一生懸命に水夫を励まし、櫓を漕ぐ。漸く危険区域に船が差蒐つた時、賊船の二三艘は早くも舷々相摩する処迄、近づいて来た。さうして錨を初稚丸に投げつけた。初稚丸は到頭おつつかれて了つた。グヅグヅして居る間に八艘の船は初稚丸の周囲に船垣を作り、各自に弓を満月に絞つて威喝を試みて居る。ヤッコスは大声をあげ、

ヤッコス『オイ、貴様等は賊船ではないか。俺を誰と心得てる。賊船頭のヤッコスだぞ。俺は今大黒主の命令によつて三五教の宣伝使を捕縛し、キヨの港の関所に送る途中だ邪魔をひろぐと容赦は致さぬぞ』

八艘の船を統率して居た海賊のデブは舷頭に立ち、

デブ『あ、親方で厶いましたか。えらい失礼を致しました。貴方のお乗込の御用船とは知らず、よい獲物が現はれたと、全隊を引率れて、ここ迄おつ駆けて来ました。誠に済まない事を致しました。何卒お許しを願ひます』

ヤッコス『今後は必ず心得たが宜からう。其方が安閑として此湖上に悪性商売が出来るのも皆此ヤッコスがバラモン軍の目付頭になつてる余徳ぢやないか。俺が一つ首を振らうものなら、忽ち数千人の軍隊を以て貴様達を捕縛し、且貴様等の住宅を皆知つてるから、妻子眷族も召捕つて重い成敗に会はされるのだ。それよりもこれから北へ北へと進んで、三五教の宣伝使が七八十人やつて来るから、それを捕縛すべく進んだが宜からう。それを巧くやつたならば、其方に望み次第の褒美を、関所の役人に執持つて貰つてやらう。さア行け。後に居る奴は皆弱虫許りだ。一番強い奴はここに五六人ふん縛つて連れて来たのだ。グヅグヅしてゐると影を見失ふかも知れぬぞ。早く船を引返し、真北に向つて進んだが宜からう』

デブ『はい、承知致しました。何分宜しう願ひます。さア皆の者、舳を北に向け、急速力で漕ぎ出せ』

と命令した。忽ち八艘の船は船首を北に向け一生懸命にグイグイと櫂の音賑しく鳥の飛つ如き勢で遠ざかり行く。

§ 2

Yakkosu: 'Ahahahaha, well, Sendenshi-sama of Tamakuniwake, even an Akunin might be of use at a time like this. If I were the Yakkosu I was until yesterday, I would not know what kind of Muhon I might start here, but moved by your infinite Jinji, I have become terrified of what I have done until now, and today I have finally attained a feeling like a Ningen. Although I know it is truly inexcusable for one who has heard the Oshihe of the Kamisama to speak lies, I thought I must take Rinkiōhen Shiyochi in this Baahi, so I spoke a lie that was not in my heart. Nanitozo, I beg you to apologize to the Kamisama on my behalf, Anatasama.'

He says this while showing his Magokoro on his face and pleading.

Tamakuni: 'Hahahaha, ah, I am impressed. The Hitokuhijinshiyu has suddenly become a Niyoraisama. In that case, Dalu and Meeto-sama, since there is no longer any Shinpai of being eaten, why don't we wash away the grudges of the past into the lake, and cross this lake together, Ichirentakushiyō in the same boat, Waki Aiai and Uchitoke?'

The Brahman group, as well as Meeto and Dalu, reply 'Hai' with a happy look, Sashiutsumu, and even let tears well up in their eyes.

Yakkosu began to sing while operating the oar. Everyone tapped the gunwale and joined in lively. The sound echoed across the water surface, and it seemed as if it would startle the Ryūjin at the bottom of the sea.

Yakkosu: 'Since long ago on the waters of Lake Terumon / Called a demon and a devil / I would capture passing ships / Steal treasures and strip away clothes / Even taking the lives of noble people / To pass those days / Even the Akugyaku-mudō Yakkosu / Was intimidated by the power of the Brahman army / And scattered much gold and silver as bribes / Successfully ingratiating myself with the Sekimori of the port of Kiyo / Chosen as the Metsuke-gashira of the Brahman / Secretly employing pirates / Spending my days in evil and falsehood / This Yakkosu also saw his destiny reach its end / The sea I thought I knew well / Due to a sudden storm, my course / Was mistaken, and I ran aground on a reef / Shattering the ship into splinters.'

Yakkosu: 'Here the five of us were completely naked / Diving through the waves, at last / Three of us, skilled in swimming / To Tsumi Island, where the waves rage / And people fear to approach / Barely swam ashore / Pressed by hunger, we tried to / Slaughter and eat the criminals / Fighting with all our might / Our bodies exhausted / And as we were about to breathe our last / The Sendenshi of the Oshihe of the infinite Jinji of Sango / Appeared and saved our / Precarious lives / The gratitude of having the pure Oshihe / Explained to us patiently / Even we, who were pathless, / Were awakened by the voice of the God / While choked with tears of gratitude.'

Yakkosu: 'Taken aboard the Hatsuwakamaru / To return to the port of Kiyo / While drifting on the waves / The eight ships floating ahead / Were surely the pirate ships led by / Debu, my subordinate / Seeing them, I quickly / Changed my course / And headed toward the dangerous shoals in the lake / The pirate ships, accustomed to the lake / Followed as if shooting arrows / Thinking us unexpected prey / They surrounded us from all directions / This is the time to repay the high grace / Of the benefactors who saved my life / Deeper than the sea / Fortunately, to Debu and the others under me / I lined up threats and lies / And finally managed to scatter them / For the first time, my heart felt light.'

Yakkosu: 'The moonlit sky is clear / It is truly a joyous occasion / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara / May the spirits be blessed / Let us cross the reef-dotted lake / Safely to the other shore / And let our party arrive easily / At the port of Kiyo / When I think, when I think, since long ago / I did not know the heart of God at all / Turning my back on goodness and truth / Being single-mindedly devoted to evil and falsehood / Clouding my heart / Is truly the height of foolishness / When I reflect on it, the world to come / Becomes very terrifying / The Kaminaohi who created this world / The Ōnaohi with a vast heart / In the world of men, everything / Should be looked at and listened to again with Naohi.'

Yakkosu: 'Great God of Sango-kyō / I pray that you forgive / The sins of the body I have committed until now / Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over / Whether the moon waxes or wanes / Even if this lake water dries up / Even if I lose my life / How could the Yakkosu who has once / Dedicated his Magokoro to God / Drown in evil? / Have mercy, Kannagara / Before the Sumer-ōkami / I pray with awe and reverence.'

While singing like this, he gradually turned the bow of the ship southwest again, riding the current and sliding across the moonlit sea surface.
