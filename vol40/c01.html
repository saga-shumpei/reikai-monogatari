Chapter 1: Great Cloud Mountain [1085]

大雲山〔1085〕

§ 1

In the early autumn when the sky is clear, the wind is cool in the land of the Moon. Though the flowers scatter, in the capital of Haruna, the Brahman religion was established. In a grotto of Mount Taiunzan, Onikumohiko built a mansion, renamed himself Ōkuronushi, and with the direct lineage of Bontenō, practiced the Magatsuwaza of deceiving this world. Leading a multitude of armies, holding the scriptures in his left hand and drawing his sword in his right, he brought the majority of the Setsuteirizoku in the land of India, a country of over seven thousand provinces, under his command. The ferocity of his power was such that it would fell even a flying bird. At that very time, a Kamutsukasa was propagating the Mioshihe of the princess of Ural, Uralhiko.

Tokoyamihiko appeared in the Dekatan plateau of the land of the Moon, unfurling the banner of his religion. He too, holding the Koran in his left hand and a sword in his right, challenged the Brahman religion. As his power gradually increased, the foundations of the Brahman religion began to face peril. Furthermore, Hinodewake, who conveys the Oshihe of the Lord God Susanoo, had built an Iso mansion on the mountain of Ubusuna. The reputation of the Lord of the Eight Islands was like the rising of the sun in the eastern sky, and the Brahman Kyōshiyo, unable to remain calm, gathered in the grotto to discuss various ways to open a counter-offensive.

While they were huddled in consultation, the messenger Teruji rode back in a rush. He reported that the followers of the Lord God Susanoo were gathering their allies and approaching the Iso mansion. He claimed to have clearly sensed that their force was as swift as a lion or a rapid current, and that they were advancing with banners flying and drums beating. He warned that if they hesitated at this moment and let the days pass in vain, they would not be able to recover even if they bit their own navels in regret. He urged them to quickly send elite soldiers to fundamentally overturn their plans and strike them down, lest the capital of Haruna suddenly collapse into ruin, and pressed them to make preparations.

Upon receiving this report, which mixed truth and falsehood, the Kamutsukasa and Ōkuronushi were startled. As they consulted with the left and right guardians on what to do, the sound of footsteps approached again. Looking to see who it might be, they saw Kerisutan, a scout sent to the land of Karumata, sweating profusely. He reported that Tokoyamihiko, who had entrenched himself in the land of Karumata, was growing in power like a fierce tiger day by day. He was leading a multitude of troops, crossing mountains and fields, and attacking the capital of the Moon, planning to overthrow the castle in one stroke and exterminate the Brahman religion from its very roots. Their plan was clearly visible, as if held in the palm of one's hand.

He wiped his sweat and urged them to send brave, death-defying soldiers as soon as possible, even for a single moment, to strike him down in one blow and avert the disaster of the country, lest they regret it later. It was laughable to see him trembling in fear at the sound of the wind and the cry of cranes as he made his report. Ōkuronushi changed his expression and turned to Ōashiwake to ask what should be done. Ōashiwake puffed out his chest and declared, 'I am a divine general of martial valor. Even if the Lord God Susanoo or Tokoyamihiko were to appear and attack our main castle with the force of a lion, what is there to fear?'

He continued, 'Receiving the divine power of the Brahman religion, I shall chase down, scatter, and mow down all those wretches who dare to oppose us, drive the enemy back a thousand miles, and save the Lord from this crisis. In any case, I shall consult with the various Kamutsukasa and then decide whether to advance or retreat.' With a sour look on his face, he sat down heavily on the floor with his legs crossed and masked his anxiety with a heroic laugh.

Onikumohiko, along with the left guardian Oniharawake, the right guardian Kumoiyawake, Ishionohime, Onikumawake, and four or five other executives, had gathered at the hideout of Ōkuronushi in the grotto of Mount Taiunzan to plot the measures to be taken against the San-go religion and the Ural religion. Onikumohiko had frequently heard urgent reports that the wife and children of Onikumawake had converted to the San-go religion and, as propagandists, had disrupted the religious lines of the Brahman religion. His eyes burned with suspicion, and before anyone knew it, a great barrier had been built and a great trench dug between the two. Onikumawake, too, was unhappy and restless; eventually, he resigned from his post as left guardian and, together with his subordinate Kamutsukasa, hid in his own mansion. He sat in meditation in the shrine where Bontenō was enshrined, praying that the religious leader Ōkuronushi would return to the path of virtue as soon as possible, that the teachings of Daijizaiten would be fully manifested, and that the whereabouts of Kunihikohiko, the child of Ōkuniwake, would be discovered so that he might ascend as the great religious leader of the capital of Haruna. At the same time, he prayed day and night for the whereabouts of his wife and children. However, today, due to a rare summons from Ōkuronushi, he had appeared at this meeting against his will, as he could not refuse the command of his lord. The executives gathered here were all subordinates whom Ōkuronushi relied upon as his right-hand men, and they were all figures of the highest trust. The presence of Onikumawake in this assembly was like mixing rice husks with white rice, or pouring water into oil, and it was inevitable that a sense of estrangement would arise.

§ 2

Ōkuronushi stood up and addressed the assembly,

Ōkuronushi: 'The reason I have summoned you all here today is that an emergency has broken out which cannot be overlooked for even a single day. Originally, our Brahmanism was in the Tokoyojiyō of the land of Tokoyo, and when Ōkuniwake, having received the Shinmei, crossed over to Ejiputo and caused the Shintoku to shine in the Yomo, the hateful Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō attacked our Honjiyō and trampled upon the sacred Seijiyō of the Shin. We, too, being unable to match the enemy in numbers, moved our residence to Kenonkiyō in Mesopotamia together with the Kyōshiyu of Ōkuniwake, and just as we had begun to open the path for the Shingefu, those persistent Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō led a Kototama army, claimed to be the command of Shin Susanoo-no-Mikoto, and attacked us with sudden force. Responding to each other from within and without, they destroyed the Honjiyō of the Brahman again. We had no choice but to swallow our tears, suffer the separation of parents from children and husbands from wives, and finally, together with our loyal and valiant subordinates, we crossed over to Jitentōtō. Just as we were starting the Shingefu again, it was broken by the Kototama of the Shinshi of the Ananai-kyō. Filled with unbearable regret, we gathered the remnants once more, came to this capital, conquered the greater part of the seven thousand and more countries of the Moon Kingdom, and now, with the momentum of the rising sun, we were about to expand the Shingefu to the entire Ashihara-no-Mizuho-no-Kuni and demonstrate the power of the Brahman. Just then, we heard that the evil Shin Susanoo-no-Mikoto, the mastermind who closed the Heavenly Rock Cave, has gathered his subordinates again and, in concert with Kogane-yama, Kōkasu-yama, and Iso-yakata, plans to overthrow our Honjiyō once more. If we do not press upon the enemy's stronghold now and annihilate them, Brahmanism will be like a candle in the wind. Relying upon your loyalty and valor, I desire to eradicate this calamity. Starting with Samori, all of you must embody my will and deliberate on the best method.'

Having declared this, he gave a light glance and hid his figure in the inner room.

Ishiu-no-Hime appeared majestically in the seat after Ōkuronushi had left, and very solemnly,

Ishiu-no-Hime: 'Just as the Great Kyōshiyu has commanded, our religion is facing a crisis of life or death. Quickly concentrate your deliberations, reveal your utmost sincerity, and serve in response to the heart of the Great Kyōshiyu.'

As she declared this, Samori stood up and said,

Samori: 'For the sake of Brahmanism and for the sake of Ōkuronushi, we are prepared to spare neither our lives nor our bodies. Therefore, unless we deliberate carefully, we cannot decide this great problem lightly. I assert this: if we think about it now, there are powerful executives with double hearts lurking within this castle, who are communicating with the Ananai-kyō and the Uraru-kyō, plotting to overthrow our religion by responding to them from within and without. First, unless we investigate these villains, there is a fear that any brilliant plan or strategy will be leaked to the enemy, and the objective will never be achieved.'

He glared with his eyes wide and intentionally stared at the face of Onikumawake. Onikumawake remained calm, not even changing the color of his face as he waited.

Umayoriwake, the Uwamori, stood up, struck the table, and raised his voice,

Umayoriwake: 'Just as Oniharuwake-sama said, it is an obvious fact that the ringleader of the enemy is lurking within this castle. If it were not so, we would not have been able to expand the Brahman religion freely as a secret country of the world until today, moving as if through an uninhabited field. Suddenly, the Kshatriya of each region have raised the flag of rebellion, showing signs of disobedience toward the noble Brahman Shin. At a time when the hearts of the people are restless and there are signs that the disturbances of the world are about to break out, the report that they are coming to attack with many Shingun led from Ubusuna-yama and Karumata-jō cannot be a lie. Let us now punish the ringleader lurking in the castle, make him the first blood sacrifice of our departure, and launch our expedition to subdue the enemy. What do you all present think?'

Onikumawake stood up and said,

Onikumawake: 'What a strange thing to hear. That there is a ringleader in the Honjiyō of Brahmanism who communicates with the enemy—who is that person? Such an evil Shin must be punished as soon as possible, and the calamity of the nation must be removed from the roots; otherwise, no matter how strong our divine power may be, Brahmanism will never reach a place of peace. According to the words of Samori-dono, I heard it as if you know for certain that the ringleader is lurking in this castle. Who is that evil person? Please announce it quickly.'

Before he could finish speaking, Oniharuwake opened his eyes wide with a start, raised his voice, and with his face flushed deep red,

Oniharuwake: 'Be silent, Onikumawake-dono. The very culprit of whom you speak is none other than that wicked and lawless scoundrel, Onikumawake. It goes without saying that there is no one else in this castle but Onikumawake. Confess the circumstances in detail at once and show your sincerity, or else commit seppuku like a man before us.'

Onikuma: 'These are words I cannot comprehend from Samori-dono. On what evidence do you say such things? Though I may be withered and thin, I am Onikumawake, a pillar of the Brahman religion; I cannot let such reckless remarks pass by unheeded.'

Oniharu: 'Ha, ha, ha! To be so brazen as an evildoer is exactly what this is. How well you can utter such transparent lies with a straight face. There is not a single blind person in the castle of Haruna. If you cannot even see through such things, how can you possibly fulfill the important duties of a Samori?'

Oniharu: 'Is your statement based on solid evidence? Come, I wish to hear it. Come, what say you?'

Oniharu: 'Come, what of that?'

Oniharu: 'Come, come, what is it? I shall await your reply.'

Oniharu: 'Come, what of that?'

Oniharu: 'Come, come, come!'

The two men were fired up from both sides. Migimori stood up abruptly and said,

Migimori: 'Aiya, both of you, wait for a moment.'

As he restrained them, the two reluctantly returned to their seats, glaring at each other.

Kumo-yori: 'The words of the Samori are likely nothing more than a conjecture, synthesizing various reports from many scouts, that Onikumawake is surely one who communicates with the enemy. In my opinion, I believe there are two or three reasons why Onikumawake-sama has been subjected to such suspicion. If I were to list them here, first, the fact that Onikumawake-dono's wife, Mukadehime-dono, has now converted to the Sangokyo and is traveling the world as a magnificent missionary—this is the first point that does not please the Lord Daikokunushi, and it is the fuse that ignited the suspicion. ...Secondly, the rumor that Koitohime-dono has crossed to the island of Ryugu, become a queen at Chion-jo, spread the Sangokyo, and even caused his subordinate Tomohiko to convert to the Sangokyo—this is the second cause of suspicion. Next, even though his wife and children are devoted to the Sangokyo and there is no longer any prospect of them returning to the Brahman religion, he continues to live a bachelor's life, which makes it appear as though he intends to build a family again with his wicked and lawless wife and children—this is the third seed of suspicion. Next, when the Lord Daikokunushi reprimanded Onikumahime-sama for her impropriety and ordered her to retire to a separate residence, Onikumawake-dono adopted an extremely rebellious attitude toward this and harbors ill feelings toward the new wife, Ishinohime-sama—this is also a seed of suspicion. A deep chasm has been dug between the Lord Daikokunushi and Onikumawake, and they lack mutual understanding. ...Next, even though he holds the power of military command, he receives the trust of many of the country's people despite having no military force, unlike the Lord Daikokunushi who holds both military and religious authority. I suspect that this, too, has become a seed of suspicion. However, I believe that you, as one who serves the Gods, would not have the spirit to sow such seeds of suspicion... This is what I believe. Now is not the time to repeat such internal strife. It is a national crisis where we must prepare for the enemy's invasion as soon as possible and destroy the enemy from the roots; therefore, shall we not discard minor differences, unite for the greater cause, and work together in harmony to prepare for this national emergency? This is the unfeigned sincere confession—no, advice—of Migimori.'

He spoke with dignity, defending Onikumawake against the false charges, and then sat down. Oniharuwake stood up again with a displeased look,

Oniharuwake: 'I truly cannot comprehend the words of Migimori; we cannot agree at all. In that case, Migimori-dono, I leave Onikumawake's person entirely to you, so I ask that you supervise him so that there will be no mistakes.'

雲依『左守の仰せは数多の斥候どもの種々の注進を綜合して、これは正しく鬼熊別が、敵方に款を通ずる者ならむとの推定に過ぎますまい。私が考へますには鬼熊別様の斯かる嫌疑を受けられたのも二三の原因があるだらうと思ひます。今茲に羅列すれば、先づ第一に鬼熊別殿の妻蜈蚣姫殿は今は三五教に帰順し、堂々たる宣伝使となつて天下を布教し居らるる事、これが第一大黒主さまの御気勘に叶はぬ点で疑惑の起る導火線で厶る。……又第二は小糸姫殿が竜宮の一つ島へ渡り、地恩城に於て女王となり三五教を拡め、部下の友彦までも三五教に帰順せしめたるとの噂、これが第二の疑の原因。次には妻子は三五教に心酔し、最早バラモン教に復帰する形勢もなきに、何時までも独身生活を続け、悪虐無道の妻子と再び家庭を作らむとの御所存と見える、これが第三の疑をまく種。次には大黒主さまが鬼雲姫さまの不都合を詰り、別宅を造りて退隠を命じ給うた時、之に対して極力反抗的態度を用ひ、新夫人の石生能姫に対し悪感情を抱き居らるる事、これも亦疑惑の種。大黒主と鬼熊別との間には深き溝渠が穿たれ、意思の疎通を欠きし事。……次には兵馬の権を握り、片手に教権を掌握し玉ふ大黒主よりも、武力なき身を以て、数多の国人の信用を受け居らるる事。これ亦疑惑の種となつて居るのだらうと私は推察致します。併し乍ら神に仕へ給ふ身を以て左様な疑惑の種を蒔く如き御精神では厶いますまい………と私は信ずるのであります。今は斯様な内紛を繰返す時では厶らぬ。一時も早く外に向つて敵の襲来に備へ、且つ敵を根底より滅亡させねばならぬ国家の危機だから、小異を棄て、大同に合し、協心戮力して此国家の大事に備へようではありませぬか。これ右守が偽らざる至誠の告白否忠告で厶る』

と堂々として鬼熊別の寃罪を弁護しつつ説き来り説き去り座に着いた。鬼春別は不機嫌顔にて再び立上り、

『如何にも心得ぬ右守の御言葉、吾々は一向合点が参らぬ。然らば右守どの、鬼熊別の一身に就ては、貴殿に一任しますから、キツト過ちのなき様に御監督を願ひます』

§ 4

Kumoyori: 'Although it is a great task that I never expected to be under the supervision of Onikumawake-sama, whose divine virtue is high, in this case today, it cannot be helped; I shall accept the supervision as you command.'

Onikuma: 'This I cannot understand. If we were wicked and lawless rebels, it would be another matter, but for what reason do you supervise us, who are loyal retainers and righteous warriors? Though I cannot bear the extreme indignation of being suspected of something I did not do, I am considering the great emergency of the state and am practicing patience and self-restraint; I ask that you refrain from such concern toward me.'

Iso: 'Please leave this matter to me. Oh, Onikumawake-sama, I have caused you great worry. In the present world, sincere people are oppressed and doubted, and it is an age where great villains flourish, so on the other hand, there will surely be something good for you in being subjected to such suspicion. Please cheer up, and from today onward, I ask that you come to the castle and serve daily for the sake of the state and for the sake of the teachings.'

Onikumawake, secretly shedding tears of gratitude at the words of Isonohime, said:

Onikumawake: 'Yes, thank you very much. Then, I shall follow your words and decide to report for duty from tomorrow. I humbly ask for your forgiveness for my impropriety until now.'

Isonohime changed her tone sternly and said:

Isonohime: 'Samorikiharunowake-dono shall depart for the mansion of Iso, leading his subordinate soldiers. Also, Ōashiwake shall lead his troops and advance to attack the main castle of the Ural religion in the Karumata country. Until Kiharu-wake returns in triumph, Onikumawake-dono shall serve as the Left Guardian as before. The Right Guardian shall remain as before; all of you, be mysterious, unite your hearts, join hands, and engage in divine service. By the order of the Great Pontiff, I, Isonohime, shall execute the power of proxy.'

At these words, everyone bowed their heads and showed their consent, saying:

Everyone: 'Hahaa.'

Incidentally, the Right Guardian Kumoyoriwake could not resist the trend of the times and was outwardly in agreement with the Left Guardian, but in reality, he was moved by the beautiful heart and day-to-day actions of Onikumawake, and in his heart, he secretly revered and respected Onikumawake. Therefore, he pitied the innocence of Onikumawake and spoke in his defense. Also, Isonohime was secretly in love with the manly appearance of Onikumawake, who possessed a dignified bearing. For that reason, she exercised her proxy power to make Onikumawake, whom the Daikokunushi did not particularly like, the Left Guardian, stopped the Right Guardian who was showing sympathy for Onikumawake, and sent off Kiharu-wake and Ōashiwake, who were always slandering Onikumawake. It is said that a woman's beauty can topple a castle. Indeed, there is nothing more terrifying than a woman's position. Even the Daikokunushi was wrapped in the clouds of love for this Isonohime, and regardless of good or evil, he never disobeyed her by even a single word or phrase.

因に右守の雲依別は時の勢に抗し難く、左守と表面バツを合せてゐたが、其実鬼熊別の美はしき心と日夜の行動に感激し、心中潜かに鬼熊別を畏敬尊信してゐた。それ故鬼熊別の無辜を憐れみ弁解的弁論をまくしたてたのである。又石生能姫は鬼熊別のどこともなく男らしく、威儀備はる容貌に、心私かに恋着してゐた。それ故大黒主の余り好まぬ鬼熊別を代理権を執行して左守となし、鬼熊別に同情をよせつつある右守を止め、常に鬼熊別を讒言する鬼春別、大足別を出陣させて了つたのであつた。女の美貌は城を傾くるとか云ふ。実に女位恐ろしきものはない。大黒主も此石生能姫には恋愛の雲に包まれて、善悪に関らず、一言半句も背いた事はなかつたのである。
