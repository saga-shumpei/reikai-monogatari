Chapter 8: Messenger 者 [1092]

使者〔1092〕

§ 1

Kerisu and Tāukusu, the two, keeping the command of Terukuniwake with joy and courage, mounted their chestnut horses, dashed out of the forest of Kurusu, and headed straight for Ōnohara. As the wind blew, they combed their hair and rushed forward, leaping over valleys and crossing mountains. The voices of insects gathering in the autumn fields sounded very sorrowful. As they followed the path through the wilderness, their hearts were filled with vigor and their spirits were courageous, and it was valiant of them to advance toward the rock cave of Mount Kiyoharu.

At the foot of Mount Kiyoharu, the two left their horses and, while climbing the rugged slope, sang the Sendenka of Ananai-kyō as they went.

They sang and sang as they advanced.

Kerisu and Tāukusu, the two, tethered their horses at the foot of Mount Kiyoharu and, while facing the fierce mountain wind, climbed on, putting their strength into every step with cries of 'Eiya, eiya.' Kerisu sang along the way.

Kerisu: 'When Kami appears in the world, to distinguish between Zenshin and Jiyashin, though it is said that man is the child of Kami and the palace of Kami, how can the human body judge Kami? The Kamuzukasa of Ananai-kyō, Kamususanōnōokami, with His infinite Jinji and Goseitoku, has appeared as the Kami of 567. Though it is heard that the Kamuzukasa of Daikokunushi, who governs the Brahman religion, is a noble Kami, when one traces the source, the Kamuzukasa of Ōkunibetsu, a descendant of Ōkunihiko, the Tokoyogami-ō Jizaiten born in the land of Tokoyo, opened the teachings. This orthodoxy is the teaching that Kunibetsuhiko, the noble child, appeared to protect and govern the Brahman religion. However, Magatsuhiko appeared, banished Kunibetsuhiko, posed as the leader of the religion, changed his name to Daikokunushi, and appeared in the capital of the moon as a Magatsukami. Good and evil are clearly understood by this. When one traces and investigates in detail the source of Ururu-no-hiko, who follows the teachings of Ururu, he is one who, while alienating the orthodox lineage of the Banko-shinō, the divine pillar of Shionagahiko who opened this world, arrogantly claims to be the descendant of Hachiō-ōkami. Ururu-no-hiko and Ururu-hime deceive others by claiming to be the true lineage, spreading the teachings of crookedness far and wide, and throwing this world into chaos. The cruelty of their methods is such. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. May the spirits be blessed, and may the barriers of Ananai-kyō, the Ururu religion, and the Brahman religion be removed as quickly as possible. May we obey the one teaching of Kuninotokotachi-ōkami, who created and solidified heaven and earth, and for the sake of Kami and for the sake of the world, may the world unite and fulfill sincerity as quickly as possible. Please protect us, before the noble Kami of Ananai. Kerisu and Tāukusu, the two, pray with reverence and awe. Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, if we protect the one teaching of sincerity of Ananai, what could possibly not be accomplished? What kind of crookedness should we fear? Kami is with us. Man is the child of Kami, the palace of Kami. This noble divine instruction is the teaching of Ananai-kyō. Compared to the Brahman religion, there is a difference as vast as heaven and earth. The sun and moon shine clearly in the sky, illuminating our heads, and with the breath of Kami, the clouds of doubt hanging over our hearts are blown away, and they shine purely. Kamunaobi, who created this world, and Ōnaobi, whose heart is vast, say: in the human world, look again and listen again with Naobi. Correct your own mistakes. The Kamuzukasa who protects such clear teachings, Terukuniwake, has forgiven our hundred sins, saved our lives, and cared for us. Without doubting us, the still-fresh believers, even a little, he has entrusted us with such a noble mission. His heart is like the sea, and his soul is like the sky. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. Because the protection of Kami is deep, the hearts of the Brahman followers who were lost until now have completely awakened, and what joy it is to serve the teachings of the Kami of supreme benevolence and supreme love. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. May the spirits be blessed.'

Singing thus, they were climbing the steep slope of Mount Kiyoharu. They had already reached the third station of the mountain. From here on, the slope became increasingly steep, the path grew worse, and it was a difficult road that could not be climbed easily. Tāukusu, putting strength into his fingertips with every step, began to sing while keeping time, gasping for breath as he climbed.

§ 2

Untokodotsukoi, haa haa haa, fuu fuu fuu fuu, it is hard to breathe. The cliff and precipice are a difficult path. If I were to fall in a place like this, my body would instantly tumble into the thousand-fathom valley. My head would be crushed, my arms broken, and my hands, feet, and whole body would become a mess, and I would likely become a delicious meal for the wild beasts, untokosho.

Untokodotsukoi, haa haa haa. Listen, Kerisu, be careful. The path ahead is a difficult barrier. You must fulfill the command of Terukuniwake successfully, untokosho. If you do not return and report the completion of your duty, you will not be able to repay the grace of having your life saved, untokosho. Once again, a fierce wind blows.

Shall I climb while gripping the trees around here tightly, putting strength into my fingertips? If I am blown by such a fierce, dokkoisho, winter wind, my body feels as if it will scatter. The fellows of Poro and Shamu must be, at this time, dokkoisho, with their eyes shining, untokosho. The officials of Ōdaruwake have set out for the land of Karumata, and while their master is away, they are cooking in the back room, sitting cross-legged, untokosho, dokkoisho, dokkoisho. They take out delicious sake, drink until they are full, and ramble on, untokosho, becoming dead drunk and shaking their heads left and right like a papier-mache tiger, untokosho.

They must be telling tall tales. I do not know what is written in the letter of Terukuniwake, but the fellow Poro will surely be surprised, dokkoisho. Untokodotsukoi, I cannot help but imagine his face as if I am seeing it right before my eyes. Whether it be rain, hail, or wind, or the rain of earthquakes, thunder, and fire, there is no doubt that confusion, dokkoisho, will arise. At that time, Kerisu, dokkoisho, you must be firm and not be deceived by the evil spirits. Once you have realized the true teachings, untokosho, haa haa haa haa, you must not turn back on the path of God.

The path that is hard to climb is the slope. Advancing on the path of truth is like climbing this slope, untokodotsukoi. If there is even a moment of carelessness, you will tumble back, gara gara gara gara. You must not ride the cart of sharp, pointed stones and fall to the bottom of the valley, dokkoisho. When God appears in the world and distinguishes between good and evil, right and wrong, the merit of that, dokkoisho, will ring out widely across heaven and earth. Like thunder, dokkoisho, it will awaken the sleeping, drive away the crookedness of the heart, and save us in the palace of God, which is like a crystal ball. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara, dokkoisho, dokkoisho, divine spirits.

May you bless us, and may you make our firm hearts even firmer, O great pillars of the officials of the three-five God. I pray before the presence of the great God, Kami-Susanoo-Ōkami. Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, untokodotsukoi, even if the mountains split and the seas dry up, do not forget the teachings of the three-five God that you have once realized. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara, Kerisu, steady your spirit and hold on for a little while longer. If you climb further, there is a rock cave. If you see the faces of Poro and Shamu and turn back as before, falling into Brahmanism, you will surely incur the wrath of God.

I am your close friend. Because I care for you, I will warn you here for a moment, even though I know it may be tedious. Untokodotsukoi, dokkoisho. Aitata-tsuta, aitatsuta. Have you forgotten to watch your footing because you were talking too much? The sharp stones, untokosho, have put me on the cart of the slope, and I have struck my knee hard. The red blood, dokkoisho, is flowing out, tara tara tara. When I look at the color of this blood, my soul must surely have been purified. The fresh blood spurts out, and the eastern sky glows crimson, like the great Sun God. I submit to the path of truth.

If you keep your Nikutai active from morning till evening, your Kinniku will gradually function, and the Ketsueki circulation will improve. Untokodotsukoi, dotsukoishiyo, there will be no sorrow of Utsuketsu. At last, the Riyōnin arrived in front of the Ihaya guarded by Pōro, Shamu, and others. The Futari stood quietly at the entrance of the Ihaya, and when they secretly peered at the Yōsu inside, they could hear the noisy voices of Avici and hellish suffering coming from the depths. Kērisu and Tārisu, the Riyōnin, folded their arms and tilted their heads, saying,

Kērisu and Tārisu: 'Hatena.'
