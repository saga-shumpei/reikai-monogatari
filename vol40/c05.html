Chapter 5: 忍ぶ恋 [1089]

忍ぶ恋〔1089〕

§ 1

Onikumawake and Isonohime sat in the inner room, and as they looked at each other, a curtain of silence fell for a while.

Onikumawake, within his heart, remained silent and waited for Isonohime to begin speaking, thinking that despite the report from Kumahiko the night before, and despite the fact that the great bridge had been destroyed on the way, it must be for some deep reason that she, as a woman, had come to visit him alone without attendants and without regard for her status.

Isonohime, too, was overwhelmed by the solemn attitude of Onikumawake as if it were something new, and the many things that had been piling up in her heart to express had vanished somewhere before she knew it; not knowing what words to utter, she tilted her head in a state of hesitation and agitation, and remained silently looking down.

Thus, for about a quarter of an hour, time passed mercilessly in silence.

As if making up her mind, Isonohime blushed slightly and said, 'Isonohime: "Regarding my visiting your residence alone, without attendants, as a woman, to see you who live a life of celibacy, I am sure it must be a great nuisance to you. Your wife and daughter have joined the San-go-kyo, and you alone keep your austere life, continuing your celibacy, and laboring day and night for the sake of the Path and the country; I am truly filled with admiration for your noble resolve."'

Onikumawake finally opened his mouth and said, 'Onikumawake: "What is your business? Please tell me quickly. If I am suspected by Lord Daikokunushi again, it will be a mutual nuisance; come, tell me the nature of your business quickly."'

As he urged her, Isonohime said sadly, in a tearful voice, 'Isonohime: "Yes, I do not know where to begin; until just now, my heart was full of things I wanted to say, but upon seeing your solemn figure, they suddenly hid themselves somewhere. Please let me speak slowly, so I ask you to listen with patience."'

Isonohime continued, 'Isonohime: "As you know, I have been subjected to various suspicions by Lord Daikokunushi, and even stripped of my sacred office of Samori; somehow, an indescribable ominous cloud has been drifting between us, and the atmosphere has been so bleak that it felt as if rain, wind, or thunder could break out at any moment. However, during the consultation on the conquest of the foreign religion yesterday, I was reappointed as Samori through your intercession, which is an honor beyond my station, but I do not know if this will instead become a great disaster for me. If the appointment were from the bottom of Lord Daikokunushi's heart, I would gladly accept it, but even though it is an execution of proxy authority, Lord Daikokunushi surely cannot trust me. I thought of declining immediately on the spot, but that would cause friction and make a peaceful resolution difficult. Furthermore, with a formidable enemy ahead and most of our military forces deployed elsewhere, Haruna Castle has become weak in its defense; thinking that acting out the folly of brothers fighting within the walls would be a disadvantage to the Path, I swallowed the words of refusal that were on my lips and accepted the position of Samori, suppressing my regret. To tell you the truth, my heart is already weary of this fleeting world, and I desire neither status, fame, wealth, nor a wife. I have long wished to hide in the mountains and forests, making the clear wind and the bright moon my friends and spending the rest of my life there, but seeing the internal situation of the Brahman religion today, I cannot do such a selfish thing. I feel there is no greater sin of unfilial piety toward the Great God than that, so I have decided to accept the duty against my will. And you, was there anything unusual on your way?"' Onikumawake replied, 'Onikumawake: "Yes, there was nothing particularly unusual, but on my way here, the Tsukumo Bridge had been struck down by someone, so I had no choice but to go about a ri downstream, cross the Hyakudai Bridge, and visit your mansion. According to what was said on the way, though I do not know if you will be offended by my saying this, there is a rumor that it was struck down by someone of your kin for some reason. I have come here actually worried that this matter might reach the ears of Lord Daikokunushi."'

§ 2

Another seed of Kengi has increased. I am already prepared for Nanigoto. My life is entrusted to the Kamisama for Itsusaibanji, and no matter what Sainan may befall me, I feel not the slightest fear. However, there is something I cannot Gaten regarding your coming here alone. It is a matter I cannot Gaten, that a person like Onikumohiko-sama, who is so deep in Shitsutoshin and who does not leave your side even for a moment, would allow you to go out alone, and on this day of all days. Is it not that you have had a Fūfugenkuwa and slipped out of the Jiyōnai?

No, never, never. I have obtained the Riyōkai of my husband and have come here alone in secret.

Well, this is becoming even more difficult to Gaten. There must be some deep Keiriyaku in this... No, Isonohime-dono, to speak plainly, it is even disgusting to speak to a Dokufu like you.

Oh, what are you saying? Do you hate me that much? Why, for what reason...

Even if I do not state the reason, if you ask your own heart, you will surely understand. Try to think well. You have cast out the precious Okusama, sat yourself shamelessly in her Atogama, and are showing off your queenly face with Heiki. That behavior of yours is not pleasing to Onikumowake. Because you do such things, you have incurred the wrath of the Kamisama, and the San-go-kyo and the Ural-kyo have come to attack toward the capital of Haruna. Repent of your past sins as soon as possible, and for the sake of apologizing to the Okusama, and for the sake of the conversion of Daikokunushi-sama, end your life with a noble suicide. Without that much sincerity, this divine work can never be accomplished.

He said this in a stern manner, as if scolding her. At the intensity of his demeanor, Isonohime had no words to return, and she collapsed to the spot in tears with a cry.

After a while, she wiped her eyes, lifted her face, and said in a thin voice,

Your words are truly appropriate. I feel the same as you, and I cannot tell you how much my heart aches regarding this matter.

Why do you do things that make your heart ache so much? With just one decision of yours, could it not be otherwise?

I do not know if I will be looked down upon as an unchaste woman for speaking of my husband's shame, but now that it has come to this, I must tell you everything. Please, listen for a while. I was originally the daughter of a believer of the San-go-kyo, and my name was Isono-ko. Separated from my parents in my childhood, while I was wandering here and there, Daikokunushi-sama, in the middle of a hunting walk, saw me once and told me to come to his house, took me back, and I was treated kindly by the couple and served as a maid to the Okusama. One night, though it is shameful to say, due to the unreasonable demands of Daikokunushi-sama, and knowing it was wrong to the Okusama, I yielded to the demands of Daikokunushi-sama because of the weakness of a woman's heart, not wanting to spoil his mood. From then on, my master began to pull me to his side from morning till night and speak only of complaints against the Okusama. I could not stand it, so I offered my opinions in various ways, but he would not listen, and in the end, he drove the Okusama out just like that. I have also been slandered by the world in various ways, and finding no reason to live and feeling ashamed to walk outside, I have decided to commit suicide many times to show the purity of my heart and apologize to the Okusama, but a voice was heard from the middle of the sky, telling me to wait, so I have prolonged my unpleasant days until today. However, treacherous people and flatterers are rampant in the mansion, and those who report on your situation to Daikokunushi in a bad light increase in number every day. Since my master is a short-tempered man as you know, he has often threatened to bind you and subject you to severe punishment. Thinking of this, I cannot die even if I were to die now. Taking advantage of the fact that my master will listen to what I say no matter what, I have been warning him until today, thinking it would be a disaster to lose the pillar of the Brahman religion so easily, and I have been protecting you from the shadows. Please, I ask you to demonstrate your sincerity for the sake of the path and the country once more, and protect this religion and the country with me. If I were to commit suicide and die now, your situation would immediately become dangerous. No, no, you are a divine official with unparalleled divine power, so you would not be struck down easily, but in these days of intense internal trouble and external pressure, when two tigers are grinding their teeth against each other, neither side will remain whole. Whichever one is wounded and falls, the destruction of the Brahman religious country will be clearer than seeing fire. I beg you to understand the logic of this and understand my spirit. I came here saying that I would probe your inner thoughts, smoothing over the situation before my master, because I wanted to consult you about this matter.

§ 3

She told him the Ichibushijifu with tears in her eyes. Onikumawake softened his Kahoiro slightly,

Onikumawake: 'Isonohime-dono, was it so? Not knowing of such pure and noble intentions, I have until now looked down upon you and hated you as a Dokufu and a Kanpu; this is truly due to my own Fumei. Please, forgive me. Whatever one may say, in this world of Ankoku, the depths of the human heart are Tautei impossible to understand. I am the same; it is likely that the rift between myself and Ōkuronushi-sama, which should have been Enman, was entirely due to mutual Gokai after being slandered by Amata Neijin. Ōkuronushi-sama should not have been such an evil person, but it must be that he was caught off guard when he felt a bit of Goanshin, and his Mitama was attacked by a Magakami. Ah, I wish to somehow drive away that Akuma.'

Isonohime: 'Hai, thank you; you have said it well. Please, thinking of the path and the country, endure your Fuyukuwai and Gotojiyō to fulfill your duties as a Sakimori completely. I will provide my support from within, as much as I am able. However, I must tell you in advance that Ōkuronushi-sama will not be easily converted. Unless Oniharawake, who headed to the mansion of Iso, and Ōashiwake, who headed to the country of Karumata, are defeated and meet their end, that stubborn will of his will Tautei not be broken. Please, I ask you to go to your post with the great resolve to risk your life and pass through the blades for the sake of the path and the country. Fortunately, I have the favor of Ōkuronushi, so that part is very convenient. I shall arrange for Onikumahime-sama to be restored as the original wife when the opportunity arises. Regarding that, I alone could not possibly succeed, so shall you and I join forces to first purify the castle of Haruna and drive away the Akuma?'

Onikumawake: 'For a woman to have such resolve, I am truly impressed. If that is the case, I too shall be moved by your sincerity and risk my life to undertake a great reform. I pray for your support from within; in truth, although you may not know it, last night, under the secret orders of Ōkuronushi-sama, several dozen of his attendants attacked my mansion, attempting to take my life under the cover of night. Having heard of that plot from someone, and through the arrangements of the Karo Kumahiko, I had that bridge dropped; therefore, I am prepared for a problem to arise between Ōkuronushi-sama and myself sooner or later.'

Isonohime heard this, was surprised and stunned, and while trembling, said,

Isonohime: 'Soriya, is that the truth? It was about to become a terrible situation. Ya, I shall return now and investigate it without making it obvious. I will never let such a difficult situation befall you again... Ah, I wish to stay longer, but if I stay too long, it will sow seeds of suspicion again; it is Namagoshi-oshi, but I will take my leave now...'

She watched Onikumawake with a strange look in her eyes. Ah, that even such a righteous-hearted Isonohime should have a heart lost in love—it is well said that there is no distinction between high and low. Onikumawake, not knowing in his dreams that Isonohime had such feelings, shed tears of joy, gripped Isonohime's hand tightly, and said,

Onikumawake: 'Kore, Hime-sama, please take great care. Your body is a precious existence; as long as you and I join our strengths, the capital of Haruna will be safe.'

He gripped her hand even more firmly. Isonohime, having her hand held tightly by Onikumawake, whom she had long admired, felt her heart pounding with joy. She extended her slender hand uncertainly, gripped Onikumawake's hand back with all her might, looked up at his face with a sidelong glance, and with a single drop of tears like rain, looking back again and again with reluctance, she left the mansion quietly. Onikumawake saw the Hime off to the entrance, bade her farewell there, and promised to attend his duties without fail in the afternoon, bidding her a temporary farewell. Later, Onikumawake faced the shrine, recited a Norito of gratitude and prayer, and... Ah, has the Brahman God not yet abandoned us? Arigata, Mottainaya... he joined his hands and shed tears of gratitude like a waterfall.

『石生能姫殿、左様で厶つたか。かかる清き尊きお志とは知らず、今まで貴女を毒婦、奸婦と見くびり憎んで居りましたのは誠に私が不明の致す所、どうぞ赦して下さい。何を言ふも暗黒の世の中、到底人間の心の底は分るものでは厶いませぬ。私だつて其通り、数多の侫人ばらに讒訴され、円満なるべき大黒主様との仲に垣が出来たのも全く互の誤解からで厶いませう。大黒主様もあの様な悪い方ではなかつた筈ですが、何時の間にやら稍御安心なさつた虚に乗じ、曲神に身魂を襲はれ給うたので厶りませう。あゝ何とかして其悪魔を退散させたいもので厶いますなア』

『ハイ有難う、よく云つて下さいました。どうぞあなたはお道の為、国の為と思召し、不愉快を忍んで、御登城下さいまして、左守としての職責を完全にお尽し下さいませ。私が及ばずながら内助の労を取りますから。併し乍ら前以て申上げておきますが、大黒主様は中々容易に改心は出来ますまい。イソの館に向つた鬼春別やカルマタ国に向つた大足別が一敗地にまみれ、往生をした上でなくては、到底あのキツイ我は折れますまい。どうぞ、あなたはお道の為、国の為に身を挺して刃の中をくぐる大覚悟を以て御出勤を願ひます。幸ひ私は大黒主の寵愛を得て居りますから、其段は大変に好都合で厶ります。折を見て鬼雲姫様を元の奥様に直つて貰ふやうに取計らひませう。それに付いては到底私一人の力で及びますまいから、あなたと私と力を併せて、ハルナの城内を先づ清め、悪魔を郤けようでは厶いませぬか』

『そこ迄の女の貴女の御決心、イヤもう実に感服仕りました。左様なれば、私も貴女の真心に感じ、身を挺して大改革にかかりませう。何卒御内助を願ひます様、実の所昨夜あなたは御存じなけれども、大黒主様の御内命にて近侍の者等数十名、吾館へ襲来し、夜陰に紛れて吾命を奪はむと致されました。其計略を或者より承はり、家老の熊彦が計らひにて、あの橋を落させておいた様な次第で厶いますから、何れ大黒主様より一問題が私に対し持上がるものと、覚悟は致して居ります』

石生能姫はこれを聞いて驚き呆れ、身を震はしながら、

『ソリヤまあ真実で厶いますか、大変な事になるとこで厶いました。ヤ私がこれから帰りまして、それとはなしに探つてみませう。又あなたに難題のかかるやうなことは決してさせませぬから………あゝモウ暫く御邪魔が致したいので厶いますが、余り長くなると又疑惑の種を蒔きますから、お名残惜しう厶いますが、これにて失礼致します…………』

と妙な目使ひにて鬼熊別を見守つた。あゝ斯くの如き心正しき石生能姫も恋には迷ふ心の闇、上下の隔なしとはよく云つたものである。鬼熊別は石生能姫に左様な心ありとは、夢にも知らず、嬉し涙を流しながら、石生能姫の手を固く握り、

『コレ姫様、随分気を付けなさいませ。貴女の体は大切なお身の上、私と貴女と力を合はして居りさへすれば、ハルナの都は大丈夫で厶います』

と一層強く手を握りしめた。石生能姫は日頃思ひ込んだ鬼熊別に手を固く握られ、嬉しさに胸を轟かせ、覚束なげに細き手を伸べて、力限り鬼熊別の手を握り返し、流し目に顔を見上げて、ホロリと一雫涙の雨と共に名残惜しげに後振返り振返り、館をしづしづと立つて帰り行く。鬼熊別は玄関口まで姫を見送り、そこにて別れを告げ、午後は必ず参勤すべきことを約して、暫しの別れを告げた。後に鬼熊別は神殿に向ひ、感謝祈願の祝詞を奏上し………あゝ未だバラモン神は吾等を捨て給はざるか、有難し勿体なや………と両手を合せ、感謝の涙を滝の如く流すのであつた。
