Chapter 9: 雁 Messenger [1093]

雁使〔1093〕

§ 1

At the border of Fusa and the Moon, in the north of Afghanistan, stands Mount Kiyoharuyama, towering to press upon the clouds. It is known far and wide as the first and most impregnable fortress and barrier of the Brahmanic Kyō. By the command of Ōkuronushi, the divine pillar of Mount Kiyoharuyama, Ōdaruwake, led a great number of soldiers to the capital of the land of Karumata. He went forth to slaughter in a single battle the group of Tokoyamihiko, the descendants of the soul of the Hiko of Ural, and to protect the peace of the Brahmanic Kyō. After he departed, the place became a hollow shell, and he made the impregnable, isolated stronghold into a power, appointing Poro as the guardian of the temporary cavern.

After he had departed, the cavern fell into a state where both system and order were completely ruined. Day and night, the dozen or so guards engaged in a vigorous Giuinbashiyokukuwai, rotting their own entrails. However, when the divine ministers of San-iku-kyō, the party of Terukuniwake, radiated the Kototama lines upon them, they fled in confusion, running hither and thither. The propaganda envoys, who were rushing ahead while protecting their parents and younger sister, overlooked this miserable state. After they returned, it became the same old drinking and feasting party again. Drink, make merry, sing, and dance! The moment ahead is a dark night. After the darkness, the moon will rise. The moon is the moon, but it is the end of one's fortune.

The divine pillars of the Brahmanic Kyō, who are fickle, hesitant, and deceitful, say: 'Whether we win or lose in battle, the rise and fall of the nation is not a matter for us to concern ourselves with. To drink sake from morning till night, to eat sweet things, and to live in ease while passing the days—that is the path of civilized humanity.' Deteriorating into something like the Ural Kyō, Poro, Rēru, and the others, including Hāru, Eruma, Shayamu, and Kiruku, along with the remaining believers, fought over the sake, saying it would be a loss not to drink. While becoming dead drunk and unsteady on their loins, they crawled around inside the cavern, revealing the true form of great serpents, and it was laughable to see them rampaging in madness.

Poro, while forcing his tangled tongue to move, said: 'Hey, Rēru, we have finally had the old man and the old woman taken back, and the party of propaganda envoys of San-iku-kyō, whom we had gone to such lengths to trap, has also been completely snatched away due to the betrayal of Yatsukosu. It can be said that our mission is now at an end. I was holding on in this lonely cavern to keep that couple confined, to make them talk the iris into submission from their own mouths, and to make her the wife of Ōdaruwake-sama, but now that it has come to this, there is no help for it. Shall we not return to the main building? Even if Ōdaruwake-sama is absent, the divine minister Kodaruwake is still there with many subordinates to guard it, so let us retreat to that point. If we dawdle and that propaganda envoy fellow comes back to strike us, then this time, Poro might truly be beaten to a pulp. That is why, while there is still time, I am letting you study and perform this Giuinbashiyokukuwai with special exceptions, so that you may drink the juice of Poro and have no regrets later. Lately, you have been quite unstudious, have you not? At first, you would drink four or five to of sake with only sixteen people, but what is this? Lately, seventeen or eighteen of you gather and get dead drunk on a mere one to and five or six shō of sake. At this rate, there is no telling when the sake in this cavern will run out. Rēru, why don't you whip everyone into shape and make them a little more active?'

Rēru replied: 'I am always encouraging you to the point of derailing from the rail, so it is no use telling me to study any further. If there were some Ural Kyō fellows here, and we added five or six new ones to the group and let them drink, it would certainly be quite efficient. If you are going to drink sake, drink it from a kettle; if you do not like the kettle, drink it from the mouth of a jar. If you do not like the mouth of a jar, jump in and drink it. Drink it in such a way that the Shōjō fellow is terrified and runs away in astonishment. Drink, drink, drink a lot! The more you drink, the more it becomes your own virtue. Decadent show, decadent show... if we did that, it would be quite interesting, would it not? Aahn.'

Poro: 'Oi, Shamu, are you not studying your drinking lately? You are just eating rice and acting like a slob, Shamu-Shamu; can you really become an executive of the Giuintau like that? If you dawdle, the Ananai-kyō might arrive while the sake is still here. If that happens, although we are not of the Ananai-kyō, we are followers of Muteikaushiyugi, so if sweet sake remains, a sense of Shifuchiyakushin will linger and we will not be able to escape cleanly. Is it not rather thoughtless to let the enemy drink our sake?'

Shamu: 'Nah, the Ananai-kyō are Ningen just the same. Even if that guy drinks it, sweet sake is still sweet. It might go much smoother than having the guys from the Ural-kyō help us. No matter how much they drink, since they are Ananai-kyō, there is no fear of it sitting heavy in their stomachs, and unlike us, there is no danger of them becoming completely mentally incapacitated. Say, Haru, when the Ananai-kyō comes... I will say: "Well, well, welcome. Since our great Ontaishiyō is Fuzai, we cannot offer you the finest poisoned sake, and this is not the best sake we have, but how about a cup?" When I do that, there is no one who will not smile at the sight of sake; no matter how much they are Ananai-kyō, they will immediately relax their Saugau, and with a gurgling sound in their throats... "Oh, what an unexpected Gochisō I have received," they will say, and once they narrow their eyes and gulp down one cup, it is all over. One cup is sweet, two cups are also sweet, three cups are still sweet, and with four, five, a hundred, a thousand cups, in the end, they will lose their composure, forget all about the Ananai-kyō, and are bound to become members of the Giuinbashokukai. Once that happens, we will try not to drink much ourselves. We just need to time it for when they are sufficiently drunk and bury every one of them who comes into that pit; even if a hundred or two hundred Ananai-kyō come, there is no need to be surprised. Is that not a brilliant plan?'

As they were talking, Eruma and Kiruku, who had been passed out drunk until now, were moved by something and rose up with a start.

Eruma and Kiruku: 'Koriya, you lot, you all tried to kill us two with a scheme, did you? I am desperate enough to die!'

Thinking their dreams were reality and harboring such delusions, they immediately rushed into the inner room, drew their long swords with a flourish, and slashed indiscriminately at the group of Poro, Rēru, Shamu, Haru, and ten or twelve others. Some had their cheek flesh carved away, some had the tips of their noses sliced off, some had their ears lopped off, their arms cut, and their fingers sent flying.

Poro and others: 'Hey, hey, what are you doing?'

They shouted this while defending themselves with sake bottles, bowls, cups, and trays. The sounds of bottles and bowls breaking—pachi-pachi, gachiya, gachiya—and the roars and shouts of 'Un!', 'Kyaa!', 'Aita!' arose all at once and could be heard even outside the cave.

Kērisu and Tākusu, wondering what sudden emergency had occurred, advanced deep into the cave while watching their footing, only to find the inside of the cave transformed into a scene of agony and carnage, a veritable battlefield.

Kērisu immediately raised a voice like thunder.

Kērisu: 'Kora!'

He shouted with one word. It seemed that the power of the Ananai-kyō was imbued in that Kototama from nowhere, and with that voice, Eruma and Kiruku dropped their swords with a clatter, sat down hard on their bottoms, and fell over backward.

The group led by Poro also dropped all the weapons they held with a clatter at the sound of that voice, and they all fell over backward as well.

All of them, struck by the power of Kērisu's Kototama, had their bodies stiffened, and they could only roll their heads around, groaning with pale faces.

Tākusu began to sing a propaganda song in a clear voice.

Tākusu: 'Receiving the command of Ōkuninushi no Mikoto, We head toward the mansion of Iso. Polo and Reeru, who knew Keerisu and Taakusu from the Brahmanism faith, were struck with terror at the sudden, mysterious divine power possessed by the two, and at their singing of the Ananai-kyō propaganda song. タークス『きまつた事よ。鬼の居ぬ間に心の洗濯を遊ばしたのだ。誰だつて今の人間は面従腹背とかいつて、本人の前ではペコペコと頭を下げ………お前さまのことなら命でも差上げますと、二つ目には巧妙な辞令を使つてゐるが、其前を離れると、すぐに打つて変つて悪口を言つたり反対的の行動を執るものだ。それが所謂現代思潮だ。いはば時勢に忠実な行方と言はば云へぬことはない。何事も神直日、大直日に見直し聞直し、善意に此場は解釈しておく方が穏かであらうよ。俺だつて、昨日までならキツトさうだ。ポーロさまに決して負けるものぢやない。吾々だつて片彦将軍に何といつた………仮令三五教の宣伝使幾万騎押寄せ来るとも、命の限り奮闘を続け、不幸にして命がなくなれば、七度生れ変つて、バラモン教の為に三五教の司を殲滅致さねばおきませぬ………と誓つた間もなく直に此通り三五教へ帰順して了つたのだから、人間のやる事は如何しても矛盾は免がれない。オイポーロさま、実は俺達は最早三五教の信徒だ。これからイソの館へ修行に参る所だ。其途中に於て三五教の宣伝使………つまり俺達の親分、照国別命から信書をことづかつて来たから、何が書いてあるか知らぬが、よく検めて読んでくれ』

§ 4

Hearing the name Terukuniwake, Pōro felt his heart tremble as he received the letter, broke the seal, and read it slowly. His hand holding the letter was shaking incessantly. The contents of the letter were as follows.

Terukuniwake: 'First, I, Terukuniwake, a missionary of Ananai-kyō, send this letter to Pōro, the caretaker of the cave at Kiyoharuyama. My parents have long been under your generous care, spending their days in peace and comfort; having tasted all the hardships of the world, they have finally realized the grace of the noble Kami. Furthermore, my younger sister has also realized the kuōdaimuhen (boundless) nature of the Shintoku (divine virtue) of Kami, and has become a true believer of Ananai. I deeply believe and am deeply grateful that this is, in essence, the gift of your persecutory sympathy. Also, the missionary Ihahiko has changed his name to Yatsukosu and has snuck into your cave mansion to explore the various doctrines of Brahmanism; I am convinced that this will provide the greatest convenience for his future activities, and for this, too, I humbly express my gratitude.'

Terukuniwake: 'Next, as for us and our party being thrown into, or rather, having fallen into a dark pit, the realization that inattention is the most fearful thing serves as a good warning for our future activities, and for this, too, I humbly express my gratitude, as it is entirely due to your grace. Since all people are bunrei (divided spirits) and buntai (divided bodies) of the noble Creator, to establish narrow names for teachings and to contend with one another in faith and battle over principles is the greatest sin of impiety against the Daiji-Daihi (Great Mercy and Great Compassion) Moto-tsu-Oyagami (Original Ancestor God).'

Terukuniwake: 'Kērisu and Tākusu have immediately grasped the doctrines of Ananai and have quickly converted. I am sending this letter to you while dispatching them to the Iso mansion on Mount Ubusuna, so that they may become true Kamibashira (divine pillars). If you, by any chance, affirm the words of Terukuniwake, come to the Iso mansion with these two and receive instruction from the Kami such as Hinodewake and other Kamitsukasa (divine officials). Kōkō-tonshu (With fear and trembling, I bow my head).'

It was written thus. Pōro shed tears of admiration, read this divine text aloud once more, and let everyone hear it. Rēru, Shiyamu, and all the others praised the missionary Terukuniwake with one voice, and were struck with wonder at the tenpō-muge (unobstructed in all directions) and kōmyō-kakushiyaku (brilliantly shining) nature of the Ananai-kyō doctrine; they changed their hearts and decided to follow the two and proceed to the Iso mansion. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.

原文（近代文語）

ポーロは照国別と聞いて、胸をビクつかせながら信書を受取り、封おし切つてソロソロと読み下した。信書を持つ手は頻りに慄へてゐる。其文面は左の通りである。

『一、三五教の宣伝使照国別より清春山の岩窟の留守職ポーロに一書を送る。吾両親は、永らく汝の手厚きお世話になり、安楽に月日を送り、あらゆる世の艱難を嘗めた為、漸くにして尊き神の恩恵を悟り、又吾妹も同じく神徳の広大無辺なるを悟り、正しき三五の信徒となりしも、要するに汝等が迫害的同情の賜物たることを深く信じ深く感謝する。又岩彦の宣伝使はヤツコスと名を変じ、汝が岩窟の館に忍び込み、種々雑多のバラモン教の教理を探り得たるは、向後に於ける彼が活動上、最も便宜を得たるものと確信し、これ又謹んで感謝する次第である。

次に吾々始め一行の者、暗黒なる陥穽に放りこまれ、否陥落したるより、不注意の最も恐るべきを悟りたるは、今後の吾々が活動上に於ける良き戒めにして、全く汝等の恩恵に依るものと、これ又謹んで感謝する。人は凡て尊き造物主の分霊分体なれば、狭隘なる教の名を設けて、互に信仰を争ひ、主義を戦はすは、大慈大悲の元つ御祖の神に対し、不孝の罪、これより大なるはなかるべし。

ケーリス、タークスの両人は直ちに三五の教理を悟り、速かに入信したれば、今よりウブスナ山のイソ館に遣はし、天晴れ誠の神柱となさむ為に差遣はす途中、此手紙を汝に謹んで呈する。万一汝等にして照国別の言を肯定するならば、此二人と共にイソの館に参り、日の出別の神始め其他の神司より教を受けられよ。恐惶頓首』

と記してあつた。ポーロは涙を流して感歎し、再び此神文を読み上げ、一同に聞かせた。レール、シヤム其他一同は異口同音に照国別の宣伝使を称讃し、且三五教の教理の十方無礙、光明赫灼たるに打驚き、心を改め、二人に従つてイソ館へ参進することとなつた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
