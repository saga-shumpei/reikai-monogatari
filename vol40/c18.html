Chapter 18: 沼の Moon [1102]

沼の月〔1102〕

§ 1

The Jifugoya Mangetsu is floating on the Afuhinuma, which is clear and quite vast. The Enmanseirō moon is in the sky, and its Tsukikage is also reflected on the surface of the pond, swaying in the Sazanami. Two Sendenshi happened to pass by there. These are the Futari, Ōgonhime and Kiyoteruhime.

Ōgonhime: 'The countenance of the moon, in such good spirits today, is almost like Ōgoniro. Shall we rest here by the bank of this pond while admiring the moon?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother-sama. The color of the moon is Ōgonhime, is it not? And the place where it shines clearly and brightly seems to be Kiyoteruhime. If so, it is just as if you are the body and I am the function; I wish to make this Shiyaba Sekai into an Ōgonsekai as soon as possible, and cause the Ananahi Oshihe to shine throughout the Tenka. What is the name of this swamp?'

'They say it is called the Afuhinuma. The Aozora is reflected in it, and even the shadows of the stars are floating there. It is an indescribable Keshiki. Shall we compose some poems here?'

'Yes, that would be fine. Please start with one, Mother-sama, and I will add the lower phrase.'

Ōgonhime: 'From the Ōzora to the Minasoko, the Afuhinuma.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'In Ōgoniro, the moon shines bright.'

Ōgonhime: 'The form of the Ōgon jewel shines across heaven and earth, clearly illuminating all.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'In the light of the clearly shining moon, it is reflected in the swamp and becomes even more clear.'

Ōgonhime: 'This swamp, where the moon floats and even the stars float, must be the form of Takamagahara.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'I wish to illuminate the upper and lower realms thoroughly with the Ananahi moon Oshihe.'

Ōgonhime: 'When I gaze at this pond water shining brightly, it seems to resemble the sky of the clouds.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'On the surface of the pond where even the wind has calmed, the Ōgon moon shines clearly.'

Ōgonhime: 'When I gaze at the form of the moon on the night of the full moon, it is a pleasant surface of the swamp.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'When I see the moon of the swamp breaking into waves and moving, I cannot get enough of the sight.'

Ōgonhime: 'Tonight, I think I shall sleep soundly by the bank of the swamp and rest the fatigue of my Mitama.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'My soul is like the moon I see now; though it seems it will break, it does not break. The Tsukikage that clears across the Ōzora must be the form of Kiyoteruhime. The form of the clearly shining moon must be the light of Lord Susanoo. The form of the god Tsukiyomi, the Mizumitama, shines even to the bottom of the swamp.'

Ōgonhime: 'Ohohoho, Kiyosama, you are quite skilled. It seems Ōgonhime has run out of poetic seeds as well.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Mother-sama, then I shall begin, so please add the latter phrase.'

Ōgonhime: 'That would also be interesting; try it.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'I am now looking at the moon of the Afuhinuma.'

Ōgonhime: 'On the path to opening the Oshihe of the moon.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'When I look at the moon, this world is also enjoyable, even though it is a world of people with many waxings and wanings. Shall I reveal my heart to the moon that shines clearly, as I am one who illuminates the world beneath heaven? The master of this swamp must have much happiness, gazing at the clear moon night after night. Every time I look up at the light of the sun, my eyes are dimmed; only the moon alone nourishes the eyes. In a world where the light of the sun shines everywhere, the light of the moon is also wonderful. Both the sun and the moon are the blessings of the Lord shining in the Ōzora for the sake of the people of the world. How grateful I am for the world where the Ananahi moon Oshihe shines like the morning sun. I gazed at the Afuhinuma where both the sun and the moon float among the waves, with a heart as red as the sun. Today, mother and daughter gaze at the Afuhinuma and admire the light of the moon. The Oshihe of the Brahman god must be like the form of the dark night of the last day of the month.'

Ōgonhime: 'The light of the Ōgon moon that illuminates and clears the dark night is indeed valiant. When I gaze far into the Ōzora on the last day of the month, it is the dark night of Lord Ōkuninushi. The soul of the Mikoto of Onikuma-wake is the moon wrapped in the clouds crossing the sky. The Ōzora will soon be the Kamudachi of Haruna, where the Ananahi moon shines clearly.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Ohohoho, I have run out of my small supply with this as well. When I have time again, I will take out a great deal from the warehouse and show it to you.'

Ōgonhime: 'Ohohohoho, Kiyosama, thanks to being by the side of Mokusuke-sama, you have become quite good at comedy.'

Kiyoteru: 'The voice of the cock announcing the dawn and the Shinonome, / Shall the Kami of the sunrise soon ascend?'

Ōgon: 'Ho ho ho, the Kotsukei has begun in earnest. Well, to soothe the fatigue of this journey, I shall compose a Daku as well. / A Kamuzukasa with a crimson heart at the Afuhinuma, / Gazing at the white Ananahi moon. / The manifestation of the Hime of Ōgon appears, / Watching the broken moon of the Afuhinuma.'

Kiyoteru: 'That broken moon must be about you, Mother. My moon is a perfectly round one.'

Ōgon: 'Ho ho ho, what a daughter who does not fit in hand or foot. / Though I speak of an unbroken moon, it is Tomohiko, / A half-broken moon shattered by the waves. / It is Oka that you lie before your mother, / Trying to deceive me through and through. / When even the half-moon becomes a full moon, / It is the Ōgon moon that has no gaps anywhere.'

Ōgon: 'Ho ho ho ho, if we keep talking about the moon, the moon will turn over and reveal its scars. The material for poems has run out for now. Come, come, let us sleep soundly here.'

Kiyoteru: 'I shall accompany you and sleep by your side. Ho ho ho.'

Laughing thus, while bathing in the moonlight, they spread their straw raincoats and lie down innocently on the shore of the swamp.

To such a place, breaking the moon floating on the swamp, seven or eight black shadows came running, kicking through the waves with a splashing sound.

Eru: 'Ah, I have met with a terrible fate. I encountered the two, Mukadehime and Koitohime, at the Minamizaka of Tamayamatauge, and upon the Meirei of the Taishiyō of Karu, I attacked them Shiyanimuni, but I was easily thrown into the Tanisoko, and while I was being dragged by demons to the Ichizu River in a state of Kizetsu, a propagandist of the Ananahi-kyo appeared and made me drink some unknown nectar, and when I thought that, I was collapsed in the Tanisoko having a dream. It was a truly frightening dream, but when I came to my senses, it was ridiculous; nevertheless, that Taishiyō of Karu, having been awakened during his peaceful sleep, uttered a million flatteries calling him a savior of his life, and I cannot stand the nausea. In the end, he has sunk into the Ananahi-kyo; he is a fellow whose mind changes as easily as a cat's eye. We have never changed our original intention and have dedicated our lives to Lord Ōkuninushi, so we cannot do such weak-minded things. Tell me, Kiru-kō, is he not truly a spineless weakling?'

Kiru: 'Oh, that is right. I also felt so ill that I said to such a propagandist... Yes, yes, thanks to you, my life was saved... and such ridiculous things, flattering him just to escape the situation, but it feels as if I lost a gamble I did not even play, and I feel so disgusted I cannot bear it. One for the sake of revenge, and one for the sake of achieving merit before Lord Ōkuninushi, I considered the breathing of everyone while they slept, and quietly attached a rope to the waist of the propagandist, and set up a mechanism to tighten his throat by pulling it all at once, but that propagandist fellow, being a great old tanuki, must have been feigning sleep, for before I knew it, he used magic and tied us up instead, and the suffering when he made Reibu pull the rope—I was worried that this time I would truly have to travel to the Yūkai. Since you lot are such dull-witted fellows, you raised your voices... and because you blurted out that it would be a divine punishment to return evil for good to one who saved your life, the scheme failed, and we ended up getting nothing at all. If you do not be careful from now on, you do not know what kind of fate you will be met with. You are truly fools. Mukadehime and Koitohime will surely bypass this swamp and come here, so you must not make a mistake this time. Are we not supporting our wives and children in comfort thanks to Lord Ōkuninushi? Think about it well. You cannot sustain your lives by eating only fruit. If we do not receive support from Lord Ōkuninushi, our entire families will have to starve to death. How can you forget such a master of great grace, and why is the propagandist who saved only your own life so grateful? Moreover, it is as if he were awakened from a peaceful sleep. How could we ever replace Lord Ōkuninushi, who allows so many of us to live in constant security? Everyone, is that not so?'

Harukimi: 'Excuse me, Senden-shi-sama, perhaps Ōgonhime-sama and Seishōhime-sama are resting somewhere beyond this swamp.'

キル『ナヽ何だあ、蜈蚣姫だ、甘い事吐すない、一途の川の星々婆ア奴、ガキも人数だ。八人と一人では叶ふまい。サア突貫々々』

清照『妾は三五教の宣伝使小糸姫だ。バラモン教の悪人共、一人も残らず冥途の旅立をさしてやらう。サア覚悟はよいか』

キル『ヤア此奴は又若い脱衣婆アだ。コリヤ両人、蜈蚣姫や小糸姫の名をかたつても駄目だぞ。一イ二ウ三ツ』

といひながら、八人は二人に向つて武者振りつくを、

『エヽ面倒』

と母娘二人は首筋つかんで葵の沼の真中へ、一人も残らず、バサリバサリと投げ込んだ。八人は此勢に辟易し、一生懸命に再び沼の真中をバサバサバサと北を指して逃げて行く。

八人の奴は驚きの余り、照国別の休んでゐる所へ、以前の怖さを忘れて又もやバサバサバサと命からがら上つて来た。レーブは此姿を見て、

『アハヽヽヽ、コリヤ八つの蛙、如何したのだ。いかにカヘルだと云つて、再び元の所へ逃げカヘル奴があるかい』

キル『ヤア…………此奴アしまつた、余りビツクリして忘れてゐた。前門の狼、後門の虎だ。オイ、レーブ、こらへてくれ。向ふへ渡ると蜈蚣姫、小糸姫の名をかたつて婆や娘がヒユードロドロと出やがるなり、こつちへ来れば又此通り、進退これ谷まるだ。モウ改心するから許してくれ。頼む頼む』

レーブ『たとへ宣伝使がお赦しになつても、貴様の様な不都合な奴は此レーブが赦さぬのだ。オイ、カル、貴様も一つ手伝つてくれ。この八匹の蛙を元のドブ池へ突込んでやるのだから』

『ヨシ来た』

とカルは立上り、手に唾して、片つぱしからドブンドブンと沼を目がけて投込んだ。八人は又もやバサバサバサと浅き沼を命カラガラ南を指して逃げて行く。

春公『モシ宣伝使様、ヒヨツとしたら、黄金姫様、清照姫様は此沼の向方あたりにお休みになつてるのかも知れませぬぜ』

照国『いかにもさうかも知れない、一つ此沼を渡つて、追つついて査べてみよう。サア一同用意だ』

と云ひながら、早くも照国別は裾をからげ、浅き沼をバサバサと歩み出した。照、梅、春、レーブ、カルの一行は照国別の後に従ひ、月照り渡る沼の中をバサリバサリと、波に円を描きながら急ぎ行く。
