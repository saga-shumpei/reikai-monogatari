Chapter 2: 出陣 [1086]

出陣〔1086〕

§ 1

The Kamuzukasa of the Brahman religion, Oniharuwake, acting as the Daikyōshiyu, received the commands of Ōkuronushi and Isonohime. With Katahiko and the two generals of Ranch as his left and right wings, he led over three thousand cavalrymen, departing from the capital of Haruna. Beating the Jinganetaiko and blowing the Horagahi with a loud blare, they marched toward the Ananai-kyō and the mansion of Iso with their banners and drums in grand array. The ferocity of their force was such that even the Kishin were struck with terror, as if they were on the verge of death.

Ōdaruwake, who served as the commander of the army, likewise led over three thousand cavalrymen with Kugihiko and the two generals of Yell, marching toward the stronghold of Karmata where the Ural-kyō had entrenched themselves. Warriors of equal prowess advanced, and the grass and trees of the mountains and fields bowed before them of their own accord; tigers, bears, lions, and wolves all trembled and fled in fear. It was a truly valiant march, with the neighing of horses, the sound of bridles, and the clatter of hooves ringing out sharply as they raised their war cries to launch the attack. It was a truly magnificent sight.

The preparations for the departure were completed in haste. Ōkuronushi, Isonohime, Onikumawake, Kumoiyawake, and other executives saw the troops off and celebrated their success. Afterward, they entered the inner palace of the main castle in Haruna and held a simple banquet, where it was decided that Onikumawake would return to his own mansion for the time being. Kumoiyawake also returned to his own mansion that day, offered prayers for victory before the gods, and retired to sleep.

The night deepened, and the surroundings were wrapped in a quiet, gloomy, and desolate atmosphere. Until the hour of the ox, when the night storms raged, Ōkuronushi and Isonohime spent the night talking about the past and the future.

Ōkuronushi: 'Ah, ever since I became the Daikyōshiyu of the Brahman religion, I have tasted every hardship and suffering for the sake of the world and the Way. At last, I established a stronghold in the Land of the Moon, but no sooner did I think I could find some peace than the saying "good things are often thwarted by demons" proved true. The wretches of the Ananai-kyō and the Ural-kyō envy the prosperity of my religion, and now they seek to destroy us by attacking this main castle from both sides. I am so filled with hatred for these detestable wretches that I have become hypersensitive, and I have hardly been able to sleep lately. The only comfort I have in my days and nights of suffering is the gentle words of Isonohime. Ah, why is the world so full of calamities? The more I think about it, the more I have come to loathe this floating world. I wish to have my son succeed me as Daikyōshiyu as soon as possible so that I may retire to the mountains and forests with you and enjoy the rest of my life in peace, but my son is, as you know, weak-willed and sheltered, and his future is truly worrisome. Is there no way to escape this suffering?'

He sighed heavily and looked dejected. Isonohime smiled and replied,

Isonohime: 'Ho ho ho, my lord, why do you speak such weak words? How can you carry the Land of the Moon on your shoulders with such frailty? Is it not the divine will of the gods to bring not only this tiny Land of the Moon but the entire Toyoashihara no Mizuho-no-kuni to submit to the Brahman religion, and to bestow the dew of grace upon all people? With such a weak will, you will not even be able to maintain the Land of the Moon. Please, pull yourself together and cheer up. As the ruler of a nation, is it not contrary to the divine will for you to be captivated by a lowly woman like me, while Onikumahime-sama, with whom you pledged to live and die together, and who has contributed so much as your wife, is left in retirement and continues to live in tears day and night? When I think of that, I cannot find peace in my heart. I beg you to invite your wife back to the main castle as soon as possible and join her in the divine work in harmony. If you would then lower my position to that of a maidservant, I, Isonohime, am prepared to serve you both with all my loyalty; please, I beg you to grant me this. This is my only wish in life.'

出陣の用意は急速に整うた。大黒主、石生能姫、鬼熊別、雲依別其他の幹部は出陣を見送り成功を祝し、終つてハルナの本城の奥殿に進み入り此処に簡単なる酒宴を催し、鬼熊別は一先づ吾館へ立帰る事となつた。雲依別も亦其日は己が館に帰り、神前に戦勝祈願の祝詞を奏し寝に就いた。

夜は深々と更け渡り、咫尺暗憺として閑寂な気に包まれ、夜嵐吹き荒ぶ丑満の頃迄、大黒主は石生能姫と共に来し方行末の事等語らひ夜を更かしつつあつた。

大黒『あゝあ、吾こそはバラモン教の大教主となつて以来、世の為、道の為にあらゆる艱難辛苦を嘗め尽し、漸くにして月の国に根城を定め、稍安心と思ふ間もなく好事魔多しとやら、三五教、ウラル教の奴輩吾教の隆盛を妬み、今や双方より此本城を攻撃し吾等を亡ぼさむと致す憎くき奴、余りの事に神経過敏となり、夜も碌々に此頃は寝た事もない。せめて石生能姫の優しき言葉を心の頼みとして日夜を送る苦しさ。あゝあ世の中は如何してこれほど災の多きものだらうか。思へば思へば浮世が嫌になつて来たわい。早く大教主の役を伜に継承さして其方と共に山林に隠れ、光風霽月を楽しみ余生を送りたいと思ふ心は山々なれど、伜はあの通り文弱に流れ世間知らずの坊んちやん育ち、実に前途は心細いものだ。何とか致して此苦艱を免るる道はあるまいかな』

とハアハアと吐息をつき悄げ返る。石生能姫は打笑ひ、

『ホヽヽヽ旦那様の其お言葉、何とした弱音をお吹き遊ばすのでせう。そんな弱い事で如何して此月の国を背負つて立つ事が出来ませうか。神様は此チツポケな月の国ばかりか、豊葦原の瑞穂国全体をバラモンの教に帰順せしめ、恵みの露をば万民に霑し与へむとの御神慮では御座りませぬか。左様な意志の薄弱な事では月の国さへも保つ事は出来ますまい。チト心を取り直して元気を出して下さいませ。一国の王者たる身を以て妾の如き卑しき女に心魂を蕩かし、偕老同穴を契り給ひし鬼雲姫様、特に内助の功多き奥様をあの通り退隠させ、日夜涙の生活を続けて御座るのを他所にして、旦那様は妾の様な女を弄び給ふは御神慮に叶はぬ事ではありますまいか。それを思へば妾も安き心は厶りませぬ。何卒一日も早く奥様を本城に招き入れ、夫婦睦まじく神業に参加して下さいませ。そして妾の位置を下して婢女となし下されば、御夫婦に対し力限りの忠勤を励む石生能姫の覚悟、何卒許して下さいませ。これが妾の一生の願ひで御座います』

§ 2

Ōkuronushi: 'Ha ha ha ha ha! You say that I, Ōkuronushi, am small-minded, but are you not too small-minded yourself? Did you not say this when you first shared a bed with me? You said, "I am truly filled with Arigatanamida that you, Dannasama, would cherish someone as Futsutsuka as me, but I am worried about Okusama, and my heart is not at peace; that is all I am concerned about." Because of that, did I not have Onikumohime, who had been with me for so long and shared my hardships, move to a Betsuke, and did I not do as you wished? Now that it has come to this, it troubles Ōkuronushi for you to say such things. You are the Niyōbau I have permitted; you need not show Enriyo to anyone. You must hold your head high, become the flower of the Honjiyō and the Kuīn, and help me with my Shingefu with all your might, both in the shadows and in the light, or else I shall be in trouble.'

Ishionohime: 'Dannasama, when I said that I was concerned about Okusama, it was Motsutai; I did not wish for you to cast Okusama aside. I feel truly sorry to be cherished by a Dannasama who has an Okusama. I only meant that I have no face to show Okusama.'

Ōkuronushi: 'Is that not why I kept Onikumohime at a distance, to remove the seed of your Shinpai?'

Ishionohime: 'That is a bit of a misunderstanding of my Reuken. No matter how far away Okusama may be, my heart will never be at peace. I feel even more sorry for her than before. She is cursed by many Buka and Kokumin as an Eujiyo, a Kijiyo, and a traitor; how could my heart be at ease with this? Please try to understand my feelings. You always say you love me with your lips, but because you do not grasp my true heart, you end up tormenting and hating me.'

She presses her sleeve to her face and weeps bitterly.

Ōkuronushi: 'Is there anything I have not done when you asked it? Even today, you told me that Onikumawake, a man I had ordered into seclusion for obstructing the path of the teachings, was exercising proxy authority without even consulting me, and you insisted that I appoint him as Sashimori and entrust him with the authority of the castle—and I did so, despite there being many others. It is a matter of great importance to the realm for me, and although it is not something I would normally consent to, I am enduring it, suppressing my anger, just to uphold your argument and not hurt your feelings. If there were ever a chance that this country might fall into the hands of a foreign religion, I could never rest easy here. The only reason I am enduring this great matter for us is because you are Kaai to me.'

Ishionohime: 'Does that Onikumawake appear to be such a bad person in your eyes? You are too good-natured, so you adopt the slander of wicked people one by one, and you do not use the words of the wise and virtuous. Where else could you find a priest who thinks so much of the Brahman religion? This is a matter that concerns your great affair and also my great affair; can such an important decision be made so easily, based on a little emotion or whim? Please clear the clouds from your heart and try to understand the true heart of Onikumawake correctly.'

Ōkuronushi: 'If I hear it that way, perhaps it is so, but Onikumawake's Niyōbau has finally defected to the Sangyōkyō, and his daughter, Koitohime, has also become a splendid propagandist for the path of Sangyōkyō, devouring the fields of the Brahman religion and obstructing the path in various ways. That the inner workings of the capital of Haruna are all known to the Sangyōkyō must mean there is someone among those who serve close by who is colluding with them. If there is someone colluding, then according to the words of Oniharawake, it stands to reason that it can be none other than Onikumawake. Ishionohime, even knowing this, do you still trust Onikumawake?'

Ishionohime: 'That is surely a misunderstanding on your part, my dear. There is no reason for him, of all people, to possess such a base nature. If you doubt people, there is no end to it; the good and evil, right and wrong of people will be judged directly by the Lord God. Even if you are the representative of the Lord God, you are still a Lord God who possesses a human body; how can you know the good and evil, right and wrong of the human heart? If you would wipe away all the clouds and mists of your heart, return to the heart of Kannagara, and place your hand upon your chest to think, you might understand a little. If Onikumawake-sama truly had such ambition, he who carries the trust of the Kokumin on his shoulders alone could do anything. Because you hold the power of arms, people look up to you as both King and Great Teacher, but their hearts have already drifted away. Only those who brush the dust from your beard approach your side, leading you into an ever deeper abyss. Among all these many retainers, from my impartial perspective, there is not a single person other than Onikumawake-sama who is a true person who can be of real help to you. I, Ishionohime, with all my heart, beg you to open your heart to Onikumawake-sama as soon as possible and exert your best efforts for the sake of the path and the country.'

『そりや貴方お考へ違ひでせう。あの方に限つて左様な卑しい根性をお有ち遊ばす道理は厶りませぬ。人を疑へば何処までも限りのないもの、人の善悪正邪は神様が直接にお審き遊ばしませう。仮令貴方は神の代表者としても矢張り人間の肉体を有つた神様、如何して人の心の善悪正邪が判りませう。一切の心の雲霧を払拭し惟神の心に立ち帰り、胸に手をあててお考へ遊ばしたらチツと御合点が参りませう。もしも鬼熊別さまに左様な野心がありとすれば、あれだけ国民の信用を一身に担うたお方、どんな事でも出来ませう。貴方は兵馬の権を握つておいで遊ばす故、国王とも大教主とも仰いでゐるものの、人心は既に離れて居りますよ。髭の塵を払ふものばかりお側に近寄つて貴方を益々深い淵に陥れるものばかり、本当に貴方の力になる誠の者は此沢山な御家来の内、妾の公平なる目より見れば鬼熊別様より外に只の一人もありませぬ。何卒一時も早く鬼熊別と胸襟を開いてお道の為、国の為、最善の力をお尽し遊ばす様に石生能姫が真心をこめてお願ひ致します』

§ 3

Ōkuronushi, even if he tried to refuse at first when Isonohime spoke, could not, due to the weakness of love, completely exclude her wishes. Ōkuronushi finally yielded, saying,

Ōkuronushi: 'In that case, I leave the matter of Onikumawake to you. Please be very careful not to be deceived by him, and tell him to exert his strength for our sake.'

Isonohime: 'I am satisfied with your prompt consent, Ōkuronushi-sama. If that is the case, I shall visit his mansion early tomorrow morning to fully probe his intentions, and if he is a good man, I will command him to appear daily and make him your consultant. If there is even a dewdrop of evidence that he hides a needle in his heart, then I must surely take decisive action. I will go to Onikumawake's mansion early tomorrow morning, so please be aware of that.'

Ōkuronushi: 'Why not summon him here to be examined instead of going all the way there yourself? A woman should not cross thresholds so easily.'

Isonohime: 'Ho ho ho! Those words of yours, Dannasama; a woman of today is not a woman unless she is heralded as a flower of society. A husband's success depends entirely on his wife's skill in social circles. Even if I go to Onikumawake's residence, I will never do anything to tarnish your face, so please rest assured and let me probe the true spirit of Onikumawake to the very end.'

Ōkuronushi: 'In that case, I leave everything to you. Tomorrow, from early morning, visit his mansion quietly so that others do not notice, and thoroughly verify his inner heart. Now, the night has grown quite late. Shall we retire?'

Isonohime: 'Yes.'

Having answered thus, Isonohime spread the bedding, and the couple, having finally settled their hearts for the first time in a long while, went to sleep peacefully.
