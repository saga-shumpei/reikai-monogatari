Chapter 3: 落 Bridge [1087]

落橋〔1087〕

§ 1

The sky was overcast with heavy clouds, and the autumn evening was gloomy, windless, and muggy; unlike the autumn in the mainland, in these early autumn days, there was no sound of pine crickets or bell crickets, but only the voices of cicadas clinging to the treetops and chirping in fragments, and the noisy, clamorous sounds of kutsuwamushi, which made one feel as if they were nothing but a portent of the outbreak of unrest within and without Haruna Castle in the Capital of the Moon. The master of the mansion, Onikumawake, having finished the meeting in the rock cave of Mount Taiun, returned home in low spirits, sat in the inner room, folded his arms, and was in a state of sighing and gasping.

To such a place, Kumahiko, who served as the chief retainer, pushed open the sliding door and entered, and while bowing politely, said:

Kumahiko: 'Excuse me, Dannasama. I have heard that a grave suspicion has been cast upon you, and I have secretly heard from my close friend that the Lord Ōkuni-nushi will send some of his attendants to take your life under the cover of night. Please, I beg you to be on your guard. An assassin might arrive at any moment...'

Onikumawake smiled calmly and said:

Onikumawake: 'Hahaha! You must not be startled by the wind and the cranes. How could a blade of injustice be struck against Onikumawake, who serves the true God with a sincere heart? You must never worry. In such tumultuous times, various rumors are bound to arise, so you too must think about things calmly and never be agitated.'

Kumahiko: 'I am not a man who would be agitated by ordinary matters, but I have certain proof. A man named Tomoyuki, who serves as an attendant to the Lord Ōkuni-nushi, is actually my brother-in-law, and he secretly whispered this to me. We cannot afford to dally. There is no doubt that they will surely attack tonight. If this turns out to be wrong, this Kumahiko will never appear before your eyes again.'

Onikumawake: 'In fact, I have just returned from attending the assembly in the rock cave of Mount Taiun, where I argued before the Lord Ōkuni-nushi, resolved various suspicions, and finally, through the recommendation of Ishionohime-sama, I was reappointed as the Left Guardian as before. There surely cannot be any such thing. It is likely just some kind of mistake.'

Kumahiko: 'No, I have heard that matter well from Tomoyuki. However, that is the very cause of the great upheaval tonight. The Lord Ōkuni-nushi is a man of deep jealousy, and since Ishionohime-sama, whom he loves so dearly that he cannot forget her whether sleeping or waking, took your side and appointed you, whom the Lord Ōkuni-nushi hates most, as the Left Guardian, intending to have you oversee all educational and state affairs within the castle, the Lord Ōkuni-nushi is beside himself with anxiety. Yet, he could not deny the words of his beloved wife, Ishionohime-sama, so he consented against his will. Since then, the Lord Ōkuni-nushi has thought that it would be a disaster if he did not dispose of you as soon as possible, and he has ordered many of his attendants to attack this mansion tonight to finish off Onikumawake in the strictest secrecy, as instructed by Ishionohime-sama. One of them, Tomoyuki, secretly informed me of this with a confidential letter, so there can be no mistake. Now, Dannasama, this is no time to be so idle. You must either prepare for defense as soon as possible, or you must flee this mansion while there is still time, lest you be left with the regret of being devoured. Though I am incapable, I, Kumahiko, must follow you wherever you go, and even if we must suffer hardships and difficulties together, I must protect your person. Now, please make your decision quickly...'

As he urged him, Onikumawake laughed aloud and said:

Onikumawake: 'Hahaha! How interesting the world is. Yesterday's enemy is today's friend, and today's friend is tomorrow's enemy; like the clouds in the vast sky that change from yesterday, constant change is the way of the world, and whatever happens is the karma of destiny. Do not be agitated, do not be flustered. There is no other means to take than to leave everything to the heart of the Brahma, Indra, and the Lord of Freedom who created this world.'

空一面にドンヨリとかき曇り、あたり陰鬱として風もなく蒸暑き秋の夕べ、内地の秋とは事変はり、初秋の今日此頃は松虫鈴虫の声もなく、梢にとまつて千切れ千切れに鳴く蝉の声、轡虫等喧しく騒ぎ鳴きたつる有様は、月の都のハルナ城の内外に穏かならぬ事の勃発する前兆にはあらずやと思はるるばかりであつた。館の主鬼熊別は大雲山の岩窟に於ける会議を終へて、悄然として吾家に帰り、奥の一間に座をしめて、双手をくみ、青息吐息の体であつた。

斯かる所へ家老職を勤めてゐた熊彦は襖を押しあけ入り来り、叮嚀に会釈しながら、

『モシ旦那様、承はりますれば、貴方様に大変な嫌疑がかかり、大黒主様が近侍の誰彼を遣はして、夜陰に紛れ、旦那様の命を取りに来るとの急報を自分の親友よりソツと聞きました。どうぞ御用心下さいませ。今にも刺客が参るかも知れませぬから……』

鬼熊別は平然として打笑ひ、

『アハヽヽヽ風声鶴唳に驚いてはならぬ。真心を以て真心の神に仕ふる鬼熊別に如何して不義の刃が当てられようか。決して心配は致すものではない。かやうな騒々しい時にはいろいろの噂の立つものだから、お前も冷静に物を考へ、決して騒いではならないぞ』

『私も大抵の事ならば騒ぐ男では厶いませぬが確な証拠が厶います。大黒主様の近侍に仕へてゐる友行といふ男、実は私の義理の兄弟で厶いますが、彼がソツと私まで耳うちをしてくれました。グヅグヅしては居られませぬ。キツと今夜攻寄せて来るに間違ひはないので厶います。これが違うたら、此熊彦は二度とあなたのお目にはかかりませぬ』

『現に俺は今、大雲山の岩窟に集会に参り、大黒主様の面前に於て議論を戦はし、種々雑多の疑惑を解き、漸く氷解されて、遂には石生能姫の推薦に依り、元の如く左守に任ぜられ帰つて来た所だ。決して左様な事はあるまい。大方何らかの間違ひだらう』

『イヤ其事は友行から能く聞いて居ります。併しそれが今晩の大事変を起した原因です。大黒主は嫉妬の深い人物、そこへ寝ても醒めても忘れられぬ惚れ切つた石生能姫さまが、旦那さまの肩を持ち、大黒主の最も嫌ひ給ふ旦那さまを左守に任じ、城内一切の教務及び国務を総括せしめむとされたので、大黒主は気が気でならず、ぢやと云つて最愛の女房石生能姫の言を打消す訳にもゆかず、イヤイヤながら承諾したので厶います。それより大黒主は一時も早く旦那さまを亡き者に致さねば大変だと考へ、石生能姫さまに極内々で今夜の内に鬼熊別をやつつけて了へと、数多の近侍に命じて今宵御館へ襲来することになつたので厶います。其中の一人なる友行がソツと密書を以て私迄知らしてくれたので厶いますから、メツタに間違ひは厶いますまい。サア旦那さま、さう安閑としてゐる時ぢや厶いませぬ。一時も早く防戦の用意を致されるか、但は今の内に此館を逐電なさらねば、呑噬の悔を残すとも及びませぬ。及ばぬながらも熊彦がどこ迄もお供を致し、苦労艱難を共々に嘗めても、旦那様の御身辺を守らねばなりませぬ。サア早く御決心を……』

と促せば、鬼熊別は高笑ひ、

『アハヽヽヽ何とマア世の中は面白いものだなア。昨日の敵は今日の味方、今日の味方は明日の敵、昨日に変る大空の雲、千変万化は世のならひ、どうなり行くも宿世の因縁だ。騒ぐな、あはてな。只何事も此世を造り給ひし梵天帝釈自在天の御心に任すより外に取るべき手段はない』

§ 2

Speaker: 'That may be so, but is it not rather unwise to stand by and watch while knowing in advance that you will be attacked by the enemy? If you do not devise some method or means to counter this, how will your goodness be understood in the world? If you are destroyed by them in vain this evening, when will your resentment be cleared... or rather, when will your doubts be resolved?'

Speaker: 'We do not resent others, nor do we hate our enemies. Even if one were to leave one's wife and children and attain a most splendid position, the life of a person is such that one cannot know what will happen the next moment; there is no other way but to leave everything to Kami. If Kami wishes to kill us, He will likely use the hands of others to do so, and if He decides that there is still a need for us in this Shiyaba, He will likely leave us alive. The next moment is darkness to the eyes of Ningen. One should simply enjoy the heart of the Setsuna and do one's Saizen as a Kamuzukasa.'

Speaker: 'Ah, even after I have said this much, will you not listen, Dannasama? It is no longer a matter of right or wrong. I am truly sorry, but before I see the pitiful figure of my Dannasama, I shall take my leave and commit Setsupuku here. If that is the case, Dannasama.'

Speaker: 'Aha, what a hasty man you are. Even if you ask for leave, I will not grant it, and even if you ask me to let you die, I will never let you die. The relationship between master and servant is not such a shallow thing. If you wish to die, it would be better to see my end and die after that. To commit suicide of your own accord before your master is the height of thoughtlessness.'

Speaker: 'This is indeed the result of my youth and hot-headedness; I have done a truly inexcusable thing. It is the duty of a servant to follow the will of his master; I shall say no more about this. Please, I beg you, forgive me only for the breaking of the bond between master and servant. I will never again do anything rash before my Dannasama. If I am to die, I shall face the approaching enemy and die in battle before the horse of my Dannasama.'

Speaker: 'Hey, hey, talking of dying in battle is extremely inappropriate. No matter what kind of enemy comes, leave it to them to do as they please, for Kami will surely make things turn out well...'

Speaker: 'Yes.'

The attendants serving near Daikokunushi, watching for the hour of the ox, slipped out from the back gate, headed toward the mansion of Onikumawake, which was surrounded by a forest of betel nut trees. They were clad in black, wearing straw sandals and leggings, and advanced carrying hand spears. How strange it was; in the blink of an eye, a bridge over a deep moat, about five ken wide, had collapsed without difficulty. The group stopped.

Speaker: 'Hey, this is a disaster. It seems that Onikumawake, having foreseen our approach through his divine vision, has already collapsed the bridge. If we go around to the lower bridge, it is about one and a half ri from here, and by the time we do that, the night will have dawned. A troublesome thing has happened.'

Speaker: 'Hey, if they have gone so far as to collapse the bridge and make preparations, they must be ready on the other side as well. Even if Onikumawake has few subordinates, there are certainly seventy or eighty men kept within the mansion. Every one of them is a fearless warrior. Our strength will surely not be enough. If it were a surprise attack, we might have been able to break into the inner room and finish him while they were sleeping, but now that it has come to this, it is an open battle. Hey, how about we call it off for tonight? Then, we can let the enemy lower their guard, and after two or three days have passed, we shall attempt a night raid.'

『吾々は人も恨まない、又敵も憎まない。妻子には離れ、何程結構な身の上になつたとて、一寸先は分らぬ人の身の上、ただ何事も神に任すより手段がない。神さまが吾々を殺さうと思へば、人の手をかつてお殺し遊ばすだらうし、まだ娑婆に必要があると思召したら、殺さずにおかれるだらう。一寸先は人間の目からは暗だ。只刹那の心を楽しみ、神司としての最善のベストを尽せばいいのだ』

と涙を夕立の如くパラパラとこぼしながら、早くも懐剣を引抜き、腹十文字に掻切らむとするを、鬼熊別はグツと其手を握り、

『アハヽヽヽ、何と気の早い男だなア。暇をくれと云つても暇はやらぬ、死なしてくれと申しても決して死なしはせぬぞ。主従の間柄といふものは左様な水臭いものではない。お前が死にたければ、俺の先途を見届けて其後に死んだがよからう。主人より先に勝手気儘に自殺するとは不心得千万だ』

ときめつけられて、熊彦は気を取り直し、

『これはこれは若気の至り、血気にはやり、誠にすまない事を致しました。主人の意志に従ふのは下僕の役、モウ此上は何事も申しませぬ。どうぞ主従の縁切ること丈は赦して下さいませ。決して旦那さまより先へは早まつた事は致しませぬ。同じ死ぬのならば、寄せ来る敵と渡り合ひ、旦那さまの馬前に於て、斬死を致します』

『コリヤコリヤ斬死などとは不穏当きはまる。如何なる敵が来るとも、彼がなすままに任しておけ、神さまがよきやうにして下さるだらうから……』

熊彦は、

『ハイ』

と答へてさし俯むき、左右の肩を上げ下げしながら、声を忍ばせ、しやくり泣きつつあつた。

大黒主の側近く仕へたる侍従の面々は、丑満の刻限を伺ひ、裏門よりソツと脱け出し、檳榔樹の林に包まれたる鬼熊別が館を指して、黒装束に身をかため、草鞋脚絆を穿ちながら手槍を提げ進み行く。如何はしけむ、如時の間にやら横幅五間ばかりの深溝の橋梁が苦もなく墜落して居た。一同は立止まり、

甲『ヤアこりや大変だ。鬼熊別の奴、早くも俺達の行くのを天眼通力にて前知したと見え、橋を落して了ひよつた。下手の橋へまはれば、これより一里半ばかり、さうかうしてる間に夜が明けて了ふ。困つたことが出来たワイ』

と呟いてゐる。これは熊彦がひそかに部下数人に命じ、主人の危難を救ふべく落させておいたのであつた。

乙『オイ、橋を落して用意をして居るくらいなれば、先方にも準備をして居るだらう。何程鬼熊別に部下がないと云つても館の中に抱へてある部下の者は七八十人は確に居る。何奴も此奴も皆命知らずの強者ばかりだ。到底吾々の力では及ぶまい。騙討ならば彼奴等の眠つてゐる内に、奥の間へふみ込んで仕止められぬ事もないが、モウ斯うなつては公然の戦ひだ。オイ今晩はモウ中止したら如何だ。そして敵に油断をさせ、二三日経つた所で、ソツと夜襲を試みることにしようかい』

A: 'Even so, Goshiyujinsama has placed his trust in us, and did he not say with tears in his eyes that he must by all means borrow your strength? Though there are many Tsuwamono, that the Gomeirei was given to those like us who perform Okuzuto must truly be called a Kuōei. It is precisely because his trust is deep that he has allowed us to serve in such a Himitsu Goyō. In the face of that trust, we cannot simply return home unashamed.'

B: 'No matter how much you say it is a Gomeirei, the bridge has been dropped, the enemy has Sūbai the Seiriyoku, and it is Tautei Dame. Let us somehow create a Kōjitsu and muddy the waters for Konban.'

A: 'Do not say such outrageous things. As those in the Bunzai of Kerai, is there such a thing as deceiving one's Dannasama? Tatohe our Inochi be lost, it is our duty to fulfill this Shimei. Setting aside the Seihi of the matter, my conscience will not permit it under any circumstances. We must somehow cross this bridge to the other side, give satisfaction to my conscience, and be praised as fellows of peerless loyalty, must we not?'

B: 'Hahahaha, I am amazed to hear of conscience and peerless loyalty. That being said, I too wish to achieve this purpose even if I must grind my body to powder for the sake of the master, but what can I do with a body that has no wings? If we cannot cross this bridge, it is Dame no matter what is said. Look, this torrential stream flowing down from Mount Daiun; if one should accidentally fall into the water, it would be losing both one and two, just like being bitten by a dog.'

A: 'No, to tell the truth, although I say such things, my conscience is also my conscience. It has become a little bit suspicious. It seems the peerlessly disloyal sub-guardian spirit is slowly starting to raise its head... no, it is not. While we are doing this, the night draws near to dawn. If that happens, our excuse will rather stand; because that bridge was down, we were delayed by the bridge-building work, and since the night has finally ended, let us start over and go for the night attack—has not a sweet Kōjitsu been created? This is entirely the great merciful heart of Daijizaiten-sama loving us; oh, how grateful, how solemn. I pray, Jizaiten-sama, that this bridge remains unbuilt forever... no, I do not say that. Those are the words spoken by Onikumawake. Please, may a perfect bridge be built as quickly as possible, and may Dannasama's hated enemy perish; I earnestly beseech your protection.'

B: 'Ufufufu.'

All: 'Ihishihihi.'
