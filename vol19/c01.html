Chapter 1: High 熊 Mountain （謡曲調） [646]

高熊山（謡曲調）〔646〕

§ 1

In the spring midnight when the plum blossoms scent the air, a strange radiance suddenly appeared by the bedside of a youth who tended cattle. As he gazed with wide eyes, the heartless cruelty of the world, the deep purple and the blue-yellow, the Great Way of Kami like white jewels, and the five-colored jewels of a red heart flew to and fro within the chamber of the humble hut. Before he knew when, they aimed at the youth's body, seeping into his chest, his belly, his shoulders, and his back. The youth, suddenly undergoing a transformation of spirit, being an Utsusomi nearing his thirties, took up his brush of Inochige; no sooner had he done so than he reflected the true moonlight upon the sea of the Sumisuzuri, where he labored. Illuminated by the Mitama of Kami, he approached the alcove and wrote swiftly upon the wall: Ametsuchiōmotōomikami. A youth who had been consumed by the thought that, contrary to yesterday, there was no Kami in this world today, was chanting the divine name with reverence and bowing, and just as he was apologizing for the sins of his own body, someone knocked at the gate.

Divine Messenger: 'I am the messenger of Konohana-hime, who manifests upon Mount Tenkyō. I shall guide you to the land of the pedestal, where the plum blossoms open and the lotus leaves flourish, in the age of Miroku of Matsuoka. Open this gate quickly.'

Youth: 'How strange, that there should be one knocking at the golden gate in the middle of this night. The wind is calm and even the waters are still; as it is the hour of the Rat, pray visit me after the dawn has broken.'

Before the Matsuoka Divine Messenger had finished speaking, he slid the door open and entered with composure.

Divine Messenger: 'I am the messenger of the Imperial Great Kami of Mount Tenkyō. Now, Konohana-sakuya-hime-no-Mikoto stands upon the Lotus Mountain and gazes high into the western sky; as auspicious clouds trail and the starlight shines wondrously, it must be a sign of the manifestation of the true man of the divine plan. You shall swiftly, most swiftly, receive my divine words, soar through the sky of the clouds, push open the eight-fold clouds of heaven, and depart for the village of Anō, which surrounds the blue-walled mountains of Tanba, where the sky of the heart is also clear. There, you shall proclaim my divine words and bring them back to welcome me; such is the divine decree. Follow me at once and ascend to Mount Tenkyō.'

The youth, growing more suspicious, gazed upon the face of the Matsuoka Divine Messenger and remained for a while in a daze. At that very moment, a gust of spring wind blew fiercely, scattering plum petals as it passed over the exterior of the house. Suddenly, exquisite music was heard within the dwelling, and while he watched the plum petals fluttering down upon his head, the messenger was wrapped in a purple mantle and became white clouds.

Youth: 'Ah, am I a bird traversing the sky? Riding upon the clouds far on high, I gaze, forgetting myself, at the people of the earth, each caught in their joys and sorrows, gesturing with hands and feet. Truly, the world of men is a curious thing. Yet, I fear lest I become too carried away by the excitement and fall to the earth. Alas, Great Kami, Great Kami, I pray you guard my body forever and ever, for a thousand generations and eight thousand generations.'

原文（近代文語）

梅咲き匂ふ春の夜半、牛飼ふ男子の枕辺に、忽然として現はれ出でたる異様の輝き、眼を睜り眺むれば、世人の無情残酷を、うら紫や青黄色、神の大道も白玉や、赤き心の五色の玉は、賤が伏屋の室内を、右往左往に飛び交ひて、何時とはなしに男子の身体目蒐けて、或は胸に、或は腹に、肩に背中に滲み込み、男子は忽ち心機一転して、三十路に近き現身の、命毛の筆執るより早く、苦労する墨硯の海に、うつす誠の月照の、神の御霊に照らされて、床の間近く立寄りつ、壁にさらさら書き下ろす、天地大本大御神、今日は昨日に引替へて、此世に神なきものをぞと、思ひ詰めたる青年が、恭敬礼拝神号を唱へつつ、吾身の罪を詫ぶる折しも、門の戸を叩き訪ふ者あり。

神使『吾こそは、天教山に現はれ給ふ木の花姫の御使、弥勒の御代を松岡の、梅花も開く蓮葉の、台の国に導かむ。早く此門あけさせ給へ』

若男『あら訝かしや、此真夜中に金門を敲く人の有りとは。風も静まり水さへも、子の正刻に候へば、夜の明くるを待つて訪れ給へ』

松岡神使は詞も終らぬに、戸をさらりと引き開け、悠々として入り来り、

神使『吾は天教山の皇大神の御使なり。抑も木花咲耶姫命、蓮華の山に立たせ給ひて、西の空高く望ませ給へば、瑞雲棚引き、星の光奇く照させ給へば、神の仕組の真人の現はれ給ふ瑞祥ならむ。汝迅く迅く我神言を奉じ、雲井の空を翔つけて、天の八重雲押開き、心の空も丹波の、青垣山を繞らせる、穴太の郷に出立ちて、我神言を宣り伝へ、迎へ帰れとの御神勅なり。早々吾に続かせ、天教山に参上り給へ』

男子は益々訝かりつ、松岡神使の顔を打眺め、暫し茫然として居たりけり。時しもあれや一陣の春風烈しく、梅の花片を撒いて、家の外面を亘りゆく。忽ち屋内に美妙の音楽聞え、梅の花片チラチラと、男子が頭に降かかるよと見る間に、紫の被衣に包まれ、あと白雲となりにける。

若男『嗚呼吾は空行く鳥なれや。遥に高き雲に乗り、地上の人が各自に、喜怒哀楽に捉はれて、手振り足振りする様を、吾を忘れて眺むなり。実に面白の人の世や。然れども余り興に乗り、地上に落つる事もがな。嗟、大神よ大神よ、千代に八千代に永久に吾身を守らせ給へかし』

§ 2

With a single stroke of the brush, a writing was left behind in the humble house of Fuseya, and at the mouth of the mountain where the Shiyōjiyu grew thick, the Shinshi Matsuoka, clad in Yōfukusugata, suddenly transformed into a strange Kami with Hakuhatsu of Iyō appearance; he took the Onoko by the hand, and they proceeded eagerly through the thicket of the forest, until at this place, as the moon of the ninth day of Kisaragi tilted toward Seizan and the curtain of darkness closed once more, they arrived before the Gankutsu of Senjiyō.

The Shinshi Matsuoka made an Ichirei to the Onoko, and the Shinshi said, 'This is the Gankutsu of Takakumayama, famed as the Ishiya of Takaamahara, where the hundred Kami of Ametsuchi gather at times in Kamutsudo, and where there are Shijifuhachiko of Hōza. Here I shall part from thee. Thou shalt cast off the coarse garments of Utsushiyo here, and as a Shinjin of the Mizu no Mitama, serve the divine work of Miroku. Farewell, farewell.'

Scarcely had he spoken when his form vanished, and he was seen faintly drifting as a white cloud in the distant sky, a mystery to behold. The Onoko suddenly felt his body harden, transcending time and space, standing beyond the reach of cold and heat, and before he knew it, his form had changed; he was now clad in white robes, endowed with golden wings, and like an arrow shot forth, he pierced the eight-fold clouds of heaven along a bridge of light shining in the sky, and in an instant, his body stood upon the summit of Mount Sumeru. A Shinjin, riding a white horse and parting the white clouds, appeared before the Onoko, dismounted, approached with firm steps, and grasped the left hand of the Onoko tightly.

The Myōjin said, 'I am Obata Daimyōjin. Upon this occasion of the emergence of the divine age of Miroku, in accordance with the command of the Amatsukami and Kunitsukami, thy Mitama, which has manifested for a time in Tanshu, has been commanded to explore the three realms of Shin, Yū, and Kami, and to participate in the divine work; such being the divine decree, for thirty-five years past, I have communed with Konohana-hime, received thy Mitama, and caused thee to be born as a child of my clan. Do not doubt this, not even in a dream.'

The Onoko was startled and uttered a single 'Hai,' then bowed his head, and just as he was overcome with tears, he was awakened by the wind blowing through the pines; looking about him, he wondered what this could mean, for though he knew not the place, it was somehow familiar, and he found it a marvel that he was sitting upright before a pure Gankutsu. The Onoko tilted his head, and the Wakao said, 'In the depths of this deep mountain, far from the villages of men, I know not what mountain this may be, yet for some reason, it is a place that stirs the heart with courage. Until yesterday, I was an Onoko who tended cattle, diligent in the occupation of the lowly, yet now my own attire shines on all sides; purple, blue, red, white, and yellow, with plum blossoms scattered upon a white ground, and the pattern of pine needles upon the hem of my skirt. When did I change my garments? Suddenly, I have taken the form of a goddess, and my heart is calm as the spring breeze that brushes over the blossoms of the trees; it is a feeling of utmost serenity.'

原文（近代文語）

と唯一筆の落し書、賤が伏家に遺し置き、松樹茂れる山の口、洋服姿の松岡神使、俄に白髪異様の神人と変化し、男子が手を把り、林の茂みをイソイソと、進むで此処に如月の、九日の月西山に傾きて、再び閉ざす闇の幕、千丈の岩窟の前に着きにけり。

松岡神使は男子に一礼し、

神使『此処は名におふ、高天原の移写と聞えたる高熊山の岩窟にて候、天地百の神たちの、時有つて神集ひに集ひ給ふ、四十八個の宝座あり。吾はこれにて袂を別たむ。汝は此処に現世の粗き衣を脱ぎ、瑞の御霊の真人として、五六七の神業に奉仕せよ。さらばさらば』

と云ふかと見れば、姿は消えて白雲の、彼方の空に幽かに靉く訝かしさ。男子は忽ち身体硬化し、時間空間を超越し、寒暑の外に立ちて、何時とはなしに身変定の、今の姿と白装束、黄金の翼身に備へ、一道の空に輝く光の架橋、矢を射る如く天の八重雲切り抜けて、須弥仙山の頂上に、早くも其身は立ちにける。白馬に跨り、白雲別けて駆来る一人の神人、男子の前に立ち現はれ、馬を乗り棄てツカツカと立寄り、男子の左手をシツカと握り、

明神『われは小幡大明神なり。此度五六七の神世出現に際し、天津神国津神の依さしのまにまに、暫時丹州と現はれ給ふ汝が御霊、現幽神三界の探険を命じ、神業に参加せしめよとの神勅なれば、三十五年の昔より、木の花姫と語らひて、汝が御霊を拝領し、我が氏の子として生れ出でしめたり。ゆめゆめ疑ふ事勿れ』

男子は驚き『ハイ』と一声、さし俯き、涙に暮るる折しもあれ、松吹く風に覚されて、四辺を見れば此は如何に、処はシカと分らねど、何処とはなしに見覚えの、有難や、清き岩窟の前に端坐し居るこそ不思議なれ。男子は首を傾け、

若男『人里離れし此深山の奥、何処の山かは知らねども、何とはなしに、心勇ましき所かな。牛飼ふ男子の昨日まで、賤の職業に励精みし身の、四辺に輝く吾身の服装、紫青赤白黄色、白地に梅の花は散り、裳裾に松葉の模様、何時の間にかは更衣や、忽ち変る女神の姿、心も凪ぎて春風の、木々の木の花撫で渡る、いとも長閑な思ひなり』

§ 3

Suddenly, music was heard in the Kokū, and what had until Imamade appeared to be a Gankutsu, whether it were a dream, reality, or illusion, was a Daikiuden of Saugon and Muhi, unfolding without Saigen in Yomo.

The Kogane roof tiles shone in the morning sun, and when one gazed at the Tanima in the Ganka, upon the surface of the pond where green hues drifted, Oshi in Tsugahi were here and Kashiko.

Ayame flowers, though out of season, bloomed in profusion, and the voices of Momotori singing were such that one might think, 'Is this how the Tengoku and Jiyōdo must be?'

Aa, while he was lost in Shian, tilting his head and wondering, 'How strange, how strange,' there was a voice from the Anata of the Kiuden,

'Zuigetsu, Zuigetsu,'

it called out.

The Onoko strained his ears to this voice, and as he stood there, the Wakaiotoko said, 'Who is this Tarebito who calls me Zuigetsu? There is no one here but myself; this is a mysterious matter.'

At that very moment, flapping golden wings and appearing toward this place, with a golden kite in the Sentō, followed by peacocks, phoenixes, Kalavinka, and Yatagarasu, he saw that before he knew it, the body of Wani had advanced once more to the vicinity of the moon.

As he watched, the golden doors were opened, and from within appeared the figure of a goddess like a plum blossom, holding a jewel in her hand, with two handmaidens carrying small branches of pine and plum, and she spoke with quiet words.

'I am she who, three thousand years ago, together with Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, left the sacred land of Nakatsu-mikuni and arrived at the land of the roots; but having attained the time of the plum blossoms opening at once, I soared again to the heavens, and now the time has come for the peaches in the garden of Seiobo to shed their flowers and bear fruit, which I shall offer to Sumerōkami.

'If the time is delayed, it will be a great matter; by the consent of Obata-myōjin, from this moment I shall borrow the body of your life.'

As he watched her speak, her figure vanished into white smoke, and suddenly he was astride a heavenly horse, escorted by Obata-myōjin, gazing upon the world outside the universe, and pointing toward the earth, he descended.

Where is this place? It is before the throne of before; it is a mysterious sequence of events.

This chapter is dictated in the manner of a Yōkyoku, so those who are experts, please amend the length of the words or refine them as you see fit for chanting.

原文（近代文語）

忽ち虚空に音楽聞え、今迄岩窟と見えしは、夢か現か幻か、際限もなく四方に展開せる、荘厳無比の大宮殿、黄金の甍旭に輝き、眼下の渓間を眺むれば、緑漂ふ池の面に、鴛鴦の比翼の此処彼処、時ならぬ菖蒲の花も咲きみだれ、百鳥の唄ふ声、天国浄土も斯くやあらむと思はるる許りなり。嗚呼訝かしや訝かしやと、首を傾げ思案に暮るる時しもあれ、宮殿の彼方に声ありて、

『瑞月、瑞月』

と呼ばせ給ふ。男子は此声に耳を澄ませ、

若男『瑞月とは誰人なるか、われより外に人も無し。怪しき事よ』

と佇む折しも、黄金の翼を飜し、此場に向つて現はれ来る、黄金の鵄を先頭に、孔雀、鳳凰、迦陵頻伽、八咫烏、何時とはなしに王仁の身は、又もや月の付近まで進み居るよと見る中に、黄金の扉は開かれて、中より現はれ給ふ梅花の如き女神の姿、二人の侍女に松梅の小枝を持たせ、御手に玉を携へて、言葉静かに宣り給ふ。

女神『われこそは三千年の其昔、国治立の大神と共に、中津御国の聖地を後にして、根底の国に到りしが、一度に開く梅花の時を得て、再び天に舞ひ昇り、今は西王母が園の桃、花散り実のる時ぞ来て、皇大神に奉らむ。時遅れては一大事、小幡明神の承諾に依りて、今より汝が命の体を借らむ』

と言ふかと見れば、姿は消えて白煙、忽ち其身は天馬に跨り、小幡明神に送られて、宇宙の外の世界を眺め、地上を指して降り来る。此処は何処ぞ、以前の宝座の前、不思議なりける次第なり。
