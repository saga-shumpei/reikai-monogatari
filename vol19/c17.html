Chapter 17: Kotodama 車 [662]

言霊車〔662〕

§ 1

Looking up, how distant is that ancient past. In the boundless Daiuchiu, when Tenchi were not yet fixed, and in that time when Inyōmibun, a single entity sprouted like Ashigahi and contained Ōga, suddenly transformed and became Kami. This is the Ōmoto of Tenchi, Ōkunitokotachimikoto. From that Mitama, the Ōkami of Kuniharutachi, who appeared as the ancestor of Tenchi, joined forces with the Himegami of Toyokuninushi, and making their Mikokoro one, they created a beautiful Sekai, gave birth to the Shichijifugosei of Kototama, created Chiyorozu of Mitama, gave birth to the lands, and created Aohitogusa and Yamakaha.

Having finished creating everything in detail, the Ōkami of Kamuizanagi and the Ōkami of Kamuizanami were granted the Nuhoko of Ama, and just as they were entrusted with the Ōmiwaza of Shiurikosei, the Mikoto of the Kami Susanoo, who appeared, with awe, governed the great ocean. The Ōkami of Kuniharutachi and the Himegami of Toyokuninushi, taking the Ōmikokoro as their own heart, broke their hearts into a thousand pieces. Bearing the Chikura-no-Okido, they sought to govern the land of Yashima, and though they distributed their hearts, the strange spirits that had become the ugly Mitama of Amatarashi-hiko and Enaba-hime gathered and congealed, and it became an era where Yamata-no-Orochi, ugly foxes, ugly women, searchers, and crooked demons ran wild.

The clouds of Taishureiju covered the land, and the world ended up in eternal darkness. The Ōkami of Amaterasu, who governs the land of the Sun, feared this state of affairs, and deeply shut herself in the Iwayato, trembling and hiding. The hundred Kami were startled, gathered at the river of Yasu, and as a result of their deliberations, they decided that the Ōkami of Kami Susanoo should be the redeemer of the hundred thousand crimes of the Kami and humans of the four directions of Tenchi. When they drove the Kami Susanoo from Takamagahara, the Kami Susanoo, having no choice, cast aside his noble rank.

Drifting on the great ocean, he sought to subdue the Magatsukami on the mountain ridges of the eighty islands and hundred lands of the islands, and to begin the divine age of Miroku by harmonizing them with Kototama, and while enduring a hundred sorrows, he wandered far and near through the land of Yashima; how noble he is.

Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should overturn, the Amatsukami and Kunitsukami, the hundred Kami and the hundred people, drawing the sword of Izumi of the one Kototama of Makoto, seek to subdue the Magatsukami and Yamata-no-Orochi that obstruct Tenchi, and to sweep away the calamities of this world.

The Ōkami of Kami Susanoo, while upholding the Sangokyō which forgives all sins, and with a heart as wide as the Kami Naohi, looking back to see things as Oonaohi, while bestowing painful divine commands upon the eight pillars of Hime-miko who appeared from the palace of flesh, rouses his Yamato-gokoro and his heroic heart, and advances.

Hiding in the mansion of Iso, Hinode-no-Kami and Konohana-hime-no-Mikoto, together with the others, pour the dew of grace upon the world beneath heaven, throughout all the lands in the four directions, without leaving a single corner; how grateful we are.

Appearing as the divine Haniyasuhiko and Haniyasuhime, the Wakemitama of Kuniharutachi and Toyokuni-hime-no-Mikoto have manifested at the foot of Ōgonsanka, intending to illuminate this dark world; with the bitterness of a hototogisu crying out eight thousand and eight times, as if spitting blood, they have piled up the long years, solidifying the foundation of the Miroku Shinsei in a state of tokihakakiha.

In Toyoashihara no Mizuhokuni, Akitsu no Shima, Tsukushijima, the land of Tokoyo, and the island of Takasago, they have established the Kamuzukasa and Kunitamagami in each place; gradually, the Ame no Iwato begins to open, and Eiko-hime-no-Mikoto appears, as does Etsuko-hime-no-Mikoto, whose teachings bloom like flowers, and the purple Hime-no-Mikoto, who drifts in the sky, appears.

At the foot of the four-tailed peak, the center of Jitentōtō, where the brocade loom weaves, they firmly and minutely solidify the Keirin of the warp and weft of the yūgen-myōjō divine realm; the Keirin, which opens all at once like the fragrance of plum blossoms, is as deep in color as the thousand-year-old pine, and the color of the heart is also that of the sacred ground of Tanba, where the two pillars, Tamateruhiko-no-Mikoto and Tamateruhime-no-Mikoto, reside.

Waiting for the season, the Itsunomitama and Mizunomitama flow most purely, and the Wachi River washes away the defilement of the heaven and earth that have become completely turbid; lying in a room in the back of Shōunkaku, which stands on the riverbank where rows of pine trees are lined up, the noble Keirin of the Miroku Shinsei is recounted; Ah, Kannagara-kamugami-kamugami, may the spirits be blessed.

As far as the eye can see, in the deep blue sky, Tamateruhiko-no-Mikoto and Tamateruhime-no-Mikoto appear clearly, and the story begins of how they initiate the divine work of the spirit of Idunome in the era of Miroku; moved by the infinite benevolence and sincerity of Lord Susanoo-no-Ōkami, who protects the Sangyōkyō, even the Magatsu-kami is impressed and repents from the bottom of its heart, and the Kamuzukasa of the Uranai-kyō submits to the original teaching; the story is told by the hand of Matsumura, as he recounts the auspicious tale of Takahime, Takayamahiko, and Kurohime, whose sins and impurities were redeemed by Matsuhime of the pine heart, who opened the door of the heart at the foot of Mount Takakuma, and who, having had their souls forged by the god who forbids the approach of demons, rushed into the cave of Takakuma, welcomed the noble child, and participated in the divine plan to govern the world beneath heaven peacefully and tranquilly, with a sincerity that has blossomed like a flower after three thousand years, now at the dawn of the Miroku divine age, as the Sendenshi of Matsuchikubai, beginning with Tsuki-Yuki-Hana, the Yashimanushi who opens the teaching, pulls the thread to illuminate the world with the one truth of the Ōmoto, without distinction between friend and foe, through the Kototama of Koto-yori-wake.

Mujinibosatsu's mountain-top master, illuminating the head and illuminating the body, the mountain-top. The flesh-palace of Tsukiteruhiko, the Kuwanjizai that opens the Kototama. The Sanjifusansau and also the Shisau, the Shinriki of Meuonbosatsu, now revealing the Tsuzu volume. Illuminated by the eternal Haruhi, it is a joy to tell the Monogatari.

Blowing away the Kumokiri blocking the Yomo with the Miiki of Kami, the heart is pure and the body is pure. The Mirokuden in the blue Misora, the Shinriyoku of Hongūyama, reveals the Riusei of Ōmotokyō in the color of its robes, as if celebrating the Yukute. The Kototama-kaku wears a golden crown above the clouds.

Towering and guarding the lower world, the Kyōso-den with its shining roof tiles, where the teacher of the teaching is enshrined. The Kinryū-den and the Kyōshu-den, the treetops are green, and the garden of Kami where the spring breeze blows softly. The Suisui-tei floating on the water, the Zuigetsu shining on the earth and water, tells the story without end. The opening of the Zui no Mitama, the power of Kami is also the Izunomitama. The thundering sound of the Isuzu waterfall, the strange and noble Monogatari of the age of Kami, whose limits are unknown. Resounding through Takamagahara, the wind of Kami with a cool voice from the ever-higher Kototama-kaku. Blowing forever and ever, making the ugly plants and trees bow, clearing the clouds that block the hearts of the people of the world.

To guide and save them into the divine land of eternal spring where flowers bloom is truly heroic. Ah, Kannagara Kami-kami, may the spirits be blessed.

More than twenty years of the reign of the sage, where the sun and moon are aligned and peace prevails. In the fifth year, in the month of harmony, in the middle of the night when the cold wind rages. Within the grounds of the Hongū and Shingū, in the place known since the distant age of Kami as a noble sacred site, the Ryūmon-kan divine mansion. The Ushitora no Sumera Ōkami, who appeared there, is the pine flower of three thousand years of endurance and patience. In the house of the humble where no one breaks the branch, the widow, the flesh-palace of Deguchi Nao-ko, was possessed like lightning. The Kototama proclaimed is the plum blossom of the three thousand worlds, the time has come for it to bloom all at once. Sitting on Mount Sumeru, he turns the world where the Magatsu-kami rages.

By turning them toward Kami with the Miiki of Kami, and opening the age of Miroku, he stomps and shouts with the Izunomitama, raising the fallen of the Izunomitama. Opening the mouth of the exit of Kami, the heroism of solidifying the foundation of Ōmotokyō. The humble woman of the humble house awakened her eyes to the voice of Kami, and in the dark night where black and white could not be distinguished, she sought to return the world to the age of Kami of the sunrise, of the dazzling Asahiko. Morning and evening, taking up the brush of her life-hair, recording the words of Kami with a single heart. For twenty-seven years, as if it were but a single day, the words she served were revered as the noble voice of Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami.

The Morobito gathering here, without distinction of far or near, Kisenraueu all together, approach the Seichi. In the Iyachiko of the Miizu of Kami, Ōmoto prospers Hibi, like the Toyosaka rising of the Asahi, illuminating the Kuniguni of Yomo.

Appearing as Henjiyōnanshi, weaving the Tateito of the brocade loom, the Shigumi has been prepared, and here Nanatose of Tsukihi have passed in waiting. At that very time, in the autumn of the Misoama-plus-one year, the Ikumitama of Henjiyōniyoshi, having received the Oshihe of Kami, left the village of Anao behind, and this became the connection to visit the Sumika of Henjiyōnanshi.

And here, appearing as Hataorihime, copying the Mioshihe of the awe-inspiring Kami, which is Kakemaku, onto the Nihibumi of the Izu-no-shigumi, the Tasukijifuji reveals the Shinreikai. When the Pharisees, narrow-minded and shallow in wisdom, misunderstood the Shinchoku of Kingentamakotoba and made groundless Kototama, the pure Mioshihe was gradually wrapped in clouds, the sky of Takamagahara grew dark, and the hearts of men could not distinguish black from white.

The Mizu-no-mitama, grieving, determined to clear away these clouds and mists, and while in the midst of such heart-pain, suddenly a thunderous roar fell from above the clouds, and like a bird in a cage unable to move, the flock of sparrows chirping 'Chu' finally lowered their voices. With the Kamigoto of the Mizu-no-mitama, determined to awaken the eyes of the Pharisees and the people of the world to the noble Oshihe of Kami and save them, the noble heart of Kami, which stands to proclaim even with a body weary of illness, is copied onto the brush, the story of the origin of the prosperity of the Matsu-no-yo. Appearing as Garyōnyorai and lying in Shōunkaku, comparing the two persons, Sagai Isao who searches for fallen leaves and Yuasa Kiyotaka who cleanses filth in the hot springs, to the roles of Kongōdōji and Setaka, the Kami-bashira that had fallen over, with the utmost sincerity and devotion, seeks to restore.
