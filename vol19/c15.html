Chapter 15: Mountain Kami の滝 [660]

山神の滝〔660〕

§ 1

Matsuhime was guided by Kunadomenokami and shown into a small, two-room chamber beside the gate.

Kunadomenokami: 'As you have come to worship at this Shinzan as a woman in the dead of this Anya, there must be some deep Wake. As it is my Yakume to guard this Sekishiyo, I have a Hitsueu to ask you this Ichiō, so please state your Jijiyō without concealment or reservation.'

Matsuhime: 'It is an Ohazu matter to speak of, but I have been making a Taihen Torichiga until now. At the mansion at the foot of Takashiroyama, a Deyashiro of the Uranai-kyō, I have been a woman of deep sin, constantly causing Ojiyama to Lord Susanōnōokami in opposition to Ananai-kyō. My Shishiyō, those called Takahime and Kurohime, taught doctrines that were in great Hantai to Lord Susanōnomikoto, and I took them to heart; I continued my activities day and night with the resolve to expand the Uranai-kyō for the sake of the nation and to destroy from the roots the Ananai-kyō, which is under the jurisdiction of Kotoyorihiko and Yashimanushinokami, of the faction of Susanōnomikoto. However, Lord Susanōnomikoto is not the kind of person we ordinary mortals think, but the Redeemer of the world of great mercy and compassion; Takahime-sama was the first to realize this, and being unable to sit or stand still, she consulted with Kurohime-sama, and a detailed letter arrived for me as well. Therefore, as I must have the sins of Takahime-sama and Kurohime-sama forgiven, I, as their disciple, could not sit or stand still either; I wished to perform some kind of severe ascetic practice, manifest a meritorious deed, and take that as a gift to Ananai-kyō to have the sins of my masters and myself forgiven. With that alone in mind, I abandoned the mansion at Takashiroyama and came here alone, trudging to this sacred mountain to perform asceticism, intending to somehow welcome Tamateruhiko-sama and return to Ananai-kyō with him as a gift.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Ah, I see. I had also heard vaguely of this from Kototeruhime-sama. However, although you seem to have undergone a considerable change of heart, there are still many auxiliary guardian spirits lurking within your belly, so even if you go as you are, Tamateruhiko-sama will not accept you. There is a waterfall of the mountain Kami ahead; you must perform severe asceticism there for seven days and seven nights to drive out the auxiliary guardian spirits, return to your most pure and refined original heart, and polish yourself like a crystal ball before you can go, or it will be of no use.'

§ 2

Matsuhime: 'Indeed, it must be so. I shall not loathe any kind of severe asceticism, no matter what it may be; please, give me your orders.'

Kunadomenokami: 'The training here is extremely harsh; are you able to endure it to the end?'

Matsuhime: 'I do not mind how harsh it may be. Even if my life should be lost, if only my master's sins are erased, I shall be satisfied with that.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Ah, that is an admirable resolution. If that is so, from this moment without delay, perform your training at the Waterfall of the Mountain Kami; although there is no fasting from food or drink in the Way of the Kami, for the sake of atoning for your own sins, the sins of your master, and the sins of your subordinates in general, you must fast from food and drink for seven days and seven nights, and on top of that perform severe asceticism, or your sins will truly never be erased.'

Matsuhime: 'I humbly request your favor in all things.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Katsu and Take, both of you, come out here for a moment.'

Immediately upon these words, the two appeared at this place.

Katsukō: 'What is your business with us?'

Kunadomenokami: 'It is nothing else, but since this Matsuhime-sama is to perform seven days and seven nights of severe asceticism at the Waterfall of the Mountain Kami, you must take turns providing her with sufficient care. During the asceticism, you must never show her sympathy or pity. You are to treat her with as much cruelty as possible. Otherwise, it would be a repeated discourtesy and offense against the Kami, and the sins would never be extinguished no matter how much time passes; if you wish to save Matsuhime-sama, you must let her perform a sufficiently strict discipline.'

Katsukō: 'Yes, I have understood. Since we must also serve as gatekeepers, Take-san and I will take turns looking after her.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Ah, yes. Supervise the young one well, and without any negligence, let her perform sufficient severe asceticism, and polish her into a splendid human being.'

The two bowed, and said:

'Now, Matsuhime-sama, let us go to the waterfall basin immediately. Just as it is quite hot and painful when one is given a large moxibustion treatment, yet the pleasure when a great illness is completely cured because of it is something that cannot be described in words, I shall now give you a large moxibustion. However, I am never doing this out of hatred, so I ask that you do not think ill of me.'

Matsuhime: 'I, whose sins are heavy, will contentedly undergo any kind of harsh discipline. I humbly request your favor.'

Katsukō: 'All right, all right, come this way, you woman Matsuhime. If you keep dawdling, shall I crack your head open?'

He suddenly manifested a great change in his words and actions.

Matsuhime: 'Yes.'

She replied and followed him.

Katsukō stood in the lead, while Takekō followed behind Matsuhime, striking her back and poking her buttocks with a wooden club.

Takekō: 'Hey Matsuhime, why are you dawdling? Why don't you walk faster? What a nuisance. You come along after the sun has set, and just when we were thinking of sleeping comfortably, we have to escort you to the waterfall and treat you to some serious abuse. This is the punishment for your arrogance and for opposing the Ōkamisama.'

While saying this, he struck Matsuhime on the back of her head with the wooden club. Enduring the pain, Matsuhime said,

Matsuhime: 'Thank you very much. Will this perhaps lighten my sins even a little?'

Takekō: 'What? Even if I strike you a hundred or two hundred times, or crack your head open, do you think your sins will be easily cleansed?'

Katsukō: 'Hey Takekō, that is too much.'

Takekō: 'What do you mean, too much? How did you hear the Okotoba of Kunadomenokami? Did He not say that if you have any kindness for Matsuhime, you should abuse her thoroughly?'

Katsukō: 'Well, that is true, but you should not do anything so excessive. It is fine to abuse her thoroughly, but there is a limit to everything; do not lose your sense of humanity. Do you understand?'

Takekō: 'You act so superior, standing at the front, ignoring the Okotoba of Kunadomenokami, and furthermore, are you trying to hinder Matsuhime's shiugiyō and make her heavy sins even heavier?'

Matsuhime: 'Excuse me, you two, please do not argue on my account. It would be disrespectful to the Kami.'

Takekō: 'Eh, that woman Matsuhime, why are you interfering so much? If you spout such nonsense, I will give you another treat. Or perhaps I should use this club to strike you with all my might, enough to make your head fly off?'

Matsuhime: 'I am grateful for your repeated kindness. However, I am sorry for causing you trouble. Since you must be tired, please rest for today.'

Takekō: 'Do not say such clever things. It seems that getting your head struck is painful after all. For some time now, I have been feeling so irritated that I have wanted to strike any rock or tree I saw, and my arms have been aching to do so. Today, fortunately, I am obeying the command of Kunadomenokami and will treat your head as much as I like, to my heart's content. Be grateful. Aragyō is a painful thing, is it not? It is a bit different from the level of the Uranai sect, where you just splash water on yourself from morning till night like a frog or something. No matter how painful it is, even if you are on the verge of death, it is only a matter of seven days and seven nights of endurance. If you fail in your shiugiyō here, you will be cast down to the deepest land for all eternity for your own sins of obstructing the path of the Ōkamisama, and you will have to suffer infinite agony. This much is just the beginning of the evening. Since this suffering is not even like one hair on a cow, you must perform your shiugiyō with courage.'

Takekō: 'Matsuhime-sama, I am sorry, but please bear with it for a while. Kunadomenokami is a very strict Kami and will not show even a fraction of leniency, so I, too, have actually hidden tears in my heart and acted rudely. Since the world is what is called a curse, the Kamisama will change great calamities into small ones, so I will now strip down to my bare skin and help you with your practice. That is the logic by which seven days' worth can be finished in three and a half days. You probably think your head was split and bleeding, but please be at ease, for that is not blood. Takekō: 'It certainly will. The Kamisama will forgive you if you just perform the form. Why would He choose to make the beloved children of the world suffer? The heart with which you decided to apologize by performing an ascetic practice at the risk of your life has already forgiven your sins. You, as you are now, no longer have any sins at all. You have the heart of a truly newborn baby. However, since it is such a pleasant waterfall, it would be good if you purified yourself even further. But Kunadomenokami, though he may look like that, is actually an incarnation of Enma-sama, so he is very strict with rewards and punishments. Even Katsukō, who just left, is a truly kind and compassionate person. However, he is foolishly honest, so he is worried, thinking that I truly abused you.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, is that so? I have no words to express my gratitude. Please continue to guide me in every way.'

竹公『松姫さま、御気の毒ですが、どうぞ暫らく辛抱して下さい。来勿止神は中々厳格な神で寸分も仮借をしませぬから、私も実は満腔の涙を隠して、失礼なことを致しました。併し乍ら到底貴女の身体では、此の荒行は続きますまい。世は呪と言うて神様は、大難を小難に祭り替へて下さるのだから、私もこれからスツパリと素裸体になつて、貴女の行を助けて上げよう。さうすれば七日のものは三日半で済むといふ道理だ。お前さま、頭を割られて血が出たと思つてゐるだらうが、ありや血ぢやありませぬから安心なさい。私が紅殻の汁を棒の先の革袋に括りつけて撲つたのですよ。血と見えたのは袋の紅殻だ。撲られた割には痛くはありますまいがな』

松姫『ハイ、さうでございました。別に何処も痛んで居りませぬ。斯んなことで神様の御意に召すやうな荒行が出来ませうかな』

竹公『出来ますとも。神様は形だけをすれば赦して下さいます。可愛い世界の氏子に何を好んで辛い目をさせなさいませう。貴女が生命がけの荒行をして、御詫をしようと決心なさつた其の心が、既に貴方の罪を赦して居ります。唯今の貴女は最早ちつとも罪は無いのですよ。本当の生れ赤児の心ですワ。併し乍ら余り気分のよい滝ですから、清めた上に浄めてお出でになつたら宜敷からう。併し来勿止神は、あゝ見えても実際は閻魔さまの化身ですから、中々賞罰を厳重になさるのです。今帰つた勝公だつて本当に優しい、慈悲深い人間です。併し乍ら彼奴は馬鹿正直ですから私が本当に貴女を虐待したのだと思つて心配をして居るのです』

松姫『アヽさうでございますか。なんとも御礼の申しやうは御座いませぬ。何分よろしう御指導を願ひます』

§ 6

Thus two or three days passed, and the morning of the fourth day arrived.

Matsuhime: 'What a solemn scene this is. Nichirinsama shines upon this waterfall, painting a seven-colored rainbow, while the indescribably delicate songs of the birds and the hues of the evergreens make it seem just like Tengoku, does it not?'

Takekō: 'Indeed it is so. Because your heart has been purified, all things in Uchiu appear solemn and grand, and the whole environment has been transformed into a paradise.'

Matsuhime: 'Takashiroyama is also a place richly endowed with scenery, but it can by no means be compared to this.'

Takekō: 'That was because your heart was clouded. Look at it again next time; it will be even more splendid than this scenery.'

Just as they were speaking thus, Katsukō came running with a smiling face.

Katsukō: 'Ah, Matsuhime-san, Take-san, thank you for your hard work. Today is only the middle of your ascetic practice, but a command has come from Kunadomenokami-sama that your training is already complete, so you may immediately come and worship at the mountain. Please rejoice.'

Matsuhime: 'That is more gratifying than anything else.'

Facing the waterfall basin, she finished offering a prayer of thanksgiving, and then the three of them set off together, returning toward the hermitage of Kunadomenokami.

Takekō: 'Kami-sama, thanks to you, Matsuhime-sama's ascetic training has ended without incident.'

Matsuhime: 'Kunadomenokami-sama, through your deep and broad grace, my defiled spirit has been cleansed.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Ah, was it so? Splendid, splendid! With this, you are now magnificent enough to be presented anywhere. Thanks to your training, we shall also be able to welcome Tamateruhiko-sama. Your master's sins shall also be completely forgiven; you have done well to endure such painful practice. Ah, Takekō, you too have shown great concern and thoughtfulness. You are the only one who truly understands my heart.'

And he softly wiped his tears of joy with his sleeve. For a while, a curtain of silence fell. At that moment, four men came running hurriedly to the front gate.

Men: 'Excuse us, please open this gate.'

Katsu stood up and opened the great stone gate to the left and right with a creak. Seeing the figures of the four men, Katsu was astonished.

Katsukō: 'Hey, you are those outrageous fellows who came the other day, Tanimaru and Onimaru of Brahmanism, and you have brought two more allies with you. Thinking it was Tamateruhiko-sama, you carried a large rock home as if it were precious, but realizing your mistake on the way, you must have come a second time to welcome him. Regardless of anyone else, it is the strict command of Kunadomenokami-sama that you alone shall not be permitted to pass through this gate!'

§ 7

Tanimaru and Onimaru sat flat upon the ground, and while shedding tears, they spoke.

Tanimaru, Onimaru: 'Pray, Monbansama, the Tanimaru and Onimaru of today are different from the two of the other day. Please, be at ease.'

Katsukō: 'Even if you say you are different, your appearance and your figure, is there not one thing about you that has changed?'

Tanimaru, Onimaru: 'Yes, in form there is not the slightest change, but my heart has changed as much as heaven and earth.'

Katsukō: 'You are an increasingly suspicious fellow. The gods teach that the skin may change at any time, but the Mitama does not change so easily. Furthermore, what is this about your heart having changed? You are a fellow I cannot understand at all.'

Tanimaru, Onimaru: 'That doubt of yours is most reasonable, but the clouds and mist of my misunderstandings and arrogance until now have cleared, and I have been reborn with a soul like the clear sky and the bright sun. No matter how much a Ningen may exert their cleverness or wisdom and fret, it is of no use. I have come to realize deeply that if the Lord God does not grant permission, even at the very last moment, the situation can be overturned. Ah, there is nothing more terrifying than the heart. Until now, I thought the Sangyō, the Ural-kyō, and the Uranai-kyō were my enemies, and I devoted myself entirely to conquering those enemies, continuing my great activities. However, to my surprise, those great demonic enemies were all lurking within our own hearts. Having received such a blessing, there are no enemies under heaven, no others, no demons, and no great serpents. We added extreme violence to the missionary of the Uranai-kyō called Matsuhime, and we beat her to the point where she was almost half-dead; now, I regret this so much that I cannot sit or stand still. The four of us thought that we must find the whereabouts of Matsuhime-sama and apologize, and while searching here and there, we asked a woodcutter we met on the road. We heard that three or four days ago, at dusk, a woman had gone up toward the sacred mountain. Thinking that this must surely be Matsuhime-sama, we were overjoyed and the four of us came together to meet her and offer our apologies. Please, let us pass through here. Furthermore, we must also apologize for the rudeness we showed you earlier. I pray that you will let the past flow away like water and forgive our mistakes.'

Katsukō: 'Well, well, a strange thing has come to pass. I have heard that the autumn and winter skies are easily changed, but this is truly a great upheaval of heaven and earth. Everyone, please wait a moment. Matsuhime-sama is still here, so I shall ask her and then let you meet her.'

He then hid his shadow within the gate.
