Chapter 16: Jewel 照彦 [661]

玉照彦〔661〕

§ 1

Kunadomenokami is sipping hot water in good spirits with Matsuhime, Take, and four other men.

Monbangashira Katsukō enters this place,

Katsukō: 'Excuse me, Kamisama, those fellows from the other day have brought two new faces, and four of them have come in total.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Ah, I see. They have surely repented and are apologizing, are they not? They are likely the humans called Tanimaru, Onimaru, Terujī, and Koronbo. It would be best to guide them here quickly.'

Katsukō: 'I understand. However, they say they wish to apologize to Matsuhime-sama.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Ah, is that so? Then that is even better.'

Before long, led by Katsukō's guidance, the four men appear at this place, cross the threshold looking fearful, prostrate themselves flat on the dirt floor, and apologize with their heads to the ground.

Kunadomenokami: 'Oh, you are Tanimaru and the three men with him, are you not? How is it? Have you submitted to the Goshinriki of the Kamisama?'

Tanimaru finally lifts his head,

Tanimaru: 'Oh, we have been extremely rude and have no excuse. Is that Matsuhime-sama over there? How are you? Are you not in pain? We were merely manipulated by the demons in our hearts and committed nothing but rudeness. Today, the four of us have come together to seek your presence and apologize. Please forgive our many sins.'

The four of them lower their heads at once.

Matsuhime: 'No, no, not at all. It is I who must apologize to you. Thanks to you, I have received wonderful divine virtues.'

Kunadomenokami: 'Everyone, that is the dirt floor. It is cold, so please come up to the tatami room, even though it is a dilapidated house.'

Tanimaru: 'No, no, how could we? It is too fearful. How could such sinners sit in the same room as you?'

Kunadomenokami: 'You have already been saved from your sins. You are precious children of the Kamisama, so there is no need for such reserve. Reserve is actually the root of rudeness toward the Kamisama, so please come up obediently as I say.'

Terujī: 'Come now, everyone, let us accept the kind offer and go up.'

He steps over and goes up first. The three men say,

The three men: 'Pardon our intrusion.'

And fearfully, they climbed up into the tatami room. Take draws hot water and offers it to the four men.

Tanimaru: 'Matsuhime-sama, you are going to welcome Tamateruhiko-sama from here, are you not?'

Matsuhime: 'Yes.'

Tanimaru: 'Please do not hide it. Now that we have repented, we no longer hold such inconvenient thoughts as wanting to welcome Tamateruhiko-sama, right, everyone?'

§ 2

Teruhiko: 'Sayō de gozaimasu. Thanks to the grace of the Kamisama, such Shifuchiyakushin has slipped away from our Nentō. However, to apologize to Matsuhime-sama, we wish to accompany her to the Gankutsu of Takakumayama and serve her in every way we possibly can.'

Kunadomenokami: 'I understand the Magokoro of Minamina well, but if this matter is done with Otetsudahi, it will not become the Tegara of Matsuhime-sama. It would be best for Matsuhime-sama to go Ohitori. Minamina, please wait here for her. In the meantime, let us exchange Iroiro of the Ketsukō stories of the Kamisama.'

Ichidō, having no Yūki to retort, answered that they understood, and decided to wait for the Buji Kito of Matsuhime. Matsuhime, feeling eager and spirited, walked up the Tanimichi covered in Karekusa with a light step. From the Zenpau, a man and a woman appeared, smiling, and bowed politely.

Kamikunimori: 'I am the guardian of this mountain, Kamikunimori; I am Kuniyorihime. Are you not Matsuhime-sama?'

Matsuhime: 'As you say, I am a clumsy person; I humbly ask for your kind favor. Is the Kamisama Tamateruhiko-sama in good spirits? How is Kototeruhime-sama doing?'

Kamikunimori: 'Yes, yes, both of them are in exceptionally good spirits, and since this morning, they have been in particularly high spirits, waiting eagerly for your arrival. Come, we, a married couple, shall guide you. It is a very overgrown and steep mountain path, so I shall take your hand.'

Matsuhime: 'No, no, please do not trouble yourself; it would be disrespectful to the Kamisama. There is no reason why I cannot go where others can travel.'

Kuniyorihime: 'In that case, I shall lead the way.'

The couple then proceeded to climb toward the Gankutsu with Matsuhime in the middle.

Kamikunimori: 'Now, this is the entrance to the Gankutsu. It is before the forty-eight Hōza. Let us pay our respects once, and then I shall guide you to the inner part.'

Matsuhime smiled happily, paid her respects to each of the forty-eight Hōza, and was guided by the couple Kamikunimori deep into the Gankutsu.

Kuniyorihime: 'This Gankutsu has ups and downs in various places, so please be careful. If you proceed seventeen or eighteen chō inside, a magnificent mansion of the Gankutsu is built. This is the mansion of Tamateruhiko-sama.'

国依姫『左様なれば妾が先導を致しませう』

松姫は嬉しさうにニタリと笑ひ、四十八の宝座を一々礼拝し、神国守夫婦に案内されて岩窟の奥深く忍び入る。

国依姫『此岩窟は上り下りが、所々に御座いますから、御用心なさいませ、十七八丁奥へ進みますと立派な岩窟のお館が築かれて御座います、此処が玉照彦様のお館』

§ 3

Matsuhime: 'Arigata.'

With this simple expression of thanks, she turned right and then left through the tunnel inside the cave, and after going up and down, she finally arrived in front of the mansion. A man appeared standing in front of the mansion, waiting for the arrival of Matsuhime.

Matsuhime: 'Oh, are you not Kumako? Why have you come to such a place?'

Kumahiko: 'Yes, because you left the mansion and ran away at dusk the other day, I sought to follow you, wishing to beg you to return to the mansion on Takashiroyama. As I was about to rush out, Osetsu-sama and Tatsuwaka forcibly held me back. To my regret, I left my physical body at the mansion, and only my soul has sought your whereabouts and guided me here. When you were subjected to such a terrible ordeal by the four ruffians at Sakahitauge, I cannot tell you how much I suffered. I clung to your body and was struck in your stead; look, my wounds have not yet fully healed.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, so you left your physical body behind at the mansion and came here. What did you do about the rest?'

Kumahiko: 'Yes, the physical body is being guarded by the primary guardian deity called Chiyohiko.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, is that so? That was a great trouble for you. Please return quickly, for I am already safe.'

Kumahiko: 'Please let me accompany you for a little while longer.'

Shinkokumori: 'Oh, hearing that, you must be someone's ghost.'

Matsuhime: 'This is Kumako, a man who lives in my house and is very devoted to his master; he serves as a gatekeeper and receptionist. His single-hearted sincerity has prevailed, allowing his soul to manifest a ghostly form and protect me all the way here.'

Kuniyorihime: 'What a strong sincerity, he is a person who is truly devoted to his master.'

Matsuhime was already tearing up for some reason. The figure of Kumako vanished like smoke.

Suddenly, the door of the mansion opened, and the dignified figure of Kototeruhime appeared from within.

Kototeruhime: 'Oh, so you are Matsuhime. I am Kototeruhime no Mikoto; for certain reasons, I shall not reveal my true name for the time being. Tamateruhiko-sama, who is sleeping in the inner chamber, is a noble Izunome no Mitama who will participate in the divine work of the Miroku Divine Government in the distant future. You must serve him with great care and deliver him to Kunitakehiko no Mikoto, who resides at the foot of Yotsugi-oyama. If you do so, not only you, but the entire faction of Takahime and Kurohime will be pardoned for the grave sins of having opposed the Great God of Mizu no Mitama until now, and you shall be able to participate in the divine service of the spirit world and achieve great merit. Shinkokumori and Kuniyorihime shall protect Tamateruhiko no Mikoto together with Matsuhime and escort him to the sacred land of Aya.'

国依姫『何と誠の強い、師匠思ひの方ですなア』

松姫は早くも何故か涙ぐんで居る。熊公の姿は煙の如く消えて仕舞つた。

忽然として館の戸は開かれ、中より言照姫の威厳に満ちた姿が現はれた。

言照姫『ヤア其方は松姫であつたか、妾は言照姫の命、様子あつて本名は今暫く名乗りませぬ、奥に寝ませらるる玉照彦様は遠き未来に於てミロク神政成就の神業に参加遊ばす尊き伊都能売之御霊、其方は大切に奉侍し、世継王山の麓に在す国武彦の命にお届けあれ、然らば其方は云ふに及ばず高姫、黒姫一派の、今迄瑞の御霊の大神に射向かひまつりし重大の罪を赦され、神界の御用に参加し、偉勲を建つる事を得む。神国守、国依姫は松姫と共に玉照彦の命を保護し奉り、綾の聖地に送らるべし』

§ 4

As soon as these words were finished, the figure of Kototeruhime vanished suddenly. Matsuhime, in awe and reverence, sang the heavenly Kazūta, and upon finishing, spoke quietly:

Matsuhime: 'I am one called Matsuhime, and having just received the Gomeirei of Kototeruhime-sama, I have come to welcome Mikotosama and proceed to the Seichi of Aya. I pray you, permit me this noble Goyō.'

No sooner had she finished her Itsushin Kiguwan than Tamateruhiko-no-Mikoto stood up, and swaying his small Karada, climbed happily onto the lap of Matsuhime. Matsuhime respectfully held him in her Futokoro, and guarded by the Mikunimori Fūfu, finally departed from the Gankutsu, bowed again before the Hōza, and at last returned to the hermitage of Kunadomenokami.

Kunadomenokami and the others, including the Monban Katsu, Take, Roku, Hatsu, and others, as well as Tanimaru, Onimaru, Terujī, and Koronbo, were lined up on the Naimen of the gate, offering their Hōgei. As Matsuhime, accompanied by the Mikunimori Fūfu, walked out of the gate while bowing quietly, to her surprise, countless divine beings clad in white, numbering in the hundreds, were lined up on both sides of the road; the physical and astral bodies of Eikohime, Etsukohime, Kamehiko, Tsunehiko, Wakahiko, Murasakihime, and other propagandists of the Sango-kyō and Uranai-kyō were intermingled, respectfully offering their Hōgei. From nowhere, subtle music arose in all directions, and Matsuhime, without thinking, stepped forward, and before she knew it, had arrived at the hermitage of Etsukohime at the foot of Mount Yotsugi. Here, the divine beings of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime stood side by side, their divine virtues manifesting day and night; Zuiun drifted across without distinction between day and night, and Takahime, Kurohime, and others of the Uranai-kyō gathered in joy, and thus the foundation of the Miroku Shinsei was solidified.

と言葉終るや否や、言照姫の姿は忽然として消えて仕舞つた。松姫は畏み慎み、天の数歌を謡ひあげ、終つて言葉静かに、

松姫『妾は松姫と申すもの、唯今言照姫様の御命令を拝し、尊様をお迎へ申して綾の聖地に向ひます。何卒妾にこの尊き御用をお許し下さいませ』

と一心に祈願し終るや、玉照彦の命は立ち上り、小さき身体を揺りながら、松姫の膝に嬉しげに上らせられた。松姫は恭しく懐中に抱き奉り、神国守夫婦に守られ、漸く岩窟を立ち出て、再び宝座を伏し拝み、来勿止神の庵に漸く帰りついた。

来勿止神を始め、勝、竹、六、初、其他の門番及び谷丸、鬼丸、テルヂー、コロンボは門の内面に整列して奉迎しつつあつた。松姫は神国守夫婦を伴ひ、静々と目礼しながら門を出づれば豈図らむや、数多の白衣を着せる神人幾百人ともなく、道の左右に整列し、英子姫、悦子姫、亀彦、常彦、若彦、紫姫、其他三五教、ウラナイ教の宣伝使の肉体及び幽体相交はり、恭しく奉迎して居る。何処ともなく微妙の音楽四方に起り松姫は思はず足も進み出で、何時の間にか、世継王山麓の悦子姫の庵に着き居たり。茲に玉照彦、玉照姫の神人は二柱相並び給ひ、日に夜に神徳現はれ、昼夜の区別なく瑞雲棚引き渡り、ウラナイ教の高姫、黒姫其他も嬉々として集まり来たり、ミロク神政の基礎を固むる事となりにける。
