Chapter 19: Mountain と Sea [711]

山と海〔711〕

§ 1

Sadahiko undid his koshiobi, wrapped the tamabako, which was enveloped in many layers, securely at both ends, and packed it so that it could be hitched lightly over his shoulder. Haruhiko was astonished and said,

"Hey Sadahiko, wrapping such precious Goshinpau in what is this—your filthy sanshiyakuobi clinging to your own skin? Do you not realize you are desecrating the majesty of the jewel? And what is that posture? Undressing to your bare skin is disgraceful."

Sadahiko: 'If you will not tear your obi in half vertically and give it to me, there is no help for it. I shall have to pluck some fujizuru and make an obi out of that.'

"Eh, can you make a journey like that? It is disgraceful. Tie your own obi and go. You will desecrate the divine majesty of Shingiyoku."

"Nay Haruhiko, it is not so. As many Brahmins are wandering about this mountain stretch, we must not let them suspect it as a valuable item and target us. The jewel, wrapped in many layers, has little risk of being defiled. It is dangerous if we do not go thus."

"However dangerous you may say it is, isn't that going a bit too far?"

"This is a precious mission, the likes of which happens once in a kalpa. To celebrate the auspicious sign of the second opening of the Iwato, Kotobukibake-sama has ordered this peerless and once-in-a-lifetime duty here at the summit of Futatabisama. Because failing would be terrible, going like this is the safe way."

Haruhiko said,

"I somehow feel as though it were a waste. However, since having the vital treasure seized by the enemy would be a major disaster, I shall do as you say and go. Now, I will give you half of my obi."

With that, he ripped it straight down the middle with a tearing sound and handed it to Sadahiko. Sadahiko said,

"Nay, thank you. With this, my belly-band is tightly fastened. However, Yoshikohime-sama, Hatsukohime-sama, if you two set out in such fine attire, you will be tracked by villains and it will be to no avail. Please devise some scheme."

Yoshikohime: 'Yes, both of us shall wear our clothes inside out and go pretending to be mad.'

Sadahiko: 'Ah, that is a brilliant plan. As expected of Yoshikohime-sama. Now, now, let Sadahiko help you change.'

As he attempted to rise, Yoshikohime and Hatsukohime shook their heads from side to side,

Yoshikohime: 'No, absolutely not. I am also Yoshikohime; I must manage my own affairs.'

So saying, she untied her obi with a flurry and finished changing her clothes inside out.

When Hatsukohime also attempted to take off her clothes, Yoshikohime tilted her head slightly and said,

"Wait a moment. If there are two mad people, we might arouse suspicion instead, so please become the mad daughter."

Hatsukohime: 'If so, then you are my mad mother. Now, let us go anywhere.'

Yoshikohime: 'Hey Sadahiko-kō, Haruhiko-kō, what kind of fellows are you? You are quite the comical Hyottoko rogues.'

Sadahiko: 'This is unexpected, Yoshikohime-sama. How can you utter such unsightly and harsh words, my lady?'

"I don't know you, I don't know you! Ah, to think I would end up traveling companions with such foolish Hyottoko rogues—it is regrettable. I feel like I'm going mad."

"Yoshikohime-sama, if you start acting mad now, Haruhiko will not be able to bear it."

"Ise is sustained by Tsu, and Tsu is sustained by Ise. A third-rate actor holds the jewel—koriya, koriya, koriya."

Sadahiko: 'Yoshikohime-sama, please keep your jokes within reason. We still have a long journey ahead, and if you keep up this mad act from now on, we won't last.'

"What, you call me mad? Ah, how regrettable. I have heard that there is such a person in Brahmanism: Onikumawake's wife, Mukadehime—that is none other than myself. You are the coward missionary of Hinomoto-kyō; eat this Mukadehime's backside! Is it regrettable? Is it mortifying? What a helpless expression you have on. Ohō-hō-hō-hō."

Brushing away the hand clinging to her,

Tamanohime: 'What, even you think I have gone mad? Ah, how filthy. I cannot stay in such a place for even a moment.'

Tossing the sash wrapping the two jewels over her shoulder, she ran off along the mountain paths like clouds and mist.

Hatsuwakahime, not to be outdone, followed right behind Tamanohime and ran like an arrow.

Fearing they would escape, Sadahiko and Haruhiko pursued them with all their might.

Before they knew it, the figures of Tamanohime and Hatsuwakahime were no longer visible.

Sadahiko: 'Oi, Haruhiko, hasn't something terrible happened?'

Haruhiko: 'Because you did not hold on firmly, a badger has possessed her at last and carried her away. Ah, there is no help for it now, I have no excuse to offer to the gods. Shall I throw myself down from this precipice and die to make my apologies?'

Sadahiko: 'Even if you say so, if you do a thing like that, it will only add to your sins in the divine realm.'

Thus they worried and lamented with troubled looks.

From a clump of trees over yonder,

Tamanohime: 'Oyi, Haruhiko-sama, Sadahiko-sama, over here, over here!'

She was calling out.

Haruhiko: 'Ya, their whereabouts are known. You mad wretch, sticking only your face out beneath that small pine by the bald mountain—if we go from the front, it will be terrible if she escapes again. Shall we take a roundabout way and catch her stealthily?'

Leaving the mountain path, the two of them forced their way through the thicket of trees, getting caught in spider webs, until they finally drew close to Tamanohime.

Tamanohime: 'Those two honored persons, you have come. While entrusted with your duties by chance, having your jewels snatched by Tamanohime must be unbearable, isn't it? Come, come, Hatsuwakahime-sama, let us go without paying attention to such a hiyottoko fellow. Ho-ho-ho-ho!'

With a mocking laugh, as if vanishing into thin air, she once again bolted at full speed through the thicket and hid her figure somewhere.

The two of them chased after her with all their might.

Through Hatsuwakahime's thoughtfulness, small branches were broken off here and there to leave a trail.

Sadahiko: 'Ya, as expected of Hatsuwakahime-sama. Such good wisdom for a child. Leaving these as a signal for us to come, she has broken and marked the small branches here and there. Oi, shall we search for these and run, hey, Haruhiko?'

Haruhiko: 'Oh, exactly.'

Binding their heads with twisted towels, the two pursued the trail guided by the broken branches.

In the pine grove at the foot of Mount Takatori, seven or eight men sat in a circle on folding stools, engrossed in whispering.

Man A: 'Oi, aren't there sometimes terribly strong women? Those fellows effortlessly hurled our senior bosses' pinball and cannonball into the waterfall basin, and what is more, they tossed us down to the bottom of the valley, causing us to suffer like this, and in the very end, even the teachings master of Mukadehime met such a fate. That wretch might well be the reincarnation of some mighty god.'

Man B: 'Nonsense, that wretch is someone called Tamanohime who became a servant to Takatorihime of Mount Takatori and used to draw water from the pure spring. At that time we let our guard down thinking they were just women and children, which is why we suffered such a blunder. No doubt she will be prowling around this neighborhood sooner or later. By all means, we must capture that wretch, force her to confess the whereabouts of the treasure of the Three-Five Religion, and occupy it for the Brahman religion; otherwise, our sect will never expand in this Earth-Reversing Island. Somehow, let us track down her whereabouts once and achieve our goal.'

三人の童女は又もや手を拡げて、玉能姫の前に進み来る。玉能姫は紫の宝珠の函を取り上げ、恭しく頭上に捧げ、次で三人の童女の手に渡した。童女はものをも言はず微笑を浮べたまま、玉函と共に同じ岩穴に消えて了つた。玉能姫は怪しんで穴を覗き見れば、童男、童女の姿は影もなく、只二つの玉函、微妙の音声を発し、鮮光孔内を照らして居る。

二人は恭しく天津祝詞を奏上し、次で神言を唱へ、天の数歌を歌ひ、岩蓋をなし、其上に今童女が捨て置きし、黄金の鍬を各自に取り上げ、土を厚く衣せ、四辺の小松を其上に植ゑて、又もや祝詞を奏上し、悠々として山を下り行く。

玉能姫は、

『お二人さま、えらう御待たせしました。さア、もう御用が済みました。帰りませう』

佐田彦、波留彦両人は口を揃へて、

『それは結構で御座いました。御目出度う。これから私等が一度登つて来ますから、暫らく此処に待つて居て下さいませ』

初稚姫『モー御用が済みましたのですから、一歩も上つてはなりませぬ。さア帰りませうよ』

佐田彦『折角此処迄苦労して御供をして来たのだから、埋めた跡なりと拝まして下さいな』

初稚姫は首を左右に振つてゐる。玉能姫を見れば、是亦無言の儘首を左右に振つてゐる。何処ともなく雷の如き声、

『一刻も猶予はならぬ。これより高砂へは寄らず、淡路島を目標に再度山の麓に船をつけよ。サア、早く早く』

と呶鳴るものがある。此言葉に佐田彦、波留彦は、

『ハイ、畏まりました』

と玉能姫、初稚姫を迎へ入れ一生懸命に艪櫂を操りつつ、再度山の方面指して帰り行く。
