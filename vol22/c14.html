Chapter 14: 初稚姫 [706]

初稚姫〔706〕

§ 1

Mokusuke: 'Kami appears openly and distinguishes good from evil; the Kamunahohi who created this world, and the vast-hearted Ōnahohi. Truly, whatever happens in the world of man, look straight and see straight, listen straight and hear straight; correct the faults of your body and proclaim them anew. As sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, stepping off the path of sincerity, Takahime, whose heart is arrogant, is trapped by her petty stubbornness and protects the brocade palace. Tamateruhiko and Tamateru Hime's mikoto, and the mikoto of Kotoyoriwake, treating the divine will as lightly as the wind that blows through the sky, the retribution for the body that let it flow by unheeded. On the summit of Mount Takatori, the golden incarnation of Kami who appeared, with a warning'

Mokusuke: 'Pebble plunged down into the ravine, a shiyaba world suffering and agonizing. With just the attitude of one's heart, this country created by Kami opens up freely as Tengoku, Jiyōdo, or Jigokudau. The shiyura and beasts created by one's own body, tormented by the wicked spirits within the heart, Takahime, the clear spring instantly becomes muddy water, surging back inside her chest. A most tragic spring day of today: flowers bloom and are fragrant, the wind is balmy, little birds sing and butterflies dance; the spring of Tokoyo wrapped in flowers is right before her eyes. The great highway of Kami, deeply enveloped in thick white smoke, eyes and nose and even mouth cannot function—how miserable. Following that, Wakahiko'

Mokusuke: 'The war cry of fiery zeal, missing that shout, falling from the distant plain of Takamanohara, struck in body by the pebbles of Kami, instantly collapsing and falling flat upon the earth, in the fleeting moment when breath is all but gone, the plum blossoms of the opening heart. The paradise of Tengoku and Jiyōdo, first realized within the chest. The sins of the body committed until now, and the defilement of the heart, instantly blown away by the wind of enlightenment, for the first time here, becoming the true pillars of hemp, the true master officials. Ah, Takahime, Wakahiko! Shiyaba is none other than Jakkō-jiyōdo! Kami, Buddha, wicked spirits, great serpents, ugly females, wolves, the mountain of needles that scorches the heart, the wheel of fire that afflicts the body'

Mokusuke: 'Instantly vanishing water spirit, by the virtue of the thousand-seat oketo of Kami Susanoo-no-Ōkami, the divine words that make us sweep away the clouds and fog of the sky of the heart, rejoicing in them morning and evening, do not forget the precious grace. Kami is with thee. Yet, how could the right Kami ever dwell in the master of a warped spirit? Ah, Kamnagara-kamugami! Receiving the divine favor, pushing open the rock door of the heart, like a boat proceeding with its true sails hoisted in the breeze of the Akashi shore, vanishing amidst the waves, wishing even a single day sooner to sweep away sins and transgressions deeper than the eight-thousand-fathom sea, the four great deities of the Haraedo'

Mokusuke: 'Sweep them away, as children of Kami, born into this world, Takahime, Wakahiko, followed by Tamanohime, Kinsuke, Ginko, and all other religious children who serve Brahmanism, starting with table tennis, and Canaan ball, iron, bears, and many other disciples! Quickly straighten up your spirits. Kami appears openly and distinguishes good from evil; whether the morning sun shines or is clouded, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even though the great earth should sink, the single path of sincerity of Kami shall remain unchanged for thousands of ages. Facing this unchanging single path of sincerity, becoming the light of the age of pine, becoming flowers, becoming salt, purifying the impurities of the world, giving it flavor, praised as a pillar of Kami, to the extent that there is no cause for shame, polish yourselves and attain enlightenment, O children of Kami!'

Mokusuke: 'Mokusuke, who serves Kami, maintaining his red heart through and through, with the mikoto of Hatsuwakihime, has brought the divine spirit of Tamanohime here to this place. You, Takahime and Wakahiko, wake up to the voice of Kami! The gathering clouds hanging over your heart clear away instantly, and the light of the sun and moon shining upon you is right before your eyes. Ah, Kamnagara-kamugami! May your august spirits flourish and be at peace!'

So chanting, Tokiokishi-no-kami Mokusuke carried Hatsuwakihime upon his back, and together with Tamanohime, appeared pointing toward this place.

At the sound of this sendenka (prophetic song), the four persons—Takahorihime [Takatorihime], Wakahiko, Kin, and Gin—recovered their bodily freedom as before and stood up, choked with tears of joy before Mokusuke, joining their hands, expressing their gratitude, and bowing their heads respectfully.

Mokusuke: 'Everyone, you have received a tremendous blessing, have you not?'

Takahorihime: 'Yes, thank you very much. Owing to our profound misunderstanding, we have no words to pucker our opened mouths until now.'

Wakahiko: 'We were left utterly dumbfounded, just as the divine oracle foretold.'

と歌ひつつ時置師神の杢助は、初稚姫を背に負ひ、玉能姫と諸共に此場を指して現はれた。

此宣伝歌の声に鷹鳥姫、若彦、金、銀の四人は身体元の如く自由となりて立ち上り、杢助の前に嬉し涙に咽びながら両手を合せ、感謝の意を表し、恭しく首を垂れて居る。

若彦『御神諭の通りアフンと致しました』

§ 2

Mokusuke: 'So many guard officials holding takeyari are protecting us. When did these people come out?'

Takatorihime: 'High, they are solely the deeply benevolent persons who have come to drive out the demons lurking in our hearts.'

Wakahiko: 'These people seem to be subordinates of Mukadehime-sama of Brahmanism. They have taken care of us warmly. Please kindly say words of gratitude to them from yourself.'

The members of Brahmanism, whose bodies had become stiff like sticks and rigid, could still move their heads freely from the neck up, shook their heads at each other, looked at each other, lowered their voices, and whispered,

Smart: 'Hey, Kanan, aren't you saying something unpleasant? Pointing at us who were subjected to a barrage of abuse and dangerous experiences, you tell us to say thanks. This expression of gratitude is quite tough. If we don't stay firm, the curtain of a tragic death from an airplane crash falling from mid-air might be cut and dropped. It is a troublesome thing, ah.'

Kanan: 'No matter what you say, since we've been hit by this immovable kanashibari just like this, we have no other choice than to apologize. Even if you say you are going to resist, with such wooden statues, what on earth can be done?'

Hatsuwakahime, who was taken down from Mokusuke's back, stood before everyone and suddenly fell into a state of kamigakari. Everyone turned their gazes toward Hatsuwakahime without prior arrangement. Hatsuwakahime said calmly,

Seendenka Takatorihime, Wakahiko, and all other people, even though it is said to be a world where Yamata no Orochi rampages and a saha world of darkness and lawlessness, once you have opened the iwato of your hearts, this present world of darkness and mumyō is no longer a saha world, but a heavenly pure land. It is the paradise of supreme joy and supreme music of saha-as-the-jakkō-jōdo. If you wish to bring Yamata no Orochi to submission through kototama and participate in the divine work of the Miroku divine government, first make the saha world of your heart become a heavenly pure land. This world becomes either heaven or hell depending on your heart. The wind is pure and the mountains are blue, the river flows eternally, the tips of the trees blow out green buds, flowers smile and small birds sing, butterflies dance, the music of nature is constantly heard, all creation dances comfortably, making our eyes rejoice and our ears glad, and fragrant scents nourish our noses. Tree fruits ripen and grains mature, fish leap, vegetables contain smiles and wait for us to eat them. Great roads open grandly, iron bridges, stone bridges, and wooden bridges are spanned, travelers going on the road have inns when evening comes and are courteously accommodated, given hot water and given food and provided with warm bedding, and wherever one goes there is nothing that is not the condition of heaven. If you wish to go far, there are steam trains, electric trains, and the convenience of postal telegraph. While entrusting your life to such a flawless divine country, why is it that you perceive this very thing as a saha world and view it as a world of darkness and mumyō? It is because your heart is dark, because your mihana is soiled. Does not the propaganda song say, 'The god who created this world is Ōnaobi, his heart is also vast Dai-naobi'? Ah, if you approach all sentient beings with the heart of Naobi—which is like the benevolence of such a Naobi god where the height of heaven allows a hundred birds to fly, and the depth and breadth of the sea allows fish and turtles to dance—then none of them is anything other than the light of gods, and none is not grace. There are no ogres and no enemies, no darkness and no defilement. Reflect in your true hearts as early as possible, and toward everything look calmly anew and listen anew, and promptly re-proclaim your previous misunderstandings. This is the great divine grace given by the Kannagara Miroku to all things. Ah, Kannagara tamachihau masase.

Having finished saying this, Hatsuwakahime returned to her original state, and once again entrusted her lovely young figure to Mokusuke's back.

Takatorihime and Wakahiko could not utter a single word, clung to the ground, and trembled with tears of gratitude that knew no end. What had hitherto seemed to be Tamanohime was a spiritual body, and she vanished as if erasing herself. The figures of Mokusuke and his son also, how it happened, could not be caught by the eye, Smart Ball and the following people also vanished before one knew it, and the heavenly sun, where white clouds drifted, brilliantly looked down upon this scene. Ah, Kannagara tamachihau masase.

と云ひ終つて初稚姫は元に復し、再び杢助の背に愛らしき幼き姿を托した。

鷹鳥姫、若彦は一言も発し得ず地に噛りつき、感謝の涙止め度なく身を慄はして居た。今迄玉能姫と見えしは幽体にて、かき消す如く消え失せた。杢助父子の姿も、如何なりしか目にも止まらず、スマートボール以下の人々も何時しか消えて、白雲の漂ふ天津日は煌々として此光景を見下したまひつつあつた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
