§ 1

Takatorihime, Wakahiko, and Tamanohime, the three who had built a hut at the roots of the rocks on the side of Takatoriyama and shut themselves away with the intention of erecting the Mihashira of the Oshihe of Ananai-kyō, as if harboring something in their hearts, descended to the Tanigaha and were performing the Misogi, when at that very moment, a man who had skimmed through the Chiukū and descended like a dove plunged with a splash into the Takitsubo.

Startled by the sudden Dekigoto, the three opened their eyes wide and Jiyukushi-ed the Takitsubo, where the figure of a man came floating up, swayed by the ripples of the Suimen.

Wakahiko said, 'This is Taihen!'

And leaping up, he jumped into the Takitsubo, caught him under his arm, and with much ado rescued him.

This was the figure of a man in the prime of his Genki, twenty-four or five years of age.

Bringing his mouth close to his ear in Iroiro ways,

And performing the Kamuwaza of Tamayo by crying, 'Oi, oi,' and chanting the Kazūta of Ten with all his Chikarakagi, he at last breathed back to life, and while looking around the Atari with a kyorokyoro gaze, he looked at the three and said,

'Where is this place? When did I come to such a place? People I do not know everywhere... What is your name, honored one?'

Wakahiko: 'This is the Chiufuku of Takatoriyama, the shooting range (kyōjō) of the Oshihe of Ananai-kyō, the dwelling of Takatorihime.'

'Please forgive me. I beg you to spare my life alone.'

'Who would ever take the life of you, whose life has just been saved? Calm yourself a little. What is your name?'

'Yes, as for my name, I am sure... I remember it was Gin.'

'Ha, ha, ha, ha! To remember your own name as having been Gin is rather funny, isn't it? From the look of you just now, you seem to have fallen from Ten, but from what country on earth have you come?'

'Yes, please wait a moment. Even if you ask me so abruptly, it seems my soul has moved to some other lodging, and I cannot give a clear answer.'

'Ah, that must be so, it cannot be helped. You fell from the great sky. Well now, I will slowly have you change your clothes, so rest here, calm your spirit, and after that it would be best to talk. Takatorihime-sama, something rather strange has happened, hasn't it?'

Takatorihime: 'As it is said that there are many children of the Hinodewake-kamisama everywhere, perhaps they have taken pity on our sincerity and come down from Ten to reinforce us. At any rate, we must treat him with care. Now, now, Tamanohime-sama, you must have him change his garments.'

Tamanohime replied, 'Ai,' took out Wakahiko's change of clothes, made the man change into them, led him by the hand into a room, laid him down quietly, and set about wringing out the man's wet garments and hanging them on tree branches to dry.

Wakahiko: 'Hey, Tamanohime, is there any mark on the lining of those clothes? Look closely and find out for me.'

Having said this, he once again went with Takatorihime to the side of the former waterfall basin, chanted the Amatsunorito, and earnestly began performing Mizugori.

While drying the garments and carefully searching to see if there were any marks, Tamanohime's eyes caught sight of a mark reading 'Gin.'

Tamanohime: 'Ha... when that gentleman said a while ago that he thought it was Gin, it seems it was indeed not an illusion. Even so, for someone to descend from Tenjiyō into that abyss must be for some reason. Once his mind has calmed down, I shall think about it and ask him in detail.'

So she murmured to herself as she walked up to the bedside where the man was lying horizontally and looked, seeing that the former man had sat up on the Shiyōjiyō, and was looking around his surroundings in wonder.

Tamanohime: 'Mosimosi, Anata, how is your health?'

Silver Duke: 'Yes, I feel a great deal better. But what sort of place is this?'

Tamanohime: 'This is the archery ground of the Izumo-shinshū on Mt. Takatori. You fell headlong from the sky into the waterfall basin; exactly where did you come from?'

Hearing that it was the archery ground of the Izumo-shinshū, the Silver Duke was struck with astonishment in his heart,

Silver Duke: 'Yaa, this is terrible. Unawares, was I knocked flying by the golden statue, fell right into the midst of the enemy, and was saved by the enemy? If I say something like this is careless Brahmanism, it will be terrible. Is there not some good idea?'

and folding his arms, he was plunged in reflection.

Tamanohime: 'Please do tell me. Just now, when I was wringing and drying your garments, a mark saying Gin was attached to them.'

Hearing this, the Silver Duke was struck with even greater astonishment in his heart, but putting on an indifferent manner,

Silver Duke: 'I am called the Homeless Nyorai, one who dwells in heaven, dwells on earth, and at times dwells even beneath the earth. The one with the mark of the character for Gin was given to me by Tanabatamahime-sama when I crossed the Milky Way because my clothes had become too old. Is this place still the earth? Viewed from the land of Tenjiyō, it is an indescribably filthy place.'

Tamanohime: 'Is the land of Tenjiyō that beautiful?'

Silver Duke: 'Hee-hee, it is, oh, it is incomparable.'

Here Kinsuke came to the hermitage of Takatorihime, expressed his intent of repentance together with Ginkō, and became the right-hand man of Wakahiko, participating in the divine work. Ah, whose divine incarnation could this golden standing statue that had enveloped Kinsuke have been?

銀公『又空中滑走をやらねばなりませぬから、近寄つてはいけませぬ。然し貴方等はお出でなさいませ。そして肩や背中を撫でておやりなさいませ。金像がプリツと肩を動かしたが最後、中天の空まで……飛行機ぢやないが……上りつめ、又滝壺へ真逆様に陥ち込むと云ふ芸当が演ぜられます。私はもう懲りこりしました。金像の金の字を聞いても胆が潰れます』

若彦『お前は金銀を得むが為めに今迄利己主義の行動を続けて来た男だから、閻魔さまでも忽ち地蔵顔になると云ふ金の顔を見るのは余り悪くはあるまい。サアサア行かう、お前のやうな者を留守させて置けば、如何な事するか分つたものぢやない。留守の間に赤鼬でも這はされたら大変ですよ』

『滅相な、生命を助けて貰つた恩人の館に、赤鼬を這はして済みますか。いたちて神妙にお留守をいたちます。何卒早く貴方等、探険にお出でなさいませ』

鷹鳥姫『如何しても銀公さまが動かぬと云ふのだから、若彦さま、お前さまは此処に銀公さまの監督がてら留守して居て下さい。玉能姫さまと二人、探険に行つて参ります』

と欣々として山頂の光を目標に登り行く。鷹鳥姫、玉能姫の二人は鷹鳥山の光目標に登り見れば、銀公の云うた金像は背の高さ五丈六尺七寸もあらうかと思はるる許りに伸長して突立ち、二人の姿を見るより、鷹鳥姫を左手に引掴み、

『オイ、まだ俺の所へ来るのは早い、もとへ帰れ』

と猫の首筋を掴んだ様に鷹鳥山の中腹目蒐けてポイと放つた。右の手に玉能姫を同じく提げノソリノソリと五歩六歩東に向つて歩み出し、

『お前は彼辺へ行け』

と又ポイと投げた。忽ち金像は煙となつて、巨大な爆音と共に消えて了つた。後に金助の肉体は、

『アヽア、偉い神さまになつたと思へば、矢張り元の金助か。こりや、マア、如何した訳だらう。何は兎もあれ、もう斯うなる以上は三五教の信者だから、鷹鳥姫さまの庵を訪ねて帰順の意を表し、使つて貰はうか』

と独語しつつ山頂を降り行く。

茲に金助は鷹鳥姫の庵に来り、銀公と共に改心の意を表し、若彦の股肱となつて神業に参加する事となつた。あゝ此金助を包み居たる黄金の立像は何神の化身であらうか。
