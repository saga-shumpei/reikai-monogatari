§ 1

Tokiokashinokami, Hatsuwakahime, and Tamanohime suddenly concealed their forms in this place. The figures of several tens of people, including the Smart Ball players of Brahmanism who had pressed forward before one knew it, also vanished like smoke.

Takatorihime and Wakahiko looked at each other's faces, and driven by a sense of wonder,

Takatorihime: 'Now, Wakahiko-sama, is this not strangely marvelous? When I asked you to keep watch and climbed to the summit of this mountain together with Tamanohime-sama, the golden standing statue shone dazzlingly on all fours, solemn beyond compare and seemingly unapproachable; yet when I thought I had plucked up courage and drawn near to Her side, She stretched out Her left and right hands and hurled both of us two into mid-air, and afterwards it was like a dream. When I awoke and looked, I had fallen before the garden of our hermitage. Then, when the voice of the kami was heard from heaven and various teachings were bestowed, indeed I was awestruck, and my sins up to now appeared before me like a mountain, bringing an indescribable anguish of heart. Realizing completely my misunderstandings until now and thinking that the kami had forgiven my sins, at the critical moment when the Brahman people rushed to attack with bamboo spears to pierce and destroy us two, Mokusuke-sama appeared in this place carrying Hatsuwakahime-sama on his back and accompanied by Tamanohime-sama while singing a propaganda song. When I thought he had lowered Hatsuwakahime-sama down from his back, Hatsuwakahime-sama entered a state of possession by the kami, minutely explaining the karmic connection of this mundane world as the serene light of the Pure Land. When I bowed down prostrate, thinking how truly grateful it was, the three forms vanished like smoke. Truly, when strange things happen, there is no end to them!'

Wakahiko: 'It is just as you say for me as well. That the voice of the Goddess was heard from heaven, that various precious teachings were bestowed, and that Mokusuke-sama's party actually came out here can surely be neither a dream nor a mere vision. Moreover, it is also a fact that the people under Mukaderhime's command came rushing to attack. Since the two of us are practically saved by Mokusuke-sama and his father, we cannot just remain silent. From now on, we must visit Mokusuke-sama's lodging and go to offer our thanks.'

"Is that so? You have had a hard time, Anata, but I shall stay keeping watch here while serving the kami. Please go to offer your thanks once. Kina-san, Gina-san, you too were saved together. Go along with Wakahiko-sama to Mokusuke-sama's dwelling and go pay your thanks."

Kina and Gina all at once,

"Hi, thank you, we shall accompany you."

So saying, they choked with tears of gratitude. Here, Wakahiko rinsed his mouth and washed his hands, and together with Takahime, Kina, and Gina, facing the sacred presence, reverently intoned the Amatsu Norito, proclaimed the divine words, and leaving the hermitage behind, descended tracing the rugged mountain paths.

Just as they approached a somewhat flat grove of large trees at the foot of the mountain, a dozen or so men who had been taking a midday nap stood up straight the moment they saw the three figures, spread their arms wide on the path ahead,

"Ho, you must be Wakahiko of Hinodekyō. Using the evil deity called Tamanohime, you are the ringleader of the wicked kami who cast us into the pure spring, and you have made our hands, feet, and faces covered in wounds. Come, from now on we shall exact our revenge, prepare yourselves!"

Saying thus, they brandished clubs from all directions and rushed to attack.

Although Wakahiko upheld the principle of absolute non-resistance, when the enemy's momentum became excessively fierce and perilous, aiming at a pine tree with fine branch-spreading on all sides, he scampered up like a monkey. The two, Kinsuke and Gingō, took a small pine stump as a shield,

Kinsuke: 'Oi, Smart Ball, Kanann Ball older brother, your anger is most justified, but this is not something for these propagandists to know. You have fallen into the pit of the heart that you yourselves created. The enemy is lurking within your hearts. Now, calm your spirits. Even though you were just saved from peril by Mokusuke's daughter Hatsuwakahime and fled away like clouds and mist, do you forget that benevolence and still harbor hostility toward Hinodekyō? Think about it calmly, you fellows.'

『ヤーお前さまは若彦の宣伝使さま、鷹鳥山の庵に於て身魂を研き、旁御教を鷹鳥姫と共に四方に宣伝して御座ると聞いて居たが、今日は又如何なる風の吹き廻しか、此の杢助が隠家を訪ねて御越し遊ばしたのは、如何なる御用でございますか』

『最前は鷹鳥姫様始め吾々一同、いかい御世話になりました。御礼を申さむかと思ふ間もなく、貴方は初稚姫さま、玉能姫と共に御帰り遊ばしたので、鷹鳥姫さまも一つ、言葉の御礼に行つて来ねば済まないから「若彦、お前御礼に行つて来い」との仰せ、遅れながら只今参りました』

『此の日の暮紛れに三人連れで、此処へやつて来るとは合点が行かぬ。此の杢助は二三日前から閾一つ跨げた事はござらぬ。随つて貴方を最前とやら御助け申した覚えはござらねば、何うぞ此儘御帰り下さいませ』

と膠も杓子もなく、榎で鼻を擦つたやうな挨拶振り、稍面を膨らし、目を凝視めて不機嫌顔、若彦は合点行かず、暫し答ふる言葉も知らなかつたが、思ひ切つたやうに、

『玉能姫は貴方の宅に御世話になつて居りませぬか』

『それを訊ねて何となさる。三五教の宣伝使たるものは、一切を神様に任せ、総ての執着を去り、師匠を杖につかず、人を相手とせず、親子女房血類を力にすなとの教ではござらぬか。何を血迷うて鷹鳥山の霊場に玉能姫を伴れ込み、穢らはしくも此の杢助の宅に玉能姫は居ないかなぞと以ての外の御心得違ひ、左様な腐つた魂の宣伝使には今日限り絶縁致します。此の閾、一歩でも跨げるなら、サア、跨げて見なさい』

§ 4

The clearing of Tamanohime's throat in the inner room gave a special stimulus to Wakahiko's ears. Saved by Mokusuke, Tamanohime lay with her ailing body here while anxiously listening to the exchange with Wakahiko. Eager to meet and see him as if ready to fly off, her heart burned like bamboo grass, yet she was blocked by the barrier of public eyes and Mokusuke's firm words from which there was no turning back; sobbing without any reply, she bit into her bedding and wiped away falling tears with her sleeves.

Though Mokusuke was not such a creature of wood and stone as to be unable to fathom the hearts of the two, hiding his tears driven by the waves of compassionate love for the couple, he raised his voice on purpose:

Mokusuke: 'Hey, because it is twilight, your face cannot be clearly discerned, but your voice closely resembles Wakahiko's. You are probably none other than Wakahiko himself. Nevertheless, in Ananai-kyō, numerous selfless sendenshi, brilliant like a galaxy of stars, make the jewel of their hearts shine, forge ahead along the path of Kami's oshihe, and as those who guide yobito, none are such cowards as to have their hearts stolen by niyōbau, abandon the hall of oshihe, and come all this way to visit. You are either making the sacred name of Kami—or rather, the pseudonyms of sendenshi—into a mockery, or you belong to the class of thieves who deceive this world. Because such impostors as you wander about everywhere, you first bring disgrace upon Ananai-kyō, cause the organization of shishishūdan—or rather, loyal patriots—to be misunderstood by the world as a coward's sect, and blaspheme the holiness of Kami; you shall henceforth return to your own dwelling, polish your shintama with single-minded devotion, become a kami-jin whose name and reality match, and then become a sendenshi if a sendenshi is what you desire. If you hate that, it would be best for you to become a wandering vagrant just as you are, knock at people's doors, and expose the shame of a beggar. Pondering a thousand and myriad times the heart of Mokusuke who says this, quickly depart from this place along with the two honorable thieves of honor. Moreover, as for the sendenshi called Tamanohime, for the sake of kamikai, she shall be separated from her husband for a while, become the shield of Susanoo-no-Ōkami, and until she achieves brilliant exploits, she shall not meet her husband.'

Raising his voice for the husband and wife to hear, these words of compassion from Mokusuke felt to Wakahiko as if a wedge were being driven into his chest; joining his hands, he bowed low to Mokusuke's hermitage, and looking back again and again with lingering reluctance, together with the two men, he became completely enveloped by the curtain of darkness.

Later, with a moistened voice, Mokusuke spoke to himself:

Mokusuke: 'Though I say it is for the sake of Ōkami and yobito, this action of Mokusuke's is like splitting living wood; Wakahiko, please do not resent me. Even so, Tamanohime, it would have seemed good if you had let us meet even for a single glance, yet thinking me a hard-hearted Mokusuke, she must surely be resenting me. Earlier, upon receiving the notification of Hatsuwachihime-sama, just as I was heading toward Takayamahiko for rescue, Tamanohime said she would accompany me. At that time, scolding her mercilessly, I carried Hatsuwachihime-sama on my back, and while leaving my heart behind, singing a sendenka, when I went to Takahimahime's hall, just as Kami's prophecy foretold without a single fraction of difference, a tragic curtain had fallen. Tamanohime's yūtai seemed to have come, now visible, now hidden. Ah, it cannot be helped. However, though it is easy to let them meet now, there is also the secret command of Yosowakewake-no-Mikoto, and furthermore, the stern instructions of Ōkami of supreme benevolence and love; however much I may perceive Tamanohime's innermost feelings, I cannot disobey the words of Kami-sama, and oh, the anguish in the chest of this Mokusuke, caught in the binding trees of Kami's oshihe and human emotion. Ah, you two, this present painful parting is the departure to reach the capital of victory; please ponder even a little within your hearts the feelings of Mokusuke.'

Even the stalwart Mokusuke, entangled in emotion, was choked with quiet tears. In the inner room, the sound of the single-stringed koto played by Hatsuwachihime and Tamanohime gently touched the eardrums.

原文（近代文語）

奥には玉能姫の咳払ひ、若彦の耳には殊更刺激を与へた。玉能姫は杢助に救はれ、此処に病気の身を横へながら、若彦との問答を心痛めて聞いて居る。飛び立つばかり会ひたさ見たさに、玉能姫は心は矢竹に焦れども、人目の関や、抜きさしならぬ杢助の堅き言葉に遮られ、何と返答もないじやくり、夜具に食ひつきハラハラと涙を袂に拭ひつつあつた。

杢助は二人の心を察し得ない程の木石漢にはあらねども、二人を思ふ慈悲心の波にせかれて涙を隠し、態と呶鳴声、

『ヤイ、黄昏のこととて顔は慥かに分らねど、其の声は若彦によく似たり。恐らくは若彦に間違ひなからうかも知れぬ。併し乍ら三五教には不惜身命的宣伝使の数多綺羅星の如く、心の玉を輝かし神の教の道を猛進し、世人を導く身分として女房に心を奪はれ、教の館を捨てて遥々訪ね来る如き腰抜けは一人も御座らぬ。汝は神の名否宣伝使の雅号をサツクとなし、此世を誑かる泥坊の類ならむ。汝の如き偽物、諸方を徘徊致すに依つて、第一三五教の面汚し、獅子身中否志士集団の団体をして腰抜教と天下に誤解せしめ、神の神聖を冒涜するもの、汝は是より己が住家へ帰り、一意専念身魂を研き、名実相合する神人となつて、然る後宣伝使が希望ならば宣伝使となれ。それが嫌なら只今の儘流浪人となつて人の門戸を叩き、乞食の恥を曝すがよからう。斯く申す杢助の心は千万無量、推量致して名誉泥坊の二人と共に疾く此場を立去れ。又玉能姫とやらの宣伝使は、神界のため夫に暫く離れて素盞嗚大神の御楯となり、華々しき功名を致す迄、夫に面会は致すまいぞ』

と声張り上げて夫婦に聞かす杢助が情の言葉、若彦は胸に鎹打たるる心地、両手を合せ杢助の庵を伏し拝み、名残惜しげに振返り振返り、二人の男と共に、闇の帳に包まれてしまつた。

後に杢助は声を湿らせながら独言、

『大神のため、世人のためとは云ひながら、生木を裂くやうな杢助が仕打ち、若彦必ず恨んで呉れな。それに就ても玉能姫、せめて一目なりと会はして呉れたら良ささうなものだのに、気強い杢助であると嘸恨んで居るであらう。最前初稚姫様の御知らせに依つて鷹鳥山へ救援に向ふ折りしも玉能姫は御伴をしようと云つた。其時無下に叱りつけ初稚姫様を背に負ひ、後に心を残しつつ宣伝歌を歌ひながら鷹鳥姫が館に行つて見れば、神の御告に寸分違はず、悲惨の幕が下りて居た。玉能姫の幽体は又見えつ隠れつ来て居つたやうだ。嗚呼無理もない。併し乍ら今会はせるは易けれど、言依別命様の御内命もあり、且又至仁至愛の大神様の厳しき御示し、何程玉能姫の心情を察すればとて、神さまの仰には背かれず、神の教と人情の締木にかかつた此の杢助の胸の苦しさよ。アヽ両人、今の辛き別れは勝利の都に達する首途、杢助が心の中も些は推量して下され』

と流石剛毅の杢助も情に絡まれ、潜々と落涙に咽んでゐる。奥には初稚姫、玉能姫が奏づる一絃琴の音、しとやかに鼓膜をそそる。
