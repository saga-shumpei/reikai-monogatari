Chapter 6: 見 Dance 客 [698]

見舞客〔698〕

§ 1

Takahime returned to her home in low spirits, complaining of a headache, and took to her bed with a Nejihachimaki, suffering from Daihatsunetsu and Daikumon.

Enshiu and Bushiu were doing their utmost to provide Kaihō in various ways.

Tamaharuwake, hearing of Takahime's Biyōki, came to visit her along with his wife, Okatsu.

Tamaharuwake addressed Enshiu, who was working in the front garden,

Tamaharuwake: 'Enshiu-dono, I have heard that Takahime-sama is feeling somewhat unwell; how is her Goyōsu?'

Enshiu: 'Yes, ever since she gave an Enzetsu at the Yahirodono the other day, she has been saying that her Kanjin treasure turned into a stone, and in her anger, she threw it into the Tameike. Since then, she has been complaining of feeling unwell and has remained in bed, and day after day, she has been saying nothing but Uhagoto. It is truly a troublesome situation.'

Tamaharuwake: 'If it is no Sashitsukahe, please tell her that Tamaharuwake and his wife have come to offer their Mimahi.'

Enshiu: 'I understand.'

He went into the inner room, leaned close to her ear, and said,

Enshiu: 'Takahime-sama, the Sendenshi Tamaharuwake has come to offer his Mimahi.'

Although Takahime had fallen into a state of unconsciousness, she suddenly regained her senses at the mention of the word 'Tama' (jewel), and said,

Takahime: 'What, the Tama has appeared? That is splendid. Bring it to me quickly.'

She sat up. Enshiu, fearing that if he revealed the truth that it was not the Tama but Tamaharuwake who had come, Takahime would be disappointed and fall into a critical condition again, said nonchalantly,

Enshiu: 'Yes, the Tama has appeared.'

Before he could finish his sentence, Takahime said,

Takahime: 'Bring it here quickly.'

Enshiu replied,

Enshiu: 'Yes.'

He went out to the front and said,

Enshiu: 'Tamaharuwake-sama, Okatsu-sama, please proceed inside. Takahime-sama is waiting for you very eagerly.'

Tamaharuwake and Okatsu proceeded into the inner room, and upon seeing her, they found Takahime sitting on her sickbed with a Nejihachimaki, her face bright red.

Tamaharuwake: 'Takahime-sama, I heard that you were suffering from a Biyōki, and I was so concerned that I came to visit you.'

Takahime: 'I do not have any particular Biyōki, but my Kanjōdama reached its limit and caused a fever. It seems that when someone who is always healthy sleeps for once, it creates quite a rumor. Ah, I am already fine.'

Okatsu: 'My husband is always indebted to you, and I should have visited you sooner, but I have been quite rude.'

Takahime: 'Are you the wife of Tama-sama? Truly, you are a lovely and beautiful person; Tamaharuwake-sama, you are a fortunate man. By the way, Tamaharuwake-sama, what are you all saying regarding the loss of the Nyoihōju-no-Tama?'

§ 3

Takahime-sama, do you not know the saying that one should be quick to blame oneself rather than others?

I have known such things since long ago. Try thinking about it carefully. The diamond-like indestructible Hotsushiyu and the purple jewel are, so to speak, things that were once within my own body, and they are the same as my own Mitama. Is it not just a difference of whether I spat them out from my belly or did not? Ah, if it were like this, if only I had kept them swallowed in my belly, such a Buchōhafu would not have occurred. Because you dragged in that useless Kobiki Mokusuke and that Ama-tsuchiyo like Ohatsu and made them force the Hotsushiyu out of Takahime's belly, it has come to this. If you trace the origin of this Daisekinin, Tama-sama, you are the one who must bear it. Next is Mokusuke's daughter Ohatsu; if you are going to Kuchigota, try doing it now.

Takahime-sama, you say such outrageous things. Are the spitting out of the jewel and this recent Funshitsu not separate Betsumondai? I am somewhat Meiwaku when you mix them up like that.

That Rikutsu of yours is bad. You are, so to speak, a Shinmaimono Hashitayakunin. I am the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake; even as fellow Sendenshi, there is a gap between Ten and earth. Do you intend to watch calmly while I fail? My failure is, so to speak, the same as the self-destruction of the Sangyōkyō. Even if you fail once or twice, it will never affect the Sangyōkyō. In any case, be aware of the Daisekinin, say that you stole it, take on the responsibility, and for the time being, muddy the waters of this place. In the meantime, this Takahime will search for the whereabouts with my divine eye, clear your innocence, and then it will be said that Tamawakewake-sama is a splendid person, and your credibility will increase all the more. Those who serve the gods must have at least this much sacrificial spirit; if you cannot do that, then return your position as a Sendenshi. Now, Okatsu-sama, is what I say unreasonable? If it is unreasonable, please tell me clearly that it is unreasonable.

Perhaps because she was suffering from some mental abnormality, she spouted out selfish and arbitrary Rikutsu.

Tamawakewake: 'Well, well, Takahime-sama, please calm yourself. Since you are a little bit agitated, I am afraid it will be harmful to your illness, so I will take my leave for today.'

Hey, hey, are you trying to smear this grave Daisekinin onto this Takahime? Perhaps you secretly did something, did you not? Your behavior seems suspicious indeed.

'If you treat me as a Biyōnin, you say things that are too much. From now on, I will also go and report this to the Kyōshiyu, Kotoyoriwake-sama.'

'What is Kotoyoriwake? Is he not the husband of Kotoyorihime? If I may say so, he is my younger sister's husband and the same as my own younger brother. Leaving aside Takahime, who is possessed by the true Hinodewake-no-Kami, what good would it do to say anything to such a person? He may look somewhat grand in the Ningenkai with his Chie and learning, but from the perspective of Kami, he is like an Akanbo. Why will you not listen to what Takahime says?'

She glares with her eyes shaped like a Sankaku. Okatsu, unable to bear her tears of regret, collapses on the spot, weeping.

Takahime: 'If things could be settled by crying, it would be an easy matter. I would like to cry too, but until I find the whereabouts of the Ontakara of the Shinsei Jiyōjiyu, how can I be so Kiraku as to just sit here and cry? Do you not understand how much more painful it is for Takahime, who endures and exerts herself, than for you, who opens your mouth wide and wails?'

Tamaharuwake: 'In any case, I shall take my leave for Kefu. I will think it over carefully and return with a Gohenji.'

'Hold on, you Fūfu, you must not move even an inch until this Kaiketsu is reached.'

'What a Meiwaku this is. Okatsu, what shall we do?'

Okatsu raises her voice again and begins to wail loudly. Takahime, while scratching the Kanadarahi by her bedside with her nails, says impatiently,

'Ah, I want the Tama. I want the Tama. Even if I have the Tama, it is nothing but the heads of trashy Ningen, so it is of no use. To think that so many Konnyaku-dama have gathered here, will you not firmly... Tama-sama...'

She strikes Tamaharuwake on the head with the Kanadarahi with a loud clang. Tamaharuwake mutters,

'This has become a troublesome matter; what she says is completely incoherent, and in the end, her soul has departed and she has gone mad.'

Hearing this, Takahime pouts and says,

'What, does it look to you as if I have gone mad?'

'You, who mock me as having gone mad, seem to be in a very dire predicament. Hahaha!'

He laughs loudly in a fit of desperation. Takahime becomes angry and says,

'Such rude behavior toward your superior.'

Conversely, being called rude by the one who had just struck him on the head, Tamaharuwake takes Okatsu by the hand and says,

'Come, Okatsu, sitting here for long is fearful; let us return home until your spirits are calmed.'

They abandon the scene and run out to the front. Takahime is shouting in the inner room like a madwoman.

Mokusuke, who came to visit upon hearing of Takahime's illness, leading Ohatsu by the hand, encounters the couple Tamaharuwake at the gate and says,

'Oh, is that you, Sensei?'

'Is that you, Mokusuke-sama? Ohatsu-sama, thank you for coming.'

'How is Takahime-sama's condition?'

'Oh, it is terrible. I was struck with a clang. She resents me, Ohatsu-sama, and especially you, Mokusuke-sama, very much. Be careful, for if you get struck with a clang again, you will not be able to endure it.'

'I do not understand the reason at all.'

'You do not need to think about it; if you go inside, you will understand. I am in a hurry, so please excuse me for going ahead.'

Saying this, he and his wife Okatsu head back toward their home in a flurry.

とあべこべに、頭をこづいた方から無礼呼はりを浴びせかけられ、玉治別はお勝の手を取り、

『サアお勝、長坐は畏れぢや、気の鎮まる迄家に帰らう』

と此場を見捨てて表へ駆出した。高姫は狂気の如く奥の間で怒鳴つて居る。

高姫の病気と聞いて見舞にやつて来た杢助は、お初の手を引き、門口で玉治別夫婦にベツタリ出会し、

『ヤア、先生か』

『杢助さまか、お初さま、ようお出なさいました』

『高姫さまの様子は何うですか』

『いやもう大変です。カンと叩られて来ました。大変に、私やお初さま始め、杢助さまを恨んで居ますよ。用心なさい、又カンとやられちや耐りませぬからなア』

『テンと訳が分りませぬなア』

『別に勘考せいでも奥へお出になれば分ります。一寸私は急ぎますから、お先へ御免蒙ります』

と云ひながら女房のお勝と共に、慌しく吾家をさして帰つて行く。
