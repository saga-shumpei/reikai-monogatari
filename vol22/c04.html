Chapter 4: Jewel 探志 [696]

玉探志〔696〕

§ 1

Following Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, the five, Kurohime, Takayorihime, Tatsukuniwake, Teristan, and Karlins, made a Sanpai at the Nishiki no Kiuden, and just as Kotoyoriwakenomikoto proceeded deep into the palace for Shinmu and the five were about to return to their Iheji, they encountered Takahime, Murasakihime, and Wakahiko, the three of them, at the Jifujigaitō.

Takahime: 'Well, well, Kurohime-sama, Takayorihime-sama, and everyone else, please come to Takahime's house for a moment. There is something I wish to ask you about, which is of great importance.'

At these words, which seemed to carry a hidden Imi, Kurohime felt as if her heart had been pierced. However, feigning indifference, she replied,

Kurohime: 'Yes, I do not know what your business is, but I am just now on my way back from a Sanpai. When would be a good time for me to come?'

Takahime: 'Everyone, since we three are going to make a Sanpai, please go ahead and wait at my house. I will return immediately. Takayorihime-sama, Tatsukuniwake-sama, Te-sama, and Ka-sama, please wait together as well.'

Leaving behind this sharp-toned Kotobajiri, she hurried off along the Sanpaimichi. With an Ichimotsu in her heart and lost in Shian, Kurohime stopped by Takahime's house, and the five of them gathered together, waiting for her return.

Teristan: 'Excuse me, Kurohime-sama, Takahime-sama's complexion was strange. As they say, evil deeds travel a thousand miles; could it be that the commotion from this morning has reached Takahime-sama's ears?'

Kurohime: 'Well, she did seem different than usual; this has become a troublesome matter. You, because you do such useless things, everyone is worried.'

Te and Ka tilted their heads and said,

Teristan and Karlins: 'Yes, I was in the wrong. However, since the Kyōshu-sama has forgiven us, shall we not speak of that matter anymore? If we make a fuss and render the Kyōshu's Kotoba in vain, it must not leak out to the world.'

Kurohime: 'Hmph, that may be convenient for you, but I, as the one responsible, have lost a great deal of face. You are truly a person one cannot let their guard down around. Teristan, even in a relationship where we share our food, I cannot let my guard down; you are truly a troublesome person. I suppose you will reform from here on out, will you not?'

Karlins: 'Since the incident has veered off in a different direction from the jewel, there is nothing to be done. Well, in any case, since it has become a matter of us two having stolen it, there is no help for it, no matter what is said.'

言依別命に従ひて黒姫、鷹依姫、竜国別、テーリスタン、カーリンスの五人は錦の宮に参拝し、言依別命は宮殿深く神務の為めに進み入り、五人は各家路に帰らむとする時しも、高姫、紫姫、若彦の三人と十字街頭にピタリと出会した。

高姫『これはこれは黒姫さま、鷹依姫さま、その他御一同、一寸高姫の宅まで来て下さい。折入つてお訊ねしたい事が御座います』

意味あり気な此の言葉に黒姫はハツと胸を刺される心地がした。されど、さあらぬ態にて、

『ハイ、何用か存じませぬが、妾は今参拝の帰り路で御座います。何時参りましたら宜しいでせうか』

『皆さま、妾等三人は参拝して来ますから、先へ妾の宅まで帰つて居て下さい。直に帰りますから。鷹依姫さまも、竜国別さまも、テーさまも、カーさまも御一緒に待つて居て下さい』

と撥ねた様な言葉尻を残して忙しさうに参拝道に進み行く。黒姫は胸に一物、思案に暮れながら高姫の宅に立寄り、帰宅を五人一同打揃ひ待つて居た。

テーリスタン『モシ黒姫さま、高姫さまの顔色が変つて居ましたな。悪事千里と云つて、今朝の騒ぎが高姫さまの耳へ這入つたのぢやありますまいか』

黒姫『サア、何だか何時もに変る気色だつた、困つた事になりましたな。お前、仕様もない事をするものだから各々に心配をするのだよ』

テー、カーは首を傾け、

『ハイ、私が悪う御座いました。併し教主様がお赦し下さつたのだから、もう彼の話は言はぬやうに致しませうかい。折角教主の言葉を無にしてガヤガヤ騒ぐと、世間へ洩れてはなりませぬから』

『ヘン、貴方には都合が宜しからうが、責任者たる妾は大変に面目玉を潰しました。本当に油断のならぬお方ぢやなア。テーリスタン、これから口の物を喰ひ合ふ様な仲でも油断は出来ませぬぜ、本当に困つた人だ。もう是きり改心をするでせうな』

カーリンス『玉から事件が方角違ひに外れて居るのだから仕方がない。まあまあ兎も角、吾々両人が盗んだ事に成つて居るのだから、何と言はれても仕方がないさ』

§ 2

Takahime: 'Hmph, to say that it cannot be helped because it has come to this—you certainly have a way with words. Ho ho ho ho!'

As she said this, Takahime came running back in a hurried, mincing gait, shaking her body with a bouncy motion.

Takahime: 'Now, Wakahiko-sama, Murasakihime-sama, please come inside.'

She led the way.

Wakahiko and Murasakihime: 'Hai.'

The two replied and passed into the inner room.

Kurohime: 'Takahime-sama, you have returned remarkably early.'

Takahime: 'I cannot even offer my greetings in my usual leisurely manner. My, you are certainly sitting there quite calmly. Have you discovered the whereabouts of the Ōgon no Tama?'

Kurohime: 'Eh, even now... I do not... know. But from whom did you hear about this?'

Takahime: 'You seem to think that no one knows, but there is not a single person who is unaware of the quarrel you two have been having since last night. Everyone has heard it.'

Kurohime: 'It is truly a matter for which I have no excuse.'

Takahime: 'You say you have no excuse, yet you have lost the Kanjinkaname Goshinpau and can still remain so at ease?'

Telistan: 'If you please, Takahime-sama, it is not Kurohime-sama who is at fault. It is the two of us, Karlins and I, who did such and such.'

Takahime: 'Do not ask, it is filthy. Such things have already reached my ears. However, the essential responsibility lies with Kurohime-sama. Kurohime-sama, what will you do? I wish to hear your Goriyōken.'

Kurohime: 'Since Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama has taken everything upon himself, I shall say nothing further.'

Takahime: 'Do you think your responsibility is fulfilled by that? To smear everything onto Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama and remain so nonchalant—is there not a limit to your lack of sensitivity?'

Kurohime: 'Even if you say that, is it not true that there is nothing to be done? Since it fell into the hands of the Brahmins long ago due to the deep-seated schemes of the Yamata no Orochi, how could it ever return to its original state? It was my mistake to have employed people like Telistan and Karlins.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Tel and Kar, what do you intend to do?'

Telistan: 'Hai, I have no excuse.'

Karlins: 'There is nothing that can be done.'

Takahime: 'How can you say such things! Now, Kurohime-sama, in order to fulfill this responsibility, you must search through the grass as long as you have life, regain possession of it, and return the treasure for the fulfillment of the divine government. Why are you looking around so restlessly?'

§ 3

He struck his seated knees against the tatami with such force that it left an indentation, and he let out a fierce roar.

Kurohime: 'I, too, have made up my mind.'

'I would have you abandon this resolution of yours, which is nothing but a constant resort to blades at every second word. How can you be so irresponsible? Come now, be off with you at once. And until that jewel is in your hands, you shall not see my face again. Takayorihime-sama, you too are under suspicion, so you cannot simply remain silent. Tatsukuniwake-dono, you cannot remain silent either, for the sake of clearing your parent's name. As for the two of you, Te and Ka, whether you truly stole it or not, I do not know, but you must make one more effort, step out into the Sekai, and the five of you must separate and search throughout the Godaishiu. Is that not so? Wakahiko-sama, Murasakihime-sama, you cannot simply sit there blankly guarding the palace that has lost its jewel, now that the Ōgon jewel has been stolen. Murasakihime-sama, Wakahiko-sama, give me your answer.'

Thus, he vented his anger indiscriminately, even lashing out at the two who were uninvolved.

Wakahiko: 'Ah, one never knows where the Tobashiri will land. However, I shall listen to the Goiken of Kotoyoriwake-sama and act accordingly. Since Murasakihime-sama is now in a Jiueu, this is not something I can decide on my own.'

Takahime: 'Can you really speak so calmly, saying that you have no direct responsibility? Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama is a weak-willed fellow, which is why he does not deal with such a blunder by Kurohime-sama. However, as the Kyōshu, he cannot simply blame anyone, so he manifests the true nature of the Mizu-no-Mitama and appears calm on the surface as if nothing has happened, but there is no doubt that in his heart, he is worried. Unless one is a Mitama who can understand ten things from hearing one, one cannot perform the essential divine service. Come, Kurohime-sama, what will you do? Even if the Kyōshu, Kotoyoriwake, forgives you, I, Takahime, will not. I once established the Uranai-kyō and, together with you all, defied the Hensei-joshi, but that was because I misunderstood the heart of Lord Susanoo-no-Mikoto. Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama, who was born from the Mitama of the Hensei-joshi, is taking all that sin upon himself, and when I think of that, I feel so sorry for him that I cannot bear it. As for the Takamagahara of the earth, I, Takahime, shall from now on support Tamateruhiko-sama and Tamateruhime-sama and show you that I can perform the divine service splendidly. Come, why do you not prepare yourselves quickly?'

と坐つた膝を畳が凹む程打つけて雄猛びした。

黒姫『妾も決心して居りますよ』

『二言目には刃物三昧の決心は廃めて貰ひませう。そんな無責任な事がありますか。サアサアとつとと出なさい。さうして其玉が手に入らぬ事には再びお目には懸りませぬよ。鷹依姫さま、お前さまも嫌疑が掛つた身体ぢや、黙としては居られますまい。竜国別さまは、親の疑を晴らす為に是も黙としては居られまい。テー、カーの両人も本当に盗つたか盗らぬか、そりや知らぬが、もう一苦労して世界に踏み出し、五人が五大洲に別れて探して来ねばなりますまい。さうぢやありませぬか。若彦さま、紫姫さま、黄金の玉を盗られた玉無しの宮を、ヌツケリと番して居る訳にはゆきますまい。紫姫さま、若彦さま、返答を聞かせなさい』

と無関係の両人にまで腹立ち紛れに八つ当りに当る。

若彦『あゝあ、何処へ飛沫が来るか分つたものぢやない。併し私は言依別さまの御意見を伺つて其上に致しませう。紫姫さまも今では重要な位置に居られるのだから、之も自分の自由にはなりますまい』

高姫『お前さまに直接責任がないと言つて、そんな平気な事を言つて居られますかいなア。言依別命様は柔弱な奴灰殻ぢやから、斯んな黒姫さまの失態を何とも処置をつけないのだ。然し教主として誰を悪いと云ふ訳にもゆかず、瑞の御魂の本性を現はし、表面は何喰はぬ顔して平気に見せて御座るが、心の裡は矢張り御心配して御座るに間違ひない。一を聞いて十を悟る身魂でないと、肝腎の御用は勤まりますまい。サア黒姫さま、如何なさいます、言依別の教主が赦されても、此高姫が承知致しませぬぞや。妾も一度はウラナイ教を樹て、お前さま等と共に変性女子に背いて見たが、それも素盞嗚尊様のお心を取違ひして居つたからだ。変性女子の身魂からお生れ遊ばした言依別命様も自分が其罪を一身に御引受け遊ばして御座るのぢや、それを思へば妾はお気の毒で堪らない。地の高天原は此高姫が是から玉照彦さま、玉照姫さまを守り立てて立派に御用を勤めて見せます。サア早く何とか準備を為さらぬか』

§ 4

Kurohime: 'I am also a Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, and I shall certainly work in some way to show you my devotion.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'We, too, shall take our leave and set out on a journey around the Sekai to clear the Kengi of Hahauhesama. And we shall search for the Arika of the jewel.'

Takayorihime: 'Nay, though I am called a Toshiyori, I still have Genki. I shall travel all over the Sekai until I find this jewel, no matter how far I must go.'

Takahime: 'That would be very good; it is as it should be. Now, Te, Ka, what do you intend to do?'

Teristan: 'Even if I must crawl through the belly of the Yamata no Orochi, I shall not rest until I find the Arika of the jewel.'

Karlins: 'I am of the same mind. However, Takahime-sama, I must say this: please, I beg you to assist the Kamisama Kotoyoriwake in keeping the Niyoihotsushiyu and the purple jewel safe so that they are not lost.'

Takahime: 'Yes, yes, even without you telling me such things, I am being careful beyond measure. Do not worry, and go and find the Arika of the jewel as soon as you can. And when you find the Arika, please send word by Kototama immediately. Everyone, is that your intention... is that understood?'

She said this in a sharp, striking tone.

'Yes, we understand. In that case, we shall go and bid farewell to the Kyōshu-sama Kotoyoriwake for a moment.'

As the five of them were about to stand up, Takahime stopped them and said,

'Wait a moment. If you are to serve the Kamisama, it is the true way to do so without the Kyōshu Kotoyoriwake knowing. The Kyōshu is prone to tears, and if he says something soft again, your resolve will immediately weaken, so I shall explain it to him in a good way. Now, be off with you quickly.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Ah, what a great disaster; I wonder if we are being driven out of Takamagahara by Takahime-sama.'

'If you do not like it, you need not go.'

Takahime glared at them. The five of them, having no choice, left Takahime's residence dejectedly, bowed from afar to the Nishiki no Miya, prepared their travel attire, and set out toward various parts of the Sekai in search of the jewel.
