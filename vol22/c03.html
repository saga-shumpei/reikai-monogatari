§ 1

Kurohime finished her morning visit to the Palace of Brocade, stopped by the house of Tatsukuniwake on her way back, entered a back room, and began to whisper with Takayorihime.

Kurohime: 'Takayorihime-sama, what do you think is the greatest treasure in the world?'

Takayorihime: 'I believe that the sincerity of heaven and earth is the greatest treasure, more than the Niyoihotsushiyu, more than the balls of Ōgon, and more than the purple jewels.'

Kurohime: 'Ah, is that so? That is indeed reasonable. But what have you done with that treasure?'

Takayorihime: 'As I have a clouded Mitama, I cannot obtain the true treasure, which is a shameful thing before the Kamisama. The Kamisama is pushing it toward me, saying to take the jewel of sincerity quickly, but as a Ningen, my Mitama is so clouded that I cannot receive it. I pray morning and evening, wishing to obtain the treasure called sincerity by some means.'

Kurohime: 'That is not so for you. You must find the balls of Ōgon more Ketsukō than the jewel of sincerity. Where have you secretly hidden the ball of Ōgon, the sole treasure of the Ananai-kyō?'

Takayorihime: 'Eh, what are you saying? Those are words I cannot Gaten. What are you saying has happened to the ball of Ōgon?'

Kurohime: 'Do not play innocent and act stupid. You must remember it in your heart. No matter how you try to hide it, there is no missing it when I, Kurohime, stare at it with these eyes. Already, Teristan and Karlins are speaking in a tone that implies you ordered them to steal the ball of Ōgon.'

Takayorihime: 'Did those two, Te- and Ka-, say such a thing? On what evidence would they tell such an outrageous lie?'

Kurohime: 'Hmph, you play the fool well. It is that ball of Ōgon you dug up from the root of the pine tree. When you were boasting as the leader of the Alps-kyō at Takaharuyama, you let Tokukō infiltrate the holy ground and made him think about the whereabouts of the jewel, did you not? That fellow called Toku is the one who informed Mukadehime of its location. He has now entered your Ananai-kyō with a false conversion, pretending to know nothing, has he not? In truth, it is certain that with his guidance, both Te- and Ka- stole the ball of Ōgon that I was keeping, under your instructions. I am now prepared to risk my life. I will pull off your head and die with dignity myself; now, what do you say?'

At this sudden Kitsumon, Takayorihime was astonished, standing Bauzen with a Matsusao face, and Mitsu-ing Kurohime.

She laughed unpleasantly. Takayorihime, looking bewildered, shed tears profusely.

Takayorihime: 'Ah, Lord God, please, I beseech Thee, distinguish this black from white. I am now suffering from a most Taihen difficult accusation.'

She pressed her hands together in prayer. Kurohime raised her voice sharply.

Kurohime: 'Takayorihime-sama, do not act like a fool. Kurohime is not the type to swallow such lies; hen, my stock is a little different from yours.'

Takayorihime: 'Kurohime-sama, are you saying that in earnest? Are you trying to impose a difficult accusation upon me that I never even dreamed of, and smear your own crime of poor supervision onto me? Even though I look like this, I have once served as the leader of a religious sect. If you say such reckless things, I will not forgive you.'

Kurohime: 'To whom are you saying that you will not forgive? It is I who cannot forgive you. What a brazen spirit you have.'

Takayorihime: 'Kurohime-sama, you must have some grudge against me to blow such a difficult accusation at me. If so, that is fine, I have my own thoughts as well. If you were someone without children like you, it might be fine, but I have one adorable son in heaven and on earth. If I am accused of such a thing, how can my son stand in this world? If it is said that the mother of Tatsukuniwake stole a treasure in the sacred land, does that not stain not only my son but even the names of my ancestors? What evidence do you have to say such unreasonable things? I cannot perform such tricks as drinking and vomiting like Takahime-sama. You must think that I have swallowed it in my stomach, do you not?'

Kurohime: 'That is likely within your own belly. One cannot tell what is in a person's belly from the outside, you see.'

Takayorihime: 'If that is so, I shall cut open my belly to show you, in order to prove my Ketsupaku. In return, what will you do if you find that I have not swallowed it?'

Kurohime: 'It is not only in the belly that one can hide a jewel. Can it not be hidden in the earth, in a storehouse, in a river, or anywhere else? Even if you try to deceive Kurohime with such shallow talk, it will not work; this Kurohime is a little different, so I will not be taken in by your Kuchiguruma. Ohohoho!'

She jerked her chin, squared her shoulders, stuck the tip of her tongue out slightly to her lips, and even blinked her eyes to show her mockery. Takayorihime cried out in frustration and mortification.

Kurohime: 'Do you think matters will be settled by crying? Why do you not speak Daudau? Even if you try to intimidate me with tears, they are Dōzen to the tears of an Oyama; do you think I would fall for such a trick?'

She jerked her chin again to make a fool of her. Takayorihime wept all the more in her Harada.

Hearing the voice, Tatsukuniwake, who had just returned to the Kadoguchi, came running.

Tatsukuniwake: 'Mother, is something wrong? What has happened? Ah, Kurohime-sama, good morning. Is my mother unwell?'

Kurohime said with a Nikuniku look:

Kurohime: 'Well, you are Tatsukuniwake, is she not crying precisely because she is wicked? She is weeping because she is choked up and cannot answer. Though it is not for me to say about your own parent, she is truly a shocking Akutau.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Kurohime-sama, that my mother is an Akutau, what is the reason for that?'

Kurohime smiled and said:

Kurohime: 'Foxes of the same hole, it is amazing how you have placed such Shinrai in each other. Between you, Teristan, and Karlins, with these Shikon of evil gathered together, you can commit any Akuji. There is no room for Yudan or a moment's respite. Ohohohoho!'

She swayed her upper body as she spoke.

Just then, Teristan and Karlins entered in a disheveled state. Seeing this, Tatsukuniwake said:

Tatsukuniwake: 'Hey, you two, Ter and Kar, what is with those faces? Kisama, did you have a fight?'

Since Kurohime-sama failed in her duty to guard the precious jewel, she should not be in a position to say such things. I sympathize with her, but is it not too much to call us two, and even Takayorihime-sama, thieves? I want to find a way to clear the suspicion of Kurohime-sama.'

Te and Ka: 'Who would commit such a theft of the jewel, as if fearing Shinbatsu? Baka, baka!'

They repeated this again and again. Kurohime, Tatsukuniwake, and Takayorihime all stood up at once.

Kurohime, Tatsukuniwake, Takayorihime: 'You two, who communicate with the Gokuakubudau Mukadehime, are no longer to be tolerated!'

With this, the five of them became embroiled in a great Ōgenkuwa.

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto finished his Hairei at the Nishiki-no-miya, passed by the front of this place Shizushizu, and hearing the Sauzau Monōto, he entered the inner room to see what was the matter, only to find this commotion.

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Now, now, everyone, why are you engaging in such a Kenkuwa, considering you are Sendenshi and believers?'

Kurohime: 'Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama, these two Koitsu have stolen the jewel that I was keeping. They gave it to Mukadehime. They are insolent fellows who call us Bakamono and Chōrō us.'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Teristan, Karlins, you are truly admirable fellows. That is how it should be; you are the models for the believers of Sangokyō. You have done well to obtain the true jewel.'

The two, Teristan and Ka, were overcome with tears of joy.

Teristan and Ka: 'Hai, hai!'

They said only this, and remained there, clinging to the tatami and weeping.

Kurohime: 'Moshi-moshi, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama, what are you saying? Is there any reason to praise such thieves? You must be out of your mind.'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Ah, Kurohime, Takayorihime, Tatsukuniwake, Teristan, and Karlins-dono, everything is according to the plan of the Lord God. Do not worry; it must be that the Lord God has a deep intention. From this moment on, it is best not to speak of the jewel. It is troublesome for both sides, so please leave everything to me.'

Kurohime: 'Does it not matter if the jewel is gone?'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'I will take responsibility. Everyone, please forget about this from now on.'

He took a nusa from his bosom,

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Harae-tamae, Kiyome-tamae!'

He said, waving it left, right, and left.

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Now, everyone, this has completely resolved the matter.'

Kurohime remained seated, thrusting out her left arm, tilting her body, and staring at the face of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto as if to bore a hole in it, in a posture as if her soul had been sucked out like a soft-shelled turtle.

Kurohime: 'Hee!'

She replied with a long response, her expression unsettled.

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Now, everyone, let us go to worship at the shrine.'

He stood at the lead. Everyone finally felt relieved and left the house of Tatsukuniwake to worship at the Nishiki-no-miya.

と連発する。黒姫、竜国別、鷹依姫の三人は一斉に立ち上り、

『極悪無道の蜈蚣姫に款を通ずる両人、もはや了簡ならぬぞ』

と茲に五人は入り乱れて大喧嘩をおつ始めた。

言依別命は錦の宮の拝礼を終り、静々と此前を通り、騒々しき物音に何事ならむと奥へ入り来り見れば、此の騒ぎ。

言依別命『これこれ皆さま、宣伝使や信者の身を以て何喧嘩をなさるのか』

黒姫『言依別命様、此奴両人、私が保管して居る玉を盗んだのはテー、カーだ。蜈蚣姫に渡してやつたのだ。馬鹿者よと云うて、私等を嘲弄する不届きな奴で御座います』

言依別命『テーリスタン、カーリンス、お前は実に感心な奴だ。さうなくてはならぬ、三五教の信者の亀鑑だ。誠の玉を能くも手に入れたなア』

テー、カーの二人は嬉し涙に暮れて、

『ハイハイ』

と云つたきり畳に食ひついて泣いて居る。

黒姫『モシモシ言依別命様、お前様は何と云ふ事を仰有る。こんなドラ盗人を褒めると云ふ事がありますか。何うかして居ますなア』

言依別命『アヽ、黒姫、鷹依姫、竜国別、テーリスタン、カーリンス殿、何事も神様の御計らひだ。御心配なさいますな、神様に深き思召のある事でせう。只今限り玉の事は云はないがよい。互に迷惑ですから、何事も私に任して置いて下さい』

黒姫『玉がなくてもかまひませぬのか』

言依別命『責任は私が負ひます。皆さま、これきり忘れて下さい』

と懐より幣を取り出し、

『祓ひ給へ清め給へ』

と云ひながら左右左に打ち振り、

『さあ皆さま、これですつかり解決がつきましたよ』

黒姫は坐つたまま左の腕を突つ張り、体を斜にして言依別命の顔を穴のあくほど凝視め、鼈に尻を抜かれたやうなスタイルで、

『ヘー』

と長返事しながら落着かぬ面色である。

言依別命『サア皆さま、お宮へ参拝しませう』

と先に立つ。一同は漸く胸を撫で下し、錦の宮に参拝せむと竜国別の家を立ち出でた。

The sun of early spring shone brilliantly, even dazzlingly, upon the heads of the six.

The golden orb is also a Niyoi-hotsushiyu, / The purple gem is also a Hotsushiyu, / The Kongaufue Shingiyoku / Is also called the Niyoi-hotsushiyu. / Among them, the Shingiyoku / That Takahime had swallowed / Is the greatest of all divine treasures. / Kurohime, having lost the location / Of the golden orb / Entrusted to her by Kotoyoriwake-no-mikoto, / Could not settle her heart. / Suspecting even Teristan, Karlins, / And Takayorihime, / She fretted in various ways, / And in the midst of the confusion, / Kotoyoriwake appeared. / For a moment, the situation / Was calmed, yet the clouds and mists / In the sky of her heart / Could not be cleared, and at that very moment,

It happened that they encountered Takahime / At the crossroads / As she was on her way to worship / At the Nishiki-no-miya. / Kurohime and the other four / Were invited to Takahime's residence, / And Kurohime, being pressed / To answer for the responsibility / Of losing the precious divine treasure, / Finally steeled her resolve there. / Looking back at the sanctuary, / Determined to search for the location / Of the golden orb, / Takayorihime, Tatsukuniwake, / Teristan, and Karlins— / These five set out across the world, / Searching every corner of the lands / In the four directions. / How pitiful it is to seek elsewhere / Under the white clouds, / Following the deep divine plan of the spirit world. / Nevertheless, this time,

The location of the orb / Is within the heart of the god Kotoyoriwake. / Fearing the command of the god, / He had hidden it deep within his heart. / As a person who is not a god, / There is no way to know / This divine strategy. / How wretched it is to depart weeping. / From this point on, the five are manipulated / By the threads of the mechanism of the spirit world, / Serving unknowingly / In the divine work of subduing demons. / As this strange tale of the age of the gods / Proceeds in its oral narration, / Gradually, it will become more interesting, / And one will come to realize the deep divine intent. / Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, / May the spirits be blessed.
