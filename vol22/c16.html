Chapter 16: 千万無量 [708]

千万無量〔708〕

§ 1

Tamanohime: 'The flow of water and the destiny of men / Yesterday or today upon the Asukagaha / Changing to deep pools and rapids in this world / Born into the divine miiki / Becoming husband and wife / Becoming parent and child, even in the age of the gods / Bound by the thread of destiny / Knowing well that this fleeting world of utsusemi / Has no means of untangling, yet / Enveloped in the clouds of rinne / The path of love struggling to advance / The wretchedness of wandering in yamiji / The sun shines across, the moon waxes / Or in a world where it wanes / The things that change not are parent and child / The unending nagori of younger sister and older brother / Deep vows and like shirakumo / Thou to the east and I to the west / Wandering south and north / When shall we meet again upon Afumiji?'

Tamanohime: 'Even Mino and Ohari are without fixed abode / Entrusting the divine grace to Tohotohomi / Praying at the mountain of Fuji in Suruga / To the onkami of Konohanahime / Having the kahi of making prayers and hanging them / A joyful afuse upon the shore of Miho / The sounding of the pine trees where / The urana sweeps across / Flowing on as the wind wills / Chosen as the august shields of the living kami who saves this world / As the divine-appointed proselytizers / Disguising their bodies in a myriad of transformations / Taking a hundred hardships upon their bodies / The sincerity to save the people of the world / The firmly congealed bond of husband and wife / Upon the summit of Mt. Takatori / Emitting a golden radiance / The divine vow for the salvation of all sentient beings / The foundation of the heavenly pure land'

Tamanohime: 'To solidify it eternally and steadfastly / The auspicious reign to be governed is the age of Miroku / Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami / Toyokunihime no Ōkami / Kami Susanō no Ōkami / Receiving the divine decrees of the three august spirits upon the nape, and opening the world / The color of the heart is also that of Wakahiko / Where now is the life of my husband? / Though we met, oh, how we met / Separated by the barrier of human eyes / Even his voice, properly / Unable to even hear, Tamanohime / Tending a frail dream-path / My thoughts in the forest of Ikuta / The august spirit of Wakahimegimi / Settling eternally and steadfastly / Restoring the age of the gods once more / The divine work of saving gods and men across the four quarters of heaven and earth'

Tamanohime: 'My own august spirit, which is a joy to guard / Verily, the destiny to come is delightful / Ah, my husband, Wakahiko / Thou dost come wandering afar / To visit this hermitage where I lie hidden / The pure and noble august heart / Scooped up gently and finely into the breast / Returned to vanity / Surely thou wilt not make me resent thee / As long as the living kami who saves this world / Dureth, thou and I / Once again, when shall we be like the twin pines / In the evergreen eternity / Even though there be an age when frost is borne upon them / Speaking together of ancient days / There shall be things to rejoice and delight in / Ah, Kannagara kamigami / May your august spirits grow and bless us / May Wakahiko, who is my husband / Richly guard his destiny'

Tamanohime: 'Though I am a body of a woman / Reverencing the gods, beneath the heavens / Cherishing the spirits of the four quarters / The pure heart, not for a moment / Letting go from my breast, praying to the gods / Of heaven and earth, to the limit of my body / To the limit of my heart, the single sincerity of migisa / Though the people of the world whose bottom of the heart is Shiranui / May make commotion however they will / Solely for the august sake of the imperial gods / Harmonizing the hearts of husband and wife / Though my body is parted alive to east and west / Whatever hardships may come / Thy life entrusted to the gods / I too, hereafter, according to the august words of the Ōkami / Stepping across distant lands like shirakumo / As a proselytizer of the divine office / Crossing mountains and fields, crossing rivers'

Tamanohime: 'Floating upon the sea, unto the land of Tokoyo / To the very end of the island of Takasago / I will proceed and go, Kannagara / May your august spirits grow and bless me'

With these words accompanying the ichigenkin, together with the voice of the song, it echoed deep and mournful outside the hermitage.

Mokusuke sighed deeply for a while, then appeared powerlessly in the very place where the two women were playing the koto.

Mokusuke: 'Hatsuwakahimėsama, your tone has become very fine. Tamanohimesama, your tone is also quite well... no, it seems to bear a somewhat sorrowful tone. Is there something weighing upon your heart? For the color of the heart immediately appears upon the kototama.'

Tamanohime: 'Yes, the divine will is so wondrous and soaks deep into my body, and further, the kindness of others touched my breast, so I was choking with tears of gratitude.'

Mokusuke: 'In this world, if there is joy, there is sorrow; after sorrow, a truly joyful flower is bound to bloom. The cherry blossoms have been ruthlessly scattered by the night storm just like this, but the beauty of them scattered all over the surface of the garden is yet another layer deeper than the blossoms gazed upon the branches. In human affairs, there is no other way than to leave everything to the august will of God. Whatever hardships and sufferings you may encounter, you must never regret them. I too was separated by death from a single wife, and once I felt the impermanence of this floating world carrying an infant like a sad male mandarin duck, but I thought, "Wait a moment, for things to come to this is not human work; there must be something with a deep divine will. The wife who died by death seems pitiable, but the great compassionate God will surely help her to a place much more splendid than now. Ah, if I regret it, my dear wife will not be able to reach the divine realm either, and will wander, suffer, and agonize in the rinne. To forget is the best thing of all." Once I made this firm resolve, rather, being single is after all easier and finer. If I say such things, you may despise me in the bottom of your hearts or laugh, saying, "You are a heartless husband," but now, however much one regrets, nothing can be done. Since you too were born into this world with a human body, and furthermore, having been employed as noble proselytizers, your case differs from ordinary mortals of the world; just as your joys are deeper, your sufferings will also be deeper still. That suffering is the whip of God's grace. Whatever happens, please never worry at all.'

Though he spoke thus cheerfully with his lips, somehow Tamanohime's heart was also fathomed, and the color of sympathetic tears appeared in his voice.

Tamanohime: 'Thank you so much from the bottom of my heart for your endless kindness. Thinking that you intend to make us, husband and wife, into splendid Kamisama, for this repeated consideration, I feel as though you are a Kamisama.'

So saying, she stopped playing the koto, placed both hands on her knees, lowered her head, and her pitifulness in hiding her tears was touching. Hatsuwakahime opened her lovely lips,

Kami appear on the surface, Distinguishing good and evil; This world's creator, Kamunahohi, With a heart so vast, Ōnahohi. Now whatever may happen in the world of men, Before the august presence of the Kami, Offering the utmost of one's soul, Devoting solely the truth of Kototama, Morning and evening without fail, Praise and revere them, Kamunagara! May the Mitama multiply and prosper, And all beneath the heavens, Without exception, without a single one left, according to the heart of Sumekami, May they flourish and be saved into peace! Kami are together with us, Kami are together with us. Making the Miiki of Kami our staff, Making the path of truth our strength, Though the rough waves rage upon the ocean,

And tigers crouch in the fields, towering high, Whatever hardships may arise, how august! We shall surely reach the capital of victory. Extol and praise the grace of Kami, Fulfill and exhaust the path of Kami! Fulfill and exhaust the path of man! Man is the child of Kami, the palace of Kami; Man is the child of Kami, the palace of Kami.

So singing to the end, she once again took the single-string koto in her hands and wore a pleasant smile.

Suddenly outside the window, it grew noisy, and the footsteps of more than ten people came pattering. Though their faces could not be clearly discerned in the rising moonlight of that moment, the wriggling figures of human shadows entered the eyes of the three as if close at hand. When they looked, it was a big brawl. Binding tightly one man, they carried him to the front of Mokusuke's hermitage, and gathering around, they were letting fall a rain of fists.

Kō: 'Hey, have you met your doom? Treating us, the believers of Brahmanism, like evil Kami, you built a nest on Mount Takatori, made your wife Tamanohime use magic, threw us right into the middle of the pure spring, and made us suffer numerous physical wounds—that is payback. Come now, since it has come to this, our things, let us stop your breath and send you off on a journey to the ten million lands of the dead. Well now, Wakahime, you dared to wander all the way here to Ikuta Forest, didn't you?'

And once again he was letting fall an iron rain of fists regardless of the place.

The heart of Tamanohime, who saw the actual situation of her husband being beaten before her eyes, was as if tearing apart. Even if she were to break the laws of heaven and earth, to fly out and requite the villains with even a single sword stroke was her ardent desire, but being drawn by the calm and collected Mokusuke, she embraced her painful breast and feigned nonchalance.

Mokusuke: 'Tamanohime-sama, what an interesting thing has come to pass. While sitting, I have been shown a drama from inside the window. This too must be your gracious intention. Come now, quickly offer a congratulatory litany of praise to the Kamisama. I shall leisurely gaze at this drama. If you think him a person of necessity whom the Kamisama must use no matter what, even if a million enemies should attack, even if you should suffer a rain of iron fists, you will not sustain an injury even as thick as a cormorant's down. Though I know not whence he hails, it sounds like a man called Wakahime, who closely resembles your husband's name. Oh, what a spineless man he was. Haha-ha-ha-ha!'

He glossed it over with a forced laugh. Tamanohime, beside herself, quietly began to chant the Tianjin Norito while suppressing her pounding chest. Somehow her voice was trembling. From the body of Wakahime, who had been thrown to the ground and tormented by numerous villains, a five-colored spiritual light suddenly emitted, and with a single loud thud and a mysterious sound, when Mokusuke and Tamanohime looked out the window, the human shadows had vanished somewhere without a trace, and near the window, a single white fox trotted along leisurely, dragging its thick tail as it advanced toward the other side of the forest.

Mokusuke: 'Ahahaha, the Kamisama are teasing our feelings to the very end, aren't they!'

Tamanohime brought both hands together,

'Kamunagara Mitama Sakiwahai Masase.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Sumekamishin protect us, bless us.'

玉能姫『何から何まで、御親切に有難う御座います。吾々夫婦の者を立派な神様にしたててやらうと思召し下さいまして、重ね重ねの御心遣ひ、神様の様に存じます』

俄に窓の外騒がしく十数人の足音、バタバタと聞えて来た。折柄昇る月影に顔は確と分らねど人影の蠢めく姿、手にとる如く三人の目に入つた。見れば大喧嘩である。一人の男を引縛り、杢助が庵の窓前に運び来り、寄つて集つて拳骨の雨を降らして居る。

甲『ヤイ、往生いたしたか、吾々バラモン教の信徒を悪神扱ひしやがつて、鷹鳥山に巣を構へ、貴様の女房の玉能姫に魔術を使はせ此方を清泉の真中へ放り込み、身体に沢山の手疵を負はせやがつた、其返報がへしだ。サア、もう斯うなつては此方のもの、息の根を止めて十万億土の旅立さしてやらう。こりや若彦、能うのめのめと生田の森まで彷徨うて来よつたなア』

と又もや鉄拳の雨を所構はず降らして居る。

現在眼の前に夫が打擲されて居る実況を見たる玉能姫の心は張り裂ける如く、仮令天地の法則を破るとも、飛びかかつて悪者に一太刀なりと酬いたきは山々なれど、泰然自若たる杢助に心惹かれ、苦しき胸を抱き平気を装うて居る。

杢助『玉能姫さま、何と面白い事が出来ましたな。坐ながらにして窓の内から活劇を見せて貰ひました。これも有難き思召でせう。サア早く神様に感謝の祝詞を奏上なさいませ。私はゆつくりと此活劇を見物致しませう。神様が如何しても使はねばならぬ必要の人物と思召したならば、仮令百万の敵が攻め来るとも、如何に鉄拳の雨を蒙るとも、鵜の毛で突いた程の怪我も致しますまい。何処の人かは知らねども、貴女の夫の名によく似た若彦と言ふ男らしう御座います。さてもさても腑甲斐ない男もあつたものだなア。アハヽヽヽヽ』

と作り笑ひに紛らす。玉能姫は心も心ならず、轟く胸を抑へながら静に天津祝詞を奏上し始めた。何となく声は震うて居た。地上に投げられ数多の悪者に苛責まれ居た若彦の身体より、忽ち五色の霊光発射し、ドンと一声、不思議の物音に杢助、玉能姫は窓外を眺むれば、人影は何処へ消えしか跡形もなく、窓近く一つの白狐ノソリノソリと太き尾を下げて森の彼方に進み行く。

玉能姫は両手を合せ、

『惟神霊幸倍坐世』
