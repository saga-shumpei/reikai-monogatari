Chapter 17: 生田の Forest [709]

生田の森〔709〕

§ 1

Plum blossoms of Sanzensekai, scattering their fragrant scent and bearing fruit, adorning the spring fields of Yomo, even the cherry blossoms have fallen, and like the wild deutzia blossoms blooming in profusion, pure white as the heart of Kami, near the edge of the forest of Ikuta, Tamanohime who rivals the flowers, and Hatsuwakahime walking together as a pair, gazing upon the scenery of Shiyoka, receiving the divine revelation at the summit of Futatabiyama, climbing ever onward—how truly enchanting it is.

Just as Mokusuke was solemnly offering a Norito alone before Shinzen, a visitor knocked upon the Monko,

Yoromau, yoromau

thus calling out, there was a Sendenshi who had come. Mokusuke finished his Reihai before Shinzen, opened the door of the gate, and said,

Ya, sonata wa Kuniyoriwake no Sendenshi, naniyō attete Mokusuke ga yakata o otazuna sasuta ka

Kuniyoriwake strode straight across the threshold of the gate, passed with Mokusuke into the Zashiki, and placing the Tabakobon before them, the two sat facing one another and spoke:

Kefu tsinta no wa yo no gi de wa gozaranu. Anata wa sokonaku Hinodewake-san o hajime, shigoro no geyō ikasama naru koto o uketamawaru. Koto no jitsuhi o tugaramu tame, Kuniyoriwake senden no tochū, Ki no Kuni yori toru mono mo toriakezu hikikaeshi, koko ni tsirimashita. Anata wa Taitengyō toka o tatete orareru sōda. Kamisama ni taishi goburei de wa gozaimasenu ka

Mokusuke opened his mouth wide and burst into loud laughter:

Nani koto naramu ka to omoeba, sayō na otazune de gozaru ka. Mokusuke ga sokonaku no shinkō o hirugaeshi, Taitengyō o aratani hira ita no wa yo no gi de wa gozaranu. Laughing as he rose to his feet, washed his hands at the garden spring, rinsed his mouth, and put on his formal attire,

Now, Kuniyoriwake-sama, let us offer worship before the divine presence.

Prompting him thus, they clapped their hands twice, bowed twice, and began chanting the Amatsu Norito. Kuniyoriwake also sat properly behind Mokusuke and reverently finished chanting the Norito.

Mokusuke: 'Kuniyoriwake-sama, where do you intend to go from here?'
