§ 1

Takahime fell into a state of intense mental derangement, and being delirious with fever, she roared like a fierce tiger, her cries of Haukaudogau echoing and vibrating throughout the Okuguwai.

Enshiu and Bushiu were startled, rushing into the inner rooms and out to the front, not knowing what measures to take.

Bushiu: 'Oi Enshiu, what shall we do? Is this not a Taihen? Taihen, Taihen!'

They were running about in a state of Urota.

Mokusuke, while pulling Ohatsu by the hand, opened the gate door with a clatter and entered with an Iuiu air.

Mokusuke: 'Oi, Enshiu, Bushiu, what are you making such a noise about?'

Enshiu: 'Please Oki that voice; the Meidō is becoming more intense by the Kokukoku. I do not know if Asamayama is going to Bakuhatsu or if Takahimeyama is going to Haretsu, but a terrible Saudau is beginning. We are in a state of Shiushiyōraubai, wondering where we should go for Hinan.'

Mokusuke: 'Ahahaha, it is indeed a magnificent Meidō.'

Enshiu: 'No matter what you say, it is a fever that is rising and falling between Sanjifuhachido and Yonjifudo, so she is exhaling quite a magnificent smoke. It is not a state where one can remain by her side. Please, you must calm her down.'

Mokusuke: 'Even Dr. Omori could not do anything about this Meidō. However, I, Mokusuke, shall perform a Chinkon and try to calm her.'

With that, he and Ohatsu proceeded into the sickroom of Takahime.

Takahime was rampaging, standing and sitting while wearing a twisted headband, all the while banging on the bottom of a metal washbasin with a clanging sound. Mokusuke clasped his hands together, quietly chanting the heavenly number song, 'One, two, three, four...', and with a single shout of 'Un!', he emitted a spiritual light from his fingertips, illuminating Takahime's face. Takahime gradually returned to a state of calm, collapsed onto the floor, and lay there groaning 'Un, un' while breathing heavily with her shoulders.

Mokusuke, while stroking Takahime's shoulders, said in a low voice, 'Moshi-moshi Takahime-sama, you appear to be suffering greatly, but since everything is done by the Lord God, please do not worry at all and keep your spirits firm. The Kyōshu-sama of Kotoyowawake is also remaining perfectly calm.'

At this voice, Takahime stood up abruptly, grabbed Mokusuke by the chest of his garment with a sudden jerk, hunched her shoulders, shook her voice, and with her eyes narrowed and teeth grinding with a screeching sound, she said: 'Are you not Mokusuke? Even if Kotoyowawake says whatever he may, the fact that the precious jewel of the utmost importance was lost is a terrible blunder for the achievement of the Shinsei. All of this is because you brought Ohatsu along and forcibly dragged her out of the Ikumiya of Takahime, which is why I have met with such a fate. That I have become strange since then and fallen into such an illness is all because of you, Mokusuke. You are a great Magatsuno who obstructs the achievement of the Shinsei. You are likely the Yamata no Orochi in disguise, are you not? Come, confess and reveal the whereabouts of the jewel. If you do not, I shall not let you go, no matter where you try to flee.'

Speaker: 'If the condition is that critical, then it is all the more reason. I must see her by any means. Move aside, do not be a nuisance.'

They push and shove their way in. Looking, they see Takahime held in Mokusuke's arms, sleeping soundly.

Kuniyoriwake: 'Ah, Ohatsu-sama, Mokusuke-sama, everyone, thank you very much for your trouble. How is her condition?'

Mokusuke: 'Yes, I am troubled by the state you feared. It seems to be a mental derangement, and she is babbling nonsense, so in truth, I have been refusing visitors. But you have come. It is likely hopeless now.'

He answers powerlessly in a voice raspy with a tone of hopeless despair.

Ohatsu smiles and says,

Speaker: 'Everyone, do not worry. There must be a deep reason for this.'

At this moment, Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto appears here to visit the sick, accompanied by Kotoyorihime, Otama-no-kata, Kototeruhime, Murasakihime, and Wakahiko, takes a seat at the bedside, recites the Amatsu Norito, and chants the Heavenly Number Song to pray for recovery.
