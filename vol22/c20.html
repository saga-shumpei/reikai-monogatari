Chapter 20: 三の Soul [712]

三の魂〔712〕

§ 1

Tokiokashinokami gazes out the window, bathing his body in the fragrance of the early summer's green leaves through which the divine plan's cold wind blows. Just then, from the shade of the woods in the forest, Tamanohime comes walking back joyfully, leading Hatsuwakahime by the hand, together with two rough men. Mokusuke pulls open the window, claps his hands, and welcomes them. Takahime and Kuniyoriwake, who had been visiting this house since two or three days before, were staying here while engaging in debates on religious doctrines with Mokusuke.

Takahime: 'Mokusuke-samaya, you clapped your hands from the east window just now, but Nitsutensama is turning toward the west, you know.'

Mokusuke: 'No, it is because Nitsutensama and Guwatsutensama have come right here now.'

Takahime: 'Do not utter such things as Nitsutensum and Guwatsutensam not going to Kuniyoriwake.'

Saying this, she peers out the window and says,

Takahime: 'Ya, they have returned. Mokusuke-sama, how wonderful, you must have been worried until now, right?'

Mokusuke: 'Yes, Mokusuke was also worried a little bit.'

Making a strange face, Takahime says,

Takahime: 'You speak with unconcern using your mouth, but it seems that you are still anxious about Hatsuwakahime-sama's affairs, don't you?'

Mokusuke: 'As for Hatsuwakahime-sama's matters, I am not worried because the Kami are with them, but I was thinking whether they managed to successfully perform their important divine task or not... However, judging by those complexions, it seems they have accomplished the divine task successfully.'

Takahime: 'An important divine task... what on earth is such a thing? Please let Takahime hear about it as well.'

Smiling broadly, Mokusuke says,

Mokusuke: 'Yes, the important and secret divine task from Kotoiyoribake-no-Mikoto... was undertaken by both Tamanohime and Hatsuwakahime.'

Takahime: 'Putting aside me, the living shrine of the Hinode no Kami, to entrust such important divine tasks to children and the wives of young men like those... Kotoiyoribake lacks a good eye. Therefore, they say he has no eye for using people. What a troublesome, high-collar leader of the teaching he is!'

Mokusuke laughs happily, saying,

Mokusuke: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

Kuniyoriwake pushes open the door of the gate, greets them politely, and says,

Kuniyoriwake: 'Everyone, thank you for your hard work. Was everything settled safely?'

The two women, filling their faces with smiles without uttering a single word, lowered their waists respectfully and crept in joyfully together with the two men.

Upon seeing them, Mokusuke says,

Mokusuke: 'Hatsuwakahime-sama, Tamanohime-sama, Tanimaru-sama, Takakō-sama, thank you for your hard work.'

Tanimaru: 'I have been bestowed the name of Missionary of Sadanohiko by Kotoiyoribake-no-Mikoto. Takakō-sama has been bestowed the name of Missionary of Haruhiko, so please call us with that in mind from now on. Osechi... no, no, we accompanied Tamanohime-sama and Hatsuwakahime-sama and went on the divine task of Kamishima... no, of the Kami. Oh, it was truly a magnificent affair.'

Mokusuke: 'Be that as it may, let us offer our gratitude to the Kami and partake of the celebratory sacred sake.'

Takahime: 'Ah, that is magnificent indeed. However, what kind of divine task did you go to perform? Please let Takahime also hear the details. Now, Tamanohime-sama.'

Tamanohime: 'This matter alone cannot be spoken of for thirty-five hundred thousand years. It will probably become clear in the future somehow.'

Takahime: 'What... my, how distant... what a long-winded thing.'

Mokusuke: 'It is hard to say at which location it was settled, but actually, the adamantine wish-granting jewel and the purple precious jewel that you had once swallowed have returned to the hands of the leader of the teachings of An-Naka-Kyo [Izunome], and you went on the divine task of burying them in a certain spiritual land. The golden jewel was buried by the leader of the teachings of Kotoiyoribake himself somewhere in a spiritual land, it seems. With this, the three sacred jewels are now complete... Takahime-sama, please rejoice.'

Making a bewildered face, rolling her tongue, and opening wide her eyes, Takahime says,

Takahime: 'Hee, ke-ke-ke-ke, how magnificent.'

Having said just that, she hangs her head with a face that looks happy, sad, and displeased all at once.

Kuniyoriwake claps his hands and laughs.

Hahaha, has the living shrine of the Hinode no Kami also peacefully passed away? Truly, it is a matter of great pity. However, Takahime-sama, you must be pleased as well. By now, you should be able to give up attachment to your vice-guardian deity. From now on, single-mindedly follow the instructions of the Kyōshiyu and perform your duties in Shinkai.

Takahime: 'Ha! How ironically the Kamisama acts. Searching for the whereabouts of the jewel day and night, striving to fulfill the service of completing Shinsei, and making this Takahime—who has been active with a force of a thousand cavalry and a single rider—look utterly foolish while entrusting the vital ichirin keirin to people beyond expectation... What a strange Kamisama... No, what a strange Kyōshiyu this is. The kyō of Kyōshiyu must mean the beast radical with a king, ohohohoho.'

Sadahiko: 'This is a remark I cannot simply let pass, Takahime. What is the meaning of such a derailment? Until now, Tanimaru would have remained silent, but I am now a propagandist commanded by the Kyōshiyu. I will not let you off unless you correct your statements.'

Haruhiko: 'Just as the propagandist Sadahiko has said, it would be best for you to promptly correct your words, or so Haruhiko thinks.'

Takahime: 'Since we have already arrived at the terminal of the Takahime Railway, the station of Afun, there is no room for derailment, no way to correct my words, and no station to transfer at all, is there not? Ohohohoho.'

Incidentally, Itsukiyoriwake no Mikoto once carried out a mysterious keirin in the sacred ground of Mount Takakuma, returned to the sacred ground to participate in divine work, widely propagated the divine revelations of the divine priests of the Brocade Palace, Tamateruhiko no Mikoto and Tamateruhime no Mikoto, overseas, and cultivated numerous divine beings engaged in the conquest of Yamata no Orochi, becoming a heroic deity of the age of the gods whose name shook the realm. Moreover, Mokusuke reappeared as the original Tokiokishi no Kami, assisted the Kyōshiyu in the Yahiroden of the sacred ground, faithfully served alongside Hatsuwakahime, and came to be called the pillar stone of Izumuru Kyō. Tamanohime remained in the forest of Ikuta, bore a certain divine command to enshrine the divine spirit of Wakazakurahime no Mikoto, and served as the vanguard of the 567 Shinsei.

Wakahiko traveled all around the entire Jitentō Island and finally, by the command of Shinkai, came to serve the divine spirit together with Tamanohime. Kuniuriwake, because his elder brother Masaura went off to the land of Persia, unavoidably served at the Takeshi Shrine in the village of Utsuyama to transmit the divine teachings and devoted himself with filial piety to his father, Matsukahiko.

Takahime stayed in the sacred ground serving the Brocade Palace, but she pursued Kurohime overseas, encountered the true Hinode no Kami, realized for the first time the true nature of her own guardian deity, repented, and continued her activities at full speed. Sadahiko and Haruhiko remained under the shadow of Itsukiyoriwake no Mikoto to assist in the divine work.

因に言依別命は、一旦高熊山の霊地に神秘の経綸を遂行し、聖地に帰りて神業に参じ、錦の宮の神司玉照彦命、玉照姫命の神示を海外にまで弘布し、八岐大蛇の征服に従事する数多の神人を教養し、其名を天下に轟かした神代の英雄神である。また杢助は元の時置師神と現はれ、聖地の八尋殿に於て教主を助け、初稚姫と共に忠実に奉仕し、三五教の柱石と呼ばれる事となつた。玉能姫は生田の森に止り、或神命を帯びて稚桜姫命の神霊を祀り、五六七神政の魁を勤めた。

若彦は自転倒島全体を巡歴し、終に神界の命によりて玉能姫と共に神霊に奉仕する事となつた。国依別は兄の真浦が波斯の国へ出で行きしを以て、已むを得ず宇都山郷の武志の宮に仕へて神教を伝へ、父の松鷹彦に孝養を尽した。

高姫は聖地にあつて錦の宮に仕へつつありしが、黒姫のあとを追うて海外に渡り、真正の日の出神に出会し、初めて自己の守護神の素性を悟り、悔い改めて大車輪の活動を続けた。佐田彦、波留彦は言依別命の膝下にあつて、神業を輔佐することとなつた。
