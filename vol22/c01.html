§ 1

The Mototsumioya of Heaven and Earth, Kuniharutachinōokami, due to the ferocity of the ugly Magatsu, had no choice but to become a Hitorigami together with Toyokunihimenomikoto and hide His divine form. Here, the two pillars, Kamuizanaginōokami and Kamuizanaminōokami, who are the Miko of Ōkuniharutachinomikoto, revering the Mikoto of the Amatsuōkami, sought to create and solidify the land that was drifting like a Kurage. Receiving the Shinchiyoku, They stood upon the Ukihashi of Heaven, and with the Nuhoko of Heaven, They stirred the Doromizu drifting over the Toyoashihara no Mizuhonokuni, churning it with the sound of shiokōoro, kōoro. From the drops falling from the spear, Onokorojima was formed, upon which They descended to the Tenkyōzan, pounded and solidified the Mihashira of Heaven and the Mihashira of the Land, and circling the Mihashira of the strike from the left and right, They met and sought to restore the Nakatsukuni of Toyoashihara once again to the Mototsukuni of the Kamiyo. Together with Konohanahimenomikoto and Hinodewake, They consulted, united Their hearts, and exhausted Their strength for the completion of the Divine Land. However, the ugly Magatsumi, which had appeared from the spirit of Amatsuhiko and Enabohime, regained its place and rampaged without restraint. The eight million Kami and humans once again had their hearts twisted, heading in directions they should not, and the world once again became a state of eternal darkness.

Here, Amaterasuōmikami and Kamususanōōkami appeared as the Miko of Izanaginomikoto and preached and admonished the true path to the Amatsukami, Kunitsukami, and the eight million Kami and humans. Yet, the world became defiled day by day and month by month; the crimes of breaking ridges, filling ditches, releasing sluices, frequent sowing, inserting stakes, skinning alive, skinning backwards, and the excrement-door crimes filled Heaven and Earth. Crimes such as cutting living skin, cutting dead skin, white-leprosy, the crime of violating one's own mother, the crime of violating one's own child, the crime of a mother violating a child, the crime of a child violating a mother, the crime of violating livestock, the calamity of insects, the calamity of high-gods, the calamity of high-birds, the crime of killing livestock and performing sorcery, and many other crimes emerged. As the world became increasingly shrouded in dark clouds, unable to distinguish black from white, Susanoōōkami had no means to govern the Ashihara no Nakatsukuni and broke His heart day and night. As He wept and lamented, Kamuizanaginomikoto descended from Heaven and asked Susanoōnomikoto the reason for this. When Susanoōnomikoto replied that He took all the sins and evils of the earth upon Himself, hid the evil of the subordinate Kami or the ugly Magatsu of Yamata-no-Orochi, and that it was because the Kototama and heart-conduct of His own pillar were evil, Izanaginōokami became enraged.

Izanaginōokami: 'Since thou hast no qualification to rule the ocean plains, go unto the land of thy mother.'

As He solemnly declared this, Susanoōnomikoto ascended to Takamagahara to report the details of the matter to His elder sister, the Ōkami. At this time, the Kami who guarded the mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees were startled and shaken, and the world became increasingly dark. The elder sister Ōkami declared that Her younger brother had a dark heart. Here, placing the Ama-no-Yasukawa in between, They performed Misogi in the Ama-no-Manai, and by proving the Itsu-no-Mitama and Mizu-no-Mitama, the elder sister Ōkami declared and distinguished that She was the spirit of Hensei-danshi and Her younger brother was the spirit of Hensei-joshi.

Before the Daikōzui, he had commenced the divine work with Jerusalem as the center, but here he appeared as Kunitokotachinomikoto's bunrei, Kunitakehiko, descended to Jitentōtō, and together with the divine Susanōnōokami, began to build the foundation of the Miroku Shinsei. From then on, Jitentōtō was firmly established as the site for the divine work of world unification.

The Nyoi Hōju that emerged from the essence of Kuninotokotama, along with the Golden Jewel and the Purple Jewel, are regarded as the three sacred treasures in the divine world and are considered the most precious objects. These three jewels are called the Zuirei. The land where these jewels are enshrined is endowed with the divine constitution and Kannagara to unify the Toyoashihara no Mizuhonokuni.

Here, Kunitokotachinomikoto made Tenkyōzan his entrance and exit, and Toyokunihimegami made Naruto her entrance and exit, taking charge of the governance of the earth, remaining hidden from the world for a long time, and waiting for the time of the fulfillment of the Miroku Shinsei. Susanōnomikoto hid his bunrei, Kototamawakemikoto, in the earth and had him participate in the divine work as Sukunahikonanomikoto, but now he has appeared again as Kotoiyorawakemikoto to protect the three sacred treasures.

By the divine work of Kotoiyorawakemikoto, the three sacred treasures were enshrined in the Nishiki no Miya, and just as he was about to commence the fulfillment of the Shinsei, it was decreed by the command of Kunitokotachinomikoto and Toyokunihimegami that since the time was not yet ripe, he should wait for the spring when the plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds open all at once to reveal the three sacred treasures to the world; thus, Kotoiyorawakemikoto secretly received the divine command and hid them deep within a certain location on Jitentōtō, and it is the origin of this divine work that I intend to dictate in this volume. As they are divine treasures of unfathomable transformation, being tangible yet intangible, intangible yet tangible, voiceless yet vocal, and vocal yet voiceless, it is natural that they cannot be seen by ordinary eyes.

§ 3

Bathed in the moonlight of a winter night where the cold wind rages, Kurohime, whose heart is clouded by dark shadows that never clear, had been secretly entrusted by Kotoyoriwakenomikoto to store away the golden jewel. Wishing to check its whereabouts, she rose at the hour of the ox and walked alone, dejected, to the foot of a single pine tree on Yotsuoyama. She quietly opened the lid of the Ishibitsu she had hidden there, but unexpectedly fell over with a thud. Saddened by the empty shell of her responsibility—the jewel being gone—she rose again, wondering what she should do, and remained lost in thought, when suddenly, startled by the sound of footsteps, she regained her composure and stood up.

She sought the shade of the trees and began to return.

Two men who had been lurking approached the root of the pine tree.

Kō: 'Ah, I thought Kurohime-sama's behavior was suspicious, so I followed her. It seems likely that Kurohime has been hiding the golden jewel that Mukadehime had occupied, having gained the trust of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto.'

Otsu: 'But did Kurohime-sama not fall on her backside in surprise as soon as she opened the lid? Perhaps it has been lost. If that is the case, Kurohime is completely useless.'

Kō: 'She must have fallen on her backside because she was startled by the brilliance of the jewel; that is certain. Since no one else knows she hid it in a place like this, no one could have discovered it. Even Murasakihime-sama herself does not know of it...'

Otsu: 'No, Kurohime's figure is truly suspicious; she seems to be fading away. Shall we not try to search for her quietly? If we dawdle, her figure will vanish completely.'

Kō: 'Come, let us go quickly. Her figure has already disappeared, has it not?'

They looked around the area, peering here and there. Suddenly, there was the sound of someone plunging into the pond below with a splash. The two were startled and rushed to the edge of the pond, but there was no sign of anyone; only ripples were swaying on the water's surface, drawing circles. Even the reflection of the moon was shattered, overlapping and moving in long shapes like skewered dumplings.

Kō: 'Ah, there is a pair of footwear left here. This must certainly be Kurohime-sama's. Oh, this is terrible! Kārinsu, you return quickly, report to Kotoyoriwake-sama, and lead a large group of believers to send out a rescue party. I will remain here to guard the place until then.'

Kārinsu: 'Do not say such foolish things. While we dawdle, will it not be all over?'

No sooner had he said this than Kārinsu stripped naked and plunged into the pond, which was beginning to freeze over with thin ice. He dived into the water, grabbed Kurohime, and finally managed to pull her up to safety. Kurohime was already breathing her last.
