§ 1

With frost upon her Kashira, having passed half of this life, an old woman with a wrinkled face is on a Hitoritabi. The sound of insects gathering in the Kareno is heard faintly like a Danmatsuma. Even the rustling of the wind feels somehow lonely in the Aranohara, as she proceeds, leaning on her staff.

In the Susukihara by the roadside, seven or eight Sekitafu with missing heads are buried, covered in moss and so aged that the characters are barely legible. Beside them stands an Ishijizau with its head removed, holding a jewel in its Hidarite, while its Migite has the Oyayubi and Nakayubi joined in a circle, with the Hitosashiyubi pointing sharply toward the sky. It appears that even the Jizau, thinking that Shiyujiyō without Kane are difficult to save, is so Shifunenbuka that even after having its head severed, it cannot let go of the desire for money, thus exposing its Shifuchiyakushin here as well.

The old woman gazes intently and repeatedly at the jewel of the Jizau.

Takahime: 'Hey, who are you? Is that jewel not the Niyoihotsushiyu? How did you steal this Shinpō that I, Takahime, have kept in Hizau? It must be divine retribution that your head is in this state. Come, hand it over to me at once.'

She struggles to forcibly snatch it from the hand of the Ishijizau. From behind the Sekitafu, making the ears of miscanthus rustle, two old women and three men appear, calling out in weak voices.

Voices: 'Takahime, Takahime.'

Takahime is startled by these voices and casts her eyes toward the reed field; to her surprise, it is a group of five: Kurohime, Takayorihime, Ryūkokuwake, Teristan, and Karlins.

Takahime: 'Hey, Kurohime-sama, what has become of the golden jewel? I instructed you five to divide the work, search the whole world, receive that jewel, and perform the Kototama talk, so why are you dawdling in a place like this?'

Kurohime: 'I was treated unreasonably by you, and it became so unbearable that we all five threw ourselves into the sea of Tango and died. To clear this grudge, we have possessed you to take your life. Now, the five of us will gather, pull out your head, pull out your hands, pull out your feet, and torture you to death; prepare yourself.'

So saying, she reveals a pale face, hangs both hands down in front from the chest like a mantis, and drifts toward Takahime from the front, back, left, and right, swaying with the wind.

Takahime: 'Where do you think this place is? Are you still wandering after having gone to the Meido? Well, there is no help for the dead, so I shall forgive you now; you had better attain Buddhahood quickly.'
