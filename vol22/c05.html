Chapter 5: 壇の Bay [697]

壇の浦〔697〕

§ 1

The sacred ground, with the Nishiki-no-Miya palace as its background—where the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu and the purple jewel were newly enshrined—gained a sense of vitality, and the Shinjin were filled with expressions of Kietsu, as the Shintoku increased day by day. Suddenly, the Ōgon jewel kept by Kurohime was stolen by someone, and Kurohime, bearing the responsibility, left the sacred ground secretly at night with the five, Takayorihime, Tatsukuniwake, Te, and Ka, each setting out to search for the whereabouts of the jewel; this was immediately Kenden among the Shinjin, and once again, a sense of Fuan drove them, and a somewhat lonely feeling drifted through the sky of the sacred ground. Starting with the Kanbu of the Ananai-kyō, the Shinto gathered their heads here and there, and the talk was entirely about this matter. Takahime gathered the propagandists and believers in the Yahiro-dono, which was attached to the high tower near the Nishiki-no-Miya, and opened a lecture meeting to give them a word of caution.

Everyone gathered until there was no room to stand, competing to be first, wondering what the eloquent Takahime would say regarding this sudden incident. Takahime immediately stood up on the platform, and while lifting her eyes with a hint of anger, she began to speak earnestly.

Takahime, while glaring at the entire assembly, said: 'Minasama, thank you very much for attending today. Regarding the sudden occurrence of a major event for the Ananai-kyō, it goes without saying that we must be careful in the future, and we must consider what should be done about this situation. It is a grave problem for the sake of the Lord God, for the sake of the country, and for the sake of all humanity. That is to say, as you know, due to the vast and boundless Shintoku, the one jewel of the Kunitsukuni, the Kongaufue treasure jewel, which I once swallowed into my belly for the sake of the divine administration of the spirit world, was enshrined again in this palace as a divine treasure through the mediation of Ohatsu-sama, upon whom the spirit of Konohanahime-sama descends. The other purple jewel, along with the repentance and submission of Takayorihime, also became a treasure of the Nishiki-no-Miya, and together with the Ōgon Niyoihotsushiyu brought from Aokumoyama earlier, the spiritual power and the physical form were complete, and just as we were rejoicing that the opportunity for the realization of the divine government had undoubtedly arrived, due to the carelessness of Kurohime, the important Ōgon jewel was lost, which is regrettable beyond words. Even though such a major incident has occurred, are you all not concerned at all? This rumor should have reached your ears many times already. Yet, the fact that no one has come to me until today to even say that a festering nose has burst is such cold-heartedness. Surely the Lord God must be satisfied with your sincerity.'

§ 2

Sutebachi said this in a spiteful tone, lashing out at everyone. Kuniyoriwake appeared upon the dais where Takahime stood,

Kuniyoriwake: 'I ask you, Takahime-sama. We have been holding repeated discussions among the executive members regarding this matter, but as we do not know the whereabouts of Kurohime-sama, which is the most essential point, we cannot even investigate what should be done. I have heard that you, in a despotic and arbitrary manner, have banished Kurohime and three others; by whose permission did you do such a thing? It seems that you should have consulted with us, the executive members, at least once. Is there not something lurking behind the scenes regarding this? I pray you, here at this assembly, provide us with a detailed report to clear our suspicions.'

Takahime: 'Kuniyoriwake-sama, be silent. How could you, a pilgrim-turned-propagandist, possibly understand the affairs of the Divine World? Everything is part of the Divine Plan, so as the saying goes, the stake that sticks out gets hammered down; you had best be a little more prudent.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'This is outrageous. How can I remain silent at this? Furthermore, where do you derive the authority to dispose of Kurohime and the others as you please? Without consulting the divine will of Tamateruhiko-sama and Tamateruhime-sama, and ignoring the opinions of the Kyōshu as well, is it acceptable for you to act so selfishly? If you are capable of such things, then the two Guji, the Kyōshu, and the executive members are all unnecessary, are they not?'

Takahime: 'I do not listen to such things said by mere physical bodies. I have spoken according to the instructions of the living shrine of the Hinodewake. Do you think the Divine Plan can be understood through wisdom or learning?'

Kuniyoriwake: 'You speak of the Divine World at every turn, but you certainly possess a very convenient place to hide, do you not? If there was no need to consult us, why did you invite us?'

From amidst the assembly,

Voice: 'Kuniyoriwake-sama, we are counting on you. Stand firm, stand firm.'

someone jeered.

Takahime: 'I have by no means banished Kurohime-sama. I explained the principles to her and clarified where the responsibility lies. Thereupon, Kurohime-sama voluntarily tucked up her skirts and went on a quest for the jewel. This is not the time for you all to be looking around so aimlessly. Searching for three or five people in this vast world is like trying to find a pearl dropped into the great ocean. You, who always swear to dedicate your lives and everything else for the sake of the Path, why can you not practice silent execution without this Takahime having to say it? The Gods always say that when the time of crisis comes, there are only those who run away. Truly, the words of the Gods do not differ by even a hair's breadth. Now, everyone, what will you do? It is certainly not only Kurohime-sama's responsibility. Kuniyoriwake-sama, you still do not understand the affairs of the Divine World at all. Return to your seat at once.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'I understand that you are the guardian of the Kongaufue jewel, but are you sure about this? You should not speak of other matters too much. You ought to think a little about the saying that today's fault is another's, and tomorrow's fault is one's own.'

Takahime: 'What are you prattling on about? As long as I, Takahime, am guarding it, I will not let anyone, no matter how great, touch it with even a single finger. If by any chance the jewel were to be lost, you would never see me again.'

She squared her shoulders, thrust her chin forward slightly, and spoke with a hateful air. From the midst of the assembly,

Voice: 'Since you would swallow it if it were different, it is surely safe. Perhaps you have even swallowed the Ōgon jewel. Kuniyoriwake-sama, I leave it to you.'

came the heckling. Takahime raised her voice even more sharply,

Takahime: 'In this situation where one is worth a thousand, who is the thoughtless person heckling like a theater-goer or a parliament of the Nijitsuseiki? Hiding your face and disguising your voice, how cowardly and base. Why do you not appear before Takahime and speak your opinion openly? Is that not cowardly? For a mere woman, what is that attitude for a grown man?'

she shouted. The entire assembly clapped their hands,

Voice: 'Wah! Wah!'

and laughed and clamored.

Takahime: 'Everyone, what do you think this seat is? Gathering in such a sacred Yahirodono, is this not extremely insolent? If you do not like what I, Takahime, say, I shall have you leave at once. Even if there are many of you like dried sardines, not one of you is of any use. How pathetic the people are.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'Everyone, those who do not like the words of Takahime-sama, please leave as requested.'

From the midst of the crowd,

Voice: 'If it is as Kuniyoriwake-sama says, that those who do not like it should leave, then there will be no one left but Takahime herself. Is that acceptable?'

they shouted. Takahime became furious,

Takahime: 'If you are content to have your account in the divine world severed, then you had better leave at once.'

From the midst of the crowd,

Voice: 'Even if you sever our account, it is fine as long as it is not severed by the divine world.'

someone shouted. The hall was instantly in an uproar, like a boiling cauldron, or like a hornet's nest that had been broken open.

At this time, Kototamayoriwake-no-Mikoto appeared calmly on the platform together with Wakahiko, Murasakihime, and Tamajiwake.

§ 4

Ichidō clapped their hands and welcomed Kotoyoriwakenomikoto.

The many Shinjiya, who had been on the verge of Sanran, sat down once again, and with Katazu, they waited to see how the arrows of the Ronsen that the Hanagata Yakushiya Kotoyoriwakenomikoto would aim at Takahime would turn out.

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto faced the Manza, and with a gentle voice, he asked the Manza with a most Heisei Taido and a Bishō:

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Minasama, I hear you have gathered here today at the invitation of Takahime-sama, but was there any coherent Ohanashi to be had?'

From the Zachiu, one man stood up abruptly and said:

Man: 'Futokueuriyō, I do not understand what is what. Kurohime-sama is complaining and blowing heat at all of us, saying that her jewel has been stolen, and we cannot stand it.'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'If that is all it is, is it the Jiken of the loss of the Ōgon jewel? You need not have the slightest Goshinpai. Since everything is the Keirin of the Lord God, there is no inconvenience for anyone before the Lord God, so please be at ease.'

Takahime pursed her lips and said:

Takahime: 'Hey, hey, Kyōshu-sama, what are you saying? You have lost the true, pure jewel that turns the three thousand worlds into crystal, and yet, as the person in charge, how can you say such carefree and irresponsible things? That is why people say you are a henjō-joshi, a stray, good-for-nothing. Please have at least a little sense of responsibility.'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Is this not the Nishiki-no-Miya protected by the Ōkami who lets the world play freely, and furthermore, is it not protected by the Shinjin Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, and is this land not the center point of the Jitentōshima, the palace of the Takamagahara of the earth? Everything is the Keirin of the Shinkai. There should be no need for Goshinpai. If you blame Kurohime-sama too much, you too will find yourself in trouble.'

Takahime: 'Eh, you weak-willed Kotoyoriwake, I have lost all patience with you. From now on, I intend to carry this Takamagahara on my back. You also have a responsibility. Step down from the seat of Kyōshu until the Ōgon jewel is back in our hands. It is Hinodewake who commands you through the mouth of Takahime.'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'I am not one who clings to the position of Kyōshu. However, this sacred ground is not a place that will be settled by you becoming the Kyōshu. Before long, you too, like Kurohime-sama, will have to go in search of the jewel.'

Now everyone, I am giving your eyes a feast, so do not let your hearts fall into famine.'

『そんな事仰有るまでもなく、此高姫が一つよりない首を十でも二十でも進上致しますワイな。そんな間抜と思つて御座るのですか。チツト黒姫とは品物が違ひます。あんまり見違ひして下さいますな。お前さまは教主と云つても、ホンの看板も同然、斯んな所へ出て来る場合ぢやありませぬ。スツ込んで居なさい、空気抜けさま』

『あなたの保管して居られる玉を一寸此処で皆さまに拝ましてあげて貰ひたい。斯う云ふ人心不安な時は噂は噂を生み、金剛不壊の玉も、紫の玉も紛失したげな……と大変な評判が立つて居ますから………』

『エー人間と云ふ者は仕方のないものだナ。天眼通でチヤンと見えて居る、決して紛失なんかして居ませぬ。直にお目に掛けます。折角妾が保管して置いた秘密場所を見せた以上は又場所を替へねばならぬ。どんな奴が信者に化けて這入り込んで居るか分つたものぢやない。妾は黒姫の様に松の木の根元へ隠し、毎晩々々、降つても照つてもお百度参りをして終局に人に嗅ぎつけられる様な拙劣な事はやりませぬワイなア、ヘン』

と稍軽侮の色を大勢の前に曝しながら、

『皆さま玉を拝ましてあげる。目が潰れぬ様にシツカリとしなされ』

と云ひながら、八尋殿の畳を一枚剥り、中より恭しく桐の箱を取り出し、

『皆さま如何です。斯う云ふ近い所に隠してあつても分りますまいがな。それだから灯台下は真暗がりと云ふのですよ。チツト身魂を研きなされ。言依別様、お前さまの命令した所とは違ひませうがな。お前さまの命令通り行つて居らうものなら、黒姫の様にサツパリな目に遇うて居るのぢや。サア蓋を開けて検めて御覧』

『どうぞ貴女開けて下さい』

『さうだらう　さうだらう。此玉は実地誠の御神徳がないと、何程教主でも、身魂の曇りが現はれて、恥しうて、面を向ける事も出来ませぬワイ。サア皆さま、目のお正月を為してあげるから、心の饑饉を起してはなりませぬぞや』

§ 6

With a look of Tokuige, he said, 'Now, here is the Ontakara of the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu, and one is the purple Mitama. If your Mitama is not polished, people may call it Giyokusekikondō and see it only as an Ishikure. They say that even a horse that can run Senri will end up as a Doba if it does not find a Hakurau. Everyone, fix your eyes firmly, polish your Mitama, and Goran... nay, Haikuwan it.'

He pressed his lips into a line, and with an Ōhei Tsuratsuki, he held the Tamabako above his head Nisankuwai, and as soon as he quietly removed the Ōhi and opened the lid of the Karaudo, his Ganshiyoku suddenly turned to a Sauhakushiyoku, he curled his tongue, rounded his eyes like an owl, hunched his shoulders high and thin, buried his neck halfway into his shoulders, and stood there in Mugon.

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Takahime-sama, a magnificent Reikuō must be shining, must it not?'

Kuniyoriwake stepped forward briskly, peered into the Tamabako, and said, 'Oh, what is this? Everyone, contrary to the thought that it was a jewel, it has turned into a stone before we knew it. Whose responsibility is this? We may be about to see a magic trick where many heads must be surrendered for a single neck. Nevertheless, a terrible thing has occurred.'

Everyone was left dumbfounded and astonished.

Murasakihime: 'Moshi Takahime-sama, what is the reason for this?'

Takahime: 'There is no reason. Even if it looks like a stone like this, it is still the genuine Niyoihotsushiyu. It is because the Mitama of all you people are bad that the Hōju-sama has turned into a stone. Since the Kami are like a mirror, and because your hearts are just like stones and tiles, it changes according to your will. That is why it is called Niyoihotsushiyu. ...Now, now, Niyoihotsushiyu-sama, you must be very mortified. At least for two or three days or a week, everyone, go and perform water purification. If you do that, you will be able to worship the true divine body of the Hōju.'

From among the crowd, someone said, 'Oi, Takahime-sama, no matter how much we polish it or look at it again, isn't a stone still just a stone? You cannot say that there is not even one person or half a person among us whose soul is polished. Besides, there is no one here who says it is a jewel. It was taken from you because your arrogance is too strong. If that is not the case, you are likely planning a rebellion, intending to re-establish the Uranai religion, having conspired with Kurohime, letting Kurohime take the golden jewel and return to Fusa-no-miyako first, while you do something with the remaining two jewels.'

と得意気に、

『サア此処に金剛不壊の如意宝珠の御宝、一つは紫の御玉、身魂が研けて居らぬと、玉石混同と云つて、石塊に見える人もありますよ。千里の馬も伯楽を得ざれば駑馬で終るとやら、皆さま、シツカリ眼を据ゑ、身魂を光らして御覧……否拝観なされ』

と口を一の字に結び、横柄な面付しながら、二三回玉箱を頭上に捧げ、静かに被覆を外し唐櫃の蓋を開けるや否や、顔色サツと蒼白色に変じ、舌を捲き、目を梟の如く円くし、肩を細く高くこぢあげ、首を半分ばかり肩に埋め、無言の儘立つて居る。

言依別命『高姫さま、立派な霊光が輝き給ふでせうなア』

国依別はツカツカと進み寄り、玉箱の中を覗いて見て、

『ヤアこりや何だ。皆さま、玉と思ひの外、何時の間にか石に変つて居りますよ。これは誰の責任でせう。一つよりない首を沢山に渡さねばならぬ手品が見られようかも知れませぬ。とは云ふものの大変な事が出来致しました』

一同はアフンとして呆れ返るばかりであつた。紫姫は、

『モシ高姫さま、こりや又何うした訳ですか』

『何うでもありませぬ。斯うして石に見えてもヤツパリ正真正銘の如意宝珠、お前さま達一同の身魂が悪いから、宝珠様が石に化けちやつたのだ。神さまは鏡も同様だから、皆さまの心が石瓦同然だから、皆さまの意の如く変化遊ばすのだ。それで如意宝珠と申します。……コレコレ如意宝珠様、さぞあなたは御無念でせう。せめて二三日か一週間、皆さま水行をしてお出でなされ。さうしたら本当の宝珠の御神体が拝めますよ』

大勢の中より、

『オイ、高姫さま、何程研いても、見直しても、石はヤツパリ石ぢやないか。此中に一人や半分、魂の研けた者がないとはいへまい。それに誰も玉ぢやと言ふ者がないぢやないか。お前さまはあんまり慢心が強いから奪られたのだよ。さうでなくばウラナイ教を再設する積りで、黒姫と申合せ、黒姫に黄金の玉を持つてフサの都へ先へ帰らせ、自分は残りの二つの玉を何々して謀叛を企むのだらう』

§ 7

Takahime became furious and shouted:

Takahime: 'To the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake of the one true Yamatodamashihi, what kind of things are you saying? Come out here now. I shall settle the Kokubiyaku for you.'

The Ōzei shouted all at once:

The Ōzei: 'The Uranai-Kyō, the Saisetsu of the Uranai-Kyō... the Chiyōhonnin of the Giyakuten-Riyokau to the path of evil...'

They shouted this in Kuchiguchi. Takahime became like Retsukuwa and stomped her feet on the Danjiyō, saying:

Takahime: 'Ah, Zannen, Zannen! I feel as though I want to cut open this belly and show it to you all.'

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto: 'Takahime-sama, everything must be according to the Gotsugafu of the Shinkai. First, please Goanshin. Goichidō-sama, I firmly believe that there is some Gotsugafu of the Shinkai in this. Please calm yourselves. I shall take the Sekinin...'

He soothed them gently. The Ichidō, having no choice but to accept the greeting of the Kyōshiyu, muttered to themselves, bowed to the Nishiki-no-Miya, and returned to their respective homes.

Takahime puffed up her face, tucked the stone jewel box under her arm, and while exposing a countenance like a Yasha to the cold wind, ran straight back to her own residence. On the branch of an Enoki tree nearby, two or three crows swayed in the cold wind, bobbing up and down on the tips of the branches, and crying out 'Ahau, Ahau' as they hovered in the air.
