§ 1

To the west Maruyama, to the east Kokumo, bounded by mountain and river, hard by the lined pines, giant trees of cypress, pine, cedar, and oak towering high to seal the skies—in their shade stands a shōshiya structure of round pillars with a bamboo roof; blue, white, and red garden stones are arranged harmoniously without apparent design, and deep within the paved garden a faint voice of conversation is heard.

Unawakingly hearing it, Tomohiko, as if drawn by some invisible thread, slips unwittingly through the gate and stands in no time at the doorway; this place is the residence of Takahime, and deep inside is the faint voice of someone, and though he knows not whose guest it might be, come what may, he will knock on the door, peer into the master's state, exactly so, exactly so, he murmurs to himself, suddenly rapping upon the front door:

Tomohiko of the Way of Teachings, to Takahime who has returned after a long while to the mansion, desiring to express his respect and offer his greetings without losing a moment, has come seeking you; if you have no objection, pray open the front door quickly. When he calls thus, Yasukō speaks from within: Even as it is, Tomohiko, because you are of such an obstinate and bad disposition, you are not to Takahime-sama's liking; just a moment ago Kunisama and Akihiko-sama came and babbled this and that, and perceiving the situation to be unsettled, they tucked up their skirts and departed.

If you are a man of worth, pray reflect a little upon things; in the inner room Takahime, Takayamahiko, and Kurohime have placed Natsuhiko and Tsunehiko before them and are holding a secret talk. Secrets are secret to the end—that is Takahime-sama's favorite saying; today the wind blows foul, so your departure is likely your gain; rather than returning with your manhood diminished, it would be better to become intimate with the pure-souled Princess of Thêrês, and after comprehending the precious words of the gods, amass the materials for a quarrel, leave this place, start afresh, and make a grand return.

For a man of seven feet to be crushed by Takahime or Kurohime-sama and foam at the mouth is an unsightly thing; you are my favorite person, a beloved fellow with a red nose, whom Katsukō-sama used to call the reincarnation of Konohanahime; a lotus blossoming all at once, this place is the lotus stage of the sacred land, and at its foot is the divine mansion; whether true or false, possessed by a lineage mitama, there dwells the Hinodewake God; the Otohime of the Dragon Palace Sea also makes Kurohime-sama her organ, and even the tengu mitama draws close, forming a couple with Takayamahiko, the three making a magnificent foundation of the world with settled faces, yet the red-nosed tengu has come, nose colliding with nose, and should another trouble arise,

How could Yasukō-sama and Katsukō-sama remain by your side? Even Yasukō-sama, a hero who fears neither earthquakes, thunder, nor rain of fire, cannot endure Takahime's breath; please return, thinking you are saving a single man's life; at the crucial climax, in the midst of secret talks, if she hears you have come, a sudden storm will rise, pillars will fall, the roof will peel off, a battlefield of extreme peril—ah, Kaminagara kamigami-maishima-shite, holding the white jewel, the savior of the land of Jeanne, here a useless propagandist, above the gods there are yet higher gods.

Do not get angry and scold me, thinking my mouth is foul, for Takahime was saying even today: It is not I who say it, but the living palace mitama of the Hinodewake God that possesses and expounds it; even amidst such matters, should it reach Takahime's ears, it will be a catastrophe, with upheaval between heaven and earth right before your eyes, so hurry back and go! When urged thus, Tomohiko breathes out through his nose with a humph: Takahime, that witless old hag, stamping her feet with heroic vigor, no matter how powerful she may be, I am the Tomohiko of Brahmanism, the man proclaimed throughout the world, so what is there to fear in the likes of Takahime? Could one or ten women be feared and yet one remain in this world, you coward! saying thus while trying with all his might to push open and enter the front door, [Voice from within]: In this situation of a thousand riders against one, if the likes of Tomohiko are let in, how could I fulfill my duty as a gatekeeper? Hearing Takahime's nagging later is more than I can bear; Tomohiko, do you understand the reason of things so little? Come back again tomorrow morning when the rough waves have calmed down; at that very time I will gladly attach 〓__GT_45980__〓__GT_45988__ and come out to the gate to welcome you, please, I beg of you! when he lets out a weeping voice, Tomohiko stops in his tracks, feeling that to make mischief like raising waves on flat land is inexcusable, softens his heart, and changes his tone: What you say is also reasonable...
