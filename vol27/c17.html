Chapter 17: 沼の女 Kami [799]

沼の女神〔799〕

§ 1

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto and Kuniyoriwake departed for Takasagojima, Wakahiko left for Onokorojima, and Terukohime and Kiyokohime followed after Kotoyoriwake; after they had left, Kiyohiko and Teruhiko were left behind on this remote island together with their father Tsunekusu, and they felt a loneliness as if they had been banished to a distant isle. From this time onward, the association of the father and the three children grew more and more intimate, the proper order between father, son, and brothers was well maintained, and they became increasingly the object of profound reverence for the numerous natives.

On this island there was an extremely vast marsh called Riukiunuma, densely overgrown with rushes. One night, in a dream of Kiyohiko...

The two, Kiyokohime and Terukohime, appeared on the opposite shore of the marsh, stretched out their white and slender hands toward Kiyohiko, and sang and danced thus:

Kiyokohime and Terukohime: 'If you are going to Riukiu, put on your waraji; Riukiu is a field of stones, a field of small stones.'

Having dreamed this and awakened, Kiyohiko turned to the native attendants Emu and Semu and asked:

Kiyohiko: 'Is there in this island a vast and boundless marsh filled with pure springs, called Riukiunuma?'

The two, Emu and Semu, nodded affirmatively without a moment's hesitation:

Emu: 'It exists, it exists; there is indeed a splendid marsh where rushes grow densely around the borders, it is comparatively shallow, and unlike other marshes, its bottom is a pleasant one paved as if with small pebbles. In its center there is a splendid rock made of coral, and a large hole is open in that rock. If you enter that hole, the interior is a spacious chamber of a thousand mats, and sometimes splendid beauties appear from that hole, two of them, unfurling golden fans and dancing and whirling about.'

Semu: 'The people of this village only hear of it in legends, and no one has ever approached it out of fear.'

Kiyohiko: 'If you know of it, can you guide me there?'

Emu: 'I will guide you, but it would be a terrible thing if you were to carelessly step right into the marsh.'

Kiyohiko: 'Teruhiko, won't you go too? Beauties not a whit different from Kiyokohime and Terukohime are waiting for us two brothers with their fans unfurled.'

Teruhiko: 'Brother, isn't that just a dream? Because your mind is too taken up with Kiyo-sama and Tero-sama, you saw such a dream. It is bound to be the guise of a giant serpent. As for me, well, I think I'll pass.'

Kiyohiko: 'My, how faint-hearted. At any rate, why not go for the sake of the experience? It is not as though we have any other urgent business. Isn't everything a matter of experience? If you intend to become the supreme ruler of this island in the future, it will be necessary to explore every nook and cranny. ...Father, what do you think? We two brothers wish to go on an exploration once, taking Emu and Semu as our guides...'

Tsunekusu: 'No matter what is said, this is the secret realm of the world. It is a trouble, but I would like you to investigate it once. ...Both Emu and Semu, it is a trouble for you, but please act as guides for the two of them.'

The two, Emu and Semu, consented without a moment's hesitation. Thereupon the four, together with Tsunekusu, chanted the Amatsu Norito, finished praying for success, and equipped lightly with waraji and kyahan, carrying a single staff each, and wearing on their heads hats woven flat from basho leaves, they parted the grass beneath a cloudless blue sky and arrived at the edge of Riukiunuma. The distance was almost equivalent to ten ri of the present day. By the time they reached the lake shore, the sun had already sunk behind the peak of Mount Sex, and the shadows of the mountain were beginning to cover the lake surface.

Kiyohiko stood on the bank of the marsh, gazed out over the lake surface, and sang thus:

Kiyohiko: 'Purified by the teachings of the gods, Kiyohiko who has polished his soul, His body and soul shine brightly in all directions, Teruhiko, the missionary of propagation. In the marsh of Riukiu, forever Pets in tranquility, her heart pure, Kiyokohime, Terukohime whose body and soul shine. A pure affinity of Kiyo and Kiyo, A bright affinity of Tero and Tero. Guided by Emu and Semu, Yearning and longing to meet you, I have come forth, a gentle man. The sun hides behind Mount Sex, And the raven-feathered night draws near. Like the pure, pure morning sun, Like the bright, bright heavenly sun, Shining and resplendent, Truly a man and a man of beauty, Seeking the woman from within a dream, Feeling as though dreaming within a dream,'

Kiyohiko: 'The man who has come seeking you even unto this place, O Goddess of the swamp, if you have a heart, pray understand the poignant yearnings of this man. Although it is said to be within a dream, did you not call upon me with a pure heart? Striking bare your white, tender, snow-like young breast, and beating upon it yearningly, with true armlets and jewelled armlets, intertwining our arms together, with long thighs, joining water and fire, shall we not become the guardian deities of this island? O Kiyokohime in the dream, O Princess Teruko, you who have come from afar, O Kiyohiko who has come seeking, and the true heart of Teruko— do not treat it as vain, O Master of the swamp.'

Thus he sang.

Teruhiko, as though unable to wait for the end of Kiyohiko's song,

Teruhiko: 'Hidden, hidden has the sun disk become upon the summit of Mount Seeks, in the cool shadow enfolding the swamp, our hearts have also grown cool. Our hearts shine brightly, our mitama are pure; at the bottom of the swamp where pebbles are aligned, the leaping of small fish is clearly visible. It is not only the small fish that dance; Teruhiko's heart leaps up with courage, and unawares my hands and feet begin to dance. Shine, shine brightly, light of the heart, O Kiyohiko aniki, a portion of the divine soul that has taken up residence within a pure mitama! That we two brothers have come to visit the two sisters is for no other reason than this: from a desire to meet the woman of the swamp whom aniki saw in his dream last night, we set forth from the wooden Marudono, sent off by Emu and Semu, and came parting the grassy fields. Understanding a man's heart, appear quickly in your true form! Upon the coral reef floating in the swamp, if we listen to the words of Emu and Semu, we hear that maiden-like maidens with golden fans unfolded wide always appear. Entrusted with the sovereignty of the land of Ryukyu, we two have appeared here, and receiving upon our bodies the light of the sun and moon, blending the hearts of two and two, as husband and wife islands of the north and south, to bind a pledge for a thousand ages, we are the men who have come yearning for you. Do not return this with cruelty, O Master of the swamp.'

So singing to a close, the four of them advance through the shallow swamp—which seemed paved with beautiful sand—startling small fish, making unseasonable waves as they splash forward.

Far in the distance, the shadow of a coral reef floated darkly; the sun finally sank below the horizon, and the curtain of darkness gradually descended. A cool breeze caressed the faces of the party, and the depth of the water now reached up to their thighs. Suddenly the island brightened with a gentle glow. The four of them, their hearts somehow emboldened, advanced with the illumination as their guide.

Suddenly an eight-fathomed crocodile appeared; from here the depth of the water suddenly increased, making it utterly impossible to advance any further. Just as they stood bewildered in hesitation, the eight-fathomed crocodile stretched itself out before them like a bridge. Crocodiles, numbering not even they knew how many, formed a single-file battle line as if building a long bridge with the coral reef as their base, and showed a clear intention as though to say to the four men: cross over this very way...

Kiyohiko and the other three, while reciting divine words, stepped falteringly over the backs of the crocodiles, and at last safely reached the brightly glowing coral reef.

When Kiyohiko looked back, the forms of the eight-fathomed crocodiles that had appeared until now had vanished like foam, and afterwards drifted peacefully like fish scales upon the water.

Kiyohiko, to celebrate their safe arrival at the coral reef, with a heart somehow light and eager, sang and danced once again.

Kiyohiko: 'This is the central point of Ryukyu, Is it the Takasago Island or the Chin Island, concealed in the everlasting bedrock of the beloved wife within a dream? The jewel-dwelling place of the Goddess of Chin, if you are going to Ryukyu, go wearing your straw sandals. Ryukyu is a stony plain, a pebble plain; the two nice fellows who sang and made you hear— where are you hiding your bodies now? We are men who have come seeking you from afar; it is an insult not to come out to greet us. Am I not also a man of some account? Are you incarnations of dragons, or the end of heavenly maidens, or merely illusions of Kiyoko and Teruko? The truth or falsehood is something our eyes, bewildered by love, cannot clearly discern. The form of you who danced in your dream, your white skin and white thighs, and the feeling when you displayed your thick breasts dangling down— that I can never forget. A man lost in the dark paths of love, that brothers in the swamps of Ryukyu should become the captives of love— if Kiyohiko, who never even dreamed of this, understands your crimson heart, then break the dream and let reality appear, O Princess Kiyoko and Princess Teruko, appear in your forms quickly! Because I want to meet you, because I want to see your faces, because I desire to be with you for a thousand and eight thousand ages, I raised my voice in words before my father, and even invited my younger brother along— coming all this way is foolish indeed! O Kiyohime, Teruhime, if you have a heart, let your dream forms become reality, appear quickly, leaving behind the revolving island of the gods—'

At this moment, from within the cavern, four men wearing striped robes emerged after removing the stone door; bowing to the four, they beckoned in silence and led the way down into the dim cavern. The four followed behind, slightly bending their backs within the narrow cavern, ascending and descending to the right and left, until they reached a brilliantly bright open space.

The welcoming man indicated with hand gestures that they should rest here for a while. The four sat down on suitable stone projections and rested their breath for a while. The welcoming man disappeared just like that, to parts unknown. Clear and resonant music echoed from all sides.

After a while, two beautiful women appeared in this place, leisurely, wearing crowns adorned with necklaces upon their peach-colored faces, holding jade-branch offerings in both hands; one was slightly round-faced with a somewhat fuller body, and the other slightly younger with a slender face and a body correspondingly somewhat slender, possessed of the thirty-two bodily marks like Kannon, with peerless beauty and grandeur, and an unapproachable majesty that filled the surroundings, causing Kiyohiko and Teruhiko to feel slightly cowed, and they could only stare at their figures in blank astonishment. Instantly, drawing open the partition door of the inner room and appearing from within, the two of them, Kiyohiko and Kiyokohime, with radiant complexions and smiles brimming on their faces—Kiyokohime taking Terukohime's hand, and Kiyohiko taking Teruhiko's hand—guided them into a beautiful living room spread with Riukiu mats, which seemed ill-suited for a cavern. Bananas, strawberries, persimmons, eggplants, apples, and various other beautiful fruits, the flesh of red clams which were special products of the swamp, and rock mushrooms were arranged in great numbers, and here the two couples were to forge the auspicious bond of husband and wife.

From this time onward, the two of them, Kiyohiko and Kiyokohime, centering around this swamp, became the guardian deities of the vast island of Riukiu, and their descendants flourished for eternity, being revered like gods, and the numerous natives gladly submitted to their virtue, and the world was ruled in great peace. Next, Teruhiko, together with Terukohime, crossed over to the southern islands, similarly becoming the guardian deities of this island, where their descendants prospered and they were respected by the natives like gods and like parents.

The southern island is also called the island of Tama. Today's Yaeyama Islands are what remain of a part of the island of Tama. Teruhiko and his wife from time to time crossed the distant sea routes from the island of Tama, expanding their sphere of influence as far as the northern part of the island of Taiwan.
