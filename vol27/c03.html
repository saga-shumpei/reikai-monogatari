Chapter 3: Fish 水心 [785]

魚水心〔785〕

§ 1

Takahime, Kurohime, Takayamahiko, Natsuhiko, and Tsunehiko, these five people, finished their evening meal while indulging in talks about various matters, and opening the windows to worship the moon, they are absorbed in whispering talks.

Takahime: 'Natsuhiko, Tsunehiko-san, did you not go to the mansion of Akiyamahiko to welcome the five-colored Mitama, following the Kyōshiyu of Kotoyoriwake?'

Natsuhiko: 'Yes, we went. It was truly a most splendid thing. Somehow, the five people, Hatsuwakahime, Tamanohime, Tamaharuwake, Kiusuke, and Omin, received five-colored magnificent jewels from Otohime-sama at a place called the dragon's palace of Lake Suha on the single island of Riugū, and from the spirit that it would be too presumptuous to make them their own achievement, Hatsuwakahime-sama handed the purple jewel over to Yoshikohime-sama, and following that, the four honored persons handed those jewels without a word to Ryūrihime, Mukadehime, Tomohiko, and Teles-hime, so the story goes. Even human beings, if they go on abandoning themselves and yielding to each other in that sort of way, everything will go smoothly, doesn't it?'

Takahime: 'What! Ryūrihime and Mukadehime, that Tomohiko and Teles-hime, such fellows performed such a service? No matter how depleted of personages one might say it is, is it not altogether too outrageous? And those jewels are probably enshrined in the sacred place now. Since the flesh shrine of Otohime-sama of Riugū and Kurohime-sama are present here, so to speak, Kurohime-sama crossed over to the island of Riugū for two or three years and arranged things. Moreover, it is precisely because this Takahime, by the convenience of the Kami, drove Kurohime-sama out from the sacred place that it has served a purpose after all. There is probably not a single person who understands such a matter. Whatever one might say, because a muddling fellow like Mokusuke is the general affairs manager, you see.'

Natsuhiko: 'Everyone knows that well. They say that these were bestowed entirely thanks to Hinode no Kami-sama and Otohime-sama of Riugū, you know.'

Takahime: 'That is settled without saying, is it not? However, unless it is understood which one is the flesh shrine of Hinode no Kami and which one is the flesh shrine of Otohime-sama of Riugū, it will not do.'

Tsunehiko: 'That is settled without needing to be said. To what place would they attach themselves if not to the physical body of the lineage? Even Otohime-sama is surely fixed as the honored person who has accompanied the living shrine of Hinode no Kami from of old, is she not? Because of that, Kotoyoriwake Kami-sama said that he wanted to open the jewels and let the believers all worship them once, but until the living shrine of the vital lineage returns, we cannot open them, and he enshrined them somewhere himself. Because you do not show your face at all at the mansion of Kotoyoriwake-sama, he is waiting, you see.'

Takahime: 'It appears that Kotoyoriwake has also been able to reform quite a bit lately. It seems he is gradually realizing that this physical body is the living shrine of Hinode no Kami. Hey, Otohime-sama of Riugū, concerning that as well, is it not slightly incomprehensible? Even if we do not go to visit, once that is understood, they ought to come to express their gratitude to Hinode no Kami and Otohime-sama of Riugū. Putting the root and the tip upside down is indeed most excessive.'

Natsuhiko: 'Never, never, Kotoyoriwake-sama has no such thoughts whatsoever, but because you always say that Kotoyoriwake is a chaff-heap or a four-legged spirit, Kotoyoriwake-sama's heart is full of the desire to visit your honorable residence, but he refrains, saying that since manpower is scarce, if something like having to perform the autumn great purification arises once again, he would feel sorry for you.'

Takahime: 'Such worries are unnecessary. If they possess spirits capable of understanding the living shrines of Hinode no Kami and Otohime-sama of Riugū, the four-legged spirits must have already retreated without fail, so please say that Takahime and Kurohime are waiting eagerly and come over once. There are also various things I want to tell them and make them listen to. No matter how wise a Kyōshiyu one might say he is, since he is a young person of tender years without experience who has not graduated from the university of society, there are mountains of things that must be said, but thinking he would find them annoying again, I have remained silent until now. If that is true, Kotoyoriwake is also quite admirable. Ot-ho-ho-ho-ho.'

Natsuhiko: 'Since a magnificent Mitama has been enshrined, all the Shinjiya are waiting, saying they wish to Haikuwan it. Since no one can open it unless you open it with your own Onte, please, I beg you to calm your mood, visit the Nishiki no Miya, and consult with Kotoyoriwake-sama.'

Takahime: 'That is where the path goes astray. Even if you say Kotoyoriwake is the Kyōshiyu, that is something determined by Ningen; is it not a Shitsurei matter that he does not even come out once to meet at the Niku no Miya where the truly authentic Hinode-no-Kami-sama and the Otohime-sama of Riugū are Oshizu?'

Natsuhiko raised his hand to his head as if it were difficult to speak, and

Natsuhiko: 'I think what you say is reasonable to a certain extent, but without being so Shikakuba, I believe it is appropriate for you to visit Kotoyoriwake-sama as the Kyōshiyu. However, you must not meet him directly either. No matter how much you dislike it, please arrange a meeting through the hands of Sōmu Mokusuke-sama. This Natsuhiko has received the divine virtue that this is the proper way.'

Takahime: 'Is it not that I cannot meet with such ill-mannered people as that Mokusuke or Kuniyoriwake, for it is absurd? Secondly, they lie down flat like four-legged beasts or something, and it is impolite... they are the kind of people who even lie down and talk in front of the Ikemiya. Because of that, even Kuniyoriwake mimics the monkeys in the same way, and thinking it is fine, he lies flat on his back to respond. When I, this Takahime, admonish them saying, "Have some sense, is that not Shitsurei?", they are such hopeless fellows who quibble, saying that even in the Reikai Monogatari, they lie on their backs and lift their legs up to preach the magnificent causal connections of the Shinkai. It is a cause for celebration that Kotoyoriwake-sama, being a good-natured person, is happy to use such people. Above all, if we do not get rid of them from now on, the San-go-kyō will never be of any use.'

Natsuhiko: 'Your words are truly reasonable. I, too, am sometimes taken aback when Mokusuke-sama lies on his back and gives us various instructions, and when I question him about the reason, Mokusuke-sama's way of speaking is interesting. "In the current world of Hyakki-Yagyō, all Ningen are walking upright, mimicking Ningen, while ghosts, snakes, and demons are doing so. And because they are just going sideways like crabs, it is unbearable. If you want to reform today's society, you cannot achieve the Tatekae or Naoshi without doing things that people do not do. Look at today's society; is the current not flowing sideways, endlessly? That is the so-called path of heaven and earth and nature. River water and seawater should also flow sideways. Trains carrying many human lives are also long sideways. Is it not the same for the rails? If the rails were to sit down or stand up, would the train not capsize immediately? Rivers flowing sideways are vast and without stagnation, and mother birds raising their lovely chicks hold them carefully within their wings and sleep in the nest. Even to hatch eggs, one must lie down, or they will not hatch. Even Noah's Ark was moving and flowing sideways on the water surface, and even the Suiheisha movement..." he said.'

Takahime: 'Is there such a quibble? Look at the pine trees and bamboo grass in this garden. Are they not all standing straight up from the ground? The ones lying sideways are only trees whose trunks have rotted and been blown down by the wind. They are only dead timber that has been cut at the root and the tip and had their bark peeled. Is there anything decent among those lying sideways? That is why I say Mokusuke is no good.'

Putting force into it, he unintentionally struck the threshold with his clenched fist with a thud, and was about to say 'Ouch, ouch, ouch,' but the moment he uttered 'Ow...,' he grimaced and began stealthily rubbing it with his left hand—such was his pitiable state.

Tsunehiko: 'How arguments can be made to fit any direction whatsoever. There is fire within water, and there is water within fire; fire burns by borrowing the power of water, and water is moved according to the power of fire—by whatever way you hear it, the logic aligns, doesn't it? That must be why the Lord God proclaims that the warp is the transformation-male and the weft is the transformation-female. With the warp alone it is ultimately useless, and as expected, without the weft, the fabric of brocade cannot be woven.'

Takahime: 'It is that very weft which is no good. Because the shuttle of the weft drops or the threads break, they say that the future of the transformation-female is hopeless... The mitama that weaves the weft of the loom is indeed bitter, If one is passed through, one is struck in return— What can be accomplished by a Kotoyoriwake who is blowing such weak sounds? No, no, it is not certain that Kotoyoriwake cannot do it either. Lately, she has greatly begun to reform, so against the warp of the transformation-male, I will become wise and show you how to weave a magnificent loom. It will be fine as long as even the weft-role follows just as the wise one speaks. ...Now, Kurohime-sama, is that not so?'

Kurohime: 'Precisely, precisely, there is not a single hair's breadth of difference from what you, Anata, proclaim. If only Mokusuke-sama would reform even an hour earlier, I would have nothing more to say at all.'

Tsunehiko: 'On Mokusuke-sama's side, he was tilting his head and thinking deeply, saying, "If only Takahime-sama and Kurohime would reform even a day earlier, I would have nothing more to say at all..." Even Kuniyoriwake has actually been made to serve as your adversary, you know. He brought a stone the other day, saying it was a relish for sake, did he not? That cannot be said in a loud voice, but it is entirely the instruction of Kotoyoriwake-sama.'

Takahime: 'What, Kotoyoriwake did... is that not going too far?'

Tsunehiko: 'Kotoyoriwake-sama had a deep intention in making Kuniyoriwake do such a thing, and to test whether your august self would become angry or not—saying that if you would get angry, the time to serve in the august business of the jewels had not yet arrived, and drawing out your spirit by saying that if you are a Takahime-sama who can endure and forbear such a thing, then you are already all right. You are surely getting angry, aren't you?'

Takahime: 'Eh, to have such a tiny spirit as to get angry—can that be called Yamato-damashii? Because it is Takahime's attitude that the great ocean rejects no dust and swallows the muddy currents of a hundred rivers without becoming muddy. It is a boundless great spirit, as they say: just as the heavens are high and let all birds fly at will, and just as the seas are wide and deep and let fish and turtles leap at will... Humph... do not underestimate me so much. To test me is far too presumptuous. It is simply because your own heart is small. To attempt to measure the great spirit of a living Kamisama by the scale of one's own heart is mistaken from the very beginning. However, has Kotoyoriwake reached even that point? Truly a lovable thing. ...If so, Tsunehiko-sama, you—when you meet Kotoyoriwake-sama, you must report it in a manner that looks genuinely authentic, saying that Takahime-sama was smiling with calm self-possession and serene composure, as if a matter of that degree were like the wind blowing from nowhere... Whoops-a-daisy... thoroughly embedding that genuinely authentic, magnificent attitude into your belly when you tell him.'

Tsunehiko: 'At any rate, is it fine if I just tell the truth of today? Because it is a sacred Way where not even a tiny lie can be told.'

Takahime: 'Eh, as expected, do not speak of it yet. Since I will go to see him directly and show him that magnanimous bearing, not a single thing about today must be spoken of.'
