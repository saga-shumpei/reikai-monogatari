Chapter 7: 猫の恋 [789]

猫の恋〔789〕

§ 1

Tamateruhime caused the purple sacred jewel to be guarded by Hatsuwakahime, Tamanohime, and Otamanokata, while she returned to her own mansion.

Beginning with the executive leaders, everyone once again chanted the Amatsunorito and it was decided that they would each return to their respective lodgings.

Takayamahiko once returned to the mansion to prepare his travel attire, and just as he was about to set off hastily from the mansion along with the two men, Earl and Ace, he encountered Kurohime face-to-face at the doorway, who had come back with her hair disheveled like a yaksha.

Crying out that this was a supreme emergency, Takayamahiko attempted to rush out through the back door.

Kurohime collapsed right on the spot, suffered a spasm, and stretched out stiffly.

Even the unflappable Takayamahiko could not simply abandon her and run away.

Takayamahiko: 'Ace, water! ... Earl, it's a spasm.'

Calling out thus, he tended to her.

Kurohime had hidden the black pupils of her eyes somewhere, showed only the whites of her eyes, and was breathing heavily with a loud wheezing sound.

Ace and Earl took water into their mouths and sprayed it wildly onto her face.

Takayamahiko placed his mouth close to her ear and repeated the anti-soul incantation of 'One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand' several times.

Groaning with an 'Un', Kurohime looked all around her with darting eyes.

Kurohime: 'Ah, I do not know who you are, but you have saved me graciously.'

Takayamahiko: 'Ah, Kurohime, have you come to your senses? Well now, you are safe with this. From now on, Takayamahiko will sever his ties with you and go to find the jewel either on the Isle of Ryūgū or the Isle of Tsukushi, so please accept this and give up our past relationship.'

Looking resentful, Kurohime spoke.

Kurohime: 'Takayamahiko-sama, you are too cruel. That I collapsed just now was because your heart is so heartless. To think that you will suffer a terrible retribution like a murderer without a blade or a demon of the underworld... Ah me... that is what makes me sad. Since our bond was tied by the gods, I beg you to reconsider once more.'

Takayamahiko: 'No matter what is said, a man's word, once uttered from his mouth, cannot be retracted. First things first, for the time being, let us part ways here. Kurohime, farewell...'

So saying, he attempted to leave.

Kurohime swiftly drew the hidden dagger from her bosom.

Kurohime: 'Takayamahiko-sama, you have taken care of me for a long time. My love is nine suns and five bu deep, and I no longer have any hope of living in this world. I will commit suicide cleanly here, and my vengeful soul that resents you will condense into a demon, and I will surely show you by pulling off your very head! Ah, Kannagara Tamachihachimase.'

She pressed it tightly against her throat to show him.

Takayamahiko: 'Suicide is the greatest of all sins. Now, Kurohime-sama, no matter how nine suns and five bu long the dagger is, you cannot cut your throat from the chest side, can you? You are indeed very good at acting. I am not a Takayamahiko to be fooled by such tricks. Ha ha ha ha!'

Kurohime: 'Oh, what a pity, how mortifying! If that is so, shall I truly show you by cutting? If I say I will cut, I will surely show you I can cut.'

Takayamahiko: 'This bond that was once severed, how could there be any reason for it to be joined again? Henceforth, between the two of you and me, there is no chain of any kind. Total strangers are the same. Please cut yourself as you please.'

Kurohime: 'That is unacceptable, Takayamahiko-sama. Under heaven, there is no such thing as a stranger... Such is the teaching of the Omotokyo; have you forgotten that? Do you intend to leave a pitiful woman to die a lonely death? You were not such a heartless man; by what celestial demon have you been bewitched? Your words are merciless and cruel beyond compare to a demon, a snake, or a villain, and even in death I shall never forget this!'

Takayamahiko: 'Nay, for the sake of the divine realm, they say one must forget one's home, forget one's body, and forget one's wife and children. When a man heads to the battlefield, the three forgettings are essential. ... Kurohime, farewell...'

So saying, he attempted to go.

Kurohime: 'Hey, hey! Earl and Ace, both of you, grip tightly onto Takayamahiko's feet! You must not let him go, absolutely not!'

原文（近代文語）

玉照姫は紫の宝珠を初稚姫、玉能姫、お玉の方に守らせ乍ら、我館に帰らせ給うた。幹部を始め一同は更めて天津祝詞を奏上し一先づ各自の宿所に帰る事となつた。

高山彦は一旦館へ立ち帰り旅装を整へ、アール、エースの二人と共に早々館を立ち出でんとする時しも、髪振り乱し夜叉の如くに帰つて来た黒姫と門口でピツタリ出会した。南無三宝一大事と高山彦は裏口より駆出さんとする。黒姫は此場に倒れて癪を起してフン伸びて仕舞つた。流石の高山彦も之を見捨て逃げ出す訳にもゆかず、

高山彦『エース、水だ。…アール、癪だ』

と呼ばはり乍ら介抱して居る。

黒姫は目の黒玉を何処かへ隠して仕舞ひ、白目ばかりになつて「フウフウ」と太い息をして居る。エース、アールは口に水を含んで無性矢鱈に面部に吹き付ける。高山彦は口を耳にあてて反魂歌の「一、二、三、四、五、六、七、八、九、十、百、千、万」を数回繰返した。黒姫は「ウン」と呻き乍ら、

黒姫『ア、何方か知りませぬが、よう助けて下さつた』

と四辺をキヨロキヨロ見廻して居る。

高山彦『ア、黒姫、気がついたか。マアマア之で安心だ。これから高山彦はお前と縁を断り、竜宮の一つ島か、但は筑紫の島へ玉探しに行くから、これまでの縁と諦めて下さい』

黒姫は怨めしさうに、

黒姫『高山さま、お前も余りだ。妾の今卒倒したのもお前の心が情無いからだよ。刃物持たずの人殺、冥土の鬼にエライ成敗を受けなさるのが…妾や…それが悲しい。神の結んだ縁ぢやもの、何卒モ一度思ひ直して下さいませ』

高山彦『何と言つても男の一旦口から出した事、後へひく訳にはゆかぬ。先は先として一先づ此場は離別を致す。黒姫、さらば……』

と立ち去らんとする。黒姫は隠し持つたる懐剣、ヒラリと引き抜き、

黒姫『高山彦さま、永らくお世話になりました。妾の恋は九寸五分、最早此世に生て望みなし。妾は此処で潔く自害を致し、貴方を怨める魂魄凝つて鬼となり、屹度素首引き抜いて見せませう。アヽ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

と喉にピタリと当てて見せた。

高山彦『自殺は罪悪中の罪悪だ。これ黒姫さま、何程九寸五分だつて胸の方では喉は斬れませぬよ。随分芝居がお上手ですね。そんな事にチヨロマカされる高山彦では御座りませぬワイ。アツハヽヽヽ』

黒姫『エー、残念や、口惜しい。そんなら本当に斬つて見せようか。斬ると云うたら屹度斬つて見せませう』

高山彦『一旦断つた此縁、再びきられる道理があらうか。最早お前と俺との二人の間には何の連鎖もない。赤の他人も同様だ。勝手にお斬りなさいませ』

黒姫『そりや聞えませぬ高山さま、天ケ下に他人と云ふ事は無いもの……と三五教の御教、お前はそれを忘れたか。憐れな女を見殺しにする御所存か、それ程情ないお前ではなかつたに、如何なる天魔に魅られたか。お前の言葉は鬼とも蛇とも悪人とも譬方なき無情惨酷さ、死んでも忘れは致しませぬぞや』

高山彦『イヤ、もう神界の為めには家を忘れ、身を忘れ、妻子を忘れるとかや。男子は戦場に向ふ時には三忘が肝腎だ。……黒姫、さらば……』

と行かんとする。

黒姫『コレコレ、アール、エースの両人、高山さまの足に確り喰ひついて居るのだよ。屹度放しちやなりませぬぞえ』

§ 2

Futari wa Takayamahiko no riyōashi ni kui-tsuki-nagara,

Āru: 'Ā ā, inu mo kuwanu fūfu kenkuwa no gisei ni kyōserare, zuibun tsutome bōkō mo tsurai mono dana ā'

Takayamahiko: 'Koriya koriya, Āru, Ēsu no riyōnin, hayaku hanasanu ka'

Kurohime: 'Kesshite hanashi-chya narimasenu zo. Kore kore Takayamahiko-sama, otoko wa shikii o matageru ya inayaya shichinin no teki ga aru to iu koto o shitte imasu ka'

Takayamahiko: 'Aha hachacha, iya mō goshinsetsu na gochiui, arigatō gozaimasu. Makoto hitotsu no kokoro de ireba, sekai wa teki no kage o mitai to yūtemo miru koto wa dekinai. Sankasaumoku, jinrui chōjiu giyobetsu ni itaru made, mina wareware no mikata bakari da. Hito o mitara dorobou to omoe nado to yū saigishin ni kararete iru ningen no me ni wa, nani mo kamo teki ni mieru darō ga, wareware ni wa kamisama ni omakase shita ijiyō hitori no teki mo nai. Omae ni sōte ireba kono yo no naka de teki o tsukuru bakari dakara... Nanitozo shinpai shite kudasaru na. Omae mo kore kara kaijin o shite, seken no hito ni kawaigararete kure. Sore ga Takayamahiko no wakare ni nozomi omae ni atafuru taisetsu na senbetsu da. Takahime-sama ni mo nanitozo yoku yūte oite kudasai. Kanarazu kanarazu shūjakushin o dashite wa narimasenu zo. Kyō kara kokoro o aratame hontō ni umare akago ni nary, karini mo ryūgū no otohime nado to oosonata koto o iwanai yō ni shinasai. Sayōnaremba kore nite... Kurohime-sama, ohima itashimasu'

Kurohime: 'Takayamahiko-sama, soriya anata, honshō de ōyōru no ka. Shibai jya arimasumai nā'

Takayamahiko: 'Honshō de nūte nani to seou. Fūfu no michi wa jinrin no oomoto da. Sore o wakarō to iu Takayamahiko no mune no ura, sukoshi wa suiryō shite kure. ...Sā Āru, Ēsu, kore kara ikō. ...Kurohime-sama, kore nite shibaraku owakare itashimasu'

To awateshiku kidasu.

Sikaru tokoro e hashitte kita Tamajiwake,

Tamajiwake: 'Yā, Takayamahiko-sama, iyō onode desu ka'

Takayamahiko: 'Hai, nanibun yoroshiku negaimasu yo'

Kurohime: 'Nani to yūtemo hanashi wa senu'

To shigami-tsuku. Takayamahiko wa 'ē mendō' to atami o hitotsu kuwasu ya inayaya, Kurohime wa 'un' to sono ba ni ōji ni taorete shimatta.

Tamajiwake: 'Nani to Takayamahiko-sama, ranbō na koto o itashinasu na'

Takayamahiko: 'Kō shite okaneba shikata ga nai kara... Kono ma ni watashi wa mi o kakusu kara, ato wa tanomimasu yo. Kō shite koko o booshi de gutsu to osu-toe-ba iki o fuki-kaesu... Tamajiwake-sama, koko da yo. Nanitozo nijyū-fun bakari matsu-tsutte kudasai'

Tamajiwake: 'Shōchi itashimashita'

"Sayōnaremba" to Takayamahiko wa futari o tomoni, ashibaya ni dore e ka sugata o kakushita. Tamajiwake wa toki o mi-hakarai Takayamahiko ni kyōwatta tsubo o booshi ni chikara o irete gutsu to osuita. "Un" to iki fuki-kaeshita Kurohime wa shihō o kyorokyoro mi-mawashi,

Kurohime: 'Ā zannen ya, tōtō nigerareta ka. Ē shikata ga nai. ...Omae wa Tamajiwake-sama, yō mā tasukete kudasatta'

Tamajiwake: 'Takayamahiko no yatsu, ayashimanu ranbō na otoko da. Nagaraku sōte kita nyōbō ni atami o kuwashite iki o tome, Chikushi no shima e nige-te yuku to wa fudeuki senman na mono da. Omae-sama mo kore de me ga sameta darō. Tora, ōkami to isshō ni neru yō na mono da. Watashi mo omae-sama o ikasō to omotte, nan hodo hone o orita ka wari-masenu. Tōtō tsubo ga wakatte iki o ireta toki, anata ga mono yūta no mo mina kamisama no okage, ā mottainai. Kore kara issai no shūjakushin o sutete Ookamisama no kansha kigan no norito o sōjō shimashō'

"Hai, arigatō" to Kurohime wa Tamajiwake to ai-narabi, kiyute no koe mo shizuyaka ni nishiki no miya no hōmen ni mukatte kansha kigan no kotoba o sō shita.

Tsuyuni iu, nishiki no miya no kamitsukasa wa jūzen no tōri Tamateruhiko, Tamateruhime no futari ai-narabarete kamigyo ni hōshi sare, Eikohime erabarete Kotoyoriwake-mikoto no fuzai-chū kyōshu no yaku o tsutome-raruru koto to natta. Soshite Tōsuke wa kyōshu dairi ken sōmu to natte seichi ni tsukae ta.

原文（近代文語）

二人は高山彦の両足に喰ひ付き乍ら、

アール『アヽア、犬も喰はぬ夫婦喧嘩の犠牲に供せられ、随分勤め奉公も辛いものだなア』

高山彦『こりやこりや、アール、エースの両人、早く放さぬか』

黒姫『決して放しちやなりませぬぞ。コレコレ高山さま、男は閾を跨げるや否や七人の敵があると云ふ事を知つて居ますか』

高山彦『アハヽヽヽ、イヤもう御親切な御注意、有難う御座います。誠一つの心で居れば、世界は敵の影を見たいと言つても見る事は出来ない。山河草木、人類鳥獣魚鼈に至る迄、皆我々の味方ばかりだ。人を見たら泥坊と思へ等と云ふ猜疑心に駆られて居る人間の目には、何も彼も敵に見えるだらうが、我々は神様にお任せした以上一人の敵も無い。お前に添うて居れば此世の中で敵を作るばかりだから……何卒心配して下さるな。お前もこれから改心をして、世間の人に可愛がられて呉れ。それが高山彦の別れに臨みお前に与ふる大切な餞別だ。高姫さまにも何卒よく言うて置いて下さい。必ず必ず執着心を出してはなりませぬぞ。今日から心を改めて本当の生れ赤子になり、仮にも竜宮の乙姫等と大それた事を言はない様にしなさい。左様なれば是にて……黒姫さま、お暇致します』

黒姫『高山さま、そりや貴方、本性で仰有るのか。芝居ぢやありますまいなア』

高山彦『本性で無うて何とせう。夫婦の道は人倫の大本だ。それを別れようと言ふ高山彦の胸の裏、些とは推量して呉れ。……さあアール、エース、これから行かう。……黒姫さま、これにて暫くお別れ致します』

と慌しく駆出す。

斯かる処へ走つて来た玉治別、

玉治別『ヤア、高山さま、愈御出でですか』

高山彦『ハイ、何分宜しう願ひますよ』

黒姫『何と言つても放しはせぬ』

と獅噛みつく。高山彦は「エー面倒」と当身を一つ喰はすや否や、黒姫は「ウン」と其場に大の字に倒れて仕舞つた。

玉治別『何と高山さま、乱暴な事を致しますな』

高山彦『斯うして置かねば仕方が無いから……此間に私は身を隠すから、後は頼みますよ。斯うして此処を拇指でグツと押して貰へば息を吹き返す……玉治別さま、此処だよ。何卒二十分ばかり待つとつて下さい』

玉治別『承知致しました』

「左様ならば」と高山彦は二人を伴ひ、足早に何れへか姿を隠した。玉治別は時期を見計らひ高山彦に教はつた局を拇指に力を入れてグツと押した。「ウン」と息吹き返した黒姫は四方をキヨロキヨロ見廻し、

黒姫『アヽ残念や、到頭逃げられたか。エー仕方がない。……お前は玉治別さま、ようマア助けて下さつた』

玉治別『高山彦の奴、怪しからぬ乱暴な男だ。永らく添うて来た女房に当身を喰はして息を止め、筑紫の島へ逃げて行くとは不届き千万な者だ。お前さまも是で目が醒めただらう。虎、狼と一緒に寝る様なものだ。私もお前さまを活かさうと思つて、何程骨を折つたか分りませぬ。到頭局が分つて活を入れた時、貴女がもの言うたのも皆神さまのお蔭、アヽ勿体ない。是から一切の執着を捨てて大神さまに感謝祈願の祝詞を奏上しませう』

「ハイ、有難う」と黒姫は玉治別と相並び、拍手の声も淑やかに錦の宮の方面に向つて感謝祈願の言葉を奏した。

因に言ふ、錦の宮の神司は従前の通り玉照彦、玉照姫の二人相並ばれて御神業に奉仕され、英子姫選ばれて言依別命の不在中教主の役を勤めらるる事となつた。そして東助は教主代理兼総務となつて聖地に仕へた。

§ 3

Takayamahiko, Akihiko, Terusuhime, Natsuhiko, Sadahiko, and O-Tama no Kata were in the sacred land, occupying the positions of executives and engaging in Shingefu. Tamanohime returned to the mansion in the forest of Ikuta and was to engage in Shingefu together with Komahiko. Also, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, together with Kuniyoriwake, was to cross over to Nanbei and Takasagojima, search for the whereabouts of Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake, and additionally be dispatched for the purpose of Senden.

Takahime was to go to Takasagojima, leading along two men, Haruhiko and Tsunehiko, in order to pursue the footsteps of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto and take back the four jewels. Mokusuke was to lead Hatsuwakahime, Tamaharuwake, Isorashihime, Kamehiko, Otohiko, Ōryūhime, Mukadehime, and others, heading toward the sanctuary mansion of the Ubusuna mountain range in the country of Persia. Umekohime is graciously ascending Mount Caucasus with two or three attendants, conducting Senden along the way. Kurohime, hearing that Takayamahiko had fled away to Ryūgūjima or the island of Chikushi, and on one hand aiming to deliberate about the jewels while investigating the whereabouts of her husband, was to leave the sacred land behind and depart with three attendants following her.

原文（近代文語）

高山彦、秋彦、テールス姫、夏彦、佐田彦、お玉の方は聖地にあつて幹部の位置を占め神業に従事しつつあつた。玉能姫は生田の森の館に帰りて駒彦と共に神業に従事する事となつた。又言依別命は国依別と共に南米、高砂島に渡り、鷹依姫、竜国別の行衛を探ね、旁宣伝の為めに出張さるる事となつた。

高姫は言依別命の後を追ひ四個の玉を取り返さんと、春彦、常彦の二人を引き率れ、高砂島に行く事となつた。杢助は初稚姫、玉治別、五十子姫、亀彦、音彦、黄竜姫、蜈蚣姫其他を率ゐ、波斯の国のウブスナ山脈斎苑の館を指して行く事となつた。梅子姫はコーカス山に二三の供者を従へ途々宣伝をし乍ら登らせ給ふ。黒姫は高山彦が竜宮島又は筑紫の島に逃げ去りしと聞き、一方は玉の詮議を兼ねて夫の行衛を捜査すべく聖地を後に、三人の供者を従へ出発する事となつた。
