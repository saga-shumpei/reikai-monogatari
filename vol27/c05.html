§ 1

If we gaze upon it, how lofty is the young palace of Hisakata, of Takaamahara, respectfully replicated upon the earth, its great pillars firmly set, with the cross-timber chigi soaring high in Takaamahara, the miraculous mansion built in reverent service, joined to the brocade palace, the vast and majestic Hall of Eight Fathoms—starting with Hidekohime, and Umekohime, Isokohime, Hatsuwakahime, and Tamanohime, beginning with Otohiko and Kamehiko, Mokusuke the sōmu, and all other yakuin and shinjiya, solemnly drawing near to the Hall of Eight Fathoms, quickly overflowing both inside and outside the sanctuary.

Just as planned, the ritual was safely concluded. In the upper seat sat Mokusuke the sōmu, followed by Hidekohime, Isokohime, Umekohime, Hatsuwakahime, and Tamanohime, and Lady Otama, while in the highest seats were Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime in waiting; among the leading cadres were Kamehiko, Otohiko, and Kuniyoriwake, and starting with Akihiko, Natsuhiko, and Tsunehiko, sitting alongside Hidekohime were Kōryūhime, Mukadehime, and Princess Teles in waiting, while Tomohiko took his place among the upper seats of the cadres. Dividing the crowd, coming up with high spirits, Takahime, Kurohime, and Takayamahiko, acting in their roles for today's divine service of jewel examination, adorned in splendid attire, took seats one step higher even than Hidekohime.

Mokusuke: 'I am by nature a dull-souled, inferior-vessel, most foolish and idiotic bearer of a mitama, and I am hardly suited to fulfill the duties of sōmu; yet, finding the divine command impossible to ignore and against my own wishes accepting the appointment, I am discharging a portion of our sacred tasks with the assistance of you all, for which I deeply and warmly thank you, seeing this entirely as the grace of your universal sympathy. Therefore, having somewhat in mind for the sake of the divine realm, I desire to perform yet one more service, and I have already recommended my successor. Since the Kyōshu-sama is in retirement today due to a sudden illness, we cannot ask for his opinion, but as my successor I have thought of asking for the trouble of Tōsuke-san of Awaji Island, and I have confidentially submitted an inquiry regarding this. Now, today is truly a most auspicious day, and as you have heard, from Ryūgūjima the five-colored manijubels have been safely enshrined, and Kotoyorivake no Mikoto-sama has in any case assumed the supreme administration; today, it is the Kyōshu's words that we are to request the examination of Takahime and Kurohime, and to let all the shinjiya behold them in reverence, so with that intention please leisurely witness the sacred sight. Since you will not be able to behold them a second time, I will say just a single word so that you may fully receive the divine virtue on this occasion.'

The gathered assembly applauds like falling rain. Mokusuke, accompanied by Hatsuwakahime, Tamanohime, Isokohime, and Umekohime, proceeds deep into the inner sanctuary, opens the nearby sacred seat with a golden key, and holding high above his head five separate willow boxes one by one, appears solemnly upon the high seat of the Hall of Eight Fathoms, where the five willow boxes are neatly placed upon the dais.

Takahime stands up straight upon the dais, and glancing around at the assembly,

Takahime: 'Everyone, today is truly a most auspicious day. Up until now, the three divine treasures of the Spirit of Mizu were enshrined here, and today once again the five divine treasures of the Spirit of Iku are safely enshrined, making this the unprecedented and peerless primary auspicious day on which you are blessed with the honor of reverent viewing. Since the Gods enact the divine providence of pulling back and casting forth, it is something well known to you all that they bring forth the crucial sutra of the Spirit of Iku later, and put forth the weft of Mizu first, performing transformations and disappearances beyond all ultimate comprehension. Up until today, our going as far as the neighborhood of the South Seas to search for the three jewels was by no means to seek out such weft-role jewels. Since jewels are quite prone to the possession of monsters, the likes of this Takahime and others made it appear as though we were searching for the three treasures, turning all attention in that direction, and at that time, the personages who become the foundation of the Sun God and the Otohime of Ryūgū performed arrangements deeply concealed within Lake Suwa of the Secret Village—where people can scarcely go—and that this was from the very beginning a matter well known to the two of us, nay, a matter arranged by us, is a fact. That we caused Hatsuwakahime, Tamanohime, Kōryūhime, Umekohime, Mukadehime, and five other divine officiants to perform this sacred task is precisely the infinite benevolence and arrangement of the Sun God and the divine compassion of Otohime-sama of Ryūgū. If you do not understand that, you will never be able to comprehend the arrangement of the Mitsugaku Gosoku Shinsē. Fortunately, the sincerity in the divine service of the wise Hidekohime and of Kotoyorivake no Mikoto—who has achieved a slight repentance—has manifested, enabling the Manijubel of Ryūgū to be enshrined in the sacred land, and the role of receiving and examining it is specifically one that both I, Takahime, and Kurohime ought to perform. Therefore, from this very moment we shall perform the inspection of the jewels, so everyone, it is best that you reverently behold them. Whatever may become of the three jewels, I know nothing of it. These five jewels of this time are indeed the crucial, vital, and grand sacred enterprise and great treasures of our divine work; moreover, because Tamajiwake and other half-enlightened mitama have handled them, I am worried as to whether they might be slightly tainted. Since the light of the jewel manifests in accordance with the mitama, it is truly a fearful thing, you see. Come, come, from here we shall take out from the box the yellow jewel entrusted by Omin to be handed over to Princess Teles, and examine it... Kurohime-sama, despite your trouble, please come over here for a moment. And you, Omin-sama, Princess Teles, since you are directly involved, take your places in waiting here.'

佐田彦『杢助さまに申上げます。今朝より言依別命様は御病気と仰有つて、御引籠りになつておいでなさいましたが、余りお静かですから、ソツと障子を開けて中へ這入つて見れば、萩の机の上に斯様な書き置きがして御座いました』

と手に渡す。杢助開いてこれを見れば、

『此度青、赤、黄、白の四個の宝玉を始め三個の玉、三つ四つ併せて都合七個、言依別命都合あつて、或地点に隠し置いたり、必ず必ず玉能姫、玉治別、黄竜姫其他此玉に関係者の与り知る所に非ず。然し乍ら杢助は願ひの如く総務の職を免じて、淡路の東助を以て総務となす。言依別は何時聖地に帰るか、其時期は未定なり。必ず我後を追ひ来る勿れ』

と書いてあつた。杢助は黙然として涙をハラハラと流し、千万無量の感に打たるるものの如くであつた。
