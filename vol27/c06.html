Takahime: 'Since you have resigned from general affairs, you surely have no right to keep holding your ground in such a high place until when. Get down briskly. Now from here on, whether Kotoyoriwake absconds or Mokusuke resigns, I wonder if Takahime must become the Kyōshu and carry on in this Yahiroden after all. Times are something that must be awaited.'
