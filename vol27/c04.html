§ 1

Old pine trees and plum groves, maple leaves in autumn crimson and crape myrtles, mokukoku, fragrant olives, firs, and tara trees, mingling their green and crimson hues to form the secluded and elegant divine garden, reflecting their shadows in the Kinriuike pond whose waters ripple like fish scales, while the Kototamakaku soars into the region of clouds and surveys the lower world with majestic gaze, its divine awe resounding brightly and vastly in all four directions throughout the Kyōshiyuden of the Sananahi divine teaching.

Gathering closely within the wide hall of Yata, starting with Umekohime, and Isokohime who serves diligently day and night along the great path of the Kami, Eikohime who dispels the darkness, Kamehiko who praises ten thousand generations, Otonohiko of the Isuzu waterfall, and Tamanohime whose very heart shines brilliantly.

Starting with Tamazakewake, Hatsuwakihime and Mokusuke together with Kotoyoriwake occupy their seats in the inner grand hall, and are deeply engaged in mutual discussions concerning the management of the Mani wish-granting jewel bestowed by Tamayorihime.

Just at that moment, a trio appears at the entrance, announcing their arrival with repeated apologies, upon which Tamazakewake steps out to welcome them, and from the very first glance, curtsies with elaborate courtesy, wears a smiling face, and bends his waist low, saying:

Takahime-sama or Kurihime-sama, Takayamahiko's divine ritualist, you are most welcome indeed, Kotoyoriwake's divine ritualist, and all you other many notables, you have been waiting so eagerly since this morning, please, please step right through.

Takahime makes a slight bow and speaks: 'Since everyone has been waiting so long, let us be guided to the inner chambers; Kurihime-sama and the two divine ritualists of Takayamahiko, come along together, let us proceed.' Kurihime and her husband, completely silent and without uttering a word, stamping their feet softly, follow behind and walk into the inner room.

Takahime: 'Now then, without even showing deference in the presence of Kotoyoriwake-sama, Eikohime-sama, and all the other notables, I, humble Takahime, have dared to wait upon you without fear, and surely I must be defiling your living room. Please reconsider and rehear everything through the wide and compassionate heart of Kaminonao-hinaonaohinao, and without blaming this old frame, please cherish me.'

All of them at once place their hands upon the floor and pay their respects.

Kotoyoriwake: 'Takahime-sama, that is too near the edge; here seats have been prepared for the three of you. Please be seated right here.'

Takahime: 'No matter what, being merely a soul whose spirit is unpolished, a living shrine of the false Hinode kami, and possessing only a soul of body-dominant and spirit-subordinate nature, it is truly awe-inspiring for me to sit upon such a proper seat. A corner of the garden will suffice quite well, but yielding to your kind words, I have come to occupy the lowest seat among the notables. Please do not worry about such a thing.'
