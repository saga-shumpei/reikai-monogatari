Chapter 14: Grass 枕 [796]

草枕〔796〕

§ 1

At the foot of Mount Hijiyama, where clouds soar, lies the pure Hinumanai. In the sacred ground where Toyokunihime eternally abides in serenity, Terukohime, whose heart's hue served morning and evening, and Kiyokohime, whose mitama was supremely pure, received the divine mikoto. As sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, starting with Umeko-hime, along with Hatsuwakahime, Tamanohime, and Tamaharuwake's party, riding over the waves of Kaiyōbanri upon the single isle of the floating Riugū, which is Lake Suha—Yatagarasu, receiving directly from the hands of the divine mikoto of Tamayorihime, who eternally guarded the Mani jewel, straddled golden wings.

Soaring high across the spacious sky, through ten and twenty layers of the veiled ama no iwato, hearing that they descend to the garden where the evergreen pines grow luxuriantly, at the rare mansion of Akiyamahiko's human-child officials, the two goddesses received permission from Ōkami and crossed over the village where Hisatsugu weaves brocade. With the autumn leaves coloring the peaks on all four sides, and Chōzenji upon their path, riding past Ōno and Yamada, they arrived at Kami no Miyazu. Worshipping the shrine of the heavenly deities to the right, stepping swiftly past Iwabuchi, Monju, and Momoyogizaka, tracing the stormy shore of the crashing waves, they reached the port of Yura. Lingering before the gate of Akiyamahiko's domain to observe the state of affairs,

Thinking it was like the after-festival of the tenth-day chrysanthemum, and hearing that the Mani jewel had swiftly and easily arrived at the brocade sacred land, the two goddesses grew impatient in spirit. Passing the rural area of Tsukigao Maruhachie to Tanabe Inn, with the sun once more in the sky over Onochiru, riding past Ikenouchi, into the valley between mountain and mountain where even the shadow of the sun is unseen, Makuragō. As the moon shines on Katakata no Uesugi, opening their hearts at Umesako, Nishihatta, Engaki, and Mikatabara, crossing the great bridge of Aya, washing their heart and gallbladder in the flow of Kogumo, they advanced to the sacred realm. A majestic divine mansion is built, and the ten-fold kamimon sparkles, reflecting in the moonlight,

With a beauty that defies even painting, autumn is growing ever deeper, dyeing the trees of the village of Aya. Gradually arriving before the gate of the Palace of Brocade, they prostrated themselves in worship. Appearing before the presence of the two pillars of deities, Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, and once more seeking the divine decree, they borrowed the mouth of Kototobawake no Mizu no Mikoto, and with quiet words proclaimed: 'You shall depart from this sacred land earlier than even a single day, facing south to the Seto sea, parting the waves, and crossing over to the divine isle of Riūkiū.' Hearing the leaves of words proclaimed by her, reverently receiving them, the pair of goddesses prostrated themselves in worship before the Palace of Brocade, tracing the stream of Kogumo upstream,

Urging their way along the mountain path, tracing the narrow valley roads of Takasu, Yamaga, Otonase, and Saitobara, crossing the log bridge where the stream of Hirose widens, they advanced through Wachi, Honjō, Nakayama, Shinden, and Goma no Sato. Seeking onward to the Tono-daigawa, borne joyfully across Funaoka, their destination is Chizuma, Sogaya, the flower-hach of Sonobe, leaving Koyama and Matsubara behind. Though they had no wings, the station of Toba; with roads wide at Hirose and the town of Yagi, crossing back and forth across the deep riverseki, Chiyogawa's great rivers and small rivers, with the divine might rejoicing at Ooimura, Amada Kintoku rejoicing, deep within the mountains of Anoo where Tamateruhiko was born, they climbed toward Takakuma.

Ah, Kannagara kami kami! May your august spirits flourish! The divine mountain where the morning sun strikes straight, the divine peak where the evening sun shines bright, beneath the azaleas of three leaves where a thousand ryo of small gold pieces were buried, the golden rooster announcing the dawn in the age of the gods, amidst the pine forest. In the autumn wind that blew by chance, the tips of the trees themselves playing subtle music, with the voices of small birds singing pure, the red faces of wild persimmons here and there dangling—Terukohime, as if seeing the august figure of Tamateruhiko even now; Kiyokohime, whose divine throne is also pure, crept secretly into the cavern, and for twenty-one days of autumn's long nights, minutely taught and admonished by the divine decree of Konohanahime,

Worshipping the deep august virtues, descending the mountain and stepping swiftly down the valley path, the gods of the mountain, once again washing their mitama beside the waterfalls where the sound of water is loud, escorted by Namatsumazurushikami, the Sazaigayama dark as if the great sky of towering pines were sealed. Ah, the valor of climbing out of breath! Bathing in the light of the thirty-fifth moon overhead, dividing the rugged mountain paths of Rokkagaya, Inukai, Hakui, and Yuyagatani, Sasayoromizaka and Hosonogawa, crossing Nakagawara once more, passing right through the village of Kibe, the deeply emotional Ikeda-gō, Kami-dadaisuke, and Kuwazu village.

§ 2

Crossing the village of Ashiya and Itami, Striking shins at Inano and Tsuneyoshi, Wounded by autumn pampas grass, Often resting at Shibano village, The sun is already in the sky over Nishinomiya, Staying here for one night, Setting out together with the morning sun, Without the grief of hurting reeds, Advancing safely thanks to the Ōkami, Truly prospering Honjō and Mikage-chō, In the forest of Ikuta, famously renowned, Enshrined by Wakahimegimi, The divine residence of Hime of Tamano, Spending one night here until dawn, Komahiko's heart is also emboldened, Being treated with great kindness and hospitality, Proceeding onward to the port of Hyōgo, The new boat moored at the beach, Was bought by paying the price, Truly upon the surface of the sea of Akashi.

Crossing theura of Nami Takasago, Ieshima, Nishijima, Shōdoshima, Looking out to the left at Toyono-shima, Close beside the Kojima Peninsula, Just as they advance, upon a reef, The boat is stranded and both persons, Wondering what to do, with hearts of Murakimo, Suffering in their minds, at that very moment, While parting the moonlit waves, Hastening toward this direction, Rescued by a single boat, Here the two Himegami, Saved from a dangerous place, According to the will of Kami, upon the rocky shore where Ishimatsu thrives of Riugū, Mooring the boat and landing ashore, Along the mountain path where strawberries ripen, A party of four men and women together, To the dwelling place of old man Tsunekuna, To the auspicious person, of Ōtsuki, They arrived at the natural hotel.
