Chapter 37: Dance Melody of the Heavenly Maiden [137]

天女の舞曲〔137〕

§ 1

Standing in the midst of the great banquet in the grand hall of Yaata, Asahihime waved her long sleeves most gracefully as she danced, and began to sing from her own lips. That song ran:

"The morning sun rises in abundant glory, The divine light shines ever closer, The world ruled by Kiyoteruhiko Shall endure eternally; Though the trees and grasses of Mount Chōkō are fragrant And the flowers bloom, Though the voices of the hundred birds sing peacefully, There are clouds to the moon and wind to the blossoms; Enticed by the cold tempest Blown from the land of Tokoyo, The trees and grasses at the foot of the mountain and in the valley bottoms fall, The flowers scatter, And gods and mortals weep and cry out all at once. Those voices will soon resound Above the mountain ridge of Chōkō, And it will become a world of eternal darkness. O Kiyoteruhiko-no-Kami of pure mind, Take heed around your own person; Reflect upon and behold in the Masumi mirror The antics of horned Ushikuma and Onimaru and the others. The tempest will soon rage violently, And the flames will presently flare up. Take heed, Kiyoteruhiko! For if it becomes eternal darkness, Mount Chōkō too—Massehime, The messenger of Kuninaohime Who illumines the degenerated latter age, Asahi-no-Myōjin is right here. Drawing and brandishing the sword must be done now; Breaking their horns and chastising them is for this very moment. Ōmichiwake and Yashimahime Wait in attendance behind. Hurry, hurry, come, make haste! A moment's hesitation will leave A thousand years of regret; come, be swift!"

Upon hearing Asahihime's allegorical song, Kiyoteruhiko ordered his retainers to bind Ushikumawake on the spot; however, even the formidable Ushikumawake had indulged in heavy drinking and could not move freely, so he was easily surrounded from front and back, left and right by the subordinate divine officials and bound. At this moment, a war cry was heard at the foot of the mountain. Kiyoteruhiko sprang to his feet and said:

"It must be an attack by rebels! Divine generals, equip yourselves with armor and head out to defend!"

Thus he commanded, but they were dead drunk, having lost their senses, and driven solely by the terror of a sudden surprise, merely ran about in all directions within the castle. There was not a single brave warrior who headed for the battlefield.

「反逆者の襲来であろう。神将らは武装を整えて防戦に向かえ」

と命令を下したが、酒に酔いつぶれて正体を失い、ただただ寝耳に水の恐怖心にかられて、右往左往に城内を走り回るばかりであった。一人として戦場に向かう勇者はいなかった。

§ 2

At that very moment, Onimaru stood at the front of the battle line, leading a multitude of demonic troops, and broke into the castle,

"Onimaru is here, to present himself before Kiyoteruhiko no Mikoto. I am indeed a senior retainer of Tokoyo-no-kuni; Onimaru is a temporary name to conceal myself from the world, and in truth I am Takatorawake, a spy of Yatsuō Daijin. Disguising myself in order to occupy Chōkōzan and colluding with Ushikumawake, my plot has been to overturn this main castle from its very foundations; heavenly fortune has now rotated here, and the dawn of fulfilling my long-cherished ambition has arrived. Kiyoteruhiko, with no hope left, will you surrender this main castle and submit to the country of Tokoyo, or will you commit seppuku on this spot? What is your answer?"

Thus, as he attacked toward the inner palace like a raging Ashura King, Kiyoteruhiko and Masehime fitted arrows to their powerful bows, stepped forward to face Takatorawake, and just as they were about to loose an arrow, how it happened is not known, but both bowstrings snapped cleanly, rendering both bows useless; and just when the husband and wife, driven to an impasse, were already left with no choice but to commit seppuku, Asahihime dragged Ushikumawake forward while still bound, leveled a dagger at Ushikumawake's chest, and facing Onimaru, said,

"If you attempt to inflict harm upon my lord, I will stab your master to death right now."

Glaring at him thus, Takatorawake remained standing like a Niō and gnashed his teeth, and just as he was sighing with no way to take action, numerous footsteps were suddenly heard from the front. At the very instant Onimaru casually glanced behind him, Asahihime, faster than unsheathing a dagger, thrust it into Onimaru's chest; Onimaru let out a single cry of "Ah!", collapsed on the spot, and breathed his last.

と阿修羅王が荒れ狂うかのように、奥殿を目がけて攻め寄せて来るのを、清照彦と末世姫は、強弓に矢をつがえ、立ち出て鷹虎別に向かい、一矢を放とうとしたまさにその時、どうしたことか、弓弦はぷつりと切れ落ちて双方とも役に立たず、進退窮まって夫婦はもはや切腹を余儀なくされていたまさにその時、旭姫は牛熊別を縛られたままその前に引き出して来て、短刀を牛熊別の胸に突きつけ、鬼丸に向かって、

「お前が我が主に向かって危害を加えようとするならば、妾は今すぐお前の主を刺し殺そう」

§ 3

Having thoroughly battered and distressed Onimaru's subordinates who had pressed upon the foot of the mountain, scattering the enemy in all directions and riding the momentum of victory to ascend the mountain, the heroic figure of Ōmichiwake, who had entered to rescue the castle in its dire peril, now appeared in this place and, letting out a loud voice like a bell, announced the particulars of the divine assistance.

Princess Asahi was greatly delighted, and proceeding into the interior, related the particulars of the victory to Kiyoteruhiko and Princess Mase, and also related the achievements of Ōmichiwake one by one. Kiyoteruhiko immediately granted an audience to Ōmichiwake, thanked him for his meritorious deeds, and offered to immediately cast aside his own position and yield it to Ōmichiwake and Princess Yashima, and,

"We, as husband and wife, shall serve for a long time as your vassals,"

he urged repeatedly, showing his sincere heart upon his face.

Caught entirely unawares, Ōmichiwake felt greatly troubled; he immediately feigned deafness and muteness, mimicked idiocy, and dismissed Kiyoteruhiko's words completely as wind past a horse's ears. Princess Asahi spoke emphatically, declaring that Michihiko possessed merely useless physical strength and was an idiot devoid of any ability to govern, so Kiyoteruhiko had no choice but to abandon the idea.

From this point, Kiyoteruhiko selected upright divine persons from the lower ranks within his domain and appointed them to heavy responsibilities, whereafter not a single disturbance occurred; Nagatakeyama, true to its name, saw the world prosper for a long time and the divine virtue shine high and far across the four directions, so that the evil deities of Tokoyo finally vanished completely.

Ōmichiwake vanished like smoke from Nagatakeyama, and Princess Yashima likewise concealed herself before anyone knew it. The divinely inscrutable actions of the two who appeared like clouds and vanished like mist, as well as the wondrous miracles of the transformations and appearances of the two white foxes, Takakura and Asahi, will become clear through the tales to follow.

旭姫は大いに喜び、奥へ進み入って清照彦と末世姫に戦勝の次第を語り、かつ大道別の功績を逐一語った。清照彦はただちに大道別と引見し、その勲功に感謝し、ただちに自分の地位を捨てて大道別と八島姫に譲り、かつ、

「私たち夫婦は、貴殿の従臣として永く奉仕いたしましょう」

と真心を顔に表して、しきりに勧めた。

予想外の事態に大道別は、大いに迷惑を感じ、直ちに偽りの聾唖を装い、痴呆の真似をして清照彦の言葉を馬耳東風と葬り去った。旭姫は言葉を尽くして、道彦は腕力のみが無駄に強くて、治世の能力のない痴呆者であることを言明したので、清照彦はやむを得ずこれを断念した。

これより清照彦は、領内の正しい神人を下級から選抜し、重任に就かせたので、その後は一度の紛擾も起こらず、長高山はその名のように、世は長く栄え、神徳は高く四方に輝きわたり、常世の邪神はついにその影を没した。

大道別は長高山から煙のように消え失せ、八島姫もまたいつの間にか姿を隠した。雲のように現れ、霞のように消え去った二人の神変不可測の行動や、高倉と旭の二匹の白狐の変現出没の神妙な奇跡は、今後の物語によって判明することであろう。

『吾ら夫妻は、貴下の従臣として永く奉仕せむ』

と赤心を面に表はして、しきりに慫慂したりける。

案に相違の大道別は、大いに迷惑を感じ、直ちに偽の聾唖を装ひ、痴呆を真似て清照彦の言を馬耳東風と葬り去りぬ。旭姫は口をきはめて道彦の力量のみ徒に強くして、治世の能力なき痴呆者たる事を宣明したれば、清照彦は止むを得ずこれを断念したりけり。

これより清照彦は、領内の正しき神人を下級より選抜し、重任に就かしめたりしより、その後は一回の紛擾もおこらず、長高山はその名のごとく、世は長く栄え神徳は高く四方に輝きわたり、常世の邪神はつひにその影を没したりける。

大道別は長高山を煙のごとく消え失せ、八島姫もまた何時のまにか、姿を隠したりける。雲のごとく現はれ、霞のごとく消え去りし二人の神変不可測の行動、高倉、旭の二白狐の変現出没の神妙奇蹟は、今後の物語りによつて判明するならむ。
