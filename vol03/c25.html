Destruction of the Evil Spirits

第二五章　邪神の滅亡〔一二五〕
§ 1: Tsukiyohime's Malice and the False Charge of Seduction

Unto Hachiō Deity Azumawake and Azumahime were born three daughters: Tsukiyohime, Tsuiteruhime, and Satsukihime. Tsukiyohime, the eldest daughter, was violent, malicious, and rebellious by nature; constantly associating with hordes of wicked demons, she allied herself with evil spirits and devoted all her energies unto plunging Mount Takoma into perpetual chaos. In contrast, Hidehiko, a faithful and righteous chief minister of Azumawake, lamented her ever-worsening atrocities and repeatedly remonstrated with her in tears. Yet Tsukiyohime refused to heed a single syllable; consumed by bitter hatred, she resolved to frame Hidehiko with false charges and eliminate him. Using every cunning device, she corrupted several officials within the fortress of Mount Takoma, drawing Ichimorihime, Hatanohiko, Tanagahiko, Kunahirawake, and Takeyohiko into her wicked cabal. Conspiring day and night to bring ruin upon Hidehiko, she instructed a beautiful maiden named Hōkahime to execute a treacherous trap. One day, as Hidehiko prepared to depart as an official envoy unto Ryūgū Castle under the sacred command of the Hachiō Deity, Hōkahime suddenly blocked the castle gate. Clutched tightly unto his sleeve to restrain him, she gazed into his eyes and cried: 'Why hast thou deceived me all this time, cruel man? Alas, the bitter shame and grief!' Trembling from head to toe, she wept aloud in choking agony. Hidehiko could not comprehend her bizarre accusations in the slightest, gazing upon her in utter bewilderment. Suddenly, Hōkahime threw her arms around him, wailing desperately: 'O heartless and treacherous husband! How canst thou feign such innocent ignorance? Was I merely a plaything for thy lust? Hast thou forgotten our sacred vow to cross the Mountain of Death and the River of Hades together? To abandon me without a single word of farewell and depart on an envoy's mission unto Ryūgū Castle is cruelty beyond measure! O bitter sorrow! O hateful grief!' Gnashing her teeth, she threw herself violently upon the ground, screaming at the top of her lungs. Confronted with this inexplicable scene and completely unaware of Tsukiyohime's vile ambush, Hidehiko thought with compassionate sorrow: 'Hath poor Hōkahime lost her wits? What a pitiful soul!' Reaching down, he gently took her hand to raise and comfort her. At that very moment, Tsukiyohime sprang from her hiding place alongside her conspirators—Ichimorihime, Hatanohiko, Tanagahiko, Kunahirawake, and Takeyohiko—and shouted with a commanding voice: 'Bind the treacherous villain Hidehiko, who hath violated the sacred laws of Heaven and Earth!'

§ 2: The Divine Judgment and the Doom at the Waterfall of Takoma

At Tsukiyohime's command, Tanagahiko and his comrades seized rough hemp cords without mercy, binding Hidehiko tightly hand and foot and dragging him before Hachiō Deity Azumawake: 'We have captured the villain who hath broken the holy statutes of Heaven and Earth and defiled the maiden Hōkahime! To permit such an unrighteous criminal within this citadel would corrupt public morals and stain the august name of the Hachiō Deity! Punish him with utmost severity and banish him from the castle to root out this evil! The proof is witnessed with our own eyes!' With such deceitful eloquence they presented their slanderous accusation. Yet Hidehiko remained serene, fearless, and composed, stating with spotless dignity: 'I submit myself entirely unto the wise judgment of the Hachiō Deity.' Beyond this single declaration, he uttered not a single word in self-defense. Hachiō Deity Azumawake listened unto the accusation in solemn silence; then, without speaking a word, he rose and retreated into the inner sanctuary. Kneeling before the sacred altar, he prayed fervently for divine revelation. Receiving the celestial guidance, the Hachiō Deity summoned Hidehiko, Tsukiyohime, and all the ministers to assemble before him. Approaching Hidehiko with gentle courtesy, he apologized deeply for the wicked conduct of his eldest daughter Tsukiyohime and comforted him with tender words. Then, turning unto Tsukiyohime, he severely rebuked her treachery, issuing a stern reprimand unto the conspiring ministers as well. Through this unmistakable divine oracle, all suspicion against Hidehiko melted away like ice, and righteousness and wickedness were revealed as clearly as the sun and moon! Prostrating toward heaven and earth in deep gratitude for the complete vindication of his innocence, Hidehiko departed in high spirits on his mission unto Ryūgū Castle. Humiliated that her plot had collapsed, Tsukiyohime trembled with fury. Desperate to accomplish her goal and curse Hidehiko unto death, she took Hōkahime unto the Waterfall of Takoma, praying unto the evil dragon spirit of the pool for seven days and seven nights. Yet at the completion of her unholy rites, Hōkahime suddenly transformed into a hideous monster with six faces and eight arms, leaping into mid-air! Dense black clouds billowed across the northeastern sky, swiftly stretching unto the northwest until heaven was plunged into pitch darkness wherein neither day nor night could be distinguished. Monstrous black dragons swooped down toward Tsukiyohime's head, breathing venomous fumes and thrusting out tongues of fire to devour her! Tsukiyohime shrieked in terror: 'Art thou not the apparition of Hōkahime? I commanded thee not to torment me, but to destroy Hidehiko! Why dost thou confound thy mission and turn against thy mistress?' From within the swirling black clouds, an august voice thundered: 'Thou art truly wicked and abominable! Hōkahime hath now become an evil dragon to destroy thee! He who diggeth a pit for another falleth into it himself; the evil that proceedeth from thee returneth upon thine own head! Divine retribution faileth not by a hair's breadth; taste the fruit of thy sins!' Instantly, iron spearheads rained from the sky like a tempest, leaving no avenue of escape. Barely managing to scramble back into her inner bedchamber, she gasped for breath; but in that very instant, venomous dragons burst from the four corners of the room, turning into a roaring conflagration. Choking smoke enveloped her, raging flames shot toward the heavens, and Tsukiyohime was burned unto a crisp, meeting a miserable and instantaneous death. Tsukiyohime, who had once gloried in arrogance, was cast by divine judgment into the pitch-dark path of the underworld, subjected unto further torments in the spiritual realm. Alas, Heaven showeth no partiality! Assisting the righteous and destroying the wicked, the divine admonition of humanity reflecteth truth without distortion, like a spotless mirror. That which we must shun is evil, and that which we must revere is the eternal way of the Supreme Deities! In such an age of darkness and rebellion, God manifesteth to commence the great divine work of renovating the three realms—divine, spiritual, and physical—and establishing the heavenly paradise of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty upon earth. We who are born in the divine land and sustained by its bounty must humbly dedicate ourselves unto this sacred mission, deeply aware of our solemn responsibility to repay the boundless grace of Heaven!

と下知の声とともに、田長彦らは何の容赦もなく荒縄おつとり、手足をしばりて八王神の御前にまかりいで、 『天地の律法を破り、芳香姫を玩弄せし曲者捕へたり。かかる曲者を城内に止めおくは、風儀を乱し秩序を破るの恐れあるのみならず、八王神の御名の汚れなり。すみやかに厳罰に処し、当城を追放し禍根を絶ちたまへ。証拠は吾ら数名の実地目撃せるところなり』 と言辞たくみに無実の奏上をなしたりしが、日出彦は、神色自若として恐るる心なく、 『ただただ賢明なる八王神の御裁断を請ひ奉る』 と言ひしまま、一言も発せざりける。 八王神は、黙然としてこの訴へを聞きつつありしが、何の言葉もなく、ツト立ちて宮殿の奥深く入りたまひ、ただちに神前に端坐して神教を請ひたまひ、日出彦、月世姫その他一同を召し出し列を作らしめ、日出彦にむかつて叮嚀に言葉をかけ、吾が長女月世姫の不都合きはまる行動を謝し、かつ、懇ろにこれを慰撫したまひ、月世姫に向つて今後を戒め、その他の神人にも厳しく訓戒したまひ、ここにいよいよ神示によりて日出彦の疑ひは氷解され、正邪の判断は、日月のごとく明らかとなりにける。 日出彦は無実の疑ひ、まつたく神の明白なる教示によりて晴れわたりたれば、天地にむかつて拝謝し、急ぎ竜宮城さして出発したりける。 月世姫は謀計のガラリはづれたるに失望し、いかにもして初志を達せむと、蛸間の滝に芳香姫をともなひ、悪竜の神に七日七夜祈願を籠めけるが、芳香姫は忽然として六面八臂の邪鬼と変じ、中空に駆けのぼるよと見るまに、東北の天に怪しき雲塊あらはれ、たちまち西北の空にむかつて延長し、昼夜を弁ぜざる常暗の空となり、あまたの黒竜は月世姫の頭上目がけて降りきたり、盛ンに毒気を吹きかけ、火炎の舌を出して月世姫を喰ひ殺さむとす。このとき月世姫は声をあげていふ。 『汝は芳香姫の変化にあらずや。妾の汝に命ずるところは、妾を苦しめよとには非ず。日出彦を悩ませ滅亡せしめむがためなり。何をまちがへてかかる反対的行動をとるや』 と絶叫したりしに、その時黒雲の間より声ありて、 『汝は実に悪逆無道なり。芳香姫は今や悪竜となりて汝を滅ぼさむとす。他を呪はば穴二つ、自己に出るものは自己に還る。天の賞罰は、寸毫もたがふことなし、思ひ知れや』 と言葉の下より鎗の穂尖は雨の降るがごとく、危険身にせまりて寸毫も免るるの余地なかりけるが、辛うじてわが居室に逃げいりホツト一息つぐ間もなく、家の四隅より毒竜あまた出現して瞬くうちに火炎と化し、烟は身辺をつつみ、猛火炎々として天に冲し、月世姫は黒焦となりて敢なくも亡びける。今まで稍さえたる月世姫も、神の懲罰によりて暗路をたどり幽界のふたたび刑罰を受くるの破目に陥りしなり。アヽ天地に依怙なし、善を助け悪を亡ぼし、世界の神人を戒めたまふこと、実に明鏡の物を写して余蘊なきがごとし。慎むべきは悪事にして、恐るべきは天地神明の大道なり。神はかかる暗黒無道の世に出現して、神、幽、現の三界の立替立直しの神業を開始し、真善美の天国を地上に樹立したまはむとす。神の国に生れ、神の国の粟を食む神の子孫たる吾人は、つつしみて神業に奉仕し、神恩に報いたてまつるべき責任の重大なるを深く自覚すべきなり。
