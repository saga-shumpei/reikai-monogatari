Volume 3 • Part 6: Mount Seiun

The Fox's Tail

第二一章　狐の尻尾〔一二一〕
§ 1: The Seduction of Mount Himalaya and the Counterfeit Silver Jewel

Upon Mount Himalaya, the pure silver jewel was consecrated as the guardian soul of the land within the Palace of Silver. Takayamahiko was appointed Hachiō Deity, assisted by his wife Takayamahime, while Himarayahiko served as Yatsugashira Deity alongside his wife Himarayahime, maintaining peaceful governance throughout the realm. Tamakuniwake served faithfully as Chief Priest of the Palace of Silver. Now, Tokoyohiko of the Land of Tokoyo plotted to steal this silver jewel as well, dispatching his retainers Taketorahiko, Taketorahime, and Takeyoribetsu with subtle stratagems. Day and night, Taketorahiko hovered around the sanctuary under pretext of worship. Sensing danger, Tamakuniwake secretly crafted an identical mock jewel to place upon the altar. Meanwhile, the spies approached Tamakuniwake's wife, Kunikahahime, showering her with rare gifts and honeyed flattery until she fell entirely under their sway. One day, Taketorahiko whispered tempting counsel into her ear: 'If thou dost persuade thy husband Tamakuniwake to steal the silver jewel and present it unto our Lord Tokoyohiko, our sovereign will appoint you both as Hachiō Deity of Mount Himalaya! Consider the young pine sapling growing beneath the shade of a giant tree: it remaineth forever stunted, thin in trunk and sparse in needles. But the pine standing in open sunshine groweth mighty with towering branches piercing the sky! Man hath but one lifetime, yet a name endureth for eternity. Rouse thyself, seize the silver jewel, and achieve glorious renown!' Dazzled by rare gems and promises of glory, Kunikahahime consented with joy, promising to win her husband's compliance. Returning home, she urged Tamakuniwake to steal the sacred soul. Tamakuniwake was deeply shocked by his wife's treachery, but remembering divine guidance, he feigned agreement to outwit the enemies: 'There must be profound reason behind this. For the sake of my beloved wife, I shall steal the jewel so that we may advance together!' Kunikahahime hurried to inform Taketorahiko, who rejoiced that his ambition was at hand. Arriving at Tamakuniwake's residence under cover of darkness, the three spies were treated to a lavish feast of delicacies. Around the hour of the ox, Tamakuniwake led them unto the Palace of Silver, unlocked the shrine portal with a golden key, and emerged carrying a leaden counterfeit jewel concealed in a large hempen bag: 'I have successfully secured the jewel! Let us hasten unto the shores of the Lake of Jewels to make the delivery, lest our presence here be discovered!' Leading the way in darkness, they reached the shores of the Lake of Jewels. There, Tamakuniwake suddenly pointed at the three spies and laughed: 'Ye have disguised yourselves with great cunning; yet how disgraceful that white fox tails are dangling from behind your robes! I myself am the ancient Great Badger of Mount Himalaya, yet I never show my tail as ye do!' Stunned that their disguise had been penetrated, the three spies exclaimed: 'We are indeed white foxes of the Land of Tokoyo! Yet how doth thy tail remain completely hidden?' Thereupon, Tamakuniwake puffed up his chest in triumphant pride.

§ 2: The Badger's Triumph and the Drowning in the Lake of Jewels

Tamakuniwake stroked his chest with triumphant laughter: 'Because I stand in constant defense of the pure silver jewel, its divine virtue granteth me the power to penetrate any disguise! If ye but touch this jewel, your fox tails will instantly vanish, so that none in heaven or earth can detect you!' Hearing this, Taketorahiko could contain himself no longer: 'I beseech thee, allow us to hold this jewel!' Tamakuniwake shook his head resolutely: 'Out of the question! This sacred jewel cannot be shown unto any living soul until it is presented unto the Great Sovereign of Tokoyo!' The three spies joined their palms in desperate pleading: 'The road unto Tokoyo is long and perilous! If other deities spot our tails on the journey, we are utterly destroyed! In thy boundless mercy, let our hands touch the jewel but once!' Tamakuniwake feigned reluctant concession: 'If such be your urgent prayer, I shall grant it. Reveal your full natural forms as white foxes, leap inside this hempen sack, and touch the jewel to your hearts' content!' Instantly, all three transformed into white foxes and scrambled headlong into the bag. Quick as lightning, Tamakuniwake pulled the cord, knotted the mouth tightly, and slammed the bag with tremendous force against the rocky ground again and again! 'Aha, wicked demons, taste thy punishment! The fox boasteth of seven transformations, but it can never triumph over the eight transformations of the badger!' As the foxes shrieked in agonizing torment within the bag, Tamakuniwake bound a heavy stone unto the sack and hurled it with all his might into the abyssal depths of the Lake of Jewels! The waters parted with roaring waves, and the counterfeit jewel, together with the trapped white foxes, sank forever into the muddy depths. When word reached Tokoyohiko that his emissaries had perished at the bottom of the lake, he was convinced that the silver jewel was lost forever in the watery abyss, and all attempts to seize the treasure of Mount Himalaya came to a permanent end.

現代日本語訳
玉国別は得意満面の笑みを浮かべて答えた。「我は純銀の神宝を近くでお守りしているゆえ、その玉の神徳によっていかなる神の正体も見通すことができるのだ。貴殿らがこの玉に触れさえすれば、その狐の尻尾もたちまち消滅して誰にも見破られなくなるぞ！」 これを聞いた武寅彦らは矢も盾もたまらず、「どうか我らにその玉を持たせてくだされ！」と羨ましそうに顔を覗き込んだ。玉国別は首を横に振り、「滅相もない。この玉は常世国の八王大神に献上するまでは他見無用である」ときっぱり断った。 武寅彦らは両手を合わせ、「常世国への帰途は長く、途中で尻尾を見つかっては命がございません。お慈悲にただ一度だけでも手で触れさせてください！」と必死に歎願した。 玉国別はわざと不承不承を装って言った。「そこまで申すなら望みを叶えてやろう。三人とも白狐の完全な正体を現し、この麻袋の中へ飛び込んで自由に手を触れるがよい！」 そう言われるや否や、三神はたちまち白狐の姿へと変じ、先を争って麻袋の中へと飛び込んだ。玉国別は素早く袋の口を堅く縛り上げ、「悪神ども、思い知ったか！ 狐の七化け、いかに巧みに化けようとも、狸の八化けには遠く及ばぬわ！」と叫びながら、袋を大地へ何度も激しく叩きつけた。白狐どもは激痛に耐えかね、悲痛な叫び声を上げて命乞いをした。 玉国別は「邪神の手先の愚かな狐め、容赦はならぬ！」と言い放ち、袋に重い大石を固く縛り付けて、玉の湖の深淵へと力任せに投げ込んだ。湖水は左右に裂けて波浪が逆巻き、偽玉と白狐どもはブクブクと音を立てて湖底深く沈没したのである。 この凶報が常世国の常世彦の耳に入るや、純銀の真の国魂が白狐とともに玉の湖の底深く沈んでしまったものと固く信じ込み、これ以降、ヒマラヤ山白銀の宮の神宝を奪取しようとする邪悪なる陰謀は、完全に跡を絶ったのであった。
原文（近代文語）
『さればとよ。我は純銀の玉を近く守りをれば、その玉の徳によりて天地の間にいかなる貴き神も我が正体を見きはむるものなし。貴下らもこの玉に一度手を触れたまひなば、我らのごとくよく化け果さるべし』 と笑ひつついふ。武寅彦は矢も楯もたまらず、 『われにこの玉を持たせたまはずや』 と羨まし気に顔をのぞき、玉国別の首はいづれにふれるやを凝視しをる。玉国別はたちまち首を左右にふり、 『なかなかもつて滅相千万、この玉は常世国の八王大神に奉るまでは他見は許されぬ』 ときつぱりと刎ねつけたりければ、武寅彦らは両手を合せ、 『常世国まで帰る道はなかなか長し。万一途中にて我が尻尾を他神に発見せられなば身の一大事なり。お慈悲にただ一度わが手に触れさせたまへ』 と歎願するを、玉国別はわざと不承不承に、 『然らば望みを叶へさせむ。三人とも一度に白狐の全正体をあらはし、この麻袋に飛び込み、おのおの自由に手を触れられよ』 と言ひ放てば、ここに三人ともたちまち白狐と変じ、先を争ひて布袋に飛び込みにけり。玉国別は、手早く袋の口を締め、 『サア悪神ども思ひ知つたか。狐の七化け、如何にたくみに化けるとも、狸の八化けには叶ふまじ』 といひつつ袋を大地に幾度となく抛げつくれば、白狐は痛さに堪へかね苦しき悲しき声をあげて救ひを求めける。玉国別は、 『邪神の眷属馬鹿狐、容赦はならぬ』 といひつつ袋に重き石を縛りつけ、玉の湖の深淵へどつとばかりに投げ込みにける。たちまち湖水は左右にひらき波浪たち騒ぎ、擬玉も狐と共に、ブクブクと音をたてて湖水の底深く沈没したりける。このこと常世彦の耳に入り、純銀の国魂は玉の湖の底深く、白狐と共に沈めるものと確信されたりければ、これより白銀の宮の国魂を奪はむとする計画は、あとを絶ちにける。
