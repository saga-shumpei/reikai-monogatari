Volume 3 • Part 7: Mount Kunlun

Dark Clouds of Mount Takoma

第二四章　蛸間山の黒雲〔一二四〕
§ 1: The Enshrinement of Mount Takoma and the Strife of the Two Deities

Upon Mount Takoma, the bronze-colored National Soul was enshrined. Azumawake was appointed Hachiō Deity to preside over divine duties, wedded unto Azumahime who assisted him domestically; Kunitamawake was appointed Yatsugashira Deity, wedded unto Kunitamahime to assist in governance; Komayohiko served as chief priest, and Komayahime dedicated herself to the sacred rituals. However, upon this very same peak, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto had previously consecrated a guardian National Soul by divine decree from Earthly Takamanohara, even constructing a magnificent sanctuary. Consequently, two distinct National Souls were enshrined side by side upon a single mountain! Thereupon, the divine spirits of the two National Souls manifested their powers and fought bitterly for sovereign supremacy. Mount Takoma was perpetually shrouded in sinister storm clouds; tempestuous gales raged, torrential downpours battered the earth, uprooting ancient trees and smashing river embankments. Floods of turbid water inundated the lowlands, depriving both divine beings and mortals of peaceful dwellings. Envy and discord deepened with each passing day and month, while across the firmament, a pitch-black bridge of clouds stretched from the eastern unto the western horizon, plunging heaven and earth into darkness so impenetrable that one could not see one's own hand. Seizing upon this cosmic turmoil, countless evil phantoms that haunt the dark night swarmed across the earth; the ground shook incessantly, and an lawless realm emerged wherein even the leaves of grass spoke discord. Disastrous conflagrations, catastrophic floods, and deadly pestilences erupted everywhere, threatening divine beings and mortals with total extinction. Day and night, the Palace of Dōnō trembled violently, exhaling ominous vapors, while simultaneously the ancient Sanctuary of the National Soul detonated with deafening crashes like a hundred claps of thunder. Terror-stricken by these ceaseless portents, mortals and deities lived in trembling dread, barely surviving from dawn to dusk. Because the divine guardian spirits themselves fought for supremacy without a single day of truce, their spiritual essences possessed the presiding rulers: one spirit possessed Hachiō Deity Azumawake, while the other possessed Yatsugashira Deity Kunitamawake, inciting them unto perpetual frenzies of violence. The spirit of the earlier National Soul in particular possessed Kunitamawake, driving him to defy every order of the Hachiō Deity. Each insisted upon sovereign rights and refused to yield, clashing like cats and dogs, mutually incompatible as fire and water. The chaos deepened endlessly; divine beings and mortals groaned under agonizing misery, and the shouting voices of raging deities rose like swarms of summer flies, creating a living hell. Civil strife raged unabated, followed by widespread famines, until venomous serpents, tigers, wolves, fiends, and grotesque apparitions filled the earth. This desperate calamity reached the ears of the Divine Ancestor, Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, who delivered an august celestial oracle through the lips of Kuninaohime-no-Mikoto unto the Chief Archangel, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto: 'There are not two suns in the heavens, nor two kings upon the earth; such is the eternal law of Heaven and Earth! Having previously consecrated a National Soul upon Mount Takoma, ye failed to notify that deity and newly enshrined a second sacred jewel upon the very same mountain, thereby overturning cosmic order and sowing the seeds of civil war! Though the Hachiō and Yatsugashira deities bear no light guilt for succumbing to worldly ambition and throwing the world into turmoil, this disaster is ultimately the fruit of the administrative failure of enshrining two National Souls upon one holy mountain. Therefore, the two deities must not be punished immediately; this whole catastrophe is your supreme responsibility! By the boundless benevolence of the Kami-naobi and Ō-naobi deities, Heaven rectifieth, overlooketh, and pardoneth this trespass, holding ye guiltless for this time. Bring forth fruit worthy of repentance without delay: establish Mount Takoma as the boundary, divide the territory into north and south, assign their respective jurisdictions, suppress the rebellion, and report back unto the Divine Ancestor!' Such was the solemn oracle proclaimed from Heaven.

§ 2: The Mediation of Ōyashimahiko and Ōdaruhiko

Chief Archangel Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto was overwhelmed with awe and trembling contrition, repenting deeply of his lack of foresight and virtue; together with Ōdaruhiko, he set out with haste toward Mount Takoma. Arriving at the holy mountain, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto addressed Hachiō Deity Azumawake, while Ōdaruhiko addressed Yatsugashira Deity Kunitamawake, expounding the divine decree and conveying with deep reverence the boundless majesty of the divine benevolence. Hearing the earnest instruction of the Chief Archangel, Hachiō Deity Azumawake immediately recognized his former transgression; awed by the dread majesty of the National Soul, he prostrated himself, swearing from the bottom of his heart that he would serve the divine mission with pure sincerity and never again violate any heavenly command. In contrast, when Ōdaruhiko expounded the true path of cosmic righteousness and the awesome majesty of Heaven unto Yatsugashira Deity Kunitamawake, exhausting all words to awaken his conscience, Kunitamawake’s countenance instantly turned ferociously savage. Uttering violent blasphemies, he refused to submit; driven unto frantic madness, he lunged forward to bite at the face of the angelic envoy Ōdaruhiko! Undismayed, Ōdaruhiko knew the true nature of the beast: crossing his fingers in the sacred posture of spirit-pacification, he released a thunderous sacred kotodama cry—'Ū!' At that single divine sound, Kunitamawake was hurled backward onto the earth, writhing in agony and spewing copious foam from his lips. As the angelic envoy loosed another piercing arrow of holy kotodama, dense black smoke billowed from the body of the Yatsugashira Deity, and before their eyes the demonic form of an eight-tailed golden fox emerged from his flesh, fleeing into the western sky like a vanishing mist!

§ 3: The True Repentance of Kunitamawake and the Cleansing of the Skies

Having observed the fierce discord and jealousy unleashed between the guardian deities, the demonic spirits of Tokoyo took cunning advantage of their delusions of greed, anger, and ignorance, successfully possessing the Yatsugashira Deity. Their wicked scheme was to overthrow the Hachiō Deity, bring ruin upon the Yatsugashira Deity, and plunge all Mount Takoma into utter chaos. Now that the wicked golden fox had been driven out, Yatsugashira Deity Kunitamawake awakened unto his true senses. Weeping before the angelic envoy Ōdaruhiko, he confessed his past insolence: 'Because my spiritual foundation was weak and my resolve feeble, I became a vessel for evil spirits, scorning the gods, insulting my superiors, and violating the sacred statutes of Heaven and Earth!' Prostrating himself in deep contrition, he begged to receive whatever severe punishment was due. Thereupon, radiant with a compassionate smile, Ōdaruhiko gently spoke: 'Enshrining a second National Soul upon a holy mountain where one was already consecrated was the blunder of the Chief Archangel and our presiding council; therefore, the responsibility resteth equally upon our shoulders. Yet the merciful Supreme Deity hath displayed boundless magnanimity regarding this crisis, rectifying, overlooking, and pardoning all our transgressions through the grace of the Kami-naobi and Ō-naobi deities, washing away our mutual guilt. Therefore, rest thy troubled heart in peace!' Hearing of this overwhelming divine benevolence, Kunitamawake shed rivers of grateful tears, manifesting profound repentance from the depths of his soul and pledging loyal service unto Hachiō Deity Azumawake. From that moment, heaven and earth became harmonious, rulers and subjects were united in peace, and a golden age dawned wherein not even a branch was shaken by tempestuous winds. The pitch-black clouds that had stretched across the firmament like a sinister bridge rapidly dispersed and melted away as if wiped clean; the heavens shone radiant, the earth was purified, and an earthly paradise of joy and harmony manifested in glory! Henceforth, using Mount Takoma as the boundary, the two National Souls were separately consecrated: the northern realm was placed under Hachiō Deity Azumawake, and the southern realm under Yatsugashira Deity Kunitamawake. Thus, from the previous autocratic order of divine kingship, the noble dawn of democratic divine governance was gloriously inaugurated!

国魂の神の嫉妬が燃え上がった狂乱の隙を洞察した常世国の邪神どもは、貪り・怒り・愚痴という三毒の迷いに付け入り、巧みに八頭神の体内に憑依してその野望を遂げようとしたのである。邪神らは八王神を打ち倒し、八頭神をも失脚させて蛸間山全域を混乱に陥れようと画策していたのであった。しかし悪狐が追い払われたことで八頭神・国玉別はようやく迷妄から覚醒し、天使大足彦（Ōdaruhiko）に向かってこれまでの数々の無礼を深く詫びた。「己の精神が脆弱で意志が弱かったばかりに、ついに邪神の器となってしまい、天地の神を無視し、長上を侮蔑し、天地の律法を破ってしまいました」とその大罪を心から悔恨し、地にひれ伏してどのような処罰も受ける覚悟を申し出たのである。 これを聞いた大足彦（Ōdaruhiko）は、満面に温かな慈愛の笑みを浮かべて次のように語り聞かされた。 「国魂の神がすでに鎮祭されている霊山に、重ねて再び国魂の神を奉斎してしまったのは、天使長をはじめとする我ら指導陣の経綸の失策による結果であり、その責任は我らにも同様にあるのだ。しかしながら慈悲深き国祖の大神は、今回の混乱に対して広大なる大御心を垂れさせ給い、神直日・大直日の神徳によって見直し、聞き直し、宣り直し給うて、我ら双方の重き罪をすべて赦し、忘れてくださったのである。どうか安心して心を安らかに保たれるがよい」 と諭された。国玉別はこの深重なる神恩の尊さと忝さに打たれ、涙を滝のように流して衷心より改悛し、以後は八王神・吾妻別に忠誠を尽くして仕えるようになった。これによって天地は和順し、上下の秩序は正しく治まり、松の神代のごとく永遠に風も枝を鳴らさぬ泰平の聖代が招来されたのである。 それまで天空に巨大な橋のように横たわっていた不気味な黒雲は、たちまち四方に散乱して拭い去ったように消滅し、天は晴れわたり地は清まり、神人と民が和楽して歓喜する地上極楽の浄土が現出した。これより二つの国魂は蛸間山を境として南北に分けて祀られ、国土は二分されて北方は八王神・吾妻別が治め、南方は八頭神・国玉別が主管することとなったのである。専制的な君主的神政の経綸から、ここにいよいよ公平なる民主的神政の端緒が開かれたことは、実に意義深く目出度いことであった。
原文（近代文語）
国魂神の嫉妬的発動の狂態を洞察したる常世国の邪神は、貪・瞋・痴の迷につけ入り、たちまち憑依の目的を達し、進ンで八王神を倒し、八頭神を失墜せしめ、蛸間山を攪乱せむとしゐたりしが、八頭神は始めて覚醒し、天使にむかつて以前の無礼を謝し、我が精神空弱にして意志強からず、つひに邪神の容器となり、神を無視し長上を侮蔑し、天地の律法を破りたる大罪を悔い、低頭平身罪に処せられむことを願ひけり。大足彦は、 『国魂の神ある上に重ねて再び、国魂の神を斎りたるは天使長以下の経綸を誤りたる結果なれば、その責任は吾らも同様なり。されど仁慈ふかき大神は、この度の事件に関しては寛大なる大御心をもつて、神直日大直日に見直し聞き直し詔り直したまひて、吾らがたがひの大罪を忘れさせたまひたり。心安く思召されよ』 と満面笑をうかべて宣り聞かせたるに、国玉別は神恩の尊く忝なさに涙を滝のごとく流し、衷心より改悛の情をあらはし、八王神に忠実に仕へける。それより天地和順し上下よく治まりて、松の神代の常永に時津風枝も鳴らさぬ聖代を招来したりける。 今まで天空に橋状をなして横たはりし黒雲は、次第に散乱して拭ふがごとく、天明らけく地清く、神人和楽の極楽浄土を現出したるぞ目出度けれ。これより二個の国魂を南北に分ち祭られ、国土を二分して、北方は八王神吾妻別これを主管し、南方は八頭神国玉別これを主管することとなりぬ。君主的神政の神界の経綸も、ここにいよいよ民主的神政の端を啓かれたるぞ是非なけれ。
