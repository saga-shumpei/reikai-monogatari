Volume 3 • Part 7: Mount Kunlun

A Single Cry of the Crane

第二三章　鶴の一声〔一二三〕
§ 1: The Earthly Paradise of Mount Kunlun and Iwatamahiko's Conscience

Upon Mount Kunlun, the sacred Crimson National Soul was enshrined with profound reverence within the newly constructed Sanctuary of Kōnō. Iwatamahiko was appointed to the sacred office of Hachiō Deity, assisted in divine duties by his wife Iwatamahime, while Ōshimahiko was appointed Yatsugashira Deity, supported in administration by his wife Ōshimahime. The deities Akiyohiko and Akiyohime served faithfully as presiding priests of the Sanctuary of Kōnō, and the entire domain of Mount Kunlun was governed in profound peace and tranquility. Iwatamahiko was a deity detached from worldly fame or selfish gain, endowed with peerless loyalty. Dedicating all precious gems and rare treasures unto Earthly Takamanohara, he humbled himself and cherished the multitude of his subjects with compassionate benevolence. Under his virtuous governance, the four quarters prospered in harmony; the people drummed upon their bellies and stamped upon the earth in rejoicing, divine beings and mortals happily pursued their callings, and a miniature model of the Messianic earthly paradise was gloriously established. On account of this supreme virtue, the sun and moon shone with crystalline radiance in the heavens, not a single trace of foul vapor lingered in the azure firmament, the seasonal rains and gentle breezes maintained their cosmic harmony, lush trees and grasses flourished upon the fertile earth, the five grains ripened in abundance, birds sang and blossoms smiled, and throughout all four seasons a gentle spring warmth pervaded the land, so that Mount Kunlun was envied across the earth as the foremost paradise of creation. Yet in the midst of this earthly paradise, Iwatamahiko's soul was torn by profound pangs of conscience: 'The realm is governed in sublime tranquility; divine beings and mortals cultivate their own fields to eat and dig their own wells to drink; not a single voice of anger or discontent is heard within any household, and all live in delightful harmony. In such an auspicious age of peace, the strict statutes of Heaven and Earth have become mere dead letters, and the sacred office of Hachiō Deity appeareth to be nothing more than a useless vanity. To occupy such an exalted throne day and night, tormented by idleness while enjoying unearned ease, filleth my heart with unbearable shame before the Supreme Deities of Heaven and Earth! I must promptly resign the holy office of Hachiō Deity and descend among the common people, to labor and rejoice together in divine work alongside all mortals!' Having reached this firm resolve, he secretly summoned Yatsugashira Deity Ōshimahiko into his presence and revealed his inner intention. Ōshimahiko was struck with utter astonishment upon hearing these words. For a time he remained speechless, gazing upon the face of the Hachiō Deity; then, shaking his head vehemently, tears streamed down his cheeks as he earnestly remonstrated: 'The sublime peace and auspicious prosperity enjoyed across Mount Kunlun today are solely the fruit of your exalted divine virtue! We, your subordinate divine ministers, together with the countless deities and mortals below, revere your sacred presence and can never cease our boundless gratitude! If your divinity should abandon this holy throne and descend into the common fields, what other divine minister could possibly govern this great realm? To revere superiors, cherish inferiors, and preserve the peace of the commonwealth is the heaven-sent mission of a true sage! I beseech you, by your great mercy and compassion, abandon this thought of resignation!' With tears and choking cries, Ōshimahiko pleaded with all his heart; yet Iwatamahiko answered him with unwavering solemnity.

§ 2: The Direct Appeal of Ōshimahiko and the Oracle of the Crane

'As Hachiō Deity, I was graciously entrusted with this exalted office by the Great Deities of Takamanohara; yet unto this day I have accomplished no notable merit in the realm. Whenever I reflect upon the boundless depth of this divine benevolence day and night, painful remorse overwhelmeth my heart, granting me not a single hour of peace. While the divine subjects and mortals below labor unceasingly in divine duty, dripping with sweat and toil, I dwell in idle luxury within the inner palace shrouded in mist, achieving nothing of lasting value. To squander my fleeting days without fulfilling my duties filleth me with awe and trembling before the Supreme Deities of Heaven and Earth! Today’s sublime peace is solely the precious fruit of the devotion and toil of faithful ministers like thyself. I beseech thee, grant my earnest plea: ascend thou immediately unto the exalted seat of Hachiō Deity and govern all divine administration in my stead! As for myself, my wife and I shall retreat unto the deep mountains, donning the simple robes of wandering ascetics to pray unceasingly unto Heaven and guard the eternal peace of the divine realm! When a noble soul hath once formed a firm resolve, no remonstrance or restraint can alter his mind!' The unyielding resolve upon his countenance was firm as stone, impossible to sway. Knowing full well that the Hachiō Deity was naturally selfless, pure, and detached like crystalline water, Ōshimahiko realized that mere words would be powerless. After remaining submerged in deep sorrow and silence, he resolved to speak: 'Your pure and upright conscience is something that both gods and mortals admire without ceasing. No matter how passionately we may plead, we know well that you will not easily renounce your noble purpose. Yet your sacred spirit and person are not a private possession to be cast aside at will! By the eternal ties established in the distant age of the gods, the inviolable order of sovereign and subject hath been ordained, springing directly from the bountiful commission of the Supreme Deities of Heaven and Earth. Let us hasten immediately unto Earthly Takamanohara to receive a sacred divine decree, and we shall report the divine will unto you in full detail. In the meantime, I beseech you, leave all matters entirely in our hands!' Hearing this fervent and solemn plea, Iwatamahiko conceded: 'Thy words are sound and righteous. I entrust all matters unto thy care; manage them as thou deemest best.' With these words, he withdrew into the inner sanctum. Mounting the Heavenly Rock-Boat with his attendants, Ōshimahiko sliced through the celestial gales with the speed of lightning, arriving at Ryūgū Castle. Requesting an audience with Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto, he presented an earnest petition for a divine ruling regarding the Hachiō Deity’s resignation. Recognizing the gravity of the crisis, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto hastened directly unto the Great Sanctuary of Earthly Takamanohara, appearing before Kuninaohime-no-Mikoto to recount every detail and implore a celestial ordinance. Purifying her garments and body, Kuninaohime-no-Mikoto stepped into the inner sanctuary of the Great Shrine and reverently besought the will of Heaven. Calling Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto before her, she proclaimed the divine decree with august solemnity: 'By virtue of sacred bonds established in the primeval age of the gods, Iwatamahiko hath received the holy office of Hachiō Deity upon Mount Kunlun; this is the immutable, eternal ordinance of the divine realm! A lord must eternally remain a lord, and a subject must eternally remain a subject throughout all generations! For a sovereign to degrade himself into a subject, or for a subject to leap unto the sovereign throne, violateth the fundamental truth of Heaven and Earth and contemptuously disregardeth the sovereign will of the Supreme Deity! Once a divine decree hath gone forth, it can never be altered! The divine word is as luminous as the sun and moon, and cannot be violated in the slightest! Moreover, sacred rank and holy office are righteous desires bestowed by Heaven, an indispensable dignity upon the heads of rulers. Why doth Iwatamahiko bring forth such an obvious question, thereby troubling the serene mind of the Supreme Deity? Whether in life or in death, all belongeth unto the divine will! Let him reflect and repent of his foolish error without an instant’s delay, and faithfully fulfill his sacred duties as Hachiō Deity according to the divine decree! Never again let him bring forth such frivolous doubts to trouble the heart of Heaven!' Receiving this stern oracle with profound reverence, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto offered grateful homage, summoned Ōshimahiko, and expounded the divine decree unto him in thorough detail. Overjoyed, Ōshimahiko flew back unto Mount Kunlun upon the Heavenly Rock-Boat and presented the heavenly proclamation before Iwatamahiko. Naturally upright, truthful, and righteous, Iwatamahiko stood in awe of the supreme oracle, repented deeply of his former delusion, and resumed his sacred throne as Hachiō Deity. For several centuries thereafter, Mount Kunlun enjoyed unbroken peace, tranquility, and celestial bliss. From this episode, mortals may comprehend how inviolable and august is the supreme command of Heaven!

大島彦は大いに歓喜し、急ぎ天の磐船で崑崙山へと飛んで帰り、八王神・磐玉彦の御前に平伏して、仰ぎ受けた神示の一切を恭しく復命奏上したのである。もとより正義を愛し至誠純直なる磐玉彦は、この至高なる神勅を畏れ敬い、己の前非を深く悔悟して、再び元の八王神の聖職に復帰された。その後数百年の長きにわたり、崑崙山は真に至治至楽の泰平なる聖代を謳歌し続けたのである。神命の犯すべからざる尊厳は、これによっても深く窺い知ることができるであろう。
原文（近代文語）
『我は八王神として、高天原の大神より重職を忝なうし、何の功労もつくさず、日夜神恩の深きを思ふごとに、慙愧の念胸に迫りて苦しく、一日として心を安んずることあたはず。下神人は日夜営々兀々として神業に奉仕し、汗油をしぼりて勤勉神業を励むなる世に、吾はいたづらに雲深く殿中にありて安逸の生を送り、何の活動をもなさず、曠職いたづらに光陰を消するは、天地の大神に対したてまつり恐懼にたへず。今日の至治泰平は、要するに貴下らが誠実と苦心の賜なり。すみやかに吾の思望を許し、貴下は直ちに八王神の位＠に昇りて神務を主管されたし。吾はこれより夫妻ともに山野に隠れ、修験者となりて神明に祈り、神政の万歳を守らむ。男子たるもの一度決心したるうへは、いかなる諫言も拒止も耳にはいり難し』 と決心の色面に現はれ容易に動かすべからず。大島彦も、平素寡欲にして恬淡水のごとき八王神なれば、如何ともするに由なく、ただ黙然として深き憂に沈みゐたりしが、ヤヽありて大島彦は口をひらき、 『貴神司の潔白なる御神慮は、神人ともに常に歎賞おかざるところ、今更いかに諫めたてまつるとも、初志を翻させ給ふことなからむ。されど貴神司の身魂は貴神司の単独に処置さるべき軽々しきものに非ず、遠き神世の因縁によりて上下の名分定まり、天地の大神の優渥なる御委任に出づるものなれば、吾らはこれより直ちに地の高天原に参上がり神示を蒙りしうへ、その結果を詳しく奏上せむ。何はともあれ、それまでは何事も吾らに一任あらむことを』 と力をこめて歎願したりしに、磐玉彦は、 『貴下の言道理にかなへり、万事は一任すべし。よきやうに計らひくれよ』 と言を残して奥深く姿をかくしたまひける。 大島彦はただちに天の磐船に乗り、従者をともなひ空中風をきつて竜宮城に到着し、大八洲彦命に謁をこひ、八王神の退隠の件につき、裁断を下されむことを奏請したりける。ここに大八洲彦命は、すぐさま地の高天原の大宮にいたりて国直姫命に拝謁し、前述の次第を逐一奏上し、神勅の降下を願ひしに、国直姫命は衣服を更め、身体を清め、大神殿に進みいり、恭しく神勅を乞ひたまひ、大八洲彦命を近く召し、容姿をあらため厳かに神示のおもむきを伝達されたり。その神示の大要は、 『磐玉彦は遠き神代よりの御魂の因縁によりて、崑崙山に八王神の聖職を拝するは動かすべからざる神界の一定不変の経綸なり。君は万古君たるべく、臣はまた万古末代臣たるべし。王にして臣となり、あるひは下賤の地位に降り、臣にしてたちまち王の位＠に進むごときは、天地の真理に違反し、かつ大神の御神慮を無視するものなり。神勅一度出てはふたたびこれを更改すべからず。神の一言は日月のごとく明らかにして一毫も犯すべからず。かつ名位は神の賦与する正欲にして、長者たるものの欠くべからざる栄誉なり。磐玉彦いかなればかかる明瞭なる問題を提出して、大神の御神慮を悩ませ奉るぞ。たとへ生くるも死するもみな大神の御心のままなり。一時も早く片時もすみやかに神慮を反省し、もつて神勅のまにまに八王神の聖職を奉仕し、今後ふたたびかかる問題を提出し神慮を煩はすこと勿れ』 との厳命なりける。 大八洲彦命は神示を拝し、恭しく礼を述べ、大島彦を近く招きて、神示を詳細に諭達したまへり。 大島彦はおほいに歓び急ぎ崑崙山に飛還し、八王神に一切の神示を恭しく復命奏上したりけり。素より正義純直の八王神は、神勅を重ンじ前非を悔い、ふたたび元の聖職につき、その後数百年のあひだは、実に至治至楽、泰平の聖代は継続されたり。神命の犯すべからざるは、これにても窺ひ知らるべし。
