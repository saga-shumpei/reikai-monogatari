Chapter 30: Embracing Unified Return [130]

抱擁帰一〔130〕

§ 1

Kasugahime and Takekurawake lived in marital harmony, spending joyous days like the vast expanse of a spring day. Michitsurahime, swayed by love for her child, tacitly permitted this. Wakahiko accompanied Takasumiwake and worshipped again at the Palace of Ubatama, while Kasugahime, having finished worshipping accompanied by Haruhime, came down the steps, and the gazes of Wakahiko and Haruhime flashed toward each other like lightning. Kasugahime sharply noticed this and felt a twinge of jealousy arise, but Wakahiko perceived Haruhime's feelings toward him and immediately directed a strange gaze at Haruhime. Haruhime silently and bashfully gazed at Wakahiko's face. Kasugahime, standing before them and watching this scene, could not help but burn with the flames of jealousy all the more.

Takasumiwake, whose divine grace and stature were double those of Takekurawake, wore formal attire, smiled, and spread his arms wide to block Kasugahime's path; Kasugahime, without heed for circumstance, turned cool eyes toward Takasumiwake. From then on, the two trusted each other, lived in harmony, and began an extremely close and intimate relationship.

After that, Kasugahime's attitude toward Takekurawake changed completely and became cold. Takekurawake discovered the extremely intimate relationship between Takasumiwake and Kasugahime, was greatly enraged, led numerous retainers, and raided Takasumiwake's dwelling under cover of night to take revenge; Takasumiwake was startled by the unexpected attack and sent a swift messenger to Ōmichiwake to plead for help.

Thereupon, Ōmichiwake, intending to undertake the labor of arbitration, set out from his mansion alone, arrived at Takasumiwake's residence, and strictly ordered Takekurawake, who was surrounding the outside of the estate, to disperse immediately. At that time, Takasumiwake and Wakahiko were surrounded by Takekurawake's subordinates and had no means of doing anything. Takekurawake showed signs of hesitation and wavering at Ōmichiwake's strict command. However, the excited subordinates, unstoppable like a surging flood, raised war cries and continued shouting without ceasing, "Destroy Takasumiwake and Wakahiko!"

When Ōmichiwake addressed Heaven and recited the Amatsu Norito with pure kotodama, Takemitsuhiko no Mikoto, the spirit of the Hagun Star, immediately descended from Heaven and cast fierce spirit swords like rain onto Takekurawake's head; Takekurawake instantly turned pale, pressed his hands together, and apologized to Takemitsuhiko no Mikoto for the crime of his turbulent conduct. Takemitsuhiko no Mikoto immediately mounted a purple cloud and returned to Heaven.

Thereupon, Ōmichiwake, seeking to hold a banquet of reconciliation between both parties, invited them to a spacious hall built by the Long Bridge of Orochi, had them firmly promise each other to completely sever ties with Kasugahime, and having fully enjoyed the pleasure, each departed from the banquet at will.

春日姫と竹倉別は琴瑟相和し、春の日の洋々たるように楽しい日々を送っていた。道貫姫も子の可愛さに引かれて、これを黙許した。若彦は鷹住別に従って烏羽玉の宮に再び参拝したが、春日姫は春姫を従えて参拝を終え、階段を下りてきたところ、若彦と春姫の視線は稲妻のように互いに閃いた。春日姫は目ざとくこれを見てやや嫉妬の念が湧き起こったが、若彦は春姫の自分に対する心情を察知し、直ちに春姫に対して異様な視線を発射した。春姫は黙然として若彦の顔を恥ずかしそうに見守った。この様子を目前に立って眺めていた春日姫は、ますます嫉妬の炎を燃やさざるを得なかった。

神品骨柄において、竹倉別の倍にも勝る鷹住別は、正装のまま笑顔を作って春日姫の前に大手を広げて立ちふさがったので、春日姫は前後の分別もなく鷹住別に涼しい目を向けた。二人はこれより互いに信じ合い調和し、水も漏らさぬ親密な交際を始めた。

それ以後、春日姫の竹倉別に対する態度は打って変わって冷淡となった。竹倉別は鷹住別と春日姫のきわめて親密な関係を探知し、大いに憤り、数多くの従者を引き連れ、夜陰に乗じて鷹住別の住居を襲って仇を討とうとしたので、鷹住別は不意の襲撃に驚き、大道別に急使を走らせて救援を求めた。

ここに大道別は仲裁の労を取ろうとただ一人、館を出立して鷹住別の住居に至り、邸外を包囲する竹倉別に向かって速やかに退散するよう厳命した。このとき、鷹住別と若彦は竹倉別の部下の者たちに身辺を取り囲まれ、どうすることもできない状態であった。竹倉別は大道別の厳命に少し躊躇し逡巡する様子であった。しかし、逸り立った部下の者たちは出ばなの水の勢いのように止めることができず、鬨の声をあげて「鷹住別、若彦を滅ぼせ」と叫び続けてやまなかった。

大道別が天に向かって、天津祝詞を言霊清々しく奏上したところ、たちまち破軍星の精霊である武満彦命が天上より降りてきて、竹倉別の頭上へ猛烈な霊剣を雨のように投下されたので、竹倉別はたちまち顔色が青ざめ、合掌して武満彦命に我が行動の不穏なる罪を陳謝した。武満彦命は直ちに紫雲に乗って天にお帰りになった。

ここに大道別は両者の和睦の宴を開こうとし、大蛇の長橋のほとりに建てられた広殿に招待し、かつ互いに春日姫との手を断然切ることを固く約束し、歓を尽くして宴席を各自思い思いに退場した。

§ 2

Just as Ōmichiwake returned to the foot of Mount Kōyō together with Takasumiwake and Wakahiko, dozens of black shadows of unknown identity appeared from within the dense forest, and seizing the hands and binding the feet of Ōmichiwake, Takasumiwake, and Wakahiko, there were those who tied thick ropes to them and dragged them along the highway. This was indeed the lawless deed of the retainers under Takekurawake.

At this time, countless boulders fell from atop Mount Kōyō toward Takekurawake's group and injured several retainers; this was because Katsuhiko, the elder brother of Wakahiko who had been in Rome, seeking to save his younger brother from grave danger and foreknowing Takekurawake's plot, had been lying in wait atop this mountain. Takekurawake was daunted by Katsuhiko's courage, abandoned his subordinates, fled to Hachiō-ōkami, and finally became his military commander. From then on, it became the heyday of Takasumiwake and Kasugahime. Wakahiko finally became the husband of Haruhime and faithfully served at the Ubatama Shrine.

Between Kasugahime and Takasumiwake, a relationship like honey was forged, and Haruhime composed an impromptu song to celebrate this. The song:

"Is it the dawn of spring's third month, Haze across the mountains on all sides Shimmers far and wide, and in the spring breeze Fragrant are the blooming plums; The bush warbler that comes to sing in the treetops, Opening the valley's door with hōhokekyō, Chanting sutras with hōhokekyō, The spring of today—the sutra—loved by the monk; Clothed in garments of mist, The mountains on all sides present Haruhime's Green robes in every detail, Dressed so tranquilly; Gentle is the breeze in your spring, The evergreens in the forest of Kasuga Dyed with a thousand years of color, The cherry blossoms smile, and the autumn maples, Flushing their youthful faces, Beckon onward in your reign, Kasugahime of spring's radiant air, Takasumiwake like the thousand-year pine; Takekurawake, who parted The maiden and youth permitted by the gods, Is now parted and departs for the eternal world; Though the world be as dark as the jet-black night, The shining camellia, May the spring of eight thousand ages last forever, May the blossoms never fall, wherever it may be, May the colors never fade, throughout eternity; Though the storms of the Urals be fierce, Upon you blow the divine wind and the god of fortune, The eight-thousand-age camellia and the udumbara, Spring when flowers bloom, O Kasugahime; Your renown ascends ever higher, Climbing to look out over the plain of heaven, Gazing far into the distance, from the peak of Mount Mikasa Rises the pure moonlight of the full moon night, Unwaning, A round and refreshing household, Together with the fragrance of the garden's white plums And crimson plums, forever Be not lost, wherever it may be, Remain fragrant until the age of Miroku; Spring of blossoms, an hour worth a thousand pieces of gold, Neither aging nor dying through countless ages, The garden pine that alters not its fidelity, Just as its thousand-age greenery Flourishes in deep verdure, for eternity Change not, Kasugahime; Though the world be as dark as the jet-black night, The bond between the two is the crimson tie Of autumn maples, knotted like kelp; Though there be a lonely world where stags cry In the deep mountain of the heart, and the winter wind Blows and scatters the leaves from the trees, Growing old together in harmony, Spend the days and months, Kasugahime; Even though the mountains on all sides turn crimson And Sahohime weaves brocade, Wearing garments of mist for eternity, Peacefully beneath Mount Kōyō, Spending a joyful reign; Associated with that auspicious sign, Cheerful and auspicious Haruhime's Heart is ever Wakahiko, Keep this flourishing heart Forever continuing, Kasugahime, Together with Takasumiwake, forever."

The song thus celebrated by Haruhime also vanished like a brief dream of a spring night, and Kasugahime finally came to taste the sorrow of the broken mirror of parting, going mad, raving wildly, and falling into despair.

Who, indeed, is this Kasugahime?
