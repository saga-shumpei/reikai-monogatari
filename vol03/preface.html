Preface to Volume 3

第三巻 序文
Onii, Deguchi Onisaburo（王仁　出口王仁三郎）
§ 1

Thirty years of the Great Reconstruction (Tatekae) since the manifestation of Ushitora no Konjin (the Golden Deity of the Northeast) have at last reached the fifty-fifth year of Meiji—that is to say, the eleventh year of Taisho (1922)—attaining the long-awaited divine moment when 'the plum blossoms bloom across the three thousand worlds all at once.' Furthermore, twenty-five years since the manifestation of Hitsujisaru no Konjin (the Golden Deity of the Southwest), the divine teachings that 'the peach and plum trees say nothing, yet a path naturally forms beneath them' have also blossomed in this glorious spring of peaches. In the dawn of my fifty-second year, illuminated by the radiant light of the moon, I set forth to dictate these visionary tales of exploration throughout the Spirit World; while myriad herbs and birds cease their endless disputes, and humanity at last comprehends in rich detail the divine designs underlying the sacred dates of the Third of the Third Month (3/3) and the Fifth of the Fifth Month (5/5). Becoming thus the herald of a restored Divine Age is indeed solely due to the almighty power of the appointed Time. In the sacred automatic writing received on September 8 of the thirty-third year of Meiji (1900), it is revealed:

It is the mission of Kaichō (Onisaburo) to explain and interpret these revelations in minute and tender detail. Pushing the Kami into a corner, relying upon their own stubborn egos, and still walking about spreading malicious falsehoods—many such intermediaries and teachers will surely emerge! And so forth.'

§ 3

Just as the divine revelations affirm, the sacred writings of Oomoto can never be truly and authentically interpreted unless both the Masculine and the Feminine functions are united. Nevertheless, whenever something inconveniences the personal circumstances of an individual's personal guardian deity, there immediately arise guardians who declare, 'The writings of the Feminine must not be accepted as they are; they must undergo spiritual scrutiny (saniwa)!' How truly lamentable and troublesome this is! When on September 8 the appointed role of the Feminine arrived in exact accordance with the divine oracle, and I withdrew to dictate the tidings of the Spirit World, once again arrogant persons along the way began whispering complainingly behind the scenes. Seeing so many magnificent people appearing who presume to 'judge and examine' the inspired brushwork of the Feminine, even the Kami in heaven must surely feel bewildered and astonished!

§ 4

Furthermore, concerning the abstract divine instruction of 'March 3rd and May 5th in the fifty-fifth year of Meiji,' I hear that there are saniwa examiners who, calculating that the 55th year of Meiji corresponds to the 11th year of Taisho (1922), expect that some physical material fortune will be bestowed upon the person of the Feminine Soul, or that some sensational, worldly spectacle will occur regarding Oomoto's divine plan during this year. However, between the Will of God and the minds of human mortals, there exists an infinite abyss of difference, as between heaven and earth! By human intellect and worldly speculation alone, the profound truth of the Divine Plan can never be comprehended. The term 'the fifty-fifth year' signifies the specific year when the divine cosmic governance that has been prepared across thirty years since the twenty-fifth year of Meiji (1892) begins to manifest concretely upon the outer surface of reality.

§ 5

'The Third of the Third Month' (Mitsuki-Mitsuka) signifies the day when the manifestation of the Moon Deity—the Three Sacred Souls—shines brilliantly across the Three Realms of Heaven, Earth, and Humanity. The character for day ('hi') is read 'ka' in the sacred science of word-spirits (Kototama), and 'ka' denotes shining radiance. This divine oracle was given to foreshadow that the true spirit of the Masculine brushwork recorded over the past thirty years has at last been fully understood, and that the spiritual light of the Feminine Soul, corresponding to twenty-five years of dedicated service in the Way, is steadily dawning upon the world. That after twenty-five years of obstruction by worldly barriers, serious seekers are finally beginning to lend a sincere ear to the interpretation of the divine plan revealed through the Feminine Soul is precisely what the oracle meant by proclaiming, 'It is an auspicious day for the Feminine.' The auspicious portent of the Sun and Moon rising above the eastern sky to illuminate all realms amidst the darkness of heaven and earth is what is termed 'the Fifth of the Fifth Month' (Itsutsuki-Itsuka). In Kototama, 'Itsu' (five) signifies 'Emergence' (itsu / to come forth); thus, 'Itsutsuki-Itsuka' bears the sublime spiritual meaning of 'the Emergent Moon and the Emergent Sun' (Idetsuki-Idehi). The celestial wind of twenty-five years begins to blow today, making crystal clear the warp and woof of divine teachings, heralding the arrival of the reign of fifty-five years (the emergence of divine mind and spiritual consciousness), celebrating the cosmic moment when the profound divine will is destined to be fully revealed to the universe!

§ 6

When the moonlight emerges into the world and completely illuminates the darkness of all realms, this constitutes 'the Fifth of the Fifth Month' in the true sense of word-spirit science; it is thoroughly obvious that it never refers to mere calendar days! Because dark clouds of spiritual ignorance seemed to envelop heaven and earth everywhere—people declaring that they would abandon their faith if nothing extraordinary happened on March 3rd or May 5th—I have offered this concise explanation here. Even with this explanation, ambitious people will probably appear who argue, 'The words of the Feminine Soul must be subjected to saniwa examination!' Such is the nature of this spiritually darkened world.

Readers must comprehend that it bears the sacred meaning of rejoicing at the commencement of the grand cosmic mission of the Divine Spirit itself. Let everyone understand that bearing the atonement of the thousand tables of sin-offerings (Chikura no Okido) is the innate, Heaven-endowed divine mission of the Mizu-no-Mitama (the Feminine Soul of Mercy)!

§ 9

The fifth day of the fifth month is the festival of boys, the Festival of Iris; the third day of the third month is the festival of girls, the Festival of Peach Blossoms. For twenty-five years since the Feminine Soul appeared in the Holy Land, walking quietly like the peach and plum trees that speak not yet form a path beneath them, she now gloriously manifests before the world to expound the Four Noble Truths (Suffering, Accumulation, Cessation, and the Path) and brilliantly unfold the divine principles of Dao, Law, Propriety, and Temperance. Witnessing the dawn light of fulfillment in the divine governance of the spiritual realm, and advancing toward the auspicious omen of the morning sun dispelling darkness, this year is indeed one that both the Divine Realm and the Human Realm ought to bless with boundless joy!

五月五日は男子の祝日、菖蒲の節句である。三月三日は女子の祝日、桃の節句である。女子の御魂聖地に出現してより二十五年の間桃李物言はず自ら蹊をなせしもの、ここに目出度く世にあらはれて苦、集、滅、道を説き、道、法、礼、節をはなばなしく開示することとなつたのも、神界経綸の神業成就の曙光をみとめ、旭光照破の瑞祥にむかつたので、神人界のともに祝福すべき年であります。
§ 10

In this volume, the name 'Daizizaiten' (Maheshvara) refers to none other than the Great Deity Ōkuninushi-no-Kami spoken of in the sacred Shinto texts, who is also known by such divine titles as Ōkunihiko-no-Mikoto, Yachihoko-no-Kami, Ōnamuchi-no-Mikoto, Ashihara-Shikoo-no-Kami, and Utsushi-Kunitama-no-Kami. Possessing matchless physical prowess and valor, this Deity presented the sacred Spear of National Consolidation (Kuni-muke no Hoko) to the Heavenly Grandchild, clarifying the supreme moral bond between Sovereign and subject, and faithfully upholding the path of absolute loyalty. In this narrative, He is referred to as Daizizaiten or Tokoyo Shin'ō (the Divine King of Tokoyo).

現代日本語訳
本書『霊界物語』の中にしばしば登場する「大自在天（だいじざいてん）」とは、我が国の神典に記されている「大国主神（おおくにぬしのかみ）」の御事であって、大国彦命、八千矛神（やちほこのかみ）、大己貴命（おおなむちのみこと）、葦原醜男神（あしはらしこおのかみ）、宇都志国魂神（うつしくにたまのかみ）などの数々の御神名を有し給う偉大なる神格であります。 この神は武勇絶倫なる力を備えながらも、天孫降臨に際して国平矛（くにむけのほこ）を謹んで天津神へと奉呈し、君臣の大義名分を世に明らかにして忠誠の道を全うされた至誠の神であります。本物語におきましては、この神を「大自在天」、あるいは「常世神王（とこよしんのう）」と申し上げているのであります。
原文（近代文語）
この物語のうちに大自在天とあるは、神典にいはゆる、大国主之神の御事であつて、大国彦命、八千矛神、大己貴命、葦原醜男神、宇都志国魂神などの御名を有したまひ、武力絶倫の神にましまして国平矛を天孫にたてまつり、君臣の大義を明らかにし、忠誠の道を克く守りたまふた神であります。本物語にては大自在天、または常世神王と申しあげてあります。
§ 11

While Daizizaiten is originally the name of a Buddhist deity found in Buddhist sutras, in the spiritual realm it designates the august person of Ōkuninushi-no-Kami. This deity was a heroic divinity who wielded the mighty authority of the Spear of Eight Generations (Yachihoko) to govern the realm under heaven. However, it is the fundamental divine will of the Imperial Ancestral Deities to govern the world through the Sacred Jewel, the emblem of peace, and the Sacred Mirror, the emblem of heavenly wisdom.

§ 12

Furthermore, that the Great Deity Panku (Shionagahiko, also known as Shionawahiko) is an inherently good deity has already been stated in the preceding volume. Having been revered by turbulent spirits who devised various schemes, and actively maneuvering across the divine realm to resist the divine administration of Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto, He gave rise to numerous turbulent upheavals, as recounted previously. Therefore, in order to save this world, the two creator deities Izanagi and Izanami descended from heaven centered upon our divine homeland to strive in the sacred enterprise of repair and consolidation. I trust that this wondrous, blessed narrative will become ever clearer as the chapters unfold. May the Divine Spirit bestow supreme blessing!
