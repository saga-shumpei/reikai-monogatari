Volume 3 • Part 4: Mount Kijō

The Power of Faith

第一二章　信仰の力〔一一二〕
§ 1

Returning unto Mount Kijō to reconnoiter the fortress, Murayukihiko encountered Kiyokuma and a company of troops outside the walls. Recognizing Murayukihiko at once, Kiyokuma commanded his retainers to surround him from all four sides; capturing him without difficulty, they dragged him straight into the citadel and sternly interrogated him regarding the whereabouts of Kototamawake-no-Mikoto. Feigning innocent ignorance, Murayukihiko replied: 'Kototamawake-no-Mikoto must surely be within the castle! Why are you so flustered, posing such absurd questions unto me and taking me captive? Methinks you are so drunk that you foolishly intend to make sport of me! I am the attendant of Angel Kototamawake-no-Mikoto! However cowardly or faint-hearted a deity you might deem him, what hardship would compel him to flee secretly from the castle? Restrain your delirium within reasonable bounds!' Opening his mouth wide, he burst into derisive laughter. Taking him in grim earnest, the demons within the castle flared in fury, cursing and persistently demanding: 'Thou must know the whereabouts of the deity! Confess every single detail!' Though they leveled a volley of harsh interrogations against him, Murayukihiko maintained absolute composure, fearing nothing, and feigned greater ignorance while dissolving into mockery.

現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）
鬼城山の偵察に向ひたる村幸彦は、清熊らの一行に城外において出会したり。清熊は村幸彦の姿を見るなり直ちに従臣らに命じ、四方より包囲して難なくこれを捕縛せしめ、直ちに城内に連れ帰り、言霊別命の所在をきびしく訊問したりける。村幸彦は空とぼけて、『言霊別命は城内にましまさむ。汝らは何を狼狽して吾に向つてかかる奇問を発し、かつ吾らを捕縛せしや、思ふに汝らは酒興のあまり、滑稽にも我を愚弄する心算ならむか。いやしくも天使言霊別命の従臣なり。いかに卑怯未練の命なりとて、何を苦しみて城内をひそかに脱出するの要あらむや、囈語もほどほどにせられよ』と大口を開けてからからと打笑ひける。城内の魔神どもは真剣になり、たちまち憤怒の色を現はし、口々に罵りつつ執念深くも、『命の所在を汝は知るならむ、逐一白状におよべ』とたたみかけて厳しく訊問の矢を放ちしが、村幸彦は神色自若として何の怖るるところなく、ますます空とぼけて笑ひくづれける。
§ 2

Thereupon the demons cried: 'Dealing with such a madman is like pushing a shop curtain! Away with bother! Strip off his garments, douse him with freezing water from overhead, and cool down his frenzy!' No sooner had they spoken than they stripped him stark naked, dragged him unto the edge of a pond coated in thick ice, broke the ice, plunged him into the frozen depths, and poured thousands of ladles of freezing water over his head in turns! Murayukihiko prayed deeply within his heart unto God, constantly reciting the Heavenly Prayer (Amatsu-Norito) in a soft murmur. While they expected his body to freeze and perish instantly, dense white steam billowed vigorously from Murayukihiko's skin! Feeling not the slightest chill, he displayed a cheerful countenance like one enjoying a hot bath, and smiled broadly: 'Hey, demons! The bath is too hot! Won't you give me a little more cold water?' He laughed aloud. The demons could not fathom this at all: cast into freezing water beneath a bitter sky, with countless ladles of icy water poured over his head, not only did he show no pain, but cried out that the bath was too hot and asked for more water—this was beyond sane comprehension! Thinking there was no use tormenting such a colossal lunatic in the pond, they pulled him out; yet steam continued to rise copiously from Murayukihiko's body! This was entirely due unto the power of Murayukihiko's faith and the profound divine assistance of Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto.

§ 3

Suspicious of this miraculous sign, Miyamahiko and Kuniteruhime tied Murayukihiko naked unto a pillar along the drafty northern corridor in the howling wind, gathering around to heap mockeries upon him: 'Thou art a raving lunatic! A blundering fool despite thy looks!' Bringing forth numerous bamboo brooms, they beat and stabbed his entire body, striking his head hundreds of times with broom handles! Yet Murayukihiko felt no pain, smiling serenely: 'How feeble is the arm-strength of the demons of Mount Kijō!' He mocked them with a thrust of his chin. Miyamahiko flared in blazing fury: 'Then we shall show thee our true strength! Retainers, bring iron clubs and shatter his face with all your might!' At his command, retainers wielding iron clubs struck Murayukihiko with all their power from front, back, left, and right. Yet because Murayukihiko prayed deeply in his heart and recited the Heavenly Prayer, even the ferocious blows of iron clubs felt like nothing more than a feather against armor, and he mocked the demons' weakness with sharp wit! Growing increasingly enraged, the demons spat phlegm at his face to humiliate him; yet wondrous to behold, none of the phlegm reached Murayukihiko's face, but soared into mid-air, raining back down upon the demons' own faces multiplied dozens of times in quantity and filth! Afflicted by his own foul phlegm, Miyamahiko's pale, oval face was coated like slimy paste, turning into a comical sight like a paper doll spat out after being chewed!

§ 4

Enraged beyond limit, Miyamahiko drew his great sword and slashed at Murayukihiko; yet Murayukihiko's body stood firm like a stone Jizō statue; though slashed and thrust, it yielded no response, and the blade bent and notched, turning into the ragged edge of a saw! Retreating unto the inner chambers, Miyamahiko put on a twisted headband, a red loincloth, hitched up his hakama high, and gripped a massive spear. Crying 'Ei!' with the speed of lightning, he thrust at Murayukihiko's chest; yet the spearhead bent as frail as a scallion leaf, and striking only empty air, Miyamahiko staggered forward dozens of paces and collapsed heavily upon his face! Looking upon the thick hemp ropes binding him hand and foot, Murayukihiko laughed aloud: 'What use is binding me with such rotten rope? Cease your idle games! Your foolish schemes are as ridiculous as pasting a paper target with nasal mucus, worthy only of laughter!' Before his words finished, with a single shout of 'Ei!', the seemingly unbreakable thick ropes snapped into tiny fragments!

§ 5

Watching this unfolding miracle from nearby, Sugahiko and Kozakurahime ran forward, prostrating with both hands upon the floor, addressing Murayukihiko: 'What manner of sublime and mighty divine being art thou? We now repent our former offenses, begging forgiveness for our sins of rebellion from the depth of our hearts!' They spoke with trembling awe. Beside them, Miyamahiko and Kuniteruhime burst into loud weeping; deeply moved by the supreme majesty of divine virtue, they returned unto their true divine minds as though awakening from a dream. Reverently submitting unto the direct decree of Chief Angel Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto, they willingly surrendered Mount Kijō, and leading all retainers unto Earthly Takamanohara, expressed their submission and swore to render faithful service.

§ 6

Thus Mount Kijō was auspiciously settled: Matetsuhiko became Hachiō Deity, reverently enshrining the Sacred Gray Jewel within the auspicious sanctuary; Motoaruhiko became Yatsugashira Deity, while Matetsuhime and Motoaruhime remained within the citadel to assist their husbands. Though the Laws of Heaven and Earth are exceedingly strict and must not be violated in the slightest degree, when genuine repentance from the deepest roots of the heart is recognized, forgiveness is granted immediately! Truly, nothing is more splendid than wholehearted repentance. That the crimes of Miyamahiko and Kuniteruhime, who had committed the extreme limits of heinous wickedness, were pardoned and that they were permitted to participate in the sacred enterprise of Earthly Takamanohara, must truly be called the radiant manifestation of the Supreme God's boundless benevolence!

現代日本語訳
こうして目出度く鬼城山は平定され、真鉄彦（まてつひこ）が八王神となって神宝「灰色の玉」を瑞の御舎（みずのみあらか）に恭しく鎮祭し、元照彦（もとあるひこ）が八頭神となり、真鉄姫と元照姫が共に城内にとどまって夫の神政を補佐することとなったのである。 天地の律法は極めて厳格であり、微塵たりとも犯すことは許されないが、心の根底からの真の改心が認められた時には、直ちに赦免が与えられるのである。誠に「改心に勝る結構（善美）はない」と言うべきであろう。現に悪逆無道の限りを尽くした美山彦や国照姫らの大罪を赦し、地の高天原の大神業に参加せしめられたことこそ、至高神の無限の御仁慈の発露に他ならないのである。
原文（近代文語）
ここに目出度鬼城山は真鉄彦、八王神となつて、灰色の玉を瑞の御舎仕へまつりて恭しく鎮祭し、元照彦は八頭神となり、真鉄姫、元照姫は共に城内にとどまり、夫の補佐を勤むることとなりける。天地の律法はもつとも厳重にして毫末も犯すべからざるものとはいへども、発根より改心と認められたる時は直ちにこれを許さるるものなり。実に改心にまさる結構は無かるべし。現に悪逆無道の極みをつくしたる美山彦、国照姫の罪を赦し、地の高天原の神業に参加せしめたまひしは、大神が無限の御仁慈の発露といふべし。
