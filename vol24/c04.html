Seeing a familiar maiden in Kenonkiyō, they were struck with astonishment, and for a while remained speechless, but at length calming their hearts, they pressed their hands together and asked with beating hearts, 'Ah, how strange, how mysterious! Is it a dream, a reality, or an illusion? Is it Isokohime or Umekohime? And you, Uzuhime and Imakohime, my companions, why have you come wandering into the vast sea? Surely there must be a profound reason for this; pray tell me in detail.'

Isokohime turned toward Koitohime and said,

'You are the daughter of Onikumawake-sama of Kenonkiyō; how on earth have you come to such a place as this? And how is Tomohiko-san faring?'

'Rather than that, you four people, to think of you arriving in such a place aboard a boat—we cannot comprehend it at all. For what purpose and to where are you heading? Please let us hear about it.'

'There are deep and subtle circumstances for this. We shall tell you leisurely by and by, but I beg that you, Anata, will open your mouth first.'

Koitohime answered, 'Hai,' and without concealing a single detail, she recounted the full story from the time she left Kenonkiyō, parted from Tomohiko, and fled all the way here. The four were astounded at Koitohime's audacious and daring nature, quite unbefitting her years.

Umekohime, with a look of dismay, said,

'Your personality has certainly changed drastically.'

'Indeed it has; since I am to be the future queen of the single island of Ryūgū, a mere girl or young woman as I was hitherto cannot govern a multitude of countrymen.'

Thus, even before the shadow of the island was yet visible, she was a bold and dauntless woman who had already swallowed the island of Ryūgū in her mind.

Isokohime and Umekohime, setting aside the question of good and evil, thought she would be ideally suited as a queen for a barbarous and uncivilized land; taking it as good fortune that they had boarded this boat, they resolved in their hearts to utilize the many days it would take to reach the island of Ryūgū to make her master the teachings of Izunome, thereby constructing a spiritual paradise... They recounted in detail how they had set out from Kenonkiyō, intending to propagate the teachings of Izunome in all directions, when they were suddenly captured by the faction of Katahiko and Kugihiko of Brahmanism and came drifting to this sea; removing the barriers between friend and foe, they successfully instilled the teachings of Izunome into Koitohime while aboard the ship of shared fate.

From the very boat, Koitohime already assumed the airs of a queen, treating Isokohime and Umekohime exclusively as her advisors or staff, while Imakohime and Uzuhime were treated as her personal attendants. Rejoicing that they had secured a fine instrument, Isokohime and Umekohime outwardly designated Koitohime, a sixteen-year-old shrewish maiden, as their leader; and at last, the five women arrived safely at the port of Kusu on the island of Ryūgū, moored their boat to the shore, and proceeded into the vast single island at their own discretion while singing propaganda songs. The sun gradually set, and the four quarters were enveloped in utter darkness; the five women laid out raincoats by the edge of a certain mountain stream and peacefully went to sleep.

The voices of fierce beasts began to roar so loudly as to shake even the mountains. The bold and dauntless five women treated the sounds as lightly as the soughing of pine wind or the playing of a koto, took those noises as a bookmark for slumber, and passed the night here without a care. Plucking wild fruits from all around to fill their bellies, making their way through mountain paths where the grass grew tall enough to swallow their bodies, letting their voices of propaganda songs echo and resound against the mountain echoes, they advanced and advanced until they emerged into a certain flat settlement. It was a somewhat broad plain like the bottom of a mortar, surrounded by mountains, and on the mountain slopes holes were bored, with each household puffing out smoke like charcoal kilns. The five climbed up upon a slightly elevated large rock in the center of the plain, and at the top of their voices chanted the Amatsu Norito and began to sing their propaganda songs. Perhaps startled by these voices, from the countless holes on the mountain slopes came crawling out dark-skinned men and women—some five, some ten, twenty from a single hole—each grasping implements in their hands, and they gathered around the large rock where the five stood like a swarm of ants. This place was known as Kayanogahara, which was reputed to be somewhat of an urban area even on the single island. Seeing the five fair-skinned beauties standing atop the rock, the company firmly believed that they were heavenly maidens descended from the heavens, and shedding tears of joy, they pressed their palms together from all four directions and eight quarters, bowing low in reverence and salute.

At such a place, from the depths of the mountains, the sound of a conch shell echoed resoundingly as 'bu-bu', and upon looking, a big man leading hundreds of fierce men, straddling a spirited horse, came stepping briskly and appeared at this place,

Hey, from what land have you drifted ashore and arrived? This Hitotsujima is a secret realm that does not permit the landing of people from other countries. By whose permission have you come forth? Quickly descend from that rock and relate the circumstances one by one.

Koitohime remained poised and self-possessed, her entire face brimming with a smile as she watched over the entourage of the big man. The four sendenshi likewise folded both hands, stood solemnly on both flanks of Koitohime, and gazed intently upon the faces of everyone.

The large man raised his voice roughly,

This one is a person called Burangī, the grand chieftain of Hitotsujima. Are you not terrified of this one's might? Quickly descend from your seats this very moment and submit to our bonds.

Glaring fiercely with eyes like a demon and staring them down intently, Koitohime smiled a gentle smile and

Foolish Burangī, I have received the command of the Amatsukami, and just now, leading four attendants, I rode upon five-colored clouds and descended from heaven to this Hitotsujima. Since this land is the true serene realm decreed by the gods for me to govern, will you henceforth offer your sincere hearts to me and serve? If not, I shall dispatch heavenly censure to cause a rain of spears to fall, and using thunder bullets as punishment, I shall strike down and annihilate you and your fellows. What is your reply?

When she spoke thus decisively, even the mighty Burangī tilted his head at the sublime figure of the woman, and for a while remained lost in thought. The hundreds of fierce men prostrated flat upon the earth in full armor, and together with the residents of Kayanogahara, folded their hands toward the five and were lost in tears of profound joy.

Witnessing this spectacle, Burangī bent his pride, once more dismounted from his horse, and prostrated flat upon the earth to express his intent of surrender. Koitohime spoke with graceful words,

By virtue of your surrender to Amatomete no Tanaestablishment-hime, I shall make you my attendant official and employ you heavily. Serve us with absolute sincerity.

When she applied the Kototama artfully in this manner, the entire company was struck with emotion, revered the five sendenshi as divine beings, guarded them front and rear, and guided them to a city amidst a slightly unfolding beautiful plain, welcomed the five into a spacious hall, prepared heartfelt hospitality, and expressed their sincerity with utmost cordiality.

Here, the five were lauded as the flowers of Hitotsujima, propagated the teachings of the thirty-five gods in all directions, and their resounding fame echoed across the entire island. Naming this place Jionkyō henceforth, Koitohime was finally elevated by the islanders to become queen and came to be renamed Kōryūhime.

Here, Ikogohime made Imagohime her follower, had Umegohime and Uzuhime wait on the left and right of Koitohime, and it was decided that they would head toward Jitentōshima, yearning after and proceeding along the sacred footsteps of the divine god Izanagi and Susanō no Ōkami. There was a woman called Kuronbā who was the wife of Burangī. Both husband and wife were at an age where they had crossed the milestone of fifty by four or five years. 『此方は一つ島の大棟梁ブランジーと言ふ者である。此方の威勢に恐れぬか。一時も早く座を下り我等が縛につけ』

と鬼の如き眼を光らしグツと睨めつけたるを、小糸姫は莞爾と笑ひ、

『愚なりブランジー、妾は天津神の命を受け、只今四柱の従者を率ゐ、五色の雲に乗り此一つ島に天降りしものぞ。此国は妾が治むべき神の定めの真秀良場なれば今日より妾に誠心を捧げて仕ふるか。さもなくば、天譴を下して槍の雨を降らせ、雷の弾を以て懲戒の為め汝等を打滅し呉れむ。返答如何に』

とキツと言ひ渡せば、流石のブランジーも崇高なる女の姿に首を傾け、暫し思案に暮れて居た。数百人の荒男は武装の儘大地に平伏し、五人に向つて萱野ケ原の住民と共に両手を合せ随喜の涙に暮れて居る。ブランジーは此光景を見て我を折り、又もや馬を下り大地に平伏して帰順の意を表したり。小糸姫は言葉淑やかに、

『汝は天津乙女の棚機姫に帰順せし徳に依つて、妾が従司となし重く用ひむ。飽迄も誠を以て吾等に仕へよ』

と巧く言霊を応用すれば一同は感に打たれ、五人の宣伝使を神人と敬ひ、前後を護りて稍展開せる美はしき原野の中の都会に導き、広殿に五人を迎へて心よりの馳走を拵へ、いと懇に誠意を表したりける。

茲に五人は一つ島の花と謳はれ、三五の神の教を四方に宣伝し、其驍名は全島に轟き渡りけり。此処を是より地恩郷と命名し、小糸姫は遂に島人に挙げられて女王となり黄竜姫と改名する事となりける。

茲に五十子姫は今子姫を従へ、梅子姫、宇豆姫を小糸姫が左右に侍せしめ、自転倒島さして神素盞嗚大神の御跡を慕ひ進み渡る事となりぬ。ブランジーの妻にクロンバーといふ女あり。夫婦何れも五十の坂を四つ五つ越えたる年輩なり。ブランジーはクロンバーと共に今は黄竜姫の宰相役となり、遠近に其名を轟かして居た。クロンバーは或時黄竜姫に拝謁を乞ひ奥の間近く進み入り、

『黄竜姫様に折入つてお願ひが御座います。妾の夫ブランジーは貴女様のお見出しに預り、宰相として恩寵を辱なうし、此島に於ては飛ぶ鳥も落す勢となりました。クロンバー身にとり有難く御礼の申し上げ様も御座いませぬ。御存じの通り大男の不束者で御座いますれば、何卒御見捨てなく末永く使つてやつて下さいませ。妾は実は此島の生れではなく、聖地エルサレムに仕へて居りました者で御座いますが、大切なる玉の紛失せし為め其所在を探ねむと、竜宮の乙姫様の生宮として今年で殆ど満二年、残る隈なく探せども今に所在は分らず、何卒々々貴女の天眼力を以て御示し下さらば有り難う御座います』

黄竜姫は厳然として、

『是は珍らしき汝の願ひ、其玉と申すは如何なる玉なるぞ』

『ハイ、左様で御座います。金剛不壊の如意宝珠に黄金の玉、紫の玉の三つの御宝で御座います。今迄は自転倒島の三五教の東本山に納めありし処、何者にか盗み取られ今に行方が分りませぬ。黄金の玉は妾が保管致して居りました所、何者にか盗み出され、又残り二つの玉は噂に聞けば是亦行方不明との事、何卒貴女の御神力を以て、此島の何れの地点にあるやお示し下さらば有り難う存じます』

§ 4

Ōriyōhime smiled with a composed and magnanimous air,

Ōriyōhime: 'That sacred jewel is not hidden here in this Riugūjima. It is concealed at a certain point on Onokorojima and cannot be easily excavated. By now, you should abandon your attachment to the jewel and, together with Buranjī, faithfully devote yourself to the affairs of state.'

Having said this, she hid her figure in the inner chamber as if fleeing. Afterward, Kuronā murmured to himself,

Kuronā: 'Ah, there is nothing to be done. Having lost the golden jewel, having been scolded and driven away by Takahime-sama, and having heard through the whisper of the guardian deity that it was hidden on one of the islets of Riugū, I came searching all the way to this place. Yet, even if I were to use the people of the land to search this vast island for three or five years, it would be like trying to catch a cloud in the sky. According to the words of Ōriyōhime-sama, the three treasures are not on this island. What on earth should I do? Without that jewel, there is no way I can return to the sacred land and show my face to Takahime-sama. Although this Kurohime and I, together with her husband Takayamahiko, are living on this island under foreign names as Buranjī and Kuronā, now that things have come to this, no matter how splendid an office we are entrusted with, it is of no account compared to the sacred land. Ah, how I wish to return quickly!'

Just as he was speaking, Buranjī appeared upon the scene,

Buranjī: 'Hey Kurohime, let us hurry back to the mansion. We must not displease the honorable mood of Ōriyōhime-sama.'

Kurohime: 'Takayama-sama, what are you saying? I have already grown to hate this island. No matter how much we search, there is no way we can find a clue on this vast island. At this point, I am past caring; I shall return once to the sacred land, rebuild the Izunome-kyō from its very foundations, and drive out the leader of Kototama-wake—otherwise, my vexed spirit will never be pacified. Is not all the hardship we, husband and wife, have endured in coming to this island across ten thousand ri of waves entirely on account of Kototama-wake? Oh, how vexing, how mortifying! I am desperate now, and from this moment I shall go mad, so please be prepared for it.'

Buranjī: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha! Has your usual hysterical fit broken out again? Now, now, let us go back home, drink some wine leisurely, and consider the matter after that.'

Patting her back three or four times, he took Kuronā by the hand and returned to his own mansion.
