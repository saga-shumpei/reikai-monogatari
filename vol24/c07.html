Chapter 7: メラの滝 [737]

メラの滝〔737〕

§ 1

Having set sail from Shōzu Island in the Sea of Seto, and having presently crossed Õshima, Riukiujima, Taiwan, and the Philippine Archipelago, the party of Takahime finally arrived at a corner of Ambon Island, renowned as the Dragon Palace Island of the South Seas.

In general, murky waters surged throughout this region, and drinking water was obtained solely by catching rain. However, this island alone lived up to its name as the Dragon Palace Island; clear springs gushed forth everywhere, and the island was divided into two, named Oshima and Meshima. On the Meshima side hung two dragon harps: Tsuriiha Waterfall, also known as Odaki, and Melah Waterfall, also known as Medaki. The Odaki side rushed down tumultuously from between rocks, while the Medaki side utilized artificial bamboo gutters to form a waterfall from somewhat slender water gushing out from the roots of a great tree. In this island, the vegetation of the world grew lushly, and among the numerous towering rocky islands, the trees were verdant, making it a particularly conspicuous treasure island. Even on the scorching summer days, when one went to the edge of this waterfall, the foliage blocked the heavens, the cascades plunged forth with a clear, murmuring sound, and it was truly a sacred place overflowing with myriad measures of cool refreshment, which might well be called the number-one earthly paradise of the South Seas.

Takahime and Mukaderhime set their eyes upon this island first, rejoicing and emboldened as if they had already obtained the three sacred jewels hidden by Tamanohime, believing they were surely lodged on this island; they vied with one another to land, and advanced their steps toward Odaki. In their excessive joy, they forgot to tie their boat to the rocky shore. Due to a rather strong wind at that moment, the boat drifted away into the distance of the offing with the momentum of a thousand ri in a single sweep. Yet the party did not realize even in a dream that their boat had drifted away; in high spirits, they advanced to the foot of Tsuriiha Waterfall, cast off their sweat-stained garments, and jumped into the basin of the waterfall to taste the cool refreshment all to themselves, and began to chant the divine words with the feeling of being revived with all their might.

For three days and three nights, the company took ritual water ablutions and recovered their vigor, ate the fresh nuts of the surrounding trees so that their strength was suddenly augmented, and it was decided that they would thoroughly search the entire island without exception for the jewels. Dividing their stations—Takahime on Meshima and Mukaderhime on Oshima—whenever they saw even the slightest suspicious stone, they tore it away, churning it up from the very roots as if digging for mountain yams, and turned over every single one of the lovely green mosses that carpeted this island like a felt rug. From beneath the mosses, strangely shaped snakes, centipedes, geckos, and lizards appeared without interruption, leaping and biting at the bodies of Takahime and the others with utter disgust. Yet the party, their souls extracted by the whereabouts of the jewels, without any concern, chanting 'Kaminagara Tamashichifu Masasei' on everyone's lips regardless of the time, resting when tired, scooping water when thirsty, and plucking and eating fruits from everywhere when hungry, turned over the earth and stripped off the moss all the way to the peak of the island mountain with a momentum like that of upland-rice beetles devouring a giant plum tree down to the very last leaf, and completed their search. Almost three months were required during that time.

Takahime and Mukaderhime, with tenacious persistence, this time descended to the shore, and turning over every single large and small stone from the roots to examine them, found nothing but water striders and crabs; not a single thing resembling a jewel was to be found. Even the usually resolute Takahime and Mukaderhime lost their patience, gathered once again at the foot of Odaki, settled their hips, and engaged in water ablutions. While each examined the seashore, their hearts were taken by the jewels, and not a single person noticed that there was no longer even a trace of the boat in which they had ridden.

Smartball, who had been taking water ablutions centering around the waterfall basin for some seven days and seven nights, went out to the seashore alone, and looking closely, was struck with shock to see no sign of the boat. He walked around the circumference of the island many times to search and examine it, but could not find it anywhere. Startled, he appeared before the waterfall basin,

『エヽ油断のならぬ。何程諸善神の集まる竜宮島でも、寸善尺魔とか云ふ悪神が高姫の気を引きに来よつたな。併し乍ら高姫の弁舌、否言霊に、仕方なく四足の性来を現はし、……キーキー、キウキウ……と啼いてゐやがる。野良鼠か、栗鼠か、鼬か貂か、又も違つたら豆狸か、一時も早く此場を立ち去れ。日の出神の生宮の前も憚らず、四足の分際として高い所に上ると云ふ事は、天地顛倒も甚だしい。シイシイ』

と頻りに歯の脱けた口から唾を飛ばしながら叱つて居る。貫州は益々可笑しさに耐へ兼ね、脇の辺りで『キウキウ』と笑ひ出したり。此処へ濃霧の中を両手を前に突き出し、盲が杖無くして歩くやうに、探り足にやつて来たのは蜈蚣姫なりき。貫州は皺嗄れ声を出し、

『如何に高姫、汝の願ひ叶へてやらう。其方は蜈蚣姫を此島に一人残し置き、貫州を連れて逃げだした方が都合がよいとの意志を表示したであらう。表面は蜈蚣姫とバツを合せて居るが、其方の心の中は決してバラモン教では無い事はよく分つて居る。唯三個の玉さへ手に入れば、蜈蚣姫は何うでもよいのだ。何うだ、神の申す事に間違ひあるまい』

高姫は聊か迷惑顔しながら、

『モシモシ蜈蚣姫様、何処に居られましたの。私はどれだけ心配したか分りませぬワ。ようマア無事でゐて下さいました。此通り濃霧に包まれて一尺先は分らぬやうな事で御座いますから、種々の枉津が現はれて、今お聞きの通り貴女と私の仲を悪くし内輪喧嘩をさせ、内部から結束を破らせようとするのだから、用心なさいませや』

§ 4

From above the waterfall, Kuwanshiu:

Kuwanshiu: 'Mukadehime and company, do not fall into the cunning trap of Takahime. The true God of Hinodewake is here.'

Mukadehime: 'Ah, thank you very much. Your divine words surely do not contain a single mistake. I will be careful from now on... Listen here, Takahime-sama, the Kamisama is honest. Ever since you suddenly adopted a metamorphic attitude in the cave of Kunishiroyama, my suspicions that there was something peculiar about you have never wavered, and as my calculations predicted, the true Hinodewake Kamisama has now proven it. Now, what do you say? Come, Takahime-sama, do you have a reply?'

Kuwanshiu from within the mist:

Kuwanshiu: 'Mukadehime is also quite a character. She intended to cleverly use Takahime to make her search for the jewel, then skillfully fleece her on top of that, and serve Takahime a salted octopus pot. But Kami is upright just as you say, and never tells even the slightest lie.'

Takahime with a smug face,

Takahime: 'Mukadehime-sama, look at that, aren't you the one with the wicked heart?'

Mukadehime: 'Since it is a gathering of evil and evil, to speak of it is simply boorish. Ho ho ho ho.'

She laughs it off.

At this time, a giant bear bee peculiar to this island, about five suns in size, buzzing loudly, collided with Takahime's head like a pebble, and with the remaining momentum bounced off Mukadehime's bridge of the nose, and the bee desperately clung to her nose and pierced both nostrils sharply like a sharp sword. Mukadehime, crying out 'Ouch ouch,' collapsed on the spot with her hands holding her nose. Mukadehime jumped to the conclusion that Takahime had targeted the bridge of the nose with an iron fist,

Mukadehime: 'Lawless Takahime, to strike a cowardly blow is the utmost cowardice. Hey, Smart Ball and the rest of you, come quickly and tie up Takahime.'

She was shouting thus. Before one's eyes, her face swelled up, and her nose, eyes, and mouth were swollen shut. Takahime, startled,

Takahime: 'Listen here, Mukadehime-sama, it is not me. The bear bee bit you. Please do not take it the wrong way.'

From within the mist above the waterfall,

Kuwanshiu: 'The centipede has been stung by a bee. Seeing this, let Takahime repent. You have hitherto displayed an arrogant demeanor like a hawk descending upon Suzumegahara, but shall I have the bear bee sting Takahime's eyes once more? This side is the spirit of the bear bee. Because you are excessively proud, and both of you are trying to climb on top of other people's heads, you have met with such a punishment. If you wish to become such a great person and climb on top of people's heads, it would be better for you to become a rat in the ceiling. Wishing for divine virtue such that people step aside and let you pass, you came all the way to the South Seas to search for the jewel; if you wish to become that great, become a nightsoil carrier. Everyone, absolutely everyone, will step aside and let you pass. I shall teach you one more good thing. If you become a thief, people will fear you. If you wish to gain divine virtue and make people afraid, no special trouble or time is required. Just acquire teeth capable of biting through railways, eating gravel, and swallowing steel warships. If you do so, the scoundrels of the world will not stand a chance against you in word or deed. And if you make a mistake again, become a leprosy patient or a scabies patient.'

Laughing within her throat with a 'Kiu kiu' sound. Suddenly a cool breeze arose, the dense fog enveloping all around suddenly cleared away, and the scenery became a distant view spanning a thousand leagues. Kuwanshiu, startled, attempting to run while covering his face with his sleeve so as not to be seen by Takahime, missed his footing and dropped heavily right at Takahime's feet.

Takahime: 'Yikes!'

She cried out and leaped two or three steps backward, and upon looking closely, it was Kuwanshiu.

Takahime: 'Hey, are you Kuwanshiu or not? I was wondering why things didn't make sense, and what kind of a prank are you playing? Retribution is swift, and you must be suffering now for turning a backward somersault just like this, aren't you?'

Kuwanshiu: 'Oh, indeed it was completely inexcusable. It was simply because the guardian deity appeared.'

Takahime: 'Stop talking such nonsense. Secondly, repeating the word "guardian deity, guardian deity, guardian deity" like a habit... I won't fall for that trick. Rather than that, let's get on a boat right now and go search for the jewel.'

Kuwanshiu: 'Yes, yes, indeed.' (Kuwanshiu hurriedly boards the boat.)

Mukadehime-sama: 'Mukadehime-sama is suffering like this from being stung by bees, what do you intend to do? Is not the way of Kamisama to help even an enemy? And yet, when you spoke of getting into a boat, is there not no boat at all?'

Stroking Mukadehime's back and then trying to soothe her by stroking her chest. Suma-tobo-ru, Kiusuke, Omin, and other attendants, taking advantage of the clearing of the thick fog, ran to this place, astonished at the sight of the two, and stood there without even speaking, with their mouths wide open in a daze.
