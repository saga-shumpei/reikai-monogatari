Chapter 3: Waves 濤の Dream [733]

波濤の夢〔733〕

§ 1

Disgusted by Tomohiko's vulgar and base attitude, and shrinking in horror from him as if he were a serpent or a scorpion, Koitohime, the Queen of the island of Ceylon, seized the opportunity when Tomohiko had drunk himself into a stupor and fallen completely senseless. Leaving a letter of divorce behind her, and packing her hara-maki with heavy gold, she departed from the port of Shiro, having two blackamoors, Chanky and Monkey, steer the boat, and glided with all her might across the moon-lit unabara.

Up in tenjiyō, the Mirror of Jiyōhari hung solemnly, reflecting the suiriku and shinrabanshiyō of daichi. This very boat in which Koitohime was now traveling seemed to be part of a tennenguwachiu suspended right against the face of the moon. Escaping at last from the kokō and drawing a breath of relief, Koitohime murmured to herself...

Koitohime: 'Ah, can there be anyone in the world as sinful as I? The grace of a father higher than the mountains, the grace of a mother deeper than the seas—indulging in that grace and falling under the proverb that a child knows not a parent's heart, out of all people, how could I have fallen for such a base man as Tomohiko, whom everyone looks upon and loosely detests like a serpent or a scorpion? I am beginning to doubt my very self. As is usual with a shifunenbuka man, by this time he must have sobered up, looked around him in dismay, and upon seeing the letter I left behind, fallen back in astonishment, baring those hideous eyes of his and flying into a rage, no doubt. It seems both pitiful and somewhat amusing. Is my heart a demon, a serpent, a god, or a Buddha? I cannot fathom my own heart. Be that as it may, that man called Tomohiko drinks himself to death from morning till night whenever he has money, shattering his body and rotting his soul, having almost completely lost his qualifications as a human being. Therefore, perhaps this shock will straighten out his sense a little bit. If only he would become a true human being, I for one would not bear him any grudge. If there is even a spark of conscience left in that man, he will surely pull himself together and become a splendid human being. If so, the drastic measure I am now taking by abandoning him and running away will instead prove to be the seed of happiness for that man; his rotten soul will be purified, his body shattered by alcohol will grow robust once more, and he will be endowed with divine power sufficient to act for the sake of shinkai and society... Tomohiko-dono, you must be utterly indignant upon seeing my parting note. However, consider this too as a whip of grace directed toward you by me, and accept it with gratitude. You must never, never be bewildered. A broken pot finds a matching lid; find a woman suited to your station and live happily as husband and wife. An oil-lamp and a hanging bell—mismatched matches are the root of ill fortune, as the golden saying of old has it. Farewell to the guardian spirit of Tomohiko and to your flesh; with this, I bid you farewell for all kalpas to come.'

So she said, tossing her chin and chattering away in an arrogant style as if there were no one else beside her. The innocent moon hung leisurely in the pure sky like the Mirror of Jiyōhari, looking as though it were intently watching and listening to Koitohime's monologue inside the boat. Taking advantage of finding themselves floating right in the middle of the vast ocean, Chanky and Monkey exchanged glances, gradually shrugging their shoulders and stiffening their bodies into square shapes, sitting timidly like mechanical dolls on either side of Koitohime,

Chanky & Monkey: 'My, what a round, lovely face today's moon has! It has an exquisite countenance just like Queen Koitohime. Look up at the sky, and there is Moonlit Tathagata like a wish-granting jewel; look into the boat, and there is the apparition of the pure white Luminous Tathagata shaming the snow. Since we too were born men, we would love to shake hands with such a beauty, ah-ha-ha-ha!'

they let out a contrived laugh.

Chanky & Monkey: 'Oi, Chank, what are you talking about with such ticklish indirectness? A ri or two would be one thing, but dragging the moon in the great sky all the way down here—such roundabout ways are out of fashion in this world. It is an age that values simplicity and promptness above all. Right here at the bottom of the sea, a splendid moon is drifting along with the waves. This boat crossing over the moon is just like the heavenly bird-boat ridden by the tennin. Behold... at the bottom of the sea are the shadows of stars too numerous to count by the hundreds of thousands. With this great sky enveloped by moon and moon, star and star—even if they say our skin is black, even if they say our lips are thick, we have now come to soar through tenjiyō just like this, so what reason do we have to hesitate before the princess of Hinodewake? A frail woman of barely sixteen—is there any reason she should not yield to our freedom, armed as we are with solid, iron-hard arms like these? In this boundless unabara, how could we endure it without a single joy? ... Now, Koitohime, compared to that crooked nose or buckteeth of Tomohiko whom you called your servant and brought along, there is no telling how many times more splendid we are. Even if our skin is dark, we are like light-pickled eggplants; how about compromising with us?'

§ 2

Ho ho ho ho ho, now you two blackamoors, there is a limit to even making jokes. If you think you can do a rude thing just because I am a woman, do not think I will let it pass, eh!

Aha ha ha ha, you sure talk a big game. When the worst comes to the worst, it is a world where physical power triumphs over mere words. Since things have turned out this way, everything is completely up to us, and accepting all of our demands while resigning yourself to fate is for your own good, you see. How pitiful, after making Tomohiko—who was yearning so desperately for you—drink until he was dead drunk, stuffing your belly with travel money the whole while, and sneaking away like a daring, ridiculously bold fellow unfitting of your years; and as for Tomohiko, who was left behind... To be made a fool of by a grown man of thirty at the hands of a mere slip of a girl scarcely past her shoulder-length hair days, how could he ever show his face to the world again? 'Ah, how mortifying, how vexing! Even to the bottom of the thousand-fathom sea, I will search for Koito, and if I cannot utter my resentment I cannot die even in death'—the soul of that resentful man crystallized to become a sub-guardian deity and has possessed both of us completely. Ain't karma a terrifying thing? Words like these are not being spoken by the blackamoors at all; Tomohiko's soul is borrowing our mouths to say them. Well then, what is your answer?

With a ferocious look, puffing out their shoulders and arms, and with sweat-smelling bodies, they press in closely from both sides.

Ho ho ho ho ho, now, now, you blackamoor-sama, what on earth are you? Such cowardly nonsense, talking about Tomohiko's soul and all... Why don't you just come right out and say that a blackamoor chanky-monky has fallen in love with the Queen-sama?

My, what an open-minded Queen-sama! That makes sense, since you are a brazen hussy who could toy with a grown man at fifteen or so and run away from your hometown, you're bound to have just about that much nerve. Well then, Koito-hime-sama, to start anew, we two are your sweethearts from the bottom of our hearts. You shouldn't hate us all that much, right?

Ho ho ho ho ho, oh, is that so? Are you really that obsessed with me? You must be peerless and nice after all, I suppose.

Even without you saying it, nice is understood. How about it? Will you listen to the wishes of both of us?

I am not deaf, you know. Have you not been listening to every single word since the very beginning?

That is not the kind of listening I mean. The point is, will you grant our demands or not?

How absurd! Who would throw sheep's eyes at black men who look like lumps of charcoal? Taking it for a crow's play, I have been watching it all with amusement since the beginning.

You damned witch... Even if I look like this, I am a man! Despite being a woman, are you going to mock a stark-naked grown man?

No matter how big your frame may be, your liver is so awfully small that even your sack looks small after all, and I can't help it.

You are going to make a fool of us no matter what, aren't you? All right, all right, since it is up to us where we take this ship, we will make you suffer until you are at your wits' end, so bear that in mind.

As long as we have boarded the same ship, when I suffer, you suffer just as well. I am a guest like this, so I sit back and watch with my hands folded, but you are in a station of life where you cannot get through a single day without labor. You may as well row freely to the ends of the Makoto no Kuni. I think of this spacious sea surface like paradise, and I like drifting for three or ten years even, if need be.

What a bold woman! As one would expect from having the blood of Onikumawake flowing in her veins, she has something unusually different about her. Hey, Monkey, if we aren't careful, this creature might even be a demon. No matter how much grit one is said to have, there is no way one's torso could be this settled at fifteen or sixteen. She might even be an incarnation of Takakura or Asahi who serve the guardianship of Mi-Iku-Oshie. ...Hey, lift your bottom for a second and see. Is she dragging a tail or something behind her?

Saying this, while intently staring at the back of Koitohime,

Monkey: 'As I thought, this wretch is the genuine Koitohime. ...... Hey, Monkey, from here on out it is action without words.'

Chanky: 'Yosh, understood.'

And as Monkey from the front and Chanky from the rear leaped upon Koitohime with wild tiger-like movements, attempting to take her by force, Koitohime smoothly and evasively dodged their thrusts to the right and to the left, struggling with them for a while; but just as she was pinned down by the two mighty men and cried out 'Kya!' a single boat carrying four people came dashing through the waves to this place, and when one woman struck stunning blows at the two men, the two of them collapsed flat on their backs inside the boat with a mere groan.

Koitohime was saved from danger by this unexpected rescue ship, and turning to the one woman,

Koitohime: 'Thank you very much for rescuing me from such a perilous place.'

And peering through the moonlight,

Koitohime: 'You are Imakohime-san, why on earth did you ever condescend to come to such a place?'

Hearing this, Imakohime was astonished,

Imakohime: 'Such a person as you are none other than Koitohime-sama, the young lady of the vice-leader of Kenonkiyō. Last spring, joining hands with Tomohiko's propagation emissary, you went off to parts unknown, and your parents' lamentations were beyond description. It was so pitiable that even a casual onlooker could hardly bear it. Now then, you ought to return home as early as a single day allows and put your parents' minds at ease.'

Koitohime: 'No, no, no matter what, I must go to the Island of Ryūgū. Having some circumstances, I parted from Tomohiko, and I am now in the midst of crossing the ocean. Is the head temple of Kenonkiyō prospering more and more?'

Imakohime: 'I became a handmaid of Isokohimésama, the august daughter of the Great Deity of Izumo, Lord Susanoo, and a believer of Izumo; but though I racked my brains in various ways to make Gorumohiko and your parents repent, it was all to no avail, and finally the propagation emissary of Ame-no-Minakanushi-no-Mikoto arrived, Gorumohiko and your parents hid themselves somewhere, and Kenonkiyō has now become a sacred ground of Izumo. And as for me, while I was in the middle of propagation with Isokohimésama and Umekohimésama, I was seized by subordinates such as Katahiko and Kugihiko, and I am now in the midst of being carried away in this boat.'

Hearing this, Koitohime was greatly astonished,

Koitohime: 'If that is so, you are a traitor who has defected to Izumo. Beginning with Gorumohiko, you are likewise the enemy of my parents; come, under these circumstances, prepare yourself.'

So saying, she smoothly drew her short sword and attempted to slash at her. Isokohimésama, Umekohimésama, and Uzuhimésama watched this scene calmly and steadily without getting flustered or impatient from aboard the boat in which they had arrived.

Imakohime spoke with a gentle voice,

Imakohime: 'Now, now, calm yourself. No matter how impatient you may become, here there are four women and you are alone, so it is utterly hopeless after all. Rather than that, why don't you put your courage to use, cross over to the Island of Ryūgū, and begin the propagation of the Way? I too will lend you my strength.'

Koitohime resolved herself, judging that the fortunes of victory and defeat were already decided,

Koitohime: 'The world is entirely a thing created by the deities; so to speak, the people of the world are the august children of the deities. Viewed from the eyes of the deities, both you and I are all sisters; let us cleanly sweep away all matters up to now into the river—no, let us sweep them into the sea, and joining hands, shall we not serve the deities?'

Imakohime: 'That is truly splendid. ...... Isokohimésama, Umekohimésama, Uzuhimésama, what are your thoughts?'

The three of them nodded all at once.

Koitohime: 'Look, just as those three people are of the same mind with me, come, let us go together from now on in a single boat. However, we must revive the two men and save them, I suppose.'

Imakohime put her strength into a shout of 'Un!' and revived them. Presently, the two men came to their senses, shedding tears and offering apologies.

『私は三五教の大神、素盞嗚尊様の御娘子五十子姫様の侍女となり、三五教の信者で御座いましたが、鬼雲彦様や、貴女の御両親に改心して頂かうと、種々心は砕きましたなれど何うしても駄目、とうとう天の太玉命の宣伝使が御入来になり、鬼雲彦初め、御両親は何処へか身を匿され、顕恩郷は今や三五教の霊場となつて居ります。そして妾は五十子姫様、梅子姫様と宣伝の途中、片彦、釘彦等部下の為に促へられ、此船に乗せて流されました途中で御座います』

と聞いて小糸姫は大いに驚き、

『さすれば貴女は三五教に寝返りを打つた謀反人。鬼雲彦様を初め、妾の両親の敵も同様、サア此上は覚悟をなされ』

と懐剣をスラリと抜いて斬り掛らうとする。五十子姫、梅子姫、宇豆姫は、乗り来し船の上より、騒がず焦らず端然として此光景を打ち看守つて居る。今子姫は言葉淑やかに、

『マアマアお鎮まり遊ばせ。何程貴女がお焦慮なさつても、此通り此方は四人の女、貴女は一人、到底駄目ですよ。それより貴女の度胸を活用し、竜宮の一つ島へ渡りお道の宣伝を開始なさつたら何うでせう。妾もお力になりまする』

小糸姫は勝敗の数既に決せりと覚悟を極め、

『世界は皆神様のお造り遊ばしたもの、謂はば世界の人間は神様の御子で御座います。神の目から御覧になれば妾も貴女も皆姉妹、今迄の事はスツカリと河へ流しイヤ海に流し、相提携して神様に奉仕しようではありませぬか』

『それは真に結構で御座います。……五十子姫様、梅子姫様、宇豆姫様、貴女方の御考へは如何でせう』

三人一度に頷く。

『アレ彼の通りお三人共、妾と御同感、さア是から御一緒に一つの船で参りませう。併し乍ら二人の男に活を入れ、助けてやらねばなりますまい』

と今子姫は『ウン』と力を籠めて活を入れた。忽ち二人は正気づき涙を流して謝罪つて居る。

§ 4

'Well, well, you two black persons, you have taken great trouble for a long time.',

'I am henceforth becoming a Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō and will make a pilgrimage to every corner of Sekai, so you two go back with this.',

And when she took Koban out of her Kuwaichiyū and threw them, the two pushed them to their foreheads and said,

'Indeed, after committing Goburei, we have received so many coins, thank you very much.',

'If that is so, please board the ship yonder.',

'We will turn back to the port of Shiro with this ship, but in the unlikely event that we meet Tomohiko-sama, what should we say to him?'

'Ah, it will be safest to say that you do not know.'

The two said 'Yes, thank you' with Kanshiya while skillfully manipulating the oars, and rowed back toward Tōhoku.

Here, the five women took turns manipulating the oars, drifting at the whim of the waves, and eventually safely landed on an island in Australia.

原文（近代文語）

『これはこれは二人の黒ン坊さま、長々御苦労であつた。妾は是より三五教の宣伝使となつて、世界の隅々迄巡歴するから、お前達はこれで帰つてお呉れ』

と懐中より小判を取り出し投げやれば、二人は押し頂き、

『誠に御無礼を到しました上に、之程沢山お金を頂戴致しまして有り難う御座います。左様なれば貴女は彼方の船にお乗り下さいませ。私共は此船で錫蘭の港に引返します、万一友彦様に遇うたら何う申して置きませうか』

『アー知らないと云うて置くが無難でよからう』

二人は『ハイ有難う』と感謝し乍ら手早く櫓を操り、東北さして漕ぎ帰る。茲に五人の女は代る代る櫓を操りながら、浪のまにまに流されて、遂にオーストラリヤの一つ島に無事上陸する事となりける。
