Takahime, invited by Takayamahiko and Kurohime, was led into the vast Saishiyōshitsu, where they passed the time swapping kuwaikiudan.

Kurohime: 'Takahime-sama, you have come such a long way to visit me. Have you figured out the arika of the kogane no tama? Thinking they might have hidden them somewhere on this island, I crossed the raging waves to come here. I still do not know the arika of a single jewel.'

Kurohime: 'I will not let this unpardonable Kotoyoriwake, Osetsu, and Ohatsu go without settling this grudge!'

Kurohime: 'Now, now, Takayamashiko-sama, what a cold-hearted thing for you to say! Those are the treasures of the three thousand worlds. Takahime: 'Well, just now... looking quite grand, they were made to ride in a New Zealand native's hand-cart and came all the way to this front porch. By now, they have probably advanced together with Centipede Princess into the inner palace and are boasting in front of Lord Yoshikohime-sama, truly making my blood boil... At the jewel forest in New Zealand, they subjected me to a terrible insult. Tomohiko, who abducted the original Koitohime-sama, that Tamajiwake of Tadagaku who acts like a pinball, and along with them Osetsu and Ohatsu—oh, they made me suffer through hell. No matter how much I tell myself... This is the way of the Gods, look anew and listen anew... or try to suppress this bitter mortification, how on earth can one endure this? Exposed before a multitude of people, never since I was born have I experienced such a mortifying thing!'

with tears welling up uncontrollably, she let out her voice and wept aloud all at once as if a dam had burst.

Kurohime: 'Osetsu, Ohatsu, and that fellow Tadagaku—they must be somewhere inside this very castle. Come now, I will drag them out and put them on the tightening-rack, and make them confess!... Hey, all you retainers, capture that group of a man and a woman who arrived just a moment ago and bring them right here!'

At Kurohime's command, seven or eight menservants waiting in the next room shouted,

Menservants: 'Hai!'

Even as a queen of a nation, let alone the words of the living shrine of the Hinodewake, I cannot move even a single fraction against my own will.

『竜宮の乙姫の生宮、確に承知仕りました』

かかる所へ七八人の男ドヤドヤと入り来り、

『宰相様に申し上げます。城内隈なく探しましたが、宣伝使らしき者は一人も居りませぬから、屹度裏門から逃げ去つたに相違ありますまい。一足なりと逃げ延びぬ内に、サア皆さま捜索に参りませう』

と促す。三人はツト此の城を立出で、傍の山より峰伝ひに、玉能姫、初稚姫、玉治別一行の捜索に立向ひける。

高山彦も終に顕要の地位を棄てて高姫、黒姫と共に「タカ」の港に現はれ、一隻の船に身を委せ、浪のまにまに玉能姫一行の後を追はむと漕ぎ出したり。

玉能姫、初稚姫、玉治別其他の一行は、遥かの山上より霊眼を以て三人が此島を後に帰り行くのを眺め、ヤツト胸撫で下ろし、再び城内に悠々として帰り来たりぬ。初稚姫一行は、蜈蚣姫のアンボイナ島に於ける危難を救ふ可く船を与へたる其好意を黄竜姫より感謝され、山海の珍味を饗応され、愈茲に三五教を確立し、黄竜姫を女王兼全島の教主と定め、各自に手分けをなし、梅子姫、宇豆姫諸共に、全島隈なくあらゆる生霊に三五の大道を宣伝し、自転倒島の聖地に向つて凱旋する事となりにける。
