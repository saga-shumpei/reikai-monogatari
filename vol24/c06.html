Chapter 6: アンボイナ Island [736]

アンボイナ島〔736〕

§ 1

The ship carrying Takahime and Mukadehime advances as it threads its way through countless large and small islands, now to the right and now to the left, at the mercy of the waves. Enveloped suddenly by a dense fog where they could not distinguish an inch before them, they came to a pass where they knew not whether to continue their navigation, only to run the risk of striking the vessel against rocks and meeting with destruction and sinking. Both old crones, hardened though they were, and everyone aboard the ship were utterly perplexed, filled somehow with a desolating atmosphere; and the hot coagulation swirling up from the region of the navel toward the throat, as if stirring up the interior of the body in a spiral shape, caused the head to hear the sound of alarm bells beating wildly, and they were tormented by the thought of a cataclysm and earth-fiends, not knowing where to place their bodies. For no apparent reason, a loathsome sound echoed; with ghoulish lamentations giving them goosebumps, their courage shrank like a thread, and at the slightest wind beyond this, it came to the verge of snapping like the thread of the jewel-string of life. From nowhere, a loathsome voice resounded high above their heads.

Ahhh, Takahime and Mukadehime, the two of you who stubbornly push your own way to the very end, listen well with ears cleared out to the words of Amenoyaharahikonomoto no Mikoto. You have not yet had enough of evil. If it is evil, let it be evil; become absolute, thoroughgoing great evil. Great evil is precisely great goodness. Such mixed good and evil, fickleness, duplicity, and the great monster of unceasing arrogance—this indeed is true evil. What is called evil refers in all things and matters to the workings that bring matters to a conclusion for the sake of the divine realm.

Well, you are shaken and chilled to the bone upon hearing a disgusting voice, spiritless cowards that you are. Why do you not manifest the Hinode Kami of conventional means right now and here? What has become of Daikokunushi no Mikoto? You are dilatory, half-hearted propagandists who yield and submit unconditionally to the devil's egoistic words. What a thoroughly pitiful and wretched creature you are, oh!

Takahime immediately speaks:

Ahhh, I know not what god you may be,

Ahhh, to say that if one is to do evil, let it be great evil, is something I cannot accept in the least. This Takahime maintains and upholds the pure essence of the Yamato spirit, which is solely devoted to goodness.

Well, no way, no way, I cannot agree with your changeable, feminine words. Now, Mukadehime-sama, why don't you too stop sitting there dumbfounded and shrinking away, and ahhh, iiii, together join forces and give me your support? When else, if not now, should we manifest the true divine power of the gods? Ahhh, iiii, what a spiritless person you are!

A strange voice from the air:

Uhhh, what a troublesome thing. Takahime's attachment is adhesive to the very end; in this world of constant changes in existence, you will soon have to turn a reverse somersault. As a reward for rebelling against the leader of Kototama, your eyes are blinded and your ship in the offing has been taken by the waves; with no island to cling to, having reached this case of nine deaths and one life, can you still not repent?

Ehhh, boasting proudly as if there were none like yourself, traveling all over the world and blowing your own horn, the prime instigator of Magatsuhahi who leads true human beings astray—big-nosed Takahime is at her death agony today. Yet, what a pitiful creature she is! While you are accustomed to saying that you have no desires in this fleeting world, in reality your attachment to the fleeting world is the deepest of all, a summer thunderstorm crone who puffs up her shoulders grandly and terrifies others with a loud voice......

Ohhh, having become in your very nature indistinguishable from a demon, a snake, or a devil, can you still not realize it? A demon of terrifying attachment grows horns and comes pursuing your footsteps, and the grand plan of the Great Deity is to let you pass away right here and now. Now is the time to tuck in your tail and repent. What is your answer?

Takahime, refusing to lose, speaks once more:

Uhhh, do not utter such annoying things.

Ehhh, uttering a voice whose origin is unknown,

Ohhh, even if you try to frighten me, the living palace of Hinode Kami—no way, no way, I shall not pass away by such a trifling matter. I am this Takahime, spoken of as the sister of Mukadehime, who gave birth to Kōryūhime, famed as the queen of a single island; whoever you may be, god or Magatsuhi, you would do well to exercise a little discernment regarding matters.

From the air:

Kahhh, do not repeat yourself; I have no ears to listen. Like a procession of frogs marching blindly ahead, mountain-like gigantic frogs will appear before you, and a scheme has been laid to crush and destroy your clever mind and soul in a single bite. Even if you speak of praying to the gods in times of desperation, it is no use now that things have come to this. Since the gods will not listen, you would do well to understand it thus.

ヒヽヽ日の出神の生宮が堪忍袋の緒を切らしたら、何程偉い神でも堪りませぬぞ。

フヽヽ不都合千万な、此方の行動を非難するとは何れの神だ。

ヘヽヽ屁でもない理屈を並べて閉口さそうと思うても……ン……此高姫さまは一寸お手には合ひませぬワイ。

ホヽヽほんに訳の分らぬ廻しものだ。斯んな海の中へ我々を引張り出し、一寸先も見えぬ様な濃霧に包んで置いて、暗がりに鶏の頸を捻ぢる様な卑怯な計略、其手は喰はぬぞ。

マヽヽ曲津の張本。

ミヽヽ身の程知らずの盲目神。

ムヽヽ蜈蚣姫と高姫が。

メヽヽ各自に神力のあらむ限りを発揮して。

モヽヽ耄碌神の其方を脆くも退治して見せよう。

ヤヽヽ八岐大蛇だの、狐だの、狸だのとは何たる暴言ぞ。

イヽヽ意地気根の悪い。

ユヽヽ油断のならぬ胡散な痴呆もの。

エヽヽえー邪魔臭い。

ヨヽヽよくも、ヨタリスクを並べよつたな、ようも悪魔の変化奴。

ラヽヽ乱臣賊子、サア正体を現はせ、勇気凛々たる日の出神の生宮、大自在天の太柱、グヅグヅ吐すと貴様の素首を引き抜いてラリルレロとトンボリ返しを打たしてやらうか』

空中より一層大きな声で、

『ワヽヽ笑はせやがるワイ。我身知らずの馬鹿者共、手のつけ様のない困つた代物だ。

ヰヽヽ何程言うても合点の往かぬ歪み根性の高姫、蜈蚣姫。

ウヽヽ煩さくなつて来たワイ。艮の金神国治立尊の御前に我は是より奏上せむ。

ヱヽヽ襟を正して謹聴して待つて居らう。やがて御沙汰が下るであらう。

ヲヽヽ臆病風に誘はれてヲドヲドし乍ら、まだ。

ガヽヽ我の強い。

ギヽヽぎりぎりになる迄。

グヽヽ愚図々々致して居ると。

ゲヽヽ現界は愚か。

ゴヽヽ後生の為めに成らないぞ。

ザヽヽ態さらされて。

ジヽヽジタバタするよりも。

ズヽヽ図々しい態度を改め。

ゼヽヽ前非を悔い改心致して。

ゾヽヽ造次にも顛沛にもお詫を致せ。

ダヽヽ騙し歩いた。

ヂヽヽ自身の罪を。

ヅヽヽ津々浦々まで白状致して廻り、玉に対する執着心を只今限り綺麗薩張此海に流して仕舞へ。さうして仕舞へば又神の道に使つてやるまいものでもない。

デヽヽデンデン虫の角突き合ひの様な小さな喧嘩を致し。

ドヽヽ如何してそんな事で神界の御用が勤まると思ふか。

バヽヽ婆の癖に馬鹿な真似を致すと終には糞垂れるぞよ。

ビヽヽ貧乏揺ぎもならぬ様になりてから。

ブヽヽブツブツと水の中に屁を放いた様な小言を申しても。

ベヽヽ弁舌を何程巧に致しても。

ボヽヽ木瓜の花だ、誰も相手になる者はないぞよ。

パヽヽパチクリと目を白黒致して。

ピヽヽピンピン跳ねても、キリキリ舞ひを致しても。

プヽヽプンと放いた屁ほどの効力も無いぞよ。

ペヽヽペンペン跳ねても。

ポヽヽポンポン言つても、もう日の出神も通用致さぬから覚悟をしたが宜からう。汝果たして日の出神ならば、此濃霧を霽らし、天日の光を自ら浴びて船の方向を定め、アンボイナの聖地に渡れ。其時又結構な教訓を授けてやらう』

高姫、蜈蚣姫は返す言葉も無く、船の中に両手を合せ、負けぬ気の鬼に妨げられて謝罪り言葉も出さず、俯向いて謝罪と片意地との中間的態度を執つて居た。何時しか濃霧は霽れた。よくよく見れば船は何時の間にやら南洋一の聖地、竜宮島と聞えたるアンボイナの港に横着けになり居たりける。
