§ 2

Kiyokahime sings again.

Looking up at the vast Amatsumisora, I lament the future of the world. Both heaven and earth are wrapped in Hahiiro, and the world is about to become Tokoyami. How shall this Hahikumo clear away, without the power of my Kototama?

Blow, Tokitsukaze, in the Ōzora, and upon the earth, and upon us. As long as this Inutsu Funiki hangs over us, Hitogusa will gradually perish. The voice of the Guwanmeikorō Shishi, the voice of the Shinshinkiei horse, echoing in the north and south, becoming Naifuru and thunder, soon the Hiru-no-kuni will break. Thinking of this, even for a Katatoki, how can I rest my body and enjoy the moon on the mountain of Takakura? Though flowers bloom, though the moon shines, though birds sing, to my eyes and to my ears, all appear as the color of Baukoku, and sound as the voice of Jigoku. Ah, I wish to purify and clarify this painful present world, to restore it to the Miyo of the ancient Inka, to bring about the Hiru-no-miyako of Tengoku where Shiminbeudō prevails, birds sing, and flowers bloom in fragrance. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Kiyokahime of the flower pedestal, Harukohime where tree buds sprout, grant me, O Amatsukami, this first journey that pains the feet of one unaccustomed to treading the Takasago. Before the presence of the Kunitsukami, raising high the weak voice of the bush warbler emerging from the valley door, I pray earnestly, I pray earnestly.

To such a place, more than ten mountain bandits in masks and headscarves appeared, scattering about. Seeing the two figures, the one who seemed to be the leader of the thieves opened his giant eyes, and while surrounding them, drew his long sword in a row.

Leader: 'Hey, you women, where are you from and who are you? At a glance, your appearance, your belongings, and your clothes suggest you are not women born of ordinary commoner families. Confess your origins to Genkurō, every detail. If you disobey, be prepared for what follows from my side.'

Kiyokahime, terrified at meeting thieves for the first time, changed the color of her face and was trembling. Harukohime, while shielding the princess, resolved that even if twenty or thirty thieves were to swarm and attack with weapons, she would reveal the secret techniques of the jujutsu she had trained in daily, throw every one of them into the mountain stream, and punish them. While making this resolve, she feigned ignorance and, intentionally acting calm, placed both hands on the ground.

Haruko: 'Yes, I am a celestial being who guards Mount Takakura. You speak with a very imposing manner and seem to be asking me something, but even if you are thieves, humans should possess manners. To think of me as a weak woman and try to intimidate me from the start is not becoming of a man, is it not cowardly? I do not know what your business is, but as I am a celestial maiden who can manipulate heaven and earth freely, if it is a matter of truth, I shall listen to anything. In return, if it is something against the path of righteousness, I shall not forgive it in the slightest.'

She said this decisively. Genkurō was somewhat taken aback by the composure of Harukohime's words, but they were only two women, and he had more than ten fearless, reckless warriors with him. Whether they were celestial beings or celestial maidens, he did not know, but if he were to shrink back before such women, he would lose credibility in handling his subordinates from now on. He resolved that since he had come on so aggressively, he would force them into submission, and intentionally becoming overbearing, he said:

Genkurō: 'Hahaha, you talk big, you slut. Celestial being? That is a blatant lie. It is just a cover-up to hide your embarrassment and escape the fear of us. I am not a Genkurō-sama who can be deceived by such things. I am the devil Genkurō, who leads hundreds of subordinates at the mountain fortress of Takateruyama and threatens men and women passing by. Without spitting out any excuses, will you take off your clothes and hand them over, or will you become Genkurō's woman? Now, what is it? I shall have your answer immediately.'

Harukohime's composure became even more settled. Kiyokahime was praying with all her might in her heart for the salvation of God.

Haruko: 'Hotsuhohohohoh! So you are the Akuma Genkurau, appearing at just the right moment. I have been waiting for your shiyutsugen. It is a nuisance; even if you bring twenty or thirty deku-no-bo, they offer no resistance. Bring all your kobun and line them up before me. I shall chastise them one by one. To encounter the chiyōhon of the Akuma, Genkurau, while on my way to an akumataiji, what a good saisaki it is. One or two are a mendau. Genkurau, come, attack all at once! I shall manifest the shinriki of a Tennin and chill your liver.'

No sooner had she said this than she arranged her tasukijifuji, tightened her hachimaki behind her gentle face, and placed her hand on the hilt of her kuwaiken, taking a migama. Encouraged by this, Kiyokahime also regained her composure, and again, with her akadasuki and shirohachimaki, she drew her kuwaiken from its scabbard and, mega Genkurau, closed in step by step. Haruko-hime kept her eyes on the dozen or so kobun, standing in a migama to strike them down should they approach. Under a single command from Genkurau, the kobun were to attack the two women. However, their leader Genkurau, seeing the figures of the two, felt a slight sense of infatuation... 'No matter how strong they are, they are but two women, not even worth one of my arms; yet, it is a pity to kill such beauties in vain. I want to save them somehow...' he was already caught in love.

Haruko: 'Genkurau, why do you not lay a hand on two frail women while spouting such big words? Are you afraid of our power? Answer me. I am a propagandist saving the world. How could I fulfill the duties of a Tennin if I harbored fear of the likes of you? You are a cowardly and irresolute man.'

Genkurau: 'What? A woman or two is not even worth one of my arms, but it is a pity to scatter such a famous flower. Therefore, for a while, I am just watching you as if you were a potted flower with its roots cut. Thinking that you will soon meet a fleeting end, I cannot help but be moved to tears of sympathy. My, the more I look, the more beautiful... nay, the more adorable you are.'

Haruko: 'Ugh, how filthy! As a mere thief, to speak of a Tennin as adorable or beautiful—is that not a bit of an exaggeration? Rather than uttering unnecessary complaints, roll up your flag, tuck your tail, and leave this place at once. Ugh, how filthy! Shoo, shoo!'

With this bold and fearless behavior, as if chasing away a cat, Genkurau’s anger reached its peak.

Genkurau: 'I did not wish to commit unnecessary slaughter, but since it has come to this, a man cannot retreat due to his pride. Hey, women, I shall make you cry soon enough, so prepare yourselves... Hey, hey, kobun, attack the two of them!'

As he gave the order, the dozen or so kobun, acknowledging the command, approached the two women like the King of Asura. Although Haruko-hime defended herself with all her might while shielding Kiyokahime with one hand, the assault of the peerless, strong enemies caused her strength to fail, and they reached a critical moment where they were about to fall into his poisonous fangs. Genkurau sat on a rock, calmly smoking a cigarette, looking down at the scene. At the very instant Kiyokahime was about to be captured, a propaganda song was heard, vibrating the surrounding air. Ah, what will be the outcome of this?

春子『源九郎とやら、其方は大言を吐き乍ら、なぜ孱弱き二人の女に手出しをせぬのか、吾々の威勢に恐れてゐるのか、返答せい。妾は天下を救ふ宣伝使だ。汝如きに怖れを抱いて、どうして天人の職が勤まらうぞ、卑怯未練な男だなア』

源『ナアニ、タカが女の一人や二人、片腕にも足らねども、可惜名花を散らすは惜いものだ。それ故暫時、根株を切つた鉢植の花だと思つて眺めてゐるのだ。やがて果敢ない終りを告げるだらうと思へば、聊か同情の涙にくれぬ事もないわい。テモ偖も見れば見る程、美しい……イヤいぢらしい者だワイ』

春子『エヽ汚らはしい、泥棒の分際として、天人に向ひ、いぢらしいとか、美しいとか、チツと過言であらうぞ。要らざる繰言申すよりも旗をまき尾をふつて、此場を早く立去れ、エヽ汚らはしい、シーツ　シーツ』

と猫でも逐ふ様な大胆不敵の挙動に、源九郎は怒り心頭に達し、

源『要らざる殺生はしたくなけれ共、モウ斯うなれば後へは退かれぬ男の意地、コリヤ女、今に吠面かわかしてみせる、覚悟をいたせ……ヤアヤア乾児共、両人に向つて斬りつけよ』

と下知すれば、心得たりと、十数人の乾児は二人の女に向つて、阿修羅王の如くに迫り来る。春子姫は一方の手で、清香姫を庇ひ乍ら、力限りに防ぎ戦へども、剛力無双の敵の襲撃、早くも力尽きて、彼が毒牙にかからむとする危機一髪の場合となつて来た。源九郎は岩上に腰打かけ、平然として煙草を燻らし乍ら、此光景を見下ろしてゐる。清香姫は今や捉へられむとする一刹那、あたりの空気を振動させて宣伝歌が聞えて来た。あゝ此結果は何うなるであらうか。
