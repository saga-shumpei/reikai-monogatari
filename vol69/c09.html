§ 1

At the absent lord's residence of Aishiu, the chivalrous man of Yokokōji, the senior gang leaders of Genshiu, Heishiu, Tōshiu, Kichikō, Sanshiu, Senshiu, Saushiu, and Mokushiu have gathered hundreds of their subordinates, stirring up a slight sense of anxiety over the tardiness of their oyabun's return, while downing cold saké and intensely deliberating on a countermeasure.

Heishiu: 'Hey, Elder Genshiu, today makes the tenth day since the oyabun was arrested, but not even a whisper or a fart has come from him yet. As subordinates, we cannot just sit idly by and watch like this. Ain't there some scheme to rescue him out?'

Genshiu: 'Well, waiting through today should be all right. Venerable Spring Princess of Takasagojiyō graciously mediated, and... though it cannot be helped to say right away, she grandly declared before the multitude that she would surely rescue him within ten days... so there can hardly be any mistake. We believe in the precious words of the princess and are waiting quietly. In exchange, if the oyabun cannot return by the end of today, we too cannot remain unconcerned. We intend to gather every single ally and go forth with extreme measures. Well, for the moment, grant it to me and wait.'

Heishiu: 'Humph, that may be so, but warn't she just saying that to stall for time and bamboozle the likes of us? If so, we have our own resolution prepared, you know.'

Iwakō, from the side,

Iwakō: 'Hey, Elder, don't worry. If you speak of Spring Princess of Takasagojiyō, she is a deeply merciful goddess of universal humanism, endowed with an understanding of the times. Even if it is delayed by a day or two, she will surely fulfill what she uttered, even at the cost of her life, so it would be best to quietly wait here.'

Heishiu: 'Don't speak in such an arrogant tone, you being at the very bottom of the senile tier. What would the likes of you understand about that? Spouting off as if you know when you've never even worshipped Spring Princess. This ain't your stage to go poking your nose into such a place. Off with you and go clean the toilets.'

Iwakō: 'Well, since I cannot go against what you say, Elder, it ain't like I won't clean the toilets, but isn't today the critical moment of deliberation where it's make or break? No matter how much of a lowly underling I am, I'm certainly part of the great oyabun's inner circle. My sincere heart in thinking of the oyabun doesn't differ a bit, whether I'm an elder or an underling. If it were any other matter, I'd observe proper order and wouldn't pop up here to express opinions, but since this is a major issue concerning the oyabun's very person, please let me voice my opinion too.'

Heishiu: 'With the decrepit brain of a senile old fogey, how could any resolution to an important problem ever be reached? You cheeky little smart aleck, go on and stay over there... truly, what an annoying fellow you are. This Heishiu here, though you might not know what I think, am the founder of the Desperate Resolve Society. Elder Genshiu and the two of us, asked by the senior councilors of Isahiko, created the foundation of the Desperate Resolve Society. Although we hold up Lord Aishiu as our oyabun, the founders of the Desperate Resolve Society are still us, after all. So to speak, we are the gods of chivalry. Chivalry has its own laws, so you lot keep your proper order and stay put.'

Tōshiu: 'Hey, Elder Heishiu, you shouldn't put him down so mercilessly. Even this Iwakō has a side a bit different from ordinary subordinates. In such precious circumstances, it should be necessary to listen to anyone's opinion for reference.'

Heishiu: 'That may be so, but making that somehow disagreeable face and cutting in with sarcastic quips makes me mad as hell and I can't stand it. This Iwakō might just turn out to be a cold-hearted traitor, who knows. His gaze somehow looks suspicious as hell, and I can't help it. However, because the oyabun always says "Iwa, Iwa" and drags him along as baggage wherever he goes like a clinging purse, we've been letting him off thinking the oyabun might have some thought behind it, but he's truly an irritating bastard. Carrying around a mindset like a senior councilor of Takasagojiyō, that's what he's doing.'
