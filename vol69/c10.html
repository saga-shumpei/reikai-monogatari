Chapter 10: 宣両 [1755]

宣両〔1755〕

§ 1

In a very lonely Udonya in Kaharamachi on the outskirts of Jiyōka, four or five Wakamono are drinking cup-sake with Udon as a snack, looking at the Shinbun delivered to them, and letting the flowers of conversation bloom in various ways.

Kō: 'Hey, the world has become quite Butsusō, hasn't it? Just look at the Shinbun these days. Isn't there one or two things appearing that make our blood run cold, no matter when you look? The Ōjishin in the capital of Hiru, the Haretsu of Mount Etna... and then, Kuniteruwake-sama of the capital of Chin has abandoned a Keneu Chii and become a Jinrikishiyafu, or perhaps the Raujiu Ihaharuwake has had his Inbō Roken and hidden himself, or something of that sort. Isahiko Raujiu's Kanai, a Taruonna of great Taihiyō and Himan named Tarunohime, has caught something called Sadismus or other, and is slashing and hitting people, and is being thrown into Raugoku. The Oyabun of Yokokōji is being thrown into prison; it is a great uproar. And Harunohime-sama is very high-collar, riding a horse and dashing all around, and on top of that, there are robbers here, fires there, murders, adulteries, bankruptcies of companies and banks, embezzlement by executives, fraud, and the fall of the ruble notes; truly, the world is at its end. I am so worried about how we are to continue our lives from here on out.'

Otsu: 'That is why the general public is threatened by the voices of the difficulty of living, the human heart is deteriorating day by day, and Socialism and Anarchism are spreading. This, too, is an influence of the times, so it cannot be helped.'

Kō: 'Even if you say that, we were born in a country of the family system, so I do not want to agree with ideologies that would break the family system. There are various arguments on the Shinbun paper about the abolition of private property or the nationalization of land, but it has become a troubled society, hasn't it.'

Otsu: 'There is no need to be troubled, is there? You speak so grandly, but as someone who does not own even a cat's-forehead-sized piece of land and has even pawned his wife's yumaki, it is not for you to spit out whether you like or dislike the family system. For us, Socialism or Anarchism is fine. As long as humans have an attachment to life, if we do not lead a complete life, there is no choice but to retire from being human, as it would be worse than cattle or horses.'

Hei: 'Yeah, that is right. Even I am being worked to the bone from morning till night for the sake of the bourgeois, and because of the inconsistency between labor and wages, I am living a miserable life. I do not even have a change of clothes for my wife, I cannot even buy a pair of geta for my children when the New Year comes, and I am resigned to a life where my large family is charged high rent and we sleep like fish in a three-mat or two-mat room, so I truly think there is no value in living in this world at all. In the teachings of San-go-kyō, it is said that God will appear on the surface and distinguish between good and evil, and I wish to meet a world where a saving God appears quickly, corrects this violent, cruel, self-centered, and survival-of-the-fittest world, and where we can obtain the happiness and peace of equality for all four classes. If I speak of such things in a loud voice, I will be immediately caught by the authorities and fed smelly rice; it is truly a time when one cannot hold one's head high if one is a weakling.'

Otsu: 'That is why Anarchism and Socialism have begun to raise their heads. It has been said since long ago that God will appear on the surface, but it does not seem like He will appear at all, does it? I hear that the Savior will appear from the heaven of the East, but there has never been an instance where the light or the divine spirit that saves us has appeared; it is truly a bitter and dark world.'

While they were talking like this, the voice of a propaganda song was heard.

As she sang this, the young female Sendenshi passed in front of the shop. Kō, Otsu, Hei, Tei, Bo, and the others chased after the Sendenshi, following her wherever she went. The Sendenshi, wearing a veil, a straw raincoat and hat, and dressed in hand guards, leggings, and straw sandals, struck her Kongō-zue staff while keeping time with her feet, and proceeded without knowing that the five men were following her.

Coming from the other side, riding on a horse, was Aisu, a chivalrous man from Yokokōji. While still on horseback, Aisu stood at the crossroads and began to sing in a loud voice.

'People of the capital of Chin, awaken quickly! The world of material civilization has already come to an end. This country of ours appeared in Takamagahara in the olden days. Momogamihiko descended from heaven, and while letting fall the dew of mercy, protected the ancestors of Nanjira. He established the land of Kami, a land of Shinjin-wairaku, to stand forever. The land of Chin is indeed the land of Kami. Establishing the holy Oshihe of equality of the four classes and universal love, and while the government and the people took steps in unison, the world was a peaceful country, and it was an era that was governed in tranquility. However, from the recent era, the crooked teachings of a strange country, which is a Tokoyo, have spread into the land of Chin. First of all, the souls of the ugly officials who stand above have been deluded by materialism. They have incessantly instilled the teaching of the survival of the fittest and self-interest. The suffering of the living beings is like a night boat on the Shirakawa; they sleep with high pillows, and there is no peaceful rest in the Taikakaurō. They are Magatsu-kami who do not know, while receiving the ridicule of holding sinecures and eating without working. What the upper class does, the lower class imitates. If the upper stream is muddy, the lower stream becomes muddy. The middle-class fellows are all overwhelmed by the upper class, and today, when the backbone of the nation has hidden its shadow, what shall we do? With no means left, we pray to Kami, and to save the suffering living beings, we have organized the Haisui-kai. While taking it upon ourselves to act with chivalry, we shall break the noses of those wretches who monopolize the rights of the living beings and engage in private profit, and we shall carry out morphine injections. If they still do not realize it themselves, I have the sword of justice. For the sake of the land of Chin, I am prepared to sacrifice my precious life, and in place of the living beings, I intend to carry out a great cleanup. People rich in benevolence and righteousness, people strong in chivalry, gather under our banner and participate in the great work that shakes heaven and earth! The time is now. Heaven and earth are overturned, the upper and lower are reversed, and good and evil, right and wrong are mistaken.

Evil people prosper more and more in the world, and good people are about to perish. Even while seeing this current situation, those who stand by with folded arms to protect their own safety, even if their appearance is human, their bodies are but vessels for beasts. Awaken quickly, awaken! Cast off the old clothes filled with falsehood, suspicion, and sin, and change into new clothes filled with benevolence, progress, and happiness. There is no blade that can oppose justice. To us who follow sincerity, how could there not be the protection of Kami? Living beings, living beings, rise up! Rise up and overthrow the ugly kind. If Nanjira do not rise up and overthrow them, Nanjira shall soon perish. The Yomi-zaka of human rise and fall.'

Dona: 'Shake, oh shake, for now is the time!'

The Onnasendenshi looked at the figure of Aishiu from behind her Himenpu, and perhaps thinking something, she stealthily hid herself in a Yokomichi. This was Harunohime, who had disguised herself as a Sendenshi and was going around the Shichiu for Senden. The five men who had followed the Onnasendenshi were so captivated by Aishiu's Enzetsu that they did not even notice they had lost sight of the Onnasendenshi's Yukuhe.

Aishiu turned his horse's head back, aiming toward the direction of his home in the Yokokōji, and sang as he rode back in abundance upon his horse:

'The Kami shall appear in the open, To distinguish between good, evil, wrong, and right. Though this Mioshihe has been passed down From ancient times to the present, Never have I heard of an instance Where the Kami has appeared until now. To save the Butsushitsukai of the Gendai, And to construct the Tengoku of the Kami, Unless it be by the power of a Shinjin, Who is equal to the Kami in his Nikutai, This world shall never be able to stand. We have felt this truth in our hearts. Leaving behind the land of Hiru, We advance into this capital. First, making our own bodies the Daiichi, As a Gisei, we show the model of Saisei. To awaken the eyes of the sleeping monks, Sendenshi, Biku, and Bikuni of this world, And to wash away the mud of this world, We have steeled our resolve and become Kyōkaku. Morning and evening, we strive To fulfill the true purpose of human love. Ah, Kannagara Kami-Kami, Kami is the true spirit universal to all things, And man is the vessel to govern the Keirin of heaven and earth. Kami and man are united here, To manifest a great, infinite, absolute, beginningless, and endless power. As shown in the Mioshihe of the Ananaikyō, I, Aishiu, a Kyōkaku filled with chivalry, Have resolved to manifest this in reality. No matter what obstacles may arise, Do not fear, do not yield, do not hesitate. Kami is with thee. How could any evil assail the body of one protected by Kami? Come, all people, rise up! Take courage, take courage for the sake of the world. Cast aside wealth and honor, And devote yourselves as a Gisei for the world of Kami that is to come. Ah, Kannagara Kami-Kami, May the spirit be blessed.'

As he sang, he headed toward his own mansion, returning grandly on horseback. Along the path, people gathered like mountains, and the popularity in the Shichiu was like a boiling cauldron.
