The soul of a man of Yamato is firmer than iron or steel. Take courage, my followers! Advance, advance to the land of Hiru! Even if Mount Takateru is steep, even if the blowing storm is fierce, and even if the path ahead is long, how could a man of Otokodate falter? To be praised in the world as a man among men, and to save the world—this is our hope, this is our desire. Ah, Kamunagara, may the spirits be blessed.'

Kuniteruwake's group of five—Komaji, Ichi, Uma, and Asa—arrived at this place, wearing twisted headbands, their pitch-black arms thrust out, dragging their dust-covered, hairy shins. Seeing and hearing the strange cries of people, and sensing that this was no ordinary matter, they approached to find a large man cornering two frail women. Kuniteruwake, thinking this the moment to prove his manhood, stripped down to a single loincloth and leaped into the fray, shouting in a loud voice,

Kuniteruwake: 'Wait, wait! I have taken charge of this fight.'

Standing with his legs spread wide, his voice caused everyone to retreat two or three ken to catch their breath. Genkurō looked at him coldly and said,

Genkurō: 'Hey, I do not know what kind of fool you are, but since you have entered our fight, you had better settle it properly. If you are just going to offer empty greetings, you had better not. Do you have the guts to finish this?'

Kuniteruwake: 'Hahaha! You senile fools, listen well. Are you not wicked thieves who rob travelers? Though I may look like this, I am a chivalrous man of the world. I am a brother who does not spare his life for the sake of justice. I did not come here to mediate; I came to punish you for robbing travelers. How about it? Will you and all your men repent and become true human beings? I await your answer.'

Genkurō: 'Wahahaha! You mantis-like fool, I am not the kind of Genkurō to be frightened by such threats. We strip people bare and steal their wealth. If anyone resists, I will take their life to achieve my goal, however regrettable that may be. Instead of making empty threats, you had better leave at once, just as you are, in your bare skin. If you continue to meddle, I will take your life, regretfully.'

Kuniteruwake: 'Hahahaha! They say that thieves are brazen, and that is well said. If you can take it, then try!'

Genkurō, his hair standing on end, commanded,

Genkurō: 'Hey, my followers, why are you hesitating? There are only four or five chivalrous men; tear them apart!'

Upon his order, more than ten petty thieves attacked from all directions. Kiyokohime and Harukohime, gaining strength from this, dodged the enemy, moving back and forth, slashing and mowing them down. In an instant, the dozen or so men had their noses sliced, arms grazed, and legs stabbed, and they fled in disarray, looking pathetic. Seeing this, Genkurō, with unmanly cowardice, swung his sword toward the summit of Mount Takateru, protecting his rear and dragging his allies away as he fled.
