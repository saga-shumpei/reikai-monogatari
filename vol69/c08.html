Chapter 8: Spring 乃 Love [1753]

春乃愛〔1753〕

§ 1

Ever since Kuniteruwake, the heir apparent of Takasagojiyō, mysteriously vanished from within the castle without a trace, Matsuwakahiko and Isahiko had desperately undertaken his search. Matsuwakahiko, serving as senior statesman, supervised affairs of state both great and small, while Isahiko employed men such as Kenpei and Torishimari, devoting his entire strength exclusively to the search for the heir apparent Kuniteruwake. However, upon being widely publicized in the newspapers that he had disguised himself as a rickshaw puller and slipped into the Fukamizochiyō rickshaw station, they were even more deeply astounded; disguising themselves, they probed the alleys of the city day and night without distinction, meticulously examining the physical traits of anyone resembling a rickshaw puller, and pursued their active search outside their home. Furthermore, upon hearing that Aisu, the chivalrous swordsman of Yokokōji, had delivered a seditious speech and that signs of public demoralization were increasingly visible, Isahiko undertook the duty of searching himself, with sleepy, swollen eyes.

Isahiko's wife, Tarunohime, was an unsightly woman embracing a massive belly resembling a four-toe sake cask. Moreover, she was a patient suffering from extreme sadomasochism. Now, sadomasochism does not refer to jealousy or hatred, but rather denotes a pathological state in which an individual derives sexual excitement and satisfaction by inflicting upon the person of the opposite sex whom they love most such cruel acts as ordinary people could not even imagine; from a medical standpoint, such a category of human beings is called sadomasochism, or sexual sadism. Such a perverted sex maniac is one for whom almost no eminent physician or medicine holds any hope of cure. Tarunohime was afflicted by this malady. Possessing a greasy, obese, and robust physique, she was utterly unable to suppress the agitation of her sexual desires; growing the horns of jealousy within her empty bedchamber night after night, and seeing her husband Isahiko fail to return evening after evening, she thought that perhaps he had cultivated a new romance elsewhere. 'I am originally an unsightly female, and by no means possess a style that a husband would love. For him to constantly stay away from home every single night like this, when he should be one of the senior ministers of the nation, and never show his face even to his wife, he must certainly be dragging some woman into some rendezvous somewhere and grinning foolishly. If it comes to this, excessive fondness turns a hundred times into bitterness. When my master finally returns home today, I shall load a pistol with a bullet, aim straight for the center of his brow, and strike him dead in a single blow; then I shall fry the genital organ between his thighs—which has become the plaything of some hateful rival woman—and feed it to the foxes,' she thought, burning with a dreadful flame of spite, growing the horns of jealousy, standing her hair on end like a madwoman, drawing her eyebrows vertically, and waiting in readiness while emitting sighs. Toward retainers and maidservants she vented her spleen on every possible occasion; whatever she looked at or touched grated against her nerves, she shattered pieces of furniture, pulled her husband's garments out from chest drawers and ripped them apart, and smashed everything his husband had used—including tableware, geta, and shoes—into tiny fragments, until there was no longer any way to soothe her. At that moment, someone arrived saying they were a messenger from Spring Yoshikohime-sama of Takasagojiyō, and delivered a letter addressed to Isahiko.

Tarunohime briskly took the letter into her hands, and upon looking at the envelope, she saw written: 'To Senior Minister Isahiko-dono, from Yoshikohime.' Upon beholding these letters, she once again stood her hair on end, clenched her teeth, and while making her large nostrils dilate, tore the seal off with a snap; when she unfolded and looked at it, there appeared the most beautiful traces of brushwork, written fluidly and smoothly in the Okie school style. Tarunohime... 'Now I have grasped good evidence...' she said, panting heavily as she read on:

I hereby present these few lines to you. The other day, you spoke to me with various kinds of kindness concerning my personal circumstances; even though I thought at first I must refuse, considering the future of the nation, and furthermore weighing the opinions of my parents, I have accepted your sincere heart, and it has come to pass that I shall finally comply with your noble intent—this is already a matter known to you. Hereafter, let us open our hearts to one another, cut down the barriers between high and low, embrace and return to oneness, and open up our innermost minds to each other; adopting a friendly attitude just as in the affection between husband and wife, I desire to exert my utmost efforts for the sake of the nation, and thus I notify you of my intention. Happily and respectfully.

So it is written. Then, Tarunohime of Sadethmus pierced the heavens with her enraged hair, suddenly displaying her cruel nature, wielding a pistol and a great sword in her left and right hands, and as a parting tip, either shooting, slashing down, or cutting apart the stewards, housekeepers, and maids. Regarding everyone she met along the thoroughfare as an enemy, she hacked and mowed them down, rampaging wildly... The whereabouts of my enemy are within Takasagojiyō... with her hair disheveled like a yashiya, foaming at the mouth, she rampaged on. Coincidentally, she encountered her husband Isahiko riding a swift horse toward this direction at the Baba of Takasagojiyō, abruptly severed the horse's legs, pierced a wind hole in the horse's belly, and made it tumble down on the spot. Without recognizing her own wife in that transformed appearance of distorted features, Isahiko commanded Kenpei and the officers to capture her without difficulty, and had her thrown into the prison at the edge of the town.

Tarunohime was thrown into the neighboring prison cell where Aishu, the boss of the chivalrous commoners, was bound, with her limbs tightly tied. And becoming almost half-crazed, she was chattering away recklessly.

Tarunohime: 'Ah, how regrettable, how vexing, what crime did I have to be shoved into such an ugly prison? I am none other than Tarunohime-sama, the wife of Isahiko, who was respected as a senior councilor in Takasagojiyō. And yet, that wretch Kenpei, without knowing my noble status, blindly ties me up and throws me into a filthy dungeon—what is the meaning of this? Before long, I will pay you back, so you had better realize it. Ah, how regrettable, hah, how vexing. Once these bindings are loosened, I will smash such a flimsy prison with a single blow, and starting with Yoshikohime who snatched away my husband, I will pull out the raw head of my husband Isahiko, and show you all how I take vengeance on my enemies. Hate the priest and you even hate his robe. The governor of Kuniyawake, the youngest daughter princess, and Matsuwakahime—I will hack and mow them down from one end to the other, and how can I not vent my resentment? Now that things have come to this, Tarunohime is a demon, a devil, a Yashiya Myōō, an Ashura-ō. Even if I turn this world into a muddy sea, how could I ever leave my resentment unvented?'

Grit-grit-gritting her teeth, she shouts the same thing over and over, day and night without distinction.

Aishu, bound in the neighboring room, feels interest upon hearing Tarunohime's mad soliloquy and sings.

'Since it is a dark world of the black jewel, the Magatsugami Steals even into the depths of the prison. Sadethmus is sick, raging mad night and day Tarunohime maddened by violent sexual desire. Thus he is humming.
