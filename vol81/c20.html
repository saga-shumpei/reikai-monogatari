Chapter 20: 悔悟の Flower [2047]

悔悟の花〔2047〕

§ 1

Nārisu, the Samoritsukasa who had left the Jiyōnai after leaving a Sutezerifu for the fake King Eemusu, the fake Princess Chinriu, and the Uba Araragi, appeared with hundreds of Naito in tow, marching in perfect Taigo through the streets in Daikonran where the Gunshiu were clamoring, and standing at the Jifujiro, he sang in a loud voice.

Nārisu: 'Kunitsukami of the land of Sāru, / People of Kitayamajiyō, / Be calmed, for I am / Nārisu, who attacked the land of Idomu / And raised the Kachidoki. / Now having become the Kami of Samori / And returned to Kitayamajiyō, / King Eemusu has had his Inochi / Stolen by an Akugami and is strange. / A fake Eemusu has Kunrin, / And the Akugiyakubudau Araragi / Has had her daughter pose as the consort / And wielded Bōi. / But the Dantai of Aikokushishi / Attacked the Akumara, / And they vanished like smoke in an instant. / Now that it has come to this, people, / There is no need to make a disturbance any longer. / While protecting the land of Sāru in Tokoshihe / And in Heianbuji, / Be at peace in your respective trades / And enjoy your Kurashi of the day. / I shall now return to the Jiyōnai / Leading the Naito, / And with the resolve to restore / The completely disordered order / And establish a righteous government. / Kunitsukami and people of the land, / You should set your hearts at ease. / Be calmed, all people. / And you, the hundred other Kunitsukami, / Sheathe your spears for now. / Do you, the people of the land, not fear / The achievements of King Eerusu, / Who has gone far away / To bring the land of Idomu to submission / And reigns in glory? / If King Eerusu returns to this land / With his many armies once again, / Your Kurashi will surely become / Even more peaceful and joyful. / Be calmed at once, quickly. / I am Nārisu of Samori. / The true Akumara have perished. / There should be no reason / To raise waves on flat ground; quickly / Be calmed as you were before. / Afterwards, we shall listen in detail / To your wishes, / And shall fulfill your objectives.'

Just as he sang this, a Yūshi came advancing gracefully on horseback, pushed by the Gunshiu from the other side, and it was the renowned Yūzuki.

Yūzuki: 'The Kitayamajiyō, / Where the Akumara swarmed day and night, / Has been calmed. / The sincere heart of the brave men / That we lead, thinking of the country, / Must have reached both heaven and earth. / The Akugiyakubudau Araragi, / The treacherous and cunning Senriu, / And even the fake King Eemusu, / Who was rumored to be the spirit of a salamander, / Have now completely perished. / Now that it has come to this, we / Shall make the Kami of Samori our strength, / And cleanse and clarify the disordered Kunihara / To be as it was before, / Making it an age of supreme peace and prosperity. / Ah, Kototama, Kototama, / By the grace of the Kami of heaven and earth, / The Magagamis who bring calamity to the land / Have completely hidden their shadows. / You people, set your hearts at ease. / The land of Sāru has been born. / The Kunihara that was going to ruin / Is now revived by the sincere hearts / Of you, the Gunshiu; what joy! / Now, from here on, people of the land, / Trust in Nārisu the Samori, / And entrusting all things to him, / Live in peace of heart. / Ah, Kototama, Kototama, / Before the divine majesty of the Kami, / We offer our gratitude.'

Thus, the Samori and Yūzuki met at the Jifujiro while still leading the masses, and while celebrating with each other that the riot had been safely quelled, Yūzuki sang first.

Yūzuki: 'The clouds of eternal darkness have cleared, and the moon and sun shine clearly. / You are indeed the Kami of Samori, for you have untangled the knots of this disordered world. / The Magagamis have all perished and disappeared. / From here on, I shall rely upon you, my lord.'

Nārisu: 'I returned from the distant land of Idomu, / And saw this disturbance before much time had passed. / Making the sincere heart of Lord Yūzuki my strength, / I shall serve in Kitayamajiyō.'

From this point on, Nārisu the Samori, together with Yūzuki, the leader of the Aikokushishi Dantai, protected by Naito, entered deep into the Jiyōnai, took care of all the aftermath, and gathered the high-ranking retainers in one room to hold a celebration for the pacification of the national disorder. The main attendees were Nārisu, followed by Yūzuki, Takitsuse, Yama-kaze, Aoyama, Murasaki, Tamayama, and dozens of other high-ranking retainers.

Aoyama: 'By the divine majesty of the Kami of heaven and earth and the Samoritsukasa / And the Yūzuki-tsukasa, it has been settled. / The Kunihara, which had been disordered like mowed reeds, / Has been settled by your power. / The Kunitsukami and the people of the land had been suffering and gasping / Under the tyranny. / If it is like this, there is nothing to worry about; the land of Sāru / Will surely prosper more and more.'

原文（近代文語）

贋のエームス王や、贋のチンリウ姫を始め、乳母アララギに捨台詞を残し城内を立ち出でたる左守司のナーリスは、群衆の犇き立てる大混乱の巷に数百の騎士を従へ、隊伍整然として現はれ来り、十字路に立ちて、声高らかに歌ふ。

『サールの国の国津神 木田山城の人々よ 鎮まり給へ吾こそは イドムの国に攻め寄せて 勝鬨あげしナーリスよ 今は左守の神となり 木田山城に帰りしが エームス王は悪神に 生命奪はれ怪しかる 贋のエームス君臨し 悪逆無道のアララギが 娘が妃となりすまし 暴威を振るひ居たりしが 愛国志士の団体に 攻め立てられし悪魔等は 忽ち煙と消えにけり かくなる上は人々よ 最早騒ぐに及ぶまじ サールの国を永久に 平安無事に守りつつ

各業に安んじて 其の日の生活を楽しめよ 吾は之より城内に 騎士を率ゐて立帰り 乱れ果てたる秩序をば 全く元に立て直し 善政を布かむ覚悟なり 国津神等国人よ 心を安んじ給ふべし 鎮まり給へ諸人よ 其の他百の国津神 一先づ鉾を納めませ エールス王は遥々と イドムの国を言向けて 時めき給ふ功績を 汝等国人恐れずや エールス王が軍隊を 数多引連れ此の国に 再び帰りますならば 汝が生活は弥益も

安く楽しくありぬべし 一時に鎮まれ疾く早く 吾は左守のナーリスよ 真の悪魔は亡びたり 平地に波を起すべき 理由は無からむ速かに 元の如くに鎮まれよ 後は吾々汝等が 望みを詳細に聞え上げ 其の目的を達すべし』

斯く歌ふ折しも、向ふの方より群衆に押されながら、馬上ゆたかに進み来る勇士は、音に名高き夕月なりけり。

夕月は歌ふ。

『悪魔の昼夜にはびこりし 木田山城は鎮まりぬ 吾等の率ゆる大丈夫の 御国を思ふ真心は 天と地とに通じけむ 暴逆無道のアララギも 奸佞邪智なるセンリウも 蠑螈の精と聞えたる 贋のエームス王までも 今は全く亡びたり もう此の上は吾々は 左守の神を力とし 乱れ果てたる国原を 清め澄まして元の如 至治太平の世となさむ ああ惟神々々 天地の神の御恵みに 国に仇なす曲神は 全く影を隠しけり 汝等心を安んぜよ

サールの国は生れたり 亡び行くなる国原は 汝等群衆の真心に 蘇りたる嬉しさよ いざ是よりは国人よ ナーリス左守を信頼し 一切万事を委ねつつ 心平穏に引けよかし ああ惟神々々 神の御稜威の御前に 感謝を捧げ奉る』

斯くて左守と夕月は十字街頭に大衆を率ゐたるままで邂逅し、互に暴動の無事治まりしを祝し合ひつつ、夕月は先づ歌ふ。

『常暗の雲は晴れにつ久方の 月日は清く輝き渡れり 汝こそは左守の神よ乱れたる 此の世の縺れを解かせ給へり 曲神は残らず亡び失せにけり いざ是よりは君に頼らむ』

ナーリスは歌ふ。

『遥々とイドムの国より帰り来し 間もあらなくに此の騒ぎみし 夕月の君の真心力とし 吾は仕へむ木田山城に』

これより左守のナーリスは、愛国団体の隊長夕月と共に騎士に守られ、城内深く浸入し、一切万事の後片附をなし、重臣等を一間に集めて国乱鎮定の祝賀会を催しける。重なる参会者はナーリスを初め夕月、滝津瀬、山風、青山、紫、玉山等の数十人の重臣なりける。

青山は歌ふ。

『天地の神の御稜威と左守司 夕月司に治まりしはや 刈菰の乱れ果てたる国原も 君の力に治まりにけり 国津神国人等は悪政に 苦しめられて喘ぎ居しはや かくならば思ふことなしサールの国は いや益々に栄え行くらむ』

§ 2

Murasaki sings:

Murasaki: 'Hidden in the countryside for long days, I have come to investigate the state of the land in detail. Though I warned repeatedly that an extraordinary crisis would arise, my words went unheeded. With many Maga lurking within the castle, the country has fallen into ever-increasing disorder. A strange woman, Araragi, has proclaimed her hegemony, and Kitayamajiyō has turned to darkness. Though purple clouds drift in the Misora, clouds in the Nakazora have covered all in black. My anxiety over what the future might hold has turned into a mere dream. Since the King of Eales went out to battle, the land of Sahl has fallen into deeper chaos.'

Tamayama sings:

Tamayama: 'A strange woman came from Idumu, and the land of Sahl has been thrown into disorder. Because of the demonic woman captured and brought back as a prisoner, Kitayamajiyō has begun to tilt. Now that this day has come, our hearts are at peace. I have spent my mornings and evenings racking my brains over how to save the life of a country that was on the verge of ruin.'

Yamakaze sings:

Yamakaze: 'What a demonic woman she is, to have bewitched the Mikokoro of our young king of Aems. The young king of Aems, deceived by her Mashiyō, has had his Inochi brought to a Haka end. The salamander, said to be the master of the iris pond within the castle, has lost its king. From now on, command the spirit of the salamander to clear away the calamities of the land.'

Takitsuse sings:

Takitsuse: 'The flow of the Kitagawa has become terribly muddy. Since the demonic woman was captured and brought here, I have wished to let the King of Idumu know of this state of peaceful governance. Our King has destroyed the castle of Idumu and achieved merit for eternity. If I were to show the King of Idumu the figure of this pacified land, he would surely rejoice.'

Yūzuki sings:

Yūzuki: 'Having served in Kitajiyō for a long time, I have lamented the age as it descended into chaos. Ever since Araragi entered Kitayamajiyō, the hearts of the people have been in turmoil. Many times I thought to strike down Araragi, but I endured it to no avail. The time of Heaven has finally arrived, and leading the masses, I have struck down the Maga. Protected by the grace of the gods, I feel the joy of fulfilling my long-held desire. Just at that moment, hearing that the Samori no Tsukasa was returning, I stood up with courage. An army that has attained the harmony of the people shall never perish and will always be victorious. I shall take upon myself the sin of having disturbed the castle and descend to the countryside.'

Samori no Tsukasa Nārisu sings:

Nārisu: 'With the gathering of the pure hearts of the people, the shadows of evil have vanished and perished. The chaos of the karigomo has finally been quelled, and as I proclaim the celebration before the gods, I find that by following the words of the King of Eales, the country was in disorder upon my hurried return. If my return to the country had been delayed even a little longer, the land of Sahl would surely have been self-destroyed.'

Just as this was sung, the vice-general Chinrin, who had returned in flight leading several thousand knights, advanced deep into the inner palace and, while offering a salute to the Samori no Kami Nārisu, sang:

Chinrin: 'The King of Eales has sadly departed from this world. Princess Satsukusu has also lost her life. The Samori of Chikuta, as well as the military strategists of Eaman, have all perished together. The forces of King Adzumi have surged back strongly, and our allies have been broken with ease. Thinking that there was no further use for the land of Idumu, I led my knights and returned in haste.'

Upon this report, the Samori and the others, including Yūzuki, turned pale and remained in a state of stunned silence for a while.

Nārisu sings in astonishment:

Nārisu: 'Could I have imagined that our King, whose fame for martial valor was so high, would depart from this world? Hearing that the consort of Satsukusu has also lost her life, I cannot bear the sorrow. I wish to hear how it came to be that even the Samori and the military strategists have passed away. To hear this news just as I was rejoicing in the pacification of the land of Sahl...'

Chinrin sings:

Chinrin: 'Though I know not the reason why, our King must have met with the censure of the gods. From what the people say, it seems the Lord God has decreed it all as a warning. In any case, if this is the retribution for having seized the land of others, there is no remedy.'

The Samori sings:

此の報告に左守を始め夕月其の他の面々は、顔色をサツと変へ、茫然として暫し無言の幕を続け居たりける。

チンリンは歌ふ。

左守は歌ふ。

'How fearful are the things I hear, the state of war / Where one seeks to seize another's land / The lineage of the King of Eales has perished / How shall we protect the land of Saar?'

Yufuzuki sings with a look of dismay.

'Be that as it may, for people, simply / One must walk the path of truth / Even our King, whose power shines like the sun and moon / Lives in a world where there is a time to perish / From today, making truth alone my strength / I shall govern the land of Saar in peace.'

Takitsuse sings.

'Lured by the ugly Magatsu called greed / The King has lost his Mikuni / Had he remained in this vast land of Saar / Such lamentations would not have been / As a retribution for relying too much on his own power / The King has lost his Inochi / The fate of the King of Eales / Who met the misery of Zenmetsu was lonely / Without the truth of Aizen, the human body / Both body and soul shall eventually perish.'

Yamakaze sings.

'Crossing over the steep Ōsakayama / I grieve for the King who cast away his Inochi / Our King attacked the castle of Idumu / And cast away his noble Inochi / Though I think that even if I grieve it will not suffice / Still, it is a night of such lamentation / If one proceeds in all things with truth alone / I believe there shall be no errors in the world.'

Samori sings.

'If it be so, then there is no help for it, we / Shall only proceed on the path of truth / The King of Eales has left us a warning / For Toha as he departed / Fearing and revering the Kami of Ametsuchi / We must proceed on the path of truth.'

Having finished singing thus, Narisu of Samori announced the misfortune of the King of Eales' family to the general public within the castle, gathered the representatives of the people to conduct a grand funeral ceremony, constructed a solemn shrine for the Lord God within the castle of Kidayama, and never neglected to pray that the government be conducted righteously morning and evening.

斯く歌ひ終り左守のナーリスは、城内一般にエールス王一族の不幸を発表し、国民の代表者を集めて盛大なる葬の式を執り行ひ、木田山の城内に荘厳なる主の神の御舎を造営し、朝な夕なに正しき政治を行はせ給へと祈願怠りなかりける。
