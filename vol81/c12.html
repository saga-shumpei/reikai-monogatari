Chapter 12: Tree 田 Mountain 颪 [2039]

木田山颪〔2039〕

§ 1

Chinriuhime, the daughter of King Azumi, was captured by the enemy castle along with her Uba, Araragi, and Araragi's daughter, Senriu. While enduring the shame of the ropes in the first Raugoku, they lamented the Ukiyo and sang of their Jiyutsukuwai to one another.

Chinriuhime's song: 'How wretched this Ukiyo has become, as I now suffer the shame of the ropes. What has become of my Tarachine? I am Kokoromoto, wondering where the Idomu Castle has gone. Attacked by an unexpected enemy army, has my Tarachine vanished like dew? Though there were Moromoro Ihenoko and Ikusabito, they must have perished without even a Gahi to show for it. The land bestowed by my Oyaoya must have been seized by the Bōgiyaku hands of Eales. My strength exhausted, I was captured by the enemy, and now I drift in a deep pool of tears. It is a sad state for me to be in, thinking that my Inochi, like a jewel-string, may be measured at any time. Wondering how I might escape this suffering, I live for a moment with the Kami as my strength. I have become a person without hope, for I have no way to enjoy the moon over the Minanogaha. It is in retribution for capturing the innocent Ningiyo that my Tarachine's country has met its Kunihoro.

Sent by a knight over a distance of Suhiyakuri, I lament my life in this cold Raugoku. If the Kami of Ametsuchi exist in this world, show me the moon of the Minanogaha once more. If my Tarachine still has Inochi in this world, I pray you let it reach me, even if only in a dream. It is sad to think of myself as one without hope, and I weep in loneliness Asayufu.'

Araragi sings: 'Hearing the Kotoba of the Himegimi's lament, it is sad that I have no place to be. For twenty years I have served as Mitomo to the Himegimi, and I have never left her side. Sent far away to the enemy's land, it is sad to see the Himegimi suffer such Uime. Separated from the compassionate King of Idomu, it is painful to see such Uime in the enemy castle.

Praying to the Kami of Ametsuchi, I shall clear the distress of my Himegimi this day. How foolish I am to have allowed such Uime to befall her while I was the one guarding the Himegimi. Himegimi, please forgive me; there is no way to resist the power of time in any matter. Nevertheless, please be at peace, for I possess a certain strategy.

It is also sad to think that my daughter Senriu is also a captive in this painful state. How I rack my heart wondering how I shall apologize to the King of Azumi. The water of the Minanogaha must be flowing clearly, reflecting the moon without a mind. Eales must be drinking sake while admiring the moon at the high palace of Idomu Castle.

Thinking of Eales's conduct, I feel hatred; he seized the land of Idomu without effort. Eales must be sitting in Idomu Castle in arrogance, with a look of triumph. There is no path where evil triumphs over righteousness; it shall surely perish by the wrath of the Kami. For a little while longer, endure the shame of the ropes and wait for the spring when the flowers bloom. King Eales shall surely perish, for his deeds are contrary to the Way.

Imprisoned in the Raugoku with the Himegimi, I resent King Eales Asayufu. Where is my King, and is the Queen safe? I wish to hear news of them, but it is of no use; the cuckoo cries. In the sky where the Satsuki rain falls softly, the cuckoo cries as it passes, leaving behind a single note. When I gaze at the sky where the cuckoo has cried, it is a Satsuki darkness where I cannot distinguish even an iris.

It goes without saying for the Himegimi, but my daughter and I are also cuckoos in the dark night. If I had the strength, I would break this Raugoku, but it is of no use for a woman's arms. Is it an Oni or an Orochi that binds my innocent Himegimi in such a Raugoku? In a world where Oni and Orochi run wild, the Kami is the only true strength. Separated by the Kidagawa, we who are bound in this Raugoku are like rats in a bag. Our Inochi, like a jewel-string, is held by the enemy, and I hear the sound of rain in my lonely state.'

Senriu sings: 'The Himegimi's lament is justified, and I too weep, finding my mother's resentment to be right. By slaughtering the castle of peace, Eales has revealed his demonic nature. Is it not sad that we live in a world where evil Kami run wild? Though I think of how to clear today's resentment, my heart only grows cloudy. My heart, facing the void, wanders in darkness, and Asayufu I sadly hear the cuckoo. If there were a way to save the Himegimi's Inochi, I would not spare my own. I shall offer my Inochi in place of the Himegimi and my mother; I pray to the Kami. My Inochi is light, but the Himegimi's Inochi is heavier than the great mountains. It is sad to think of the distress of the Himegimi, who is the successor to the Idomu country. I rack my heart Asayufu wondering how I might save my Himegimi.'

Satsukiyami approaches this Hitoya, / And even Ayame is indistinguishable, as my heart goes mad.

Chinriu-hime sings:

Does the Kami not exist, or is He not present in Tenchi? / Does He not look down upon such lamentations? / Since my father is Ō, surely he will preserve his Inochi and stand again. / I think that my father will shine, and Kotomu and harmonize this land. / If my father's army wins, the Satsukiyami will clear, and I shall worship the Tsukihi again. / Though it is a hopeless wish, somehow I see a light in my soul. / To destroy the land of my innocent father, / And the Yuyu of the Magatsu who revels in it. / The Magatsu King of Eales shall perish, touching the light of the Kami of Tenchi. / If the true Kami exists in Tenchi, He will surely help my father. / Thinking of the Yukusue of Eales, which holds the fate to perish, it is pitiful. / Though I now expose the shame of the Nahame, / Soon I shall appear in the world as light.

In the depths of my Murakimo heart, I know not what, / But there is one light within me. / Enjoying the world that is to come, I must endure the shame and suffering of Kefu.

Araragi sings:

As I hear the Ō Mikokoro of Himegimi, / My soul has begun to shine. / My soul, which has suffered and struggled Asayufu, / Has begun to take courage at the Kotoba of Hime. / Knowing that if there is darkness, there is a world of light, / I have suffered Asayufu with an Orokagokoro.

Senriu sings:

Hearing the Mikokoro of Himegimi, I too / Have felt my heart's steed take courage. / I too, mounting the steed of Tsukihi, / Think to advance to the Toha Yasudo. / Even in this sad body of a captive, I hear the tidings of Tenchi, / Following the wind. / Even if my Karatama is bound, my soul / Runs through Tenchi in Jiiujizai. / Even in this body without Jiiu as a captive, my soul / Runs through Tenchi freely.

At this point, the Riyōnin, Asazuki and Yufuzuki, approach quietly, hiding their footsteps, and Asazuki sings first:

I am Asazuki, and Yufuzuki, who serve / Close to the side of King Eames. / Is the Himegimi who hides in this house / Not Chinriu-hime? / I have guessed that the Himegimi, whose attire is noble and pure, / Is Chinriu-hime. / I have come, pitying the Hime who suffers / Within the dim Hitoya. / If there is a heart for fish, there must be a heart for water; / Think so of my Kotoba. / If you follow the Kotoba that I shall speak, / I will not let you suffer the sorrows of Kefu. / Rather than suffering endlessly in the Hitoya, / Will you not wish for a Yasudo quickly? / The young King has set his heart upon you, Hime. / Please be swayed, Chinriu-hime-dono.

Chinriu-hime, while feeling indignant at Asazuki's song, sings solemnly:

Should I follow the Kotoba of the Magatsu, / Who has stolen the land of a people without resentment? / Even if my Tama-no-o, my Inochi, is taken, / How could I be swayed by the Kotoba of the Magatsu? / In Eales, where the resentment of a thousand autumns is heavy, / Even if I die, I shall not submit. / Do not speak such useless repetitions, however lowly; / I am the child of the King of Azumi. / I think it is filthy even to hear / The Kotoba of a lowly official like you. / I am now prepared to throw away my Inochi; / Should I submit to the enemy King?

Yufuzuki sings:

Though I think your Kotoba is right, Himegimi, / Please reconsider this place for once. / Even if your Tama-no-o Inochi dies, it will not reach him; / Please reconsider it for your own sake. / Since you are the child of the King of Azumi, / I will serve you with sincerity. / Become the consort of my young King and flourish; / The suffering of Kefu will soon melt away.

Chinriu-hime sings:

No matter how much you exhaust your Kotoba to entice me, / I will not consent to the Kotoba of the Magatsu. / Rather than becoming the consort of the King of Eames and flourishing, / I shall become a demon of the Hitoya. / I will throw away my Tama-no-o Inochi, become a demon, / And think to clear my father's resentment. / Thinking of my father's suffering, how could I / Become the consort of the enemy King? / Restore the land of Idomu to my father, / And only then approach me. / Until the castle of Idomu returns to my father's hands, / I will not listen to your Kotoba. / Before you speak words to me who has no hope, / Return the country to my father. / If I have my father's permission, I / Would not refuse to become the consort.

Chinriu-hime: 'In a world filled with falsehoods, how could I possibly follow your words? Only with the permission of my father would I ever respond to your entreaties. Should I surrender my body to the enemy of my parents? I am a child of man, not a beast. Since I am one who does not cling to the life of my precious soul, I shall not desire the dreams of glory. At the very least, return my Tarachine to the main castle, and only after that may you proceed. I, too, shall not dwell in the land of Saru; send me back to the land of Idumu. Ever since I heard that King Aems loves me, my soul has been on the verge of shattering. Swallowing a myriad of tears, I now pine for my parents in this prison.'

Yufuzuki sings:

Yufuzuki: 'Hearing the steadfast heart of Himegimi, my tears flow without end. How sad it is for me that I have no way to hear the righteous words of Himegimi. I am moved by the firm heart of Himegimi, who does not cling to the life of her precious soul. Yet, I have come here seeking to clear the distress of King Aems. Enduring the resentment of a thousand autumns, I pray you yield to King Aems for a little while. It is not that I do not know the heart of Himegimi, but I urge you for the sake of the nation. How pitiful is King Aems, who loves Himegimi with the limit of his precious soul. If one lacks a heart of compassion, one is no different from wood or stone; I pray you think on this and yield.'

Chinriu-hime sings:

Chinriu-hime: 'O spineless young King, who forces me into a dark prison and speaks of love! If Aems possesses a heart of compassion, why does he make me suffer in this prison? Unless this matter is resolved first, I shall give no answer of yes or no. My ears are utterly defiled by hearing that the enemy king of Aems is impatient.'

Asazuki sings:

Asazuki: 'Though I perceive the sincerity of Himegimi's heart, I have no path forward. Until I hear the gentle words of Himegimi, I think I shall not leave this place. Himegimi, I pray you endure this frustration for a while and consent to the light of the future.'

Chinriu-hime sings:

Chinriu-hime: 'I shall not accept any sweet words, no matter what they may be. If my life is one that must end, then hearing the words of the King of Aems only makes me want to die all the more.'

Asazuki and Yufuzuki, having no words to return to the strong heart of Chinriu-hime, who would not budge an inch, left the place dejectedly. As a result of various consultations, they began to devise shallow plans, such as using water torture, starvation, and fire torture, to see if they could make her yield to the love of King Aems.

『姫君の心の誠察すれど われは進まむ道さへもなし 姫君のやさしき言葉聞くまでは われは此の場を去らずと思ふ くやしさをしばし忍びて姫君よ 末の光りと諾ひ給はれ』

チンリウ姫は歌ふ。

『如何ならむ甘き言葉も承知はじ われは死すべき生命なりせば エームスの王の言葉を聞くにつけ われは一入死にたくなりぬ』

朝月、夕月は、梃でも棒でも動かぬチンリウ姫の強き心に返す言葉も無く、すごすごとして此の場を立ち去り、いろいろと相談の結果、水責め、食責め、火責めをもつて、エームス王の恋心に靡かせむかと、種々浅はかなる計画をめぐらしつつありける。
