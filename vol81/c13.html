Chapter 13: 思ひの掛 River [2040]

思ひの掛川〔2040〕

§ 1

In the inner chamber of Kitayamajiyō, King Ames remained all alone, lost in silent contemplation and suffering from love, while singing a lament in a faint voice.

‘Born into this world twenty years ago, Under the knees of my father and mother, Raised as a precious Miko, Morning and evening, served by various Officials and Sobame, I have spent a joyful Shiyunjiu, And now, welcoming the spring of my twentieth year, I have begun to know the pathos of things, Sinking into the abyss of agony, Caught in a Kohiji that should not be, Morning and evening, the suffering, I have no way to speak of, only to weep, My body is caught in love, Day and night, my Karatama withers, My precious Inochi is also Mōrō, My thoughts know not their Yukuhe, Ah, what shall I do, the Hime I love, Is truly the daughter of the King of the enemy land, The King of Azumi, they say, Even if my father’s power excels in every way, No matter how deep the affection, This love alone, how could it ever, Be fulfilled, there is no way, Until I think my precious life-thread Inochi will vanish, Morning and evening, I have yearned, My lover, for whom I would stake my Inochi, Truly, it is sad that she is the Manamusume, Of the King of the enemy land, When I think of it, no matter how I yearn, Will my thoughts ever reach her, Now that it has come to this, my father’s, Destruction of the Idumu land, His heartless deeds are hateful, And at the same time, I feel resentment, For the Hime for whom I yearn, staking my Inochi, How shall I ever clear, Her hatred, like the Satsukiyami, Blocking the sky of a summer night, The Hototogisu cries until its voice is hoarse, My body is filled with thoughts of coughing blood, Asazuki and Yufuzuki, the Futahashira, My devotion has now come to this, What answer is there in the summer night, How shall I bind my short-lived heart, The clear cries of the Momotori, And the fragrance of the Momobana, Are to me but ugly voices, And the flowers of ugly Ogusa, Everything I see and hear, Is but a seed of sorrow, a seed of grief, Only a seed of lamentation, I do nothing but weep, Now that it has come to this, my Inochi, There is no point in living, I shall end up as the algae of the Kitagaha, And hiding deep at the bottom of the water, Shall I wash away the agony of love, Ah, how sad is my Kohiji, Ah, how hateful is my father’s, Heartless behavior, now that it has come to this, Does it make me weep in my Kohiji? Fleeting is the state of this floating world, How unreliable is this heartless world.’

‘Because the person I love is an enemy, she doubts my sincerity and returns hatred in turn, When I hear that my lover is the Miko of the King of my enemy land, the more I hear, the sadder it becomes, Though I think it is a love that cannot be, I have no heart to force her to submit with my power, How pathetic it is to love the fragrance of flowers that have no heart, When I think of it, love is truly sad, When I think of the sincerity of the Hime who does not permit my love, I cannot deeply hate her, I am now caught by the demon of love, And wander in darkness, unable to see the path ahead, Though I think she is not a flower to be plucked, I am one who has no way to change my thoughts, Even if my body becomes the algae of the river, May this love never be lost for eternity, She is a Hime who listens not to the living Kototama of Asazuki, Nor the affectionate words of Yufuzuki, I shall win over the lady-in-waiting Araragi, And move the heart of the Hime.’

Just as he sang this, Asazuki and Yufuzuki respectfully approached the presence and sang.

Asazuki: 'Though we have shot various Kototama at the target, It does not move, even if the thousand-pulling rock were to stir, The heart of the Hime of Chinriu is like a great rock, It does not move even a bit, Though we have exhausted our hearts to negotiate, There is no good news, and no way forward, Without a face to show our King, We have been suffering, together with Yufuzuki.'

Yufuzuki sang:

Yufuzuki: 'The thought that I would illuminate the pure heart of the young King, Has also become a dream, Her heart resenting her father is deep, And the Hime of Chinriu does not move at all, My power has now reached its limit, and beyond this, I think I shall change my tactics and face it.'

King Ames sang:

King Ames: 'I have read the efforts of you two in various ways, But my heart is lonely, Beyond this, I shall call out the lady-in-waiting Araragi, And first, I must speak to her and reconcile, If I tempt her with profit, the lady-in-waiting Araragi, Will surely submit, what do you think?'

『さまざまと汝等二人が働きを 吾よみすれど心さみしき この上は侍女のアララギ呼び出し 先づは言向け和すべきかな 利を以てさそへば侍女のアララギは 必ず靡かむ如何に思ふぞ』

§ 2

Asazuki claps his hands and sings:

Wakagimi's Mikoto I revere, Araragi I shall summon, to entice the heart of the Hime.

Yufuzuki sings:

If the heart of Araragi moves, surely Chinriuhime-sama shall also submit and come.

Thus, Asazuki and Yufuzuki ordered the jailer to bring Araragi before the King while still bound. Araragi, who was always alert and missed nothing, quickly grasped the situation and was led before King Ames, standing there with composure. Asazuki first turned to Araragi and sang:

You who serve the Hime of Chinriu, are you the nurse Araragi? Let me ask you this. The Hime, confined in a painful prison and unable to move, must be relying on you for strength. Rather than facing various tortures, is it not more peaceful to accept rank and glory? Depending on your heart alone, Chinriuhime-sama and Senriuhime-sama could flourish like flowers. To kill the Hime of Chinriu or to let her live forever like a flower, that is your power. Araragi, calm your heart and answer. You stand at the boundary of life and death. If you slight the generous heart of my King, the lives of the three of you shall be in peril.

Araragi, without fear and with a smile filling his face, sings:

Though I am but a lowly one, I shall convey the King's heart to the Himegimi. Since I have served her for twenty years, she will surely accept my Kototama. However, as the Hime bears a grudge against her Onchichi and Onhaha, it is difficult to guarantee. I shall pour out all the Kototama I possess and try to move the heart of the Hime.

Asazuki, softening his expression, says:

Araragi, your words are good; pray, devote your sincerity for the sake of Wakagimi. If you satisfy the heart of Wakagimi, you too shall flourish as a flower of the Mikuni. Preserve your eternal life and bloom and fragrance in this castle, flourishing purely.

King Ames sings:

Relying on you, the clever Araragi, I shall await good news from the Hime. If my longing becomes your strength, I shall reward you with rank.

Araragi sings:

I am grateful for the declaration of Wakagimi-sama; I shall fulfill it even if I must cast away my life.

From this point, Araragi left the King's presence, was escorted by the attendant gods of Asazuki and Yufuzuki, and returned to the prison where Chinriuhime-sama was confined. To move the heart of Chinriuhime-sama, he exhausted his words and sang:

Chinriuhime-sama, pray listen. I was led before the presence, and I saw with my own eyes the King's words, so terrifying and various. Having been told them, I was struck with fear. We three are but bodies that will vanish like the evening dew by tonight. Water torture and fire torture are but trifles; we are to be subjected to every kind of torment and put to a lingering death. I have used my Kototama well, and for the sake of appeasing the heart of King Ames, I have broken my heart in a thousand ways. It has been worth it, for the reply of Himegimi-sama has become the boundary that decides life and death. Chinriuhime-sama, my sovereign, you who are the seed of all things while life remains. No matter what grudge you may hold,

If there is no life, there can be no means to repay it. For now, pray soften your heart, submit to the heart of King Ames, and preserve your precious life. I, Araragi, feel the same resentment as you, Himegimi, but as there is no other way, I have sworn, with fear and trembling, to offer you, Himegimi-sama, as the consort of King Ames. Pray forgive me, Himegimi. It is the scheme of Araragi, filled with sincerity, to let you meet your beloved Onchichi and Onhaha. Pray do not think ill of it. I beseech you to listen fully and in detail to the sincerity of this Araragi, whose loyalty is fixed and single-minded,

And satisfy the longing heart of King Ames, Kannagara. Because I believe this to be the plan of the Gods, I pray with all my sincerity. If you do not consent, the noble body of the daughter of the King of Azumi will be instantly deprived of her precious life. With no way to return the enmity, you will become a demon of grudge, and for ages and generations to come, you will likely sink into weeping and lamentation. I pray that you will yield and grant our request.

§ 3

Araragi: 'Though I am not ignorant of the heart of Himegimi, I urge this for the sake of your Inochi. I think only to support the Inochi of Himegimi in Buji, and to clear the grudge of your Mioya.'

Chinryū Hime sings slightly:

'How heartless are the words of the nurse Araragi. Should I entrust my body to an enemy? Born as the daughter of a Mononofu, even if I die, I shall not regret it. As I listen to the impolite words of Araragi, my soul feels as if it will perish, yet I shall not commit the Fukau of serving the enemy of my Chichihaha by clinging to my fleeting Inochi. Even a thousand times ten thousand sweet words are but the whispers of a corrupted song to me.'

Senryū Onna sings:

'Though I think your words, Himegimi, are correct, I pray you endure for a little while. Have pity on our Inochi, which are bound to the heart of Himegimi alone. The answer of Himegimi concerns the precious Inochi of us three. I beseech you, Chinryū Himegimi, save our Inochi, both my mother's and our own.'

Chinryū Hime sings:

'Though you are hateful enemies, when I think of the Inochi of you mother and child, a hesitating heart wells up within me. What shall I do? My body can neither go nor return, and I am in more agony than if I were to die. When I think of killing Araragi and Senryū Hime, my own Inochi feels sorrowful. Even if it does not accord with my heart, for now, I shall comply with the Onkotoba of King Aames.'

As she finishes singing, Asazuki and Yufuzuki appear from the Monokage and sing with clear voices.

The song of Asazuki:

'Alas, alas, at the greatness of the heart of Hime, Kitayamajiyō has been revived. King Aames will surely rejoice to hear of your pure Mikokoro. I, too, hearing the Onkotoba of Chinryū Hime, think of the prosperity of Inochi.'

Yufuzuki sings:

'How grateful I am; upon the surface of the sea of devotion, the clear moon has risen. As I listen to the Ō heart of Chinryū Hime, I weep Otokona from the shadows. What a testament to the prosperity of this grateful Miyo. We shall welcome the consort to King Aames. Come then, I shall convey the Magokoro of Hime to the Mimahe of the King in detail. Araragi, Chinryū Hime, Senryū, be at peace, for we shall welcome you soon.'

Araragi sings:

'How grateful I am; by the Magokoro of Chinryū Hime, our Inochi has been saved. Please convey the Ikukototama I have declared to the Mimahe of King Aames.'

Asazuki sings:

'I shall convey the merits of Araragi's devotion to the King for you. Await good tidings, for I shall now return to the Mimahe of the King to report.'

Thus, as the dawn of King Aames's love finally appeared, the King immediately released Chinryū Hime and the others from prison, had them change into splendid garments, and arranged for them to enter the King's palace.
