Chapter 4: 遷座式 [2031]

遷座式〔2031〕

§ 1

As a result of the Hotsuki of Azumi-ō and the devoted efforts of the Gunshinra for one hundred days and one hundred nights, a most Saugon and auspicious Miaraka was built upon the summit of Tsukimitsuyama.

Thereupon, Azumi-ō completed the Shiubatsu of seven days and seven nights, ascended to the Shinden with reverence to perform the rites of purification, and offered the Norito of the Sengūshiki.

The Kamigamitachi straightened their collars in this Seijiyō, waiting in Kiyōku. The text of the Harahi Norito says:

‘With awe and trembling, in the Mahoraba of the Shibitenkai, the Takahi no Miya, where the Ōmiyahashirafutoshi is firmly established and the Chigitakashi is raised high in Takaamahara, the Lord God who dwells eternally and Ushiha the Daiuchiu, the Lord God of the High Spear and the Lord God of the Divine Spear, before Your noble presence, I, Azumi-ō, the Lord of the Idomu Castle and the chief priest, speak with reverence, respect, and profound awe. The land of Azumi has been deeply blessed by the Lord God; the fields are lush and the fruits of the trees are abundant. While the Kunitsukami were spending their days in joy under the thick blessings of every morning and evening, the governor of the land of Saru, Eersu, led a multitude of soldiers across the peaks of Ōe-yama, occupied the Pearl Lake, and advanced to surround the Idomu Castle—which we have protected from our ancestors, generation after generation, and which is known as a paradise of peace—attacking with bows and arrows. When I stood to face and shoot back at this fierce enemy, alas, the greater part of my allies were destroyed by the enemy, and my daughter has gone missing. That such a calamity has befallen me is entirely due to the sin of slighting the will of my ancestors, forgetting the blessings of the Lord God, and governing with selfishness; thus, I repent of my past errors and, from the sincerity of my heart, have realized that I am a child of the Lord God. As a sign, even if only one in a thousand, of my heart of repentance, I have firmly established the Ōmiyahashirafutoshi on the Upper Iwane where the evergreen trees grow, the most pure and cool place on the summit of Tsukimitsuyama, and I invite the divine spirit of the Lord God to descend. I have placed various delicious foods of the sea, rivers, mountains, and fields upon the eight-legged tables, and I offer sacred sake, sacred food, and sacred water. I pray that You will listen peacefully and calmly, and grant my request: that this sacred place of Tsukimitsuyama may prosper more and more as a land of eternal summer, and that, receiving the blessings of the Lord God, You may allow me to recapture the Idomu Castle. Once the Idomu Castle has returned to my hands, I vow to teach and admonish both high and low to guard against hearts of arrogance, to realize the blessings of fire, water, and earth, and to fulfill the divine will of the Lord God. I look up and pray that the Lord God will descend to this great hall, dwell with Your noble spirit eternally, and cause not only the land of Idomu but also the entire land of Saru to be moistened by the dew of the Lord God’s blessings, and to possess straight and righteous hearts. With awe and trembling, I offer this prayer.’

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand.

May there be ten thousand years of prosperity.

May there be eighty million blessings.

Having finished singing thus, he bowed again before the divine presence, and:

‘The divine shadow of the heavenly God of the distant heavens, I have surely worshipped. Through the grateful descent of the God, our land, Shall surely prosper more and more. From this day when the spirit of the Lord God descends, Our land shall be at peace. Looking up to the heavens and bowing to the earth, morning and evening, I shall serve the Lord God. Tsukimitsuyama is pure, and because the spirit of the Lord God, Shall dwell here eternally, The grasses and trees have become vibrant in color. In this moment of the God’s descent, My mistaken heart is suddenly purified. I am a child of God, a palace of God. Dwelling eternally in this palace, May You illuminate the islands of Isako.’

So saying, he clapped his hands and returned to his original seat.

Muraji-hime bowed before the divine presence and sang quietly:

‘Though there are eighty days, this day is truly a lucky day, I know the resurrection of my soul. The Lord God has descended to this sacred place, And my soul does not cease to be courageous. Having piled up the days of lamentation, happily, I have met this lucky day. Regarding the future of my beloved daughter Chinriu-hime, Protect her, O Lord God. As long as my daughter has life, even for one day, Let me see her with my own eyes soon. Somehow my heart is happy and courageous, My hands and feet are dancing wildly. Return to the castle protected by our ancestors, And let us perform the divine rites.’

Muraji-hime sings:

‘Gazing upon the Misugata of the Mihashiragami, I could not lift my head. Feeling as if crushed from the Zujiyō, I trembled in awe of the Miizu. That I knew not that a noble Kami dwells in the Ametsuchi, the retribution for this sin has come. It is fitting that the Eerusu should attack. The castle of Idumu, which defied the Kami, knew not that the Ametsuchi is the Sumika of the Kami. The sin of living in pride is fearful. Not only the Nanukananayo of purification, but even for Momokahi, I shall cleanse and clarify my Karatama and Mitama. The sin of attempting to welcome the Mitama of the Lord God into this Nihidono is fearful. If the clouding of our Mitama is not cleared, how shall the Mihashiragami Amo? We have done a fearful thing, to serve the Kami while harboring a clouded Mitama. Though the Shinden is solemn, the Lord God does not settle therein; how anxious I feel. Upon the already polished, I shall polish my Mitama further, and serve before the Mimahe of the Kami.’

Shiuran sings:

‘We have done a fearful thing, to invite the Kami without reflecting upon our own defiled bodies. The groaning and shaking of the Shinden, as if it would collapse, must be the Shinbatsu. From Kefu, I shall reform the way of the Yumiya and become an officer of the Kototamaikusa.’

Naaman sings:

‘Serving as a Kami of Samori for many years, I did not realize this error. While fulfilling the role of Hohitsu to my King, it was I who led the King astray. Lord God, pray forgive my Inochi; if Thou shouldst summon it, I would not loathe it. That Chinriu-hime was snatched away by the enemy, I feel sorrow, thinking it is our own sin.’

Taaman sings:

‘Serving the King for long Tsukihi, I came without realizing the blessings of the Kami. All sins that can be called sins have gathered, and it must be that the castle of Idumu has perished. Thinking that this, too, is the sin of us having forgotten the Kami, there is no Okiba for my body.’

Azumi-ō sings:

‘Do not lament, you all; it was all my sin of slighting the Kami. From Kefu, let us reform our hearts and advance, embraced by the heart of the Kami of Aizen. No matter what Tsumikagoto there may be, the Lord God of Aizen shall surely save us.’

Shiuran sings:

‘Hearing the gentle heart of my King, tears fall from me of their own accord. Now that it has come to this, there is no use in lamenting; I shall only purify my heart and serve. Detached from the desires of the earth, I shall follow the Kannagara and the sincerity of the Kami.’

Muraji-hime sings:

‘I shall cast aside treasures that have form, and polish my heart to seek the treasure that has no form. My soul was clouded, and I did not realize it until I heard the noble teachings of the Lord God.’

Thus, each sang their reflections, offered Norito of gratitude before the Kami, and while retreating, descended from the Shinden at the summit of Mount Gekkō, and returned to the hastily built castle.

The moon in the vast sky shone brightly, and the chirping of a hundred birds and the fragrance of a hundred flowers, which were not of the season, and music in the air resounded clearly; the short spring night finally dawned. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.

『年古く左守の神と仕へつつ この過ちを悟らざりしよ 吾王の輔弼の役を勤めつつ 王を誤らしめし吾なり 主の神よ許し給はれわが生命 よしや召すとも厭はざりせば チンリウ姫敵に奪はれ給ひしも われらが罪と思へば悲しき』

ターマンは歌ふ。

『長からむ月日を王に仕へつつ 神の恵を悟らずに来し 罪といふ罪のことごと集まりて イドムの城は滅びしなるらむ かくなるも吾等が神を忘れたる 罪と思へば身の置場もなし』

アヅミ王は歌ふ。

『汝たちは嘆かふなかれ皆われが 神をなみせし罪なりにける 今日よりは心あらため愛善の 神の心に抱かれ進まむ 如何ならむ罪科あるも愛善の 主の大神は救ひ給はむ』

シウランは歌ふ。

『吾王の優しき心聞くにつけ われ自ら涙こぼるる 今となり歎くも詮なし村肝の 心清めて仕ふるのみなる 地の上の欲に離れて惟神 神の誠に従はむかな』

ムラジ姫は歌ふ。

『形ある宝を捨てて形なき 宝求むと心を磨かむ 吾魂は曇りて居たり主の神の 貴の教を聞くまで悟らず』

かく各自述懐を歌ひ、神前に感謝の祝詞を奏上し後しざりしながら、月光山の頂上なる神殿を降り、俄造りの城内に帰り行く。

大空の月は皎々として輝き渡り、時ならぬ百鳥の囀り百花の香り、空中の音楽嚠喨として響き渡り、短き春の夜は遂に明け放れたり。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
