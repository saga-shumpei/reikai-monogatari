Chapter 15: 厚顔無恥 [2042]

厚顔無恥〔2042〕

§ 1

The Ketsukonshiki of King Aames and Hime Chinriu in the Ōoku was so Saugon that the Uba Araragi suddenly felt jealous and her Yashin surged up. Taking advantage of the fact that Hime Chinriu looked exactly like Senriu, she concocted a Warudakumi, successfully trapped the Hime, and had her sentenced to Entau. She was a Kusemono of such Kanneijiyachi that she was beyond hatred. King Aames, not knowing it was a Kahedama of the Hime, was deceived by the Nisemono, and deeply believing her to be Hime Chinriu, he spent his Chiuya with care and Chiyōai. Araragi would secretly meet the eyes of her own daughter who had become the Ōhi, and sticking out her tongue, she would Hohoe. The Ketsukonshiki was finally over, and on a Getsumei night ten days later, it was decided to gather the Jiushin within the Dennai and hold a Shiyukugakuwai.

King Aames and the Amata Jiushin were moved by Araragi's Kōhei Shiyochi and offered their Sanji with every word they could muster. King Aames, too, was so filled with Kantan at Araragi's Kōhei Shiyochi that he entrusted her with everything, having her handle all the administrative affairs of the Dennai. Araragi's reputation rose like the morning sun, and as there was a fear that anyone who opposed her will even slightly would be executed, imprisoned, or sentenced to Entau, no one dared to speak of Araragi out of fear.

The banquet of celebration was held. King Aames stood and sang:

With a public heart you have cast aside the private, Araragi, your diligence was great. Without covering the sins of your own beloved child, You declared she be sent to the island, such was your honesty. Thinking of Araragi's daughter, I am moved to pity.

Araragi stood and sang:

Though your words, my King, are to be revered, Do not disturb the laws of the land. Though she is my child, she is a sinner against heaven and earth, My King, do not be partial and forgive her. My daughter has broken the treasure of the land, How could she possibly escape that sin? Even if you, my King, forgive her, would the Kunitsukami Forgive this transgression? If my daughter is to be forgiven, I shall take her place and serve the punishment.

The Ōhi sang:

Araragi, who has served me for twenty years, Surely the gods know your public heart. For twenty long years you nurtured her, Yet you have the courage to judge your own child's sin. Since Senriu's sin is heavy, let her remain forever Locked away on the hidden island. There is no law in our land To forgive a great crime worthy of ten thousand deaths. I, now standing by the side of the holy monarch, Think to illuminate the darkness of Sār. Araragi, with your pure heart, Assist me in my governance. Araragi, more courageous than any man, You are the strength of the land of Sār.

Araragi sang:

I am grateful for the declaration of Hime Chinriu, I shall surely guard it and not deviate. From today, I shall stand above the hundred officials, And assist in the King's governance. My King, do not worry at all About Senriu who committed the sin. If you forgive her because she is the child of my blood, The laws of the land of Sār will be disturbed. With a sincere heart thinking of the King and the nation, I shall not shed tears of lamentation.

Asazuki sang:

You are a courageous lord, Araragi, And you illuminate the heart of the King. While softening the firm heart of Hime Chinriu, You have invited today's joy. Morning and evening I worship the noble figure of Hime Chinriu, And think of the excellence of the land. The young King is very healthy, And I am happy to see his good spirits. Yet, it is sad for Hime Senriu, Who was sent to the hidden island. With your pure heart, great Lord, declare it again, And forgive Hime Senriu.

Araragi stood up abruptly and sang:

My King, you must never forgive her, For the laws of the land are strict. As I hear the words of the official Asazuki, I grieve for the sake of the nation.

Yūzuki sang:

Hime Senriu, who mistakenly broke the treasure of the land, Is a pitiable person. Though you say the laws of the land are strict, Should one forgive a sin committed without intent? If you punish a sin committed unknowingly, The land will not be governed well. Yūzuki, risking her life, asks the King For the forgiveness of Hime Senriu.

大奥に於けるエームス王とチンリウ姫の結婚式の余り荘厳なるに、乳母のアララギは俄にねたましく野心むらむらと起り、如何にもしてチンリウ姫のセンリウに酷似せるを幸ひ、悪計を捻り出し、うまうま姫を罠に陥れ、これを遠島の刑に処せしめしは、憎みても余りある奸佞邪智の曲者なりける。エームス王は姫の替玉とは知らず、贋物をつかまされ、チンリウ姫と深く思ひ込み、昼夜心を用ひて寵愛してゐる。アララギは、しすましたりと王妃となりしわが娘と、窃かに顔を見合はせ、舌を吐き出し微笑んでゐる。いよいよ結婚式は済み、十日を経たる月明の夜、殿内に於て重臣を集め、祝賀会を開かるる事となりぬ。

エームス王始め数多の重臣は、アララギの公平なる処置に感激し、各口を極めて讃辞を呈し、エームス王も亦、アララギの公平なる処置に感嘆の余り、一切万事を委託して殿内の総ての事務を処理せしめたれば、アララギの声望は旭日昇天の如く、彼が意に少しにても逆らふ者あらば、悉く手打ちにされ、投獄され、或は遠島の刑に処せらるるのおそれありければ、何れも恐れを為してアララギの事を口にする者なかりける。

祝賀の宴は開かれた。エームス王は立つて歌ふ。

『公の心を持ちて私を 捨てしアララギいそしかりける 最愛の吾子の罪を包まずに 島に流せと宣りし素直さ アララギの娘の事を思ひ出で われは憐れを催しにけり』

アララギは立つて歌ふ。

『吾王の御言葉畏しさりながら 国の掟を乱し給ふな 吾子とはいへど天地の罪人よ 依怙なき王は許し給ふな 吾娘国の宝を打ち破り 如何で其の罪逃るべしやは 吾王はよし許すとも国津神は この過ちを許すべきかは わが娘許さるる事あるならば われは代りて罪に服せむ』

王妃は歌ふ。

アララギは歌ふ。

『ありがたしチンリウ姫の御宣言 たしに守りて違はざるべし 今日よりは百の司の上に立ち 王の政治を補ひまつらむ 吾王よ罪を造りしセンリウに 必ず心配らせ給ふな 血を別けし吾子なりとて許しなば サールの国の掟は乱れむ 王思ひ御国を思ふ誠心に 歎きの涙われはしぼらじ』

朝月は歌ふ。

『けなげなるアララギの君ましまして 王の御心照らし給へり チンリウ姫堅き心を和めつつ 今日の歓び招きし君はも チンリウ姫の崇高き御姿朝夕に 拝みまつりて国の秀をおもふ 若王はいと健かにおはしまして 御機嫌よきが嬉しかりけり さりながらかくれの島にやらはれし センリウ姫は悲しかりけり 大君の清き心に宣り直し 許させ給へセンリウ姫を』

アララギは、むつくと立つて歌ふ。

『わが王よ必ず許し給ふまじ 国の掟は厳かなりせば 朝月の司の言葉聞くにつけ われは御国の為に悲しむ』

夕月は歌ふ。

『過ちて国の宝をこはしたる センリウ姫は悲しき人かも 国の掟厳かなりとはいひなながら 無心の過ち許すべきかは 知らず知らず過ちし罪をきためなば かへりて国は治まらざるべし 夕月は生命をかけて吾王に センリウ姫の許しを願ふ』

§ 2

Ōhi sings:

Asazuki and Yufuzuki, your words / Though I think them well, I shall never forgive you for eternity / To have broken the treasure of the country, which is fearful to even behold / There can be no sin greater than this / The sin of shattering the soul of the ancient land of Sāru / Is heavy indeed / The hateful Tsumibito who broke the Suishiyō / Kuwabin passed down from the age of Oyaoya / The Senryū who shattered the Ontakara / Which is fearful even to touch with one's hands / As long as I have Inochi, I shall not forgive / The sin of shattering the country's treasure.

Araragi sings:

Himegimi, your Mikotonori is truly bright / It shall illuminate the darkness of the land of Sāru / There is no one in the world who does not think of / The crane of the night and the pheasant of Yakeno, my Miko / Yet, though you say they are my children / There is no way to forgive this sin alone.

Takitsuse sings:

Araragi-dono, your Ō spirit / Makes my tears fall as I hear it / The Mikuni that is governed by such Kōhei and Mushi Araragi / Must surely be peaceful / Though you are a woman, you surpass the demons / You, Ō-dono, are the light of the country / Support the Matsurigoto of Wakagimi from morning till evening / And remain forever / From today, even the Tokoyami land of Sāru / Shall shine like the Amatsu-hi / Himegimi is the Manamusume of the King of Idumu / That you face the world wisely and Ō-ly / Means that soon the Riyōgoku of Idumu and Sāru / Shall flourish as an age of Shichitaihei / The wide flow of the Kidagawa shall also from today / Be clear and pure in the light of Hime / The wind blowing down from the ridge of Mount Daiei / Has surely become warm / Even the tribes of tigers, bears, lions, and wolves / Gather under the grace of the King / It has become a blessed age, in the land of Sāru / Where Araragi-dono, the official, resides / The land where the cuckoo once sang in the rain / Is now clear and pure without a shadow / The mountain wind crossing the sea of trees on Mount Daiei / Blows coolly into this mansion.

Yamakaze sings:

I, who have seen the prosperity of this country / Which has no precedent since ancient times, am joyful / Both fields and mountains are dressed in green garments / Celebrating the land of Sāru / The King possesses the cool heart / Of the Yamakaze that crosses the verdant sea of trees.

Asazuki sings again:

Thinking of the heart of Hime, whom we sent / To the island hidden in the depths of the waves, I am sad / My soul whispers to me / That she is not the true Hime, though it is fearful to say.

Chinriuhime, with horns in her eyes, sings in a hurried voice:

As I hear the unpleasant words of Asazuki / My soul trembles / Please listen to the unpleasant words of Asazuki / If you think of me, my King, as beloved / Though they say we are similar, Senryū and I / Have a distinction of noble and base, high and low.

King Ames sings:

Chinriuhime, your words are good; I shall rebuke / The unpleasant words of Asazuki / As the shadow of Asazuki fades into white / It shall soon vanish with my words / While making Asazuki bear the heavy sin / Exile her far away to an uninhabited island.

Here, the King's command being difficult to defy, Asazuki, having incurred the wrath of the King and Ōhi, was, to her misfortune, sentenced to the punishment of exile to a distant island as a great Tsumibito, while being bound all over with rough ropes right there on the banquet seat.

Alas, Asazuki was immediately subjected to a heavy punishment for the suspicion cast upon Chinriuhime, and while being watched by the crowd, she was dragged out of the castle, and finally came to the sorrowful fate of being exiled to an island.

King Ames, Chinriu, and Araragi, watching her retreating figure, smile pleasantly, and Araragi sings:

In the bright judgment of the King, Asazuki / Had no words to return / Asazuki tried to deceive Hime / And attempted to disturb her with calculated words / The judgment of the gods of heaven and earth is before our eyes / Asazuki now has not even a shadow / If we do not strictly enforce the laws of our country / It will soon fall into disorder from top to bottom / Seeing the correct verdict of my King / I feel hopeful for the future.

Chinriuhime says:

What a pleasant thing to see, even if it is unpleasant / The sin of slighting me has been repaid / If there is any suspicion before me, whatever it may be / Speak it out, and I shall judge it directly.

ここに王命もだし難く、朝月は王と王妃の怒りにふれ、忽ち宴会の席上より全身を荒縄に縛られながら、大罪人として遠島の刑に処せられしこそ是非なけれ。

エームス王、チンリウ、アララギはその後姿を打ち見やりながら、愉快げに微笑みつつアララギは歌ふ。

チンリウ姫は、

『心地よき事を見るかなゐやなくも われをなみせし罪酬い来て わが前に疑ひあれば何事も 言挙げせよや直に判決かむ』

§ 3

King Ēmusu sings:

‘The evil plot to disrupt and destroy the Uchito of Kitayamajiyō hath been Miyabu; Asazuki on the Uhabe feigned sincerity, yet was a tiger hiding its claws. The Magakami shall be driven out from my mansion and wander upon the Aranami. If any speak Kotoba of suspicion concerning the circumstance of Chinriuhime, they shall be cast away. The hearts of those who suspect so righteous a Hime to be a Nisemono are indeed clouded.’

Takitsuse sings:

‘Beholding now with my own eyes this righteous Sabaki, my heart trembleth; though the Jitsugetsu shine in the sky, in the Nakazora Kurokumo arise and reach not the Chijiyō. Most precious was the pure Sabaki of my King, who hath swept away the Kurokumo; strange indeed are the hearts of the officials who suspect the Himegimi whose Onsugata is so Kedaka.’

Yamakaze sings:

‘The heart that casteth suspicion upon so bright a Hime was indeed Magatsu; obeying my King and the Mikoto of the Hime, I shall serve them in body and soul for Chiyo; taking the bright soul of Araragi-no-kimi as my strength, I shall reverently serve.’

From this time forth, within Kitayamajiyō, Araragi brandished her authority and used the wicked, crafty, and evil-witted in heavy positions, while righteousness-minded persons were all subjected to unjust Nankuse, and were either killed, exiled, or cast into prison; thus evil persons dominated more and more, riots broke out in various places within the country of Sāru, and voices of resentment filled the mountains and fields, unavoidably bringing about a dangerous state of the nation.

Hereupon, Chinriuhime, caught in the evil plot of her nurse Araragi, was forced away from her own child Senriu, and moreover was made to bear the crime of destroying the national treasure, being exiled to Kakurejima; this island was a demonic island where, when evening came, the entire island was submerged by Aranami, and the humans and animals thereupon were drowned to death. Araragi feared the discovery of her evil plot, and therefore specifically had her mistress Chinriuhime sent to this island. Meanwhile, Asazuki incurred the King's wrath and was abandoned on a solitary island consisting solely of rocks, called Arashima, located about fifty miles off the coast of Kakurejima, spending days and months of lamentation while feeding on fish and shellfish, awaiting the appointed time of Heaven.

Beguiled by the evil plot of Araragi, Chinriuhime was exiled away; Asazuki likewise, by Araragi's scheme, was exiled toward Arashima; though the evil god may bloom and flourish for a time, when the hour cometh, it shall fragilely perish.

これより木田山城内はアララギが権威を振ひ、奸佞邪智の輩を重用し、正義の士は悉く難癖をつけ、或は殺し、或は流し、或は牢獄に投じければ、悪人益々跋扈して、サールの国内各所に暴動勃発し、怨嗟の声は山野に満ち、国家の危き情勢を馴致したるぞ是非なけれ。

ここにチンリウ姫は、乳母アララギの奸計にかかり、吾子のセンリウと強ひられ、且つ国宝破壊の罪を負はされ、かくれ島に流されけるが、この島は夕さり来れば荒浪の為に全島没し、これにある人畜は溺死するといふ魔の島なりけり。アララギは奸計の発覚をおそれ、特にこの島に主人のチンリウ姫を送らせたるにぞありける。又朝月は王の怒りにふれて、かくれ島より約五十哩ばかり沖にある荒島といふ岩石のみにて固まりし一孤島に捨てられ、歎きの月日を送りつつ魚介を餌食として、天の時を待ちゐたりける。

アララギの悪しき謀計に乗せられて チンリウ姫は流されにけり 朝月も亦アララギの計略に 荒島さして流されにけり 悪神は一度は花咲き栄ゆとも 時の到ればもろく亡びむ。
