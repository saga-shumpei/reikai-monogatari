Chapter 1: イドム Battle [2028]

イドム戦〔2028〕

§ 1

To the southwest of Takateruyama, upon the vast sea of Made, lies an island nation of considerable area, which is called the island of Isago. In the center of this island, a great mountain range lies from east to west, which is called the Ōsakasanmiyaku. The region south of the Ōsakasanmiyaku is called the land of Idomu, and the region to the north is called the land of Sāru. This island is the oldest island to have emerged among the islands of the sea of Made, and many Kunitsukamira dwell there. For decades, the two lands of Idomu and Sāru have engaged in ceaseless warfare, each seeking to occupy the other's territory. Many Kunitsukamira have tasted the bitterness of misery, and their longing for the kaurin of the Kiuseishin is like the feeling of one waiting for clouds and rainbows during a great taikan.

In the middle of the Ōsakayama, there is a large lake, which is called the Shinju-ko. The Shinju-ko is a great lake, ten ri from north to south and twenty ri from east to west, spread across the mountainside at an altitude of about twenty meters. Strangely, as if it had welled up from the bottom of the sea, the lake water contains a thick salty taste, and even if one wades across the water, it only submerges one's knees. In this Shinju-ko, many mermaids with human faces and fish bodies reside. They build dwellings on the shore of the lake, thatching their roofs with reeds just like the Kunitsukamira, and their way of life is almost identical to that of the Kunitsukamira.

Since this lake is on the south side of the Ōsakayama, it is naturally within the territory of the land of Idomu. When the Kunitsukamira of Idomu capture the mermaids and torment them in various ways to make them weep, the mermaids grieve and shed tears like waterfalls. Strangely, all those tears turn into pearls, the beauty of which is beyond description. Therefore, the Kunitsukamira of Idomu treat them as their sole treasure, adorning their heads and chests with them, and boasting of their beauty. Furthermore, when they ingest them, their bodies instantly emit light, and they give birth to beautiful children. Thus, the Kunitsukamira compete to obtain them, and making it their sole business to capture mermaids through various schemes to collect their tears. Consequently, all the Kunitsukamira of Idomu are handsome men and beautiful women, and ugly men and women have finally ceased to exist. In contrast, the Kunitsukamira of the land of Sāru, north of the Ōsakayama, all have dark skin, curly hair, are short in stature, and are only ugly men and women.

Here, the King of Sāru, Eirusu, seeking to occupy this Shinju-ko by any means to improve his race, led a great army, crossed the Ōsakayama to the south, and began his march toward the Shinju-ko. Upon hearing this, the King of Idomu, Azumi, summoned the Left Guardian, the Right Guardian, and the divine military strategists to the great hall to deliberate on the strategy to annihilate the army of the land of Sāru.

The name of the King of Idomu is Azumi. The name of the Queen is Muraji. The Left Guardian is called Nāman, and the Right Guardian is called Tāman. The military strategist is called Shiuran, the daughter born between Azumi and Muraji is called Chinriu, and the lady-in-waiting is called Araragi. King Azumi of Idomu gathered the war gods and sang to deliberate on the strategy for conquering the land of Sāru.

Idomu's land from ancient days, By the water and fire of the Lord Ōkami, Appeared as a beautiful land. Spring, summer, autumn, winter in order, Five winds and ten rains forever, Moistening all of the land, Enjoying supreme peace and tranquility, In a world most auspicious, the land of Idomu. By the blessings of the Shinju-ko, The Kunitsukamira are all, Exceedingly pure and beautiful, Both in soul and body, Maintaining long life and divine virtue, Having enjoyed them with gratitude, but, Days pass and months flow, The light of the stars also shifts, In the years that have passed, The King of the land of Sāru, Due north of the Ōsakayama, Resenting this good land,

Occupying the Shinju-ko, Seeking to steal the treasure of the mermaids, Leading many troops, Coming to attack, how hateful. We are not fond of war by nature, But if it comes to this, To protect this divine land, I summon the gods of war, To punish the evil deeds of the enemy, And drive them beyond a thousand ri, To govern and protect as a peaceful land, As it was in the past, is my heart. Left Guardian, Right Guardian, and all, Strategists and all Kunitsukamira, Observe my decree well, And advance the army toward the enemy camp as soon as possible. Regarding that, there are various, Means available, so Kunitsukamira, Cooperate with one another.

大栄山の中腹に大なる湖水ありて、之を真珠湖といふ。真珠湖は約二十メートルの山腹に展開したる南北十里、東西二十里の大湖水なるが、不思議にも海底より涌出せるものの如く、湖水皆濃厚なる塩味を含み、水上を徒渉するも僅に膝を没するに過ぎざる湖水なりけり。この真珠湖には人面魚身の人魚数多住居し、湖辺の汀辺に国津神同様茅を以て屋根を葺きたる家居を構へ、その生活の様も殆ど国津神に酷似せり。

この湖は大栄山の南側にあるを以て無論イドムの国の領域なりけるが、イドムの国津神は人魚を捕へ来りて、色々と苦しめ泣かしめる時、人魚は悲しみて涙を滝の如く流しけるに、不思議や其の涙は悉く真珠の玉となりて、その美しさ言はむ方なし。故にイドムの国津神等は之を唯一の宝として頭に飾り、胸に飾り、その美を誇る。又之を内服する時は身体忽ち光を放ち、且美しき子の生るるを以て、国津神は競ひて之を得む事を欲し、色々の計略を以て人魚を奪ひ、涙を採る事を唯一の業務となしける。故にイドムの国津神は何れも美男美女のみにして、醜男醜女は終に跡を断つに至れるなり。之に反して大栄山以北のサールの国津神は何れも肌黒く、髪はちぢれ、背は低く且醜男醜女のみなりける。

茲にサールの国王エールスは、如何にもして此の真珠湖を占領し種族の改良を計らむとし、大軍隊を率ゐて大栄山を南に越え、真珠湖に向つて進軍を始めける。之を聞くよりイドムの王アヅミは、左守、右守を始めとし、軍師の神々を大広間に呼び集め、サール国の軍隊を殲滅すべく軍議を凝らす事となりける。

イドム王の名はアヅミといふ。王妃の名をムラジといふ。左守をナーマン、右守をターマンといふ。軍師をシウランといひ、アヅミ、ムラジの間に生れたる娘をチンリウといひ、侍女をアララギといふ。イドム王のアヅミは軍神を集め、サール国征伐の軍議を凝らさむとして歌ふ。

『イドムの国は昔より 主の大神の御水火にて 現はれ出でし美し国 春夏秋冬順序よく 五風十雨は永久に 国のことごと霑して 至治太平を楽しみし 世にも目出度きイドム国 真珠の湖の幸はひに 国津神等は悉く 優れて清く美しく 霊魂身体諸共に 長寿を保ち神徳を 忝けなみて来りしが 日は行き月は流れつつ 星の光も移ろひて 年月経にし其間に 大栄山の真北なる サールの国の国王は 此善き国を怨みつつ

Chinriu's lady-in-waiting Araragi sings:

I humbly beg of my King, / Permit me to accompany the Hime-gimi to the army. / I shall protect the person of the Hime-gimi, / And fight against the enemy army until I die.

Thus the council was decided, and they sent urgent messengers throughout the country to gather many war-gods into the castle, and it was determined that they would advance and fight against the enemy army in unison. At this time, neither too late nor too early, King Eerusu commanded his vast army, mounted a horse of dappled roan, and came attacking near the castle, waving his five-colored baton again and again. Azumi-ō, enraged like a raging fire, together with the Gunshi Shiuran, drew their bows taut like the full moon and shot at them, but the enemy consisted only of famous, fierce, and desperate warriors; in a battle lasting one day and one night, the army of Azumi-ō was easily defeated and fled toward Mount Gekkō in the south. In this battle, his daughter Chinriu, Araragi, and many other warriors were either killed or taken prisoner, and they suffered the humiliation of being bound. Thereupon, King Eerusu occupied the castle of Idomu, halted his many troops, and looked down upon the world. Azumi-ō holed up in Mount Gekkō, one hundred ri to the south, built castle walls there, and planned the restoration of the land of Idomu.
