Chapter 18: 蠑螈の精 [2045]

蠑螈の精〔2045〕

§ 1

Having removed the Shiyujin Chinriuhime through keiriyaku, and having named herself Chinriuhime to become the consort of King Aemsu, Chinriuhime, who together with her mother Araragi stood unrivaled in kensei and reigned over amata gunshin, was shōeu through the shinrin within Kitayamajiyōnai, admiring the flowers blooming anatakonata in her tsurezure.

Chinriuhime: 'Before and behind, to the right and left, I wander surrounded by fragrant flowers. Having achieved my kuwaiten desires, I now bloom like the flowers of Kitayamajiyō. Though momochibana bloom and scent the air, how could they ever compare to the fragrance of my own flowers? Seeing the purple kakitsubata reflected upon the surface of the water, I know that summer has come. The castle of Kitayama is vast, a mahoraba of purity that gathers the keshiki of sansui. Praised by yobito as the flower of this castle, I live in this world with joy. Thinking that the ametsuchi has fallen entirely into wagate, I find my own being full of delight. The King of Aemsu is in the land of Idumu, and I have become the consort of the wakagimi. I feel joy, thinking that I am fragrant forever, with nothing to cause me seibaku in this castle. I shall rule over all the kunitsukami that exist, and by serving the King, I shall illuminate the imperial reign.'

Chinriuhime: 'Because my mother is wise, she made Chinriuhime into my own self and drove her away. How pleasant it is; Chinriuhime must have perished while drifting to the island of demons. If this is so, there is nothing in the world to fear, for I rely upon King Aemsu as my strength. As King Aemsu loves me, there is nothing I cannot achieve. When I see the lily flowers swaying in the morning breeze, they are like my own pure figure. People of the world have come to call the flowers that bloom in red and white the flowers of aoi. Though I am a fake of Chinriuhime, am I not also a flower that blooms like the aoi? It is a world where even the character for snow is written in black ink; what is there to fear? Asazuki, who saw through my disguise, has been driven away by the authority of the King. Asazuki must have been exiled to the island of Shimakene in the sea of a thousand ri, and her life must have perished there.'

Chinriuhime: 'Since there is nothing to hinder me, I shall move through the world as my heart desires. The officials of the murky Kitayamajiyō submit to my Kototama without struggle. Seeing my kensei rise day by day, it must be the manifestation of the power of wisdom. Where has my King, who served long in Idumu Castle, gone? There is no shadow of him anymore. Because of the ruin of my King, I now bloom here as the flower of Kitayamajiyō. I know in my own body that good deeds are attached to evil, and evil deeds are attached to good. If this is so, there is nothing in the world to fear, if I continue to manipulate King Aemsu.'

Singing thus, she was shōeu with a carefree air. From behind, a handsome man of exquisite appearance appeared abruptly.

Handsome Man: 'I have come following the footsteps of Hime-sama. I am the cousin of King Aemsu. Gazing upon the nobility and beauty of your figure, I was drawn here by the horse of my heart. Having suddenly caught sight of your figure, I have spent my days and nights as if in a dream. Since there is no human shadow nearby, I shall offer my longing to bloom before you, Kimi.'

At this voice, the fake Chinriuhime was startled and turned around. Seeing a handsome man many times more beautiful than King Aemsu, Chinriuhime was captured by the demon of love. Enchanted, she stepped toward the man, and while firmly grasping his right hand, she blushed and sang.

Chinriuhime: 'Did I ever think that such a beautiful and charming man existed in this land? As I am one who serves King Aemsu, I have no words to answer you. Yet, I shall treasure your loving heart and imprint it upon my breast. I never dreamed in my life that such a beautiful man of valor existed in this world. If it may be so, I wish to live in Kitayamajiyō, pledging a thousand years with you.'

The handsome man sings.

Handsome Man: 'I am the cousin of King Aemsu, a humble man named Seemusu. If I may fulfill your heart, from today on, I shall speak of a thousand generations while hiding from the eyes of others. I have come here now, hiding from the eyes of King Aemsu, while longing for Hime. I shall not regret my name, my rank, or my life, if I think that there is a night to meet with you.'

『前や後右も左も芳しき 花に包まれ吾は遊ぶも 回天の望みを遂げて吾は今 木田山城の花と匂ふも 百千花咲けど匂へど如何にして わが花の香に及ぶべきかは 燕子花花の紫水の面に 写るを見れば夏さりにけり 木田山の城は広けし山水の 景色あつめて清き真秀良場 此の城の花と世人に讃へられ 吾は楽しく世に生くるかも 天地は残らず吾手に入りしかと 思へば楽しき吾身なるかも エールスの王はイドムの国にあり われ若王の妃となりぬ 何ものの制縛もなく此の城に 時じくかをると思へば楽し 国津神のあらむ限りを統べ治め 王に仕へて御代を照らさむ

斯く歌ひながら、人もなげに逍遥して居る。後の方より容姿端麗なる美男子、すつくと現はれ、

『姫様のみあと慕ひて来りけり エームス王の吾は従弟よ 御姿の気高さ美々しさに見惚れつつ 心の駒に引かれ来しはや 汝が姿ふと見初めてゆ朝夕を うつつともなく過ぎにけらしな 傍に人影なければわが思ひ 君の御前に匂はせ奉らむ』

此の声に贋のチンリウ姫は驚き振り返れば、エームス王に幾倍とも知れぬ美男子、チンリウ姫は恋の悪魔にとらはれ、恍惚として男の側に進み寄り、右手をしつかと握りながら、頬を赤らめて歌ふ。

『思ひきやかく麗はしき艶人の 此の国原におはしますとは エームスの王に仕へし吾なれば 汝に答ふる言の葉もなし さりながら汝が愛しき心根を われ忝なみて胸にしるさむ かかる世に此のうるはしき大丈夫の いますとは夢にも知らざりにけり ままならば君と千歳を契りつつ 木田山城に住みたく思ふ』

美男は歌ふ。

『吾こそはエームス王の従弟にて セームスといふ軽きものなり 御心に叶ひ奉らば今日よりは 人目を忍びて千代を語らむ われは今エームス王の目を忍び 姫を恋ひつつ此処に来りし 名も位も生命も吾は惜しからじ 君と会ふ夜のありと思へば』

§ 2

Chinriuhime sings:

'Meeting my beloved, my heart beats fast and will not cease, and my face is flushed with heat. If tomorrow comes, shall I not speak of our thousand-year vow with you in this forest?'

Semusu sings:

'As I listen to the words of your precious affection, the steed of my heart cannot cease its prancing.'

Singing thus, he vanished somewhere like smoke.

Chinriuhime stands in a daze and sings:

'Oh, the mystery of it all; the beautiful Semusu of love has vanished like smoke. Semusu, even more beautiful than King Eemusu, is indeed my very Inochi.'

Singing thus, she returns slowly to the palace.

Araragi welcomes her at the entrance:

'Where have you been, and why are you alone without even your Tomobito? I was startled that you were nowhere to be seen, and my heart was broken into a thousand pieces. From tomorrow, go forth with your Mitomo; walking alone must be dangerous.'

Chinriuhime sings:

'Enticed by the pure fragrance of the Momobana, I wandered alone without knowing. Within the Kitayamajiyōnai surrounded by water, what could there be to fear? Since this castle is as we desire, it is well to play with a peaceful heart.'

Just as she sang this, King Eemusu, who had been waiting in some suspense due to the Hime's absence, appeared on the scene and said:

'Are you returning now? My heart has been greatly agitated. From tomorrow, you must play accompanied by your Jijiyo; walking alone does not accord with Wagai.'

Chinriuhime smiles and sings:

'I was standing in the back garden praying for the happiness of my King, and I was offering Kamigoto.'

Thus, the day was wrapped in the darkness of Tasogare, and the Fūfu went to bed in harmony, but on the Akuruhi, as a Teigen of Chinriuhime, it was decided to float a boat on the Shōbu pond within the castle and attempt a half-day of pure leisure.

The preparations for the boat excursion on the Shōbu pond were completed. However, even though it was called a boat, it was merely the trunk of a large tree hollowed out with a stone chisel, so there was not much room for many people to ride; there were only King Eemusu, Chinriuhime, Araragi, and two other Jijiyo.

The King, looking at the purple flowers blooming on the shore of the Shōbu pond, sings cheerfully:

'How delightful it is to pole through this pond water where the Shōbu bloom and play with the flowers that speak. As I watch the purple of the flowers reflected on the bottom of the water, what a charming boat excursion this is. The true carp and scarlet carp lurking at the bottom of the eight-fathom deep pond must be startled. It is unprecedented since ancient times to float a boat and play in this pond for the first time. Though it has been handed down since ancient times that a demon dwells in this pond, how peaceful it is today. It is a delightful boat ride to play together with the brave woman Araragi.'

Araragi sings:

'I am astonished at the cleverness of the King's words. Is it not Chinriuhime, rather than Araragi? As for this aged Araragi, the fragrance of flowers has already faded, so there is no scent to offer.'

King Eemusu sings:

'The flowers that bloom in spring are good, but I also find the fragrance of autumn flowers hard to discard. It is a pure day today where the rainy season sky has cleared, and the irises and Shōbu are fragrant. Because the attire of Chinriuhime is pure, even the Shōbu and the irises look bashful.'

Chinriuhime sings:

'How happy and reassuring are the words of my King. I shall offer my Inochi to serve you. Blooming as the living flowers of my King's hand, I shall serve as the foundation of Kitayamajiyōnai.'

Just as she sang this, a wonder occurred; the pond water suddenly boiled over, water columns rose in various places, and due to the raging waves, the dugout canoe was instantly overturned, and King Eemusu fell headlong into the water and never appeared again.

Here, barely escaping with their Inochi, Araragi, Chinriuhime, and the other Jijiyo crawled up to the shore and preserved their precious Inochi.

The handsome man named Semusu who had appeared before Chinriuhime the previous day was the master of this pond and the spirit of a giant salamander; it was his scheme to suddenly make the pond water leap to overturn the boat, take the King's Inochi, and become the husband of Chinriuhime to rule over this castle.

茲に生命からがら、アララギ、チンリウ其の他の侍女は汀辺に這い上り、玉の生命をつなぎける。

先の日チンリウ姫の前に現はれし、セームスといふ美男は此の池の主にして、巨大なる蠑螈の精なりけるが、俄に池水を躍らせて舟を顛覆せしめ、王の生命を奪ひとり、チンリウ姫の夫となりて此の城にはばらむとする計略なりける。

§ 3

From this time, Fushigi, Araragi, and the two Jijiyo, though their Inochi were spared, became unable to see anything as their eyes were dazzled and their throats choked, nor could they speak. Therefore, they remained unaware that the King had fallen into the water and suffered Dekishi.

Here, the Spirit of the Imori, having become King Aems, sat composedly in the Okuden, keeping Chinriu-hime close by his side, and as he indulged in the pleasure of Fugi, the Kokusei grew disordered day by day, which was truly wretched.

Chinriu-hime, while noting that he resembled King Aems in some way, frowned with Fushin at the slight difference in his Yōsu and sang:

Chinriu-hime: 'Thinking King Aems had fallen into the Chichiu and lost his Inochi, I was surprised; though I think you are King Aems, there is something about you that does not sit well with me. I suspect you might be that Adebito with whom I spoke the other day.'

The Spirit of the Imori sang:

Spirit of the Imori: 'How foolish you are, Chinriu-hime; I am indeed that Sems whom you met the other day. Fortunately, King Aems has perished; come, let us live together from now on. Even if you lament, the one who has departed will not return; pray, live your life with me quickly.'

Chinriu-hime sang:

Chinriu-hime: 'Little did I think you were Sems, the Yasaotoko, who has revived my soul. I, too, had grown weary of King Aems ever since I first Miso your form. You are my husband of Tokoyo, my husband of love; I shall offer my Inochi and serve you.'

The Spirit of the Imori sang:

Spirit of the Imori: 'You are not the true Chinriu-hime either, but must be a Nisedama of Senriu-hime. I, too, am not the true King Aems, but the Itoko, Sems the Yasaotoko. Is it not Omoshiro that two Nisemono should pledge their Nise in this castle? Araragi has lost his sight and become mute, and seems not to perceive my scheme. From Kefu, for your sake, I shall heal Araragi's illness and save him for Toha.'

Chinriu-hime sang:

Chinriu-hime: 'Will you save my mother? How grateful I am; you are indeed my husband. With good things piling up one after another, I am drawn to you, my beloved. Continue to impersonate King Aems and rule over Kitayamajiyō.'

The Spirit of the Imori sang:

Spirit of the Imori: 'Your Kotoba is well-taken; I shall continue to impersonate King Aems and rule. How Omoshiro my world is, now that I think of myself as the master of the castle of Kitayamajiyō.'

Thus, the false Chinriu-hime and the false King Aems, who was the incarnation of the Spirit of the Imori, lived deep within Kitayamajiyō, and the Kokusei grew disordered and declined day by day, until it reached a state beyond recovery, which was truly unavoidable.
