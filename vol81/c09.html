Chapter 9: 維 New の叫び [2036]

維新の叫び〔2036〕

§ 1

The Kokuō of the land of Sāru, who had governed the Hokuhan of the island of Isago with Bōriyoku, crossed over Mount Ōsakayama and led Insotsu of Amata Tsuhamono, pressing his attack upon the castle of Idomu, which had been heard of as a Rakudo on Chijiyō. He routed and scattered the Kokuō and others, acting with great Habu like a Kafumori in a village without birds, yet how long could Ten permit the tyranny of King Ērusu? Suddenly from beneath his very Shitsuka, the flames of Kuwaen welled up, and atop the steep Kabeta crags on the Kishibe of the Minanogaha of pure flow, he was urged to drink severe sake by his trusted wife; during a fleeting moment when his Tamayura steps failed him, pushed from behind by his wife's Mikoto, it was miserable how he vanished into the bubbles Suichiu with a great splash. Thereupon the Ōhi Sakukusu secretly met with the Samori no Kami Chikutā away from Hitome, boasting of her Shiyōri of love, yet still unsatisfied, she resolved to capture the Ningiyo of Lake Shinjiyuko, leading Suhiyaku of knights to press upon the pearl lake. Although they approached with grand banners and drums, she fell victim to the plot of the chief of the Ningiyo and fragilely perished; beginning with Sakukusu-hime, not to mention Chikutā the Samori, it was miserable how the many hundreds of knights all became prey to the fish tribe of the lake of pearls. Hereupon, the royal castle of Idomu lost its vital central ministers, instantly displaying the details of eternal darkness. The hundred ministers were astonished, fluttering about in confusion to the right and left, scurrying around, but what could be done? The turmoil of the land of Idomu, having lost the pillars of the state, was so dreadful that one could not bear to look. Hereupon the strategist Ēman led Insotsu of Amata knights, taking up position in the castle of Idomu, making careful plans without neglect to calm the disorder of the country, grieving his heart day and night. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara! How fearsome is the divine punishment of Kami!

Ēman solemnly buried the bodies of Sakukusu-hime, Chikutā, and the others, and while observing ten days of mourning, he composed a poem of reflection.

'Extremely lonely is our country; We have lost the pillar of the state. Having touched the wrath of the Kami of heaven and earth, Such grief is without precedent in the world. Thinking it the retribution for the crime of banishing Azumi and Muraji from Idomu Castle, It fills me with dread. Enveloped in darkness bound for Tokoyo, Idomu Castle, Where is it going? My heart is uneasy. Though I have now become the commander of the army, There is no way to govern, beneath the moon over white waves. In the vast sky, the unfeeling moon shines bright; It seems as if it laughs at our grief. The moon reflected in the flowing Minanogaha has also grown hazy, For the tears of my eyes fall like rain. Praying to the moon, "How shall the castle of Idomu be preserved?" The moon does not answer. The mountains and rivers have faded in color; The curtain of darkness in my chest does not clear. Thinking of the ultimate fate of the King who oppressed the people and indulged in luxury, It fills me with dread. The deeds of Queen Sakukusu grew wilder day by day and month by month, And thus she perished. Enticed by the base heart of Chikutā, Alas, the Queen passed away. The mysterious nature of King Ērusu's end— The clouds of my soul have not yet cleared. Seizing the country of others by military force— Today's grief must be the retribution. Mountains, rivers, trees, and grass all seem to raise their voices at once, Lamenting the tottering country. Everything I see and hear brings tears; How shall I bring life to this world? Nārisu, the Umi, whom I relied upon as my strength, Has been sent back far away to Sāru. If only we had Nārisu the Umi now, I would not have to break my heart so much. On this day when there is not even a friend to converse with, I only weep in loneliness. Though there are three thousand Tsuhamono, without a King, The land of Idomu cannot maintain discipline. Here and there, soldiers gather, And I hear they are plotting evil things. If the soldiers become one and attack, The castle of Idomu will instantly perish. Thinking of how to sever the disorders of this world, My heart is enveloped in darkness. Only now do I regret the deed of King Ērusu, Who banished Azumi and Muraji. Relying on the strength of his power, King Ērusu Seized the castle of Idomu. I too followed King Ērusu, A sinner who advanced the army. The ministers who serve this castle morning and evening— Their hearts seem to be scattered in a thousand directions. Thinking to serve the country to the very end, It is lonely that there is no true person. Thinking to govern the world tangled like dried rushes, It is to no avail for powerless me. The three thousand soldiers act individually, Plotting matters and refusing to follow.'

§ 2

I wish to escape from the castle of Idumu while there is still time, and return to Mototsumikuni.

Within the castle of Idumu, since the passing of King Aers, Princess Sax, and other high-ranking retainers all at once, it was as silent and desolate as a fire extinguished; only the strategist Aeman remained alive, racking his heart and soul day and night to plan the restoration of the country.

The story changes: the various Kunitsukami, suffering under the tyranny of King Aers, were filled with voices of resentment throughout the land. Upon learning of the passing of the King and his followers, patriots from towns and villages rose up, and the voice of the Restoration surged everywhere like the incoming tide. Among them, the two heroes, Mark and Rat, the great leaders of the patriotic faction, sensing that the time had come, appeared everywhere in the capital and the countryside, and from horseback, they loudly shouted and urged the Kunitsukami to rise up.

The crowd blew conch shells, struck gongs, and beat drums, causing demonstrations to occur everywhere, presenting a scene so noisy that even the mountains seemed to crumble. Mark gathered the crowd in the plaza outside Idumu Castle, and while standing tall on his horse, he sang the song of the Restoration in a loud voice.

Mark: 'People of the land of Idumu, the autumn to rise up has come. The heavens are dark, and the light of the sun and moon has sunk into the earth. While our King of Azumi and Muraji was taken away, we have followed the enemy king; the divine punishment has come to reward us, and we have tasted the suffering of the burning coals. Since it has come to this, we have no choice but to die of hunger. My people, my brothers, the land of Idumu is the paradise that sustains the life you inherited from your ancestral gods. This beautiful and good land has been taken by Aers of the land of Saru, and day and night we have been suffering, with our wives and children taken and our homes and storehouses burned.

Rise up, rise up, now is the autumn. Protect the fatherland and plan for the eternal peace of the country. We shall now proceed to the royal castle, riding side by side. Do not hesitate. To protect the land of Idumu forever is for your own sake. Advance, advance, come, advance, until the peace of the country comes, until the traces of the demons are extinguished.'

Rat sings.

Rat: 'Ah, people of the land, people of the land, the autumn for us to rise has come. Our country, where we entrust our lives forever and enjoy, has been taken by the land of Saru, and we have seen sad and sorrowful times. Heaven will never side with tyranny; it has taken the life of the unrighteous King Aers, and subsequently destroyed Sax, Chikuta, and other evil spirits, and has begun the purification. You, the various people of the nation, the autumn to clear away the daily resentment has come. Now is the autumn to protect the lives of the people and bring about eternal peace. Be brave, be brave, rise up. I am a child of God, a palace of God; if you have the courage to advance,

you shall never be defeated in battle. Do you not know that the strategist Aeman, all alone, is holding out in the castle of Idumu, plotting and scheming to torment and afflict us, the people? Now is this autumn, this autumn. To destroy the strategist Aeman and make the light of the country shine, to return to the heaven of the original past, is your responsibility. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu. There is no blade that can stand against justice. We have the protection of God. You too are children of God, palaces of God; never hesitate. Advance, advance, come, advance, until the peace of the country comes, until the great ambition of the Restoration is achieved, until the demon Aeman perishes.

Until this great enemy perishes, advance without flinching, without tiring. Mark and Rat shall stand at the head and advance steadily. Follow us, people of the nation. To protect your lives, to avenge your enemies, attack and gather at the castle of Idumu.'

Thus, the crowd attacked the castle of Idumu in a great force, and the strategist Aeman, upon seeing this scene, was so surprised and panicked that he leaped from the high hall, jumped into the rushing current of the Mizunokawa River, and vanished like white waves.

The clan of Aers, who oppressed and acted arrogantly toward the people, all became demons. Chikuta, while inciting the Queen to do evil, was destroyed by the wrath of God. The position of Aers, who ousted King Azumi, was but a dream. The glory of Aers was also but a dream of a single year; what a miserable world. The people, feeling that this was the moment, rose up and attacked the castle of Idumu.
