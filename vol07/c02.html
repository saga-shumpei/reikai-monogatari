Chapter 2: Encounter of the Three Divine Stewards [302]

第2章　三神司邂逅〔302〕

§ 1

From the summit of the mountain a clear and cool voice was heard,

"The world had ended in eternal darkness, and the Yahirodono built by Kuni-no-Mihashira-no-Ōkami, who went forth to the land of Yomi, had the pillars of true wood rot away and the world of the gods leaned toward collapse; Yasuyohiko rises and helps it, and Maganehiko is steadfast of heart. Appearing in the heavenly realm, together with Mizu-no-Mitama, to consolidate the origin of this world, they spread the teachings of heavenly truth into heaven and earth without a shadow, making the world of the gods most peaceful and tranquil, and while maintaining the parental throne, the drifting land was made ever firmer and solidified by Ōgotomoshio-no-Kami who has appeared, accomplishing the completion of the divine administration.

We are the messengers of the gods. Solidifying firmly the foundation of the Land of the Rising Sun for thousands of ages, stepping forth as the chief of the divine age, spreading the teachings in all directions throughout Shikishima, I am the Master of Yasuyo, I am the Master of Magane. Appearing before you now, the one called Ōgotomoshio-no-Kami is an incarnation of Yamata-no-Orochi coiled in the Ural Mountains; ten years ago in the past, he shut himself in this sacred mountain, tore through the divine placenta of the central land of the Land of Lush Reed Plains, with a deep plot to throw this world into disorder, hiding in these deep mountains, gathering numerous evil deities, and scheming to rise again, how wretched! Ame-no-Mihashira-no-Ōkami,

Having discerned all the plots of the evil spirits, Yasuyohiko for thousands of ages, Maganehiko as firm as rock and everlastingly, shall form and solidify this present world, and it is Hinode-no-Ōkami who illuminates the foundation of the peaceful Land of the Rising Sun. How gallant that he guides us to this mountain of profound divine plan! This mountain, towering with the ocean behind it to the east, south, and west, is an impregnable iron wall. May you, divine Mikoto, be enshrined as firm as rock and everlastingly on this mountain, and protect the throne of the heavenly imperial deities. I shall become the Master of the Left Guard, and through Maganehiko, the divine land of the Yamato islands shall be made ever firmer, ever enduring without shaking,

ruling and breaking here the deep plot of Tateiwa, and subduing all the crooked spirits. Yasuyo shall become the deity of the Right Guard, pounding and solidifying the floating island where wild waves rage with the divine majesty, becoming the deity Yasuyohiko, and peacefully protecting the Land of Urayasu for thousands of ages. The sacred mountain directly struck by the morning sun, the sacred peak directly struck by the setting sun, towering among a hundred mountains and a thousand valleys as the sacred mountain of Ōdaigahara, appear eternally as Hinode-no-Kami, and become Hinode-no-Kami of the morning sun who preserves the teachings of the gods of heavenly and earthly doctrines."

While singing thus, they advanced close to the side of Hinode-no-Kami, who was standing before the cavern.

現代日本語訳

山の頂から涼しい声が聞こえて、

『世は常闇となり果て、黄泉国にお出ましになった国の御柱大神がお建てになった八尋殿の、真木の柱が朽ち果てて倒れかかった神の世を起こし助ける康代彦、心も堅い真鉄彦。天津御国に現れて、瑞の御魂とともにこの世の根元を固めようと、天津誠の御教えを天と地に隈なく行き渡らせて、神の世をきわめて平らかに安らかにし、親の位を保ちつつ、漂う国をいよいよ堅く締め固めた大事忍男神が現れ給い、神政成就を成し遂げる。

われらは神の御使いであるぞ。千代に八千代に日の本の礎を堅く搗き固め、神代の長となり出て、教えを四方に広める敷島の、われは康代の司であるぞ、われは真鉄の司であるぞ。いま汝の前に現れて、大事忍男神というのは、ウラルの山に蟠る八岐大蛇の化身であり、今より十年その昔、この神山に立て籠もり、瑞穂の国の中つ国の神の胞衣を打ち破り、この世を乱す深い企みをもって、この深山に隠れ潜み、数多の邪神を狩り集めて再挙を図る浅ましさよ。天の御柱大神は、

魔神の企みをことごとくお覚りになり、現世を千代に八千代に康代彦、堅磐常磐に真鉄彦が造り固めて、浦安の日の出づる国の礎を照らす日の出の大神であるぞ。仕組みも深きこの山に導き給う雄々しさよ。東・南・西に海原を控えてそびえるこの山は、難攻不落の鉄壁であるぞ。汝の命はこの山に堅磐常磐に鎮まって、天津日嗣の皇神の御位をお守り申し上げよ。われは左守の司となり、大和島根の神国を、真鉄の彦がいよいよ堅く、いよいよ常久に揺るぎなく、

治めてここに立岩の深い企みを打破し、曲神をことごとく平定しよう。康代は右守の神となり、荒波猛り狂う浮島を神の稜威によって搗き固め、康代の彦の神となり、浦安の国を心安らかに千代に八千代に守るべきである。朝日の直射す神の山、夕日の直射す神の峰、百山千谷のその中にそびえ立った大台ヶ原の御山と永久に日の出神と現れて、天教地教の神々の教えを守る朝日子の日の出神とおなりなさい』

と歌いながら、岩窟の前に立っている日の出神のそば近く進み寄って来る。
