Chapter 9: The Faint-Hearted Man [309]

第9章　弱腰男〔309〕

§ 1

Hinode-no-Kami then climbed Mount Tenkyō, reached the Shrine of Konohana-hime in Aokigahara, and related in detail the course of his divine teachings' propagation thus far; Konohana-hime was deeply impressed and ordered him to depart again for the country of Tokoyo. Hinode-no-Kami had an audience with Tsuki-no-Mihashira-no-Kami and Ame-no-Mihashira-no-Kami, received various divine edicts, and joyfully set out once more on the path of propagation.

He became a passenger on the Tokoyomaru, which was now about to set sail from Tagonoura toward the country of Tokoyo. On board, numerous people were crowded to full capacity to travel either to the country of Kara or to the country of Tokoyo. The ship advanced west and west across the ocean, where waves were quiet and winds were calm. Drifting upon the quiet sea for several tens of days, it finally arrived at the shore of the country of Gesshi, where Mount Seiun stood, and here everyone landed and rested from the fatigue of the voyage. Due to the conditions of the shifting winds, this ship fell into a situation where it had no choice but to anchor in this harbor for about a month. Hinode-no-Kami, suffering from tedium, penetrated deep into the mountains and attempted propagation in this land.

This place was the foot of a slightly high mountain called Mount Shirayuki, continuous with Mount Seiun; the mountains and fields were verdant, various flowers of grasses and trees bloomed in profusion crowding the space, and the sight of butterflies sporting among the flowers and the voices of birds flying about the treetops and singing were truly the height of tranquility. Hinode-no-Kami sang propagation songs all day throughout the long day, and at last became exhausted and felt drowsy; laying himself down amid the roadside flowers and grass with his arm as a pillow, he was watching the faint clouds disperse. This was the entrance to Mount Shirayuki. To such a place, two or three people appearing to be villagers came running while panting for breath, and seeing the figure of the propagation missionary lying by the roadside, spread both hands, opened their mouths wide, and

"Ah!"

they were astonished as if to cry out, and fell flat on the spot. Hinode-no-Kami found it strange and,

"For what reason do you look upon my form and become frightened?"

when he thus inquired,

Villager A: "Are you not a propagation missionary who is a retainer of Ura, Ura, Uralhiko's, g-god? P-p-p-please overlook me in this place. I will state everything without concealing a thing. Some time ago, a propagation missionary of Ananaikyō named Iwai-hime, a truly beautiful female missionary, appeared in this village and taught us various things; beginning with the chieftain of our village, young and old, men and women gathered before the Rock Kami deep in this mountain without a single person remaining behind, and with that woman in their midst, were hearing various splendid discourses. The chieftain and his wife were entirely impressed by those words, and it was decided that Shirayukigō would revere the Kami of Ananaikyō without a single person left out. Today as well, while we were conducting that festival, missionaries of Uralhiko appeared, abducted that woman and carried her off into the depths of the mountain, and our chieftain and his wife were tied up to a great tree. Then, glaring fiercely with wide eyes, they roared, 'If you would flee, try fleeing, you fellows; since missionaries of Uralhiko surround this mountain, you had better cast away Ananaikyō right now on this spot and revere the god of Daichūkyō; if you commit disobedience, we will destroy all of you, starting with the chieftain!' I barely escaped the jaws of death and managed to come this far, but finally I have been found by a missionary of Daichūkyō. Please, spare only my life!"

田子の浦から今や常世国に向かって出航しようとする常世丸の船客となった。船中には数多くの人々が、あるいは唐国へ、あるいは常世国へ通うべく満員に乗っていた。波は静かで風が凪ぎわたる海原を西へ西へと進んで行く。静かな海に漂うこと数十日、ついに青雲山のある月氏国の浜辺に到着し、ここで一同は上陸して船路の疲れを休めた。この船は風の吹き回しの都合によって一ヶ月ほどこの港に停泊することを余儀なくされる状況に立ち至った。日の出神は退屈に苦しみ、山深く分け入ってこの地で宣伝を試みた。

「あっ」

とばかりに仰天して、その場に倒れ伏した。日の出神はいぶかしく思って、

「お前たちはなぜ、私の姿を見て驚くのか」

と尋ねると、

甲「貴下はウラ、ウラ、ウラル彦の、か、神様の御家来の宣伝使様ではありませぬか。ど、ど、ど、どうぞこの場は見逃してくださいませ。私は何もかも包み隠さず申し上げます。先頃からこの村に三五教の宣伝使祝姫という、それはそれは美しい女性の宣伝使が現れて、色々なことを教えてくれまして、我が里の酋長をはじめ、老若男女は一人も残らずこの山奥の岩神の前に寄り集まり、その女性を囲んで、種々の結構なお話を承っておりました。酋長夫婦はすっかりその言葉に感心いたして、白雪郷は一人も残らず三五教の神様を祭ることとなりました。今日もその祭を行っております所へ、ウラル彦の宣伝使が現れ、その女性を掠奪して山の奥へ連れて行ってしまい、私どもの酋長夫婦は大木に縛りつけられてしまいました。そうして『逃げるなら逃げてみよ、お前たち奴めら、この山はウラル彦の宣伝使が取り巻いているから、今この場ですっかり三五教を捨てて、大中教の神を祭ればよい、背くならば、酋長をはじめお前たち奴めらを滅ぼしてしまう』と、恐ろしく目を剥いて怒鳴られました。私はようやく虎口を逃れてここまで来ることには来ましたが、とうとう大中教の宣伝使様に見つけられました。どうぞ命ばかりはお助けください」

§ 2

As they wept shedding tears, Hinode-no-kami was startled and rose abruptly, saying:

"I am a missionary of the Ananai teachings. From this moment I shall rescue your villagers. Guide me swiftly."

As he said this and began to sing the missionary hymn once more, the three men, Kō, Otsu, and Hei, suddenly took heart, and

Otsu: "Come, now all is well! Did not the chief tell us that no matter what happens, we must not abandon this deity, and that when all seems hopeless he will surely deliver us? And what of you? Just a moment ago, while the tears were not yet dry on your weeping face, you were whining, 'The all-important missionary has been abducted, the devout chief and his wife are tied to a tree, yet the deity of the Ananai teachings does not help us; as the saying goes, one should yield to the powerful, so let us cast off our former faith entirely and join the Daichū teachings.' And yet, what is the meaning of your high spirits now?"

Hei: "That was you! Did you not just say so yourself? Thinking that it would be unbearable if you were reported to the missionary, you try to make him think it was I who said it; but he is a living deity, so he knows all too well!"

Otsu: "He who strikes first wins. Before you could speak, I took the initiative."

Kō said in a low voice:

"Hey, you two! Rejoice as you will, but the enemy is many, while on our side there is only a single missionary. However strong he may be, his strength must have its limits. Who knows if this missionary too will be bound up like the woman and taken deep into the mountains? Do not be so glad; you ought to think twice."

Otsu and Hei sighed, saying:

"Is that so? That is troubling indeed. But since we have not yet seen his skill, what if we quietly followed behind him, keeping in and out of sight?"

Hinode-no-kami said:

"Hey, you three, what nonsense are you muttering? Quickly step out in front and guide me. My heart grows impatient."

Kō: "Noble deity, are you certain you will be all right? There are many frightfully terrifying fellows over there."

Pointing his finger, he continued:

"If you go straight along this mountain like this, turn this way, turn again like this, then like this, this, and this, and proceed straight ahead, you will find everyone tied up there, having their heads struck and jabbed, shrieking like tortured monkeys. Following the sound of their cries, please go at once. Farewell!"

Saying this, he tucked up the hem of his robe and tried to flee. Hinode-no-kami said:

"Hey, wait!"

And saying so, he firmly seized him by the hair at the nape of his neck.
