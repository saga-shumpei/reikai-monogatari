Chapter 24: Landing at Tsukushi [324]

第24章　筑紫上陸〔324〕

§ 1

Hinode-no-kami sang:

"Both the heavenly skies and the vast sea, Leaving behind the island of treasures of Ryūgū, Whose very name is auspicious, Kanmurijima, where Masumi-no-hime resides eternally, Blown as the wind of Shinado blows, Drifting and drifting, now here Faintly gazing upon the outline of the island of Tsukushi, Ah, the foolishness of the soul of my own body, Knowing naught of the deep design of the Sumegami; A brave man devoting his heart, In reverence to the divine word of Konohana-hime, Who resides high with cross-beams on the revered mountain of Fuji in Suruga, Crossing the eight hundred sea routes, And Sumiyo-hime, the sky of the heart, This island where the divine spirit resides eternally,

Crossing right before our eyes The rare islands that are spoken of As the treasure islands crafted by the Amatsu-mikami, The joy of once more searching for the treasures of this island Is just as the heavenly sunrise appeared In the eastern sky Upon the dark world where black and white cannot be discerned, Is just as it appeared."

"Hey, did you hear that song just now? In the broad daylight, he spoke of darkness before the eyes, or gloom, did he not? You heard him say the sun rises from the eastern sky, did you not? Isn't that a bit strange? When the sun deity is tilting toward the western sky, why would someone who is supposed to be a missionary teaching people say such incomprehensible things?"

"That is why you are hopeless. You only catch scraps of everything, and while knowing less than half of what an ignorant person knows, why do you make such derailed lectures as if you were someone who knows? Being coupled with you makes me feel miserable. You simply understand far too little."

"'Understand nothing, understand nothing'—what do you mean I don't understand? Isn't it you who understands nothing? While being a person with a wife and child, and reaching the age of fifty, you did ○○ to the ○○ of Osan at your place, got caught by your wife, and because of that your wife raised the horns of jealousy, talking of dying or living or demanding a divorce, starting quarrels every single day that even dogs wouldn't eat, which was a huge nuisance to the neighborhood. Even when Chief Mokuhei worried about it and explained the reasoning thoroughly as if chewing it up for you to swallow, didn't you blurt out that you wouldn't separate from her even if you died, or that you didn't understand? And yet you dare say that I understand nothing!"

"Come, come, the ship has arrived. Passengers, unless you wait about ten days for the wind here again, you cannot go to the Land of Tokoyo. If you dawdle, there is no telling where this ship will go. Won't you disembark quickly?"

「さあさあ、船が着きましたよ。お客様、またここで十日ばかり風を待たなければ、常世の国へは行けはしない。ぐずぐずしていると、この船はどこへ行くか分かりやしないぞ。早く降りないか」

§ 2

"Do not be so noisy. I cannot stand up."

"What do you mean you cannot stand? What are you doing?"

"You all go ashore first; I will go up afterward."

"What a malicious fellow you are. You intend to pocket whatever is left behind after everyone goes up; my rear is astounded at you!"

"It is that very rear."

"What do you mean by rear? Haha, you have gone and soiled yourself while sitting down! Haha, now I see. Stay still! If you move around, it will stink. What a foul fellow!"

The boatman, looking hurried, said,

"Hey, will you not stand up quickly?"

"Yes, yes, I am standing up now."

"What is with that dung-dropping posture?"

"I truly did drop it."

The boatman, flushing bright red, said,

"Clean it up completely! If you do not clean it, I will not let you go ashore, you dung-dropper!"

Hinode-no-Kami went ashore together with the two missionaries, and singing the propagation song once more, advanced deep inland without looking behind.
