Chapter 3: The White Dragon [303]

第3章　白竜〔303〕

§ 1

Clearer than water, far more refreshing than the wind, with a brightness rivaling the moon, and with a countenance more graceful than a flower, a maiden deity suddenly appeared before the cavern. Placing both hands down before Hinode-no-Kami and the other two deities, and bowing most courteously without uttering a single word, she advanced deep into the dim cavern hole. Deeming that they must not let their guard down, Hinode-no-Kami crossed his hands, tilted his head, was lost in thought, and for a while spoke no words; however, Yasuyohiko-no-Mikoto straightened his collar, adjusted his posture, and clapping both hands, began a prayer to heaven and earth. The sound of the hand-clapping resonated through the great and small gorges, and the unseasonable echoes clapping hands here and there were just as if hundreds and thousands of people were clapping their hands all at once.

Resolving that unless one enters the tiger's den one cannot catch the tiger's cub, and intending to explore this strange cavern of unknown depth to discover whether it was indeed the dwelling of good deities or the lair of evil demons, the three deities strove to be first and advanced within. No matter how far they went there was no end, turning to the right, bending to the left, now ascending, now descending, until at last they reached the inside of a cavern with a high ceiling and a wide breadth.

Strange to say, this cavern beyond the reach of sight gradually opened up, revealing a hazy light from various places, presenting a spectacle almost like a night with a hazy moon.

Hinode-no-Kami stopped and, facing the two deities,

"You two deities, look closely toward that one direction,"

he indicated, whereupon the two deities strained their eyes, looked in the direction he pointed, and shifting their gaze, a palace of incomparable solemnity appeared. The two deities involuntarily

"Ah!"

raised their voices in astonishment, and keeping watch before and behind, left and right, they advanced aiming for the palace.

Is this the Heavenly Rock Cave or a land of immortals? To the right and left, before and behind, The rock doors are solidified with rocks; This firm and everlasting cavern Is a sacred realm of immortals crafted by a deity, Hollowed out by Ihatsuchibiko. It seemed as though heavenly beings and maidens Had arrived unseasonably To play hundreds of musical strains; Their hearts grew bold and their feet advanced. Advancing, the three divine persons At last arrived here.

When they looked closer, yet another solid stone gate was constructed. And the vermilion-lacquered palace soaring upon the summit of the stone gate stood majestically, as if pressing against the ceiling of the cavern.

Hinode-no-Kami exerted his adamantine strength, put all his might into it, and was about to push open the leaves of the rock door, when from within, in a voice of an unknown deity,

"Thou Hinode-no-Kami, sweetly taken in by our scheme, having seized upon victory to slip inside this cavern and insolently strayed into the dwelling place of the Eight-Forked Serpent with eight heads and eight tails—how utterly fleeting! Now that matters have come to this, ye are already rats in a bag, fish in a cauldron; whether we strangle ye to death, burn ye to death, broil and eat ye, or boil and eat ye, is entirely up to our will—how pitiful that thy heavenly fortune has run out!"

現代日本語訳

水よりも清く風よりもたいそう爽やかで、月にまがう明るさであり、その顔容は花よりもあでやかであった姫神が、忽然として巌窟の前に現れた。姫神は日の出神をはじめとする二柱の神の前に両手を支え、たいそう慇懃に会釈しながら物も言わず、小暗い巌窟の穴深く進み入った。油断はできまいと日の出神は両手を組み、頭を傾けて思案に暮れてしばらく言葉もなかったが、康代彦命は、襟を正し容貌を改め、両手を打って天地に祈願を始めた。拍手の声は大峡谷小峡谷に響き渡り、思いがけない山彦が、あちらこちらで手を打つありさまは、百人千人が一時に手を打つかのようであった。

三柱の神は、虎穴に入らずんば虎子を得ず、怪しい底も知れないこの巌窟を探検し、果たして善神の住処であるか、悪魔の巣窟であるか、探検しようと先を争って進み入る。行けども行けども際限がなく、右に折れ左に曲がり、あるいは上りあるいは下り、ようやくにして天井が高く横幅の広い巌窟の中に到達した。

不審なことには、見渡せないほどのこの巌窟は、次第に展開して、所々から朧げな光を現し、ほとんど朧月夜のような光景である。

日の出神は立ち止まり、二柱の神に向かい、

「二柱の神よ、彼方の一方をよくご覧になりなさい」

とお示しになると、二柱の神は目を凝らし、指さす方を見、目を転ずると、荘厳で例えようもない宮殿が現れ出ていた。二柱の神は思わず、

「ヤア」

と驚愕の声を張り上げながら、前後左右に心を配り、宮殿を目がけて進み行く。

天の岩戸であろうか仙境であろうか 右も左も前も後ろも 岩戸を岩によって固めている 堅固で永久不変の巌窟は 石土毘古のうがった 神のお造りになった神仙境であり 天人や天女が不意に やって来て数々の音楽を 奏でるばかりに思われて 心も勇み足が進む 進みゆく三柱の神人は ようやくここに到着したのである。

近づいて見ると、またしても堅固な石門が築かれてある。そして石門の頂にそびえ立っている朱塗りの宮殿は、威厳高くそびえて巌窟の天井を圧するばかりであった。

日の出神は、金剛力を発揮し、力を込めて、岩戸の扉を押し開こうとすると、中から誰の神の声とも知れず、

「汝、日の出神よ、我々の計略にまんまと乗せられ、勝ちに乗じてこの巌窟の中に忍び入り、身の程知らずにも八頭八尾の八岐の大蛇の御住居所へ迷い入ってきた、その果敢なさよ。こうなった上は、もはや汝らは袋の鼠、釜中の魚である。締め殺そうと、焼き殺そうと、炙って食おうと、煮て食おうと、こちらの心次第であり、天運が尽きた憐れさよ」

§ 2

...so it laughed loudly, raising its gong-like voice with a dry clatter. While the three deities ground their teeth, slanted up the corners of their eyes, laid their hands upon the hilts of their swords, and cast their eyes in all directions, the eastern sky flushed with crimson, and the surroundings suddenly became like day. Deep within the strange cavern, there was no reason for the sun deity to appear; wondering what manner of apparition it could be, the three deities gazed together toward the light.

Matetsuhiko laid his hands upon the pillars of the stone gate, exerted his adamantine strength, and wrenched up the gateposts with all his might, whereupon the gate collapsed on the spot with a creaking crash. Inside the gate, enveloped by a swarm of giant serpents too numerous to count by hundreds or thousands, the beautiful woman who had appeared earlier was sitting properly and composedly. Hinode-no-Kami raised his voice,

"Even the solid rock door where the eight-forked serpent lurked, By the sincere heart Of Matetsuhiko, Has had its gateposts shattered, And delightfully, as light snow Melts away, it has been removed. The power of sincerity entrusted to the deities Reaches everywhere without end, And the world shall expand and open for eternity. Having tracked down the dwelling Of the countless crooked spirits and giant serpents, They shall ring out as rust on the swords Of the three deities who maintain sincerity. Even the immovable rocks of such a firm rock mountain, Like decaying wood Tossed and toppled by the wind, Shall be cut down by the ten-span sword, The blade that penetrates with sincerity; With Ihatsuchihiko presiding as chief, Let us banish the wickedness of this world.

Though the world be as dark as the jet-black night, Hinode-no-Kami appears, Hacks the eight-forked serpent to pieces, And causes all the hundreds of crooked spirits To yield to my kotodama. If there be any who come to turn their blades against us, Come, come, indeed come forward! There is no enemy that can oppose the sword Of the sincere heart that defends the honorable land. There is no blade that can oppose the deity Of the sincere heart that saves this world. Even though heaven and earth should change, Even though the great earth should turn into a sea, The power of sincerity saves the world. The savior deities have appeared: The Ikimitama of Hinode-no-Kami, The Sachimitama of Yasuyohiko, The Aramitama of Matetsuhiko— Manifesting as three souls, Becoming the deity Kamu-Susanoo-no-Kami,

Beginning with the head of the giant serpent, All of its kin down to its tail Shall be cut down; prepare yourselves! They shall be destroyed; prepare yourselves!"

...as he began to sing in a resounding voice, the Magatsukami, who possessed a fair countenance, was moved by this song, or so it seemed, for it instantly transformed into a white dragon, came windingly before the three deities, expressed its submission and welcome, and vanished while swinging the tip of its long tail forward, backward, left, and right. Looking around, not a trace remained of the palace that had been so exceedingly magnificent until then, and the swarm of tens and hundreds of giant serpents had disappeared nowhere to be seen; the surroundings had reverted entirely to the original hazy night.
