Chapter 19: The Vessel of Innocence [319]

第19章　無心の船〔319〕

§ 1

Untying its hawser, the ship pressed forward toward Ryūgū Island, which had become the sacred crown of Kuniharu-tachi-no-Ōkami. It was a perilous sea route dotted with sunken reefs, where at any moment the vessel might be wrecked. Praying to the two deities, Awanagi and Awanami, for safety at sea, Hinode-no-Kami once again sang a song of propagation:

"Kuniharu-tachi-no-Ōkami, Who is the Parent of the land, Appeared upon the lotus pedestal, Removed His sacred crown, And cast it toward the ocean. The divine spirit congealed into an island, Becoming Kanmurijima. By the blessings of Toyotama-hime-no-Kami-no-Mikoto And the noble Tamayori-hime-no-Mikoto, Who dwell enshrined forever upon Kanmurijima, May the sacred ship sail onward in peace. Across the ocean where raging waves roar, And through the world darker than pitch darkness, The morning and evening sun shine radiant; The propagator who opens the teachings Of the Great Way of heaven and earth, Formed by the congealing of the divine spirit, Has appeared as Hinode-no-Kami, Kanmurijima bearing blessings upon its crest.

Iwai-hime who worships heaven and earth, Pitying this harsh and sorrowful world With the divine heart of Omonagi-no-Kami, Grant us a safe passage To the Eternal Land, O Heavenly Deities, O Great Deity Kamu-izanagi-no-Ōkami, O Great Deity of the Moon, Notatehiko, Notate-no-Hime, and the Pivot Deity Who guards the earth of raw metal, And Watatsumi-no-Kami who guards the wave-paths, Dispel the darkness of the Eternal Land, Dispel the darkness of the Eternal Land."

Singing thus, the ship pressed forward. Tayorihiko said,

"Hey, Tokihiko, why do you not quickly apologize to Hinode-no-Kami? Why do you not bow and say, 'I will never drink sake for the rest of my life. I will only partake of sacred sake on celebratory occasions'? Yoshihiko, you must do the exact same thing."

Tokihiko replied, "I am already like a fire in a privy—utterly desperate and reckless. If I must give up the sake I love more than my wife or my food, who on earth would go to such an Eternal Land? Ah, I want to drink, I want to drink!"

Yoshihiko said, "Tokihiko, can you truly not reform your heart no matter what? You are hopeless."

Tokihiko retorted, "How many years do you think it has been since you yourself declared that you would reform? You got dead drunk at the banquet of the Yahiro-dono, fell into Takekuma's trap, and had your jewel stolen before anyone else. From then on, you kept saying, 'Ah, I must refrain from sake, I ruined myself with sake,' walking around pale-faced and timid every single day—but that lasted barely two or three months. Slowly your true colors emerged, and until just moments ago you were guzzling sake, getting properly drunk until you could hardly move your tongue, and yet you dare speak of having reformed! When your drunkenness wears off again, your throat will burn fiercely, and from inside that fat belly of yours, that splendid secondary guardian spirit will leap out and urge you, saying, 'Hey, Yoshihiko, it is all right [yoshi-hiko] to drink sake; it is all right [yoshi-hiko] to break the rules; drink and make merry, Yoshihiko; even if an inch ahead is pitch dark, it is all right [yoshi-hiko]; and if the moon comes out after the darkness, it is all the more right [yoshi-hiko]!' And so, your throat will burn, and since by nature you are foul-mouthed and gluttonous, you will say 'all right, all right' to everything and drink again. Reforming for just a fleeting moment accomplishes nothing at all. If you are going to reform, do it so that you never change for ten thousand ages to come.... I despise such weak-willed nonsense. In this lifetime, sake is the one thing of which I have no intention of repenting!"

§ 2

Tayorihiko: "Bah, you are hopeless fellows. Why do you like such pungent, acrid stuff? You too are companions of the privy-worms or the knotweed-eating insects that the passenger spoke of earlier."

Tokihiko: "Hey, hey, won't you give it a rest? Hinode-no-Kami will hear you."

Tayorihiko raised his voice,

"I am speaking so that it can be heard! Will you not stop, no matter what? Do you want to go to the place of Uralhiko no matter what?"

he yelled in a loud voice deliberately. Tokihiko and Yoshihiko covered both their ears and shrank back.

Tayorihiko: "What are you doing, holding your own ears?"

Tokihiko and Yoshihiko: "We are covering them so that Hinode-no-Kami cannot hear. It is because you are speaking in such a loud voice."

Tayorihiko: "Even if you do foolish things like covering your ears while stealing a bell, Hinode-no-Kami hears and understands perfectly well. Look, look, He is turning this way right now with a terrifying face! Confess that you will reform yourselves cleanly and completely!"

At this time, among the nearby passengers, one squeezed out a faint voice,

"Drink and make merry, for an inch ahead is darkness; After the darkness, the moon will rise. In any case, this world is about lust and sake; Without sake, in this world One cannot live merrily and delightfully. If living in tears is a whole life, Living in laughter is a whole life as well. Hanging a one-sho bottle around my neck, The wife who vowed we would be married Through two lifetimes or three is nothing to me; Sake is my true wife. Could there be a wife more beloved than sake? Could there be anything sweeter than sake? The fellow who declares he hates sake, Is he an odd, eccentric fool? Since we have been born into this world, Let us commit double suicide with sake and side-dishes; Even if we die and fall into hell, There is no demon that dislikes sake. If you show the bottle to that dreadful demon, With a smirk His fierce face will instantly break into a smile. This world, the other world, and the world of the gods Cannot be crossed without sake; There is no god who does not receive sacred wine. The fellow who declares he hates sake, Is he no god, but a wolf? A pale-faced god of poverty, A stingy god, a rotten god! What on earth is this Hinode-no-Kami? Without sake, the sun does not shine; Without side-dishes, the day does not break. Dokkoisho, dokkoisho! Dokkoisho, dokkoisho! Ponder it carefully as if looking into a tokkuri bottle; The offered cup spins round and round; The spinning floating world is lust and sake. Dokkoisho, dokkoisho!"

and thus there was one who began to sing in a low voice.

Tokihiko and Yoshihiko could bear it no longer, and letting out pathetic voices,

Tokihiko and Yoshihiko: "Ah, if we are to quit sake, quit we will; If we can leave it unconsumed, we will not drink it either. Even so, that the gods of heaven and earth Receive sacred sake is strange to me. If drinking sake is an evil deed, From now on, cleanly and completely, I shall reform myself and drink sacred sake! I shall reform myself and drink sacred sake!"

carried away, they began to sing thus, dancing frantically.

Tayorihiko's eyes widened, and swinging his iron fists again, he struck both of their faces with rapid, continuous blows until they could take no more.

The Ship of No-Mind carried these whispers and advanced steadily toward Ryūgū Island.
