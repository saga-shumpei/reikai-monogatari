Chapter 35: Sermon to a Wooden Idol [335]

第35章　木像に説教〔335〕

§ 1

Hinode-no-kami turned toward everyone and said,

"Gather here, all of you. Now I shall gouge out the eyes of this wooden statue and show them to you."

As soon as it heard these words, the wooden statue began to roll its eyeballs even more violently. In front of the wooden statue, Yashimawake, Haruhime, Natsuhime, and Akihime, along with Hinode-no-kami, Omonage, Iwaihime, and more than a dozen other beauties lined up in a row.

Hinode-no-kami turned toward the wooden statue and said,

"Now, everyone, I shall now chant for this wooden statue a norito that it hates. In addition, let us sing the propagation song that the wooden statue dislikes. Everyone, please sing along with me."

Saying this, he recited the Amatsu Norito, and when they all finished singing the propagation song in unison, the five wooden statues were shedding tears from both eyes like waterfalls.

Thereupon, Hinode-no-kami sang a song, calling it a sutra for the wooden statues, and had Iwaihime, along with Haruhime, Natsuhime, and Akihime, dance to the song.

Hinode-no-kami: "Where tigers and wolves roar and rage, At the border of the land of Kumaso, Renowned in the capital of the land of Hi, The god Kotobiwake Has strength to spare and is covered in lumps; Loving sake, he gets dead drunk, And after getting drunk, he breaks bowls, Kicks down doors, runs amok, And behaves with utmost violence; Drinking too much offered sake, His body has transformed into a wooden statue, Only glaring and rolling his eyeballs— How pitiful that is! The retribution for drinking So much of the beloved sake has swiftly arrived: What Kotobi hates more than anything, The hateful, hateful propagation song, And another hated great norito, When offered to him, tears welled in his eyes, And after weeping like a waterfall,

Surely something joyful will come. O god Kotobiwake, Renew your rough heart, Transform into the heart of God, And rule gently Over the people of the land of Hi, O Kotobiwake. We of the Ananai teachings Do not harbor hatred toward you. Tracing back to the origin, we are all Brethren born as children of God. Neither in heaven, on earth, nor in the world, Could there ever be strangers. The God who distinguishes between good and evil Has appeared openly. In everything in the human world, Rectifying sight and hearing through Naohi, O propagation missionaries of divine teachings, Soften your heart a little. If you remain stubborn, just like this, Your body will become like stone,

And you will not be able to move an inch. Discern the teachings of God, And return to a heart of sincerity, O god Kotobiwake, And attendants who follow him!"

When he sang thus, the wooden statues shed tears even more.

§ 2

Hinode-no-Kami raised his clear voice,

"Hito, futa, mi, yo, itsu, muyu, nana, ya, kokono, tari, momo, chi, yorozu,"

and recited the divine text, whereupon the wooden statue of Toratenwake suddenly became limp and returned to his former free body. Immediately, as Toratenwake leaped toward Hinode-no-Kami, raising his fist once more in the very instant he was about to strike a blow, his body stiffened once more and became like a wooden statue again.

Hinode-no-Kami: "In your house, there is a strange wooden statue. Does it rage at times? It is a wooden statue that frequently raises its arms."

Yashimawake: "Yes, yes, just a moment ago, it raised them repeatedly. It is truly an amusing doll."

Hinode-no-Kami once again sang the divine song of one, two, three, four. The wooden statue again returned to the freedom of its body just as before.

Toratenwake: "No, truly, I am humbled. Please forgive me. From this day forward, I will reform my heart."

Thus he wept aloud in the bitter tears of a man.

All present looked at one another's faces and grinned. What will become of Toratenwake hereafter?

現代日本語訳

日の出神は涼やかな声を張り上げて、

「ひと、ふた、み、よ、いつ、むゆ、なな、や、ここの、たり、もも、ち、よろづ」

と神文を唱えたところ、虎転別の木像はにわかにぐにゃぐにゃとなって元の自由な身体に戻った。たちまち虎転別は日の出神に向かって飛びついたまま、またしても拳骨を振り上げてポカンと打ちかかろうとする刹那、再び身体は硬直し、また木像のようになった。

日の出神「あなたの家には、妙な木像がありますね。時々暴れるのですか、よく腕を振り上げる木像ですね」

八島別「はいはい、先ほども先ほどとて、よく振り上げましたよ。実に面白い人形ですわ」

日の出神はまたしても一二三四の神歌を歌う。木像はまた元の通り身体が自由に戻った。

虎転別「いや、どうも恐れ入りました。どうか許してください。今日限り改心をいたします」

と男泣きに激しく泣いた。

一同は顔を見合わせてニヤリと笑う。虎転別の今後はどうなるのであろうか。
