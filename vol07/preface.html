Preface • The Eve of Setsubun, Divine Expeditions & Hermeneutics

霊界物語 第七巻 霊主体従 午の巻 序文・凡例・総説
By Master Onisaburo Deguchi (大正十一年瑞月)
The prefatory matter to Volume 7 of Reikai Monogatari (午の巻), dictated under extraordinary spiritual urgency in early February 1922:
1. Preface (序文): Dictated during the four days immediately preceding the Setsubun Festival, describing the cosmic onslaught of spiritual adversaries and the blossoming white plum of the Three Thousand Worlds.
2. Explanatory Notes (凡例): Outlining the central epic of Volume 7—the great expedition of Hinode-no-Kami from Mount Ōdaigahara through Ryūgū Island (Australia) to Tsukushi Island (Africa).
3. General Introduction (総説): A vital hermeneutical warning against rigid literalism and intellectual calculations of sacred dates, revealing the esoteric kotodama principles governing the prophetic dates of Oomoto.
Section 1

Preface • The Eve of Setsubun and the Plums of Three Thousand Worlds

第一節　序文（節分祭前夜の緊急口述と白梅の和歌）
English Translation

With the Setsubun Festival only four days away, my companions pressed upon me, demanding: 'By the eve when the famed ogres of Mount Ōe visit each home, you must without fail dictate through the Seventh Volume!' Like the prickly needles of holly and the pungent heads of sardines, they parted the mists of the foothills and rushed upon me wide-eyed, appearing for all the world like a legion of fierce demons. Left with no alternative, here at Ryūgū-kan—in the Kinsui-tei pavilion floating upon the water—I laid down my unsettled, five-foot mortal vessel just returned from the hot springs. Like the scratching rotation of a cracked record, or sake gurgling out heedlessly from an overturned flask, words poured endlessly from this Deguchi without restraint. I bow my head low in profound humility and shame at this clumsy output, and simply leave it at that.

Yet this is the eerie quiet before an earthquake: the Divine Realm announces an urgency that escalates moment by moment. In the realm of human thought, monstrous fiends and colossal serpents run rampantly amok with extreme fury, bringing the Three Realms to an undeniably perilous juncture. Even so, just as the ripening rice bows its head humbly toward the earth, we must remain vigilant; for if our guard is dropped, the steed of wild fortune will run mad, and wicked deities—eagerly biding their time—will strike at the great pine of Oomoto, seeking to reduce tens of thousands of years of divine governance to total ruin. Weathering the relentless rain of spears and the gales of venomous slander with the supple yield of a willow, we must now stand firm as the God of Rock. Though this deeply bound chronicle cannot be easily unraveled, we patiently await the season when the plum blossoms shall breathe forth the fragrant breath of spring. Amid the interwoven patterns of the brocade that heralds the 'Plum Blossoms of the Three Thousand Worlds,' I pray that you quiet your spirit and savor the profound Divine Will concealed within.

Recorded by Deguchi Onisaburo

The blossoms fall away, and where they bloomed, both day and night, the fruit swells with life upon the white plum tree standing in the garden.

Explanatory Notes • The Mission of Hinode-no-Kami Across Continents

第二節　凡例（大台ケ原より竜宮島・筑紫の島への宣伝使物語）
English Translation

1. This volume was completed within the four days between my return to Ayabe from Iwai Hot Springs and the Setsubun Festival. Its narrative recounts how Hinode-no-Kami, the august scion of the Great Deity Izanagi, journeyed from Mount Odaigahara across Australia—known as Ryūgū Island—unto the continent of Africa, known as the Island of Tsukushi. There, He proclaimed the Divine Way, brought salvation to the people of the world, and appointed guardian spirits to watch over the souls of each nation.

1. In short, as set forth in the General Remarks to this volume, this *Reikai Monogatari* can by no means be interpreted through mortal intellect alone. It is therefore best to simply read the text precisely as it is written, allowing each reader to receive and understand its truth according to the measure and readiness of their own soul.

An auspicious day in the first month of Taishō 11 [January 1922] Recorded by Onisaburo

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

一、本巻は、岩井温泉から綾部に帰還した後、節分祭までのわずか四日間で完成いたしました。その内容は、伊弉諾大神（イザナギノオオカミ）の御子である日の出神が、大台ヶ原から豪州（すなわち竜宮島）を経て、アフリカ大陸（すなわち筑紫の島）へと渡り、神の道を宣べ伝えて世の人々を救済するとともに、各国の国魂の守護職を任命される物語であります。

一、要するに、本巻の総説にもあります通り、この『霊界物語』を人間の知恵をもって解説することは到底不可能です。ですから、すべて書かれている文字のまま素直に拝読し、おのおのの身魂（みたま）の相応にしたがって受け止めていただければ、それで十分であると考えます。

大正十一年一月吉日 王仁 記す

General Introduction (Part 1) • The Divine Word Beyond Intellectual Calculation

第三節　総説（上：言霊と数理的解釈、経緯不二の真相）
English Translation

The revelations and teachings of the Divine Realm can by no means be fathomed through the mathematical and intellectual faculties of modern humanity. God is the Kotodama—the Word—which is the Way itself; divine messages must be understood primarily through the spiritual essence of the word. Regarding the dates 'March 3rd' and 'May 5th' found in the Divine Revelations (Shin'yu), people contaminated by modern materialism seem to engage in endless, heated debates. Yet, looking at Foundress Nao Deguchi’s original handwritten texts, where phrases like 'the Reconstruction of the World shall begin in the 30th year of Meiji' continued to be written even around the 33rd year of Meiji, it is abundantly clear that divine revelations do not conform to modern, literal interpretations.

Furthermore, the Foundress's holographs constitute the raw *Ofudesaki* (Automatic Writings), and it was the *Omoto Shin'yu* that was published after these writings were selected, edited, and arranged according to divine instruction. Some people call this the 'warp' (vertical) scripture, distinguishing it from the 'weft' (horizontal) scripture of the Henjo Nanshi (Onisaburo Deguchi), claiming they believe in the warp but reject the weft. However, if one wishes to understand the truth of the indivisible unity of the warp and weft, reading the Foundress’s raw manuscripts will make it plain. When published as written, the phrases appear quite fragmentary, and shallow believers often misperceive numerous passages as self-contradictory. All the writings penned by the Foundress from the 25th year of Meiji until the 8th day of the 9th lunar month of the 5th year of Taisho were the products of her period of ascetic training. That contradictions existed therein becomes evident upon reading the Foundress’s own script dated the 9th day of the 9th lunar month of that very year.

In that writing, she confessed that she had misunderstood the role and methods of the Henjo Nanshi until that day. To criticize and cavil based on these unrefined, developing writings—which were, in fact, edited and compiled into the *Shin'yu* by the Henjo Nanshi himself—is an error stemming from the critic's utter ignorance of the fundamental truth. Driven to an unavoidable impasse, I now disclose the unvarnished facts without reserve.

In essence, for twenty-five years—from the 25th year of Meiji to the 5th year of Taisho—the Foundress served divine affairs within the state of *Miken-Shinjitsu* (the realm prior to perceiving the absolute truth), pioneering the fundamental sacred groundwork toward the realization of the Divine Governance. I, the Henjo Nanshi, entered the faith in the 31st year of Meiji, but my sacred work under the stage of *Miken-Shinjitsu* lasted only two full years, until the 33rd year of Meiji; thereafter, my work belonged entirely to the stage of *Ken-Shinjitsu* (the realm of perceiving the absolute truth). Spiritually speaking, that I had advanced into the realization of absolute truth eighteen years ahead of the Foundress will become self-evident to anyone who reads her original handwritten scriptures with profound discernment.

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

神界からの示しと教えは、現代人のように数理的・論理的な思考力だけで推し測ることなど、到底できるものではない。神とは言霊であり、すなわち「道」そのものである。したがって、神の言葉を本質として解釈すべきなのだ。『大本神諭』にある「三月三日」「五月五日」などの数字についても、現代の物質主義に毒された人々は、大いに議論を戦わせているようである。しかし、開祖・出口なおの直筆の文章に「明治三十年で世の立替え云々」とありながら、明治三十三年頃になっても依然として同様に記されているのを見ても、神界の教示を現代風の理屈で解釈しても一致しないことは明白である。

また、開祖の直筆は、いわゆる「お筆先」であり、そのお筆先を神の示しに従って選び整え、公表したものが『大本神諭』である。これを開祖の「経（たて）の筆先」と呼び、変性女子（出口王仁三郎）の「緯（よこ）の筆先」と区別して、「経は信じるが、緯は信じない」などと言っている人々が方々に見受けられる。しかし、経と緯が表裏一体であり二つで一つであるという真相を知ろうと思うならば、開祖の直筆をそのまま読んでみれば明白となるはずだ。お筆先をありのままに公表したものは、極めて断片的に語句が並べられており、一見すると自己矛盾しているかのように、浅薄な信徒なら受け取ってしまう箇所が多々ある。また、開祖が明治二十五年から大正五年旧九月八日までに書かれたお筆先は、すべて御修行の時代の産物であり、そこに矛盾が含まれることは、開祖自筆の同年九月九日のお筆先を読めば明らかである。

そこには、変性女子のやり方について今日まで誤解していた、という意味のことが書かれている。そのような修行途上の未成品であるお筆先、しかも変性女子自らが取捨按配して編纂した『神諭』を見てあれこれ批判するのは、批判する側が根本の経緯（いきさつ）を弁えていないがゆえの過ちである。私はもはや看過できない状況に立ち至ったため、包み隠さず真実を打ち明けておく。

要するに開祖は、明治二十五年から大正五年までの足掛け二十五年間、「未見真実（いまだ真実を見極め得ぬ修業段階）」の境地において神務に奉仕し、神政成就のための基礎的な神業の先駆けを務められたのである。一方、変性女子は明治三十一年に入道したが、未見真実の神業は明治三十三年までの実質二年間だけであり、それ以後は「見真実（真実を見極めた覚醒）」の神業なのである。霊的な観点から申せば、開祖よりも十八年も先んじて見真実の神境に進んでいたということは、開祖直筆のお筆先を熟読すれば自ずと理解できるであろう。

Original Japanese (原文)

General Introduction (Part 2) • Esoteric Kotodama Interpretations of Sacred Dates

第四節　総説（下：三千年五十一年・九月八日の言霊的奥義）
English Translation

One must not be led astray by the interpretations of various sects and factions that are fixated merely on literal numbers—such as "three thousand and fifty-one years," "the third and fourth months," "the eighth and ninth months," "the third day of the first month," "the third day of the third month," or "the fifth day of the fifth month." In "fifty-one years," the number five signifies Izunomitama (the stern spirit of rectitude); ten represents Kami (Fire and Water, the divine principle); and one year denotes the inaugural year. In essence, it signifies that the first year in which the great afflictions and trials borne by the Great Deity over three thousand years (an eternity) became manifest, and the divine virtues began to unfold, was the first month of the twenty-fifth year of Meiji (1892). In "the eighth day of the ninth month," nine (ku) signifies consummation (tsukushi); month (tsuki) signifies Miroku; eight (ya) signifies opening (hiraku); and day/sun (hi) signifies radiant shining—all embodying the divine principle of "opening with the plum blossom and governing with the pine." "The ninth month" denotes governing with the pine, while "the eighth day" denotes opening with the plum. Furthermore, in "the third day of the first month" (Shogatsu Mikka), the character sho (正) is formed by combining "one" (一) and "stop/abide" (止); "month" signifies the light of the moon; "three" signifies Mizunomitama (the spirit of grace and prosperity); and "day" signifies radiance. Thus, the complete and unhindered manifestation of the divine virtue is termed "the third day of the first month." Hence, the interpretation of the Divine Revelations (Shinyu) is by no means an easy task. One must also read them with a clear understanding of the distinction between the original Ofudesaki and the published Shinyu.

Likewise, the *Reikai Monogatari* cannot be measured or judged by ordinary human intellect. Even if it be an exposition provided by the editors or the amanuenses, one must not take it as absolute dogma. To read the text simply, straightforwardly, and with an open heart just as it is written is the safest and most unerring path; hence, I append this note.

An auspicious day of an auspicious month, the eleventh year of Taisho [1922] Recorded by Onisaburo at the Zuishokaku Pavilion

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

「三千年と五十一年」「三月四月」「八月九月」「正月三日」「三月三日」「五月五日」などの数字にとらわれた、いわゆる○○派、○○派といった分派の説明に惑わされてはなりません。「五十一年」の「五」は厳霊（いづ）を意味し、「十」は「カミ（火水）」あるいは神を意味し、「一年」は始まりの年を意味します。要するに、三千年（無限の年月）にわたる大神の並々ならぬご苦難が表れ、その神徳が発揮され始める最初の年が、明治二十五年（一八九二年）の正月であるという意味なのです。「九月八日」の「九」は尽くす（ツクシ）であり、「月」は弥勒（ミロク）であり、「八」は開く、「日」は輝くという意味であって、「梅で開いて松で治める」という霊的意義を指します。「九月」とは松で治める意義であり、「八日」とは梅で開く意義なのです。また「正月三日」の「正」は「一」と「止」が合わさった意（一にとどまる・誠意）であり、「月」は月光、「三」は瑞霊（みづ）または栄え、「日」は輝くことであり、神徳が完全に発揮される様を「正月三日」と称するのです。したがって、神諭の解釈は安易にできるものではありません。また、『お筆先』と『神諭』の違いもわきまえた上で拝読していただかねばなりません。

この『霊界物語』もまた、人間の浅知恵によって推し量り、判断することは不可能です。たとえ編纂者や筆録者による解説であっても、それを絶対視してはなりません。ただひたすらに、ありのままの文章を素直に受け止めて拝読することこそが、もっとも安全な道でありますから、ここに一言書き添えておきます。

大正十一年　瑞月祥日 瑞祥閣にて　王仁　記す

Original Japanese (原文)

三千年と五十一年、三四月、八九月、正月三日、三月三日、五月五日なぞの数字に囚はれてゐた、いはゆる○○派、○○派の説明に誤られてはならぬ。五十一年の五は、厳の意味であり、十は【火水】、または神の意、一年は始めの年の意味である。要するに三千年（無限の年数）の間の、大神の御艱苦が出現して、神徳の発揮さるる最初の年が、明治二十五年正月からと云ふ意義である。九月八日の九はツクシであり、月はミロクであり、八は開く、日は輝くの意味で、梅で開いて松で治めるといふ意義である。九月とは松で治める意義、八日とは梅で開く意義である。また正月三日の正は、一と止と合した意味であり、月は月光、三は瑞または栄え、日は輝くことで、神徳の完全に発揮されることを、正月三日といふのである。故に神諭の解釈は容易にできない。また筆先と神諭の区別も弁へて読ンで貰はねばなりませぬ。

この霊界物語も、人智を以て判断することは出来ませぬ。たとへ編輯人、筆録者の解説といへども、肯定しては成りませぬ。ただ単に文句のまま、素直に読むのが、第一安全でありますから、一寸書加へておきます。

大正十一年瑞月祥日

於瑞祥閣　王仁識
