§ 1

"Longing to see Ryūgū, if you pass Kutsujima, the wind that sends you to Ryūgū blows."

So the boatman began to sing in an amusing melody. The sight of hundreds and thousands of white sails coming and going across the tranquil spring sea was a beautiful scene, just like a swarm of butterflies fluttering in a spring field.

The ship at last arrived at the shore of Kanmujima, where the Jewels of Ebb and Flow were hidden. Once again, all the people on the ship landed on this island without exception and visited the shrine of Ryūgū.

Hinode-no-kami ordered the two, Iwahime and Omonagi, to preach to the passengers, while he himself, accompanied by Tayorihiko, Tokihiko, and Yoshihiko, concealed himself deep within this mountain. Tayorihiko went ahead as a guide, and Hinode-no-kami followed behind him. Tokihiko and Yoshihiko followed fearfully and crestfallen, like sheep led to the slaughterhouse.

Yoshihiko: "Hey Toki-kō, because you were so stubborn, we have finally been dragged into such a mountain depth. There is no telling what will happen to us. Look at your chest; it is raising big waves and beating doki-doki—aren't you Toki-doki-hiko?"

Tokihiko: "Yoyoyo, Yoshi-kō, stop saying such things. I cannot walk even one more step. Ouch!"

Saying this, he fell flat onto the valley path. Tayorihiko looked back and said,

"Hey, won't you come quickly? What are you dawdling for? Even a slug moves faster. Deep in this mountain, fine, sweet sake wells up like a spring. We will let you drink and drink to your heart's content there, so come with joy. In all things, to look anew, listen anew, and speak anew through Kamunaobi and Ōnaobi is the teaching of the Anai religion. He says he will cause you to rejoice and let you reform. There is nothing to fear. It is sake, it is sake! Won't you come quickly?"

The moment Tokihiko heard it was sake, he suddenly regained his vigor and said,

"Oh, oh, how wonderful! Is that true?"

Saying this, he began to run, pressing steadily forward until he reached the base of a great boulder towering in the deep mountain valley. Seeing the sake with an indescribably fragrant scent welling up naturally, Tokihiko twitched his nose, made gulping sounds in his throat, and was about to scoop it up and drink it immediately, but Tayorihiko blocked him, saying,

"Wait, wait! If you drink this sake recklessly, you will lose your life. It must be after making a request to the Kami."

Tokihiko and Yoshihiko: "Even if we die, it does not matter, so let us drink quickly! We can bear it no longer, bear it no longer!"

When Hinode-no-kami glared sharply at Tokihiko's face, Tokihiko shrank in fear at the glare and prostrated himself.

Tayorihiko said, "This is the place called the Waterfall of Sake. Because it is sake brewed by Princess Otohime of Ryūgū, it must come after chanting a norito prayer and performing purification. Come, you become the Harahido, and I shall be the Saishu. Yoshihiko, you are in charge of the sacred food offerings."

"In charge of offerings? What are we to offer?"

"You are to offer the souls that you possess."

Yoshihiko and Tokihiko at once said,

"Even if you say to offer jewels, were not the jewels we painstakingly picked up taken by Takekuma?"

Tayorihiko said,

"If it is about those jewels, mine were taken as well. It is a matter of offering your souls. It means to cleanly and completely offer up the souls of the secondary guardian spirits that make you say you want to drink, want to drink sake."

Tokihiko said, "Is that soul which always groans and rises up from around beneath the navel whenever I see sake called a secondary guardian spirit? If so, I have about four of them."

Yoshihiko said, "I also have about two."

Tayorihiko said, "Offer the four souls that Tokihiko possesses and the two souls that Yoshihiko possesses. All those secondary guardian souls drink sake and ruin your souls and bodies. Offer them cleanly and completely."

Tokihiko said, "Even though you say to offer them, even if they come up to my throat, they do not come out into my mouth. Even if I cut open my belly they will not come out, so what should I do? Ah, what sweet-looking sake; I want to drink, I want to drink."

Hinode-no-kami said, "Will you not quickly begin the ritual?"

"Yes, yes, we will start now. We will briefly wash our hands, perform the water purification, and rinse our mouths. Please wait."

Tayorihiko said, "You are crafty fellows. Saying you will rinse your mouths, you are scheming to drink the sake; make haste, won't you?"

"Yes, yes, for friendship's sake, you might overlook it just a little; it would surely do no harm."

Thus Yoshihiko whispered, leaning his mouth toward Tokihiko's ear. In this manner the ritual ended without mishap, but the essential offerings, the souls, would by no means emerge.

Tokihiko said, "Now, the ritual is finished. Shall we receive the sacred sake as promised?"

Tayorihiko said, "Not yet, wait, wait; do as I say and bring forth the souls."

『そんなら好し』

と田依彦は芳彦の縛を解く。芳彦は目を、ギロギロさせながら恨めしさうに酒の滝壺を眺め居る。田依彦はまた杓に酒を汲ンで今度は時彦の鼻の先に突出す。時彦は舌を出す。田依彦は、

『オツトドツコイそれやならぬ』

と杓を後へ引く。また杓を出す。舌を出す。また後へ引くと俄に「クワツクワツクワツ」と三声叫びしその途端に咽から焼石三箇一度に飛び出し、酒の滝壺の中に『ジユンジユンジユン』と音をさせて落ち込みにける。田依彦は、

『サア時彦御祭は済ンだ。御供物も是れで終ひだ。何ぼなと酒を飲め』

と杓に酒を汲ンで口の辺へ持つて行く。時彦は目を塞ぎ口を閉ぢ、首を左右に振つて、

『もうもう酒は匂ひを利くのも嫌だ。酒は嫌ひだ、勘忍々々』

と頭を左右に振る。日の出神は時彦の縛を解きけるが、是れぎり二人は酒の匂ひを嗅ぐのも嫌になりたりける。ここに時彦、芳彦は竜宮島の宮の造営を命ぜられ、「久久神、久木神」といふ名を貰つて住家を造る役目となりぬ。
