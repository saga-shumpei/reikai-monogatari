Chapter 18: The Palace of the Ocean Realm [318]

第18章　海原の宮〔318〕

§ 1

The ship finally arrived at the port of Kutsujima in New Zealand. Landing on this island was forbidden to people. The ship called at the port merely to store in the vessel the drinking water welling forth from this island.

Hinode-no-kami, chanting the Amatsu-norito and singing a propagation hymn, landed accompanied by two missionaries, visited the shrine where Unabarahiko-no-kami is enshrined, prayed for safety on the sea, and due to the conditions of wind and waves, it was decided that they would take refuge on this island for about a month.

The people aboard the ship suffered from boredom, and once again began telling strange tales to one another and boasting of their native lands.

Among them, two or three dark-skinned large men, along with three men—a tall man with an elongated face and wide, staring eyes, and a somewhat short man with a gaunt face—were absorbed in conversation while sipping sake little by little.

"Hey, Tokihiko, if you drink too much sake, isn't Hinode-no-kami, whom we encountered at Ōdaigahara, on board? If he discovers you, it will be terrible."

"Yoshihiko, never mind that. Just now, didn't Hinode-no-kami go up into the mountain? In the meantime, apply yourself and drink, as I drink too. Hey, Tayorihiko! Don't just glare with such big eyes; drink, drink!"

"If you break the rules, I will not stand for it. Even though I hate even smelling sake, do you intend to trouble me from both sides and drink sake again? This time for certain I will report you to Hinode-no-kami-sama. Across this vast ocean, in such cramped quarters, just because you want to drink sake, you do not even announce your names and are always hanging your heads to hide your faces—if it were only you, that would be fine, but how can I endure being made to hang my head as well?"

Tokihiko: "Doesn't it make one furious? That young man and woman jumping into the sea and climbing back up, and in the end tricking the good-natured missionary into letting them become husband and wife—aren't they making fools of us? The reason we travel far across these waves to the Tokoyo is to become henchmen of Uraruhiko-sama and have him give us sweet sake to drink our fill. The missionaries or whatever they are make a fuss, saying that Kuni-no-Mihashira-no-kami fled to the Land of Ne or somewhere, but what they call the Land of Ne or the Land of Yomi refers to the Land of Tokoyo. Surely He is immersed in sake and indulging in the utmost luxury of ponds of wine and forests of meat. Isn't it because we too want to encounter such ponds of wine and forests of meat that we abandon our dear wives and go, waah!"

§ 2

Tayorihiko opened his round eyes wide, pursed his lips, and said,

"You are truly an outrageous fellow. Saying that Wakazakurahime no Mikoto has appeared in the Tokoyo no Kuni to re-establish divine governance, and that regarding this, she declares she will employ the former retainers of the Dragon Palace just as before, so let us go as quickly as possible—was all a lie to lure me out to this place, was it not?"

Tokihiko said, "Have you only now realized it was a lie? What a helpless fellow you are. That is why everyone calls you 'Helpless Hiko.' To have your dependable wife unmanned by Tamahiko whose jewel had been taken, and on top of that, to have your soul taken by me, this Tokihiko whose jewel was taken—what a pathetic state! Pitiable though it is, your innate spirit-jewel is rotten. Yet no matter how rotten it may be, it cannot be helped, for ours is an inescapable bond, having to live together like this."

Tayorihiko,

"What, talking nonsense!"

—no sooner had he said this than he clenched his fist and struck Tokihiko squarely on the side of his face.

The sake had taken hold of Tokihiko; his tongue was tangled, and his legs were tottering. Though his mouth alone was nimble, he had not an inch of control over his body. The people aboard the ship,

"A fight! A fight!"

—stood up all at once, watching. Singing the song of propagation, the God of Sunrise left behind the Palace of the Ocean God and returned toward this ship.

Tokihiko and Yoshihiko shrank like snails and prostrated themselves flat at the bottom of the ship. Tayorihiko rose to his feet with a start, and said,

"Pardon me, O God of Sunrise, I am Tayorihiko. Since parting with you at Ōdaigahara, Mametora received the splendid name of Kukunochi and has been building houses together with Ōyabiko. Putting on immense airs, he treats us like servants, which we cannot bear, so at last I have followed after you. Please, by all means, bestow a fine name upon me as well. For even if I remain Tayorihiko, it is of no avail if I have no one to rely on,"

—thus he pleaded, rolling his eyes round and round.

"Ah, is that so? If that is true, you are an admirable man. But are not the two prostrating themselves over there Tokihiko and Yoshihiko? There is a strong smell of sake. Whose sake bottle is that?"

"Yes, this is to-to-to-to-to, to-to-to-to, totally beyond my comprehension now. S-sometimes such things happen."

"Is that not Yoshihiko's?"

"Yes, yes, I think Tayorihiko is an o-o-outsider. He is d-drunk."

"What is this, have you suddenly developed a stutter?"

"Yes, i-i-it cannot be helped. Because Toki and Yoshi would not listen to what I said, I along with them—no, indeed, I have no words to explain it. Please, by Kamunaohi, overlook and unhear it. However, this fellow did not have warmed sake, but has been drinking cold sake. Being beside them, I thought I might be discovered by you, and was chilled and terrified. Since I am naturally averse to alcohol, I have not drunk a single drop. Whatever may become of Tokihiko and Yoshihiko, please forgive me alone, Hinode-no-Kami."

"Do not speak foolishness; is it well if only you are saved?"

"No, if possible, I would like you, Toki, and Yoshi to be saved as well. Hey, Toki, Yoshi, raise your faces. It is Hinode-no-Kami. Just experience having fire strike from your eyes once, and both the sake and drunkenness will wear off. What did you just spout? You said, did you not, that the deity Kuni-no-Mihashira went to the land of Tokoyo to drink sake?"

Hinode-no-Kami, while smiling,

"Enough of that. Now the ship departs. Shall I thoroughly squeeze the oil out of you inside the ship?"

The ship once again hauled up its anchor, hoisted its sails to the favorable wind, and advanced toward Ryūgū Island.
