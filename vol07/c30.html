Chapter 30: Chieftain of the Tengu [330]

第30章　天狗の親玉〔330〕

§ 1

Though treading the dark valley, Bright in heart is the missionary; Though passing along narrow mountain paths, Broad in heart is the missionary; Going forth to clear the darkness of eternal night, The missionary of the Hinode-no-Kami; Breathing away and purifying the gathering clouds that block all four quarters, The missionary; Mowing down the successive sorrows, The missionary of Tsuranagihiko; Going forth to open the land of Yashima, The missionary of Yashimawake; As the capital where they shall lodge draws near, With heart also stirred with courage is the missionary.

Singing thus, he came down the valley path. At the bottom of the valley, there were several dozen laborers absorbed in carrying stones.

Den said, 'Hey, Hachi, your boss has arrived. Look, right there he goes passing by with a smug face, humming a tune, and saying "missionary, missionary." Go quickly and pay your respects to him. You should not be carrying stones now.'

Hachi said, 'Shut your mouth. He has gone properly to the capital of the land of Hi, and together with the missionary Yashimawake, he is craning his neck like a crane, waiting for my appearance, counting the days on his fingers, wondering if Hachi is coming, if I am not coming yet. When this work is finished, I will return home. Then, bringing a splendid carriage, they will say, "Come, Master Hachi, the missionary is awaiting you. If Master Hachi does not come, the missionary cannot bear it at all. Without Master Hachi, the night will not dawn and the sun will not set. Even if there are days when no bird sings, if there were a day when Master Hachi's face could not be seen, there would be no point in living in this world."'

Den said, 'Idiot, what are you blathering about? Talking fondly of your woman, you are smitten with your hag Osen—who is like a slut, pitch-black Kurokuke, a charcoal ball, and the chieftain of crows—and you are mistaking Osen for the missionary! Hey, pull yourself together!'

And with a crack, he slapped his cheek.

Hachi said, 'A fight, is it? All right, come on!'

So saying, while tying a twisted headband, he spat on his palms and stamped his feet like a wrestler. Just as both were about to grapple with each other,

'Hold!'

someone shouted in a thunderous voice. At this kotodama, Den and Hachi were startled, and involuntarily sank down flat onto the valley floor.

Hachi said, 'Hey, Den, won't you stand up? Didn't you say we were going to fight? Stand up, stand up!'

Den said, 'Calm and composed, unmoved as a crag. Surely a great man must possess this fortitude.'

Hachi said, 'What are you spouting? You have lost your nerve and cannot move an inch, can you? Well, have you apologized, you cowardly fellow?'

と、歌いながら谷道を下りて来る。谷底には石運びに余念のない数十人の人夫がいた。

伝「おい八公、貴様の親分が来たぜ。それ、今そこを得意そうな顔をして鼻歌を歌い、宣伝使、宣伝使とおっしゃってお通りになるだろう。早く行って拝んで来い。石運びどころではないだろう」

八「やかましく言うな。ちゃんと肥の国の都へ行って、八島別の宣伝使と一緒に俺のお出ましを、鶴のように首を長くして、八は来ないか、まだ来ないかと指折り数えて時の経つのを待っていてくださるのだ。この仕事が済んだら、俺は家へ帰る。そうすると、立派な乗り物を持って、『さあ八様、宣伝使がお待ち受けでございます。八様に来てもらわないと宣伝使もまったくやりきれない。八様でなければ夜も明けず、日も暮れない。鳥が鳴かない日があっても、八様の顔が見えない日があったら、この世に生きている甲斐がない』とおっしゃるのだ」

伝「馬鹿、何を抜かしているのだ。惚気て、貴様はあばずれ女の、真っ黒けの黒助の、炭団玉の烏の親分のような女房のおせんに惚気て、おせんと宣伝使とを取り違えたりしているのだ、おいしっかりしないか」

とぽかんと横面を殴りつける。

八「喧嘩か、よし来い」

とねじり鉢巻きをしながら、手のひらに唾をして、四股を踏む。今や両方から掴み合おうとする時、

「待てっ」

と大喝する者がいた。この言霊に、伝と八はびっくりして、思わず谷底へぺたりとへたり込んだ。

八「おい伝公、立たないか。喧嘩しようと言ったではないか。立て、立て」

伝「泰然自若、動かないこと巌の如しだ。大丈夫たるもの、まさにこの気概がなくてはならないのだ」

八「何を抜かしているのだ。腰を抜かして、びくとも動けないのだろう。どうだ謝ったか、腰抜け野郎め」

§ 2

Den: "What happened just now was a joke. Please carry me on your back and take me home, for my hips will not support me. It is because he roared in such a loud voice, as if thunder had struck."

Hachi: "That is the Tengu. Since your nose is always held high, the Tengu must have tried to break it."

Stroking his nose to check,

Den: "Even so, my nose is still there. The nose is fine, but it seems the bone in my hip is broken. That Tengu fellow made a mistake."

From above,

Hinodegami: "Hey, you many people there, carrying so many stones like this, what on earth are you doing?"

Two or three people said in unison,

"This is because Lord Yashimabetsu has come to the capital of the Land of Hi to build a castle, and for that purpose we are having a terrible time from morning till night. A troublesome fellow has descended to the Land of Hi, and it is truly unbearable."

Hinodegami: "Yeah, is that so, now I understand. It must be troublesome, but would you guide me to that capital of the Land of Hi?"

Two or three people: "Yes, yes, we shall guide you. Looking at you, you are wearing a straw raincoat and woven hat, presenting a shabby appearance, but somehow your words possess an indescribable power. With the loud voice you roared just now, Den lost the use of his legs, and all of us were frightened out of our wits. You must be the chief of the Tengu. Since that Yashimabetsu fellow works us so cruelly, please make your loud voice even louder, shout at Yashimabetsu, and make him lose the use of his legs once and for all. While such a fellow is around, the people of the Land of Hi cannot live comfortably and at ease. Come, come, we will guide you."

So saying, two or three black men took the lead and guided him along the slope.

伝「さっきのは冗談だ。どうぞ俺をおぶって帰ってくれ、腰が立たないのだ。あまりにも大きな声で雷が落ちたように怒鳴るものだからな」

八「それは天狗さまだよ。貴様はいつも鼻が高いから、天狗さまが鼻を折ってやろうとなさったのだ」

鼻を撫でてみて、

伝「それでもまだ鼻はあるぞ。鼻は大丈夫だが、腰の骨が折れたらしい。天狗のやつめ、勘違いしやがったな」

上の方から、

日出神「おい、そこにいる多くの人々、このようにたくさんの石を運んで、一体何をするのか」

二、三人が口を揃えて、

「これはな、八島別さまが肥の国の都にお出ましになって、城をお築きになるそのために、我々は朝から晩までひどい目に遭っているのだ。困ったやつが肥の国へ天降って来やがって、本当にたまらないよ」

日出神「うん、そうか、それで分かった。ご苦労だが、その肥の国の都へ案内してくれないか」

二、三人「はいはい、ご案内申し上げます。貴方は見れば蓑笠をお召しになって、みすぼらしいお姿をしておられるが、どことなく貴方のお言葉には何とも言えない力がある。さっき轟かせなさった大声で、伝公は腰を抜かすし、我々一同は肝を潰してしまいました。天狗さまの親方だろう。一度あの八島別のやつ、あまりに我々を酷く使うものだから、貴方様のその大きな声をもっともっと大きくして、八島別を怒鳴りつけて腰を抜かしてやってください。あんなやつがいては、肥の国の人民も気楽に遊んで暮らすことはできやしない。さあさあ、ご案内いたします」

と、先に立って坂道を二、三人の黒い男が案内する。
