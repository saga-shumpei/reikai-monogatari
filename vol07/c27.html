第27章　蓄音器〔327〕

§ 1

Kojimawake, gesturing that he could not comprehend the voice coming from inside this cavern, in contrast to his previous vigor, squeezed out a voice like an insect's, saying,

"Who on earth of the gods are You that speak in such a manner? Declare Your divine name!"

From inside the cavern, in a loud voice once again:

"Ahahahaha! No, no, you cannot yet reform. Ohohohoho! What a terribly obstinate fellow; ufufufu, do not be off your guard, for this world is a den of demons; ehehehehe, propagandist who walks about strutting proudly, take good heed from the top of your head to the soles of your feet. Ihihihihi, strutting proudly, propagandist of Kojimawake of Ryūgū; kakaka, never make dried bonito of God; kokoko, blind fools of Kojimawake; kukuku, propagandist of a rotten soul, Kojimawake who walks about putting a lid on foul-smelling things; kekeke, God's plan which cannot be fathomed, you had better savor it well; kikiki, mad-like Kojimawake, you had better reform while the true God warns you; sasasa, cleanly shake off your delusion, Kojimawake, do not join the ranks of Ame-no-Sagume; sososo, if you think it a loss, you will not even lift a finger; if you think it your own gain, you will lay hands even on cow bones, greedy Kojimawake; sususu, propagandist who discriminates among people with likes and dislikes. Sesese, making excuses with sweet words that the back cannot be traded for the belly, the very ringleader of weak will and irresolute action; shishishi, propagandist who walks about boasting of things he does not know as if he knew them!"

Kojimawake cringed and said,

"I do not know which God You are, but I will reform now, so please forgive me with this."

"Tatata!"

"That is already plenty! Please grant me pardon; it cuts me to the bone!"

From inside the hole:

"It is not plenty, there is still more to come; unless I teach you to the full extent of the seventy-five sounds, you will not awaken: tototo, propagandist with a bewildered face, flustered Kojimawake, you had better ponder thoroughly; you can by no means escape the state of affairs here; shall I send you to the land of Tokoyo? Your reformation is utterly uncertain; tsutsutsu, Kojimawake with a crestfallen face as if having had his pot stolen on a moonlit night, strayed propagandist who is slippery and elusive; tetete, propagandist who walks about traveling through the world with a triumphant face; chichichi, despite having neither wisdom nor strength, propagandist who boasts of his power, presuming upon a tiny achievement. Nanana, pride of a long time, claiming distress and hardship for the sake of the Path, and for the sake of salvation—what nonsense! How pitiful; when I think of it, when I think of it, tears spill forth, Kojimawake; nonono, propagandist who does not comprehend God's heart which spits blood from the throat; nununu, do not slip, take care, Kojimawake; nenene, Kojimawake who deceives this world pretending to be zealous; ninini, Kojimawake who is scolded by Nitten-sama and is even disabled in his eyes!"

§ 4

From within the cavern:

"Ji-ji-ji-ji, won't you listen closely? Are you impatient, grandson of an earthquake? Bi-bi-bi-bi, I will not let you even twitch with poverty. Bu-bu-bu-bu, sour-faced missionary, Kojimawake whose military fortune has run out. Be-be-be-be, cleaning latrines suits your nature, loose-bottomed missionary. Bo-bo-bo-bo, dazed face and dropping tears. Pa-pa-pa-pa, you had better take a puff of smoke."

"Yes, yes, thank you; good grief, ah, is it finally over with this? I know not whether Magatsu or what is delivering this hard sermon long as a rock, but it truly gave me a terrible time and squeezed plenty of oil out of me. But am I perhaps dreaming? Let me pinch my cheek for a moment. Ah, it really hurts; what in the world is going on in front of this rock? They say that silence is better than speech, but this rock is truly a bizarre cavern, just like a natural, cheap phonograph. Hey, Master Phonograph, hey, I am an openly acknowledged missionary before the whole world; even I can say anything like what you say. Say as much as you like!"

"Pi-pi-pi-pi!"

"Yo, what is this, is there a brown-eared bulbul here? Hey, valley-crossing bulbul, chirp 'pi-pi' as much as you like. I am an openly acknowledged missionary before the whole world! When I kept silent and listened to you, you got carried away and lined up nearly all seventy-five sounds of kotodama, and in the end you were at your wits' end—what a disgraceful sight! Like someone with diarrhea going to the privy, 'pi-pi-pi-pi'—look, what a state that is! The hole is dumbfounded, and the privy dances!"

At this moment, the cavern let out a great roar and began to groan as if a hundred thunderclaps were crashing at once; Kojimawake was so terrified that his heart broke and his soul seemed to vanish, and he collapsed flat onto the earth.
