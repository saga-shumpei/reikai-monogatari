Chapter 44: Fukubenomo [344]

第44章　福辺面〔344〕

§ 1

At this unexpected and sudden song of propagation, everyone was astonished and looked around restlessly, but as no further voice was heard, the crowd began to chatter among themselves once again.

"What could that voice just now have been? It was exceedingly refreshing, powerful, solemn and inviolable, majestic, splendid, and rigorous..."

"Hey, you, repeating the same words over and over, you make no sense at all. Rigorous, majestic—what are you talking about? You must be one of Katoribake's companions, too."

"Shut up! As if a senseless fellow like you could ever understand!"

"Hey, Katoribake is running away, he is running away! Everyone, watch out!"

Startled by this voice, Katoribake looked back, made a gesture as if slapping a mosquito that had landed, slapped his own face sharply, placed his right hand lightly near his mouth like a cat, wiped away his falling tears with his left hand, and spoke in a faint voice like the whining of a mosquito:

"Gentlemen, please forgive me. In return, I will give you this sake to drink."

"What a fool! We are negotiating with you precisely because sake is forbidden! We are all human beings. In the entire universe, there is no one who dislikes sake, [censored], and women. Even so, we abide by the teachings of Ananai-kyō and endure without drinking the sake we love. We merely partake of the sacred sake of the deities. Even if you hang gourds so heavily from your waist that you look like a monster of Awashima-sama, trying to disrupt the world with alcohol, in this land of Tsukushi, not a single person will be corrupted by the likes of you!"

"Th-th-that is quite true; we feel the same about the taste of sake. Could there ever be anyone in this wide world who dislikes sake? Sake is indeed the parent of life!"

"What an obstinate fellow! This scoundrel has let his soul rot with alcohol. He understands nothing, no matter what is said. Hey, Katoribake, empty all those gourds right here in front of me!"

Tilting his ear slightly toward the left, Katoribake slapped his forehead sharply with his right hand and said:

"You just spoke of warming the sake, but one cannot put a gourd over the fire to warm it; what kind of gourd could this possibly be?"

"Hey, everyone, this fellow is completely drunk, making sport of us as his drinking snacks, and smugly toying with us! We thought we were tormenting Katoribake, but instead he is rambling on drunkenly and tormenting us! Isn't this completely absurd? Shall I give his head a good crack with this staff?"

「そ、そ、それはごもっとも、思いは同じ酒の味、酒の嫌いな人間が、広い世界にあるものか、酒は命の親であるぞよ」

「しぶとい奴だ、こいつは酒に魂を腐らせやがっている。何を言っても分からない奴だな。おい蚊取別、俺の前でその瓢箪をみな空にしてしまえ」

蚊取別は耳を少し左のほうに傾けながら、右手で額をぴしゃりと叩き、

「あなたはいま燗取り酒とおっしゃったが、瓢箪を火にかけて燗をするわけにも行かず、どうした拍子の瓢箪やら」

「おいおい皆の者、こいつは酒に酔い潰れて俺たちを肴にし、いい気になって弄んでいるのだよ。俺たちが蚊取別をいじめたつもりで、かえって蚊取別にくだを巻かれていじめられているのだよ。馬鹿馬鹿しいではないか。ひとつ頭をこの杖でかんとり別とやってやろうか」

§ 2

The crowd said, "Very well, very well,"

and from all directions, with broken sticks, they were about to strike, aiming at Katoriwake's head, when Katoriwake clutched the gourd at his waist in both hands, placed it atop his head, and braced himself as if to say, "Strike if you will."

The crowd fell upon him, not to be outdone. Struck, the gourd broke, and the sake splattered in all directions. Katoriwake was showered with sake from his head, suddenly turning into the newborn Buddha.

At this moment, piercing the darkness, a propagation song was heard once again:

"Born in the Eternal Realm, It is the sacred wine Brewed by the god Sukunahikona; Drink a little, in moderation. Drinking the sacred wine is good, Yet be not swallowed by the wine, you multitudes! Without drinking the sake you wish to drink, If you strain yourselves, someday again Your hearts will grow rough in dark clouds, And you must needs drink sake. After offering it to the gods, Do not merely drink the sacred wine, but receive it with reverence; Do not merely drink the sacred wine, but receive it with reverence."

Thus a clear, cool voice was heard. Katoriwake quickly caught this voice in his ears,

"Hey, all of you fellows, did you hear that song just now? You thought you had the numbers and made a fool of me, and until now, faced with such odds, I told myself to yield to the strong, holding back my rising stomach and suppressing my short temper; but no, no, no, I will tolerate it no longer! Did you hear that song just now? It sounded like 'Do not drink the sacred wine, receive it.' Drinking and receiving are the same thing. Now, since the missionary of Uralhiko, my ally, has appeared, it is truly like giving an iron club to an oni! Talk as much as you like, strike my head, strike it and regret it later!"

snorting roughly, he swung both his arms and began to boast arrogantly.

"Hey, hey, that bald gourd-face has suddenly grown frisky. If we dawdle, his kind will come out of the dark, and there is no telling what they will do. Run, run!"

Startled by this voice, the crowd scrambled over one another and fled, scattering into the darkness.

Katoriwake blocked the way in front of the bonfire all by himself, raised a great voice, and shouted,

"Who do you think I am? The foremost retainer of the peerlessly mighty Miyamahiko, who holds his mansion on Mount Kijō—"

in a slightly lower voice,

"the retainer of that retainer's another retainer's yet another retainer, Katoriwake, is none other than myself! Useless swarm of vermin, you have vanished like clouds and mist!"

thus he boasted loudly. Once again, from within the darkness,

"The deity manifests outwardly, Setting apart good and evil. The twisted spirit Katoriwake, Sneaking out of Mount Kijō, Was swept away as far as the land of Tsukushi, Exposing his gourd-face, Slapped on the head and severely pressed by strangers in another province; What a state that is! The missionary of the Teachings of 3-5, Omonagihiko, is none other than I! Omonagihiko, is none other than I!"

thus a voice called out in a great shout, mingling with song.

Hearing this voice, Katoriwake suddenly sobered up, clutched his head, and crouched down on the spot.

「家来のその家来の、もう一つの家来のまた家来、蚊取別とは我のことであるぞ。役にも立たない雲虫奴ら、雲を霞と逃げ失せたな」

と大声で歌を交えて叫び立てた。

蚊取別はこの声を聞いてにわかに酔いも醒め、頭を抱えてその場にうずくまってしまった。
