Chapter 14: Surging Billows [314]

第14章　怒濤澎湃〔314〕

§ 1

Funado-no-Kami raised the anchor with a rattling sound. Hoisting the true sail to catch the east wind that was blowing at that time and letting it swell with wind, she glided quietly over the waves across the boundless ocean while making the bottom of the ship beat like a drum. The sun set at the edge of the western sea, coloring the water's surface in gold. Though the sun hid in the sea, illuminated by the light of the moon rising from the edge of the eastern mountains, his name was the shining Hinode-no-Kami. Iwaihime who blesses the happiness of this world, Menagihiko of the continuous waves, the sky sparkled sparsely with starlight across its entire expanse, the moonlight floated and sank among the waves, and upon the waves of eternal spring, the ship pressed forward night and day. On this ship, beginning with the three missionaries, many people from various lands indulged in casual conversation, dispelling and comforting the gloom of the sea voyage. From one side of the ship, a large man with long hair mixed with white thrust out his reddish-black face and said,

"Hey, boatman, what on earth is this place?"

"Here? This place is called the sea."

"The sea is obvious. What sea is it?"

"Here? This is the Sea of Chichi."

"Humph, I understand. Is it the sea of 'Where and how are my birth father and mother, at the verge of death I want to see their faces and meet them just once before I die'?"

"I know not who you are, but do not utter such ominous things. Just an inch below is a water hell."

"You are the one who speaks ominous things; I hate such things as a water hell. When I think that such a dreadful place, like the one explored by Mizu-no-Mitama, exists down at the bottom of this sea, even sailing becomes repulsive to me."

At this time, from one side of the ship, the clear, cool voice of a woman singing was heard.

"The benevolence of a father, higher than mountains, The benevolence of a mother, deeper than the sea; Forgetting the benevolence of mountain and sea, Crossing across this ocean, Hiding myself in the land of Tokoyo; Though I have come all the way here In order to meet the man I long for, Thoroughly weary of the long sea journey, Even the means to return To the land from which I came are lost upon the waves; Should I abandon my parents and follow The man I long for, or should I abandon The husband I long for, and return To the side of the parents of deep blessings Like mountains and seas, and serve them With all my heart? At the boundary between good and evil, Knowing neither how to advance nor able to retreat, Upon this agonizing sea, Though the moon shines in the sky,

I am lost in troubled thought as the evening bell tolls, And with the yearning of longing for my homeland, The sky of my heart also becomes entirely overcast. Alas, what shall I do? Shall I cast myself into the deep sea-way of a thousand fathoms, And from the bottom of my heart apologize To the gods of heaven and earth For this sin of disobeying my parents?"

「おい船頭、ここは一体なんという所だ」

「ここかい、ここは海という所だよ」

「海に決まっているわい。なんという海だ」

「ここかい、ここは乳の海だ」

「ふん、分かった。生みの父上母さまはどこでどうしていらっしゃるのやら、命の瀬戸際にただひと目、会って死にたい顔を見たい、という海かい」

「どこの奴か知らないが、縁起の悪いことを言うな。一寸下は水地獄だぞ」

「貴様こそ縁起の悪いことを言う、水地獄なんて俺は大嫌いだ。瑞の霊が探検してきたような恐ろしい所が、この海の底の方にあるかと思えば、船乗りも嫌になってしまうわ」

このとき船の一方より涼しい女の歌う声が聞こえてきた。

妾は思案に暮れの鐘 故郷を思う恋しさの 心の空も掻き曇る ああ如何にせん、千尋の 深き海路に身を投げて 親に背きしこの罪を 天と地との神々に 心の底より謝罪せん」

『おい船頭、ここは一体なんといふ処だ』

『フン分つた。生の父上母さまは何処に如何して御座るやら、生命の際に唯一目、会うて死にたい顔見たい、といふ海かい』

『何処の奴か知らぬが縁起の悪い事を吐かすない。一寸下は水地獄だぞよ』

『山より高き父の恩 海より深き母の恩 山と海との恩忘れ この海原を打ち渡り 常世の国に身を隠す 恋しき男に会はむとて 此処まで来たは来たものの 長き浪路に倦き果てて もと来し国へ帰り行く その術さへも波の上 父母棄てて恋慕ふ 男に付くか恋慕ふ 夫を捨てて海山の 深き恵みの父母の 御側に帰り村肝の 心をつくし仕ふるか 善と悪との国境 進むも知らに退くも 成らぬ苦しき海の上 月は御空に輝けど

妾は思案に暮の鐘 故郷を思ふ恋しさの 心の空も掻き曇る 吁如何にせむ千尋の 深き海路に身を投げて 親に背きしこの罪を 天と地との神々に 心の底より謝罪せむ』

§ 2

As she began to sing, the long-haired man firmly gripped the woman's hand,

"Hey, wait. From your song just now, I understand the situation. Because of you, my son could not remain in splendid Hakusetsukyō and was finally cast out by the villagers; he fled to Tokoyo-no-kuni last month, and because of that, great trouble has fallen upon my household. My precious son broke the rules of Hakusetsukyō, was driven from the village, and though I disowned him out of worldly obligation, what cannot be forgotten by any means is parental and filial affection. That I, an old man, have crossed these sea paths from afar to pursue my son is the parental heart lost in love for its child; my wife died in distress over this matter. That you yearn so much for my son and intend to cross this ocean—such kindness is the very same as my longing for my son. Thinking of it, I am truly grateful. When I consider your pure intention and you, who love my dear son, somehow all my anger until now has completely vanished like bubbles on the water, and now I feel all the more pity for you. Surely you must have made a firm promise. If I ask you, you ought to know where in Tokoyo-no-kuni he is; please do not hide it, think of me as a parent, and let me know every detail. No matter how much I might run through mountains and fields searching, even if I were to search that vast Tokoyo-no-kuni—broader even than this ocean—for ten or twenty years, I could never find him. If you tell me my son's whereabouts, I will become parent and child with you, and the three of us will live harmoniously as a family, not minding whichever edge of Tokoyo-no-kuni it may be."

Thus did even so sturdy a rough man, enveloped in the darkness of love for his child, shed tears like broad beans rolling down regardless of his surroundings—such was his piteous state.

The vast sky, which until now had been so clear, was suddenly covered with dark clouds; the view grew vague and hazy, lightning flashed, thunder roared, the wind raged bleakly, rain fell in torrents, and with surging waves it became a truly dismal sight. The passengers, who until then had been extremely noisy with various idle talks, turned pale and were filled with an unspeakable sense of dread; but just as they seemed to utter a single cry of "Ah!", the beautiful woman threw herself with a splash into the raging waves. The long-haired man, at the top of his voice,

"Ah, my daughter—no, woman from another place, why did you throw yourself in? Is there no way to save her? Everyone, rescue her, rescue her!"

「おい待て。今の歌で様子は分かった。俺の忰はお前のために結構な白雪郷にいることもできず、とうとう里人から放り出されてしまって、この前の月に常世の国へ逐電し、それゆえに俺のところは大変な迷惑だ。大切な忰は白雪郷の規則を破って村を追い出され、俺も世間の義理で勘当はしたものの、どうしても忘れられないのは親子の情愛だ。年寄りの俺がはるばるこの浪路を渡って忰の後を追うのも子ゆえに迷う親心、俺の女房はそれを苦にして死んでしまったのだぞ。お前もそれほど俺の忰を慕って、この海原を渡って行こうという心遣いは、俺が忰を思うのと同じことだ。思えば実にありがたい。清いお前の志、俺の可愛い忰を愛してくれるお前と思えば、どうしたことか今までの腹立ちもすっかり水の泡のように消えてしまって、今は一層不憫なような心持ちがしてきた。何としても堅い約束をしているのであろう。お前に尋ねれば常世の国のどこにいるということは分かっているはず、どうか隠さず親だと思って、俺に逐一知らせてくれ。俺がどれほど山野を駆け巡って探したところで、この海よりも広い広大な常世の国を、十年や二十年探したところで、探し当てられるものではない。忰の居場所を聞かせてくれたら、俺もお前と親子になり、親子三人仲睦まじく、常世の国のどこの端でも厭わず、暮らす考えだ」

と、あれほど頑丈な荒男も、子ゆえの心の闇に閉ざされてあたり構わず空豆のような涙をぽろぽろとこぼす、そのあわれさよ。

と歌ひ始むるや、長髪の男はその女の手をグツト握り、

『オイ待て。今の歌で様子は判つた。俺の忰は貴様の為に結構な白雪郷に居る事も出来ず、たうとう里人より刎ね出されて仕舞つて、この前の月に常世の国に逐電し、夫れがために俺のところは大変な迷惑だ。大切な忰は白雪郷の規則を破つて、村は逐ひ出され、俺も浮世の義理で勘当はしたものの、如何しても忘れられぬは親子の情愛だ。年寄つた俺が遥々この浪路を渡つて忰の後を追ふも子故に迷ふ親心、俺の女房は夫れを苦にして死ンで了ひよつたぞよ。お前も夫れほど俺の忰を慕つて、この海原を渡つて行かうと云ふ親切は、俺が忰を思ふも同じことだ。思へば実に有難い。清いお前の志、俺の可愛い忰を愛して呉れるお前と思へば、如何したものか今までの腹立もスツカリと水の泡沫のやうに消えて了つて、今は一層憐なやうな心持がして来た。何ンでも堅い約束をして居るのであらう。お前に聞けば常世の国の何処に居るといふことは判つて居らう筈、どうぞ包まず親ぢやと思うて、俺に逐一知らして呉れ。俺が何ほど山野を駈廻つて探したとて、この海よりも広いダダツ広い常世の国を、十年や二十年探したとて、探し当らるるものではない。忰の在処を聞かして呉れたら、俺もお前と親子に成り、親子三人睦まじう、常世の国の何処の端でも厭はず、暮す考だ』

とさしも頑丈の荒男も、子ゆゑの暗に鎖されて四辺かまはず蚕豆のやうな涙をボロボロと溢すその憐さ。

今までさしも晴朗なりし大空も忽ち黒雲蔽ひ、一望模糊として電光閃々、雷鳴轟き、凄然として風荒れ狂ひ、雨は沛然として降り来り、怒濤澎湃実に惨澹たる光景となりぬ。今まで四方山の話に喧噪を極めたる一同の乗客は、顔色蒼白となり得もいはれぬ不安の念に満たされけるが、アツと一声叫ぶよと見る間に、麗しき女の姿は荒れ狂ふ浪に向つてザンブと許り身を投じたり。長髪の男は声を限りに、

『ヤアわが娘、いな他処の女、何故に投身したぞ。助ける術は無いか、皆の者救へ救へ』

§ 3

Even that voice was intercepted by the sound of the raging waves, and did not reach the ears of all present. The man pressed his hands together toward heaven and appeared to pray for something for a short while, but again he leaped and plunged with a splash into the sea, sending up spray, and his figure turned into white waves left behind. The pitiless surface of the sea was terrifying with mountainous raging billows, merely tossing the ship like a leaf.

Upon the deep black surface of the sea, where neither black nor white could be distinguished, a single ray of bright light came shining down, aimed at the ship. In the heavens, lightning flashed from time to time, and thunder roared, misery upon misery. Every one of them was resolved for death. Nay, they were merely praying for something in subdued whispers, each from their own mouths.

"Even the ocean of rough waves and wind, Even the wilderness where tigers and wolves roar, Are nothing at all; For the body that entrusts itself to the teachings of the Kami, The boat of the Law is peaceful at heart, Propagators of salvation for the world. Let the wind blow and blow, let the waves rage! Let the sounding thunder rumble! Even if this body becomes scraps of seaweed At the bottom of the sea and perishes, Why should I fear? Under the protection Of the precious Kami of the Ryūgū, The divine majesty that opens, saved by the sacred land Of Takamanohara for eternity, In the season of flowers in the third month of spring, Pure-hearted noble men, Said to shine light upon the dark road, Propagators of the Hinode-no-Kami, Wind and demons alike, Iwahi-hime, The rough sea's surface, Omonagi's

The ocean calmed and auspicious, The ocean calmed and auspicious! Casting oneself into the sea of a thousand fathoms, The sins of my own body, the true heart Of Kuhizamochi-hime-no-Mikoto Has pierced heaven and earth, And soon the ocean shall be calmed all across And surely become like a mirror. Truly precious is the way of the Kami; The grace of the Kami is ever broader, Like the vast ocean, Like the vast ocean."

Thus from within the darkness, with a clear voice, the propagation hymn came echoing forth.
