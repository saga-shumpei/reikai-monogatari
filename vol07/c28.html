Chapter 28: The Cavern of Wonders [328]

第28章　不思議の窟〔328〕

§ 1

The roaring sound inside the cavern became intensely stronger by the moment, as if millions of tigers and wolves were roaring fiercely all at once, causing all things—the surrounding mountains, trees, and grasses—to shudder. Kojimawake, in a state near unconsciousness, remained fallen on his back upon the ground, his limbs trembling and twitching. Hinode-no-kami,

"Oh-i, oh-i,"

signaled thus, and in response to this call, the messenger of Iwaihime and the messenger of Omonagi appeared from nowhere; here the three deities appeared before the cavern where Kojimawake lay fallen, Hinode-no-kami sang a song, the messenger of Omonagi held stones in both hands to beat the rhythm, and Iwaihime, wearing a sash of club moss, holding an evergreen pine branch in her left hand and spreading a white folding fan in her right hand, began to dance.

Iwaihime's song:

"Matching the breath Of fire and water of heaven and earth, The divine island of Tsukushi Which Kuniharu-tachi-no-Mikoto created, Surrounding the vast ocean, The land of Urayasu is a fertile land, The land of Kumaso is a garden of the gods. Is this the Heavenly Rock Dwelling, Built firm and everlasting as enduring rock? Kuniharu-tachi-no-Ōkami, Falling victim to the glib tongues And schemes of the evil, foul-hearted host of gods, Received the admonition Of the Heavenly Sun Deity And withdrew to the Land of Roots; Yet the Sumegami Hid deep within the rock cave, Living in hiding from the world, Everlastingly protecting the souls Of gods and mortals in the four directions of heaven and earth.

That great merit endures for thousands of generations; Firm as everlasting rock, Deep as hollowed rock, Enduring within this rock, Enduring within this rock. The deities who firmly and everlastingly proclaim The divine words of the Heavenly Sun's rising To open the rock cave Are Hinode-no-kami, Iwaihime, And Omonagihiko—the three deities. Turning an overturned floating boat into a stage, Bravely stamping and resounding before the rock, The herald of the sacred Kojima Appeared as Takehiwake, Ascending in accordance with the mission Given by the three heavenly deities. Yet his heart remains in perpetual darkness, The shadows of his breast not yet cleared away; The rock door of his heart is shut tight, With no means to open it.

Kuniharu-tachi-no-Mikoto of deep grace, His parted soul, The great deity Takehiwake, Guides with parental love To open the Heavenly Rock Dwelling; Receiving that divine mind Through myriad divine words, The herald of Kojima of the shiranui Dispels the tens of thousands of clouds and mists Hanging over his heart, And under the grace of the Sun Deity Shining clear and crimson in the cleared skies, The darkness of the eternal world must surely clear away. Grant forgiveness, Takehiwake, Guardian deity of the land of Kumaso! May the hearts of people be pure and clear, Submitting to the path of sincerity, And peacefully, the soul Of Sumiyo-hime-no-Mikoto In this cavern, the three deities—"

"Raising the roof-crossbeams high in sacred reverence, Before the noble crag Where the heavenly liturgy, the grand liturgy, is recited, May Takehiwake also consent To the word-soul of Hinode-no-kami, And soften his divine heart."

So raising their clear voices and singing in fine rhythm, they sweated, were possessed by the divine, and danced wildly. Omonagi-no-kami struck stones together, keeping time pleasantly, and the great sound of the cavern, violent as it had been, ceased like a dream. Kojimawake sprang to his feet, opened his narrow eyes, and looked upon the three deities in astonishment; unable to comprehend the scene of this place, wondering whether it was a dream, reality, or an illusion, he pinched his own cheek and bit his finger,

"Ah, so it was not a dream after all."

Hinode-no-kami said:

"Oh, you are the missionary Kojimawake; observing your behavior from moments ago, which seemed truly suspicious, I wondered what was the matter and stood in the shade of the trees listening, whereupon came the roaring of this cavern. What happened? Tell us the full details of what transpired."

He asked thus, and Kojimawake, while bowing silently to the three missionaries, said:

"No indeed, it was terrible. Since entering the service of the divine realm, I have achieved not a single deed of renown; in the sadness of my unenlightened mind, I misunderstood the divine will of the most merciful and compassionate Great God and went about propagating it throughout the world, finally arriving at this island of Africa one month ago. According to the people of this land, there is a magnificent cavern here that roars from time to time. Thinking it would be good for my spiritual training, I crossed steep mountain slopes and traversed valleys, and shortly after I finally arrived at this cavern, various natives came to worship in this manner, praying earnestly for something. When I listened closely, they were all people who adhered to the teachings of Tokoyo Shin'ō; thinking that this must not be allowed, I burned my bridges and, staking my life, began to sing the propaganda song of the Anaizakyō. The multitude of people intensely hated my propagation song and attempted to persecute me from all directions. 'What of it! We are missionaries, divine messengers who preach the teachings of heaven and earth. Even into fire or the depths of water, plunging in to save others is our calling,' I thought, and summoning every bit of courage, I finally led them to repent. Yet before I could even heave a sigh of relief, a strange figure appeared dimly from the depths of this cavern, mocking me with 'A-ha-ha-ha-ha, O-ho-ho-ho-ho,' enumerating every single one of our weaknesses, and, alas, utterly and severely scolding me. Our bodies and souls are not yet polished. I must now firmly resolve to correct my misunderstandings up to this point."

...thus he recounted the outline. Hinode-no-kami declared solemnly:

"This is the retreat of a precious deity; Takehiwake is a provisional divine name, and the time shall surely come when the deity proclaims the true name. Please dwell here for a long time, build a shrine in front of this cave, enshrine the spirit of Sumiyo-hime-no-Mikoto, and protect the people of the land of Kumaso, for we must cross this mountain and go to the land of Hi."

Upon hearing this, Kojimawake said,

"By what divine arrangement could this be? To unexpectedly meet the precious Hinode-no-kami-sama brings no greater joy than this. Obedient to your command, I shall receive the divine name of the deity of the rock cave of the Ōkami-sama, change my name from now on to Takehiwake, and protect this place eternally. Please rest assured,"

...thus he replied. Hinode-no-kami showed an expression of satisfaction, left this place accompanied by the three missionaries, and, singing the propagation song once again, steadily climbed up this ravine.
