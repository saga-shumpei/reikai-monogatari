Chapter 25: Takehibiwake [325]

第25章　建日別〔325〕

§ 1

Caressed by the wind of Shinado blowing and turning over the great ocean, drifted along as the repeated waves of Tokoyo rolled in without their foreseeing it, they arrived at the vast land of Africa in Tsukushijima. Bathed in the light of the heavenly sun illuminating the dark world, casting off their radiant garments, their straw-coat-and-hat attire and their legs exceedingly light, they climbed the rugged mountain. Here, a single torrential pool clashed against strange crags; pouring forth myriads of stern shouts in water spray that flew with furious rage and sudden shouting, as it struck the surface of the strange rocks, the crags turned away their strange and ugly faces, and the water rushed frantically. They came upon a path in the ravine of extraordinary and sublime beauty.

On a day that was sultry although spring, deep in the mountains of Africa, standing for a while in the shade of the trees and gazing upon this scene, the three missionaries of auspicious names sang an exceedingly invigorating propagation song, which, combining with the sound of the waterfall, was as though sweeping away the dust of heaven and earth and purifying the mountains and fields.

The three missionaries gazed at this remarkable view of the mountain stream, and Hinode-no-kami said,

"Ah, it is truly a magnificent view in all the world. We too have become missionaries and strode freely throughout the world, but this is an exhilarating landscape such as we have never seen before. The mountains and the mountain streams alike truly make me feel as though our minds were being washed."

Iwaihime said, "Indeed it is. Having continued living upon the waves for a long time, I was becoming somewhat weary of it, unworthy though it may be to say so, but the world is well made. That we are able to see such a wondrous sight of the world is entirely due to Kami's arrangement. Though people say that travel is gloomy and painful, none but missionaries could ever see such a superb view. We must surely offer our gratitude to Kami."

Omonagi said, "Ah, by the way, does not some strange sound echo from the bottom of the valley, though I cannot tell clearly whether it is the sound of the stream, the groaning of a fierce beast, or the shouting of a human being?"

Hinode-no-kami stood up abruptly and, straining his ears, said,

"Ha, indeed it is a groaning sound that somehow defies understanding. In any case, I shall go and investigate, taking that voice as my guide. Please wait here for a while, Your Excellencies."

The two deities spoke with one voice,

"No, we too shall accompany you."

Hinode-no-kami said, "If so, I shall go one step ahead. Please follow behind me, keeping yourselves in and out of sight. If anything untoward should occur, I shall give a signal, so you must not emerge until I make that signal."

春とはいうものの蒸し暑い日に、アフリカの山の奥で、しばらく木陰に佇んでこの光景を眺め、名さえめでたい三柱の宣伝使が、飛瀑の声と相まって極めて壮快な宣伝歌を歌い、天地の塵を払い落とし、山野を清めるかのようである。

三柱の宣伝使は、この谷川の奇勝を眺め、日の出神は、

「ああ、実に天下の絶景だ。我々も宣伝使となって天下を闊歩してきたが、いまだかつて見たことのない壮快な景色である。山といい、谷川といい、実に我々の心境を洗うような心地がするね」

祝姫「左様でございます。長い間波の上の生活を続けて、少々もったいないことながら飽き気味になっていましたが、世界はよくできているものですね。このような天下の奇勝を見ることができるのも、全く神様のお引き合わせ。旅は憂いもの、辛いものと申しますが、宣伝使でなくては到底このような絶景を見ることはできない。我々は神様に感謝を捧げねばなりますまい」

面那芸「ああ、ところで何やら谷底に、流れの音か、猛獣の呻き声か、人の叫び声か、はっきり分かりませんが妙な響きがするではありませんか」

日の出神は、すっと立って耳を澄ませながら、

「はー、いかにも何だか納得のいかない唸り声ですな。何はともあれ、私はその声を目当てに調べて来ましょう。貴使はここにしばらく待っていてください」

二柱は口を揃えて、

「いや、我々もお供いたしましょう」

日出神「それなら私が一歩先に参ります。貴殿は見え隠れしながらついて来てください。万一のことがあれば合図をしますから、こちらが合図をするまでは、出て来てはなりませんぞ」

§ 2

Saying so, Hinode-no-Kami advanced into the depths of the valley, searching for the voice.

Walking about two or three chō, here great rocks towering high enough to gaze up at stood erect in the ravine, and large caverns measuring five or six shaku were naturally hollowed out here and there. Several dozen men with red hair and faces black as charcoal with a slightly coppery tint, thrusting out thick lips like birds' beaks, sat cross-legged, placing a pale-faced man with somewhat poor eyesight in their midst, relentlessly mocking him. Hinode-no-Kami concealed himself in the shade of the trees and listened intently to this situation.

"Hey, you, blabbering that you are an evangelist of Ananaikyō or some such, coming to this island without guidance and singing stiff, bothersome songs—are you trying to make a total mess of our clan? What do you take this place for? It is the domain of Tokoyo-kami-ō of the land of Tokoyo, to our utmost awe. Furthermore, carrying yourself so arrogantly, blowing a loud trumpet about this world changing and separating good and evil—what is the meaning of this? If you quit being an evangelist once and for all and become our slave, well and good. If you refuse, we shall execute you right now. Give your answer!"

Not yielding in the least, the one man raised his voice that echoed in all directions,

"God appears out in the open, and separates good and evil."

"Hey, what a stubborn fellow. Are you still spouting that? Hey, you all, bring stones. Let us stuff this fellow's mouth!"

"Hey, evangelist, this is a splendid land where, to our great awe, Izanami-no-Mikoto, who appeared in the land of Tokoyo, employs the great deity named Tokoyo-kami-ō, and where his retainer, the powerful and highly commanding deity named Arakumabake, protects us. Thanks to the deity, the people here do not work at all day after day; eating figs, receiving tachibana, bitter oranges, and other splendid things, brewing wine from pears, it is a paradise where they live high-spiritedly each day, saying, 'Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness, and after darkness the moon rises.' Despite that, what are you doing, listing troublesome, absurd arguments? What nonsense are this 'renewal' and 'separation'! Come, shall we all gather now, roughly carve this fellow up, and eat him?"

"Hey, do not do such reckless things. This fellow is stubbornly unyielding, but he emits a terrifying light from those narrow eyes. He might well be a monstrous apparition of a deity that descended from heaven. If we carelessly lay a hand on him, divine punishment will strike us."

§ 3

"Do not say faint-hearted things. We are splendid parishioners of Izanagi-no-Kami. Whether it fell from heaven or sprang from the earth I do not know, but do not tremble before merely one such beast!"

One man raised his voice and,

"The deity appears in this world And divides good from evil; The countries of heaven and earth and the four directions, The eighty islands, the Great Eight-Island Land. The missionary who unfolds the teachings, The deity's grace too, Ōshima And the priestly ruler of Oshima-wake. Though his eyes are slightly poor, Not reflected in your eyes, The eyes of the heart are likened To the light of the sun and moon, Oshima-wake. Babbling without knowing the reason, The gathering of the ugly Magatsu, Tigers, wolves, ogres, and giant serpents. Deep in the mountains of the land of Kumaso, Parting the mountain path and pressing forward, I am your helping deity. The world is the land of Kumaso in perpetual darkness; Seeking to illuminate it leaving no shadow, Departing from Aya no Takama, Takehibiwake with all his heart, Appearing as Kami no Mikoto; It is the divine trust Of this Kunitama and Amatsu-hi-no-Kami no Mikoto, It is the divine trust of Kami no Mikoto. O raging deities, O ugly people, Dividing good from evil, The divine decree of the true deity."

As soon as he began to sing thus, the entire company stopped their ears, shut their eyes, and

"Ah, this is unbearable!"

prostrated themselves face down on the earth. At this time, once again, the propagation song came heard from within the forest.
