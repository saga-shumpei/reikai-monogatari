Chapter 40: The Mountain of Scarlet Foliage [240]

第40章　紅葉山〔240〕

§ 1

Receiving time and again the bullets of dew and the swords of frost, and illuminating the red sincerity of the autumn leaves dyed in blood, aiming to build up with exceedingly high pillars of true timber this divine plan like a brocade loom and like a spool of weaving thread in the divine land of Takamahara, the divine person, paying heed in all directions, making light of painful afflictions, repeatedly braving wind and rain, and sadly taking the moon and stars of the heavenly sky as bedding upon grass-pillowed travels, comes advancing forward. Sitting beneath a great tree of autumn leaves upon Mount Momiji, which is pure red in heart, the noble figure of the propagating deity, feeling pain at the sight of the mountains' colors fading day by day here and there, utters sighs and gasps, and while his pure heart prays to the Great Deity of the Moon, he shivers in his thin-robed body at the bleak winter wind that suddenly blows, yet rises and sets forth once again. Where does he go; aiming toward the capital of Moscow, Tsukiteruhiko, the propagator whose soul is as round and full as the light of the moon rising over the edge of the eastern mountains, advanced breathlessly toward the divine mansion of Michinukihiko, where Kasuganohime was born.

Amidst the passing rain that falls just then, outfitting himself in straw sandals and leggings, with a small sedge hat and a grass straw cape, lamenting the end of this world in an old quilted garment soaked with a rain of tears, he recalls that even sleeves brushing against each other is a bond from previous lives, and stumbling over a stone is the edge of a karmic tie.

Three divine persons came running and hastening, drawn by the propagation song that this propagator chanted, and appeared before him in an instant, lowering their heads to the ground, saying:

"Are you not the deity Tsukiteruhiko whom we met on the southwest coast of the Mediterranean Sea; at that time we had received the chastisement of the deities of heaven and earth, and while we were wandering on our journey like cripples losing our way, you were the manifest deity who proclaimed with spirit-imbued words that moved heaven and earth, 'The plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds shall bloom all at once,' and then concealed your figure toward the east; while we prostrated ourselves in worship toward your departure and waited for a reunion, whether through the arrival of heaven's opportune time, unexpectedly meeting you here and gazing upon your revered countenance moves us to tears like a blind turtle encountering a floating log—indeed far beyond a floating log, as if blossoms had bloomed on a dead tree—and with our faces blushing like the scattered autumn leaves of words, we endure our shame and have come out to welcome you. My father Michinukihiko fortunately passes his days in good health even now, yet he has by nature been obstinate and imperceptive, listening to the divine teachings revealed upon Mount Tenkyō with an absent mind, letting them pass like wind blowing through empty sky; his ears are stopped up like fungus, and though we, a couple with much distress, try to persuade him with instructions, he does not listen to a single word; our parents have been captured by the demon of sake raging day and night, and their hearts are wretched; though morning and evening we exert our bodies and exhaust our minds to admonish him so as to open the rock cave of eternal darkness and save them, it is pitifully like east wind blowing into a horse's ear; though birds sing and flowers bloom, our heart seeks to rectify my father's heart, and we beseech you to consider our pitiable plight of treading a dark path and grant us a single word of instruction."

折から降りしきる村雨の中、草鞋と脚絆で身を固め、菅の小笠や草の蓑を身につけ、この世の末をはかなんで、涙の雨に濡れる古布子の姿であり、袖触れ合うのも多生の縁、つまずく石も縁の端緒である。

走って駆け寄って来る三柱の神人は、この宣伝使の謡う宣伝歌に引きつけられ、たちまち御前に現れて、大地に頭を下げながら、

『貴下は地中海の西南岸にてお目にかかりました月照彦神ではいらっしゃいませんか。私たちはそのとき天地の神の懲戒を受け、道を踏み外す足萎えの旅にさまよっていた折に、天地も動く言霊で「三千世界の梅の花が一度に開く」と言挙げされ、東を指して御姿をお隠しになられた現し神であられ、私たちはその後ろ姿を伏し拝み、その再会を待っていたところ、天の時節の到来でしょうか、思いがけずここで巡り会い尊顔を拝することは、盲亀の浮木、浮木どころか枯れ木に花が咲いたように感無量となり、言葉が散り敷く紅葉のように顔を赤らめ、恥を忍びつつお出迎え申し上げました。わが父道貫彦は幸いにも現在まで健やかに月日を送っておりますが、もとより頑迷不霊であり、天教山に現れた神の教えを上の空、空を吹く風と聞き流し、塞がった耳は木耳のようであって、気苦労の多い私たち夫婦がどのように教示を諭そうとも、ただの一言も決して聞き入れず、日に夜に荒れ狂う酒の魔の虜となった両親の心は浅ましく、常暗の岩戸を開いて救おうと、朝夕に身を尽くし心を尽くして諫めますが、馬の耳に東風という浅ましさで、鳥は歌い花は咲いても私の心は、父の心を直そうと暗い道をたどる憐れさをお推し量りいただき、一言の教示をお願い申し上げます』

§ 2

...thus he pleaded with tears.

In the inner palace of Moscow, Michitsurahiko, together with many attendants, was in the midst of a great drinking banquet in the Yahirodono. The divine people together raised their voices in unison:

"Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness; After darkness the moon rises; After darkness the moon rises."

...thus the voices noisily and wildly singing resounded far outside the palace hall.

Suddenly Michitsurahiko's complexion turned pale, and he collapsed onto the seat. The drunkenness of the many divine people sobered all at once, turning into utter tumult. Michitsurahime was greatly astonished and cried out madly, "Where is Takasumibetsu? Kasugahime...!"

The divine people, seeking to find the whereabouts of the two divine directors, searched with sharp eyes and ran all about the castle. Yet there was no trace of them whatsoever.

At this time, outside the castle gate, the whispering voices of many divine people crowding together were heard. And three mysterious propagators raised their clear voices:

"Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness; After darkness the moon rises; What does it mean that the moon rises? A moon it is, but a moon of confusion; Out of breath, collapsing, out of strength, Before long life too is exhausted in the sky; When gazing at the clouds that traverse the sky, To the east and west, north and south; The end of intoxication is the gasping for breath; The pitiful end of Michitsurahiko, Seen before our very eyes; Today's moon has exhausted all divine favor; The clouded heart is pitch-black; Illuminating the dark path is Tsukiteruhiko, The propagators by the divine command; The moon is in the great sky, Takasumibetsu, And Kasugahime of peaceful spring; Seeking to save the crisis of life, With one heart upon the Single Island, The single pine where the deity resides; The eternal and unchanging divine law; The retribution of heavenly punishment for transgressing the law; Is it the sudden ruin of Michitsurahiko of Moscow? Living on in this endless world, Dreaming endless dreams, The threads of the heart tangling together, The utterly disordered Kushimitama; Shine, shine forth like the morning sun; Clear, become serene like Tsukiteruhiko; Awakening to the divine teaching, Reviving and breathing once again, Appear as directors of salvation, Appear as directors of salvation!"

...thus they stood before the gate, surrounded by many divine people, calling out in loud voices.

This voice reached the ears of Michitsurahime as if piercing her chest. The princess ordered her attendants to invite the three divine directors and have them proceed into the inner palace.

The three divine directors, still wearing their straw raincoats and sedge hats, advanced into the inner palace without the slightest hesitation, sang once again the propagation song of the three thousand worlds, and began to clap their hands and dance.

Michitsurahiko, who had collapsed with faint and dying breath, suddenly stood up and began to clap both hands and dance. The divine people were so astonished at this great wonder that they were dumbfounded, with mouths agape.

The three divine directors exchanged glances, cast off the straw raincoats and sedge hats they wore, and stood tripartite like a three-comma whorl in the center of the banquet. Looking at them, they were the three deities: Ōyashimahiko no Mikoto, Takasumibetsu, and Kasugahime.

From this time forth, Michitsurahiko, who had been so obstinate, repented of his past errors, obeyed the teachings of Tsukiteruhiko no Kami, abandoned his prominent position, became an attendant of Tsukiteruhiko no Kami, and went on pilgrimages in all directions for the salvation of the realm.
