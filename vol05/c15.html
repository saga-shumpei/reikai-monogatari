Chapter 15: The Stone-Pounding Song [215]

第15章　石搗歌〔215〕

§ 1

Through the solemn saniha of Banko-ōkami, the spirit-possessed frenzied state of Tokoyohiko, Tokoyohime, Tatsuyamawake, and the other deities was immediately subdued. Thereupon, the deities from Tokoyohiko on down were deeply impressed from the bottom of their hearts by the celestial vision of Banko-ōkami and the excellence of his divine art of saniha, and resolved to obey the command of Banko-ōkami in all matters thereafter.

Here, Banko-ōkami chose an extremely level site on the mountainside of Mount Ural, intending to build a palace, felled trees in large and small gorges, transported stones, and commenced foundation work. The sound of the deities gathering together, singing bravely while pounding the stones with thumping reverberations, continued without distinction of day or night, with such force that heaven and earth trembled on account of it. As a result of the desperate labors of the hundred deities, the foundation work was entirely completed in just over one hundred days.

The stone-pounding song at that time was:

In the ancient age of the gods, in that ancient time, The foundation firmly built By the Great Deity Kunitokotachi, who protects the world as firm as eternal rock, Was, in rainy mornings and windy evenings, Under snowy skies and rainstorms, All too fragilely overturned by a tempest of force; To restore this overturned divine age, The deities assemble this time upon Mount Ural, The assembled Lord Deity Shionagahiko, And among the Eight Kings, Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime, Seeking to illuminate the darkness of the eternal night, Armenia of loyal red heart, Where the morning sun shines purely and widely, On evening moonlit nights of clear light, In the heavenly skies where stars also sparkle, Mount Ural, where even the high-traveling clouds and soaring birds Hesitate to pass.

In this auspicious age where the morning sun Shines across Omote and Ural, Firmly building and perpetually Opening the divine governance, The August Sun Deity residing in heaven, The Moon Deity traversing the vast sky, Do you celebrate with clear light this perfected age, This very day? The wind is pure, and on the flowering trees The branches bend heavy with fruit, The mountain of pines awaiting the righteous deities, Truly auspicious spring of a thousand generations, Spring mists trailing in all directions, Waving green sleeves in prosperity, The mountain maiden of spring gracefully Dances and brings peace to Banko's Divine age eternal and unchanging, Divine age eternal and unchanging, The hundred deities rise up in high spirits, By the divine grace, the plants and trees of the surrounding mountains,

How auspicious that even the grass and trees bend low! Ah, to build this foundation ever firmer, Ever stronger, For this blissful realm of a thousand autumns and ten thousand years, We pound and condense it to the upper bedrock, We pound and solidify it to the lower bedrock, Pounding and solidifying it, on this full-moon night, How heroic is the moonlight! Mount Ural is eternal, Armenia shining in the sky, The fields are level and the mountains far, When listening to the sound of the gently blowing wind, It resounds as banko, banko. This stone-pounding that resounded as banko, banko Is ever firmer, An eternal and unchanging foundation! An eternal and unchanging foundation! Yoitosā, yoitosa, yoitosassā, Yoitosa, yoitosa, yoitosassā.

§ 2

At last, the foundation work was completed. From this point, under the command of Yatsuō-no-Ōkami, the deities divided into parties in all directions, searching the mountain ridges and valley bottoms for large and small trees; offering the bases and tips to Yamaguchinokami, they felled and collected the middle trunks, hauling them up day and night toward the mountainside of the Ural Mountains, chanting 'Eiya, eiya.'

Through the day-and-night devotion of the deities, timber was gathered like a mountain for the most part. However, the most essential ridgepole for the palace was lacking. The deities searched and sought all over the surrounding mountains, but could obtain nothing suitable. Thereupon, by the command of Banko-no-Ōkami, Tatsuyamabiko established an altar on the plain, presented various offerings, purified his mind and body, and was to ask for a divine oracle. Taking a lesson from the previous failure, Banko-no-Ōkami personally took the seat of the saniwa. Yamaguchinokami possessed Tatsuyamabiko and instructed him as follows:

'As for this ridgepole, far to the south from here, there is a spiritual mountain called Mount Yōjusan. On its mountainside stands a great tree with luxuriant foliage that hides the western sea in the morning and hides the eastern sea in the evening. As tens of thousands of Takatsukami dwell crowded upon that great tree, it is not easy to cut it down and collect it. Therefore, I will now, by the authority of Yamaguchinokami, make them move to a great tree on another mountain. Tatsuyamabiko and the many deities, prepare your tools and head for Mount Yōjusan as swiftly as possible.'

Having proclaimed this, the divine spirit quickly departed. Emboldened and rejoicing at this divine revelation, without losing any time, leading hundreds and thousands of deities to Mount Yōjusan, clearing the thorns and brambles, crossing valleys, and slashing through the thickets, they finally reached the base of the great tree.

The Takatsukami on the tree, terrified by the sight of the eight-headed, eight-tailed serpent standing at the vanguard, moved to the rear mountain just as Yamaguchinokami commanded. Seeking to fell and take this tree, the deities climbed on one another's shoulders, first caught hold of the first branch, and then many deities, carrying tools such as broadaxes and saws, began cutting from the uppermost branches.

Being the renowned and unprecedented great tree of Mount Yōjusan, this undertaking did not progress easily. It is said that cutting down this tree required nearly three years of days.

いよいよ基礎工事は竣工した。これより八王大神の指揮の下に、神人らは四方八方に手分けをして山の尾根の上や谷の底、大木や小木を探しながら、本と末とは山口神に捧げて、中津御木を伐り採り、エイヤエイヤと日ごと夜ごとにウラル山の山腹をめざして運び上げるのであった。

神人らの昼夜の丹精によって、用材はほとんど大部分が山のように集まった。けれども最も必要な宮殿の棟木を欠いていた。神人らは四方の山をあさり探し求めたが、適当なものは得られなかった。ここに盤古大神の命により、竜山別は平地に祭壇を設け、諸々の供物を献じ、心身を清めて神勅を請うこととなった。以前の失敗に懲りて、盤古大神は自ら審神の席についた。竜山別には山口神が憑依され、教え諭すことには、

「この棟木は、ここから遥か南方に当たり、鷹鷲山という霊山がある。その山腹に、朝には西の海を隠し、夕方には東の海を隠す、枝葉の繁茂した大樹がある。その大樹には数万の高津神が群がり棲んでいるため、これを伐り採ることは容易ではない。それゆえ、私はこれから山口神の職権をもって、彼らを他山の大樹に転居させよう。竜山別をはじめ数多くの神人は得物を用意し、一刻も早く鷹鷲山に向かえ」

と宣示したまま、神霊はたちまち引き取ってしまわれた。この神示によって数多くの神人は勇み喜び、時を移さず鷹鷲山に数百千の神人を引率し、荊棘を切り開き、谷を渡り、叢を切り払い、ようやく大樹の下に達した。

樹上にいた高津神は、先頭に立つ八頭八尾の大蛇の姿に肝を冷やし、山口神の命じるままに、裏山に転居してしまった。この木を伐り採ろうとして、神人は肩車をし、まず第一の枝に取りつき、続いて数多くの神人は鉞や鋸などの得物を携え、最上部の枝から伐り始めた。

名うての鷹鷲山の希代の大木であるため、容易にはこの事業は捗らなかった。この木を伐るのにほとんど三年の歳月を要したという。
