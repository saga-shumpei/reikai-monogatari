Chapter 34: Communion of Fish and Water [234]

第34章　水魚の情交〔234〕

§ 1

The solitary, scenic island, unique in heaven and earth; the skies of the minds of the gods, illuminated by the light of rare auspicious signs, cleared completely; with a harmonious and gentle atmosphere, the sky of the mind was serene without a speck of hindrance; carrying their respective tools, the various divine beings together offered prayers to the god of the mountain's crest and entrance; gratefully receiving the permission of the awe-inspiring deity, they descended into the fathomless ravine, felled a giant cedar comparable to a mast, replaced the mast, fashioned anew the stern, and rigged the ship in which they had arrived; finally bidding reluctant farewell to the far-famed solitary island and the solitary pine, they raised the mainsail and leisurely set forth; upon waves smooth as poured oil, drawing round ripples with the swaying of the ship, both mind and body opened in fellowship; sharing the same fate aboard this vessel of salvation, letting both strife and hell wash away upon the waves, the delight of indulging in talk from far and near was like the appearance of the truly auspicious old man and old woman of Takasago; having blown away the clouds and mist of the mind's sky like the mists of Ibuki, and swept away the dust at the bottom of the heart as if with a weeder or a broom, feeling the spring-like mood of the New Year, amidst a harmonious and gentle atmosphere, they set sail cheerfully toward the southwest to the rhythm of boat songs.

In the sky, thousands of seagulls flew back and forth, to the left and right, forming a delightful sight as if blessing and seeing off the party's ship. Hōribenokami was the first to open his mouth. Then, facing the divine people aboard the ship,

"As I look upon you, all of you appear to be divine people of distinguished lineage. For what purpose are you crossing this sea?"

When he thus asked, the man who was the most prominent among the divine people and possessed a robust build shifted his knees and,

"In truth, we are chieftains of small villages, but facing the celestial and terrestrial abnormalities of recent times, the anxiety of the divine people of our villages is extraordinary; when we observed what seemed like fireworks rising into the sky toward the northeast, a threefold bridge of gold, silver, and bronze colors spanned east and west across the heavens, rotating north and south, violent winds raged, torrential rains arrived incessantly, disrupting the harmony of favorable weather, thunder and earthquakes sounded unseasonably, volcanoes erupted, and the divine people on earth lost color, worrying over the future beyond the power of words to express. Furthermore, toward the northeastern sky, recently sixteen luminous stars appeared yet again, extending day by day toward the earth like golden threads, and in addition, sixteen silver stars likewise appeared in the southwestern sky, approaching the earth day by day, making us feel beyond doubt as if the gods of heaven and earth are hinting something to us divine people. Therefore, before those stars descend to the earth, we wished to head southwest to confirm their true nature and prepare the divine people of our villages, and as our duty as chieftains, we are advancing far toward the southwest,"

he began to speak languidly while tilting his head. No sooner had the chieftain's words ended than a deity waiting in the second seat immediately followed him, saying,

空には幾千羽の鴎が前後左右に飛び交い、一行の船を祝福して見送るかと思われるほどの心地よい光景であった。祝部神は真っ先に口の扉を開いた。そして船中の神人らに向かい、

「見渡すところ、貴殿らはいずれも由緒のありそうな神人と思われる。何の目的があってこの海を渡られるのか」

と問いかけたところ、神人の中で最も秀でて骨格のたくましい男が、膝を立て直し、

「実はわれわれは小郷の酋長でありますが、先頃からの天変地妖に対し、わが郷の神人たちの不安は並大抵ではなく、東北の天に向かって花火のような火光が天に衝き上がるかと見れば、大空には金銀銅色の三重の橋が東西に架かり、南北に回転し、暴風が吹き荒び、強雨がしきりに到来し、五風十雨の順調さを破り、雷鳴や地震が時に合わず鳴動し、火山は爆発し、地上の神人は顔色を失い、未来を憂慮することは言葉の尽くす限りではありません。加えて東北の天において、この頃またしても十六個の光る星が現れ、日を追ってその星は金線のように地上に向かって延長し、そのうえ西南の天に向かって銀色の十六個の星が同じく現れて、地上に日々接近しながら、われわれ神人に向かって何か天地の神が暗示されているかのような心地がしてならないのであります。それゆえわれわれはその星が地上に垂れ下がるに先立ち、西南に向かってその真相を確かめ、郷の神人に覚悟するところを持たせようと欲し、酋長の役目としてはるばる西南に向かって進むのであります」

と首を傾けながら、物憂げに語り始めた。酋長の言葉が終わるや否や、次席に控えていた一柱の神人は、すぐにその後を継いで、

と首を傾けながら、物憂しげに語り始めた。酋長の言葉終るや否や、次席にひかへたる一柱の神人は、直にその後をつけて、

§ 2

"What is still baffling to us is that the evening star appeared in the eastern sky before we knew it and emitted an extraordinary, unusual light, and various spots appeared around that star, so that we on earth were ill at ease, wondering if it might be an omen of something, and I too, having been chosen by the divine people of our village, am advancing toward the southwest. What, indeed, could this symbolize?"

So saying, he peered for a moment into the face of Hafuribe-no-kami.

Hafuribe-no-kami adjusted his posture on his knees and began to explain earnestly.

"These heavens and earth were by no means created by the power of earthly divine people. The Lord of Heaven, the deity of absolute, infinite power named Ōkunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, who alone exists in the great cosmos and fully possesses the three virtues of spirit, power, and body—infinite, absolute, without beginning, and without end—created this vast, boundless cosmos. And in this cosmos, He divided His own spirit and soul, named it Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, and caused Him to protect our earth and sky. Nevertheless, the world gradually became filled with the spirit of evil and corruption, the earthly divine people forgot the divine grace, the evil wind of putting body first and spirit second blew violently among both high and low, they indulged in selfish desires and defiled the supremely good and supremely beautiful earth, and moreover, swayed by the evil spirits of the giant serpent, the golden fox, and evil demons, the divine people standing in superior positions finally expelled the profoundly compassionate national ancestor Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto to the Netherworld and committed every manner of tyrannical and unprincipled act. Therefore, in this cosmos there is no true ruling deity, and day and night the divine people have degenerated, falling into the realm of asuras, hungry ghosts, hell, and beasts. Because of this, the earth shakes, the heavens are disordered, and celestial changes and terrestrial catastrophes occur frequently. The disasters of the world do not end with these; there are signs that a great calamity is now about to arrive. Therefore, we, in obedience to the divine decree of the deeply benevolent Nodatihiko-no-Mikoto and Nodatihime-no-Mikoto, in order to make the earthly divine people repent, to save this calamity, and to re-examine, re-hear, and re-proclaim great disasters into lesser disasters, abandoned the sacred rank of the Eight Kings, transformed into the appearance of such shabby commoners, traversed mountains, plains, rivers, and seas, and propagate the way of salvation. As the proverb says, even sleeves brushing together is karma from past lives, and even stumbling over a stone is the edge of a karmic tie; now, trusting ourselves to the same single boat, I, who impart the heavenly gospel, and you divine people, who listen to it, are by no means a coincidence, but are surely bound together by the cord of the great God's profound blessing; therefore, do not on any account fail to hear a single word of mine."

So saying, he stretched out his hand, scooped up seawater, and moistened his lips while relaxing his sitting posture.

The divine people sitting in a row, all with thoroughly strained facial complexions, tilted their heads and listened intently with pricked ears so as not to miss a single word.

「なおも私どもとして疑わしいのは、宵の明星がいつの間にか東の空に現れて非常な異光を放ち、その星の周囲には種々の斑紋が現れ、地上の私どもは何事かの前兆であろうかと生きた心地もせず、郷の神人に選ばれて私もまた西南を指して進むのであります。果たして何の象徴でありましょうか」

と言って祝部神の顔をちょっと覗いた。

祝部神は膝を立て直し、懇切に説き始めた。

「この天地は決して地上の神人の力によって造られたものではない。大宇宙にただ一柱まします無限絶対、無始無終の霊・力・体の三徳を完全に具え給う天主、大国治立尊という絶対無限力の神様が、この広大無辺の大宇宙を創造されたのである。そしてこの宇宙にはその身魂を分けて国治立尊と名づけ、わが大地および大空を守護させ給うたのである。それにもかかわらず世はだんだんと妖邪の気が満ち、地上の神人は神恩を忘却し、体主霊従の悪風は上下に吹き荒れ、かつ私利私欲に耽り、至善至美の地上を汚し、そのうえ大蛇と金狐と邪鬼の悪霊に左右されて、上位に立つ神人らは、ついに大慈大悲の国祖国治立尊を根底の国に神退けに退け、暴虐無道の限りを尽くした。それゆえこの宇宙には真の統率神がなく、神人は日夜に悪化して、修羅、餓鬼、地獄、畜生の世界へと堕ちてしまった。そのために地は震い天は乱れ、天変地異が頻繁に至る。世の災いはこれだけに足らず、一大災害が今まさに来ようとする象徴がある。それゆえ、私どもは慈愛深き野立彦命、野立姫命の神勅を奉じ、地上の神人を悔い改めさせ、この災害を救い、大難を小難に見直し、聞き直し、宣り直そうと、八王の聖位を捨て、このように見すぼらしい凡夫の姿と変わり、山野河海を跋渉して、救いの道の宣伝をするのである。諺に言う、袖振り合うも他生の縁、躓く石も縁の端とか、今や同じ一つの船に身を託し、天来の福音を伝える私も、これを聴くそなたたち神人らも決して偶然ではなく、必ずや深い大神の恵みの綱に共に結ばれたものであるから、私の一言を決して聴き落としてはならない」

と言って手を伸ばして海水をすくい、唇を潤しながら座を崩した。

居並ぶ神人らは皆、緊張し切った顔色で、首を傾げながら一言も聴き漏らすまいと耳を澄まして聞き入った。

と云つて祝部神の顔をちよつと覗いた。

祝部神は膝立直し、諄々として説き始めた。

並ゐる神人らはいづれも緊張し切つた面色にて、首を傾げながら一言も聴き洩らすまじと耳を澄まして聞き入りにける。
