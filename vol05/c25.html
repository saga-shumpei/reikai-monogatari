Chapter 25: The Oracle of the Maiden Goddess [225]

第25章　姫神の宣示〔225〕

§ 1

The moon was clear and the stars were sparse, the Milky Way flowed from the southeastern sky to the northwest, the wind played subtle music, the mountainside of Mount Tenkyō was girdled with a belt of mist, and looking out over the sea, the waves were calm and white sails, full and half-set, caught the wind and sped in all directions across the water's surface, a sight truly like white herons alighting in the sacred garden of the Ryūgū Castle.

Centering around Konohanahime no Mikoto, Amenomichiwake no Mikoto, Amenomasamichihiko no Mikoto, Tsukiteruhiko no Kami, Tarumashihiko no Kami, Iwatowake no Kami, Hahuribe no Kami, Hirokohiko no Kami, Ōta no Kami, and the other deities, with countenances brimming with courage, having now left the Koganebashi behind and resting on this Mount Tenkyō, took celestial glasses in hand, illuminating heaven above and illuminating the earth below, so that the sights of heaven and earth were as if held in the hand—nay, the sights of the three realms of the divine, manifest, and hidden could be seen through right before their eyes, and in that fascination, forgetting themselves, with one accord,

"Oh, oh, oh, this is terrible, terrible!"

so cried some, while

"Ah, how interesting!"

so cried other divine persons. Among them, Hahuribe no Kami let out a wild voice,

§ 2

"Oh, oh, this is terrible! I must go and help them,"

he said, being a flustered fellow about to rush headlong down Mount Tenkyō. Konohanahime no Mikoto, with a smile spreading over Her face, called out to Hafuribe no Kami, "Wait a moment!" Hafuribe no Kami, while climbing back up the mountain path he had begun to descend, rubbed up his nose with his right hand, stroked smoothly from the rim of his right eye to the outer corner of his left eye, blew his nose on the back of his hand, wiped that hand on his sleeve, and,

"Oh dear, oh dear, I have been truly impolite,"

with an ungainly face like a tadpole, lightly bowed his head in acknowledgment.

Tsukiteruhiko no Kami turned to Hafuribe no Kami and said,

"With such flustered behavior as yours, how did you cross the Heavenly Bridge?"

現代日本語訳

「おやおや、こいつは大変だ、助けてやらねばなるまい」

と、一目散に天教山を駆け下りようとする慌て者である。木花姫命は満面に笑みをたたえつつ、祝部神を「しばらく」と呼び止められた。祝部神は下りかけた山路を再び登って来ながら、右手で鼻をこすり上げ、右の目縁から左の目尻にかけてつるりと撫で、手の甲で鼻をかみながら、その手を袖で拭い落とし、

「これはこれは、まことに失礼いたしました」

と、おたまじゃくしのような不格好な顔つきをして、軽く目礼するのであった。

月照彦神は祝部神に向かい、

「貴殿のように慌てた挙動で、どのようにして天橋をお渡りになったのか」

原文（近代文語）

『ヨウヨウこいつは大変だ、助けてやらねばなるまい』

と一目散に天教山を駆け下らむとする慌て者である。木花姫命は満面に笑を湛へつつ祝部神を『暫し』と呼びとめられた。祝部神は下りかけたる山路をふたたび登り来りながら、右の手をもつて鼻をこすりあげ、右の目縁より左の目尻にかけてつるりと撫で、手の甲にてはなをかみながら、その手を袖にて拭ひ落し、

『貴下のごとく慌てた挙動にて、いかにして天橋を渡りたまひしか』

§ 3

When she asked thus dubiously, the Mikoto said without difficulty,

"Because I was gazing only in the direction of Tenkyōzan, I did not care in the least about what was at my feet."

Saying this, he baffled the many kami. Hafuribe-no-kami, with an unruffled face nonetheless, turned to Konohanahime-no-Mikoto and said,

"Ever since witnessing the disastrous state of the three realms, I have felt that I cannot place myself in ease for even a brief moment. Please permit me to descend the mountain as quickly as possible."

No sooner had he said this, without even waiting for the proclamation of Konohanahime-no-Mikoto, than he turned on his heel and was about to descend briskly once again toward Aokigahara. Ashimahiko-no-kami, with a wry smile, said,

"Hafuribe-no-kami, is your behavior not excessively rash? You do not yet have the permission of the Ōkami; is arbitrary action not the very thing a divine person should most strictly refrain from?"

現代日本語訳

と不審そうにお問いかけになると、命は何の造作もなく、

「私は天教山の方ばかり見つめていましたので、足元などは少しも気にかけません」

と言って数多くの神々を煙に巻いた。祝部神はそれでも澄ました顔で木花姫命に向かい、

「私は三界の惨状を目撃してから、わずかの間も安逸に身を置くことはできないような気になりました。なにとぞ一時も早く下山をお許しください」

と言うより早く、木花姫命の宣示も待たず、踵を返してまたしてもトントンと青木ケ原に下って行こうとする。足真彦神は苦笑しながら、

「祝部神、あまりにあなたの挙動は軽率に過ぎはしないか。まだ大神のお許しがないのに、自由行動は神人として最も慎むべきことではないか」

原文（近代文語）

と訝しげに問ひかけたまへば、命は雑作なく、

『私は天教山の方のみ見つめてゐましたので、足許などは少しも気にかけませぬ』

と云つて数多の神々を煙にまいた。祝部神はそれでも済ました顔で木花姫命に向ひ、

『私は三界の惨状を目撃してより、一寸の間も安逸に身を置くことは出来ないやうな気になりました。なにとぞ一時もはやく下山を許させたまへ』

といふより早く、木花姫命の宣示も待たず、踵を返してまたもやトントンと青木ケ原に下りゆかむとする。足真彦神は苦笑しながら、

『祝部神、あまり貴下の挙動粗忽に過ぎざるや、未だ大神の御許容なし、自由行動は神人のもつとも慎むべきところならずや』

§ 4

...she called out forcefully to halt him. Hahuribe-no-Kami appeared back in his original seat, blowing his nose with his fingers once again. The deities burst into loud laughter all at once, and some even laughed uncontrollably, pounding their hipbones with their fists.

Konohanahime-no-Mikoto gathered the deities, bestowed a heavenly mirror upon each of them, and handed them face-covering cloths of purple, blue, red, white, yellow, black, and other colors, saying:

"When you divine mortals go forth into the two realms of the visible and the invisible to convey the divine words, you must always use these face-covering cloths; however, this does not apply within the divine realm."

So saying, she distributed the face-covering cloths of various colors to each deity.

Hereupon, the deities divided into eight directions to propagate the divine teachings of Nodatehiko-no-Mikoto from the divine realm, each disguising themselves to face the mortals on earth and deliver warnings.

At this moment, Mount Tenkyo began to rumble. The sounds grew increasingly intense from moment to moment. Konohanahime-no-Mikoto turned to the deities and said:

"The deities who are to propagate the divine teachings of Nodatehiko-no-Mikoto have already completed the most difficult discipline at the Golden Bridge and crossed the critical hurdle, so you shall not be deceived by evil spirits again. Mount Tenkyo's rumbling now grows increasingly violent by the moment because the divine spirit of Okami has appeared here from the realm of the fire ball; it is an auspicious sign that plum blossoms blooming all at once across the three thousand worlds will bloom, scatter, and bear fruit, distributing the seeds of Su throughout the world. Therefore, I shall now relocate to Aokigahara on the mountainside. Let the deities gather henceforth at Mount Himalaya, receive once more the divine teachings of Nodatehime-no-Mikoto, refine your souls, and thereby become perfect missionaries to redeem the earthly world."

So she solemnly proclaimed, adjusting her posture. Even Konohanahime-no-Mikoto, who was naturally graceful and overflowing with affection, possessed at this moment an awe-inspiring, inviolable dignity. Overcome by that dignity, the deities involuntarily knelt upon the ground and choked with tears of gratitude. The rumblings intensified from moment to moment, until at last a great explosion burst from the summit, scattering sparks into the sky and presenting an indescribable sight. Ah, what manner of divine spiritual activity will begin at Mount Tenkyo hereafter?

と言いながら各神に各色の被面布を渡された。

ここに神々は八方に手分けをしながら、神界より野立彦命の神教を宣伝するために、各自変装して地上の神人に向かい、警告を与えることとなった。

このとき天教山は鳴動し始めた。音響は刻一刻と強烈になった。木花姫命は神々に向かい、
