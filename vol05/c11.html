Chapter 11: The Mirage [211]

第11章　蜃気楼〔211〕

§ 1

Banko-ōkami and the gods beneath him determined the suddenly appeared palace of Armenia to be their eternally unchanging dwelling place, decided their respective chambers, arranged the eight million deities, and came to conduct divine administration. Whether falling from heaven or welling up from the earth, before anyone knew it, peerlessly majestic palaces and numerous buildings had been erected. In order to celebrate Banko's divine governance, the gods entered the near and distant mountains, cut down various rare flowering trees and brought them back, and shouldering them, they sang amusing songs centered around the palace, dancing wildly while intoxicated with sake.

At that time, in midair, without knowing which deity's voice it was,

"Armenia, Armenia!"

—the shouting voice was heard incessantly. Without prior agreement, the gods looked up in the direction of the voice. Countless divine armies numbering hundreds and thousands equipped their armaments, rode upon clouds, and lined up in midair; in their center, the divine figure of the Earth Ancestor Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto appeared, directing the divine army while waving a command baton. Struck by that majesty, the gods immediately prostrated themselves upon the ground. Somehow feeling moisture over their entire bodies, their drunkenness cleared from sheer surprise, and they all began to tremble at once, shivering like the grandchild of an earthquake. § 2

"Blind deities, look at your feet!"

—he declared in a voice as if striking down upon their heads. When all of them looked at their feet in astonishment, once again Banko-daijin and the myriad deities were writhing in the muddy field, smeared with mud. Thinking that this was the palace of Armenia, they all looked toward the palace at once, but what had appeared until then to be a splendid palace was a mirage. Before their eyes, the palace soared up into the heavens, and even their own figures soared up into the air. Hachiō-ōkami and all the principal divine generals were seen ascending into the heavens together with the mirage. The deities left behind lost their wits in extreme shock and began wandering in all directions through the muddy field. In reality, Banko-daijin and Hachiō-ōkami only had their shadows reflected in the heavens; they still remained fallen into the deep mud up to their breasts, suffering and unable to move. Yet the numerous deities believed that Banko-daijin and all the divine generals beneath him had ascended into the heavens, and running about in confusion through the muddy field, they trampled upon Banko-daijin, Hachiō-ōkami, and the other divine generals beneath them with muddy feet, and all at once,

"Boohoo, boohoo!"

—they did nothing but weep.

At this time, stirring up black clouds from the direction of the Ural Mountains and illuminating the air as it advanced, an eight-headed, eight-tailed giant serpent appeared. The figures of the divine generals under Kunitokotachi no Mikoto, which had until now been appearing in one part of the heavens, vanished unnoticed, and the eight-headed, eight-tailed giant serpent, spitting fire, enveloped the entire sky with black clouds as if ink had been poured out.

と頭から叩きつけるような声で言い放った。誰もが驚いて足元を見ると、またしても泥田の中に盤古大神をはじめ、八百万の神々は泥まみれになって、のたくっていた。ここはアルメニアの宮殿だと、誰もが思って宮殿の方を一斉に見やると、今まで立派な宮殿と見えていたのは蜃気楼であった。見る見る天上に宮殿は舞い上がり、自分たちの姿までも空中に舞い上がってしまった。八王大神をはじめ、主だった神将は残らず蜃気楼とともに天上に昇っているのが見える。残された神々は正気を失い驚きのあまり、四方八方に泥田の中をうろつき始めた。その実、盤古大神も八王大神も天上に影が映っているだけで、依然として深い泥田に乳のあたりまで落ち込み、身動きもならず苦しんでいた。しかし数多くの神々は、盤古大神以下の神将が残らず天上に昇ったものと思い、右往左往に泥田を走り回り、盤古大神や八王大神以下の神将を泥足で踏みつけ、一斉に、

「オイオイ」

と泣くばかりである。

この時、ウラル山の方面より黒雲を巻き起こし、空中を照らし進み来る八頭八尾の大蛇が現れた。今まで国治立尊以下の神将が天の一方に現れていたその姿はいつしか消え失せ、八頭八尾の大蛇が火を噴きつつ、天一面に墨を流したかのような黒雲をもって包んでしまった。

原文（近代文語）

『盲神ども、足もとを見よ』

と頭からたたきつけるやうな声で云ひ放つた。いづれも驚いて足もとを見ると、またもや泥田の中に盤古大神はじめ、八百万の神人らは泥まみれになつて、のたくつてゐた。ここはアーメニヤの宮殿と、何れも思うて宮殿の方を一斉に見やれば、今まで立派な宮殿と見えしは蜃気楼であつた。見るみる天上に宮殿は舞ひ上り、自分らの姿までも空中に舞ひ上つてしまつた。八王大神はじめ、重なる神将は残らず蜃気楼とともに天上に昇つてゐるのが見える。残された神人らは性を失ひ驚きのあまり、四方八方に泥田の中をうろつき始めた。そのじつ盤古大神も八王大神も天上に影が映つてゐるのみで、依然として深き泥田に乳の辺りまで落ち入り、身動きもならず苦しんでゐた。されど数多の神人らは、盤古大神以下の神将残らず天上に昇りしものと思ひ、右往左往に泥田を走り廻り、盤古大神、八王大神以下の神将を泥足で踏みつけ、一斉に、

『オイオイ』

と泣くばかりである。

このとき、ウラル山の方面より黒雲を捲き起し、空中を照らし進み来る八頭八尾の大蛇が現はれた。今まで国治立尊以下の神将、天の一方に現はれゐたりしその姿はいつしか消え失せ、八頭八尾の大蛇の火を噴きつつ、満天墨を流したごとく黒雲をもつて包んでしまつた。
