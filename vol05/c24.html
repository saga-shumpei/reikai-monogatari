Chapter 24: The Floating Bridge of Heaven [224]

第24章　天の浮橋〔224〕

§ 1

As previously stated, the divine majesty of Utsushikunitama was manifested from the three-tiered golden hall of Ryūgū Castle, a golden pillar resembling an upright double-edged sword extended into mid-air, a golden bridge generated from its tip extended east and west with this pillar at its center, a silver bridge was generated slightly below to the left and right, and a bronze bridge was generated from its lower part extending east and west, covering the upper surface of the earth.

And before Zuigetsu, who was fascinated while watching with wonder as bodies were caught upon the hooks of golden, silver, and copper spiritual cords hanging from each bridge, and souls of the upper, middle, and lower ranks were rescued onto the golden, silver, and copper bridges according to the karmic affinity of each soul, or fell to the earth halfway, a silver spiritual cord descended, and no sooner was its terminal hook caught in the obi around his abdomen than he was pulled up into the air with dizzying speed. Overcome with dread, he instinctively closed his eyes, kept his mouth shut, and held his hands over his ears. Suddenly,

"Open thine eyes,"

a voice was heard sounding from above his head. When he instinctively opened his eyes at that voice, he had been swept up into the distant sky, and found himself made to stand upon the silver bridge. Upon the silver bridge, he saw divine men who had been hauled up here and there. All were looking down upon the earth in an attitude of fearful, utter tension. At this time, sounding from above my head,

"I am Kunihime-no-kami; I shall now bestow upon thee the divine name of Komatsubayashi-no-mikoto. Cling to this rope and descend once again to the earth, meet thy parents, brothers, friends, and acquaintances, recount the scenes of heaven, make them repent, and cause the straying divine men to adhere to the divine path,"

and as the words ended, a golden cord descended from above his head. And the form of Kunihime-no-kami was manifested only through voice, so that he was unable to gaze upon her. No sooner did he grasp the descending golden spiritual cord with both hands than, with a rattling sound like turning the wheel of a well-bucket, he was dropped straight down to the earth like a plummeting bucket.

Upon descending, he stood in an unspeakably vast wilderness. Here, there were no persons who seemed to be my parents, nor humans who seemed to be brothers or acquaintances; only packs of tigers, wolves, wild dogs, foxes, and tanuki were scattered here and there. Strangely, these ferocious beasts had built storehouses with white walls, or erected splendid gateways, and were living in beautiful, spacious houses. By no means could these be thought of as dwellings in which ferocious beasts, foxes, and tanuki ought to dwell. These were unmistakably houses where humans ought to dwell. Nevertheless, he was wondering why only such beasts were living there.

現代日本語訳

竜宮城の三重の金殿から顕国玉の神威が発揚し、あたかも両刃の剣を立てたかのような黄金の柱が中空に伸び、その末端から生じた黄金橋はこの柱を中心に東西に伸び、その少し下方からは左右に銀橋を生じ、そのまた下方部からは銅橋を生じて東西に伸び、地球の上面を覆ったことは前述の通りである。

そして各橋から垂れ下がる金・銀・銅の霊線の鉤に体を掛けられ、上・中・下の三段の身魂が各自の身魂の因縁によって金・銀・銅の橋の上に救い上げられ、あるいは途中で地上に落下する様子を、いぶかしそうに眺めつつ見惚れていた瑞月の前に、銀色の霊線が下りてきて、その末端の鉤が腹部の帯に引っかかったと見るや否や、目が眩むほどの速力で空中に引き上げられた。あまりの恐ろしさに、思わず目を閉じ口を塞ぎ、両手で耳を塞いでいた。にわかに、

「目を開けよ」

という声が、頭上の方から聞こえた。その声に思わず目を開くと、はるかな中空に巻き上げられ、自分は銀橋の上に立たされていた。銀橋の上には、所々に神人が引き上げられているのを見た。いずれも恐ろしそうに緊張しきった態度で、地上を見下ろしているのであった。このとき私の頭上から、

「我は国姫神である、汝に今より小松林命という神名を与えよう。この綱にすがって再び地上に降り、汝の両親、兄弟、友人、知己らに面会して天上の光景を語り聞かせ、悔い改めさせ、迷える神人を神の道につかせるがよい」

と言葉が終わるとともに頭上から金線が下りてきた。そして国姫神の姿は声のみで、拝することはできなかったのである。下りてくる金色の霊線を両手に握ったと見るや否や、ガラガラと釣瓶の車を回すような音がして、地上へ釣瓶落としに降ろされてしまった。

降りると自身は何とも言えない広々とした原野に立っていた。ここには私の親らしい者も兄弟や知己らしい人間もおらず、ただ虎、狼、山狗、狐狸の群れが所々に散在しているのであった。不思議にもこれらの猛獣は白壁造りの土蔵を建て、あるいは立派な門構えをし、美しい広い家に住んでいるのである。どう考えても猛獣や狐狸の住むべき住居とは思われなかった。これはどうしても人間の住むべき家である。それなのにどうして、このような獣類ばかりが住んでいるのかと、いぶかしく思っていた。

§ 2

At this moment, as the voice of Kunihimenokami,

"I shall give you this black cloth from heaven."

No sooner was this spoken than a black cloth fluttered in the wind and descended before me. I quickly took it and covered my face. When I looked through the black cloth at the herd of wild beasts, foxes, and badgers, to my surprise, they were all nothing but splendid human beings. I could not help but be astonished that even friends with whom I had been closely acquainted were mingled among them.

From that point on, I did not part with this black cloth for even a single moment. The reason was that if I removed this single obstruction from my eyes, they would revert to the forms of fierce tigers, foxes, and badgers as previously mentioned, which was truly so terrifying that I could not bear it.

While this was happening, once again a golden spirit-line descended before me from heaven. Just as before, the hook caught on my sash. This time, I quickly placed the black cloth into my bosom, and while firmly gripping the golden spirit-line with both hands, I was drawn up into the sky with the rush of a torrential surge, just as before.

After some time had passed,

"Open your eyes."

このとき、国姫神の声として、

「天上よりこの黒布を授けよう」

と言われたかと思う間に、黒い布が風にひらひらとして私の前に下りてきた。手早くこれを持って顔を覆った。黒布を透かしてその猛獣や狐狸の群れを眺めると、思いがけないことに、どれもみな立派な人間ばかりである。中には自分が親しく交際していた友人たちも混じっているのには、驚かざるを得なかった。

それからこの黒布を一瞬の間も手放すことをしなかった。その理由は、この目の障害物を一枚除けば、前述のように猛虎や狐狸の姿に変わってしまい、実に恐ろしくてたまらなかったからである。

そうこうする間に、またしても天上より私の前に金色の霊線が下りてきた。以前のように私の腹帯に鉤が引っかかった。今度はその黒布を手早く懐に入れ、両手でしっかりと金色の霊線を掴みながら、前回のように一瀉千里の勢いで上空へと引き上げられてしまった。

ややしばらくして、

「目を開けよ」

§ 3

The voice of a deity shouting this was heard. When I opened my eyes, this time I had been pulled up onto the highest point of the Ōgonkyō bridge. Relieved for the moment, when I looked around, Kunihime-no-Kami appeared smiling before me along with four or five attendant deities, and

"This bridge is called the Great Bridge of Gold, and also Ame-no-Ukihashi, the Floating Bridge of Heaven; metal qi ascended from the central fireball of the earth and became the jewel of the visible realm, and by the majestic virtue of this jewel, the Kuni-no-Mihashira pillar extended high into midair, its summit dividing into left and right, the left being the bridge to be crossed by male deities, and the right being the bridge to be crossed by female deities. This Ōgonkyō bridge is slippery, and if there is the slightest carelessness, one will slip and fall again to the earth, with the danger of bringing about destruction. Restrain your mind as though standing amidst drawn blades, and without carelessness for even a single step, keep your eyes watchful, listen intently, hold your breath, exercise every care, and cross this bridge toward the east. Furthermore, this bridge revolves in midair toward east, south, west, and north; with each revolution, the bridge body vibrates, and deities and mortals upon the bridge are in danger of being thrown off at any moment, and at times violent winds blow and sweep the deities and mortals off the bridge. Having neither railings nor foothold, though called a bridge, it is a golden log bridge and difficult to cross; yet if you do not cross it, you cannot become a pillar of God, and truly difficult is the duty of one who serves as a divine pillar,"

she solemnly declared.

Oni submissively received her instruction and, toward the east with unsteady footing, advanced step by step barefoot.

Suddenly, the Ōgonkyō bridge began to rotate from east to south. Truly feeling danger pressing close upon him, he almost completely lost his color. At this time, from the voice of a deity unknown to him,

"Be valiant, be resolute, and steadfastly carry out the divine command. God is behind you; never fear,"

such a voice struck his ears.

§ 4

As Oni heard this voice, fear and everything else were entirely wiped away, and he felt as though it were a clear breeze under a moonlit sky, with not a speck of shadow remaining in the heavens of his mind.

The Golden Bridge quickened its rotation more and more, circling through the midair exceedingly swiftly from east to south, from south to west, and from west to north, and returned again to its original east.

The eastern end of the Golden Bridge touched a certain high mountain. Looking, he saw this was the summit of the world's renowned Tenkyōzan. At this time, numerous divine beings, beginning with Konohana-hime no Mikoto, saw my form,

"Urō, urō,"

and raising both hands, they shouted thus, expressing their welcome.

Before he was aware, Oni was standing upon the summit of Tenkyōzan together with the deities. The Golden Bridge had, unnoticed, shifted its direction toward the southeastern corner.

Just then, feeling a pain as though his cheeks and nose were being torn off by the cold of the blizzard blowing across the mountain, he was struck by the violent wind and fell upon the summit; at the moment he struck his forehead, in the very instant he thought flashes of fire had burst from both his eyes, Oni was sitting quietly in the rock cave of Takakumayama, striking his forehead against a corner of the rock.

「ウロー、ウロー」

と両手を挙げて叫び、歓迎の意を表された。
