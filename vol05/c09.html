Chapter 9: The Hot Spring of the Crane [209]

第9章　鶴の温泉〔209〕

§ 1

Returning the narrative a little backwards, Tokoharuhiko had fallen from atop the Bōiwa rock, having his horns broken and dripping with fresh blood so that his entire body appeared as if clad in scarlet robes; but he was thrown into the great river of Eden by Onitakehiko, and on that occasion the blood was completely washed away, and with a pale face, he ran unsteadily into a valley toward the south. Just then, in a deep ravine between the mountains, he saw a flock of cranes, numbering countless thousands, soaring about.

When he approached, panting, he saw many cranes dancing and playing around a single, exceptionally beautiful female deity. As he looked, there was a clear hot-spring pool, and hot water was gushing forth copiously. In that natural bath, the woman dipped in and out, treating the wounded places on her body. Looking closely, it was Shiojihime, who never left his mind. Wondering how Shiojihime, who ought to have been harmoniously attending the banquet with Nanten'ō at Ken'onkyō right now, could have come to such a mountainous place, he knit his brows in doubt and stared blankly at her face.

The princess beckoned to Tokoharuhiko,

"You, too, please enter this bath,"

she signaled. Without a moment's hesitation, Tokoharuhiko stripped stark naked and leaped into this bath. Miraculously, the wound on his forehead completely healed, his horns were gone, and he became a deity of truly magnificent divine stature. Shiojihime, with an expression of great joy, here pledged the vows of husband and wife.

In the sky above, countless thousands of cranes were soaring to and fro as before. Tokoharuhiko rejoiced that his desire had been fulfilled, and for a while continued his recuperation focused on this hot spring; day by day his body tended toward refreshing health, and the two finally promised to join hands and return to the Holy Land. Suddenly, a single crane flew down from the sky above, pecked Tokoharuhiko's forehead about twice with its long beak, and pierced a hole. Tokoharuhiko was startled, placed both hands upon the wound, and hung his head, enduring the pain. The pain grew increasingly intense.

§ 2

Postponing their departure once again, it was decided to leap into the bathtub and recuperate. Day by day, the wounds healed. However, after that, he felt extreme itchiness. Tokoharuhiko scratched desperately. No matter how much he scratched, the itchiness did not cease. At last, it became painful and itchy, and grew beyond help. In an instant, horns like bamboo shoots grew out on both sides once more. Seeing these horns lengthen day by day, Shioharuhime rejoiced, completely unlike before. And stroking the horns around, or licking them, she praised in the highest terms how splendid the horns were. Tokoharuhiko, who had until now felt ashamed of these horns, became proud upon being praised by his beloved wife, and felt inclined to wait for the horns to grow splendidly day by day.

Crossing mountains and following valleys, they were finally able to return to the Holy Land. At the main gate of the Holy Land Jerusalem, Ojimawake, with white hair hanging behind him and a thin beard hanging down to his chest, accompanied by Tayorihiko and other divine people, stood solemnly on guard. At this time, as Tokoharuhiko took Shioharuhime by the hand and was about to enter the gate joyfully, Ojimawake,

"Suspicious person, halt for a moment!"

called out to stop them. The two became greatly enraged, and

"We were in the palace of Eden, and after tasting various hardships and trials from there, have finally returned here, yet you, as a mere subordinate retainer, not only fail to welcome us, but speak insolent abuse against us; as of today you shall be dismissed from your post as gate guard and made to depart for the Land of Roots!"

§ 3

"Villainous shape-shifter, learn your lesson!"

So saying, they struck at him. The horns on Tokoharuhiko's head had gradually grown and become forked. Laughing, Tokoharuhiko parried the club swung by Kojimawake with the tips of his horns, and aiming at the abdomen of Tayorihiko on one side and that of Kojimawake on the other, thrust through them with the tips of his horns.

The two collapsed there with their entrails gouged out, and

"All is lost!"

squeezed out this cry. Startled by this cry, numerous divine people rushed and gathered together, and seeing this state of affairs, became greatly enraged, attempting from all directions to slash and strike at Tokoharuhiko with naginata, clubs, and various other weapons. Mikoto's horns gradually grew sharply pointed, and before their very eyes, lengthened. The sacred ground was just like a field of carnage.

Upon the attendant's urgent report, Tokoyohiko appeared at this spot together with Tokoharuhiko and Shiojihime. At this time, the Tokoharuhiko who was inside the palace had also gradually developed horns on his head, bearing not the slightest difference from the second Tokoharuhiko who had now appeared here. It amounted to two deities of Shiojihime of the same appearance, and two deities of Tokoharuhiko of the same appearance.

The former and latter Tokoharuhiko and Shiojihime mingled with one another, so that distinguishing the true from the false became impossible. However, the point that differed slightly was the pattern of their clothing. Tokoyohiko left the scene here to take its course, and taking the hands of the former Tokoharuhiko and Shiojihime, disappeared deep into the inner palace.
