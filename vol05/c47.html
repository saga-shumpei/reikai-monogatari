Chapter 47: Amending Words, Rectifying Faults [247]

第47章　改言改過〔247〕

§ 1

Uralhiko and Uralhime temporarily grasped the divine realm of the earth at their will, with no one to rival their power and influence, and finally expelled Banko Shin'ō, installed themselves in that position, and came to call themselves Banko Shin'ō.

Banko Shin'ō sought to recover Tokoyo Castle once again, led numerous valiant divine beings, crossed the great ocean to attack the Land of Tokoyo, and pressed Tokoyo Shin'ō for submission. Tokoyo Shin'ō, Ōtakawake, and others saw through the fact that he was not the true Banko, rejected the demand flatly, and suddenly made military preparations and set about preparing for defense.

Here, hostilities between the two armies opened most fiercely. Heaven shook and earth trembled, turning into a dreadful shambles as if a storm, raging waves, and a hundred thunders were roaring all at once. The divine generals and divine soldiers on earth followed either Tokoyo Shin'ō or Banko Shin'ō, sparks scattering to the utmost, and battles began in various places.

Just at that time, the continuous daily rain became increasingly intense, the gale was terrible, and finally the giant waves of the Pacific Ocean licked the land, leading Tokoyo Castle to the point of sinking into the water. Thereupon, Banko Shin'ō attempted to turn back his demonic army for the time being and return to Mount Ural. However, the sea waves were high and the gale raged, so that they could not advance even a single step. Even the souls of the vicious giant serpent and the evil spirits of the golden fox had no means whatever to deal with this.

Generally, evil deities exert their violent power during times of peace and safety, but against the calamities of cosmic cataclysms brought about by the wrath of the deities of heaven and earth, they possess not the slightest resistance, falling into such miserable circumstances as when a dragon, having lost its time, becomes a newt or an earthworm and conceals itself in the soil or in the water. In contrast to this, good deities of utmost sincerity and truth increase their courage whenever a hardship arrives, divine power finally surges upon them like a tide, and they perform great deeds that shake heaven and earth.

Heaven roared, earth shook violently, tsunamis pressed in incessantly, and now Tokoyo Castle was about to sink beneath the water. Tokoyo Shin'ō was greatly astonished, worshiped heaven and earth, recited the Amatsu Norito, and facing toward the direction of Mount Tenkyō high in the northeastern sky,

"The plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds, The elder-brother flowers blooming all at once— Does the living deity who saves this world Dwell on Mount Tenkyō? Ah, how grateful, how noble! Does the living deity who teaches this world Dwell on the mountain of Chikyō? May the Land of Tokoyo Revere the divine virtue Of Takateruhime no Mikoto whose august authority is high. Even though Tokoyo Castle may sink, Even though we may drown in the water and die, This soul bestowed by the deities, This soul alone, eternally Please save, O Mototsu-mikami, O Sumegami of heaven and earth!"

Thus he chanted the hymn of praise. Instantly that heavenly bridge appeared in midair, and no sooner was the hook of silver wire seen to touch various parts of the bodies of Tokoyo Shin'ō, Ōtakawake, and the other awakened deities, than the bodies of the various deities were hauled up into midair.

The greater part of Uralhiko's demonic army drowned in the water and lost their lives, while the rest entrusted themselves to all kinds of boats or boarded bird-boats, fleeing for their lives toward the peak of Mount Ural.

盤古神王は再び常世城を回復しようとして、あまたの勇猛な神人を率い、大海を渡って常世の国に攻め寄せ、常世神王に向かって帰順を迫った。常世神王をはじめ大鷹別は、それが真の盤古でないことを見破り、一言のもとに要求を拒絶し、にわかに戦備を整えて防戦の用意に取りかかった。

ここに両軍の戦端は最も猛烈に開かれた。天は震え地は揺れ、暴風、怒濤、百雷が一時に轟くかのような惨憺たる修羅場と化した。地上の神将・神卒は、あるいは常世神王に、あるいは盤古神王に従い、極力火花を散らし、各地で戦闘が開始された。

折しも連日の雨はますます激しく、暴風はすさまじく、ついには太平洋の大波が陸地を舐め、ついに常世城は水中に沈もうとすることに至った。ここにおいて盤古神王はひとまずその魔軍を引き返して、ウラル山に帰ろうとした。しかし海の波は高く暴風は吹き荒れて、一歩も前進することができなかったのである。さすがに凶悪な大蛇の身魂も金狐の邪霊も、これに対してはどうすることもできる手段がなかった。

およそ邪神は、平安無事の時においてはその暴威を逞しくするが、ひとたび天地神明の怒りによって生じた天変地異の災禍に対しては少しの抵抗力もなく、あたかも龍が時機を失ってイモリやミミズとなり、土中や水中に身を潜めるような悲惨な境遇に転落するものである。これに反して至誠至実の善神は、一難が訪れるごとにその勇気を増し、ついに神力が潮のように加わってきて、回天動地の大活動を為すものである。

天は鳴動し、地は激しく揺れ動き、津波がしきりに迫って、今や常世城は水中に沈もうとした。常世神王は大いに驚き、天地を拝して天津祝詞を奏上し、東北の空高く天教山の方面に向かって、

『三千世界の梅の花 一度に開く兄の花の この世を救う生神は 天教山におわしますのか ああ有難いことよ、尊いことよ この世を教え導く生神は 地教の山におわしますのか 御稜威は高き高照姫の命の神徳を 仰がせてください常世の国よ 常世の城は沈もうとも 水に溺れて死のうとも 神の授けてくださったこの身魂 御魂ばかりは永遠に お救いください天地の 元津御神よ皇神よ』

と賛美歌を唱えた。たちまち中空に例の天橋が現れ、銀線の鉤が、常世神王をはじめ大鷹別その他の目覚めた神々の身体の各所に触れたと見るやいなや、諸神の身体は中空に釣り上げられてしまった。

ウラル彦の魔軍は大半が水に溺れて命を落とし、その残りはありとあらゆる船に身を寄せ、あるいは鳥船に乗り、ウラルの山頂を目指して命からがら逃走した。

ここに両軍の戦端は最も猛烈に開始された。天震ひ地動ぎ、暴風怒濤百雷の一時に轟く如き惨澹たる修羅場と化し去つた。地上の神将神卒は、或は常世神王に或は盤古神王に随従して極力火花を散らして、各地に戦闘は開始された。
