Chapter 36: The Resonance of Kotodama [236]

第36章　言霊の響〔236〕

§ 1

"In the ancient of ancients, in those ancient times, the Great Kami Kuniharutachi sent divine pillars to the lands of the four directions in order to straighten the twisted and crooked souls of the kami and people across heaven, earth, and the four directions; and to make known the reconstruction of the world, disguising his form in countless ways toward the east, west, north, and south, through snowy mornings and rainy evenings, without shrinking from the fields where tigers dwell, blowing and spreading the words of divine salvation on the wind of Shinato; and at the dawn when they had spread into every corner of the lands of the four directions, the divine commands of the Great Kami Nodatihiko and Konohanahime, who appeared upon Mount Tenkyō, and of the Great Kami Nodatihime, who appeared upon Mount Chikyō—

bearing these proclamations upon the back cheerfully, the moon in the sky that ponders the state of the fleeting world, shifting as swiftly as a turning wheel; the inexhaustible wish is to restore the souls, the souls of kami and people, and to save them into the pure age of the kami, combing one's hair with the wind, bathing in the violent rain; the plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds blooming all at once, the divine age of the evergreen pine, of the evergreen trees; the blossoms of trees pure of heart blooming, scattering, and bearing fruit; while the kami who drift upon the white waves, having distributed the seed of Su to the four directions, are truly pitiful. Whether the morning sun shines or is clouded over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even should heaven and earth be inverted,

the earth overturning to become heaven, and heaven turning to become earth, what can be done? Harbor no grudge for a thousand autumns, for ten thousand years. Awaken to the words of divine grace, you myriad kami! Incline your ears and listen! Fragrant to hear is this divine plan of the eighth day of the ninth month, the long month; the chrysanthemum flower of today, the ninth day, when the nine flowers are said to bloom. Preferring dumplings to flowers, the present world is filled only with kami of the body dominant and spirit subordinate. The world is perpetual darkness; swept away by the raging waves swirling up in the Naruto Straits, souls fall down to the Root Land and the Nether Land, burned by the unquenchable fires of hell, or, with blades of ice, tasting endless hardships like a slug;

like meeting a frog, heaven and earth overturn like frogs, and a horde of serpents, wielding sword-tongues like serpents, are the evil kami of giant serpents. Possessed by those evil kami, kami and people who were originally born as pure souls of the Great Kami have, without realizing it, completely degenerated into body dominant and spirit subordinate; completely degenerated into body dominant and spirit subordinate. In order to save them from this disastrous state, led by Kuniharutachi no Mikoto, myriad kami gathered together on Mount Tenkyō, offering their sincere red hearts; yet the pure hearts of the kami who devote their sincerity also drift pitifully upon the waves of the Shiranui. The kami no mikoto of the Morning Sun, who illuminates the dark road, is hidden away;

when will the Heavenly Rock Cave open? This world draws near its end; this world draws near its end; demons, giant serpents, Magatsumi, and the ugly maidens and Sagume have found their opportune time, and it has become a rampaging world; it has become a rampaging world. Ah, kami and people, kami and people! Hear the voice of divine salvation! Clear your ears and listen well! Wash your eyes and look well! Wash your eyes and look well!"

§ 2

While chanting with an entertaining melody in a strange attire, there was a deity who chanted, surrounded by many deities. Hearing this voice, Hafuribe-no-Kami felt inexplicably encouraged and, together with Hafurihiko and Sugitakahiko, charged straight toward the voice while shaking his shoulders.

Swept up by the violent wind, all things on the earth were wretchedly scattered in utter disorder, and the deities, like mosquitoes or gnats caught in a gale, were whirled up into midair. However, only the deities who had settled their minds in their lower abdomen and received divine power did not budge in the least, like rocks growing out of the earth; calmly striding against the wind through the wide plain where the gale raged, they bravely chanted a propagation song. Carried along with the roaring resonance of the wind, that voice reached Takateruhime-no-Kami on Mount Chikyo. To the ears of Masumihime-no-Kami and Hafurihime-no-Kami, it sounded particularly poignant. Whose propagation song could it truly be? Needless to say, it was the propagation song of Tsukiteruhiko-no-Kami and Hafuribe-no-Kami.

When Takateruhime-no-Kami took out a golden gohei from the inner sanctuary and waved it left, right, and left toward the gale, the wind reversed, blowing violently from the northeast toward the southwest. At that time, the two divine messengers again composed a poem. That poem sounded subtle, like music, to the ears of their husband deities who were passing by the Shrine of Dust in the southwest of the Mediterranean Sea. Masumihime-no-Kami climbed the high pavilion of Mount Chikyo and began to chant in a clear, refreshing tone:

"Looking up, high in the distant heavens clearing completely, the beloved voice of the Great Deity Tsukiteruhiko-no-Okami is heard. On a rainy morning or a windy evening, with the sincerity that cares for this world, the voice of my lord resounds throughout all lands under heaven, and echoing far and wide, now here, it has reached Mount Chikyo, it has reached Mount Chikyo. Ah, how noble! The sincere resonance of the spirit of words resounds. Is this heroic voice thunder? No, it is not thunder, but the voice of God. That very voice must surely accord with the divine will to save the world, with the divine will to save the world. Here, revering the decree of the Great Deity, day and night,

desiring overwhelmingly to save the deities of the world, the sky of my heart becomes overcast, the sky of my heart becomes overcast. Seeking to purify this clouded world, pure in heart and pure in body, at the roar of the divine Mikoto Tsukiteruhiko, without a shadow of darkness, the plants and trees in all directions bend low, bowing to serve; of heaven and earth, the deities of plants and trees, and the upright deities of mountains and rivers, gather around you; the plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds, the words taught by the Ruling Deity, the plum blossoms that open at once the rock-cave door of clouded hearts. Whether the soul of the Great Deity Tsukiteruhiko shines or clouds, to the divine work entrusted by God

there shall be no evil deity that opposes. Ah, the valiant achievement of the divine Mikoto Tsukiteruhiko! Ah, the valiant propagation of the divine Mikoto Hafuribe!"

Thus she began to chant in a clear, refreshing voice. The wind, carrying this clear voice, delivered it to the two deities residing southwest of the Mediterranean Sea. Their courage multiplied a hundredfold, unyielding and undaunted in the face of such a violent gale, the two deities once again raised their voices, giving warnings to the deities of mountains, fields, rivers, and seas, and pressed forward toward the holy land of Jerusalem.
