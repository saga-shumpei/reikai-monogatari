Chapter 42: The Twin Purity of Spirit and Jewel [242]

第42章　神玉両純〔242〕

§ 1

Snow fell thick and fluttering, and the earth was a single world of silver. The sound of the bell of Mount Nankō echoed with the impermanence of all worldly things. Along the lonely mountain path announcing the dusk, in straw coat and sedge hat, the missionary came climbing alone, pushing through the snow. Seeking to save this cruel and pitiless floating world into the warm embrace of the deities of heaven and earth, forgetting his own physical suffering, the missionary Kamizumihiko came climbing up the cloud-piercing mountain. Mount Nankō was a sacred land under the jurisdiction of Ōshimawake.

Here, Kamizumihiko, seeking to save a divine person of his former acquaintance, in the pitiful disguise of straw coat and sedge hat to conceal himself from the floating world, finally reached the mountain summit, stood before the front gate, and pounded on the doors with all his might. The gatekeeper, who had been amusing himself in the land of dreams, had his warm slumber broken, and rubbing his eyes, came out with a sullen face, saying:

"Who is it that knocks at the gate in the dead of this especially cold winter night? If you have business, come tomorrow!"

Such was his utterly curt and unaccommodating greeting.

The deity Kamizumihiko, having no choice, stood outside the gate and at the top of his voice,

"Plum blossoms opening all at once throughout the three thousand worlds, Though Mount Nankō is high, It is still lower than the stars of heaven. Though Ōshimawake is great, How wretched to be bitten by fleas! And the weakling guarding the gate Of that flea-bitten weakling— Is he a privy bug or a dung beetle?"

Thus he began to sing. The gatekeeper, raising his voice angrily, shouted:

"In the middle of this night, what kind of bug is that weakling who, possessing the body of a mere wanderer, knocks at the snowbound and snow-covered gate begging for help? You fellow who blabbers asking Ōshimawake, who is weaker than a flea, to help you—you weakling who are weaker than the dung breeding from a flea's dung, unaware of your own station, reduced to tears and ending up as a beggar in your present state, I will never open this gate!"

Thus he roared from inside the gate.

This voice roared like thunder in the ears of Tamasumihiko and Yashimahime, who were sleeping in the bedchamber. The two divine directors had their dreams broken and sat bolt upright, calling out:

"Kumawaka! Kumawaka!"

When they called, the gatekeeper replied,

"Yes!"

and ran at full speed toward the bedchamber. Kamizumihiko, while standing before the snowy gate, raised his loud voice:

"Appearing in the land of Tokoyo, The ultimate fate of Princess Yashima, Drifting and drifting along the Eden River, At the end of her drifting, saved By the deity Michihiko-no-Mikoto, Hidden in the deeply benevolent Village of Ken'on, living out her days, Rescued by the attendant deity Of Tamasumihiko, who is whiter than snow, Now completely becoming his wife, Returning to Mount Nankō, The Bald Eight-King of Ōshimawake, Stripped of the rank of the Eight Kings, Now is merely Ōshimawake; Though the many divine persons of the world, knowing no reason, Revere him as a great deity, He is no true deity, Is he dark-faced tanned paper? Is he a wolf raging in the wilderness? Even the gatekeeper scents him out"

現代日本語訳

雪はひらひらと激しく降りしきり、地は一面の銀世界となり、南高山の鐘の音は諸行無常と鳴り響き、黄昏を告げる寂寥の山路を辿る簑笠姿の宣伝使はただ一人、雪を押し分けて登って来る。冷酷無残な浮世を天地の神の温かいその懐に救おうと、自身の苦しみもすっかり忘れ、宣伝使の神澄彦は雲を突く山を登って来る。南高山は大島別の管掌する聖地である。

ここに神澄彦は、旧知の神人を救おうと、見るからに寂しい簑笠の、浮世を忍ぶ仮の姿で、ようやく山頂に登り着き、表門に立って力の限り門を打ち叩いた。華胥の国に遊んでいた門番は温かい夢を破られ、目をこすりながら、仏頂面をして出て来て、

「殊のほか寒い冬の夜の、この真夜中に門を叩くのは何者だ。用事があるなら明日来なさい」

と、無愛想きわまりない応対であった。

神澄彦神は、やむを得ず門の外に立って声を限りに、

「三千世界一度に開く梅の花 南高山は高くとも 天の星より未だ低い 大島別は偉くとも 蚤に食はれる浅ましさ 蚤に食はれる弱虫の 門戸を守る弱虫は 雪隠の虫か糞虫か」

と歌い始めた。門番は声を荒らげて、

「この真夜中に、放浪の身でありながら、雪に閉ざされ降り積もる門を叩いて救いをねだるその弱虫は何の虫だ。蚤より弱い大島別に、助けてくれとぬかす奴、蚤の糞から湧き出る糞より弱い弱虫の、身の程もわきまえず、どうしようもない涙の果てには、乞食となった今の有様、この門を開けることなど絶対にできぬ」

と門の内側から怒鳴りつけた。

この声は寝殿で眠る玉純彦、八島姫の耳に雷のように轟いた。二柱の神司は夢を破られて、むくっと起き上がり、

「熊若、熊若」

と呼ぶと、門番は、

「はい」

と答えて寝殿を目指して一目散に駆け込んだ。神澄彦は雪の門前に立ちながら、大声を張り上げて、

「常世の国に現はれし 八島の姫の身の果は 流れ流れてエデン河 流れの果は道彦の 国の命に助けられ 恵みも深き顕恩の 郷に隠れて世を送る 雪より白き玉純彦の 従者の神に救はれて 今は全く妻となり 南高山に立帰り 大島別の禿八王 八王の位を奪りはがれ 今は僅に大島別 世の諸神人は理知らず 大神様と敬へど 誠の神に非ずして 顔色黒き渋紙か 荒野に猛る狼か 門番までが嗅ぎつける」

ここに神澄彦は、旧知の神人を救はむと、見るも淋しき簑笠の、浮世を忍ぶ仮姿、漸う山頂に上りつき、表門に立つて力限りに門戸を打叩いた。華胥の国に遊楽せる門番は暖かき夢を破られ、目をこすりながら、仏頂面して出で来り、

『殊更寒き冬の夜の、この真夜中に門戸を叩くは何者ぞ。御用あらば明日来られよ』

と膠も杓子もなき挨拶なり。

神澄彦神は、已むを得ず門外に立ちて声を限りに、

『三千世界一度に開く梅の花 南高山は高くとも 天の星より未だ低い 大島別は偉くとも 蚤に喰はれる浅ましさ 蚤に喰はれる弱虫の 門戸を守る弱虫は 雪隠の虫か糞虫か』

と門内より呶鳴りつけたり。

この声は寝殿に眠れる玉純彦、八島姫の耳に雷の如く轟いた。二神司は夢を破られて、むつくとばかり起上り、

『熊若、々々』

と呼ばはれば、門番は、

『ハイ』

と答へて寝殿指して一目散に駈入りぬ。神澄彦は雪の門前に立ちながら、大音声を張り上げて、

『常世の国に現はれし 八島の姫の身の果は 流れながれてエデン河 流れの果は道彦の 国の命に助けられ 恵みも深き顕恩の 郷に隠れて世を送る 雪より白き玉純彦の 従者の神に救はれて 今は全く妻となり 南高山に立帰り 大島別の禿八王 八王の位＠を奪りはがれ 今は僅に大島別 世の諸神人は理知らず 大神様と敬へど 誠の神に非ずして 顔色黒き渋紙か 荒野に猛る狼か 門番までが嗅ぎつける

Tissue paper, astringent paper, slave deities, Is it a wolf that spouts deities, Or if mistaken, the poverty deity, Or the wind deity taking advantage of weakness? In contrast to that, we are The descendants of deities without shame before heaven and earth, The deity Kamusumihiko! Unable to handle even a single sliding door, Merely in the status of a ruined deity, There is a limit to making a fool of others! O Tamasumihiko and Yashimahime, In the muddy rice field of the Tokoyo council, You were thoroughly deceived; know your shame! Though saying so, we too are Companions completely deceived By the slave fox of that same muddy rice field. Where is Tamasumihiko? Has the fox of Yashima not yet arrived? Kon-kon-kon cries the winter fox, Kai-kai-kai cries the winter fox.

Fox wedding, its tail is white, Its head is also white, the old fox!"

...thus he was singing at random from his mouth.

Tamasumihiko and Yashimahime listened closely, their hearts leaping at every single word, frowning and tilting their heads, intent upon this voice. Tamasumihiko strictly commanded the gatekeeper to open the front gate immediately, and had the singing divine person guided and brought to his own bedchamber.

Kamusumihiko no Kami, upon seeing the two priestly directors,

"Well, it has been a long time,"

...casually addressed them.

The two priestly directors were astonished and gazed intently at his face. Kamusumihiko immediately and casually tore off the black face cloth bestowed upon him by Tentowake no Mikoto, and thrust forward his black face before the two priestly directors as if to say, "Look at this." The two priestly directors,

"Ah, are you not Hachiō Kamusumihiko of Mount Seiun? What with it being the middle of the night, and what with this unexpected visit, we have been impolite,"

...thus in trepidation, the two placed their hands upon the floor and apologized profusely. Kamusumihiko, melting instantly like spring snow, opened his heart and replied that, in order to proclaim the divine revelation of Mount Tenkyō throughout the world, he had left Mount Seiun, endured frost and snow, and was traversing and wandering across mountains, fields, rivers, and seas to battle hardships and save all divine people.

The two priestly directors were greatly astonished, and while guiding Kamusumihiko to the inner palace and courteously entertaining him, they listened to the divine revelation of Mount Tenkyō with an attitude of reverence so as not to miss a single word, and praised his courage highly. Kamusumihiko earnestly explained and warned that the end of the world was drawing near, and pressed them to repent. Just at that moment, a clamorous voice was heard from the direction of the inner palace. And as the voice of their mother,

"Tamasumihiko, Yashimahime,"

...she was calling out.

Tamasumihiko,

"Excuse me for a moment,"

...saying thus, and having Yashimahime attend by his side, he hurried into the inner palace.

Looking, at the very moment when the evil deity of Arakawa no Miya, which had possessed Ōshimawake for many years, fled in fear of Kamusumihiko's proclamation song, the decrepit Ōshimawake, his body turning like ice, passed away into the unseen realm. From this time forth, Tamasumihiko and Yashimahime, deeply moved by the sincerity of Kamusumihiko, became proclamation messengers, and not to mention within the castle of Nantakayama, wandered across various regions, coming to spread the good tidings of God.

狐の嫁入り、尾も白く、 頭も白い古狐め。』

玉純彦、八島姫は耳を澄まし、一言一言に胸を躍らせ、顔をしかめ首をかしげて、この声に聴き入った。玉澄彦は門番に厳命してただちに表門を開かせ、歌っている神人を案内させて自分の寝殿に伴わせた。

神澄彦神は二神司を見るなり、

『やあ、久し振りです』

と無造作に言葉をかけた。

二神司は驚いて、つくづくと顔を見詰めた。神澄彦は、たちまち天道別命より分け与えられた黒の被面布を無造作に剥ぎ取り、「これを見よ」と言わんばかりに、黒い顔を二神司の前に差し出した。二神司は、

二神司は大いに驚き、奥殿に神澄彦を案内して丁重に歓待しながら、天教山の神示を畏敬の態度をもって一言も漏らすまいと聴聞し、かつその勇気を激賞した。神澄彦は懇々と、世の終わりに近づいていることを説き諭しかつ改悛を迫った。ちょうどその時奥殿の方で騒々しい声が聞こえた。そうして母の声として、

『玉純彦、八島姫』

と呼び叫んでいる。

玉純彦は、

『しばらく失礼いたします』

と言って、八島姫を側に控えさせておき、急いで奥殿に入った。

見ると大島別は、長年憑依していた荒河の宮の邪神が神澄彦の宣伝歌を恐れて脱出したその瞬間、老衰した大島別は、身体が氷のようになって帰幽した。これより玉純彦、八島姫は、神澄彦の誠心に感銘を受け、宣伝使となって、南高山の城内は言うに及ばず、諸方を遍歴し、神の福音を伝えることとなった。
