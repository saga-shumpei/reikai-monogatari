Chapter 32: Wave upon Wave of Tribulation [232]

第32章　波瀾重畳〔232〕

§ 1

The gods became dead drunk on the strong sake, to the point of almost forgetting that they were passengers aboard the ship. At this time, upon the song of Hafuribe no Kami beginning with "The Three Thousand Worlds, and so forth," for some reason the gods were stricken with pain as though their heads were being shattered with an iron hammer and their chests torn apart.

The night deepened profoundly, and it reached the hour of the ox when all things were silent and voiceless. The only sound that could be heard was the agonizing groans of the gods fallen unconscious in drunkenness. Just then, in the northeastern sky, scarcely had a cluster of black clouds appeared when, with the speed of lightning, it spread in all directions before, behind, left, and right; the entire heavens became as if ink had been poured across them, and on the sea it reached the point where one could not distinguish even a few inches before one's face. Suddenly a tempest arose; the sea surface that had been so peaceful now became a place where tigers roared and dragons leapt, and sea horses shook their white manes of waves and began to bite at the hull of the vessel. Scarcely had the ship seemed to be swept up into midair like a leaf when it was hurled down once again into the fathomless chasm between the waves; the mast broke, the rudder was torn away, the stern broke in half from its center, and having lost all freedom of navigation, there was nothing left but to abandon themselves to the mercy of the wind and waves.

The gods instantly sobered up, their faces turned pale, and they took on the exact appearance of hungry ghosts who had come from hell to fetch fire. In the pitch darkness where one could not even see one's own pinched nose, showered with sea spray, their shivering and trembling as soaked rats presented a spectacle far beyond merely crossing the line of death.

Suddenly, straight ahead, they saw a single beam of bright light radiating brilliantly. This was the light from the lapis lazuli jewel of Sugitaka, the retainer of Takasugiwake. Funado no Kami tried to turn the hull toward that light, but the rudder was ripped away, the stern was broken, the mast was fractured, and the sails were torn to shreds, leaving no way to maneuver, so that he could only look up to heaven and plead for rescue. The numerous gods who had put on a show of false bravado because of the sake were seized by terror, sighing in dejection like greens sprinkled with salt or leeches sprinkled with salt, bemoaning the bitter trial they met, and among them some even cravenly squeezed out weepy voices. Hafuribe no Kami, as if knowing this was the very moment, raised his voice in the darkness, shouting with a great roar that overwhelmed the sound of the raging billows:

"The plum blossoms opening all at once across the three thousand worlds, Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if Ushigumowake is swept away by the waves and perishes, Even if the gods who consumed the sake Become mere flotsam upon the sea, What of Naruto and the Seto Inland Sea, At the critical threshold of life, at the final hour, The grace of the Kami is as the white waves, Ye foolish ones, If ye hold life dear, apologize to heaven and earth; If your apology is accepted, I shall forgive you; One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, myriad, Wash cleanly away into the sea Your myriad sins and transgressions; Do not shed merely tears, Wash away both the grime of the heart and the sins of the body, weeping from deep within; Like the cuckoo for whom no day passes without crying, Vomiting blood for eight thousand and eight calls!"

夜は深々と更け渡り、万物が静まり返って声のない丑満頃となった。聞こえるものはただ神々の酒に酔い潰れて呻く苦悶の声だけであった。折しも東北の空に当たって一団の黒雲が現れたと見る間に、前後左右に電光石火の速さをもって押し広がり、全天に墨を流したかのようになり、海上もまた至近の距離さえ見分けがつかない状態に至った。たちまち暴風が吹き起こり、あれほど平和であった海面は、ここに虎が吠え竜が躍り、海馬は白い波の鬣を振って船体に噛みつき始めた。船は木の葉のように中天に巻き上げられたかと思う間に、またしても波と波の千仞の谷間に突き落とされ、帆柱は折れ、舵はむしり取られ、船尾は中心から折れて進退の自由を失い、ただ風と波の翻弄するに任せるほかはなかった。

神々は一時に酔いも醒め、顔は真っ青になって、地獄から火をもらいに来た餓鬼の相貌そのものになってしまった。鼻をつままれても分からない真っ暗な海上で潮を浴び、全身ずぶ濡れになって震えおののくその光景は、死線を越えたというどころの騒ぎではなかった。

たちまち前方に当たってひと筋の光明が赫灼と放射するのを見た。これは高杉別の従者である杉高の瑠璃光の玉の光であった。船戸神は船体をその光のほうに向けようとしたが、舵は引きちぎられ、船尾は折れ、帆柱はへし折れ、帆はむしり取られてどうすることもできず、ただ天を仰いで救助を乞うのみであった。酒のために空元気を装っていた数多くの神々は、恐怖心に駆られ、青菜に塩か、蛭に塩、しょんぼりとして辛い目に遭うことよとため息をつき、中には卑怯にも泣き声を絞り出す者さえ現れた。祝部神はここぞとばかりに暗闇の中で声を励まし、荒れ狂う怒濤の波音を圧するほどの大音声で、

『三千世界に一度に開く梅の花 朝日は照ろうとも曇ろうとも 月は満ちようとも欠けようとも 牛雲別は波にさらわれて死のうとも 酒を食らった神々は 海の藻屑となろうとも 何と鳴門や瀬戸の海 命の瀬戸際のいまわの際に 神の恵みも白波の 愚か者どもよ 命が惜しくば天地に詫びよ 詫びが叶えば許してやろう 一二三四五六七八九十百千万 万の罪咎をさらりと海に 流してしまえ 涙ばかりを流すのではないぞ 心の垢も身の罪も 流して泣かせて腹の中 泣かぬ日はない時鳥 八千八声の血を吐いて』

Vomiting even bile, such is the sorry state now; Turning to the gods in times of distress; Even so, unless you pray, you will not be saved; Yoito-sa, yoito-sa; Shooting in the dark, if you fire many times, you will hit; It matters not what it is, pray to the gods; Yoito-sa no yoito-sa; This is the brink of life or death; A single life in the Seto sea; A single pine on a single island; By the divine light of a single jewel, Illuminate your heart and reform; No matter how high the rough waves, No matter how strong the wild winds, In the divine light of the sacred island that has appeared, The sky of the heart calms completely, The sea path calms as well, the wondrous island; Yoisa, yoito-sa; The intoxication of the sake drunk since evening,

Dispel all at once the delusions of your heart; The end of delusion is the boat of enlightenment; Know that enlightenment is the boat of salvation."

Thus he sang without end and off the cuff. The countenance of Hafuribe no Kami could not be seen clearly on account of the dark night. Yet, from the manner of his singing, one felt as though distinctly seeing his features and the movement of his limbs as if in broad daylight. Suddenly, the bottom of the boat made a rattling noise. Looking, they had been washed ashore on an island. Ah, Kannagara tamachi haemase.
