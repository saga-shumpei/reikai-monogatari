Chapter 29: The Boundless Will of the Divine [229]

第29章　神慮洪遠〔229〕

§ 1

After Amadiwake no Mikoto, Tsukiteruhiko no Kami, and the other propagation deities beneath them were selected and assigned to various places, the light of the heavenly bridge of gold, silver, and copper that had been rotating through the sky suddenly vanished like a rainbow, the sky returned to its original azure, and countless stars, large and small, centered around the Milky Way, began to radiate brilliant splendor.

Just at that time, sixteen large stars with intense rays began to shine together in the northeastern sky. The color of their light was just like gold. Once again, the light of sixteen stars appeared together in the southwestern sky. The color of their light was like pure silver. Facing this anomaly, the divine people on earth either celebrated it as an auspicious sign of the Miroku sacred government, feared it as a portent of a great earthquake, or regarded it as an omen of a barren year, and their observations were divided, with none offering a definite judgment.

Suddenly the azure sky became dark as if ink had been poured over it, and suddenly the entire sky changed to a crimson color as if blood had been shed, or turned into a gray sky, or turned yellow; the cloud colors altering from moment to moment truly gave the divine people on earth a sense of swift impermanence. On the ground, a violent storm suddenly raged, felling trees, blowing rocks away, and injuring the divine people, and no sooner did an eerie aura shroud the earth than the sun with intense light and heat suddenly appeared in the east, west, south, and north; the heat was extreme, and just when it seemed that the plants, divine people, and other animals on earth were about to wither and die, a cold wind suddenly blew in, hail fell, thunder roared across the whole sky, resounding roars arose in various places, volcanoes near and far erupted, earthquakes and tidal waves occurred in succession, and there was no one who was not gripped by feelings of anxiety.

As if illustrating the saying "calling on God only in times of trouble," the divine people on earth, who had hitherto ignored the deities and forgotten the grace of heaven and earth, looked up to heaven with hands pressed together and prostrated themselves on the ground in supplication, their distress being in a state truly beyond description. The sound of the violent wind blowing through was like the roaring of savage beasts, the sound of the waves was like ten thousand peals of thunder crashing all at once, and it became a scene in which it was impossible to fathom when heaven and earth might collapse.

Such abnormality of heaven and earth required seventy-five days. At this time, among the divine people on earth, while there were those who feared God and sought salvation, there also appeared those who cursed God after losing their wives, children, kin, and property. Some among them became desperate and sang aloud the propaganda song composed by Uraruhiko no Kami,

§ 2

"Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness; After the darkness, the moon will rise. Gathering clouds for the moon, a storm for the blossoms; When the storm passes, spring will come. Yoito-sa, yoito-sa, yoito-sa-no-sassa."

And gods who became reckless and danced in madness appeared in great numbers.

In the first place, the occurrence of natural disasters and earthly prodigies for seventy-five days was the divine plan of the Great Deity of the Sun and the Great Deity of the Moon, beginning with Nodachihiko-no-Kami and Nodachihime-no-Kami, to test the souls of the earthly divine humans. At this time, the true divine humans who knew the genuine grace of the sun and moon and felt the virtue of the great earth were in a situation where they did not even amount to one in a thousand.

Ōkunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, seeing this scene, wept tears of great sorrow.

"Ah, has the earthly world, formed as the result of my billions of years of hardships and tribulations, become so defiled and clouded? How can I purify and wipe clean this earth, and reconstruct it into the divine pure land that was my original ideal?"

Thus, for one night, He wept tears of sorrow. When the Great Deity breathed a sigh, that breath became a violent storm that raged through heaven and earth, bringing down all creation. When the Great Deity wept in sorrow, it immediately turned into heavy rain that poured down upon the earth, causing disasters of flooding in various places.

Seeing this tragic state, the Great Deity could not even weep openly; He shed tears inside His body and kept His sighs within, patiently enduring for as long as hundreds of thousands of years in order to reduce the disasters on earth even a little. The bag of patience of Ōkunitokotachi-no-Mikoto was already filled with sighs and tears, and fell into a state where it was impossible to foresee when it might burst and erupt outside His body.

Yet the Great Deity of supreme benevolence and supreme love, out of His supreme affection that pitied all creation in the universe, suppressed His bodily suffering, endured well, bore it well, and thereby waited for the time when the earthly divine humans would undergo a fundamental reform. However, the divine tears and sighs of lamentation that filled His belly could no longer be contained, and were verging on the crisis of erupting and bursting forth at once at the slightest emotion. Ah, the space between heaven and earth of the universe was truly approaching the brink of a hairbreadth crisis from moment to moment.

Because the Great Deity layered patience upon patience over many years, the breath of His anxiety faintly leaked from His nose and mouth, became giant comets, and was released into the infinite great universe. With each breath, one giant comet appeared, and in the twinkling of an eye, hundreds of thousands of comets appeared in various parts of the universe, and gradually their light became faint and vanished into the universe.

そもそも七十五日間の天災地妖があったのは、野立彦神、野立姫神をはじめ、日の大神、月の大神が地上神人の身魂を試される御経綸であったのである。このとき真の月日の恩を知り、大地の徳を感得した誠の神人は、千人のうち一人にも及ばない形勢であった。

大国治立尊は、この光景を見て大いに悲嘆の涙に暮れられた。

「ああ、私の数十億年の艱難辛苦の結果として成り立った地上の世界は、これほどまでに汚れ、かつ曇ってしまったのか。いかにしてこの地上を修祓し、払拭し、最初の私の理想であった神国浄土に改造しようか」

と一晩悲嘆の涙に暮れられた。大神が吐息を吐かれる時は、その息は暴風となって天地を吹き荒れ、森羅万象を倒壊させるのである。大神が悲嘆に暮れて落涙される時は、たちまち強い雨となって地上に降り注ぎ、各地に氾濫の災害をもたらすことになるのである。

大神はこの惨状を見られて、泣くに泣けず、涙を体内に流し、吐息を体内に漏らして、地上の災害を少しでも軽減させようと、耐え忍ばれることが幾十万年の長きにわたったのである。大国治立尊の堪忍袋は、もはや吐息と涙で満たされ、いつ破裂して体外に勃発するかも予測できない状態となった。

しかし至仁至愛の大神は、宇宙万有を憐れまれる至情から、身の苦しさを抑え、よく耐え、よく忍び、それによって地上の神人が根本的に革正する時機を待たれるのである。しかし御腹の内に満ち満ちた神の涙と慨嘆の吐息は、もはや包み隠す手だてがなく、少しの感情の高まりにも一時に勃発・破裂する危機に瀕しつつあった。ああ、宇宙の天地の間は、実に危機一髪の境地へ刻一刻と迫りつつある。

大神が長年にわたり忍耐に忍耐を重ねられたことから、その御煩慮の息は、鼻や口からかすかに漏れて大彗星となり、無限の大宇宙の間へと放出されたのである。一息ごとに一つの大彗星となって現れ、またたく間に宇宙の間に数十万の彗星が宇宙の各所に現れ、次第にその光は薄くなって宇宙に消滅した。

Yet that gaseous matter of malicious vapor dispersed throughout the universe, eventually accumulated to fill the great universe with insidious air, and all living creatures had their health impaired, thereby unwittingly shortening their lifespans. Therefore, while divine beings in ancient times maintained a natural lifespan of at least several thousand years, and the longest reached hundreds of thousands of years, these lifespans gradually shortened, leading to the disastrous state where even human beings, the supreme creatures who preside over the administration of heaven and earth, can barely sustain a lifespan of a mere hundred years.

Ah, on what day will that sacred era of the advent of Miroku come, wherein beings maintain immeasurable lifespans and endlessly enjoy supreme peace and good governance? When we behold the divine intentions of Ōkami in the spiritual realm and His benevolence, we cannot help but feel ten thousand pecks of tears welling up.

In the Divine Revelation,

"The longed-for world awaited with yearning does not arrive; rather, the age of the Latter Days of the Law has arrived and stands at the gate,"

so did Ōkunitokotachi no Mikoto express His thoughts, and we must deeply contemplate His sacred intentions.
