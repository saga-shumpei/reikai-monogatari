Chapter 13: The Episode of Divine Possession [213]

第13章　神憑の段〔213〕

§ 1

The six-faced, eight-armed fierce deity who had descended from the northeastern sky led numerous subordinates and appeared at this place where Banko-ōkami and those beneath him, pressed by starvation, their bodies emaciated and exhausted as though they were boiled leeks, were unable to move freely; thrusting and striking the exhausted and suffering deities with iron clubs, they perpetrated every conceivable cruelty and violence, subjecting them to a treatment akin to grinding miso with a pestle. Just as the divine beings beneath Banko-ōkami had lost all power of resistance and defense, falling into a perilous plight where only one in ten could survive in a hairsbreadth crisis, suddenly a violent wind arose once more, and a rain of rocks beat down upon the horde of wicked demons. In utter panic and confusion, the numerous demons were struck by the rocks falling like rain, having their heads smashed, their hipbones crushed, and their legs broken; in a wretched state, each clutching the injured demons under their arms, they fled toward the northeastern sky, vanishing like mist into clouds. Yet strangely enough, not a single divine being under Banko-ōkami was injured. They all looked at one another's faces, merely dumbfounded by the exceedingly bizarre spectacle before their eyes.

At this time, no sooner had a gust of wind been seen blowing with a swift whoosh than Banko-ōkami, Tokoyohiko, and Tokoyohime, who had been lying prostrate upon the earth in agony, suddenly took on a reddish complexion; their vitality swiftly recovered, and standing up, while clasping both hands together, they shook their bodies up, down, left, and right, leaping up and running about far and near like madmen. This was because the evil spirits of the Eight-Headed, Eight-Tailed Great Serpent and the Golden-Haired, Nine-Tailed Evil Fox, having discerned the moment when their minds and bodies were thoroughly weakened, possessed them all at once. One after another, the other divine beings likewise recovered their vitality, waving their hands, stamping their feet with rumbling sounds, and racing about far and near in every direction, their state truly resembling the panic when a hawk swoops down upon a flock of sparrows. Here and there, just as some were groaning "Un, un, ū, ū," there were also those crying, "Ya-ya-ya, yatsu-yatsu-yatsu, ka-shi-ra, yatsu-yatsu-ka-shi-ra, Yatsukashirayatsūno, o-ro-chi, orochi, orochi!" and divine beings who came to shout, "Ki-kinkinkinkin, mo-moumou, kiyukiyukiyubi, kikikin, momomou, kiyukiyukiyūbi, kinmōkiyūbi no ki-tsu-ne, kitsune, kitsune, kitsune, kitsune!" Meanwhile, on the other hand, there were divine beings shouting, "Ku-ni-to-ko-ta-chi-no-mi-ko-to, Ku-ni-no-to-ko-ta-chi-no-mi, koto!" and those blurting out, "Ke-kenzoku, kenzoku, ke-kenzoku, kenzoku, Ta-tsu-ya-ma-wa-ke-no-mikoto!" who clasped both hands together, hopping about and racing forward, backward, left, and right, in a situation that could be called the midday procession of a hundred demons. Tokoyohime abruptly stood up and said,

"O divine beings of my subordinates! I am none other than the split soul of the Great Deity of the Sun, Tamatsuhime-no-Ōkami. Regarding the relocation of the earthly Takamanohara to this Armenia at this time, it has been the divine plan of the Great Deity since the beginning of the world, and it has been divinely designated as an eternal, immutable holy ground. From this day forward, Banko-ōkami and his spouse must obey with all sincerity and devotion that which this person speaks. From this moment, the physical body of Tokoyohime is the living shrine of Tamatsuhime-no-Ōkami. Construct a magnificent palace as soon as possible, and administer divine governance in the divinely decreed land, un-n!"

Groaning thus, she fell prostrate toward heaven.

A black-hearted divine person named Tatsuyamawake, the eldest son of the angel Kototamawake of the holy land of Jerusalem, had harbored ambitions from beginning to end. For that reason, during the recent upheaval in Jerusalem, only his own faction skillfully escaped, and he had joined the camp of the evil deity Tokoyohiko. He had now also come to this Armenia, climbed to the middle slope of Mount Ural together with the deities, and joined the company fasting from food and water. Suddenly his body trembled, his complexion became like fire, and he was possessed by a spirit. Seiryūma, a dependent of the Eight-Headed, Eight-Tailed Great Serpent, possessed him and said,

"Ah, how grateful, how awe-inspiring! This very person is the Great Deity of the Sun and the Great Deity of the Moon! You divine beings, your heads are held too high, your heads are held too high; prostrate yourselves upon the earth, for there are particulars to be declared. Today is truly an auspicious day unprecedented since the creation of heaven and earth. If you open your eyes and look upon this person, your eyes will instantly be blinded. Deities who disobey the bidding of this person from now on, consider that divine punishment shall fall upon you on the spot! This person is unmistakably the Great Deity of the Sun and the Great Deity of the Moon!"
