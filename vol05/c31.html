Chapter 31: Vast Across Boundless Waters [231]

第31章　万波洋々〔231〕

§ 1

The deity of dreadful countenance, resembling King Asura and King Enra, opened his giant eyes and glared at the deities aboard the ship, saying,

"O deities, clear your ears and listen well to my proclamation!"

Thus he shouted down as though showering them from above his head. This was Ushikumowake.

The missionary on this side was the usual Hafuribe-no-Kami. Laughing casually, he said,

"One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, myriad, All the myriad kinds and multitude of things, In the grace of the Kami of heaven and earth, Not a speck of dust is left out. There is light even at the bottom of the sea; The mountain peaks and the river shallows as well, In this present world shining brilliantly, What do you think of it, blind deity? Like a blind man who has lost his cane, The painful retribution is right before the eyes. What strikes is punishment and the flesh of a blowfish. How pitiful is the four-legged deity, Raving and roaring without regard for its surroundings! The horned Ushikumowake, The very end of his fate is truly pitiful, The very end of his fate is truly pitiful."

Thus waving his hands and stomping his feet once again, all were dumbfounded by the boldness of his audacious behavior that disregarded all surroundings, and turned their eyes upon Hafuribe-no-Kami's entire body.

Ushikumowake appeared here as a missionary by order of Uraruhiko, who displayed a light like a firefly on the plain of Armenia. Gulping down strong liquor like an ox with wide-open mouth, he flaunted it before the many deities, saying,

"Sake is said to be the chief of all medicines; Will you not drink the water of life? If you drink, the heart is amused. This medicine of long life, enduring a thousand autumns and ten thousand years of joy! He who does not drink is the greatest fool under heaven. With a silly face, trembling and shivering, The green gourd that suffers in misery. If one drinks sake, the color of the face, Like the morning sun rising in abundance, Shines all across with pure complexion. On a night when the moon shines in the sky, The heart is refreshing, the spirit light and smooth; Smoothly and smoothly, matters Do not progress without sake. Sake is the parent deity of life; Could there be a child who knows not its parent? One who knows not its parent is an ogre-child; It is this sake that slays the ogre! As a relish for sake, pluck out The tongue of Hafuribe-no-Kami,

Make it into raw fish salad and eat it! You who spit out that it is dark, dark, Drink sake and see, you will turn red. A red heart is a godly heart; A red heart is a godly heart. The dark-hearted Hafuribe, Uttering pure red lies, Like having one's cooking pot stolen on a moonlit night of Tsukiyomi, With a dumbfounded face right before the eyes, It is amusing to see his crying face dry. Taking this as a relish, all you fellows, This sake that I now serve, Without hesitation or reserve, into pear-skin lacquered cups, Pour it and drink, drink, and once you drink, become drunk! When drunk, ramble on like winding a bobbin; whether the loom Can weave or cannot weave, I do not know! Ignorance is bliss, leave it alone, cast it away! An inch ahead is darkness; After darkness, the moon will rise. In such utter tedium, the cuckoo sings!"

§ 2

"Though the voice is heard, the form is not shown. Is the hidden form an oni or a demon? To an oni's prayer, I say, aversion! Do not even glance at those you dislike. Drink till you collapse on the sake you love. The owl that plotted in the evening, When we tap the bottom of the sack, Everyone's heart is the very same. Could there be any deity who dislikes sake? Rather than keeping a straight face with sour grapes, And straining with an arrogant look, Loosen the cords of your face a little. Just like meeting a Buddha in hell, Taste the flavor of this sweet sake. Get drunk, get drunk, drink till you collapse. If you get drunk, isn't that fine? Yoi, yoi, yoi-no-yoi-no-sassa, Sake, sake, sake, sake!"

Thus did he repeatedly set up defensive lines against the declaration of Hahuribe-no-Kami, brought out two barrels of strong sake from beside the seat, and scattered numerous sake cups throughout the ship.

The divine people purred in their throats as if mice were shown to cats, gathered saliva on their lips, and gazed enviously at the sake barrels. Among them, feeling like foxes shown fried tofu, some heavy-drinking divine people even appeared who did not wait for Ushikumo-wake to break open the lid of the barrel, but leaped at the barrel like hungry tigers. Suddenly the interior of the ship took on a spring-like cheer, and as the drunkenness took hold, the divine people began to beat the gunwales with their palms, keeping rhythm and dancing.

"When I go out to the beach thinking, 'Will she come? Will she come?' Even the wind in the shore's pines makes no sound. If we were to call upon the famed Otohime of the Ryūgū Sea, And have her pour the sake, how amusing it would be! How annoying is Hahuribe-no-Kami! How irritating is Hahuribe-no-Kami! Shall I smash his head with a ladle?"

Beating the gunwales tap, tap, tap, they finally struck their own foreheads recklessly with both hands and danced madly.

Hahuribe-no-Kami was originally fond of sake. He desperately yearned to take that cup, as if a hand were reaching out from his throat. The worms in his stomach welled up toward his throat, making rumbling sounds. Telling himself that this was indeed a trial of the gods, Hahuribe-no-Kami controlled his own mind, unconsciously bit his fingers, and finally, becoming enraged, bit off his own finger, whereupon he first came to his senses, and once again raising his voice, began to sing the song, 'The three thousand worlds shall bloom all at once, etc.'

と、しきりに祝部神の宣示に対して防御線を張り、座席の傍らから二樽の強い酒を出し、数多くの盃を船中に振り撒いた。

神人らは猫に鼠を見せたかのように喉をごろごろ鳴らし、唇に唾を溜め、羨ましそうに酒樽に目を注いだ。中には狐が油揚げを見せつけられたような心地となって、牛雲別が樽の鏡を開くのを待たず、飢えた虎のように樽を目がけて飛びつく酒好きの神人も現れた。にわかに船中は春めき渡り、酔いが回るにつれて、神人らは手の平で舷を叩き、拍子をとって舞い始めた。

「来るか来るかと浜へ出てみれば、 浜の松風、音もしない。 評判に名高い竜宮海の、 乙姫さまでも呼んで来て、 酌をさせたら面白かろう。 癪に障るのは祝部神よ、 癪に障るのは祝部神よ、 杓子で頭を砕いてやろうか」

ポンポンポンと舷を叩き、ついには両手で自分の額をむやみやたらに叩いて踊り狂った。

祝部神は元来、酒が好きである。喉から手が出るようにその盃を取りたくなった。喉のあたりに腹の虫が込み上げてきて、ぐうぐうと音を立てるのである。祝部神はこれこそ神の試みであると自ら自分の心を制し、思わず知らず指を噛み、ついには激昂して自ら自分の指を噛み切り、初めて気がついて、またしても声を張り上げて、「三千世界一度に開く云々」の歌を歌い始めた。
