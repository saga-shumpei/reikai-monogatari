Chapter 49: The Anguish of Parting Companions [249]

第49章　水魚の煩悶〔249〕

§ 1

Banko-shin'o, emancipated in both mind and body like an exquisite jewel, radiating an awe-inspiring majesty that swept all around him, headed toward the holy land of Jerusalem, maintaining the utmost sternness of manner, and, guided by Hinode-no-kami, arrived safely with several hundred attendant deities.

Here, the three brother deities, Mamichishiruhiko-no-mikoto, Aomorihiko, and Umegakahiko, welcomed them from the bottom of their hearts, jumping for joy as though parched beans had burst into flower, dancing with hands and feet without knowing where they stepped. On the other hand, although Kunihiko and Kunihime were the bodily parents of these three deities, they were dissolute and maliciously covetous, not observing the heavenly laws in the least, and their cruel deeds grew worse by the day and month; because evil and sinister vapors filled the surroundings, strange events arose again in profusion, and the realm was on the verge of turning into a world of darkness, it was no wonder that the three deities rejoiced and looked up to them as though having found light in a dark night. Umegakahiko led Banko-shin'o, Hinode-no-kami, and the others into a modest temporary palace, offered sake and delicacies to console their hardships from the long journey, and sang:

"The world passing into Tokoyo is black as a raven's feather, The shouts of ten thousand calamities Arise swarming like May flies, The pitiable state of gods and humans beneath heaven Treading along dark paths— To bring aid, Kunitokotachi-no-Okami Has caused the Sun-rising God who illuminates this actual world And Banko-shin'o, who guards the world unchanged for eternity, To appear, And to set up the palace pillars thick and firm In the earthly Takamanohara to govern The sacred rites of the divine age, From among the gods to Jerusalem Did the true deity, chosen above all, Appear—how thankful we are; In the joyful divine age of the Prince who knows the true path, In Aomori and in this garden where plum fragrance is pure, Three deities have appeared,

While keeping the divine laws, Firmly pounding and solidifying the foundation Of the original world, steadfast and eternal, To raise the crossbeams high to Takamanohara, To serve and govern, We addressed our beloved parents, Kunihiko And the ruler Kunihime, Yet our parents, whose hearts had grown wild, Turned a deaf ear to our remonstrances, Alas, how sorrowful! To illuminate and aid this world, Through the design of Hinode-no-kami, Banko-Okami has appeared, And if he governs, for countless ages The world will be forever peaceful; In the reign where the land of Urayasu Is peacefully ruled, pine winds blow, The leaves on the great tree's branches flourish, Cranes of a thousand years dance and play, A pine-tree reign so auspicious even to hear of,

A pine-tree reign so auspicious even to hear of, Its name shall echo as Takasago, Its name shall echo as Takasago."

Thus he sang to express their welcome. Seeing their three children rejoice from the depths of their hearts at the arrival of Banko-shin'o and, revering the Divine King, attempt to restore the holy land once more, the two priestly deities Kunihiko and Kunihime were displeased in their hearts, and sought to obstruct them as much as possible by using parental authority as a pretext. However, the three priestly deities could not bear to follow parents who defied the deities and threw the laws into disorder, causing their parents to heap even more sins upon themselves, nor could they sit idly by watching the calamity of all people beneath heaven; swallowing their tears, they adopted the stance of sacrificing family ties for the greater good, and, revering Banko-shin'o as the Supreme Deity and taking Hinode-no-kami as assistant, restored divine affairs and divine governance. Nevertheless, heaven and earth remained dreary and, as previously mentioned, overcast without clearing, and day and night a torrential rain poured incessantly like a waterfall with a rushing roar.

原文（近代文語）

盤古神王は、心身ともに解脱して玲瓏玉の如く、威風堂々あたりを払ひながら、聖地ヱルサレムを指して、最も謹厳なる態度を持しつつ、日の出神に導かれて、数百の従神と共に安着した。

ここに真道知彦命、青森彦、梅ケ香彦の三柱の兄弟神は、心の底より之を歓迎し、煎豆に花咲き出でたる如く、欣喜雀躍して、手の舞ひ足の踏むところを知らなかつた。然るに一方国彦、国姫は、その三柱の肉身の父母に坐ませども、放逸邪慳にして少しも天則を守らず、残虐の行動日に月に甚だしく、為に妖邪の気四辺に満ち、再び怪事百出、暗黒界とならむとしつつありし際とて、三柱は暗夜に光明を得たるごとく随喜渇仰したのも無理はない。梅ケ香彦は形ばかりの仮宮に、盤古神王を始め、日の出神を導き、酒肴を供へて遠来の労苦を慰め歌ふ。

『常世ゆく世は烏羽玉の暗くして 万の禍の雄叫びは 五月蠅のごとく群れ起り 天が下なる神人の 闇路を辿るあはれさを 国治立の大神は 助けむものと現世を 照らす日の出の神様や 万古不易に世を守る 盤古神王を現はして 地の高天原に宮柱 太しき立てて神の世の 聖き祭りををさめむと 神の中よりヱルサレム 選みに選みし誠神 現はれますか有難や 真の道を知る彦の 嬉しき神代に青森や 梅ケ香清きこの園に 三柱神の現はれて

聞くも目出度き松の代の 名も高砂と響くらむ 名も高砂と響くらむ』

と謡ひて歓迎の意を表したり。国彦、国姫の二神司は、三柱の吾が子の心底より盤古神王の到着を歓び、神王を奉じて、ふたたび聖地を回復せむとするを見て、心中快からず、極力親の威光を笠に着て、妨害を加へむとした。されど三柱の神司は、神明に背反し、律法を攪乱する父母を奉じて、ますますこの上に父母に罪を重ねしめ、かつ天下万人の禍を坐視するに忍びず、涙を呑んで、大義親を滅するの態度に出で、盤古神王を奉じて総統神と仰ぎ、日の出神を補佐として、神務と神政とを復活したのである。されど天地は暗澹として前述のごとく曇り霽れず、日夜覆盆の雨は、ザアザアと滝の如く降りしきりける。
