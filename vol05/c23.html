Chapter 23: The Sacred Cord of God [223]

第23章　神の御綱〔223〕

§ 1

As a result of the tyranny of Tokoyohiko, Tokoyohime, and their followers, the sacred ground of Jerusalem angered the deities of heaven and earth, strange phenomena occurred successively, disasters repeatedly arrived, and finally to Armenia, Yatsuō-daijin fled together with his subordinate deities, Eden Castle was also burned down, the Dragon Palace Castle was also struck by Shukuyūshi and reduced to nothing, the sanctuary of Mount Kanran rumbled, the three-tiered golden palace extended endlessly toward mid-air, extending in a straight line east and west at its upper end to form a T-shaped golden bridge, and the Golden Bridge also shook from beneath the earth, rose into the sky like a rainbow, gradually became faint, and finally vanished into the vast sky.

The T-shaped golden bridge began to rotate slowly from east to south, west, and north. And from various parts of the golden bridge, beautiful, thin, golden spiritual cords were generated here and there, hanging down to the surface of the earth just like the branches of a weeping willow. And at the end of those golden spiritual cords, hooks of gold, silver, copper, iron, lead, and other metals were individually attached. These are called the "August Ropes of God," and are also called the "Hooks of Salvation."

Kotofure-no-kami traveled through mountains, plains, cities, and rural areas without distinction of distance, braved the wind and rain, spared neither body nor soul, and began to proclaim the divine revelation of Mount Tenkyō in all directions. Then, upon each of the divine humans who rejoiced and yearned at that proclamation, gave thanks for the singular grace of the sun and the moon, and submitted to the righteous way, he placed the mark of the character "God" upon their heads. Yet, among those upon whom it was placed, as well as the rebels upon whom it was not placed, not a single one noticed this.

Seeing the golden bridge rotating in mid-air and the golden spiritual cords radiating from various places, the divine humans on earth at first found this strange, and taking it as a divine warning of a great upheaval of heaven and earth, driven by feelings of anxiety and fear in their hearts, they were joining their hands and kneeling in prayer toward heaven as if seeking someone's salvation. However, as the days passed and the months went by, there was no one who found this strange in the least, and just as the sun rises in the east and sets in the west every day, they held it in contempt merely as an ordinary phenomenon, their spirits gradually grew slack, and a tendency to ignore God once again arose.

At this time, Amenomichiwake-no-mikoto, Amenomanamihiko-no-kami, Tsukiteruhiko-no-kami, Iwatowake-no-kami, Tarumanahiko-no-kami, Hahuribe-no-kami, Ōta-no-kami, and other deities were proclaiming prophecies and warnings to the realm without cessation day and night.

現代日本語訳

聖地エルサレムは常世彦、常世姫らの暴政の結果、天地の神明を怒らせ、怪異が続出して変災がしきりに起こり、ついにアルメニアへ、八王大神は部下の神々とともに逃亡し、エデン城もまた全焼し、竜宮城もまた祝融子（火神）に見舞われて烏有に帰し、橄欖山の神殿は鳴動し、三重の金殿は際限もなく中空に向かって伸び、上端において東西に一直線に伸びて丁字形の金の橋をなし、黄金橋もまた地底から揺れ動いて虹のように上空へ昇り、次第に希薄となって、大空においてついにその姿を消してしまった。

丁字形の金橋は、東から南、西、北へと緩やかに回転し始めた。そうして金橋の各部からは、美しく細い金色の霊線が所々に発生し、地球の表面に垂れ下がる様子はあたかも糸柳の枝のようであった。そうしてその金色の霊線の終点には、金、銀、銅、鉄、鉛などの鉤が一つ一つ取り付けられてある。これを『神の御綱』ともいい、または『救いの鈎』ともいう。

言触神は遠近の区別なく山野や都・田舎を巡り歩き、風雨にさらされながら心身を惜しまず、天教山の神示を諸方に宣伝し始めた。そうしてその宣伝に喜び従い仰ぎ慕って、日月の格別の恩恵を感謝し、正道に帰順する神人には、それぞれの頭に『神』の文字の記号を付けておいた。しかし付けられた者も、付けられない反抗者も、これに気付く者は一柱もいなかった。

中空に金橋が回転し、金色の霊線が各所から放射するのを見て、地上の神人は初めはこれを怪しみ、天地大変動の神の警告として、胸中の不安と恐怖の念に駆られ、天に向かって何者かの救いを求めるかのように、合掌し平伏して拝んでいた。しかしながら日を重ね、月を越えるにつれて、これを少しも怪しむ者がいなくなり、あたかも日々太陽が東から出て西に入るかのように、ただの普通の現象としてこれを見下し、次第に心魂が弛み、再び神を無視する傾向を生じてきた。

このとき天道別命、天真道彦神、月照彦神、磐戸別神、足真彦神、祝部神、太田神、その他の神々は、昼夜の別なく絶え間なく予言と警告を天下に宣布していた。

§ 2

However, numerous divine beings were deceived by Uralhiko's body-over-spirit propaganda song and greatly welcomed this song, and divine beings everywhere, without distinction between mountain, field, city, or countryside,

"Drink and make merry, for darkness lies an inch ahead;

After the darkness, the moon will rise;

Though the voice of the cuckoo is heard, its form is not shown;

Is that which shows no form a demon or an ogre?"

「飲めよ騒げよ、一寸先は闇だ。

闇の後には月が出る。

ホトトギスの声は聞こえても姿は見せない。

姿を見せないのは魔物か鬼か」

§ 3

So they chanted vigorously, driven by the desires of wine, lust, and carnal passion; debauchery and gluttony prevailed, and the winds of licentiousness swept in all directions.

The divine humans upon whose heads the sign of the character "Kami" had been affixed as a result of Kotobure-no-Kami's painstaking edification were caught upon the "Hooks of Salvation" at the ends of the golden spirit-lines descending from the Golden Bridge in the vast sky; those dancing up into the midair and those being hauled up began to appear, numbering dozens each day. Among the eight million divine humans, the salvation of several dozen divine humans a day was a number that did not even equal a single hair from nine oxen.

The divine humans caught upon these hooks were the purest among the souls of the upper, middle, and lower ranks, and had been chosen by the deities. Even among the same hauled-up divine humans, those who stood erect with upturned faces, raised their heads arrogantly, walked upon the earth with noses held high, or strutted with shoulders swinging were hooked by their ears, noses, jaws, necks, arms, and the like, and could be seen feeling immense agony midway as they were pulled up. Furthermore, deities who bowed their heads, diligently applied themselves to their tasks, single-mindedly believed in the deities, and kept their eyes cast downward had the hooks catch upon the sashes at their waists and were wound up onto the Golden Bridge without the slightest suffering. In other manners, the conditions under which different parts of the body were caught depending upon the conduct of the divine humans on earth and hauled up onto the Golden Bridge were myriad. Among them, many appeared who, unable to bear the pain, just when only one breath remained, had their jaws dislocated, ears torn off, eyes blinded, arms broken, or noses bent, falling back down to the earth and forfeiting divine favor. Among them as well, those who lowered their heads, took pity on those below, bowed down to sit below other divine humans, and diligently devoted themselves to divine works were completely saved up onto the heavenly Golden Bridge.

At this time, upon the Heavenly Bridge, a second silver-colored bridge extended to the left and right just like the Golden Bridge, and from various parts of it silver spirit-lines were lowered to the earth; I saw that by the hooks at their ends, the divine humans of the "middle soul" were gradually saved and drawn up as before.

Next, a bronze-colored bridge likewise emerged to the left and right, extending east and west as before; once again bronze-colored spirit-lines were lowered to the earth from various parts of the Bronze Bridge, and the chosen earthly divine humans were drawn up onto the Heavenly Bridge as before; some were able to ascend completely, some fell midway, and some detached the ropes and hooks that had with great effort been fastened, and fled away from the spot on earth.
