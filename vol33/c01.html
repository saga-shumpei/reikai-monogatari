§ 1

The fish are lively and leap in the clear springs, truly the auspicious signs of the Takasago Island, which lives up to its name, are manifested before one's eyes in the four directions of heaven and earth. This very day, the Kami Susanoo no Ōkami has descended from heaven, appeared at the precious Kamiyakata of Argentina, and the youngest daughter of the eight maidens, the flower of spring of the youngest princess, the dew resting upon the buds of the tree flowers that bloom all at once, appears most fresh and clear.

The Kamitsukasa of the Sangokyō, the jewel of the dragon's jaw, receiving that divine virtue in his body, and shining as a Kami of salvation, the Kamitsukasa of Kuniyawake is made the master of the precious capital.

Betrothed to the youngest princess, to transmit the glory of Takasago to the four directions for a thousand and eight thousand generations, the heart-rejoicing Ōkami, facing the now-rising Amatsubi, joins his hands and prays, how noble it is.

The wedding ceremony was fast approaching this very evening, and Matsuwakahiko and Sutegohime, beginning with the many Kamitsukasa and employing the believers, were pouring all their strength into the preparations for tonight's celebration, all of them running about busily with joy written all over their faces, high and low in unity, dancing with their hands and feet, and in the midst of their great activity, forgetting themselves, Kuniyawake, who had not only failed to celebrate this occasion in the slightest but had exhausted the limits of body-master-spirit-servant, intended to marry the maiden of the Mizu no Mitama, the youngest princess, to muddy her pure Mitama, and to overturn the auspicious signs of Takasago Island, which had been built up with such effort, into a dark night where black and white could not be distinguished, and to reject, block, and break the contract for the sake of the Kami and the world, with the ferocity of a Yasha, he ran about here and there, with eyes upturned, face reddened, and mouth pursed, his steps rough, and he knocked on the mansion of Matsuwakahiko, who was busy to the point of dizziness with tonight's preparations, and the one who entered in a flurry was the usual Takahime-sama. Takahime called out from the gate in a sharp voice,

Takahime: 'Excuse me... I am the ill-fated Takahime who has taken the name of the one hated by everyone. I have something I wish to tell you urgently, so please, I wish to see Matsuwakahiko-sama, even if only for a short while. Please, I beg you to announce me.'

As they were thus arguing, Matsuwakahiko came running in a flurry from the inner room, wondering what could be the matter.

Matsuwakahiko: 'Oh, you are Takahime-sama. I do not know what business you have, but if possible, please visit me tomorrow. If that is inconvenient for you, I am thinking of calling upon you myself tomorrow, so...'

Takahime: 'In this time of emergency where life and death hang in the balance, leaving no room for hesitation, you cannot be saying such laid-back things. Since you are the ringleader, a great problem has arisen that must be settled here, even if it costs you your life. Regret afterwards will be of no use, you know.'

Matsuwakahiko: 'Oh my, I wondered what business you had, and you say it is a matter of great urgency. In that case, I have been extremely busy with preparations for my wedding and was just on my way to the mansion of Kotoyoriwake-sama, but I shall spare five minutes to hear what you have to say.'

Takahime: 'It will not even take five minutes. If you, Matsuwakahiko-sama, simply nod your head once and say... "Yes, indeed, you are right"... then everything will be resolved.'

Matsuwakahiko had already heard from Kotoyoriwake, Kuniyoriwake, and Tatsukuniwake about Takahime's obstructionist activities from the previous night, so he jumped to the conclusion that this must be the matter.

Matsuwakahiko: 'Takahime-sama... indeed, you are right, I shall do as you say... yes, yes...'

Nodding his head up and down repeatedly,

Matsuwakahiko: 'Farewell...'

Having said that, he slipped out the back door and, aiming for the mansion of Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto, fled as if vanishing into the clouds.

Takahime: 'Hey... Matsu, Wa-wa-waka, Hi-hi-hiko, why are you trembling so? Hiding in the inner room will not settle anything. ...It seems your conscience is pricking you, as you are shaking like a konnyaku and have hidden in the inner room. It is useless to do such a sly thing, like a flea hiding only its head in the seam of a futon while leaving its rear exposed. Today, I shall manifest the Hinode-no-Kami, my trump card, and invade. Today, I am not the physical Takahime. I am warning you in advance, Karl-sama, so do not complain to me later about criminal law or civil law.'

Saying this, she rushed toward the inner room with loud footsteps. Looking inside, there was no sign of Matsuwakahiko.

Takahime: 'Well now, where has he hidden?'

Saying this, she threw open the window and looked outside, where she saw the figure of Matsuwakahiko running for dear life, hitching up his skirts, heading somewhere along the back path. Stamping her feet in frustration, Takahime said,

Takahime: 'Argh, cowardly and irresolute Matsuwakahiko. If I do not settle him, this world will be nothing but a sea of mud. This is the divine work of the spirit world, one in a thousand.'

Saying this, she opened the back door and went chasing after Matsuwakahiko, tap-tap-tapping along.
