Chapter 24: Spring Autumn [939]

春秋〔939〕

§ 1

In the inner chamber of the Sōmu-yakata in a side area of Nishiki-no-Miya, Tōsuke was leaning against a kefusoku while having Sadahiko pour tea, appearing as if he were lost in deep shian.

At this time, it was Akihiko who slid open the omotedo with a rattle and came out.

Akihiko: 'Mosimosi Sōmusama, Hidekohime-sama says she wishes to see you for a moment, so please come without fail. Murasakihime and the two of them are waiting.'

Tōsuke: 'Yaa, you are Akihiko; that was gokurau,naa. If so, I shall go at once...... Sadahiko, I leave the rest to you.'

So saying, he left him behind and, together with Akihiko, went to the Kyōshiyuyakata, where he adjusted his reifuku and proceeded inside. Hidekohime and Murasakihime were waiting for Tōsuke's arrival, finding the time not coming fast enough.

Tōsuke: 'Hidekohime-sama, thank you for sending a messenger. Tōsuke has now arrived.'

Murasakihime came out to welcome him with isoiso steps,

Murasakihime: 'It was gokurausama. Please do pass through to the inner rooms.'

So she greeted him withoin and led him inside.

Hidekohime: 'Sōmusama, you have come well. Just now, the gomeirei of Tamateruhiko-sama and Tamateruhime-sama has been handed down, so a matter has arisen that I must convey to you. I am glad that you have come at once.'

Tōsuke spoke in a subotsuku tone,

'Kyōshisama, has some urgent business arisen?'

Hidekohime: 'Yes, it is not particularly urgent business, but according to the words of the two Kami-no-Tsukasa, you must henceforth take Akihiko in tow and hasten to the Yakata of the Saien in the Land of Fusa. Although the purport of the business is not known for today, it seems that Lord Susanoo strongly desires to have business with you, so I beg you to set out even a day earlier. In this regard, Tōsuke-sama, it seems the whereabouts of your child has become known,naa, and Taka—'

Breaking off his words, he suddenly stopped speaking,

'We have heard that he is present in Tsukushijima, which is called Takasago-shima, and indeed how medetō this is. Nevertheless, you have valued the office of the Sōmu and said that you would not even meet the child you lost thirty-five years ago, and Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime-samas were greatly impressed. People are one thing, but even down to the verge of birds and beasts, there can be none who do not yearn for their own child. They said the two Deities praised it very much, calling it an admirable state of heart, zoey.'

Tōsuke: 'Iya, this is already a shameful matter. I have no face to meet them. Until now, I have had a face like one eating a steamed bun in the privy, but now that such a thing has become patu, there is no longer any reason for me to remain in the seat of the Sōmu. While I was spending today in shian on how somehow I would like to offer my resignation from the Sōmu, I received the messenger of Akihiko-sama and came to inquire without taking even what I should take. In accordance with the command, I shall leave the sacred place an even earlier moment, follow the instructions of the Ōkami, and hurry to the Saien-yakata in the Land of Fusa.'

Hidekohime: 'Thank you for your immediate consent. The two Kami-no-Tsukasa-samas must also no doubt feel exceedingly satisfied. If so, may your journey be fortunate...... Since it is a distant place, please go with the utmost care...... Now, Akihiko, it is regrettable for you too, but please accompany Sōmusama and go to the Land of Fusa, ya.'

Akihiko: 'It is a gomeirei I would ask for even if not granted, and there is nothing as happy as this. When I think that I shall once again be able to have a close audience with the Ōkami, I have feelings that cannot be described in any way. Although there are indeed so many high officials, you have chosen even one like me. This too I attribute to your favor, and I offer my deep gratitude.'

Hidekohime: 'If that is so, please go and return in good kigen.'

Tōsuke: 'If so, I shall go and offer my farewells to Tamateruhiko-sama and Tamateruhime-sama.'

英子姫『早速の御承知有難う御座います。二柱の神司様も嘸御満足に思召すで御座いませう。然らば道中お仕合せよく……遠方ですから何卒お気をつけてお出で下さいませ……これ秋彦、お前も御気の毒ですが総務さまのお伴をしてフサの国へ行て来て下さいや』

秋彦『願うてもなき御命令、こんな嬉しい事は御座いませぬ。又もや大神様に親しく御面会が叶ふかと思へば、何とも云へぬ心持で御座います。能うマア沢山の幹部が御座いますのに、私如きを選んで下さいました。これと云ふも貴女様のお引立、厚く御礼を申上げます』

英子姫『左様なれば機嫌よう行て来て下さいませ』

東助『然らば玉照彦様、玉照姫様にお暇を申上げて参りませう』

§ 2

Hidekohime: 'If you please come along to the Yahirodono of the Palace of Iminishiki, the Futahashira will be waiting for us beforehand at the Shinzen, so let us go straight to the Yahirodono together.'

Tōsuke: 'If that is so, allow me to accompany you.'

So saying, along with Murasakihime and Akihiko, he follows behind Hidekohime and ascends to the Yahirodono.

On the elevated seat in the front of the Yahirodono, Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, the Futahashira, smiling gently, were waiting for the four to arrive.

From this, Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime, starting with the Kamutsukasa, followed by Hidekohime, Murasakihime, Tōsuke, and Akihiko, advanced close to the Shinzen, focused their prayers upon the Ōkami, and after praying for a safe journey over the long distance, the Futahashira Kamutsukasa themselves personally bestowed Miki upon Tōsuke and Akihiko while speaking.

Tamateruhiko: 'Tōsuke-dono, pray be safe...'

Tamateruhime: 'Serving faithfully in the Goshingefu...'

Hidekohime: 'I pray by the Kami.'

Murasakihime: 'I await the day you swiftly finish your Goyō and return in safety.'

Tōsuke sings before the Shinzen.

By the sacred words of the Futahashira Kamutsukasa How joyful it is to go to the mansion of the Saien The divine mansion of the Lord God Susanoo no Mikoto How happy is the body setting forth toward it Come now, leaving the Palace of Iminishiki Crossing the vast ocean, let us advance To the various Kyōji and believers Bidding farewell, I go afar

Hidekohime: 'The Kamutsukasa of Higashinowake where birds sing May there be blessings, so I shall pray'

Tōsuke: 'How will they make known my ancient name? Truly a shameful state of sin.'

Hidekohime: 'Even without guides of these times, to the Ōkami A person of destiny proved truly valiant.'

Murasakihime: 'The grass-pillow journey Of the Higashinowake no Kamimikoto, I think of with joy Akihiko, Kamutsukasa, pour your heart out everywhere And serve as his attendant.'

Akihiko: 'Come now, leaving the sacred ground I shall serve as the attendant of Higashinowake.'

Tamateruhiko: 'Deep is the blessing of the chihayaburu Kami Thus even the vast ocean holds no perils.'

Tamateruhime: 'By the grace of the Kami who brings spiritual bliss, peacefully Cross over the vast ocean, Lord.'

Tōsuke: 'How grateful, receiving the sacred words of the Kami Let us advance onward to the divine presence.'

Exchanging songs with one another thus, filled with joyful courage, they prepared their traveling gear, this time setting their path toward the south, stopping by at the mansion of Awaji Island from the port of Akashi, bidding temporary farewell to O-Yuri no Kata, and accompanied by Akihiko and two or three other children of his household, gliding over the waves as the days and months accumulated, they landed at the port of Taru in the Province of Fusa, and set forth toward the Saien mansion of Mount Ubusuna.

This was an arrangement made through the compassionate heart of Hidekohime, who, considering Tōsuke's position, thought that if she sent him forth on the pretext of urgent business in the Capital of Fusa, the Kami Susanoo no Ōkami would rejoice and make him a right arm, and moreover, she intended to arrange an unexpected meeting with Tatehikan no Takekunikubake and Takenoehime, who were coming to worship at the Saien mansion.

And after Tōsuke left the sacred ground, Ryūkokubake became the Sōmumu and came to protect the sacred ground.

Takahime, who did not know even in a dream that Tōsuke had left the sacred ground, presented herself at the mansion of the Kyōshu Hidekohime through the summons of Natsuhiko.

Takahime: 'Good morning. Thank you very much for inviting me this morning through Natsuhiko-sama. Might there be some unusual Goyō that has arisen?'

Hidekohime: 'It is well indeed that you have come in. By the command of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime-samas, though it may trouble you, you must go for a while from now to Ikuta no Mori and ask for their protection. And since Tōsuke-sama has now gone on an errand to the Province of Fusa, you must also protect the region of Awaji Island.'

Takahime: 'Yes, I understand it well. If it is your command, I will go anywhere. And when did the Sōmumu-sama set out for the Province of Fusa?'

Hidekohime: 'Yes, he departed from here yesterday evening.'

Takahime's complexion abruptly changed.

However, putting on an indifferent face, she said...

Takahime: 'Ah, is that so? How troublesome it must be to go to such a distant place.'

英子姫『ハイ、昨日の夕方此処を立たれました』

高姫はサツと顔の色を変へた。されど素知らぬ顔をよそほひ、

§ 3

Hidekohime: 'In this world, there is no other way than to leave everything to Kamisama. No matter how much ningen fret, things will only turn out as they are meant to be.'

Takahime: 'Are not Tamanohime, Kunitamawake, and Komahiko residing in the forest of Ikuta?'

Hidekohime: 'Yes, the hōgiyoku of Ryū and Kyū are enshrined there, and the fūfu of Kunitamawake are guarding them, but due to the arrangements of Shinkai, we must go and enshrine the jewel of Kyū in a remote island of Kii Province. Therefore, we must enshrine the hōgiyoku of Ryū in the forest of Ikuta and render goshiyugo. Since we must ask Takahime-sama to undertake this goshiyugo, we ask that you travel there together with Sadahiko, though it may be a trouble for you. Since Kunitamawake and Tamanohime are waiting for you once you go...'

Takahime: 'Such a welcome goshiyugo command, even beyond my hopes, I humbly accept.'

And suddenly becoming isoiso while fluttering her sleeves, she tachiagari,

Takahime: 'Come, Sadahiko, this is ketsukō for you too, make koshirahe quickly. If you go guzuguzu, Tōsuke-sama... no, Kunitamawake-sama is waiting impatiently for us...'

So saying, she made to rush out.

Hidekohime: 'Please wait a moment. Pray for safety on the journey before the divine altar, and partake of the sacred wine before you set out. Tōsuke-sama also partook of the sacred wine and set off toward the forest of Ikuta, taking Akihiko with him.'

Takahime: 'Yes, arigata. Whatever anyone may say, since this is an affair within the mainland, not a single moment of delay is permitted in the service of Shinkai. Please pray in my stead... Hey Sadahiko, will you not tachiaga quickly?'

Without paying proper attention to the words of Hidekohime and flurried in her heart—thinking that Tōsuke would likely be staying at the forest of Ikuta, and if she dawdled it would be like a festival after one had already departed to the land of Fusa, and she wanted to say just one more word to him, she wanted to meet him...—such feelings welled up fiercely within her, making her frantic and hurried, and without losing a moment she adjusted her travel attire, stepped across the pass of Suchiyama, and hastened her steps as she hurried toward the forest of Ikuta.
