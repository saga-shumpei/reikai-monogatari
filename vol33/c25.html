Chapter 25: 琉の Jewel [940]

琉の玉〔940〕

§ 1

Tōsuke, together with Akihiko, dropped in briefly at the mansion of Wakahiko (now Kunitamawake) in the forest of Ikuta around the break of day on the following day. In ancient times, people would calmly traverse fifty or eighty ri in the course of a single night.

Tōsuke: 'Is Kunitamawake-sama at home, I wonder?'

At his inquiring voice, Komahiko jumped out in a fluster,

Komahiko: 'Well, well, is this you, Sōmu-sama? ...Ah, you are Akihiko, it has been a long time, hasn't it?'

Akihiko: 'Um.'

Tōsuke: 'Are the husband and wife in?'

When he asked again, Komahiko replied,

Komahiko: 'Yes, having finished this morning's expression of gratitude, the two of them together—thinking that Tōsuke-sama would likely appear soon, and giving the order that I should wait a little while until then, as they were going off to the Nunobiki Waterfall to perform Mizugori—said for you to please rest at ease for a short while. They will surely be returning before long...'

Tōsuke: 'Since I have no particular business, when the couple returns, tell them that Tōsuke dropped in for a moment on his way to take up his divine duties at the mansion of the Iso in Fusa. The affairs of the Kamisama allow not a single moment of delay...'

So saying abruptly, he already stepped briskly away from the spot. Akihiko, though he had managed with much effort to meet his older brother Komahiko, felt the poignant regret of not even being able to exchange proper words, and turning back again and again with lingering attachment, followed behind Tōsuke and proceeded toward the port of Akashi.

Behind him, Komahiko clasped both hands together and muttered,

Komahiko: 'Ah, what a cold and distant person he is. I had always heard that Tōsuke-sama was a simple and honest man, but this is really too lacking in friendliness. Kunitamawake and his wife are also a pair of spouses—why on earth would they go to something like the Nunobiki Waterfall at a time like this? If they wanted to go, they should have gone a little earlier and returned sooner. Lately, because they keep muttering complaints about whether Tamanohime has swollen up or not swollen up, or whatever else, even Kunitokuni, who is soft on his wife, seems unable to sleep properly at night, and so they sleep late in the morning. That is why such a situation comes about. Oh well, why on earth are they so slow? If I were to dash off at a run right now, it would not be impossible to meet Akihiko, but if I leave here vacant again, my duties will not be fulfilled—a troublesome matter has arisen. This is why people say one should not take care of young married couples. Tōsuke-sama, too, most likely hurried off in a flurry, thinking to stop by Awaji Island quickly to see his wife Oyuri and spend even a single minute dallying with her. Indeed, husband and wife seem to be creatures who yearn for one another. I, too, want to complete my training quickly, acquire a fine wife, build a home, and... Now, Komahiko... No, my wife... I want to act all lovey-dovey like that. Ah, how dreary it is; is there anything as dull as not even being able to talk properly with one's own brothers?'

Returning thither were the two persons, Kunitamawake and Tamanohime.

Komahiko: 'Hey, Kuni-sama, Tama-sama, what are you dawdling for? Tōsuke has finally washed his hands of us and gone back. Even Akihiko...'

Kunitamawake: 'Tōsuke has gone back? Of whom are you speaking?'

Komahiko: 'Of whom could I be speaking? Kunitamawake-sama, Tamanohime-sama, please be reasonable. Tōsuke-sama, the Sōmu of the sacred ground, dropped in here for a moment; whether he thought—what a friendly couple you are—or whether he did not, I know not, but saying 'Please be well,' he trotted right back. Even Akihiko, though he met his brother after all this trouble, went all the way to the port of Akashi without exchanging a single proper word with me. Since it has only been about half an hour or so, I am going to chase after him now and see my younger brother just one more time. Please, you husband and wife, stay here and be on good terms. If you have any message, I shall convey it...'

原文（近代文語）

東助は秋彦と共に生田の森の若彦（今は国玉別）の館に翌日の夜明け頃、一寸立寄つて見た。昔は人は一夜の中に五十里や八十里は平気で跋渉したものである。

東助『国玉別さまは居られますかなア』

と訪ふ声に駒彦はあわてて飛び出し、

駒彦『コレハコレハ総務さまで御座いますか。……ヤアお前は秋彦、久振りだつたなあ』

秋彦『ウン』

東助『夫婦は居られるかなア』

と重ねて問へば駒彦は、

駒彦『ハイ、今朝のお礼を済ませ、夫婦連れにて軈て東助様が見えるだらうから、それ迄に布引の滝へ水垢離を取りに往つて来るから、一寸待つてゐて貰へとの命令で御座いました。どうぞ暫く御休息下さいませ。やがてお帰りになるでせうから……』

東助『別に用もないのだから、夫婦が帰られたら、東助がフサの国の斎苑の館へ御神務を帯びて行く途中、一寸訪問したと伝へておいてくれ。神様の御用は一刻も猶予は出来ないから………』

と云ひすて、早くも此場をスタスタと立去りにける。秋彦も兄の駒彦に折角会ひ乍ら、碌々に詞も交し得ざる本意なさに、後ふり返りふり返り名残を惜しみつつ、東助の後に従つて明石の港を指して進み行く。

後に駒彦は双手を組み、

駒彦『あゝ何としたすげない人だらう。いつも朴訥な東助さまだと聞いて居つたが、コリヤ又余り愛想がなさすぎる。国玉別夫婦も夫婦だ、何故こんな時に布引の滝なんかへ行くのだらう。行きたけりやモ少し早く往つて、早く帰つて来れば良いのに、此頃は玉能姫が膨れたとか、ふくれぬとか、何とか彼とか云つて、小言ばかり仰有るものだから、女房に甘い国公も、夜分は碌々によう寝ないと見えて、朝寝をするのだ。それだから斯んなことが出来て来るのだ。エヽまあまあなんでこれ程遅いのだろ。これから一走り駆け出せば、秋彦に会はれぬ事はないが、又不在にしておいては、おれの役がすまぬなり、困つた事が出来て来た。これだから若夫婦の世話はするものぢやないと人が言ふのだ。東助さまも大方早く淡路島へ立寄つて女房のお百合に会ひ、一分間でもいちやつかうと思うて、倉皇として駆け出したのだらう。ヤツパリ夫婦といふものは互に恋しいものと見えるワイ。おれも早く修行をして結構な女房を持ち、家庭を作つて、……コレ駒彦……イヤ女房……と意茶ついて見たいものだ。あゝ辛気臭い、男同志の兄弟にさへ碌々話も出来ぬやうな詰らぬことがあるものか』

と呟いてゐる。そこへ帰つて来たのは国玉別、玉能姫の両人であつた。

駒彦『コレ国さま、玉さま、何をグヅグヅしてゐるのだ。とうとう東助が愛想をつかして帰つて了ひました。秋彦までが……』

国玉別『東助が帰つたとは、誰の事だ』

駒彦『誰のこともあつたものか。国玉別さま、玉能姫さま、よい加減にしておきなさい。聖地の総務の東助さまがここへ一寸お寄りになり、お前さま等夫婦の不在を見て、何とマア仲の良い夫婦ぢやなア……と思はれたか、思はれぬか、そりや知らぬが、どうぞ宜しうと云つて、トツトと帰つて行かれました。秋彦までが、折角兄弟に対面し乍ら、ロクに私に詞も交さず、明石の港まで行つて了つたのですよ。まだ半時許り前だから、私はこれから追つかけて、弟にモウ一目会うて来ます。どうぞお前さま等夫婦は此処に仲好うしてゐて下さい。何か伝言があるなら申上げますから……』

§ 2

Tamanohime: 'That was indeed a most inconvenient matter... Kunitamawake-sama, what shall we do about it?'

Kunitamawake: 'Even if we were to go after him, goodness only knows how angry Tōsukesama would become, given his temperament. We had better have Komahiko go instead. ...Hey Komahiko, just go and say this: Kunitamawake and his wife said they were truly sorry and apologized. You need not say anything else.'

Komahiko: 'Right, understood!'

So saying, he tucked up his skirts, concealed his form within the forest of Ikuta, and did not return until well past nightfall.

Around the dusk of that day, Takahime arrived in a bustling manner together with Sadahiko.

Takahime: 'I say, Kunitamawake-sama, I am Takahime. By the command of the Kamisama, I have become the guardian deity of the Ikuta Forest and have come all this way. You must have known about this long ago, surely.'

Thus she clamored abruptly from the doorway. Hearing this voice, Tamanohime flew out hastily to the front,

Tamanohime: 'Oh dear, Takahime-sama, welcome indeed. Come, please step inside. We received notice from the brocade palace two or three days ago that you would be coming here, and in preparation for it we have tidied up various utensils, and today we went on a pilgrimage for misogi to the Nunobiki Waterfall to wash away the lingering traces. Come, from today you are the master of this mansion, please proceed to the inner rooms without any reserve.'

Takahime: 'Yes, thank you.'

So saying, with a somewhat restless demeanor, looking around here and there, fidgeting and seeming as though it were difficult to speak,

Takahime: 'Um... did Tōsukesama not stop by here?'

Tamanohime: 'Yes, we hear he inquired early this morning, but as it chanced, the two of us had gone to the Nunobiki Waterfall for water austerities and were out, so he could not even cross the threshold of the gate and returned together with Akihiko, so they say. In all likelihood, he will have stopped by our mansion on Awaji Island, will rest leisurely there this evening, and will come again tomorrow.'

Takahime: 'Oh my, that is terrible. No matter what, I must see Tōsukesama with my own eyes and utter a word of reproach. Nay, I must utter a word of gratitude. I say, Kunitamawake-sama, Tamanohime-sama, I am sorry to trouble you, but please hasten and launch a boat. And escort me all the way to Awaji Island. Tamanohime-sama is so very skilled at handling a boat...'

Kunitamawake: 'Again you are suddenly yearning for Tōsukesama. Is there some deep circumstance?'

Takahime: 'What should I do if there were no circumstances? He is my beloved, my beloved husband of old. Come, quickly launch the boat. I say, Tamanohime-sama, it is the wish of a lifetime, if you do not launch it quickly I will not be in time.'

Tamanohime: 'I say, Kunitamawake-sama, shall we launch the boat and send her off...?'

Kunitamawake: 'Since you ask so earnestly, I would like to send her off, but upon hearing that he is a former lover, I cannot simply go about sending her off carelessly. Without knowing Tōsukesama's mind, if we were to send her off carelessly, goodness knows what kind of reprimand we might receive. If it were the usual Tōsukesama, he would leisurely rest and go on from our house, but considering that he left without even crossing the threshold, he may have known that Takahime-sama was coming after him and fled because he found it annoying, so this is something to ponder.'

Takahime: 'Bah, what a cold-hearted creature lacking in human feeling. Can you act as a propagation envoy of Omoto-kyō with such an attitude? Why don't you show a little chic flair?'

玉能姫『それはそれは誠に不都合な事で御座いましたなア……国玉別さま如何致しませうか』

国玉別『後追つかけてでも行かうものなら、東助様の気性としてどんなに怒られるか知れたものぢやない。いつそ駒彦に往つて貰はう。……コレ駒彦、国玉別夫婦が誠にすまぬことで御座いましたとお詫をしてゐたと、是だけ言つてくれ。其外のことは何にも言はぬが宜しいぞ』

駒彦『ヨシ合点だ！』

といひ乍ら尻ひつからげ、生田の森の中に姿をかくした限り日の暮れ過まで帰つて来なかつた。

其日の暮頃、高姫は佐田彦と共に慌ただしくやつて来た。

高姫『モシモシ国玉別さま、私は高姫で御座います。神様の御命令に依つて、生田の森の守護職となり、はるばると出て来ました。お前さまは此事は疾つくに御存じでせうなア』

と門口から出しぬけに喚いてゐる。玉能姫は此声を聞くよりあわただしく表に飛び出し、

玉能姫『これはこれは高姫様、能うこそいらせられました。サアお這入り下さいませ。貴女の此所へお越しになることは、二三日以前より、錦の宮から通知が御座いまして、其用意の為にいろいろ道具の取片付も致し、今日は名残に布引の滝へ禊に行つて参りました。サア今日からは貴女は此館の主人、どうぞ御遠慮なく奥へお通り下さいませ』

『あの……東助さまはここへ御立寄りにはなりませなんだかなア』

玉能姫『ハイ、今朝早々お尋ね下さつた相で御座いますが、折あしく布引の滝へ夫婦の者が水行に参り、不在中だつたものですから、門の閾も跨げずに、秋彦と一緒にお帰りなつたといふことです。大方今頃は淡路島の吾館へでもお立寄りになつて、今晩はゆつくりお休み遊ばし、明日更めてお出でになるでせう』

高姫『事情がなうて何としよう。私の恋しい恋しい昔の夫で御座んすワイナ。サア早く舟を出して下さいなア。コレ玉能姫さま、一生の願ひぢや、早う出して下さらぬと間に合ひませぬ』

玉能姫『モシ国玉別様、舟を出して送つて上げませうかなア』

国玉別『折角のお頼みだから、送つて上げたいが、昔の恋男だなんて聞く上は、ウツカリ送る訳にも行くまい。東助さまの御心も分らず、ウツカリ送つて行かうものなら、それこそ何んなお目玉を頂戴するか分るまいぞや。いつもの東助様ならば、ゆつくりと吾家に休んで行つて下さるのだけれど、閾もまたげずに行かれたとこを思へば、高姫さまが後からお出になるのを知つて、うるさがつて逃げられたのかも分らぬから、此奴ア一つ考へものだ』

高姫『エヽ人情を知らぬ冷酷な動物だなア。そんなことで三五教の宣伝使が出来ますか。チツト粋を利かしたら如何だなア』

Just as they were speaking, it was Komahiko who came back walking briskly.

Kunitamawake: 'Oh, is that you, Komahiko? Have you met with Tōsuke-sama?'

Komahiko: 'Yes, I caught up with him conveniently at the port of Akashi, met with Akihiko, and then escorted him by boat to his residence on Ahajishima. After meeting with Yuri-sama, we shared drinks, and we brothers also received plenty.'

Takahime: 'Hey, you are Komahiko, aren't you? And is Tōsuke-sama still staying on Ahajishima?'

Komahiko: 'Oh, Takahime-sama, it is a rare pleasure to see you in such a place. You have quite an interesting history regarding the jewel, and it remains in the forest of Ikuta. You have gone through much hardship, such as coming to Chikubushima through the Kamugakari of Kuniyoriwake-sama. I still remember it from time to time and talk about it with Kunitamawake-sama. You are truly remarkable when it comes to the jewel.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Komahiko, never mind about the jewel; please tell me about that when you have leisure. How is Tōsuke-sama doing? Come, tell me quickly. Quickly, quickly, I cannot help but feel impatient.'

Komahiko: 'Tōsuke-sama, you ask? I parted with him at the Akashikaikefu. If he has gone on with that momentum, he must be off the coast of Takasago by now. I returned here all alone in a small boat. And since I was dawdling in the forest, quite a lot of time has passed. Does Takahime-sama have some urgent business with Tōsuke-sama?'

Takahime: 'No, that is enough. I shall not think of Tōsuke anymore.'

Kunitamawake: 'Now, Takahime-sama, please, the water for washing feet is ready here, so please use it and come up. We must carry out the handover of duties, and once handed over, this place is already your residence, so please relax and let us hear your story.'

Takahime: 'Yes, thank you.'

Saying this, she had Sadahiko wash her feet, and while brushing off the dust, she took off her hat, discarded her straw raincoat, and advanced into the inner room.

That night, both host and guest went to sleep peacefully, and the next day, Kunitamawake presented the Jewel of Ryū before Takahime.

Kunitamawake: 'This is the precious Jewel of Ryū that I received from Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama. Please protect this jewel wherever you go and stay in this forest for a long time. By the command of the God, I must take the Jewel of Tama along with Tamanohime and Komahiko, go to the road of Kinokuni, and enshrine it there, so I humbly ask for your favor.'

Tamanohime: 'Takahime-sama, I humbly ask for your favor.'

Takahime: 'Yes, I understand. For someone like me to be allowed to protect the precious Jewel of Ryū, what an undeserved blessing it is. I will surely guard it with great care, so please be at ease.'

Kunitamawake: 'Since you have kindly accepted, there is not a moment to lose. Now, I shall carry the Jewel of Tama and depart for Kinokuni, so please perform your duties with a cheerful heart.'

Tamanohime: 'Komahiko, come, let us go... Takahime-sama, if that is the case, we shall bid you farewell for a while.'

Takahime: 'I pray that you may perform your divine work safely. In that case, I shall see you off to the gate; hey, Sadahiko, please keep watch over this precious jewel.'

Kunitamawake: 'No, no, that is not necessary. As long as this jewel is in the mansion, you must not leave this house for a while.'

Takahime: 'If that is so, then there is no help for it. Let us say our farewells here.'

She saw them off at the gate. Kunitamawake, Tamanohime, and Komahiko were sent off by more than ten believers, and they proceeded day and night, heading toward Wakanoura in Kinokuni.
