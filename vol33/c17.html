Chapter 17: 感謝の涙 [932]

感謝の涙〔932〕

§ 1

Takashimamaru proceeded westwards and ever westwards from the land of Teru and the port of Hara, returning to Taiwan Island of Nihonkoku in Genkon.

That it took a totally opposite path and made a very great detour compared to the navigation routes of Konnichi was due to the relationship of the inclination of Chikiu and the relationship of Chōriku three hundred thousand years ago. Compared to the modern era, when ships navigate freely at will by the power of Jiyōki, it was extremely inconvenient. However, its sokuriyoku was able to navigate at over twenty nijifunotsuto of Konnichi, even at times without wind. The reason for this was that, for example, if it were a ship carrying nihiyakunin, a hundred rokai were attached to both riyōgensoku of the ship, and out of the nihiyakunin passengers, one hundred rowed the rokai with all their might, and when they became somewhat fatigued, another hundred took their place, rowing the rokai in turns. The steersman only determined the direction of the ship, examined the pilotage, and steered the rudder. When it came to large ships, they were built in two tiers, with arrangements to row the ship from both below and above. Just looking at the sides, it was a ship-building method that looked like the legs of a centipede. Therefore, with extraordinary sokuriyoku, it did not weaken in the least even against slight rough seas. Moreover, people of ancient times were generally bold, and cowards were not permitted to board from the outset. Consequently, it could be said that elderly people and child passengers who were of little use were virtually nonexistent. Unless they were persons with special circumstances, they mutually warned against and did not permit boarding ships.

The party of Takahime gradually and with the passage of days and months arrived safely at the port of Yura in Jitōtōshima. Akiyamahiko, upon the command of Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime of Nishiki no Miya, foreknew that the party of Takahime would return to the port of Yura, and having gathered a multitude of villagers, while fluttering the divine banner of Jūyō in the sea breeze, was waiting for them as if longing for them now.

To this place Takeshimamaru, kicking the waves, came back carrying the party of Takahime. The party of Takahime had changed to Takeshimamaru at the port of Kiru in Taiwan. The party of Takahime consisting of six persons, with the addition of Matsuhiko and Tsurukimi making eight in all, sent off by the welcoming company of Akiyamahiko, entered the mansion of Akiyamahiko with a valiant air and came to rest their breath. That night, all were exhausted, and just as they had finished their evening meal, carrying their crawling, limp bodies to the respective sleeping chambers given to them, they fell asleep as if crushed.

Matsuhiko, who had been ordered to act as a supervisor by Kotoyoriwake no Mikoto and had accompanied them, did not sleep a single eye that night, and together with Akiyamahiko and his wife, concentrated on secret consultations concerning matters related to the personal circumstances of Takahime and the affair of the imperial business of the Mani Hōju, finally going to bed around the breaking of day. Akiyamahiko and his wife also sought to comfort themselves from the fatigue of the previous night, and came to indulge in sound sleep until the time when the sun rose high.

From the sacred land, Tosuke and others, including Kamehiko and other members, came down the Yura River to this place in order to welcome the party of Takahime. The mansion of Akiyamahiko was filled with sudden guests, and the servants reached the utmost state of extreme business from top to bottom, being about to make preparations for the banquet.

Akiyamahiko invited Takahime, Takayorihime, and Ryūkokuwake into one room, and came to ask the three for their opinions concerning the matter of the secret letters of Ōsumakami no Ōkami and Kotoyoriwake no Mikoto brought by Matsuhiko.

Akiyamahiko: 'Takahimesama and the other two persons, thank you for your trouble for a long time from far-off places. Even in the august presence of Ōkami, you must be well satisfied. Regarding the matter of the Mani no Hōju, out of the five welcomed from Ryūgūjima, the fact that four of them have been lost is truly a great inconvenience regarding the divine world's administration. Concerning this, I must ask you to take the trouble once more to help discover the four jewels, but how is it, can I ask for your trouble?'

原文（近代文語）

高島丸はテルの国、ハラの港より西へ西へと進んで、現今の日本国台湾島へ帰つて来た。

今日の航路より見れば全然反対の道をとり、且つ非常に迂回して居るのは、三十万年前の地球の傾斜の関係及潮流の関係に依つたものである。蒸気の力を以て自由自在に航行する現代に比ぶれば、非常に不便なものであつた。併し乍ら其速力は今日の二十浬以上を、風なき時と雖も、航行する事が出来たのである。其故は例へば二百人乗りの船ならば、船の両舷側に百本の艪櫂がついてゐて、二百人の乗客の中百人は、力限りに艪櫂を漕ぎ、稍疲労したる時は、又百人これに代り、交る交る艪櫂を漕いだものである。船頭は只船の方向を定め、水先を調べ、舵をとるのみであつた。大きな船になると、二階造りになり、下からも、上からも船を漕ぐ仕掛になつて居た。恰度舷を見ると、蜈蚣の足の様に見ゆる船の造り方であつた。それ故非常な速力で、少々の荒波位には少しも弱らなかつたのである。且又昔の人は総じて剛胆者が多く、臆病者は頭から乗船を許さなかつたのである。故に余り役に立たぬ老人や、子供の船客は皆無と云つても良い位であつた。特別の事情ある者でなければ、船に乗ることを互に戒めて許さなかつたものである。

高姫一行は漸くにして、月日を重ね自転倒島の由良の港に安着した。秋山彦は錦の宮の玉照彦、玉照姫の命に依り、高姫一行が由良の港に帰り来ることを前知し、数多の里人を集め、埠頭に一行を迎ふべく、十曜の神旗を海風に翻し乍ら、今や遅しと待ちつつあつた。

此処へ竹島丸は波を蹴つて、高姫一行を乗せて帰り来るのであつた。高姫一行は、台湾のキルの港より竹島丸に乗り替へたのである。高姫一行六人外に松彦、鶴公の二人を加へて八人は、秋山彦の迎への人数に送られて、勇ましげに秋山彦館に入り、息を休むる事となつた。其夜は何れも草臥果て、夕餉を喫したる儘、這ふが如くグタリとした体を、与へられた各自の寝間に運び、つぶれた様に寝て了つた。

言依別命より監視役を命ぜられて、従いて来た松彦は、其夜は一睡もせず、秋山彦夫婦と共に、高姫の身の上に関する事、及び麻邇宝珠の御用の件に就て、ひそかに協議を凝らし、夜の明くる頃漸くにして寝に就いた。秋山彦夫婦も亦昨夜の疲労を慰すべく、太陽の高く昇る頃まで白河夜船の夢を貪ることとなつた。

聖地よりは東助を初め、加米彦其他の面々が高姫一行を迎ふべく、由良川を下つて此処にやつて来たのである。秋山彦館は俄の客にて、下僕共は上を下へと大繁忙を極め、馳走の用意に差かかつて居る。

秋山彦は高姫、鷹依姫、竜国別の三人を一間に招き、松彦が齎せる神素盞嗚大神及び言依別命の密書の件に就て、三人に対し、意見を聞くこととなつた。

秋山彦『高姫さま、其他のお二方、永らくの間、御遠方の所、御苦労で御座いました。大神様に於かせられても、さぞ御満足の事で御座いませう。就いては麻邇の宝珠の件で御座いますが、竜宮島より迎へられた五個の中、其四つまで紛失致しました事は、実に神界経綸上大変な不都合で御座います。これに就てあなた方に今一度お世話になつて、四つの玉を発見して頂かねばならないので御座いますが、如何でせう、お世話になれるでせうか』

§ 2

Takahime: 'Yes, as for me, beginning with the adamantine wishing jewel, regarding all other jewels whatsoever, I no longer have any attachment, so this matter alone I have already abandoned. In this vast world, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto must have done something, so no matter how much we search it is useless. Please, just do not speak of the jewels anymore.'

Akiyamahiko: 'To perform any manner of divine service in Shinkai cannot be done without the commands of the Kamisama and the karmic affinities proper to one's Mitama. Accordingly, Takayorihime-sama, Tatsukuniwake-sama, Kurohime-sama of another, these four persons have the karmic affinity of having to perform the divine service of the Mani jewels, but as it happened, at the time for the appearance of the five-colored Mani jewels from Riugūjima to arrive, in a divine manner you led Takahime-sama and Kurohime-sama two persons to the single island of Riugū, yet you said that upon this single island there are no longer any jewels and that you would search elsewhere, and so you returned home. Therefore, unavoidably, in accordance with the will of Shinkai, Umekohime-sama was to take charge of the purple jewel, which by the karmic affinity of her Mitama was naturally her duty to bring back to Nishiki no Miya. From then on, the blue jewel was for Takahime-sama, the red jewel for Kurohime-sama, the white jewel for Takayorihime-sama, and the golden jewel for Tatsukuniwake-sama, to serve as their divine duties, which had been decided from ancient times by karmic affinity. However, because the four persons grew impatient without waiting for the various seasons of Shinkai and went off in directions that were all mistaken, by the arrangement of Shinkai, the four pillars of Kōryūhime, Mukadehime, Tomohiko, and Teleshime performed temporary divine service all the way to this Jitentōtō. Yet, since you must now fulfill the divine service of your proper karmic affinity, by the arrangements of the Great Deity Lord Susanoo and Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, the four jewels excluding the purple jewel are hidden by Kotoyoriwakenomikoto in a certain place, taking responsibility for them. You must by all means fulfill that which is solely a matter of karmic affinity. If we fail in this divine service this time, from now on it can never be retrieved for all eternity, and thinking that if such a thing were to happen it would be a pity for you, out of the great compassion and great mercy of His divine heart, the Great Deity Lord Susanoo made His child Kotoyoriwakenomikoto bear the responsibility and take the blame, dealing with him in just that manner. Now here from Udu no Kuni, beginning with the Great Deity Lord Susanoo, who commanded Matahiko's administration, and from Kotoyoriwakenomikoto and Kuniyoriwakenomikoto, a divine book has arrived. If you please open this, the whereabouts of the jewels will surely be entirely clear. Though it is a trouble to you, I ask for one more effort in our divine service.'

Takahime for the first time realized the great compassionate heart of the Great Deity and the true hearts of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto and Kuniyoriwakenomikoto, and plunged into tears of gratitude, weeping and collapsing on the spot. Takayorihime and Tatsukuniwake also raised their voices and wept aloud at the depth of that divine grace.

To such a place, inquiring after Kurohime's whereabouts from Tsukushi no Shima, the two persons Tamajiwake and Akihiko returned, bringing Kurohime with them, and here the Mitama of the four persons came to see each other's faces after a long time.

Akiyamahiko repeatedly recounted the aforementioned circumstances to Kurohime, opened the divine book, and read it to her. The patterns of activity of Kurohime, Tamajiwake, and others in Tsukushijima shall be deferred to a later day and recounted.

Akiyamahiko raised the divine writing to his brow, quietly opened it, and read it aloud before the four persons.

That divine writing: 'This time, descending with the Mitama as Kunitachinoto and Kunitakenomikoto, and further Toyokuhimenomikoto becoming Kuniootatsunomikoto and changing again into Lord Susanoo, Kunitakenomikoto hiding in the sacred mountain of Siooyama, and Lord Susanoo hiding in the pavilion of the sacred garden of Ubusunayama, this is the karmic affinity of the Mitama to embark upon the great preparations of Shinkai to weave the loom of brocade for the accomplishment of the divine government. Regarding that, Hatsuzakihime, who is the descendant of the spirit of Wakahimeguminomikoto, shall eternally protect the adamantine wishing jewel; Tamanohime, who is the descendant of the spirit of Kuninaohimenomikoto, shall undertake the protection of the purple jewel; Kotoyoriwakenomikoto shall eternally protect the golden jewel; Umekohimeomikoto shall serve the divine service of the purple Mani jewel; Takahime shall protect the blue Mani jewel; Kurohime shall protect the red Mani jewel; Takayorihime shall protect the white Mani jewel; and Tatsukuniwake shall protect the yellow Mani jewel as the karmic affinity of their Mitama; therefore, from this time forth, the four persons shall take out the Mani jewels and head toward the sacred land of Aya. Although there may be any number of waiting Mitama, we wish to command this divine service to the Mitama of karmic affinity if possible, and since it is a divine work for myriads of kalpas and all eternity, for the reason of the delayed repentance of Takahime and the others, we cause Kotoyoriwakenomikoto to bear the sin of the delay in the divine work, and thereby command Takahime and the others to perform the Mani divine work for myriads of kalpas and all eternity. ... Lord Susanoo'

原文（近代文語）

高姫『ハイ、私は金剛不壊の如意宝珠を初め、其他一切の玉に関し、最早何の執着もなくなりましたから、此事計りは、最早断念して居ります。此広い世の中、言依別命がどうかされたのでせうから、いくら捜しても駄目です。どうぞ玉の事だけはモウ言はないでおいて下さいませ』

秋山彦『如何なる神界の御用を致すのも、皆神様からの御命令、身魂相応の因縁がなくては出来ないので御座います。就いては、鷹依姫様、竜国別様、モウ一人の黒姫様、此四人の方が、麻邇宝珠の御用をして下さらねばならない因縁で御座いますが、生憎竜宮島より五色の麻邇宝珠が現はれ玉ふ時機到来して、惟神的に高姫様、黒姫様お二人を竜宮の一つ島へお導きになりましたなれど、あなた方は此一つ島には最早玉はない、外を捜さうと云つて、お帰りになられました。それ故止むを得ず、神界の思召に依つて、梅子姫様は紫の玉の御用、これは身魂の因縁で当然錦の宮へお持帰りにならねばならぬ御役で御座いました。それから青色の玉は高姫様、赤色の玉は黒姫様、白色の玉は鷹依姫様、黄金の玉は竜国別様が御用遊ばす、昔からの因縁にきまつて居つたのです。併し乍ら、四人の方はいろいろと神界の時節を待たずお焦りになつて、何れも方角違ひの方へ往ていらツしやつたものですから、神界のお計らひにて、黄竜姫、蜈蚣姫、友彦、テールス姫の四柱が此自転倒島まで臨時御用を遊ばしたので御座います。併し乍ら身魂の因縁だけの御用を、今度は勤めねばならないのですから、神素盞嗚大神様、言依別命様のお計らひにて、紫の玉を除く外四つの玉は言依別命様が責任を負ひ、或地点にお隠しになつてゐるので御座います。どうしても因縁だけの事を勤めねばならぬのであります。今度の御用を仕損つたら、モウ此先は末代取返しが出来ませぬから、そんな事があつては、あなた方にお気の毒だと、大慈大悲の大御心より神素盞嗚大神様が吾子の言依別命様に責任を負はせ、罪を着せ、あゝ云ふ具合にお取扱ひになつたので御座いますよ。今此処にウヅの国より、松彦の司に事依さし神素盞嗚大神様を初め、言依別命、国依別命より神書が届きました。どうぞ之を御披き下されば、玉の所在もスツカリお分りでせう。どうぞ御苦労ですが、モウ一働き御用を願ひませう』

高姫は初めて大神の大慈悲心と、言依別命及び国依別命の真心を悟り、感謝の涙に暮れて其場に泣き倒れた。鷹依姫、竜国別も声を放つて、其神恩の深きに号泣して居る。

斯かる所へ筑紫の島より黒姫の所在を尋ね、玉治別、秋彦の両人、黒姫を伴れて帰り来り、茲に四人の身魂は久しぶりに顔を見合す事となつた。

秋山彦は黒姫に重ねて前述の次第を物語り、神書を開いて読み聞かせた。黒姫、玉治別等の筑紫島に於ける活動の模様は後日に稿を改め、述ぶる事と致します。

秋山彦は神文を押戴き、静かに開いて、四人の前に読み上げた。其神文、

『此度、国治立命、国武彦命と身を下し玉ひ、また豊国姫命は国大立命となり再び変じて神素盞嗚尊となり、国武彦命は聖地四尾山に隠れ、素盞嗚尊はウブスナ山の斎苑の館に隠れて、神政成就の錦の機を織りなす神界の大準備に着手すべき身魂の因縁である。それに付いて、稚姫君命の御霊の裔なる初稚姫は金剛不壊の如意宝珠を永遠に守護し、国直姫命の御霊の裔なる玉能姫は紫の玉の守護に当り、言依別命は黄金の玉を永遠に守護し、梅子姫命は紫色の麻邇の宝珠の御用に仕へ、高姫は青色の麻邇の宝玉、黒姫は赤色の麻邇の宝玉、鷹依姫は白色の麻邇の宝玉、竜国別は黄色の麻邇の宝玉を守護すべき身魂の因縁なれば、これより四人は麻邇の宝珠を取出し、綾の聖地に向ふべし。控への身魂は何程にてもありとは云へども、成るべくは因縁の身魂に此御用を命じたく、万劫末代の神業なれば、高姫以下の改心の遅れたる為、神業の遅滞せし罪を言依別命に負はせて、高姫以下に万劫末代の麻邇の神業を命ずるものなり。……神素盞嗚尊』

It was so written.

Choking with tears of gratitude, the four immediately clapped their hands and offered a prayer of gratitude to the shrine.

Akiyamahiko brought out the golden key and handed it to Takahime,

Akiyamahiko: 'Now, you four people, secretly leave my mansion through the back gate, go out to the port of Yura, cross over to Meshima, take out the four jewels of Manihotsushiyu exactly as each of you has been commanded, secretly return to the seichi, and participate in the precious shingefu. If this matter reaches the ears of the kamutsukasa and shinto of the seichi and elsewhere, it will greatly affect the shintoku and shinyō of you four, so keep everything a secret, hōtai the goishi of the Ōkami, atone for the sins up to now, and I hope that you will serve in the seichi brilliantly as the kamutsukasa of Manihotsushiyu. Come on, hurry, hurry...'

Being thus urged, the four joyfully and courageously slipped out secretly from the back door and proceeded toward Meshima.

Of this matter, beginning with Tamajiwake, Kamehiko, Tei, Kā, Tsunehiko, and other kamutsukasa, and the seichi's Murasakihime, Kōryūhime, Mukaderuhime, Tomohiko, Teerusuhime, and other kamutsukasa as well as the shinto, there was no one who knew for all eternity.

Takahime and the other three persons, through the boundless mercy and arrangement of Lord Susanoo, were forgiven of their sins and defilements, and were made to serve completely in the goyō of their mihana-sōō-innen. Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sakiwaimase.

原文（近代文語）

と記してあつた。四人は感謝の涙にむせび乍ら、直ちに手を拍ち、神殿に感謝の祝詞を奏上した。秋山彦は黄金の鍵を持ち出でて、高姫に渡し、

秋山彦『いざ四人の方々、吾館の裏門よりひそかに由良の港に出で、沓島に渡り、麻邇宝珠の四個の玉を、各自命ぜられたる如く取出し、秘に聖地へ帰り、尊き神業に参加されたし。此事、聖地其他の神司、信徒の耳に入らば、却て四人の神徳信用に関係する事大なれば、一切秘密を守り、大神の御意志を奉戴し、今迄の罪を贖ひ、天晴れ麻邇宝珠の神司として聖地にあつて奉仕されむ事を希望致します。サア早く早く……』

と急き立てられ、四人は喜び勇んで、裏口より秘に脱け出で沓島に向つて進み行く。

此事玉治別を初め、加米彦、テー、カー、常彦、其他の神司、聖地の紫姫、黄竜姫、蜈蚣姫、友彦、テールス姫其他の神司も信徒も永遠に知る者がなかつたのである。

高姫外三人は素盞嗚尊の仁慈無限のお計らひにて、罪穢れを許され、身魂相応因縁の御用を完全に奉仕させられたのである。アヽ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
