Chapter 22: High Palace 姫 [937]

高宮姫〔937〕

§ 1

Takahime showed signs of remorse from the bottom of her heart, and in order to expose all secrets by herself, she stood up and began to sing a song.

As the sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, In the lineage of the rigorous mitama, As the living palace of Hinodewake, I have persisted until now, But when I think of it, how terrifying it is, If I reveal my true nature, Appearing in the Caucasus mountains, The Hiko of Ural and the Hime of Ural, Born between the two of them, I am Takamiyahimemikoto, The sendenshi employed by Kamususanōnōokami, Starting with pine, bamboo, and plum, Ishikoridome's Azumahiko, Takahiko and others appeared, Opening the Kototamasen, She who appears as my mother, Ōgetsu, Himenomikoto returned swiftly To Armenia, At that time, I, a little girl,

Living without any means as a maiden, Led the attendant kami along, To Jerusalem in Takaamahara, Intending to explore the headquarters of Ananai-kyō, When I went and looked, a youth of exquisite features, With a countenance of brilliant floral beauty, Advanced from the direction ahead, Here I somehow felt drawn to that youth, And tried to pursue his footsteps, But the attendant kami became an obstacle, Thinking to sweetly shake them off, As the result of a thousand thoughts and myriad considerations, I stirred up trouble and scattered them, On the mountain path where I advanced, longing for The lover visible in the shade of the trees, The youth walked with extremely swift steps, And before I knew it, concealed his form In the shadow of the mountain.

With the feet of a frail woman, Having no means to catch up, weeping and falling down, When I cried out for someone to save me, My beloved person was startled, And came hurrying back to my side, How embarrassing it was, 'Hey there, traveler maiden, Was it not your voice that just now cried out for help?' Asked with wonder, Sobbing, with no way to answer, 'Oh, thank you truly, Thanks to you, the wicked one Fled like clouds and mist.' 'Please be careful, A woman's dangerous journey alone.' 'You too, in your youthful days, To go along this mountain path all alone, No matter what, Danger must be pressing upon your body, If it is possible, please, I beg you, take me and cross over This slope to the other side,' she said, wetting her two eyelashes with saliva, And mimicking weeping, whereupon the lover Sank into a slight sentiment of sympathy, 'Ah, what a dangerous mountain path, Both you and I are travelers alone, Even if prayed for, we are companions made by fate, In that case, let us go together,' And the joy of his answering thus, On the mountain path where no people pass, Burning with youthful blood, Two youths, male and female, holding hands, Joyfully and happily talking, On the way to the sacred land of Tsuchi, Before one knew it, four eyes Met fixedly in the mirror of love, How could the burning thoughts Not be reflected in each other's hearts?

Suddenly a compromise was struck, Becoming a pair close as water leaking nowhere, Concealing our bodies at the foot of Mount Gold, We built a hut and lived, while day by day my body grew heavy, Passing months and my belly swelling, A boy was born, Giving him the name Kintaro as well, While spending two or three months, North-Illuminating Kami, the sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, Appeared and began an investigation of faith, My beloved person was discovered to be The precious prince of the divine person residing in the capital of Tsukushi province, Saying that exposing his parents' shame Was painful, he became flustered and started, saying, 'Koriya Takahime, Takahime, If I hear it, you are of the Ural teachings, Born from the womb of Ōgetsuhime, How could we stay together for long? The punishment of the kami is also terrifying, Our bond ends here, Shall we give up and part here?' At these abrupt words, My heart was overturned, Though I wept, coaxed, and pleaded, He shook off his sleeve and hurried away, Fleeing into the gathering darkness, Leaving behind a single guardian sword Engraved with the character 'Azuma' And the stamp of 'Taka', Leaving this sword as a memento, Lamenting the ruthlessness of the man Who vanished like clouds and mist,

As the body of a woman holding an infant, With no means whatsoever, Writing the name Kintaro On the brocade wrap of the guardian sword And on the amulet bag, Though pitiful, abandoning the child at a crossroads, Weeping bitterly, I parted from this place, Intending to explore the En-on-gō of Mesopotamia And the Brahmin teachings, For a while I went on pilgrimage and listened to the teachings of the kami, Yet if I guard Ananai-kyō, Which my husband protected, Will the blessings and fortune of the kami grant me An era when I might meet my beloved husband once again someday? Pretending to be a mitama of the lineage Without lowering Ananai-kyō, Establishing a dwelling in the realm of Fusa, Just as I was opening the teachings, Because the whereabouts of the Hensei Joshi Did not suit my heart, I combined the teachings of Ural and Ananai To form the Uranai teachings...

§ 2

Displaying the great sign, retreating into the village of Kitayamamura, and opening the teaching there, advancing thence to the central place of the island of the deities of Orokoro, close to the port of Yura, she sent Kurohime to Makutsugahara to make her the master of the teaching, and, agitated with the desire to reform the path of the teachings of Onikumohiko and Ananahi of Brahmanism from the very roots, she broke her heart into a thousand pieces, but the infinite mercy and compassion of the Lord God Kamususanōnōokami soaked through and penetrated her body, and she entered into the true path of Ananahi, and being appointed as a master of the teaching, she has come to serve until this very day; when she thinks upon it, when she thinks upon it, how deeply sinful she is!

I am the Magatsukami of this world; the dust and dirt that I have piled up until now, there is no way to cleanse it, what an irremediable shame; at the moment when she feels shame in her heart, pondering upon the tale of Kurohime-sama, the personal circumstances of Takekuniwake, the kamutsukasa of the mansion of Takehi in the Kumasokuni of the island of Tsukushi, without a doubt he cannot be other than her own beloved child; ah, Kamunagara, kamunagara, how deep is the blessing of the deities, and in the sacred precinct of the brocade palace, the joy of having been able to search out the whereabouts of her dear child!

Nevertheless, where in the world is the life of her husband at that time now? If he is living in this world, surely he must be remembering the destination of her child together with the passing years,

and grieving his heart morning and evening; ah, Kamunagara, kamunagara, Kuninotachikotsu-no-Ōkami, Kamususanōnō-Ōkami, Kan-satsukatsu-no-Ōkami, pray save the pitifulness of wandering in the sorrowful rapids of parent and child even a day sooner, Kamunagara.

Takahime prays and entreats with reverence and purity before the presence of the deities; ah, Kamunagara, kamunagara, may your august spirits prosper!

Thus she sang in a feeble tone, and bashfully bowed her head in that place.

Kurohime contained a slight smile, and briskly speaking:

Kurohime: 'Takahime-sama, I have learned of your true origin. Oh my, how fearful a thing an age is! Up until now I believed you to be about the rank of a grandchild of Wakahimegimi-no-Mikoto, but oh, to have been born from the womb of the fearful Uralhiko and Uralhime, you are like a dream, my! And oh, it appears that you, quite like myself, played your own selfish and arbitrary will when you were young. The deities say that if you think each other people are wicked, it is all oneself that is wicked, but my, to have drawn together only human dregs like this and made them perform such important services. When I think upon this, I truly cannot help feeling grateful for the great mercy and compassion of the deities. Needless to say Kamususanōnōokami-sama and Kunitakehiko-no-Mikoto-sama, the kyōshu-sama of Kotoyoriwake must certainly have known it as well; there was a time when Kotoyoriwake-gami-sama turned to me and said that Takahime is by no means of the lineage of the mitama of strictness, and that she was a woman born from the womb of Ōgetsuhime. At that time I thought, what is the kyōshu of the super-modern style talking about without knowing the reason, there is a limit to speaking ill of others, and it made me angry, and from then on I came to think of you all the more, and Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto became an unbearable irritation to me. I had to keep the commandment not to revile others, yet what a pathetic thing for one who is a kyōshu to utter, there could be no doubt it was the protection of an evil spirit, I despised him in my heart, but thinking of it now, truly Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama is also a great personage. His august intent is to polish the soul of any evil spirit whatsoever, make it repent, and use it for a magnificent service; I am so grateful that my tears will not stop as if for the very first time, boo-hoo-hoo!'

So saying, she bursts into tears and weeps aloud. Hearing the tale of Kurohime, the assembly could only marvel, as if for the very first time, at the vast hearts of the deities and especially of Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto.

Kurohime: 'Did such a thing take place? I too shall now completely repent with this. Becoming a newborn babe, hereafter I shall follow the instructions of Yoshikohime-sama and Tōsuke-sama in all matters, and let me be used for the very ends of your service. Knowing that I am a human being with such a bad mitama and origin, you even made me perform the august divine work of the Manibōshu, that profound grace I shall never forget, no matter when it may be. Truly, did I not know until now the beautiful and gentle heart of Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto, ah, how regrettable, surely Takahime was laughed at in the depths of her heart as an unseemly woman. It is truly a pathetic thing. If my son were to hear that he had such a mother, how much he would lament. Moreover, if the man of my youth's rendezvous were to be in this world and heard of my derailment, he would surely lose all affection for me, and even though we met by great fortune, he would surely run away. Truly, an evil spirit must have been protecting me after all... Ah, deities, please forgive me. Truly, until today I have done things that cannot be undone. This time I shall truly repent. Until now, whenever I said repentance, repentance, I was only adding a margin to the price.'

Akihiko suddenly bursts out laughing,

Akihiko: 'Utfu-hu-hu-hu Takahime-sama, what kind of thing is a margin of repentance? I do not understand it at all, my!'

Takahime: 'Akihiko, please forgive me. I am so ashamed I cannot say anything at all, because it has come to pass that I am even ashamed to meet my beloved child...'

Akihiko: 'When you say that, is your repentance today a net-price repentance with a fixed price tag, without even a half-cooked margin, otshu-ho-ho-ho!'

Tōsuke: 'Hey Akihiko, keep your mouth shut. If this scene of lamentation suddenly clears up, won't the mood be completely spoiled? Come now, from here on this Tōsuke will let you hear a scene of lamentation to make atonement for my sins, atsu-ha-ha-ha-ha!'
