Chapter 2: 灰猫婆 [917]

灰猫婆〔917〕

§ 1

Karl, while angry at Takahime's Buenriyo for rushing into the inner room, went to the inner room himself only to find, to his surprise, that the figures of Matsuwakahiko and Takahime were nowhere to be seen. When he happened to peer outside from the open window, he saw that the two were in the midst of a marathon race, keeping a distance of about one Itsuchiyō.

Karl: 'What a strong-willed old woman she is! I would love to chase after her, grab her, and punish her severely, but if I were to dash out of here now, the house would be left completely Rusu. As long as they keep that much distance, I surely cannot catch up. In the meantime, Matsuwakahiko-sama will likely hide somewhere. Since I have been asked to be the Rinji Rusuban, I cannot leave this house empty for even an Itsukoku. Ah, how Zannen...'

Muttering this, he returned to the Genkuwanguchi. There, Tsunehiko and Haruhiko, the Riyōnin, came rushing in.

Tsunehiko: 'Oh, is this Karl-sama? I would like to ask you a Chiyotsuto, has our Takahime-sama arrived here?'

Karl: 'Yes, whether she has arrived or is angry, I do not know, but a very strange thing has occurred. Right now, on the wide road in the back, a marathon race between a pine tree and a hawk is taking place.'

Haruhiko: 'A hawk has wings and flies through the Kūchiu, but that a pine tree should run is something I cannot Gatsuten.'

Karl: 'Because today is such a joyous day, the mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees are all dancing and rejoicing. Even I, Tsunehiko... wait, that is not right, unlike the usual days, Haruhiko... again, that is wrong... I am feeling in such a cheerful mood, like the spring flowers blooming, and I am filled with spirit. Even though I am usually slow-moving, today my spirits are light and my feet are light for some reason. Aha-ha-ha-ha!'

Tsunehiko: 'That aside, what has become of our leader, Takahime-sama?'

Karl: 'Nothing has become of her at all.'

Haruhiko: 'Has she come or has she not? Please tell us clearly.'

Karl: 'Yes, she entered, and immediately left through the back door. The Oshima is the entrance, the exit is the origin, and the Ryūgūkan has been determined as Takamagahara. Aha-ha-ha-ha!'

Tsunehiko: 'I am beginning to feel as if Kitsunehiko has been deceived by a fox.'

Karl: 'The age of Matsuwakahiko is coming. The wide-spreading Suekohime and Kuniyoriwake-no-Mikoto will finally become husband and wife tonight. Spirits and spirits' karmic connections gather; this spirit and that spirit, this one and that one are husband and wife, that one and that one are husband and wife. Examining the karmic nature of the spirits, the wedding, the wedding, the wedding ceremony begins tonight. Raising the sails on this boat in the bay, as they say. Aha-ha-ha-ha! Oh, how joyous, or perhaps not, it is a joy since the beginning of creation... Now Tsunehiko, Haruhiko, you two, what do you think?'

Tsunehiko: 'It is truly a wonderful thing. However, I cannot simply say it is wonderful... right, Haruhiko? It is a bit troublesome, you see.'

Karl: 'Do you two not approve of this upcoming marriage?'

Tsunehiko: 'No, no, why would you say that? We are in full support. However, last night, in the middle of the night, we were shaken awake by Takahime-sama... "What is your impression?" she asked. Thinking that Kuniyoriwake-sama was indeed a great person, I carelessly said that he was indeed a great man, and she was in such a terrible rage and in a very bad mood. Since then, Takahime-sama did not sleep a wink until dawn, and in the inner room, she was muttering to herself, saying things about Kuniyoriwake and Matsuwakahiko. I could not understand it clearly, but she was complaining a great deal. I also fell fast asleep after it was nearly dawn, and when I opened my eyes, Takahime-sama was nowhere to be seen. This must be it; she has gone to Matsuwakahiko-sama's house, and once again, her natural chronic illness has flared up, and she is surely troubling him by twisting logic. I could not bear for such a joyous occasion to be spoiled, so with the single-minded desire that we two should meet Takahime-sama and offer our opinions, we have rushed here without even washing our hands or eating breakfast, in a state of panic and confusion, without even taking the things we should have taken.'

§ 2

Haruhiko: 'This is truly a troublesome old woman. I have followed her all the way from Onokorojima to here for a long time, and it has been quite an ordeal.'

Karu: 'Ahahaha, she is quite a wild horse. However, if we leave her as she is, I do not know if she might ruin this auspicious day with something inauspicious, so we cannot just stand by like this... Tsunehiko-sama, please stay here and look after the place. I shall follow after Matsuwakahiko-sama, and Haruhiko shall follow after Takahime-sama; shall we two, Karu and Haruhiko, continue the second marathon?'

Tsunehiko: 'I leave it to you, as it is a difficult matter... Karu-sama, Haruhiko-sama, come, go quickly. It must surely be at the residence of Sutekohime-sama or Kotoyoriwake-sama...'

The two of them tucked up their skirts, and with large strides, they started running with all their might, making the earth resound with a thumping sound from the back entrance.

The two of them had run for only about two or three chō. There, a deep river about three ken wide was flowing. And a log bridge was laid across it. Although the river was deep, the water was sparse, and it was a shallow stream that barely submerged half of one's shins... When they looked suddenly, the single-log bridge had been cruelly removed, and Takahime was lying in the shape of a large character on the riverbed, stretched out flat. This was because Matsuwakahiko had temporarily removed the log bridge to prevent Takahime from following him. Takahime, with her head forward, could not stop suddenly due to the momentum of running with all her might, and having no choice, she fell in, even though she knew it was a river without a bridge. The two of them,

'Ya, this is a disaster,'

they said, and managed to climb down into the river, lifted up Takahime's unconscious body, sent her to her temporary residence, provided various care, recited Norito, and performed Chinkon. Gradually, Takahime regained consciousness and began to look around restlessly.

Karu: 'Excuse me, Takahime-sama, have you regained your senses? It was a very dangerous situation. Well, since Haruhiko and I were following behind, your precious life was saved, and there is nothing more auspicious than this.'

Takahime: 'Yes, thank you for that... if I were to offer my thanks, I would fall completely into your trap, but hen, it shall not go that way. I thought I was running as if someone were pushing me from behind, and Karu, you were chasing after me and pushed me under that log bridge, were you not? Even this Takahime is not such a fool as to try to cross a river without a bridge. Because you pushed me so hard from behind, I ended up falling in due to that momentum. It is suspicious that you say you knew I had fallen into such a deep river, is it not? You must have been favoring Matsuwakahiko and pushed me down. Otsohohoho, even if you plot evil, it shall be revealed immediately, shall it not?'

Haruhiko: 'Takahime-sama, is that not too much? I am actually a witness. How can you say such unreasonable things when you have just had your life saved with such effort?'

Takahime: 'Otsohohoho, foxes of the same hole are allied and saying sweet things. No matter how deep a scheme you plot like that river, the flow of your wisdom is shallow, so the bottom can be seen immediately, hohohoho.'

Karu: 'What a hateful old woman. I have already lost my patience with her.'

Takahime: 'That is so, that is so, because you lost your patience with a hateful old woman, you pushed me down, did you not? Karu, you have confessed with your own mouth and your own hands, have you not?'

Haruhiko: 'Ah, how pathetic... here, Karu-sama, please, for my sake, I know you must be angry, but please endure it.'

'Pardon me.'
