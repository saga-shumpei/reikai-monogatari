Chapter 14: Soul の洗濯 [929]

魂の洗濯〔929〕

§ 1

Terisutan descends the slope, singing in a manner resembling Karl and keeping time with his feet. The party of five, holding their bellies and laughing, put strength into their toes with every step, descending unsteadily while relying on their staffs.

Terisutan: 'The summit of Mount Teru, / Leaving the present, a generation ago, / Though I say long ago, it is thirty years. / Masakayamazumikami-sama, / Together with Satsuki-hime, / Dwelling in the mansion of Uzu, / Opened the teaching and comforted the people. / The Mioshihe of Ananai-kyō, / High in the sky of Argentina, / The Uzu capital that shone, / Looking back at it, the three, / The Sendenshi of Matsutakyōme, / Climbed to this summit, / Lamenting the lingering traces, together with / The Keshin of the Kami Kagatora, / The historic site where they sang. / Takahime-sama directed the Kototama, / Toward the mansion of Uzu, / The intensity of the Hofukairinki, / Terisutan hears it from the side.'

Terisutan: 'It has truly become pathetic. / It is well said that a sparrow does not forget the hawk until it is a hundred. / From now on, if Takahime-sama's / Heart becomes softened, / She will surely know the pathos of things. / Being only rigid is not the way. / Ottodokkoi, it is dangerous! / If you are careless, you will not be able to bear it / If you ride the stone-wheel and fall. / Saying it is a jewel, it is a jewel, noisily / They were making a clamor, but on this path, / If you take the many stone-jewels / That have rolled down and take them home, / And show them to Kurohime-sama, / She will surely rejoice, jump, and cling to them, / And be firmly delighted. / Untokodokkoi, dokkoishiyo. / Takahime-sama, what are you doing? / Even if it is a stone, if it is round, / It resembles a jewel well. / Kuniyorawake fished it up, / It is said that you were angry / Because you received a fish. / Untokodokkoi, korewashiyo. / If you dawdle, you will tumble. / Kuniyorawake and Suetsuhime, / Those two people must also be / Tumbled completely by now. / Even if one is to fall, / It is unbearable to fall here. / If you descend a little from here, / There is a waterfall where Suetsuhime / Saved Ishikuma. / Shall we all gather and go? / As we leave Takasago-jima, / Shedding sweat and removing filth, / Purifying the Mitama and growing steadily, / Crossing the great ocean, / Finally, auspiciously, to the Mahuraba / Where the Kami of Onogoro dwells, / It is grateful to think of returning. / Dokkoishiyo, dokkoishiyo. / The Kami appears on the surface / And separates the good from the evil. / Kuniyorawake-no-Kami-sama, / The reward for good has come around, / And made him the administrator of Chin. / Because my Mitama is bad, / Just like Takahime-sama, / On this island where I have come with such effort, / Without even taking one jewel well, / Shall I return in vain? How pathetic. / By what karma / Is my Mitama so ill-fated? / Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. / May the spirit be blessed. / Untokodokkoi, dokkoishiyo. / Everyone, be careful, it is dangerous. / There, again, there is a pebble.'

Terisutan: 'If you slip, fall, and foam at the mouth, / Do not become a burden / To Takahime-sama. / Terisutan will be careful / From the heart, everyone. / Although it is said that man is the child of the Kami and the palace of the Kami, / The people of the present / Are all becoming enemies of the Kami, / And have become palaces of demons. / Standing out at the waterfall of Ken, / Purify the heart and cleanse the body, / Throw away the Magatsuhi that has attached, / Become a child of the true Kami, / And become a dwelling of the Kami. / Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. / I pray before the presence of the Kami.'

The party of Takahime, while descending the Mount Teru slope toward the west, arrived at the middle of the slope where the sound of a waterfall could be heard intensely. From here, the party of Takahime headed toward the waterfall of Ken to perform Misogi, searching for the sound. It is a considerably wide and high waterfall. This is the famous waterfall where, when the Brahman priest Ishikuma was performing Mizugori, he was bewitched by a great serpent and fell into a life-or-death situation, at which time he was saved by the party of Suetsuhime. When they approached and looked, a large man was alone, immersed in the waterfall with single-minded devotion.

Takahime, without caring about the details, threw off her thin robe and jumped toward the basin of the waterfall with a splash, but for some reason, Takahime's figure disappeared from that moment on, and nothing could be seen. The four, Ryūkokawake, Te, Ka, and Tomohiko, hurriedly became naked and crawled into the waterfall basin, searching, and began to search with all their might, wondering if Takahime's physical body had perhaps sunk to the bottom of the water. However, they could not find her whereabouts no matter what. All of them were at a loss and prayed for Takahime's physical body to surface as soon as possible.

A man finished his Mizugōri in an Iuiu manner, and while wiping his body with a towel, he came before Takayorihime.

Man: 'It is quite hot, is it not? Would you not also like to step into the waterfall? It is a very cool waterfall, and I feel as though the grime of my Mitama has been completely removed.'

Takayorihime: 'Yes, thank you very much. However, a person named Takahime-sama, who is one of my Dōkaushiya, just now jumped into the Takitsubo and her figure has disappeared... Look, just as you see there, four men have become Matsupadaka and are searching the Minasoko. What do you think? Is this Takitsubo really that deep?'

Man: 'It is not a particularly deep Takitsubo, but when I was under the waterfall just before, Takahime-sama took off her Irui and, without even praying to the Kamisama, jumped in headfirst. However, there should be no danger to her Inochi. She is likely being made to perform the Misogi rite by the Kamisama.'

Takayorihime: 'Even if you say that, a Daibun amount of time has already passed. It is impossible for a Ningen to remain alive in the Suichiu for so long with a Nikutai. What should we do to save her?'

Man: 'I am a person called Taruchīru, who used to be the captain of the Takashimamaru, but since Takahime-san is a person of great endurance, she is likely receiving the purification of the Kamisama as she departs from this Takasagojima. Well, please do not worry.'

He is smiling with a calm face. The three people excluding Ryūkokuwake searched the Takitsubo thoroughly, and as they could not find the figure of Takahime no matter what, they let out voices of despair and gathered before the two while remaining Matsupadaka.

Ryūkokuwake: 'Mother-sama, it is no use no matter what we do. Even if her Nikutai were to appear, there is no doubt that her life has already ended. A troublesome thing has happened.'

He hangs his head with a thoughtful look.

Taruchīru: 'Were you Ryūkokuwake-sama, the missionary of the Ananai-kyō?'

Ryūkokuwake: 'Oh, I do not know who you are, but I was so flustered that I did not notice you were here, and I apologize. What do you think? Is Takahime-sama already beyond help?'

Taruchīru: 'Well, do not lose heart. There is nothing to do but leave everything to Kannagara. Takahime-sama is a person with a very strong ego, so without something like this, true polishing of the Mitama cannot be done.'

Tomohiko: 'Are you not Taruchīru-sama, the captain of the Takashimamaru? I am a person called Tomohiko; it has been a long time since we last met.'

Ryūkokuwake: 'Tomohiko-sama, how do you know this person?'

Tomohiko: 'He is the person who saved us when Takahime-sama, Haruhiko, and the three of us were coming to Takasagojima in a small boat.'

Ryūkokuwake: 'We have met in a rare place. It must be some kind of divine connection. And since you have come to such a place, is there not some deep reason for it?'

Taruchīru: 'While on board the Takashimamaru, I heard the doctrines of the Ananai-kyō from Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto and Kuniyoriwake-sama, and I immediately converted. I handed over the captaincy to my son, Teruchiru, and I followed Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto and Kuniyoriwake-sama to land at the port of Hara. By the order of Kotoyoriwake-sama, I was told to propagate the faith in the land of Teru, and I have been coming to this Inui waterfall from time to time to polish my Mitama. Today, I unexpectedly met the missionary of the Ananai-kyō, and I was struck by a truly pleasant feeling.'

Ryūkokuwake: 'That is a mysterious connection, but I am so worried about Takahime-sama's situation that I do not feel like listening to your story at leisure. I will conduct the search once more, so let us hear your story slowly later.'
