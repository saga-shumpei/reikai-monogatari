Chapter 8: Spring 駒 [923]

春駒〔923〕

§ 1

Haruhiko also stood up, expressed his congratulations, and began to sing.

Accompanying Takahime-sama, Haruhiko of Akitsu Island, from his beloved Furusato, shielded by the grace of the Mitama, spreads the Oshihe to the four corners of the land. A Masurao of the Yamatōnoko, together with Tsunehiko-sama, the two of them set sail from the Seto Sea. While rowing with oars, they crossed the rough Shihoji of Namitakahime-sama's Gokenmaku. Having run their ship onto an Anshō, they were saved by the captain of the Takashimamaru, Taruchiru-sama. Arriving safely at the port of Teru on the edge of the island of Namitakasago, they sought the Arika of the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu and other jewels. Following the trail of the Mizu no Inochi of Kotoyoriwake, they faced the Mirror Pond, burning with various anxieties. Starting from the Amazon River, in the midst of the Shigure Forest, they encountered Mallband. While trembling in fear, together with the four Kami-tsukasa sent by Kotoyoriwake-sama, they passed through the great forest, crossed the bridge of crocodiles, and faced the Capital of Rabbits. With the Reikō of Bōshi-ga-take as their signal, they finally climbed to the top and arrived here most auspiciously. Becoming the husband of the youngest princess, the Kami-tsukasa of Kuniyoriwake, they shall bind the path of Imose in Kairōdōketsu forever.

With that matter settled, Takahime, wondering whether her Mitama was clouded or not, clouded her own heart, ran here and there, and finally fell into a deep river. While being saved, she used twisted logic to claim she had been pushed in, which was utterly astonishing. Once again carried to the mansion, she returned to life, and while spitting out the same poisonous, hateful words as always, Karl Haruhiko became angry and glared at her. I, feeling sudden rage, lifted a nearby hibachi in my hand and, when I prepared to throw it at Takahime, Karl-sama said, 'Wait, wait, wait a moment, stop such violence.'

As he held me back, saying this and that, the hibachi fell instantly, and the area was covered in ash. Before anyone knew it, Takahime-sama had become an ash-covered old hag. In the midst of the three of them being covered in ash, Kotoyoriwake-sama opened the front door and entered. Although he tried to admonish her by saying various things, Takahime suddenly pretended to be mute and spun her body around. Like a caterpillar on a taro leaf or a silkworm pupa, she shook her head and wiggled her hips with a sour expression on her face. Suddenly, she ran off toward the mansion of the abandoned princess, and Haruhiko thought, 'This is unbearable.'

Together with the official Karl, when I went in pursuit, Takahime, with her Furuna-style eloquence, surrendered to the Kototama of the two, Kotoyoriwake and the abandoned princess, before anyone knew it. Reluctantly, she gave her consent, saying, 'Truly auspicious, truly auspicious, I shall also attend this evening, I shall celebrate something.' When I watched with bated breath to see what she would celebrate, she only said things that were better left unsaid, searching for faults in Kuniyoriwake. I felt angry, thinking, 'There is a limit to throwing cold water on such an auspicious banquet; what a spiteful old hag.' If it were any other time, I would not have let it go like this.

However, today is a truly auspicious day, so I held back until now, thinking it would not do to fight, but my inner self would not accept it. What an astonishing old hag. Everyone must surely be angry after hearing that song of Takahime's. No matter how angry you may be, please forgive her just for this evening. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth were to sink, how could such a twisted soul ever return to its original state? A bent sword can never be returned to its original scabbard.

There is no choice but to put it into a hot fire, add a heavy hammer, and re-forge it. How could a soul that is completely bent ever return to its original state? Even the straightest staff, when immersed in water and viewed, always appears bent. In the presence of the Mizu no Mitama, if one immerses a soul with a bent heart, how could it ever appear straight? Thinking that Haruhiko had come all this way following a person who does not understand this much, I am truly, truly ashamed. I did not know she was such a person; I am too astonished for words.

Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. May she see and hear things straight and true. Takahime-sama, these days...

Haruhiko: 'Having been struck by Shinkeikuwabin hysteria, I declare and rectify my words; please forgive me, everyone, for the Goburei I have committed until now. I, this Haruhiko, who have come following in your company, offer an apology on behalf of Takahime. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. It is regrettable to speak of such things in this place, but the Fukushiyugojin who has possessed my body will not consent. I, who have chattered out insults against my will, pray to the Amatsukami, the Kunitsukami, and the Kunitamagami—please Minaho this fault of Haruhiko, and for this day alone, I beseech you to forgive me. Oh, Kuniyoriwake-sama!'

Haruhiko: 'Please live in harmony with the Sueko Hime, and for the sake of the Kami and for the sake of the world, I pray you will serve forever. I, Haruhiko, offer this request with all my Magokoro. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. May your Mitama be blessed.'

Teristan stood up and began to sing. The song:

In the Chiushinchi of Onokorojima, Appearing on Takaharuyama, Following Takayorihime, Teristan and Karlins, Became the gods of the left and right guards, And are throwing their weight around. Takahime and Kurohime, both of them, Came as far as the Forest of Heaven, And there, the two fought among themselves. Watching for the opportunity, through various Means, they pushed both of them Into a rocky cave, Thinking to make them vomit the Diamond-like indestructible wish-fulfilling jewel.

The party of Kuniyoriwake Appeared here, And through a mysterious bond, we Were initiated into the Sangokyo, And entrusted our bodies to the Kurohime mansion, Near the Palace of Brocade.

While we were serving, following the incident of the loss of the golden Jewel, We were driven out by Takahime-sama, And together with Karlins, We drifted upon the vast ocean, And encountered the divine officials Of Takayorihime and Ryukokubetsu, And the four of us, gathered together, finally arrived safely At the island of Takasago, Distributing our attention to various miscellaneous matters, While searching for the whereabouts of the jewel, In the forest of Shigure, We became the gods of the King of Rabbits, And while passing the days, Takahime and her party of eight Came strolling along to that place. 'You are safe, you are well, you are healthy, It is a joyous occasion,' Before we could finish exchanging such greetings,

While chanting the Amatsu Norito, They faced towards Boshigadake, And met squarely with the star performer of the day, Kunitsuyoriwake-sama, And emboldened, the leader of the Kototama and the others, The party of eighteen, Returned to the Capital of Uzu. That the unexpected Sueko Hime And even Kami Susanoo-no-Mikoto Were appearing here, I did not know even in my dreams. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. The divine majesty of the Kami has appeared, And the divine official Kuniyoriwake Is holding the joyous ceremony Of the union of the bed with Sueko Hime. Attending this banquet, Not to mention the sacred sake, sacred offerings, and sacred water, We were served various treasures of the sea, rivers, mountains, and fields, Singing and dancing, how valiant, How joyful to celebrate a thousand years. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. May your Mitama be blessed.

To Takahime-sama, who had been cast out, I was told various Strange songs here again, And I, thinking 'I am impressed, I am impressed,' Was tilting my head. When I say I was impressed, It is that I was truly amazed At the stubborn, competitive soul Of the ill-natured Takahime. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. At such a joyous banquet, I did not wish to have a dispute With Takahime-sama, but for the sake of the future, I offered a brief opinion As a warning to Takahime-sama.

When I think of it, before the awe-inspiring Kami, And before the presence of Sueko Hime, I beg you to forgive my impoliteness In not being reserved. Teristan offers this request With all his Magokoro. Oh, divine official of Kototama, And all other people present, Let us unite our hearts, And while relaxing, partake of the food and drink, Feasting abundantly, dancing and playing, For a thousand autumns, ten thousand years, ten thousand times ten thousand years. May the world of Takasago forever Prosper and flourish, and may the foundation of the future world Be firmly established and solidified. Let us not let this joyous banquet Flow away in vain, but forever Engrave it in our hearts and rejoice. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. We celebrate before the presence of the Kami, We offer blessings before the presence of the Kami.
