Chapter 18: Kami Wind Pure [933]

神風清〔933〕

§ 1

Akiyamahiko appeared in the spacious hall where Tōsuke, Tamaharuwake, and the rest of the assembly had gathered,

Minasama: 'Gokurau de gozaimashita. Takahimeto hajime Kurohime, Takayorihime, Tatsukuniwakenomikoto goitsukau wa yoyaku kamunagara no gokeirin ni yori, watashi no yakata made okaeri kudasaimashite, jitsu ni kore hura yorokobashii koto wa gozaimasenu. Tsuite wa Kotoyoriwakenomikotodono ga sekinin o oute, seichi o tachinoki ni narimashita daijiken no kongen taru Mani no hotsushiyu no arika ga, Takahimeto ika goichidō no netsusei ni yori, hanmei itashimashita ni tsuite wa, yagate chikaki uchi ni Mani no hotsushiyu o motte okaeri ni naru koto de gozaimaseu. Minasama wa dōzo, kore yori seichi ni kaeri, kuwangei no gojiyunbi o negaimasu. Kuniharutatsunonomikoto, Toyokunihimenomikoto o hajime, kamigaminsama no gojinji wa tōtei wareware no kataritsukubeki tokoro de wa gozaimasenu.'

Raising a voice moistened with tears of joy, he delivered his greetings. Tōsuke, Tamaharuwake, and the rest, while feeling astonished and bewildered by words contrary to Akiyamahiko's original premise, with expressions of anticlimax that Takahime and her party had vanished from the spot without a trace, hurried back toward the holy ground.

In the Yahiro Hall of the Palace of Brocade within the holy ground, Tamateruhiko, Tamateruhime, and Eikohime, together with Murasakihime, accompanied by numerous senior officials, were waiting for the return of the party. Tōsuke stepped forward respectfully before the three divine officiants, bowed his head, and placed his hands flat on the floor,

Tōsuke: 'Yura no minato no Akiyamahiko no yakata e, Takahime goitsukau o mukae no tame mairimashita tokoro, Takeshimamaru ni norikomi, Takasagojima yori goitsukau hachinin okaeri ni narimashita. Sore yori Akiyamahiko yakata ni omuhen itashi, ichiya o akashi, iroiro no kōtai ni azukari, buji no kikoku o shuku shite iru sai, Kurohime mo okaeri ni nari Takahime goitsukau yonin no katagata wa Mani no hotsushiyu no arika ga wakatsuta toka de, sotsuto dokoka e odeni ni narimashita. Tsuite wa chikajika sono tama o ete seichi e okaeri ni naru kara, hayaku kaete kuwangei no junbi o seyo to no koto de gozaimashita. Nani ga nanda ka, watashi ni wa ikkō yōryō o emasenu ga, zehi naku koko made kaete mairimashita. Ika itaseba yorokoshii koto de gozaimaseu ka. Murasakihimeto-sama, dōzo anata yori sanbashira no shinshi e yoroshiku kongon o negaimasu.'

He spoke thus. Murasakihime answered 'Hai', ascended the elevated seat, knelt before the three pillars, and reported Tōsuke's words one by one. The three divine officiants—Eikohime, Tamateruhiko, and Tamateruhime—while smiling, nodded their heads up and down. Their demeanor appeared to hold a profound conviction somewhere within. The three divine officiants turned toward the divine presence, respectfully finished reciting the liturgy, and, casting a polite glance at all the divine officiants and believers, quietly slipped deep into the inner chambers of the mansion.

Murasakihime turned toward Tōsuke and said,

Murasakihime: 'Chokoku sanbashira no ōshinshi yori uketamawarimasureba, Takahime-sama wa ashita yonin zure nite okaeri no hazu de gozaimasu kara, dōzo kuwangei no junbi o asobashite kudasai mase.'

Tōsuke: 'Hai isai shōchi tsukamimashita.'

With these words, he withdrew from the spot, devoted his full strength to preparations for the welcome, and waited eagerly for Takahime's return.

When the dawn broke on the eighth day of the ninth month, Takahime, Takayorihime, Kurohime, and Tatsukuniwake, full of joy, holding aloft the Mani jewels, returned toward the Yahiro Hall of the Palace of Brocade, proceeded straight before the sanctuary, each holding their jewel, and stood by in silence. Seeing this state of affairs, Murasakihime immediately reported it to the three great divine officiants.

Hereupon Tamateruhiko, Tamateruhime, Eikohime, and Murasakihime attired themselves in ceremonial robes and appeared silently before the four. Tamateruhiko received the blue Mani jewel from Takahime's hands, Tamateruhime received the red jewel from Kurohime's hands, Eikohime received the white jewel from Takayorihime's hands, and Murasakihime received the yellow Mani jewel from Tatsukuniwake's hands. Holding them high above their heads in a leisurely manner, they proceeded to the divine presence in the Palace of Brocade, respectfully placed them upon the altar, descended once more to the Yahiro Hall, took Takahime and the other three by the hands, guided them into the hall, and together recited the liturgy of thanksgiving and prayer. Accompanied by all eight, they proceeded toward the inner room of the Instructor's Hall, exhausted their mutual joy, and celebrated their safe return and its success.

原文（近代文語）

秋山彦は東助、玉治別其他一同の集まる広間に現はれ、

『皆様、御苦労で御座いました。高姫様初め黒姫、鷹依姫、竜国別の御一行は漸く惟神の御経綸に依り、私の館までお帰り下さいまして、実にこれ位喜ばしい事は御座いませぬ。就いては言依別命様が責任を負うて、聖地をお立退きになりました大事件の根源たる麻邇の宝珠の所在が、高姫様以下御一同の熱誠に依つて、判明致しましたに付いては、軈て近き内に麻邇の宝珠を持つてお帰りになることで御座いませう。皆様はどうぞ、これより聖地に帰り、歓迎の御準備を願ひます。国治立命様、豊国姫命様を初め、神々様の御仁慈は到底吾々の語り尽すべき所では御座いませぬ』

と嬉し涙に湿つた声を張上げて挨拶をするのであつた。東助、玉治別其他の一同は、秋山彦の案に相違の言葉に驚き且つ怪しみ乍ら、高姫以下の此場より何処ともなく消えたるに拍子抜けしたる面色にて、急ぎ聖地を指して帰り行くのであつた。

聖地の錦の宮の八尋殿には、玉照彦、玉照姫、英子姫は、紫姫と共に数多の幹部を従へ、一行の帰り来るを待ちつつあつた。東助は三人の神司の前に恭しく進み寄り、頭を下げ両手をつかへ、

東助『由良の港の秋山彦の館へ、高姫一行を迎への為参りました所、竹島丸に乗込み、高砂島より一行八人お帰りになりました。それより秋山彦館にお迎へ致し、一夜を明かし、いろいろの款待に預り、無事の帰国を祝して居る際、黒姫もお帰りになり高姫一行四人の方々は麻邇の宝珠の所在が分つたとかで、ソツトどこかへ御出でになりました。就いては近日其玉を得て聖地へお帰りになるから、早く帰つて歓迎の準備をせよとの事で御座いました。何が何だか、私には一向要領を得ませぬが、是非なく此処まで帰つて参りました。如何致せば宜しいので御座いませうか。紫姫様、どうぞあなたより三柱の神司へ宜しく言上を願ひます』

と云つた。紫姫は『ハイ』と答へて高座にのぼり、三柱の前に額づき、東助の言葉を一々言上した。英子姫、玉照彦、玉照姫の三柱の神司はニコニコし乍ら、頭を縦に振つてゐられる。其の様子がどこやらに深き確信あるものの如く見られた。三柱の神司は神前に向ひ、恭しく祝詞を奏上し終つて、一同の神司及び信徒に目礼を施し乍ら館の奥深く忍び入り給うた。

紫姫は東助に向ひ、

『只今三柱の大神司より承はりますれば、高姫様は明日四人連れにてお帰りのはずで御座いますから、どうぞ歓迎の準備を遊ばして下さいませ』

東助『ハイ委細承知仕りました』

と此場をさがり、歓迎の準備に全力を尽し、高姫の帰るを今や遅しと待ちつつあつた。

明くれば九月八日、高姫、鷹依姫、黒姫、竜国別の四人は嬉々として、麻邇の宝珠を捧じ、錦の宮の八尋殿指して帰り来り、直に神殿の前に進み、各玉を捧持して、無言の儘控へて居る。紫姫は此体を見て、直に三柱の大神司に奉告した。

茲に玉照彦、玉照姫、英子姫、紫姫は礼装を調へ、四人の前に無言の儘現はれ、玉照彦は高姫の手より青色の麻邇の宝珠を受取り、玉照姫は黒姫の手より赤色の宝珠を受取り、英子姫は鷹依姫の手より白色の宝珠を受取り、紫姫は竜国別の手より黄色の麻邇の宝珠を受取り、頭上高く捧げ乍ら悠々として錦の宮の神前に進み、案上に恭しく安置され、再び八尋殿に下り来り、高姫外三人の手を取り、殿内に導き感謝祈願の祝詞を共に奏上し、八人相伴ひて、教主殿の奥の間さして進み入り、互に歓を尽して、無事の帰国と其成功を祝し玉うたのである。

§ 2

Hidekohime: 'Minasama, zuibun gokurau de gozaimashita nā. Shinkai no gokeirin wa tautei, ningen domo no hakari shiru tokoro de gozaimasenu. Tada nanigoto mo Kamisama no gomeirei ni shitagau yori hoka ni michi wa gozaimasenu'

Takahime: 'Hai, arigatou gozaimasu. Watashi mo amari Kamisama no omichi o daiji ni omou amari, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama no yarikata o mite, Ōkamisama no gokeirin o baugai shi, futatabi Ama no Ihato o tozasu akuma no sohi to omoitsume, iroiro zatsuta to gokai o tsukai baugai nomi itashite mairimashita. Kefu to natte kaomireba jitsuni hazukashiu gozaimasu. Watashi no kaishin ga okureta bakari de, minasama ni iroiro no gokurau o kake sawagashimashita. Kotoyoriwake no kyōshiyusama mo, watashi no tame ni taihen na gokannan o asobashi, jitsuni mōwake ga gozaimasenu. Taikesha da toka, taishureijū no mitama da toka, sekai aku no eizō da toka, iroiro zatsuta to ui furashi, jama bakari itashite kimashita, kaomireba watashi koso akuma no toryo to nari, shizu shizu ni taishureijū no okonai o nashi, sekai aku no kongan o ajiwainagara hito no koto bakari kuwashiku mōshiagete kimashita. Watashi no ugu ukatsu, imamara benkai no ji mo gozaimasenu. Taikesha to iu koto wa, kesshite warui imi de wa gozaimasenanda. Amari jinbutsu ga ōkisugite, wareware no mitama de wa sokuryō suru koto ga dekinakatta tame ni, wake no wakaranu kyōshu da to omoi, taikesha da to tsute mazutta no de gozaimashita. Jinji no fukai, tautei wareware bonpu no shiru tokoro de nai koto o, fukaku fukaku mi ni shimiwatatsu de kanjimashite gozaimasu. Nanigoto asettemo, mitama no innen dake no koto yori dekiru mono de wa gozaimasenu. Douzo imama no futsugou o yurushi kudasaimasete, mitama sōō no goyō o ōseshuke kudasaimasereba, arigatou zonjimasu'

Hidekohime: 'Sono kotoba o kiite, waga mo anshin itashimashita. Tamateruhiko-sama, Tamateruhime-sama, sazo oyorokobi de gozaimaseu. Daiichi, Kunitachiruto Ōkamisama no okeshin Kunitakenomikoto-sama, Kami Susanoo Ōkamisama wa anata no gokaishin o kiki asobashite, sazo omandoku ni omososu de gozaimaseu. Anata no gokaishin ga dekite, mitama no goinnen ga gokai ni nareba, Sanguikyō wa jōge itchi shi kamigyō ni sanka shi, Miroku shinsei no kiso ga kakujitsu ni kizukiagerareru koto to yorokobi ni taemasenu'

Takahime: 'Hai, nani kara nani made, gotuui kudasaimasete arigatou zonjimasu'

Kurohime: 'Watashi wa moiga nani ni mo mōshiageru koto wa gozaimasenu. Tada kansha yori hoka ni michi wa gozaimasenu. Douzo banji yoroshiku, kongo totemo futsugou naki sama, gotuui o negaimasu'

Takayuehime: 'Watashi mo Takahime-sama ni seichi o oidasare, iroiro to kannankurou o itashimashite, ichiji wa Takahime-sama o urami mōshita koto sae gozaimashitaga, ima to natte kangaete mitemasubereba, nanigoto mo mina Kamisama no oshimikumi de, kumotta tamashii o migite, shinkai no goyō ni tatete yarau to no otorinashi de atta koto o, imama no gotoku kanjimashita. Jitsuni mōshi yō mo naki arigataki Mizu no omi no omososhi, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama no okokorozukai, orei wa kuchide wa mōshiageraremasenu'

To yorokobinamida ni kakumureru.

Ryūkokubake: 'Shinon no takaki fukaki, kansha no hoka gozaimasenu. Nanito banji futsuka na mono, yoroshiku onegai itashimasu'

Tamateruhiko, Tamateruhime are four towards applying pacification, leisurely returning to their own living room. Takahime for the first time clearing away her past self, resolved to participate in the divine service regarding the jewel of the blue mani, and was able to deeply comprehend that the service of the vajra-indestructible nyoi hōshu does not attach to her own body.

○

Here, Hatsuwakihime, who had served the divine service of the vajra-indestructible nyoi hōshu, for the first time became the kyōshu of the Yayhiroden in the Palace of Brocade, and Tamanohime, who had served the service of the purple precious jewel, alongside Wakahiko (later given the name Kunitakuwake) as husband and wife, came to serve in the divine hall of the Forest of Ikuta.

原文（近代文語）

英子姫『皆様、随分御苦労で御座いましたなア。神界の御経綸は到底、人間共の量り知る所で御座いませぬ。只何事も神様の御命令に従ふより外に途は御座いませぬ』

高姫『ハイ、有難う御座います。私も余り神様の御道を大事に思ふ余り、言依別命様の行方を見て、大神様の御経綸を妨害し、再び天の岩戸をとざす悪魔の所為と思ひつめ、いろいろ雑多と誤解を致し妨害のみ致して参りました。今日となつて顧みれば実に恥かしう御座います。私の改心が遅れた計りで、皆様にいろいろの御苦労をかけ騒がしました。言依別の教主様も、私の為に大変な御艱難を遊ばし、実に申訳が御座いませぬ。大化者だとか、体主霊従の身魂だとか、世界悪の映像だとか、いろいろ雑多と云ひふらし、邪魔計り致して来ましたが、顧みれば私こそ悪神の虜となり、知らず識らずに体主霊従の行ひをなし、世界悪の根本を敢てしながら人の事計り喧しく申上げて来ました。私の迂愚迂濶、今更弁解の辞も御座いませぬ。大化者と云ふ事は、決して悪い意味では御座いませなんだ。余り人物が大き過ぎて、吾々の身魂では測量することが出来なかつた為に、訳の分らぬ教主だと思ひ、大化者だと云つて罵つたので御座いました。仁慈の深き、到底吾々凡夫の知る所ではないことを、深く深く身に沁み渡つて感じまして御座います。何程あせつても、身魂の因縁だけの事より出来るものでは御座いませぬ。どうぞ今迄の不都合をお許し下さいまして、身魂相応の御用を仰せ付け下さいますれば、有難う存じます』

英子姫『其お言葉を聞いて、妾も安心致しました。玉照彦様、玉照姫様、さぞお喜びで御座いませう。第一、国治立大神様の御化身国武彦命様、神素盞嗚大神様は貴女の御改心をお聞き遊ばして、さぞ御満足に思召すで御座いませう。貴女の御改心が出来て、身魂の御因縁が御了解になれば、三五教は上下一致して御神業に参加し、五六七神政の基礎が確実に築き上げられる事と喜びに堪へませぬ』

高姫『ハイ、何から何まで、御注意下さいまして有難う存じます』

黒姫『私は最早何にも申上げる事は御座いませぬ。只感謝より外に道は御座いませぬ。どうぞ万事宜しく、今後とても不都合なき様、御注意を願ひます』

鷹依姫『私も高姫様に聖地を追ひ出され、いろいろと艱難苦労を致しまして、一時は高姫様をお恨み申したことさへ御座いましたが、今となつて考へて見ますれば、何事も皆神様の御仕組で、曇つた魂を研いて、神界の御用に立ててやらうとの御取りなしであつたことを、今更の如く感じました。実に申上げ様もなき有難き瑞の御霊の思召し、言依別命様のお心遣ひ、お礼は口では申上げられませぬ』

と嬉し涙にかき暮れる。

竜国別『神恩の高き深き、感謝の外御座いませぬ。何卒万事不束な者、宜しくお願ひ致します』

玉照彦、玉照姫は四人に向ひ鎮魂を施し、悠々として我居間に帰り玉うた。高姫は初めて今迄の我を払拭し、青色の麻邇の宝珠の玉に対する神業に参加することを決意し、金剛不壊の如意宝珠の御用の吾身に添はざることを深く悟ることを得たのである。

○

茲に金剛不壊の如意宝珠の御用を勤めたる初稚姫は初めて錦の宮の八尋殿の教主となり、紫色の宝玉の御用に仕へたる玉能姫は生田の森の神館に於て、若彦（後に国玉別と名を賜ふ）と夫婦相並びて、生田の森の神館に仕ふることとなつた。

§ 3

Furthermore, Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, who had served in the divine work of the jewel of ōgon, departed from the hall of Iso together with the divine spirit of Sukunahikonanokami, crossed over to Armenia, and appeared in Jerusalem, where he built a magnificent kiuden, and by means of the itoku of the jewel of ōgon and the itoku of the jewel of ryu, came to instruct and transform shinjin universally.

Moreover, Umekohime returned to the hall of Iso where her chichiōkami resided, and by virtue of the itoku of the purple jewel of mani, served at the Isoyakata of the land of Fusa to participate in the shingefu, while Takahime began her service as ōkamutsukasa in Yahirodono, becoming a subordinate of Murasakihime to serve with shinmeu, and Kurohime, Takayorihime, and Tatsukuniwake also each served in their respective shinmu according to their mitama, thus coming to diligently pursue the fundamental activities for the accomplishment of shinsei.

As a result of the self-sacrificing activities of these kami, the time finally arrived at the foot of Mount Yomio, and it came to pass that he appeared as Kuninotokotachinomikoto, commencing the repair and solidifying of the three realms of visible, hidden, and divine. This precisely is the cause by which Kunisotsu Kuninotokotachinomikoto, the ancestor of the nation, descended upon the mitama of the transformed male, the founder Deguchi, and appeared from within the courtyard of Jingū Hongū, thus teaching the doctrine of Ōmoto. Furthermore, by means of the self-sacrificing activities of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto, from the divine spirit of the jewel of ōgon, the mitama of the transformed female appeared, taking the sacred mountain of Mount Takakuma as its basal point, thereby assisting and opening the doctrine of Ōmoto. Ah, Kaminagara Tamashii Sachihakobase.

原文（近代文語）

又黄金の玉の神業に奉仕したる言依別命は少名彦名神の神霊と共に斎苑の館を立出で、アーメニヤに渡り、エルサレムに現はれ、立派なる宮殿を造り、黄金の玉の威徳と琉の玉の威徳とを以て、普く神人を教化し玉ふこととなつた。

又梅子姫は父大神のまします斎苑の館に帰り、紫の麻邇の玉の威徳に依つてフサの国の斎苑館に仕へて神業に参加し、高姫は八尋殿に大神司を初め紫姫の部下となつて神妙に奉仕し、黒姫、鷹依姫、竜国別もそれぞれの身魂だけの神務に奉仕し、神政成就の基礎的活動を励む事となつたのである。

此等の神々の舎身的活動の結果、いよいよ四尾山麓に時節到来して、国常立尊と現はれ、現幽神三界の修理固成を開始し玉ふことを得るに至つたのである。これが即ち大本の教を国祖国常立尊が変性男子の身魂、出口教祖に帰懸し玉ひて神宮本宮の坪の内より現はれ玉うた原因である。又言依別命の舎身的活動に依つて黄金の玉の威霊より変性女子の身魂、高熊山の霊山を基点として現はれ、大本の教を輔助し且つ開くこととなつたのである。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
