Chapter 16: Darkness Night の Song [931]

暗夜の歌〔931〕

§ 1

While they were talking thus, the sun sank into the waves of the Seikai, and the veil of darkness wrapped the surroundings Kokukoku. The Senkiyaku, none of whom could distinguish one from another, each rested their chin on their hand and fell into sleep. From within the darkness, a kind of song was heard as a woman's voice. That song:

Takahime: 'When I reflect upon it, in the summer of Kozo, With the Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu, The Mani treasure, with a Murakimo Heart clouded, Takahime Followed after Kotoyoriwake, Setting sail upon the sea of Seto, Seeking the Daishō Shimane floating on the waves, searching For the Arika of the Hōgiyoku, Entrusting my body to a Tanana boat, Together with Tsunehiko and Haruhiko, Just as we were coming near To Takasagojima, by error Our Mifune was shattered against the rocks, And there were times when Shintai was restricted, But by the Senchiyō of the Takashimamaru We were rescued and at last Arrived safely at the land of Teru, Searching for the Arika Of Kotoyoriwake and the Hōgiyoku,'

Takahime: 'Facing the Mirror Pond and the wild plains, Facing the Forest of Sleet, Distributing my heart and breaking my body, Facing the capital of Uzu, Kuniyoriwake and Suekohime Bound the kelp of the husband-and-wife bond, Joining that banquet, I had an audience with the noble form Of the Lord God Susanoo, And here the party of six Left the capital of Uzu, Gradually climbing the steep slope Of the Teruyama Pass to rest, Descending further and further to the west, Facing the Inui Waterfall, The great waterfall famous for its sound, When I leaped in to perform purification, I was washed away by the deep waters, And into the Pond of Jewels where waves drift, Before I knew it, I was flowing away.'

Takahime: 'The sky of my heart grew distant, And there were times when I was at a loss, But Kiyokohime and Terukohime Appeared here, Saving me and Sending me to the foot Of the Inui Waterfall—oh, the joy! Ah, Kannagara Kannagara, God's grace is before my very eyes, While having a safe meeting With the party of Takayorihime, With a buoyant heart descending The steep slope to the port of Hara, When I arrived, by good fortune The Takashimamaru was even now About to set sail, Dancing for joy at God's grace, When our party boarded, Unexpectedly Matsu-sama, By the secret command of Kuniyoriwake and Matsuwaka,'

Takahime: 'The chief of the Hiko, was gallantly Monitoring the actions Of this Takahime! The divine soul of this Takahime, Polished at the Inui Waterfall, Shines like the sun and moon, And the sky of today has swept away The dust, the refuse, the clouds and the mist; You must not let your heart be troubled. Ah, Kannagara Kannagara, Illuminated by the light of the noble God, Returning to the land of Teru, Leaving the port of Hara behind, Crossing over the eight hundred paths of the tide, Returning to the sacred land of Aya, Where the Nishiki Palace is enshrined In the center of Onogorojima. How joyful is my own lot! How joyful is my own lot! Whether the morning sun shines or is clouded,'

Takahime: 'Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the great earth should sink, The divine way of Misumaru-no-Oshie, Which Takahime has purely realized, How could my heart ever change? This soul, polished and cleared, More remarkably than the Kongaufue treasure orb, More than the Mani jewel, Flashes across the sky of today; The many stars twinkling Are they not my own heart? God is together with us, I am a child of God, a palace of God, With the crossbeams high in Takamanahara, And the palace pillars set stoutly, To the noble Mikoto of Tamateruhiko And Tamateruhime who served, And many other divine officials, Beginning with the Child of the Teachings, This Takahime of impure heart,'

Takahime: 'The sins and faults I have committed until now, Before God and before people, I shall lay them bare and sweep away The many, many sins and impurities. Ah, Kannagara Kannagara, Upon the ship Takashimamaru, Bowing to the distant sky, Deeply bowing to the seabed, The sincerity within my heart I here manifest. Ah, Kannagara Kannagara, May the divine spirit grant grace and happiness.'

Thus she sings a song of repentance. Matsuhiko, having heard this song, began to sing again from within the darkness.

Matsuhiko: 'I, Matsuhiko, who serve As a house-servant to Matsuwakahiko, Hearing the song of Takahime-sama, Out of gratitude, I shall now here Confess the details. In the words spoken By the deity Kotoyoriwake, He said that Takahime-sama Has a strong attachment to the jewels, And it would be difficult for her to repent. Kotoyoriwake also, for that reason alone, Is worrying day and night. If Takahime-sama at this time Should return to Onogorojima, He wishes to let her have the merit Of revealing the Arika of the Mani treasure orb, Though his heart is drawn in a thousand directions, As there was no way to clear the eightfold clouds That enshrouded the heart of the unreliable Takahime,'

Following behind Kamususanōnōokami, proceeding to the mansion of Iso in the land of Fusa, you, Matsuhiko, cautiously entrusting your body to the Takashimamaru aboard which Takahime-sama is riding, monitoring her actions, and having seen that she has at last returned to a pure spirit, heading toward the port of Yura, recounting fully and in detail the divine instructions of the official of the deity Kotoyoriwake to Akiyamahiko, accepting the words proclaimed by the deity, and coming here now to board the Takashimamaru, when I see how Takahime has changed from the bottom of her heart and bravely lamented her sins, ah, Kamunagara kamunagara!

When Takahime-sama returns to her true sincerity, the clouds and mist will blow away in all directions, the Miroku world will brightly and firmly stand forever; however, the divine teachings of the official of the deity Kotoyoriwake must be strictly guarded everywhere until reaching Onogorojima. This secret of mine, which has been difficult to make clear, no matter how much the divine official of Tēristan encourages me, this single percent of the secret cannot yet be revealed. Ah, Takahime, oh Takahime, preserve your present state of mind forever unchanged and unmoved! As the great pillar of the pine-tree age of the gods, bear pure glory upon your body, and for the sake of the gods and the world,

polish your soul and spirit! Matsuhiko and Tsuruhiko, both of you, attending behind your bodies, it is my resolve to make Takahime-sama fulfill her mission; do not let her think it ill. Ah, Kamunagara kamunagara! Upon the deck of the Takashimamaru, pouring out the fullness of my heart, swearing to the gods and proclaiming aloft, swearing to the gods and proclaiming down, ah, Kamunagara kamunagara, may your divine spirits be blessed!

Once again a song was heard coming from the darkness.

The sacred ground of Onogorojima, together with the propaganda envoy of Ryūkokubake, Takayorihime, having been suspected by Takahime-sama, parent and child crossed the great ocean to clear away the cloud of doubt, and after hardships and penances finally drifted ashore at Takasagojima, worrying about various miscellaneous matters while seeking the whereabouts of the jewel, heading toward the Amazon River, pouring divine grace thoroughly upon birds, beasts, insects, and fish, leaving this place once again to head toward the capital of Uzu, having an audience with Kamususanōnōokami and the youngest princess, ranged in the auspicious seat, and together with Takahime-sama crossing mountains and fields and rivers,

stopping by at the waterfall of Inui, washing each other's souls, at last arriving step by step with a party of six at the port of Hara, put aboard the Takashimamaru and returning to Onogorojima, on this ship unexpectedly Matsuhiko-sama happened to be riding, and in the strangeness of their talking about Takayorihime and Takahime, when I listened with my ears strained, hearing that Matsuhiko was sent by the official of the deity Kotoyoriwake, who deliberated deeply, my heart and spirit grew bold, and at the time when I was about to announce my name, the curtain of night was lowered, a true darkness where black and white could not be told,

suddenly I heard Takahime's blameless tale proclaimed, adorned in song, ah, Kamunagara kamunagara! Blessed by the divine spirits, feeling as if I heard Takahime-sama's true sincerity for the very first time, that joy knows no bounds; gazing up at the august sky and gazing down at the earth, tears of gratitude could not be held back, and here I proclaim the Kototama aloft, ah, Takahime, oh Takahime, obeying the true path of thirty-five without ever shifting or altering your present heart, stand broadly and thickly as the divine pillar of the Miroku world! Ryūkokubake prays with a heart filled with sincerity, ah, Kamunagara kamunagara, may your divine spirits be blessed!

So singing, the boat slides on through the dark sea surface, swelling its sails, carrying the snores of many passengers, sliding west and ever west.

Today, the president of the Taisho Nichinichi Shimbun, Tokorokaze Masahiro, visited Yugashima and returned to Osaka on the same day.

Oamoto's second leader, accompanied by Watanabe Junichi, returned to Ayabe.
