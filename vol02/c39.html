The Jewel of Venus

第三九章　太白星の玉〔八九〕
§ 1
Tsuruwaka, a faithful retainer of Ryūgū Castle, had fled to the Altai Mountains desperately protecting one red jewel from among the twelve jewels produced from the Golden Water at the peril of his life; yet falling prey to the insidious stratagems of the Takekuma faction, he had that treasure ruthlessly wrested from him. Inconsolable with grief and bitter mortification over his failure, his spirit condensed until he was transformed into a red-crowned crane, as recounted previously.

§ 10

Hastening to the next beacon of glowing white vapor, he found that it had turned into a white jewel; this too he swallowed into his abdomen just as before. In this manner, he systematically swallowed all twelve multicolored jewels—red, blue, black, purple, yellow, and other hues—into his belly. Laden with this immense weight, his body grew heavy; unable to soar high, he skimmed close to the ground and with arduous effort finally managed to reach the slopes of Mount Fuyō.
