'I comprehend the sovereign's concern, yet evil spirits run rampant across the world, and their malice is not to be underestimated. Endowed though Waka-sakurahime-no-Mikoto is with royal wisdom, as a goddess she is tragically prone to shallow foresight and excessive suspicion, continually placing trust in such treacherous fiends as Tokoyohime until the divine realm is threatened with ruin! § 5
Flaring up, he pushed back his seat to withdraw into an inner chamber. In that instant, Kamiyamahiko drew a gleaming dagger from his bosom, bared his torso, and prepared to commit seppuku. Makurahiko and the other two envoys, determined not to be outdone, bared their chests simultaneously, raising their blades to plunge them into their own bellies.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

Yet how can I bear to watch these faithful, upright officers slaughter themselves before my very eyes? Alas, what path should I take?' The anguish tearing at his heart was beyond all power of words to describe.
