§ 1
Thereupon, the false suspicions against Kototamawake-no-Mikoto having been thoroughly dispelled, he emerged unscathed from the chamber of bees and resumed his holy service in the divine administration. Nevertheless, Waka-sakurahime-no-Mikoto, possessed of an intensely suspicious nature, remained distrustful day and night, imagining that he harbored treacherous political ambitions. To make matters worse, the faction led by Kojimawake and Tayorihiko was seething with spiteful jealousy; they took an antagonistic stance against every divine duty undertaken by Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, staging multiple interferences and ceaseless vitriolic slander. Kamikuniwake-no-Mikoto and other shrine dignitaries concurred with Kojimawake's malicious assertions, until a concerted conspiracy was formed to purge and ostracize Kototamawake-no-Mikoto entirely.

ここに至って稚桜姫命の憤怒は頂点に達し、小島別、田依彦、安川彦らに大軍を率いさせ、言霊別命を徹底的に討伐して滅ぼすよう命じられました。言霊別命はこれに対してやむを得ず、花園彦、元照彦、正照彦、八島彦らに防衛の軍を指揮させ、厳重なる迎撃の防備に当たらせることとなったのであります。
原文（近代文語）
ここに稚桜姫命は大いに憤りたまひ、小島別、田依彦、安川彦をして数多の神軍を引率せしめ、言霊別命を討ち悩ましたまふことになるのである。言霊別命は已むをえず、花園彦、元照彦、正照彦、八島彦をして、これが防備に当らしめた。
