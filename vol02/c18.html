Stratagems of Sowing Discord and Bodily Sacrifice

§ 1

Seeing the formidable strength of the divine forces, Kojimawake and Tayorihiko secretly communicated with Magahiko and Magahime, conspiring to make Tokoyohime their supreme commander and shatter Kototamawake's army in a single decisive strike.

§ 3

In cowardly treachery, Kojimawake and Tayorihiko colluded with the demonic hordes to destroy their rival. As Kototamawake prepared to march, the defeated fiends from Mount Ibuki—Yasokuma, Ashikuma, and Kumaehime—joined Tokoyohime's host to avenge their prior defeat, seeking to annihilate Kototamawake from three flanks. Advancing rashly, divine commanders Masateruhiko and Mizokawahiko, alongside Ōkawahiko, Togawahiko, and Takayahiko, were ambushed and surrounded; Masateruhiko and Mizokawahiko were captured alive, while the other three commanders perished heroically in battle. Hearing this calamity, Kototamawake dispatched Mototeruhiko to assault Mount Ibuki while he rushed against Mount Takao with Takehiko. There Tokoyohime held the fortress, aided by the ferocious demon strategist Iwakurahiko and commanders Sugioka, Ebisuhiko, Yamahiko, and Dankuma. § 14
Deceived by these two counterfeit dispatches, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto assembled Takehiko and his commanders in council and decided upon an immediate retreat. Dividing his divine forces into two columns, he led one toward Rome while ordering Takehiko toward Moscow. Seeing Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's forces pulling back, enemy commanders including Iwakurahiko seized the opportunity, hurling a barrage of incendiary projectiles upon their rear. Flustered and thrown into confusion, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's forces broke into disarray. Takehiko barely escaped with his life and galloped to Moto-teruhiko, who was pressing the siege upon Mount Ibuki, crying that Moscow and Rome were in imminent danger of falling and that Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's fate was critically precarious. Overwhelmed with anxiety, Moto-teruhiko instantly lifted the siege of Mount Ibuki and rushed toward Rome. Exploiting this sudden withdrawal, Yasokuma's fiends launched a devastating counter-barrage of fireballs from behind, severely battering Moto-teruhiko's host. Moto-teruhiko barely escaped with his own life. Yet the dire dispatches claiming Rome and Moscow were in mortal peril were nothing but Kuniteruhime's deceptive stratagems of discord; both Rome and Moscow remained wholly impregnable and perfectly secure.
現代日本語訳
