The Badger's Mud Boat

第一一章 狸の土舟〔六一〕
§ 1
Here, Kuniteruhime (the alias of Takatorahime) plotted to manipulate Tokoyohime—who wielded sovereign power in the Land of Tokoyo—to accomplish her sinister designs. This Tokoyohime was the third daughter of Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto, yet possessed the fiercest and most ruthless ambition among all divine officials. Kuniteruhime aimed to overthrow the beloved retainers Kototamawake-no-Mikoto and Kototamahime, seizing supreme authority in Ry&umacron;g&umacron; Castle for herself. Journeying to Tokoyo alongside the false Miyamahiko, Kuniteruhime flattered Tokoyohime, urging her to return to Ry&umacron;g&umacron; Castle. However, knowing that Tenshi &Omacron;yashimahiko and Kototamawake already recognized them as mortal foes, the villains stayed in the shadows, sending their cunning subordinates Magahiko and Magahime to accompany Tokoyohime to audience with her divine mother, instigating her to carry out their scheme.

§ 2
Accompanied by Magahiko and Magahime and escorted by a great retinue, Tokoyohime set out to return to Ry&umacron;g&umacron; Castle; yet when she reached the foot of the Golden Bridge, struck by the sacred aura guarding the span, she was unable to cross. Obliged to pause, she wrote an epistle, bound it to the leg of a crow, and sent it forward to inform Wakazakurahime of her arrival. Wakazakurahime dispatched attendants aboard a newly gilded vessel to receive her daughter. Arriving without further hindrance, Tokoyohime heaped rare treasures from Tokoyo upon eight-legged tables as offerings to her divine mother. Overjoyed at this long-awaited reunion, mother and daughter conversed through the night, filled with spring-like warmth. On the following day, a grand welcome banquet was convened, with divine ministers flocking to celebrate the joyful reunion.

§ 9
Thus did Wakazakurahime voice her reprimand. At that very moment, Magahiko arrived as Tokoyohime's envoy, bowing politely to escort Kototamawake. Having no choice, Kototamawake joined the boating procession accompanied by Iwashirohiko and Iwashirohime. Seeing him arrive, the crowd erupted in cheers. Magahiko urged Kototamawake to board a newly gilded barge; intuition warned Kototamawake, and he hesitated to board. But Magahiko pressed insistently, and because every other vessel was overflowing with passengers, Kototamawake relented, boarding the barge and poling into midstream. The moment their craft drew alongside Tokoyohime's flagship, Magahiko abruptly leaped onto her deck! The golden boat bearing Kototamawake was in truth a crumbling mud boat made of clay washed with gold leaf—the classic badger's mud boat! Under water pressure, the vessel dissolved and plummeted into the deep! Plunging into the swirling currents, Kototamawake was dragged into the abyss, drowning in mortal peril, while the watching throng screamed in horror. From that moment, Kototamawake fell under the severe suspicion of Wakazakurahime. Yet his loyal wife, Kototamahime, steadfastly refused to believe the slander. Thenceforth, a high and bitter barrier of estrangement was erected between Wakazakurahime and Tokoyohime on one side, and Kototamawake and Kototamahime on the other.
現代日本語訳
この悪辣な讒言により、言霊別命は至高の母神・稚桜姫命から極めて重大な不信と疑惑の目を向けられることとなってしまいました。しかしながら、貞淑なる妻神・言霊姫だけは夫の高潔さを熟知しており、決してそのような卑劣な讒言を信じることはありませんでした。これ以降、稚桜姫命・常世姫の陣営と、言霊別命・言霊姫夫妻の陣営との間には、心理的な面白からぬ高く険しい不信の垣根（対立の壁）が冷たく築き上げられることとなったのであります。
原文（近代文語）
これより言霊別命は稚桜姫命の大なる疑惑を受けた。されど妻神はこれを信じなかつた。それより稚桜姫命、常世姫と、言霊別命、言霊姫の間に面白からぬたかき垣が築かれた。
