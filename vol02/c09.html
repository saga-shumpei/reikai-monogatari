The Festival on Mount Tacoma, Part 1

第九章 タコマ山の祭典　その一〔五九〕
§ 1
On a certain day, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, obeying the sacred divine decree, ascended Mount Tacoma leading a great divine host including Miyabihiko, Taniyamahiko, and Tanigawahiko, commissioning Miyabihiko to perform the solemn enshrinement rites for the Spirit of the Land (Kunitama-no-Kami). Generals Taniyamahiko and Tanigawahiko ministered the sacred purification of the Great Exorcism (Oharae), reverently offering the Grand Prayer (Futo-norito); myriad deities gathered in celestial convocation, and the magnificent festival was solemnly celebrated. Heaven, earth, and the six cosmic realms shone in immaculate clarity, without a single speck of cloud in the sky—an indescribably serene and radiant spectacle.
現代日本語訳
ある日のこと、言霊別命（真の美山彦命）は厳粛なる神命を拝受し、忠臣たる宮比彦（みやびひこ）、谷山彦（たにやまひこ）、谷川彦（たにがわひこ）をはじめとする数多くの屈強な神軍を率いて霊峰『タコマ山』へと登頂され、宮比彦を守護神祭祀の主祭官に任じて、大地の尊き守護神たる『国魂之神（くにたまのかみ）』の鎮魂鎮祭（鎮祭）を執り行わせられました。谷山彦と谷川彦の二神将は、天地の穢れを祓い清める至高の『大祓（おおはらえ）』の神事を厳かに奉仕し、恭しく清らかな『太祝詞（ふとのりと）』を奏上申し上げ、八百万の神々が神集い（かむつどい）に集い結集して、空前絶後の盛大なる大祭典が執行されたのであります。天も地も、四方上下の六合（りくごう・全宇宙）の隅々に至るまで清らかに澄み渡り、大空には一点の曇りや雲影すら留めぬ、言葉では言い尽くせぬほど麗しく晴れやかな光景が広がったのでありました。
原文（近代文語）
あるとき言霊別命は神命を奉じて、宮比彦、谷山彦、谷川彦以下あまたの神軍を率ゐてタコマ山に登り、宮比彦をして国魂之神の鎮祭を行はしめられた。谷山彦、谷川彦は大祓の神事を奉仕し、恭しく太祝詞を奉上し、八百万の神々は神集ひに集ひて、盛大なる祭事は執行された。天地六合いよいよ澄み渡り、空中一点の雲翳をもとどめざる、えもいはれぬ朗かな光景であつた。
§ 2
However, four attendant deities—Hayatorahiko, Hayatorahime, Karatamahiko, and Shimadahiko—were secretly colluding with the false Kuniteruhime and Tanohime. These four had enjoyed the deepest favors of Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, who had repeatedly rescued them from mortal peril, being their divine benefactor. When the festival concluded and the party descended to the seashore, these four deities and their attendants prepared lavish delicacies from mountain, field, river, and sea, inviting Kototamawake and the divine officers to a grand feast. Their pretext was: “Kototamawake-no-Mikoto has triumphantly fulfilled the enshrinement of the Kunitama spirit, filling us with uncontainable joy; thus we host this feast, firstly to thank the gods, and secondly to reward the arduous labors of all divine ministers.”
現代日本語訳

§ 3
Miyabihiko, rejoicing at what he took to be their heartfelt devotion, conveyed the invitation alongside Taniyamahiko and Tanigawahiko to the divine company. Delighted, the divine officers gathered upon the seaside square to feast; indulging without restraint, they drank heavily and became utterly intoxicated. Losing all sense of decorum, some sang boisterously, others stumbled in merry dances, completely swept up in drunken revelry.

§ 5
At that juncture, Hayatorahiko, Hayatorahime, Karatamahiko, and Shimadahiko straightened their robes and requested a private audience with Kototamawake-no-Mikoto. Through Miyabihiko, they petitioned to escort him into a beautiful inner shrine to banquet upon rare delicacies. Unsuspecting, Kototamawake entered the hall, engaging in pleasant conversation, expressing gratitude for their hospitality, and retiring comfortably into an inner chamber to rest. Suddenly, dense black smoke billowed across the heavens! Accompanied by a colossal, explosive roar, a towering pillar of fire shot straight into the zenith, showering molten boulders in a terrifyingly sublime spectacle. This marked the primordial eruption of Mount Etna.
現代日本語訳

said they, pouring hot water into the poisoned cup and offering it to Kototamawake. Just as Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, without suspicion, brought the cup to his lips to take a sip, Tokinohime rushed in panting for breath, snatched the cup from his hand, and swallowed the broth in a single gulp! Instantly her face turned deathly pale; thrashing in excruciating agony, she vomited black blood and collapsed to the floor. Even though Kototamawake had tasted merely a droplet, the lethal poison robbed him of his speech; unable to utter a word, he rose to flee the chamber immediately. Fearing their treason stood exposed, Hayatorahiko and his accomplices pursued him to seize and conceal his person.

§ 10
Myriad divine officers lay completely incapacitated by alcohol, not a single one able to stand upon his feet. Deprived of his voice by the poison, Kototamawake gestured desperately with his hands and facial expressions to warn the assembly of Tokinohime's poisoning and the mortal peril; yet the drunken gods failed utterly to comprehend, believing that Kototamawake was merely joining the merriment with comical pantomime! Stumbling to their feet, they mimicked his gestures, waving hands and covering their mouths in drunken laughter. Ah, the sheer agony and exasperation of Kototamawake was beyond all description!
現代日本語訳

§ 12
Desperate and voiceless, Kototamawake resolved to flee alone toward Ry&umacron;g&umacron; Island. Though valiant warriors normally, the divine troops were now stripped of wits by wine, none stepping forward to protect their commander. Only Miyabihiko, Taniyamahiko, and Tanigawahiko had refrained from drinking; perceiving Kototamawake's distress, they rose to follow and guard him. Immediately, stumbling, drunken officers grabbed their sleeves and robes, insisting: “To attend such an auspicious feast without drinking is an insult to the gods! Drink deep and linger!” The three commanders burned to reveal the poisoning and sober the throng; yet Shimadahiko and Hayatorahime glared menacingly nearby. Resorting to allegories, satirical verses, and urgent gestures, the three tried to communicate the treason. Incomprehending, the drunken throng assumed the commanders were performing festive dances, dragging them to the center of the hall and tossing them in the air with uproarious cheers—a maddening comedy of errors!

言霊別命はいかに焦慮しても全く声を出すことができなかったため、やむを得ず意を決し、ただ一柱のみで海を渡り本拠たる『竜宮島』を目指して逃げ帰ろうとされました。普段は忠勇無双を誇る神軍の勇士たちも、今は酒の毒気に精神を奪われ、このような未曾有の危急の瞬間に大将を護衛する者は誰一人としておりませんでした。その中で宮比彦、谷山彦、谷川彦の三神将だけは一口も酒を飲んでおらず、言霊別命のただならぬ様子を案じ、後を追って竜宮島へと安全にお送り申し上げようと座を立とうといたしました。すると、足は千鳥足でおぼつかなく腰も抜けた泥酔の神司たちが、三神将の手を取り足にすがりつき、『このような目出度い祝宴に列席しながら、神酒を飲まぬとは神々に対して無礼千万でござる！ さあさあ、もっとゆっくり神酒を召し上がられよ！』と、寄ってたかって三神将を取り囲み、決して放そうとしませんでした。三神将は焦慮に駆られ、言霊別命が毒殺未遂の遭難に遭った実情を叫んで泥酔した者たちの酔いを覚まさせようとしました。しかし傍らには反乱首謀者の島田彦や速虎姫が目を光らせてピタリと張り付いていたため、迂闊に真相を叫ぶこともできません。そこで三神将は、ある時は比喩の謎かけを用い、ある時は風刺の歌を唄い、ある時は手真似の身振りを用いて、速虎彦らの叛逆陰謀と言霊別命の遭難の危機を悟らせようと必死に努めました。ところが泥酔した神司たちの中にそれを察する者は一人もおらず、かえって三神将の仕草を見て『めでたい即興の歌舞音曲を披露してくださった！』と大喜びし、三神将を宴席の中央へと担ぎ出し、胴上げまでしてワッショイワッショイと大騒ぎを始めるという、実に救い難きもどかしさでありました！
原文（近代文語）
言霊別命はいかに焦慮するも言語を発することができないので、已むをえず意を決してただ一柱竜宮島さして逃げ帰らうとせられた。さすがの勇神猛卒も今は酒のためにその精神を奪はれ、かかる危急の場合に一柱としてその大将を護るものはなかつた。宮比彦、谷山彦、谷川彦は少しも酒を飲まず、言霊別命の身辺を気づかひ、後よりしたがひ竜宮島に安全に送り奉るべく、その座を立たむとするや、酒に酔ひつぶれ足は千鳥の覚束なく、腰も碌に立ちえざる衆神司は、三神司の手をとり足をとり、かかる芽出度き酒宴に列して神酒を飲まざるは神々にたいし御無礼なり。ゆるゆる神酒をいただきたまへと、寄つてたかつて三神司を遮り離さなかつた。三神司は心も心ならず、言霊別命遭難の実情を告げ、衆神司の酔をさまさむと心を焦つた。されど島田彦、速虎姫が眼を光らせて側を離れざるに心をひかれ、その真相を述ぶることができない。そこで三神司は或ひは喩言を引き、あるひは諷歌を唄ひ、あるひは手真似を用ゐて、速虎彦以下の陰謀と、言霊別命の御遭難の次第を衆神司に悟らせやうとつとめた。いづれも酔ひつぶれてこれを覚る者は一柱もないばかりか、三神司の動作をながめて、喜んで歌を詠み、戯れ踊りをなすものと思ひ違ひ、手をとり足をとり、三神司を席の中央に誘ひゆきて胴上げまでして立ち騒ぐもどかしさ。

現代日本語訳
言霊別命はあらゆる危難を排し、追手を振り切って辛うじて『竜宮島』へと辿り着き、地球の守護神たる国御柱命（くにのみはしらのみこと）の手厚い保護を受けて、ようやく本拠たる竜宮城へと無事に御帰還を果たされました。この竜宮島の地下深き岩盤は、莫大なる純金（黄金）によって形成されているのであります。これこそが現在の地球地理学上における『豪州大陸（オーストラリア大陸）』に相当する霊的聖地であり、またの名を『冠島（かんむりじま）』とも称するのであります。
