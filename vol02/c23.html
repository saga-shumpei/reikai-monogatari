Chapter 23: Ryūseihime's Wondrous Wit

第二三章 竜世姫の奇智〔七三〕
§ 1
Shouldering the palanquin of Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, Kojimawake and Takeshimahiko grimaced like monkeys biting into sour persimmons or children forced to swallow bitter herbal drafts, trudging ahead with utter reluctance. The bitter indignation raging within their hearts was easy to imagine. At length they reached a steep, punishing incline, ascending with sweat pouring from their brows. Matsudayohime assisted by pushing behind Takeshimahiko. Being a deity endowed with sharp wit and comic mirth, Ryūseihime observed this spectacle from the rear, clapping her hands with glee and dancing with comical gestures:

§ 14
Protected by the miraculous virtue of the sacred scarf bestowed by his mother, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto did not suffer a scratch; calmly scrambling up the gorge, he reappeared before Kojimawake's astonished eyes. Seeing him, Ryūseihime instantly feigned fiery indignation, heaping passionate insults upon him. A furious quarrel erupted between the two, escalating into a wrestling scuffle. In truth, this spectacle was entirely a prearranged farce. Unaware of the plot, Kojimawake and Takeshimahiko, terrified lest Ryūseihime be harmed, intervened and pummeled Kototamawake-no-Mikoto from both sides. Thereafter, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto and Ryūseihime traded blistering insults, acting like bitter mortal foes. Approaching the capital of Tokoyo, Tokoyohime dispatched an escort with two palanquins—one for Kototamawake-no-Mikoto and one for Ryūseihime. Maliciously, Kojimawake and Takeshimahiko instructed the bearers to pitch and heave Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's litter violently, tossing it high overhead and dropping it near the ground until Kototamawake-no-Mikoto was dizzy with nausea. Arriving at Tokoyohime's palace utterly parched and exhausted, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto urgently requested water. An attendant offered a golden goblet of water to quench his thirst. Stepping down from her litter and witnessing this, Ryūseihime sprang forward:
現代日本語訳
しかし言霊別命は、母神より授かった懐中の種々物の領巾の神秘なる神力によって掠り傷一つ負うことなく、悠然として谷底から崖を登り返し、小島別らの眼前へと姿を現しました。これを見るや、竜世姫は口を極めて言霊別命を激しく罵倒し始めました。ここに二柱の神の間で激しい口論が勃発し、ついには取っ組み合いの喧嘩となりました。もちろんこの争いは、敵を欺くための二人の示し合わせた芝居（八百長）でありました。真相を知らない小島別や竹島彦らは、愛娘たる竜世姫に怪我をさせてはならじと仲裁に割って入り、言霊別命を左右から滅多打ちに殴りつけました。それ以降、竜世姫と言霊別命は抜きつ抜かれつ激しい悪口を浴びせ合い、あたかも犬猿の仲であるかのように装い続けました。一行はやがて常世の都へと近づき、常世姫は多数の従臣を差し向けて出迎えさせました。そこへ二台の輿が用意され、一台には言霊別命が、もう一台には竜世姫が乗りました。小島別と竹島彦は出迎えの担ぎ手たちに密かに命じ、言霊別命の輿を前後左右に激しく揺さぶり、頭上高く掲げたり地面に落とすように揺すぶって命を苦しめました。言霊別命は激しい船酔いのような目眩に襲われました。常世姫の宮殿に到着した時、激しい揺れで極度に疲労した言霊別命は喉がカラカラに渇き、急ぎ水を求められました。常世姫の侍者は黄金の器に水を注ぎ、渇きに喘ぐ命へと差し出しました。その時、輿から降りた竜世姫はこの光景を目ざとく見止め、
原文（近代文語）

§ 16
So shouting, she snatched the goblet from the attendant's hands and dashed its contents squarely against Matsudayohime. Instantly, sparks and acrid smoke burst from Matsudayohime's sleeves; shrieking in burning agony, Matsudayohime leaped into the palace moat to extinguish the flames, barely crawling back out soaked and shivering. In truth, the draught had been laced with deadly poison and incendiary magic. The court officials rushed to Matsudayohime in confusion, providing dry garments to solace her. Feigning outrage at her violent impudence, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto berated Ryūseihime, who taunted him repeatedly: 'Coward! Coward!' Flaring with simulated fury, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto laid hand to his sword-hilt as if to cut her down; Kojimawake and Takeshimahiko sprang between them, begging for peace until the quarrel was pacified. This whole theatrical tempest was Ryūseihime's ingenious farce, staged out of profound foresight to rescue Kototamawake-no-Mikoto from being assassinated by poison.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）
