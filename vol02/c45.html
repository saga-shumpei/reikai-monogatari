Volume 2 • Chapter 45 (Cumulative 95)

The Law of Heaven and Earth

第四五章　天地の律法〔九五〕
§ 1
At Earthly Takamanohara, where the grand pillars of the sacred palace stood firm and the rooftop crossbeams (chigi) soared high into the heavens, the two great tutelary divinities Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto and Toyokunihime-no-Mikoto solemnly deliberated upon the state of the cosmos. Seeing that the divine realm had descended into extreme confusion and reached an uncontrollable crisis, they recognized that this catastrophic disorder stemmed entirely from the absence of rigorous divine statutes enacted for the myriad divine beings and heavenly humans. Thereupon, in concert with Amajiwake-no-Mikoto, they established and promulgated the Great Law of Heaven and Earth.
現代日本語訳
地の高天原に太くたくましい宮柱を打ち立て、千木を天空高く聳えさせて鎮座まします国治立命と豊国姫命の二柱の至高神は、神界がこれほどまでに混乱の極みに達し、もはや収拾がつかない深刻な事態に立ち至ったのは、諸国の神人たちを律すべき厳格な神律が未だ制定されていなかったことに根本原因があるとお悟りになられました。そこで、天道別命と心を一つに合わせ、ここに天地の根本を定むる偉大なる律法をご制定遊ばされたのであります。
原文（近代文語）
地の高天原に宮柱太しき立て千木高しりて鎮まりゐます、国治立命、豊国姫命の二神は、神界のかくまで混乱の極に達し、収拾す可からざるにいたりしは、諸神人に対し、厳格なる神律の制定されざるに基づくものなりとし、ここに天道別命とともに律法を制定したまうた。
§ 2
The sacred law comprised an internal spiritual discipline consisting of Five Precepts: 'Reflect deeply upon yourselves. Feel profound shame for your shortcomings. Repent of your transgressions with pure sincerity. Revere Heaven and Earth with awe. Awaken to righteous divine truth.' Externally, it established Three Cardinal Principles: 'First, strictly observe the marital way and steadfastly uphold monogamy. Second, revere the Kami, venerate elders, and broadly extend boundless benevolence unto all creation. Third, strictly prohibit and eradicate evil deeds such as mutual jealousy, slander, deceit, theft, and slaughter.'
現代日本語訳
その聖なる律法は、まず内面的な心の修練として、「自らを深く省みよ。己の至らなさを恥じよ。犯した過ちを心より悔い改めよ。広大なる天地を畏れ敬え。正しき真理に目覚めよ」という五箇条の厳かな戒律から成っておりました。また外面的な行動規範としては、「第一に、夫婦の神聖なる道を厳格に守り、断じて一夫一婦の義を貫くべきこと。第二に、神を敬慕し目上の者を崇敬するとともに、生きとし生ける万物にあまねく慈愛を注ぐこと。第三には、互いに嫉妬し、誹謗中傷し、偽りを語り、物を盗み、人を殺害するなどのあらゆる悪業を厳禁すること」という三大綱領を掲げられたのであります。
原文（近代文語）
その律法は内面的には、「反省よ。恥ぢよ。悔改めよ。天地を畏れよ。正しく覚れよ」の五戒律であつた。また外面的律法としては、「第一に、夫婦の道を厳守し、一夫一婦たるべきこと。第二に、神を敬ひ長上を尊み、博く万物を愛すること。第三には、互ひに嫉妬み、誹り、偽り、盗み、殺しなどの悪行を厳禁すること」等の三大綱領である。
§ 3
Prior to proclaiming this law across all nations under heaven, it was decreed that it should first be scrupulously put into practice within Ryūgū Castle and Earthly Takamanohara, setting a shining exemplar to be transmitted to all divine ministers and mortals throughout the world for firm, unswerving obedience. From this day forward, Takamanohara was governed with flawless discipline, and above all, the sacred covenant of monogamy was upheld with uncompromising rigor.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）
この律法を天下に広むるに先立ち、まづ竜宮城および地の高天原より実行し、これが模範を天下万神人に伝示し堅く遵奉せしむることと定められた。これより高天原は規律正しく、ことに一夫一婦の道は厳格に守られてゐた。
§ 4
Through the brilliant and versatile strategies conceived by the celestial angels Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto and Ōdaruhiko, both Ryūgū Castle and Earthly Takamanohara thoroughly annihilated the demonic hordes of Tokoyohime, securing enduring tranquility and harmonious peace. The divine ministers, captivated by sweet dreams of peaceful prosperity, sported amidst fragrant flowers, marveled at the luminous moon, and performed magnificent sacred kagura dances in grand celebration of the divine festivals, ushering in a joyful golden age of dance and song.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）
竜宮城も地の高天原も、天使大八洲彦命、大足彦らの機略縦横の神策により、常世姫の魔軍を伐ちはらひ、平穏無事に治まり、諸神司は太平の夢に酔ひ、花に戯れ、月を愛で、荘厳なる神楽を奏上して神の御祭を盛大に挙行し、舞ひ遊ぶ黄金時代となつた。
§ 5
Yet far-flung realms across the earth had not yet achieved universal pacification, and consequently the sacred laws of the Almighty Creator had not yet penetrated those distant reaches. In celebration of the restoration of peace in Heaven and on Earth, Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto adorned herself in magnificent raiment and attended the recreation pavilion of the assembled divine ministers, gazing down upon the festivities from her elevated seat with supreme contentment. At that moment, a youthful male divine minister blessed with captivating beauty and refined features, clothed in a splendid brocade robe with long sweeping sleeves, stepped into the center of the hall and began to dance with exquisite grace in harmony with the music. Indeed, he appeared as a singular radiant red blossom amidst a vast sea of verdant foliage. Suddenly, Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto turned deathly pale; letting out a faint gasp, she collapsed unconscious upon the dais.
現代日本語訳
しかしながら、遠く離れた辺境の国々は未だ平穏に服しておらず、したがって大神の尊き律法も全世界の津々浦々にまで行き届くには至っておりませんでした。折しも、天上天下の泰平を祝う喜ばしき宴にあたり、若桜姫命は目も綾な盛装を凝らして諸神司の集う遊楽の場にお出ましになり、一段高き御座よりこの上なく楽しげにその様子を眺めておられました。その時、眉目秀麗にして類まれなる美貌をたたえた若き男の神司が、長い袖のきらびやかな錦の衣をまとい、広間の中央に進み出て、音楽の調べに合わせてしなやかに舞い始めたのです。それは実に、万緑の茂みの中に咲き誇る一輪の深紅の花のように鮮やかな光景でありました。するとその刹那、若桜姫命の顔色がにわかに蒼白となり、深いため息を漏らすや、その場にばったりと倒れ伏してしまわれたのであります。
原文（近代文語）

§ 6
Startled and deeply alarmed, the divine commanders headed by Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto rushed forward to tend to her, conveying her gently to the inner bedchamber. Layering soft quilts eightfold, they laid her down with utmost tenderness and devoted themselves entirely to her care. Divine dignitaries visited her sickbed one after another, journeying to near and distant mountains to gather rare medicinal herbs and offering them for her relief. Yet the goddess merely shook her head from side to side in refusal; and as the days passed, her exhaustion and languor only deepened. The divine ministers exhausted every possible skill and devoted their utmost care, yet nothing exerted the slightest curative effect upon her mysterious malady. At this juncture, as though making a desperate resolve, Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto whispered a command: 'Let the sacred kagura dance be performed before me!' Promptly, the divine ministers prepared the rite, staging the kagura directly before her couch. In cadence with the sacred music, numerous maidens fluttered their flowing sleeves and danced with delicate poise; the jubilant cheers and thunderous applause of the gathered deities resounded unto the very vault of heaven.

§ 7
Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto watched the performance with fixed, intense gaze, casting her eyes anxiously in all directions; then, releasing another heavy sigh, 'Ah...!' she sank back down upon her pillows. Fearing that the excitement would worsen her critical condition, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto commanded the dance to cease; and remaining alone at her bedside through the quiet hours, he tended to her with vigilant solicitude. § 12
Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto immediately summoned Tamateruhiko and commanded him to serve as nurse at the goddess's bedside. From that day forth, the illness of Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto recovered by swift degrees, and Tamateruhiko was retained in constant attendance beside her. Whether on rainy nights or days of howling tempests, she would not permit him to leave her presence for even a fleeting moment, cherishing Tamateruhiko dearly like a priceless jewel held in the palm of her hand.
