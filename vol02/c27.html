Escaping Tokoyo Castle stark naked, Moto-teruhiko wove dry grass into a coarse straw rain-cloak and hat, fleeing to the Crimson Manor of Minohiko to knock upon the gates. Standing guard at the portal was Tatekumawake, commanding a small squad of sentries. Moto-teruhiko had smeared his face with the dark juice of mulberries to alter his appearance, presenting a ragged, miserable beggar-like figure. Believing him to be a destitute wretch serving malignant spirits, Tatekumawake rebuked him harshly and barred his entrance.

§ 13
Horrified at the revelation, Minohiko prostrated himself, apologizing profusely for his gatekeeper's rudeness. He conducted Moto-teruhiko into the inner chambers, prepared a lavish meal, and presented him with fresh ceremonial raiment. Seating Moto-teruhiko at the high place of honor while taking the lower seat on the left, Minohiko summoned Tatekumawake. Entering the chamber, the gatekeeper was startled to behold a majestic deity seated at the head of the room. Minohiko pointed toward him, declaring: 'The vagrant spirit thou didst mock at the gate is none other than Moto-teruhiko!' Gazing intently, Tatekumawake recognized the prince; in sheer mortification, he bowed down until his forehead touched the floor, groveling and begging forgiveness.

極寒の冷気が立ち籠めるスペリオル湖上において、骨身を削るような凍てつく烈風が吹き荒れておりました。しかし命には代えられぬと、猿世彦は命じられるがままに身につけていた衣服をことごとく脱ぎ捨てて湖へと投げ込みました。すると忽ち、蒟蒻の幽霊か地震の孫のように全身をガタガタと震わせ始め、やがて手足も凍りつき、呼吸すら絶え果てて、カチコチに凍りついた完全な木乃伊（ミイラ＝凍死体）と化してしまいました。港彦は言霊別命への格好の戦勝土産として、この凍りついた木乃伊を小舟に乗せ、悠然と船着き場へと引き返していったのであります。
原文（近代文語）
寒気の激烈なるこの湖上に、かてて加へて身を切るやうな寒風が吹き荒んでゐる。されど命が大事と猿世彦は、命のまにまに衣を脱ぎ捨てた。たちまち菎蒻の幽霊か地震の孫のやうに、ブルブル慄ひだし、つひには手足も凍り息さへ絶えて、完全なる木乃伊になつてしまつた。港彦は言霊別命の土産として、この木乃伊を乗せて乗場に引きかへしたのである。
