§ 6
At that time there lived a valiant martial deity named Momijiwake, endowed with both wisdom and valor. Leading a formidable divine host, he arrived requesting audience with Kototamawake-no-Mikoto. Kototamawake-no-Mikoto bade Azumawake meet him first to inquire into his intent. Hearing of Tokoyohime's treacherous machinations, Momijiwake had risen in righteous indignation, coming of his own accord to unite with Mount Manju, crush the wicked, establish peace over all the earth, and labor with one mind in the divine work of Miroku's sacred rule; with touching sincerity, he petitioned to enlist under their banner. Azumawake rejoiced and reported this to Kototamawake-no-Mikoto. Because Momijiwake was a consummate master of martial strategy, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto consulted with Waka-sakurahime-no-Mikoto and appointed him chief general of Mount Manju. By this time, Ryūgū Castle had been seized by Tokoyohime, and Earthly Takamanohara as well as the Mount of Olives had fallen into enemy hands. Thereupon Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto, supreme commander upon Mount Zion, took the Mirror of National Purity (Kuni-no-Masumi-no-Kagami) brought by the refugee Ōdaruhiko and directed its divine brilliance upon the enemy forces below. Instantly, hundreds of thousands of colossal boulders welled up from the mountain peaks and flew through midair, raining down like hail upon the demonic legions of Miyamahiko, Kuniteruhime, and Takekumawake; illuminated by the sacred mirror, their disguises were stripped away, revealing them as hideous fiends, giant serpents, and malevolent foxes, which scattered and fled in terror toward Demon Citadel Mountain.

§ 7
Thereupon Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto appointed Miyabihiko head of rites and Yasuyohiko as garrison commander with a detachment of troops to guard Mount Zion, while leading his main force in a counteroffensive to recapture Ryūgū Castle. Maganehiko advanced toward Earthly Takamanohara, Iwakusuhiko toward the Mount of Olives, and Azumawake and Ōdaruhiko toward Ryūgū Castle; bringing forth the Mirror of National Purity to illuminate the enemy and chanting the sacred words of Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto, a furious tempest arose, mountainous waves surged in fury, and the castle was nearly submerged beneath the waters. An eerie glow emanated from Tokoyohime's body, transforming her into her true form as a golden-furred, eight-tailed demonic vixen; whipping up black clouds, she fled headlong toward Tokoyo Castle, while her demonic cohorts scattered in utter rout, restoring both Ryūgū Castle and Earthly Takamanohara once more into the hands of the divine army. Waka-sakurahime-no-Mikoto, leading Kototamawake-no-Mikoto and Azumawake, made her triumphant return to Ryūgū Castle, while Takamatsuwake, Arikuniwake, and other generals were assigned to garrison Mount Manju.

と言い放つとともに、カラカラとあざ笑う高笑いの声が次第に空の彼方へと遠ざかっていきました。天稚彦は大いに激怒し、声のする大空の中空を目指して矢を射放ちました。するとその矢は天から唸りを上げて跳ね返り、危うく自らの肩先をかすめて地面に突き刺さり、まさに危機一髪の命拾いでありました。天稚彦は己の愚かさを骨身に染みて恥じ入り、これより各地を流浪しながら数々の苦難と屈辱を嘗め尽くし、すごすごと竜宮城へと帰参することとなったのであります。
原文（近代文語）
と言葉途切るとともに、カラカラと嘲笑ひの声次第に遠くなりゆくのであつた。命は大いに怒り、声する方を中空目がけて矢を射つた。矢は危くも命の肩先をすれずれにうなりを立てて落ちきたり、実に危機一髪の間であつた。天稚彦はこれより諸方を流浪し、種々の艱苦を嘗めつつすごすごと竜宮城に帰ることとなつた。
