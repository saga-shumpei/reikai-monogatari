Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's Ingenious Stratagem

第二二章　言霊別命の奇策〔七二〕
§ 1
For reasons of his own, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto left the scene of this disaster behind, speeding down the main road to the left until he reached the Crimson Mansion of Minohiko; uniting with Mototeruhiko, he laid plans for every contingency. Meanwhile, preoccupied with Ryūsehime's sudden illness, Kojimawake and the ministers failed to notice Kototamawake's departure, lavishing desperate care upon her; yet her suffering did not abate, and much time was wasted in vain.
現代日本語訳
言霊別命は何の故かこの遭難の現場を後に見て、一目散に左の大道を進み、美濃彦（みのひこ）の住む紅館（くれないやかた）へと至り、先に待っていた元照彦とともに種々の防衛計画を立てて万一の事態に備えられたのである。一方、小島別以下の神司たちは竜世姫の急病の介抱に心を奪われ、言霊別命が姿を消されたことにも気づかず、種々手を尽くして看護に当たった。されど容易に竜世姫の激しい病苦は癒えず、無駄に多くの時間を費やしてしまったのである。
原文（近代文語）
言霊別命は何ゆゑかこの遭難を後にみて、一目散に左の大道を進み、美濃彦の住める紅館にいたり、元照彦とともに種々の計画をたて、万一に備へたのである。小島別以下の神司は竜世姫の急病に心をとられ、言霊別命の影を失ひしに心付かず、種々手をつくして看護した。されど容易に竜世姫の病は癒えずして、多くの時を費やした。
§ 2
At that moment, in his panicked confusion, Kojimawake slipped and plunged into a deep ravine, striking his hip against the stones and crying out in agonizing pain from the bottom of the gorge. While Matsuyohime attended unto Ryūsehime, Takeshimahiko scrambled down into the valley to aid the injured Kojimawake, while Ryūsehime groaned in ever-deepening distress.

原文（近代文語）
このとき小島別は狼狽のあまり、傍の深き谷間に転落して腰を打ち、谷底にて悲鳴を上げてゐた。一方竜世姫には松代姫看護の任にあたり、竹島彦は谷間に下りて、小島別の看護に尽してゐた。竜世姫はますます苦悶を訴へた。
§ 3
Takeshimahiko finally hoisted the injured Kojimawake upon his back and crawled up the ravine, utterly bewildered and running in circles with two sickly deities in tow. Suddenly, as though flipping her palm, Ryūsehime recovered completely from her affliction and burst into uproarious laughter!
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

§ 4
Grimacing in severe pain, Kojimawake gradually became able to hobble with the aid of a staff after prolonged treatment. Only then did the company realize with astonishment that Kototamawake-no-Mikoto was nowhere to be found; Takeshimahiko shouted at the top of his voice into the mountains: 'Halloo! Halloo!' His voice echoed through the ancient trees, shaking the ridges, yet from Kototamawake came not the faintest response.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

§ 5
Staggering and leaning upon his cane, Kojimawake crawled forward as slowly as a slug journeying to Edo. Finding no progress, he ordered his attendants to carry him in a palanquin, until the road reached a bifurcated fork. There the travelers halted in perplexity, wondering which path Kototamawake-no-Mikoto had taken.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

§ 6
She persistently urged them to take the right-hand road. Yet the party was thoroughly bewildered as to which path to choose.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

現代日本語訳
