Exploits of the Demonic Hags

第一二章　醜女の活躍〔六二〕
§ 1
Winning the profound confidence of Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto, Tokoyohime skillfully won the trust of the divine ministers within the citadel through various stratagems; secretly plotting to make herself the sovereign ruler of Jerusalem, she brought in numerous subordinates from the Land of Tokoyo, steadily preparing for the conquest of the divine realm.
現代日本語訳
常世姫は母神・稚桜姫命（わかざくらひめのみこと）の厚い信任を勝ち得て、城内の多くの神官・神司たちを様々な手段を用いて巧みに懐柔して自らに信頼を寄せさせ、自らエルサレムの城主になろうと密かに企て、常世国の邪悪な部下たちを次々と城内に引き入れ、着々と神界侵略の準備を整えつつあったのである。
原文（近代文語）
常世姫は稚桜姫命の厚き信任を得、城内の諸神司を種々様々の方法をもつて吾に信頼せしめ、声望並ぶものなく、つひに竜宮城内の花と謳はるるにいたつた。ゆゑに常世姫の一言一行は諸神司を支配し、その威望と信徳は四方に喧伝さるることとなつた。
§ 2
In contrast, through the relentless slanders of the wicked deities, the authority of Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, Kototamahime, Iwashirohiko, and Iwashirohime had fallen utterly to the earth; stripped of Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto's trust, they were abandoned in complete isolation within the citadel.
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）
これに反し言霊別命、言霊姫、斎代彦、斎代姫の威信は、邪神の讒言のために今は全く地に墜ちてしまつた。常世姫は魔我彦、魔我姫に陰謀の真意を含め、ひそかに美山彦、国照姫に対して一切の秘密の打ち合せをなし、漸をもつて竜宮城の主たらむとし、画策これ日も足らぬ有様であつた。
§ 3
The greatest obstacle in Tokoyohime's path, a constant thorn in her side, was Kototamawake-no-Mikoto and his righteous circle of divine ministers. Unless these faithful servants were removed, she could never attain her goal. Thereupon Magahiko and Magahime commanded two wicked hags, Fujihime and Yaohime, plotting to ensnare Kototamawake into spiritual ruin through wanton temptation.

原文（近代文語）
常世姫のために最も妨害となるべき目の上の瘤は、言霊別命以下の神司である。ここに魔我彦と魔我姫は藤姫、八百姫の醜女をして、言霊別命を魔道におとしいれむとした。（醜女とは色情をもつて敵を堕落せしめむとする心の醜悪なる女性のことである）

Acting upon Tokoyohime's secret command, the hag Fujihime approached his bedside under the pretext of offering medicine, seeking to seduce his noble heart with flattering and wanton words. Naturally, Kototamawake refused to lend her an ear; on the contrary, he sternly rebuked the wicked depravity of her heart. Overcome with humiliated fury, Fujihime kicked the floor and stormed out of the chamber,

§ 8
So Fujihime wept aloud in feigned despair. Rejoicing in her heart that the trap had succeeded, Tokoyohime put on a countenance of deep gloom and stepped before Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto, magnifying Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's alleged misconduct with outrageous exaggerations. Hearing this, Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto flew into blazing wrath; assembling all the divine ministers, she recounted the affair and resolved to banish Kototamawake from the sacred citadel in divine exile. But weeping, his consort Kototamahime passionately proved his innocence, and Sakurahime likewise hastened to the scene, testifying with tears unto his stainless righteousness. Moved by the earnest testimony of the two goddesses, Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto pardoned Kototamawake from punishment. Yet the dark clouds of suspicion could not easily be dispelled; Tokoyohime's malicious slanders grew ever more virulent, until even the various divine ministers began to doubt Kototamawake's true motives and conduct, whispering rumors into one another's ears, and ill repute concerning him spread like oil seeping through cloth, far beyond the citadel into every realm across the four quarters.

Wondering what calamity had occurred, he ran swiftly toward the cries. There he beheld Yaohime coiled and crushed by a monstrous serpent, gasping for breath in mortal agony. Startled, he seized a bamboo staff lying nearby and struck the serpent's head with mighty force, killing the beast instantly.
