Volume 2 • Chapter 48 (Cumulative 98)

Deliberation on the Law

第四八章　律法の審議〔九八〕
§ 1
Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto, in concert with Amajiwake-no-Mikoto, enacted the Great Law of Heaven and Earth; as the first implementation thereof, Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto became a tragic casualty of the law, descending into the Netherworld (Yūkai). Thenceforth, through the vigilant self-discipline of the divine ministers, the law was scrupulously and solemnly observed throughout Ryūgū Castle and Earthly Takamanohara.
現代日本語訳
国治立命が天道別命と心を一つに合わせ、天地の神聖なる律法をご制定遊ばされた結果、その第一番目の厳格なる適用として、若桜姫命が法に触れてその犠牲となり、幽界へと堕ちていかれました。それより以降、竜宮城においても地の高天原においても、すべての神司たちが自らを厳しく戒め、謹慎を尽くしたため、律法は一点のゆるぎもなく厳粛に守られておりました。
原文（近代文語）
国治立命が、天道別命とともに天地の律法を制定され、その第一着手に、稚桜姫命は律法の犠牲となり、幽界に降りたまうた。それより竜宮城も、地の高天原も、神司の謹慎により、律法は厳粛に守られてゐた。
§ 2
Now, with the enactment of the law of monogamy, a vexing question arose regarding the personal standing of Hanamorihiko. Pitying Hanamorihiko's solitary condition, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto proposed selecting an appropriate spouse for him, so that as a wedded couple they might participate together in the divine mission. However, after holding a secret conclave with heads pressed together, Kamikuniwake-no-Mikoto and the assembled divine commanders resolved to dismiss Kototamawake-no-Mikoto's motion as utterly groundless. Their rationale ran as follows:
現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

"'The fundamental cause that led Ameno-wakahiko and Wakazakurahime-no-Mikoto into moral ruin was Hanamorihiko himself. Put plainly, it is Hanamorihiko's natural duty as a divine minister to share in their guilt and descend to the Netherworld to serve them!'"
