Chapter 10: 震士震商 [1243]

震士震商〔1243〕

§ 1

Harukuniwake and Tatsukō, both of them, were treated with hospitality and entertained with tea and confectionery at the barrier of the Kami Ibukidonushi, and as they were resting for a short while, there was a stoutly corpulent sixty-year-old man who briskly passed right before them.

The red-faced guard hurriedly stopped that man,

Guard: 'Hey, wait!'

He roared with a single shout. The man looked back, making a displeased face,

Yokunofukazau: 'What is this? When one is traveling back and forth along the great highway of Tenka, is there such an unreasonable thing as being told to wait and being hindered? Eh, whom do you take me for? I am a gentleman named Shiyōshii Kunshitō Shishiyaku Kizokuingiin Yokunofukazau. If you cause an obstruction, shall I hand you over to the police box?'

Guard: 'Oi, what do you think this place is?'

Yokunofukazau: 'It is a settled matter without even saying it. Is it not the barbarian and undeveloped Hokkaidō?'

Guard: 'How did you come here?'

Yokunofukazau: 'For the sake of aerial inspection, while I was riding in an aeroplane, the adjustment of the propeller was bad, and I was sent off to a place like this by wind and waves. How is it, will you not guide me to my home country? If you do so, it is not as though I would not let you drink even a cup of spoiled sake, why!'

Guard: 'Koriya, koriya, Yokunofukazau, this place is the Hades, you know. Do you not know the crossways of Heaven?'

Yokunofukazau: 'There has been neither roaring nor exploding! Do not say such frightening things! I indeed am a gentleman whose name is known near and far in the land of Fusa... No, I am a gentleman and a mercantile gentleman. When having sake poured by a male boy, I am His Excellency the Male-Pourer, and when pouring and drinking by my own self, I am His Excellency the Self-Pourer. He-e-en! Without lining up such threatening phrases and speaking of roaring or bursting, how would it be if you were to act as my guide? Even you, standing stiffly here with a face like a two-sen copper coin, are you not lacking in wit? You are hardly any good copper coin of a fellow, but I too will throw wide my great magnanimity and rescue you as a gatekeeper with a bowl's alms.'

Guard: 'Koriya, Fukazau, you are somewhat drunk on sake, are you not? Just now you vomited forth that you are a gentleman and a mercantile gentleman, but if it comes to greed, even between parent and child you initiate lawsuits, or else you blow people's slanders up out of all proportion, calculating your own fame, profit, and success in life, and making your fortune—you are a true wicked scoundrel, are you not? It is correctly recorded right here in this ledger. No matter how much your swagger may be in this temporal world, once you have come to the spirit realm it is already no use. Come, step onto the scales here, and I shall measure your crimes.'

Yokunofukazau: 'When you say that, is this place after all the Hades, I wonder?'

Guard: 'Have you realized it? You are a quake-death article who died from an earthquake as a retribution for accumulated evils, are you not?'

Yokunofukazau: 'Indeed, when I hear it put thus, it floats vaguely into my memory, why! I thought I was riding an aeroplane... When you say that, did my soul fly into the void?'

The red-faced guard, turning the pages of the ledger,

Guard: 'You said your name was Yokunofukazau, did you not? Your childhood name would be Shibugaki Dorouemon, I suppose.'

Yokunofukazau: 'Hai, you know even down to deep places quite well, do you not? There is no mistake in that.'

Guard: 'In the temporal world, you used to serve as the president and concurrently director of the Murder Railway Ranmyaku Company, did you not?'

Yokunofukazau: 'Hai, that is just how it is.'

Guard: 'Calling them preferred shares or ghost shares, you made numerous turnips and radishes into your own property without even putting out money, did you not?'

Yokunofukazau: 'Hai, there may have been such things too. However, if one does not do that, in the manifest world it is impossible to become a noble-tribe pseudo-member, and it is impossible to be called a gentleman or a mercantile gentleman; therefore, in accordance with the rules of the temporal world, I inevitably acted in a manner of survival of the fittest. Koriya, it is definitely not my sin; it is the sin of society. No matter what, the organizational system of society is made in such a way that one must do so, you see.'

Guard: 'Talk nonsense! When was such a law promulgated?'

Yokunofukazau: 'From the surface view, there is no such thing; but if you think of it from its content and spirit, since it is structured so as to slip through the backside of the legal texts, those who skillfully cut their way through this are what are called the influential persons of the temporal world. If you intend to rise to high ranks such as prime minister, or little peer, or ladle, or hysteria, if you act overly earnest and keep legal texts and the like, in the temporal world you will end up having dogs urinate on you and cats trample you flat. Since it is "When in Rome, do as the Romans do," in the temporal world even with this I am an upstanding citizen and a preeminent personage even among gentlemen. If you come here, all destinations will probably be different, but the temporal world has the temporal world's laws, and the spirit realm probably has the spirit realm's laws. Since I have not yet done even any good since coming to the spirit realm, nor do I have the leisure to do evil, to be told garbled things like this in a place like this, counting the years of a dead child, is more than I can bear, you know. Eh, somehow my mind grows impatient; to be wasting time in a place like this is first of all a loss of time. When I was doing money-lending in the temporal world, while the hands of the clock ticked pettily whether I was sleeping or waking, the interest on money was born one after another in ten-yen bills, just like printing newspapers with a rotary press, but now that I have come here I am empty-handed. From here on, I want to pioneer the Hades, become even more studious than when I was in the temporal world, become a great landowner, and spend a lifetime in the Hades. Please do not obstruct me.'

Saying this, he stood firmly with legs apart, attempting to break through the checkpoint.

At this commotion, the Kami Ibukidonushi opened the window of the checkpoint and glanced out for a moment. Yokunofukazau was struck by the divine light of Hanshin, fainted on the spot with a gasp, foaming like a crab, and began to suffer. Suddenly, several guards appeared from one side of the pavilion, placed the body of Yokunofukazau onto a handcart, and carried it away somewhere while making a harsh clattering sound. This was done to throw him into the main gateway of the underworld. Fukazau was thrown into the dark gateway, came to his senses with a start, and, whispering complaints to himself in a low voice, proceeded stumblingly toward the purgatorial hell.

Now, at this checkpoint of Yachimata, there are two officials who examine humans ascending to Tengoku and humans falling into Jigoku. For humans who should go to Tengoku, a fair-skinned, gentle guard examines them, and for humans who should go to Jigoku, a guard with a fierce expression and a red face is assigned to examine them.

Tatsukō, seeing this spectacle, harbored an indescribable fear, grasped the sleeve of Chinaruwake tightly, and trembled slightly with a look of anxiety, holding his breath and watching coldly as numerous spirits were examined. After a while, making his tin staff clatter noisily, a missionary of Brahmanism arrived. As the missionary attempted to pass through this red gate, the two guards, white and red, stood blocking the entrance and called out,

Red: 'Wait a moment, there is something to be investigated.'

The missionary looked back with a puzzled expression and said,

Missionary: 'I am a missionary of Brahmanism who introduces the benevolence and divine virtue of the Great Lord Great Ōkokuhiko no Mikoto to the world. For what purpose have you stopped me?'

Red: 'This is the Yachimata of the spiritual realm. Without examining the good and evil deeds you performed in your lifetime, we cannot let you pass through this gate. Wait here.'

Missionary: 'I cannot understand this at all. We, under the command of Ōkuninushi, are touring the moon world and heading toward the Dekatan Plateau, a person named Harris; I certainly do not remember dying. You had better stop talking nonsense. We cannot afford to delay the command of Ōkuninushi even for a moment.'

As he attempted to go again, the red guard glared with angry eyes, let out a single thunderous roar, and shouted,

Red: 'False missionary, wait a moment!'

Harris came to his senses with a start at this voice, looked around restlessly, and said,

Missionary: 'Hey, it seems this is the spiritual realm after all. I wonder when I came to such a place.'

Red: 'While claiming to preach the gospel of God to the people of the world and guide them to Tengoku, in reality you did not believe in the existence of the spiritual realm, did not acknowledge God, and remained in a state of half-belief—you are a hypocrite who has made countless humans fall into the intermediate realm or Jigoku. Now, we shall examine the sins you committed both in spirit and body by placing you before the Mirror of Chūhari.'

Missionary: 'Alas, I am now truly humbled. As you said, I have preached the path of rewarding good and punishing evil to the people of society, and preached the existence of Tengoku and Jigoku from morning till night. However, I believed that in reality there is no such place, and once a human leaves this physical body, the rest vanishes like smoke; and the state of Tengoku and Jigoku shown in the Quran was, in short, nothing more than a convenient means to regulate the minds of society. Therefore, no matter what, I could not speak definitively, and while harboring half-beliefs myself, I have preached the tidings of Tengoku and Jigoku. Thinking about it now, the fact that the world after death exists so sternly is truly astonishing.'

Red: 'Although your crime of misleading the people by wearing the label of a missionary is great, on the other hand, due to the virtue of vaguely conveying the existence of God to unbelievers, we shall spare you from going straight to Jigoku. You had better discipline yourself for a short while in this intermediate realm until you polish your mind and come to understand what the goodness and truth of God are. Since we allow you to stay in the intermediate realm for thirty days, if you can gain wisdom and realization during that time, and comprehend the goodness of love and the truth of faith, you will be able to ascend to the Tengoku appropriate to your spiritual state. If by any remote chance you fail to achieve repentance and reform within this time limit, I am sorry, but we must make you fall into Jigoku. Come, you had better proceed pointing toward the east.'

'Yes, thank you very much for granting me the grace of a special exception and a postponement of my descent into hell. That being the case, I shall now wander through the intermediate spirit realm (Chiūkai), do good for the sake of goodness with all my might, and with ample diligence impart to the wandering spirits what I have come to realize,'

'Hold on, hold on, Harris. If you think that you have come to realize something, that is a grave mistake. It is through the inner flow of the divine character of the Lord God that one perceives, becomes conscious, and attains spiritual awakening (shiyōkaku). If you ever think it is the product of your own single power, you shall instantly become a thief of heaven and must fall into hell. Do you understand, do you comprehend?'

'Yes, I understand completely. Then from here, facing east, I shall set forth upon my spiritual discipline (shiugefu).'

'You must without fail return here within the time limit. At that time, I shall examine anew the degree of your repentance and amendment, and determine your place of abode (shiyojiyū).'

'Thank you so much for your trouble, and truly I am sorry. If so, please excuse me.'

So saying, where had his initial vigor gone? Crestfallen, step by step his shadow grew faint, and vanished amidst the hazy mist.

Ryūkō pulled at the sleeve of Chikuniwake, lowered his voice, and spoke:

'Say, Sensei, even if a proselytizer comes to the spirit realm, it is utterly no good, is it not? Even if they are spoken of in the mortal world as if they were savior gods, now that they come here, do they not truly lack any presence to behold?'

'Indeed, that is so. We ourselves cannot yet go to heaven and are wandering about in the intermediate spirit realm (Chiūkai), are we not? Therefore, even if we are called crossroads coolies by folks other than the believers, there is no helping it.'

'How might one be able to go to heaven?'

'Indeed, from the very bottom of one's heart, one must become a true human being who comprehends the divine character of the Lord God, grasps the true love of God, performs love for the sake of love, performs goodness for the sake of goodness, and performs truth for the sake of truth—otherwise it will ultimately be no good. We too had become able to wield the Kototama just a little bit, and had thought that as a result of our own spiritual discipline divine power had been endowed within us, but that was a grave mistake. In every instance, it was the divine character of the August Spirit of Mizu-no-Mino-no-Kami, Lord Susanoo, that filled our spirits and used our physical bodies. When one reflects upon this, a human being cannot put forward self even a little bit; to think of anything as one's own wisdom, power, or capacity is to usurp the divine virtue of the Great Deity, making one a thief of heaven. If one were to harbor such thoughts, one would inevitably wander forever in the intermediate spirit realm (Chiūkai), or ultimately fall into the path of hell. Oh, how thankful, how venerable! From here on, we shall completely change our hitherto minds and leave everything to God, shall we not? If you think it is your own power, clouds of conceit well up right there. So saying, he joined his palms and choked with tears of gratitude. Ryūkō also silently pressed his hands together, lost in tears of thankfulness. Deity Ibukido-no-Kami nodded to the two of them, smoothly stood up from his seat, and walked deep into the interior of the mansion. When the two looked behind them, the figure of Deity Ibukido-no-Kami shone brilliantly like a round jewel, his divine form could not be distinctly seen, and a moon-like light—seven, eight, or perhaps nine circular spheres surrounding the periphery—gradually concealed itself into the inner chamber.

Whenever ordinary mortals gaze upon a deity or person of superior wisdom and spiritual awakening (shiyōkaku) from a position of inferiority, they appear like light, and one's eyes are even dazzled. This is because the divine character of God is divine goodness and divine truth, and the wisdom and spiritual awakening (shiyōkaku) emanating therefrom are precisely light. Stunned and speechless, the two cast their eyes once more upon the crossroads checkpoint and watched intently, without even blinking, at the state of the spirits gathering there.

原文（近代文語）

『ハイ、特別の御憐愍を以て地獄落の猶予期間をお与へ下さいまして有難う厶います。左様なればこれから中有界を遍歴し、力一杯善の為に善を行ひ、迷ひ来る精霊に対し、十分の努力を以て、私の悟り得たる所を伝へるで厶いませう』

『コリヤコリヤ、ハリス、其方が覚り得たと思つたら大変な間違であるぞ、皆神さまの御神格の内流に依つて、知覚し、意識し、証覚を得るものだ。決して汝一力のものと思つたら、忽ち天の賊となつて地獄へ落ちねばならないぞ、ええか、分つたか』

『ハイ、分りまして厶います、然らば之より東を指して修業に参ります』

『期限内に必ずここへ帰つて来るのだぞ、其時改めて汝の改過遷善の度合を査べ、汝が所住を決定するであらう』

『どうも御手数をかけまして、真にすみませぬ、左様なれば御免下さいませ』

と云ひながら、始めの勢どこへやら、悄然として次第々々に其影はうすれつつ、靄の中に消えて了つた。

竜公は治国別の袖をひき、小声になつて、

『モシ先生、宣伝使も霊界へ来ては、カラキーシ駄目ですなア、現界では丸で救の神様の様に言はれて居つても、茲へ来ると本当に見る影もないぢやありませぬか』

『ウン、さうぢや、俺達もまだ天国へは行けず、中有界に迷うて居るのだからなア、それだから吾々は八衢人足と、信者以外の連中から云はれても仕方がないのだ』

『何うしたら天国へ行けるでせうかな』

『さうだ、心のドン底より、神さまの神格を理解し、神の真愛を会得し、愛の為に愛を行ひ、善の為に善を行ひ、真の為に真を行ふ真人間とならなくちや到底駄目だ。俺達も少しばかり言霊が利くやうになつて、自分が修行した結果神力が備はつたと思うて居つたが、大変な間違ひだつた、何れも皆瑞の御霊神素盞嗚尊様の御神格が吾精霊を充たし、吾肉体をお使ひになつて居つたのだつた。之を思へば人間はチツとも我を出すことは出来ない、何事も自分の智慧だ力だ器量だと思ふのは、所謂大神の御神徳を横領致す天の賊だ。斯様な考へで居つたならば、到底何時迄も中有界に迷ふか、遂には地獄道へ落ちねばならぬ、有難や尊や、神様の御恵に依つて、ハツキリと霊界の様子を見せて頂き、実に感謝の至りである。之から吾々は、今迄の心を入れ替へて、何事も神様に御任せするのだなア、自分の力だと思へば、そこに慢心の雲が湧いて来る。謹んだ上にも謹むべきは心の持方である。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

と合掌し感涙に咽ぶのであつた。竜公も亦無言のまま手を合せ、感謝の涙にくれてゐる。伊吹戸主神は二人に会釈し、スーツと座を立つて、館の奥深く入らせ給うた。二人は後を眺むれば、伊吹戸主神の姿は丸き玉の如く光り輝き、其神姿は判然と見えず、月の如き光が七つ八つ或は九つ円球の周囲を取巻き、次第々々に奥の間に隠れ給ふのであつた。

凡て智慧と証覚のすぐれたる神人を、それより劣りし証覚者が拝する時は、光の如く見えて、目も眩くなるものである。神の神格は神善と神真であり、それより発する智慧証覚は即ち光なるが故である。二人は愕然としてものをも言はず、再び八衢の関所に目を放ちここに集まり来る精霊の様子を瞬きもせず窺つてゐた。
