逆襲〔1238〕

§ 1

As far as the eye can see, a withered plain stretches out, and the treetop branches of countless trees shed their feathered robes, trembling in the flesh-cuttingly bitter wind. Only the pines and cypresses remain deep green, while bulbuls, shrikes, sparrows, and the like squeeze out sorrowful tones, appealing against the heartlessness of the floating world. What is contained within my eyes is solely a silver-cold world where the faded vitality of fine ice waves is spread out. All things in the universe suspend every activity, being in the midst of the so-called winter confinement.

Gazing upon such a cruel and merciless spectacle, the impoverished weep one and all over the cold and starvation. Conversely, the wealthy throw a banquet for snow-viewing, invite seductive and beautiful princesses, tilt sake cups in green chambers, and indulge in body-master spirit-servant joys. Society is truly a varied thing. Because of winter days and accumulated snowfall, some gain no opportunity for labor and weep seeking the nourishment of life, while others, gazing at the cold snowy scenery, immerse themselves in sake, waste and abuse a thousand pieces of gold in a single night, and yet feel no regret.

Looking back, all the doings of society are impermanent. Darkened against the divine principle of cause and effect, modern people forage only for scientific knowledge and know not the eternal paradise, nor do they understand what the root country is, sending off a drunken-life dream-death meaningless existence. Some lament the heartlessness and cruelty of the world, trembling and shuddering in the freezing sky while passing an unpleasant winter day.

The dark side of human life falls upon the ground plump-plump like camellia flowers leaving the treetop. Tidings of a society where sorrow and joy mingle, the mystery enveloping the four sides of a person's body.

Ah, but yet, yet, we ourselves who have awakened of our own accord to the fire of Aizen and the light of Shinshin—how glorious we are, how fortunate! While causing the jewel-like divine character's inner flow of the God who is the lord of heaven and earth to overflow throughout our whole body, and immersing ourselves in wisdom and realization, even the harsh winter feared by worldly people—piercing like needles—and the cold, dead-like midnight air where all things hide their voices hold for us the feeling of a warm vernal sun.

Ah, Lord God, Lord God, make use of my body and soul as a part of the engine, ever eternally! Thou infinite, absolute, beginningless, and endless divine personage, cause the meat of Aizen and the blood of Shinshin to rain down upon us like the sun and like the moon.

May the divine spirits be peaceful and prosperous!

Āku: 'Wasn't it thanks to my meeting with that emissary that I reached today's position, eh? Think about it well, General Ranch. That I have come to participate in the retinue of General Katahiko and attend important meetings is after all thanks to Lord Chikokuwake, but I wonder what has become of Lord Chikokuwake. Surely that person of character could not possibly fall helplessly into the trap of General Ranch... Somehow I cannot feel at ease. We have the duty to obey General Ranch on the surface no matter what, and be careful about Lord Chikokuwake's personal safety. Speaking of which, isn't that fellow Bill grating on the nerves? Since he thinks he is Ranch's orderly, he swaggers about in a lawless manner, you see.'

『こりやエキス、其方は今何と申した。吾々両人は両将軍の片腕となつて帷幕に参列する重役だ。貴様の不在中に任命式が行はれたのだから、知らぬのも無理はないが、余りの暴言ぢやないか。此方に対し「出迎へ大儀」などと部下扱ひをなすとは以ての外の汝の振舞ひ、吾々は上役の職権をもつて其方を放逐致さうか』

『ソヽそんな事ア俺は知らなかつたのだ。間違つて居れば許して貰はなくては仕方がない。併し最も大切なる客人をお連れ申して来たのだから、さう頭ごなしに呶鳴りつけられちや、このエキスも引合はぬぢやありませぬか』

『知らなければ仕方がない、差許す、併しながら、今約束をして置くが、エキス、其方は、将軍様のお見出しに預かつて、吾々と同役になつても決して威張つてはならないぞ。なあエキス、こりやエキスの野郎、よいかエキス』

タール『やい、エキス、今アーク重役さまの言葉を能く腹に入れたか。やいエキス、エー聞いただらうなあエキス、何うだいエキス、返答は』

『さう沢山さうにエキスエキスと云つて貰つちや、お客さまに対し外聞が悪いぢやありませぬか。一口仰有つたら分つて居るぢやありませぬか』

アーク『今は俺が上役だから、今の中に沢山さうに呼びつけにして置かぬと、重役になつたら、もう呼ぶ事が出来ないからなア。エキス、さうだらうエキス、きつと羽張つてはいけないぞ。こりやエキス』

『アハヽヽヽ、何か旨い液吸うて来たと見えるな。それでエキスエキスとアークさまが云ふのだらう』

『旨い液を吸うて来よつたのだ。盗人の上前をはねて二千両、蠑螈別から五千両、都合七千両のエキス（液吸う）たから、これ位云うてもよいのぢや』

エキスは、

『ウフヽヽヽヽ』

と私かに笑ふ。
