Chapter 21: 跋文 [1254]

跋文〔1254〕

§ 1

1. Modern people do not know that there is a kind of correspondence between all things of the spiritual realm and all things of human beings, nor is there anyone who knows what correspondence is. Although there are various causes for such ignorance, the primary one is that people cling to the 'self' and to worldly society, thereby distancing themselves from the spiritual realm, particularly from the celestial realm. Those who set everything aside and love themselves and worldly society only please their external senses, and pay attention solely to worldly things that allow them to fulfill their own desires, never looking beyond; that is to say, as for spiritual things—which gladden internal senses and rejoice the soul—these are matters of no concern to them. The excuse they give in rejecting this is: 'Spiritual things are far too lofty to become the object of thought,' and so forth. However, the ancient propagandists and believers, conversely, made knowledge concerning correspondence the most important of all knowledge, and thereby obtained wisdom and spiritual enlightenment. Therefore, the believers of Izuwasan-kyō have all opened the path of communication with the celestial realm, acquired knowledge of the principle of correspondence, and obtained the knowledge of celestial beings. That is to say, the people of antiquity, who were celestial human beings, based their thinking upon the principle of correspondence just like celestial beings. For this reason, the people of ancient times were able to converse directly with celestial beings, and many were also able to behold the Lord God face to face, receiving His teachings directly. As for how the propagandists of Izuwasan-kyō and others received the direct teachings of the Lord God, polished their souls, and propagated this to the world, it is evident even upon viewing this Reikai Monogatari. As for modern propagandists, this knowledge is completely extinct, and one might even say that there is not a single person across all religious organizations who knows what the principle of correspondence is. Without knowing what correspondence is, one cannot possess clear knowledge concerning the spiritual realm. Human beings who are thus ignorant of the things of the spiritual realm are likewise unable to know what the influx proceeding from the spiritual realm into the natural world is. Furthermore, they cannot even know the relation of spiritual things to natural things. Moreover, they cannot possess the slightest clear thought concerning the activity which the human soul—called the spirit—exercises upon its body, nor concerning the condition of human beings after death; therefore, I think it is necessary to explain what is now called correspondence and what is regarded as correspondence.

2. Now, the entire natural world, whether viewed from the standpoint of the whole or from that of the parts, has a correspondence with the spiritual realm in every detail. Therefore, whatever exists in the natural world and derives the source of its existence from the spiritual realm is called its correspondent. And it should be known that the reason the natural world exists and endures is due to the spiritual realm, just as an effect subsists by virtue of an efficient cause. Since the natural world is said to be all things that are under the sun and receive heat and light from it, there is not a single thing continuing its existence thereby that does not belong to the natural world. However, the spiritual realm is the celestial realm, and those things which belong to the spiritual realm are all things that are in the celestial realm. Man is a miniature celestial realm and also a miniature world. And since both are formed by imitating the form of that which is greatest, there is both the natural world and the spiritual realm within man. That inner part belonging to his mental nature and relating to the intellect and the will constitutes the spiritual realm, while that outer part belonging to his physical body and relating to sensation and action constitutes the natural world. Therefore, when a thing existing in the natural world—that is, a thing belonging to his physical body and to its sensation and action—has the source of its existence in the spiritual realm, which is to say, when it arises from his mental nature and its intellectual and volitional powers, it is named and called a correspondent.

3. Not a single one of the propagandists of Izuwasan-kyō failed to fully comprehend the truth of correspondence mentioned above, truly because they were able to sufficiently recognize the divine nature of the Lord God. We earnestly hope that you will sink your heart into this tale, grasp where the great august will of God lies, awaken to the truth of correspondence, save all the people in the phenomenal world to the path of goodness, and after death assuredly ascend to the celestial realm, take your place in the ranks of celestial beings, and participate in the divine work.

原文（近代文語）

その一

一、現代人は霊界一切の事物と、人間一切の事物との間に一種の相応あることを知らず、又相応の何たるを知るものがない。かかる無智の原因には種々あれども、其重なるものは『我』と世間とに執着して自ら霊界殊に天界より遠ざかれるに由るものである。何事をも差し置きて吾と世間とを愛するものは只外的感覚を喜ばし、自己の所欲を遂げしむる所の世間的事物にのみ留意して、曽てその外を顧みず、即ち内的感覚を楽まし心霊を喜ばしむる所の霊的事物に至つては彼等の関心せざる所である。彼等が之を斥くる口実に曰く、『霊的事物は余り高きに過ぎて思想の対境となる能はず』云々。されど古の人なる宣伝使や信者たりしものは、之に反して相応に関する知識を以て一切知識中の最も重要なるものとなし、之に由りて智慧と証覚を得たものである。故に三五教の信者は何れも天界との交通の途を開きて相応の理を知得し、天人の知識を得たものである。即ち天的人間であつた太古の人民は相応の理に基いて思索する事尚天人の如くであつた。之故に古の人は天人と相語るを得たり、又屡主神をも相見るを得て、其教を直接に受けたものも沢山にある。三五教の宣伝使なぞは主の神の直接の教を受けてその心魂を研き、之を天下に宣伝したる次第は此霊界物語を見るも明白である。現代の宣伝使に至つては此知識全く絶滅し、相応の理の何たるかを知るものは宗教各団体を通じて一人も無いと謂つても可い位である。相応の何たるかを知らずしては、霊界に就いて明白なる知識を有するを得ない。斯く霊界の事物に無智なる人間は、又霊界より自然界にする内流の何物たるを知る事は出来ない。又霊的事物の自然的事物に対する関係をすら知る事が出来ない。又霊魂と称する人間の心霊が其身体に及ぼす所の活動や、死後に於ける人間の情態に関して毫も明白なる思想を有する事能はず、故に今何をか相応と云ひ、如何なるものを相応と為すかを説く必要があると思ふ。

抑全自然界は之を総体の上から見ても、分体の上から見ても、悉く霊界と相応がある。故に何事たりとも自然界にあつて其存在の源泉を霊界に取るものは之を名づけて、其相応者と云ふのである。そして自然界の存在し永続する所以は霊界によること、猶結果が有力因によりて存するが如きを知るべきである。自然界とは太陽の下にありて之より熱と光とを受くる一切の事物を謂ふものなるが故に、之に由りて存在を継続するものは、一として自然界に属せないものはない。されど霊界とは天界のことであり、霊界に属するものは、皆天界にあるものである。人間は一小天界にして又一小世界である。而して共に其至大なるものの形式を模して成るが故に、人間の中に自然界もあり霊界もあるのである。その心性に属して、智と意とに関する内分は霊界を作り、その肉体に属して感覚と動作とに関する外分は自然界を作すのである。故に自然界に在るもの即ち彼の肉体及びその感覚と動作とに属するものにして、その存在の源泉を彼が霊界に有する時は、即ち彼が心性及び其智力と意力とより起り来る時は、之を名づけて相応者と謂ふのである。三五教の宣伝使にして以上相応の真理を知悉せざりしものは只の一人も無かつたのは、実に主の神の神格を充分に認識し得た為であります。願はくは此物語に心を潜めて神の大御心のある所を会得し且つ相応の真理を覚り、現界に於ては万民を善道に救ひ、死後は必ず天界に上り天人の班に相伍して神業に参加せられむことを希望いたします。

§ 2

Part Two

1. The kokudo of Sushin is the kokudo of mokuteki. Mokuteki is yō itself. Therefore, it is fitting to call the kokudo of Sushin the kokudo of yō. Yō is mokuteki. Thus, when Sushin created and formed the uchiu at the beginning of shinkaku, He first accomplished this in tenkai, and next, in sekai, wherever He reached, He intended to manifest yō upon dōsa, that is, upon ketsukuwa. Passing through shiyujiyu degrees and following shidai, it must not cease even until reaching the ultimate point (shiyukiyokuten) of shizenkai. Therefore, we can understand that the correspondence between shizenkai jibutsu and reikai jibutsu—that is, between seken and tenkai—is accomplished by yō. That which unites these two is yō. And that which encloses this yō within is keitai. This keitai serves as correspondence, that is, the medium of wagafu. However, when its keitai becomes botsukausefu, we must know that there is no such thing as this. All things existing in shizenkai in accordance with jiyunjiyo among sanjiyū kokudochiu are keitai that contain yō. That is, they are ketsukuwa made for yō and by yō. Therefore, all such things in shizenkai are correspondences. However, as for human beings, so long as they live in accordance with the laws of God—so long as there is love toward Sushin and benevolence toward neighbors—their conduct is manifested in the form of yō. This is the correspondence that unites with tenkai. To love Sushin and one's neighbor is, in short, to accomplish yō. Human beings are the expedient, that is, the medium of wagafu, who cause shizenkai to unite with reikai. Now, both things, shizenkai and reikai, are endowed within human beings. Human beings become the medium of wagafu in their spiritual aspect, but if it were not so and they became natural, this state could not exist. Nevertheless, the inner flow of shinkaku continually flows into seken without passing through the medium of humanity, and it also flows into sekai jibutsu within humanity. However, it does not enter rationally.

All things that follow the laws of God correspond entirely to tenkai, whereas those that oppose them all correspond to jigoku. Those that correspond to tenkai are all related to goodness and truth, while there are none corresponding to jigoku that are not involved in falsehood and sin.

Since reikai unites with shizenkai through various correspondences, man is able to communicate with tenkai through various correspondences. The denjin in heaven do not reason by means of natural objects as human beings do. When a human being dwells in the knowledge of various correspondences, viewed from the thought residing in his heart, he is made to be equal to the denjin, and thus he unites with the denjin in his spiritual and inner personality.

The most ancient human beings on earth were tennin; they reasoned through correspondence itself, and the natural objects of the world lying before their eyes were merely the expedients by which those tennin performed their reasoning. The men of remote antiquity interchanged and conversed mutually with tennin, and the union between tenkai and seken was accomplished through them. This era is called the Golden Age. Next, the inhabitants of tenkai dwelt together with earthly men and associated with them as companions. However, the men of this age no longer reasoned from correspondence itself, but by means of the knowledge of correspondence; thus, although there was still union between heaven and man, it was not as intimate as before. This era is called the Silver Age. Furthermore, those who succeeded this Silver Age—though they were not ignorant of correspondence—did not derive their reasoning from the knowledge of correspondence. Therefore, the virtuous deeds in which they dwelt were natural ones, and they could not attain to spirituality like the men of the preceding era. This was called the Bronze Age. After this era, human beings gradually became external, finally ending in being physical; accordingly, the knowledge of correspondence completely fell to the earth, the knowledge of tenkai utterly perished, and numerous matters concerning reikai gradually became difficult to comprehend. Moreover, gold signifies the goodness of the heavenly kingdom by means of correspondence and is the condition in which the men of remote antiquity dwelt. Silver signifies the goodness of the spiritual kingdom and was the condition in which the men of middle antiquity dwelt. Bronze signifies the goodness of shizenkai and is the condition in which the men of old dwelt. Descending further, the Iron Age was manifested. Iron represents cold truth, and it is an age where goodness does not dwell within it. Considering this, the present age has completely passed the Iron Age and fallen into the mud world—a dark and benighted jigoku where both goodness and truth have sunk their shadows. Considering that the God of Kokusuzo is active day and night with unceasing love, goodness, faith, and truth in order to resurrect such a wretched world into the Age of Pine, the Age of Three-Five, and the Age of the Heavenly Kingdom, we cannot apathetically overlook this modern era. We earnestly hope that those who grieve for the nation and state will awaken their eyes to the teachings of God at the earliest possible day, strive for goodness for the sake of goodness, make truth shine for the sake of truth, and participate in the unprecedented and unsurpassed Dai-Kamigara.

Ah, Amenonakahosumi-no-mikoto!

(Incidentally, the mention of Sushin here refers to the Taigenshin)

原文（近代文語）

その二

一、主神の国土は目的の国土である。目的とは用そのものである。故に主神の国土を称して用の国土と云うても可なる訳である。用これ目的である。故に主神は神格の始めに宇宙を創造し、形成し給ふや、初めは天界において為し給ひ、次は世界に於て到る処、動作の上即ち結果の上に用を発揮せむとし給うた。種々の度を経、次第を逐うて自然界の終局点に迄も至らなければ已まない。故に自然界事物と霊界事物即ち世間と天界の相応は用に由つて成就することを知り得るのである。この両者を和合せしむるものは即ち用である。そして此用を中に収むる所のものは形体である。此形体を相応となす即ち和合の媒介である。されど其形態にして没交渉なる時は此の如きことなきを知るべしである。自然界にありてその三重の国土中順序に従つて存在するものは、すべて用を収めたる形態である。即ち用のため用に由つて作られたる結果である。故に斯の如き自然界中の諸物は皆相応者である。されど人間にあつては神の法則に従つて生活する限り、即ち主神に対して愛、隣人に対して仁ある限り、かれの行動は用の形態に現はれたものである。これ天界と和合する所の相応である。主神と隣人を愛するといふのは要するに用を遂ぐることである。人間なるものは自然界をして霊界に和合せしむる方便即ち和合の媒介者なることである。蓋し人間には自然界と霊界と二つのものは具はつて居るものである。人間はその霊的なることに於て和合の媒介者となるけれども、若し然らずして自然的となれば此の事あるを得ないのである。さはいへ神格の内流は人間の媒介を経ずとも、絶えず世間に流れ入り、また人間内の世間的事物にも流れ入るものである。但しその理性的には入らぬものである。

凡て神の法則に従ふものは悉く天界に相応すれども、之と反するものは皆地獄と相応するものである。天界に相応するものは皆善と真とに関係があるが、地獄と相応するものは偽りと罪悪に交渉せないものは無いのである。

霊界は諸々の相応に由つて自然界と和合するが故に、人は諸々の相応によつて天界と交通することを得るものである。在天の天人は人間の如く自然的事物によつて思索せない。人間にして、もし諸相応の知識に住する時は、その心の上にある思想より見て、天人と相伍するものとなすべく、かくして其霊的、内的人格に於て天人と和合せるものである。

地上に於ける最太古の人間は即ち天的人間であつて、相応そのものに由つて思索し彼等の眼前に横たはれる世間の自然的事物は、彼等天的人間が思索をなす所の方便に過ぎなかつたのである。太古の人間は天人と互に相交はり相語り、天界と世間との和合は彼等を通して成就したのである。これの時代を黄金時代と謂ふのである。次に天界の住民は地上の人間と共に居り人間と交はること朋侶の如くであつた。されど最早此時代の人間は相応そのものより思索せずして、相応の知識よりせるに由つて、尚天と人との和合はあつたけれども、以前の様には親密でなかつた。この時代を白銀時代と曰ふ。又この白銀時代を継いだものは相応は知らぬにはあらざれども、其思索は相応の知識に由らなかつた。故に彼等がをる所の善徳なるものは自然的のものであつて、前時代の人の如く霊的たることを得なかつた。これを赤銅時代と曰つたのである。この時代以後は人間は次第々々に外的となり、遂に肉体的となり了へ、従つて相応の知識なるもの全く地に墜ちて天界の知識悉く亡び、霊界に関する数多の事項も追々と会得し難くなつたのである。又黄金は相応に由つて天国の善を表はし、最太古の人の居りし境遇である。又白銀は霊国の善を表はし中古の人の居りし境遇であつた。赤銅は自然界の善を表はし古の人の居りし境遇である。更に下つて、黒鉄時代を現出した。黒鉄なるものは冷酷なる真を表はし、善はこれに居らない時代である。之を思ふに現今の時代は全く黒鉄時代を過ぎて泥土世界と堕落し、善も真も其影を没して了つた暗黒無明の地獄である。国祖の神は斯の如き惨澹たる世界をして松の代、三五の代、天国の代に復活せしめむとして不断的愛善と信真の為に御活動を遊ばし給ひつつあることを思へば、吾々は安閑としてこの現代を看過することは出来ないのである。天下国家を憂ふるの士は、一日も早く神の教に眼を醒まし、善の為に善を励み、真の為に真を光して、空前絶後の大神業に参加されむことを希望する次第であります。

あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世

（因に爰に主神とあるは、太元神を指したのであります）
