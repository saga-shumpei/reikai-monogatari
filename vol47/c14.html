Chapter 14: 天開の Flower [1247]

天開の花〔1247〕

§ 1

Harukuniwake and Tatsukō prayed with utter absorption, repenting their sins of carelessness and arrogance, while praying that their spirits might be filled with the divine character as swiftly as possible.

Then, wearing foul-smelling ragged clothes which had no reason to be in Heaven, lowering an ugly face the likes of which could not be looked at twice, and with a body from which pus dripped thickly, appearing before the two while leaning on a centipede walking stick of about three shaku, it suddenly stumbled upon rocks and began to writhe in agony. Tatsukō, startled, spoke thus:

Tatsukō: 'I say, Sensei, there should by no means be such a filthy creature in Heaven. Might this have somehow become arrogant and reversed into Hell before one knew it? Look how the four quarters are wrapped in dark clouds here, so that one cannot see a cho ahead, and has not an indescribable gloomy energy come attacking?'

Harukuniwake: 'No, no, it is surely not Hell. It is without doubt the lowest stratum of Heaven. However, it seems that there are unfortunate people even in Heaven, and it appears that there are those suffering from such a dreadful disease. We must somehow save them, but for us to say we will save them is also arrogance. I wish somehow to receive the divine character of God and be used for His service.'

And he began to join his hands in prayer to God. Tatsukō pulled at his sleeve and lowered his voice, saying:

Tatsukō: 'I say, Sensei, if you were to touch such a filthy human, your spiritual body would be tainted and you would instantly have to fall down into the group of Hell. By no means make free to attend to it. It is a serious matter.'

Harukuniwake: 'No, it is not so. Heaven is the land of love and goodness. God makes His divine character with love and faith. We too cannot maintain life without receiving the love and faith of God. And we must widely distribute this love and faith received from God to the ground. To say that we will not save these pitiful people who are suffering right before our eyes for fear of falling into Hell is what is called the heart of self-love; the heart of self-love does not exist in Heaven. Even if this place should be the very bottom of Hell, if one can throw away self-love and bathe in the light of goodness and love, Hell will instantly change and become Heaven.'

Tatsukō: 'If you put it that way, perhaps it is so. However, while coming to such a Heaven, if there should be such a thing as touching that kind of filthy human and staining the spirit that we took such pains to polish, we will not be able to return to the mundane world and save a multitude of human beings. Rather than saving just one person and staining the spirit, letting this place pass by and making the light of love and faith shine for the sake of the multitude, I do not know how much it would benefit the divine realm. This place is something to ponder.'

Harukuniwake: 'No, it is by no means so. It is provided right before our eyes, so to speak, as a test for us. If we were to let this pitiful human go by without attending to them, our love would after all not be the true love that comes from the divine character of God. It is self-love just like the natural world, the love of Hell. Such hypocritical love is not the path we should take.'

Just as they were speaking thus, the non-human who had fallen in front looked up at Harukuniwake and said:

Hinin: 'Hey, you propaganda commissioner there, I am now suffering from such a dreadful disease, and what is more, stumbling upon a rock, thus wrenching my leg and writhing in agony. Will you not come quickly and lift me up in your arms?'

Harukuniwake said:

Harukuniwake: 'Yes, I understand.'

And as he drew close in a flash and tried to lift up the body, a foul stench pierced his nose, maggots swarmed all over the body, and disgusting insects of various sorts crawled about the entire body, protruding without number from the rotted parts of the flesh. Upon the hand that Harukuniwake placed, countless maggots numbering in the hundreds crept closely, wrapping around Harukuniwake's entire body in the blink of an eye. Seeing this, Tatsukō said:

Tatsukō: 'I say, Sensei, no matter what, it will not do for you to take such a rotted human as your object. There is utterly no prospect of being saved. Just look at that, are not maggots swarming all over the body?'

治国別、竜公両人は一心不乱に油断と慢心の罪を謝し、一時も早く吾精霊に神格の充たされむ事を祈願しつつあつた。

そこへ天国には居るべき筈もない臭気紛々たる弊衣を着し、二目とは見られぬ様な醜面を下げ、膿汁のボトボトと滴る体をしながら、三尺ばかりの百足虫の杖をつき二人の前に現はれ来り、忽ち岩石に躓き苦悶し初めた。竜公は驚いて、

『もし、先生、天国には決して斯様な穢いものは居らない筈です。こりや何時の間にか慢心して地獄へ逆転したのぢやありますまいか。此通り四方は暗雲に包まれ、一丁先は見えぬ様になり、得も云はれぬ陰鬱の気が襲うて来たぢやありませぬか』

『否々決して地獄ではあるまい。最下層の天国に相違ない。然しながら矢張り天国にも不幸な人があると見え、斯様な業病に罹り苦んでゐる方がゐると見える。何とかして救うてやらねばなるまいが、吾々が救ふと云ふのは之亦慢心だ。何うか神様の御神格を頂いて御用に使つて頂き度いものだ』

と神に合掌し初めた。竜公は袖を引いて小声になり、

『もし、先生、こんな穢い人間に触らうものなら、霊身が穢れて忽ち地獄の団体へ落転せねばなりますまい。決してお構ひ遊ばすな。大変で厶ります』

『いや、さうではない。天国は愛善の国だ。神は愛と信とを以て御神格と遊ばすのだ。吾々も神様の愛と信とを受けなくては生命を保つ事は出来ない。さうして神より頂いた此愛と信を洽く地上に分配せねばなるまい。地獄におつるのを恐れて現在目の前に苦んでゐる此憐れな人々を救はないと云ふのは、所謂自愛の心だ、自愛の心は天国にはない。仮令此場所が地獄のドン底であらうとも、自愛を捨て善と愛との光明にひたる事を得るならば、地獄は忽ち化して天国となるであらう』

『さう承はればさうかも知れませぬな。然し乍ら斯様な天国へ来て居りながら、あの様な穢い人間に触れて、折角磨きかけた精霊を穢す様な事があつては、多勢の人間を娑婆へ帰つて救ふ事が出来ますまい。只の一人を助けて精霊を穢すよりも、此場は見逃して多勢の為めに愛と信との光を輝かす方が、何程神界の為になるか知れませぬぜ。此処は一つ考へ物ですな』

『いや決してさうではない。目の前に提供された、いはば吾々の試験物だ。此憐れな人間を見逃して行過ぐる位ならば、到底吾々の愛は神の神格より来る真の愛ではない。矢張り自然界と同様に自愛だ、地獄の愛だ。斯様な偽善的愛は吾々の採るべき道ではない』

斯く話す時しも、前に倒れた非人は治国別を打眺め、

『おい、そこな宣伝使、俺は今斯様に業病を煩ひ、剰つさへ岩に躓き、此通り足を挫き苦み悶えて居るのだ。早く来て抱き起して呉れないか』

治国別は、

『ハイ、承知致しました』

と、ツと側に寄り体を抱き起さうとすれば、臭気紛々として鼻をつき、身体一面に蛆がわき、いやらしき種々雑多の虫共が体一面にウヨウヨと、肉体の腐つた部分から数限りもなくはみ出してゐる。治国別がかけた手には幾百とも限り知られぬ蛆がゾウゾウと伝うて、治国別の全身を瞬く間に包んで来る。竜公は之を見て、

『もし先生、何ぼ何でも、そんな腐つた人間を相手になさつちや、いけませぬよ。到底助かる見込はありませぬよ。それ御覧なさい、体一面蛆がわいてるぢやありませぬか』

§ 2

Harukuniwake said quietly,

Harukuniwake: 'I do not know who you are, but you must be in great distress. Come, please lean on my shoulder. I shall take you to your home, no matter how far it may be.'

Hinin: 'Hmm, you will listen to everything I say, will you not?'

Harukuniwake: 'Yes, we shall attend to your needs as far as our strength allows, no matter what it may be.'

Ryūkō: 'Sensei, would you not stop this at a reasonable point? Is this not too much of a strange hobby? Even if you say that helping people is your duty, while you are carrying a body that one cannot bear to look at twice, he uses words as if a master were speaking to a servant, and he is making a fool of you... It would be proper for him to at least say a word of thanks... It is because he does not know such gratitude or duty that he is still suffering even after coming to this Tengoku. Since he is suffering the punishment of Kamisama, it should be fine even if you, a Sendenshi, do not bother with him. It stinks, it stinks, a foul odor has begun to rise.'

Harukuniwake: 'Now Ryūkō, do not harbor Manshin. Rub the feet of this person.'

Ryūkō: 'What, eh?'

Harukuniwake: 'Hey Ryūkō, it is my command. As this Hinin-sama says, let him have you rub his feet.'

Ryūkō: 'But saying that, that is...'

Harukuniwake: 'What do you mean by "but saying that"? A person with such Fureuken, I shall sever our Shitei bond from this moment on. I will not go anywhere with you anymore.'

Ryūkō: 'Eh, but saying that, how can that be...'

Harukuniwake: 'Now Ryū, lick my buttocks. Will you not lick them quickly?'

Ryūkō: 'Eh, he is making a fool of me. If I were to lick your filthy buttocks, I would rather be dead. Aaan, aaan, aaan.'

Hinin: 'You hypocrite who flaunts goodness on the surface, you too will soon catch a disease like mine; are you fine with that?'

Ryūkō: 'Th... that I would catch such a loathsome disease... I have never done anything bad. Do not make such a fool of me. To use my precious Sensei as if he were a servant or something, to have him nurse a body that one cannot bear to look at twice, and on top of that to make him take care of you... Eh, Sensei, please stop dealing with such a person.'

Harukuniwake: 'This too is the grace of Kamisama. Even the brushing of sleeves is a bond from a previous life; meeting like this in such a noble Tengoku must be some divine connection. No matter how filthy a human-sama may be, once illuminated by the divine character of Kamisama's love, I feel no filth at all. I am truly grateful. You should also take this meeting as a blessing and let him nurse you to atone for your sins.'

Hinin: 'Hey, Harukuniwake, lick the soles of my feet for a moment. It seems there is quite a lot of pus coming out. Unless you suck this pus out, I cannot walk no matter what.'

Harukuniwake: 'Yes, thank you. I shall attend to your needs.'

Saying this, he began to suck the pus from the soles of the feet with a slurping sound. Ryūkō could not bear it and said,

Ryūkō: 'You insolent fellow!'

Saying this, he clenched his fist and struck the Hinin on the head with a thud. At that moment, the figure of the Hinin, which had been unbearable to look upon due to its ugliness and filth, instantly transformed into a young and beautiful woman of exquisite appearance, and while wearing an indescribable smile, she said,

Konohanahime: 'Harukuniwake-sama, your love of God is truly thorough. Come, let us travel through Tengoku together with me. Let us leave a heartless man like Ryūkō-sama here.'

Harukuniwake: 'I cannot abandon this Ryūkō, who is a cripple in spirit, without healing him. If I cannot tour Tengoku together with Ryūkō, then there is no help for it. As I intend to share both joy and sorrow with him, I pray that you will go alone.'

Konohanahime: 'Indeed, one cannot say that the love of God is thorough unless it is so. Harukuniwake-dono, magnificent, magnificent. I am Konohanahime of Tenkyōzan.'

Harukuniwake stepped back two or three paces, placed his hands on the ground, and unable to utter a single word, was overcome with tears of gratitude. Konohanahime said with gentle words,

Harukuniwake-sama, you have thoroughly devoted yourself to the path of good. The Ōkamisama must surely be well satisfied too. The one who appeared just before as Kotoyoriwakenomikoto was none other than Kuniharutachinomikoto-sama, you see.

Yes, in the beginning, with my wisdom darkened and my spiritual discernment dull, I believed you to be solely Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama; but somehow, as I was dimly led to consider that you were an avatar of the Ōkamisama and was drowning in tears of gratitude, I received your trial, and have been favored with divine virtues beyond my utmost prayers. I humbly beg you, please bestow your benevolent eyes upon this Tatsukō as well, just as you do upon me.

Tatsukō-san, you are also a man of remarkably steadfast righteousness. If your love becomes a little more thorough, you will be able to ascend the Tengoku splendidly, having cast aside the Himenpu. I am truly filled with admiration for your sincere devotion to your master. Because your loyal and good aspiration can make up for the shortcomings of your love, polish your soul ever more and accomplish the tour of the Tengoku.

Tatsukō, choking with grateful tears, spoke:

Thank you so much for your repeated and kind instructions. If that is so, please allow me to accompany you.

This is the lowest stratum of the Tengoku; from here I shall guide you to the intermediate Tengoku community. Compared to the heavenly beings living in the lower Tengoku, the spiritual discernment, wisdom, love, and faith of the heavenly beings in the intermediate Tengoku are endowed with ten thousand times more brilliance. Therefore, unless you are equipped with truth of love and goodness, and of faith, as well as wisdom and spiritual discernment, one million times greater than in this Tengoku, even if you were to force your way up into the Tengoku, your eyes would be dazzled, your headache would be severe, your strength would fail, and you would become almost unable to know even your own life and death. Tatsukō-san has been given the Himenpu and can first explore the second Tengoku with this, but Harukuniwake-sama will by no means be able to go as you are. I shall give you the Himenpu that I possess.

Sooner said than done, she took it out from her bosom and deftly placed it upon Harukuniwake's head. From this point on, Harukuniwake and Tatsukō followed behind Konohanahime, committing their feet to the path, pointing eastward, and proceeding forward with the momentum of a thousand leagues in a single rush.

Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sachiwaimase.
