Chapter 4: Turmoil 痴将軍 [1237]

乱痴将軍〔1237〕

§ 1

Though the horse knows not its master's peril, and the hawk flies above the villain's head, Generals Ranch and Katahiko worried their heads over the formidable difficulty of the attack on the Iso Fortress; yet, despite being plunged in anguish and torment as they resorted to the stratagem of sowing discord and inflicting self-injury to finally welcome Harukuniwake into the camp, striving in every way to destroy this mighty enemy, the petty soldiers of their subordinates yielded to a festival mood and merrily engaged in sumo wrestling, clamoring noisily together.

In the inner chamber of the military headquarters, Generals Ranch and Katahiko, along with Harukuniwake—the three of them—were harmoniously and joyfully exchanging cups of sake, indulging in a banquet of reconciliation. Coming hurriedly thither was Ekisu, a mighty lover of sumo.

Ekisu: 'Ah, I address you, General. Just now, Tatsukō has defected to the Ananai-kyō and, becoming their spy, has fluttered into our camp. Since he is, as you know, exceedingly mighty, we can by no means capture him by ordinary methods, so would you be so good as to lend me your treasured enclosure for a while? If we do not bind him fast within that enclosure and make him confess every single detail, it will never do, you see.'

Ranch: 'What, Tatsukō has become a spy of the Ananai-kyō... Hahahaha, there is no need to be alarmed at all. Indeed, the supreme commander of the Ananai-kyō, Harukuniwake-sama, is thus making reconciliation with us, and we are in the midst of a banquet with host and guest melting into one another. Rather than that, it would be best to treat Tatsukō with care and pour him some sake, or the like.'

Ekisu: 'Ah, this is quite rude of me. Was it Harukuniwake-sama of towering renown who is seated yonder? This is indeed a rudeness I committed, not knowing it.'

Ranch: 'Ah, how is Tatsukō-sama getting along, I wonder?'

Ekisu: 'High, he is in tremendous spirits and is wrestling. To tell the truth, I am also one who has just come from fighting a match with him.'

Ranch: 'You too have a remarkably fine physique, so you must be strong. How did the match with Tatsukō-sama turn out?'

Ekisu: 'High, w... no... already, simply... whatever could it be.'

Ranch: 'Hahah, a struggle of dragons and tigers, neither inferior to the other; in short, it is a case of the referee holding the decision, I suppose. Well, well, if you take a draw, it will be splendid without leaving any grudges between each other. Hahahahaha.'

Ekisu: 'Heh, that... er... whatever could it be. Suddenly kicked by a horse, it is called losing, you see.'

Ranch: 'You put it well. Was it a two-bout match or a single-bout match?'

Ekisu: 'High, if you say one bout, it is one bout; if you say two bouts, it is two bouts, you know. Because water was given in the middle, I lost the first bout. In the subsequent bout, the other party won, you see.'

Ranch and the other two,

Ranch and the others: 'Hahahahaha'

laughed with their mouths wide open.

Ranch: 'Hey, Ekisu, how about having a cup of sake?'

Ekisu: '(Dodoitsu) Even if I lose at sumo, as long as there is no injury, General Ranch pours the sake.'

Ekisu: 'Hahahahaha, oh, thank you very much. Nowadays, no matter what is said, the government is adopting a policy of price adjustment and decline, so it is fine just to lower the price. There is a saying that one wins by losing, after all. If we ourselves do not set an example, there is no helping it, as bad merchants will greedily devour excessive profits.'

Katahiko: 'Hahahahaha, a sore loser... what a stubborn man to the end.'

Ekisu: 'General-sama, you too must be good at losing. How was it when you faced Harukuniwake-sama's Kototama warfare? Ehehehe.'

Katahiko: 'Now that reconciliation is concluded, I would like you to refrain from speaking of the Kajika Pass affair. Victory and defeat are matters of fortune in battle, after all.'

At such a place, Tatsukō and Tāru advanced cautiously, holding their military fans,

Tatsukō: 'I would like to ask the General for a moment: has Harukuniwake's propaganda envoy arrived yet?'

inquired from outside the door.

'Yes, if you please.'

Saying this, he pushed the door open with a rattle, and stepping briskly forward together with Tāru,

'There is nothing to be done about it. Ordinarily it is an unforgivable article, but today, in celebration of our reconciliation, I shall let it pass. Hey Tatsukō, you are quite a fortunate fellow.'

'Indeed, as you surmise, I am a fortunate fellow. Since I have finally learned the Kototama of Ananai-kyō, now that it is today, as for taking and throwing away a hundred or a thousand men of Brahmanism, it is nothing at all to me. Etsu-he-he-he.'

Ranchi: 'Tatsukō, it has been a long time. Shall we have a cup?'

'Yes, thank you. This is not the customary poisoned sake, is it?'

'Not at all, there is no zhin poison in it. Because I am drinking it right here like this.'

'Indeed, in that case, if I may presume, I will gladly accept what the Shogun-sama has partaken of. For it is quite perilous, you see.'

'The demon in your heart is tormenting your body. It appears that you are plotting something quite wicked. Is it not said that if there is evil in the heart one fears people, and also that if there is a scheme in the heart one suspects people? No doubt you harbor some ambitious design.'

'The ambition must be on your side. The fact that you brought Harukuniwake here in this manner with the intention of making him nicely drunk, watching for an opening, and doing something to him, has been thoroughly investigated and verified by this heavenly clairvoyance. Even this Tatsukō, now that it is today, is filled with the divine power and wisdom of the gods, and is somewhat different from the Hyottoko fellow I was up until now, which is why I can say such bold things. Shall I launch a Kototama and show you? Ehe-he-he.'

'You have learned such good-for-nothing things.'

Katahiko: 'No matter how much you swagger, it will be no use, I suppose. You are likely speaking of Harukuniwake-sama's affairs. A fox borrowing the tiger's might—that must be you. Oho-ha-ha-ha.'

Thus saying, while feeling somewhat afraid in his heart, he covered it up with a boisterous laugh.

Harukuni: 'Oh, I have partaken of it immensely and am quite intoxicated. Err, I should like to rest somewhere.'

Ranchi: 'Ah, you must be terribly tired. Then please take your rest leisurely in the inner room. Well, Tatsukō, how would it be if you too accompany him and rest in the inner room?'

'Yes, thank you, Harukuniwake-sama shall sleep soundly, and this Tatsukō, in order to guard against any emergency, will keep watch while not sleeping.'

'Hey Tatsukō, are you still suspicious?'

'Since my usual way of doing things is the way it is, no matter how much you speak of reconciliation, how could I possibly lower my guard? Hey, Tāru, you come along with me too.'

Katahiko: 'No, since there is a matter about Tāru that I need attended to urgently, it would be best for Tatsukō alone to keep vigil in the inner room. Now Harukuniwake-sama, I shall guide you.'

Taking the lead, he guided him to a magnificent, slightly raised, hastily built inner drawing room.

This living room was constructed with a revolving swallow-return trapdoor, such that when a single wire was pulled, one would plunge into a pitch-black deep pit. And this place served as a water prison. No sooner had Harukuniwake and Tatsukō both stepped foot into this room than the drawing room suddenly performed the trick of the revolving swallow-return, and they plunged down to the pitch-black valley bottom. Though the water had accumulated only about a foot deep, both men struck their heads somewhat against the rock cave, and pitifully, they fainted away at once. Shogun Ranchi, Katahiko, and the extract hurried to this place, clapped their hands, and laughed heartily in high glee.

アーク、タールの両人は真裸体となつて治国別の遭難を知らず土俵で四股を踏んで居る。そこへビルは鼻高々と勅使気取りになつて現はれ来り、

『アイヤ、そこに居るアーク、タールの両人、申渡す仔細がある。某についてランチ将軍の御前まで罷りつん出よ』

と下知した。アーク、タールの両人はビルの怪しきスタイルに吹き出し、

アーク『アハヽヽヽ将軍の従卒だと思つていやに鯱子張つて居やがるな。何だ、鉛の福助人形見た様なスタイルしやがつて「罷りつん出よ」もあつたものかい。貴様のスタイルこそ古物屋の店前へ日の丸の扇を持つて坐つてゐる大文字屋福助ソツクリだ、アハヽヽヽ笑はしやがるわい』

『こりやこりや両人、ビルの言葉でないぞよ。勿体なくもランチ将軍様の御仰せだ。ビルの言葉は即ちランチ将軍の御言葉だ。さあさあキリキリお立ち召され』

『エー、仕方がないなア。自分より五六段も低い奴に威張り散らされて、堪つたものぢやない。之だから宮仕へは嫌といふのだ。本当に木の角杭の様な代物に威張り散らされて何処に男が立つものか。なあアーク』

『ウン、さうともさうとも、然しながら主命はもだし難しだ。さア行かう』

此処に二人はビルに導かれ、ランチ将軍の居間に進んだ。然しながら此両人は已に已に心機一転して、内心三五教の信者となつて居た。二人の心が斯くも変つて居るとは神ならぬ身の知る由もなく、ランチ、片彦両将軍は二人を此上なきものと愛で慈しみ、一切万事の相談をなすべく幹部に抜擢して了つたのである。
