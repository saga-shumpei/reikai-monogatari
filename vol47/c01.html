§ 1

Manifesting Shiki, Shiraku, and Saugon entirely upon the Chijiyō, the Mizu no Mitama of Susanō, who descended upon the summit of Mount Ubusuna, known as the Takaamahara of the world, opened the Saidaiichi Tengoku and, wishing to save the Momobito of the Yomo Kuniguni beneath the heavens into the Mikuni of Kami, gathered the Sendenshi of pure heart from the Yomo Kuni and opened the noble Mioshihe of Kami; how noble it is.

Accompanied by the Kamutsukasa of Harukuniwake, Mankō, Harukō, and Isokō, he departed from the mansion, crossed over Kajikatauge, and while encountering a hundred troubles, he advanced through the wilderness for the sake of the Way without bending or yielding.

In the forest of Nonaka, leaving behind Mankō, Isokō, and Matsuhiko, and accompanied only by Ryūkō, he proceeded steadily through the dark night.

He passed through the mysterious forest safely and advanced to Ukigikahara, where the many troops of the Brahman were stationed.

Harukuniwake, leaving Matsuhiko, Isokō, and Mankō behind in the forest of Nonaka and accompanied only by Ryūkō, hurried along the road, intending to advance secretly into the camp of the Brahman in the forest of Ukigi, obeying the command of Kami. The guards of the mysterious forest were so intoxicated with sake that they did not notice the two passing by at all. When they advanced to the entrance of the village of Ukigi, which was the second barrier, the two, Tāru and Āku, were standing by with their large eyes wide open, like hornets guarding the outside of a disturbed beehive.

Tāru: 'Hey, Āku, it seems the east is turning white. We are being made to do this night watch, but since it is already near dawn, there should be no need for a night guard; how about ordering the pupils to take a rest?'

Āku: 'The fact that the pupils and everything else have begun to brighten is due to the light of this Āku-tō-sama. You always spit out that I am not an Āku but an evil party, but no matter how dark the night is, it is the Āku-tō that makes it bright. Therefore, one cannot tell whether the good will be in time or the evil party will be in time. Yeah, the reality world is accomplished by the intermingling of good and evil, and the crossing of beauty and ugliness. If evil is so scary to you, it would be smarter not to stick your neck into such a place from the beginning. Speaking of Brahmanism, it is evil by nature. And since it is the Āku-sama for that evil teaching, it is very suitable.'

Tāru: 'Don't say stupid things. How could there be evil in Kami? The Kami of Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko is the creator of the world. How could this world be created perfectly with evil? It is precisely because I think He is the true Kami of supreme benevolence and love that I am doing such a painful service. I was not born a soldier, nor did I volunteer to be one, but I have joined this battle, which is against my heart, dragged by the power of faith for the sake of the Way. If we dawdle, isn't the situation such that the evil Kami Susanō-no-Mikoto of the Sango-kyō might trample upon the sacred ground of Mount Ōkumo, as fearful as it is? As believers, we must protect it with our blood.'

Āku: 'No matter what you say about the world, it cannot stand without evil. I am pleased because I know that Brahmanism is evil. The guys of Sango-kyō spit out that the spirit is the master and the body is the follower, but this reality world is solidified by matter. Even our physical bodies are all matter. Even the sun shining in the heavens is matter. If we are to live in the material world, we must follow the laws of the material world no matter what; all self-love and worldly love originating from the sun of the reality world are, in short, evil. The survival of the fittest and the law of the jungle are the unshakeable truths of the reality world. Think about it carefully. Strong beasts catch and eat weak beasts, large fish swallow small fish, hawks catch shrikes, and shrikes catch and eat sparrows, do they not? Even humans beat and eat four-legged animals, capture fish tribes swimming happily in seas and rivers, peel the skins of fruits of plants enjoying nature, rub them, soak them in water, put weights on them, put them into boiling water, or roast them over fire; they do truly cruel things to fill their mouths and stomachs, and thus continue their lives. In short, humans are the ringleaders of evil. Try not doing such things by calling them evil, and see if you can preserve your life even for a day. Therefore, whether Susanō-no-Mikoto is good or evil, those who become obstacles in our construction of society must be destroyed by giving them the name of evil, even if they are good, or else we ourselves will be destroyed.'

'Well, I do not mean to say I will not obey, but this fellow called Āku is truly a wicked shiromono, and since he is a Shiyugi follower, this koitsu is no good, I suppose.'

'Moshi Sendenshi-sama, whether he looks like a good or looks like a good person, it is a world called Āku, so perhaps even I, seen from the eyes of the true Kami, might be a zennin. I humbly beg for your help.'

'This koitsu is the most notorious kiken jinbutsu even among the buka of Shiyōgun Katahiko, yet since it is said that those strong in evil are also strong in good, Harukuniwake-sama, how would it be to pardon this koitsu and have him act as our guide?'

'That is chiyōdo tsugafu well... Āku and Tāru, both of you, guide us before Generals Ranchi and Katahiko.'

Āku: 'Hai, I understand.'

And the hips that had hitherto been giving out had somehow recovered, and marching ahead briskly, pointing toward the south where the jinmaku was stretched all around, he started to walk.

Harukuniwake and Tatsukō, both of them, followed behind the two, and they had gone only about ichinichiyō. Suddenly, with a crash, their ashimoto gave way, and the two fell ruthlessly into a deep pit measuring four or five ken. At the bottom of the pit, sharp spears like a forest were erected without a single empty space. Yet, perhaps because the divine protection of Kami was thick upon both of them, they fell conveniently between the spears and did not sustain even the slightest wound. Peering down into the bottom from above the pit, Āku stuck out his long tongue with a perotsu and

'Ihihihi, what a pitiful fellow, uha-fu-fu-fu, you foolish koitsu, eh-he-he-he, serves you right, o-tsu-ho-ho-ho, interesting, interesting, atsuha-ha-ha-ha, burnt black of an Abobontan, Sendenshi of Anaari-kyō, having received the defeat of Anaari-kyō, it is best that you croak. To the strategy of Āku-sama, you must have said a-fun, right? Ihihihi, oi, Tāru, how is it, you must be in awe of the skill of Āku-sama.'

'That we fell into the work of Āku-ma is this very thing. Even Āku upholding Āku, the way of Brahmanism, at this even I was struck dumb, wai. Moshimoshi, Sendenshi of Mitsugokyō, it is by no means Tāru who is bad, so even if you change your province, please make it so that you do not haunt me; if you are to pull off this raw head of Tāru, please pull off this head of Āku.'

'Hen, since my head is made of iron, even if hundreds of millions of gōsha were to make a simultaneous raid, it is dame. Now that things have come to this, it is our victory. Sa, from now on I will report to General Ranchi and display the very first feat of arms. Even General Katahiko, since Harukuniwake, who fled so fragilely together with hundreds of troops, was neatly disposed of by the strategy of this Āku-sama, it is a great thing. It is a Gāteru Medal.'

'A Gāteru Medal of shivering gata-gata makes me dumbfounded, wai.'

'Eri, do not talk nonsense, before long I will stand shoulder to shoulder with General Katahiko, the right arm of General Ranchi, and come to command the whole army. You, stand guard over the hole here, and I am going from now on to report to General Ranchi. My figure too is something to see for the last time. When you meet me with your eyes next time, from the top of my head to the tip of my feet, it will be full of gold stripes. Look well at my face, so that you donot make a mistake and commit rudeness at that time...'

'Hen, even if you think you will perform a feat all by yourself, that koitsu is dame. If you say such a boastful thing, shall I expose the fact that you were startled and lost the use of your hips to General Ranchi-sama? To perform a feat, it will not do unless you are together with me. Is it not too mushigayosugi to try to make it your own sole feat?'

'It may not be that I cannot recognize your feat either. Before long I will give orders, so wait for a while. Ehen.'

With a cough-clear, feeling already as if he had become a shiyōgun, even his manner of speech solemn, leaning backward, walking with a long stride while gripping his fists in both hands, making the broad avenue look too narrow and swinging his arms, with an indescribably triumphant face, he goes striding along with his long compass in a nosori-nosori manner.
