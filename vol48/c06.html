Chapter 6: 狂転 [1260]

狂転〔1260〕

§ 1

In one room of the fairly spacious Saikaubu of the Yukimiyagura, General Runchi, together with Kiyoteruhime, Hatsuwakahime, and his adjutants Gariya and Kēsu, has begun a wild and extravagant revelry, beating bells, Taiko drums, and Hiyōshigi clappers.

Runchi, having sake poured for him alternately by the two Bijin, becomes Jiyōkigen and begins to blow his own Hora.

Runchi: 'Hey Hime, no, Kiyosama, tell me, between this beautiful color of the snow and the color of Runchi's face, which is more beautiful, eh?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'I shall ask you that instead; between this color of the snow and the color of my face, which do you think is whiter? Do tell me.'

Runchi: 'Hmm, that is right. It is a Sangokuichi nice, where one cannot distinguish which is snow, which is flower, or which is moon. In this vast Gotenjiku, there is likely no other shyan like Kiyosama, and the same goes for Hatsuchan. That is why I, Runchi, have forgotten my Jiyūeu ninmu, disregarded my precious self, and come here to Tsukiahi with you. It is quite a Shinsetsu thing to do, is it not? Even so, do you still like the Shisau of Katahiko?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Yes, Katahiko-sama is a pacifist, so I have come to like that Shisau of his. I was truly happy to hear his golden words, a divine melody, that martial arts training is something for cowards. It is meaningless for men to lead a beggar-like life, walking around doing martial arts training under the guise of Mushashugyō.'

Runchi: 'That is right, I feel the same. As for Mushashugyō, I also despise those martial artists who just swing their bamboo swords around, saying mask, gauntlets, and breastplate. That is not Mushashugyō, but wasted training. The Mushashugyō I speak of is somewhat different in its selection. True martial arts training is not like that. It is to think of attacking an enemy castle, or defending against an enemy that has surged forward, and to travel through various lands, checking the depth of moats, or if it is a mountain castle, from which direction it would fall or not, examining the strategic points of the castle, and if it is a castle on flat land, thinking of blocking valleys for water attacks, or burning the city for fire attacks, or cutting off water sources and supply lines; that is the training one does. One-on-one masks and gauntlets are boring, so the martial arts we speak of are, well, like this, eh? I wonder how much the martial arts Katahiko speaks of differs from the martial arts I speak of; if the vessel is large, what one says is also large. How impudent; even if one speaks of disarmament or the abolition of military forces, the final resolution must still be martial arts.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'If I may, Runchi-sama, please stop such warlike talk; it makes me feel terrified and I cannot bear it.'

Runchi: 'Hahaha, as expected of a woman, it seems you do not care for harsh talk. Then, how about singing some gentle song?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Please sing one for us, Shiyōgun-sama.'

Runchi: 'Very well, then I shall sing one. Beat the Taiko and Hiyōshigi with all your might; it is hard to sing if the accompaniment is poor.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Yes, I understand. Now, Hatsuwakahime-sama, please beat the Taiko, and I will beat the Hiyōshigi.'

Here, as Runchi becomes more intoxicated, he raises his voice like a gong and begins to sing. The two women accompany the song with Hiyōshigi and Taiko. The two adjutants, unable to bear it, stand up, swing their arms back and forth and side to side, and dance wildly with strange hip movements. 'In the capital of Haruna, famous for its name / Ishiunōhime-sama, who is like a celestial being / Becomes the target of everyone's envy / Thinking that one would like to see / Her pure face even for a glimpse / They come to visit / Therefore the capital of Haruna / Is always like a blooming spring / I thought there would not be another nice like this in the world / But what has happened now / To the desolate battlefield / Even the esteemed Ishiunōhime-sama / Tucks up her skirt and stas-tas / This nice who seems to run away / Not just one, but two of them / Have fallen into the hands of General Runchi-sama / Drinking sake together and having a blast / Making such a racket, what kind of gourd is this / Even the esteemed Daikokunushi-sama...'

If you were to see a place like this, you would surely be troubled in your heart. Is there anyone in the Sanzensekai as fortunate as I? Yoitose no, yoitose. Koreyaino, dokkoishiyo. E-e-e. Hare, yare, yoiyasa. Yoiyasa, yoiyasa, dokkoisa.

Surrounded by two beauties, I see the joys of Tengoku and Jiyōdo right before my eyes. What kind of Zense Innen is this? From the long, long ago, from the even older past, from the far-off Kamiyo, I have done good for this world. Perhaps I am meeting this fate as a reward for that. How Arigata. Fortune lies in Ten; wait for luck while you sleep.

I have heard that many times before, but I never knew it was this Ketsuko. In contrast to me, Katahiko must be in his Ima right now, sitting with his hands folded, sighing, and shedding tears drop by drop, feeling dejected. Thinking of this, I feel I must shed a few tears of Dōjiyō myself, but for some reason, I feel quite good. A, dokkoishiyo, dokkoitsuyo. Yoiyoiyo no yoitosa. E-e-e. Hare, yare, korehanosa. Dokkoisee, dokkoise.

Ah, it is painful. Ah, I shall be excused with this for now. Now, it is Kiyosama's turn. Sing, sing.

Ranchi-sama, I am unskilled at singing. Please, Anata, do sing.

E, is there such a thing as making someone else sing while you do not sing yourself? In that case, Hatsuwakahime, why don't you sing?

Even so, I am embarrassed. Na, Ane-sama, I do not know any songs.

Ahahaha, today this General Ranchi is like a Geishiya and a Taikomochi combined. Pleasing a Jiyōusama is no ordinary task. Even I, General Ranchi, who commands three armies with a single word, am weak when it comes to Kiyoteruhime-sama.

At that moment, General Katahiko came running, cutting through the air. Without announcement, he climbed up to the watchtower and appeared at the banquet seat.

General Ranchi-dono, to hold the position of commanding three armies and yet lose your head over a delicate woman, forgetting your military duties, is an outrageous act. I shall now order my subordinates to send a messenger and report the General's misconduct to the Lord Ōkuninushi of the capital of Haruna. Be prepared.

He stood there, shouting while standing like a Nio.

General Ranchi, thinking that although General Katahiko is his subordinate, if such a thing were reported to Ōkuninushi, his head would no longer be attached to his torso, suddenly sobered up from the sake. With a pale face and feeling somewhat flustered, he said:

Iya, Katahiko-dono, things have both a front and a back. It is unbearable to be as fastidious as you are. First, have a drink.

He thrust out a sake cup. Katahiko, trembling with anger, said:

I refuse the cup of a General whose very soul has rotted. It is filthy.

He struck down the cup Ranchi had offered, mercilessly. The cup fell onto the gold brazier and shattered into three pieces.

Now, now, do not say such things. First, calm your spirit. If that is what you desire, I shall yield Hatsuwakahime to you.

If there is a heart for fish, there is a heart for water. In that case, will you hand over Hatsuwakahime to me, clean and clear?

A warrior's word has no second meaning.

E, I do not like him! A man like that, who looks like a dried-up persimmon—I would rather die than be with him! E, I feel sick. Please, leave at once. Ane-sama, please scatter some salt and send him away. I love Ranchi-sama. Just hearing the name Katahiko makes me tremble, and he becomes 'Gata-gata-hiko' all over; it is disgusting. My, what a poor-looking face he has. Hohohoho.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Hey, Hatsuwakahime, you should not speak with such kimama. Why do you not kika the Mei of the Shiyōgun?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Ha-ha-ha, shiyō is useless. Those who kika the Mei of the Shiyōgun are mere zafuhiyō, teinauji no better than a satsujinki. I am an independent itsuko woman; I do not place my body in the gunseki. Even Shiyōgun-sama should have no kenri to command me. If I say I dislike it, then I dislike it.'

Katahiko: 'Eh, to be shamed upon shame, how can the face of Shiyōgun Katahiko stand? Eh, there is no shikata now. If it comes to this, I shall make gochiyūshin to Ōkuronushi-sama. Ranchi-dono, will you commit gosetsupuku, or will you take off your helmet and yield your shihaiken to Katahiko? Your hentafu will determine the matter.'

He placed his hand on the hilt of his sword and pressed forward, clicking his tongue. Shiyōgun Ranchi, driven to a corner, had no other choice and commanded his two fukukuwan with his eyes. The two fukukuwan immediately approached Katahiko from the saiu, bound his riyōte with speed, and threw him from the high hall on the third floor, aiming for the blue abyss of the valley river below with a splash.

Ranchi: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha, he fell quite pleasantly. Hey, you two, well done, well done. From now on, you are relieved of your duties as fukukuwan and are appointed as successors to Shiyōgun Katahiko and Shiyōgun Kumehiko.'

Fukukuwan: 'Hai, thank you very much.'

Ranchi: 'I ought to bestow upon you the Garter Medal, but I shall make arrangements so that it may be granted after I have made a report to Ōkuronushi-sama at a later date.'

Fukukuwan: 'Hai, thank you very much. We humbly ask for your favor in all matters.'

Shiyōgun Katahiko, his riyōte bound, was thrown from the high hall into the river, flowing down the rapids while bobbing up and down. Ranchi and the other two watched this scene, clapping their hands and cheering with excitement. Kiyoteruhime and Hatsuwakahime looked as if they were startled.

Kiyoteruhime: 'Oh my, I am frightened. Onee-sama, what shall we do? Shall we flee and return home?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'That is so. With only such frightening people here, I can no longer stay.'

Ranchi: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha, Kiyoteruhime-dono, Hatsuwakahime-dono, there is nothing to fear. I do not intend to cause you any harm at all. It is only because I wished to cherish you that I made Shiyōgun Katahiko disappear. In general, if a soldier loses his spirit just by killing a hundred or two hundred men, he cannot fulfill his military duties. It is a feeling like crushing pests on radish leaves with one's straw sandals. If one does not kill twenty or thirty thousand people in a single day, one cannot protect the nation by any means.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Ranchi-sama, please stop such scary talk. Please hold hands with the three of us by this balustrade and dance in a fun and amusing way. I am so scared that my body has begun to tremble.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Onee-sama, I have become scared too. I want to see Ranchi-sama dance with his elegant body just once.'

Ranchi: 'Very well, I shall dance for you. But why don't you join hands and dance with me as well?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Onee-sama, let us dance together.'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Then let us form a circle of five and dance merrily. Hatsuwakahime-sama, you take the Shiyōgun-sama's right hand, and I shall take his left hand.'

Ranchi: 'Hey, this is interesting.'

He untied the cord of his mask, and began to dance in a circle of five with the fukukuwan. Perhaps because they danced too much, the wigs the two beauties were wearing fell off with a thud, revealing shiny bald heads. The moment Ranchi and the two others were startled, the two beauties transformed into faces that could not be distinguished as terrifying demons, snakes, or specters, and they approached with wide-open mouths. The moment the three were surprised, they staggered and fell backward, landing on their buttocks against the balustrade all at once. The balustrade creaked, and the three fell into the blue abyss where Shiyōgun Katahiko had fallen, sinking into the water with a splash as if stones had been thrown in. O-Tami, who had been waiting below, grew suspicious because Shiyōgun Katahiko had gone up to the third floor and had not come down. When she climbed the ladder to look, two large white foxes were glaring at her with their eyes wide open. O-Tami cried out, fell backward from the ladder with a thud, and after a rattling sound and a groan, she fainted away.

From far away in the distance, a cool-sounding song of propaganda could be heard. Whose song of propaganda could it possibly be?

『ランチ様、もうそんな怖い話はよして、この勾欄の傍で三人様手を繋いで、面白可笑しく踊つて下さいな。私怖くて体が慄へて来ましたよ』

『姉さま、私も怖くなつてよ。一遍ランチさまの品の好い体で踊つて欲しいわ』

『ヨシヨシ踊つてやらう、併しお前も一緒に手を繋いで踊らうぢやないか』

『姉さま、一緒に踊りませうよ』

『それなら五人輪になつて面白う踊つて見ませう、初稚姫さま、貴女、将軍さまの右のお手を取つてお上げ、私左の手を取つて上げます』

『ヤアこれは面白い』

と顔の紐を解き、副官と五人輪になつて踊り初めた。余り踊つたためか、二人の美人が頭に被つて居た鬘はポタリと落ちて、テカテカの青坊主……ランチと二人はアツと驚く途端に、二人の美人はさも恐ろしい鬼とも蛇とも妖怪とも分らぬ顔になり、大口をあけてワツと迫つて来る。三人は驚いた途端にヒヨロ　ヒヨロと蹣き、一度にドツと勾欄に尻餅をついた。勾欄は、メキメキと音をたて、三人は片彦将軍の落ちた青淵にドブンと音を立て、石を投げ込んだやうに水中に沈んで仕舞つた。下に待つて居たお民は、片彦将軍の、三階へ上つた儘下りて来ないのに不審をおこし、段梯子を上つて見れば、大きな白狐が二匹、お民の顔を目を瞋らして白眼みつけて居る。お民はアツと云つて反身になつた儘、段梯子の上からドスン、ガタガタガタ、ウンといつたきり気絶して仕舞つた。

遥か向ふの方より涼しき声の宣伝歌が聞えて来た。果して何人の宣伝歌であらうか。
