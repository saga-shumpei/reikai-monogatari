Chapter 19: 兵舎の囁 [1273]

兵舎の囁〔1273〕

§ 1

Kō, Waku, and Emu, three of the Shiyueiren, are gathered in a room of the Jinei, indulging in Zatsudan while smacking their lips over the Ihahizake for their Sosei, following the Ika of the Ranchi Shiyōgun.

Kō: 'Oi, is this not a bit Ketai? E-n, they are truly making a Baka of us. If I had known it would come to this, I would not have followed them all the way to such a place; it is a terrible Bankuru.'

Waku: 'Oi, Kō, what are you saying? What you are Otsushiya does not Sotsū to my ears at all.'

Kō: 'It is a settled matter. A situation has arisen where the Imi does not Sotsū at all. Think about it carefully. The two Shiyōgun, Ranchi and Katahiko, fought over a woman, and in the end, they even fought over their Inochi. And that woman is a monstrous ghost. It is hopeless; she lives and dies, and a Takusan number of Maujiya are gathering in the Honei, holding a Maujiyakuwai, and saying it is a celebration... it is Kusōmoshiro-mo-nai. They are forcing us to drink this sake, which is not even tasty to Oretachi, in a Metsuta Yatara fashion. I am so nauseated, and wondering what this sake is, thinking it is the sake of Maujiya, I am so agitated I cannot help it. Think well; those who gave us money, such as Samibewake-sama, Otami-san, Chikokubake-sama, Ryūkō-sama, and the other Shiyōgun and their adjutants—eight people in total—went to the Ame-no-Yachimata or the Yūmeikai, and suddenly became faint-hearted, saying they cannot hold swords from tomorrow, or that they will quit the war, or that we should worship the gods of Sangokyō with all our might instead of fighting; are they not spouting things that sound like ghosts? I am already feeling sick of it. I went to the Saion-no-Yakata thinking I would display great merit and become a prime minister of a country, but it is all for nothing. Waku, are you not bothered by this at all, e-n?'

Waku: 'He is the Ranchi Shiyōgun, who possesses both wisdom and courage. Since a hero like Katahiko-sama is with him, we, as mere swallows and sparrows, should not put our beaks into such matters. More than that, since we have received such excellent sake, it would be better to drink it quietly and go to sleep.'

Kō: 'How can I sleep with this? We hear such unpleasant talk as the abolition of armaments or the cessation of military preparations. Can we, even as common soldiers, silently permit this? You are quite spineless, too.'

Waku: 'That is not it; it is like the priestly robes of Jōkai Nyūdō. It is as if one is wearing priestly robes on the surface while wearing armor underneath. He set a trap for Chikokubake-sama of Sangokyō and failed to kill him, and even ended up in a situation where he almost died himself; he issued such orders to the entire army to make Chikokubake-sama and others let their guard down. You are just too simple-minded.'

Kō: 'If that is the case, then he is clever. Somehow, it does not look like that. Terunsu was saying just a while ago that the two Shiyōgun and even their adjutants are chanting the Sangokyō sutras with all their might, and it seems their intention is either to disband the army or to make everyone a believer in Sangokyō.'

Kō: 'If such a thing happens, I will not let it end like this. I will immediately report it to the capital of Haruna, receive praise, and become the successor to the Shiyōgun. At that time, I will make you a secretary or something similar. And I will use you as a Kshatriya in a proper country of the Moon with a population of twenty or thirty thousand, so you had better wait and enjoy yourself.'

Waku: 'Do not talk like a Baka. If Ranchi Shiyōgun truly becomes infatuated with Sangokyō, I will become the commander of the entire army, ravage the Saion-no-Yakata, take at least one-fifth of the seven thousand countries of the Moon, and I might even use you as a Kshatriya in one of the smallest countries among them. That depends entirely on your state of mind. Ah, when I think of this, my anger has disappeared somewhere. If Ranchi Shiyōgun and Katahiko Shiyōgun sink into Sangokyō, it will actually open up a path for our advancement. If I think of it this way, rather than being angry, it is something I should applaud with both hands.'

Emu: 'How is it that your Gokigen has suddenly improved? However, the world does not always go according to your Omowakudoho. Even the Bumei-renowned General Katahiko has been defeated by Harukuniwake just like that. Well, stop such Kūsau, have a little more Miki, and wait patiently for two or three days to see if the Shiyōgunsama will truly become a follower of the Ananai-kyō, or if it is his Gokeiriyaku to conquer the party of Harukuniwake; if you say something careless and it reaches the ears of the Shiyōgun, it will be Taihen.'

The one who came to Mimaha at such a place was Terunsu, who held a fairly Sautau Ichi even within the Jinchiu. Terunsu walked in with a brisk stride,

Terunsu: 'Hey, what were you all saying just now?'

Kō: 'Hai, no, we do not remember saying anything in particular. We were saying that it would be Ketsukō if we could catch Harukuniwake in a Keiriyaku and put an end to him. We said nothing other than that. Isn't that right, Waku, Emu?'

Terunsu: 'Do not talk Baka. Did you not say outrageous things like reporting to the capital of Haruna or becoming the commander of the entire Zengun?'

Kō: 'Un, I did say that. But I was just blowing a Chiyotsuto boast under the influence of alcohol. Please think about it carefully. With a superior officer like you, Terunsu, could I ever do such a thing while bypassing you? Please try to think calmly.'

Terunsu: 'Hey, Waku, Emu, is what Kō is saying now true or a lie? How is it?'

Waku: 'Heh, it sounds like a lie, and it also sounds like the truth. He was quite intoxicated, so I could not hear well what he was saying, and even I, since it was the sake speaking, the person himself knows nothing. Please, I beg you to overlook it.'

Terunsu: 'Hey, you must not tell lies. Will you not tell the truth?'

Waku: 'He was drunk, so he says he has no memory of it because he was drunk. That is the fact.'

Terunsu: 'Yoshi, if you do not know, then not knowing is fine. Tomorrow, I will make you confess even if I have to tie you to a rack. No matter how much you offer excuses, I have firm evidence.'

Saying this, he slammed the door shut and proceeded to the next barracks, muffling his footsteps.

Afterward, the three spoke in low voices,

Waku: 'See there, Kō, you fool, because you blurted out such useless things, even I am being suspected. If Emu and I were to truly prove what you are saying, your life would be over. Look, Emu, even I would hide what I could, but if I am to suffer pain or hardship, I will tell the truth. That is the most clever way to act for my own benefit, or rather, for the preservation of my own self.'

Emu: 'That is right. Even for me, there is no joy in protecting Kō at the cost of suffering pain or hardship, and since he always acts like a leader and keeps us suppressed, the time for a good revenge has arrived. Hey Kō, are you intimidated?'

As they spoke, their tongues becoming slightly thick, they began to chatter without looking ahead. Kō, thinking it would be Taihen if he were exposed by the two, grabbed his sword and slashed at them before they could react. The two nimbly dodged, grabbed both of Kō's legs, and knocked him flat on his back with a thud. Kō was greatly enraged,

Kō: 'You two, there is no more room for leniency. Since it has come to this, it is a matter of life and death, so prepare yourselves.'

He drew his long sword and attacked. Both Waku and Emu ran out into the open, stumbling and falling on the snowy path. Kō chased them in a frenzy. Suddenly, Kō struck his knee against a stone buried in the snow,

Kō: 'Aitatta!'

Having said that, he rolled his eyes and collapsed onto the snow with his sword still drawn. Both Emu and Waku ran in a panic to Terunsu's barracks, gasping for breath,

Waku: 'Te-te-te-Terunsu-sama, Nanitozo ta-ta-ta-tasukete kudasaimase.'

He tumbled in with Emu while saying this. Terunsu felt Fushin at the hurried momentum of the Riyōnin, and said:

Terunsu: 'Are you not the Riyōnin, Waku and Emu? Why do you come here in the middle of the Yonaka in such a Sauzau manner? Has some Henji occurred?'

Waku: 'Ta-ta-ta-ta-Taihen has occurred. When I told that fellow Kō that I would Sutsupa-nuku to you what he had said, he became angry and raised his Daitau, and look... he is Otsuka-ing me here. Usually, I would not fear three or five of such fellows, but since I am Meitei to the point where I cannot even move my legs Jiubun, I cannot do anything. Nanitozo, please catch that fellow.'

Terunsu: 'Ah, is that so? What was Kō saying?'

Emu: 'Hei, he was saying that he would ride a Hayōma to the capital of Haruna to Chiyūshin that the Lunch Shiyōgun and Katahiko Shiyōgun were infatuated with the Ananai-kyō, and that he would become the commander of the entire army and become a Kshatriya of the Moon Country. When Waku and I said that if such a thing were to happen, we would look up to Terunsu-sama as our Shiyōgun, he became very angry, drew his Daitau, and tried to kill me. Such a wicked man is not for your sake. Nanitozo, please send out captors and arrest him.'

He stated this, mixing truth and falsehood.

Terunsu: 'What, did Kō say such things? He is a fellow with quite a lot of backbone. He has great promise. You go from here and call Kō, saying it is my order. There is something I wish to consult with him about.'

Waku: 'O-o-oi, Emu, you go. My legs will not move at all.'

Emu: 'Even I have had my legs taken by sake, so I cannot walk even one step, can I?'

Terunsu: 'You say you cannot walk even one step? How did you come here then?'

Emu: 'Hai, I came rolling through the snow.'

Terunsu: 'Then roll and go call him. That will be fine.'

Emu: 'He-e, please, for that alone, Nanitozo excuse me.'

Terunsu: 'No, that will not do. It is the order of a superior officer.'

Emu: 'Even if you say it is the order of a superior officer, if I go to such a dangerous fellow, my head will be gone.'

Terunsu: 'If your head is gone, it is no loss to me, so does it not matter? What does it matter if one of you small-fry warriors dies? It is an honor for a warrior to expose his corpse on the battlefield.'

Emu: 'Oi, Waku, it is a lot of trouble, but would you not go on this errand?'

Waku: 'Aita-ta, my waist has suddenly started to hurt. Oi, Emu, stroke it for me. I feel like I am going to suffocate. Ah, it hurts, it hurts, it hurts.'

Terunsu: 'Aha-ha-ha, you are nothing but a bunch of dull-bladed fellows, you cowards. For what purpose am I keeping fellows like you? I am feeding you expensive bread so that you will throw away your lives in a moment of crisis, am I not?'

Emu: 'You speak as if you are keeping chickens or pigs. That is too much. Please consider the matter of compassion at least a little.'

Terunsu: 'Do not talk like a fool. If I were to concern myself with mercy and compassion, could I do this business of killing people? It is to demonstrate cruelty upon cruelty that we practice swordsmanship and jujutsu every single day, is it not?'

Emu: 'Is that not practice for the purpose of protecting our own bodies?'

Terunsu: 'Do not talk like a fool. If it were for the purpose of protecting our own bodies, there would be no need for such a large army to gather, would there? The reason the Shiyōgun has come leading such a large unit is to annihilate the enemy. That is why I have had you carry spears and swords. Spears and swords are by no means for cutting wild boars or tanuki. They are called man-slaying knives, and they are for cutting people. If you do not understand such things, how can you fulfill the duty of a warrior?'

Emu: 'Even so, are soldiers not the guardian deities of peace?'

Terunsu: 'At times they become the guardian deities of peace, and at other times they become the disturbers of the world, building mountains of corpses in rivers of blood, thereby occupying enemy countries and storing up many spoils of war; that is the true essence of a warrior.'

Emu: 'That is just like a robber.'

Terunsu: 'You are quite a blockhead. If the army requires it, we must burn houses, we must take human lives, and needless to say, we must seize rice, wheat, money, pigs, chickens, radishes, turnips, and all necessary items without permission. Without that, how could the maintenance of an army be preserved?'

Emu: 'Oi, Waku, what Terunsu-sama is saying does not quite make sense, does it? Secondly, is there such a thing as a warrior who cuts and steals?'

Waku: 'Well, that is true.'

Terunsu, with an ice blade that flashed in the snow before one could even see him draw it, instantly severed Emu's head, and with a returning stroke, mercilessly cut off Waku's head, and the snow-covered garden instantly turned crimson.

『アハヽヽヽ、ナマクラの奴ばつかりだな、卑怯者奴が。何のために貴様の様な奴を飼うてあるのだ。マサカの時に生命を捨てさす為に、高いパンを食はしておいてあるぢやないか』

エム『まるで鶏か豚を飼ふ様に仰有いますな。そりやあまりです。チツとは情と云ふ事を考へて下さいませ』

『馬鹿申せ。慈悲や情に構つて居つて、こんな人殺し商売が出来るか。残忍の上にも残忍性を発揮するために、毎日日日剣術をやつたり柔術を稽古してるぢやないか』

『それは吾身を保護する為の稽古ぢやありませぬか』

『馬鹿申せ、吾身を保護する為なら、こんなに沢山の軍隊がかたまる必要がないぢやないか。かく迄大部隊を引率して将軍がお出でたのは敵を鏖しにするためだ。その為に槍や剣を持たしてあるのだ。槍や剣は決して猪や狸を斬るためぢやないぞ。人斬り庖丁と云つて人を斬るためだ。そんな事が分らずに武士の本分が尽せるものか』

『それでも、軍人は平和の守り神と云ふぢやありませぬか』

『或時は平和の守り神となり、或時は天下の攪乱者となり、血河屍山を築き、以て敵国を占領し、戦利品を沢山に収納するのが武士の本領だ』

『まるで強盗みた様なものですな』

『貴様は余程よい頓馬だな。軍隊の必要とならば人家も焼かねばならず、人命もとらねばならず、米麦、金銭は申すに及ばず、豚鶏、大根蕪、凡て必要品は無断徴集するのだ。それでなくては、何で軍隊の維持が保たれるか』

『おい、ワク、テルンスさまの仰有る事あ、チツと道理に叶はぬぢやないか。二つ目には、斬るの、盗むのと、そんな武士があるものだらうかな』

『そら、さうだな』

テルンスは抜く手も見せず雪にひらめく氷の刃、忽ちエムの首を薙ぎ落し、返す刀にワクの頭を無残にも斬り落し、雪の庭は忽ち紅に化した。
