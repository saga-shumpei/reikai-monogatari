Chapter 16: 途上の変 [1270]

途上の変〔1270〕

§ 1

Matsuhiko sings as he advances: 'When Kami appears in the world, to separate the good from the evil, the Kamunahohi who created this world, and the Ōnahohi of vast heart, decree that in all things in the world of men, one must look again and listen again through the Nahohi. Repent and rectify the transgressions of your body. Following the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō and Harukuniwake, when I came to the forest in Nonaka, mysterious shadows appeared, and there were moments when my heart was troubled. When did my elder brother's Inochi, together with Tatsukō-sama, abandon us and depart? Though I once resented them as being cold and indifferent to the extreme, we, whose Mitama are clouded and cannot see even an inch ahead, how could we ever hope to comprehend the profound and subtle Shigumi of the Shinkai? I shall simply rectify my heart, saying that all things are according to the will of Kamisama. Appearing on the mountain of Kogita, I unexpectedly and unknowingly encountered Matsuhime of the Uranai-kyō. Meeting and revealing our names to one another with gentle faces, while lingering on the attachment of parent and child, beginning with Gosankō-sama, and Bankō-sama who celebrates the eternal ages, and the woman warrior Otora-baba-sama, together with Aku, Taku, and Teku, we crossed the single-log bridge, passed through the mysterious forest, and at last arrived here. On a winter day with the withered trees raging, in the lonely sky of our journey, a silver world suddenly piles up.'

'As far as the eye can see, it is sparkling, a view that does not permit even a speck of dust. As I look upon this scene, I am reminded of the state of the world, so full of deceit. It is truly lamentable that it exposes the state of a cold and merciless world, which, while wrapping hundreds of thousands of sins and impurities in its belly, adorns its surface like white snow, possessing neither love nor warmth. It is lamentable that General Ranchi, Katahiko, and a hundred other soldiers gathered in the forest of Ukiki, driven by the evil thoughts of the body-mastering-spirit-subservient, have become subordinates of the evil gods, and are leading a hundred troops to slaughter the Saienkan, which has echoed in the world as the most holy and supreme heaven, the sacred place of Kami.'

'Though I was once the secretary to General Katahiko, now that I have awakened to the path of truth and have been revived in the heart of the Ōkami of infinite benevolence and mercy, how could I ever associate with the crooked? From this moment on, Matsuhiko shall dedicate his utmost heart and the limit of his body, and while illuminating the Mitama of Katahiko, General Ranchi, and others, which are clouded by the crooked, with the light of the Ōkami of great compassion and mercy, I shall lead them to the heavenly pure land and fulfill my duty as the administrator of the teachings of Kami. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase. Before the Ōmikami of the Iwa-no-Mitama and the Misu-no-Mitama, I pray with all my heart. Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, even if the mountains split and the seas dry up, how could I spare this body that I have dedicated to Kami? If the people of the world who do not know the truth call me a reckless warrior, let them call me so. Even if they slander me as a fool, my sincerity is like a mulberry bow that I shall pierce through to the end; this is my resolve, never to pull back. Having been born as a man in this world, seeing the enemy before my eyes, how could I overlook them? The sky of today, which changes from yesterday, and the village clouds hanging over my heart—where might my master be now?'

'And how is Tatsukō serving the sake of my master? Even that is a winter sky of uncertainty. With no way to seek him, as the day darkens and our party wanders on our path, guide us, O Amatsukami and Kunitsukami, and the eight million deities. Before the presence of my master, let us advance, even if only for a moment, even for a short while. Kannagara. I pray before the presence of Kami.'

It is Matsuhiko who sings this as he advances. Gosankō also sings:

'Man is the child of Kami, the palace of Kami. It goes without saying for the Shinkairei, but as one chosen as the administrator of the divine governance of the heaven and earth of the reality world, man is the original ancestor of this world. One must revere the words of Kunitokotachi-no-Ōkami, dwell in the virtues of love and goodness, and while striving in true faith, polish well the one spirit and four souls bestowed by the Amatsu-Mikami. One must cultivate the power of patience, be intimate with others, and while helping and saving others, dedicate one's sincerity to Kami and to the world, and understand that one is a noble Mitama who serves as a living god of this world, firmly and eternally. Even if heaven and earth should overturn,'

§ 2

The one true Ōmichi, from the beginning when Tenchi opened, unto the end of Okuchōnen, is like the Kakihatokiha rock, never to be moved. If we love Kami well, and while immersed in the grace of Kami, only Kamunagara, polish the wisdom that comes from Kami, and well understand the Dauri of Uchiu, if we do not fulfill the Tenshiyoku for which we were born as humans in this world, when we leave this world and arrive in the Reikai, the Seirei will be judged by Ibukidonushi, and it is Hitsujiyō that they will fall into the Jigokudau, and tumble into the truly terrifying Ankai. If one thus defiles the Mitama, what words can one have for the Oyagami who created us? Higoto and Yogoto, forge the heart and refine the liver of Murakimo, reflect upon good and evil, and do not carelessly discard the Onkei bestowed by Kami. If one falls into such a Kiyōgū, one is not filial toward the Kami who created Tenchi. From oneself to one's Shison, one will fall into the group of Magatsu, and like dust and trash, will naturally distance oneself from the Sekai, and must become a body that gathers Momochiyorozu of sins. Reflect, O people. I am the Kami of Kototama-wake, the living Mitama of Konohanahime who appeared on Mount Amegoshi. Here, the two Kami have consulted together.

In order to fill Seirei whose hearts are pure into the body of Gosankō, and to illuminate and save the people whose hearts are clouded with the light of the Shinreikai, I have followed thus far. Ah, Kamunagara. Since the divine work entrusted by Kami has also reached a turning point, I shall change my form from here, become a flame of light, return to the Saienkan, the residence of the Lord Ōkami, and before the presence of the Lord, with a heart calm and peaceful, I shall serve the great divine work. Come, come now, come now. I shall likely meet with Hinodewake and Ryūkō before long. At that time, Matsuhiko, convey the current state of Gosankō completely and in detail. If they hear your message, Hinodewake and Ryūkō will surely nod their heads thinking it must be so, and will rejoice saying it must be so, and will surely thank the divine virtue of the Lord Ōkami at once. Matsuhiko, and all the others, and the hundred believers, serve the noble divine work of Kami with a pure heart, being very healthy and clean. I shall part from you now with this. Ah, Kamunagara. May your Mitama be blessed.'

As the song ended, the figure of Gosankō vanished like smoke. Matsuhiko and all the others were stunned by this mysterious event, and while gazing up at the sky where Gosankō had returned as a great ball of light, they stood still on the path, clapping their hands and worshiping the heroic figure with tears in their eyes.

Mankō sings again: 'A mysterious thing has happened. What on earth is this? Gosankō-sama, whom I thought of as our friend, has suddenly changed into smoke and, saying he is returning to the Saienkan, has fled away in a hurry. Nevertheless, I thought Gosankō was a strange fellow. To think that he would abandon us, who have come all this way and are now left deflated, and return home on the way, he is a person I cannot understand. I do not think he fled because he was afraid of the enemy army in the Ukigi Forest, but it is pathetic that he would flee after having come all the way to the battlefield. Thinking of this, even the office of our teacher, Hinodewake, has started to seem suspicious to me.

Matsuhiko-sama, who gave up Brahmanism and switched to the San-Go-Kyō, must also feel very disappointed. After Harukō-sama had a reunion with his parent in the forest of Yamaguchi, and returned straight to the presence of the other Mikoto of the Tamakuni in the forest of the shrine, we had advanced this far taking Gosankō-sama as our strength, so to be abandoned in a place like this makes me feel uneasy. Matsuhiko-sama was a secretary serving the Brahman army. Aku, Taku, and Teku are also remnants of Brahmanism. Old woman Otora-baba gained power through the path of the divination teachings,

and after reforming as Yatsu, she followed us this far, but I do not know when she will change. Thinking of this and that, if I ponder deeply, it is truly a dangerous situation. Now, now, Matsuhiko the missionary, there should be no problem in your case, but if you happen to meet Katahiko-sama again, and your San-Go fever cools down and you return to your old ways, it will not be right before Kami. If you come to deceive Heaven and deceive your own soul, that will be the end of your life. I, Mankō, advise you never to change your heart. Do not be angry with me for saying such things.'

§ 3

Thinking of the path and thinking of you, it is only because I hold you dear; I know not if it be mere raubashin, but I offer you a chiyotsuto kugen.

Otora-baba-sama, you too must be firm. Imoriwake-sama and Otami-sama, if you should meet on the tochiu, take care that your jibiyō does not saihatsu again, and that you are not carried away by the wheels of your own mouths, causing a futsugafu like a yakebokukuhi catching fire; I add this hitokoto of chiyūi.

Following that, Aku, Teku, and Taku, you mitari must also be careful. Once a man has spoken and said that he believes in the mioshihe of Ananai-kyō, you must maintain your magokoro to the very end, wherever you may be, and serve the pure and noble shingefu of the Ōkami. Kamunagara, swear to Kami, and Mankō, you must watch over the gonin. Ah, Kamunagara, may your mitama be blessed.

斯く歌ひ来る折しも、浮木の森の陣営の入口に俄造りの物見櫓が目についた。よくよく見れば物見櫓の近傍はバラモン軍の兵卒蟻の山を築いた如く、右往左往に駆け巡りながら喚き叫ぶ其声は、一行の耳に何となく厳しく応へた。松彦一行は敵軍の中とは云ひながら、只事ならじ、或は治国別の遭難に非ずやと心も心ならず、敵の陣中を物ともせず、物見櫓の麓を指して足に任せて駆り行く。
