Chapter 1: Holy 言 [1255]

聖言〔1255〕

§ 1

There are two divisions in the Uchiu: the Reikai and the Genkai. Moreover, the Reikai has two aspects, namely Takaamahara and the land of Nesoko, and there is a boundary mediating between these two aspects, which is called the Chiūkai or Seireikai. Furthermore, the Genkai, also known as the Shizenkai, has the distinction of Chiuya and the distinction of kanshiyo, which is just as comparable to how the Reikai has the Tenkai and the Jigokukai. Human beings receive the direct or indirect nairiu of the Reikai, and through the butsushitsu of the Genkai, namely the three great principal substances of gaujiuriu, are made into what is called the nikutai, using this nikutai as a dwelling, and the seirei dwells therein. That seirei is precisely the ningen itself. In short, the kukaku of a human being is nothing more than the kiyotaku of the seirei. This genri is called reishujū. That which is the spiritual is an individual formed from the love of the divine divine character and the truth of faith. And while human beings on the one hand have thoughts of love and faith, on the other hand they have body desires to develop the physical body and live and work in the actual world. This body desire comes from what is called love. However, love toward the body is called self-love. Love that comes directly from the God is called divine love, which is the so-called universal love of loving God and loving all things. Moreover, self-love loves the self and loves the social benefits necessary for the self, which is called the desire for self-interest. As long as a human being has a physical body, self-love is also an indispensable thing, and at the same time, one must trace back to its origin and return without fail to true divine love. In short, when viewed from the Reikai, human beings are seirei, and this thing called the seirei embraces both good and evil aspects. Therefore, human beings are spiritual animals and at the same time physical animals. The seirei either ascends to become a heavenly being or falls to become an evil demon of hell, standing at the watershed of good and evil, right and wrong. And from the perspective of the divine realm, most human beings use the human physical body as a dwelling and wander in the Seireikai. Now, the good seirei are called the righteous guardian deities, and the evil ones are called the secondary guardian deities. The righteous guardian deities receive the direct nairiu of the divine character, and with the human body as an engine, were created by God to serve the purpose of the heavenly kingdom, namely the divine service; if these righteous guardian deities are not violated by the evil spirits that are the secondary guardian deities, and come to be able to thoroughly control them, they make a leap to become the principal guardian deities and join the ranks of heavenly beings. Moreover, when they become cowardly seirei who are overwhelmed by evil spirits, namely the secondary guardian deities, and acquiesce to their beck and call, the seirei themselves are dragged down together into the Jigokukai. At this time, almost the good seirei are merged into the evil spirits, and only the secondary guardian deities swagger and prance about as if they own the place. And these evil spirits are shaped by the strongest things of self-love in the Genkai, namely the various evils and falsehoods that enter from the outside. Those whose mind and body are occupied by such evil spirits are called human beings of tanshureijū. Furthermore, both good spirits and evil spirits are all lumped together and called seirei. Modern human beings, almost a hundred out of a hundred, lack the state of being heavenly beings who are the principal guardian deities, all have their register in the Seireikai, and even within the Seireikai, the vast majority occupy those who have their register in the Jigokukai, with only their superficial aspects open. Furthermore, all scholars of today are straining their brains immensely in an attempt to interpret everything in the Uchiu, repeating research upon research, yet they do not know what spiritual things are, nor do they perceive the existence of the Reikai, and with a mad and demented attitude seek to investigate the ultimate truth of the Uchiu. This is called tanshureijū research. To make matters worse, many have fallen into tanshu-reijū research. As is stated in the Ōmoto Shinyu, they are nothing more than the secondary guardian deities of the night's protection in a dark world. Those written as the big-headed ones along the way are the blind fools wandering in the Seireikai situated midway between the heavenly kingdom and hell.

宇宙には霊界と現界との二つの区界がある。而して霊界には又高天原と根底の国との両方面があり、此両方面の中間に介在する一つの界があつて、これを中有界又は精霊界と云ふのである。又現界一名自然界には昼夜の区別があり寒暑の区別があるのは、恰も霊界に天界と地獄界とあるに比すべきものである。人間は霊界の直接又は間接内流を受け、自然界の物質即ち剛柔流の三大元質によつて、肉体なるものを造られ、此肉体を宿として、精霊之に宿るものである。其精霊は即ち人間自身なのである。要するに人間の躯殻は精霊の居宅に過ぎないのである。此原理を霊主体従といふのである。霊なるものは神の神格なる愛の善と信の真より形成されたる一個体である。而して人間には一方に愛信の想念あると共に、一方には身体を発育し現実界に生き働くべき体欲がある。此体欲は所謂愛より来るのである。併し体に対する愛は之を自愛といふ。神より直接に来る所の愛は之を神愛といひ、神を愛し万物を愛する、所謂普遍愛である。又自愛は自己を愛し、自己に必要なる社会的利益を愛するものであつて、之を自利心といふのである。人間は肉体のある限り、自愛も又必要欠くべからざるものであると共に、人は其本源に遡り、どこ迄も真の神愛に帰正しなくてはならぬのである。要するに人間は霊界より見れば即ち精霊であつて、此精霊なるものは善悪両方面を抱持してゐる。故に人間は霊的動物なると共に又体的動物である。精霊は或は向上して天人となり、或は堕落して地獄の邪鬼となる、善悪正邪の分水嶺に立つてゐるものである。而して大抵の人間は神界より見れば、人間の肉体を宿として精霊界に彷徨してゐるものである。而して精霊の善なるものを正守護神といひ、悪なるものを副守護神と云ふ。正守護神は神格の直接内流を受け、人身を機関として天国の目的即ち御用に奉仕すべく神より造られたもので、此正守護神は副守護神なる悪霊に犯されず、よく之を統制し得るに至れば、一躍して本守護神となり天人の列に加はるものである。又悪霊即ち副守護神に圧倒され、彼が頤使に甘んずる如き卑怯なる精霊となる時は、精霊自らも地獄界へ共々におとされて了ふのである。此時は殆ど善の精霊は悪霊に併合され、副守護神のみ我物顔に跋扈跳梁するに至るものである。そして此悪霊は自然界に於ける自愛の最も強きもの即ち外部より入り来る諸々の悪と虚偽に依つて、形作られるものである。かくの如き悪霊に心身を占領された者を称して、体主霊従の人間といふのである。又善霊も悪霊も皆之を一括して精霊といふ。現代の人間は百人が殆ど百人迄、本守護神たる天人の情態なく、何れも精霊界に籍をおき、そして精霊界の中でも外分のみ開けてゐる、地獄界に籍をおく者、大多数を占めてゐるのである。又今日のすべての学者は宇宙の一切を解釈せむとして非常に頭脳をなやませ、研究に研究を重ねてゐるが、彼等は霊的事物の何物たるを知らず、又霊界の存在をも覚知せない癲狂痴呆的態度を以て、宇宙の真相を究めむとしてゐる。之を称して体主霊従的研究といふ。甚だしきは体主体従的研究に堕して居るものが多い。何れも『大本神諭』にある通り、暗がりの世、夜の守護の副守護神ばかりである。途中の鼻高と書いてあるのは、所謂天国地獄の中途にある精霊界に迷うてゐる盲共のことである。

§ 2

Since all Uchiu possesses the distinction between Reikai and Genkai, it is fundamentally impossible to know its true nature through only one side. To attempt to probe the true nature of the unfathomable, measureless, beginningless, endless, unknown, and unmeasurable Reikai using the so-called scientific knowledge based on the natural laws of Shizenkai must truly be called the height of foolishness and madness. First of all, unless modern scholars reform their brains, recognize even slightly the existence of spiritual things, know true goodness and realize true truth through the direct inner-flowing of Kami, and grasp the clue to comprehend it, it is like waiting for the turbid waters of the Yellow River to run clear for a hundred years. With the attitude of scholars today, no matter how many millions of years they strive, they will never be able to attain their purpose. Just as summer insects do not believe in winter snow, today's scholars have dim intellects and shallow perceptions, and furthermore are arrogant and strong in self-importance, thinking that with their own knowledge all resolution of everything in the Uchiu is achieved, or rather almost as if it were achieved, so it must truly be called blissful ignorance. Regarding the movement of celestial bodies, the rotational movement of the great earth, the circulation of the moon, the principles of cold and heat, and so on, not a single one has yet been found that has attained the truth. From beginning to full completion, filled with contradictions, clashes, and blind confusion, wandering in the realm of pitch-dark ignorance, rebelling against the brilliant light of the sun, and recognizing only the shadow of the will-o'-the-wisp of the underworld, they run about making a commotion as if they had made a great invention—from the eyes of one whose spiritual awakening and discernment have opened even a little, their pathetic state is truly like night-walking specters and demons. The scale of the actual world can perhaps be examined to a certain extent through all computational knowledge. However, no matter how great a doctor of mathematics one may be, what they ultimately reach can ultimately never be divided evenly. For example, if one divides ten into three parts, following the sequence and gradually subdividing further and further, what it ultimately reaches is still that a minute 'one' remains. This 'one,' no matter how fiercely one bristles and studies it, is ultimately a place where it cannot be done. With such a foolish and dark knowledge that even if one advances natural things, namely scientific research, anywhere within Shizenkai, cannot reach a resolution, how could one ever set even a single step inside the gate of the news of Reikai? For the orator to leak the news of Reikai to the darkly bewildered and foolish people of Genkai through the direct inner-flowing of the divine character consisting of the love-goodness and true faith of Ōkami from Reikai, and the indirect inner-flowing of various other angels, feels somewhat like casting pearls before swine. If this is said, Mizugetsu will probably be dismissed with a laugh as a madman or a megalomaniac. However, from my own eyes, I think there is no one as troublesome and uncomprehending as modern scholars. It is truly comical, since self-styled scholars who use plus and minus as their sole weapon, sketching kasuri patterns and konpeitō, and whose research into Genkai has not even reached its threshold, try to meddle in matters of Reikai and act as a saniwa. Therefore, it is inevitable that in this 'Reitai Monogatari' as well, according to the degree of wisdom and spiritual discernment of the people who read it, numerous differences arise in the degree to which they respond to the divine spirit's resonance.

The truth of the Uchiu undergoes not the slightest change from the beginning of the opening and creation down to the end of hundreds of millions of years. However, depending on the wisdom, discernment, cleverness, and foolishness of humans who contrast with this, it is reflected in various miscellaneous ways, and in short, one must know that this change is not in the truth itself, but in human knowledge itself. If modern humans could register in the heavenly realm's organization directly governed by Ōkami and join the ranks of heavenly beings, they would not need to needlessly and recklessly distress their brains, hurt their hearts, ruin their lungs, and bring about nervous breakdowns like modern scholars do, but could easily and clearly comprehend the organizational grain and pattern of the Uchiu.

§ 3

Leaving malicious gossip aside for the present, I shall now proceed to the main subject. Here, as the sole method leading to the Reikai, there is the divine art known as Chinkonkishin. Now, the state in which the human spirit directly receives the inflow of the Goshinkaku toward Daigenshin—that is, the Lord God (or referred to as Ōkami)—and harmonizes with Ōkami is called Kishin. Kishin means the return, unification, and harmonization with the divine Goshinkaku of Ōkami, who is the origin of our spirit. Therefore, Kishin is the most necessary transmitter of the truth of the Reikai as a prophet, by receiving the direct inflow of Ōkami.

Next, illuminated by the Goshinkaku of Ōkami, obtaining Chieshiyōkaku, a tennin who resides in the Reigoku and has advanced to the status of an angel, descends to human spirits, and transmits the news of the Shinkai to the human world, which is called Kamigakari. This is also called the indirect inflow of the Goshinkaku. This likewise seeks a prophet, fills their spirit, and transmits the news of the Shinkai to the human world to a certain degree.

Next, there are evil spirits that invade the human body from the outside and commit sins and falsehoods. These are called wicked spirits or secondary guardian spirits. This state is called Kamitsuki.

All false prophets and bogus saviors deeply believe in their own human spirits the whispers of this secondary guardian, and the possessed spirit itself believes it is a precious god, and what it preaches is also the words of God, believing itself by itself. All such Kamitsuki are malevolent spirits arising from self-love and worldly love, and they are those that most exceedingly bewilder worldly people and damage the Goshinkaku of Ōkami. Such Kamitsuki all have their names registered in the organization of hell, and plot to cause humans of the present world to destroy their good spirits and also to destroy their physical bodies. Those who in recent times are called masters of clairvoyance, clairaudience, or mediums are all the doings of secondary guardian spirits whose names are registered in this hellish realm. In Western countries today, they are gradually beginning to perceive through mediums that the Reikai exists outside the present world, but even among those whose research has advanced considerably, this is merely a degree of having stepped one foot into the spirit world, and it is a place where they cannot even dream of glimpsing the news of heaven. Occasionally, some mediums have appeared who gazed from a distant place at the light of a part of the lowest heaven and made conjectures. Without having thoroughly investigated the truth of the Reikai, even if not to perfection, to recklessly transmit this to the human world is rather like causing stubborn and ignorant humans to increasingly augment their feelings of doubt. Therefore, researchers of the Reikai must thoroughly investigate the ordinary character of mediums and thoroughly explore their mental nature; otherwise, driven by curiosity and conducting insincere research, it is an unavoidable matter in terms of the emotions of ideation that the scholars themselves will fall not only into the Chūyūkai, but even into the path of hell.

Now, Kishin, Kamigakari, and Kamitsuki are all generally called Kamigakari, but one must realize that there are extraordinary differences of superiority and inferiority among them. The narrator has been by the side and humbly served the Kishin state of the founder of Ōmoto for twenty and some years. The founder always said that God enters the physical body from the forehead, and he always stroked the frontal region with the right thumb. The frontal region is the Holy of Holies corresponding to the highest part of Takamanohara, and the direct inflow of the Goshinkaku of Ōkami always begins from the forehead and finally extends to the entire face. Now, man's forehead corresponds to love and goodness, and the face corresponds to everything internal to the Goshinkaku. Awe-inspiringly, the narrator served as the Shinshinsa of the founder for many long years, paid attention here, and was finally able to realize that he was the sole great prophet on earth filled with the Holy Spirit of Ōkami.

Then again, in Takamanohara there are two major distinctions: the Reigoku and the Heavenly Kingdom. I intend to add explanations by calling the tennin dwelling in the Reigoku spiritual tennin for the sake of explanatory convenience, and calling the tennin dwelling in the Heavenly Kingdom heavenly tennin. That is, the inflow coming from the spiritual tennin (indirect inflow) is felt coming from various directions of the human physical body, and finally flows into the interior of its brain. That is, it takes the whole location of the cerebrum, from the forehead and temples, as its collection point. This local part corresponds to the wisdom of the Reigoku. Moreover, the inflow from the heavenly tennin (indirect inflow) flows in from that local part called the hindbrain, where the cerebellum is located within the head—namely, beginning from the ears and reaching up to the entire neck region. That is, this local part corresponds to Shōkaku.

次に大神の御神格に照らされ、知慧証覚を得、霊国に在つてエンゼルの地位に進んだ天人が、人間の精霊に降り来り、神界の消息を人間界に伝達するのを神懸といふ。又之を神格の間接内流とも云ふ。之も亦予言者を求めて其精霊を充たし、神界の消息を或程度まで人間界に伝達するものである。

すべての偽予言者、贋救世主などは、此副守の囁きを人間の精霊自ら深く信じ、且憑霊自身も貴き神と信じ、其説き教へる所も亦神の言葉と、自ら自らを信じてゐるものである。すべてかくの如き神憑は自愛と世間愛より来る凶霊であつて、世人を迷はし且つ大神の神格を毀損すること最も甚しきものである。斯の如き神憑はすべて地獄の団体に籍をおき、現界の人間をして、其善霊を亡ぼし且肉体をも亡ぼさむことを謀るものである。近来天眼通とか千里眼とか、或は交霊術の達人とか称する者は、何れも此地獄界に籍をおける副守護神の所為である。泰西諸国に於ては今日漸く、現界以外に霊界の在ることを、霊媒を通じて稍覚り始めたやうであるが、併し此研究は余程進んだ者でも、精霊界へ一歩踏み入れた位な程度のもので、到底天国の消息は夢想だにも窺ひ得ざる所である。偶には最下層天国の一部の光明を遠方の方から眺めて、臆測を下した霊媒者も少しは現はれてゐる様である。霊界の真相を充分とは行かずとも、相当に究めた上でなくては、妄りに之を人間界に伝達するのは却て頑迷無智なる人間をして、益々疑惑の念を増さしむる様なものである。故に霊界の研究者は最も霊媒の平素の人格に就てよく研究をめぐらし、其心性を十二分に探査した上でなくては、好奇心にかられて、不真面目な研究をするやうな事では、学者自身が中有界は愚か、地獄道に陥落するに至ることは想念の情動上已むを得ない所である。

さて帰神も神懸も神憑も概括して神がかりと称へてゐるが、其間に非常の尊卑の径庭ある事を覚らねばならぬのである。大本開祖の帰神情態を口述者は前後二十年間、側に在つて伺ひ奉つたことがある。開祖は何時も神様が前額より肉体にお這入りになると云はれて、いつも前額部を右手の拇指で撫でてゐられたことがある。前額部は高天原の最高部に相応する至聖所であつて、大神の御神格の直接内流は必ず前額より始まり、遂に顔面全部に及ぶものである。而して人の前額は愛善に相応し、顔面は神格の内分一切に相応するものである。畏多くも口述者が開祖を審神者として永年間、茲に注目し、遂に大神の聖霊に充たされ給ふ地上唯一の大予言者たることを覚り得たのである。

それから又高天原には霊国、天国の二大区別があつて、霊国に住める天人は之を説明の便宜上霊的天人といひ、天国に住める天人を天的天人といふことにして説明を加へようと思ふ。乃ち霊的天人より来る内流（間接内流）は人間肉体の各方面より感じ来り、遂に其頭脳の中に流入するものである。即ち前額及び顳顬より大脳の所在全部に至る迄を集合点とする。此局部は霊国の智慧に相応するが故である。又天的天人よりの内流（間接内流）は頭中小脳の所在なる後脳といふ局部即ち耳より始まつて頸部全体にまで至る所より流入するものである、即ち此局部は証覚に相応するが故である。

§ 4

When these tennin converse with ningen, what they say is thus, and enters into the saunenchiu of ningen. All those who converse with tennin are also able to see the jibutsu present there through the light of Takaamahara. That is because the naibun (reikaku) of that person is enveloped within this light. And the tennin, through the naibun of this person, are likewise able to see the jibutsu of Chijiyō. That is, the tennin see genjitsukai through the naibun of ningen, and ningen, enveloped in the light of Tenkai, are able to see all jibutsu existing in Tenkai. The tennin in Tenkai harmonize with the jibutsu of seken through the naibun of ningen, and seken in turn comes to harmonize with Tenkai. This is called geniuitsuchi, reinikufuji, and meianitsutai.

When the Ōkami speaks with a yogenshiya, it is not as in the case of ningen in taiko, that is, kamiyo, flowing into their naibun and speaking with them. The Ōkami first fills a seirei with His kesau, and dispatches this filled seirei into the body of the yogenshiya. Therefore, this seirei, filled with the reitoku of the Ōkami, conveys His kotoba to the yogenshiya. Such a case should be called transmission rather than an inflow of shinkaku. Transmission refers to the act of notifying reikai shōsoku and the will of the Ōkami to genkainin.

And since these kotoba are seigen emerging directly from the Ōkami, they are one and all firm and unshakeable, and filled with shinkaku. Furthermore, within those seigen, each and every one contains an naigi. Now, the tennin residing in Tenkai comprehend this naigi by means of spiritual and celestial significance, and therefore can immediately understand the divine will; however, ningen attempt to interpret the seigen according to natural and scientific significance in all matters, which merely increases their skepticism so that they can ultimately reach no satisfactory resolution. Hereupon, the Ōkami, desiring to achieve the objective of Tenkai and sekai—that is, geniuitsuchi—and to bring them to the state of shinjin wagafu, descended Mizunorei into the world, caused the seigen transmitted by the direct yogenshiya to be explained in detail, and sought to teach and guide genkainin.

How the seirei comes to be filled with the shinkaku coming from the Ōkami through kesau will be clearly understood by looking at what has just been stated. The seirei filled with the august shinkaku of the Ōkami believes itself to be the Ōkami and knows only that its utterances proceed from shinkaku, knowing nothing else. And until that seirei finishes saying what must be said, it firmly and unwaveringly believes that it is the Ōkami and that what it says is the kotoba of the Ōkami; however, once it comes to fulfill its mission, the Ōkami returns to heaven, whereupon its shinkaku abruptly diminishes and its utterances lack much clarity. Therefore, at that point, it comes to perceive and acknowledge that it was indeed a seirei all along, and that its utterances were caused to be spoken by the Ōkami. The founder of Ōmoto, for instance, from the very beginning well acknowledged through the direct internal flow of the Ōkami that she was transmitting the will of God, and that her own seirei was filled with shinkaku and fulfilled the role of transmission for the sake of all people. The proof of this is clearly recorded in various places in the "Ōmoto Shin'yū". Omitting the tedium of citing it here anew, if one studies the "Shin'yū" of the founder, I believe the circumstances of this interval will become clear.

That which descended directly into the founder was the Ōmoto-kami Ōkuninotachirotsu-no-Mikoto, and her seirei were Wakahikogimi-no-Mikoto and Kunitakehiko-no-Mikoto. Therefore, expressions appearing in various places in the "Shin'yū" such as... the Ōkami, the ancestor of this world, appearing as Kunitakehiko-no-Mikoto... or... becoming one with the mitama of Wakahikohime, to make known to the people of the world all matters of the three thousand worlds (gen, yu, shin san-kai)... can be understood as the descent upon the founder—who was a tennin of Chijiyō, having her mitama, namely Tenkai, registered as a yogenshiya—of the seirei of Kunitakehiko-no-Mikoto and Wakahikohime-no-Mikoto of the so-called seireikai, filling them and causing the seigen to be bestowed.

As was stated in the previous volume, tennin are able to convey meaning in a mere one or two words—meanings that ningen could not convey without expending hundreds of words. Therefore, although the seigen demonstrated by the founder, namely the yogenshiya, are immediately comprehensible in meaning to the tennin, they cannot be easily comprehended by the dark knowledge, dim eyes, and half-closed ears of genkainin who wander in chūyu. Therefore, as a denshatsu to explain those seigen in detail and admonish the people of the world, a seirei filled with the shinkaku of the Ōkami of Mizunomitama came, according to the principle of tsugō, into the physical body of the hensei nyonin, and through her hands and through her mouth, broke down a single word or two into thousands of words and minutely divided a single page of text into hundreds of pages, causing it to flow into the naibun through the ears and eyes of the people of the world, thereby causing her to participate in shingyō as a tennin of Chijiyō. Therefore, those who can truly and profoundly understand the "Shin'yū" of the founder are seirei already registered in the ranks of the tennin, whereas seirei wandering in chūyukai must attain indirect comprehension through the detailed explanations of Mizunomitama. And those who cannot assent even to this detailed explanation, who harbor doubts and adopt a research-oriented attitude, are members of the so-called dark and ignorant crowd, arrogant half-baked scholars who have acquired knowledge through study. That such humans are already registered in jigokukai is clear according to the principle of tsugō, and such persons are difficult to save easily. The reason is that the naibun of such humans is entirely closed off, closed toward the upper realm, with only the external part open, and their thought turns their back on God and faces only jigoku at the soles of their feet. And their knowledge is darkened, their spiritual hearing is dulled, and their spiritual vision is dazzled, so that no light and no sound can easily reach their naibun. Yet because God is supreme in benevolence and love, He is troubled in mind day and night, even over such difficult beings, changing His form in various ways in order to save their jigokukai seirei. Ah, Kamunagara Tamachihauhase.

原文（近代文語）

以上の天人が人間と言葉を交へる時に当り、其言ふ所は斯の如くにして、人間の想念中に入り来るものである。すべて天人と語り合ふ者は、又高天原の光によつて其処にある事物を見ることを得るものである。そは其人の内分（霊覚）は此光の中に包まれてゐるからである。而して天人は此人の内分を通じて、又地上の事物を見ることを得るのである。即ち天人は人間の内分によつて、現実界を見、人間は天界の光に包まれて、天界に在るすべての事物を見ることが出来る。天界の天人は人間の内分によつて世間の事物と和合し、世間は又天界と和合するに至るものである。之を現幽一致、霊肉不二、明暗一体といふのである。

大神が予言者と物語り給ふ時は、太古即ち神代の人間に於けるが如く、其内分に流入してこれと語り給ふことはない。大神は先づおのが化相を以て精霊を充たし、此充たされた精霊を予言者の体に遣はし給ふのである。故に此精霊は大神の霊徳に充ちて其言葉を予言者に伝ふるものである。斯の如き場合は、神格の流入ではなくて伝達といふべきものである。伝達とは霊界の消息や大神の意思を現界人に対して告示する所為を云ふのである。

而して此等の言葉は大神より直接に出で来れる聖言なるを以て、一々万々確乎不易にして、神格にて充たされてゐるものである。而して其聖言の裡には何れも皆内義なるものを含んでゐる。而して天界に在る天人は此内義を知悉するには霊的及び天的意義を以てするが故に、直に其神意を了解し得れども、人間は何事も自然的、科学的意義に従つて其聖言を解釈せむとするが故に、懐疑心を増すばかりで到底満足な解決は付け得ないのである。茲に於てか大神は、天界と世界即ち現幽一致の目的を達成し、神人和合の境に立到らしめむとして、瑞霊を世に降し、直接の予言者が伝達したる聖言を詳細に解説せしめ、現界人を教へ導かむとなし給うたのである。

精霊は如何にして化相によつて大神より来る神格の充たす所となるかは、今述べた所を見て、明かに知らるるであらう。大神の御神格に充たされたる精霊は、自分が大神なることを信じ、又其所言の神格より出づることを知るのみにして、其他は一切知らない。而して其精霊は言ふべき所を言ひ尽す迄は、自分は大神であり、自分の言ふことは大神の言であると固く信じ切つてゐるけれども、一旦其使命を果すに至れば、大神は天に復り給ふが故に俄に其神格は劣り、其所言は余程明晰を欠くが故に、そこに至つて、自分はヤツパリ精霊であつたこと、又自分の所言は大神より言はしめ給うた事を知覚し、承認するに至るものである。大本開祖の如きは始めより大神の直接内流によつて、神の意思を伝へ居ること及び自分の精霊が神格に充たされて、万民の為に伝達の役を勤めてゐたことを能く承認してゐられたのである。其証拠は『大本神諭』の各所に明確に記されてある。今更ここに引用するの煩を省いておくから、開祖の『神諭』に就いて研究さるれば此間の消息は明かになることと信ずる。

開祖に直接帰神し給うたのは大元神大国治立尊様で、其精霊は、稚姫君命と国武彦命であつた。故に『神諭』の各所に……此世の先祖の大神が国武彦命と現はれて……とか又は……稚姫君の身魂と一つになりて、三千世界（現幽神三界）の一切の事を、世界の人民に知らすぞよ……と現はれてゐるのは、所謂精霊界なる国武彦命、稚姫君命の精霊を充たして、予言者の身魂即ち天界に籍をおかせられた、地上の天人なる開祖に来つて、聖言を垂れさせ給うことを覚り得るのである。

前巻にもいつた通り、天人は現界人の数百言を費さねば其意味を通ずることの出来ない言葉をも、僅かに一二言にて其意味を通達し得るものである。故に開祖即ち予言者によつて示されたる聖言は、天人には直に其意味が通ずるものなれども、中有に迷へる現界人の暗き知識や、うとき眼や、半ば塞がれる耳には容易に通じ得ない。それ故に其聖言を細かく説いて世人に諭す伝達者として、瑞の御霊の大神の神格に充たされたる精霊が、相応の理によつて変性女子の肉体に来り、其手を通じ、其口を通じて、一二言の言葉を数千言に砕き、一頁の文章を数百頁に微細に分割して、世人の耳目を通じて、其内分に流入せしめむ為に、地上の天人として、神業に参加せしめられたのである。故に開祖の『神諭』を其儘真解し得らるる者は、已に天人の団体に籍をおける精霊であり、又中有界に迷へる精霊は、瑞の御霊の詳細なる説明に依つて、間接諒解を得なくてはならぬのである。而して此詳細なる説明さへも首肯し得ず、疑念を差挟み、研究的態度に出でむとする者は、所謂暗愚無智の徒にして、学で知慧の出来た途中の鼻高、似而非学者の徒である。斯の如き人間は已に已に地獄界に籍をおいてゐる者なることは、相応の理によつて明かである、斯の如き人は容易に済度し難きものである。何故ならば、其人間の内分は全く閉塞して、上方に向つて閉ぢ、外分のみ開け、その想念は神を背にし、脚底の地獄にのみ向つてゐるからである。而して其知識はくらみ霊的聴覚は鈍り、霊的視覚は眩み、如何なる光明も如何なる音響も容易に其内分に到達せないからである。されど神は至仁至愛にましませば、斯の如き難物をも、種々に身を変じ給ひて、其地獄的精霊を救はむと、昼夜御心を悩ませ給ひつつあるのである。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
