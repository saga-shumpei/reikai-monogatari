Chapter 13: Moon 照 Mountain [1267]

月照山〔1267〕

§ 1

Harukuniwake and Tatsukō, guided by twelve angels, advanced deep into the palace. However, this palace was called the front hall or the front shrine of Tosotsuten, and it was not the innermost Goten. The innermost Goten is called the Daishiseishiyo, and it is the Oima of the Ōkami. Into this Ima, no matter how virtuous an angel may be, one cannot step even a single step. It is called the Wakamiya of the Sun, and it is the place where the Ōkami manifests His Misugata as the Sun of Takaamahara. Therefore, only those Tennin with the most excellent Shiyōkaku can behold His figure from afar, as if looking into the Yata mirror. Because the Kami exists in a state of total Enman, He appears here as the Sun, and His Mihikari radiates light that is beyond comparison, perhaps hundreds of times that of the sun of the Shizenkai, making it impossible to gaze upon Him easily. However, there are times when the Ōkami appears as the Sun before groups of Tennin. At such times, adopting an attitude of Wako-Dojin, He appears to the Tennin of the first heaven with seven times the light of the sun on earth, to those in the second heaven with five times the light, and to those in the third heaven with twice the light, as is customary. This is merely a general outline, and the intensity of the light varies depending on the individual virtue of each group and each Tennin. Sometimes, He also appears within a group as a single Tennin. Furthermore, as for the divine plan and preparations of the Ōkami in the innermost Daishiseishiyo, no matter how excellent the Shiyōkaku of a Tennin may be, they cannot clearly perceive it. The reason is that the divine will of the Ōkami, who is most noble, most supreme, most wise, and most holy, is beyond the reach of the thoughts of Tennin and is a realm that their Shiyōkaku cannot attain.

In the heavenly realm of Takaamahara, that which integrates all things is virtue. It is the degree of the nature of this virtue that leads to the differences in the heavenly realm. Moreover, this integration of the various Tennin is by no means the achievement of the Tennin themselves, but the work of the Ōkami, who is the source of virtue. The Ōkami guides all Tennin, harmonizes them, manages them, and allows them to act freely as long as they abide in that virtue. Thus, the Ōkami causes the Tennin to find peace in their respective places, and allows them to enjoy their lives by attaining love, faith, wisdom, and Shiyōkaku. Therefore, one cannot easily approach the side of the Ōkami. Kototamawake-no-Mikoto advanced deep into the inner sanctuary, and reported that Harukuniwake and Tatsukō had come up for training in touring the heavenly realms due to circumstances in the divine world, and having received the permission of the Ōkami, it was decided that a grand banquet would be held in the front hall. First, Kototamawake-no-Mikoto sat in the center, Seiōbo sat alongside occupying the seat to the right, and seats were provided for Harukuniwake and Tatsukō beside them. Then, the twelve angels placed beautiful grape wine on cloud-legged tables, and respectfully holding them at eye level, placed them quietly before the four. Next, beautiful peach fruits were placed one by one on the cloud-legged tables, and one was placed precisely before Harukuniwake, and one before Tatsukō. Seiōbo first offered a glass cup to Kototamawake-no-Mikoto and poured the wine to the brim. Kototamawake-no-Mikoto received it with both hands, drank it, rinsed it in the water of the finger bowl, took paper from his bosom, wiped the cup clean, and offered it to Harukuniwake. Harukuniwake received it with both hands, had it filled again to eight-tenths by Seiōbo, drank it after receiving it with both hands several times, and again rinsed it in the finger bowl, wiped it clean, and passed it to Tatsukō. Tatsukō received the cup with both hands, and was overcome with tears of gratitude. Seiōbo moved her knees closer and had the cup of Tatsukō filled to about half. With this, the cup-passing came to an end. One heavenly maiden held the sake bottle and cups at eye level just as they were placed on the cloud-legged tables and carried them to the next room. This wine was the most precious gift, which could be likened to the blood and flesh of the Ōkami. When one drinks this sake, all the impurities of the heart are wiped away, one is granted immense divine power, and one can sustain eternal life. Next, Seiōbo gave the two peaches brought by the heavenly maiden to the two men and ordered them to eat them immediately in this place. The two men received them as commanded, and while marveling at the flavor, quietly finished eating them. Mysteriously, the seeds were as small as grains of rice, and before they knew it, the seeds had also passed down their throats. These peaches were fruits that had ripened in the front garden, and they are auspicious divine fruits said to grant a lifespan of three thousand years. Seiōbo did not utter a single word, but kept watching the faces of the two with a happy smile. The twelve heavenly maidens played flutes, drums, hand drums, and kakko drums, sang interesting songs in clear voices, and the four youngest-looking heavenly maidens among them finished dancing the butterfly dance with long sleeves gracefully, bowed all together, and hid themselves in the next room with the rustling sound of their garments. Then, Tatsukō was granted the divine name Tamayoriwake, and his joy and excitement were beyond measure; he tried to express his gratitude by singing a song, but struck by the light of the heavenly realm, he too could not utter a single word.

§ 2

The reason Seiōbo did not utter a single word at this Enseki toward these Futari was that they were in the Zenden near the presence of the awe-inspiring Ōkami, and thus she exercised Enriyo. Since even a deity of such noble Shiyōkaku as Seiōbo expressed Kinshin and would not utter a single word, Kototamawakenomikoto also maintained Chinmoku, and Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake could not have spoken even if they had wished to.

After a while, the former Mahihime appeared in this place, leading two beautiful Fujin. Harukuniwake felt as though he had Miobo of those two women from somewhere, and as he tilted his head and looked closely, Anihaka, the woman in the lead was Murasakihime. Then, Murasakihime, holding Tamateruhiko in her arms with a look of joy, whispered something to Tamateruhiko with her Heujiyō and took a seat on the left side of Seiōbo. When he looked closely at the other woman, it was Otama-no-kata. Otama-no-kata held Tamateruhime in her arms, and while whispering something to Tamateruhime with her expression in the same way, she took a seat on the right side of Seiōbo Shizushizu. Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake were startled and, without thinking, said 'Ah,' but they could not continue their words. Seiōbo gave a signal with her eyes to Murasakihime and Otama-no-kata. Murasakihime stood up smoothly, took a seat in front of Harukuniwake, and while wearing an indescribable smile, had him hold Tamateruhiko in his arms. Otama-no-kata also stood up smoothly, advanced before Tamayoriwake, created a smile with joy, had her hold Tamateruhime, and the two returned Shizushizu to the left and right of Seiōbo. After a while, two Tennyo appeared before Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake; one held Tamateruhiko and the other held Tamateruhime as if they were precious, and they proceeded quietly toward the palace of the Ōkami in the inner sanctuary, muffling their footsteps. Kototamawakenomikoto gave a silent greeting to Seiōbo, and beckoning the two, left this palace. The two likewise gave a silent greeting to Seiōbo and followed behind Kototamawakenomikoto, leaving the Zenden. From there, they passed through the middle gate and came out to the front gate.

Incidentally, that they were made to hold Tamateruhiko and Tamateruhime in this Zenden must have had a deep meaning, but the two could not understand what that intent was. However, the time will surely come when that meaning becomes clear. It is the hope of the author that the reader will enjoy waiting for the time of its revelation.

The three came out to the front gate. Countless beautiful Ten-nin seemed to be celebrating the future of Kototamawakenomikoto and the other two, each waving beautiful golden flags in the divine wind. Thinking whether it was a dream, the two proceeded with Kototamawakenomikoto toward somewhere unknown, while reciting the Amatsu Norito, sent off by the voices of joy of many Ten-nin, along a level road that looked as if it were paved with gold sand, where beautiful trees and flowers grew on both sides of the path.

Kototamawakenomikoto accompanied the two to a certain beautiful mountaintop, and while gazing at the scenery in all directions, sat down here to rest. The two likewise sat down at his side, struck by the majesty of the heavenly realm and left speechless.

Kototama: 'Harukuniwake-sama, Tamayoriwake-sama, you have received great blessings; I congratulate you.'

Harukuni: 'I have no words to express my gratitude. Through your person, the Ōkami has guided the likes of us to such a noble divine court and even bestowed such wonderful gifts upon us; I cannot contain my feelings of gratitude.'

Tamayori: 'After receiving various divine virtues and even being bestowed with a divine name, I truly do not know what to say, and I have realized that my mission is becoming increasingly significant. This is entirely due to the benevolence of the Ōkami and your intercession, and I am truly overwhelmed with gratitude.'

Kototama: 'I have merely acted as an intermediary according to the command of the Ōkami, so I am troubled to receive your gratitude. Since I believe that everything in the heavenly realm is the work of the Kami, I am truly troubled to be thanked. To receive words of gratitude from you is, in short, to claim the divine virtues of the Kami as my own. I beg you, from now on, no matter what happens, do not speak words of gratitude to me. This is the one thing I strictly request. Please, offer your gratitude to the Kami.'

Harukuni: 'Yes, thank you very much; I shall surely keep this in mind from now on.'

Saying this, they joined their hands together and offered a prayer of gratitude toward the Shibikiu.

'This place is a paradise in the Daiichitengoku, and it is called Seiyōzan. Everyone, please remove your Himenpu. Now that you have come this far, you will be able to see clearly even if you remove them. Since you have been given the Inochi no Sake in the Zenden, and in addition, have been granted the magnificent fruit of the peach, you will no longer feel dazzled even if you tour the various Shiyodantai of the Saikautengoku, and you will also understand the Gengo of all the Tennin clearly. My mission regarding you ends here. Please feel free to travel around the heavenly organizations, and I hope that after you pay homage at the Gekikyūden of the Reikoku where the Moon Ōkami resides, you will return to your manifestation; farewell.'

Saying this, he transformed once again into a great ball of light and hid his heroic figure in one direction of the heavens. The two watched his departure and bowed down for a while, but Harukuniwake turned to Tamayoriwake and said,

Harukuniwake: 'Ya, Tamayoriwake-dono, is this not truly a magnificent thing? I had heard that Kototama-wake-no-Mikoto-sama was a god of old, but he appeared before us now, bestowed upon us various magnificent teachings, and then, becoming a five-three-lord, descended to guide us; his nobility and the gratitude I feel are such that I, Harukuniwake, can no longer find the words to express my thanks.'

Tamayoriwake: 'As you say, we have been granted such magnificent guidance; there could be no greater joy than this. Ah, God, may this joy and glory shine upon us forever; Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase.'

Harukuniwake: 'Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase.'

Clapping their hands, they moistened their sleeves with tears of gratitude. From this point, the two visited the various Shiyodantai of the heavens one by one, received unexpected hospitality from the Tennin of each organization, and while living in a state filled with gratitude, they crossed over Seiyōzan to visit the Gekikyūden of the Reikoku, proceeding while offering Norito along the way, aiming for the center of the Reikoku.

The scene suddenly changed, and the two stood on a great field where purple, red, yellow, and white flowers were blooming in fragrance.

Tamayoriwake: 'Moshi, Harukuniwake-sama, this vast field as far as the eye can see, where a hundred flowers are in full bloom, could this be what is called the Reikoku?'

Harukuniwake: 'Saa, it does seem like the Reikoku. I would like to meet some Tennin and ask.'

Just as they were speaking, a messenger of propaganda, making his surroundings shine with spiritual light, came from behind with quick steps, calling out, 'Oi, oi!' The two stopped and waited for the messenger to approach them.

Messenger: 'I am a messenger of propaganda of the Reikoku named Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto. Are you not the two, Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake?'

The two were startled and knelt on the ground.

Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake: 'Yes, as you say, we are the two, Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake. Were you Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto-sama, the previous incarnation of Gesshōhiko-sama whom we long for daily? Not knowing this, we have been very rude. Please, we beg you to forgive our impropriety.'

They replied while drowning in tears of joy. Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto took the two in his train and walked toward the east with such swiftness of foot. The two, not wanting to be left behind, followed him with all their might, running in short, quick steps. Before they knew it, they had arrived on a small, high hill. Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto turned to the two and said,

Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto: 'This is the most famous mountain in the Reikoku, called Gesshōzan. As you know, this mountain is a truly flat place. If you proceed further into the interior with me from here, there is a magnificent palace called the Gekikyūden, which is the palace of the Moon Ōkami-sama. Saa, it is but one more step; let us hurry.'

Saying this, he began to walk quickly again. The two finally reached the front of a gate decorated with seven treasures. Many beautiful Tennin came out to welcome Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto's return, expressing their welcome with music and songs. Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto returned the greetings to each of the Tennin one by one, and passed through the seven gates to enter deep into the residence. The two followed behind, bowing to the many Tennin with vigor, and hurried toward the Gekikyūden.

と云ひながら両手を合せ、茲に両人は紫微宮の方に向つて感謝の祝詞を奏上した。

『此処は第一天国の楽園で、聖陽山と申します。皆さま、被面布をお除りなさいませ。最早此処迄参りました以上は、お除りになつても能く分ります。貴方は前殿に於て生命の酒を与へられ、加ふるに結構な桃の実迄も与へられ給うたのですから、もはや最高天国の諸団体を御巡覧になつても目の眩むやうな事もなく、又総ての天人の言語も明瞭に分ります。私はこれにて貴方等に対する今回の使命は終りました。どうぞ自由に天国団体をお廻り遊ばし、月の大神のまします霊国の月宮殿に御参拝の上、御帰顕あらむ事を望みます、さらば』

と云ひながら、又もや大光団と化して、天の一方に其英姿を隠させたまうた。二人は其後を眺め、暫く伏し拝み居たりしが、治国別は玉依別に向ひ、

『ヤア玉依別殿、実に結構な事では厶らぬか。言霊別命様は昔の神様と承はつて居たが、現在吾前に現はれて、種々と結構な教訓を垂れたまひ、又もや五三公となつて身を下し、吾等を導きたまひし其尊さ、有難さ、治国別はもはや感謝の言葉さへ出なくなりましたよ』

『仰せの如く結構な御導きに預かり、こんな嬉しい事は復と厶いますまい。アヽ神様、この悦びと栄えをして永遠に吾等に臨ませたまへ、惟神霊幸倍坐世』

『惟神霊幸倍坐世』

と手を拍ち感謝の涙に袖を霑して居た。是より二人は天国の諸団体を一々訪問し、各団体の天人より意外の優遇を受け、感謝に満ちた境涯を送りながら、是より霊国の月宮殿に詣でむと聖陽山を乗り越え、霊国の中心を目当に道々祝詞を奏上しながら進み往く。

局面忽ち一変して紫、赤、黄、白さまざまの花咲き匂ふ大野ケ原に二人は立つて居た。

『モシ治国別さま、見渡す限り際限なき百花爛漫たる大原野、これが所謂霊国で厶いませうかなア』

『サア、霊国らしう厶いますなア。誰人か天人に会つて尋ねたいものです』

と話す折しも、一人の宣伝使、霊光に四辺を輝かせながら後より足早に、オーイオーイと声かけ進み来る。二人は立ち止まり、宣伝使の吾側に近づくを待つて居た。

『私は大八洲彦命と申す霊国の宣伝使で厶います。貴方は治国別、玉依別のお二方では厶いませぬか』

二人はハツと大地に踞み、

『ハイ、仰せの通り、治国別、玉依別の両人で厶います。貴方は吾々が日頃慕ひまつる月照彦様の前身、大八洲彦命様で厶いましたか、存ぜぬ事とて甚い失礼を致しました。何卒不都合の段お赦しを願ひます』

と嬉し涙に暮れながら答へた。大八洲彦命は両人を従へ、スタスタと東を指して進み往くその足の早さ。二人は後れじと一生懸命にチヨコチヨコ走りになつて従いて行く。何時の間にやら小高き丘陵の上に着いて居た。大八洲彦命は二人に向ひ、

『此処は霊国一の名山、月照山と申します。此山は御存じの通り実に平坦な場所で厶います。是より私と奥へお進みになれば、月の大神様の宮殿なる月宮殿と云ふ立派な御殿が厶います。サア、も一足です、急ぎませう』

と又もや急ぎ歩み出した。二人は漸くにして七宝をもつて飾られたる門の前に辿りついた。数多の麗しき天人は大八洲彦命の帰館を出で迎へ、音楽や歌をもつて歓迎の意を表するのであつた。大八洲彦命は諸天人に一々挨拶を返しながら、七つの門を潜つて邸内深く進み入る。二人は後に従ひ勢よく数多の天人に会釈しながら、月宮殿さして急ぎ往く。

§ 4

Ōyashimahikonomikoto guided the two into the depths of the hall, commanded the many tenniyo to serve rare fruits and wine, and had the shiyotennin perform kabuonkiyoku to soothe their riyojiyō.

The two, choking back tears of kanshiya, partook of the rare onjiki at the command of Ōyashimahikonomikoto, while never neglecting to recite the Amatsunorito in their kōchiu.

The Ōkami who appeared in this place, wearing kinshiyokusanran giyoi and bearing an indescribable smile upon a beautiful yōbau, was none other than Seiōbo, who had guided them through the Tauen in the Shibikiu earlier.

Behind Seiōbo, a kiyodai getsukuō followed and shone like a shadow.

Ōyashimahikonomikoto bowed respectfully and addressed the Ōbo, 'Thanks to you, both Harukuniwake and Tamayoriwake have finally completed their training in the heavenly realm; this is entirely due to the grace of the Ōkami-sama, and I offer my deepest gratitude on their behalf.'

The two bowed their heads in sudden realization and remained in awe.

Seiōbo approached the two, took Harukuniwake's hand in her left and Tamayoriwake's in her right, shed tears, and said, 'Thou two have well respected the divine command and fulfilled the inspection of the heavenly and spiritual realms; thy sincerity is beyond praise. Thou two shall now return toward the Amenoyachimata, and thy students, Ark and Tar, will surely come to meet you. Then, thou two shall return to your original physical bodies and be able to participate in the divine work of Lord Susanoo; though the parting is endless, we must bid farewell here.'

With her voice choked with emotion, she turned back repeatedly and returned toward the okuden.

The two bowed down to her retreating figure in sudden realization, filled with boundless emotion.

Ōyashimahikonomikoto turned to the two and said, 'Farewell, I shall take my leave here; do your best for the divine work.'

Having said this, he once again became a brilliant light and hid his form in the eastern sky.

From this point on, the two, while reciting norito, crossed the middle heaven, passed over the lower heaven, and headed toward the Amenoyachimata to participate in the divine work.

大八洲彦命は二人を導き、殿内深く進み、数多の天女に命じ、珍らしき果実や酒などを饗応し、歌舞音曲を諸天人に奏せしめ、其旅情を慰めた。二人は感謝の涙に咽びつつ、大八洲彦命の命のまにまに珍らしき飲食を喫しつつ、口中に天津祝詞の奏上を怠らなかつた。奥殿より金色燦爛たる御衣を着し、麗しき容貌に得も云はれぬ笑を湛へ、此場に現はれ給うた大神は、最前紫微宮に於て、桃園の案内をされた西王母であつた。西王母の後には巨大なる月光が影の如くつき従ひ輝いて居た。大八洲彦命は恭しく頭を下げ、王母に向ひ、
