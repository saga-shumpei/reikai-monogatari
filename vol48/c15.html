竜公『今幽界で蠑螈別、エキスの両人に面会し、睾丸の掴み合ひをして来た所だ。大方それから考へると、最早肉体は冷たくなつて、現界の人ではないかも知れないよ』

タール『ナニ、俺達と今一緒に倒れた所だ、彼奴ア酒の量が多いのでよく寝てるのだ。俄にブチヤケるやうな雪が降つて、瞬間に一尺も積つたのだ。本当に不思議な雪だつたよ。何はともあれ、竜公さま、お前は冥土の知己だから、一つ気をつけてやり給へ。亡者卒業生だからなア、亡者が亡者に対するのは、身魂相応の理によるものだからなア、アツハツハヽヽ』

§ 4

Tatsukō kicked the snow away with his foot to see Imoriwake rising up, sitting cross-legged in the snow with his eyes closed. Ekisu, too, having had the snow cleared away by Tatsukō's foot and his head kicked, regained consciousness; he sat in the snow with his eyes still shut, moving his mouth in a chewing motion. Imoriwake, in a frenzy, spoke in a disjointed, spiritless voice:

Imoriwake: 'General Katahiko, return Otami! Hey, you fair-skinned brat, what have you done with my woman? Will you not bring her out to this place quickly? ...Oh, Otora has come, it hurts, it hurts... you are pulling on my kintama, ouch, it hurts, I cannot breathe! Ekisu, hey, pull on Tatsukō's kintama!'

He continued to chatter in broken fragments. Ekisu, for his part, cried out in a spiritless voice:

Ekisu: 'Ah, ah, ah, it hurts, it hurts, it hurts! My kintama is being crushed, it is being crushed!'

Aaku tilted his head and said:

Aaku: 'What a strange thing this is. No sooner does Tatsukō-sama regain consciousness and complain of his kintama hurting, than Imoriwake begins to talk of kintama, and even that fellow Ekisu joins in to blather about kintama. This is amusing. Ehehehe, hey, you general of the kintama, will you not wake up? Pull yourself together!'

He twisted Imoriwake's nose with all his might.

Imoriwake: 'Ouch, ouch, ouch! That fellow Otora is pinching my nose again... forgive me, forgive me!'

Aaku: 'Hahaha, hey, Imoriwake, it is me, it is me! Will you not open your eyes? Do you know where you are?'

Imoriwake: 'I do not know where, it is in front of the Tsujidau. Take me to the Jinei of Ukiki quickly.'

Aaku: 'This is the Jinei of the forest of Ukiki. You must have been blinded by drink because you consumed too much sake.'

He slapped his cheeks repeatedly with the palm of his hand. Imoriwake suddenly came to his senses and looked around to see Ekisu sitting beside him, pale as snow. Then, seeing Harukuniwake and Tatsukō standing there, he folded his hands in wonder,

Imoriwake: 'Hate, hate.'

He said, peering into their faces as if to bore holes in them.

Harukuniwake: 'Imoriwake-sama, Ekisu-sama, this is the Jinei of the forest of Ukiki. I, too, had my soul depart from my body for a while and went on a journey through the Yachimata. You also met me in the Yachimata, did you not? However, since we have now returned to the Genkai, please be at ease. More than that, I am concerned about the fate of General Ranchi, General Katahiko, and Otami-sama. Some emergency may have broken out at the watchtower. Come, let us go.'

Hearing of Otami's peril, Imoriwake's drunkenness faded, and he returned to his senses. He mounted his horse at the Jinei, and Harukuniwake, Tatsukō, and the other four aligned their horses' heads, riding off toward the watchtower, their horses' hooves sounding bravely as they left their prints in the snow.
