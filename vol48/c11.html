Chapter 11: Spirit 陽 Mountain [1265]

霊陽山〔1265〕

§ 1

The trees that grow in Takaamahara are adorned with the seven treasures, as mentioned in the Buddhist scriptures, such as gold, silver, menau, shiyako, ruri, hari, and suishiyō; from their trunks, branches, and leaves to their flowers and kuwajitsu, their beauty is truly beyond what words can describe. Even in the jinjiya, dendau, and other jiyūtaku, when one enters the naibu, it is a spectacle of such saukuwan and birei that it is astonishing, as it corresponds to the virtue of aizen and the light of shinshin. The Tennin who organize the Tengokudantai governed by the Ōkami generally occupy their jiyūkiyo in high places. Their bashiyo resembles the summits of mountains that rise above the shizenkai of the earth. Furthermore, the Tennin who form the Reikokudantai of the Ōkami have established their jiyūkiyo in slightly lower places. It is much like a hilly region. However, the Tennin who reside in the lowest parts of Takaamahara have built their jiyūkiyo in places of magnificent scenery that resemble rocky terrain. All these things exist according to the principle of correspondence between love and faith.

The Tengoku of the Ōkami is entirely a land of thought, so that internal matters correspond to high places, and external matters all correspond to low places. Therefore, high places represent the aizen of Tengoku, low places represent the aizen of Reikoku, and rocks represent shinshin. This is because rocks possess a nature that is immutable through all ages, and thus correspond to shinshin. However, although the Reikokudantai are in lower places, they are still established upon hills that rise above the earth. The Hongū-yama in the sacred land of Aya is a fitting example of this. The reason why the Reikoku reside in places slightly lower than the Tengokudantai is that all Tennin of the Reikoku prioritize the virtue of faith and treat the virtue of love as secondary. Because it is a state of faith-first and love-second, the Tennin of this land cultivate wisdom and enlightenment, realize the truth of the universe, and subsequently embody the love of God; as many are dispatched to various groups as messengers of Tengoku, they occupy positions that are neither the highest nor the lowest, but almost in the middle. Therefore, even though the great ancestor of the world, Ōkunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, appears in the sacred land of Aya at an altitude of about two hundred feet, Konohanahime-no-Mikoto establishes her Tengoku-like center at Tenkyōzan, which is thirteen thousand feet above sea level; this is due to this very principle. However, Konohanahime-no-Mikoto receives the command of the Reikoku, and is sent to fulfill her sacred duty to spread the love of God not only to Tengoku, but also to the Chūyūkai, the Genjitsukai, and even the Jigokukai, and to serve in the role of harmonizing God and man; thus, even though she dwells in the Tengokudantai, she occasionally fills spirits with her presence through manifestations, or manifests directly to teach and guide all people.

Furthermore, among the Tennin, there are those who do not live in groups. That is, those who build their jiyūkiyo separately, house by house, such as the aforementioned Uzhiko-yakata, are examples of this. These Tennin, especially those who are at the center of their groups or are of great stature, build their jiyūkiyo by selecting the center of Takaamahara. The reason is that among the Tennin, they are the most excellent in wisdom and enlightenment derived from love and faith, their light shines brilliantly, and they are central figures in accordance with Kannagara; thus, it is due to the providence of the Ōkami, based on the depth of their virtue and the high degree of their correspondence. Furthermore, because Takaamahara is a world of thought, its extension represents the state of goodness, its breadth represents the state of truth, and its height represents the distinction of both goodness and truth viewed from the perspective of degree. Also, in the Reikai, as previously mentioned, there is no concept of time or space, only changes in emotion. And those thoughts transcend time and space, extending, expanding, and rising infinitely according to the degree of their correspondence.

大神の天国は、凡て想念の国土なるを以て、内辺の事は高き所に、外辺の事はすべて低い所に相応するものである。故に高い所を以て天国的の愛善を表明し、低い所を以て霊国的の愛善を現はし、岩石を以て信真を現はすのである。岩石なるものは万世不易の性質を有し、信真に相応するが故である。併しながら霊国の団体は低き所に在りとはいへ、矢張地上を抜く丘陵の上に設けられてある。丁度綾の聖地に於ける本宮山の如きはその好適例である。霊国は何故天国の団体よりも稍低き所に居住するかと云へば、凡て霊国の天人は信の徳を主とし、愛の徳を従として居る。所謂信主愛従の情態なるが故に、此国土の天人は智慧と証覚を研き、宇宙の真理を悟り、次で神の愛を能く其身に体し、天国の宣伝使として各団体に派遣さるるもの多きを以て、最高ならず最低ならず、殆ど中間の場所に其位置を占むる事になつてゐるのである。故に世界の大先祖たる大国常立尊は海抜二百フイート内外の綾の聖地に現はれ給ふにも拘はらず、木花咲耶姫命は海抜一万三千尺の天教山に其天国的中枢を定め給ふも、此理によるのである。併しながら木花姫命は霊国の命を受け、天国は云ふに及ばず、中有界、現実界及び地獄界まで神の愛を均霑せしむべき其聖職につかはせ給ひ、且神人和合の御役目に当らせ給ふを以て、仮令天国の団体にましますと雖も時々化相を以て精霊を充たし、或は直接化相して万民を教へ導き給ふのである。

又天人の中には団体的に生活を営まないのがある。即ち家々別々に住居を構へてゐるのは、丁度前に述べた珍彦館の如きは其例である。而して此等の天人は其団体の中心地点及大なるものに至つては高天原の中央を卜して住居を構へてゐる。何故なれば彼等は天人中に於ても、最も愛と信とによる智慧証覚の他に優れて、光明赫灼として輝き渡り、惟神的に中心人物たるが故に、大神の摂理によりて、其徳の厚きと相応の度の高きによるが故である。而して高天原は想念の世界なるが故に、其延長は善の情態を表はし、其広さは真の情態を表はし、其高さは善と真との両方面を度合の上より見て区別することを表はすものである。又霊界に於ては先に述べた通り、時間空間などの観念は少しもない、只情動の変化あるのみである。而して其想念は、時間空間を超越し、無限に其相応の度によつて延長し拡大し且高まるものである。

§ 2

Harukuniwake and Tatsukō, the two of them, fell into the trap of General Katahiko and his cohorts at the camp of Ukiki, and plummeted into a deep, dark abyss. There, they fell into a state of unconsciousness. The period during which they toured the Chiūkai, the lowest levels of Tengoku, and finally the highest Tengoku and Reigoku, seemed like a very long journey; however, in terms of Genkai time, they had been in a state of unconsciousness for less than two hours. Yet, due to the extension of their saunen, they experienced a long-duration tour that felt as though it had taken a Genkaijin more than a month. Furthermore, the more intense the changes in their emotions, the more extended the experience becomes in Tengoku.

Harukuniwake and Tatsukō were guided by Gosankō, the manifestation of Kototama-wake-no-Mikoto, and while being taught the details of the state of Tengoku, they climbed almost to the center of Reiyōzan. At this moment, Gosankō became a small ball of light, barely visible to the eye, and suddenly pointing to the east, drew a line of light in the air and hid his form, vanishing to parts unknown. The two stood on the summit of Reiyōzan, and while looking down at the scenery in all directions, they were lost in the reality of the moment, praising the magnificence of Tengoku. They did not notice at all that Gosankō had hidden his form in this place.

Harukuniwake: 'Moshi, Sensei, I have heard this place is called Reiyōzan, but it is truly a wonderful place. Is this not already the highest Tengoku? Whether one speaks of the trees on all sides or the appearance of the mountains, no painter's hand could ever hope to depict them. I wish that we could quickly finish our duties in Genkai and spend a joyful life in such a place.'

Tatsukō: 'It is indeed a magnificent place. Genkaijin make such a fuss about art, aesthetic living, and cultural living, but compared to this scene, in its quality, its quantity, and its beauty, it seems there is no comparison at all. And the indescribable refreshment of my spirit—truly, I never even dreamed that such a wonderful place could exist. Although I, this Harukuniwake, served for a long time in the mansion of the Saien, which could be called the first Tengoku, because my love and faith were not yet complete, I did not think the earthly Tengoku where the Great God of Mizu-no-Mitama dwells was so splendid. After all, no matter how solemn and beautiful, it is of no use when the eye of the heart is not open. It is just like casting pearls before swine. Thinking of this, we must polish and purify the spirit bestowed upon us by God until the very end, and harmonize and return to the divinity of the Great God. Ah, I thank the Great God before His presence with gratitude for guiding us to such a state of supreme joy and supreme happiness through the mouth of Gosankō-sama, and for allowing us to bathe in infinite joy. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haema Se.'

While saying this, he clapped his hands, finished reciting the Amatsu Norito, and sang the Ama-no-Kazauta. When he looked around, to his surprise, he could not discern even a trace of Gosankō's form as far as his eyes could reach. Harukuniwake was startled and said:

Harukuniwake: 'Yaa, Tatsukō-sama, where could Gosankō-sama have gone? We can no longer see that spiritual form which was shining like the moon until just now, can we?'

Tatsukō: 'Indeed, this is a serious matter. What shall we do?'

Harukuniwake: 'No matter what we say, there is nothing to be done; this must also be a trial from God. In my excessive longing for the scenery on all sides, I had removed Gosankō-sama's kind guidance from my mind. All of Tengoku is a land of correspondence and harmony. It is something that harmonizes and unites through love and faith. That is to say, it was through our saunen that we were able to be with the noble divine person. It is because of the shifting of our emotions that we have let Gosankō-sama slip from our hearts. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haema Se.'

He clasped his hands again.

Tatsukō: 'Indeed, that is how it was. I, too, was so happy that, although I was a person who should still be wandering in the Chiūkai, I was guided to such a noble Tengoku. I carelessly felt as if I had climbed up here on my own, and it was a mistake for me to think of Tengoku as if it were my own possession. Sensei, as it is written in the divine revelation of the Izuno-Mitama, Takamagahara is a wonderful yet terrifying place, is it not? As it is shown, if one is off guard, Tengoku instantly changes into Jigoku, light changes into darkness, and God transforms into a demon; truly, it is the way one holds one's heart that one must be cautious of.'

『アハヽヽヽ、往生致したか。ヨシ、然らば此処に此通り黄金を以て作りたる素盞嗚尊の像がある。治国別、竜公共に命が助かりたくば、此像に向つて小便をひつかけ、其上此岩石を以て木端御塵に打砕き、大自在天様に帰順の誠を表はせ。否むに於ては、其方が身体は木端微塵、地獄に突き墜し、無限の責苦を加へるが、どうだ』

治国別は初めて顔をあげ、大口あけて高笑ひ、

『アハヽヽヽ、拙者は厳の御霊、瑞の御霊の大神を信仰致す誠の宣伝使だ、仮令汝如き悪神に脅迫され、或は責め殺さるることありとも、吾心霊は万劫末代、大神に信従するのみだ。治国別はこれ以外に汝に答ふることはない、どうなりと勝手に致したがよからう』

『勝手に致せと申さいでも、此方が制敗を致してくれる。併しながら竜公、其方は憎くき奴なれど、一旦某が部下となつたよしみによつて制敗は許して遣はす。其代りに治国別をこの金剛杖を以て打ちのめせ、さうすれば汝の誠が分るであらう。何うぢや、治国別を打ちのめす勇気はないか。矢張其方は二心を持つてゐるのか。返答致せツ』

§ 4

He shouted, 'Dona!' At his voice, strangely, the surrounding mountains began to tremble with a rattling sound. Tatsukō remained for a shibashi with his hands clasped in shian, but suddenly he became itakedaka,

Tatsukō: 'Hey, you akugami and chiyōhon, Katahiko, there is no mistake that you were once my shiyujin. That you have left your shiyujin is because your kaudō has turned against Ten and departed from goodness. Even if I were to be subjected to itsusun-kizami or gobu-dameshi for your sake, how could I ever turn a single finger against the onjiyō-filled Harukuniwake-sama? What do you think this place is? It is the shinsei bashiyo of the Dainitengoku; do not think this is some place like Ōkumoyama, and do not speak lies. In heaven, there should be no falsehood, persecution, or evil; in short, you must be a demon of heaven.'

While saying this... he clapped his hands, saying, 'Itsu no Mitama, Mizu no Mitama, mamori tamae, sakihae tamae,' and began to chant the Kamugoto in a clear, resonant voice, without fear or hesitation. Harukuniwake also began to chant the Kamugoto with Tatsukō in a very composed tone. Katahiko, having gathered many subordinates before anyone knew it, brandished a metal rod, showing his intention to crush both men with a single blow. Harukuniwake and Tatsukō stood their ground, and with steady hearts, without panic or haste, finished chanting the Kamugoto. As soon as they recited, 'Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase,' the figures of Katahiko and all the others who had been standing there until a moment ago vanished like smoke, and a fragrant scent filled the air, while clear, resonant music could be heard incessantly.

Tatsukō: 'Haha! How impudent! You Yamata-no-Orochi, who guards the Brahman religion, to think you would dare to disguise yourself and come even to this heaven, only to be bewildered by the noble Kamugoto and vanish like a fart! How truly noble is the divine power of the Kami. Ah, thank you. Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase.'

Harukuniwake: 'Tatsukō-sama, this is the Dainitengoku, and furthermore, the summit of Reiyōzan. There is no reason for a Yamata-no-Orochi to come here; this must have been a test from the Kami. I, too, once thought it was an attack by a demon, but when I reconsidered, there is no reason for a demon to come to such a heaven. If he were truly a demon, we would have thought it was heaven, become arrogant, and fallen into hell... I thought this, but then the heaven within my heart opened, I bathed in the inner flow of the divine character of the Kami, realized that it was indeed the Dainitengoku, and I have no choice but to believe that what appeared to be Katahiko was the doing of a noble angel testing our hearts. You must never, even in a dream, think of such things as demons.'

Tatsukō: 'Your words are most correct. Now, sensei, what shall we do? Shall we descend from Reiyōzan and have the group in heaven train us once more?'

As they were saying this, there suddenly appeared a divine being with a beautiful appearance, looking about thirty years of age. The divine being approached close to Harukuniwake, grasped his hand firmly, and said,

Divine Being: 'Harukuniwake-sama, there are countless and boundless groups in the Dainitengoku, but you have passed the examination of the Dainitengoku. Tatsukō-sama as well, it seems you have attained a great deal of shōgaku. From here, I shall guide you to the innermost first heaven and the spirit realm. However, compared to this Dainitengoku, the light of the highest heaven is equivalent to nearly ten thousand times as much. And the various heavenly beings who dwell in that heaven are filled with wisdom and shōgaku that come from goodness and truth, and one cannot easily face them. When you meet the heavenly beings of that group, your eyes will instantly be dazzled, your words will falter, your head will ache, your chest will be constricted, and your limbs and body will wither, causing extreme pain; but since you both have passed the examination of heaven, please perform your pilgrimage training of the innermost heaven using this veil. I shall guide you.'

He took the lead and climbed, parting the clouds. The two of them, while taking turns chanting the Kamugoto and the Amatsu-Norito with all their might, crossed the bridge of clouds and climbed upward. This divine being was Kototama-wake-no-Mikoto. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase.

原文（近代文語）

と呶鳴りつけた。其声に不思議にも、あたりの山岳はガタガタガタと震動し始めた。竜公は少時双手を組み思案にくれてゐたが、忽ち威丈高になり、

『コリヤ、悪神の張本片彦奴、汝は拙者の一時主人に間違ひはない。其主人に離れたるのは汝が行動天に背き、善に離れたるが故だ。仮令拙者が汝の為に一寸刻みか五分だめしに遇はされようとも、恩情深き治国別様の御身に、何うして一指をそむることが出来ようか。ここを何と心得て居る、第二天国の神聖な場所だ、大雲山などとは思ひもよらぬ、詐りを申すな。天国には虚偽と迫害と悪はない筈、其方は要するに天国の魔であらう』

と云ひながら………「厳の御霊、瑞の御霊、守り給へ幸はへ給へ」と拍手し、音吐朗々と怖めず臆せず神言を奏上し始めた。治国別も竜公と共に神言をいと落着いた調子で奏上し始めた。片彦は何時の間にやら数多の部下を集め、金棒をふり上げ、只一打に両人を粉砕せんず勢を示してゐる。治国別、竜公両人は胆力を据ゑ、声調ゆるやかに騒がず焦らず、神言を奏上し終り、「惟神霊幸倍坐世」と唱ふるや否や、今迄ここに立つてゐた片彦他一同の姿は煙の如く消え失せ、四辺に芳香薫じ、嚠喨たる音楽さへ頻りに聞え来るのであつた。

『アハヽヽヽ、猪口才千万な、バラモン教を守護致す八岐大蛇奴、畏くもかかる天国迄化けて来やがつて、尊き神言に面喰ひ、屁のやうに消え散るとは、さてもさても神様の御神力は尊いものだ。アヽ有難う厶います。惟神霊幸倍坐世』

『竜公さま、ここは第二天国、しかも霊陽山の頂だ。八岐大蛇の来るべき道理がない、大方これは神様の御試しだつたらう。私も一度は悪魔の襲来かと考へてみたが、よくよく思ひ直せば、かかる天国に悪魔の来るべき理由がない。若しも彼果して悪魔なりとせば、吾等は天国と思ひ、慢心して地獄に墜ちてゐたのであらう……と考へてみたが、忽ち心中の天海開けて神様の御神格の内流に浴し、矢張第二天国なることを悟り、且片彦と見えしは尊きエンゼルの、吾等が心を試させ給ふ御所為と信ずるより外に途はない、必ず必ず悪魔などと、夢にも思つてはなりませぬぞや』

『仰せ御尤もで厶います。サア先生、どうでせう、これから霊陽山を下つて、又天国の団体を修業さして頂きませうか』

かくいふ所へ、忽然として現はれ給うたのは、三十恰好の美はしき容貌をもてる一柱の神人であつた。神人は治国別の側近く寄り、其手を固く握り、

『治国別さま、第二天国の団体は無数無辺にありますが、貴方は第二天国の試験に合格致しました。又竜公さまも其通り、余程証覚を得られたやうです。之から拙者が最奥第一の天国及び霊国を御案内致しませう。併しながら此第二天国に比ぶれば、最高天国の光明は殆ど万倍に匹敵するものです。而して其天国に住む諸天人は、善と真とより来る智慧証覚に充ち、容易に面を向くることが出来ませぬ。其団体の天人に会ふ時は忽ち眼くらみ、言句渋り、頭は痛み、胸は塞がり、四肢五体萎縮して非常な苦痛で厶いますが、貴方等両人は天国の試験に合格されましたから、其被面布を以て最奥天国の巡覧的修業をなさいませ。拙者が案内を致しませう』

と先に立ち、雲を踏み分けてのぼり行く。二人は一生懸命に神言や天津祝詞を交る交る奏上しながら、フワリフワリと雲の橋を渡つてのぼり行く。此神人は言霊別命であつた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
