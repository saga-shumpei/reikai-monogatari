Chapter 17: 甦生 [1271]

甦生〔1271〕

§ 1

Shiyōgun Ranchi, Katahiko, Gariya, and Kēsu, these four, stood by the side of Zaininbashi, and while exposed to a Kanpū that seemed to cut through their very skin, they waited while trembling, taking what little strength they could from the faintly audible voice of the Sendenka.

Looking toward the Shihau, the Meikuwan that had until now surrounded their Shinpen were nowhere to be seen, and the figures of the Garigarimaujiya had all vanished without a trace; yet, the Koana through which they had crawled remained blocked and unrecognizable, and whether they should cross this bridge to the other side was a sight so Kiken that it seemed they would fall into a Tenraku one hundred times out of a hundred.

The voice of the Sendenka grew gradually louder.

Following that, the voices of Sūhiyakunin shouting 'wai-wai' echoed from the Zengosaiu.

The four, their hearts not their own, were sunk in an abyss of Zetsubau, wondering how things would turn out.

To such a place, there appeared Kotsuzen a Megami of Tanrei Yōbau, and while accompanied by two Jijiyo, she descended before the four like a dove; the Megami, in a gentle voice, said:

Megami: 'Anata, are you not the party of Shiyōgun Ranchi who follows the Oshihe of Daijizaiten-sama?'

The four, feeling as if they were being revived, suddenly became cheerful and even their voices were full of vigor as they replied:

Four: 'Hai, as you say, we are the Shiyōgun Ranchi and his followers. Truly, because of our sins, we have been cast into such a place and are at our wit's end. We have repented of our sins up to this day and have returned to the heart of a newborn babe, so please, save us from this crisis.'

Megami: 'That must be very distressing. If you swear to reform your conduct of body-dominant and spirit-subordinate, I shall assist you. I am one who serves by the side of the Great God of the Sun who resides in the Tushita Heaven; if you understand what I say, I shall surely save you. In truth, the Great God observed your peril and commanded, "Go before them as quickly as possible, explain the truth, and save them; if the time is lost, it will be a great disaster." Taking nothing but what I had, I descended from the Tushita Heaven and came here. Listen to that. That Sendenka is the voice of the song for your salvation.'

Katahiko: 'Hai, thank you very much. I can hear the song, but the song is vague and I cannot understand its meaning at all.'

Megami: 'That song is a prayer song to God by the Senden-shi of the San-Go-Kyo to save you; now, listen carefully.'

With these words, she took an Ōnusa from her bosom and waved it left and right, and wondrously, the ears of the four opened wide, and the meaning of the song became increasingly clear. The four joined their hands, knelt upon the earth, and listened intently so as not to miss a single word of the song. The song:

In the deepest reaches of Takamagahara, Appearing in the Sun's Wakamiya, The Great God of supreme benevolence and supreme love, Possessed by the Yamata-no-Orochi, the ugly fox, And the crooked demons, Mistook the path of virtue, Clouded the wisdom and enlightenment, And to the petty desires of body-dominant and spirit-subordinate, Became a Brahmin who squandered his floating life, Becoming a subordinate of Daikokunushi, And against the Great God of Susanoo, Who is the incarnation of the God of Miroku, And who appears in the eternal and unchanging, Spiritual land of the Ubusunayama, To slaughter the noble mansion, Receiving the command of Daikokunushi, Shiyōgun Ranchi and Katahiko, Leading many subordinates, Established a camp in the forest of floating wood, And in the midst of planning their strategy,

The Senden-shi of Chikokuwake entered, Suddenly an evil heart erupted, And the noble messenger of God, To the bottom of a dark hole a thousand fathoms deep, Was cast down, and the crooked deed, Immediately brought retribution upon his own body, His eyes were blinded, and the transformation god, Passing as a beauty beyond compare, While mutually kindling the Shura, Devised a strategy of counter-espionage and self-sacrifice, And mutually took each other's lives, Suddenly the spirit and the body, Parted and wandered into hell, Unable to advance or retreat, Seeing that plight, And as if commanding me, Thou, the God of Murasakihime, Together with the two heavenly maidens, Descend to the land of the roots,

After examining the true heart of those four, If there be even a little that shines with sincerity, Preach the path of truth, And drive them back to the Saha world, And let them raise the fruit of, Improving goodness and correcting faults, So did the Great God command, And with reverence and awe, I, Have descended here now, O Lord of the army, thou now, Distinguish my Kototama, Touch the love of the noble God, And return again to the present world, If thou hast a sincere heart to serve the Great Divine Work, I shall now save thee to safety, Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, By the noble imperial command of God, I convey this command to you four.

The Kotoba came sounding with grace. Looking closely, the voice of the Sendenka was not from elsewhere, but was being sung from the mouth of the Megami. However, the Tennin, filled with Shinkaku, did not use their mouths like Gendaijin; they used a kind of Goji, emitting words from all Shihen and stating their intent, so it is only Dauri that the Yonin of Maujiya did not notice.

General Ranchi finally gained strength and answered the angel with a song.

Ranchi: 'In the deepest Saiō of Takaamahara, appearing in the Wakamiya of the sun, by the imperial command of the noble Kami, I humbly and respectfully pray before the Onmahe of the purple Hime no Mikoto who has descended from heaven. I am the Brahman Ōmikami, who, having purified my body morning and evening at the Seijiyō of Taiunzan where Ōkunihiko is enshrined, and having attained the fruits of Nangiyō and Kugiyō, finally realized the Okuga of the path. Chosen here by the Kamubashira Ōkuronushi, I have been serving as a teacher. At this very time, the Kamubashira of the Ural religion and the Izunome religion, leading a multitude of troops, is attacking the capital of Haruna in the land of the moon that shines across the sky.

The rumors were heard clearly. Here, the Kami Ōkuronushi, becoming greatly enraged, and though I know not if it be good or evil, intending to raise an army and advance toward the mansion of the Ubusuna, ordered Kiharuwake to take charge and entrusted him with many soldiers. We, who were subordinates of Kiharuwake, followed his orders, and when we came to the forest of Ukiki, we defiled our hearts with a strange woman, and resenting our colleagues as rivals in love, we committed acts of wickedness and lawlessness; how regrettable it is. Now that it has come to this, how could I continue to exhaust myself in evil? From this moment on, I repent of my evil and return to the path of sincerity.

I shall serve and obey the teachings of the Sumerōmikami with devotion. O noble messenger of the Kami, pity this state of affairs and please, by all means, save us. If I am forgiven and can return once more to the Genkai, I shall dedicate my entire body to compensate for the sin and guilt of having turned my blade against the Kami Susanoo no Ōkami, and I shall advance upon the path of sincerity; this is my heart's resolve. Before the Onmahe of the noble messenger of the Kami, I harden my heart and pray in detail. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, the soul I have once pledged is for the sake of the Sumerōmikami.

Even if I must cast away my life, I shall not cast away my sincerity, not even one step, nor shall I look back; as you clearly perceive this, please have pity, before the Onmahe of the purple Hime no Mikoto, I humbly and respectfully pray.'

Thus he chanted in a thin voice. Katahiko also apologized for his sins with a song.

Katahiko: 'Cleanse and save my spirit that has become mired in so many sins and defilements. I have lost my way on the path of Kannagara sincerity and fallen into the land of Ne no Kuni. Without knowing it, everything became crooked, not knowing it was for the sake of the Kami and the world. I only beg that you forgive these many sins and save me to the Gense.'

Gariya: 'I, too, was wrapped in filthy desires and fell into the darkness where I could not distinguish black from white. Wrapped in the very deep grace of the Kami, I am happy to leave the land of Ne no Kuni. I am anxious about how I can ever repay this grace of the Sumerōkami. Yet, since my spirit was originally that of a noble Ōkami, I shall return purified.'

Keesu: 'My heart was clouded by the desires of the body, and I, who wandered by the riverbank of Ne no Kuni, am pathetic. I have pained my heart a thousand times over, wondering how to escape this suffering. Blessed by the grace of the Kami, the purple Hime has descended.'

Ranchi: 'There are no other Kotoba to say than that it is grateful and unworthy. The dew of the Ōkami's grace has moistened even the land of Ne no Kuni and the land of Soko no Kuni.'

Murasakihime: 'The body whose darkness of heart has cleared shall return peacefully to the Arawashimikuni. However, once you return to the Gense, do not think of the deeds of the crooked as if they were a dream.

Everyone, it was excellent. It seems the path for you to return to the Gense has opened. I, too, am overjoyed. However, the guardian Kami of this land is Kanayamahiko no Ōkami-sama, and I must obtain his permission once. I shall go and make the request.'

Before the words were even finished, a beautiful cloud arose, and the Shinshi vanished toward the north, over the Zaininbashi. The Yonin looked at one another, and while heaving a sigh of relief,

Ranchi: 'Ah, Katahiko-dono, I have truly done a terrible thing. To be possessed by an Akuma, to suddenly harbor such an Akushin, and to be locked away in a place like this—it is truly a shameful matter. I pray that even when we return to the Genkai, you will let the past grudges flow down the river, and that we may foster an even deeper Goshinkau.'

He apologized to Katahiko from the bottom of his heart. Katahiko replied to this, speaking with great joy,

Katahiko: 'Shiyōgunsama, please do not say such Motsutai things. It is all my fault. While in a position to command Amata Gunzei, I disturbed the Kiritsu of the Jinchiu, allowed my heart to be stolen by a woman, and ultimately caused an unexpected Katsutō; the guilt for that is something I should bear for the Daibubun. I humbly ask that you forgive my sins until now, and I pray for an even deeper Goshinkau than before.'

He spoke with his heart fully opened. Gariya and Kēsu, the Riyōnin, listened to the Monogatari of the Riyōshiyōgun, shrinking back and letting out sighs of Kantan. Just then, the party of Harukuniwake, Matsuhiko, Ryūkō, Bankō, Aku, Taku, and Teku came flying through the air and lined up before the Yonin.

Harukuniwake: 'Katahiko-sama, your repentance has reached the ears of the Lord God, Kanakatsuyō-Ōkami, the spirit of the land. By the command of the Lord God Kanakatsuyō, I have come to welcome you in order to save you for the Genkai.'

Katahiko: 'Yes, thank you very much.'

He was moved to tears. Matsuhiko turned toward Shiyōgun Ranchi,

Matsuhiko: 'Shiyōgun-dono, your prayer of repentance has reached the presence of the Lord God of the Earth, Kanakatsuyō-Ōkami. By divine command, I shall send you back to the Genkai; next, Gariya and Kēsu, the Riyōnin, shall likewise return to the Genkai.'

The three of them bowed their heads, saying, 'Yes, thank you.' The moment they did, they were awakened by the sound of voices, and found the Yonin lying in the lower room of the watchtower in the Ukiki-no-mori, being cared for by Amata people. Meanwhile, Otami had been saved by Otora. When Shiyōgun Ranchi and Katahiko looked closely at their bedside, to their surprise, they saw Harukuniwake and Matsuhiko, who had saved them at the foot of the Zaininbashi in what they thought was a dream, as well as Ryūkō, Bankō, Aku, Taku, and Teku. The four of them had been rescued from the river by the party of Harukuniwake and Matsuhiko, had been made to listen to the Sendenka, and had been revived by the power of the Amatsu-Norito and the Ama-no-Kazauta. Also, when Otami was startled by the voice of Imoriwake and looked around, she saw Imoriwake, Ekisu, Āku, Tāru, and Otora-bā-sama-no-menmen kindly caring for her at her bedside. From this time on, Shiyōgun Ranchi and the others who had traveled through the Yūmei repented their past wrongs from the bottom of their hearts, joined their hands for the first time before the Lord Susanoo-Ōkami, apologized for their sins of rebellion, and finally submitted to the path of Sango. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haema se.

と頭を下げる途端、ザワザワと聞ゆる人声に目を醒ませば、浮木の森の物見櫓の下座敷に四人は横たはり、数多の人々に介抱されて居た。さうしてお民はお寅に救はれて居た。ランチ将軍、片彦は枕許をよく見れば、豈図らむや、今夢ともなしに罪人橋の麓にて救はれたる治国別、松彦を初め、竜公、万公、アク、タク、テクの面々であつた。彼等四人は治国別、松彦の一隊に死体を河中より救ひ上げられ、宣伝歌を聞かされ、且つ天津祝詞と天の数歌の功力に救はれ蘇へつたのであつた。又お民は蠑螈別の声にハツと気がつき四辺を見れば、其枕許には蠑螈別、エキス、アーク、タール、お寅婆アさまの面々が親切に介抱をして居た。是よりランチ将軍を初め幽冥旅行の面々は心の底より前非を悔い、初めて神素盞嗚大神の御前に両手を合せ、反逆の罪を陳謝し、遂に三五の道に帰順する事となつた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
