Otami: 'Yes, I am honored. However, a soldier is something that is not needed in times of peace, but in times of chaos like this, we cannot do without them. In that respect, soldiers are the pillars of the nation and the guardian deities of peace. Nevertheless, if the world is pacified by your divine power and military might, and a peace of truth, goodness, and beauty is established, you will surely quit being a soldier; nay, there will be no need for it, will there?'

While saying this, he looked at the sake poured from the mirror, and while clutching the barrel with both hands, he began to drink it down greedily. Suddenly, Imoriwake collapsed on the spot in a drunken stupor, and here the Zakone of the four Yohidore began. Omin Ichihaya departed from this place and proceeded toward the inner room.

The snow-whipping brought by the cold wind poured down without mercy upon the four who had fallen to the Chijiyō, and Shidaishidai the snow became finer, making a sound like scattering sand, until it seemed as if Yukibusuma had been erected on all Shihen. In an instant, the Karada of the four were completely covered in Sekisetsu.
