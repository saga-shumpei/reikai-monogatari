Chapter 21: Great 会合 [1830]

大会合〔1830〕

§ 1

In the back room of a medicinal herb wholesaler, known as the Hiyakumanchiyōjiya of the port of Suga, the young master Iruku, his aged father Arisu, Hime Dariya, Hime Yoriko, Hime Hanaka, the gatekeepers Aru and Esu, and even Tamakiyowake, who had been the Kamuzukasa of the Suga Shrine, are gathered with their heads together, immersed in a secret discussion.

Yoriko: 'Arisu-sama, Iruku-sama, and everyone else, I must offer my apologies. You went to the trouble of spending a large sum of money to build a magnificent shrine for me, and I was even entrusted as the master of the dojo, enjoying an honor far beyond my station. However, it was due to my own arrogance that I hung a sign calling it a religious question-and-answer center, which became the cause of this disaster. I have caused extraordinary damage to everyone, including Tamakiyowake-sama, Dariya-hime-sama, and the entire family, and for that sin, I deserve ten thousand deaths. When I think about it deeply, I am a sinful person who hid in Mount Ōra and even became a boss of thieves, committing evil deeds and wicked acts; how could I possibly serve in the residence of the Gods? It has come to the point where even this hard-won sacred ground must be handed over to Hime Takahime of the Uranai sect. This is all due to my own inadequate sins, so I beg you to punish me as much as you desire.'

Arisu: 'Yoriko-hime-sama, please do not trouble yourself with such thoughts. Even if we spent thirty or fifty thousand in money to build a shrine, it does not mean that the property of my house will collapse. Once it has been handed over to the Uranai sect, there is nothing to be done; why don't we build an even more magnificent divine shrine, many times greater than the previous one, and show them by challenging the Uranai sect? Now, my son, what do you think? They say that in old age one should follow one's children, so I leave it to your opinion.'

Iruku: 'Since the property of this house was originally built upon a foundation of sin, it is an inevitable result that no matter how magnificent a shrine we build, it will not be of use to the Gods. I certainly do not think it is a waste. If I have the permission of the Gods, I would like to throw out all of our family's property to build a magnificent shrine.'

Ari: 'Indeed, as my son says, I leave everything to you. Everyone, please consult thoroughly with my son. I am of old age, so I will excuse myself and go to rest in the detached building.'

So saying, he drags his post-illness body while leaning on a floor cane and heads toward the detached building.

Tamakiyo: 'I, too, have endured hardships and difficulties in Kamiya Village for many years, and as a result of deep consideration and long-suffering, I wished to revive the path of San-Go. Through the initiative of this house, a magnificent shrine was built, and I became the Kamuzukasa of the Great God, serving with great joy and courage as my ancestors also accumulated merit. But now that it has come to this, there is nothing I can do. I shall return to Kamiya Village from here, serve as a village official as before, and bring peace to the villagers.'

Dariya: 'Wait, Tamakiyowake-sama, are you not being too impatient? Please, wait for a while until something is decided. Before long, the missionaries of the San-Go sect will surely arrive, so I would like you to consult with them thoroughly before making your decision.'

Tamakiyo: 'A defeated general does not speak of war. In that case, I shall take advantage of your words for a little while and stay.'

Aru: 'Wait, young master, please do not worry at all. That woman called Takahime will surely show her true colors soon, so please wait a little while. Esu and I are doing our best to investigate her background. Within the next ten days, I will surely show you her being kicked out along with her husband. That fellow Kyūbā is also a suspicious character. Why don't you leave things to take their course for a little while?'

Iruku: 'Indeed, I also think she is a suspicious person. Surely the Gods will judge between good and evil. The Great God Kuninotokotachi-no-Mikoto and the God Susanoo-no-Mikoto are not such sluggish Gods that they would leave a sacred place to such a wicked person and look on in idle peace.'

As they were talking, the five members of the Ananai-kyō, the Sendenshi Terukuniwake, Terukō, Genshinbau, Kōro, and Koburai, came bustling in.

Terukuniwake: 'Excuse me, I am the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō, Terukuniwake. I hear that Umekō once received great kindness from you, but has he not yet returned to your house?'

Iru: 'Oh, were you the renowned Sendenshi Terukuniwake-sama of whom I have heard? I am Iruku, the master of this house. You have come at a good time. Please, do come inside.'

Terukō: 'I am Terukō, a disciple of Terukuniwake-sama. I look forward to your favor.'

Genshin: 'I am a Shiugenjiya named Genshinbau. I humbly ask that you keep me in mind and favor me with your acquaintance from this day forward.'

Kōro: 'I am Kōro, a third-rate fellow who is neither particularly good nor bad. By some strange fate, I was saved by Terukuniwake-sama and his party when I was shipwrecked, and I have accompanied them since. Please, use me as a gatekeeper or in any capacity you see fit.'

Kobu: 'I am Koburai, and I make my living as a wandering knight-errant, though whether I am truly famous or not is another matter. I humbly ask for your favor.'

Iru: 'Oh, everyone, you are most welcome. In any case, please come inside with Sendenshi Terukuniwake-sama. A matter of concern has arisen in our house, and we are in the midst of discussing it.'

Terukō: 'I do not know what your concern may be, but fortunately, our teacher is here, and we shall be happy to assist you, however humble our efforts may be.'

Iru: 'Yes, thank you very much. I appreciate your kindness.'

He greeted them, served tea and sweets himself, and entertained them with all his might.

Terukuniwake: 'Your house appears to be a very old and distinguished one. The mossy charm of the garden stones and the stone lanterns are truly magnificent. It is a splendid room to soothe the fatigue of a journey; I am sorry to soil such a fine room.'

Iru: 'What are you saying? To have you grace our humble, dilapidated house is the greatest honor for our entire family. Our descendants will surely speak of this with joy for generations to come.'

Tamakiyo: 'I am Tamakiyo-wake, a believer of the Ananai-kyō from Kamiyamura. Terukuniwake-sama, I humbly ask that you keep me in mind, and I look forward to your guidance in the future.'

Terukuniwake: 'Oh, are you Tamakiyo-wake-sama, of whom I have heard? It is a rare pleasure to meet you here. I look forward to our acquaintance. By the way, Iruku-sama, I heard just now that your house is in the midst of a consultation regarding some trouble. If our presence is an intrusion, we shall withdraw.'

Iru: 'No, please do not worry at all. In truth, it concerns the matter of the Suga no Miya...'

He told the whole story in detail.

Terukuniwake said, 'Hmm,' and remained with his hands folded, lost in thought for a while. Then, as if he had resolved something, he slapped his knee, nodded three or four times, and said,

Terukuniwake: 'I understand. Please leave it to me. I shall certainly find a solution to this.'

Iru: 'I humbly place my trust in you.'

Yori: 'Sendenshi Terukuniwake-sama, I am one who has received great kindness from your disciple, Umekō-wake-sama. This is my younger sister, Hanaka. By a strange turn of fate, it has been unofficially decided that she will become the wife of Umekō-wake-sama. I humbly ask for your kind mediation.'

Terukuniwake: 'Ah, I have already heard from Umekō-wake-sama about Yori-ko-hime-sama and Hanaka-hime-sama. Everything depends on the will of the Gods, after all.'

Hanakahime: 'Ano, Dannasama, no, Umekōwake-sendenshi-sama, it has been a long time. I do not know how long I have been waiting for you.'

梅公『仇話は扨おいて海上からほのかにスガ山の聖地を見れば怪しい雲が立つてゐました。何か変つた事はありませぬかな』

イル『ハイ、お察しの通り、その事に就きまして今、相談会を開いて居つた処で御座います』

梅公『高姫、杢助、キユーバー等の悪人が聖地を占領せむとしてゐるのでせう』

イル『ハイ、已に占領されて了つたのです。最早今となつては、手の出しやうも御座いませぬので……』

梅公『決して心配なさるな、梅公別がキツト解決をつけませう、悪人輩を一言のもとに叩き出し、もとの聖地に回復せむは吾方寸に御座います。ヨリコさまもダリヤさまも、玉清別さまも安心して下さい、キツトもとの通りにしてお目にかけませう。大方、高姫と云ふ奴、ヨリコさまの素性を洗ひ、理窟づくめに放り出したのでせう』

ヨ『ハイ、お察しの通で御座います。何分汚れた魂で御座いますから聖場を守る等云ふ大それた事は出来ませぬ、妾の慢心から柄にも合はぬ宮仕を致しまして聖場を汚し、皆さまに対し申訳がないので、この通り悄れ返つてゐるので御座います』

梅公『昔は昔、今は今、仮令如何なる悪事があつても、悔い改めた以上は白無垢同然、一点の罪も汚れもあらう筈がありませぬ、左様な御心配は御無用になさいませ』

と慰めおき、照国別、イルク、玉清別を別室に招き、高姫追放の計画に密議をこらし悠然として再びもとの座に帰り来り、

梅公『大空に塞がる黒雲吹き払ひ 月日を照す科戸辺の神。 よしやよし醜の黒雲包むとも 科戸辺の神吹きや払はむ』

これより一同はスガ山回復の策戦計画の準備に各々手分けをして取かかり、明日を期して大挙スガ山に神軍を進むる事とした。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
