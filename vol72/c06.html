Chapter 6: Night 鷹姫 [1815]

夜鷹姫〔1815〕

§ 1

Just as the two, Eugenbau and Takahime, had appropriated a kobune tied to Tama Island and advanced about shigochiyō upon the lake, they encountered a single kobune speeding directly toward them like an arrow. Takahime, catching sight of the boat with sharp eyes, felt her heart throb with a start, while her complexion turned crimson. Observing her condition, Eugenbau conceived a slight suspicion:

Eugenbau: 'Newly recognized Takahime-sama of Chigusa, or rather, Niyoteisama—at your formidable dexterity, I, Mokusuke, am struck with kiyōgaku, nay, with kangeki. Kimeuchiyōrai, gonjiyōsaihai, gonjiyōsaihai.'

Takahime: 'Oh dear, Mokusukesama, you say such strange things. At what have you become so deeply moved? To suddenly become so formal as to say gonjiyōsaihai—please stop teasing me so carelessly.'

Eugenbau: ''Though I hid it, it has appeared in my face—my love, to the point that people ask whether I am lost in thought.' You surely know this poem from the Hiyakuninitsushiyu, do you not?'

Takahime: 'Hmph, do not make a baka of me. I know a poem of that degree quite well. What on earth are you trying to say by that? Are you not saying something exceedingly strange?'

Eugenbau: 'Just now, upon seeing the young man in that single boat approaching over there, did you not dye your face crimson like autumn leaves? He must without doubt be your lover whom you cannot forget whether awake or asleep. That is precisely why I said you possess a formidable dexterity.'

Takahime: 'I wondered what you were talking about, and here you are being jealous again! Is it not rather senseless to be jealous upon the water? Come now, stop saying such senseless things and work the oar.'

Eugenbau: 'Rather than working the oar, the truth is I wish to share in that man's good fortune with women. He is, after all, the sole handsome man under heaven loved by Takahime-sama of Chigusa, the Queen-consort of Tolman Castle. How much more enjoyable in this world it must be to cohabit with a young man having skin like silk crepe, than to live together with a hairy man like me whose identity as a tiger or a lion is unclear! Ah, one ought not to be born an ugly man.'

Takahime: 'What are you saying? Please stop tormenting me so needlessly.'

Eugenbau: 'Do you truly mean to say that you do not know that man?'

Takahime: 'There is no other path for me than to say that what I absolutely do not know, I do not know.'

Eugenbau: 'As the living shrine of Hinode-no-Kami and the divine soul of the Great Miroku-sama of the bottommost bedrock, you surely would not tell a lie, would you?'

Takahime: 'That goes without saying.'

Eugenbau: 'Then let us make a promise here between us. To see whether you know him or not, let us give chase to that boat and have you meet that young man. If he looks at your face from his side and says something to you, I will by no means allow you to say you do not know him. It is the rule of this country that unrelated men and women do not exchange words. Moreover, it is the rule that if they have had a relationship even once, they must speak words to each other even in secret. How about it, Takahime? If you say you do not know him, shall we investigate?'

Takahime: 'Oh, what a jealous and persistent person you are! Let us wash such matters down the water and proceed to the port of Suga even one moment sooner.'

Eugenbau: 'The more you speak like that, the more my suspicion merely increases. If it should turn out that you had a relationship with him, what will you do for me? Come, let us decide that beforehand.'

Takahime: 'Since I cannot bear to be suspected like this, please investigate as you please. If you do so, your suspicion will surely be cleared. For my body is as clear as the bright sun in a clear sky.'

Eugenbau: 'All right, out it comes! Come, now is the time when the disguise shall be stripped away. Takahime-sama, hold yourself steady.'

Takahime: 'Say whatever you wish. In return, if it is understood that there was no relationship between that man and me, what will you do for me?'

Eugenbau: 'Ha-ha-ha! There is nothing to do this way or that. If it is understood, it will be splendid for you as your suspicion is cleared, and I too shall be overjoyed as my jealousy is removed. On the off chance that what I say is wrong, I pledge absolute obedience to whatever you say in the future. However, if I win, you will listen to whatever unreasonable demands I make of you, will you not?'

Takahime: 'It is a matter of course, due to the Kenkuwa of Amoya.'

Saying, 'Well, this is Omoshiro,' Eugenbau turns the Senshiyu and chases after a boat. The boat pulls alongside the place where the boat he had stolen was tied up. Eugenbau, calling out like Kumagahi, reaches the Kishibe quickly.

While gazing intently at the figures of the two, Umekōwake says:

Umekōwake: 'Oh, if it is not Chigusa no Takahime-sama, I have been away from you for a long time. I slept soundly in your Ima and caused you great Shitsurei, but I am happy to see you are doing so well, and it seems you have taken a Ritsupa husband. I am not exactly a Tanin to you, either. However, women are creatures whose hearts change easily. Please do not let your heart change as it did in my time, and cherish your new husband. Do not be Goanshin, for even if I say this, I am not here to force a Saien upon you. And so, for what purpose have you both come to this island together?'

Takahime: 'Well, well, I do not know who you are, but there is a limit to mistaking one person for another. Indeed, I am certainly Chigusa no Takahime, but in this wide world, there must be other women with the same face and the same name. It is very troublesome for me, a woman who has a husband, to be told such things.'

Umekōwake: 'Takahime-sama, do not play the fool. I do not have such failing eyesight as to mistake a person, and since I have been searching for you, seeing your figure even in my dreams without a moment of interruption day or night, how could there be any risk of me being mistaken?'

Eugenbau, his face flushing red and his whole body trembling, glares intensely at Takahime and Umekōwake.

Eugenbau: 'Hey, you greenhorn, by whose permission did you do such things with my precious wife? Come, let me hear your reasoning, and depending on your reply, I shall show no mercy. Hey, you Empress, no, you harlot, night-hawk, streetwalker, common woman, ten-sen whore, low-class maid, mat-laying wench, you have made a fool of a man of eight shaku until now, have you? Come, let us settle this judgment properly.'

Takahime: 'Now, Mokusuke-sama, are words like streetwalker, ten-sen, and mat-laying wench not too heartless? I truly know nothing of this. This person must have lost his mind upon seeing my beauty; otherwise, there is no reason for him to say such things to me, a stranger.'

Eugenbau: 'Well, there is no fear of this greenhorn escaping if I leave him on this island, but in return, I shall take charge of this borrowed boat.'

Saying this, he ties it firmly to the stern of his own boat and rows about two or three chō out to sea.

Eugenbau: 'Now, night-hawk-sama, since it has come to this, it is my turn. Will you tell me the truth?'

Takahime, finding herself in a predicament where she can no longer hide the truth, makes a confession, mixing truth and falsehood in a faltering manner.

Takahime: 'The savior of the mansion of the former Saien, one of the three crows of Lord Susanoo-no-Mikoto, the celestial being of the First Spirit Kingdom, the peerless hero and great man of the world who has mastered the art of Kuruwa, the husband of mine who has no flaws whether seen from the front, the back, the top, or the bottom—even the great Chigusa no Takahime cannot help but click her tongue in admiration at his keen insight.'

Eugenbau: 'What is this, listing my long name? Do you think I will fall for that just because you try to flatter me? Even good words and beautiful phrases depend on the time and place. You loose woman, you harlot, I do not want to hear such flattery. There is no mistake that he is your lover, is there? If you are a woman, act like one and confess plainly.'

Takahime: 'Oh, well, if it has come to this, I am past caring. Come, do whatever you like with me. If I am abandoned by you, I no longer have any reason to live in this world, so I have made up my mind. Come, kill me quickly.'

With such Kusodokiyō, she leaned against him.

Eugenbau: 'If you wish to be killed that much, I am prepared to do so without Enriyo, but if I kill you, I am the one who will be troubled immediately. I must use your Bibau to perform a Hitoshibai, after all.'

Takahime: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, that may be so, but my dear Anata, how could you lay a blade upon this Kahai Niyōbau of yours? That is the beautiful part of Ninjiyō; I have come to like your Mia spirit even more.'

Eugenbau: 'Do not make a fool of me, you wretched Ama. More than that, will you fulfill your Yakusoku and listen to everything I say?'

Takahime: 'Yes, I shall listen to anything. Even if you, Omae-sama, tell me to die, I will not say I dislike it. (In a low voice) Though that is not the case, well, I will listen to anything, so please tell me.'

Eugenbau: 'Then, to show your Seii to me, lure that man skillfully and throw him into the Forest of Demons. If you do that, Aitsu will have his Inochi taken by ants and spiders, and I will have no Shinpai about you wagging your tail at him, and I will be able to sleep peacefully at night. How is it, do you have Tokushin? Your silence means you refuse, does it?'

Takahime: 'No, no, I would never, ever say I refuse. If it is for your sake, I shall stake my Inochi and Ketsukau to show you, so please, hurry and moor the boat.'

Eugenbau said 'Tenami Haiken' and returned to the spot where Umekowake had landed. Seeing that the situation between the two was unusual, Umekowake was worried that if a great quarrel were to break out on the lake, he would immediately jump into the lake to save them from their crisis, and thus he had been watching the situation intently. Eugenbau, who was as tall as the clouds, landed with Takahime.

Eugenbau: 'You greenhorn there, listen well to what I have to say. I am Mokusuke, the Tokiokishi who serves as the general manager of the Saien mansion. You must be the small-fry who acts as a sandal-bearer for the incompetent propagandist Terukuniwake. I hear you have been intimate with my Niyōbau, but I will overlook it today, so you had better be careful from now on.'

Umekowake: 'Oh, were you the highly rumored god of the Tokiokishi, Mokusuke-sama? I was ignorant and have been very rude. Takahime-sama and I have both felt love once, but we have not yet reached any conclusion. Therefore, we are as good as total strangers, so there should be no reason for me to be blamed by you.'

Eugenbau: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha, your mouth is quite skillful. There is no need to go on and on; if you ask your own conscience, you will understand. If one asks if there is a demon, one should answer no, but if one's heart asks, how shall one answer? You know that Dōka, do you not? I am a man, so I will not pursue this further. It would affect the dignity of a Tokiokishi to catch one or two greenhorns and make a fuss, so I will take a magnanimous approach and let it pass. Be grateful.'

Takahime: 'If I may, Umekowake-sama, even if the god of the Tokiokishi says that, he is not the kind of person who hates you, so please do not think badly of him. However, it is useless to be in love with me, so I will warn you strictly about that point. Since you are also a fledgling propagandist, why don't you save the lives of the men and women who are suffering in this Forest of Demons as your first feat? If you do that, Mokusuke-sama's anger will melt away, and you will have achieved a feat. How about it? Won't you show some chivalry and Ketsukau?'

Umekowake was an incarnation of Kototama-wake, so it was impossible for him not to perceive the true nature of Takahime and Eugenbau. Furthermore, he had already seen through his vision while on the Tokiwamaru that two young men and women had been deceived by Takahime into the Forest of Demons and were being tormented by ants and wrapped in spider silk. For that reason, Umekowake had steered his small boat and landed here alone to save the two. Umekowake had immediately performed the divine work of Chinkon in the Forest of Demons and sent strong divine spirits, so he knew that neither the spiders nor the ants could do anything. Therefore, he had been watching the dispute between Eugenbau and Takahime on the boat with complete composure. He was well aware that the deep intention behind Takahime's suggestion that he show chivalry and save the two was to lure Umekowake into the Forest of Demons of ants and have him eaten alive. For that reason, Umekowake agreed immediately and, before the eyes of Eugenbau and Takahime, jumped into the Forest of Demons with complete composure. Eugenbau and Takahime clapped their hands and laughed loudly, approached the entrance of the bamboo grove, thrust out their chins, slapped their backsides, indulged in all sorts of insults and mockery, left behind the parting words 'Take your time eating,' and entrusted themselves to the boat once more, floating away to parts unknown. Umekowake safely rescued the two, sat for a while at the base of a large ginkgo tree, listened to the various stories of how things had unfolded from the two, and then the three of them entrusted themselves to a single small boat and headed toward the port of Suga. Ah, Yorokobi-tamae, Kami-nagara-rei-sai-wai-mae-mase.

梅『ヤ、貴方が噂に高き時置師の神、杢助様で御座いましたか、存ぜぬ事とて偉い失礼を致しました。高姫さまと私との仲は双方共一度は恋慕致しましたが、未だ要領は得て居りませぬ。それ故赤の他人も同様ですから、余り貴方からお咎めを蒙る訳も御座いますまい』

妖『ハヽヽヽヽ、口は調法なものだのう、ゴテゴテ云ふにや及ばない、お前の良心に問うたら分るだらう。

人問はば鬼は居ぬとも答ふべし 心の問はば如何こたへむ。

と云ふ道歌を知つて居るだらう、俺も男だ、敢て追及はしない。高が青二才の一匹や二匹つかまへてゴテゴテ云ふのは時置師の沽券にも関するから、寛大の処置を取つて不問に付しておく、有難う思へ』

高『もし梅公別様、時置師の神様はあゝ仰有つても決してお前さまを憎むやうな方ぢやないから悪く思はないやうにして下さい。併しあたいに恋慕したつて駄目ですから其点は固く堅く注意しておきますよ。お前さまも宣伝使の卵ださうだから、一つ手柄初めにこの魔の森に落ち込んで苦んで居る男女の命を救けておやりなさい。さうすれや杢助さまの怒もとけ、お前さまの手柄も立つと云ふもの、どうです一つ侠気を出して決行する気はありませぬかな』

梅公別は言霊別の化身で高姫や妖幻坊の正体を感知しない筈はない。さうして魔の森に高姫に誑かされ、二人の若き男女が蟻に責められ蜘蛛の糸にまかれ苦んで居る事は、既に已に常磐丸の船中に於て透視して居るのである。夫故に梅公別は両人を救ふべく小舟を操つて一人此所に上陸したのである。梅公別は早速鎮魂の神業を魔の森に修し、強き神霊を送つて居たから蜘蛛も蟻も如何する事も出来ないのを知つて居た。それ故泰然自若として妖幻坊、高姫の船中の争を見物して居たのである。今高姫が侠気を出して二人の男女を救へと云つた心の奥底は、梅公別をあの蟻の魔の森に飛び込ましめ、喰ひ殺さしめむと企んで居る事もよく承知して居た。それ故梅公別は二つ返事で承諾し妖幻坊、高姫の目の前で泰然自若魔の森へ飛び込んで仕舞つた。妖幻坊、高姫は両手を拍つて高笑ひ、竹藪の入口に進みよつて腮を突出し尻を叩き所在罵詈嘲笑を逞うし「ゆつくりお喰れなされ」と捨台詞を残し、再び船に身を任せ、何処ともなく浮び行く。梅公別は無事に二人を救ひ出し、暫し大銀杏の根下に腰打ちかけ、種々の成行き話を二人より聞き取り乍ら三人一つの小船に身を任せ、スガの港をさして進み行く。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
