Chapter 10: Pure の Song [1819]

清の歌〔1819〕

§ 1

The night is high in the Hisakata sky, the land of the moon that shines and illuminates. In the Suga mountains of the Toruman country, the ancient pines of Chitose are moss-covered. Momodori and Chidori sing in the morning and evening, celebrating the Miyo for Chiyochiyo, their chirping voices valiant. On the branches of the old camphor trees, eagles emerge to build their nests. The Tokiha pines are deep in color, and the Tazu cry as they cross over to build their nests. The Sansuimeibi mountain of Kami, the Onhokora of the Sanō Jinjiya, though broken and distorted by the Amekaze of Ikusennen, its Shintoku shines and spreads throughout the Shichisenyokoku of the moon land.

The Haru lake is Yōyō, and the fragrance of the wind blowing in, filling with waves, is sweet. Rice, wheat, beans, and millet ripen well; cattle, horses, sheep, pigs, and Rakuda, all Kachiku grow well. It is known as the Uzukamikuni where the blessings of Kami are overflowing. Looking at the Kuninaka where the peaks of Taikauzan tower and the Kamiyama of Suga are Utsusau and lush, the prosperity of the Toruman country is faintly visible, and the appearance of the age of Kami is recalled. Yorikohime and Hanakohime, together with the master Iruku, call and gather many villagers. With hearts set on the Suga mountains, they fell trees from the large and small valleys, and offer the base and the tip to the Imperial Great Kami of the mountain entrance, from morning until night, chiyon-chiyon, using sacred axes and sacred planes.

The sound of the saws is valiant, and the joy of cutting and finishing the wood is great. Before the great front of the Sanō palace, they cleared and purified the wide garden that had been laid out, stood and lined up the five-colored offerings, and began the Ishizuki festival. The sacred song of the Ishizuki festival is as stated below.

Ishizuki Song: The medicine wholesaler of Suga town, Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud. Strike through to the bottom of hell. Clear the Suga Kamiyama, level the earth, scatter salt, pound it firmly into the upper bedrock, pound it solid into the lower bedrock. Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud, until the bottom of the Ryūgū is pierced. The medicine wholesaler of Suga port, the Great Kami of infinite mercy who created heaven and earth, is honored to be served as the Tokiha-Tokiha shrine. Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud, until the cauldron of hell is cracked. The medicine wholesaler of Suga port, Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud.

The Mizutama who descended from heaven to the sacred ground of the Usunayama. Fearing the strict words of the Great Kami, Kami-Susanō-no-Ōkami, this sacred ground, which is the finest scenic spot of the first moon, and which the ministers of Kami from Brahmanism and Uralism have visited and sought many times, has now become a world that shines brightly. Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud, until the bottom of the Ryūgū is pierced. The medicine wholesaler of Suga port, Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud.

The missionary of the San-Go-Kyō, the minister of Kami, Umekōwake, stands facing the Aura mountain. He speaks to the Genshinbō and the Shīgō, the terrifyingly named strong thieves and the corrupt monks, and while raising a song of triumph, he causes the Azusa bow to cross the Haru lake. He is the sacred hero who saved the crisis of Dariya-hime among the passengers on the boat. Since this minister of Kami is present, even if the Suga Kamiyama is deeply wrapped in clouds and demons attack, or if ogres and great serpents come to assault, how could one fear? Kannagara, they shall vanish in the light of Kami. Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud, until the bottom of the Ryūgū is pierced. The medicine wholesaler of Suga port, Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud.

Yorikohime and Hanakohime, are they the Tanabata-hime or Chichihime who descended from heaven? Are they the reincarnation of Sakuya-hime who appeared on the Tenkyō mountain? Their faces are white, their eyebrows are thin, and their hair is the color of a crow's wet feathers. Even to look at them once makes one feel dizzy, and the eyes grow dim at their enchanting beauty. Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud, until the cauldron of hell is cracked. The medicine wholesaler of Suga port, Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud.

As long as two incarnations of Benten-sama descend from heaven, the Shintoku of this great palace will shine day, night, and month. The dark sky of the moon land will clear up purely, Kannagara. The divine majesty of Kami is awe-inspiring. Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud, until the cauldron of hell is cracked. The medicine wholesaler of Suga port, Yōi-yōi, strike with a thud.

Thus, the ground-breaking ceremony was completed, and subsequently, the pillar-raising ceremony, the ridge-pole raising ceremony, and the completion ceremony were held within a mere sixty days. As a result of the diligent efforts of the carpenters, plasterers, and assistants, it was decided to perform the transfer ceremony. On the long-awaited fifth day of the fifth month, the completion ceremony of the Suga palace was finally held. Tamakiyowake of Kamitani village served as the chief priest, the master Iruku served as the head of the sacred offerings, and the three princesses, Yoriko, Hanako, and Dariya, served as attendants. With the sounds of the Yagumo-koto, koto, hichiriki, and drums, the transfer ceremony was completed without incident. From here, it was decided to hold the rice-planting ceremony at the sacred offering field at the foot of the Suga mountain. The order of the ceremony is briefly described as follows.

On the morning of the fifth day of the fifth month, all the Saiin take their seats at the Kami's throne. The local Nōfu stand in rows along the banks of the Shinsenden, then offer Shinsen and recite Norito; next, the Saiin and all the Sankeishiya perform Raihai, and upon completion, the Saiin proceed to the Tetsusen. The Nōfu partake of the Miki, and then commence the Taue Giyōji. The Saishiyu, Tamakiyowake, begins the Ondō, and the Ōkashiya follow his voice. The Nōfu enter the Shinsenden to perform the ceremony of plowing, and while singing Dauka, they proceed in rows through the Shinsenden in the directions of Tōzainanboku, leveling their hoes to plow the Shinden, and the ceremony concludes with the placement of a hei in the Seichiu of the Shinsenden.

Ondō: 'Look, see there / The clouds across the mountain of Suga / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi / Beneath the horizontal clouds / Is our fatherland / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi / Look up and see / The great cold bird / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi / Looking down / The famous place of Suga is the landing / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi / My husband / Pulls the net at the beach of Kajika / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi / May it catch / In every mesh of the nine-fold net / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi / How auspicious is the seed of the taro / Hoi, hoi, yāhoi'

Once the plowing is finished and the water is let in, this time the Saotome cross their red tasuki in a cross shape, dress in beautiful garments, and descend into the rice paddy.

Saotome's Song: 'The rice fields are as many as Mount Fuji / The sun hangs brilliantly on the edge of the mountain / The young master of my place / Prefers the thin fields / A thousand koku of thin fields, a thousand koku of thick fields / Seventeen or eighteen, if all dance together / Until the moon and stars come out and the mosquitoes cry / Where did you and I begin to plant the rice? / At the base of the good house of nine-down-eight / Seventeen or eighteen, the clear water of the narrow field / The people who see it want to lend a hand / The waist of seventeen is a graceful waist / Attaching a naruko to the graceful waist / The crow at dusk is a dirty bird / It circles above the hat, saying go up and finish / The Thunder Kami is a fickle Kami / He puts his drumsticks in pawn / And seems to frequent the pleasure quarters.'

In the house of Alice, a medicinal wholesaler at the port of Suga, the spring of Ichiyōraifuku suddenly arrived. The construction of the Suga no Miya was completed without incident, Alice's illness healed as if wiped away, and Dariya-hime, who had been missing, returned two days before the festival, sent by Tamakiyowake of Kamiyamura. The only regret was that the Umekōwake-sendenshi had not yet arrived. Alice, while opening a fan with the Hinomaru, dances at the banquet seat in joy and celebration.

Yōkyoku: Alice: 'The world is high in the eternal sky / Waving the heavenly feather robe / Deep in the mountains of Suga / It resembles the divine form / Of Konohana-hime who descended from heaven / Yoriko-hime and Hanaka-hime / The face of Dariya-hime / Is adorned with the spirit of Mizu / Folding the ten-span sword into three / Placing the heavenly Yasukawa in the middle / Rinsing it in the heavenly Manai / Taking it and shaking it with a rustle / Biting it and blowing it upon the Sōgami / Becoming the mist of Ibuki / The beautiful figures of Ichikishimahime, Takirihime / And Takitsuhime / I feel as if I am worshipping them before my very eyes / On a winter night when the withered wind blows / In my old body that seems to be confused / I feel as if I have met the spring.

Revere and respect the heavens and earth / The work of the Kami is extraordinary / High in the sky of the moon's realm / It is as if it surpasses the light / Of the sun and moon shining across / Ii, ii / Now, the foot of Mount Suga / From the divine age of the distant past / Has been passed down and told / As the residence of the Imperial Great Kami / Because it is a precious village / With the clear Lake Haru in the north / The high peak of Ōtaka in the south / Mount Kanegatake towering in the east / Mount Aogumo towering in the west / Standing like a mountain screen / The Amatsukami and Kunitsukami / Gather here, and the joy of serving / As the precious palace / Is like already living in the heavenly kingdom.

原文（近代文語）

五月五日早朝祭員一同神の座に着く。土地の農夫等神饌田の畔に列立し、次いで神饌を供し祝詞を奏上し、次に祭員、参詣者一同礼拝し、終つて祭員は撒饌に移る。農夫は神酒を戴き、次に田植の行事に着手す。斎主の玉清別は音頭の発声をなし、謳歌者声を次ぐ。農夫等神饌田に入りて耕の式をなし、道歌を歌ひながら神饌田を東西南北に列を作つて進行し、鍬を揃へて神田を耕し、終つて神饌田の正中に幣を立ておく儀式である。
