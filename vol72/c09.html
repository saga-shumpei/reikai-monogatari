Chapter 9: 欠恋坊 [1818]

欠恋坊〔1818〕

§ 1

Wondering how the fate of his child might be, the father, Arisu no Oyaji, waited in agony; then, the two returned, sent by the Ananai-kyō Umekōwake, the administrator of the Kami. Upon their return, he was overcome with such intense joy that he fell into a state of delirium; as his illness grew more severe, complaining of a headache, he cast aside all his Zaisan and hid deep within the inner chamber. Dariya-hime, struck with alarm, sought to heal her beloved father's illness, and so, in the dead of night, she secretly visited the Sanō Jinjiya on Mount Suga, praying with all her Magokoro.

Just then, as if to blur the Usuyami, a Hakuhatsuiyō Monokage appeared suddenly. Approaching the front of the shrine with Iuiu, it stood solemnly and spoke in a hushed voice: 'I am the one known as the pillar of the precious Ananahi, Kamususanō no Ōkami. Your house has been known since ancient times as the Hiyakumanchiyōjiya of Suga Port, and is the Enpu for ten thousand people. Because the retribution for those sins has now come, your mother Ichihaya hid herself from this world, was saved in a certain Yamazato, and while raising a thin wisp of smoke, has been living a Nisōseikuwatsu, praying for the Meifuku of your house, which is truly pitiful. Furthermore, your father Arisu is now suffering from a Jiubiyō.'

'He is afflicted and his life is in critical condition. If you wish to overcome this crisis safely, follow my teachings and visit the presence of the Sakae no Kami, who has descended since the age of the Kami in the valley of Mount Ōtani, as soon as possible. I shall guide you, avoiding human eyes on the night path, and help you, Dariya-hime. What is your answer?' When he spoke thus solemnly, Dariya tilted her head, and though she felt suspicious, she remained silent, lost in thought; but then, a flash of light shone brightly. Startled, she looked up, and the light vanished as quickly as it had appeared. Moved by this mysterious event, Dariya's heart stirred.

Having steeled her heart, she replied: 'Whether you are Kamususanō no Ōkami or an incarnation of the Sanō Jinjiya, I cannot tell, but you are a person beyond the realm of humanity. Even if you were a Kijin, since you possess such miraculous arts, any wish would surely be granted swiftly. I shall follow behind you and go anywhere you lead. Please guide me,' she said, pressing her hands together. The Yōsō who had disguised himself as Kamususanō no Ōkami was Genshinbō, who had holed himself up in a cave on Mount Ōra, lamenting the virtuous men and women, plotting rebellion; he was the incarnation of the false Tentei, the end result of a false savior, known as the Yama-ko bōzu.

Within his heart, Genshinbō was dancing with joy, yet he proclaimed his Kototama with great solemnity: 'Excellent, excellent, Dariya-hime. You think your mother left this world three years ago, but that is not the case. I sent my kinsmen to dig up the soil of the graveyard, brought her back to life, and she is hiding in a hermitage deep in the mountains. First, I shall let you meet your mother, and then, I have a plan to save your father from his Jiubiyō. Follow me,' he said, and pulling Dariya-hime's hand, he walked briskly along the dark mountain path toward the castle town of Tarahan, through the sparsely populated wilderness, while chanting in a strained voice.

Hannya Shingyō, Haramitsu-kyō, and even the Fumon-bon, he chanted as he headed toward the castle town of Tarahan.

Dariya-hime did not dream that this was the notorious monk, the villain Genshinbō of Mount Ōra, and having forgotten that Yoriko-hime and Hanaka were staying at her home, and having heard that her mother, whom she thought dead, was alive in a deep mountain retreat, she had no time to verify the truth or falsehood. Deeply believing this Yōsō to be the incarnation of the Kami Kamususanō, she had slipped out into the night, heading for the castle town of Tarahan. Genshinbō, having spoken whatever came to his mind, had successfully deceived this beautiful daughter of a famous apothecary, hoping that if he could win her over and make her his wife, he could somehow find a way to claim the Hiyakumanchiyōjiya's Zaisan for himself, despite the existence of her two brothers, by using both charm and greed. Though he had lured her this far, he was troubled in his heart, wondering where he should take her next.

§ 2

Along the banks of the Tarahan River, there is a fairly extensive forest of Tokihagi trees. As this forest was situated by the riverside, rocks of the Sendefujiki were lined up there. The two took their seats upon these rocks and rested while gazing at the flow of the river.

Dariya: 'Excuse me, Keshinsama of the Ōkami, in which direction is the mountain where my mother resides? Please inform me for a moment.'

Genshin: 'Hmm, yes, yes, eh... let me see... that peak there is Takamitsuyama, and then, beyond that, eh, is Kishiyama, Kahanamiyama, eh, and beyond that, you should be able to see a peak shimmering in a cobalt color. That is called Ōtaniyama, and at its foot, well, eh, your mother is there. And there, there is a shrine of the God of Prosperity. That God will grant any wish you make. I shall guide you there now, but since the road is quite bad, I thought you would have trouble, so I decided we should go by resting from time to time.'

Dari: 'My, what a high mountain it is; there must still be a long way to go until we reach that place, must there not?'

Gen: 'That is so, it is probably about thirty ri; since I am taking a woman of weak feet along, I must consider that it will take at least three days.'

Dari: 'Ah, is that so? Even if it takes ten days instead of three, if I can meet my mother and my father's illness is cured, I shall not mind it in the least. I humbly beg for your favor.'

Gen: 'Now, Dariya-sama, do you believe me to be a true incarnation of God, or do you think I am a corrupt monk? I would like to hear the truth of the matter.'

Dari: 'Well, as an incarnation of the Ōkami, you are just a little... if I may say so, forgive me, but you seem somewhat lighthearted; yet as a commoner, you excel in every point, and you possess miraculous arts that a mountain priest could never perform. To eyes as ordinary as mine, I cannot even grasp a scale of the dragon. Even if you were a mountain priest, because you possess the divine virtue of emitting light from your body in the dark of night and illuminating the surroundings, I believed that if I followed your words, you would surely grant my wish, and thus I requested your guidance.'

Gen: 'Haha, indeed, you are quite a talented woman. If you had followed me believing me to be an incarnation of God, it would not be interesting at all, but if you followed me out of admiration for the fact that I possess mysterious arts, even if I am a mountain priest, that is all the more promising. Well then, I shall reveal everything to you: I am the incarnation of the Heavenly Emperor, the savior sent from heaven, the peerless wise monk of the world, a strategist of unparalleled intellect, nay, a Mahatma, a holy hero. How is that, Dariya-hime-dono? You must be surprised, must you not?'

Dari: 'Ho-ho-ho, you are just like that person Genshin-bo of Mount Aura.'

Gen: 'Osa, that is right; I am indeed the incarnation of the Great Tengu who has lived for many years on the mountain of Aura, Genshin-bo.'

Dari: 'Ho-ho-ho, my, what great power you have; your ego is inflated to an incredible degree. If that is the case, Genshin-sama, you must be a lecherous priest who drags any beautiful woman he sees into a cave and satisfies his animal desires whether she likes it or not. You probably tricked me by saying you would let me meet my mother, and are planning to drag me into some cave somewhere. I shall excuse myself from here on. I would rather let a crow peck at me than let a corrupt monk-sama do so. Eh, my, my, you have made a fool of me. A woman of these times will not be fooled by such lies. If you feel frustrated, bite your tongue and die. Farewell.'

Leaving these parting words, she attempted to run away. As soon as Genshin-bo whistled three or four times with a shrill sound, seven or eight masked, rough-looking men appeared from the thickets of the forest, and without letting her say yes or no, they took her by the hands and feet and, together with Genshin-bo, carried her off, heading straight toward the direction of Tani-guku Mountain along the field path.

At Arisu's house in the port of Suga, Dariyahime had made a midnight pilgrimage to the grove of the Sanō, and when dawn came she had not returned; wherefore he ordered the gatekeepers Aru and Esu to search thoroughly among the trees of Mount Suga, but though they searched with all their might, they found neither shadow nor form of her, and losing heart, they returned at dusk of that day with pale faces. Her elder brother Iruku, thinking that if he brought such news to his gravely ill father the sickness would only worsen, gave strict instructions to the servants and forbade them to say anything at all to his sick father Arisu about Dariyahime. Iruku then visited the living room of Yorikohime and Hanakahime,

Iruku: 'Pardon me, Yorikohime-sama, are you already asleep? I am sorry to disturb you late at night,'

so he inquired, whereupon from within came the gentle voice of Yorikohime,

Yorikohime: 'Yes, I am not asleep yet. Is that voice yours, Iruku-sama? Pray enter. My younger sister is already asleep from the fatigue of her propagation work, but please enter without hesitation.'

Iruku: 'Yes, thank you; then I beg your leave to enter. Ah, though it is sudden, I have come to ask for a little of your wisdom. It is none other than this: my younger sister Dariyahime, distressed by my father's serious illness, made a pilgrimage to the shrine of the Sanō on Mount Suga around midnight last night, but even when morning came she had not returned. I was extremely worried and sent the gatekeepers Aru and Esu to search all through the mountains, but though they called and shouted, there was no answer whatever, like a stone thrown into a pond, and at dusk they returned dejectedly. Might she not have been kidnapped by some evil person?'

Yorikohime: 'Oh, hearing this for the first time, I am truly shocked; how greatly you must be worried! Since my divine vision has not yet opened, I cannot give you any direction one way or another, but dear me, she must surely have been deceived by a bad fellow and taken away to the depths of some mountain. Nevertheless, by no means be worried; as long as there is the protection of the Lord God, nothing terrible will happen.'

Iruku: 'Is that so indeed? Just when I thought she had been rescued by Umekōwake the propagation envoy, to be seized by an evil person again—my younger sister is truly of ill fortune,'

イル『ヤア、そのお夢はキツト正夢で御座いませう、六十日と云へば長いやうですが、直ぐに経ちます。どうか其時迄父が生きて居つてくれれば宜しいがな』

ヨリ『一切を神様に任したお父上、仮令御病気でもお命に別条は御座いませぬ。お宮の普請が立派に出来上がつた上に、ダリヤ姫さまはお帰り、お父上は御本復と云ふ事になるでせう。お宮の出来上りとダリヤさまのお帰りとお父上の御全快と「目出度目出度が三つ重なつて鶴が御門に巣をかける」と云ふ瑞祥がやがて参りませう。何事も神様にお任せして時節をお待ち下さいませ』

イルクは、

『ハイ、有難う、夜分にお邪魔致しました、何分宜しうお願申します』

と吾居室さして帰り行く。
