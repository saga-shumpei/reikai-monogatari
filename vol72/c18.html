Chapter 18: 法 Castle Crossing [1827]

法城渡〔1827〕

§ 1

Yorikohime, having seen through that Takahime, who had come to visit, was a mischievous cow who could not be handled by a single rope, whether it be vinegar or konniyaku, dressed herself in an outfit of pure white shirorinzu from top to bottom, let her hair hang long down her back, held a chiukei in her hand, and while walking with a leisurely gait along the rauka with the sound of kinuzu rustling, she leaned against the mondafuisu.

Yori: 'What keshin of a nanigami are you, O Takahime-kimi, with the fragrance of shiraume flowers? Even the flowers that bloom higher than the hisakata no ame would not reach your attire. You are truly the kamubashira of the Uranai-kyō, and I hear you are the sacred Hinodewake.'

Taka: 'How hateful is your face, teasing me, the miizu Takahime, by stringing together such seji. I am not a kami who eats tsuishiyō, so wash your face, Yorikohime. Today is the turning point of your seishi, the appearance of a kami who distinguishes zenaku.'

Yori: 'This is unexpected, the onkotoba of Takahime-sama; Yorikohime is also taken aback. I am Yorikohime, of the true Kami; why should I fear the rough winds of the ugly? I think that terrifying face of yours is that of an oni living in a mountain cave.'

Taka: 'What rude things are you spouting, you mountain-girl slave who rose from being a thief? I am stunned by the slyness with which you try to defile the shrine of the Kami by composing elegant poems. From now on, the true Hinodewake will appear and illuminate the darkness of your heart.'

Yori: 'My spirit is the moon and sun shining in the sky, even through the day and night. I am a living kami who has received the inner flow of the angel descending from the hisakata no ame.'

Taka: 'For a mere thief-turned-upstart to call herself a living kami is laughable. You are a wolf in sheep's clothing, imitating the Kannon-sama who eats tails.'

Yori: 'Whether you are a wolf or an Ōkami-sama, I do not know, but my spirit always shines. My spirit will shine brighter than the light of the sun and moon in the sky to illuminate the four directions.'

Taka: 'You have blathered, you spirit nesting in magatsu, you firefly-light slave of the moon and sun of the mochi-tail.'

Yori: 'The firefly light that sews through the darkness of the samidare also becomes a guide for those traveling at night. There are those who have become pillars of the country by gathering many fireflies to read their books.'

Taka: 'You act so grand, but your logic is like the evening moon hiding behind a mountain, soon to become darkness. If you look at the light of Takahime, even the miizu of the Kami in the great sky will be dazzled.'

Yori: 'Are you a yamabushi of the great Takayama? You blow such great horagai morning and evening.'

Taka: 'The horagai is a sacred tool of the true Kami, said to drive away the evil spirits of this world. A coward who cannot even blow one horagai has no worth living in this world.'

Yori: 'Even if the soul dies, the sound of the horagai will surely echo as Takahime.'

Taka: 'You are an akugami who has disturbed the world by becoming the wife of Genshinbō, the horagai blower. You are a beggar saimon who goes around villages blowing the horagai and shaking a shakujō. You are the mountain god of the great horagai blower of Mount Aura, blowing your horn again at the Suga no Miya.'

Yori: 'Blow whatever hot air you please; I shall blow it away with the wind of Shinato.'

Taka: 'You are known as the reincarnation of the oni of Mount Ibuki; you are the magatsukami of this world.'

Yori: 'You are the ugly magatsukami who goes around blowing great horagai, saying Miroku, Miroku.'

Taka: 'Hey, Yoriko, do you have no guard at your mouth, standing up to this living kami?'

Yori: 'Whether I stand up to you or lie, I do not know, but there is no response in your quiver.'

Taka: 'Rather than standing in a utagaki with no response, I shall try spinning the kototama-guruma. Come now, answer my interrogation; this is where your seishi divides.'

Yori: 'I shall answer any question, just as the current flows in the rapids of the Hayakawa.'

Takahime, while clenching her fists, stood up with a fierce look, glaring at Yorikohime, her voice tone very rough, her face flushing red, and hitting the table with her fan, 'Hey, hey, Empress Yoriko, from here on it is a direct interrogation. What is the name of the living kami who is the root of the roots, who created the origin of heaven and earth?' When she said this, Yoriko smiled and replied, 'If you are wondering what kind of difficult question it might be, is it just that? The origin of heaven and earth is without end and without beginning, infinite, absolute, and eternal. It is the ancestor of the country who dwells in stillness, the Kami Kunotokotachi-sama. Apart from this one pillar of Kami, there is no other true Kami.'

Takahime: 'How is it, Kamutsukasa?' As she peered into his face, Takahime laughed with a 'fufun' at the tip of her nose.

Takahime: 'What an incomprehensible Kamutsukasa; I am so astonished I cannot speak. The Kamisama of Daiji and Daihi, wishing to save all Tenkabanmin by leading them to Anyōjiyōdo, has been bestowing his heart upon them while hiding his body in the Sokotsuihane. After waiting for the Jisetsu and enduring iroiro of Kannankurau, he finally appears here as the Ōkami of Miroku. Where is the Ikimiya of that Kamisama, Yoriko-sama? Please guess it correctly. If I lose, I shall show you myself, living and dying right before your eyes, Genzai.'

As she said this, Yoriko laughed in Azawara.

Yoriko: 'Anata's statement is incorrect. If one examines the Mifumi of Kami, the Kami who exists at the beginning of this world is the Ōkami of Kunitokotachi. All other hundreds of Kamigami are merely other names for angels; there is nothing outside of this.'

As she said this, Takahime arched her back with a 'gutsu'.

Takahime: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho-ho-ho! This is Omoshiro, Omoshiro! Are you teaching such things to the Hitobito of the world with such an air of importance, you Mekuragami of Ananai-kyō? If the Ōkami of Kunitokotachi is truly in this country, bring him before me. If you cannot do that, he must be a Kami of Kūsau and ideals. The Kami that this Takahime asks of is a Kami who, while possessing a living physical body, lives, works, and saves people while alive. Where is that Kamisama? I want you to let me know.'

As she said this, Yoriko laughed aloud.

Yoriko: 'The Kami who possesses a physical body is at the sacred site of the Ubusunayama, where the Chigi are built high. It is the Ōmikami of Susanoo, the great pillar of the three thousand worlds; there is none other than this. Although I do not know for certain which Kami of the Uranai-kyō you protect, it cannot be a great Kami.'

As she said this, Takahime became angry.

Takahime: 'Since Kami is pure and clean, and his benevolence is infinite, he has not even the evil of the dew on a rabbit's fur. If he is a Kami who uses people who kill others to steal money, deceive many men and women, and engage in thievery, he cannot be a true Kami. If I examine your origins, you are a Magatsukami who was the boss of the mountain bandits on Mount Aura. You are a Magatsukami who sits in this sacred site, where the Ōkami of Susanoo is correctly and purely enshrined, and defiles the Kami. Reform quickly, and leave this seat as soon as possible, Yoriko-sama. No matter how much you reform, just as ink does not wash off a white cloth no matter how many times you wash it, you are a person with a flaw. It is not in accordance with reason for a spirit with a flaw to serve the work of Kami. Do you have a reply to this? This Takahime does not do things like thievery. I am the great pillar of the true Kami, the root of all roots. If I have a flaw, please search for it. In the first place, the true Kamisama, according to the principle of spiritual correspondence, has the protection of a Kami of good for the good, and a Kami of evil for the evil. A spirit with a flaw has a Kami of flaws, and a pure spirit has a pure Kami. This is the correspondence of heaven and earth.'

As she said this, Yoriko looked down, left Takahime alone, and retreated into a room with a dejected air.

Watching her back, Takahime opened her mouth wide and laughed loudly.

Takahime: 'Oho-ho-ho-oho-ho-ho! I have revealed the true form of the fox and the raccoon before the eyes of Hinodewake, with no way for her to hide it, and she has fled into the back, dejected, with her tail tucked between her legs. Truly, this is satisfying. From now on, even Yoriko will not have the courage to stand against this Takahime. She will likely write a confession of error and come to me, saying, "From today, I entrust this mansion to you; please hide the origins of my sinful self," with pleas and entreaties. Ah, how Omoshiro, how pleasant! From today, this Kami-yakata will be mine, as if a rice cake had fallen from a shelf. It is clear that it will fall into my hands. The sign I wrote, saying that if I lost the debate I would hand over my role, is the proof. If one waits, the wind of the sea route will blow. The Kami appears in the open and distinguishes between good and evil, right and wrong. This teaching is not the teaching of the Ananai-kyō, nor is it the teaching of Kami. This is the living word and the living proof of the Uranai-kyō, which Takahime has executed before your very eyes. Sen-shū ban-sai, man-man-sai! I revere and tremble before the Ōkami of the Uranai-kyō, and I offer my gratitude for the grace of having protected the success of this day, the day of the living sun.'

キユ『ハヽヽヽヽ馬鹿だのう。足許に火がついて、尻が熱うなつて居るのにまだ貴様達は気がつかぬのか。まあ見て居れ、今に法城の開け渡しと来るから、その時は吠面かわくな。又薬屋の門番に逆転して番犬の境遇に甘じワンワン吠ながら勤めるのが関の山だ。何とあはれな代物だな、ウフヽヽヽ』

問答席にはヨリコ、花香、ダリヤ姫の三人が高姫とさし向ひになり、法城開け渡しの掛合中である。

ヨリ『千草の高姫様、すつぱりと法城を開け渡しますから受け取つて下さい。貴女の問答には決して負るやうな女ぢやありませぬが、妾も一つ感じた事が御座います。何程立派な器でも焼つぎにした器はやつぱり創物です。貴女の最前仰有つた通り如何にもオーラ山の山賊の女頭目として世人を苦しめ、所在罪悪を犯して来ました。かやうな罪深い身霊をもつて至粋至純なる大神様の前に仕へまつるのは冥加の程が恐ろしう御座います。到底妾は汚れた罪の重い体、神様の御前に出る資格は御座いませぬ。貴女は今日迄どんな事を遊ばしたか神ならぬ身の妾、些しも存じませぬが、妾に比べては余程清らかなお身霊と拝察致します、是から一先づスガの薬屋に引き取りますから、後は御勝手になさいませ』

高姫『ホヽヽヽヽ、成程お前さまも比較的よく物の分る人だ。最前生宮の云うた言葉に感激して身の罪を恥ぢ、法城を開け渡す、その御精神、実に見上げたものですよ、併し創物はどこ迄も創物ですから足許の明い中、トツトとお帰りなさるがよからう』

ヨリ『妾の妹の花香、ダリヤも妾に殉じて退席すると云ひますから、どうかこれも御承知を願ひたう御座います』

高『何程上面は綺麗でも創物のお前さまに使はれて居つた代物だから、どうせ完全な器ぢやあるまい。自発的に退かうと云ふのはこれも感心の至りだ。何とまあ神界の御経綸と云ふものは偉いものだな、ホヽヽヽヽ』

と笑壺に入つて居る。そこへキユーバーが得意面を晒し肩肱を怒らし大手を振つて四人の前に入り来り、

『千草の高姫どの天晴々々、功名手柄お祝ひ申ます。ヤイ、ヨリコ、花香、ダリヤの阿魔女態ア見やがれ。俺の権勢はこの通りだ。サアこれから玉清別の野郎もアルもエスも叩き払ひだ。エヽ、臭い臭い、鼻が汚れるワ、腐り女、腐り野郎奴一刻も早く出て失せろ』

と仁王立になり蜥蜴が立ち上つた様なスタイルで四辺キヨロキヨロ睨め廻して居る。
