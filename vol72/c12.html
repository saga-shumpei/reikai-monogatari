Chapter 12: 懺悔の生活 [1821]

懺悔の生活〔1821〕

§ 1

Covering myself with Ōkuronushi, I behaved as if the Moon Country of Shichisenyokoku were my own. Holding the military forces of Ōdaruwake in one hand, and the Kiyōten of Sukobutsuen in the other as my strength, I sought to grasp in my palm all the myriad countries scattered across the great plateaus of the Tarahan Castle and Dekatan, starting from the land of the god Toruman. Yet, has the Tenun of Kyūbā, who grew arrogant in heart, reached its end here? The Senshinbanku plans were fundamentally destroyed by the Shinriki of Umekōwake, the Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, and I was left without a Yorube, wandering the Ōnohara, my clothes soaked through by the rain.

As I combed my hair in the blowing wind, I finally arrived at the port of Suga, where I witnessed a Zendaimimon Daikeiji. At the Kiuseki of the Sanō Kami, the rare Miaraka of the Ōkami of Ananai-kyō stood with its chigi high, and even the katsuogi glittering, casting its shadow upon the shores of Lake Haru. In this scene of blinding brilliance, I clicked my tongue and walked on. Dressed in the light attire of a traveler hiding from the world—straw raincoat, hat, waraji sandals, and wearing hand-coverings and leggings—I leaned upon my kongō-zue staff and climbed the rising mountain path, gasping for breath. Beside the shrine stood a most magnificent great hall, with the words 'Religious Debate Hall' written in bold strokes.

Seeing this, I chuckled to myself, thinking, 'Regarding religious debate, in this vast Moon Country, there should not be a single person who can surpass Kyūbā. The Tenun is finally circulating, and the time of Ichiyōraifuku has arrived. It feels as though I have encountered the spring when the eight-thousand-generation camellia and udumbara flowers bloom, and the light of Kami grows ever longer. Ah, how interesting, how interesting!' I retied my monk's headband and thought, 'I know not what kind of fellows they are, but they are merely women. If I exert my secret arts in a fierce battle, what effort or time could it possibly require? Like leaves scattering in the wind, like dew vanishing in the morning sun,'

'Like spring ice melting, or like letting out a fart, they will crumble without a trace or form. That they will tuck their tails and flee in a rout, scattering like clouds and mist, is as if I see it before my very eyes right now. I know not what kind of women they are, but they surely cannot withstand the sharp tongue of Kyūbā, the sole savior of the world and the peerless in divine virtue. They will break their composure, cast aside their shaku, fall flat upon the ground, and surely beg me, saying, "Please, by all means, add me to your disciples." Ah, how interesting, how interesting! Though the May sky may be cloudy, the sky of Kyūbā's heart is clear, and as the sun and moon shine in the heavens, the ridge of the shrine at the foot of Mount Suga,'

'will shine across the land of Kami, and its occupation is already within my plans.' As I thus boasted to myself, I stood at the entrance of the debate hall and peered inside. I was welcomed by Hanako-hime, a beautiful woman who deceives like a flower, and I was startled, knitting my brows. While waves surged in my heart, I feigned ignorance, and while saying I was merely visiting the grounds while covering my garden geta, I prepared my tactical plans. Now the critical moment had arrived; the absurdity of my being fired up, thinking I would wrap Yoriko-hime in smoke with my tongue spitting fire!

Led by the guide, I leaned against the debate chair in the inner room, and when I gazed at the figure of Yoriko, I was startled enough to lose my soul at her unexpected nobility. Yet, being a rogue by nature, I steadied my resolve and fought for a few exchanges, but it was like a mantis waving its arms against a dragon chariot. In a defeat that defied words, I lost my strength and collapsed, and was cared for by the two princesses, and I spent the night in this mansion wearing their bedding.

When dawn broke, the empress Yoriko-hime approached the side of Kyūbā and said, 'O founder of the Sukobutsuen sect, I apologize for last night. Having seen, heard, and been corrected by the truth, you have now leisurely stayed at this mansion. Become my servant. In this mansion of only women, it is impossible without a man. I shall have you heat the bath. I shall have you cook the rice. Not to mention repairing the teeth of geta, you shall take a dustcloth and clean the tokonoma, and even use a bamboo broom to sweep every corner of the mansion clean of leaves and dust. If you dislike that, I shall have you clean the toilets. The outhouse where only women go—it has a fragrant scent, you know.' When she said this, Kyūbā agreed, saying, 'Indeed, Empress-sama, you are quite right. Although I have thought until now that I was a great man, to be humbled by you, a woman, and to be so spineless that I cannot even open my mouth—it means my training is still insufficient.'

§ 2

I have gradually come to understand. From now on, I shall lead a life of Zange, and I will diligently attend to the cleaning of the Chiyōzu, which people so dislike. Even the Taikafu began as a Zōrimo for Nobunagakō; if one rises from the bottom, the Dodai is truly solid. Knowing that a Keikuwaku that crosses the clouds is accompanied by Kiken, I have completely undergone Kaishin. Nanitozo, please look upon me with favor and employ me with patience. Once I have achieved a truly Atsupare Shiugefu, I shall serve even in the smallest Goyō of the Kamisama of Ananai-kyō.' As he spoke, Yoriko nodded and said, 'If there is no falsehood in thy Kotoba, then that shall be well. From now on, be careful and perform the cleaning of the Chiyōzu. Now, Hanaka-hime, Dariya-hime, from today this old man Kyūbā is established as the Shimobe of this place. Enriyoeshiyaku is unnecessary; use him thoroughly.' With such an expression of Tokui, she shone before the Futari and entered her Ima. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. Was he afraid of the light of the Kami? Or, having something in mind, has Kyūbā momentarily become foolish? He cannot be expected to live contentedly in such circumstances, as if he had fallen from above the clouds to the bottom of the earth. Is it rain, a storm, or perhaps wind? Is it an earthquake, thunder, or a flood? A body that is not a Kami has no way of knowing.

Hanaka-hime and Dariya-hime, while harboring feelings of anxiety, followed the words of Yoriko and employed Kyūbā as their Shimobe. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. The wonder of the divine Keirin. Tamakiyowake-no-Kamitsukasa, together with Dariya-hime, while carving the steps of the palace, bowed before the Kami and recited the Norito. At that moment, Kyūbā, holding a broom, gazed at the back of Dariya-hime until he could pierce a hole in it. 'Ah, truly, how beautiful and graceful her figure is. A maiden without a single flaw. I am a man, after all; is it possible that I cannot win a woman? I have infiltrated Toruman Castle, and I am a man of proven skill who even made Chigusa-hime, known as the greatest beauty under heaven, fall in love with me. Is she not just the daughter of a mere apothecary? If I devote my sincere heart to her, there is no doubt she will yield to me. While living in the Onnago-no-shima or the Otohime-yakata of the Ryūgū, to be cleaning the rear of a woman from morning till night, acting submissively, is not clever. Ee, when I think of it, it is frustrating. My heart-monkey and mind-horse have begun to go mad; no matter what, I cannot endure it. Yet, I must be patient for a little while longer. If I were to reveal my true colors in a place like this and be expelled, my essential and vital scheme would again become a mere painting of a rice cake. Enduring shame, to suffer morning and evening is for the sake of the future. Just you wait, Yoriko-hime, Hanaka-hime, and Dariya-hime. I shall make it so that you must send me your amorous glances. That is the skill of a man. I will not be short-tempered, thinking of the fifty of today rather than the hundred of the future. For Kyūbā, who carries a great ambition, the saying that great talent matures late is a precious golden rule. Love-clouds, blow away for a while,' he said, blowing upon himself, and the sight of him sweeping with a broom was comical.

Tamakiyowake, with Dariya-hime following behind, descended the stairs. When Kyūbā saw them, he stood in the garden, gazing at the clouds moving through the sky, his heart filled with deep emotion. Seeing this, Dariya approached his side and tapped him on the back, saying, 'Kyūbā-sama.' When she laughed, Kyūbā was startled, and while rubbing his hands together and bowing his waist, he said, 'Hai, hai, truly my apologies. Enchanted by the clear resonance of the Kotoba of you two-sama, I was unintentionally and unknowingly lost in a trance. Now, now, allow me to accompany you to the palace.' As he spoke, Tamakiyowake-no-Tsukasa waved her right hand and said, 'Kyūbā-sama, there is no need for worry at all. With Dariya-hime and the two of us together, nothing untoward will happen. In that case,' she said, and left, heading toward the Kamiyakata.

Watching them go, Kyūbā clicked his tongue repeatedly and said, 'Chee, damn it, do not make a fool of me. Walking together so intimately, continuing their sweet whispers, they went off as if to show me. Although I think something is suspicious, if I do not endure this place as well, I fear my essential and vital ambition will not be achieved. It is precisely because of that that I grit my teeth and endure. Ah, it cannot be fulfilled, it cannot be fulfilled, I cannot endure it. It is as if my eyeballs are going to pop out!'
