Chapter 10: Phantom Cloud 晴 [852]

妖雲晴〔852〕

§ 1

Ishikuma corrected his posture once more, and facing the water surface again, began to emit Kototama. This man was one whose spirit was not yet stable; when he was in his element, he would boast recklessly, but as soon as he felt a bit weakened, his complexion would turn deathly pale.

Ordered by Suekohime-sama to perform the norinaoshi (rectification of invocations) again, and deeply troubled by the advice of Karl while feeling the pain in his heart, he steeled himself with an inevitable, stubborn resolve and began to chant once more.

Yet, he manifested a sense of sorrow that lacked harmony, coming from nowhere in particular, without leaving anything to be desired.

His song:

Appearing as the younger brother god / Of the great Amaterasu-ōmikami, / Susanoo-ōmikami is / The Mizumitama of infinite benevolence, / The suffering daily troubled / By demons, great serpents, and evil gods, / Through the blessings of the living Kototama, / Purifying, reviewing, and listening again, / To rectify the Yashimakuni, / In the morning rain or the evening wind, / Without wearying of the snow-piled fields, / Crossing over distant mountains and rivers, / Crossing the great ocean, / Saving all things in the universe, / How grateful we are.

Appearing as the precious child of Sumeōkami, / Suekohime-sama appeared at the waterfall of Ken, / When Ishikuma of the Brahman religion / Was targeted by the serpent god, / And his life was in danger, / She manifested the Mikokoro of Ananai-kyo / Perfectly and in detail, / Not to mention the serpent god, / She even saved the very soul of this Ishikuma. / I, in my lowly human body, / Without fearing heaven and earth, / In the sanctuary of Takateruyama of dark clouds, / Building a hall and boasting to the people of the world / That I am a messenger of God, / Emitting and disturbing the Kototama that had become murky and defiled, / Unknowingly, I had become / A captive of the ugly evil gods. / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, / Receiving the blessings of the spirit, / The sins and defilements filling heaven and earth, / Purified and saved by the Kototama of Suekohime-sama, / Now I have completely become / A servant of the God of Ananai-kyo. / Deep in the bottom of the pond of Tatsumi, / Settled firmly and eternally, / Before the presence of the serpent god, / I, with reverence and awe, / Chant the living Kototama.

O serpent god, O living god, / Thou art also a child of the Kami born / Of the water and fire of the imperial Kami of heaven and earth, / Even if thy form may change, / If thou lookest with pure eyes / Upon the noble Mikokoro of God, / How could there be any discrimination? / Thou art also a child of God, a palace of God, / I am also a child of God, a palace of God, / Let us be intimate and friendly with each other, / And taste the precious power of the Kototama / That opened the way of the Kami of Ananai-kyo, / Which transmits the teachings of heaven and earth, / And opening the rock-door of the heart, / Which is the essence of the soul, as soon as possible, / May the light of the moon and sun beautifully / Shine upon thy heart. / I am a person of lowly status, / Thou art the noble child of God.

I have no power to chant / And transmit the Kototama unto thee, / Yet I, too, / Chosen as a precious pillar / By the protection of the noble God, / Have come from afar / To save thy spirit. / O serpent god, O living god, / With a calm and peaceful heart, / Do not despise the Kototama of a lowly person, / But calm thy heart and listen deeply. / This heaven and earth are very vast, / No matter how wide the pond may be, / No matter how deep the water bottom may be, / When compared to the boundless great sky, / This pond does not even count / As a thing of any consequence. / Rather than lurking in such a place, / Purify thy heart with the power / Of the Kototama that fills heaven and earth, / Soar high into the great sky, / Sit at the right hand of the noble God, / Make the rain fall and the wind blow, / Bestow moisture upon the Aohitogusa, / And become a Kami who serves / The divine administration. / Although thy body / May hide in the bottom of the pond without finding its time, / The Tokitsukaze, / The wind that blows, will suddenly / Spread between heaven and earth, / And with the wind, rain, thunder, and lightning, / Protecting the fulfillment of the divine government, / Thou art the living god who possesses the quality. / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, / Infer the heart of God, / And listen in detail / To my Kototama, Kannagara. / Before the presence of the serpent god.

§ 2

Serving in Ananai-kyō, the servant of Kami, Ishikuma, reverently and respectfully clapped his hands eight times and offered counsel, saying: 'Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may your Mitama be blessed and prosper! '

This time he chanted the Kototama with the utmost courtesy and in a manner befitting fine words and beautiful expressions. Yet the spectacle on the water's surface remained exactly as it had been before.

Suekohime: 'Your Kototama this time may truly be said to have entered Kami and reached a wondrous state. However, as for why its effect does not appear, how do you think of it? '

Ishikuma: 'Yes, no matter what is said, because the sins of the past are deep, the Kamisama of the Orochi also makes light of it, thinking with such a mindset: "What does that filthy little fellow mean by speaking in such a pretentious and self-righteous way!..." and thus will not listen to it. I am truly ashamed.'

Sueko: 'Ah, did you think about it in that way? That is truly a good way of thinking. Please make sure not to forget that mindset. And you do not need to enter Ananai-kyō separately. Also, I remember you saying you would dedicate a splendid mansion like Takayamahiko to Ananai-kyō, but please have no such worries whatsoever. The true teachings of Kamisama are not such small, compartmentalized things. Things like making various small elegant names such as Ananai-kyō, Brahmanism, or Uralism, packing people into their respective divisions, and engaging in disputes over a snail's horns can never accord with the divine will of the Great Loving and Compassionate Great Kami. The true Way penetrates past and present, extends across East and West, is single and unmixed, eternal and vast; by being imprisoned in such names as a church or a sacred site, the heart of the true Kami cannot be understood. If you also recognize that there are good points within Ananai-kyō, use those; Brahmanism is fine too, so excise the parts you find to be bad, and if there are good things, it does not matter in the least whose words they are. I hope that you will take the good and mend the short, and expand the complete and flawless teachings of Kamisama throughout the world. Every time I am called a propagandist of Ananai-kyō, I feel as though I am being locked inside some cramped box, and it is truly painful. All teachings of Kami must be liberated freely and easily, without a single bond, and must be Kamunagara. Please, with that intention, henceforth exert your sincerity to the utmost for the sake of the world and for the sake of Kamisama. For this is the truth of the Way of Ananai, directed toward the Mototsu Mitoya Great Kami who created this world, Kuninotokotachi no Mikoto, and other precious Kamisama...'

Ishikuma let tears fall in a steady stream,

Ishikuma: 'Truly impartial and unselfish, with clear reason and righteousness, the teachings of Hime-sama—ah, indeed, today I have received most marvelous divine virtues. From now on, I am resolved not to hold a small mind like the one I have had up until now, but to strive diligently so as to accord with the heart of the Great Kami. I earnestly beg that you will not abandon me, and that you will guide me, a fool, a hundredfold.'

He joined his palms, and his voice was even choked with tears of gratitude.

Sueko: 'Moshi Sutegokohime-san! Would you be so kind as to offer the Kototama to the Kamisama of the Orochi? Since Ishikuma-sama ended with such fruitless results as you saw, as a replenishment for that, I ask you to make a valiant effort.'

Suteko: 'If it is so, I shall obey your command and chant the Kototama.'

So saying, facing the water's surface, which was surging and churning like a mountain, she began to chant the Kototama with a voice cool and pure.

'In the pond of the southeast, ever abiding, the living Kami, before the august presence stands Sutegokohime. The Kototama bestowed by the Kamisama of Heaven and Earth, here with reverence I chant. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, the land of Kami created by Kami, the land of Kami protected by Kami. The multitudes of people born forth, and all things in the universe without exception, is there any that does not receive the grace between Kami and Kami? O Kamisama of the Orochi, O living Kami, that very soul and spirit received from Kami, although you speak of the times and seasons, why is it that you lurk in this bottomless pond? Even the heavenly august sky is ever so high, yet you should soar and protect this world...'

Thou, is not thy body indeed chosen as the precious shield of Sumekami, a noble mitama? Having accepted the divine miiki of Kototama that grants and saves the life of Eienmukiu to Shinrabanshiyō all, sooner by a day, even for a moment, parting the waves of Miike, return as an ikigami of Amatsumisora. Serving the divine oshihe of Ananahi, Sutekohime, with sincere magokoro, earnestly and wholly implores thee. Ah, Kamunagara! Receiving the mitama of Kami, manifest as a noble kami of the boundless Ōsora—the precious miko of Kamususanōnōokami, Suekohime.

Having received that Kototama, I exhort it to thy body. I exhort it to thy body. Ah, Kamunagara! May thy mitama prosper and bless us, and do not overlook the Kototama of Sutekohime as the sound of the wind blowing through the sky or the rapids of a river. Before the presence of the kami Orochi, with all my heart I humbly proclaim, having revealed the full measure of my heart.

Thus she finished chanting. Although the Kototama of Sutekohime was extremely simple, the heaven-bestowed spirit was pure, without a single spot of defilement and without dark clouds; and since the moon of suchness shone and gleamed like a mirror in the sea of the heart, the efficacy of that Kototama manifested remarkably, so that the high raging waves gradually subsided, the black clouds wrapping the surroundings suddenly cleared away, the scattered rain abruptly ceased falling, and in Amatsumisora the golden sun began to shine brightly. Ah, Kamunagara tamashihai mashimashite.
