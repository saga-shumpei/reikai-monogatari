Chapter 21: Kami 王の祠 [863]

神王の祠〔863〕

§ 1

Kuniyoriwake and his party, letting their feet go as they pleased while bathing in the morning sun, began to slowly climb the great mountain pass of Mt. Arashika, which faced southeast and met a dead end ahead.

This spot was no longer touched by this year's drought and was extremely safe; the trees of the mountains were of beautiful colors, shining brightly in the morning sun and looking lively.

Their hearts swelled with courage, and feeling somehow joyful, they unknowingly spent half a day on this steep slope and reached the summit of the pass.

Looking toward the northeast, the capital of Hiru is lined with dwellings, thin and long like a sash.

In terms of the number of households, numbering nearly two or three thousand, it is a great metropolis for this era.

Also, looking down to the southwest, Mt. Higurashi, where the towers of the Ural religion are shrouded, looks as close as if one could reach out and touch it, clad in blue-green robes of green, with the eighth station and above wrapped in clouds.

Kiji turned to Kuniyoriwake and said, 'I say, Propagator-sama, that high mountain wearing a white cloud hat in the direction of the southwest is none other than that Mt. Higurashi, you know.'

'It is certainly a spot chosen for its scenic beauty, isn't it?'

'Surrounded on three sides by mountains and backed on one side by the clear stream of the Higurashi River, it is a place that the fellows of the Ural religion are extremely proud of, calling it a location of four-gods correspondence.'

'As for the capital of Hiru, since it is built amidst a vast and open wilderness just like that, its convenience is very great, but in terms of strategic defense, compared to Mt. Higurashi, it seems to fall very short, doesn't it?'

Kuniyoriwake said, 'Indeed, the Ural religion has also found quite a fitting location, hasn't it?'

'However, when the vital Higurashi River has dried up completely just like that as of late, it must be most inconvenient in terms of transportation.'

'Since it is a world where every advantage has its disadvantage, if it were us, we would still like the capital of Hiru much better after all.'

Machi said, 'If you speak of liking it, as for us who have climbed up this cool breeze and hot slope with sweat dripping in torrents, rested our breath on the mountain top, looked out over the scenery in all directions, and nourished the vast and noble spirit, we have truly come to feel as though we had climbed right up to heaven.'

'No matter what is said, there is nothing like climbing a high mountain and looking down upon the world below, you know.'

'Rather than hiding in a narrow valley with a fuss and shouting every day about this and that, mountain climbing is truly a pleasant thing from time to time.'

Kuniyoriwake said, 'Now, everyone, shall we set off?' and briskly walked down the mountain path.

The two whispered in low voices, 'We want to rest just a bit more...', and reluctantly followed behind, descending the steep slope.

As they looked, a small shrine was built by the side of the mountain path.

Two or three large camphor trees sealed the heavens and served as the role of an umbrella against this shrine.

When they looked closely, a young woman with a worn-out face was kneeling before the shrine, earnestly pouring forth prayers for something.

Both Machi and Kiji recognized this already, and said, 'Hey, Propagator-sama, please look at that.'

'Over there, there is a shrine dedicated to the Eternal Great God.'

'And yet, some woman seems to be praying devotedly there, so let us drop by and see how things are.'

'On this lonely mountain path, for a young woman to come and visit this shrine, there must be some deep cause and circumstance behind it.'

Kuniyoriwake said, 'Ah, indeed, an old shrine is standing there, isn't it?'

'A truly magnificent camphor tree is flourishing.'

'If one thinks of trying to become a large tree to this extent, it must have passed through several thousands of years of time and frost.'

'Unlike weak human beings like us who die off in two or three hundred years, the camphor tree maintains a lifespan of several thousand years, still remains lush with flourishing branches and foliage, faces storms wherever it is without showing the slightest disturbance, and continues its life on this high mountain—it is truly magnificent.'

'Thinking of this, I have the feeling that there is nothing quite as great as plants, you know.'

'If the camphor tree has a spirit and furthermore can utter words, it would let us hear the state of the distant Age of the Gods, but alas, nothing can be done about that either.'

Machi said, 'Well now, leaving that aside, please look at that woman.'

'Is she not growing quite emaciated and frail?'

'At any rate, how would it be if we were to approach the front of the shrine and look into the matter?'

Kuniyori: 'Anyway, since we have come to pay our sankei to Kamisama, it would be well to inquire of Him.'

So saying, he marched up briskly to the front of the shrine. The three knelt before the shrine, clapped their hands and bowed twice in saihai, chanted the Amatsunorito purely and refreshingly, and when they finished, rested their breath by sitting upon long stones nearby.

Kiji knelt before the shrine, drew close to the side of a woman who was praying intensely with rakurui over something, and while gently and pitifully stroking and rubbing her back,

Kiji: 'Moshimoshi, I know not what okata you may be, but this is a most tremendous goshinkau. Since it is said that the goshinrei of Tokoyoshinōsama are omatsu at this shrine, when I consider that you are omai hither, you are likely an okata of the Uralic religion. That you, a frail woman, all by yourself, should make sankei at the shizen of this Takayama and offer oino makes me osatsu that there is some profound circumstance behind it. If it is a matter within our power, we think we should somehow lend a hand and consult with you, so please, if there is no hindrance, tell us even roughly. Though inadequate, we shall be your support.'

At these words of Kiji, imbued with sympathy, the woman finally raised her face,

'Hai, I am called Erina, residing at the foot of Mount Arashika. My father, named Esu, is a missionary of the Uralic religion, but a month ago a missionary of the Ananai-kyō stopped by, and as a result of conversing about various precious matters with my father throughout the night, my father also rejoiced greatly, kept that missionary at our home for four or five days, preached and explained the virtues of Ananai-kyō even to the believers of the Uralic religion, and receiving the goshintoku of Kamisama, he was rejoicing with great elation. However, this matter instantly reached the ears of the supreme general of the Uralic religion in what is called the Land of Hiru—the leader of the Uralic religion who established a sacred place and opened the Uralic religion in a cave on Mount Hinodewake—and a messenger came saying that my father Esu must come immediately; my father, rejoicing with elation, went to that sacred ground, but thereafter there was no word or sign whatsoever, and together with my mother I was worrying terribly, yet four or five days ago a missionary of the Uralic religion came to inquire, and saying that Esu-sama had lodged a missionary of Ananai-kyō at his own home and furthermore preached and exhorted Ananai-kyō to the believers of the Uralic religion, he was thrown into a dark water dungeon within the cave of Mount Hinodewake and is suffering tremendous torment, and since you, as wife and children, might be seized at any moment, beware—there was a kind person who secretly gave this notice. Upon hearing this, my mother instantly suffered a spasm, fell down upon a bed of severe illness, day by day her body grew weaker and wasted away without a trace, not a single drop of water or food could pass her throat, and she has fallen into a tragic fate with no other path than to await death just as she is. Therefore, I visit the shrine of the founder of the Uralic religion, Tokoyoshinōsama, day by day, and pray that He will save my father from his peril and heal my mother's illness,'

she recounted the circumstances without concealing them, tears in hand.

Kiji: 'That is truly, truly heartbreaking to hear. Even I have been a believer of the Uralic religion until now and have made sankei to the sacred ground of Mount Hinodewake some two times, but truly the Uralic religion, when you think of it now, is a cruel religion. It thinks nothing of a person's death, and because it teaches that one will be saved into heaven, it is an incomparable honor—it teaches such incomprehensible things that one simply cannot bear it, wah. However, that your honorable father lodged a missionary of Ananai-kyō for four or five days must be the result of his becoming disillusioned with the Uralic religion and realizing the beautiful aspects of Ananai-kyō. Look here, there must surely be a karmic connection. To hear such a story in a place like this is surely the guidance of Kamisama. Please, please do not worry. We shall surely, surely help your honorable father and honorable mother-sama.'

From this time forth, Kuniyoriwake arrived together with Elina at the home of Esu at the foot of Mount Arashika, prayed to heal the illness of Elina's mother Teeru, and after staying for several days, proceeded onwards pointing toward the capital of Hiru.

マチ『アハヽヽヽ何を吐かすのだ。余り調子に乗つて失敗をせぬ様にせよ。……そんなら宣伝使様、キジ公の後に従ひ、これより日暮シ山に立向ひ、ウラル教の大将ブール其他の奴原を片つ端から言向け和し、エスの宣伝使を救ひ出し、日ならず凱旋の上、ヒルの都の楓別命が御館に於て御面会申しませう。……サア、キジ公の大将、早く出立遊ばせよ』

と、からかひ乍ら、早くも此場を後に、先に立つて元来し坂路を帰り行く。

キジ『オイオイ大将を後にして、先へ行くと云ふ事があるものか。待つた待つた』

と呼はり乍ら、

キジ『宣伝使様、エリナ様、左様なら、後日お目にかかりませう』

と言葉を残し、マチの後を追つかけ行く。

これより国依別はエリナと共にアラシカ山の山麓エスの宅に至り、エリナの母テールの病を癒やさむと祈願し、数日逗留の後ヒルの都を指して進み行く。
