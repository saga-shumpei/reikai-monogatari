Chapter 3: Pure めの滝 [845]

清めの滝〔845〕

§ 1

Ishikuma of the Brahman religion, who had firmly established his base on Mount Takateruyama, expressed his gratitude to Suekohime and others, and stated his feelings through a song. That song,

Appearing in the land of Tokoyo, Tokoyoshinō Jizaiten, Established by Ōkunihiko, The Kamuzukasa of the Brahman religion, Appearing on the treasure island of Takasago, Where even deep blessings manifest, Making Mount Takateruyama his base, Ishikuma, whose heart is firm as well, Guarding it with steadfast faith, Obeying the laws of the teachings, To the believers who come in obedience, Without any mercy, through the severe practices of Crossing the fire-walking and the bridge of swords, Plunging to the riverbed, into the thorny room, Making them leap in stark nakedness, Wearing wooden clogs with driven nails, Damage and break the physical body given by God to shed blood, Thinking of it as a sacrifice to God, The deeply obsessed Kamuzukasa,

Soothing the wrath of God, To be saved in the heavenly pure land, And to escape the suffering of the future, The completely deluded Brahman, Who had strayed from the true path, Now at last realized it. Ah, Kannagara kamigami! Jinci-infinite Ōkami, Beginning with the blue-haired mortals, Creating birds, beasts, insects, and fish, The true parents and the three-five Teachings of God, Suekohime, Hearing the words proclaimed, Our hearts like village-liver Are clear and bright in the sky, Illuminating our souls with the divine majesty Of truly precious Kototama, Enchanted by the evil deity of the great serpent, While protecting our bodies that cannot even move, Inside our hearts,

When the Kototama of repentance is proclaimed, The darkness of the chest opens instantly. At the Kototama of Suekohime, Even the great serpent is deeply moved, Shedding hot tears, Rebuilding the steed of the heart That aimed to swallow me, The dead attachments of Asura instantly Clear away, and their form becomes water vapor. The dirt of the heart also falls and cleanses, Manifesting in the rapids of the swift river, Where Seoritsuhime brings happiness, All enemies have completely vanished. Ah, Kannagara kamigami! Receiving the blessing of the Mitama, Even the perverse Ishikuma, Saved by the great grace of Suekohime, Now already Deeply illuminated by the light Of the imperial gods of Izumoshinkō.

God appears in the front To divide good and evil, Kamunaohi who created this world, Oonaohi whose heart is also vast, In human life, whatever it may be, Look back and listen straight with Naohi, Proclaim and rectify the faults of the body, In the Kototama of truth alone, How precious is the teaching of God That saves the people of the world! Even if heaven and earth should change, My heart sworn to God, How could it ever change? Tatsuyohime, Who settles in the land of Takasago, Before the Mitutama no Ōkami, With all one's heart and single-mindedly, I shall swear, as a propaganda emissary Serving the god of the Brahman, From today onward, the teaching hall Built on Mount Takateruyama,

Dedicated to Izumoshinkō, To the imperial god of the great origin who created this world, Erecting a hemp pillar, for the sake of the world, With all my might, with all my body, I shall serve, Kannagara. Suekohime, the descendant of God, Suekohime who casts away even defilements and impurities, Before the lord where sins are also present, Ishikuma, whose crimes are heavy, Prays with all his heart. Ah, Kannagara kamigami! Looking straight into the heart of God, From now on to the capital of Chinpuku, Guide us along. Confessing the sins of the oppression Hitheto given to the people of the mansion of Chinpuku, Becoming a servant forever, As a gatekeeper of the capital of Chinpuku,

Serving and manifesting my sincere heart, Forgive me, Mizu-no-Mitama. Before the presence of the Mitzu-no-Mitama and Ara-no-Mitama, I reverently and respectfully pray. Ah, Kannagara kamigami! May the Mitama be blessed and prosper.

Sutekohime began to sing once more. That song,

This waterfall, gazed upon As if viewing the heavenly Kawara vertically, Suekohime who has appeared As the precious child of Mizu-no-Mitama, To wash away the mud of this world, The Eden River whose flow is pure too, Purifying the body and soul and washing the feet, Sutekohime, casting away the dirt of the heart,

Taking along the servant gods, Along the eight hundred paths of tides, the eight tide paths, Under the protection of Akitsuhime, Crossing at last to the land of Teru, Landing at the port of Hara, Crossing right across the land of Teru, Advancing to the mountain foot of Mount Teru, At the hour of the moonlit evening, Lying down upon the grassy field, While worrying about the past and the future, Just as she fell asleep, Suddenly a human voice was heard,

原文（近代文語）

高照山に本拠を固めたるバラモン教の石熊は、末子姫、其他に感謝の意を表し、歌を以て所感を述べたり。その歌、

『常世の国に現れませる 常世神王自在天 大国彦の樹てられし バラモン教の神司 深き恵も高砂の 宝の島に現はれて 高照山を根拠とし 心も固き石熊が 信心堅固に守りつつ 教の法を遵奉し 服従ひ来る信徒に 何の容赦も荒行の 火渡り剣の橋を越え 水底くぐり茨室 赤裸にて飛び込ませ 釘の打ちたる足駄はき 神より受けし肉体を 損ひ破りて血を出し 神に対する贄と 思ひ詰めたる神司

悔悟の言霊宣りつれば 忽ち開く胸の暗 末子の姫の言霊に 流石の大蛇も感歎し 熱き涙をこぼしつつ 吾を呑まむと狙ひたる 心の駒を立直し 修羅の亡執忽ちに 晴れて姿を水煙 心の垢もおち滝津 速川の瀬に現れませる 瀬織津姫の幸はひて 仇悉く消えにける あゝ惟神々々 御霊の幸を蒙ぶりて 心佞けし石熊も 末子の姫の御恵に 救ひ出され今は早 三五教の皇神の 光に深く照されぬ

神が表に現はれて 善と悪とを立わける 此世を造りし神直日 心も広き大直日 只何事も人の世は 直日に見直せ聞直せ 身の過は宣り直せ 誠一つの言霊に 世人を救ふ三五の 神の教の尊とさよ 仮令天地は変る共 神に誓ひし吾心 いかで変らむ高砂の 国に鎮まる竜世姫 国魂神の御前に 真心こめて只管に 誓ひまつらむバラモンの 神に仕へし宣伝使 高照山に築きたる 教館を今日よりは

三五教に献り 此世を造り玉ひたる 世の大本の皇神に 麻柱まつり世の為に 力の限り身の限り 仕へまつらむ惟神 神の御末の末子姫 曲も穢れも捨子姫 罪もカールの御前に 罪科重き石熊が 心を尽し祈ぎ奉る あゝ惟神々々 神の心に見直して 珍の都にこれよりは 吾等を伴ひ給へかし 珍の館の人々に 今迄与へし虐げの 罪を陳謝し奉り 従僕となりて永久に 珍の都の門番に

仕へまつりて赤誠を 現はしまつる吾心 許させ給へ瑞御霊 厳の御霊の御前に 謹み敬ひ願ぎ奉る あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ』

捨子姫は又歌ひ出したり。其歌、

『天の河原を縦として 眺めし如き此瀑布 瑞の御霊の珍の子と 現れ出でませる末子姫 此世の泥を滌がむと 流れも清きエデン河 身魂を清め足洗ひ 心の垢を捨子姫 従僕の神を召連れて 汐の八百路の八汐路を 秋津の姫の御守りに 漸う渡りテルの国 ハラの港に上陸し テルの国をば横断し テル山峠の山麓に 進み来りて月の宵 芝生の上に横たはり 来し方行く末案じつつ 眠りにつきし折柄に 俄に聞ゆる人の声

何人なるかと目を醒まし 話の様子を伺へば バラモン教の神司 信徒共に五人連れ 妾のいねし傍に 腰打かけて無駄話 高照山の石熊が 神の言葉に従ひて 顕恩郷より渡り来る 三五教の宣伝使 末子の姫や捨子姫 ハラの港に上陸し テル山峠を乗越えて 珍の都に進み行く それの途中を待伏せて 二人の女神引つ捉へ 高照山の館まで 連れ帰れよとの石熊の お指図なれど連日の 疲れに足も早痛み
