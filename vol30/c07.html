Chapter 7: 提燈の Light [849]

提燈の光〔849〕

§ 1

Spreading straw raincoats on the ground in the shade of the camphor trees and bathing in the light of the ninth-day moon leaking through the branches, they could not fall asleep right away, and staying up late into the night, they talked over the mysterious destiny that had arisen in the course of today. Kaāru, having been in high spirits, felt somehow joyous and emboldened by the arrival of Suekohime and Sutekohime, and furthermore, thrilled with the delight that Ishikuma, the Kyōshiyu of the Brahman religion, had yielded to Ananai-kyō without difficulty, he could not sleep and began to drive his habitual chatterbox cart.

Or else, if you want to greet them, since you fellows are carrying lanterns, you'd better organize a search party and search for them of your own accord.'

The loud and reciprocal arguing voices of the four men standing by the roadside echoed in the ears of Suekohime, whose ears were sensitive at night. When Suekohime slowly raised her body and looked yonder, a single round paper lantern marked with the crest of the ten celestial bodies and the shadows of two or three men could be seen glimpsing through the trees in the forest. Suekohime, worrying and feeling it would be a pity to wake up Sutekohime and Ishikuma, who were soundly asleep after much trouble, guided by the light and with groping steps, finally made her appearance on the highway.

Suekohime: 'Are you not Kāru-sama? And whose person is the light of that lantern, I wonder?'

At this voice the four men were startled,

'Ha! Just now, by the command of Matsuwakahime-sama, we are messengers who have come to welcome your honorable party.'

Suekohime: 'That is truly, truly kind of you; from a distant place, you have indeed come. Pray, come this way.'

Kāru: 'Truly, all honorable ones, though it is a gloomy-looking thatched hut to behold, it is Kāru's residence, come, pass right on through to the inner parts without reservation.'

Suekohime: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho!'

Three Men: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha!'

Laughing thus, following behind Suekohime, they groped their way into the shade of the forest where the two were sleeping.
