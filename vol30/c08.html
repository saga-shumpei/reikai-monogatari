§ 2

Among the seven gathered here,

Leaving aside the two nice ones,

This man, outstanding and great,

Though he lurks in the country of the nether roots,

Slaughtering and scattering in accordance with his desire with a mouth-halberd,

To reconstruct the dark world

As the reign of sunrise,

Regardless of the division between old officials and new ones,

Ahead of others, today's journey

To slaughter and scatter,

Could this pond, where ferocious evil gods rage,

Be abandoned and left behind?

With the divine power of ancient gods,

Elegantly directing the great serpent with words,

So that a hundred people may peacefully

And calmly live, solely and earnestly

Prays the sincere missionary,

With a divine heart purer than snow,

The seven selected and chosen again and again

Now here for the sake of the people of the world.

The habitats of the murky evil gods sounding ra-ri-ru-re-ro

Swept and purified as quickly as a moment,

So that the people in the strange country

May have epidemic disasters peacefully,

Driving away both demons and great serpents,

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara,

The bravery of washing away impurities

In the Kototama of the Isosuzugawa river,

With the gods manifesting in the front,

Separating and establishing good and evil,

Swiftly targeting the ugly great serpent

Lurking in the southeastern pond,

Subduing and pacifying it with the Kototama of the true way,

Sweeping away all the distortions of the people of the world,

Karl the official's skill

Is just like this for now,

Returning to the capital of Utsu

Before the presence of Matsuwakahiko,

The reliability of blowing the conch shell,

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara,

May the divine spirit bless us,

So that there may be no signs of failure

In the Kototama of Ishikuma-sama,

Karl devoutly prays.

Targeted by the male great serpent

Who appeared in the northwest waterfall,

A living Fudo avatar right in this world,

The more I reflect upon it, the more

There is no such shameful thing.

Even so, after all, Ishikuma

Is at the climbing path of arrogance,

Facing the youngest princess,

The Kototama to be declared

Toward the ugly god of the southeastern pond

Is what he pleaded: please leave it to me,

How pitiful was that pleading heart.

If you want to be entrusted with it that much,

There is no need for hesitation, do it boldly and rapidly,

Let us wrap it around the great serpent.

Wrapped by it, Ishikuma wraps his tongue,

Wrapping his tail and winding a headband

Tied in front, marching briskly,

With his life barely hanging by a thread, fleeing

In a single scatter right before one's eyes.

Hearing the Kototama chanted by Karl,

Do not bear a grudge, because I think of you

As my precious one,

I will not tell you bad things.

Even though the youngest princess is present,

Ishikuma, whose strength is insufficient,

Is an old fox borrowing the tiger's majesty.

To subdue the great serpent of the pond with Kototama,

What kind of talk is that?

Not knowing one's own standing has its limits.

Quickly change your mind

And while being humble, it is good to follow

Behind the princess-sama.

No matter how weak you may look,

You face the great serpent,

And you have no qualification to subdue and pacify it.

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara,

Reflect upon the heart of the gods.

Up until yesterday's day, opposing

The teachings of the thirty-five gods,

The Magatsuhahi god who has harbored

A thousand-transforming and ten-thousand-changing strategy,

The ugly dog you released

Is just staying at the strange mansion.

Although you say you have reformed,

You are a sinner against the gods.

No matter how much the youngest princess-sama

Has granted permission, to hold your nose high

And stand at the vanguard is what kind of behavior?

Your future is worrying.

I do not know if your mouth is Karl's or whose,

But out of friendship I say this to you.

If even a little bit of my sincere kindness

Reaches you,

Today it is good to hold back.

I warn you over and over again.

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara,

May the divine spirits bless and make us thrive.

Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over,

Whether the moon waxes or wanes,

Even if the great earth should sink,

With a soul like yours,

Could the great serpent be directed and pacified?

Remaining restrained is the number one priority.

Though it is repetitive, I am warning you.

Whatever you do, do not take

My words in a bad way.

O gods, please behold

The red inner chest of Karl.

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara,

Return to the heart of the gods,

Commune with the heart of the gods.'

Chanting thus, beating time with his feet, he advances onward. Ishikuma tilts his head at this song of Karl, crosses his arms, falls into deep thought, and follows weakly.
