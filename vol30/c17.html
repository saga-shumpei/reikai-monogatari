Chapter 17: 出陣 [859]

出陣〔859〕

§ 1

In the mountainside of Mount Higura in the land of Hiru, they dug a vast grotto and established a sacred site for the Ural religion, corresponding with the main temple on Mount Rocky; thus, the Ural religion, which had once been on the verge of ruin, raised its head again, expanding its influence from the northwestern part of the land of Haru throughout the entirety of Hiru. This grotto was called the sacred site of Mount Higura. Here, Būru served as the Kyōshiyu, and the two Sendenshi, Yūzu and Anan, assisted Būru, dispatching numerous Sendenshi to all four directions and exerting great effort in the propagation of the Ural religion.

In a room in the back, the Kyōshiyu Būru, along with the two, Anan and Yūzu, had piled up beautiful and fragrant apples, and while peeling them and savoring them with a click of the tongue, they were holding a meeting of the Kanbu.

Būru: 'In this land of Hiru, Momijihikonomikoto's son, Kaedewakemikoto, is propagating the San-iku-kyō, and his influence is not to be underestimated. Furthermore, a faction of the Brahman religion, Ishikuma, has suddenly raised its head again of late, rushing toward the stronghold of our Ural religion. Many of the people of the land are wavering in their allegiance, and now, due to these two religions, the efforts of the Ural religion over several hundred years are on the verge of being overturned from their very foundations. If we do not somehow drive away the followers of these two religions, we shall have no excuse to offer to the main temple on Mount Rocky. The divine revelation we have long preached—that the end of the world is near, repent, for the gates of heaven shall soon open—was not in vain. Seeing this great drought and great famine of this year, where the mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees are almost withered to death, what person, no matter how stubborn or spiritually dull, would not feel the impermanence of life upon seeing this, and not loathe this present world and yearn for heaven? Deeming that this opportunity must not be missed, we heard that many of the people of the land were gathering at the valley stream of Mount Mikura, where the Kunitamagami is enshrined. We traveled from afar for the sake of propagation, and just as the majority were about to submit to our teachings and be saved in heaven, the Sendenshi of San-iku-kyō suddenly appeared on the scene, preached the doctrine of the physical being supreme and the spirit being subordinate, and once again overturned the Ural religion from its foundations. To overlook such a heretical religion, as Sendenshi of our Ural religion, is to have no words of apology to offer to our founder Tokoyohikonomikoto, nor to the Kyōshiyu of Uralhiko. In addition, to have suffered such a crushing defeat as we did yesterday in the forest of Arashi, is this not a matter of the utmost regret? ...Anan-dono, how do you think we should recover from this declining situation? I wish for you to speak without reservation.'

He asked this while peering intently. Anan sat with his hands clasped for a while, looking downward and lost in thought, but then he clapped his hands together with a sharp sound, smiled with a grin, and said,

Anan: 'Kyōshiyu-dono, if you entrust everything to me, I shall make not only the San-iku-kyō but also the followers of the Brahman religion submit, leaving not a single one behind. However, if we miss this opportunity, we will never be able to achieve our goal. If we wait even ten days, judging from today's weather, heavy rain will surely fall, and the mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees will instantly revive and turn green again, just as if one were looking into a mirror. Therefore, while there is still time, we must dispatch all the Sendenshi to the four directions and prey upon the weaknesses of the people of the land... "If you do not repent, you are now on the verge of ruin; repent of your hearts until now and entrust your bodies to the Ural religion. If you do so, we shall soon pray to heaven and pour down merciful rain, make the mountains, rivers, grasses, trees, and humanity drunk with the joy of revival, and manifest and bestow the pleasures of heaven upon the earth once again. Repent quickly, for heaven is the domain of those who believe in the Ural religion..." If we begin a lion-like, thunderous campaign at this time, I feel as if I can see with my own eyes the many people of the land casting aside the Brahman and San-iku religions, which have misled them until now, as if they were worn-out sandals, and competing to be the first to gather to our religion like a rising tide. If you would please entrust this to us, I am prepared to take off my coat and work as the leader of the Sendenshi, employing numerous Sendenshi to do so.'

Thus he spoke eloquently and joyfully, one word following another.

Būr: 'The defeat of our allies in the Forest of Storms, against a mere single Sendenshi—was it not truly a mortification beyond description? Even now, when I recall it, it is truly infuriating. Are you two not strong only before me, yet softening the moment you see the shadow of the enemy, making me fear you are mere followers of the so-called Kagebenkei? I cannot help but harbor some anxiety.'

Anan: 'Ahahaha! Even regarding this, do not worry in the slightest about our profound strategy, whose heavenly secrets must not be leaked. We will surely manifest a great victory and present it to your sight.'

Būr: 'If so, I trust you both and entrust everything to you. Be extremely careful.'

With that, he entered a private room. Afterward, the two looked at each other with thoughtful expressions,

Anan: 'Hey, Yūzu, this has truly turned into a troubling matter, has it not? Kaedewake no Mikoto is a divine general equipped with truly peerless divine might throughout the ages; Kotoyoriwake and Kuniyoriwake likewise possess unfathomable power. The five-colored spiritual lines emitted by those two possess a might that we can by no means approach. Moreover, Ishikuma of Brahmanism is quite a meticulous fellow; we must never let our guard down. I cannot help worrying: in this situation of a thousand riders to one, how can we possibly annihilate them?'

Yūzu: 'That is precisely why we exercised our wits before the Kyōshu-sama, framing various words and showing off our bravado to impart strength to the Kyōshu's heart. Under a brave general there are no weak soldiers. Making a weak general act effectively as a brave general is the mission of us two. Come, Anan-dono, from here on let us gather all the Sendenshi, and with hundreds of believers, first take advantage of the dark of night to raid the mansion of Kaedewake's Sendenshi. Are the preparations of long swords and bamboo spears complete?'

Anan: 'Though the gods permit the use of long swords and bamboo spears only in times of extraordinary emergency, is it not yet the time to stand up with weapons?'

Yūzu: 'Now, those words of yours lacking wits—is this situation of a thousand riders to one not precisely a great emergency? If we do not use weapons at such a time, when ever shall we use them? Even though the enemy is small in number, they are peerless heroes through the ages; bringing them to submission by mere verbal eloquence is quite unthinkable. Therefore, if we do not smash their stronghold with sudden violence, it is a grave matter concerning the grief and joy of the Ural Religion. This is where the life-or-death crisis becomes clear; the rise and fall of the Ural religious state is at this very moment. ...Come, Anan-dono, make your decision quickly.'

Anan: 'To the words of Yūzu-dono, renowned for peerless resourcefulness, I, Anan, shall give my assent.'

Yūzu: 'Your prompt consent is truly appreciated.'

With these words he rose from his seat, entered another room, summoned the Sendenshi gathered in the Sendenshi common room to his living room, and strictly ordered them to launch an attack without delay upon the night raid of Hille's mansion. Without a single word of dissent, all obeyed Yūzu's words, arranged their armaments, and marched grandly with drums beating and flags flying, together with hundreds of subordinates, heading toward the mansion of Kaedewake no Mikoto in the capital of Hille.

ユーズ『それだから、俺達はユーズを利かして、教主様の前でいろいろと言葉を構へ、威張つて見せ、教主の心に力を与へたのだ。勇将の下に弱卒なしだ。弱将をして能く勇将たらしむるは、両人の任務である。サア、アナン殿、これより宣伝使を全部引つれ、又数百人の信者を以て、先づ第一に楓別の宣伝使の館を夜陰に乗じ、襲撃することにせう。大刀竹槍の用意は出来て居るであらうか？』

アナン『大刀竹槍を使ふは変事に際してのみ用ゐることを、神明許させ玉へ共、未だ武器を以て立向ふべき時ではあるまいぞ』

ユーズ『さてユーズの利かぬ其お言葉、千騎一騎の此場合は即ち大変事のことでは御座らぬか？　斯様な時に武器を用ゐざれば、何れの時に用ゐむや。仮令敵は少数と雖も、古今無双の勇者、到底、口先の弁舌を以て帰順せしむることは思ひも寄らざる事なれば、短兵急に暴力を以て彼等の牙城を屠むらなくては、ウラル教の休戚に関する大問題だ。危急存亡の分るる所、ウラル教国家の興亡此時にあり。……サア、アナン殿、早く決心あれ』

アナン『智謀絶倫と聞えたるユーズ殿の言葉、アナン賛成致しませう』

ユーズ『早速の御承諾、実に有難し』

と座を立ち、別室に入り、宣伝使の溜り所に在る宣伝使を吾居間に呼集め、ヒルの館の夜襲に時を移さず着手せむ事を厳命した。一同は一も二もなく、ユーズの言葉に服従し、武装を整へ、ヒルの都の楓別命が館をさして、数百人の部下と共に、旗鼓堂々と進み行く。
