Chapter 11: Kotodama の妙 [853]

言霊の妙〔853〕

§ 1

Though the marvelous use of Kototama possesses absolute and infinite power—where a single voice can well shake the heavens and earth, and a single sound can well drive and chide wind, rain, thunder, and lightning—there is a tremendous divergence depending upon the righteousness or wickedness of the people who employ it. Until yesterday, those who opened Brahmanism, continued erroneous faith, whose minds were twisted, whose souls were clouded, and whose Kototama was clouded—no matter how perfectly and eloquently they might set forth beautiful words and upright speeches, they could not impart even a hair's breadth of that moving inspiration to all things in the universe. This is truly the reason why the divine law is stern and inviolable. Again, the Kototama of Sutegokohime—whose soul was well polished, rich in compassionate love, and whose heart ever possessed the virtue of tolerance—was extremely simple compared to the former. Yet, even the violent, lawless, and hideous great serpent could not in the least resist the Kototama, which issued forth from the sincere heart of universal protection and surged from the pure and immaculate lower abdomen of Sutegokohime, an immovable and inviolable presence. No matter how stubborn the evil deity might be, there was utterly no room for it to resist this; gradually its mind softened, the waves grew calm, the rain stopped, and the dark clouds enveloping the surroundings were successively swept away by the authority of the Kototama. Even from this, it may be understood that the divine treasure of Kototama—the supreme treasure of the divine realm—cannot be easily employed by a turbid body and soul. Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sakihai Mase.

Suekohime stood up with solemn sternness, and facing the water's surface, which had finally grown calm across its expanse, she began to chant in a clear voice. Her song:

How wondrous are these heavens and earth created by the true God! The heavenly August sky is an ocean of blue clouds, with a Milky Way whose depths are unknown. Beholding all creation, flowing purely and endlessly, the starlight sparkles with eternal and beautiful brilliance. When the August Sun Deity rises into the Eastern sky, and once again conceals His pure form in the West, at night the Great August Deity of the Moon casts forth a pure light, and drops the dew of blessing upon all things in the lower world. The sun and moon cross the heavens purely, like the countless grains of sand upon the shore. A hundred stars of dazzling radiance, some white and some red, intermingling pale and deep colors,

How noble is the decoration of the boundless firmament! Turning our eyes to view the fertile land of rice ears in Ashihara, the mountains and fields are lush and verdant, and the thousand grasses of the moorland bloom in a riot of blue, red, white, yellow, and purple—what joy! The flow of the rivers is exceedingly pure; the kinds of rice, wheat, beans, millet, and panicum grass ripen to the point of crowding the space, and delicious tree fruits in the fields and on the mountains are fragrant with branches heavy laden. Mirroring the divine realm of the heavenly august sky right upon this soil, the gods and men of the four quarters, trees and grasses, birds and beasts, down to the smallest of insect tribes, receive the divine fire and water, and are endowed with noble spirits,

Heaven and earth embrace each other, shadow and sunlight fold together, men and women affectionately unite, upper and lower know no separation, and opening their hearts to one another, they live in this world which is the land of gods, a living reflection of Takamanohara, the matching mirror of heaven and earth! Ah, Kannagarakamigami, may the august spirit of the gods bring blessing! Winds blow across, appearing in the pond of Tatsumi amidst the raging waves—O serpent deity, O living deity born of the divine fire and water! You are a child of God, a palace of God; I too am a child of God, a palace of God. Not only you and I, but also the mountains, rivers, trees, grasses, birds, beasts, large fish, small fish, and insect tribes shall not fail to be omitted from the divine grace,

How much more so is your noble form, far surpassing the human body, growing ever thicker and longer, dwelling upon the land and dwelling in the water, riding upon the clouds to soar and ascend the vast sky—while possessing this divine power by birth, why do you settle within this narrow pond and inflict evil calamities upon the people of the world? When the Great Deity God Susanoo proclaimed and transmitted the awe-inspiring Kototama regarding the Yamata-no-Orochi and hideous deities that coil throughout the land of Yashima, blowing away the mists of Ibuki and granting peace to all things—even just one of these divine works,

To fulfill it, to nurse all things with the milk of blessing, and to save them—Suekohime, accompanied by the princess Sutego, bearing a bud-like body of only sixteen years, exposed to the rain, lashed by fierce gales, treading upon frost, crossing through the snow, and at length arriving at the shore, crossing the surging waves of the raging ocean of Wada, finally passing over the land of Teru, climbing up and down the steep slopes of the Teru mountain pass on her own two feet, whipping her horse forward as a pair of travelers, possessing the delicate bodies of women, why do they cross over to this island at the ends of the earth? It is to aid and save the bodies and souls of the three realms of manifestation, the hidden, and the divine. Ah, Kannagarakamigami, the Kototama of the youngest princess born from the divine fire and water...

言霊の妙用は一声よく天地を震動し、一音よく風雨雷霆を駆使し叱咤する絶対無限の権力あれ共、之を使用する人々の正邪に依りて、非常なる径庭のあるものである。昨日迄バラモン教を開き、誤りたる信仰を続け、心は拗け、魂は曇り、言霊の曇りたる者は、如何に完全に、能弁に善言美詞を述べ立つればとて、万有一切に対し毫末も、其感動を与へざるは、実に神律の厳として冒す可らざる所以である。又魂よく研け慈愛に富み、心中常に寛容の徳ある捨子姫の言霊は、前者に比して極めて簡単なものであつた。されど暴悪無道の醜の大蛇も、厳として動かす可らざる捨子姫の清明無垢の臍下丹田より迸れる万有愛護の至誠より出でたる言霊には、如何に頑強なる邪神と雖も、到底之れに抵抗するの余地なく、漸く心和らぎ、浪静まり、雨は止みあたりを包む黒雲も次第に、言霊の権威に依つて払拭されて了つたのである。これにしても神界の最大重宝たる言霊の神器は、混濁せる身魂の容易に使用し得可からざる事を知らるるであらう。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世。

§ 2

Hearing everything fully and in detail, 'Do leave this pond, one day sooner, And ascend to Ame. Whatever sins there may be, Upon the shoulders of Kamususanō, who bears the thousand-fold offerings of absolution, Like spring snow instantly melting, Flowers are crimson and leaves are green. Let thy life feel Our Kototama, And right now, Abandoning this old nest, Return to thy original Mikura. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, May thy Mitama prosper and be blessed.' Thus the song ended.

Wonder of wonders, the pond water parted instantly to the left and right, and the form of a white dragon suddenly appeared; Suekohime advanced close to it, shedding tears of gratitude in a steady stream, bowing her head, and for a while remained prostrate without moving a limb. After a short while, the white dragon contracted its body until it finally became invisible to the eye. ── With the sound of music heard above their heads, as all looked up and gazed at the sky, numerous celestial beings descended dancing in joy over the dragon god's liberation, and guarding the dragon god of Tatsumi's pond—which had transformed into the figure of a most lovely goddess—they vanished high into the heavenly vault.
