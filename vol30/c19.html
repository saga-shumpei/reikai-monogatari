Chapter 19: 蜘蛛の児 [861]

蜘蛛の児〔861〕

§ 1

Fluttering white banners numbering no one knew how many tens in the night wind, and with banners and drums in grand array, viewing the hundreds of groups advancing from both sides of the riverbank, Kuniyoriwake laughed out loud,

Kuniyoriwake: 'Ahahaha, Machi-sama, Kiji-sama, things are getting interesting, aren't they? Last night in the Arashi Forest, hundreds of fellows of the Ural Church, while surrounding us alone ten and twenty layers deep, were easily scattered into the wind by Kuniyoriwake's Kototama, and those fellows of the Ural Church who fled like clouds and mist seem this time to have put their arms in order and come out in a rally of a grand comeback. What an entertaining thing has come forth! How about you two dividing yourselves into north and south for the sake of an initiation commemoration, engaging both sides of the enemy, and harassing them freely in all directions? In exchange, since Kuniyoriwake will grant infinite divine power, if by any chance you see any disadvantage for yourselves, there is every calculated assurance of shooting down the enemy with the divine power of the jewel, so shall we try it out experimentally?'

Kiji: 'That is a wish even without praying for it, my lord; though I may not possess equivalent divine power when it comes to the Land of Teru, I am a hero proclaimed to have no equal in physical strength, so there can hardly be any such interesting opportunity as this. No matter with what weapons they come to attack, with this single arm alone I am quite secure. Pray let me take charge of that duty.'

Machi: 'I do not possess strength like Kiji's, but when it comes to exercising wits, I intend never to fall behind others. If so, let both of us divide into the north and south of this river and try waging a fierce battle. Ah, how interesting, how interesting, I feel as though heavenly fortune has circulated and I have met the spring where Udonge flowers bloom and fragrance drifts.'

Thus rolling up arms covered with muscles of strength, swinging them round and round, and putting a twist into them.

Kuniyoriwake: 'If so, you two, try doing it once... Kuniyoriwake will become the commander-in-chief and make it his business to inspect the battle situation from atop this log bridge. Ah, what a fine moon. Such an entertaining thing scarcely ever occurs. Nevertheless, as much as possible, make sure not to take the lives of the enemy, you two.'

Kiji: 'Very well, I shall throw them into this dry riverbed without leaving a single one, and build a mountain of human beings for you to see.'

While saying thus, the large forces of the Ural Church had already drawn near. Machi hid her body in the grass on the north side, and Kiji in the grass on the south side, waiting for the enemy to approach as though it were now too late, making her arms hum, swallowing dry saliva, and holding back.

The southern detachment was led by Anan, and the northern detachment had Yūzu as its general, making a solemn spectacle as they descended the riverside with fierce momentum. Machi suddenly blocked the enemy's front and spoke with a loud voice,

Machi: 'Hey, hey, propagandists of the Ural Church, and you little brutes following them, listen well! I myself am a propagandist of the San-go-kyō, Izoriyoriwake-no-Mikoto; in the ravine of Mikurayama, you weak warriors blew bubbles at the divine power of my Kototama and fled away so brittle, come now, since we have known beforehand by divine decree that you are leading many people here, carrying weapons, and attacking downward, I shall pull out the bare necks of every single one of you without leaving a soul, and guide you across to the spirit realm, the heaven you yearn for most. Prepare yourselves!'

Thus she shouted aloud. Yūzu was put out of countenance by this sudden Kototama filled with authority from nowhere, and since he was in a position where, leading a multitude of subordinates, he had advanced grandly this far, retreat was also impossible, so forcing down his throbbing chest,

Yūzu: 'Wh-wh-what are you saying? Are you Izoriyoriwake-no-Mikoto, who venerates the heretical teaching of body-master and spirit-subordinate? However great the momentum of a lion king may be, against a great army like clouds and mist, and however much you may struggle alone in a solitary army, it is beyond the reach of your power; rather than throwing away your life by doing unnecessary things, surrender obediently to our military gate.'

Machi: 'What, insolent in the extreme! You yourself should bow your head in silence before someone, apologize for your crimes, and return safely with your precious life. If you fellows are those who regard reaching heaven as supreme glory, sending you off to the spirit realm without leaving a single one is a very easy thing indeed, but doing so would not serve as a disciplinary punishment. I shall make you suffer in this mundane world which you detest the most, prepare yourselves. Ahahaha!'

『アハヽヽヽ、マチさま、キジさま、面白くなつて来たぢやないか。昨夜荒しの森で数百人のウラル教の連中、吾々一人を十重二十重に取巻乍ら、脆くも国依別の言霊に吹散らされて、雲を霞と逃去つたウラル教の手合が、今度はどうやら武装を整へ捲土重来の挙に出よつたらしい。なんと愉快な事が出て来たものだ。お前達両人は、入信記念の為に一つ南北に分れて、両方の敵に当り、縦横無尽にかけ悩まして見たら如何だ。其代りに国依別が無限の神力を与へるから、万々一お前達の不利を見たならば、球の玉の神力を以て、敵を射倒して了ふ成算が十分にあるから、試験的にやつて見やうではないか？』

キジ『願うてもなき其お言葉、私もテルの国に於ては相当に、神力はなけれ共、腕力並ぶ者なしと言はれて居る豪傑ですから、こんな面白い機会はありますまい。如何なる武器を以て攻め来る共、此腕一つあれば大丈夫です。何卒私を其任に当らして下さい』

マチ『私はキジの様な力は有りませぬが、気転を利かす段に於ては、決して人後におちない積りです。そんなら両人が此川の南北に分れ、一つ奮戦激闘をやつて見ませう。あゝ面白い面白い、天運循環し来つて、優曇華の花咲き匂ふ春に会うたる心地がする』

と力瘤だらけの腕をまくり、ブンブンと振りまはし、撚をかけて居る。

国依『そんなら両人、一つやつて見よ……国依別は司令長官になつて、此丸木橋の上から戦況を調べる事にせう。あゝ良い月だ。こんな愉快な事は、滅多にあるものぢやない。併し乍ら成るべくは、両人敵の生命をとらない様にしてくれ』

キジ『宜しい、一人も残らず此空川へ放りこみ、人間の山を築いてお目にかけませう』

斯く云ふ間、ウラル教の大部隊は最早間近くなつて来た。マチは北側を、キジは南側の草の中に身を潜めて、敵の近付くのを今や遅しと、腕を唸らせ、片唾を呑んで控えてゐる。

南の一隊はアナン之を率ゐ、北側の一隊はユーズ之に将として、勢ひ凄まじく川辺を下り来る物々しさ。マチは忽ち敵前に塞がり大音声、

マチ『ヤアヤア、ウラル教の宣伝使、それに従ふ小童共、よつく聞け！　吾こそは三五教の宣伝使、言依別命、御倉山の谷間に於て、此方が言霊の神力に泡を吹き、脆くも逃げ去つたる弱武者共、サア吾々は神勅に依つて、汝が茲に数多の人々を引きつれ、武器を携へ、攻め下り来る事を前知せしを以て、汝等一人も残らず素首を引抜き、汝の最も希求する霊の国、天国へ引導を渡してくれむ。覚悟を致せ！』

と呶鳴り立てた。ユーズは藪から棒の、どこともなく権威に充された此言霊に辟易し、数多の部下を率ゐ、堂々とここまで進み来れる手前退却もならず、轟く胸を無理に押へ、

ユーズ『ナヽヽ何と申す。汝は体主霊従の邪教を奉ずる言依別命なるか。如何に獅子王の勢あればとて、雲霞の如き大軍に向つて、如何に孤軍奮闘すればとて、汝の力の及ぶ所にあらず、要らざる事を致して、命をすてるよりも、神妙に吾軍門に降れ』

マチ『ナニ猪口才千万な、汝こそ某が前に閉口頓首して罪を謝し、貴重なる生命を無事に持つて帰れ。汝等は天国に至る事を無上の光栄とする者ならば、一人も残さず、霊の国へやつてやるのはいと安き事なれ共、さう致しては懲戒にならぬ。汝の最も忌み嫌ふ娑婆世界に置いて苦めてやらむ、覚悟を致せ。アハヽヽヽ』

§ 2

Yūzu: 'What a strange thing you say,you! For human beings, life is precious. Do you think you can perform divine work without a physical body? Talk nonsense, thou art the one whose life I shall take right now, and I shall give thee the viaticum to Jigoku; prepare thyself... Hey, hey, Monodomo, charge and cut down Kotoyoriwake!'

Waving his baton, he gives the command, and the Amata Buka surround Machi Hitori, slashing at him. The opposing side is an Ōzei, while this side is Tadahitori; dodging nimbly, he hid himself in the thick grass. The Ōzei are panicked and flustered, sending sparks flying against each other's blades, starting a Dōshiuchi with a clatter—how ridiculous it is.

Again, Kiji, heading toward the southern bank, faces a unit led by Anan, suddenly appearing from the bushes, and with a Daionjiyōvoice,

Kiji: 'Hey, thou must be a propagandist of the Ural Kyō. I am none other than Kuniyoriwake, the divine minister of the Izumo Kyō. A summer insect flying into the fire, well indeed hast thou come out. Come, bathed in the Kototama bullets emitted from this side, I shall cause thee to abandon the Ningen world thou dost loathe most and save thee in heaven. How is that, art thou glad?'

So he shouted aloud, and laughed loudly with an air of indifference. Hearing that it was Kuniyoriwake, Anan, while trembling in his heart, put on a bold front,

Anan: 'Ahahahaha, what dost thou say, Kuniyoriwake? Pretending to be defeated, you hid your figure quickly in the valley river of Ogurayama, and again feigned a defeated army in the forest of Arashi, making it our strategy to lure you here—a demented person who carelessly came out and vanished. Now that it has come to this, it is your hundredth day; will you obediently surrender to our military gate, or if you blurt out nonsense, I shall pull off your head to cut off the harm to the world, and cast your Shinkai down into Jigoku... Come, Buka no monodomo, charge and cut down Kuniyoriwake.'

When he gives the command, answering 'Oh,' the Amata ninzu brandish their bamboo spears and charge with Kiji Hitori as their target. Kiji spreads his arms wide, and as every single fellow comes, he grabs them and hurls them plop into Higurashigawa, building a mountain of people of several tens in the twinkling of an eye.

Yūzu's Buka on this side are all fighting with long swords, and Anan's Buka in the south are all fighting with bamboo spears against Kiji Hitori. However, caught in Kiji's hands, already several tens have been thrown into the river, bruising their hips, breaking their legs, unable even to stir for the pain, and trembling in fear. Kuniyoriwake extends his fingers from the top of the log bridge, emitting a spiritual light like a searchlight, illuminating this region.

Thinking that Anan can no longer succeed, waving and swinging his baton,

Anan: 'Retreat! Retreat!'

Repeatedly calling out, they scatter and rout in all directions, losing their order.

Yūzu on this side loses his composure at the Dōshiuchi of his own allies, and having no other choice, waves his baton, and following Anan in the south, orders, 'Retreat! Retreat, retreat!' At this voice, Yūzu's Buka, each as they please, race ahead along the road they originally came and flee—how ridiculous it is. Viewing this, Machi laughs aloud,

Machi: 'Ahahahaha, how fragile they are. I did not think that even this much divine power would come out, but it is truly wondrous. This must also be entirely due to the divine virtue of the gods of the Izumo Kyō, and next, because I ate the divine fish used by the gods. It is truly a grateful thing. Well, well, I must report the battle situation in detail to Kuniyoriwake-sama. Even aside from that, when that intense light appeared from nowhere, the way they were flustered, struck by the enemy's light, was truly exhilarating.'

So murmuring to himself, he returned triumphantly to the side of Kuniyoriwake.

Kiji, who had headed toward the south, also with a triumphant look, swinging his arm full of muscle lumps, came back,

Kiji: 'Sendenshi-sama, thank you truly very much. Since I was born, there has never been such an enjoyable thing. In the midst of the battle, when you sent forth that intense spiritual light, the steadfastness and interest of it—oh, I have been given an enjoyable impression never to be forgotten for the rest of my life. Ahahahaha.'

ユーズ『何と其方は妙な事を申す奴、人間は生命が大切だ。肉体なくして神業が勤まると思ふか。馬鹿を申せ、汝こそ今某が、生命をとり、地獄の引導を渡してくれむ、覚悟致せ……ヤアヤア者共、言依別に向つて斬つてかかれよ』

と采配ふつて下知すれば、数多の部下はマチ一人を取まき、斬つてかかる。向うは大勢、此方は只一人、すばしこくも体を躱して、草の茂みに隠れて了つた。大勢はあわてふためき、互に刃に火花を散らし、ケチンケチンと同士打を始めてゐる其可笑しさ。

又一方南岸に向うたキジは、アナンの引率せる一部隊に向ひ、叢より忽然と現はれ、大音声、

キジ『ヤア其方はウラル教の宣伝使であらう。吾こそは三五教の神司国依別なるぞ。飛んで火に入る夏の虫、能くマア出て来よつたなア。サア是より此方の発射する言霊の弾丸を浴びて、汝の最も忌み嫌ふ現界を棄て天国に救ひ与へむ。どうぢや嬉しいか』

と呶鳴り立て、大声で人もなげに笑ひ出した。アナンは国依別と聞いて、心戦き乍ら、空元気を出し、

アナン『アハヽヽヽ、何と申す、国依別、敗けたと見せかけ御倉山の谷川に於て逸早く姿をかくし、又荒しの森に於てワザと敗軍を装ひ、汝をここにおびきよせむとの吾等が計略、うつかりと出てうせた痴呆者。モウ斯うなる上は百年目、神妙に吾軍門に降るか、ゴテゴテ吐さば、汝が素首引抜いて天下の害を絶ち、汝が身魂を地獄に追ひおとしくれむ……サア部下の者共、国依別に向つて斬りつけよ』

と下知すれば『オウ』と答へて数多の人数、竹槍をすごき、キジ一人を目当に突込み来る。キジは大手を拡げ、来る奴来る奴、ひつ掴んでは日暮シ川にドツと許り投げ込み、瞬く間に数十人の人山を築いた。

此方のユーズの部下は何れも長剣を以て戦ひ、南方のアナンの部下は何れも竹槍を以てキジ一人に向つて戦つてゐる。されどキジの手に掛つて、最早数十人は河中に投込まれ、腰を打ち、足を摧き、痛さに身動きも得せず、慄ひ戦いてゐた。国依別は丸木橋の上より指をさし伸べ、サーチライトの如き霊光を発射して、此域を射照らしてゐる。アナンは最早叶はじと思ひきや、采配を打ふり打ふり、

アナン『退却！　退却！』

と連呼し乍ら、散り散りバラバラに算を乱して敗走する。

此方ユーズは味方の同士打に度を失ひ、止むを得ず采配を打振り、又もや南方のアナンに傚つて『退却！　々々！』と号令する。此声にユーズの部下は思ひ思ひに元来し道を先を争ひ逃げて行く其可笑しさ。マチは之を眺めて高笑ひ、

マチ『アハヽヽヽ、脆いものだなア。俺もこれ丈の神力が出るとは思はなかつたが、実に不思議だ。これも全く三五教の神様の御神徳、次いでは神様のお使ひになる神魚を食つた為でもあらう。実に有難いものだなア。ドレドレ国依別様に戦況を詳さに報告せねばなろまい。それに付いても何処ともなく、強烈なる光の現はれた時、敵の光に打たれて狼狽する様は実に痛快であつた』

と独言ちつつ、国依別の傍に意気揚々として帰つて来た。

南方に向うたキジも亦勝ち誇つたる面色にて、力瘤だらけの腕を打ふり乍ら帰り来り、

キジ『宣伝使様、実に有難う御座いました。生れて以来、斯様な愉快な事は御座いませぬ。戦ひの最中、あなたより強烈なる霊光を送つて下さつた時の其気強さ、面白さ、いやモウ終生忘る可らざる愉快な印象を与へられました。アハヽヽヽ』

Kuniyori: 'Hey, you two, well done, splendid work! With that much courage, you'll be just fine as missionaries of Ananai-kyō. Kuniyoriwake is fortunate too. What wonderful disciples you've turned out to be!'

Kiji: 'Just as you say, given your peerless divine power and complete fullness of spirit, it is entirely by the arrangement of Kamisama that such heroes and gallants have become your disciples.'

Kuniyori: 'Ahahaha, you certainly know how to blow your own horn, don't you! Well, how amusing, you wouldn't be worth much without that kind of spirit!'

Machi: 'Even I shouldn't be considered too unsuitable a retainer for you. What about it, Sendenshi-san, what are your impressions...'

Kuniyori: 'Ahahaha, you're both so matched that if you lift a pole together it might snap. There's no difference in your strength, which makes it quite interesting. Well, from now on, I expect you to keep your pride in check and devote yourselves wholeheartedly to the divine realm.'

Both: 'Understood! Having realized through today's trial of strength that we already possess divine protection, what in all the world is there for us to fear?'

Shaking their arms and roaring with valor, how comical they were. Kuniyoriwake... thinking to himself, What strange fellows have fallen from the sky... smiled secretly as he led the two along the path of the dawning night, crossing the river and quickening their pace northward and ever northward, singing a sendenka in a loud voice as they headed toward the capital of Hiru.

The moon that had shone in the sky gradually faded in light, the stars successively sank their shadows, and the eastern sky, like an unfurled piece of painted silk, began to flush with crimson.

と腕を揺つて雄健びする其可笑しさ。国依別は……妙な奴が降つて来た者だ……と心秘かに微笑み乍ら二人を伴ひ、明けかかる夜の道を、河を渡つて、北へ北へと足を早め宣伝歌を声高く歌ひ乍ら、ヒルの都を指して進み行く。
