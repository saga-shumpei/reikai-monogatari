Chapter 50: The Emergence of the Dead Sea

第五〇章　死海の出現〔五〇〕
English Translation • § 1

Onikuma and Onihime had been defeated in their clash with Takekuma, becoming wicked spirits of Mount Ural and Lake Baikal and temporarily withdrawing into hiding, so that a period of relative tranquility returned to the Dragon Palace Castle.

English Translation • § 2

In recognition of their distinguished services, Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto conferred upon Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto and Wakahimegimi-no-Mikoto the exalted divine rank of Angels of the Spirit Realm (Reikoku Tenshi). Meanwhile, Takekuma harbored bitter hatred over the betrayal of Takasugiwake and Moritakahiko, scheming to destroy them in revenge; to achieve this, he felt it imperative first to eliminate the Angel Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto.

Having recovered the fortress of Eden, Takekuma enlisted Nakasakihiko and Ōtorahiko as generals with Ushikuma and Ushihime as advisors to mount a fresh rebellion, requesting reinforcements from Kitsunehime on Mount Kijō. Kitsunehime marshaled her captains Makihiko, Takahime, and Matsuyamahiko, plotting to pincer-attack the Angel Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto from front and rear.

English Translation • § 4

Warned by the secret intelligence of Sarutobihiko and Kikuhime, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto learned of the rebellious mobilization of Takekuma and Kitsunehime. He appointed generals Hanateruhiko, Hanateruhime, Kagawahiko, Hayakunihiko, Toyamahiko, and Sakurahiko to guard each gate of the Dragon Palace Castle; free from anxiety over his rear, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto departed for Mount Zion accompanied by generals Takasugiwake, Moritakahiko, and Jidaihiko to carry out the divine mandate of reporting the Great Deity's decree to the Heavenly Deities. Hearing the Mikoto's report, the Heavenly Deities resolved upon a divine strategy to chastise the treason of Takekuma and Kitsunehime. At that moment the heavenly angel Amenomeahiko-no-Mikoto descended from heaven onto the plateau of Mount Zion accompanied by a host of celestial warriors, declaring to Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto:

So speaking, he struck the ground with a golden head-mallet (Kabutsuchi); thereupon, auspicious sacred vapors arose from the earth of Mount Zion, soared high into the heavens, and descended once again before Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto. These are called the Kabutsuchi Jewels.

Thus bringing forth three sacred jewels with ringing resonance, Amenomeahiko-no-Mikoto departed, declaring, "Sweep away and annihilate the demon hordes with the power of these sacred spiritual essences!" and led the assembled deities as the celestial angel returned to heaven. Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto looked up toward heaven and prostrated himself upon the earth, offering deepest thanksgiving from his heart for this boundless, immense divine favor.

English Translation • § 8

Takekuma and Kitsunehime exerted all their strength, encircling the Dragon Palace Castle tightly from front, rear, left, and right on all four sides. The valiant Kagawahiko and the divine officers within the castle marshaled their full might to repel them; the surging demonic enemy host suffered heavy casualties, losing fully one third of their entire army. At that moment the spy Sagume secretly reported, "The Angel Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto is upon Mount Zion!" Hearing this, Takekuma and Kitsunehime wasted not a single moment; stirring up ominous dark clouds and summoning raging gales, they launched a furious, headlong assault toward the skies of Mount Zion.

English Translation • § 9

At this moment Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto took out one of the Kabutsuchi Jewels bestowed upon him by Amenomeahiko-no-Mikoto and hurled it high into the air toward the demon army led by Takekuma; thereupon, the jewel detonated with a thunderous roar, transforming into tens of thousands of yellow dragons that launched a ferocious assault upon Takekuma from front, rear, left, and right. In this ferocious aerial battle Takekuma was stripped of his divine power of flight and crashed miserably to the ground alongside all twelve genuine and counterfeit jewels, instantly transforming into the form of a black dragon and collapsing upon the earth. Before long Takekuma struggled to his feet, attempting with desperate effort to rally his forces for defense; at that very instant, from high in the heavens above, the two deities Konkatsukane-no-Kami and Hitsujihime-no-Mikoto hurled Heavenly Reverse Spears (Ame-no-Sakahoko) straight down aimed at Takekuma's head! One spear struck Takekuma directly in the head, and the other pierced deeply into his back; he fell prostrate on the spot, vomiting dark black blood, and met his wretched, final demise.

English Translation • § 10

The immense torrent of blood shed by Takekuma overflowed across the earth to become a vast body of water; this is known as the "Dead Sea." The sinister soul of Takekuma subsequently became the vengeful spirit of the Dead Sea. That the waters of the Dead Sea are bitter, salty, and distinctly viscous is due to the crystallization of the sacred spiritual essence of the Heavenly Reverse Spear combined with the clotted essence of the demon's blood. Takekuma's evil spirit later transformed anew into Bōfurihiko, relentlessly haunting and tormenting the Angel Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto on countless occasions. Furthermore, the evil spirits subordinate to Takekuma likewise all became wicked fiends dwelling in this lake. Their vengeful spirits spread far and wide throughout the entire world, inflicting diverse calamities and curses down to the generations of later ages. Their dreadful method is to lure human beings toward deep pools, rivers, ponds, and oceans, possessing them as spirits of death and dragging them down to drown. Therefore, unless the waters of this Dead Sea are thoroughly cleansed and purified through the divine enterprise of Misogi purification rites, the tragic loss of drowning victims across the world shall never cease.

現代日本語訳
竹熊が流した夥しい血は大地に溢れ返り、一つの巨大な湖水となりました。これを「死海（しかい）」と申します。竹熊の凶悪なる霊魂はその後、死海の怨霊となりました。死海の水が苦く、辛く、異様な粘着性を帯びているのは、天の逆鉾の神聖なる精気と、凝固した悪魔の血の精とが結晶したためであります。竹熊の悪霊は再び姿を変えて「棒振彦（ぼうふりひこ）」となり、天使・大八洲彦命を執念深く幾度となく悩まし苦しめました。また竹熊配下の悪霊たちもことごとくこの湖水の邪鬼となりました。そしてその怨霊の群れは世界中に広く拡散し、後世の末に至るまで様々な災厄や祟りをなすに至ったのであります。その恐るべき方法は、深い淵や河川、池や大海原などに人々を誘い込み、死神となって取り憑いて水底に引きずり込み溺死させるのであります。ゆえにこの死海の湖水を神聖なる禊身（みそぎ）の神業をもって清め祓わなければ、この世界から溺死者の絶えることは決してないでありましょう。

English Translation • § 11

Against the most ferocious demonic army of Kitsunehime, which came surging like a raging tide from the skies behind Mount Zion, Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto hurled the second Kabutsuchi Jewel bestowed upon him high into the heavens. Thereupon the jewel instantly detonated with tremendous roaring force; Kitsunehime's entire army, terrified before the awesome divine majesty, reached the very height of panic and crashed down upon the summits of the barren mountains standing steep around the Dead Sea, their bodies dashed upon the sharp rocky crags and meeting a miserable demise. Kitsunehime's evil spirit transformed anew into Takatorahime; all the circumstances of how she joined hands with Bōfurihiko in an attempt to thoroughly torment and vex Ōyashimahiko-no-Mikoto shall become fully apparent as they are progressively recounted in later volumes.

English Translation • § 12

The ten genuine sacred jewels and the two counterfeit jewels formerly in Takekuma's possession sank once to the deep bottom of the Dead Sea; yet as long years elapsed, they gradually arose into the sky as ominous spiritual vapors, plummeted down across various lands of the world, scattered malignant spiritual miasma, and brought severe affliction and torment upon every living creature. Even the ten sacred divine jewels of such supreme sanctity that had emerged from the Golden Water were corrupted into evil spirits through defilement by Takekuma's unholy blood, transforming into sinister malignant orbs scattered across diverse nations that manifest all manner of evil deeds. The lands upon which these tainted jewels were scattered became the territories cursed with the most unwholesome, degraded Kunitama (national guardian spirits) in all the world.

English Translation • § 13

Parting the dense clusters of billowing clouds from a corner of heaven, countless celestial angels numbering in the hundreds and thousands appeared, gradually descending toward the earth... and at that very instant, Zuigetsu's body suddenly felt piercing, extreme cold; and when he started and opened his eyes, he found himself standing before the rock cavern of Mount Takakuma, exposed to the biting, freezing winds.

天空の一方から群がる村雲を押し開いて幾百千とも知れぬ無数の天使の群れが現れ、地上へと次第に降りてくるのが見えた……その刹那、瑞月（王仁三郎）の身体はたちまち極度の寒気を感じ、ふと目を覚まして見れば、我が身は高熊山の巌窟の前において身を切るような寒風に吹き晒されていたのでありました。
原文（近代文語）
天の一方より村雲押開きて天使の群、幾百千となく現はれ、地上に漸次降りくるよと見るまに、瑞月の身体はたちまち極寒を感じ、ふと眼を開けば、身は高熊山の巌窟の前に寒風に曝されてゐた。
