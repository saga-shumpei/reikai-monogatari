Chapter 39: The Fate of the White Jewel

第三九章　白玉の行衛〔三九〕
English Translation • § 1

The twelve sacred jewels born from the essence of the Golden Water possessed no efficacy whatever if employed individually. However, neither the twelve divine officers who had picked them up nor the Takekuma faction were aware of this truth; each party remained firmly convinced that possessing one jewel yielded one measure of power, and possessing two yielded two measures of supernatural virtue.

原文（近代文語）

English Translation • § 2

Takekuma accordingly plotted first to obtain the white jewel held by Tayorihiko. Realizing, however, that persuading Tayorihiko to yield the jewel willingly would be no easy matter, Takekuma devised a clever trap: using material greed, he seduced Makohiko—the man whom Tayorihiko trusted above all others—to serve as his co-conspirator. Makohiko was a remarkably handsome youth. Furthermore, Tayorihiko had an elder sister named Kusakahime, the wife of Mametora, who was likewise a woman of extraordinary beauty. Kusakahime had long harbored a secret romantic longing for Makohiko.

大虎彦の手に入った宝玉は、やがて竹熊の手へと渡されました。竹熊は悪計が後になって露見することを恐れ、道中で大虎彦に命じて魔子彦を亡き者にさせたのでした。悪霊の企む仕組とは、実にどこまでも狡猾で陰険極まりないものなのであります。
原文（近代文語）
大虎彦の手に入つた玉は、やがて竹熊の手に渡された。竹熊は謀計の後に破れむことを恐れて、中途に大虎彦をして魔子彦を亡ぼさしめたのである。悪霊の仕組は実にどこまでも注意深い、いやらしきものである。
