Chapter 44: The Green-Haired Tortoise

第四四章　緑毛の亀〔四四〕
English Translation • § 1

Kamewaka guarded the green jewel with his very life. Caring nothing for prestige or material wealth, he focused his entire soul solely upon safeguarding this jewel. However, at the banquet in the Eight-Fathom Hall, Takekuma's trickery had forced numerous poisonous vermin down his throat; his health was fatally impaired, his body turned yellowish-green, and he passed into the unseen realm. Kamewaka's wife Kamehime wept bitterly to heaven and earth, crawling at his feet and head in ceaseless mourning; her cries of grief echoed on the winds, bringing unending somber gales and dreary rain for a hundred days and nights.

Takakurahiko deliberately composed himself, washed his hands, and rinsed his mouth; facing heaven, he joined his palms together in prayer for a long time, as though entreating a divine oracle. On his sickbed, Kamewaka groaned incessantly in agony, already in the throes of death. Kamehime's heart was torn with unbearable distress; she resolved that even if she must lose her own life, she must save the life of her most beloved husband Kamewaka. Meanwhile, when she looked at Takakurahiko, he prayed leisurely toward heaven without the slightest sign of haste. Having concluded his prayer at his own unhurried pace, Takakurahiko stepped into the chamber. At this moment Kamehime waited in agonizing suspense for Takakurahiko's instruction, as a parched soul yearns for water. Thus he instructed her with plausible solemnity. Rejoicing with high courage as though receiving aid from heaven, Kamehime immediately constructed a high platform and enshrined the green jewel at its center. At that very instant the entire sky abruptly darkened, and dense black clouds enveloped heaven and earth so that one could not distinguish an inch ahead. At that critical moment, a black dragon emerged from within the clouds, seized the jewel in its claws, and vanished into the western sky! After several days, this jewel passed into Takekuma's possession. The false Kamewaka whom she had believed to be her husband suddenly transformed into a giant dragon; likewise Takakurahiko returned to his true form as the great furnace of Galilee, stirring up clouds and vanishing from sight, leaving Kamehime behind. Kamehime stamped her feet in bitter mortification, and her grieving spirit condensed until she transformed into a green-haired tortoise (Ryokumō-no-Kame) and plunged into the sea of the Dragon Palace. It is said that tortoises live for ten thousand years. Kamewaka was fed poisonous vermin at the banquet in the Eight-Fathom Hall and died young as a result; ever since, tortoises born from Kamehime's spirit pay strict heed to hygiene, never eat poisonous vermin, and maintain extraordinary longevity.

原文（近代文語）
と誠しやかに教示した。亀姫は天の佑けと喜び勇んで直ちに高台を造り、その玉を中央に安置した。その刹那一天たちまち掻き曇り、黒雲濛々として天地をつつみ、咫尺を弁ぜざるにいたつた。時しも雲中に黒竜現はれ、その玉を掴みて西方の天に姿をかくした。数日を経てこの玉は、竹熊の手に入つたのである。今まで夫と思ふてゐた偽の亀若は、にはかに大竜と変じた。また高倉彦はガリラヤの大なる竈に還元し、亀姫を後に残して雲をおこし姿をかくした。亀姫は地団駄踏んで侮しがり、精魂凝つて遂に緑毛の亀と変じ竜宮海に飛び入つたのである。亀は万年の齢を保つといふ。亀若は八尋殿の宴会において毒虫を食はせられ、それがために短命にして世を去つた。それから亀姫の霊より出でし亀は、衛生に注意して毒虫を食はず、長寿を保つことになつた。
