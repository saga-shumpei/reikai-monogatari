Chapter 14: Journey through the Divine Realm (Part 1)

第一四章　神界旅行の一〔一四〕
English Translation • § 1

Advancing along a narrow, gourd-like path all by myself with a sense of urgent haste, I suddenly heard the shouts of dozens of people echoing from the mountains behind me, calling out without naming anyone specifically.

現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）

English Translation • § 2

Looking back, though I thought I had advanced a few hundred yards, without my realizing it I had returned once more to the original Crossroads of Destiny. There lay a fallen man with a blackened, filthy face who appeared to be falling into hell. His physical body had just expired in the manifest world, and his astral form had collapsed here, while the loud shouts heard earlier were the cries of his relatives calling back his soul (tama-yobi). Even as I watched, the earth cleaved open, and the pitch-black form of this man in his mid-thirties plunged hundreds of fathoms straight into the bowels of the earth! This puzzled me immensely, for there ought to be an established road leading to hell, yet he plunged straight into the abyss with rapid momentum. In worldly medical terms, such a person would appear to have suffered sudden death from cerebral congestion, apoplexy, or heart failure without leaving a will. Thereupon, I blew upon the natural flute, and amidst celestial light, Fuyō Sennin manifested from the heavens.

現代日本語訳
そこで何となく後を振り返って見ると、もう二、三町も歩いて来たと思ったのに、いつの間にかまた元の八衢に戻っていました。そこには地獄へ堕ちていく者と見えて、真っ黒な汚い顔をした男が倒れ伏していました。これは現界で肉体が息を引き取ったばかりの者で、その幽体がこの場所に横たわったのであり、先ほどの大声は親族たちが魂呼びをしている声であったと分かりました。そうして見ている間に、その真っ黒な三十五、六歳の男の姿が、地が裂けると同時に何百丈とも知れぬ地の底へと急転直下に墜ち込んでいきました。これが私には不思議でなりませんでした。地獄へ行くにも相当の道筋があるはずなのに、いきなり地の底へ墜落していったからです。現界から見れば脳溢血や心臓麻痺などで遺言も遺さず急死したような状態です。そこで私が天然笛を吹いてみると、天の一方から光となって芙蓉仙人が現れてくださいました。
原文（近代文語）

English Translation • § 5

“In both his past and present lives, this man committed evil deeds, and especially incurred grave sin by demolishing the shrine of the local clan deity. Deeming it an old shrine, he bought it cheaply, sold off the metal fittings, burned the timbers, or used them as firewood. Within a week he was struck with illness resembling the black plague. Consequently, the instant he drew his last breath, the earth cleft open and he plummeted into the bottomless abyss. This represents the heaviest sin in hell: he died agonizing, vomiting blood and froth and clutching at empty air, and fearing contagion, worldly officials doused his corpse in kerosene and incinerated it.”

English Translation • § 9

“When a mortal dies, the soul passes through the sequence from Dying Existence (Shi'u) to Intermediate Existence (Chū'u), and from Intermediate Existence to Arising Existence (Shō'u). The instant breath ceases in the manifest world, Dying Existence begins, and the transition to Intermediate Existence occurs almost simultaneously. For forty-nine days the soul remains in Intermediate Existence; on the fiftieth day begins Arising Existence, where one's spiritual parents and spiritual siblings are determined. In that sphere, natural mountains, rivers, trees, and houses exist, though unseen by mortal eyes; the soul is reduced to the size of a three-year-old child, and feelings of spiritual kinship awaken instinctively.”

現代日本語訳
『すべての人は死ぬと、死有から中有へ、中有から生有へという順序をたどる。現界で息を引き取ると死有となり、死有から中有になるのは殆ど同時である。それから四十九日の間を中有と言い、五十日目から生有と言って霊的な親や兄弟が定まる。そこには山河や草木や家屋など万有はあるが目には見えず、幽体は三歳の童子のように小さくなり、中有になると同時に親子兄弟の情愛が霊的に湧いてくるのである』
原文（近代文語）

Involuntarily opening my eyes, the former horrors had vanished, replaced by an incomparably magnificent jewel-throne towering before me! In that very instant, hearing the whispering wind in the pines, I realized to my astonishment that I was sitting upright upon the Toad Rock of Mount Takakuma!
