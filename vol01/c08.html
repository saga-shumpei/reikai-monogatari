Involuntarily, I chanted twice: “Amaterasu-Ōmikami, Ubusuna-no-Kami, Kamunagara Tamachihaemase!” Miraculously, he was restored to his original handsome form, crawled out of the puddle, and thanked me with a joyous countenance. So the voice bellowed. A deafening clamor arose, and hundreds of giant-mouthed monstrosities charged straight toward us. Trapped without escape, we were thrown into panic. No matter how fervently I chanted the sacred name, they did not retreat in the slightest but pressed closer. Mysteriously, the beasts now bore human faces. Among them, a gigantic chieftain brandishing a great sword lunged toward us; just as we were about to be cut down, the golden-skinned Goddess in white robes materialized once more amidst brilliant light. “Wave the sacred hire!” she commanded, and vanished. Drawing the sacred cloth from my bosom, I swung it up, down, left, and right. The monsters steadily retreated. Yet before we could rejoice, a monstrous serpent suddenly appeared, opened its immense maw, and swallowed us both whole! We groped our way forward through the serpent’s belly. Our bodies, which had suffered so grievously from the cold, felt as though soaking in a warm hot spring. Then, with a thunderous roar, we plunged down hundreds of thousands of feet into the bottomless abyss!

Countless tens of thousands of frozen icicle pillars stood like pillars around me. We realized we had plummeted straight down from that towering cataract to the subterranean floor. If we moved even a fraction of an inch, the razor-sharp blades of ice would pierce our flesh. Unable even to rise, I looked toward my companion Funaki and saw that his torso was pierced through by a long, jagged sword of ice, like a fish upon a skewer, and he was writhing in agony! Gathering every ounce of my strength, I gasped out brokenly, syllable by syllable: “A-ma-te-ra-su-O-ho-mi-ka-mi-sa-ma!” Divine grace manifested instantly: freedom of movement returned to both my body and Funaki’s. The roaring waterfall vanished into thin air, transformed into a vast, desolate wilderness of unbroken snow!

English Translation • § 20

Protruding from the snow were the hands, feet, and heads of hundreds of frozen souls. Suddenly, with a sound like a collapsing mountain, an avalanche of snow cascaded upon me, burying my entire body! I tried frantically to wave the sacred hire, but my hands had frozen stiff like solid iron and refused to obey. Pouring my soul into each syllable, I chanted: “Kamunagara Tamachihaemase!” Fortunately, movement returned to my limbs. Looking around, I saw Funaki buried under the snow with only his hair visible. Waving the sacred hire over him two or three times—left, right, left—Funaki emerged from the snow with a look of anguish. From the heavens, the golden light appeared once more, illuminating our surroundings.
