Genesis (Origin of the Mission)

発端（ほったん）
English Translation • § 1

Ever since I was guided by a divine messenger on February 9, Meiji 31 (1898) to Mount Takakuma in Anao, Tanba, to complete a week of spiritual ascetic discipline—thereby mastering the essentials of Divine Clairvoyance (Tengantsū), Clairaudience (Ten'nitsū), Universal Mind-Reading (Jitashintsū), Heavenly Speech (Tengontsū), and Knowledge of Past Lives (Shukumeitsū)—bringing the divine teachings of the deities to where they stand today has been fraught with ceaseless turbulence and infinite tribulations. Revolts of former elders, desertion of followers, misunderstandings from authorities, persecutions by sectarian religionists, coordinated onslaughts by relatives and friends, along with heated vitriol and mockery in newspapers, magazines, and books—words on paper can scarcely capture it all. Here, I briefly recall the course of these twenty-four years since the founding of our teachings and present a glimpse of it from memory.

Within Ryūgūkan (The Dragon Palace Hall), two supreme lineages of divine providence are distinctly divided: the Divine Lineage of the Henjō-Nanshi (Male-Transforming Female Spirit / Spiritual Masculine in Female Flesh) and that of the Henjō-Nyoshi (Female-Transforming Male Spirit / Spiritual Feminine in Male Flesh). The Henjō-Nanshi proclaimed prophecies and warnings of the advent of the Divine Theocracy, transmitting divine revelations amidst untold hardships, administering the baptism of water to body and soul, and awaiting the rebirth and second advent of the Savior. Just as John cried in the wilderness for nearly seven years before encountering Christ, the physical temple of Henjō-Nanshi, possessing a female form endowed with male spirit, engaged in the sacred work of the Izu-no-Mitama (Spirit of Divine Severity) at the age of fifty-seven. From New Year's Day of Meiji 25 unto New Year's Day of Meiji 45—spanning twenty full years—through the baptism of water, she purified all corrupted physical and spiritual systems of this world and manifested the divine strategy for cosmic renewal. The Great War in Europe was indeed an eruption of this sacred work of Izu-no-Mitama and a profound warning unto the three thousand realms.

English Translation • § 12

Unless one truly understands the actual divine administration, it is impossible to fully minister unto this unprecedented and supreme sacred mission. In the Divine Revelation, there is mention of the rebuilding and rectification of body and soul (Mitama). When hearing 'Mitama,' many seem to assume it refers solely to the soul. However, 'Body' (Mi) designates the physical body or the material realm, while 'Soul' (Tama) designates the spirit, heart, mind, and the divine realm. Throughout the cosmos, spirit is primary and fundamental, whereas the physical form is secondary and subordinate. The execution of the renovation of the physical, material realm is commanded by the Ancestral Deity Ōkunitokotachi-no-Kami, while the radical renovation of the mental, spiritual realm is the divine authority of Toyokuninushi-no-Kami. Therefore, in all cosmic reality, the spiritual realm is primary and the manifest realm is subordinate; hence this is proclaimed as **Spirit Dominates Body (Reishutaijū)**.
