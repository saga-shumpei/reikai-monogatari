Chapter 24: The Foundation of the Divine Age and the Integration of Divine Breaths

第二四章　神世開基と神息統合〔二四〕
English Translation • § 1

In the Divine Realm, Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto manifested as the Izu-no-Mitama (Spirit of Divine Severity), employing the Naohi-no-Mitama (Direct Spirit) Henjō-Nanshi (Male-Transforming Female Spirit) as his instrument for propagating divine theocracy; while Toyokumono-no-Mikoto utilized the Spirit of Divine Breath Integration as her vehicle, both manifesting at the Ryūgūkan (Dragon Palace Hall) to repair and solidify the three thousand realms from the earthly Takama-ga-hara.

現代日本語訳
神界においては国常立尊が「厳（いづ）の御魂」として顕現され、神政を発揚する「直（なほひ）の御魂・変性男子（へんじょうなんし）」を機関とし、豊雲野尊は「神息統合の御魂」を機関として、地の高天原より三千世界を修理固成するために竜宮館へと御出現になられました。
原文（近代文語）
神界においては国常立尊が厳の御魂と顕現され、神政発揚直の御魂変性男子を機関とし、豊雲野尊は神息統合の御魂を機関とし、地の高天原より三千世界を修理固成せむために竜宮館に現はれたまうた。
English Translation • § 2

In the Dragon Palace Realm, the dragon goddess Otomehime-no-Mikoto, who had endured three millennia of excruciating trials, manifested by taking birth through the womb of the Henjō-Nanshi lineage, offering all her rare treasures to take part in the second cosmic rectification of the world. Among all dragon deities, Otomehime-no-Mikoto had originally been the most avaricious, consumed entirely by self-interest and personal greed. Lurking deep in the trenches of the modern Pacific, she was struck by fierce submarine volcanic eruptions, suffering not only the torment of alternating extreme heat and cold daily, but having her flesh scorched by virulent toxic heat; unable to endure the agony, she resolutely abandoned all past worldly desires, perceived the magnificent restorative mission of Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, and became the very first deity to surrender and pledge allegiance unto him.

現代日本語訳
竜宮界においては、三千年の長きにわたる艱難辛苦を嘗め尽くした竜神の乙米姫命（おとめひめのみこと）が、変性男子の系統の肉体の胎を借りて出現し、二度目の世の立替えの神業に参加すべく、所有する一切の珍宝を献上されました。この乙米姫命は、竜神たちの中でも最も強欲で貪欲な神であり、自らの欲望ばかりを追求する極めて利己的な神でした。彼女は現在の太平洋の海底深く潜伏していましたが、海底の至る所から猛烈な火山噴火が巻き起こるのに遭遇し、身には日々三寒三熱の責め苦を受けるばかりでなく、猛毒の熱気に焼かれ、その激痛に耐えかねて過去のすべての欲望をきっぱりと捨て去り、国常立尊の修理固成の大神業を悟って、真っ先に帰順した神であります。
原文（近代文語）
竜宮界においては、三千年の長き艱難苦労を嘗めた竜神の乙米姫命は、変性男子の系統の肉体の腹をかりて現はれ、二度目の世の立替の御神業に参加すべく、すべての珍宝を奉られた。この乙米姫命は、竜神中でも最も貪婪強欲な神であつて、自分の欲ばかりに心を用ひてゐる、きはめて利己主義の強い神であつた。それが現代の太平洋の海底深く潜んでゐたが、海底の各所より猛烈な噴火の出現するに逢ひ、身には日々三寒三熱の苦しみを受けるばかりでなく、その上に猛烈な毒熱を受けて身体を焼かれ、苦しみにたへずして従来の凡ゆる欲望を潔く打ち棄てて、国常立尊の修理固成の大業を感知し、第一番に帰順された神である。
English Translation • § 3

Thus all gold, silver, gems, and treasures were conveyed to the seabed by various dragon retinues, forming a mountainous hoard upon the ocean floor. Though located in the deepest abyss of the world, in the destined hour through the activation of the divine work, it shall be manifested upon the dry land. In essence, these are merely material treasures which may serve as auxiliary materials for the divine mission, but can never constitute the true treasure of salvation. When Otomehime-no-Mikoto first surrendered, she brought those riches unto the Great God; yet because the Great God possesses the true, supreme spiritual treasure of Sincerity (Makoto), things that appear splendid to mortal eyes are scarcely prized in the Divine Realm. Nevertheless, cherishing the sincere heart of her repentance in renouncing all the treasures she had valued more than life itself, he pardoned her past transgressions. Having reformed her heart, cast aside all wealth, discerned the true divine will, and discovered a treasure surpassing even the Mani Jewel, she attained the exalted station of a divine being, manifesting as the consort of Hinode-no-Kami (the Rising Sun Deity).

English Translation • § 4

Next, the Golden Deity of the Earth, Kanekatsu-Kaname-no-Kami, who had been thrust into the darkest, most defiled depths beneath the earth, was liberated from all torment as celestial fortunes rotated upon the reappearance of Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, manifesting at the earthly Ryūgūkan for universal salvation. This divine being is the fifth daughter of Wakahimegimi-no-Mikoto. This Kanekatsu-Kaname-no-Kami seized supreme authority over the central core of the earth to accomplish the grand work of cosmic repair and solidification, dedicating it unto Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, who presides over universal spiritual governance.

It was divinely ordained that the Mizu-no-Mitama should serve as the auxiliary aid to Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto's sacred work, acting hand in hand with Kanekatsu-Kaname-no-Kami under the decree of heaven and earth. This was the state of affairs in the Divine Realm millennia ago in high antiquity, when separate worldly nations had not yet been established and the entire earth was unified as one.

English Translation • § 6

Thereupon, the deities of the Banko-Daijin (Shionagahiko) lineage and the Daijizaiten (Ōkunihiko) lineage gathered legions of demonic gods, storming forward frantically to shatter the divine plan of the Great Deity and conquer earthly Takama-ga-hara. However, to attack earthly Takama-ga-hara, they had to cross the mighty River Jordan. Across the Jordan spanned the Great Golden Bridge, which revealed the true nature of good and evil at a single glance. Thus, their very first priority was to destroy this colossal bridge. The Banko-Daijin faction advanced led by Musashihiko—a gigantic black serpent. Next appeared Harukohime, an evil deity in the form of a malevolent fox, followed by Ashinagahiko, a fierce demon, throwing their full might into destroying the Great Golden Bridge.

However, because this great bridge was rooted in the primordial bedrock of the earth's foundation, it could not be easily destroyed. Driven to their wits' end, the evil deities realized they must capture Kanekatsu-Kaname-no-Kami, the supreme spirit of the earth's depths. To this end, using every insidious stratagem, they unleashed swords of the tongue and spears of the brush alongside all manner of spiritual weapons to relentlessly assault the Mizu-no-Mitama; simultaneously, donning masks of righteous deities, they slandered the Mizu-no-Mitama before the Izu-no-Mitama, attempting to instigate an expulsion campaign against him. Reflecting carefully, the Izu-no-Mitama saw through the wicked plot in the hearts of the evil gods and instantly rejected their demands. At that moment, the true identities of Ashinagahiko the demon, Harukohime the malevolent fox, and Musashihiko the giant serpent were illuminated by the Divine Mirror, exposing their deceit; scattering like mist across mountains and seas, one fled swiftly to the northern realms, one to the southwest, and one far into the western lands.

要するにヨハネは神界、幽界の修理固成の神業には、月の精なる水を以てせられ、キリストは世界の改造にあたり、火すなはち霊をもつて神業に参加したまふのである。故にキリストは、かへつてヨハネの下駄を直すにも足らぬものである。ヨハネは神界、幽界の改造のために聖苦を嘗められ、キリストは世界の人心改造のために身を犠牲に供し、万人に代つて千座の置戸を負ひて、聖苦を嘗めたまふ因縁が具はつてをられるのである。これは神界において自分が目撃したところの物語である。
English Translation • § 15

When the three realms are fully repaired and solidified, the Izu-no-Mitama of John shall manifest as the Miroku-Ōkami (Maitreya Supreme Deity), while Christ ministers to the sacred governance of Miroku. Therefore, Christ acts on the visible forefront of the world's spiritual stage, while behind the scenes, John safeguards Christ’s sacred person, ushering in the Divine Age.

現代日本語訳
そしてヨハネの「厳の御魂」は、三界を修理固成された暁には「五六七（みろく）大神」として顕現され、キリストはその五六七神政の神業に奉仕されるのであります。それゆえキリストは世界の精神界の表面に立って活動し、その裏面においてヨハネがキリストの聖体を守護しながら、真の神代を招来されるのであります。
原文（近代文語）
そしてヨハネの厳の御魂は、三界を修理固成された暁において五六七大神と顕現され、キリストは、五六七神政の神業に奉仕さるるものである。故にキリストは世界の精神上の表面にたちて活動し、裏面においてヨハネはキリストの聖体を保護しつつ神世を招来したまふのである。
English Translation • § 16

To see with ears and hear with eyes, To eat with nose and smell with mouth— Unless one transcends thus, God cannot be known.

現代日本語訳
耳で見て、目で聴き、鼻でものを食べ、口で匂いを嗅ぐようでなければ、神の真実を悟ることはできない。
原文（近代文語）
耳で見て目できき鼻でものくうて　口で嗅がねば神は判らず
English Translation • § 17

Ears, eyes, mouth, and nose all perceiving, Hands, feet, head, and belly fully awake— Such is the boundless spirit of the Eight-Eared One.

現代日本語訳
耳も目も、口も鼻もすべてが通じ、手足も頭も腹までもがことごとく通じるのが、真の「八ツ耳（八方の神意を察知する全知全能の霊覚）」である。
原文（近代文語）
耳も目も口鼻もきき手足きき　頭も腹もきくぞ八ツ耳
