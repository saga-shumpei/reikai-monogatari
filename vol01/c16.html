Chapter 16: Journey through the Divine Realm (Part 3)

第一六章　神界旅行の三〔一六〕
English Translation • § 1

To use the metaphor of a folding fan, I felt as though I had crossed the ribs and reached the white paper surface. Breathing a sigh of relief, I lay down upon the nearby turf to rest for a while. Just then, from far away to the north, a thin, faint, sorrowful voice like the whining of a mosquito echoed eerily, calling out to me, “Ohoi! Ohoi!” While I was lost in thought over what to do, four or five voices called out from behind me to the south to halt me. They sounded somewhat like the voices of my mother, my grandmother, and my neighbors. Suddenly drawn by those voices from the south, before I knew it, my physical body had returned to my home in Anao.

現代日本語訳

English Translation • § 9

Casting aside all worldly attachments, I advanced ever northward in accordance with the divine command, only to find myself unexpectedly returned to the original Crossroads of Heaven (Yachimata). Thinking that I had made a regretful turn, I glided swiftly along the path I had come, traversing the fan-shaped road until I reached the white paper expanse once more. At that moment, 'Kou' came escorting me, still naked, as far as halfway across the paper of the white fan, but then vanished without a trace. Still as before, that thin, sorrowful, eerie voice echoed in the distance. Drawn as though magnetized by electricity, my body surged northward and ever northward. On one side flowed a mighty river, lined with picturesque ancient pines along its banks; on the left, sheer precipitous cliffs towered aloft, forming a narrow choke point between mountain and river through which one must pass. Arriving at that bottleneck, a man and a woman abruptly poked their heads out from beneath the earth, gradually revealing their entire bodies, and blocked the narrow defile to bar my passage.

From somewhat to the southwest, another tremendous shrieking voice resounded. Immediately following the sound, I found an old blind man possessed by a badger spirit, surrounded by a multitude of vengeful spirits who gouged his eyes, hoisted his body into midair, and mercilessly tortured him. Beneath the old man's shoulders rested a pole to which ropes were fastened; a throng of spirits gathered around a thick cable attached to a central pillar, alternately slackening and hoisting it. Whenever he plummeted, he was plunged down to the bottom of the river abyss; whenever hoisted aloft, he was scorched by the blazing solar heat. The terrifying speed with which he was hauled up and dashed down was dizzying. This old man was named 'Yoko'.

English Translation • § 23

Inquiring as to why he suffered such horrific retribution, it was revealed that this man had been exceedingly greedy during his earthly life. Loaning money at usurious rates, he seized houses and estates as collateral, foreclosing upon countless dozens of homes to amass vast personal wealth. Driven to despair by his ruthless avarice, numerous victims had cast themselves into wells, hanged themselves, or had their families and siblings torn apart. Because of their fierce, unquenchable malice, all those souls had plunged into the realm of beasts to join the packs of foxes and badgers; now, all those living wraiths and deceased spirits were attacking him mercilessly from both inside and outside his body, relentlessly seeking to claim his life.
