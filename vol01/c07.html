Chapter 7: Judgment at the Astral Tribunal

第七章　幽庁の審判〔七〕
English Translation • § 1

Here, by the gracious permission of the Great King, I was permitted to observe the court of judgment together with my guardian deity (Ubusuna-no-Kami) and Fuyō Sennin. Seated upon a towering dais high above was the Great King himself, and on seats two or three feet below him sat grim-visaged nether officials in solemn rows. In the lowest court of judgment, countless souls were prostrated on the floor in fearful submission. Looking around, I saw that the travelers who had waded across the River of Serpents after me were already mingled among the great throng awaiting their sentences. Thinking they would be Japanese alone, I observed that there were also many Chinese, Koreans, Westerners, and others. I was reminded of a certain senryū verse:

Suddenly glancing up at the Great King's countenance, I gasped in sheer terror and nearly collapsed. At that crucial moment, the guardian deity and the hermit caught and supported me firmly from both sides. Had it not been for their support, I would surely have fainted away. The visage of the Great King, which until moments ago had radiated gentle grace, inviolable dignity, and an infinite smile, had suddenly turned crimson; his eyes grew unimaginably huge, his mouth tore open to his ears, and tongues of roaring fire flickered from his throat! The nether officials assumed equally dreadful countenances so frightening that one could not look upon them directly, and the judgment hall was instantly shrouded in suffocating dread.

The Great King gestured to one of the officials seated in the middle row, who reverently came before him. Bestowing a ledger of judicial records upon the official, the latter bowed deeply, raised it to his forehead, returned to his seat, and began calling out each sinner’s name and reciting their verdicts aloud. One by one, guards seized the summoned convicts and marched them out of the tribunal. Unlike worldly trials, there was neither preliminary examination, appeal, supreme court, nor defense counsel; it was an extremely concise proceeding consisting solely of the pronouncement of judgment. Advancing about four or five hundred yards, I encountered a wide, deep river without a bridge. Glancing into the murky depths, I beheld countless indistinguishable souls who had plunged into foul water composed of blood and pus, their bodies so completely covered in swarming leeches that not an inch of bare flesh remained unbitten! The travelers wailed and shrieked in excruciating agony. I too had to cross this chasm, but lacking wings, how could I ever leap across such a broad and yawning gulf? From behind, red-faced demonic guards pursued me with leveled spears. Driven to the ultimate extremity, unable even to weep, I suddenly remembered the sacred scroll bestowed by my guardian deity. Taking it from my bosom, raising it reverently to my brow, and unrolling it, I saw inscribed in magnificent, vibrant brushstrokes: “Amaterasu-Ōmikami, Kamunagara Tamachihaemase!” The moment I cried out this prayer with all my soul, my body flew through the air, and I landed safely upon the opposite shore!

四、五町（約400〜500メートル）ほど進んだ所に、橋の架かっていない深くて広い川がありました。何気なく覗き込んでみると、誰とも見分けがつかない無数の人々が、汚れた血とも膿ともつかない濁水の中に転落し、全身に無数の蛭（ひる）が群がって隙間もないほど血を吸われていました。旅人たちは耐え難い苦痛と悲痛に満ちた叫び声を上げて泣き叫んでいました。私もこの溝を渡らねばなりませんが、翼のない生身の身体でどうしてこの広く深い溝を飛び越えることができるでしょうか。後方からは赤ら顔の番卒が鬼の形相に化け、鋭い槍を突き出しながら追い迫ってきます。進退ここに極まり、泣くに泣けず悶絶していた時、ふと思い出したのは先ほど産土の神から授かった一巻の巻物でした。懐から取り出して頭上に掲げ、押しいただいて開いてみると、畏れ多くも『天照大神、惟神霊幸倍坐世（かむながらたまちはひませ）』と筆跡も墨色も美しく鮮やかに書かれていました。私が無我夢中で『天照大神、惟神霊幸倍坐世』と唱えた途端、私の身体は宙を飛び、溝の向こう岸へと渡っていたのです。
原文（近代文語）
四五丁ばかり往つた処に、橋のない深い広い川がある。何心なく覗いてみると、何人とも見分けはつかぬが、汚い血とも膿ともわからぬ水に落ちて、身体中を蛭が集つて空身の無い所まで血を吸うてゐる。旅人は苦さうな悲しさうな声でヒシつてゐる。自分もこの溝を越えねばならぬが、翼なき身は如何にして此の広い深い溝が飛び越えられやうか。後からは赤い顔した番卒が、鬼の相好に化つて鋭利の槍をもつて突刺さうとして追ひかけてくる。進退これきはまつて、泣くにも泣けず煩悶してをつた。にはかに思ひ出したのは、先ほど産土の神から授かつた一巻の書である。懐中より取出し押しいただき披いて見ると、畏くも『天照大神、惟神霊幸倍坐世』と筆蹟、墨色ともに、美はしく鮮かに認めてある。自分は思はず知らず『天照大神、惟神霊幸倍坐世』と唱へたとたんに、身は溝の向ふへ渡つてをつた。
English Translation • § 20

Crestfallen, the nether guards trudged back the way they had come. Breathing a momentary sigh of relief, I resumed walking, only for an intense, paralyzing cold to strike, freezing my limbs until I could scarcely move. Just then, a radiant, golden light appeared in the gloom. As I gazed in wonderment, a brilliant orb of celestial radiance descended gently, alighting just two or three feet before my very feet!

現代日本語訳
番卒たちはすごすごと元の道へと引き返していきました。ひとまず胸を撫で下ろして歩を進めると、急激に凄まじい酷寒が襲いかかり、手足が凍りついてどうすることもできなくなりました。そこへ忽然と現れたのは黄金色の神々しい光でした。ハッと息を呑んで驚き見つめる中、その光の玉が私の足元二、三尺（約60〜90センチ）の所へと、ふわりと舞い降りてきたのでした。
原文（近代文語）
番卒はスゴスゴと元の途へ帰つてゆく。まづ一安心して歩を進めると、にはかに寒気酷烈になり、手足が凍えてどうすることも出来ぬ。かかるところへ現はれたのは黄金色の光であつた。ハツと思つて自分が驚いて見てゐるまに、光の玉が脚下二三尺の所に、忽然として降つてきた。
