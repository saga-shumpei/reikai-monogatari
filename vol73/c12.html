Chapter 12: 水 Fire の活動 [1843]

水火の活動〔1843〕

§ 1

As already mentioned, the seventy-five Kami appeared through the sound of the Ikukototama of Izunome, which resounded and resounded throughout the Daiuchiukan, never ceasing, and resounding in abundance.

The Kototama of Su resounded and resounded, and finally brought forth the Butsushitsu of fire and water within the Daiuchiukan. Somosomo, all Reikon and Butsushitsu are things born of the Kototama of Su. Now, the nature of fire flows horizontally, and the nature of water flows vertically. Therefore, fire rises vertically through the power of water, and water flows horizontally through the horizontal power of fire. Although Genreigakushiya of old said that fire is vertical and water is horizontal, when one reaches the Kongen, this is not so; fire cannot burn or shine without water, and water cannot Riudō without the added power of fire, eventually solidifying into Hiyōchiu. The ice of a winter day is the Honshitsu of water from which the Hataraki of fire has departed; by this principle, one should know that water performs its Hataraki vertically, and fire moves horizontally.

The Kuōsai and Ennetsu in the Tenkai are also the power of Suiki contained within. In the Shibitenkai, the Daitaiyō appeared and initiated a left-hand rotation, taking a course from east to west, and there was no Taiin revolving from west to east. As the Ennetsu was Mauretsu and did not become a mechanism to allow Shinjin to dwell in Zetsutaiteki Anjiu, the Kami Taigen-Izutsuo-no-Kami ascended to the peak of Takachiho, and for many years and months, offered up the Ikukototama; as the Kototama of the Ōkami condensed in the universe, the great Taiin was manifested here. The great Taiin, shining with much Suiki and the power of fire, initiated a right-hand rotation, taking a course from west to east, and guards the Shinjin of the Tenkai. Ten-no-Michitachi-no-Kami uses the Daitaiyō as a mechanism to carry out all manner of Keirin, and Taigen-Izutsuo-no-Kami uses the great Taiin as a mechanism to guard the universe and Tenkai; thus, the Tenkai became the regulation of fire and water, and came to see the prosperity of all things surpassing what was before.

Taigen-Izutsuo-no-Kami settled in the realm of the great Taiin and appeared as a Kami of supreme benevolence and supreme love, and it is awe-inspiring that He shall remain settled eternally, even unto the world after hundreds of billions of years. The Ōkami of supreme benevolence and supreme love, even reaching today after hundreds of billions of years, continues to guard all the heavens and earth of the universe while growing ever younger, and now, at the time when the earth is about to perish, in order to descend the Kamirei of the Mizu-no-Mitama into the world and cause the divine work of renewal to be performed, He has descended into a palace of flesh and, amidst persecution and ridicule, continues to devote Himself from beginning to end, which is truly awe-inspiring.

The Ōkami who settles in the Daitaiyō is revered as the Izu-no-Mitama, and the Kamirei who settles in the realm of the great Taiin and takes charge of the protection of the universe is revered as the Mizu-no-Mitama. The name of the Kami formed by the union of the two Kami, Izu-no-Mitama and Mizu-no-Mitama, who establish a divine government of supreme benevolence and supreme love, is called Izunome-no-Kami. That is to say, Izu is Izu (rigor) and is fire; Nome is the power of water, the power of water; water also initiates the Hataraki of Mizu (grace) and thus becomes the Mizu-no-Mitama. From the opening of the Shibitenkai to this day, hundreds of millions of years later, He has finally manifested as Izunome-no-Kami, appearing in the Mahuraba of the modern earth (tentatively called Earth), which is the center of the Daiuchiukan, and with a physical body, it has become an age where He devotes Himself to the divine work of universal renewal.

The union of Izu-no-Mitama and Mizu-no-Mitama Is revered as the Izunome-no-Mitama Izu-no-Mitama resides in the solar realm And performs the Hataraki of fire Mizu-no-Mitama resides in the lunar realm And performs the Hataraki of water Through the Hataraki of the truly rigorous Izu-no-Mitama The fire that wraps the universe shines The truly graceful Mizu-no-Mitama brings happiness And gives the power of water to the universe The two Kami, solar and lunar, spoken of here Are not the Yin and Yang seen from our earth Fire moves by the power of water, and water Flows by the power of fire Nothing can be accomplished by water alone It is the help of fire that brings water to life How could fire alone burn or shine It moves by borrowing the power of water The Kototama of fire and water is called Kamimizu And also, the great path of the universe called Kamimizu

原文（近代文語）

大宇宙間に鳴り鳴りて、鳴り止まず、鳴りあまれる厳の生言霊ス声によりて、七十五声の神現れ給ひしことは、既に前述の如し。

スの言霊は鳴り鳴りて、遂に大宇宙間に火と水との物質を生み給ふ。抑々一切の霊魂物質は何れもスの言霊の生むところなり。而して火の性質は横に流れ、水の性質は縦に流るるものなり。故に火は水の力によりて縦にのぼり、又水は火の横の力によりて横に流る。昔の言霊学者は火は縦にして、水は横なりと言へれども、其の根元に至りては然らず、火も水なければ燃ゆる能はず光る能はず、水も亦火の力添はざれば流動する能はず、遂に凝り固まりて氷柱となるものなり。冬の日の氷は火の気の去りし水の本質なり、此の理によりて水は縦に活用をなし、火は横に動くものなる事を知るべし。

天界に於ける光彩炎熱も内包せる水気の力なり。紫微天界には大太陽現れ給ひて左旋運動を起し、東より西にコースを取るのみにして、西より東に廻る太陰なし。炎熱猛烈にして神人を絶対的に安住せしむる機関とはならざりしかば、茲に太元顕津男の神は高地秀の峯にのぼらせ給ひ、幾多の年月の間、生言霊を奏上し給へば、大神の言霊宇宙に凝りて茲に大太陰は顕現されたるなり。而して大太陰は水気多く火の力をもつて輝き給へば、右旋運動を起して西より東にコースをとり、天界の神人を守らせ給ふ。天之道立の神は大太陽を機関として、凡百の経綸を行ひ給ひ、太元顕津男の神は大太陰を機関として宇宙天界を守らせ給へば、茲に天界はいよいよ火水の調節なりて以前に勝る万有の栄を見るに至れり。

太元顕津男の神は大太陰界に鎮まり給ひて至仁至愛の神と現じ給ひ、数百億年の末の世迄も永久に鎮まり給ふぞ畏けれ。至仁至愛の大神は数百億年を経て今日に至るも、若返り若返りつつ今に宇宙一切の天地を守らせ給ひ、今や地上の覆滅せむとするに際し、瑞の御霊の神霊を世に降して更生の神業を依さし給ふべく、肉の宮居に降りて神代に於ける御活動そのままに、迫害と嘲笑との中に終始一貫尽し給ふこそ畏けれ。

大太陽に鎮まり給ふ大神を厳の御霊と称へ奉り、大太陰界に鎮まりて宇宙の守護に任じ給ふ神霊を瑞の御霊と称へ奉る。厳の御霊、瑞の御霊二神の接合して至仁至愛神政を樹立し給ふ神の御名を伊都能売神と申す。即ち伊都は厳にして火なり、能売は水力、水の力なり、水は又瑞の活用を起して茲に瑞の御霊となり給ふ。紫微天界の開闢より数億万年の今日に至りていよいよ伊都能売神と顕現し、大宇宙の中心たる現代の地球（仮に地球といふ）の真秀良場に現れ、現身をもちて、宇宙更生の神業に尽し給ふ世とはなれり。

厳御霊瑞の御霊の接合を 伊都能売御霊と称へまつらふ 厳の御霊太陽界に在しまして 火の活動を行ひたまふ 瑞の御霊太陰界にましまして 水の活動を行はせたまふ いづいづし厳の御霊の活用に 宇宙を包む火は光るなり みづみづし瑞の御霊は幸ひて 宇宙に水の力を与ふ ここに説く太陽太陰両神は 我が地より見る陰陽に非ず 火は水の力に動き水は火の 力によりて流動するなり 何事も水のみにしては成らざらむ 火の助けこそ水を生かすも 火のみにていかで燃ゆべき光るべき 水の力をかりて動くも 火と水の言霊これを火水といひ 又火水と言ふ宇宙の大道

§ 2

When the kototama of water and fire unite, they become suikuwa, and become the suikuwa of all things in the uchiu. Fire and water are namely kami; water and fire are namely suikuwa. Through the hataraki of inyō and the katsuyō of water and fire, the spirit of fu, namely power, manifests itself. Rain, wind, snow, frost, and hail are all formed depending on the doahi in which water and fire intersect. The jitsugetsuseishin shining in the ōzora and the thunder and lightning are also the power of suikuwa. A world where only the lineage of fire gains the upper hand has no other path than to grow cloudy. To burn up all things with mauretsu power is the susabi of fire. A world where only the power of fire is active becomes disordered and cloudy, and can never be governed. In the hundreds of kuniguni upon the chijiyō, calamities arising day by day are a world of fire alone. To rectify a world that has utterly fallen into disorder, the Mizumitama has emerged together with the Izumitama.

With the power of the Mizumitama alone, how could all things flourish, if fire did not exist? Bringing forth fire and water into the world with proper anbai, the hataraki of Izunomenokami is precious. Izunomenokami has descended to the chijiyō and has begun uchiukausei. The Izumitama protects the yōki, and the Mizumitama protects the in, and the country is governed. The Mizumitama appears as a god of supreme benevolence and supreme love, and it is the divine age when the pillar of heaven is erected. If not for the merits of Izunomenokami, the future destination of the world would surely perish. The divine work of nurturing and assisting all things was indeed the power of the Mizumitama. Only by having the two divine works of fire and water do the activities of heaven and earth arise one after another. Now, through the merits of Izunomenokami, I think to illuminate the cloudy divine age.
