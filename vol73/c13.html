Chapter 13: The Episode of Kami の述懐 Song (Part I) [1844]

神の述懐歌（一）〔1844〕

§ 1

Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami, using Taiin as a Kikuwan, kept his Mitama in Getsukai while his Nikutai served at the palace of Takachiho morning and evening. Wishing to carry out the Shigumi of Kami, he distributed Oshihetsukasa here and there to serve the Tenkaikeirin; yet, many Bonshin, who misunderstood the Mioshihe of the strict Mitama, inclined only toward Koshintekishōjiyōkyō. They did not grasp the Goshingefu of the Tenkaikeirin, which is Kuniu and Kamiu, and it is truly regrettable that they spoke of all manner of falsehoods and hindered him with all their might. Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami ascended the peak of Takachiho, worshipped the Ten, worshipped the earth, and chanted his Jiyutsukuwai.

The Lord God's command is not a trivial thing, yet there is no Yochi left for me to lay my hands upon. My Miwaza of Kuniu, Kamiu, and creating the myriad things—can I not bring it to completion? The Mikoto of the Lord God is awesome; morning and evening I think of wanting to bring forth Kunitama. Though I myself harbor no strange heart, the hundred Kami treat my path with indifference. The ugly whispers of the Bonshin, who possess hearts devoid of Yutori, are indeed dreadful. If I were to follow the hearts of the Bonshin, there would be no such thing as being hindered. But if I comply with the hearts of the Bonshin, I shall not accord with the divine will of the Lord God; what shall I do? Not knowing the great Shigumi of the Lord God, they speak ill of me; how wretched it is. The heart of the Lord God is deep and also vast; how could the lesser Kami ever comprehend it? If the Bonshin sit in numbers like the grains of sand on the beach, there is no remedy.

For the Kami who are quick to perceive the heart of the Lord God, the Shigumi of this age of few Kami is painful. Although Mihishiro-Kami are distributed far and near, I am in a state where there is no way for us to meet. My wish, held morning and evening, to bring forth Isukuwashi-Kami has also come to naught. Of the Tenkai created by the Lord God, the Kami of Seimeishingo are few. I have long suffered, wondering how I might fulfill the command of the Lord God. Even if the Imperial Kami were to command, there is no way for the Sashime or the Sakashime to meet. Though I think to go against the command, I am one who hesitates, thinking of the disorder of Tenkai.

The eighty Himegami whom the Lord God granted to Akitsuo-no-Kami for the sake of the Tenkaikeirin spent long years in vain, merely waiting for the divine command. In particular, the eight Himegami who served close by his side were also frightened by the whispers of the Bonshin, which were too strong, and they abandoned their Miwaza in vain, only waiting for the time to arrive. In the end, they grew old and could not complete their Miwaza; the world became increasingly clouded and desolate, and even the once-splendid Tenkai saw evil spirits spread day by day, until it reached a state beyond recovery—a truly regrettable situation.

Akitsuo-no-Kami exerted his great courageous heart and attempted to carry out his Miwaza, and how many times did he endeavor to set right the horse of his heart, which is the seat of the soul? Yet, in the end, he was hindered by the ferocity of the hundred Kami, and it is an eternal regret that he could not carry it through. The eight Himegami who served close by the side of the deity addressed their Jiyutsukuwai to Akitsuo-no-Kami. Their song:

Does my Lord Kimi not know the heart of Suzukohime, who received the Mitama of the Lord God? There is no way to untie the knotted heart, for there is no leisure to serve the Miwaza. Suzukohime, because of the defilement of Tenkai, has fallen into the deep flow of water. Tenkai is clear and vast; should I shrink away while harboring a clouded heart? Does Kimi not understand the sincerity with which I think to serve as long as the life of the Tama-no-o lasts? My heart has become lonely, for there is no remedy while serving by your side morning and evening. Though I am a body that serves Kimi morning and evening, I am one whose Mitama is in a state between dream and reality. Is it a dream, is it not, is it reality, or is it an illusion? It is the divine figure of Kimi, which is no illusion. Praying morning and evening at the palace of Takachiho, my time has not yet arrived. It is truly wretched to spend these days, which the Great Kami commanded, in vain.

Understanding the great heart of the Lord God, and grasping the will of Kimi, I weep. Even weeping is not free for me, a body that exists in Kami; for one who is in Kami, it is especially painful.

Akitsuo-no-Kami, replying to this, chanted:

It is not that I do not understand the heart of the Himegami, but endure and wait until the time arrives. Since I am not a body of wood or stone, I know your sorrowful heart.

§ 2

Suzukohime-sama the Kami sings:

If it be thus, I cannot endure even for a moment, / I grieve for the weakness of Kimi's heart. / Should there be anything to fear in Ametsuchi, / If it be the divine command of the Lord Ōkami?

Akitsuo-sama the Kami: 'In any case, I pray you wait for a while, / For I too have preparations for the spring.'

Thus they exchanged songs with each other and waited for the time to arrive. Asakahime-sama the Kami also sang a Miuta:

Though my heart that longs for Kimi is not in darkness, / I, Asakahime, am burning with longing. / O Lord Ōkami, extinguish the fire in the breast of Asakahime, / Whose love is not shallow. / Deign to understand the deep heart of Asakahime, / Who serves Kimi morning and evening. / Thinking of Kimi as weak-hearted, / Asakahime feels distressed morning and evening. / I welcome the heart of the clouded Kami, / And grieve for the weakness of Kimi's heart. / There is no way to extinguish the raging flames, / How many times have I thought of dying? / Since you are Akitsuo-sama the Kami, reveal yourself clearly, / And let us meet openly in this world. / It is not a matter of meeting but once, / Can a young woman's body endure this? / The teachings of the stern Mitama Kami are heavy, / Yet I live while resenting their excessive rigidity. / Since it is the path permitted by the Lord Kami, / How could there be any cause for hesitation?'

Akitsuo-sama the Kami replied to this with a Miuta:

Asakahime, whose sincere heart is not shallow and is pure, / I too know of thy suffering. / It is not that I am weak-hearted, but wait a while longer, / Until the true Kami emerges. / Even I am grieving morning and evening, / Because I have not yet fulfilled the divine Miwaza of the command.

Asakahime-sama the Kami sings again:

My heart that has harbored resentment morning and evening, / How wretched is Asakahime. / Feeling as if I stand in the midst of burning fire, / I weep morning and evening longing for Kimi. / I find some small comfort in having disclosed, / The sincerity of my Murakimo heart before Kimi.'

Uzukohime-sama the Kami sings a Miuta before Akitsuo-sama the Kami:

My Murakimo heart is wrapped in flames, / And I only resent the cold Kimi. / Even if the Momogami should waver, / How could one measure the divine Miwaza of the Kami? / Since Kimi is Hikoji, do not hesitate, / For the sake of the Kami and for the sake of the Shigumi. / Serving near your Onsoba morning and evening, / It is painful to see Kimi. / Wishing to reveal the noble heart of Uzuhime, / Even though I speak before Kimi, / The threads of my heart that longs for Kimi are in a thousand pieces, / Tangled and tangled, with no way to unravel them. / Though I am in a position to serve near your Onsoba, / It is a body that cannot even speak its mind. / Please pick up and blow away the words of the crab-walking Kami, / Do not listen to them. / Blow away the ugly words of the ugly weeds, / In the mist of the Kototama's breath. / It is painful to serve near your Onsoba, / And yet it is also sorrowful for me to leave your Onsoba. / Not knowing when the Miwaza will end, / I am sad for my own body passing the days. / Beyond this, I shall steady the horse of my heart, / Grant me a promise for one night.'

Akitsuo-sama the Kami, replying, sings:

Though I see the dream of the arm-pillow night after night, / The pain is that I cannot meet thee. / I cannot make excuses to the Lord Kami, and for the woman at my side, / My body is painful, lacking the worth of a man. / Wait for the time, my dear one, / Until the hearts of the Kami clear.

Uzukohime-sama the Kami sings again:

How sad it is that I have caused Kimi distress, / By repeating the complaints of a shameful woman. / Though my face flushes with shame and pain, / I shall express my sincere heart while enduring. / Beyond this, I have no power to distress Kimi, / Shall I leave it to the Kami and wait for the time? / If it were not for the divine command of the Kami, / I would not be suffering to this extent.'

Umesakihime-sama the Kami also expresses her thoughts in a song:

While meeting the spring when the plums bloom in the second month, / I am a flower of the manifest world with no way to be fragrant. / I am one who feels lonely in this celestial world, / Where the general spring atmosphere drifts. / Does Kimi not find pitiful the fleeting flower of me, Umesakihime, / Who blooms as spring begins? / Since I am Umesakihime, for whom Ametsuchi blooms at once, / How could I think of small things? / Accepting the painful heart of my lord, / I shall be silent, though I am a body of spring. / Knowing that I am a flower with no way to open, / My longing for Kimi only increases. / The first flower of Umesakihime, who blooms as spring begins, / Was struck by frost as it tried to open. / Let snow fall, let frost and hail come, / I am Umesakihime, who holds the spring. / Thinking to wait for the time of the Kami, / Steadying my heart time and again, / I weep at the pain of the Miwaza of Kimi, / Who illuminates this clouded world.'

§ 3

Akitsuo no Kami chants:

Akitsuo: 'Meeting the Makoto of the Kimi of Magokoro, I feel at peace, yet still I am sorrowful. I do not fear the wild rampages of the Momogami, but I hesitate, thinking of the world falling into chaos. If there were no path of the Izunomitama in the present world, I would think my Kamuwaza easy. Yet, without the light of the Izunomitama, the power of Mizu cannot be fully equipped. Thou alone knowest my heart well, and I, too, cherish thy heart. Though thou art a woman like the creeping vine, I feel a sense of Kanshiya for the Ō-ness and redness of thy heart. Wait for a while longer, for it is not as if there are no Tsukihi left for me to be with thy heart. My heart, which thinks of Kamuwaza from Asayufu, is gladdened by the heart of the Kimi who awakens it.'

Umesakuhime no Kami chants again:

Umesakuhime: 'Hearing the Kototama of my Itoko no Kimi, I felt as if I were meeting the spring when the plum blossoms bloom. Leaving myself to the heart of Kamunagara Kimi, I shall endure for Ikutose. How happy I am to have revealed the depth of my Murakimo heart before the Kimi now. Even if the Tenkai should break, I shall never forget the Makoto of my Itoko no Kimi. Even in the Tenkai created by the Lord God, there are worries such as these from Asayufu. Just as there is a precedent for Ujimushi to breed in Mashimizu, why should the Tenkai be any different?'

Hanakohime no Kami's song:

Hanakohime: 'Hanakohime, who blooms with Tokijiku fragrance in the Tenkai, has flowers with neither scent nor luster. I am one who should bloom as a flower of the Tenkai; why, Kimi, dost thou not pluck me? Thinking that perhaps thou art a Kimi without flower or fruit, I am one who is moistened by the rain of tears from Asayufu. Very well, very well, even if the Momogami mock me, do not hesitate to pluck me. For Hanakohime, who has met the spring of Tengoku, the Shigure falls in her heart as if it were Tokijiku. Thinking even of my life, which is like a string of jewels, I am perhaps a clover flower blooming in the field. Should I hesitate in my fear of Kamuwaza and let it pass in vain? It is painful to be a body waiting for the day of Kamuwaza while attending to a cold Kimi from Asayufu. Until I think my life, like a string of jewels, shall end, the flames in my breast are burning high. The hot heart, resembling the flames that scorch the sky, is also painful. While I hear the teachings of the Izunomitama no Kami, I cherish the Mizunomitama. Without looking at the grand design, the Momogami perhaps chatter about trivial things. Drive away the ugly Magatsukami who hinder the great Keirin of the Kamikai.'

Akitsuo no Kami replies and chants:

Akitsuo: 'To one who preaches the teachings of the Kami of Aizen, how could I cast aside the ugly Magatsukami? I think to preach and persuade them as much as my power allows, and to illuminate them with love and goodness. If there were no heart of Aizen, I myself would not hesitate in the Kamuwaza of Keirin. The Kamuwaza of the fresh Mizunomitama is a vow to abandon not even one Kami. Bound by thy words, which have both flower and fruit, I have become sorrowful at thy Makoto.'

Hanakohime no Kami chants again:

Hanakohime: 'That thou shouldst declare that I am a Mitama with both flower and fruit is something I would trade my life for, and I am happy. Very well, very well, even if there is no day for me to meet the Kimi, I think I shall not resent nor lament. I cherish the heart of the Kimi, who endures even while mixing among the Magatsukami, and does so with such Ō-ness. Because I, a woman, know the Magokoro of the Kimi, I have not uttered a single word of complaint. I shall wait for the time when I should perform the Kamuwaza without fearing anyone, and live in joy.'

花子比女の神の歌。

『天界に非時匂ふ花子比女の 花は香もなく艶だにもなし 天界の花と咲くべき吾身なり 岐美は何故手折りまさずや 花も実も無き岐美かもと朝夕に 涙の雨に潤ふ吾なり よしやよし百神如何に譏るとも 躇ふことなく手折り給はれ 天国の春に逢ひたる花子比女の 心に時じく降る時雨かな 玉の緒の命までもと思ひつつ 吾は野に咲く紫雲英の花かも 神業はただに畏しためらひて ただ徒らに過すべきやは 朝夕につれ無き岐美に侍りつつ 神業の日を待つ身はうたてき 玉の緒の命死せむと思ふまで 胸の炎は燃え盛りつつ 炎々と御空をこがす火炎にも 似て苦しもよあつき心は

厳の御霊の神の教は聞きながら 瑞の御霊をあはれと思へり 大局に目をつけずして百神は 小さきことに言さやぐかも 神界の大経綸を妨ぐる 醜の曲神打払ひませよ』

顕津男の神、答へて謡ひ給ふ。

『愛善の神の教を説く身には 如何ではふらむ醜の曲霊を わが力及ばむ限り説き諭し 愛と善とに照さむとぞ思ふ 愛善の心しなくば我とても 経綸の神業ためらひはせじ 瑞々し瑞の御霊の神業は 一神も捨てぬ誓ひなりけり 花も実もある言の葉にほだされて 悲しくなりぬ汝が真言に』

花子比女の神は又謡ひ給ふ。

『花も実もある身魂ぞと宣らすこそ 命にかへて嬉しかりけり よしやよし岐美に逢ふ日のあらぬとも 吾はうらまじ歎かじと思ふ 曲神の中に交こり雄々しくも 忍ばす岐美の心をいとしむ 女の子吾岐美の真心知る故に 只一度の言挙げせざりき 神業を誰はばからず勤むべき 時を待ちつつ楽しみ暮さむ』
