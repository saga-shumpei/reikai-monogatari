Chapter 34: 国 Soul の発生 [1865]

国魂の発生〔1865〕

§ 1

Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, accompanied by the Itsuhashira, crossed the Hayase of Himuka and emerged into Giyokusenkiyō. Among the Yasohimegami, he encountered Yotsukasahime, the exceptionally wise Kuhashime. Through the Miwaza of right and left, the Iki and Iki solidified, and the moon's dew settled deep within the Tainai of the Himegami. From this time, the Himegami, as days and months passed, grew heavy with child, and she did nothing but joyfully await the auspicious day of the Miko's birth.

The Itsuhashira who served as Mitomo, with Ōmononushi placed in their midst, and the other four Kami, set out to open the Kunihara in the west, east, north, and south.

With the Mikoto of Akitsuo no Kami, he departed from the noble palace, and it suddenly became lonely. The eastern sky was colored with Shinonome, and the noble land was draped in Shiun. In the Mahoraba of Shinonomekoku, the palace of Yotsukasahime was built, beautiful and pure.

Here, as the time for Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami to part from the Himegami drew near, with the moon full, he performed Misogi in the clear water in the center of the garden morning and evening, praying that no harm might befall the Miko, a sight most awe-inspiring.

The abdomen of the Himegami grew larger day by day, and, breathing with difficulty, she sat respectfully before the Hikoji no Kami and chanted an Uta:

'Though it was but a single vow, my abdomen has grown large as the months have passed. Morning and evening, I pray with sincerity that no harm may come to the child within my womb. May this womb open peacefully and the Miko be born; do not leave me until the child has grown. However noble the Ōmiwaza may be, I feel the task of bearing a Miko is not light.'

Akitsuo no Kami chanted:

'Hearing that the Miwaza entrusted by the Lord God has been fulfilled and the Miko is to be born, I am filled with joy. O Mihishiro given to me by the Lord God, your merit has truly manifested. Hearing that you already carry the Miko, I find no way to sleep with you, my beloved, in my longing. This palace where the Miko of the Lord God is carried is a pure place of Takamagahara. Remain at peace morning and evening, while guarding the Miko in your womb.'

Yotsukasahime no Kami chanted:

'Tightening the mind's rope morning and night, though it is painful, I shall protect the Miko.'

Just as they were chanting thus, her abdomen suddenly pained her, and Hikoji no Kami, startled, chanted an Uta clearly:

'Where is Ōmononushi no Kami? Kawamorihime no Kami, where are you? Come quickly!'

At this, Ōmononushi no Kami and Kawamorihime no Kami appeared here eagerly, and Ōmononushi no Kami said:

'Clear, clear! The time for the Miko to be born has come. O Kami of the moon and sun, protect her. If the Himegami, who is to give birth peacefully, is the Miko of the Kami's trust, then from today, the land of Shinonome shall be a pillar of the Kami, and having been born, it shall flourish ever more.'

Kawamorihime no Kami, filled with joy, said:

'The time for the Miko I have awaited to be born has come. O Kami of heaven and earth, protect her. Yotsukasahime no Kami, remain calm. The Miko shall be born in peace.'

Yotsukasahime: 'From the day I began to serve in the task of bearing the Miko, this day has become the most painful of all.'

Kawamorihime: 'Since the Miko is one upon whom the water and fire of the Lord God have rested, you shall give birth to the Miko peacefully and safely.'

Just as they were chanting thus, a cry of 'Ua' was raised, and a Miko like a jewel was born. Beginning with the two Kami, male and female, Ōmononushi and Kawamorihime, the two Kami rejoiced, prepared the birth bath, washed and purified the Miko's body, placed her in a proper seat, and chanted:

'The vast sky is clear without clouds, and the earth shines as the Miko is born. The sky of Shinonome clears, and the jewel-like Miko, the Kunidama no Kami, has been born. Now that this Miko is born, the land of Shinonome shall surely be at peace and flourish.'

Akitsuo no Kami, in his excess of joy, worshipped the heavens, prostrated himself on the earth, and while pressing his hands together in prayer, said:

'The rock door of the vast heaven has opened. Through the noble power of Yotsukasahime, from today, the moon, the sun, and the stars shall shine more purely, and this Kunihara shall be illuminated. Through the ever-widening grace of the Lord God's majesty, you have brought today's joy.'

Kawamorihime no Kami chanted:

河守比女の神は謡ひ給ふ。

§ 2

Shinonome no kunihara akurani keri Mizu no mitama no miko umaremaseba Kono yakata ni hime no mikoto wo kabahteshi Hisashiki ware wa mukuirarekeru

Hereupon, Ōmononushi holds up the jewel-child born unto them and offers sacred felicitations.

Ashibiki no yama mo ōno mo kototama no Iki wo awasete kotogimatsuramu Ametsuchi no ichido ni akururu omoi kana Kami no yosashi no miko no idemashi Ōmononushikami wa kefu yori miko no tame Akaki kokoro wo towa ni sasagemu

The deity Ōmotoakitsuo bestows upon the child born on this auspicious day the august name of Himukahime.

Kono miko wa himuka no kawa no mashimizu Rei nariseba himukahime tofu Himukahimegami no megumi ni ohitachite Kore no mikuni wo ushihagimaseyo Ware miko to omoedo tadashiku nushi no kami no Miko ni shiariseba ukagaimatsuru mo Yotsukasa no himegami yo miko no umaremashi Kefu yori himukahime ni tsukaeyo

Yotsukasahime recites an august waka:

Kashikoshi ya hikoji no kami no ōkami nobotoru Unaji ni ukete mamorimatsuramu Nushi no kami no mimizu ni naridashi miko nareba Ware wa itsukamu asana yunawo Kono miko ya ohitachimashite shinonome no Tsukasa ni narasu to omoeba toutoki

The deity Ōmononushi recites a celebratory waka:

Himukahime takaki yakata ni umaremashinu Haya shinonome no kuni wa aketari Shinonome no sora ni noborasu hi no kami no Hikari ni hitoshi miko no sugata wa Himukahimegami no mina koso kashikokere Shinonome no kuni ni umaremashinureba Himukagawa nagaruru shimizu mashimizu wa Miko no ohitachi hitashi koso sure

Hereupon, Her augustness Himukahime grows up rapidly under the daily nursing of the deity Ōmononushi and the deity Kawamorihime, and upon the milk of the sincere bosom filled with the loving affection of Yotsukasahime; therefore, the deity Akitsuo, rejoicing that the divine work has been accomplished, entrusts all matters to the deity Ōmononushi in order to serve once again in the divine work of birthing deities, takes leave of his spouse-deity, and sets forth on a journey of birthing deities, far and away.
