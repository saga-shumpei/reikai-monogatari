Chapter 25: Kotodama の滝 [1856]

言霊の滝〔1856〕

§ 1

Somosomo, Takateruyama of Shibitenkai is what is called Sumisenzan in the Butsushiyo, also known as Mount Sumeru, and again called the mountain of Ibuki. Next, Takachihoyama is also known by the other name of the Takahiyama of Ten, and is a high mountain next in rank to Takateruyama.

The height of Takateruyama, according to the Sokuriyōhafu of Konnichi, is three hundred and thirty thousand shaku, its mahari exceeds eight thousand eight hundred ri, and the number of Ōgahiogahi flowing in the yomo is five thousand six hundred and seventy. Among them, the one that is deepest and widest, joining the amata of Tanimizu and flowing toward the Tōhau, is called Himukagaha; the one flowing toward the south is called Hinatagaha; the one flowing toward the west is called the River of the Moon; and the one flowing toward the north is called the Sumeru River, ichimei Takaterukaha.

The height of Takachihoyama is also in accordance with this at three hundred thousand shaku; the Azumagaha flows in the east, the great river of the south flows in the south, the great river of the west flows in the west, and the Takachihokaha flows in the north, all pouring into the great ocean of Shibitenkai. Furthermore, regarding the number of valleys, Takachihoyama did not differ greatly compared to Takateruyama.

However, compared to Mount Sumeru, Takachihoyama has more rocks, its mountain form is steep, and it appears to have the difference of being rugged. Both mountains are always drifted by seven-colored clouds, and although the spirit of the gods wraps the mountains and releases spiritual energy in the yomo, evil spirits accumulated in the valleys here and there, and evil gods appeared, and finally, a crooked god like the great serpent of Nakatsutaki appeared.

Here, the divine officials of Takahinomiya, having finished deeply comforting the spirit of Korokohime, in order to command and pacify the great serpent of Nakatsutaki, accompanied by the Great Mother God, Ōmononushi no Kami, Akiharu no Kami, Mechio no Kami, Masumi no Kami, and others, a hundred gods in total, followed the difficult path of the valley with its rising and falling rocks, and while purifying the evil spirits of the valley by reciting the Kototama of rigor, they courageously advanced and arrived at the foot of Nakatsutaki.

Taigenakitsuo no Kami, in order to mourn the spirit of Korokohime, remained at Takahinomiya, prayed for the safety of the party of the Great Mother God, and in order to command and pacify the god of the great serpent, sat upright before the great divine presence and was reciting the Kototama of Mizu.

In that divine song of Kototama:

'Emerging at the roots of the Nakatsutaki of Mitsukuri, may there be no harm in the divine words of the gods. Cast away the crooked god who causes the calamity that clouded and muddied the heavenly realm. Oh gods, command and pacify the great serpent of Nakatsutaki where Korokohime became a sacrifice. Without the achievements of the Lord God, how could this crooked god ever submit? The crooked god dwelling in the waterfall cascading from the peak of Takateru is a fierce god indeed. No matter how fierce and strong the crooked god may be, it cannot reach the power of the living Kototama. The princess god, while resenting the crooked god of the great serpent, must be helping from the high palace of heaven.'

Having finished chanting this, he prostrated himself before the divine presence and prayed for the success of the party.

Here, the party of the Great Mother God lined up around the basin of Nakatsutaki, and each of them recited the living Kototama.

Great Mother God: 'Oh crooked god hiding in the basin that is clear and pure, come out quickly. I am the Great Mother God who appeared from the spirit of A, and I shall recite the Kototama. I shall tame the crooked god of the bottom, the divine pillar of the distant Takahinomiya. If you fear the rigorous power of Kototama, oh god of the great serpent, submit quickly. May the ten-thousand-jo waterfall fall and strike the head of the great serpent from the sky. Oh pitiful great serpent lurking at the bottom of the water, unaware of the Kototama recited even to this extent.'

The Kototama of the Great Mother God, filled with water and fire, had no effect; the basin still held blue waves, and only the sound of the cascading waterfall shook the surrounding forests.

Here, Ōmononushi no Kami, with solemnity, recited a Kototama song.

'Oh ugly great serpent, appearing as if you do not hear even the Kototama of the divine words of the Great Mother God. I shall offer to you, the ugly god, the Kototama that the Lord God has bestowed. Oh god of the great serpent, lurking long at the bottom of the water, submit gracefully. Thinking that I would help you, I came while reciting the rigorous Kototama as much as possible. I am the divine official Ōmononushi; Takateruyama and the waterfall are also mine. This waterfall is the waterfall of Kototama that was bestowed upon me by the divine words of the Lord Great God. Do you hide as a great serpent while the evergreen pines grow thick and close off the heavens, making it dark even in the daytime? With the living Kototama of the Lord God, I shall illuminate even the bottom of the water of eight thousand fathoms.'

原文（近代文語）

抑々紫微天界の高照山は、仏書に所謂須弥仙山にして、スメール山と言ひ又、気吹の山とも言ふ。次に高地秀山は、又の御名を天の高日山と称へ、高照山に次ぐの高山なり。

高照山は高さ、今日の測量法によれば、三十三万尺にして、其周囲は八千八百里にあまり、大峡小峡の四方に流るる数は、五千六百七十条あり。その中最も深く広く数多の谷水を合して東方に向つて流るるを日向河と言ひ、南に向つて流るるを日南河と言ひ、西に向つて流るるを月の河と言ひ、北に向つて流るるをスメール河、一名高照河と言ふ。

又高地秀山の高さも之に準じて三十万尺、東には東河流れ、南には南の大河流れ、西には西の大河、北には高地秀河流れて、紫微天界の大洋に注ぐ。而して高地秀山も谷の数、高照山に比して大差なかりける。

ただ高地秀山は、スメール山に比して岩石多く、山姿峻しく、屹然たるの差違あるが如し。両山共常に七色の雲ただよひ、神霊の気山を包みて霊気を四方に放てども、あちこちの谷間には邪気鬱結して邪神現れ、遂には中津滝の大蛇の如き曲神現れ出でたるなり。

ここに高日の宮の神司等は、如衣比女の霊を厚く慰め終りて、中津滝の大蛇を言向けやはすべく、大御母の神、大物主の神、明晴の神、眼知男の神、真澄の神等その他、百の神々を伴ひて、岩石起伏の谷の難路を辿りつつ、厳の言霊宣り上げて、谷間の邪気を祓ひ乍ら、勇み進んで中津滝のふもとに着き給ひぬ。

太元顕津男の神は、如衣比女の霊を弔ふべく、高日の宮に在しまして、大御母の神の一行の無事を祈り、大蛇の神を言向けやはすべく、大御前に端坐して、瑞の言霊宣り給ひつつありき。

その言霊の御歌に、

『三ツ栗の中津滝根に出でましし 神の神言につつがあらすな 天界を曇らせ濁せし禍ひを 起す曲神を放らせたまへ 如衣比女犠牲となりし中津滝の 大蛇を言向けやはしませ神よ 主の神の功績なくば如何にして この曲神のまつろふべきやは 高照の峰より落つる滝津瀬に 住む曲神は厳しき神はも 曲神はいかに厳しく強く共 生言霊の力におよばむ 比女神は大蛇の曲神恨みつつ 天の高宮ゆ助けますらむ』

と謡ひ終り、神の御前にひれ伏して、一行の成功を祈り給ふ。

ここに大御母の神等一行は、中津滝の滝壺の周囲に整列して、おのもおのもに生言霊を宣り給ふ。

大御母の神『澄みきらひ澄みきらひたる滝壺に かくるる曲神とく出でませよ 吾こそはアの御霊より現れし 大御母神言霊宣らむ 久方の高日の宮の神柱 底の曲神を吾はきためむ 言霊の厳の力をおそれなば 大蛇の神よ早やにまつろへ 万丈の滝は空より落たぎち 大蛇の頭打ちたたけかし かく迄に宣る言霊を知らずがに 水底に潜むあはれ大蛇よ』

大御母の神の水火を込めての言霊も何の功なく、依然として滝壺は青き波をたたへ、滔々と落つる滝の音のみ四辺の森林を震はせにけり。

ここに大物主の神は、儼然として言霊歌を宣り給ふ。

『大御母神の神言の言霊も 聞かず顔なる醜の大蛇よ 主の神の与へ給ひし言霊を 醜神汝に吾はたむけむ 潔ぎよくまつろひ来れ水底に 長くひそめる大蛇の神よ なるべくは厳の言霊宣り上げて 汝助けむと思ひつつ来し 吾こそは大物主の神司 高照山も滝も吾もの この滝は主の大神の神言もて 吾に賜ひし言霊の滝よ 常磐木の松は茂りて天を閉ぢ 昼なほ暗く大蛇しのぶか 主の神の生言霊に八千尋の 水底までも照し明かさむ』

§ 2

Just as He sang thus, a light of blinding brilliance appeared from the depths of Yachihiro, and Orochi floated up to the Suimen, writhing to the right and to the left, before diving once more into the Minasoko.

Here, the Kami Manakoshirio, having obtained the power of the myriad Kamitachi, proclaimed a Kototamauta.

'The Magakami lurking in the Takateruyama / I have stood to face to subdue and pacify / O Orochigami, if it be thus, you shall have no recourse / Submit and serve the Ikukototama / The retribution for harming Himegami before my eyes / O Orochi, you shall perish now / Yet, not to destroy, but to save through the Ikukototama / The Momogamitachi have appeared / This Miyama is governed by the Kami Akitsuo / If this be a Sugado, depart quickly / If you hide in this Minasoko forever / I shall not forgive, but shall strike and slay you / How foolish and pitiful the raging Kami / Who dares to defy this pure Kamikuni.'

Just as he sang thus, staining the Takitsubo of Chihiro crimson, Orochi again revealed its body on the Suimen, writhing Zengo and Saiu, and finally sank deep into the Minasoko. Here, the Kami Akeharu sang.

'Living on the Takateruyama of the Hisakata no Ten / O Orochigami, I shall strike and slay you / If you hold your life of the tama no o dear / Submit and serve the Ikukototama / I have come up accompanied by the Momogami to remove the calamity of the Kamikuni / Even if Orochi hides deep in the Minasoko / There is no way to escape the arrow of Kototama / O wicked Orochi that swallowed the Himegami of Takahi no Miya / Whose merits were great / Since you too are a mitama born of Kami / I think it a pity to simply cast you away / If possible, accept my Kototama / And follow the good to submit / Do you not know the merits of the Kami Akeharu / Even to the depths of the abyss of Yachihiro / Even if you hide in the depths of Chihiro / How could I leave you without the Ikukototama / The Momogami are here now, so how / O Orochi, could you have the power to resist?'

At this Kototama, Orochi of the Minasoko floated up while turning its crimson belly, and opening its giant mouth, it spat out black poisonous vapors for several hundred jo, and suddenly the surroundings became like a dark night, to the point where one could not distinguish even a shaku.

Here, the Kami Chikamio, wishing to exorcise this evil spirit, sang a Kototamauta.

'The Magakami spitting black water and fire like the ubatama / While raging as if this were the end / The Kami Chikamio is here, O Orochi / Calm your heart and come to submit / Though the Magakami wraps the water and fire in pitch black / My Kototama shall blow it away / O Kami Shinatohiko, appear at once / And exorcise the evil spirit wrapping the valley.'

Just as he sang thus, shaking the tips of the hundred trees of the entire mountain, the wind of Shinato blew violently as if to make the rocks fly, and the pitch-black poisonous vapor spat by Orochi scattered without a trace; the ten-thousand-jo waterfall hung white, and the Takitsubo appeared deep red, stained with the blood of Orochi. It seemed that Orochi had again hidden in the Minasoko, only blowing bubbles up to the Suimen.

Here, the Kami Masumi, straightening his posture, worshipped the Ten and prostrated himself to the earth, and sang a Kototamauta.

'Driven away by the breath of the Kami Shinatobe / The black clouds suddenly blew and scattered / The majesty of the Kototama is noble, and through the severe Kototama / Of Chikamio, the wind has emerged / Swaying the hundred trees of the Takateruyama / The Kami Shinato has been born / Even the Orochi of the waterfall that brings calamity to the Kamikuni / Unable to endure, has gone into hiding / Come then, I, the Kami Masumi, shall proclaim the severe Kototama / Over the water and fire of the Lord Kami / O Orochi, lurking deep in the Takitsubo of Yachihiro / Do you not know the severe Kototama / Drawing and raising the sword of the severity of Kototama / I shall harass you until you submit / I am the word of the Kami Masumi of Ten / Your power shall surely be exhausted / While softening the Ikukototama that strikes and slays / I am the one of sincere heart who seeks to subdue and pacify / O Orochi of the Minasoko, understand fully / The Kototama I proclaim, and I shall spare your life.'

As he sang thus, Orochi floated to the Suimen with a gentle appearance different from before, shedding tears from both eyes and bowing its head many times; suddenly, it took the waterfall water into its mouth, raised a mist of breath, caused clouds to well up, and while making rain fall, it fled high toward the summit of Takateruyama and toward the Ten.

原文（近代文語）

斯く謡ひ給ふ折もあれ、八千尋の底より、まばゆき許りの光現れ来り、大蛇は水面に浮び上り、右に左にのた打ち廻りつつ、又もや水底に潜り入りぬ。

ここに眼知男の神は、万の神等の力を得て、言霊歌を宣る。

『高照の山にひそめる曲神を 言向けやはすと立ち向ひたり かくならばせむ術なけむ大蛇神 生言霊にまつろひ奉れよ 比女神を吾が目の前にて害ひし むくいよ大蛇今に亡びむ 亡ぼさず生言霊に救はむと 百神達は現れましにける この御山顕津男の神知食す 清所なりせば早く去れかし いつまでもこの水底に潜むならば 吾は許さじ斬りてはふらむ 澄みきらふこの神国を畏れなく 荒ぶる神のおろかさあはれ』

斯く謡ふ折しも、千尋の滝壺を紅に染め乍ら、又もや大蛇は水面に体を現し、前後左右にのた打ち廻り遂に水底深く沈みける。ここに明晴の神は謡ひ給ふ。

『久方の天の高照山に棲む 大蛇の神よ斬りてはふらな 玉の緒の生命惜しけく思ひなば 生言霊にまつろひ奉れ 吾は今神国の禍ひのぞかむと 百神伴ひ上り来しはや 水底に大蛇は深くひそむ共 言霊の征矢さくる由なけむ 御功績も高日の宮の比女神を 呑み喰ひたる大蛇悪らしも 汝も亦神より出し身魂なれば ただに放るは惜ししと思ふ なるべくは吾言霊を諾ひて よきに従ひまつろへよかし 八千尋の淵の底まで明晴の 神の功績を汝は知らずや よしやよし千尋の底にひそむ共 生言霊にやらはで置くべき 百神は今ここにあり如何にして 大蛇よ刃向ふ力あるべき』

この言霊に水底の大蛇は、紅の腹をひるがへし乍ら浮き上り、巨口を開いて黒き毒気を吐く事数百丈、忽ち四辺は暗夜の如く、咫尺を弁ぜざるに至れり。

ここに近見男の神は、この邪気を祓はむとして、言霊歌をよみ給ふ。

『烏羽玉の黒き水火はく曲神は 今を最後と荒れくるひつつ 近見男の神ここに在り汝大蛇 心なごめてまつろひ来れ 曲神の水火は真黒に包めども ふきて放らむわが言霊に 科戸比古神よ忽ち現れて 谷間を包む邪気祓へかし』

斯く謡ひ給ふや、全山の百樹の梢をゆるがせて、科戸の風は、岩も飛べよと吹き荒れつつ、大蛇の吐ける真黒き毒気は、跡かたもなく散りうせて、万丈の滝は白くかかり、滝壺は大蛇の血潮に染みて真赤く見えぬ。又もや大蛇は水底にしのびたりと見え、ブクブクと水泡を水面に吹き上ぐるのみ。

ここに真澄の神は、威儀を正して、天を拝し地に伏して、言霊歌を詠ませ給ふ。

『科戸辺の神の伊吹きに退はれて 黒雲忽ち吹き散りにけり 言霊の稜威貴し近見男の 厳言霊に風出でにける 高照の山の百樹をそよがせて 科戸の神は生れましにけり 神国に禍なす滝の大蛇さへ 得たまりかねて隠ろひにけり いざさらば真澄の神は主の神の 御水火に厳の言霊宣らむ 八千尋の滝壺深くしのぶなる 大蛇よ厳の言霊知らずや 言霊の厳の剣をぬきかざし まつろふ迄を攻めなやまさむ 吾こそは天の真澄の神言ぞや 汝の力はつきむとすらむ 斬りはふる生言霊をやはめつつ 言向けやはすと真心の吾よ 水底の大蛇よ吾宣る言霊を つぶさに悟れ命を助けむ』

斯く謡ひ給へば、大蛇は以前にかはる優しき姿を水面に浮べ乍ら、両眼に涙を流し幾度も頭を下げ、忽ち滝水を口にふくみ伊吹の狭霧を吹き起し吹き起し、雲を湧かせ、雨を降らせ乍ら、高照山の頂高く、天に向かつて逃げ行きぬ。

§ 3

Here, Ōmihaha-no-Kami, feeling the Iriyoku of the Kototama of Masumi-no-Kami, composes a Miuta.

Ōmihaha-no-Kami: 'Awe-inspiring is the Kototama of Masumi-no-Kami, by which the hideous Orochi has been revived. In the Tengoku where the light of Aizen drifts, there is nothing that cannot be saved. Even the Orochi that troubled Himegami has been saved by the Kototama of the Lord. Illuminating the heart of Aizen, I too shall mingle with the myriad of Kami.'

Masumi-no-Kami chants.

Masumi-no-Kami: 'In the Kamikuni where both Ten and Earth are clear and pure, how the power of Aizen shines alone. Even the Orochigami, with a Murakimo heart so foul, is a Miko of the great Ōkami. This Orochi must have appeared as the Miiki of the Lord God became clouded and solidified. Because I believe it was born of the spirit of the Lord God, I have helped the Orochi and let it escape.'

Ōmononushi-no-Kami, moved by the pure heart of Masumi-no-Kami, chants.

Ōmononushi-no-Kami: 'Feeling the gentle heart of Masumi-no-Kami, where both Ten and Earth are clear, it is reviving. My soul also feels ashamed before the Magokoro of Masumi-no-Kami. If the power of Aizen shines strongly, even the hideous Magami will bow and submit.'

Akeharu-no-Kami chants.

Akeharu-no-Kami: 'The Jiyaki of Takateruyama shall also be clear from this Kefu of Akeharu, and the Tsukihi shall shine. From this Kefu, Takateruyama shall be peaceful, and the Momogami shall perform their Miwaza.'

Thus, the Orochi of Nakatsutaki, struck by the living Kototama of the Momogami, was revived and departed high into the Ten.

原文（近代文語）

ここに大御母の神は真澄の神の言霊の威力を感じ給ひて、御歌よまし給ふ。

『畏しや真澄の神の言霊に 醜の大蛇は蘇りたり 愛善の光ただよふ天国に 救はれぬものはあらざりにけり 比女神をなやまし奉りし大蛇さへ 主の言霊に救はれにけり 愛善の心照して吾も亦 万の神に交はらむかな』

真澄の神は謡ひ給ふ。

『天も地も真澄にすめる神国に ひとり輝く愛善の力よ 村肝の心汚き大蛇神も 主の大神の御子なりにけり 主の神の御水火のくもり固まりて 現れ出でにけむこれの大蛇は 主の神の霊に生りしと思ふ故に 吾は大蛇を助け逃しつ』

大物主の神は、真澄の神の清き心に感じ給ひて、謡ひ給ふ。

『天も地も真澄の神の優しかる こころに感じて蘇りつつ 魂も真澄の神の真心に 吾恥かしくなりにけらしな 愛善の力の強く輝かば 醜の曲神もなびき伏すなり』

明晴の神は謡ひ給ふ。

『高照の御山の邪気も明晴の 今日より清く月日照るらむ 今日よりは高照山は安らかに 神業仕へむ百の神たち』

かくして中津滝の大蛇は、百神の生言霊にうたれ、蘇りつつ天高く立ち去りにける。
