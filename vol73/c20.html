Chapter 20: 廻り逢ひ [1851]

廻り逢ひ〔1851〕

§ 1

Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami, having established the Amenomihashira and the Kuninomihashira in the Yahirodono of the Takahi Palace, performed the divine works of the right and left, and calling upon Yukuehime-no-Kami, performed the divine work of marriage.

Ōmihaha-no-Kami offered a blessing:

In the Heaven, Entrusted by the Lord Ōkami, With a pure Mitama, the Kamutsukasa, Residing in the Eastern Palace, Governing the Kamikuni and teaching the world, Though thou hast proclaimed the noble Mikoto day and night, The hearts of the Momokami have inclined to one side, And there is none who fulfills the Mimune, Lamenting that the divine works were delayed, Morning and evening, Akitsuo-no-Kami has now appeared here, As the ruler of the land, And with Yukuehime, whom the Kami entrusted, Thou performest the work of marriage. On this good day and auspicious hour, Beginning with the Lord Ōkami, The hundred Kami who soar in the Ame, The eighty Kami who soar in the Kuni, Gather in joy and proclaim the Ōminori.

How joyous and auspicious it is to celebrate! The horses neigh, the Kirin sing, The Hōō dance down from the Heaven, The Kariyōbinga sing with clear voices, And sing of the spring of Tokoyo. Purple clouds trail over Takateruyama, And in the lands of the four quarters, All grasses and trees take on a rich appearance and bloom, Noble round fruits are full and abundant, The rice seeds growing in the sacred field, Thicken day by day and flourish as the eternal, Abundant ears of eighty sheaves, Celebrating the age of the Kami of the Amatsukamikuni, This day, from the beginning of this celestial realm, Is a day of unparalleled reverence, The divine work of marriage today, Is the foundation of the Shibi celestial realm, The noble divine work of Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami,

May it shine ever wider and ever higher, Like the shining Amatsubi. Born from the west, the auspicious Mitama, Like the face of the Moon-no-Kami, Born clear and cool, Giving moisture to this land without a corner left out, Causing the hundred grasses and trees to live, By the dew of grace. Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami, We serve by celebrating the Kami, The Amatsubi shines, the Moon is full, Above the sacred land, the five grains, Ripen until the place is crowded, The Kami of the four quarters sing of the world, And the age of the Kami, rejoicing and enjoying, Is because of the appearance of Kimi, Flourishing for a thousand and eight thousand generations, While the scent of the white plum blossoms, Perfumes the four quarters.

How awesome is the flourishing age of the Kami! How awesome is the flourishing age of the Kami!

Akitsuo-no-Kami, in response to the blessing of Ōmihaha-no-Kami, sang a song of gratitude.

Guided by Kimi who knows my heart, I have come this day to the eternal home, Following the command of the Lord Kami, in the Yahirodono, How happy I am to open the path of marriage. Were it not for the Mikoto of Ōmihaha-no-Kami, This joy of today would not exist. From this day forth, the Mikoto of Ōmihaha-no-Kami, I shall serve as my true mother. I shall illuminate the entire Amatsukamikuni, With the living Kototama. In the true and excellent place of the land where the Kototama shines in the Heaven, This palace stands firmly. Coming to this noble and pure place of Takateruyama, The sky of my heart has also cleared. Though the mountain of Takateru is high, it seems, It cannot reach the heart of Ōmihaha-no-Kami.

Yukuehime-no-Kami sang:

The Heaven has cleared, the Heaven has cleared, the land has cleared, my heart has cleared, Meeting the spring of Takateruyama. By the arrangement of the Mikoto of Ōmihaha-no-Kami, My spring heart has ignited. I think to bring forth the soul of the land, one after another, By making the ignited spring heart live. If there were no divine child in the Mikoto of Akitsuo-no-Kami, How could the divine work be accomplished? Since I have become the vessel of the Lord Kami, I shall not dislike any work. Hand in hand with Kimi, my beloved husband, I wish to solidify this Kamikuni. To the Ōkami who is Ōmihaha-no-Kami, I shall serve as a child from this day of birth.

Ōmihaha-no-Kami smiled and sang a song:

Residing in the palace of the two pillars of Yahiro, It is awesome to think that you will give birth to the soul of the land. From today, I shall become the administrator of the palace, And serve to assist the divine work of Kimi.

Manachio-no-Kami sang a celebratory song:

The purple clouds over Takateruyama, which pierces the Heaven, Are becoming deeper and deeper. Does Takateruyama also rejoice? Especially, Today the light seems to shine clearly. Takateruyama also has evergreen trees, green, And looks as if it is celebrating this good day. Manachio-no-Kami, who serves in this pure place morning and evening, Is happy. From today, reside in this palace, And establish the pillars of the Kamikuni. Now that the two pillars have appeared here, There is nothing to fear in this Kamikuni.

§ 2

Akeharu no Kami, having taken his seat at the wedding, recited a Miuta:

From the eastern sky to the west shining across, the Amatsuhikage is pure. From the west to the east clearing across, the light of the moon is Sugasuga. Born as the Mitama of the moon, Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, serving the divine work of the Kami's command, now here in the Yahiro no Mitono, has appeared. Having wed Yukuehime, he meets the Ame no Mihashira, and establishes the Kuni no Mihashira. Creating the land, creating the Kami, creating all things, he appears to illuminate this Kamikuni; how noble he is. I am Akeharu Kamutsukasa. The clouds and mists that block the Yomo, I clear away with the Ikukototama, and serving before the Ōmahe morning and evening,

On this good day and auspicious hour, how joyful it is to meet, Kamunagara. May you two Futahashira shine forever, without change for a thousand or eight thousand generations, as long as the thread of life endures. I bow before your Mimahe and offer my congratulations.

Yukuehime no Kami recited a poem in response. Her Miuta:

The divine words of Akeharu no Kami, you are indeed a valiant Kami, a male Kami. Though I am but a frail Himegami, my heart for the land and my sincere devotion to the Kami, I swear to the Kami, shall never be forgotten. As long as I am in this palace, morning and evening, without fail, I shall serve the divine Miwaza. Please protect me, Akeharu no Kami, for I entrust myself to the sincerity of your divine words, Kamunagara. I swear this by the Kami.

Though the curtains of clouds that drift and linger are deep, with the Kototama of a woman that shall blow them away, today, when heaven and earth open at once, is the light of the Lord Ōkami. The divine words of the Sumerakami are awesome, and the hearts of the Kunitsukami are loving, and they shall illuminate the land.

Here, Chikamio no Kami sang a song of celebration:

As the time draws near for the eternal flowers to bloom in the Kamikuni, I sing, unable to contain my joy. The Kamutsukasa who guards the palace of Takachihono, here in this pure place, the Takateruyama, and the mountain of Takateru, from today shall increase its radiance, and the plains of the land shall become clear. Since the divine words of the two Futahashira have been born, the Kunitsukami shall be encouraged. I, too, in my overflowing joy, have celebrated the age of the Kami, aided by the Kototama.
