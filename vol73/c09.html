Chapter 9: 香具の Tree の実 [1840]

香具の木の実〔1840〕

§ 1

The Lord Ōkami, Amanominehio no Kami, who dwells serenely in the palace of Shibi within the innermost spiritual realm of the Shibitenkai, commanded the deity Samori to pluck and gather the fruits of the Kagu tree—which bloomed with eternal flowers and bore fruit while growing ever more luxuriantly and flourishing in the pure courtyard of the palace—and when they were gathered, their number reached eighty.

Hereupon the Lord God caused the Kototama of SU to resound throughout the void, and having made the fruits of the Kagu tree to be grasped in His right hand and blown upon with His breath, an exquisite Goddess was born from His august mouth, who moved into the fruits of the Kagu tree, and thereupon the form of an exquisite Goddess came into being. The name of this Goddess is called Takanohimeno Kami. Next, having grasped a single tree fruit and rinsed it in the crystal waters of the jewel-spring, and having blown His august breath upon it, yet another Goddess came forth into being. This one is called Suzukohimeno Kami. Thus did all the eighty fruits of the Kagu tree manifest as noble Goddesses to serve as the august pillars of the heavenly grand strategy.

Shaking the august bell, the awe-inspiring Kototama blessing and bestowing peace, The eighty Goddesses have indeed now manifested. The Lord God breathed His divine breath upon the noble tree fruits, And caused the seeds of the heavenly realm's grand strategy to be born. Had there not been the divine work of the deity of Kuni-no-umi, This heaven and earth could never have been opened. The creation of the land and the birth of the august children by Akitsuo no Kami Were the foundations of the great grand strategy. Creating the land, and further creating heaven, creating the gods, And the achievements of Akitsuo no Kami who gave birth to the children of men. The eternal fruits of the Kagu tree possess The flavor of the SU of the Lord God. The divine words of Akitsuo no Kami, making the eighty Goddesses Their august mihashiro, have opened up the land. The eighty Goddesses, who purely Brought forth and nurtured the Kuni-mitama gods of various lands, The achievements of the birth of the Goddesses brightly illuminate The great universe and the great chiliocosm with ease.

Hereupon the deity Taiten Akitsuo no Kami, reverently receiving the divine words of the Lord God, united in august marriage with Takanohimeno Kami, and settled permanently to dwell in the palace of Takachihino-miya, opening the land and governing the gods, blending and drawing together the breaths of water and fire to generate clouds and cause rain to fall, thereby granting moisture unto all the heavenly realms, whereupon all things were generated within the land, lush ears of sacred rice ripened in the broad and long sacred rice fields of heaven, and tree fruits matured, so that it came to pass that the divine work of the Great Tasting (Daijō) gradually proclaimed its completion.

Akitsuo no Kami, reverently receiving the divine words of the Lord God, caused Utsukohimeno Kami, Asakahimeno Kami, Umesakihimeno Kami, Hanakohimeno Kami, Kaguhimeno Kami, Sayokohimeno Kami, Suzukohimeno Kami, and Sawakenohimeno Kami to serve near him and engage in the divine work. These are also called the eight pillar Goddesses. Besides these, seventy-two other Goddesses were distributed to the lands of the east, west, south, north, far, and near within the realm of the Shibi Palace, making them the august mihashiro of the gods, and through them he carried out the great grand strategy—how truly awe-inspiring it was.

原文（近代文語）

紫微天界、最奥霊国紫微の宮居に鎮まり居ます主の大神、天之峯火夫の神は、宮の清庭に弥茂り弥栄えつつ非時花咲き実る香具の木の実を、左守の神に命じてむしり取らせ給へば、其の数八十に及べり。

茲に主の神は虚空にスの言霊を鳴り出で給ひて、香具の木の実を右手に握らせ呼吸を吹きかけ給へば、艶麗なる女神の霊御口より生り出でまして香具の木の実に移らせ給ひ、茲に艶麗なる女神の姿生り出でましぬ。この女神の名は高野比女の神と申す。次に一つの木の実を手握り玉の清水に滌ぎ給ひて御息を吹きかけ給へば又もや女神成り出で給ふ。之を寿々子比女の神と申す。かくして八十の香具の木の実は、いづれも天下経綸の御柱として貴の女神と現れ出でませり。

御鈴ふる厳の言霊幸はひて 八十比女神は現れましにけり 主の神は貴の木の実にみいきかけて 天界経綸の種を生ませり 国生みの神の神業のなかりせば この天地は開けざるべし 顕津男の神の国生み御子生みは 大経綸の基なりけり 国を生み又天を生み神を生み 人の子生める顕津男の功 非時の香具の木の実は主の神の スの味ひをもてるなりけり 顕津男の神の神言は八十比女を 御樋代として国をひらけり 国々の国魂神を清らけく 生みおほせたる八十の比女神 比女神の生みの功は大宇宙 大千世界をやすく照せり

茲に太元顕津男の神は、主の神の神言かしこみ高野比女の神にみあひて、高地秀の宮に永久に鎮まり居まし、国を拓き神ををさめ、水火の呼吸をくみ合せもやひ合せて雲を生み、雨を降らせて、あらゆる天界に湿りを与へ給へば、国土に万物発生し、天の狭田長田に瑞穂の稲は実り木の実は熟し、大嘗の神業漸く完成を告げ給ふ事とはなれり。

顕津男の神は主の神の神言かしこみて宇都子比女の神、朝香比女の神、梅咲比女の神、花子比女の神、香具の比女の神、小夜子比女の神、寿々子比女の神、狭別の比女の神を近く侍らせ神業に奉仕せしめ給ひぬ。之を八柱の女神となも言ふ。この外七十まり二柱の比女神を紫微宮界の東西南北、遠近の国土に配りおきて、神の御樋代となし、大経綸を行ひ給ひしぞ畏けれ。
