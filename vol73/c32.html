Chapter 32: Jewel Spring の Moon [1863]

玉泉の月〔1863〕

§ 1

On the far bank of the Himuka River, in the center of the great Daiheigen that opens to the Tōnanpau, stands the spacious Kamuyakata. Guided to Giyokusenkiyō, the Kami Ōmotoakitsuo, accompanied by the Kami Ōmononushikami, Masumigami, Chikamio, and Teruogami, and Akeharu, whose long-held thoughts have finally cleared, was led by Kahamorihime and emerged into this mansion. As they gazed in admiration at the rare Keshiki and entered the mansion, they happened upon Yotsukasahime, one of the Yasohimegami. As their faces met for the first time, their cheeks flushed with mutual warmth, and they exchanged the Kototama of purity, engaging in the divine Miwaza of the Kami's guidance.

Purifying their hearts and cleansing their bodies, they served with reverence and awe; truly, the Miwaza is sublime. Calling this entire Heigen the Shinonomekiyō, they united Iki with Yotsukasahime, and while giving birth to the Kunitamagami, they performed the Ōmiwaza of dwelling in tranquility. This was described in detail and with precision. Aa, Kannagara, Kannagara, under the protection of the Lord God, I pray with awe and reverence that the tales of the ancient age of the Kami be revealed widely, without omission or loss, as Mizutsuki sits in meditation at Suimeikaku, east of Ten'onkyō. Aa, Kannagara, Kannagara, may the spirits be blessed.

Here, the Kami Akitsuo, together with the Kami Yotsukasahime, strolled through the vast gardens of Giyokusenkiyō where the evergreen trees flourish, and ascended quietly to the high hall of the three-storied tower erected in the southeast corner, where they looked out over the shape of the land and composed divine poems.

Akitsuo: 'As far as the eye can see, this great field is a land of Shinonome, where purple divine vapors drift. Since the soil of this land is fertile, it is a beautiful land where the five grains shall surely ripen in abundance. When I look upon the purple clouds rising from the Takateru mountain, my soul flourishes. This vast expanse of land I look out upon is as clear as the sky of Shinonome. I, who set out to give birth to the Kami of nation-building, am filled with joy to have found this pure place. The light of the daytime moon hangs over the Takateru mountain that towers in the southwestern sky. It is the age of the Kami, where the moon crossing the heavens advances from the west to the eastern sky. I, too, have appeared as the spirit of the moon, and I have moved eastward to open up the land. While the Amatsubi illuminates this mansion, the moon in the sky protects the world. The Kototama of the Lord God has gathered and condensed, and thus the sun and moon have appeared in the sky. My spirit and Yotsukasahime have united Iki, and the moon is now about to become full.'

Yotsukasahime: 'I am a maiden who has long awaited you in this mansion, revering the divine words of the Lord God. Though eighty days have passed, this auspicious day is filled with the joy of heaven and earth opening. I shall soothe my heart, which flutters like melting snow, and sleep with you, my lord, night after night. This mansion is the floating bridge of heaven, the heavenly pillar built high by the Kami. This land of Shinonome, which opens endlessly to the southeast, is clear. Dwell forever in this mansion and give birth to the Kunitamagami. The evergreen forest that swells and expands to the edge of the vast field I look out upon is pure. You, my lord, who were born to be the light of the land whose expanse is boundless and beyond reach, have come to me. Through our union of one night, left and right, you have placed the divine child within my body. From here on, I shall offer my red heart to you, my lord, and serve you morning and evening.'

Akitsuo: 'Receiving the dew of the moon's grace from the high heavens, you are the princess who has already conceived. I wish to embrace your youthful chest, which flutters like melting snow, as white as the bark-cloth rope. Having awakened from the dream of the one night we slept together, I now stand facing you, the princess. The land of the Kami of Shinonome is indeed magnificent. The evergreen trees, the pines and the camphor trees, flourish long and forever. The hundreds and thousands of flowers adorn the beautiful land. The white plum blossoms with eternal fragrance, and the figs bear fruit forever in this beautiful field. Sent by the greenery of the Takateru mountain, I have met my princess of Shinonome. Standing with you on the floating bridge and looking out, this land of Shinonome is endless. Serve with reverence day and night, and protect the spirit of the Lord God until the divine child is born.'

Yotsukasahime: 'You, my lord, who harbor the spirit of the Lord God, dwell forever in this mansion. From the high hall of the floating bridge of heaven in the high heavens, you and I look out upon the shape of the land. When I look at the shape of the land with a heart purified of all worldly thoughts, it opens like a fan. The Himuka River flows to the northeast, and the land of Shinonome is endlessly wide to the southeast. The Takateru mountain towers in the southwest, and the Himuka River borders the northeast. Steam rises from this vast field, and it is a brave land that lives forever. Come then, Kami Hikoji, descend from the floating bridge, for the evening draws near.'

§ 2

Leading the way, as they descended from the high hall of the Sansōrō, the two Kami again emerged into the Sugado of the garden, and standing beside the Tamaizumi, they rested for a while. The clear spring of the Tamaizumi reflected the Misugata of the Meo Futahashira purely and freshly, as it was, clear and bright like a mirror. The Ogami, observing the Enmanseirau reflection of the moon floating in this clear spring at Yufugu, chanted:

'The moon of the Hisakata Misora, too, is reflected in this water, shining purely and brightly. Does Tsukiyomi, reflecting the Ōzora here, fill the dew of blessings? Though it is the moon I look up to, the moon I see now shines beneath my eyes. The dew of the blessings of the Hisakata moon has surely dwelled within thy Mihara. The face of the Miko who shall emerge when the moon is full, is it not the moon reflected in this mirror? How pure it is, standing at the bottom of the spring, just like the Misugata of my dear sister.'

Yotsukasahime-no-Kami chanted:

'Even at the Minasoko, there is the light of the Amatsumisora, and the Tsukihi pass over this Tamaizumi. Above the head of the pure Kimi, the moon smiles and hangs. I looked up and I looked down, the moon of the Ōzora is pure, resembling Kimi and me. Ame and earth have become one, and the dew of the moon is gathered here in this Tamaizumi. Reflecting our pure Misugata in the Tamaizumi, we wait for the Tama-no-Kami-no-Ko to be conceived. How majestic like the Takateru-no-Miyama, the Kimi of the clear flow of the sun-facing stream.'

Just as the two Kami stood on both sides of the Tamaizumi, celebrating the joy of the Miko having been conceived, Ōmononushi-no-Kami came advancing while whispering softly over the Masago of the garden, and chanted respectfully with a clear voice:

'The Kami standing at the Tamaizumi are the moon and the moon, the Misugata of Ame and earth. This spring, which harbors the moon of the Hisakata Misora, is Yotsukasahime. Reflecting the treetops of the evergreen, the Tamaizumi is bright with the moon that does not hang. The moon that crosses the Ame has also descended to the spring; what a magnificent hall that harbors the dew. Leaving the Takateru-yama Takahi-no-Miya, I gaze upon the moon of the Tama-no-Izumi. May the Futahashira settle here and give birth to a Miko, like the stars. The stars of the Ōzora have also descended to the Tamaizumi, letting their shadows drift and guarding the moon. I am Ōmononushi-no-Kami-Tsukasa, and I shall guard this Kami-kuni eternally. As the shield of Hikogami, I, Ōmononushi, shall serve this country forever.'

Akitsuo-no-Kami chanted:

'How awe-inspiring is the Kami-sen of Ōmononushi. It resonates with my soul, fulfilling my heart. If we accomplish the work of Kami-umi, the country of Shinonome will flourish in a rich, ascending prosperity. The country of Shinonome, where the Amatsuhi ascends in rich prosperity, is a clear land of eternal spring. Thou art Ōmononushi, who has been appointed as the ruler of the land of eternal spring and has descended here.'

Ōmononushi chanted:

'I shall guard the path of the Kami who have finished the divine work of Miko-umi in detail.'

Yotsukasahime-no-Kami chanted:

'Bathed eternally in the dew of the moon's blessings, I shall nurture the Miko in my Mihara.'

Thus, each and every one of them stood by the Tamaizumi, finished chanting their songs of reflection, and quietly entered the inner palace.
