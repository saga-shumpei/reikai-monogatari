Chapter 30: Day 向の River Waves [1861]

日向の河波〔1861〕

§ 1

The Kumade of the path is without obstacle, and the God Ōmotoakitsuo, with the sky of the God Masu being clear, and the eastern sky also being Akeharu, the divine merit is Teruogami. Together with Ōmononushi, they bid farewell to the Seijiyō of Takateruyama and depart. The Amatsu sun shines, the moon is clear, and Takateruyama rises high above the Unpeu, towering over the Shiun, and the state of the Tengokujiyōdo, shining in the distance of the Shibitenkai, is pure.

Here, the God Akitsuo, together with the Itsuhashira, while looking toward the west at Takateruyama, departs with a light heart to govern the eastern land and give birth to the Kunitamagami. The flow of the Himukagaha lies across their path, and the Itsukau of Kamigami, wondering how they shall cross this Hirokaha, remain lost in Shian for a while, and each of them recites a Miuta.

Miuta of the God Akitsuo,

'As I look out, the Hirokaha knows no bounds, how blue the surface of the water is. The Himukagaha falling from the peak of Takateru, flows blue, filled with spring. When I look back at the trail of Takateruyama, purple clouds rise up on the One. Having left the Takahi no Miya, where the Miizu of the Lord God is pure and clear. Even if the rapids of the Himukagaha are strong, I shall cross by proclaiming the Kototama of Mizu. Since it is a divine land helped by the blessing of Kototama, what is this torrent to me?'

The God Ōmononushi also recites a Miuta,

'The dew on the trees of Takateruyama that brushes the heavens, flows here and becomes a river. While gazing at the force of the Himukagaha water, I am reminded of the great serpent of the waterfall. If I am to cross the Himukagaha where both moon and sun flow clearly, I shall do so in a boat of Kototama. This flow, whose depth is unknown and deep blue, floating the moon and sinking the sun. This water flows in four directions and brings life to the hundred grasses and trees of the Kunihara. The dew of the blessing of Mizumitama having gathered, this Himukagaha must have been born. The sound of the murmuring water echoes loudly. The flow falling from Takateruyama, since the Mizumitama is here, I shall cross the deep Himukagaha with ease.'

The God Masu also chants,

'Within the clear heaven and earth, pure and clear, the flowing Himukagaha is pure. As far as my eyes cannot reach, the flow of the Himukagaha is vast and fierce. The flow of the shallows of this river is clear, reflecting the sky and turning blue. I have now come to perform the divine work, following the Mizumitama. The Himukagaha lying across the path of the divine work, its depth must be the heart of the God. Does the spirit of Takateruyama drip down? This river water is truly clear.'

The God Akeharu recites a Miuta,

'The endlessness of the flowing water must be the manifestation of the Miizu of the God. I stand on the shore, looking at this river that has not even a bridge to cross. O Amatsukami of the distant heavens, hear me, and dry up the river water so that I may pass. I have become anxious, wondering how I shall cross this river. It is regrettable that there is no way to cross this river where both moon and sun float in the waves. It is a divine work to give birth to the Kunitamagami. If you have a heart, O river, step aside. Does this river god not hear the pure, bright, and correct true Kototama?'

The God Chikamiotoko recites a Miuta,

'Even if the flow of water washing the shore is high, I think I shall cross by the blessing of the God. Even if I were to perish as mere scum of the water, what should I fear, for I am a body of the God. The Himukagaha that obstructs our departure to give birth to the Kunitamagami is hateful. For a while longer, I shall proclaim the living Kototama and appease the god who guards the river. It is the Mizumitama that creates the land, with the divine words of the Lord Ōkami of the distant heavens. Do you not know that the dew of the blessing of Mizumitama gathered and the Himukagaha was born? O god who guards the river, O water of the Himukagaha, if you have a heart, listen to my Kototama.'

The God Teruo chants,

'The God Teruo, of both moon and sun, is here, and I shall speak to the god who guards the river. O priest of the Takahi no Miya of the distant heavens, do not obstruct the path of the imperial progress. The heavens are high and also wide, and have no limit. The Himukagaha is narrower than a belt. I, being a God, feel ashamed to be troubled by the flow of this narrow river. Even if it is wide, when compared to the vastness of heaven and earth, what is the flow of the Himukagaha?'

原文（近代文語）

道の隈手もつつがなく 太元顕津男の神は 真澄の神の空清く 東の空も明晴の 神のいさをは照男神 大物主ともろともに 高照山の聖場に 別れを告げて出で給ふ 天津日は照る月は冴ゆ 高照山は雲表に 高く紫雲をぬき出でで 天国浄土のありさまを 紫微天界の遠近に 輝きゐるぞ清しけれ。

ここに顕津男の神は、五柱の神と共に高照山を西に眺めつつ、東の国を治め、国魂神を生まむと、心いそいそ出で給へば、日向河の流は前途に横はり、一行の神々は如何にして此の広河を渡らむかと、暫し思案にくれながら、各も各もに御歌詠まし給ふ。

顕津男の神の御歌、

『見渡せば限りしられぬ広河の 水のおもての青みたるかも 高照の峰より落つる日向河 春をたたへて青く流るる 高照山あとふりかへり眺むれば 紫の雲尾根に湧き立つ 主の神の稜威も清く澄みきらふ 高日の宮を我出でにけり 日向河水瀬はいかに強くとも 瑞の言霊宣りて渡らむ 言霊の幸ひ助くる神国なれば この激流も何のものかは』

大物主の神はまた御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『天を摩す高照山の木々の露 ここに流れて河となりぬる 日向河水の勢ながめつつ 滝の大蛇を思ひ出づるも 月も日も清く流るる日向河を われ渡らばや言霊の舟に 青々と底ひもしらぬこの流 月を浮べつ日を沈めつつ この水は四方に流れて国原の 百の草木を生かしこそすれ 瑞御霊恵みの露の集りて この日向河は生り出でにけむ せせらぎの音たかだかと響くなり 高照山ゆ落つるながれは 瑞御霊ここにいませば底深き 日向の河も安く渡らむ』

真澄の神はまた謡ひ給ふ。

『澄みきらふ天地の中にすみすみて 流るる日向の河は清しも わが眼路の届かぬまでに広々と 流れはげしき日向河はも この河の瀬々の流れは澄みきらふ 空をうつして青みたるかも われは今瑞の御霊に従ひて 神業の為来りけるかも 神業の道に横ふ日向河 深きは神の心なるらむ 高照の山の霊気の滴るか この河水は真澄みたるかも』

明晴の神は御歌詠まし給ふ。

『滔々と流るる水のはてしなきは 神の稜威の現はれなるらむ 渡らはむ橋さへもなきこの河を 見つつ岸辺に吾は立ち居り 久方の天津神たち聞召し わが通るべく河水干させよ 如何にして吾はこの河渡らむと 心細くもなりにけらしな 月も日も波間に浮ぶこの河を 渡らむ術のなきぞ悔しき 国魂の神を生ませる神業ぞ 心しあらば河よ退け 清きあかき正しき真の言霊も この河神は聞召さずや』

近見男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『岸を洗ふ水の流れは高くとも 神の恵みに渡らむとぞ思ふ よしやよし水の藻屑と消ゆるとも 何か恐れむ神の身われは 国魂の神生みまする旅立に さやる日向の河ぞうたてき 今しばし生言霊を宣り上げて 河守る神を言向和さむ 久方の主の大神の神言もて 国造ります瑞御霊ぞや 瑞御霊めぐみの露の集りて 日向の河の生れしを知らずや 河守の神よ日向の河水よ 心しあれば吾言霊を聴け』

照男の神は謡ひ給ふ。

『月も日も照男の神は此処にあり 河守の神にものを申さむ 久方の天の高日の宮司 みゆきの道よ妨げするな 天は高くまた広くして限りなし 日向の河は帯より狭しも この狭き河の流れを行きなやむ われ神ながら恥かしみ思ふ 広くとも天地の広さに比ぶれば ものの数かは日向の流は』

§ 2

The Muhashira of Kami stood on the bank of the Himukagaha, and while they were chanting Miuta, they were at a loss in their journey; just then, a Megami appeared, straddling a Hakuba, parting the Minase of the Himukagaha to the Saiu. Following behind her, she led Rokutō of horses, pushing through the waves as she advanced toward this side. Akitsuo no Kami, seeing this sight, rejoiced and chanted:

Akitsuo no Kami: 'Ah, the Kototama of the noble Mizumitama has appeared, and the Kahamori no Kami has been born. Now I know the merit of the Kahamori no Kami; how swift and pure are the horses she leads.'

Just as he chanted this, the Kahamori no Kami suddenly ascended the bank together with the horses, leaped down nimbly, and while expressing Keii before the Muhashira of Kami, she spoke:

Kahamori no Kami: 'I shall speak to the Mizumitama, who has been born from the spirit of the Lord God. I am the Himegami who guards and serves the Himukagaha morning and evening. Waiting impatiently for this day's departure to give birth to the Mizumitama Kunitamagami, I pray you mount these horses quickly; I shall dam the flow of the Himukagaha for a while to let you see.'

Here, Akitsuo no Kami, while expressing gratitude, chanted:

Akitsuo no Kami: 'Beyond saying how grateful and humble I am, my words cannot express it. Because of the nobility of the merit of the Kahamori no Kami, my Mitama is being revived. Astride the backs of the Shirogane Harukoma, I shall cross the flow of the Himuka.'

Omononushi no Kami chanted:

Omononushi no Kami: 'The merit of the Kahamori no Kami is indeed noble, for you have even led six horses for us.'

Kahamori no Kami:

Kahamori no Kami: 'These horses are offered to the Kami in our company; please mount the white horses of Heaven in peace.'

Masumi no Kami also chanted:

Masumi no Kami: 'Since I heard the neighing of the white horses, the flow of the Himuka has been parting. The Kahamori no Kami, who was born leading horses from the riverbed, is a noble Himegami.'

Chikamiwo no Kami also chanted:

Chikamiwo no Kami: 'I have no way to repay the sincerity of the Kahamori Himegami. O Kahamori Hime no Mikoto, please guard the Mizumitama and send us to the other shore.'

Akiharu no Kami chanted Miuta:

Akiharu no Kami: 'My heart, which was troubled, is now clear and bright; there is nothing to compare to the joy of the Kami. Through the arrangement of the Kototama of the Kahamori Himegami, we shall cross this swift river in peace.'

Teruo no Kami chanted Miuta:

Teruo no Kami: 'Though I am Teruo no Kami, the sun and moon in the great sky, this river alone had troubled me. I offered the Kototama of the Lord God and waited for the appearance of the Kahamori Kami.'

Thus, every Kami expressed their gratitude. The Kahamori no Kami smiled and replied with Miuta:

Kahamori no Kami: 'I am the Kahamori Hime, born of the water and fire of the heart of the Mizumitama. Since I heard that you were to cross this river, I have been waiting with my horses lined up. These horses are the horses of ten thousand leagues that I raised in Komano-ga-hara; their footing is swift.'

Having chanted thus, she mounted the horse she had ridden in the lead once again, and Akitsuo no Kami and the other five Kami mounted the horses one after another; with the sound of the horses' hooves sounding brave, they reached the opposite bank in a straight line across the wide river that had been dammed.

Here, Kahamori Hime no Kami, looking back at the party of Kami from horseback, chanted:

Kahamori Hime no Kami: 'While the Himukagaha water is shallow, I have ferried the Mizumitama across; now, river, fill up!'

As she declared this, the river water that had been dammed for a while suddenly soaked both banks at once, and the sight of it swirling and flowing looked truly fierce. The Kahamori no Kami, from horseback, pointed toward the forest far in the distance and chanted:

Kahamori no Kami: 'At the end of the vast field as far as the eye can see, my dwelling seems to be shrouded in mist. Come then, Mizumitama and hundred Kami, come to my house and rest for a while. Illuminated by the majesty of the Kototama no Kami, I have held back the river water for a while.'

Chanting this Miuta, she took the lead and hurried toward the forest shade in the far distance. Omononushi no Kami chanted from horseback:

Omononushi no Kami: 'Leaving the Takahi no Miya, crossing over great mountains and small mountains, crossing over many small rivers, here at last is the Himukagaha. When we reached the bank, the Minase was torrential, deep at the bottom, and there was no way to cross. But starting with the Mizumitama, the Kami stood together on the bank, gazing at the flow of the river, and chanted the living Kototama, yet there was no sign, only the waves raging and wild. Just then, the Kahamori Hime no Kami, who was born by parting the riverbed to the Saiu, appeared straddling a Hakuba, leading six white horses, and ascended to this bank. Starting with the Mizumitama, we of the party were given white horses, what joy it was. We mounted the horses and followed behind Kahamori Hime, speeding our feet across the riverbed that had been shallow for a while. When we finally reached the bank, the water of the Himuka river stood up in rough waves all at once and washed the bank. The fierceness of this spectacle felt even stronger than that of a great serpent in a waterfall. The forest misting in the distance is the palace of Kahamori Hime no Kami. In any case, I have accepted the deep divine plan of the Kamikai, and following the Mizumitama, I advance joyfully. Ah, Kannagara, may the Mitama be blessed.'

原文（近代文語）

六柱の神々は、日向河の岸辺に立ち、御歌うたひながら、茫然として行き悩ませ給ふ折しもあれ、日向河の水瀬を左右に割りて、白馬に跨り現れ給ふ女神あり。後方に六頭の駒を従へながら、波を押し分け此方に向かつて進み来るあり。顕津男の神はこの体を見て喜ばせ給ひ、

『あな尊瑞の言霊現れて 河守の神生れましにけり 河守の神の勲を今ぞ知る ひかせる駒の迅さ清さよ』

かく謡ひ給ふ折しも、河守の神は忽ち岸辺に、駒諸共駈け上り給ひ、ひらりと飛び下り、六柱の神の前に敬意を表しながら、

『主の神の霊に生り出で給ひたる 瑞の御霊にものを申さむ われこそは日向の河を朝夕に 守り仕ふる比女神なるぞや 瑞御霊国魂神を生まさむと 今日の旅立ち待ちわびにつつ この駒に早く召しませ日向河の 流も暫しせきとめて見む』

ここに顕津男の神は感謝しながら、

『ありがたし忝しと申すより 吾が言の葉は出でざりにけり 河守の神のいさをの尊さに わがたましひは甦りつつ 白銀の春駒の背に跨りて われは越えなむ日向の流を』

大物主の神は謡ひ給ふ。

『河守の神のいさをぞ尊けれ 六つの駒までひかせ給ひつ』

河守の神。

『この駒は御供の神に参らする 天の白駒安く召しませ』

真澄の神はまた謡ひ給ふ。

『白駒の嘶く声を聞きしより 日向の河の流割れつつ 河底ゆ駒ひきつれて生れませる 河守の神は貴の比女神』

近見男の神はまた謡ひ給ふ。

『河守の比女神たちの真心に 報いむ術もわれなかりける 河守の比女のみことよ瑞霊を 守りて彼岸に送りたまはれ』

明晴の神は御歌詠まし給ふ。

『なやみてし心も今や明晴の 神の嬉しさたとへむものなし 河守比女神の神言のはからひに この速河を安く渡らむ』

照男の神は御歌うたひ給ふ。

『大空に月日照男の神ながら この河のみはなやみたりける 主の神に瑞の言霊宣り上げて 河守神の出でまし待ちしよ』

と何れの神も、感謝の意を表し給ふ。河守の神はにこやかに、御歌もて答へ給ふ。

『われこそは瑞の御霊の御心の 水火より生れし河守比女よ この河を岐美渡らすと聞きしより 駒を並べて待ち居たりける この駒は駒野ケ原にわが飼ひし 万里の駒よ足元迅し』

かく謡ひ給ひ、真つ先に乗り来し駒に再び跨り給へば、顕津男の神を初めとし、五柱の神はつぎつぎ馬背に跨り、せきとめられし広河を、駒の蹄の音も勇ましく、一文字に彼方の岸に着き給ひける。

ここに河守比女の神は、馬上より一行の神を見返りながら、

『日向河水あせにつつ瑞御霊 渡しまつりぬいざ河満てよ』

と、宣り給ふや、暫くせきとめられし河水は、一度にどつと両岸を浸しつつ、渦巻き立ちて流るるさま、実に凄じく見えにける。河守の神は馬上より、遥か彼方の森林を指ざし乍ら、

『見の限り広き大野の末にして わが住む館はかすみけらしな いざさらば瑞の御霊よ百神よ わが家に来りて暫し休ませ 言霊の神の稜威に照らされて われは河水しばしとどめし』

と御歌うたひつつ先に立たせ、遥か彼方の森蔭さして急ぎ給ふ。大物主の神は馬上豊かに謡ひ給ふ。

『高日の宮を立ち出でて 大山小山打ち渡り 小川の数々うち越えて ここにいよいよ日向河 岸辺につけば滔々と 水瀬はげしく底深く 渡らむよしも無かりしが 瑞の御霊をはじめとし 神々ともに岸に立ち 河の流れを眺めつつ 生言霊を宣りつれど 何のしるしもあら波の 伊猛り狂ふばかりなり 折しもあれや河底を 左右にわけて生れませる 河守比女の神司 白馬に跨り悠々と 六つの白駒引きつれて 此方の岸にのぼりまし 瑞の御霊をはじめとし

われら一行白駒を 与へ給ひし嬉しさよ われら馬背に跨りて 河守比女の後より 暫しあせたる河底を 足を速めて飛ばせつつ 漸く岸に着きぬれば 日向の河の河水は 一度にどつと荒波を 立てつつ岸を洗ひ行く この光景の凄まじさ 滝の大蛇のそれよりも 一入強く感じけり 彼方にかすむ森林は 河守比女の神館 何はともあれ神界の 深き経綸を諾ひつ 瑞の御霊に従ひて われは楽しく進むなり ああ惟神々々

御霊幸倍坐世よ』

ここに瑞の御霊顕津男の神の一行六柱は、漸く河守比女の神館に駒を下り、奥庭深く入り給ふ。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
