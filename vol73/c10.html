Chapter 10: 婚ぎの御 Song [1841]

婚ぎの御歌〔1841〕

§ 1

Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami, fearing the Ōmikoto of the Lord God, determined Takanohime-no-Kami, who was born of the Tokijiku-no-Kagu-no-Konomi, to be his Seihi, and there performed the Kamuwaza of Yorizashi before the Mimahe of the Lord God, leading the Yahoyorozu of Matsuritsukasagami to chant solemn Norito, and having Mita the Amenomihashira and the Kuninomihashira, the Ogami circled the Mihashira from the left and the Megami from the right, and again before the Mimahe, he spoke the Futonorito as follows:

‘I humbly and reverently pray before the Mimahe of the Lord God who dwells in the Kakemaku-mo-aya-ni-tōtoki Hisakata-no-Miyai. The Hisakata-no-Takaamahara is clear and bright, the Kuniguni of this Shibitenkai are pure and clear, the Tanatsumono are ripening in abundance, and the fruits of the trees are ripening with branches bending low; the Kamikuni of the divine Yorizashi has now been born clearly and in detail before our eyes. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, by the noble grace of Kami, as we determine this good day and this good hour to be the marriage bond, that Kami declares that I shall take Takanohime-no-Kami as my wife and govern the world is truly noble beyond measure. From this moment, I receive the Ōmikoto of the Lord God into my body, and to the limits of the heavenly walls and the boundaries of the earthly retreats, with a pure and righteous heart of Amatsu-Makoto, I shall soothe and cherish all Kami, pacify the rough Kami, and serve the Ōmikoto. I look up and pray that, receiving the divine authority of the Lord God, you may bring the Kamuwaza of Yorizashi to completion. Iroha nihoheto chirinuruo, wakayo tareso tsune naramu, uwi no okuyama kefu koete, asaki yume mishi ehi mo sesu; without forgetting this good day even for a moment, and forevermore, making the heart of sincerity the warp and weaving the sincerity of love and goodness as the woof, without tangling the threads of the divine loom, and without disorder, I shall serve the Great Keirin of the age of Kami. From the Lord God above to the end of the hundred Kami below, I pledge my sincerity and serve; I declare that the Lord God and the Yahoyorozu-no-Kami should hear this with peace and tranquility.’

Here, Takanohime-no-Kami chants the marriage blessing with a clear voice that resonates through the universe. Her song:

‘In the Hisakata sky there are no clouds, the water and fire of Su are clear and bright, illuminating the Kamikuni. The Great Taiyo shall shine its divine authority until the end of the universe, prospering day by day. This auspicious sign of the Kamikuni is awe-inspiring. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand Kami, may my Kamuwaza be protected, and may I repay the Ōmikoto of the Lord God. Takanohime’s sincerity, please accept; although I am but a Megami, I receive the divine authority and light of the Lord God upon my neck, and together with my husband Kami, uniting our hearts and joining our strength as one, like the number of grains of sand on the beach, we shall give birth to noble children, distribute them throughout the world, and serve the Kamuwaza of the Great Keirin. How joyful it is. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu. Akitsuo-no-Kami-Hikogami, fill my weak soul with noble strength, and serve the Kamuwaza of Yorizashi clearly and in detail. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand; this good day of blessing and completion is awe-inspiring.’

The Kami who gathered for today’s marriage blessing were Tōtsumiyuki-no-Kami, Katateru-no-Kami, Tamanoo-no-Kami, Hinomoto-no-Kami, and twelve others.

Tōtsumiyuki-no-Kami, not weary of the distance of ten million ri, crossed the Ama-no-Ukihashi and was the first to arrive at this banquet. Tōtsumiyuki-no-Kami offered a blessing. His song:

‘In the great palace of the Lord God in the heavens, born of the Tokijiku-no-Kagu-no-Konomi, Takanohime-no-Kami descends to this place from the southwestern heavens. Determined to be the wife of Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami, solemnly circling the Amenomihashira and solidifying the Kuninomihashira, she becomes the flower of Takaamahara and looks down upon all Kami; how noble she is. I, fearing the Kamikoto of the Lord God, have traveled west, east, south, and north, without distinction of near or far, teaching the Kamikuni and spreading the way, a Kamibashira serving the Kamuwaza. Seeing today’s celebration, I have realized even more deeply the endless, pure, and noble Kamuwaza of the divine Keirin. The western Miyai is the Kami of the Ama-no-Michitachi, and in the eastern palace is Ōmotoakitsuo-no-Kami, each governing their own.’

原文（近代文語）

太元顕津男の神は主の大神の大神言を畏み、非時の香具の木の実に生りませる高野比女の神を正妃と定めて、茲に依さしの神業を大神の御前に執行はせ給ひ、八百万の祭司神を率ゐて厳かなる祝詞を奏し給ひ、天之御柱、国之御柱を見立て給ひて、男神は左より、女神は右より御柱を廻り、再び神前に太祝詞白し給はく、

『掛巻も綾に尊き久方の貴の宮居に鎮まり給ふ主の大神の御前に慎み敬ひ願白さく。久方の高天原は澄みきらひ、此の紫微天界の国々は清く清しく、五穀は豊かに実り木の実は枝もたわわに熟しつつ、神の依さしの神国は今目のあたり〓__GT_13105__怜に委曲に生れましぬ。嗚呼惟神々々神の尊き御恵に、今日の良き日の良き辰を婚ぎの綱と定めつつ、高野比女の神を妻となし世を治めよと神の宣らすこそ実にも尊き限りなれ。吾は之より主の神の大神言葉を身に受けて、天の壁立極み国の退立限り、清き正しき天津誠の心以てあらゆる神を撫で慈しみ、荒振神を言向和し大神言に仕へ奉らむとす。仰ぎ願はくは主の大神の御稜威を蒙ぶりて、御依さしの神業を生り遂げさせ給へ、いろはにほへとちりぬるを、わかよたれそつねならむ、うゐのおくやまけふこえて、あさきゆめみしゑひもせす、けふの良き日を微日の間も、忘るる事なく何時迄も、誠の心を経となし、愛と善との真心を緯に織なし、御機の糸の縺れなく、乱れもあらに神の世の大御経綸に仕へ奉らむ。上は主の大御神より下百神の端までも、吾真心を誓ひ奉り仕へ奉る事の由を、主の大神を初めとし、八百万の神平けく安けく聞召さへと宣る』

ここに高野比女の神は、婚ぎの神祝言を声朗らかに宇宙に響けと謡ひ給ふ。その御歌、

『久方の空に雲なくスの水火は 澄み切り澄みきらひて神国を照す 大太陽は宇宙のあらむ限りまで 稜威を伊照し給ひて日々に栄行く 神国の此の瑞祥ぞ畏けれ 一二三四五六七八九十 百千万の神達に わが神業を守られて 主の大神の大御心に 報い奉らむ高野比女の真心を 諾ひませよ吾はしも女神の身にしあれど 主の大神の稜威と御光を頸に受けて 吾夫の神と諸共に 心を合せ力を一に結び合せ 浜の真砂の数の如 貴の御子をば生み生みて 普く世界に分り配り 大経綸の神業に 仕へ奉るぞ嬉しけれ 嗚呼惟神々々

顕津男の神比古神は 妾の弱き魂を 貴の力にみなぎらせ 神の依さしの神業を 〓__GT_13105__怜に委曲に仕へませ 一二三四五六七八九十百千万と 祝ひ納むる今日の良き日ぞ畏けれ』

今日の婚ぎの神祝に伊寄り集ひし神々は、遠津御幸の神、片照の神、魂之男の神、日之本の神以下十六柱におはせり。

遠津御幸の神は千万里の遠きを厭はず、天の浮橋を打渡りつつ真先に此の宴席に集ひ玉ひけり。遠津御幸の神は祝し給ふ。其の御歌、

『天なるや主の大神の大宮に 非時実る香具の木の実と生れまして 高野比女の神は西南の天より此所に降ります 太元顕津男の神の女と定まりて 厳かに天の御柱廻り合ひ 国の御柱固めつつ 高天原の花となり 諸神の上に望ませ給ふぞ尊けれ 吾は主の大御神の神言畏み 西東南や北とかけ廻り 近き遠きの差別なく 神国を教へ道布きつ 神業に仕へる神柱 今日の寿ぎ見るにつけ 神の経綸の果てしなく 清く尊き神業を 愈深く覚りけり 西の宮居は天の道立の神 東の宮には太元顕津男の神 各も各もに領有ぎ給ふ

§ 2

This Mikuni is where the Iki of Iroha is clear and serene, without turbidity or cloudiness. The Amatsuhi shines brightly in the Nakazora, radiating Kuōsai of Nanairo. The Shibitenkai glows and shines even more abundantly. A hundred Kamigami rise up with courage, hastening to today's Minori, meeting with joy unknown before. How blissful it is before this Mimahe! We celebrate and revere it through Chiyo and Yachiyo. Ah, Kamunagara, let the Mitama be blessed and abide!'

Notes

When we interpret the Kototama chanted by Akitsuo no Kami as 'Iroha ni hoheto chirinuru wo',

'I' is the symbol of water and fire lined up; the right is water, the left is fire.

'Ro' is the Kototama where water and fire solidify to become water and fire, opening in the Uchiu, which is called 'ha'.

The Kototama opening in the Uchiu and functioning in Zengosaiu like a figure is 'ni'.

Through this Hataraki, a single point appears, which is the Kototama of 'ho'.

The state where 'ho' rises gradually higher, swells, and expands is called 'he'.

The Kototama of 'to' is the completed Kototama of water and fire.

Water and fire complete and produce Jimi in the Uchiu, which is called 'chi'. 'Chi' is the meaning of mother's milk nurturing a child. Also, it is the meaning of the great earth, which is the management arena for Banbutsuhatsusei.

The Kototama of 'ri' is the Kototama where the male and female two deities combine water and fire and stand side by side.

The Kototama of 'nu' is the symbol of water and fire devoting themselves to divine work with minds mutually harmonized, sleeping, and warm.

The Kototama of 'ru' is the Way of husband and wife or all the ordinances of the heavenly realm.

The 'wo' is the heart.

The 'wa' speaks of the state of harmonizing, mingling, and giving birth to divine children.

The 'ka' is the meaning of embracing each other and shining together, and what common speech calls kakan is also the meaning of this Kototama.

The 'yo' is the symbol of the husband and wife two deities establishing a household.

The 'ta' is the meaning of perfection and sufficiency.

The 're' is the meaning of the husband singing and the wife following.

The 'so' is the meaning of the upper, lower, left, and right being aligned. It is the state of clapping hands with the five fingers of the left and the five fingers of the right put together.

The 'tsu' is the meaning of continuing eternally, and what people of the world call 'up to the eight thousand generations of the yamatsubaki' is also the same.

The 'ne' is intimate and is the meaning of husband and wife sleeping together.

The 'na' is the symbol of the two lining up and sleeping.

The 'ra' is the meaning of left rotation and right rotation, speaking of the state at the time of marriage.

The 'mu' is the meaning of steaming, generating, and producing sons and daughters.

The 'u' is the meaning of moisture, and also the Kototama that is emitted in a heaven-vanishing and earth-perishing case.

The 'wi' is the meaning of the time when pleasure reaches its extreme.

The 'no' is the meaning of moisture gushing forth from a single object.

The 'o' is a heart calmly settled.

The 'ku' is the symbol of husband and wife joined together.

The 'ya' is the meaning of more and more.

The 'ma' is the meaning of guarding the Way of husband and wife for tens of thousands of years with a sincere heart.

The 'ke' is the meaning of the body's defilement.

The 'fu' is the Kototama that blows away, meaning to blow away the defilement of man and woman.

The 'ko' is the child,

The 'e' is the placenta.

The 'te' is the meaning of shining brightly, meaning to shine the fire at the end of the divine work in the dark night.

The 'a' is the meaning of everything appearing through the fire lit in a dark room.

The 'sa' is the meaning of avoiding; the male deity avoids seeing the face of the female deity, and the female deity feels shy and avoids seeing the face of the male deity.

The 'ki' is the meaning of spirits rising high and the heart becoming eager.

The 'yu' is the meaning of abundance, a Kototama where relations have become favorable.

The 'me' is the meaning of the seed of the divine child beginning to lodge, just like blowing out a tree bud.

The 'mi' is the Kototama that has gradually become a fetus.

The 'shi' is the meaning of completion, the Kototama where here at last they settle down like a husband and wife for the first time.

The 'we' is the meaning of rejoicing and being glad, the Kototama of smiling and flourishing mutually upon seeing the birth of the child.

The 'hi' is the meaning of Hiko and Hime.

The 'mo' is the meaning of gathering together, the Kototama of family harmony.

The 'se' is the meaning of the rapids of a river, the Kototama of a state where there is not a single point of perversity in the relationship between husband and wife and it is pure.

The 'su' is the Kototama where it finally clears up completely, and parent and child live affectionately in the world.

Ah, how reverent.

In the world, there are those who believe that this Iroha song was made by the priest Kūkai, but that is a mistake. The Iroha song is a divine song that resounded from the Kototama of Taien Akitsuo no Kami in the ancient times of the heavenly pole Shibikyu, and Kūkai merely created the Hiragana characters to express them in simple and easy characters. Therefore, you must know that the Iroha song was not chanted by Kūkai. In all, the Iroha song is not only the meaning of marriage, but it is something that chants the truth of the generation of everything in the universe.

原文（近代文語）

此の神国はいろはの水火も澄みきらひ 濁らひも無く曇りなし 大太陽は中天に 輝き給ひ七色の 光彩を放たせ給ひつ 紫微天界は弥益も 光り輝き渡らひつ 百の神々勇み立ち 今日の御式に馳せ寄りて 例も知らぬ喜びに 逢ふぞ嬉しき此の神前 千代に八千代に寿ぎ奉る 嗚呼惟神神霊幸ひ坐ませよ』

ろは水と火の固まりて水火となり、宇宙に開く言霊を、はといふ。

言霊宇宙に開きて前後左右に活用く象は、に也。

此の活動によりて一つのヽ現はる、即ち、ほの言霊也。

ほは次第に高く昇り膨れ拡がる態を、へといふ。

との言霊は水火の完成したる言霊也。

水火完成して宇宙に滋味を生ず、之をちといふ。ちは子を育つる母乳の意也。又万物発生の経綸場たる大地の意也。

りの言霊は女男二神水火を合せて並び立たせる言霊也。

ぬの言霊は互に和らぎ寝み温かき心を以て神業に尽す水火の象也。

るは夫婦の道又は天界の総ての定まりし言霊也。

をは心也。

わは和らぎ睦み御子を生み給ふ態を言ふ也。

そは上下四方揃ふ意也。左右の指の五本と五本と合せて拍手せし態也。

あなかしこ。

世には此のいろは歌を以て僧空海の作りたるものと信ずるものあれども誤りなり。いろは歌は天極紫微宮の昔、太元顕津男の神の言霊より鳴り出でし神歌にして、空海は只平易簡単に文字に現さむとして平仮名文字を作り出したるものなり。故にいろは歌は空海の詠みしものにあらざることを知るべし。すべていろは歌は婚ぎの意味のみに非ず、宇宙万有一切発生の真理を謡へるものなり。

§ 3

Commentary on the Shiyukuka of Takanohime-no-Kami.

The song of one, two, three, and four, which Takanohime-no-Kami proclaimed on the occasion of the Giyoen of marriage:

One is Rei, fire, and the sun.

Two is power, and the blowing Iki.

Three is body, and Genso.

Four is the world of the Sekai.

Five is to emerge.

Six is to burn.

Seven is to become the earth.

Eight is the meaning of Iyoiyo Masumasu.

Nine is the meaning of congealing and hardening.

Ten is the meaning of Kuwansei.

One hundred is the meaning of Moromoro.

One thousand is light, and the blood of Chishiho.

Ten thousand is the meaning of emerging in the night.

If one summarizes and briefly explains this, it is a Kototama meaning that the Sekai emerges through Reiriyokutai, the Iki of Suikuwa burns up, the earth is formed for the first time, the energy of Suikuwa Iyoiyo Masumasu congeals and hardens, the Kuwanzenmuketsu Uchiu Tenkai is completed, and the light of the Moromoro earth appears in the Anya and enters the eyes of all things; one should know that this was sung to praise the Shintoku of the Zaukuwasanshin, and to express the intention of emulating that virtue to Shiurikosei the Shibitenkai and to illuminate the pure place where the Shiyoshin may Anjiu.

原文（近代文語）

高野比女の神が婚ぎの御宴に際し言挙げ給ひたる一二三四の歌、

一は霊也、火也、日也。

二は力也、吹く呼吸也。

三は体也、元素也。

四は世界の世也。

五は出る也。

六は燃る也。

七は地成る也。

八は弥々益々の意也。

九は凝り固るの意也。

十は完成の意也。

百は諸々の意也。

千は光也、血汐の血也。

万は夜出るの意也。

之を大括して略解すれば、霊力体によつて世が発生し、水火の呼吸燃え上り、初めて地成り、弥々益々水火の気凝り固りて完全無欠の宇宙天界は完成され、諸々の地の光は暗夜に出現して総てのものの目に入るといふ言霊にして、造化三神の神徳を称へ奉り、其の徳にあやかりて紫微天界を修理固成し、諸神安住の清所に照さむとの意を謳ひ給ひしものと知るべし。
