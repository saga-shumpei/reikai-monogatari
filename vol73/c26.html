Chapter 26: 主 Kami の降臨 [1857]

主神の降臨〔1857〕

§ 1

The great Orochi, lurking in the depths of the Nakatsutaki, a waterfall of ten thousand fathoms that hangs high in the upper reaches of the Takaterugaha, raised clouds and brought down rain through the Kototama of the Amatsumakoto of the Ōmihaha-Kami and the Masumi-Kami, and though it fled high into the Ōzora, the Eujiya-no-ki enveloped the Tanidani of the mountains; the Seizan, once so pure, was constantly covered by Kurokumo, and at times Baufū would arise, toppling Jiyumoku, destroying Miyai, and bringing down Gaū to torment the Shiyoshin, while destroying Denpata, and as this harm grew more severe day by day and month by month, the Kamuzukasa of the Takahi-no-miya gathered the Shiyoshin, and to remove the harm from Tenchi, they built a new Saidan solemnly before the Ōmimahe, placed Kusagusa-no-umashimono of the mountains, fields, and Kahaumi upon a table of eight legs as an offering, presented sake, food, water, and salt, and offered prayers for the peace of the land.

Akitsuo-no-Kami purified his body in the Tama-no-miike that welled up bubbling from within the residence, and with a clear voice, he offered the Amatsunorito before the Ōmimahe and prayed. His declaration was as follows:

‘With awe and reverence, I address the Lord Ōmikami, who dwells in stillness having erected the thick pillars of the great palace in the Amatsutakamiya, and I humbly revere and worship from afar before the Ōmimahe of the Lord of the Heavenly Peak Fire. Originally, this Shibi-no-tenkai was brought into existence by the Kototama of the Amatsumakoto through the Ōmikoto of the Ōmikami; the plants and trees of the mountains and fields flourished day and night, the hundred Kami enjoyed a beautiful age of the gods, the morning and evening winds were gentle, the falling rain was moderate, and as it moistened all things, the Kamikuni, as ordained by Kami, appeared here in its entirety. While those hundred Kami praised the great divine work of the Ōmikami, rejoiced, and fulfilled their sincerity of the hempen pillar, and while the Shibi-no-tenkai grew ever more vast day by day and month by month, opening ever wider and flourishing ever more, at that very moment, a hideous Orochi lurked and dwelt in the Nakatsutaki, where the waterfall falls from the high peaks of the Takateruyama, harming the myriad Kami, and there was nothing born in heaven or growing on earth that did not suffer its calamity. Therefore, the hundred Kami have gathered here at this sacred site, and with the Norito of the Amatsumakoto, we humbly pray to soothe the heart of the Ōmikami, to blow away the clouds and mist of the four directions, and to restore the age of the gods to its former state. May you hear this state of affairs peacefully and calmly, may you grant your consent, may you listen with compassion and in detail to our sincerity of the hempen pillar, and may you protect this Kamikuni so that there may be no evil, no impurity, so that the raging Kami may hide their shadows, so that all may return to the path of the true word, and so that we may all serve the divine work for the sake of the Kamikuni,’ the Kamuzukasa of the Takahi-no-miya, Akitsuo-no-Kami, prayed with reverence.

As he spoke the Futo-norito-goto with a clear voice, a fragrant wind arose from the peaks of the Takateruyama, the Eujiya-no-ki was instantly blown away, the Kurokumo that had enveloped the mountain ridges vanished without a trace, purple clouds drifted across, and the light of the Amatsubi and the light of the moon shone brilliantly upon the earth, which was truly awe-inspiring. Suddenly, subtle music resounded in the four directions, and while holding flags of red, white, blue, purple, and yellow in their hands, the eighty Kami served before the tail of the Lord Ōmikami; at the majesty of his descending calmly into the pure courtyard of the Takahi-no-miya, Akitsuo-no-Kami, the Ōmihaha-Kami, the Ōmononushi-no-Kami, and the other Shiyoshin knelt in the pure courtyard of the palace, and while struck by the solemn divine spirit, they welcomed him with reverence. Akitsuo-no-Kami bowed three times to the Lord Ōmikami with great respect, served before his tail himself, and invited him onto the Shiseiden of the Takahi-no-miya.

Akitsuo-no-Kami, having witnessed the descent of the Lord Ōmikami, could not contain his awe, and in his excess of reverence, he trembled even in his voice and recited a poem with trepidation:

‘Awe-inspiring is the Lord Kami, Who descends from heaven in majesty. Descending from the Takachiho mountains, I have come here now to give birth to Kami. Born of the living Kototama of the Ōmikami, The Shibi-no-tenkai is a beautiful Kamikuni. How solemn is the Lord Ōmikami, Who has descended to the Takahi-no-miya in the distant sky. I pray that you grant me strength, To protect this Kamikuni eternally. As days and months have passed, The air of Eujiya has welled up. How shall I dispel this evil spirit? I ask for the divine word of the Lord Ōmikami.’

原文（近代文語）

高照河の上流に、高くかかれる万丈の瀑布中津滝の淵に潜める大蛇は、大御母の神、真澄の神たちの天津真言の言霊によりて雲を起し雨を降らせつつ、大空高く逃げ去りたれども、妖邪の気は山の谷々を包みて、さしもに清き聖山も黒雲常に覆ひて時に暴風を起し、樹木を倒し宮居を破り、豪雨を降らして諸神を苦しめ、田畑を破り其害日に月に烈しくなりければ、高日の宮の神司は諸神を集めて、天地の害を除かむと大御前に厳めしく祭壇を新に造り、山の物、野の物、河海の種々の美味物を八足の机代に置き足はし、御酒御饌御水堅塩を奉りて国土平安の祈願を籠め給ふ。

顕津男の神は、邸内より滾々として湧き出づる玉の御池に身を滌ぎ、声も清しく大前に天津祝詞を奏上し祈り給ふ。其宣言、

『掛巻も綾に恐き、天津高宮に大宮柱太しき立てて鎮まり坐す主の大御神、天の峯火夫の大神の大御前を謹み敬ひ、遥に遥に拝みまつる。抑々これの紫微の天界は、大御神の大御言もて天津真言の言霊に生り出で給ひ、山野の草木は日に夜に栄え、百神達は美しき神代を楽しみ、朝夕の風も和やかに、降り来る雨もほどほどに、総ての物を浸しつつ、神の依さしの神国は全く茲に現れぬ。彼百神達は大御神の大神業を称へまつり、喜びまつりて麻柱の誠を尽し、紫微天界は日に月に弥広々く、弥開けに開け弥栄えに栄えける折しもあれ、高照山の峰高く、落ち来る滝の其中津滝に醜の大蛇の潜み棲みて、万の神々を害ひまつり、天に成るもの地に生ふるもの悉く其の禍を蒙らざるものなし。故ここをもて、百の神々をこれの斎場に神集ひ、天津真言の祝詞もて大御神の御心を和め奉り、四方の雲霧吹き払ひ神代の昔にかへし奉らむと、謹み敬ひ願ぎ申す。此有様を平らけく安らけく聞召し相諾ひ給ひて、我等が麻柱の誠を〓__GT_13105__怜に委曲に聞し召し、これの神国は曲もなく汚れなく荒ぶる神は影ひそめ、真言の道に立ち帰り、共々に神国のため神業に仕ふべく、守らせ給へと高日の宮の神司、顕津男の神謹み祈り奉る』

と声も爽やかに太祝詞言宣り給へば、高照山の峰より、香しき風吹き起りて、妖邪の気は忽ち吹き払はれ、尾上を包みし黒雲は跡なく消えて紫の雲棚曳きわたり、天津日の光、月の光は皓々として地上に光を投げ給ふぞ畏けれ。忽ち四辺に微妙の音楽響き、紅白青紫黄色の旗を手に手に翳しつつ、八十の神達は主の大神の御尾前に仕へつ、高日の宮の清庭に悠然として天降ります尊さに、顕津男の神、大御母の神、大物主の神其他の諸神は宮の清庭に拝跪し、荘厳の神気に打たれ乍ら、謹み敬ひ迎へまつる。顕津男の神は恭々しく主の大神を三拝し、自ら御尾前に仕へまつり、高日の宮の至聖殿上に招ぎまつりける。

顕津男の神は主の大御神の降臨を拝しまつりて恐懼に堪へず、謹みの余り御声までも慄はせ給ひて恐る恐る御歌詠ませ給ひける。

『かけまくも綾に畏き主の神の 天降りますこそ尊かりける 高地秀の山を下りて我は今 神を生まむとここに来つるも 大神の生言霊に生り出でし 紫微天界はうまし神国よ 久方の高日の宮に天降りましし 主の大神の厳々しきかも 願はくはわれに力を賜へかし この神国を永久に守るべく 日を重ね月を閲してやうやくに 妖邪の空気は湧き出でにけり いかにして此の邪気をば払はむかと 主の大神の神言請ひける』

§ 2

Here the Lord Ōkami stood in Genzen, and while holding a jewel in His Yunde and waving a wand in His Mete in the manner of Saiusa, He solemnly proclaimed:

Speaker: 'O land where the Kototama of Amate shines, if the Makoto of the Kototama grows turbid, the land shall fall into chaos. If the resonance of the Ikukototama is not heard morning and evening, this Tenkai shall grow cloudy and chaotic. The Kototama is the power of all things. Purify your hearts and proclaim it morning and evening. From the Miiki of the Kototama of the clear and bright Su, righteous and noble Kami shall be born. The Kototama of the turbid Kami congeals in the world, and thus Magakamitachi are born. Know that even the ugly Orochi of Takateruyama is the retribution for the turbid Kototama of the Kamigami.'

Having finished proclaiming this Ōmikotonori, the Lord Ōkami hid His Misugata as if vanishing upon the Shiseidenjiyō. Here, Akitsuo no Kami, though he had been proclaiming the Ikukototama morning and evening, realized that there was some cloudiness and turbidity within his own Mitama, and repenting greatly, he chanted a Miuta to convey the depth of his heart to the Momogamitachi:

Speaker: 'Though it be presumptuous, I, the Kamutsukasa of the Takahi no Miya, shall speak before the Momogamitachi. I was struck by the Ikukototama of the Lord Kami who descended from the Hisakata no Ame. While purifying my Mitama through Misogi morning and evening, my heart remains turbid and wretched. Since Yukuehime-sama departed, has my heart grown cloudy, or has the Age of the Kami grown cloudy? Fearing the teachings of the Lord Kami, from this day forth, I shall wash my soul. The Yasohime-sama whom the Lord Kami bestowed upon me were also solely for the sake of Kuniumi. Alas, I have wandered, led astray by the heart that knows love and longing, before the Hime-sama. Even loving the Kami and longing for the Kami, if it be not for the sake of Makoto, the world shall be disordered. While making Yasohime-sama weep with love and longing, I ponder my own errors in restraint. How wretched it was to fear the slander of the Bon-kami and seek to protect my own peace.

O Momogamitachi, take heed; from this day forth, I shall serve the Kototama of the Lord Kami. No matter how wisely the Momogamitachi may deliberate, I shall not deviate from the mission of the Kami.'

Having chanted thus, Akitsuo no Kami repented greatly that he had been unable to serve the divine work of Kuniumi and Kamiumi in accordance with the Ōmikotonori of the Lord Ōmikami, attributing it to the impurity of his own heart, and declared that henceforth, no matter how the Bon-kami might speak or slander him, he would cast aside his own honor and respond to the divine command with single-minded devotion. Here, the Kamigami lined up and listened respectfully to the divine words of the Lord Ōmikami, and accepting the declaration of the Kamutsukasa, they repented as if for the first time that they had been obstructing the divine work due to the cloudiness of their own small hearts, and first, the Ōmioya no Kami proclaimed words of apology:

Speaker: 'I grieve for myself, who knew not the divine work of the Kamutsukasa born by the mission of the Lord Kami. No matter what divine obstruction there may be, be valiant for the sake of the divine work. I measured the small hearts of the Momogamitachi and disobeyed the divine words of the Lord Kami. I, too, failed to grasp the divine words of the Lord Kami and thought like a Bon-kami. You are the Kamutsukasa of the mission of Ame; you are not a Kami to be compared with us. From this day forth, I shall set right the horse of my heart and serve you, the Ōmioya no Kami.'

Ōmononushi no Kami chanted a Miuta:

Speaker: 'O Kamutsukasa of the Mitama of the moon that crosses the Ame, should the Momogamitachi deliberate? I knew not the divine work of you, who scatter the seeds of the Lord far and wide. The Hi no Kami has the divine work of the Hi, and the Moon has the divine work of the Moon; each has its own divine work. I ponder the foolishness of the heart that judges the Ōkami with the sincerity of a Bon-kami. The sincerity of a Bon-kami differs when compared to the Makoto of the Moon no Ōkami. The divine work that the Ōkami declares to be good, and that which appears evil to the eyes of the Bon-kami—the divine work that the Bon-kami thought good has become an obstruction to the Ōkami-waza. The Kamigami have their respective duties; how should a small Kami deliberate?'

Here, Akitsuo no Kami, rejoicing that the two Kami had somewhat understood his divine work, chanted a Miuta:

Speaker: 'At the Kototama of Ōmioya and Ōmononushi, my heart has begun to find peace. From this day forth, I shall not listen to the whispers of the ugly, and I shall serve before the Kami with an open heart. My heart was darkened by the figure of Yukuehime-sama, and it seems my Kototama grew turbid. If there are Kami who accept my divine work, my heart and soul shall not grow cloudy. If my heart grows cloudy, the Kototama will instantly grow turbid, and the Age of the Kami will seek to fall into chaos. What must be feared is the heart and the Kototama. I shall wash and purify them morning and evening.'

Having thus proclaimed with an open heart, he clarified the divine work bestowed upon him by the Lord Ōkami and declared that he would carry it out without fear or hesitation; this is known as the foundation of the origin of the Tenkai Keirin.

原文（近代文語）

ここに主の大神は儼然として立たせ給ひ、左手に玉をかかへ右手に幣を左右左と打ち振りながら、厳かに宣り給ふ。

『言霊の天照る国よ言霊の 真言濁れば国は乱れむ 朝夕に生言霊の響なくば この天界は曇り乱れむ 言霊は総てのものの力なり 心清めて朝夕宣れよ 澄みきらふスの言霊の御水火より 正しき尊き神はうまれむ 濁りたる神の言霊世に凝りて 曲神達は生れ出づるなり 高照山醜の大蛇も神々の 言霊濁れる酬いとこそ知れ』

斯く大神宣を宣り終りて、主の大神は至聖殿上に消ゆるが如く神姿をかくし給ひぬ。茲に顕津男の神は朝な夕なに生言霊を宣りまつれども、わが霊魂のいづくにか曇り濁りのある事を悟りて大に悔い給ひ、百神達に向つて、心の丈をのべ伝ふべく御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『おほけなくも高日の宮の神司 百神達の御前に申さむ 久方の天より降りし主の神の 生言霊にわれ打たれける 朝夕を禊に霊魂清めつつ 未だ濁れるわが魂うたてき 如衣比女神去りしよりわが心 曇りしものか神代は曇りつ 主の神の教畏み今日よりは わが魂を洗はむと思ふ 主の神のわれにたまひし八十比女も ただ国生みのためなりにけり 恋すてふ心起りていたづらに 迷ひぬるかな比女神の前に 神を愛し神を恋ふるも誠心の ために非ずば世は乱るべし 八十比女を愛と恋とに泣かせつつ われつつしみの違へるを思ふ 凡神のそしりを恐れ身を安く 守らせむとせし事のうたてさ

百神よ心したまへ今日よりは われ主の神の神言に仕へむ 百神はいかに賢しくはかゆとも 神の依さしに我は違はじ』

と謡ひ給ひて顕津男の神は、主の大御神の大神宣のまにまに、国生み神生みの神業に仕へまつり得ざりし事を、わが心の汚きより出でしものと大に悔い給ひ、今後は凡神達如何に言はかり譏り合ふとも、自己の名誉を捨てて只管に神命に応へむと宣言し給ひしなり。茲に居列ぶ神々は主の大御神の神言を謹聴し、又宮司の宣言を諾ひ、己が小さき心の曇りより神業を妨害し居たる事を今更の如く悔い給ひて、先づ大御母の神よりお詫の言霊を宣り給ふ。

『主の神の依さしに生れし宮司の 神業知らざりし吾を悲しむ 如何ならむ神の妨げありとても 神業の為めには雄々しくませよ 百神の小さき心を押しはかり 主の神言に背きたまひし 吾も亦主の神言を悟らずて 凡神のごと思ひけらしな 岐美こそは天の依さしの神司 われ等に比すべき神におはさず 今日よりは心の駒を立て直し 岐美に仕へむ大御母の神は』

大物主の神は御歌詠まし給ふ。

『天渡る月の御霊の宮司 百神達のはかゆべしやは 主の胤を彼方此方にまくばらす 岐美の神業を知らざりにけり 日の神は日の神月は月の神 おのもおのもに神業ありける 凡神の誠をもちて大神を 議ゆる心の愚しさを思ふ 凡神の誠は月の大神の 真言に比して差別ありける 大神のよしと宣らする神業も 凡神の目に悪しと見ゆるも 凡神のよしと思ひし神業は 大神業の妨げとなりぬ 神々はそれ相当の職務あり 小さき神の議ゆべきかは』

茲に顕津男の神は、二神のわが神業をやや諒解したる事を喜び給ひて、御歌よまし給ふ。

『大御母大物主の言霊に 我は心もなごみ初めたり 今日よりは醜の囁き耳とせず 我おほらかに神前に仕へむ 如衣比女の姿に心暗まされ わが言霊は濁りたるらし わが神業諾ひたまふ神あれば 心の魂は曇らざるべし わが心曇れば忽ち言霊も 濁りて神代は乱れむとすも 恐るべきものは心よ言霊よ 朝な夕なに洗ひ清めむ』

斯くおほらかに宣示し給ひて、主の大神の賜ひしわが神業を明かにし、怯めず臆せず遂行せむ事を言挙げし給ひたるぞ、天界経綸発祥の基礎とこそ知られける。
