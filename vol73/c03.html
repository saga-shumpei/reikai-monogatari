Chapter 3: 天之 High Fire 男の Kami [1834]

天之高火男の神〔1834〕

§ 1

The Lord God commanded the Kami of Takahoko and the Kami of Kamuhoko to create Takaamahara, bringing forth the Kototama that revolve to the south and gather in the center, bringing forth the Kototama that govern the outside by revolving to the north, and revolving successively from the northeast to manifest the essence of Seion, becoming the ultimate origin of Baniu, and bringing forth a power with nowhere that does not give birth to all things. This Kototama freely and limitlessly protects and permeates the inside and outside of Shidaitenkiu, appearing as the Iki of Uchiu to become pillars, extending to Hatsupau and reaching their limits without stagnation. Governing Hatsukau, opening Rikugafu, penetrating Honmatsu, clearing and penetrating limitlessly, through the application of the exhaled Iki and inhaled Iki, they govern Hatsukiyoku. Inheriting this Shinriki, the subsequent Shiyoshin settle in the center of Takaamahara, fixing their dwellings in the Shibikyū sphere layer, emitting a kind of moisture to create clouds and fog, and further bringing forth the primal particles of fire, making the Shibi sphere layer ever purer, more beautiful, and thoroughly clear, bringing forth the Kami named Sayao_no_kami to open the spiritual realm of Shibi limitlessly and endlessly, and here they opened a divine dwelling of peerless purity and beauty. The other august name of Sayao_no_kami is called the Kami of Amenotakahikano_kami. Both are great Kami born from the Kototama of Takaamahara, and their divine majesty shines brilliantly upon Hatsukau.

The Kami of Amenotakahikano_kami, together with the Kami of Amenotakachihikano_kami, combining their strengths and making their hearts one, consolidated and repaired the heavenly world, descending from time to time to the Sōmei sphere layer, and in order to open the dwelling places of Amatsukami, they brought forth various stellar realms here, and day and night without interruption, living, acting, sounding, sounding, and never ceasing to sound, they remained. The two Kami, Amenotakahikano_kami and Amenotakachihikano_kami, brought forth the deities of the heavenly realm from the Kototama of Taka, built the magnificent and peerless Shibikyū to envere the divine spirit of the Lord God, and reverently worshipping day and night, they settled forever. The deities of the myriad stellar realms residing in the Shibi sphere realm increased in number day by day and month by month, manifesting hundreds of billions of divine beings, and served in the construction of the spiritual realm of this sphere layer.

When one reflects that from this hundreds of billions of myriads of years have passed down to the present day, one cannot help but be astounded at the truly remote age of the creation of the Uchiu. The spiritual realm of the Shibi sphere layer is called the Tengoku Shibikyūkai, and without an instant's interruption, the Kototama of Taka-taka shine, and east to west, west to east, south to north, north to south, up to down, down to up, sounding, sounding, and never ceasing to sound, the primal particles of the Kototama finally reached the point of bringing forth the deities of seventy-five voices. The Lord God appeared from a single point of the dot, finally completing the Shibikyū sphere layer in the great void, successively bringing forth five kinds of sphere layers to open the spiritual realm, and making it the peaceful dwelling place of the Shiyoshin, how awe-inspiring it is. Ah, the mysterious and unfathomable power of the Kototama.

原文（近代文語）

主の神は高鉾の神、神鉾の神に言依さし給ひて高天原を造らせ給ひ、南に廻りて中央に集る言霊を生み、北に廻りては外を統べる言霊を生み、次ぎ次ぎに東北より廻り給ひて声音の精を発揮し万有の極元となり、一切の生らざる処なき力を生み給ふ。此の言霊は自由自在に至大天球の内外悉くを守り涵し給ひ、宇宙の水火と現れ柱となり、八方に伸び極まり滞りなし。八紘を統べ六合を開き本末を貫き無限に澄みきり澄み徹り、吹く水火吸ふ水火の活用によりて八極を統べ給ふ。此の神力を継承して、以後の諸神は高天原の中心に収まり紫微宮圏層に居を定め、一種の水気を発射し給ひて雲霧を造り、又火の元子を生み給ひ、紫微圏層をして益々清く美しく澄み徹らしめ給ひ、狭依男の神を生み給ひて紫微の霊国を無限に無極に開かせ給ひ、茲に清麗無比の神居を開き給ひぬ。狭依男の神の又の御名を天之高火男の神と言ふ。何れもタカアマハラの言霊より生りませる大神にして神威赫々八紘に輝き給ふ。

天之高火男の神は天之高地火の神と共に、力を合せ心を一にして天の世を修理固成し給ひ、蒼明圏層に折々下りて、天津神の住所を開かむと茲に諸々の星界を生み出で給ひて、昼夜間断なく立活き鳴り鳴りて鳴り止まず坐しぬ。天之高火男の神、天之高地火の神の二神はタカの言霊より天界の諸神を生り出で給ひ、荘厳無比なる紫微宮を造りて主神の神霊を祀り、昼夜敬拝して永遠に鎮まり給ふ。紫微圏界に坐ます万星界の神々は、其数日に月に増し行きて数百億の神人を現し、此の圏層の霊界建設に奉仕し給ふ。

これより数百億万年を経て今日に至りたるを思へば、宇宙創造の年代の遠き実に呆然たらざるを得ざる次第なり。紫微圏層の霊界を称して天極紫微宮界といひ、寸時も間断なくタカタカの言霊輝き、東は西に、西は東に、南は北に、北は南に、上は下に、下は上に鳴り鳴りて鳴り止まざる言霊の元子は、終に七十五声の神々を生み給ふに至れり。主の神は一点のヽより現れ給ひて、終に大虚空に紫微圏層を完成し、次第に五種の圏層を生み給ひて霊国を開き、諸神の安住地と成し給ひしぞ畏けれ。嗚呼言霊の玄妙不可思議力よ。
