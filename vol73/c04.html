Chapter 4: ◎__sp_021__の Kami Voice [1835]

◎__SP_021__の神声〔1835〕

§ 1

In the age of the gods, during the celestial era before this supreme and vast heavenly sphere had yet been established, the primordial world of Heaven was such that the divine Kototama molecules of the infinitesimal points, vast, boundless, and densely mist-shrouded, were scattered like fog; and the infinitesimal point molecules, vast, boundless, spiritual, and limpid, were dark, vague, and wondrous.

Drifting, spotting, and fierce; constant, extreme, and casting; level, moving, and cavernous; tall, white, and boundless; stern, divine, and tranquil; shining, flashing, and refined; full, already, and steady; spreading, swelling, and coagulating; rising, soft, and inserting; advancing, sour, and black; descending, pressing, and approaching; red, charcoal, and stopping; thus they contained all creation and were ultimately pure.

What is said in the chant of the divine Kagura old man's Sanbasō as, 'Tatātarārī, tararīrā, tarari, agari, rarārītō, chirīya, tarari, rararitō,' is a word into which this mystery has transformed, and it explicitly manifests the description of the opening of the celestial world. Therefore, this spiritual voice as a whole is called in a single word 'Su'. When one explains in detail the divine spirit of this 'Su' voice, the true substance of the Grand Ultimate at the primordial root of all things in the universe, the order of its establishment, and the true image of the great creation through countless kalpas and ages can all be clearly derived one by one.

Now, as for the word 'Su', since it is 'Su' and nothing else, it has already perfected the seventy-five spiritual essences, and purely, each and every one maintains its true position. And as for what is called that true position, all are facing one another with distance and nearness alike perfectly maintaining the color of pure and dense union through opposing force, realizing their true aspects; supreme and ultimate, they are eternally constant, with a vital energy that is approaching and dotted. This is the time when the so-called supreme, dense breath is about to resound as a voice, containing divine machinery within its limpid depths. Therefore, those who are human in this world must first of all clearly know the meaning of this 'Su'. Why is this? Because 'Su' is the ultimate origin of the Sovereign.

原文（近代文語）

此の至大天球の未だ成立せざるスの神時代の天の世は、唯至大浩々而氤氳ぎたる極微點の神霊分子が撒霧に撒散而、至大浩々霊々湛々たる極微點分子が玄々漠々妙々たり。漂々點々烈々兮、恒々極々鋳々兮、平々運々洞々兮、几々白々渺々兮、剛々神々寂々兮、照々電々精々兮、満々既々着々兮、汎々膨々凝々兮、登々軟々挿々兮、進々酸々黒々兮、降々責々臨々兮、赤々炭々止々兮焉して万性を含有し極乎として純々たり。神代神楽翁三番叟の謡に、

『タータータラーリ、タラリーラー、タラリ、アガリ、ララーリトー、チリーヤ、タラリ、ララリトー』

と言ふは、此の神秘の転化したる語にして、天の世開設の形容を顕示したるなり。故に此の霊声を総て一言にスと謂ふ。此のス声の神霊を明細に説き明かす時は、世界一切の太極本元の真体及び其の成立の秩序も、億兆万々劫々年度劫大約恒々兮大造化の真象も、逐一明かに資り得らるるなり。

蓋しスの言たるやスにしてスなるが故に、既に七十五声の精霊を完備して、純乎として各自皆その真位を保ちつつあり。然して其の真位と謂ふは、皆両々相向ひて遠近皆悉く返対力が純一に密合の色を保ちて実相しつつ、至大極乎として恒々兮、活気臨々として点々たり、所謂至大氤氳の気が声と鳴り起むと欲して、湛々の中に神機を含蔵するの時なり。故に世に人たる者は先づ第一に此のスの謂れを明かに知るべきものとす。何故なればスは皇の極元なればなり。
