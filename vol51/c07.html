Chapter 7: 曲輪 Jewel [1322]

曲輪玉〔1322〕

§ 1

At a point about twelve or thirteen steps up the Kaidan, Bunsuke had his face scratched by Eugenbau, was knocked down, groaned with a 'Ugh,' and for a while, his consciousness faded. Therefore, he knew nothing at all about Takahime, Hatsu, and Toku who had run after him. He remained under the impression that they were all still resting at the Kyōshiyuyakata.

When he finally came to and looked, he heard a sound like a beehive coming from his bosom. Bunsuke thought:

Bunsuke: 'Well, this is Fushigi. I wonder if I collided with Mokusuke-sama, lost my senses, and am now delirious.'

Thinking this, he put his hand into his bosom and found a round, not-too-heavy lump remaining there. The surface was soft like Ishiwata, and when he held it to his ear, it was groaning, 'Uun, un.' Bunsuke spent some time holding it in his palm and pressing it to his ear, pondering.

Then, he suddenly slapped his knee with one hand and said:

Bunsuke: 'Ah, this is a beehive. If I carelessly break it, it will be unbearable to have these evil eyes stung by it. Mokusuke-sama is quite Itazurazu; he must have tucked it into my bosom thinking I could not see. Because he came down with such great force, he collided with me and I was knocked down. My face feels a bit stinging. It seems I scraped my face on a stone.'

He judged this to himself.

Bunsuke: 'And it would be Kaaisau to the bees if I crushed it; however, if I leave it around here, it will be troublesome if someone plays a prank with it. I wonder if I should put this into the Mitamabako. Un, fortunately, there is an Akibako for a ball here. It seems to be Chiyōdo the right Guahi.'

Saying this, he put the ball into the thick Itabako, tied it tightly with rough white hemp, placed it by his side, and once again busily colored his painting of the sunrise over the pines. He did not think it strange that the box was jumping up and down naturally, assuming that the bees were struggling violently, and he placed a rare stone paperweight on top of it. The groaning became increasingly intense.

Bunsuke: 'Haha, it seems the bees have finally broken the hive and are coming out. Eh, eh, they say the hive is broken, but it is certainly noisy, is it not.'

He muttered to himself, and once again busily moved his paintbrush.

The scene changes. As Eugenbau fled, he dropped the Kuruma-no-tama, which was indispensable for his transformation arts, somewhere, and his physical condition suddenly began to deteriorate. If this Kuruma is separated from the skin for one day and night, the transformation will be revealed. Then, because Sumāto roared from the top of the mountain with a voice like thunder, he collapsed flat onto the grass by the roadside. Takahime caught up to him with all her might and said:

Takahime: 'Hey, Mokusuke-sama, are you collapsed in a place like this? Come now, get up, get up. Are you not injured anywhere?'

Eugenbau searched his bosom, realized the Kuruma was missing, turned pale, and said:

Eugenbau: 'Ah, I failed. I have lost the essential treasure. If I do not have this, my true form will be revealed immediately. Ah, what should I do?'

Takahime: 'Hey, Mokusuke-sama, you just said that your true form would be revealed, but what on earth does that mean? And you mentioned something called a Kuruma just now; what does that Kuruma do?'

Eugenbau: 'Un, this is called the Kongō-fukai-no-nyoi-hōju; if one only has that, the world becomes free and at one's will. I have finally dropped it. Ah, what a troublesome thing I have done.'

Takahime: 'What, the Kongō-fukai-no-nyoi-hōju? Where did you get that, Mokusuke-sama? I, too, have suffered quite a lot regarding that Hōju. I once swallowed it into my stomach, so it is frightening how fate works that you were the one holding it. How, Mokusuke-sama, did it come into your hands?'

妖幻『イヤ、両人、今晩は天津祝詞は免除しておく。沢山の天人様がお出でになると、一寸御挨拶に困るからなア、ハツハハハ』

『コレ、今日は杢助さまの御挨拶で、許して上げるけれど、明日からはキツト天津祝詞を上げるのだよ』

両人は、

『ハイ承知致しました』
