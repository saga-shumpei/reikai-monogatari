Chapter 10: 女異呆 Ugly [1325]

女異呆醜〔1325〕

§ 1

The Magakami of Eugenbau, using the jewels of Magawa, projected a dream-like Rōkaku. He cleverly deceived Takahime, whose Shinkon was led astray by Meiri and love, and lured her into a forest where Kayakusa grew in Baubau profusion. He then dazzled her eyes with dust, refuse, Funneu, palaces of Shizen and Shibi, and a hundred other Chinpin, drawing her into a Mamiana. There, the Magakami sought to disturb the true path of the Ikigami who saves Tenchi, to turn the world below into a Makai of darkness and mud, and to exert his Baui to enjoy himself for Tokoshihe; he exhausted all his Shinriki to achieve this, which is truly a Yuyu Shidai.

Those who served Takamiyahime, Takako and Miyako, were in truth the Yōkai known as Gensobau and Genmabau, the two arms of Eugenbau. Because of the pride in Takahime's heart, she was led by the tricks of the Maga, and while falling into a Makai from which there is no escape, she stood emboldened, believing she had achieved an unparalleled rise in the world. It is pitiful that the Kinmō Kyūbi Shūshin, who is also the Giritenjō of the Hinodewake, was likewise deceived by Eugenbau and is now rejoicing in being used as an instrument of the Akuma. Though she was once struck by unconsciousness after being afflicted by cold and heat,

After a while, she revived. When she looked around, the two handmaidens, Taka and Miya, were busily caring for Takahime-dono, decocting medicine and boiling water, serving her with a heart and soul devoted and undistracted. Watching this, Takahime could not even feel anger; she softened her expression and turned to the two, saying: 'Truly, are you the incarnations of the precious jewels, the Nyoihōju, the Shiomitsutama, or the Shiohitutama? I am truly humbled. I feel it is somehow wasteful to have a spirit like yours serving me, even if I am the Hinodewake. I beg of you, please do not mind Takahime, but appear upon the throne,

and manifest the Kongōfukai Shinriki completely and in detail, so that you may cause the teachings of the noble God to shine in the world and illuminate the path of Uranai. I beseech you.' As she joined her hands and pleaded, the two shook their heads and said: 'No, no, we have received a solemn command from Takamiyahiko-sama of the main castle to serve near Takamiyahime-dono, who is irreplaceable in heaven or on earth. Though we are unworthy, we pray that you will use us, and employ us even for one ten-thousandth of the divine work of the Hinodewake.' Their posture as they joined their hands and prayed was truly admirable, and Takahime became all the more emboldened.

Takahime said: 'Takako, Miyako, what kind of spiritual connection do you have? To serve near Takamiyahime, who is the lineage of the Hensei-danshi, the return of the Tokoshihe-hime, and the Ikimiya upon whom the Giritenjō of the Hinodewake descends—there can be no greater happiness in the world than this. From now on, you must be prudent and listen to everything Takamiyahime says without disobeying a single word. If one lets Takamiyahiko hold the Nyoihōju, his Shinriki appears so magnificently and shines purely; but if he lets go of that precious jewel, he places his name in the world of men and is a brave general superior to ordinary people.

However, humans can never reach the level of God. Although this Takahime appears in the form of a human, she is the moon god who dwells in the palace beyond the deepest, and even further beyond, of the Takamagahara, in the Tenkyoku Shibikyū. She is the child of the Hi no Ōkami, and the fleshly palace of the noble spirit that is the Giritenjō of the Hinodewake, than which there is nothing higher. Once the Shinjin-kōgō is achieved, Takamiyahime is the Giritenjō, and I am the Hinodewake. If I am to protect this dark, clouded world as the Hinode, the Hinodewake-sama must surely be the one to perform the task.

The divine age of 567 is the era of the Hinode. Even if one speaks of the Miroku-no-kami, whether it be the Iwa-no-mitama or the Mizu-no-mitama, it is but another name for the Hinodewake. Because you are young, it is no wonder you do not understand even if I tell you such things. However, if you are a spirit of Kongōfukai, you are a living soul that once entered my womb and was born. If so, you are my daughter. Through the law of transformation, you appeared in the world, and here we have a reunion of mother and child. Truly, it is a happy thing. Surely you must be happy too. From now on, the three of us, with one heart, joining hands, shall be the three goddesses of the Mizu-no-mitama.'

Takako: 'The Kongaufue Niyoihotsushiyu, its half-split Mitama, the manifestation of the Omikami of Taka-musubi who guards the fire and heat of the world, has appeared here as the Hime of Takako. May the Mitama be blessed.'

She finished singing and dancing. Takahime entered a state of bliss, loosened the strings of her face, and dreamed of the light of the future. Miyako sang again.

Miyako: 'The Kami-tsukasa who has appeared as the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake. As the spring of Ichiyō-raifuku blooms, becoming the Tsukasa-gami of Takamiya Castle and the consort of Takamiyahiko, you shall become the mother who saves the myriad spirits of the three thousand worlds. Your appearance is truly noble. I am the other half of the Niyoihotsushiyu that guards the water. Appearing as Miyako-hime, the joy of serving near Takamiyahime! The original Mitama of the Mizu-no-Mitama, which emerged as the jewel-drop of the Omikami of Kamu-musubi, the Mizutama that washes this world. Izuno and Mizu stand side by side, appearing in Takamiya Castle. If we guard the two of you, the three thousand worlds will be at peace and tranquility forever, the Miyo of Kami where even the branches do not rustle. The Miroku-shinsei will suddenly manifest upon this earth, and the Shintoku of the Hinodewake will shine before our very eyes. I look up and rejoice. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Miyako-hime of the water-spirit, I vow before the mother-kimi with all my heart.'

She finished singing and sat down gracefully. Takahime said,

Takahime: 'My, what wonderful things are piling up one after another. Could this be a dream?'

While pinching her cheeks, spitting on her eyebrows, and poking and pressing her navel, she addressed the guest in her belly,

Takahime: 'Hey, Giritenjiyō-dono, Kinmōkyūbi-dono, and other kenzoku, do you know of this rise of Takahime? What do you think? If Takahime is dreaming, tell me clearly that it is a dream. It is too wonderful to be true.'
