Chapter 3: 犬 Horse の労 [1318]

犬馬の労〔1318〕

§ 1

Matsuhime finished her Hairei at each Jinshiya, entered her Ima, and was examining a Shinshiyo. Just then, O-Chiyo returned in a flurry, slammed the door at the Kadoguchi shut, and barred it from the inside. Seeing this, Matsuhime wondered and asked:

Matsuhime: 'Hey, O-Chiyo, why are you barring the door like this in the Yabun?'

O-Chiyo: 'Yes, a strange, suspicious person has come. I do not know if they might come here too, so I am keeping them from entering if they do.'

Matsuhime: 'Is it not strange to close the door and bar it in the middle of the day? Perhaps a group of Hirudorobō has come in a band. Does it not matter? Since the Kamisama are here, it is Daijiyōbu no matter what comes.'

O-Chiyo: 'What, Mother, if it were just a thief, I would not mind at all, but an Ōbakemono has come. Just now, when I was playing with O-Kiku-sama under the peach tree, there was a woman with a repulsive face who said she was Takahime, and another was a large man whose ears were twitching. They are surely Bakemono without a doubt. They must have come thinking they would trick you, Mother, so you must never meet them. That is why I hurried back and closed the door.'

Matsuhime: 'If you say Takahime-sama, she is the Shishiyōsama of Imoriwake-sama. And now she is a distinguished Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō; why would she come suddenly without even an announcement? Well, this is very strange. I had a peculiar dream last night, too; could it be that they are Bakemono? No, no, it is impossible for Bakemono to come to this sacred place in the daytime. No, if it is Takahime-sama, I must meet her. Well, how strange.'

Saying this, she tilted her head.

O-Chiyo: 'If it were Takahime-sama, the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō, she should have more dignity. She was a woman so vulgar... with such an indescribable, base appearance that I got goosebumps just looking at her. And the man she is with is a misshapen, strange-looking fellow who looks like a bell thief. No matter how I look at it, I cannot see them as human. They are truly Yōkai.'

Matsuhime: 'Well, you say strange things. And did Bunsuke-sama at the reception say anything?'

O-Chiyo: 'Bunsuke-sama was talking to the strange woman who called herself Takahime, but he said he would come to report to Matsuhime-sama once. When I heard that, I thought it would be terrible if a blind man like Bunsuke, understanding nothing, went to tell you, Mother, such useless things, so I came back a step ahead to warn you. Mother, you must never meet those two.'

Matsuhime: 'Even so, in the way of the Kamisama, must we not meet anyone? Even if they are Bakemono or Magatsu, is it not enough to preach the teachings of the Kamisama and make them repent?'

O-Chiyo: 'But such people, who knows what they are plotting. No matter what you say, Mother, O-Chiyo will not let such Bakemono in.'

Matsuhime: 'Well, leave everything to me. You are still a child, so you should not interfere in every little thing.'

While the mother and child were talking like this, there was a sound of knocking on the door at the Kadoguchi. This was Bunsuke from the reception, there to report to Matsuhime that Takahime had arrived.

Bunsuke spoke from outside the door:

Bunsuke: 'Excuse me, Matsuhime-sama, it is Bunsuke. Could you please open the Kadoguchi for a moment? I have urgent business and have come to consult with you.'

Matsuhime: 'Yes, please wait a moment. The child has been playing a prank... I will open it right away... Hey, O-Chiyo, will you not open the gate quickly?'

O-Chiyo: 'Mother, if you open the gate, Bunsuke will come in.'

'Is it not opening in such a way that one may enter?'

'But Mother, you must not be drawn into what Bunsuke says. That old man seemed to be terribly impressed by the Bakemono...'

Saying this, she removed the sliding door and opened it wide. Bunsuke, tottering, stepped over the threshold and looked around in all directions. However, he could not see Matsuhime clearly. Being visually impaired, he had no choice but to speak by following the sound of her voice. Matsuhime said,

'Oh, please, do come up.'

She brought out a zabuton and, taking Bunsuke by the hand, seated him.

Bunsuke: 'Ah, it is a nuisance to be old and have such poor eyesight.'

'Even so, you have your shingan opened, which is wonderful. Even if you cannot see with your eyes, you are able to paint such detailed pictures, so is that not wonderful? By the way, Bunsuke-sama, has some urgent business arisen?'

'Hai, a matter has suddenly arisen that I wish to consult with you about, orii. It is truly distressing... I do not know where to begin; it is a chiitenpen, and I cannot find the kotoba to express it.'

Ochiyo said from the side,

'Hey, Bunsuke-sama, that will not do. That person is a Bakemono, so you are being deceived. Do not say anything to Mother. Come now, go home quickly. If your ashimoto is unsteady, I shall lead you by the hand.'

Matsuhime: 'Hey, Ochiyo, what are you saying? You are a child, so keep quiet. Bunsuke-sama, please forgive her. It is truly troublesome that this child has not been properly educated. She is a perfect match for Okiku-sama. If one has bad friends to play with, one becomes completely influenced by them. It is truly a nuisance for the parents as well. By the way, Bunsuke-sama, what is this matter that is so distressing?'

'Hai, the truth is that Takahime-sama has arrived. Furthermore, even Mokusuke-dono, the general manager of the Saien no Yakata, has come, and it seems that someone has spoken ill of you. They say that as of today, you are to be relieved of your duties as Kyoshu, that Takahime-sama will become the Kyoshu, and that Mokusuke-dono will be dispatched to supervise. They are waiting below now. I have been under your care for a very long time, but when I think that I must part from you, it is truly heartbreaking.'

Matsuhime said calmly,

'Ho ho ho ho, I thought something terrible had happened, but is it just that? That is wonderful. Actually, I have wanted to leave this place and engage in great activities with my husband, but I have had no choice but to serve here until today. That is truly wonderful.'

'Hearing that, I am somewhat relieved. No, the mind of those in high positions is truly difficult to understand. It must be so. I have heard that it is the honor of the cherry blossoms to be struck by the night storm and scatter cleanly, leaving none behind. No, splendid, splendid, your resolve is admirable; I am truly moved.'

He was wiping his tears with his sleeve. Ochiyo said from the side,

'Hey, Bunsuke-sama, you are blind, so you are being deceived by a Bakemono. Mother, are you really intending to rush out of here just because someone like that came? Has there not been any word from the Saien no Yakata yet? Even if someone comes, you must not deal with them. It has already been forty or fifty days since Otora-sama was taken away by Magahiko, and there has been no word at all, has there? Since they are coming from the same Saien no Yakata, there should be a private report from the Lord God Yashimanushi, and there should be some kind of notification from Magahiko-sama as well. You will be in for a terrible experience unless you investigate it thoroughly first.'

Matsuhime: 'Indeed, that is so. There is some reason in what you say. No, Bunsuke-sama, does that Takahime-sama bring some kind of official order from the mansion of Iso? Or does she perhaps possess a letter from Kyōshiyusama or Magahiko-sama? Please go and ask her that.'

'Yes, I shall go and ask, but since the god Tokiokashi, one of the Sanbagarasu, is currently on an official visit, there is no need to inquire. If it were anyone else, it would be a different matter, but as she is the Sōmu of the mansion of Iso, it would be better not to ask.'

Chiyo: 'Now, Bunsuke-sama, if you are not going to ask, I shall go from here and inspect whether she is a Honmamono or a Nisemono. Mother, that would be fine, would it not?'

'Now, now, O-Chiyo, what are you saying? You must not say anything at all today. Mother is imposing a Kankōrei upon you.'

'But in this situation of Senkiitsuki, can you really expect me to be silenced by Mother’s Kankōrei?'

'Ah, what a troublesome daughter you are.'

'Ah, what a troublesome mother you are.'

Bunsuke: 'A troublesome situation has arisen, indeed.'

Chiyo: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha.'

Hearing this laughter from outside, the one who entered was O-Kiku.

O-Kiku: 'O-Chiyo-sama, what is so funny? You are laughing quite heartily.'

Chiyo: 'O-Kiku-sama, you have come at a good time. Just now, Bunsuke-sama came out and said that that Bakemono is Mokusuke-sama and Takahime-sama. Mother is taking it seriously, so it is so funny I cannot bear it.'

O-Kiku: 'Indeed. A strange creature has arrived. When I heard it was Takahime, I thought she would be a more dignified lady, but she is like a Bakemono. She says she is Mokusuke-sama, but her ears move back and forth like a beast’s. In any case, that person is a strange Bakemono. However, because that old woman said, "I am Takahime, and since I am the mentor of Matsuhime-sama, go and call her quickly," I had no choice but to come. If you please, Matsuhime-sama, you must not meet such a person. However, I had to give some kind of answer, so I came here partly to report this to you.'

Matsuhime: 'Well, thank you for coming. O-Kiku-sama, did you think she was suspicious?'

O-Kiku: 'She is a very strange creature indeed. Surely, she is a fake.'

Matsuhime: 'O-Kiku-sama, if that is the case, I am sorry to trouble you, but please say this to that so-called Takahime-sama: "Matsuhime is currently in the midst of divine business, so she will see you as soon as she is finished. Until then, please wait at the Kyōshi mansion and have some sake." Please convey that I said this. Bunsuke-sama, please return with her as well. And I ask that you treat her with care so that there are no blunders.'

Bunsuke: 'Yes, I understand. Now, O-Kiku-sama, let us return.'

Led by the hand by O-Kiku, he walked down the stairs with a clattering sound. Afterwards, O-Chiyo lowered her voice and said,

'Mother, Takahime is the real one. But the one called Mokusuke who followed behind is surely a Bakemono. You must deal with her with that in mind.'

'How did you come to know such a thing?'

'Even so, an angel whispered it in my ear. I was told to be very careful, Mother.'

'Since you have angels descending upon you from time to time, you are truly a convenient person. And did they say what that Bakemono is?'

'It seems that is a great demon named Yōgenbō, who occupies a significant position in the world of evil parties. However, it is said that he fears the Sun God immensely, so when he walks during the day, he wears a deep sedge hat and rarely goes outside. It is said that during the day he is always sleeping in the forest. Since Takahime-sama has been bewitched by that Yōgenbō and is again throwing her weight around with her sense of duty to heaven, it is all the more troublesome.'

Ochiyo: 'But she is making such a fool of us, so I want to say at least a little something. If I let her worship the Kamisama once, she will be Bitsukuri, will she not? It is my pleasure to see that.'

Mother: 'My, what a foul-mouthed child you are. Is it that Omoshiro for you to see people get Bitsukuri? You are a troublesome Tenba.'

Ochiyo: 'Even so, because she is an Akuma who deceives the world, torments people, and Baugai the path of the Ōkamisama, if I do not punish her a little, the duty of you, Mother-sama, who serves the Kamisama, will not be fulfilled. Even for me, to Kankuwa a Bakemono is what one calls Shiyokumufuchiujitsu. It is precisely at a time like this that I must perform Samaniwa sufficiently.'

Mother: 'However, if Takahime-sama is the real one, she is my great benefactor and teacher, so I must meet her and offer my greetings. And if she is being deceived by such an Akuma, I must warn her, or else my duty as a disciple will not be fulfilled. Ah, a troublesome matter has arisen.'

As they were talking, a huge, fierce dog came in wagging its tail gracefully. Looking at it, something like a letter was hanging around its neck.

Matsuhime: 'Ah, this must have come as a messenger carrying a letter from somewhere. Here, here, O-inu-sama, I do not know where you came from, but it was a great effort. Let me see, let me see the letter.'

Saying this gently, she stroked the area around its neck two or three times to show affection, took the tied letter, and looked at the address: 'To Matsuhime-sama, Kamuzukasa of Kogitayama, at the forest of the shrine, from Hatsuwakahime.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, this is a letter from Hatsuwakahime-sama. Has something unusual happened? Here, Ochiyo, please close the gate for a moment. It might be a secret matter.'

Ochiyo looked around outside, and feeling relieved that no one was coming out, she stroked her chest, quietly closed the door, and bolted it firmly. It goes without saying that this fierce dog was Smart, the beloved dog of Hatsuwakahime.
