Chapter 12: 初 Flower 姫 [1327]

初花姫〔1327〕

§ 1

Katahiko spoke gently to the three maidens,

Katahiko: 'Excuse me, young ladies, may I ask you a question? When was that magnificent castle visible over there completed?'

The three women pretended not to hear him at all, and kept busily picking flowers. Katahiko drew even closer and spoke in a louder voice,

Katahiko: 'Young ladies, I ask you a question.'

At this voice, the three looked surprised and fixed their eyes on the faces of Katahiko and Ranchi. Then Takahime said,

Takahime: 'Ah, you startled me. Who are you, sir?'

Katahiko: 'I am the remains of General Katahiko, who led the Brahman army to this Ukiki forest four months ago and was stationed here. The person here is our superior, General Ranchi. Like me, he has cast off his military uniform, and we are now missionaries of the Ananai-kyō.'

Takahime parted her flower-like lips, put on a charming air, and said in a seductive voice,

Takahime: 'Ah, is that so? That is a noble position you hold. I am the daughter of Nyoio, and my name is Hatsuhanahime.'

Katahiko: 'Well, that is a strange thing. Is Nyoio-sama not a Kshatriya of the Kōran country in the Moon World?'

Takahime: 'Yes, that is correct. Recently, I moved my country here with my father, leading many retainers, and the castle has finally been built after working day and night.'

Katahiko: 'Well, what a strange thing this is. No matter how wealthy one may be, for such a castle to be built in such a short time—Ranchi-dono, is it not strange?'

Ranchi: 'Indeed, it is strange beyond measure.'

Takahime: 'Oh ho ho ho ho, oh my, the look of wonder on you two gentlemen's faces... My father Nyoio came from the Kōran country four months ago, and after ordering tens of thousands of subordinates, it has finally been completed just like this. Since my father possesses the wish-fulfilling jewel, he can do anything. And you said you are now missionaries of the Ananai-kyō, but my father has also suddenly converted to the Ananai-kyō, and he has invited Hatsuwakahime-sama, who came from the Saien mansion, and she is currently staying in the inner chambers. I would be grateful if you would stop by, as my father would also be pleased.'

Katahiko: 'What are you saying? Hatsuwakahime-sama is staying within this castle? Since when has that been the case?'

Takahime: 'Yes, two or three days ago she departed from the Saien mansion for the forest of shrines, and then she visited this Kuruwa castle. My parents heard her noble words and are now completely believers of the Ananai-kyō. Hatsuwakahime-sama said that missionaries of the Ananai-kyō named Katahiko and Ranchi would soon be passing by, so I have been waiting here with these two ladies-in-waiting, picking flowers, hoping to welcome you if you should come. Would you not please stop by for a moment?'

Katahiko tilted his head slightly and turned to Ranchi,

Katahiko: 'Ranchi-dono, what is your opinion on this? Between the claim that Hatsuwakahime-sama is staying here and the presence of such beautiful maidens, I cannot make any sense of it at all.'

Ranchi: 'Indeed, I also find it very strange. If a construction as magnificent as this could be done, there should have been at least some rumors about it, yet for such a mirage-like castle to appear suddenly—I suspect this may be the work of a demon.'

Takahime moved to Ranchi's side and said,

Takahime: 'Excuse me, uncle, what is a demon? Please, do tell me.'

Ranchi: 'Ha ha ha ha ha, I shall tell you. When we speak of a demon, we mean an evil spirit.'

Takahime: 'Do you think of this magnificent mansion as the dwelling of an evil spirit? If so, have I been taken captive by an evil spirit and brought to such a place?'

Miyako: 'Elder sister, if that is the case, then I too am being used by this so-called Magami. If you please, Hatsuhanahime-sama, I beg you to grant us sisters our leave.'

She says this with a fearful air, trembling and weeping.

Takahime: 'Now, now, Takako, Miyako, I am the daughter of the concubine of the awe-inspiring Niyoiōsama; if you speak such things, I shall not tolerate it. To point to the mansion of the Setsuteirisama of the Kōran country and call it the castle of a Magami is beyond the pale. If you dare say such a thing once more, I shall never forgive you.'

Takako: 'Even so, Jiyōsama, that Oji-sama said it was the work of a Magami. When we sisters heard that, we became somehow afraid. I beg you, please grant us our leave here. And there are still about ten of my friends who are under your care; please, I ask you to forgive them all.'

Miyako, wiping her tears with her sleeve again,

Miyako: 'If you please, Ojiyōsama, I beg of you. I do not mind even if I am killed, but I beseech you to save my ten friends. In exchange, I shall pierce my throat and die here. Ah, Kamunagara tamashii chihahe mase.'

Before she can finish saying this, she draws the kuwaiken from her bosom and attempts to thrust it into her throat. Takahime rushes forward in a panic, snatches the kuwaiken away, and says with an air of irritation,

Takahime: 'Now, Miyako, what kind of thoughtless thing are you doing? If you please, travelers, because you say such groundless things, it causes trouble for Hatsuhanahime; please, do persuade these two handmaidens.'

Ranchi: 'No, I have spoken imprudently before you children, and I am truly sorry. Now, now, handmaidens, it would be troublesome if you took what I said literally. Because it was so magnificent, I merely said that it might be the work of a Magatsukami. I do not say it is definitely the work of a Magatsukami; it would be troublesome if you jumped to such a conclusion.'

Miyako: 'No, no, no matter what you say, what you said is the truth. Without saying such empty words of comfort, I beg you to let me die. Rather than remaining a captive of a Magatsukami with the body of a frail woman, it is better to die.'

She collapses in tears. Katahiko, unable to bear the pity of it, approaches and tries to soothe Miyako,

Katahiko: 'If you please, Ojiyōsama, I am truly sorry. It is all a lie, so please do not take it to heart.'

Miyako: 'No, no, no matter what you say, I think it is just your attempt to comfort me. You spoke well; there is no doubt it is the work of a Magatsukami. Come, Takako-sama, let us flee quickly.'

She acts as if she is about to run off.

Takahime: 'Now, Takako, Miyako, no matter how much you run, my father will have you chased on horseback, so it is useless. Do not listen to what such an Oji-sama says, and let us return together. Will you not listen to what your master says?'

She asserts firmly. Takako, wiping her tears with her sleeve,

Takako: 'Hatsuwakahime-sama said in her words... that a missionary never speaks lies or falsehoods. This person is a missionary-sama; how could he speak lies? I will believe the words of Hatsu no matter what.'

Katahiko: 'Ah, this is a difficult situation; what should I do?'

Miyako: 'I beg you, Oji-sama, please come once. And if it is indeed the mansion of a Magami, I beg you to take me and flee. My friends are also here, about ten of them.'

Takahime: 'You said you were originally a general and are now a distinguished missionary of the San-Go-Kyō, did you not? And yet you say things that cause trouble for me; does that fulfill your duty?'

Katahiko and Ranchi both place their hands on the grass,

Katahiko and Ranchi: 'No, Hime-sama, we have been truly rude. We beg you to reconsider and listen again.'

Takahime: 'I am one called Hatsuhanahime, a name that sounds much like that of Hatsuwakahime-sama. If I recall correctly, you said we were sisters in spirit. Come, would you not please come into the castle once for the sake of your mission?'

'No matter what you say, since Harukuniwake-sama has commanded that we must not approach, we cannot do so, no matter what happens.'

'Come, let us do so.'

The two reached this decision, and speaking in unison, the pair said:

'Very well, we shall accompany you and take care of you.'

'That is a prompt answer; I am grateful. Hatsuwakahime goes without saying, but my parents will surely be delighted as well. Come, Takako, Miyako, there is no need for worry anymore. The Sendenshi-sama are coming with us.'
