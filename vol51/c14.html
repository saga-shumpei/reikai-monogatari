『ハハハハ、イヤもう恐れ入りました。義理天上様、今後はキツと慎しみませう』

§ 4

'Hey, Takasama, Miyasama, why are you giggling so? Is it that funny? Children are truly helpless creatures, aren't they?'

Takako: 'Even so, Mother, isn't it funny? You are having such a silly quarrel, aren't you, Miyasama? It is uncontrollably hilarious, is it not?'

Eugen: 'Hahahaha, hey, Takamiyahime-sama, the children are laughing at you.'

'It is because you say such absurd things that the two of them are laughing.'

Takako: 'Still, Mother, it was funny in your room, was it not? Father was not there at that time, was he? You made such funny gestures all by yourself that we two could not help but laugh.'

Eugen: 'Ahahahaha, you must have seen me doing something silly, I suppose.'

Miyako: 'Yes, that is right. You were shaking your hips, twisting your mouth, talking to yourself, and falling in love with your own reflection, and you said, "If I show this figure to my dear husband, he will surely be delighted." Isn't that right, Takasama? I am not mistaken, am I?'

Takako: 'It was truly just like that; Mother, you are a very amusing person.'

Eugen: 'Ahahahaha.'

Takahime: 'Ah, to be teased and mocked by my husband and my own children—it is a difficult thing to be born a beauty. Hohohoho, ahahahahaha, fufufufufu.'

The four of them laugh all at once. The Mamedanuki, who had been lying dead after being struck on the head by Takahime, suddenly regained consciousness at the sound of this laughter, scrambled up, ran around the room two or three times, and quickly hid its figure through the window.

Takahime: 'My, is it not strange that a tanuki should live in such a magnificent palace? If we kept a dog, it would surely run away, would it not?'

Eugen: 'No, as I always say, I was born in the Year of the Monkey, so I despise dogs. That is why the proverb says that a bad relationship is like that between a dog and a monkey.'

'By "monkey," you mean the man giving the woman a dismissal, and by "dog," you mean the wife giving her husband a dismissal and returning home, I suppose. From now on, let us both be careful not to say such ominous things as "dog" or "monkey," shall we?'

'I am being careful, but it is troublesome because you are the one who always breaks our promises, ahahaha.'

'If that is the case, since it is time to prepare for the night, I shall withdraw to my room.'

'Hey, Takamiyahime-dono, is it not a bit much for you to monopolize the two maids... no, the children? Could you not leave one of them here with me?'

'Indeed, if you are left alone, a man alone, one never knows when the devil might tempt you. Hey, Takako-chan, it is a bit of a chore, but please stay by your father's side and attend to his needs. And if any other woman should come in here, be a good girl and let me know secretly.'

Takako says in a deliberately loud voice:

'Yes, if anyone, whoever she may be, comes into Father's room, I will certainly let you know in secret.'

'Hey, Takako-sama, is there such a thing as a secret? Oh, what a dim-witted child you are.'

Eugen: 'Hahahaha, you are cautious to the very end; alas, I am overwhelmed. In short, Takako is my monitor, is she not? Oh, how scary, how scary. Hey, Takako-sama, please go easy on me. No matter what happens, you must never report back to Takamiyahime, ahahahaha.'

Takahime: 'Oh, say whatever you like, if that is how it is.'

She takes Miyako by the hand, and with her shoulders swaying, the brave flapping of her sleeves, and her long hakama trailing behind, she returns, walking with the elegant, rhythmic gait of a high-ranking courtesan.
