Eugenbau and Takahime were drinking sake together while whispering sweet nothings, and as they waited for the reply from Hatsukō and Tokukō, Takahime was busy soothing the unreasonable demands of the drunken Eugenbau. Takahime: 'Hey, you two, that took a long time, didn't it? How did it go? Matsuhime said yes, didn't she?'

Hatsukō: 'Well, oh my, how should I put it. She is truly formidable, really, she is quite beyond our control.'

Takahime: 'Beyond your control? Do you mean to say that Matsuhime will not listen to what Giritenjiyō says?'

Hatsukō: 'Hey Toku, you are the proxy for Takahime-sama, aren't you? You go ahead and report to her.'

Tokukō: 'Yes, Takahime-sama, when I spoke of various things according to your orders, that woman Matsuhime, perhaps possessed by the Kinmaukiubi, was in such a fierce state that we could not possibly handle her with what we said.'

Takahime: 'What do you mean by not being able to handle her? In short, are you saying she will not listen to what Takahime says?'

Tokukō: 'Well, I would not say she does not listen, but she says that various things are living inside you, Anata. And she said that Mokusuke-sama is a monster called Eugenbau of Taiunzan. She was fired up, saying she intends to do something to drive you out.'

Takahime: 'What? Calling Mokusuke-sama Eugenbau? That is truly outrageous. If we dally with that woman Matsuhime, there is no telling what she might say next. Hey Mokusuke-sama, won't you wake up? She is saying that you are not the real Mokusuke, but a Bakeshiu.'

Eugenbau: 'Hahahaha, she called me a monster, did she? That is to be expected. Even the Mizu no Mitama of the Henshējoshi is called a great monster, so I should be honored if I am also called a monster. Rejoice, Takahime, this shows how great and sublime my character is, ahahahaha.'

Hatsukō: 'Even so, I do not think Matsuhime meant it in that sense when she called you a monster; she was saying you are definitely Eugenbau of Taiunzan.'

Takahime: 'What an outrageous woman. If we let her say such things, it will hinder our purpose. We must do something about this. It would be easy for me to go and settle it, but that would be too undignified. Hey Hatsukō, Tokukō, strike her down decisively as I said before. Now that you have become my right-hand men, this is the time for you to prove your worth.'

Hatsukō: 'Yes, yes, I will strike her down, but she is quite a tough one, and actually, well, er, what should I say.'

He is scratching his head vigorously.

Takahime: 'Hey, how unsightly. You have been beaten by Matsuhime, haven't you? By the way, Mokusuke-sama, you said to strike her down, but you wouldn't have me do anything too rough, would you? Matsuhime is my disciple. No matter how much she opposes me, I must take care of her.'

Eugenbau: 'What, Takahime-sama, you are quite the philanthropist. My, how admirable. Then why did you poison Chin-hiko or give him a cup filled with the blood of a dragon? I suppose there are depths within depths, ahahahaha.'

Takahime: 'Hey Hatsukō-sama, Tokukō-sama, you must certainly not do anything too rough. However, since self-defense is an exception, please do your best to work according to Mokusuke-sama's words.'

Hatsukō: 'I will do anything, but this fellow Toku is a coward, so I get distracted and cannot work as I would like.'

Eugenbau: 'Then why don't you go alone and take care of it? She is just one woman, isn't she? If you cannot do even that much, how can you carry out a great mission?'

Ochiyo, together with Smart, was playing by plucking azalea flowers and the like, and before they knew it, they had advanced into the forest in the valley ahead. 'Now, Smart, what are you doing? Be a little more obedient.'

She slapped him sharply on the side of the face. It was Okiku who came running up in a hurry. Okiku was panting for breath, and seeing that Ochiyo was here, she seemed relieved at last and said,

'That is true.'
