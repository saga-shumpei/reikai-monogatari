Chapter 19: 偽強心 [1334]

偽強心〔1334〕

§ 1

Gariya asked Kēsu, 'Where did you take off your Kimono?'

Kēsu replied, 'I was so absorbed in Sumau that I forgot where I took it off. The Tanuki yarau must have snatched it away.'

'It must be somewhere, then,' he said, and searched with Itsushiyōkenmei, but there was only one staff and nothing resembling a Kimono.

Kēsu: 'That Tanuki fellow, he must have dragged it off to the Mamiana to use as a Shikimono. Ah, what a Zannen.'

He gnashed his teeth and proceeded further and further north. There was a Kahanaga covered in Aomo, just about an Itsukenhaba wide. The depth was on Heikin about Shisun. The Sannin lay down in the river in Katami, dammed the water, tore the Kahabata grass to use as a substitute for a Tenuguhi, rubbed their Karadajiu, and finally managed to wash off the Shiuki.

Kēsu: 'Now, this is Hadakahiyakukuwan. A Ningen cannot understand their own strength unless they fall this far. From now on, it will get warmer Ichinichiichinichi, so being naked is Ketsukō. Hey, Hatsukōwake, Tokukōwake, let us hurry to the mansion of the Saien.'

Hatsu: 'Hey Toku, if we stop by Kohokitayama, we might be able to get at least one piece of old clothing, but I have done something a bit dishonorable, so I cannot stop by in times like this. Ah, what a trouble.'

He folded his arms and was lost in thought. From nowhere, a kasa, a mino, and clothing for three people came fluttering down. The three picked them up quickly, and when they looked closely, they were their own Kimono. And before they knew it, they were bone-dry, and no matter how much they sniffed, the Shiuki was gone. They were stiff and crackling, perhaps from being starched heavily.

Hatsu: 'Haha, the Tanuki fellow must have been scolded by the Kami and finally did the laundry. You see, Toku, this is why one cannot give up faith.'

Toku shed tears of joy, put his hands together, and prayed in gratitude, 'Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese.'

However, in reality, half-rotted mats appeared to them as fine clothing. The three changed into them happily, and said, 'Now, this is fine. Let us go to the mansion of the Saien at last.'

Hatsu and Toku hurriedly held them back.

Hatsu: 'Excuse me, you, please wait a moment. Since there is no doubt that the formidable enemies of the San-Go-Kyō are hiding in this vicinity, how about you defeat that fellow once? It will be a good souvenir for you as well.'

Gariya: 'Who are you referring to as the formidable enemies of the San-Go-Kyō?'

'They are the Okishi-no-Kami Mokusuke, who used to be the general manager of the mansion of the Saien, and the fellow called the propagandist Takahime. That fellow has been stirring up a great rebellion lately.'

'Hearing from the teacher Chigokuwake, I had heard that while Takahime-sama is suspicious, Mokusuke-sama was a pillar of the San-Go-Kyō, so that is a strange thing to hear.'

'That is because he is wearing a cat mask. He showed his tail while trying to occupy the sacred ground of the forest of the shrine, became a couple with Takahime, fled to Kohokitayama, plotted another rebellion at the sacred ground of Kohokitayama, was struck by divine power, fled, and stole the treasure of the mansion of the Saien, the indestructible Nyoi-Hōju, and escaped. We must retrieve that treasure at all costs, or the San-Go-Kyō will be finished.'

Kēsu: 'What, did such a thing happen? Ningen are truly incomprehensible. Gariya-sama, this is not something we can ignore. With these two as our guides, we must seek out their whereabouts wherever they may be and retrieve that treasure, or our mission will not be fulfilled.'

Gariya: 'That is so. Hey, you two, which way did that Mokusuke and Takahime go?'

Toku: 'They ran this way from the forest of Kaishi two or three days ago. Since this is a straight road, have you not encountered anything suspicious? It is a pair, a woman of about fifty and a big man of the same age.'

と歯ぎしりしながら北へ北へと進んで行つた。丁度一間巾ばかりの青藻を被つた川流れがある。そして深さは四寸位平均になつてゐる。三人は交代に川に横たはり、水を淀めて川端の草を千切り、手拭に代用して体中を擦り、臭気を漸くにして洗ひ落した。

ケース『さア、之で裸百貫だ。人間はここ迄落ちぶれなくちや力が分らない。之から一日々々暖かくなるのだから裸でも結構だ。おい初公別、徳公別、急いで斎苑の館へ参る事にしよう』

初『おい徳、小北山へ寄れば、古着の一枚位は何とか云つて貰へるだらうけれど、一寸義理の悪い事がしてあるので、こんな時には立寄る訳にも行かぬわ。ああ困つたな』

『治国別の先生から承はれば、高姫さまは何うも怪しいが、杢助さまは三五教の柱石だと聞いてゐたのに、それは又妙な事を承はるものだな』

ケース『何、そんな事があつたのか。何うも人間と云ふものは分らぬものだな。ガリヤさま、こいつは一つ聞き棄てにはなりませぬぞ。此両人を案内者として、何処に居らうとも彼奴の在処を索め、その宝を奪ひ返して行かなくては吾々の役が済みますまい』

ガリヤ『そりや、さうです。おい御両君、その杢助、高姫は何方へ行つたかな』

徳『怪志の森から此方へスタスタと二三日前に走つて来よつたのです。此処は一筋街道だから、貴方怪しいものに出会ひませぬか。五十位な女と同年輩の大男と二人ですよ』

§ 2

Kesu: 'Gariya-sama, we have not encountered such a thing at all. If that is the case, perhaps it is hiding in some small mountain deep within this Ukiki Forest. In any event, shall we not search for it with all our might?'

Gariya: 'I understand. Hatsu-sama, Toku-sama, come, let us search this Kayanogahara from here. It may be lurking in hiding, having taken fright at hearing our voices. However, as this is a vast wilderness and the reeds are growing tall, let us search while keeping in contact with one another, ensuring we do not separate by more than five ken.'

'Yes, that would be well.'

With the deliberation concluded, they proceeded deeper and deeper, searching while pushing through the thick growth of kayakusa.

From behind them,

'Oi! Oi!'

came a voice, calling out with a high-pitched cry. When the four men turned back to look, they saw two beautiful girls, one twelve or thirteen and the other sixteen or seventeen, beckoning to them from atop a high rock by the roadside. Hatsukō tilted his head to listen,

'Ah, that voice belongs to Chiyo-sama and Okiku-sama. There must be some unusual matter that has occurred. Hey, Toku, let us return to the rear for a moment. If you two would be so kind, would you mind turning back for a while?'

'Whatever the case may be, let us turn back.'

With that, Kesu and Gariya followed the two and finally returned to the front of the rock where the girls were standing.

Hatsu: 'Are you not Chiyo-sama and Okiku-sama? Has some urgent business arisen that you have called me to stop?'

Chiyo: 'It is not exactly urgent business, but Takahime and Mokusuke have built a magnificent encampment in this Ukiki Forest just as you see, and by using magic, they have suddenly constructed a castle and even a town, drawing in the believers of the Sangokyo from the koguchi. Therefore, Matsuhime-sama went out saying she would try to persuade Takahime and Mokusuke. I followed her, but they immediately threw Matsuhime-sama into a prison. I escaped through the back gate and fled all the way here. Hatsu-sama, Toku-sama, would it not be possible for you to go and rescue Matsuhime-sama?'

'Oi, Toku, what shall we do?'

'Well now, if it has come to that for Matsuhime, should we not rather return to the rear and hold our ground at Kobutayama?'

'How can you suggest such an unreasonable thing? We must somehow rescue Matsuhime-sama, apologize for our past rudeness, and have her use us as before. This is a good opportunity to make amends.'

Gariya: 'Now, now, young lady, is this Matsuhime-sama your teacher?'

Okiku: 'Yes, she has a fine husband named Matsuhiko-sama, who is the leader of the teachings at Kobutayama.'

'Ah, then we must rescue her no matter what. We owe a great debt to Matsuhiko-sama. Come, let us go, Kesu.'

'Ah, interesting, interesting. I know the layout of Ukiki Forest well. I know the location of the prison and everything else like the back of my hand, so come, let us get to work.'

Chiyo: 'Please, I beg you to save my mother. I will act as your guide.'

Kesu: 'Ha, very well, very well, do not worry. Are you not crying? Ah, it is no wonder, since your mother has met with such a fate. However, now that we are rushing in, it will be fine, so do not worry. Come, Hatsu-sama, Toku-sama, shall we go?'

'Agreed, agreed.'

With this, the four men and two women quickened their pace, heading toward the front gate of the fortress in Ukiki Forest. As they approached the main gate, seven or eight young, beautiful women clad in figured brocade came toward them, carrying baskets in their hands, picking violets and plucking dandelions, whispering to each other with joyful expressions. The four men stood mesmerized, their souls cast into the void by such splendor and grace.
