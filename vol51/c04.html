Chapter 4: 乞食劇 [1319]

乞食劇〔1319〕

§ 1

Matsuhime quietly broke the seal of the letter and raised it respectfully to her forehead as she read through it. Gradually her countenance changed and her hands trembled, for the contents of the letter appeared to be of no ordinary gravity. Having finished reading the letter, Matsuhime let out a sigh of relief.

Chiyo: 'Mother, what I said was not wrong, was it? Takahime does not come by order of the Hall of Iso. And that person calling himself Mokusuke must be a bakemono. It is written here that this dog is Hatsuwakahime-sama's beloved dog, named Smart.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, ah, what a treacherous evil world this is. I cannot remain idle like this. Nevertheless, according to the words of Hatsuwakahime-sama, I must reform Takahime-sama strictly through pure goodness to the very end. Yet in Hatsuwakahime-sama's words... it is written: "Since you are the Kamutsukasa of Kogitayama, you must never leave this place under any circumstances." If Takahime-sama should stubbornly insist on remaining here as the Kyōshiyu to the end, whatever shall I do? If only Magahiko-sama were here, we could hold a good consultation; this is truly a troublesome matter.'

Chiyo: 'Mother, you must not worry at all. In any case, she will surely visit the shrine at least once, so when she opens the door of the upper shrine, she will certainly be startled and flee. The angel-sama told me so.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, is that so? I truly hope things go smoothly. However, you too must take Smart-sama and go play for a while somewhere out of Takahime-sama's sight. It would be inconvenient if you were present.'

Chiyo: 'In that case, Mother, please stay strong. Please ensure that you do not get dragged into this. Here, Smart-sama, you are a lovely dog, aren't you?'

Saying this, she embraced the dog around its neck. Smart licked Chiyo's cheek with her thin, flat tongue. Surprised, Chiyo pushed Smart down onto the garden floor. Smart remained turned on her back in a carefree manner, paddling the air with her legs.

Chiyo: 'Ah, this dog is a female. Now, my dear little Suma-chan, since it is early spring and the weather is so fine, let us go play in the woods together with Chiyo. If there are any rabbits, let us give them a scare.'

Saying this, she stroked the dog's head. Smart rose up briskly and followed behind Chiyo to play in the mountain forest. Left behind, Matsuhime clasped her hands together in solitude and fell deep into thought.

Matsuhime: 'Ah, ah, Takahime-sama is a truly difficult person. How can a true conversion be brought about for her? According to Hatsuwakahime-sama's letter, her spirit has been completely deranged of late, and she has become a free lodging house for evil nine-tailed golden foxes, toads, serpents, badgers, and weasels. Furthermore, the one calling himself Mokusuke is not Hatsuwakahime-sama's father, but Yōgenbō of Mount Daikumo; bringing such a truly repulsive bakemono, acting like a married couple, coming out to a place like this to drive Matsuhime away and make herself the Kyōshiyu—whatever is the meaning of this? I have no personal attachment to being the Kamutsukasa of this place, but I cannot simply hand this place over to evil gods and leave. Doing such a thing would be unpardonable before the gods. I must be prepared to fight a lonely battle to the very end here. Ah, Kunitokotachi-no-Ōkami-sama, Toyokunihime-no-Ōkami-sama, Konohanahime-no-Ōkami-sama, Kanakatsu-Kaname-no-Ōkami-sama, protect me and bless me, Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase, Kannagara Tamachi Hae Mase.'

Thus she prayed with all her heart.

Thereupon, Hatsu and Toku came running over noisily. Treading unsteadily with trembling feet, the two spoke:

Hatsu and Toku: 'Matsuhime-sama, ah, we have come to make a brief report; Takahime-sama, the missionary of Sango-kyō and former leader of Uranai-kyō, has arrived. And she says, "Why does Matsuhime not come to greet me when she knows I have arrived? Though she said she would come out as soon as her business was finished, she has not yet come out," and she is in a tremendous rage. She further says, "From today, Takahime is the Kyōshiyu of this hall. Mokusuke-sama has come to supervise," speaking with truly formidable arrogance. If you do not quickly go to pay your respects, a major incident affecting your very life will take place, so we have come quietly to warn you.'

Even if Takahime-sama were to become the Kyōshiyu of this place, as long as the Jimu has not been handed over, this place remains under the jurisdiction of Matsuhime. Therefore, although I said I would ask, I cannot ask on my own, so please have Takahime-sama and Mokusuke-sama come out here. That is the only proper way.

Hatsu: 'Matsuhime-sama, what a fierce attitude you have. They say you cannot win against a crying child or a Jitō, so it might be to your advantage to yield from your side. I am sure I mean no harm. Since you have been here for four years, counting the start and end of the years, it would not be pleasant to be suddenly ordered to vacate today. I do sympathize with that. However, you should accept this as Innen, and it would be best to meet Takahime-sama and Mokusuke-sama with a Sunaho heart. If you do, they will surely make arrangements that are convenient for you.'

Hatsu: 'No matter what you say, there is no such Riiu. As for my Shikaujiyō, Takahime-sama is my Shishiyō, so I feel bad, but from the perspective of public duty, I am the Kamutsukasa here, so I have no reservations. I have matters I must investigate as part of my duties, so please convey to the two of them that they must come here immediately.'

Hatsu: 'Even so, with such a terrible temper, it seems unlikely to move. If I were to convey such a thing, I would be stripped of the duties I have worked so hard to attain as the right arm of Mokusuke-sama. Isn't that right, Toku?'

Toku: 'Un.'

Matsuhime: 'Hey, Hatsu-sama, you just said that you have become the right arm of Mokusuke-sama, didn't you?'

Hatsu: 'Yes, I certainly said so. Since the husband of the new Kyōshiyu Takahime-dono, Mokusuke—also known as the god of Tokiokishi—has made us, Hatsu-sama and Toku-sama, the two arms of Mokusuke-sama who manages the Sōmu of the Saien no Yakata, and we who command all the propagandists, even you, Matsuhime-sama, now that it has come to this, cannot help but obey the orders of us, Hatsu-sama and Toku-sama. How is it? I shall await your reply.'

Matsuhime: 'Ho ho ho ho ho. With a figure like a Sanesuke doll or a dried grasshopper, you certainly do boast. I do not know if you have become the two arms of Mokusuke-sama, but while you are here, you must listen to the orders of this Matsuhime. You must at least receive a notice of dismissal from your duties from Magahiko, and then, upon the recommendation of Mokusuke-sama, receive a proper letter of appointment from Yashimanushi-sama. It would be best if you woke up from such dreams.'

Hatsu: 'No matter what you say, it is no use. It was actually spoken from the mouth of Mokusuke-sama. And Takahime-sama is the witness. Hen, there is no mistake about this, right, Toku-kō?'

Hatsu-kō: 'Un, un, un.'

Matsuhime: 'Ho ho ho ho ho.'

Hatsu-kō: 'Look here, Matsuhime, this is extremely rude. I am the second-in-command of the Saien no Yakata, known as the right arm of the Sōmu. How can you laugh when you see my face, or rather, how can you look down on me? If you do not know the difference between public and private, the root and the branch, and self and others, you can never be a Kamutsukasa. The truth is, although it was while drinking, Mokusuke-sama entrusted us with full authority and ordered us to drive Matsuhime out. Now, the words of Hatsu-kō are the words of Mokusuke. Now, tuck up your skirts and get out quickly. If you delay, I shall not forgive you.'

Matsuhime: 'Utsufufufufu. My, how good you are at beggar theater. Now, I will give you one mon, so please go home. I have seen enough.'

The two stood in a Niōdachi posture, placing Matsuhime in the middle, and now, clenching their fists, they were about to pounce like Hichō. Matsuhime remained Taizenjijiyaku, not in the least disturbed, and stared into the eyes of the two. Although the Riyōnin tried to strike, for some reason, it seemed as if light emanated from Matsuhime's body, and their eyes were dazzled, making them unable to pounce. Matsuhime, with a calm heart, sang a song.

'The old fox borrowing the tiger's might, Appearing on the mountain of Kogita, Invading the Matsuhimeyakata, Blowing unreasonable demands, In a cowardly and unrefined manner, the two, Line up their threatening words, With that appearance like a wooden puppet, Clenching their fists, Trembling, how laughable it is. As she finished singing, the two shed tears falling from both eyes. And they moved their heads slightly, expressing their intention to repent. Matsuhime instantly released the spiritual binding. The two returned to their original state and ran out to the front, pattering away. Did those two truly repent?
