Chapter 11: 乙女の遊 [1326]

乙女の遊〔1326〕

§ 1

Takahime, with two attendants, was filled with smiles, and as if intoxicated by the supreme joy of having gone to Hōraizan, she leaned against a kefusoku in a state of ecstasy, feeling that even if she were to hold the entire Uchiu in her hands, it would not be as joyful as this, the opening of her fortune, when the door was suddenly flung open and, with loud footsteps, Takamiyahikonomikoto, wearing a hexagonal crown of brilliant gold, arrived in high spirits.

He immediately turned his back to the floor and sat down, burying his knees upon a thick, fluffy kinuzabuton.

Takahime, while displaying her charms in a most joyful manner, said, 'This is indeed my husband, Takamiyahiko-sama, you have visited my ima well. I have been longing for your arrival, feeling as if a single moment were a thousand autumns. I am so happy.'

She said this with tears in her eyes. Eugenbau said, 'Nay, Takamiyahime-dono, I have been rude for not showing my face for so long. You must have been very lonely.'

'Yes, fortunately, my two daughters have been attending to me, so I do not feel overly lonely, but when I cannot see your figure, I feel lonely nonetheless, I know not why.'

'Ahahahaha, so it seems you do miss this Takamiyahiko after all. Well, it must be so. That we have become husband and wife in this way must be a bond from the Kamiyo that cannot be severed.'

'By the grace of the Lord God, to have such a reunion of parent and spouse within such a noble palace is truly a joy beyond compare. Your majestic figure, the rejuvenation of Takamiyahime, this golden palace and jade tower, and furthermore, the two beauties, Takakohime and Miyakohime, who are like the divine jewels of indestructible diamond adding flowers to brocade—surely not even the magnificent spectacle of the Tengyokushibikyū nor the scenery of the Ryūgūjō could compare to this.'

'That is as it should be. By the miraculous divine power of the indestructible diamond wish-fulfilling jewel, I have moved the palace of the Tengyokushibikyū to the earth, and have also pulled up the most beautiful parts of the Ryūgū from the seabed to build this great castle; it is only natural that the golden palace and jade tower at its center, and the Takamiya palace of the Kuruwa castle, are beautiful, ahahahaha.'

'That... no, no, Takamiyahiko-sama, what is the extent of this castle?'

'Hmm, indeed, it is one hundred chō from east to west, and one hundred chō from north to south; it is quite vast. In this palace at its center, the joy of holding the world with you and these two is exceptional. However, Takamiyahime, listen well. Until yesterday, the remains of the camps where the vanguard of the Brahman army, the two generals Ranch and Katahiko, were stationed were scattered here and there, a desolate wilderness that was painful to behold, but by the magic of divine transformation, the fields, forests, and hovels were instantly transformed into a capital of flowers. Although this building, which has exhausted the essence of heaven and earth, has finally been erected by pulling the castle from the heavens and the seabed, from now on you must join forces with me. If we do not use our means to lure every single one of the wretches of the Sangyō-kyō and Uranai-kyō, who hinder our actions, into this castle and destroy them both in spirit and flesh, it will be difficult to maintain this glory for ten thousand generations. The most fearsome is Susanoo-no-Mikoto, who oversees the Sangyō-kyō. There are many others who follow him, such as Higashinowake-no-Mikoto, Yashimanoshi-no-Mikoto, Hinodewake-no-Mikoto, Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto, Amenomehitotsu-no-Mikoto, Hatsuwakahime-no-Mikoto, and others, but these are the figures we must first target as our enemies. If we do not destroy every single wretch who follows them, the great ambition of us husband and wife will not be achieved. Takamiyahime, what means do you intend to take from now on? I wish to hear it, ahahahaha.'

'My husband, nay, my Lord, at this point, there is no need to ask such things. From now on, I shall slip out of this castle gate every day, take my two daughters with me, lie in wait for passersby near the fire-watch tower, and using this beauty and eloquence of mine, I shall lure them all into this castle and make them surrender. Please do not worry at all.'

§ 2

Takamiyahiko: 'Ah, it is done, it is done. As expected of Takamiyahime-dono, then I shall rest in the inner palace and attend to the daily divine duties; you, take Takako and Miyako with you, and before the fire watchtower, not to mention those passing by, capture every single one of the Ananai-kyō Sendenshi and the Yatsubara who have submitted to Ananai-kyō and are coming to worship at the Iso mansion, and bring them back into our castle.'

Takahime: 'Your command shall be fulfilled. Now that I, Takahime, have become so youthful again, I shall exert all my strength and use every means to lure them in; please, by all means, be at ease.'

Takamiyahiko: 'Indeed, hearing that puts my mind at ease. After all, this Ukiyo is about wine and women, and another important thing is Kensei. No matter how much of a Chishiya or Gakushiya one may be, or even a Seijinkunshi, without Kensei, one cannot flourish in this world. First, we must collapse Ananai-kyō, and unless we give peace to the Lord Daikoku-nushi-no-Ōkami, not to mention the Moon Kingdom of over seven thousand countries, the three thousand worlds will be in chaos like tangled hemp, and furthermore, into our world of evil spirits... No, it is clearer than seeing fire that the world of evil spirits intends to destroy us. If we do not advance before me and destroy the mansion, we shall end up being destroyed by them. No matter what miraculous power the wish-fulfilling jewel may have, the enemy also possesses a divine treasure. You must be vigilant... now, set forth from the gate to manifest the glory of your first battle.'

He declared this with a tone that was solemn and dignified, as always. Takahime replied:

Takahime: 'Yes, I understand. I shall surely perform a feat to show you. Now Takako, Miyako, follow your mother.'

She fluttered her brocade sleeves in the breeze, and while swaying her body with a 'shona shona' motion, she held up her hem, parted from Takamiyahiko, walked along the long corridor, put on her golden clogs at the entrance, and headed toward the foot of the fire watchtower in the Uki-no-mori, moving with a sliding gait like a high-ranking courtesan, 'shonari shonari.'

Takahime, together with her two attendants, took off their divided skirts, hid them in the room beneath the fire watchtower, and each carrying a long-handled basket, they pretended to be absorbed in picking violets and dandelions. Then, gazing at the winter camellia flowers blooming there as they fell naturally, she recalled the ancient Armenian era and said:

'In the calm / early spring / small garden, / lonely and forlorn, / the small winter camellia / like a maiden's lips. / The dripping green leaves / are like a sick person / drenched in rain / since last night. / The camellia flower / trembles faintly. / I am now / tormented / by the painful figure / of that lover of mine, / projecting the past / into the present / and gasping for breath. / A tearful mood / drifts in the surroundings / and attacks my small chest. / Oh, this camellia flower, / it resembles my figure / and the thoughts of my love.'

Saying such things, she became completely like an eighteen-year-old, reminiscing about the past and floating that romance like a dream, pinning her thoughts on the camellia flowers. She had become a figure of such innocence that, no matter how one thought about it, one could not believe these were the words of Takahime, who had been tossed by the waves of the world, played every political trick, crushed fierce men like demons, and tormented divine beings. Yet, even though a hidden dragon may sink into an abyss, once it encounters wind and clouds, it is a mystery that the obsession and flame-like eloquence, which could shake heaven and earth, stir up black clouds, and lick up countless stars beneath the black clouds, gushed forth from Takahime's aged body without regret. As Takahime was in her innocent form, holding out her white hands and picking dandelions as if afraid, two men came along wearing straw raincoats and hats, with straw sandals and leggings, gripping diamond staves in their left hands, and singing a propaganda song as they advanced.

'The God appears in the world / to distinguish between good and evil spirits. / The Lord Kuninotokotachi-no-Ōkami, / who created this world, / took upon himself / the ugly sins and crimes / of all the gods and men / of heaven and earth, / left the heavenly pole of the Shibi-gū, / and descended to the bottom of the earth. / Ah, nevertheless, the Great God, / with a heart of infinite mercy, / wishing to save and help this world, / worries in a thousand ways. / Changing his form, / wandering near and far, / protecting the people of the world, / without minding a hundred hardships, / how grateful it is / that he protects us. / I, who served the Brahman religion, / am the general of Ranchi. / I am General Katahiko.'

Receiving the command of Ōkuronushi Mikoto, Kamususanō no Ōkami appeared at the mansion of Iso. To destroy Him and purify the evils of the world, they led a great number of troops, arranged their ranks, and marched majestically through the forest of Ukiki and Mount Kajikayama. Just then, they were persuaded by the Shinjin of Shinrikimusau, a Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, and came to understand the true path of Ananahi. They abandoned their arms, became disciples of Hinodewake no Mikoto, and stopped for a long time at the forest of Kurusu and the mountain pass of Tēmusu. There, they listened to the Mioshihe of Tengoku and Jiyōdo, and realized the Honbun of Jinsei.

My Shinkau became ever firmer, and even with millions in gold or beauties who could deceive a celestial maiden, I developed a courageous heart that would not stir with impure thoughts. This is entirely the gift of the rain of benevolence, which the Sumeragami descended to bestow upon us to save us. Ah, Kannagara kamunagara, may the divine spirit be blessed.

When I look at the forest ahead, I see the deeply impressive Ukiki-hara. It is a familiar land where I once led a great number of troops and stayed in camp. As the days and months passed, the scene has changed completely. What kind of great person has appeared to build such a magnificent city? Oh, how strange.

High above the clouds, glittering are the great mansions and high towers, with the sunlight shining upon the tiles of gold and silver. This situation is incomprehensible. Katahiko, you Sendenshi, how do you see that scene? It is suspicious,' I asked. Katahiko tilted his head, and with a heavy mouth, replied, 'Just as you say, my lord, it is truly a strange sight. Come, let us hasten from here, quicken our steps, and investigate the truth of it. What do you think?' When he asked this in return, Ranchi replied, 'Indeed, an investigation is most appropriate.' As they walked along the road, they talked, and tapping the ground with their two staves, they arrived quietly at this place, at the foot of the fire-watch tower.

For Ranchi and Katahiko, the traces of the time they were stationed there four months ago had vanished like smoke, and indescribably magnificent castles and city streets stood in rows, with the fire-watch tower shining in brilliant gold, illuminating the surroundings. The two stood still in wonder, and with wide eyes, they looked around restlessly in silence. Ranchi finally opened his mouth,

Ranchi: 'Nay, Katahiko-dono, is this not strange? There is no trace of the camp where I, as a general, set up quarters with you, and this grand structure, like a capital of a thousand years, and the state of the bustling city streets standing like teeth of a comb—is it not like a dream?'

Katahiko: 'Indeed, it is truly ten thousand times strange, just as you say. Perhaps it is the evil scheme of the Akugami. Even if it were any Shinjin, I could not imagine that such a project could be completed in a short period of time. Well, well, what a strange thing. Nay, there are three young women at the root of the camellia tree over there, picking flowers. Shall we capture those women and inquire about the state of affairs inside this castle?'

Ranchi: 'Indeed, that would be well.'

Saying this, they advanced toward the three maidens. The surroundings were becoming spring-like, and from between the withered grasses of last year's memory, the sprouts of green grass were growing thin and soft. The voices of small birds echoed all around like music.

『成程、貴殿の申さるる通り実に不思議千万で厶る。もしか悪神等の悪企みでは厶るまいかな。如何なる神人と雖も、かくの如き事業を短日月に完成すべしとは思ひも寄らぬ。さてもさても不思議の事よ。いや、向ふの椿の木の根元に妙齢の女が三人、花を摘んでゐる様です。彼の女を捕へ、此城内の様子を伺つて見ようではありませぬか』

『成程、それも宜しからう』

と云ひながら三人の乙女の方へと歩を進めた。

四辺は春めきて、去年のかたみの枯草の間から、青草の芽霧が細く柔かく伸びて居る。小鳥の声は音楽の様に四辺に響いて来た。
