The Kami is truly raising a voice and suffering. Because Yashimanushi no Mikoto and Kotoorowake no Mikoto are nose-high people of the middle path whose wisdom is formed by learning, they do not let the words of the spirits of the Tenjin of the Reikoku enter their ears at all, and the Kami is truly troubled.
