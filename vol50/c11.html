Chapter 11: 鸚鵡返 [1305]

鸚鵡返〔1305〕

§ 1

In the Miyakata of the forest of the shrine, Mokusuke appeared; his true Shiyōtai is a beast of mystery, possessing the hybrid nature of a lion and a tiger, known in the world of Eumi as the right arm of the Yamataorochi that coils around Taiunzan. Known as Eugenbau, he appears and disappears here and there, using Shinshiyutsukibotsu Eujiyutsu to torment the Yobito, turning the Chijiyō world into a Makai, destroying all goodness, and plotting to make the world one of Jiyaaku and Kiyogi; his madness is Yuyu.

The Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, Tamakuniwake, has served with Tansei the strict Mitama and the Mizumitama. As he comes forth to trample the sacred shrine of the noble Kami, he deceives Takahime-tsukasa, who has had her soul stolen by the demon, and posing as husband and wife, he remains in hiding. Suddenly, the Sendenshi famous in the way of Ananai-kyō, Hatsuwakahime, arrives with her spiritual light, accompanied by a fierce dog. Fearing this, he begins to flee, but Smart, who serves the Kami, sees through his true Shiyōtai and suddenly summons his courage. He latches onto the Kyūtsu, who possesses a body many times larger than his own, and bites into the area between his brows. As they grind their teeth near the valley water flowing through the forest, the Eumi is suddenly struck with astonishment.

Pointing toward the mountain ridge as if the clouds were mist, he flees for the time being. Smart, having injured his leg, returns while limping and enters the living room of Hatsuwakahime. While licking his wounds for a while, his self-healing is ingenious. Takahime-tsukasa, in her sadness of not knowing that Mokusuke is a ferocious and fearless Kyūshin, loves and yearns for him as her own husband, and secretly plans evil deeds. First, she plots to poison Uzuhiko and Shizuko-no-kata, who are eyesores to her, and by falsely claiming Kaedehime as her own child, she intends to take a position in the sacred grounds forever, and while the Kami Susanoo-Ōkami resides in the hall of the Saien, she whispers with her head bowed, plotting to obstruct his divine strategy for the salvation of the Sekai; how terrifying.

An angel appeared at the bedside of Kaedehime, solemnly as Monju Bosatsu, the incarnation of Kototama-wake. Accompanied by the brave Smart, the angel commanded that she save her parents from their crisis, gave her the divine elixir of Hyakudoku-kaisan, hid his body in the clouds, and departed to parts unknown. Kaedehime awoke with a start, and upon checking the fist of her hand, she found she was clutching the spiritual medicine she had received in her dream; how mysterious. Eugenbau Mokusuke, following his own will, drove Smart away from this place for the time being, but before they could find peace, Takahime, in order to heal the wounds of Mokusuke-tsukasa, sought to secretly obtain the Tamatake growing on the treetops of a great cedar.

Hiding from the eyes of others, she leaned a ladder against the trunk of the great tree. With her heavy body swaying, she finally reached the high first branch, and while getting caught in spider webs, she searched the treetops to the right and left. Suddenly, she recognized the two eyes of an owl and, thinking this was the Tamatake, reached out with joy and courage. The owl, startled, pecked at the two eyes of Takahime with its hook-like beak with all its might. Taken by surprise, Takahime fell from the high place with a thud, suddenly crashing to the ground; her eyes blurred, her waist ached, and she was left breathless.

Iru, who served as the receptionist, was astonished at the sight and immediately spread the word throughout the hall. Uzuhiko, Shizuko, and the young men Iru, Iku, Saru, Haru, and Teru carried Takahime into the living room. Through the earnest nursing of Hatsuwakahime, Takahime caught her breath for a while, but she urged them to summon Mokusuke-sama quickly; how pitiful is her heart. Her face suddenly swelled up, and the terror of her having changed into a hideous visage that one could not bear to look at twice was great. Fearing her as a monster of the forest of the shrine, no one would approach her.

Nevertheless, the Sendenshi who guards the teachings of Ananai-kyō, Hatsuwakahime, kindly cared for Takahime-tsukasa. As she nursed her for seven or eight days, the days passed, and her two eyes suddenly opened wide as before, the pain ceased abruptly, and even the swelling of her face subsided. Before they could even rejoice, Takahime, fearing the divine power of Hatsuwakahime, began to plot evil schemes once again; how wicked it is.

§ 2

Takahime, thanks to the kind care of Hatsuwakahime, finally recovered from the pain in her lower back, and the swelling on her face subsided, her eyes beginning to shine again like those of a falcon. It is said that forgetting the heat once the throat is past is the way of the shōjin; possessed by a kiyōrei, Takahime began to spout insults twice as much as before, and even the remarkable Hatsuwakahime and Uzuhiko found her more than a handful to deal with.

Takahime, taking advantage of her recovery, visited the mansion of Uzuhiko with a jaunty air.

Takahime: 'Oh, excuse me. Is Uzuhiko-sama in good gokigen? Is Shizuko-sama also doing well? I have been suffering from an injury for a long time, but you, my goshinsetsu anatasama, despite knowing how much I was suffering, did not come to visit me even once. I truly appreciate your goshinsetsu. As expected of a married couple who serve Ōkamisama jikijiki, you are so thoughtful about everything. Since I must repay this goshinsetsu, I may be a bother, or rather, I may be a nuisance for a little while.'

While speaking from the kadoguchi, she entered the room without even being told to come up, sat down heavily in front of the brazier, leaned against a pillar, and puffed on a cigarette, her arrogant posture truly detestable. Kaedehime gracefully opened the sliding door and appeared before Takahime.

Kaedehime: 'My, you are Giritenjō-sama, aren't you? That was truly a pity the other day, hee hee hee hee hee. You must have had quite the shugyō with that, didn't you? You served your father and mother such precious poison, it was truly a pity, wasn't it? Ho ho ho ho ho.'

Takahime, startled and looking askance, replied:

Takahime: 'Now, Kaede-sama, no matter how young you may be, I cannot let such akugen pass. You said that I, Giritenjō, poisoned my parents. On what evidence do you say such things? This must be a plot to attach a false bad name to me, Giritenjō, and drive me out by pinning an innocent crime on me. Everyone here is nothing but an akunin, not worth a damn. As long as such akuma infest this mansion, no matter how much you say you want to drive me out, I, Takahime, will not budge an inch. And what are Uzuhiko-sama and Shizuko-sama doing? Even though their own daughter is saying such rude things as "you fed us poison," they do not even come to offer an excuse; you are all yotsuashi mitama, every one of you. That is why it is so hard for me, Giritenjō. Because such ignorant akunin are defiling this magnificent mansion, even the shinriki musō Mokusuke-sama became a victim of yours and suffered such deep wounds, and now his figure is nowhere to be seen. It is likely because you are so defiled that the seijō muku Mokusuke-sama disliked you and returned to the Saien mansion. Oh, what a hopeless magatsu has gathered here. Starting with Yashimano-nushi, who is oblivious to the hopeless teachings of the hensei joshi, women like Tamakuniwake and Isokohime do not even consult me, the essential Giritenjō-sama, nor do they seek permission, but on their own whim, they have appointed the Uzuhiko couple—whose spirits are possessed by hawks, kites, tanuki, or weasels, I know not which—to be the great kamitsukasa in the sacred grounds where Ōkamisama resides. On top of that, you have brought in half a dozen pieces of junk laborers, fallen warriors of Brahmanism who are good for nothing, and you defile the sacred grounds day by day. It seems that the sacrifice of Mokusuke-sama was not enough, for even his wife, Giritenjō, has suffered for so long; this, too, is a chiza no okido I have borne for the sake of you and your parents. During that illness, it would have been decent to come at least once and offer a perfunctory greeting, saying, "Giritenjō-sama, how are you feeling? How is the medicine?" Truly, you are ungrateful, worse than dogs or beasts. Where have the Uzuhiko and Shizuko couple hidden themselves, fearing my shintoku? Come now, Kaede-sama, bring them here, for I must give them my opinion once.'

Ama: 'I do not like you. Because you are a terrible Daiakuma who conspired with Mokusuke's Eugenbau to feed my father and mother a wicked medicine called Dokusan, no one will ever visit you, thinking it is a fitting punishment, no matter how much you suffer. How pitiful you are. Ho ho ho ho ho.'

Takahime: 'Now, you little Ama. What are you saying, acting so small, and what evidence do you have to claim I fed them Dokusan? As proof that it is not poison, are your parents not perfectly fine? They have not developed a single fever, nor have they coughed even once; if you speak such unreasonable things, I shall not have you staying in this mansion.'

Ama: 'Thank you very much for your concern. My father and mother said they did not even want to see the face of a terrifying person like you, so they quietly went to pay their respects at the Iso mansion last night. This place is under the jurisdiction of Uzuhiko, and I do not know if you are Giritenjiyō or Fugiritenjiyō, but just wait for two or three days; there is no doubt that an eviction order will come down from the Iso mansion, he he he he he, how pitiful for you.'

Takahime: 'Truly, truly, even though you are but a child, Ama, what kind of Muhon are you plotting? You went to slander this Ikimiya all the way to the Iso mansion, claiming I committed Dokugai. Hey, you hateful little Kozura, I will make you regret this soon enough; even if an eviction order comes, as long as the Gomeirei of Hinodenokami does not descend, I will not budge.'

Ama: 'O ho ho ho ho, Hinodenokami-sama? That is a lie, a lie. Because you were plotting such evil things, my father and mother found it unbearable and were just saying they might go to report it to the Iso mansion. Since they have not gone yet, they must have gone to the shrine to offer prayers. If you repent now and apologize for feeding my father and mother poison, I will intercede for you and stop them from going to the Iso mansion. Tenjiyō-sama, how about it? If this matter is reported, even Hinodenokami-sama would be troubled, would he not?'

Takahime: 'Where is there anyone who is troubled by being falsely accused? I am a little different from a person like the future Sugawara no Michizane, who was meekly exiled in four directions for a false charge and spent his whole life crying. I will sue you from this side for the crime of false accusation. No matter what you say, I will not accept it. Now, quickly drag your parents here, make them press their heads to the ground before Giritenjiyō-sama, and apologize with their posteriors in the air like a flower vase. If you do that, depending on the circumstances, I might just suppress my anger and spare you.'

Ama: 'O ho ho ho ho, hey you foolish Tenjiyō-sama, evil Tenjiyō-sama, and stubborn Fugiri-Tenjiyō-sama, it will not go that way. You consulted with Eugenbau and mixed the Dokusan you brought from the land of India into their sake and rice to make them eat it. However, my mother and father are the incarnations, or rather the messengers, of Kototama-wake-no-Mikoto, and they had received a wonderful, wonderful spiritual medicine called Shintan from Monju Bosatsu-sama, so your Dokusan did not work. Now, what do you say? If what Kaede says is wrong, I will pray for Monju Bosatsu-sama to appear here right now, and then you will have to face your end.'

Takahime: 'I know nothing of such things. Do not go on babbling like a child. Would Takahime, the Zen-Ichijō and the Ikimiya of Hinodenokami, ever do such a thing even in a dream? That is just a dream you saw out of your own prejudice. And what is unforgivable is that you dared to call my husband, Mokusuke-sama, Eugenbau. Now, what evidence do you have for saying such a thing? I will sue you for the crime of slander.'

Ama: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, the words of a minor or a young girl do not hold up in law. Truly, how pitiful; the efficacy of your Dokusan is absolutely useless before divine power. Now, now, please go home quickly.'

Takahime was greatly enraged, grabbed Kaede by the chest, clenched her fist, and said,

'Hey, you swallow that chirps on and on, I shall treat you to this turban shell pot-roast!'

While saying this, she struck the head of the lovely young girl three or four times in succession.

'Aree, murder!'

Hearing Kaede's scream, the five who came running in a flurry were Iru, Iku, Saru, Haru, and Teru.

『無実の罪を着せられて困る者が何処にあるか。無実の咎でおめおめと親子が四方に流され、泣いて一生を暮した未来の菅公見たやうな人間とは、ヘンちつと違ひますぞや。誣告の罪で、此方の方から訴へてやるのだ。何と云つても承知はせないぞや。さあ早く両親をここへ引きずり出して、義理天上様に、頭を地に摺りつけ、尻を花瓶にしてあやまりなさい。さうしたら都合によつたら、虫を押へこらへてやらぬものでもない』

『オホホホホ、オイ馬鹿の天上さま、悪の天上さま、どつこい不義理の天上さま、さうは往きませぬぞや。お前さまが妖幻坊と相談をして、印度の国から持つて来た毒散をお酒の中や御飯の中にまぜて喰はしたのだ。けれど、家のお母さまやお父さまは言霊別命様の御化身、いやお使ひ、文珠菩薩さまから、結構な結構な神丹と云ふ霊薬を頂いてゐらつしやつたのだから、其毒散が利かなかつたのよ。サアどうですか。若し楓の云ふ事が違ふなら、今文珠菩薩様を念じて此処に現はれて貰ふから、さうしたらお前さまも往生しなくちやなりますまい』

『そんな事は知らぬわい。子供だてらツベコベ言ふものぢやない。苟くも日出神の生宮たる善一条の高姫が、夢にもそんな事を致すものか。それはお前達の僻みから、そんな夢を見たのだ。そして許し難い事は、吾夫杢助様を捉まへて妖幻坊だと云つたらう。サア何を証拠にそんな事を云ふのか、誹謗の罪で訴へますぞや』

『ホホホホホ、未丁年者や少女の言葉は法律にはかかりませぬぞや。真にお気の毒さま、毒散の効能も、神力の前にはサツパリ駄目ですなア。サアサア早く帰つて頂戴』

高姫は大いに怒り、楓の胸倉をグツと取り、拳を固めて、

『エエ、ツベコベとよく囀る燕め、この栄螺の壺焼をお見舞ひ申すぞ』

と云ひながら、可憐なる少女の頭を三つ四つ続け打ちに打つた。

『アレー、人殺ー』

と楓が叫ぶ声を耳にし、バラバラと駆けつけて来たのはイル、イク、サール、ハル、テルの五人であつた。
