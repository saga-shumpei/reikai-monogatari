§ 1

In the quiet window where the spring rain falls incessantly, the air in the Atari softens, and the head of Takahime, who is easily heated by things, feels as if she is sitting on a Jiyotan, warm and glowing, though she knows not why. Takahime, due to the Tsurezure of having many guests hidden within her Fukuchiu, is lost in a Kūsau of occupying the mansion of Iso, and is alone in her Etsubo.

The Kiyōrei called Giritenjiyō, lurking within her abdomen, is somehow in high Genki these days. This is because of the joy that Hatsuwakahime, by the Meirei of Takahime, has been confined to Uzuhikoyakata, forbidden from appearing in the Shinden or the Ōhiromahe for a while, and has furthermore driven away Smart.

However, as Smart is a Reijiu capable of Hengenshiyutsubotsu and Jiiujizai, he has by no means gone Doko. He merely stays near the person of Hatsuwakahime, and whenever he hears the Ashioto of a person, he quickly realizes it and strives to hide his body under the Yukashita.

Takahime took out an Ichigenkin and began to sing. Of course, Takahime does not possess the art of playing the koto or the like. It was the Toad-sensei who was possessing her, using her body in a Jiiujizai manner to play the koto.

Having finished playing, she put the Ichigenkin aside, placed both hands neatly on her knees, and while gazing at the hanging scroll written by her own hand in the tokonoma,

'Ho-ho-ho-ho,'

She laughed with joy. Yōgenbō came from the toilet, walking through the corridor with heavy Ashioto, opened the sliding door and entered,

'Takahime, you possess a mysterious hidden talent. I thought you were a fanatical woman devoted solely to faith, knowing nothing of such cheerful things. My, I am impressed.'

'Ho-ho-ho-ho, they say a talented hawk hides its claws. Until now, this Takachiyama has also been hiding her claws, but today, with this spring rain, I felt somehow lonely yet also festive, and I felt as if playing the koto would be in great harmony with heaven and earth, so for the first time in a while, I tried plucking the strings.'
