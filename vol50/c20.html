Chapter 20: Devil 払 [1314]

悪魔払〔1314〕

§ 1

Hatsuwakahime and Uzuhiko, seeing the commotion in Takahime's room along with Kaede, ran over to see what was the matter, and this was the scene that met their eyes.

Hatsuwaka: 'Mother, are you not being too rude to the Chiyokushisama? Please speak to them a little more politely and gently.'

'Oh, you should stay out of this, as it is not a place for you to jump into. No matter how many times I tell you, you keep hiding the smart under the bamboo mat behind my back, and now Tautō Mokusuke-sama has gone off somewhere, has he not? You are truly an unfilial Ama. Before long, Shinbatsu will strike you, and you will have to undergo Kunigahe. Read the Fudesaki of Giritenjiyō-sama. If things go poorly, Uzuhiko-sama, Shizuko-sama, Kaede, and you too will suffer Goshinbatsu and lose your Inochi. It is clearly written in the Ofude, you see.'

'That may well be so. After all, we are all those who have received the cup smeared with the blood of the dragon. However, Mother, please be at ease. Thanks to that, we have received divine virtue, so no matter how much poison evil gods or toads may serve, it will have no effect on us at all, so we are safe.'

'Oh, you cheeky thing, knowing nothing at all... just stay out of it. This Giritenjiyō-sama knows everything there is to know. Being clear on the phenomena of the formless spirit world, I have also reached the principles of the physical realm. Not to mention religions and sects, I know everything from the fundamental standard of order to the path of repentance, the path of the four seasons' operation, the great path of the divine nation, the path of good and evil, right and wrong, the connection of guardian deities, the divine law of immeasurable longevity, the path of the five virtues, the basis of progress and development, the great foundation of sincerity, the principle of Kototama, the divine connection of the sun and moon, not to mention the divine power of fire, water, and earth, the foundation of the teachings of Maitreya, the principle of one spirit and four souls, the truth of the universe, the origin of characters, the path of loyalty and filial piety, the foundation of peace, the teachings of universal brotherhood, to the state of cause and effect—there is nothing I do not know. Much less, from the connection of the great ancestors to the connection of the gods who solidified this muddy sea of the world, the nature of the evil spirits of the officials from the top to the bottom of the Saienkan, I know everything as if looking into a mirror. No matter how much one says that one cannot win against a crying child or a local magistrate, I am not a spineless spirit who would be cowed by direct envoys like Yajihiko or Yotahiko and say, "Yes, is that so? Well, then there is no help for it. I shall leave at once." You will gain nothing by staying here forever, so please leave quickly. This place is a structure built by Giritenjiyō Hinodewake alone. Now everyone, please leave quickly. Do not come here to interfere. If Lord Susanoo complains, the heavenly beings of the spirit realm, the lineage of the transformed male, and the spirits of Giritenjiyō will explain the reason of things and make him repent, so you lot had better leave while you can. This is the shooting range of Giritenjiyō, where we uphold the teachings of the transformed male through the teachings of Kinmō Kyūbi-sama and the protection of the Princess of the Dragon Palace, and... the teachings of Kan-shōyō-shin. Now, now, leave, leave. Ah, it makes the day pass by to be dealing with such ignorant fools. Excuse me for a moment, I am going to the restroom. I shall talk to you at leisure later, so please wait around there. Ah, what a troublesome lot they are. Even the gods are quite something. It is back-breaking work to try and lead such blind and deaf ones to the path of truth. This body is also exhausted. My shoulders, arms, and lower back have started to creak. Ah.'

Saying this, she stood up and went to the restroom alone.

Looking across at the forest, Mokusuke of Yōgenbō was running along, beckoning with all his might for her to come quickly. Once beckoned by Mokusuke, Takahime could no longer bear it. No sooner had she slipped her feet into the sandals that were there than she chased after Mokusuke with all her might. And from that moment on, she was never seen again in the forest of the shrine.

と云ひながら便所に一人立つて行つた。

向ふの林を見渡せば妖幻坊の杢助が、早く来れと一生懸命手招きしながらトントン走り出す。高姫も杢助に手招きされては最早堪らない。そこにあつた上草履を足にかけるや否や、一生懸命に杢助の後を追ひ駆け出して了つた。そして、それつきり祠の森へは姿を見せなかつたのである。

§ 2

Sumāto watched the two fleeing away, and without attempting to chase them, he stood crying out, 'Wauzu, wauzu, wauzu.'

Hatsuwakahime, Yasuhiko, Kunihiko, and the others watched Takahime's flight, yet none of them made a move to pursue her. This was because they all felt as though they had performed an Akumabara. If Takahime had firmly established herself in the forest of the shrine, it would have been impossible to move her with ordinary means. However, because she had her spirit captivated by the Eugenbau Mokusuke and had fled this mansion where she had been struggling, it was truly a supreme Shiahase for Uzuhiko. The fact that Sumāto did not follow her, and that Hatsuwakahime had asked the Chiyokushi to wait for a while, were all things anticipated based on such a Dekigoto. In such a time, the Eugenbau had truly performed a good Goyō for the sake of the Ananai-kyō.

The Eugenbau and Takahime went down the slope, step by step. The Kiyori between them was separated by only about two or three chō. Nevertheless, Takahime pursued him with Shifunenbuka, and finally caught up at the forest of the mountain pass, where the two decided to spend the night.

Yasuhiko and Kunihiko bade farewell to Uzuhiko and climbed up the Kajika Pass to report the details of the situation to the mansion of the Saien. The two hasty individuals, Iku and Sāru, had somehow chased after Takahime and flown out of the mansion. Hatsuwakahime instructed Uzuhiko thoroughly on the future whereabouts, bade farewell to the three, Haru, Teru, and Iru, finished her worship at the shrine, and offered prayers that Takahime might repent as soon as possible, deciding to live here for two or three days before setting out on her journey again. Naturally, Sumāto followed the princess like a shadow.

The three, Iru, Haru, and Teru, visited the shrine to give thanks for the departure of the evil spirit, recited the Amatsu Norito, and sang a song to express their gratitude.

Iru: 'I, the Iru-tsukasa, who serve at the reception of the forest of the shrine, before the presence of the Sumerōkami, with reverence and awe, observe that Takahime and the Magatsunu of the Eugenbau, fearing the divine virtue of the God, have fled in a single bound. This sacred ground is now as it was before, pure without a speck of dust, and has settled most peacefully. Before the presence of the Lord, I humbly offer my gratitude for that grace. Although the true form of the evil spirit of the Eugenbau, who calls himself Mokusuke-tsukasa, is not clearly visible to our eyes, he is no ordinary fellow. Is he a tiger, a wolf, a lion, or a bear? Or perhaps a demon monster? He is an Magatsu-kami of unknown origin.

He appeared in this mansion uninvited, morning and evening, drinking sake, eating, and blustering, and never once did he chant a single Kototama. Together with Takahime-tsukasa, who is said to be possessed by the golden-haired nine-tailed fox and is the living shrine of the Hinodewake-no-kami, he has disturbed this sacred ground; truly, he is a hateful villain. We prayed secretly morning and evening as to how we might drive away this calamity, and the Sumerōkami heard our prayer most peacefully. How joyful it is that He has driven away the Magatsu-kami who disturbed the teachings.

From now on, we shall revere the commands of Uzuhiko-sama as the words of God, and morning and evening, we shall serve with all our sincerity. Now that the Magatsu has fled so comfortably, the believers will also rejoice and gather again as before, and we shall receive the divine virtue once more. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu. May You protect this mansion, for Your divine majesty is manifest. I humbly pray for this. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, how could we ever deviate from the teachings of the Sumerōkami?

We three, uniting our hearts, serve the Great God Susanoo-no-Ōkami who has appeared in the mansion of the Saien. While serving the pure great path of Ananai that He protects, and receiving the protection of Haniyasuhiko and Haniyasuhime-no-mikoto upon our necks, we shall open up the path even to the ends of the one hundred and eighty islands of the four directions. Kannagara. With awe and reverence, I pray for the protection of the noble God.'

Haru sings again.

Haru: 'A soldier of Brahmanism, serving as its gatekeeper, I, the lowly official Haru, have received the precious grace of the Ōkami of Ananai-kyō. My Shinki has suddenly undergone Itsuten, and I have deserted the Jinei in the forest of Ukiki together with Yoru and Teru. Thinking to make a pilgrimage to Isoyakata, I arrived and heard that the Sendenshi of the Ananahi Tamakuniwake was staying in the forest of the shrine. Since then, I have been looking up to his Mioshihe and serving in the precious Miaraka. Participating in his Shingefu, the shrine was finally built, and the Sengūshiki was completed. Before we could even rejoice, the Tamakuni-wake-no-Mei bestowed upon us a heavy Shimei, and he ascended on the Seito against the Akuma. Starting with Isoko-hime, who appeared as his Tsumagami, Imako-hime has returned to the Isoyakata with purity. He left Uzuhiko and his child behind, appointing them as officials of the shrine, and we were each assigned various duties of the hall. Just as we were serving, having purified our hearts and cleansed our bodies, the twisted-hearted Takahime, in whom the nine-tailed golden fox dwells, suddenly appeared here. She declared, "I am the Hinodewake, the living shrine of Giritenjō," and with a very rough breath, she sat her large bottom down and occupied the forest of the shrine for a long time.'

Haru: 'She spreads the teachings of the ugly, and plots to obstruct the Shingefu of the Isoyakata; she is truly a troubled wretch. While I was secretly worrying about this and praying to the Sumerōkami, the Yōgenbō came again, calling himself the official Mokusuke. Together with the official Takahime, they are holding their ground in the depths of the hall, plotting to disturb such a precious sacred place; it is truly an abominable situation. While I was wondering what to do, the Kami-tsukasa Hatsuwakahime-sama appeared here, leading the fierce dog Smart. Concealing her Shinpenshigi divine power, she waits for the time when Takahime and Yōgenbō will flee of their own accord. We are surprised and deeply moved by her composed conduct.'

Haru: 'Ah, Sumerōkami, Sumerōkami! Though we are foolish, if we purify our hearts and cleanse our bodies, and serve before the Great Presence with nothing but sincerity morning and evening, please have mercy on us and grant us even one ten-thousandth of the divine power of Hatsuwakahime-sama. I humbly pray for this. Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth itself should sink, how could I break the words I have sworn to the Kami? Before the Kami, following the official Uzuhiko, I shall fully and minutely preach the Mioshihe of the Sumerōkami to the people who have gathered. May I be allowed to repay even one ten-thousandth of the great grace, Kannagara. I humbly pray before Your presence.'

Having finished singing, she descends the stairs and returns to the original reception desk.
