Chapter 18: Peace 国 Messenger [1312]

安国使〔1312〕

§ 1

At Uzuhikoyakata, six individuals—Uzuhiko, Shizuko, Kaede, Hatsuwakahime, and Yasuhiko and Kunihiko, direct messengers from the Iso Mansion—whispered together, their heads gathered in earnest dialogue.

Uzuhiko: 'I am deeply grateful for your trouble in coming as messengers from Ōkamisama from so far away. Being inadequate as we are, assigned a grand duty beyond our strength, we are unable to perform our service properly, which must surely cause trouble for Yashimanushinomikoto-sama. Doubtless you are messengers sent to scold us for our shortcomings.'

Yasuhiko: 'No, no, it is by no means so. There is no one who does not know your sincerity. Kyōshiyusama is also greatly delighted, so please be at ease.'

Uzuhiko: 'Is that so? It is truly awe-inspiring that he looks upon and listens to us, inadequate as we are, with a broad heart and corrects us.'

Shizuko: 'In any case, we humbly ask for your favor.'

Yasuhiko: 'Yes, I shall arrange things well. First of all, please be at ease.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Yasuhiko-sama, your coming this time must be regarding that matter of Takahime-sama, is it not?'

Yasuhiko: 'Just as you have surmised, that Takahime committed insolence toward Vice-Patriarch Higashinowake at the Iso Mansion, and moreover engaged in wild extravagances everywhere, slipped out of the mansion under cover of night, fled directly to this hall, and being controlled once again by evil spirits, her interference with the divine work is most extreme; hence it is an order that she be expelled from this hall as quickly as possible and driven back to Jitentōjima.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'How shocked Takahime-sama must surely be by that! Might it not be possible somehow to have Takahime-sama repent peacefully and allow her to serve here for a short while?'

Yasuhiko: 'Were it your request, we would never disobey. However, as we have come as messengers of the Patriarch, we cannot act on our own discretion in any way.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Indeed, that is entirely reasonable. If it is an order of Kyōshiyusama, it cannot be helped. However, will you not leave it to me for a little while here? I wish to somehow save Takahime-sama's soul. If she is expelled now, her mind will become increasingly wild, and she may become unable to do anything whatsoever. Should you meet Yashimanushi-sama, would you not kindly request him to leave it to Hatsuwakahime for a short while?'

Yasuhiko: 'Yes, I have understood. I shall return immediately and try to request a postponement.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Thank you for your immediate consent.'

Kunihiko: 'Nay, Uzuhiko-dono, I have heard that Takahime-dono has brought in a somewhat suspicious man, but what kind of person is he?'

Uzuhiko: 'Yes, we do not know the truth of the matter, but he is indeed a suspicious person. He says he is Mokusuke who came from the Iso Mansion, but he absolutely refuses to meet Hatsuwakahime-sama. That is the first thing I find strange; I have thought of asking Hatsuwakahime-sama about it once, but thinking it too impolite, I have refrained without asking.'

Kunihiko: 'How strange! Director-General Mokusuke attends the Iso Mansion every day. It is mysterious in the extreme. Have you not noticed any suspicious points?'

Uzuhiko: 'Yes, I do not know why, but he seems to dislike dogs exceedingly. The other day he said he took a walk in the forest, stumbled, and hit his forehead against a stone; but when I stole a glance, it was not a wound from falling down, but deep tooth marks as if bitten by a dog were attached.'

Kunihiko: 'Indeed, that is all the more suspicious. Yasuhiko-dono, what do you think? This cannot be passed over unheard. We ought to investigate his true identity.'

Yasuhiko: 'Well, since we came as messengers instructed to investigate everything without exception, we must thoroughly investigate not only Takahime but also the person who calls himself Mokusuke, and report back.'

Kunihiko: 'Sayō, let us proceed immediately.'

Uzuhiko: 'Please wait a moment. I must think this through thoroughly; if the other party senses our intent, they might flee again, so I shall go and observe the yōsu for a moment.'

Kaede: 'That Chiyokushisama claims to be married to Takahime-sama, and from morning until night, he does nothing but drink sake and attends to no Jimu at all. Furthermore, he attempted to kill my father and mother with a medicine called Dokusan. However, Monjiyubosatsusama appeared in a dream and gave me Shintan, so thanks to that, the poison did not take effect. If not for that, my Riyōshin would have passed away long ago. I truly cannot sleep even for a moment until something is done about such a wretch. While claiming to be the father of Hatsuwakahime-sama, he is so terrified of his true Shiyōtai being revealed that he has not met the Hime-sama even once; lately, he has locked Hatsuwakahime-sama in this mansion, and he and Takahime are the only ones in his Ima, allowing no one else to pass. That fellow is surely an Eukuwai without a doubt. And Takahime-sama believes the Eukuwai is Mokusuke-sama. Is it not logical that Mokusuke-sama of the Iso mansion cannot be both over there and over here?'

Yasuhiko: 'Well, the more I hear, the more incredibly strange it sounds. There is no doubt that this is an Eukuwai. Hatsuwakahime-dono, what are your thoughts on this?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'In my estimation, the monster calling himself Mokusuke is surely the demon known as Yōgenbō who resides on Mount Ōkumo. No matter what, my beloved dog Smart is terrified of him, and I cannot help but be concerned.'

Yasuhiko moved two or three steps closer, eyes wide and lips pursed, and said: 'Hime-sama, if you are aware of this, why have you allowed it to continue until now? Why not set Smart upon him and destroy him? Would that not also open Takahime's eyes?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'I have thought of doing so, but if I were to act so suddenly, Takahime-sama would suffer great shame, and I fear her disappointment and despair would be immeasurable. I am keeping my busy self here, waiting for the right time, hoping she will gradually come to realize the truth.'

Yasuhiko: 'Hime-sama, this is no time to be dawdling. Since we two have accepted this, we cannot return as things stand. Now, we shall begin the subjugation of the Eukuwai, so please, leave it to us two.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Defeating such an Eukuwai is a task I could do before breakfast. Even Smart alone would surely drive him away. However, even if I were to drive him away, he would undoubtedly go to another region and commit evil deeds. Therefore, I intend to keep him here for a while and cause the Eukuwai to repent from the bottom of his heart, so I am tempering my light and waiting for the right moment.'

Kunihiko: 'My arms are itching for action and I cannot help it. If I may, Hatsuwakahime-sama, do not speak of such long-term matters; please leave it to me. I shall surely exterminate him for you to see.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Impatience is a loss; please, wait a moment. It would not do to disobey the words of Chiyokushisama, but no matter what, he appeared claiming to be my father. Even if he is a monster, I cannot in good conscience cruelly torment one who bears the name of my father. Even if he is an impostor, how could I oppose him in the name of a father? Even if you were to exterminate him, now that it has reached the ears of this Hatsuwaka, I shall stop you at all costs. If you were to do it without my knowledge, it would be unavoidable, but now that it has come to this, you surely cannot commit such a cruel act against me.'

May Thou sweep away all the crookedness of this world, sweeping it away completely, and may Thou protect the peace of Urayasukuni, Kamunagara. Awe-struck by the edicts of Kami, Yasuhiko, who has come from the Seijiyō of Ubusunayama, together with Kunihikotsukasa, bows before the presence and prays. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may Thy Mitama be Sachihahe.

Kunihiko sings again: 'I am the Sendenshi Kunihiko, and the man known as Yotaotoko, Yotahiko of Takatorimura, together with Yajihiko-sama, while on our way to Caucasus, when we stayed at the inn of Otake, the Sendenshi of Hinodewake, the Kamuzukasa serving under his subordinates, appeared here, and while we were coming in haste astride our horses to the foot of Kojikatauge, together with the Kamuzukasa Otohiko, we two were left behind, and while fleeing, surrounded by the Metsukera of the Ural road, we fell into a thousand-fathom valley at the steep slope of Kojikatauge, and instantly became people of the netherworld.'

'Arriving at the river of Sanzu, we were lectured at length by Datsueba-sama, and after crossing the river, we encountered bronze wooden statues and other strange beings two or three times. Here, having finally polished our Mitama, I am Kunihiko, who served as a Sendenshi. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, bathed in the blessings of Kami, I have now joined the group of officials at the noble Saienkai, propagating the path of the teachings, and upon returning, how noble it is to have been commanded as the direct messenger of the forest of the shrine this time.'

'O Great Imperial Kami, O Hundred Kami, who have appeared at this shrine, I pray that Thou wilt command me to fulfill my mission completely and in detail, Kamunagara. Before the noble presence of Kami, both Yasuhiko and Kunihiko, in awe and trembling, pray. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may the Mitama of Kami bless us, and may the ugly spirits of Yōgenbō and the ugly nine-tailed golden fox, who dwell in this hall, awaken as soon as possible to the true path of the Ōkami, turn away from their evil deeds and lawlessness, cease to commit crookedness in the world, and become pillars that save the people of the world, Kamunagara. We pray with all our hearts to Kami.'

Having finished singing, they bowed reverently, descended the stairs, and once again, led by the guidance of Iru and Sāru, they patrolled every corner of the vast forest, examined in detail the site where Yōgenbō had met with disaster, and even confirmed that the bloodstains left behind were not human blood. After finishing, they went up to the Yahiro-den, and after resting for a while, they sipped tea while discussing their intention to inspect the living quarters of Mokusuke and Takahime.

Mokusuke and Takahime, being mere mortals, had no way of knowing that Yasuhiko and Kunihiko were now coming to inspect their quarters, and they had begun various interrogations with Haru.

原文（近代文語）

此世の曲を悉く 伊吹払ひに払ひのけ 浦安国の浦安く 守らせ給へ惟神 神の詔を畏みて 産土山の聖場より 参り来ませる安彦や 国彦司と諸共に 御前に畏み願ぎまつる ああ惟神々々 御霊幸倍ましませよ』

国彦は又歌ふ。

『吾は国彦宣伝使 高取村の与太彦と 名を知られたる与太男 弥次彦さまと諸共に コーカス参りの其途中 お竹の宿に泊る折 日出の別の宣伝使 部下に仕へる神司 此処に現はれましまして 駒に跨がりいそいそと 小鹿峠の麓まで 来る折しも音彦の 神の司と諸共に 吾等二人は残されて ウラルの道の目付等に 取囲まれて逃ぐる折 小鹿峠の峻坂に 前後に敵を受けしより 千尋の谷間に落ち込んで 忽ち幽冥の人となり

三途の川まで到着し 脱衣婆さまにいろいろと 小言八百並べられ 河を渡りて銅木像 其外怪しき者共に 二度三度出会し 茲に漸く身魂をば 研きすまして宣伝使 仕へまつりし国彦ぞ ああ惟神々々 神の恵を身に浴びて 今は尊き斎苑館 司の群に加はりて 教の道を宣伝し 帰りて見れば此度の 祠の森の直使をば 云ひつけられし尊さよ 此御社に現れませる 皇大神よ百神よ 何卒吾身の使命をば

完全に委曲に復り命 申させたまへ惟神 尊き神の御前に 安彦国彦両人が 畏み畏み願ぎまつる ああ惟神々々 神のみたまの幸はひて これの館に住まひたる 妖幻坊の醜霊や 金毛九尾の醜狐 一日も早く大神の 誠の道に目を醒まし 悪逆無道をひるがへし 世界に曲を行はず 世人を救ふ御柱と ならさせたまへ惟神 神かけ念じ奉る』

と歌ひ終り、恭しく拝礼をして階段を下り、又もやイル、サールの案内に連れて広き森林内を隈なく巡視し、妖幻坊が遭難場をも詳しく実見し、落ちこぼれたる血糊の、人間の血にあらざる事迄よく確め、終つて八尋殿に上り、暫し休憩の後、杢助、高姫の居間を臨検せむと相談しながら茶を啜つて居た。

杢助、高姫は安彦、国彦が今や吾居間に臨検に来るとは、神ならぬ身の知る由もなく、ハルを相手にいろいろと訊問を始めて居た。
