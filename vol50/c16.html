Chapter 16: 鷹鷲掴 [1310]

鷹鷲掴〔1310〕

§ 1

Iru, wearing a reishō, entered the hitoma to curry favor with Takahime, wrote it out with daisokuriyoku, immediately gripped the jikihitsu in his right hand, grabbed the sanpau in his left, reached the front of Takahime's ima, placed it down neatly and teinei as before, held it up mehachibu, and from outside the sliding door, in a most sauchiyō kotoba, called out:

Iru: 'The Giritenjiyō of Hinodenokami, tadaima gojiyurai. Aiya, Takahime-dono, pray open this sliding door.'

Takahime, finding the kotoba overly sauchiyō, thought it fushigi and slid the door open with a snap, only to find Iru standing there respectfully holding the sanpau with the fudesaki upon it.

Takahime: 'What is it, are you not Iru? You have a bad stomach, you gave me such a bitsukuri. Hen, you should not speak so grandly of Hinodenokami. Hinodenokami-sama resides within the tainai of this Takahime. How could He possibly reside within a person such as you?'

Iru, remaining standing there, said:

Iru: 'Aiya, Takahime, listen well. Hinodenokami may dwell within your tainai, but He may also dwell within the fudesaki. The fudesaki I have been made to hold is the very goshintai of Hinodenokami. At times He dwells within the tainai of Takahime, and at times He dwells within the fudesaki, and since I am made to guard it with my own nikutai, the Iru of this moment is the ikimiya of the Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami. If you treat it somatsu, you shall be punished. Do not make a torichiga. If you go afun, you will suffer from memahi. There is no machiga with Hinodenokami. You must not despise one who has fallen on hard times. You do not know what kind of okata may have a goyō for you.'

Takahime: 'Ah, ah, how precious, you have become half-mad from being struck by the goshintoku of Hinodenokami-sama. This is why they say pearls before swine, gold coins to a cat. Come, quickly hand it over here; it will not do to have two Hinodenokami.'

She tried to snatch it away with a sudden pull. Iru deliberately gripped the sanpau tight. Takahime put her strength into it, and at the moment she gave a hard tug, the top and the legs of the sanpau made a creaking sound and split in two. With that force, the fudesaki grazed the air and fell right into the charcoal fire that was glowing red. In an instant, the three volumes of the fudesaki became a black dragon of smoke and ascended to heaven.

Takahime: 'Ah, ah, what is this, Iru, what have you done? You have turned the jikihitsu that the Kami-sama went to the trouble of writing into nothing but smoke.'

Iru: 'How grand Hinodenokami-sama is. At last, a magnificent goshintai has appeared. As you know, fire emerged from the brazier and became Hinodenokami, and just like that, it became a black dragon and ascended to heaven wrapped in smoke. No, it must not be smoke, it must be the giri of morning mist and evening mist. That is why the Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami revealed His ryūtai and ascended to heaven. My, what high goshintoku you have, Takahime-sama. Ehehehe, I am truly filled with awe.'

He said this off the top of his head, mocking and praising Takahime at the same time. Takahime had intended to be angry, but caught up in Iru's wit, she felt a strange mixture of joy and irritation, stroked her chest to calm herself, and said:

Takahime: 'The jikihitsu has ascended to heaven, but well, well, it is magnificent. Since you had copied it, perhaps I shall have you rewrite it once more. It is not often that such magnificent fudesaki appear. Here, Iru, quickly bring that copy of the fudesaki here.'

Iru: 'Hee, I had intended to copy it, but because the fudesaki has such high goshintoku, it emitted a halo, my eyes were completely dazzled, and each and every character seemed to raise its head like a snake and move about like a ryūjin-sama; for those of us without such shintoku, it is impossible to copy, and even to read it makes my eyes go makumaku. That is why, thinking I would have you copy it, I have brought the goshintai here just like this.'

§ 2

Karakahizuki's Iru, while having in fact copied it perfectly, says such things on purpose.

Karakahizuki: 'Oh, how thoughtless you are. If you could copy it well, why did you not have others copy it?'

Iru: 'Because you, Anata, gave the Gomeirei to have Iru copy it, I thought I could not go against the words of Hinodenokami-sama, so I did not even show it to others.'

Karakahizuki: 'Oh, what a thoughtless man. Ah, I do not know what to do... You must remain in Kinshin and wait over there. And I shall entrust the Uketsuke to Haru. Do you think you can be in a good mood after committing such a Buchōhafu?'

Iru: 'Yes, there is no Shikata. However, my Shiyugojin may have copied it in the Reikai, so if it appears, you will surely forgive me.'

Karakahizuki: 'Do not say such Baka things. Do you think your Shiyugojin could write such things? Even for this Takahime, because the Shiyugojin cannot write it, he writes it through the hands of this Ikimiya. Ee, how disgusting, go over there. It makes me sick to even look at your face. You have burned three volumes of the Ketsukō Fudesaki, so how can you make Maoshiwake to the Kamisama?'

Iru: 'Thinking to meet the Tennin of the Reigoku, Giritenjiyō-sama may have Tenjiyō-ed using the art of Kesau. It is not something to be so discouraged about.'

Karakahizuki: 'Ee, noisy, go over there. If you dawdle, this broom shall pay you a visit.'

She said this, her face turning bright red, becoming desperate, and shouting in a state of near-madness. Iru left with one word, 'Yes,' opened and closed the sliding door in a hurry, hunched his shoulders, stuck out his tongue, and returned to the Uketsuke while hiding his footsteps, his chin trembling. At the Uketsuke, the four, Haru, Teru, Iku, and Saru, were reading the copy of the Fudesaki while laughing loudly.

Iru: 'Hey, do not laugh in such a loud voice, for Giritenjiyō is very angry right now.'

Haru: 'Nani, is there any reason to be angry? Did he not return saying, "Copy it quickly and fasten it to your belly"? I am currently trying my best to fasten someone else's loincloth. Ahahahaha.'

Saru: 'After having us copy such difficult characters, why is Takahime-sama so angry? If she was going to be angry, why did she tell us to copy it? She is truly saying things that make no sense.'

Iru: 'Nani, that may be so, but that Takahime, in the end, mistakenly dropped three volumes of the Fudesaki into the fire and burned them up. That is why she is in such a bad mood.'

Saru: 'Ihihihi, that serves her right, she must have made quite a strange face.'

Iru: 'She made a face just like the Fudō-sama above the hearth when struck with a burning stake, and glared at me intensely. And because she said, "You must have that copy, bring it here at once," I, being very indignant, said, "That Fudesaki had such high dignity that it radiated a halo, and each character was active, looking as if it stood up its neck like a Ryūjin and crawled around, so I could not copy it well." Upon that, even the great Takahime turned pale, and her disappointment and discouragement were truly exhilarating. That is why I told you not to read in a loud voice.'

Saru: 'Ahahahaha, indeed, that is interesting. Hey everyone, shall we read it out loud here?'

Iru: 'If you do such a thing, Takahime will come after us.'

Saru: 'Nani, I will keep watch in the hallway, so we will read it so it can be heard over there. And if Takahime comes, we will hide the Fudesaki under our bottoms and pretend we know nothing. If she asks, "Were you not just reading it?", we can just say, "Giritenjiyō-sama entered Iru's belly and read the Fudesaki in such a loud voice," can we not?'
