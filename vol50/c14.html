Chapter 14: 虬の盃 [1308]

虬の盃〔1308〕

§ 1

Takahime, from that time on, harbored an intense hatred for Hatsuwakahime, Kahedehime, Uzuhiko, and Shizuko, and, covering herself with a night-garment, she traced the path of a terrifying Onigokoro, plotting how she might destroy them. Yet, no matter how she pondered, the ordinary way would not suffice. Furthermore, should a critical moment arise, the formidable Smart would emerge. This was Takahime's primary Zutsū. Once things had come to this pass, no matter how much she might demand that Smart be returned, Hatsuwakahime would surely not return her. Moreover, she considered that it would be utterly Dame to exercise her Kenri as a mother, to Kiyōatsu her, and to force her to follow her own will. Thus, Takahime, after consulting with the Akuko in her Fukuchiu, devised an Itsukei. It was nothing other than a kind of Eujiyutsu. She was taught that by wringing the blood of a dragon, sealing Hiyakuchiu in a jar, preparing a blood-stained silk for the Chōfuku of the four in question, burning it in the fire of a Goma, keeping it hidden within the jar, and, under the guise of a Wagafu Shiyuen, secretly applying it to the cups of the four without their knowledge, those who drank the wine would lose their divine virtue, incur hatred by acting against the hearts of others, and eventually lead themselves to ruin.

From that time on, Takahime made it appear as though she were merely taking a walk in the forest, while she searched for the dragon and hunted for the Hiyakuchiu, devoting her full strength to this terrifying plan. Having finally gathered the items according to her order, she hid them under her own floor and waited for the time to arrive.

Takahime, thus thinking to destroy the four at some unknown time, smiled to herself, feigning gentleness and kindness on the surface, refraining from saying too many complaints or speaking ill, and striving so that the four would trust her and open their hearts to her. Indeed, there is nothing as fearful as when one is led astray by a female evil spirit and reaches the peak of fanatical heat. A woman is at once the most weak-hearted and the most strong. Once she has made up her mind, as it is commonly said, a woman's single-mindedness can pierce even a rock, and it is not easily moved. Takahime had steeled her resolve to carry out such a terrifying evil plot.

That such a scheme existed was something that no one, save for Hatsuwakahime, could perceive.

With all preparations for the plot complete, Takahime went herself to the residence of Uzuhiko, making a polite smile and bowing, while squeezing out a deliberately gentle voice:

Takahime: 'Oh, please forgive me. The other day, as I was recovering from an illness, my head was in a strange state, and it seems I spoke very rudely to you all. As I was quite delirious, I have no memory at all of what sort of improprieties I may have committed. Today, the Heavenly Hinodewake-sama told me of what I had done while in a dream-like state, and I was truly shocked. Even though I regret it and feel that it is truly inexcusable, it is a case of crying over spilt milk; it seems I was terribly rude to Hatsuwaka-sama, Kahede-sama, and you, the married couple. Having heard this from the Heavenly-sama, I could not sit still, and so I have come, suppressing my shame, to apologize. I beg you to please forgive my sins.'

She spoke in a weeping voice, shedding empty tears as she apologized. Hatsuwakahime knew the very bottom of Takahime's heart well. She had seen through the fact that this magic was merely the ravings of a fierce spirit and possessed no effect whatsoever. Therefore, if she only kept Takahime's evil plot within her own heart, the world would be at peace. However, it was truly pitiful that Takahime-sama was being incited by a demon to harbor such a heart. She was firmly resolved that she must somehow make her repent at this time.

Uzuhiko: 'This, this... for Takahime-sama to say such things, what are you talking about? How could I endure being apologized to by you? It makes me feel quite ticklish. Everything happened because we were lacking. I beg that you will continue to scold us well in the future.'

'No, no, it is I who am at fault. I suffer from Shinkeibiyō, and as I Tokidoki commit Datsusen, I am always causing Gomeiwaku to Hitosama; thus, I am truly sorry both to the Kamisama and to you all. It is not a matter for me to come and invite you so brazenly, but I have come, wearing a mask and trembling with fear. Regarding this, as a token of my apology and as a joy to wish for Jitsukon with you, I wish to offer you Tesei Gohan and sake. It is not a very far place, so would you please come? No matter what is said, you are all Goshinsetsu people, so I have come here firmly believing that you would at least come to my Ima.'

'Hey, how could that be? It would not do for me to receive Gochisō from you. In fact, it is I who wish to Sashia to you.'

'Please do not Otsushiya like that and listen to my request. Is it that I am truly not to your liking? If so, then there is nothing to be done. I have no choice but to pierce my throat and die.'

She squeezed out Soranamida again in a Kaumeu manner.

Shizuko: 'Look, Uzuhiko-sama, since she is speaking so Shinsetsu, how about we accept her Sewa?'

'Hmm, that is true. It would be Setsukaku Oboshimeshi to refuse, and it would be rather disrespectful, so perhaps I shall rely on your Kotoba and visit.'

'Father, Mother, if you are going to Takahime-sama's place to receive sake and Gohan, please take the Shintan with you. It is the one that Monju Bosatsu-sama gave us for the Nidome, is it not? If we take that, no matter what poison is inside, it will all disappear. Takahime-sama, you would not be putting any poison in this time, would you? Even if you did, since we have the Shintan, it would not matter at all, though.'

She pretended to be an innocent child on purpose, attempting to crush the courage of Takahime.

Uzuhiko: 'Look, what kind of rude things are you saying? What reason would Takahime-sama have to do such a thing? You have been having dreams.'

'It is fine to just dismiss everything as a dream, is it not? Hatsuwakahime-sama, you must have said the same, did you not? However, I refuse to go to the place of Giri-Tenjō-sama and be treated to even a cup of tea.'

Shizuko: 'Look, Kaede, that is why people say you are a problem. Truly, truly, there is no helping you. Please try to take the dirt from under the fingernails of Hatsuwakahime-sama and drink it.'

Takahime smiled broadly on purpose, hiding the surprise in her heart with a casual air, and suddenly forced a laugh,

'Ho ho ho ho, even though you are young, you think that what you see in your dreams is reality, do you not? What a truly cute and honest Kaede-sama you are. Now, Kaede-sama, please do come along with everyone.'

'In that case, Aunt-sama, let us go. Hatsuwakahime-nesama will be coming with us too, will she not?'

'Since Hatsuwaka-sama, whom you like, is also coming, please do sit at the table together and enjoy this old woman's heart. I shall partake with you as well.'

'Everyone, since Mother is being so Shinsetsu as to go that far, come, let us go.'

She urged them. The three members of the family, as if given confirmation by the words of Hatsuwakahime, felt at ease and decided to line up in the Ima of Takahime.

Takahime, while fawning and presenting every kind of charm, thought in her heart,

'The time for my desire to be fulfilled has finally come. If I miss this moment, there will never be another good opportunity.'

Thinking this, she did not let others handle the food, and saying that she would show her true heart today, she was bustling about the kitchen all alone, which was suspicious.

Takahime finally prepared the food for five people, finished warming the sake, and placed a cup smeared with the blood of a dragon on each of the four trays.

'Mother, this cup, colored like the blood of a dragon, is truly a delight to the spirit. It tastes as if the sake were brewed from a jar sealed with a hundred insects.'

As she spoke, she peered for a moment at Takahime's face. Startled by Hatsuwakahime's words, Takahime dropped the ceramic sake decanter to the floor with a thud. The ceramic decanter was instantly ordered to commit setsupuku, and it spilled out all the sake it had been holding to its full capacity.

'Mother, what a terrible thing you have shown us. What kind of ritual is this?'

'This, you see, is an ancient ritual to show a sincere heart—to prove that there is nothing in Takahime's belly, and that it is as clear and pure as this unadulterated sake.'

Hatsuwakahime feigned ignorance, and with an impressed look on her face, said,

'How well-versed you are in the ancient traditions, Mother. Please, do pour a cup for me.'

She thrust the cup forward intentionally. Takahime, relieved by Hatsuwakahime's casual words, smoothed her chest in relief, put on a smile, and said,

'Ah, very well, very well. I shall pour for you, Hatsuwaka-yama. Now, bring out your cup.'

Hatsuwakahime had the cup filled with sake, drank it down with a gulp, and showed it to them. Then, she passed the cup to all the guests and finished off the feast, which had been protected by charms against poison, without any reserve or hesitation. Then, Uzuhiko took his wife and children, offered his deep thanks, and returned to his mansion. Since Takahime told her, 'Go and play with Kaede-sama at your leisure,' Hatsuwakahime took this as a blessing, went to Uzuhiko's mansion, and with an innocent face, spent her time talking about various matters of the Way.

Takahime watched the four of them leave, and after they were gone, she shut the sliding doors and spoke to herself:

'Ah, at last, I have seen the dawn of my desire fulfilled. The soul of Tokoyohime is truly formidable, is it not? If one does as I have done, their wisdom naturally dulls, their body withers, and their popularity falls—it is plain to see. Ah, how pleasant this is. Ah, from today on, this Tokoyohime can sleep with her pillow raised high. Ah, Kamunagara Tamachi Haeyamase.
