Chapter 13: 盲嫌 [1307]

盲嫌〔1307〕

§ 1

In the living room of Hatsuwakahime, Hatsuwakahime and Kahedehime are sitting with a round hibachi placed between them, and a conversation begins in gentle voices.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Kahede-sama, you may feel angry, but to endure that is what is called being a yūshiya. It is said that anyone can endure what is easy to endure, but to endure what is hard to endure is true endurance, and there is no virtue greater than patience. All things in this world are settled in peace through patience, and conversely, disturbances arise through a lack of patience. The character for "endure" is written with the character for "heart" beneath the character for "blade," is it not? To swallow a blade in one's chest—such bitterness and regret—to be able to withstand this is what it means to endure. Kokusōōkami-sama created this vast world and allowed many shinjin to live in peace, and just when one might think it is time for a rest, due to Kinmaukiubi and other akugami, Kuniharutachinomikoto was, on the contrary, reviled even by Yahoyorozu-no-Kamisama as an akugami and a cursed god, and was burdened with the chikura-okido, suffering hardships beyond description; yet, despite all that bitterness and regret, He did not utter a single word of complaint. The living god of the Saien-no-Yakata, Susanoo-no-Mikoto-sama, was also falsely accused of crimes beyond description, had the hairs of His head plucked out one by one, His fingernails and toenails torn off, His beard cut, and on top of that, His noble self was banished from Takamagahara as a wanderer; yet, He did not hold a grudge in the slightest, but took the sins of all the people under heaven upon Himself, manifested as the Mizu-no-Mitama, and now, with the intention of guiding misguided humans toward the path of virtue and saving them so that they may enjoy the life of the heavenly kingdom forever and ever without letting even one person fall into the abyss, He has opened the San-Ikkyō to the world, and I, too, am serving as His hands and feet to propagate it throughout the world. You are also the daughter of Tanehiko-sama, who serves this noble Ōkami, and since you are a pure and noble believer of the San-Ikkyō, no matter how unreasonable the demands Takahime-sama may make, you must not resent her even for a word. If a human being gets angry even a little, demons will take advantage of that opening to destroy their spirit, and eventually destroy their physical body as well, so there is nothing as frightening or as detrimental as getting angry.'

Kahedehime: 'Hai, thank you very much. I, too, have been told by my father that patience is the most necessary thing, that patience is the foundation of all success, and that it is the basis of character; but as I am an immature woman, waves of emotion suddenly rose in the sea of my heart, and as a woman who should be obedient, I uttered violent words toward Takahime-sama. Now that I think about it, I am truly ashamed. The Kamisama must surely hate my selfishness and stubbornness.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'No, no, please do not worry at all. If you realize that and cultivate the virtue of patience from now on, the Kamisama will certainly not blame you; on the contrary, He will be very pleased, and will cast the light of love upon both your spirit and your body, making you the purest, most beautiful, and greatest being in this world.'

Kahedehime: 'Your various teachings sink deep into my body, and tears of joy are spilling out before I know it. Now, Hatsuwakahime-sama, will Takahime-sama reform her current evil heart? If not, we cannot help but worry about the situation of our parents.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'That is quite understandable. It is truly a touching sentiment that you, as a daughter, are so concerned about your parents. However, I am sorry to say that Takahime-sama has various evil spirits swarming within her body, so it is beyond our power alone. That said, I do not wish to let Takahime-sama fall into the path of evil. We must make her a fine person through benevolence and patience, for otherwise, we who serve the San-Ikkyō cannot fulfill our duty to the Kamisama.'

'How might we be able to save Takahime-sama?'

'Beyond this, there is no other way but to borrow the divine power of the Lord God. And no matter what is said, when we face her with sincerity, kindness, true intent, and patience, she will surely become a good person through the grace of the Lord God. It is as if we have been given a Shikenmondai by the Lord God. If I cannot make Takahime-sama repent, I cannot go about calling myself a Sendenshi.'

'You are truly doing a great Gokurausama. I, too, shall henceforth make patience my priority and treat Takahime-sama with the same respect and love as my own Chichihaha.'

'Ah, you have said it so well. Arigata-gozaimasu. Now, Kaede-sama, since your father and mother must be waiting for you, I am sorry to seem as if I am driving you away, but please return for now and come again to visit. Since Takahime-sama was just dragged away so smartly and is in a state of great Giyakujiyō, if she were to see your face, her usual Biyōki might recur again.'

'If that is the case, I shall take my leave. You and I shall join our hearts to, well, Takahime-sama...'

Before the words were finished, Takahime kicked open the sliding door and burst in with the force of a Yasha, her expression of anger terrifying. She glared sharply at the two of them, and while trembling with voice, said:

'They say there is nothing that remains hidden and does not eventually appear. What were you two consulting about? You were saying just now, were you not, that you and I would join our hearts to target Takahime, and so on? If you are plotting such evil, it will surely be revealed by divine punishment. Even if you think no one knows, Heaven knows, Earth knows, others know, and I know. Now, you two, I can no longer forgive you. What were you plotting? Now, confess clearly. You, Kaede, you tricked this Giritenjō no Ikimiya, grabbed my leg from behind, threw me into the garden, and caused me to scrape my shin and bleed from my knee, did you not? Now, what are you going to do about it? I will not forgive you anymore.'

'I have truly done a terrible thing. Please, I beg you to forgive me. I was in the wrong.'

'Hmph, you say that so easily. "I was in the wrong," what does that even mean? If things could be settled just by saying you were wrong, the world would be full of evil deeds. A word for a word, what one receives must be returned. Since you scraped my shin, which is so precious that not even an ant should be allowed to touch it, I cannot let this pass and have my sense of duty satisfied. I will now strike you as much as I please in return, so show me your shin. In this world, duty is important. This Giritenjō will give you a full return of favors.'

She shouted this with a trembling, angry voice, mixed with tears.

'Oh, Mother, please, I beg you to forgive her. She is still young and does not know better; I shall also advise her to repent and, by the grace of Kaminaohi and Ōnaohi, to be careful from now on.'

'Hmph, you, Hatsuwaka, how can you say such transparent things? Were you not just now with Kaede, saying that the two of you would join your hearts to target this Takahime? This Giritenjō is not so easily deceived by such things. Did you not say that you turned away that evil dog by listening to the orders of Mokusuke-sama? Furthermore, you must have hidden me somewhere, and it was your plot that caused me to suffer such a terrible experience and be dragged to the depths of the forest. If Iru, Iku, Saru, Haru, and Teru had not come for me, I would have been killed. You are a devil, a great villain who wears a beautiful face but hides needles in your heart. From today, I dismiss you. Ugh, how filthy; do not call me Mother. You are the mastermind of a murder, a demon girl; this Giritenjō will not tolerate you any longer.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Mother-sama, please do not be so Muiki in your anger. Dōzo, listen to me Hitotoho.'

Takahime stuck out her tongue with a jerk, made a red face, jerked her chin three or four times, wore a Reishō, and with a mocking Kuchiburi, said:

Takahime: 'Hmph, so that is what you Otsushiya. You are quite a Nakanaka old tanuki. Ever since you came here, I thought you were no mere tanuki. But thinking you were the daughter of my beloved, beloved Mokusuke-sama, I have treated you with Kaai until now, and you have become Zōchiyō; to plot such an outrageous thing, are you not a Gongodaudan Daiakutau? If you speak with such Shinsetsu, Naze did you not come to save me when I was being dragged by that Inuchikushiyō? Even if we speak only in terms of Meigijiyō, are you not my daughter? If you had even a little bit of sincerity, you should understand Girininjiyō. Even a naughty child like Iru, Iku, or Saru came to save me in a moment of crisis, did they not? And what is this Nanigoto? You dragged the Ama named Kaede, who caused me such an Ōkega, into your own Heya, and were plotting a grand Inbō to do such-and-such to me while acting so Kiraku, were you not? Eh, if I dawdle, I might be done in by you Mahetachi; he who strikes first controls the others. Kakugo nasare.'

As she said this, she swung her club, and at the very moment she was about to strike Hatsuwakahime, Smart suddenly rushed in, let out a single 'Woof,' caught the hem of Takahime's garment, and pulled her down backward once again. Hatsuwakahime turned to Smart and said:

Hatsuwakahime: 'Here, you, what kind of violent thing are you doing? Will you not let go quickly?'

She chided him. Smart was trembling as if in a fit of rage. However, finding it difficult to disobey his master's command, he obediently let go of the hem with a snap. Takahime stood up with a jerk and struck Smart on the head with a palm broom with a thud. As Smart was about to jump at her in anger, Hatsuwakahime gave a single command, 'Here,' and Smart, looking disappointed, hung his head.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Here, Smart, you must never threaten Mother-sama. However, since you are a clever dog, it was great that you did not bite her anywhere, even though you dragged Mother-sama along.'

She stroked his head and neck, soothing Smart's heart. Takahime raised her voice in anger:

Takahime: 'Eh, you filthy Hatsu, you Ama named Kaede, take that beast and get out of here. If you dawdle, Giritenjō will be desperate. I do not know what I might do.'

She was in a state of almost total madness. Thinking that making her too angry would be a pity, Hatsuwakahime spoke gently, placed both hands on the floor, and said:

Hatsuwakahime: 'Mother-sama, I am terribly sorry. If that is the case, I shall follow your command and wait over there, so if you have any need for me, Dōzo, please clap your hands.'

Takahime: 'Hmph, I am not the sort to eat such flattering words. Go, go, hurry off to Uzuhiko-sama's place or somewhere, and hold a consultation meeting on the conquest of Takahime to your heart's content. Shoo, shoo, shoo, you beast.'

Saying this, she spat from her toothless mouth, swung her broom, and tried to sweep them out.

Hatsuwakahime and Kaede, together with Smart, said:

Hatsuwakahime and Kaede: 'Please excuse us.'

They said this, left the place in a hurry, and headed toward the mansion of Uzuhiko.

Afterward, Takahime gritted her teeth in frustration and, raising a savage voice like that of a tiger or wolf, cried out:

Takahime: 'Eh, how regrettable, how vexing. Caught in the schemes of evil gods, the essential Mokusuke-sama has been injured and gone off somewhere, and while this Giritenjō is called a living shrine of God, because of Uzuhiko's sorcery, my eyes have been poked, my waist has been sprained, and on top of that, I have been thrown at an Ama-tsuchiyo like that Kaede, and dragged by a beast; truly, can one endure this in silence?'

と云ひ捨て、匆々に此場を辞し珍彦の館をさして出でて行く。

『エー、ザ残念や、口惜しやな。悪神共の計略にかかり、肝腎の杢助様は怪我を遊ばし何処かへお出でになり、此義理天上は神の生宮と云ひながら、珍彦の魔法使のために目をこつかれ、腰を挫かれ、其上あんな楓の様な阿魔ツチヨに投げつけられ、ドン畜生には引きずられ、実に之が黙つて辛抱が出来ようか』

§ 4

Saying this, she threw the Hibachi, pulled out the trays, bowls, and dishes from the Todana, threw them out into the Koguwai, and having performed a Kaguramahi without Ikan, accompanied by the sounds of 'Gatan-bishan, gachiya-gachiya,' she sat down heavily in the Mannaka of the Zashiki, shook her head in Jiyōge and Saiu, and sobbed.

Suddenly, from within the Fukuchiu of Takahime, a voice laughed of its own accord:

Voice: 'Watsuhahahaha, I am the Shiyugojin of Hatsuwakahime. I have entered at the request of Hatsuwakahime to destroy this Nikutai. The other one is the Shiyugojin of Kaede. How Kogimi it is, ufufufufu.'

However, this voice was by no means the Shiyugojin who had entered at the request of Hatsuwakahime, nor was it the Shiyugojin of Kaede. It was the voice of the Akuko that was Izen lurking within the Tainai of Takahime.

Yet, because Takahime believed that both of them had Shinnifu into her Nikutai to destroy it, watching for an opening, she could not bear it.

From that moment, she felt such hatred for the two, like Dakatsu, and Ketsushin that if she ever found an opening, she would punish them.

Whenever an Akurei intends to harm or torment a person, it adopts such a Shiyudan.

For instance, there are many among the demons who, intending to destroy the Ōmotokyō, quietly enter the Nikutai of a person with some Shiyakuwaiteki influence, whisper slander against the Ōmotokyō from within, and attempt to destroy it through the hands of such people.

Furthermore, lesser demons enter the Nikutai of the sick and babble, 'I have been sent by Ōmoto to take your life,' plotting to ruin its reputation.

Such Akuma appear frequently in every age.

Therefore, those who are officials or believers must be fully conversant with the news of the spirit world and must not be led astray by their deceitful words.

Now, Takahime, while striking her stomach with her fists as usual, raised her voice in anger like a madwoman:

Takahime: 'Hey, you ringleaders of evil gods, Hatsuwakahime and the living spirit of Kaede-hime, what do you think you are doing? The soul of Toyohime, who would die for such things, is not the living palace of the righteous Hinodewake. Come, you had better confess the details of your plot one by one; if you do not, I have my own intentions. Well, answer me!'

From within her stomach, the voice replied:

Voice: 'Yes, I have truly done a terrible thing. I am the living spirit of Hatsuwakahime. And the other one is the living spirit of Kaede-hime. We both joined our powers and entered, plotting to destroy the living palace of Hinodewake. However, because your divine virtue is so strong, we can do nothing. Oh, it is painful, it is painful, please forgive us, please forgive us.'

Thus, it apologized from within the stomach, using the voices of Hatsuwakahime and Kaede-hime.

However, in reality, it was still the words of the original fierce spirit, and that fierce spirit, fearing the majesty of Hatsuwakahime, plotted to make them fight with the Nikutai of Takahime, drive both of them out, and dwell in the Nikutai of Takahime at its leisure to achieve its own purpose.

Upon hearing this, Takahime shouted:

Takahime: 'Yes, very well, you four-legged soul of evil and lawlessness, I will not forgive you!'

With these words, she tucked a club under her arm and, with a ferocity like that of the raging King of Ashura, spitting the heat of flames and with her hair standing on end, she headed toward the mansion of Mezuhiko.

Hatsuwakahime and Kaede were talking quietly.

Takahime appeared there and shouted:

Takahime: 'You four-legged creature of evil and lawlessness, know your place!'

Just as she raised her oak club to strike, Smart came running through the air from nowhere, and with his great strength, he knocked Takahime down to the ground with a thud.

Seeing this fierce dog, Takahime was terrified, shrank back, fled to her own living room, braced the sliding doors from the inside, and sat trembling, covered in her bedding.

Smart chased after Takahime, scratched at the outside of the door, and growled incessantly:

Smart: 'Woo-woo, woo-woo, wow-wow-wow.'

Both Takahime and the Akurei within her body shrank at this sound, becoming small and trembling, huddled together like a chestnut left on a branch.

『ワツハハハハハ、俺は初稚姫の守護神だ。初稚姫に頼まれて此肉体を亡ぼすべく入り込んだのだぞよ。もう一人は楓の守護神だ。何と小気味のよいことだわいのう、ウフフフフフ』

とひとりでに笑ひ出した。然し此声は決して初稚姫に頼まれて這入つた守護神でもない、又楓の守護神でもなかつた。依然として高姫の体内に潜居してゐる悪狐の声である。されど高姫は全く両人が高姫の肉体を亡ぼすべく、吾肉体に隙を窺うて侵入して来たものと思うてゐるから堪らない。これから両人を憎む事蛇蝎の如く、隙さへあれば両人を懲してやらねばおかぬと決心したのである。凡て悪霊が人を傷つけ又人を苦しましめむとする時は、右の如き手段を採るものである。例へば大本教を破壊せむとする悪霊は、或社会的勢力を有する人間の体内にソツと入り、内部より大本教に対する悪口を囁き、之等の手によつて破壊せむとするものも魔の中には沢山あるのである。又稍小なる魔に至つては病人の体に入り込み「此方は大本から頼まれて其方の命を取りに来たものだ」等と口走り、名を悪くせむと企むものである。斯の如き悪魔は何時の世にも頻々として現はれ来るものである。故に役員たり信者たりするものは、充分に霊界の消息に通じ、彼等の詐言に迷はされてはならぬのである。

と喚き立てる。腹中より、

『はい、真に済まない事を致しました。私は初稚姫の生霊で厶ります。そしても一人は楓姫の生霊で厶ります。どうかして吾々二人が力を協せ、日出神の生宮を亡ぼしてやらうと企んで這入りました。併しながら貴女の御神徳があまり強いので、如何する事も出来ませぬ。ああ苦しい苦しい許して下さいませな許して下さいませな』

と初稚姫、楓姫の声色を使つて腹の中から詫び出した。然し其実は依然として、もとの兇霊の言葉であり、其兇霊が初稚姫の威光に畏れ、何とかして高姫の肉体と喧嘩をさせ、ここを両人とも追ひ出し、悠々閑々として高姫の体内に棲み、わが目的を達せむと企んだのである。高姫は之を聞くより、

と喚きながら棍棒を小脇に掻い込み、珍彦の館をさして阿修羅王の荒れたる如き勢凄じく、火焔の熱を吐きながら頭髪を逆立て進み行く。初稚姫、楓の二人はヒソビソと話をしてゐた。そこへ高姫は現はれ来り、
