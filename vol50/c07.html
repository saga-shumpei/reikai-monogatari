§ 1

Hatsuwakahime walked quietly and visited the mansion of Uzuhiko, gently opening the door at the entrance.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Gomen nasaimashi, I am Hatsuwakahime. While on my way to the capital of Haruna as a Sendenshi, I paid Sanpai to the Ōkamisama and have been under the care of Takahime-sama, so I am staying here for a while; I humbly ask for your Gojitsukon.'

Surprised by this voice, Kahedehime appeared by gently sliding open the fusuma, and while bowing with Teinei, she set down a paulownia Hibachi and spread out a Zabuton.

Kahedehime: 'Were you the Sendenshi Hatsuwakahime of such Geumei? Oh my, thank you so much for visiting. In truth, having heard that you had come, I had wished to have the Haigan of you once, but my Riyōshin said, "A Busahafumono without Kyōiku like you should not appear before a person like an angel; it would be a Goburei, so stay back," and so I have been Shitsurei. Oh my, you have visited me with your noble Onmi. Please, do come up here. I shall serve you some tea.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Hai, thank you for your Goshinsetsu. I have been under your care in many ways. By the way, where have Uzuhiko-sama and Shizuko-sama gone? It seems I cannot see their faces.'

Kahedehime: 'Hai, my Riyōshin went out just a moment ago, saying they were going to offer thanks to the gods. They are likely visiting the shrine of the gods.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'They must have gone to offer thanks for the Shintan.'

Kahedehime was startled by these words and, looking up at Hatsuwakahime's face, her hands trembled slightly.

Kahedehime: 'My, how do you know such detailed matters?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Hai, unable to watch the crisis of the married couple, I created a spiritual medicine called Shintan by the revelation of Kototama-wake-no-mikoto-sama, and I had intended for Smart to carry it and deliver it into your hands.'

Kahedehime: 'Ah, was that so? If that is the case, are you Monju Bosatsu-sama? Ah, how noble, how Arigata.'

She choked with tears of gratitude. Before anyone knew it, Smart had crawled under the floor and appeared here, wagging his tail.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Now Smart, you must not wander about carelessly. How did you come here? You are truly a spiritual beast; you listened well to what I said and saved the couple from their crisis. You are truly as agile as your name, Smart, implies.'

She was praising him. Smart was wagging his body and tail as if happy. Kahede finally raised her face, saw Smart's figure, and was startled a second time.

Kahedehime: 'Ah, the dog that Monju Bosatsu-sama brought last night is this one, is it not? From this dog's mouth, three grains of Shintan were delivered to my hand. And when I thought that Monju Bosatsu-sama had bestowed the Shintan upon me and immediately taken the dog and returned somewhere, the dream awakened; when I opened the hand I had been gripping tightly, that Shintan was there. You are truly a living god; it is fearful for me to be kept by your side like this. And there is one thing I cannot understand no matter what: why did you become the child of a person who is not very good, like Takahime-sama?'

Hatsuwakahime smiled and said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Hai, there will come a time when you understand.'

She said only that and did not answer. Kahede pressed her further and asked again.

Kahedehime: 'I have heard that you are the daughter of Mokusuke-sama, is that correct?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Hai, that is so. However, this Mokusuke-sama here...'

She said only that and closed her mouth. Since Kahede was a person of keen intellect, she quickly grasped the intent. Then, she lowered her voice.

'Truly, what a situation; it is safer to say nothing at all, is it not?'

Thus, with an Ishindenshinteki understanding, they finished their exchange of words simply with their eyes.

At this moment, Uzuhiko and his wife returned from the back entrance, having finished their Hairei while leaning on their staff of goosefoot. Sensing their Ashioto early, Kaede opened the door to the back entrance and said in a joyful voice,

'Father, Mother, welcome home. The Kamisama from Yazen has come to visit us. The very Monjiyubosatsusama who bestowed upon us the Shintan.'

She whispered this in a Kogoe.

Uzuhiko: 'What, Monjiyubosatsusama has come here? Then I must Maoshia my thanks Sugusama.'

Shizuko: 'Because the Akuma have been spreading so much, I have not been able to sleep properly at night even while staying in this Seijiyō. Oh, how Arigata, that Monjiyubosatsusama has come to us.'

She was already speaking in a Namidagoe. Following behind Kaede, the Fūfu went up into the Zashiki, bowed their heads before Hatsuwakahime, and while Shiyakurina, they were unable to utter even Ichigon, lost in tears of Kanshiya.

'If I may, Uzuhiko-dono, Shizuko-dono, your daily service to the gods is truly exhausting. I am a messenger of Hatsuwakahime. I have come suddenly and am causing you trouble. Because Kaede-sama spoke so kindly to me, I have ended up staying for a long time.'

She greeted them with a voice as soft as a bell. Uzuhiko raised his head with a start, deeply moved by her appearance, which was filled with the spiritual energy of Hatsuwakahime,

Uzuhiko: 'You are the incarnation of Monjiyubosatsusama; how wonderful that you have come to save us. Now, Shizuko, why do you not offer your thanks quickly?'

Shizuko: 'Hatsuwakahime-sama, incarnation of Monjiyubosatsusama, how wonderful that you have saved us. We shall never forget this kindness.'

She wiped her eyes, which were wet with tears, with a handkerchief.

Hatsuwakahime looked troubled, waving her slender hands from side to side,

Hatsuwakahime: 'No, no, please do not thank me. It puts me in a difficult position. In truth, it was the Kamisama of Kototama who commanded me to do so. Please, offer your thanks to the Lord Ōkami. I do not have the power to save people such as you.'

Uzuhiko: 'What humility you possess, my lady; I am truly moved. By the way, Hatsuwakahime-sama, I heard that you are the daughter of Mokusuke-sama who appeared yesterday; is that so?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'No... yes.'

She gave an indecisive reply.

Kaede turned to her parents,

Kaede: 'Father, Mother, you must not say such rude things. Why, how could such a person be the father of a Hime-sama? There is a deep reason for this. However, please do not say anything more about this from now on. It would be a nuisance to Hime-sama, you see.'

Uzuhiko and his wife nodded at Kaede's words,

Uzuhiko and Shizuko: 'Yes, yes, indeed, indeed, I understand. We are grateful for your service. Please, we beg you to use your divine power to drive away the Akuma.'

The Fūfu pressed their hands together and bowed down once more.

Kaede: 'Since we do not know who might be listening to such talk, shall we sing a song or two in a lighthearted manner?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'That is a good idea, Kaede-sama, please sing one for us.'

Kaede could not refuse the words of Hatsuwakahime... and with the preface that it was embarrassing, she sang,

'Deep in the bottom of the human heart,

When one parts the waves of a thousand fathoms,

In the shade of the rocks where the eyes linger,

Does a hideous crocodile dwell.'

Hatsuwakahime nodded repeatedly and smiled slyly,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Indeed. That was very well done. Shall I also recite a humble verse? Ho ho ho.'

While laughing, she recited,

'Inviting all to the Heavenly Kingdom,

Where the flowers of the trees bloom all at once,

The Mizumitama who bestows,

The eternal joy of the Dharma.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'It is truly embarrassing. Ho ho ho ho.'

She let out a laugh from her small lips, which were like dew on a plum blossom.

珍彦『ハイ有難う、ようまア云つて下さいました』

Coming in a hurry to such a place were the two receptionists, Iru and Haru. Without any Enriyoeshiyaku, they pushed open the door at the Kadoguchi,

Iru: 'Excuse me, Uzuhiko-sama, something terrible has happened. Please come, it is truly terrible.'

'Your words are hurried; what is this terrible thing?'

Haru: 'Yes, it is about Takahime-sama, it is a terrible matter. Please come. We cannot handle it on our own.'

Shizuko: 'Has Takahime-sama perhaps lost her Gokigen and is in a state of Goritsupuku?'

Hatsuwakahime said,

'No, no, it is not like that. While trying to take the Tamatake, she was pecked in the eyes by a Fukurodori, fell from the top of the Ōsugi, and must have bruised her waist bone a little. Please do not worry, she will heal immediately.'

Iru: 'Excuse me, Hatsuwakahime-sama, how can you remain so calm? Is she not your mother by duty? Come, please go quickly. Your father is injured, and your mother has fallen from a tree and is suffering; this is no time for such things.'

'Yes, I will go at once, so please tell that to Mother. Now, Smart, hide yourself somewhere quickly.'

Saying this, Hatsuwakahime left the mansion in a flurry to save Takahime from her danger, and hurried her steps toward the Ōsugi. Takahime, while breathing painfully, said,

'Ah, so it was you, Hatsuwaka, thank you for coming. Mother, just because I wanted to cure Father's illness, I climbed up to get the Tamatake and met with this fate. Father told me to get it without anyone knowing, but because I was so clumsy and was seen by others, it will no longer be effective. Please, do not mind me; he is under the trees in that forest, so please go quickly and tend to him. I have the protection of the Hinodewake, so there is no need for worry. Come, go quickly, I am beside myself with anxiety.'

'How could I go and abandon Mother's danger just because you say so? Once the bond of parent and child has been formed, please do not say such distant things, and let me tend to you; this is the beginning of my filial piety toward you.'

She is applying the very words that Takahime once said to Yōgenbō.

'Hey, will you not listen to what your parent says? I suppose that is how it is. Since you are not a child born of my own womb, you have neither gratitude nor duty. It cannot be helped if you will not listen. I shall give up now.'

'Mother, I am sorry, but I will go to tend to Father. Please do not despise me as an unfilial wretch. Here, Haru-sama, Iru-sama, please, I entrust Mother's care to you. Mother, farewell.'

Saying this, she left the scene. Takahime continues to leak breaths of agony as before.
