§ 1

Takahime heard the sound of footsteps of Hatsuwakahime returning, and with a look of longing, said, 'Hatsuwakahime-sama... is that you?'

Hatsuwakahime replied, 'Yes.'

She slid open the shiyōji, and when she looked, Takahime’s entire ganmen was swollen like a kanpiyō, her saugau changed to the point where it was difficult to hanbetsu where her eyes or nose were, and she looked exactly like an eukuwai. Furthermore, her swollen eyes had tentaku toward her forehead, and her nose protruded without buenriyo out of her reii, looking like a mountain with an irregular sanyō, like a takayama wearing clouds as a belt. Her lips were swollen thick like the hem of a yogi, and her thin hifu, like a ranjiyuku jiyukushi, was unpleasant, shining faintly with a reddish and purple hue.

Hatsuwakahime was startled, and for a moment, no kotoba came out. And she thought in her heart... Ah, what a terrifying face this is; it is just like a kuwaibutsu living in jigoku. Perhaps this is the hatsuro of Takahime-sama’s naitekishiyōgai. Inaina, this is the jijitsu; it is truly fubin.

Whispering to herself, 'I must find a way to help her quickly, but since she is a person of deep sin...', she neatly aligned her wooden clogs facing the entrance, brushed off the dust with a handkerchief with a light tapping motion, and quietly drew near to Takahime’s side, saying in a voice full of sympathy,

'Okā-sama, you have suffered a terrible injury. I will do my utmost to nurse you back to health, so please, I beg of you, be at ease.'

While saying this, she wondered when it had arrived, and stroked the head of Smart. Smart wagged its tail happily and began to circle the tatami room.

Takahime, from the side of her distorted mouth, spoke in a voice mixed with a nasal tone like a broken bellows, 'Yes, thank you for your kindness. And has Mokusuke-sama not returned yet? I wonder if his condition is a little better. It is such a time that both parents have suffered such great injuries at once, so you must be very worried. You are going to great trouble.'

Hatsuwakahime decided that if she told her Mokusuke was not there, Takahime would be greatly disappointed and discouraged, so taking advantage of the fact that she could not see, she must say something to put her mind at ease, and said,

'Yes, Otō-sama was resting by the side of the mountain stream, but when I went there... and tried to call out, "Otō-sama, Otō-sama...", he sat up with a start, and with a face that looked quite cheerful, said, "Ah, are you Hatsuwakahime? How good of you to come. I have suffered an unexpected injury, and it is truly shameful as a parent to have you see such an unsightly face. 'Ah, is that so? That is more wonderful than anything. Mokusuke-sama also has too strong a will, so perhaps the Lord God caused him to be injured as a warning. Since he will surely become a little gentler because of this, once both husband and wife have fully recovered, we will be able to devote ourselves even more to the divine work and live in harmony within the family. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese.'

'Okā-sama, how is your condition? It must be bothering your spirits.'

'Nani, it is not a matter of my spirits being bad due to an injury like this, but I feel quite inconvenienced because I cannot see well. However, thanks to the Lord God, I feel as though I can see the surroundings faintly. At this rate, I shall surely recover soon.'

'Please, I wish for you to heal as quickly as possible. What a person of bad luck I am. To say nothing of Otō-sama, just as I was rejoicing that I had gained a kind Okā-sama, you have suffered such a severe injury; how can I bear this? I feel as though I cannot live in this world.'

She said this while weeping.
