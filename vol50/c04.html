Chapter 4: 御意犬 [1298]

御意犬〔1298〕

§ 1

After Takahime had left, Hatsuwakahime could not help but burst into laughter, 'Ho ho ho ho,' in a small voice. Then, startled by the sound of her own laughter, she spoke to herself in a whisper:

Hatsuwakahime: 'Poor Takahime-sama. And that Kinmaukiubi, the evil fox, is once again exerting itself in the forest of the shrine, obstructing the Goshingefu at the Iso mansion, and trying to lead many Seirei and Jinmin astray. The Kankitsu and hateful nature of its Yarikata. Although Takahime-sama is a person of Netsushin, she lacks Jiyōshiki, so she is always prone to falling into Kiyōmau, and just like that, she has become a captive of the Akuma. I wish I could help her, but unless she wakes up, there is Tautei no hope for her Fukukuwatsu. It is truly pitiful that she thinks the Shishi and Tora, the Riyōsei Eumi possessing a Nikutai, is her father, Mokusuke. The Kinmaukiubi, the evil fox possessing Takahime-sama, cannot see the manifest world unless it is through Takahime's Nikutai, so she is being deceived by such a monster. Neither the possessing spirit nor Takahime herself knows that it is a monster. Takahime herself acknowledges the existence of the Kyōrei, but from the side of that monster, it is impossible to see the true form of the Kinmaukiubi possessing Takahime. In short, a monster and a monster are engaging in blind, groping, and delusional actions with Takahime-sama's Nikutai between them. However, this is a point of the Lord God's meticulous attention. If the monster disguised as Mokusuke-sama and the evil fox within Takahime's body were to recognize each other's true nature and see each other's form, they would respond to each other from within and without, worsening Takahime's condition further, and there is no telling what kind of poison they would spread throughout the world. Ah, the grateful thoughts of the Lord God.'

She was choked with tears of gratitude as she spoke. Smart rested his head on Hatsuwakahime's lap, as if he understood her Hitorigoto.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Now Smart, you have bad manners; why do you not sit properly?'

Smart, while twitching his ears, did not even attempt to get up.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Ah, that is right, Smart, please bear with it. You have injured your leg, so it is no wonder you cannot sit even if I tell you to. This was my fault; please forgive me.'

She gently stroked his head. Smart wagged his tail happily, as if to express his gratitude.

Smart stood up abruptly, shook his body, and with a fierce expression, stood on his front legs, showing an intensity as if to pounce on something. Then, he growled in a low voice, 'Grrr.' While stroking Smart, Hatsuwakahime said in a gentle voice:

Hatsuwakahime: 'Now Smart, I do not know what has come, but you must never get worked up. Until I give the command, no matter what comes, you must never growl or pounce. I know everything well, but there is a reason for this, so please, I beg you, be quiet.'

When she admonished him, Smart bowed his head and wagged his tail to show his consent.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Truly, though you are a beast, you are wise. You are my retainer. You will follow me wherever I go. And once you have completed the magnificent Goshingefu, you will be reborn as a human again, become a splendid messenger of truth, guide all the people of the world, and serve faithfully so that you may lead a comfortable life in heaven. Truly, you are a strange dog, unlike any other. You are an ideal dog, both brave and obedient.'

She was constantly stroking his head and neck, showing her affection. Smart finally bent his legs, rubbed his chin against the tatami, closed his eyes, and showed an obedient attitude.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Smart, you saw through that monster disguised as Mokusuke, and just a moment ago, you chased after it and fought it, and that is how you got this wound, you are truly a brave Smart.'

Just as they were praising him, the aforementioned Takahime returned quietly.

Takahime: 'Ah, Mother, thank you for your hard work. Was Father there?'

Mother: 'Yes, I managed to meet him after much effort. When Mokusuke-sama was strolling through the forest, he caught his foot on some Fujikazura, struck his brow against a rock, and suffered a terrible injury; he was washing the wound in a mountain stream.'

Upon hearing this, Smart raised his head abruptly once more and began to growl, 'Woo-woo.' Hatsuwakahime hurriedly stroked his head and said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Now, Smart, be quiet. Listen to what your master says.'

While soothing him calmly, she intuited with her sharp mind,

Hatsuwakahime: 'I wonder, did the monster have its brow bitten by this Smart?'

However, she feigned ignorance, and with a gentle tone and a face full of smiles, she said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Oh, Mother, Father has done something truly dangerous. Is it not a serious injury? I am filled with worry. And will he be returning soon?'

Mother: 'It is not a particularly serious injury, but he is a man of very strong will; even when I told him you were here, he would not easily be moved.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'What did Father say about me? He must have been angry, surely.'

Mother: 'Why, Hatsuwaka-sama, is there anyone who would be angry when their own daughter has come to see them? If he were the kind of person to be angry at such a thing, he would not be human.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Even so, my father said most sternly that until the business of the capital of Haruna is finished, he will not meet with me, no matter what happens.'

Mother: 'That is what makes Mokusuke-sama who he is. He is truly a great man. It is said that a mother's love is the love of a cow licking its calf, while a father's love is the love of the autumn frost; there is something in it that cannot be expressed in words. No matter how sternly he speaks, his true heart is filled with tears of immeasurable depth that surpass a mother's love. However, since this Takahime and you are parent and child in spirit, and also parent and child by the obligations of the flesh, the measure of that love is something a birth mother can never reach. Please, open your heart and follow what this mother says.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Yes, I understand. I leave everything to your kind guidance.'

Mother: 'If I may, Hatsuwaka-sama, would you be willing to listen to what I have to say?'

Hatsuwakahime: 'This is a formal request; what could you possibly be asking of someone like me?'

Mother: 'The truth is, I want you to get rid of this dog that you are so fond of. Mokusuke-sama dislikes dogs intensely, and he says most firmly, "It is not permitted to bring such a four-legged creature into this sacred mansion. It will defile the holy ground, so get rid of it; otherwise, I shall not stay here."'

Hatsuwakahime, sensing the truth behind this, feigned ignorance and said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'How strange that is. My father is particularly fond of dogs; even at the mansion of the Saien, he never once sent the dogs away. And is it not strange that he would have a sudden change of heart? Could it be that he is so afraid, or rather, dislikes this dog?'

Mother: 'No matter what you say, this is a place of sacred divine work. If you keep a defiled four-legged creature here, it will offend the Lord God. That is why Mokusuke said to get rid of the dog, to express his reverence toward the Gods.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Yet, at the sacred ground of Ubusunayama, many dogs are kept. Even at the Honzan, there are so many dogs, so why is it that not even one can be kept here? This dog is my only friend and my strength.'

Mother: 'Hatsuwaka-sama, are you saying you do not wish to part with this dog? The reason there are many dogs at Ubusunayama is that the spirits of Susanoo-no-Mikoto, Yashimanu-sama, Tōsuke, and the other officials are clouded; that is why four-legged creatures roam about in such a magnificent sacred place. Furthermore, dogs are kept there to prevent the spirits of foxes and badgers from entering. If one possesses divine virtue, one does not need to borrow the power of dogs; the spirits of foxes, badgers, great serpents, and toads will not appear. Does the fact that dogs are kept at Ubusunayama not show that there is no divine virtue there?'

Hatsuwakahime, while being amazed at the Baugen of Takahime, feigned ignorance in a gentle manner and replied,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Is that so? The Goshintoku is indeed a precious thing.'

Takahime: 'As expected of Mokusuke-sama's daughter, you have good understanding in everything. Truly, you have a Suishiyō Mitama. Since you have understood the matter so quickly, you will send this dog back to the Iso mansion, will you not?'

Smart, looking as if he could not bear the anxiety upon hearing the Mondafu of the two, began to growl softly again, 'Woo-woo-woo.' Hatsuwakahime stroked Smart's head, leaned her mouth to his Mimimoto, and whispered something in a Kogoe. Smart obediently lowered his head and closed his eyes.

Takahime: 'Now, since it is your father's instruction, please send it away quickly. That is the greatest Kaukau toward your parents. And since it is the Ōmichi of Ananahi toward the Kamisama, you will surely return it obediently, will you not?'

Hatsuwakahime nodded and replied,

Hatsuwakahime: 'How could I disobey the Kotoba of my mother and father? Although Smart is of the greatest Daiji, if it is what my Goriyōshin wish, I cannot disobey. Ah, regrettably, I must part with Smart.'

Takahime: 'What a Jiujiyun and good daughter you are. Truly, I wonder why Mokusuke-sama was blessed with such a splendid daughter. It is no wonder that Yashimanu-sama made you a messenger of propaganda.'

Takahime was praising Hatsuwakahime excessively.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Mother, is there anything worth praising so much just for sending away a dog? Please do not trouble yourself. However, even though it is a beast, since I went to the trouble of bringing it this far, I will send it off to the gate or about one or two chō away, and there I will speak to it earnestly and instruct it not to return here again.'

Takahime: 'Ho-ho-ho, what a polite child you are. You could just drive it out from the gate, but to send it off yourself as if you were its master—is that not a bit too much for your station? Hey, you beast, you are blessed by the gods. To be sent off by my daughter—you are a lucky one. However, I, Takahime, have also felt that this dog is somehow frightening—no, no, I have felt a sense of dislike and fear toward it. Ah, now I am finally relieved. The golden-haired nine-tailed fox is also...'

Takahime started to say this, then suddenly clamped her mouth shut, rubbed her own stomach vigorously, and muttered to herself,

Takahime: 'Hey, be careful.'

She was reprimanding herself. Hatsuwakahime again feigned ignorance and asked,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Mother, you said "be careful," but is there something amiss? As I am still lacking, please give me your guidance.'

Takahime: 'Eh, no, it is nothing at all, just a fleeting thought... I said that because this sacred ground is a holy place in many respects, one must be careful in all things.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Yes, thank you very much. I will go and send the dog just that far. Mother, please wait here. Father will likely return soon as well.'

Leaving these words behind, she accompanied Smart and proceeded beyond the slope for about one or two chō. She led Smart to a point where the mansion could no longer be seen, hidden by the mountain's skirt. While stroking his head, neck, and back, she said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Now, Smart, you are a great one. The monster disguised as Mokusuke and the evil fox lurking within Takahime's body are very afraid of you. That is why the two of them are pressing me to "send you away." However, since I have formed a bond of master and servant with you, I hate to be separated from you even for half an hour. You feel the same, do you not? But in the current situation, there is nothing else I can do, so once you pretend to have returned, please hide quietly under the floor of my living room when the sun sets. I will secretly bring you food so that you do not go hungry.'

As she exhorted him earnestly, Smart wagged his tail and nodded his head several times, showing in his expression the spirit of saying, 'I understand everything.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Did you understand? Ah, I am relieved now. Please be sure not to show your figure while I am here. However, at night, we shall play while embracing, my dear Smart. Since you are a female dog, I do not mind embracing or kissing you, ho-ho-ho.'

Laughing, she parted from him and returned to the mansion. Smart waited impatiently for the sun to set, then quietly hid himself under the floor of Hatsuwakahime's living room, faithfully keeping his master's instructions and taking on the duty of protecting Hatsuwakahime.

『ハイ有難う厶います。一寸其処まで犬を送つて参ります。お母さま、此処に待つて居て下さい。やがてお父さまも帰つて下さるでせう』

と言葉を残し、スマートを伴ひ一二町ばかり坂の彼方に進み、山の裾に隠れて館の見えない地点までスマートを伴ひ往き、頭や首背を撫でながら、

『これスマートや、お前は偉いものだなア、杢助に化けて居る怪物や、高姫の身内に潜んで居る悪い狐がお前を大変怖がつて居る。それだからあのやうに二人が私に「お前をいなして呉れ」と迫るのだよ。併し私はお前と主従の約束を結んだ以上は、仮令半時の間だつて離れるのは嫌だよ。お前だつてさうだらう。併し今の場合どうする訳にも往かないから、一旦お前は帰んだ事にして、日が暮れたらそつと私の居間の床下に隠れて居て下さい。御飯をソツとお前の腹の減らないやうに上げるから』

と懇々と諭せば、スマートは尾をふり首を数回も縦にゆりながら「万事承知致しました」と云ふ心意気を表情に示して居る。

『分つたかな、アアそれで私も安心した。きつと私が此処に居る間は姿を見せないやうにして下さい。併し又、夜分には恋しいお前さまと抱合つて遊びませう。お前さまは雌犬だから、私と抱擁したつてキツスをしたつて、構ひはしないわネー、ホホホ』

と笑ひながら別れて館に帰つて来た。スマートは日の暮るるを待ち兼ね、ソツと初稚姫の居間の床下に身を忍ばせ、主人の言ひつけをよく守り、且初稚姫の保護の任に当る事となつた。
