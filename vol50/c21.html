§ 1

Iku: 'Come now, come now, come. The enemy has already fled. If we let this one escape, it is a grave matter. We must catch up quickly, grab the two of them on the way, throw them into the flow of Kajika, and we must eradicate the obstruction of the San-go-kyo. You and I have been serving the teachings of the Brahman Kami, and we have done evil things without regret until now. Untokodottsukoi, once we have repented to atone for those deep sins and to spend our afterlife in the heavenly realm, we must achieve some merit.'

Iku: 'We must stand before the presence of the Kami. Otherwise, we will have no souvenir for the Kami of the Saien mansion. When I look back, what is that fellow Sāru doing? The more you come, the more you lag behind. Truly, you are yattokoshiyo. Dottsukoi dottsukoi, it is tiresome. Why are your legs so slow again? Aitatatatsuta, pattariko. At last, I hit my shin. When I think of this, is it that the Kami-sama is telling me not to pursue? No, no, that cannot be so. Once I have made up my mind, I will pursue them no matter how far, witness their end, grab their throats firmly, and from now on, Takahime will repent and self-destruct...'

My heart cannot help but speak.

Though my mind is scattered in a thousand ways,

My shins, the Kanjinkaname,

Because I struck them so hard,

Though my heart is in a rush,

My body somehow will not move.

He should have caught up by now,

Stop the pain in my legs quickly.

With both hands pressed together, this Iku,

Humbly prays to You.

Thus, while collapsed on the mountain path, he continues to sing. It was Saru who came walking toward this spot with unsteady footsteps. Saru was startled to see Iku collapsed with blood flowing from his leg, and he let out a tonkiyō cry:

Saru: 'Hey, aren't you Iku? What happened? What happened?'

Iku: 'What do you mean, what happened? Because you were dawdling so much, I kept looking back and running, thinking, "Won't you hurry up?" and that is why I tripped on a big stone and fell. Oi, Saru, don't worry about me and run ahead quickly. If you keep dawdling, those two will disappear once the sun sets. I will follow after you as soon as my leg recovers.'

Saru: 'Even if you say that, how could I possibly abandon you when you have such an injury? This is a place where many wolves appear, and since the sun will set soon, it is unbearably dangerous. Don't say such things and let me tend to you. My, what a terrible injury this is.'

Iku: 'I don't care about myself, but if you don't go quickly, won't you lose them? Which is more important, me or the path? Think about it well. What does it matter if I lose one leg? If it is gone, I can just attach a prosthetic leg. Now, go quickly, go!'

Saru: 'No matter what, as a friend, I cannot bear to abandon you here and leave. Your complexion is looking quite bad.'

Iku: 'Ah, you are such a coward. Are you not saying such things because you are afraid of those two?'

Saru: 'Well, that is true. If I were going with you, I would feel strong, but even if I chase after such a monster and an old hag, if I am alone, I will be the one defeated instead. In truth, I intended to send you ahead and follow to support you. If the essential Iku has collapsed, even if I go alone, a perfect Iku-Saru team cannot be formed. Since defeat is certain, if it is to be such a defeat, it is far wiser not to go.'

Iku: 'You fail because you rely on others. What does it matter if there are five or ten humans? If we pray to the God of infinite divine power and go, we will surely break their pride and surely accomplish the divine task. Now, go, go! Aitatatata, I feel like I am about to lose my breath. It may be that recovery is hopeless.'

Saru: 'You are a weak-spirited fellow. Why don't you pray to God yourself?'

Iku: 'Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. I have truly been unrefined. However, no matter what happens to me, I do not mind. Please, grant Saru divine power, drive away his cowardice, and I pray that he will summon his courage and charge forward. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara, Kamunagara Kamunagara.'

Just as he was praying, a voice like a cracked bell came from the nearby thicket:

Voice: 'Ahahahaha, look at that spectacle. Caught in Mokusuke's trap, what a state you are in. How pleasant it is, ahahahaha.'

Iku: 'Ya, he is there, he is there. Oi, Saru, grab him! I cannot move because my leg is in such pain.'

Saru: 'My body is also stiffening up and I cannot move. Aaaaa, what shall I do? Aitatatata, something has even started to feel strange in my lower back.'

§ 3

From within the woods,

Eugenbau: 'Uahahaha, this is it, Riyōnin of Iku and Sāru, now Mokusuke shall prepare you both as a rough dish and devour you. What a pitiful sight, indeed. Hey, Takahime, take this oak staff and go split the heads of those two in two.'

Takahime, who appeared abruptly from beneath this voice, stood before the two, and while brandishing the oak staff, said,

Takahime: 'Hey, you two, this Takahime has no desire to strike or kill you, but since I cannot disobey the words of my husband, Mokusukedono, you had best give up and resign yourselves, for your lives end here. Still, like a moth flying into a flame, it has come to pass that I must commit unnecessary slaughter.'

Iku: 'Hey, Takahime, are you trying to kill us by taking advantage of the fact that I have injured my leg? Very well, how interesting. I shall let you kill me. Hey, Sāru, you too, let yourself be killed; we have the Goshiyugo of the noble Ōkamisama, so we are not such fools as to throw away our lives for the sake of Akuma so easily. Come, Takahime, or rather, Eugenbau, do as you will.'

Takahime: 'If you wish to be killed that much, I shall kill you. However, Riyōnin of Iku and Sāru, do you not have a mind to Kaishin and accompany me? Uogokoro means Mizugokoro. Since it is of no use for the Ikimiya of the Giritenjiyō Hinodenokami to kill a couple of Hahimushi like you, how about it, do you have a mind to Kaishin and accompany me? This Kami is one who saves even his enemies.'

Iku: 'Stop your rambling and kill us quickly. Hey, Sāru, you are a coward, so surrender to Eugenbau and Kinmōkyūbi and beg for your life; what a troublesome fellow you are.'

Sāru: 'No, I am a man as well. Hey, Takahime, Mokusuke of Eugenbau, do as you will. I shall bite into your throat and take your life instead. Prepare yourself.'

Takahime: 'Well, there is no helping it once one becomes a fool. If you wish to be killed so much, it is a useless slaughter, but it cannot be helped. How is it, are you prepared?'

She raises the oak staff.

Iku: 'What the hell are you doing? You look like a mantis carrying a straw mat, so come on, do it quickly and finish us off.'

Just as this was happening, making a loud rustling sound, Mokusuke of Eugenbau held a giant rock high above his head with both hands, in a posture ready to hurl it at the two. No matter how brave the two were, if they were to be struck by this rock, there would be no choice but for their bodies to be smashed into smithereens. The two were at their wit's end, closed their eyes in resignation, and prayed to Ōkami with all their might. Suddenly, from their feet, the voice of Sumāto cried out, 'Uuu-uuu, uu-uu, wau-wau-wau,' and Eugenbau, along with Takahime, remained frozen with the rock held high and the staff brandished, then ran off with all their might, and Eugenbau tripped over a rock and fell flat. Takahime tripped again and, while still holding the club aloft, tumbled over with a grunt, and in that moment, she struck the back of Eugenbau's head with the club with a sharp crack. Eugenbau let out a strange cry, yelping twice. It could not be heard as a human voice by any means. A curtain of darkness descended upon the surroundings, making it pitch black, and only the sound of the valley stream could be heard flowing steadily.

Incidentally, Iku's festering wound was completely healed at once along with the voice of Sumāto.
