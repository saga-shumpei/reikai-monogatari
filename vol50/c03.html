Chapter 3: High Demon 腹 [1297]

高魔腹〔1297〕

§ 1

Hatsuwakahime visited the Shinden in the forest of the shrine, offered prayers for protection during her long journey, and returned to the living quarters of Takahime. Takahime followed from a distance, advanced near the Shinden, and as she gazed upon the back of Hatsuwakahime, she was struck with astonishment and clicked her tongue at the perfection of her Shinkaku, which seemed to emanate from nowhere. Takahime was moved by that Shinkaku and, from the bottom of her heart, respected Hatsuwakahime as if she were a Kami. However, she was also driven by a sense of fear from somewhere, and at the same time, a feeling of jealousy began to sprout due to the greatness of that Shinkaku. The Kinmaukiubi Akuko, which had been lurking within the body of Takahime and keeping silent, having been overwhelmed by the great Shinkaku of Hatsuwakahime until now, took advantage of the slight emergence of jealousy in Takahime, entered her void, and suddenly whispered:

Kinmaukiubi Akuko: 'As you may know, I am the Kinmaukiubi Hakumen no Akuko who possessed Tokoyohimenomikoto in the Kamiyo and exhausted every possible sin. However, now that the time has come and the Great Kami of Miroku has descended upon the earth, we cannot continue to commit evil forever. Since I am the ringleader of evil, I know all the schemes of every evil Kami in the world. If I am strong in evil, I am also strong in good. I am the Kinmaukiubi Hakumen no Akuko. And you are the reincarnation of the Mitama of Tokoyohimenomikoto. Now that things have come to this, you must reveal everything and inform the Great Kami of Mizu no Mitama of the evil schemes. Therefore, since that Hatsuwakahime is the reincarnation of Hatsuzakurahimenomikoto, she is by no means a Kami-hito whom you can rival. Rather, since I must repent thoroughly, you should also revere that Hatsuwakahime as your teacher and participate in the Kami-waza together.'

The spirit thus sweetly deceived Takahime. Takahime deeply believed the words of the evil spirit and completely changed her attitude toward Hatsuwakahime. Hatsuwakahime finished her worship at the Shinden, and while descending the stairs, she thought to herself in private:

Hatsuwakahime: 'The spirit of the Kinmaukiubi Akuko has possessed that Takahime. Since that evil spirit possesses a form, if I reveal the truth, it will either destroy her physical body immediately or flee to inhabit another suitable body and continue to confuse the world. It is better that I maintain an attitude of Wako-dojin to the utmost, keep that evil spirit residing in the body of Takahime for a long time, and if it repents from the root, that would be excellent; but even if it does not repent, as long as I keep it hidden within the body of Takahime, there is no fear of that spirit escaping outside. In short, one should view the body of Takahime as a prison that binds an evil spirit that disturbs the world. Since it is a thoroughly evil spirit, if it is illuminated by the light of the Kami, the evil spirit will suddenly become desperate and hinder the Kami-waza all the more. It is better, even if it may go against the divine will, to play the fool for a while and associate with it, and eventually, I shall save both Takahime and the spirit to the heavenly realm.'

With this resolution, she returned to the living quarters of Takahime with an indifferent face while praying to the Great Kami. The spirit, using the tongue of Takahime, spoke with words decorated to sound very gentle:

Takahime: 'Hatsuwakahime-sama, the Mitama of the Hensei-danshi, thank you for your gracious descent. I am Takahime, a missionary of the San-go-kyo, as you know. Due to the circumstances of the Great Kami, the Kinmaukiubi Akuko, the ringleader of the evil Kami, has lurked in my body, but has been influenced by my sincerity and has finally repented, confessing all the evil it has done until now. Therefore, since it says it knows everything about the schemes of all the evil Kami, I intend to build a great gate in this forest of the shrine together with my guardian Kami, thoroughly examine the causal connections of all people, lead them to repentance, and then send them to the hall of the Saien. I beg you, O Mitama of the Hensei-danshi, please permit this. And on the other hand, Hinodewake, who is of the lineage of the Hensei-danshi, is walking around investigating the whole world by the command of the Iwa no Mitama, and I wish to know about the origin of the origin of the beginning of the world, the causal connections of the great ancestors of the people, what kind of causal connections the Mitama of Daikokunushi has, and what the work of the Otohime of Ryugu is. There are many missionaries in the current San-go-kyo, but they all try to think about the matters of the Kamikai with wisdom and learning, so they understand nothing. Since you are a good Kami of the pure Yamato-damashii of the Hensei-danshi, you know everything about the three thousand worlds, but the other guardian Kami, missionaries, and believers are completely useless. Therefore, would you not make it your duty to have me build a great gate here, examine the causal connections one by one, and have them visit the hall of the Saien?'

The Kiyōrei thus cleverly disguised itself as Takahime and began to make selfish requests. Hatsuwakahime saw through every one of its Kankei. However, as mentioned before, Hatsuwakahime avoided passing Saniha upon this Akurei.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Anata seems to be a person of firm Shinjin who is well-versed in the Reikai. I was born as the daughter of the humble Mokusuke, and as I am still young and an Orokamono who has not even accumulated any Shiyakuwaiteki experience, I cannot possibly understand the Shōsoku of the Reikai. Therefore, it is out of the question for me to grant such things to Anata. If the Kamisama recognize your Goshinriki, you will surely receive permission from the Ōkamisama of Mizu no Mitama. I have no such Kengen whatsoever, so I ask you not to say such things. I humbly request Gokyōjiyu from Anata-sama, who possesses such high Shintoku, as I am still lacking.'

She veiled all her radiance and became like an ordinary person. Takahime grinned and said,

Takahime: 'Ah, that is how it should be, that is the honest truth. To think that Mizu no Mitama-sama is such a curious collector of rare things, to have someone as young as you walking about as a messenger of propaganda. Well then, I, this Takahime, shall explain the root of the root of the Inen here, so you must train yourself thoroughly. Otherwise, it is an act of reckless folly to walk about for propaganda without understanding the reasons, or to attempt to pacify the Ōkuninushi.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'In any case, I am a lacking person, and I wish to request the guidance of the experienced Anata-sama.'

Takahime: 'Yes, yes, you are a crystal soul. You are truly an obedient and cute girl. From now on, you must listen to what this auntie says. No, not auntie, we are mother and child who can never be severed, so please associate with me with that in mind.'

Hatsuwakahime, smiling inwardly while intentionally feigning ignorance, said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Auntie-sama, just now Anata said that you and I are mother and child who can never be severed, but are you referring to a mother and child of Mitama? I, a dull-witted person, cannot grasp this at all.'

Takahime: 'The mother and child of Mitama go without saying; you are the precious daughter of Mokusuke-sama, are you not? That Mokusuke-sama has, by the Inen of the Kamisama this time, become the husband of this Takahime, the genuine Nikumiya of the good at the time of the conversion of Tokoyohime-no-Mikoto. Neither Mokusuke-sama nor this Takahime wish to become husband and wife like young people at our age; it is not a sightly thing to do. We feel as if we are pressing burning iron to our chests, but this too is for the sake of the Kamisama, for the sake of saving all people, and we are participating in the Goshin-gyō while bearing the Chiza no Okido. People who do not understand the reasons may say various things, but if I were to worry about what the people say, I could not perform the service for the Miroku Shinsei. Let the people who do not know the reasons say what they will about the marriage of Mokusuke and Takahime; let them laugh, let them slander. How could people who cannot even see an inch ahead understand the secret of the one-rin of the divine plan? With a resolution as firm as a rock, we have pledged our marriage here, so Hatsuwakahime-sama, please be prepared for that as well, and think of me as your mother. I, too, shall revere you as my most precious child. That we become mother and child was determined by the Ōkamisama from the very beginning of the creation of heaven and earth, from the time of the old, old, long ago, and even further back than that, so please be prepared for it. Since you are an obedient and wise person in all things, you must have understood well what this Nikumiya of Takahime is saying, have you not?'

Hatsuwakahime had clearly seen through the fact that she was being deceived by a Kiyōrei, an animal between a lion and a tiger, and not the real Mokusuke. However, intentionally feigning ignorance, she said,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Takahime-sama, when did my father promise such a thing with you? This is the first I have heard of it. My, my, what a mysterious connection this is. And where has Father gone?'
