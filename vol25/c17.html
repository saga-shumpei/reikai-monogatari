Chapter 17: Forest の囁 [763]

森の囁〔763〕

§ 1

Having lost the golden jewel, banished by Takahime, and braving trials and hardships to head for an island in Oceania to search for the whereabouts of the jewel, Kurohime, together with her husband Takayamahiko, became the chieftain-like figures of the single island, tamed numerous natives, and for a time, by means of military force, was planting her sphere of influence in the lands of the eastern half.

Thither came Koitohime, Isokohime, Umekohime, Imakohime, and Uzuhime, beauties of exquisite and peerless countenance; the reverence of the natives for them was particularly intense, and Takayamahiko and Kurohime realized there was no room to ostracize them, so the shrewd pair immediately put on a disguise to become subordinates of Koitohime, and finally surrendered from the bottom of their hearts to Koitohime, serving in the Jion Castle in the capacities of Boulanger and Cronberg, and for two or three years, with single-minded devotion, made the natives search for the whereabouts of the jewel. Nevertheless, not a single thing resembling the jewel could be laid hands on, and just as they were sinking into thoughts of near despair, they happened to encounter Takahime and her entourage arriving at this island; having no longer any use for the principal island, even if they were to grasp the whole of Oceania in their hands, it could not compare to the three divine treasures which are the treasures of the three thousand worlds. Hesitating and wavering, lest the whereabouts of the precious jewel be discovered by yet someone else, Takahime, Kurohime, and Takayamahiko led along Aru (Aizō) and Ēsu (Eizō), whom they had accustomed to their hands day by day, committed their bodies to a somewhat large camphor-wood boat, secretly set sail from the port of Taka, and rowed out amidst trailing white waves.

Gradually traversing the billows of the Pacific, weaving through numerous islets, passing by Bakan, returning to the Sea of Seto, and at last resting their boat at Sumoto in Awaji (around present-day Iwaya), with Takahime in the lead, the party of five advanced toward the mansion of Tosuke, the chieftain of Sumoto. Behold, it was an exceedingly magnificent residence, and at the front gate two gatekeepers stood formidably at post like A-Un Niō. If one looks closely and gazes at their faces, they are none other than Abukō and Hachikō, whom they had encountered at the Mokusuke mansion in the Ikuta Forest.

Takahime: 'Oh, you are Abukō and Hachikō... How on earth did thieves rise in the world to that extent? Since the Coming of the Hinode no Kami, report to the master of the mansion, Tosuke-dono, at the earliest possible moment, that the Hinode no Kami has made His glorious advent.'

Thus she speaks in an arrogant and commanding manner.

Abukō: 'Since the master is absent of late, we have been told that no matter who comes, we must not let them pass through this gate, and thus the two of us are guarding it strictly; so even if it were the Hinode no Kami, or even if it were His Augustness Kuninotokotachi, passing is absolutely impermissible. When the master is at home there are no gatekeepers at all, but the master has gone off on official divine business to such and such a region, and during his absence a pack of bewildered folks—such and such four or five people coming in a group—will arrive, so you must never let them in. If you should break my command and let them pass inside the gate, I will immediately dismiss you. If I do that, you too will end up falling between two stools like the gadfly and the bee... such is his stern command, so passing is absolutely out of the question... right, Hachikō, isn't that so?'

Hachikō: 'That's right. When His Augustness Kuniyoriwake came out from the Ikuta Forest to welcome Him, someone called Takahime, or Tonbiyime [Tondihime], or Karasuhime, or Kurohime was bound to come fussing and fuming to this mansion, so it would be best to have Abukō and Hachikō, who are familiar faces, act as gatekeepers... and so, after consulting with Tosuke-sama, we are serving as temporary gatekeepers. Divine beings are truly great things. Just like the Hinode no Kami, He knew it beforehand, so it's too much to bear, ah-ha-ha-ha.'

Takahime, raising her voice slightly,

'As the likes of Abukō and Hachikō risen from thieves, calling these sublime divine pillars a kite, a kite-hawk, or a crow—what kind of foul-mouthed things are you saying? You must have been told this by that rascal Kuniyoriwake.'

Abukō: 'Such a matter doesn't matter in the least. Who told it, I know not, but there is probably no one in the whole world who doesn't know. Since you are feather-strong fellows who fly off as far as the three worlds of Australia even though a magnificent jewel is hidden right near here, it cannot be helped if you are likened to birds. If you dawdle, Kuniyoriwake and Tosuke-sama will sniff out the whereabouts of the jewel and rearrange the burial of the treasure so that it won't be taken by you again, it seems; whatever happens, since a splendid jewel will be enshrined in the holy place, they went off to welcome it or receive it. While you are not around, at the holy place... rumor has it, anyway within a short time, the five-colored jewel will be enshrined, and once they receive it and hide it from you again, perhaps Takahime and Kurohime's illness will also be healed, Kuniyoriwake-sama said half in jest. Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

To think that little runts like Hatsuwakahime and the likes of Tamanohime performed the service of the Matsudai... Komahiko: 'In any case, because it is the divine administration of the spiritual realm that transcends time and space, whether past, future, or present, we ordinary mortals simply cannot understand it. Ah-ha-ha-ha-ha.'

Takahime: 'Somehow there is a presence of someone in the inner room. Are they smoking tobacco, tapping the fire brazier with a pipe? A pine-needle-scented fragrance has begun to drift out. Who is in there, confess!'

Komahiko: 'Ha, two or three mice must be rampaging in the inner room.'

Takahime: 'Even so, smoke is coming out, isn't it?'

Komahiko: 'The mice must be smoking tobacco, I suppose.'

Takahime strides forward, pulls open the sliding screen of the inner room, and leaps in, and upon seeing the figures of the two,

Takahime: 'This is a fine surprise, both Kuniorowake and Akihiko, using your absence with such supreme cowardice, hiding your figures in the inner room, do you treat us as nuisances—!'

putting strength into the end of her words, she bristled her horns and shouted fiercely. Kuniorowake tumbles flat onto his back in the Mokusuke style, stretching his hands and feet straight up into the air,

Kuniorowake: 'Chu, chu, chu.'

making the squeaking sound of a mouse. Akihiko likewise rolls over and falls, similarly stretching his hands and feet toward the ceiling,

Akihiko: 'Kutsu-kutsu, kiyu-tsu, kiyu-tsu, kiyu-tsu.'

holding back his laughter beneath his armpits. Takahime:

Takahime: 'What a grossly improper thing you are doing. Mimicking four-legged beasts, your true guardian deities have manifested. Ah, things cannot be concealed, things like mitama... illuminated by the majestic light of the Hinode no Kamisama, this pitiful state, such a mitama is trusted by that dandy Kotoorowake... I have truly grown sad. The executives are all color-blind, they have no eye for discerning people, so it is a truly troubled state. True things are ostracized, and such fellows climb as high as privy beetles, you see.'

Kuniorowake: 'Chu, chu, chu.'

Akihiko: 'Kuu, kuu, kuu.'

Kuniorowake: 'Simply... the mitama is wandering in the void, u-tsu-fu-ha-ha-ha. Kiyu-tsu, kiyu-tsu, kiyu-tsu, kiyu-tsu.'

suppressing laughter all over his body, causing ripples to run through it.

Takahime: 'Hey, hey, Kurohime-sama, Takayamahiko-sama, come and look for a moment, a terrible thing has come to pass. Heaven has become earth, and earth has become heaven, the true nature of the mitama has manifested, and a human being who walks with his feet in the air has appeared. Please, everyone, come along and recite the Amatsu Norito, and pray for them so that they may return to their original human forms. Ah, Kannagara Tamachihami Masachiwase, Kannagara Tamachihami Masachiwase.'

with a pitiful expression on her face, she offers up her prayers with all her might.
