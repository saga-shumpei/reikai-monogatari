Chapter 2: 与太理縮 [748]

与太理縮〔748〕

§ 1

In the inner sanctum of Chionjiyō Castle, Ōriyōhime and Mukadehime, having dismissed their attendants, sit with drooping heads and gloomy expressions, drawing their heads close together and indulging in whispering talk about something.

Mukadehime: 'Ōriyōhime-sama, a grave matter has arisen. That spiteful and warped Hanamaga Tomohiko has crossed Mount Nelson to the west, hoodwinked the ferocious savages, won over an enchantress named Princess Tales, hoisted the banner of Ananai-kyō on the surface, gathered crowds, and come attacking this Chionkiyō. His scheme is to make you, whether you like it or not, his wife again as before, and he is presently attacking the main castle—so report gold, silver, and iron. By any chance, if such a thing comes to pass and Tomohiko attacks this place, what kind of resolution do you intend to take? I would like to hear your sacred intention.'

Ōriyōhime: 'Mother, you need have no such worries. As the saying goes, gentleness overcomes hardness; no matter what kind of stubborn and spiritually unrefined Tomohiko he may be, I shall pierce straight to his bowels with the sword of my three-inch tongue and make him delightfully repent. The likes of Tomohiko are not even worth counting. If such a person were frightening, how could this wild and uncivilized solitary island ever be developed? Do not indulge in needless anxiety, ho ho ho ho ho.'

Saying this, she puts her sleeve to her pursed mouth and laughs effortlessly. Mukadehime, with a serious and anxious expression, says,

Mukadehime: 'No matter how clever you may say you are, since you are still young in years, you can remain so optimistic, but the stubbornness of love is something else again and truly cannot be let out of our sight. Because it is Tomohiko, who has been lamenting even in his sleep and waking that he was made a fool of by Koohime, for a time—judging the time not yet ripe—he folded his tail, closed his eyes, hid his claws, and behaved like a cat. But now, taking advantage of having risen to a momentum where he could even shoot down flying birds in the village of Jiana, he is preparing for battle and making a grand return, considering this very moment as the time to vent his usual pent-up grievances. In the end, this will by no means be settled simply or twice over. You may laugh at it as the reckless folly of an elderly person or the repetition of an old woman's anxious ramblings, but the elderly are the treasure of the house and have accumulated experience, so please listen just a tiny bit to what your mother says. Regrets afterwards will be of no avail.'

Ōriyōhime: 'Oh, my! What an uncharacteristically worried expression on Mother's face. Did not you, Mother, also perform extraordinarily daring deeds in Enonkiyō? Not only that, but when you were defeated at Mount Onigashima on Jitentō Island, established a fortress at Mount Sangu-no-take, and subsequently made great movements at Mount Magatani-take and Mount Kunishiro-yama, you had a vigor like that of a yaksha, did you not? And yet today, I never expected such weak tones to issue from Mother's mouth. As for this Ōriyōhime, even should a hundred Tomohikos or ten thousand Tomohikos come, by virtue of the divine power of the Gods of Ananai-kyō and the might of my Kototama, I shall pacify and lead them, make them repent of their past wrongs, polish them into bodies and souls of supreme goodness and supreme beauty, and save them—such is my resolve, so please do not worry, never, never.'

Resting her elbow on the armrest and taking a hearty gulp of honeysuckle tea, she says,

Ōriyōhime: 'Mother, please go to sleep. The night is already growing late. In any case, the journey to Mount Nelson is several hundred li; even if Tomohiko is coming to attack, there are still ten days or half a month left before we need to worry. There is no need to be overly flustered.'

Mukadehime: 'Vigilance is our greatest enemy; we cannot afford a single moment of delay. I have just instructed everyone to prepare for defense and dispatch. They will be setting out shortly.'

Ōriyōhime: 'By whose command did you order that dispatch?'

Mukadehime: 'Well, by my own arrangement...' Ōriyōhime: 'That is extremely inappropriate. As a private individual, you are my mother; yet speaking from the path of the gods, I am equivalent to the founder and teacher. To issue such commands without listening to my orders, confounding public and private matters, and confusing self and others, root and branch—is this not troublesome? Please revoke it quickly.'

Ōriyōhime: 'That is extremely inappropriate. As a private individual, you are my mother; yet speaking from the path of the gods, I am equivalent to the founder and teacher. To issue such commands without listening to my orders, confounding public and private matters, and confusing self and others, root and branch—is this not troublesome? Please revoke it quickly.'

Mukadehime: 'No matter how much you strut around saying you are the kyōshiyu, a parent is still a parent. If one does not listen to what a parent says, one is no different from a demon. Then you cannot be called a person who protects the divine path of Kamisama. This Mukadehime, being a dog that has eaten wild boars, possesses all kinds of experiences. Right now, just because I want to let my lovely daughter's light shine, I am closing my eyes and hiding my claws, playing the part of Hahinekobaba, but when it comes to the crunch, I will instantly turn into a toraneko. If a tiger closes its eyes, hides its claws, and pretends to be meek, people will look down upon it as a useless burden forever, thinking it has grown old or muddled, but sooner or later, this Mukadehime, at this time of the nation's rise and fall, cannot go on hiding her claws forever. I am resolved to reveal the true nature of a toraneko and perform great activities from now on. In times of peace, it is fine enough to have you serve as the taishiyō, but in such an emergency, how can we get by with lukewarm things? Ah, I must pull off that hateful Tomohiko's head and make him realize the truth. Ugh, what a troublesome affair. Am I to go through one more ordeal for an old woman?'

Kōryūhime: 'Mother, you are impossible because of that. Have you forgotten the teachings of Izumisho?'

Mukadehime: 'Ah, what an inflexible daughter you are. The teachings of Izumisho are truly convenient in an era of great peace under heaven, but at this critical juncture, how can anyone listen to such weak and non-resistant doctrines as they are too agonizingly slow? For Kamisama to command the adoption of non-resistance is a riddle meant to say... do this, do that... You, no matter what, are still young in age, so you listen honestly and it is troublesome. Saying things like milk still clinging to the corners of your mouth... how on earth can this Chion Castle be maintained?'

Putting strength into her last words, she struck the tatami mat with a clenched fist to show her determination.

Kōryūhime: 'Ah, how lamentable is my mother's spirit; how can I ever make her truly repent? ...O Kamisama, I pray that even for a moment the true path may be understood by my precious mother, who is all I have in the world; please grant light to the mirror of her heart and illuminate its darkness. Ah, Kamunagara Tamashii Sakiwafu Sae.'

So she clasped her hands and held back tears.

Mukadehime: 'No matter how much people say they have reformed, our mitama originally possess the essence of evil. To give an example, if muddy water is poured into a large bowl, and through the power of time the mud settles at the bottom while the surface becomes crystal clear, the moment something shakes it, the mud that had been resting at the bottom floats up again and turns right back into muddy water—that is the natural order of things. This is the path of Kamunagara. Is it not the law of heaven and earth to face body with body, spirit with spirit, and force with force? Even the explanation of Einstein's theory of relativity says as much, does it not? If you keep saying such outdated things like you do, you will end up suffering as if your crab legs were torn off and your bird wings were plucked away. Is there any parent in the three thousand worlds who does not think of their child? It is solely because you are dear to me that I speak of things not to your liking, but a parent's advice and eggplant flowers never bear a single barren blossom. Good medicine tastes bitter to the mouth, and sweet things are the source of intestinal worms; so as my lifelong plea, please give up your opposition to our going out to battle. I shall henceforth lead Kiyokimi's Sajinogamii, make the flower of old age bloom, and fight a single battle as a parting gift for the netherworld, so do not, by any means, display a weak spirit and crush your mother's hard-wrought martial valor. A parent joins hands and begs you. Should we go over the sea, our corpses will soak in the water; should we go over the mountains, our corpses will grow grass; by the side of the Great Deity shall we die, never shall we die in peace, and we shall look back no more—pressing ever forward and closing in ever tighter, chasing down Tomohiko's forces behind every mountain ridge and cutting them down at every river rapid to bring them to submission, making them look foolish and punishing them as I planned, so do by no means interfere.'

Changing her countenance with a look of fury, she snatched up the naginata from the nageshi sooner than it takes to tell,

Mukadehime: 'Ōriyōhime, farewell...'

And made as if to stride out from this place.

Ōriyōhime: 'As the Queen of Chionjiyō and the Kamuzukasa of Ananai-kyō, I now formally expel Mukadehime from Ananai-kyō and strip her of her membership. Receive this judgment with gratitude.'

Mukadehime retreated two or three paces, glared fiercely at Ōriyōhime,

Mukadehime: 'Even if the ties of parent and child are severed, are we not still parent and child? The affection of a parent and child can never change no matter where. Even should Mukadehime incur the wrath of the gods of heaven and earth and suffer the torments of water and fire, I shall not begrudge it for the sake of my child. Now that I have been expelled from Ananai-kyō, I shall no longer be bound by you; from this moment on, I will act with complete freedom. I shall forthwith lead the warriors, headed by Kiyokō, and display glorious feats of arms. Koitohime, farewell.'

As she again made to walk out, Ōriyōhime spoke in a stern voice,

Ōriyōhime: 'Mukadehime, having now been expelled from Ananai-kyō, you surely have no right to command Kiyokō, who serves as the left guardian, nor any of the others. Do as you please and go forth entirely alone. Like a moth flying into the flame, you have drowned in the love of your child and forgotten the love of the true God; I shall punish you in place of heaven. ... Hey there, is Kinshū not present? Come quickly and bind Mukadehime!'

Thus she called out.

Just at that moment, the three—Kin, Gin, and Tetsu—appeared briskly upon the scene, and seeing this state of affairs between parent and child, knitted their brows in doubt.

Kinshū: 'My word, my word! Whatever deep circumstances there must be behind the two of you to make you look thus, but first of all, please calm yourselves.'

Ōriyōhime: 'There is no need to speak of the circumstances. It is an order from me, Ōriyōhime, Queen of Chionkyō. Bind Mukadehime hand and foot!'

Kinshū: 'My word, my word! This is completely contrary to expectations, O Queen; your words utterly fail to make any sense to me. Whatever matters may exist, as a child, to tell us to bind our own mother—who is irreplaceable in heaven and on earth and to whom we owe the grace of the mountains and the seas—what an unfilial command! Has the Queen gone mad?'

And he wiped away his tears.

Mukadehime: 'My word, my word, Kinshū, what you say is true. Father and mother are likened to heaven and earth. I have heard that a father's grace is higher than heaven, and a mother's grace is heavier than earth. Though you are the Queen of this Chionkyō, which derives its name from the grace of the earth, Koitohime has forgotten a mother's grace—what is called the grace of the earth. Now then, from here on, using a parent's authority, I banish Koitohime. You three—Kin, Gin, and Tetsu—listen to the command of this Mukadehime and prepare to counterattack against Yotahiko's forces. That is not to say, however, that you should carry even an inch of steel upon your persons. You are to make the enemy yield with the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases and the conduct of kindness. Do not misunderstand this, most certainly do not.'

Kinshū: 'Your words are clear and righteous, my Lady, and Kinshū has understood them well. However, for what purpose is the naginata you now hold in your hand?'

Mukadehime: 'This is not a weapon to mow down the enemy. It is a weapon to encourage our own forces. If you dawdle and hesitate, this Mukadehime may well mow you all down one by one. Now, you people, follow Mukadehime! I have no use for Koitohime.'

And once again she hastily attempted to stride out from the place.

Ōriyōhime: 'Since I, Ōriyōhime, Queen of Chionkyō, have expelled Mukadehime, you three—Kin, Gin, and Tetsu—have no need to obey her commands. Compose your hearts and listen well to my words.'

At these words, the three prostrated themselves.

Ginshū: 'What strange things we are hearing! For what fault have you taken the disciplinary action of expulsion against Mukadehime? We would like to hear your reasons for once.'

Tetsushū: 'Your mind has been derided by the heat of recent times, and you utter unreasonable and outrageous wild words, O Lady Ōriyōhime. To your commands, which run counter to the principles of heaven and earth, we absolutely cannot render obedience.'

Ōriyōhime: 'I now formally expel both Gin and Tetsu as well.'

蜈蚣姫『金、銀、鉄の言ふ事を綜合すれば、どうやら左守神の清公が張本人と見える。……清公を之へ呼んで来なさい。金州、サア早く』

金州『ハイ、お言葉で御座いまするが、叔母の死んだも直休み、漸く内乱鎮定の曙光を認めた処ですから、少し休養を願つてお使ひを致しませう』

鉄州『実際の事を申しますれば、清公さまは御存じの通り、実に立派なお方で御座います。ブランジーとして実に申分なきお方、然し乍らクロンバーが無ければ陰陽合致致さず、それが為に宇豆姫さまをクロンバーの位置に据ゑ度いと、吾々仲間の者は内々運動を開始して居ました。処が肝腎の宇豆姫さまは察する処、鶴公さまに秋波を送り、ブランジーの清公さまに、エツパツパを喰はさむとする形勢ほの見えたれば、何とか事を構へ、右守神鶴公さまを先頭に、スマートボール、貫州、武公、チヤンキー、モンキー、其他の連中を城外に放り出し、城門を固く鎖し、時を移さず無理往生にしてでも宇豆姫さまをクロンバーの役に就かせ、夫婦合衾の式を挙げさせ度いと鳩首凝議の結果、一寸狂言を致したに過ぎませぬ。此暑いのに何百里もあるネルソン山の彼方迄、誰が偵察に参る者がありませうぞ。全く以て真赤な嘘言で御座いました。身の過ちは宣り直せと言ふ神様の御教を奉体遊ばす黄竜姫さま、人をお赦し遊ばす慈愛の権化、滅多にお叱り遊ばす様な、天則違反的な行為には出られますまいから、安心して実状を申上げました』

と流石鉄面皮の鉄州も、稍羞恥の念に駆られてか、俯向いて真赤な顔をして居る。

蜈蚣姫『エーエー、しようもない悪企みをして此妾まで心配させ、親子喧嘩までオツ始めさせた太い奴だ。……ナア鶴公さま、油断も隙もあつたものぢや御座いませぬなア』

鶴公『お言葉の通り実に寒心致しました。然し乍ら之全く神様の吾々に対するお気付けでせう。之に鑑み今後は、人の言ふ事を軽々しくまる聞きしてはなりますまい』

蜈蚣姫『斯く事実の判明した上は何をか言はむ。今日は之にて忘れて遣はす。サア妾と共に神殿に於て、感謝祈願の祝詞を奏上致しませう』

と先に立ち、一同と共に神殿に足音静に進み入る。
