Chapter 17: 晨の Wonder 愕 [1719]

晨の驚愕〔1719〕

§ 1

The Izayohi moon paled in the sky, the Hoshikage was faint, and the eastern sky began to whiten to the sound of cawing crows. Kolton suddenly awoke and looked at the Atari, but there was no sign of Dariya-hime, who looked as if she were wrapped in white Usuginu over Hijirimen, nor was the Rakanzura Buotoko Balgie to be seen.

Wondering at this Fushigi, he looked at the sleeping face of the Ōoyabun Shakanna and saw the Katakana characters 'Moinu' written on his forehead. Turning to look at Genshinbau, he saw that his forehead was also marked with the characters 'Nenneko' in a woman's handwriting. Kolton immediately shook Genshinbau awake.

Kolton: 'Excuse me, Tenshinbausama, the Okusama has disappeared. Dōzo, please wake up. ...Oyabunsama, this is Taihen, please wake up quickly. An Ōsaudō has occurred.'

Genshin: 'What, you say the Oku has disappeared? That is Taihen. Ōkata, she must have gone to Pasapana or somewhere; go and search well.'

Kolton: 'But on Oyabunsama's face, it is written in a woman's handwriting as Moinu, and on your face, it is written as Nenneko.'

Genshin: 'Ya, indeed, Moinu is on Shakanna's forehead, and on your forehead, it is written as Saru and Kaheru. On my face, Nenneko. Hatena. While we were sleeping soundly, this is a riddle of Saru, Inu, and Kaheru here. This is Taihen. If we let that Aitsu escape, it will be an Ichidaiji for the Gankutsu. There is a fear of Himitsu being Rōei. Oi, Kolton, Tokurei the Kobun and chase after them.'

Kolton: 'Hai, I understand. Ōoyabunsama, how do you think about this?'

Shakanna: 'If I let that Aitsu escape, I cannot help but be bewildered. Why were you not keeping watch? How could you drink that base sake and fall asleep like that?'

Kolton scratched his head, looking as if it were difficult to say.

Kolton: 'Hei, as you know, I, Sessha, Kochira, Watashi, Yatsugare am a non-drinker who dislikes sake, but that beautiful, beautiful Tenshinbau's Okusama, Dariya-hime-sama, looked at me with strange eyes, made a smile, filled her pink cheeks with laughter, and with her hands, like white, beautiful ivory work... Kolton-sama, sa, please have a cup... she said to me, so he-he-he-hei, I just... thinking I would show off my manliness by going along with the mood, I just gulped it down. Yes, I drank it. Then, without knowing what was happening, I got dead drunk and fell asleep. Dōzo, I beg for your forgiveness, your understanding, your pardon, I ask you one and two times over. I humbly entreat you.'

Shakanna: 'E, are you still drunk? What are you saying? What on earth did you do with the Hime?'

Kolton: 'E, there is nothing to be done. If I knew what had happened, I would never have let her escape. She may have taken Balgie by the hand and run away.'

Genshin: 'Che, ee, what a useless fellow you are. Sa, quickly wake up the Kobun, make arrangements in all directions, and bring Dariya-hime back.'

Kolton: 'Hen, do not act so important. Are you and I not of the same rank? Just because your wife has absconded and run off, you should not be barking at me like that. If you want your wife searched for, ...please, brother, go and search for her... you should ask me with a request. I, Kochira, Sessha, Boku, have no right or duty to listen to your orders. I, Boku, Sessha, only listen to the orders of the Ōoyabun and work. If I have the orders of the Ōoyabun, I will tuck up my skirts and set off at any time. Hen, do not act so important. You dunce, you cuckold, you fool, you idiot, you blockhead.'

Shakanna: 'Hey, Koruton, you look like you still haven't sobered up from your drunkenness. However, we cannot remain like this. Quickly wake up your subordinates, it's a search, a search!'

Koruton: 'If you please, Oyabun, Oyakata, Danna, Daitōmoku, there is no need for you to worry to such an extent. It's not as if the Oyabun's wife, the old woman, has run away and fled, and searching and looking for the whereabouts of a wife is a responsibility that falls upon the double shoulders of the Keshin of Tentei, the Tenrai Kiuseishiyu, Tenshinbau-sama, would it not?'

Shakanna: 'Tch, what a hopeless rascal. That is why I always tell you not to drink alcohol. Since you said you don't drink alcohol, I promoted you and used you heavily, yet what kind of attitude is this? Is there ever a time when you sleep without even knowing that the characters for monkey and frog have been written on your forehead by the hand of Dariyahime, you idiot?'

Koruton: 'Idiot or whatever, it's fine; if such a beautiful woman treats one gently, must not a man become a great fool of the realm? More than that, Oyabun, on the forehead part of your forehead-mouth, it is written in large letters with the handwriting of Moinu and Dariya-sama. Why are you also sleeping and fast asleep to the point that you don't even know when such mischief has been done to you? Is not Tenshinbau-sama the same? Because you have such a wife, the innermost chamber of such a beautiful beauty, and while holding your wife, the old woman, you relax your mind, feel at ease, let your fat droop, and become infatuated, how foolish, how idiotic, being scribbled upon as a lullaby and the like, your face in its facial appearance is just like a kindergarten student's scrap paper. Pull yourself together a bit, please.'

Genshinbau: 'Ah, it cannot be helped, being this way I myself cannot linger hesitatingly. Come, from now on the honorable master himself will set out to search. Shakanna-dono, please lend me your subordinates somehow.'

Koruton: 'That's it, Tenshinbau-sama, you are entirely right. When the core inner chamber, the wife, the old woman, has gone into hiding and concealed her figure, we cannot just remain motionless like that. Come, I too will help, so let us set out to search for the whereabouts of Dariya-sama. Because I, I, and myself wish to gaze upon and worship that gentle face once more, hehehe.'

When Shakanna entered the barracks-built earthen floor where his subordinates were gathered, whoever they might be, it was a scene of utter devastation and disorder: some using sake bottles as pillows, some biting and holding sake cups while sleeping, others sprawled out with their hip-guards and loincloths fully exposed—it was completely like a child's toy box having been flung open. Shakanna let out a single thunderous roar, somewhat containing angry vigor,

Shakanna: 'Hey you lot, will none of you wake up? Is it not already daybreak? What is this state of affairs. A grave matter has suddenly broken out at the master's residence. Quickly open your eyes and get up, get up!'

Koruton adopted an imposing and fierce tone on the tail-ends of his words, squaring his shoulders right up to them, forcefully making use of a fellow whose yesterday's drunken vapors still lingered and whose root of the tongue could not freely operate,

Koruton: 'Hey you lot, what are all of you doing? Until when will you slumber and sleep? What is this attitude? Will you not quickly get up and rise? What troublesome rascals. It is already the dawn of the morning sky. The wife, the old woman, of the Keshin of Tentei, Tenshinbau, who went out last night, has run away and fled, and has become missing without her whereabouts being known. Come, quickly, quickly, make preparations, preparations!'

At this voice, each and every one of them wore a strange expression, just like a soft-shelled turtle stung on the tail, or like having their testicles pulled out while sleeping, opening their mouths like brazier stoves and making cat-like ablutions, some running out in a panic while clutching sake bottles, some getting flustered and wearing their clothes inside out—it was a scene indescribable by any means.

Genshinbau borrowed two hundred subordinates and made arrangements in all four directions, setting forth with blood surging in his face in order to search for the whereabouts of Dariyahime. Behind remained the three of them: Shakanna, his daughter Subaruhime who had turned fifteen years old this year, and Koruton.

スバ『お父さま、どうしても行かなくちやならないの。私もう暫く此処に居り度いのだけどなア。もう十日もすればダリヤの花が咲くのだもの。あの美しいダリヤの花の唇に吸ひ付いて遊びたいのよ』

§ 4

Koruton: 'Eh, Yoshikohime-sama, what an easygoing thing you are saying. If we dawdle, the lives of the three of us will be lost, you know.'

Subaru: 'Why do you and Father fear so much? Aren't there two hundred kobun? No matter what comes, with this many aren't we able to defend against it? I hate going to live in such a small hut deep in the mountains.'

Koruton: 'If you dislike it because the house is small, I, watashi, setsushiya will construct and build a large kaoku to give to you, you know.'

Subaru: 'If so, wait a minute, let's go together with Father. If I come to dislike it, we will come back here again.'

Koruton: 'Yay, I've hit the fortune. Oyabun-sama, it's already daijiyōbu. Yoshikohime-sama said she will go. Please rejoice and be glad.'

From this, gathering close necessities, Koruton wrapped them in an ōburoshiki and, carrying it on his back while the three of shiyujiyū traced along the edge of the steep tanigaha, they came to hide in a bauoku three ri deep in the mountains.
