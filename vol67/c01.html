Chapter 1: Plum の Flower 香 [1703]

梅の花香〔1703〕

§ 1

Even the crooked schemes on Mount Ōra and the ominous shooting stars of the giant cedar were blown out by the breath of Kami; once enveloped in the darkness beneath the trees, upon clearing, the plum blossom fragrance of the pure Sanzensekai and the dark clouds wrapping the mountain ridges scattered smoothly. In the vast plain under the clear sky, high in the midnight air, starlight sparkled across the entire surface like inlaid jewels. The whispers of demons, the rejoicing of fierce beasts, the cries of foxes and raccoon dogs, and the sounds of insects all stopped completely, bringing absolute silence to heaven and earth. It was just as if water were sprinkled moderately on the soil of a swelling garden: there was not a single speck of windblown dust, nor any clouds or mist. A gentle breeze brushed against people's faces, and the clean, cool spirit soaked deeply through their internal organs. The bell's voice of impermanence, the crying sound of the cuckoo of the law of birth and extinction, and the screech of the owl of extinction and absolute cessation all turned into a pure realm of tranquil Nirvana. With lips like red flowers and a nose shaped like a magnolia bud, two gentle, mysterious, and shining eyes like black beans carrying dew, eyebrows like those of Jizō, the utmost of locality's beauty, the utmost of goodness, and overflowing charm on her face, yet harboring the claws and fangs of jackals and wolves in her heart, desiring to grasp the realm under heaven, breaking her liver and gallbladder day and night, the supreme chieftain of bandits—who could be compared to a bodhisattva on the outside but a yaksha on the inside, the incarnation of wicked demons and ferocious ghosts—Yorikohime-jotei was struck by the innate divine spirit gushing from the mouth of Uaikō. Instantly arousing cataclysms and strange portents within her heart, with the sound of the reformation army's trumpet echoing in her breast, her internal organs simultaneously gave rise to a regenerative activity. The greedy desires wishing for despotism, coercion, and nobility, along with the wicked party of self-love, suddenly caused their shadows to lurk. Returning to the true nature of Kannagara and the sincere heart of a newborn baby, forgetting the self-interest and desires of her physical body, she died and was reborn into a great universal love that obeys Kami, loves Kami, loves people, and loves all things universally.

At the very moment when the flower of repentance opened in the heart of Yorikohime, the fruit of love and goodness ripened, and she was bathed in the radiance of true faith and gentle flavor, her true heart responded to heaven and earth. The heavens became high, clear, and serene, without a speck of cloud, blurring the vast sky of stars inlaid like seven treasures. From the eastern wilderness overlooking and parting the thousand grasses, the waxing moonlight rose. It was precisely like a watermelon in full perfection, neatly and skillfully cut right down the middle with a sharp kitchen knife: a silvery half-ball with distinct contours suddenly illuminated heaven and earth, making even the figures of ants moving on the ground clearly visible.

The gigantic chieftain of bandits with white hair and a youthful face, disguised as a ascetic practitioner, employing three thousand subordinates, indulging the greed of jackals and wolves, looking up to Yorikohime as a master strategist and revering her as a great boss, audaciously conquering the Great Lord of the capital of Haruna and seizing hegemony over seven thousand regions of India, hiding deep within the secrets of his breast—never forgotten in dreams through the frost of dawn and the rain of evening—he had stationed himself on cloud-piercing Mount Ōra, wearing mist as a sash and haze as a veil, regarding the rustling of tree leaves as the wind of a fan, fixing the blue sky as a ceiling, grass as a mattress, and a skull as a pillow for a brief nap. Relishing the meat of tigers, wolves, lions, and bears, like King Asura or like a demon king, and at times like a comet, exhaling the aura of specters and evil in all directions, conquering the four seas with a single monk's staff, troubling and tormenting all heavens and all goodness with the magical sword concealed in his heart, acting willfully, and wishing to freely agitate heaven and earth—harboring illusory, rainbow-like, mirage-like, castle-in-the-air delusions, he had smugly poured his mind and body into his reckless objective. That very Shīgō had his mind and gallbladder seized by the kototama of Uaikō, the minister of Kami of Misoshikyo, had the defilements of his internal organs wiped away, and was struck by his divine spirit. At the single moment when his mind and spirit suddenly turned into those of a terribly weak routed soldier who trembled in fright even at the hand-like gestures of withered pampas grass rustling in the night gale, faced with the drastically transformed words and actions of Yorikohime—whom he had relied upon as his strength—he now lacked even more the courage to rebel. The dark clouds of his mental sky that he had hitherto enveloped were blown away by the wild winds of Mount Ōra, revealing a heart like the clear waxing moon. He suddenly prostrated himself flat on the earth, filled with longing, adoration, and yearning for the beautiful shadow of the gentle, elegant, and pure moon goddess, returned to his innate nature, and the demon vanished instantly like smoke. Hearing the whisper of Kami deep within his breast, touching its spiritual light, encountering the flavor of sincere love, and becoming completely like a different person, his long white hair turned the color of platinum and grew increasingly glossy. His complexion was purified and bettered to the extent that one might suspect he was a goddess of heaven, free of sins, free of defilements, without a single speck of hatred, and without the clouds and mist of desire. Henceforth, solely through the divine protection of the deities of heaven and earth, he came to aspire to tread the path of sincerity, perform the deeds of sincerity, fear Kami and love Kami with trembling awe, and dedicate his heart and strength to Kami day and night.

Next, proclaiming himself the heavenly Kiuseishiyu, the incarnation of Tentei, and the living deity of Mount Ora, wielding a venomous tongue to delude the masses of Tenkabanmin, and attempting to exhaust every conceivable evil, that crafty scoundrel of cunning and depravity, Genshinbau—sole Irogaki of Tenka, tormented by the flames of Jiyōyoku, and as a result of Hanmonkunau, forgetting shame and about to stoop to the acts of Jiuchiku, the false Kiuseishiyu and false Niseyogenshiya, that Maisu—was struck by the divine light of Ananai-kyō, assailed by the Shinsotsu of righteousness, and finally repenting, came to pledge to act together with the great chieftain, Yoshikohime-dono, and Sīgo. The waters of the Ora River flowed gently, deep and blue, reflecting the vast sky of glittering stars upon its mirror-like surface where even the bottom was invisible, and cradling in the softly blowing ripples the myriad fragments of the compassionate countenance of Nyorai of Moonlight, thus floating the phenomenon of a brilliant, luminous world of heaven and earth. The eastern sky began to drift with crimson clouds and purple haze, the myriad birds in the trees blessed the eternal and boundless future for thousands of generations, the sound of the murmuring shallows seemed to speak of some cosmic mystery, and even in the echo of the wind one might suspect the indwelling of the divine voice. Yoshikohime-dono and the other wicked felons experienced the heaven of their hearts suddenly opening like lotus flowers, and at the very instant it began to fragrance, parting the five-colored clouds, a golden crow suddenly ascended, stamping a perfectly round Hinomaru amidst the banner clouds, and further displaying the auspicious omen of the divine age of sunrise to all creation in heaven and earth. As the vanguard of the glorious reign where auspicious omens unfold, deities and men, the miscreants, beasts, birds, insects, and fishes gathered on this mountain all shared the sentiment of blessing the reign of Isago in unison. Ah, May the divine spirit bless and prosper us.

Purifying the three realms of visible and invisible spirits, rebuilding and restoring them to the auspicious age of the primordial creation of heaven and earth, causing deities, men, and all creation to bathe in the blessings of the golden world, and descending from heaven to execute the primal will of the universe, manifesting as the rigorous spirit, appearing as Ōkuninotokotono-Mikoto and commencing the divine work, the sole living deity of the universe affectionately and earnestly guides deities, men, and all creation morning and evening. Wishing to propagate the great divine teaching of Aizen and Shinjin throughout Tenka and to embark upon the execution of the apparition of the divine administration of Isago, Lord Susanoo of the deities dropped sacred traces on Mount Ubusuna, manifested as the spirit of purity to sweep away the defilement of the three realms, and established a pure and undefiled new heaven and earth, staying His spirit at sacred sites in mountains and regions across the world, nurturing numerous missionary envoys, and dispatching them to the four quarters of Tenka. Ume Kōji, who was one of the dispatched divine pillars, accompanied the missionary envoy Terunodewake as an attendant, and served in the war of pacification against the evil deities of the capital of Haruna, was a man of valor with towering spirit whose mirror of the heart shone resplendently, well-versed in the divine, penetrating the innermost depths of the masses' hearts as if gazing through crystal without missing a single detail, furthermore possessing a heart of absolute purity and cleanliness, discerning divine law, conversing with reason, whose deportment was ever nimble yet neither too light nor too heavy, a fine man who attained the Golden Mean. Wherever he went, myriads of flowers bloomed in profusion, hell was instantly transformed into paradise, and the wilderness where fierce beasts ramped was transformed into a paradise of blooming flowers where birds sang and butterflies danced. Strong and upright in spirit, bowing not before wealth and honor, yielding not to martial might, ever content with his office, interpreting everything as the divine will, leaking no discontent in whatever circumstances he found himself, grieving not, nor despairing when plunged into any tragic plight, leisurely and carefree, he rejoiced and was glad of himself; when calamity approached, he regarded it as the whip of heavenly grace, and when joy arrived, he cautioned himself with heaven's admonition, without a single hair's breadth of laxity, and dealing with the world with optimism. Truly the archetype of divine men, the model of missionary envoys, from whichever aspect of heart, word, or deed one might view him, there was not the slightest gap to interject a single criticism. Following Terunodewake, he faithfully guarded the sentiments of master and disciple, concealing his own numerous fine points that surpassed his master, yielding his virtue to his superior, rich in sympathy, well-loving of his colleagues, and there was none among those who met his eyes or entered his ears who did not bathe in the virtue of his transforming influence. Originally Ume Kōji was a divine pillar specially chosen by the Great Deity, harboring infinite secrets, entrusted with the key to the wondrous and mysterious secret gates, and a supreme divine man without fear anywhere in the universe. Therefore, he calmly entered and left the lair of demons as a solitary horseman, plunged into the packs of wolves and jackals, meeting occasions and dealing with changes, saving the peril of one man and two women, and without the need to expend a thousand words like other missionary envoys, he was a hero who made even the head of the ferocious evil deities, Yoshikohime and her faction, repent with a sudden change of heart. Even his master, the missionary envoy Terunodewake, could not even peek into a part of his divinity. Nevertheless, with an attitude of blending with the dust of the world, he regarded Terunodewake—whose virtue of Aizen and light of Shinjin were inferior—as part of the divine economy, respected him as his lord and master, and desired to always act as a junior toward Lord Teru and other colleagues. Whether Ume Kōji is a demon, a deity, a true man, or simply the incarnation of the Great Deity, it is hoped that readers will discern it for themselves through the subsequent tales.

From this time, Yorikohime, upon the recommendation of Umekō Hanaka, returned to the village of Tarai, met her mother Sanyo, apologized for her past sins of filial impiety and unruliness, and vowed to repent from now on, to comfort her mother's heart in her old age, and furthermore to devote her whole life to the path of Aizen for the sake of God and the world. Mother Sanyo wept tears of gratitude as she reflected that the two sisters, owing to the results of Umekōtsukasa's trials and hardships and the outpouring of his loving compassion, had safely accomplished their mother-and-child reunion, and she could not cease respecting Umekō as a true Ikigami. Next, the two persons, Shīgōbau and Genshinbau, went to Sandā's house, apologized to his parents for their evil deeds up to this day, and vowed to repent and serve a part of the divine work for the sake of all the people under heaven. Sandā's parents rejoiced as if in a dream, prepared one hundred flavors of food and drink to entertain Umekō and others, and furthermore decided to grant Shīgō numerous parcels of land to engage in the enterprise of reclamation. He, Shīgō, followed Sandā and Sugakohime, looking up to the two as his masters, cleared the boundless forests and fields owned by Sugakohime's father Janku, made three thousand subordinates engage in the work, built a great metropolis, managed a new village, and spent his life praised as the true man of the village of Tarai. Father Janku heard from afar that Sandā and Sugako had returned home safely, that they had been saved by Umekōtsukasa, and that Shīgō had been made a chief manager to employ several thousand subordinates and engage in the work of reclamation while the enterprise developed day by day, and he greatly rejoiced, attributing it entirely to the grace of God, devoted his whole life to God, participated in the divine work, and did not return to his native place until he exposed his corpse. And Janku made his fierce name run through the castle town of Barugan as a brave warrior of the volunteer army.

Genshinbau, who had once repented, grew worse again, disputed with Shīgō, and leading a disgruntled faction of over three hundred men out of three thousand subordinates, crossed the peak of Ōra, pillaged private houses, and hid his figure heading toward the direction of Mount Jikyō.

The heart of the Kami of Kannagara is like the white plum fragrant in the morning sun. Like the star-shadows twinkling in the sky of Kisaragi when the plum blossoms begin to bloom. The manly vigor of Yorikohime, who in all things followed the teachings of God and became a pillar of God in the world. The warriors who fear the sins of the present world, think of the afterlife (shigo), and return to God. Sandā and Sugako returned to the village of their birth and opened the new village for eternity. Genshin, of the crooked path where light and darkness cross, changed his heart and became an ogre. The fate of Genshin, wandering in the mountains and fields as a crooked person whose liver-heart of the village was clouded.

之よりヨリコ姫は梅公花香の勧めにより、タライの村に立帰り、母のサンヨに面会し、今迄の不孝不始末の罪を謝し、今後は悔い改めて、老後の母の心を安んじ、且つ神の御為世の為に、愛善の道に生涯を投ぜむ事を誓つた。母のサンヨは二人の姉妹が梅公司の艱難辛苦の結果と慈愛心の発露に仍つて、無事母子の対面が出来た事を涙片手に感謝し、梅公を真の生神として尊敬して止まなかつた。次にシーゴー坊や玄真坊の両人はサンダーの家に至り、彼が両親に向つて、今日迄の悪業を謝し、且悔改めて天下万民の為に神業の一部に奉仕せむ事を誓つた。サンダーの両親は夢かと許り喜んで、梅公其他に対し、百味の飲食を調理して之を饗応し、且つシーゴーには数多の所有地を与へ開拓の事業に従事せしむる事となつた。彼れシーゴーはサンダー、スガコ姫に従ひ、両人を主人と仰ぎ、スガコ姫が父のジャンクが所有せる無限の山林田畑を開墾し、三千の部下をして之に従事せしめ、大都会を造つて………………新しき村を経営し、タライの村の真人と謳はれて生涯を送つた。父のジャンクは遥にサンダー、スガコの無事に帰宅せし事、梅公司に救はれし事、及シーゴーを一の番頭となし、数千の部下を使役して開墾の業に従事せしめ、日々事業の発展しつつある事を聞知し、大に喜んで、全く神の恩恵となし、一生を神に捧げ、神業に参加し、屍をさらす迄、吾郷里に帰らなかつた。そしてジャンクは義勇軍の勇士としてバルガン城下に驍名を走せた。

一旦悔い改めたる玄真坊は再び悪化して、シーゴーと論争し、三千の部下の中、不平組三百余名を引率し、オーラの峰を渉つて民家を掠奪し乍ら、地教山方面指して姿を隠したのである。

惟神神の心は白梅の 旭に匂ふ姿なりけり。 梅の花香ひ初めたる如月の 空に瞬く珍の星影。 何事も神の教にヨリコ姫 世の神柱となりし雄々しさ。 現世の罪をば恐れシーゴー（死後）を 思ひて神に帰るつはもの。 サンダーやスガコは生れし己のが村に 帰りて新村永久に開きぬ。 光暗行きかふ曲の玄真は 心変りて鬼となりぬる。 村肝の心曇りし曲人と 山野に迷ふ玄真の果。
