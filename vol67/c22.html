Chapter 22: 憧憬の Beauty [1724]

憧憬の美〔1724〕

§ 1

While hiding deep within the inner chambers of His mansion, the Taishi hung the portrait of Princess Subaru in the tokonoma, yearning morning and evening for her pristine beauty, and letting His thoughts wander far to the humble cottage of the commoner in Asakuradani. Arina, His favored retainer, had been ordered to thirty days of confinement, having no companion to talk to, and was suffering from truly lonely thoughts, but whenever he looked at the portrait of Princess Subaru, he extinguished the flames of his anguish and torment.

'However, today is already the day when Arina will be pardoned and become a free man. He must be wanting to see Me, too. I also wish to see Arina soon,'

He murmured to himself, sinking into melancholy. Thereupon, the senior retainer Halchin appeared timidly before Him,

Halchin: 'To Your Imperial Highness, without any change, beholding Your sacred and robust countenance, I consider it the height of awe and joy for my own person.'

Taishi: 'Ah, are you Halchin? The other day, at the time of the sudden outbreak of the great commotion within the palace, I heard that you braved danger to hold back Samori and rescue Namori from peril, which is truly the height of admirable conduct. Won't you come closer and tell Me some interesting and lively story?'

Halchin: 'Yes, I am deeply humbled and terrified. As an humble retainer, I merely performed the duty that I ought to fulfill, and therefore receiving words of praise is more than I can bear with blushing shame. I had thought of wishing to ask audience once, but deeming it far too awe-inspiring, I have refrained until today. Your Highness has favored Samori's son Arina beyond measure, causing him to wait by Your side day and night without distinction, which is indeed the utmost perfection of fortune; however, if Your Highness trusts only a single servant, grave errors will arise, and therefore, with Your wise and holy consideration, I beg of You to allow other retainers also to draw somewhat closer to You.'

Taishi: 'Ahahaha, a multitude of retainers swarm about, but since there are no retainers to My liking who seem like true men, I have no choice and, lonely though it may be, I am keeping Arina close to Me. What do you think of Arina's character? Openly disgorge your impressions before Me without reservation.'

Halchin: 'Yes, to make various comments regarding Your Highness's favored retainer is something I can endure with nothing less than trembling fear as a retainer's station. I sincerely beg that this matter alone be pardoned.'

Taishi: 'What, is such hesitation necessary? Try to speak your mind to the extent that you think. Since I will also hear your opinion regarding Arina's conduct, and when I judge it inconvenient, I intend to ban his coming and going from now on.'

'Yes, as expected of the wise Taishi-sama, precisely because of this, the nation of Tarahan shall remain unchanged through all ages, and I, a humble retainer, feel a joy beyond measure, like receiving rain in a time of severe drought. If that is so, I shall speak: that fellow Arina is an impudent man unworthy even of his father, preaching civilization and newness in all things, and he is a disloyal and unrighteous demon who cares not a whit if the foundation of the state is endangered or what becomes of the royal family. If Your Highness continues to keep such a person close and trust him for all time, it is not guaranteed that a major emergency will not suddenly break out for the sake of the royal family and the state. Please reconsider with your wise and holy deliberation, and adopt even a little of this retainer's words. For the sake of the royal family, for the sake of the state, unable to help it, and resolved to die, this Halchin has come to offer direct remonstrance, well knowing Your Highness's wrath.'

Taishi: 'Uhm, is that so? Can that fellow called Arina look like such a villain to your eyes? One who preaches a new ideology awakened to the times—is it not impossible to accept that he ruins the royal family and the state? In today's world, it is impossible to govern the people using oppressive and autocratic methods as before. If we do not adapt to the times and conduct government appropriate to the times, the state will be endangered instead.'

Halchin: 'Your Highness's imperial decree is most reasonable, but His Majesty the King's worry and the all-night anguish of all the senior retainers—if we trace the origin, it is simply because that greenhorn flattered Your Highness, and, fearfully enough, lured Your noble self out into the wilderness where fierce beasts rage, making You undergo various hardships. To bring such a disloyal and unrighteous rebellious subject close to Your side cannot be for Your benefit. I earnestly desire that Your Highness reconsider this matter alone.'

While outwardly shouting grandly for the royal family and for the nation, if one were to illuminate their inner hearts with X-rays, there is nothing within any of them save self-love. Truly, with such retainers holding political power, the Talahan nation is in a perilous state indeed! I have neither a single confidant nor a teacher. Day and night, my surroundings are enveloped by an atmosphere of desolation, and I am drifting in the abyss of disappointment and discouragement. As the proverb has it... He who is about to drown will clutch even at a single straw... and the like. Viewing Alina, who has deeply penetrated my heart, as my sole friend and strength, I am agonizing day and night, somehow hoping to save the nation from falling. To ostracize Alina is precisely tantamount to ostracizing me. If you wish to torment me, you, the high officials, should put your heads together in consultation, and subject Alina to whatever oppression or ostracism you see fit.'

Haru: 'Your Highness spoke as though not a single person among the senior vassals truly thought of the royal family or loved the nation, and as though all were slaves to self-love. That is saying far too much. It is precisely because we think of the royal family and worry about the future of the nation that we, your retainers, suffer in our hearts without sleeping a wink at night, are we not? I humbly ask that you give the matter some consideration.'

Futo: 'What you fellows call thinking of the royal family and the nation is, in short, for the sake of self-protection. When our nation is destroyed by some foreign enemy and the royal family perishes along with it just like Slavo, surely there will be no one left to protect my person alone. Since I reign as the lord and royal of the country, however minor it may be, you fellows likewise wish to utilize the royal family to gain various conveniences. The destruction of the royal family is precisely the destruction of you all. That is why you make a fuss, putting on loyal faces about the royal family and the nation! Ha, ha, ha, ha!'

To such a place, Alina was pardoned from thirty days of confinement, and with high spirits, without an attendant to announce him, he entered the Crown Prince's living quarters. As soon as the Crown Prince saw him,

Futo: 'Ah, Alina! You have come gladly. Thirty days of confinement must have been quite trying, I suppose.'

Alina, placing both hands upon the floor,

Alina: 'Your Highness remains ever unchanged, and to gaze upon your robust countenance is a boundless joy. During the thirty days of confinement, I gained the convenience of exchanging opinions closely with my father, which proved most exceptionally fortunate. Even my father, stubborn and perverse though he is, has repented of his past errors, has finally awakened to the trends of the times, has perceived the heart of Your Highness, and has sworn that henceforth, regarding whatever Your Highness undertakes, he will offer no interference.'

Futo: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha! Is that so? Well, that was a commendable achievement. Splendid, splendid indeed! Just now, a certain stubborn fellow came here and kept saying all sorts of idle things, so I was truly at my wit's end. However, since this Halutin is the man of distinguished merit who held you back from behind when your father made a commotion within the palace, you must praise him as well.'

と米搗き螽斯宜しく、此場を辞して帰り行く。

太『アハヽヽ、到頭、偽善者が一人、退却しよつた。サア之から余とお前と水入らずだ。何か面白い感想は無いかな』

アリ『ハイ、別に変つた感想も浮びませぬが、あの頑固爺奴、何と云つても目が醒めないのです。頑固党のハルチンが御前に控へて居りましたので、ワザとにあんな事云つて気を引いてみたので厶います。中々何うして何うして頑固爺の頭は駄目で厶いますよ』

太『アハヽヽ、お前も面白い芸当をうつ男だな。ハルチンが掌を返した様に賛成した時の可笑しさ。余も大に人情の機微に付いて研究をしたよ。時にアリナ、此画像を見よ。何時も此掛物から浮出して来て余に物を言ふやうだ。お前が監禁中は此画像を唯一の伴侶として、煩悶の焔を消してゐたのだ。実に麗しい者ぢやないか』

アリ『殿下、それ程スバール嬢がお気に召しましたか』

太『ウン、ズツと気に入つた。寝ても醒めてもスバール嬢の姿が吾目にちらつき、恥かし乍ら、硬骨無情の余も恋といふ曲者に捉はれたやうだ。何程画姿をみてゐても、殿下とも何とも云つてくれない。何とかしてモ一度実物に会つてみたいものだが、此頃の厳重な警戒線は、到底破る事は出来まい。之許りが実は煩悶の種だ。察してくれ』

アリ『殿下、それ程迄思召しますなら、私が彼れシャカンナを説き伏せ、スバール姫をタラハン市迄、迎へて来ませうか』

太『さうして貰へば有難いが、併し何うして殿中へ入れることが出来ようぞ』

アリ『到底今日の場合、殿中へお呼び寄せになる事はチツと困難で厶いませうが、日頃殿中へお出入を致す、生花の宗匠タールチンを、黄金の轡をはめて買収し、彼が離室にスバール嬢様をかくまわせ、隙を窺つて殿中を脱け出し、時々お会ひ遊ばして、御楽みなされては如何で厶いませうか』

太『そんなら能きに取計つてくれ。どうにも斯うにも、余は堪へ切れなくなつて来たのだ』

アリ『キツと目的を達して帰ります。どうか凱旋の時をお待ち下さいませ』
