Chapter 9: ダリヤの香 [1711]

ダリヤの香〔1711〕

§ 1

Dariya was confined in the secret room of the ship's hold, and had resolved that by the time this ship arrived at the port of Suga, she would die at the hands of Ali's brutality. And heroically, she composed farewell poems and the like, waiting for the arrival of her hour of death. It was Kōkusu who, sneaking with stealthy steps to the lock and twisting it open, entered there—Kōkusu, whom she had been abducted by when she went for a pleasure trip to a detached island riding in a small boat. Kōkusu lowered his voice and said,

Coke: 'Look here, Dariya-sama, even at the slowest, this ship will arrive at the port of Suga by tomorrow evening, so you will be killed tonight. How is it? I am thinking of lowering a small boat, putting you in it, rowing you to the detached island, and saving you, so it's a matter for consultation, but won't you become my wife?'

He coaxed her thus, breathing out the smelly, smelly breath of cheap doburoku from a face like a bullfrog that had missed its cry. Dariya arched her willow eyebrows, made a gesture as if brushing away a bee, and said,

Dariya: 'Ugh, how filthy. Daring to help me out now under the guise of kindness, making a debt of gratitude out of it, and saying such shameless things as asking me to become your wife—my, how could you utter such a thing! The fact that I have fallen into such a plight is entirely the work of you and your kind, isn't it? If anything, you are my enemy. Isn't it because of you that I am losing my life? No matter how much you may say your life is precious, is there any reason I would yield to the likes of you, who have such villainous, despicable, thief-like guts? Ugh, how filthy! Hurry up, now go away over there! My chest has started to heave with nausea. By the way, what is your name called? I want to hear it as a souvenir for the netherworld.'

Kōkusu: 'Well, you see, I am Kōkusu, famed as the right-hand man of Boss Ali. No matter what, life is our capital, so rather than coming up with such bad intentions, it would be better for you to listen to what I say. No matter how much of a famous flower you are, once you scatter from the twig, you are not worth even three mon. Your appearance is a rare beauty in the world. Lips of red tsubaki flowers, eyebrows of willow, eyes of sun and moon—isn't it a breathtaking style whether viewed vertically or horizontally? To let this famous flower scatter in vain is a great loss for the country. Nay, it is to lose a beauty of the world uselessly upon the earth. I lament for the sake of the world that you will scatter this very evening. How is it? It is a matter for consultation, but don't you have a mind to escape together with me? And how would it be if you became my wife and we lived together affectionately? No matter how much this face of mine looks like a hyottoko or is half-blind, an indescribable flavor is contained somewhere within it. Look at that dried squid. It has a withered, wrinkled-up, unsightly appearance, but when you chew it, it has a remarkably sweet taste. It contains an indescribable flavor. If you roast that for a moment over a distant fire, the flavor becomes even better. How is it? Do you have a mind to follow the will of this Kōkusu? Since you too are standing at the narrow straits of life, you should endure it even if my manliness is a little lacking. No matter what, endurance is gold, so I will not say anything bad. It is for your sake. In part, it is for my sake. Well, do you understand reason just a little?'

Dariya: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, just because you are called Kōkusu, what a black face you have! Your true calling is to become fuel for ships. To approach me, the heaven-born beauty Dariya-hime, and fawn over me as if courting a crucian carp—that is quite unbefitting your station. You had better give up in good time! Oh, what a disagreeable, petty scoundrel you are.'

Kōkusu: 'Hey, hey, Dariya-hime. Don't go jumping around so frantically like a caterpillar. If a person lacks affection, they are just like wood and stone. To be born specially as a human being and become a cold-blooded creature equal to wood and stone—you no longer have the qualifications of a human being. How would it be if you gave a human-like, woman-like reply?'

コークス『俺はな、アリー親分の片腕と聞えたるコークスといふ哥兄さまだ。何と云つても命が資本だから、そんな悪い了見を出さずに、俺の云ふ事を聞いた方が可からうぜ。何程名花だつて、梢から散りおつれば三文の価値もない。お前さまの容貌は天下に稀なる美貌だ。丹花の唇柳の眉日月の眼、縦からみても横から見ても惚々するスタイルぢやないか。此名花をムザムザと散らすのは国家の為に大なる損害だ。否天下の美人を可惜地上に失ふといふものだ。俺は天下の為に、お前の今晩散る事を惜むのだ。どうだ、物も相談だが、私と一緒に逃出す気はないか。そして私と夫婦になつて睦じう暮したら何うだい。何程此顔はヒヨツトコでも、メツカチでも、云ふにいはれぬ味が、どつかには含んでゐますよ。あの鯣をみなさい。干つからびた皺苦茶だらけ、みつともない姿をしてゐるが、しがんでみると随分甘い味がしますよ。何とも云へぬ風味が含まれてゐる。それを一寸遠火に焼くと、尚更味がよくなる。どうだい、此コークスの意に従ふ気はないかな。お前さまも命の瀬戸際に立つてゐるのだから、些と許り男が悪うても辛抱するのだな。何と云つても辛抱は金だから、悪い事は云はない。お前の為だ。一つは俺の為だ。いいか、些とは道理が分つたかい』

ダリヤ『ホヽヽヽヽ、いかにもコークスといふ丈で、黒い顔だこと。お前さまは舟の燃料になるのが天職だよ。天成の美人ダリヤ姫に向つて、恋の鮒のと、しなだれかかるのは身分不相応といふもの。可いかげんに断念したが可からうぞや。あたイケ好かない、ケチな野郎だな』

コー『オイオイ、ダリヤ姫。さう芋虫のやうにピンピンはねるものぢやない。人は愛情がなくては、木石も同様だ。折角人間に生れて、木石に等しい冷血漢になつちや、最早人間の資格はありませぬよ。お前さまも人間らしい。女らしい返答をしたら何うだい』

Dali: 'Ho ho ho ho, to speak like a human to humans and like a beast to beasts is the dao of Tenchi, is it not? That is the way of Kamunagara based on the principle of sauō. You there, do you still think of yourself as an ordinary Ningen?'

Coke: 'Oi, amateur. Do not speak such disrespectful things. While you are in a state where your head could fly off at any moment, what kind of outrageous gofutaku are you uttering? Transcending Ningen, you are the reincarnation of a three-ken-four-ken Gongen. If you misjudge me too much, it will not be to your advantage. If I send this single tetsubō as a visit to your side of the face, it will be a farewell to this world with a single cry of kyatsu. You will have to set out on the Shide journey to the Meido whether you like it or not. Oi, throw away such foolish thoughts, and just as I say, why don't we quietly slip away from koko? And whether you become my niyōbau or not is a matter for later. If you dilly-dally and lose your inochi, as I said just now, it will be a loss to the chijiyō, you see.'

Dali: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, thank you very much for your trouble. I consider it the utmost kuōei to be killed at the hands of Arisama. Even if I am to be killed, and even if I were not killed by a yokai bastard whose true identity is unknown—whether you are a Ningen, a tanuki, or an itachi—like you, it is painful enough to have you yammer on at me. Much less talk about becoming husband and wife or saving me, what kind of arrogance are you putting on? Come, come, go home quickly, go home quickly. If the captain finds you in a place like this, the base of your woven hat will fly into empty space.'

Coke: 'The truth of the matter is that it would bring me peace of mind to be killed together with you. Soon the captain will probably come to kill you, so won't you please die together with me? At the very least, taking that as a comfort to my heart, I intend to accompany you to the Meido no matter what.'

Dali: 'Ah, my head hurts, what a detestable bastard you are. Come, come, please leave quickly. Shoo, shoo, shoo. Come back the day before yesterday, come back the day before yesterday. If you keep dawdling, I will stick an incense stick for you.'

Coke: 'You really think of people as green snakes or spiders, don't you? Even if you stand a broom upside down and put a head covering on it, this Coke will not budge an inch, no matter what. You too, abandon your stubbornness to a reasonable degree and say yes with a single mouthful. That single word yes is the time when your fortune is decided.'

Dali: 'Who has any foolishness to say yes or no to the likes of you? Take a brief counsel with your own face. Or rather, it would be best if you took counsel with your wisdom. No matter how much you think you can pluck me, think of me as a pine flower blooming on a high peak, give up cleanly, and go do some kettle-boiling instead. Your face looks a lot like a monkey. Stop harbouring such unreasonable ambitions as a monkey trying to grasp the moon reflected in the water, and serve Captain-dono faithfully. If you do that, then when the New Year comes around again, you might even be given a slice or two of okubina mochi to eat, ho ho ho.'

Coke, having perhaps decided that debate was utterly useless and that direct action was the only way, unleashed the vigor of a fierce tiger, suddenly pinned Dali down right there on the spot, and attempted to execute 'ochiko, utsuko, hatena'. Dali squeezed out a voice of utmost desperation, crying out with all her remaining inochi while writhing her body, 'Arai, help me, help me.' Captain Ari, happening to pass in front of the confinement room at that moment, thought he heard a suspicious voice and put his hand on the door, only to find that someone must have entered already, as he pushed it wide open without even locking it. When he looked, there was the aforementioned scene. Ari flashed his kaiken and stabbed Coke from behind right through the spine, praying that the blade would pierce even the bones. Coke let out a shriek of 'atsu!', grasped at the empty air, vomited black blood right on the spot, and collapsed.

Ali: 'Dariyasama, it was a dangerous affair.'

Dariya: 'My, when I wondered who it might be, it turns out to be even the captain himself. You have come very well. This wretch tried to usurp your hard-earned pleasure and went as far as violence, but since you have come right in the nick of time, it is first of all most convenient for your sake, is it not? Because I am a person who must die by your hands, I was waiting and looking forward to you teasing me to death and taking your pleasure in it.'

Ali: 'Dariyasama, I too have turned over a new leaf. Please do not laugh at me as a coward. Let us go up onto the deck, look at the starlight, and enjoy ourselves.'

Dariya: 'This is cowardly indeed; why have you changed what you once resolved upon as easily as turning your hand over? I am opposed to it. Come now, just as you originally intended, kill me cleanly. Since I once resolved upon death, I should feel ashamed of my heart if I were to bend my initial resolve. If you will not kill me, then I shall commit suicide with my own hands and perish.'

Sooner than it could be said, she snatched away Ali's dagger and, at the very instant she tried to thrust it against her own throat, Ali panicked, leaped upon her hand, and snatched away the short sword. In that momentum, Ali severed one of his own thumbs. Dariya, startled, picked up that finger, attached it to Ali's hand, undid her own lower sash, and bound it around with a bandage in a whirl. Fresh blood flowed out and stained the copper-sheathed ship's bottom.

From the open doorway, the voice of a song, exceedingly fluent, came to be heard. Ali and Dariya, the two of them, strained their ears and listened to that song with longing.

The small ripples washing against the lake of Haru / On the side of the port of Suga / Selling medicines in all four directions / Wandering about hither and thither / Hoping to find the whereabouts of my younger sister / Praying to the gods, yet / Bathed in the awe-inspiring dews of grace / So as to be washed, the lake of Haru / Wandering hither and thither / No matter which ship I search / In the sadness of not seeing even the shadow of my beloved and dear younger sister / Crying out to heaven and lamenting to earth / Upon the deck of the Namikirimaru / As I wring out my tears

By the divine virtue of the Isoshikyo kami-tsukasa Umeikowake / Now embraced, my mother too / While simply praying for the peace of the afterlife / Of my younger sister whose whereabouts are unknown

Singing thus, with a sharp, ticking sound, he approaches this way. Dariya-hime, thinking to herself whose voice it could be that sounded vaguely familiar somewhere... while nursing Ali's injury, she had been tilting her ear to listen, but now firmly convinced that it was her older brother Irku, she let out a faint voice from inside the confinement room,

Dariya: 'Hark, are you not my older brother-sama? I am Dariya, you see.'

ダリ『モシ貴方はお兄イさまぢや厶いませぬか。妾はダリヤで厶いますが』

§ 4

Hearing this voice, Iruku rejoiced wildly, peered into the room through a crack in the door, and uttering an "Ah!", for a brief while was unable even to utter a sound.

Dali: 'My dear brother, when I heard your song just now, I learned that you did not abandon your foolish younger sister, but neglecting your family business, searched everywhere for my whereabouts. Not knowing you possessed such a kind heart, until now I thought of you like a half-brother, and please forgive the sins of me who treated you with neglect. Not knowing such a pure and beautiful heart, I harbored biased thoughts, bore you grudges, and always went on pleasure boat trips to the island just to relieve my melancholy. On the way back from one, retribution struck me, and I was captured by pirates, locked up in a place like this. Just as I was about to have my chastity violated by an evil scoundrel named Kōkusu who is being killed right here, I was saved by this Senchiyōsama. Please, brother, convey my deepest gratitude to Senchiyōsama.'

Iruku: 'Ah, is that so, it must have been a terrifying place. Oh, Senchiyōsama, my sister has been immensely indebted to you. I do not even know how to begin expressing my thanks, for I am so overwhelmed with gratitude that I know no words of thanks.'

Ari: 'Not at all, I did not have such a good heart as to save Dariya, but something mysterious came over me, and I simply felt inclined to save her. Your father was my enemy; thinking somehow I wanted to avenge my grudge, I finally became a pirate and waited for the opportune moment for revenge. However, my subordinate Kōkusu deceitfully captured and brought your younger sister, so I thought at least by killing this girl I could slightly console the soul of my late father, and I had decided on a plan to kill Dariyasama. But, deeply moved by her truly splendid resolve and her composed demeanor, now all my hatred and resentment have completely cleared away, and instead I have come to take Dariyasama's side. Not only that, as you see, when I had cut off my thumb and was suffering, through Dariyasama's nursing the throbbing finally stopped, and conversely it is I who must offer my thanks.'

Then, with tears, he recounted how his mother Anna had her chastity forcibly violated by Iruku's father and became his wife in tears, and how his father, resenting this, had thrown himself into Lake Haru and died. Listening to the entire story from beginning to start, Iruku, drawing a deep sigh, silently stared at the faces of the two. From this time on, Arī received the earnest divine teachings of Uaikō, overturned his evil mind, completely abandoned piracy, and became a guardian deity for passengers crossing the waters of this lake, his noble name being forever praised throughout the world.

Around the dusk of the following day, the Namikirimaru safely arrived and moored at the port of Suga.

原文（近代文語）

此声にイルクは狂喜し乍ら、ドアのすきから室内を覗き込み、アツと云つたきり少時は声さへ発し得なかつた。

ダリ『お兄いさま、最前のお歌を聞きますれば、不束な妹をすて給はず、家業を他所にして、妾の所在を捜してゐて下さつたさうですが、そんな親切なお心とは知らず、今迄腹違の兄さまの様に思ひ、おろそかに致してゐました妾の罪を許して下さい。そんな清い美はしいお心を知らず、僻根性を出して、お恨み申し、いつも憂はらしに島へ遊覧に行つた、その帰りに、冥罰が当つて、海賊に捉へられ、かやうな所に押込められ、ここに殺されてるコークスといふ悪性男に操を破られむとしてる所を、此船長さまに救はれた所で厶います。どうぞ兄さまから船長様へ、宜しく御礼を云つて下さいませ』

イルク『あゝさうであつたか、怖い所だつたな。イヤ船長さま、どうも妹が大い御世話になりました。何からお礼を申してよいやら、余り有難くつて、お礼の言葉も存じませぬ』

アリ『イヤ決して、私はダリヤを助けるやうな良い心は有つてゐなかつたのですが、何だか不思議なもので、つい助ける気になつたのですよ。貴方のお父さまは、私の仇敵、何とかして仇を打ちたいと思ひ、たうとう海賊になつて敵討の時機を狙つてゐたのです。然る処手下のコークスが貴方の妹を甘く生捕つて来てくれたので、せめては此娘を殺し、亡父の霊魂を聊か慰めたいと思ひ、ダリヤさまを殺す計画をきめたのですが、余り立派なお志と其落ついた挙動に感服し、今は全く恨も何もサラリと晴れて、却つてダリヤさまのお味方をするやうになつたのです。それのみならず、此通り、拇指を切落し、困つてゐた所、ダリヤさまの介抱で漸くウヅキも止まり、却て御礼は私の方から申上げねばならないのです』

と自分の母のアンナが、イルクの父に無理往生に操を破らせられ、泣きの涙で女房になつたことや、又自分の父が之を恨んでハルの湖に身を投げて死んだことなどを、涙と共に物語つた。イルクは始終の話を聞いて、深い吐息をつき乍ら黙然として二人の顔を見つめてゐた。之よりアリーは梅公の懇篤なる神の教を受け、悪心を翻し、海賊をサラリと止め、此湖水を渡航する船客の守り神となつて、其美名を永く世に謳はれた。

翌日の夕暮頃、波切丸は無事にスガの港へ横着けとなりにけり。
