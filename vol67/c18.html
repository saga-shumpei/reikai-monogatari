Chapter 18: Moon 下の露 [1720]

月下の露〔1720〕

§ 1

Shakanna burned down completely the buildings attached to the cave at the foot of the mountain of Taniguko, and taking Subaruhime and Koruton in train, he hid himself in Asakuradani, sending a lonely month or so in hiding from the world. The full moon of the fifteenth night shone high overhead. Shakanna and Subaruhime lay down inside the room, and already even the sound of snoring could be heard outside. Koruton was restless and unable to sleep, and as he watched the shadow of the moon reflected in the flow of the mountain stream, breaking playfully and flowing away, he fell into a mood of fascination. From there, stepping on and snapping dry twigs peki-peki from the mountain above, two youthful noblemen descended.

Kikōshi: 'Ah, I beg to ask you something, what kind of place is this?'

Koruton: 'This is the depths of the mountains. And it is the bank of a mountain stream. Since it is a place where no one has come since ancient times, how could it have a name or the like? Well, it will do if you just name it here. What on earth are you doing wandering around like this in these mountain depths at this time of night? And what is your name? Let me hear it.'

Kikōshi: 'Well, my name is Arina; the other person is my master, but we simply yearned for the scenery of the mountains and fields, and without knowing it, we strayed into such a place, and we had thought to settle down on top of this mountain and spend the night in the open field, but the voices of fierce beasts kept ringing out unceasingly. When I thought that we could not go on like this and looked toward the bottom of the valley, a faint firelight was flickering among the trees, so I thought this must surely be a house where people live. Come what may, guided by that fire, we managed to trace our way and arrived all the way up to this place, with the two of us, master and servant, caught in thorns and injuring our feet, or slipping and tumbling down. We earnestly beg you to grant us lodging for the night.'

Koruton: 'Since this is a woodcutter's hut, there is no one here besides me. And even if you ask me to let you stay and lodge for a night, there is no food to eat, nor are there any futons for bedding, so nothing at all can be done. Rather than becoming a burden in such a wretchedly dirty interior, go about three ri from here toward the east. There, they say, is a large cave. I sincerely and utterly beg to be excused.'

Arina: 'That may well be so, but today for three days and three nights, we have not taken decent food, our bodies are as exhausted as ropes, and saving our very lives, we have come guided by this light. To be refused by you here would make my master lose heart terribly. Even I no longer have the courage to take a single step further. Will you not please let us spend the night inside the woodcutter's hut?'

Koruton: 'Eh, what an obstinate man you are. If I say no, I say no no matter what. How could I ever let someone like you, a goblin of a Tengu, lodge here? Now, now, hurry up and go back home. Although it is a thatched hut, in short, to put it plainly, the house of this dwelling is the living place of my humble self's residence.'

Taishi: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha,' laughed with a feeble voice.

Koruton: 'Hey, you goblin of a Tengu, what is so amusing? It is not as if I have some particularly interesting livelihood or life; I am just a single woodman, working away diligently all by myself. Ah, do not give me that disgusting smile. Eh, since a month ago I haven't been able to do a single decent thing. Genjinbō came bustling along, and ever since then it has become such a tragic and miserable plight, and when I have run away in flight and come all the way here, hiding out of pessimism toward the world, the goblins of the mountain's Tengu slowly sniff it out and ask me to let them stay and lodge for a night—it is simply more than I can endure. Just for letting Genjinbō stay one night, such a huge commotion and uproar broke out. I am completely fed up with lodging guests. Please look elsewhere. I, myself, my humble self, am now going to retire and sleep. Farewell...'

Sooner said than done, he concealed his figure inside the woodcutter's hut and bolted the door firmly from within.

The two no longer had the courage to walk, and gazing up at the face of the moon while looking at the sky, they recited their lamentations.

Taishi: 'Though the Sango moon shines bright in the vast sky, The sky of my heart is shrouded in clouds. How shall I manage, having come relying on the fire's glow? Truly, the cold and heartless gaze of the woodcutter.'

Alina: 'Ah, I am about to perish in this deep mountain together with Taishi-no-kimi. My stomach is empty, my legs are weary and without strength, And I think only the moonlight gives me strength. If the master of this lodge has a heart, Surely he would let us rest in peace this night.'

Taishi: 'Ah, Alina, even my taut courage has vanished somewhere, and I have become utterly disheartened. What a merciless thing the world is.'

Ari: 'You are entirely right. The world nowadays is a vicious place where if one is seen as fallen, people kick them as they pass; thus human kindness is thin as paper, and they surely will not let us stay in this house either. Nevertheless, since we can no longer walk a single step, let us sleep beneath the moon while gazing at the flow of the mountain stream.'

Inside the dwelling came the voice of Shakanna,

Sha: 'Hey, Koruton, what were you talking to yourself about outside just now?'

Koru: 'Well, it is nothing like talking to yourself. Two Tengu guhin came along and started making a fuss, asking to stay the night and what not, so I refused and fended them off with all my might and utmost strength. They were rather persistent and stubborn fellows, indeed.'

Sha: 'Still, you were speaking with a human voice, weren't you? Surely they were not Tengu. Anyway, why don't you let them stay?'

Col: 'N-No way, how could I let such monstrous phantoms inside the house? Fear terrifies me, and my limbs are trembling and shaking. Please do not say such things.'

Sha: 'Wait, wait, I will go and check for myself. You cannot make sense of it.'

So saying, he pushed open the crude brushwood door and stepped outside, only to see two youths with fair complexions that rivaled the snow, their faces illuminated by the moon, already lying down.

Sha: 'I know not who you might be, but sleeping in a place like this will cause the night dew to fall upon you and is bad for your health. Though it is a wretched thatched hut, if you do not mind, why do you not stay here for the night?'

At this voice, the two rejoiced as if hearing the saving voice of an angel, slowly raised themselves, bowed politely, and in weary voices said,

Ari: 'I am a youth living in the city of Tarahan, but longing for the scenery of the mountains and fields, as I sought out scenic spots one after another, I lost my way. For three days now, without even eating bread, I wandered deep into this mountain, and finally recognizing a faint fire's glow, I made my way here. I asked your young man about various things, but I was severely refused, and so my lord and I, in our distress, had resigned ourselves and were just resting at your doorstep.'

Sha: 'What, a resident of the city of Tarahan? Hmm, well, anyway, please come in and stay. If you are hungry, there are a piece or two of bread, so I shall offer them to you. Fortunately, tea is also boiling nicely.'

The joy of the two was beyond comparison, and gratefully thanking him briefly for his kindness, they followed behind the master, squeezed through the entrance of the extremely small thatched hut, finally felt at ease, sat down on the straw mat spread over the wooden floor, and drew a sigh of relief.

Sha: 'Hey, Subaru, give some bread to these two gentlemen. And light a torch for them. Draw some tea as well.'

Subaru said, 'Yes,' and with a bashful look, took out some bread, placed it on his lovely, jewel-like palm, and held it out before the two. The two,

シャ『十年以前には、カラピン王様の左守と仕へてゐた、私はシャカンナだ。王妃の君が悪霊に誑惑され、日々残虐な行為を遊ばされ、国民の怨府となり、已にクーデター迄起らむとした。其危機を救はむ為に、女房と共に、死を冒して直諫し奉つた所、カラピン王様は大変に御立腹遊ばし、大刀を引抜いて、此左守を切りすてむと遊ばした刹那、吾女房が身代りとなつて、其場に命をおとし、私は城の裏門より逃出し、やうやう六才になつた娘を背に負うて、此山に逃込んで、時の至るを待つてゐたのだ。お前の側で、こんなことは言ひたくないが、お前の父のガンヂーは右守となつて仕へてゐたが、残虐非道の行為を遊ばす王妃様をおだて上げ、益々国難を招き、殆んど収拾す可らざる、国家は破目に陥つたのだ。今は私の後を襲ふて左守の司となり、国民に道を布いて、大変な人気ださうだ。それを聞いて自分も、少し許り国家の為に安心はしてゐるが、何とかしてガンヂーを戒め、モウ一層、善い人間になつて、国政をとらせたいと、それ許りを念じてゐるのだ』

§ 4

Ari: 'The more I hear, the more awestruck I am, and I cannot cease from wondering. As you say, I do not think my father is a man of overmuch goodness. Yet, because of the deep trust placed in him by Daiōsama, he has managed at last to unify a great many officials, and the Kokuka remains governed in a modest way without any excessively large Saudau. Nevertheless, saying that one never knows when a Jihen might break out, Wakagimisama and I are Shijiu lamenting it.'

Sha: 'Wakagimisama, though this is a dismal place, please make yourself comfortable. I have indeed been most Shitsurei.'

Tai: 'Nay, I am Ketsukō. Please do not trouble yourself over me.'

Sha: 'Yes, thank you very much. By the way, Arinasama, unlike your father, you are a youth of unmatched Seichiu. Please, since I have become a Yosutebito like this and can no longer participate in Kokusei, I ask that you assist Wakagimisama and conduct a splendid Seiji.'

Ali: 'For your various words of Goshinsetsu, my father must surely have caused you much trouble, yet without any anger toward him, you speak such kind Kotoba to me. Your impartial and selfless sincerity is truly more than I can thank you for.'

Sha: 'It seems the night has grown quite late, and Wakagimisama is likely tired as well, so please rest leisurely here tonight, and tomorrow we shall leisurely have you tell us your story. Come, Wakagimisama, please go to sleep.'

Tai: 'Ah, so you were Samori no Shakanna, who still leaves behind the renown of a loyal retainer! It appears Heaven has not yet abandoned the Tarahan country. What say you? Would you not think over it once more and have the mind to save the Tarahan country, which is now on the verge of perishing?'

Sha: 'You are too gracious, O words of the Crown Prince! Such aged bones are no longer of any use. Rather than that, please employ this Alina heavily, solidify the foundations of the royal family, and place the Kokuka in the safety of Mount Tai. That is at least my sole reliance for my old age.'

Tai: 'Ah, well in any case, I am too exhausted, so let me sleep. Forgive me, Shakanna.'

So saying, he lay down with a tumble, and was already snoring like thunder. Hearing this conversation, Koruton was startled, crept beneath the floorboards, became covered in spiderwebs, and spent the entire night without getting even a wink of sleep. The sound of air passing in and out of his ten noses and mouths sounded just like a blacksmith's bellows. The spring night advanced without mercy. The moon in the vast sky, brimming with a smile across its full face, purely illuminates this bizarre encounter of master and servant, whose fortunes are extraordinary in the extreme.
