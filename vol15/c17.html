Chapter 17: Feast in the Grotto [584]

窟の酒宴〔584〕

§ 1

Within the spacious hall, enclosed by rocky walls on all four sides, the sounds of string and bamboo instruments resonate clearly, while a great riotous dance of drinking and revelry unfolds.

Looking across the scene, one sees in the center a pile of countless Yaku hides, upon which sits the Hinodewake, possessing a countenance that appears hateful to behold, drinking deep draughts of a black, ink-like liquor while being served by a multitude of men and women.

The scores of men and women, all bearing countenances that suggest some peculiar vice, are dancing in a frenzy in their naked state, beating drums, blowing flutes, plucking bowstrings, and striking stones together, in a manner resembling the daylight procession of a hundred demons.

Hinodewake: 'Ah, the intoxication has taken hold most severely. How is it, all you people, will you not perform some interesting feat for me?'

Kurohime: 'Come now, everyone, let us perform a play of the subjugation of Susanōnomikoto. Chinsuke, you shall be Susanōnomikoto; I, Kurohime, shall play the role of plucking your beard, and Takahime shall play the role of pulling out your fingernails and toenails.'

Chinsuke: 'Oh, heavens, that is preposterous; who would ever take on such a role? I have no recollection of ever doing anything evil that would warrant having my nails pulled out.'

Kurohime: 'Do not lie, do not lie; you are a fellow who lights fires to your nails, thinking only of stingy deeds and tormenting others; you are a creature with nails as long as a hawk's, a destitute wretch; no matter what, this old hag shall corner you and pull them out, I tell you!'

Chinsuke: 'The one with long nails is none other than you, old woman. You are a greedy hag who frightens travelers by the riverbank and pulls out the very souls of their bodies. You should pull your own nails out first.'

Kurohime: 'You are a Chinsuke who talks back with such endless logic; oh, how hateful, shall I pinch your cheeks?'

With these words, she pinches Chinsuke's cheeks hard with her sharp, hawk-like claws.

'Ouch, ouch, it hurts, it hurts! Will you not let go?'

Kurohime: 'I will not let go, I will not let go; once a god has set her claws upon you, she shall never, ever let go. The time for letting go is the night of the Kōshin vigil; I am Kurohime, the sufferer who sees no evil, hears no evil, and speaks no evil. Eat your own backside, you pitch-black Chinsuke, you who know nothing of hardship.'

Chinsuke: 'Oh, oh, old woman, it hurts, it hurts, that is too much, my cheek is going to be torn off!'

Kurohime: 'It must hurt a little; until the blood flows, nay, until your cheek is torn off, I shall never, ever let go. You stunted, petty rascal, has your mind finally come to its senses? You are a fellow who again tries to outwit the Uranai-kyō. If by chance the fellows of the Sangō-kyō should appear, you are the sort who would immediately perform the swift technique of the swallow-return like a black, black swallow. If I, this Kurohime, glare at you with my black eyes, there shall be no mistake.'

Chōnsuke said, 'Pray, Takahime, I beseech you to offer a greeting.'

Chōnsuke cried, 'I shall repent, I shall repent! From this time forth, I shall not even utter the word of good; I shall uphold evil to the very end.'

Takahime said, 'Hush, Chōnsuke, what are you saying? This is the Oshihe of the Uranai-kyō, which is of the single path of good.'

Chōnsuke replied, 'Yes, yes, yes, it is because it is the Uranai-kyō that I speak of the reverse. When I say evil, it is good; when I say good, it is evil.'

Kurohime said, 'What a man of poor Gaten you are. There is a bottom to the bottom, an interior to the interior, and a reverse to the reverse. Oh, my hands have grown weary; I shall let you off for now. Nay, I must still extract the fat, but as this old woman's hands can no longer endure, I shall take Chiyotsuto and Itsupuku. Ehehehe.'

Chōnsuke said, 'I cannot understand a word of what you all are Otsushiya. I have become Satsupa, unable to discern whether you favor good or whether you favor evil. I pray you, tell me clearly whether it is good or whether it is evil, and I shall follow either path.'

Takahime said, 'The Oshihe of Kami is that which cannot be discerned as either good or evil. As a Ningen, how could you possibly distinguish Zenaku so clearly? You need only follow whatever Takahime says, saying "Yes, yes" and "Indeed, indeed," and obey in a state of Mekura Metsupafu Maujiyū.'

Chōnsuke cried, 'Ah, again and again, must you pinch my face, strike me, and pull out my nails before I can be brought to Kaishin? When it comes to that, ah, ah, what was it? I have forgotten, I have forgotten. Even if I wished to hide, I feel as though I have nowhere to go. Oh, how terrifying, oh, how Arigata, oh, how painful, oh, how it hurts!'

Kurohime said, 'As I thought, Kisama still has an attachment to the Sangō-kyō. Very well, though I am not Susanoo-no-Mikoto, I shall pull out every hair of your head, your beard, and your nails until not one remains. Come, Kyūsuke, bring me the nail puller.'

黒姫『貴様の言ふ事は何が何だか、テンと分らぬ、こらこら久助、様子は如何だ。女は何処に居る。』

久助『女どころの騒ぎですかい、たつた今、貴方等の頸は胴を離れますよ、何卒しつかりと用意をして下さい』

高姫『お前達は何を狼狽へ騒ぐのだ、昔の昔のさる昔の根つ本の天地の始まりから、何も彼も調べて調べて調べ上げた此方ぢや、仮令百億万の敵押し寄せ来るとも、此高姫のあらむ限りは大丈夫だ、しつかり致さぬか。してして女は何と致した』

此時門前に勇壮なる宣伝歌の声、四辺を轟かし響き来たりぬ。

高姫、黒姫、蠑螈別を始め、一同は俄に頭痛み胸は引裂く許り苦しくなつて、其場にドツと倒れたり。アヽ此結末は如何なり行くならむか。
