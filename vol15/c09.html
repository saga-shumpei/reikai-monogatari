Chapter 9: Wild Yam Soup [576]

薯蕷汁〔576〕

§ 1

In the beginning of the far-off Kamiyo of Chihaya-might, the ugly Mitama of Adaruhiko and Ebahime, who rebelled against the Kami's Kyō, did congeal and become the Yamataorochi and the Kinmaukiubi Hakumen Akuko, clouding the Iki of Tenchi; and Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime, appearing in the land of Tokoyo, dwelt within the body of Bankodaijin to disturb the world; and though they once submitted to the Mioshihe of the Kami and returned to Magokoro, it was but for a moment, and passing on one after another, they dwelt again within the bodies of Uralhiko and Uralhime; and the power of their Magawaza which disturbeth Tenchi having vanished, they withdrew into the depths of the Miyama of the Yasoshima and Yasokuni of the land of Tokoyo, and making the Mitama of men their abode, they established the teachings of Brahmanism and Uranai; and summoning many evil spirits to the depths of Kitayama where even birds pass not, and branding it the Uranai-kyō, they go forth once more to disturb the land, and most grave is that Magawaza.

Takahime, the mistress of the manor, having been rescued from peril by the Sendenshi Yasuhiko, Kunihiko, and Michihiko, expressed her gratitude and invited the party into the manor, insisting upon welcoming them to entertain them with great courtesy.

The party of five were ushered into a beautiful chamber, where they stretched their limbs and relaxed in a leisurely manner. Takahime appeared in this place and said:

"Well, well, the three Sendenshi, you have indeed rescued me from a perilous situation. This must surely be the arrangement of the Great Deity Daijizaiten, the principal object of worship of the Uranai-kyō in which I daily believe. The Kami possessed the Sendenshi of Misogi-kyō and rescued me from my peril. So to speak, you were merely used as the tools of the Kami, and within you, Daijizaiten-sama resideth. Truly, I thank you for your labor as tools. There is nothing special to offer, but please partake at your leisure."

Having thus spoken, she gradually vanished into the next room.

Kunihiko said, "What is this? Is that not a strange greeting? We, according to the doctrine of Misogi-kyō, regarding not an enemy as an enemy, risked our lives to save her. And yet, what is this? To say we were used as the tools of Daijizaiten and such insolent talk; it seems the spirit of sectarianism can never be cast off."

Michihiko said, "Now, now, does it not matter? We have only to make those fellows surrender to Misogi-kyō one by one. Let her say what she will, and the bottom of her heart will naturally become clear. One should not seize upon the ends of words and speak so fussily. Unless we engage in the salvation of all beings with a tolerance as vast as the ocean, if we raise our eyebrows and pant with indignation at such a thing, it may be said that we lack the worth not only of a Sendenshi but even of a believer."

Even as they were thus conversing, Takahime of Izen entered, causing three maidservants to carry barley rice piled in bowl-vessels with chipped rims, over which a viscous substance was thickly poured.

"Oh my, everyone, you must be truly weary. Being in a mountain home, we ought to offer some hospitality, and so barley rice with Tororo is prepared. Please eat plentifully of even such as this. Owing to this sudden Kiyakurai, I searched among numerous bowls, yet could find no more than three with chipped rims. As for the two Mitomo, though I told them softly a short while ago to eat, they made Kisaburō, so please be patient. Vessels such as might be served to you were barely found to the number of three. The rest are all magnificent, Kuwanzen and Muketsu vessels. Though it appears in this manner, there is rarely any Kizuka of phlegm or the like being Konnifu into it. Please, pray eat Takusan and Takusan. Oh-ho-ho-ho-ho!"

Thus speaking, with a repulsive laugh, she thrust out her white Deba, and with slow, heavy steps concealed her form once more within her original Ima.

Kunihiko said, "What an outrageous wretch who would Bujiyoku us to the uttermost! She wears a face just like the old woman at the Sanzu River, and the cord of my endurance bag hath broken at last!"

Saying this, he cast the barley rice with Tororo from the bowl-vessels all across the room. The room became slippery, turning completely into a mud-paddy of Tororo.

Again he cast the bowl-vessels for two persons into the adjacent room, and the adjacent room likewise became a mud-paddy of Tororo.

Kunihiko said, "Ah, now my stomach's bile hath settled! It would be even more amusing if that old hag were to slip and fall!"

Yasuhiko said, "Hey, Kunihiko, what a violent fellow thou art! How could a propagator of Anagyo ever buy a fight? Against whatever mighty enemy, we must to the end maintain non-resistance and conquer through sincerity. What a pitiful thing thou hast done! As of today I pronounce thy excommunication, so be aware of it!"

Kunihiko said, "That is precisely why Anagyo is called a spineless religion! Before thou excommunicatest me from thy side, severance of diplomatic relations shall be declared from mine!"

Thus becoming desperate, he tied his headband into a twist and forcefully stamped his feet again and again; startled by this raging and violent noise, Takahime and several men and women appeared upon this spot.

Takahime said, "Oh my, oh my, Propagation Envoys of Anagyo, I am truly impressed by your most magnificent doctrine. Words of splendour are spoken by your mouths, yet your deeds are even more admirable; while lodging in another's room, you scatter all over the room the feast prepared with all our hearts, kick down the sliding doors, break the wooden frames of the paper screens—nay, such violent outrage! By such truly magnificent doctrine, even we of Uranai-kyō are moved beyond measure, so struck dumb that our mouths open and cannot close. Exactly as the doctrine of Anagyo teaches, there is no place to step either hand or foot. Oh-ho-ho-ho-ho! Here, everyone, you had better take well into your bellies the magnificent doctrine of these Propagation Envoys!"
