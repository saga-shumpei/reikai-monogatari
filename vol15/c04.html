§ 1

Onikumohiko and his wife, deeply drunk on the fine wine, leapt into the moat in seeming agony, revealed their true form as the Serpent of Eight Heads and Eight Tails, raised up wind and cloud, and riding the clouds, hid themselves toward the sky of the land of Fusa. The valiant captains and fierce soldiers left behind, having unwittingly drunk the poisoned wine, had fallen into a dying state, and so dreadful was the scene of their groaning and agony that none could bear to look upon it. Futodama-no-Mikoto, pitying them, at once offered up a prayer to Heaven for the dispelling of the poison and their recovery, and when he drew the Futodama-gushi from his bosom and waved it left, right, and left, wondrous to tell, the divine virtue was at once made manifest. All of them regained their vigor and gathered before the Mikoto and the seven, choked with tears of gratitude; for the great mercy of their lives being spared they repented from the bottom of their hearts, pressing their palms together in utmost reverence, and leaping for joy, clapping their hands and lifting their feet, singing merry songs and dancing wildly, they regaled the company of missionaries.
