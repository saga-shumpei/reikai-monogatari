Chapter 23: The Eighth-Day Moon [590]

八日の月〔590〕

§ 1

Kotoyoriwakenomikoto bade farewell to Yashimanushi no Kami and the other Kami, and pledged to meet again in the days to come.

Tamahiko of the pure heart, / The shadows of Tsukihi part the Murakumo, / To the Yomo, Izuhiko, / The Miizu of Kami grows ever deeper, / High and wondrous is Kusuhiko, / The three together, bearing wide blessings, / Leaving behind the mansion, / In the absence of Kamususanō no Ōkami, / They bestrode horses of Senri, / The sound of the bits was valiant, / Gathering the Tazuna, they proceeded steadily, / Over the three slopes of the Mitama of Mizu, / The steeds of the heart and the steeds they rode, / Valiantly, with a rhythmic clatter, / Toward the lake of Biha, where the voice is cool, / They descended, pointing toward the Hamabe, / Over the Unabara where the waves are Nodoka, / Together with their steeds, into the Mifune, / They advanced, parting the waves, / Just then, from the Tōnan, / As they hoisted the Maho to the wind, / The Funaashi was swift, toward the foot of the mountain of Kōkasu, / To the port of Ki, / Here they laid the Mifune to rest, / And once again mounted their steeds, / Climbing the steep and rugged path, / With a rhythmic clatter, they ascended, / Matsushirohime, awaiting news of the Lord, / Prostrated herself before the presence of Kami, / The sincerity of her prayer, like the hempen pillar, / The propagandist of the teachings of Kami, / Beginning with Kotoyoriwake, / The divine ministers of the three Mitama, / Tamahiko, Izuhiko, and Kusuhiko, / Appeared at last in this place, / To the evergreen tree before the shrine, / They tethered their steeds and proceeded quietly, / Entering the precincts, / The four at once, before the great altar, / Bowed their heads in reverence.

The sound of their clapping was clear, / And the Kototama they proclaimed was soft, / Like the flowing of water, / Just then, prostrating before the altar, / Praying for the welfare of Sumerōkami, / Before the great altar of the Kami of Kuninotokotachi, / The Himegami offered her petitions, / Her voice, a cool Futo-norito, / Having finished proclaiming the Kototama clearly, / She descended the steps quietly, / Looking down at the foot of the stairs, what did she behold? / The propagandists, the messengers of Kami, / Of the one sincerity, the hempen pillar, / The party of Kotoyoriwake, / Perceiving them in this place, / She hurried down the steps, / And prostrated herself before the four, / Asking of the welfare of Kamususanō no Ōkami, / Her voice trembling with tears, / The darkness of her heart was piteous, / The propagandist Kotoyoriwake, / Spoke in detail of the tidings of Kamususanō no Ōkami, / Without concealment or hiding, / And Matsushirohime rejoiced exceedingly, / Ah, how grateful, how grateful, / For the blessings of Sumerōkami, / She ran up the steps once more, / Calming her heart, rejoicing in the deep blessings of the Sumekami, / To give thanks is indeed virtuous, / Appearing in the manifestation of Kami, / Distinguishing between good and evil, / Kaminao-bi who created this world, / Ōnao-bi of the wide heart, / In all things in this present world, / See and hear through the Naobi, / And rectify the perversities of the world.

The way of the Kami of the Izunome, / Shining across the countries of the Yomo, / Its merit is noble, / The tale of the ancient age of the Kami, / The fifteenth volume is now concluded, / In the spring of the Taishō year of Mizunoto-inu, / The eighth day of the third month of the lunar calendar, / The fourth day of the fourth month of the new calendar, / The words that reveal the age of the Kami, / The five hundred and ninetieth section is now complete, / Like the weaving of the loom, the Mizutsuki, / Lying horizontally, even the joints of the Kuretake, / Do not align in the seven-five meter, / From the heart of Katō-mura, / Fixing the heart, with the spirit of Chihayaburu, / The connection of the ancient age of the Kami, / Is here newly explained, / How noble is the birthday of today, / How auspicious is the birthday of today.

言依別命は、八島主の天使其他の天使と別れを告げ、後日の面会を約したまひぬ。

清き心の玉彦や 月日の影は叢雲を 四方に掻き分け厳彦や 神の御稜威も弥深く 高く奇しき楠彦の 広き恵を三人連れ 神素盞嗚の大神の 留守の館を後にして 千里の馬に跨がりつ 轡の音も勇ましく 手綱掻い繰りシトシトと 瑞の御魂の三つの坂 心の駒も乗る駒も いと勇ましくシヤンシヤンと 声も涼しき琵琶の湖 浜辺を指して下り行く 浪も長閑な海原を 駒諸共に船の中 浪を分けてぞ進みける 折から吹き来る東南の

風に真帆をば掲げつつ 船脚早くコウカスの 山の麓へ紀の港 此処に御船を横たへて 又もや駒に打ち乗りて さしもに嶮しき嶮道を シヤンコ　シヤンコと登りつつ 君の便りも松代姫 神の御前に平伏して 祈る誠も麻柱の 神の教の宣伝使 言依別を始めとし 玉彦厳彦楠彦の 三つの御魂の神司 此場に漸く現はれて 社の前の常磐木に 駒を繋ぎて静々と 境内さして進み入り 四人一度に大前に 頸根つきぬき畏まり

打つ拍手の音も清く 詔る言霊はさやさやと 水の流るる如くなり 折しも御前に額づきて 皇大神の身の上を 守らせたまへ国治立の 神の命の大前に 乞ひのみまつる姫神の 声も涼しき太祝詞 清しく言霊宣り終へて 静々御階段を下り来る 階下を見ればこは如何に 誠一つの麻柱の 神の使の宣伝使 言依別の一行が 此場にあるに心づき 慌てて御階段をかけ下り 四人の前に平伏して 神素盞嗚の大神の 御身の上は如何にぞと

問ふ言の葉も涙声 心の闇ぞ哀れなる 言依別の宣伝使 神素盞嗚の大神の 其消息を詳細に 包まず隠さず宣りつれば 松代の姫は雀躍りし 嗚呼有難し有難し 皇大神の御恵と 又もや御階段を駆け上り 心静めて皇神の 深き恵を嬉しみて 感謝するこそ殊勝なれ 神が表に現はれて 善と悪とを立て分ける 此世を造りし神直日 心も広き大直日 唯何事も現世は 直日に見直せ聞き直せ 世の曲事は宣り直す

三五教の神の道 四方の国々照り渡る 其功績ぞ尊けれ 古き神代の物語 十五の巻を述べ終へし 大正壬戌の春 陰暦弥生の上八日 新の四月の上四日 神代を明かす言の葉も 五百九十の節も今 緯機織なす瑞月が 横に臥しつつ呉竹の 節さへ合はぬ七五調 岩より加藤村肝の 心定めて千早振 古き神代の因縁を 此処に新に説き明かす 今日の生日ぞ尊けれ 今日の生日ぞ目出たけれ。
