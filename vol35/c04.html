Chapter 4: 村の入口 [968]

村の入口〔968〕

§ 1

Magokō, having approached the sanroku of Mukafutauge, caught the sound of noisy hitogoe, and stepping forward briskly while singing the Sendenka, was plotted against by Sankō's teshita at dusk, had his teashi bound, and was buried in the dochiu. Rescued from kiushiitsushiyō by Kurohime, he was now making his way with Torakō and party toward the fire kunimiyako. As they advanced to bring Sankō of Yakatamura to Kotomukeyaha, they went down the steep sakamichi, keeping time with ashibiyōshi and singing songs.

Guided by Kurohimetsukasa, Leaving Onokorojima behind, And coming to the island of Tsukushi, A vast country beyond thought, Mountains and rivers pure, fields green, Bananas, ichijiyuku, and other fruits, Delicious tree fruits in the ochikochi, Ripening on the tips of the kigi, Ah, is it paradise or Gokuraku, Or simply a divine park? Truly a delightful august realm. In the middle of Tsukushi-gatake, At that very moment when my legs gave way, Kurohimetsukasa smiled coldly, Prompting Fusakō and Yoshikō, And went up leaving me behind. Truly, when I think of it, anger rises, A demon hag who knows no pity, I held this in my chest until this very moment, And chanted 'Dokkoishyo' to Kurohimetsukasa.

With 'Untokoshyō' in my heart, Everyone, please take care, A giant centipede has appeared, If you get stung by its legs, it's unbearable, I was harboring resentment in my heart, Where on earth has the party of Kurohimetsukasa gone, A lonely journey with unknown whereabouts, Trusting the cries of birds and beasts as friends of the heart, Trampling and parting roots of rocks and trees, Dragging my heavy legs, With the fire kunimiyako as my aim, Just as I was advancing, Mistaking the path in Mukaiyama, In the forest at the foot of the toge, Very noisy human voices, Wondering what kind of incident had arisen, 'Untokodokkoi Dokkoishyo' Parting the thickets into a narrow path,

Peering and going to see, A multitude of men gathered together, Binding up three men and women, Jostling and picking quarrels saying this and that. This fellow, deceived by the deep schemes of an evil villain, Some shang was caught, And was about to be violated, Could this be left unseen? The divine way of Misoshirigi, Seeing the distress of people before one's eyes, One cannot pull back afterwards, A sense of chivalry born in one, Thinking that unfeeling things like what Kurohimetsukasa did cannot be done, Throwing one's liver to the winds, Hiding one's body in the shade of the trees, If I call out as Takayamahiko, Famed even in the fire kunimiyako,

Sankō, the giant snake at the beginning, and His henchmen sneered, And because they blustered for me to raise my face, Since a person who is not a god has no way of knowing Their deep schemes, I suddenly looked up at the sky, While wondering what on earth was hiding on top of this tree, Suddenly sand and gravel rained down, 'Untokodokkoi' caught in that stratagem Of blinding the eyes, My eyes grew dizzy and suddenly Squatting down on the great earth, The villains from behind Threw a rope around my neck, Felled me backward and bound my hands and feet Without regard for place, Suddenly digging a hole in the great earth, How cruel, the three of us Deep in the dochiu with 'Untokoshyō' Buried with our lives hanging by a thread.

To be buried in such a deep mountain interior Means it is already useless, Regretful though it is, right here, 'Untokodokkoi' is it that the thread of jewel-like life Is about to fade away? Ah, it cannot be helped, it cannot be helped, The sins of a previous life have come to recompense, In this country ten thousandri across the ocean, Am I to fall into such a plight? My wife whom I left behind in the country, Oyasu must surely be lamenting, While thus grieving in my heart, During the night that passed, My body began to grow light, Ah, how mysterious, how mysterious, Wondering what kind of god has appeared And is causing us to be saved, Before I could even think, 'Untokoshyō' Inside my mouth that had grown cold, Cold rain fell upon my face,

Surprised, pulling myself together, If I look all around me with a start, The unexpected Kurohime Peering into my face with a voice of astonishment, Ya ya, are you Magokō? Ma ma, it was good, it was good, The color of her face like seventy-six, Flourishing like a peony, Uttering gentle words 'Dokkoishyo', 'Untokodokkoi' that Kurohime Bestowed, 'Dokkoishyo', My, what is this again, 'Untokoshyō', The blowing about of a cool breeze, That such a harsh old hag Should save me Is an unprecedented great miracle, A matter that does not make sense, Having thought this far and come hither, 'Untokodokkoi' it is dangerous, Right there is a viper, 'Untokoshyō'

Ah Kannagara Kami Kami, / Kurohime, the messenger of the Kami, / Wandering to the foot of Mukōtoge mountain, / Came and saved our crisis. / I am a wicked fellow from the beginning, / Currying favor with the Great Serpent's foster child, / Plotting all sorts of mixed schemes, / Even rising to the position of Chief Staff Officer. / Makoto, who knows no human feeling, / Sent many foster children to Tora-kō-sama's absent home, / Tied up Ai no Kata, / Under the shade of the camphor trees where people do not pass, / Sitting upon a boulder, / Making me serve with a 'Heave-ho', / A tree stump is rolling right there, / Everyone, please be careful.

'Untokodotsukoi dotsukoishyo'

Torakō-sama and Ai-sama,

Surely you must be 'dotsukoishyo',

Feeling quite proud and pleased, no doubt.

Singing thus, they proceed onward. Things that advance come quickly, and already they draw near to the entrance of the village of Yakata. The mansion of Sankō the Orochi scarcely reveals its ridge amidst the densely wooded oak forest.
