野宿〔975〕

§ 1

Awakened and caused to sit up by the voice of Magokō, who was earnestly chanting the Norito in the dead of night, Aoi looked upon this figure of Magokō with wonder and came stepping briskly to his side. That was when he had already finished the Norito and breathed a sigh of relief.

Magokō-sama, what time might it be now? There still seems to be some space before daybreak, but it appears you have woken up terribly early.

Magokō gazed at Aoi's figure in the starlit night and started in astonishment, waving his hands about back and forth while saying:

Moshiboshi Aoi-sama, please stay right there just as you are. If you come near me, I am bound by the threads of love and end up in such a state that my body cannot move even a twitch. Please, I beg of you, right over there is quite sufficient. Do tell me whatever business you have. Even if you come closer, I have thoroughly reformed, so no matter how lovingly Aoi-sama casts amorous glances my way, Magokō's heart of iron and stone is, alas, not going to budge by lever or by pole. For a woman is a demon, you see. She is the sort of creature who wraps the men of the whole world around her little finger so that they cannot make a single bodily movement. Ah, now, now, Aoi-sama, if you draw close to me like that, I simply cannot bear it, for there is somehow an unbearable scent of you...

Thus he earnestly peddled the resale of the dream he had just seen.

Ho-ho-ho-ho, oh my, what a fearful expression on Magokō-sama; are you not a man yourself? Pull yourself together firmly for a moment.

Is this a re-examination of my dream from just now? You are saying the very same kind of thing, my word.

Now look here, Magokō-sama, did you dream or something? Truly, you are a carefree person.

Carefree indeed; I cannot sleep even a whit. Because you came out and scolded me and hauled me in, no matter what I do I simply cannot sleep. Please go to sleep without making mischief to a reasonable degree. If we do not rest our bodies a bit during tonight, our vigor will be drained away for tomorrow's Kototama battle, you see.

What an utterly incomprehensible person speaking sleep-talk; pull yourself together firmly.

So saying, Aoi pinched his cheek for a moment.

Ouch, ouch, it hurts. Ah, yet to be pinched by warm, soft, white, slender fingers is somehow a delightful thing. Moshiboshi Aoi-sama, not the slightest hesitation is necessary. I leave my face, my rear, and my arms entirely up to you, so please make yourself completely free with them.

Ho-ho-ho-ho, now look here, Magokō-sama, Torakō-sama is awake, you know. Please do not say such excessive things.

Ah-ah, it is becoming strange once more. It seems as though that transforming female from just a moment ago is about to be born again from the depths of my belly... Ah, Kannagara Kamunakara samakiwamasu. I humbly pray that the Great Deities of Heaven and Earth will blow away and clear away this Magokō's clinging attachment to love, just as the wind of Shinato blows away the eightfold clouds of heaven.

Torakō suddenly awoke at this talk and said:

Aoi, what are you doing over there? If you do not go to sleep quickly, we will have to make great exertions tomorrow.

Magokō-sama is making foolish remarks and causing a fuss, so no matter what I do I cannot sleep.

Magokō-sama, will you not go to sleep quickly?

Torakō pressed him firmly.

I do not quite know why, but whether it is a dream or reality Aoi-sama has come to my side twice already, pulling me, pinching me, and acting playfully, so I cannot sleep a single bit. If you do not give a sermon to your wife for me, Torakō-sama, this handsome man Magokō will truly be troubled.

A-ha-ha-ha, what a carefree man you are. ...Now, Aoi, so that Magokō-sama falls sound asleep, let him drink your milk or sing a lullaby and put him to sleep. The rest of us want to sleep for another spell, you see.
