Chapter 15: 救の Jewel [979]

救の玉〔979〕

§ 1

Aio stands up and sings the propagation song.

Having thus simply launched the Kototama, the hitherto fierce wind abated its force, the roaring of the beasts gradually grew lower and departed far away, and the various monsters floating on the lake's surface diminished their forms hour by hour; yet since they did not easily come to total destruction, Torakō, wearing a twisted headband, stamping and roaring with majestic valor, raised his loud voice and refreshingly fired the Kototama.

Thus he finished singing. Yet somehow, the lake's monsters changed their shapes in various ways, and countless numbers such as tako-nyūdō, magaki, and yotsume-kozō floated forth; fireball monsters in the shape of tadpoles gathered together like embankments with a roar numbering in the hundreds and thousands, surrounding the four men and women ten and twentyfold. The four were struck in their noses by an indescribably raw and strange stench, their chests choked, their stomachs pained, their eyes blinded, and they grew so exhausted that now they could do nothing whatsoever.

Before they knew it, the night cleared up brightly; the monsters emerging from the lake's surface diminished one by one and two by two, and when the sunlight illuminated the earth, the monsters' forms vanished completely, leaving only the azure waves drifting leisurely across the surface of the lake.

§ 2

The four sat down properly on the bank of the pond, talking over the strange apparition of the previous night, washing their hands and purifying their bodies in the water of the lake, and just as they finished chanting the Amatsunorito, there was a Sendenshi who parted the thicket of trees and appeared at this place. When looking closely, it was Tamaharuwakenomikoto. Magokō leaped for joy and cried out with great delight,

Magokō: 'Well, well, Tamaharuwake-sama, we have heard your voice countless times, but this is the very first time we have laid eyes on you; thank you so much for coming... Hey you three, this is the renowned Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, Tamaharuwake-san.'

Aai and Sankō, overcome with tears of joy, knelt before Tamaharuwake and expressed their deep reverence.

Tamaharuwake: 'I am Tamaharuwake. Everyone, your Kototama rolled remarkably well. The deity of the Orochi must have shown you quite a variety of interesting tricks, didn't it?'

Magokō: 'I am Torakō. Were you aware of the scene from last night?'

Tamaharuwake: 'Yes, I dashed down Hakusan Pass in a single breath, went on ahead, hid myself in this forest, and amusingly watched your Kototama battle. It came very close to being extremely dangerous.'

Magokō: 'Just a little bit more and we would have been stuck to by the fire-ball demon, but strangely enough, it came within about three shaku and from then on simply would not draw any closer. With all that momentum, why on earth was it unable to close in by another two or three shaku?'

Tamaharuwake: 'It came right up to the outer edge of your spiritual garments. Frightened by the majesty of the spiritual garments, it could not draw any closer than that. And that fellow the Orochi seemed to be plotting even greater schemes, but I was performing Chinkon from the shade of these trees toward the surface of the lake. Because of that, the fierce subordinates of the Orochi, along with wild beasts such as tigers, lions, bears, and wolves running rampant in this mountain forest, had no way to inflict harm upon you and all fled far away. And I hear that Magokō-sama has become a splendid Sendenshi called Magokōwake, is that right?'

Magokō: 'Yes, well, I was just asked temporarily and tried it out. However, since things don't go very smoothly at all, I was thoroughly impressed by how tough being a Sendenshi is. Taking advantage of the fortune of Tamaharuwake-sama's arrival, I shall return the post of Sendenshi of Magokōwake right now. Please accept it back. Oh my, it was truly hard work.'

Tamaharuwake: 'Ha ha ha ha, who on earth appointed you as a Sendenshi? Did you borrow it temporarily even from Kurohime-sama?'

Magokō: 'No, not at all; since this is a republic, I became a Sendenshi pushed forward as a result of a unanimous national election. Oh really, I went through some very thin times.'

Tamaharuwake: 'Now everyone, staying here will get us nowhere. Since there are three or four large floating islands in this lake, let us go over to them to rest, cross over to those islands this evening, open up a single Kototama battle, and fundamentally pacify and harmonize the deity of the Orochi. Everyone, let us go.'

As he took the lead and followed along the lakeshore, the four breathed sighs of relief, their vigor suddenly renewed, and they followed closely after him.
