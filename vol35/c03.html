Chapter 3: 進隊詩 [967]

進隊詩〔967〕

§ 1

Torakō followed the mountain path while keeping time with his feet, and began to chant in a quick, trotting run.

'Untokodotsukoi' the night has broken The forest of Mukafu has cleared The roaring voices of wolves, lions, and bears Have also grown still Birds sing here and there The eastern sky is tinged with madder The sun god rising over Toyosaka Illuminates the bodies of us The party following the mountain path 'Untokodotsukoi' at your feet Let everyone take heed Kurohime-sama is elderly Her legs especially must be heavy Large rolling stones Are tumbling down this slope If you should carelessly become a rolling stone Sliding down with a rattling clatter You are bound to suffer unexpected injury Taking Kami as our strength, making the three-five Path of sincerity our staff,

Venomous snakes and centipedes crawl about back and forth Parting the grassy path we advance Ah, Kamunagara kamunagara By the protective grace of the precious Kami May our party of nine together Reach Yakatamura without any misfortune Safely at the mansion prepared by The great serpent Sankō! Without knowing that back at my home Sankō Has led many henchmen To crowd around, mistreating my wife and My younger sister Ume, And taking them away deep into the mountains To force upon them an unreasonable demand, 'Untokodotsukoi dotsukoishyo' Following behind Kurohime-sama Who set out for the mansion of Takehi, Climbing up the steep slope,

The Kami of Kenkokuwake was invited To the banquet of celebration And served sweet sake of the water of life Until fully content with 'Dotsukoi' With the intoxication swirling and swaying unsteadily, Leading Shin, Kyu, and Hachi, Descending the slope and coming Into the deep valley road of Hinokuni, When we looked, the serpent's henchman Rokukō Leading many henchmen, Armed with clubs and swords, Tying his head-band tightly with a ring, Tucking up his skirts, 'Dotsukoishyo', Was waiting pompously. Even Torakō was taken aback, And while praying to Kami in his heart, Pulling himself together with a bold look and a roar, Picking up whatever pieces of wood were at hand, And aiming straight for the center of the swarming enemy 'Dotsukoi', he thrust his way in,

'Yattokodotsukoi', it is dangerous Where on earth has the initial momentum gone? Chased like a cat, 'Dotsukoishyo' Like a rat, 'Untokoshyo' Shrinking upward as Rokukō Takes to his heels in a single dash, His underlings scattering with 'Yattokoshyo', Losing their wits, 'Untokodotsukoi', escaping and dispersing Like a spider's web scattered abroad! Ah, Kamunagara kamunagara With the profound mercy of Kami We were saved from perilous places, And when I turn back and call the names of Tamakō, Shin, Kyu, and Hachi, How comical it is that the four hiding in the shade of the trees, With their faces covered in spiderwebs, Their legs trembling and looking terrified, Appear before us. Be that as it may, even for a single moment

If I dash off like an Idaten runner To hurry back to my home And inquire into the safety of Ao, With momentum to spare, 'Untokoshyo', Unexpectedly and unknowingly plunging straight Into the mountain path of the Mukafu Pass, Though I think of turning back, Ah, wait a while, in the three-five Teachings of Kami there is no such thing as the two characters Of retreat, 'Untokodotsukoi', Calling to mind those words we were taught, I pressed onward, Seeing a dangerous log bridge, Flying across it like a bird, At the very moment of climbing the slope, How wondrous that Kurohime, Whom I never expected, came to this place Caring for Ao and Ume, Together with Magokō and Kanekō! Ah, is it Tora-sama or Kurohime?

Is it Ao, Ume, or Mago-sama? Is it the serpent's henchman Kanekō? Is it Torakō's own kinsman Shinkō? Or could it be Kyuhiko or Hachikō? To meet in such a miraculous place, This too is surely the flawless Divine arrangement of Kami. There is nothing quite so auspicious as this. So saying, everyone joined both hands by the roadside And praised the divine name of the Kami of Heaven and Earth, While the voices chanting In thanksgiving and prayer the Grand Futonori Resounded purely into the mid-heavens as day broke. Ah, Kamunagara kamunagara Receiving the divine favor, Three men and women having been saved from perilous places by Kurohime, In the joy of returning safely, Torakō-sama also leaped like a sparrow.
