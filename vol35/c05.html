§ 1

Kurohime and her party of nine finally arrived as the three senior retainers of the great serpent appeared at the front gate. The two or three young fellows who had been acting as gatekeepers, starting with the three men and women buried deep in the forest, and Torakō—who had been terrified even just hearing their names—were completely struck dumb, gazing with pale faces and vacant looks as the party arrived leading the henchmen and even accompanied by the Sendenshi.

Kanekō assumed a bizarre posture, purposely raised his voice into a high-pitched whine, and cried:

Ho-i, ho-i! Genkō, you rascal, Kanekō-sama has returned! A-ai-sama has also come back from the dead, rising from the grave, and we've come to claim your necks, so you'd better be prepared. My body died because I felt such bitter resentment, but my spirit has returned to life, and since I've come to thank you for your words, hurry up and let the boss of the three senior retainers, along with Yosakō, Tokukō, and my sworn brothers Taka and Kanu, know all about this! Hiyu-doro-doro-doro...

Even though the sun of the declining afternoon was still blazing down relentlessly, he imitated the ghosts right in front of the gate, thrusting both hands forward with a whoosh two or three times, moving back and forth a few yards to the left and right while wearing a puckered, grimacing face.

Genkō and two other henchmen were startled and scurried off to hastily report to Yosakō, Tokukō, and the others, who were drinking heavily and rambling drunkenly in the banquet room. Knowing the interior of the mansion like the back of his hand, Kanekō led the group straight into the banquet hall without a shred of hesitation or restraint.

The comical sight of Yosakō and Tokukō losing their backbone and panicking around was so funny that it took sheer willpower not to burst out laughing. Kanekō put on a fake voice once more and said:

Ho-i, ho-i, Yosaku, you bastard. You forgot the bond of brotherhood, suddenly slipped a rope around my neck, bound my hands and feet, and oh so ruthlessly buried me alive. Look at this, my neck has grown this long!

As he spoke, he forcibly stretched his neck out, rolled his eyes wildly, stuck his tongue out as far as it would go, let both hands dangle eerie and limp in front of him, and trembled violently as he declared:

Hiyu-doro-doro-doro...

Yosakō, with his legs completely paralyzed, pressed his palms together and pleaded:

Ya, Big Brother, please forgive me! I certainly wasn't the one who killed you. It was the boss's orders. As you well know, I begged him to spare you and let you go, but since he wouldn't listen to me no matter what, I had no choice but to do that pitiful thing. Please, have mercy on me! In return, I will definitely pray for your peace in the next world.

The comical sight of him turning deathly pale, pressing his palms together, and trembling was too much. Torakō burst out laughing and shouted:

Aha-ha-ha-ha! Hey, Yosaku and Toku, both of you, pull yourselves together a bit, will you? We're not ghosts or anything of the sort. We're genuine, lively men among men—Tora of Takenomura. You were kind enough to take such good care of A-ai for us, and since we're so deeply grateful, we've come to thank you. Even my little sister Oume was kindly looked after—you guys are truly heartless. Now, just calm your minds and listen to what Torakō-sama has to say!

My, my, was it you, the boss of Takenomura? Please let bygones be bygones and wash all past troubles away, and spare my life, I beg of you!

Hey, Yosaku and Toku, I'd really love to see what you two did with your testicles just once.

Well, well, showing them to you would be easy enough, but from sheer fright, they seem to have moved out and relocated somewhere else... The testicles themselves are quite clever, you see. Figuring that if they got dragged around by Torakō-sama, even their parent born in the same year would suffer, they neatly hid themselves away. You really are a clever fellow, aren't you? Ever since you were young, with that wrinkled face of yours, I always thought you were an unlikable bastard, but at a time like this, it certainly comes in handy. They've always been limp and flaccid, never mustering an ounce of strength, but it seems they come in handy when the chips are down. Even my adamantine jewel has gone off somewhere. Did it carry them off and run away, I wonder?
