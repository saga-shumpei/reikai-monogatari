Chapter 12: 自称 Kami 司 [976]

自称神司〔976〕

§ 1

Having finally managed to reach the summit of Shirayamatauge, the four men and women, while being blown by the gale of the peaks, wipe their sweat and comfortably gaze around at the surrounding mountains and fields as they try to take a short rest.

Far away toward the Tōhoku, something shining white can be seen. It is a part of the lake of Suppon, filled with deep memories where Harukō and Otō were swallowed by Orochi.

As if Sankō cannot help but feel a sudden pang of grief when that lake catches his eye,

Sankō: 'Everyone, away over yonder there is a place shining faintly white. That is for me a place unforgettable whether I sleep or wake, the old battlefield of my parents......'

Having said thus, he hangs his head and sheds tears.

Magokō: 'Oh come on, is it just such a tiny lake? Even if it were filled to the brim, it's nothing much to speak of. Since we are together with Kamisama this time, we'll be fine. There shouldn't be anything like getting swallowed easily.'

Sankō: 'No, no, it is not a small lake such as you see now. It is merely that its part is visible, being enveloped by the mountains. It is a vast lake so immense that the eye cannot even reach its bounds. No matter what anyone says, it is the water source of the Nile River, so it is a tremendous thing. Everyone, let us tighten our bellybands well from here on.'

Magokō: 'At a time like this, it would be terribly convenient if there were just one true missionary, wouldn't it.'

Torakō: 'Magokō-sama, weren't you a missionary?'

Magokō: 'Since I am merely an attendant to a missionary, I am utterly lacking in wit, alas. However, even if there is no such title, if one has sincerity in the heart, one will surely be able to pacify and harmonize with Orochi sufficiently. Because the name is the guest of the reality, merely having the pen name of a missionary will never enable one to do the work. First of all, if you feel insecure, the three of you please do something to elect this Magokō as a missionary. If you do that, since I will perform great activities where name and reality meet......'

Torakō: 'Because the name is the master of the reality, no matter what, we cannot go on without a name. It would be boring if it turned out to be an unnamed battle, you see.'

Magokō: 'How grateful I am, now then, it is a general election. Everyone has the right to vote, so please cast your votes quickly. However, since it is a secret ballot, I ask you to vote with that thought in mind.'

Torakō: 'Regrettably, we have neither voting paper nor ballot box, so what should we do?'

Magokō: 'First, let us divide the constituencies into the first district, the second district, and the third district, and since I am standing as a candidate, please, verbal voting is also fine, so I ask for the early start of the election.'

Torakō: 'How much will you put down for a single vote? One hundred yen or so would probably be cheap. If you thought of running for the House of Representatives for just a moment now, it would take at least fifty thousand yen, and at most one hundred thousand yen, you see.'

Magokō: 'You can judge the goods upon your return; if you vote and see that it is of no worth, there is no hindrance even if you cancel it. If that is the case, it would be better to just state it with your voices without any voting at all. Things will be settled more simply and quickly......'

Torakō: 'Do not utter such an unthinkable thing as omitting the vote. In that case, I, Torakō, shall declare my candidacy for missionary, so everyone, please bestow upon me your precious single vote. With only one candidate, there are no competitors, and the election lacks all zest. Everyone, please grant me your votes. I shall ask for one vote from my relative, Ai-sama. If I do that, with one more vote, three against one, it will be a grand victory......'

Magokō: 'This is troublesome, even if Sankō were to vote for you, both would be tied in score. What will you do if it turns out that way?'

Torakō: 'Shall we decide the elder to be the winner?'

Magokō: 'If we do that, Torakō-sama, how old are you?'

Torakō: 'It seems I am two or three years older than you.'

Magokō: 'This is troublesome, if so, is this a defeat without fighting? Ah, how regrettable, I shall make a flamboyant debut when there is another general election or dissolution. This time let us abandon the thought. If so, it is Torakō-sama's solo stage; whether Sankō-sama abstains or I abstain, victory is without a doubt.'

虎公『年長者を当選者ときめませうかい』

孫公『さうすると、虎公さまは幾つですか』

虎公『あなたよりも二三年古いやうです』

孫公『困つたなア、さうすると戦はずして敗北かなア。エヽ残念な、又次期の総選挙か解散があつた時に華々しく名乗つて出る事にしよう。今度は断念致しませう。さうすると虎公さまの一人舞台だ、仮令三公さまが棄権しようが、私が棄権しようが、当選疑なしといふものだ』

Sankō: 'Now, Magokō-sama, stand firm in the electoral arena and fight. Does not Ananai-kyō contain no such two characters as retreat? Though I lack in ability, I shall vote for you. If you do so, victory in the election with a great majority is beyond a doubt. Even if there is only one person, since I am Sankō, three votes are quite safe, ha ha ha!'

Torakō: 'The whole thing is cancelled. Magokō-sama, become a temporary sendenshi and guide us. Since I shall withdraw myself...'

Magokō: 'Yay, how grateful I am! If so, from this very moment I am Magokōwakenomikoto, sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, so I beg for your intention in that resolve. To celebrate having become a sendenshi, let us perform a kototamasen here upon this mountain. Instead of a political manifesto, I shall chant a senken happyō sendenka, so I ask for your careful listening...'

Now the Kami appear at the front, And separate the wise from the foolish. Things are not what they seem to the eye, Scolded as a nuisance by the black-hime-tsukasa, Morning and evening grumbled at, And called a foolish man, This Magokō-shi too, for no matter how long, Cannot remain hesitating and undecided. In the innermost depths of the heart, There is a wisdom invisible to men. The light of wisdom is something that, Hidden, will never fade away. When there is a sharp awl inside a bag, It breaks through sharply, And its pointed tip reveals itself. Thinking it was perhaps the Hakusan Pass, I came, but what manner of wind's blowing is this? An enormous serpent of the Lake of the Moon and the Soft-Shelled Turtle Has appeared, Eating Sankō's parents as its thanks, It followed along from Jitentōshima. Magokō-shi has been chosen, A sendenshi beyond all expectation. There is nothing as happy as this! O Sankō-sama and Tora-sama, Whether you are Kami or ryūjin, Or Konohanahime, though I know not, You are fellows whose mihama shine exceedingly. The black hime of Ananai-kyō, Recognizing this divine power of Magokō Which she had been unable to see through, With the momentum of unanimous consent, Elected him—what a splendid fellow! If there are many electors like you In the world, The evil governance of body-master-spirit-servant Will surely be eradicated utterly. Saints, sages, and philosophers, Buried in the fields forever, In the present age where heads cannot be raised, Oh, what manner of good fortune is this again? The sendenshi Magokōwake, To manifest divine power is from now on! Ah, kannagara kami-kami, Kami are with us indeed. We are the children of Kami, the palaces of Kami. Taking the lives of Sankō's parents, The great serpent lurking in the soft-shelled turtle's lake, As a sendenshi endowed To the utmost with boundless divine power, Magokōwake has appeared, Designating Sankō and Torakō as my loving ones And as attendant Kami, To press forward is indeed valiant! Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the great earth should sink, I am a sendenshi who cannot resign! Once grasped, I will not let go. That attachment is just like a soft-shelled turtle Having clamped its jaws on, No matter what, come what may, I will not let go! The plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds Opening all at once, the evergreen Pine trees of the age of Kami have come round, And Magokō, fallen low in the world, Upon this very peak of Hakusan Sticking out into the clouds, Valiantly, as a sendenshi who is the messenger of Kami, Ever since I have been appointed, Even to the remotest corners of the fields and mountains, To the wild fields where fierce wolves, lions, and bears Run mad, without shrinking, Exerting my heart, exerting my body, Not to speak of the island of Tsukushi, To the land of Tokoyo and the depths of the islands of Takasago, Let us ride right in, And make the precious divine light Shine across brightly before our very eyes! Ah, kannagara kami-kami, May your august spirits be blessed and prosperous!'

From somewhere out of the midair, the voice of the sendenshi's song is heard coming. The four people, drawing their eyebrows together in doubt, listen with strained ears.

The Kami appear at the front, And separate the good from the evil. Though it looks like good, it is an evil spirit, Though it looks like evil, it is a soul of good. With a twisted heart and darkened wisdom, Unable to see an inch ahead, The mihama of ordinary mortals gather together, Electing the precious sendenshi of Kami— What kind of business is this? The heart of Kami and the human heart of this fleeting world are like the reverse and obverse. No matter how one wins an election And obtains the laurel crown, Unless Kami permits it, the true power Of sincerity will never come forth! Ah, kannagara kami-kami, Even the heart of Kami upon this very peak Of Hakusan, in various ways Grieving the heart and hurting the breast, The crooked deity lurking in the lake water—

Though you fight against that fierce force / And carry out lawless elections, / Since it has not a particle of effect, / Let all of you take heart anew / And quickly return to sincerity.

The boss of the village of Yakata, / And Sankō who swung his influence, / Torakō of the village of Takeno, / Even dear Ai born of noble stock, / Being intoxicated with the teachings of Kami, / Now up upon the mountain completely / Have let their mitama degenerate.

While being in this deplorable state, / To attempt a Kototama warfare / Facing the pond of the soft-shelled turtle where the Orochi lurks / Shows a lack of self-knowledge. / Even reckless kefukaku / Now finding their lives dear to them / Must be blowing such coward's tunes— / Not just coward's tunes, but a wind of timidity

Is blowing upon this summit. / Men who from morning till night, haughtily / Raising their shoulders and elbows, swinging their big arms, / Went around blowing their horns— / Why, losing your senses, to Magokō, / Whose affection Kurohime has cast off, / Do you cowardly and meanly make requests? / You should place your hands upon your breasts a little / And try to rethink it. / I am Tamajiwake-tsukasa, / And on behalf of Kami to you all / I sincerely give this warning. / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, / Mitama-sachihaimashimaseyo.'

With just that said, the voice stopped dead.

The four men sought the source of the voice and peered down into the valley here and there, but not even the shadow of anyone looking like a person was to be seen about there. Here the party of four descended the steep slope of Hakusan Pass heading northeast.
