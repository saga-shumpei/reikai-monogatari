Chapter 16: 浮 Island の Flower [980]

浮島の花〔980〕

§ 1

Extending twenty ri from east to west and thirty ri from north to south, within this lake are three floating islands that change their positions from moment to moment, drifting like duckweed in a wondrous manner.

Tamaharuwake opens his golden fan, and while fanning and beckoning, wishes to draw this island close.

Tamaharuwake: 'O gods residing upon the floating island, if you have hearts, come draw near and I shall revere you. This island floating lightly on the surface of the water, whose dwelling place of the gods might it be? I shall save and illuminate with the divine majesty of Kami the crooked kami hiding secretly in the island's shadow. Both the manifest world, the hidden world, and the world of Kami are the realm ruled by Sumeōkami. From the breath of Kuniharutachi no Mikoto, does this island manifest, alas? To the limit of his precious life, Tamaharuwake has come hither in order to save you. The life of Tamaharuwake no Kami came inheriting the divine breath of Susanō. Deep is the sacred vow of Kamunagara, so surely I shall save all manner of crookedness. Oguchi, orochi, torō, and shishi, kuma too, are children born from the water and fire of Kami. I have now received the divine word of Kami and have come hither to save you. The teaching of the thirty-five deities saves the world, and is Tamaharuwake of absolute sincerity. My soul shines like a wish-granting jewel, illuminating even to the bottom of the sea. The righteous path of Kami is to make even the ugly kami lurking and hiding in the lake bottom float up and save them. Even the human world, where rising and sinking come alternately, will flourish eternally if entrusted to Kami. Though the bottom of Lake Suppon be deep, it is not equal to the depth of the divine grace. Come now, let us open and behold the breath of the strict Kototama of Tamaharuwake. In the age that opens up, may the end of the fan be broad and prosper, flourish even to the bottom of the lake. That the grandson of the teaching of the thirty-five deities acted fraudulently as an official of Kami. If it were an age without falsehood, Kami would not have grieved to this extent. Ah, Kami, universally save all people, even to the descendants of the orochi at the bottom of the lake. Though the fury of ogres, orochi, and ugly ones be strong, I shall illuminate them with the soul of Tamaharuwake. Magakami is also the spirit of Sumeōkami; how could one treat it as a trivial dream? When I come and look, here four children of Kami are chanting the Kototama with all their might. The cloudiness of the Kototama became more and more profound, failing to penetrate to the bottom of the lake. Even the Magatsuhi no Kami lurking in the depth of eight thousand fathomshira must be gazing up at the heavenly sunbeams. The pine island, the bamboo island, and then the plum island; they were indeed the forms of three spirits. Since the divine breath of Susanō is pure, my Kototama also rings out clear. Resonating and resonating, overflowing in resonance, how noble was the divine majesty of Kami of Mount Amatsuhie. Konohanasakuyahime no Kami appears and saves the people troubled by crookedness. If you entrust everything to the heart of Kami, what thing in the world would there be to fear? From the ancient age of the gods when the world was turbulent, the heart of Kami has never changed. Fire and water, since the ancient age of the gods, neither their color nor their taste has changed.'

Just as Tamaharuwake is chanting thus, one beautiful island floating on the lake's surface, moved by the power of the Kototama, leisurely draws near the lake shore. When he approaches and looks, it is an unexpectedly wide floating island. Standing at the forefront, when Tamaharuwake is about to cross over to this floating island, from whence nobody knows, a voice is heard:

Voice: 'Wait a while, divine official Tamaharuwake. This floating island is the transformation of crookedness. Feigning to be an island where beautiful pines grow, it is the wicked work of crookedness seeking to afflict you. Direct your village-liver hearts, ye five pillars, This lake where the crowds of crookedness gather. The living spirit of Susanō no Kami no Mikoto, I am Kotoyoriwake no Kami. The life of Kotoyoriwake no Kami shall appear and lend strength to Tamaharuwake. Hinodewake, illuminating the pitch-black dark night, The life of Kami is also here right now. The living spirit of the life of Konohanahime no Kami, Katori no Wake has also come creeping hither. Abundant is the blessing of the spirit of Kamunagara, Illuminating even to the lake bottom.'

So it is heard from the empty sky, from nowhere in particular.

Bowing his head with a start, clapping his hands and bowing twice, Tamaharuwake expresses gratitude for the divine grace, and facing the approaching pine island, with a cool voice casts forth the living Kototama.

Tamaharuwake: 'Kami Susanō no Ōkami, Konohanasakuyahime no Kami, As whose living spirit they have appeared, The propagandist of Katori no Wake, The living spirit of the jewel soul, The divine official Kotoyoriwake, That the divine person Hinodewake Would soar high in the heavenly sky And descend to protect and aid The battle of our entourage's Kototama, Ah, how noble, Kamunagara and Kami, The protection of Kami is right before our eyes. Tamaharuwake leaps up with courage, As far as my Kototama continues, Taking absolute sincerity as a shield, The deep heart of the Great Deity of Infinite Benevolence, Not to mention the blue-eyed grass of the four lands, Lurking in this lake water...'

玉治別はかく詠ふ折しも、湖面に浮べる美はしき一つの島、言霊の威力に感じてや悠々として湖畔近く寄り来る。近寄り見れば、意外なる広き浮島なり。玉治別は先頭に立ち、此浮島に渡らむとする時、いづくよりともなく声ありて、

『待て暫し玉治別の神司 此浮島は曲の変化ぞ。 美はしき松生ふ島と見せかけて 悩まさむとする曲の醜業。 村肝の心を配れ五柱 曲の集へるこれの湖。 素盞嗚の神の尊の生御霊 われは言依別の神ぞや。 言依別神の命が現はれて 玉治別に力を添へむ。 烏羽玉の闇夜をてらす日出別 神の命も今ここにあり。 木の花姫神の命の生御霊 蚊取の別もかくれ来にけり。 惟神神の御霊の幸深く 湖底までも照らし行くなり』

と、何処ともなく中空より聞え来る。

玉治別はハツと頭を下げ、拍手再拝、神恩を感謝し、寄り来る松島に向つて、声も涼しく生言霊をうちかけたり。

『神素盞嗚大御神 木の花咲耶姫神 其生霊と現れませる 蚊取の別の宣伝使 瑞の身魂の生御霊 言依別の神司 日出別の神人が 吾等一行の言霊の 戦を守り助けむと 天空高く翔らせて 下り玉ひし尊さよ あゝ惟神々々 神の守りは目のあたり 玉治別は勇み立ち 吾言霊のつづく丈 誠一つを楯となし 仁慈無限の大神の 深き心を四方の国 青人草は云ふも更 これの湖水にひそみたる

Even unto the descendants of Orochi, shall We leave them unsaved and unhelped? Now appearing as Ukishima, listen well, O ugly Orochi! In the way of Kami, not even a speck of dust possesses falsehood or impurity. Feigning to be a pure island, to deceive and afflict warera, you scheme and plot with wickedness— how pitiful is your heart! Ah, appearing as Matsushima, O ugly mitama, from this now on, the spirit received from Kami, into the lake purified by oshihe, wash and cleanse it, and like the good designs of Kami of ametsuchi, O mizumitama, return unto your original form! Even the purest mitama, if clouded, cannot surpass a stone. Tamaharuwake's magokoro,

imbued, admonishes thee. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, quickly return to the great path of Kami! If your spirit returns unto Kami, even without prayer, the Sumekami shall forgive your sins, and in Kamikuni filled and overflowing with inochi, sakae, and yorokobi, shall graciously and peacefully save you. The sendenshi of Tamaharuwake, proclaims the kototama here. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may your mitama flourish and prosper!'

No sooner had the song been finished than Matsushima, which had hitherto appeared before their eyes, overturned with a rumble, at which very moment exposing scales of white and red checkered stripes upon the surface of the lake, raised rough waves before one's eyes, and sank deep into the depths of the lake. That sight was truly a dreadful one.

Once again, a single island floats leisurely hitherward. Looking closer, fine black bamboos grow densely over the entire surface of the island. From among those bamboos, three peerless beauties surpassing flowers appear, extending gentle hands, and though they speak not with their mouths, their expression shows it, and they beckon repeatedly. Tamaharuwake, having learned his lesson from the previous Matsushima, does not move easily, folds his arms, and performs chinkon facing this island; whereupon the island gradually draws near to the lake shore, and the previous goddesses emerge from the bamboo grove, stand at the water's edge of the floating island, and gaze smilingly upon the figures of Tamaharuwake and his party.

Tamaharuwake: 'When I look upon the forms of the kamibito coming aboard the Takeshima, my heart rejoices. Even so, how can I let down my guard, having witnessed the marvel of Matsushima? Marvelous is this Takeshima; how is it that three maidens have appeared? They seem to resemble the three mitama born of the august deity, Lord Susanoo. Drifting amidst the small waves, the bamboo island— how beautiful are the shadows of the three maidens!'

Oai also chants:

'How grateful, how reverend, as I bow low, how beautiful is the form of Kami. The divided mitama of the august deity, Lord Susanoo, have now appeared before my eyes. Treading the great path of Kami, Kamunagara, I shall protect the path of mizumitama. The three mitama and the five mitama aligned together, protecting Kamikuni of Chikushi. Yashimawake, who descended upon the peak of Mount Amenoshihe, am I, the daughter of the august mikoto of Kami.'

So chanting, she bows low toward the three goddesses. One of the goddesses, with a voice most gentle,

'You, lord, who have come to this lake of the island of Tsukushi with a true heart to save the people of the world. To hear that you are indeed the august descendant of the mikoto, Yashimawake, brings me such joy. I am none other than Matsu-no-hime-mikoto of the age of Kami, while your heart continues to flourish. In the flourishing divine land, may you endure forever, ever more prospering, O beloved lord.'

Oai: 'How grateful that the mizumitama has appeared and cleared away the clouding of my body. O Torakahiko, my august husband, for my sake, pray protect him for all eternity.'

The next goddess chants again:

'O beloved princess, your life is perhaps the wondrous child of the mikoto, Takehinatawake. The august mikoto, Yashimawake, ascended to the mountain of Amenoshihe and flourished. Your mother, who is the august mikoto of the myriad-fold fabric, now dwells upon the mountains of the Himalayas. The benevolence of parents, higher than the mountains of the Himalayas— you must never, ever forget it.'

Oai responds to this:

'So long as the Yamato spirit of Shikishima remains within me, I shall press forward upon the great path of Kami. How regrettable it is that I ever acted contrary to the august heart of my divine parents. How wondrous indeed is my destiny, advancing ever in accordance with the divine will of Kamunagara. Leaving behind the distant cloudy skies, I have spent my days in the rustic fields of Takeno.'

The three goddesses chant again:

こめて汝を諭すなり あゝ惟神々々 神の大道に早帰れ 神に帰りし霊ならば 祈らずとても皇神は 汝が罪を赦しまし 生命と栄光と歓喜に 充ち足らひたる神国に 安く救はせ玉ふべし 玉治別の宣伝使 言霊ここに宣り了る あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ』

と歌ひ了るや否や、今迄目の前に現はれたる松島は、グレンと顛覆した途端に、白と赤とのダンダラ筋の鱗を湖面にさらし、見る間に荒波を立て、湖中深く沈みける。其光景は実に凄じきものにてありき。

又もや一つの島、悠々として此方に浮び来る。よくよく見れば、島一面に黒き細き竹が密生してゐる。其竹の間より花を欺く妙齢の美人、三柱現はれ、優しき手をさし伸べて、早く来れと、口には言はねど、其形容に現はし、手招き頻りなり。玉治別は以前の松島に懲りて、容易に動かず、両手を組み、此島に向つて鎮魂を修しければ、島は追々湖畔に近より来り、以前の女神は竹藪をぬけ出で、浮島の水打際に立ちて、ニコヤカに玉治別一行の姿を見つめゐたり。

玉治別『竹島に乗りて寄り来る神人の 姿を見れば心栄えぬ。 さりながら如何で心を許さむや われ松島の怪しきを見て。 怪しきはこれの竹島いかにして 三人乙女の現はれにけむ。 素盞嗚神の尊の生みましし 三つの御霊に似ましけるかも。 小波の中に漂ふ竹の島 三人乙女の影美はしも』

お愛は又詠ふ。

『有難やあら尊やと伏し拝む 神の姿の美はしき哉。 素盞嗚神の尊の分御霊 今わが前に現れましにけむ。 惟神神の大道を踏みしめて 瑞の御霊の道を守らむ。 三つ御霊、五つの御霊と相並び 守らせ玉ふ筑紫の神国。 天教山尾の上を降りし八島別 神の命のわれは娘ぞ』

と歌ひ終り、三人の女神に向つて伏し拝む。女神の一人は声も淑やかに、

『真心をつくしの島の此湖に 世人救ふと来ります君。 八島別神の命の御裔ぞと 聞くわれこそは嬉しかりけり。 われこそは神世を松の姫命 汝が心の栄えまちつつ。 栄えゆく神の御国に末永く いや栄えませ愛子の君よ』

お愛『有難し瑞の御霊の現はれて わが身の曇り晴らし玉へる。 虎若彦夫の命をわが為に 弥永久に守り給はれ』

次なる女神又詠ふ。

『愛子姫汝が命は建日向 別の命の珍の御子かも。 八島別神の命は天教の 山に登りて栄えましけり。 敷妙の神の命の汝が母は 今ヒマラヤの山にましける。 ヒマラヤの山より高き親の恩 ゆめゆめ忘れ玉ふまじきぞ』

お愛、これに応ふ。

『敷島の大和心のあらむ限りは 神の大道に進みて行かむ。 父母の神の命の御心に 反きし事の歎かはしきかも。 惟神神のまにまに進み行く わが宿世こそ不思議なりけり。 久方の雲井の空を立ち出でて 武野の鄙にわれは暮しつ』

'O Magokōtsukasa of the divine teachings of Ananahi, Now the crookedness of the Orochi has vanished away. Come now, direct the Orochi of your heart, And advance upon the path of truth. O Sankō of the divine Yakata of Kamunagara, Your chichihaha reside in Kamikuni. When we gaze upon this world from the divine mime of Kami, There are neither foes nor mikata; how precious is this. That your foes lurked within this lake— Ah, how your heart wandered in delusion. Blow away the clouds of your murakimo heart, And let shine the oshihe of the moon as you desire. O Tamaharuwake-notokoro-no-Mikoto, the Kamuzukasa, Go swiftly to the Kunimiyako of Fire. That dark clouds hang over the skies of the capital of Fire, Is yuyu; advance with speed. Come now, we three mitariotome shall depart, O you gonin, dwell in peace.'

Even as they thought they heard these words, together with the island, where could it have gone? Not a trace remained; behind, upon the konpeki komen, only waves gently swelled and undulated.

From this time, Tamaharuwake and his itsukau, in order to bring the magatsukami of this lake to kijiyun, implored the Sumeru Ōkami and offered prayers for one day and one night; whereupon the lake waters suddenly parted in two, taking the form of a beautiful goddess who, without a word to the gonin, respectfully finished bowing and worshipping, immediately raising clouds and soaring leisurely high up into the heavens. This was because the huge three-headed Orochi lurking in this lake had escaped the sufferings of the three cold and three hot hells through the divine spiritual virtue of Kami, and suddenly transforming into the figure of a beautiful goddess, had been saved into the heavenly realm.

Hereupon Tamaharuwake, together with his itsukau, once again crossed Hakusan Pass, stopped by at the mansion of the Sankō in the land of Kumaso, spent the night, and hastened forward toward the divine land of Fire. Kamunagara Tamachihau Mase.

原文（近代文語）

『三五の神の教の孫公司よ 大蛇の曲は消えうせにける。 いざさらば心の大蛇を言向けて 誠の道に進ませ給へ。 惟神神の屋方の三公よ 汝が父母は神国にあり。 世の中に神の御目より眺むれば 仇も味方もなきぞ尊き。 此湖に汝が仇のひそむとは よくも心の迷ひしものよ。 村肝の心の雲を吹き払ひ 照らせたきもの月の教を。 玉治別貴の命の神司 とく行きませよ火の国都へ。 火の国の都の空に黒雲の かかるは忌々しとく進みませ。 いざさらば三人乙女は立ち去らむ 五人の人よすこやかに坐せよ』

といふかと見れば、島諸共に何処へ行きけむ、跡形もなく、あとには紺碧の湖面に、波穏かにうねりゐるのみ。

是より玉治別一行は此湖の曲津神を帰順せしむべく、皇大神に請ひのみまつり、一日一夜祈願をこらせば、忽ち湖水は二つに分れ、美はしき女神の姿となりて五人に無言の儘、恭しく拝礼し終り、直ちに雲を起し、悠々として天上高く昇り行けり。これは此湖にひそみし巨大なる三頭の大蛇、神の霊徳に依つて三寒三熱の苦をのがれ、忽ち美はしき女神の姿と化して、天国に救はれたるなりき。

ここに玉治別は一行と共に、再び白山峠を越え、熊襲の国の三公が館に立寄り、一夜を明かし、急いで火の神国さして進み行く。惟神霊幸倍坐世。
