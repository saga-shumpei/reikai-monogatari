Chapter 14: 空気 Flame [978]

空気焔〔978〕

§ 1

The party of four at last reached the southern shore of the lake of Suppon. By this time the night had completely fallen, and an atmosphere of mournful wailing and deep desolation pressed upon their bodies. Magokōwake, the self-styled propagator who was the most essential figure, made his teeth chatter like the grandson of an earthquake, turning pale in the face and cowering in utter fright. A gale fierce enough to blow away rocks blew incessantly, with a terror beyond description; Magokōwake clung tightly to the root of a tree, barely preventing his own body from being blown away.

The three others also crouched flat at the root of a tsuge tree, merely waiting for the wind to pass. The lake made a sound as if suddenly boiling, and began to bubble with a gurgling noise. Although it was a dark night, the bubbling color of the lake surface was clearly visible. After a while, pillars of waves swelled up here and there like towering giants, and their sound as they crashed and fell upon the lake surface was truly dreadful, making one's hair stand on end. From here and there within the lake, countless fireballs of blue, red, white, yellow, and other colors appeared, dragging long tails through midair and emitting a droning roar as they rushed wildly in all directions. When one looked, they had the appearance of tadpoles, with various repulsive and terrifying faces attached to the orb part. These monsters gathered close to the four and flew around their heads back and forth, left and right, yet for some reason they did not draw near to their persons. The closest they came was a mere one or two ken at most.

Ai: 'Tonight is a strange night indeed. The great serpent god is surely displaying various orbs and holding a welcome party for us travelers. What a thoughtful god, ohohoho. Thanks to the fireball-sama, even the record-breaking wind has completely stopped. That terrifying bubbling and the giant water pillars seem to have sunk somewhere. This must be entirely due to the divine virtue of the propagator-sama, Magokōwake. Say, Tora-ko-sama, Sankō-sama, how truly magnificent is the divine virtue of a propagator, ah.'

Torakō: 'The serpent fellow has just started the third Sanbasō. From here on is the real spectacle. Such things are merely a fraction. From here on is where Magokōwake the propagator will have to sweat. I say, Propagator-sama, how are you? No matter how long you cling to the tree stump, the tree will not speak.'

Magokōwake made his teeth chatter and replied,

Magokōwake: 'No, no, no, no, ta-ta-this has become a terrible business. Truly a... most... delightful affair. Somehow I lack a supply of divine power, so in such a case I am... not a man who is easily startled.'

Torakō: 'Hahahaha, as long as Lord Magokōwake is holding his ground here, we are as solid as a massive rock. Say, Sankō-sama, you too must feel at ease. First, let us have the propagator sing a propagation song, and appease and pacify the serpent fellow, shall we?'

Sankō: 'Won't you try singing a propagation song for the sake of Sankō (as a reference)? I say, Magokōwake-sama, please grant us this one favor.'

Magokōwake: 'This is why becoming a leader of things brings added responsibility and trouble. Peaceful times seem extremely pleasant, but being placed at the vanguard in times like this is terribly hard... Oh, wait now, the general's role is to devise strategies within the tent. Becoming a shield against orbs is what common soldiers do. At any rate, since the propagator will take charge of the rear, Sankō-sama, please fight the first battle. Just as you see, fireballs are multiplying every moment. If we dawdle, there is a risk of the enemy gaining the initiative, so please act as the advance guard with youthful spirit. It is an order from the Great Elder Mago.'

Ai: 'You simply must ask the teacher, otherwise this battle is hopeless. It is the time to test the divine power of the San-Go propagator, who possesses the spirit of swallowing the enemy without fighting. Come now, come now, do not hesitate and do it... Torakō-sama, Ai-sama, how would it be if you asked him so?'
