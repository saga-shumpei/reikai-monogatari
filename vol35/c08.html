§ 1

For a while the banqueting was over. The three great figures of Orochi invited Torakō, Oai, Umé, Magokō, Kurohime, and Kanekō into his beautiful private living room, and while talking about various matters, they were relaxing and holding a secondary gathering. A multitude of kobun also went out to the tanbo each after their own mind, being in high spirits as they wrestled, lay down, and talked idle chatter. Since there were numerous kobun here and there, minor kozeriahi had begun, but anyhow, since it was a joyous kefu, they exercised restraint toward one another, and without any major kenkuwa, it was extremely buji.

Rokukō, Tokukō, Takakō, and the six kobun of Torakō, namely Shin, Hisu, and Yatsu, were dead drunk on sake, and sitting heavily right on the spot just as they were, they unbent and indulged in various conversations.

Shinkō: 'Hey Shinkō, isn't your aneki a quite wonderful article? If you are stared at even for a moment out of nowhere with those gentle eyes, don't you feel like your body is being drawn to her? And so, taking advantage of the fact that I was drunk... Hey, how about that? Since you guys lack alertness, didn't you miss a chance of a lifetime? They say that striking first controls the person, so you must have enjoyed it sweetly.'

Now, now Shinkō, today the boss is absent, so well, drink sake leisurely... Oai will pour it for you... or something like that, gripping a warmed sake flask with those white slender hands... Come on Shinkō-sama, put out your cup... she says. At that, this Shinkō throws off his wits for a moment thinking it is when the ogre is away, thrusts his cup forward smoothly, and Oai-sama takes the warmed sake flask with that soft hand, applies her left little hand to the bottom of the flask, stretches her waist smoothly, and kneeling upright, goes glug glug glug—whoops, it's spilling, it's spilling... that is the style... is what I would like to say, but that was a dream I saw some time ago. Now, now Shinkō, what kind of incomprehensible man are you? Even though you act like a flattened spider in front of Lord Tora, when he is absent you rage like a rat in a cupboard, and you don't listen to a single thing I say, do you? A spineless man is what you are. Come over here for a moment, and put out your hand... being told this, the fellow Shinkō, because he is a born tempo coin... You, close your eyes for a moment... goes as far as to pull that off. Acting like a fool, when the fellow Shinkō closes his eyes, Oai-sama pinches ashes in her left hand, pinches glowing embers with chopsticks in her right hand, places the fire on his palm, and at the same time, has ashes placed on his tongue... Ahaha, pooh pooh pooh, she is a fool who runs off to the back stream while saying that... so he is a fellow who cannot be talked to at all.'

Yashimawake no Kamisama had a wife named Shikitahehime-sama, and born between them was Aikohime-sama, and as her younger sister there was a beautiful young lady called Yorikohime-sama. As it happened, their Goriyōshinsama had Toyoteruhiko-sama, the son of Toyohiwake-sama of Toyo no Kuni, taken as their adopted son to make him succeed the lineage, but since Aikohime-sama naturally detested aristocratic life from birth and was a person of commoner principles, she disliked the splendid adopted son Toyoteruhiko-sama, slipped out quietly from the mansion wrapped in the shadows of the Yain, and while she was making her way along the Yomichi wearing her Ōgon udewa and diamond kubikazari just as they were, suddenly two or three men appeared from the shade of the woods, abruptly stood in front of the Hime, taking her by the hands and feet, knocked her down by the Kusahara's Michibata, and just as they were about to commit violence and outrage, our boss Torakō-sama, accompanied by this Shinkō, came staggering along in a tipsy, slightly drunken state like a plover, singing a kiyari from the direction yonder. Three bad fellows gathered together and were holding down the Hime, making her shriek. When the boss and I flew out and let out a great roar... Wait a moment, you villains... and shouted with a voice like thunder, those three fellows were startled by that sound and fled in all directions like clouds and mist... You cowardly and base villains, we don't fix our eyes on those who run away... when we peered at the Hime's direction in the moonlight, she was an unequalled beauty surpassing snow, and the diamonds were illuminated by the moon, glittering like stars all over her head. Diamonds on her fingers, and rings of Ōgon on her feet and arms as well. Thinking that this surely could not be the young lady of an ordinary household, Boss-sama acknowledged... Excuse me, I know not what kind of lady you might be, but to make a dangerous solo journey by night with such boldness, it must have been a perilous affair, ne... saying thus as he took the Hime's hands, gently raised her to her feet, brushed off the dust and consoled her, the Hime-sama finally raised her face... I know not who you may be nor from where, but in this case of nine deaths and one life, you have truly saved me; since I am in the midst of my journey, I have no way to express my gratitude, so please accept this... and she removed every single diamond shining on her head, gathered everything from the foot rings to the armbands into one, and tried to hand them over to the boss. Thinking that Torakō the boss would joyfully leap at them, with an attitude like showing gold coins to a cat... Look here, young lady, I didn't help you for the sake of getting such things, so please keep them... when he said so, that princess said... Please accept them; you are my parent of life, and I am the eldest daughter of Tatehinatawake no Mikoto; since I grew to loathe aristocratic life and wanted to go down to the countryside and do even chores as a water-maid, I slipped away, and such things are no longer necessary; moreover, my heart, which once thought to give them to you, cannot be overturned no matter what, so by all means please accept them out of your mercy... she begged, joining her hands. The boss... I too am a chivalrous man of Takenomura, and once I say I do not need them, I will not accept them no matter what... and he began to persist. Since no conclusion could be reached no matter what, this Shinkō stepped into the middle, and finally made the boss understand. When he did so, the boss... If that is so, Hime-sama, I gratefully accept your kind intention... accepting them... and the boss said, 'Once I have received them, Hime-sama, can I do with them whatever I please?' When he said that, the Hime-sama replied... Once I have handed them over to you, they are your belongings, so please do as you please with them... as she offered them. Without waiting for the boss to say... Then I shall do as I please... he threw them without a single sigh of regret into the deep valley stream flowing nearby. And so that Hime-sama fell in love with the boss's generosity and had been infatuated with him. However, seemingly too shy as a woman's heart to show even a hint of such a thing, she hid her true identity, took up residence doing water-maid chores in the house of Shichibē of Takenomura, and stayed there for about two years.
