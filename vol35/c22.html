Chapter 22: 当違 [986]

当違〔986〕

§ 1

The two men who appeared before the gate of Takayamahiko in the Land of Fire were none other than Fusakō and Yoshikō.

Monbansama, please let us through.

From within the gate, the gatekeeper Karukō spoke: 'Not at the threshold where the heart's rock-door is firmly shut, Nor through the gate where the crooked pass. Clarify the Great Way of Kami from within your heart, Beneath the heavens there is no obstruction. This gate is the threshold which people of righteous hearts Pass through with arms spread wide. If you open the gate of your own breast, instantly This iron gate will open of itself.'

From outside, Fusakō said: 'A gatekeeper who speaks in riddles keeps watch here, We have run right into a troublesome fellow.'

Yoshikō sang in response: 'Even though you look upon me as lowly, Open the iron gate of Kami lightly. Rather than wasting time on useless matters, Open the gate of your heart and let us pass. I am indeed a child of Kami from Onokorojima, There should be no threshold where Kami do not pass. Crossing over as the Sumekami command, Do not treat the child of Kamisama lightly.'

Karukō spoke from within the gate: 'How could the iron gate possibly open so lightly, In an age when the fury of crookedness is strong? In an age of darkness where the crooked kami masquerade as true kami, And deceive mankind.'

From outside the gate came the voice of Fusakō: 'Hesitate not, I am the great deity illuminating the head, The kami who opens the gate of prosperity.'

Karukō spoke from within the gate: 'Come now, O great deity illuminating the head, Advance through this iron gate.'

As he chanted thus, he pulled the bolt with a clatter and flung the gate wide open to left and right, whereupon Fusakō and Yoshikō exchanged a slight bow and stepped lightly further and further into the interior.

At the reception desk in the entranceway sat a man of about fifty with a slightly slender face.

Fusakō: 'I am one called Fusakō, who has accompanied Kurohime of Hinodekyō to this place. Is Kurohime-sama being cared for here?'

A Kurohime-sama of Hinodekyō is a propagandist well known throughout the world for her golden jewels, but she has not yet appeared here.

Is the master of this house also a person named Takayamahiko?

That is correct. The master is Takayamahiko, and the mistress is a splendid priestess named Aicohime.

Is Takayamahiko-sama at home? I would like to make a brief inquiry...

He spoke with a significant air.

I am the receptionist Tamakō, but I hear that the master Takayamahiko went out somewhere early this morning. However, we at the reception desk do not know the details.

Pardon me, but if Takayamahiko-sama is away, would it be possible for you to let us meet the mistress for a moment? Since the former wife of Takayamahiko-sama will arrive later anyway, I believe that if I could have a brief audience and make inquiries beforehand, we might recognize the dawn of an amicable resolution beforehand, so please do something to mediate for us.

Out of the question. While the master is away, the mistress cannot grant an audience to a man. Unfortunately, please abandon this thought. And what is the name of the person you call the master's former wife?

Puffing out his chest slightly and speaking in a formal, theatrical tone, Fusakō said:

Unspeakably, she is none other than Kurohime-sama, the great propagandist of Hinodekyō. Since we are the arms and legs of that Kurohime-sama, it would be best for you to treat us with great courtesy. No matter who Aicohime-sama is, once she hears of this matter, she will not be able to avoid meeting us.

With his shoulders squared in anger, steam rising from his bald head, and looking like an octopus wearing a formal kamishimo, he stiffened his shoulders into sharp angles.

Ha-ha-ha-ha, isn't that a terrible mistake? The master, Takayamahiko-sama, is still young and in the very prime of his strength. And welcoming Aicohime-sama was said to be the very first time he ever took a woman, so there is no way he could have such an old crone for a wife. There must be some mistake.

と意味ありげに云ふ。

『私は受付の玉公と申しますが、何でも高山彦の御主人は、今朝早々何処かへ修行にお越しになつたと聞いて居ります。乍併受付の吾々は詳しい事は存じませぬ』

『何卒すみませぬが、高山彦様がお留守ならば、一寸奥様に会はして下さる訳にはいけますまいか。いづれ後から高山彦様の前の奥さまが見えますから、それ以前に一寸お目に掛つて御伺ひして置けば、前以て円満解決の曙光を認め得るものと存じますから、何とか一つ取りもつて下さいな』

『滅相もない。主人の御不在中に奥様が男の方に御対面は遊ばしませぬ。残念ながら何卒諦めて下さいませ。さうして御主人様の前の奥様とは、何と云ふお方で御座いますか』

房公は少しく胸を張り、切り口上にて、

『勿体なくも三五教の大宣伝使黒姫様で御座る。吾々は其黒姫様の股肱の臣で御座るから鄭重にお待遇なさるが宜からう。如何に愛子姫様だとて此事をお聞きになれば、お会ひにならぬと云ふ訳には参りますまい』

と肩肱怒らし禿頭に湯気を立て、章魚が裃着た様な恰好で、肩を四角に固くなつて居る。

『ハヽヽヽヽ、そりや大変な大間違ひぢやありませぬか。御主人の高山彦様はまだお年がお若い屈強盛りです。さうして愛子姫様をお迎へ遊ばしたのが、女をお持ちになつた最初だと云ふ事ですから、そんな年をとつたお婆アさまを女房に持つて居られる筈はありませぬ。何かのお間違ひでせう』

§ 2

Ah hah hah hah, how on earth have they managed to match their bellies from top to bottom like that. Leaving the faithful Kurohime-sama—who crossed the waves of Banri and came searching and yearning all the way after her husband—out in the cold, taking a young woman as a wife, and trying to make her way through this world in merriment and amusement is a cunning calculation. Takayamahiko-sama too has truly fallen quite low. While making up the backside of a sixty-year-old, he ought to have some sense about it. I wonder if he has taken a young mistress and grown young again, becoming like a vigorous person in the prime of life. With human beings, how one holds one's heart is what matters most. But where in the world can Kurohime-sama be wandering about? If by any chance she were to arrive in a place like this, that truly would be a terrible disaster, wouldn't it.'

At this time, the voice of an ichigenkin was heard sounding from across a single room; the master of the song was Aikohime, the female master of this house.

'From the distant age of the divine when Chihaya stirs, Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, Desiring to save gods and men Across the four quarters of heaven and earth, Most peacefully, most tranquilly, In the spring of Tokoyo, Distributed his heart into a thousand ways, Without distinction between night and day, Without parting of far and near, Treating high and low alike as one, Bestowing the dew of grace, Spreading the teachings of Misoshikyo To the eighty islands of the islands and eighty countries, Even to the depths of Morokoshi mountain, How wondrous that he causes them to unfold. My dear lord is the manifestation Of the supreme virtue of the jewels Entrusted by Amaterasu Sumeōkamigami, Becoming the god Ikatsuhikone, Serving as a child Of God Susanō no Ōkami, Proclaiming the teachings of severity Without a single gap across the lands of heaven and earth, As the missionary of Takakuniwake, Descending from Mount Tenkyō, The missionary of Yashimawake or Futomiyohime, Succeeding behind the divine decree, Changing his name to Takayamahiko, Enduring in this world, Waiting for the reign of Misago, How honorable is the divine offspring. This humble self is also the same Mizu no Mitama, A precious child born Of God Susanō no Ōkami, Calling herself Aikohime, By the words of her father Ōkami, In the Kengenkyō village of Mesopotamia, Building a hall of Brahmanism And opening the teachings, While the crooked deity Onigumohiko Remained long hidden in his presence And used his heart, at that very time, The missionary Futotamahiko Appeared and made manifest Futotama's divine majesty, Whereupon Onigumohiko was startled And fled away like clouds and mist. We eight sisters, Leaving the Kengenkyō village, Each and every one disguising our bodies, At the very time of spreading the teachings of Misoshikyo In the four directions, Because of the demon my younger sister Was afflicted and upon the waves, Surpassing the myriad hardships flowing far, And proceeding to transmit the great path of Ōkami, Ah, courageous sisters! Now in what fields, upon what mountains, Are my beloved younger sisters wandering? Ah, Adonai, gods, gods! May the divine spirit bless us, That even a single day sooner the sisters May bring their safe faces together, And the age of peace to rejoice, Before the gods of Misago, Solemnly we pray. My dear lord, receiving The great imperial decree of the sovereign gods, Has gone forth to the waterfall of Katsura. Ah, Adonai, gods, gods! By the grace of Amaterasu Sumeōkamigami, May my dear lord peacefully complete A hundred days of misogi purification. Aikohime, reverently, Plucking and sounding the pure jade koto, Freshly and cleanly prays. Ah, Adonai, gods, gods! May the spirit prosper and bless us.'

The voice singing thus reached their two ears as if piercing right through them.

'Excuse me, Tamakō-san, wasn't that voice just now Aikohime-sama? From the sound of that song, I have a feeling it is not Takayamahiko-sama, whom our teacher Kurohime-sama is searching for. Pray tell, from where did the master of this place arrive?'

'Until two or three years ago, this divine hall was protected by the missionary Yashimawake no Mikoto and his wife who descended from Mount Tenkyō; but Hinodewake no Kami-sama came over from Mount Tenkyō with an imperial divine decree stating that since the affairs of Konohanahime no Mikoto were busy, they should return to Mount Tenkyō as before. Together with Hinodewake no Kami-sama, they evacuated this capital, and after that, God Susanō no Ōkami led and arrived with Ikatsuhikone no Mikoto—who was born as the severe spirit of Amaterasu Ōmikami—made Aikohime-sama, the eldest daughter of Susanō no Mikoto, his wife, and returned. Unlike other missionaries, he is a divine official of exceedingly high divine virtue, and ultimately a living god, you see.'

'What on earth! I'm coming to understand nothing at all of what is what. Hey Yoshikō, listen here, we must think about this for a moment.'

'It is a story as if one were bewitched by a tengu of Hinokuni Pass. We cannot stay here like this; after finding the whereabouts of Kurohime-sama, we must devise some plan or other... Tamakō-san, thank you very much. We will disturb you again. Give our regards to the mistress as well...'

So saying, they hastily put on their straw raincoats and hats, struck their iron staves, and went out toward the front gate.

と云ひ捨て慌しく蓑笠をつけ金剛杖をつき乍ら表門指して出でて行く。
