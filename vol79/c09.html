Chapter 9: The Episode of 祝賀の宴 (Part I) [1990]

祝賀の宴（一）〔1990〕

§ 1

Ōtatsumihiko-no-Mikoto, the Ōjiya of Riugūjima, having determined Urarakahime, the beloved daughter of the Mioya-no-Kami of the land at the foot of Minakamiyama, to be his consort, began to govern Unabaraguni from Dairiuden; hearing that Wadatsumihime-no-Kami, daughter of Ōwadatsumi-no-Kami, was opening the suimon of the Mirror Lake to come and celebrate this Zendaimimon Keiji, all the Jiushintachi were in a state of Uōsaō and Kinkijiyakuyaku, and began all preparations to welcome the Himegami. Both inside and outside the Dairōmon, they newly spread Kinshiya and Ginshiya, cut the tips of cedar and cypress trees, and paved the path for the Gotsūkau with Aoba, neglecting no preparations.

As time passed, Wadatsumihime-no-Kami appeared as the Mitsukahi of her father, and while slightly surprised that the preparations for the welcome were entirely complete, she sang:

'Coming here, the foundation of the dragon island is pure, The faces of the people of this island are radiant. Not a single speck of dust casts a shadow in this garden, I know the sincerity of the Lord Kami. Surpassing the age of the gods, unprecedented, I shall celebrate this joy. The path I tread is filled with the green mats Of cypress leaves. Even as I walk, there is no sound of footsteps on the green mats, Is it the color of the dew of the Kami's blessing?'

Just as she sang this, each person refined their formal attire, and with Ōtatsumihiko-no-Mikoto at the head, Ototohime-no-Kami and many attendant Kami formed a procession and advanced solemnly before the Himegami, finished their respectful bows, and first, Ōtatsumihiko-no-Mikoto said:

'Urarakahime, who descended from the vast heavens, By the grace of the Kami, you have become my wife. I pray that you will convey this state of affairs in detail To the great Father Kami. I think of the fatigue of the Hime-no-Mikoto Who emerged from the bottom of the eight-fathom lake. From today, this island foundation shall also prosper, Now that Tsuyoo and Urarakahime have come across.'

The Himegami replied to this:

'The Lord who encounters this Keiji, Unprecedented since the opening of heaven and earth, is noble. From today, be intimate with Ototohime-no-Kami, And protect the land of the sea.'

Ototohime-no-Kami sang while clapping her hands:

'Wadatsumihime-no-Kami, whose name is fearful to utter, I welcome your arrival with joy. Led by the thread of the divine plan of the Kami, I have crossed over to this island. Everything that meets my eyes is rare, And this island country is also new. Being powerless, I wonder from here on, How I shall open up this island country. I pray that you, Himegami, will add your strength, To me, a maiden still young in years. At night in dreams, by day in reality, on this island, I am lost, like the white waves of the island. I am on the island where white waves wash ashore, And I rely only on the power of the Kami.'

Tsuyoo stood by the side of Ototohime-no-Kami and sang:

'Having just received the high teachings, I am Tsuyoo, a weak man. Celebrating this unprecedented joy of today, Your heart, O Hime who has come, is fearful to behold. I have determined to remain on this island foundation forever, And open up the land. I have spread white sand around the pure garden, This day of welcoming the Hime is auspicious.'

Wadatsumihime-no-Kami replied to this:

'The Kami of the vast heavens must surely approve Of the joy of this island foundation. From today, the dragon gods and the fish tribes, Shall pass their time in peace of body and soul. Even the dragon gods who possess human form, Are not yet in their complete state. As time passes, this form will be renewed, And the day of being born as true humans is near. Without the water and fire of the Kunitsukami, how could the dragon gods Be born into human bodies? Somehow, today is a pure day. The bottom of the lake is also clear, If I tell my Father Kami of this state of affairs in detail, He will surely be pleased. Because they are not yet in their complete human bodies, The power of the living Kototama does not reach them. Tsuyoo and Urarakahime, you are Kunitsukami, Proclaim the living Kototama.'

Here, Tsuyoo and Ototohime-no-Kami, who possess human bodies completely, stood at the head of Wadatsumihime-no-Kami, and with clear voices, sang the seventy-five sounds of the living Kototama:

'A-O-U-E-I KA-KO-KU-KE-KI SA-SO-SU-SE-SHI TA-TO-TSU-TE-CHI NA-NO-NU-NE-NI HA-HO-FU-HE-HI MA-MO-MU-ME-MI YA-YO-YU-E-I RA-RO-RU-RE-RI WA-WO-U-E-I GA-GO-GU-GE-GI ZA-ZO-ZU-ZE-JI DA-DO-ZU-DE-JI BA-BO-BU-BE-BI PA-PO-PU-PE-PI One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, Millions and billions of Kami, Please protect us.'

原文（近代文語）

竜宮島の王者たる大竜身彦の命は、水上山の麓なる国の御祖の神の愛娘、麗子姫を妃と定め大竜殿に海原国の政を始め給ふに就き、大海津見の神の娘海津見姫の神は、前代未聞の慶事を祝せむとして鏡の湖の水門を開き入り来り給ふと聞き、あらゆる従神等は右往左往、欣喜雀躍し、姫神を歓待せむとあらゆる準備に着手してゐる。大楼門の内外には金砂銀砂を新しく敷き詰め、杉桧の梢を伐り、御通行の道を青葉にかためて、準備をさをさおこたりなかりける。

かかる所へ時うつりて、大海津見の神の娘海津見姫の神は、父の御使として現れ給ひ、歓迎の準備全く調へるに稍驚き給ひつつ、

『来て見れば竜の島根は清しけれ この島人の面かがやける 塵一つかげを止めずこの庭に 主の神の真心を知る 千早振る神代をかけてためしなき この喜びをことほぎ奉らむ 吾いゆく道の面は桧葉の 青き莚に満たされにけり 歩むさへ足音のなき青莚 神の恵の露の色かも』

かく歌はせ給ふ折もあれ、各自礼装を凝らし、大竜身彦の命を先頭に、弟姫の神あまたの御供の神等、粛々と列をつくりて姫神の前に進み来り、恭しく最敬礼を終り、先づ大竜身彦の命は、

『久方の御空ゆ降る麗子姫 神の恵に妻となしたり 願はくばこの有様をまつぶさに 父大神に伝へ給はれ 八千尋の湖の底より出でましし 姫の命のつかれを思ふ 今日よりはこの島ケ根も栄ゆべし 艶男、麗子渡り来ませば』

姫神はこれに答へて、

『天地の開けし時ゆためしなき 慶事に会へる君ぞ尊き 今日よりは弟姫神と親しみて 海の食国護らせ給へ』

弟姫神は拍手しながら歌ふ。

『掛巻くも畏き海津見姫神の いでまし吾は嬉しみ迎ふ 惟神神の経綸の綱にひかれ 吾この島に渡り来しはや 吾目路にふるるものみな珍しく また新しきこれの島国よ 力なき吾は是よりいかにして この島国をひらかむとぞ思ふ 願はくば姫神力を添へよかし まだ年若き乙女の吾に 夜はゆめ昼はうつつとこの島に 吾は迷へり島の白波 白波の打ち寄す島に吾ありて 神の力にのみぞすがるも』

艶男は弟姫神の側に立ちて歌ふ。

『今のさき高き教を蒙りし 吾は艶男よわき男子よ ためしなき今日の喜び寿ぎて 来ませる姫の心畏き とこしへにこの島ケ根に吾ありて 国土ひらかむと思ひ定めし 白砂をしきまはしたり清庭に 姫を迎ふる今日ぞめでたき』

海津見姫の神は之に答へて、

『千早振る神もうべなひ給ふべし この島ケ根の喜びごとを 竜神や魚族たちも今日よりは 身もたましひも安く過ぎなむ 人間の形そなへし竜神も いまだ全き姿にあらず この姿月日けみして新たまり まことの人と生るる日近し 国津神の御水火なければ竜神は 如何で生れむ人の身体に 何となく今日は清しき日なりけり 湖の底ひも明らかにして 父神にこの有様を詳細に 語らばさぞや喜び給はむ 人間の全き身にしあらざれば 生言霊の力もおよばず 艶男や麗子の君は国津神 生言霊を宣らさせ給へ』

ここに人体を全く備へたる艶男並びに麗子の弟姫神は、海津見姫の神の先頭に立ち、声も朗かに七十五声の生言霊を歌ふ。

『アオウエイ カコクケキ サソスセシ タトツテチ ナノヌネニ ハホフヘヒ マモムメミ ヤヨユエイ ラロルレリ ワヲウヱヰ ガゴグゲギ ザゾズゼジ ダドヅデヂ バボブベビ パポプペピ 一二三四五六七八九十 百千万千万の神 守らせ給へ』

§ 2

Repeating this time and again, they ascend the stairs of the Dairiuden with a leisurely gait and advance deep into the Okuden.

Wadatsumihime-no-Kami takes her seat on the elevated dais, while Ōtatsumihiko-no-Mikoto and Otohimegami sit slightly below, attending on either side. Other goddesses take their places in order, and countless Tatsugami and Urokuzu gather, celebrating the day's Keiji with cries of 'Urou-urou,' clicking their tongues in delight at the various Chinmikakau, and dancing in a frenzy until the Dennai seems to burst.

Uraraka stands in the center of the crowd and sings a Kototamauta:

Asahi shines and Yufuhi illuminates, The myriad of shining stars, Show the colors of prosperity. Tanabatahime has descended from heaven, And this island floating upon the waves, She makes a heaven where flowers bloom and bear fruit, While guarding and opening it, Until the end of eight thousand generations, She protects it as a land of bliss and purity. How noble it is that she opens, The entirety of this young land. The pure water of life, the true pure water, Gushes forth in the sacred ground of the noble, And it is a joy that the Kami's island is filled, With boundless happiness. I have heard that this Shimakene, Has been a land where golden flowers bloom since ancient times. After the golden flowers and silver flowers scatter, Lapis lazuli, giant clam shells, Coral, and agate beads bear fruit. Pearls and jade buds hang heavy, It is a joy that they sway, the Kami's island. From today, the capital of the dragon, Without distinction between old and young, Shall flourish ever more. With the thread of life growing ever longer, The world is in the shade of the Takasago pines, Let the cranes come and cry, let the wild geese come, May the mountains flourish in green, May the seas also be green forever, Bring forth a hundred Urokuzu, In the peace of the Urayasukuni, Drawing in the lands that are called lands, Of the endless flourishing sea, Make the glory of the Ryūgū island, Shine forever and ever. Ah, since the children of the Kami, who are Kannagara, Have appeared here, They shall see the height of flowers, In an eternal spring and summer. Dance, dance, sing, sing, Until the pillars of heaven and earth tremble, Dance, go mad, forever, Never mind one day or two, Continue for seven days and seven nights, Celebrate and sing this joy forever, Celebrate and sing, sing, children of the island.

Following this lead, tens of thousands of Tatsugami and Urokuzu fill the inside and outside of the Dairiuden, dancing in a frenzy, a grand spectacle that seems to shake heaven and earth.

Wadatsumihime-no-Kami sings from the second seat with a clear voice:

The heavens are clear, the land is clear, how wonderful, The rain of blessings from heaven and earth, Has fallen upon this Shimakene. Let the hundred Tatsugami and Urokuzu, Sing of this good day and good hour, As a foundation for a thousand generations. It is good to sing, it is good to dance, It is right to dance, for the joy of this island, Which has no equal since the time heaven and earth opened, Celebrate it and do not let it go beyond this island. Though it is an island born of the spirit of the Lord God, Since the Arazu-o-no-Kami has not yet arrived, The dragon bodies, not even counted among, The still lowly Kunitsukami, Their pitiful and fleeting state, Has been changed by the arrival of the noble man and Uraraka-no-Kimi, Who have crossed over to this place. This Shimakene shall also rise up, And surely become a true land. Though the eight-fathom palace is built, With its green tiles and red walls, it is beautiful, The various bodies that dwell within, Have not yet attained perfection. Following this day's Keiji, Protect the noble man, And give birth to and multiply the Kunitsukami and the human race, Like the number of grains of sand in heaven, Comparable to the stars in the sky, Give birth and multiply forever.

Having sung thus, she returns to her original seat. The many Tatsugami and others rejoice at this song, forgetting themselves as they dance in a frenzy. Here, Ōtatsumihiko-no-Mikoto stands up abruptly, advances into the crowd, and sings with a resonant voice:

The vast sky has cleared, the land of rough metal, Has opened. The Amatsubi, Shines through, and Tsukiyomi, Without a shadow, lets the dew of blessings, Fall upon this Shimakene. The stars in the sky are like sand, Each shining, Celebrating the joy of this day. The wind that blows is also soft, And the shade of the hundred-fold Katsura trees is clear, Small birds sing and butterflies dance, And the sound of insects chirping in the grass, Is heard ever so clearly. Ah, the heaven has been born, And the Hōrai island has appeared. From today, I shall give birth to and multiply the seeds of the Kami on this island, And peacefully protect and sustain, The Tatsugami and various families and relatives who dwell here, Kannagara. Rejoice and be brave, all you who gather, Inside and outside this palace, Sing, dance, dance, and go mad, Until the bottom of the earth falls out.

As he sings, the hundred Tatsugami and Urokuzu dance and go mad in every direction, as if this were their last day.

Uraraka-hime, in her capacity as Otohimegami, stands with correct posture and sings from above the stairs:

I am the one who has reached the age of seventeen, Born as the child of the great ancestor of the land, Who appeared at the foot of the mountain, Above the waters of the Mahoyaba of the Ashihara land. By the grace of the divine Keirin, I have unexpectedly crossed over to this island, And become the ruler of the Ryūgū, Becoming one who builds and solidifies the age of the Kami. You Tatsugami, you Urokuzu, From today, this Shimakene shall also flourish, Like the cedar trees, one after another, And the flowers of the eight-thousand-generation camellia and Udonge, Shall bloom and be fragrant at all times. Rejoice and be brave, all of you.

Having sung briefly, she finishes and returns to her original seat.

原文（近代文語）

と幾度となく繰返し繰返し、大竜殿の階段を悠々と上り、奥殿深く進み入る。

海津見姫の神は設けの上段の席に着き給ひ、稍下つて大竜身彦の命、弟姫神は左右に侍り、其他の女神は次ぎ次ぎに列を正し、数多の竜神魚族まで集り来り、「ウロウウロウ」と今日の慶事を祝し合ひ、諸々の珍味佳肴に舌鼓を打ち、殿内もわるるばかりに踊り狂ひ舞ふ。

麗子は群集の中央に立ち、言霊歌をうたふ。

『朝日輝く夕日は照らふ 輝く星のかずかずも 栄の色を示すなり 七夕姫の天降りまして 波の上に浮くこの島を 花咲き実る天国と 守らせ開かせ給ひつつ 八千代の末に到るまで 楽土浄土と守らひつ 稚き国原まつぶさに 開かせ給ふぞ尊けれ 生命の清水真清水は 貴の聖地に湧き出でて 限り知られぬ喜びに 満つるも楽し神の島 この島ケ根は昔より 黄金花咲く国ときく 黄金の花や白銀の 花散る後に瑠璃や硨磲 珊瑚、瑪瑙の玉実る

真珠や翡翠の蕾たわたわに 揺るるも楽し神の島 竜の都は今日よりは 老と若きのけぢめなく いやますますに栄ゆかむ 玉の緒の生命ますます長くして 世は高砂の松のかげ 鶴も来て鳴け雁も来よ 山はみどりに栄えませ 海もみどりにとこしへに 百の魚族うみおほせ うら安国のうら安く 栄えはてなき海原の 国土とふ国土を引きよせて 竜宮の島の誉をば いやとこしへにかがやかせ ああ惟神神の子の ここに現れ来し上は いやとこしへの春夏の 花の盛りを見るならむ

踊れよ踊れよ歌へよ歌へよ 天地の柱のゆるぐまで 舞へよ狂へよいつまでも 一日二日はまだおろか 七日七夜も引きつづき この喜びをとこしへに 祝ひ歌へよ祝ひ歌へよ 祝へよ歌へよ島の子よ』

この音頭につれて数万の竜神魚族は、大竜殿の内外に満ちあふれ踊り狂ふ状、天地もゆるがむばかりの盛況なりき。

海津見姫の神は次席より声さわやかに歌ふ。

『天晴れ国晴れ面白や この島ケ根に天地の 恵の雨は降りにけり 百の竜神魚族も 今日の吉き日の吉き時を 千代のかためと歌へかし 歌ふもよろし舞ふもよし 踊るもうべよ天地の 開けし時ゆためしなき この喜びはこの島の 外へはやらじと寿ぎ奉れ 主の神の御霊に生りし島ながら 顕津男の神来まさねば まだ浅ましき国津神の 数にも入らぬ竜体の あはれはかなき有様も 艶男の君、麗子の君は ここに渡らせ来ませしなり この島ケ根もうき立ちて まことの国となりぬべし

八尋の殿は築かれて 碧瓦赤壁美しけれど 住むもろもろの身体は 未だ全きを得ざるなり 今日の慶事に相つぎて 艶男の君を守り立てて 国津神等人種を 産めよ殖せよ 天の真砂のかずのごと 御空の星にくらぶべく 産めよ殖せよとこしへに』

斯く歌ひて元の座に着き給ふ。数多の竜神等はこの御歌に歓ぎ喜び、吾を忘れて舞ひ狂ふ。ここに大竜身彦の命はすつくと座を立ち、群集の中に進み出で、声も朗かに歌ふ。

『久方の空は晴れたり荒金の 地は開けり天津日は 射照り透らひ月読は この島ケ根を隈もなく 恵の露を降しまし 御空の星は真砂の如く 各自にかがやきて 今日のよろこび祝ふなり 吹き来る風もやはらかに 百津桂木のかげ清く 小鳥は歌ひ蝶は舞ひ 草葉にすだく虫の音も いやさやさやに聞ゆなり ああ天国は生れけり 蓬莱島は現れぬ 吾は今日よりこの島に 神の御種を産み殖し 住む竜神や諸々の 家族親族を安らかに 守り生かさむ惟神

喜び勇めこの殿の 内外に集ふ諸々よ 歌へよ舞へよ踊れよ狂へ 大地の底のぬけるまで』

と歌へば、百の竜神魚族等は、今日限りとばかり右往左往に舞ひ狂ふ。

麗子姫は弟姫神の資格にて、姿勢正しく階上より歌ふ。

『葭原の国土の真秀良場水上の 山の麓に現れませる 国の御祖の大神の 御子と生れて十七の 春を迎へし吾なるぞ 神の経綸の幸はひに 吾は思はずこの島に 渡らひ来り竜宮の 王となりて神の世を 造り固むる身となりぬ 汝竜神よ魚族よ この島ケ根も今日よりは 樛の木のいやつぎつぎに栄えつつ 八千代の椿うどんげの 花も時じく香るべし 喜び勇め諸々よ』

と簡単に歌ひ終り、元の席に着く。
