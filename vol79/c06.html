Chapter 6: 再会 [1987]

再会〔1987〕

§ 1

Escorted by the Kami Shihotsuchi and having landed on Riugūjima, Adeyaka stood before the iron gate,

Adeyaka: 'I indeed am the child of the Kami Kunitsumioya Make haste and cause the iron gate to be opened Seeking the traces of Uraraka, now here Have I come; oh iron gate, open swiftly.'

From within the gate, as the voice of a Megami,

Megami: 'Though I know not from which direction you are a Kami, this iron gate Without the permission of the Lord I shall not open Wait yet a little while, for to the august presence of Otohimegami I shall go and make inquiry.'

The Kami Shihotsuchi stood outside the gate with a solemn voice,

Shihotsuchi: 'I indeed am the Kami Shihotsuchi; inform the king of the Tatsugami That a person has been sent hither To open the island of Riugū, guiding a Masurao Have I come unto this place.'

The Megami hurried, running through the somewhat lengthy interior of the castle, and entered toward the inner chamber of Dairiuden, approaching the residential room of Otohimegami, clapping her hands, and with a quiet voice chanted in song to report:

Megami: 'What Kami you are I know not, but escorted by the Kami Shihotsuchi You have stood at the gate When I asked your name, you proclaimed yourself to be Adeyaka Praying to meet the Hime, you wait Shall I open the iron gate of the shore's edge? Desiring to hear your august word, I have made pilgrimage.'

The Kami Otohime, with longing, joy, and bashfulness becoming one, while changing even the color of her countenance swiftly, with an innocent face,

Otohime: 'Be that as it may, or however it may be, make haste And open the iron gate to welcome him As for Ōtatsumihiko, unfortunately upon the high mountain of Ibuki He is secluded, and thus things may not reach perfection, but Treat him with utmost cordiality, for that person Is the child of the ancestral Kami of the mountain above the waters.'

The Megami,

Megami: 'How awe-inspiring, obeying the august word of my lord I shall swiftly go to welcome him Since the august courtyard is very spacious, even though I make haste Pray wait for about half an hour.'

Having thus finished chanting, she withdrew from the august presence, and swaying her human-faced and dragon-bodied form back and forth and left and right, she drew nigh unto the iron gate of the shore, removed the bolt from within, and filling her whole face with smiles,

Megami: 'Since you proclaimed yourself to be the child of the ancestral Kami The Kami Otohime has greatly rejoiced Come now, serving as your companion I shall send you forth to the august presence of my lord Do you also advance together, O Kami Shihotsuchi The Kami Otohime awaits you.'

The Kami Shihotsuchi,

Shihotsuchi: 'Come now, following the words of the Hime Do you, Lord Adeyaka, advance I too shall follow in your august rear and proceed Unto the hall of the magistrate of the Tatsugami.'

Adeyaka chanted softly along the way.

Adeyaka: 'Ah, how mysterious, how mysterious! At the roots of the grass in the wild of Ōnogahara Turning into a soul and uttering a final vow Princess Uraraka now here Preserving her life had passed away That I should meet her once again From the age of the distant Kami of antiquity Is a joy without parallel Ah, is it a dream? Is it a dream? Is it a dream, a reality, or an illusion? No matter how much I ponder and reflect, Having ended up as a person of the other world Right after she spoke words to me She turned into a ball of fire and over the lake Darted away—to Uraraka That I should meet her once again in this world Is a dream beyond all expectation Though I think she lives by virtue Of the august divine instruction of the Kami Shihotsuchi Yet this is a riddle of today still hard to solve. Yet even so, I myself Am one who once left this world Saved by the compassion of the Kami Resurrected once more into this world Just as there are instances of living people Princess Uraraka too, in like manner, Rescued by some Kami or other, Saved upon this dragon isle, Must be one who shines brightly. Ah, how mysterious, how mysterious! Hearing that this is a floating world of dreams, Feeling as though treading a dream road, To meet a dream Hime in a dream realm— Today indeed is a joyful occasion! Ah, Kannagara Kamigami! May your august spirits prosper and bless us!'

Here, passing through the second tower gate, they advanced right through the middle of the courtyard paved with golden and silver sand toward the inner palace.

At the entrance of the inner palace, two serving maids, wearing white upper garments and crimson hakama, revealing slightly the tails of their dragon bodies, welcomed them courteously,

Serving Maids: 'Crossing the vast ocean from afar, having descended from heaven How noble is the attire of you, our lord! On this island, through the advent of the Kami Otohime All things have been resurrected Coming forth together with the Kami Shihotsuchi We adore the child of the ancestral Kami.'

水火土の神に送られて竜宮島に上陸したる艶男は、鉄門の前に立ち、

『われこそは国津御祖の神の子よ 早く鉄門を開けさせ給へ 麗子のあとを尋ねて今此処に 来りしものよ鉄門早や開け』

門の中より、女神の声として、

『いづ方の神か知らねど此の鉄門 主の許しなければ開かじ 今しばし待たせ給はれ弟姫の 神の御前に伺ひ来らむ』

水火土の神は声巌かに門外に立ち、

『われこそは水火土の神よ竜神の 王に知らせよ人送り来つと 竜宮の島を開くと大丈夫を われは導き此処に来れり』

女神は急ぎ、やや長き城内を馳せながら、大竜殿の奥の間さして参入し、弟姫神の居間に拍手しながら、声も静かに歌もて奏上する。

『何神かわれは知らねど水火土の 神に送られ門に立たせり 御名を問へばわれは艶男と宣らしけり 姫に会はむと待たせ給へる 汀辺の鉄門を開きまつらむや 御言聞かむとわれは詣でし』

弟姫の神は、恋しさと嬉しさ恥かしさが一度になり、顔の色までサツと変へながら、何喰はぬ顔にて、

『とにもあれかくにもあれやいち早く 鉄門を開き迎へまつれよ 大竜身彦は生憎伊吹の高山に 籠らひ給へばゆきとどかねど ねもごろに待遇して来よ其人は 水上の山の御祖の神の子よ』

女神は、

『畏しや君の御言に従ひて われ速に迎へ来らむ 御庭はいと広ければ急ぐとも 半時ばかりは待たさせ給はれ』

かく歌ひ終り、御前を罷り下り、人面竜身の体を前後左右にゆすりながら、汀の鉄門に近づき来り、中より閂を取り外し、満面に笑を湛へて、

『御祖神の御子におはすと宣りしより 弟姫の神は喜びましける いざさらばわれは御供に仕へつつ 君を御前に送りまつらむ 水火土の神も諸共進みませ 弟姫神は待たせ給へり』

水火土の神は、

『いざさらば姫の言葉に従ひて 艶男の君進みましませ われも亦御後に従ひ竜神の 司の館に進みゆかなむ』

艶男は道々小声にて歌ふ。

『ああいぶかしやいぶかしや 大野ケ原の草の根に 魂となりて言挙げし 麗子姫は今此処に 生命保ちてみまかりし われと再び会はむとは 遠き神代の昔より 例もあらぬ喜びぞ ああ夢なるや夢なるや 夢か現かまぼろしか 如何に考へすませども あの世の人となり果てて われに言とひすぐさまに 火団となりて湖の上を たばしりゆきし麗子に 又も此世に会はむとは 思ひもよらぬ夢なれや 水火土の神の御教に 生きておはすと思へども なほ解きかぬる今日の謎

さはさりながらわれとても 一度は此世を去りしもの 神の情に助けられ 再びこの世に蘇り 生きたるためしもあるものを 麗子姫も其の如く 何れの神にか救はれて この竜島に助けられ 輝き給ふものならむ ああ訝しや訝しや 夢の浮世と聞くからは 夢路をたどる心地して 夢の国なる夢の姫に 会はむ今日こそ楽しけれ ああ惟神々々 御霊幸倍坐世』

ここに第二の楼門を潜り、金砂銀砂の敷きつめられし庭の真中を奥殿さして進み入る。

奥殿の入口には、二人の侍女白き上衣に紅き袴を穿ち、少しく竜体の尻尾を現しながら、慇懃に迎へ、

『海原をはろばろ越えて天降りましし 君のよそほひ尊きろかも この島は弟姫神の出でましに 総てのものは蘇りける 水火土の神と諸共出でましし 御祖の神の御子を拝む』

§ 2

Adeyaka responding to this,

Adeyaka: 'Rescued across the plain of the sea by the god Shihotsuchi, having crossed over and come to the island of rare wonders, treading upon the gardens where gold and silver sands gleam, you have revived with a pure heart— is not she who is called the goddess Otohime none other than Urarakahime of Minakamiyama?'

The attendant goddess responding to this,

Attendant Goddess: 'Your lord's words are true; Otohime is indeed she who was called Urarakahime. Rejoicing at your advent, Urarakahime awaits you; come, I shall guide you.'

Chanting thus, together with the god Shihotsuchi, they led the way to the inner palace.

Although the goddess Otohime rejoiced to see her elder brother's visit, since she had already become the wife of the mikoto Ōtatsumihiko and was in a station revered as the ruler of this land, no matter how much she yearned for him as a beloved brother, she could not simply converse at ease as in former days. Moreover, feeling great awe toward the attendant deities as well, her heart throbbed with emotion.

Though the brother and sister had spoken of their love to one another and vowed their very lives together, because they had not yet received the permission of their parents, there had been no union between them, and their relationship remained pure and undefiled. Thus Adeyaka harbored no resentment in his heart that she had become the wife of the mikoto Ōtatsumihiko, and seeing her dimly through the bamboo blinds with a mind at peace, he sang:

Adeyaka: 'In the brief moment when your body was hidden, my heart grew impatient and I was distraught. With the spirit of you who lurked among the thickets, how I longed to converse even for a little while! Following the trail of you who fled, transformed into a ball of fire, I have come hither to this place, seeking you. Thinking perhaps you had perished, I too flung my body into the waters of Lake Tamayako. By a fortunate chance rescued by the god Shihotsuchi, my life has revived once more. If you, who are now no younger sister but the ruler of this land, stand as its sovereign, a hesitant doubt stirs in my heart.'

From within the blinds, the goddess Otohime sang with a clear voice,

Otohime: 'Glimpsing the face of my lord and cousin, the fire in my heart has leapt into flame. Even so, since our bond is purely of the untainted, noble sort, there can be nothing to be ashamed of in the world. Before the august presence where the god Shihotsuchi abides, let us make our pure hearts known to each other.'

Adeyaka sang,

Adeyaka: 'Awe-inspiring are the words of the princess goddess! From now on I shall revere you as the lady of the dragon's island. Since our hearts, deep within, are pure, I shall not lament even if my life should end. Not long after you crossed over to this island, you became its ruler, o valiant one! If I should convey this state of affairs to our parents, they will surely rejoice as though returned from the dead.'

The god Shihotsuchi,

Shihotsuchi: 'The pure heart and valor of the princess goddess shall I relate in detail to the dragon-bodied Hiko. The god Shihotsuchi will bear witness for you both, that you are a brother and sister of pure heart.'

As things were thus, led by Harukihiko and Natsuwakahiko, the mikoto Ōtatsumihiko returned leisurely, followed behind by Akimizuhiko and Fuyukazehiko. Beholding the scene before them, with a most joyful air they exchanged nods of greeting with the two, and said,

Ōtatsumihiko: 'You must be the god Shihotsuchi, a single deity— which of the gods is it that has so well come hither? The island-realm of Ryūgū has at last been opened by the precious light of the goddess Otohime. Is not Adeyakahiko, of whom I have heard tales from the goddess Otohime, your very self?'

Adeyaka responded in song,

Adeyaka: 'I am indeed the elder brother of Urarakahime, a kunitsukami named Adeyaka. That unexpectedly I pursued Urarakahime from behind and have crossed over to this island root, and that Otohime has become the wife of your mikoto and serves you—to hear this brings me great joy.'

The mikoto Ōtatsumihiko responded with majestic solemnity,

Ōtatsumihiko: 'How marvelous it is that a dear brother and sister have descended from heaven to this island! Come then, advance further into the inner chambers; various fine things shall I set before you. Bowing down in reverence before the achievements of the god Shihotsuchi, I have been plunged into tears of joy.'

As things were thus, the goddess Otohime swiftly raised the blinds, and bowing respectfully before the mikoto Ōtatsumihiko,

Otohime: 'My lord has returned! My lord, with a serene heart you converse with the brother! Hearing the august mind of my lord, now once more I find myself overcome with tears of joy. Come then, let us follow my lord and advance into the great inner hall, together with my lord Adeyaka.'

Adeyaka responded immediately in song,

Adeyaka: 'Let us follow along behind your august steps into the great inner hall; do you lead the way, o goddess Otohime! Advance before us, if the god Shihotsuchi be the very parent of life itself!'

Hereupon, the two deities followed in the wake of the goddess Otohime, and together with the mikoto Ōtatsumihiko, ascending the steps of the corridor, they advanced into the great inner hall.

Escorted by the god Shihotsuchi, has Adeyaka reached the iron gate by the shore? Guided by the princess goddess, and once more passing through the iron gate, he enters the inner palace of Ryūgū. Treading barefoot upon the sands of gold and silver, he was ushered into the sitting room of Otohime. Even before he could exchange words with the goddess Otohime, Ōtatsumihiko had returned. Ōtatsumihiko greatly rejoiced at the visit of the two and guided them to the inner chambers. Following right behind the august footsteps of the goddess Otohime, Shihotsuchi and Adeyaka entered the great inner hall.
