Chapter 17: 還元 Dragon Kami [1998]

還元竜神〔1998〕

§ 1

Shirahagi, while sinking into deep sorrow together with Shiragiku, entered the forest of Yutsukatsuragi, and standing in the shade of a Komorigi, sang of her Jiyutsukuwai, intending to weep to her heart's Zonbun.

Shiragiku: 'Though it is fragrant, there is no one to Tao the single flower; alas, I am but a wild Nogiku. The single Nogiku trembling in the Ibukiyama storm, whither shall it drift? Though flowers are fragrant in many ways, the flower of the Shiragiku is pathetic, hidden in the grass. Where is the fragrance of the flower of Adeyaka? I have no way to hear news of the wind. The Shiragiku flower that the Lord Adeyaka would have Tao-ed, is it not destined to wither from the start? The flower of the Shiragiku, its cotton-covering blown away, has withered in the dew of tears. Without even the dew of the affection of the Lord I love, alas, the Shiragiku is about to wither. The dew of the spray of the Takitsuse, is my Inochi as a Shiragiku but ephemeral? When I look at the Mizukagami, the flower of the Shiragiku in the palace of the dragon blooming in Yahe is shameful. There is no Kahi in having enjoyed the fragrance of the chrysanthemum for Yachitose, for the Akikaze has blown.

To think that I would offer the Shiragiku to the Mimahe, adorning myself with it, was but a dream. The fragrance of the Shiragiku, which preserves the Shirogane Iroka, has faded and the Kogarashi is cold. You are pale, and the color of my Shiragiku is deep; it is sad to pine for the days that were. The flower that was fragrant in the precious Shimane of the Tatsugami scatters in the Kogarashi, ephemeral as it is. The depth of my heart that yearns for you, has the Lord ascended to the Kumoi? Thinking that even if I lament, it will not return, I still grieve in the shade of the forest. Because of you, the extension and contraction of my living Inochi in this world is a sad, Utakata dream. If we did not meet, I would not have to hurt my heart like this; the suffering of your faded Iroka.'

Shirahagi sings:

'You have already become the Kogarashi blowing upon the Utena of the Adahagi in my form. Did you loathe the appearance of the Shirahagi leaning in the dew, and hide yourself in the clouds? Though I lament, I know not what to do; I have become a pathetic flower, the Shirahagi. Even if I run through the Ama and run through the earth, I think only of following the Lord I love. Thinking of the Inochi that dies in vain, I wish to meet you once more. If my heart blooms purely and correctly, the dew of the Shirahagi will conceive. The flower of the Shirahagi, which vividly scents the country, will not wither in the dry dew. I do not begrudge the Inochi of the dew of the Shirahagi, but what shall I do about the child I have conceived? The goddess of this village has all surely conceived the child of Adeyaka. Even if a precious, beautiful child is born, how sad it is to think that it has no father.'

At such a time, Ominaeshi, sharing the same thoughts, covering her face with her long sleeves, entered alone and sorrowfully, and seeing the two goddesses hiding in the shade of the Katsuragi tree, she was slightly surprised and said:

'Shirahagi and Shiragiku, fragrant at the foot of Ibukiyama, am I not, Ominaeshi, superior to you? Since your figures have not been seen all night long, I have not slept and the dawn has come. The Himegami are all hiding their figures, lamenting in the shade of trees here and there. I too have come secretly to this forest shade, wondering if I should weep, avoiding the eyes of others.'

Shirahagi and Shiragiku, seeing Ominaeshi arrive, opened their eyes in surprise and sang bashfully:

'The heart that loves is the same, and today, as fellow friends weeping together, is a sad day. Even if we lament, we must pray to the gods of the Ama and earth, for it may not reach them, and wait for the day we meet. Weeping and weeping with no way to act, I am hiding my shame and lamenting in the shade of the forest.'

Just as they were singing thus, there was a Kami who came in, rustling the forest of Yutsukatsuragi. When they looked closely, could they have imagined it? It was the figure of Adeyaka, whom they loved and yearned for, risking their Inochi.

The three goddesses were startled and could not even speak, gazing blankly at the figure of the pure man. Adeyaka, standing upright on the lush branches of the Yutsukatsuragi, sang quietly:

'Drawn by the rope of sincerity, I have now crossed over to the living Kototama. It is not that I do not know the lament of the Himegami, but please forgive my presence today. My body has returned to Mount Minakami. I am the Mitama drawn to you. Though I think of returning far to Mount Minakami, I cannot move by the power of your sincerity. In any case, forgive me; after some years, I will surely come to see you. Kakitsubatahime is on Mount Minakami, and she must be shining upon the people of the country. How could I forget the gentle heart of the Shirahagi, fragrant at the foot of Ibukiyama? How could I forget the pure appearance of the Shiragiku? Wait for a while. I keep the gentle hand-gestures of the Ominaeshi flower in my heart, longingly and joyfully. Now then, I will calm my Mitama on the top of Mount Ibuki and guard the imperial garden.'

白萩は白菊と共に深き憂に沈みながら、百津桂樹の森に分け入り、思ふ存分泣かむものと、籠樹の蔭に立ちて述懐を歌ふ。

白菊『匂へども手折る人なき一本の あはれ野菊は吾なりにけり 伊吹山嵐にふるふ一本の あはれ野菊はいづらになびかむ いろいろと花は匂へど白菊の 花はかなしも草にかくれて 艶男の花の香りはいづらなる 風の便りを聞くよしもなし 艶男の君の手折らす白菊の 花はもとより枯れむとすらむ きせ綿を吹きはらはれし白菊の 花は涙の露にしをれつ 吾恋ふる君の情の露もなく あはれ白菊枯れなむとすも 滝津瀬のしぶきの露も白菊の 吾ははかなき生命なるかも 八重に咲く竜の宮居の白菊の 花恥かしも水鏡見れば 八千年の菊の香りを楽しみし 甲斐もあらなく秋風吹きぬ

白菊をかざして御前に奉ると 思ひしことは夢なりしかな 白銀の色香を保つ白菊の 薫りはあせて木枯寒し 君は淡く吾白菊の色は濃く 在りしその日をしのべばかなし 竜神の貴の島根に匂ひてし 花ははかなく木枯に散るも 吾思ふ心の丈も白菊の 君は雲井にのぼりましけむ なげきても返らぬものと思ひつつ なほ歎かるる森の下かげ 君ゆゑに生きの生命の延びちぢみ ある世はかなし泡沫の夢 会はざればかくも心をいためまじ 君が色香のあせたるくるしさ』

白萩は歌ふ。

『君は早や吾の姿にあき萩の うてなに吹ける木枯なりしよ 白萩の露にかたむくよそほひを 君はいとひて雲がくれせしか 歎けども如何にせむすべしら萩の 吾はかなしき花なりにけり 天かけり地かけるとも恋ふる君の 後をば追はむとひたに思ふも いたづらに死する生命の思はれて 今一度を会はむとぞ思ふ 吾心清く正しく花咲けば 想像妊娠まむ白萩の露 あざやかに御国に匂ふ白萩の 花もしをれぬ乾ける露に 白萩の露の生命は惜しまねど 想像妊娠みし子をいかにせむ この里の女神はことごと艶男の 御子をまさしく想像妊娠める よしやよし貴のうまし子生まるとも 父なき思へば如何にかなしき』

かかる時、同じ思ひの女郎花は長袖に面を覆ひながら、只一人悄然として入り来り、桂樹の蔭に二人の女神のひそめるを見て、稍驚きながら、

『伊吹山匂ふ白萩、白菊の 君にまさずや吾は女郎花よ よもすがら君の姿の見えぬより 吾はい寝ずに明けにけらしな 姫神はいづれも姿をかくしつつ あなたこなたの樹蔭に歎けり 吾も亦人目をよぎて泣かむかと この森かげにしのび来にけり』

白菊、白萩は、女郎花の来れるに驚きの目を見はりながら、恥かしげに歌ふ。

『恋すてふ心はおなじ友垣の 共泣く今日はかなしき日なるよ 歎くとも及ばざるらむ天地の 神に祈りて会ふ日待つべし せむすべも泣く泣く吾は森蔭に 恥をしのびて歎かひ居るなり』

斯く歌ふ折しも、百津桂樹の森をそよがせて入り来る神あり。よくよく見れば思ひきや、生命をかけて恋ひ慕ふ艶男の姿なりける。

三人の女神は、はつと驚きながら物をも得言はず、呆然として清しき男子の姿を眺め居る。艶男は百津桂樹の茂枝に直立しながら静に歌ふ。

『真心の綱に引かれて吾は今 生言霊に渡り来れり 姫神の歎きは知らぬにあらねども 今日の吾身を許し給はれ 身体は水上山に帰りたり 君にひかるる御魂の吾よ 水上山遠く帰ると思へども 汝が誠の力に動けず 兎も角も吾を許せよいく年の 後には必ず来りまみえむ 燕子花姫は水上の山に在りて 輝きにけむ国人の上に 伊吹山麓に匂ふ白萩の やさしき心吾忘れめや 白菊の清きよそほひ如何にして 吾は忘れむ暫しを待ちませ 女郎花やさしき花の御手振りを 恋しく楽しく心に止むる いざさらば吾は伊吹の山の上に 身魂鎮めて御園を守らむ』

§ 2

Saying this, Adeyaka placed his Reishin upon the suddenly blowing Unakaze, and hid himself deep within the Shirakumo that encircled the mountain ridge.

Here, the three goddesses Shirahagi, Shiragiku, and Ominaheshi, realizing that Adeyaka and Kakitsubata were living deep in the mountains of Minakami with their physical bodies, could not restrain themselves; desiring to cross over Tamayako by any means and fulfill their long-held wishes, they instantly transformed into their original Riutai, plunged into the Kochiu with a splash, and began to swim across the surface of the waves, heading toward the south.

At last, the three Mihashira Tatsugami arrived safely at the Hamabe of Urami, and as it was the middle of the night with the moon shining brightly, they secretly ascended from the Kahaguchi of Ōigaha toward the Seichi of Minakamiyama. The Tatsugami, having once undergone Kuwangen, could not easily maintain their human faces, and thus they came to dwell in hiding in a place where trees grew thick, called Fuji-no-oka on the opposite bank of the Ōigaha.

From this time on, Adeyaka was drawn night after night by the souls of the three Tatsugami, and suddenly longing for the banks of the Ōigaha, he would ride his horse and cross the river whenever he had leisure, to play in the valleys of Fujigaoka.

The beach of Naminohana-sakae-i, since the Tatsugami came across, is called the beach of Urami.

原文（近代文語）

と言ひつつ、さつと吹く湖風に艶男は霊身をのせ、山の尾の上を取り巻く白雲の奥深くかくれける。

ここに白萩、白菊、女郎花の三女神は、艶男、燕子花の二人は肉体共に水上の山深く住めることを悟り、矢も楯もたまらず、如何にしても玉耶湖を打ち渡り、日頃の思ひを達せむと、忽ち元の竜体と変じ、ざんぶと許り湖中に飛び込み、波の面をおよぎながら、南をさして進み行く事とはなりぬ。

漸くにして三柱の竜神は、浦水の浜辺に安着せるが、恰も月照り渡る真夜中頃なりければ、大井川の川口より窃に水上山の聖地をさして上る事とはなりぬ。一旦還元したる竜神は容易に人面を保つ事能はず、大井川の対岸なる藤の丘と言ふ樹木密生せる個所に忍び棲む事とはなりぬ。

之より艶男は三竜神の魂に夜な夜な引き込まれ、俄に大井川の川辺恋しくなりて、遑ある毎に駒をうたせ川を渡りて、藤ケ丘の谷間に遊びける。

波の花栄居の浜も竜神の 渡り来しより浦水の浜とふ。
