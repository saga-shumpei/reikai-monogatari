Chapter 8: The Episode of 相聞 (Part II) [1989]

相聞（二）〔1989〕

§ 1

Adeyaka was avoiding the sharp edge of the love of the Megamitachi, and while he was letting out deep breaths in the shade of the Yutsu Katsuragi by the shore of Kagamiko, an Otome in Biyakue and Kōko suddenly appeared from the thicket nearby, holding a full bloom of Shirahagi flowers in her Mete, and while offering a Tamamohi in her Yunde, she approached close to the side of Adeyaka, and with a face full of smiles, she placed the water of the Tamamohi before Adeyaka.

Shirahagi: 'O rare Adeyaka-kimi, partake of this Mohi. As the heat of Kefu makes the Nodo thirsty, I am but an Otome of the Shirahagi, moistened by the dew, bashful of my flowers. Do you not admire the frail figure of the Shirahagi that sways even in the gentle breeze? The flowers of the Shirahagi are pure, resembling the Mashimizu that is transparent to the very bottom of Kagamiko. With the joy of Kefu, I offer you Mohi in the Tamamohi, adding the fragrance of the Shirahagi. While you scoop the Mashimizu, please admire the fragrance of the flowers. The Shirahagi blooms lonely on Mount Ibuki, knowing no distinction between Natsufuyu.'

Adeyaka replied to this:

Adeyaka: 'Grateful am I for the kindness of the Otome-kimi. I shall partake of it in the shade of the Katsuragi. The water of the lake is pure, and the flowers of the Shirahagi are lovely; is that the figure of an Otome? Though Momochibana bloom and are fragrant, it is sad that I have not the strength to Tao.'

Shirahagi sang:

Shirahagi: 'Where does your heart lie? I am also a bashful Shirahagi flower. Nevertheless, please Tao the Shirahagi, even until the dew upon the flowers spills over. Moistened by the dew of your affection, O Kimi, I, the Shirahagi flower, shall live. Though Momobana and Chibana bloom and are fragrant on this island, I am but a single Hitomoto of Shirahagi. If the wind blows, will the dew spill and the flowers scatter? Quickly Tao this single Hitomoto of Shirahagi.'

Adeyaka sang:

Adeyaka: 'I, who see the Shirahagi flowers as beautiful, am but an Ichijiyuku growing upon a rock. I am one who bears no fruit, growing in the shade of an Ichijiyuku that has no flowers.'

Shirahagi sang again:

Shirahagi: 'The depth of your troubled heart is visible even to the tips of the Shirahagi; you are the mountain wind. No matter how fiercely the mountain wind may blow, the Shirahagi in the Kokage does not intend to scatter. I shall offer to you, Kimi, the dew upon the Shirahagi flowers I hold as a token of my heart. Even if there is no dew of affection, I shall bloom slenderly only by your side. This is my longing, which I have barely managed to hide from the eyes of others until now.'

Adeyaka, feeling flustered by the words filled with the affection of Shirahagi, looked out at the surface of Kagamiko as if nothing were the matter. Shirahagi, pointing to the small waves on the water's surface, said:

Shirahagi: 'Look upon the small waves of Kagamiko; the heart at the bottom must be unmoved. My longing, the depth of which even Kagamiko does not know, is reflected in the fragrant Shirahagi flowers. Do you not know, or do you not look upon, the depth of my longing that goes eight thousand fathoms deep? Do you not think that I would have no regrets even if my tama-no-o life were to vanish for your sake? If it be so, I shall not move even if my life should die, until you understand my deep heart. Though Momogusa and Chigusa bloom and are fragrant on Mount Ibuki, there is no flower that surpasses your appearance. Are you, Kimi, whom I long for, staking my tama-no-o life, but a heartless thistle flower?'

Adeyaka sang:

Adeyaka: 'Struck by your various Kototama, I have not even a word of reply. Having come from afar, treading upon the crests of the waves, I did not think I would have such troubles. Surrounded by the Otome of the Ryūgū island, my path forward has been blocked. When I think of the grace of my parents, which is higher than the mountains, a heart of spring cannot arise. I am one who trembles, exposed morning and evening to the blue storm blowing from the ridge of Mount Ibuki. Because the storm of love of the Ryūgū is strong, my body is about to scatter in vain.'

Just as he was singing this, three Megamitachi appeared, parting the waves of Kagamiko to the left and right. The one who seemed to be the leader stood in front, and the slightly younger Otome stood in the rear, and while crossing the water surface with ease, they approached the shade of the Yutsu Katsuragi where Adeyaka was resting, smiling gently. Upon seeing this, Shirahagi immediately prostrated herself on the ground, and while shouting 'Woo, woo,' she continued her respectful worship without fail, remaining crouched without moving, with her head pressed to the ground. The Megamitachi bowed slightly in silence to Adeyaka, who was resting in the shade, and opened their crimson lips.

原文（近代文語）

艶男は女神等の恋の鋭鋒をさけて、鏡の湖畔なる百津桂木の樹蔭に太き息をもらしつつありしが、忽然として辺の叢よりあらはれたる白衣、紅袴の乙女は、右手に白萩の花の満開せるを持ち、左手に玉水盃を捧げながら、艶男の側近く進み寄り、満面笑みを湛へて、玉水盃の水を艶男の前に差し置き、

『珍しき艶男の君よ御水召せよ 今日の暑さは咽喉のかわけば わらはこそは露にうるほふしら萩の 花はづかしき乙女なりけり そよと吹く風にも靡く白萩の かよわき姿を愛で給はずや 白萩の花は清しも鏡湖の 底まで透ける真清水に似て 白萩の香りを添へて玉水盃に 御水奉る今日の嬉しさ 真清水を掬ばせながら白萩の 花の香りを愛でさせ給へ 夏冬のけぢめもしらに白萩は 伊吹の山に淋しく匂ふも』

艶男はこれに答へて、

『ありがたし乙女の君の志 われいただかむ桂木の蔭に 湖の水は清しも白萩の 花は床しも乙女の姿か 百千花咲き匂へども吾にして 手折らむ力なきぞかなしき』

白萩は歌ふ。

『汝が心いづらにあるか白萩の 花はづかしき吾にもあるかな さりながら手折らせ給へ白萩の 花におく露こぼるるまでも 汝が君の情の露にうるほひて 吾は生くべき白萩の花よ この島に百花千花匂へども ただ一本の白萩のわれ 風吹かば露やこぼれむ花散らむ 早く手折らへ一本の白萩』

艶男は歌ふ。

『白萩の花美しと見る吾は 岩の上に生ふ無花実なるよ 花のなき無花実のかげに繁りたる 草花にして実のなき吾なり』

白萩は又歌ふ。

『なやましき心の丈をしら萩の 梢もみゆく汝は山風 山風は如何にはげしく吹くとても 木蔭の白萩散らむとはせず わが持てる白萩の花に露とめて 心のあかしと君にまゐらす よしやよし露の情はあらずとも 君のみそばに細く咲くべし 人の目を漸くここにしのび草 しのびかねたるわが思ひかな』

艶男は白萩の情のこもる言葉に、ほつと当惑しながら、こともなげに鏡の湖面を眺めてゐる。白萩は水面の小波を指さしながら、

『みそなはせ鏡の湖の小波の 底の心は動かざるらむ 鏡湖の底ひもしらぬわが思ひ 写して匂ふ白萩の花よ 八千尋の底ひもしらぬ思ひねを 君は知らずやみそなはさずや 君のために玉の緒の生命消ゆるとも 悔いなき吾と思召さずや 斯くならば生命死すとも動かまじ 深き心をくみとらすまでは 伊吹山百草千草匂へども 君のよそほひに勝る花なし 玉の緒の生命をかけて恋ひ慕ふ 君は情なき薊の花かも』

艶男は歌ふ。

『さまざまの汝が言霊に打出され 吾はいらへの言葉だになし はろばろと波の秀ふみて来ながらに かかるなやみは思はざりけり 竜宮の島の乙女に囲まれて 吾は行手をふさがれにけり 足引の山より高き父母の恩 おもへば春の心起らじ 伊吹山尾根より吹き来る青嵐を 朝夕あびて戦く吾なり 竜宮の恋の嵐の強ければ わが身はかなく散らむとするも』

斯く歌ふ折しも、鏡湖の波を左右に分けて、あらはれ来る三柱の女神あり。主と思しきは前に立ち、稍年若き乙女は後辺に立ち、悠々として水面を渡りながら、艶男の憩ふ百津桂木の樹蔭に、莞爾として寄り来る。白萩はこれを見るより、忽ち大地にひれ伏し、「ウオーウオー」と叫びながら恭敬礼拝怠りなく、頭を大地につけたるまま身動きもせず、うづくまり居る。女神は樹蔭に憩ふ艶男に軽く黙礼し、紅の唇を開き、

§ 2

"'Since I heard that the Miko of the Mioyagami exists, Splitting the watery depths, I have come. I am the beloved daughter of the god Ōwadatsumi, The deity called Wadatsumihime. Although the base of the island of Riugū is still young, Build it up using thy strength. Build and solidify this island, give birth to Mikos, And let them protect it forever and ever. The base of the island of Riugū is an island base of love, A divine destined island where flowers of love bloom. To this island where Amata Himegami dwell, Thou who hast crossed over art indeed a god of salvation. Rescue the Megami who do not possess A complete Jintai, in accordance with the divine will. All the hundred kusaki of Ametsuchi Have been planted and grown on this island base. This island is the Mahoraba of the god Wadatsumi, Where I also come and play Oriori. The mirror-like lake with pure water is the entrance To the Miyai of the god Wadatsumi. Having come here, thou must not hold a weak heart; It is an island base where flawless Mikos ought to be born. Grant every single request of the Yasohime, And cause this island base to flourish.'"

Adeyaka timidly answered this:

"'How can I meet a hundred Megami, When I am a single person with a weak Karatama? Having unexpectedly crossed over to this island base, I have been set upon by a hundred Otome. Though I think that I, a man, shall not flinch, How shall I subdue their jealous hearts? Knowing it is the nature of Himegami To possess jealous hearts, I am hesitating.'"

The god Wadatsumihime spoke gracefully:

"'Speak not of weak-hearted things, for thou art a man; Thou oughtest to be Ō and pure. Since thou art a lord bearing the name of Adeyaka, How couldst thou be overcome by a jealous heart? Since the Megami of this island are all Kami without exception, There is not a dewdrop of jealous heart in them. Since it is the work of building and solidifying a young Kuni, Proceed without hesitation.'"

Adeyaka answered this, singing:

"'Though I think the hearts of the Himegami are good, I am a mere Kunitsukami, far beneath them.'"

The god Wadatsumi spoke:

"'Passing the days and spending the time, Tsugitsugi, Mayest thou proceed with a brave and manly heart.'"

Singing thus, the Himegami quietly hid her form deep within the palace of Ōriushinhiko-no-Mikoto together with her two handmaidens, lightly stepping over the ears of silver grass. Adeyaka and Shirahagi solemnly saw the Himegami off, stretching up and up to gaze until her divine shadow could no longer be seen, whereupon Adeyaka, struck by feelings of doubt, crossed his hands, let out a deep breath, and sank into thought. Shirahagi approached to the point where her white-robed sleeves touched Adeyaka's breath, and opening her gaze coolly as if dripping with dew, she displayed all her charms and attempted to grasp Adeyaka's left hand; at this, Adeyaka was astonished as if by a bolt from the blue, leapt backward twenty or thirty steps, and stared dumbfounded at Shirahagi's face. Shirahagi raised a sorrowful voice and sang:

"'Alas, the lord whom I cherish Was a tempest blowing through the treetops of the Shirahagi. However strong the rain, hail, and tempest may be, I, the Shirahagi, shall not falter. Since thou art the lord who hast crossed over to this island, I shall by no means let thee escape, with a fierce heart. Didst thou not hear the divine words of the Himegami? Without faltering or flinching, pray snap me off. Art thou frightened of my humble Karatama, And rejecting me, O heartless lord? Though my Karatama was born as a dragon body, How could there be any difference in the heart called love?'"

Adeyaka hardened the pit of his resolve, and seeking to escape the danger of the moment for a short while, sang glumly with folded arms:

"'Overcome by shame and fondness, I merely Hesitated on the path to snap thee off. Even so, pray wait for a short while, For I have a certain illness. Waiting for this illness to be cured, there shall be a good day When I shall meet thee, the Shirahagi.'"

Shirahagi sang obediently, her whole face shining:

"'There was value in exhausting my innermost heart; The heart of Lord Adeyaka hath moved. In my joy, my soul hath brightened; No matter how many days I wait, I shall hold no grudge. Not knowing thou wert such a gentle lord, I am ashamed of my heart that troubled thee. Come then, I shall escort thee to the palace; Pray set out quickly from the shade of the Katsuragi trees.'"

At the wind of Adeyaka's words, Shirahagi's heart bent quietly; Though his innermost heart was not satisfied, Adeyaka Returned to the palace together with Shirahagi.

『御祖神の御子あれますと聞きしより そこひを分けて吾は来にけり 吾こそは大海津見の神の愛娘 海津見姫とよばるる神なり 竜宮の島根は未だ稚けれど 汝が力をもちてつくれよ この島をつくり固めて御子を生み いや永久に守らせ給へ 竜宮の島根は恋の島ケ根よ 愛の花咲く神さだめ島 姫神の数多住まへるこの島に 渡りし君は助けの神ぞや 人体をうまらにつばらに備へざる 女神を救へ神のまにまに 天地の百の草木をことごとく この島ケ根に植ゑ生はしける この島は海津見の神の真秀良場よ われも折々来りてあそべる 水清き鏡の湖は海津見の 神の宮居の入口ぞかし

ここに来て弱き心をもたすまじ 全き神子を生むべき島根よ 八十姫の願ひことごときき入れて この島ケ根をにぎはせ給へ』

艶男はおそるおそるこれに答ふ。

『如何にして百の女神にまみえなむ 身体の弱きひとり身吾は ゆくりなくこの島ケ根に渡り来て 百の乙女におそはれにける 男の子吾ひるまじものとおもへども ねたみ心を如何にをさめむ ねたましき心をもつは姫神の 常と知る故ためらひ居るなり』

海津見姫の神は淑やかに宣り給ふ。

『心弱きことを宣らすな汝は男の子 雄々しく清しくあるべきものを 艶男の名を負ひませる君なれば ねたみ心におそはるべしやは この島の女神はのこらず神なれば ねたむ心は露程もなし 稚き国土をつくり固むる業なれば ためらふことなく進ませ給へ』

艶男はこれに答へて、

『姫神の心はよしと思へども 吾には及ばず国津神の身よ』

海津見の神は、

『日を重ね時をけみして次々に 心雄々しく進ませ給はむ』

斯く歌ひながら姫神は静々と二人の侍女と共に、芒の穂の上を軽く渡りながら、大竜身彦の命の宮殿深く御姿をかくさせ給ひぬ。艶男と白萩は姫神を粛然として見送り、御影の見えぬ迄伸び上り伸び上り眺めて居たが、ここに艶男は不審の念にうたれつつ、諸手を組み、太き息をもらし思案にくれてゐる。白萩は白衣の袖の艶男の息のさはるところまで近寄り来り、露の滴るまなざしを涼しくひらきながら、あらゆる媚を呈し、艶男の左手を握らむとせしにぞ、艶男は青天の霹靂の如く驚き、二三十歩後方に飛び退き、呆然として白萩の面を見つめてゐる。白萩はかなしき声をはり上げながら、

『情なやわがおもふ君は白萩の 梢をもみゆく嵐なりけり 雨霰嵐は如何に強くとも たゆまざるべし白萩の吾は この島に渡り来ませる君なれば 如何でのがさじ弥猛心に 姫神の御言葉汝はきかざるや たゆまずひるまず手折らせ給へ いやしかるわが身体におぢおそれ いなみ給ふか情なき君 身体はよし竜体と生るとも 恋てふ心に変りあるべき』

艶男は決心の臍を固め、しばしの虎口を遁れむと、腕を組みながら憮然として歌ふ。

『はづかしさ恋しさあまりてただ吾は 手折らむ道をためらひにけり さりながらしばしを待たせ給へかし 吾には一つの病ありせば この病癒ゆるを待ちて白萩の 君にまみえむよき日あるべし』

白萩は満面を輝かしながら素直に歌ふ。

『村肝の心つくせし甲斐ありて 艶男の君の心うごきぬ 嬉しさにわが魂は輝きぬ 幾日まつとも吾は恨まじ 斯の如やさしき君と知らずして なやめ奉りし心はづかし いざさらば御殿に送り奉るべし 早立たせませ桂木の蔭を』

艶男の言葉の風に白萩は 心静かに打ちなびきたり 村肝の心満たねど艶男は 白萩と共に御殿にかへれり。
