Chapter 20: 産の悩み [2001]

産の悩み〔2001〕

§ 1

Adeyaka's betsumei is called Tachibana. Adeyaka-Tachibana, together with his wife Kakitsubata, attempted a half-day of seiiu by floating a new boat on the Ōigaha, in the early summer when the sky was clear and the wind was pure. The water was yōyō, shining in the natsuhi, and as the current flowed gently, it created riyōmishinshin with the soft sazanami that blew.

Adeyaka sings:

The yufugure in the village where the hanatachibana blossoms fall / The wind also carries their scent, how pure it is / The scent of the tachibana, so refined, upon the minomo / Has drifted, in this early summer / Upon the feet that tread where the tachibana scent lingers / The fragrance remains, how pure it is upon the boat.

Kakitsubata sings:

With the hanatachibana, fragrant with its scent, as my guide / I have come from the shimane of the riugū / When compared to the hanatachibana of the riugū / You are a person of such refined fragrance / The appearance of you, my beloved, for whom I yearn / Is like the fragrant akara-tachibana / The tachibana that blooms, brightening the satsukiyami / You are the light of my very inochi / Yearning for the scent of the blossoms that are not ordinary / I, the tachibana, upon the minomo at yufugure / The blossoms of the tachibana are refined, and even their fruit / Even the blossoms are hitoshiho, being magnificent / Feeling as if I have touched the branches of the tachibana / I have clung to your sleeves / Along the kahabe of the Ōigaha where the water is clear / The purple-scented kakitsubata, how pathetic / To you, the tachibana whose scent is pure asayufu / I have entrusted my inochi / Night after night, bathed in your scent / I long for the hanatachibana.

My sleeves are fragrantly scented, even while being exposed / To the wind blowing over the river upon the boat / Is the pillow of my dreams night after night also fragrant? / With the scent of the sleeves of you, the hanatachibana / The stars from the hisakata kumoma have also descended / And shine upon the tachibana / You, who scent the asayufu of my dwelling / Are you staying close to the ihakakitsubata? / Pushing the pole along the kahabe where the kakitsubata is fragrant / I have no worries in today's play / Though the scent of the blossoms may not transfer, I swear by the kami / That I pray for the happiness of us futari.

Adeyaka sings:

With you, the kakitsubata, weaving the patterns of the waves of the Ōigaha / I enjoy playing in the river / The color of the sazanami also scents of purple / You are perhaps a blossom of the riugū, so refined / The tachibana whose shadow is cast upon the kahamizu / The face of you, whose blossoms are beautiful / The scent of the hanatachibana that permeates my sleeves / As I play in the boat with you, my beloved / When I think of the arm-pillow of my younger sister, where the kakitsubata is fragrant / The spring seems to have been long / Does the moon also permit the sincerity of us futari, pledged in the evening breeze? / You are smiling / Your appearance, drooping as if withered by the dew of a murasame / Is lovely / The dreams of the kakitsubata that pass through the pillow of my nap / Are they new night after night? / Even the fragrance of the wind blowing beneath the tachibana / Cannot compare to your color and scent / It is clear to see the kakitsubata that scents purely in my garden / Reflected in the water.

Kakitsubata sings:

Bound by the words of your affection, my lord / The satsukisame falls before my eyes / In this pool where the mashimizu is deeply filled / We two play without a care / While the shirahagi blooms purely at the bottom of the water / We are gazing at the shadows of us futari / The ominaeshi, swayed by the wind blowing over the tail of the mountain / Its blossoms are reflected at the bottom of the water / The gentle figure of the ominaeshi, scenting on the slope of the mountain above the water / Reflected in the kahamizu / My body, which is permitted by you to play in this pool every evening / Is happy / Your affection, my lord, is even deeper than the bottom of this pool / Of water / Please love me for a long time, I, the kakitsubata, who crossed over the waves of a thousand layers / Do not cast away the kakitsubata, which I planted from the island of the ryūjin, into the tokoyo.

Adeyaka sings:

With the scent of the hanatachibana, I, a boy / Shall protect you until a thousand generations / The kakitsubata that scents purely on the migiwa / It is a clear sight from upon the boat / When I see the shadow of the blossoms reflected at the bottom of the water / My soul has also been moistened / When I look at the hayase above the water, at the fujigaoka / I see mysterious smoke rising in the vicinity / I know not who lives there, but seeing the smoke at fujigaoka / It seems there are people / Shall I approach and try to ask, oh kakitsubata? / I wish to know your heart.

Kakitsubata, with a look of slight surprise, sings:

Because the rapids of the se above the water are fast, it would be dangerous / Please play in this fuchi-se / Why is it that while watching the smoke of fujigaoka / My soul does not cease to tremble? / Is it perhaps where the ryūjin dwells? Asayufu / Clouds and mist rise in the vicinity of fujigaoka / Although I am a person who fears nothing in this world / The vicinity of fujigaoka feels melancholy.

原文（近代文語）

艶男の別名を橘と言ふ。艶男の橘は妻の燕子花と共に、空澄み渡り風清き夏の初めを、大井ケ淵に新しき舟を浮べて半日の清遊を試みた。水は洋々として夏陽に輝き、流れゆるやかにして、そよ吹く小波を立て涼味津々たり。

艶男は歌ふ。

『橘の花散る里の夕暮は 風も匂ひて清しかりけり 橘の香り床しく水の面に ただよひにけり夏の初めを 橘の匂へるかげをふむ足に かをりのこるか舟の上清しき』

燕子花は歌ふ。

『香に匂ふ花橘をしるべにて 竜の島根ゆ吾は来にけり 竜宮の花橘にくらぶれば 香り床しき君にもあるかな わが恋ふる情の君のよそほひは あから橘匂へる如し 五月闇あかして匂ふ橘の 君はわが身の光なりけり ただならぬ花の匂ひにあこがれて 水面に夕を橘のわれ 橘の花は床しもその実さへ 花さへ一入めでたくあれば 橘の枝にふれにし心地して 君がみ袖にすがりぬるかな 水清き大井ケ淵の川ぞひに 紫匂ふかきつばたあはれ 朝夕の香り清しき橘の 君に生命を任せけるかな 夜な夜なを君が香りにひたされて 花橘を偲びぬるかな

わが袖は床しく香れり舟の上の 川吹く風にさらされながらも 夜な夜なの夢の枕もかむばしや 花橘の君が袖の香 久方の雲間の星も降り来て 橘の上に輝き給へる わが宿の朝夕を匂ひたる 岩燕子花と伊添ひゐるかも 燕子花匂ふ川辺に棹さして 思ふことなし今日の遊びは 花の香はうつらずあれと神かけて 二人が幸を祈りけるかな』

艶男は歌ふ。

『大井川波の綾織る燕子花の 君と楽しく川遊びすも 小波の色も紫匂ふなる 君は床しく竜宮の花かも 川水に影をしたせる橘の 花美しき汝が面かげ 袖にしむ花橘の香りすも 愛しき君と舟に遊べば 燕子花匂へる妹が手枕を 思へば春は久しかりけり 夕風に契る二人の真心を 月もゆるすかほほ笑み給へる 村雨の露にしをれてうなだるる 君のよそほひ愛しかりけり うたた寝の枕に通ふ燕子花の 夢は夜な夜なあたらしきかも 橘の下吹く風のかむばしさも 汝が色香に如かざりにけり わが庭に清しく匂ふ燕子花 水に写して見るはさやけし』

燕子花は歌ふ。

『わが君の情の言葉にほだされて わがまなかひに五月雨の降る 真清水を深く湛へしこの淵に 思ふことなく二人遊ぶも 白萩は水底に清く咲きにつつ 二人が影を眺めゐるかも 尾の上吹く風にゆられて女郎花 花は水底にうつろひゐるも 水上山斜面に匂ふ女郎花の やさしき姿うつれる川水 宵々を君に許されこの淵に 遊ぶわが身は楽しかりけり いとこやの君の情はこの淵の 水底にまして深かりにけり 千重の波ふみて渡りし燕子花の わが身を永く愛でさせ給へ 竜神の島より植ゑし燕子花の 花は常世に捨てさせ給ふな』

艶男は歌ふ。

『男の子吾花橘の香りもて 幾千代までも君を守らむ 汀辺に清しく匂ふ燕子花 見るもさやけき舟の上かな 水底にうつらふ花の影見れば わが魂もうるほひにけり 水上の速瀬を見れば藤ケ丘の あたりにあやしき煙立つ見ゆ 何人の住めるか知らず藤ケ丘の けむらふ見れば人のあるらし 近寄りて尋ねみむかも燕子花 汝が心を知らまほしけれ』

燕子花は稍驚きの色をうかべて歌ふ。

『水上は瀬の速ければ危ふからむ これの淵瀬にあそばせ給へ 藤ケ丘のけむらふ見つつ何故か わが魂は戦きやまぬも 竜神の棲めるにやあらむ朝夕に 藤ケ丘辺に雲霧立つも 世の中に恐るることなき身ながらも 藤ケ丘辺はもの憂かりけり』

§ 2

Adeyaka sings:

'Even so, I shall not go, Kakitsubata.

I do not think of climbing the hill you loathe.

I, too, have felt my heart grow strange.

The clouds and mists that rise at morn and eve,

The Amatsuhi has begun to tilt; come then,

I shall traverse the Hanazono and return to the mansion.'

Thus, the two moored their boat at the Migihabe, and while wandering right and left through the flowering hills, they returned toward the mansion. The two finished their Yufuhan in harmony, and just as they were engrossed in Yomoyama talk, the Jintsū suddenly became intense and Sanke began; they sent Masago and Shirasago to the pre-arranged Ubuya, who firmly closed the brushwood door and returned to the mansion. When they conveyed to the presence of Yamagamihiko and Kahakamihime that the time for the Miko to be born was drawing near, the two Raushin rejoiced greatly and immediately went to the Shinden to offer prayers and Kotoba.

'To the Lord Ōmikami who dwells in the Hisakata no Ame, whose name is awe-inspiring even to mention, we humbly and reverently pray before the Ōmioya who opened this Kunuchi. Alas, alas, our child Adeyaka, and Kakitsubata with whom he tied the Imose bond on a previous day, as the days are fulfilled and the months are full, the time for the Miko to be born draws near; therefore, by the deep, wide, and thick blessings of the Lord Kamitachi, grant that she may give birth to a beautiful Miko safely and peacefully. May the born Miko be the eternal ruler of this land, keeping life forever without loss or incident, and protect the peace of the Kunitsukami. We, as a couple, have already grown old and relied upon our only child Adeyaka as our strength, and how fortunate it is that the womb of Kakitsubata-hime, whom we welcomed from the Ryū no Shimane, has become full, and today has become a joyous day. We look up and pray that Kakitsubata-hime may safely, peacefully, purely, and clearly finish giving birth to a noble child in the Ubuya, and we offer offerings before the great presence, placing various sweet delicacies of mountain, river, and sea upon the eight-legged table as if they were a horizontal mountain; please listen to this peacefully and calmly.'

Having reverently finished reciting the Norito, they came quietly to the living room of Adeyaka, and seeing him, Adeyaka was folding his arms, silent, and his face was pale. Yamagamihiko and his wife, wondering, said:

'It is a good day for the birth of the ruler of our country,

Why do you sit there so despondent?

Like the day the mountain at the head of the water was first opened,

There could be no day more joyous and good than this.'

Adeyaka finally lifted his face, and with a heavy mouth, sang slightly:

'Though it is the height of joy, I am despondent,

Thinking of the suffering of my wife's birth.

Crossing the mountain pass of the life of the Tama no O,

It is painful to think she is giving birth to a Miko.

Thinking it is for my sake, Kakitsubata,

My heart has become sad.

While praying that the born child be healthy,

I have another worry of my own.

Thinking, what if, what if she gives birth to a Ryū no Miko,

It is a painful evening.'

Kahakamihime, trying to comfort the heart of Adeyaka, sings:

'Because the blessings of the gods of heaven and earth are deep,

The wise child of the gods will be born safely.

Do not let your heart be troubled,

The beautiful Miko will be born safely.

Celebrate the joyous day of today,

When the Miko, the treasure of our house, is born.

Since Kakitsubata-hime behaves bravely,

Today's Ubuya will be peaceful.'

Just at that moment, Masago, who had been serving as a guard from afar, entered hurriedly, and with a face full of smiles, said:

'The beautiful voice of the jewel has sounded clearly,

From within the brushwood door.

My lord, rejoice, for your noble Miko,

Has now let out a birth cry.

When I thought to open the door and enter, the Himegami,

Refused me sternly.

The feeling of the opening of heaven and earth drifts about,

I report this with joy and excitement.'

Yamagamihiko sings with joy:

'How grateful, blessed by the grace of the gods,

My grandchild has been born safely.

Do not open the door of the Ubuya for a while yet.

The mother dislikes having her form seen.

Do not approach the Ubuya for seven days and seven nights.

If the mother is still young in blood,

It is feared that she might be startled and her life might end.

By all means, do not open the door of the Ubuya.'

Having given this warning, he bowed again at the Shinden, thanked for the safe delivery of Kakitsubata, and together with Kahakamihime, proceeded to the Shinden as the night had deepened. Adeyaka, in his excessive joy, wondered how his wife was and how his child was; even if it were his father's strict warning, could he stop without seeing the face of his beloved wife or the Miko who was waiting? He crept through the moonlit garden, approached the Ubuya, and peeked through the door. Who could have expected it? His wife Kakitsubata, of gentle appearance, had turned into a full Ryū-tai with skin like a sword, and was sleeping peacefully while holding the child of jewels. Adeyaka, from the moment he saw it, lost his nerve, and screaming 'Ah!', he fled. Hearing the sound, Kakitsubata awoke, and ashamed that her husband had seen her miserable form, she pushed open the door and plunged headlong into the deep bottom of the Ōigafuchi. The name of the child born was named Ryūhiko.

原文（近代文語）

艶男は歌ふ。

『さもあれば吾は行かまじ燕子花 いとへる丘に登ると思はず 吾もまた心あやしく思ひけり 朝夕べに立つ雲霧を 天津陽は傾きにけりいざさらば 花園をぬひて館にかへらむ』

斯くて二人は汀辺に舟を繋ぎおき、花咲く丘を右へ左へたどりながら、館をさして帰りけり。二人は睦じく夕飯をすませ、四方山の話にふける折しも、俄に陣痛激しく産気づきければかねて設けし産屋に、真砂、白砂は、送り行きて柴の戸を堅く閉ぢ、館に帰り来り、山神彦、川神姫の御前に、御子生れませる時の迫りしを伝へければ、二人の老神はいたく喜び給ひて、直ちに神殿に参詣で、祈の言葉を捧げまつりぬ。

『掛巻も綾に畏き久方の天にまします主の大御神、この国内を開き給ひし大御祖の御前に謹み敬ひ願ぎ奉る。あはれあはれわが子艶男と、先の日妹背の契を結びたる燕子花はも、日足らひ月をみたして御子産まむ時の迫りければ、主の神等の深き広き厚き大御恵みに依りて、安く穏かに美子を産ませ給へ。生れし御子は此の国の永久の司として、喪無く事なく永久に命を保ち、国津神の安きを守らせ給へ。吾等夫婦は既に年老いて唯一柱艶男を力と頼みゐたりしに、幸なるかも竜の島根より迎へ来りし燕子花姫の御腹満ちて、今日目出度き日とはなりにけり。仰ぎ願はくば燕子花姫の産屋は安く平らけく清くさやけく貴の子を産み了せ給へと、大前に御幣帛奉り山川海野の種々の甘美物、八足の机代に横山の如く置き足らはして奉るさまを、平らけく安らけく聞し召せと申す』

と敬々しく祝詞を宣り終へ、静々と艶男が居間に来て見れば、艶男は腕を組み、黙然として顔青ざめゐたりけり。山神彦夫婦はいぶかりながら、

『我国の司生れますよき日なるを 汝は何故沈み居るにや 水上山ひらき初めてゆ今日の如 目出度きよき日はあらざらましを』

艶男は漸く面をもたげ、重々しき口にてわづかに歌ふ。

『嬉しさの限りなれどもわが妻の 産みの悩みをおもひて沈める 玉の緒の生命の峠ふみ越えて 御子を産ますと思へば苦しき わが為にならむと思へば燕子花 心かなしくなりにけらしな 生れし子は健かなれと祈りつつ 他に一つのわれなやみもてり 若しや若し竜の御子をば産まむかと 思へば苦しき今宵なりけり』

川神姫は艶男の心を慰めむとして歌ふ。

『天地の神の恵みのふかければ 安く生れむさかしき神の子 村肝の心悩ますことなかれ 美しき御子は安く生れむ わが家の宝の御子の生れ来る 今日の目出度き日がらを祝へよ 燕子花姫は雄々しくふるまへば 今日の産屋はうら安からむ』

かかる折しも、遠く看守の役を務めたる真砂は、あわただしく入り来り、満面笑みを湛へながら、

『美しき玉の御声は柴の戸の 中より清くひびき来れり わが君よ喜び給へ貴御子は 今産声をあげさせ給へり 戸をあけて入らむと思へば姫神は いたくこばませ給ひたりけり 天地の開けし心地ただよひて 勇み喜び知らせ奉るも』

山神彦は喜び歌ふ。

『ありがたし神の恵みの幸はひて わが孫安く生れたりけり 今しばし産屋の御戸を開くまじ 産婦は姿見らるるをいとふ 七日七夜産屋に近づくことなかれ 未だ血の若き産婦なりせば 驚きて生命死せむもはかられず 必ず産屋の戸はひらくまじ』

と戒めおきて再び神殿に額き、燕子花の安産せし事を感謝し、川神姫と共に、夜も更けたればとて寝殿に進み入る。艶男は余りの嬉しさに、妻は如何に、わが子は如何にと、たとへ父の厳しき戒めなればとて、最愛の妻や待ち設けたる御子の顔を見ずして止むべきやと、月照る庭を忍び忍び、産屋に近寄り戸の外よりすかし見れば、豈計らむや、姿優しき妻の燕子花は、全身太刀膚の竜体となりて、玉の子を抱き安々と眠り居るにぞ、艶男は一目見るより胆をつぶし、あつと叫びながら逃げ行く音に、燕子花は目をさまし、わが浅ましき此姿を夫の君に見られたるかと恥らひの余り、戸をおし開けて驀地に大井ケ淵の底深く飛び込みにける。生れたる子の名は竜彦と名づけたり。
