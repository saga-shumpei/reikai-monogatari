Chapter 10: The Episode of 祝賀の宴 (Part II) [1991]

祝賀の宴（二）〔1991〕

§ 1

Here, the Megami who are the Jijiyoshin of Dairiuden stand one after another and sing Shiyukuka.

Shirahagi sings:

Did I think of this Shimane as lonely for Tokoshihe? The Miyo where flowers bloom and scent has come. The Shirahagi scenting on the Nazohe of Ibukiyama, Today is also bathed in the dew of blessing. Tormented by being blown by the Yamakaze, I, Shirahagi, have also begun to scent. Being one who joins the group of Tatsugami, I saw the graceful Misugata of the Kunitsumikami. I realized the noble merit of Kototama, As light is added to this Shimane.

Kichikau sings:

The flowers of Kichikau have also finally begun to bloom, In the noble Ibuki of the Kunitsumikami. While the flowers of Kichikau scent lonely, Until this very day, they were tossed by the wind. From today, the sound of the wind blowing on Ibukiyama, Shall also echo and resound most clearly. Adorning myself with purple flowers, in Shimane, How long I have waited for you, my Lord. Thinking of the Yukusue of the island that is opening, My Ogokoro has begun to well up. While my body sways in the softly blowing wind, Today I feel Ō and my heart flutters. Bowing down before the face of my Adeyaka Lord, I think I shall not begrudge this dew-like Inochi. Having grown for a long time in Shimane, I am happy to overflow with the joy of today.

Yamabuki sings:

The Yamabuki growing on the Shimane of the Tatsugami, The flowers are smiling and the treetops are dancing. The yellow flowers of the Yamabuki also, Have flushed faces from the sake of the joyful banquet. The Misugata of my Sugasuga and Adeyaka Lord, Is it the moon emerging from the mountain of Ibuki? Seeing the noble figure of Otohimegami, Who appeared as the King of Riugū, it is clear. I shall revere and serve her as the Megami, Who has descended from the Hisakata Amatsukuni. My heart has become happy for some reason, As the Lord of Reishi descends from heaven. The Amatsubi shines brilliantly and the hundred flowers, Have become Shimane where they bloom serenely. The Yamabuki scenting and hanging over the surface of the pond, Today the flowers also scent straight and true. While looking at the water mirror, I think that the appearance, Of the Yamabuki flowers is also clear today. Opening the water gate of the mirror lake that I look up at, Alas, the Himegami has appeared. Since the Himegami has appeared from the bottom of the lake, I live in this auspicious day of today.

Nadeshiko sings:

On this island where the Tatsugami have lived for Tokoshihe, The day for the young Nadeshiko to sway has come. Even in the frail body of the Nadeshiko, today, Is moistened by the dew of the blessings of heaven and earth. Through the pure hands of the Sumegami, today, Has become a day of joy for my Nadeshiko self. I, Nadeshiko, pray to heaven, That the joy of today may continue for Tokoshihe. Tormented by being blown about by the Yamakaze, I, Nadeshiko, have been revived. The fish tribes also rejoice and in this garden, Gather together and proclaim their congratulations. The lovely fish tribes and the Nadeshiko too, Today are brave and worship with adoration.

Ominaeshi sings:

On this island where only Ominaeshi dwell, A male, an Adeyaka Lord, has come across. Though I am an Ominaeshi scenting in the field, today, When the Amatsukaze blows well, it is joyful. On the ridge and at the foot of Ibukiyama, Today is a day when the blooming flowers also rejoice. On this day when the Amatsukaze blows well, I proclaim my congratulations with a pure heart. Though I am a Nayogusa, when the water and fire of the Hikogami touch me, I shall become Ō. Seeing the Misugata of my Ō and Adeyaka Lord, I tremble at the foot of Ibuki. Alas, the Himegami who emerged from the bottom, Of the clear mirror lake reflecting the shadows of flowers. The beautiful God who resided in the Suijo mountain palace, The noble God has come. Hearing that you are the Lord who flourished on the mountain of Suijo, I feel a special longing for you. Being blown about by the Yamakaze, I am a Nayogusa, Flower who feels shy. Overflowing with unexpected joy, my heart of love, I am shy to have sung of it.

Tsubaki sings:

Shimane, where even the Momotsukatsura grows thick, Has begun to open from today. From today, the Tsubaki flowers of the Katsuragi, Shall increase in crimson and flourish for Tokoshihe. Even to the treetops of the Tsubaki where crimson flowers bloom, Today shines with the dew of blessing. The God who proclaims congratulations to the Dairiuden, As a fortification for the Miyo of Tokoshihe, is awe-inspiring. The Tatsugami also, meeting the spring of Tokoyo for the first time, Have begun to open the scent of the flowers of their hearts. I pray that even for eight thousand generations, the Tamatsubaki, And the gorgeous marriage of my Lord may last. The Misugata of the God who appeared from the Ashihiki mountain, And from the lake, was Ō. May you flourish without end, O Tamatsubaki, Of the words promising to last for eight thousand generations. The Kunitsukami have crossed over to this island, And have caused the joy of today to open. It is a joy to hear of things that even the Tatsugami, Of the Kamitsuyo had not heard.

思はずも嬉しさ余りて恋心 歌ひしわれは恥かしきかな』

椿は歌ふ。

§ 2

Following the Kami who is Otohime, who is both valiant and gentle, I too shall devote myself.

Shiragiku sings:

Since ancient times, there has been no such precedent as this, for the age has become one where the Shiragiku flowers bloom fragrantly.

More than the Shiragiku flowers, the island-root where the Kunitsukamira, who are even more desirable, emit their fragrance.

Because you are the Lord of flowers blooming in such a radiant manner, it is only natural that the Tatsugami should be moved.

Longing for the Lord of flowers who smiles and flourishes so radiantly, the Momogami have gathered.

How great is the joy of Ōtatsumihiko.

I, Shiragiku, am unable to measure it.

On the morning when I scoop the pure water of the Magatama pond,

O Lord of the Adeyaka whom I have seen and fallen in love with.

Worshipping the face of the Lord of the Adeyaka, bowing low,

I, Shiragiku, am moistened by the dew.

How I have yearned for the figure of the Adeyaka Lord,

Reflected upon the Minasoko.

The face of the Lord reflected in the clear spring water,

Even the beauty of the Shiragiku could not reach it.

Standing upon the mat where I should recite words of celebration,

I feel ashamed that it has turned into a Kohika.

On the Suganiha of the island floating in the Unabara,

I have seen and fallen in love with the pure Lord.

Yamagiku sings:

Bearing the name of Yamagiku, I, more than ever,

Yearn for the Miko of the Minakami mountain.

Were it not for the joy of this day, I, Yamagiku,

Would have no fortune to meet you.

Celebrating the marriage of the Uraraka Hime,

The Amatsuhikage shines radiantly.

In a joy that has no precedent since ancient times,

I, Yamagiku, am filled with happiness.

Bowing to the wind blowing over the One of Ibukiyama,

I, Haka, crumbled into dew.

Though I am a body blown about by the Yamakaze,

From today I shall live valiantly and purely.

Shichiku sings:

Though I, a Nayokusa, am a body of a goddess,

The heart of the flower is valiant.

A blessed Miyo has come, and upon me, a Nayokusa,

You have bestowed the dew of grace.

Growing thick on the roadside of the Tatsugami island,

The sun and moon shine even upon the shade of the flowers.

Bathing in the dew of the grace of the moon and sun,

I bloom faintly as a flower.

From today, with the appearance of the noble Kami,

The island-root of the dragon shall surely flourish.

The sound of insects hiding and chirping at the roots of the grass,

Is heard equally clearly today.

This island floating clearly upon the waves,

Is opened today as a peaceful land of the heart.

Celebrating the appearance of the beautiful Miko,

The banquet of today has become lively.

I, a Nayokusa, am also valiant and brave,

How joyful it is to sing of this good day.

Drawn by the words of the songs of the Kami,

I find myself dancing without knowing why.

Mountains, fields, and the great Unabara are all clear,

What a fine day it is today, so clear and serene.

The fish tribes also celebrate this fine day,

And have risen from the bottom of the lake.

Large fish and small fish gasp upon the surface of the waves,

Celebrating the prosperity of the Miyo today.

Calling and gathering all the numbers of the Tatsugami,

You, my Lord, invite them to the banquet mat.

Without a single one being left behind, they gather at the Great Hall,

To assemble and celebrate the Miyo.

Hikeshi sings:

I, Hikeshi, am a frail goddess,

Let us stand and dance at today's banquet.

My weak heart, which resembles the Hikeshi flower,

Is braced by today's joy.

I shall not let this joy escape elsewhere, for as long as,

The weak power of the Hikeshi remains.

Though waves may rise and storms may blow, from today,

What shall I, Hikeshi, fear?

Since I heard that the Kami Otohime would appear,

I have been waiting impatiently for this fine day.

The Lord of the Adeyaka has come, and the Kami Otohime,

Shines upon the island-root of the dragon.

Ayame sings:

The island, once wrapped in deep mist and indistinguishable, has become a peaceful land,

As the sun and moon shine upon it.

The Ayame grass, blooming with purple on the shore,

Is intimate with the dew of grace today.

Reflecting its shadow in the flowing water,

The Ayame flower has begun to bloom.

The purple of the Ayame, blooming out of season,

Seems to excel in color on this fine day.

Thinking of the endless fortune of the Miyo,

My soul has taken flight.

Kakitsubata sings:

The Kakitsubata blooming by the clear water,

I shall offer on this fine day.

May this island-root of clear water be filled with joy,

And have fortune forever.

Today, when the Hime of Watatsumi appears,

I feel as if heaven and earth are opening.

In the fortune of the grace of the Kami of heaven and earth,

I encounter a joyful banquet today.

Though it is a land where the Kototama shines and assists,

Today I have heard the voice for the first time.

Having firmly built the foundation for ten thousand generations,

Remain forever, Kami Otohime.

The Kototama of the Lord of the Adeyaka thunders,

And has begun to shake even the bottom of the lake.

Embraced by the endless grace of the Kami,

I shall live on this island-root.

Sakuragi sings:

The Sakuragi flowers shall bloom beautifully,

For the Kami who proclaims the Kototama is present.

I shall pluck the Sakuragi flowers that bloom radiantly on the mountain edge,

And offer them to you, my Lord.

Celebrating the Miyo of Ōtatsumihiko,

The mountain cherry blossoms are fragrant in the morning sun.

Even the cherry blossoms that would scatter fruitlessly in the night storm,

Shall preserve a thousand generations by the grace of the Kami.

Adorning myself with the cherry blossoms of the Yamato spirit that bears the name,

I shall celebrate before you, my Lord.

Besides these, there are many hundreds of celebratory songs of the Tatsugami, but as it would be too tedious, I have decided to omit them.
