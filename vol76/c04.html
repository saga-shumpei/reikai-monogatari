Chapter 4: Monster しの巌 Mountain [1921]

怪しの巌山〔1921〕

§ 1

Smitten by the living Kototama of the deity Unarizu, the deity Yasomagatsumi became clouds and mist, driven by the western wind; though once it fled far away into the eastern skies, here it rallied its battle array anew, determined to obstruct the divine work of Kami to the very last, tearing old cotton as it spat forth clouds one after another, piling them limitlessly thousands of fathom high, and at last becoming a vast colossal crag piercing the heavens, building up a mountain of rocky bones stretching over several hundred li, and creating a deep gorge of a thousand fathoms in its front, striving with all its might as if to prevent even a single step forward—how hateful indeed!

Here, as the entourage of the deity Takanohime were advancing with the bits of their steeds aligned, day and night alike, their way was blocked by the unexpected rocky mountain lying across their path, and they were at a loss in thought for a while; here the deity Unarizu, proclaiming that the fierce roaring of the ugly Magatsumi was imposing indeed, raised a thousand-fathom crag from the side high above his head, and uttering a single cry 'Un' as he struck it toward the bedrock of the deep gorge, the crags rubbed against each other, and at the light of the fire leaping forth, the crooked deity was startled, and even the mountain of crags, so firm as it was, began to shudder and retreated backward.

The birth of fire in the Shibi heavenly realm began with the hurling of crags by the deity Unarizu. The deity Magatsumi, startled by the fiercely leaping light of fire, chilled its liver, and no one knew where its hitherto courage had gone; the rocky mountain stretching over several hundred li also gradually grew faint in shadow, and at last became white clouds, miraculously vanishing far away in the midheaven.

The deity Takanohime, seeing this state, composed a sacred poem out of excess of admiration.

The fire born of the merit of the deity Unarizu scattered and drove away the crooked deity; into a mountain of rocky bones the crooked deity transformed and blocked my path ahead. The flame that appeared through the friction of thousand-fathom crags will burn up everything; the light of fire seen for the first time in the heavenly realm illumines the four directions and drives away the crooked. I have realized that fire comes forth from the bedrock, by the divine work of the deity Unarizu.

The deity Unarizu chants a sacred poem.

Though the crooked deity transforms into a mountain of rocky bones and obstructs my way, what shall I fear? The merit of the god of fire born from the grinding of crag against crag is noble, and it worships me. Terrified and frightened by the sparks scattered at the bottom of the valley, even the eternal rocky mountain begins to crumble; though it appears to be a mountain of firm and everlasting crags, it is also a mass of clouds and clouds. Proclaiming the A-O-U-E-I living Kototama, I will scatter this rocky mountain like clouds.

Chanting thus, the deity Unarizu brightly and clearly chants a sacred poem.

By the Kototama of the heavenly true words A-O-U-E-I, the rocky mountain of bones shall vanish without a trace; perish, O Magatsumi, in the heavenly sunlight of the vast sky shining brilliantly, Ka-Ko-Ku-Ke-Ki. Sa-So-Su-Se-Shi, even the crooked deeds of the obstructed ugly Magatsumi shall vanish in the water and fire of the living Kototama; Ta-To-Tsu-Te-Chi, let us see if we cannot crumble the mountain of the crooked formed where dark clouds pile upon one another. Na-No-Nu-Ne-Ni, this rocky mountain which draws out its tail long and wide in the vast field shall vanish before long; Ha-Ho-Fu-He-Hi, by the achievement of the wind blowing from the sky, this rocky mountain too shall scatter as clouds. Ma-Mo-Mu-Me-Mi, deep though the ferocity of the ugly Magatsumi be, I possess the Kototama sword; Ya-Yo-Yu-E-I, though the eighty Magatsumi obstruct to the utmost of their might, how could I, who am a deity, be troubled? Wa-Wo-U-We-I, the crooked rocky mountain born by gathering power by chance I will shatter right now; One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, may the gods of a hundred thousand protect us.

As they chant thus, the mountain of rocky bones stretching over several hundred li serpentiningly gradually turns into smoke and shatters, scattering at the will of the wind—how joyful it is.

The deity Amatsumeo, seeing this state, chants a sacred poem.

Splendid, splendid, by the achievement of the deity Unarizu, the ugly rocky mountain has already crumbled; even the deep gorge of the crooked deity's stratagem has been concealed into dry riverbeds. The crooked deity who, born in the midst of Heaven and Earth, knows not the grace of the Lord God has begun to realize the abiding place of the god of fire, that he dwells in the midst of crags. In order to destroy the ugly fortress of the crooked deity, it is fire of mighty power; that gods dwell even in the rugged earth and in fire I have realized for the first time. The crooked deity, frightened and terrified by the divine light of fire, has hidden its shadow beyond the clouds; thus the mountain of the crooked contrivance that gathered power to the utmost has crumbled. In the heavenly realm born in the water and fire of Kototama, what is noble could it be Kototama? Though holding not a single weapon, guarded by the water and fire sword of Kototama, I shall go forth.

Umesakuhime no Kami chants her divine poem:

『夜も昼も神の恵みに抱かれて 天界に住むわれはたのしも 楽しかるこの天界に生れあひて かこち過せしことを今悔ゆ 言霊の幸はひたすくる天界に われは亡びの道を歩みし 知らず識らず亡びの道を辿りけり 妬ましき心いやかさなりて 御樋代神かたみに妬み嫉みつつ 高地秀の宮居を曇らせしはや 清らけき心の玉をかがやかし かたみに仕へむ神の御前に 主の神の七十五声の言霊に 国津神たち数多生れにき 国津神の上に立てよと主の神の 依さし給ひしわれ等なりける 国津神の心におとる魂線を もちて仕へむことの難きも 真心のあらむ限りを照らしつつ 世のため神のためにつくさむ

いざさらば百神駒に召しませよ 東の宮居は遥かなりせば』

高野比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『鋭敏鳴出の神はわが行く先に立ちて 進ませたまへこの広原を 天津女雄の神は後方を守りつつ 進ませたまへ東の宮居へ』

斯く歌ひ給へば、鋭敏鳴出の神は、高野比女の神其他一同に黙礼しながら、ひらりと駒に跨り、いざや道案内せむと、馬背に鞭うち蹄の音も勇ましく、鈴の音を四辺に響かせながら、春風わたる青野ケ原を進ませ給へば、一行は轡を並べてしづしづと御心も朗かに進み出で給ふ。
