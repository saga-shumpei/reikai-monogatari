Chapter 5: 露の宿 [1922]

露の宿〔1922〕

§ 1

Riding abreast upon the bits of eleven steed through the endless center of Ōnogahara,

As the spring breeze carries the scent of flowers,

Tinkering with the stray locks at the temples,

Reining in the bridle steadily, step by step,

Crossing great rivers and small streams,

Advancing valiantly as they set forth,

That landscape appears precisely

As if painted by a renowned artist

Upon a picture scroll.

Takanohime no Kami halts the hooves of the steed in the center of Ōnogahara, and while gazing at the clouds moving across the sky, calmly composes and chants a sacred poem.

'The shadows of the white clouds crossing to and fro in the eternal sky

Flow down from Takachihoyama.

As the sanctuary of Takachiho's mountain also draws near,

My soul and my steed have galloped forth bravely.

The corners of the road on our homeward journey are without impediment,

Ah, how we have crossed safely by the grace of the Kami!

The shadow of the midday moon grows pale and, as it approaches the edge of the mountain,

It is about to grow into twilight.

Illuminated by the eyebrow of the crescent moon,

The steed of arrow-bamboo heart has grown valiant.

Takachihoyama, too, is dimly visible in the distance,

And it is about to grow into twilight upon this wide plain.

Trusting in a lodging of a single night's dew,

Tomorrow we shall return to the mountain of Takachiho.

White clouds well up over the ridge of Takachiho

And it was an evening flowing toward the west.

Even the blowing wind ceases without a trace, making it quiet,

Shall I sleep in the springy Ōno, bathing in the dew?'

Umesakuhime no Kami composes and chants a sacred poem.

'The neighing of the spring horse echoes high

Upon Ōnogahara where the day is about to close.

When I look at the shadow of the moon leaning against the edge of the mountain,

It is as sharp as a reaping sickle.

The fierceness of the ugly Magatsumi has also faded away and vanished,

And the stars of the August sky have begun to sparkle.

The stars of the great sky, meeting eye to eye,

Are continuing their eternal whispers, are they not?

Gazing up at countless countless star-shadows numbering in the tens of thousands,

My heart is carefree.

Splitting the great sky in twain, forever

Flowing in silver sand, the Milky Way indeed,

The Milky Way lying across the eternal sky too,

Does its destination continue to the ocean?

The sound of insects also echoes ever more clearly and softly,

And my eyes have suddenly grown sleepy.

Blown by the spring breeze, the long journey of the steed

Shall I rest for a while upon a couch of grass,

Wearing the night-garment patterned with the stars of the great sky,

And sleep for a single night upon the couch of the great earth?'

Kaguhime no Kami composes and chants a sacred poem.

'Following the divine words of Takanohime no Kami,

Have we made pilgrimage to Amatsutakamiya?

Piling up the journey of the steed for seven days and seven nights,

Tonight, too, I shall sleep upon a couch of grass.

The steed that travels ten thousand miles also stretches its legs upon the earth,

And rests from the weariness of the journey.

This steed is a gentle steed indeed, aiding the journey of ten million miles

And seeking no reward.

If not for the work of digging while living in the celestial realm,

Our daily life would be peaceful, would it not?

Aiding me for many a thousand ri and leaping forth valiantly,

How lovely is the heart of the steed!'

Suzukokohime no Kami composes and chants a sacred poem.

'Piling up the journey of ten million miles, now already

We have drawn near to the palace dwelling of Takachiho.

Thinking that when tomorrow comes we shall return to the palace dwelling of Takachiho,

I am so joyful I cannot even sleep at night.

Piling up the pillow of a single night's dew,

To await the good day of returning is joyful.

The fierceness of Magatsumi no Umishi was also purified in the water and fire

Of the Kototama, and we have come home.

With the Kototama power of Hayabinade no Kami,

We have come through the corners of the road without impediment.

Becoming a river, and again becoming a mountain, and becoming a cloud,

Did Magatsumi obstruct the road?

However fiercely Magatsumi may rage, or however much it may argue,

It could not reach the living Kototama.

Setting out on the journey to the palace dwelling of Chikushi in the eternal sky,

I have awakened to the various sorrows of the world.

If one dwells in the palace dwelling of Takachiho where the wind is pure

And the view wondrous, one does not know the state of the world.

I have come to realize

Every manner of thing in the ever-shifting world by piling up journeys.

That my soul was muddied like black clouds

I realized upon making pilgrimage to the palace dwelling of Chikushi.'

Asakahime no Kami composes and chants a sacred poem.

'Piling up a journey of ten million ri far away,

It has already become a journey of a single night.

The mountain of Takachiho is both yearning and dear,

For the August Spirit of Kimi abides therein.

The sanctuary of the palace dwelling of Takachiho where I am used to living

Was indeed my eternal life.

Leaving the sanctuary of eternal life behind,

I think that once again I shall set out on a journey.

Nevertheless, after receiving the permission

Of the August Midashiro Kami and others, I think I shall determine it.

In the great sky the stars twinkle, and upon the earth

The dew on the grass blades shines like jewels.

The sound of insects lurking and singing at the roots of spring grasses too

Grew ever sharper as the night deepened.

Though there are tens of thousands of starlight beams, they do not equal the light

Of the bow-shaped moon.

The shadow of the new moon has vanished at the edge of the mountain,

And a mass of darkness has spread over the earth.

Piercing the curtain of darkness, the starlight

Countlessly twinkles in the dead of night.'

原文（近代文語）

果てしも知らぬ大野ケ原の真中を十一頭の駒の轡を並べつつ、

花の香運ぶ春風に 鬢のほつれをいぢらせつ 手綱かい繰りしとしとと 大河小川を乗り越えて 勇み進むで出で給ふ その風景はさながらに 名高き画工の描きたる 絵巻物の如見えにける。

高野比女の神は、大野ケ原の真中に駿馬の蹄を留めて、空行く雲を眺めながら心静かに御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『久方の空に往き交ふ白雲の かげは高地秀山より流るる 高地秀の山の聖所も近づきて わが魂わが駒勇み出でけり 帰り行く道の隈手も恙なく 神の恵みに渡り来しはや 昼月のかげは白けて山の端に 近づきにつつ黄昏れむとすも 三日月の月のまゆみに照らされて 矢竹心の駒は勇みぬ 久方の高地秀山もほの見えて この広原に黄昏れむとすも 一夜の露のやどりをたのみつつ 明日はかへらむ高地秀の山へ 高地秀の尾の上に白雲湧き立ちて 西へ流るる夕なりけり 吹く風もあとなく止みて静かなる 春の大野に露あびつ寝むか』

梅咲比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『春駒のいななき高く響かひて 日は暮れむとす大野ケ原に 山の端に傾く月のかげ見れば 利鎌の如く鋭かりけり 曲神の醜の猛びも消え失せて 御空の星はきらめき初めたり 大空の星は目と目を合はせつつ 永久のささやき続けゐるかも 幾万と数かぎりなき星かげを 仰ぎつわれは心はろけし 大空を二つに割りて永遠に 銀砂流るる天の河はも 久方の空に横たふ天の河も その行先は海に続けるか 虫の音もいやさやさやに響きつつ わが目俄に眠くなりたり 春風に吹かれて長き駒の旅を しばし休めむ草の褥に 大空の星の模様の夜具を着て 大地の褥に一夜を眠らむ』

香具比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『高野比女神の神言に従ひて 天津高宮に詣でけるかも 七日七夜駒の旅路を重ねつつ 今宵も草の褥に眠らむ 万里行く駒も脚をば地にのべて 旅のつかれをやすらひ居るも この駒はやさしき駒よ千万里の 旅をたすけて報酬を求めず 天界に生きてほりする事なくば 日々の生活は安けかるらむ 幾千里われを助けて勇み立つ 駒の心のうるはしきかも』

寿々子比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『千万里の旅を重ねて今ははや 高地秀の宮居に近づきにけり 明日ざれば高地秀の宮居にかへらむと おもへば楽しく夜も眠られず 一夜の露の枕を重ねつつ 帰らむよき日待つは楽しき 曲津見の醜の猛びも言霊の 水火に祓ひて帰り来にけり 鋭敏鳴出の神の言霊力もて 道の隈手もつつがなく来し 河となり又山となり雲となりて 曲津見は道にさやりけるはも 曲津見は如何に猛るも議ゆとも 生言霊に及ばざりけり 久方の筑紫の宮居の旅に立ちて 世のさまざまの憂をさとりぬ 風清く眺め妙なる高地秀の 宮居にし住めば世のさま知れずも うつり行く世のさまざまの事毎を 悟らひにけり旅を重ねて

わが魂は黒雲の如濁らへりと 筑紫の宮居に詣でてさとりぬ』

朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『千万里遠の旅路を重ねつつ はや一夜の旅となりける 高地秀の山は恋しもなつかしも 岐美の御霊のとどまりませば 住みなれし高地秀の宮居の聖所こそ わが永遠の命なりける 永遠の命の聖所を後にして 再び吾は旅立たむと思ふ さりながら御樋代神等の御許しを 受けての後に定めむと思ふ 大空に星はまたたき地の上の 草葉の露は玉とにほひつ 春草の根にひそみ鳴く虫の音も いや冴えにつつ夜は更けにけり 星光は千万あれど弓張の 月の光に及ばざりけり 山の端に新月の影消え失せて 闇のかたまり地に拡ごれり 闇の幕とほして仰ぐ星かげの 数限りなくまたたく夜半なり』

§ 2

Uzukohime no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Continuing a long journey until tonight, I have come to realize the various aspects of the world; In the vast sky there is not a single cloud, As millions of stars begin to shine, Shining in countless numbers across the full heavens, I shall sleep through the night taking strength from the starlight; The youthful land of Ōnogahara, when spring comes, Becomes a carpet of flowers and blooms fragrantly; Laying out this flower carpet ever more splendidly, on a spring night, It is a joy to sleep and listen to the sounds of insects.

Sawakehime no Kami chants a sacred poem.

White clouds linger over the peaks of Takachiho, And the night of the journey through Tohono has grown late; Thinking that tomorrow I shall return to the sacred palace of Takachiho, My heart is stirred and my eyes remain awake; When I think of the time when our path was blocked By Magatsumi, tonight feels truly peaceful; The Kototama of Unarizu no Kami resounded Throughout Ametsuchi, and the evil spirits vanished; Now once again, I have come to realize The precious power of the Kototama through our repeated journeys; Thinking of the blessings of my birth in Mikuni, Where the shining light of Kototama assists us; Looking out, the distant fields are hidden Beneath the dark curtain that had wrapped them so deeply; Setting out on our journey day after day, at last, Spending a single night as a grass pillow; Unconscious of the weariness of the grass-pillow journey, The horses sleep in peace.

Hanakohime no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Even I, wearied by the long journey, feel happy Thinking of it as just a single night's lodging; I am filled with joy thinking that tomorrow I shall report back to the Great Presence at the sacred palace of Takachiho; The sacred palace of Takachiho is ever pure, with purple Clouds rising up over its sacred mountains; Soaring into the distant Amatsumizora, The spring of Mount Takachiho is lovely; The mountains of Takachiho, where a hundred thousand flowers bloom in full, must be The very form of Mikuni.

Sayokohime no Kami chants a sacred poem.

As the night grows late and I cannot sleep, stepping barefoot Upon the dew of the grass feels so pure; Thinking that tomorrow I shall return to the sacred palace of Takachiho, My heart dances and I cannot sleep; Day after day, pondering the joys and sorrows of the journey, A single night has passed; Finding even a single night's lodging in the fields to be impatient, How my heart yearns as we approach the holy place; I find it a wondrous thing that the divine mihishiro gathered together To worship at the palace of Shibi; Blessed by the grace of the Lord God, the night is peaceful, And for how many days have we continued our journey; My heart suddenly leaps with courage and I cannot sleep, Listening to the sound of the horses' hooves; Floating with the gently cloudy spring atmosphere, I shall return to the divine garden where the winds are calm; Tomorrow I shall return and perform misogi In the clear river flowing through the garden of the Great Palace.

Amatsumesu no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Serving as an attendant to the gods of the mihishiro, We have drawn close to the sacred palace of Takachiho; Come now, let us sleep until the break of dawn Upon the grassy fields where the spring qi drifts.

Thus the Kami chanted their poems of reflection and awaited the break of dawn, and once again, with the brave sound of their horses' hooves, around noon of the following day, they at last safely returned to the sacred sanctuary of the palace of Takachiho.
