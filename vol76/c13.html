Chapter 13: Morning 駒の別れ [1930]

朝駒の別れ〔1930〕

§ 1

The five pillars of Kamutsukasatachi serving at the sacred sanctuary of Mount Sakakiyama, wishing to console Asakahime-no-Kami for the fatigue of her long journey and concurrently holding a farewell banquet, gathered in Yahirodono, arranged various delicious food offerings upon Tsukueshiro, decorated the hall with the flowers of Momogimomogusa, and while singing and dancing, feasted merrily.

When Hatatsukurio-no-Kami chanted a sacred song, the four pillars of Kami, matching the rhythm of the song, clapped their hands and stamped their feet to dance.

As the morning sun shines straight upon Mount Sakaki, While the evening sun glows faintly over Mount Momiji; The mid-heaven is deep azure, the great earth is vast, Yet amidst it all, on a solitary journey for the sake of my Kimi. Though the distant road is a journey for Kimi's sake, Even if my life should perish, I shall fear nothing; Kimi is the pillow of a myriad-ri journey of sleep, Seen in the dreams of a night spent sleeping alone. Yearning afar for the tracks of Kimi's august rear, I, Asakahime, have wandered forth to come hither. The moon is in the mid-heaven, the starlight shines clear, Welcoming the Hime is Mount Sakakiyama; Since even a thousand or ten thousand ri is for love's sake, How could I ever weary of Kimi's side?

As the Kami all beat their hands and synchronized their foot-clapping, singing and dancing along, Asakahime-no-Kami, with her face slightly flushed, chanted an august song.

How embarrassing is this day's banquet among a hundred Kami, While being teased, my face grows hot indeed. Even if I am teased and laughed at, what of it? If there be a day when I meet my beloved Kimi, I am one who vowed, even while longing thus for Kimi to the end, To offer up this jeweled-string-like life. Though the pines upon the peak of Mount Sakakiyama should wither, My heart that yearns for Kimi shall never perish. Even if heaven and earth should turn to darkness, I would not shrink, For since Kimi is the Kami of dazzling light, Though I heed not the raging of Maguwasuhitokamitachi, yet seeking him forth, It is simply because my love overflows. If it be thus, I shall bare every single thought of mine, And chill the livers of the Kami all. Though I be laughed at, my heart shall not change; though I be reviled, I shall not abandon the path of love as long as life shall last. Though my life should die, oh, by Kimi's side, Our souls shall commune and mingle water and fire. Howsoever the hundred Kami may deliberate, I shall not falter upon the steep slope of love. Now then, since my yearning for Kimi has grown so dear, I shall depart from this place even a moment sooner.

At Asakahime-no-Kami's resolute sacred song, the hundred Kami, biting their tongues in awe, stopped the movement of their dancing hands and each chanted an august song.

The sacred song of Hatatsukurio-no-Kami.

More marvelous even than the blossoms of white plum, Hime's words, how magnanimous they are! I knew not that she was a Hime of such intrepid countenance, I have no means to face and serve her respectfully. Oh magnanimous Hime, magnanimous indeed, Who has chanted such an august song of love! I think upon the good fortune of Hinodewake-no-Kami, To be yearned after by such a brilliant and wise Hime. How honest is the heart of Hime, Who declared she would spurn neither road of many ri nor distance! If I too receive the august permission of Kami, I shall take such a valiant Hime to be my bride.

Asakahime-no-Kami chanted her august song.

If you think to match yourself in a glance with a wise Hime, Gather in your divine soul and tighten it for a while. How whimsical a public personage you are to declare That holding a loosened divine soul, you will meet a wise Hime. Unless you are a Kami excelling in eyebrows, eyes, and countenance, Even a crow would likely refuse to become your wife. A hundred Kami, I beg you, forgive my leaves of words; If this be true speech without falsehood, Among the Kami dwelling here, not one by one Has an august visage matching that of a Goddess. Serve forever in the shrine of the palace of Sakaki, And strive diligently in the sacred tasks. This banquet opened for my sake, Has it not grown dull, as the Kototama grows wild? Anyway, my dear Kimi of my back yearns for me so, Since he excels in both eyebrows, eyes, and countenance, Though there are Kami in great numbers, as the Kimi of my back, There is not even a shadow of a soul to look up to.

The sacred song of Sakibohoruo-no-Kami.

Even the white plum blossoms, once they have scattered, are a floating world Where even little birds no longer linger upon the twigs. I, who possess such an unsightly visage as this, Shall keep reverence and awe and speak not of love. Yet in this floating world, while there are no superior male Kami, There is no Goddess who excels her; How marvelous are the divine words of Asakahime-no-Kami, Resembling the cherry blossoms at their height. Yet flowers never stay forever upon the twigs, How pitiful is the midnight when they scatter in the storm! Even the moon crossing the heavens has its waxing and waning, And Asakahime too is now at her height. When the time comes, Asakahime will presently wither; Go forth swiftly to the side of the Kimi of your back. It is a world where cherry blossoms scatter even in a brief moment, Hurry away to the side of the Kimi of your back.

朝香比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

§ 2

Asakahime no Kami replied to this,

As for my murakimo heart, though it hurries and chafes, There is no way to cross the banri yamakaha; Yet even in the dream-vision of the radiant mitama, My tamashihi is awakening.

Nakasakio no Kami, though astonished, chants a miuta.

Upon the mat where amata ogami are gathered, does the himegami Proclaim her amorous thoughts without a shred of hesitation? I never imagined until this very moment That a kuhashime could be so bold and wise. What a fascinating himegami she is! When the kimi of her back Utters only tsuyagoto out of season, Born in kamikuni, I had never before encountered Such a bold megami. Being a kuhashime yet a sakashime, Asakahime no Kami is indeed a deity who surpasses all in omokatsu and imuka! Stepping forth before such a bold megami, All the ogami were struck isuku.

Sayofuke no Kami chants a miuta.

Because the himegami came to this sugado, The mountains of sakaki are overflowing with blossoms. Smitten by the kototama proclaimed so purely and boldly, I have not even a word left to return. Before this bravery of the himegami, Magatsumi Must have fled away making clouds into mist! How bold of the himegami to bare her sincerity without hesitation And feel no shame whatsoever.

Chikakiyoko no Kami chants a miuta.

The himegami, who is the mihishiro of Akitsuno no Kami, I suppose she would naturally be just as she is. Akitsuno no Kami, too, must be astonished At the sincerity of the himegami who came from afar. In this world, may we advance with courage and Pave the path we ourselves must tread! As the bravery of Asakahime no Kami is valiant, The cloud-and-mist of the demons will hide their shadows. The one-eyed, eight-mouthed Magatsu too, it is said, Fled and scattered before the living kototama of the himegami. Welcoming the pure divine words of the himegami, Spring revives upon the mountains of sakaki. Being together with the himegami, I feel as though Eternal spring flowers are blooming and fragrant.

The kami, with unaffected simplicity, opened their hearts to one another and thus concluded their banqueting without ceremony.

Here, Asakahime no Kami remained for that single day upon this sacred mountain, nourishing her soul with the voices of a hundred birds and the fragrance of a hundred trees' flowers; then, on the following morning, once again whipping her horse, as she was about to set forth from the wild plains where mist was rising, she chanted a miuta.

The mountains of sakaki, where I let my tamashihi wander For three days and three nights, are mountains of yearning! Captivated by the warm hearts of a hundred kami, The days slipped by before I knew it. Having played so happily and joyfully on Mount Sakaki, May these memories endure for a thousand ages! As long as the great heavens exist, it has become A sky of blue clouds on this auspicious day when I depart. Farewell, a hundred kami; setting out from here upon a grass-pillow Journey, may you dwell in peace! Serving morning and evening in the great miya, Open up the world with living kototama. I feel a lingering reluctance this morning to part From the kami who have become so dear and familiar. Not a single cloud hangs upon my heart; We conversed without any reserve whatsoever.

Kizukurio no Kami chants a miuta.

Thinking that the dear Asaka no Hime is departing On her journey today, I feel lonely indeed. My thought that we might dwell together and converse For many years has also ended up a dream. I had no way to stop the himegami, Who serves in the divine work of creating the land. O himegami, if you meet Akitsuno no Kami, Convey us to him in complete and intimate detail, you. Praying for the auspicious progress of Akitsuno no Kami, Tell him that we serve the kami, O kimi. When it rains, I will think of the himegami; when the wind blows, I will yearn for you, I, Kizukurio. Mount Sakaki, where the himegami stood, Will once again grow lonely day by day; Yet because of you, kimi, whose flowing water cannot be stopped, I resign myself and watch you depart.

Chibanachio no Kami chants a miuta.

Even the hundred flowers blooming in the spring fields are destined to fall; Go with care upon your journey through the great plains! How boldly you set forth, Asakahime! As the divine words of the kami illuminate the world, Your steed neighs loudly as you advance. I pray that your journey, kimi, may be peaceful.

Nakasakio no Kami chants a miuta.
