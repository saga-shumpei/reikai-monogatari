Chapter 8: Goodness 言 Beauty Spirit [1925]

善言美霊〔1925〕

§ 1

Here, Asakahime-no-Kami, having her heart's steed of yearning for Akitsuo-no-Kami run wild and refusing to be reined in, blew away even the earnest remonstrances of Mihishirogami-no-Kami, Miyatsukasagami-no-Kami, and others, poured forth with all their hearts and efforts, as a wind blowing in emptiness, whipped her white horse, and galloped forth toward the southeast into the dusk sky, how valiant indeed she was. The Mihishirogami and others remaining behind, sighing with deep grief, returned peacefully to the sacred hall of the palace in Takachiho, and wishing for the safe journey of Asakahime-no-Kami, offered various delicious foods, and respectfully recited prayer words before the great august presence.

First, as the preparations for the festival were completed as usual, Unarizu-no-Kami, in his capacity as the official of the palace, prostrated himself directly before the great august seat, and in a clear voice reverently spoke the Futonorito.

Even to speak of it with awe is wondrous, erecting the thick great palace pillars on the lower bedrock of Mount Takachiho, making the chigi high-rise in Takamanohara, before the great august presence of the Lord God who eternally and immovably possesses and reigns over this sacred place, I, the ritual master Unarizu-no-Kami, with reverence, respect, awe, and trembling, humbly speak. Whether it be a divine plan or a divine arrangement, Asakahime-no-Kami, who served morning and evening as one of the pillar deities under the divine assignment of Mihishirogami-no-hime, not even heeding the Kototama of remonstrance and stopping given by the myriad deities, whipped her swift horse and set forth with valiant heart, wishing to visit and serve at the august abode of Tainomoto Akitsuo-no-Kami. Since things have come to this, there is no way for us to stop her with our sincere hearts, and having no other recourse than to entrust it to the Kamagara Kami and pray that the Hime-no-Kami's journey may cross safely, peacefully, and smoothly, the deities consulted together and offered today's festival, piling up various delicious foods of sea, river, mountain, and field like horizontal mountains upon the eight-legged offering desks, and listening to the manner of the offering peacefully, calmly, and gently, may Asakahime-no-Kami proceed safely and without blemish to the sacred place even at the winding bends of her path. Should there be any faults committed, may the Kamunaobi and Ōnaobi gods see them aright, hear them aright, and rectify them aright, and protect and bless her day and night so that there may be no blemishes in the departure of the Hime-no-Kami's words, folding their knees like deer and prostrating, and piercing the roots of the neck like wild boars, they humbly and reverently offer their prayers and speak. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand, may the divine spirits of Kamagara multiply and bless us, may the divine spirits of Kamagara multiply and bless us.

Takohime-no-Kami stood in the courtyard of the festival and chanted an august poem.

To the great deity who eternally dwells in the august palace of Takachiho, I will offer a prayer. Asakahime-no-Kami departed at evening, let there be no evil on the bends of the path. Asakahime is a victorious deity, a deity facing forward, she departed without listening even to my Kototama. The steed of the heart of Asakahime, who set out on her resolve, could not be stopped. Since it is thus, there is no recourse, I think I will lean upon the warm grace of the Lord God. I fear for the body of the Hime advancing through the wild plains where Magatsugahi fiercely rages and runs mad. Wishing to pile up perilous travel pillows, Asakahime has valiantly set forth. Up to this point, she carries through such a disposition, Asakahime is a victorious deity. I, Mihishirogami, being entrusted as the official, not even a leaf of words to speak an apology comes forth. Making my heart negligent, I did not understand the heart of Asakahime until now.

My fault of not understanding, Kamunaobi and Ōnaobigami, please rectify it.

Unarizu-no-Kami chanted an august poem.

I did not notice the valiant disposition of Asakahime-no-Kami and fell into slumber. The steed of the heart of Asakahime, ordained as such from of old, could not be stopped. Are the divine words of Asakahime-no-Kami truly those of a deity facing forward, a victorious deity? She sets forth alone into the endless wilderness, protect this valiant Hime. Magatsugahi changes its form in various ways, seeking to obstruct the Hime's path. However strong the ferocity of Magatsumi may be, let her proceed without loss or harm. Though there are eight hundred myriads of deities, no one possesses the valiant heart of Asakahime.

§ 2

Umesakuhime no Kami recited a sacred poem.

The wild plains of the southeast have high mountains, yet even in early summer the atmosphere of spring drifts about. The white plum blossoms scatter their fragrance here and there, surely comforting the journey of Himegami. Treading through the mountain paths where white plum blossoms are fragrant, she urges her white-maned steed with a whip. By the thick grace of the Lord God, Asakahime no Kami will advance peacefully. In the heavenly realm where the blessings of Kototama are bestowed, obstructing Magatsuhi shall certainly perish.

Yet, while the hardships of the travel upon the grass pillow press hard upon me, Asaka no Hime, I wish to pray morning and evening before the divine presence that there be no misfortune for Asaka no Hime.

She sets forth bravely into the spring fields where white plum blossoms scent the four quarters, with a rich and generous heart.

Kaguhime no Kami recited a sacred poem.

How bustling is Shibitenkai, fragrant with the untimely tree fruits of Kagu! In the evening dusk of the courtyard where cherry blossoms lie scattered, Asaka no Hime stands all alone. How magnificent is Himegami, who departed without listening even to the Kototama imbued with the sincerity of the Kamigami! Seeing off the receding figure of Himegami, tears of an unknown origin merely burst forth from me. When I think that today might be the limit and end in a long parting, I grow sad and tearful.

Striking her steed with a whip across the great plain, she travels alone; the heart of the valiant Hime is heart-wrenching. Thinking that this journey is born of the excess of longing for her beloved husband, I too grow sad. To extinguish the flames of the fire in her breast, yearning for divine thought and longing for her husband, did she not set forth? Neither burning fire nor overflowing waters does she disdain; for the sake of her path of love, she holds her life not dear. Because of her husband, to whom she offered her life as a bead string, Asakahime no Kami must indeed be thus.

Well, well, though the obstructions of Magatsumi be thick, pierce through them with public sincerity.

Suzukohime no Kami recited a sacred poem.

Praying with a purified heart that there be no misfortune even in the winding corners of Asakahime's path, she must have offered her prayers. The figure of Hime, who set forth urging a steed of renown with blows of the whip, was truly valiant. Desiring to pierce through with the piercing arrow of her heart that longs for her husband, she mounted her steed and galloped forth. The image of the public figure advancing across the great plain where spring has departed and summer has come can be well imagined.

Advance without the calamity of insects or the hindrance of Takatsugami. Though once I sought to stay her, what shall I do? Since it has come to this, I will simply pray.

May the path upon which Himegami advances be peaceful, free from the misfortunes of birds of the high heavens. Crossing mountains, traversing fields, and crossing mountain torrents, how valiant is the figure of the public person going forth! If it is thus, we who remain behind can only pray for the journey of Himegami.

Utsukohime no Kami recited a sacred poem.

Leaving behind the sacred precinct of the palace of Takachihu, Himegami has set forth, crossing mountains and rivers. Repeating the pillows of a journey of several thousand li, how magnificent is Himegami as she goes forth alone! Without listening to the deterrent divine words of the hundred deities, valiantly has Hime set forth.

Samewakehime no Kami recited a sacred poem.

After a journey of many tens of days in the palace of Chikushi, before long Himegami has set forth on a journey once again. There is no means to stop the steed of the heart of Himegami, whose spiritual soul and divine soul are mighty. Setting forth across the wilderness for several thousand li, Asaka no Hime is a valiant deity. In the hardship of passing the months and days in vain, Asaka no Hime must have set forth.

Without even the permission of the Lord God, Asakahime no Kami stands quite alone. The undertaking of the divine words of Asakahime no Kami will surely return and prove pleasing to the Kami. Since it is a deed not pleasing to the will of the Lord God, the steed of Asaka no Hime will not run. I doubt the divine will in the valiant figure that galloped forth into the darkness of twilight.

If the heart of Mura-gimi is illuminated and the water and fire of Kototama are purified, all things will surely be accomplished, yet Asakahime no Kami will certainly unite her water and fire with Akitsumiko no Kami.

Realizing that the land of the west is immature, she must have gone to make them understand an age where the Kami of the Mihashiro does not exist. Is this a journey to become the Mihashiro Kami of the land of the west and give birth to the Kunitama no Kami?

It is heard that dark clouds gather densely over the land of the west, where Great Magatsumi dwells. She must have set forth simply to correct the arbitrary deeds of Magatsumi. Since Asakahime no Kami is a deity of surpassing countenance, even Great Magatsumi will simply bow in submission. There has never been a deity so valiant, though Mihashiro Kami are many.

§ 3

Hanakohime no Kami chanted a sacred poem.

The cherry blossoms on the peaks of Takachiho have already fallen, And the garden has truly become one of green leaves; In the fields and on the mountains, young leaves and tender grasses sprout forth, And the summer sky has come to welcome them. As she crosses the mountains and rivers where green leaves bud, riding upon the back of a horse, Ah, Asakahime is setting forth! Asakahime surely, Must be suffering from the fierceness of Magatsumi. Thinking upon the travel-woes of Asakahime, Alas, my tears stream down along my cheeks! In the lands of the West, black clouds hang heavily, And the Maga of Sūyatogoru spews forth fire. At an unseasonable hour, black clouds rise up in flocks, Enveloping the sky and making the western lands gloomy. Neither moon, nor sun, nor stars cast their light in the western land; Surely she has set forth to fashion and build that realm. The courageous disposition of Asakahime, Morning and evening have I ever realized! Thinking thus with absolute resolution, upon a grass-pillow journey She has set forth, and now indeed I understand it. Now that it has come to this, with no way to stop her, It is all I can do to pray that she remains free from harm. If Asakahime grandly builds up her achievements, How great an honor it will be for Mihishirogami! Among the eight pillars of Mihishirohimegami, How joyous it is that a courageous Kami goes forth!

Sayokohime no Kami chanted a sacred poem.

The blossoms of the red peonies have fallen, and in the garden pond, The purple of the irises has begun to emit its fragrance. Reflected in the murmuring stream of the great garden, How lustrous and bright are the iris flowers! Leaving behind the sacred place just when the irises bloom, Asakahime set forth upon her journey. Due to the anguish of her burning heart, Asakahime surely could not even perceive the irises with her eyes. Blooming upon the face of the garden, the irises and the rabbits-foot irises, Being unable to distinguish which is which, Without knowing the rights and wrongs of Asakahime's departure today, I shall remain silent. Whatever may happen, to entrust all to the will of the Lord God Must be the true duty. No matter what grand achievements one may strive to accomplish, Anything without the permission of Kami is in vain. Without relying upon the living Kototama of the Lord God, My courageous heart to advance will not arise. To think that I merely passed away long days and months in vain Was indeed a perversion of my own heart! Serving Kami morning and evening and proclaiming the Norito Was the very duty of Mihishirogami. Ah, the Kami who brought forth Mihishirogami To pacify this heavenly realm where the earth is still young! Setting aside for a moment the divine work of giving birth to children, She must have been born forth as the Mihishiro of Kototama. Since it is thus, morning and evening for the sake of the world, Would that Mihishirogami proclaim the Kototama! If even for a single day I were to neglect the living Kototama, I realized that it would become a world of turmoil.

Amatsumeō no Kami chanted a sacred poem.

Following after the Himegami of Mihishiro, How wondrous are the things I see and hear! Leaving behind the palace of Tsukushi with all my true heart, have I not served in the palace of Takachiho? As I watched the departure of Asakahime, How I gazed in fascination at her courageous figure! That she is such a courageous Kami, I, in my foolishness, had not realized. From now on, morning and evening in the great palace, I shall pray and protect the safety of Hime. To give birth to the National Guardian Kami of the western lands, She must have courageously gone forth all alone. Now that it has come to this, it is useless to regret; with a true heart Shall I pray before the presence of Kami.

Thus, the Kami prayed for the safety of Himegami before the great palace, each chanting their retrospective poems, and quietly proceeded to retire to their designated chambers.

小夜子比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『丹牡丹の花はくづれて庭池の 菖蒲の紫匂ひ初めたり 大庭の瀬見の小川にかげうつす 菖蒲の花のつやつやしかも 菖蒲咲くころの聖所を後にして 朝香の比女は旅立たしける 朝香比女は燃ゆる心の苦しさに 菖蒲も目にはうつらざりけむ 庭の面に咲ける菖蒲や燕子花 何れをそれと別ち兼ねつつ 朝香比女の今日の旅立ちよしあしの あやめもわかずわれは黙さむ 何事も主の大神の御心に 任すは真のつとめなるらむ 如何ならむ太しき功たつるとも 御神の御許しなきは仇なり 主の神の生言霊に依らずして われは進まむ雄心起らず 徒に長き月日を送りしと 思ふは心のひがみなりしよ

朝夕に神に仕へて祝詞宣るは 御樋代神のつとめなりける 地稚きこの天界を固めむと 御樋代神を生ましし神はや 御子生みの神業はさておき言霊の 御樋代として生れ出でしならむ かくならば朝な夕なに世の為に 御樋代神は言霊宣らばや 一日だも生言霊をおこたらば 乱るる世なりと悟らひにけり』

天津女雄の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『御樋代の比女神等に従ひて 珍しき事を見聞きするかも 真心を筑紫の宮居あとにして 高地秀の宮居に仕へつるかも 朝香比女神の旅立ち送りつつ 雄々しき姿に見とれけるかな かくの如雄々しき神にいますとは 愚かしきわれは悟らざりしよ この上は朝な夕なを大宮居に 祈りて比女の幸を守らむ 西方の国魂神を生ますべく 雄々しく一人出でましにけむ 今となりて悔むも詮なし真心を 持ちて祈らむ神の御前に』

かくの如く、神々は大宮居の前に比女神の無事を祈りつつ各自述懐歌をうたひて、静かに定めの居間に就かせ給ひける。
