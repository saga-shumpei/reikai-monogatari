When the Hour of the Rooster (Tori) arrives, one wraps up (torimatome) the day's labors, tidies up all about, partakes of the evening meal, gives thanksgiving to God once more, and when the Hour of the Dog (Inu) arrives, all gather together and retire to sleep (inuru)!'

§ 20

Thus she sang in a tranquil melody. Involuntarily sighing with awe, Katoriwake stepped heavily back unto his room; lying down beside the four sleeping companions upon the bedding, he waited eagerly for the arrival of the dawn.
