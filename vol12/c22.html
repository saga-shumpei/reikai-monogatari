Chapter 22: The Assault on Ichishima [518]

一嶋攻撃〔518〕

§ 1

Clouds block the vast heavens and mists envelop the ocean; across the realms of the four quarters, not a drop of divine grace remains, and mountains, rivers, plants, and trees weep in barren desolation, plunging the world into a pitch-black night where black cannot be distinguished from white! Let the Inland Sea now be illuminated! In order to save the hundred deities and hundred mortals for endless generations of the Divine Age, Lady Miyukihime reverently treasures the divine decree of Kamu-Susanō-no-Kami with deepening devotion! The sincere heart that cannot be dissolved dwells upon this singular island—Lady Tagirihime weeping hot tears! By the divine authority of the ten-span sword revealed upon Mount Caucasus, cleaving through clouds and mists on all sides to dispel the foul crooked spirits, upon this sacred island where Takasugiwake was sequestered,

Firmly erecting the pillars of the palace to mourn and pray for the world, stands the sacred maiden Miyukihime, the Mizu-no-Mitama! The blowing wind smells of blood, the din of men and horses is incessant, shouting battle-cries and war-shouts resound, and the realm is in chaotic uproar like flocks of birds startled into flight, so that even the cries of seagulls far out at sea sound dreadfully forlorn! This is renowned Sardinia; digging up steel and iron from the Alps under divine protection and forging hundreds of weapons, valiant soldiers serving as precious souls have assembled thick as clouds!

Serving as a divine maiden in the sacred shrine of Mount Caucasus, Lady Miyukihime—one of the three sisters of Moon, Snow, and Flower—was endowed with a martial and heroic disposition; deeply inspired by the virtue of the ten-span sword, she excavated iron and steel from the Alps and forged diverse weapons, gathering heroes and champions from across the realm unto this island to prepare for the subjugation of demons in service of the divine governance of the nation.

High upon the roof of the palace rose the chigi crossbeams and katsuogi logs; the tips of the chigi were fashioned as razor-sharp double-edged blades, both ends of the katsuogi were sharpened like swords, and the compound was encircled by a palisade of blades, forming an impregnable fortress that permitted no entrance to crooked spirits.

Martial deities vied to assemble upon this singular island, relentlessly practicing martial arts day and night to sweep away evil deities and bring peace unto all gods and men. The sounds of clashing blades, archery, and horsemanship echoed across the Inland Sea, reverberating as clear as day even unto Mount Tenkyō, where the central pillar deity Amaterasu-Ōmikami abode.

Amaterasu-Ōmikami was inwardly plagued day and night by grave displeasure, suspecting that ignoring the divine Way of Heaven and Earth—which rectifies, relistens, and reproclaims all crookedness through words of good and beautiful speech—to forge lethal weapons and drill in warlike arts might be an evil ambition harbored by Her younger brother-deity Susanō no Mikoto to conquer and occupy Takamanohara.

太敷立てて世を偲ぶ 瑞霊の深雪姫 吹き来る風も腥く 人馬の音は絶間なく 矢叫びの声鬨の声 世は騒がしく群鳥の 群れ立つばかり沖つ鳥 沖の鴎の声さへも いと佗しげに聞ゆなり ここは名に負ふサルジニヤ 神の守りもアルプスの 鋼、鉄取り出でて 百の兵器造りつつ 珍の御魂と仕へたる 心も猛き兵士は 雲の如くに集まれり。

コーカス山の珍の宮に、御巫子として仕へたる、月雪花の姉妹の一人、深雪姫は、尚武勇健の気質に富み、十握の剣の威徳に感じて、アルプス山の鋼鉄を掘出し、種々の武器を造り備へて、国家鎮護の神業に奉仕せむと、天下の英雄豪傑を此島に集め、悪魔征討の準備に備へつつあつた。

宮殿の屋根は千木、勝男木を高く、千木の先は鋭利なる両刃の剣を以て造り、勝男木もまた両端を剣の如く尖らせ、館の周囲には剣の垣を繞らし、曲津の侵入を許さず用心堅固の金城鉄壁なりける。

武勇の神は先を争うてこの一つ島に集まり来り、天下の邪神を掃蕩し、遍く神人を安堵せしめむと昼夜間断なく武術の稽古に余念なく、剣戟射御に勤む声は瀬戸の海を越えて、遠く天教山に鎮まります撞の御柱の神、天照大神の御許にも、手に取るが如く轟き渡りぬ。

天照大御神は、善言美詞をもつて世の曲業を、見直し聞き直し詔り直すべき天地惟神の大道を無視して、殺伐なる武器を造り武芸を励むは弟神素盞嗚命の高天原を占領せむとする、汚き心のあるならむと、内心日夜不快の念に駆られ給ひつつあらせられた。

宮殿の屋根には千木と勝男木を高く掲げ、千木の先は鋭利な両刃の剣として造り、勝男木もまた両端を剣のように尖らせ、館の周囲には剣の垣を巡らせて曲津神の侵入を許さぬ厳重強固な金城鉄壁としていた。

天照大御神は、善言美詞をもって世の邪曲を見直し、聞き直し、宣り直すべき天地惟神の大道を無視して、殺伐たる武器を造り武芸に励むのは、弟神である素盞嗚命が高天原を侵略占領しようとする邪悪な野心によるものであろうかと、内心日夜不快の念に駆られておられた。

§ 2

Four or five warriors, having finished their martial drills, climbed to the scenic summit of the singular island, gathering tree fruits and sipping sake from gourds while indulging in miscellaneous conversation.

Kō: 'We are forced to drill in swordsmanship and spearmanship day and night until our bones and bodies are completely exhausted! Once swords and spears are done, we are compelled to practice archery and horsemanship! On this isolated island with no enemy in sight, for what possible reason are we drilled so severely?!'

Otsu: 'To hone martial virtue during times of peace is the very essence of the warrior's art! When war suddenly erupts, panicking in haste is utterly useless! Warriors are those who protect the nation! Because constant martial discipline is essential, we are made to train daily!'

Hei: 'Day after day the sky is overcast and impenetrable mists shroud the earth so that one cannot see even six feet ahead; the world has become so dangerous that mortals can no longer live in safety! That each may protect himself, the merciful God encourages martial arts!'

Kō: 'In the doctrine of the Anai teachings, does it not say: "God harmonizes and reconciles through Kotodama; it is not a body-master spirit-subservient teaching of using weapons for conquest, invasion, or annexation! We unify the world morally through righteousness!" Why go through all the trouble of arming to the teeth and stirring up waves upon flat ground?! It is just like the Ural sect!'

Otsu: 'Indeed! It is a practice that completely contradicts the teachings of the Anai doctrine in name and reality! One cannot say it in a loud voice, but surely an eight-headed serpent or demon has possessed Lady Miyukihime to make her do this!'

Hei: 'If that is truly the case, we are suffering for nothing! To be subjected to bone-crushing training only to accumulate great sins violating the celestial laws—that would be utterly intolerable!'

Tei: 'That God trains in martial arts and amasses weapons is solely to rescue all mortals when extraordinary emergencies arise! Why would the merciful God ever take pleasure in lethal slaughter?! Devils fear the authority of the sword, reform their hearts through martial virtue, and submit to the righteous path! However sublime the Kotodama of good and beautiful words may be, they cannot enter the ears of completely clouded evil deities; therefore, God manifests His great mercy by showing them His formidable might to compel their reformation—such is His divine plan! Susanō no Mikoto may appear at first glance to be a fearsome, fierce deity fond of battle, but He by no means delights in bloodshed! Despairing of this corrupt world, weeping tears of compassion day and night wishing to return unto the serene realm of the Moon Deity—whereupon His Father Deity descended from heaven, reprimanding Him fiercely: "With such fainthearted weakness, how could you ever govern this world?! Having chosen you of peerless valor and commanded you to consolidate the ocean land where devils run rampant, such effeminate conduct is outrageous!" Greatly angered, He sent Susanō to ascend unto Takamanohara to bid farewell to His elder sister Amaterasu-Ōmikami! Inheriting that divine spirit, Lady Miyukihime, being a martial and heroic goddess, trains in arms as a precaution for emergencies! Martial arts are never for violent subjugation, but for benevolent embrace! It is unquestionably a divine stratagem born of pure sincerity to dispel demons and rescue all mortals!'

Kō: 'How intimately knowledgeable you are! Where on earth did you come from? You arrived at this training hall only recently, yet your martial prowess is truly magnificent!'

Tei: 'Who, me? I was originally a farmer—Torakō of Mitoshi Village!'

Kō: 'Aha! You are that famous self-styled Ushitora-no-Konjin! No wonder you are such a massive fellow!'

Torakō: 'Look! I cannot tell for certain, but it seems a commotion has broken out at the mansion! Come, everyone, we have no time to dawdle! Let us hurry to the mansion!'

With Torakō in the lead, the entire group rushed down the hill, sprinting headlong toward the mansion.

甲『お前はよく詳しい事を知つて居るなア、一体何処から来たのだ。此道場へ来てから未だ間もないに、武術は中々立派なものだなア』

丁『俺か、俺は元は百姓だ。御年村の虎公と云ふ男だよ』

甲『ヤア、お前があの名高い自称艮の金神だな、道理で大きな男だと思つたよ』

虎公『アア、確に夫とは分らぬが、何だか館は騒動がおつ始まつたやうだ。サア皆の連中、愚図々々しては居れない。早く館へ駆けつけよう』

と虎さまを先頭に一同は丘を下り、館を指して一散走りに駆けり行く。

と、虎公を先頭に一同は丘を駆け下り、館を目指して一目散に走っていった。
