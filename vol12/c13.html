Chapter 13: Autumn Moon Waterfall [509]

第13章　秋月滝〔509〕

§ 1

Seeking means to dispel the gloom of perpetual night and purify the mortal world, along the Nile River their hearts and flesh were seduced by the teachings of Ural-hiko; righteous deeds were concealed deep in the thickets of trees upon Mount Sinai, and the horrific defilement spread by the crooked deities who confound this realm surged forth. Even the Shūgetsu Waterfall beneath the sky was muddied by rains of sludge; what fell from above was mire and ash, heaping high upon the earth so that feet stumbled along the rugged valley paths. Following the fall of ash on a summer day, freezing snow accumulated—neither summer nor winter, indistinguishable whether spring or autumn, the disordered seasons disrupted all normalcy as the perpetual night dragged on. Yet the divine grace manifested ever more brilliantly: seeking to clear the heavens, Takamitsuhiko, Kunimitsuhiko, Tamamitsuhiko who purifies the souls of crookedness, Priestess Iwaihime like a blooming first blossom, and Katoriwake—the company of six souls advanced toward the deep valley.

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

§ 29

Hatsu-ko: 'How utterly mysterious! I cannot fathom what this means! Nevertheless, after such a long time, the Heavenly Rock Door has opened, and I have beheld the face of the honorable Sun God, faint though it may be! Ah, how grateful, how blessed! Let me recite the Heavenly Norito with reverence!'

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

初公「はて不思議だ、何が何だかさっぱり訳が分からん！ しかしながら久しぶりに天の岩戸が開かれたと見えて、かすかながらもお天道様（日輪様）のお顔を拝むことができた！ ああありがたい、もったいない！ 天津祝詞を謹んで奏上いたしましょう！」

原文 (Original Japanese)

初公『ハテ不思議だ、何の事だか訳が分らぬ。然し乍ら久し振で天の岩戸が開けたと見えて、かすかながらも日輪様のお顔を拝むだ。アヽ有難いありがたい天津祝詞を上げませう』
