Chapter 18: The Water Buffalo [514]

水牛〔514〕

§ 1

The heavens and the earth, hitherto enveloped in darkness, were illuminated by the suddenly appearing fifteenth-night moon; gazing upon Tachibana Island emerging far out upon the brightly shining Ocean of Kure, all the passengers beat upon the ship's gunwales and raised louder and higher shouts of joyful acclaim, pressing onward toward the island's silhouette in buoyant spirits like seagulls singing across the open sea. The boatman, his voice fresh and clear, began to sing as the spring breeze wafted by:

'Deep in the seabed the Dragon Palace is seen; the true balance between Heaven and Earth! I too desire to behold the treasures of the Dragon Palace; part the waves, O Ocean of Kure!'

From a corner of the vessel, a giant of a man stood up toweringly:

'Though the moon illuminates the Ocean of Kure, when gazing forth upon the purple cloud arriving from beyond Mount Caucasus, is it a jewel of gold or a pristine pearl? The snowy skin of the heavenly maiden, her floral countenance and crescent brows! The divine priestess Tachibanahime, leading her attendant deities to illuminate the darkness of perpetual night, has danced gracefully down—is that sacred figure Tachibanahime, or Princess Miyukihime, or the radiant Lady Akizukihime no Mikoto shining without shadow? How heroic, cleaving the white clouds high in the sky! Soaring up Mount Caucasus, pacifying through Kotodama the foul spirits of the Lady of Ural, and duly completing sacred rituals, down arrives the missionary of Ishikoridome!

O missionary Tokiokishi no Mikoto, manifested as the sacred sash of the Great Deity Kamu-Izanagi-no-Ōkami! Tightening the belly-band firmer than the stones of Ishikoridome, driving away the crooked spirits attacking at every turn along each mountain ridge, scattering them across each river shallow, and purifying all: when embarking aboard the Kunitakemaru upon the Ocean of Kure, the Sea Deities were enraged by foul whispers and perverse words, unleashing tempestuous winds, churning wild waves, and swirling whirlwinds to shatter the vessel—yet when reciting with a purified heart the most exalted Great Norito revealed by the Deities of the Anai teachings who abide eternally upon Mount Tenkyō, Mount Chikyō, and Mount Kogane,

The Heavenly Deities, Earthly Deities, and the Gods manifested across the vast sea gave their divine assent; the winds subsided and even the raging billows were peacefully stilled! Looking up, that sacred island guarded by Lady Tachibanahime is right before our very eyes! Entrusting our hearts unto the moon shining high in the heavens, this vessel advances carrying the defilements of mortals heavy with sins. O Four Great Deities of the Purification Portals, cleanse the sins, impurities, and grime of the soul from these mortal bodies! Offering our sincere red hearts purely before the Imperial Deities, following the single flame of the Rising Sun Deity, we offer our true devotions on this day's journey!

Let the hundred, thousand, ten thousand people now aboard the Kunitakemaru rejoice in the profound grace of the God of Heaven and Earth, purifying body and soul in the brine of the rocky shore to serve Him! If all accords with the sincere Way of the Great Deities of Heaven and Earth, even a single utterance of Kotodama shall be accepted by God! Pray, pray, all people; pray with a single unified heart! Ah, Kannagara Tamachihaemase!'

§ 2

Having sung thus, he returned to his seat.

Kō: 'Well, thanks to that, it seems to have become crystal clear that God exists in this world! If the missionary had not prayed for us at that moment, by now we would have been buried inside the belly of fish! From the heavens, a beautiful goddess appeared leading a great host of attendants and showered down exquisitely fragrant blossoms upon us! Henceforth, I will never doubt God again! If one thinks God does not exist, He exists; if one thinks He exists, He does not—at any rate, I have fully grasped that God resides in the single sincerity of the human heart!'

Otsu: 'That is precisely what I am always telling you! How immense is the difference between your heart before acknowledging God and your heart after acknowledging Him!'

Kō: 'Hitherto, I felt so uneasy about this world, as though everything ahead were pitch-black; trembling in constant anxiety, fearing the world, suspecting others, and ultimately suspecting even my own wife, tormented by the delusional obsessions of the asura realm! But today, for the first time, my heart has become as refreshingly clear as if the entire world had burst into brilliant sunshine! Above all, this is naturally due to the grace of the merciful God who protects us, but also entirely thanks to the missionary of the Anai teachings who prayed with sincere devotion unto Heaven and Earth!'

Otsu: 'Do you know who it was that just sang that missionary chant?'

Kō: 'His voice sounded somewhat familiar, but he has changed so splendidly that I cannot recall immediately.'

Otsu: 'Is he not the very gentleman we met some time ago at the mansion of Princess Kujakuhime on the Plain of Kuronogahara?!'

Hei: 'Right you are! When we set out as constables to arrest them at the mansion of Princess Kujakuhime, the five of us were hoisted up like kittens by the scruff of our necks, trembling in terror as to what would become of us—whereupon he gave us sake to drink and expounded magnificent teachings unto us! When we told him we were fed up with the constable's job and wished to quit, he said, "That is your assigned calling!" Truly, one must never commit evil deeds! Vast though the world may be, one never knows where one might run into someone! Hey you fellows, why don't we offer our thanks and at the same time apologize for the insolence we committed upon Mount Caucasus?!'

Kō: 'That requires careful thinking! The four of us—Ox, Horse, Deer, and Tiger—have turned our coats so often, siding now with one party and now with another while committing plenty of shameful deeds! If we carelessly reveal our identities, there is no telling what dreadful punishment we might suffer this time! We had better feign complete ignorance!'

乙『そんな、股の間に何か挟んでいるような気味の悪い真似ができるものか。この船には三五教の宣伝使が六人も乗っておられるのだぞ。ともかく従順に尾を振ってこの場を切り抜けるのだ。改心したような顔をしていればいいのだ。しかし俺たちは心の底から改心しているが、どうしても貴様が根底からの改心ができぬというなら、貴様だけでも従順にして改心したふうに見せていればいいじゃないか。あちらから「おい、そこにいるのは牛、馬、鹿、虎じゃないか」と言われてからでは、格好がつかないじゃないか。お月様がお出ましになってあたりが明るくなり、風が止んで波が静まり、やれ楽になったと思えば、三五教の宣伝使の顔がありありと見え出した。こちらが見えるのと同様に、向こうからも俺たちの顔が透き通るように見えているに違いない。ああ、照る月も恨めしいが、曇るのも恨めしいだろうなあ、牛公よ』

牛公『まあまあ、ちょっと考えさせてくれ。何だか大勢の前で謝罪したり叱られたりするのはみっともなくてたまらない。まあ行くところまで行こうじゃないか』

丙『貴様は淡白さのない無精な男だなあ。徳利に味噌を詰めて逆さに振って出すような男だ。綺麗な座敷の真ん中で裃を着けたまま大便を垂れ流して、立つに立てないような格好だ。貴様は牛公だから、さっきからグズグズ言って謝りに行こうというのにビクともしないのは、大方股に牛糞でも挟んでいるのだろう。体よく、海があまりに荒れて怖かったので牛糞が出たと白状したらどうだ』

牛公『鹿公の言う通り、大きな声では言えぬが、実は動くことができないのだよ、糞忌々しい！』

§ 4

At that very moment, the missionary Tokiokishi stood up toweringly and stepped forward toward them, parting the crowd:

'Well now, is that you, Ushikō?! What a wicked scoundレル you are! How did you find that recent tempest? It was because an evil villain like you was aboard that the Dragon Deity was so enraged! I shall now pull the demon right out of your body!'

'What?! Pull off my neck and head?! Please wait, I beg of you!'

'Nay! There is a saying: doff your hat when you meet! Suiting the occasion, this is Tokiokishi's drastic remedy—do not move from that spot!'

Otsu: 'Hey, Ushikō, really don't move! If you move, the stench will be unbearable!'

Paying no heed to details, the divine priest Tokiokishi stepped straight before Ushikō. Letting out a screeching shriek of 'Gyaaah!', Ushikō plunged with a splash into the moonlit waves, and with a bubbling sound, his black form vanished into the white foam of the ship's wake.

Hoisting its sails high in the favorable wind that had begun to blow, the Kunitakemaru relentlessly cast aside this tragicomedy and continued sailing onward and onward.
