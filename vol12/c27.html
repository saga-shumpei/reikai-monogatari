Chapter 27: The Heavenly Airship [523]

航空船〔523〕

§ 1

Uralhiko no Mikoto and Uralhime no Mikoto styling themselves the Divine King Pangu, established bases at Mount Ural and Armenia; opening their capital upon Mount Caucasus as their second strategic center, their authority was peerless—yet driven from their capital upon Mount Caucasus by the missionaries of the Anai teachings, they fled back toward Mount Ural and Armenia, assembling hordes of demonic deities to plot their resurgence. However, as Kamu-Susanō-no-Mikoto displayed His radiant martial valor upon Mount Caucasus near Armenia, reigning supreme across the realm, even these formidable demons found no way to strike; leaving Miyamahiko and Kuniteruhime to hold Armenia to the death, Uralhiko himself crossed over unto Yomijima to devise a second campaign.

Uralhiko and Uralhime were originally deities of pure and sincere devotion; yet just as noxious pests attack luscious fruits, through a momentary lapse of spiritual vigilance, they became possessed by the eight-headed serpent, evil foxes, and wicked demons, enslaved by these evil spirits to tread unintended paths of perversity and raise the banner of rebellion against the true God. Miyamahiko too had once been reconciled and returned unto the righteous path by Tsukiteruhiko no Mikoto and Ashimahiko no Mikoto; yet possessed once more by evil spirits, his heart was darkened, and yielding to the enticements of Kuniteruhime, he became Uralhiko's subordinate once again, dedicating himself to atrocious wicked deeds.

In the sacred city of Armenia, Miyamahiko outwardly styled himself Uralhiko no Mikoto and Kuniteruhime assumed the name of Uralhime no Mikoto, putting on a bold front and assembling myriad demons to defend this capital unto the death, plotting to launch a world-shaking enterprise in concert with Yomijima.

Accompanied by the incarnation of the deity Tsukiteruhiko, the missionary of the Anai teachings, Hahoribe-no-Kami, departed from Mount Kogane, setting sail from the port of Yoru in Tsukushi to annihilate the demons of Yomijima. This vessel was a great ship named the Tsukushimaru. Because the Tsukushimaru sailed past Ryūgūjima along Yomijima toward the land of Tokoyo, the vast majority of passengers were migrants bound for Tokoyo. Hahoribe-no-Kami and Tsukiteruhiko became passengers aboard the Tsukushimaru, continuing their voyage for dozens of days. Diverse portents arose across the ocean: islands appeared where none had been, colossal boulders suddenly surged up and submerged, the sun and moon were darkened without light, the wind smelled strangely of blood, and the voyage was an indescribably grim and unpleasant ordeal. Aboard the ship, various conversations began as usual.

Across the ocean surface of perpetual gloom, where day could not be distinguished from night, the ship swerved right and dodged left to evade surfacing boulders; moving with sluggish pace like a blind man traversing a barren wasteland without a staff, it was an agonizingly perilous voyage tossing upon the waves.

Suddenly a furious tempest struck, towering waves like mountains surged up threatening to swallow the Tsukushimaru, and in extreme peril, all the passengers clasped their hands and prayed fervently in hushed whispers. Thereupon, Hahoribe-no-Kami arose and began to sing:

'God manifests visibly upon the surface to separate good from evil! The morning sun is hidden without light, and the moon has fallen unto the earth without a shadow! By the Kotodama bestowed by the Imperial Deities, blow away the foul fury of the eight-headed serpents and evil spirits coiled across the vast ocean, across the lands of the four quarters! A missionary proclaiming the Anai Way that reconciles even the evil spirits of the great ocean! Even if the world turns to perpetual gloom, even if Yomijima sinks beneath the sea, the Land of Tokoyo shall drift eternally upon the waves, receiving the divine grace of Amaterasu-Ōmikami and Kunitokotachi-no-Ōkami! The mortals drifting upon the white waves,

Shall fall into sorrowful shallows and suffer, just as Yomijima sinks day by day and month by month! Ah, all mortals, all mortals! Yield unto God's teachings, polish the spirit of your Naohi, and pray before God morning and evening, pray well! I am a missionary of the Anai teachings who goes forth to save this world! Protected by Tsukiteruhiko, manifested as the deity Hahoribe to cleanse the crookedness of this world, a divine minister who reconciles the eight-headed serpent blocking the pass of Yomotsu-Hirasaka, sweeping away the world's evils to rescue mankind! God manifests visibly upon the surface,

To divide good from evil! O Kaminaho-no-Kami who created this world, O Ōnaho-no-Kami of boundless heart! In all human affairs, revise and relisten in rectitude, and reproclaim the errors of the self! God shall surely protect you! Ah, Kannagara Tamachihaemase!'

Miraculously, the tempest ceased abruptly; and before the calm waters could even settle, a northwest gale blew forth, sending the Tsukushimaru flying like an arrow toward Yomijima. Contrary to expectations, Yomijima drew rapidly near. Suddenly Yomijima emitted a thunderous roar, beginning to submerge visibly beneath the ocean with terrifying speed, at which spectacle all the passengers gasped in wide-eyed horror.

Hahoribe-no-Kami: 'O passengers aboard, as I sang earlier, we are missionaries of the Anai teachings! Even though Yomijima is sinking, by the divine power of Kotodama I shall cause it to surface once more upon the sea! The power of faith is sublime indeed! Behold the miraculous efficacy of my prayer and reform your hearts!'

『ヤア貴神は正鹿山津見神』

『ヤア貴神は祝部神で御座るか。サア早くこの御船に乗らせ給へ』

祝部神は、

『全く救ひの船だ、有難し忝なし』

と磐樟船にヒラリと身を托し、中空高くかすめて天教山を目蒐け、一瀉千里の勢にて天を轟かしつつ阿波岐原に漸く降り着きにける。

俄に聞ゆる松風の音に目を開けば、豈図らむや、十四日の月は西山に沈み、高熊山の霧立ち昇る巌窟の傍に瑞月の身は端坐し居たりける。

📖 現代日本語訳

東風が俄かに吹き起こり、筑紫丸は帆をいっぱいに膨らませながら、一瀉千里の勢いで波の上を滑るように疾走していった。船中の人々は黄泉島に残る祝部神との別れを惜しみ、手を打ち、笠を振り、袖を振って、その姿が見えなくなるまで名残を惜しんだ。

島の曲津神たちは祝部神の言霊の息吹を恐れ、雲霞の如く黄泉比良坂を目指して逃げ去っていった。祝部神は足を速めて飛ぶ鳥のように、月照彦神の冥護のもと黄泉比良坂の坂上へと登っていった。

坂の上には、日の出神がかつて用いられた千引の岩があった。この巨岩の上に端座して神言を奏上していたまさにその時、天地を揺るがす大音響とともに、あれほど広大であった黄泉島は海中へと忽然として沈没し、残るはただ千引の岩一つのみとなった。折から荒波が千引の岩を洗い、祝部神の身体をも今まさにさらおうとしたその刹那、天空を轟かせて飛来し舞い降りる天の磐樟船（航空船）があった。見れば日の出神が遣わされた堅牢無比の神船であり、正鹿山津見神が乗り組んでおられた。

祝部神『おお、貴神は正鹿山津見神！』

『おお、貴神は祝部神であられるか！ さあ、早くこの御船にお乗りなされ！』

祝部神は、

『まことに救いの船、ありがたし、かたじけなし！』

と磐樟船へとひらりと身を躍らせた。神船は中空高く雲をかすめ、天教山を目指して一瀉千里の勢いで天を轟かせつつ飛行し、やがて阿波岐原へと静かに舞い降りた。

俄かに聞こえてきた松風の音にふと目を開けば、何ということであろうか！ 十四日の月は西の山へと沈み、高熊山の霧立ち昇る巌窟の傍らに、瑞月（王仁三郎）の身は静かに端座していたのであった。
