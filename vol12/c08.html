Chapter 8: Song of Reminiscence [504]

第8章　思出の歌〔504〕

§ 1

Unchecked by barriers, the courser of the passing months and days Rounds bountifully into the month of Kisaragi; On this very day, the ninth morn of the month, Beckoned unto the divine rock cavern of Mount Takakuma in towering majesty, To establish the sacred reign of Miroku (567), As divine messenger of the mountains of the Nine Peaks and Eight Seas upon Mount Matsuoka! The moon already tilted toward the western hills, The wind grew tranquil and the flowing waters hushed; At the stroke of midnight, slipping quietly from my humble dwelling, Into the deep mountainous road of the divine realm unbeknownst to mortal man, To pluck the sacred blossoms—on this very day Twenty-five years have blown past like the seasonal tide-winds! O Izu-no-Mitama (Spirits of Stern Purity) and Mizu-no-Mitama (Spirits of Gracious Virtue), The radiance of the sacred teaching shines without a shadow, Flowing crystal clear along the Ogumo River; Beside the riverbank where evergreen pines flourish, Stands high the pavilion of Shōunkaku, Where at last the narrative of Volume 12 commences!

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

月日の駆ける駒には関所もなく、豊かに巡りめぐって二月（如月）の上九日の今日という日を迎えた。 尊き稜威も高き高熊山の神の岩窟へと誘われ、五六七（みろく）の御代を築くべく、松岡の九山八海の山々の神使として立ったあの日。 月は早くも西に傾き、風も静まり水も静まりかえった子の正刻（真夜中十二時）、粗末なわが家をそっと抜け出し、奥深い山道を経て、人に知られぬ神国の霊花を手折ってから、今日でちょうど二十五年の歳月が潮風のように巡り去った。 厳（いづ）の御魂、瑞（みづ）の御魂の御教えの光は隈なく輝き、清らかに流れる小雲川のほとり、常磐の松が生い茂る川辺にそびえ立つ松雲閣において、いよいよ第十二巻の物語を口述し始めるのである。

§ 2

The sacred tidings of the primordial Divine Age Flow as smoothly as the currents upon the shoals of the Otonase River, Gliding forth effortlessly like a spinning phonograph; The opening portal of Mizu-no-Mitama, Where birds sing seeking the fragrant plum blossoms! From the distant capital in the east, The Sankōdō hall shines with the brilliance of Mizu-no-Mitama; The root of the Pine of Oomoto endures eternally, And the teachings of the Moon of Ananai Are flawless, radiant, and peerless without equal. Through the thoughtful arrangements of Master Hattori Shizuo, Weaving the silk brocades of Aya, The valley door opens wide, and the warbler Sings with tranquil voice unto the world, Proclaiming the vast and wondrous divine way! Upon the sacred rock of Deguchi in Kamino, The flourishing thousand-year pine stands auspicious; The Kamunahohi Deity of the primordial teaching, A divine breeze that stirs not even a leaf upon the bough!

現代語訳 (Modern Japanese)

§ 3

Boundless across the seas within and without, The mountains swell, pregnant with rich twin buds; In the flowering spring of Kitamura, Radiance shines bright in the heavenly sky! In this sacred age at Matsumura, The teaching of Princess Konohana-hime Manifested upon the sacred peak of Mount Misen, Ever held fast within the depths of the heart, Reveals the divine narrative of the Fuji age. The heavenly deities of Mount Fuji Endeavor to convey the path of truth unto the Central Reed Plains forever; Nodatihiko and Nodatihime Chant the great solemn Norito of the sacred teachings; The declared Kotodama envelops all, Reaching from mountain peaks down to the depths of the valleys, Sweeping away the gathering dark clouds in the four directions! Companions of the true path of divine wisdom, The blossoms of their hearts waft with pure fragrance!

§ 4

Tsutaetaro of the Sovereign Great Deity, Possessing the heroic valor of the true Yamato spirit, Cleansing away all empty, futile slander; Kyutaro Iwata alone as scribe Entrusting the declared Kotodama unto the living brush, Continuously recording the sacred words! How sacred is this birthday of mine! In the year of the Metal Rooster (Kanoto-Tori, 1921), Hearing the divine voice on the eighth day of the Chrysanthemum Month (October 18), Possessed by the divine spirit, The mouth of Deguchi opened wide To dictate the singular, truthful, wondrous Divine Age narrative of the Spirit World! Completing the oral recitation of the first two volumes in full and thorough detail, Even through dark nights the autumn festival of the gods Passed without hindrance; arriving at the Pavilion of Zuishō, The auspicious foundation of ten thousand generations, I led one hundred companions unto the famous Mount Takakuma,

§ 5

Praising the noble majesty of the sacred rock cavern; Visiting my birthplace in Miyagaito where the gods assemble, Prostrating in reverent prayer before the Ubusuna Guardian Deity; Returning then to the Garden of Teachings in Kameoka of auspicious name; The gateway of the blossoms of words, Associated with the Mizu-no-Mitama, Recording half of Volume 3 before returning to the Ryūgū-kan; In the Kyōshu-den facing the Golden Pavilion (Ōgonkaku), Setting the brush into the hands of the three noble sons of the teaching, Buffeted by the mountain storms upon Hongū-san and Shibi-san, How vigorously was Volume 4 dictated and brought forth! An inexhaustible, endless story, Opening the inception of Volume 5 of the Spirit of Five (Miroku), Entrusting myself unto the swift chariot of Kotodama!

§ 6

Unto the thousand-year hot springs of Iwai, Where the blessings of the deities warmed both body and soul; Securing the Miroku (567) guest room on the third floor of the spinning Komaya inn, Unchanging and intimately harmonious, Narrating the six chapters of Volume 6! When the thirtieth day of the twelfth month arrived, As urged by the inner courser of my heart, Aboard the swift-running train, Returning safely unto the Heavenly realm of Ayabe, Dictating Volume 7 while resting at Kinsuitei, Finishing the narration in just four days! Here the festival of Setsubun of the Water Dog year (Mizunoe-Inu, 1922) passed in peace; Adding yet another year, becoming once more a resident of the Zuishō Pavilion, After the festival concluded, Leading two hundred and fifty companions unto the peak of Mount Takakuma,

§ 7

Ascending in pilgrimage where the divine virtues blossomed forth into Volume 8! Volumes 9 and 10 were turned back before half was reached, Lying down in the inner chamber of the Kyōshu-den in the Heavenly Realm of Ayabe, At last the heart of Volume 10 was auspiciously compiled; Returning to Shōunkaku at Naminoki, On the evening of the seventh day, the narration of Volume 11 was accomplished! On this deeply memorable eighth day of Kisaragi, At last I take up the task of Volume 12! Recording even one ten-thousandth of the magnificent conditions of that sublime Divine Age, Committing each detail faithfully with the brush, Celebrating my birthday on this very day, I record these words for the sake of all mortal souls across the world! Ah, Kannagara! Kannagara! (Living in harmony with the Divine Will)
