Chapter 26: The Resplendent Autumn Moon [522]

秋月皎々〔522〕

§ 1

Floating amidst the vast expanse of Lake Biwa lies Takeshima. Manifested as the Mizu-no-Mitama of Kamu-Susanō-no-Ōkami, a branch spirit of the ten-span sword, Lady Akizukihime the divine priestess leveled the island's summit, erecting high chigi crossbeams for her sacred palace; morning and evening she consecrated the Mizu-no-Mitama of the Imperial Deities, offering auspicious prayers with sincere devotion to dispel the layered clouds barricading Heaven and Earth and radiate the glorious divine majesty! While she was reciting the Great Norito within the inner chamber of the Kuretake Palace,

There resounded at the foot of the hill thunderous battle-cries! Wondering whether it was the surging of offshore tempests or the roar of waves, she swiftly mounted the high tower and looked straight down—whereupon an unimaginable host of warriors swarmed thick as clouds and mists, brandishing steel halberds and slaughtering the hundred islanders indiscriminately! Overwhelmed by their savage onslaught, the people fled in utter confusion in all directions—a spectacle so heartbreaking that even detached onlookers could not bear to witness it! Setting torch to the dense bamboo thickets, fanned by the lake breeze, billowing smoke surged up amidst the crackling thunder of bursting bamboo, transforming this sacred island instantly into an infernal battlefield of the asura realm—a tragedy too pitiable to behold!

Lady Akizukihime stood up:

'The enemy presses close! Is Takakurawake not here?! Where is Tatsuyamawake?!'

At her call, rubbing his eyes, Takakurawake sprang forth:

'What is Your command, my Lady?!'

Lady Akizukihime: 'Takakurawake, mount the high tower at once and strike the signal drum!'

'Understood!' he replied, racing up the tower like a bird of prey:

'Who can this vast army be that assaults this supremely sacred island?! Is it the demonic army of Princess Ural, or the divine host of Mount Tenkyō?! At all events, prepare for defense!'

Leaping down lightly in a single bound, he shouted:

'Tatsuyamawake, where are you?! The enemy presses close in violent savagery, setting fire to the bamboo groves to sack this sacred island in a single assault! All hands, prepare for defense!'

At his summons, Tatsuyamawake appeared:

'An unexpected enemy assault! Who can the foe be?! I shall reconnoiter at once!'

As Takakurawake commanded him to go quickly, Tatsuyamawake leaped upon a chestnut steed, maneuvering the reins down the winding, zigzagging mountain path toward the foot of the hill. When Takakurawake assembled all remaining occupants of the mansion, there were merely forty-eight souls in all, counting men and women alike.

'All of you, an army thick as clouds and mists has assaulted our island! Outnumbered so vastly, if we meet arms with arms and force with force, defeat is certain! Far better that you wash your hands, rinse your mouths, sit before the Kuretake Palace, reverently chant divine words and sing missionary songs to receive divine protection, and reconcile the advancing foe through Kotodama! I shall now advance to the inner chamber to protect Lady Akizukihime!'

Hurling these words, he hurried into the inner sanctuary. As commanded, all cleansed themselves, sat upright before the Kuretake Palace, and chanted the Heavenly Norito with ringing voices. Ascending the high tower, Lady Akizukihime confronted the oncoming host with unruffled, serene poise, reciting the auspicious words of the Norito, followed by her prayer unto Heaven and Earth:

'Looking up unto the lofty heavens, O Kamu-Izanagi-no-Ōkami who rules the high skies; O Kamu-Izanami-no-Ōkami who rules the vast ocean; Kamu-Susanō-no-Ōkami and Tsukiteruhiko no Mikoto, radiant with pure light in the sky, Ashimahiko, Sukunahiko, and Hirokohiko—by the grace of your divine spirits, reconcile the foul host, and placate peacefully and serenely the hundred foes assaulting Takeshima! Let the raging deities advancing in the furious pride of the ten-span sword be moderated and illuminated by the light of living Kotodama, so that across the four quarters under heaven, no enemies remain, and all impurities, sins, and crookedness are swept away by the divine wind!

God manifests visibly upon the surface to separate good from evil, aiding the good and reconciling evil deities through the divine Way! In all mortal affairs, revise and relisten in divine rectitude, and reproclaim the crooked words of the world! If the true God exists, dispel the layered clouds choking this island and shine the true sun and moon without delay! Before the Great Imperial Deity who created this world, reverently and awe-inspiringly do I pray!'

As she finished her song and descended from the tower, Takakurawake came galloping back on horseback:

'I report unto Lady Akizukihime! We thought the attackers were the demonic host of Uralhiko and Uralhime, but lo, it is Amatsuhikone no Kami, one of the five noble sons of Amaterasu-Ōmikami! Brandishing steel halberds and torching the bamboo groves, seizing every fleeing islander, shattering buildings, and slaughtering creatures in unbridled rampage! Against tens of thousands of advancing troops, we have barely forty souls, young and old, men and women alike! When human hubris swells it seems to triumph over Heaven; since it has come to this, there is nothing left but to end our lives honorably! To be slain by nameless enemy foot-soldiers would be a disgrace unto eternity; I shall lead the way unto the underworld!'

§ 3

Baring his torso, just as he was about to plunge his dagger into his flank, Tatsuyamawake leaped forward like a flash, snatched the dagger from Takakurawake's hand, and shouted in a booming voice:

'Lord Takakurawake! You are a divine priest serving the awesome God! In such a crisis, what madness is it to throw away the precious life bestowed upon you by God?! To exhaust all heart and might unto the very last breath, and only then entrust your fate unto Heaven—is that not the true path for us who teach mankind?! Desist for a moment! How could the true God, who sees through good and evil, truth and falsehood as clearly as in a mirror, ever abandon us?! Suicide is the supreme crime among all crimes! In such an hour of peril, why do you not pray unto God?!'

Takakurawake: 'Ah, Lord Tatsuyamawake! Blinded by the sudden assault and bewildered in my course, forgetting God and forgetting the Way was my utter folly and deepest disgrace! As you say, I shall now mount the high tower and pour out my prayers unto the God of Heaven and Earth!'

Composing himself, he ascended unto the high tower.

Leading tens of thousands of warriors, flush with victory, Amatsuhikone no Kami stormed up to the front gate. As the occupants of the mansion cried out in terror, the triumphant army flooded through the gates like a tidal wave. At that moment, from the high tower of the inner sanctuary, the sublime, crystalline tones of an ichigenkin lute resounded, accompanied by the clear, pure chanting of the Heavenly Norito. At that sacred sound, Amatsuhikone's heart was softened; standing spellbound, he listened intently. Presently, flinging his sword, bow, and arrows unto the earth, he clapped his hands and joined in chanting the divine words. Inspired by their general's sudden transformation, the soldiers all cast down their weapons, sat upright upon the ground, clapped their hands, and joined in chanting the Heavenly Norito with ringing voices!

Singing missionary songs, the deities Tokiokishi and Yukihirawake stood behind the divine host, dancing and singing with comical, joyous gestures and stamping feet. Accompanied by Takakurawake and Tatsuyamawake, Lady Akizukihime appeared, fluttering her long sleeves with consummate grace:

'Tōtō tarariya, tōtarari, tarariya tarari, tōtarari!'

Spreading her fan and stamping rhythmically upon the ground, she danced with celestial ecstasy. Takakurawake, Tatsuyamawake, the enemy general Amatsuhikone no Mikoto, Tokiokishi, and Yukihirawake all stepped into the center, joining handclaps and foot-stamps with Lady Akizukihime, dancing in wild jubilation with all distinction of friend and foe utterly forgotten!

と扇を開いて地踏み鳴らし舞ひ狂ひ玉ふ。高倉別、竜山別を初め神軍の大将天津彦根命、時置師神、行平別神は中央に現はれ、秋月姫と諸共に手拍子足拍子を揃へ、敵味方の区別も忘れて狂ふが如く踊り廻る。

と、心を落ち着けて高楼へと登っていった。

天津彦根神は数万の神軍を率いて勢いに乗り、表門へと迫ってきた。館の老若男女が悲鳴を上げて逃げ惑う中、勝ち誇った神軍は怒涛の如く門内へと乱入した。その時、奥殿の高楼から荘厳なる一絃琴の音が爽やかに響き、天津祝詞を奏上する声が清々しく響き渡った。天津彦根神はその祝詞の声に心が和らぎ、茫然として耳を傾け聞き入った。やがて太刀や弓矢を大地に投げ捨て、両手を打って共に神言を奏上し始めた。大将軍のこの急変に多くの兵士たちも感染し、皆武器を捨てて大地に端座し、両手を打って天津祝詞を高らかに唱和し始めたのである。

時置師神と行平別神は宣伝歌を歌いながら神軍の後方に立ち、面白おかしく手振りを交え足を踏み鳴らして歌い舞った。秋月姫は高倉別と竜山別を従えてこの場に現れ、袂をしとやかに翻し、

『とうとうたらりや、とうたらり、たらりやたらり、とうたらり』

と扇を開いて大地を踏み鳴らし、狂おしく舞い給うた。高倉別、竜山別をはじめ、神軍の大将・天津彦根命、時置師神、行平別神も中央に進み出て、秋月姫と共に手拍子足拍子を揃え、敵味方の区別も忘れて夢中で踊り狂った。

§ 4

At that moment, the black clouds clustering in the heavens were blown away by the winds of Shinato; the bright sunlight blazed forth resplendently, and all suspicion against Susanō no Mikoto was completely dispelled. Rejoicing in great triumph, Amatsuhikone no Kami led his troops across Lake Biwa to make a glorious return unto Mount Tenkyō. Remaining behind, Tokiokishi and Yukihirawake administered the healing mist of divine breath unto every slain and wounded islander, reviving the dead and healing the injured; and chanting the Heavenly Counting Song over the scorched forests, they restored the mountains unto their former verdant splendor.

Furthermore, the missionary Takamitsuhiko was also among the valiant deities who had secretly arrived upon this island with Tokiokishi and Yukihirawake, hiding within the forests and chanting the Heavenly Counting Song to resolve this catastrophe in peaceful harmony. Lady Akizukihime plighted her troth unto Takamitsuhiko, abiding long upon this island to take part in the divine work. And the second brother, Tamamitsuhiko, wed Lady Miyukihime of the singular island in the Inland Sea, returning unto Mount Manju as the successor to his father Iwakusuhiko no Kami to serve eternally in the divine enterprise.

原文（近代文語）

この時天上に群がれる黒雲は科戸の風に吹き散りて、天日の光晃々と輝き始め素盞嗚命の疑は全く晴れ渡つた。天津彦根神は喜び勇むで数多の将卒を引連れ、琵琶の湖を渡りて天教山に凱旋せり。後に残りし時置師神、行平別神は、或は殺され或は負傷に悩む嶋人に一々伊吹の狭霧を施し、死したる者を生かし傷つける者を癒やし、焼けたる林は天の数歌を歌ひ上げて再旧の如く青々と緑の山に化せしめける。

茲にまた高光彦の宣伝使は時置師神、行平別神と共に窃にこの嶋に現れ来り、森林の中に身を潜めて天の数歌を歌ひこの惨状を平和に治めたる勇神なり。秋月姫は高光彦と結婚の約を結び、永くこの島に留まりて神業に参加し給ひぬ。又、中の弟玉光彦は瀬戸の海の一つ島なる深雪姫を娶り、万寿山に立ち帰り父磐樟彦神の後継者となりて永遠に神業に参加し給ひけるとなむ。
