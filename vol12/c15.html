§ 1

Amidst the mountain tempests and pine winds blowing before the rock cavern of Mount Takakuma, awakening as a messenger of God, Zuigetsu gazed up at the night sky of the fourteenth day. The starlight gradually grew misty and hazy, and the moon was already on the verge of concealing itself behind the peak of Mount Misen; the morning gale blew and cocks crowed. Prostrating in prayer before the rock cavern of Mount Takakuma, taking the brush in hand for the sake of the world, in the town of Ayabe at the Matsuoka pavilion, gathering the Heavenly Deities, he joyfully sets brush to paper for this fifteenth chapter of Volume 12! The story revealed in this spiritual dream: Priestess Iwaihime pleasing unto the divine mind, Katoriwake the divine messenger, Takamitsuhiko of divine spirit, Tamamitsuhiko of sacred light, Kunimitsuhiko of lofty divine majesty, and Yukihirawake fluttering in the wind—these six divine officials advancing to dispel and purge the crooked spirits of Miyuki Waterfall with the heavenly words of good and beautiful speech (Zengen Bishi).

附言

§ 12

Spirits destined to become husband and wife, souls destined to become parent and child, and souls destined to become master and disciple are intrinsically fixed and unchanging from the foundation of the world. Nevertheless, due to worldly obligations or various mundane circumstances, people are sometimes unavoidably led to marry an ill-matched soul or enter into a relationship of teacher and disciple; yet where there is no genuine affinity between spirit and spirit, the bond inevitably fractures midway. That the angel Katoriwake, fearing that until Priestess Iwaihime met her destined spiritual spouse she might marry a different soul and ruin her heavenly mission halfway through, devised diverse means, provisionally naming her his own consort-deity until the ordained time arrived—this was none other than the divine protection of God's supreme compassion and mercy! Therefore, before entering marriage, mortals must receive the sanction of their local guardian deity (Ubusuna-no-Kami) and obtain divine revelation; otherwise, dominated by the body-dominant and spirit-subservient circumstances of social status, wealth, worldly reputation, social obligations, emotional sympathy, or romantic infatuation, they will spend an entire lifetime in marital unhappiness. Thus, the way of husband and wife, which is the foundation of human ethics, must receive the permission of God, and one must never marry lightly or rashly. There are cases where one must take a second wife, the so-called 'wife of the second life (nisei no tsuma)'; this occurs when the soul did not harmonize with the first wife, or even if it did harmonize, the physical body could not support the spirit, resulting in untimely death. Nevertheless, whether in terms of deep love or domestic management, the second wife is almost always inferior to the first wife. In short, though bearing the name of wife, the second wife is generally merely a surrogate for the first wife. However, there are also cases where the second wife surpasses the first wife in every respect; that is when the spiritual bond between husband and wife was absent in the first marriage, and the second wife represents the true spiritual union. If both marriages are spiritually mismatched and one partner passes away midway, even if a soul of true spiritual harmony is discovered on the third occasion, it is the unwritten law of the Divine Realm that one can never take a third wife.

Come, from this moment on, unless we reflect upon the conceited pride that unknowingly creeps into our hearts and embody the true mind of God, far from subjugating the crooked deities of the Five Waterfalls, we ourselves shall be vanquished! Ah, now that Master Katoriwake has departed, I feel as though I were abandoned all alone upon an isolated island where not even a bird flies!'

§ 16

Tamamitsuhiko: 'Indeed! We must each tighten our belts and set to work! Unless a person takes up a position with his back to the wall, no great deed can succeed! With resolute decision and the fury of a rampaging lion, we must first expel the demons possessing our own spirits; only after our souls become pure and immaculate do we acquire the qualification to conquer evil spirits! We imagine that the demon dwells in the great cataract, but lo and behold, within the depth of our own heart hangs the great cataract of the Shirase River, and therein nests the demon serpent! An external foe outside the body can easily be vanquished, but an internal enemy within the heart is hardest of all to subdue! Before charging into the demon of Miyuki Waterfall, shall we first conquer the demon within each of us, or bring it into willing submission, before setting to work?!'

§ 17

Takamitsuhiko: 'Ah, precisely so! Confronting evil spirits is exactly like shooting an arrow at a target. Then that arrow unfailingly strikes the dead center of the target! First of all, to rectify one's own soul is the paramount essential!'

§ 18

Kunimitsuhiko: 'The enemy is at Honnō-ji; the enemy of the self lurks within one's own heart! 'God manifest stands revealed, to sever good from evil! The divine way where good and evil are non-dual—if you think good, it turns to good; if you think evil, it turns to evil! The moment they finished singing, the Heavens and Earth that had been locked in pitch-black gloom for a hundred days and nights suddenly burst open as if dawn had broken in full glory! The Sun shone brilliant and golden in the sky, the tumultuous roar of the waterfall ceased instantly, and the ferocious screams of tigers, wolves, lions, serpents, and demons vanished without a trace, returning peace unto the universe!
