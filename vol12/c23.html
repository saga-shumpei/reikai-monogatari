Chapter 23: A Swift and Fierce Assault [519]

短兵急〔519〕

§ 1

Gazing across the ocean below from the high tower of Miyukihime's palace upon the singular island, an endless host of warships, numbered by hundreds and thousands, advanced in majestic fish-scale formation pressing relentlessly toward the island with formidable menace. Deeming this no ordinary matter, Miyukihime summoned her veteran retainer Takasugiwake:

'Lord Takasugiwake, gazing upon the ocean from this high tower, I see countless warships advancing upon us—go and ascertain with certainty whether they be the demonic army of Uralhiko, or the divine army of the Great Imperial Deity revealed upon Mount Tenkyō!'

Receiving this command, Takasugiwake replied:

'I have fully understood! I shall descend the mountain at once, investigate the truth of the matter, and report immediately back!'

No sooner had he spoken than he leaped upon his steed and galloped clattering down Miyukigaoka toward the shore. Miyukihime then summoned Ōkuniwake near:

'Lord Ōkuniwake, the vast army assaulting this mountain is no ordinary occurrence. Even if it be the demonic army of Uralhiko, you must never oppose them with weapons! To pacify and reconcile crookedness through the Kotodama of good and beautiful words is the supreme divine will of Kamu-Susanō-no-Mikoto! Although this mansion abounds with countless weapons and warriors, their purpose is by no means the slaughter of enemies! These sacred treasures exist solely to awe and bring into righteous submission the armies of crooked spirits lurking in the hearts of gods and men through the virtue of the sword; therefore, keep bows sheathed in their bags and swords housed in their scabbards—confront all enemies with weapons at rest, and transmit this strict order unto every officer and soldier!'

Thus did she solemnly proclaim.

Ōkuniwake: 'The enemy swarms like clouds and mists toward this mountain, slaughtering islanders and torching houses and forests; the raging flames are already burning close unto this very mansion! Was not our daily martial discipline intended precisely for such a crisis as this?! Now is the hour for our trained bowmen and stalwart soldiers of tempered spirit to display their valor—if not now, when shall we ever fight?! To watch passively as we are burned and destroyed by the enemy is unbearable! Though God is supreme benevolence and love, suiting the times He wields the sharp sword of subjugation! How much more so for Your Divinity, born of the virtue of the ten-span sacred sword enshrined upon Mount Caucasus! Have You lost Your senses?! I implore You to reconsider once more!'

Miyukihime: 'Swords must not be wielded lightly! The sword is a deadly weapon! To meet evil with evil and repay violence with violence is the method of ordinary mortals! As an angel proclaiming the Anai teachings across the realm, and as a missionary, to cast aside the Kotodama of good and beautiful words and meet arms with arms is what my heart can never permit! Entrust all things unto the God of supreme benevolence and love! To honor martial virtue and revere bravery means the "stance of no stance"—having weapons yet using none, possessing no weapons yet using weapons, enduring with fortitude, overcoming ferocious madness with gentleness, confronting evil with good, and facing demons with God! Softness overcoming hardness is the military strategy of the divine host, the heavenly stratagem of the Six Secret Teachings! Never forget this principle! I shall now retire into the inner sanctuary, offer divine prayers before the Great Deity, and reconcile the advancing foe through Kotodama! Unto the lowest private soldier, on this day alone, under no circumstances must anyone bear weapons!'

Thus proclaiming, she leisurely prepared to retire into the inner sanctuary. Clinging unto Miyukihime's sleeve, Ōkuniwake pleaded:

'Pray wait, wait but a moment! The shouts and arrow-cries draw nearer and nearer—no matter how one seeks to reconcile them through good and beautiful words, it cannot avail against raw violence! I shall now lead my officers and soldiers to cut down and sweep away the advancing foe in every direction, demonstrating our long-trained valor! Grant permission for this alone, I beseech You!'

Clenching his fists and trembling in heroic agitation, he implored her. Miyukihime, calm and unhurried, sang in measured, tranquil tones:

'God manifests visibly upon the surface to separate good from evil! O Kaminaho-no-Kami who created this world, O Ōnaho-no-Kami of boundless heart! In all mortal affairs, revise and relisten in rectitude, and reproclaim the crooked words of the world! There is no sword that can ever prevail against righteousness! The power of Sincerity alone saves the world! Taking God as your strength and leaning upon the Anai teachings as your staff, no matter what enemies approach, reconcile them through God's auspicious words, and harm not the foe! God is with you! God carries through His sincerity! Save humanity through Sincerity! Now is the trial of body and soul! By the single disposition of the heart, good instantly turns into evil,

And evil instantly turns into good—the very watershed of good and evil, righteousness and perversity! Under heaven, there are in truth neither friends nor foes! Friends at times become foes, and foes turn into friends! Entrust all human affairs unto God, entirely! Do not err through anxious haste! God dwells together with you! Whether the sun shines or clouds gather, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth sinks or this sacred island burns to ashes, God will surely behold our sincere red hearts and deliver us unto safety! With the sacred spear of singular sincerity, reconcile the approaching enemy, manifest the power of God, and let the divine majesty blaze forth!'

Singing thus, she disappeared into the inner sanctuary.

The army of tens of thousands torched the entire island; fanned by the wind, billowing black smoke enveloped all quarters, and the soldiers stood in tense excitement, holding their breath awaiting the order for battle.

Ōkuniwake folded his arms, sighing in deep anguish and utterly at a loss as to what to do—when at that very moment, his horse's hooves clattering loudly, Takasugiwake came galloping back, vaulted lightly from the saddle, and shouted before Ōkuniwake:

'Lord Ōkuniwake! Why have you made no preparations for defense?! The enemy surrounds this mountain with tens of thousands of horsemen from all four sides, torching the forests and threatening to reduce this mansion to ashes—why do you hesitate?!'

He slapped his knee and roared; but seeing Ōkuniwake fold his arms in utter silence with tears trickling down his cheeks, Takasugiwake spat in bitter contempt:

'Bah! Unbefitting your renowned valor, in this desperate crisis of life and death, to cower before the enemy's might and sink into craven sorrow—what cowardly behavior! Since it has come to this, consulting with you is utterly useless! I shall now lead the mansion's soldiers to fight our final battle and decide victory or defeat in a single stroke!'

Striding fiercely toward the front, Ōkuniwake called out to him:

'Lord Takasugiwake! Your words are entirely reasonable! As the presiding officer of this mansion, I too cannot bear to stand by idly and let the enemy trample us! Nevertheless, the divine purpose of the supreme God of benevolence to save humanity must be weighed with utmost gravity! Pray reflect deeply for a moment!'

'Those words hardly sound like Lord Ōkuniwake! Cowardly excuses! Are you not the chief minister of Miyukihime who guards this island?! If it is occupied by the enemy through such spineless hesitation, how could you ever face Miyukihime?! Hear that—the enemy's battle-cries shaking the very mountains! Since your consent is hopeless, I shall act independently on my own initiative, slaughtering the cloud-like host with our long-trained martial might!'

He rushed impetuously toward the gate.

'Wait, Lord Takasugiwake! Wait but a moment!'

'What?! In such a crisis, can there be a moment's delay?! "The victor is the government army, the defeated are rebels!" Lord Ōkuniwake, I shall show you my valor!'

Hurling these words, he dashed toward the front gate; and just as he was about to order the troops into battle, the deity Tajikarao returning from Miyukigaoka witnessed this scene and spoke:

'Well, well! Things have become extraordinarily amusing! Shall we sit back leisurely, sip some sake, and enjoy the spectacle of the enemy's procession?!'

Takasugiwake: 'Are you not Tajikarao of Mitoshi Village, the self-proclaimed Ushitora-no-Konjin?! In such an hour of mortal peril, why do you act so leisurely and carefree?! In this desperate crisis, prepare for battle!'

'Aha ha ha! Delightful, delightful! Takasugiwake's panic is enough to make a belly laugh! Calm down, calm down, let's watch the enemy's attack as a side-dish while having a drink! Hey, someone bring sake, bring sake! Deadly things like swords, spears, and bows are not to be used in a sacred precinct where God abides! Weapons are instruments of death!'

『大国別殿の言葉とも覚えぬ。卑怯未練な陳弁、貴神は本島を守り給ふ深雪姫の神の宰神ならずや。斯かる卑怯未練の御心掛にて闇々敵に占領されなば、何を以て深雪姫の神に言解けあるか。アレアレ聞かれよ、山岳も轟く許りの敵の叫び声、到底貴神の賛成は覚束なければ、吾れは是より単独にて自由行動に出で、本島に攻め寄せ来たる雲霞の如き大軍を、日頃鍛へし武力を以て鏖殺せむ』

『ヤアヤア高杉別殿、暫く暫くお待ちあれ』

『何ツ、此期に及むで暫時の猶予がならうか、勝てば官軍負くれば賊、大国別殿、拙者が武勇を御目に掛けむ』

と云ひ捨てて表門へと駈出だし、部下の将卒に向つて、戦闘準備を命令せむとする折しも、深雪ケ丘より帰り来れる手力男の神は此体を見て、

『ヤア、大変に面白くなつて来ましたね。一つ敵軍の行列を緩りと、酒でも飲むで見物致しませうか』

と膝を叩いて怒鳴りつけたが、大国別は何の返答もなく腕を組んだままうつむき、涙さえ頬を伝うのを見て取った高杉別は、忌々しそうに吐き捨てた。

『ええい！ 日頃の武勇にも似合わず、一騎当千のこの時に敵の勢力に萎縮し、狼狽のあまり悲嘆に沈むとは、卑怯未練な振る舞いである！ もはやこうなった以上、貴神に相談しても何の益もない！ 私はこれより館の将卒を率い、ここを最後と一戦を交え、勝敗を決してくれよう！』

と言い捨てて表門へと駆け出し、部下の将卒に向かって戦闘準備を命じようとしたその時、深雪ヶ丘から戻ってきた手力男神がこの様子を見て言った。

『やあやあ、大変面白くなってきましたね。一つ敵軍の行列をゆっくりと酒でも飲みながら見物いたしましょうか』

『アハハハ、やあ面白い、面白い！ 高杉別のその狼狽ぶりは、へそが茶を沸かすわい！ アハハハ、まあまあ落ち着いて敵軍の襲撃を肴に一杯やろうじゃないか。おい誰か酒だ、酒だ！ 殺伐たる剣や槍や弓などは、神のお鎮まりになる聖地で用いるものではない。武器は凶器だぞ』

§ 4

Takasugiwake flared into towering fury:

'Your wanton, irresponsible words shall serve as a blood sacrifice for our departure into battle!'

Drawing his blade, he slashed straight at his head. Tajikarao seized a massive gatepost, yanked it out from the ground, hoisted it high overhead, and pinned Takasugiwake down.

Takasugiwake: 'Villain! Pretending to be a loyal ally while conspiring with the enemy to seize this sacred precinct! Even if I am slain and descend unto the Hidden Realm, my loyal spirit shall remain upon the earth to punish your wicked schemes!'

Tajikarao: 'Aha ha ha! Pray don't misunderstand, Lord Takasugiwake!'

So saying, he tossed the gatepost aside. At that instant, leaping like a bird of prey, Takasugiwake lunged:

'Traitorous villain, taste my blade!'

He thrust fiercely at Tajikarao's flank. The moment Tajikarao nimbly dodged aside, Takasugiwake missed his mark, stumbled forward seven or eight paces propelled by his own momentum, and fell flat upon the ground with his drawn sword.

Dense black smoke enveloped the mansion ever more thickly; fanned by the wind, the crackling roar of the mountain forests burning and the tumultuous clamor of the advancing army drew nearer and louder by the second.
