Chapter 7: Awakening [503]

第7章　覚醒〔503〕

§ 1

Leading Hatsu-ko in their train, the four divine missionaries left the city of Iho behind; invoking the seventy-five Kotodama sounds, they crossed the Isthmus of Suez, carrying the divine teachings unto the realms across the four quarters, unto all mortal folk, arriving at last in the vicinity of the First Waterfall along the Shirase River. The cries of birds were silenced, and the trees stirred not; a dread stillness settled over all, and pitch-black darkness crept across the earth.

§ 3

Nodding in agreement, the company sat down beneath the dark canopy of the trees, spreading straw raincoats to lie down. Exhausted by the arduous trek of the day, the four missionaries soon fell into deep slumber, snoring like rumbling thunder. Yet lest wild beasts or evil phantoms attack during the night, Hatsu-ko volunteered to stand vigilant guard alone.
