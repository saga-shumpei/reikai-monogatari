Chapter 24: The Virtue of Kotodama [520]

言霊の徳〔520〕

§ 1

The deity Tajikarao appeared at the main gate, calmly and composedly awaiting the arrival of the hostile forces.

Amenohohi no Mikoto led a vast host; the soldiers held torches in their hands, torching and destroying everything on all sides as they advanced. Behind them marched a company of troops brandishing bloody blades—the spectacle looked just like the infernal realm itself.

Appearing before the gate, Amenohohi no Mikoto shouted arrogantly toward Tajikarao from horseback:

Amenohohi: 'Hark! What deity are you?! A crooked spirit subservient to the rebellious, wicked Hayasusanō?! Bearing the divine decree of the Pillar Deity of Mount Tenkyō, I have marched forth to subjugate you all! Already this island has been virtually burned to the ground, and most of your officers and soldiers have perished as rust upon our blades—you have no strength left to resist! Open this gate honorably and surrender!'

Remaining mounted upon his steed, he blustered haughtily. Tajikarao beamed with a genial smile, flung wide the double gates to left and right, and spoke:

'Come, come! The gates have been opened wide just as you see! Pray enter freely, I beseech you! Your vast army of soldiers must surely be utterly exhausted! To set fire to an island of this magnitude and burn it down must have been no ordinary labor! Thanks to you, the ferocious beasts and poisonous vipers ravaging this island have been virtually wiped out! You must be hungry, you must be thirsty! Here we have prepared vast quantities of rice balls and sake! Even if tens of thousands of guests partake, we shall not fall short! Pray come in and dine at your leisure! To look so terrifying with bristling shoulders and tensed frames will only give you stiff shoulders! We are those who uphold the divine will of Kamu-Susanō-no-Ōkami, rectifying and reproclaiming all through the Kotodama of good and beautiful words; never, ever would we put poison in sake or rice! Pray partake at your ease!'

Amenohohi: 'Villain! Having exhausted all stratagems, you scheme to annihilate us with poison! I shall not fall for such a trick!'

'Dear me, what a dreadful misunderstanding! In that case, Tajikarao shall taste them before your eyes!'

So saying, he dipped a ladle into a sake cask, gulped down two or three cups, and promptly devoured five or six rice balls on the spot.

'Very well, we shall rest for a while! In the meantime, let those within the mansion prepare to surrender the fortress!'

'Come now, there is no need to speak so harshly! Mortals born alike of the divine fire and water of Heaven and Earth—by the disposition of the single heart, there are neither friends nor foes! We who are born of God's sacred water and fire share all joys and sorrows of the world in common destiny!'

'In this desperate crisis, you spout such carefree trivialities! Some deep secret plot must surely be hidden behind this! Amenohohi is not so easily deceived by such talk!'

'If Tajikarao has any secret, it is solely the secret of the Kotodama—ruling this world in universal peace without sickness or strife, keeping Heaven and Earth pure and the realm tranquil through the virtue of the seventy-five sacred sounds and auspicious words of good and beautiful speech!'

At that moment Takasugiwake stepped forward:

'Hark, Lord Tajikarao! I have just entered the inner sanctuary before Lady Miyukihime to inquire of her divine will—and the admonition of the Mizu-no-Mitama was indeed to reconcile raging deities through Kotodama! Truly, Takasugiwake is struck with admiration at your conduct! Lord Ōkuniwake shares your view entirely!'

'Indeed, so it must be! O Lord Amenohohi, though this mansion is outwardly adorned with weapons and maintains many valiant warriors, as you see, confronting your raging assault army, our swords remain sheathed and our bows stored in their bags! To delight the foe without shedding blood and treat the enemy as friends is the will of the merciful God! Fathoming the true sincerity of the Great Deity, we pray you report these matters in detail unto Amaterasu-Ōmikami!'

'Your conduct is so contrary to our expectations that our raging fury seems to have vanished into thin air! Hark, soldiers and officers, it is the command of Amenohohi! Cast off your armor, lay down your weapons, assemble here, and take your rest!'

At these words, the battle-ready soldiers disarmed and gathered joyfully, drinking sake, feasting upon rice balls, and dancing in exuberant delight—transforming this asura battlefield instantly into a paradise on earth!

At that moment, guided by Ōkuniwake, Lady Miyukihime appeared in the spacious courtyard where the soldiers were joyfully mingling and drinking, and began to sing with a refreshing, clear voice:

'By the decree of the Mizu-no-Mitama revealed upon Mount Caucasus, far from the sacred mountain unto this divine island of Sardinia, Lady Miyukihime appeared to mourn the state of the world! To quell the rage of myriad crooked spirits, I breathed forth the sacred breath of Kotodama; yet as the utterly clouded spirits would by no means yield, I forged and prepared many ten-span swords to protect the realm! God's heart was by no means to rule the world vainly by the sword or subjugate crooked spirits through bows and arrows! Forging sacred swords to solidify the spirit of the heart and inaugurating the divine work to open the Divine Age,'

'The Central Pillar Deity Amaterasu-Ōmikami revealed upon Mount Tenkyō deemed it ever more suspicious, deeply disdaining it and dispatching Lord Amenohohi to assault this place—a clear proof that She doubted our sincere hearts deeper than the Inland Sea where white waves surge! Upon this singular island of pure Sincerity, where all matters under heaven are rectified, relistened, and reproclaimed through the seventy-five sounds of Kotodama! Purified at the Heavenly Well of Amenomanai, born from the mist of the divine breath chewed and blown forth, I am the goddess Tagirihime no Kami! Serenely and peacefully return unto heaven, and before the Great Deity, declare the sincere red heart of Kamu-Susanō-no-Ōkami without delay, O Lord Amenohohi!

Whether the sun shines or clouds gather, whether the moon waxes or wanes, could there ever exist a defiled heart against our Sovereign?! Let the Heavenly Deities behold, let the Earthly Deities know! Illuminated by the single flame of the Rising Sun Deity shining in the sky, report God's pure sincerity in detail unto Takamanohara, O Lord Amenohohi! Let the Deity who separates good from evil manifest visibly upon the surface, throwing wide open the dark rock door of suspicious mortal hearts!

In all human affairs, revise, relisten, and reproclaim in divine rectitude, O Heavenly Deities! Before Amaterasu-Ōmikami shining pure in the heavens, reverently and awe-inspiringly do I offer: the valiant ten-span sword is the pure sword of the Maiden Deity's divine word, and I dedicate this radiant spirit, the Mizu-no-Mitama, before the Great Deity!'

When her song ended, Lord Amenohohi was utterly disarmed by the astonishing scene; overcome with remorse, that stalwart general wept profusely.

Suddenly the heavens roared, and a colossal blazing light descended, swirling with a rushing sound around and above both armies. The deities looked up in unison, gazing in awe at this spectacle. The blazing light instantly transformed into a radiant male deity, standing poised in mid-air and gazing down upon the assembled host.

This deity was the Rising Sun Deity, noble offspring of Izanagi no Mikoto, descending with divine decree from Mount Tenkyō to testify unto good and evil, truth and falsehood.

Presently he dissolved into white smoke and vanished into the sky; exquisite music resounded, gradually fading into the distance. Through the descent of Amenohohi, the pure sincerity of Susanō no Mikoto was fully illuminated, and he immediately prepared to report this truth unto Takamanohara.

忽ち白煙となつて中空に消え玉ひ、後は嚠喨たる音楽聞え、次第々々に音楽の音も遠くなり行きぬ。いよいよ菩比命の降臨によつて、須佐之男命の麗しき御心判明し、命は直に高天原に此由を復命さるる事とはなりける。

天教山にお現れになった天照大御神はいよいよ怪しいとお思いになり、深く嫌悪されて菩比命に命じ、ここに攻め寄せ給うたのは、我らの心を白波の立つ瀬戸の海よりも深く疑われた証拠である。七十五声の言霊によって世のすべての事柄を直日に見直し聞き直し、言向け和して宣り直す誠一つのこの一つ島よ。天の真名井において濯ぎ清め、噛み砕いて吹き捨てられた息吹の狭霧より生まれた我は、多紀理毘売神である。心平らかに安らかに、神須佐之男大神の、

赤き真心をことごとく具さに天へ帰り、大神の御前において速やかに宣り伝え給え、菩比の神よ。朝日は照ろうとも曇ろうとも、月は満ちようとも欠けようとも、大御神に対して邪悪で穢れた心などあろうはずがない。天津御神もご覧あれ、国津御神も知り給え。大空に輝く朝日の出ずる日の出神の一つの火に照らし、神の真心を高天原に詳しく宣り伝え給え、菩比の神よ。善と悪とを峻別する神が表にお現れになり、疑い深き人々の心の闇の岩戸をまっすぐに開かせ給え。

ただ人の世の何事も直日に見直し聞き直し、宣り直し給え、天津神よ。大空も清らかに照り渡る天照大御神の御前に、謹み敬い畏みて、勇猛に現れ出でた十握の剣は、姫神の神言の清き剣であり、光り輝く神の御霊、瑞の御霊を大御神の御前に捧げ奉る』

と歌い終えると、菩比命は思いがけないこの場の光景にすっかり力を抜き、懺悔の念に堪え兼ねて、あれほど勇猛であった武将もただ涙に暮れるばかりであった。

たちまち天空が轟き渡り、この場に舞い降りた巨大な火の光が、両軍の頭上を前後左右に音を立てて旋回し始めた。神々は一斉に天を仰ぎ、この光景を見つめた。火の光はたちまち美しい男神の姿へと変わり、中空に佇んで一同を見下ろされた。

この神は伊邪那岐命の尊き御子である日の出神であった。正邪善悪の証しのために、天教山より神勅を奉じて降臨されたのである。

神はたちまち白煙となって中空に消え去り、後には高らかな音楽が聞こえ、次第に遠ざかっていった。天菩比命の降臨によって須佐之男命の清らかな真心が判明し、命は直ちに高天原へと復命されることとなったのである。
