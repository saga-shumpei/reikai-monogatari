Chapter 13: 黒 Long 姫 [1349]

黒長姫〔1349〕

§ 1

Four or five men were sitting in a circle at the summit of Amabikitauge, warming themselves while burning a faint blue fire. Each had the appearance of a partisan, with shaggy beards, and they were tossing things that looked like human arms into the fire, gnawing on them with a motion like playing a flute.

At this moment, Bunsuke's eyes became quite introspective and clear. Bunsuke thought, '...There is a loathsome fellow here; this is another trial. However, one who has already died can hardly have his life taken again. Well, there is no choice but to leave it to Kamunagara...' and having steeled his resolve, he approached while singing a Sendenka in a faint voice. One of them spotted Bunsuke with a sharp eye and said,

Kō: 'Hey traveler, I would like you to wait a moment.'

Bunsuke, deciding to act the tough guy, feigned composure and replied,

Bunsuke: 'Even if you did not ask me to wait, I would like to pass through here, as it has become quite cold. And you lot appear to be in the thieving trade; do you make a bit of profit?'

Kō: 'Well, the wind of recession has blown all the way to Yachimatakaidau, so things have been hopeless lately. From my perspective, you are quite a great fellow. You skillfully wear the mask of virtue, pose as an intermediary for the Lord God, draw pine trees, black snakes, and turnips on the edges of scrap paper, and are a master at taking money without any effort while being thanked. I would like you to teach us as well. We have been consulting since earlier about opening a seminar for thieves here, but since there is no suitable teacher, we are actually quite troubled. Researching how to steal in a way that is welcomed without violating the law is the wisest way of life, so would you not become our teacher, the instructor of us from Kobokuyama?'

Bunsuke: 'Do not say such foolish things; I was receiving legitimate compensation for legitimate reasons. Because you lot have the nature of thieves, you interpret everything in the world as thievery. How could a thief-teacher understand the state of mind of a propagandist who is a Puritan? If you want to be taught such things, Ohara-san, who was influential in the manifest world, will likely come along soon. When he does, show him full respect and approach him with reverence. He was a hero who commanded a great number of thieves even in the manifest world. My field is different, so I must decline this; it would be a lifelong disadvantage if it hindered my journey to heaven.'

Kō: 'You are selfish after all. It is no good if you are still attached to self-love and worldly love even after coming to the spirit world. Why not stop saying such things and manfully teach us your secrets?'

Bunsuke: 'Because you lot do not understand the spirit of a Puritan, it looks like thievery to you, but receiving what people offer with joy is, in short, like receiving it from the Lord God. Since I am receiving the treasures of the Lord God indirectly, I am not a thief. You lot are petty thieves who try to snatch from passersby and take things by force. Better yet, why not repent right here and accompany me? I will surely take you to heaven.'

Otsu: 'Hey Kōshū, it is useless to deal with such a farting old man. In any thieving organization, someone as weak and spineless as this cannot provide control even if we crown him as our leader; we need a great arch-thief like Ohara-sama to have the power of control.'

Bunsuke: 'That is right, that is right, my field is different, so I am no good. I am a man who has been like a fart from the beginning, which is why I am called Heibei or Bunsuke.'

Kō: 'What a hopeless blind man you are. In that case, I will excuse you, so you had better leave this place quickly. However, this checkpoint is called the "Double Surprise of Amabikitauge," so you cannot pass without being surprised once. You always said in the manifest world that the lid of the surprise box would open, did you not? Since this is the realization of that, I am about to put it into action, so you may be surprised as you please; you possess the right to anguish, suffering, and astonishment in a Kamunagara manner, so it is your specialty. Ihihihihi.'

原文（近代文語）

天引峠の頂上に四五人の男車座となつて、青い火をチヨロチヨロ焚きながら、暖を取つてゐる。何れもパルチザンのやうな面構、髯をモシヤモシヤと生やし、何だか人の腕のやうな物を、火の中へくべては、横笛を吹くやうな調子で口に当ててしがんでゐる。此時文助の目は余程内分的になつて、明かになつて来た。文助は……厭な奴が居やがる、此奴ア又一つ悶錯だワイ。併しながら一度死んだ者が命を取られるやうなこともあるまい。エエ惟神に任すより仕方がない……と決心の臍を固め、幽かな声で宣伝歌を歌ひながら近よつて行く。其中の一人は目ざとく文助を見て、

甲『オイ旅人、一寸待つて貰はうかい』

文助は悪胴をきめて、ワザと平気を装ひ、

『待つて貰はうと言はいでも、一あたりさして貰ひたいのだ、大分寒うなつたからな。そしてお前等は泥棒商売と見えるが、チツと儲かりますかな』

『ヤアもう不景気風が八衢街道まで吹きまくつて来たものだから、一向此頃は駄目だ。お前は俺から見れば随分偉い奴だ。ウマく善の仮面を被つて、神様のお取次と化け込み、鼻紙の端に松の木や黒蛇、蕪大根を描きよつて、苦労なしに礼言はして金をとる剛の者だから、一つ俺達にも教へて貰ひたいものだ。ここで泥棒講習会を開かうかと云つて、最前から相談して居つたのだが、根つから適当な先生がないので、実の所は当惑してゐる所だ。うまく法律にふれない様に、喜ばれて泥棒する方法を研究するのが、最も賢明な処世法だから、一つ小北山の先生、吾々の教導者になつて下さるまいかなア』

『馬鹿なことを言ふな、俺は正当の理由に仍つて正当の報酬を頂いて居つたのだ。貴様等は泥棒根性があるから、世界一切の事が皆泥棒的に解釈が出来るのだ。ピユリタンとしてのプロパガンディストの心事が泥棒先生に分るものかい。こんなことが教へて欲しければ、やがて現界に羽振を利かして居つた、大原さんがやつて来るだらう。そしたら十分に敬礼を表し、敬して近付けるのだ。現界に於ても、大多数盗を擁してゐた豪傑だからのう。俺は畑が違ふから、こればかりは御免だ、天国行の邪魔になると、一生の不利益だからのう』

『ヤツパリお前は利己主義だな。幽界へ来ても自愛と世間愛に執着してゐるから駄目だよ。そんなこと言はずに、男らしく秘密を教へて呉れたらどうだい』

『お前達はピユリタンの精神が分らないから泥棒に見えるのだが、人が喜んで献つたものを戴くのは、つまり神様から下さる様なものだ。神の宝を間接拝受するのだから、盗人ではないよ。お前達は往来の人を掠めて無理往生に取らうとする小盗人だよ。一層のこと、今此処で改心をして俺のお供をしたら何うだい、キツと天国へつれて行つてやるがなア』

乙『オイ甲州、こんな屁古垂爺を相手にしても駄目だぞ。すべて泥棒団体といふものは、こんなヒヨロヒヨロなレストレントの力のないやうな者では、頭に戴いた所で、統一が出来ない、ヤツパリ大原さまのやうな、大悪盗でないと、コントロールの力がないからな』

文助『さうださうだ、畑が違ふのだから、私には駄目だ。元から屁こいたやうな男だから、平兵衛ともいひ文助ともいふのだから』

甲『何と四方のない盲だなア。それなら免除してやるから、キリキリと此の場を立つたがよからうぞ。併し此関所は天引峠の二度ビツクリといふのだから、一つ吃驚せなくちや通過は出来ない。ビツクリ箱の蓋があくぞよと、いつも現界で云うて居つただらう。それの実現だから、これから一つ実行にかかるよつて、自由自在に吃驚するがよからう、煩悶苦悩驚愕の権利は、お前が惟神的に保有してるのだから、お手のものだ。イヒヒヒヒ』

§ 2

Bunsuke: 'I am Bunsuke, whose Yamatodamashihi is of the purest Suishiyōmitama and who cannot be shaken. Try to surprise me as much as you like. I am an angel endowed with the Shinriki to sweep away any Akuma, any Kiyōfu, and any Shiuji in an instant, by the Meuhafu of Kamunagara, and by what is called sublimation. Come, surprise me, surprise me!'

Speaker: 'You are such a blind, reckless Bakamono with such an overbearing Iki that you are beyond talking to. You are the one who will end up surprised. Now, get through here quickly.'

Bunsuke: 'Even if you do not tell me to pass, I shall pass by the Kenri of Jiiu. It is said that the Tauri does not speak, yet it creates a path of its own; the way is already prepared. Hmph, no need for your concern, I shall excuse myself and go ahead. Even though I look like this, I have received a Jiudai message from Kamisama, so I will not permit the interference of Dorobau like you. Et-he-he-he!'

He wrinkles his narrow eyes, laughs, and goes down the mountain pass, tapping his staff with a clatter. After Bunsuke has descended about four or five chō, he finds a roof that is little more than a frame, where stone statues of the six Jizō are lined up. He stops abruptly, sits on a half-rotted saddle eaten by insects nearby, and when he looks closely, he sees scribbles written in thick black ink on the weathered pillars. What catches his eye without him intending to read it is... 'The blind missionary Bunsuke will soon pass through here; if so, there is a matter to discuss. The clan of the black snake, gather here.'

Bunsuke looks at this and mutters to himself, 'Haha, thanks to me painting black snakes from morning till night, calling them Ryūjin-sama, handing them to believers, and having them enshrined in hanging scrolls and frames, I have been provided with excellent food and drink. The black snake fellows must think my passing is a great virtue and are planning to hold a welcome party. Well, that is to be expected. Even though no one serves them, they are being enshrined as if they were Ōkami-sama despite being mere insects, so it is no wonder they are happy. Ah, one must help even the birds and beasts, I suppose. Ah, Kamunagara Reikō Hōbai-se, Kamunagara Reikō Hōbai-se. Because Matsuhiko-sama of the San-Go-Kyō came and complained about this and that, I stopped painting black snakes and have only been painting the Sun-sama on pines, but if I had continued from then, I would surely have been appreciated even more... No matter how much I paint the Sun-sama, it is not as if the Sun-sama would be pleased, so I should have kept painting the Ryūjin-sama that suited my nature. If one does things that are not in accordance with one's spirit, it ends up amounting to nothing.'

Just then, three beautiful women of a marriageable age suddenly appear.

Kurochōhime: 'Excuse me, are you not Bunsuke-sama? I am called Kurochōhime; you have made us suffer quite a lot. From morning till night, you have made us wrap ourselves around pine trees, leaving us unable to move; you are a cruel person. Now, we shall offer you our thanks.'

Bunsuke: 'You appear to be the spirits of the Black Ryūjin, but I had you enshrined so magnificently, so what is it that you lack? Being mere beasts, you have been treated as Kamisama; instead of being grateful, I have no ears to listen to your complaints in a place like this.'

Kurochōhime: 'We have fallen into the path of beasts, so it is in accordance with our spirit; to be raised to such a seat of Kamisama and enshrined makes our eyes dizzy and our heads ache, and it is unbearable. That is why our resentment has gathered, and it has caused your eyes to lose their sight. It is truly a nuisance to be treated beyond our station. Because of you, how many thousands of our kin have suffered? And you, claiming that if one enshrines these, evil and disaster will be avoided, have you not been imitating Kamisama? You are the very person who gave our kin such preposterous names as Ryūjin-sama, spread rumors that they were gods with great Shinriki, made the fools of the world worship them, and caused them to act like charlatans. Everyone says that being enshrined by the side of Kamisama was so painful they could not stand it.'

原文（近代文語）

『大和魂の生粋の水晶魂のビクとも致さぬ文助だ。幾らなりと吃驚さして御覧。如何なる悪魔も、恐怖も、醜事も、忽ち惟神の妙法に仍つて、所謂ザブリメーシヨンに仍つて一掃する神力が備はつてゐるエンゼル様だ。サア、吃驚さしたり吃驚さしたり』

『余り向ふ意気の強い盲滅法界の馬鹿者だから、話にならぬワイ。こつちが吃驚して了ふワイ。サア、キリキリ此処を通れ』

『貴様が通れと云はなくても、自由の権利で通るのだ。桃季物言はず自ら小径をなすというて、チヤンと道がついてるのだ。ヘンお構ひ御無用、お先へ失礼致します。此文助は斯う見えても、神様から、重大なるメツセージを受けてゐるのだから、汝等如き泥棒の容喙は許さないのだ。エツヘツヘヘヘ』

と細い目に皺をよせ、笑ひながらコツリコツリと杖を突いて峠を下つて行く。文助は四五町ばかり降つて行くと、其処に形ばかりの屋根があつて、石の六地蔵が並んでゐる。ツと立寄つて、傍の虫の喰ひさがした足の半腐つた鞍掛に腰を打かけ、よくよく見れば古ぼけた柱に墨黒々と楽書がやつてある。見るともなしに目についたのは……盲の宣伝使文助がやがてここを通過するだらう、さうすれば一つ談判がある。黒蛇の一族は此処へ集まれ……と記されてあつた。文助は之を見て独言、

『ハハア、おれが朝から晩まで、竜人さまだと云つて、黒蛇を書いては信者に渡し、掛字や額に仕立てて祭らしてやつたお蔭で、結構な飲食を供へて貰ひ、黒蛇の奴、俺の行方を大に徳となし、歓迎会でも開きよる積だなア。そらさうだらう。誰一人お給仕をしてくれる者がないのに、虫の分際として大神さま格に祀つて貰ふのだから、喜ぶのも無理はないワイ。あああ人はヤツパリ禽獣に至る迄助けておかねばならぬものかいな。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世惟神霊幸倍坐世。三五教の松彦さまがやつて来てゴテゴテ言ふものだから、黒蛇の画かきも中止して了ひ、松に日輪様ばかりを描いて居つたが、あれから引続いてやつてゐたなら、まだまだ沢山に喜ばれただらうに……何程日輪様を描いた所で、日輪様が喜んで下さる筈もなし、ヤツパリ性に合うた竜神さまを描いてをつたがよかつたのだ。霊不相応なことをすると、却て何にもなりやしないワ』

斯かる所へ妙齢の美人が三人連れで忽焉と現はれて来た。

『モシ、貴方は文助さまぢやありませぬか、私は黒長姫と申します、随分苦しめて下さいましたね。朝から晩迄、松の木にまき付いたなりで、身動きも出来ぬやうな目に遇はし、殺生なお方ですワ。サア是から御礼を申しませう』

『お前は黒竜神の精霊と見えるが、あれだけ立派に祀らして上げたのに、何が不足なのだ。畜生の分際として、神様として貰つて、喜ぶことを措いて、こんな処で不足を聞く耳は持ちませぬワイ』

『吾々は畜生道に堕ちたもの、霊相応ですから、さやうな神様の席へ上げられ祀られては、目が眩み、頭が痛み、苦しくてなりませぬ。それだから吾々の怨みが塊まつて、お前さまの目が見えなくなつたのだ。分に過ぎた待遇をせられては本当に迷惑だ。お前さまのお蔭で、私達の眷族が幾千人苦しんだか知れやしない。そしてお前さまは之を祀つておけば、悪事災難が逃れるとか云つて、神様の真似をしたでないか。吾々の眷族を竜神さまだなどと大それた名をつけ、そして大変に神力のある神のやうに言ひふらし、世界の亡者に拝ませて、栃麺棒をふらさした張本人だ。神様の側に祀られて苦しくてたまらなかつたと、皆が云つてゐる』

§ 3

Speaker: 'I did not draw it because I wanted to hear such complaints. I did it with good intentions, thinking that I would raise even one spirit that had fallen into the world back up to the world. Are you not going to acknowledge that spirit of mine? It is quite troublesome, is it not?'

Speaker: 'How you do talk. Where in the world does a human being have the power to raise those who have fallen into the world? That all lies within the Gokengen of the Kamisama. You, who attempt to usurp the Shintoku of the Kamisama, are a thief of Ten. That is why you have come to have to pass through this Amabikitauge of Nido-Bitsukuri. Oh, how hateful. I shall now crush your Gotai to pieces to satisfy my grudge, so prepare yourself for that. And your Karada shall become a Kurokuchinaha, join our Nakama, and work as a slave. Since a part of your Mitama has completely Hiyōi into that Kurokuchinaha you drew, it is only Atarimahe by the Dauri of Shizen that your Reishin should become a snake. You say with the tip of your tongue that it is for the sake of the Kamisama and for the sake of Yobito, but having us draw the figures of our Nakama and worship them was nothing but an ambition to achieve what is Ihayuru Selbst-Zweck.'

Speaker: 'Do not talk nonsense. How could one who serves the path of the Kami have such a heart? Everything I did was out of a sincere heart, following the great will of the Kami, wishing to save even the smallest insects. No matter how great a serpent you may be, there is a limit to how much you can slander a snake. I would like you to interpret my actions with a little more Zeni.'

Speaker: 'No matter what you say, it is clear that you were doing it for self-preservation. You are a hypocrite who treats the Kami like dried bonito. Why do you not humble yourself and refuse even when asked by others? In the Fudesaki of the Izunomitama, is it not decided who is to write the Goshingo and the Kamigata? If one is written by a person who is destined to do so, the spirit of the Kamisama is contained within it, and even a snake can attain liberation, but when drawn by one without authority, it only increases the suffering and adds to the sins.'

Speaker: 'Even if you say that, I am also a person who is registered in the organization of heavenly beings in heaven. Is it possible for a snake or the like to be drawn by the honorable hands of a Henshō-Danshi? You should be satisfied with my painting; do not become so arrogant.'

Speaker: 'Ho ho ho ho, think carefully about which one of us is being arrogant. That is why the Kamisama calls you blind and deaf. The moment I blow this whistle, the kenzoku who have been tormented by you will come here, so prepare yourself.'

No sooner had the speaker said this than they blew a whistle, a shrill sound. Suddenly, the grass, the fragments of trees, and the leaves all around turned into pitch-black snakes, each growing a single horn, and like waves crashing in, they attacked Bun-suke from all sides with their mouths wide open. Bun-suke swung his staff wildly, let out a voice like that of a dying man, trampled over the swarm of snakes, and fled toward the northwest, barely escaping with his life. Instantly, a fierce mountain wind arose, and the numerous snakes soared high into the mid-air, dyeing the sky pitch-black, and scattered to a point several hundred ken ahead of where Bun-suke was running. Bun-suke, beside himself, stumbled and fell as he advanced, all the while reciting the Kamigoto.

原文（近代文語）

『そんな不足を聞かうと思うて描いたのぢやない。一人でも世に堕ちた霊を世に上げてやらうと思つて善意を以てしたのだ。チツと其精神も買つて貰はなくちや困るぢやないか』

『よう仰有いますワイ。世に堕ちた者を世にあげる様な力が、人間の分際としてどこにありますか。それは皆神様の御権限にあるのだ。神様の神徳を横領せむとするお前さまは天の賊だよ。それだから、こんな天引峠の二度吃驚を通らなくちやならぬ様になつたのだ。エエ恨めしい。これから五体をグタグタに咬み砕いて恨を晴らすから、其積りでゐなさい。そしてお前の身体は黒蛇となり、私達の仲間に入り、奴となつて働くのだ。あのお前の描いた黒蛇には、スツカリお前の霊魂の一部が憑依してゐるから、自然の道理でお前の霊身は蛇となるのは当然だ。お前は口の先で、神様の為世人の為と云つてゐるが、私達の仲間の姿をかいて祀らすのは、所謂ゼルブスト・ツエツクを達せむとする野心に外ならなかつたのだ』

『馬鹿を云ふな。神の道に仕へる者が、どうしてそんな心になれるか、何れも神の大御心に倣うて、虫ケラまで助けようと云ふ真心からやつたのだ。何程大蛇のお前だとて蛇推するにも程がある。チツとは善意に解して貰ひたいものだな』

『何と云つても、セルフ・プリサベーシヨンの為にしてゐたことは、瞭然たるものだ。お前は神を松魚節にする偽善者だ。なぜ自分は謙遜して、人に頼まれても断りを云はないのだ。厳の御霊の筆先には、御神号や神姿は書く人がきまつてるぢやないか。きまつた方に書いて貰ふのなれば、所謂神様の霊がこもつてゐるから、蛇だつて解脱することが出来るが、権威なき者に描かれては益々苦しみを増し、罪を重ぬるのみだよ』

『それだと云つて、俺もヤツパリ天国の天人団体に籍をおいてる者だ。蛇なんぞを勿体ない、変性男子の御手で描いて貰ふといふことがあるか。俺の絵で満足すべきものだ、余り増長するない』

『ホホホホホ、どちらが増長してゐるのか、よく考へてみなさい。それだから盲聾と神様が仰有るのだ。今に口笛を吹いたが最後、お前に苦しめられた眷属が此処へやつて来るから覚悟をなさい』

と云ふより早く、ピユーピユーと口笛をふいた。俄に四辺の草も木の片も木の葉も真黒けの蛇となり、一本の角を生やし、波の打ち寄する如く、文助の四辺を力一杯口をあけて襲撃して来た。文助は杖を打振り打振り、キヤアキヤアと断末魔のやうな声を出し、蛇の群を踏み越えて、命カラガラ西北さして逃げて行く。忽ち強烈なる山颪となり、数多の蛇は中空高く舞ひ上り、空を真黒に染めて、文助の走つて行く数百間の前まで飛散してゐる。文助は心も心ならず、神言を奏上しながら、倒けつ転びつ進み行く。
