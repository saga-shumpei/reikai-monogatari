Chapter 7: Jewel 返志 [1343]

玉返志〔1343〕

§ 1

At the reception of Kogitayama, Bunsukejii-sama had his head severely cracked by the two, Hatsu and Toku, and subsequently fell ill with a fever, lying in bed and occasionally muttering delirious words, which caused great distress to the many believers and officials. Furthermore, Magahiko was absent, and the two, Hatsu and Toku, had fled, causing the operational organization to suddenly face the calamity of near-total suspension. Okiku, being a spirited woman, took on the dual roles of receptionist and shrine attendant, performing prayers for the sick who came to worship, or having them listen to sermons, and appearing at the reception desk, she was extremely busy to the point of dizziness. Okiku felt overwhelmed, her body feeling as if it were made of rope, and she began to grumble a little.

Okiku: 'Ah, the role of the receptionist is nothing much; I always thought Bunsuke-sama was just drawing pictures for fun. I had assumed he was doing it out of boredom with nothing else to do, but when I try doing it myself, it is quite busy. I must perform Chinkon, and I must also offer prayers; truly, truly, it was hard work for Bunsuke-sama too. If only he would recover quickly; even though beautiful flowers are blooming everywhere like this, I cannot even go flower picking. On top of that, because Hatsuwakahime-sama has arrived, Matsuhime-sama is too busy to help me even a little, and Ochiyo-sama seems busy with the shrine's chores and such, so I have truly become fed up. If only Maga-sama would return at least; what a thoughtless fellow he is. Mankō-sama is also Mankō-sama; I wonder where on earth he is wandering. If he would just return, I would tease him to pass the time with a look as if he were love-struck. Ah, it cannot be helped; no matter how much I exert myself, the old women keep coming one after another, and Okiku-sama cannot bear it. Perhaps I should take a nap here for a while. With the days being so long and the nights so short, I am sleepy beyond measure. Even the camellia flowers are dozing off, dropping their heads one by one, startling the carp in the reservoir and dyeing the water surface deep red. Since I am a living being too, I suppose I cannot help but take a little rest.'

Saying this to herself, she fell fast asleep. Just then, an old man of about sixty came trudging along, carrying a girl of twelve or thirteen on his back.

Old Man: 'Yes, excuse me, I am a Shuda from the nearby neighborhood, but my granddaughter has gotten a sea bream bone or something stuck in her throat, and she is writhing in agony, on the verge of losing her breath. Would it be possible for you to remove it through the divine virtue of the gods?'

Okiku, who had been dozing, suddenly opened her eyes.

Okiku: 'Uniya, uniya, uniya, uniya, welcome. It is a very long day, is it not? What time is it now?'

Old Man: 'It is still the hour of the horse. I have a matter I wish to request urgently, so I have come hoping for your assistance. This is my granddaughter, but something is stuck in her throat, and I am troubled; would it be possible to have you perform what is called Chinkon?'

Okiku: 'Hey, very well, but I must ask for your address and name.'

Old Man: 'Yes, I shall tell you my address and name later. As you can see, my granddaughter is in a critical situation, so I would like you to perform the prayer quickly.'

Okiku: 'In that case, I shall make an exception, perform the procedure later, and make the request first. However, if I do not hear the child's name, I cannot make the request.'

Old Man: 'That is quite reasonable. The girl's name is Takino.'

Okiku: 'Yes, very well. Now, bring her here. If we pray to the Ōkami, He will save her immediately. Now, Ojii-sama, please come up.'

Old Man: 'I am terribly sorry to ask, but as you can see, I am wearing straw sandals, so my feet are dirty. Please, I beg you to bring only the girl up.'

§ 2

She took it down from her back. The girl rolled as if tumbling and went before the Saidan where Okiku was praying. Okiku wore purple hakama, and over her white Kimono, she put on a Kakui, held a Chiukei, and reverently performed the Sōjiyō of the Amatsunorito, concentrating on her Kiguwan. Despite Okiku’s Itsushiyōkenmei Kiguwan, shedding sweat like Netsutau, the effect did not easily manifest, and the girl only suffered and writhed more and more. When Ochiyo came down the Kaidan to the Uketsuke during a break in her duties, she saw a suspicious old man crouching in the corner of the garden with a pale face. Looking at the Shinden, she saw Okiku concentrating on her Itsushiyōkenmei Kiguwan. Seeing this, Ochiyo said,

Ochiyo: 'The Uketsuke is completely Akiya. Well, I shall serve as a Dairi for a while.'

Saying this, she sat down at the Uketsuke with a thud. Then, two men came up the Sakamichi, panting with a wheezing breath. These were the two, Iku and Saru.

Iku: 'Gomen-nasai, I am Iku from the forest of the shrine, and this is Saru. Has Hatsuwakahime-sama by any chance stopped by with a dog called Smart?'

Ochiyo: 'You have come at a good time. Please, have a Goitsupuku. She has been here since Yazen, but she seems to be having a lively Ohanashi with her mother. I will let you know when I am free, so please go and visit the shrines in this Keidai once.'

Iku: 'Yes, thank you. In any case, let us go and pay our respects. Hey, Saru, she seems to be only twelve or thirteen, but she is quite composed. Since a small child like this can manage the Uketsuke at Kokitayama, it is quite something. It is a great difference from those teachers who act important, show their faces at the Uketsuke only to drink sake, use up paper claiming it is Fudesaki, and just wait for the sun to set.'

Saru: 'Truly, it is admirable. Hey, Uketsuke-sama, what is your name?'

Ochiyo: 'Why do you ask my name? You have no particular business with it, do you?'

Saru: 'Oh, I am humbled; then I shall ask no more.'

Iku: 'Hahaha, Saru, you were finally done in. Know your shame.'

Saru: 'What are you saying, you? Did you not have the essential crystal ball taken by a dog? You thought it was a dog, but you were deceived by a tanuki, fed a fool's errand, and on top of that, you were showered with insults and abuse; you have disgraced yourself, so do not act so important.'

Iku: 'That is mutual. Is there anyone who comes to a place like this and says such foolish things?'

Ochiyo: 'My, you people have faces that look like the air has leaked out of them from somewhere. I heard you say just now that you had your ball taken; you are truly people like those who drop a marble, ho ho ho.'

Iku: 'Oh, this is embarrassing, I am being struck on the mask, the gauntlet, and the torso. Oh, how scary, how scary. Come, Saru, let us go.'

Saru: 'Hey, wait a moment. This old man is suspicious, is he not? He is trembling violently when he sees our faces.'

Iku: 'Truly, he is a strange old man. Hey, old man, where on earth did you come from?'

Old man: 'Please, do not say such things. My grandchild has caught a terrible illness and is suffering, so I have come here to ask for prayers. It will affect the illness, so you should hurry and go to the shrine.'

Saru: 'Hey, Iku, in any case, greeting the gods is essential. Come, let us go.'

Saying this, they left the Uketsuke and began to visit the many shrines one by one. Okiku was praying with Itsushiyōkenmei. The girl began to suffer and writhe more and more, and perhaps the fish bone stuck in her throat had fallen deeper; her breath was almost cut off, and she began to grab at the air in a trance. Ochiyo was startled, and when she approached, she saw a large tanuki's tail visible from the girl's buttocks. Thinking that this must be a monster, she immediately rushed outside and beckoned to the two, Iku and Saru, saying, 'Hurry, hurry.' The two, thinking that some urgent business had arisen, cut their pilgrimage in half, decided to pray later, and came back quickly. The figure of the old man who had been trembling beside the Uketsuke until now had vanished, and a beautiful woman of marriageable age was sitting there. Thinking that they had seen this woman somewhere before, the two followed Ochiyo to the Shinden and performed the Sōjiyō of the Norito. The girl began to suffer even more. At the moment Ochiyo tapped the girl's Senaka twice while singing the song of the heavenly numbers, there was a sharp sound, and what flew out was not a fish bone, but a crystal ball about one sun in diameter. Before everyone could be surprised and say 'Ah!', the girl instantly turned into an old tanuki, and the woman who had been at the Uketsuke also turned into a large tanuki, and they hid their figures over the mountain with Itsushiyōkenmei.

Sāru: 'I did not know that such a glib-tongued Otomego existed, and thus I came to visit.'

Iku: 'This Otome does not seem to be one to be handled by ordinary means; her background of being raised by chivalrous commoners is showing.'

Ochiyo: 'Since I shall not blame you if you apologize, you had better be prudent from now on.'

Iku: 'I am made an Ōbaka by a tanuki, laughed at by an owl, and once again I am playing the fool.'

Sāru: 'Ahahaha, I am so astonished that I cannot speak. Bakemono appear Anatakonata. Try applying saliva to this woman's eyebrows; surely a Shitsupo will be attached.'

Ochiyo: 'With a face like an Omoshiro tanuki, it is Atarimahe that you should be pinched.'

Sāru: 'To be made a fool of by two Otome to this extent, where can a man stand with dignity?'

Iku: 'More than anything, if the Suishiyōdama can be obtained, I shall endure whatever is said to me. It is precisely because there are such Tenbamusume that a Baketanukiyarau has revealed its Shitsupo. Ochiyo-sama, thanks to you, the Haugiyoku has been returned, so I shall bow to you.'

Ochiyo: 'O-Kiku-sama, what kind of Kami is it that gave the jewel to such Koshinukeotokora?'

O-Kiku: 'It must be the status of a Giritenjiyō Hinodewake Kami, if it is a Kami that hands it over without even examining the soul. A true Hinodewake Kami would never hand it over to such a tonma man.'

While mocking each other in this way, they gathered at the reception desk to moisten their throats with hot water, and from there, the two began to visit each shrine.
