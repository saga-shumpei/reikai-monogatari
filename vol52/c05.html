『ナーニ、今手を伸ばして探つて見たけど、何も居やせないわ』

『それでも俺の目には、貴様の前に何だか黒いものがある様だ。一寸撫でて見ようか』

『おい、サール、貴様は今、何だかバサバサやつてゐたぢやないか』

『ウン、到頭化物の奴、俺の睾丸を狙ひに来よつたので、髻をグツと握り殴つてやつたのだよ』

『アイタツタ、おい、イク、来て呉れ。何だか俺の周囲に化州の奴、ひつついてゐるやうだ。ああ惟神霊幸倍坐世惟神霊幸倍坐世』

§ 4

Yotahiko: 'Hey, what is it, what is it? Where, where?'

While saying this, he tried to tickle the other's throat, thrusting his hand out suddenly. The moment Saru also thrust his hand out nonchalantly, he felt something strange and gripped it hard.

Saru: 'Ouch, ouch, ouch! It is me, it is me! It is Iku, it is Iku, it is Iku!'

Yotahiko: 'Well now, you Iku, you are the one who struck my head, are you not? If you pull such mischievous tricks, I will not forgive you.'

Iku: 'Hmph, you were the one who gripped my topknot with all your might and struck me with all your strength, were you not?'

Yotahiko: 'Hahahaha, retribution is right before our eyes.'

As they were talking, a ball of light about two shaku in diameter appeared, illuminating the space between the trees in the forest. From within that ball, eyes, a nose, a mouth, and even eyebrows appeared, laughing merrily, 'Ehehehehe.' Because of this, their feet were suddenly illuminated. When they looked closely, they saw that at their feet, two old tanuki, Gensaubau and Genmabau, had piled up a mountain of giant centipedes, each about a shaku long, and were plotting to sting the two men to death. The two old tanuki, illuminated by this light, fled into the clouds and mist, and the giant centipedes ran with all their might to hide in the darkness of the forest. Then, this light gradually reduced its volume and came rolling to the side of the two men as a small ball. The two were not particularly surprised, and said:

Yotahiko and Iku: 'This must be the incarnation of the Kami who saved us. If this Hikaridama had not been in this darkness, we do not know what kind of fate might have befallen us. Namuhikaridaimiyōjinsama.'

They joined their hands together from both sides and offered their gratitude. Before they knew it, the two fell into a doze. The crows in the forest of Yamaguchi cawed, announcing the dawn. Startled by the sound, they awoke and looked around to see a crystal ball about an inch in diameter rolling beside them. This was a divine treasure bestowed by Hinodewake to save the two from their peril. Taking advantage of the break of day, the two placed the ball in their bosoms and, while chewing on bread, ran off in pursuit of Hatsuwakahime.
