Chapter 21: 狸妻 [1357]

狸妻〔1357〕

§ 1

Gariya, Kēsu, Hatsu, and Toku were led by eight Bijiyo into a beautiful and spacious inner Hitoma, where they were invited to enter.

Kēsu was somehow struck by the beauty of the women and the Sōgon of the Dennai, and was taken by Atsuke.

Gariya kept his eyes on the alert Shijiu, staring intently at the Fukakai Kuōkei of the Atari.

Hatsu and Toku, seeing the Jiyōshiyu of Magawajiyō, Takamiyahiko and Takamiyahime, appear on the scene, were tilting their heads repeatedly; although their Yōbau had changed quite a bit, Takamiyahime resembled the Takahime they had seen at Kogitayama in some indefinable way.

Takahime looked at Hatsu and Toku and Uchiwara,

Takahime: 'Hohohohoho, Hatsu-sama, Toku-sama, you must be surprised by this, must you not? This Takamiyahiko is Mokusuke-sama. And although Takamiyahime looks this young, I am indeed Takahime. It is thanks to the Shinriki of the Magawa that you retrieved for me that such a Saugonbirei Jiyōkuwaku was Dekiaga in an instant, and that my Yōbau has returned to that of my Jifuhachi, becoming such a beautiful woman of such fine appearance. You have done a good deed, have you not?'

Hatsu: 'Even though you made us go to such lengths, you vanished like a puff of smoke from the Forest of Kai-shi, left two people who could not even stand behind, and then threw stones at us as you fled; that is a bit cruel, is it not? Hatsu was holding a grudge, you know.'

Takahime: 'Hohohohoho, I was merely testing your courage. Now, from here on, you shall be the chief stewards of this castle. Perform your duties faithfully. Hatsu shall be the Left Guardian, and Toku the Right Guardian; tell me, Takamiyahiko-sama, that is acceptable, is it not?'

Yōgen: 'I shall leave everything to the Queen's judgment. Since this is an age of Empress worship, there is nothing else to be done, ahahahaha.'

Hatsu: 'I am so taken aback by this unexpected promotion that I feel as if I have been bewitched by a tanuki.'

Takahime: 'Hey, Hatsu-kō, or rather, Hatsu-tsukasa, what an imprudent thing to say, claiming to be bewitched by a tanuki. This is the Takamagahara of the earth. Magatsu-kami and the like can never approach this place. You must keep this in mind from now on.'

Toku: 'I am honored beyond measure to have been appointed as the Right Guardian for this extraordinary promotion. Since this is entirely the reward for our life-risking activities in retrieving the jewel of the Magawa, it is not too much of an advancement for me to become the Right Guardian. I shall humbly accept it as my rightful due.'

Yōgen: 'Ahahahaha, I cannot tell if you are happy or complaining, as I cannot understand a word of it. What a strange greeting that is.'

Takahime: 'It seems that from wrestling with tanuki and being subjected to the farts of weasels, he has suffered some mental abnormality, and his words are contradictory and self-defeating. Since this is the Magawajiyō, after all, Toku-kō cannot be serious, ahahahaha.'

Takahime arched her willow-leaf eyebrows and said,

Takahime: 'Hey, Toku, to offer such a greeting after being appointed Right Guardian, are you not insulting us? It is not as if the position of Right Guardian could not be filled by anyone but you. In that case, I shall withdraw the offer now. Instead, I shall ask Kēsu.'

Kēsu: 'Yes, I would be happy to serve as the Right Guardian. If you wish, I would not mind serving as the Left Guardian as well.'

Takahime: 'It cannot be both. You must serve as the Right Guardian. Furthermore, both the Right Guardian and the Left Guardian must perform their duties alongside their wives. It has been decided that the wife of the Left Guardian shall be Hatsuwakahime, and the wife of the Right Guardian shall be Miyanohime, so we shall hold a grand wedding ceremony soon.'

Hatsu: 'Ehehehehe, it is like a dream. Hey, Kēsu, excuse my rudeness to Hatsu-kō, I am sorry.'

Kēsu: 'Hmm, tell me, Takamiyahime-sama, the titles of Left Guardian and Right Guardian are job titles, are they not? Would it not be appropriate for each person to choose their own spouse as a human being? To make women mere appendages to the job titles of Left Guardian and Right Guardian is a bit strange. I would like to ask for free marriage in this regard. If that is not possible, I would like you to make me the Left Guardian.'

Say, Gariya, it looks like a magnificent palace, but somehow it seems I can see a grassy field. Even though it looks like this, it might still be a tanuki's den.'

Gariya: 'What, how could that be? It is a splendid palace in a fortress. Since Mokusuke-sama, Takahime-sama, and even Hatsuwakahime-sama are here, you should not worry about such things.'

Speaker: 'Hey, it is strange.'

As they were saying this, four or five beauties appeared, dressed in elaborate costumes. One woman clung to the neck of Tokukō, rubbing her soft cheek against his face, and said,

Speaker: 'Oh my, what a fine man Toku-sama is. I have never seen such a witty and spirited person, have you, you four?'

The four nodded at once. The woman who clung to Tokukō was named Sabelhime.

Speaker: 'Toku-sama, don't say such difficult, pedantic things and come to my living room. You fought a sumo match with a tanuki, didn't you? The delusions from that time remain, and everything looks like a tanuki to you. Even I could not stay in such a terrifying tanuki's den. If you think about me being here, it cannot be a tanuki's den. It is the site of the camp of General Lunch of the Floating Tree Forest, so let us go, with Sabel.'

Toku was wrapped in a cloud of doubt and pondered for a while, but Sabelhime's appearance was impossible to abandon, and at last, caught by the rogue of love, he lowered the corners of his eyes and was led to Sabelhime's living room. Toku was invited into a splendid room and, together with Sabelhime, continued sweet whispers in a murmuring tone. Then, the previous four beauties opened the door and entered, saying,

Speaker: 'Oh my, elder sister, it is a bit cruel to monopolize the handsome man. I will also become Toku-sama's wife... I too... and I...'

They clung to Toku's body from all sides, licking his ears and hands, showing their affection. Tokukō's soul was in ecstasy; he felt not the slightest pain or itching even as his ears were bitten off and his fingers were gnawed, and he opened his mouth in a square shape, drooling. His blood was sucked from his ears and toes, and he gradually turned pale, feeling so good that he fell fast asleep. Gariya, determined to see the true nature of this to the end, kept reciting the divine words in his heart without a moment's pause, while wearing a vacant expression and observing the situation. Takahime was thinking, 'This Gariya is a fellow not easily eaten; I must persuade him skillfully,' and was exerting all her strength.

Speaker: 'Gariya-sama, I have heard that even in Brahmanism you are a warrior possessed of both wisdom and courage, but you are a person of character whose majestic air sweeps the surroundings. In truth, my husband, Takamiyahiko-sama, is also earnestly hoping to make you his deputy. Would it not be possible for you to become the vice-lord of the castle?'

Gariya feigned acceptance, thinking to uncover the whole situation, and with a voice that sounded intentionally happy, said,
