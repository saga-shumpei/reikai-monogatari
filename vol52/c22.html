Chapter 22: 空走 [1358]

空走〔1358〕

§ 1

Gariya saw that there was red Ikichi on the Kuchimoto of Sabelhime, and he stared at her, thinking that this Koitsu was indeed a Fushigi creature.

Sabel, glared at by Gariya, trembled with Miburu, and suddenly laughed aloud, Sabel: 'Hohohoho, what a manly face you have; why do you glare at me like that?'

Gariya: 'Because there is red blood on your Kuchimoto, I thought it was a Fushigi and took a look.'

Sabel was startled, wiped her lips with the sleeve of her Kosode, and said, Sabel: 'This is because I applied rouge; I was in such a hurry that it ran, and you discovered a Busaiku sight. How is it, do you find it disagreeable, Anata?'

Gariya: 'It is not that I find it disagreeable or anything, but I dislike a Bakeonna who bites people's ears or kisses their private parts. How did it go with Toku-sama? You must be Zuibun Manzoku, I suppose.'

Sabel: 'Yes, I left Toku-sama to the four people. I truly cannot help but love a person who has the character of Ga attached to them. Tell me, Anata, you surely cannot hate me that much, can you?'

Saying this, she tried to bite Gariya's ear. When Gariya gripped Sabel's arm firmly, what had appeared to be an arm shining like Zaugezaiku was actually the arm of a Furudanuki covered in hair. Gariya put all his Zenshin strength into his grip and would not let go even a little. Sabel immediately revealed her Shiyōtai, becoming a Furudanuki and struggling with her body. Gariya immediately took a Hosohimo from his bosom, tied her four legs tightly, and hung her from the Tenjiyōura. His intention was to bring the Riyōnin, Kēsu and Hatsu, to Koko and wake them up. The tanuki cried out in a Himei with Itsushiyōkenmei. The ones who were surprised by this voice and came were Eugenbau and Takahime.

Eugenbau: 'Ya, Gariya-sama, what is this? You have obtained quite an extraordinary thing.'

Gariya: 'Yes, if I prepare some tanuki soup and have a cup, it should be Zuibun sweet. That Sabelhime is a disguised villain who tried to bite off our ears. All the beautiful women here are likely nothing but tanuki. Do you not see that every single woman's ears are moving in unison? Ya, your ears are moving too, Omae-sama.'

Eugenbau: 'Ahahahaha, since this is a place where the movement of the air is intense, the extremities of the body are likely swaying and moving in the wind. However, since I will cook this tanuki myself, please leave it to me.'

Saying this, he tried to take the tanuki to the next room. Takamiyahime was startled and did not say a word... that Sabelhime was a tanuki, what a thing one cannot let one's guard down against... she whispered to herself, clicking her tongue in secret. Takamiyahiko forcibly took the Furudanuki in his arms and hid himself in his own living room. This was to untie the rope and save his own subordinate. The one who had been disguised as Sabel was Gensōbō.

After that, Gariya went to the living room where Kēsu and Hatsukimi were having a secret talk to investigate the situation, and as he held his breath and pressed his ear against the wall, it seemed that the four men and women were screaming and flirting with each other in sweet, cloying language. The four people in the room did not even dream that Gariya was standing outside listening to the situation, and they were lost in their own world, scattering sparks in their competition for women and men, at a time when the battle was truly at its height.

It was in the mood that the Battle of Sekigahara, which would divide the world, and the decisive battle of Mt. Tenno were now imminent in an instant, and they were doing their best, completely absorbed in it.

Hatsuwakahime: 'No matter what anyone says, Hatsu-no-atae likes Kēsu-sama. And Hatsu-sama also likes him, after all.'

Ehehehehe, hey Hatsu-kō, how is it? It belongs to Kēsu, after all, doesn't it? You should be patient with Miyanohime, Kisama.'

ガリヤはサベル姫の口許に赤い生血がついてゐるのを見て、いよいよ此奴ア不思議な奴と目を注いだ。サベルはガリヤに睨みつけられ、ビリビリと身慄ひしながら、俄に笑ひ声、

『ホホホホホ、あのマア男らしいお顔わいの、なぜ其様に私を睨ましやんすのですか』

『お前の口許に赤い血がついてるので、不思議だと思つて覗いたのだ』

サベルは驚いて、小袖の袂で唇を拭き、

『これは紅をつけましたの、余り慌てたものですから、つひ流れまして、無細工な所を、貴方に見付けられたのですよ。どうです、貴方はお厭ですか』

『厭でも何でもありませぬが、私は人の耳にかぶりついたり、○玉にキツスするやうな化女は嫌ですよ。徳は何うなりましたか、随分満足して居るでせうな』

『ハイ、徳さまは四人のお方に一任しておきました。私、本当にガの字のついた人が好きでたまらないのですよ。ねえ、貴方、余り憎うはありますまい』

と云ひながら、ガリヤの耳に喰ひつかうとした。ガリヤはサベルの腕をグツと握つてみれば、象牙細工のやうな光つた腕と見えてゐたのは、毛だらけの古狸の手であつた。ガリヤは全身の力を籠めてグツと握り、チツとも放さぬ。サベルは忽ち正体を現はし、古狸となつてヂタバタ体をもがいてゐる。ガリヤは直に懐より細紐を取出し、四つ足を固く括つて、天井裏に吊り下げて了つた。そしてケース、初の両人を此処へ引寄せて、目を醒ましてやらうとの考へであつた。狸は一生懸命に悲鳴をあげて泣き叫ぶ。此声に驚いてやつて来たのは、妖幻坊と高姫の二人であつた。

妖幻『ヤア、ガリヤさま、コリヤ何ですか、えらいものが手に入りましたな』

『ハイ、狸汁でも拵へて一杯やつたら、随分甘いことでせう。サベル姫なんて、うまく化けよつて、吾々の耳を咬み取らうと致した曲者ですよ。ここに沢山ゐる美人は皆狸ばかりでせう。どの女もどの女も、一斉に耳が動いてるぢやありませぬか。ヤ、お前さまも耳が動きますね』

『アハハハハ、何分空気の動揺が烈しい所ですから、身体の末端が風に揺られて動くのでせう。併しながら此狸は私が手料理致しますから、お任せ下さい』

と云ひながら狸を下げて次の間に行かうとする。高宮姫は吃驚して、一言も言はず……あのサベル姫が狸であつたか、何とマア油断のならぬものだなア……と秘かに舌を巻いてゐた。高宮彦は無理無体に古狸を引抱へ自分の居間に姿を隠した。これは綱を解いてやつて自分の家来を助ける為である。サベルに化けてゐたのは幻相坊であつた。それからガリヤはケース、初公の密談の居間へ行つて様子を探らうと、跫蛩音を忍ばせ壁に耳を当てて聞いてゐると、四人の男女が金切声を出して、甘つたるい言葉つきで何か意茶ついてゐるやうである。室内の四人はガリヤが外に立つて様子を聞いてることは夢にも知らず、現をぬかして、女の取合、男の取合に火花を散らして正に戦ひ酣なる時であつた。天下分目の関ケ原、天王山の晴戦は今や瞬間に迫れりといふ調子で、あらゆるベストを尽し、夢中になつてゐる。

『初稚姫のあたえは、何と云つてもケースさまが好きです。そして初さまもヤツパリ好きですワ』

『エヘヘヘヘ、オイ初公、どうだ、ヤツパリ、ケースのものだらう。貴様は宮野姫で辛抱せい』

§ 2

Kese: 'Do not talk like a Baka. I have been set on Hatsuwakahime-sama from the beginning. Miyanohime-sama is yours.'

Kese: 'No matter what you say, my wife is Hatsuwakahime-sama.'

Miyano: 'No matter what anyone says, I love Kese-sama. And I love Hatsuwaka-sama too. I, Miyano, will take both of you as my husbands.'

Hatsuwaka: 'I, Hatsuwaka, will also take both of you as my husbands.'

Kese: 'My, how painful it is to be born an Irōtoko. Why did my parents have to give birth to such a handsome man? They should have thought about the Meiwaku to the child when they were doing the Seizau. It is an Arigatameiwaku.'

Kese is riding on this Chōshi and is Unubo. Gariya can no longer stand it, forces the door open, and when he jumps in, he sees that both Kese and Hatsuwaka have had their earlobes completely torn off by an old Furudanuki and are lying there covered in blood. The Furudanuki, having lost its escape route, runs around the corner of the room like a mouse. The moment Gariya finally pins down Itsupiki of the tanuki, he is bitten on the upper arm... the moment he says 'Aitata' and lets go, the Nihiki of Furudanuki hide themselves with Itsushiyōkenmei. After a while, the voice of a Sendenka is heard clearly.

'Woo, wan-wan.'

The voice of a Mauken. Looking at the Atari, Gariya is standing in the Mannaka of a Kayano where the grass is Baubau. And both Kese and Hatsuwaka have their faces Ichimen covered in mud, their earlobes Hanbun bitten off, and are groaning while covered in blood. The master of the Sendenka is the true Hatsuwakahime. And her Aiken, Smart, runs around in all directions, chasing the Furudanuki, and with such vigor to bite them to death that even Yōgenbō, Genmabō, and Gensōbō cannot remain there; using the art of Kuruwa, they place Takahime on a cloud and, while showing their thick reddish-brown tails flickering in the air, head toward the southeastern sky. Inside the bamboo grove, covered in spider webs, Ranch and General Katahiko are trembling with pale faces. Tokukō has had his earlobes torn off and is lying stretched out in the shape of a large character on the Shikushiku Plain.

Hatsuwakahime turns to Gariya,

Gariya: 'Are you not a believer of Sangyō?'

Gariya: 'Ah, I have no face to show you. Knowing it was a den of tanuki, I thought I would investigate it, so I came this far, but instead, I was done in. Are you the true Hatsuwakahime-sama? Thanks to your divine power, the eyes of all of us have been opened. Arigatō-gozaimasu.'

He is expressing his gratitude. Just then, General Ranch and General Katahiko enter, helping Tokukō, and with tears, they thank Hatsuwakahime for saving them from danger; from this point on, they resolve to change their hearts and proceed toward the forest of the shrine. Hatsuwakahime explains and admonishes the truth thoroughly to the six people, and with Smart following her, she departs toward the southwest while singing the Sendenka.
