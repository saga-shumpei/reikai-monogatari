Chapter 27: 胎蔵 [1363]

胎蔵〔1363〕

§ 1

Tokiokashinokami Mokusuke left the lion in the care of the guard, proceeded to the mansion of the Shinpanshin of Yachimata, Ibukidonushi, and in a room in the back, he and Ibukidonushi were engaged in a conversation.

Ibukidonushi: 'Ah, Tokiokashinokami-sama, the work of Senden must be quite exhausting; I sympathize with your Gokushin.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'Indeed, in this clouded world, beings like us cannot perform even one ten-thousandth of the Oboshimeshi of the gods, so I am filled with Zanki. Therefore, the reason I have visited you this time is by the Gomeirei of Kamususanōnōokami. There is a woman called Takahime who was in Ananai-kyō, and her Giyōjiyō is truly troublesome. She has been deceived by a Seirei of the Kiyōtaukai, a Eukuwai named Eugenbau, and believing it to be me, she runs about here and there as Tokiokashi or Mokusuke, causing the people of the world to be greatly confused. For that reason, taking advantage of the fact that she has come to the Reikai this time, I would like you to arrange it so that she is not returned to the Genkai for a while.'

Ibukidonushi: 'Indeed, I must do something about the words of the Ōkami. However, as for that Takahime, if I look at the book of life and death, she still has twenty-eight years of lifespan remaining. It is easy to keep her in the Reikai, but she must eventually be returned to the Genkai; if she is kept for too long, her physical body will become useless. If there is no objection to changing her physical body, I shall make some arrangement.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'Please, I ask that you keep her here for two or three years, and if you would allow the Seirei of Takahime to dwell in the body of a woman who is destined to come to the Reikai three years from now, it would be very convenient.'

Ibukidonushi-kami closed his eyes for a while and pondered, but soon he nodded and said,

Ibukidonushi: 'Yes, that will be fine. Since there is a suitable body coming to the Reikai three years hence, I shall arrange for Takahime's Seirei to dwell in that body and have her live in the Genkai for twenty-eight years.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'Oh, I am truly grateful for that. If so, I shall take my leave.'

Ibukidonushi: 'Tokiokashinokami-sama, er, just now, great villains such as Keisuke Ōhara, Kyōsuke Katayama, and Akujirō Takada have appeared here, but since the judgment is about to begin, would you not like to observe it for a moment? The judgment for Takahime will also begin from now.'

Tokiokashinokami: 'No, if Takahime is there, I shall decline, despite your kind offer, ha ha ha ha.'

Ibukidonushi: 'I will not insist. If that is the case, please give my regards to the Ōkami. I am going to the court of judgment from here.'

He stood up swiftly, walked along the corridor, and went to the judgment hall. Mokusuke called the guard to bring the lion, leaped onto its back, and while the lion's roar of 'Utsu' shook the surroundings, he rode off at full speed, aiming for the direction of Mount Ubusuna, galloping through the mid-air.

In the Yachimata of the Chūyūkai, Ibukidonushi remains forever, And to those who wander in, He examines each and every Seirei, Placing them upon the scales, Pure and innocent souls, According to the love of each master, To the Reikoku of Takamagahara, Or to the heaven of the three stages, He sends them as their Reisō befits, And the Seirei of extreme evil and lawlessness, He casts down immediately into hell, And those Seirei who are neither good, Nor strong in evil, For a fixed period of time, He releases widely into the world of Chūyū, Until their spirits are purified, To ascend to Takamagahara, Accumulating the virtues of love and good, Polishing the wisdom of faith and truth,

The Seirei who have attained enlightenment, He sends all up to heaven, And those Seirei who do not repent and remain forever, Strong in their evil hearts, He wipes away their tears and casts them, Into the dark hell. Keisuke, who has now appeared, Kyōsuke Katayama, and Akujirō, These three villains, Are given a very strict judgment, And are immediately cast down, To the bottom of the dark hell, To taste infinite eternal suffering, With both hands hanging before them, In a state of utter dejection, Their faces pale and trembling, Their appearance shaking in fear, Suddenly changing into the wretched form, of the Eukuwai they are, And the bumbling Mokusuke who came later,

The two, Otsuya and her partner, were judged for the great sin of adultery, and were separated to the left and right into the hellish path of the Shikiyokukai, proceeding with great sorrow.

Following them, Takahimekamutsukasa was judged by Ibukidonushi, and for those three years, was cast out into the Chiūkai, wandering the Arano, tasting various Kannan and Shinku, until the clouds of Gajiyō and Gaman cleared, and at last, she became a person of sincerity, appearing again in the Genkai to devote her sincerity for the sake of the Ananai-kyō; yet, her Jiyōnen erupted once more, and being deceived by Eugenbau, she hid herself in a certain hill in Karmata in the land of Indo.

To exhaust every evil act together with Eugenbau is truly a miserable state of affairs; thus is told the Monogatari of the Reikai, reflecting the truth.

The Tokitsukaze of the fifty-second year, holding the key to the Miroku Taizō, the Miroku-kami clearly explains and exhorts the Kujūmetsudō, filling the spirits of the Komatsubayashi with purity, and conveying the path to the prophets who guide this world, bestowing the great grace of leading all people universally to heaven; do not cast this aside in vain, O people, and you believers who have gathered at the Moto of the Ananai-kyō, and you hundred Tsukasa, calm your hearts and place your hands upon your chests.

You must ponder this well and deeply; ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may your spirits be blessed; even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, even if the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, and even if the seas should dry up, be prepared that the Kototama of the divine path shall never be exhausted even to the end of ten thousand kalpas, and believing well in this teaching, dwell in the virtues of love and goodness, and let the light of faith and truth shine upon the world, Kannagara; receive the wisdom and enlightenment, and as this body remains as it is, join the ranks of the Tennin and fulfill the pure duty of human life; God is with us, and God is with thee.

There is nothing more precious than God, who becomes our staff and pillar to save our bodies, for man is the child of God and the shrine of God; look up and revere Him, O people; born of the divine water and fire, living in the land created by God, eating the millet given by God, and planting the sincere teachings of God deep within your hearts, do not forget them even for a moment; the reason man is man is to receive the divine character of the Sumer-Ōkami into his body and serve the divine work of the Shinkai; ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, I offer my sincere heart before the presence of God and serve Him with gratitude.

Fearing the divine words of God, the fifty-two volumes were brought to completion; this book was written as a bedtime story upon the bed, while having a disciple take the brush; although there were various ugly obstructions, it was completed under the protection of God.
