Chapter 3: 楽屋内 [1339]

楽屋内〔1339〕

§ 1

Iku and Sāru, the two of them, took a path through the forest from the back entrance, slipped through a shallow valley for about two or three chō, emerged onto the main road, and from there rushed to the forest of Yamaguchi, singing songs with all their might as they descended the steep slope, determined to be permitted to accompany Hatsuwakahime by any means necessary.

Iku: 'Following the Brahman army, while I was serving as a guard in the rock cave of Kiyoharuyama, Matsuhiko and Tatsukō appeared, welcomed Itakōtsukasa, and surrendered to Ananai-kyō. Moved by that sincere heart, we too completely surrendered to the Oshihe of the Ōkami, and serving in the forest of the shrine, we have served until now. But the Sendenshi who descended, parting the eight-fold clouds of Ame, Hatsuwakahime, her Shintoku has stolen both heart and soul, and now we have become true believers of Ananai-kyō. Nevertheless, although the divine officials of the Saienkwan are as numerous as the stars in the sky, officials who can be called the incarnation of love and goodness are rare. Aside from the divine person of Hatsuwakahime, there is no true god who can save us. From here on, we shall follow her footsteps wherever she may go, and illuminated by her Shintoku, chosen as messengers of the path of sincerity, living as officials of the Oshihe in the earthly world, and dying as officials of the Reikoku and Tenkoku, we are a pair who must perfectly and thoroughly fulfill the final purpose of life and achieve merits that replace heaven and earth. Advance, advance, come, advance! Aiming for Yamaguchi, swiftly, what are the rocky, undulating valley paths to us, Takahime?'

Iku: 'Even if Yōgenbō uses all kinds of magic on the way and tries to torment and attack us, what is there to fear? We are the brave men of Yamato, the sons of Shikishima, illuminated by the light of the gods, breaking through the mountain path where demons rage at top speed. This departure is truly valiant. "Untokodotsukoi dotsukoishiyo." The slope has gradually become steep. Sāru-tsukasa, be careful. This is the very place where Yōgenbō and the ugly official Takahime appeared. "Untokodotsukoi," come on! This time I am fine. The Sendenshi who has gathered the strength of a million people into one, starting with Hatsuwakahime, and Smart-sama are coming. Even if a hundred Yōgenbō or ten thousand Takahime come, once it has come to this, it is as solid as a rock. Ah, how interesting, how interesting! This body entrusted to the gods, I run with single-minded devotion for the sake of the gods and for the sake of the world. "Untokodotsukoi, yatsutokoshiyo." Sāru-tsukasa, what are you doing? Even if you say your compass is shorter than mine, this is too much. You are a fellow with such slow feet. If you dawdle, Hime-sama will look at your back, and what will you do if she calls out, "Hey, hey, wait, you two"? The farce that we squeezed out our wisdom to plan this far will come to nothing. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. May the spirits be blessed, and may the divine official Hatsuwakahime graciously allow us two to accompany her on her long journey. O Ōkami, please arrange this and let us fulfill our single-minded desire. I pray before the precious presence. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, whether the earth is submerged in mud, this departure we have decided upon is like a mulberry bow that will not be pulled back. Following her wherever she may go, illuminated by the divine character of Hatsuwakahime, we are the Yamato-damashii that will not stop until we have fulfilled our duty. Ah, how valiant, how valiant!'

Iku: 'Speaking of which, Haru, Teru, and the likes of Iru are fools. Why didn't they use their wits and excuse themselves to go ahead? As expected, what people without wisdom do is always lacking somewhere. When the moment of truth comes, they are like deflated air. When I think about it, it is funny. Please protect us, Kannagara. I pray before the presence of the gods.'

Sāru sings again.

Sāru: '"Untokodotsukoi dotsukoishiyo." No matter when I come to see it, this slope is a difficult place that makes walking painful. Wait a moment, you Iku. Why are you in such a hurry? The divine official Hatsuwakahime will surely come from behind. Just go along slowly. If you stumble by mistake, Takahime and Yōgenbō, who have been watching for your weakness, will appear, and what will you do if you are made to suffer as you were before? Are you planning to be saved by Smart again, and this time, to be saved from the jaws of death by Smart again? That is too greedy. There is a saying that you will not find a loach under the willow tree twice or three times.'

サールは又歌ふ。

§ 2

Do you understand? Have you forgotten the teaching to take the overturning of the front cart as a warning for the rear cart? There is a limit even to being a reckless warrior. Even if it is said that there is no retreat in the teachings of Ananai-kyō, it is something to think about, even a little, to rush forward recklessly.

Ah, you fast-footed fellow. Your figure has disappeared. You ran along this mountain path like an arrow, but your eyes must have been clouded; you stumbled on a stone again, overturned with a thud, and while suffering, clutching your shin and groaning, you must surely be laughing and crying. Haste makes waste; take it one step at a time.

I shall proceed slowly. 'Ottodokkoi,' this is a steep slope. There is no place to plant one's feet. If one were to slip by mistake, that would be the very place to cast away one's life. No matter how lowly one's status may be, this is an Ikumitama of Kami, an Ikimiya that Kami deigns to inhabit. People should not forget the responsibility of their own Jishin while standing in this world. No matter how great one's Mitama may be, a body is necessary to work in the Genjitsukai. To preserve both spirit and body in perfection and serve the great divine work of the Ōkami is the duty of a human being. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-haemase. Blessed by the grace of Kami, the two of us are safe.

Running down this steep slope, we shall wait for the appearance of Hatsuwakahime in the forest ahead, and by exhausting a thousand words and ten thousand phrases, we must do our utmost to be allowed to accompany her. Even if she does not listen, through the protection of the Imperial Ōkami, we shall borrow the body of Hatsuwakahime and use her Kototama to be permitted to accompany her. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, if we do not achieve this purpose, we shall not return to the forest of the shrine, even if we two must die. Please have mercy on our sincerity and grant us permission, O Great Kami. We pray before your precious presence. 'Untokodokkoi, dokkoishiyo.'

The road has gradually become gentler. Iku-san is likely resting his breath around here. He must have broken through the great barrier without incident. This, too, is entirely due to the pure and noble protection of Kami. I am happy and offer my gratitude.

While singing this, he went 'tonton-ton' along the narrow valley path, keeping a distance of two or three chō, appearing and disappearing among the trees, and finally arrived at the foot of a large oak tree at the entrance to the mountain.

Yotahiko: 'Hey, Saru, how is it? You are quite slow, aren't you? Even if you were wearing iron sandals, you should have been able to come faster. I have been here and cooling down, and since I still cannot see your figure, I was slightly worried that you might have met Yōgenbō on the way again and been defeated; if that were the case, I was going to pick up your bones. Well, well, I will praise you a little for arriving here safely. However, what is with your face? Isn't it pitch black?'

Saru: 'When I was slipping out from behind the mansion, I thought it would be terrible if I were discovered, so I followed behind you wearing a charcoal sack, and perhaps the charcoal dust got on me. I feel as if everything around here has become gloomy.'

Yotahiko: 'Hahaha, because you sweated while wearing a charcoal sack, the charcoal juice has blended well, and your face looks like a piece of calligraphy paper. But what happened to that charcoal sack?'

Saru: 'Do you think I have the leisure to think about where I dropped it or threw it away? Because you abandoned me and ran off with all your might, I had to look ahead and watch my footing, and I truly had a hard time, but I finally arrived here while praying to Kami. Fortunately, there is a mountain stream flowing here, so I will go wash my face and hands, so please wait for me. There should still be quite a while before the Princess arrives.'

Yotahiko: 'Wait, wait. That blackness of yours might be very convenient. Your face is so black that one cannot tell which way you are facing. With this, let us perform a kyōgen to move the Princess's heart and get her to let us accompany her successfully. I, too, would like to paint something on my face; is there not something convenient?'

Sāru: 'Actually, I slipped the Oshiroi that Kaede-sama uses into my bosom without thinking and brought it here. I will give this to you, so you paint your entire face with it and become a white-faced Jiyōdono, while I, fortunately with this black face, will become a black-faced Jiyōdono, and we shall return to the original Yashiki... that is how we will bluff. If we do that, Hatsuwakahime-sama might return to the forest of the original shrine. If she does not return, you should immediately untie your obi, hang it swiftly on this oak tree, and hang your chin to swing to and fro.'

Nanige: 'Oh, that is Omoshiro.'

Taking the Oshiroi that was in Sāru's bosom and smearing it thickly onto his face, the white powder stuck to the beard growing all over his cheeks, and he ended up looking just like a Biyakuko. The Futari broke off suitable tree branches, held a fan in one hand, and regarding the twig in the other hand as a bell, they waited at the base of the oak for Hatsuwakahime to approach, having devised various Kufū to begin dancing the Sanbasō all at once as soon as her figure appeared.
