Chapter 24: 応対盗 [1360]

応対盗〔1360〕

§ 1

Jifugorokunin Seirei suddenly gathered around Takahime and were clamoring noisily. Takahime finally stood up, sat herself upon a square stone on the roadside, and while placing the dozen or so people before her, began a lecture full of digressions.

Takahime: 'Listen here, everyone, I, Takahime, am about to give a public speech, so listen well. Is this world not an Ankokusekai where the Ihato of Ama is tightly shut due to the Akugami of Susanōnomikoto, where Akuma run rampant throughout the Tenka, and where Chimimauriyō are Jiuman? If this world is left as it is, this Ketsukō land of ours will instantly fall into Gakidau, Chikushiyōdau, Shiyuradau, and Jigokudau. You people are working Eieikotsukotsu, running about day and night for the sake of Shirishiyoku, and being consumed by greed, you are exhausted and your complexions are haggard; you are almost like hungry ghosts. How pitiful it is that while suffering the torments of Jigokudau in this world, you rejoice, saying there is no world as Ketsukō as this. The Lord God of supreme benevolence and supreme love cannot bear to see this tragic state, and the time having arrived, Ushitoranokonjin Ōkunitokotachinomikoto, who had long been confined to the northeast corner, has taken the physical palace of a hensei-danshi possessed by the spirit of Wakahimegiminomikoto, and has begun to perform the Tatekaetatinaoshi of the three thousand worlds. Regarding this, unless we raise the Yaoyorozunokami who have fallen into the world, give them each their names, and worship them, it will not become a Shinkoku. As for those who will serve in this mission, Otohime-dono, who long resided at the bottom of the Ryūgū sea, is the first to reform, and by joining with the Hinodewake of Giri-Tenjō, she will perform the service in foreign lands. Kanekatsuyōjin is the Konjin of the earth; because his ego was too strong, he was pushed into a filthy place and degraded to the status of a god of the privy. Even though he is now being raised into the world, because his ego is still strong, he only hinders the great ambition, and since there is no other way, his lineage spirit was dropped into the world to become an Ikumiya of Giri-Tenjō, and his physical body, possessed by the spirit of Daishōgun-sama, was made his husband to serve the three thousand worlds. However, because this physical palace of Daishōgun-sama was not at all sufficient, Tokiokishigami-sama, known as one of the three crows of San-u-kyō, was made the husband of this physical palace to perform the service of Tatekaetatinaoshi. In connection with this, the spirit of Ōhirokimasamune-dono is also used for the service to establish the Ketsukō world of Miroku, so this Takahime is the savior of the three thousand worlds; everyone, open your ears and listen well. It is the Ikumiya of Hinodewake that grips the Yamata-no-orochi and the Akugami of Kinmōkyūbi by the belly and forces them to reform. The people of the world are all blind, so they probably cannot hear what this Ketsukō physical palace is saying. If you are going to reform, now is the time. Later reform will be too late. If there is any person among you who understands the truth, try raising your hand. I will gladly make you my kenzoku and use you for this Ketsukō service.'

Emerging suddenly from the crowd was Otoshi. Otoshi stepped forward toward Takahime and gripped her hand firmly.

Otoshi: 'Excuse me, Takahime-sama, thank you for the kindness you showed my father while he was alive.'

Takahime: 'I do not know who you are, but among all these people, it seems they are all blind and cannot understand what this Ikumiya says; even when I tell them to raise their hands, there is not a single hungry ghost who does so. Besides, you are a virtuous one. Whose daughter are you, anyway?'

Otoshi: 'Yes, I am the daughter of Bunsuke.'

Takahime: 'What, Bunsuke's daughter... how could there be such a grown woman? This is strange... Aha, I understand. That old man, pretending to know nothing, secretly made a woman and had a child like this. What an untrustworthy man he is, ohohoho.'

原文（近代文語）

十五六人の精霊は忽ち高姫の周囲に集まり来つて、ワイワイと喚いてゐる。高姫は漸くにして立上り、道端の方形の石に腰打ち掛け、十数人の人を前におきながら、脱線だらけの宣伝を始めかけた。

『コレコレ皆さま、高姫が大道演説を致しますから、よつくお聞きなされ。此世の中は素盞嗚尊の悪神の為に、天の岩戸はピツタリとしまつて、悪魔は天下に横行し、魑魅魍魎充満する暗黒世界ではありませぬか。此世を此儘にしておいたならば、結構な此お土の上は、忽ち餓鬼道、畜生道、修羅道、地獄道に陥りますぞや。お前さま等は、営々兀々として、私利私欲のために日夜奔走し、欲にからまれ、疲れ切つて顔色憔悴し、殆ど餓鬼のやうで厶いますぞ。此世からなる地獄道の苦しみを致しながら、こんな結構な世はないと申して喜んで厶る其憐れさ。至仁至愛の大神様は此惨状をみるに忍びず、時節参りて、永らく艮の隅に押し込められて厶つた艮の金神大国常立尊様が稚姫君命の霊の憑りた変性男子の肉宮をかつて、三千世界の立替立直しを遊ばすやうになりましたぞや。それに就いては、世に落ちて厶つた八百万の神様を世にあげて、それぞれお名をつけ、祭つて上げねば神国にはなりませぬ。今度のお役にお立ち遊ばすのは、永らく竜宮の海の底にお住ひなされた乙姫殿が第一番に改心を遊ばして、義理天上の日の出神と引添うて、外国での御用を遊ばすなり、金勝要神は大地の金神様で、余り我が強うて、汚い所へ押し込まれ、雪隠の神とまで成り下り、今度世に上げて貰うても、ヤツパリ我が強いので、御大望の邪魔になるばかりで、どうにもかうにも仕方がないので、系統の霊を世に落して義理天上の生宮となし、大将軍様の憑つた肉体を夫と遊ばして、三千世界の御用にお使ひなされたなれど、此大将軍様の肉宮はチツとも間に合はぬによつて、三五教の三羽烏と聞えたる時置師神様を、此肉宮の夫と致し、立替立直しの御用を遊ばす仕組で厶るぞや。それに就いては大広木正宗殿の霊も御用に使うて、結構な五六七の世をお立て遊ばすのだから、此高姫は三千世界の救主、皆さま耳をさらへて、よつく聞きなされ。八岐大蛇も金毛九尾の悪神も、グツと肚へ締め込んで改心をさせるのが、日の出神の生宮だ。世界の人民は皆盲だから、此結構な肉宮の申すことが耳には入らうまいがな。改心するなら、今の中ぢやぞえ。後の改心は間に合はぬぞや。此中で誠の分りた人民があるなれば、手を挙げてごらんなさい。喜んで此方の眷属と致して結構な御用に使ふぞや』

群集の中よりヌツと顔を出したのは、お年であつた。お年は高姫の前に進み寄り、其手をグツと握り、

『モシ高姫様、父が生前に御世話になりまして有難う厶ります』

『お前は誰だか知らぬが、これだけ沢山居る中に、此生宮の言ふことが分らぬ盲ばかりだとみえて、手を挙げと言うても、一人も手を挙げる餓鬼やありませぬワイ。それに又お前は奇篤なことだ。一体誰の娘だい』

『ハイ、文助の娘で厶います』

『ナニ、文助の娘に……そんな大きな女があるものか、此奴ア不思議だなア……ハハア、分つた、あの爺、素知らぬ顔をして居つて、秘密で女を拵へ、こんな子を生んどきよつたのだな。何とマア油断のならぬ男だわい、オホホホホ』

Speaker: 'No, no, I am one who left the Genkai at the age of three and came here. Thanks to that, I have grown up to be this fine adult.'

Speaker: 'Haha, what a strange thing for a woman to say. Are you not a lunatic? You look somewhat like Bunsuke, and if you were his illegitimate child, such a child might exist, but one who died at the age of three could not possibly be alive in this world... How strange.'

Speaker: 'Takahime-sama, this is the Yachimata of Meido. It is certainly not the Genkai. The reason so many people are gathered here like this is that they are all spirits of the Genkai and the Iukai.'

Speaker: 'Wait a moment, I must reconsider this. Hearing that, the situation around here seems somewhat different. That you came to the Reikai at the age of three and have grown up to be this adult is truly a Fushigi thing, hmm.'

He bites his tongue, tilts his head, and is lost in Shian. The white-colored Shiyuei beckoned to the Ōzei one by one. The first to be beckoned and approach was an old man of about eighty leaning on a cane.

Speaker: 'What is your name?'

Speaker: 'Yes, I am called Keisuke.'

Speaker: 'Is your Guahi poor? Your Kahoiro is a bit pale, is it not?'

Speaker: 'I thought I was going to a place like a Suteishiyon, and then I felt something strike my chest. At that moment, I thought "ah," and before I knew it, I had come to such a place.'

Speaker: 'How old are you?'

Speaker: 'Yes, I am sixty years old.'

Speaker: 'Your hair is so white that you looked about eighty. You are quite a wicked man, are you not? You are the one who ordered your subordinates to smash the temple in Jerusalem, are you not?'

Speaker: 'No, that is preposterous, it was certainly not me. Katayama-kun gave the order, and since I could not hold my position as president of the thief company without obeying that order, I had no choice but to order my subordinates. I am by no means the principal offender.'

Speaker: 'Then you are an accomplice. Very well, this fellow is not something I can handle easily. I shall request a strict judgment from Ibukido-nushi-no-Kami. Now, pass through this gate.'

The white Shiyuei pushed him into the gate. The white-haired old man walks unsteadily, like a sheep to the slaughterhouse. Behind him, a thin man of about sixty is being judged by the white one.

Speaker: 'Who are you? State your name.'

Speaker: 'Yes, I am called Katayama Kyōsuke.'

Speaker: 'Indeed, you have been quite active in the military clique. I do not know how many tens of thousands of spirits you have sent to the Iukai for your sake; you are a very famous man in the Iukai. This cannot be judged here either. Now, go inside!'

He pushed him into the gate once again. The next old man to arrive is leaning on an iron cane.

Speaker: 'Are you not Takada Akujirō?'

Speaker: 'Yes, I am the mastermind of outward virtue and inward vice, and I worked quite hard thinking I would become the world's greatest millionaire. However, due to an unforeseen disaster, I have wandered into such a place, and I have no face to show you.'

Speaker: 'Is that cane not made of iron? Why do you bring such a thing to a place like this?'

Speaker: 'This is what they call an iron rod for an ogre; ever since I was in the Genkai, I have been playing the role of an ogre. This is the only weapon with which I smashed all the banks and companies, gathered them all together, and piled up immense wealth, so I cannot let go of this no matter what.'

Speaker: 'I shall take this iron rod. Now, hand it over quickly.'

Speaker: 'That is preposterous; how could I hand over this rod, which is more precious than my life?'

Speaker: 'If you keep holding this, when you meet the judgment of Ibukido-nushi, you will surely fall to the bottom of hell. That is why I am telling you to hand it over here. If you do so, you might be allowed to stay in the world of Yachimata.'

と舌をかみ、首を傾けて思案にくれてゐる。白い色の守衛は、大勢の者を一々手招きした。先づ第一に招かれて近寄つたのは、八十ばかりの杖をついた老爺である。

と白の守衛は門内へつき入れて了つた。白髪の爺はヒヨロ　ヒヨロしながら、屠所の羊の様に歩み行く。後には細長い六十位な男が白に審判を受けてゐる。

と又もや門内へ押込んだ。次にやつて来た爺は鉄の杖をついてゐる。

Speaker: 'Do not say such Metsusau things. Even if you try to steal by saying such sweet things, I will not fall for that trick. Although this Kanabō looks like this, it has a Kachi of Niokuen. The Niokuen produced from this iron rod, so to speak, this rod is like a Tegata for Niokuen. No matter when I am sent to Jigokukai, as long as I have this, I am Daijiyōbu. Since Jigoku no Sata mo Kaneshidai, it is a Chiyōhō treasure that allows me to instantly defeat any Oni or Enma and become the Ōjiya of Jigokukai. No matter what you say, I will not hand this over, so please give up.'

To this place, a Shiyuei with an Akazura came.

Speaker: 'Ya, you are Takada Akujirau, are you not? You have appeared at a good time. Come, come inside. You are not allowed to bring that Kanabō even one Itsupo inside the Monnai.'

Speaker: 'Hahahaha, I do not know if this is the Yachimata of Meido or what, but it is a place where convenient thieves are Haikuwai. This is Takada's Yuiitsu Buki. No matter what anyone says, I will not let go of it; if you can make me let go, then try it. Any Kenriyoku or divine power must surely bow before money.'

Speaker: 'You are a fool. In the Reikai, do you think material treasures are needed? It is only in the dark Genkai that money holds sway.'

Speaker: 'But is it not said that Jigoku no Sata mo Kaneshidai?'

Speaker: 'Using money to control things is the so-called way of Jigoku.'

Speaker: 'Look there, there is no concern that a person like me will go to Tengoku. Since before I was born, I was prepared to go to Jigoku. That is why, if I go to Jigoku, I will need money; no matter what you say, I will not let go of this.'

Speaker: 'In that case, you prefer Jigoku to Tengoku, do you?'

Speaker: 'That is correct. There are likely many people in Jigoku, and as long as I have money, I can hold sway, so please, I would like you to send me to Jigoku. No matter how much of a Jigoku it is, if I have Niokuen in money, I can do anything.'

Speaker: 'I cannot allow a person with such an unprincipled mind to take money and go to Jigoku. Leave it here.'

Speaker: 'No matter what you say, I will not let go of this fellow.'

Speaker: 'If so, I shall make you let go with my power.'

With a single grunt of 'Un', as soon as he cast a spiritual binding, Takada's hands went numb, and the iron rod fell to the ground with a clatter. Instantly, he twisted Takada's arms behind his back,

Speaker: 'You Ōtaitosuninto-yatsu.'

Saying this, he tied him up with a monkey-knot, kicked him in the rear with a thud, and threw him into the Monnai. Takahime stood on tiptoe from among the crowd, watching this scene with a smile.
