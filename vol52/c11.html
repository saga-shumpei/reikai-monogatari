Chapter 11: 千代の菊 [1347]

千代の菊〔1347〕

§ 1

Okiku sings:

Okiku: 'The peach blossoms of Sanguwatsumitsuka / Scatter across the garden of Kogitayama / As spring approaches, somehow / Even the voices of the singing Kotori / Sound all the more Nodoka / Shishijifuroku, the chrysanthemum flowers / I, this Okiku, have passed one more / Morning and evening, at the Ōmahe / Joining in the celebration of Bunsuke / The Okina who served so purely / Standing here in this Seijiyō / The Yakuin, Tashiseisei / Without fearing the front, a single word / Of Kototama they perform / Whether the Asahi shines or clouds over / Whether the moon waxes or wanes / Whether Chii or Tenpen occur / If one but entrusts one's Itsushin / To the Kamisama and serves / There is nothing in the world to fear.'

Okiku: 'The resurrection of Bunsuke-sama / The Monogatari of the Reikaimiyage / Hearing it, the Kuōdaimuhen Goshintoku of the Kamisama / Is truly Arigata to behold / O Mamehito, the hundred officials / To sweep away the mud of this world / Descending to Chijiyō, the Ananahi / Oshihe you have opened / Kuniharutachi no Ōkami and / Toyokunihime no Ōkami / The Itsunomitama and / Mizunomitama, who are the incarnations / I revere the teachings morning and evening / Understanding and savoring them in my heart / To fulfill those deeds faithfully / And repay the heart of the Lord / Is our primary duty / Opened by Takahime-tsukasa.'

Okiku: 'The gods of the Uranai-kyō / Are a world of fearsome villains / Possessed by the spirits of the ugly / They revere and respect the ugly path they wrote / More than anything else / Leading the people of the world astray / From Imoriwake and Magahiko / Even to the mother, Otora / Day and night, piling up deep sins / Clouding this world / Yet the Lord of supreme benevolence and love / Looks upon them with a broad heart / And will surely forgive, Kannagara / The heart of the Lord is clearly / Known as if held in the hand / Bunsuke-sama is a good example / Addicted to the Uranai-kyō / Bearing the thousand-fold burden of the Oki-do / To purify this defiled world / The Lord Susanoo no Ōkami.'

Okiku: 'While slandering Him as an evil demon and wicked god / They have transmitted the teaching / If Bunsuke were to leave this world / He would instantly fall into the path of infinite hell / The sins of having turned against the Lord / Would be counted by the Meikan / And he would fall into a miserable fate / Fearing that this is a grave matter / I prayed morning and evening / To save the sins of Imoriwake, Magahiko / And the mother / Yet, how could the heart of the Ōkami / Be measured or known by us people? / If one repents and returns to the Great Path / The Ōkami will surely forgive / Leading one to an infinite paradise / And granting a life of perfect fulfillment.'

Okiku: 'Truly, I have understood with gratitude / Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami / For the sake of the Lord and for the sake of the world / From now on, I shall tighten my sash / Forgetting the petty desires / Of this world full of disasters / Not drowning in water, not burning in fire / Treasures that do not rust, decay, or rot / Stored in the heaven of Takamagahara / To live an eternal life after death / I, this Okiku, have made my resolve / The sky of my heart has cleared / The sun and moon shine, and the glittering stars / Flash upon my spiritual body / I have become a body that knows / Joy and ecstasy beyond compare / Ah, Kannagara, Kami, Kami / The sins we have committed / Before the Lord / I repent with reverence and awe.'

Ochiyo sings again:

Ochiyo: 'With the air of eternal spring / The mountains in all directions are green / The present world has been resurrected / Flowers bloom and scent, butterflies dance / The joy of the Kotori singing / The sacred ground of the mountain of Koki / Once, by the winter gale / Blown, the light of the Law was extinguished / And it had already become dangerous / But Matsuhime-tsukasa appeared / Morning and evening, with sincerity / Devoting herself, the path of the Lord / While she was serving / The sacred domain that Imoriwake and Magahiko / Had trampled / Gradually bloomed again here / And became somewhat lively.'

Ochiyo: 'At this time, Matsuhiko-kamitsukasa / Beginning with Gosan-kō-sama / Aku, Taku, Teku, and the Mankō-shi / Led them here and stood them up / On the path of the Ananahi teaching / The world of fearsome villains / The ugly demons were driven out / Enshrining the true Ananahi / Of sincerity alone / Offering a heart of sincerity / That is righteous, pure, and very red / It was worth serving / Now, at last, the season of spring / Where plum blossoms bloom has passed / The Yayoi sky where a hundred flowers bloom / The grass grows lush and green / It has become a spring of eternal life.'

Ochiyo: 'Bunsuke, who drew / Kabu, Daikon, Kurohebi / And a hundred other portraits / And distributed them to the Mamehito in all directions / Has finally opened his eyes here / The Itsunomitama and Mizunomitama.'

The Keirin of the warp and weft, / Having been made to realize, / The Henkefushin of until now, / Is Tatenaho. / The Asahiko shining on the Atari, / The Hinodewake and Konohanahime, / Having truly understood the Oshihe of Kami, / The greatness of having banished, / The Nisegami of Hinodewake, / Who called himself Giritenjiyō. / The Hotsukinin of the Uranai faith, / Takahimetsukasa appeared, / And with Eugenbau no Mokusuke, / While establishing a Honkiyo in Koko, / Planning to raise a Hitohata, / When they tried to persuade the Tsukasara, / With clever Kotoba, / By the Mihikari of Sumeōkami, / Their crooked hearts were Shōha, / And at that Totan, / They fell from the Danganjiyō.

While the two were nursing their wounds, / They stuffed the treasure of the magician, / The Kuruma no Tama, into the inner pocket of Bunsuke, / And fled like white waves. / Caught by small desires, / And having turned their backs on Kami, the two, Hatsu and Toku, / While following behind, / Their farce was overdone, / Their posteriors were torn and bleeding, / And enduring the pain in their feet, / They followed along step by step. / Bunsuke, who remained behind, / Gazing at the Kuruma no Tama, / That remained in his bosom, / Fearing it as the Bunbun-dama, / He sealed it tightly in a small box, / And while he was guarding it, / Hatsu-san and Toku-san returned, / And to steal the Kuruma no Tama,

They struck Bunsuke-sama, / And taking advantage of the moment he fell, / They fled away with a hateful air. / From that day on, Bunsuke-sama, / Fell into a state of unconsciousness, / And began to babble, / Things that made no sense, which was sad. / To such a place, the Tsukasara of the faith of Sango, Iku and Saru, / Manifesting the divine power, / Of the Yōkō no Tama bestowed by Hinodewake, / Completely revived Bunsuke. / Bunsuke-sama wandered in the Reikai, / And the secrets that even in the dreams of Genkai-jin, / Could not be understood, / He witnessed in detail and in full, / And the preciousness of him conveying, / The outline before us.

From the point of understanding the state of the Reikai so clearly, / Should we not further wash and purify our hearts, / Improve our daily conduct, / And serve so as to fulfill the heart of Kami? / When I think and think, the world of men, / Is truly a miserable thing, / But the life after death is surely, / A paradise filled with glory, / And one can be saved into the heaven of perfect fulfillment, / And be able to lead an eternal, / Pure and righteous life. / Respect and love Kami, / Do not go against the heart of Kami, / Work and strive in good deeds for the sake of the world, / And for the purpose of the Kami-kai, / Which has sent people down to this world,

Serve so as to fulfill that purpose, O hundred people. / Together with us, make a vow before the Great Presence, / And pray earnestly for the happiness of your bodies. / Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemae, / Grant us thy divine grace. / Even if the morning sun shines or clouds over, / Even if the moon waxes or wanes, / Even if natural disasters occur, / As long as Kami is in this world, / If you uphold sincerity alone, / You shall surely be saved. / We are the children of Kami, the shrines of Kami, / Because we are more wonderful and precious, / Than all things in the world, / Keep the order of Kami well, / And immersed in the full virtue of love and faith, / Become the flowers of this world, / Become the light, and become the salt.

Washing away the impurities and driving away the demons, / Praised as the flowers of heaven and earth, / It is a joy to fulfill the duty of being human. / We who have grown up in the Kamikuni, / Are truly the most fortunate of beings. / Look up and revere, O children of Kami, / Go forth courageously on the path of virtue. / Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemae, / May your spirits be blessed.

Besides this, there are countless celebratory songs of the Tsukasara of Kami and the believers, but they are omitted here. Now, Bunsuke was toasted by many people, and since he could not refuse their kindness, he felt a slight headache; being originally a non-drinker, he became intoxicated at once, tripped on the stairs, fell to the ground, and once again fell into a state of unconsciousness. Here, Matsuhime and the others suddenly sobered up from the celebratory sake, and decided to perform Misogi in the Kajika River to pray for the recovery of Bunsuke's illness. The voices of the earnest prayers of the many officers and believers seemed to resonate to the nine heavens and shake the mountains.

此外神の司等並に信徒の祝歌は数限りなくあれども此処には省略する。扨て文助は数多の人々に盃をさされ、折角の志を受けぬ訳にも行かぬので少しく頭の痛む身に、元来下戸の事とて忽ち酩酊し階段を踏み外して地上に顛落し、又もや人事不省に陥つた。ここに松姫外一同は忽ち祝酒の酔も醒め、河鹿川に禊して文助の病気平癒を祈る事となつた。数多の役員信者の熱心なる祈願の声は九天に響き山岳も揺ぐばかりに思はれた。
