Chapter 10: Spirit 界土産 [1346]

霊界土産〔1346〕

§ 1

At the Shinden of Kogitayama, a grand Saiten was held to express Shiyukui for the Sosei of Bunsuke, and a Nahorahi banquet was prepared. Matsuhime and other general Yakuin and Shinjiya gathered at the Ōhiromahe, and the Shinjiya who had been Sanrō, wishing to partake in the Kuōdaimuhen Shintoku bestowed upon Bunsuke by Kami, came from their respective Shiyukushiya and became intoxicated with the Miki of Kuwanki. Bunsuke began to sing slowly.

Infinite, absolute, beginningless, and endless / Transcending life and death / The Sumeōkami who created this world / Through the Shintoku of the Sumeōkami / The Hitogusa born into this world / Are all children of Kami, shrines of Kami / How noble it is to live on / Through both the manifest and hidden worlds / Maintaining Eienmukiu life / I, once pulled by the cord of grace / Of the Ōkami / By chance, entered the spirit world / Without knowing it / Mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees / Were no different from the reality of this world / As I walked upon the earth / I knew not at all / That my own body had once died / Thinking of this, the body of man / As is taught by Kami / Is a spiritual being / Known to flourish forever / In the land of Kami / As immortal and ageless / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara / The joy of having once / Traveled to the land of Kami / Even when awakened and in this world / I cannot easily forget / The light of the truly joyful spirit world / Is still manifest now / It is as if I am placing my body / In the divine world of Takama.

Through the care of Matsuhime-tsukasa and other / Hundred Tsukasa / I returned once again to this world / And when I observed the state of the four quarters / I felt deeply / That this world is truly the Shaba-sekai / A place where those who wander / Are stained with sin / When I reached the spirit world / Even we, whose eyes could not see / Witnessed all things clearly / Without a single corner left unseen / My mood was especially pleasant / And I walked the path with a light body / I knew not at all / The pain of walking / As one does in the world on earth / When people of the manifest world are in a hurry / And walk the path with quickened feet / Their breathing inevitably becomes labored / The beating of their hearts suddenly / Becomes intense and their breath is stifled / Their throats become parched and sweat emerges / And though they feel the suffering / Of tired feet, the journey / In the world of Kami is the opposite / I proceeded just as I wished / With ease and without any suffering / Truly, this world is a world of suffering / The saying that it is a world of defilement to be shunned / I deeply realized / Was a misconception / That I had once thought / Was merely a convenient means of the sages.

The land where Kami dwells / Is a paradise without even a speck / Of hatred, jealousy, or ugly deeds / It is filled with the virtues of love and goodness / And shines with the light / Of faith and truth / It is like the eternal spring of Tokoyo / Where one knows not of days or lands / Thinking of this, the Keirin of the Ōkami / Whose benevolence is infinite / Has no room for doubt / O believers and Tsukasa / Who gather before this great presence / When people are in this world / Follow the times and seasons / And keep the laws of the country well / Clarify, realize, and practice / The great path of the Five Constants and Five Relationships.

Understand the divinity / Of the Ōkami who opened / The first and foremost land of Kami / Accumulate the virtues of goodness and truth / And be filled with the beautiful / Wisdom and enlightenment that come from Kami / Pass through the life of this fleeting world / In a state of perfection / Prostrate yourselves before the Ōkami / For all your sins / And repent them all / To prepare for opening the gates / Of the heavenly kingdom / This Bunsuke says, and furthermore / May everyone be of one heart / Restrain your conduct / And for the sake of the land of Kami / Pray to the great presence / To be endowed with the divine power / To fulfill the great path of our San-Go.

Pray, O hundred people, pray / This is the story / That Bunsuke personally saw, heard, and / Experimented with / Upon reaching the spirit world / I tell it as a souvenir story / To the people who have gathered / For the ritual of my return from the land of the dead / Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara / May your spirits be blessed / Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over / Whether the moon waxes or wanes / Even if the earth should sink / The land of Kami will not move in the slightest / If you place your body / In the constant faith and joy of the eternal / Is there anything / That you would need to fear? / Reflect, O hundred people / Both I and you, let us be restrained / And in order to repay the grace / Of being born in this land of Kami / With all our hearts and all our bodies / We must offer our sincerity.

Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara / I finish stating here / With reverence / A portion of what Bunsuke / Saw and heard / Before the presence of Kami / Ah, how grateful, how grateful / The grace of Kami that knows no bounds / The divine majesty that knows no end.

原文（近代文語）

小北山の神殿にては、文助が蘇生したる其祝意を表する為に、盛大なる祭典を行ひ、且直会の宴を張つた。松姫を始め其他一般の役員信者は大広前に集まつて、文助が神より与へられたる広大無辺の神徳にあやからむと参籠せる信者は各宿舎より来つて歓喜の神酒に酔うた。文助はソロソロ歌ひ出した。

『無限絶対無始無終 生死の上に超越し 此世を造り給ひたる 皇大神の神徳に 生れ出でたる人草は 何れも神の子神の宮 永遠無窮の生命を 保ちて顕幽両界に 生き通し行く尊さよ われは一度大神の 恵の綱にあやつられ ふとした事より霊界に 知らず知らずに突入し 山河草木悉く 現実界に変りなく 大地の上を歩みつつ 吾身の嘗て死去したる 事は一つも知らざりき 之を思へば人の身は 神の教にある如く

目かひの見えぬ吾々も すべての物をありありと 残る隈なく目撃し 殊更気分も麗しく 身も軽々と道を行く 地上の世界を行く如き 苦痛は少しも知らざりき 現界人は気を急ぎ 足を早めて道行けば 必ず呼吸切迫し 心臓の鼓動忽ちに 烈しくなりて息塞り 喉は渇き汗は出で 足は疲れて苦しさを 覚ゆるなれど神界の 旅行は之に相反し 何の苦もなく易々と 思ひの儘に進みけり 実にも此世は苦の世界 厭離穢土ぞと言ふことは

只聖人の方便と 思ひそめしは誤謬と 深くも感得したりけり 抑も神の坐す国は 恨み嫉みも醜業も 塵ほどもなきパラダイス 愛と善との徳に充ち 信と真との光明に 輝き渡り日限も 土地さへ知らぬ長閑なる 常世の春の如くなり 之を思へば大神の 仁慈無限の御経綸 ゆめゆめ疑ふ余地もなし 此大前に参集ふ 信徒等よ司等 人の此世にある時は 時世時節に従ひて 国の掟をよく守り 五倫五常の大道を

明め悟り実行し 最第一の神の国 開き給ひし大神の 其神格を理解して 善と真との徳を積み 神より来る美はしき 智慧証覚に充たされて 仮の浮世の生涯を 完全無欠に相送り 凡ての罪を大神の 御前にひれ伏し悉く 悔い改めて天国の 門戸を開く準備をば 此文助は云ふも更 皆さま心を一つにし 身の行ひを慎みて 神の御国の御為に 吾三五の大道を 尽しまつらむ神力を 具備させ給へと大前に

祈れよ祈れ百の人 これ文助が霊界に 至りて親しく見聞し 実験したる物語 黄泉路帰りの礼祭に 集ひ給ひし人々に 土産話と述べておく ああ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ 朝日は照るとも曇るとも 月は盈つとも虧くるとも 仮令大地は沈むとも 少しも動かぬ神の国 常住不断の信楽に 身をおくならば何事も 恐るることやあらざらむ 省み給へ百の人 われ人ともに慎みて 此神国に生れたる 恵に報いまつるべく

心の限り身のきはみ 誠を捧げまつるべし ああ惟神々々 神の御前に文助が 見聞したる一端を 此処に謹み述べ終る ああ有難し有難し 限りも知らぬ神の恩 果てしも知らぬ御稜威』

§ 2

Having finished the song, he expressed his gratitude to all present for the various kind care he had received during his state of suspended animation, briefly described his future policy for serving the Kami, and took his seat. Next, Matsuhime sang.

Matsuhime: 'Having served well the Uranai-kyō opened by Takahimetsukasa, and having yearned for the doctrine of Shiri-metsuretsu as the Great Way of Shizen-shibi, Bunsuke-sama, the receptionist, has at last been illuminated by the Mihikari of Ananai-kyō, understood the sincere heart of the Ōkami, and become a true believer who serves the Shinden most faithfully morning and evening. The Kami, cherishing such a noble Mahito, have sent him back to this world once more, and it is a matter of awe to contemplate the divine will that he should complete the divine work left in the Genjitsukai and return to report before the Kami.

The Kami Naohi who created this world, and the Ōnaohi whose heart is vast; in all things in the world of men, look upon them with Naohi and listen with Naohi. The edict of Zengen-bishiku, which contains the profound and subtle truth, teaches us to repent for the faults of our bodies, and how grateful we are for it. Hatsukō and Tokukō, both of them, were deceived by the ugly Magatsuhi of Yōgenbō and Takahime, and they struck and tormented this Mahito, who served the Ōkami most faithfully morning and evening, until he reached a state of suspended animation; yet, when one reflects upon the truth of this, it is entirely an inscrutable divine Keirin of the Kamikai.

Bunsuke-sama, for that very reason, must have been guided to explore even the truth of the Reikai, which one would never have dared to hope for, and to return to this world once again to guide the people of the world. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase. If one only leaves all things to the Kami, there will be no harm. No matter how clever a person may be, as long as one lives in the Busshitsukai, one cannot understand the deep truth of the Yūgen-bimyo Kamikai. It is useless for a humble and weak human body to fret, no matter how much one tries to fathom the truth. As I listen to the story of Bunsuke-sama, it strikes my heart deeply, and my soul feels as if it has suddenly ascended, and I am filled with a heart of joy, having clearly realized the state of the Kami no Mikuni.

From now on, Matsuhime shall make Bunsuke-sama her teacher and father, cast aside all attachments, and serve most faithfully. Please forgive me, Mahito. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, the Kami no Kuni does not move in the slightest. It is not something that the people of the Genjitsukai can fathom. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase. Since we have no other means than to proceed according to the will of the Kami, we have no choice. After Hatsuwakahime no Kamitsukasa had explained most earnestly the state of the Tengoku-jōdo, the Jigokudō, and the Chūyūkai, and had returned,

Bunsuke-sama was revived, and Mahito appeared on his right and left, and how grateful I am that they taught the truth of the Reikai in detail. Ah, all people, all people. As long as you have life in this world, draw near to the Kami, love the Kami, accumulate the virtues of Zen and Shin, live as a model for this world, and in order to serve as a divine messenger after death, listen silently to the teachings of Ananai-kyō, and do not mistake your way of life. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase. I humbly offer my gratitude before the Kami for this blessing.'

Iku stood up and began to sing.

Iku: 'Ah, how grateful, how grateful. We, who have now been clearly shown and taught the profound and subtle Keirin of the Kamikai, which we never expected, are indeed the most fortunate of people as those living in this world. As I listen to the story of Bunsuke-sama and the teachings of Matsuhime-sama, my heart is somehow emboldened and my arms tingle, and I feel as if I cannot remain idle for even a moment. Suddenly, the blood in my whole body surges, and tears of joy overflow from my heart.

Yet, Hatsuwakahime no Kamitsukasa, whom I relied upon as my life and my staff, leaving the commotion of the night behind and departing—what does this mean? There is a point I cannot understand, that such a gentle Kami-hito would leave Bunsuke-sama's crisis behind and return. Although I say this, we are foolish people who cannot see what lies ahead. I shall resign myself, thinking that the heart of the princess, who is endowed with the virtues of love and faith, which excel in wisdom and enlightenment, is not something that can be fathomed by our limited, shallow intelligence, and I will say nothing more about this. Nevertheless, I must carry through my original intention to the end. Even if I should fall into Jigoku by going against Hatsuwakahime, I shall be a sincere Masurao who devotes himself for the sake of the Kami and for the sake of the world.'

と歌ひ了り、一同に向つて自分が仮死中種々親切な介抱に預かつたことを感謝し、且将来の自分の神に仕ふる方針に就いて略叙し自席に着いた。次に松姫は歌ふ。

『高姫司の開きたる ウラナイ教によく仕へ 支離滅裂の教義をば 至善至美なる大道と 渇仰したる受付の 文助さまも漸くに 三五教の御光に 照らされ給ひ大神の 誠の心を理解して 朝な夕なに神殿に いと忠実に仕へたる 誠の信者となり給ふ かかる尊き真人を 惜しみ給ひて神々は 再び此世に追ひ返し 現実界に残したる 其神業を完成し 神の御前に復命 申させ給はむ御心 仰ぐも畏き次第なり

イクは立上つて歌ひ出した。

『ああ有難し有難し 思ひ掛なき神界の 深遠微妙の経綸を 今目のあたり明かに 説き示されし吾々は 此世の中の人として いと幸福の者ぞかし 文助さまの物語 松姫さまの御教訓 聞くにつけても何となく 心は勇み腕は鳴り 只一刻もグヅグヅと して居れないよな心持 俄に湧き出し全身の 血は漲りて歓楽の 涙は胸に溢れけり さはさりながら命とも 柱杖とも頼みてし 初稚姫の神司 夜前の騒ぎを他所にして

Kami shall surely save you, Matsuhime-sama, Okiku-sama, And all the other hundred officials, For your great Sewa I am grateful. From here I go to Kogitayama, To offer Hairei before the presence of Kami, And whipping my Kurige of the knee, I entrust my body to the Tokukifuretsushiya. Like an arrow shot, I must follow, And pursue your Onato without delay. Do not laugh at me, I pray, Calling me a man of strong Gaman. Though I have been called until now, A Chiyototsumushiya of the Brahman army, A man who does not even turn his head, While protecting the Yakuō jewel, I shall tread through the darkness of Tokoyo, Illuminating with the light of Kami, The Hitobito suffering on the floating shoals, Continuing my Shiyashin Kuwatsudō. Successfully facing Haruna, Participating in the Daishingefu, And returning to report at the Iso mansion, When I shall speak, at the Kogitayama, Visiting the Daishinden, The Ohanashi piled up like mountains, Before the presence of you Minamina-sama, The time to Maoshia is truly, What I look forward to with joy from now. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, May your spirits be blessed.'

Having finished singing, he urged Saru and, already leaving this place, attempted to pursue Hatsuwakahime. Matsuhime exhausted every possible word to stop the departure of Iku and Saru, and tried to persuade them by embodying the will of Hatsuwakahime. However, how could a hasty man, a wild warrior, incline his ears to such words? Together with Saru, he offered Hairei to Kogitayama, and like a single-plank bridge crossing the heart of pure goodness, the two of them, with their strange attire, moved forward, aiming their steps toward Ukikigahara.

首尾よくハルナに立向ひ 大神業に参加して 斎苑の館に復命 申さむ折は小北山 大神殿に参詣で 山と積れる御話を 皆々さまの御前に 申上ぐべき時こそは 今より楽しみ待たれける ああ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ』

と歌ひ了り、サールを促して早くも此場を立出で、初稚姫の後を追はむとした。松姫は百方言葉を尽して、イク、サールの出立を止むべく、初稚姫の意を体して説き諭した。されどはやり男の猪武者、いかでか其言葉に耳を傾くべき。サールと共に小北山を拝礼し、善一筋の心を渡す一本橋、二人の身なりも怪シの森、運ぶ歩みも浮木ケ原を指して進み行く。
