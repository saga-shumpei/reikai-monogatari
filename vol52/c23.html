Chapter 23: 盲動 [1359]

盲動〔1359〕

§ 1

At the Yachimata Sekishiyo, which manifested the season of autumnal shigure where one moment it would rain and the next it would clear up, Bunsuke sat upon a low bench, listening with a thumping heart to the judgment of the amata spirits passing through this Kuwanmon. There arrived an old woman with a haughty expression, her face caked in oshiroi. Bunsuke, thinking what a fushigi creature had appeared and that her judgment would surely be omoshiro, waited with some kiyōmi.

She appeared to be a spirit possessing a nikutai, approaching with a swaying, unsteady gait while looking slightly downward. She bumped squarely into the gate of the Sekishiyo, struck her forehead, and exclaimed:

Takahime: 'Aitata! To build an Akamon in such a place without notice and make a passerby strike her head is beyond the pale. It is a great futsugafu that you would do such a thing when the Giritenjiyō-sama of Hinodewake is passing through... Hey, you appear to be the Monban here, why do you not take your shiyokumu seriously? If you continue such taida behavior, Hinodewake will not shiyōchi it.'

She spoke with a fierce kenmaku. Bunsuke, hearing the name Hinodewake, looked closely and realized it was Takahime. Takahime had been snatched away by Eugenbau, and while flying through the air, she had been dropped by Eugenbau at a certain point on the Dekatan Plateau, falling from the sky onto a sandy field where she had fainted. In the meantime, her spirit had wandered to this place. However, Takahime was not in the least aware that she had lost her sanity or that she had come to the Reikai, and she felt as if she were still walking in the Genkai.

The red guard shouted in a loud voice:

Red Guard: 'Takahime, wait a moment, there is an investigation to be conducted.'

Takahime: 'Hen! It is laughable for a mere Monban to investigate the Giritenjiyō Hinodewake-sama. There is something I must investigate from this side instead. Where have you hidden the Kami of Tokiokishi, one of the three crows of the San-go-kyo? Come, confess clearly. If you dally, I shall cast you not only into the Yachimata of Heaven, but into the very bottom of the cauldron of Hell.'

Red Guard: 'You are a ghost who was dropped from the air by a demon called Eugenbau on the Dekatan Plateau, fainted, and arrived here. Once you have come here, you must follow the rules of the Meido. As I am about to examine your crimes, you had best confess everything without hiding a thing.'

Takahime: 'Ohohoho, what is with that solemn face of yours, as if a single koku of rice cost a hundred ryo? It seems you too have been struck by the difficulty of life lately and find your accounting painful. If you follow the Giritenjiyō-sama of Hinodewake, there will be no recession or worries in this world. It is I, the living shrine Takahime-sama of Hinodewake, the savior of the three thousand worlds. My, my, there are so many pitiful people in the world. That is why they say that unless the restoration of the Genkai, Yūkai, and Shinkai is carried out as soon as possible, the Miroku Shinsei will not be achieved. Ah, the Hinodewake-sama who must save the people of the world, and this nikutai-miya of Takahime, it is no ordinary task. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.'

The red guard, finding Takahime's derailment too intense to investigate, and knowing she was not dead in the Seishibo and would return to the Genkai before long, thought it best to dismiss her and send her back without a serious investigation, and said:

Red Guard: 'Oi, Takahime, what do you think this place is?'

Takahime: 'Hen, do not say things like preaching to Buddha. There is a limit to how much you can make a fool of someone. Is this not about one ri before the Daimon Shrine? You must be the kenzoku of the Yakuzagami of Susanoo-no-Mikoto. Rather than standing stiffly in a place like this, why not listen to the teachings of this Giritenjiyō nikutai-miya and serve the purpose of opening the great gate? I shall tell you something splendid.'

§ 2

Bunsuke pulled the sleeve of Takahime and said,

Bunsuke: 'Excuse me, Takahime-sama, I have met you in such an unusual place. I am Bunsuke of the Ananai-kyō.'

Takahime: 'Oh, I thought there was a strange man here for some time, and it is you, Bunsuke. But my, you have such a lonely face; what are you doing in a place like this? Come, Bunsuke-don, follow me. I shall let you hear the true essence of the Uranai-kyō. What could such red-faced or blue-gourd-faced ones possibly know? It is I, this Takahime, who has grasped the root of the root of the origin of the world. How could gods who sprang up midway, or those with high noses whose wisdom is merely a product of learning, understand the truth? ...If you wish to hear, come and visit. When the Kami appears in the front, becoming the Giritenjiyō Hinodewake, Takamiyahimenomikoto, I shall explain everything about the world to you. ...Hmph, do you think a midway high-nose like you, who is acting as a gatekeeper, could possibly understand the truth of the Shinkai? Come, come, Bunsuke-don, follow me.'

Akazura: 'Takahime, it is a little too early for you to come here. From now, return to the Genkai, fully display your mad behavior, and you will realize it only after you are unable to move hand or foot. Only then will you understand the preciousness of the Ananai-kyō and the Mikokoro of Susanōnomikoto-sama. It hinders my work, so leave this place at once.'

Takahime: 'Hmph, Akazura-sama, it must be inconvenient for you that I am here. Haha, this is, as I suspected, the gateway to the Ananai-kyō after all. You must have successfully dragged the Kami of Tokiokishi into this. I will not move, not even if you use force. Come, come, quickly bring the Kami of Tokiokishi out here.'

Akazura: 'The Kami of Tokiokishi is in charge of the general affairs of the Saien mansion. Since he is still in the Genkai, there is no way he would come here. My, what an ignorant thing you are.'

Takahime: 'Hmph, you speak so smoothly, ho ho ho ho ho. It seems you have taken the evil teachings of the hensei-joshi into your belly, as you lie so skillfully. I am not a Giritenjiyō who would be deceived by such things, Akazura-sama.'

She narrowed her eyes, jutted out her chin, and mocked him.

Bunsuke: 'Excuse me, Takahime-sama, this is the barrier of the Yatsumado of the Meido. This is certainly not the Genkai, so you should not say such things. Come, return at once. Then, apologize to the Ananai-kyō, return to a sincere soul, and pray so that you may ascend to heaven anew.'

Takahime: 'Oh my, Bunsuke-don, you are playing dumb. It seems that the spirit of the hensei-joshi has possessed you quite thoroughly. It is a path where not a single lie can be told. The path of Ananai, solidified with lies, oh ho ho ho ho, I, Takahime, am truly impressed. Since your eyes are bad, you must be dreaming. Why don't you pull yourself together?'

She slapped his face repeatedly. Bunsuke, feeling a little offended, said,

Bunsuke: 'Now, Takahime, I have explained things to you this much, yet you still do not understand? Why do you not follow the words of the official and return? What is with that white powder on your wrinkled face? Is this not the behavior of a complete madman?'

Takahime: 'Hmph, no need for your concern. Even like this, since To-sama says it is fine, I do not need to be seen by blind people like you. Come, I will now step into the inner sanctum, meet To-sama, and whether you like it or not, I will take you back to the Uranai-kyō. Even though I look like this, this Takahime is different from before. As the wife of Takamiyahiko, the lord of the Kuruwa castle, I am the living shrine of the Hinodewake. Without saying such things, just once, follow me to the Kuruwa castle in the Ukiki forest. Even you would be surprised if you saw that palace. Having learned the art of flying high in the mid-heaven through the Kuruwa law of divine wonders, there is no one in the world that this Takahime fears anymore. Please associate with me with that in mind.'

Takahime, seeing this figure, rejoiced greatly,

Takahime: 'Hohohohoho, what a feat, what a feat! Mokusuke-sama, how on earth did you become such a great person? To be able to use such a Mauaku Karajishi with Jiiujizai, you are indeed my husband. Hey, Bunsuke-dono, look at that! Since he can perform such a feat through the Hofuriki of the Magawa, the Uranai-kyō must be something great, must it not? Can even one person from that Ananai-kyō do such a thing? I would love to show Hatsuwakahime, Harukuniwake, Kotoyoriwake, and Tōsuke the Atsupare Mushiyaburi of Mokusuke-sama. Ehehehehe, Namu Mokusuke Daimiyōjin-sama.'

Such was the Oka of her pressing her hands together and bowing. Mokusuke leaped lightly from the back of the Shishi, and without even glancing at Takahime, he turned to the red Shiyuei,

Mokusuke: 'I am sorry for the trouble with your duties. Please convey that Mokusuke of the Ananai-kyō requests an audience with Ibukido-nushi-no-kami-sama.'

The red Shiyuei bowed his waist many times, and while showing his respect, he ran quickly into the gate. Takahime: 'Hey, Mokusuke-sama, stop your jesting at a reasonable point. After being thrown out by Tōsuke of the Saien mansion, it is filthy to say such things as Mokusuke of the Ananai-kyō. Come, let us go home together.'

'Yes, Arigata-gozaimasu. I ask that you keep this lion in your Oazuka for a while.'

She attempts to walk ahead. Mokusuke follows behind and begins to pass through the gate. Takahime clings to his sleeve, lets out a Kanakirigoe, and with Namidamaji, says:

'Hey, Mokusukesama, is this not too much? This is the Bashiyo where the Yatsura of the Ananai-kyō gather. Why, after making such a firm Yakusoku, do you commit Henshin now? Do you not fear the Giritenjiyō Hinodewake?'

'Takahime-sama, I am going to meet Ibukidonushisama by my own Kenri. Anata, please return from here.'

He attempts to leave. Takahime bites onto his sleeve and will not let go, saying:

'No, no, no matter what you Otsushiya, as long as the eyes of this Takahime are black, I shall not let you pass through the gate of the Ananai-kyō, not even by Hitoashi. An-an-an-an-an, a man's heart is like the autumn sky; even if they say it changes, this is too much. A-e, Zannen, Zannen, kuku, Kuchio.'

'Ahahahaha, what an Omoshiro Shibai I have been shown. Ah, Bunsuke-dono, follow behind me.'

As he says this, he kicks out, and Takahime involuntarily releases his hem, rolls two or three times like a ball along the Kaidau, and at its Shiuten, falls flat, spread out in the shape of a large character.

Mokusuke and Bunsuke shut the gate with a clatter and hide their figures in the inner garden. Takahime, lying in the shape of a large character, moves her hands and feet while shouting:

'This woman, if one traces her origin, is the reincarnation of Tokoyohime-no-Mikoto, who was born bearing the body of a man in woman's clothing, Takamiyahime. Since I was young, I was a mischievous girl nicknamed 'man-woman'; once I became husband and wife with Tōsuke-sama and even bore a child, but because Tōsuke's heart was too heartless and cruel, I gave him a clean break at the Saien mansion, returned to the forest of the shrine, became husband and wife with Mokusuke-sama, and now I have built a Kuruwa castle in the forest of Ukiki, changed my name to Takamiyahime, and am the divine pillar of the Uranai-kyō; just wait and see what happens.'

The spirits coming to the Yachimata hear this voice, and each hurries their steps, rushing over in a scattered crowd.
