Chapter 20: 険学 [1356]

険学〔1356〕

§ 1

The four, while inwardly struck with awe at the gigantic figure of Takamiyahiko, who was a Henge of Eugenbau, prayed deeply in their hearts to Kami, asking that if any harm were to befall them, they might be saved quickly. Eugenbau laughed aloud, 'Kara-kara'.

Eugenbau: 'You must be Gariya and Kēsu, who served close to the side of General Katahikoshiyōgun, a subordinate of Ōkuronushi of the capital of Haruna. And you two are Hatsukō and Tokukō; you are both fellows who are quite cunning when it comes to evil deeds. You may be submitting to the Ananahi-no-Oshihe as if you were virtuous now, but you are the type who would reverse back to the evil path as soon as the wind blows. What spineless cowards you are, ah-hahahaha.'

Gariya, mocked in this way, became fired up, clenched his fists, and grinding his teeth, spoke: 'I am indeed Gariya, who served in the Brahman army. However, I am not such a spineless coward as to ever change my heart. Once one has realized the true path, the Sendenshi are more noble than any general or castle lord. Although the majestic Ōkuronushi may berate his three armies, hold the power of life and death, look down upon the world, construct golden palaces and jade towers in the capital of Haruna, and build fortresses to stand as impregnable iron walls, what does such a thing matter to me? The castle towers and corner turrets that soar into the heavens, or the royal palaces paved with marble—such things will soon be reduced to a mess. And their remains will turn into fields of desolate weeds, left to grow over the eaves with creeping vines; it is plain to see that they will fall into a miserable fate. Just so, this Takamiyahiko castle will also eventually fall into a fate of decline. What is Takamiyahiko? What is so great about the lord of a castle? Other than faith itself, which possesses eternal and indestructible life force through the virtue of Aizen and the light of Shinjitsu, there is nothing in this world that is truly noble. What is the use of things that perish in less than ten years, which clever fools and vulgar men of the world call their life's work, political power, profit, or stocks? Because we have realized this truth, we have abandoned the Brahman military register and followed the path of faith to enter into eternal and indestructible life. What is this, Takamiyahiko? Is it not improper to repair the camp of us former Brahman soldiers and occupy it without a word? Come, tell me, for whom did you build such a magnificent castle? I would like to hear your answer.'

With his fear gone somewhere, he swung his arms, his courage increased a hundredfold, and he began to chatter incessantly. Eugenbau opened his mouth wide and laughed heartily.

Eugenbau: 'Ah-hahahaha, do not strike, do not strike; what is the use of talking back? Fifty now is better than a hundred at the end; it is fine for humans to live boldly and briefly. Hey, you lot, you have come into my castle and been struck by its majesty; you should study a little about how magnificent, beautiful, and gorgeous the material aspect is. Everything is for the sake of observation. How about it? The lord himself says he permits it, so it should be fine, right?'

Gariya: 'Hey, you called Takamiyahiko, to build such a magnificent structure in a mere three or four months is, for Gariya, highly suspicious and beyond my understanding. Furthermore, this forest of Ukiki is a demon's den where monsters and Henge appear and torment travelers, a place beyond description. A fellow who builds a castle in such a place cannot be a mere tanuki; it is time for a clever monster to retreat. Now, move aside, move aside.'

Eugenbau: 'Ah-hahahaha, your suspicion is perfectly reasonable; I am by no means a suspicious person. Originally, I too was a Sendenshi of the Ananahi-no-Oshihe, but due to certain circumstances, I withdrew from the mansion of Saien, changed my name to Takamiyahiko, and reigned here. Once you have submitted to the path of Ananahi, you must pay a visit here at least once. In truth, I am Mokusuke, the Tokioki-shi, who is the father of Hatsuwakahime. How about it? Would you not like to take a rest?'

四人は妖幻坊の変化なる高宮彦の巨大な姿に内心打驚きながら、心に深く神を念じ、吾身に危害の加へらるる事あらば、速に助け給へと祈つてゐた。妖幻坊はカラカラと打笑ひ、

『其方はハルナの都の大黒主が部下、ランチ、片彦将軍の側近く仕へて居つたガリヤ、ケースであらうがな。そして二人は初公、徳公の両人、随分貴様も悪事にかけては抜目のない代物だ。今は殊勝らしく三五の教に帰順してゐるが、一つ風が吹けば、又もや悪道へ逆転致す代物だらう。ても扨ても意気地のないヘゲタレ男だなあ、アハハハハ』

と嘲弄されてガリヤは躍起となり、両手の拳を握り、歯ぎしりをしながら、

『拙者は如何にもバラモン軍に仕へて居つたガリヤである。併しながら決して変心致す様な意気地なしでは厶らぬぞ。誠の道を悟つた上は、将軍よりも城主よりも尊いのは宣伝使だ。堂々たる大黒主が三軍を叱咤し、生殺与奪の権を握つて世界を睥睨し、ハルナの都に金殿玉楼を構へ、城寨を築いて、堅牢無比の鉄壁と構へてゐるなれども、拙者は左様なものが何になるか。天に聳ゆる天主閣や隅櫓、まつた、大理石を以て畳み上げられた王宮、左様なものは今にメチヤ　メチヤになつて了ふであらう。そして其跡は満目荒涼たる雑草の野辺と変じ、八重葎の軒に茂るに任すのみ、果敢なき運命に陥るは目のあたりだ。其如く此高宮城も、やがては凋落の運命に陥るであらう。高宮彦が何だ。曲輪城の城主が何偉い。愛善の徳と信真の光によつて、永久不滅の生命力を有する信仰其ものより外には、世の中に決して尊きものはない筈だ。世の中の利巧な愚物や俗漢が、畢生の事業とか、政権とか、利益とか、株式だとか云つてゐるやうな、十年もたたずに亡びて了ふやうなものが何になるか。吾々は此真理を悟つたが故に、バラモンの軍籍をすてて、永久不滅の生命に入るべく信仰の道を辿つたのだ。何だ高宮彦、吾々元バラモン軍の営所を何時の間にか修繕致し、黙つて占領致すとは不都合ぢやないか。サア、誰にこたへて、斯様な立派な城廓を造つたのだ。返答聞かして貰はうかい』

と何時の間にやら恐怖心は何処へか行つて、腕を打ち振り、勇気百倍して無性矢鱈に喋り出した。妖幻坊は大口をあけて高笑ひ、

『アツハハハハ、叩くな叩くな、へらず口を叩いてそれが何になる。末の百より今の五十、人間は太く短く暮せば可いのだ。コリヤ其方共、吾城内に来つて其荘厳に打たれ、且物質的方面の如何に荘厳優美にして且華美なるかを、チツとは研究したがよからうぞ。何事も見学の為だ。どうだ、城主が直接に許すといふのだから大丈夫だらう』

ガリヤ『ヤア、高宮彦とやら、僅か三四ケ月の間に、斯様な立派な普請をなさるとは、ガリヤに取つては不審千万、合点が参らぬで厶る。そして此浮木の森は妖怪変化出没し、行人を苦しむるや実に名状す可らざる魔窟である。斯様な処に城廓を構へるやうな奴は、只の狸ぢやあるまい、気の利いた化物はすつ込む時分だ、サ、どいたりどいたり』

『アハハハハ、お疑は御尤も千万、拙者は決して怪しき者では厶らぬ。元は拙者も三五教の宣伝使なりしが、思ふ仔細あつて、斎苑の館を脱退し、吾名を高宮彦と改めて、ここに君臨致したものだ。其方も三五の道に帰順した以上は、一度ここへ参拝致さねばなるまい。実の所は、某は初稚姫の父親なる時置師の杢助だ。どうぢや、一度休息して行く気はないか』

§ 2

Gariya: 'Things have come to a point I cannot quite grasp. Ah, but since we have lived in this forest of Ukiki for a while now and have come to know the Chiri well, it is also a diversion to witness the Arisama of this Uitenpen world. In that case, I shall take the liberty of observing it. What do you all think?'

Gariya asked the three. The three bowed their heads in silence, expressing their agreement.

Gariya: 'In that case, Takamiyahiko-dono, Gariya and the rest of us shall trouble you for your hospitality.'

Although he said this firmly with his mouth, he thought in his heart, 'This fellow is undoubtedly the Oyadama of the Bakemono. I shall pretend to be easily deceived by him, and after I have fully considered the Yōsu, I will either make him submit to the Shinriki of Ananai-kyō, or I will destroy him from the Kontei. It is a choice between two options. This must also be some arrangement of the gods...' While nodding to himself in his heart, he followed the words of Yōgenbō with an indifferent air. Yōgenbō suddenly softened his expression and spoke politely,

Yōgenbō: 'Oh, you all, that is the spirit of a Puritan of Ananai. My daughter, Hatsuwakahime, is waiting in the back, so please meet her once. It is not for a parent to praise his own child, but she is truly of heavenly beauty. It is truly a mystery how such a daughter was born to a rugged man like me. This, in short, must be the arrangement of Heaven, ah-ha-ha-ha.'

Gariya: 'What are you saying? Is the famous Hatsuwakahime-sama present? In that case, Gariya would also like to see her by all means.'

Case: 'Is she still single?'

Yōgenbō: 'Yes, she is single. I have prayed morning and evening with a parent's heart, wishing to let her marry if there were a suitable husband. It seems there is one person among you who would not be a disgrace as a husband for Hatsuwakahime. Oh, this must also be because the time of Heaven has come, ah-ha-ha-ha.'

Gariya thought to himself, 'What, you Bakemono, I will not fall for that trick...' but Case and the other two were completely won over. And as they walked along, they smiled at each other, wondering who it was that said there was someone among them who would become the husband of Hatsuwakahime, and thinking, 'Could it perhaps be me?'

Case: 'Eh, if I may, Castle Lord-sama, from Case's eyes, it is unfortunate that a man who would become the husband of Hatsuwakahime-sama is not here. They are all just mediocre men, after all. Perhaps there might be one among them who would pass muster.'

Yōgenbō: 'I would like to make that person my adopted son and have him become the lord of this castle.'

Case: 'Indeed, it is a truly magnificent mansion. When I was serving as an adjutant to the general, the camp was a semi-permanent structure, a miserable and crude thing that was not worth looking at, but your Shinriki is truly great. I never dreamed in my wildest dreams that such a thing would come to pass in such a short time. It is truly magnificent. I would like to have a parent like you, oh-ho-ho-ho.'

Yōgenbō: 'Well, I wonder if there is anyone who would become a child even to a man like me. If Hatsuwakahime sees you, she will surely be pleased to set her eyes on one of you four.'

Case: 'And who is the man who has caught your eye? Is it the G-mark, or H, T, or K? Which one might it be?'

Yōgenbō: 'It would be the person with the K.'

Gariya: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha, the one with the K, that is a good target for the beast-teacher, ha-ha-ha-ha, it is fitting for his spiritual phase, after all.'

He muttered. However, Yōgenbō and the others were so engrossed in the conversation that they did not notice Gariya's whisper.

At last, crossing the wide garden within the castle, which was blooming beautifully with violets, dandelions, and Chinese milk vetch, they passed through countless gates of brilliant gold and arrived at the entrance. Here, tables and chairs decorated with seven treasures were lined up, and white plum blossoms, blooming with rich fragrance, were arranged in large vases. Yōgenbō Takamiyahiko took the lead and went up to the entrance. Eight beautiful women, their faces filled with smiles, took the hands of the four men one by one and led them to their respective living rooms.

『どうも合点の行かぬ事になつて来た。ああ併しながら此浮木の森は吾々が稍暫し住みなれて、地理もよく知り居れば、有為天変の世の有様を目撃するも亦一興、然らば御免を蒙つて拝見さして頂かうかな。各方如何で厶るかな』

とガリヤは三人に問ひかけた。三人は無言のまま首を下げて賛成の意を表した。

『然らば高宮彦殿、ガリヤ以下一同、御世話になりませう』

と口ではキツパリ言ひ放つたものの、心の中で思ふやう、此奴アどうしても妖怪の親玉に相違ない。此方の方から甘く騙されたやうな風を装ひ、スツカリ様子を考へた上、三五教の神力に帰順させるか、但は根底から打ち亡ぼしてやるか二つに一つの思案だ。これも何かの神様のお仕組だらう……と心にうなづきながら、さあらぬ体にて妖幻坊の言葉に従ふ事となつた。妖幻坊は俄に顔色を和らげ、言葉も叮嚀に、

『イヤ各方、それでこそ三五のピユリタンで厶る。拙者の娘初稚姫も奥に控へ居れば、一度は会つてやつて下さい。親の口から褒めるぢやないが、実に天稟の美貌だ。こんな武骨な男に、なぜあんな娘が出来たかと思へば実に不思議だ。之も要するに天の配剤でせう、アハハハハ』

『まだ独身でゐられますかな』

『ハイ、独身者で厶いますよ。どうか適当な夫があれば、持たせたきものと、親心で朝な夕なに祈つて居りました。どうやらここに初稚姫の夫として恥かしからぬ御方が、たつた一人交つて厶るやうだ。イヤ是も天の時節が来たので厶らう、アハハハハ』

ガリヤは何、此妖怪奴、其手は喰はぬぞ……と腹の中できめてゐたが、ケース他二人はスツカリ降参つて了ひ、そして此中に初稚姫の婿となるべき者があると云つたのは誰であらうか、ヒヨツトしたら俺であるまいかなどと、互にニコニコしながら跟いて行く。

ケース『エーもし城主様、初稚姫様の夫になるやうな男は、ケースの眼からは、生憎此処には居らないぢやありませぬか。何れもへボクチヤ男ばかりですからな。此中に一人は、それでも可成り及第する奴があるかも知れませぬな』

『どうか其方を養子となし、ここの城主になつて貰ひたいものだ』

『成程、実に立派なお屋敷で厶いますな。私が将軍の副官をして居つた時にや、半永久的の建物で、見る影もなき粗末至極な陣営でしたが、貴方の御神力は偉いものです。少時の間に斯様な事にならうとは、此ケース、実に夢にも思ひませぬでした。実に立派なもので厶いますワ。私此様な親が持ちたいもので厶います、オホホホホ』

『サ、私のやうな男にでも、子になつてくれる人がありませうかな。初稚姫が見たら、さぞ此四人の中の一人に目をつけて喜ぶ事でせうよ』

『そして貴方のお目に止まつた男といふのは誰で厶いますか。ガ印ですか、但はハかトかケか、どちらで厶いませうな』

『ケのつくお方でせう』

ガリヤは、

『ハハハハ、ケのつく、獣先生にはよい対象だ、ハハハハ、ヤツパリ霊相応かな』

と呟いた。されど妖幻坊も他の連中も、一生懸命に話に実が入つて、ガリヤの囁きに気が付かなんだ。

漸くにして菫、蒲公英、紫雲英などの美しく咲きみちた城内の広庭をよぎりながら、金色燦爛たる隔ての門を幾つともなく潜つて玄関口についた。ここには七宝をもつて飾られたる卓子や椅子が並べられ、大きな瓶に芳香馥郁として咲きみちたる白梅の花が活けられてあつた。妖幻坊の高宮彦は先に立つて、玄関を上り行く。八人の美しい美女は満面に笑を湛へて、四人の手を一本づつ取り、各居間へ導いて行く。
