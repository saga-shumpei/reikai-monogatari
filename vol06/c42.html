Chapter 42: Encounter on the Way [292]

第42章　途上の邂逅〔292〕

§ 1

The missionary of the Ananai-kyo, parting the paths of clouds that rise in the east and the west and advancing onward, having departed from Mount Ogonzan—Hisakatahiko, whose other name is Kumojiwake-no-Kami—continued day and night from afar, singing the missionary song; that which he approached was the capital of Rome, and he advanced heading for Italy, which is governed by Mototeruwake of pure heart.

Sitting down upon a stone by the roadside, gazing at the sky, whenever he looked upon the shifting forms of the passing clouds, he reflected upon his own utterly changed appearance; having abandoned his pitiable wife and children, set upon a long journey without any destination, just as he was letting out a long sigh,

"Kaminaobi, who created this world, Onaobi, whose divine soul is vast, In all matters whatsoever of the human world, Re-examine and re-hear uprightly; Re-confess the transgressions of oneself."

There was a missionary who came singing thus in a clear, cool voice. Kumojiwake-no-Kami pricked up his ears and listened intently to that voice with fond nostalgia.

"Manifesting upon Mount Tenkyozan, Circling around the Tsuitatenomihashira and meeting, Ame-no-Mihashira-no-Okami, Kuni-no-Mihashira-no-Okami, Seeking to illuminate the darkness of this world, With this thought in chest, using the Nakatsu-mikuni as placenta, Forming the lands and giving birth to gods, Offering the breast of blessings Unto the green human grass, unto herbs and trees, And unto all things, and eternally Illuminating, the divine majesty of the three pillars of Deities extends beneath heaven Unto the lands of the four directions; Mitsubahiko, Revering the divine imperial decree, In order to open the Ananai-kyo, In the attire of straw raincoat, hat, Straw sandals, and leggings with heartfelt devotion, Changing his name to Hiromichiwake, Widening the path of the divine teachings; Finding it hard to cross The eight hundred sea-routes of tides, as far as The white clouds lie prostrate, to the extremity Where blue clouds trail heavily, Kumojiwake, The missionary of the noble imperial decree, Where now does he continue His journey of wandering? Here is the border of Italy, And the capital of Rome draws near, Yet the divine minister Mototeruwake Has submitted to the god of the Urals; Ever since the day I heard this, The sky of my heart has clouded over. Amidst the heavily falling rain, Today's journey of Hiromichiwake: For the sake of God and country, Having departed from Mount Ogonzan, Upon this long journey unaccustomed to walking, Exhausted is my form, Exhausted is my form. Looking up at the birds passing through the sky, Each returns on the path toward its home; The birds flying in the sky, seeking shelter In the leafy treetops, Parent and child harmonizing together, Shall enjoy themselves with peace of mind. Unreliable in this human world Is my own life, not knowing its own tomorrow; When I turn over in thought The hearts of my wife and child left in my homeland, I know not whether to advance nor whether to retreat Under the evening sky of an unknown land. Ah, yet even so, Yet even so, reared by the grace of God, We are the precious children of God; God is my father, my mother; This is no lonely solitary journey In the dusk, but advancing together With the divine soul of the Great Kami Whose grace is exceedingly abundant. Precious, holy missionary! The transgressions and sins of the past He uprightly re-examines, re-hears, And re-confesses: Precious indeed is Haniyasu-hiko-no-Mikoto! Precious indeed is Haniyasu-hiko-no-Mikoto!"

Singing thus, he advanced trudgingly under the twilight sky. Kumojiwake-no-Kami, seeing the figure of Hiromichiwake-no-Kami, rejoiced greatly and,

"Ah, Hiromichiwake-no-Kami!"

when he called out thus, the missionary was astonished by these sudden words, and gazing closely, found him to be the missionary Kumojiwake. The two divine ministers joined together here, entered Rome, made Mototeruwake-no-Kami submit, and had him serve the theocracy of Izanagi-no-Mikoto.

Hiromichiwake-no-Kami was the former incarnation of Futodama-no-Mikoto, who, when Amaterasu-Omikami hid within the Heavenly Rock Cave and the universe was plunged into darkness, offered a grand tamagushi before the Rock Cave and consoled the divine mind.

現代日本語訳

東や西に立つ雲の、雲路を分けて進んで来る三五教の宣伝使、黄金山を出立して、久方彦のもう一つの御名である雲路別の天使は、夜昼兼行で遥々と宣伝歌を歌いながら進んで来たのはローマの都であり、心も清らかな元照別の治めるイタリアを目指して進んで行った。

道端の石に腰を掛け、空を眺めて流れて行く雲の変わりゆく姿を見るにつけて、すっかり変わり果てた我が身の姿、いとしい妻子を振り捨てて、どこを当て所とも知れない長い旅路に、長い溜息を漏らす折から、

「この世を造られた神直日よ 御魂も広大な大直日よ ただ何事も人の世は 直日に見直せ、聞き直せ 身の過ちは告白し直せ」

と声も涼やかに歌いながらやって来る宣伝使がいた。雲路別天使は耳をそばだて、その声を懐かしそうに聞き入った。

「天教山に現れ出でて 撞の御柱を巡り合って 天の御柱大御神 国の御柱大御神 この世の闇を照らそうと 思いは胸に、三つ栗の 中津御国を胞衣として 国土を造り、神を生み 青人草や草や木や 万の物に御恵みの 乳房を授け、永遠に 照らし給う三柱の 神の御稜威は天下の 四方の国々、三葉彦は 神の勅命を畏れ敬って 三五教を開こうと 心尽くしの蓑笠や 草鞋、脚絆の身なりで 広道別と名を改めて 神の教えの道を広く 塩の八百路の八潮路を 渡るのも難しく、白雲の 横たわる限り、青雲の たなびく果てまでも、雲路別の 貴い勅命の宣伝使は 今はどこを漂泊の 旅を続けられているのだろうか。 ここはイタリアの国境 ローマの都も近づいたが 元照別の神司が ウラルの神に服従したと 聞いた日からも、我が 心の空はかき曇る。 雨がしきりに降る中を 広道別の今日の旅 神と国とのそのためを思って 黄金山を出立して 歩き慣れない長い旅路に 疲れ果てた我が姿 疲れ果てた我が姿。 空を行く鳥を眺めれば それぞれ我が家への道へと帰って行く。 空を飛ぶ鳥も、生い茂った 梢に宿を求めながら 親も子も共に仲睦まじく寄り添い 心も安らかに楽しむのだろう。 頼みにならないのは人の世の 明日をも知れない我が命である。 故郷に残してきた妻や子の 心をあれこれ思い巡らせば 進むことも、退くことも 分からない国の暮れの空である。 ああ、そうではあるが そうではあるが、神の恵みによって生い育った 我らは尊い神の御子である。 神は我が父であり、我が母である。 夕暮れの寂しい独り身の 旅ではなく、御恵みの まことに厚い大神の 御魂と共に進むのである。 尊く清らかな宣伝使よ、 過去の罪や過ちを 直日に見直し、聞き直し 告白し直される埴安の 彦の命こそ尊い。 彦の命こそ尊い」

と歌いながら黄昏の空をとぼとぼと進んで来た。雲路別天使は広道別天使の姿を見て大いに喜び、

「ヤア、広道別天使よ」

と声をかけると、宣伝使は唐突なこの言葉に驚き、よくよく眺めると、雲路別の宣伝使であった。二柱の神司はここに手を取り合ってローマに入り、元照別天使を帰順させて、伊弉諾命の神政に奉仕させたのであった。

広道別天使は、天照大御神が天の岩戸にお隠れになり、天地が暗黒となった時、岩戸の前に太玉串を捧げて、神の御心を慰めた太玉命の前身である。
