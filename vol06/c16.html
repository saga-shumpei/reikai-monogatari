Chapter 16: The Great Deluge (Part II) [266]

第16章　大洪水（二）〔266〕

§ 1

As if the world were verging upon the kalpa of fire, several intensely hot suns appeared at once, radiating heat onto the earth, and the water melted from the icebergs of the great earth flowed and gathered toward the earth, the depression at the center of the great earth; cold water momentarily increased upon the earth, until even the ridges of such high mountains gradually came to submerge from view.

At this time, the stars altered their positions, the sun shook to the front, back, left, and right, and the earth convulsed, filled with an indescribable great sound. The sun was enveloped in black clouds, the earth turned to darkness, and it became a scene where one could not discern what was close at hand.

The three-tiered golden palace that had stood in that Dragon Palace Castle extended boundlessly into midair, forming heavenly bridges of gold, silver, and copper colors; from each heavenly bridge, spiritual cords of gold, silver, and copper colors were hung down, and with the hooks of salvation at their ends, they began to lift the righteous divine men onto the bridges.

Like swarming ants, numerous divine men were rescued onto the heavenly bridges, and the heavenly bridges began to rotate once more. Extending east and west, the heavenly bridges turned through the air to the south, west, north, and east, delivering the rescued divine men to the summits of Mount Tenkyō, Mount Chikyō, and several other high mountains, and gradually lifted up onto the heavenly bridges with the hooks of salvation the righteous divine men who were sinking and suffering in the rapids of sorrow.

At this time, the missionary of Mount Tenkyō had unawares stood upon the golden bridge, casting a golden spiritual cord into the sea of mud and pulling up the drifting righteous divine men. Then, waiting until the heavenly bridge was filled with divine men, the heavenly bridge once again rotated to the east, south, west, and north like a crane, and they were conveyed to the high mountains suited to their souls. In the divine revelation:

このとき星はその位置を変え、太陽は前後左右に動揺し、地は激動して形容し難い大音響に満たされた。太陽は黒雲に包まれ、地上は暗黒と変わり、咫尺を弁じない光景となった。

かの竜宮城にあった三重の金殿は、中空に際限もなく延長して金銀銅色の天橋を成し、それぞれの天橋からは金銀銅色の霊線を垂れ下げ、その端の救いの鉤をもって、正しき神人を橋の上に引き揚げ始めた。

このとき天教山の宣伝使は、何時の間にか黄金橋の上に立ち、金色の霊線を泥海に投げ、漂流する正しき神人を引き揚げつつあり。而して天橋に神人の充満するを待ちて、またもや天橋は起重機のごとく東南西北に転回し、その身魂相当の高山に運ばれゆくなり。神諭に、

§ 2

"When the time truly comes, for the person of sincerity, the deities have watched over them, so even from within the muddy sea, they shall be plucked up."

Thus was it revealed, and it is brought to mind.

Among those who were rescued, whether it should be called an oversight even by the eyes of demons, or whether it was by the deep design of the missionaries, even Uraruhiko and Uraruhime, who had been so treacherous and wicked, were rescued atop the copper bridge. And Tokoyo-shin'ō, as well as Banko-shin'ō, were also rescued atop the gold bridge.

Uraruhiko was carried to Mount Altai, and many other divine humans were also set down here. On this mountain, countless ants of various sizes lay piled high upon the summit, for all the ants had foreknown the flood and had fled first of all to the top of the mountain.

The deity Uraruhiko-no-Kami was carried to the mountain of ants, his entire body was covered in ants, various parts of his body were pierced through with sharp needles, and, unable to endure the extreme agony, he went down the mountain a little and tried immersing his whole body into the muddy water, but the ants bit into him with all their might, the pain grew even more intense, and he crawled back up toward the mountain of ants once again.

と示されていることが、思い出されるのである。

救い上げられた者の中にも、鬼の目にも見落としと言うべきか、あるいは宣伝使の深い経綸があってのことか、あれほど悪逆無道であったウラル彦、ウラル姫も銅橋の上に救い上げられた。そして常世神王をはじめ盤古神王もまた金橋の上に救われていた。

ウラル彦はアルタイ山に運ばれ、その他の神人も多くここに降ろされた。この山は大小無数の蟻が山頂にうずたかく積もっていたが、すべての蟻は洪水をあらかじめ知り、山の上に真っ先に避難していたのであった。

ウラル彦神は蟻の山に運ばれ、全身を蟻に包まれ、体の各所を鋭い針で突き破られ、非常な苦悶に耐えかねて少し山を下り、泥水の中に全身を浸してみたところ、蟻は一生懸命に食いついて、苦痛はますます激しくなり、またしても蟻の山へと這い上がっていった。

§ 3

The bald head of Katoriwake was also here, but at this time alone, his bald head appeared as if hair had completely grown. It was entirely the result of ants having swarmed upon it.

The divine beings carried to this Mount Altai were solely the most wicked divine beings; the most virtuous divine beings were saved on the two mountains of Tenkyō and Chikyō, and the most wicked were saved on Mount Altai.

The wicked ones who habitually maintained egoism and sucked sweet nectar appeared to have had their entire bodies become sweet, and in contrast to the ants joyfully swarming over them, the divine beings who had tasted bitter experiences for the sake of the world had bitter bodies, and not a single ant could swarm upon them. In the Hidden Oracle,

"Ants swarm upon sweet things (gratitude). Ants do not swarm upon bitter things (ingratitude),"

what is written thus must be a revelation of the scene in this tale. Ah, it is an uneasy situation as to where the earthly world will head henceforth.
