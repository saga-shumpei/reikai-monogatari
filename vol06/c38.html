Chapter 38: The Golden Palace [288]

第38章　黄金の宮〔288〕

§ 1

Takahiko-no-kami, seeing that Kumotsukami showed true repentance, released the spiritual binding on everyone while chanting, "Hii, fū, mii." At once, all of them regained freedom of movement, shed tears, threw down their weapons upon the earth, crowded before the propagandist and knelt to apologize for their insolence, and Kumotsukami, wiping away his tears, recounted the whole truth step by step.

"As your honor knows, this mountain has enshrined the golden jewel from antiquity by order of Kuniharutachi-no-Mikoto, and the two deities Tamamorihiko and Tamamorihime have protected it as chief priests. Then Kamisumihiko became one of the Hachiō-shin and protected the whole area of this mountain, while Azumawake was in charge of the divine administration; however, Kamisumihiko of the Hachiō-shin became a propagandist before the Great Flood and departed for the holy land of Jerusalem, after which the stage was left entirely to Azumawake. However, this time, Uralhiko, the son of Tokoyohiko, established a divine capital in the holy land of Armenia and dispatched propagandists to various regions; some years ago, a powerful emissary named Onitsukami, who was one of those propagandists, came to this mountain, and after negotiations with Azumawake, Azumawake finally surrendered to Onitsukami and submitted to the divine capital of Armenia. Therefore, in order to enshrine the golden spirit of the land in the divine capital of Armenia at last, it was decided that the Palace of Gold would be moved to Armenia, and as Uralhiko was soon to come to this mountain with many attendants to receive the jewel, we were engaged day and night without rest in cutting open the mountain road under the strict command of Azumawake. Nevertheless, since your noble honor arrived and allowed us to hear the precious teachings of God, the evil spirit of the serpent that had been lurking within our hearts seems to have fled, leaving us with a feeling as if heaven and earth had been enlightened; when we reflect upon our conceit and misunderstandings up to now, we feel so thoroughly ashamed that we wish we could crawl into a hole if there were one."

Thus he spoke out, showing his sincerity upon his face. The propagandist nodded and said,

"By your truthful confession, all mass of suspicion has melted away. In that regard, where is Azumawake, the guardian deity of this mountain, right now?"

To this inquiry, Kumotsukami replied,

と真心を顔に表して申し立てた。宣伝使はうなずき、

「お前の偽りのない告白によって、すべての疑団は氷解した。それにつけても当山の守護神である吾妻別は今どこにいるのだ」

との尋ねに、雲掴は、

と真心を面に現はして述べたてにける。宣伝使は打ち首肯き、

『汝の詐らざる告白によつて、総ての疑団は氷解した。それに就いても当山の守護神吾妻別は今何処に在るぞ』

との尋ねに、雲掴は、

§ 2

"Yes, at present, in preparation for moving the goshintai of the Palace of Gold to Armenia, he has been performing the ascetic practice of fasting day and night. However, Tamamorihiko-no-kami, who is the all-important chief priest, says that moving this shrine to Armenia does not accord with the divine will, and he is said to be greatly opposing it. If the all-important shrine keeper does not consent, no matter how great Azumabiko no Mikoto is as the guardian of this mountain, he can do nothing; yet, once having promised Uralhiko, he must fulfill it, and if by any chance he were to breach it now, it is clearer than looking at fire that this mountain would be burned down and destroyed by Uralhiko, so, in order that Lord Tamamorihiko-no-kami might give his consent, he is preparing on the one hand, while performing the practice of fasting on the other. In truth, I call myself Kumotsukami and serve as the head of humble laborers, but in reality I am an assisting divine official to Azumabiko, named Kumobetsu. Therefore, if it is about this mountain, I know everything in detail, but at present Azumabiko is truly caught in a dilemma, agonizing in distress. It is truly pitiful to behold."

So he spoke out, darkening his complexion and sighing.

Takahiko-no-kami said to Kumobetsu:

"Do not worry; what will save this mountain from calamity is only the power of the Amatsu Norito and kotodama, and the virtue of the propaganda hymns. Moreover, the Palace of Gold shall never be moved to Armenia. This should be moved to Mount Kogane. On Mount Kogane, a deity of infinite benevolence has appeared and established a splendid teaching, so as quickly as possible this should be reverently moved to Mount Kogane and made into the spirit to protect the divine administration of the deity Izanagi no Mikoto who resides in Takamanohara. Therefore, we have come to this mountain as propagators."

Thus, he recounted his mission for the first time. This Takahiko-no-kami was the predecessor of Ame-no-Koyane no Mikoto, who recited the Amatsu Norito before the Heavenly Rock Cave when Amaterasu-Ōmikami later concealed herself within the cave.

From here, guided by Kumobetsu, they climbed to the mountain summit, met with Azumabiko and Tamamorihiko-no-kami, and expounded in detail the doctrines of the Anai teachings and the divine virtues of the Great Deity Izanagi; finally, Azumabiko submitted to Izanagi no Mikoto and displayed supreme loyalty. And thus, it came to be that the Palace of Gold, together with the jewels, would be relocated to the sacred ground of Jerusalem.

「はい、この頃は黄金の宮の御神体をアーメニヤに遷す準備のために、昼夜断食の行をしておられます。しかし肝心の宮司である玉守彦天使は、このお宮をアーメニヤに遷すことは、御神慮にかなわないと言って、大変に反対をされているそうであります。肝心のお宮守がご承知でなければ、いくら当山の守護職である吾妻別命も、どうすることもできず、そうかといって一度ウラル彦に約束された以上は、これを履行しなければならず、万が一今になって違背するようなことがあるとすれば、当山はウラル彦のために焼き滅ぼされることは火を見るより明らかであるというので、玉守彦天使様のご承知が得られるようにと、一方では準備をするとともに、一方では断食の行をしておられるのであります。私は実は雲掴と申して、身分の低い人夫の頭領をしておりますが、実際は吾妻別の補佐の神司で、雲別と申す者であります。それゆえ当山のことならば、何事も詳しく存じておりますが、現在のところ吾妻別は実に板挟みとなって、苦しみもだえておられます。誠に見るもお気の毒の至りであります」

と顔色を曇らせ、ため息をつきながら述べ立てる。

高彦天使は、雲別に向かって、

「ご心配はいりません、当山の災いを救うのは、ただ天津祝詞と言霊の力と、宣伝歌の功徳のみであります。また黄金の宮は決してアーメニヤには遷りません。これは黄金山に遷せばよい。黄金山には仁慈無限の神様が現れて、立派な教えを立てておられますから、一刻も早くこれを黄金山にお遷し申し上げ、高天原に鎮座されます神伊邪那岐命の御神政御守護の御魂とするべきものです。それゆえ私たちは当山に宣伝使となって参ったのです」

と、初めて自分の使命を語った。この高彦天使は、のちに天照大御神様が岩戸隠れをなさったとき、岩屋戸の前で天津祝詞を奏上された天児屋根命の前身である。

これより雲別の案内にて山頂に登り、吾妻別、玉守彦天使に面会し、三五教の教理や伊邪那岐の大神の御神徳を詳細に説き示し、ついに吾妻別は、伊邪那岐命に帰順し忠誠を抜きんでたのであった。そして黄金の宮は、玉とともにエルサレムの聖地に遷座されることとなった。
