Chapter 19: The Four Deities of Purification [269]

第19章　祓戸四柱〔269〕

§ 1

Ruling the divine country of the sun, The Great Deity Kamu-izanagi, Together with the Great Deity Kamu-izanami, The Great August Deity Tsuki-no-mihashira, Appeared on the golden bridge In the sky between heaven and earth, Thrust down the heavenly jeweled spear, Revered the no-Mikoto Of Takamimusubi-no-Ōmikami And Kamumusubi-no-Ōkami, Stirred the muddy sea with a churning sound, Made Onogoroshima, which dripped from the tip of the spear, the placenta, And, seeking to build and solidify the divine country, Led by the hands of His two consorts, Crossed the golden bridge, Descended to Mount Tenkyō in Watarai, Conversed with Princess Konohana, And sought to build the everlasting age of pine, firm as rock, Unto a thousand and myriad generations.

Ruling the world of the moon, The Great August Deity Kamu-izanami, Together with the August Deity Tsuki, The ruler of the divine country of the sun, Erected a jeweled pillar purer than snow, And auspiciously performed The divine festival of moon, snow, and flowers; Like tree blossoms blooming all at once, She transformed Her body into thirty-three forms, Seeking to establish the reign of Miroku, The age of pine through the long period Of five billion, six hundred and seventy million years, First gave birth to Awashima, And to the islands of Iyo-no-futana, Tsukushishima, Toyoashihara-no-nakatsukuni, Oshima and Meshima, Iki and Tsushima, And the islands of Sado and Awaji, Assigning each of the national spirits And guardian deities of mountains, rivers, trees, and grass Born thereby to their respective duties.

Presiding over the governed age, The Great August Deity Tsuki-no-mihashira, The Great August Deity Ame-no-mihashira, And the Great August Deity Kuni-no-mihashira— These three Great Deities Are the three pillars dwelling in heaven Of whom the Ancestral Deity of the Realm spoke. Truly noble and gracious Is the tale of the ancient Age of the Gods, Which is so auspicious to hear this spring day. Anticipating a hundred plants and trees, The white plum has burst into fragrance; Its scent, purer than snow, And the evergreen trees blooming all at once, The evergreen pines growing luxuriantly— In this truly noble divine country, The two deities, Nodatihiko and Princess Nodati, Sitting upon Mount Shumisen And protecting it, Repaired and solidified the two realms of the visible and the hidden,

And, seeking to solidify the realm of the base, Designated the crater of Mount Tenkyō As the exit and entrance of the deities; Here lies the realm of fire. The sincere heart, firmer than rock, Cares nothing for raging flames; The soul that neither burns in fire Nor drowns even in water reached the rapids of Naruto And arrived at the realm of the base, And, seeking to save the numerous souls Suffering from having fallen into the precarious shallows, Took the agony of infinite hell Entirely upon His own body; The pent-up tears endured and endured Since three thousand years ago Have cleared, giving way to the joyful divine age, Of which this is the foundation revealed— The tale of the divine exit. On the Setsubun when demons are said to come, And in spring when the warmth rises in all directions,

Precious indeed is the season to come, When flowers bloom on the scattered roasted beans.

Due to the great cataclysm of heaven and earth, the earth tilted toward the southwest, which caused changes in the atmosphere of the vast sky, and neither the Ame-no-iwakusafune nor the Torifune, which numerous divine people had used as their sole weapons, possessed any efficacy whatsoever against the vibrations of the universe. At that time, what proved most useful was solely the Ark of Divine Revelation; there was only the rope of salvation hanging down from the three bridges of gold, silver, and copper, and Kotohirawake, who transformed into a turtle, swam through the muddy sea, and rescued the righteous divine people by carrying them to high mountains.

§ 2

Beholding this spectacle from heaven, Takamimusubi no Kami, the guardian deity of the left of Ōkuniharutachi no Mikoto, and Kamumimusubi no Kami, the guardian deity of the right, bestowed the Heavenly Jeweled Spear upon Tsuki no Ōmikami, who was their own spirit, and upon Kamuizanagi no Ōkami and Kamuizanami no Ōkami, causing them to stand upon the Floating Bridge of Heaven, which was a golden bridge; they stirred the ocean, which drifted and churned like a jellyfish, making the sound of shio-kooro-kooro, and through the breath of the Great Sun Deity, brought forth a breath of freshness in the universe, dried all things upon the earth, and caused all defilements and dust to be swept away. The deity born of this breath is called Ibukidonushi no Kami.

And in order to strip away the clothing of the vegetation across the earth that was smeared with mud, they raised a wind, and adding rain to the wind, cleansed them. The deity who appeared from this water and fire is called Hayaakitsuhime no Kami. Again, they opened rivers among the mountains, driving away all impurities completely. This breath is called Seoritsuhime no Kami. Seoritsuhime no Kami carries away all impurities from various regions of the earth to the vast ocean, and carrying them off to the subterranean realm to wander and vanish—this breath is called Hayasasurahime no Kami. These four deities are designated as the Haraedo deities, and were made into the great organ of the divine realm for the total metabolism of the universe. By this organ, everything from the sun, the earth, the moon, the stars, down to humanity, animals, and plants breathes perfectly, and with the functions of metabolism completely provided for, each is made to live its life fully. This divine work is said to be the purification and exorcism performed at Aokihara of the navel-place, of the Honeki and A-O-U-E-I of the nine mountains and eight seas.

Incidentally, concerning the divine rite of the purification of the Honeki and A-O-U-E-I of the nine mountains and eight seas, although it holds a profound and delicate meaning in the study of kotodama, this shall be explained in detail on a later day when leisure permits.

現代日本語訳

天上よりこの光景を眺めた、大国治立命の左守神である高皇産霊神と右守神である神皇産霊神は、我が精霊である撞の大御神、神伊弉諾の大神、神伊弉冊の大神に天の瓊矛を授け、黄金橋である天の浮橋にお立たせになり、海月のように漂い騒ぐ大海を、潮許袁呂許袁呂（しおこおろこおろ）にかき鳴らされ、日の大神の気吹によって、宇宙に清鮮の息を起こし、地上の一切を乾燥させ、すべての汚穢や塵埃を払い退けさせられた。この息から成り出でられた神を伊吹戸主神という。

そして地上一面に泥にまみれた草木の衣を脱がせるために風を起こし、風に雨を添えて清められた。この水火から現れ出られた神を速秋津比売神という。再び山々の間に河川を通じさせ、一切の汚物を神退けに退けられた。この御息を瀬織津比売神という。瀬織津比売神は、地上の各地から大海原へ、すべての汚れを持ち去り、これを地底の国へと持ち去り失わせる、この御息を速佐須良比売神という。以上の四柱の神を祓戸神と称し、宇宙一切の新陳代謝を司る神界の大機関とされた。この機関によって、太陽、大地、太陰、列星、および人類や動植物に至るまで完全に呼吸し、かつ新陳代謝の機能が完全に備わって、それぞれがその生活を全うさせられた。この神業を、九山八海の火燃輝（ほねき）のアオウエイの、緒所（臍）の青木原に御禊祓いなさるというのである。

ちなみに九山八海の火燃輝のアオウエイの御禊の神事については、言霊学上甚深微妙な意味があるが、これは後日暇を得て詳説することとする。

原文（近代文語）

天上よりこの光景を眺めたる、大国治立命の左守神なる高皇産霊神、右守神なる神皇産霊神は、我が精霊たる撞の大御神、神伊弉諾の大神、神伊弉冊の大神に天の瓊矛を授け黄金橋なる天の浮橋に立たしめ玉ひて、海月の如く漂ひ騒ぐ滄溟を、潮許袁呂許袁呂に掻き鳴し玉ひ、日の大神の気吹によりて、宇宙に清鮮の息を起し、地上一切を乾燥し玉ひ、総ての汚穢塵埃を払ひ退けしめ玉ひぬ。この息よりなりませる神を伊吹戸主神と云ふ。

而して地上一面に泥に塗れたる草木の衣を脱がしめむため風を起し、風に雨を添へて清めたまひぬ。この水火より現はれたまへる神を速秋津比売神と云ふ。再び山々の間に河川を通じ、一切の汚物を神退ひに退ひ給ふ。この御息を瀬織津比売神と云ふ。瀬織津比売神は、地上の各地より大海原に、総ての汚れを持ち去り、之を地底の国に持佐須良比失ふ、この御息を速佐須良比売神と云ふ。以上四柱の神を祓戸神と称し、宇宙一切の新陳代謝の神界の大機関となしたまふ。この機関によつて、太陽、大地、太陰、列星、及び人類動植物に至るまで完全に呼吸し、且つ新陳代謝の機能全く完備して、各其の生活を完全ならしめ給へり。この神業を九山八海の火燃輝のアオウエイの、緒所（臍）の青木原に御禊祓ひたまふと云ふなり。

因に九山八海の火燃輝のアオウエイの御禊の神事については、言霊学上甚深微妙の意味あれども、これは後日閑を得て詳説する事となすべし。
