Chapter 21: The Stately Pillar of Cypress [271]

第21章　真木柱〔271〕

§ 1

Another divine name of Izanagi-no-Ōkami is called Ama-no-Mihashira-no-Kami, another divine name of Izanami-no-Ōkami is called Kuni-no-Mihashira-no-Kami, and another divine name of Amaterasu-Ōkami is called Tsuki-no-Mihashira-no-Kami.

These three deities came forth to Aokigahara of Mount Tenkeu, made Tsuki-no-Mihashira-no-Kami the central pillar, erected the Yahirodono hall, worshipped Amatsumioya-no-Ōkami, appointed Tsukiteruhiko-no-Kami as the ritual officiant, gathered Darumahiko, Sukunahiko, Hirokohiko, Takateruhime, Masumihime, Kotodamahime, Tatsuyohime, Hahuribe, Iwatowake, and other various divine beings, and proclaimed the Great Liturgy of the Heavenly Ritual; whereupon the eternal heavenly skies and the reed-plain land of abundant ears drifting upon the great ocean grew pure, bright, and clear, and through the vital power of the myriad souls of the four deities of Haraedo, the beautiful land of the deities was established once again.

Hereupon, Izanagi-no-Kami placed the Tsuki-no-Mihashira pillar in the center, circled around this pillar from the left, and Izanami-no-Kami circled around from the right to meet him; thus, they fashioned and solidified heaven and earth, and chanted auspicious and fair words to give birth to lands, give birth to islands, give birth to deities, give birth to human beings, and bring forth the deities of mountains, rivers, and all vegetation. Their divine song:

Izanagi-no-Kami: "In the boundless sky that knows no end, The primordial sky of that vast expanse, The utmost end of the heavenly firmament, The fiery radiance of nine mountains and eight seas: A-O-U-E-I, The five pillars of A-O-U-E-I; Ka, Sa, Ta, Na, Ha, Ma, Ya, Ra, Wa— Through the word-spirits of the father and mother Of these nine pillars, The breaths that resound forth are: Ki, Shi, Chi, Ni, Hi, Mi, I, Ri, Wi; Ku, Su, Tsu, Nu, Fu, Mu, Yu, Ru, U; Ke, Se, Te, Ne, He, Me, E, Re, We; Ko, So, To, No, Ho, Mo, Yo, Ro, Wo; Following these: Ga, Go, Gu, Ge, Gi; Za, Zo, Zu, Ze, Ji; Da, Do, Dzu, De, Dji; Ba, Bo, Bu, Be, Bi; Pa, Po, Pu, Pe, Pi; Bringing forth seventy-five sounds,

The Great Divine Ancestor who created The boundless heaven and earth, Kuniharitachi-no-Ōkami, Whose deity guarding the left Is the lofty spirit of mind-over-body, Takamimusubi-no-Ōmikami, And the primordial deity of the pristine soul, Kamimusubi-no-Ōkami; Whose breath congealed, and forever Solidified the plain of heaven And the vast ocean into an age of the deities, As bestowed by the proclamation Of the ancestral deities of Takamanohara; While reverently serving that command, From the left of the Tsuki-no-Mihashira pillar Erected eternally and firmly, As I go around and attend, The light of the shining sun is clear, The moonlight is pure and serene, Radiating across Aokigahara;

Together with the vast ocean, Purely governed is the land of the deities, Purely governed is the land of the deities; How good it is, oh, the land of the deities, How delightful and wondrous, oh, the garden of the deities!"

§ 2

While chanting thus, she began to walk around the Tsuki Pillar from the left.

At this time, going around the Tsuki Pillar from the right, here the two deities met each other from both sides, and Kuni-no-Mihashira-no-Kami, gazing upon the male deity's beautiful and manly form, could not contain her joy and recited an august poem. That august poem was:

"Descending from the far-off heavenly sky, Upon that golden bridge, The two deities of Moon and Tsuki, Led by the hand at the edges by the two deities, At the Heavenly Floating Bridge in Watarai, The divine festival of moon, snow, and flowers Having been purified and governed, Izanagi-no-Kami-no-Mikoto, Awe-inspiringly, Going around the Tsuki Pillar, Circulating and revolving, now here, With my delightful lord, like twin pines growing together, Unmoving through a thousand and ten thousand generations, The foundation of the divine age of pines Having been built and firmly established, the palace pillars, The manifest divine age into the world of Miroku, The sound of the bell of the Benevolence Three Assemblies Echoed through Aokigahara; The placenta of the august womb was beautiful, Growing up beneath the heavens, The mountains, rivers, trees, and grasses, all the

Giving birth to humans, birds, beasts, Down to insects and fish, Like the stars of the heavenly sky, Generating and multiplying by that august power; His form, of which one never tires of beholding, How pure, like a clear mirror; How pure, surely like a clear mirror; Vying in radiance with the sun and moon, Born as the deity of the sun and moon, O divine spirit, ah, how wonderful! Beloved man, beloved man! Such an auspicious husband deity, Even if one soars across heaven and races over the earth, Seeking to the ends of anywhere, Could never be found, ah, how precious! The husband deity of Ame-no-Mihashira, His manly form, ah, how wonderful! 'Beloved man, O man!' thus, Joyfully in today's festival, I offer blessings with good and beautiful words:

The sound of bells with seventy-five tones, All auspicious, through the Plain of Heaven, Echoes as the August Imperial Land, Echoes as the August Imperial Land: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, Hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of divine auspicious words; One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, Hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of divine auspicious words; Unchanging through a hundred generations, through a thousand generations, Unchanging through a hundred generations, through a thousand generations, You and I, of heaven and earth, Shall become the mirror, eternally, Shall become the divine ancestors, eternally."

Having thus offered blessings, she gave birth to Awashima. This Awashima was appointed as Sukunahiko-no-Kami and Kunitama-no-Kami. However, this island was not counted among the offspring, and Sukunahiko-no-Kami, longing for the traces of Nodatehiko-no-Kami, came to set forth on an expedition into the hidden realm.
