Chapter 45: The Deity Ōtoshi [295]

第45章　大歳神〔295〕

§ 1

Through the day-and-night labor of the two missionaries, who spared no effort, the rice-planting was finished far more easily than expected. Here, the house of Kuma-kō, the most spacious in the village as he oversaw it, was opened, and the banquet of the Sanaburi-matsuri was held. And all the peasants of the village, saying, "This is the cleansing of our lives, the smoothing out of wrinkled testicles," received the sacred sake, smacked their lips over the rice cakes and dumplings, and sang.

At this time, Tora-kō, who called himself Ushitora, was an exceptionally large man even among the gathered company. He was a champion who did not change his facial color in the least even after drinking two or three shō of sake. Opening a large mouth like that of a lion dance, he would grab three or four rice cakes or dumplings at once, toss them into his mouth, and the moment he closed his lips, he would gulp them down with a sound. He was truly a hearty eater. Drinking and eating, drinking and eating, even this great drinker gradually felt the alcohol take effect, and seemingly becoming light-headed, he began to groan. "Guzzling like an ox and gorging like a horse" must have originated with this self-styled Ushitora no Konjin. Opening his big mouth and slapping his flat, large palms, like a sugar merchant's charcoal shovel, with smacking sounds, the self-styled Ushitora no Konjin made a voice like the roaring of oxen and tigers, and

Tora-kō: "Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness; after the darkness, the moon will rise."

Thus he began to sing. Many peasants widened their eyes, pursed their mouths, and

"You speak outrageous things! Say it again, sing it over!"

They said in unison. Caring nothing at all, Tora-kō once more

"Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness,"

he began to sing out. Tagosaku became desperate and said,

"Hey, look here, Tora! How can you sing Uralhiko's propaganda song in such an auspicious place? Take it back, take it back! If you do not consent, you will be expelled from this village!"

Tora-kō: "If you expel me, go ahead and expel me; I too will leap away... Crawling out and leaping, an earthworm, and the towering summer clouds... I am already prepared to crawl out of this earthy village."

Mokubee: "Tora, you are a fellow like an earthworm. If you utter such nonsense, know that this is a gathering of those who send frogs flying and chop earthworms! Shall I chop you in two with an adze-hoe?"

Kumojiwake, the missionary who was listening to this childish dispute over liquor between drunken men, clapped his hands and began to sing to restore the humor of the gathered company:

"Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is God! Rejoicing in the blessings of God, Shed your sweat, press out your fat; Sweat and fat turn into sake. No poverty can catch up with diligent labor; If you work, there is no dark night; It is always a moonlit night with rice to eat. Drink and make merry pleasantly; Drink and work, break a sweat; Sweat and fat turn into sake. This sacred sake offered to God Is an honorable medicine that prolongs life. In a world where even poison becomes medicine, Do not drink too much medicine, Stop at the point where it does not turn into poison. The God who distinguishes good from evil Manifests outwardly And protects us who work, And protects us who work."

虎公「飲めよ騒げよ一寸先は闇だ、闇の後には月が出る」

と歌い始めた。大勢の百姓どもは、目を丸くし、口を尖らせて、

「貴様はけしからぬことを言う、言い直せ、歌い直せ」

と口々に言った。虎公はいっさい構わず、またしても、

「飲めよ騒げよ一寸先は闇だ」

と歌い出した。田吾作は躍起になり、

「これ、おい、虎、こんなめでたい席で、ウラル彦の宣伝歌を歌うということがあるものか。言い直せ、言い直せ、承知しないとこの村を追い出されてしまうぞ」

虎公「追い出すなら追い出すがいい、俺もまた跳ねる……這い出て跳ねる蚯蚓や雲の峰……もうこの泥臭い村を這い出る覚悟だ」

杢兵衛「虎、貴様は蚯蚓みたいな奴だ。そんなごたくを並べると、ここは蛙飛ばしの蚯蚓切り様のお集まりの席だぞ。手斧鍬でちょん切ってしまってやろうか」

泥酔者の他愛もない、この酒の上の問答を聞いていた宣伝使の雲路別は、一座の機嫌を直そうと手を打って歌い始めた。

「飲めよ騒げよ一寸先は神だ 神の恵みを喜んで 汗を流せよ脂を絞れ 汗や脂は酒となる 稼ぎに追いつく貧乏はない 稼げば闇の夜はない いつも月夜に米の飯 飲めよ騒げよ心地よく 飲んで働け汗をかけ 汗と脂は酒となる 神にお供えしたこの神酒は 命を延ばすお薬だ 毒も薬となる世の中に あまり薬を飲みすぎて 毒とならない程度でやめよ 善と悪とを立て分ける 神が表に現れて 働くわれらの保護をする 働くわれらの保護をする」

泥酔者の他愛なき、この酒の上の問答を聞いてゐた宣伝使の雲路別は、一座の機嫌を直すべく手を拍つて歌ひ始めける。

§ 2

Singing with an amusing melody, he sang a song improvised on the spot and danced for them. Hearing this song, everyone rejoiced greatly and once again stood up and danced wildly. Torakō, Tagosaku, Mokubē, Kumakō, and all the rest completely sobered up from their drunken quarrel, and taking each other by the hand, brought this banquet to a spirited close.

The two missionaries conveyed the proclamation of the Ōkami to the peasants of this Mitoshi Village and taught them the improvement of agriculture. From then on, the harvest was abundant year after year, and fruitful years continued. The villagers joyfully revered Angel Kumojiwake as the deity of peasants. Finally, Kumojiwake encouraged the way of agriculture, preached the doctrines of Anaikyō, and here became Ōtoshigami.

Torakō surpassed the crowd in physical strength and, unlike his hideous countenance, was an exceedingly honest man. From this point on, he became a disciple of Hiromichiwake, followed behind the missionaries, and devoted himself to proselytization starting with the city of Rome and extending to the rest of the world. This Torakō was gradually influenced by the missionaries, his soul became increasingly purified, and he finally became a splendid missionary. At the time of the incident of the Heavenly Rock Cave, Tajikarao-no-kami, who pushed open the rock cave, was the reincarnation of this man.

と節面白く、口から出まかせの歌を歌って、舞って見せた。一同はこの歌を聞いて非常に喜び、またしても立って踊り狂った。虎公も、田吾作も、杢兵衛も、熊公も、一同は酒の上の争いもすっかり醒めてしまい、互いに手を取り合って勇ましくこの祝宴を終えた。

二人の宣伝使はこの御年村の農民に、大神の宣示を伝え、かつ農業の改善を教えた。それから年々収穫が多く豊年が続くことになった。村人は喜んで雲路別天使を、農民の神様として尊敬した。ついに雲路別は農業の道を奨励し、三五教の教理を説き、ここに大歳神となった。

虎公は腕力が人並み外れて優れ、醜悪な顔つきに似合わず、至って正直な男であった。これより広道別の弟子となり、宣伝使の後に従ってローマの都をはじめ、その他世界の宣伝に努めた。この虎公はだんだんと宣伝使に感化されて心魂がますます清まり、ついに立派な宣伝使となった。天の岩戸の変に際して、岩戸を押し開けた手力男神はこの男の生まれ変わりであった。
