Chapter 5: Feasting Cups in Disarray [255]

第5章　抔盤狼藉〔255〕

§ 1

Suddenly, cheerfulness arose in the grand hall of the mansion, sake and fruits were carried in abundantly, even the lowliest of the menials sat lined up in their seats, large barrels and jars were placed in the center, and with ladles made of sliced bamboo, they dipped and drank, dipped and drank, and began to chant with all their might. And as drunkenness came over them, a scuffle over the ladles began; their heads were lightly tapped with the ladles, a bald-headed old man broke out in numerous lumps on his face and head, their tongues gradually became tangled, and they were manifold: some weeping, some laughing, and some growing angry.

First Servant: 'Hey, all of you fellows, isn't this strange and sickening? Miyamahiko puts on the airs of a general, works us hard every single day as if we were the descendants of enemies or something, drinks sake all by himself, melts both body and soul on Kasugahime's lap pillow, and on top of that, shows off his delight to others by telling her to rub his legs and massage his hands, drinks sake all by himself, and has never once told us to drink even a single mouthful. Whenever I cleaned up the vessels, I used to satisfy myself by licking each cup and sniffing its aroma. Today, though Kasugahime used to detest him just as a lean horse throws off its load, by some whim of the wind, he claimed that the little nun herself blabbered for him to marry her, and that stingy Miyamahiko finally felt like leaping across the caldron of hell, telling us to drink our fill of rotting sake; it is truly infuriating. Even if he looks down on us and takes us for fools, there should be a limit!'

So saying, he rolled up his sleeves, and while striking his own belly two or three times with his fist, puffed out his face and spoke.

Second Servant: 'Do not speak in such a loud voice; it is in front of everyone. What will you do if there is a fellow who, hoping to be allowed to lick a ladleful again, reports to the general the slander you just uttered?'

First Servant: 'Whatever will be, it is none of our business. As for Kasugahime, General Miyamahiko will do something about her. We have no prospect of doing anything anyway, so we might as well drink this rotten sake and quietly go to sleep.'

甲「おい、皆の者ども、妙でおかしいではないか。美山彦が大将面をして、毎日毎日、俺たちを敵の末か何かのようにこき使い、自分ばかり酒を呑み、春日姫の膝枕に身も魂もとろけさせ、おまけに足を揉め、手を揉めと人に嬉しいところを見せつけ、自分ばかり酒を呑んで、俺たちには一口でも呑めと言ったことはありはしない。俺は、いつも器を片付けるときに盃を一つ一つ舐めて香りを嗅いで満足していたのだ。今日は春日姫に、痩せ馬が荷をひっくり返すようにして嫌がられていたのが、どうした風の吹き回しか、尼めの方から結婚してくれと抜かしたとか言って、けちん坊の美山彦が、地獄の釜の一足飛びをするような気にとうとうなりおって、腐りかけた酒を俺たちにたらふく呑めと言ってくるのだ、実に業腹だ。甘く見て馬鹿にするにも程があるではないか」

と腕をまくって、自分の腹を二つ三つ拳で叩きながら、顔を膨らませて言う。

乙「大きな声で言うな、皆の前だ。また杓で一杯でも舐めさせてもらおうと思って、貴様の今言った悪口を大将に告げる奴がいたらどうするのだ」

甲「どうするもこうするも俺たちの知ったことではない。春日姫は美山彦の大将がどうにかするのだろう。俺たちはどうするあてもありはしないし、まあ腐った酒でも呑んでおとなしく寝ることだ」

と腕を捲つて、自分の腹を二つ三つ拳で叩きながら、面ふくらして云ふ。

乙『大きな声で云ふな、皆の前だ。また杓で一杯も舐らして貰はうと思ひよつて、貴様の今云つた悪口を大将に告げる奴があつたらどうする』

甲『どうするも、かうするも俺らの知つた事ぢやない。春日姫は美山彦の大将が、どうかするのだらう。俺らはどうするあてもありやしないし、マア腐つた酒でも呑ンでおとなしく寝る事だよ』

§ 2

Hei: "Hey, it seems the gathering has grown too lonely; how about singing a song? That propagation song of Uraruhiko-no-Kami is the gospel of heaven to us. 'Drink and make merry, for one inch ahead is darkness, and after the darkness the moon will rise'—he speaks so sweetly; as long as we have sake, we need neither a wife nor anything else."

Tei: "No matter how heavenly a gospel it may be, can one get drunk on sake one cannot drink? Though they say sake is the leader of a hundred medicines, or the water of life, unable to drink the sake I wish to drink, being worked hard every day, even if I drink the red water of Niagara thinking of it as sake, I do not get thoroughly drunk at all, do I? When I think of this, it is a sad fleeting world."

Such was the comicality of his beginning to weep slowly.

Bo: "Hey, is there anyone who weeps and snivels in such an auspicious place?"

Tei: "How could I not weep? Tonight Miyamahiko is weeping intimately with Kasugahime. We are his henchmen; it is only natural to weep."

§ 3

Bo: "There is a great difference between your weeping and Kasugahime's weeping. It is just as different as a bush warbler perching on a plum branch and singing to welcome the cheerful spring, compared to a chicken whose neck is wrung and feathers plucked, crying aloud at the top of its voice at death's door."

Kō: "Recently, that strangely uncanny madman from Mount Tenkyō was wandering all around here, going about proclaiming whether it would shine or cloud over, whether people would float or die, or something about cuckoos. Because of that, the ground begins rumbling day after day, and the rain falls incessantly and weepingly just like your tears. The water in the valley has turned to red mud, so that we cannot even drink water properly, can we? I wish we could do something to cast such a fellow into Niagara Falls as soon as possible."

Otsu: "Yes, that propagandist? He came out tonight. In the inner chamber, stiffly postured with wide, glaring eyes, that fellow whose life is already spent called Tsuki-teruhiko, and that fellow called Starving Hiko because his belly is empty, have fallen into Miyamahiko's trap and are now practically caged birds. If only we dispose of them, the rain will likely stop, and the earth rumblings will probably cease too. Because they go around spreading such horribly ill-omened things, the weather only keeps growing worse and worse. Whenever I hear those songs they twitter, my head strangely throbs and nauseates me, and I feel as though my chest were being pierced with a bamboo spear."

Bo: "That is precisely where Miyamahiko is great. Out of great benevolence to rescue you from the suffering of hearing those so-called propaganda songs, he smoothly lured those propagandists into this mansion, and tonight they are scheduled to be handled roughly. Well, taking that as our relish, let us drink sake leisurely and make a night of it."

With that, every single one of those fellows collapsed dead drunk, in such a state that not a soul among them could properly stand upright.

In an inner chamber, Miyamahiko and Kasugahime were magnificently attired as if for this day alone, proceeding with their wedding ceremony. Meanwhile, Haruhime of exquisite countenance was attending to the sake serving. Kasugahime displayed her charms to the utmost, forcibly pressing sake upon Miyamahiko while singing an entertaining song. Miyamahiko repeatedly drained the sake cups as Kasugahime urged him, until at the height of intoxication, declaring that his head ached and was dizzy, he collapsed heavily on the spot, snoring like thunder, and fell fast asleep without a shred of consciousness. Just as Haruhime stood up, she collided with a tall, long sake barrel; the barrel overturned and poured sake like a waterfall onto Miyamahiko's head. Miyamahiko rolled over with his hands motioning as though groping through empty air, groaning in torment.

Accompanied by Haruhime, Kasugahime advanced into the inner sanctuary, related the entire state of affairs to Tenshi Tsukiteruhiko, and bade Haruhime summon Tarimahiko, who was waiting in an adjoining room; whereupon the four deities, male and female, convened an emergency conference. Ah, what shall be the outcome of this council?

とどの奴も皆へべれけに酔いつぶれ、ろくに腰の立つ者もいない有様であった。

奥の一間では、美山彦と春日姫が今日を限りと盛装を凝らし、婚礼の式を挙げつつあった。そして容色の麗しい春姫が酌を務めつつあった。春日姫は力の限り媚びを呈して美山彦に無理やりに、面白い歌を歌いながら酒を勧める。美山彦は春日姫の勧めるままに酒杯を重ね、ついには酩酊の極みとなり、頭が痛み眩暈がすると言いながらその場にどっと倒れ、雷のような鼾声をあげて正体もなく寝入ってしまった。春姫は立ち上がると途端に背の高い長い酒樽に衝突し、樽は転げて美山彦の頭上に酒を滝のように注いだ。美山彦は両手で虚空を探るような手つきをして寝返りを打ち、苦しげに呻いている。

春日姫は春姫を伴い奥殿に進み入り、月照彦天使に委細を物語り、春姫に一室に控えていた足真彦を招かせ、男女四柱はここに緊急会議を開いた。ああ、この会議の結果はどうなるのであろうか。
