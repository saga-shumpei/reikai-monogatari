Chapter 47: Niō and Kannon [297]

第47章　仁王と観音〔297〕

§ 1

Bestowing the name Iwahiko upon this large man, Hiromichiwake no Kami advanced toward the center of the capital of Rome while singing the customary propagation song. Today was said to be the birthday festival of Mototeruwake no Kami, and houses had raised red, white, and blue banners, expressing their congratulations.

Then, a crowd of several thousands, wearing twisted white headbands and purple sashes crossed in an X-shape, came running in ranks shouting 'Wassho, wassho,' beating on stones, teabowls, gongs, and tin cans. And singing the propagation song of Uralhiko, they charged headlong toward this direction with tremendous force like a tidal wave. As Hiromichiwake no Kami was about to advance while singing,

"The deity appears manifest before us, cleanly separating the good from the evil,"

a man who appeared to be the leader of the crowd clenched his fist toward the side of the face of the missionary singing this song, and struck him as if to send his head flying. The missionary put on an indifferent face and once again began to sing the song.

Man: "What a stubborn fellow. Do you still babble?"

Clenching his fist like a turban shell, he beat him down indiscriminately. Iwahiko held back his Niō-like physique, clenched his fists, gritted his teeth, and stamped his feet in frustration. However, recalling the divine words in the propagation song, 'Look again with uprightness, proclaim again,' and having no order from the missionary, he merely remained standing firm like a guardian deva on the main road, gritting his teeth.

Perhaps astonished at the sight of this man, the crowd halted midway and did not advance a single step. The onlookers in the rear ranks shouted,

"Hey, hey! What are you doing? Will you not advance? Will you not advance?"

While being struck and beaten, the missionary calmly sang the propagation song in a low voice and recited the Amatsu Norito. Instantly, the moment the large man raised his clenched fist above his head, his whole body stiffened, turning into the likeness of a bronze statue, and he did nothing but roll his eyes about. On this side, the giant Iwahiko stood motionless and upright, glaring with angry eyes, puffing his cheeks, pursing his lips downturned, and holding his clenched fist raised. The other man stiffened with his fist clenched and raised, his mouth hanging open; suddenly, it was as though the A-un Niō guardians had appeared at the crossroads of Rome. From within the crowd,

"It is the Niō, it is the Niō!"

someone shouted, and following that, the crowd once again spoke in unison,

"It is the Niō, it is the Niō, what a truly well-matched pair of Niō!"

「神が表に現われて　善と悪とを立て分ける」

と歌いながら進もうとするところを、群衆の中の頭らしき男は、この歌を歌っている宣伝使の横面めがけて拳を固め、首も飛んでいけと言わんばかりに殴りつけた。宣伝使は素知らぬ顔をして、またしても歌を歌い始めた。

男「こいつはしぶとい奴だ。まだほざくか」

群衆はこの男の姿を見て驚いたのか、途中で立ち止まって一歩も進まずにいる。後列の野次馬は、

「おいおい。どうしているのだ。進まぬか、進まぬか」

「仁王さまだ、仁王さまだ」

と叫ぶ者があり、それに続いて群衆はまたしても口を揃えて、

「仁王だ、仁王だ、実によく似合った仁王さまだ」

§ 2

...and they began to chatter idle words.

At this time, from the side, a beautiful female missionary, once again,

"Kamunaohi, who created this world; Ōnaohi, whose spirit is vast;

In all things of the human world, view them anew and hear them anew in rectitude,"

appeared at this place while singing.

The crowd, with one voice after another,

"Hey, look, look! Just when we thought a magnificent Niō had appeared, now it is the Kanzeon Bosatsu of great mercy and great compassion, endowed with all thirty-three aspects. Worship, worship!"

cried out in unison.

"Yo-yo!"

Saying so, thousands of the crowd surrounded her from front, rear, left, and right; even the crossroads of the capital's grand avenue, wide as it was, became packed like sushi, such that there was no space for the wind to pass through. As the female missionary began to sing the missionary song, many onlookers who indulged in abusive language even appeared among the crowd.

The female missionary raised her slender, white hand and waved it left, right, and left. Those among the crowd who had uttered abusive words remained with their mouths open, stiffened unable to close them, and people saying "Ah, ah" while drooling appeared here and there.

At this time, from ahead, someone came riding in a magnificent vehicle with a solemn procession. Before and behind the vehicle, many attendants guarded and cleared the way, advancing steadily toward the crossroads. This was the procession of Genshō-betsu-tenshi, the lord of Roma Castle, who was on his way to worship at an island in the Mediterranean Sea, also celebrating his birthday.

§ 3

The crowd scattered in all directions. The Niō remained standing side by side at the crossroads, holding their breath in the stillness of A-un. The outrider faced the Niō,

"To the right, to the right!"

he called out. The Niō did not utter a single word, stiffly holding their ground at the crossroads. Hiromichiwake no Kami stood under the eaves of a certain house by the roadside, watching this spectacle.

Outrider: "You insolent fellow! When told to go to the right, why do you not go to the right? What are you thinking? It is the procession of Mototeruwake no Kami, the revered lord of Rome castle. Move to the right at once!"

As he said this, he appeared somewhat startled by such immense men standing with clenched fists, and shouted in a trembling voice.

The palanquin gradually advanced. The Niō did not move in the slightest. The outriders A, B, and C timidly drew near and looked up at these giant men. Looking closely, the only things moving were their eyes.

A: "Aha, these are fabricated figures. The people of Rome, knowing that the castle lord would pass today, seem to have made and erected standing Niō here instead of an arch. However, if they do not leave more space, we cannot pass through like this. What thoughtless fellows!"

B: "No, this one is human. Look there, he is glaring with wide-open eyes. Opening such enormous eyeballs to frighten us is just a bad prank. Hey, will you not step aside? If you do not move, I will poke your eyes!"

C: "Do not act rashly! What if a kami has taken this form? What will you do then? Divine punishment will strike!"

A: "If it is a kami, shall I try asking a favor? Excuse me, Lord Shibugami!"

B: "As if there could be any such thing as Lord Shibugami!"

A: "Even so, does he not have a color just like shibugami paper?"
