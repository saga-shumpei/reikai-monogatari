Chapter 14: The Black Dragon and the Red Dragon [264]

第14章　黒竜赤竜〔264〕

§ 1

To return a little to the story, Hinode-no-Kami, who had escorted Banko Shin'ō from the palace on Mount Ural and returned to the holy ground of Jerusalem, was anxious about the approaching end of the world, and unable to bear standing idly by as calamities befell the realm, enshrined Banko Ōkami in Jerusalem, and, becoming a missionary himself, appeared on this Mount Chōhaku.

On Mount Chōhaku, a white jewel was enshrined. Arikunihiko and Arikunihime presided over this, having Iwanagahiko administer the divine government. However, this Mount Chōhaku had completely submitted to Uralhiko. One night, Arikunihiko had a terrifying dream, in which a storm continued for several hundred days, turning the entire earth into a sea of mud, submerging even the towering Mount Chōhaku under the water, and causing all the divine beings to drown; yet he himself ran and climbed onto the branches of a great tree on the mountain summit, whereupon numerous snakes climbed up the tree and bit the hands and feet of the husband and wife. The muddy water on the ground increased more and more, finally submerging even that great tree, and just as his own head was also about to be submerged, a beautiful deity appeared from the heavens, hooked him with a hook of gold wire, hauled him up into midair, and saved him. In the midst of this, the moment he opened his eyes and looked down at the earth, his sash caught on the hook snapped, and just as he thought he was plunging headlong into the sea of mud where raging billows roared like mountains, he woke from sleep. Thereupon the husband and wife immediately visited the Palace of Shiratama, prayed to the Great Deity of Heaven and Earth, and gathered the divine beings of the mountain to give them warning, but not a single one, beginning with Iwanagahiko, believed this. Nevertheless, day and night a gale raged, strange stars appeared in the heavens, three comets appeared and disappeared endlessly, and the husband and wife were deeply pained at heart. 話は少し後に戻つて、ウラル山の宮殿より盤古神王を奉じ、ヱルサレムの聖地に帰還したる日の出の守は、世の終末の近づけるに憂慮し、天下の災害を坐視するに忍びずとして、盤古大神をヱルサレムに奉安し、自らは宣伝使となりてこの長白山に現はれたるなり。

長白山には白色の玉が祭られてある。而して有国彦、有国姫が之を主宰し、磐長彦をして神政を掌らしめられたり。然るにこの長白山は、悉皆ウラル彦に帰順して居たりける。有国彦はある夜怖ろしい夢を見たるが、その夢は暴風雨が幾百日か続いて、大地一面に泥海と化し、さしもに高き長白山も水中に没し、神人皆溺死を遂げたるが、自分は山頂の大樹の枝に駈登りけるに、数多の蛇樹上に登りきたつて、夫婦の手足を噛むだ。地上の泥水はますます増加して、遂にはその大樹をも没し、今や自分の頭も没せむとした時、一柱の美しき神が天上より現はれ来り、金線の鈎に引懸け中空に捲き上げ、救ひたまふ。途中に目を開いて地上を見下す途端に、鈎に懸りし吾帯はプツリと断れて、山岳のごとく怒濤の吼り狂ふ泥海に、真倒さまに顛落せし、と思ふ途端に眠りは醒めたり。それより夫婦は直ちに白玉の宮に詣で、かつ天地の大神に祈願し、山の神人を集めて警戒を与へたれど、磐長彦はじめ一柱も之を信ずる者なかりける。然るに日夜暴風吹き荒び、天空には異様の星現はれ、三箇の彗星の出没きはまりなく、夫婦は非常に胸を痛めつつありき。かかるところへ天教山の宣伝使、日の出の守は、

§ 2

"'The power of sincerity saves the world,'"

...someone sang while climbing up. That voice entered the ears of the husband and wife as if piercing them with needles. As soon as the gods of Mount Chōhaku heard the song of the missionary, they immediately felt a headache, suffered violent pain in their chests, and were in such a state as to writhe in agony, tossing and turning upon the earth. Iwanagahiko [said],

"'The voice of an evil god who harms the realm, I shall conquer it,'"

...and notching an arrow to his bow, he shot sharply aiming at the missionary. However, those arrows all missed, and not a single arrow struck. Hinode-no-kami did not yield in the least, and while singing the missionary song, he advanced into the palace atop the mountain. The husband and wife rejoiced, guided Hinode-no-kami to the inner palace, and received kind instruction. Hinode-no-kami made clear that three years from now the end of the world was finally to arrive, and singing the missionary song once again, he descended the mountain and vanished to somewhere unknown.

From that time on, the husband and wife prayed to God day and night, and commanded the gods within their domain to build an ark, but they were hindered by Iwanagahiko, who had charge of the crucial divine government, and did not attain their objective. Thereupon, the husband and wife made up their minds, prayed to God for one hundred days and one hundred nights, and finally restored their bodies into a black dragon and a red dragon, took white jewels in their mouths, descended the Ōryoku River, crossed the vast ocean, ascended Mount Tenkyō, and served the Ōkami intimately.
