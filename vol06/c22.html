Chapter 22: Boundless Divine Works [272]

第22章　神業無辺〔272〕

§ 1

Here, Ame-no-Mihashira-no-Kami did not rejoice at the proclamation of the goddess, but became exceedingly angry, and gave vent to that anger through a poem. His poem:

"By the word of the Amatsu-Mikami, Having come forth as the pillar of heaven, I am the divine official appointed by the deity, Who is to govern Takamanohara; The divine spirit of pure light, Like the morning sun high in the clouds; You are the deity-official of Toyoashihara, Who is to govern the earthly land; Between heaven and earth, naturally, There is a distinction between high and low; There is a difference between heavy and light; Heaven is above, earth is below; Man is heaven, woman is earth; Heaven coming down and earth being above, This inverted divine work Greatly differs from the mind of Mototsu-Mikami And is an error; Heaven is above, earth is below; Man is above, woman is below; Mistaking heaven and earth,

And erring between above and below, How could we give birth to pure divine offspring? How could we give birth to a pure land? Let us return once again to the origin, Fully apologize to Amatsu-Mikami For this offense of presumption, And once again petition For the divine command, Walk around the pillar anew, And speak the kotodama again; come, farewell; Come, come, farewell, O you, Mikoto."

Thus, with a somewhat dissatisfied demeanor, the male deity returned to his original place, and facing these divine words whose reason and principle were clear, the female deity also, having no words to reply, submissively returned to her original place.

The island that came into being at that time was Awashima, as mentioned before. Awashima was an island that appeared in the center of the present-day Pacific Ocean, but owing to this divine work of inverting heaven and earth, its foundation loosened, and it eventually drifted and flowed to the southern extremity, becoming the barren island of the South Pole spoken of by geographers.

And as the national soul of this Awashima, Sukunahiko-no-Mikoto, who was the reincarnation of Kototamawake-no-Mikoto, had no means to lift a hand or foot, and finally became the deity Hiruko, boarded a frail reed boat, remained long in the Land of Tokoyo, and half of his spirit fell down to the Land of Ne, where he served in the salvation of the hidden realm.

Through this karmic connection, he appeared in later ages as the Savior in the land of Judea, and through the karmic connection of the error in circling the pillar, he tasted the agony of the cross and became the Redeemer of all people.

Here, the two deities Izanagi and Izanami soared up again to the presence of the Amatsukami and requested the divine decree of the Ōkami. The Ōkami strictly commanded them, in accordance with the proclamation of the male deity, to rectify the kotodama of inverting heaven and earth and not to repeat the error.

Here, the two deities showed the reality of correcting their words and rectifying their error, placed the pillar in the middle again, and as the male deity circled from the left and the female deity from the right, meeting each other, the male deity first composed a poem. His poem:

と、やや不満の様子で、男神は元の場所に帰られたところ、女神もその理義明白な神の言葉に対して、返す言葉もなく再び元の場所に、唯々諾々として復帰された。

その時成り出た島は、前述のように淡嶋であった。淡嶋は現在の太平洋の中心に出現した島であるが、この天地逆転の神業によって、その根底は緩み、ついに漂流して南端に流れ、地理学者のいわゆる南極の不毛の島となったのである。

そしてこの淡嶋の国魂として、言霊別命の再来である少名彦命は手足を下す手だてもなく、ついに蛭子の神となってか細い葦舟に乗り、常世の国に永く留まり、その半分の身魂は根の国に落ちて行き、幽界の救済に奉仕されたのである。

この因縁によって、後世ユダヤの国に救世主となって現れ、御柱を巡り合った過ちの因縁によって、十字架の惨苦をなめ、万民の贖罪主となったのである。

ここにイザナギ・イザナミの二尊は再び天津神の御許に舞い上がり、大神の神勅を請われた。大神は男神の宣言のように、天地顛倒の言霊を改め、過ちを繰り返さないよう厳命された。

ここに二神は言葉を改め過ちを改める実を示し、再び御柱を中央に置き、男神は左から、女神は右から、巡り回って互いに出会う時、男神がまず御歌を詠まれた。その御歌、

と稍不満の態にて、男神は元の処に帰り給ひけるに、女神も其理義明白なる神言にたいし、返す言葉もなく再び元の処に、唯々諾々として復帰し給ひたり。

その時成り出でましたる嶋は、前述のごとく淡嶋なりき。淡嶋は現今の太平洋の中心に出現したる嶋なるが、此天地逆転の神業によつて、其根底は弛み、遂に漂流して南端に流れ、地理家の所謂南極の不毛の嶋となりにける。

而て此の淡嶋の国魂として、言霊別命の再来なる少名彦命は手足を下すに由なく、遂に蛭子の神となりて繊弱き葦舟に乗り、常世の国に永く留まり、その半分の身魂は根の国に落ち行き、幽界の救済に奉仕されたるなり。

この因縁によりて、後世猶太の国に救世主となりて現はれ、撞の御柱の廻り合ひの過ちの因縁によりて、十字架の惨苦を嘗め、万民の贖罪主となりにける。

ここに諾冊二尊は再び天津神の御許に舞上り、大神の神勅を請ひ給ひぬ。大神は男神の宣言のごとく、天地顛倒の言霊を改め、過ちを再びせざる様厳命されたり。

ここに二神は改言改過の実を表はし、再び撞の御柱を中に置き、男神は左より、女神は右より、い行き廻りて互ひに相逢ふ時、男神先づ御歌をよませ給ひける。其御歌、

"To cleanse the mud of the floating world, / By the word of the Amatsumikami, / Crossing over the golden bridge / Spanning Takama-ga-hara, / Descending upon Onokoro Island, / Ever more firmly thrusting and solidifying / The eight-fathom palace into the upper rock base, / Leveling it into the bedrock below; / The sacred pillar erected in the midst / Of this fair age, / Towering over the Awagihara / Of renowned Takama-ga-hara / By Tachibana of Hyūga in Tsukushi; / Having accomplished the true equilibrium of heaven and earth / And the divine rituals of the moon, snow, and flowers, / Here now the two deities— / You to the right, I to the left, / The left being the husband, the right being the wife— / Circling round and round, now here, / The parents giving birth to the pure realm.

The pure figure of the great deity Kami-Izanami / Is like white plum blossoms / Bursting into bloom all at once. / Ah, fair goddess! / Ah, lovely countenance! / If you are present, / Even should an age come when the morning sun appears in the western sky / And the moon in the eastern heavens, / The heart of husband and wife shall remain united, / And to establish for ever and ever / The age of the pine, lasting and prosperous, / Is truly peaceful and serene. / Come then, come, / By the word of the Amatsumikami, / Giving birth and leveling the peaceful lands, / Thrusting and solidifying the eighty islands, / Down to the myriad deities and the plants and trees, / In the fair age of the spring of Hōrai, / Blooming sacredly as the wood blossoms

The radiance of the moon is so clear and bright, / Ever more clear and bright, and yet more clear; / From the point of the Heavenly Jeweled Spear, / The double-edged sword blade that brings peace, / Dripping down—the island of Onogoro / Is truly sacred indeed! / The island is truly sacred indeed!"

Thus did he chant this song of praise.
