Chapter 20: The Unfathomable Depths of Good and Evil [270]

第20章　善悪不測〔270〕

§ 1

The two deities, the Ancestor of the Nation Kuniharutachi no Mikoto and Toyokunihime no Mikoto, having borne the thousandfold offerings of purification, were to retire to the Root Land and the Bottom Land; yet, as their hearts of supreme mercy and compassion could not bear to sit idly by and watch the great calamity about to come upon the divine world and the visible world, they temporarily concealed themselves in Mount Tenkyō and Mount Chikyō, changed their names to Nodachihiko no Kami and Nodachihime no Kami, and discerned the future of the two worlds, divine and visible;

"Now that Tsuki no Mihashira no Kami, Ame no Mihashira no Kami, and Kuni no Mihashira no Kami have descended, and the divine work of repairing and solidifying has gradually commenced, we shall proceed from here to repair and solidify the hidden realm and save all souls into Heaven,"

Saying this, the husband and wife, the two deities, joined hands, threw themselves into the severely violent crater of Mount Tenkyō, and descended to the Realm of the Root and Bottom, which is the fireball realm at the center of the earth; and Nodachihime no Mikoto, parting from here, entered into the realm of the Earth Tides in its southwestern corner.

That a soul of supreme benevolence, supreme love, supreme sincerity, and supreme truth suffers no injury to itself even in the midst of any fierce fire, and is neither defiled nor drowned even when drifting in any muddy torrent, is entirely the proof of the propagation song, "The power of sincerity saves the world." When that soul is great and possesses infinite power, because there is no evil poison of greed, anger, and ignorance that is turbid and dark in the mind, evil thoughts are thereby extinguished, and even fierce fire becomes a cool, refreshing breeze.

Nodachihiko no Kami and Nodachihime no Kami felt the fiercely violent crater as though they were being blown upon by the cool breeze of early autumn, and leisurely proceeded to the Realm of the Root and Bottom. For example, though small insects such as fleas, mosquitoes, and lice are tormented with their entire bodies burned even by the butt of a Shikishima cigarette, a farmhand places that same cigarette butt on his palm while transferring the light, feeling not the least heat on the back of his hand. Just as a three-year-old child suffers exceedingly if made to bear a load of five kanme, whereas a man in his prime handles it with his fingertips without any hardship, all toils and tribulations are due to the strength or weakness of one's own soul. When sinful humans are cast into fire, while writhing in boundless suffering, their bodies are ruined, and they finally come to be burned into ashes. But just as there is a gigantic animal that treads out with the edge of the claw of one foot even that fire capable of burning a human, feeling nothing at all, so for Nodachihiko no Kami and Nodachihime no Kami—whose divine stature is great, whose divine virtue is boundless, and who may be called the very essence of Onogoro Island—to cast themselves into the fierce fire of Mount Tenkyō, which may be called a part of their own bodies, must be an easy task.

現代日本語訳

国祖・国治立命、豊国姫命の二柱は、千座の置戸を負って、根の国、底の国に御退隠あそばすこととなり、大慈大悲の御心は、神界、現界のまさに来ようとする大惨害を座視するに忍びず、しばらく天教山および地教山に身を隠し、野立彦神、野立姫神と改名して、神、現二界の前途を見定め、

「ここに撞の御柱の神、天の御柱の神、国の御柱の神の降臨があって、修理固成の神業もややその緒に就いたので、われはこれより進んで幽界を修理固成し、よろずの身魂を天国に救おう」

と、夫婦二神は相携えて、あれほど激しい天教山の噴火口に身を投じ、大地中心の火球界である根底の国に落ち行かれ、野立姫命は、これより別れて、その西南の隅にある地汐の世界に入られた。

至仁至愛至誠至実の身魂は、いかなる烈火の中でもその身魂を損なうことがなく、いかなる濁流に漂ってもその身魂が汚れ溺れることがないのは、まったく『誠の力は世を救う』という宣伝歌の実証である。その身魂が偉大で無限の力があるときは、心中に一切の混濁溟濛たる貪瞋痴の悪毒がないので、悪心はここに消滅し、烈火もまた清涼な風となるのである。

野立彦神、野立姫神は、あれほど激しい噴火口を、初秋の涼風に吹かれているかのような心地がして、悠々として根底の国に赴かれた。たとえば蚤や、蚊や、虱のような小虫は、『敷島』のタバコの吸い殻によってもその全身を焼かれて苦悶するけれども、野良男はその同じタバコの吸い殻を手のひらに載せて吸い継ぎながら、手の甲の熱さを少しも感じないようなものである。三歳の童子に五貫目の荷物を背負わせれば非常に苦しむけれども、壮年の男子はこれを指先で何の苦もなく取り扱うように、すべての辛苦艱難というものは、自己の身魂の強弱によるものである。罪深い人間が火中に投じられると、限りない苦しみに悶えながらその身を損ない、ついには焼かれて灰となるに至る。しかし、巨大な動物があって人を焼くほどのその火をも片足の爪の端で踏み消し、何の感じもないかのように、神格が偉大で、神徳が無辺であり、淤能碁呂島の御本体ともいうべき野立彦神、野立姫神においては、我が身の一端ともいうべき天教山の烈火の中に投じられるのは、容易なわざであるはずである。

§ 2

Humans, whose wisdom is dim and whose strength is frail, unless they by all means seek the salvation of the great Kami, cannot possibly atone through self-power for the sins of the soul committed by their own bodies. Therefore, human beings must merely believe in Kami, obey Kami, perform good as much as possible, reject evil, and thereby fulfill their duty to be administrators of the divine governance of heaven and earth. As a saying of a Western philosopher states:

"God helps those who help themselves,"

—so it is said. It is indeed so. Yet this is finite, and it is utterly impossible for human beings to absolutely produce the eternal happiness of the soul. If a person attempts to perform one good deed, they are bound unconsciously to be committing twice as much evil. Therefore, in human life there is neither absolute good nor absolute evil. Within good there is evil, within evil there is good, within water there is fire, within fire there is water, within yin there is yang, within yang there is yin, and through the intermingling of yin and yang, good and evil, and the intertwining of beauty and ugliness, light and dark, all things of the universe are brought to completion. Therefore, regarding the true distinction between good and evil as proclaimed by a certain sect, not to mention humans, even Kami would likely be unable to accurately distinguish it.

For when Kami created all things, Kami created them by means of the three great primal elements of spirit, power, and physical form. Spirit is good, and physical form is evil. And the power generated from spirit and physical form is a mixture of good and evil. This is called the power of the universe, or termed divine power, and spoken of as the virtue and majesty of Kami. Therefore, that good and evil are not two, and beauty and ugliness are one and the same, is the true reality of the universe.

That which was heavy and turbid became the earth, and that which was light and pure became heaven. Yet by the sky alone, there is no place for all things to grow, and by the earth alone, it is impossible to absorb the breath of the true Kami; heaven and earth unite, yin and yang harmonize, and thereby all things of the universe are eternally preserved. Furthermore, depending on time, place, and position, good may become evil, and evil may also become good. Truly, the standards of good and evil are complex, and are not within the scope that can be easily discerned and known by the petty intellect of the human mind. Therefore, regarding the judgment of good and evil, only Ōkami, the Great Primal Spirit of the universe, possesses that authority, and we humans possess absolutely no qualification to judge all good and evil.

§ 3

Judging others recklessly encroaches upon the authority of the Great Kami, and it must be called the height of presumption.

People should merely contemplate correcting the evil within themselves and moving toward good, and should reflect upon the fact that they have no qualification whatsoever to judge others.

If those who love us are not necessarily good people, and those who torment us are not necessarily evil people, we must simply realize that there is no other way than to stand aloof from good and evil, love and hatred, and to obey the Way of the Gods (Kannagara).

Ah, kannagara tamachihaemase.

The recorder says: 'Because there is a risk that contemporary people who are trapped in a life of falsehood and vanity may stumble over this passage, they especially need to read it thoroughly and carefully savor its meaning.'
