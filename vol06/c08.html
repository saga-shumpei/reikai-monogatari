Chapter 8: The Vernal Sea [258]

第8章　春の海面〔258〕

§ 1

The boat at last reached the shore. The boatman quietly rolled up the white sail. Kasugahime lent her ear and listened with deep interest to the song of the missionary, who was chanting while spreading a white folding fan at the bow of the boat and celebrating their safe landing.

'Upon the waves of the ten-thousand-ri ocean, Both the heavenly firmament and the great sea Are entirely calm on this day\'s journey; Catching the wind of Shinato with full sails and half-sails, While pregnant with the grace of the gods, The joy of reaching the other shore Is that of proclaiming and transmitting the teachings of God. The heart on this journey, like that of Hirokohiko— How pure is the heart of God! How vast is the grace of God! When looking up, high in the distant Heavenly sky shines the moon, Bathing oneself in its spotless light; Crossing this floating world is as upon a boat, Between the waves steered by the helm, Floating and sinking; in order to save The divine people who suffer from the grief of eternal darkness, Setting forth on this departure; The morning sun shines in the faint sky, The rough waves of the world also, of Terutae...

Casting off fine garments for straw coat and hat, With a light heart beneath the sky of travel; Even the rough sea where waves rise and rage, Through the grace of Mototsu-Ōkami, Has calmly subsided on this spring morning; Though snow lingers on distant mountains, By the seashore there are signs of spring, Plum blossoms blooming with fragrance, and the bush warbler Opening the door of the valley as the incoming tide arrives, The travelling garments treading through the breaking tide; Shining in the sky, Kasugahime, Though faintly indistinct whether I know the husband, The maiden standing on the wharf— Is she not the daughter of the Eight King Deities, Together with Michinukihiko of divine sincerity, The consort of the lord Takasumiwake, whose majesty is exalted? Flocks of plovers clamoring by the rising waves; As the waves grow gentle, gracefully Appearing, how valiant is her figure!

I am on Hirokohiko\'s long journey To spread in the world the teachings of God That are said to save this world; A missionary of the noble capital, Held in awe even by the birds flying in the sky, The governor Kamikunawake; Pray declare your name clearly; Just as the moonlight is without shadow, Blowing away the clouds of the heart, I shall purify the land of the gods; Look with favor upon the traveling form Of the missionary called Kumojiwake, Hirokohiko, O Kasugahime!'

As soon as he finished chanting, Kasugahime, standing on the shore while opening a white folding fan, quietly opened her gentle flower-like lips and answered him with a song. That song:

'In the ancient age of the gods, in Takamanohara, Ever since that day when Kuniharitachi no Ōkami, Born as the ancestor of the gods, Concealed Himself, The winds that blow have smelled of blood, The visitations of the raging deities Are ceaseless; alas, the tears Of all the gods have fallen relentlessly like rain, Rivers and seas overflowed, mountains collapsed, The colors of a hundred trees and herbs faded, And to illuminate this present world enveloped in eternal darkness, Born as the maiden child Of the governor Michinukihiko of Moscow, Completely forgetting that I am a woman, Bound to a single cord of sincerity Thinking of the world, Without loosening the reins of the heart, Advancing as led, upon a horse\'s back, Treading through perilous mountain paths...

『神代の昔高天原の 神の祖と生れませる 国治立の大神の 隠れましたる其の日より 吹き来る風は腥く 荒振る神の音なひは 絶間もあはれ諸神の 涙は雨と降り頻り 河海溢れ山崩れ 百の樹草の色も褪せ 常世の闇の現世を 照さむためとモスコーの 道貫彦の司の子と 生れ出でたる手弱女の 女の身をも打忘れ 世界を思ふ一筋の 誠の綱に繋がれて 心の手綱ゆるみなく 曳かれて進む馬の背の 危き山路を踏みさくみ

§ 2

On the ocean where rough waves roar, Bathing in the tide, the face whiter Than falling and piling snow, And the arms white like radishes, Directly embracing the youthful chest, Holding true jeweled hands, arms embracing each other, Parting in life without time to rest, The missionary, teacher of the divine majesty of Takasumiwake, Wringing sleeves in joy and sorrow, At the parting roads of right and left, The eight hundred tides, the eight-fold salty paths, Crossing the wave paths to the Eternal Land, The propagation song resounding everywhere, Plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds Blooming all at once, the evergreen Pine tree's constancy is deep in color; Until growing white-haired together like piled deep snow, Before there is even time to pledge, a storm blows, Wandering on mountain paths and fields,

Sympathizing with the suffering of her husband, Abandoning the comfortable capital of Moscow, While grieving her heart at even a faint breeze, Like the cuckoo crying eight thousand and eight calls, Though she calls and cries out, the human heart Of the present world is utterly clouded; Without even a person to listen, relying on the summer night's Moon, on Mount Kijō, Abducted by the wicked person Katori no Wake, Imprisoned in the crooked fortress of the deep mountains, While enduring three years of sorrow And spending sad days and months, Miyamahiko in the depths of the deep mountains, Their destiny, by the angelic minister Tsukiteruhiko, Being saved, The gathering clouds hanging over the heart Have also been blown away, together with Tears gushing like Niagara Falls,

Parting and coming here to the Sea of Kii, A solitary journey of a frail woman, At the whispers of tigers and wolves, At this time of a lonely heart, Ah, beloved missionary, The ruin of Kamikuniwake, My small heart too, by Hirokohiko, The divine minister, like the morning sun Rising upon the paired pine trees, The sky of the heart shines clear, Peaceful spring's Kasugahime, Peaceful spring's Kasugahime."

Thus, through the song, she revealed her identity.

Thus the boatman lowered the small boat and transported the numerous passengers toward the land. Hirokohiko was put aboard the small boat first and reached the shore, and the two, exchanging glances over something, vanished into the nearby forest.
