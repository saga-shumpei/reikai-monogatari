Chapter 50: The Great Perplexity [300]

第50章　大戸惑〔300〕

§ 1

Following the guidance of the officials, the party of the missionaries was leisurely guided to the inner sanctuary. Mototeruwake greeted them in fluster and consternation, rubbing his head against the tatami mats, saying,

"You have kindly granted the petition of us whose souls are utterly clouded and defiled. Come, right this way,"

and taking the lead himself, he guided them to a high tower with a fine view. When the missionaries climbed the high tower accompanied by the two gigantic men and looked, rare delicacies and fine wines from the mountains, fields, rivers, and seas were lined up so crowdedly that there was hardly any room. Then Mototeruwake guided the two missionaries to the seats of honor, saying,

"I shall leisurely listen to your pure teachings later. First, please take your meal,"

urging them to be seated, with sincerity showing upon his face. The Angel Hiromichiwake said,

"Then, excuse me,"

and took the prepared seat, and the two gigantic men also sat down in the lowest seats. Izumohime stiffly took her prepared seat, and said,

"Well, well, Lord Mototeruwake, this is quite a luxurious feast. Since I have been witnessing the miserable state of the human race in the world who are sunken in the rapids of grief, eating tree leaves and licking tree roots, barely carrying on their daily living, I have not seen such delicacies for many long years. Even though this is said to be the age of the deity Ōgetsuhime-no-kami, it is truly an utterly shocking state of affairs. However, since this is your kind intention, I shall gladly accept it. Because such a feast is far too precious for our mouths to receive, I shall distribute it to the birds, beasts, and fishes."

No sooner had she spoken than from the high tower toward the deep moat below she cast down in pieces all the meal dishes given to her. Mototeruwake turned red in the face, said not a word, hung his head, and merely wept tear after tear. Unable to eat this delicacy either, the Angel Hiromichiwake, saying that he too would distribute it to all living beings, threw it down without regret aiming at the moat below, and turned to Mototeruwake, saying,

"Rather than our partaking of such delicacies, it feels incomparably better to distribute them to all sentient beings. Whether something is sweet, delicious, or tasteless is merely a matter while passing through the three inches of the throat. Fortunately, I hear that today is your birthday. In your position as the lord of a realm and a castle, bestowing benevolence upon all sentient beings is, I deem, an important deed that one who is a master to the people ought to perform. It is auspicious for us, and all sentient beings will surely rejoice and celebrate your birth."

§ 2

Having finished speaking, he returned to his original seat. With these delicacies set before them, Iwahiko and Kumahiko could not bring themselves to eat, and although they pondered this way and that whether they, showing an unyielding spirit, should also cast them down aiming for the moat below, their throats kept rumbling uncontrollably; therefore, Iwahiko turned to the gathered company and said,

"I too, on behalf of all sentient beings, gratefully receive this."

No sooner had he spoken than he opened his mouth wide and began to eat. Kumahiko as well,

"I also, bit by bit,"

saying so, cleared away the vast amount of food and drink completely. Izumohime stood up, sang a song, and began to dance in celebration of the birth.

"The world has fallen into perpetual darkness, As the rock-boats soaring through the sky And the heavenly bird-boats dance in frenzy. The sun and moon shine bright in the heavens, Yet clad in splendid garments, They scour mountain, field, sea, and river without exception, Prowling about and gathering delicious food Heaped up like lying mountains. It has become the realm of the deeply arrogant Ōgetsuhime no Mikoto, And Kanayamahiko no Mikoto And Kanayamahime no Mikoto, who act as her guides, Have appeared, Fashioning in plenty swords and blades That harm the people of the world, Even cannons, muskets, and arrowheads, Shamefully crossing swords far and wide. Suspicious teachings have spread, Leading astray the hearts of worldly people, And even the governor Mototeruwake

Has become the deity Ōtomadohiko no Kami. This world grows ever cloudier; Seeking to illuminate the clouded floating world, Emerging from the cloud-paths, Izumohime Has appeared here, together with Hiromichiwake no Muchi no Mikoto, who widely spreads the divine way. There are times when they appear At the crossways of vertical and horizontal, And when one strains the ears to listen To the shouting voices rising in crowds, Rather than the sound of 'urō, urō,' It sounded like 'perish, perish.' Grieving over the perishing world, Ame no Mihashira no Ōkami, Who has manifested upon the mountains of the Nine Mountains and Eight Seas, Wishing to govern the world in peace and tranquility, Entrusted the matter to Haniyasuhiko no Muchi no Mikoto And Haniyasuhime no Muchi no Mikoto,

Causing the Ananaikyō to be founded, Distributing the propagators of the divine teachings To the lands in all four directions. Paining the great divine heart, Regarding the divine grace as beyond the white clouds, You have completely become prisoners of the deity Ōgetsuhime, And being ignorant of the suffering of the lower people— Are you Buddhas, demons, or serpents? Ah, Lord Mototeruwake, master of the castle! Ah, Lady Mototeruhime, mistress of the house! From this auspicious and blessed day of birth, Renew and rebuild your body and soul, Revere the deities, cherish the people As your own wife and children, Realize the great favors of heaven and earth, And keep to the righteous path! Judging others is surely not A deed that a mere human being should do. Do not judge those below; show them compassion!"

Because there is the lower, there is also the higher; Because there is the higher, there is also the lower. The higher and the lower, joining together in full array, Uniting their strength, While fastening their innermost hearts into one, Correcting by proclamation the crooked things of the world, Reflecting upon the Naohi-no-Mitama, May you conform to the mind of God. Thus they sang solemnly and grandly, concluded their dance, and took their seats.

Hereupon, Mototeruwake and his wife, who had hitherto unwillingly continued their bodily-dominant, spiritually-subordinate actions due to the oppression of Uralhiko, now, through the practical admonition of these two heralds, became ashamed of their weak will and irresolute conduct, roused a great courageous heart to fundamentally reform the divine government, complied with the divine revelations of the Ōkami, and came to serve the divine rule of Izanagi-no-Ōkami. Far and near, without anyone knowing who said it first, the names of these two deities were praised as Ōtomadoko-no-Kami and Ōtomadome-no-Kami.

Hiromichiwake was captivated by Izumohime's cool voice and her graceful dance, and unconsciously stood up from his seat, placed his hands upon the balustrade of the high tower, and gazed in fascination. In an instant, no sooner had the balustrade creaked than the body of angel Hiromichiwake plunged splashing into the deep moat below. Shivering from the cold, when he came to his senses, behold, contrary to all expectation, the body of Oni was exposed to the cold wind upon the rectangular rock of Mount Takakuma.

A Guidepost for the Path

The Heavenly Emperor, bestowing upon the Spirit of Mizu an infinite Naohi spirit, sent him down with a profound divine will in order to illuminate the dark world, remove the grime, cleanse the mud, and destroy the demons. Those who desire to be saved in heaven shall be saved, and those who rebel against the Mizu-no-Mitama shall bring ruin upon themselves.
