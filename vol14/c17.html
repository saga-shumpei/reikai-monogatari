Chapter 17: A Momentary Respite [567]

第17章　一寸一服〔567〕

§ 1

When God manifested openly upon the face of the earth, dividing the righteous from the wicked, began this wondrous epic of the Age of the Gods. In the departed year of Kanoto-Tori (1921), in the Chrysanthemum Month (ninth month of the lunar calendar), the boundless grace of the Kami, desiring to redeem the mortals of this world, commenced at midday upon the cherished eighteenth day (two times nine). Within the detached pavilion of Shōun-kaku, consecrated to the sacred pine of the divine age, accompanied by the murmuring clear waters of the Ogumo River and the wintry gales sweeping through the lush green avenue of pine trees, the Kototama thundered through heaven and earth! Its glorious virtues resounded far over the Great Bridge and through the sacred capital of Aya (Ayabe), like the crystal waters of the Jordan flowing beneath God's profound benevolence—a sacred epic resounding in pristine clarity, unveiling the divine realms of good and evil,

Expounding the true lineage and origins of mortal souls, fathoming the unprecedented warp and woof of the Three Realms (Heaven, Earth, and the Netherworld) along the innermost recesses of the Way! Serving in the pavilions of Takamanohara and the Dragon Palace surrounded by green-fenced mountains, three devoted disciples—Toyoji Sotoyama, Shigeo Sakurai, and Seiji Taniguchi (standing at the gateway of the valley, the entry to Deguchi)—joined their hands, together with Chōzō Itō with brush ever flowing like the morning sunbeams, creating new transcriptions. To unfold in full and minute detail this sacred epic of five hundred and sixty-seven sections, dedicated unto Miroku (567), the God of the Future, their deep hearts were knit in solemn vow (Katō), striving to proclaim and transmit the luminous teachings of the Great Origin (Oomoto). Even as the Auspicious Moon (Zuigetsu, Onisaburō) shined resplendent in the eternal sky, weaving the divine decrees of warp and woof,

To establish them steadfastly for thousands and myriads of ages, reclining upon his couch, he set into motion the chariot of his tongue (the holy vehicle of divine utterance)! Safely traversing the grand highway of spiritual communion, he reacheth the culmination this very night. In the fragrance of blossoming plum trees during the second month (Kisaragi), on the twenty-fifth, twenty-sixth, and twenty-seventh days, he safely brought to completion this narrative of the Miroku (567) Volume. In the inner chamber of Shōun-kaku beneath the lamplight, he unveiled the sacred mysteries: the scribe holding the brush was Master Sotoyama; chanting of the Miroku realm were Matsumura and Tanimura from the deep mountains; completing the discourse on the divine plan was Kitamura; and knit in firm resolve (Katō), opening the celestial portal of the divine realm, spoke the holy lips of Wani (Onisaburō)! Making all manifest for the redemption of humanity, blessing and celebrating the grace of the peaceful divine reign through ages eternal,

Hereupon, in glorious celebration, the dream-epics of the Spirit Realm have safely achieved their great fourteenth volume! Long live the Miroku Divine Governance for ten thousand years! Long live the Miroku Divine Governance for ten thousand times ten thousand years!

(Postscript) This volume was dictated and completed within three days: the 25th, 26th, and 27th days of the second month of the lunar calendar in the eleventh year of Taishō (1922).

📖 現代日本語訳

神が表舞台にお現れになり、善と悪とを立て分ける不可思議な神代の物語。去りし辛酉の年（大正十年）、神の聖なる教えを菊月（陰暦九月）に、神の恵みは世の人々をお救いくださり、肉からぬ（二九＝十八、憎からぬ）十八日の真昼頃、松の神代に因む松雲閣の離れの間にて、清らかな小雲川の流れや、緑滴る松並木を吹き抜ける木枯らしに送られて、天地に轟き渡る言霊の功徳は広く大橋や綾の都（綾部）にも満ち、大神の深き恵みによりヨルダン川の清流の如く清らかに響き渡った物語。善と悪との神界の、

身魂の素性を説き明かし、前人未聞の三界（天界・地界・幽界）の経綸を探る真理の深奥。青垣山をめぐらせた高天原や竜宮の館に仕える門弟たち、外山豊二、桜井重雄、出口の入口なる谷口（谷の口）、名も清治（谷口雅春）の三人の男たち、朝日の影の伊藤長き（伊藤長蔵）筆のすさびも新しく、五百六十七節（五六七章）の五六七（みろく）の神に因んだこの物語を詳細に解き明かそうと、肝に銘じ心に堅く（加藤）誓い合った。明るき神の教えの大本を述べ伝えようと、大空に輝く瑞月（出口王仁三郎）が、縦糸と横糸を織り成す神の教えを、

千代に八千代に打ち立てようと、床の上に横たわり、転がし始めた口車（神の宣託の言霊の車）。通う大道も恙なく歩み通した今日の宵、梅が咲き匂う如月（陰暦二月）の二十五、二十六、二十七日に恙なく、五六七の巻（第十四巻・五六七号）の物語を、松雲閣の奥の間にて灯火の下で説き明かす。筆を執る人は外山氏、五六七の御代を松村や深山の奥の谷村氏、神の経綸もここに恙なく語り了えて北村氏、固く（加藤）結ばれた神界の扉を開く王仁（出口王仁三郎）の口。世の人々のために明らかにし、治まる御代の広大な御恵みを、千代に八千代に言祝ぎたたえて、

ここに目出度く『霊界物語』の夢物語、十余り四（第十四巻）の大巻を作り終えた。五六七（みろく）神政万々歳！ 五六七（みろく）神政万々歳！

（附言）本巻は大正十一年旧暦二月二十五日、二十六日、二十七日の三日間にわたって完成した。
