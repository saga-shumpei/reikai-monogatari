Chapter 12: Fox 穴 [1512]

狐穴〔1512〕

§ 1

Chiruteru, the master of the mansion, having been strangled with all her might by his wife Chiruna with a tea-bag, fainted with a groan. Gradually recovering from the pain, he once again let the flames of love burn, and ordered Heeru's Yunkeru to bring the Hime to him for care. Feigning illness, he lay in the inner chamber, groaning 'un, un, un, un'. Though he waited and waited, there was no word from Yunkeru. Pricking up his ears to listen, he saw that instead of doing his duty, Yunkeru was doing his best to embezzle the Kanjinkaname-no-naisu. Enraged like a Rasetsu, his hair standing on end, he stood up, clutching the tea-bag in one hand, and went to the Hime's Sumika.

To his surprise, he found Yunkeru being held down by the Hosōde of Hatsuwakahime, gasping and suffering in a most Oka manner. Chiruteru could not help but burst into laughter, his Shitsuto forgotten. Chiruteru: 'Ahahaha, Hime-sama! Truly, your Goshinriki is Atsupare. This Captain is deeply impressed. That such a brave Jiyojiyōbu should come to this mansion is truly a Tamamono of Kami. It is as if Bonten, Taishiyaku, Jizaiten, and Ōkunihiko-no-Mikoto have shown me that I should cherish you, who have come to comfort this Captain who has been separated from his Niyōbau, and that we should pledge to be together for Nise and Chitose. Truly, I am grateful.'

Chiruteru: 'You wretch, Yunkeru! Without fear or hesitation before the Hime-sama, you, a commander of the army, have dared to commit such violent and lecherous acts. You are a brute of the highest order. From now on, this Captain dismisses you. Leave this place at once, wander the windy fields, and end your days as a beggar. Become a beggar, and repent before heaven and earth for the sin of tormenting the Hime-sama, who is like a celestial maiden. Come, come, leave at once! This mansion is the home of this Captain for a thousand generations, and it is no place for the souls of those as impure as you.'

Chiruteru: 'Ibuki-do-nushi-ōkami, I pray you, sweep away this excrement-producing machine that has defiled this mansion, quickly and swiftly, with the winds of Shinatobe. Ah, Kannagara-tamahi-mamori-tamae. I pray to you on behalf of the Hime-sama, the messenger of Kami. Ahahaha, hatsuha, ahahaha! How truly pathetic you look. This is the end for you, Yunkeru, who did not know your own place. How pitiful it is to see divine punishment arrive so suddenly and bring such utter shame upon you. Ihihi, hitsuhi, ihihi!'

Hatsuwakahime: 'I do not think this man is all that hateful, but I did this to test his strength. Nevertheless, there is something about the breath of a Masurao that is strangely pleasing.'

Chiruteru, hearing this song, looked surprised.

Chiruteru: 'This is strange. Do you pity the vessel of the Magatsu-no-kami? I am anxious.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'The man who bound his Nise-no-tsuma and threw her into the dark storehouse is hateful. I, too, have become a Niyōbau, and I fear I might be bound and thrown into the storehouse as well. Rather than entrust my body to a man with a rough heart, I shall seek a man with a gentle heart.'

Heeru, raising his face from under the hand that Hatsuwakahime had suddenly released,

Heeru: 'This is strange. I did not know the heart of Hatsuwakahime, and I regret my resentment. I shall cast aside my position as commander of Yunkeru's army and stay close by the Hime's side.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'How I long for the Captain, who is even more noble than Yunkeru.'

Chiruteru: 'Hatsuwakahime, I now understand the heart of Kami. I regret my resentment and lamentation. Yunkeru, what do you make of these words? Give up, for this is a love you cannot attain.'

Heeru: 'Even if you say such things with your lips, Heeru reaches the depths of your heart. No matter how skillful the Captain's demonic prayers may be, who would listen? Especially since the Hime-gimi, who is equal to Kami, knows the impurity of your heart.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'I have no desire for such artificial ranks as Captain or Yunkeru. I only desire a man who is a man. Since women are delicate creatures, I do not care if he is dark-skinned, lame, one-eyed, buck-toothed, or pigeon-chested; I only wish to have a man of great strength as my husband.'

With that, he immediately lunged at Chiruteru. Chiruteru said, 'What an impudent fellow,' and they instantly grappled and began to push and kick each other. Sweat oozed from their naked bodies like a waterfall, and as they slipped and slid, the two suddenly separated to the left and right. Pushing, thrusting, and shoving, they fought with desperate activity, and the intensity of their struggle appeared like that of a dragon and a tiger. However, Chiruteru's strength seemed to prevail, and Heru was finally pushed down and thrown into the deep Otoshiana. Immediately, Teku became stark naked and lunged at Chiruteru. Chiruteru dismissed him, saying, 'What an impudent small-fry warrior,' and grappled with him. While making the earth shake with their stomping, like Tosa dogs biting each other or Tajima cattle goring each other, they exhausted all their secret techniques, became covered in sweat, and for about half an hour, they snarled at each other for dear life. Hatsuwakahime-sama watched the two fighting and laughed happily, 'Ohohoho. Ohohoho.' Finally, Chiruteru was twisted down by Teku and was cruelly thrown into the Otoshiana. This Otoshiana was about four or five ken deep, and a deep basement was built at the bottom, and it was equipped so that one would not get hurt even if they fell. Teku felt relieved, his tense heart relaxed, and he collapsed on the spot, exhausted. Prior to this, the four, Wakkusu, Heruman, Ekisu, and Eru, had passed through the gate of the Sekishiyo, and hearing a strange sound in the backyard, they ran over to see two men wrestling in the nude, so they stood nearby and watched to see how the match would turn out. テク『ウン　ヨシ、女房の云ふ事なら、何でも聞いてやる。女房に対して忠実な、親切な、慇懃な、それはそれは柔しい、同情の深い、誠に結構なテクさまだ。こんな亭主をもつた女房も世界一の幸福ものだ。お前は幸運の神に見舞はれたものだ。俺も亦其通りだ。「よい嬶持つたら一生の徳だよ、近所も喜ぶ、テクさまも喜ぶ、テクさま所か悴も喜ぶ」。オイ、バラモン信者のワックス以下外三人、恋の勝利者のテクさまが此館の関守だ。さうして此ナイスが内事一切を構ふのだ。貴様もよい所へ来て呉れた。今日から俺の家来にしてやらう、サア此方へ来て一杯飲め』

ワックス『有難う厶います……エキス、ヘルマン、エルどうだ、矢張り私の予言は違ふまいがな。キヨの港に着いたら意外の喜びが湧いて来ると云つたらうがな』

エル『そりやさうだ。尻の千も叩かれて苦労した苦労の塊の花が咲いたのだからなア』

ワックス『シー、尻の話はもうやめて呉れ、馬鹿だな。こんな所まで来て恥を曝す奴があらうかい』

テク『こりや　こりや四人の家来共、何を云つて居るのだ。新夫婦のお祝ひ酒でも呑まないか。この館には確り仕込むであるから幾ら呑みても大丈夫だ。十日や二十日飲み続けても少しも構はぬのだ。親方が大黒主だから太いものだぞ』

ワックス『ヤ有難い、お目出たう、それなら御言葉に甘へて頂きます』

初稚『モシ、貴方こちの人、テクさま、妾のお手々を握つて頂戴、握手しませうか』

テク『ヨシ、お出た。握手だらうがキッスだらうが些とも遠慮は入らぬ』

と猿臂を伸ばして柔かい初稚姫の手を握つた。忽ち其手から白い毛がモジャモジャと生え出した。ハツと驚いた途端に驢馬のやうな大きな白狐となつて竹箒のやうな太い尻尾をブラリブラリと振つて居る。テクは「こいつは耐らぬ」と一生懸命裏門より、命辛々逃げ出した。ワックス外三人は呆気に取られ、逃げ行く途端以前の陥穽に一蓮托生、四人ともにバサバサと落ち込むで仕舞つた。

初稚姫に変化て居た怪物は、三五教を守護する、旭の白狐であつた。旭はノソリノソリと庭園の木の茂みを潜つて何処ともなく、其姿をかくした。
