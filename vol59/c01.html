Chapter 1: 逆艪 [1501]

逆艪〔1501〕

§ 1

Across the vast expanse of Lake Kiyo, the weather suddenly changed, and the ship was tossed up and down, left and right, by the surging waves.

Wacks, a man of evil and lawless ways, had his essential helmsman swept away by the raging billows, leaving him unable to advance or retreat in the misery of the sea route.

Regaining his composure, he stood up and began to row the oars with reckless abandon, anxious to reach some shore, but his inherent nature was that of a bat in a village without birds, one who had strutted and boasted in a corner of Mount Terumon.

The divine punishment for this immediately came to pass upon the lake; the more he grew anxious, the more the old, decaying ship creaked and groaned.

Wacks and the other two were blinded by the surface of the waves, losing sight of even the direction as they struggled against the surging billows and left their fate to Heaven.

Wacks: 'Bonten, Taishiyaku, Jizaiten, and the Great God Ōkunihiko, I pray Thee, protect us. Encountering this storm upon the sea, we tremble in awe of the divine trial and are finally resolved to repent. For the sake of saving all sentient beings, we shall shave our long hair, renounce this world to become monks, and while striving in constant diligence, we shall serve the true teachings of the Great God of supreme benevolence and love. Ah, O Imperial Gods, O Imperial Gods, have mercy upon the repentance of us four and cease this hurricane. I pray to Thee with a sincere heart. No matter how strong the demons may be, no matter how many the possessing spirits, even if Smart should come, the path of the Brahmin's teaching, which is sincerity alone, shall save the world. May the mountain wind of Mount Terumon calm quickly and protect the fortune of our journey. I pray for this earnestly. We, who were expelled from the sacred ground of Mount Terumon after having painful moss added to it, have no choice but to leave for the capital of Haruna in the Moon Country, to reform from the bottom of our hearts, and without sparing our lives, to offer our souls to the Great God Daikoku. It is my resolve to spread the divine will in all directions with a single, undisturbed heart. Please, O Imperial Gods, let us proceed safely to the flower-blooming shore of Kiyo Port. I pray to the Gods with all my heart.'

He was rowing the oars while singing this with all his might. Whether the Great God of the Brahmins had heard Wacks's prayer, or whether the Great God of the Omoto of the San-Go-Kyo had granted it, the hurricane miraculously ceased instantly. The terrifying, raging waves and billows gradually calmed until they were as still as a mirror, and the waves began to sparkle in the sunlight. Mount Terumon appeared ahead, revealing its majestic figure high above the clouds, with a belt of clouds tied around its mid-slope, looking down upon the lake surface with composure. Wacks, as if having received welcome rain in a parched rice field, suddenly recovered his energy; the fear and trembling of his soul, which had been praying to the Gods in a time of desperation, had vanished somewhere, and he began to talk back with his usual insolence.

Wacks: 'Hey, Ex, you extortionist teacher Herman, you six-hundred-yen robber, and you, El, the crusher of testicles, what are you dawdling for? Why don't you lift your heads? You are truly hopeless creatures. Look at this, even the hurricane and the raging waves have calmed down instantly at the prayer of Wacks-sama, and now they are looking as if they were just dreaming. Surely, the power of Wacks-sama is truly great, is it not?' Ex: 'Hah, you talk big. When you were attacked by fear, trembling like a general, and started begging with that pitiful voice, your face was something that could not even be painted. At such times, we were composed and immovable like a mountain, and we left our fate to Heaven with that attitude. You are so obsessed with life that you act so disgracefully at such a time. What is this, you are not even manly. Running back and forth on this narrow, limited boat, your wretchedness is truly a disgrace to us men.' Wacks: 'Hey, what are you blathering about? You will be punished again, and this time the boat will surely capsize. If that happens, even if you cry, Wacks's savior will not know you. It is to your advantage to repent quickly. If you do not repent, I will show you something terrible... you know that is what the Great Jizaiten-sama has instructed, don't you?'

Wakkusu: 'In any case, we must also cross this Unabara, so we can catch up on the way, squeeze the oil out of them sufficiently, and make them meet their Ōjiyō.'

Ekisu: 'I am waiting for us to drift. I do not know how much more at ease I would be if we reached the Rikuchi as soon as possible and traveled on foot from there. After all, there is no fear of drowning in the mountains by accident.'

Wakkusu: 'The other party is sailing in a straight line, while if we are to cross mountains and valleys and follow a difficult path, who knows when we will reach the port of Kiyo. How about we decide to proceed along this Suiro?'

Ekisu: 'No matter what, it is impossible, is it not? Because everyone, this one and that one, is nothing but a fool who has no knack for steering a boat.'

Eru: 'Hey, why not hang that fool on this Kitakaze and sail in a straight line? If you do that, there is no need to break your bones operating the oar, and the God of Wind will naturally carry us to the other party.'

Wakkusu: 'Indeed, that is a good idea.'

He pulled the rope with a rattling sound and hoisted the sail, which had turned brown. Immediately, the sail caught the wind like a bow, and while making a sound of 'saa, saa, saa, saa,' the boat slid forward with great force. Eru, holding the oar in his hand with an uncertain air, began to steer the boat while singing a Kōno song.

Eru: '(Oiwake) Even a tiger crosses a thicket of a thousand ri, why can this lake not be crossed? (Yasugibushi style) There is one who failed to steal the precious jewel of the God's mansion. The moon is in the sky at the Terumon mansion, and the figure of Debisu is like a flower or snow. Is it a butterfly or a maggot that comes yearning for the scent of flowers? The son of the steward Wakkusu, who has accumulated karma, is now crying on the lake. Crying until dawn at the Terumon mansion, is this the awakening from the dream of this world? (Ryukyu style) The wind sends the boat from the north, the wind that sends it is the breath of Kerina. First, a living parting from the Yakkan-jiji, and a tearful parting from Princess Debisu.'

Wakkusu: 'Hey, hey, Eru, what are you spouting? It is useless. You, it would be better if you rested a little. I will take the oar from now on and sing one for you.'

Eru: '(Ryukyu style) Wakkusu-sama, who has been exposed, is getting angry, and the waves are rising.'

While singing this, he let go of the oar suddenly. Wakkusu grasped the oar quickly.

Wakkusu: 'Hey, that was a close call, I almost had the oar taken by the waves. Can you not be a little more careful? Once this is taken, we cannot steer the boat where we want, can we? You are an Ahau.'

Eru: 'Hmph, well, let me remain an Ahau. If one becomes a clever person, a wise person, or a skillful person, one is used as a tool by all the fools. If one is even a little bit talented, they pester me, saying, "Eru-sama, please write this signboard," or "Please write the name of this bridge," or "Please write the cover of this ledger," and I am made a plaything for the fools. One should not become a scholar or a wise man. Wakkusu-sama, I leave it to you. You are truly magnificent when you hold the oar. It looks as if you are putting strength even into your toes. Do not let yourself be knocked away by the handle of the oar you are holding, like the boatman before.'

Scratching the tip of his nose three or four times, he lay down on the bottom of the boat. Wakkusu grasped the oar and gazed out over the wide surface of the lake.

エキスさま甘いエキスふ新し男 蛸のお化けと人が云ふ。 吸いついて鼠泣きせうと夢みた男 猫に逐はれて逃げ出した。 猫かぶり薬鑵爺の機嫌をとりて 居つた甲斐なく馬鹿にされ。 肝腎の金は他人にぼつたくられて 尻にお金の叩き払ひ。 天葬式泣いて笑うて悔んで踊る 義理泣き女のホクソ笑』

ワックス『コラ、エキス、湖上で死ぬだの死なぬのと、ナニ不吉なことをほざくのだ。又、颶風が襲来するぞ。チツと言霊を慎まぬか』

エキス『手がだるい、腹が立つ、極道息子と湖上を越せば あちら此方に信天翁。 信天翁、運上取らうとワックス目がけ バタバタ翼を打つてゐる。 ゆすられて、泣き泣き放り出す惜しい金 首をつなぐと泣く涙』

ワックス『オイ、ヘルマン、エキスの奴、仕方がないから、汝一つ目出度い唄を唄つて宣り直してくれないか』

ヘルマン『さうだなア、のり直さうと云つたつて、外に空舟もなし、矢張乗続けるより仕方がないぢやないか。玉国別は甘く乗直して、サツサとお先へやつて行きよつたが、俺達は一体行末が案じられて仕方がないワ。最前から、実は前途を案じ、チツと許り憂愁の涙に沈みてゐた所だ。あーあ。仕方がない、……寄辺渚の捨小舟、どこへ取つく島もなしか……ぢやと云つて、湖水に投身して魚腹に葬られるのも、何だか気が利かないやうだし、あゝ何うしたら可からうかなア。俺やモウ世の中が厭になつたのだ。何とかして三五教のムニヤ　ムニヤ　ムニヤ』

§ 4

Wakkusu: 'Ya, what do you say? You mean to say you wish to become a Deshi of Ananai-kyō, do you?'

Heruman: 'Naani, it is just that if I do not stand up as a man to rival Ananai-kyō, I shall not be able to show my face in Seken. We had schemed against the Tamakuniwake party to that extent, and just when we had reached the point of nine-tenths of the way to taking our revenge, a Megamisama like Manjushri Bosatsu or Avalokiteshvara came to meet them with a magnificent ship, and they are even capable of such a feat as crossing the Kaijiyō riding on a dog. No matter what one says, they are something impressive, even if they are our enemies.'

Wakkusu: 'Saa, that is the very Yuen of why a Mahafutsukahi is a Mahafutsukahi. Is it not said that there is no Fushigi in the Shiyōhafu, and that a Kunshi does not speak of Kuwairikiranshin? It is surely Jiyahafu.'

Heruman: 'Even so, compared to your Biriyokuranshin, is it not much better? Somehow, I am beginning to... like... that Ananai-kyō. I cannot help but feel that there is a tremendous Kejime between the Kamisama of Ananai-kyō and the gods of Brahmanism in terms of Seijiya and Zenaku.'

Wakkusu: 'Which do you say is the righteous and which is the evil?'

Heruman: 'As for calling it evil, I cannot judge it suddenly. Well, I will not know until I go as far as I can. However, rest assured, as I am a Brahmanist by nature, I am not a spineless man who would easily drown in the teachings of a heretical sect. But think about it carefully; are we not the Puritans of Brahmanism? Despite that, we were whipped and expelled from the Brahman Hall in front of a crowd, so perhaps we have been abandoned by the gods. If that is so, as they say there is a god who picks up what another god has discarded, if we have indeed been discarded, since a person cannot stand in this world without a religion, we must think of something.'

Wakkusu: 'Nani, do not worry. Once we arrive at the port of Kiyo, everywhere is within the sphere of influence of Brahmanism, so if we can skillfully capture the Mahafutsukahi of Ananai-kyō, or if we cannot manage by force, we shall report them to the checkpoint and show our merit; then we can once again be magnificent Puritans of Brahmanism, receive our travel permits at the checkpoint safely, and go to the capital of Haruna as we please. My, how fast the boat is! This is entirely thanks to the gods of Brahman. Saa, Eru, move aside, I shall take the oar and handle it for a while.'

Saying this, they took turns gripping the oar, and aided by a favorable wind, they arrived safely at the port of Kiyo on the evening of the third day.

ヘルマン『邪と申して、俄に判断がつかないワ。マア行く所迄行かねば分らない。併し乍ら安心してくれ、俺は素よりバラモン教徒だから、メツタに外道の教に溺没するやうな無腸漢ぢやないからのう。併し乍らよく考へてみよ、俺達四人はバラモン教のピュリタンぢやないか。それにも拘らず、バラモン館を大勢の前で笞刑をうけて放逐されたのだから、神様から見放されたのかも知れないよ。さすれば捨てる神もあれば拾ふ神もありといふから、実際捨てられたとすれば、人は無宗教で此世に立つてゆけないから、何とか考へねばなるまい』

と言ひ乍ら、代る代る櫓を握り、順風に助けられて都合好くキヨの港へ三日目の夕方安着したりける。
