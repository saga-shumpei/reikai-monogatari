Chapter 23: 鳩首 [1523]

鳩首〔1523〕

§ 1

Yakkosu, Hāru, and Sabōru, the three of them, huddle in a corner of Itahikomaru, exposing faces of Fuan as they whisper in Mitsudan.

Yakkosu: 'Hey you Riyōnin, this fellow is a bit suspicious. Itahiko, one of the Sendenshi who abandoned us and left, has become the Taishiyō, built this many ships, and is leading the Ichizoku of Shiyōjiyō back; there must be some deep scheme behind it. If he does something like a Mane of scratching us in front of the Shiyōjiyō, all those Shiyōjiyō will gather in one Hitotoko, and since they are fellows good at Mane, they might even take our only Inochi, ending it all. Thinking of this, I have no desire to drink sake anymore; what do you think?'

Hāru: 'Nonsense, I hear that Ananai-kyō is a Muteikaushiyugi and Hakuaishiyugi, so even if they killed the likes of us three, it does not mean the rice of the Sekai would become cheaper, nor is there any Dauri that the Akuma would be Konzetsu, so they would not Metsuta do such a thing. Well, you should feel Anshin. I have a feeling that they might somehow save us.'

Sabōru: 'No, you cannot be that Anshin. They probably intend to take us to some rocky shore and subject us to monkey attacks. That fellow called Ananai-kyō is sly, so fearing that if he kills people with his own hands it would be a violation of divine law, he intends to borrow the hands of the monkeys and finish us three off as Brahmins. It is better to strike first, for he who strikes first controls the others. Shall we not twist off the neck of Itahiko? If we do that, the monkeys will surely do a Mane, and every ship will twist off the necks of their captains. No matter what, the Shiyōjiyō have a familiarity with us, having lived together for even three days. Also, when we were about to be swallowed by the great serpent, we provided support, and since they are beasts that know gratitude, there is no Dauri that they would not save us when they see our danger. However, since the fellows called monkeys do a Mane of what others do first, the one who is late is defeated; shall we not carry out the plan?'

Yakkosu: 'Wait, wait, it is not just Itahiko alone; a strong man called Anchī is on this ship, so if we carelessly make a move, it might end up in a situation where a cornered mouse bites the cat. Why don't we say this or that, make them drink a Dotsusari of sake to get into their good graces, get them dead drunk, and then sweetly do the deed just like wringing the neck of a sleeping bird?'

Hāru: 'Do you Riyōnin doubt people to the very end? They say that suspicion creates ghosts, and the ghost of your own heart torments you. No matter how much of a magician of Ananai-kyō he may be, he cannot torment those who are gentle. Well, it might be safer to pray to Lord Daijizaiten than to worry about such things in advance.'

Yakkosu: 'Ah, anyway, I am in such Fuan that I cannot bear it. However, as Sabōru said, one side is a Sendenshi with peerless divine power, and the other is a strong man, so first let us sweetly get into their good graces, get them dead drunk, and then carry out the plan. That is the best means and method. Hey, Sabōru, you are always proud of your voice, so try singing with a beautiful voice that will make one tremble. If you do that, Itahiko will surely let his guard down.'

Sabōru, while jerking his head three or four times, began to sing from the corner of the ship in a thin, cool voice.

In the world of the three thousand Sekai, There are four things that are precious. The first and most precious, Is the sun that rises in prosperity. Next, guarding the night, Is the moon, full, clear, and bright. He who created and solidified the earth, The guardian god of Ananai-kyō, Lord Ōkunotokotachi-Ōmikami. By the grace of this Lord God, Bonten, Taishaku, Jizaiten, And Lord Ōkunohiko-no-Kami, Are living in this world. One more precious person, Famous in Ananai-kyō, Itahiko-tsukasa who guards this ship. To be on the same ship, With such a precious person, And to be returning, Across the mirror-like sea, is a blessing.

ヤッコス『まてまて、伊太彦一人ぢやない、此船にはアンチーといふ力強が乗つてゐるから、ウツカリ手出しをせうものなら、それこそ窮鼠却て猫を咬むやうな破目になるかも知れぬ。何とかかとか云つて、沢山酒を呑ませ機嫌を取つて酔ひ潰し、寝鳥の首を締めるやうに甘くそこはやらかそぢやないか』

ハール『お前達両人はどこ迄も人を疑ふのか。疑心暗鬼といつて、自分の心の鬼が自分を責めるのだ。何程三五教の魔法使だとて、おとなしい者を苦しめるこたア出来ぬからのう。マアそんな取越苦労をするよりも大自在天様を御祈願した方が安全かも知れぬぞ』

ヤッコス『あ、兎も角俺は険難で堪らない。併し乍らサボールの言つた通り、一方は神力無双の宣伝使、一方は力強だから、先づ甘く機嫌を取り酒に酔ひつぶし、其上決行しよう。それが最良の手段方法だ。オイ、サボール、汝常から声自慢だから、一つ慄ひつくやうな美声を出して唄つてみよ。さうすりやキツと伊太彦が気を許すに違ひない』

サボールは首を三つ四つ縦にしやくり乍ら、細い涼しい声で、船の隅の方から唄ひ出したり。

『三千世界の世の中に 尊いものが四つある 第一番に尊きは 豊栄昇るお日イ様 次には夜を守ります 円満清朗のお月様 大地を造り固めたる 三五教の守り神 大国常立大御神 此神様の御恵で 梵天帝釈自在天 大国彦の神様も 此世に生きて厶るのだ モ一つ尊い御方は 三五教で名も高き 此船守る伊太彦司 こんな尊い御方と 一つの船に乗せられて 鏡のやうな海原を 帰つて行く身は有難い

§ 2

The Lord God of supreme benevolence and supreme love, without distinction between birds, beasts, insects, and fish, protects the life of each and every one of them, praying that it may endure even for a single day; how grateful we are for this. Furthermore, is it possible that He would not have compassion for the Ikimiya where the Kami resides, which is to serve the great Daikeirin of the Tenchi Kami? Even if the Kami has ever caused humans to suffer, perhaps by borrowing the hands of the Shiyōjiyō, the very essence of love would fundamentally disintegrate, and the qualification of the Kami would become zero. Could it be that He would not let us realize such a simple Dauri? We offer our Magokoro to such a benevolent Kami, serving Him morning and evening.

The Kamuzukasa of Ananai-kyō, and especially Itahiko-tsukasa, who is endowed with a most beautiful Mitama, is called by the people a precious Keshin of the Kami. Protected and returning with such a noble Shinjin, we feel as if we are riding on an Ōbune, and without worrying about the future, it would be best to partake of the excellent sacred sake and accompany the Shiyōjiyō-sama. Now, now, Senden-shi, bound by the deep grace of the Kami of Ananai-kyō, whenever I see your face, I think of you as a celestial being of the spiritual realm of Takamagahara. When I think of this, the Kami who protects the teachings of the Brahmins...

It feels as if there is a distinction between heaven and earth, like the moon and a snapping turtle, or clouds and mud; I cannot help but feel this way. From now on, I shall abandon the teachings of the Brahmin and become a true believer of Ananai. We, who have served as spies and worked as pirates as a side business, repent from the bottom of our hearts and become your disciples. No matter how many sins we may have, please remember the heart of the Tenchi Kami and do not kill us. I have already polished my soul, which does not even cast the shadow of an evil Kami, into a mirror-like Mitama, and with your pure soul,

please peer into the depths of my heart from corner to corner, clear away our doubts, and please help the three of us. Brahma, Indra, and the Lord of Freedom—oops, wait, that is wrong. I pray before the Great Kuninotokotachi-Ōmikami, the ancestral Kami of heaven who created and solidified the Tenchi, and the hundred other Kami. Whether the sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth sinks, once we have repented, we shall never return to our former selves. The Tenchi Kami is also watching; please be at ease and partake of plenty of the excellent sacred sake. If you would do so,

the three of us will simultaneously take direct action—heave-ho! We shall offer our sincerity to the Kami, both directly and indirectly. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase, may the spirits be blessed.'

Itahiko: 'The ugly officials of the Brahmin are impatient and suspicious, the three of you. I shall clear my heart for you three; be at ease, you ugly officials of the Brahmin.'

Haal: 'I am grateful; since hearing those words of yours, my heart has widened and blossomed with joy.'

Yakkos: 'The clouds and mist of doubt have cleared, and I feel the joy of returning while being rocked by the waves of the vast ocean. All people are the living Mitama of the noble Kami; those who cause suffering are demons or devils. I, too, repent for having troubled the world while being called a demon or a great serpent.'

Anchee: 'Gathering in the small corner of the ship, three people full of doubt and four people with the sake. Itahiko-no-Kamitsukasa, be careful of the hearts of those who decorate their outward appearance.'

Itahiko: 'Everything is just Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase; I leave it all to the grace of the Kami. While sliding over the five-hundred-fold waves of the vast ocean, I set my heart on the shore of Suma. The Shiyōjiyō-bune returning is valiant, and it brings back the spring of Tokoyo.'

Haal: 'Is Itahiko-no-Michitsukasa a Kami? My heart flourishes at your Kototama.'

Yakkos: 'Everything is left to the helm of the ship of Itahiko-sama's heart. Yet, I fear when the rough winds will blow and capsize the ship.'

Haal: 'A heart of doubt becomes a demon of darkness; clear the clouds from your chest quickly.'
