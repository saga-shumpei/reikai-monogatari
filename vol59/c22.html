Chapter 22: 五葉 Pine [1522]

五葉松〔1522〕

§ 1

Following the command of Itahiko, Anchi, who was selected as the Fukukuwan to welcome the Shiyōjiyō, stood at the bow as the Sentō and began to sing a Gaisenka with great courage. The Amata Shiyōjiyō were stirred to excitement, and clapping their hands in unison, they took the Hiyōshi while crying out 'Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu,' which was truly Omoshiro.

Anchi began to sing. The song was as follows: 'When I came to the island of the Shiyōjiyō, the Saagarariuō, who had been lurking deep at the bottom of this lake, saw that the Shiyōjiyōō had reached Kiiu, and leaping out from the sea, it coiled itself around the Ōihayama, which is known as the fortress of the island. It hung its long neck down, opened its great mouth wide, and spewed Dokuen, intending to swallow every single one of the Shiyōjiyō gathered on this island and fatten its own belly. How terrifying it was. Just then, Itahiko, the messenger of the Ananahi Kami, appeared leading Nijifu ships and chanted a Kazūta with a clear voice, and even the mighty Saagarariuō found its Shintai trapped there. Unable to endure the agony of its entire body being in Shōnetsu, it spat out its Guren tongue, writhed in Guren-guren, and suffered in torment until it fell into the Unabara and fled. How Oka it was.'

'Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu. Don-doko, don-doko, doko-doko-don. Thinking of the time when I was saved by the grace of Kami while suffering and writhing, having sunk into the water with the ship after encountering a terrifying great sea wind three years ago when I snuck out of the mansion with Bachiru-sama to fish for the fish of the Kosui, it feels as if I have already left hell and ascended to heaven.'

'Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu. Don-doko, don-doko, doko-doko-don. Drink, drink, drink, drink plenty. This sake, stored in the sake warehouse of Nijifu-ko, is for the Shiyōjiyō-sama to drink. Since Bachiru-sama is the parent of your souls, and the Shiyōjiyōhime has possessed the Saberu-hime of the mansion and is living a double life, you three hundred and thirty-three innocent Shiyōjiyō guests have nothing to worry about. You are returning to the spacious mansion at the southern foot of Mount Adsumo, where your fathers and mothers appear in Tokiwa-kashiwa. Rejoice and be brave, Shiyōjiyō. Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu.'

'Don-doko, don-doko, doko-doko-don. Even with so many passengers, they are all just the Kya-tsu-kya-tsu crowd who cannot speak human language, so I feel somewhat unenthusiastic. Nevertheless, all living things between heaven and earth are divided spirits of Kami. If I resign myself to the fact that I have brought foreigners who do not share a language, my heart will be at peace, but for the number of heads there are, there is hardly anyone to talk to.'

'Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. The present world of the eternal night is filled with the clamor of ten thousand evils; the roots of rocks and trees are in an uproar, and even the leaves of the grass speak out. With talk of universal suffrage, socialism, equality of the four classes, and so on, they are running about in a frenzy and cannot be settled. Compared to that miserable state, the rule of those who only say 'Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu' and do not assert their own opinions is an easy thing. Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu. Don-doko, don-doko, doko-doko-don.'

'It is written in the Fudesaki of the Ananahi-kyō that if the true virtue of Kami is present, people will remain silent, bow their heads, and receive the virtue of Kami without uttering petty logic. Thinking of this, Shiyōjiyō-sama, do you receive the grace of the Kami of heaven and earth in your spirits? Truly, when I look at the orderly group of Shiyōjiyō, I begin to dislike the human world. If they were human, it would be fine, but when I think that I have been living with wolves in human skin and foxes and badgers in disguise, it has become truly terrifying.'

'Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. Man is the child of Kami, the palace of Kami. How long will heaven remain silent, and how far will the earth refuse to speak? Man should emulate the Kami in heaven and earth; rather than chattering about useless things, one should receive the virtue of Kami in one's heart, always keep one's mouth firmly shut, and never speak ill of others or talk behind their backs. Not speaking is better than speaking. When I observe that even the Shiyōjiyō, who do not know language, live by the grace of heaven and earth, I suppose there is no need for language. Setting aside the true goodness and beauty, the good words and beautiful phrases that Kami has bestowed...'

§ 2

To make people angry and resentful, words without propriety should not be spoken; Shiyōjiyō-sama is a good mirror, I am truly impressed.

Kya-tsu kya-tsu kya-tsu kya-tsu, kya-tsu kya-tsu kya-tsu. Dondoko, dondoko, dokodokodon.

If I return safely to the village of Suma from here, I shall become like a newborn Akago and perform the practice of Mugon.

Ah, how brave, how brave. Upon the lake where the waves have calmed, and the wind is cool upon the lake, Hiyakuchō spread their wings and dance and play most joyfully. Is this the divine Mikuni upon the sea? Daishōmusū of Urokuzu, while escorting our boat, leap about pin-pin shyan-shyan, celebrating Bujitaihei.

They are escorting our Itsukau. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. How grateful I am for the blessings of Kami. Kya-tsu kya-tsu kya-tsu kya-tsu, kya-tsu kya-tsu kya-tsu. Dondoko, dondoko, dokodokodon.

I shall not speak, for it was because I spoke that my father became the Hitobashira of Nagara. If the pheasant did not cry, it would not be shot. If you sing a song, sing it pleasantly; a song is the voice of the Kami of Tenchi. Lining up the boats and advancing to Shiyōjiyō-jima, such is the Miyōga of the body of Anchi. The fifty-ninth volume, Shinzenbiai, the story of Shiyōjiyō Ketsupaku. The Nori of Kami that explains and teaches the Reikai exactly as it is. If one does not speak, the state of the distant Kamiyo cannot be made known to the people of the present world. Akiko Katō, pointing her lips, grabs the neck of her fountain pen and scribbles away. While stripping the snow garment from Mount Ōyama, she looks down upon the peaks of Kōrai. In the Yayoi sky without even a speck of cloud, I lie in the boat and travel through the sky. The Ark is another honorable name for the dictation stand.

The Goyo pine decorated on the floor. The Goyo pine that maintains the age of a thousand years, like the needle of a fountain pen, the leaves of the five spirits also flourish.
