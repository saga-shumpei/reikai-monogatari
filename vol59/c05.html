Chapter 5: 有升 [1505]

有升〔1505〕

§ 1

Hatsuwaka: 'If you are indeed Teku-sama, what is the nature of the business you have with me from the Captain-sama? I request that you convey it to me with all speed.'

Teku: 'I am a little intoxicated, so I may perhaps digress. I shall offer my apologies in advance. Well... it is nothing else but... that... that... if you pursue me with such short-tempered haste, I have no... leeway to counterattack. Let us first await the silence of the beauty-battery, and then proceed to the offensive at our leisure.'

He is a foolish stray who, having been suddenly deceived and granted the title of lieutenant, is thinking of conducting negotiations using military terminology for everything out of sheer joy.

Hatsuwaka: 'Batteries, attacks, counterattacks—these are dreadfully bloodthirsty words you use. I would be grateful if you could speak a little more gently.'

Teku assumed the posture of 'attention,' spread one hand upward near his chest, adopted a style resembling a Shinto priest holding a ritual tablet, and while leaning back slightly,

Teku: 'I am a lieutenant. I am one who has been specially dispatched to this camp as a messenger and scout by the order of the Captain-dono today. The mission of which I speak is nothing else. At the camp of Birch on the southern foot of Mount Adumos, a victory celebration for the soldiers was held. Regarding that, the Battalion Commander-dono dispatched me to this garrison as a special envoy. Since a banquet with only men is somewhat lacking in interest, the order is to summon the peerless, nice Hatsuwaka-hime-dono. In other words, this Teku is the military envoy of the Captain. I desire that you follow military law, or rather, follow the order of your superior, and depart, or rather, march out to the front.'

Hatsuwaka: 'Ohohoho, Teku-sama, you speak of such rigid things. I dislike sake, so I cannot possibly go to such a camp. Furthermore, if a woman enters the camp, military discipline will be disturbed, so I must decline this request above all else.'

Teku: 'This is outrageous. What do you take me for? I am a military police lieutenant. If you do not exercise a little caution when you speak, I shall be truly troubled. If you disobey the order, you shall be judged by military law and sentenced to execution by firing squad. This is a matter you must consider carefully. In truth, the Captain-sama has become infatuated with your beauty, has almost lost his soul, and the current state of the battle is such that he cannot hold back his desire. He has given a strict order to fire mortars or field guns if necessary to capture the castle. Come, I pray that you will quickly present yourself at the military gate.'

Hatsuwaka: 'This is yet another troublesome matter. I pray you do not speak of such things and return home. You say such things because you have been drinking sake. Is there any reason why a Captain-sama, who should be a leader of men, would invite a woman of my station into the camp?'

Teku: 'Oh dear, is there such a thing as a woman not being able to go to camp? Did the wife of the soldier named Keimei of Ueno not enter the camp, offer sake to her husband, and help him achieve military merit? You may be the second wife... or perhaps a candidate for the first wife. Come, I pray you give me a good report quickly. Or rather, I hope that you will march out to the front with me.'

Hatsuwaka: 'No matter what you say, I cannot bring myself to set foot in the camp. I am resolved that no matter what you say, I will not go.'

Teku: 'Hey, Princess-sama, you must not steal my trademark. "Arimasu" is a term reserved exclusively for soldiers, you see.'

§ 2

Chirunahime had been standing in the shade of the trees until now, listening to the dialogue between the two, and upon hearing the report from Teku that her husband was enamored with Hatsuwakahime, she became almost frantic, stamping her feet in the shade. At the sound of her footsteps, both Kanna and Heru were startled, and when they crept closer to look, it appeared to be none other than Chirunahime. Kanna said in a low voice,

Kanna: 'Is that not you, Okusama?'

Chiruna: 'You, did you hear what was just said? Since it is the case that Dannasama is infatuated with that woman, why have you not obtained the desired result quickly as I instructed?'

Kanna: 'Well, we have every desire to obtain the result, but it cannot be done as quickly as eating chiyazuke on an empty stomach.'

Chiruna: 'Oh, how irritating. Do you not realize what might happen if you keep dawdling? It is pathetic that with two rough men like you present, you cannot do anything about such an Amatsuchiyo. Come, show some courage and do something about it.'

Heru: 'Okusama, if we fail, will you take responsibility for it, Anata?'

Chiruna: 'There is no need for such worry. If you only proceed with strength, anything will succeed. If you keep dawdling, they might take that woman away and have her accompany Dannasama. Oh, how frustrating, how regrettable, how vexing. Is it that with two men present, you cannot do anything about a woman of that caliber?'

Kanna: 'Okusama, if you go that far, let us try one thing. However, if we do something too rough and lose her sympathy, it will be no good, so let us try to move her heart and achieve our goal by singing a song here. If you please, Okusama, you also use a feigned voice and support us.'

Chiruna: 'Come, try it quickly; if it does not go well, I will support you.'

Kanna: 'The commander of the Brahman army is here / Let us go and question Hatsuwakahime.'

Heru: 'Princess, do not abandon the heart of the man / Who comes secretly, lost in love for thee.'

Chiruna: 'If I surrender my body to the impure warrior of Chiruteru / I shall be laughed at by the world. / The lord of the Chiruteru army is rare in this world / And Chiruna Hime is here to hold him. / My love is a boat crossing the vast ocean / Braving the waves to reach the island of the gods. / O life of Hatsuwakahime, quickly / Leave the mansion and come out to the moon.'

Hatsuwakahime from within,

Hatsuwakahime: 'Believing that a god resides in the capital of Haruna in the land of the moon / I have come forward with longing. / Nevertheless, for a while longer in the land of Izumi / I shall rest my body and stay my feet.'

Chiruna: 'If you are staying your feet in this village / Then surrender your body to Kanna and Heru.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'How can one body serve two people? / I serve only the gods. / I have heard that the lord of the Chiruteru army is wise / But how could I ever surrender my body to him? / Can I ever endure serving the lord of Chiruteru / Who already has a wife like a young grass? / I am merely resting my breath for a while / Bound by the deep affection of Chiruteru.'

Teku: 'The life of the goddess Hatsuwakahime is destined / To be the second wife of the captain. / If you say you absolutely refuse, I am prepared / To drag you to the camp. / Come, if we do not go quickly, the sake will grow cold / It is to be used as a side dish for the sake. / If you abandon the pure man of the captain / And side with Kanna, your body shall be carved up. / The fellow called Kanna is a man / Like an ogre who carves and eats people. / Like carving while hanging Kanna over Heru / Each and every piece, Heru's love-garment.'

Kanna: 'It is already a matter of desperation, so let us display our valor / And try to see if we sink or swim. / Hey, look there, the hatred of that face of Teku who comes / To interfere with love. / From here on, it is direct action and brute force / I shall carry Hatsuwakahime away. / To be deceived by a clever excuse for an elbow-iron / How could a man's face stand anywhere?'

§ 3

Hatsuwaka: 'Teku-sama, dōzo, as everyone outside is saying various things as you see, I am very troubled. Dōzo, please return. And if you have business with the Captain-sama, let us meet again after you have returned. Also, I will serve as your drinking companion, though it may not be much, so dōzo, please speak well of me there.'

Teku: 'Even so, the Dannasama is very much in love with you, and if I do not have you come, my position as a lieutenant will also become zero. If you will make me a lieutenant, dōzo, please come quickly. This is my lifelong request.'

Teku became Yakekuso and began to sing in a loud voice.

Teku: 'It is no use, it is no use, it is all no use. With such a Kirei face, you reject even the words of the Captain of the Brahman army. Do not boast because your Kiriyō is good. Because there are as many Amatsuchiyo like you in the Sekai as there are. There is a limit to your Manshin. With a style as if you had attached eyes and nose to an Aobeutan, you are Chiyokozai Senban, a Bijinzura. Both Aiso and Koso are exhausted. Outside, there is Kanna-sama, and there is Heeru-sama who is mad with love. It would be better to give up. No matter how much one tries to persuade such an uncomprehending Mero, it is absolutely no use. Even though I have received the rank of military lieutenant from the Captain with such difficulty, I am in a situation where I must return it helplessly. I am frustrated and cannot bear it, but even if I say so, there is no way to deal with this witch. That Chilteru-Captain is so infatuated, lowering his eyes, treating the most important, most important Okusama as a nuisance, dragging her to the drinking seat, and trying to boast of his manliness, but it is pitiful. A wife who has such a husband must have a troubled heart. My heart has become sympathetic toward the feelings of Chiruna-hime. I have heard from people before that women are demons. If one glares with the cool, round eyes of a woman, even an iron castle will collapse with a rattle. Mountains, fields, houses, and storehouses will all be made into a mess. If such a woman appears, the Captain of the Brahman religion will also lose his composure and lose his strength, like a loach killed by sake, and while forgetting his essential military duties, he will surely fall into the tragic fate of being dismissed. When I think about it, I feel sorry for him. This Teku-sama has also completely given up on this mission. Taking the opportunity, I will return the position of spy, become the head clerk of Bachiru's house, drink sweet sake to my heart's content from morning till night, and live this short floating world interestingly, drinking and making merry, for the future is dark. More than anything, the taste of sake. For us, things like beauties are never worth counting. Ah, Kannagara-kamigami. The taste of warmed sake. For me, who lives on cold sake and sea bream soup, there is no pleasure that surpasses this in the three thousand worlds. Hatsuwaka-hime-no-Amatsuchiyo-sama, goodbye, I will take my leave, and while adding one excuse after another to the Captain-sama, I will report it badly. It would be good to report in detail that she is a stubborn woman who refuses to come out, saying that she will never go to the Captain's side even if she dies, because she is infatuated with both Kanna and Heeru. You had better think about it very carefully. Now that it has come to this, it is Yakekuso-sake. Come, warmed sake, warmed sake. The drunkenness has begun to wear off. I will return as soon as possible and drink sake. Everyone, goodbye to the sake.'

With that, he stomped his feet in a fit of anger, rattling the tatami and shoji, and went home, disappearing into the darkness.
