§ 1

Chiruna-hime entered the hitoma and, growing the horns of rinki, began tearing at her own hair, throwing her hairpin, and overturning her kiyōdai; the shitsunai suddenly became as if a nihiyakutōka storm were blowing through it. Returning home in an itsupaikigen state was Captain Chiruteru.

Chiruteru raised his voice from the kadoguchi:

Chiruteru: 'Hey, niyōbau, I have returned, why don't you open up quickly? It seems you have placed a tsutsupari against the door from the inside, as it will not open no matter how much I push or pull. Ah, if it were like this, I should have brought some heishi back with me; everyone, this doitsu and that koitsu, all got drunk and ended up in the gutter. I had anticipated that a teikiatsu would be attacking the mentei of the mountain Kami today, but what has happened here? Hey, will you open it or not, will you open it?'

He was striking the door with his clenched fists with itsushiyōkenmei.

Kanna was startled and hurriedly opened the door.

Kanna: 'Ah, Dannasama, welcome home.'

Chiruteru: 'Hmm, the gunmu was so busy that I ended up being late; the oku must have been waiting impatiently, I suppose.'

Kanna: 'Yes, about that okusama, I cannot say it in a loud voice, but the keisei is quite kenaku, so it is not yōi to approach her. I was waiting in fear, thinking that if you returned, a commotion would surely begin. I would like you to please enter the bedroom quietly without making any noise.'

Chiruteru: 'What, is the oku angry? No, that is interesting. I have been waiting for her to get angry and jump out of her own accord. Hey, Kanna, I have brought a good souvenir for you. It is in the number one warehouse, and it is a very nice target. If you can incite the niyōbau to jump out on her own, I will make that nice your niyōbau, so I snatched it away quietly. It is quite a magnificent thing, you know.'

Kanna: 'As expected of Captain-sama, thank you for your various considerations. After all, the nice in the back does not suit our kind.'

Chiruteru: 'What, did you speak to the nice in the back?'

Kanna scratched his head vigorously and said with difficulty:

Kanna: 'Yes, when I took a moment to probe the intentions of the nice, she is quite an impressive person. That fellow Teku came out boasting that he was a lieutenant, but he was silenced without a single retort and fled while talking nonsense. Truly, she is not a nice that suits the kind of humans. And she said, "I would like to meet the Captain-sama and hear the details," so please be happy. There is certainly a pulse.'

Chiruteru: 'The matter of the nice is something that cannot be helped by your power. Do not meddle; if you make her impatient, you will end up getting neither one nor the other. Since she says she wants to stay at my house for two or three days, there is no doubt she has a favorable opinion of me. However, since I have a niyōbau, that nice is also being reserved. You must be tactful about that, or it will be no good. If I did not have a niyōbau, it is a settled fact that she would come to me even if I left her alone, ufu-fu-fu.'

Kanna: 'Then, I shall try to struggle with it. If I intend to make the okusama angry, I will say some bad things about the Dannasama, so please be aware of that in advance.'

Chiruteru: 'Good, as long as the objective is reached, it is fine; I do not choose the means. I leave that part to you. Do it gently. However, if you make her too angry and she commits suicide, I will be in trouble. Please judge the situation and arrange it to the extent that she jumps out of the house.'

Then, the Shiyōji was torn with a crashing sound, and the Hibachi was sent flying out the window with a clattering noise. The sound of Setomono breaking was like kenkeraken, followed by gachya gachya gachya gatagatagata, and the sound of Shiko being stomped was dondondon, dosundosundosun.

Kanna: 'Excuse me, Okusama, if you behave so violently, will it not be troublesome for the Okusama who comes after?'

Chiruna: 'Hey, leave me be. This Heya is my Jiiu; whether I smash it to pieces or whatever happens, I shall not be a burden to any of you.'

Gatagata, dosundosun, baribaribari.

Startled by these Monōto, Chiruteru cried out,

Chiruteru: 'Hey, what are you doing?'

With a Ketsusau, he came running, grabbed Chiruna by the topknot with a Washizuka, and clenching his fist in his right hand, struck her three or four times. Chiruna, in a state of desperate Nobose, let out a Kinkirigoe and said,

Chiruna: 'Hey, you Akushiyōoyaji, this is Chiruna's Imawa farewell, a Shinimonoguru struggle.'

With a Mushiyabu, she grabbed his Kintama and pulled with all her might. Chiruteru collapsed on the spot with a groan.
