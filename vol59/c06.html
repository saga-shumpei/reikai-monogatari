Chapter 6: Cloud 隠 [1506]

雲隠〔1506〕

§ 1

Akisu, brandishing the Ōbishiyaku, ran back and forth through the crowd, exerting all his power to delight the many guests to the fullest, and added to the excitement of the drinking party by singing a song.

Akisu: 'In the forests of Mount Adsumosu, where the eagles build their nests and the hawks dwell, for this reason the villagers of Suma do not see the faces of sparrows or Mozu, nor have they even heard their voices. The Shiyōjiyō-sama, too, have all slipped away, leaving not a single one behind, and it is now a world of eagles and hawks. Nevertheless, today, sparrows, Mozu, and wrens, along with Senchōmanchō, have all come, and chirping and fluttering, they dance wildly, drunk on sake. Could there be any event more Medeta than this? Everyone, do not stand on Enriyo, but break the embankments and drink. Look, just as you see, the captain of the Kiyo Sekishiyo of the Brahmins has arrived and joined us at the drinking seat.'

Singing with such joy, his spirit is light with courage. That the mansion of Bāchiru-sama, known as the wealthiest Shiyuda in the village of Suma, has manifested the Zuishiyō of Kuwanminitsuchi is an event without precedent since ancient times. The Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, the Kamutsukasa of Tamakuniwake, Masumi-no-hiko, Itahiko, and the three-thousand-hiko-shi Debisu-hime, people famous in name, have appeared at this mansion, and by completely removing the barriers between Ananai-kyō and the Brahmins, they have joined the banquet in harmony, manifesting the truth of universal brotherhood, which is the will of the Lord God.

Everyone, please rejoice. In the village of Suma in the land of Idzumi, although the Shiyōjiyō of Mount Adsumosu are no longer visible, their spirits have somehow possessed our own. Without distinction of age or gender, everyone without exception is drunk on sake; the illness of the non-drinker has gone somewhere, and all have ended up as heavy drinkers, crying, laughing, and getting angry. It is amusing to see the thousand forms and myriad states of that condition manifested in detail. Drink, make noise, dance, and go wild. Dance and sing forever. Drink dry every one of the twenty storehouses of sake, and offer them to the Shiyōjiyō-kami, Shiyōjiyō-hiko, and Shiyōjiyō-hime.

I, who serve my master, cannot function without drinking sake. Ah, how amusing, how amusing. This is entirely the blessing of the noble God of the Brahmins. Celebrate, celebrate, take courage, take courage. I beg you all to raise your voices together and praise the long life of Bāchiru-sama. Banzai, Banzai, Banzai! The crane dances in the spring of a thousand years, and the turtle sings of the summer of ten thousand years. Spring and summer are the seasons when all things flourish and prosper. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. Whether it be warm sake or cold, drink plenty as you please and roll, roll, all you people. The way to comfort the heart of the Shiyōjiyō-hime is none other than to drink sake. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara.

Having received the sacred sake of the Lord God, everyone, from now on, firmly unite your hearts and combine your strengths, making the lord of the village of Bāchiru your parent, and make the village of Suma peaceful, calm, and prosperous. You must build the Heavenly Pure Land, receive the blessings of God, and fulfill your duty as human beings in the village of Suma. When celebrating, celebrate well; when playing, play well; drink, eat, work, and let us all, high and low, live happily and amusingly in this world. This is the first act of filial piety toward the Lord God.

Come, come, drink, come, drink. Dance, dance, dance, dance. No matter how much you dance and perform, this floor built from the depths of the golden hell will not easily collapse. This garden hardened with earth is like the wish-fulfilling jewel that is indestructible. It is a ship of the Law that requires no worry. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. I shall rest now. Everyone, please take my place and sing. Drinking and eating is not the only art. Show off your hidden talents here, make everyone laugh until their jaws loosen, and it is good to change the dwelling of your navel. In this banquet permitted by the heavens, who needs etiquette or nonsense?

§ 2

Everyone is at liberty to do as they please; this is the true Tengoku.

Before anyone knew it, Chiruteru had led more than ten of his subordinates to break into the inner chamber, hoisted the sleeping Davis-hime, who had become exhausted from drinking, gagged her, slipped out through the back gate of the mansion, and quietly placed her inside a storehouse at his own residence. Michihiko suddenly awoke and, looking beside him, saw that the figure of Davis-hime was gone. However, thinking that perhaps Davis had merely gone to the Benjiyo, Michihiko did not pay much mind to it and went back to sleep. Itahiko, while enjoying the sight of the crowd in the Hironiha crying, laughing, and engaging in Kozeriahi while bathed in the Yotsuyu, wandered aimlessly toward the back gate.

Watching more than ten men carrying something that looked like a woman and slipping away—though he could not be certain in the darkness—Itahiko stood with his arms crossed and pondered for a while. 'Could that perhaps be Davis-hime? She looks remarkably like her. However, if I speak carelessly and am proven wrong, it would be a Taihen matter. In any case, I shall check the Nema of Davis-hime,' he thought, nodding to himself as he slipped through several rooms, only to find Michihiko sleeping all alone under the dim light of an Andon. Itahiko rushed into the Zashiki and, shaking Michihiko awake, said:

Itahiko: 'Oi, oi, Michihiko-sama, what has become of Davis-hime-sama?'

Michihiko: 'Ah, you startled me. You shook me awake while I was fast asleep, and I nearly lost my soul. I was having a terrible dream.'

Itahiko: 'Oi, this is no time for dreams. What do you think has happened to Davis-hime-sama? Pull yourself together!'

Michihiko: 'In truth, I was dreaming that Davis had been taken away somewhere by the Baramon gang. How strange things are. I wonder where the Hime has gone.'

Itahiko: 'Your dream is surely a true vision. As I was unable to sleep, I was watching the drunken crowd and wandered to the back gate, where I saw fifteen or sixteen rough men carrying a woman and running away. Because it was night, I could not see clearly, so I came here to investigate if it might be Davis-hime-sama. Oh, this is no time to be sitting around. We must devise a plan.'

Michihiko: 'Oi, Itahiko, please do not make too much of a scene. If the enemy ends up killing the Hime because of it, there will be no remedy, so for now, it is best to remain silent until we know more. However, who do you think it was that snatched the Hime?'

Itahiko: 'In my estimation, it was the subordinates of Chiruteru of the Baramon army. They had been drinking with all their might until now, but when their shadows suddenly vanished, I went to the back gate and saw them carrying a woman away, so it is surely them. I will support you, so shall we sneak into Chiruteru's mansion now, observe the situation, and bring her back?'

Michihiko: 'Ya, that is grateful. It is a trouble for you, but I shall accept your help. However, keep this a secret from Tamakuniwake-sama for a little while longer.'

Itahiko: 'Un, understood. Come, let us slip out from the back gate and go on a reconnaissance.'

With that, the two of them, still in their sleeping robes, dashed out from the back gate and proceeded toward the mansion of the gatekeeper.
