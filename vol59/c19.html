Chapter 19: 猩々 Boat [1519]

猩々舟〔1519〕

§ 1

Tamakuniwake and his party were served a dinner, and when they had worshipped the Kami again and were about to retire to sleep, Sāberuhime brought her mouth close to the ear of Tamakuniwake and spoke quietly.

Sāberu: 'Excuse me, Senseisama, would you be able to send a ship to save the three men left behind on Shiyōjiyōjima? I beg you to ask the Kamisama, and if there is no objection, I wish for you to save those three. And one more request: would it be possible to build two shrines in the forest of Tenō as soon as possible, and enshrine the Great Kami of Ananahi in one, and the Great Kami of Baramon in the other?'

Tamakuni: 'Indeed, that is a fine idea. However, I had a direct intuition that the many Kenzoku on Shiyōjiyōjima were originally those who served at the shrine on Mount Azumosu; if there is no objection, I would like to prepare many ships and bring every one of them back to the forest of Tenō. What do you think?'

Upon hearing these words, Sāberuhime suddenly jumped up in joy, and while making a strange sound, 'Kya-tsu, Kya-tsu,' she clasped her hands together.

Sāberu: 'I am a Shiyōjiyōhime. Because I have left behind many children and grandchildren, I worry about them so much that I cannot even sleep properly at night. You have spoken very well. If I could only have the permission of the Kamisama, I would like to welcome every one of them here.'

Tamakuni: 'Ah, that is even better. If so, I shall prepare the ships early tomorrow and order the villagers to go and bring them back.'

Sāberu: 'Would it be possible for you to have one or two of your disciples go as well?'

Itahiko, who was nearby, caught wind of this.

Ita: 'Sensei, Itahiko will take on that duty. I suggest that Mitsuhikohiko-sama and his wife stay at the mansion for a while and draw up the blueprints for the shrine construction. And if you, Sensei, could preach to the villagers for a while, and Masumihikohiko-sama could fill in during that time, that would be truly excellent.'

Tamakuni: 'No, I absolutely cannot allow a rash person like you to go. We shall ask Mitsuhikohiko and his wife.'

Ita: 'Hey, Mitsuhikohiko and his wife, listen, one who has a wife should not go out on such a rough sea. If it were a bachelor like me, even if I were to die, there would be no need to worry about a wife grieving, so it is very convenient. I would like you to yield that role to me.'

Mitsu: 'If I have Sensei's permission, I will do whatever it takes.'

Ita: 'Sensei, I implore you to give the order to me.'

Tamakuni: 'Hmm, very well, I shall leave it to you. It would be best if you chose suitable people to take with you.'

Ita: 'Oh, how grateful I am, I am dancing with joy, ehehehe. Now, this is the Arctic expedition team. Hey, Anchī-sama, you are the vice-captain. Akisu and Kāru, you two are the squad leaders. Since the head clerk Teku must manage all household affairs, he is not permitted to join the expedition. Now, Akisu and Kāru, you two, please select strong men. Since we are going to welcome Shiyōjiyō-sensei, please ensure you select and bring along spirits of Shiyōjiyō purity. Also, if there is a dilapidated old boat, please count it in. This is the ship to carry the nine human-transformations of Yakkosu, Hāru, and Sabōru. Ahahaha.'

Akisu: 'There is no such dilapidated boat.'

Ita: 'Ah, it cannot be helped. Since they are in human form, please choose the sturdiest ship among them to take. By the way, I wonder how many Shiyōjiyō there are in total.'

Sāberu: 'Yes, I believe there are three hundred and thirty-three.'

Ita: 'Indeed, three hundred and thirty-three spirits of Shiyōjiyō purity, and three human-transformations called Hāru who are the heads of the people of the Sabōru-shop, the lazy ones of Baramon and Yakkosu. Akisu and Kāru-sama, please prepare everything quickly and without fail. Now, at last, Itahiko has become the commander-in-chief of three hundred and thirty-three plus three. Ahahaha. Oh, Sensei, thank you very much. This is my gateway to success, the threshold of my advancement; I will become the president of an immigration company and be very active. When I return in triumph, please set off fireworks, have all the villagers line up on the beach, and chant 'Long live Itahiko.' This is the greatest joy for us.'

Tamakuni: 'Very well, proceed with care.'

§ 2

Saberhime: 'Itahiko, lord of the Oshihe, come forth at once to welcome my child.'

Itahiko: 'This is a strange thing to hear, that you call a swarm of Shiyōjiyō my child.'

Saberhime: 'Though the body may be born as a Shiyōjiyō, the spirit remains unchanged from that of a human. The people of this present age are but vessels of beasts; within the beast dwells the human soul.'

Tamakuniwake: 'How fascinating are the Onkotoba of Saberhime; hearing them, I feel a sense of shame.'

Masumihiko: 'All people have become dwellings for beasts, and not even a shadow of a true human remains. Even I, in this dwelling of a soul stained by sin, feel a sense of shame.'

Michihiko: 'In this age where the eighty Magatsu rage in terror, there is no way to stand as a human being. Therefore, the human heart becomes an ogre, and becomes an Orochi to traverse the world.'

Itahiko: 'This is quite something, Michihiko-tsukasa, such Yomaigoto; do you declare the Miyai of Kami to be beasts?'

Debisuhime: 'The Kototama that my lord has declared refer to beasts wearing human skin. Itahiko, the Chin-no-tsukasa, is a Kamisama; he is the Kami of humans within the human.'

Itahiko: 'Very well then, Akisu, Karl, and Anty, prepare the Shiyōjiyō ships.'

From this time on, Itahiko did not sleep soundly even at night; he commanded Akisu, Karl, and Anty, selected rough men skilled in seamanship, gathered ships from the port of Kiyo and other nearby shores, and finally prepared twenty small boats, each fully loaded with sake barrels, completing all preparations by the time dawn broke to welcome the kin of the Shiyōjiyō.
