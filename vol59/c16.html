Chapter 16: 開 Cavern [1516]

開窟〔1516〕

§ 1

Itahiko was still sitting at the entrance when Wax, Ex, Herman, and El tumbled in. Next, as if a barrier had been suddenly smashed open, seventeen or eighteen young men in Churick attire came falling in, one on top of another with a thudding sound.

Itahiko: 'Well now, you many guests, you have come down here quite well. Now, this is the Kotakeya at the bottom of the slope. Unlike the Ōtakeya, we certainly do not poke our guests from under the floorboards with spears, take their lives, strip them of their kimonos, and pack them into barrels to be thrown into the sea. So, please feel at ease and stay with us. I will do my best to serve you at a low price. Including lunch, it will be three yen per person for your stay. Ah, what great prosperity. An inn is not a lost cause if it has this many guests. You are quite busy too, Bantō-sama.'

The group rose up, murmuring and whispering to one another.

Kō: 'Hey, because you would not listen to what I said, we have ended up falling into this Naraku. What on earth are you going to do about it?'

Otsu: 'Do not talk nonsense. The black post that served as our target was always standing there, was it not? We should have passed by the side of that. It seems someone must have changed the position of the post, which is why we made this blunder.'

Kō: 'Do not talk nonsense. That was not a post; did I not tell you to be careful because the situation looked different? Yet, because you insisted on your own way saying "What of it?", we have all fallen into a place like this. There is no other way out but for someone who knows the situation to come and save us. Well, just be patient until we turn into mummies.'

Itahiko: 'Welcome, guests. This is a newly opened inn. I will serve you at a low price.'

Kō: 'Hey, are you not that fellow from the San-iku-kyō? What are you doing here?'

Itahiko: 'Well, the business of being a missionary did not pay off very well, so I suddenly opened a cavern hotel and changed my trade. The captain of the Chiruteru and the Junker of the Heeru are also in the back. Now, please enter without hesitation; thank you for your continued patronage.'

Otsu: 'What is this? This is a strange affair. However, we must enter the back, meet the captain, and be saved as soon as possible.'

Without caring about the details, they pushed their way into the back.

Chiruteru: 'Well, did you all fall into this place at once?'

Kō: 'Yes, we searched for your whereabouts, Danna, but since we could not see you anywhere, we thought you might have checked into the cavern hotel, so I brought my subordinates to come and meet you.'

Chiruteru: 'Well, that is very kind of you. Thank you for coming to meet me. You have left the entrance to the barrier open, have you not? If only that is open, it is an easy matter to get out. Mitsuhiko-sama, Davis-sama, please be at ease now. I thought we would never be able to get out for all eternity, but my subordinates have opened the entrance and come to meet us.'

Kō: 'Well, if you had come to meet us by opening that entrance on purpose, it would be convenient, but we fell in without knowing it, so, well, we are truly in trouble. I wonder if there is a secret passage somewhere.'

Chiruteru: 'Ah, it is hopeless now. Every single one of us has fallen into this place. There is no one left to come and save us, is there? Regrettably, we have no choice but to become mummies here. Mitsuhiko-sama, what do you think?'

Mitsu: 'I do not know why, but I have a feeling that the Kannushi of Tamakuniwake will surely come to save us. Therefore, I am not worried in the slightest. Everyone, please calm yourselves and take a rest.'

§ 2

Ichidō lay on the rustling tatami mats, whispering to one another, waiting for the savior to appear. Michihiko, together with Debisu-hime, began to clap his hands and pray with itsushiyōkenmei: 'Ōkami of Ananai-kyō, Ōkami of Brahmanism, protect us, save us.' Itahiko remained sitting in an agura position as before.

The sun seemed to have set, and once again, phosphorescence began to flicker and flash.

Itahiko: 'How beautiful it is when night falls. It is just like a city that never sleeps. There is no need to light lamps, and no oil is required; it is truly economically made.'

He was spouting his usual ahauguchi. Suddenly, with a thud, thud, thud, like an avalanche, the four unexpected arrivals—Tamakuniwake, Masumihiko, Anchi, and Teku—fell in. Thinking that the Brahman group had fallen in again, Itahiko assumed the mood of a yadoya bantō and said:

Itahiko: 'Welcome, welcome. My, my, thank you for your continued patronage, dear guests. I am the bantō of the Iwaya Hotel, which has just opened for business. It is also known as the Kotakeya at the foot of the slope. Now, I shall serve you with all my heart, so please stay. However, since this is a wood-rent hotel, we provide no food. Hehe-hehe-he.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Is that not a strange voice, Masumihiko?'

Masumihiko: 'Tamakuniwake-sama, we have fallen into a formidable place. It seems there might be an exit somewhere.'

Hearing these words, Itahiko realized it was Tamakuniwake's party and, feeling happy and speaking with vigor, said:

Itahiko: 'Ah, was it you, Sensei? I am Itahiko. I finally fell into this pit and, finding no way out, I had resigned myself to my fate, but how on earth did you come to such a place?'

Tamakuniwake: 'Ah, so you are Itahiko. We have met in a strange place. And tell me, are Michihiko and Debisu-hime here?'

Itahiko: 'Yes, yes, they are in the back. Please, do meet them quickly. While you are at it, there are about two dozen soldiers of the Brahman army, including Chiruteru and Heru, packed in here. Thanks to them, this cavern hotel has become quite lively.'

Masumihiko: 'Hey Itahiko, this is no time to be talking so lightheartedly. We must find some way to escape from here, must we not?'

Itahiko: 'You are Masumihiko, aren't you? It was kind of you to fall in and join us. But now that it has come to this, all is lost. There is no use in being impatient. Leave everything to Heaven, do not panic, do not fret, and calmly await the arrival of your end.'

Teku: 'Ah, well, there is no help for it now; I must resign myself to my fate. If we are to be saved, we shall all be saved at once, and if we are to die, we shall all die together. We can no longer maintain our physical bodies. Once fallen here, there is no food, and no one will throw any in for us; ah, what a troublesome situation this has become.'

Tamakuniwake, followed by the three, proceeded with Itahiko toward the back. Many people were muttering with various half-sobbing voices. Tamakuniwake began to sing in a loud, clear voice.

Tamakuniwake: 'Man is the child of God, the palace of God. As long as God exists in heaven and earth, how could He not save us? Starting with Michihiko-tsukasa, may Chiruteru, Heru, and the other heavenly benefactors keep their hearts peaceful and serene. Kannagara. The grace of God dwells even in the dew on the single leaf of grass and tree. No matter how deep we have fallen into this subterranean cavern and see no sunlight, the noble and pure Sumera-Ōkami who protects Ananai-kyō will surely save us. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth itself sinks, the power of sincerity will save the world. All of us gathered in this cavern, let us unite our hearts, praise the great grace of Sumera-Ōkami, and if we reform from the bottom of our hearts and follow the path of sincerity, He will surely save us. Do not weary your hearts. Take courage, take courage, all people. If you take courage, courageous things will come. If you lament, lamentable things will come. Even if your body sinks to the bottom of the thousand-fathom sea, what of it? As long as there is the protection of God, though you may think to die, you cannot die. Conversely, if Ōkami were to abandon us, even if we lived peacefully on the earth, He would summon our lives. In all things, simply Kannagara.'

Thus they expressed their inner thoughts and distracted themselves from the pain in their hearts. Suddenly, from nowhere, from above their heads, the sound of a fierce dog was heard: 'Woo-woo, woo-woo, wow-wow-wow.' Mitsuhiko, jumping for joy, said:

Mitsuhiko: 'How grateful I am; the voice of Smart, whom Hatsuwakahime accompanied, has come to be heard. Now that Smart has appeared here, Hatsuwakahime must be coming near.'

Debisu-hime: 'Now that the Kami-hito of Hatsuwakahime-kimi has appeared, what is there to fear? The time for the people to be saved has come; that barking voice is the voice of Kami.'

Just as they were singing this, with Smart in the lead, Hatsuwakahime removed the lock on the gate of the checkpoint, descended the stone steps, and appeared with a smile, holding a light in her left hand. Tamakuniwake and the others, in their excess of joy, approached close to Hatsuwakahime and wept aloud. Unexpectedly, they all joined their hands together and, following behind Hatsuwakahime, were finally able to escape the Ihaya safely. This Hatsuwakahime was by no means an incarnation of a white fox. She had received the command of Kami and was sent to save Tamakuniwake and his party from their crisis. Following behind Hatsuwakahime and Smart, when they returned to the entrance, Iku and Saru were standing there, guarding it with solemnity.

三千彦『有難や初稚姫の伴ひし スマートの声聞え来にけり。 スマートが此処に現はれたる上は 初稚姫は近く来まさむ』

デビス姫『初稚姫君の命の神人が 現ます上は何か怖れむ。 人々の早救はるる時は来ぬ 彼の吠声は神の御声』

かく歌ひ居る時しも、スマートを先に立てて初稚姫は関所の庭の錠を外し石段を下つて燈火を左手に捧ながら莞爾として現はれ来る。玉国別外一同は欣喜雀躍の余り初稚姫の傍近くよつて声を放つて泣き伏しぬ。一同期せずして両手を合せ、初稚姫の後に従つて漸く岩窟を無事に抜け出す事を得た。此初稚姫は決して白狐の化身では無かつた。神の命を受け玉国別一行が危難を救ふべく向はせたまふたのである。一同は初稚姫、スマートの後に従ひ、入口迄還つて見れば、イク、サールの両人が厳然として警固して居た。
