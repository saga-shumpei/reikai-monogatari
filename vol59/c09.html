Chapter 9: Darkness 内 [1509]

暗内〔1509〕

§ 1

Tamakuniwake and Masumihiko, exhausted from the long journey by sea and heavily intoxicated by the furumahizake, had fallen into a deep, death-like slumber that night. First, they performed chōzu to rinse their mouths, worshipped the Tōten, then proceeded to the Shinden to offer norito, and rested for a while. Just then, Bachiru appeared with a changed expression.

Bachiru: 'Excuse me, Senseisama, a terrible thing has happened. It is truly inexcusable.'

Tamakuniwake: 'What do you mean by a terrible thing?'

Bachiru: 'Yes, I too was so exhausted that I fell fast asleep, so I know nothing of what happened in the middle of the night, but Michihiko-sama, Itahiko-sama, and Debisu-hime-sama cannot be found anywhere, no matter how much we search. According to the villagers, the back gate was opened and the Brahman soldiers bound the three of them and took them away; I have done a truly inexcusable thing.'

Tamakuniwake: 'What, the three of them were abducted by the Brahman army? Ah, that is terrible. Masumihiko, we cannot just sit here like this. Shall we go and storm the Brahman checkpoint to investigate the situation?'

Masumihiko: 'Indeed, a terrible thing has happened. Come, let us go.'

Bachiru: 'Excuse me, Senseisama, please wait a moment. The Brahman checkpoint is guarded by a company of fierce soldiers, so it would be dangerous for just the two of you. Fortunately, as the villagers have been coming here day and night to offer congratulations, why not choose several dozen strong men from among them to accompany you? I, too, will lay down my life this time to repay the debt of having my life saved. Please, will you not do so?'

Tamakuniwake: 'No, please do not worry at all. It would not do for it to be said that the messenger of the propaganda, relying on the help of many, stormed the place. Furthermore, the other side is armed, and it would not do if the villagers were to be injured, so we two shall go directly.'

Bachiru: 'If you say so, there is no help for it. Instead, I shall accompany you.'

Tamakuniwake: 'No, that is not necessary. However, as we are only two going into the enemy's midst, this may be the last time you see our faces. Please, understand the heart of the Lord God well, and lead and cherish these villagers.'

At that moment, Sāberu-hime pushed open the sliding door softly and entered, appearing as if she were tumbling in with tears.

Sāberu-hime: 'Tamakuniwake-sama, Masumihiko-sama, a terrible thing has happened. Please, by the divine virtue of the Lord God, rescue the three of them and return safely. I shall pray to the Lord God for your safe success.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Grateful for the thick robe of your kindness, I shall advance while wearing it upon my body. With the sleeves that deeply wrap my sincerity, I shall mow down and slaughter the ugly villains.'

Masumihiko: 'To save our friends who are tormented by the evil gods, I shall go as the Lord God wills. Bowing before the Great Lord, I pray, May this journey be truly fortunate.'

Bachiru: 'With the Kototama struck with sincerity, How can the enemies who oppose us prevail? Yet, please proceed with caution, For there are deep traps and schemes.'

Sāberu-hime: 'Tamakuniwake-kami-no-mikoto and Masumihiko, Please be wary of the enemy's mansion.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Grateful for this life I have offered to the Lord God, Even if I cast it away, how could I fear?'

Masumihiko: 'Bound by the thread of the Imperial God's destiny, Though it is my body, now I shall untie it.'

Bachiru: 'Please take my servant Anchī with you, He is a strong man of great power.'

Tamakuniwake: 'My path is to rely on no one and lean on no staff, I shall advance only with sincerity. To disregard your kind intention, Is not my desire, but please forgive me. Michihiko must be clashing with the enemies, With the Kototama of severity at this very moment.'

Masumihiko: 'Since this is a battle of Kototama, I shall not fear, even in the enemy camp armed with weapons of death. The day has come to pacify the enemies possessed by the Magakami through the power of Kototama.'

Anchī: 'O Kamuzukasa, please take me with you and let me exhaust the limits of my Magokoro. Even if I must surrender my Inochi to the enemy, how could I regret this body I have cast aside?'

Tamakuniwake: 'What brings me joy, replacing the Inochi of the thread of jewels, is the sincerity of your heart.'

Thus, they exchanged poems with each other. Just as Tamakuniwake and Masumihiko were about to take off their Sendenshi robes and change into the clothes given by Bachiru to depart, the drunken Teku appeared and came walking up.

Teku: 'Hey, pardon me. Until today, I was a spy working under the Metsukeyaku of the Brahman Kyō. On one hand, I became a sworn brother to the ringleader of the pirates, Yakkosu, and I did nothing but various kinds of evil deeds. But after receiving some mysterious Hanniyatau from Tamakuniwake-sama, the demon lurking in my heart was expelled from within me, and now I have completely returned to a human heart. Therefore, since there are many traps in the mansion of Chiruteru, I, this Teku, shall guide you. If you go carelessly, you will meet with a terrible fate. No one else knows those secrets. Since no one knows them except for the soldiers guarding the barrier, it is dangerous.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Oh, you are Teku-sama, are you not? Well, thank you. Are there really that many traps prepared?'

Teku: 'Yes, yes, there are traps here and there. Once you fall into such a place, you cannot get out. And lately, there is a beautiful woman staying all alone in the detached house. They say her name is Hatsuwakahime-sama. The captain of the barrier guards has lost his head over that woman, and because of that, a marital quarrel has begun; oh, the ugliness inside is beyond words.'

Tamakuniwake: 'What, you say Hatsuwakahime-sama is there? What does she look like?'

Teku: 'Yes, I cannot say for certain, but at a glance, I think she is seventeen or eighteen years old. However, there is something youthful about her, like a girl of fifteen or sixteen, and she is adorned with jewels all over her body. She is a very beautiful person, indeed.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Hmm, I have heard that Hatsuwakahime-sama is not the type of person to adorn herself with such jewels. It is likely a case of the same name but a different person. Is that not so, Masumihiko?'

Masumihiko: 'Yes, that must be so. There are many people with the same name in the world, after all.'

Tamakuniwake: 'Ah, in that case, shall I ask you to guide us to the mansion?'

Teku: 'No, thank you for your prompt decision. I know almost every place, so if you follow behind me, I will not let you be treated rudely. And if you meet that woman once, you will surely know the truth. Your disciple may have fallen into a trap. If you are careless, your Inochi will be in danger. Since many soldiers gather and throw stones from above, it is truly unbearable.'

Bachiru: 'Teku-sama, I heard from Akisu that you said you became the head clerk of this house; will you really become our head clerk? Anchī is also saying that she wants to rest for a while, so it would be very convenient if you would become the head clerk.'

Teku: 'I accept. Even before you spoke, I had already decided on my own to be the head clerk, so what objection could I have? I was a villain at first, but having been illuminated by the light of the gods, I have come to fear evil, and I am determined to perform at least one good deed to atone for my sins, so I humbly ask for your favor. Now, Tamakuniwake-sama, Masumihiko-sama, let us go.'

§ 4

Into the midst of the enemy I must go,

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

In any case, upon the power of the Ōkami,

I shall rely and advance.

Before the presence of Tamakuniwake,

I humbly inquire once more.'

Having finished singing, he sought the opinion of Tamakuniwake. Tamakuniwake deemed the words of Anchi to be reasonable, and stood still on the path, pondering for a while. Teku opened his mouth without ceremony,

Teku: 'Excuse me, Minasama, fortunately due to various circumstances, I must approach Chiruteru. Regarding that, I have a great advantage in probing the intentions of Chiruteru, and also in searching for the whereabouts of Michihiko-sama and the others, so I would like to ask you all to rest in this dense forest for a while until sunset, and I think it would be better for you to set out after I have made a preliminary investigation and the sun has set; what do you think of this idea?'

Tamakuniwake: 'Indeed, perhaps the night time might be better after all. The divine oracle also indicates, "Hitherto it has been said that sunset is bad, but from now on, whatever is begun at sunset shall be good." In that case, Teku-sama, I am sorry for the trouble, but please go to the mansion of Chiruteru and conduct as much reconnaissance as possible. Until then, we shall wait here in the shade of the forest, offering our prayers.'

Teku: 'No, thank you for your immediate agreement. In that case, I, Teku, will go and skillfully investigate the situation. Please, do rest and wait leisurely in the depths of this forest. Farewell.'

Saying this, he tucked up his skirts and ran off with a rhythmic sound along the narrow field path where summer grasses grew thick. The three sat down in the shade of the forest trees, waiting for the time to arrive.
