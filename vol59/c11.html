Chapter 11: 黒白 [1511]

黒白〔1511〕

§ 1

Heru came with great enthusiasm to the front of the mansion where Hatsuwakahime was secluded, but somehow the threshold felt high and his heart began to quail.

Heru: 'Hey, are you cowardly Fukushiyu fellows trying to prevent the actions of the Seishiyugojin again? I am not a Heru-sama who would hesitate or waver at such a thing, you insolent fools. All units, halt!'

From within his belly, the Fukushiyu all replied: 'Hai!'

Heru: 'Good, good, keep silent for a while. No, it is better if you sleep. When the trumpet of the Hijiyōshōshifu sounds, that is when you shall rise all at once. Until then, I order the entire Fukushiyuzentai to rest. In the meantime, go back to your Kiyōri or somewhere and drink your Jijibaba's milk. Ahahaha, at last, the Fukushiyu fellows have fallen silent. However, it seems the cowardice is still clinging to them. Hey, cowardice, you too should hurry back to your Kiyōri, become a Zaigōgunjin, and engage in the Nōkōshiyō. When an emergency arises, exchange your plow for a rifle, your abacus for a sword, and your chisel for a spear to show the reality of your service to the state. Until then, I exempt this Heru's internal national active-duty soldiers. Be grateful. Ahahaha, it seems the cowardice has retreated a little, or rather, has returned home. Hey, I shall sing a song to make the princess's spirit ecstatic. I have been called a man of strong character, but what a soft-boned man I am when it comes to women. This Hill 203 is quite a tough nut to crack. Simply blowing a conch shell with my mouth and a trumpet with my rear will not easily produce results. They say the sages of the future governed the world through rites and music. Archery, horsemanship, and calligraphy are the very end of the end. First, I must enrich the rites, play music, produce subtle sounds, sing a famous song, and move the princess's heart. They say that songs soothe the hearts of the gods and move heaven and earth. How much more so for the human heart. Oh, the song, the song!'

If one analyzes the character for love (koi), it is the heart that says 'I yearn, I yearn'...

Hey, this is old. If she has reached the age of Hatsuwakahime, she must have already heard it. I wish she would think it is a song I invented, but if she thinks it is a moldy, second-hand song, my status as a man will fall. Alright, let me try to think of something.

If I think in my heart that you are dear and lovely, the other party says you are a dear person (Koi).

With this, it has become completely new. However, since it is just a rehash of the reversal, it seems the original is better after all. Well, this time, shall I analyze the character for love (ai) and sing it?

If your lovely heart pierces through, you will surely receive it.

This time, it is the 'supreme' of supremeism, the best. Ehehehe, I hope I can do this well.

The best of love is but one thing: on this earth, there is only you.

Hatsuwakahime-sama, you who have floated a boat on the Heavenly River, while wielding a golden oar, crossing over the ocean of Kiyo, and descending to this mansion. Your flower-like face and moon-like eyebrows, wearing robes of stars, in the very form of a heavenly maiden, you shine brilliantly in this mansion. How noble you are! Even if the morning sun shines and glows, even if the moon's form is clear, when compared to Hatsuwakahime, they are miserable and cannot even approach you. Your white face that deceives the snow, your skin smooth and slippery like a crystal ball. After all, the truth of heaven and earth is that white is the beginning of color, and black is the end of color.

Ushitora is indeed the mature woman; color is what the mature woman of Ushitora points to. White and black come together, the grid of the Go board, the structure of warp and woof, which the Ōmikami has played with. When red overlaps, it becomes black; when black changes, it becomes white. Hatsuwakahime's white skin and Heru no Tsukasa's black face, this is truly the return of the Ushitora, the return of the spirit of rigor. Hatsuwakahime is the Mizumitama, the return of the Kōtaijin, the princess god of Kunishitsuchi. Combining the water and fire of rigor and fluidity, the marriage bond is eternal, circling the heavenly pillar, giving birth to the various precious children of mountains, rivers, grass, and trees.

Learning from the ancient records of the great god Izanagi, constructing an eternal heavenly pure land upon this earth, to save the hundred gods and men, the Kōjin has auspiciously sent down the black-colored old man and the white-colored old woman here. Hatsuwakahime no Kami-tsukasa, no matter how much you dislike Heru, it is a bond ordained by the gods. Please reflect upon it, Kannagara. Awakened to the teachings of the gods, it is reflected clearly like a mirror in the spirit of Heru. The master of this house, Chiruteru, has abandoned his essential wife elsewhere and is chasing after distant and near seductive women...

They cast aside their souls to rot, with endless marital quarrels, and all their household goods rattling about. From time to time, they make a clamor and dance, as if living in a haunted mansion. Wearing a gourd-mask of blue and white, and donning the guard's hat of Kiyo, they brandish a captain's face. How hateful it is that they have bound the Shinjiyo, the divine Mikoto of Hatsuwakahime who descended from heaven, and confined her mercilessly in a storehouse, intending to make her a bedchamber companion, having leveled false accusations against the Princess Chiruna. To be a demon-beast who feels no shame for such cruelty—do not let them lead you astray, for where there are tears, blood also flows.

Heeru, a man of single-minded loyalty and courage, received a divine oracle last night: 'You are a Mitama with deep connections from the age of the gods. You are a Tennin who, in days of old, served the way as the husband of Hatsuwakahime. As the age of Miroku approaches, I shall transform you into a woman, and you shall appear as the divine minister of Hatsuwakahime, a transformed man. Build and solidify the divine nation in detail,' the Lord God solemnly proclaimed. 'How precious is this command! As the divine minister of Hatsuwakahime, this is surely no lie, for there are no two words in the speech of God. Place your hand upon your heart, correctly understand this divine decree, and take Hatsuwakahime, whom God has joined, as your wife.

Live in harmony and intimacy, and participate in the divine work, O Mizumitama. Even if the transformed woman proclaims that the morning sun shines or clouds over, and even if the earth itself sinks, the way of husband and wife shall not change. In any case, this floating world does not go as the human heart desires. Bear each other's faults, compensate for shortcomings, live in harmony, and solidify the fire and water of heaven and earth. If my Kototama enters your ears safely and in detail, then offer a swift reply, O Princess Mikoto.'

Truly, the deep Heeru, here reverently receives the divine decree and proclaims it before the Mikoto: 'Ah, Kannagara-kamu-kamu, grant me your divine grace.'

Hatsuwakahime opened the door with a sudden snap from within, and while watching Heeru's figure, she smiled.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Wondering whose Kototama this might be, I opened the window to find a fascinating lord. Your heart is sad, repeating such various and severe Kototama.'

Heeru: 'Since I am a man among men, how could I let my heart be disturbed by a woman? Nevertheless, I cannot disobey the words of God, and I convey them to your Mikoto. This love is not a human love, but a divine love; it is the mirror of love is best.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'It is suspicious; since I hear it is the word of God, it is not that I have no way to disobey.'

Heeru: 'How can I unravel the ambiguous Kototama of the Princess? My longing has no way to be resolved. The arrow of love, shot by a brave man, will surely pierce even the rock.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Even if you say so, I am a divine messenger, and there is a strict prohibition against taking a husband. My body, which advances by firmly keeping the prohibition, has no way to be tempted by the storm of ugliness. If this were a world without deception, I would not have to torment my soul so much. For me, love is the great God of the moon residing in the white clouds of the sky.'

Heeru: 'I, too, am the god attached to this barrier, the majestic figure of Mount Terumon.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Spring has flowers, summer has mandarin oranges, autumn has chrysanthemums, and winter has the lonely flower of the narcissus. I think that a man who cherishes me, who has no one to pluck me, is equal to a god. Although my sincerity reaches my soul, there is no help for it, as I am a Tennin. People of this world are one, but although there are signs that we do not meet, I have none of this. Without knowing my body, which is that of a fickle man, how futile it is for you to be led astray.'

Heeru: 'No matter what, I shall keep the teachings of the Sumeru-Ōkami and make you my wife. No matter how much you shake, I will follow you without bending, even to the bottom of the sea.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Did I ever think that a person I did not expect would have such deep affection? It is sad that I have no way to draw from it.'

Heeru: 'You, who push the living carriage of soft and precious Kototama, are the Osu-Kami of heaven. The young grass of Love-Best Nasunogahara blooms while being trampled and crushed.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Even if trampled or cut, the fig tree, which has no shadow to see, does not bloom. My body, which advances only toward the way of God, has no precedent for blooming even when the spring breeze blows. Give up on my figure, which has no flowers, and seek flowers that bloom and scent the fields. Even if the purple clover blooms brilliantly, I am not one to become an ornament for the bed.'

Heru: 'Upon the field, the Genge flowers bloom, a Hanamushiro spread, I think to sleep at ease. Your words, so vexing, I can no longer bear to hear. Awakening the Yamatogokoro of a man's heart, I shall not stop until I have taken you by force.'

While saying this, he slid open the Omotedo, strode straight up to Hatsuwakahime, extended his Enpi, and attempted to grasp her hand firmly.

Hatsuwakahime quickly released her hand, caught him by the Erigami, and tossed him out of the window as if she were carrying a cat, with a light Chōshi.

Heru rose up, and without any Shiyōko, advanced once more into the Zashiki to approach Hatsuwakahime.

Heru: 'Once a man has declared the Iji of love, he is not the kind of man to Hekota in the Chiuto. From here on, Heiwa Shiyudan will be Tautei Dame. Prepare yourself, for I shall make you yield beautifully.'

As he lunged with a Mushiyabu, Hatsuwakahime, without any effort, pressed him down firmly.

Hatsuwakahime: 'Ho ho ho ho, Heru-sama, please stop this Jiyōdan. You must be drunk on sake. Please rest here until your intoxication has faded a little.'

Heru: 'I am by no means drunk. If I say I am drunk, it is that I am drunk on your beauty. This too has arisen entirely from you; there is no one but you who can calm my heart. I never intended to love you from the beginning. But as soon as I saw your figure, I was instantly possessed by a spirit, and unable to restrain myself, I approached you in obedience to the command of God. It is by no means Heru's own thought.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Ho ho ho ho, at your age, how can you say such things. Unless I administer Bringing-up-father, you will surely be Dame. Even if you think to deceive me by saying it is the command of God, while it might work on other women, it will not have the slightest effect on me, so please refrain from such lies.'

Heru: 'No matter what you say, I cannot save face as a man. Please, let me go. If I am held down with such great strength, my breath will cease.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'What does it matter if your breath ceases? You said you would follow me even to the bottom of the sea, did you not, ho ho ho ho.'

She laughed softly. Just then, the captain of the mansion's gatekeeper, Chiruteru, came running with his feet clattering and his face pale.
