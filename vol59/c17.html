I bit my own thigh with such intensity, the pain of which, while grinding my teeth in regret, I swore to clear my grudge. Clenching my fists, I struck my noble face two or three times; a mistake for which I cannot turn back time. The origin of this matter is this Kanna, for not knowing the difference between black and white, and being lost in greed. Please forgive me, Chiruna-hime. Man is the child of Kami, the palace of Kami; not originally a child of an ogre. Born in the belly of the Orochi, I am neither a snake nor a crooked spirit. All are the Wakemitama of Kami, possessors of the Suishiyōdama. Yet, just as the Tsukihi in the Ōzora are momentarily hidden by Kurokumo,

My soul, too, has before I knew it become a captive of Akuma, and I have committed an unexpected folly. That it has come to this is the retribution for the sins my own body has committed. I no longer hold any grudge against Chiruteru-sama or the Hime-sama. Bonten, Taishiyaku, Jizaiten, I pray with both hands joined and my Magokoro offered, beseeching you to save the two of us from our suffering, Kamunagara, in this darkness where I cannot even discern black from white.

Just as she sang this, the surroundings suddenly became noisy, and the sound of dozens of footsteps could be heard. The two strained their ears, watching the situation for a while to see who was attacking. Suddenly, with the sound of a door opening with a click, they saw Hatsuwakahime, Chiruteru, and many other propagandists and soldiers standing there. Chiruna-hime immediately flew out of the storehouse and, aiming at Hatsuwakahime, pounced like a yaksha. Hatsuwakahime nimbly dodged her body,

Hatsuwakahime: 'I am the Kami of Hatsuwakahime, who transmits the path of the true Sango. Chiruna-hime, do you mistake my form and hold a grudge? It is a matter of concern.'

Chiruna-hime: 'Looking closely, I know not why, but your appearance has changed. Please forgive me.'

Chiruteru: 'My beloved wife, take heart. I too have been saved by Hatsuwakahime.'

Chiruna-hime: 'Please forgive my sin of having turned my blade against my husband, with the heart of Kami.'

Chiruteru: 'Lured by the crookedness nesting in my chest, I have committed an unexpected sin. From today, I shall set my heart's horse aright and love Chiruna-hime deeply.'

Chiruna-hime: 'I am grateful. Having heard those words of yours, even if I were to die, I would not hold a grudge.'

Ekisu: 'Having received the punishment of five hundred lashes, I have come from afar, and here my dream has awakened.'

El: 'My lamentation for returning evil for the deep compassion and arrangements of Michihiko. If not for the basin of Michihiko's mercy, how could my body have been preserved? Though I am one who has received the blessings of sea and mountain, how painful it is to have targeted you as an enemy.'

Iku: 'Following behind Hatsuwakahime-kami-no-mikoto, we have advanced, sometimes in front and sometimes behind.'

Saru: 'Having searched for the mansion of the barrier guard at Kiyo Port, I have encountered people I did not expect to meet.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'Then I shall depart from here, following the decree of the Sumeragi-kami.'

Hatsuwakahime: 'You must advance, making the Magokoro of Tamakuniwake-kami-no-mikoto your strength.'

While singing thus, they bowed to one another, and as soon as they left the gate of the mansion with Smart in tow and quick footsteps, their figures vanished like mist. Hatsuwakahime had mounted the back of Smart and hurried through the trees toward the capital of Haruna. Iku and Saru, fearing it would be terrible to be abandoned by the Hime-sama they had worked so hard to catch up with, took whatever they could and, while firmly clutching the crystal jewel in one hand, chased after them with a thumping sound of footsteps.
