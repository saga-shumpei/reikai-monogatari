Chapter 14: 舗照 [1514]

舗照〔1514〕

§ 1

Michihiko of the God's Oshihe, Having rescued Debisuhime, Together with Itahikotsukasa, In front of the garden of Chiruteru-kan, While hiding their bodies in the tree shade, Triumphantly they were returning. Suddenly with a heavy crash beneath their feet, An unexpected bottomless, Sliding downfall pitfall. The three without any injury, Arrived safely in a chamber beneath the earth, Looking at the four surroundings it was wondrously strange, An unexpectedly spacious cavern, Shining with glittering phosphorescence, An enduring rock cave of Tokihakakiha. In the pitch-black night where even black and white could not be distinguished, Ascended into a world of light, While feeling as if having done so, Leisuredly the three held hands with one another, Along a grand broad road shaped like a kamaboko,

As if advancing, briskly, Leaving it to their feet, they explored forward.

Itahiko: 'Just as I felt like we had fallen with a heavy crash down into the earth, our surroundings became a shining world of light sparkling and glittering. Dear me, what a truly mysterious thing this is. Michihiko-sama, could it be that we are dreaming?'

Michihiko: 'No, it is by no means a dream. We have fallen into the enemy's trap and dropped into a pitfall. The whole vicinity around here is a place with many underground caverns. Everything sparkling in this dark night is phosphorescent ore. However, there is no doubt that in ancient times this was a place where fine humans dwelt. Let us take a rest somewhere around here.'

Itahiko: 'It is a relatively smooth and well-accustomed rock cave. With it being like this, if we search somewhere, there must undoubtedly be plenty of rooms reserved. Let us search thoroughly once, and if there be a room, well, you and your wife should set up a new household. I shall become a temporary manager and serve your needs. Say, Okusama, would that not be splendid?'

Debisuhime: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho-ho, Itahiko-sama, how can you speak of such easygoing things? This is the enemy's mansion, and we know not when we might be subjected to a smoke attack or a sake-bottle attack, yet you are truly an optimist.'

Itahiko: 'It is a fall-timism, falling with a crash from a pitfall into the depths of the earth! Well, well, it is fine. Let us enjoy the present moment. Behind us we still have Master Tamakuniwake, and Masumihiko also remains, so they will surely search us out and rescue us without fail. Well, without borrowing trouble in advance, let us enjoy this moment. Even if we regret it, nothing can be done, can it? If we play the fool for a while, there is nothing painful at all. For there is no happiness in this world like becoming a fool and a madman. The God always tells us to become fools and madmen, and indeed, nothing is as easygoing as a fool and a madman, ah-ha-ha-ha!'

Michihiko: 'Let us turn back now. No matter how far we go, there is no end to it. It is said that from here toward Berujisutan and Afuganisutan, there are many such rock caves. Because there are even quite famous ones called the Rock Caves of Uko.'

Itahiko: 'At any rate, let us search thoroughly once more.'

So saying, when they returned to the original spot and searched all around the area, beside the hole into which they had fallen there was a slightly indented place. When Itahiko gave it a firm push, a spacious rock cave appeared, and phosphorescent ore was sparkling all around.

Itahiko: 'Oh, wonderful! Let us decide to hole up here for a while. With facilities prepared to this extent, food and water must surely be somewhere.'

So saying, he took the lead and stepped inside.

As the three advanced vigorously deeper inside, there was something that rustled against their feet. Looking very closely, woven mats made of plantain leaves were spread all over.

Itahiko: 'Oh, this is an unexpectedly rare guest room! Well, let the three of us sleep together in a heap here at our leisure. However, if I am in your way, I shall step back for a while.'

Debisuhime: 'Thinking we were dropped into the Rock Cave of Uko, Oh, the joy of sleeping upon woven mats. Sedge mats spread out so crisply for the trio, Sleeping as if in a dream, how joyful it is.'

Let us spread the Takubusuma well and, as a Mitarizu, sleep while waiting for the night to break.'

Debisuhime: 'Well then, Itahiko-tsukasa and Michihiko, let us settle our hearts and go to sleep.'

Michihiko: 'There are no clouds hanging upon my Murakimo heart, if I am with you, who are like the flower and the moon.'

Itahiko: 'The flower is Hime, and Itahiko-tsukasa has become the figure of the moon to Michihiko.'

Michihiko: 'The Kami shall praise and save the Magokoro of Itahiko and Debisuhime. Let us follow the will of the Kami in all Nanigoto and wait for the Ama-no-Ihato to open.'

Thus, while chanting Misohitomoji to each other, they fell into a sound sleep that night.

The world on the ground seemed to have gradually brightened; the phosphorescent ore on all sides faded little by little, and a new light pierced through from nowhere. Itahiko was lying down with his chin in his hand, like the manager of an inn, in the entrance room. A man fell down there with a thud. If one looked closely, it was Herr of Yunker.

Itahiko: 'Oh, welcome.'

Yunker was surprised by this voice.

Herr: 'Oh, ah, who are you, and when did you come here?'

Itahiko: 'I have just recently opened an inn, and the facilities are not yet fully prepared, but please wash your feet and pass through to the back. A first-class room is five yen a night, though that is without lunch. It is seven yen including lunch. In return, I have advertised that tips are abolished, so it is relatively cheap. However, while I will not accept tips, if it is as a souvenir, I will not decline even one hundred or one thousand yen.'

Herr: 'Ha ha ha, I will endure it as long as I do not have to starve. Since I have been visited by the god of poverty lately, I cannot pay a very high room charge. Please, I ask for a second-class place.'

Itahiko: 'As I have just opened, the facilities are not ready, so you must endure it a little. And although I have nothing for you to eat, I have an inexhaustible supply of demon's bracken, twisted rice cakes, or nose-pinching dumplings, so you may eat as much as your arms can handle.'

Herr: 'No, that is quite enough. It is fine as long as you let me stay.'

Itahiko: 'Then I shall leave it to your wishes. However, the room charge is in advance, so please keep that in mind. I do not need a tip, but it smells a little like urine, though it is very warm and just right for drinking.'

Herr: 'I thought I would trouble you to let me stay, but I cannot bear to be made to drink urine tea, so I will urinate from this side. Thank you very much. I will trouble you at the next inn.'
