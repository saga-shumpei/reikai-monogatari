Chapter 14: 打合せ [1224]

打合せ〔1224〕

§ 1

At Matsuhimeyakata, casual conversation began and continued until late into the night.

Iso: 'I say, Matsuhiko-sama, inadvertently we spent quite some time idling away at Kogitayama. The sendenshi of Harukuniwake must surely be waiting for us anxiously. How is this, they say it is a matter of completely re-enshrining the Kamisama, but if we were to re-enshrine such a multitude of omiya-sama one by one, two or three days will surely not be enough to settle the matter. If we go about doing things like that, will not the essential goyō be delayed?'

Matsuhiko: 'That is indeed so, yet we cannot simply leave things as they are and proceed, which is quite troublesome. Depending on circumstances, we must travel all the way to Jerusalem. If we do so, even at best it will take about a year, so rather than that, how would it be if we leave everything in the hands of Matsuhime?'

Iso: 'How would it be if we also leave Mankō-san behind together with her for a while?'

Aku: 'I say, sensei, if we were to leave such a fellow behind, you will be hoodwinked by foxes once again and it will be all for naught. Otora-san, Magahiko, and Mankō form a triad of the easily deceived. However, if we were to leave this triad of the easily deceived in a place like this, it will once again become a den of kori. And since Mankō-san was brought along as part of the retinue from the mansion of Iso by Harukuniwake-sama, it is probably not something you can decide entirely at your own discretion, anata.'

Matsuhime: 'Ah, that is true, Iso-kō-san, would you not be so good as to ask the Kamisama and see?'

Iso: 'Yes, I understand. Then I shall ask and see.'

So saying, he joined his hands and entered a state of selflessness for a while.

Iso: 'Ah, I understand. It seems there is still no obstacle for about three days. Let us decide to begin the re-enshrining of the Kamisama from early tomorrow morning; in regard to that, is it not necessary for us to obtain the consent of Otora-san and Magahiko-san?'

Matsuhime: 'That is of primary importance. Teku-san, I am sorry to trouble you, but please ask Otora-san and Magahiko-san to come here.'

Teku: 'I understand.'

So saying, she left her seat and descended the stairs.

Aku: 'Matsuhime-sama, it seems quite a good number of days have passed since you came here, but you have indeed enshrined only strange kamisama.'

Matsuhime: 'It is truly so comical I cannot bear it. They are kamisama who wander about getting frayed and frayed at every turn, you see.'

'Megure Shrine and Tanemono Shrine, Ikuba Shrine and Daimon Shrine, are there not quite a number of other strangely named ones besides? My, what arbitrary divine names and shrine names have been attached to them.'

'Even so, the world is vast indeed. Everyone without exception makes earnest efforts to come and worship, so it is a wondrous thing.'

'Are they not praising Hensei Danshi to the utmost and disparaging Hensei Joshi?'

'Until two or three years ago, they used to attack Hensei Joshi to the utmost, calling them the mirror of evil, but lately the living shrine of Hensei Danshi ascended to heaven, so having no other choice, they are desperately making excuses solely for Hensei Joshi. Men and women weave the brocade loom with warp and weft. And the righteous heavenly Hinode no Ōkami investigates matters throughout the whole world and tells Hensei Joshi directly, they used to say with such, oh, such a grand and imposing manner.'

Mankō: 'It seems they have reformed themselves to a considerable degree, doesn't it.'

Matsuhime: 'No, no, that can hardly be the case. If they intend to occupy the believers of Miura-kyō, since the influence of women is strong nowadays, unless they speak well to both sides, people will not get hooked, so they are saying such things purely as a strategy. Once things have proceeded ninety-nine percent, they will suddenly flip things over and declare that women are the mirror of evil, so we must not lower our guard. Nevertheless, since such a multitude of the retinues of Hensei Joshi have come here like this, the fact that the essential founder eloped hand in hand with a woman must ultimately mean that divine punishment has struck them.'

§ 2

Matsuhiko: 'Wearing the mask of goodness, the crooked kami deceives the world; how dreadful it truly is. On the surface, they proclaim love and goodness, while concealing crookedness within along the path of ugliness. In every age, the hypocrites are the ones who flourish, while the righteous wither away. Yet now that the Miroku Kami has been born, the demons no longer have any means to prosper.'

Matsuhime: 'The hearts of Imoriwake, Magahiko, and Torabaa-sama are like a cat's eyes. Day and night they rot their intestines with wine, plunging into the pitiful state of becoming a shrine of the crooked. Self-proclaimed as the Ushitora no Baa-sama, they ceaselessly destroy the Hitsujisarugami. They have strayed into the ugly teachings of Magatsumi, their ears like tree fungus no matter what is said to them. How truly astonishing that they could be led so far astray, not hearing a single word of the true teaching.'

Iso: 'Compared to the wondrous palace of Isoyakata, it is as different as heaven and earth. Though the mountain storm atop Kogitayama is fierce, it quickly subsides into the sound of the pine winds.'

Mankō: 'Having come here, I have had countless suspicious things poured into my ears until they are numb like octopuses. Ears, eyes, mouth, and even nose are numbed, as a body surrounded on all sides by crookedness. Yet the divine majesty of Kami shines brilliantly, and the ugly crooked kami have fled and vanished.'

Aku: 'Do not fret your hearts in restless anxiety; simply leave everything in the hands of Kami. You deceive the people of the world by claiming you will drive away and scatter the evil kami to enshrine the fundamental Kami.'

Taku: 'It is truly ridiculous that people believe Yurahiko to be the living palace of the princess of supreme righteousness. Yet precisely because they believed it, we are able to rebuild the mountain of Kogita. If Takahime and the Chief Kurohime were to hear of this, they would surely burn with nostalgia.'

Teku: 'White-tailed and white-headed, the old fox has deceived those three people, hasn't it? Where is Imoriwake hiding now, longing and yearning after Otami's footsteps? The heart of Magahiko must surely be freezing over, having had Otami, for whom he burned with love, taken from him.'

From this point, adding Torabaa, Magahiko, Okiku, Bunsuke, and others, they held a meeting in the inner room of Matsuhiko's mansion from early the next morning concerning the purification, transfer ceremony, and other matters to enshrine the Miroku no Ōkami, and it was decided that each would return to their own rooms to pass the night.

Ah, Okaminaru Tamamuchikubasase.

By the way, from nine o'clock this morning until eleven o'clock tonight, over fourteen hours, I finished dictating eight hundred and one sheets of manuscript paper. This is the record up to today. (Mizuki)
