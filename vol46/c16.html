§ 1

Torabaa-sama was wearing a completely transformed, vibrant, and fresh expression, unlike anything seen since the establishment of Kogitayama, and light on her feet, holding a shiyurobauki and saiharahi, flutter-flutter-flutter-flutter, swish-swish-swish, she completed the method of spiritual purification and was absorbed in the cleaning of the interior. Coming over there with his hands thrust inside his tsutsusode looking cold, blowing out cold air with a puff-puff, was Magahiko. He entered exposing a lonely appearance, almost reaching the peak of disappointment and discouragement, as if catchers had emerged from the bottom of Jigoku, indescribable.

Torabaa: 'Magahiko-sama, take a look at the mirror for a moment; your face, despite your young age, is withered like that of Hachijifujii-sama. It won't do unless you change the way you hold your mind a little.'

Maga: 'It feels too foolish, the world has become lonely, a cloud of discontent comes unbidden to attack, and I feel as if there is no place upon the earth to set my body. Besides, Torabaa-sama, you alone today are wearing a very fresh and cheerful face, aren't you? Did you even research some Dr. so-and-so's rejuvenation method? Or perhaps you indulged in the Niconico Magazine? It is a tremendous transformation.'

Torabaa: 'With just a Niconico Magazine or a rejuvenation method, do things suddenly spring up with such energy? With such trash twisted out from human brains, how could one ever attain such a cheerful mood?'

Maga: 'Then what should be done? Somehow everywhere is becoming gloomy and festering, and I cannot help being assaulted by an air uninteresting as if shoved inside a toilet on a cold winter day, so that nothing can be done.'

Torabaa: 'It is because you lack true understanding regarding Kami. If you but understand Kami, instantly, like me, Jigoku is transformed and you can advance into the boundary of Tenkoku.'

Maga: 'Even if you say to understand Kami, human wisdom has limits. Believing in this precious teaching of Uranai and chanting to the Kami while being hoodwinked by a fox to make a fool of myself, I therefore doubt the existence of Kami.'

Torabaa: 'When one does not acknowledge the existence of Kami and forgets the salvation of Kami, both mind and body are exhausted and destroyed. And when one directly touches the love of Kami and the faith of Kami and truly understands, instantly a shower of joy drenches my whole body, and both spirit and flesh flourish in an ageless and immortal manner. However, at Hegre Shrine and Tanemono Shrine it will not do. You too, in good measure, return the pen name of Giritenjō Hinode no Kami, and become a single sinner. If you but cultivate and nurture the virtue of humility—becoming a humble single servant and taking the shoes of a beggar—in the field of your heart, Tenkoku will open instantly.'

Maga: 'Even if you say that, I cannot abandon Yurahiko-sama, Hegre Shrine, Tanemono Shrine, and the deities of Daimon Shrine whom I have believed in with all my heart until now. Changing so fickle-mindedly like the sky of these recent days, sincerity could hardly be maintained.'

Torabaa: 'Do you not regard the great benevolence of Kami Susanoo Ōkami with gratitude? Can it not be understood that He is the Savior?'

Maga: 'I cannot believe it to the end. Torabaa-sama, think well; if you believe in Susanoo no Mikoto, is there any need to separately establish the Uranai teaching, construct the shrine of Kogitayama, and set up a faction?'

Torabaa: 'That is what is called repentance. When you realize that you were mistaken, to correct it immediately is the duty of human beings. No matter how strong Magahiko-sama's divine power may be, compared to the divine virtue of the August Spirit of Mizu who governs the soil of Arakane, it is but a drop in the ocean; how could it ever compare? Place your hand upon your chest and think well.'

Maga: 'Torabaa-sama, the reason you are speaking so fresh and full of energy is, in short, because you received a souvenir of thirty thousand ryo from Imoriwake, isn't it?'

Hotsu-ho-ho-ho-ho, what an incomprehensible man you are, there is simply no way to help you. My dear husband, while you yourself are sunk in the abyss of misfortune and sorrow, living a life of tears, how can you possibly think you can save others? First save yourself, and once you understand yourself, will you not finally be endowed with the complete divine power to save the world and transmit the Way? Think about it carefully, suppose there is someone drowning in a river here. A person passes by just as that individual is about to drown and die; if that person were blind, though they might hear the cry for help, they could never be able to save them. At such a time, unless one is a person skilled in swimming, physically robust, and with a clear sight, saving that drowning victim would be utterly impossible. Therefore, you too must first strengthen yourself, illuminate yourself, and receive your own divine power to the fullest twelve parts. Once divine power is possessed, the rapture of joy will naturally assail one's own person and surroundings. I too, since the preceding night, having been illuminated by the compassionate light of Kami, from the depth of sorrow, have finally been saved into the realm of bliss and delight. Somehow, I would like to save you and bring you to the same mental state as mine, but since the distance between us is so great, pitifully I suppose I cannot save you. However, if it seems I cannot save you as a first step, how could I possibly save all the people? Ah, it seems as though I am undergoing a test from a formidable Kami. Breaking through Magahikotōge is by no means an easy feat. Ah Kami, by your compassionate light, please grant me true light and true love, illuminate the crookedness lurking in the heart of Magahiko, and somehow guide us both in spirit and body to the heavenly pure land. I earnestly beseech and pray for the divine grace of Kami.'

'Ah, dear me, it is no use after all, what a pitiful thing. I too must save this Otora-san as a first step, or else I shall ultimately be unable to save all the people. Please, Yura-rikohiko no Kami, the Great Kami of Hegure Shrine, by your glory, authority, and affectionate love, grant a single beam of radiance to the heart of the pitiful old woman Otora-bā-san, this elderly lady whom Magahiko deeply respects and loves, so that she may truly trust you, understand you, and thoroughly realize your love, and I earnestly and humbly beg that you let fall a special divine grace upon this elderly lady. Ah, Kannagara Tamashii Sakihamau Mase, the Heavenly Kami of the Sun of the Last Age, the Great Kami of Miroku Accomplishment, Yoshikohime of the Rising Morning Sun, Hinodewake no Kami of Righteousness in Heaven, Ōhirokigimasa-sama, Daishōgun-sama, Tokoyohime-sama, I earnestly beseech and pray to you.'

'Now Magahiko-sama, I told you already not to utter those divine names in my presence, what an impossible person you are, will you never wake up from your delusion, oh what should I do, Kannagara Tamashii Sakihamau Mase, Kuninotokotachi no Ōkamisama...'

'Ah what can I do, how can I dispel Otora-sama's delusion, ah Kannagara Tamashii Sakihamau Mase.'
