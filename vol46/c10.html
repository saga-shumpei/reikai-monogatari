Chapter 10: 唖 Fox 外れ [1220]

唖狐外れ〔1220〕

§ 1

Wasted with love, Magahiko had driven away Hirugitsune with a blank and foolish expression on his face, slouching heavily down the slope until he advanced unconsciously to the edge of the bridge. Just at that moment, coming from the opposite direction, dressed in indescribably beautiful attire and letting her jet-black hair—enough to deceive even a wet crow—stream smoothly down behind, wearing purple hakama and draping a maple-leaf-patterned kazuki loosely around herself, she advanced ploddingly toward this side. Although he knew not who she might be, he stood still, thinking she was a woman whom somewhere he recognized, and gazing with his mouth hanging wide open and his finger in his mouth.

The woman gradually crossed the wooden bridge to this side, stopped right before Magahiko, and laughed softly, 'Ho-ho-ho-ho.' If it were nighttime, Magahiko would have been startled and fled from the spot, but taking advantage of it being still broad daylight, with a facial expression that did not flinch in the least, he peered closely at her. To his utter astonishment, it was none other than the woman he pined for, Ootami-san of Kinugasamura! Starting with a 'ha' of surprise and stroking his chest, 'Hey, hey, I say, Ootami-san, you are truly a terrible person. Joining hands with Imoriwake and dealing me an elbow blow, to slip away under cover of darkness and disappear goodness knows where is simply too much. Taking this chance of meeting here, I shall lay out my manifold grievances.

No matter what, you must listen to me! I shall hold you, leap with you into this river with a splash, and go off to the so-called other world with the resolve to do so. What say you to my reply?' As he pressed close upon her, the woman laughed brightly once more with a 'ho-ho-ho-ho,' smiling most merrily. Thinking it rather suspicious, Magahiko stepped right up and grabbed her slender arm tightly, whereupon Ootami-san revealed little dimples popping up on both cheeks—dimples deep enough to swallow mountains, paddy fields, and storehouses alike. While making her waist sway softly and shaking her neck seductively, how joyous was her leaning against him! Contrary to his expectations, Magahiko hugged her waist tightly and said, 'Hey, hey, I say, Ootami-san, I knew not your heart.

Please, I beg you, forgive me! I, too, am truly overjoyed. Is it a dream, a reality, or an illusion? If it be a dream, a dream is fine, but O gods, protect us so that it may never awaken unto eternity's end. I humbly pray for this. That being said, Ootami-san, what has become of Imoriwake? That is the one thing I wish to hear.' When he pressed her thus, Ootami laughed aloud and said, 'The way I made it appear as if I were casting amorous glances at Imoriwake-sama was solely because I had the purpose in the depth of my heart of wanting to be with you. It was precisely for that reason that I flattered Imoriwake-sama, took advantage of last night's darkness to lead him away into the forest amidst the fields, and hid twenty thousand ryo of gold there.

Using clever words to persuade him, talking that complete fool round, I finally accomplished my purpose and tucked the twenty thousand ryo of money right here into my bosom before returning dejectedly. From now on, all is well! At the sacred ground of Mount Obakita, you shall be the Kyōshu and I the wife. If we have this much money, we can cast aside both Sueisama and Yoshikohime, and make preparations to seize sovereign authority over Mount Obakita. Come, come, let us begin this from now on! Though fools who do not understand say that money is the enemy of this world, money is indeed the ally of one's own body. If one only has money, there is nothing that cannot be accomplished. No matter how foolish a man may be,

he appears wise solely through the virtue of money. Even to a person who would not bestow a single mon or half a-sen, one goes about treating them as "Danna-sama, Danna-sama" and makes one's way through the world with the air of master of all. There is nothing quite so splendid as this! Magahiko-san, have you understood the depths of my heart? I pray you, get along harmoniously with me for all eternity, cherish me as your wife, and live with me to the very end.' When she spoke thus, Magahiko was utterly astonished; not only did he hold the ceremony of union with the woman he loved, but she brought as a dowry an immense sum of money such as he had never laid eyes on since his birth—what could be greater? Yet what weighed on his mind was the matter of Imoriwake. Changing his tone, Magahiko said,

"That is truly splendid. However, I cannot help feeling as though a single worry lies across our path, acting as an obstacle to supreme happiness and bliss. Can we not devise some scheme? If Imoriwake should happen to return to this place by some chance, what shall you and I do? This is my primary concern. What shall we do?" When he inquired thus, Ootami laughed aloud with a "ho-ho-ho" and said, "Do not worry in the least! A woman like me who instigates such a rebellion—where could there be any flaw in her? In the forest amidst the fields, I squeezed his testicles and made him change his province! Now that things have come to this, all is well. We are husband and wife in the broad light of day! Let us make haste to Mount Obakita this very instant!"
