Chapter 3: 噛言 [1213]

噛言〔1213〕

§ 1

The night cleared up completely, and the signal Taiko of the Ōmon Jinshiya Hiromahe could be heard at Shichigosan. Isokō, Mankō, Otora, Magahiko, and the others, Aku, Taku, and Teku, passed through the midst of several dozen Shinjiya and took their seats before the altar, which stood one step higher; Magahiko first recited the Amatsunorito. Following this, Otora once again recited the Kamigoto.

Kamigoto at the mountain of Kogita.

'The Kami dwells in the mountain of Kogita, and the Ōkami of Mirokujiyōjiyu, through the agency of the Toyosakanobo Hime no Mikoto, gathered the Kami of Usoyahoyorozu into the Gakitsudo, and deliberated in the Gakihaka, and thus Ōhirokimasamune and Suzunohimenomikoto caused the water and nasal mucus of Doroashihara to be known as the Ankunokuhirahime no Mikoto. In the land where they caused heads and shame to be hidden, they caused the Kami who take the sweat and fat to be turned around in a circle of wild geese, and swept them out with a Kami-broom; this year, they cut even the single folding screen of the Iwane-kinetate-anakusa, plucked the hair from their heads, and before they knew it, painted the burns on their heads with ink, and caused them to hide their heads. The night wig and Takayamahiko no Mikoto, which were thus placed upon them, were determined to be the Yanchya-onna, and with ink painted upon their white-haired heads, knowing the Magatsutaka in the belly of Takahime, they blocked the ear-holes of the Suzunoyakata no Mikoto, hid the wig of the head and the burns of the sun, deceived them as gentle women, and the various sins that the red strangers, who would burst into tears in the mouths that speak, might have committed, and the Magatsu-tsumi, such as having their heads shorn, ears pulled, eyeballs set on fire, buttocks scattered, combs stuck into bald heads, the skinning of the heads of chicken-sellers, the peeling of reverse hair, and the sins of feces, urine, and farts, were distinguished as Magatsu-tsumi; and the foul sins, such as the cutting of raw skin, that is, body odor, the cutting of dead skin, that is, the tobira, the white-day bed-making, the black-day bed-making, and the sin of doing lewd things with a woman of a different age who is like one's own mother, day and night, the sin of panting and saying scolding words, the sin of committing bestiality, the disaster of lice crawling on the collar, the disaster of Takahime-kami, the disaster of the black-hime bird, the sin of borrowing and failing to pay back, the sin of doing things cleverly, the sin of extortion here and there, will emerge in abundance; if they emerge thus, by the disordered words of the Magatsu-kami, the money that cannot be spared will be cut off, leaving neither principal nor interest, and at the end of my own body, which feels like spitting blood, furthermore, leaving this Otora-hime behind, and furthermore, taking the original Otami with him, hiding his eyes until the very end, taking the two apart, as a fat old woman of the Magatsu-chicken-seller, in traveler's attire, he crossed the river and fled. If it comes to this, there is no help for it; the Magatsu-kami shall push open the rock-door of Maga, and through the eight-fold clouds of Maga, split them into a thousand pieces with the severity of a thousand, and shall listen; the crushed Kami shall cling to the buttocks of Takahime and the buttocks of Kurohime, and shall sniff and listen to the excrement of Takahime and the excrement of Kurohime; having listened thus, the sins, such as they are, have been filled to the brim, and like the foolish stubbornness of the stingy Kami, like the blowing of bubbles, they have given birth to evil karma, and like the evil Kami and the poverty Kami blowing away the evening sacred sake, like farting and defecating and sweeping them into the privy, like the fallen foam returning to the source and counter-attacking, thinking that there is not a single drop of the sake that was drunk, and suffering in the belly and suffering in the spirit, falling headlong from the buttocks of Takahime and the buttocks of Otami, the Kami called Seiwaruhime, who dwells in the rapids of the Kajika River, shall swallow the Imoriwake into the belly of the great body; if she swallows him thus, the foam of the burnt excrement of the foolish buttocks will fly about, and the Kami called Anaaketsuhime shall take Ōhirokimasamune and Otami, make them her wife, and drink sake; if she drinks sake with her wife thus, the Kami called Hehanatoshu, who dwells in the fart-blowing door, shall fart in the land of roots and the land of the bottom; if he farts thus, the Kami called Haradachisurihime, who dwells in the land of roots and the land of the bottom, shall rub and lose them; if the cattle and horses cry thus, may there be love, such as love, in the body and heart of the vacant Otora, and may you grant that it be fulfilled, and may you deliberate, and may you listen with your foolish ears pricked up to the words that say to be careful, as I thrust my head in, and thrusting it in, I speak falsely; ah, since it cannot be fulfilled, may your eyeballs pop out.'

Otora: 'Now, everyone, thank you for your hard work. By now, generally, Imoriwake-sama should also be returning. If by any chance he does not return, Shinbatsu will strike, and since he must die a miserable Notarejini, whether he likes it or not he will surely return. Because Matsuhime-sama is offering her prayers for him, there should be no more delay. Ōhirokimasamune's Flesh Shrine, Sadakohimenomikoto, Yukinagaharunomikoto, Kototaruhimenomikoto, Suzunohime-sama, Momogamihikonomikoto-sama, Chijiyōhimenomikoto-sama, you have always been under my care, so if you have any Shiyōnen, even just for today once is enough, reach out and by the help of Ōkamisama, pull and bring Ōhirokimasamune-sama's Flesh Shrine here, holding everyone of them by the sleeves or hems. If this is something that cannot be done, you are foxes inferior to dogs. No matter what, if you do not thrust Ōhirokimasamune-sama's Flesh Shrine out before me within Kefujiu, this Otora will no longer serve you either! Far from serving you, since I will throw you all out of the shrine and burn you up, today is a decisive moment for you as well, one against a thousand. Is it better for a fox to attain Shiyutsuse, proclaim the name of Kami, and be served splendidly, or is it better to be thrown out? This is a point where you must ponder deeply, even regarding me, Otora; surely you have no reason to sit there with a blank and indifferent face.'

Before she knew it, forgetting those around her, she began to chatter in a loud voice, and suddenly coming to her senses, she held her sleeve to her mouth, and while hiding her face, she quickly concealed her figure in the founder's lodge.

Gosankō and Mankō let Aku, Taku, and Teku mingle among the believers, instructed them to catch and hear the rumors of all, and headed up toward Matsuhime's lodge.

Together with the echoing of the Shichigosan drum, the believers who had gathered from accommodations here and there, shedding tears of gratitude before the grand gate of the shrine and sniffing their noses, reciting the Toshigoto, how admirable it is indeed!

Grandma Otora, gripping a chūkei firmly in her right hand, folding her sleeve with her left hand, with her footsteps graceful, making a rustling sound with her light blue hakama, with Gosankō, Mankō, and three others following behind, appeared before the altar, prostrated herself, finished clapping her hands, and as usual chanted the Amatsuwritogoto, and proclaimed the raw words of Kami;

Yet perhaps for the reason that her heart was disturbed, if one pricked up one's ears from the side and peered, it sounded just like the incomprehensible Norito of before; whether it was the fault of her heart or the fault of her ears, or perhaps the mischief of Magatsuhi, it was a matter that did not make sense, Gosankō, Mankō, and the believers sitting in a row also tilted their heads, and were lost in thought.

Nakagami-sensei's Aku, putting on the airs of a bat in a birdless village, leaped up onto the altar, placed a tall desk in front of him, drank a gulp of water from a cup, and holding forth with the air of an orator—the comedy of that style!

Feeling in his bosom, he took out tissue paper, blew his nose with a honk, folded the paper over once more,

Blew his nose again with a honk, wiped the sleep from his eyes and cleaned the tartar from his teeth, shoved it unceremoniously into his bosom, drank another gulp of water, and with a single cough cleared his throat:

Aku: 'Now, now, ladies and gentlemen of the full house, while it is well that you should have faith in the Kami of this place, if you are not quite careful, business prosperity, healing of illness, and longevity of descendants will not go at all as you wish. Imprinting the state of the heavenly paradise in the hearts of everyone, the intermediate realm and the path of hell, unless you have embodied the precious teachings of various miscellaneous Kami, no matter how much faith you may have tried practicing, it is useless. I was originally a person who served the Kami of Brahmanism, but unexpectedly in the forest in the fields, I happened to meet Hinodewake, the propagation envoy of Misogikyō, and Matsuhiko's party,

And thoroughly understanding the true path, I have followed them all the way here. Though I am still a lukewarm believer and do not firmly know the doctrines, the eyes of a bystander are not so very different after all. Compared to Brahmanism, the Uranai teachings might be somewhat better. That being said, everyone, do you think that the Kami enshrined here is enshrined as the Kami of the moon and sun, the fundamental origin of heaven and earth, the Kami who served at the beginning of the world? If it is a Kami of that precious sort, how is it that Takahime-sama, who opened the Uranai teachings, threw down her helmet to Misogikyō, and together with Kurohime-sama, became an official of Kami?'

If you ponder this point well, you will surely understand the value of the Oshi of divination. Masamune-sama's Nikumiya, who had come to be a Shinjiya, has surely escaped, hidden by the darkness, with Otami-san of Kinugasamura; they must have crossed the Itsuponbashi, climbed over the forest of Nonaka, and by now, they must have fled to the vicinity of the forest of the shrine on Kajikayama. I was disgusted to watch the way Otora-baba-sama was startled, her hair disheveled, as she chased after them. Even after seeing this, do you all still intend to continue your Shinkau in the path of divination? I wish to ask for your opinion for a moment.

If it is all the same, you should believe in the Oshi of the Ananahi Kami, fulfill your Honbun for which you were born into this Genkai with joy and courage, and quickly make preparations to ascend to the Tengoku Jiyōdo. Matsuhime-sama has, after a long time, met her husband from whom she had been separated for a while, and they have successfully performed the Nano of husband, wife, and child. We have replaced the three shrines with the stone shrine built on the Chiyōjiyō, and by enshrining the original Kami of this world, we have come to receive the blessings of the Kami fully, fearing and dreading nothing in heaven or earth, having performed the Chinsai of the true Mitama of the Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami.

It has now come to the stage where we must replace the shrines, leaving not a single one, up to the edge of the Komiya on Kohitakayama this very night, and re-enshrine the Kami who taught the Mitama of the Hensei-danshi. You will all surely be surprised and flustered. If you do not like it, you had better leave this place now and head straight back to your own homes. You who have been taken in by such smooth talk will not easily accept what I say. If there is even one person among you who understands, it would be best for you to participate in the Gosen-gū here tonight.

I will not force you, so I will just keep this in mind. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. May your Mitama be blessed.'

Thus, Aku-kō exposed the true nature of the deities enshrined on Kohitakayama. The hundred or so followers of Kintarō who had gathered suddenly began to clamor, the situation becoming suspicious by the moment, and from every corner here and there, voices cried out:

Speaker: 'Drag that fellow called Aku-kō down, beat him to a pulp! He is a destroyer of the Dōjō, a destroyer of the shrine! We cannot hear such things and let them go. The Ikumiya of Minister Rintōbiten will not permit it!'

On the other hand, there were those who shrieked in weeping voices, 'The Ikumiya of Iwateruhime will not permit it!' Others, claiming to be the Nikumiya of the Ananahi Jōju no Ōkami, the Ōkami of Kiso-yoshihime, and the Ō-teru-sumera-ō-mikami, suddenly went mad, attacked Aku on the platform, rained iron fists upon him, and grabbed his legs to drag him down, staging a scene just like the elected officials at Hibiyagahara. Aku nimbly slipped through the crowd and hid himself. Instead, Taku was caught, and as compensation for the evil words and violent language, he was showered with a rain of iron fists and fell to the ground, half-dead. In an instant, the wide front of Daimon Shrine became a scene of agony and cries, and a great battlefield erupted. Ah, may your eyes pop out since you cannot succeed.

斯の如くアク公は小北山の祭神の素性をスツパ抜いた。百人許り集まつてゐた金太郎の信者は俄に騒ぎ出し、形勢刻々に怪しくなり、彼方此方の隅々から、

『アク公とやらを引摺り落せ、叩き伸ばせ、道場破りだ、宮つぶしだ。俺達は、こんなことを聞いて見逃しにやならない。リントウビテン大臣の生宮が承知致さぬ』

と喚き立てるものがある。一方には岩照姫の生宮が承知致さぬぞやと、金切り声を絞つて泣声でぞめく。其外五六七成就の大神、木曽義姫の大神、大照皇大神宮の肉宮などと自称する信者が、俄に狂ひ立ち、壇上のアクを目がけて襲撃し、鉄拳の雨を降らす、足を取つて引摺りおとす、恰も日比谷ケ原の選良其儘の光景を演出した。アクは人込の中を、スバしこく潜つて姿を隠した。其代りにタクが捉へられ、悪言暴語の代償として鉄拳の雨を浴びせかけられ半死半生となつて、其場に倒れて了つた。忽ち大門神社の広前は阿鼻叫喚、一大修羅場が勃発することとなつた。あゝ叶はぬから目玉飛出しましませよ。
