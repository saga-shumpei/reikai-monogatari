Chapter 23: Golden 華 [1233]

黄金華〔1233〕

§ 1

Otora stopped on the way, looked back at the party, and said:

Otora: 'Listen, everyone. That forest in the middle of the field that stands out clearly in that direction is called the Forest of Mystery, and it is said that the subordinates of General Ranchi are keeping watch there these days. If you turn left from the corner of that forest, you will find the main road, but since this path has become a side road, they are surely checking the people coming and going at that human-character intersection. Shall we consult here and prepare to break through safely?'

Matsuhiko: 'We are candidates for the propagandists of the Ananai-kyō, which is known throughout the world. Even if there are dozens of enemies lying in wait, there is no need to be afraid, is there?'

Otora: 'No, that is out of the question. Is the Brahman not in the midst of marching with the Kamuyakata of the Ananai-kyō, Ubusuna Mountain, as its objective? For you, the propagandists of the Ananai-kyō, and especially you who have been dispatched from Ubusuna Mountain, to appear there is like a moth flying into a fire; it is as if you are throwing away your precious lives. You must not be stubborn in a place like this. A hard skin is brittle and easily broken, while a soft twig does not break in the wind; it is said that no matter how hard teeth are, they perish before the soft tongue. Being merely strong is not the heart of God. In any case, let us rest here for a while. I have not walked for a long time, so my transportation system has become unreliable. Please, do rest.'

Matsuhiko: 'This is why traveling with an old woman is troublesome. However, it cannot be helped. Hey everyone, shall we agree to Otora-sama's proposal and take a rest for a while?'

Gosan: 'Yes, that would be fine.'

They spread their straw raincoats on the grass by the roadside and sat down. A Yutsu-tsumagushi comb was lying in front of where Otora-baba-sama sat. Otora picked it up quickly, and when she looked at it closely, she saw it was her own comb, which she recognized. This was a tortoiseshell comb that Otora had made by sparing no expense during her days as a chivalrous woman. Otora gazed at it intently and said:

Otora: 'Well now, I had lost this comb long ago after coming to Kohokuzan, but I never dreamed it would be lying in a place like this. Most likely, Imoriwake-dono secretly did something and gave it to Omin. If so, Omin must have dropped it when she passed through here. If Omin passed through, then Imoriwake-dono must have passed through as well, without a doubt.'

She reached this conclusion. However, the Otora of the present moment was no longer the Otora of yesterday. Her attachments and jealousy had been wiped away cleanly, and she had become a soul as clear as the blue sky. Until yesterday, if she had seen this comb, her appearance would have changed instantly, invisible horns would have grown on her head, and she would have roared like a lion or a tiger. However, since being illuminated by the light of God, it goes without saying that it was entirely thanks to the teachings of God that she was able to pick up her own comb, which had been held by Omin, her rival in love, without feeling the slightest jealousy, and without even changing her expression, she was able to smile calmly.

Mankō peered at the comb Otora had picked up and said:

Mankō: 'Otora-sama, is that not a comb that seems to have some meaning? It seems that some mysterious connection is entwined with you regarding this comb.'

Otora: 'That is so. If it were until yesterday, the threads of connection would have been tangled, but now that it is today, there is nothing. Some woman must have dropped it and left. We might catch up with her or meet her further ahead, you see. Mankō-sama, would you mind keeping it for me until we meet the owner who lost it?'

Mankō: 'A tortoiseshell comb is not something a man should carry. If you, a woman, were to carry it, it would suit you and be appropriate; although this is a light object, I must humbly decline. Besides, is it not bad to pick up a comb? It is said that it leads to regrets. It is said that when one has great worries and is distressed, one throws the comb away on the road to escape that worry. Whoever picks it up picks up that distress, so they will surely meet with disaster. Why don't you just throw it away there?'
