Ah, Catastrophe!

ああ大変〔195〕

§ 1

Hereupon, Yatsuō-ōkami consulted with the deities, gathered their unanimous opinions, and, in order to report to the Great Deity of the Sun, the Great Deity of the Moon, and Hirome-ōkami residing in heaven that the National Ancestor was stubborn and utterly unfit for the governance of the earthly world, led numerous deities while shaking heaven and earth, went up to the Young Palace of the Sun, was granted audience with the Great Deities, and petitioned to the utmost of his words for the retirement of the National Ancestor.

Even though they were the Great Deities of heaven, because their ancestral deity was the National Ancestor Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, they were greatly astonished; and that they grew anxious to somehow cause the National Ancestor to change his mind, to implement somewhat milder divine work and divine rule upon the earth, to soothe the hearts of all the deities, and to unify the deities under the National Ancestor's authority as before, must be said to have been a truly natural course of action as a feeling between kin.

Hereupon, the Great Deity of the Sun and Hirome-ōkami residing in the Young Palace of Heaven, together with the Great Deity of the Moon who was the presiding deity of the lunar realm, issued a divine command to Yatsuō-ōkami and the deities under him to descend to the lower land and await orders until further notice; and they descended, complying submissively.

Ah, the National Ancestor Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto obeyed the divine command of the Primordial Ancestor of the great universe, Ōametsuchitokotachi-no-Mikoto, and, from the very beginning when heaven and earth were not yet divided and yin and yang were not yet separated, as the supreme guardian deity of the earthly world at the center of the great land, accomplished the great undertaking of repairing and consolidating, governed the divine land that drifted like floating jellyfish, and strictly executed the laws. However, because the Great Deity's measures were overly strict and rigid, as the deity in charge of the chaotic era, he could not avoid being somewhat unfit. Therefore, the subordinate deities felt extreme inconvenience in the implementation of the divine administration. Fortunately, all the deities expressed approval of the flexible and freely adaptive administrative policies of Yatsuō-ōkami, who sought to implement harmonious and accommodating divine measures, and of Daijizaiten; and finally, all the deities gathered at Tokoyo Castle and came to resolve that the National Ancestor should retire.

The three Great Deities deemed the condition of the earthly world unavoidable and tried, swallowing their tears, to grant the petition of all the deities. However, reflecting that once he had been appointed the ruler of the earthly world, the divine edict was momentous and not something to be altered lightly, they did not readily grant the petition of all the deities, and at once addressed the National Ancestor, using all kinds of words, either soothing him or attempting to persuade him, so that he might conduct a somewhat milder divine administration.

Yet, the sacred will of the National Ancestor, supremely upright, supremely straightforward, supremely strict, and supremely impartial, was not easily swayed even by the commands of the three Great Kami.

The three Heavenly Great Kami, having almost no means to intervene, here summoned Toyokunihime no Mikoto, the spouse of the National Ancestor, to heaven, and sought to have her undertake the labor of admonishing the National Ancestor so that he might conduct a divine administration adapting to the trends of the times. Toyokunihime no Mikoto, receiving the divine command, descended to the Holy Land, exhausted every manner of speech to convey the divine will of the Amatsu-kami, and admonished him with tears, yet the National Ancestor Kami, who was by nature single-mindedly upright, had no liking for conducting such an accommodating divine administration, resolutely and sternly refused his spouse's admonition, expounded upon the inviolable sanctity of the laws of heaven and earth, and yielded not in the least.

Thereupon, Toyokunihime no Mikoto had no choice but to ascend to heaven once again, and reported to the three Great Kami that the National Ancestor's resolve was firm and utterly impossible to move.

Just at that time, Hachiō-ōkami climbed up to heaven in pursuit of Toyokunihime no Mikoto, respectfully presented a petition before the Sun Ōkami residing in Ame no Wakamiya, and requested a judgment. The Sun Ōkami looked upon the petition presented by Hachiō-ōkami, suddenly changed countenance, and let out a deep sigh. In its text, it stated:

"Although the National Ancestor Kuniharu-tachi no Mikoto is spoken of as a holy one who is supremely strict, supremely upright, and strictly observes the laws, in truth he has many actions quite to the contrary; in fact, as soon as the previous Tokoyohiko no Mikoto held a great council at Tokoyo Castle, he personally imparted a secret plan to Onitakehiko of Mount Ōe, a retainer of the Holy Land, and, exhausting every form of political craft and intrigue, threw the great council of the supremely strict and supremely holy divine beings into turmoil and entanglement, and ultimately overturned it from its very foundation. Beginning with us, there is no longer a single divine being in the terrestrial world who places trust in the National Ancestor. As representatives of the divine beings of the earthly realm in general, we humbly petition that the National Ancestor be promptly retired, and that the divine authority of the National Ancestor be granted to Banko-ōkami Shionagahiko no Mikoto, who is gentle, sincere, and foremost under heaven in renown. Respectfully stated."

The divine-human beings of the earthly world in general came up to heaven time and again, frequently petitioning for the retirement of the Kokuso Ōkami; the three Ōkami restrained, soothed, and sought divine plans to moderate them, but in the end, the three Ōkami together descended in person from heaven to the holy ground, and swallowing their tears, conveyed telepathically to the Kokuso Ōkami that he should leave the holy ground of Jerusalem and descend to the Nether World. The Kokuso Ōkami perceived the deep feelings of the three Ōkami, and spontaneously said,

"I am originally obstinate, spiritually unenlightened, and unable to comprehend the times, and having caused the earthly divine-human beings to turn into a world of eternal darkness like this is entirely the sin of my own lack of wisdom; therefore, I shall now fall to the Nether World, taste bitter trials, and expiate that guilt,"

and thus personally bearing the thousand tables of expiatory offerings, he displayed his intention to retire.

Ah, the Kokuso exerted the divine character of supreme justice, supreme uprightness, supreme strictness, and supreme love, and strained his heart and soul for many years to make the earthly world a divine land of supreme peace and order; yet that great divine work was ultimately not accepted by the myriad divine-human beings, and he was instead regarded as if he were an evil deity or demon; beholding with reverence that great divine heart of great mercy and great compassion, by which he offered his own person as a sacrifice for the sake of world peace and resolved upon his own retirement, who could fail to weep?
