Volume 4 • Chapter 35

Cold Water Upon the Head

頭上の冷水〔185〕

§ 1

Due to the misgovernment of Momogamihiko no Mikoto, the Holy Ground Jerusalem once again suffered repeated confusion and discord, and like ice melting in the sun, it leaned toward decline and destruction day by day and month by month. The Kokusō-ōkami was just like a crab whose limbs had been torn off, driven to an impasse with no means to do anything. The Yatsuō of each mountain and each land gathered once again at Tokoyo Castle and came to the inevitable point of putting their heads together to consult on the restoration of the Holy Ground.

At this time, as Hirowaka and Oniwaka, the two envoys of Tokoyohime from the Holy Ground, came down riding the Ame-no-Torifune, the Yatsuō Tokoyohiko immediately led the envoys to a room and asked the purpose of their visit. The two stated in detail that the tragic state of the Holy Ground was too pitiful to look upon, and that if it were left as it was, the Holy Ground would have no choice but to perish.

Tokoyohiko, who had returned to his heaven-bestowed original mind, whose original guardian spirit's activities were complete, and who had been reformed into a good deity of supreme goodness and supreme beauty, also felt a kind of anxiety at this time, and looking up to heaven, let slip a sigh of lamentation. The spirit of the serpent with eight heads and eight tails, which had been aiming for this opening, laughed clatteringly from overhead,

"You coward, you faint-hearted one! With a weakling like you, let alone Tokoyo Castle, even if you fret over the rescue of the Holy Ground, what ability could you possibly have? Return quickly to your original mind, stir up the courage of the aramitama, open the enlightenment of the kushimitama, and rather than fretting over trivial matters, why do you not manly manifest a heroic heart? One who lacks confidence and decisive action is no different from a maggot. Promptly stir up a great heroic spirit, and dare to carry out the heroic divine work of cutting the tangled hemp with a sharp sword. I am indeed the divine messenger of the Hi no Ōkami dwelling in the Hi-no-Wakamiya; never doubt this!"

No sooner had it spoken than the voice stopped abruptly. Just as the Yatsuō Tokoyohiko, in gasping distress with his hands clasped, was sitting quietly in the inner palace expending thousands of thoughts and myriad deliberations concerning what measures to take, once again there was a voice in the sky:

"I am Ōkunitokotachi no Mikoto. Kunitokotachi no Mikoto has now fallen into a plight and is in extreme distress. While you hold a sacred post serving the divine work, what makes you suffer, hesitate, and waver at such a time of life or death? To exist in name only applies to you. Rise up swiftly! In this world, there is nothing to fear except God. Without worrying about a single thing, confer with the Yatsuō deities of each land, and hasten swiftly to the Holy Ground Jerusalem. God shall be by your side and protect you."

このとき聖地から常世姫の使者として広若、鬼若の二人が、天の鳥船に乗って下ってきたところ、八王常世彦は、直ちに使者を一室に導いて来訪の理由を尋ねた。二人は、聖地の惨状は見てもいられず、このまま放置するならば、聖地は滅亡するほかないということを詳細に申し立てた。

と言うかと見るや、その声はばったりと止まった。八王常世彦は青息吐息の様子で両手を組み、奥殿に静かに座してその処置についてあれこれと思慮を重ねている折しも、再び天空に声があった、

「我は大国治立命である。国治立命は今や窮地に陥り、非常なる苦境にある。汝は神業に奉仕する神聖なる職に就きながら、このような危急存亡の場合において何を苦しみ躊躇逡巡しているのか。有名無実とは汝のことである。速やかに奮い立て、世の中で恐れるものは神のほかにない。一つも憂慮することなく各地の八王神と相談し、速やかに聖地エルサレムへ馳せ参ぜよ。神は汝に寄り添って守るであろう」

§ 2

Having finished calling out in a loud voice, the voice of the ogre stopped abruptly once again.

While wandering in dense fog, Tokoyohiko could not bear to sit idly by upon hearing of the distress of the most merciful and compassionate Kokusō Ōkami; he made a firm resolution, attended the great assembly hall where the deities were gathered, and conveying to the various deities the proclamations of Ōkuniharutachi no Mikoto and one other deity, urged them to make their decision. However, this one who called himself Ōkuniharutachi no Mikoto was an utter false god, being an ogre of six faces and eight arms guarding Daijizaiten.

Hearing the words of Tokoyohiko, the numerous Hachiō, in their excessive concern for the sacred ground, had no sense of discretion before or after, nor did they have the leisure to consider whether that voice was the word of a righteous god or the word of an evil god, and unanimously agreed with Tokoyohiko's words. Thereupon, Tokoyohiko, seeking to save the sacred ground with utter sincerity, boarded the Ame-no-Iwakusafune together with the Hachiō, and, making the heavens roar, arrived safely at the sacred ground of Jerusalem.

Momogamihiko no Mikoto was terrified at the spectacle of the Hachiō descending wing to wing, and,

"Tokoyohiko has once again aroused an evil heart, occupied this sacred ground, and committed an act of outright rebellion seeking to displace the Kokusō and occupy even that position. Deities of the sacred ground, take up arms immediately and annihilate these rebels!"

he shouted in hoarse command; however, the deities of the sacred ground were terrified or paralyzed with fear by the superior strength of that force, and like mice chased by a cat, some fled seeking their own personal safety and some hid, so that not a single deity obeyed the command of Momogamihiko no Mikoto. Momogamihiko no Mikoto, in panicky confusion, advanced into the great palace and had an audience with Kokusō Ōkami,

§ 3

"Tokoyohiko has plotted rebellion and, leading numerous Hachiō and other deities, has aggressively pressed his attack; how shall we deal with this?"

When he submitted this report, Kokusō Ōkami rose up resolutely and said:

"The cause of matters having reached this point is the retribution for your breaking the divine laws and taking loose and illicit actions; therefore, apologize for your sin toward heaven as swiftly as possible, immediately resign your post, and declare your utmost sincerity."

Having strictly declared this, he went deep into the inner palace. Momogamihiko no Mikoto, having no recourse, tearfully and dejectedly left the palace, and as he was about to return to his own residence, Tokoyohime, elaborately attired in formal dress together with Kasugahime and Yashimahime, entered and said:

"The Hachiō Ōkami could not bear to sit by and watch the turmoil in the holy land, and has arrived together with numerous deities to save the holy land. Archangel, please report this matter promptly to Kokusō Ōkami."

As she spoke these words gracefully, Momogamihiko no Mikoto went to the palace once more, and as he was about to submit a single word from outside the sliding door to report this situation to Kokusō Ōkami while straining his sorrowful voice, the Great Kami spoke merely a single word from within:

"There are no two words to the word of Kami; swiftly apologize for your sin toward heaven and earth, and never come before Me again."

Because he declared this with stern words, Momogamihiko no Mikoto, having no alternative, stepped down from the palace and returned once more to his residence with a look of melancholy upon his face.
