§ 1

As Tokoyohime had notified each of the eight hundred and eighty-eight deities in polite detail that Yatsuō Ōkami had completely recovered from his illness and was to continue the conference, the deities vied with one another to assemble in the grand hall, and, as was customary, Yatsuō Ōkami, together with Tokoyohime, Kasugahime, Yashimahime, and the other deities of Tokoyo Castle, revealed their magnificent appearances upon the central raised seat as if adorned with flowers. At this time, Yukinarihiko immediately mounted the platform, congratulated with utmost eloquence the complete healing of Yatsuō Ōkami's sudden illness and his attendance once again at this important conference, shouted "banzai" together with the deities, and caused Sarudahime, Izumohime, Kasugahime, and Yashimahime to perform an elegant and noble dance piece upon the stage to express their congratulations. The charming and graceful appearances of the four women were just as if cherry blossoms had bloomed upon willow branches, accompanied by the fragrance of white plums. Wearing golden eboshi upon their heads, matching pink robes, and trailing long scarlet hakama, the four women synchronized their hand-clapping and foot-tapping all at once, appearing like butterflies playfully fluttering and sporting among the wild flowers of a spring field. The deities, their hearts and souls captivated by this tranquil scene, gazed upon it, forgetting themselves.

Yukinarihiko stood upon the stage and began to sing in an elegant tone; with a delightful melody resembling a warbler meeting the spring sun, opening the valley door, and singing of spring upon the twigs of a white plum, or a bell cricket singing with a cool voice in the evening of an autumn field, he instantly made the surrounding air pure and fresh.

His song:

Revering the divine heart of the mighty deities, The nation-spirits of the four quarters of heaven and earth, The chiefs of the Eight Kings and the eight heads, The hundred deities, the eight million, Gather in divine assembly in the Land of Tokoyo; Tigers, wolves, lions, and giant serpents, Demons, Sagume, and Magatsumi, Draw near and gather together, To blow away the clouds of the heart, Sweeping and purifying for the divine age,

The auspicious light shining wondrously, Weaving the great august loom of twill and brocade, The governance of the divine age, Warp and weft, firm and everlasting as rock, The divine spirit entrusted by the deities Appearing in the world, to protect The peaceful land of the beautiful, Glorious Miroku Ōkami, In the Land of Tokoyo of auspicious heart, Tokoyohiko rules.

"Remove the violent vessel, Reform it from its very foundation, Swiftly remove it and conform Unto the Great Kami; Auspicious is the deity of the divine heart of the pine era, Auspicious is the deity of the pine heart."

Yukinarihiko completely reversed his previous adamantly oppositional attitude, composed a song in support of Hachio-okami, and astonished the various divine officials with this sudden change. Joyfully and in silence, Hachio-okami listened intently to Yukinarihiko's song of praise.

Tokoyohime could not make sense of Yukinarihiko's actions, tilted her head repeatedly, and appeared like one lost in deep contemplation.

That Yukinarihiko adopted such an altered attitude was because he knew full well that Hachio-okami was an impostor. The four maidens bowed to the entire assembly and quietly descended from the platform while exuding an indescribable charm. At this moment, there was a divine official who requested the floor from the lowest seat and ascended the platform.

This was Hachio Arikunihiko of Mount Chohaku, a divine official whose nature was mild and consistently of the utmost sincerity. His head showed a slight baldness at the crown, his height was slender and tall, and he appeared endowed with dignity and virtue from somewhere within. Having now heard Yukinarihiko's suddenly altered song, unlike his usual gentle disposition, he stood up fiercely and ascended the platform. Seeing that his complexion was unusual, the various divine officials worried over what he might say. Opening his mouth, he said:

"O divine officials of the entire assembly, regarding this great conference, countless doubts are piled up within our hearts. In the first place, speaking of the blunder in the muddy field, the uncanny successive appearance of women of identical form, the sudden illness of Hachio-okami, and the strange incident concerning Moritakahiko, the occurrence of endless mysterious events if one counts them—is this state of affairs not truly worthy of being called a den of monsters and apparitions? Not only that, but is not the softening and sudden change of Yukinarihiko, the deputy of Archangel Hiromunehiko of the Holy Land of Jerusalem, and his acrobatic action of the 'swallow's turn,' exceedingly suspicious and impossible to foresee? Thinking on it, has not Yukinarihiko also suffered an abnormality of mind as a result of fatigue day after day? Or has he not been possessed and deceived by the evil kami and devils that have been disrupting the hall since yesterday, forgetting his important mission and performing such an unsightly act of altering his views and changing his discourse? The more we ponder, the only things that come are what we cannot comprehend, and by all means we cannot help but doubt to the utmost. In short, does this conference not begin in strangeness and end in strangeness? We cannot help but conclude that the Great Ancestral Kami, in order to destroy from the very foundation the rebellious objective of pernicious egalitarianism—which goes against the august will of Kuniharu-tachi-no-Mikoto, the Ancestor of the Land, seeks to abolish the sacred thrones of the Hachio kami established by the Divine Realm, and aims to establish a brigand-like theocracy—has applied the harsh whip of chastisement arising from the divine mind unto all kami and men. Therefore, although it is discourteous, we shall withdraw from this conference as of today. Divine officials, please do not consider our action as something that disturbs this conference."

Having finished speaking with force in his voice, just as he was about to descend from the platform,

"Wait, wait!"

there was a divine official who shouted in a loud voice.

『満座の諸神司よ、吾々は今回の大会議については、許多の疑問胸中に山積せり。第一に泥田の中の失態といひ、同じ姿の女性の続出せる怪といひ、八王大神の急病といひ、森鷹彦の異変といひ、数へきたれば限りなき怪事の続出すること、あたかも妖怪変化の巣窟ともいふべき有様ならずや。しかのみならず聖地ヱルサレムの天使長広宗彦の代理たる行成彦の軟化豹変、燕返しの曲芸的行動の不審千万にして逆睹すべからざるに非ずや。思ふに行成彦も、連日の疲労の結果精神に異状をきたせしにはあらざるか。ただしは前日来議場を攪乱しつつありし邪神悪鬼に憑依され、誑惑されて、その大切なる使命を忘却し、かかる変説改論の醜を演じたるには非ざるか。熟考すればするにつけ腑に落ちぬことのみ、如何にしても、吾らは何処までも疑はざるを得ぬ。要するに、今回の会議は怪より始まりて怪に終るにあらざるか。吾々は国祖国治立命の聖慮に背き、神界の御制定になれる八王神の聖座を撤廃し、野武士的神政を樹立せむとする悪平等主義の、反逆的目的を根底より破壊せむとの、国祖大神の御心に出づる諸神人への厳しき懲戒の鞭を加へさせたまひしものと断ぜざるを得ず。ゆゑに吾々は失礼ながら今日かぎり本会議を脱退せむ。諸神司よろしく吾々の行動をもつて本会議を乱すものとなす勿れ』

と声に力をこめて述べをはり、降壇せむとするや、

『暫く、しばらく』

と大声に呼ばはる神司ありける。
