Appendix: Second Travelogue Poems of Mount Takakuma Pilgrimage

附録　第二回高熊山参拝紀行歌

§ 1

After the Setsubun festival of the great and righteous Mizunoe-Inu year ended, the way of the divine Deguchi expands greatly, opening the entrance of the benevolent third generation; into the great sky where the mind is also pure, straight, righteous, and clear, beginning with Yukawa Kan'ichi, the president of the Ōmoto Zuishōkai, to worship the forty-eight sacred seats of the high Takakuma where divine virtues are exalted, and to ease the divine will, the sincere hearts of the sturdy men with faith firm enough to melt gold and iron, serving the imperial country, are affirmed even by the gods of heaven and earth, remaining above the clouds; the sincerity conceived to dedicate to the gods and the sovereign is the Iwata belt unknown even in the dreams of worldly people, which Onisaburō, the former religious leader of the Great Origin who endured the long span of twenty-five years; the blossom of the teachings too, in Sakurai, is the white plum blooming in the governance of universal brotherhood in the divine world, whose fragrance wafts in all four directions awaiting like the pine heart; parting the gathering clouds in all four directions, sitting upon Mount Sumeru, the majesty of the god of Ōmoto who created heaven and earth richly manifests in the nations, islands, mountains, and rivers within and without; in the northern sky where the light of the heavenly plain is also strong, the Asajigahara plain broadens ever more, without distinction between far and near; though the world is deep in foot-dragging mountains, it must have become a felicitous era of benevolence and unity between god and humankind; to the east is Ogumo, to the west Yotsuo, Yoshimi village across the river, inside which stands the valiant Dragon Mansion; the Setsubun festival having ended, believers of one mind vied to reach the station, shaken by the train they valiantly advanced toward Higashio; even its name is auspicious, the Manjuen, the grand headquarters of the Zuishōkai; crossing the high bridge of Wachi in the true divine forest of Yoshihito, with the heart of evergreen pines, they entered the Zuishōkaku; the Ōi River also changes its name, flowing down through the Hozu gorge, its water current increasingly grand and roaring; parting through the bamboo grass and brushwood of Takakuma, Kunitakehiko and Kowadahime, yearning for the sacred divine vestiges, climbed the high divine mountain where evergreen pines flourish with fine color, without resting; enveloped by fields and valleys, the single-leaf ferns growing thick upon the crags are vivid green and vigorous, flourishing with a fine view of the three valleys; a party of two hundred and fifty people, the voices of their prayers clear and resonant, the children of god advancing along the slope overgrown with wisteria vines along the divine way of one mind, ran like flying arrows across the grass covering the knees; the sincere hearts like gold and iron of officers and believers are truly valiant; upon the mountain of pines where fine colors overlap, their sturdy feet advance well, along the edge of the pond rather than a seashore, the troop of virtuous men and beautiful women steadily traces the path under the forest, without even shrinking before bears.

§ 2

In the fourth month of the Kinoene year, at Egashira; knowing neither right nor left, opening the gateway and climbing up. Deeming the ascent of Ōharayama and Kyōzuka as good, wearing the divine wind and the sky of Fukui as a woven hat, once again advancing toward the mountain of the Kami. The scenery of Umanose, gradually growing dark, is fine; a young man, heading toward the deep mountains on the path of the night, exceedingly peacefully crosses the Inagawa river, which there is no reason to refuse. Viewing on the right hand the Kaeru-iwa rock fashioned by the Kami, passing the miscanthus field, as the mountain entrance draws near, taking the rumbling voice of the echo—unlike the usual—as a guide, peacefully quickening steps into the embrace of the Kami of universal benevolence and impartial love, along the Hasegawa river, treading through the thickets of Yaetsu and wisteria vines, advancing through Tamakogahara. The roadside grass along the cleared path grows thick; how truly precious are the teachings of the Kami.

§ 3

O, divine mountain supreme in my native place! The force of the auspicious water flowing purely at the foot of the bridge of Obata, which carries people across the world, resounds with loyalty and filial piety. Miyagaito of deep origin; as the head of the divine Deguchi, the rare Kotodama of the Great Kami of the worldly Great Origin, the auspicious omens of the turtles of Shiga are also fully fulfilled. Awaiting the reign of Miroku, the path of the teachings is also exceedingly clear; aided by a rickshaw, spirited on the distant road stretched like a bow, crossing valley after valley at a single stroke, chanting the grand Norito peacefully and clearly. Tracing old memories, for the first time knowing the taste of hardship; in Kameoka, auspicious even in its name, Mr. Mori Yoshihito and Mr. Higashio, with the spirit of the evergreen pine, courageously after a long time, climbing up is delightful; the divine village where plum blossoms are fragrant. The sunlight of the morning sun rising vigorously, the foolish face of a Tenpō-era old man: 'You are quite an Ichisuke,' so slighted, a leather pouch, a blockhead's castella cake, Yoshimatsu who cannot be called good; the son's power, shining talent; Miyagaito in Anou, Sogabe; Ueda Kisaburō, a foolish dunce, also in his snotty childhood, the divine power fragrantly wrapped in his small heart revealed itself, a divine art superior to men, wishes granted with deep smiles; obeying the divine teachings, declaring let us proceed clearly, the brave man pressing forward dividing the mountain path, without even looking back, to test the everyday power of faith, the small son of a dirt-poor peasant, departing from his shabby dwelling, carrying his heavy body, stepping over the rocks and foremost, climbing upward without casting a sideways glance. Pleasantly becoming the mountain of Takakura, the virtue of the Kami; the naughty rogue clear-voicedly crossing the mountain stream is also to devote himself for the sake of the world, the descendant of the Nakatomi; a carefree person of idle tranquility, resolving that one cannot leave the divine service of the worldly Great Origin without cleanly fulfilling it, headlong into the path of the Kami, casting both heart and body, pondering somehow or other the divine scriptures that cannot easily be solved, illuminated by the divine light, the sky of the heart has also dawned.

When the blossoms of Yoshino open, the time is truly good; deeming it a spring that multiplies divine virtues, with a spirit nimble and valiant from the depths of the heart, receiving the dew of the long night road, carrying this one and only body into the mountains, crossing valley streams and aiming all alone for the divine mountain of Takakura where pine trees flourish, relying upon the gods and Buddhas, establishing and carrying through single-mindedly the path of utmost purity and goodness that crosses early into the divine age where cranes nest in thousand-year pines, this merit also resounds as the greatest in the entire world across the vast and everlasting realm.

§ 5

The path to manifest the divine virtue of the tranquil land lies in reverence for the kami, followed by honoring the sovereign and loving the nation. The divine messenger governs the world, leveling it evenly and opening the path of the blossoming teachings. The treasury of divine virtue is profoundly revered; across mountains, rivers, and fields, the governance of the flowers of faith blooms fragrant under divine protection. Like the morning sunlight rising over the eastern mountain ridges, the auspicious reign of Miroku brings joy unmatched since ancient times. Divine light shines upon the heads of the righteous walking through this world, in this divine land where households flourish like green young spring groves. In this rock-firm, auspicious era delighting by the eternal tortoise-jewel well, the central land of fertile reed plains is governed by the lord of benevolence and righteousness.

Divine protection grows ever deeper, the conduct of the people is upright, and even in deserted mountain depths the sweet scent of chrysanthemums wafts; both the common folk and the nobility advance in pure and heroic vigor. Truly, in all matters, the myriad lands are polished, pacified, and opened. Through the divine way of universal benevolence, the hearts of valiant men firm in justice are refreshed by the assisting kami of the sacred spirit. Mitsubahiko, recounting the conditions of the ancient age of kami and dispelling the delusions of the populace, appears in the high heavens of Aya, sonorously declaiming across cosmic space. Within this sacred grove of divine protection, in a life of single-minded loyalty, this is the training ground at the source of the Imperial Kami's teachings that all must visit at least once.

The way is righteous and grand, transmitted throughout the realm; the flowers of the teaching are ever fresh, and companions sharing the same spiritual path unite their strength to purify the ways of this world from the very foundation. Scattering the thick clouds in all four directions across a world fraught with disaster, this is the divine reign of absolute sincerity. Discarding the old and welcoming the new, reflecting the light of the sun and moon upon the sea of the heart, the august dignity of the Sumiyoshi kami is deeply revered. Thousand-year cranes spread their wings across the marshes, singing praise of the divine era in this eternal land of pines. Purifying the realm in all directions with the water of the jeweled well and governing it as a peaceful capital, one hears that the time arrives when the clouds in the northern sky part and the starlight shines high above.

Exclaiming, 'Ah, what a blessed divine age,' the eight million heavenly kami, the eight million earthly kami, the populace, the beasts, and the tiny insects all receive the morning dew of the same grace, transcending good and evil. Waiting for the days and months when the rain of benevolence falls, the divine hall of Ōmoto is joyous and delightful. Auspicious and flourishing, the land of the rising sun is a divine realm devoted to benevolence and justice. The profound divine grace of the sacred katsura tree is vast and peaceful, and awaiting the impending sacred reign, the hearts of the people are expansive, pure, and reassuring.

The people of Western nations also join as friends, relying on the power of the kami dwelling high above the clouds and gathering one after another before the kami. Blessed with the reassuring longevity of the tortoise, the light of the kami, born as the parent of this world, peacefully illuminates from the crossroads to the deep mountains. Everyone in sequence partakes of this serene teaching of flowering plum blossoms, carrying the teachings of the Great Imperial Sovereign Kami that govern heaven and earth through the valleys of Mount Ōhara and the western hills to the farthest corners of the land. No matter how violently and fiercely the ferocious warriors may attack, we are aided by the divine protection; the parental heart is warm, comforting without anger, far surpassing human love, and the great blessings that devote their heart to the nation are truly noble. The companions of the teachings abound; humbly serving the central pillar of the path of truth opened by the Great Primordial Deity, all is peaceful. Like forest tops brushing away the dense clouds, the reigning era shall last exceedingly long, and auspicious events follow one after another day by day. Becoming a bamboo broom that purifies and sweeps away both great and small deities of calamity, the three spirits manifest, and through the divine teachings of Mount Takakuma, the waves become tranquil toward the other shore; like a turtle swimming across the manifold waters, how valiant is the divine majesty. Venerating and rejoicing in it as a treasure in a dark world where black and white cannot be distinguished, crooked spirits are firmly subdued through the Heavenly Ritual Prayers, and the world has cleared. § 11

In the thriving and auspicious Way of the kami, where the great treasures of people abundantly spread and flourish in the Land of Fresh Rice Ears, the bond between sovereign and subjects is pure, and this Land of Eight Islands is well governed, leading all into the Great Way of the kami of Hinomoto, the land of Yamato, reaching even to the far borders of the Eternal Realm. Before the Sovereign of Sun Succession, the pines upon the rocks stand firm, faithfully guarding all the mountain rivers, grass, trees, and even the stones that serve so well. Amidst a world constantly shifting between deep abysses and shallow rapids, parting the clouds of the distant heavens, the two deities, male and female, reveal themselves; from the heights of the mountain down to the west and east without a single shadow, all is brought to peaceful order. Encircled by the green-fenced mountains, thick palace pillars are firmly erected upon the bedrock deep below; the treasures of the fields of this tranquil land are the farmers and warriors, and as the world advances into great peace, harvested grains are offered before the kami like towering mountains, the smoke of abundant hearths rises high, and the clear springs coursing beneath the trees are beheld as divine blessings. The stream of the soul of the fresh rice ears, which established the foundation of a thousand generations, is like a long and pure, blessed river. In this divine proving ground for the reformation of the world, boast not of lineage, origin, or family standing, but dedicate all your strength for the sake of the Way without a moment's pause. Guided by the ever-widening divine aid of Takamanohara, those who have become officials of the kami shall protect the world steadfastly and forever. Among the helping deities, the divine virtue of the especially revered Kunitakehiko-no-mikoto is eminently surpassing and manifest. At the foot of the slope of Yomotsu, strictly discerning between good and evil while the divine warriors maintain a firm guard, the blessed soul of the Great Kami of the Sea, the helping deity of this tranquil land, softens and pacifies even the wicked deities of misfortune; ever vigorous is the breath of the God of Ushitora, providing pure, clear, and immaculate divine protection. Blessed by the apportioned spirit of the Sun Deity of the Heavenly Kami's Realm, the officials of the kami devote themselves entirely to single-minded loyalty. In the Land of Eight Islands, revere above all else the teachings of the kami, too awe-inspiring even to utter; those who dedicate themselves wholly to the kami and the sovereign shall keep their names and seats pure and good, resolving never to take upon themselves the base and vile names of the Middle Land, forever defending the Way of divine sincerity. Deguchi Oni, who harmoniously, clearly, and without stagnation speaks and pacifies all through the spiritual words created by the Sovereign Kami of the Middle Land, with a renewed and tranquil heart, was born in the village of Sogabe where the waters of the Obata River run clear, bearing the childhood name of Ueda Kisaburō. Wielding the brushhairs preserved by divine assistance, he roughly traces the deep spiritual ties of Mount Takakuma, scratching his head in sheer embarrassment as he leaves behind this worthless verse.
