Awakening of Reverence for Kami

第一一章　敬神の自覚〔161〕

§ 1
§ 1: Five Precepts of Repentance and the Fox Revelation

Tokoyohiko and the eight hundred and eighty-eight divine ministers, having opposed the will of the Supreme Deity and disregarded cosmic law by convening and attending this assembly, incurred divine wrath and suffered the unexpected humiliation of the muddy field. Stricken with remorse, the divine ministers realized that before holding the council, they must first enshrine Ame-no-Minakanushi-no-Okami (also known as Okunitokotachi-no-Mikoto), the Primordial Great Spirit of Heaven and Earth, offering rare delicacies of mountain, field, river, and sea, and convene their assembly in sincerity and truth under divine protection.

Within the cosmic laws of Heaven and Earth are five inner precepts: 'Reflect. Feel shame. Stand in awe. Repent. Deeply comprehend.' They realized they must strictly observe these commandments, recognizing that this trial was truly the profound and wondrous whip of grace wielded by the merciful Supreme Deity.

Deeply repenting their past errors, the deities placed their hands upon their chests in profound self-reflection, feeling acute shame for their shallow intellect and ignorance, standing in awe of the inviolable majesty of the Sovereign Deity of Heaven and Earth, and naturally comprehending the great truth that evil cannot overcome righteousness.

In truth, Hachio-Okami had harbored a grand scheme: to overrun the sacred land of Jerusalem (the Earthly Takamanohara), gradually force the retirement of the Creator, and usurp the sovereign authority of Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto by setting up Banko-Daijin as a temporary ruler until he could push him aside and establish his own perpetual theocracy. Yet through this humiliating failure, Tokoyohiko returned to his original conscience, and together with Tokoyohime, gave birth to genuine repentance. Though abandoning his scheme to overthrow the Creator, he still secretly aspired to serve as prime minister under Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto.

Furthermore, the two maidens, Kasugahime and Yashimahime, who had mounted the dais during the second session to pour forth persuasive rhetoric, were in truth two white foxes named Takakura and Asahi! Transformed into maidens through the brilliant strategy of Omichiwake, they had executed a masterly counter-espionage deception to shatter the insidious ambitions of the wicked deities. The future deeds of these white foxes shall truly be a fascinating spectacle to behold.

天地の律法には、「省みよ。恥ぢよ。畏れよ。悔い改めよ。克く覚れよ」との五箇条の内面的戒律があり、これを的確に遵守せざるべからざることを自覚した。これぞ全く大慈大悲の大神の、甚深微妙なる恩恵の鞭であった。

諸神人はここに翻然として前非を悔い改め、我が心胸に手を当てて反省し、各自の思慮の浅薄にして無知なりしを恥じ、天地主宰の大神の威厳の犯すべからざるを畏み、邪は正に敵し難き大真理をおのずから悟り得たのである。

思えば八王大神は、地の高天原なるエルサレムの聖地を蹂躙し、あわよくば漸進的に国祖の大神までも退去せしめ、自ら国治立命の職権を奪わんとする方法手段として、盤古大神を擁立して時を待って盤古大神を押し込め、万古不易的に八王大神の神政を樹立せんことを企てていたのであるが、今回の失敗に八王大神・常世彦は本心に立ち返り、常世姫もまた夫とともに「悔い改め」の心を起こした。ここに八王大神は、国祖の地位を奪わんとする大陰謀のみは断念したものの、国祖を奉じて自ら聖地の宰相神たらんとする目的のみは夢寐にも忘れざるものであった。

第二回の議席に現れ、侃々諤々の雄弁を振るい、満座の神人をして舌を巻かしめた春日姫と八島姫の二女性は、その実は白狐の高倉（たかくら）と旭（あさひ）であった。二女に化けた白狐は、大道別（おおみちわけ）の周到なる妙策に出でたものであり、邪神の野望を破壊せんとするための反間苦肉の神策にして、敵本主義の謀略に出でたものであった。この白狐の今後の行動こそ、実に面白き見ものとなるであろう。

§ 2
§ 2: The Sacred Altar and the Transformed Countenances

Preparing to convene the third session of the council, the deities first erected a solemn altar in the great hall of Tokoyo Castle. Deciding by unanimous council to offer sacred delicacies of sea, river, mountain, and field, they appointed Miyabihiko as chief ritualist and Miyamahiko alongside others as assisting priests, celebrating the sacred rites with great solemnity. At that moment, the firmament cleared without a single speck of cloud, a warm breeze blew gently across the earth, birds sang melodiously upon the branches, and an indescribable fragrance enveloped the surroundings, presenting a tranquil spring scene as if the divine reign of Miroku were already mirrored upon earth.

The gloomy faces of the deities—which had hitherto resembled sour pumpkins, turnips, gourds, eggplants, melons, and wild yams with crude features—were filled with courage and hope, blooming with radiant cheer. From the depths of their souls, the deities tasted boundless delight and joy.

As declared in the Omoto Divine Oracle: 'According to the posture of one's heart, the very countenance of the face is transformed; therefore the disposition of the heart is of paramount importance'—this is truly a supreme maxim.

現代日本語訳

§ 3
§ 3: Tokoyohiko's Solemn Confession and Celestial Harmony

The third session of the council opened in solemn serenity amidst joyous celebration. The divine ministers took their assigned seats—this time no longer in an open mud field, but within the authentic great hall of Tokoyo Castle. Remembering the previous day's humiliation, some deities stamped their feet carefully upon the floor, while others reached down to stroke the floorboards to test whether the hall was genuine. Some pinched themselves to feel pain, sighing with relief, whispering: 'It seems this time is indeed the real council chamber!'

The proverb 'A scalded cat fears cold water' must refer to such circumstances as this. As written in the Divine Oracle: 'The opening of the true national assembly can never be accomplished by mortals, however long they labor. Unless God opens it, it cannot open. God shall open it and show it forth; therefore, repent!' This is indeed an eternal, unshakeable truth. When even the ancient deities brought about such disaster without the sanction of the Creator, how much more so must it be with councils convened by mortals steeped in iniquity, where flesh rules over spirit! It is all the more impossible.

Approaching before Kami, Tokoyohiko prostrated in reverence and chanted the divine liturgy. Ascending the central dais with dignified solemnity, he surveyed the assembly and spoke:

'In our haste for success, we forgot to pray unto Kami and seek divine assistance in deliberating our sacred proposals; thus provoking divine wrath, our council collapsed into chaos, and we tasted the bitter humiliation of utter disaster. Repenting our past folly, we must henceforth join together in sincerity and truth for world peace! The previous two sessions closed in turmoil without achieving a single decision because they failed to align with divine will. Now let us inaugurate this sacred council afresh under divine guidance!' Thunderous applause erupted from the deities, welcoming the declaration of Hachio-Okami.

At that moment, exquisite celestial music echoed from above, and heavenly maidens danced with fluttering robes in the gentle spring breeze. The savage roars of tigers, malicious foxes, and raging lions, alongside the menacing thunder of Heavenly Bird-Boats, were completely silenced, replaced by boundless peace and heavenly harmony.

現代日本語訳

羹（あつもの）に懲りて鱠（なます）を吹くという譬えは、このような時のことを指したものであろう。神諭に、『国会開きは人民が何時まで掛かっても開けは致さぬぞよ。神が開かな開けぬぞよ。神が開いて見せようぞよ。改心なされ』とあるのは、実に千古不易の至言である。太古の神人でさえも、国祖の御許しなくしては、このような失敗を演じるものであるのに、ましてや罪悪の淵に沈んだ、体主霊従の人間の開く会議においてをや。なおさらのことというべきである。

常世彦は、まず神前に進み、恭しく拝跪して神言を奏上し、静かに中央の高座に登り、謹厳の態度にて諸神人席に眼を配りて言った。

「我らは成功を急ぐのあまり、神に祈願したてまつり、神助の下に神聖なる議案を討究することを忘却したるがために大神の神怒に触れ、議場はたちまち混乱に混乱の惨状を現出し、四離滅裂の苦き経験を嘗めたのである。今より我らは諸神人とともに、悔悟して世界平和のため誠心誠意をもって終始せざるべからず。今日までの二回の会議は怪事頻々として湧き起こり、一つも決定に至らずして幕を閉じた。これ全く神慮に叶わざるがための結果に外ならざれば、今より改めて神聖なる会議を神助の下に開かん！」と宣示した。諸神人は拍手して八王大神の宣示を迎えたのである。

この時、天井には微妙の音楽が聞こえ、天男天女は天の羽衣を春風に靡かせながら舞い遊び、以前のすさまじき猛虎、悪狐、獅子の咆哮や怒号の悪声、天の鳥船の轟き渡る示威的光景に比べれば、天地霄壤の差があることを覚えしめたのであった。
