Limitation of Armaments

第二八章　武器制限〔178〕

§ 1
§ 1: The Spiritual Anatomy of Biological Weapons in the Divine Age

The limitation of armaments among divine beings in the Divine Age was not a superficial, half-hearted measure like certain modern worldly conferences that merely restrict the tonnage of battleships and submarines; rather, it involved the actual removal of a portion, or the entirety, of the living celestial armaments attached to the physical bodies of divine beings. Primordial dragons possessed formidable sword-like armored hides and countless sharp, blade-like horns upon their heads, using them both to display fierce aggression against foes and to protect their own bodies as essential defensive weapons during continuous warfare. Therefore, the abolition of individual weapons proposed by Hachiō-Ōkami at the Tokoyo conference was an undertaking of supreme significance for the divine realm. Dragon gods were restricted to having only two sharp horns, with all extra horns extracted without exception; their imposing sword-skin armor was mercilessly peeled away, transforming into soft, supple scales. Among them were dragons whose horns were entirely removed, leaving them without any defensive armaments, just like the snakes of today.

Furthermore, primordial beasts such as fierce tigers, lions, giant wolves, and great bears possessed not only razor-sharp fangs and claws, but also sturdy celestial wings capable of free flight through the sky, and coats of fur stiff and sharp as steel needles, making them virtually invulnerable in both offense and defense. When partial disarmament was enacted, the divine soldiers of these beasts had their sharp needle-like bristles plucked out without mercy, and their natural wings—so convenient for flight—were ruthlessly severed.

In addition, wicked foxes such as the golden nine-tailed white-faced fox, as well as silver-furred and iron-furred fox spirits, had their sharp, stiff needle-fur completely removed, allowing only soft fur to grow in its place. In compensation for this loss, they were granted intellectual wisdom and cunning on par with divine beings, and assigned the duty of serving loyally as retainers of the divine realm. However, there are distinctions between good and evil among fox spirits: righteous foxes serve the divine realm as white foxes (Byakko), an unbroken tradition from primordial times to the present. Although many people in the world call fox spirits 'Inari-no-Ōkami,' the word 'Inari' originates from 'Iinari' (sustenance / grain production), being the sacred divine name of the God who presides over the fundamental clothing and food of humanity.

また金毛九尾白面の悪狐をはじめ、銀毛や鉄毛の狐神などは、その鋭利にして固き針毛を全部脱却させられ、軟弱なる毛を生ずるのみに改められた。その代償として智慧の力を神人に劣らぬほどに授けられ、神界の眷属として忠実に奉仕する役目を定められた。しかし狐神にも善悪正邪の別があり、善良なる狐神は白狐として神界の御用を勤めているのは、太古より今に至るまで変わらぬ真実である。世には狐神を稲荷大神と称える者が数多くあるが、稲荷とは「飯成（いいなり）」の意義であり、人間の衣食住の根元を司り給う尊き神の御名なのである。

§ 2
§ 2: The True Sacred Mission of the Inari Fox Deities

Toyoukehime-no-Kami, Toyukegami, Miketsukami, Ukanomitama-no-Kami, Ukemochi-no-Kami, and Ōgetsuhime-no-Kami are all one and the same divine entity—the supreme, benevolent Ancestral Deity who foremost assisted the cosmic mission of the Heavenly Ancestor and whose boundless benevolence nourishes every single human soul under heaven.

In truth, the fox spirits were originally holy messengers of this great deity, messengers of distinguished merit who held the seeds of the five cereals in their mouths, carried them across the continents, and sowed them across the plains of every country to instruct mankind in agriculture. As civilization gradually developed and the seeds of the five grains spread to every corner of the earth, their agricultural duties came to an end. Thereupon, the Great Deity bestowed upon these foxes superior intellectual wisdom, naming them 'Byakko' (white fox), and commissioned them to investigate events occurring throughout the world in minute detail and report them clearly to the divine beings. Therefore, one must know that 'Byakko' signifies a fox that reports (shiroshi-ageru) all earthly affairs unto the gods, and by no means refers to the physical color of its fur! Even wild foxes and evil foxes naturally turn white as they grow old, just as human hair turns white in old age regardless of noble or humble rank. Hence, regardless of whether its fur is brown, red, or black, any fox that faithfully serves the divine realm is called a Byakko.

§ 3
§ 3: The Evolution of Dragons into Humanity and the Cosmic Path

Moreover, fierce birds that soared through the air possessed not only magnificent wings, but also concealed deadly venom sacs beneath their throats, capable of spewing lethal poison to dissolve the flesh and bones of adversaries; these venom sacs were also completely removed as a result of this conference, enabling divine beings and animals on earth to pass their days in true peace and security. Likewise, fish and sea serpents living in the ocean previously possessed sharp, needle-like bristles covering their bodies like sawfish or hedgehogs for attack and defense; these needle-bristles were also entirely removed, leaving behind only fins, scales, and teeth.

In this manner, the dragon races whose armaments were removed gradually evolved through successive generations, eventually being reborn as human beings, or ascending to become sacred gods. Similarly, lions, tigers, leopards, bears, and wolves evolved with the passage of the ages, transforming into humans, horses, cattle, dogs, and cats. Among them, kings of beasts like lions, tigers, and leopards whose souls advanced toward goodness were reincarnated as humans. For this reason, certain human beings and races still retain physical and facial traces of lions, tigers, or leopards to this day. The disposition of such people often inherits the wild spiritual traits of antiquity, being emotionally cold, indulging solely in sensory appetites for food and lust, and acting in a manner where physical desires dominate spiritual truth (taitō-reijū).

When Onisaburō expounds such theories of spiritual evolution, some individuals may become offended and complain that he insults mankind. However, Onisaburō is uneducated and has never studied anthropology; he merely records what he witnessed in the spirit realm while in a state of spiritual inspiration during his ascetic practice of Chinkon Kishin (Spirit Quieting and Divine Return) in the sacred cave of Mount Takakuma, guided by divine messenger spirits. As to whether these revelations are true or false, he has no worldly answer to give.

かくして生体武装を除去された竜族は、世代を経て漸次に進化し、人間として生まれ変わり、あるいは清浄なる神へと昇華していった。また獅子、虎、豹、熊、狼なども世とともに進化して人間や牛馬、犬猫などに生まれ変わったのである。百獣の王たる獅子や虎豹の中でその身魂が善進したものは人間へと変化した。ゆえにある人種のごときは、その容貌に今なお獅子や虎、豹などの面影を留めているのである。かかる性質は太古の獣性が遺伝し、人情が冷淡で色食の欲に耽り、体主霊従の行動をとる者が多いのである。

王仁がかくのごとき神霊進化の真実の説をなす時、人間を獣の末裔と侮辱したと怒る人士もあるかも知れぬ。しかし王仁は元来無学で人類学などを研究したこともなく、ただ高熊山の神山に使神に導かれ、鎮魂帰神の修業の折に霊感者となり、神界探険の際に霊界で見聞した真実をそのまま語るものゆえ、世俗の虚実の議論には答えようがないのである。
