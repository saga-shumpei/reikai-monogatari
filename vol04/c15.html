Chapter 15: The Izumo Dance [165]

出雲舞〔165〕

§ 1

The weather was serene and clear, without a single speck of cloud in the whole azure sky; the east wind gently caressed the earth, the Rocky Mountains soared high beyond the clouds in fresh raiment, the plants and trees over all the mountains and fields vied in beauty, their branches and leaves brought forth flowers of yellow and crimson, white and purple, red and black, and blue and scarlet, and their sweet fragrance was rich and abundant, just like the garden of Eden. Myriads of birds fluttered their wings gently as they soared, butterflies fluttered across the fields in all four quarters seeking blossoms, the flickering of the heat haze swayed leisurely in the spring breeze, and in this tranquil spring scene of Tokoyo, where gods and men alike were filled with joyful countenances, heaven, earth, gods, and men were brought spontaneously to sing and dance. In the grand hall of Tokoyo Castle, which towered loftily against such an agreeable sky, Hachiō-ōkami, reflecting on the previous day's failure, along with the various Hachiō, Hachitō, and Kunitama of each mountain and region, took their respective council seats. With a smile on his gentle face as usual, Hachiō-ōkami, accompanied by Tokoyo-hime, Kasuga-hime, and Yashima-hime on the central elevated dais, mounted the platform in solemn dignity. It was Izumo-hime who requested to mount the platform from the lowest seat. Izumo-hime praised the tranquil spring of Tokoyo and said:

"This is entirely brought about by the abundant virtue of Hachiō-ōkami; the color of today's sky is unlike that of yesterday, and not a cloud of worry hangs over the heart. Ah, is this heaven, the pure land, or paradise? Ah, I shall offer celebrations for such an auspicious and precious omen,"

and, clapping her hands, she began to sing in a delightful cadence. Her song ran:

"The divine age of supreme beauty and supreme joy, even though thousands, tens of thousands, or myriad aeons of days and months pass by, doth not change; though the divine governance shall never falter for all eternity so long as heaven and earth endure, the transient world shifts like the seven turns of the hydrangea; even in this present world, utterly altered, there was once in ancient times such a world of auspicious omens and supreme felicities. O Tokoyohiko of the land of Tokoyo, O Tokoyo-hime, who are ye? Though ye are the scions of the exalted deity, the sky of your hearts is completely clouded over; bewitched by the evil spirits of the eight-headed, eight-tailed serpents born of the souls of Tarachine and Eba, and by the evil fox of Tokoyo with golden fur, nine tails, and a white face, persistently, to achieve the purpose of placing the body over the spirit, ye trampled the sacred land and the sacred castle; ending in discord like the crossed beaks of the crossbill, ye fled back helplessly to Tokoyo, and exerting tyrannical force, ye summoned and gathered into the castle of Tokoyo with sweet words the Hachiō who guard the mountain ridges in all directions, the Kunitamatsukasa, and the various Kamitsukasa; the number of Hachiō, Hachitō, Kunitama, and other gods and men assembled who utter lies is more than eight hundred pillars; their heads glisten and gleam, and even in the blink of intensely flashing lightning, the gods assemble in the castle of Tokoyo on this long day; the chief aim of their gathering and deliberating is that Hachiō-ōkami Tokoyohiko has troubled his heart in a thousand ways to fulfill his longstanding deceitful plot; has it borne fruit? How grateful! The Tokoyo Council shall eternally become the vanguard of the destruction of peace and spread abroad; even if there were a day when the roaring of thunder resounds through the skies of the four quarters, and down to the bottom of the ocean, draining completely the sea of Naruto and Seto where the sound of a cracked bell peals forth, this council of the land of Tokoyo shall inevitably become a storm among the pines. Amidst the storm of the pines and the sound of waves, the world in which one prays to the deities of heaven and earth for salvation with all one's voice is, alas, still far off in the eternal darkness; the Tokoyo Council is utterly unreliable. In an unreliable world, the one who serves as a staff and as strength is none other than Kunitokotachi-no-Kami, the ancestor of the land who formed and solidified heaven and earth. Though the deity is enshrined in the council hall, without permission, it is an empty shrine; behold, behold, the contrivances of the Tokoyo deities shall presently be overturned from their very foundation. Having ignored, by sheer force, the mediation of Hiromunehiko, the Archangel who guards the holy ground of Jerusalem, the retribution is severe and right before your eyes; falling down stricken by the divine power of Kunitokotachi-no-Kami, ye are bound to repeat the folly of the god-men Yasomagahiko, Katoribewake, and Hiroyoribewake. O Hachiō-ōkami, cleanse the heart within thy breast before the deity; cleanse thy heart before the deity."

Izumo-hime, possessing beautiful features and a kind of majestic dignity, gracefully danced with her long sleeves and sang upon the platform before the watchful eyes of the gathered divine functionaries.

現代日本語訳

「これこそ全く八王大神の盛徳の致すところであり、昨日に変わる今日の空の色、心にかかる群雲もありません。ああ、これは天国でしょうか、浄土でしょうか、それとも楽園でしょうか。ああ、このような尊い瑞祥を私は祝い申し上げましょう」

と、手を打って節面白く歌い出した。その歌は、

「至美と至楽の神の世は、 百千万歳、億兆の 月日を経ても変わらない。 神の経綸は天地の ある限りは永遠に 動かないけれども、現世の 世は紫陽花の七変化のように変わり、 すっかり変わり果てた今の世も、 昔に一度はこの通りの めでたく慶ばしい世であった。 常世の国の常世彦よ、 常世姫よ、何者であるのか。 尊い神の後裔であるが、 心の空はすっかり曇り、 天足と胞場の御魂から 生まれ出た八頭 八尾の大蛇の邪霊や、 金毛九尾白面の 常世の悪狐に魅入られて、 体主霊従の目的を 達成しようと執拗に 聖地と聖城を踏みにじり、 イスカの嘴のように食い違い、 やむを得ず常世へ逃げ帰り、 暴威を振るって四方の山の 尾根の上に守る八王や、 国魂司や諸神司を 常世の城に甘言を もって誘い招き集め、 集まる八王、八頭、 国魂、その他の神人の数、 嘘は八百余柱の 頭の光がピカピカと 激しく輝く稲妻が またたく間も、長い日の 常世の城に神々が集い、 集まって議する眼目は、 八王大神常世彦が 長年の奸計を達成しようと さまざまに心を砕いた、 その甲斐があったのか、ありがたいことだ。 常世会議は永遠に 和親を破壊する先駆けと 成り果てて、雷が 轟くばかりに四方の空、 海の底まで破れ鐘の 響く鳴門や瀬戸の海を 返して干す世はあるとしても、 常世の国のこの会議は 松の嵐となるにちがいない。 松の嵐や波の音、 声を限りに天地の 神に救いを祈る世は、 ああ、まだ遠い常闇の、 常世会議こそ頼りがない。 頼りなき世に杖となり、 力となるのは天地を 造り固めた国の祖、 国治立の神のみである。 神を議場に祀ってはいても、 許しがなければ空の宮である。 常世の神のからくりは、 見よ、見よ、今に根底から 覆されるであろう。エルサレムの 聖地を守る天使長、 広宗彦の執り成しを、 威力に任せて無視した 報いは厳しく目の当たりに、 国治立の神力に 打たれて倒れる神人たる 八十枉彦や蚊取別、 広依別の二の舞である。 八王大神よ、胸中の 心を洗え、神の前で。 心を洗え、神の前で。」

容姿端麗にして一種の威厳を備えた出雲姫は、多くの神司が見守る壇上で長い袖をしとやかに舞い歌った。
