§ 1

To Tokoyohiko no Mikoto, the Archangel of the Holy Ground of Jerusalem, Takatsukihiko was born and gradually grew up, assisting his father with the greatest of meritorious deeds, and the Archangel's renown resounded throughout the realm like thunder, with none failing to praise his good governance, so that for a time it truly became an auspicious age of great peace throughout the land.

However, carelessness is a formidable enemy, and if there be even the slightest opening, the spirits of evil deities that fill the universe will instantly strike, inflicting harm upon the soul or upon the body, or defiling and clouding one's conscience, and ultimately targeting that person to use their body and soul as a vessel, arousing an evil mind and causing them to carry out evil deeds.

In the Ōmoto Shin'yu also:

"Because devils are constantly targeting the body and soul of human beings, unless one maintains the state of mind of standing amidst drawn swords, one knows not when their body and soul will be made into a plaything at will by devils. Therefore, humans must return to the mind of God, have faith in God, and never be careless in the slightest."

When Tokoyohiko no Mikoto grew somewhat at ease in the peace of the divine realm and held a boat-banquet on the Ryūgū Sea with numerous attendants, the evil spirit of the great serpent with eight heads and eight tails, which had been lurking deep at the bottom of the Ryūgū Sea biding its time, revealed its true form before the deities and humans as if to say 'now is the time,' deliberately transformed into Takatsukihiko before the deities and humans, entered the residence of Tokoyohiko no Mikoto, and troubled the deities and humans.

Although Tokoyohiko no Mikoto and the deities and humans of the Holy Ground all fully knew that one of the two Takatsukihiko was a transformation of an evil deity, because they were unable to discern which was true and which was false, they had no choice but to let the two of identical appearance dwell in the residence. "Having been tempted by an evil deity out of a slight desire of the heart, I eventually built a fortress in Tokoyo-no-kuni, and though I afflicted and troubled the deities, including the awe-inspiring Ancestral Great Deity and successive archangels, the deeply benevolent Ancestral Deity praised our repentance and appointed me to the unworthy position of regent of the Holy Ground; thus, in order to repay the great favor of our rebirth, we strictly observed the divine laws in all sincerity, strove in the divine administration, and served the Ancestral Great Deity; yet, perhaps because my heart unconsciously slackened, just as I floated a boat upon the Ryūgū Sea to make merry, an evil deity appeared from the bottom of the sea, took the form of my beloved child, boldly came to live in my residence, and I was unable to discern the true from the false; from then on, wishing by all means to know the truth, I concentrated my prayers day and night to Amatsu Ōkami and the Ancestral Great Deity, yet the retribution for the sin I once committed arrived, my mind and soul darkened, I lost the spiritual power of divine clairvoyance, and furthermore, from that time I began to feel pain in various parts of my body, and that I have now fallen into such a critical condition is also retribution for my deep transgressions; therefore, seeing the miserable end of my person, repent as soon as possible, and do not arouse even the slightest evil mind or wrongdoing."

Having left this last will, he passed away as if falling asleep. It is said that when a bird is about to die, its cry is mournful; when a man is about to die, his words are good. Indeed, how true it is; even Tokoyohiko no Mikoto, who had once exercised such violent authority, reflected from his true heart, felt ashamed of that evil mind, repented of his wrongdoing, feared the divine constitution as solemn and inviolable, realized the principle of life and death, coming and going, and cause and effect, which is the Great Way of Heaven and Earth, his body and soul became completely purified, and under divine assistance, he passed away as if sleeping peacefully. Ah, what is to be feared is solely the attitude of one's heart.

From the time Tokoyohiko no Mikoto ascended to heaven, the deities of the Holy Ground dispatched express messengers in all four directions and issued reports to the Eight Kings of each mountain and region, as well as to the general guardian officials; whereupon the eight million deities, beginning with Mount Manju, were astonished at this disastrous news, vied with one another to swarm to the Holy Ground, and while mourning his ascension, ardently desired that a successor be determined as soon as possible, assembling here in the Great Hall of Jerusalem Castle. As they were about to select Takatsukihiko, the eldest son of Tokoyohiko no Mikoto, as Archangel and petition for the approval of the Ancestral Great Deity, just as was widely rumored throughout the realm, two Takatsukihikos appeared.

The various divine officials struggled to discern the true from the false, and though they continued the conference in the Great Hall for seven days and seven nights, because the transformation was so thorough that it was impossible to distinguish between before and after, or true and false, they could not install either of the two archangels, true or false, and the deities wandered in a dense fog, troubled by this strange fact.

Takatsukihiko appeared in the Great Hall, recounted the various strange events of the Holy Ground from the apparition of the evil deity serpent lurking in the Ryūgū Sea down to the ascension of his father, and said,

"There is a matter that will prove that the one accompanying me like a shadow is a transformation of that great serpent. May the deities realize the truth by this. I possess an amulet bag bestowed upon me by my father; behold this."

As he pressed him thus, the evil deity instantly changed color, making no reply and uttering not a word. In the very moment he was about to bite the true Takatsukihiko, the divine words "Kannagara tamachihaemase" suddenly and spontaneously burst forth from his mouth, whereupon the false deity immediately revealed his true form through the majesty and virtue of those divine words:

"Ah, how deeply regrettable, how vexing! I shall await another opportunity to avenge this grudge!"

No sooner had he spoken than he appeared as a terrifying eight-headed, eight-tailed giant serpent, stirred up gathering clouds, flew through the heavens, and plunged his monstrous form far into the western sky. Takatsukihiko suddenly stood up, and said:

"The various divine officials have witnessed with their own eyes the demeanor of the evil deity just now, and have surely discerned the true from the false. I am indeed Takatsukihiko, the eldest son of the Archangel Tokoyohiko no Mikoto. Henceforth, concerning the theocracy of the holy ground, I hope that all divine officials will unite in cooperation and assist me."

Scarcely had he finished delivering this polite greeting when he was suddenly seized with chills and trembling, his complexion abruptly turned pale, and clutching his belly, he cried out in agony. The divine officials, taken by surprise, supported the Mikoto, conveyed him to his residence, and had attendants nurse him attentively.

This amulet pouch was a contrivance suddenly conceived through the scheme of his younger sister, Satsukihime, having sprung from a desperate measure to reveal the true identity of the evil deity. Playing such political tricks and deceits is something that divine persons must most strictly refrain from.

Moreover, Takatsukihiko's sudden falling ill was not an authentic sickness; rather, taking advantage of the vulnerability in his heart—namely, the Mikoto's relief and a slight pride in his divine virtue—the evil spirit of the eight-headed, eight-tailed giant serpent that had hidden itself in the western sky possessed his physical body with a speed that left not even room for a hair's breadth.

『アヽ残念至極口惜さよ。我は永年この聖地を根底より顛覆せむと、海底に沈みて時を待ち、つひに高月彦と変化し、聖地の攪乱に全力を尽したりしに、高月彦の神言によりてその化けの皮を脱がれたれば、いまは是非なし、ふたたび時節を待つてこの怨みを報ぜむ』

と言ふよと見るまに、見るも恐ろしき八頭八尾の大蛇と現はれ叢雲をよびおこし天空をかけりて、遠くその怪姿を西天に没したりけり。高月彦は忽然として立ちあがり、

『諸神司はただいまの邪神の様子を実見して、その真偽を悟りたまひしならむ、吾こそは天使長常世彦命の長子高月彦なり。今後聖地の神政については、諸神司の協力一致して御輔翼あらむことを希望す』

と慇懃に挨拶を述べ終るや否や、たちまち悪寒震慄、顔色急に青ざめ、腹をかかへて苦悶の声を放ちければ、諸神司は驚きて命を扶けその居館に送り、侍者をして叮嚀に看護せしめたり。

この守袋は妹五月姫の計らひにて、俄に思ひつきたるカラクリにして、邪神の正体を現はすための窮策に出たるものなりける。かくのごとき権謀術数を弄するは、神人としてもつとも慎まざるべからざることなり。

また高月彦の急病を発したるは、真正の病気ではなく、命の安心とややその神徳にほこる心の隙に乗じて、西天に姿を隠したる八頭八尾の大蛇の邪霊が、間髪を容るるの暇なきまで速く、その肉体に憑依したる結果なりける。
