The Universe of Divine Revelation (Part 3)

神示の宇宙　その三〔198〕

§ 1

Regarding my statement in the previous session that the sun is a dark body whose substance possesses no radiance whatever, and that the earth is a luminous body, doubts arose immediately one after another; therefore, to be certain, I shall here once more explain the relationship between fire and water. However, being originally unlearned, having continued a life deep in the grassy mountains, and furthermore having been strictly forbidden by divine command from researching new academic studies since the Meiji era, having spent twenty-four years in the circumstance of a village hermit, I have no idea at all to what point the research of today's academic world has advanced. In a world of rapid progress, since these words are uttered from the mouth of one who has been cut off from the reading world for twenty-four years, falling behind the times is entirely natural, whoever may consider it. Academic progress in the world has been rapid, just like when Urashimako in the old tale returned from the Dragon Palace; if I were to see today the new astronomy, physical geography, and other fields of study, I think I would surely be seized with a sense of wonder. But standing outside the sphere of today's science, I merely tell an experiential tale just as divinely revealed.

"Conforming to the divine, placing my body outside the void, / day and night I tell tales that never cease"—this will not only utterly fail to enter the ears of scholars touched by the atmosphere of modern civilization, but may also be regarded as a kind of megalomania; nevertheless, believing the words of Holy Christ that "God does not reveal Himself to the wise and the strong, but reveals the truth unto the foolish and the weak," it seems to me that the true God may have revealed the truth of the universe to such a foolish and weak person as myself.

All water is white, and is the element of light. In the center of water, there is a dot, which causes the water to flow freely. If this dot escapes from the center of the water, it solidifies firmly into ice and does not flow in the least. Therefore, that from which the dot has escaped from water is called kōri, or called hyō. When there is no water in its center, fire likewise cannot burn and shine. In short, what moves water is fire, and what moves fire is water. Therefore, an object that contains not a single shred of moisture will not burn under any circumstances.

The sun likewise has water appropriately injected into its center from the water sphere, burning in the sky and radiating light; and the earth likewise regulates the natural light of icebergs and water from the fireball within the ground, appropriately emitting its own light.

Next, the reason why the movement of the various stars appears from the earth to have great differences in speed is that, due to the differences in distance and altitude in the positions of the stars as seen from the earth, they appear to move rapidly on the one hand and appear to move slowly on the other. But generally, stars near the earth and low appear fast, and stars far and high appear slow in their movement.

現代日本語訳

私は前の席において、太陽は暗体であって、その実質は少しも光輝を持たないと言い、また地球は光体であると言ったことについて、早速疑問が続出しましたから、念のためにここに改めて火と水との関係を解説しておきます。しかし元来の無学者で、草深い山奥の生活を続け、かつ神界からの厳命で、明治以後の新しい学問を研究することを禁じられ、あたかも里の仙人の境遇に二十四年間を費やしたものでありますから、今日の学界の研究が何の点まで進んでいるかということは、私には全然見当がつきません。日進月歩の世の中において、二十四年間読書界と絶縁していた者の口から吐き出すのですから、時勢に遅れるのは誰が考えても当然のことであります。昔話にある、浦島子が竜宮から帰ってきた時のように世の中の学界の進歩は急速であって、私が今日新たな天文、地文、その他の学問を見ましたならば、さぞ驚異の念に駆られるであろうと思います。しかし私としては今日の科学の圏外に立ち、神示のままの実験的物語をするだけです。

『神ながら虚空の外に身をおきて日に夜に尽きぬ物語する』――現代文明の空気に触れた学者の耳には到底入らないのみならず、一種の誇大妄想狂と見られるかもしれませんが、しかし『神は賢き者、強き者に現さずして、愚かなる者、弱き者に誠を現し給う』という聖キリストの言葉を信じ、愚弱なる私に真の神は、宇宙の真理を開示されたのではなかろうかとも思われるのであります。

およそ水は白いものであって、光の元素である。水の中心には、一つの「ゝ」があって、水を自由に流動させる。もしこの「ゝ」が水の中心から脱出した時は固く凝って氷となり、少しも流動しない。ゆえに水から「ゝ」の脱出したのを、氷（こおり）といい、または氷（ひょう）という。火もまたその中心に水がない時は、火は燃え、かつ光ることはできない。要するに水を動かすものは火であり、火を動かすものは水である。ゆえに、一片の水気も含まない物体は、どうしても燃えない。

太陽もその中心に、水球より水を適度に注入して、天空に燃えて光を放射し、大地はまた、氷山や水の自然の光を地中の火球より調節して、その自体の光を適度に発射している。

次に諸星の運行に、大変な遅速があるように地上から見えるのは、地上より見て星の位置に、遠近、高低の差があることにより、一方には急速に運行するように見え、一方には遅く運行するように見えるのである。だが、概して大地に近く、低い星は速く見え、遠く高い星はその運行が遅いように見える。

原文（近代文語）

『神ながら虚空の外に身をおきて日に夜に月ぬものがたりする』現代文明の空気に触れた学者の耳には到底這入らないのみならず、一種の誇大妄想狂と見らるるかも知れませぬ、然れど『神は賢きもの、強きものにあらはさずして、愚なるもの、弱きものに誠をあらはし玉ふ』と言へる聖キリストの言を信じ、愚弱なる私に真の神は、宇宙の真理を開示されたのでは無からうかとも思はれるのであります。

凡て水は白いものであつて、光の元素である。水の中心には、一つのゝがあつて、水を自由に流動させる。若しこのゝが水の中心から脱出した時は固く凝つて氷となり、少しも流動せない。故に水からゝの脱出したのを、氷と云ひ、又は、氷と云ふ。火もまたその中心に水なき時は、火は燃え、且つ光る事は出来ぬ。要するに水を動かすものは火であり、火を動かすものは水である。故に、一片の水気も含まぬ物体は、どうしても燃えない。

太陽もその中心に、水球より水を適度に注入して、天空に燃えて光を放射し、大地はまた、氷山や水の自然の光を地中の火球より調節して、その自体の光を適度に発射して居る。

次に諸星の運行に、大変な遅速のある様に地上から見えるのは、地上より見て星の位置に、遠近、高低の差あるより、一方には急速に運行する如く見え、一方には遅く運行する様に見えるのである。が、概して大地に近く、低き星は速く見え、遠く高き星はその運行が遅い様に見える。

§ 2

For example, this is the same principle as when, while a train is in motion, one opens the car window and gazes at the near and distant mountains: the mountains located nearby appear to run rapidly in the direction opposite to the train, while the mountains located in the distance appear to remain motionless, or, even if running in that opposite direction, appear to run extremely slowly.

As mentioned above, the Moon (Taiin) revolves along a fixed orbit midway between the sun and the great earth, maintaining its position in mid-air through the gravitational attraction of the Triangle Stars, the Three Stars, the Pleiades, and the Big Dipper. Between the moon and these stars, with the moon at the center, maintenance is kept through a kind of subtle spiritual cord, just like a sympathetic nervous system.

The relationship between the sun and the various stars of the sky is likewise maintained through a kind of subtle spiritual cord, like a sympathetic nervous system with the sun at the center; and through the moderate regulation of the eight great divine powers of movement, stillness, dissolution, condensation, attraction, relaxation, combination, and division, while remaining stable in the same position, it eternally continues minor automatic inclination and major automatic inclination, carrying on the respiratory function of the sun itself.

That the great earth also causes the Earth at its center to maintain contact with the various tide-spheres, carrying on respiratory function through the regulation of fire and water, is the same as with the sun. With the Earth at the center, the various tide-spheres within the ground maintain the stability of the Earth through subtle spiritual cords like a sympathetic nervous system.

Furthermore, the meaning of referring to the Earth's surface as the north pole of the great earth is that kita means, as previously stated, 'fire and water descending'; and as shown in Figure 6 (see illustration), it means the center where the water and fire of the sun, the water and fire of the center of the great earth, the water and fire of the icebergs in the four directions on the great earth, and the water and fire of the moon have descended.

That human beings are born on the land of the Earth and act is called ikita (fixing of water and fire). Therefore, the Earth is a place of peaceful habitation for living beings and a field of activity and divine administration. Also, that water and fire, that is, spirit and physical body separate and so-called death occurs, being called mikaru (withering and stopping of the body; fixing of the withering of water), signifies the time when the regulation of fire and water is broken. However, when viewed from the perspective of the spirit, there is no birth, no death, and no distinction between old and young, but living through for all eternity; and viewed from the spirit, that is, our primary guardian deity, it is merely changing its container.
