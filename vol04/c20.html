The Attitude of the Elder

第二〇章　長者の態度〔170〕

§ 1
§ 1: Tokoyohiko's Dignified Bearing and the Peril of Hidden Desire

Stirred by Mori-Takahiko's blistering denunciation and Sarutahime's flawless, water-tight song of Kotodama, the assembled divine ministers began to return to their true conscience, awakening anew to the awe of divine majesty and the inviolability of cosmic law. Telepathically sharing doubts regarding the real intentions of Hachiō-Ōkami, a quiet wave of unease rippled across the assembly hall.

Hachiō-Ōkami Tokoyohiko possessed long hair cascading down his back; he was tall and powerfully built, pale-complexioned, with clear eyes, well-formed brows, a nose neither high nor low, and a robust frame exuding divine stature, naturally commanding the authority of an elder and chieftain. Entirely indifferent to the fierce attacks and mockery of the assembly, he treated the assault of formidable foes as though a cloud of butterflies were fluttering against an armored warship of steel—unhurried, unruffled, radiating the serene composure of a spring breeze after rain. Yet even a deity of such towering stature, the moment a single grain of selfish desire sprouted within his heart, would immediately plunge into the dominance of flesh over spirit. Whenever a void of selfish desire opens, the wicked spirits swarming through the cosmos seize the opportunity to invade the body, altering the divine personality and fanning the flames of greed.

Thus, when their heaven-bestowed divine souls held sway, Lord Tokoyohiko and Lady Tokoyohime were paragons of wisdom, benevolence, and valor; but when possessed by evil spirits, they suddenly turned violent, at times even descending into unexpected cowardice. However virtuous a deity may be, if an empty void of attachment or desire lingers in the heart, it instantly becomes a vessel for demons. Truly, the posture of the heart is of paramount consequence! In contrast, a deity of single-minded sincerity devoid of selfish desire never cowers, grieves, or despairs; like Yukinarihiko, standing fearless within the enemy citadel, he pours forth his honest convictions to achieve his sacred mission.

八王大神・常世彦は、黒髪が長く背後に垂れ、長身魁偉にして肌は白く、清らかな眼、端正な眉、鼻は高からず低からず、骨格たくましく神格を備え、何処となく長者・頭領たる権威がおのずから備わっていた。諸神人の猛烈な攻撃や嘲罵に対しても少しも意に介さぬ様子で、いかなる強敵の襲来も、あたかも鋼鉄の軍艦に蝶々が群がり襲ってきたかのように悠々として騒がず、光風霽月の爽やかさを惟神（かんながら）に発揮していたのである。しかし、かくの如き高邁なる神格を備えた八王大神であっても、心中に僅かでも欲望の念が萌すならば、たちまち体主霊従の暴挙を敢行して憚らぬまでに神格が一変してしまうのであった。心に一点の私欲が生じるや、宇宙に充満する邪神がその隙に乗じて体内に侵入し、直ちにその神格を変質させ、悪心と欲望とをますます増長させるからである。

ゆえに八王大神も常世姫も、天授の精魂が体内を完全に支配している時は、実に智仁勇兼備の至聖至直なる神格者となり得る人物であったが、邪神が憑依した時の二人は俄かに狂暴となり、時に依っては意外なる卑怯者へと変貌してしまうのであった。いかに善良なる神人であっても、心中に空虚があり、執着があり、欲望がある時は、直ちに邪神の器となってしまう。実に恐るべきは心の持ち方である。これに反し、至誠一貫して僅かの執着も欲望も持たぬ神人は、いかなる逆境にあっても恐怖せず、嘆かず、失望することなく、行成彦の如く敵城にありながらも少しも恐れず、滔々と所信を述べて天職を全うし得るのである。

§ 2
§ 2: Tokoyohiko's Masterly Appeal for Voluntary Abdication

With dignified and stately composure, Tokoyohiko stood upon the dais. Beaming with a warm, gentle smile, he rested both hands neatly upon the table, bowing slightly as he addressed the assembly with paternal earnestness:

'Ah, ye divine ministers of the assembly! Before the Eight Kings whom I most revere, and before all of you who embody benevolence and justice, I humbly pour forth the sincere red heart I have harbored for years, praying that with your sympathetic understanding we may fulfill the purpose of this assembly for world peace!

The Primordial Cosmic Spirit, Ōkunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, created the great universe—bringing forth the sun, moon, earth, and stars to accomplish His boundless divine labor—so that all living things might thrive in supreme peace and bliss, fully executing the sovereign will of the cosmos. As He created the sun, endowing it with divine soul, spiritual power, and spirit body to appoint its guardian deity; and as He endowed the moon with soul, power, and body to establish its guardian deity; so upon our earth He appointed the divine split spirit of Ōkunitokotachi-no-Mikoto to govern and protect creation—the very Lord Kunitokotachi whom we revere day and night.

Though wise ministers like yourselves might laugh at words from an unworthy servant like myself, I have spoken from the foundation of cosmic law to praise the boundless grace of the Creator, well aware of the gravity of this assembly which rivals the very creation of heaven and earth.

You know well that our Earthly Sovereign, Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, labors ceaselessly for universal peace and development. That our Divine Parent exerts boundless compassion day and night to grant each being its rightful place is a mercy beyond all words; and born as children of the Almighty, we must make His heart our own, dedicating our utmost service unto God.

To realize His ideal upon earth, the Creator venerated the land guardians and appointed the Eight Kings and Chieftains to establish righteous governance. Yet looking upon the world today, discipline in every territory governed by the Eight Kings has collapsed over the changing seasons, falling into irreparable confusion and strife—a reality you ministers know most keenly.

Can leaving such disorder unhealed truly align with the Creator's holy will? Though it pains me to utter such words, can we truly claim that the divine will has been faithfully observed? What must our Divine Parent think when He gazes upon the world's tragic plight?

When I reflect in secret during the dead of night upon the heart of Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto, hot tears stream unbidden down my cheeks! Grieved by our failures, yet having once conferred these offices, He respects your honor and withholds open rebuke, anxiously awaiting your return to conscience.

Looking upon your indecision, He laments that the day grows dark while the road remains long! Does not the Almighty yearn in His sorrow for the Eight Kings of every mountain to voluntarily resign their offices? Divine ministers, I plead that you ponder this deeply!' In this masterly fashion, he urged the voluntary abdication of all the Eight Kings.

「あゝ満場の諸神司らよ。吾が最も敬愛する八王をはじめ、慈愛と正義の権化とも称すべき神人らの前に、謹んで吾が胸中に深く永年納め置いた赤心を吐露し、以てその同情ある御理解を得て、這般の大会議の目的を世界平和のために達成せんことを、天地の神明に誓い、至誠をもって貫徹したいと希望する次第であります。

§ 3
§ 3: The Sudden Collapse of Hachiō-Ōkami upon the High Dais

Invoking the authority of the Creator as his shield, Tokoyohiko's imposing argument left the Eight Kings and Chieftains completely speechless; seized with deep humiliation, they could only sigh heavily. At that very instant, what sudden catastrophe struck? Hachiō-Ōkami Tokoyohiko's complexion turned deathly pale; crying out 'Ah!', he collapsed face-down upon the rostrum! How will this astonishing disaster ever be resolved?

現代日本語訳

原文（近代文語）
