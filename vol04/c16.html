Chapter 16: Sweet Words and Fair Speeches [166]

善言美辞〔166〕

§ 1

Izumohime, singing a mixed song of elegance and sarcasm instead of delivering a speech, expressed her wrath and convictions without regret before the divine officials; therefore, Tokoyohiko and Tokoyohime were exceedingly displeased in their hearts and, remaining cold, listened intently to Izumohime's song, almost without seeming to listen.

In general, song is the essence of kotodama that softens the divine will of the gods of heaven and earth, imparts cheerfulness to all things, and illuminates the hearts and souls of divine beings. Therefore, there was no room for Tokoyohiko to vent his wrath against it or to rebuke her.

The conferences among present-day humans use only speech, and none lends an ear to refined voices such as song, but rather they have come to prohibit it. Therefore, in modern conferences, people spit foam at every matter, slant their eyes, scatter the sparks of dispute, clash swords, shout with delight, and regarding any issue, with cold and sharp words amidst discontent, they must resignedly accept decisions based on the foolish multitude called the majority vote of survival of the fittest. In the distant past of the divine land of Akitsushima Urayasu no Kuni, it was a land where the blessing and help of kotodama shone across heaven and lived, beginning and ending with good words and beautiful speeches; yet in this era, the application of kotodama, the Great Way of the gods of heaven and earth, has become utterly disordered and has finally ceased to leave any trace. At present, divine beings who state their will through elegant and noble song are extremely few, practiced only slightly among high-ranking divine beings. Therefore, in this Tokoyo conference as well, it was left to the freedom of the divine beings: there were those who used direct speech, those who expressed their will solely through song, and those who spoke mixing speech and song. Divine beings whose kotodama was pure and bright all endeavored to resolve difficult problems through waka.

Here, Tokoyohime was driven into a situation where she had to sing a reply song in response to Izumohime's song expressing her opinion; therefore, Tokoyohime, dragging her long sleeves upon the dais, sang her assertion with a clear and smooth voice. That song was:

"The Great Ancestor who formed and consolidated heaven and earth, The Great God Kunihitotachi, By whose myriad blessings this land was born; Though there are many lands called lands, And many gods called gods, The true God is but one; Created by God, inhabited by gods, Where evergreen pines grow thick, And white plums of wondrous color and scent Bloom and flourish, such a divine land, Apart from the Land of Tokoyo, There can be no land so noble; The Land of Tokoyo shall forever Open and prosper beneath heaven, And the lands and islands of the four directions as well, From the eastern sky sparkling, Together with the light of the morning sun Rising in radiance, The land that is brightly governed Is the very mahoroba of heaven and earth, the Land of Tokoyo."

現代日本語訳

出雲姫は口頭での演説に代えて優雅と皮肉との混交歌を歌い、自己の憤怒と所信とを遺憾なく諸神司の前に訴えたので、常世彦と常世姫は心中甚だ面白くなく、冷然として出雲姫の歌を聞くともなしに聞き入ったのであった。

およそ歌とは天地神明の聖慮を和らげ、万有に陽気を与え、神人の心魂を照らし明るくする言霊の精華である。それゆえに常世彦もこれに向かって憤怒を発し、叱責する余地がなかったのである。

現代の人間同士の会議は、すべて言論のみを用い、決して歌などの風雅な声に耳を傾けるものはなく、かえってこれを禁止するに至っている。それゆえに現代の会議は何事につけても口角泡を飛ばし、釣り上がった目で論争の花を散らし、鎬を削って快哉を叫び、いかなる問題に関しても言葉は冷たく尖り、不平不満の間に優勝劣敗的な多数決という、衆愚本位の議決に甘んじていなければならない。秋津島浦安国の神国の遠き昔は言霊の幸い、助け、天が照り渡り生ける国であって、善言美詞をもって終始していたのに、もはやこの時代は、天地神明の大道である言霊の応用も乱れに乱れ、ついにその跡を絶つに至った。今日においては神人が優雅にして高潔な歌をもってその意志を述べるものは甚だ少なく、ただ上位の神人の間にわずかに行われていたのであった。ゆえに今回の常世の会議においても、神人の自由に任せ、直接の言葉によるものと、単に歌のみによって意志を表白するものと、言葉と歌とを混合して口演するものとがあったのである。言霊が清く朗らかな神人は、すべて和歌によって難問題を解決しようと努力したのであった。

ここに常世姫は、出雲姫の意見表明の歌に対して返歌を歌わねばならない破目となったので、常世姫は長い袖を壇上に引きずりながら、声音清く滑らかにその主張を歌った。その歌、

『天地を造り固めた大御祖である 国治立大神の 数々の恵みによって生まれた国 国という国は多いけれど 神という神は多くおられるけれど 真の神はただ一柱 神が造り神が住む 常磐の松が生い茂り 色と香りが妙なる白梅が 咲いて賑わう神国は 常世の国をよそにして 尊い国はあるまい 常世の国は永遠に 開け栄えて天下の 四方の国々や島々も 東の空からきらきらと 輝き昇る朝日の 光とともに明るく 治まる国は天地の その真秀良場である常世の国』

出雲姫は口演に代へ優雅と、皮肉との混交歌を歌つて、自己の憤怒と、所信とを遺憾なく、諸神司の前に訴へたるにぞ、常世彦、常世姫は心中甚だ面白からず、冷然として出雲姫の歌を聞くともなしに聞き入りにける。

§ 2

Higher than the Rocky Mountains that tower into the sky of the land of Tokoyo is their renown; clouds traversing the sky hold it in awe and veer away, such is their great majesty; Tokoyohiko, like the sun shining eternally; pure in heart and pure in body, Tokoyohime is purer than snow; seeking to illuminate the darkness of the eternal night, stirring up a sincere heart, clearing away the clustered clouds of the world; with thoughts scattered like the tidal flats of Chikushi, the deep grace is like the sea of Shiranui, where the cries of suffering from the divine people drifting upon the waves are pitiful; unable to look away and abandon this pitiful plight, here Hachiō no Ōkami, seeking to pour the dew of divine grace, higher than the mountains and deeper than the sea, upon all the myriad grasses,

opened this gathering with the heart of God and of parents; the eight leaders who serve the god Hachiō, who preside over the mountains and plains and have gathered here, the Kunitama gods and the hundred gods, numbering eight hundred pillars in total; uniting the sincere hearts of the eighty-eight pillars into one, consolidating their vitality into one, to mow down the vile weeds in all four directions under heaven, to purify them, and before Ame no Mihashira no Kami who resides in heaven, so that Toyoashihara no Mizuho no Kuni ruled by Kunitokotachi may be governed ever more peacefully and securely for eternity, and to protect the divine land of Urayasu, this divine gathering of the castle of Tokoyo first takes up the sacred office of Hachiō;

morning after morning the wind of Shinato blows away the spiritual mist in all directions; the heavens become bright and the earth pure, without discrimination between high and low, being close like parents and children, rejoicing in God's grace, only waiting for an era of peaceful rule; a world like the white plum blossoms that constantly diffuse their fragrance is the brilliantly beautiful land of the gods, where the divine gathering of Tokoyo is opened; yet, suspecting this pure gathering of such noble divine people, speaking words of an unclean heart, Izumohime, rising like twilight hanging in the sky, open the door of your darkened soul, accept the sincere heart of the gods of Tokoyo, and for the sake of God and the land, lift the clouds and mist of suspicion from your breast and the curtain of darkness;

may the merit of this gathering ordained by God progress smoothly, communing and harmonizing, and may you conform to the august will of Kunitokotachi, who is the heavenly deity and the ancestral parent of the land, O hundred gods; conform to it, O hundred gods; this indeed is the prayer of Tokoyo; this indeed is the prayer of Tokoyo."

Having sung thus, she bowed to the assembled divine officiants, and said,

"I hope that you will enthusiastically support this world-saving proposal of Hachiō no Ōkami,"

closing her gentle, flower-like lips, and slowly took her prepared seat upon the dais.

このように歌って列座の神司に一礼し、
