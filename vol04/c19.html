§ 1

Sadahime, who had entered the castle accompanying Yukinarihiko from the divine capital of the holy ground of Jerusalem, was troubled that her younger sister Izumohime, though having mounted the platform to compose a sarcastic poem and making the various divine people marvel, had somewhat impaired her divine character; seeking to remedy that fault and maintain the divine character of a divine messenger of the holy ground, this princess, usually quiet, decisively resolved her mind, shyly requested the right to speak, and quietly appeared upon the platform. Sadahime by no means matched the glamorous beauty of Izumohime. Yet her mouth was somehow firm, and her cool eyes possessed a wondrous power capable of drawing in the various divine people unawares. Sadahime stood upon the platform blushing somewhat shyly, and in those blushing cheeks an indescribable familiarity welled up. The princess, reflecting on her station as a woman, avoided fierce speech, sought to state her impressions through the power of kotodama as gentle as possible, and substituted an elegant poem for speech.

Though the eternal land of China is far away, The sincere heart that yearns for the age of the gods Is, both in China and in Japan, everlastingly Unchanging; blessed By the august spirits of the gods of heaven and earth, The divine people who were born Have upright and righteous hearts, And while advancing upon the path of sincerity, As civilization opened, unawares Clouds of eternal darkness arose over the world;

O many kami assembled at the gathering of the kami of the fortress of Tokoyo: reflect deeply upon what the heart of the Sume-ōkami and the law of the decrees may be. Though the land of Tokoyo be vast, and though the kami of Tokoyo be strong, you mingle before the foreign kami whom the Kami do not permit, contending in words and brandishing the sword of the tongue,

scattering sparks and clashing ridges of your blades against one another—how wretched it is! The kami of the Eight Kings are the office entrusted by the Sumekami. To guard it purely, sacredly, and solemnly is the prerogative bestowed upon the spirits of the Eight Kingdom Kunitama. Despising even that authority, as if casting away worn-out footwear, the hearts of the kami discard it without regret.

Ah, it has become eternal darkness! Ah, it has become eternal darkness! If they swiftly cast away the fierce weapons borne upon the bodies of the unruly kami, and let the wind of Shinado blow away the strife of this world, Sarudahime also shall raise her left and right hands to guide them, and the Sume-ōkami surely shall commend that achievement.

If but a single pillar among the kami of the Eight Kings be lacking, the reign of this fleeting world shall instantly turn into a reign of crookedness, tilting, falling into disorder, and collapsing. O Eight King kami permitted by the Kami, O eight-headed kami, and O many kami: let not the flowers of the words of Sarudahime, who descended as an envoy of Takamanohara, be scattered by the midnight tempest of eternal darkness.
