Chapter 9: 狂 Monster Battle [1815]

狂怪戦〔1815〕

§ 1

Ohana: 'Uitenpen is the way of the world. That heaven becomes earth and earth becomes heaven, such is the state of this Ukiyo, and perhaps this is the story of you and me. Sleeping or waking, in dream or reality, in the Ikimiya of the Hinode no Kami, from the tips of my hair to the ends of my toes, I have poured my body and Mitama, and I have fallen in love with you, my dear. By what wind of Fukimaha has it come to be that I am in such a relationship with you, a couple pledged for Nise? Surely, the Kamisama of this Seijiyō will also grant us their blessing. No matter how much one speaks of high-minded things like Kami or Buddha, as long as humans possess a Nikutai, the desires of the flesh will spring forth. To spend one's days in vain without knowing how to fulfill them—'

Ohana: '—is to trample upon the pleasure given by heaven. Even if one brandishes the idea that "love is best" and fulfills the desires of the flesh with Jiiujizai, there is no Riiu for the Kamisama to interfere. Ah, how Omoshiro, how promising! This precious Kuwanraku, I was deceived by Otora-baba-sama, who told me that the Mitama of the Otohime of the Riugūkai must undergo Kaishin. She lied to me so sweetly, saying that if I ever touched a man, the sins and retributions of eighty thousand kalpas would appear all at once, and I would suffer the torments of extreme cold and extreme heat three times a day, and thus she kept me hooked for ten years. Truly, the more I think about it, the more of a fool I am. This Ohana, awakened to love—'

Ohana: '—now has a heart like a rock, unmoved by bow or gun. Once I have made up my mind, you must cast aside your fickle heart and pledge to grow old together with me to the very end. I have cast away my life and my treasures, and I have entrusted this body to you. Whether you burn me or boil me to eat, I shall never express any dissatisfaction. Nevertheless, Danna-sama, you are truly cold-hearted. While you have a wife with whom you pledged for Nise, you carelessly cast aside your sense of chivalry and said you would sneak through the gates of the police station as a substitute for Torokkii-sama. Chivalry and benevolence are fine, but if you pay such useless sacrifices—'

Ohana: '—you will not be able to live in this world. This much, Danna-sama, if you think I am dear to you, please give up. In this world where popularity is poor, one never knows when a disturbance will arise. If I am to become a victim every time that happens, how can this Ohana ever stand on firm ground? I did not know that having a soldier as a husband would mean being made to feel such painful thoughts, not even having a moment to rejoice. I do not know if it is Yamato-damashii or not, but please stop it from now on. Your dear wife joins her hands and begs you with tears. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-hae-mase.'

Ohana: 'O Great Kami of the Uranai faith, please stop the heart of my husband, which shifts in a thousand changes, and nail it to my Mitama so that it may never leave, as if driving in a nail. This is my lifelong wish. The thing called fate is a strange thing. In this land ten thousand miles overseas, I never thought I would come to love Mamoriyakewake-sama so much that I would lose my way on the dark path of love, unable to see even my own feet. Have I gone mad? I have become suspicious of my own self, and I have become unable to understand anything. Truly, women are fragile when it comes to men. A man's every frown and smile feels as if a five-inch nail is being driven into my chest—'

Ohana: '—and painful thoughts come over me. Though I wear frost on my head, my heart remains that of a sixteen-year-old. My chest throbs, my breath catches, and I care nothing for shame or public opinion. That I have come to have such a heart is because of you, who are a sin. In the midst of this vast heaven and earth, you are the only one for me. You are my life, my strength. If you were to die, this world would be nothing but hell. If I am to fall even to the very bottom of the depths of hell, I would not mind if it were together with you, my beloved. Do not abandon me so coldly when I think of you this much. If you do abandon me—'

Ohana: '—I will become a demon, a great serpent, and pull out your head, Danna-sama. I warn you in advance. Ah, how reliable, how reliable! This place is the center of the world, the holy city on the Mount of Olives where the precious Kami dwells. I feel as if I have grown young by thirty or forty years, and as I walk hand in hand up the steep mountain, I feel as if I am being led by a hundred angels to climb up to the paradise where the flowers of heaven bloom and bloom. Ah, ah, it is because I have lived long that I have come to understand the true, true taste of love after growing old. How grateful I am. Even before the Hinode no Kami and the Great Miroku, and Ikimiya-sama—'

Such a joyful and pure feeling, I have never experienced until now. Truly, you are the Kiuseishiyu, an angel of Amatsumikuni. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may your Mitama be Sachihahe.

Ohana: 'Ho ho ho ho, Yamoriwake-danna-sama, if you could understand my feelings, you would not hate me so much. Please, I pray you, pour your love upon me eternally. I hate it when you wander off or look the other way. Even though you have a fine wife like me, because you have the nature of a Gunjin, you show unnecessary Gikefushin and get yourself arrested by the foolish Keisatsu as a substitute for someone like the Bauakuburai Trotsky. Please stop doing such things. No matter how much you talk about saving the Sekai, it is unbearable to be subjected to Haritsuke like Christ-sama.'

Yamoriwake: 'Is it not fine to be subjected to Haritsuke together with you? Your name will remain for Mangofumatsudai. Did you not say, as if it were a Kuchiguse, that if you and Otora-sama saved the Banmin of the Sekai, a wonderful name would remain for Mangofumatsudai? To become a Giseishiya of the Sekai in this Jerusalem, where it is said that Christ once hung on the Jifujika and redeemed the sins of the Banmin, and to leave a name for Matsudai is a thrilling thing for a human. Is that not so, Ohana-sama?'

Ohana: 'I hate that! Please stop using such distant words as Ohana-sama. No matter how much a name may remain, if life is gone, can one not taste physical pleasure?'

Yamoriwake: 'Is it not fine to die and be together happily in the future? If you do that, you will not need to worry about being taken back by Otora-sama, and it will be the safest place in the universe. I am not such an Ahau as to become a substitute for someone like Trotsky, but I just wanted to make you think, "He is a man of strong Gikefushin..." in this way, so I performed a play. Furthermore, because many farmers' groups and labor groups were hanging around, it was my strategy to make the Sekai believe that the man called Yamoriwake of Hinodewake-jima is a man full of Gikefushin and that he is the true Kiuseishiyu. After all, if a human is not widely known, he cannot do his work. Even that Uzunbara-Chyanda, if you actually associate with him, is a human of less than a fraction. From my perspective, he is a small person who does not even amount to the tip of my little finger. Because he happened to cause an incident and his name echoed throughout the Sekai, the Ahau of the Sekai began to carry him on their shoulders, calling him the second coming of Christ, the appearance of Miroku, the Messiah, and so on. For a self-promotion strategy, entering among the laborers and giving them a taste of something is the best trump card, ha ha ha ha.'

Ohana: 'Ho ho ho ho, what a cunning person you are. With that much wisdom, why have you been clinging to a Botsubungyōkan like Otora-sama until now?'

Yamoriwake: 'Otora-sama is of the lineage of the Hensei-danshi, is he not? Even if he keeps babbling about things that are off-track and incomprehensible, he is of the lineage, so the Botsubungyōkan group of the San-go-kyō come clinging to him secretly. It is because I had a great ambition to use that, in short, to plot the overthrow of the San-go-kyō and take the place of the Hensei-joshi.'

Ohana: 'What a person, one cannot judge by appearances. Even Yamoriwake-sama and Hōyō-sama were saying that you were a man who would be fine if only given sake to drink, but the more I hear, the more reliable and wonderful a man you are. However, that is also no wonder; you have the skill to bewitch Ayame-no-Ohana, who is like a cock-a-doodle-doo with spurs or a wild cat with a split tail, who has understood both the sour and the sweet of the affairs of the Sekai, ho ho ho ho. You are a master against whom one must be constantly on guard. Yamoriwake-sama, no, Danna-sama, please perform some poetry recitation with your favorite voice, which sounds like a bell cricket. It is not balanced if only I am made to sing.'

'Very well, if it is your wish, I shall perform a Shigin or whatever you desire.'

He raised his voice like a Doragoe and opened his mouth wide,

'The moon sets, the crow cries, and frost fills the sky of heaven,

At dawn, I see the great river bearing a thousand soldiers... Zest...'

Ohana: 'Now, Danna-sama, please stop with such old-fashioned Shigin. I would like you to sing about me instead.'

Morigomoriwake: 'Very well, then I shall try one in the Shinpa style. It is a sentimental song that will make your teeth ache. Ahem.'

'With heaven as the background,

With earth as the stage,

Waving the sleeves of the clouds,

I act in the great Daiuchiu.

Ah, born as a human, yet not a human,

I know the first Kuwanki since my Seitan in this Gense.

One like Otora is not worth counting.

Her face is like an Atlas,

It is like the Gekko Bosatsu shining in the sky,

Furthermore, in Otora's pocket,

She has only a thousand gold coins left.

In this Gense where gold is omnipotent,

To carry ten thousand gold coins,

Ayame-no-kimi is indeed,

The greatest winner in terms of wealth.

With this lady, there is this gold,

With this lady, there is this husband.

Is the common saying of an ogre with an iron club,

Something that tells of this very situation?

Ah, how pleasant is the mountain of Kanran,

Becoming husband and wife, we let all things in heaven and earth view this graceful figure.

You and I monopolize the happiness of the Uchiu,

Becoming a Kami of happiness while still alive,

Learning the immortality of living through ten thousand kalpas,

We intend to live eternally with heaven and earth.

Ah, how promising, how promising,

The evening on the sacred mountain of Kanran.

The moon is bright, ascending the stairs of five-colored clouds,

The stars are brilliant, emitting the light of gold and silver.

Heaven is pure, and earth is also pure,

I am pure, and you are also pure.

This half-day of pure leisure truly has the feeling of cleansing the heart and soul.

Katsu!'

Ohana: 'Oh, you startled me. Opening your mouth like a tanuki and shouting Katsu, what was that? I thought I was going to be bitten.'

Morigomoriwake: 'You are so cute that I felt like biting you from the head down, ahahaha.'

Ohana: 'Ohohoho, my, how charming you are, Danna-sama. With that voice, you sound like you eat lizards or cuckoos, so I cannot let my guard down for a moment.'

Morigomoriwake: 'Hey Ohana, let us be silent and go. It seems there is someone in the shade of those trees, and it is a bit unsightly. You follow me at a distance of two or three ken. And it is troublesome if you call me Danna-sama where there are people.'

Ohana: 'Very well, I shall not call you Danna-sama from today on. You are a person who changes his mind so easily.'

She was already growing the horns of jealousy.

Morigomoriwake whispered,

'Ah, it is well said that women and small men are difficult to nurture. If you treat them gently, they become conceited; if you speak strongly, they bark; and if you kill them, they are demons that will haunt you as ghosts, ah, ah.'

Though she could not hear the whisper clearly, Ohana heard the sound of the sigh, and fearing that this was the boundary of his usual change of heart, her complexion changed instantly, and she wore a face like a toad that had failed to croak.

From under a tree five or six ken away by the roadside, a laugh that sounded like tiles being smashed came echoing forth.

『あまりお前が可愛ので頭から噛ぶつてやらうと思つたのだ、アハヽヽ』

『オホヽヽヽ、あのまア旦那様のほどのよい事哩のう。その声で蜥蜴喰ふか杜鵑式だから一寸も油断は出来ないわ』

『おいお花、もう黙つて行かう、どうやら、あの木蔭に人が居るやうだ、些と許り見つともないからなア。お前は二三間離れてついて来て呉れ。さうして人の居る所で旦那さまなぞと云つて貰つちや困るよ』

『ハイ、旦那さまつて今日限り申ませぬ。よう気の変るお方ですな』

と早や悋気の角を生して居る。

守宮別は小声で、

『あゝ女子と小人は養ひ難しとは能くいつたものだな。柔しく云つたら自惚る、強く云へば吠える、殺せば化けて出ると云ふ魔物だからなア、アーア』

お花は小声でハツキリわからねどアーアの声を聞き、こいつは又例の心境変化の境界線ではないかと心配のあまりサツト顔色変り蟇蛙の鳴き損ねたやうな面をさらし居る。

路傍の五六間先の木の下から瓦をぶちやけたやうな笑ひ声が聞え来りける。
