Chapter 12: The Temptation [12]

誘惑〔12〕

§ 1

Burabāsa stands alone, looking dejected while gazing at the moon in the Saukū. The woman who came walking briskly toward him was Mariya, who had been guiding him through the Seichi with sincerity for the past month.

Mariya: 'Seishisama, are you here all alone? I had thought you were with Salome-sama again.'

Burabāsa: 'Ah, so it is you, Mariya-sama. It is quite something for you to come out alone at this hour of the night.'

Mariya: 'Yes, I followed after you, and though I know it may be a Gomeiwaku, I slipped out of the colony quietly to come here. Just as I was thinking of having a quiet talk with Salome-sama, a terrible Jiyamamono arrived and caused me such worry. They say there are clouds for the moon and storms for the flowers; the world does not always go as one wishes. Ho ho ho ho.'

Burabāsa: 'That is a strange Kotoba to hear. Salome-sama visits this Tauzan from time to time, and I have met her by Gūzen on this mountain two or three times, but there is no particular reason why I must speak with her in Naimitsu, so please do not worry yourself. I offer my full Kanshiya for your Goshinsetsu Taido.'

Mariya: 'A Seishi does not tell lies; if your words are true, then I am relieved. By the way, there is one thing I would like to ask of you; would it be possible for you to hear it? I trust you have read the letter I sent you the other day?'

Burabāsa: 'Indeed, two or three days ago, Arabu brought it to the Catholic monastery, saying it was a letter from you, but I have kept it in my pocket without even opening it yet.'

Mariya: 'You must not understand my Magokoro. Or perhaps you despise me. You have come all the way to the Seichi across the vast ocean, and you must think that your pure body has been contaminated by germs, and that you should not read a letter from a filthy woman. If that is the case, then so be it; I must think of something else, for if you will not read the letter, it is useless to give it to you, so please return it.'

Burabāsa: 'Mariya-sama, it troubles me to have you get so angry. I had no such thoughts at all. I was so immersed in my research of the Seichi that I simply forgot about it.'

Mariya: 'If you can forget my letter, then I must not be on your mind at all. Ah, how frustrating!'

Burabāsa: 'Mariya-sama, how could I ever forget you? When I arrived at the Jerusalem station, I met you on that road immediately, and I will never forget the impression of that moment when I met you unexpectedly in a strange land. Please, do not think ill of me.'

Mariya: 'Did you not say during our tour of the Seichi that you would love me forever? Those warm words of yours soaked into my very bones, and now that it has come to this, I am caught in the snare of love and can do nothing. My life is as good as held in the palm of your hand. Please, read that letter once by the moonlight. And I wish to hear your clear answer.'

Burabāsa: 'If that is the case, then regarding your kind intentions, whether I follow your words or not is a matter for later, but in any case, I shall read it here.'

He took the letter from his pocket, broke the seal, and began to read it with his heart pounding............

§ 2

I, who most deeply revere the Seishisama of Rutobaha, Burabasa-sama, present this letter to you, confessing the depth of my heart's desire. Seishisama, that you suffer morning and night for the sake of all humanity and all things in the Zensekai is truly noble and beyond my gratitude. I deeply apologize that a Daizainin such as I should approach you and increase your suffering all the more. Ever since I first laid eyes upon you, I have been caught in a Jiyōdau of love that cannot be expressed in words, and I continue to suffer in Kumon day and night. To escape this suffering, I pray to Kamisama Asayufu, exert my Daiyūmaushin, discipline my own heart, and though I whip myself time and again, crush my heart to powder, this Bonnaukuwa of intense Jiyōnetsu cannot be extinguished by the will of a weak woman. I am sunk in an abyss of Hanmon and Kunau, tormented by the demons of my own heart. Ah, who is there that can save both my spirit and body? There is nothing but the noble and warm love of Seishisama. I desire to be held in your broad, loving embrace, Seishisama, to the very end. I wish to offer my body and soul entirely, and then, for a while, to fall into a state of unconsciousness and sleep. Seishisama, you may think me an unseemly and base woman, but might being held by you not be the very preparation that will enliven my life and allow me, before long, to sprout beautiful buds and engage in great activity? Both my spirit and body are exhausted by the flames of love. The fire of my life is now on the verge of extinction. Seishisama, who conveys the teachings of the merciful and compassionate God, I beseech you, please bring me back to life once more. In a quiet, secluded place where few people come, please hold me in your warm, loving hands and bring about my resurrection. Even if, for that reason, I were to face tens of thousands of enemies or receive the insults and mockery of tens of thousands of people, I would never fear. I believe this, too, is one of the divine intentions of Kamisama. And would not allowing me to live and work for you result in Seishisama being able to act throughout the world? Your suffering, Seishisama, is my suffering, and my suffering is surely your suffering. Whether to let this pitiful woman live or to kill her rests entirely within your heart. Furthermore, my death must be the death of my master. That a man and woman separated by ten thousand miles of ocean should meet at the Jerusalem station, I believe to be a promise from a previous life, where some truly mysterious force bound us together, as if we were destined to become one. I firmly believe that for you and me to breathe in unison and serve the divine work is entirely the divine plan of Kamisama. If it is for the construction of the Miroku divine government, then as it is the will of Kamisama, I must obey no matter what it may be. I cannot think that my loving you, my master, is ever, ever a sin. Please, I beseech you, love me with absolute love. I do not demand an eternal love. The time has come for both my spirit and body to be transformed. Even if it were for but a minute, it would be enough if I could only be held in your warm embrace. I am offering my life and soul to you, my master, for the sake of the fulfillment of the divine nation. I earnestly request your favorable reply with all possible speed.

Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese. From Mariya.

To my master.

Burabasa finished reading the letter, let out a sharp sigh, and remained silent, his hands clasped and his head bowed.

Mariya: 'My master, you will surely listen to my pitiful heart and my request, will you not?'

§ 3

Speaker: 'I have fully understood your Magokoro. However, as a Sendenshi who serves the Oshihe of Rutobaha, which is strict regarding the Itsupuitsupu Seido, no matter how passionately you may love me, I cannot enter into a Renai Kuwankei; please, I ask you to Minaho and Norinaho this matter.'

Speaker: 'If you Otsushiya such things, are you saying that you intend to Migoro me? I am well aware of the Itsupuitsupu Seido and the foundation of Jinrin. However, those are Riseiteki Kenkai, and the Jiyōdau of love is not something so Kisokuba. Are you going to let this woman, who is withered and gasping for breath from love, fall into Monshi? If I had never met you, I would not be suffering from this Hanmon and Kunau. I think you are an Akuma who has come to destroy me from Hinodejima. Kamisama has bestowed upon us the precious thing called Renai. If this Renai were not meant to be freely exercised, why would He have caused such thoughts to arise in me, a woman who serves God day and night? Please do not say such things, and if you have even a single drop of tears of compassion, please grant my wish. It would surely not become a sin of immorality. I do not ask you to make me your wife. If I am coldly rejected by you here and now, I will make the Sea of Galilee my final place. That would surely not be to your honor either. That is why I wrote in this letter that my death might be your death.'

Burabasa, while clasping his hands and sighing,

Speaker: 'Ah, the devilish hand of temptation reaches everywhere. Is the heart of a man, firm as a rock, to be shattered by the heart of a single woman? It is well said that the world is full of small virtues and great demons. Ah, what should I do?'

He muttered in a low voice and sank into deep thought. Mariya, like a bird in flight, clung to Burabasa from behind and embraced him with all her might. Burabasa, surprised, thought to himself,

Speaker: 'Ah, there is no help for it; if I coldly shake off a woman who has fallen into such intense love, there will surely be a mistake. I know not if it is a violation of the laws of heaven, but for a while, I must leave things as she says, and gradually explain the truth to awaken her.'

Nodding to himself, he changed his tone and said,

Speaker: 'No, Mariya-sama, I am truly grateful that you think of me so much. I am truly overwhelmed with gratitude. However, it has not yet been a month since I arrived here. I must keep my body pure for the next seventy days to fulfill a certain mission, so I ask for your patience until I have completed my one-hundred-day ascetic practice.'

Speaker: 'Are you not just saying such things to soothe me and deceive me? I can only think of it as an excuse to escape the situation. I would like you to give me a definitive answer.'

Burabasa sighed and fell into a long silence. Mariya also remained silent for a while, trembling, but then, as if she had made up her mind, she opened her mouth and grasped Burabasa's hand firmly,

Speaker: 'It has been almost a month today since I first met you, and for some reason, despite having lived a life of celibacy, I fell in love from that moment, and this past month has felt as long as a thousand years. My long, silent love has rotted and broken my brain. And now I am tasting the Hanmon and Kunau of love. I cannot bury this as a secret forever. Please, thinking of it as saving one woman, please understand my love. If you wish to laugh at this intense Renai, then laugh. Or if you wish to slander it, then slander it. I am already a madwoman suffering from love. Everything that appears in my eyes—the mountains, rivers, grasses, and trees—appears as the figure of my beloved teacher, so perhaps I am mad. Ah, it is painful; who could have caused such a strange love? Ever since I met you in the town of Jerusalem, I have become a complete prisoner of love. I believe it is a love given by Kamisama. When one is given love, one should live while tasting that love to the fullest. My loving you, my teacher, can never be irrational or immoral. I cannot help but believe it is the will of Kamisama. When solemn, sacred love transforms into universal love, we may come to know its preciousness, greatness, and beauty. Does the human love of the Oshihe of Rutobaha not mean such a thing? The love that loves humanity itself is the love of Kamisama, which is to say, the love for finding oneself, and it may be the beginning and the end of the love that should ascend to the heaven where one must build oneself. That you, my teacher, do not understand me is truly a tremendous sorrow. I, too, am registered in the American Colony, waiting for the second coming of the Savior, and while I have the mission of saving the whole world, how could I be floating in a frivolous love? I must be reborn anew by the hands of my teacher. I must be resurrected in both spirit and flesh. By loving and being loved by you, and by being united with you, I am given new strength. If you say that this Renai of mine is irrational, then please remove it with your divine power. Yet, the warm face and gentle words of the teacher I have come to love and yearn for have flowed into my whole body and become my blood.'

『妾は貴方に初めてお目にかかつてから今日で殆ど一ケ月、どうしたものかセリバシー生活をやつて来た身であり乍ら、その時から恋におち、此一月の間も殆ど千年のやうに長きを感じました。妾のあまり永い沈黙の恋は妾の頭脳を腐らし破つて了ひました。そして妾は今恋の煩悶苦悩を味はつてゐます。私は之を何時迄も秘密として葬り去る事が出来ないのです。何卒一人の女を救ふと思つて妾の恋を諒解して下さい。此猛烈な恋愛を笑ふなら笑つて下さい。又誹るなら誹つて下さい。もはや妾は恋に悩む狂人です。妾の目に浮かぶものは山川草木一切が恋しい師の君のお姿になつて見えるのですもの、狂つてるのかも知れませぬ。あゝ苦しい、こんな不思議な恋を誰がさせたので御座いませうか。エルサレムの町でお目にかかつてから妾はスツカリ恋の捕虜となつて了ひました。妾は神様から与へられた恋だと思つて居ります。恋を与へられた時は思ひきり恋を味はひつつ生るもので御座いませう。妾が師の君を恋ふる事は決して不合理でも不道徳でも御座いますまい。神様の御旨だと信ぜられてなりませぬ。厳粛な神聖な恋が変つて博愛となつた時は、尊さと偉大さと美しさとを知る事が出来ませう。ルートバハーの御教の人類愛は斯様な意味を云ふのではありますまいか。人類愛そのものを愛するの愛、それは神様の愛で、即ち自分を見出す為めの愛であり、自分自身を建設すべき天国に昇るべき愛の初めであり終りでありませう。師の君が妾を理解して下さらぬ事は実に絶大なる悲しみで御座います。妾もアメリカンコロニーに籍をおき、救世主の再臨を待ち、全世界救済の使命を持ち乍ら、どうして戯れの恋に浮かれて居れませうか。妾は師の君の手によつて新に生れなくてはならないのです。霊肉ともに復活せねばならぬのです。師の君と愛し愛され、貴方と結ぶ事によつて新に力を与へらるるので御座ります。もし此妾の恋愛が不合理だと仰有るのならば貴方の神力で取去つて下さいませ。とは云ふものの一度恋ひ慕ふた師の君の温い御顔とそのやさしいお言葉は妾の全身に流れて血となつて居ります』

§ 4

Speaker: 'I never dreamed that I would encounter a problem of love in this sacred place, having received the command of the Genshiyuku Kamisama, a place which is holy and must not be violated. However, it seems that the emotion of love is the same for people of any country. I do not have the Yūki to ever bury your Goshinsetsu. Nevertheless, even without forming a suspicious Kuwankei, if only our hearts melt into one another, is that not enough for love to be perfectly maintained? We who serve the Oshihe of Reishiyutaijiu... then, let us become lovers in a spiritual sense. Now, please, let go of that hand.'

Speaker: 'No, no, I wish to be held and comforted by the loving Mite of my teacher, Kimisama, day and night, forever. I want to be held in your precious embrace and sing of love while wearing a Hohoemi. ...Ah, may there be much happiness upon my teacher, Kimisama, whom I love and adore...'

Speaker: 'I am grateful for your Goshinsetsu, but please do not touch me until the Hiyakunichi of my Shiugiyō is completed. If suspicious thoughts arise, it will become a Jiyama to my training.'

Speaker: 'Do you not realize that a dangerous matter is approaching your Goshinpen? I am worried about that, and I cannot help it. Therefore, if you conclude an agreement with me, who holds the reins of the American Colony, it is only natural that you will escape your danger. The Jews are a race that is difficult to assimilate, you see.'

Speaker: 'Is there some danger approaching my person? Even if any kind of enemy comes, I have entrusted myself to Kamisama, so I am not surprised by such things; please be at ease first.'

Speaker: 'You are optimistic, but there are many demons surrounding you. Even if I try to follow the words of my teacher, Kimisama, and try to give up on love and adopt an attitude like that of a stranger passing on the roadside, I cannot do it. When I think of your Goshinpen, tears come and I cannot stop them. Therefore, I do not want to leave your side.'

Speaker: 'Mariya-sama, are you not threatening me by saying such things? I cannot help but think that you are trying to fulfill your desire for love by employing quite malicious means.'

Speaker: 'No, no, how could that be? As a Puritan who believes in the Oshihe of the true Kamisama, how could I tell a lie? The divine punishment of Kamisama is terrifying. I do not wish to leave your side, even if we do not make love, in order to protect the Goshinpen of my teacher, Kimisama. Since you came to this town of Jerusalem, many people have come to submit to you, calling you the Seishi-sama of Hinodewake, but I wonder how many people truly love you. Everyone seems to respect you with all their might, but I cannot help but be anxious. Also, since you have not been here long, you do not yet understand the local customs and human sentiments, you see.'

Speaker: 'Then, I will leave it to your opinion. Do as you wish. However, I would like to request a delay for these seventy days. Since I have accepted your request, you must also accept my request in return.'

Speaker: 'It cannot be helped. Then, I will endure. Please be careful not to let other women be your companions. Even if you meet Salome-sama, you must not exchange words with her. It would not do for danger to attack your Goshinpen because of that.'

Speaker: 'Ha ha ha ha! I understand now that you, Mariya-sama, have been saying from the beginning that demons are targeting my Goshinpen and that danger is attacking me. My, you are quite cunning... you are a woman, indeed. Ah ha ha ha!'

Speaker: 'Etsu he he he! Say whatever you like. However, please be very careful. Now, let us go home together.'

§ 5

Speaker: 'In that case, I shall take a turn around the mountain and return, so you, Anata, please go home Hitoashisaki. If only seventy days pass, I shall have you walk with me as a pair, like Rakuda, both day and night. Ahahaha!'

Speaker: 'If you are not pleased, let us return ahead. Please wait until the dawn breaks. Even a Yotaka shall come, perhaps.'

Leaving behind this Sutezerifu, she descends Kanranzan.

Watching her go, Burabāsa sighs and strokes her chest in relief, saying,

Speaker: 'Ah, what a troublesome thing this is. How shall I manage to overcome this Nankuwan? This, too, is likely a test from the Kamisama. Ah, Kamunagara Chihahe Mase, Kuniharutachinōokami-sama, I humbly pray for your Goshiyugo so that I may not fall into the Iuwaku of the Akuma. Please grant me Zetsutairiyoku, for I am weak of heart.'

Joining her Riyōte together and offering Haishiya to the Tenchi, she began to Shōeu all over the summit of Kanranzan. Before a small, weathered shrine, a single shadow is stirring. The moon, covered by a veil of Hakūn, casts a light of an Itsushiyu Iyō nature upon the Chijiyō, neither quite day nor night.
