Chapter 18: Sincerity と偽 [1824]

誠と偽〔1824〕

§ 1

In the second room of the Sōin hotel, Yamoriwake and Ayako are talking incessantly about something, whispering and murmuring to each other.

Yamoriwake: 'Look here, Ayako, did you not try to pull my kintama and strangle me to death yesterday? I cannot fathom why you would come to visit me again when you hate me so much. Did you come to demand payment for the last time, perhaps?'

Ayako: 'I came to visit you thinking that you were a man living a dokushin life, but because a bakemono-like woman was sleeping between us, I could not help but feel jealous, and I truly apologize for losing my head and forgetting myself. Please, I beg you to forgive me.'

Yamoriwake: 'Eh, very well, very well, what is past is past. That woman called Ohana is an inran hag who is not what she seems. I got into a strange relationship with her and found myself in a situation as if I had stuck my feet into a torimochioke. Fortunately, thanks to you coming and causing a kenkuwa, Ohana has given up, and I am actually rejoicing that it is for the best.'

Ayako: 'I had intended to spend the yūbe talking leisurely with you, and I even asked my oyakata-sama for time off and went to the trouble of coming here, but because of that commotion, I was separated from you, my beloved, and sent back to Ariakerō; the zannen I felt then! I could not sleep a wink all yūbe. Fortunately, since my father is injured and lying in bed, I begged my oyakata to let me go and nurse him, and only then was I able to come to your side, my beloved. You are truly a sinful man. Even though you are really Yamoriwake-sama, you deceived me so well by calling yourself Urushiwake. You have a deep relationship with Otora-sama, a relationship with Ohana-sama, and on top of that, you have established a relationship with me; you are a hero of womanizing and widow-seducing who delights in making women fret, so I truly cannot feel at ease. I have thought many times that I should cut ties with such a fickle man, but for some reason, you, my sinful beloved, are so dear, so dear, that I cannot forget you. Please, use me as a servant and keep me by your side. I will not harbor such desires as wanting to become your legal wife.'

Yamoriwake: 'What a splendid woman you are. The yaku of your father is a hopeless case, but I wonder why a splendid lady like you was born from the seed of such a man. It is exactly as if a sparrow had given birth to a hawk.'

Ayako: 'Ho ho ho, I am happy that you praise me, but if you disparage my father, who is the most important person, my stomach will be a little upset. You know, danna-sama.'

Yamoriwake: 'Ya, this is rude of me, I was truly wrong. If I were to make a woman like you my wife, it would be a lifetime of happiness. You might even become a model of a good wife and wise mother.'

Ayako: 'I do not wish to become what is called a good wife and wise mother today. Do not the sages of Tang, such as Confucius and Mencius, preach only the morality of women while saying nothing at all about men? In the first place, we must decide what kind of woman a good wife refers to, or else it is meaningless. Regarding this matter, even the teachers at the women's university would not be able to give an accurate explanation. They leave the husband to only have his evening drink and cook his rice, and call that a good wife, but if you ask me, it is something that forces one to be a faithful servant. There are many men who, when a child plays a prank, only scold the wife saying that her upbringing is bad, without understanding what a wise mother is.'

Yamoriwake: 'That is indeed so. You say very clever things. That is because you possess the qualifications to become a so-called good wife and wise mother. Oi, Ayako, there is no woman I love more than you in the three thousand worlds. How about this, let us cast aside all hardships from now on, become heroes of love, and become a model of a good couple?'

'As a Zenriyō wife, I shall serve you to the end, but if you, Danna-sama, keep so many Kuwashime, the Seken will not call you a Zenriyō husband.'

'That is true, I have indeed done a troublesome thing. That Shifunenbuka Otora and Ohana will not let things be as they are, and there is no Dauri in it; it would be better if they died in the Biyōin, it would be better indeed.'

'Danna-sama, how can you say such a Mujiyō thing? I was furious Yūbe, but after returning home and thinking it over, no matter how much I yearn for you, Danna-sama, with Otora-sama and Ohana-sama, who are so much older, here, it would not do for me, young as I am, to have you all to myself. No matter how much you, Danna-sama, love me, I feel sorry for the two of them. If I were to compete for love with my youth and Bibau, no matter how much Otora-sama and Ohana-sama might Kiba and struggle, I would not be able to endure it. I would surely take the Getsukeikuwan, but a Ningen cannot act in such a Wagamama and Katsute way. Please, bring harmony to Otora-sama and Ohana-sama, and live in Nakayo. I shall become a Gejiyo and serve you with Chiujitsu.'

Yamoriwake, astonished at the unexpected nature of Ayako's words, stared fixedly at her face and said,

'Oh, Ayako, you are truly a goddess. Moreover, a goddess of peace. I, too, shall completely reform my heart and become a Zenriyō husband, so please do not abandon me.'

'Yes, how could I abandon you? But Danna-sama, if I had reached the age of Ohana-sama, I might surely have been abandoned just like Ohana-sama. When I think of that, I feel so sorry for Ohana-sama and Otora-sama that I cannot bear it.'

While they were talking, it was Otora who came climbing up, guided by Tsūro, with a countenance like a Yasha.

'Yes, excuse me. The hated Otora has intruded for a moment, but please, even for a short while, I wish to have an audience with Yamoriwake-sama.'

So she stood blocking the entrance to Room 3 and shouted. Hearing this voice, Yamoriwake was terrified, pulled the bedding over his head, and said,

'Oh, this is terrible, Otora has come!'

While saying this, he was trembling all over.

'Ho ho ho, Danna-sama, how weak-hearted you are. Hasn't Otora-sama come to visit you? You have not committed any particular evil deed, so why not meet her squarely?'

'Oh, be quiet, be quiet. Tell her I am away and send her back, I beg you.'

'How can you tell such a lie when you are not away? In that case, shall I meet her in your stead?'

'No, no, you will be beaten again and end up having to go to the Biyōin. No one is a match for that mad old woman. Besides, if she sees your beautiful face, her horns of jealousy will grow even sharper, and who knows what she will do to you.'

'Ho ho ho, what are you saying? One woman and another woman, no matter how strong she is, it is not as if she is beyond measure. In that case, I will go and greet Otora-sama. Please find an opportunity to come out and greet her.'

Saying this, she opened the door and appeared gracefully in Room 3, where she saw Otora occupying a seat in front of the brazier, puffing away at her tobacco.

'Ah, is this Otora-sama? Thank you so much for coming to visit Danna-sama. As he is feeling a little unwell and resting, I shall offer you greetings in his stead. I am a Geisha named Ayako, who serves the Ariake family. By chance, I have been favored by Yamoriwake-sama and am being taken care of by him. Please do not abandon me, and I humbly ask for your kindness.'

She bows with both hands on the floor and offers an Ingin Aisatsu.

Otora: 'Are you the Ayako-sama of the highly renowned Ariakeya? The more I look at you, the more beautiful a child you are. It is no Muri that Yamoriwake-sama has fallen head over heels for you and become so infatuated.'

Ayako: 'As I am a woman of humble Sujiyō, I am normally unable to approach a personage such as yourself who performs the Goyō of the Kamisama, but I have been forgiven after being purified by Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi, and by being Minaho and Kikinaho. It seems my father was a great Goyakukai to you, and I offer you my Arigata Orei.'

Otora: 'Who is this person you call your father?'

Ayako: 'Yes, he is that good-for-nothing drunkard, the Yaku.'

Otora: 'My, what a Fushigi thing fate is. Now that you mention it, your eyes look very much like the Memoto of Yaku-sama. I wonder how such a man as Yaku-sama could have such a daughter. By the way, Ayako-sama, I hear rumors that you drove Ohana-sama away, and I felt truly delighted when I heard that. Please, do your utmost to eliminate that devil. It is for the sake of Yamoriwake-sama, after all.'

Ayako: 'Otora-sama, if I understand correctly, you had a very deep relationship with Yamoriwake-sama, beyond that of master and disciple; is that truly the case?'

Otora: 'It is true; we are a couple whose souls were joined by the Kamisama.'

Ayako: 'And did you yield him to Ohana-sama?'

Otora: 'I never yielded him. That wretch Ohana used various schemes to deceive Yamoriwake-sama and stole my man away. She is truly a hopeless harlot.'

Ayako: 'That must be very distressing for you. I sympathize with your feelings. It seems that I, too, have ended up stealing Yamoriwake-sama away.'

Otora: 'That is so. However, you did not take my man. Ohana took my man. And then you took him from Ohana. That is why, rather than resentment, I feel nothing but gratitude toward you. Because you took him, my heart has been cleared. Truly, in terms of your beauty, your style, and your cleverness, you are the perfect match for Yamoriwake-sama, ohohoho.'

Ayako: 'I am truly sorry; I am humbled.'

Otora: 'Now, Ayako-sama, I hear Ohana is hospitalized at the Hakua Hospital, but you must not lose even if she returns. Use your beauty and charm to bewitch Yamoriwake-sama, and turn his heart so that he does not give even a single glance toward Ohana. I will support you with all my might.'

Ayako: 'I have no face to show you.'

She hangs her head and hides her face. Yamoriwake had been observing the situation from earlier, but since Otora's tone was so calm, he finally felt relieved. He lets out a yawn and comes out into Room 3.

Yamoriwake: 'Oh, my, Otora-sama, thank you so much for coming. I have been having my testicles squeezed by that wretch Ohana, and as you can see, my face has been scratched and welts have formed, so I have been lying down. Please, do have some tea.'

Otora: 'Hohoho, what a strange face. You are being punished by heaven because you are too fickle. It is entirely because Hinodewake used Ohana's hands to discipline you. If you do not repent to a proper degree, something terrible will happen. And why on earth were you infatuated with such a dark-skinned woman as Ohana? You must have been bewitched by a devil.'

Ayako: 'It would be a nuisance to your conversation, so I will excuse myself for a moment. I shall go and visit my father in his hospital room.'

The truth is, even though you and I are doing this Senden every day, our Gunyōkin is not very Hōfu, so we cannot afford to rent a Ritsupa house. I have been sweet-talking Ohana and have reached the Nodomoto, and I am Hachikubu Seikō, so please do not be so flustered and just watch for a while. If you do that, you will understand my Seii, for no matter what one says, it is a world ruled by money.'

Otora: 'I see, I understand now. However, the fact that you exchanged nuptial cups is a bit suspicious, is it not?'

Yamoriwake: 'Who would ever exchange nuptial cups from the bottom of their heart? I only did that much to make Ohana feel at ease.'

Otora: 'Indeed, I never thought that such a foolish thing could happen to you, Yamoriwake-sama. The God of Hinode is truly great after all. Just as you said.'

Yamoriwake: 'Ha ha ha ha, it cannot be helped.'

I am using my Rikutōsanryaku to make her act as if she is in love with me, to stir up Ohana's competitive spirit, to make her attachment to me even stronger, and to make her throw out the ten thousand ryō so that I can secretly hand it over to you. How sweet it is.'

Otora: 'As expected of someone raised as a soldier, you are truly great when it comes to military strategy. Even the God of Hinode would surely be struck with awe at your divine schemes and strange tactics, Yamoriwake-sama. Ho ho ho ho.'
