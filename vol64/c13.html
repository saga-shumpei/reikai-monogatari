§ 1

Due to the riot yesterday, the Kenpei and police are looking with Chimanako, questioning every person Yukika, and spreading a Keikaimau to ensure no Shiyugishiya Iriko.

Yamoriwake, while wearing his Shinchō Yōfuku, arrived at a Chiten where the roof of the station was faintly visible, when one police officer approached him with a brisk gait.

Officer: 'Please wait a moment. I shall perform a Shintai Kensa.'

For Yamoriwake, more terrifying than the police or anything else was the pursuit of the Utagahibuka Ohana. Because he had been walking while looking around Achirakochira, he was suspected by the police and was successfully caught.

Yamoriwake: 'I am Yamoriwake. There is no reason for me to be searched by the police. Even so, I am a high-ranking Bukuwan of Hinodejima. If you do such Ranbau things, I shall complain to the Teikoku embassy, expose the atrocities of the Jerusalem station, and cause an international incident; release me. I have urgent business.'

Officer: 'There is no need to be so flustered, is there? Since I recognize your way of walking, looking around Achirakochira and being restless, as suspicious behavior, I shall search your Shintai for the time being.'

Yamoriwake: 'If you wish to search, you may do so. Take care that you do not suffer from a guilty conscience later, hen, you are making a fool of people.'

Saying this, he unbuttoned his clothes himself, and in the middle of the main street, he became stark naked, exposing his ochiko and the nubō of his pohora for all to see.

Officer: 'No, that is fine, eh, I apologize for the trouble. Please put on your clothes.'

Yamoriwake: 'Even if you do not tell me to put on my clothes, they are my clothes, I shall wear them as I please. Do not wag your chin unnecessarily.'

Saying this, he slipped into his Yōfuku and ran at full speed toward the brothel where the Shirakubi, whom he had glimpsed about ten days ago, was staying. When he became naked, he dropped his purse containing three thousand yen, and since he did not seem to notice, the police officer wondered if it might contain secret documents. Since he claimed to be a high-ranking Bukuwan of Hinodejima, he might be a military spy. Thinking that if it contained secret documents regarding military preparations, the police sergeant could instantly become a police inspector, and due to various such thoughts, he quietly picked it up and decided to take it back to the main station to examine the contents in front of the chief. Yamoriwake, feeling relieved that the purse with three thousand yen was surely in his pocket, climbed the stairs of the brothel with a haughty attitude, entered a room on the third floor with a good view, and was clapping his hands incessantly. Just as he thought he heard a high-pitched woman's voice from downstairs saying 'Hai,' soon after, the person who came up the stairs with a thumping sound was the fair-skinned beauty of seventeen or eighteen he had seen before. While Yamoriwake was suddenly wiping the foam from the corner of his mouth, removing eye mucus, and blowing his nose, he waited with a composed face, and then,

Woman: 'Customer, konchiwa, today arii...'

Yamoriwake: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, what is this today arii, I do not understand what you mean. I am not sugar, I am the incarnation of the soil of Sado. Will you not speak more clearly?'

Woman: 'Hai, as for me, even if I am called by the Prime Minister, even if I am invited by a Chiji-sama, or even if I am called by a Kazoku-sama, I get by with arii today. As for the officials of today, if they differ by one grade in rank, they are indeed very important people. When a Kacho-sama goes to the office of a Kyokucho-sama, he stands in a position of rigid attention, saying... ha, ha, ha, ha, and is thus respectful. The Kyokucho-sama, being a Kyokucho-sama, sits sprawled in his chair in an unseemly manner and says... how has the business of such and such gone, do it well... Then, the Kacho-sama says... haa, with an Ochi-ni-san style, and leaves looking terrified. When that Kyokucho-sama goes to the side of a Minister, he is even more respectful than the previous Kacho-sama. When it comes to the Prime Minister, he is quite imposing. That Prime Minister-sama calls me, his mistress, from time to time, but oh, when it comes to women, he is quite something. Even if I grab his pubic hair, pinch his nose, or grab his chin whiskers and make him go pakupaku, he loses his composure and says... Koriya Ayako, do not do such unreasonable things... and says that, so a geisha possesses absolute infinite authority. Therefore, please consider it a great honor that I said arii to you, customer.'

Just as he was saying this, the sound of footsteps climbing the stairs, ton-ton-ton, was heard.

Ayako: 'Okyaku-sama, it seems the sake you have been waiting for has arrived.'

Guest: 'Ya, that is gōki. Quickly, quickly, the hototogisu of Machikaneyama.'

Here, the two exchanged sake cups with chōchō-nannō and wasted time on trivial talk. Both Imoriwake and Ayako were peerless drinkers, and in the blink of an eye, they finished off seventy or eighty warmed sake bottles. Once Ayako got drunk, she was a woman with no restraint, and she was a formidable person who would set the table herself. Imoriwake was increasingly entering the state of laughter.

Urushiwake: 'Ha, I had forgotten the most important thing. To show my appreciation...'

While saying this, he put his hand into his pocket, but as his purse was not there, he was startled.

Urushiwake: 'Oh, this is a disaster, I have made a mistake...'

Ayako: 'Urushiwake-sama, have you forgotten something?'

Urushiwake: 'I dropped it... I am crestfallen... This is troublesome...'

Ayako: 'That is indeed a trouble. Why do you not report it to the police? There might be an honest finder who has turned it in.'

Urushiwake: 'In truth, when I was suspected by a policeman at the four-way intersection there and was subjected to a body search, now that I think of it, it seems it was already gone. I must have been picked by a thief somewhere. However, Ayako, even though I look like this, I am the son of a millionaire in my home province, so if I send a single telegram, it will be sent by telegraphic money order before a week passes. So, for the sake of the bond we have formed as husband and wife, could you please pay for the expenses here with your money?'

Ayako: 'Yes, since it is for you, who is no one else to me, I shall pay it. When the money arrives, please be sure to come back.'

Urushiwake: 'Very well, very well, how could I ever forget you? You are the one who saved me from suffering deep humiliation before this proprietress today, and furthermore, we have become inseparable, so how could I ever forget this? Ah, it cannot be helped. I am going to go take a look at the Mount of Olives for a moment, so please wait here for me.'

Ayako: 'Is there any need for you to go to such a place? Are you more in love with the Mount of Olives than being by my side?'

Urushiwake: 'What, how could that be? I do not want to leave your side even for a moment, but with nothing in my pocket, I cannot feel at ease while being taken care of, can I?'

Ayako: 'Between you and me, it does not matter if you stay for three or five days. Even if I have to pawn my clothes, I will support you for three or five days, so please, be at ease.'

Urushiwake: 'All right, then I shall take the liberty of troubling you for two more days. In fact, I have reserved Room Number One at the monastery hotel, so if I go there and stay, I can live for five or ten days even without money.'

Thus, the two opened their hearts to each other and became lovers, and as Ayako had said, Morimiyawake stayed for two nights and, around noon on the third day, returned to the monastery hotel with a nonchalant face.
