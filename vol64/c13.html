§ 1

Burabasa saw a Hitokage before the Miyashiro, and said:

Burabasa: 'The Monokage before the Miyashiro, I wish to ask who you might be. I am a propagandist who has left the Island of Hinode and come climbing up here.'

A Hitori man replied:

Hitori man: 'I am the Kamuzukasa who guards the American Colony, the Okina known as Subatsufuodo. The moon in the Ōzora is wrapped in Ahakumo, and I have Mimaga your Misugata, have I not?'

Burabasa: 'Anata, were you Subatsufuodo-sama? This is, this is, Shitsurei of me. For you to have come out at this Imagoro as a Gorautai, all alone without even a companion, there must be some Riiu for it, I suppose.'

Subatsufuodo: 'Nay, even though I have aged, my heart is Yahari that of the Jifuhachi of old. Without knowing where, I was stirred by Ainetsu and Hitsupa to this place. Ahahaha, no matter how much I try to Jiyunpō the path of the Ōkamisama and avoid setting my eyes upon women, the Kusemono called Shineniba that lurks in my heart manipulates a Goshiyaku Danshi with Jiiujizai. Humans are truly weak-willed creatures. In this world, even a brave general who can command three armies with a single gesture will have his bones and flesh crushed in an instant upon meeting the glance of a frail woman. Ahahaha.'

Burabasa felt a shock in his chest... Could this old man have heard his conversation with Mariya from earlier? That present remark was full of meaning; how embarrassing this is. To have the initiative taken by the old man like this leaves him with nothing to say. The punishment is immediate; why did he not reject Mariya's threats at that time? He is disgusted by his own weak will. No, no, it is not that his will is weak, it is because of the Kusemono in his heart. Because he left his wife and children on the Island of Hinode eight thousand miles away, and feeling the loneliness of being Hitori, he had forgotten to rely on the power of the absolute infinite God, and thus a devil rose up in his heart and he uttered such a weak, temporary, evasive lie. Ah, what a terrible thing he has done. How shall he answer this Okina...

He stands with his hands clasped, looking down.

Subatsufuodo: 'Ahahaha, Burabasa-sama, surely you would not use temporary, evasive words as a propagandist of Ruutobaha? Women are relatively honest creatures, so they take a man's words seriously. If they discover even a single word of falsehood in a man's speech, they will stake their lives on it. Have you not made a promise to some woman?'

Burabasa: 'Hai, er, what is it. I was forced by circumstances and made a promise for a moment. It is truly embarrassing. Did you hear our secret conversation in its entirety?'

Subatsufuodo: 'Ahahaha, though I have aged, my ears have not yet retired. Since you were speaking of love and negotiating in such a loud voice, I heard it as if it were right in front of me. I heard every detail. You were quite thought of, were you not? Ahahaha.'

Burabasa: 'It is truly troublesome. It was like a negotiation with a nine-sun-five-bu blade held to my throat, so I had no way to respond other than that, and I said things I did not mean. I would never, as a propagandist, feel attachment to a woman from my heart.'

Subatsufuodo: 'If that is the case, you lied to that Mariya-sama? Is that not truly outrageous? I had heard that the people of the Island of Hinode are liars and not to be trusted, but I thought that you, who propagate the path of truth, would not have even a speck of falsehood. If a propagandist is like this, then not a single person from the Island of Hinode can be trusted. How can a Savior appear from such a place? Ah, how disheartening it is.'

Speaker: 'No, no, I am by no means a liar just because I am from Hinodewake. I spoke of things that were not in my heart only because I had no choice but to help that woman. I made the promise against my will, wishing to give peace of mind, if only for a moment, to her who is consumed by the heat of love.'

Speaker: 'How can the Shingefu of the Jiyōjiyu of Shinsei be accomplished with such a weak will? You, too, are a person of weak will, contrary to your appearance. We Jews, having had our nation destroyed two thousand six hundred years ago and having become a people of Baukoku, were despised by the Jinrui of the Sekai like Dokai, and only after two thousand six hundred years of Shinku did we recover the land of Palestine granted by the Kamisama. Among the Jews, there is not one person of weak will like you.'

Speaker: 'Ah, I am humbled. Once told so, I have not a single word of excuse. From now on, I shall correct my heart, establish and uphold Makotohito, and cultivate a fervor and Shinkauriyoku that will not be outdone by the Jews.'

Speaker: 'You will not make the words you just said to Mariya-sama into waste paper, but will carry them out, will you not? It would be a serious matter indeed if you were to speak even a lie to a Jewish woman. For the sake of your own person and for the sake of the Way, I offer you this warning out of grandmotherly concern. In truth, ever since Mariya-sama met you, she has fallen into love, sighing morning and evening, a pitiful sight to behold; I thought to act as an intermediary, so I came ahead to listen to the negotiations between the two of you. I truly wish for you to keep your promise without fail. She is a woman of sincere heart with truly strong faith, so if you were to break your promise, it would be the same as deceiving the Kamisama. A calamity might suddenly befall your person as retribution. Come, please tell me clearly once more. If you do so, I shall instruct Mariya-sama for seventy days so that she does not hinder your practice.'

Burabāsa is pained at heart by the words of the old man from which there is no retreat, and is troubled, wondering what he should do. After a while, as a result of various deliberations, he speaks as if resolved:

Speaker: 'Yes, I will surely keep my promise. Please tell Mariya-sama to be at peace.'

Speaker: 'If that is the case, you must have been living a life of celibacy even in Hinodewake. If not, there would be no reason to make a promise even in jest, just because a woman fell in love. Just as Mariya yearns for you, if you have a wife, she must surely be yearning for you. As a missionary transmitting the Way of God, you would not commit such heartless, nay, unfaithful acts, would you?'

Burabāsa is at a dead end, unable to return a word, and is lost in thought, wondering if he should throw himself into the Sea of Galilee to escape the pain, as an excuse for his predicament. Subatsufuōdo laughs aloud and says:

Speaker: 'Ha ha ha! Even if you throw yourself into the Sea of Galilee, your sin of falsehood will not disappear. Now, what will you do?'

Speaker: 'Ah, it cannot be helped. I never dreamed I would fall into such a trap; it was wrong of me to deceive Mariya-sama and send her back just to escape for the moment. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemase. Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, please forgive me.'

He apologizes with tears. After a while, when he lifts his head, the old man from before is nowhere to be seen, and the moon is shining brightly upon the roof of the old shrine through the rents in the robes of pale clouds. Burabāsa, feeling suspicious, offers a prayer at the shrine, walks back along the path he originally came, and while lamenting his own darkness and ignorance, descends Mount Olive and heads toward the monastery hotel.

Incidentally, the old man who appeared before the shrine and looked like Subatsufuōdo was an incarnation of Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami.

§ 3

The Ōkami, wishing to refine the Mitama of Barabasa, appeared in a Kesau to bestow Kunkai upon him.

When he had returned to the Kabegiha of the Garden of Gethsemane, he encountered a tall, slender beauty with skin of absolute whiteness, wearing a pale white Katsugi, who was approaching in a hurry. As Barabasa stood still, opening his round eyes to gaze at her and wonder who the woman might be, she stepped forward without any Enriyoge, approached his side, and grasping Barabasa's hand in a Muzafusa manner, shook it two or three times.

Salome: 'You have returned quite early today, have you not? I thought you were still on the mountain and came in such a rush. Has Mariya-sama already returned?'

Upon hearing her voice, Barabasa knew it was Salome. And at the mention of Mariya's name, his heart leaped as it never had before, and his cheeks were dyed in crimson. Salome gripped his hand even more firmly.

Salome: 'As expected of the Sendenshi of Hinodewake, there is not a single person in the Shichiu of Jerusalem who does not know of your Gomeibau. I myself would wish to be by the side of a popular person like you, even if only for Itsutoki.'

『ヤア此奴は些怪しいぞ、化州だな。本当のサロメさまがどうしてこんなお転婆式の事を仰有るものか。大方金毛九尾白面の悪狐が瞞して居るのだらう。今山上で大神様に叱られて来た所だ』

『ヘン、何程甘く化たつて駄目だ。日の出島から選抜されて来るやうな、プロバガンディストだから其手には乗らないのだ。今に尻尾を現はしてやらう。ド狐奴』

と後の一言を雷の如く呶鳴りつけた。サロメは、

『オホヽヽヽ』
