Chapter 14: Flower 曇 [1820]

花曇〔1820〕

§ 1

Ohana, together with Yaku, sat in the next room in Tanza, waiting impatiently for the return of Yamoriwake.

Ohana: 'Say, Yaku-sama, Danna-sama said he was going to the religious independence movement and left here, and today marks the third day, yet his figure is still not to be seen; do you not think it is a bit suspicious? Perhaps, taking advantage of the fact that he is holding that money, he has slipped away to Otora-sama’s place, has he not?'

Yaku: 'What are you saying? Danna-sama is the last person to do such a foolish thing. He was so fed up with Otora-sama that he spoke of her as if she were a centipede.'

Ohana: 'Even so, when that Danna-sama lets out a yawn, it is dangerous. He has a habit of using that as a boundary line to turn over a new leaf. From now on, whenever Danna-sama goes out, you, Yaku, must follow him, even if you have to stay hidden.'

Yaku: 'It is no good to be suspicious between husband and wife until then. If a husband does not trust his wife and a wife does not trust her husband, they are not a true couple. Danna-sama is the last person to do such a foolish thing. I, Yaku, will certainly guarantee it.'

While they were worrying thus, led by a boy, a police officer from the Jerusalem station came and asked:

Police Officer: 'Is Yamoriwake-sama in?'

Seeing the figure of the police officer, Ohana thought:

Ohana: 'Ha! Perhaps the independence of the church is being permitted due to Yamoriwake’s movement. He must have come to investigate under secret orders from the higher authorities to report that. I must hold myself with sufficient dignity at this time.'

Having made up her mind, she suddenly pursed her lips and said:

Ohana: 'I am the daughter of Zion, the great savior who harbors the spirit of Konohanasakuya-hime-no-mikoto, the fleshly palace of Ayame-no-Ohana. Police-dono, come, come and investigate. There is no mistake that I am the daughter of Zion. Here, Yaku-dono, since Yamoriwake is absent, you should take my place and attend to the police-dono.'

Yaku: 'This is, this is, Police-sama, thank you for your hard work. Um, what is it? Has the permission for religious independence been granted? I, a person of low status, Yaku, have also been waiting for the good news with my neck craned.'

Police Officer: 'No, I have not come for such a matter. In fact, the evening before last, I encountered your master, Yamoriwake-dono, on the station street, and as the patrolling officer deemed his behavior suspicious and questioned him, it was determined that he was not a suspicious person, so he was immediately released. After that, as a purse containing a large sum of three thousand yen was found dropped, I have brought it to the main station. Upon examining it in the presence of the chief, a business card with "Yamoriwake" written on it, along with room numbers one, two, and three of the Monastery Hotel, was found inside, so I have come to return it after a thorough investigation. Please stamp this register. I have written the receipt here.'

Ohana, who had been pretending to be a god, heard this and suddenly broke her divine form, stepped briskly up to the police officer, and said:

Ohana: 'This is, this is, Police-sama, thank you so much for coming. For Yamoriwake to have dropped a large sum of three thousand yen, he must have thought he had no excuse and absconded somewhere. He is a good-natured person, no matter what one says. Yes, I have certainly received it. Please convey my regards to the chief-sama. Here, Yaku, will you not see him off to the gate?'

The police officer received the stamp on the receipt and, refusing Yaku’s escort, walked away quickly.

As Ohana grabbed Yaku and fretted, piling up and tearing down her worries about Yamoriwake’s situation, Yamoriwake came strolling back. Even Ohana, seeing Yamoriwake’s figure, braced herself, thinking, 'There is a scent of face powder; I must probe this fellow’s belly.'

Now that I have asked Danna-sama, you said it was Ayako, did you not? In this vast town of Jerusalem, there is no one else named Ayako but my own daughter. How truly it must be by the Goinnen of the Kami that I was allowed to save Danna-sama's Inochi.

Hey, Yaku-sama, I thought you were a Hitorimono, yet you have a daughter?

Heh heh, I have one Tafukumusume, but she does not treat me like a parent anymore, so we are in a state of Kandau-dōyō.

Oh, enough already, why don't you go to sleep, you Yaku fellow, you are noisy.

In the sacred Seijiyō of Aya-no-Takama, Tennin and Tenjiyo dance their dance. A beautiful Bijin like a flower, what is this talk of Ayako or dung? The 'Aya' of Ayako is the 'Aya' of the iris. The Hataorimushi of figured brocade has come to Jerusalem. No matter how great a Bijin in the Sekai may be, she cannot compare to Ayame-sama. She cannot compare to Ikimiya-sama. Dokkoisho, dokkoisho.

Ah, my head aches today, so everyone, let us sleep, let us sleep.

He covers himself with a Futon and snores a Karaibiki, wondering about the Keisei of Ohana. Ohana bows before the Shinzen, drawing a Mikuji and casting a hexagram, and she does not sleep a wink that night, staying awake until the cock crows. Ah, how will this Ketsukuwa settle? Will it be the arrival of a Baufū and Dotau, or a cataclysm of earth and heaven, or the sudden outbreak of a great catastrophe with thunder rumbling and earthquakes rolling? I ask you to watch closely and await the next chapter.
