§ 1

As Yamoriwake became increasingly intoxicated, he grew self-absorbed, forgot everything, and began to chatter.

Yamoriwake: 'Hey, Otora-sama, the reincarnation of Takahime, and Ohana-sama, the reincarnation of Matsukurokurohime, and Magahiko, the reincarnation of—well, Magahiko-sama himself, how about it? Otora-sama: 'My, Otora-sama, what shall we do? With our essential Tsūben in this state, is there anything we can do? Perhaps it would be a shortcut to go to Mount Zion and meet with Burabāsa instead.'

Ohana-sama: 'That will not do. Since the Lord God has said that once a word is spoken it shall never change, it would be a great offense for the people to change the schedule on their own.'

Otora-sama: 'Even so, if we wait for Yamoriwake to sober up, who knows how long it will take.'

Yamoriwake: 'Haha, it is no use, it is no use; you can see that even by looking at this newspaper, can't you? The credibility of Burabāsa is something significant. There is no way we can overthrow this. Besides, Dr. Subāru also called you a false savior, so no matter how hard you try, it is no good. That is why I am drinking beer in desperation. Hey Magahiko, you are not a man who cannot drink, so why don't you have one? Hey, they say the beast called the Kunko can see even the smallest insect at night, but when it is broad daylight, it is blinded and cannot even see a great mountain; but no matter what anyone says, Otora-sama is a golden-furred nine-tailed Kunko, so it is hopeless. Why don't you just give up already?'

With that, he sat down with a sullen look and began to grumble. Otora tried various ways to soothe and persuade him, and it was decided that they would pull Yamoriwake by the hand and head toward the American Colony on the outskirts of the town. Staggering in his walk, with his hands held by the two women as if the main road were too narrow, he finally arrived at the gate of the colony. Many men and women, both old and young, were staring with puzzled expressions.

Yamoriwake: 'Hey, excuse me. I am a person who has come all this way accompanying the savior Hinode-no-Kami from Hinodewake. Is there a person here called the peerless nice Maria-sama? And I heard in the Miyako Shimbun today that a handsome man named Jacob-sama is also here; if you are at home, I would like to meet you for a moment.'

Maria, hearing the commotion at the gate, stood out to see what was the matter and found four unfamiliar men and women standing at the gate, whispering about something. Maria had met Burabāsa and had learned quite a bit of the Hinodewake language. Therefore, she had come to understand the general meaning.

Maria: 'Where have you come from? Are you from China? Or from Korea? Judging by your appearance, you seem to be from a very distant place.'
