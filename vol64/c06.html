Chapter 6: 金酒結婚 [1812]

金酒結婚〔1812〕

§ 1

Yamoriwake, together with Ohana, escaped from Otora's Reijiyō and entered a cafe on a side street seven or eight chō away, thinking that once they had fled this far they could finally feel at ease, and pulling the reluctant Ohana by the hand, he forced her into the back room to enjoy a glass of sake.

Yamoriwake: 'Hey, woman—no, Ohana—just as we were in the middle of our essential wedding cup, that wretch Otora returned and ruined everything; it was as if a storm had blown through a forest of flowers in full bloom. It was utterly joyless. I spilled the sake you had so kindly poured for me onto my lap, and I feel so out of sorts that I cannot help it, so why don't we have a drink here to celebrate our wedding anew?'

Ohana: 'Indeed, even I, because your sake, which is the nectar of your affection, was poured in such a hurry, it all spilled out of my mouth and not even a third of it remained. We are safe now that we have come this far. Shall we take our time? It is the celebration of a lifetime, after all. However, I would be a little troubled if Otora-sama were to come chasing after us.'

Yamoriwake: 'What are you saying? She was sitting on her backside in a faint, so there is no chance of her coming here. Even if she did, what of it? A husband and wife are sharing a cup, so she has no right to make a fuss. If we worry about such things, we are living in a world of demons, and we would not be able to sit still for a single day. The path of the Gods is one thing, but for humans, the path of food, clothing, and shelter is also important, so we must act accordingly.'

Just as they were talking, a cafe waitress wearing a snow-white apron brought them coffee and said,

Waitress: 'Excuse me, customers, what would you like to have?'

Yamoriwake: 'Well, first of all, bring me a bottle of sake. And I would like some grilled eel, sea bream sashimi, and a clear soup, along with a single sake cup, if you please.'

Waitress: 'Will you not be calling for a geisha? If you like, sir, we have some suitable beauties available.'

Yamoriwake: 'Hmm, I suppose so.'

Ohana: 'Hey, mother, please be so kind as to arrange one for us. No matter what, he is still young, you see. If there is no geisha, the sake will not taste sweet enough.'

Yamoriwake: 'Well, if there is a need for it later, I shall ask. For now, just bring the sake.'

He said this while looking somewhat puffed up. The woman left for the front of the shop with quick steps. A dark cloud drifted over Ohana's face.

Ohana: 'Now look here, Yamoriwake-sama, shall we just drink one bottle and leave this place? Are you not really making a fool of me? Not even calling me madam, but calling me mother—it is too ridiculous to bear.'

Yamoriwake: 'Come now, is that not fine? It is even better to be seen as a mother. The older a person looks, the more value they have, you know.'

Ohana: 'Even so, you are making too much of a fool of people.'

Just as they were talking, the previous woman returned with the sake and side dishes prepared and said,

Waitress: 'Customers, I have kept you waiting a long time. If you have any further requests, please do not hesitate to clap your hands.'

Yamoriwake was captivated by the woman's fair complexion, her cool eyes, and her fresh, youthful appearance, and a bit of drool began to drip two or three sun from the corner of his mouth. Ohana, a brave woman when it came to such matters, could not let this pass; waiting for the woman to leave, she grabbed Yamoriwake by the lapels, shook him three or four times, and said,

Ohana: 'Hey you, are you making a fool of me? Drooping your unsightly eyes and drooling—do you like that kind of harlot that much? You have a rotten soul. Come, I shall have you cut your belly with this short sword.'

Yamoriwake: 'Now, now, wait a moment. It is troublesome when you misunderstand like that. The reason I drooled was because the worm in my throat was demanding the sake I had been waiting for. The reason I narrowed my eyes was also because I wanted to drink sake. Looking at the face of my wonderful, wonderful, virtue-filled, and flawless Ohana-sama, how could my heart wander elsewhere? You are a bit hysterical, which is a problem. It is troublesome to have you suspect me at every turn. Even Otora did not harbor such suspicions.'

Ohana: 'I suppose so; it must be that you favor O-Tora-sama. I am such a fool that I was deceived by you and lured out to a place like this. Oh, how terrifying! If I were to remain dazed and foolish with a man like you, the money I had painstakingly sent from my home would all be drunk away and taken for the bribery fees of a prostitute. Ah, I am glad I realized this in time. This must surely be because Otohime-sama of Riugū warned me, saying, "This man is not to be trusted; no matter how sweetly he speaks, do not be taken in by him, for you will not be able to repent in time." Ah, thank you, Otohime-sama. I was truly a fool. Boo-hoo-hoo.'

She sobs and sinks down.

Moriyamawake: 'Now, Ohana, it is troublesome for you to be so angry and sulky. Would it not be better to clear away your suspicions to a reasonable degree? If you take what I said while under the influence of alcohol and use it to pick a fight, how can even a brave naval lieutenant endure it? You should simply overlook and disregard what was said while drinking.'

Ohana: 'You have the nerve to say "while under the influence of alcohol" when you have not even taken a single sip yet.'

Moriyamawake: 'You are always singing the line, "If one can be satisfied just by looking at a face... then a drinker would get drunk just by looking at the barrel." However, that line is the exact opposite of reality. When I look at your face, my mind goes haywire. In the same way, when I look at a sake bottle, I become entranced and feel a sense of tipsy intoxication, so I would like you to understand that. When you speak so shrilly, the taste of the sake becomes unpleasant and I cannot help it.'

Ohana: 'That may be so, but even if you look at my wrinkled face, which is not the face of a cafe waitress, the sake will not taste delicious. Come now, please drink your sake slowly, pay the bill, and go home. It is painful for me to stay even for a moment in a place where I am treated like this. You are making me go through such a bitter experience, as if you were cutting my throat. Ah, if only I had not made this promise.'

Moriyamawake, becoming desperate and reckless, decided to throw away his ten thousand ryō and intentionally acted in a grand manner.

Moriyamawake: 'Ohana-sama, are you saying that you wish to cancel our marriage promise? No matter how much I may think you are dear, if you are going to express such a lack of affection as to say you wish you had never made the promise, then I suppose it is your intention to cancel it. It would be an honor for Moriyamawake to be abandoned and cast aside by Ohana-sama in the vastness of the ocean. Come, please break off our relationship. You need not hesitate at all.'

Ohana: 'Now, you impatient Moriyamawake-sama, who said I would break off our relationship? I did not make this promise with such shallow thoughts as to break it off now. You are only saying such things because you have grown tired of this Ohana and are trying to run away, aren't you?'

Moriyamawake: 'Do not talk nonsense; did you not say that you would run away from here? You heaped abuse upon me, telling me to go enjoy myself with a prostitute, and expressed your lack of affection toward this husband of yours. I am a soldier, and I do not say womanish things. I do not demand that you stay with me against your will if you dislike it. Whether this bond holds or not depends entirely on your own heart, does it not?'

Ohana: 'Oh, now I understand your sincerity. You will stay with me no matter what, won't you? You are truly so dear that it is hateful.'

She clings to Moriyamawake's shoulder.

Moriyamawake: 'Stop that, you are going to spill the sake. It does not look very decent. Look, look, I can hear the footsteps of the cafe waitress.'

Ohana: 'Hmph, what if she comes? Are we not a couple in the eyes of the world? It is thrilling to show the cafe waitress this auspicious and precious sight. They are really making a fool of people, calling me "mother" and such. Tell me, Moriyamawake-sama, even if heaven becomes earth and earth becomes heaven, and the three thousand worlds are destroyed without a trace, the flower and fruit of love between your heart and mine will never fade, will they?'

Yamoriwake: 'Hmm, that is true. Otora must be frantic by now, searching for me like a madwoman, but is it not truly exhilarating?'

Ohana: 'Did you not say that you would never speak of Otora again? It seems you still have lingering attachments, as they slip out at the tip of your tongue so often. Oh, how vexing!'

She pinches his cheek with all her might.

Yamoriwake: 'Stop this nonsense! Let go, let go, will you not let go?'

Ohana: 'I shall not let go until this cheek is torn off.'

She pulls even harder. Yamoriwake, with fluid dripping from his eyes, nose, and mouth, cries out in a low voice, 'Ouch, ouch, ouch, ouch!' Hearing the footsteps of a maid, Ohana lets go instantly.

Yamoriwake: 'How terrifying! You are a woman, are you not? I never knew you were such a woman until now. You are truly fierce.'

Ohana: 'Indeed I am. I am the formidable one nicknamed Ohana the Murderer. In my younger days, I had such a reputation that I killed many without even holding a blade.'

She speaks with a mocking air. A maid enters, saying:

Maid: 'Guests, would you like a refill?'

Yamoriwake hurriedly wipes the tears and nasal fluid from his face with a handkerchief and says:

Yamoriwake: 'Ah, anything is fine, so bring plenty. My logistics department is here in the form of my wife...'

Ohana: 'Please bring us plenty of hot sake and something simple to eat. Money is no object, but no geisha, please; my wife is here with me.'

The maid is startled and says:

Maid: 'Oh, my, was it you, Madam? I mistook you for his mother earlier, and I apologize. In that case, there will be no need for geisha. Ho, ho, ho!'

She laughs and leaves.

Yamoriwake: 'Now, Ohana, that was incredibly tactless. Since there is a mother-and-child age gap between you and me, it would have been fine if the maid had said that. Even if I look seventeen or eighteen in your eyes, to the world, I am an old woman with the backside of a sixty-year-old.'

Ohana: 'Do not say such false things as mother and child. Besides, if we say we are husband and wife, they will not bother us with annoying services like geisha.'

Yamoriwake: 'Indeed, you are quite right. Ohana, you are always one step ahead of me as an actor, heh, heh, heh!'

Outside, laborers are drinking sake from cups and talking without regard for their surroundings.

Laborer: 'Hey, Tonku, we pulled off a sweet deal, did we not? We went to help that old woman Otora and got twenty yen each. With this, we can drink sake comfortably for ten or twenty days. By the way, that man called Salamander or Lizard who is always with Otora, he might be Otora's lover. That woman Otora always keeps saying he is her disciple, but he is surely clinging to her. If we get proof of that and try to blackmail them, we could probably earn another twenty or thirty yen.'

Tonku: 'Hey, Teku, do not say such wasteful things. The other party is a god, is she not? Besides, the three of us were soundly defeated by Otora-sama's divine power, saved when we had no life left, and on top of that, we have been entrusted with a grave mission. If you want money, just ask Otora-sama and she will give you as much as you want. Did she not say at that time that she would give us as much as we wanted if we needed money?'

Teku: 'That is true. Well, let us squeeze it out of her slowly and bit by bit. By the way, where did Tsūro go?'

Tonku: 'Did he not go chasing after the tracks of the Yaku? He must have dashed off, thinking he would catch the Yaku, drag him before Otora-sama, and get his hands on the reward money.'

Teku: 'However, when I went to urinate in the back room earlier, the guest I caught a glimpse of looked just like Yamoriwake and Ohana-sama. Since Yamoriwake-sama is a man of many tricks, he might be doing something behind Otora-sama's back with Ohana-sama.'

Tonku: 'What, you say Ohana-sama and Yamoriwake are in the back? Ah, that is interesting. Come, that is another twenty yen.'

Saying this, Tonku and Teku sneak toward the back room.

『オイ、トンク、ぼろい事をやつたぢやないか。あのお寅婆アさまを助けに行つて大枚二十円づつ。これで十日や廿日は気楽に酒が呑めると云ふものぢや。時にあのお寅について居る、蠑螈とか蜥蜴とか云ふ男、あれはテツキリお寅のレコかも知れないよ。お寅の奴、何時も自分の弟子だ弟子だと吐してけつかるが、あれは屹度くつついてけつかるのだらう。その証拠を押えて一つ強請つてやつたら又二十円や三十円は儲かるだらうからなア』

『これテク、ソンナ勿体ない事を云ふな。先方は神様ぢやないか、おまけに吾等三人はお寅さまの神力に一耐りもなく打つ倒され、命の無い所を助けて貰ひ、其上重大の使命迄仰せつかつて居るのぢやないか。金が欲しかつたらお寅さまに云へば幾何でも呉れるよ。あの時も金が欲しけれや幾何でもやると云つたぢやないか』

『それやさうぢや、まあ悠くりとポツポツに絞り取る事にせうかい。時にツーロは何処に行きよつたのだらうかなア』

『彼奴は何だか、ヤクの跡を追ふておつかけて行つたぢやないか。ヤクを捉まへて、お寅さまの前に引きずり出し、褒美の金に有り付かうと思つて、抜目なく駆け出しよつたのだよ』

『併し、裏の座敷に一寸俺が最前小便しに行つた時、チラツと目についた客は、どうも守宮別とお花さまのやうだつたが、箸まめの守宮別さまの事だから、お寅さまの目を忍んで、お花さまと内証で、○○をやつて居るのぢやなからうかな』

『何、お花さまと守宮別が裏に居ると云ふのか、あゝそいつは面白い。サア復二十円だ』

と云ひ乍ら、トンク、テクの両人は裏座敷を指して忍び行く。
