Chapter 19: 笑拙種 [1825]

笑拙種〔1825〕

§ 1

Burabāsa and Mariya, the two of them, wishing to pray for the day of Christ's Sairin to come as soon as possible, joined their hands and sat in Tanza before the shrine on Kanranzan from the early morning, pouring their hearts into their prayers. Then, Yakobu and Sarome, the two of them, came climbing up while singing a song called the Voice of Muga, and seeing the figure of Burabāsa, Sarome said,

Sarome: 'Oh, my, my, it seems our Shinen is not shallow, for I have met you again in this Seichi. How have you been? I had been thinking I would like to visit you once, but after parting from you, I had moved to the Armenia region with Yakobu-sama, as the Seken is quite noisy. First of all, I am most happy to see your Gosauken face.'

Burabāsa: 'Oh, how rare this is. Sarome-sama, since then, we have been Uchita and I have been remiss in my Goshōsoku. First of all, it is most wonderful that you are also Gosauken. Some time ago, at a Shiyoten in Jerusalem, I saw the Shōsetsu you wrote titled "Hōō Tatsu ni Ten," and I felt as if I had met you in person. You have become quite Ojiyōzu, have you not?'

Sarome: 'Oh, I am embarrassed. Lately, times are bad and books do not sell, so every Shiyoten owner is complaining. If it were the usual times, they would give me a considerable amount of manuscript fees, but they give me only a mere pittance, so I cannot make a living by writing manuscripts.'

Burabāsa: 'Sarome-sama, Yakobu-sama, it has been a long time since I have seen you. I have read your Shōsetsu, but Mariya thinks that the material seems to have been taken with Kanranzan as the center.'

Sarome: 'Yes, in truth, I used the romance between you and Burabāsa-sama as the backbone, and tried to weave in the story of the hardships of myself and Yakobu-sama, but it does not go as I think, so I am truly embarrassed.'

Mariya: 'Sarome-sama, to use me and Burabāsa-sama as material, that is cruel.'

Sarome: 'That is mutual, you know. Even Burabāsa-sama published "Kusaka Kaizan," did he not? When I read that, my face turned bright red, and I do not know how much I had to be reserved toward Yakobu-sama, ho ho ho.'

Yakobu: 'No matter what one says, since only first-class writers are gathered here, we are always the ones being made a target. If only I had a knack for the pen, I would like to expose the not-so-simple relationship between Mariya-sama and Burabāsa-sama, ha ha ha. In truth, this Himegami-sama says she wants to climb Kanranzan once more to prepare material for a Shōsetsu, so I have come all the way from Armenia to see if there is any good seed. I arrived safely at Jerusalem Station at six this morning, rested for a moment at the Ariake house, and have just climbed up here now.'

Mariya: 'Oh, how dangerous, how dangerous, please do not write about me anymore.'

Yakobu: 'Even newspaper reporters need hush money, do they not? How much will you pay to Sarome?'

Mariya: 'This, I am humbled. I shall offer you about eight hundred million units of lies. Ho ho ho.'

Yakobu: 'Hey, Himegami-sama.'

Sarome: 'I dislike that, Yakobu-sama, calling me Himegami-sama. Why do you not call me Sarome?'

Yakobu: 'In that case, Sarome-sama.'

Sarome: 'Calling me -sama and such, I dislike that.'

Yakobu: 'In that case, is Kanranzan acceptable?'

Sarome: 'That is the pseudonym of Sarome.'

Mariya: 'Ho ho ho, how close you two are, it is just like a novel. I read that Konjiki Yasha by Kōyō Sanjin, and it is quite interesting; it is a structure where one is broken by love and wins by money.'

Sarome: 'That is read as Kōyō Sanjin, not Kōyō Sanjin. And I think it is more appropriate to call the name of that Shōsetsu Konjiki Yasha.'

Mariya: 'I am well aware of the Chiyoshiya's Meigi and the Yomikata of the Shiyomotsu, no matter how Mugaku a Mariya I may be, but I merely said it as a Chiyotsuto of a Shiyare, oh-ho-ho-ho.'

While they were talking thus, Ayako of the Ariakeya, bringing along a single box-bearer, climbed up gracefully.

Burabāsa, looking at her at a single glance:

Burabāsa: 'I say, Sarome-sama, the target-sama has come. She is a considerably superior beauty, is she not?'

Salome: 'Indeed, if she is a beauty of that degree, she must be quite popular. However, showing such a beauty to Jakobu-sama or Burabāsa-sama is poison to the eyes, isn't it, Mariya-sama?'

Mariya: 'That is certainly so, but that woman named Ayako is reputedly a heavy drinker, and once she gets drunk, she loses all sense of before and after and shows her ugly side. However, her sense of obligation is reputed to be the first in Jerusalem.'

Salome: 'If you have heard anything concerning Ayako, would you not let Sarome hear it?'

Mariya: 'I have heard a great deal. Concerning her relationship with Imoriwake-sama, who has come from Sun-Rise Island, there is said to be an Omoshiro romance. That man named Imoriwake is a hopeless person when it comes to women, and far from a triangular relationship, he is said to be enacting a quadrilateral relationship.'

Salome: 'Oh, that must be Omoshiro. Shall Sarome also try to investigate a bit?'

Jakobu: 'It seems that Ayako is also approaching this shrine to pray, so shall we not step back into the shadow of the nearby trees?'

Jakobu sneaks into the shadow of the trees.

Salome: 'That would be fine.' At this, the whole party approached the dense shadow of an olive tree about ten ken away, broke off the twigs of the olive tree to make a mat, and set their hips down here. Ayako of the Ariakeya, without any suspicion and without heeding her surroundings, began to offer her prayer before the shrine.

Ayako: 'O God, I have committed Taihen sins. Please forgive me. Having said that, by no means can I cut off my thoughts of that man. However, that man has two ardent female lovers who have crossed the slope of fifty, so I can by no means contend against them. Nor do I wish to trouble others by contending to gain victory for myself, but I pray that the three women may understand one another from the bottom of their hearts and protect Imoriwake-sama, and that I may never be abandoned by Imoriwake to the very end. Also, my father Yaku suffered an injury and is lying at the Catholic Monastery Hotel, so I pray that he may quickly receive grace and return to his original healthy body. Furthermore, Ayame no Ohana-sama is also currently hospitalized, but I pray that she may recover her full health as quickly as possible; for my sake, Ohana-sama met with such an ordeal. Also, it is my sin that I led Imoriwake-sama's heart astray. Please forgive this as well.'

While she was thus praying, Ohana, who was supposed to be hospitalized and suffering, climbed up hastily while leaning on a walking stick with relatively energetic vigor, and seeing the figure of Ayako fervently praying... What a strange woman is there... she cocked her head and pondered, but realizing it was Ayako of the Ariakeya, she flared up in a rush of blood to her head, placed her hand on her neck, hoisted her up like pinching a cat, clenched her right fist, and struck her repeatedly, thud-thud.

と小面憎相に又腮をしやくつて見せる。綾子は此間にお花の隙を伺ひ、逸早く箱屋と共に、木蔭へ身を隠して了つた。お花は綾子の姿が見えなくなつたのに気がつき、

『ヤア、すべた奴、何処に逃げよつた。生首引抜かねばおかぬ……』

と地団太ふみ乍ら、四人の止むるのもふり放し、一生懸命、髪ふり乱し、西坂をトントントンと地響うたせ乍ら降り行く。

四人は一度に岩石でも砕けた様な調子で『ワハツハヽヽヽ』と笑ひこける。橄欖山の木の茂みから山鳩が『ウツフ　ウツフ、ウツフヽヽヽ』と啼いてゐる。
