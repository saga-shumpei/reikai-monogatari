Chapter 3: Grass 居 Valley 底 [1809]

草居谷底〔1809〕

§ 1

Tonku, Teku, and Tsūro, the Sannin, carried the Futari from the back entrance of the Sōin hotel, running with all their might regardless of the Dauro or the Tahata, and climbing deep into the valley of Kidron along the Kahabe, they carried them to their own Kariya that barely shielded them from the Uro, and threw the Futari roughly onto the ground. During this time, Yamoriwake had almost sobered up, his round eyes bulging, and having said only 'Un', he glared at the faces of the Sannin. Otora, being a Kachiki woman, endured the pain while bruising her Koshibone when she was thrown onto the Daichi, and said:

Otora: 'Look here, you three Arabs, to bring the Nikutai of Otora-sama, the Savior of the Sanzensekai, the Great Miroku of the Sokotsuihane, and the Futobashira of the Kami of Hinode, here as a Mikoshi is fine, but why are you not a little more careful when setting me down? The Mikoshi has suffered a little Itan, you Bakayarau who know neither Giyōgi nor Sahafu. Now that you have brought me here, there must be some deep Takumi behind it. You had better confess clearly. I shall hear the reason for whom you were requested. Hey, Yamoriwake-sama, will you not pull yourself together? What kind of Gōriki is this for? That is why I told you not to drink sake so recklessly. You, in your own right, what is the Ikumiya of the Otohime-sama of Ryūgū doing? You are all such thoughtless porters. Hey, Arab, will you not confess before the Kami quickly? Shall I have it so that you cannot stand on your legs and loins due to divine punishment?'

Tonku: 'Ha ha ha ha. You crazy old woman, what are you spitting out? We were not requested by anyone. The money you have in your bosom is our objective. Since even the judgment of hell depends on money, you had better hand it over quickly. If you dally, your life will be forfeit. The other fellow is so drunk on sake that his legs and loins cannot stand. To twist and crush a mere old woman is but a task for the early evening. If you do not hand over the money, so be it. I will take it as I please. Now, prepare yourself.'

He stretched out his monkey-like arms and grabbed her chest tightly. At that instant, Otora raised her leg and kicked Tonku's testicles with all her might. Tonku said only 'Uun' and collapsed onto the ground in the shape of a large character. Teku and Tsūro were startled and tried to revive Tonku. Seeing that opening, Yamoriwake pulled Teku's legs and knocked him down face-first with a thud. Otora grabbed Tsūro by the nape of the neck and, hardening her knuckles, struck and beat him six or seven times. Tsūro felt his eyes swim and collapsed there once again.

Otora: 'Now, Yamoriwake-sama, take the vine there and tie up everyone, this one and that one, while you can. From now on, we must interrogate these three strictly and make them confess the conspiracy of Burabāsa. How pleasant this is; as expected of the Ikumiya of the Kami of Hinode, it is truly magnificent, is it not? Hey, Yamoriwake-sama, you must be impressed by the Ikumiya of the Kami of Hinode, right? Even if a thousand or ten thousand Arabs come, the moment this Otora snorts with a "humph", everyone will fall down in a mess, just like the great wind of the two hundred and tenth day blowing through the barracks of Tōkyō. Since the Kami of Hinode came to the Seichi, he has gradually risen in the world and acquired such great divine virtue. Now that this is the case, there will not be a single person who will criticize me even if I call myself the Savior of the Sanzensekai, he he he he. How truly delightful it is. After bitterness comes joy; they say one reaches the capital of victory by overcoming all difficulties. You too, stop drinking sake a little and listen to what I say from the bottom of your heart. What kind of foolish speech are you giving at the Sōin hotel? You said you do not trust me as much as Burabāsa, did you not? With such thoughts held by the chief of staff, who is the most essential person, how can such a great ambition be achieved? Take heed a little. If you do not listen to what I say, I will twist your nose, though I am not Otora-sama of Kokitayama.'

§ 2

Tonku: 'I have already undergone Kaishin. Since the Asagahogata Chiyoku has been smashed to pieces, I cannot endure it, ufu-fu-fu.'

Tonku began to groan, 'Un, un.' Otora gripped Tonku's topknot tightly.

Otora: 'Hey, Tonku, have you submitted? Have you reached Ōjiyō? How is it? Come, confess who asked you to do this.'

Tonku: 'Yes, ha-ha, I will confess. Please let go of that hand; my hair is coming out.'

Otora: 'What if it comes out? It is your own head, is it not? I intend to take it off along with the flesh. Confess if you are in pain. If there is Uogokoro, there is Mizugokoro. If you only confess, I will have Kamunahohi and Ōnahohi examine and hear your past sins and forgive them. And since I will give you money as a reward for confessing, you had better confess quickly. Though I may look like this, this Otora is neither a tiger nor a wolf, but an Ikigamisama who saves the Ujiko of the Sanzensekai. While I am an ogre or an Orochi to those who come as enemies, I am a Ketsukō, Ketsukō Daikuwankiten Megamisama to those who follow me.'

Tonku: 'Yes, I will confess. In return, please untie the bonds of Teku and Tsūro. It is of no Shikata if only I am saved.'

Otora: 'Hey, hey, Yamoriwake-sama, what is this? You are just continuing to smoke Tabako so comfortably, will you not help me a little? You are a man who lacks wit.'

Yamoriwake: 'It is Tautei impossible for a human to help a Kamisama. Well, I shall just watch at my leisure. How foolish. Can one watch such a thing without even sake? Otora-sama, you are quite fierce as well.'

Otora: 'It is indeed fierce, for I intend to take the fierce livers of three Arabu fierce men and cook them in a fierce manner. I am a very miraculous Kamisama. Hey, Arabu Tonku, it is enough to give the gist, so why will you not quickly confess who asked you? Is it better to have your life taken? Or is it better to receive money? How is it?'

Tonku: 'The truth is, I was asked by Yaku-sama, who is always coming and going to your place. Yaku-sama gave us three twenty ryō each. And he said that when Otora-sama became dangerous, we should snatch you up and escape somewhere from behind the monastery.'

Otora: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, how sweet, you have been coached to the very end. That fellow Burabāsa is using a strategy of deception and self-injury, and by saying you were asked by Yaku, he is trying to make this Otora fight with a devoted believer. He is a scoundrel who cannot be let out of one's sight for a moment. Hey, Tonku, tell the truth. It was not Yaku-sama, was it?'

Tonku: 'No, I am telling you the honest truth. Yaku-sama, who serves as the receptionist at the Spirit Castle, entered among the audience when you were giving a speech at the monastery. Because the audience's mood was very bad and there was a murderous atmosphere, and there were those who were secretly plotting to kill you, Yaku-sama was worried. He secretly put twenty yen each into the pockets of us three who were nearby and whispered, "Please carry Otora-sama and Yamoriwake-sama and escape from this place. If you do not, the lives of the two of you will be in danger." So, for the sake of earning twenty yen, we carried you two out. Then, since it seemed like you had a great deal of money in your pocket, clattering about, we thought we would make a double profit after coming this far, so we tried to act a bit rough. Please, somehow, forgive us. However, if it were not for us three, your life might not have been spared. This is the honest, genuine, and unexaggerated confession.'

Otora: 'Yes, indeed, please let go of my head, it hurts and I cannot help it. Aita-ta-ta-ta, it hurts!'

Otora: 'Thinking your head was Burabasa because I was so engrossed in the conversation, I am sorry I hurt you with all my might; well, please endure it. This is also a misfortune of the times. Now, now, Yamoriwake-sama, since the matter is understood, please untie Teku and Tsūro. One cannot understand things without listening to the story; it was truly pitiful. Having said that, the crime of the three of you suddenly threatening this Otora cannot be forgiven, so you cannot be angry just because you met with a painful experience; let this be a clean slate. Now, let us consult thoroughly with you three from here on.'

Tonku: 'We three are cunning when it comes to evil, so we will join in any consultation if it leads to money. I also have deep, deep resentment toward that Burabasa. We three had gathered to take Mariya by force, thinking to hold a Nenbutsukau and enjoy ourselves, but because that Burabasa came along singing a song of no use, the play we had painstakingly set up was ruined at the crucial moment, and our desires for Ochiko, Utsutoko, and Hatena were not fulfilled. We returned to our home dejectedly, and the son would not agree to it, becoming Ochiko and Koteno, and the five-man group set up a mast and caused a great commotion in the middle of the night, with muddy water coming out, he-he-he-he-n, oh, it was truly an absurd affair, aha-ha-ha!'

Otora: 'Ufu-fu-fu, with such thick lips and a pitch-black face, you are quite gluttonous, eating a bit too much. However, if there is such a history and resentment remaining toward Burabasa and Mariya, how about this: join our group of the opposition, and since I will give you as much money as you want, would you not like to go out into the town of Jerusalem and give a great speech attacking Burabasa?'

Tonku: 'Yes, that is agreeable and acceptable, we will do it grandly. Say, Teku, Tsūro, you two agree as well, right?'

Teku: 'Yes, that is reasonable. It is a thrill to peel the skin off Burabasa, the rival in love for Teku-sama, and make it so he cannot stay in this town of Jerusalem; it is a relief, it is as if my bile has gone down. Very well, it is interesting, interesting, like the belly-drumming of an interesting tanuki. Come, Tsūro, no matter how painful it is, shall we not walk the streets of Jerusalem in all directions, display our eloquence in Furuna, and spread the word so that the citizens understand Burabasa’s crimes?'

Tsūro: 'Yeah, that is interesting. Shall we look up to the living palace of the Great Miroku, Otora-sama of the Hinodewake, as our Great General, and Yamoriwake-sama as our Chief of Staff, and set out to assault the town of Jerusalem from all sides?'

Otora: 'Now, now, Tonku-sama, you must never say that you were asked by Yaku. You must say in a loud voice that you did such bad things because you were asked by Burabasa, but that you were afraid of the divine power of the God of Hinodewake and confessed to atone for your sins. Do you understand?'

Tonku: 'Yes, it is difficult and a bit troublesome to speak while twisting the facts, but well, we shall attack Burabasa as much as possible for your sake.'

Otora: 'Though I hold no heart of hatred for Burabasa, / I think to bury him for the sake of the world. / I shall drive Mariya-hime, who defiled the holy place with lust, / A thousand leagues away to see.'

Yamoriwake: 'If only the Medicine Buddha were here, / No matter the trouble, it would not be painful. / When I drink sake, my heart is always like a mountain in spring, / And flowers bloom while I laugh. / Otora-sama, I do not care for flowers, / My favorites are wine and beer. / Christ, Shakyamuni, Confucius, and the gods, / Are all worthless fellows compared to sake.'

Otora: 'Once again, Yamoriwake-sama, your blasphemy, / Even your bowels are rotting away.'

Yamoriwake: 'Even when rotten, sake and sea bream taste good, / And it is even better if you drink before they rot. / The scent of rotten sake is better / Than the scent of a rotten pond mussel.'

Otora: 'With a pine mushroom eaten by bugs dangling between your legs, / What are you calling a rotten shellfish?'

Tonku: 'Three or five stinking fellows gathered, / Holding a stinking consultation at the bottom of the valley.'

Teku: 'Otora-sama repeats the threads of her tricks, / Intending to pierce Mariya’s belly.'

Tsūro: 'Even if it is painful, for her sake I must go to town, / And give a hateful speech.'

Otora: 'Now, let us quickly leave this place and return, / O-hana is likely waiting at the spirit castle.'

Saying this, the party of five returns, pointing toward their encampment.

お寅『ブラバーサ憎む心はなけれども 世人のために葬らむとぞ思う。 聖場を色で汚したマリヤ姫を 千里の外に逐やりて見む』

守宮『吾は唯薬師如来がましまさば 如何な悩みも苦しからまじ。 酒呑めばいつも心は春の山 笑ひ乍らに花は咲くなり。 お寅さまお花さまでは気が行かぬ 蒲萄酒ビール酒が好物。 キリストも釈迦も孔子も神さまも 酒に比べりやしやうもない奴』

お寅『又しても守宮別さまの罰当り 腸までが腐つて居るぞや』

守宮『くさつても酒と鯛とは味がよい 腐らぬ先に呑めばなほよし。 ドブ貝の腐つたやうな香ひより 酒の腐つた香がよろしい』

お寅『虫の喰た松茸股にぶらさげて 腐れ貝とは何を云ふぞや』

トンク『くさいやつ三人五人集まつて 臭い相談谷底でするも』

テク『お寅さま手管の糸を繰返し マリヤの腹を突かむとぞする』

ツーロ『つらうても彼の為めなら町に出て 嫌な演説せねばなるまい』

お寅は、

『さア早く此場を立つて帰りませう お花が霊城に待つて居るだろ』

と云ひ乍ら、吾営所を指して一行五人帰り行く。
