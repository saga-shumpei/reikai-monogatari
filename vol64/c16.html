Chapter 16: 誤辛折 [1822]

誤辛折〔1822〕

§ 1

At the end of the narrow Roji of the Turkish inn, in the Reijiyō of Miroku that Otora had established, Tonku and Teku, together with Otora, were huddled together, three heads close, indulging in a whispered conversation.

Otora: 'Now, Tonku-sama, do you truly have no idea where that Yamoriwake-sama and Ayame-no-Ohana have gone?'

Tonku: 'Well, they are like smoke, like Mamono, so I have absolutely no Kentau of their whereabouts. However, rumor has it that Ohana-sama and Yamoriwake-sama have made a Fūfu Yakusoku. I heard this story at the Jifujigaitō from someone the night before last, and I intended to report it to you Satsusoku, but as Ikimiyasama was in a state of Gobiyōchiu, I actually held back, fearing it would cause you further anxiety.'

Otora changed her Kahoiro and said,

Otora: 'What, the two of them are married? Tell me, is that really true? It cannot possibly be true.'

Tonku: 'No, no, to tell you the truth, Anata, what should I say. The two of you, what should I say. Did you not encounter them, Yamoriwake and Ohana-sama, walking hand in hand, and in a fit of Haratachi, did you not Sotsutau? What I said about hearing it as a rumor was actually just Shiyōguwatsu Kotoba; in reality, I myself witnessed them walking together looking intimate. Right, Teku, that was the case, was it not?'

Teku: 'Yes, that is exactly right. At that time, Ikimiyasama was struck with a sudden fit of rage, became dizzy, and collapsed to the ground, did she not? The police doctor came, the crowd gathered like a mountain, and it was such a chaotic scene. It was only with great effort that Ikimiyasama regained consciousness, and we returned here while guarding Ikimiyasama in three rickshaws.'

Otora: 'Indeed, hearing that, it comes back to my memory as if in a dream. How strange, have I completely forgotten such a major problem until now?'

Tonku: 'That is certainly so, it was a severe fever. Until yesterday, you were only speaking nonsense, and we two do not know how much we have nursed you.'

Otora: 'Indeed, that hateful, hateful wretch Ayame-no-Ohana. After receiving such earnest education for ten years, to bite the hand of me, her master, and on top of that, to steal away another woman's man—she is a creature inferior even to a dog or beast. Can I let this pass as it is? Even if the two of them run to the ends of the heavens or burrow into the earth, as long as this life of Ikimiyasama remains, I will break through rocks to find them, and I will not rest until I have their severed heads.'

With her face flushed crimson, she clenched her fists, struck her own chest two or three times, and once again collapsed flat.

Tonku: 'Hey, Teku, what shall we do? I wonder if she will end up going insane in the end.'

Teku: 'Well, that is why I warned you not to speak of Yamoriwake and Ohana, but because you, Tonku, spoke so thoughtlessly, it has come to this. Is it not troublesome that a man has such a loose tongue?'

Tonku: 'Even if you say that, there was no way to keep it hidden forever, and I thought her spirit had stabilized quite a bit, so I just mentioned it. Even I would not have spoken so carelessly had I known it would come to this.'

Teku: 'At any rate, shall we draw some cold water and cool her head? If she were to die in a place like this, it would be troublesome if the police suspected us of having killed her.'

Tonku: 'Would it not be better to just run away now, Tonku-tonku, Teku-teku? It seems it will be hopeless anyway.'

Teku: 'Do not say such stupid things. If we were to do that, we would only be suspected all the more. They say that even sharing the shade of a tree or drawing water from the same river implies a deep connection. How could we abandon Otora-sama, who has provided for us even for three days? If we were to do such an ungrateful thing, would it not bring shame upon the Arab party? How could we, who pride ourselves on being Puritans of Islam whose principle is absolute obedience, ever do such a thing? For the sun in heaven would never forgive us.'

That is a good point you have realized. Come, go quickly. I will take charge of everything else.

Very well, then I shall go and offer my prayers now.

Saying this, he walks up toward Kanranzan, bathed in the setting sun. When he arrives before the shrine on the mountain, he sees Burabāsa praying with all his might for something. Tonku approaches him and says:

Tonku: 'Excuse me, are you not a Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō?'

Burabāsa: 'Yes, that is so. Are you not Tonku-sama? I was terribly rude to you some time ago.'

Tonku: 'Oh no, I am humbled by your greeting. It was entirely my fault, so please, I beg you, do not speak of such things anymore.'

Burabāsa: 'By the way, is Otora-sama in good health?'

Tonku: 'Yes, thank you. To tell you the truth, Otora-sama, Ohana-sama, and Yamoriwake-sama had a great Ōgenkuwa, and in the end, Yamoriwake-sama said something about marrying Ohana-sama, and they took each other by the hand and ran out of the Reijiyō to spite us. Ikimiya-sama's anger was beyond words. Then, that fellow Yaku, who was at the reception, unleashed a sharp tongue while Ikimiya-sama was already distressed. Just as Ikimiya-sama was about to strike Yaku, that fellow Yaku grabbed a garden broom and dashed out, and on the way, he struck Ikimiya-sama's face with the muddy broom and added all sorts of insults. Ikimiya-sama, who has a short temper, finally became hysterical, and that has led to her current fever, and she is doing nothing but complaining. In this state, I fear her life is uncertain, so I have left Teku to look after her, and I have come to this shrine to pray. Oh, I am truly worried and in a difficult spot.'

Burabāsa: 'Hearing your story, it is truly a pitiful situation. I cannot simply hear this and ignore it, so putting aside our usual relations, I shall go to the Reijiyō. And I shall pray that she may recover as quickly as possible.'

Tonku: 'Yes, thank you for your kindness, but she has always cursed you as if you were an enemy, so if she sees you arrive, she may become even more hysterical, and it would be a disaster if things got out of control, so your kindness might actually be counterproductive. If possible, I would like to decline your visit.'

Burabāsa: 'Ha ha ha, you are very cautious. However, human beings are not like that. It is the duty of humans to help one another when disaster strikes. No matter how strong-willed Otora-sama may be, there is no reason for her to treat my kindness in vain. She will surely be pleased. And I would like to take this opportunity to soften Otora-sama's heart; we are both humans who came from the same Hinodewake, and I wish to see the dawn of harmony, so I insist on visiting.'

Tonku: 'Well, I am truly grateful for your intentions. However, I do not know anything about this, so please do not tell Ikimiya-sama that you heard about her illness from me, as that would be a problem. You must act as if you came on your own accord, or else the aftermath will be troublesome.'

『きまつた事ですよ、三千世界の救世主、底津岩根の大弥勒の生身魂、日出神の生宮を認めない様な妄昧頑固の身魂を何うして愛する事が出来ませうぞ。日の出様が一生懸命に艱難辛苦を遊ばして、立派な立派な、結構な、心易い、暮しよい、みろくの大御代を建てようと遊ばしてるのに、悪魂の変性女子にとぼけて、此世を乱さうと憂身をやつしてゐるお前さまだもの、之位な大きな敵は世界にありませぬぞや。此神は従うて来れば誠に結構な愛のある神なれど、敵対うて来る身魂には鬼か大蛇のやうになる神ざぞえ。お前さまの心一つで楽に立派に御用致さうと、苦しみてもがいて地獄落の悪魔の用を致さうと、心次第で何うでもなるですよ。コンナ事が分らぬやうで、ヘン、宣伝使などと、能う言はれたものですワイ。改心なされ、足元から鳥が立ちますぞや』

§ 4

'Hai, Arigata-u gozaimasu. I shall come to visit again later, so sayō-nara.'

Ikimiya: 'Ho ho ho ho, at last, that Yachimataninsoku fellow, struck by the Goikuō of Ikimiya-sama, has fled with his tail between his legs, looking as miserable as a rat fallen into a ditch. Ho ho ho, hey, he talks with such an Erasau air about Teku and Burabāsa, but once he faces me, he is not worth even Sanmon. He has fled for his life just like a Tefutefu pinned down by a broom, hee hee hee.'

Teku: 'Moshi Ikimiya-sama, that is terrible, even I was astonished, Teku.'

Ikimiya: 'It must be terrible. Even you would be astonished, would you not? That senile Shōbenshi, look at the way he fled. That is why I tell you to believe in the Shinriki of this Ikimiya.'

Teku: 'Ikimiya-sama, is that not wrong? You were in a state of Jinjifusei until just now, and Burabāsa-sama appeared and emitted Goshiki Reikuō from his Shitō to heal your Biyōki, did he not? And you, to say that your Biyōki has been cured since Yufube, how could you tell such a lie? I am saying that I am astonished at your Yarikuchi, with your strong Gamanshin.'

Ikimiya: 'Be silent. You are but an Arab darkie, how could you possibly understand the Gokeirin of the Shinkai? If you say such things, those who would oppose this Ikimiya shall be sent home at once. Ah, my Kibun is bad. Hey, hey, the whole place has become full of Akuma. Here, Teku, bring some salt; there is an Akuma possessing your body, and I shall sweep it away cleanly from now.'

Teku: 'No, that is Ketsukō enough.'

As he said this, Tonku came returning, making the narrow path of the alleyway resound with his heavy footsteps,

Tonku: 'Ya, this is, this is, Ikimiya-sama, when did you become so well? I was worried, so I ordered Teku to look after you, and I have been to the shrine at Jerusalem to pray for your recovery from your Biyōki. Truly, the divine virtue of the gods is high, is it not?'

Ikimiya: 'That is very kind of you, Arigata-u... you would be pleased if I said that, but hmph, who asked you to do such a thing? Ikimiya, the great Miroku, the savior of the three thousand worlds, Ikimiya-sama of the Hinodewake, where is there a god who could cure the Biyōki of her physical body? You had better stop acting senile.'

Tonku: 'Oi, Teku, is this not a bit strange? She must be delirious from her Biyōki, must she not?'

Teku: 'Now, now, do not be so noisy; there is no end to it no matter how long you talk. No matter what is said, she is the savior of the three thousand worlds, so it is enough just to obey her Mei and follow her.'

While saying this, he signaled with his eyes that one should not engage her too much. Otora pulled the futon over her head and fell into a peaceful sleep.
