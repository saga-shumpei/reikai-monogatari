Chapter 16: The Vanishing of Heaven and Earth [16]

天消地滅〔16〕

§ 1

Neither clearing nor fully clouding, on the Kanranzan, in the moonlit Misora, a voice of Muga speaks.

The Yukusaki is to the place where the voice of Muga speaks.

Setting out on the path of the Law, guided by the voice of Muga.

The voice of Muga, which surpasses even the love of Chichihaha.

Since that night we parted in tears, I have wondered where and how you are.

I have not even forgotten you in my sleep, yet no matter what, this is too much.

There is a woman wandering about the Chiyōjiyō of Kanranzan while saying this. This was Mariya, who holds the Giuji of the American Colony. To avoid the Jiyonan of Mariya, Burabāsa had quickly left the Sōin hotel and was hiding in a Sauan built in the Keikoku of Mount Zion. Mariya was singing like this, pining for love, and searching for the whereabouts of Burabāsa. To such a place, the gentle singing voice of a woman was heard from the thicket of trees on Kanransanjiyō.

In the green wind, the flowers scatter.

Lamenting the passing spring evening.

Summer comes into my own heart.

Lying on the bed of the evening Kotefu.

Thinking of the flowers that shine in the morning light.

The agonizing dream does not yet awaken.

The moon of Genjitsu is high in the sky.

On the Kanran mountain where the Aoba shines.

At least let my heart find rest.

It was Salome who came out as if floating in the Shizushizu Tsukiyo while singing this. Oriori, the Riyōnin meet under the moon on this mountain. However, they do not feel very comfortable with each other, nor have they ever spoken deeply. Both, unexpectedly, were seized by a sense of sympathy, and as if drawn by something, the two approached until they could clearly see each other's faces even in the hazy moonlight.

Mariya: 'There is no way for the evening ablution to return.'

'There is no way to stop the scattering flowers.'

Salome: 'The blooming of the cherry blossoms is the time I spent in intimacy with you.'

'Though the moon clears many times,'

'And the days pile up many times,'

'There is no way to meet you.'

Mariya: 'Summer comes amidst tears.'

'The fireflies that fly about every night,'

'Are they not like my own body, burning with longing?'

Salome: 'Now, the memories of sadness,'

'The fireflies that fly about every night,'

'Are they not like my own body, burning with longing?'

Thus, the Riyōnin found common ground and confessed their feelings as losers in love. From this point on, Mariya and Salome became as close as sisters, and they came to open their hearts and talk to each other.

Mariya-sama, from your song just now, I realized that your situation is exactly like mine. Truly, the world does not go as one wishes, does it?

Yes, thank you. I have come to dislike this world. Abandoned by the man I set my heart on, I no longer have any joy in this world. Even if Christ were to appear from the Orion constellation, once I have been broken in love as a human, I have no more joy. The second coming of Christ is nothing to me.

She is becoming like a half-mad person.

Was it not to wait for the second coming of Christ that you continued your single life for so long?

The Christ I am waiting for is not such a high and distant God. He is a man of deep affection and purity who will satisfy my desires of love. For my own person, the one I look up to as Christ is Burabāsa-sama, who came from the island of Hinodewake.

I, too, am waiting for the second coming of Christ. However, if there is a person of deep affection who can satisfy my heart, that person is the true Christ to me. The one who gives water from a clear spring to a parched soul, and gives life to a heart that is about to wither, is what is called Christ. And do you not know where Burabāsa-sama has gone?

With these words of parting, Sarome went to the construction site near where the foundation work for Zion University had been laid, sat down, rested her body for a while, and began to pray once again.

Burabasa, who had been hiding in the Valley of Zion to avoid the sharp edge of love, concluded that since the night had deepened, Mariya could not possibly be coming, and he climbed the Mount of Olives to pray for the safe second coming of Christ. A gentle woman's voice of prayer could be heard inside the construction shed. Burabasa thought, 'Could that voice be Mariya? If it were Mariya, I would be caught again and it would be troublesome. However, to heartlessly abandon a woman who yearns for me so much seems cruel, and yet, if I follow her will, I feel as if I have committed a sin. I am scolded by the Keshin of the Great God, alas, what should I do? It is a painful matter. Wait, wait, there is no difference in salvation between saving all the people of the world and saving one woman. Even if I let one woman die to save the people of the world, it might not be the best path. Alas, perhaps I have fallen into self-love. Even if I fall into hell to save that woman, saving her is my true heart. Well, if it comes to this, there is neither good nor evil. Rather than hiding in the Valley of Zion and making a woman commit a sin, I will become the sinner and save Mariya; that is the duty of a man. Even if I am not there, or if I fail, it will not hinder the second coming of Uzunbarachiyandā. Christ hung on the cross for the sake of all people. I feel sorry for my wife left in my country, but my wife is also the wife of a missionary. She will likely endure that much sacrifice. Well, I do not care anymore; it would not be very good to let such a passionate woman die,' and having debated within his heart, he made his decision and stepped into the construction shed.

§ 3

Once Burabasa had made up his mind in this way, there was no longer anything left in the Uchiukan. In this vast Sekai, there existed only the figure of Mariya. The voices of the Yamabato and the sounds of the insects that had been audible until now were gone; everything, Itsusaibanji, had vanished somewhere, and with neither Ten nor earth remaining, nothing was reflected in his heart but the figure of Mariya. Therefore, the figure of Sarome appeared entirely as Mariya, and he had not a Mijin of leisure to reconsider that she might be another person.

Meanwhile, Sarome was offering her Kiguwan while thinking of Yakobu, and she thought within her heart:

Sarome: 'Even if my Riyōshin say whatever they may, and even if the people of the Seken revile me as a Darakuonna, what do I care for such things? What is there to be concerned about in savoring my own love? For the sake of that person, there is neither Ten nor earth. Even if I were to bury all Shinrabanshiyō, there is none other than Yakobu-sama who gives life to my heart. What do Chii and Meiyo matter? What is the life of a Kizoku? Even birds and beasts enjoy love in Jiiu. There is no reason why a Ningen, the leader of all Banbutsu, should not enjoy love. I shall part the grass to search, find Yakobu-sama, cast aside my Chii and Meiyo, and apologize for everything until now. Because my own Ishi was weak, I caused Yakobu-sama unexpected sorrow... Yakobu-sama, please forgive me. Even if I should fall into Jigoku, I shall fulfill my promise to you. For that is the true nature of a woman...'

Taking advantage of the fact that there was no one nearby, she ended up speaking in a loud voice without realizing it.

Burabasa, while hearing that Sarome was speaking of Yakobu, could still only think of her as Mariya. The two, man and woman, gathered together and held each other tightly. And they entered a realm of selflessness where Ten and earth, the Mount of Olives, and even their own bodies seemed to have vanished somewhere. After a while, as if suddenly coming to his senses, Sarome said,

Sarome: 'Ah, Yakobu-sama, you have come. My single-minded devotion must have reached your soul. From here on, I offer you both my body and soul, and I shall never let my heart wander elsewhere, so please cherish me.'

Burabasa, hearing the name Yakobu, became greatly enraged and said,

Burabasa: 'You unfaithful wretch of a Mariya, you have toyed with me well. To use me as a proxy for the Yakobu you so passionately love—is there not a limit to how much you can make a fool of me? I thought there was no woman I loved other than Mariya, but ugh, how filthy. Do as you please. With this, the confusion in my heart is cleared. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.'

Sarome, still caught in the reality of the moment, was convinced that Burabasa was Yakobu. Hearing him say there was no woman he loved more than Mariya, she said,

Sarome: 'Ugh, you wicked man, you Yakobu-wretch, you have made a fool of this Sarome well enough. This body of mine, for which I have cast away my life, from here on, I shall be desperate and let you know the consequences.'

She drew a short sword for self-defense and slashed at him. Burabasa ran around the construction hut, crying, 'Mariya, wait, wait.' Just then, the suspicious Mariya, thinking that perhaps Sarome was saying such things because she was hiding Burabasa, returned from halfway and, seeing this scene, was struck with surprise and said,

Mariya: 'If I may, Sarome-sama, please wait a moment.'

She clung to her arm. Sarome, raging like a Yaksha, said,

Sarome: 'Ugh, you rival in love, you Mariya-wretch, this is the grudge for taking Yakobu from me, prepare yourself!'

She raged violently. Appearing there was the heartbroken man Yakobu, who had come following after Sarome. Yakobu raised his voice and said,

Yakobu: 'Here, here, Sarome-sama, have you lost your mind? Please wait a moment. I am Yakobu.'

『あゝヤコブ様、ヨウ来て下さいました。妾の一念が貴方の魂に通じたので御座りませう。もう此上は身も魂もあなたに捧げまして決して外へは心を散らしませぬから可愛がつて下さいませ』

ブラバーサはヤコブと云ふ声を聞いて大に怒り、

『こりや不貞腐れのマリヤ奴、よう私を翻弄して呉れたなア。お前の熱愛して居るヤコブの代理に己を使ふとは、馬鹿にするのも程があるではないか。己はマリヤより外に愛する女は無いのだと思つて居たのにエヽ汚らはしい、勝手にどうなとしたがよからう。俺もこれで胸の迷ひが取れた。あゝ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

サロメはやつぱり現になつてブラバーサをヤコブと思ひつめて居た。マリヤより愛する女が無いと云ふのを聞いて、

『エヽ悪性男のヤコブ奴、ようもようも此サロメを馬鹿にしよつたなア。命を捨てた此体、もう此上は破れかぶれ思ひ知つたがよからう』

と護身用の短刀を抜いて切つてかかる。ブラバーサはマリヤ待つた待つたと作事小屋のぐるりを逃げ廻つて居る。かかる所へ疑ひ深いマリヤは、サロメがアンナ事をいつて、ブラバーサを隠して居るのでなからうかと、中途より引返し来り、此体を見て打驚き、

『もしサロメ様、マア待つて下さいませ』

と腕に食ひつく。サロメは夜叉の如くに怒り狂ひ、

『エヽ恋の敵マリヤ奴、ヤコブを取りよつた恨だ、覚悟を致せ』

と猛り狂ふ。其処へ又現はれて来たのはサロメの後を追ふてやつて来た失恋男のヤコブであつた。ヤコブは大声をあげて、

『これこれサロメさまお気が狂ふたのか一寸待つて下さい。私はヤコブで御座います』

§ 4

Salome, drained of energy by this voice, stands still, clutching the Tantau in her hand.

The moon, Kaukau, has begun to illuminate the edge of the mountain.

The faces of the Yonin have become clear all at once.

Mariya, trembling, holds the hand of Burabasa tightly.

Mariya: 'Seishisama, where have you gone? I do not know how long I have been searching for you.'

Burabasa: 'Hmm, so you were Mariya. It being the middle of the Yachiu, I made a terrible Hitochiga. You must have been watching that Kuwatsugeki, must you not?'

Mariya: 'Ho ho ho ho ho.'

Salome: 'Ho ho ho ho ho.'

Burabasa: 'What, a Hitochiga? Come, Salome-sama, Yakobu shall never part from you, so please prepare yourself; it is a matter of Inochi.'

Salome: 'I, too, am prepared to stake my Inochi. Because Burabasa-sama appeared to be you, I made a terrible Machiga. Oh, it is most Ketsukō that you are Buji and have no Kega. Let us all, Minasama, offer our thanks to the Kamisama here.'

The Danjiyo Yonin sat in Tanza upon the Chijiyō and gave thanks for the success of their love.

The flow of the Jordan River, the sound of the wind blowing over the peaks, and both Ten and Chijiyō gradually began to Kaiten before the four.

Ah, Kamunagara Chihahe-mase.
