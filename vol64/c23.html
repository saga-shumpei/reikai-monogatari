Chapter 23: The Hidden Scheme [23]

暗着〔23〕

§ 1

Captured by the desire for fame and profit, the four ugly souls of men and women, flustered and clamoring, left Kogitayama behind and boarded the Kishiya with great vigor; as the sound of the wheels roared and shook the Daichi, they headed toward Atedo, passing Arashiyama, Hanazono, Nideu, Kiyōtoeki, Mukafumachi, Yamazaki, Takatsuki, and even Ōsakaeki, until they reached Hiroshima, faced the barrier of Bakuwan, boarded the Tenpukumaru, and finally landed at Fuzan, crossing the bridges of Keijiyō, Heizeu, and Afuriyoku, and proceeding steadily along the Kuōki railway of Manshiu, advancing day and night.

After more than twenty days on the Kishiya, they finally arrived safely at the sacred site, the world-renowned Jerusalem, and wandered about the city; they rushed headlong to climb the Mount of Olives, looked out over the scenery of the four directions, and stood there, their hearts leaping and their minds astonished.

Otora sighed with relief,

Otora: 'Ah, good heavens, at long last, having crossed eight thousand miles of land and sea, we have reached our destination. What a place, it looks so much like Okebuseyama. However, both in the state of the mountains and the condition of the trees, does it not feel somewhat lonely, unlike the Okebuseyama of Hinodedeshima? And are they not building a large school, intending to educate the foreign spirits extensively? I wonder what kind of four-legged race it is that would ruin this magnificent sacred site by piling up bricks and stones.'

Morimiyawake-sama looked troubled and said,

Morimiyawake-sama: 'Now, now, Otora-sama, you must not speak so loudly. That is called Zion University, a place where scholars of the world are gathered to improve the world's ideological sphere. In short, it is being managed by Jewish scholars and doctors of the world, claiming they will protect Hinode. If it were Hinodedeshima, no one would laugh no matter what you said, but we have come to such a place where the language is not understood, so it is troublesome if you say such useless things. Since you do not understand foreign languages, please do as I say in all matters.'

Otora: 'Hmph, are foreign languages really that precious? Did not the Hensei-danshi-sama say that he would govern this world with everything using the forty-eight characters of the Iroha? Would it not be fine to make the foreigners repent from now on and have them learn the language of the Iroha forty-eight characters of Hinodedeshima? Without that much authority, how can you become the Hinode-gami of the three thousand worlds? You, too, are saying things that are quite incomprehensible. Ohohohoho.'

Morimiyawake-sama: 'Are you going to act up again? But no matter how much you act up, it is fine, as there are few foreigners who understand Japanese. Now, shall we go and see the construction work of Zion University?'

Otora: 'Now, Morimiyawake-sama, this is no time to be saying such carefree things. We must quickly search for the whereabouts of Burabāsa, who is said to have secretly hidden away from Okebuseyama, and negotiate, or else our plans will not stand.'

As they were talking, Dr. Subāru, the founder of Zion University, came along leaning on a cane. Morimiyawake-sama spoke to him fluently in his specialty, English. The doctor also shook hands with Morimiyawake-sama quite pleasantly and talked for about half an hour. The main point was... the general story that Morimiyawake-sama had sent the savior Hinode-gami from Hinodedeshima. Dr. Subāru listened seriously, but in the end, he burst out laughing, grinned, and parted ways with them. Morimiyawake-sama thought that if he were not recognized by this doctor, the Hinode-gami would be finished, but he pretended to be calm, feigning ignorance. Otora, taking advantage of the fact that Dr. Subāru's figure was no longer visible, began to chatter again.

Otora: 'Now, Morimiyawake-sama, you were chirping like a sparrow, but what on earth were you saying? Did you not tell him that the savior Hinode-gami has descended?'
