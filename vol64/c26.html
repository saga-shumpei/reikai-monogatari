Chapter 26: The Abandonment [26]

置去〔26〕

§ 1

Yamoriwake and his party followed the alleys and the field paths on the outskirts of the town, and at last reached the banks of the Jordan River.

Yamoriwake: 'Look here, O-Tora-sama, this river is what they call the noisy Jordan River. Take a good look at it.'

O-Tora: 'What a filthy river it is. I had heard it called the Jordan River, so I thought it would be a much wider river, but it cannot even compare to the Ōigaha that flows at the foot of Kogitayama. And the water of the Ōigaha is Kirei, but this water is not even worth talking about.'

Magahiko, acting like a Kosai, said:

Magahiko: 'It is called the Ōigaha because it is big.'

O-Tora: 'Look here, Maga-yan, why are you babbling on so noisily? If that is so, do you know the reason for the name of the Jordan River?'

Magahiko: 'When you speak of the Jordan River, it is the place where John performed the baptism of Christ. That is why they took the character "Yo" from John and called it the Jordan River.'

O-Tora: 'Ha ha ha, calling it the Jordan River is a corruption. The truth is that it is called the Yorodan River. It is a river where Yamoriwake walks along tottering like this, and proceeds step by step toward the church of the Bahai Kyō. That is why it is the Yorodan River. Ah, I feel as if my drunkenness is wearing off. I wonder if there is any place around here that sells cup sake.'

Yamoriwake: 'Hey, again with the Sakesake, what is this? I will let you drink to your heart's content once our business is finished, so please have some Shinbau.'

O-Tora: 'Well, it cannot be helped, I suppose I shall obey the Meirei of the Jiyōu-sama. My, they have built houses in the trees, like birds' nests or something. Those might be the homes of Tengu. Because so many Tengu from Kogitayama have come, the Tengu fellows have been clever enough to build houses like that to welcome us.'

O-Tora: 'The great Tengu of the mountain of Kokita, / Crossing over the Hatau of ten thousand ri, / Aiming for the Seichi, they come along, / Bumping their heads here and there, / Even the Takahime with the long Tengu nose, / Is now completely Dame, you see. / The Tengu fellows of the same Nakama, / Shedding tears of a little Dōjiyō, / On the branches of the trees along the Jordan River, / Must have hung their nests. / Truly, there are no demons in the world, / It is a world of nothing but Tengu. / Dokkoisho, dokkoisho.'

Yamoriwake: 'I must pull myself together. There is a house over there. That is likely the mansion of the Bahai Kyō. Look here, O-Hana-sama, this time it is your turn. If I let the essential Savior speak, it will lower his dignity. Excuse me, Jiyōu-sama, no, I mean O-Tora-baba-sama, this time please keep silent. O-Hana-sama and Magahiko-sama will fight sufficiently.'

O-Tora: 'Look here, Yamoriwake-sama, do not call me O-Tora-baba-sama. I am the Hinodewake from beginning to end.'

Yamoriwake: 'Yes, I understand. Ah, what a troublesome thing this is.'

Saying this, they arrived at the front of the church. A woman of peerless beauty, holding a paper fan in one hand, was playing with pinks at the gate. This was the famous Princess Salome. Yamoriwake and Magahiko, seeing the beautiful woman's figure, narrowed their eyes, opened their mouths agape with saliva dripping down, and had completely relaxed the sphincter muscles of their faces.

Magahiko, in a strange voice with the rhythm lost, said:

Magahiko: 'Excuse me, young lady there, are you not the famous Salome-sama?'

Salome: 'Yes, that is correct. You appear to be people from a distant land, but do you have some business with this Salome?'

O-Hana, with an arrogant look, said:

O-Hana: 'It is precisely because we have business that the living shrine of the Hinodewake, the Savior of the three thousand worlds, has come all this way to visit you. Now, show us the way.'

Salome: 'Yes, and when will that Savior arrive?'

O-Hana: 'Look, he is here, right this way.'

Otora: 'Hee, are you the Kiuseishiyu? What an unexpected person you are.'

Otora stepped forward, seizing the moment:

Otora: 'It may be unexpected, but there is a Yogen from long ago that says, "At an unexpected time, an unexpected person shall appear and perform an unexpected Goyō." It is to fulfill that Yogen that this unexpected Kiuseishiyu has unexpectedly come to visit you. By the way, Salome-sama, I saw the Shinbun today, and it seems that you, a Shiyukujiyo of your Mibun, have performed a Seigeki with that scoundrel called Barabasa, who has a face like an owl with large Medama. I have come to visit you because I wish to hear your Goichiu regarding that matter. And do you truly know who the Kiuseishiyu is? If you do not understand that, it is Dame no matter how long you continue your Shiugefu here.'

Salome: 'This is the Kadoguchi, so please enter inside. Here, there is a Seishi named Baha-ullah-sama. Please meet with him and listen carefully.'

Otora: 'Do you, Mahesama, acknowledge this Kiuseishiyu of the Hinodewake? If you acknowledge me, it is fine to enter. If you are a Shikimau who cannot understand that, this Ikimiya of the Hinodewake will not enter so Karugaru. Depending on how you act, Mahesama, it is not impossible that I might enter. And depending on how you let me enter, Mahesama, it is not impossible that I might not leave.'

Salome: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, you are not quite right in the head. The Yōki has been quite bad lately, so please use Goyōjin. The asylum is quite far from here, you know.'

Otora: 'The asylum is the highest mountain in Hinodeshima. In Hinodeshima, we call it Tenkyōzan. Since I am the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake who has descended from that magnificent Takamagahara, please investigate well. No matter what you say, my face is the spitting image of a globe... because my face is said to make the three thousand worlds free and at will, you will surely understand once you take a look.'

Salome burst out laughing,

Salome: 'Oho-ho-ho-ho-ho.'

Magohiko seemed unable to bear it any longer,

Magohiko: 'What a beautiful woman she is. She is just like Konohanahime-sama of Tenkyōzan... She seems more like the Kiuseishiyu than Takahime-sama. No matter if Takahime-sama is the Kiuseishiyu, as an individual, it has nothing to do with me. If this person were to even hold the hand of Magohiko, she would truly be the Kiuseishiyu-sama. I would be revived.'

Ohana said with a sullen and harsh tone,

Ohana: 'What kind of impolite things are you saying? Is there such a thing in the teachings of Hinode-sama? Repent a little. You have such bad manners... Hey, hey, Salome-sama, that scoundrel Barabasa is in this house, is he not? Since it is said that you performed that Seigeki with him, you must surely be hiding him.'

Salome: 'Ho-ho-ho, what are you saying? That person is at the foot of Mount Zion. I have only met him four or five times on the Mount of Olives. Please, do search for Mount Zion. Farewell.'

Thinking them a nuisance, Salome entered the gate and quickly locked it from the inside.

Magohiko: 'Otora-sama, shall we go home now? In any case, this fellow is definitely at Mount Zion. More than that, let us return quickly, for if Morikawawake does not have some beer, his body will not hold up.'

Otora: 'That may be so. Come, let us return. If we dawdle, the sun will set. ...Today, we shall return quietly like this and come back to observe the situation. That scoundrel Barabasa is surely hiding in this house. I must grab that fellow, make him cry out in front of that woman, and force him to testify that the Hinodewake is the Kiuseishiyu. However, it is already late today, so let us return once, fix our strategy firmly, and come again. Magoyan, you hide in those bushes and observe the situation. And report the situation to me.'

Otora-sama: 'That is Setsushiyō, Yamoriwake-sama. Even I cannot bear it unless I return to the inn and have my Bansan.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Ah, you are a Yowamushi. It cannot be helped; then let us return.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Well, well, that was exhausting. Let us eat even a disagreeable Yōshiyoku Bansan and have a slow Saudan. Now, Yamoriwake-dono, I do not say you should not drink beer, but please show some Shinbau with at least half a dozen. If you drink two dozen like you did this morning, my Hinjiyaku purse will be completely dried up.'

Yamoriwake clapped his hands and called for the boy. The boy answered 'Yes' and instantly appeared on the scene.

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Prepare the Bansan quickly. And bring half a dozen beer, then another half a dozen.'

Boy: 'Yes, I understand. However, since the beer is very Kauka, I request the Kanjiyō for this morning. Unless that Kanjiyō is settled, I cannot bring any more.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Now, how much is the Kanjiyō?'

Boy: 'Yes, it is a little Kauka, but that is the most Kichiyō and Kauka item stored since two Nihiyakunen Izen, and it cannot be made for less than one hundred pounds per Itsupon. Please, I would like to receive two thousand four hundred pounds.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'What a Kauka thing. No wonder I thought it was sweet. Now, Otora-sama, please bring out the money.'

Otora-sama: 'Two thousand four hundred pounds, is that four hundred times two sen? That would make eight yen, would it not? What a Kauka thing.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Hey, hey, Boy-sama, you must not tell jokes. If you say two thousand four hundred pounds, that is twenty-four thousand ryō, is it not?'

Boy: 'Yes, that is correct.'

Otora-sama: 'Aii, I do not know! Where would such money exist? Why did you not ask the price before drinking, you? I have lost all patience. Where is there anything that one drinks up twenty-four thousand ryō at once?'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Even so, it is worth that much. Thanks to it, your lifespan will be extended by about ten years.'

Otora-sama: 'Hmph, stop it. Do you think I care if your lifespan is extended or shortened? Why did you not consult with your purse before drinking?'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Even if I wanted to consult with my purse, it is empty. Since you are the one holding all the money, it cannot be helped, can it?'

Otora-sama: 'Ah, it cannot be helped; then I shall pay for you. Here, here, Boy-sama, I will pay right now, so please bring another half a dozen or so. It is a great trouble to take it out, so I will pay in the meantime.'

Boy: 'In that case, I shall try consulting with the manager. If the manager says it is fine to take it, I shall bring it.'

The boy went down to the floor below. Afterwards, Otora looked displeased.

Otora-sama: 'Here, Yamoriwake-sama, is this not too much? Are you trying to make me feel shame?'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Are you not the Hinodewake who makes the three thousand worlds free? Since you always say what is money, I drank with spirit.'

Otora-sama: 'Even so, is it not a waste? Is it not, Ohana-sama?'

Ohana-sama: 'That is a terrible place to be fleeced. I was surprised.'

As they were talking, the boy brought a dozen beers.

Boy: 'When I told the manager just now, he said, "It is unlikely that a person like you would have trouble with money," so I have brought them.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Good, good, leave them there.'

Boy: 'Please call me if you have further business. I have preparations for the Bansan to attend to.'

He went down the stairs. Yamoriwake drank the dozen beers while making a gurgling sound in his throat, drinking like a whale drinking the tide, and collapsed on the spot and fell asleep.

Otora-sama: 'Here, Magoyan, Ohana-sama, there is absolutely no money for twenty-four thousand ryō. I only have three hundred yen in my purse at last. Now, it will be a disaster if we dawdle, so let us sneak out from the back gate.'

『これ曲やん、お花さま、二万四千両の金はどうしても無い。やうやく懐に三百円しかない。サア愚図々々して居ては大変だから裏門からそつと逃げるのですよ』

§ 4

Yamoriwake-sama: 'What shall we do?'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'There is nothing to be done, is there? Since there is no money, and the beer is in our bellies, they will surely make us pay it all. Ohanasama and I have not drunk a single drop, so we have no obligation to pay. Come, come, let us escape from here while we can, and flee to where Burabasa of Mount Zion is.'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Is Hinode-no-kami-sama going to commit a dine-and-dash?'

Yamoriwake-sama: 'Yes, whatever. If you do not like it, stay here.'

Saying this, he hurriedly left behind a wallet containing three hundred yen and fled through the back door. Yamoriwake awoke in the middle of the night and looked around, but since he could not see Otora and the others, he thought they might have gone to relieve themselves and went back to snoring soundly. When morning came and the three were still nowhere to be seen, he clapped his hands to call the boy.

The boy came in bowing repeatedly.

Boy: 'Is there something you require, guest?'

Yamoriwake: 'Where have the three people in my party gone?'

Boy: 'They left through the back door last night. I thought this might be a dine-and-dash, but since a large sum of three hundred ryō was left behind, I left it as it was for the time being.'

Yamoriwake: 'Hmm, how shall I pay for last night's beer?'

Boy: 'Guest, do not worry. When I said one bottle was one hundred pounds last night, it was a joke. In fact, one bottle is half a dollar, so please give me twelve dollars. And if you add last night's and pay eighteen dollars in total, that will suffice.'

Yamoriwake: 'Ha ha ha, what is that? I shall pay for it all. I will give you a tip too, so take as much as you like. Now, since I will be staying here until this money is gone, take good care of me.'

Yamoriwake spent another night there, settled the inn's bill, and set off, wandering aimlessly toward the bottom of the valley of Mount Zion in search of the three.
