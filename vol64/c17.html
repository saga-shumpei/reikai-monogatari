Tora-san: 'Hey, hey, what are you dawdling for? I told you to bring the tea quickly.'

Tonku: 'Yes, I am bringing it now. Please wait a moment.'

Tora-san: 'My, what a slow man you are. You are so sluggish that it is funny enough to make my navel boil tea.'

Tonku: 'Even I, having heard the negotiation between the Ikumiya-sama and Burabāsa last night, have had my navel boiling tea since last evening. Truly, Burabāsa's attitude was unbearable, was it not?'

Tonku diverted the conversation to a side path, trying to buy time for Teku to return by inserting even a moment of leisure. Tora-san, because Tonku said something that sounded like an attack on Burabāsa, forgot even her thirst and said, 'That is right, isn't it? Tonku, if you saw that attitude, it would not just be your navel boiling tea, your testicles would even go abroad, would they not?' Tonku replied, 'Yes, yes, that is certainly so. My liver is crushed, and not only am I startled, but my testicles are curling up, and my navel is boiling tea hot enough to burn my stomach. Oh, it was truly chaotic. It is hilariously funny. No matter how grand he acts, when he appears before the Ikumiya-sama, it is just like a mouse coming near a cat; he has no way to say "meow" or "squeak." Oh, Teku, that rascal is such a witless fellow. Even if he manages to buy a teapot, it will take five or ten minutes for the water to boil, so what shall I say to stall for time in the meanwhile?'

『成程、委細承知致しました』

と云ふより早く、窮屈の皺苦茶婆アの小言を聞いて居るより、外の空気に触れた方が面白いと、匆々に出でて行く。その後でお寅は干瓢に鰹のだしを入れ、グツグツと膨れる処迄煮き上げ、神様にもお供へをし、自分とトンクとの分をしまひおき、あとの残つた干瓢を、暫らく水に浸し、甘味をぬいて了ひ、再び土瓶の中へつツ込み、シスセーナをやつて、再び火鉢に土瓶をかけ、グツグツグツとたぎらし、テクの膳を出して、皿に一杯盛つておいた。暫くすると、トンク、テクは怖さうに帰つて来た。

§ 4

Teku: 'If it please you, Ikimiyasama, I met with Dea on the way, and I have returned safely.'

Ikimiya: 'Ah, that is truly a great labor, thank you. Come, you must be hungry; please eat your breakfast. I have been waiting for you, holding back my own hunger, thinking that I would eat with you.'

Teku: 'Oh, my. To think that you, Ikimiyasama, have done the cooking yourself. Hey, Teku, shall we partake? It is a meal prepared by the very hands of Ōmirokusama. Such an honor is rare indeed.'

Ikimiya: 'Today I, Ikimiya, am doing the cooking, but from tomorrow, I shall ask Tonku-sama to do it.'

Tonku: 'Yes, I understand.'

The three of them surrounded the dining table and began to gobble up their breakfast, with dried gourd as a side dish.

Teku: 'My, oh my, does this dried gourd not taste wonderful? It has a flavor that I cannot even begin to describe.'

Tonku: 'Hmm, it is delicious, but is it not a little smelly?'

Teku: 'What, the smell is its value; it is the aroma of bonito broth.'

Tonku: 'I wonder if that is so.'

While saying this, both Tora and Tonku were eating it as if it were sweet, so he, thinking it suspicious himself, finished it all without leaving a single piece.

Ikimiya: 'Ho ho ho ho, now Teku, how was it? You have been granted special protection. This is in return for what happened earlier. I have repaid you just a little, so please do not think ill of me, ho ho ho ho.'

Teku, with a face like a frog in a small rice field that had failed to croak, fell silent. It was Tsūro who came walking back to that spot.

Tsūro: 'Please forgive me. I am sorry for being so late. I have just returned. Oh, Tonku, Teku, are you already back?'

Tonku: 'You, where have you been? Ikimiyasama has been very angry. You are like a cannonball. It is troublesome because you do not know how to return once you have left.'

Tsūro: 'What, I had a great deal of business, and I am sorry, but in return, I have brought back plenty of souvenirs for Ikimiyasama. I have uncovered secrets that even the Buddha would not know.'

Ikimiya: 'Now, Tsūro, what kind of souvenirs have you brought for me? I suppose you have uncovered secrets between Ohana and Ōhiroki-sama.'

Tsūro: 'No, your insight is terrifying. Actually, I followed Yaku, but since I lost track of him, I felt apologetic and spent two or three days as a guest at the American Colony. I heard that Ōhiroki Masamune-sama and Ohana-sama were going to establish a New Uranai religion at the Catholic monastery hotel, so I went to see it secretly. There, that fellow Yaku was lying flat like the character for 'large' at the bottom of the staircase, stretched out. A crowd of boys had gathered, making a clamor, and a doctor was coming out; it was a great commotion.'

Ikimiya: 'My, oh my, the retribution of heaven is a fearful thing. It must be the reward for striking the face of this Ikimiya with a mud broom. That has eased my heart a little. Truly, the gods are great. And did you not hear how Ohana and Ōhiroki Masamune-sama are doing?'

Tsūro: 'Yes, yes, whether I heard or not, something terrible has happened. Just as Ōhiroki-sama of the Miya-wake lineage was holding a wedding ceremony with Ohana-sama and attempting to establish a new religion, a prostitute named Ayako of the Ariake family came to meet them. A great quarrel broke out with Ohana-sama; Ōhiroki-sama had his hair pulled and his eyes rolled back, and Ohana-sama was struck on the head, developed a fever, and was talking in her sleep, so she has been admitted to the Philanthropy Hospital.'

Ikimiya: 'Ho ho, what a great person the God is. As Hinodewake-sama always says, if you keep your sincerity, God will strike your enemies. Evil will surely not prevail over good, but that woman called Ayako of the Ariake family is a clever one. To quarrel with Ohana, who has spurs growing on her, is truly promising. Well then, I must go to visit Miya-wake-sama. Now that I know his whereabouts, I cannot delay even for a moment. Tonku, Teku, please keep the house properly. You must never boil urine tea or anything of the sort. Now, Tsūro, lead the way.'

Tsūro: 'Yes, I understand.'

He went out, taking the lead. Tora, with her buttocks wiggling and swaying in the air, went out to the main street, hired an automobile with Tsūro, and hurried toward the Catholic monastery hotel.
