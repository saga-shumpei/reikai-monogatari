Chapter 17: The Extortion [17]

強請〔17〕

§ 1

Three Arabs came in a hurry to the Kakurega of Burabasa, who had built a Sauan in the valley of Mount Zion to avoid the sharp edge of love. Burabasa, having been unexpectedly bound by the Aijiyō of Mariya on Kanranzan, had himself become a prisoner of love before he knew it, and though they had made a Fūfu promise, now that he came to think of it, he began to feel as if he had committed a grave Jiuzai.

‘Ah, why am I so Gumai? Not once but twice have I fallen into the Iuwaku of love, and having come all this way from the Seichi across the Banri of seas, how can I offer a Maoshiwake to the Kamisama for such a thing? Furthermore, I have no Ihiwake to offer to the Kyōshiyu of Rūtobahā. This has become a troublesome matter.’ Thus, he sat in Tanza within his Sauan from early in the morning, lost in tears of Kuwaigo.

There, the three Arabs pushed open the brushwood door and came Irikita in a boisterous manner.

Teku: 'Hey, good morning. I am an Arab who is always used for the construction work at the Zion Daigaku on Kanranzan, but last night we three saw something rather interesting, so we have come to consult with you.'

Burabasa: 'And what might be the unusual matter for which you have come to visit my Sauan?'

Teku: 'Hmph, do not try to play dumb. What about the live drama of last night? Even if you think no one knows, the heavens know and the earth knows, and it echoed clearly enough to reach the ears of us three. Nay, not just our ears; with these two black eyes, I watched from the corner of the construction site. Two men and women performed a quite splendid live drama, did they not? Are you going to deny even this?'

Burabasa: 'This is somewhat troublesome; I have not stepped a single step out of this Sauan for the past two or three days. That must surely be some kind of mistake.'

Upon hearing this, Tonku said:

Tonku: 'Hmph, do not make a fool of me. I am neither deaf nor blind. You are said to be a missionary of Rūtobahā who came from Hinodewake-jima, but is it acceptable for a missionary to tell lies? People from various countries have come to this Seichi, but you are the only one who tells lies. You are a person who should be recognized as a representative of Hinodewake-jima. If that representative is a liar, it is determined that the people of Hinodewake-jima are all liars; is that acceptable to you? Today, when the second coming of Christ is near, a nation that tells lies will be expelled from the league of the world and must become a wandering people like the Jews of the past. Pull yourself together and tell the truth. Your single lie becomes a lie for all of Hinodewake-jima. Since the Miyako Shimbun, the Seichi Shimpo, and the Kaikyo Shimbun are all published here, if we were to meet the reporters and speak of the reality of last night, you would not be able to stay here. You have deceived a Jewish woman... you must face crucifixion as an enemy of all the Jewish people, but is that acceptable? Depending on how you act, we have our own thoughts on this matter. Well, how is it? I want to hear a clear answer.'

Burabasa: 'This is the height of annoyance lately. I have absolutely no memory of such a thing.'

Tonku: 'Do not talk nonsense. It is no use even if you hide it. Teku has already investigated everything, even regarding Sarome and Yakobu. If you dally, the four of you will become the target of Jewish resentment and will immediately fall into the fate of annihilation; thinking that is pitiful, we three have come to negotiate.'

Burabasa: 'What are you saying I should do?'

Tonku: 'Look at that, you do have a memory of it after all. Throw out ten thousand ryō here in exchange for your life. If you do that, we will keep silent. Besides us three, no one has seen it but the moon, so as long as the moon does not speak, there is no fear of it being known. If we were to tell this to a reporter from the Miyako Shimbun, they would surely give us a reward of twenty or thirty thousand ryō. After all, one side is a missionary of Rūtobahā, and the other is the noble daughter Sarome-sama... In any case, we have found a good source of profit. Ihihihi!'

原文（近代文語）

シオン山の谷間に草庵を結んで、恋の鋭鋒を避けて居たブラバーサの隠家へ慌だしくやつて来たのは三人のアラブである。ブラバーサは橄欖山上に於て、ゆくりなくもマリヤの愛情に絆され、自分も何時の間にやら恋の虜となり、夫婦の約束をしたものの、今となつて考へてみれば、何とはなしに重罪を犯したやうな心持がして来出した。……あゝなぜ私は之丈愚昧だらう。一度ならず二度迄も恋の誘惑におち、はるばる聖地から万里の海を渡りてここ迄来乍ら、かやうなことで何うして神様に申訳が立たう。又ルートバハーの教主に対しても言訳がない。困つたことになつたものだ。……と朝早うから草庵の中に端座して悔悟の涙にくれてゐた。そこへ三人のアラブが柴の戸押あけドヤドヤと入来り、

テク『ヤ、お早う。わつちは何時も橄欖山のシオン大学の工事に使はれてゐるアラブだが、夜前一寸面白いことを吾々三人が見たので御相談に参りやした』

『して又あなた方が私の草庵を訪ねて下さつたのは、何か変つたことが御座いますかな』

『ヘン、トボケまいぞ。夕べの活劇は何うだい。誰も知らぬかと思つてゐても、天知る地知ると言つて、チヤンと吾々三人さまの耳につつぬけるほど響いたのだ。イヤ耳ばかりでない、此二つの黒い眼で、作事場の隅から覗いておいたのだ。二組の男女が随分立派な活劇をやつたでせう。之でも違ひますかな』

『之は聊か迷惑、拙者は此草庵よりここ二三日、一歩も出たこともありませぬ。ソリヤ大方何かの間違ひでせう』

と聞くよりトンクは、

『ヘン、馬鹿にするない。おれは聾でも盲でもないぞ。お前も日の出島からやつて来たルートバハーの宣伝使だといふことだが、宣伝使はウソを云つて可いのか。此聖地へ各国の人々が出て来てるが、ウソをつく奴アお前ばかりだ。お前は日の出島の代表者とも認めらるべき者だ。其代表者が嘘つきとあれば日の出島の人間は一体に嘘つきと定つて了ふが夫れでも可いのか。キリストの再臨に間もなき今日、嘘を云ふ国民は世界の連盟から排斥され、今迄のユダヤ人の様に放浪の民とならねばならないぞ。しつかり性念を据ゑ、本当の事を云つたら何うだ。お前一人の嘘が日出島全体の嘘になるのだ。ここには都新聞も聖地新報も亦回々教新聞も発刊されてゐるから、俺達が記者に会うて夕べの実状を喋らうものなら、汝は此処に居るこた出来ないのだ。ユダヤの女をチヨロまかしやがつて……ユダヤ人全体の敵としてハリツケに会はなならぬが、それでも可いか。汝の出様によつて此方にも考へがある、サアどうだ。判然と返答を聞かして貰はうかい』

『此奴ア近頃迷惑の至りだ。拙者はソンナ覚は決して御座らぬ』

『馬鹿云ふない。汝が隠したつて駄目だ。サロメにもヤコブにもチヤンとテクが調べ上げて来てあるのだ。グヅグヅしてると、四人の奴ア、ユダヤ人の怨府となつて、忽ち寂滅為楽の運命に陥るが、それが可哀相だと思つて、おれ達三人が談判に来たのだ』

『何うすれば可いと云ふのだ』

『ザマア見やがれ、ヤツパリ覚があるだらう。汝の命とつり替への一万両、ここへオツぽり出せ。さうすりやおれ達や沈黙を守つてやる。俺達三人の外にやお月さまより見たものはないのだから、お月さまが仰有らぬ限り分る気遣はない。こんな事を都新聞の記者にでも話さうものなら、二万両や三万両の報酬を呉れるに違ひない。何しろ一方はルートバハーの宣伝使、一方は貴族の娘サロメさまといふのだからな……何しろ可い金儲の種を見つけたものだ。イヒヽヽヽ』

§ 2

Speaker: 'What, ten thousand ryō? That is tautei impossible. Ah, for the sake of our Sekai dōhau, please be patient and accept one itsupū. Perhaps there will be some... Umeaha later on...'

Speaker: 'Even if you say one itsupū, even one itsusen is an itsupū. Ten thousand ryō is also an itsupū. If it is an itsupū, that is fine, but I would like you to state clearly how much it is.'

Tsūro said:

Speaker: 'Hey, Teku, you do not need to be so noisy as if your backside were on fire. No matter what, he is a cunning Sendenshi who can manipulate the Betsupin Salome and Mariya with Jiiujizai. He would not do anything that would make us lose face. It would be better to leave it to the will of the Seishi.'

Speaker: 'In that case, I will leave it to you. Please make sure that Teku does not lose face.'

Burabāsa, having no other choice, wrapped up Hiyakuen, pushed it forward, and said:

Speaker: 'Here, please be patient with this. It is a Sainan for me as well. I did not fall in love of my own Jibun, and since I met with such a Sainan through the Shizen Nariyuki...'

Teku took the package and said:

Speaker: 'Indeed, you have met with a terrible Sainan. I would like to meet with such a Sainan many times myself... Excuse me, Seishi-sama... I suppose it is fine if I check the contents here for a moment, is it not?'

Speaker: 'Please, feel free to open it as you wish.'

Teku untied the package, looked at it, and made a sour face:

Speaker: 'Hey, do not make a Baka of me. Who would come here to say such unpleasant things for a mere Hiyakuriyō of eye-dirt money?'

Saying this, he threw it on the spot.

Speaker: 'If you do not need this precious money, I shall put it back where it was.'

He quickly picked it up and put it into his pocket. Tonku said:

Speaker: 'Hey, Teku, Tsūro, it is useless to deal with a guy like this. It seems he values money more than his life. There is no need to bother anymore; let us go to the Miyako Shimbun and get a big reward. I feel sorry for this guy and the other three, but it is their own fault, so it cannot be helped. Come, let us go home.'

Tsūro turned to the two and said:

Speaker: 'Hey, wait a moment. Our goal is not something like money. More than that, it would be fine if you just handed over Mariya to us... Hey, Sensei, instead of ten thousand ryō, would you write us a document stating that you will hand over Mariya to us, even if it is a bit expensive? It would be better for you than squeezing your empty pockets, would it not?'

Speaker: 'Mariya is not my woman. Furthermore, even if she were my wife, I could not give her to you while ignoring her own Ishi. Well, let me think about it for two or three days.'

Speaker: 'Hmph, what are you blathering about? Whether we take Mariya or not is up to Teku. The other day on the Mount of Olives, just when we were about to make Mariya our own, you spouted such nonsense that we thought the officials had come and fled, but later we realized it was you, and we gnashed our teeth. Hey brothers, if we bind this guy and bury him in the valley of Zion, Mariya will be ours. Come, do it! One, two, three!'

Speaker: 'All right, here we go!'

With that, both men swung their clubs and attacked Burabāsa. Burabāsa recited the divine words with all his might. The three were struck by the Kototama, and crying out, 'Hey, this guy is dangerous!', they stumbled and scrambled to flee in different directions.

『ヨーシ来た』

と両人は手ンでに棍棒を打振りブラバーサに打つてかかる。ブラバーサは一生懸命に神言を奏上した。三人は其言霊に打たれ、『エー此奴ア大変だ』とこけつ転びつ先を争ひ逃げ散りて行く。
