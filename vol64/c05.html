Chapter 5: The Order of the Most Holy [5]

至聖団〔5〕

§ 1

Burabāsa, accompanied by Subatsufuōdo, made his way to the American Colony. As about a hundred Shinjiya were in the midst of offering their Kiguwan with Itsushiyōkenmei before the Saidan, he and the Raushinshi performed Reihai from the Matsuseki, praying that the day might soon arrive when the Seishiyu would make His Kaurin and initiate the Shingefu for the sake of all Tenkabanmin.

When Ichidō finished their Reihai, they noticed Burabāsa sitting in Tanza beside the unusual guest Subatsufuōdo, and they gazed at him with brows knit in Fushigi. Subatsufuōdo turned to Ichidō and spoke with quiet Kotoba:

Subatsufuōdo: 'Minasama, this Okata is a Seishi named Burabāsa who has come Harubaru from Takasagojima, having received a Shinmei. He is staying at the Sōin hotel, and since he has come on Goshiyutsuchiyō as a Senku for the Sairin of the Messiah, shall we not all engage in friendly Kausai with him?'

Upon hearing these words, Ichidō brightened in Kahoiro as if brought back to life, and with one voice, they repeatedly chanted, 'Thank you, thank you.'

Burabāsa turned toward Ichidō, returned their greeting with deep respect, and began to deliver a short speech of salutation.

ブラバーサはスバツフオードに伴はれて、アメリカンコロニーへと歩を運んだ。百人ばかりの信者が、祭壇の前で一生懸命になつて祈願を凝らす最中であつたので、老紳士と共に末席の方から礼拝をなし、天下万民の為に一日も早く聖主の降臨されて神業を開きたまふ日の来れかしと祈りつつありけり。

一同礼拝を終つて、珍らしき客のスバツフオードの傍に端座せるを見て不思議の眉を潜めて眺めて居る。スバツフオードは一同に向ひ、言葉静に、

『皆さま、此御方は高砂島から神命を奉じて遥々お越しになつたブラバーサと云ふ聖師ですよ。僧院ホテルに御宿泊の方だが、メシヤ再臨の先駆として御出張になつたのですから、お互に親しく交際をさして戴かうぢやありませぬか』

一同はこの言葉に生き復つた様な面色を浮べて、異口同音に『サンキウサンキウ』と連呼するのであつた。

ブラバーサは一同に向かひ厚く礼を復し、且つ一場の挨拶的演説を始めかけた。

§ 2

Burabāsa: 'Goichidōsama, as Seishi-sama kindly introduced me earlier, I am Burabāsa, a missionary of the Lūt Bahā group, dispatched from Takasagojima bearing a divine Shinmei as a forerunner for the Sairin of the Messiah. Having crossed the Kaiyō of Hatsusenmairu, I arrived safely at the station in the noble Jerusalem yesterday evening; however, as it was my first arrival in this Tauchi, I did not understand the customs of the land. While I was walking along the main road at Yufugure, worrying about how I might go to Kanranzan, I unexpectedly met Mariya-sama, a believer of your group, on the way. Not only did she guide me to the Catholic monastery hotel, but despite the late hour, she even showed me around the city and allowed me to pay my respects at the beloved Kanranzan. I am deeply, deeply grateful for this, as it is entirely the fruit of your group being impartial and selfless, and having thoroughly mastered the great divine will of God. Furthermore, even the kind Subatsufuōdo Seishi-sama went out of his way to visit me at the hotel, and I was granted various magnificent teachings. Having listened to the state of your group's pure faith and the beautiful way of life that can be called the crystallization of love, I was choked with tears of gratitude. When I think that even in this dark world where demons run rampant, a holy group of truth, goodness, beauty, and love like yours has been created, I cannot help but be grateful for the benevolent great divine will and the thorough divine Keirin of God. Today, I too have been illuminated by the light of God's benevolence and have realized how deep and noble the love of the Ōkami is. I do not know about the people of the world, but as for Takasagojima, where I was born, until fifty years ago, it was in a state that would be embarrassing even to speak of. Things like the love of Christ, the benevolence of Confucius, and the compassion of Buddha were taught to us as something extremely mysterious, difficult to understand, and high and distant, which could never be reached by the hands of ordinary people. The missionaries of each sect and religion made too much of a mystery out of the teachings of the gods and Buddhas, and treated the divine truths of benevolence, love, and compassion as if they were things outside of this world. However, by the divine law of the circulation of heavenly fortune, two saviors, Iwao and Mizu, appeared in Takasagojima in the Far East, which is called the land of God, and their high, great, broad, and distant love became something extremely close and familiar to us, something that we could not separate from our daily lives even if we tried. How grateful and noble it is. I believe that the fact that you, Goichidōsama, have also well mastered the true essence of love, and while waiting with all your heart for the Sairin of Christ, have transcended the barriers of nationality and religion to establish a holy group, is truly a grand and infinite divine work for all people under heaven, and I cannot stop thanking you for the spirit of your group. It is not that I wish to speak of the shame of my own country, but today, regardless of nationality or religion, I would like to say a word for your reference from the perspective of love for all humanity. To you of your group and the young people of today, my home country of Takasagojima was divided into small enemy states of three hundred feudal lords to an extent that is almost unimaginable; this was the state of things only fifty years ago. Not to mention between Kōshū and Otsushū, even within the same Otsushū, between Āru and Sendā, and between Shieru and Antā, they were in a state of complete enmity, and once one stepped outside one's own village or barrier, it was a reality where one's life could not be guaranteed. Not only that, it was said that the noble part of Bushidō was that each person should be prepared to have seven enemies if a man left his house with a sword at his waist, and it was known as the essence of the spirit of the land of God. Moreover, it was even seen as a common privilege of the samurai to kill commoners, saying that they had the right to kill without punishment. In fact, even thirty years ago, the words Kōshū-jin or Otsushū-jin carried a very distant and cold meaning. Even today, words like government official or commoner give the feeling that a kind of strong barrier has been built. Our fathers, that is, those who participated in the Ishin war, were often surprised when they looked at our childhood, saying that the young people and children of today have become very obedient. In our childhood, we still had a kind of hostility toward children in other towns and villages, and fights between children of different towns and villages were not very rare. It was known as a common thing to see children from other towns, throw stones and roof tiles at them, and shout with joy when they injured them. Because it was to that extent, the era before the Ishin, that is, the era when the three hundred feudal lords were entrenched in various places and constantly fighting, was quite bloody, and one is surprised when one hears the stories of the elders. Today, even children's fights have become extremely rare. It has become such that children of today cannot imagine children of one town and village regarding children of another as enemies. If I think about what this is for, it must be because the self-interested groups of the three hundred feudal lords of Takasagojima, by the will of the Emperor who rules over all, all repented of their past mistakes, brought their sincerity of submission, bowed down before the Emperor, and threw everything away. Because of that, the hearts of the people greatly softened, and the spirit of equality in all directions began to flow through the people. Whatever one may call barbaric, there is nothing more barbaric than holding hostility toward each other and fighting. Therefore, if we define a barbarian as one whose scope of love is extremely narrow, and a civilized person as one whose scope of love is extremely broad, then I cannot help but thank you from the bottom of my heart, as you are truly those who have caused the flower of the great civilization within civilization to bloom ahead of the world. The great god of great compassion and great mercy has carried out a three-thousand-year Keirin to make the whole world on earth a heavenly paradise and to give rest and glory to all people, and now he has lowered his holy traces in Takasagojima. And as a preparation for his descent into this Jerusalem, which is the original residence of the Ōkami, he sent down the holy master of the Mizu spirit to redeem the sins of all people. Also, the fact that the people of the Israelite nation, who are the chosen people of God, have become the leaders, gathered people from various countries to this holy land, and built magnificent halls like the American Colony, is something for which I cannot say how grateful, happy, and promising it is for me. If you would allow me to join as one of the members of this holy group, there would be no greater honor for me. Ah, Toyuke-ōkami-reikō-hōza-se!'

大文明の花を開かせ玉ふたものと、衷心より感謝に堪へない次第で御座います。大慈大悲の大神様は全地上の世界をして、天国浄土と為し、万民に安息と栄光を与へむが為に三千年の御経綸を遊ばして、今や高砂島に聖跡を垂れ玉ひました。そして大神の元の御屋敷たる此エルサレムに御降臨遊ばす、その準備として瑞の御魂の聖主を下し、万民の罪を贖ひたまふこととなつたので御座います。又神の選民たるイスラエル民族の方々が主唱者となつて各国の人々をこの聖地に集め、メシヤの再臨を信じてアメリカンコロニーの如き立派な殿堂を作られて居られる事は、私に採りまして実に何とも言へぬ有難い嬉しい頼もしい事だか判りませぬ。願はくは私もこの聖団員の一人に加へて頂きますれば、身の光栄是に過ぎませぬ。アヽ惟神霊幸倍坐世』

§ 3

With a round of Hakushiyu, he descended from the platform with a spirited air. The Raushinshi immediately ascended the platform and said:

Raushinshi: 'Having just heard the words of the Seishi, I have understood the purpose of this visit to the Seichi. I am certain that all the Danin here share the same opinion as I do. That Takasagojima, which was once a place of constant Sōtō day and night due to being divided and separated, was unified fifty years ago, and that the two Genzui Futahashira of the Kiuseishiyu appeared, must surely be the Shinen Gokeirin of the Ōkamisama of the Sekaiitsutai and the Sairin of the Messiah. Since the removal of all barriers within the country has led to the progress and prosperity of today, it is an undeniable truth that if we proceed further to stop Sōtō throughout the world, love one another, and remove the fundamental hostility between nations and races, human culture will become sacred, and at the same time, the level of its welfare will be greatly enhanced. In short, depending on the breadth of our mutual love, we become either barbaric or civilized. To bring about world peace, that is, for the emergence of the Miroku Shinsei, we must first remove the tangible and intangible great barriers between the peoples of all nations. To dream of world peace and the fulfillment of the Miroku Shinsei without taking this step is surely a foolish act, akin to trying to bring about the fusion of water by keeping the vessels separate. Therefore, we, the members of this organization, are taking the lead in the world to demonstrate the true essence of peace and are engaged in preparations for the Sairin of the Messiah. Today, with the arrival of the Seishi from Takasagojima, I am so moved by the vast and boundless nature of the Gokeirin of the Kamikai that I have expressed these few humble words in place of a formal greeting. I hope that all the Danin will join this Seishi in devoting themselves to the completion of this unprecedented and unparalleled great divine work.'

He descended from the platform with a composed manner. All the Danin applauded, agreeing with the views of Seishi Subatsufo-do, and the hall was filled with a harmonious atmosphere. Maria of Magdala, who had been looking down and choking back tears of emotion until now, slowly rose to her feet, walked briskly onto the platform, and began to deliver a speech with a solemn attitude. The audience applauded to welcome Maria's lecture.

Maria: 'I have just heard the speeches of both Seishi and felt greatly encouraged. I consider it a miracle of miracles that you have come all the way from the distant seas and met with me on the street in front of the station in Jerusalem. There is not the slightest room for doubt that Baraba-sama has truly been entrusted with a divine mission and has come as the forerunner of the Sairin of the Messiah. Together with all of you, I celebrate the Kōrai of the Seishi for the sake of all the people of the world, and I cannot contain my overflowing joy. The descent of the Messiah, the Sairin of Christ, and the fulfillment of the Miroku Shinsei may differ in name, but I believe they all mean the same thing. It is entirely the Gokeirin of the Kamisama that such a happy world is to come, but a living shrine must appear to serve that divine work. First of all, we, as the children of God and living shrines of God, must remove these two great barriers, both tangible and intangible, from the world. The greatest of the tangible obstacles are external military preparations—excluding police armaments—and the closure of national territories. I also believe that the greatest of the intangible barriers is the hostility between nations and races. I also believe it is the hostility between religious groups. To remove these tangible barriers of the world, I believe we must first begin by removing the intangible ones. The Holy Christ said that the Kingdom of Heaven is within you. The Holy Abdu'l-Baha taught that world peace must be built within the hearts of people. The Buddha taught to spread the heart of compassion to the ten directions of the world and not to set limits. The teachings of the Holy Master of the Turku also place the harmony of all humanity as the absolute condition for the fulfillment of the Miroku Shinsei. To speak of the sacred, the spiritual, or the divine is not to speak of anything mysterious or unfathomable; it is the heart of human love—that is, to build no barriers against other nations or races, and to open one's heart and hold feelings of friendship just as one would toward one's own friends. The sole root of these barriers is the self-centered heart and the ego. Fortunately, our sacred organization is a gathering of people who have cast off the heart of self-love and leave everything to the heart of the Ōkamisama; therefore, I am deeply and profoundly grateful that the Ōkamisama has graciously invited the Seishi all the way from Takasagojima and bestowed him upon our sacred organization. Amen.'

Maria finished her speech and descended from the platform while bowing lightly to the audience. The sound of applause echoed like a sudden shower of hail even outside the hall.

Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haema Se.

『只今聖師のお話によつて、今回の聖地御出向も了解いたしました。この団員も定めて私と同じ御意見だと思ひます。個々分立して日に夜に争闘の絶間が無かつたと云ふ高砂島が、今より五十年以前に於て統一せられ、又厳瑞二柱の救世主が現はれたまふたのも、メシヤ再臨世界一体の大神様の深遠なる御経綸で御座いませう。国内の凡ての障壁が取り除かるる事によつて、今日の向上と繁栄を来たす事になつた以上は、猶も進んで世界中が争闘を止めて相親愛し、各国各人種などと云ふ根本的敵愾心を取り去る事によつて、人類の文化は神聖なものとなり之と同時にその福利の程度も大変に高めらるること疑なき真理であります。要するに吾々お互の親愛の範囲の大小によつて、野蛮ともなり文明ともなるのです。世界の平和を来さむがため、即ち五六七の神政出現のためには、各国国民間の有形と無形の大障壁を第一着に取り除かねば駄目です。この挙に出ずして世界の平和、五六七神政の成就を夢みるは恰も器具を別々にして、水の融合を来さうとするものと同様の愚挙では有りますまいか。それ故、吾々団体員は世界に率先して平和の真諦を示し、メシヤ再臨の準備に従事して居るもので御座います。今日は高砂島の聖師の御来着によつて、私は神界の御経綸の洪大無辺なるに感喜の余り、茲に一言蕪辞を述べ御挨拶に代へました。何うか団員諸氏もこの聖師と共に空前絶後の大神業の完成に尽されむ事を希望いたします』

と悠然として演壇を下つた。団員一同は拍手してスバツフオード聖師の説に賛同し和気藹々として堂に溢るるばかりであつた。今迄うつむき勝で感涙に咽んで居たマグダラのマリヤは、やをら身を起してツカツカと演壇の上に立ち上り、謹厳な態度を以て一場の演説を始めかけた。一同は拍手してマリヤの講演を迎へた。

『妾は只今両聖師の御演説を承はりまして、大に心強く感じました。海洋万里の遠方から遥々御越しになり、エルサレムの停車場前の街路に於て妾と会合されました事は実に奇蹟中の奇蹟だと考へます。ブラバーサ様は全く神様の御使命を帯び、メシヤ再臨の先駆として御光来になつた事は寸毫も疑ふ余地は御座いませぬ。妾は皆さまと共に世界万民の為に、聖師の光臨を祝し且つ満腔の喜悦に堪へないので御座います。メシヤの降臨キリストの再臨、五六七神政成就とは名称こそ変つて居りますが、要するに同じ意味だと考へます。斯る目出度き世界になるのも全く神様の御経綸で御座いますが、その神業に奉仕する生宮が現はれなくては成りませぬ。先づ第一に神の子神の生宮たる吾々は、世界にあらゆる有形無形この二つの大なる障壁を取り除かねばなりませぬ。有形的障害の最大なるものは対外的戦備≪警察的武備は別≫と国家的領土の閉鎖とであります。又無形の障壁の最大なるものとは、即ち国民及び人種間の敵愾心だと思ひます。又宗教団と宗教団との間の敵愾心だと思ひます。此世界的の有形の大障壁を除く為には先づ無形の障壁から取り除いて掛らねば成らないと思ひます。聖キリストは天国は爾曹の内に在りと言はれて居ます。聖アブデユル・バハーは世界の平和の人々の心の内に建てられねば成らぬと教へられて居ます。仏陀は慈悲の心を十方世界に拡めて限界を設けるなと教へられて居ます。ツルク聖主の御示教も先づ第一に世界人類の和合を以て五六七神政成就の絶対条件として居られます。神聖とか精神とか霊的とか申すことは、別に不可思議な神秘なものでは無く、人類愛の心即ち他の国民や人種に対して少しの障壁も築かず、胸襟を披いて自分の友人に対すると同様に友愛の心を持つ事で御座います。この障壁をなす唯一の根元は自己心と自我心です。幸に我聖団は自己自愛の心を脱却し、唯何事も大神様の御心に任す人々の集まりで御座いますから、大神様も御嘉賞遊ばして遥々と高砂島から聖師を招き、我々の聖団に与へて下さつたものと厚く深く感謝する次第であります。アーメン』

マリヤは茲まで演じ了り、一同に向つて軽く一礼しながら壇を降る。拍手の声は急霰の如く場の外まで響き渡つて居る。

アヽ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
