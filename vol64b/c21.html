Chapter 21: 不意の官命 [1827]

不意の官命〔1827〕

§ 1

In room number three of the Catholic Sōin hotel, Yaku is keeping watch alone as a chokonun, his bodily wounds having healed to about eighty-nine percent. It was Ayame no Ohana who returned there in a flurry.

Ohana: 'Ya, you are Yaku, well, you have kept watch quite properly. During my absence, did Tora or Ayako come by?'

Yaku: 'Yes, not even a single mouse, nor any other living creature has come.'

Ohana: 'Where has Yamoriwake-sama gone?'

Yaku: 'Heh,' Yaku said while scratching his head,

Yaku: 'While you were hospitalized, Okusama, Tora-sama came, and they were whispering together, looking quite happy.'

Ohana: 'What, Tora came?'

She said this, her blood rushing to her face as if she were about to have a fit of rage.

Yaku: 'Now, now, Okusama, she did come, but Danna-sama gave her a sharp elbow, and in truth, she scurried away. As for that, whether it felt good or not, it was as if three to of bile had been cleared from my system.'

Ohana: 'No, no, that is a lie. They say a former wife does not lie, so the first thing you said must be the truth. Do you think I am an Ohana who would be deceived by such comforting words and have her mood improved? Hmph, do not you dare join in and make a fool of me.'

Yaku: 'Yes, quickly, already, completely, yes, what is it. That, um, that, that thing, that is what it is.'

Ohana: 'Here, Yaku.'

Saying this, Ohana struck the frame of the hibachi with her kiseru,

Ohana: 'Junk like you should leave at once. Instead of me paying you a salary, you should pay what is owed here and leave. I have no more use for you.'

She turned away coldly. As Yaku was hanging his head and scratching it vigorously, Yamoriwake returned, his footsteps echoing on the stairs. The moment Ohana saw him, she grabbed him by the lapels, her voice trembling,

Ohana: 'You, you scoundrel, there is a limit to how much you can make a fool of people. You piece of junk, where have you been wandering about today? Will you not confess clearly?'

Yamoriwake: 'Now, now, Ohana, there is no use in being so noisy, is there? Well, let go of me. I heard you had gone to visit Mount Carmel, so I chased after you, but since I could not see you anywhere, I came back here. I have not been anywhere else, you know.'

Ohana gripped his lapels with all her might, shaking him,

Ohana: 'You scoundrel, there is a limit to how much you can blind people. You rode in a car with Tora and sang propaganda songs, did you not? Ugh, how stifling, I am going back to my home country from now on. Go live and flirt with Tora.'

Yamoriwake: 'Well, that is true. I did, in fact, ride in a car. However, there is a reason and circumstance for this. That wretch Tora, with extreme disrespect, held a grudge against me because you and I got married, and she was singing propaganda songs about you and me. I was so furious I could not stand it, so I jumped on the car intending to push her off, but then I saw you, so I searched around town and came back. If you harbor such ill will, I cannot go on either, can I?'

Ohana: 'Indeed, if I hear it that way, perhaps that is so.'

She let go of him suddenly.

Yamoriwake: 'Ah, with this, I am at least acquitted. Ohana Daimyōjin, no, no, Konohanasakuya-hime-no-Mikoto-sama, thank you for saving me. I am grateful and offer my thanks.'

Ohana: 'Ohohoho, and do you have that propaganda leaflet with you?'

とツンとして横を向く。ヤクは俯向いて頭をガシガシとかいてゐると、階段をトントンと響かせ乍ら、帰つて来たのは守宮別である。お花は一目見るより武者振りつきグツと胸倉を取り、声をふるはせ乍ら、

『こりやこりや、お花、さうやかましう云つても仕様がないぢやないか。マアそこ放してくれ。俺やお前が橄欖山へ詣つたと聞いて、後を追つかけて来たのだが、ネツカラ姿が見えないので、ここへ帰つて来たのだ。それより外は、どつこへも行つては居ないのだからな』

お花は胸倉を一生懸命に掴み、こつき乍ら、

『こりや極道、人を盲にするにも程がある。お寅と自動車に乗り、宣伝歌を歌つてけつかつたぢやないか。エーエ、辛気臭い、私やもう之から国許へ帰る。お寅と意茶ついてお暮しなさい』

『そら、さうだ。俺も実は自動車に乗る事は乗つた。然し、之には曰く因縁があるのだ。お寅の奴、失敬千万な、俺とお前が結婚したのを遺恨に持ちよつてな、お前と俺との宣伝歌を作つて、歌つてるので、業腹で堪まらぬから、自動車からつき落してやらうとヒラリと乗つた所、お前の姿を見たものだから、町をあちこちと探して帰つて来たのだよ。さう悪気をまはしちや、俺だつてやりきれないぢやないか』

『成程、さう聞きやさうかも知れませぬね』

とパツと手を放す。

『ア、これで先づ先づ無罪放免だ。お花大明神、否々木花咲耶姫命様、ようマア助けて下さいました。有難う感謝致します』

『オホヽヽヽ、そしてその宣伝ビラは、お前さま持つてゐるのかい』

§ 2

Yamoriwake: 'Nay, I was in such a haste that I could not fetch even a single sheet, Ichimai. Yet I remember the Monku of the song clearly.'

Ohana: 'As you are Yamoriwake-sama with such a fine Kioku, you surely must remember it. Pray sing it for me to hear Chiyotsuto right here.'

Yamoriwake: 'Very well, very well, do not be angry.'

So saying, he sang a propaganda song right off the top of his head as a test.

O Strike him, punish him, Yamoriwake, Strike her, punish her, Oyame-no-Ohana! Those two Futari of garbage, They are Akugami who disturb this world!

Yamoriwake: 'Thus, such things she spat forth! Is it not outrageous? Itsutai that Otora creature has become Hankiyōran, you see.'

Ohana: 'Naruhodo, she utters terrible things indeed. Now then, pray let me hear what comes next.'

Yamoriwake: 'Yamoriwake's Ōhiroki, And that Ohana creature, the Niseotohime, Plotting a great Yamako, Establishing the Kyō of a new Uranai, To overthrow the true Uranai Kyō And overturn this Reijiyō, Plotting to seize control of the Tenka, They are creatures more terrible than demons or serpents! Awaken, realize, O Jerusalem! O Hitobito of Rauniyakunanniyo, The true, true Kamubashira Is none other than Denokami!'

Yamoriwake: 'Thus, she spat forth such things, and being Hontau angry, I got into the Jidōshiya Chiyotsuto to hold a Danpan. That Otora creature is a person whom neither a lever nor a pole can move.'

Ohana: 'Neither a lever nor a pole can move her; yet it is that very Otora-sama whom you longed for, so thinking I would not know, you secretly concluded a Mitsuyaku, did you not? Nanihodo you may employ the military tactics of Sun Tzu and Wu Qi to trick me, Ohana-sama, I shall not pass even a single copper of these ten thousand ryō to you. Ho-ho-ho-ho. What do you think of these Sendenka flyers?'

So saying, she produced four or five sheets from her bosom and tossed them forth to show him.

Yamoriwake: 'Naruhodo, these... are not what Otora scattered, are they? The Monku is different!'

Ohana: 'They are not what Otora scattered. They were scattered by the garbage called Yamoriwake! Now then, can you offer a reply even to this? Ihi-hi-hi-hi!'

Yamoriwake: 'Nay, I surrender; I am no match for you any longer. Forgive me, for I shall reform my heart from this day forth.'

Ohana: 'Hen! Do as you please. Whether you reform your heart or yield to arrogance, it is your own soul, so my patience is completely exhausted. Hereafter I shall have no relation with you whatsoever. From now on I shall return to my homeland and blow about your and Otora-sama's derailments with umbrella upon umbrella, so be prepared for that! Ho-ho-ho-ho!'

While Yamoriwake was bewildered, unable to utter a single word, Otora came pulling up beside them in a Jidōshiya and climbed up. Otora had fainted for a time, but through the care of a physician she instantly recovered fully, and taking nothing with her in her haste, she had come to see whether Ohana had captured Yamoriwake again and was doting upon him.

Otora, beholding this sight:

Otora: 'Well, well, is it Ohana-sama? How wonderfully intimate you two are! Ho-ho-ho, it is truly envious indeed!'

Ohana set her bad belly firm, looking down her nose at Otora:

Ohana: 'Otora-sama, Nanihodo you may shower us with charming smiles, it is useless. The ten thousand ryō of money is mine, so there is no reason it can be put to your use. How well you two planned together with Yamoriwake-sama to come this far! As expected of Ikumi-sama who once became my master, what formidable skill; I am truly filled with admiration. Now, now, take this animal back with you as you please, for I have not the slightest lingering regret for him!'

Yamoriwake, hearing the phrase "this animal" strike his ears, grew furious and bristled with anger:

Yamoriwake: 'Hey! If I remain obedient, you presume upon it; what do you mean by calling a full-grown man an animal?'

He clenched his fists, attempting to strike at her. Ohana remained calm and composed:

Ohana: 'Ho-ho-ho, the empty dance of a Yanosuke puppet! Do not perform such clumsy tricks as scaring people by wearing a demon's mask. Ah, how foolish! Is Ohana one to yield to such things? A play plotted together with the old hag Otora—now that the backstage is so clearly visible, hen, I feel sorry for you. Rather, tears of sympathy pour forth from my side! Ihi-hi-hi-hi!'

守宮別は言句も出ず、当惑してゐると、そこへ自動車を横づけにして登つて来たのは、お寅である。お寅は一時気絶してゐたが、医師の看護によつて忽ち全快し、又もやお花が守宮別を捉へて、脂下つてゐないかと、取る物もとり敢ず、やつて来たのである。

§ 3

Yamoriwake: 'Hey, Ohana-sama, isn't this really too much? Having been by my side for Jifunen, do you still not understand my heart?'

Ohana: 'Indeed, I really cannot understand it. For you are the Ikimiya-sama of the Hegre-Hegre-Hegre Jinjiya, a Hegre Mushiya who exhausts every conceivable kind of Terentekuda, transforming yourself through Senpenbankuwa and continually wandering astray and playing the trickster.'

Just as they were thus abusing each other, the Kautōgakari of the Jerusalem Police Station entered together with Futari of Jiyunsa, saying:

Kautōgakari: 'I would like to meet Yamoriwake-sama for Chiyotsuto, so please Toritsu me to him.'

The boy who was crouching Kaika, rubbing his hands together like a Kometsuki grasshopper and bowing his waist Ikudo, replied:

Boy: 'Yes, certainly.' He led the way and Annai them to Sangaushitsu.

Kautōgakari: 'Well, hum, I am sorry to say that from Honshiyo, an order of Taikiyo from the Seichi has been issued against Yamoriwake-sama, Otora-sama, and Ohana-sama, so please place your Ukein upon this Shiyomen.'

Yamoriwake: 'This is outrageous. I have no recollection of doing anything bad that would warrant receiving an order of Taikiyo.'

Kautōgakari: 'At any rate, please Goran by reading this Shireishiyo. ...'It is recorded: 'Inasmuch as it is recognized that you disturb the Fūgi of the Seichi and cause Baugai to the Chian, you shall retreat from the Seichi Inai Mitsuka. Should you by any chance refuse, you shall be thrown into prison for three years.' ... I am sorry for you, but you must make your preparations.'

Having obtained the Ukein from the three persons, he descended the stairs with loud footsteps and went away.
