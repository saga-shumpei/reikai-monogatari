Chapter 11: 狂擬 Monster [1817]

狂擬怪〔1817〕

§ 1

Yamoriwake and Ohana finally climbed to the top of Mount Kanransanjiyō, stood in the cool shade of the trees, and while exposing their faces to the wind, they looked relaxed and happy; as they were embracing and kissing for a while, a strange rustling sound came from the woods behind them, which startled the two of them so much that they sprang apart about three sangen, just like cats after they have finished mating.

Ohana: 'What is it, startling people like that? I thought it was Otorasama again, but that hawk, it truly turned my kimodama upside down.'

Yamoriwake: 'Ha ha ha ha, since Otora is the reincarnation of the mitama of the Takahime of old, it is not entirely without innen that a hawk should appear and startle you, fu fu fu fu.'

Ohana: 'Oh, how disgusting, there is nothing good about anything that starts with the sound "ta". Tanuki, Tagosaku, tawake, Takahime, there are plenty, plenty more "ta" words, but they are all things I do not like; and the worst one of all is Takabugashi, and has not even the Lord of Ikahinu finally turned into a demon?'

Yamoriwake: 'Utsu fu fu fu fu, what about Takaamahara, even though it also starts with "ta"?'

Ohana: 'The type of "ta" in that and the "ta" in these are different.'

Yamoriwake: 'How about the "ta" for beating and eating?'

Ohana: 'I am satisfied to be beaten and eaten by someone I love.'

Yamoriwake: 'What about Dr. Tagore or Dr. Stahl?'

Ohana: 'I do not like those blue-eyed, red-bearded Ketaujin at all. When they first came from Onokorojima together with Otorasama, that Ketau-sama who was chattering in English with you, Danna-sama, seemed to have a "ta" attached to him as well.'

Yamoriwake: 'Well, that person, you see, is the famous Dr. Stahl, the doctor of talismans, a professor at Zion University.'

Ohana: 'University professors are such unreliable things. Did they not fail to recognize a distinguished person like you or a beauty like me?'

Yamoriwake: 'No matter what you say, with that style of Otora's, at first glance, there is no dignity. Even for me, it was very difficult to introduce lead as silver and a heron as a crow. Since it was a thinly, thinly plated savior, the raw material showed through immediately, so there was nothing I could do. However, if it were you, the raw material would not be easily seen; after all, you were raised in the capital, so you are not at all comparable to something that has grown old living in a dilapidated house at a mountain temple like Otora. If you had called yourself Hinodewake at that time, it might have gone well.'

Ohana: 'Well, that is true, is it not? No matter how much you polish it, gold is gold and tile is tile. If, Danna-sama, I were to call myself a savior from now on, do you think I would succeed?'

Yamoriwake: 'That is a matter of course. With you, it will be fine. You are a certified savior.'

Ohana: 'Now, Danna-sama, I have a proposal; why do we not set up a New Uranai Religion to oppose Otorasama and peel off the mask of Brabasa? And why do we not try to be revered as the saviors of all the people of the world?'

Yamoriwake: 'That sounds interesting, but as for the role of the savior, should it be you or me?'

Ohana: 'That is decided without even saying it. Even Amaterasu Ōmikami-sama is a woman, is she not? There is no such thing as a male god of peace.'

Yamoriwake: 'Indeed, listen to that, that is right.'

Ohana: 'Even Christianity is spreading throughout the world precisely because it features the Holy Mother Mary. Buddhism is no good with only Amida-sama. Because it features Maya-fujin, who gave birth to Shakyamuni-sama, and also Kanzeon Bosatsu and Benzaiten, who are endowed with the thirty-two marks, Buddhism has spread through the world like a wildfire and has maintained its life to this very day, three thousand years later. Does not even the San-go-kyo feature a goddess called the Konjin of the Earth? No matter what, if you want to start a religion, you must feature a woman.'

Speaker: 'In that case, well, the world will end up completely in a state of Jiyosondanpi, won't it?'

Speaker: 'Why, that is so, even in Ananai-kyō it is said that Reishiyutaijiu is the way, is it not? Does not Rei signify Jiyosei, and Tai signify Dansei?'

Speaker: 'Indeed, Gomotsuto, I understand. In that case, from now on I shall revere you as Ohana Daimiyōjin.'

Speaker: 'Oh no, I dislike being called Ohana. I am the true Ohana born in Nanihazu, as the Kikuwajin Hakase Wani sang: Nanihazu ni sakan ya konohana fuyugomori, ima o harube to sakan ya konohana. Please call me Konohanahimenomikoto. Look at that Mount Zion towering in the clouds; does that mountain not resemble the Fujisan of Hinodejima? Do not the people of the Sekai revere that mountain and call it the daughter of Zion?'

Speaker: 'Indeed, you are certainly one step ahead of me as an actor. In that case, from today I shall formally revere you as the daughter of Zion, Konohanahimenomikoto, and the Daikyōshiyu of the new Uranai-kyō.'

Speaker: 'Ho ho ho ho, do as you please.'

Speaker: 'Are you pleased? Ah, excellent, excellent. With this, the savior of the three thousand Sekai is decided, and the organization of the new Uranai-kyō is established. Now, shall we attempt a grand endeavor with a capital of ten thousand yen?'

Speaker: 'Now, Danna-sama, you speak of ten thousand yen again and again, but if you spend this ten thousand yen, it will be gone. We must keep this money for an emergency and cover our living expenses with the income from the new religion; otherwise, it will not do.'

Speaker: 'My, you are quite the economist.'

Speaker: 'Why, that is so. In the heart of a great city where competition is as fierce as plucking the eyes from a living horse, I am a veteran who bought a fine house from nothing and stirred the hearts of all the men in the city, known as Ayame no Ohana. If one does not know economics, nothing in this world will succeed. If one has money, even a fool looks wise, and one will be flattered as a member of the House of Peers, a member of the House of Representatives, or a chosen representative of the nation. Whether it be a contradiction-member, a coal-wearing-member, a camphor-child-grave-member, or a tombstone-member, it is all the power of money. If I were a penniless wretch, I would look like a fool in your eyes, Danna-sama. And the wrinkles on my face would likely look even deeper.'

Speaker: 'Indeed, I am impressed. However, if we are to establish a new religion from here on, we need a little capital. First of all, we must campaign to the government to receive authorization for religious independence, and we must be prepared for a considerable investment. Since you are to become the savior of the Sekai in one leap, you must be prepared for a little sacrifice.'

Speaker: 'I know well enough that a little campaign money is necessary.'

Speaker: 'It is said that for the ○○-kyō to become independent, one million yen in campaign funds was required, and when the ××-kyō became independent, they scattered five hundred thousand yen. Even if one wants to become a mere puppet politician, five or ten thousand yen will fly away. And suppose, by bad luck, you fail to be elected. It is like throwing one hundred thousand yen into a gutter. Furthermore, your name as a defeated candidate will be broadcast to the whole world. Thinking of that, spending ten thousand yen on campaign funds is cheap, as there is no chance of losing.'

Speaker: 'It is pointless to become the leader of a religion after spending ten thousand yen on campaign funds. Could we not succeed with at least three thousand yen?'

Speaker: 'That is true. If you go out and campaign openly, two or three thousand yen of pocket change will never suffice, but depending on the method of the campaign, it is not impossible to manage with three thousand yen. However, this performance is a play that only I can pull off.'

'Yes, I have certainly understood. Sayō nara.'

They exchanged this light Aisatsu and went down the Sakamichi.

Ayame no Ohana, who was suspicious of everything, came along on tiptoe, Sashiashi and Nukiashi, intending to probe the Jitsupi and hear the Mondafu between Yamoriwake and Tagore, but having heard the single word 'I rely on you, I Shiyōchi,' she finally felt Anshin and was beaming with joy.

Yamoriwake, when he looked back and saw Ohana suddenly poke her face out from the thicket of trees on the Sakamichi, thought, 'Ah, a Shimatsu...', and his Kahoiro changed instantly, but being an Ōchiyakumono by Guwanrai, he feigned ignorance and said,

'Ya, Ohana, were you here? You must have heard every bit of the conversation between the Hakase and me.'

'Yes, I did not hear the Zenbu, but as expected of Yamoriwake-sama, what a great Udemahe, I am also Kanshin. It seems the Hakase has Shiyōchi for us.'

At these words, Yamoriwake felt as if he had escaped from a Koketsu, and while stroking his chest with relief and breathing out with a 'phoo' from the tip of his nose, he said,

'Oi, Ohana, my Udemahe is quite something, isn't it? If it comes to this, you must put up the Sanzenen for the Undōhi. I am sorry to dig into your purse again, though.'

'If it is money for the Mokuteki to reach Jiyōjiyu, I do not mind even if it takes Ichimanriyō. Come, let us go and see the Ritsupa architecture of Zion Daigaku before returning.'

'Even if you say let us return, now that we have jumped out of the Reijō, we have no lodging, do we not? Where on earth do you intend to return?'

'Ho ho ho ho, how naive you are, Yamoriwake-sama. Even if we stayed in a Reijō like that place which is like an alleyway of a Turkish inn, the reputation in the world is so bad that it cannot be helped. Shall we not go and rent a room at a monastery hotel and start our activities in a grand manner?'

'Indeed, that is interesting. Come, let us return quickly; it seems the oil in my engine is about to run out. My sake tank has begun to cry out that it is empty.'

'Let us go to the hotel and have some more sake to our hearts' content, ho ho.'

As they were laughing and trying to descend the Mount of Olives, they met Yaku, who had been working at the reception of the Reijō, coming along briskly.

Yaku, catching his breath, said,

'Ya, you two, I have been looking for you very much. Come, let us return.'

'Here, Yaku-sama, you heard about the state of Otora-sama, did you not?'

'Yes, I went wandering around the streets of Jerusalem looking for your whereabouts, and when I saw a crowd of people like a black mountain at the crossroads, I peeked from within the crowd to see what kind of rare Nanigoto had occurred, and there was Otora-sama with her eyes rolled back. As a result of the police doctor flying over to give her injections and various other care, Otora-sama finally breathed back to life. I certainly saw Tonku and Teku putting Otora-sama into a rickshaw, and while they were guarding her from front and back with their own rickshaws, they were heading back toward the Reijō. It will be troublesome again in two or three days when her body recovers.'

'Here, Yaku-sama, you were so venomous to me, what have you come for?'

'What have I come for? Because I thought I would like to be made your servant. I went to the employment agency to have them find me a job, but there were so many applicants, with five thousand people for three hundred jobs, so there was no way my turn would come around. Please, think of it as saving one man's life and use me. In return, I am fine with just a bowl of food.'

'Since I have remarried Yamoriwake-sama today, please keep that in mind. And please observe the movements of Otora-sama from time to time and report them to me. If you can perform that well, I will use you, thinking of it as keeping one watchdog, ho ho ho ho.'

'If you please, Ohana-sama, or rather, Okusama, is calling me a watchdog not a bit cruel? Isn't that right, Danna-sama?'

He looked at Yamoriwake's face.

'Fu fu fu fu, well, I will treat you well. Try being used by the two of us. I will not treat you as harshly as Otora-sama does.'

'Yes, thank you. If it is something that can be of use to you, I will do anything.'

Here, the three of them hurried down the Mount of Olives, and in order to take lodging at the monastery hotel, they took an automobile from the foot of the mountain and proceeded in high spirits.
