Chapter 5: 横恋慕 [1811]

横恋慕〔1811〕

§ 1

Chasing after Yaku, Otora rushed out of Reijiyō like Yashiya. Tonku, Teku, and Tsūro followed behind Otora, and it was decided that they would divide into three directions to search, running about the main and side streets of the shichiu.

Behind them, Ohana and Yamoriwake were sitting on rattan chairs surrounding a round table, and while remaining silent and somewhat tense, they were looking at each other's faces.

While letting out a big yawn, Yamoriwake said,

Ohana-sama.

Do you have some business with me?

Ah-an, Ohana-sama.

What is it?

Ah-an, Ohana-sama, I say...

What is it? Ah, you are so tiresome. If you have business, please tell me.

Ah-an, you should be able to understand about that much, really, really.

Are you lonely because Ikimiya-sama is not here? You must be very bored.

Ah-an, hey Ohana-sama, do you not understand?

I do not understand.

Heh, when I say ah-an, it should be mostly obvious, right?

Whenever Yamoriwake-sama says ah-an and yawns toward the sky, you end up changing your mind, abandoning the work you were doing until then, drifting off somewhere, and always making Otora-sama anxious; but as for me, Ohana, I do not feel anxious at all, so there is nothing I can do.

Ah-a, sa... ke...

Ho-ho-ho-ho, are you saying you want sake? That is an easy task. However, if I were to let you drink sake while Otora-sama is away, I do not know how much I would be scolded. Even without that, she went off after speaking so harshly to me, so please be patient for a while. She will likely return soon.

No, I have had enough of Otora-sama's strong ego, her making unreasonable excuses, and her deep suspicion. I intend to give up on Otora-sama as of today.

He-hen, you say such clever things. You, who have never forgotten her for even a second, whether sleeping and dreaming or awake and in reality—to think you can say such transparent lies. Yamoriwake-sama, you are quite the experienced person. Even I, Ohana, who have been through a thousand battles in the ways of love, must yield and surrender to you.

No, truly, I have had enough. With that yawn of ah-an as the boundary line, I have completely cut off my thoughts of Otora-sama. More than that, I want a pure, honest woman raised in the capital. Even if she is a bit older, first of all, her skin is different.

Hey, Yamoriwake-sama, if you say such jokes, Otora-sama will pinch your nose again.

Once I have cut ties with Otora-sama, she is a total stranger. If she tries to pinch my nose, I will not stay silent. I am a man, after all; I will immediately go and report her to the Jerusalem police.

Truly, Yamoriwake-sama, have you really had enough? You must be lying.

What, I am serious. We are in front of Otohime-sama, so how could I tell a lie?

If what you say is true, I will open my heart as well; I, Ohana, have also completely lost all affection for Otora-sama as of today. I am thinking of returning to my home country from now on. However, it is a long journey, and I cannot return alone, nor can I speak with foreigners, so I am troubled. If there were someone like you who can speak English, it would be wonderful to have you accompany me, but the world does not always go as one wishes.

Ohana-sama, even if you say you want to return, you need travel expenses; how much do you have in total?

Yes, my daughter said she mortgaged our house to raise money, so she sent me about ten thousand ryō, which I deposited at the local post office, so I should not have any trouble with travel expenses.

Ohana: 'That goes without saying; we are in this together. After all, I have things I must demand of you as well.'

O-Tora, in her extreme rage, was driven to madness and, remaining seated on the floor with a thud, found her breath stifled, her mouth agape, and her upper and lower lips snapping together like a crucian carp intoxicated by mud; taking advantage of that moment, Yamoriwake, fearing that staying long with O-Hana would be dangerous, slipped through a narrow alley and fled toward the direction of Mount Kanran.

『ハツハヽヽヽ、お寅さま以上に愛する女が出来たものだから、愛の深い方へ鞍替したのですよ。それが恋愛の精神ですからな。どうか今迄の悪縁と諦めて下さい。酒を一杯のんでもゴテゴテ云はれるやうな不親切な女房では、やりきれませぬからな』

『こりやお花のド倒しもの、人の男を寝とりよつて思ひ知つたがよからうぞ』

と云ふより早く、そこにあつた角火鉢を頭上高く振り上げ、お花と守宮別との真中を目がけて投げつけた。灰は濛々と立上り咫尺暗澹となつた。お寅はあまりの腹立しさに気も狂乱しドツと尻餅をついたまま、息がつまり口をアングリ、鮒が泥に酔ふたやうに上唇、下唇をパクパクかち合せてゐる、その隙に乗じ守宮別はお花と共に永居は恐れと、細い路地を潜つて橄欖山の方面さして逃げて行く。
