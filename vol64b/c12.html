Chapter 12: 開狂式 [1818]

開狂式〔1818〕

§ 1

Yamoriwake, Ohana, and Yaku rented three connected rooms in the Sōin Hotel, and in the innermost room, they enshrined the Gohonzon of the new Uranai-kyō, the daughter of Zion, Konohanasakuyahime, and decided that Ayame no Ohana would serve as her Ikimiya and Kyōshiyu. The Hotsukishiya were the three of them, a Fūfu and a Shiyujiu, and after the Saiten was completed safely, they moved on to the Nahorahi Shiyuen. To celebrate the founding of the Shinshiukyō, Yamoriwake sang a Shiyukuka from his suspicious Kuchimoto, which was intoxicated with sake.

'Heaven is pure and Earth is pure, The Goreitai of purity and innocence. Ayame no Kimi is now here, The savior of the Sanzensekai. On the mountain of God, famous in the world, As the daughter of Zion, Konohanasakuyahime, She has appeared. To the people sinking in the floating rapids, With the divine virtue of love and goodness, Wrapping the spirit, the light of truth Shines upon the world. How noble it is that she has appeared To open the Ame no Iwato. Now, at the beginning of the world, Lord Izanagi no Ōkami, Lord Izanami no Ōkami, The Fūfu gods appeared. They beheld the Ame no Mihashira and the Kunihashira, And said, "Thou, go around to the right, And I shall go to the left." They declared that they would meet, And began the work of marriage. They gave birth to many children, And at the end of the birth, they created even the mountains, rivers, And the gods of the grasses and trees. Finally, the brilliant and radiant Amateru no Kami was born. Following the ancient tradition of how they established The divine nation in the vast world, We, imitating the two deities Nagi and Nami, Have exchanged sake cups with Ayame no Kimi. For the sake of the gods, And for the sake of the people of the world, we have established this sacred place, And have secured this holy ground. This festival is to save Even the hundred grasses and trees. Ah, Kannagara Kannagara, It is because Ayame no Kimi is here, And because the fellow Yaku is here, That the main pillar of Yamoriwake, Is supported, and today is as it is. Truly, truly, it is magnificent, That the founding of the Shinshiukyō, Has been accomplished in perfection. If Otora had been here, From one to a hundred, from the chaff, To the scraps of rapeseed, She would have pointed and criticized everything. Being pushed around by the Ikimiya of the Hinodewake god, We would never be able to lift our heads for the rest of our lives. Thinking of this, the quarrel the other day, Has turned out to be our Great happiness. In the old proverb, It is well said that all human affairs are like The horse dung of the old man at the frontier. My fierce goddess, No matter how much you call it divine work, It is unbearable, and even my favorite Sake begins to taste bad. Ayame no Ohana, the Kyōshiyu-sama, Who has appeared as the daughter of Zion, Though I have no right to speak, Even if frost is visible on my head, It seems to add a refined charm. This is also entirely the work of the gods, Without a single leak. Yamoriwake also feels as if he has become Twenty or thirty years younger. Ah, how grateful, how grateful. Above all else, first of all, Drinking the sake of the water of life, I have the beautiful lady of the past, Serving at my side, a graceful figure. From between lips like plum blossoms, Appear from time to time, The luster of teeth like ivory, The color of nails like agate, And the glow of cheeks like plum blossoms. I feel as if I have monopolized The happiness of heaven and earth. Ehe-he-he-he, ehe-he-he-he. If that fellow Otora, Were to peek at this place for a moment, She would surely cry, she would surely be angry. She would grab our hair, And scream in a piercing voice, Causing a great commotion in the neighborhood. The sake bottle would dance in the air, And the trays and tea bowls would clatter, Crushed into tiny pieces. It would surely result in a miserable scene, Like a mountain of flowers after a storm. Ah, Kannagara Kannagara, I pray for the divine favor of the spirit.'

Ohana: 'Oho-ho-ho, as expected of this person, what a magnificent and witty song. Even listening from the side, my chest feels clear and my head feels refreshed. Why have you kept such wisdom hidden until now, Danna-sama? It is well said that the cat that catches mice hides its claws. Regarding the person of Ayame no Ohana, Danna-sama, you are truly a good find.'

Nikumiya: 'Very well, be at ease regarding that matter. It is like a weasel caught in the fish preserve of Tanaike. There is no chance of us being separated until the very end of our lives.'

Ikimiya: 'Even Otora-sama, whom you cherished so much, you discarded like a worn-out shoe, pretending not to know her at all. I cannot bear it if I were to be treated as the second Otora-sama.'

Nikumiya: 'If you worry that much, there is no end to it. The degree to which I love you is just like ants clinging to a lump of sugar. If it were different, it would be like a snake aiming for a frog; I would stick to you thoroughly to the very end, you know.'

Ikimiya: 'Ho ho ho, to say that I am like ants on sugar is not something I find very gratifying.'

Nikumiya: 'Even so, Ohana, or rather my wife, Ikimiya-sama, it is something to be grateful for. There is a saying that people appreciate sweet things, but do not appreciate acrid or bitter things, is there not?'

Ikimiya: 'Danna-sama, you are looking down on me as being quite sweet, are you not? It is too much to compare me to sugar.'

Nikumiya: 'But Ohana, you are sweet. Since all things called flowers possess sweet nectar, do not bumblebees and hornets buzz and bite into them? It is only natural that I, too, would want to suck the nectar of Ohana. Because Ohana is not only sugar, but also possesses the soil of Sado which is even sweeter than sugar, I like you all the more. Ehe he he he.'

Ikimiya: 'Again and again, please do not say such old-fashioned things as the soil of Sado. Ho ho ho ho.'

Nikumiya: 'Now, Nikumiya-sama. Since today is the celebration of the founding, I would like the essential Ikimiya-sama to sing a declaration song.'

Ikimiya: 'I feel somewhat embarrassed and cannot sing.'

Nikumiya: 'Hen, what are you saying? Now that you are married, have you learned to be shy like a young girl? Whether the sun is a snake, ants are sea bream, caterpillars are whales, mountain yams become eels, or bamboo leaves become loaches, you must sing the declaration song today. I must write down that song, send it to the printer, make flyers, ride in an automobile, and scatter them throughout the city.'

Ikimiya: 'My, oh my. It is nerve-wracking to try to become a savior. In that case, the daughter of Zion, Ikimiya of Konohanahime, shall sing the declaration song. Please write it down without missing a single word.'

Nikumiya: 'Yes, very well. I have understood. I know. However, Yaku-sama has a much more skillful pen. Yaku, would you become the scribe for me?'

Yaku: 'Yes, I shall humbly accept the task.'

Nikumiya: 'Un, good, good. Ah, with this, the singer, the listener, and the scribe—the three beats are complete. How auspicious, how auspicious. Now, Ikimiya-sama,'

Nikumiya: 'Sing, oh sing, / Singing will not diminish your beauty, / Ah, kori ya kori ya.'

He sings and stands up to begin dancing. Ayame no Ohana holds a fan with the rising sun design in both hands, and dragging her long hem, she begins to perform her specialty dance with grace.

Ayame no Ohana: 'This place is the center point of the world, / Even the world of darkness is Palestine, / The capital of wonders, Jerusalem. / When the time comes for the plum blossoms of the three thousand worlds / To bloom all at once, / The unprecedented savior, / The daughter of Zion, Konohana / Sakuya-hime descends again, / Using the body of Ayame no Ohana / Freely and at will. / Grandmother Otora and Burabasa, / And the incomprehensible messengers, / Are struck down in the blink of an eye. / To open the most refined and pure holy path / Is this precious evening. / Let the flowers bloom and the butterflies dance, / Let the thousand-year cranes dance in, / Let the turtles crawl in and celebrate, / Worship the house of God. / Soon, the independent religion...'

Raising the great banner, showering the rain of joy upon the people of the Sanzensekai, the Shiyōshinshiyōmei Kiuseishiyu, who is sung of as the Ikigami-sama, the dawn of that day draws near. The Otohime of the Riugūkai has granted leave as of today, and the Nikumiya of Konohanahime-no-Mikoto, who is endowed with the Sanjifunisau and the Sansau, has now appeared here as the daughter of Zion. Saving every corner of the Sanzensekai without missing a single soul, this day is truly Medeta. The Ten is clear and vast, and upon the earth, the Aogusa of spring are sprouting, as if awaiting the Daikaurin of the Savior since the Kaibiyakuirai.

Thinking of it, thinking of it, how Arigata it is. Rejoice and celebrate, O hundred people. Adore the Kiuseishiyu, the Nikumiya of Ayame-no-Ohana.

Ohana: 'Ho ho ho ho, please, bear with me just this much. I feel somehow embarrassed and cannot continue any further.'

Yaku: 'Meumeu. Atsupareatsupare. You are truly the Savior of the Tenka. Yaku, you must have been impressed as well. Compared to Otora-sama, which one do you think is more Ritsupa?'

Yaku: 'Well, that is so, it is truly so.'

Yaku: 'That is so, but that does not answer the question; I am asking which one is superior.'

Yaku: 'Hee hee, well, they are in completely different leagues; they cannot be compared at all.'

Yaku: 'Now, now, Yaku-sama, which one do you say is superior?'

Yaku: 'Yes, well, no matter what one says, no matter what, it is as it is. Well, as expected, the one that is superior is superior.'

Yaku: 'You are a man who says strange things. Will you not speak clearly?'

Yaku: 'Since the person herself is here, please try to guess for the most part.'

Ohana, thinking that she herself was being praised, filled her face with a smile, narrowed her eyes, and while glancing at Yaku's face, said: 'Ho ho ho, as expected, Yaku-sama has a discerning eye. That is why you are the supporting pillar of Moriyamawake-sama; I am counting on you.'

The Naorai ceremony finally concluded, and Ohana, while keeping the three thousand yen in cash she had withdrawn from the post office in her pocket, left behind word that she was going to campaign for the independence of a new religion. Moriyamawake left the hotel in a wandering manner, and instead of going to Tagore's mansion, he headed toward the brothel in front of the station.
