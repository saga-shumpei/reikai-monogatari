Chapter 15: 騒淫ホテル [1821]

騒淫ホテル〔1821〕

§ 1

Yamoriwake was thinking with bated breath about the state of Ohana's mind, but as there was no major arrival of low pressure, he felt at ease and ended up sleeping until past ten o'clock the next day. Ohana, feeling that something was not quite right, did not sleep a wink, her corneas bloodshot, and from early in the morning, she shook Yaku awake, seated herself in the next room, and in a slightly lowered voice,

Ohana: 'Hey, Yaku-sama, you said last evening that you have a daughter named Ayako, didn't you?'

Yaku thought to himself, 'I carelessly spoke of my child; if there is any connection to Yamoriwake, I will be caught in the middle and unable to remain in this house. It is best to play the fool,' and he steeled his resolve.

Yaku: 'Yes, I did say that. But that was a lie. I just said such a thing for the sake of amusement. Why, how could a person like me have a daughter, even half a one? If I had a daughter, I would not be in such distress.'

Ohana, while tapping the edge of the hibachi with her long pipe, shook her head from side to side.

Ohana: 'No, no, that won't do. If Ohana's eyes take one look, no matter how much you hide it, it is no use. Come, tell me quickly. You are a good boy, after all.'

Yaku: 'No matter how much you say I am a good boy, I am a single man; I have no good child or bad child, no wife, no spouse, no mistress, no geisha, nothing at all.'

Ohana: 'No matter what you say, your face is that of a lady-killer who has just shed his bitter skin. Whether seen from the front or the side, you have a style that makes women fawn over you. With your willow-like eyebrows, your sharp eyes, your dark pupils, and the shape of your nose and mouth, is there any woman who would cast aside a handsome man like you? You have a face that looks like you have killed many women. Come, come, please tell me the truth. I will never complain to Yamoriwake-sama, and I will do nothing that would cause you trouble. Instead of only favoring Yamoriwake-sama, why don't you show a little favor to Ohana as well?'

Yaku: 'Ah, ah, what a troublesome thing this is. If I please you, I do not please the master, and if I think of the master, I am scolded by you; I have no place to stand either.'

Ohana: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, look at that. The frog has finally confessed from its own mouth, has it not? Where exactly is this geisha called Ayako kept?'

Yaku: 'Yes, there is no other way, so I will tell you. But if, because I spoke, you two have a marital quarrel, I will have no choice but to run away from here. If that happens, like a stray dog that has lost its master, I will have no choice but to become a vagrant. That would also bring shame upon my dear daughter, and unless I am given a guarantee for an emergency, I cannot speak.'

Ohana: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, what a shrewd man you are. But given your position, it is understandable. In that case, I will slip you a hundred-yen note, so tell me everything.'

Saying this, she took a hundred-yen note from her purse and slipped it into Yaku's pocket.

Yaku: 'Ha, as expected of Ohana-sama, the savior of the three thousand worlds, the daughter of Zion, the living shrine of Konohanasakuya-hime, the Ayame Ohana-sama. I receive this with gratitude. I bow down and pay homage, I respectfully request, I respectfully request again.'

Ohana: 'Hey, hey, that is gloomy; is that not irrelevant? Please tell me the facts quickly. If you dawdle, won't Yamoriwake-sama wake up?'

Yaku: 'Yes, in that case, I will tell you. There is indeed a daughter named Ayako. Now, please forgive me with this.'

Ohana: 'Look at that, you do have a daughter, don't you? My eyes were not mistaken, were they?'

Yaku: 'No, truly, I am overwhelmed. The insight of the living shrine is something that we, mere commoners and low-born, can never fathom, heh, heh, heh.'

Daughter: 'What is this, a strange place.'

Saying this, she opened the door to the nigaushitsu and stepped inside, where she found the man she longed for sleeping and dreaming of the night. Ayako approached his side and said,

Ayako: 'Excuse me, Urushiwake-sama, it is Ayako. Please, wake up. Do you have any idea what time it is?'

Morimiyawake seemed to hear Ayako's seductive voice, for he rose up restlessly and said,

Morimiyawake: 'Oh, was it you, Ayako? I had a scary dream. I will return to the hotel for a moment.'

Ayako: 'Ho ho ho, my dear husband, what are you sleep-talking about? This is the nigaushitsu of the Catholic Monastery Hotel.'

Ayako: 'Is it wrong for a wife to come looking for her husband? Do not make such a fool of me. I thought your behavior was suspicious, so I came to investigate, and I find a monster-like woman sleeping in the next room, do I not? That must be your leco. Oh, how frustrating and regrettable.'

She then clawed at Morimiyawake's face without regard for the place. Long claw marks appeared on Morimiyawake's face, running like streaks of rain from his forehead down to his throat, leaving welts.

Morimiyawake: 'Ah, forgive me, forgive me! If you claw at me like that, it hurts, does it not?'

Ayako: 'Eh, you thought I was a young, naive girl and made a fool of me, did you? Now, it is a matter of life and death. I will bite into your throat and take your life!'

Yaku was considering the Yōsu from outside the door, but as the Keisei felt Fuon for some reason, he tried to open it and enter; however, since the lock was fastened from the Naibu, he could not advance even an Itsupo, and was stamping his feet in Jidanda outside the door.

Inside, Yamoriwake and Ayako were in the Matsusaichiu of a fierce Genkuwa; Yamoriwake was having his Tanenasu squeezed with all her might, and while screaming, 'Kanninkannin, Hitogoroshi,' he managed to undo the lock and escaped with his Inochi Karagara to Room Daisangaushitsu.

Ohana, who had been in a state of Sotsutau due to this Monōto, regained consciousness and saw Yamoriwake and the young woman locked in a struggle, grappling with each other.

Ohana, flaring with rage, shouted:

Ohana: 'Hey, you slut, stealing another woman's man, prepare yourself. This Ohana is also in a state of Shinimonoguru.'

She grabbed Ayako by the hair and dragged her around. Being Saina by both Yamoriwake and Ohana, Ayako cried out:

Ayako: 'Hitogoroshihitogoroshi, Father-sama, please help me.'

Even Yaku, unable to bear seeing the Kinan of his own child, shouted:

Yaku: 'You Inran Baba.'

He clenched his fists and struck Ohana, making no Kubetsu between her head and face, in a Chiyōchiyōhatsushi manner.

Amidst the strange and unusual sounds of 'Kya-kya, Gata-gata, Batan-batan, Dotan-dotan,' the boys of the hotel also came rushing up the stairs, each trying to be the first, and they too became embroiled in the fighting and beating; as there was no knowing when it would end, Yaku decided to go to the police to report the matter and request reinforcements, but as he ran down the stairs, he accidentally fell, vomited blood, and ended up stretched out flat like a crushed frog.

This commotion was finally settled when the hotel manager intervened; Ayako was sent to the Ariake house, and Ohana was admitted to the hospital in Jerusalem until her injuries could heal.
