Chapter 10: 拘淫 [1816]

拘淫〔1816〕

§ 1

In the shade of the trees on the slope of Kanranzan, four or five Druze, Arabs, and Yudayajin are indulging in zatsudan with an omoshirosau, still wearing their raudōfuku.

Bargan, one of them, says: 'Oi, Gakushii, they say you went to the war the other day, but did you receive a Kinshikunshiyō or something? You went off saying you would return after performing brilliant kōmiyō and tegara, and were sent off by the kinjiyogatsupeki with great fanfare, but I have never even heard of a Gaisenihahi, and before I knew it, you had come mahimodo to us fellow raudōshiya; what on earth happened with the situation of the battle?'

Gakushii: 'Because of that hanran by the Druze tribe, I was hired by the Futsugun and when we shingun to Jier, the capital of Jebel Druze, the power of the Druze tribe was shiyōketsu, the Futsugun was easily uchiyabu, and in a miserable state, we were scattered to shihau and hatsupau for a time. At that time, I was a military laborer, and in truth, I was doing transport in the rear, but because cannon shells were falling nearby with a thud, everyone lost their nerve, and it was a miserable state where the essential military laborers were being carried on stretchers by the soldiers.'

Bargan: 'Why was the elite Futsugun, with such well-equipped weaponry, so easily uchiyabu by the Druze tribe, a mere mob? Isn't that a bit unbalanced?'

Gakushii: 'That is what they call war being a fickle thing. Whether the side with more soldiers wins, or the side with better-equipped weapons wins, or the side with no weapons but fewer soldiers wins, it is a matter of luck, so you never know. At any rate, the Druze tribe were well-versed in the geography down to the last private, and on top of that, they had harmony among the people, and since they had that much popularity, it stands to reason they would win even just on that bluff. With only about twenty thousand rebel troops, fifty thousand French soldiers threw everything they had—airplanes, cannons, and supply wagons—only to be defeated while barely saving their lives, and because the Druze applied the enemy's weapons and continued to fight to the bitter end, the Futsugun finally had the capital of Jeda occupied. It has truly become a time when the strong are weak and the weak are strong.'

Bargan: 'If that is the case, we too are struggling as the weak of society, soaked in sweat beneath the horizon, but I wonder if there will ever be a time when we can raise our heads again.'

Gakushii: 'Indeed, it is a world of impermanence. Since it seems some god or other has said that the world will not be allowed to remain as it is forever, the future is surely the realm of us proletarians. Well, do not worry so much, and be patient for a while; even today, as I was walking peacefully through the town of Jerusalem, those spies kept tailing me, saying my appearance was ugly or suspicious. And when they speak... they ask where you came from, where you are going, what your business is, how old you are, what your name is... and they perform such worthless meddling that one cannot even walk the road in peace. It is because the officials standing above us have become as nervous as guard bees in a beehive poked by a child.'

Bargan: 'It is truly a hopeless world; if we leave it like this forever, the world will surely become a mess. I think that at this rate, a great revolution will occur within ten years.'

Gakushii: 'That is right, for the cries of poverty and unemployment have become this loud. If the bourgeois class and the officials do not wake up soon, right now, our positions will be reversed with them, and they will be in a miserable state. I cannot die until that world comes. We have been oppressed by them for generations, and it is the duty of us descendants to wipe away the shame of our ancestors. Just before, at the foot of this mountain, a man called Trotsky was gathering labor groups and farmers' groups and giving a radical speech, and when I listened, everything from one to ten was perfectly reasonable. However, if I were to be seen listening to such things, I would be caught in the police web like a spider's web and thrown into a dark place whether I liked it or not, so thinking that a wise man does not approach danger, I came here walking briskly, and your gathering, surely by now, might have some wild commotion starting.'

『オイ、ガクシー、汝は此間の戦争に行つたといふ話だが、金鵄勲章でも貰つたのか。花々しき功名手柄をして帰るなぞと云ひよつて、近所合壁に送られ、大変な勢であつたが、凱旋祝も根つから聞いた事もなし、いつの間にか吾々労働者仲間に舞戻つて来よつたが、一体戦ひの状況は何うなつたのぢやい』

ガクシー『ドルーズ族のあの叛乱によつて仏軍から雇はれ、ジエーベル・ドルーズの都、ジエールに進軍した時、ドルーズ族の勢猖獗にして、仏軍は手もなく打破られ、みじめな態で四方八方へ、一時は散乱して了つたのだ。其時俺は軍夫として、実の所は後の方に輸送をやつてゐたが、大砲の弾が、間近にドンドン落ちて来るので、何奴も此奴も腰を抜かし、肝腎の軍夫が、兵隊に担架に乗せられて運ばれるといふ惨めな態だつたよ』

『あれ丈軍器の整うたフランスの精兵が、なぜ又暴民団体たるドルーズ族に脆くも打破られたのだ。チとバランスがとれぬぢやないか』

『そこが所謂戦争は水物といふのだ。兵数の多い方が勝つ共、武器の整頓した方が勝つとも、又は武器の調はない兵数の少ない方が勝つとも、それは時の運だから分らないワ。何しろドルーズ族は一兵卒に至る迄地理には精通して居る上、人の和を得てる上、あれ丈の人気だつたから、其虚勢丈ででも勝なねばならぬ道理だ。僅か二万位の叛乱軍に五万のフランス兵が、飛行機も大砲も輜重車も、何もかも打ちやつて、命カラガラ敗北して了ひ、ドルーズは敵の武器を応用して、あく迄も頑強に戦ひを続けるものだから、仏軍はたうとうジェーダの首都を占領されて了つたのだい。本当に強い者の弱い、弱い者の強い時節になつたものだ』

『さうすると、俺達も社会の弱者として、地平線下に汗にひたつて蠢動してゐるのだが、何時か又頭をあげる時があるだらうかな』

『あらいでかい、有為転変の世の中だ。いつ迄も世は持切にはさせぬと、どつかの神さまもいつてゐる相だから、未来は必ず吾々プロレタリヤの天下だ。まあまあクヨクヨ思はずに、暫く辛抱するのだな、今日も今日とて、エルサレムの町を温順う歩いてゐると、俺の風体が醜いとか怪しいとか云ひやがつて、スパイの奴、何処迄も尾行してうせるのだ。そして吐す事にや……君はどつから来た、そして何処へ行く。何の用だ。年は幾つだ。姓名は何といふ……などと三文にもならぬおせつかいを遊ばすのだから、道も安心して歩けやしないワ。丸で上に立つてゐる役人共は、子供につつかれた蜂の巣の番兵蜂の様な神経過敏になつてゐやがるのだからのう』

『本当に約らぬ世の中だの、何時迄も此儘にして置こうものなら、世界はメチヤメチヤになるだらうよ。どうしても此調子では十年たたぬ内に大革命が起るだらうと思つてゐるのだ』

『そらさうだ、生活難や就職難の叫びがこれ丈喧しくなつて居るのだもの。ブル階級や役人共も可いかげんに目を醒ましやがらぬと、たつた今、俺達と地位転倒して彼奴等は惨めな態になるだらうよ。俺や其世が来る迄は死んでも死なれないのだ。先祖代々から彼奴等に虐げられて来たのだもの、祖先の恥を雪ぐのは、吾々子孫たる者の義務だからなア。最前も此山麓でトロッキーとかいふ男が、労働団や農民団を集めて過激な演説をやつて居つたが、聴いてみれば一から十迄御尤も至極だ。併し乍ら、あんな事を聞いて居らうものなら、蜘蛛の巣をはつた如き警察の網にかかつて、厭応なしに、暗い所へブチ込まれちや大変だと思ひ、君子は危きに近付かずといふ筆法で、ここ迄スタスタやつて来りや、君たち御連中の御集会、屹度今頃にや、何か乱痴気騒ぎが始まつてるかも知れないよ』

§ 2

Speaker: 'If only a firm Giseishiya would appear, it would be fine. If that happened, Oretachi could seize the Giyofu no ri and live in Anraku, but every Doitsu and Koitsu is just a crafty Ningen, and since no Ōbaka appears who would stake his own Shinmei and stand at the Yaomote, it is utterly Dame. In times like these, if one is not an Ōbaka, one cannot accomplish the Kaizau of the Sekai, you see.'

Speaker: 'That is so. Even the Shiuchiyō of the Druze tribe, Kambus, stood at the Yaomote with great force at first, made his Niman Minshiu carry Buki, fought bravely against the French army, and for an Ichiji achieved a Daishiyōri; but when it came to the crucial moment, he suddenly became Ojikeda and fled to an Anzen Chitai, so the Shiki of the Zengun suddenly fell into a state of Sosau, the capital they had taken with such effort returned to the hands of the French army, the leaders were all bound, a huge indemnity was imposed upon the Druze, and though it was finally settled by the issuance of an amnesty decree due to the entreaties of Kambus, the Druze fell into a terrible plight. If there were only someone who would be a Giseishiya thoroughly to the end, such things would not happen, but no matter what one says, they are a rabble, so they cannot oppose the Brahmins to the very end. When I think of this, is not the future of us proletarians also dark? It is of no use to secretly urinate on the gate of the government office or defecate there in spite, and even if one tries to blow up one or two of the leaders with a bomb, it is like chasing flies off one's food. One after another, worse fellows appear, issuing ever stricter laws against us, and they make us face situations as if our hands were tied with stone, such as arresting us even for talking when three gather, so it is indeed a season where the weak are weak and the strong are strong... but there is no help for it. A season where the strong are weak and the weak are strong comes only about once in ten thousand years. It seems a propagandist called Barabasa has come from Hinodewake and is babbling that a savior will soon appear, or that God will appear in the open to distinguish between good and evil, or that the bottom will become the top and the top the bottom, but this is likely nothing more than a kind of advertisement for spreading religion. No matter how much religion preaches love, if it does not give us bread, we cannot maintain our right to exist. ...Oh, a noisy sound has started down the mountain. It seems that fellow Trotsky has started a struggle with the police squad. Shall we slowly go down and watch the exhilarating scene of the battle? Even if I lurk here in the shade of a tree like a lion's cub or something, cursing the world and screaming, there is no income of a single mon, and no pleasure either.'

Speaker: 'Wait, wait, this is not the time to go out. It would be Taihen if we got caught in the crossfire and were struck down; it is a clever way of living to first observe the silence after the storm. Rather than that, shall we tell some interesting stories out of spite?'

Speaker: 'How could there be any interesting stories for Oretachi? In our miserable circumstances, being worked hard by the bourgeois class from morning till night, given only a meager wage, and barely sustaining our lives with toil, we cannot understand any interesting flavor at all, and there is only suffering. It was five or six years ago, but since there was no help for it, I was working as a rickshaw man, and the landlord, while taking higher rent than others, would call me names like a servant or something, shouting "Gakushii, Gakushii," and even order me to clean the toilet. I was furious and could not stand it, but he would tell me to get out of the house if I was afraid, and at this time when every corner of the back alleys was packed with the poor, if I were thrown out of here, the parent and child would immediately have to sleep outdoors, so I had to endure it, but in the end, he even started calling my wife by her name without any honorifics. It was so disgusting and nauseating that my chest felt like it was going to burst. So, I picked up a stray dog that was starving and dying on the side of the road, gave it the landlord's name, and shouted in a loud voice, "Hey, Gonshu, Gonshu..." and every time I did so, I would punch it on the head with my fist, thinking it was the landlord Gonshu. At that time, it was a little exhilarating, but the stray dog was a huge eater, so my wife and children were about to starve. I could not help but be taken aback by this, but still, a Ningen has his pride. If I cowered in such a place, I would not be a man, so I put unnecessary effort into it, and no matter how much I worked, it was all taken by the dog. Just when I was exhausted, the landlord Gonshu suddenly started keeping three large dogs, and he gave them my name, my wife's name, and my son's name, and the whole family would gather and call them out, so I thought there was no help for it if I cowered here. I thought I would gather more dogs and give them the names of the landlord's whole family and call them out, but when I thought about it carefully, there were three couples in that big house, and with children and grandchildren, there were twenty-eight of them in total, so I finally gave up, rolled up my flag, sheathed my sword, and for a time fled to the city of Jēru. It is truly a hopeless situation.'

『誰でも可いから、確りした犠牲者が現はれると可いのだがなア。さうすりや俺達ア、漁夫の利を占て安楽に暮せるのだけれど、何奴も此奴も小ざかしい人間許りで、自分の身命を賭して矢面に立つといふ大馬鹿が出て来んで、サツパリ駄目だ。かういふ時にや、どうしても大馬鹿でなけりや、世界の改造が出来ないからのう』

『そらさうだ。ドルーズ族の酋長カンバスでさへも、始めは大変な勢で矢面に立ち、二万の民衆に武器を携帯させ、フランス軍と勇敢に戦ひ、一時は大勝利を博しよつたが、いよいよ茲といふ所で、俄に怖気立ち、安全地帯に身を逃れよつたものだから、全軍の士気頓に阻喪し、折角取つた首都も再び仏軍の手に帰し、重立つた者は何れも縛につき、ドルーズ族へは莫大な賠償金を云ひ付けられ、ヤツトの事で、カンバスの哀願に仍つて、大赦令を布かれ、一件落着するはしたものの、ドルーズは酷い破目に陥つたものだ。徹底的にどこ迄も犠牲になるといふ奴さへあれば、あんな事は無いのだけれどな、何と云つても烏合の衆だから、バラモンには最後迄敵する事は出来やしないワ。之を思ふと吾々プロレタリヤの前途も暗澹たるものだないか。腹いせまぎれに、夜中密かに役所の門に小便を屁りかけたり、糞を垂れた位では何にも効はないし、大頭の一疋や二疋爆弾でやつてみた所で、飯の上の蠅を追ふやうなものだ。先ぐり先ぐり次から次へと、だんだん悪い奴が現はれて、益々吾々に対して厳しい法律を発布したり、三人寄つて話をしても拘引するといふ、石で手をつめたやうな目に会はすのだから、矢張、弱い者の弱い、強い者の強い時節だ……と云つても仕方がない。強い者の弱い、弱い者の強い時節は万年に一度位しか、廻つて来るものぢやない。何だか日出島からブラバーサとかいふ宣伝使がやつて来て、今に救世主が現はれるとか、神が表に現はれて善と悪とを立別けするとか、下が上になり、上が下になるとか、ほざいてゐるやうだが、これも一種の宗教拡めの広告に過ぎないだらう。何程宗教が愛を説いても、パンを与へてくれなくちや、吾々は生存権を保持する事が出来ないのだからなア。……ヤ、何だか山下に当つて、騒がしい声がし出したぞ。トロッキーの奴、どうやら警官隊と格闘を始めたらしいワイ。ソロソロ降りて壮快な戦闘振りを見物せうぢやないか。獅子の子か何ぞのやうに、かう木かげに潜伏して、世を呪ひ、悲鳴をあげて居つても、一文の所得もなし、愉快もないからのう』

『おけおけ、コンナ時に出るものぢやない。側杖をくつて打ち込まれちや大変だ、先づ嵐の後の静けさを見聞するのが、処世上悧巧なやり方だ。それよりも腹いせに何か面白い話をせうぢやないか』

『俺達に面白い話があつて堪らうかい、朝から晩までブル階級に酷きつかはれ、僅な賃銭を恵まれて、孜々として僅に露命をつないでる悲惨な境遇にあつては、到底面白い味も分らず、苦しい事許りだ。俺が五六年前の事だつたが、仕方がないので、人力車夫をやつてゐると、家主の奴、人並よりも高い店賃を取り乍ら、従僕か何かのようにガクシーガクシーと口ぎたなく呼つけにしやがつて、雪隠の掃除までいひ付けくさる。劫腹でたまらないが、恐れば家を出て行けと云ひやがるし、裏店の隅々迄貧民でつまつてゐる此際、此処を放り出されたが最後、忽ち親子が野宿をせなくちやならず、仕方がないので辛抱して居ると、しまひの果にや、おれの嬶の名を呼捨にさらすのだ。けつたいの悪いの胸糞が悪いのつて、胸が張裂ける様だつた。そこで俺は道路の端に餓ゑて、死にかけてる野良犬を一疋拾つて来て、そいつに家主の名を付け、大きな声で、……コラ権州々々……と口汚なく喚き立て、其度毎に拳骨で頭を、大家の権州だと思ひ、撲りつけてやつた。其時や、チツと許り痛快だつたが、野良犬の奴、大変な大喰をしよるので女房子供の腮が干上り相になつた。此奴にや一つ俺も面くらはざるを得なかつたが、それでも人間は意地だ。こんな所で屁古たれちや、男が立たないと、要らぬ所へ力瘤をいれ、働いても働いても、皆犬にしてやられる。苦み果ててる矢先へ、大家の権州奴、大きな犬を俄かに三疋も飼ひやがつて、其奴に俺の名と女房の名と伜の名を付けやがつて、家内中が寄つて集つて呼びつけにしやがるので、俺もこんな所で屁古垂れちや仕方がない。もつと犬を集めて大家の家内中の名をつけて、呼つけにしてやらうと思つたが、能く能く考へてみれば、大きな家に三夫婦もけつかつて、子や孫総計二十八匹も居やがるものだから、たうとう根負して旗をまき、矛を収めて、一時ジェールの都迄逃出して了つたのだ。本当に仕方のないものだよ』

§ 3

Speaker: 'Ha ha ha ha ha, that was a failure, wasn't it? That is why those fools called ancient sages and gentlemen used to spout nonsense like, "Submit to the powerful," or, "The few cannot defeat the many." Even if you say there are no interesting stories, it cannot be all failures. If you have been a jinrikiya for a long time, you must have had some profitable experiences too.'

Speaker: 'No, it is nothing but failures. Eh, it was one evening some time ago. As I was walking in the suburbs of the capital of Jēru, a man who looked like Hotei, big and fat like a date-pudding, came up to me and said, "I am the God of Fortune who bestows blessings upon all people, so could you take me for about an hour?" When I asked, "How much money will you give me?" he said, "If I give you money, the fortune will flee. If you just let me ride, your house will surely prosper from tomorrow." Thinking, "This is a godsend, I do not mind working for free for an hour, and even if I had my fortune told, it would cost thirty or fifty sen," I let the heavy, fat God of Fortune ride and wandered around the town to the right and left. When he said, "That is enough," I lowered the shafts, and he said, "I will go to the benjiyo for a moment." When he entered the benjiyo, his figure disappeared, so since I also felt the urge, I entered the benjiyo, opened the doors of many setsuchin, and checked them one by one, but there was neither shadow nor shape. "This fellow is truly a God of Fortune, his figure has vanished. Surely there must be fortune from tomorrow," I thought, and returned to my home. When I tried to put away the cart, a wallet was left there. When I lifted it, it was quite heavy. "Ah, this is a score. Truly, it is the God of Fortune. Thank you, I humbly accept," I said, holding it above my head five or six times, enshrined it on the kamidana, sprinkled salt, purified the whole area, and when I opened it, a large sum of one hundred ryō in gold coins stared back at me. "This calls for a celebration," I thought, and gathered my jinrikiya friends and neighbors. Thinking I would spend about thirty yen of that money, I showed one hundred yen to the restaurant, ordered a feast, and while I was spending lavishly, drinking and singing, praising the God of Fortune with all my might, that God of Fortune from last night came along with the police, fixed his eyes on the wallet on the shelf, and blurted out, "This is the money I forgot on the cart last night." He did not say thank you or express any gratitude, and the police fellow, looking as if I were guilty of concealing lost property, glared at me and left. I felt so ashamed that I could not even face my wife, and I felt like I wanted to crawl into a hole if there was one. Since then, the restaurant fellow comes every single day to dun me for the thirty yen. No matter how much I work, I cannot make three yen, let alone thirty, so I made my wife understand the situation, and we moved again to the edge of the slums. It was just one night of false joy. A person with bad luck is bad at everything they do. Ah, I have truly grown sick of this world.'

Speaker: 'Ufu fu fu, I would have liked to see your wife's face at that time.'

Speaker: 'When she puffed up with a face like a botched Imado-yaki Daruma, I lost a bit of my dignity too. Eh, since I am already feeling ashamed, I will tell you one more story. This is also a story from when I was pulling a jinrikiya. When the Sun God had hidden half his body behind the edge of the western mountains, a customer came and said, "Hey, cartman, I am a person who has come to see the capital of Jēru, so will you take me until people's faces can no longer be seen for ten sen?" Thinking this was a good deal, I thought, "If I walk three or five chō, the sun will surely set completely. A ten-sen profit in that time is a good deal," so I gave him a quick yes, let the customer ride, and pulled away. Even after two or three hours passed, he did not say he would get off, and in the end, I was made to pull the cart until the break of dawn. Even then, the customer said, "Can't you still see people's faces?" Thinking it strange, I looked up at the sky, and what do you know, it was a moonlit night of the fourteenth day.'
