§ 1

Kingusuhime stands up, opens her silver fan, and sings and dances.

To guide the people of this manifested world into the Great Path of Kami is entrusted as the divine ministry, and it is our noble duty. Kuniharutachinōokami, Shihonagahikonōomikami, before the August Presence of Ōkunihiko, we offer our sincere hearts and pray. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, may our mitama be blessed and prosper.'

Emsu stands up and chants a celebratory song.

'The Jizaiten of the land of Tokoyo, enshrined with Ōkunihiko, the divine hall of Brahmanism, dwelling within Kenonjiyō, the noble miko of Ōkuniwake, our lord Kuniwakehiko, oppressed by the foul-hearted Magakami, abandoning the sacred land far behind, enduring a hundred afflictions while crossing over the great ocean, arriving at the white island floating upon the waves, reaching this wondrous land, and opening up the Great Path of Brahmanism far and near with all your heart—how wondrous it is! The people of the island of Ceylon, yearning for the august virtue of our lord, gathering without distinction of far and near, bathing in the grace of ōkami, moved to tears by the august benevolence of our lord, at last making our lord the king, cutting the trees of the great and small valleys to construct a wondrous hall, reverently proclaiming him the King of Sagaren, combining the three virtues of lord, teacher, and parent, serving him with one accord as the divine official and great sovereign, when such times existed—alas, the evil demonic wind fiercely blew, like a swift-running horse terrifyingly invading deep into the castle, seizing Princess Kērusu, and plotting to spread the teachings of Ural without exception throughout this country, the plotting Magatsu no Ryūun, heavenly fate having here reached its end, now completely and without concealment or hesitation, confesses all past misdeeds before the august presence of our Great Lord, and a world where salvation is sought has now arrived.

When I think of it, when I think of it, on the previous midnight, wishing to view the moon at the Wisteria Forest, ascending to the peak, wiping my sweat in the night breeze blowing past, praising the light of the moon while descending the slope—at that very moment, I slipped and fell into a pitfall! Wondering what on earth this uncanny thing could be, when I peered closely, I heard a voice I never expected: whose wicked deed is this, and who is the fallen person? I sent my attendants running home to fetch many a plow and hoe, and using them, at last pulled him up. When I looked at him silhouetted against the moon, who could have expected that it was the Magakami who opposes our lord morning and evening, the foul-hearted Ryūun? The regret I felt when I realized this! Now that things have come to this, there is nothing more to be done, resigning myself to leaving it to the august will of the gods of heaven and earth, I returned home, but before I could spend even a single night in peace, Ryūun was captured by the arresting servants, and the vexation of being thrown into an unexpected prison!

Ah, however that may be, however that may be, since the gods are of supreme love, how could they ever overlook the demon Ryūun? Ah, wait a while, wait a while! Purifying our hearts and purifying our bodies, by the august salvation of the noble gods, escaping from this prison, and for the sake of the King of Sagaren, blowing away all the foul gods harboring the Yamata no Orochi with the divine breath, and rebuilding this into a sanctuary of purity and spotlessness is right before our eyes. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, O August God Ōkunihiko, pray that you will pity the sincere loyalty we render—at the moment we pray thus, Anan, Sēru, Uinchi, and the officials of Zemu seize the opportune moment, organize an army of righteousness, and advance to save us—how joyous it is! Courage is here increased a hundredfold, and full of spirit we advance toward the living quarters of Ryūun. Alas! Starting with Yūzu, true officials such as Anan, Sēru, and Shirureng falling straight into a hastily made pitfall inside the room—all people say: 'In this hall where crookedness nests, there is peril in advancing deeply; let us turn back for now, reorganize our military preparations, cut down and slaughter Ryūun and his followers, and exterminate them,' yet how frustrating it is to turn the army back!

Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, if the gods dwell in this world, why do they not drive away evil and help the righteous gods? Grumbling thus with various thoughts, the company for the time being concealed their presence. The King of Sagaren, assisted by Tērisu and Emsu, descends the slope of the Kawamori River, letting time pass, and arrives at the village of Koito in Matsuura.

While he gathers righteous warriors and once more advances preparations for the punitive expedition against Ryūun, the divine official of Kitakuhiko descends like a dove, followed by the appearance of Kunikohime, the propaganda envoy of Princess Kiyoko, who together with our Great Lord, uniting their hearts and making their divine power as one, advance with the spirit of chanting propaganda songs—how brave it is! The grace of the gods blessing us, building the foundation of firm and everlasting rock for today's joy in the age of pines, forever clearing the clouds of Mount Hakugun, firmly establishing the courtyard of the capital of the sacred land, fixing our dwelling of a thousand ages upon the very stones of Senbiki, revering the gods and soothing the people—

The day of ruling now has come, The dawning gateway of Miroku's reign, Like the flowers of the trees blooming all at once, The august figure of Sakuayahime, In the single hour of the lotus blossom, How miraculous is her emerging fragrance! Although there are many other congratulatory poems by numerous divine officiants, they have been omitted due to space limitations.

By the way, through the mediation of the one-eyed god of Ame, King Sagaren took Kimikohime as his consort and came to reign eternally over the island of Shiro; furthermore, Ames took Kiyokohime in marriage through the mediation of the one-eyed god, and serving close to King Sagaren as the right guardian god, devoted himself to the divine government of the unity of the manifest and hidden realms; Princess Kērusu repented, became a disciple of the one-eyed god, was permitted to be a propaganda envoy, and made a tour of preaching throughout the lands of the four quarters under heaven; and Ryūun, being banished from this island, returned to his home country of India, reformed his heart, and engaged in the propagation of the Great Way, so it is said. Kamunagara Tamashii Sachihaimaseyo.

此の外数多の神司の祝歌は沢山あれども、紙面の都合に依りて省略したり。

因にサガレン王は天の目一つの神の媒酌に依り、君子姫を娶つて妃となし、シロの島に永久に君臨する事となりぬ。又エームスは目一つの神の媒酌にて清子姫を娶り、サガレン王が側近く右守神となつて、顕幽一致の神政に奉仕し、ケールス姫は悔い改め、天の目一つの神の弟子となり、宣伝使を許されて天の下四方の国々を巡教し、竜雲は此島を放逐され、本国印度に帰り、心を改めて大道の宣伝に従事せしといふ。惟神霊幸倍坐世。
