Shīru, the sentry at the outer gate of the castle of Kauji, while sipping wine all by himself, was waiting impatiently for the return of Besu, who had advanced deep into Okuden.

Seeing Besu returning with a smile to such a place, he eagerly and pressingly demanded:

Besu: 'Hey, Besu, how was it? The outcome in Ōoku, or rather, the yōsu...'

Besu: 'It is much ado about nothing. Steel-nerved, without a shred of fear, I stealthily slipped into the innermost sanctuary of Riūnō, and upon peeking in to see what kind of major crisis had suddenly erupted amidst that appearance of Princess Kērusu, lo and behold, it was sheer peace and tranquility, peace across the realm, the fulfillment of the land, the reassurance of the four classes, and an atmosphere of auspicious omens filled the chambers, while the Okimono of Fukurokuju decorated on the floor was bowing and greeting me with a bent waist—such was the leisurely state of affairs. Besu: 'Even if I tried to report, the truth of the matter is there is nothing I can do. Sappari-ahun has lost its way in the void. It was like peeking into a Rakan-dō where faces of wonder are fully displayed. Yet, I have thoroughly pierced through even the unfathomable facts deeply shrouded in black clouds by a certain wise man, so I must be rather brilliant. No matter what anyone says, being the owner of a sharp intellect, it is limited to this Besu alone who touches the core of all facts, ufufufufu futsu. Ah, the big tank of urine is overflowing and my kidneys are about to burst.'

Shīru: 'My, what an incomprehensible thing has come to pass. That fellow Besu is only lining up evasive answers and striving to dodge the crux of the matter. There must be some deep reason for this. I must skillfully offer him wine, drink him under the table, and make him vomit the truth; ordinary dishes won't do. Shall I pull out his backbone, open him up right to his belly, and expose everything like loach hotpot?'

So murmuring, he waited. Besu finally finished urinating and came walking back.

Shīru: 'Hey, how about tasting a bit of wine? Surrounded by dark clouds on all sides like this, and in a sultry, windless zone, isn't it simply unbearable? Drink and make merry, an inch ahead is darkness; after the darkness, the moon comes out... It is Yakushi Nyorai, Hannyatō, Kanrosui, Shakka, and Jesus. The great savior who saves us into heaven is neither Pangu Shenwang nor Tokoyo no Shinō. If you drink, your heart immediately lifts, and it is the god of wine who allows you to roam freely at will through heaven. The god who actually saves us into heaven appears right before us, you see!'

Besu: 'If you rely too much on the god of wine, your duty as a gatekeeper will be neglected, and you will end up having the character for dismissal bestowed upon you instead of the character for office. You must bear that slightly in mind.'

When you start saying that is when you have truly learned the joy of wine. They say that wine which is not forced cannot be drunk, so this Shīru-sama is thus pouring wine for you. Hahaha!'

§ 4

As soon as the song was finished, the singer of the song already made his appearance at the gate.

Both Siru and Besu, with their drunken eyes hazy and dazed, felt as though their hearts were pierced by the voice of this propaganda song, and when they timidly threw open the front gate with a snap, an old man with white hair and a strange appearance was standing like a Nio statue in solemn dignity, leaning on a thick staff with his left hand and tightly grasping a fan with his right hand.

Siru, startled by the strange figure of the old man and tangling his tongue, said, 'I do not know what grandfather you are, but today the mansion of this castle is a public holiday, so please go back. Tomorrow is Sunday again, the day after tomorrow is International Day, the day after that is the memorial day of the King's father, the day after that is his birthday, and the day after that is the princess's birthday. And then the birthdays of officials and the memorial days of parents will continue, so please come by excluding those days. Just as we are cheerfully getting high and drunk on Hannya-to, it is not amusing for a bleak one-eyed old man to come along. You probably came thinking you would fix Ryūn-sama's wicked heart, but it is utterly useless. Since nobody will listen to such meddlesome anxiety, rather than regrettably catching a cold in your mouth, it is to your advantage to hurry on back. If you speak, your lips grow cold in the autumn wind; come, come, go on home, go on home.'

'Ahahaha, what an amusing gatekeeper to have existed. Given that there is a man of such stylish taste acting as a gatekeeper, a scene of a heaven blooming with a hundred flowers must surely be unfolding deep within the inner palace. The way of Ryūn, who is full of harmonious vapor with superiors and inferiors united and sharing in that joy, is truly the height of admiration. Oh dear, people must not be otherwise. Drink, make merry, an inch ahead is darkness, and after the darkness comes the moon. Ahahaha.'

Besu: 'Hey, this fellow is an amusing dead-loss. Since the inner palace is such-and-such, I shall arrange to speak to Ryūn-sama to have the air inside the castle completely washed clean. Hey there, old grandfather, wait right there for a while.'

'Ahahaha, without needing to wait for Ryūn's reply in particular, this one-eyed old man will boldly force his way in.'

Besu: 'Hey, hello there, if you do that, it will be a-a-a great disaster. This gatekeeper would not be able to bear suddenly becoming a footwear seller's sign from today and being kicked upstairs; if we dawdle, our heads will fly. I beg for a short delay until we hear the reply from the inner palace. A gatekeeper must strictly guard his duties as a gatekeeper, you see.'

'Ahahaha, you fellows are precisely why you are not accepted in the modern world. Because you serve too prudently as a gatekeeper, you are finally judged by the general... that fellow is the most suitable person for a gatekeeper... and you are forced to serve as a lowly gatekeeper for your whole life. The gatekeeper of the Eternal Land's Eternal Castle was once promoted to the God of the Right Guard as a result of failure. How can a stubbornly honest person like you manage to continue living completely in this world? Oh my, oh my, what a pitiful, spineless man. Ahahaha.'

So saying, he shook his shoulders and laughed loudly.

Siru: 'Hey, this fellow is quite a conversational old man. He utters famous arguments and brilliant theories that hit every vital spot. Hey, Besu, he is quite promising for the future. Go quickly and report to the inner palace. It is surely the Ural religion... didn't he say... drink, make merry, an inch ahead is darkness?'

Besu: 'Oh, that is so. This fellow is amusing!'

And Besu hurriedly advanced toward the inner palace.
