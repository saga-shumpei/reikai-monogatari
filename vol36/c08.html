Chapter 8: 悪現 Spirit [996]

悪現霊〔996〕

§ 1

Anan and Sēru's detachment headed toward the mansion, while Sāru and Uinchi rushed fiercely toward the prison in order to rescue Tārchin, Princess Kingusu, Zemu, Ēru, Shirurengu, and Yūzu.

Bēru fought with all his might alongside his subordinate prison guards, but finally thinking he could not win, he hid his form like clouds and mist and vanished.

Sāru and Uinchi successfully rescued all the loyal retainers, and next advanced pointing toward the king's mansion to join Anan and Sēru's detachment. Ryūun and Princess Kērusu, with changed complexions in the inner chamber, held hand spears tucked under their arms, and postured themselves ready to thrust if anyone approached.

Anan, Sēru, and the others who advanced first, along with Shirurengu and Yūzu, unluckily fell into a hastily made deep pitfall inside the mansion.

Sāru, Uinchi, and the others, including Tārchin, Princess Kingusu, Zemu, and Ēru, judging that to advance any deeper now might lead to what kind of plight was hard to tell, weighed the gravity of the situation and retreated backward, coming out to the front and continuing the battle once again.

Keriya and Haruma wielded their command batons up and down, gathered elite weapons everywhere, and defended and fought with the limit of their lives, so that almost more than a third of the attacking forces were cut down and fell in the twinkling of an eye. Sāru and Uinchi, having no other choice, retreated from here for a while along with Tārchin, Princess Kingusu, and others, and returned their army to Barī's mansion to devise a plan for a future comeback.

Ryūun gathered his subordinate generals and soldiers, praised to the utmost limits of his mouth the great exploits of today's defense, and had the exterior of the castle guarded thoroughly. And being surprised at the invisible figures of Sagaren-ō to start with, and Eimusu and Tērisu whom he trusted utterly, he commanded Keriya and Haruma once again to send catchpoles in all directions to search strictly for the whereabouts of the king and the other two. However, the whereabouts of the king had finally become completely untraceable.

Ryūun, to comfort his subordinate generals and soldiers, spread straw mats in the wide courtyard of the castle, and while guarding all directions, held a grand banquet. At that banquet, Ryūun sang in a loud, clear voice.

O Lord Shionagahiko, Who hast manifested as the Ancestor of this world, Thy divine majesty now shines bright, And it has become an age of the teachings of Urals. With the lineage of Lord Ōkunihiko, Sagaren-ō, who boasted in the world, And his ugly evil deity were by Ryūun's Boundless and vast divine virtue Blown away without a trace, And vanishing into smoke, they disappeared. I from now on shall become the ruler Of Shiro Island, and all officials And a hundred men entirely To the teachings of the god of Urals Obeying together, the dews of grace Abundantly I shall moisten. Ah, Kannagara kamigami! The divine authority of Lord Shionagahiko, Though it is a thing not beginning today, The grasses and trees of the four quarters all

Without leaving a single frond, bow down and prostrate. Ryūun, who obeys the teachings Of such a noble great deity, Receiving into his body the divine power of The myriad gods of Takamanohara And the guardian deities of the land, the Kunitsukami, Like the sun and moon eternally Shining across, my divine majesty— Praise it, ye hundred officials! Princess Kērusu to begin with, Left-guard and right-guard divine officials, Keriya and Haruma need not even be spoken of, Bēru, Mēru, and even Yōru, Obeying my divine virtue, Serve purely before me! I am the saving deity who rules This world in peace. As long as Ryūun rules In this Shiro Island, ogres and giant serpents, Come howsoever monstrous malice may attack,

To the extent that there is nothing to fear, Upper and lower harmonize with each other, Matching hearts and making strength Into one, I rule over this— Protect this divine country! Ah, Kannagara kamigami! With a sincere heart before the presence of God I offer up and pray.

Saying that even evil has three tenths of logic, he became pleased in a certain way, shamelessly and audaciously exposing his thousand-layered mask of a face before the multitude, and looked triumphant.

Princess Kērusu tagged along behind him in a good mood, herself singing and herself dancing, praying for Ryūun's martial fortune and his happiness—that song.

Lord Shionagahiko, Who hast appeared in Takamanohara, The teachings of Urals protected By the great deity, Master Ryūun, official of God, Thy boundless and vast divine virtue Having manifested, now here Island of Shiro peacefully, Very tranquilly thou rulest; It has truly become an age of saints. Rejoice and be courageous, ye hundred officials, And people of the land all together, From the bottom of your hearts rejoicing in The divine virtue of Master Ryūun, Praise it, Kannagara! The power of God is before our eyes; Sagaren-ō of weak heart, His majesty's command, the stained Brahmanism morning and evening Revering as if it were his life, Disordered this world.

§ 2

Due to the fierce rage of the Magatsu no Kami, the castle of Kauji day by day, was declining, and the disordered and ruined state, like mown rice, of having no means to govern, weeping and entering sleep, at the very critical moment of utter suffering, the Kamutsukasa of the paths of Urals, Riūn of lofty Shintoku, riding upon the clouds of Amatsumisora, came down far to this place, manifesting his Senpenbankuwa Shinriki, guiding wareware to the great path of Kami, saving us into the precious divine Onkuni, how venerable it is. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, what sort of Shiaha is this for me, the darkness of my breast that until now was clouded, without a shadow in the wind of Shinado, being blown away, in the sky of the murakimo,

heart, the moon shines, the light of the stars sparkles and shines, becoming a body that shines all over. Beginning with Keriya and Haruma, and the other hundreds of Tsukasa, assenting to our Kototama, from now onwards, exhausting all our Magokoro, exhausting our bodies, not letting the Mioshihe of Riūntsukasa leave the heart, guarding it well, saving and helping the Tamigusa under heaven, making it an unshakeable and undecaying Miyo for Tokoshihe, submitting and obeying the Amatsumakoto no Ōmichi, Kamunagara. Before the mimahe of Shihonagahiko, opening up all the Magokoro that Kerusuhime has, reverently and devoutly praying. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara,

May the Mitama be blessed. And so, becoming cheerful, they sang, danced, and finished, and pulling the hand of Riūn, they advanced deep into the inner room.

Keruya, facing all the Tsukasa and handmaidens, proudly proclaimed and transmitted through a song, that song.

Is it indeed a wondrous Miyo, pushing the punt-pole into the riverbed of heaven, the master Riūn who descended, displaying his Kōdaiunben no Shinriki, now here, becoming the lord of the capital of Kauji, togethers with Kerusuhime, to widely spread benevolent governance, how venerable is his raising of the voice in declaration. Found by the venerable lord, I become the Hidari-no-mamorigami, and everything within this castle, making my single body bear the responsibility, under heaven, cherishing the Tamigusa of the four quarters, ah, the precious and rare reign of peace. We, repaying one ten-thousandth of the precious grace, while exhausting the utmost of our hearts, morning and evening, before Ōkami

and the lordly mimahe, diligently, we are resolved to serve. Beginning with Migi-no-mamorigami, and the other hundreds of Tsukasa, reaching down to Aohitogusa, fearfully and reverently adoring the Awase of this lord who descended from heaven, do not rebel even by a single word. Nevertheless, the black-hearted Taruchin, Emuzu, Terisu, Zemu, Yeru, and others, may prepare their army once again, and it is unpredictable whether they will come to attack. At that time, all of you, without shrinking, without timidity, making the power of Ōkami a shield, for the sake of the lord, for the sake of the people, of the cord of jewels, fight without grudging your lives. Even if life should be cast away, for the sake of Kami, for the sake of the lord, the life cast away, when it reaches the mimahe of Kami in heaven,

will be granted a precious seat of treasure, and that soul, for Tokoshihe, safely, happily, delightfully, will be saved in the garden of joy. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, öffering Magokoro before the mimahe of Kami, Keruya now here, swears the full measure of her heart. Those people who think themselves [capable], as early as a single day, sharpening the murakimo heart and training the body, becoming warriors defending this earth, do and fulfill it, Kamunagara. Kami, guarding you, for a thousand years, eight thousand years, without destruction, manifesting lofty renown, shall make you prosperous spirits. The Kototama that Keruya now proclaims,

carve into your heart, and not even for a moment, must you ever forget it. Hidari-no-mamorigami, now here, taking the place of Riūntsukasa, states and leaves [this] to you all. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara, May the Mitama be blessed.

So proclaiming, she leisurely took her seat.

Although there are plenty of deviant songs by Migi-no-mamorigami Haruma and other people, since they are too wordy, they shall be omitted.
