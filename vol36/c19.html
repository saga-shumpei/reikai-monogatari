Chapter 19: 紅 Lotus の舌 [1007]

紅蓮の舌〔1007〕

§ 1

The gatekeeper Bes hurried once more to the sitting room of Riūn and Princess Kērusu, respectfully placing both hands on the floor,

Bes: 'With all due reverence, I report to you. Just now, an eerie old man with white hair appeared before the gate. When I thought he was an old man, he became flat; then he grew long; his face had four or five of them; his body looked as if it were pieced together at the back; and a monster appeared with a very great number of both eyes and noses. No matter what is said, he persists and will by no means go home. By no means can we gatekeeper weaklings drive him away. However, drink and be merry, for just an inch ahead is darkness, and after the darkness comes the moon...... he says such wonderfully pleasing things, hehehehe. Surely that might have been a god of the Ural religion in disguise. Good or evil, it is something beyond all comparison.'

Hearing the words of Bes, Riūn tilted his head for a while, but suddenly pointing his mouth,

Riūn: 'Bes, are you not heavily intoxicated? Your eyes are flickering. Because it was seen with your drunken eyes, it must have appeared to change its shape in such diverse ways. It is surely no monster at all.'

Bes: 'Yes, because I have tasted just a little, the condition of my eyes may also be disordered; but whatever else may be the case, he is a person of an unusual sort. By all means please grant an audience and examine him thoroughly.'

Princess Kērusu spoke from the side with a quiet voice,

Princess Kērusu: 'Be that as it may, it would be well to guide that man here.'

Bes: 'Yes, understood.'

So saying, Bes left his seat, going out to the front while stepping precariously and wobbling. After a while, the former Bes guided the white hair old man and appeared timidly before Riūn,

Bes: 'The monster I reported just now is this very one. Please examine him thoroughly, and with the divine power of the Ural religion drive him away. If such things wander about, we cannot even perform our duties as gatekeepers properly.'

Riūn: 'Hey, Bes, do not say unnecessary things. Depart quickly!'

Bes: 'Yes, hey Kasyu, if you do not do it firmly, it will not do.'

So reviling with foul words, he left the place.

The old man: 'Are you the one called Riūn, who succeeds to the post of King Sagaren and conducts government in the capital of the divine land?'

So he asked, uttering a voice like thunder. At the voice possessing bottomless power from nowhere, even Riūn was taken aback and shrank back two or three steps staggeringly,

Riūn: 'Yes, as you command, Riūn is myself. As for your visiting this Riūn, for what kind of business is it? And what might your honorable name be? I desire to ask to hear it.'

The old man: 'I myself am a propagandist of the Anago religion, the one-eyed god of heaven who served in the divine work of opening the heavenly rock cave. If there is anything about which I desire to give admonition to you, I am one who has made old and weary steps and come far away to this place.'

Hearing that it was the Anago religion, Riūn changed his complexion a little, but somehow overwhelmed by his unapproachable majestic appearance,

Riūn: 'Were you the famed one-eyed god of heaven? This is indeed a long journey, most welcome is your arrival. By all means, to the inadequate Riūn, I humbly beg for your favor in guidance.'

The old man: 'Ahahaha, your diplomatic skill is also quite splendid indeed, and you appear to be modernized to a very great extent. Nevertheless, there is something I wish to ask Lord Riūn. That which I wish to ask is none other than the present circumstances of King Sagaren. Even if one be the preceptor of the Brahman religion and the body of the sovereign of a nation, that you have come to wander in mountains and fields cannot be without some reason. I desire that this background be confessed in detail before me.'

Riūn: 'Yes, to this there are various reasons as well, but too much intricate interference is a nuisance beyond measure; I earnestly hope that this talk will be cut short, and that you will grant instruction in the doctrines of the Anago religion.'

Ryūun: 'Yes, indeed it is a difficult problem.'

Ryūun: 'A difficult problem it is a problem. But I am asking what kind of resolution should be made. Ah, crouching down over there must be Princess Kērusu. Let us hear your opinion.'

Princess Kērusu: 'Yes, I am truly filled with awe. I have no way to make any reply whatsoever. There is no longer any means left other than to leave it to your judgment.'

Ryūun: 'If you do not reform your hearts while there is still time, search out King Sagaren whom the two of you expelled through strategy, shoulder a whip of thorns, make a heartfelt apology to the king, and cleanse your sins, then divine punishment will arrive instantly, and to encounter the admonitions of rain, earthquakes, thunder, and fire is already drawing close before your eyes. If the two of you do not quickly make up your minds, the danger surrounding your persons is approaching moment by moment. If the uncalled-for god of one eye presumes to make a meddlesome remark, that is the end of it. The god of one eye has nothing further to advise the two of you. You would do well to question your consciences carefully and take the best course. If fate permits, we may meet again. Farewell...'

Before he had even finished speaking, with a tap-tap sound of his staff echoing down the corridor, pointing toward the front, he went away.

Ryūun and Princess Kērusu saw off the receding figure of the god of one eye, dumbfounded and knowing not what to do, their complexions suddenly turning pale, and letting out heavy sighs.
