Chapter 5: バリーの館 [993]

バリーの館〔993〕

§ 1

Riūn imprisoned the King of Sagaren as a madman, and threw every single person loyal to the King into prison, beginning with the Samorinokami couple Tārtin, as well as the Umorinokami Ames, Zem, El, Shirleng, and Yūzu, who were Brahmin believers. Now, with no one left to fear, together with Princess Kērusu he began to act as if he were the sovereign. Engaging in increasingly unbridled conduct, his wickedness grew daily, and the voices of grief and resentment echoed throughout the country. Even Riūn, naturally concerned about the trend of public sentiment, refrained from publicly proclaiming himself king. Instead, he put forward Princess Kērusu as a figurehead while operating as the mastermind behind the scenes to seize control of government affairs. He appointed Keriya as Samorinokami, Haruma as Umorinokami, and promoted Bēru, Mēru, and Yōru to important positions, spending his days and nights immersed in banquets and leading a life of uninhibited dissipation.

And so, seeking to capture and cast Sāru, Anan, Sēru, Winchi, and other members of the faction of justice into prison, he dispatched subordinates in all four directions to begin their search. However, the four men had already concealed their identities and hidden deep within the mountains of Barry. There, they gathered numerous loyal subjects of the King, plotting to overthrow Riūn and restore the King to his former position. Furthermore, they racked their brains day and night to rescue those suffering in the dark prisons under Tārtin and his companions as swiftly as possible.

Riūn made Tēris, whom he relied upon as his most cherished disciple, the chief warden of the prison, having him monitor the movements of everyone. Yet Tēris was a man of the deepest prudence, having foreseen the current predicament of King Sagaren. From the very beginning, he had ingratiated himself with Princess Kērusu and Riūn, thereby winning their absolute trust. Consequently, Riūn promoted Tēris to guard the prison, and with this arrangement, he stroked his chest in relief, believing all was well.

Deep within the mountains of Barry stood a huge cavern, the interior of which served as a natural dwelling. Here, those loyal to King Sagaren concealed their forms for a time, revolving plans for the punishment of Riūn.

Sāru turned to all present,

Sāru: 'By this day, Riūn must also be stroking his chest in some relief and letting down his guard. Unless we manage somehow to attempt a nocturnal raid, seize him, and teach him a harsh lesson, peril will press ever closer day by day upon the persons of the King and all loyal followers, leaving us to regret our biting of navels. How shall we best proceed?'

As he deliberated with the company, Anan stepped forward,

Anan: 'First of all, I consider the moderate approach to be to rescue Tārtin and the others, launch a sudden and swift attack to capture Riūn, and then leisurely ask Princess Kērusu to reform her heart. Recklessly killing people is something Heaven does not permit; should it become unavoidable, punishing him is a matter of consequence, but if at all possible, would it not be better to capture him first, press him for repentance, and if he absolutely refuses to listen, resort to measures at that time?'

Winchi: 'We cannot afford to be indulging in such half-hearted measures at a time like this. Since loyal retainers and righteous men have gathered in this manner, nothing surpasses launching a coordinated assault to destroy Riūn and eradicate the evil for the sake of the state.'

Sēru: 'First and foremost, it is crucial to rescue Tārtin, Ames, and the rest. However, this is no easy task. Do you all possess some conviction?'

Anan: 'I certainly do. Coordinating with Tēris from both inside and out, we can first have him watch for the right moment to rescue Tārtin and others, and if we launch our attack taking that as our opportunity, it should be an easy feat.'

Winchi: 'No matter what is said, in a world running after fame and profit, those who follow evil are the great majority, and since we are opposing the multitudes with a few, we cannot be overly optimistic. Moreover, Tēris is the very right arm upon whom Riūn relies; there is little likelihood that he will betray him just as we order.'

原文（近代文語）

竜雲は、サガレン王を発狂者として幽閉し、左守神のタールチン夫婦を初め、右守神のエームス、ゼム、エール、シルレング、ユーズ等のバラモン教信者にして王に誠忠なる人物を残らず獄に投じたれば、今は誰憚る者もなく、ケールス姫と共に国王気取りになつて、益々傍若無人の行動をなし、悪逆日に増長して、怨嗟の声は国内に轟き渡る事となつた。竜雲も遉は人心の向背如何を顧み、公然王と名乗る事をなさず、ケールス姫を表に立て、自分は黒幕となつて国政を掌握し、ケリヤを左守神とし、ハルマを右守神となし、ベール、メール、ヨールの三人を抜擢して重要なる位置に据ゑ、日夜、茗宴に耽り、放埒不覊の生活を送りつつあつた。

さうしてサール、アナン、セール、ウインチなぞの正義派を盡く捕へて獄に投ぜむと、部下を四方に派して捜索し初めた。されど四人は、早くも姿をかくし、バリーの山中に隠れ、数多の正しき王の部下を集めて、竜雲を滅ぼし、王をして再び元の地位に立たせむと企劃しつつあつた。さうして、タールチン以下の冥窓に苦しむ人々を一日も早く救ひ出さむと、昼夜肝胆を砕きつつあつたのである。

竜雲は秘蔵の弟子と頼みたるテーリスをして牢獄の番人頭となし、一同の動静を監視せしめつつあつた。されど此テーリスは最も思慮深き人物にして、サガレン王の今日の境遇を予知し居たれば、初めよりケールス姫、竜雲に取り入り、絶対的の信任を受けて居た。それ故に竜雲はテーリスを抜擢して牢獄を守らしめ、これにて大丈夫と安堵の胸を撫で卸しつつあつたのである。

バリーの山奥には巨大なる岩窟があつて、中は天然の住家となつて居る。此処にサガレン王に忠実なる人々は暫し姿を隠し、竜雲誅伐の計画を廻らしつつあつた。

サールは一同に向ひ、

『最早今日となつては、竜雲も稍安心の胸を撫で卸し気を許し居るならむ。何とか致して夜襲を試み彼を引き捕へ、目に物見せてやらずば、王様を初め忠実なる人々の身辺は刻々に危険迫り、呑臍の悔を残す事あるべし。如何にすれば宜敷からむや』

と一同に計れば、アナンは進み出で、

『先づ第一にタールチン以下を救ひ出し、短兵急に攻め寄せて竜雲を奪ひとり、徐にケールス姫の御改心を願ふをもつて穏健なるやり方と考へます。漫りに人を殺すは天の許さざる処、止むを得ざれば彼を誅伐するも詮なけれど、成らう事なら一旦彼を捕縛して改心をせまり、どうしても聞かざれば其時の手段に致さうでは御座らぬか』

ウインチ『この期に及んで、そんな手緩き事はして居られますまい。斯の如く忠臣義士の集まりし上からは、一挙に攻めよせ竜雲を滅し、国家の為め害をのぞくに如くはありますまい』

セール『先ず第一に、タールチン、エームス以下を救ひ出すのが肝腎で御座らう。併し乍らこれは容易の業では御座らぬ。皆さまは何か確信が御座いますかな』

アナン『確にあります。テーリスと内外相応じ、先づ彼をして時を窺ひタールチン以下を救ひ出さしめ、それを機会に攻め寄せなば易々たる業で御座らう』

ウインチ『何を云うても名利に走る世の中、悪に従ふものは大多数、吾等は寡をもつて衆に当る事なれば、余り楽観は出来申さぬ。又テーリスは竜雲の股肱と頼む大の味方、さう註文通り彼が返り忠を致す気遣ひもありますまい』

§ 2

Anan: 'Goshinpai wa gomuyō de gozaaru. Theris wa konnichi aru koto o yochi shi, wazato Riūn no nodomoto ni hai, amaku kare ga kokō to natta mono kofue, hitoya no kantoku o meijita no de gozaimasu. Kitsuto Theris wa wareware no itaru o matte iru de seu.'

Winche: 'Hatashite sore ga makoto nari to sureba, jitsu ni kono ue naki kautsugafu de gozaaru. Nani toka kare ni menkuwai suru kikuwai wa gozaarumai kana a.'

Anan smiled cheerfully,

Anan: 'Takusan ni shiyudan wa gozaaru. Mazu goichidō ga yōchi no keikuwaku o totoerareta ue, watashi wa Kaujijiyō chikaku susumi-iri, wazato Riūn no buka ni torae rare, nifurau shi te Theris ni ai, kono keikuwaku o hisoka ni shiawase, koto o hakaru de gozaarau. Mazu watashi wa kore yori Kauji no miyako chikaku ni susumimashu. Kochira no hō ni jiyunbi ga totoeba hitoya no kinpen ni yoru hisoka ni arawarete, bakuchiku no ahizu o shite kudasai. Sasureba watashi wa Theris to tomo ni, naibu no hitobito o hitoya yori hanateshi ue, bakuchiku o motte ahizu o itashimashou. Sono oto o kiku to tomo ni omotemon ni sassotousareta shi. Kanarazu uchi yori mon o hiraki, tomo ni tomo ni dai-katsudō o itashimashou. Kore yori hoka ni keiryaku wa arimasenu.'

Saal: 'Naru hodo kekkna bō de wa arimasu ga, Riūn mo mata, shinpen fushigi no yōjutsu o tsukau yatsu nareba, wareware no keiryaku o zenchi shi kaette teki ni hakirare, hantai ni mesaruru yō na koto wa arimasumai kana a.'

Anan: 'Kesshite sayō na shinpai wa hairimasenu. Riūn wa sukoshi mo jinryoku wa motte imasenu. Moshi, hatashite kare ni jintsuuryoku ga ari to sureba, Theris no kokoro o kanpazu ni wa irimasumai.'

Winche, Saal, and Seer nodded in agreement, and involuntarily burst into laughter, 'Aha-ha-ha-ha.'

Anan chanted a poem as a blessing for the journey:

Ah, how joyous, how joyous, The time has come to slay the foes of heaven and earth, And to rescue our sovereign. Riūn of the Ural teachings, Before long, in the island of Ceylon, In the capital of Kauji he appeared, Planning all manner of miscellaneous things, Close by the side of Princess Kelesu, He served and seduced the princess, Day by day mounting in wickedness and lawlessness. Taruchin, the guard deity of the left, Beginning with Princess Kings, Eames, Yuse, Zem, Eale, Even Shirureng, ruthlessly Thrown into the depths of the prison, While gathering evil men who obey him, Close at hand, to finally carry out His wild ambitions morning and evening, Plotting ill deeds, Imprisoning King Sagaren, Even flying birds would fall, So formidable was his might, Hard to look upon with contempt. Yet, how could the Great Deity of Heaven and Earth Pardon such outrageous violence? Riūn, the ringleader of perversion, Who goes against the reason of heaven and earth, The time has come to destroy him. Ah, how joyous, how joyous, People of matchless loyalty, Gathering in this hidden dwelling, For the sake of our lord and our country, We marshal our forces and advance grandly Toward the castle of Kauji. The gods of heaven and earth, acknowledging Our sincerity, Will grant us eternal and great achievements, Promptly preparing our equipment, And striking down the perverted deity. To pacify the realm under heaven, And govern the people of the four quarters in peace, Tranquilly and harmoniously. I shall now dart ahead, Heading straight for the capital of Kauji, To be sweetly captured by the minions of that Riūn, Entering the prison, Meeting again with Theris-sama, Arranging all our plans, Until the divine armies of Saal, Seer, and Winche Come forth to attack, I shall prepare all things. Ah, Such is the Will of the Gods, Let your divine spirits be blessed and happy! Hear without fail our prayers, We who are impatient with loyalty. Though the castle of Kauji be high, Though the surrounding moats be deep, Before a single-minded heart of loyalty, There can be no enemy that dares oppose us. Ah, how valiant, how valiant, The time has come to rescue our lord! Ah, Such is the Will of the Gods, All you righteous people, Prepare your readiness from behind, And advance forth swiftly. I shall set out first. Whether the morning sun shines or is overcast, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, Even if the great earth sinks down, The power of truth saves the world. The gods appear in the outer world, To stand and divide good from evil. Though it be not the Izumo teachings, Yet the Great Straightening Deity who created this world, The Great Straightening Deity of expansive heart, In human life, whatever may happen, Look straight upon it, hear straight upon it. The errors of the body are proclaimed aright, Reflecting upon the heart of the gods. Even the lawless and wicked Riūn, If he changes his heart and submits, Shall surely have his life spared. Come, come now, come, come now, May you all be safe, Command the divine army and follow after us.'

Promptly adjusting his travel attire, he set off toward the capital of Kauji.
