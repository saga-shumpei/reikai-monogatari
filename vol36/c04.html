§ 1

In the inner chamber of the mansion built at the foot of Aokigaoka, not far from the capital of Kauji, Tārchin has begun a whispered conversation with his wife, Kingshime.

Tārchin: 'What a troublesome situation has arisen at the Oyakata. That fellow Riūn has cleverly ensnared Kingshime-sama, his Ōbō growing day and night, and he treats Ōsama Sagaren as if he were a mere Haika. If we leave this to his Haunin, it is clearer than fire that the Kaujijiyō and the land of Shiro will be smashed to Kotsupamijin, collapse into a Metsuchiya Kuchiya state, and cause a Daisauran. Unless we devise some effective Shiyudan now to eliminate that Kuwaibutsu, I fear for the safety of Ōsama. Do you not have any good ideas, my dear?'

Kingshime: 'It has truly become a troublesome matter. If the Ural Kyō, which has a different Shiushi, is planted in this Mikuni where the people are governed by the Baramon Kyō, the Funran will Tautei never cease. Furthermore, that Akusō Riūn uses Senpenbankuwa sorcery to steal the heart of Kingshime-sama, and now, fearing no one, he is a hateful fellow who commits every kind of lawlessness. We must do something about this.'

'The loyal Shirurengu and Yūzu have also been thrown into prison because of Riūn, and now they suffer for crimes they did not commit. Though I rack my brains and suffer a thousand agonies trying to save them, Kingshime-sama is deeply suspicious and Riūn's power cannot be underestimated. Even as a god of the left guard, it is truly regrettable that I can do nothing about this, though one might say it is the way of the times. If we leave things as they are, all the good people will fall victim to Riūn's poisonous tongue and be destroyed, leaving only the wicked to run rampant; in the end, there is no telling what Riūn might do. He is by no means a man to be satisfied with his current position. He is a Kuwaibutsu full of ambition, so just as one cuts the branches before felling a great tree, it is hard to predict when or what kind of fate we might be cast into. There is no time but now to punish him. Tell me, Kingshime, I hear you have received a love letter from Riūn?'

Kingshime blushed slightly at her husband's words,

Kingshime: 'Yes, it is just as you say. It was such an outrageous matter that I could not bring myself to speak of it, and I have been suffering greatly in my heart. However, since you already know, what should I hide? Please, look at this.'

As she handed over a love letter, Tārchin took it quickly, opened it, and read it as follows:

Riūn: 'I, Riūn, secretly send this letter to Kingshime. If you show this letter to your husband, your life will be at an end. Also, you must never speak of this to others. Since the moment I first laid eyes on you during your appearance, I have been unable to restrain my feelings of love; I am caught by the demon of agony morning and night, and I am left sighing in despair. Therefore, since I intend to achieve a certain goal in the near future and become the King of the island nation of Shiro, I want you to abandon your husband now, pretend to be single on the surface, and become my secret wife. If it is your wish, I shall employ Tārchin as heavily as before. This is a matter of great importance concerning your own fate and the life or death of your husband, so I beg you to grant me a favorable reply; I am counting the days, waiting for your answer. Farewell...'

It was written thus. Tārchin smiled with a grin,

Tārchin: 'Hahaha, what a foolish fellow. If I have this, it is interesting; I shall be able to devise any kind of strategy... Here, Kingshime...'

He brought his mouth close to her ear and whispered something, to which Kingshime nodded with a gentle smile.

From then on, on the evening of the second day, a letter from King's Hime arrived quietly by the side of Riūn.

Riūn, rejoicing as though his desire had been fulfilled, broke the seal and looked closely, and found the following text written:

'Lord Riūn, that I, a woman as inept as myself, should have received a kind and detailed letter from you, the living god of peerless divine power, is the supreme honor of a lifetime for my own body. I had been thinking of offering my reply immediately, but whatever I might say, being a person who has a husband, I had not even the leisure to write a reply, and at last today, taking advantage of the absence of my husband Tārutchin who went to the mansion of Koito, I penned this letter. My husband will likely not return for four or five days. Therefore, wishing to hear detailed talks and also desiring to express the feelings within my heart, would you please make every arrangement to dispense with all obstacles and come over to the forest of Fuji no Mori by the evening of the day after tomorrow? If you are to come, I would like you to give me a letter in reply, sealed strictly, to this messenger. Although meeting at my own house is an extremely easy thing, there is much coming and going of numerous people, and besides, should it be said that I was talking with you during the absence of my husband, I feel the reputation in the world would somehow be unpleasant, and therefore I earnestly and strongly beg you to take the trouble of coming as far as the summit of Fuji no Mori.'

Seeing this written, Riūn rejoiced greatly, immediately penned a reply, and handed it to the messenger man.

Tārutchin had dug a pitfall in a certain place in Fuji no Mori, covered it abundantly with fallen leaves upon that, and was lurking in the shade of the trees waiting for Riūn to come, thinking the time was not yet ripe.

Riūn, not knowing even in dreams that there was such a plot, with the sorrow of one who is not a god, acting triumphantly, slipped quietly out of the castle, wrapped his face deeply, and climbed the moonlit mountain path heading toward the summit of Fuji no Mori.

Not knowing that there was a deep pitfall in the center of the narrow path, just as he climbed leisurely, intending to meet King's Hime, he stepped falsely and fell completely crash into the pitfall. Tārutchin said not a word, scooped up earth, filled in the pitfall, and went walking back toward his own house with an unconcerned face.

At this time Eimusu was climbing while enjoying the moon on the mountain summit of Fuji no Mori together with two or three subordinates, but around midnight, upon returning, he unknowingly stepped falsely on that pitfall, and he himself also fell into it. However, because it had been filled suddenly with soft earth, the leveled earth as well only sank down heavily by some four or five shaku. At that time, somehow, he felt as if something warm like hair had touched his feet. Eimusu, together with two or three subordinates, caused hoes and shovels to be brought from his own mansion, and while letting sweat stream down, thinking someone or other must have been buried alive, wishing to rescue and give relief, he dug up the earth with all his might, and when he rescued and looked out, who could have expected it, it was Riūn of the Uraru Teaching who brandished violent authority in the castle day by day. Eimusu thought within his heart... Ah, to have helped such a failed fellow, if things were like this, I should not have rescued him... and though he regretted it, it was already of no avail.

Riūn, having been rescued,

'You have saved me from a perilous place, what person might my benefactor be?'

saying thus, he peered into his face, and

'Ah, you are Eimusu, you have truly saved me well. In any case, upon returning to the castle, I shall make some judgment or other, so do not go anywhere and wait for me,'

spoke and cast aside, and promptly descended the hill of Fuji no Mori and went back.

Eimusu, together with Saru and two other subordinates, came to the living room of Riūn, driven by a somewhat anxious sentiment, since he had saved his life, he certainly would not speak of any shortage, and he came having made up his mind that surely some words of gratitude or the like would be spoken.

Riūn felt favorable emotions in his heart regarding the fact that Ams had saved his Inochi. Yet Ams held a reputation as the Umori no Kami of King Sagaren, and furthermore being a zealous Shinjiya of Brahmanism, Riūn constantly hated him as a thorn in his own eyes. Therefore, Riūn would not let this opportunity slip, unleashing an Akushin that anyone who became a Moku-teki to his own Boku-gai must be utterly destroyed regardless of Zenaku, and spoke strictly toward Ams.

Ams: 'Being helped from a dangerous Inochi last night, for that matter it is indeed an occasion for Riūn to offer Kanshiya. Nevertheless, since you are those who plotted with Tārtin, Princess Kings, and others, harboring the ambition to race and bet this Riūn to begin with, Princess Kērusu, and King Sagaren, as this is a Rekizen Jijitsu, for the sake of a warning I shall define you with a Hangiyakuzai, so it would be well if you made up your mind accordingly.'

When he pronounced this with a Genzen demeanor, Ams was utterly dumbfounded by Riūn's words which were contrary to his expectations.

Ams: 'By what evidence do you speak of us as Hangiyakushiya? While rescuing human life, to be charged with an unexpected false accusation is an unprecedented affair. Might Lord Riūn not have gone mad?'

As he pressed on him with heightened agitation, Riūn listened to it with cold indifference, and while deliberately putting on a forced smile,

Riūn: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha, how well indeed you have put on an innocent face. That explanation I shall leisurely listen to afterwards... Hey, hey, fellows, quickly bind the rebel Ams and cast him into the prison!'

He cried out. Several catchpoles, having awaited this in advance, seized Ams without pausing for any yes or no, and immediately cast him ruthlessly into the dark prison-house.

By the orders of Riūn and Princess Kērusu, Tārtin and Princess Kings were likewise captured under the same fate, and became persons groaning within the prison.

Sāl barely escaped this place with his body and immediately ran to the mansion of Zem and Ēru, reporting the actual situation one by one. Zem and Ēru, together with Sāl, without losing a moment came to the mansion of King Sagaren, and impeached Riūn's violent behavior. King Sagaren was greatly astonished, and immediately had his chamberlains summon Princess Kērusu and Riūn.

『汝竜雲、吾に向つて発狂とは何事ぞ。手打ちに致して呉れむ』

と大刀をスラリと引抜き、斬つてかからむとす。数多の近従は王の背後よりムンヅと許り抱き止めた。竜雲は一同に向ひ、

『王さまは御病気におはしませば、御全快遊ばすまで、座敷牢にお隠し申せよ』

と下知する。ケールス姫は何とも云はず、首を垂れて、サガレン王に顔を見せぬやうに努めてゐる。

其間に憐れや王は竜雲の腹心の部下の為に、発狂ならざる身を発狂者として一室に監禁さるる事となつて了つたのである。

此日風強く雨さへ降り来り頗る冷気を覚ゆ
