Chapter 1: 二 Teaching 対立 [989]

二教対立〔989〕

§ 1

The tropical land of the moon, which protrudes into the southwestern extremity of the Asian continent, was in later ages called Tenjiku, and is now called Indo. The great isolated island floating out in the sea at the southeastern extremity of this country is called the Island of Shiro. In modern times it is called Seirontau, and it is an island rich in historical origin where Shiyakaniyorai, the founder of Bukyō, was born.

Shiyaka spread Bukyō from this island to Indo, Chibetsuto, Annan, Siam, Shina, and Chōsen; its kyōsei spread eastward, finally extending its seiriyoku as far as our own Onokorojima of Nihonkoku. Bukyō has generally become a religion of the iushiyoku jinshiyu. In contrast to this, Christianity has become a religion of the white race in major part. Such Caucasian races as Turkey and Greece also received the greatest degree of influence from Bukyō.

The meaning of calling it the Island of Shiro is that 'shi' is an abbreviation of 'isowagaki'. 'Shiwagaki' means that the four sides are made into a natural stronghold with water, and a fence is built. The meaning of the kototama 'ro' is that there is a ruler and a people, that it possesses independent land, and that it constructs fortifications and is governed by a king. From the age of the gods, this island had achieved a remarkable development of human culture. It was a civilized country in the age of the gods second only to Jerusalem. Therefore, this is called the Island of Shiro. Moreover, another meaning of shiro is that shiro is a corruption of shiru, meaning the island where the king who rules over all-under-heaven resides.

By the way, as for the word island (shima), 'shi' is water, and 'ma' is the kototama of revolving. Therefore, in ancient times, the island was a designation for a place where there were neither human dwellings nor any human habitation. However, even in this tale, calling things by the names of islands such as Takasago Island, Chikushi Island, and Onokorojima would sound truly contradictory if spoken from the meaning of this kototama. Nevertheless, valuing ease of understanding in contemporary nomenclature, they were simply called islands in a modern fashion. In reality, it is more appropriate to have called them shiro.

After the samurai of our country raised their heads, military heroes vied for supremacy in various regions, each built a resident castle, and boasted of their martial might; the fortifications and territories are collectively called castles (shiro) because they dug moats around mansions and surrounded them with water. Occasionally there were anomalous ones where a mansion was built on top of a mountain and called a castle. Therefore, this was specifically called a mountain castle, with the character for mountain prefixed to it. Also, the character for island (shima), when written with the mountain radical and bird, or mountain crown and bird, and made to be read as shima, is because numerous avian tribes inhabited maritime islands. Calling it Chikushi Island or the Australian island is written with the triple-water radical and province, and is used in modern times. This is the most appropriate designation from the standpoint of word meaning. Until Shiyaka appeared in later ages and founded Bukyō, this Island of Shiro had been almost the center of Brahmanic power. In later ages of Brahmanic doctrine, the teaching that all human beings are of three races—those born from the head of Dajizaiten, those born from the torso, and those born from the feet—permeated deeply into the marrow of the nation's people; the race said to be born from the head were the so-called aristocrats of this country, who stood at the head of the people, indulged solely in idle leisure, and yet mistakenly believed this to be the truth of Izanagi's Way. Furthermore, the class of people born from the torso of Dajizaiten all stood above the people and were in the position of rulers who conducted politics. In addition, the people belonging to the class said to have been born from the feet engaged industriously and diligently in morning and evening labor, presenting the appearance of being almost production organs for the food, clothing, and shelter of the upper classes.

Shiyaka was born in a certain isolated island of this country as the son of a king named King Jōbon, and was called Prince Siddhārtha. He stood up with the thought of breaking down this unfair and unreasonable custom of Brahmanism, allowing all the myriad people to equally bathe in the blessings of Heaven, establishing the doctrine of the equality of self and others, and saving the four sufferings of birth, old age, sickness, and death; thus he founded what is called Bukyō. And that this Shiyaka is a reincarnation of the wakamitama of the Great God Kami-Susanoo, Ōhayashihiko-no-Mikoto, and later Tsuteruhiko-no-Kami, is as shown in Volume 6 of Reikai Monogatari.

原文（近代文語）

亜細亜大陸の西南端に突出したる熱帯の月の国は、後世これを天竺と称へ、今は印度と云ふ。此国の東南端の海中に浮び出でたる大孤島はシロの島といふ。現代にては錫蘭島と称へられて、仏教の始祖釈迦如来が誕生したる由緒深き島である。

釈迦は此島より仏教を、印度、西蔵、安南、シヤム、支那、朝鮮と、其教勢東漸して、遂に自転倒島の我日本国にまで、其勢力を及ぼしたのである。仏教は概して、有色人種の宗教となつてゐる。之に反してキリスト教は、大部分白色人種の宗教となつてゐる。土耳古、希臘の如きコーカス人種も亦、仏教の感化を受けたこと最も大なるものがあつた。

シロの島といふ意義はシは磯輪垣の約りである。シワ垣とは四方水を以て天然の要害となし、垣を作られてゐるといふ意味である。ロといふ言霊の意義は、国主あり人民あり、そして独立的土地を有し、城廓を構へて王者の治むるといふ事である。神代の昔より此島は非常に人文が発達してゐた。エルサレムに次いでの神代に於ける文明国であつた。故に之をシロの島といふ。又シロといふ別の意味はシロは知るの転訛にて、天下をしろしめす王者の居ます島といふことである。

序に島といふのはシは水であり、マは廻る言霊である。故に古は島には人の家もなく、又人類の棲息せざりしものの称へであつた。然乍此物語にも高砂島、筑紫島、自転倒島などと島の名義を以て呼んでゐるのは、此言霊の意義より言へば実に矛盾せし如く聞ゆるであらう。さり乍ら、今日の称呼上分り易きを尊んで、現代的に島と称へた迄である。其実はシロといつた方が適当なのである。

我国の武家が頭を上げてから、各地に群雄割拠し、各自に居城を作り、其武威を誇つた其城廓及び境域を総称して城といつたのも、館の周囲に堀を穿ち、水をめぐらしたから城と云うたのである。偶には山の上に館を建てて城と呼んでゐる変則的のものもあつた。故に之を特に山城といつて、山の字を冠してゐたのである。又島といふ字は漢字で山扁に鳥を書き、又山冠に鳥を書いてシマと読ましてあるのは、海島に数多の鳥族が棲息してゐたからである。筑紫の島とか、オーストラリア島とかいふのは、三水扁に州と書いて、現代用ゐて居る。之は字義の上からは最も適当な称呼である。此シロの島は後世、釈迦が現はれて、仏教を起す迄は、殆どバラモン教の勢力の中心となつて居たのである。後世のバラモン教は、すべての人間は大自在天の頭より生れた種族と、胴から生れた種族と、足から生れた種族と三種あるといふ教理が、深く国人の脳髄に浸み込み、頭より生れたりと称する種族は所謂此国の貴族にして、人民の頭に立ち、遊逸徒食にのみ耽り乍ら、之を惟神の真理と誤信してゐたのである。又大自在天の胴から生れた階級人は、すべて人民の上に立ち、政治を行ふ治者の地位にあつた。又足から生れたと称せらるる階級に属する民族は、営々兀々として朝暮勤労に服し、上級民族の殆ど衣食住の生産機関たるの観をなして居た。

釈迦は此国の或一孤島の浄飯王といふ王者の子と生れ、悉達太子といつた。彼は此バラモン教の不公平、不道理なる習慣を打破して、万民を平等に、天の恵に浴せしめむと思ひ立ち、自他平等の教理を樹立し、生老病死の四苦を救はむとして、彼の仏教なるものを創立したのであつた。そして此釈迦は、神素盞嗚大神の和魂、大八洲彦命、後には月照彦神の再生せし者たることは、霊界物語第六巻に示したる通りである。

§ 2

Before the great inclination of Chikiu, it was not as excessively tropical as it is now. Having attained a neutral climate, it occupied a position in an extremely livable temperate zone. However, by the era of Shiyaka's birth, it had already become an island nation close right beneath the Sekidau. Needless to say for Indo, the inhabitants of this Seirontau were all dark-skinned and possessed a complexion tinged slightly with yellow.

Kimikohime, the seventh daughter of the eight maidens of Kamususanōnōokami, together with her handmaiden Kiyokohime, left behind Kenonjiyō in Mesopotamia, the Honzan of Brahmanism, with the intention of engaging in the Senden of Ananai-kyō in the land of Fusa. At that time, she was captured by a faction of Kunihiko of Brahmanism, and all five sisters were placed upon half-broken boats and cast adrift to the mercy of the waves. Kimikohime, together with her handmaiden, crossed the raging waves and tumultuous billows, finally drifting ashore at Cape Dondra on the island of Shiro, and from there, day after night, she set forth to seek out the Kamudate where Tomohiko had concealed himself together with Koitohime in former years.

A few miles apart from this Kamudate, there was a place called the Capital of Kamuchi. In this place, two persons, King Sagaren and Princess Kells, constructed their mansion and governed approximately seven parts out of ten of this island nation. King Sagaren served Brahmanism, while his consort, Princess Kells, served the Ural religion.

It goes without saying that the language of the people of this country entirely used Sanskrit. However, in order to make it easy for general readers to comprehend, the narrator sets out to describe it using Japanese as much as possible.

Slightly to the south of the Capital of Kamuchi, there was a slightly elevated and scenic hill where Sal trees grew densely. There, three black-faced men, who appeared to be of the middle class, were gazing at white lotus flowers blooming in scattered patches amidst the developed wilderness, scooping wine and exchanging cups, indulging in idle chatter. One was named Shireng, another was called Yūz, and the other man was called Vēl. All of them were minor officials serving King Sagaren. Today, having been granted a holiday, they were attempting a day of pure pleasure to view the lotus flowers here.

Shireng: 'Oi, is not King Sagaren truly to be pitied? Being unable to publicly enshrine Brahmanism which he loves to that extent, because Princess Kells is of the Ural religion, the power on the princess's side has grown flourishing, and inside the mansion, before one knows when, a religious conflict drifts with an indescribable desolate and cold atmosphere. The king must also find it exceedingly unpleasant. Somehow, I wish to reconstruct it into the Brahmanism which we serve. Not only the king, but are not we ourselves truly unpleasant, so that we do not feel like attending properly to affairs of state?'

Yūz: 'No matter what you say, Princess Kells dotes upon the divine priest Ryūun of the Ural religion beyond measure, and now she treats him with more respect than King Sagaren, so is there any help for it whatsoever? Moreover, that monster called Ryūun employs all sorts of miraculous and mysterious sorcery, sweetly captivating Princess Kells, and with today's state of affairs where his power and influence are peerless, if such talk as this were carelessly to reach Ryūun's ears, that would truly be terrible. How about dropping this talk already?'

Vēl: 'Nani, to think of tucking our tails and surrendering to a wandering vagrant like Ryūun, whose origin as an ox bone or horse bone we do not even know! I am thinking somehow to subdue that monster, open the eyes of Princess Kells, and gain the peace of mind of Lord Sagaren. For this is the best path that we, as subjects, should devote to our lord.'

Shireng: 'By the way, that fellow Ryūun is said to have a certain relation with Princess Kings, the wife of Lord Taruchin of the Left Guard, but have you heard about it?'

原文（近代文語）

地球の大傾斜せしより以前は、今の如く余りの熱帯ではなかつた。気候中和を得、極めて暮しよき温帯に位置を占めて居たのである。併し釈迦の生れたる時代は、すでに赤道直下に間近き島国となつて居たのである。印度は言ふに及ばず、此錫蘭島の住民は何れも色黒く、少しく黄味を帯びたやうな膚をして居た。

神素盞嗚大神の八人乙女の第七の娘、君子姫は侍女の清子姫と共にバラモン教の本山メソポタミヤの顕恩城を後にして、フサの国にて三五教の宣伝に従事せむとする折しも、バラモン教の釘彦の一派に捉へられ、姉妹五人は何れも半破れし舟に乗せられて波のまにまに放逐されたのである。君子姫は侍女と共に激浪怒濤を渡り、漸くにしてシロの島のドンドラ岬に漂着し、それより夜を日についで、先年友彦が小糸姫と共に隠れゐたる、神館を尋ねて進み行くこととなつた。

此神館より数里を隔てて神地の都といふがあつた。此処にはサガレン王、ケールス姫の二人が館を構へ、此島国の殆ど七分許りを統轄して居た。そしてサガレン王はバラモン教を奉じ、其妃のケールス姫はウラル教を奉じて居た。

此国の人々の言葉は残らずサンスクリツトを用ふるは言ふまでもない。されど口述者は一般の読者に諒解し易からしむる為、成る可く日本語を以て、述べることとしておく。

神地の都の少しく南方に、娑羅双樹の密生したる小高き風景よき丘陵がある。そこに二三の中流階級と覚しき黒い面の男が、展開したる原野の中に点々として咲き乱れて居る白蓮華を眺めて、酒汲みかはし、雑談に耽つて居る。一人はシルレングといひ、一人はユーズと云ひ、も一人の男はベールといふ。何れもサガレン王に仕へて居る一部の役人であつた。今日は休暇を賜はつて、ここに蓮の花見をすべく、一日の清遊を試みて居たのである。

シルレング『オイ、サガレン王様も本当にお気の毒ではないか。あれ丈好きなバラモン教を公然と祀ることも出来ず、ケールス姫様がウラル教だから、姫の方の勢力が旺盛になり、館の内は何時とはなしに、信仰争ひで、何ともいへぬ殺伐で冷たい空気が漂うて居るやうだ。王様もさぞ不愉快な事であらう。何とかして吾々の奉ずるバラモン教に立替へたいものだなア。王様計りか、吾々共も本当に不愉快で、政務も碌に執る気にならないぢやないか』

ユーズ『何を言つても、ケールス姫様がウラル教の神司竜雲を殊の外寵愛し、今ではサガレン王様よりも尊敬して居られるといふ体裁だから、何うにも斯うにも仕方がないぢやないか、又あの竜雲といふ怪物は、いろいろと神変不思議の妖術を使ひ、ケールス姫を甘く籠絡し、権勢並ぶものなき今日の有様だから、ウツカリ斯んな話しでも竜雲の耳へ這入らうものなら、それこそ大変だ。モウ此話しは打切りにしたら如何だ』

ベール『ナニ、どこの牛骨か馬骨か知れもせぬ風来者の竜雲如きに、尻尾を巻いてたまるものかい。おれは何とかして、あの怪物を征伐し、ケールス姫様の御目をさましサガレン王さまの御安心を得たいと思うて居る。これが吾々臣下たる者の、君に尽すべき最善の道だからなア』

シルレング『時にあの竜雲の奴、左守の神のタールチン殿の奥様、キングス姫と○○関係があるといふことだが、お前聞いて居るか』

Beru: 'Even as I listen, that in the first place gets on my nerves. That is why I had an interview with Tārchin-sama the other day and offered various pieces of advice, but no matter what, since it is a henpecked household, what Tārchin-sama said was... "It is not a case for the likes of us to meddle in what Ryūn-sama, who could shoot down a flying bird today, does, so just stop saying such things henceforth"—and so he imposes a gag order. Truly, what a coward he is. That cowardice of trying to preserve one's own position by means of nepotistic relations is indeed something we should not even place upwind of us. Might there not be some ingenious plan to somehow peel off the hide of Ryūn's face?'

Yūzu: 'As for that, methods are countless. However, those who would accomplish a great matter must not handle affairs lightly. First, one must ponder deeply and in silence, spy out the enemy's emptiness, wait for the opportune time, and carry it out, you see.'

Beru: 'As for that execution, how is it supposed to be done?'

Yūzu: 'Hey, Beru, saying such things, aren't you coming here having become a spy for Ryūn? Somehow your watching looks suspicious. Starting by speaking ill of Ryūn yourself, you are probing our true feelings, aren't you? I am not Yūzu-sama, who fails to understand such things.'

Beru: 'This is outrageous. Who would ever allow themselves to be employed in advance by such a monster? No matter how much of a renowned man Beru is, I cannot speak of such secrets, you know.'

Yūzu: 'As I thought, you confessed, didn't you. What is this about not being able to speak of secrets? It cannot be mistaken that, asked by Ryūn to probe our true feelings, you made the lotus-viewing an excuse and lured us out all the way hither. Now, things having come to this, we can no longer let you go... Hey, Shirurengu, while it is still now, shall we make an end of the fellow Beru?'

Shirurengu: 'Good, understood.'

So saying, he lunged fiercely toward Beru. Yūzu grappled with Beru from behind, attempting to throw a rope around him. Even Beru, with all his might, began a struggle taking on the two of them, and the three of them, tangling and grappling, tumbled all together from the top of the hillock into the lotus pond at the foot, rolling down with a rumble.

By this time, the moon had already revealed its half-circle form and began to shine overhead. The Milky Way flowed purely from the direction of Jerusalem toward the other side of the Indian Ocean. A soughing wind caressed the surface of the lotus pond, and the sound of the leaves brushing together was heard patter-patter. In the muddy pond, making the sounds of a splash and a plop, the three of us covered in muddy water were grappling with each other with all our might and on equal terms.

From the densely foliated branches of the sala trees, an owl was hooting loudly: 'Hōsuke hōhō, dorotsuku doronbo, gorotto kayase, bōbō.'

娑羅双樹のこもつた枝から、梟が『ホウスケホウホウ、ドロツクドロンボ、ゴロツトカヤセ、ボーボー』と鳴き立てて居る。
