Chapter 23: Snow 達磨 [1011]

雪達磨〔1011〕

§ 1

King Sagaren rose up with a majestic air, spread his golden fan, ascended the central high seat, and with a dignified presence chanted and danced.

Ah, how grateful, how grateful! The divine grace of the gods of Ametsuchi, Filling and overflowing to every nook, Illuminating the mitama Of us and all people indeed. On the island of Shiro where stormy winds rage, At the summit of Hakūnzan, The rising dragon cloud, Enveloped in the foul breath of the ugly one, The sendenshi of Brahmanism, The kamutsukasa of Kuniwakehiko, Around King Sagaren, Day and night grew perilous indeed. At this very moment, with loyalty Harder than stone, the tsuhamono Came forth, evading calamity And rescuing me while guarding us To the mansion of Koito in Matsūra where flowers bloom, For a thousand and eight thousand generations, unshakeable. Lurking in the solid ihaya, Blowing away the kumokiri of Hakūnzan With the wind of Shinano, Surrounding and darkening the capital of the divine land Tenfold and twentyfold with dragon clouds, Encouraging the divine hosts To sweep them away, morning and evening, Just as we strove in martial arts, The divine grace was not shallow; Parting the deep fog, the sendenshi of the three-five teaching of the northern light Descended like a dove, Explaining the divine mind of the gods of Ametsuchi Fully and in detail, With a heart of utmost benevolence and utmost love, Directing us to face the stronghold of magatsuhi, Oh, the preciousness of those teachings! The sendenshi of Tārchin and Ems, Of peerless loyalty, Rejoicing in the words of the old man, Discarding completely the martial arts They had cultivated day and night, and becoming kanagara, Kneeling before the presence of the gods, Releasing the kototama of absolute sincerity, To bring all magatsuhi into accord through words, And to bathe in the grace of heaven and be saved, Just as they resolved their minds, The Savior of the Mikazunomitama, Lord Susanoo no Ōkami's Eight maidens' Kunikohime, And Kiyokohime of pure heart's hue Came hither once again, How grateful it was that once more They proclaimed the divine teachings! I, thereupon, leading Kunikohime And the other sendenshi, At the foot of Hakūnzan, Came to see the brightly shining divine land castle, And within the castle, Black smoke was rising in four directions, And the tongues of scarlet lotus Were with the momentum to lick up Every single building far and near, As I stood watching for a while, Suddenly resounded the voice of the gods: 'To save the multitudes who are the children of gods Is now at this very time!' Forgetting my own body, I rushed headlong Into the midst of the black smoke, And waving my baton to give orders, Kunikohime, Kiyokohime, Tārchin, Ems, Kingshime, and the pure sendenshi of Therese Swiftly rescued the multitudes of people Suffering and enveloped in smoke, Brought them out to the castle's riding ground, Cared for them in all sorts of ways, And tended and guarded them—how precious it is! At that very moment, the sendenshi song was heard, As we listened with reverent hearts, Purified and filled with joy, The one-eyed sendenshi of heaven Who appeared in the village of Koito Raised his cool voice high, And it echoed, parting the smoke, Majestic was his divine figure! Ah, kanagara, kanagara! God has protected us, And has saved every single person Who had turned away from the Way. We too had once despised The foul deeds of the dragon clouds, Yet looking anew with naobi and listening anew, When we reflect, how precious it is! They too are children of God, palaces of God, Not beings who were impure from the origin. Without knowing it, their heart's citadel Was captured by the magatsukami, And how pitiful that they were practicing Wrongful deeds straying from the Way! They are by no means beings to be blamed. What should be blamed is our own heart. Because there was the slightest negligence, That we stirred up the upper and lower classes Of the capital of the divine land was also I— When we think thus, how fearful before the gods, Begging for forgiveness for our sins Which have no way of being excused, We prostrate ourselves before the presence of the gods of Ametsuchi, And with awe and trembling, we pray! Though the morning sun shine, though it be cloudy, Though the moon wax, though it wane, Even if the great earth should sink, Though the castle of the divine land should burn, Ever wider shall be the heart of God, And exceedingly pure shall be our mitama, Throwing all grudges and enmities Into the swift rapids of the valley stream, Straight ahead into the paradise of pure hearts, Without distinction of high and low, senior and junior, Without separation of men and women, Without reflecting upon How the way of teachings may be, Together we shall serve before the presence of God! Ah, kanagara, kanagara! May the mitama be prosperous and blessed, Beginning with Kershime and the ugly sendenshi of the dragon clouds, And all the people who followed in service— I pray you, forgive their sins! King Sagaren now and here Manifesting the sincerity of his heart, Before the presence of Ōsakamikoto, With awe and trembling, prays! Ah, kanagara, kanagara! May the mitama be prosperous and blessed!"

サガレン王は悠然として立ち上り、金扇を拡げて中央の高座に登り堂々として歌ひ舞ふ。

『あゝ有難し有難し 天地の神の御恵は 伊行きたらひて隈もなく 吾等を始め人々の 身魂を照らし給ひけり 荒風すさぶシロの島 白雲山の頂きに 吹き起りたる竜雲が 醜の御息に包まれて バラモン教の宣伝使 国別彦の神司 サガレン王の身辺は 日に夜に危くなりにけり 時しもあれや真心の 岩より固き兵士の 現はれ来りて災を 逃れて吾を救ひつつ 花咲く春を松浦の 小糸の館に守り行く 千代も八千代も揺ぎなき

固き岩窟に隠ろひて 白雲山の雲霧を 科戸の風に吹き払ひ 神地の都を十重二十重 包みて曇らす竜雲を 神の軍を励まして 取り除かむと朝夕に 武道を励む折もあれ 神の恵みの浅からず 深霧分けて北光の 三五教の神司 鳩の如くに降りまし 天地の神の御心を 完美に委曲に説き給ひ 至仁至愛の心もて 曲の砦に向へよと 教へ給ひし尊さよ タールチンやエームスの 忠誠無比の司等は 翁の言葉に悦服し

日に夜に励みし武術をば 全廃なして惟神 神の御前に跪き 誠一つの言霊を 放ちて曲を悉く 言向和し天国の 恵みに浴し救はむと 心を定むる折もあれ 瑞の御霊の救ひ主 神素盞嗚大神の 八人乙女の君子姫 心の色も清子姫 又もや此処に来りまし 再び神の御教を 宣らせ給ひし有難さ 吾はそれより君子姫 其他の司を引連れて 白雲山の山麓に 薨輝く神地城 来りて見れば城内は

黒煙四方に立ち昇り 紅蓮の舌は遠近の 建造物を悉く 嘗め尽さむず勢に 暫し見とれて居たりしが 忽ち響く神の声 神の御子なる諸人を 救ふは今や此時と 吾身を忘れて黒煙の 中に自ら突進し 采配振つて下知すれば 君子の姫や清子姫 タールチンやエームスや キングス姫やテーリスの 清き司は逸早く 煙に包まれ悩み居る 数多の人を救ひ出し 城の馬場に連れ来り 色々様々介抱し 労はり守るぞ尊けれ

折りしも聞ゆる宣伝歌 謹み敬ひ心をば すまして嬉しみ聞く程に 小糸の里に現はれし 天の目一つ神司 涼しき声を張り上げて 煙を分けて響きたる 其神姿ぞ雄々しけれ あゝ惟神々々 神は吾等を守りまし 道に背きし人々も 残らず助け給ひけり 吾等も一度は竜雲が 汚き仕業を憎みしが 直日に見直し聞直し 反省すれば尊しや 彼も神の子神の宮 もとより汚れし者ならず 知らず知らずに曲神に 心の根城を奪はれて

道に外れし曲業を 行ひ居たる憐れさよ 決して責むべきものならず 責むべきものは吾心 少しの油断ありしより 神地の都の上下を 騒がせたるも吾なりと 思へば恐ろし神の前 詫ぶる由なき吾罪を 許させ給へ天地の 神の御前に慴伏して 畏み畏み祈ぎ奉る 朝日は照るとも曇るとも 月は盈つとも虧くるとも 仮令大地は沈むとも 神地の城は焼けるとも 神の心の弥広く 吾の身魂のいと清く 怨みも仇も谷川の 早瀬に捨ててスクスクと

清き心のパラダイス 上下尊卑の分ちなく 老若男女の隔てなく 教の道の如何をば 省みずして相共に 仕へ奉らむ神の前 あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましまして ケールス姫や竜雲の 醜の司を始めとし 従ひ奉りし諸人の 罪をば許させ給へかし サガレン王が今此処に 心の誠を現はして 皇大神の御前に 畏み畏み祈ぎ奉る あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ』

§ 2

The god Ame no Mahito stands up and chants a congratulatory song.

Who created heaven and earth, Manifested as the divine Mioya of this world, The Izumitama of Kuniharutachi, The Mizumitama of Toyokunihime, Wove their mitama in the shape of a cross, And caused the teachings of the Ananahi moon To unfold in all four directions. The kami-mitama of the auspicious mitama, Kamususanōnōokami, Drew tight the sacred pillar of the teachings of Izumi and Mizu, Dispatched Yaotome to Mesopotamia, And, bringing to submission Onikumohiko, Who barricaded himself within the Brahman religion, Throughout every remaining nook and corner Of the rich reed-covered land of Mizuhokuni, With the infinite benevolence of Ananahi's teachings, Desired to save a hundred kamibito, Thus did He deeply concern Himself. Nevertheless, the might of Magakami Remaining fierce indeed, Building fortresses upon the mountain ridges and river rapids, Spreading far and wide defiled teachings, The world grew ever more obscured. The kamibito under heaven Suffered and lamented, while mountain and river Withered and dried up to the furthest bounds, Even the dew on the foliage lost its luster, And the sun and moon were veiled in clouds. Desiring to clear away such wondrous eternal darkness Across the great ocean plain, Gathering a hundred kami upon the mountains of Ame-kyō and Chi-kyō, To let shine the great august heart Of the living kami who saves this world, He bestowed divine offices, And throughout the eighty countries and eighty islands of the land, Leaving none out, saving all without fail, Proclaimed the great gospel of salvation. Ah, Kannagara, kami, kami! Awe-struck by the august words of the kami, Undeterred by wind and rain, wearing out sandals and combs, Those propagation messengers who transmit the teachings, Among them the supremely noble prince and princess, The divine official of Kiyoko no hime, Captured by the crooked officials Who served Onikumohiko, Enduring tens of thousands of sufferings, Aboard a half-broken, ribless small boat Upon the raging, wildly surging ocean, floating purely to the island of Siro, Safely arriving at Cape Dondra, Deepening ever more the blessings of the kami, Ascending the valley paths of Mount Kamori, Arriving at the village of Ito, And in the rock cave of the dwelling of a thousand ages, Where the hidden divine official resides, Meeting with King Sagaren, Expounding the true way, Leading the army of the kami, And to rescue the ugly official Ryūun And Princess Kērusu, who coil themselves Within the towering, lofty divine mansion At the foot of Mount Shirakumo, Together with King Sagaren, straddling dapple-grey colts, Oh, the valor of their advancing! From the four directions of the mansion of Siro, Black smoke suddenly rose upward, And tongues of crimson lotus flames licked away countless structures, While Princess Kērusu and the lives of all others Appeared like a lamp in the wind, Therefore, forgetting their own bodies, Beginning with the divine official of Ananahi, Along with the divine official of the Brahman religion, King Sagaren leaped forward promptly, Advancing amidst the black smoke, And under his stern commands, Every single person in the city of the sacred ground was saved, And how precious it was to give thanks For the august blessings of the kami! Ah, Kannagara, kami, kami! With the mitama bringing peace and harmony, The life of the official of King Sagaren, Who had been attacked by the ugly majin and concealed his body, Likewise safe and sound, Bathed together in the blessings of the kami, Tārutin, Kingusu, Tērisu, Ēmusu, Zemu, Ēru, And all the other hundred officials, Their hearts opening up from the bottom, Standing side by side at today's banquet, The joy of superiors and inferiors in peace and harmony Making the heavenly paradise appear right before one's eyes, Proclaiming that the Sahā world is immediately the tranquil light pure land, How precious it is to celebrate before the kami, With reverence and awe we praise and revere Them! Ah, Kannagara, kami, kami! May the mitama bring peace and harmony!

天の目一つの神は立ち上り祝歌を謡ふ。

『天と地とを造らしし 此世の御祖と現れませる 国治立の厳霊 豊国姫の瑞霊 御霊を十字に綾なして 三五の月の御教を 四方に開かせ給ひけり 瑞の御霊の神霊 神素盞嗚大神は 厳と瑞との御教を 麻柱ひ給ひて八乙女を メソポタミヤに遣はされ バラモン教に立て籠る 鬼雲彦を言向けて 豊葦原の瑞穂国 残る隈なく三五の 仁慈無限の御教に 百の神人救はむと 心配らせ給ひけり さはさり乍ら曲神の
