Chapter 20: Rock Cave の邂逅 [1008]

岩窟の邂逅〔1008〕

§ 1

A woman's propaganda song echoing before the natural rock cave in the village of Matsūra.

When Kami appears in the visible world, To set apart good and evil, Kamunahohi, who created this world, Ōnahohi, whose heart is also vast, In human life, whatever may happen, Look upon it straight, listen to it straight, Correct the errors of your body, The divine way of Ananai-kyō. If we trace its very beginning, Appearing as the Ancestor of the land, Kuniharutachinōomikami, Toyokunihimenōomikami, These two pillars of Rigidity and Gentleness, By the wicked works of the divine decrees Of Shihonagahiko and Ōkunihiko, Oppressed, they descended once To the land at the bottom of the roots, Making them endure a hundred hardships, Deep beneath the bedrock of the nether world, Concealing the Kamibito.

The Mikokoro that grants blessings Is higher even than Amatsusora, And deeper than Riugūkai. Nodachihime and Nodachihiko, Appearing as deities of salvation, Divide the spirit of Rigidity; The branch-spirits of the divine decrees Of Haniyasuhiko and Haniyasuhimesan, Together with the noble spirit Of the divine Kami Susanoo no Ōkami, Illuminating the world with the pure, great wisdom Of the supreme and heavy divine realm, Giving birth to the mihana of Kamibito In the four quarters of heaven and earth, How noble it is to assign Instructors far and near. I, a lord born as a child of Kami Susanoo no Ōkami, Awe-struck by my father's divine words, in Kenshōnō-gō of Mesopotamia,

The divine hall of Brahmanism, Close beside Onigumohiko, Serving and wishing to transmit The pure teachings of Ananai, That I began to think was but a fleeting moment, Now has become a dream. We, a band of eight sisters, Leaving Kenshōnō-gō behind, Each and every one of us, with a propaganda song, Chanting as we advanced, Captured by a faction of Kugihiko of Brahmanism, Alas, five sisters linked together, Each and every one upon a shelving, broken boat, Placed and cast away at the whim of the waves, Though receiving the divine grace,

Riding over a thousand and ten thousand waves, Drifting in the midst of the great sea, The view so pure of the island of Shiro, Safely arriving at Dondora Cape, Day and night transmitting and proclaiming The great path of Ōkami, At last we have come hither. Ah, Kanagara Kamikami, May your august spirits bless us, Residing in the mansion of Koito, In the divine island and divine land of Shiro, Beginning with the noble decree Of the divine official of King Sagaren, Official Tāruchin, and Princess Kings, And a hundred other officials besides, Bathed in the dew of blessings, May you overthrow the ugly schemes Of the foul-hearted Ryūun from the very roots, And restore him to his throne as before.

Nevertheless, the infinite benevolent Mikokoro of Ōkami Never harms people, Takes no lives, and with the hemp-pillar's Path of infinite benevolence and righteousness, Illuminating the sky of the heart, The thought to save and help Never misses the great matter. I am a lord born as a noble child of the spirit of Gentleness, Kiyokohime serving in attendance, Appearing right here now, To open up a sincere heart To the people of Brahmanism, The Kototama that we advance to you, Hear it fully, hear it in detail. Though the morning sun shines or clouds over, Though the moon fills or wanes, However strong Ryūun may be, When the true Kami appears And lets fly the Kototama of absolute sincerity,

The crooked deities too Shall be instantly smitten by the divine might, And vanish like clouds and mist. Ah, Kanagara Kamikami, May your august spirits bless us.

Thus stamping her feet and chanting valiantly, she advances before the mansion.

Eymus, drawn unconsciously to this propaganda song, steps forward before the two propaganda emissaries and makes a respectful bow, to which the two also return a courteous bow.

'I am a young female propaganda emissary of Ananai-kyō. The one I have brought here now is Kiyokohime, who is also a propaganda emissary. I am one who, due to certain circumstances, was captured by the subordinates of Onigumohiko, drifted all the way here, and finally arrived. Might you be Kunibiko-sama, the son of Ōkuniwake no Kami? If it is no trouble, I should dearly love to have an audience.'

'Ah, if that is so, please wait a moment. Eymus, I shall go within and inquire. Until then, please be seated and rest.'

エームスは知らず知らずに此宣伝歌に引きつけられ、二人の宣伝使の前に進み寄り丁寧に礼をなせば二人も恭しく答礼し、

『妾は三五教の若き女の宣伝使で御座います。今此処に伴つて居りますのは清子姫と云ふこれも宣伝使で御座います。妾は或事情の為め、鬼雲彦様の部下に捕へられ、此処まで流されて漸く漂着して来た者で御座います。サガレン王様と申すのは、大国別神様の御子息国別彦様では御座いますまいか。お差支なくば、何卒拝顔が致したいもので御座います』

『ハイ左様ならば暫くお待ち下さいませ。エームスこれより奥へ参り伺つて来ます。何卒それ迄お腰を卸してお休みを願ひます』

§ 3

Alas, the five sisters, a pitiful sight, adrift in a frail makeshift boat perilous to behold, cast out without oar or paddle into the vast ocean expanse, overwhelmed by terrifying dread, yet taking Kami as their strength and the sincerity of Ananahi as their staff, crossed the eight-hundred-fold paths of the tide, drifting at the mercy of the waves, and landed at Cape Dondora on Shiro Island, floating in the midst of the great sea where trees and grasses grew lush, arriving here day and night in succession to save the future of King Sagaren, for if humans are the children of Kami and the shrines of Kami, held within the deep embrace of the compassionate Ōkami and advancing with sincerity alone, what fierce fury of crookedness could possibly cause any fear in a heart attuned to Kami.

Come, from this moment on, withdraw all petty divisions such as the teachings of Brahmanism or the doctrines of the Kami of Ananahi, join hands and help one another, vanquish the Yamataorochi, ugly foxes, and Magaoni lurking upon this island of Shiro, rebuild the land of Kami perfectly and thoroughly just as it was in ancient times, and forever open and serve the age of Miroku, thus do Kunskihime and the sovereign reverently and solemnly pray before the presence of Kami, ah, may Kami be blessed, may your divine spirits prosper and grant peace.

Having finished chanting thus, King Sagaren and Kunskihime here opened their hearts to converse after long separation, and joining hands together, they drove out the Magatsuhami possessing Ryūun, Princess Kērusu, and other divine priests, restoring them to their original minds, and resolved to restore the capital of the sacred land of Shiro once more into an earthly paradise of supreme governance and peace, leading King Sagaren at the forefront alongside Tārchin, Princess Kingusu, Tērisu, Eemusu, and other leaders, together with throngs of sincere men and women, with banners flying and drums beating, they rode on horseback, singing propoganda songs, and marched onward toward the capital, may the divine spirits prosper and grant peace.
