Chapter 7: 蒙塵 [995]

蒙塵〔995〕

§ 1

King Sagaren was confined to an itsushitsu, slandered as a hatsukiyōshiya, and kept under genjiyū surveillance by kanshinin, watched without distinction between chiuya. King Sagaren took out an ichigenkin and sang in a quiet voice.

'My father is the Kami of Ōkuniwake, who appeared in the capital of Iho and opened the Oshi, but was driven away by the Kamutsukasa of Ananai-kyō, Natsuyamahiko and Hafurihime, and a hundred other Kamibito. He went to the land of Kenon, taking Onikumohiko with him, and opened the Oshi there, but soon my father departed this world. Onikumohiko became the Oshihetsukasa of the Brahmins, abandoned me, and would not look back. Having no choice, I appeared in the capital of the Kauji on the island of Shiro, and gradually spread the Oshi to the Yomo countries, teaching and guiding the Kunitami until I became their King. Just as this Kunuchi was being governed peacefully, the Riūn of the Ural path appeared from nowhere, rōraku Princess Keerusu, and day by day increased his seiriyoku and fushiyoku. His conduct was bōjakubujin, and he plotted evil things to seize this Kamishiro. Now, I am a bird in a cage, slandered as one whose heart has gone mad. The tears I shed in my helplessness are like the drifting waves of the rough sea. Danger presses upon my body every moment, and my life is one whose tomorrow I cannot know. If the true Kami exists, I pray that Thou wilt suppress the Magatsukami as soon as possible, and restore me to the throne as before. Save the loyal and incomparable Taarchin, Princess Kingsu, Aamesu, Yūzu, Saaru, Zemu, Eeru, and Shirurengu. The Magatsukami, crazed by fame and profit, have forgotten my past favors. The foul-hearted Riūn has slaves who bow before him and brush the dust from his beard, filling the halls inside and out. Justice has perished and evil prospers; the world has become a state of eternal darkness. The sky of my heart is covered in layers of thick clouds, and I can only weep with no way to clear them. Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-mae-mase. May the spirit of Kami grant blessings, and remove this suffering as soon as possible, even for a moment. Kami is with us. If we fulfill the heart of Kami, what could we not accomplish?'

'The way of Kami is to help the good and destroy the Magatsukami. O Jizaiten, shine this upon my heart as soon as possible. I pray with reverence and awe before the presence of Ōkunihiko. Whether the sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, no matter how the Majin may rage, as long as the true Kami exists, how could He not protect the Mahito who single-mindedly guards the way of truth? Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-mae-mase. O Kami, have mercy upon us and save us as soon as possible. I pray not for my own sake alone, but to save the Aohitogusa growing on the roots of the island of Shiro. I pray that Thou wilt protect them.'

'Ah, Kannagara-tamachi-mae-mase. May Thy spirit grant blessings.'

Thus he sang his reflections, his heart calm, in harmony with the sound of the koto.

Teerisu appeared here, and taking advantage of the fact that there was no one around, sang in a quiet voice from outside the room, weaving into her song the message to King Sagaren that the faction of Anan was planning to rescue him. Her song:

'The Mahuraba on the roots of the island of Shiro, standing tall on the summit of the Aogaki mountains, is an evergreen tree whose color is a deep, fresh green. Just as its branches and leaves were flourishing, the evil demon-wind blew fiercely, plotting morning and evening to topple that great tree. The force of the clouds of the fierce-hearted tornado was strong, and though it once enveloped the peak, the Shinato-no-kaze that blows from the breath of Kami is a Kannagara that should never cease to blow. While entrusting your heart to Kami, the strong-powered Tokitsukaze that blows away the eight-fold clouds in all directions is about to blow now. O King Sagaren, wait a little longer, for it is upon you.'

'The clear sun and moon are Teerisu, Anan, Saaru, and the pure spirits of Seeru, Uinchi, and Aamesu, which shall become rain and wind to wash and purify your surroundings; wait a little longer. May the spirit of Kami grant blessings, and the joy of sweeping away the spirits that nest in the Yamata-no-orochi and the ugly fox shall be yours. In the middle of the night of the Ariake moon, when even the water sleeps, I shall watch for the hour of the ox-full, and save your life to restore you as before. In this Kamikuni, your majesty shall shine brightly, and you shall be revered by the Kunitami as their great Lord and Master. With the moon and sun, your light shall compete, and you shall see it with your own eyes. May your heart be calm and peaceful.'

と心静かに、琴の音に合せて、述懐を歌ひつつありぬ。

テーリスは此処に現はれ来り、あたりに人無きを幸ひ、室の外より声も静に歌ひてサガレン王に、アナンの一派が君を救ひ出すべく計画しつつありとの事を、歌に装ひて述べ立てたり。其歌。

『シロの島根の真秀良場や 青垣山の頂きに そそり立ちたる常磐木の 色も青々若緑 枝葉も茂る勢に 醜の魔風の吹きすさび 其大木を倒さむと 朝な夕なに計らひつ 心も猛き竜巻の 雲の勢強くして 一度は峰を包め共 神の御息に吹きうつる 科戸の風はいつまでも 吹かずにあるべき惟神 神に御心任せつつ 八重雲四方に吹き分くる 強き力の時津風 今吹かむとすサガレンの 君の命よ今暫し 待たせ玉へよ汝が上に

清き月日はテーリスや アナン、サールを始めとし セール、ウインチ、エームスの 清き身魂は雨となり 風ともなりて君の辺を 洗ひ清めむ待て暫し 神の御霊の幸はひて 八岐大蛇や醜狐 巣ぐふ身魂をサラサラに 払ひ除くる喜びは 有明月の真夜中頃 水も眠れる丑満の 時を伺ひ汝が命 救ひまつりて元の如 此神国にサヤサヤに 御稜威輝き国人の 大主師親と仰がれて 月日と共に其光り 競ひ玉ふも目のあたり 心平に安らかに

Thus, he lined up his own arbitrary logic, confined King Sagaren in a secret room, bound his freedom, and without shame, launched words of evil and rebellion, attempting to force an abdication. The King, stunned by the excessive insolence of Ryūun, remained silent and looked down, offering no response, staring toward the direction of Ryūun's voice.

At such a moment, the sound of firecrackers echoed outside the castle, along with the shouts of hundreds of people, 'Waa Waa.' Sensing that this was no ordinary event, Ryūun, followed by Hime Keerusu, stood up from the scene in a pale panic.

Ryūun: 'Surely, this must be the attack of Anan's faction; prepare for defense at once.'

As he issued orders to the temporary left and right guards of the castle, Keriya and Haruma, the two gathered the people within the castle and armed them in a rush. While they were in a state of confusion and panic, running up and down, Anan's army had already broken through the front gate and was charging into the depths of the castle with war cries.

Eemusu and Teerisu, gaining momentum from these sounds, stood up with courage. Watching for the moment when Ryūun, Hime Keerusu, and the others were distracted by their defense preparations, they took King Sagaren, hid from public view through the back gate, and escaped to parts unknown.
