Chapter 10: Rock 隠れ [998]

岩隠れ〔998〕

§ 1

King Sagaren sings along the road.

In the land of Tokoyo, the Jizaiten, As a descendant of Ōkunihiko, I who was born into this world Am the Kamutsukasa of Kuniwakehiko. Driven out from the capital of Iho, To the Kenonkiyō of Mesopotamia, Together with Onikumohiko, Just as I was transmitting the teachings, The Kamutsukasa of Ananai-kyō, Futodamakami, appeared before us. When he released the Kototama of Zengenbiji, Onikumohiko, who falsely claimed to be The supreme leader of the Brahmans, Trembled with fear and panic, Exposed his hideous form, And fled away to no one knows where. Treating me, who appeared as a child And a descendant of Ōkuniwake, as a rare prize, He oppressed me morning and evening, And the divine punishment for his rampant violence

Was truly a terrifying warning. Ever since then, I fled from the land of Kenon, Roamed the land of Fusa, the land of the Moon, Wandered across all mountains and rivers, Enduring a thousand hardships and myriad sorrows, Crossing over the strait of Bona, Arriving safely on the shores of Shiro, Passing through the hardships of facing rain and wind like combing with rain and brushing with hair, At last advancing into the capital of the divine land, the bungalow, Thanks to the efforts of spreading far and wide The Brahmanism opened up by our ancestral deities, All the myriad people rejoiced and submitted, Until at last I was pushed forward to become king, Called King Sagaren, Together with Princess Keels, The teachings and rituals of the gods Vowing morning and evening to the great deities,

Serving them even at this very moment, When suddenly raising clouds and descending, the hideous dragon cloud of Magatsuhi Was slaughtered by the sword-like tongue, And the princess was completely taken captive, Coming toward me and urging me To believe in the Ural teachings. Ah, Kannagara, gods, gods, The august spirit and body of mine Who am a descendant of Ōkunihiko Ōkami, How could I ever obtain the state Of serving the teachings of the Urals! Thinking that the wrath of the gods is also terrifying, Steeling my heart, all alone, I kept the teachings, but Day by day, the power of the evil deity Prospered, and now right here, I am a cold pillow of grass in my ruthlessness, A stray small boat on a drifting shore. If I approach seeking a shade to rely on,

The dew beneath the pine of the ever-verdant tree Still wets my sleeves, and is cold. Nevertheless, the dew of the grace Of the imperial deities, without drying, Moistened my very body, And by the peerless loyalty and sincerity Of Thames and Ames both, I was at last saved from imminent peril, And this single body has safely Fled and arrived all the way up to this place. Now that I think of it, how grateful it is! Even more than when both Theris and Ames, Who served Ananai-kyō, Served me, While upholding Brahmanism, With their hearts maintaining a single sincerity Like a hempen pillar, They helped me and now right here, The sincere heart with which they guided me, Clearly the gods of heaven and earth

Must be perceiving it, Kannagara. Before the august presence of the gods, I offer My sincere heart and humbly give thanks. Whether the morning sun shines or is cloudy, Whether the moon waxes or wanes, No matter how furiously the evil deity rages, The power of sincerity saves the world. Upholding sincerity alone, Entrusting one's body solely to the gods, Not grieving over the past, nor worrying over the future, Safely guarding the present moment alone, the way of the gods, To speak good in this very moment, To do good, and to think good— This indeed is the way that a human being, Born as a child of the gods of heaven and earth, Should observe with caution morning and evening, And fulfill to the utmost. Ah, Kannagara, gods, gods, May the august spirit of the gods bless us,

All the way until reaching the village of Matsuura, Without any twists or turns, free from any hindrance, With a calm and peaceful heart, May we advance, Kannagara. Kunitokotachi no Ōmikami, Before the august presence of Ōkunihiko Ōkami, I bow with awe and pray. Ah, Kannagara, gods, gods, May your august spirit bless and prosper us."

As the party had been fleeing day and night along rugged valley paths, their legs grew tired and their bodies somehow fell into a mood of fatigue. Thinking that having fled this far they could finally be at ease, they rested beside a huge boulder that appeared as if it had sprouted from the earth by the side of the valley path, and while reminiscing about the commotion of the night before, they were deeply and endlessly lost in conversation.

Already the captors under the command of the dragon cloud, led by Yol, holding sharp-edged hand spears, dressed in extremely light attire, equipped with straw sandals and puttees, wearing black hoods and nimbly clad in black garments, were waiting impatiently for King Sagaren's party to flee and arrive. This valley path had steep mountains on the east and west standing like walls, and they had descended amidst the fog along the narrow path of the Morimori River flowing through the valley. Because of the dense fog, human figures two or three ken ahead could by no means be seen.

一行は嶮岨な谷道を、夜を日についで逃れ来りしこととて、足も疲れ体も何となく倦怠の気分になつて来た。もはや此処迄落ち延ぶれば一安心と、谷道の傍に土から生えたるが如く現れたる大巨岩の傍に休息して、一昨夜の騒動を追懐しながら、窈々と話に耽りつつあつた。

早くも竜雲の部下の捕吏は、ヨールに引率され、名自鋭利なる手槍を提げ、装束もいと軽々しく、草鞋、脚絆の扮装にて、黒頭巾を被り黒の衣を甲斐々々しく纒ひながら、サガレン王一行の落ち延び来るを今や遅しと待つて居た。此谷道は東西に嶮しき山、壁の如くに屹立し、其谷間を流るる河森川の細道伝ひに霧籠む中を下り来たのである。濃霧のため、二三間先の人影はどうしても見る事は出来なかつた。

Sannin at last recovered from the fatigue of their shintai, and as they were about to stand up again and descend the tanimichi, a suspicious hitogoe was heard coming from a little toward the shimote. Theris had a kogoe-like familiarity with that voice from nowhere in particular. Theris lowered his voice to a kogoe and said,

Theris: 'If Your Majesty, that voice is certainly the voice of Yōru, Ryūn's buka. That cunning wretch Ryūn, aiming to capture Your Majesty on the road, has cleverly outpaced us and is making people wait here, it seems. Koriya, you must not be careless. First, let us lower our voices for a while... Fortunately in the nōmu, shall we decide to wait for them to tsūkuwa?'

King: 'Their hakarigoto is watertight to the very end, truly astounding. As you say, let us lurk here for a while and peer into their dōsei.'

Eīmusu: 'If Your Majesty, look, the voices of shigonin or so have drawn near in a buzz. Let us quickly hide behind this rock.'

Nōodding, the King hid his person together with Futari behind the daiganseki. It was a daiganseki thought to be about chiyokukei shijiyō. Shigonin, enveloped in the nōmu, hid behind that rock and decided to wait for Yōru's buka to tsūkuwa.

Not knowing even in a dream that King Sagaren's itsukau were hiding behind this rock, Yōru came before the great rock whispering something in a kogoe, and said,

Yōru: 'Now everyone, rest your breath here for a while, nourish your eiki, and let us decide to wait for the akunin-tomo of King Sagaren, Theris, and Eīmusu to fall behind and reach here. This chiten is truly an exquisite spot. Rock mountains stand sheer to the east and west, one side is a tanimichi like a narrow throat, the north is a steep slope that seems to pierce the chest, and they will surely come here. If so, a rat in a bag is just the same. Well, leisurely drinking water, nourish your eiki, successfully capture all three alive, and shall we receive words of praise from Lord Ryūn? Now is the time for success in the world. Missing this opportunity, on what day will it ever be possible to manifest outstanding cleverness? Thinking and thinking, truly good fortune has come turning toward us, wai!'

Retto: 'Yōru-sama, what shall we do if King Sagaren should by any chance not come to this road? That would be completely useless, naa. He is surely not limited to coming southward. If the King should take the road to the north, cross far over Bōka Bay, and escape toward the Deccan Plateau of India, that would be nothing but wasted labor and a fatigue profit, and is there anything at all that can be done about it?'

Yōru: 'That is where unputenpu lies. If the god of happiness protects us, surely the King's itsukau will certainly take this road. If the god of happiness dwells in Karl's itsukau instead, surely the King's itsukau will certainly take the road crossing the Bōka Strait to run to India. Having said that, it is not possible to go to both sides with a single body, and there is a proverb... He who chases two hares catches neither... It is better for us to obey simply as the gods will. Then the gods will gaze upon our sincerity and surely grant us a feat of arms. More than that, it would be a major disaster if the hakarigoto leaked out. For a while now let us keep silence and wait for the King's troop to draw near. Up to now we have abused our speech organs considerably each for himself, but since we have now faced the battlefield just the same, I strictly command a blackout of kototama. All you fellows, speak quietly. Nay, if you want to speak in silence, it is fine if you spoke.'

Bitto: 'Already the hikaoruma are exhausted to the limit, the raw materials for kototama are also lacking, and we have unavoidably fallen into a plight where both mouth and legs must experience a blackout. How is it, everyone, the warehouse of the belly has evidently become quite empty, and the throat has begun to blow a steam whistle. Shall we store bento into the belly right here, nourish the eiki, and wait for the right time?'

Yōru: 'That is also good. Now, bolt it down quickly, bolt it down quickly.'

Saying this, each of them produced rice balls and was cramming them into their mouths with a look of delight.

The party of the King held their breath, straining their ears to listen so as not to miss a single word of this conversation.

Retto: 'My belly is full with this already. Those worms in my belly kept shooting arrows of urging incessantly, but as the owners of the hangar, we are now able to say that we have fulfilled our responsibility. I have come to feel a mood of carefree leisure, as if saying that the rise and fall of the nation is none of my concern. Hey Bitto, lend that gourd over here. No matter what, since we are facing the Sagaren King of high divine virtue, we cannot possibly stand against him unless we are in a cheerful drunken mood, you see.'

Yōru: 'Hey Bitto, let us gather our spirits right here and wait for the enemy. Bring that wine out here. However, since this has a remarkably strong alcohol content, you must not drink too much. First of all, since your legs will give way first, drink with caution. You must never overdo it.'

With that, starting from himself, he began guzzling straight from the mouth of the gourd that held about five shō, finished passing the gourd to Retto, slapped his own forehead sharply with both hands,

Yōru: 'Ah, what kind of wine is this? I have never tasted such sweet wine since I was born. I wonder why on earth it has become so sweet.'

Bitto: 'That would indeed be so. It is called gourd wine, a special kind that makes the flavor of the wine exceptionally good. Moreover, since it has been sloshing around for three whole days today, it is truly at the right stage for drinking. Furthermore, because your body feels relaxed all over, it seems even more delicious. ... When one is thirsty, even muddy paddy water tastes like the nectar of immortality ... is there not a dodoitsu saying just that?'

Yōru: 'Hey Retto, do not hog it all to yourself, and pass it over to my side quickly.'

Bitto: 'Now, now, Commander Yōru, that is somewhat unreasonable of you. Did you not take a sip earlier? Since Retto drank just now, the next turn is mine. After that, we must distribute it to the two persons, Ranchi and Rūzu. Please wait until it has gone around once. From a while ago, the worms in my throat have been rumbling and urging me on ...'

Retto: 'If so, drink quickly. And finish the round quickly, so you can hand it over to Yōru-sama.'

Bitto: 'Understood.'

Sooner said than done, adopting the philosophy of removing one's hat the moment one meets someone, he began drinking greedily straight from the mouth of the gourd. Then, passing it around in a circle, they somehow forgot their own mission and completely emptied the wine in the gourd. The five of them gradually became intoxicated with the alcohol taking away their footing, and they began to babble loudly at one another.
