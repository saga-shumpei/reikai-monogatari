Chapter 3: Moon 休殿 [677]

月休殿〔677〕

§ 1

Tatsukuniwake, Tamaharuwake, and Kuniyoriwake, the Sannin, sat in the inner chamber of the Chin no Yakata, enjoying the dinner prepared by Umeteruhime, and the host and guests relaxed and indulged in conversation about all sorts of things.

Umeteruhiko: 'Based on the poem by Tamaharuwake-sama earlier, I have understood that you are going to Takaharuyama in Tsu no Kuni. However, is it not a bit of a detour to go from here?'

Tamaharuwake: 'It is a bit of a detour, but there is a reason for this. In fact, I had intended to come by way of Fukuchiyama, passing through Kaibara and crossing the Oni no Kakehashi, but on the night before departure, I had a terrible dream... and so I changed my route to this way. I realized that it would be proper to pay my respects at the Gankutsu of Takakumayama, where Tamateruhiko-sama appeared. Since we are starting a Kototama battle against the demons, we must undergo a great deal of training. The propagandists of Takahime and Kurohime, regrettably, thought they could intimidate the enemy from a high place without any effort by riding an airship, and so they left, but even today, more than three months later, there is no news from them; that is why Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto has sent us secretly. Aside from us three, no one among the people of the sacred land knows of this. There are many spies from Brahmanism and Alps-kyo who have infiltrated as believers, so one cannot be careless with what one says. Even if there were no spies from other religions, it is troublesome because if we inform the executives or the believers, they will immediately blab about even the most important matters. It is so strange that I wonder why they cannot keep a secret to that extent. Since it was said that we should tell no one but the three of us, we must keep the secret to the very end, you see.'

Kuniyoriwake: 'Oi, Tamaharuwake, you just said that the executives blab, but even though the two of us said nothing, did you not sing such a secret in a loud voice in front of the gate? The saying about the monkey laughing at its own backside refers to you.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Ha ha ha ha, the secret has been exposed after all, has it not? One says, "This is a secret, so I will tell no one but you, so please do not tell anyone." The person who hears it says, "Yes, yes, I will never tell," while saying to the next person, "This is a secret, so I cannot tell anyone. I have told only you, so please be sure not to speak of it to others," while swearing them to secrecy. It is repeated in that way from one person to the next. And the person who said they would tell no one but one person ends up telling everyone they meet, even without being asked, saying, "You are the only one," and in the end, the secret spreads. Things said as a public advertisement are not paid attention to by anyone, so they do not spread, on the contrary. It is the style of "deciding on only you, but then having an affair or a whim of the day," so it is troublesome. Is that not so, Kuniyoriwake?'
