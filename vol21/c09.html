Chapter 9: 改悟の酬 [683]

改悟の酬〔683〕

§ 1

Even without rain, the Mizukasa of the Kosui increased, and already the water drifted up to the Ichi of the noses of the Sannin. The single rock that had towered in the Kosui was now submerged in the Suichiu, and it appeared as though only three black heads were floating on the Komen. The moon was suddenly enveloped in Kokūn, and fine snow, through which one could not distinguish Shiseki, began to fall abruptly; the Kanrei was as if it would cut the body. While the Sannin were fretting with one another over how to handle the fact that their Inochi was approaching its Shiyunkan, they still did not perform the act of praying to Kami. Suddenly, three Kuwakiu appeared, illuminating the Ankoku Suimen with a flash, and raged about the Jiyōge and Saiu of the bodies of the three. Just as it seemed the Kosui had split in two, three beautiful Megami appeared from the Kotei, and while holding small jewels in their Yunde and drawing sharp Moroha swords in their Mete, they appeared before the three in a Shizushizu manner, and before they knew Itsu, the rock appeared high above the Suimen. Then, to the point where not a single drop remained at the base of the rocky island, the water split to the Saiu and dried up. It was a mistake to see them as three Megami; the one standing in front was the six-year-old Ohatsu, next was Tamajiwake, and next was the large man, Mokusuke.

Enshu: 'Ya, you are Tamajiwake-sama, please, I beg you to save only my Inochi.'

Tamajiwake: 'This Kosui is the dwelling place of a serpent of the kenzoku of Yamata no Orochi. It is entirely the work of the serpent that the water of this lake was split to the Saiu. If you quickly repent from the bottom of your heart and return to the path of Makoto, it shall be well; otherwise, I shall punish you thoroughly with such Shinpen-fukashigi no Shinjutsu. If you continue to be stubborn, an event such as being buried in the belly of the serpent will occur right before your eyes.'

Sunshu: 'Please, save me just this once. I shall never commit evil deeds again.'

The Kosui, as far as the eye could see, gradually decreased in Mizukasa, and finally, even the Kotei appeared, leaving only a single river flowing through the center.

At this time, Mokusuke, Tamajiwake, and Ohatsu again approached this place while singing a Sendenka. The song:

Mokusuke: 'The Kototama of Seoritsuhime-Ōkami / I revere, and Tamajiwake / The messenger of Kami, to the lake of Tsuda / To the dwelling of Magatsu, I speak and turn / To save the world from disaster / With a haraobi in my heart, the Tokookishi / Enduring the world of the divine command / Disguising my body as a humble woodcutter / Changing my name to Mokusuke / From the Kotei of the lake of Tsuda / I wait for the time to purify it / Just at that moment, the three-five / The Senden-shi of the teachings of Kami / Tamajiwake came to visit / Mourning the soul of the wife / Who had deep attachment / To the suffering of the land of the Kotei / To repay the divine grace / Of having saved her, now here / Together with my daughter Ohatsu'

Appearing, the thread of life was saved, and upon that, appearing at the Drum Waterfall, swinging a steel hoe, cutting down the rocks so hard, suddenly the bottom of Lake Tsuda was revealed. Here, the three of them, eagerly, seeing the single rock to which the lives of Enshiu, Bushiu, and Sunshiu were entrusted, came and looked, and how is this? Is it our own figures or an illusion? The three Megami, not differing by a single Sunbun, have appeared here. Illuminated at last by the Mihikari of the Kami's grace, their Kiyosugata of Reinikuitsuchi, we are already the Futobashira of the true path of the Shinkai; truly, it is the limit of holiness.

Ah, you three Hitobito, from now on, Torinaho your hearts, cast away your small desires, and cause the single flower of sincerity to bloom, which shall not wither for Mangomatsudai. Become the Mitate of the Kami, bearing the taste and fragrance of the peach fruit, and serve Ichihaya. Utsushiyo is but a Yumemaboroshi Ukiyo. For Ikuchiyo, without limit, may your Inochi flourish as the land of the Kami. Become Kami while still alive, and become people who save the Yobito. Quickly change your hearts and follow us.

Tamajiwake: 'Though Mount Takaharu be high, and though Takayorihime be fierce, to submit and harmonize to the Kototama of sincerity alone is before our eyes. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara. May your spirits be blessed, and protect us, beginning with Mokusuke-shi and the incarnation of the Kami, Ohatsu-jo, deeply, so that we may gain complete victory in this Kototama battle without incident. Make these three physical shrines into the noble shrines of the Kami, washing and purifying them to bless their spirits. Use them for the divine work of achieving the divine government. Before the presence of the Lord Susanoo, who governs the Saien-kan, the divine command of Konohana-hime, who dwells eternally on Mount Tenkyo.

May the Oni and Orochi lurking in the heart, and the Shikome and Sagame, rejoice and transform into true Kami. Protect this physical body so that it may stand and work in Utsushiyo, healthy forever, as the age of the Kami. Kannagara. May your spirits be blessed.'

As Tamajiwake finished singing at the top of his voice, the figures of the three who had appeared until now became the original Megami once again, and while dancing the dance of the Tennyo, they ascended toward the mid-air, and finally, their divine forms could no longer be seen. The three, Enshiu, Sunshiu, and Bushiu, shed tears like a waterfall while choking with gratitude. From behind the three, purple clouds rose high above their heads, making a sound like steam, and from within them, like a mirage, the three Megami appeared, holding bells in their right hands and opening fans marked with the crest of the sun and moon, dancing wildly in the mid-air. This was because, since the three assistant guardian Kami of Enshiu, Sunshiu, and Bushiu had left their bodies, their main guardian Kami within their spirits had rejoiced and revealed their divine forms to express their joy. While everyone was marveling at this miracle and reciting the Amatsu-norito, the three, Unshiu, Sanshiu, and Koshiu, appeared before Mokusuke, leading by the hand a Megami of beautiful complexion, and repenting their past wrongs, they shed tears and pressed their hands together in prayer. When they looked at the Megami who had appeared here, led by the three, how could this be; it was the figure of Osugi in the prime of her youth, as she was ten years ago, and so Mokusuke, without thinking, did not know what to do.

Ohatsu: 'Ah, is it the Seirei of my mother? How glad I am that you have remained safe.'

Osugi's Seirei: 'Mother, thank you for coming here.'

Ohatsu clings to the Seirei of Osugi and collapses in tears of joy. From behind the three men of Kafushiu, Unshiu, and Sanshiu, purple clouds rise once again, and as before, a beautiful Megami appears, performing a Bukiyoku in the Kūchiu, before hiding her Sugata once more within the clouds.

The Bauakubudau Tauzoku, Sanshiu, Unshiu, and Kafushiu, are moved by the Magokoro of Mokusuke's daughter, Ohatsu. Although they had once stolen the Kingin and returned, they feel a strange sense of being pulled by their Ushirogami, and without realizing it, they are drawn to the grave of Osugi. At this moment, a shadow emerges from the grave; it is Osugi, who should have died thin and withered, but she now appears with a Chiunikuchiuzei, her complexion Akumade white, and her Genki Akumade Ōsei. From this point, Osugi departs from the Shiyaba of Shifuchiyakushin and ascends to Tengoku, praying for the happiness of her husband and her Hitotsubudane, her daughter, whom she left behind, and joins the ranks of the noble celestial beings. When one considers this, how fearful are Shifuchiyakushin and desire. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemase.

From this point, Tamajiwake explains the path of truth earnestly to the five people including those from Enshiu, and after promising to meet again, he bids them farewell here. Together with Mokusuke and Ohatsu, he braves the hardships and ascends vigorously toward the cavern where Takayorihime holds her ground, singing the song of propagation.
