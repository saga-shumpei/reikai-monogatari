§ 1

Towering to press upon the clouds, upon the summit of Takaharuyama, the divided spirit of Yamataorochi, which has possessed Ōkuniwake, who founded the Brahman Kyō, the ugly Magahi has taken its stronghold, glaring down upon the mountains, fields, rivers, and seas; together with the mountains of Ōe, Mikunidake, and Rokukafuzan, it blows forth cold Makaze, and Takayorihime, a subordinate of the Mukade Hime, breaks her heart morning and evening, her pigeon-breast heaving; the depths of the Shigumi are like a split rock, and the scorching of the gall is truly terrifying.

Holding the Seto Sea to the south, looking down upon the village of Naniha to the southeast, and overlooking the overlapping mountain ranges to the northwest and east, they built a rock cave at the absolute peak of Takaharuyama, established a sect of the Brahman Kyō, called it the Alps Kyō, and were troubled in heart day and night, seeking to hold the Jitentōtō Island within the sphere of influence of Yamataorochi to the very end. At the foot of the mountain lies the long and narrow Lake Tsuda. In the waters of this lake, many snake gods, which are the alter egos of the great serpent, lay in ambush, exhaling evil spirits day and night, causing the air on the earth to rot. Takahime and Kurohime abandoned the main temple in Kitayamamura, Persia, left Imoriwake and Magahiko to guard the rear, and as proof of their repentance and conversion to the Sangokyō, they rode an airship that had come from the land of Persia to persuade and harmonize Takayorihime of the Alps Kyō, arriving at the foot of Takaharuyama. From here, the two changed their appearance into that of pilgrims and climbed the high mountain, which stood like a wall, intending to advance to the rock cave of Takayorihime of Takaharuyama.

At about the fifth station of Takaharuyama, giant rocks called the Forest of Heaven stand in a row, and in the midst of the lush trees in the center, there is a small shrine. This is called the Palace of the Ryūjin. This Ryūjin is praised as a god who freely controls rain and wind, and Takayorihime respected it as her only guardian deity. For that reason, she strictly forbade anyone from approaching this territory. Two rough men named Teristan and Karlins were guarding this Palace of the Ryūjin firmly. The two are sleeping on the rocks, snoring loudly. Takahime and Kurohime finally climbed up to this place.

Takahime: 'Are these not magnificent rocks standing in a row? Let us take a rest in this place with such a beautiful view. There is still quite a long way to go to the summit, after all...'

Kurohime: 'That would be fine.'

They pushed open the door of the go-board-shaped gate and advanced into the depths.

'Ah, there is a strange shrine here. This must be the only weapon of the rumored Takayorihime that brings rain and raises wind. Shall we try to convert it? As they say, he who would shoot the general must first shoot his horse, so it might be a shortcut to convert the followers of this evil god who raises rain and wind.'
