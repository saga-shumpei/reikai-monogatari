§ 1

Tamaharuwake, Mokusuke, and Ohatsu, the three who had finally climbed up to the side of the shrine in the forest of Ame located on the mountainside of Takaharuyama, rested their legs on the flat rocks arranged in a row in the surroundings and indulged in whispering.

Tamaharuwake: 'It has been almost two futatoki since we arrived here, but Tatsukuniwake has not appeared, nor has Kuniyoriwake shown his face; I wonder what on earth has happened. There should be no concern that they have gone ahead. Since the plan was to meet in this forest and ascend as a group, they surely would not ignore our promise and go up alone. Ah, how slow they are.'

Mokusuke: 'What, there is no need for worry; they will appear before long. As the saying goes, speak of the devil and he shall appear; when one is talking about a person, they just happen to come. They will surely come climbing up here majestically before long.'

Tamaharuwake: 'Both Kuniyoriwake and Tatsukuniwake have quite the physiognomy of being prone to trouble with women, so I wonder if they have been deceived by some demonic woman on the way and have forgotten the essential Goshingefu.'

Mokusuke: 'Never, never, such worry should be unnecessary. When we parted at the shore of the lake of Tsuda, they showed signs of a very firm resolve. They should have no dealings whatsoever with women. They are as firm as a withered tree leaning against a cold cliff in the middle of winter, so I cannot recognize them as the kind of people who would cast their eyes on women.'

Tamaharuwake: 'No, one cannot be too reassured. After all, their past is their past, and they were quite flamboyant when they were young. Even if they appear to be as rigid as Ishibekinkichi in front of many people, like a golden helmet that will not budge even with a lever or a pole, once the rogue called the fire of passion in their hearts begins to spew smoke, they are the sort of things that might be enveloped in that soot and smoke, lose their sight, and derail to some outrageous place. Kyōshu Kototamayoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama perceived their inner feelings and even said at the time of their departure that they must never get involved with women, as this Kototama battle is a trial for you missionaries, so they are truly unreliable things. Even an ogre or Enma would not have the concern of getting angry upon seeing the face of a beautiful woman. How much more so for humans. No matter what one says, their history up to now is their history.'

Mokusuke: 'There should be no concern that such women are wandering about on this mountain path where ranges of mountains overlap. As for living creatures, there are only wild boars, wolves, snakes, foxes, and badgers. As for humans, there are only the members of the Alps sect wandering about; what I worry about more than women is whether they might be encountering the groups of the Alps sect and repeating hard battle after hard battle, and are spending their time because of that. Ōtaniyama is also a place with quite a lot of evil gods, and Rokkōsan is a point where the followers of Thelistan are sometimes stationed leading their subordinates, so they might be dealing with them. Since there are many of those fellows wandering about just as they bullied the woman last night, they must be fighting a hard battle.'

Tamaharuwake: 'Ya, what happened to your forehead? Is that not a terrible wound?'

Tatsukuniwake: 'The moment I stumbled and fell on a stone, I struck my forehead.'

Tamaharuwake: 'That is strange. One would think you would strike your nose, which is one part higher, so why on earth were you struck on the forehead?'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Ah, there is a slight reason for this, and I cannot clearly explain it. Please, as there is a firm promise regarding this matter, do not ask me about it.'

Tamaharuwake: 'It is not a matter of a promise. You were surrounded by the subordinates of the Alps sect and had your head struck, were you not? We have gone to such lengths to gather the three of us, hoping to return unscathed and having achieved a splendid victory, so it is truly regrettable that we have produced a wounded man.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'No, no. I have not met a single person from the Alps sect. The journey was extremely safe and peaceful.'
