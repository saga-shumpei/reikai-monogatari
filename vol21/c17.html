Chapter 17: 酒の息 [691]

酒の息〔691〕

§ 1

Gathering many of her subordinates in a rock cave at the summit of Mount Takaharuyama, known as the Karihonzan of the Alps-kyō, the leader of the Alps-kyō, Takayorihime, was waiting for the time to come while training her spirit and working with the intensity of a Nejihachimaki Daishiyarin to revive the Shingefu of Daijizaiten Ōkuniwakenomikoto; stroking the Kojiwa on her forehead and tapping her Nagakiseru, she beckoned her two right-hand men, Teristan and Karlins, close to her knees, and with foam gathering at the corners of her mouth, she spoke:

Takayorihime: 'You two, Teristan and Karlins, why don't you give up drinking at some point? I have a feeling that ever since the important secret documents of the Alps-kyō were lost, they have somehow ended up in the hands of the Ananai-kyō. No matter how firmly we fortify our Eugai, once that map is seen, there is no choice but for this headquarters to fall. This is no time to be drinking and rambling on in such a dangerous situation. If you do not pull yourselves together, this castle will not be maintained.'

Teristan, thoroughly drunk and slurring his words, replied:

Teristan: 'To think that Teristan would be so careless about such things—hen, that is quite different. Do not underestimate a hero of the world so much. I have no motivation left, eh, Karlins?'

Karlins: 'Oh, indeed, indeed. As they say, cold water on an old man—when cold water is splashed on us, even the sake we have painstakingly drunk sobers up. We drink sake to get drunk, ramble on, and cultivate the Kōzen no Ki. It is not easy to endure climbing up and down these steep mountain paths many times a day. We once occupied an airship that Takayorihime-sama arrived on, and just when we thought we were buzzing along, we lost our direction due to a deep fog and crashed it against the side of Mount Ōtani, smashing it to pieces. At that time, ah, it was a terrible disaster, I broke my precious elbow, and it has only just begun to heal. Even so, it speaks to me from time to time, and on cold mornings, it throbs with pain. I am risking my life to this extent for the sake of the Alps-kyō. What is a cup or two of sake compared to that? Takayorihime-sama, if you intend to be a leader of men, you must have a slightly larger heart. If you keep nagging like that, everyone will lose interest and run away, won't they, Teristan?'

§ 2

Speaker: 'You are all such a nuisance when you drink. It is not that I begrudge the sake itself, but it is the Yakamashi aftermath that troubles me. If a Gauketsu like Mokusuke, who they say has already stuck his head into the Ananai-kyō, were to come here, would that not be a Taihen matter?'

Speaker: 'Why, is there any need for such Shinpai? That is what you call Raubashin. Since our Taishiyō is still a Chiubaa, it would be well if you stopped at the level of Chiubashin, but because you worry with such Fukaan, you end up causing Sogo in our Keikuwaku, and everything we do ends up in a Kuhichiga, as mismatched as the beak of a crossbill. Whether it is Mokusuke or a Kumosuke, what is there to fear even if five or ten dozen of such fellows come in a bundle? How could we ever save the Tenkabanmin by having them join the Brahman-kyō or the Alps-kyō with such an attitude?'

Speaker: 'Are you taking a Hankauteki taido toward me, your Kyōshiyu, unlike your usual Kingen selves, just for today?'

Speaker: 'There is neither rebellion nor submission in us. It is the sake that is speaking as its heart desires. I have heard a verse somewhere that says, "Be patient, the sake says, my wife." It is by no means our bodies that are speaking. Since it is the sake that speaks, please scold the sake. Sake is a good thing... no... it is a bad thing after all. Ah, come now, floating and floating, is it only the gourd that floats? Our hearts are floating things too. Living in this floating world on three-fun-five-rin, an optimist like a floating dragonfly—without this, a human cannot live a long life. Obā-sama, instead of making such a complicated face, why don't you drink some sake yourself and become a "feces-floater," if not quite a flood in the privy?'

Takayorihime puffed up her cheeks, glared at the two with all her might, and said, 'Speaker: 'You two have the duty of leading this large group, yet what thoughtless behavior is this? Do you despise the Kyōshiyu of the Alps-kyō?'

Speaker: 'In any case, since they are a breakaway group from the Brahman-kyō, they are merely a branch store, a reseller, or perhaps a religion that infringes on design registration rights; where is there any value to respect? Until now, we have been wearing a mask, but the essential purple jewel has been swallowed by Takahime, and Kurohime and Takahime are quite formidable, possessing the magical power to escape even through the eye of a needle if they wish. The reason she is acting so submissive, eating nothing for a hundred days and remaining calm, is because she has something to rely on in her heart. If we dawdle, this mansion will be attacked from three sides by the Ananai-kyō, and we will soon face the misery of being unable to move, like a crab with its legs torn off. It is right before our eyes. Oh, it is a fire in the privy! It is a burning mess! There is no future in dealing with an old woman like you. Come, get angry if you wish. A woman with one foot in the coffin will never be a match for a robust Teristan or a Karlins, ha ha ha!'

鷹依姫は面を膨らし、一生懸命に二人を睨め付けて、

『お前達二人は此大勢の団体を、統率して行かねばならぬ役目でありながら、何といふ不心得の事だえ。お前達はアルプス教の教主を軽蔑するのかナ』

『どうでバラモン教の脱走組だから、支店や受け売か或は意匠登録権侵害教だ、何処に尊敬する価値がありますかい。今迄は猫を被つて居つたが、肝腎の紫の玉は高姫に呑んでしまはれ、黒姫と高姫は中々豪のものだから、針の穴からでも、出ようと思へば出るといふ魔力のある奴だ。彼奴がアーして神妙に百日も物を喰はずに平然として居るのは、何か心に頼む所があるからだ。愚図々々して居ると此館は三方から三五教に攻撃せられ、蟹の手足をもいだ様な、身動きもならぬ憂目に逢ふのは目睫の間に迫つて居る。エーもう雪隠の火事だ。焼糞だ。お前の様な婆に相手になつて居つても末の見込がない。サア怒るなら怒つて見よ。棺桶に片足を突込んだ婆と、屈強盛りのテーリスタン、カーリンスには到底歯節は立つまい、アハヽヽヽ』

§ 3

He pressed the Tokuri to his mouth and drank with great gulps, then struck his own forehead with his right hand.

Karinsu: 'Now, Karinsu, is this not a potent sake? It is far more effective than the old hag's nagging, Ohohoho, Ahahaha!'

He was guzzling the desperate drink with reckless abandon.

Ohatsu, who had turned six years old, appeared there with quick, firm steps without saying 'excuse me' or anything else.

Ohatsu: 'Oji-sama, please pour a cup for me too.'

Oji-sama: 'Well now, where did you come from? You are truly a cute child. Drinking sake while receiving lectures from the old hag's face is not sweet at all, but even if you are a child, pour me a drink with those cute hands. However, the Tokuri is heavy, so be careful not to drop it.'

Ohatsu: 'Oji-sama, if a Tokuri like this is heavy, how could you have come into this Ihaya all by yourself?'

Oji-sama: 'That is true. You are a clever one. And did you come here all alone?'

Ohatsu: 'No, no, my father carried me here on his back. Father is now pulling out Takahime-sama and Kurohime-sama, making that old hag called Takayorihime-sama repent, and saying he will make both Te and Ka his Kobun, so he was consulting in the forest of Ame.'

Oji-sama: 'What, your father said he would make me his Kobun? If one becomes a Kobun to such a Taishiyō, there is no one in the world to fear. It would be terrible to follow a Taishiyō who grumbles even if one drinks just a little bit of sake, in a way that one would not leave him even if one died like a louse's egg. Well, the sake seems to have gained a bit of flavor now. Hey, old hag, these Te and Ka are no longer your subordinates; they are, if I may say so, disciples of the martial arts master, Mokusuke-sama of Yutanigatake, no, they are his sworn brothers. Come, hurry up and surrender the castle and get out. If you dawdle, the missionaries of the Sangokyō might appear here, bore a large wind-hole in your belly, run a club through it, and carry you off to the holy land. It would be to your advantage to tuck up your skirts and get out quickly while it is still light, would it not, Karinsu?'

Karinsu: 'How cold and distant you all are. Did you not swear that you would accompany me wherever I go?'

Oji-sama: 'You are a fool. If you do not say that, you will not be treated as important, so as a conventional means of living, I merely used smooth diplomatic language, right, Terisutan?'

§ 4

'How easily your hearts change, indeed.'

'It is a settled matter; there is a saying to follow the world of the times. The world will not remain the same forever. When the times change, even the Kami change. Do not say such foolish things. As expected of an old woman, your head is outdated. Shall I let out a bit of your old blood and replace it with new blood?'

As he suddenly clenched his fist to strike, Ohatsu blocked him and said,

Ohatsu: 'Now, now, Oji-sama, you must not be so violent. Obaa-sama, you have truly trusted a wicked fellow, haven't you?'

Kora, kora, though you are but a child, what wicked things are you saying? Even though I look like this, I am a new human being who adapts to the times and leads a splendid cultural life. Do not underestimate me so much.'

Ohatsu: 'Oji-sama, because I am a child, you do not know what I might say. It is wrong for a grown man to catch a child who has not even reached school age and argue with her, ho ho ho.'

'Ya, as expected of the young lady of Oyabun Mokusuke, you are truly impressive. ...Young lady, please speak well of us two to your father and treat us with favor.'

Ohatsu: 'For an adult to ask a child to treat him with favor, is that not a bit strange? You are a peculiar old man.'

'Are you two really intending to become the kobun of Mokusuke?'

'That is a settled matter; hurry up and go somewhere else. If the propagandists of Sangokyō appear soon, it will be inexcusable to have Kurohime and Takahime shut away in such a place. ...Kārinsu, you supervise this place and keep watch over this old woman. I am going to the inner chamber to ask the two, Takahime-sama and Kurohime-sama, to come out of the Iwato, do you understand?'

'Yosh, yosh, I have understood, go quickly. If you dawdle, the propagandists of Sangokyō will have already broken through the difficult barrier and arrived at the forest of heaven, which one might call the 203 Meter Hill, so if we are to surrender the castle, it is better to surrender it now for the sake of future profit.'

Saying this, he hurriedly ran off toward the rock cave where Takahime and Kurohime were confined.
