恩愛の涙〔688〕

§ 1

Tamaharuwake crosses the Kosui of Tsuda and ascends the front of Takaharuyama to attack. Mokusuke follows behind, carrying Ohatsu on his back.

The sun has gradually set, and the moon is wrapped in Yukigumo, its form flickering in the Tenkū in an Oboroge manner.

As the party stops in the shade of a certain tree to rest, the hurried footsteps of many people approach moment by moment.

Tamaharuwake and Mokusuke peer through the shade of the Oborozukiyo to see what is happening, and they see a group carrying a woman with a Sarugutsuwa in her mouth, grunting as they bear her.

Suspecting something, the two hold their breath and watch as the many men set the woman down in a slightly wider space in the shade of the trees before them, remove the Sarugutsuwa, gather around, and begin an interrogation.

Man: 'Hey woman, where are you from? Confess.'

Woman: 'I am a person from the capital, Gozaimasu.'

Man: 'Do not talk Baka. If I glare at you with these black eyes, there is no mistake. You are a country woman, are you not?'

Woman: 'Why do you ask such a thing?'

Man: 'I ask because it is necessary. Confess everything clearly and be done with it.'

Woman: 'I am a person born in the capital of Naniwa. I have no father, no mother, no brothers or sisters; I am a pitiful solitary person who wandered into these deep mountains and was captured by you, but I do not remember doing anything wrong. Please, let me go.'

Man: 'You are undoubtedly the wife of the person who obtained the important documents of the Alps-kyō. Now, confess truthfully the whereabouts of your husband and the whereabouts of the documents.'

Woman: 'I wondered what it was, but this is a strange inquiry I never expected; this is the first time I have heard of such things.'

Man: 'You are the wife of... I forgot his name... the propagandist of the Sangokyō, are you not?'

Woman: 'No, no, absolutely not; I am no such person.'

Man: 'You are a stubborn and brazen woman. Even if we subject you to water torture or fire torture, we will not let you go until you confess.'

Woman: 'No matter what you say, I do not know what I do not know.'

Man: 'This one will not speak with just a simple approach... Hey everyone, let us strip her naked and make her perform some interesting tricks.'

Others: 'That is good, that is good.'

With that, the group strips the weeping woman naked.

Man: 'Now woman, say woof. If you do not speak, this stake will pay a visit to your head.'

Woman: 'No matter what you say, a human cannot imitate a beast.'
