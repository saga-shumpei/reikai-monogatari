§ 1

Terisutan appeared before the Mitsushitsuzen,

Terisutan: 'Excuse me, it is I, Terisutan. Takahime-sama, Kurohime-sama, how is your Gokigen?'

Takahime: 'You are Terisutan, are you not? You always treat us with Keibetsu, so what is the meaning of this Teinei way of speaking only today? It is likely because the Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō have arrived that you are saying such Tsuishiyō. Isn't that right, Kurohime-sama? Is he not a man with no Nukeme?'

Terisutan: 'No, that is certainly not the case, but I have been moved from the bottom of my heart by your Goshintoku. Dōzo, please come out quickly.'

Kurohime: 'Even if you say come out, did you not lock it with an Ebijiyō as if we were mere Kamisama? You may think you have put the two of us into an Ihamuro, but these are the Ketsukō Omito of Takahimedaimiyōjin and Kurohimedaimiyōjin, you know. What do you think you are doing? Unless you bring an Ōnusa to purify us thoroughly, offer many gifts, recite the Amatsu-norito in full ceremonial attire, and perform the dance of the Iwato-biraki, these Megamisama will not come out so easily, you know.'

Terisutan rattled the stone door with the key and threw it open,

Terisutan: 'Now, Dōzo, please come out.'

Kurohime: 'Ah, thank you. Now, Takahime-sama, shall we go out?'

Takahime: 'Kurohime-sama, what are you saying? Are you senile? Hey, hey, Terisutan, you have opened the door of someone's residence without permission; whose authorization did you receive? How about I sue you for trespassing?'

Terisutan: 'Takahime-sama, please do not be so stubborn; I beg of you, please come out.'

Takahime: 'If you beg me to come out, I might consider it. Today, I shall exempt you from the offerings and the Norito. In truth, I have no desire to remain in such a dark place for even a moment longer. Now, now, Kurohime-sama, you go out first. You have seemed to want to go out for quite some time now.'

Kurohime: 'Please go out first, Sensei. It would be too rude otherwise.'

Takahime: 'In that case, I shall take the liberty of going first. Thank you for your trouble for so long. Now that I have come out this far, I, this Takahime who has swallowed the miraculous purple jewel and the wish-granting luminous jewel, even if tens of thousands of heroes were to attack, I could scatter them all with a single snort. Shall I now go and bring Takayorihime to submission through the power of Kototama?'

Takahime: 'What a remarkable child there is. I have never heard since ancient times of an adult being saved by a child. Jisetsu is a Ketsukō thing indeed.'

Kyōshu-dono: 'As it is in the mountains, there were no feasts to offer, and I had ordered Teristan and Karins to serve you proper food three times a day, but I suppose I could not offer you anything that would please you.'

Teristan: 'Hey, old woman, are you shifting your responsibility onto us? You said that if we kept them like this, Takahime would become shriveled. Now that it has come to this, do not use such cowardly double-talk. Right, Karins, there is no mistake in what I am saying, is there?'

Just then, from the Omoteguchi, singing a Sendenka, Tatsukuniwake, Tamaharuwake, and Kuniyoriwake entered in the Sentō, followed by Chikarazuyo no Mokusuke and six other believers of the Alps religion, crowding into the room.

Tamaharuwake: 'Yo, you are Takahime-sama and Kurohime-sama. Yo, well, I am glad you have remained safe. We received the inner command of Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama and finally climbed up to this mountain from three directions, heading for the Kototama battle. Ah, this is wonderful. 'That is truly, truly, thank you all for your hard work. You have come well. Mokusuke-sama, it seems Tamaharuwake-sama has been greatly indebted to you. I offer you my deepest gratitude.'

'Everyone, you have come well. By the way, has this Obaa-sama not yet performed Kaishin?'

'Come now, Obaa-sama, now that it has come to this, it is useless to be stubborn. Repent of everything completely, return to a heart of sincerity, and live in this world purely and beautifully as a living shrine of the glorious Kamisama.'

With these words, determined by the small lips of Ohatsu in a voice that somehow possessed a deep power, even the stubborn Takayorihime shed tears, and finally, she burst into tears and collapsed on the spot.

Tamaharuwake: 'And what was the name of that son of yours?'

Takayorihime: 'Yes, I do not know what has become of him or where he is now, but as for his features, his nose was ten percent higher than other people's. His name is Tatsuwaka. He was quite a scoundrel and caused his parents much worry, but I wonder how he is doing now, ah me.'

Tamaharuwake: 'Now, now, Tatsukuniwake, did you not say your name was Tatsuwaka? And you once told me that you had a mother. There is something about the eyes and the high-bridged nose that resembles this old woman. Could it be that she is indeed your mother?'

Tatsukuniwake clasps his hands together, sighs deeply, and lets tears fall one by one.

Ohatsu: 'There is no mistake that the son of Takayorihime is Tatsukuniwake, the Sendenshi of the Ananai-kyō. Kami has arranged this to bring you to this mountain for the reunion of parent and child. In consideration of Tatsukuniwake's Kaishin, I shall forgive the sins of Takayorihime. Tatsukuniwake, the woman you met in front of the old shrine last night was a Keshin of mine.'

Tatsukuniwake remains silent, presses his hands together, and is overcome with tears of joy.

Takayorihime wipes away her tears and says: 'Ah, so you were my son Tatsuwaka? Oh, I am so glad you have undergone Kaishin. You have become a splendid Sendenshi. From this moment on, I too shall undergo Kaishin, so please, I beg you to forgive my sins.'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Was it you, Mother? I am so happy to see you.'

Takayorihime: 'You, what happened to the scar on your forehead? I fear you were hated by someone and injured, were you not?'

Tatsukuniwake: 'Yes.'

Ohatsu: 'Through this scar on his forehead, Tatsukuniwake has had the sins of his Mitama completely removed and has been reborn with a crystal Mitama. It is by virtue of this that the reunion of parent and child has been permitted. He has by no means engaged in any strife, so Takayorihime, you may rest assured. If we continue to speak forever, there will be no end to it, so come, everyone, let us recite the Amatsu-Norito together, offer words of gratitude and prayer, and proceed to the sacred ground all together.'

At these words, everyone bows their heads in surprise, rinses their mouths, washes their hands, recites the Amatsu-Norito, and sings the Sendenshi-ka in a loud voice, led by Tamaharuwake.

『さうしてその伜の名は何と云ふ方でしたか』

と玉治別の問ひに、

『ハイ、今は如何なつたか行方は分りませぬが、顔の特徴と云へば一割人より鼻の高いもので御座いました。そして名は竜若と申します。偉いまあ極道で親に心配をかけよつたが、今頃はどうして居る事か、アーア』

と袖を絞る。玉治別は不審さうに、

『コレコレ竜国別、お前も竜若と云つたぢやないか。そして一人の母があると話した事があるなア。何処やら此婆アさまに目許、鼻の高い具合がよく似て居るやうだ。もしや此婆アさまぢやあるまいかな』

竜国別は両手を組み、ウンと吐息しながら涙をホロホロと流して居る。

お初『鷹依姫の伜は三五教の宣伝使竜国別に間違ひはない。親子の対面させるために、神が仕組んで当山へ差し向けられたのです。竜国別の改心に免じ、鷹依姫の罪を赦して上げよう。竜国別の宣伝使、昨夜古き社の前にて汝の逢うた女は妾の化身であつたぞや』

竜国別は無言の儘両手を合せ、嬉し涙にかき暮れる。鷹依姫は涙を払ひ、

『アヽ、其方は伜の竜若であつたか。ヨウ、マア改心して下さつた。立派な宣伝使になつたものだ。もう是限り母も改心するから、何卒妾の罪をお詫して下さい』

『母様で御座いましたか、お懐かしう存じます』

『お前、額の疵は如何なさつた。矢張人に憎まれて怪我をしたのぢやないかな』

『エヽ』

『竜国別は此額の疵によつて、身魂の罪をすつかり取払はれ、水晶の身魂と生れ変れり。其徳に依り親子の対面を許したのである。決して争ひなどを致したのではないから、鷹依姫御安心なさるがよからう。何時まで云うても果しがなければ、サア皆さま、一緒に天津祝詞を奏上し、感謝祈願の詞を捧げて、聖地へ一同うち揃うて参りませう』

との言葉に一同ハツと頭を下げ、口を嗽ぎ、手を洗つて天津祝詞を奏上し、宣伝歌を玉治別の音頭に連れて高唱する。
