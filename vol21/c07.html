誠の宝〔681〕

§ 1

To the dwelling of Mokusuke at the Sanroku of Mount Yuyagadake, the demons of the heart, Unshiu, Sanshiu, and Kafushiu, who had fallen in a sudden turn from the Shinkoku of Takaamahara to the Nesoko no Kuni in order to achieve an Omoshiro-nu Mokuteki, approached the Kobiki no Koya with their flawed feet looking around restlessly.

Unshiu: 'Now, Kiyōdai, this is the Shiyōnenba. We must completely Matsusatsu the Meishi called good here, and carry through with evil to the Akumade. However, one who intends to commit evil cannot do so if they reveal an Akusau. Since we must wear a mask of goodness, or else the enemy will Misu our Uchikabuto, Sanshiu, you must chant a Shiyushiyō-rashii sutra.'

Sanshiu: 'Even if you tell me to chant a sutra, I do not know anything at all, so there is no Shikata.'

Unshiu: 'Anything will do. Just line up things around there at random. Let me try saying one. Ah, how much must I educate you? Being a teacher who has caught a low-intelligence child is no small task. Just say the names of the tools and everything there in reverse. First, a folding screen and sliding doors, a pot and a cauldron, a sake bottle, cedar and pine, the gate, and other names of ours. Standing at the gate, you say: Bubeu, Mafusu, Benāmakā, Chibahi, Shibahi, Tsuma, Gisu, Dokā, Shiuun, Shiusan, Shiukō, Kewaruhā, Mātā, Kēwā, Nīku, Tsutā, Kewā, Riyōnikū, Sukemokunobōniyōnō, Gisūōsān, Dāshin, Dāshin, Waikawaika, Wakaimatsū, Kahanō, Kanādē, Kutabetsunatsute, Ruōdē, Roā, Hanniyaharamitashingiyō, Un, Abokiya, Sugikōnorīmōde, Bōdorono, Shiuren, Orīyāmāshīta, Asa, Asa, Rekorakaharekano, Rakadawo, Ramoi, Shiyoma, Hanniyaharamitashingiyō, that is how you say it.'

Sanshiu: 'Even if you say such things, I do not understand. Why do you not say it in a way that is easier to understand?'

Unshiu: 'Not being understood is the value of a sutra. Are there any of those octopus-headed monks or propagandists these days who can read a proper sutra?'

Sanshiu: 'Hey Kafushiu, it would suit you well. If you do not like being a temporary monk, how about becoming a propagandist for the Ananai-kyo and singing a propaganda song well?'

Kafushiu: 'That is a shorter path. It would be Omoshiro if our intentions were communicated to each other. Now, I will perform a propaganda song. If I do that, Mokusuke might surely lower his head, wag his tail, and jump at it. You all follow me in a low voice behind Lord Ka.'

§ 6

Unshiu, while making a rumbling sound in his throat, said, 'Is that true, Mokusuke-sama? However, if we speak of the three men's Keiriyaku here completely, we might end up having our ties severed by the Oyakata of Enshiu.'

Mokusuke replied, 'Is it not fine to have ties severed with a Dorobau? Do you think of being a Dorobau as such a Ketsukō Shiyōbai?'

Unshiu said, 'I want money, and it is painful to have ties severed with the Oyabun of Enshiu. Hey, Sanshiu, Kafushiu, shall we reveal the Himitsu and take the money to go home? No, if we reveal the Himitsu and take the money, our Koken will be lowered. No matter how strong this fellow is, his limits are known. Come, the three of us, let us pounce on this fellow and tie him up to take him back.'

No sooner had he said this than they pounced on Mokusuke from three sides. Mokusuke, as if he were merely catching a Tefutefu, said, 'How Koshiyaku, you Hahemushi fellows!'

He knocked the three of them down together, held them firmly between his legs, clenched his fist like a Sazae, and said, 'I have explained things this much and acted in an Otona manner, yet you try to climb over me and do such things. Mohaya, you have no hope of repentance. Come, the moment this fist touches you, your life will be at an end. I shall make you accompany my wife.'

Just as he was about to strike, his six-year-old daughter Ohatsu ran out to the scene and said, 'Otōsama, wait a moment, please. And please give this money to these people.'

Mokusuke said, 'I have been working day and night to save this money, thinking that after you grow up, you might find a good husband and live in comfort. This money is not mine after all; in my heart, I have already given it to you.'

Ohatsu said, 'Otōsama, then please give it to me now.'

Mokusuke said, 'Oh, I will give it to you anytime. Now? I shall give it to you now.'

Ohatsu said, 'Then I have received it. Hey, you three gentlemen, I will give all this money to you, so take it and go home. In return, please use this to start any kind of Shiyōbai, and from now on, quit such a frightening Shiyōbai. Otōsama, please, I beg you to save these three men.'

Mokusuke said, 'Very well, very well. Hey, you three lucky fellows, listen well to what my daughter says, take this money to start some kind of Shiyōbai, and do not do bad things hereafter. Now, take it and go home quickly.'

The three bowed their heads at once and said, 'We are truly sorry for what we have done. In that case, we shall borrow it for a while and return. We will surely return this.'

Ohatsu said, 'I did not lend it; I gave it to you, so I do not need it returned. Having such a frightening thing is not for the sake of my future. Ah, Otōsama, I feel relieved now. You have truly saved me. Just because of this money, I was so afraid every single day that I could not even sleep. Mother also became ill like that because of this money.'

The three men, no sooner than they took the money bundle Ohatsu handed them, fled the scene like clouds and mist.

Mokusuke held Ohatsu in his arms, and while overcome with tears, said, 'Ah, Ohatsu, I am grateful. I have obtained a treasure more precious than gold or silver. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemase.'

He pressed his hands together in prayer, lost in tears of joy.

At that moment, the sound of the night storm blowing by shook the rain shutters with a rattling noise.
