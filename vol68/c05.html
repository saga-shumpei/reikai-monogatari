Chapter 5: 変装太子 [1729]

変装太子〔1729〕

§ 1

In the inner chamber of the Crown Prince at Tarahan Castle, Crown Prince Sudarman and Alina are talking closely together as usual, leaning their heads close to whisper a certain secret.

Alina: 'Taishisama, how was it last night? Surely Princess Subāru must have rejoiced as well.'

Blushing slightly and turning his face away as if to hide it from Alina, the Crown Prince replies,

Taishi: 'No, truly it was delightful. There is nothing quite as joyful as the fulfillment of life's romance. I felt as though I had been born anew. I am grateful for this, attributing it entirely to your devoted efforts.'

A: 'How extravagant! What are you saying? For a subject to exert all their strength for their lord is only natural. However, the house of Taruchin was a humble thatched hut without a shred of dignity; it must have been terribly cramped for you. That I caused one of your exalted station to frequent such a place is entirely due to my own inadequacy.'

Tai: 'Even so, there was no other suitable house to hide the Princess, and you did everything in your power for my sake. Such worry is quite unnecessary. As for myself, always living and sleeping in a spacious mansion, I grew quite fond of that poetic thatched hut. I learned the flavor of commoner life, and for the first time yesterday I came to appreciate the ease of commoners and the sweetness of having everything settled simply without fuss—it was truly blessed. I felt as though I had become a human being for the very first time. Ah, why was I born into such a station as this? Coming and going through the gates, I am seen off and welcomed by throngs of grandiose guards, in a style precisely like escorting a tiger in a zoo. If it were possible, I would gladly exchange places with you.'

A: 'It is only natural that you should think so. I can well imagine the cramped circumstances of your life. However, since you are a Child of God who descended from heaven with the mission to become the sovereign of the Tarahan nation, this alone is something that cannot be helped. Therefore, I am striving to the utmost so that you may have your freedom as much as possible.'

Tai: 'The truth is, Alina, I have a special favor to ask of you. Will you not grant it? It is the earnest desire of my lifetime.'

A: 'Given that my body and soul have received boundless grace through generations of ancestors, I shall stake my life on whatever you ask.'

Tai: 'I am satisfied with your prompt consent. The truth is, Alina, I would like you to disguise yourself as me and stay here within the palace for a while.'

A: 'Indeed, that is a brilliant idea. Are you planning to make me your double while you frequent the hiding place of the Princess? For half a day or a day, I suppose I could pass for you. However, if it goes on too long, the tail of a transforming fox will show.'

Tai: 'Ha, ha, ha, ha! Whether it is a transforming fox or a transforming badger, I know not, but since your face is the very image of mine, there is little chance of turning tiles into gold. I beg of you.'

A: 'Since it is the command of the Crown Prince, I shall humbly accept whatever may come, but even if I manage to pass myself off in your precious golden person, the gold leaf applied will soon peel right off; thus, this is a remarkably grave duty for me. I too shall try assuming the role of the Crown Prince for today, whether for a day or half a day, and taste the mood of a crown prince. Pray, do you go and taste the mood of a commoner for a while.'

Tai: 'Ah, how fascinating, I beg of you! From this evening, mingling with the dusk, I shall pull my head-covering down snugly, wrap myself in a laborer's garb, hum a tune, and set forth. Would you quietly procure such clothes for me?'

A: 'Anticipating that such a command would surely be given, I have properly prepared them.'

Tai: 'Do you happen to have acquaintances among the laborers?'

A: 'No, I cannot say I have acquaintances as such, but I bought them at a second-hand shop in the side street.'

Futo: 'No matter what, I seem unable to remain still. My soul appears already to have visited the tea-master's mansion in the forest of Mukō. Hey, I can bear it no longer; lend me working clothes!'

Futo: 'Ahahahahaha, do not worry about such things. As early as this morning, I practiced leaping over the back high wall. When the mind is set, what is there that cannot be accomplished? Futo: 'Ah, today of all days, leave me to my freedom. Even the feat of leaping upon dark clouds must be forgivable for the sake of romance. Ah, I wonder how Princess Subaru is doing. Surely she is stretching out her white neck, opening her window right now, peering out to see the figure of me going to her. Ah, how adorable she is... Hey, Subaru, I am coming now, so wait for me. I certainly will not do such a thing as abandon you. What I said—"I will love you forever and ever until the age of Miroku"—is a promise I shall rarely tear up as waste paper.'

Arina scratched his head, and

Come, let me teach you how to wear the Raudōfuku. Quickly take off your Kini.'

Taking Arina's words as a hint, the Crown Prince unhesitatingly stripped off his Kini and ended up entirely Matsupadaka. Arina took out the Raudōfuku from the large trunk he had brought and had the Crown Prince put it on. With a broad smile, the Crown Prince said,

Ta: 'Oi, Arina, what do you think? Do I look the part of a laborer?'

A: 'Indeed, you look very well suited for it. You look like a laborer complete with a golden signboard. Since Denka is blessed with great virtue, whatever costume you wear truly suits you wonderfully. As a laborer, too, you are truly splendid. No wonder Himesama falls so desperately in love with you.'

Ta: 'Even better, I would never want to part with these clothes for all eternity. I should like to become a laborer, live in a cramped nine-and-a-half-mat back alley, and have Himesama look after me as a devoted housewife, even if it means experiencing a simple life just once, ahahaha. Oi, Arina, I leave the rest to you.'

Before he had even finished speaking, seizing the opportunity of his newfound lightness, and pulling his cheek-covering scarf tightly down, he vaulted over the high wall like a monkey, skillfully leaped across the deep moat, and concealed himself within the dense grove of the castle's riding ground. Left behind, Arina stood dumbfounded and sighed,

A: 'Ah, a troublesome matter has arisen. I can only hope he reaches the tea-ceremony master's mansion safely. Ah, shall I wrap myself in this Kini, which I have never worn even once since I was born, and pass myself off as the Crown Prince until tomorrow morning?'

Draping the Kini over himself, he put his own clothes away in the trunk, deliberately let down the bamboo blinds with an air of great solemnity, placed a paulownia brazier in front of him, spread out numerous floor cushions, and sat there in solemn state like a disguised demon.

A: 'They do say even a monkey looks good in fine clothes, and how well this suits me. Let me go to the next room and take a look in the mirror.'

So saying, he stood up promptly, entered the mirror room, and muttered to himself,

'Ah, even to myself I look so strikingly like the Crown Prince that I can scarcely tell the difference. If I keep up this disguise for a lifetime, my tail will rarely ever be caught. Denka loves the life of a commoner, and I am much the same; yet, having been born a man, to ascend the throne just once is also a manly task. If the Crown Prince wishes me to take his place forever, for his sake I would gladly do so. It is also an honor for myself. Still, I wonder if I shall be able to cleverly deceive the eyes of Daio Denka, my father Samori, and the other senior vassals. This is precisely what is called the art of leaping upon dark clouds. The Crown Prince risks danger to fulfill the fullness of his romance, while this Arina is about to risk even greater danger to ascend the throne. Even Tokugawa Tenichibō would run away barefoot, ahahaha. No, but the elder vassals might come to pay their respects at any moment. Come, I must sit in solemn state upon the Crown Prince's throne.'

§ 4

Then, leaving the Hall of Mirrors once more, the Taishi sat down in the living room with an innocent face.

Led by an okujiyochiu, his father Samori came to visit, pretending to inquire after the Taishi's gokigen.

Samori clapped his hands twice with a pon-pon sound, bowed deeply to the floor, and spoke.

Samori: 'Ah, old minister Samori respectfully comes to inquire after your highness's gokigen. I consider it a matter of supreme congratulation that our father the daiōsama is also without any okaha and happily administers his goseimu. While trembling with awe, as an old minister, I humbly submit a word to your highness for the sake of the ōke: my son Alina has grown excessively indulged by your highness's gochiyōai, no longer regards his parent as a parent, utters akugenbaugo, and babbles incomprehensible nonsense about democracy or kiyōsanshiyugi, causing this father to tekozu greatly. Moreover, claiming he is on goyō by your highness's side these days, he has not returned to my mansion even ichido. Since this konban happens to be the meinichi of my bausai, I wish to have him sanpai before the reizen; therefore, I beg you to grant him leave until miyōchō. I have come to make this earnest petition.'

Alina's heart pounded with a start, and his ganshiyoku suddenly turned pale, his lips even trembling, yet he was an ōchiyakumono after all.

Gathering his breath firmly into the lower abdomen below the navel, with supreme audacity, he performed the Taishi's vocal imitation for the very first time.

Alina: 'Oh, are you the old minister Samori? You have done well to enter the palace despite your aged body. I consider it satisfactory. As you say, since my father extremely soundly administers affairs of state, by all means, do not distress your heart. Since it is already nighttime, and I have matters I wish to study a little, retreat from this place at once. I shall permit you to meet me again tomorrow.'

Samori: 'While trembling with awe, it is not that I dare disobey your highness's command, but no matter what goyō there may be, please return Alina to me for this konban alone.'

Alina: 'That Alina went out saying he would return to my father's mansion before two o'clock. I surmise he must have crossed paths with you on the way.'

Samori: 'Ah, is that so? This was a rude thing to say. Then this old minister will also hastily return home; please excuse me.'

So saying, hurriedly guided by the okujiyochiu, he went down.

Seeing him off, Alina let out a sigh of relief and spoke.

Alina: 'Ah, that was like leaping right over hell. However, it is as though I have broken through the front line where dark clouds soar. Since he is mistaking his present son for the Taishi and going home, it is already all right. Transforming myself so skillfully that even that sharp-witted old tanuki could not see through my true identity is entirely the protection of Heaven. But the other difficult pass will be when the Daiōsama appears. Ah, fretting over worries beforehand is forbidden. Well, at that time, the wind of that time will blow again. Ah, delightful, delightful! I somehow feel as though I have become the king of the Tarahan country. Hee-hee-hee-hee!'

With audacious fearlessness, he let out a triumphant laugh.

The veil of night was drawn, and from room to room the flames of silver candles began to flicker.

原文（近代文語）

と又もや鏡の間を立ち出でて、太子の居間に何喰はぬ顔して坐り込んだ。そこへ奥女中の案内で父の左守が太子の御機嫌伺ひと称し訪ねて来た。左守はポンポンと二拍手しながら低頭平身し、

左『エヽ老臣左守謹んで殿下の御機嫌を伺ひ奉ります。父大王様にも御変らせなく御政務を臠はせたまふこと大慶至極に存じ奉ります。畏れ乍ら殿下に、老臣として王家の為めに一応申上ますが、臣の悴アリナなるもの余り殿下の御寵愛に溺れ親を親とも思はず、悪言暴語を放ち、デモクラシーだとか、共産主義だとか訳の解らぬ事を申て、此父を手古擦らせます。それに此頃は殿下のお傍に御用なりと申し、一度も吾館へ帰つて参りませぬ。どうか今晩は亡妻の命日で厶いますれば、霊前に参拝させ度く思ひますれば、どうか明朝迄お暇をお遣はし下さいませ。折り入つてお願ひに参りました』

アリナはハツと胸を轟かせ、俄に顔色青ざめ唇さえビリビリと慄ひ出したが遉の横着物。臍下丹田にグツと息を詰め、大胆至極にも初めて太子の口真似をやり出した。

ア『やア其方は老臣左守で厶るか。老体の身をもつて好くも入内致した。余は満足に思ふぞ。汝の申す通り父は極めて健全に政務を臠すによつて、必ず必ず心痛致すな。もはや夜間の事でもあり、余は少し研究したい事もあれば、一時も早く此場を退却せよ。又明日面会を許すであらう』

左『恐れ乍ら殿下の仰せを否むでは厶りませぬが、如何なる御用が厶いませうとも、今晩だけはアリナをおかへし下さいませ』

ア『其アリナは二時以前父の館に帰ると申て出ていつた。察する所汝と途中で入れ違ひになつたのであらう』

左『アヽ、左様で厶いましたか、これは失礼な事を申上ました。それでは老臣も急ぎ帰宅を致しませう、御免下さいませ』

と云ひ乍ら倉皇として奥女中に手を引かれながら下り行く。後見送つてアリナはホツと一息つきながら、

ア『アヽ、地獄の上の一足飛だつた。併し乍ら暗雲飛び乗りの第一線を突破したやうなものだ。現在の悴を殿下と間違へ帰るやうだからもう大丈夫だ。彼の抜目のない狸爺が吾正体を看破する事が出来ない迄巧に化け済ましたのも全く天の御保護だ。だがも一つの難関は大王様のお見えになつた時だ。エヽ取越苦労は禁物だ。まア其時は又其時の風が吹くだらう。あゝ愉快々々。もう何だかタラハン国の国王になつたやうな気がする。イツヒヽヽヽ』

と大胆不敵にも会心の笑を漏らして居る。

夜の帳は下ろされて間毎々々に銀燭の火が瞬き出した。
