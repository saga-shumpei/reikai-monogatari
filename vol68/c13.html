Chapter 13: 蛙の口 [1737]

蛙の口〔1737〕

§ 1

The residence of Umori no Tsukasa Sakurensu, which held an extreme fear of the outbreak of disturbances in the castle town on Goguwatsuitsuka, was strictly guarded by several tens of guards and police officers, and did not permit even the shadow of a suspicious person to draw near.

Threading the gate amid such heavy vigilance, a single woman entered with a composed air; she was Shinobu, the head maid who served deep within the palace.

Without the slightest hue of fear, brandishing upon her shoulders the authority of serving within the palace, she appeared at the entranceway,

Shinobu: 'Umorisama, I come on an errand from the palace. Am I permitted to pass?'

she inquired.

The gatekeeper, Sāru, bowing respectfully while speaking,

'Ah, Shinobu-sama, you have indeed entered. As I will presently report to my master, I ask that you wait here for a brief moment,'

he spat out, and stepped stealthily into the inner chambers. After a short while, Umori came forth smiling broadly with a grin floating across his entire face, and in an exceedingly courteous tone,

Umori: 'Ah, well, well, could this be Shinobu-sama? Come, please pass straight through to the inner chambers. I shall humbly hear the purport of your business.'

Although the official post of this Shinobu was vastly inferior compared to Umori, because she was one who served close to the private chambers of Daiō-denka, she was endowed with a certain authority. Moreover, even though they were Sasumori and Umori, they were always forced to yield a step toward the court ladies of the palace.

This was because, should they by any chance incur the wrath of the court ladies, there was fear that the influence would instantly extend to their respective positions.

Even Umori, skilled as he was in craftiness and wicked wisdom, habitually displayed the utmost flattery, spared no sycophancy, and exerted treatment that held her above all else, especially toward this head maid Shinobu.

Guided by Umori with a composed demeanor, brushing past the shrubbery of the garden, Shinobu was led to a detached room visible yonder, which, though not overly spacious, was somehow tasteful and refined. Placing a Shende brazier in the center, the two put their heads together and indulged in a secret conversation.

Umori: 'This is truly, truly thanks to you for visiting us since early morning. Awakening late, the cleaning within the house has also gone unattended. Due to the recent disturbances, the menservants and maidservants have also fled, and you grace us with your arrival at a most extremely inconvenient place; it is truly the height of shame. And what sort of matter might be the purport of your business in coming here today? If you would be so good as to let us hear your command, I should be truly grateful.'

Shinobu, with solemn dignity, straightened her deportment, and looking down upon Umori with words somewhat grave, spoke:

'My coming today is for no other matter; as an imperial envoy of Daiō-denka, there is a matter I wish to impart to Umori-dono. Humbly receive and listen.'

Umori started, lowered his head, and retreating two or three paces,

'For the Imperial Envoy-sama, your toiling is immense. The purport of the imperial message from Denka, I respectfully receive and obey.'

Shinobu: 'That I have come today as an envoy is for no other matter. "As you too know, Prince Sutaruman has come to go missing, and even as Daiō-denka is amidst illness, in the midst of anxiety, yet again Princess Banna-hime has been lost from sight since last night. The palace is in a tumult, turning high and low. As ill luck would have it, Sasumori no Tsukasa, owing to the disaster the other day, has struck his sternum and moans upon a sickbed, and has not yet attended the court. With no other recourse, I have dispatched police in all directions, and though we search through the night, as yet there is not a single clue. Though I hear that you, Umori, are likewise ailing, in today's case, endure your slight illness, rouse your courage, and attend the court."'—such is the imperial message. Umori-dono, what is your reply?'

Umori: 'Ah, fearfully and awesomely, the purport of the imperial envoy I have respectfully received. Straightway, purifying my person and making my preparations, I shall attend the court, and I humbly beg that you will be so good as to intercede for me favorably before the presence of Daiō-denka.'

Shinobu: 'Your prompt consent—even Daiō-denka will no doubt be well pleased with Umori's true loyalty. If so, I take my leave of you with this.'

As the words ended, he stood up abruptly and made as if to set out on his return journey. Umorisama bowed low, and while expressing his respect, saw the Chiyokushi off until he had left the Genkuwan.

Shinobu went out as far as the Omotemon, then returned once more and stood at the Genkuwanguchi.

Shinobu: 'Umorisama, are you at home? I am Shinobu, the Jiyochiugashira, and though I am a person of humble Mibun, I have a matter I wish to request of you. Therefore, I beg you, Genkuwanbansama, to use your Betsukaku discretion and convey my request for an audience with Umorisama.'

Sakurensu, who had been stationed in the room next to the Genkuwan until now, head tilted in deep Shian, heard this voice, slid open the partition screen with a swish, and appeared.

Umori: 'Ah, is that you, Shinobu-dono? You have come at a good time. Please, come inside. I have many things I wish to Saudan with you as well.'

Shinobu: 'Oh, my, Umorisama, to see your healthy face is a cause for great joy. I am truly sorry to have startled you so early in the morning, being the thoughtless person that I am.'

Umori: 'No, no, I am humbled by your greeting. Please, do not speak so formally, and come into the inner room. I have some private matters to Saudan.'

Shinobu: 'Yes, thank you. In that case, I shall take the liberty of entering the inner room.'

So saying, she followed Umorisama and took a seat in one of the rooms of the inner parlor. Umorisama, with a very haughty air, puffed on a cigarette and said,

Umori: 'Ha ha ha ha, Shinobu-dono, you must have been quite worried about the recent commotion.'

Shinobu: 'Yes, to say I was worried is not enough to describe it. As the Chiyokushi said just before, the palace is in a great uproar. And since even Arina-sama has gone missing, my anxiety is beyond measure.'

Umori: 'Ha ha ha ha, it seems that Arina-sama is the one you are most concerned about.'

Shinobu: 'Ho ho ho ho, that is certainly true, for he is the husband with whom I pledged my life. As his wife, how could I possibly remain calm? I ask for your understanding.'

Umori: 'Oh, I am humbled by this. There was no public announcement of a marriage, so when did you two enter into this secret vow?'

Shinobu: 'I ask for your discretion. Is it not a bit cruel to ask a woman of my age such prying questions? Ho ho ho ho.'

She blushed slightly and hid her face with her sleeve.

Umori: 'Even in the midst of such chaos, you have the composure to enjoy the flavor of romance; you are truly a great heroine. This Umorisama is astonished, no, I am filled with admiration. By the way, Shinobu-sama, according to what the Chiyokushi said earlier, Banna-hime-sama is also missing. With both the Crown Prince and the two of you absent, the castle has lost its pillars, and I cannot help but feel deep concern for the future of the royal house.'

Shinobu: 'On that point, I am of the same opinion as you. However, since the Crown Prince and the Princess both detested noble life so much, I suspect that they took advantage of the commotion to hide in some deep mountain and live a simple life.'

Umori: 'Ah, Shinobu-dono, you do not seem to be very concerned about such a crisis for the royal house. As a subject serving deep within the palace, is that not highly inappropriate?'

Shinobu: 'It may be inappropriate, but what can I do? Even if I say anything, the essential people are not here. Many police and spies are running about in all directions, searching with eyes like cormorants and hawks, yet they cannot be found. There is nothing more that human power can do. Everything is as the Kamisama wills it.'

Umori: 'Ah, I am quite astonished. However, Setsushiya has seen through the bottom of your heart, so will you not reveal everything here without concealment and lay it bare? As they say that birds of a feather flock together, if this Umori strikes his belly, it is the same thing for you too; I am a man whose inner heart is not white, ah-ha-ha-ha.'

Shinobu: 'Umorisama, the bottom of your heart is also well understood by me. You intend to elevate the younger brother Prince Yeru to the throne on this occasion, have him marry Banna, and you yourself intend to become a maternal relative, assume total control of state affairs, and accomplish your grand ambition, and thus you are plotting various schemes, are you not?'

Pointed at by the star, Umori wavered slightly, yet being the formidable rogue that he was, he purposely thrust forward an unconcerned face,

Umori: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha, Shinobusama, your clairvoyance is a failure. Why should I harbor such an ambition? Please think well upon Setsushiya's everyday actions.'

Shinobu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, Umorisama's transparent words, I have deduced them thusly based on your everyday actions. No matter how much you may reveal of your secrets, I shall never let them out to anyone, so please be at ease.'

Umori: 'Eh, as for Setsushiya's matters, I shall tell you later. If I have various blameless bellies searched into, this Umori cannot endure it, ha-ha-ha. Rather than that, Shinobusama, shall I pluck out the secrets of your heart?'

Shinobu gave a start before she knew it, yet being a rogue herself, she purposely feigned nonchalance, and while letting a smile linger on one cheek,

Shinobu: 'Come, please say whatever you like. My heart is utterly pure and clean, and I have not even a single point of ambition or desire.'

Umori: 'At your persistent and strong-willed method, even the formidable Umori has rolled his tongue in awe. You intend to place Sashizu's son, Arina-dono, upon the throne, while you yourself become queen and live amid dazzling glory, hailed as the famous flower of the Tarahan country, do you not?'

Shinobu: 'Umorisama, when I wonder what it is all about, you say such strange things that I have no memory of in my body—nay, things I did not even expect in my heart. I am not such a wicked person as to plot a conspiracy like that.'

Umori: 'Atsu-ha-ha-ha, if you have that much courage, you are a personage not unworthy to be the queen of a country. Furthermore, Sashizu's son Arina-dono is a rare man of talent in recent times. Generous and magnanimous, discerning in mercy, well-versed in human feelings, and on top of that, of a neat and beautiful appearance with the high renown of a handsome man, he is a person of talent whom we would not be ashamed to wear upon our heads as the sovereign of the country—Shinobusama, you have noticed a fine point. Ya, Umori too has been thoroughly impressed. As you know, in the state of affairs today which is almost equal to pitch darkness, if you were to take total control of the government with Arina-sama's courage and Shinobusama's daring, surely the nation would be governed safely and peacefully. In return, once the objectives of Arina-sama and you have been achieved, you will doubtless promote this Umori and use him in the role of Sashizu, the Inspector-General of State Affairs, will you not?'

Thus, he attempted to lure her skillfully and expose the frog's intestines. Wise though she seemed, being a woman after all, she answered as if highly delighted and said,

Shinobu: 'As expected of the wise Umori-dono, I am filled with reverence for your clairvoyant power. It is just as you have surmised. And Arina-sama has already finished his promise with the Crown Prince. Therefore, it is no wonder at all that Arina-sama should become the Crown Prince.'

Umori: 'Indeed, the more I hear, the more all matters and details show attentive care, a water-tight statecraft; and Umori is more than ever full of hopeful joy, unable to contain his delight. Regarding this, there is one grand obstacle here, and we must somehow eliminate it.'

Shinobu: 'What is this thing you call an obstacle?'

右『成程、承はれば承はる程、万事万端、注意が行届き、水も洩らさぬ御経綸、愈右守、末頼もしく欣喜に堪へませぬ。ついては、ここに一つの大妨害物が厶いますが、之を何とかして排除せねばなりますまい』

シ『妨害物と仰有るのは何物で厶いますか』

§ 4

Ugi: 'It is none other than this: to leave the Crown Prince as he is would be a source of future trouble. Even supposing that His Imperial Highness the Crown Prince should not harbour the intention of ascending the throne once again, since he is a personage of the golden branches and jade leaves, if some wicked and rebellious faction were yet to enthrone the Prince, raise aloft the banner of the truth of the unbroken imperial lineage, and come surging upon us, would not your hard-earned happiness turn into a mere dream? You surely know the whereabouts of the Prince; we must first take measures from this direction, must we not?'

Shinobu: 'Indeed, as you say, the obstacle to the future is the Prince. Fortunately, as I know his whereabouts, I have no objection to informing you if you so wish.'

Ugi: 'And does the Great King know of this too, I wonder?'

Shinobu: 'Oh no, how could he possibly know? I have heard the details from Lady Alina.'

Ugi: 'Indeed? Ah, that is a stroke of fortune. In that case, we must make the Prince a captive, arrange matters so that he may never rise into this world again, welcome Lady Alina back as early as possible to cause her to ascend the throne, hold anew the ceremony of the bridal torches, and have her bear the heavy burden of state affairs; therefore, pray inform me of the exact whereabouts of the two.'

Shinobu: 'Now, Lord Ugimori-sama, do not speak so loudly. The heavens have mouths and the walls have ears; I beg of you to lend me your ear.'

So saying, she whispered something into Ugimori's ear in a rustling manner. Ugimori, while secretly leaping for joy in his heart that his scheme was going according to plan, deliberately put on a serious countenance and spoke.

Ugi: 'Ah, I understand. Lady Shinobu, please be at ease. As for the Great King, I pray you handle matters appropriately. And as you know, since I have ailments in both eyes and feet, and moreover am afflicted by the cold currently in fashion, it will hardly be possible for me to attend court for the next two or three days—be sure to explain it to him nicely in that way. Above all, to dispose of the Prince and welcome Lady Alina is our single most pressing and urgent task.'

Shinobu, within her heart:

'How settled this is; the Office of Ugimori is also a relatively easy fellow to deal with. Blinded by greed, he has been played upon by my eloquence, blurted out his true feelings, and moreover allowed himself to be well and truly deceived for my sake.'

So smiling, she takes pride in having cleverly deceived the one who is actually being deceived. Truly, she is the possessor of a shallow wit. If Pangu, the Divine King, were to descend upon this place, he would surely pity her heart and at the same time cause her to smile.

Shinobu cheerfully bade farewell to Ugimori and, with nimble steps, went walking back toward the inner palace. Watching her off from behind, Ugimori burst out laughing,

'Hahaha, at last I have conquered the old tanuki of Shinobu. No matter how clever a woman may appear, a woman is a woman. Even a woman who serves as the principal of a peeresses' school and is called the number-one talented woman under heaven is bossed about by a strange ascetic like Ashino, conceives even the seed of passion, and exposes her folly to the world; therefore, no matter how great she is said to be, a woman is still a woman, hahaha! At last, blinded by the light of this Ugimori's wisdom, not even knowing that it will become a hardship for her dearest husband, she has blurted out her true feelings and gone back, hehehe.'

Thus he was alone, laughing in his sleeve. The one who pushed open the sliding screen softly and entered was Princess Sakuran.

'My lord, splendid, splendid! That is indeed my husband, the Lord Administrator Ugimori-sama. Or rather, the Minister of State in the near future. Truly, a treasure house of intellect is none other than my lord! Having listened to your present negotiation from behind the screen word for word, I have fallen utterly and hopelessly in love with my lord's extraordinary and unfathomable wisdom, hohoho.'

Ugimori assumed a fierce and majestic air,

'Ehehehe, my skill is, well, roughly like this. You had better wait and keep your eyes wide open for my future activities, hehehe.'

With his arms folded, he shook his body up and down, making even the floorboards creak and weep.
