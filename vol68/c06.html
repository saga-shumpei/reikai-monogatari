Chapter 6: 信夫恋 [1730]

信夫恋〔1730〕

§ 1

As the setting sun inclined toward the mountain edge, the bell of the distant temple tolled and echoed, warning of the fleeting state of this world of shiyogiyōmujiyō.

In the brilliance of the silver candles shining at every interval, the features of Hensautaishi appeared ever purer and more radiant, and his form, attired in brocade garments, seemed even to surpass Sudarman-taishi, imbued with majestic dignity.

Alina felt drawn to him by an unknown compulsion, and even found herself inclined to suppress her very sighs.

Thereupon, with the gentle sound of rustling silk, a woman bowed her head outside the bamboo blind, clapped her hands twice, and spoke.

Woman: 'Though it is with the utmost awe, I address you, Denka. Learning that you were to be visited this evening, your favored minister Alina-sama has returned home to his residence, leaving you, Denka, all alone with a somewhat lonely expression upon your countenance; thinking that I might become your companion for conversation, I have come secretly and without fear, acting upon my own womanly impulse.'

This interior maid was the daughter of a wealthy merchant of the TaraHan jiyōshi, and she was one who served as a maid within the palace as part of her training in etiquette. And her name was called Shinobu.

Alina, with a grave tone in his words:

Alina: 'Aiya, are you not Shinobu of the inner chambers? I have no need of a woman. You had best withdraw immediately.'

Shinobu: 'No, no, say whatever you may, taking advantage of Alina-sama's absence this evening, I wish to see you closely, Denka, and since I have matters to express, say what you will, I will not retreat a single step from here.'

Alina: 'This is audacious beyond measure! Do you not heed my words?'

Shinobu: 'Ho ho ho, how could I possibly heed Denka's words? What do you suppose was my purpose in coming to serve as a maid in this palace? It was because I wished to meet you, because I wished to behold your countenance.'

Alina: 'This is outrageous! In a place as sacred as the palace, such seductive words—you insolent woman, you depraved creature! Do you not know your station? Begone!'

Shinobu, laughing 'Ho ho ho ho,' boldly flicked the bamboo blind aside with a pop, advanced close to Alina's knees, gazed intently into Alina's face until it seemed her eyes would pierce through, and laughed with a smirk.

Shinobu: 'Otsu ho ho ho, how wondrously well it suits you, ah, Hensautaishi-sama. I truly thought you were the genuine person. You are more skillful than a fox's seven transformations or a badger's eight.'

Alina: 'Koriya Shinobu, do not mistake me. I am by no means an impostor. I am the genuine, authentic Sudarman-taishi. As a woman, do you feel no fear before the throne?'

Shinobu, casting a sidelong glance, drew herself firmly up against Alina's knees.

Shinobu: 'Moshi, Alina-sama, that will not do. Come, will you listen to what I have to say, or will you not? If you will not listen, I will expose everything before the Great King!'

Alina: 'Atsu ha ha, have my tail finally been caught, eh? There is no help for it. Though I am disguised so well, why did you realize that I am Hensautaishi? Koriya, one can never be carelessly off guard.'

Shinobu: 'Even if the Great King were to look upon you, or even if my late father were to look upon you, you appear as nothing other than the true Crown Prince, so no matter who it might be, not a soul would think you a false prince. However, the reason I came to serve in the palace is truly solely because I desire to draw close to you. Ever since I laid eyes upon you at the garden party on such-and-such a day at the residence of the Minister of the Right, my soul has been crushed and captured by that demon called love, and sleeping or waking I cannot forget your figure, so I importuned my parents with various unreasonable demands, bestowed abundant bribes upon the Minister of the Right, and through such means became an interior maid. Desiring to see you closely just once and express the full measure of my feelings, I watched for an opportunity morning and night, but you were always at the Crown Prince's side and never once were you alone; for about a year now I have had no occasion to speak with you, and as a result of worrying and suffering, my body has wasted away as you see. Today, following in your footsteps, when I hid in this room and observed the circumstances, I firmly believed that this was a private conversation with the Crown Prince, and that you were truly acting as the prince's substitute; when I peered closely through the blind, it was unmistakably none other than Alina-sama. Come, now that things have reached this pass, whether you are willing or not, you must let me fulfill my love. You seem to have gone to welcome a peerless beauty known as Princess Subaru, but no matter how great a beauty she may be, her body can scarcely be made of gold. I am confident that I am not a woman to be lightly cast aside either. Alina-sama, what do you say? I beg you for a prompt reply.'

Alina thought within his heart,

Alina: 'Ah, I have failed. Behold, a serious crisis has suddenly broken out. However, given Shinobu's inevitable and forceful demand, I cannot simply reject her outright. No, if I were to reject her, she might become a bitter enemy of love and a sworn foe of my life, pressing upon me until it ends in my own ruin. And yet, although this Shinobu may be slightly inferior in physical beauty compared to Princess Subaru, viewed either vertically or horizontally, she is a woman well above the average. Even if I take the next step and make this creature my beloved, it will hardly lower Alina's sense of honor.'

Determining his resolve in the flash of a moment, and speaking deliberately with gentle words,

Alina: 'Ah, Lady Shinobu, our relationship is one that must hide from human eyes, so please be mindful of your surroundings. This Alina is not made of wood or stone; ever since I caught a single glance of your figure, I have been seized by the hound of earthly passions, my mind-ape and intention-horse have run wild, and I could neither stop nor stay. Until this very day, in the abyss of enduring love, within the breast of Alina sunk in tears, how could I ever express my feelings to you? As one who is a novice when it comes to women, ever since the bud of love's heart sprouted, I felt too ashamed even to meet you, and even when it came to sending letters, I hesitated. Today, hearing your gentle heart for the first time, I too feel content.'

Shinobu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, that you too are content is quite a well-made story. Then you will surely grant my love, won't you?'

Alina: 'Lady Shinobu, you too will grant my love, won't you?'

Shinobu: 'Yes, how could I ever disobey the will of His Highness? Please love me eternally.'

Alina: 'However, Lady Shinobu, now that things have come to this, I cannot go on playing the substitute for the Crown Prince and acting like a stone guardian dog in such a cramped state. By now, the Crown Prince is likely exchanging sweet whispers with Princess Subaru. Ah, when I think of that, it truly has become utterly foolish. Rather than all this, why don't you and I take each other by the hands, live in a back-alley tenement of nine shaku and two ken, and you become my domestic wife while we lead a simple, commoner's life? If it is for your sake, I would be satisfied even as a beggar.'

Shinobu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, do not say things like Crown Prince Sutaruman. My, Lord Alina, I have a matter to discuss with you: didn't the Crown Prince say that he loves a commoner's life? Taking advantage of this, why won't you impersonate the Crown Prince to the end, become the ruler of the Talahan Kingdom, and choose me as your queen? Is there any joy greater than this?'

Alina: 'What an audacious thing to say! Even I was startled out of my wits.'

Shinobu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, how can you say such a thing. Weren't you just saying earlier, "Rather than all this, shall I disguise myself as the Crown Prince and carry out a conspiracy that would make even Ten'ichibō flee barefoot?" Didn't you mutter those very words to yourself? Hearing those words, I realized all the more that you are a truly great person, and my feeling of romantic adoration has grown all the stronger. Please open up from the bottom of your heart and tell me everything. Though I am a townsman's daughter, I am a great monster who dares to gaze upon the realm. I am Princess Shinobu, who graduated from a women's university and earned the praise of being a talented woman. Would my soul be captured by mere romance alone? I have constantly studied the character of seemingly clever men to see if there is no great person who would fundamentally rescue the present Talahan Kingdom, and I have realized that there is no suitable person other than you. At first, knowing you to be a great person, believing you to be a person of grand character who would accomplish momentous deeds in the future, and wishing to gain an opportunity to approach you, I used various stratagems even so far as to become a maid working here in the palace, and I watched over the actions of His Highness and yourself. But realizing your exceedingly noble intentions, a genuine romantic love for you has flared up, ho-ho-ho-ho. Please cherish me forever, and you will exert your utmost efforts for the national reconstruction together with me, won't you?'

A: 'Birds starting up from beneath one's feet—such is this very thing. I had thought that all the men and women serving in this palace were mere worm-eaten old-headed bits of junk, yet that a genius alive with new knowledge like you should be lurking here—even the likes of myself has not suspected until this very moment. Ah, how reliable, this is more than I could have wished. Then I shall go on posing as Prince Sudarman to the very end, while you for a little while shall become a chief lady-in-waiting and show your face from time to time. And so that you are not seen through, acting as a shadow and as the sun, you are to protect my personal safety.'

Shi: 'Your Highness's words are far beyond my deserts; I humbly accept this service. Pray do not worry in the least, absolutely not.'

A: 'Ah, well done, well done! Your very words—I deem them to satisfy me.'

With that, already in the frame of mind of having become a prince, he had completely changed even his manner of speech.

Shi: 'Moshi, Lord Prince, if it becomes too prolonged, there is a danger of being suspected, so this evening I shall withdraw with this. I beg for your utmost favor in all things.'

A: 'Ah, satisfactory indeed, satisfactory! It would be well for you to return to your room and sleep peacefully.'

Shi: 'If that is so, please slumber well too, Prince. Your handmaiden shall withdraw to the maids' quarters.'

With a soft, gliding step, leaving her heart behind, she smiled faintly and turned back toward her own room.

Alina watched Shinobu's retreating figure as she walked away,

A: 'Ah, what a fine style! Walking like that with her hem dragging, moving with a gentle sway—whether it is due to my partial eyes or not, she is superior even to Princess Subaru. As expected, I too am a handsome man. Tonight, while His Highness the Prince sleeps holding the breast of that youthful, light-snow-like Princess Subaru, I thought I would be left embracing only my own knee caps, unable to sleep properly for envy, and pining away until dawn—how strange a thing it is. As expected, where there is one thing, there are two. If the Prince is satisfied, I am satisfied too. Yet that Shinobu lacks cleverness. Fearing the eyes of others, she has gone and returned to the maids' quarters. It will not do for me to steal over to her side secretly either, for if I do, the authority of the Prince will become zero. If she loves me passionately to the core, she might not sleep a wink tonight. Surely drawn by that rogue called romance, she might creep over to my room in a slow, shuffling manner.

Quenching the flames of my burning breast, For a while I will conceal it, on this path of hidden love. If ours are a pair whose bond must hide from human eyes, For a while I will conceal it, on the dark path of love.

Ah, somehow I have come to feel in a strange mood. Well, the night seems to have grown late, and at this hour there is no need for a courtesy call. Come, today I shall take my ease and sleep this prince's sleep, too.'

Uttering thus, he entered the bedchamber, stretched himself out upon the sofa, and exhausted to the utmost, fell into a slumber with snores like thunder.

The night deepened in solitary gloom, and it came to be the hour of the ox when even the waters sleep. Bursting the banks of rain-clouds across the whole sky, a torrential rain began to beat loudly upon the rafters of the mansion. Caught by the rogue of romance, Shinobu the head maid, who had been unable to doze, taking the sound of the rain as good fortune and considering the breathing of the other maids, while hushing her footsteps, softly, softly crept into Alina's bedchamber. Alina was completely lost in the night boat of Shirakawa, fast asleep with snores roaring like rough waves. Shinobu drew near the sofa, placed her hand softly upon Alina's chest, and in a low voice...

Shi: 'Moshi... Moshi, Alina-sama, Alina-sama.'

She shook him awake. Alina, startled, sprang up with a gasp, rubbing his eyes,

A: 'Wa—what is it? What has happened?'

Just as he was about to dash off, Shinobu pulled back his sleeve and said,

Shi: 'First of all, pray compose yourself; I am by no means a suspicious person. I am Shinobu, whom you dislike. To hear my voice and try to flee in such a fluster—is that not going too far? Did you deceive me this evening? Ah, I am vexed, I am mortified. Now, above all else, I shall reveal everything, so prepare yourself for it.'

§ 4

And already it turns into a crying voice.

Alina is startled and says,

Alina: 'Ah, were you Shinobu? Ah, I am relieved by that. Far from ever disliking you, I was sleeping thinking only of you, when my father Samori came, revealed my true nature, and tried to bind me tightly—I saw that in a dream and was startled. How could I ever dislike you? And is nothing the matter in the palace?'

Hearing these words, Shinobu also appears somewhat reassured, and says,

Shinobu: 'Ah, hearing that, I understand your heart. Please be at ease. The palace is extremely peaceful and safe. Even when I entered the bedchamber, your figure flickered before my eyes, and I could not sleep a single wink; I could hardly wait for the break of day, and longing to see your face, I stole past human eyes and came here.'

Alina: 'Un, is that so? Because of that, I too am finally relieved. You have done well to come. I couldn't get a proper night's sleep either. You were on my mind...'

Shinobu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, my, what a convenient mouth you have! Not even knowing that I was stealing here, you were having a midnight dream—where does one press to be able to say such slick things? You truly are a hateful lord.'

So saying, she put strength into the area around his knee and pinched him with a stepmother's pinch.

Alina: 'Aita-ta-ta-ta, aren't you doing a terrible thing? That's no way to mistreat a man. As I thought, you are here to torment me. By making a person go through painful experiences, do you feel good?'

Shinobu: 'That is so indeed. Because you are hateful, you are so lovable... There is a popular song that goes, "Because I love you, I strike you not a bit; because I hate you, could I pinch you not a bit?" Shall I pinch you more and more?'

This time, she twisted his upper arm with all her might using a stepmother's pinch.

Alina: 'Aita-ta-ta-ta, hey, hey, don't do such a terrible thing. Being treated with affection is fine, but being in pain I decline.'

Shinobu: 'A weak-loined man who caves in from being pinched by a woman has no qualifications to speak of love. A man and woman whose true loves fit together perfectly—for them, the fact that fresh bruises never cease is proof of intimacy.'

So saying, she roughly scratched his cheek.

Alina: 'Chyo, it hurts! No matter how much you say you are in love, if you make me go through such toxic experiences, I can't stand it. Won't earthworm welts form on my face?'

Shinobu: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho, what is an earthworm welt? Men, don't they break through even precious treasures? You do not know how much more painful that is.'

Alina: 'Ew, my, what a good tomboy you are. Women nowadays, that is why they are disliked... No, they are liked, ehe-he-he.'

Just as they are dallying thus, the sound of an alarm bell ringing frantically rings out: jang-jang-jang-jang.

Starting with a shock and opening the window to look, heading in the direction of Samori's mansion, a massive fire is breaking out, scorching the burning sky.
