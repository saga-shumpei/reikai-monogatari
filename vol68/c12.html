Chapter 12: 妻狼の囁 [1736]

妻狼の囁〔1736〕

§ 1

Wakaba rustles in the early summer wind, as Yamahototogisu, lurking in the shade of dense trees, raises a mournful voice, lamenting the future of the nation of Tarahan created by Kami.

Plum and apricot fruits begin to ripen, and in the distant and near paddy fields, numerous Shiyuda plant the seedlings of Inochi; gazing at this scene, while each and every one wears a rain hat and straw cape against the falling Samidare, they form ranks in threes and fives, and their bravery in singing in the paddy fields is such that, with the turning of the good season of planting which occurs but once a year—three hundred and fifty days out of the twelvemonth—there are moments when human hearts are likewise filled to the utmost with Kibau.

A great fire breaks out in the city of Tarahan, and suddenly mobs rise in insurrection, setting fire to the grand mansions of the privileged class and the wealthy, while the outrageous Shiyuda gangs plunder women and violate money, and various ideologues rise up at once, spitting on their hands, declaring that now is the very time to vent their customary grievances, and fighting by turning the strictly guarded police force to their front; their momentum is like flames burning through a withered field.

Here the military is dispatched, and though the rioters are at last suppressed for a time, yet with the expectation that a great disturbance will surely break out again at some moment, the minds of the people in the castle town of Tarahan are filled with terror and void of even a moment of peace.

The Kamitsukasa of Samori and Umamori, deeming that there is a danger of their own authority swiftly declining, devise all sorts of means to summon the army and police with airtight vigilance, so that even the discontented crew hide their shadows for a time; King Karapin, suffering from a severe illness, has no means left to oversee affairs of state, while the Prince has slipped away and hidden his shadow amid the confusion, and Gandzī, the Tsukasa of Samori, though burning within his heart, loses his strength to the advancing years, his courage is suddenly dashed, and though he is derided as a mere useless encumbrance, he remains unaware of it.

He strikes his withered arms with a thud and roars valiantly; his wretched appearance is like a mantis standing and brandishing its axes, and oh, the ridiculousness of his style! Foul-hearted Sakurensu, even more than seeing this state of affairs, tilts his head together with Sakuranhime, the wife of Azuma, thinking that the future of the nation is like a lamp before the wind, and ponders strategies for before and after; how vexing indeed it is.

Sakurensu: 'Sakuranhime, as the world gradually becomes this disorderly, I too cannot afford to be careless. The world is completely transformed from what it has been until now, and the time seems to have approached for privileged classes and capitalist classes like us to perish. If we leave things as they are, I cannot sleep, worrying whether not only the nation of Tarahan, but the royal family and our class as well, might experience the misery of hell before long. How do you think today's social conditions will turn out?' Sakuran: 'As you have said, the world has gradually reached a dead end. Not to mention the economic, political, and religious worlds, even in the field of practical enterprise, everything has reached a dead end, resulting in truly miserable conditions. However, it is said that when things reach an extreme they find a way out, and when misfortune reaches its extreme, it is bound to sprout the buds of happiness. Even in the great fire of the fifth, the teahouse within the castle burned down, all the national treasures returned to ashes, and even the mansion of Samori came to such a miserable pass. Nevertheless, the mansion of Umamori was only partially destroyed by the rioters and remained safe just like this; this is considered to hint that Pankogami-sama has a great mission regarding the family of Umamori. In a society that has fallen into such a chaotic state, if one is seen as weak, one is instantly crushed and destroyed. Therefore, at this juncture, we must exert our utmost strength for the sake of the nation, carry out an unprecedented grand plan, seize the breath of the sovereign and subjects above and below, reveal the power of Umamori, show coercion and authority, and suppress the arrogant hearts of the people.' Sakure: 'What you say sounds plausible enough for the moment, but at an epoch when the hearts of the people are deteriorated to the extreme and the populace almost unanimously awaits the time to annihilate our class, to idly apply petty workmanship is rather to invite the anger of all people and hasten our destruction. Yet to break through this difficult pass, win over the popular mind, and restore the nation to peace is the most difficult of tasks. No matter what sage or philosopher, there can be no room to wield a scalpel against such social conditions at this time. Ah, what a troubled matter it is. His Highness the Great King is gravely ill, and in today's state where it is impossible to know when he will depart this life, the whereabouts of the Prince are unknown, the Tsukasa of Samori is unable to endure the heavy duties of old age, and his son Arina has also obscured his tracks, so that the nation of Tarahan is about to lose all its pillars. Like a fan with its pivot missing, the internal situation has become utterly irrecoverable. If a disturbance like the one on the fifth should break out again henceforth, that indeed will be the period of extinction for the royal family and the aristocratic class. Is there truly no way to somehow retrieve this declining momentum?'

Sakura: 'Is it really such a crime to carry out a great reform of the state, save the people from the bitterness of dire poverty, and establish a plan for a myriad of years for the nation? Are you not saying something that completely defies common sense? If you win, you are the imperial army; if you lose, you are a rebel. In this world, power ultimately claims the final victory. Sakura: 'With a household that is not even governed, and with the heart of a wife you have lived with for twenty years remaining uncomprehended, how can you manage to be a great politician? Much less, how can you hope to win the hearts of the multitude of citizens?'

Sakure: 'Now, do not corner me so. Look at the great politicians of today. Sakura: 'Merely being able to preserve one's position does not make one a great politician. Is it not because Sadaichi, the chief minister of assistance, lacks true skill that the nation has fallen into its present state of anxiety? Modern politicians all hide behind the sleeves of the imperial robes, barely maintaining their positions and intimidating the populace. They are mere foxes borrowing the tiger's might. You, too, must be just like that. If you were stripped bare and cast out into the bustling streets, you would not even be able to become a clog mender, would you?'

Sakure: 'Talk nonsense; even I can attain a high official rank.'

Sakura: 'Put aside your vanity to a reasonable degree. If you were a man stripped of all support from His Highness the King, standing bare as a lone individual and acting as a brave hero charting your own destiny, the absolute peak of your capability would at best be a shabby elementary school teacher or a common policeman. That is why the people call you a dead bureaucrat.'

Sakure: 'Hey, I do not want to hear such nagging anymore. Are you not making a fool of your master?'

Sakura: 'Ho ho ho, even if I wanted to make a fool of you, it is troublesome because you cannot truly become a fool. Those in the world who are called clever or wise are always the ones who make mistakes. In contrast to that, if one becomes a fool, one remains nonchalant about everything, encounters any difficult barrier without the slightest fear or regret, does not flutter or panic at things, and does not waste effort or suffer losses in vain. I would love for you to become that kind of fool who transforms misfortune into natural fortune.'

Sakure: 'Oh dear, I have come to lose track of what is what. Does that mean my training as a fool is still insufficient? Hey, I have an inexplicable fluttering in my chest. Pour me a cup; I am going to down it hot.'

Sakura: 'While it is all very well for you to partake in wine, at this crucial juncture, you would do well to restrain yourself for a short while. Since you have a wretched habit of suffering from mental derangement and babbling any secret before people whenever you partake in wine, please abstain from alcohol before the great Lord God Pangu until this grand ambition is fulfilled.'

Sakure: 'Oh, this I cannot bear. To be made to give up wine—which is more precious to me than rice, my wife, or the country—and on top of that, to have this old man perform the dangerous feat of riding on dark clouds, is that not rather too harsh?'

Sakura: 'It is but a short while of patience. The night has advanced into the deep hours. Come, let us sleep.'

Taking his hand, she leads him into the inner chamber. From this moment onward, through the dead of night, they continued the curtained dialogue of intimate pillow talk in minute detail. What secret designs were indeed plotted here?

サクレ『ヤ、頼母しい。お前は見かけによらぬ偉女夫だ。此夫にして此妻ありだ。俺も始めてお前の心の底が解り、安心をしたよ』

サクラ『二十年も夫婦となつてゐ乍ら、まだ妾の本心が解らなかつたのですか。お側に近く寝食を共にする妻の心が、二十年目に始めて解る様な事で、能くマア今日迄右守の職掌が勤まつて来たものですなア。本当に之こそ天下の奇蹟ですワ』

サクレ『オイ、サクラン姫、馬鹿にするない。政治家は政治家としての方法があるのだ。天下国家を憂慮する余り、小さい家庭などの事に気を付けてゐられうか』

サクラ『家庭も治まらず、二十年も添うた妻の心が解らぬやうな事で、何うして大政治家が勤まりませう。まして多数国民の心を収攬する事が出来ませうか』

サクレ『ヤ、さう追撃するものでない。今の大政治家を見よ。一家を治める事は知らいでも、堂々として政治の枢機に参与してゐるぢやないか。左守だつて、決して家庭は円満でない。又左守の心と彼が悴アリナの心とは正反対だ、犬と猿との間柄だ。それさへあるに国家の元老、最大権力者として左守は立派に今日迄地位を保つて来たではないか』

サクラ『自分の地位を保ち得たのみで大政治家とは云へませぬよ。今日の国家の不安状態に陥つたのは、輔弼の重臣たる左守様に本当の技倆が欠けてゐる為ではありませぬか。現代の政治家は何れも皆袞竜の袖に隠れて、僅かに其地位を保ち、国民を威圧して居るのです。虎の威を借る狐の輩です。貴方だつて、ヤツパリさうでしよう。真裸にして市井の巷へ放り出してみたならば、履物直しにもなれないぢやありませぬか』

サクレ『馬鹿云ふな、俺だつて曲人官位にはなれるよ』

サクラ『自惚も可い加減になさいませ。貴方は大王殿下のお引立てがなく裸一貫の男として、自分の運命を開拓遊ばす勇者とすれば、精々小学校のヘボ教員かポリス位が関の山で厶いませう。それだから国民が貴方を称して死人官だと云つてゐるのですよ』

サクレ『エー、モウそんな小言は聞たくない。主人を馬鹿にしてゐるぢやないか』

サクラ『ホヽヽヽ、馬鹿にしたくても、貴方は本当の馬鹿になれない方だから困りますワ。世の中の才子だとか智者だとか言はれる人は何時も失敗許りするものです。それに引替へ馬鹿とならば、何事にかけても無頓着で如何なる難関に出会つても、少しも恐れず又後悔する事もなく、物に慌てて事に驚き気をもんで、無駄骨折に損をせない。そして禍を化して自然に幸になす態の馬鹿になつて頂きたいものです』

サクレ『あゝあ、何が何だか訳が分らなくなつて来たワイ。さうすると俺もまだ馬鹿の修業が足らぬのかなア。オイ、何だか胸騒ぎがしてならない。一杯つけてくれ、熱燗でグツとやるから』

サクラ『お酒をおあがり遊ばすのも結構で厶いますが、大事の前の小時、茲少時はお窘みなさるが宜しからう。貴方はお酒をおあがり遊ばすと、精神錯乱して、どんな秘密でも人の前に喋り立てるといふ、つまらぬ癖がおありなさるから、此大望が成就する迄は盤古神王様の前にお酒を断つて下さい』

サクレ『ヤ、此奴ア耐らぬ。飯よりも女房よりも国家よりも大切な酒を断つて、おまけに暗雲飛乗りの危い芸当を此老人にやらさうとするのは、随分ひどいぢやないか』

サクラ『少時の御辛抱で厶います。夜も深更に及びました。サア寝みませう』

と手を取つて奥の一間に導き行く。二人は之より夜を徹して細々と寝物語の幕をつづけた。果して何の秘事が画策されたであらうか。
