Chapter 15: 破粋者 [1739]

破粋者〔1739〕

§ 1

At the southern foot of Mount Namiyama, in a corner of Akinogahara, an old, thatched water-mill hut stands.

There are no human dwellings in the vicinity, and as far as the eye can reach, on the three sides of east, south, and west, the daylilies of the wilderness stretch continuously toward the heavens.

In this water-mill hut, the frame of the water-wheel is damaged and the pestle is broken, rain leaks in in various places, and now all activity has completely ceased.

The two men, Kirk and Sāman, as if they were concealing something secret within this thatched hut, serving like Niō with frog-faces displayed as duty is paramount, are indulging in idle chatter.

Kirk: 'Hey, Sāman, didn't we break our backs quite a bit the other day? When we took ten subordinates each to that old temple and went to catch him, even I for a time threw up my hands, saying, "Surely this fellow is hopeless..." But they say that if you resolutely proceed, even fierce gods avoid it, and at last we secured him. If my courage had faltered midway at that time, we would have utterly failed to capture our prize, and the hundred-yen bounties would have come to nothing. Since you fellows too, thanks to me, have come into a large fortune of a hundred yen, you surely recognize my grace at least a little, don't you?'

Sāman: 'Hah, don't talk so grandly. Didn't you meet a single glance from the Taishi, start trembling violently, flatten yourself out flat on the ground, hold your breath, and not even manage to speak properly? At that time, didn't I say, "Hey, Kirk, pull yourself together," and kick your backside with my shoe, so that you finally managed to raise your hips? Suddenly, you had your testicles grabbed by a woman, turned pale while uttering a screech, clenched your teeth, showed almost only the whites of your eyes, and the sight when you passed out was truly pathetic, so you shouldn't talk with such grandiose airs.'

Kirk: 'Even so, wasn't it I who reported with firm conviction that the hiding place of the Taishi is this old temple? That is why, no matter what anyone says, I am the first spear of meritorious service and military prowess, and my honor is resplendent throughout the realm.'

Sāman: 'No matter how much you call yourself the discoverer, if my courage hadn't been there after all, you would have grown cold in that mountain interior and become mere fodder for the wolves. I am Sāman, the savior of your life. Hey, even if you were to hand me a bonus of about fifty ryō out of the hundred, it wouldn't be too much of a loss. Considering that your life was saved for fifty ryō, it's a cheap bargain. Hearing my name causes all sentient beings to attain Buddhahood, after all.'

Kirk: 'Hah, don't say greedy things. Hand over fifty ryō this way. The Grand General of Ukomori handed a hundred yen equally to everyone, everyone, so in terms of reward for distinguished service, there is extreme unfairness. I am already sick of receiving a meager monthly salary of only about ten yen in such a lonely place like this. Since I am resigning from tomorrow onwards, you had better keep watch by yourself. Since you call yourself a savior of your own accord, even if tigers come, even if wolves come, you will be all right, ufhufuhu. What an absurd savior has appeared, itsuhichichichi.'

Sāman: 'Hey, Kirk, don't make too much of a fool of me. Isn't there the matter called "Nōmaku Samanta" even in the phrasing of the sutras? If you just chant "Samanta", the three calamities and seven perils are instantly extinguished, and it is a splendid sutra through which you escape at once from the perils of jackals, wolves, venomous snakes, thieves, fire, water, and swords. Because I am Sāman who bears that name, I am none other than Sāman the savior of the world, etsuhehêhehe.'

Kirk: 'However, I wonder how the Taishi in the basement is doing. It would be a serious matter if he were to bite his tongue and die, though. "Do not kill him no matter what; give him no food or drink, and let him die of thirst," since such is the confidential imperial command of the Ukomori-tsukasa, if he should commit suicide, it would instantly become a matter concerning our own heads.'

Sāman: 'Do not have such worries. No matter what, an peerless beauty of all ages has entered the adjacent room, you know, and peering at each other's faces through that narrow window hole, continuing sweet whispers, they might be spending their days and nights peacefully, pleasantly, and calmly. It is quite different from the psychological state of lowly wretches like us, after all.'

No matter how much of a Taishi one is, just looking at a Bijin's face won't fill one's stomach. Doesn't the proverb say... an Ikusa cannot be fought on an empty stomach... or something like that? Pressed by Kikatsu, can one afford to think about such cheerful things as love or carp? You are a fellow with precious little Rikai, aren't you.

Sa: 'Come now, as the Zokueu goes, "Tokaku Ukiyo is color and sake," sake is more important than rice, and color is more important than sake. Even if there is a separation from that Irōnna, having her face-to-face every day and enjoying sweet talks, the Kiyōchiu of the Taishi must be in a Kokoromochi like a Danpū crossing a spring field. Doesn't they say that love is the Gensen of Seimei? If I were to be loved by such a Bijin, for Hiyakunichi or Sennichi, even without drinking a cup of water, or taking a bowl of food, it would be a Tokushin. No matter what is said, it is Tenka's Meiyo, you see.'

Ka: 'Ha ha ha ha, what a Hafuhazu fool you are. Won't a human die if they cut off food and drink... there is a Zokueu that says... flowers and fruit will not bloom after death... isn't there? In this world, life is capital. Only by taking food and drink and perfectly preserving life can one understand the taste of love. Becoming skin and bone and sinews like a bamboo hat, growing thin and decadent, with the base of the wall appearing like a sign of stomach medicine, and hands and feet standing out with sinews like a bamboo basket pasted with wet paper—would there be any love or palace left in such a style?'

Sa: 'It is neither love nor palace. It is Taiko-sama, the king of sea fish. That is why he causes the actions of Taishi Reijū. Politics doesn't matter in the least, parents don't matter in the least, if only he fulfills the desire of his own love, life is fine with that... saying such things, of all things, abandoning the terminal illness of his father who received nurturing grace equal to mountains and seas, and making up his mind to hole up in Zaitokuji hand in hand with the woman he likes—he is such a stylish person, you know.'

Ka: 'That is why the parent's divine punishment struck, and he was thrown into a place like this. In other words, since it is rust coming from one's own body, it can't be helped even if it's pitiful, right?... A disaster caused by Heaven can perhaps be avoided. A disaster caused by oneself cannot be avoided... there is a teaching like that. Isn't it fitting to that to a T? To be thrown into a cage like a tiger or a wild dog, given no food and drink, and suffering in agony—it is truly a thousand times pitiful. However, since this is also self-inflicted, there is nothing to be done, wah.'

You and I, getting our hands on a great sum of one hundred yen and being able to buy our wives even a fine piece of clothing, wasn't that thanks to Taishi Reijū doing such a thing? If you treat it too cheaply, it will bring bad luck. Hey you, how about somehow buying the stub of a roasted sweet potato and tossing it in secretly? Even if you have just that much human feeling, divine punishment won't strike you too much, you know.'

Ka: 'Don't talk nonsense, if we were to do a thing like that, it would be the ruin of ourselves. No matter what is said, in a world where self-love and love for society are respected, such benevolence of Song Xiang had better be stopped. It is extremely dangerous for the protection of oneself.'

Sa: 'Even so, you, in the one-in-ten-thousand chance that the Taishi emerges into the world again and becomes a king, what do you intend to do? Saying he wants to torture me, it wouldn't be helped even if your head were to be struck off. That being so, while there is still time, paying even a tiny bit of tears of sympathy and bestowing something like the stub of a roasted sweet potato—isn't that the wisest course of action from the standpoint of self-love?'

Ka: 'Henh, now that things have come to this, he is a caged bird. Even if heaven becomes earth, earth becomes heaven, and the sun rises from the west,............ is there any chance he will ever emerge into the world? At any rate, rolling with the long paper and bowing before the strong while wagging one's tail is the sole good method for self-preservation. I firmly intend to give nothing at all.'

サ『ハイ、情色は女房が内に大切に保護致して居ります。何程夫婦の間柄でも、斯う所を隔つて住まつて居りますれば、情色の楽しみも到底味う事は出来ませぬ』

太『テモ扨ても情ない野郎だなア。サ早く此場を立去れ』

サ『殿下さうポンポンいふものぢやありませぬよ。生殺与奪の権は、言はば間接に吾々が握つてる様なものですから、チツとは監視役の私に対しては、もうチツと許り丁寧に仰有つても、余り御損にもなりますまい。余りポンつき遊ばすと、貴方の御為になりませぬぞや』

§ 4

The low-born and base-born are acting with arrogance, as if they were tormenting a fierce tiger by locking it in a kengo cage and watching from the outside. Subaruhime, peeking through the window from the next room, said,

Subaruhime: 'That Taishisama, do not lower yourself to deal with such a beast. I find it regrettable.'

Taishisama: 'Naruhodo, you are right. From now on, I shall say nothing. Hey, you rascals, you are in the way. Go up quickly and stand guard at the suishiya-goya.'

Being shouted at with a daikatsu, even the formidable Sāman, while shrinking his neck, fled from the scene like a mouse. Meanwhile, Kāku was dozing off, nodding his head while resting his chin on his hand.

Sāman: 'Hey, hey, Kāku, do you not value your shiyokumu? How can you be dozing off in the middle of hakuchiu?'

Kāku: 'Ya, is that you, Sāman? I am not dozing off in the slightest. I was looking down in chinshimokukau, conducting research on philosophy.'

Sāman: 'Hen, you say such clever things. Were you not snoring?'

Kāku: 'That is to be expected. I was conducting practical research on what the principles of snoring are in philosophy. Can you, an uneducated and illiterate person, understand philosophical research? That is why they say you have no common sense.'

Sāman: 'Eh, I am criticized by the Taishi for having no sentiment, and now I am criticized by you for having no sentiment; truly, a man's face is completely ruined. However, would you not refrain from saying so much? Since I have a wife, do I not have sentiment? You are the one living as a widower, so you must have never tasted sentiment.'

Kāku: 'Ha ha ha ha, you are confusing sentiment with common sense. Hey, look here, this Kāku has been hailed as the world's greatest man-hell and a master of seduction. This is not a range that a blockhead like you can fathom. They say a cat that catches mice hides its claws. A man who looks like he has no women often has many. Since you do not know the circumstances of this matter, you are a person unworthy of speaking of love or sentiment.'

Sāman: 'Hen, you say that, do you? With hair growing thick and messy like a kite's nest, wearing clothes that look like a procession of seaweed, you are hardly in a position to spit out such things as being a master of seduction or a man-hell. You are still sleeping, after all. Hey, go and wash your hands in that small ditch over there.'

Kāku: 'That is where you people do not understand. ...The master of love appears haggard... as they say, a person like me is actually loved by women. You, who imitate women, attaching clips to your hair, applying lime powder, and smearing your wife's menstrual blood on your cheeks to pose as a handsome man—women lose interest in the likes of you and flee after spitting at you. A creature who is fired upon by cannons from the rear and iron guns from the elbows, standing there with a face like a pigeon that has been shot with a bean-pellet, sucking his fingers and looking resentfully at the back of a woman—that is the end of a weakling like you. Hen, how ridiculous.'

Sāman: 'Leave me alone. A respectable person with a wife and one without a wife can never get along. You called me uneducated and illiterate just now, but there is no one as uneducated and illiterate as you. They call an uneducated person someone who cannot read or write, do they not? Look at yourself. You probably do not even know a single verse, ichi-hi-hi-hi.'

Kāku: 'Hey, never mind that. How is the situation in the basement? Will you not report to the captain?'

Sāman: 'There is no material to report, is there?'

Kāku: 'Ha ha, you were scolded by the Taishi and came back after apologizing, did you not? I thought your behavior was suspicious.'

Sāman: 'Indeed, indeed, and yet it was I who scolded him. Even the formidable Taishi was crying with a loud voice. He is a great person, no matter what one says. After all, I am a hero like General Sojo, who can scold the Taishi of Tarahan Castle with a single word.'

Kāku: 'Hen, what is there to boast about in that? Once inside a kengo cage, whether it is the Taishi, a tiger, or a wolf, there is nothing they can do, is there? Scolding someone is as easy as passing gas for anyone.'

Sāman: 'What, logically speaking that is true, but try facing it in reality. There is a dignity about him, and when you go before him, your body trembles, your eyes go wild, your tongue sticks to the roof of your mouth and hardens, your heart pounds, your legs go weak, and it is not easy to find the courage to scold him. If it were me, I was able to scold him even for a moment.'

Kāku: 'Atsu-ha-ha-ha, you were scolded in return, were you not?'

As they spoke, carried by the west wind blowing, the faint sound of a propaganda song could be heard.
