Chapter 20: 破滅 [1744]

破滅〔1744〕

§ 1

As for the ways of accumulating wealth, ambition for fame, and political desire, alongside self-love, the thoroughly untroubled Umori no Tsukasa Sakurensu considered that the time for the fulfillment of his daily desires had arrived, and together with his wife, Sakuranhime, without even attending to the aftermath of the great turmoil in the capital, letting the voices of popular lamentation and resentment blow past like the wind over an empty sky, he exchanged cups of liquor amidst delicacies and choice dishes, became triumphant, and let fly the dust and garbage of his heart with unconcerned composure. While it is common for petty minds to be carried away when triumphant, he was a man woefully lacking in wisdom. In these times when public sentiment was filled with intense agitation and extreme turbulence, moreover, appearing at the entranceway and dealing with visiting guests, he raised his meter high with the triumphant air of one who had already slipped into the role of Minister of State, becoming thoroughly heated up.

Umori, exhausted from his wife's pouring of drinks, with sunken and dim eyes, gazed from behind his spectacles at his wife's lizard-like face, exposing an unfathomably perplexing countenance resembling an imperfectly fired Imado-ware badger doll, clucking his tongue the while,

Umori: 'Oi, Madam—no, Wife, old woman, just how watertight and carefully thought out are my maneuvers, eh?'

Saku: 'Master-sama, what is the meaning of this? Calling me an old woman, a hag, or an old crone just like a rickshaw puller or a stablehand—is this not utterly disgraceful right here at the entranceway? If the guards were to hear such a thing, they would surely make a fool of you. When addressing me henceforth, please call me Okusan or Koshitsu, I beg of you.'

Umo: 'Ya, this is extreme discourtesy, I bow in awe before the Demon Mother Deity of Oreadani, Kama no Shita no Moeguizaemon, Enma Daiō no Suke, Hagzaemon-no-Jō Hikiusu-dono, Sakurensu's drunken wild words—please completely and utterly forgive me! Somehow, because my honorable wife's name is Sakuranhime, this Umori's mind has also gone somewhat deranged [sakuran], and somehow I seem to have grown dim. Sakuran... no, I wish you would brew even safranine and have me drink a dose. If there is no saffron, even Korean ginseng will do nicely. However, as the saying goes... buying ginseng and hanging oneself... such things also happen. In Umori's impoverished household, such expensive medicines are hardly fitting to my station. Rather than that, to gaze upon my honorable wife's beautiful face and bow in extreme gratitude and awe—who knows how much more delightful that is? Atsuha ha ha ha!'

Saku: 'Master-sama, you should mock your wife to a reasonable degree. Even if you say there are no barriers on your mouth, is this not a bit too much? At times, Master-sama, I wonder if Crown Prince-sama and Subaruhime are safe.'

Sakure: 'Un un, safe safe, fuyōjin fuyōjin [no problem, no problem]. If we leave them like that, they will naturally die of starvation, and if they do, that is our good fortune. No matter how good one's endurance may be, if eating and drinking are cut off for ten or twenty days, their lives simply cannot be sustained; to go along attaining the bliss of nirvana is an absolute and settled principle of the universe. Eh, if I had a child, I would marry her to Bannahime and skillfully manipulate national politics entirely at will, but the pity of it is that since you are a barren woman, thinking it is still better than giving her to a stranger, I yielded a position of supreme importance to my younger brother by a different father, and my younger brother will presently become Great King-sama, while I, his vital elder brother, become a subject—how utterly stupid, having to serve him with respectful devotion. However, my younger brother is merely set up as a signpost. Since I will keep the actual power firmly in the grasp of this Sakurensu, if I just think of it as a play, I can endure it, eh? Etsu he he he! Yet still, what a delightful thing it is!'

Sakura: 'Master-sama, and was that Arina in the temple of Mt. Ōmiyayama just as Shinobu said?'

Sakure: 'Na, that wretch finally took fright and fled, and reports came in that he has assumed the guise of a Buddhist monk, blowing a conch shell and roaming all about wearing a deep straw hat, so I am currently searching for him with a thousand-yen bounty attached. Once I catch that wretch, I have a plan fully arranged to throw him into the subterranean prison of the watermill hut in Akinogahara without hearing a word of protest, and let him wither and die where no one knows. Matters regarding such a fellow are not worth taking so much to heart. No matter what anyone says, once we leave the vital Crown Prince like that and do so-and-so to him, it is already my realm. Etsu he he he! What a wondrous and marvelous scheme it is!'

Sakura: 'That is truly a pleasant matter. As expected of Umorisama, nay, Daimiyōsōkan-sama, Atsupare, Atsupare. If you attain Goshiyutsuse, just as mugwort trailing on hemp, my Chii will naturally rise as well. However, if Shinobu, the Jiyochiugashira, were to hear this, she would surely be disappointed and discouraged.'

Sakure: 'In any case, that woman is a lewd Riyōbiyōbu of the Dotenba, so it serves her right. She is a Kusemono who always hangs around near Daiōsama and prides herself only on being a Mimikagi; I do not know how much it would benefit the Kokuka if such a person were to bite her own navel and die, heh heh heh heh.'

Sakura: 'Nevertheless, Dannasama, they say that schemes require secrecy, so you must exercise caution upon caution. If things go wrong, that woman might be a Bakuretsudan for the Umorike, so please handle her well and keep her under your control.'

Sakure: 'Eh, do you think Sakurens is so lacking in Nukeme to be told such things? That is just Yabo. Come, let us have a drink. Since the Dote is cut, drink to your heart's content today. It is a pre-celebration for the achievement of our goal.'

At such a moment, from outside the entrance shoji, came the voice of a woman in a rush,

Shinobu: 'Umorisama, it is I, Shinobu. Would it be an inconvenience if I were to enter?'

Umori was startled and his complexion changed in a flash; while looking at the faces of his wife, the tanuki, and the lizard, he bit his tongue with his lips, twitched his chin three or four times, and the two spoke at once,

Umori and Wife: 'Yes, it is no inconvenience. Come, please enter.'

Shinobu: 'If that is the case, I shall take the liberty. They say that the hated child thrives in the world, and though I should have bitten my navel and died, I am as lively as ever. Please be at ease, for I am by no means a Bakuretsudan. No matter what you say, your skill in placing the Taishi-sama into the rock cave at the bottom of the watermill to starve him to death was truly impressive. Tell me, you husband and wife, is it not unnecessary to turn pale and tremble so? You have even captured Arina-sama and thrown her into the dungeon beneath the earth, intending to starve her to death; I am truly astonished. How is it? Is there any prospect that your nocturnal scheme of the owl will be successfully accomplished?'

Umori: 'This is, indeed, an unexpected remark from you, Shinobu-dono. How could I ever do such a heartless thing? As a human being, to starve the Taishi-sama to death is something that one who wears a human face would not do. In truth, I was merely teasing Kakaemon while intoxicated with sake. It is merely the idle talk of a rootless plant, so please do not trouble yourself over it.'

Shinobu: 'Since you are an Umorisama who can calmly commit such outrageous and fearful acts that no human could do, a tomboy like me, who is merely a Mimikagi, cannot even approach your side. I would very much like to receive even the dirt from under your fingernails, decoct it, and drink it.'

Umori: 'This is outrageous. If you suspect me so, I, Umori, must explain every single circumstance. Now, please calm yourself and listen to the words of your humble servant, a loyal subject.'

Shinobu: 'Have you already forgotten? The other day, when I came disguised as your messenger, did you not promise to do away with the Taishi-sama? And you cleverly deceived me, saying you would raise Arina-sama to the throne and make me the queen. Your true intention was to raise Eru-sama to the throne, make the princess Banna-sama the queen, and manipulate the national government as you pleased, was it not? I heard everything, every detail, just now at the entrance. However, do not be surprised that I say this. It is a matter of consultation, but how about this? Why not make me the empress instead? If you say otherwise, I shall spread the details of your conspiracy to everyone, from the Daiōsama above to the general public below; that would be an inconvenience for you, would it not? If you say you cannot do that, then come, declare it clearly right here. They say even a tiny insect has a soul of five bu, so I have my own ideas as well.'

While the husband and wife felt a bitter pain, as if their chests were being massaged one by one with an awl at Shinobu's words,

Umori: 'Well, I am truly humbled. Since I will revere you as my sovereign lord from this very day and tomorrow without fail, and shall call you the Empress, please do not get so angry and calm down.'

Sakura: 'Just as my husband has stated, I too respect you as the Empress Dowager, and from this very day and tomorrow, I shall serve you with the rites of a vassal.'

Shinobu: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, how well you speak. Saying such things to make me at ease, you must be planning to even play a trick of a dark assassination. Judging from the way you two have done things until today, that much is just the beginning of the evening for you.'

As it came to such a pass, the two, Kirk and Thurman, hurried back and said,

Kirk & Thurman: 'We speak to Umori-sama. Ata, ata, a terrible thing has suddenly broken out.'

While Umori was startled twice at these words, his drunkenness suddenly wore off, and straightening one knee, he said,

Umori: 'What, a terrible thing has happened, and where is it? Come, will you not speak quickly?'

Kirk: 'Yes, it is with the intention of speaking that both of us came running back hastily in a hurry. How could we not speak? The Crown Prince and above all Subaru-hime, helped by the propagandists of Izunome, straddled horses and have grandly decided to return home inside the castle. And the Lord of Yell, in the god's forest of the rock mountain, by the giant woman Balance, had his neck pinched and was thrown into the Indes River, his head smashed against the rocks in the middle of the river, dyeing the river water crimson, and he floated away going burble-burble. This is no time to be dilly-dallying. Umori-sama, your neck is in danger.'

Sakurens: 'Is it not a lie, there is no reason such a thing could be.'

Kirk: 'Never, never, who would ever tell a lie? It is a report of the genuine, unvarnished truth.'

Sakura: 'That is why I did not tell you always to be careful, did I not give you my opinion? Altogether, your brain is far too crude, and that is why you can make such a blunder. Ah, how vexing, what shall I do?'

Shinobu: 'Ite-hi, hi, hi, hi, Umori-sama, once it has come to this, my being Empress and your Inspector-General of Daimyos are both utterly hopeless. Be prepared like a man. Nay, commit suicide. In truth, Princess Banna the princess too, I cleverly cajoled and lured her out of the castle, strangled her by the side of the Indes River, and threw her into the middle of the river, so together with Yell-sama she is probably exchanging a cup of water in the same Indes River. Now that things have come to this, I am sorry, but your family is extinct, your crime is light so commit seppuku, or if not, it will be reverse crucifixion. Even for me, I can no longer remain unconcerned. Come, Umori-sama, since I will assist you with your throat-cutting, cut your belly. And as for your wife, either hang herself or throw her body into the reservoir, and quickly clear away that excrement-maker. If you dilly-dally, you will expose your shame even until after death. I too will accompany you to the netherworld. I have already prepared the kaiken. The moment I cut my windpipe with this sharp dagger, it is the setup of moving to a splendid, splendid heaven.'

While they were thus consulting with each other about the end of their lives, the front of the gate suddenly became turbulent, and the Chief Inspector, following hundreds of subordinates, surrounded Umori's mansion ten and twentyfold, and the chief himself, together with several inspectors, entered inside and shouted,

Chief Inspector: 'Sakurens, Sakuran, Shinobu, all three of you are wanted. Peaceably put your hands behind your backs.'

Shouting thus, he drew out a capture rope from his bosom and carelessly, severely, and tightly bound up all three of them.

シ『イツヒヽヽヽヽ右守さま、もう斯うなりや妾の女帝も、貴方の大名総監もサツパリ駄目です。男らしく覚悟なさいませ。否自決遊ばせ。実の所は王女のバンナ姫様も妾が、うまくちよろまかして、城内からおびき出し、インデス川の辺で首を締め、川中へ投げ込んでおきましたから、エールさまと一緒に同じインデス川で水盃でもしてゐらつしやるでせうよ。もう斯うなつた以上は気の毒乍ら、貴方のお家は断絶、罪が軽うて切腹、まさか違へば逆磔刑ですよ。妾だつて、最早安閑としては居られませぬ。サア右守さま、介錯をして上げますから腹をお切りなさいませ。そして奥様は首でも吊るか、溜池にでも身を投げて、早くその製糞器を片付けなさいませ。グヅグヅしてゐると死後迄も恥をさらされますよ。私も冥土のお伴を致します。已に既に懐剣は用意して参りました。この鋭利な短刀で喉笛を切るが最後、結構な結構な天国へ国替と云ふ段取ですわ』

かく互に身の終りの相談をやつてゐる所へ門前俄に騒がしく、目付頭は数百の部下を従へ、右守の館を十重二十重に取巻き、頭役自ら数名の目付と共に入り来り、

『サクレンス、サクラン、シノブ三人とも御用だ。神妙に手を廻せ』

と呶鳴り乍ら懐より捕縄を出し無雑作に三人を厳しく固く縛り上げてしまつた。
