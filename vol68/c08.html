Chapter 8: 帰 Ogre 逸迫 [1732]

帰鬼逸迫〔1732〕

§ 1

The great fire of the city of Tarahan burned down the greater part of the city, and at last leaped to the interior of the castle, reducing a single block of chiyareu to ashes. The inside and outside of the castle turned into a hell of abikyōkuwan, where the futeishiyudadan and shiyugishiyadan united and cooperated to carry out evil deeds such as gautau, gaukan, and satsujin, performing an unbearable sanjiyō. The entire shōbautai, as well as the metsuke samurai, were kurida, and at last succeeded in putting out the fire and stopping the riotous conduct of the bauto. Samori had his teitaku burned down, and with karagara managed his buka to strive for the pacification of the disturbance; but since the commotion had finally subsided, he haughtily sought audience in the living quarters of the Great King. When he looked, the Great King, lying in bed due to old age and illness, heard of the great emergency in the castle town, and out of excessive astonishment developed a high fever, finally falling into unconsciousness. Because it was such a time of confusion, the doctors did not rush as expected, and the senior vassals were at their wits' end, gathered in the Great King's sickroom to devise measures before and after. Thinking that by now this was the time to have an audience with the Crown Prince and look for orders, Samori shined his bald head and made his presentation to the Crown Prince.

Samori clapped twice as usual, bowed low before the hanging bamboo blinds, and in a voice carrying a slight tremor,

Samori: 'I address Your Imperial Highness. Today, through the carelessness of this humble subject, a great fire broke out in the castle town; the futeishiyudadan, shiyugishiyadan, and other rioters exercised their violent might and set fires, reducing the majority of the capital to ashes. Not yet satisfied, they exercised all manner of violence such as gautau, gaukan, and satsujin, and the city of Tarahan presents a pitiful appearance like the buzzing of mosquitoes. The Great King also, out of excessive worry, suddenly worsened in illness, and a tragic event has welled forth where no one can tell when He might pass away. That we have invited such a sanjiyō is entirely the sin of us minor subjects for failing to fulfill our duty of assistance; on behalf of all the people of the realm, I prostrate myself before the steps to apologize for my crime, and as of today I beg for my skeleton. By all means, I hope that Your Highness will employ new figures awakened to the times and resolutely carry out a grand reform of affairs of state. Upon resigning from my post, the matter I wish to request of Your Imperial Highness is concerning the situation of my son. Since this humble subject is also advanced in age, and even when entering the palace must carry a cane like this useless wreck, I do not know when I shall change my country, following after the footsteps of the Great King. I earnestly beg you to kindly look after the situation of my son.'

Alina spoke in an intentionally solemn voice,

Alina: 'Ah, Lord Samori, it must have been a terrible affair. The people must be in great distress indeed. At this time of national crisis, for you to beg for your skeleton is far beyond the bounds of being unmindful of your duty. Was it not the intention of the Great King, your father, that we bestowed a high stipend upon you in ordinary times so that you might exert yourself on occasions such as this? However, to force ability where there is none is not the way of a ruler. Fortunately, since you have a wise son who is awakened to the times and well understands Our will, he will surely think of Alina as you and employ him heavily. Do not worry at all. And was your household safe?'

Samori: 'Ah, thank you for kindly inquiring. When shall I ever forget your words of benevolence and mercy? My teitaku was surrounded by the futeishiyudadan and burned down first of all. However, by the divine protection of the God of Ural, my life was saved. More fearful than that is the block of chiyareu, which gathered rare vessels of all ages and which the Great King always loved to play with—it was regrettably burned and lost. The treasures of successive generations of the Great King's family were stored in this chiyareu. Truly, for this single matter alone, this humble subject must bear responsibility and beg for his skeleton. I humbly beg for your pardon.'

Alina: 'Ah, Samori, what you say sounds reasonable to a certain extent. You shall withdraw from here, and after consultation with the other senior vassals, establish a Fukkōin and strive to return once more to the original city of Tarahan. Crown Prince, You shall release Samori from his official duties on behalf of Your father. Become the President of the Fukkōin as a project among vassals.'

タラハン市の大火災は市の過半を焼き払ひ、遂には城内迄飛火して茶寮一棟を烏有に帰した。城の内外は阿鼻叫喚の地獄と化し、不逞首陀団や主義者団が一致協力して、強盗、強姦、殺人等の悪業を逞しふし目も当てられぬ惨状を演じた。消防隊全部、並に目付侍迄も繰出して、漸くに火を消し止め暴徒の乱業を喰ひ留むる事を得た。左守は吾邸宅を焼かれ、命辛々部下を指揮して騒擾鎮撫に努めて居たが、やうやく騒動が治まつたので蒼皇として大王の居間に伺候し見れば、大王は老病にて臥床中城下の大変を耳にし、驚きの余り発熱甚しく遂に人事不省に陥つて了つた。かかる混雑の際とて、医者も思ふやうに駆けつけず、重臣は困り切つて大王が病室に首を鳩め前後策を講じて居る。左守は最早此上は太子の君に拝謁して指揮を仰がむものと、禿頭をテカテカ照らし乍ら、太子殿に奉伺したのである。

左守は例の如く二拍手しながら、垂簾の前に低頭平身し、稍慄ひを帯たる声にて、

『太子殿下に申上げます。本日は微臣の不注意より城下に大火災起り、不逞首陀団や主義者団其他の暴徒、暴威を逞ふし火を放つて都の大半を烏有に帰し、尚飽き足らず、強盗、強姦、殺人など、所在暴逆を逞ふし、タラハン市は蚊の鳴くが如き憐れな有様で厶います。大王様も御心配のあまり俄に病気新まり、いつ御昇天遊ばすやも計られない悲惨事が湧出致しました。かかる惨状を招来致しましたのも、全く小臣等が輔弼の任を全ふせざりし罪で厶いますれば、天下万民に代り闕下に伏して罪を謝し、今日限り骸骨を乞ひ奉りますれば、何卒、時代に目醒めたる新人物をば登庸遊ばされ、国事の大改革を断行されむ事を希望いたします。左守が職を辞するに当りまして、太子殿下にお願ひ致して置きたい事は、悴の身の上で厶います。微臣も老齢加はり、殿中に入内致しますにも、かくの如く杖を持たねばならぬやうな廃物で厶いますから、大王殿下の後を追うて何時国替をするやらも分りませぬ。何卒悴の身の上をよろしくお願ひ申上げます』

アリナはわざと荘重な声にて、

『ヤ左守殿、大変な事であつたのう。嘸人民が困つて居るであらう。汝は国家危急の此場合に当つて、骸骨を乞ふなどとは不心得千万にも程がある。日頃高禄を与へておいたのは斯様の際に尽させむ為めの父大王の思召ではないか。併し乍ら、不能をもつて能を強ふるは君たるものの道ではない。汝は幸ひに時代に目醒め余が意思をよく悟りをる賢明なる悴あれば、彼アリナを汝と思ひ重く用ふるであらう。必ず心配いたすな。さうして汝の家は無難であつたかのう』

左『ハイ御親切によくお尋ね下さいます。仁慈のお言葉、何時の世にかは忘却致しませうや。吾邸宅は不逞首陀団の為に包囲され、第一着に焼きつくされて了ひました。併し乍ら、ウラルの神様の御加護によりて生命は助けて頂きました。それよりも恐れ多いは、大王様がいつも愛玩してお出になりました、古今の珍器を集めた茶寮の一棟、惜くも焼き失せました。大王家歴代の重宝は此茶寮に納めてありました。実に此一事にても微臣は責任を帯びて骸骨を乞はねばなりませぬ。何卒、おゆるしを願ひ奉ります』

ア『や、左守其方の申す言葉も一応道理があるやうだ。汝は是より此処を引取り、他の重臣共と相談の上復興院を創立して、再び元のタラハン市に復帰すべく勉めて呉れ。太子、汝左守の職掌を父に代つて免除する。臣間の事業として復興院の総裁となれ』

Sashimori: 'Though your gracious gotaimei is indeed arigata and I can hardly express my kanshiya, how could a hageatama left behind by the times possibly govern the jinshin of today's world and achieve the mokuteki of fukukō? I beg of you to excuse me from this matter. The truth of the matter is that while resting quietly in my betsusau at Tamanohara, upon hearing of the fire I fell rolling down the ishidan and sustained a taihen fushiyō. Since this will serve as a pretext, bishin as well will no doubt have to kiiu before long.'

Arina: 'Hey, that is truly an unexpected and regrettable thing to have happened. If Arina were here, I would have him attend to your kaihau, but as the kuwasai broke out he rushed out of the dennai and there is still no shōsoku of him whatsoever, so there is nothing that can be done. I too must remain taizenjijiyaku on such an occasion, guard against keikiyomaudō, and in the worst manichi succeed to the position of chichiōdenka. Please convey this properly from yourself to Umori and all the rest.'

Sashimori: 'Yes, again and again thank you very much for your kind words. And I understand that my son Arina has still not returned, but could it be that in the confusion of that commotion he has fallen into the hands of others? Or else, could he have been surrounded by the fire and burned to death?'

Saying this, his voice becomes tearful.

Arina: 'Father, no, no, if it is the great illness of chichiōdenka, then I shall observe my restraint here. Arina is also pitiful, but being the sort of fellow he is, there is hardly any likelihood of him throwing away his life so easily. You may rest assured.'

Sashimori: 'Yes, thank you very much. I ask this rudely, but the tone of Denka's voice lately seems somewhat different; could it be that you have caught a cold? In your venerable august person, I beg you to take utmost care of yourself. After all, hygiene is the number one priority for human beings.'

At these words Arina looks startled, yet remains thoroughly shameless and feigns ignorance.

Arina: 'No, it is nothing like illness at all. The truth is that since this is the season of youth, my voice has broken. And because I was also a little worried about the great fire of last night, my voice has probably changed a little. Please do not worry in the least. Your son Arina will also surely be safe.'

Sashimori: 'Yes, thank you very much. Please do pay attention to hygiene. I earnestly beseech you.'

Arina: 'Old man, no, Sashimori, do not worry. I will not let human life be intimidated by the two characters for hygiene and make a semi-invalid of myself. Illness does not arise for a human being simply from how one holds one's mind. You too should keep a firm spirit and live to a ripe old age.'

Sashimori: 'Since you are busy with this and that, and it will not do to intrude for much longer, bishin shall withdraw. I earnestly pray that you will take good care of your health at this time.'

So saying, while bowing respectfully and showing reverence, he goes down leaning on his staff. Sashimori thinks to himself along the way,

'Somehow Denka's changed voice—there must be some cause for this. Without any reason it lacks the solemnity it had up until now, and today his eloquence is like a rushing river; well, that is something I cannot quite comprehend. That tone of voice is the exact spitting image of his son Arina. However, because Arina always plays crafty tricks, even his manner of speaking must have infected Denka. How awe-inspiring it is!'

So he mutters to himself as he goes back home.

Arina, letting out a sigh of relief,

'Ah, that was dangerous. Once again I was visited by the old man and my heart turned completely upside down. Fortunately I deceived the old man, but before long Umori will come along. This is indeed a troublesome business,'

Just as he is pondering with his arms folded, the one who swiftly lifts the bamboo blind and enters is Shinobu, who had finished concluding their nocturnal secret pledge the night before.

Shinobu: 'Denka, do not worry. If it is in that tone, it will be quite all right. Even his actual father could not peel off the mask of illusion and left completely convinced that you are Crown Prince Sudarman, so someone like Umori is nothing at all. And since Umori is a famously near-sighted person, please do not worry.'

Alina: 'Ah, if I were to think who it is, you are Shinobu, the chief maid, are you not? Today of all days, no cheerful matters can be spoken of. It would be well for you to retire to the living room and attend to the reception of visitors, or the like.'

Shinobu: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, Denka, how feignedly such things are spoken of by you. This maid shall not leave your side for anywhere. As for your deportment and actions, they are being investigated one by one from the next room, you see.'

Alina: 'What a formidable cordon of vigilance has been cast, indeed; it is altogether like being under surveillance. Ah, how cramped the role of Crown Prince is, too!'

Shinobu: 'If you intend to become the monarch of a single country, a degree of confinement such as this must be endured, I suppose. Since there will likely be an extraordinary number of visitors these next two or three days, please remain firmly composed.'

Alina: 'Ah, why on earth does Crown Prince Sutaruman not return? He said, "Since half a day will suffice, I wish you to substitute for me," but if Umori-san or the senior vassals come bustling in to such a place as this, in the end the cloven hoof of falsehood will be revealed, will it not!'

Shinobu: 'With a disturbance of this magnitude, however carefree you may consider Prince Taishi to be, there is no reason he should be idling away his senses over Princess Subaru. Since he will probably be returning soon, please bear with it a little longer. It is the Sekigahara of the world's destiny, the watershed of whether one becomes a monarch or is reduced to a commoner.'

Alina: 'That is so, too. Since one cannot tell who may come, it would be well for you to withdraw quickly beyond the bamboo blind. I am consumed with anxiety.'

Shinobu: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, "I am consumed with anxiety," and the like—at last your manner of speech has truly become just like that of the genuine Crown Prince. If that is so, this nuisance shall take her leave.'

So saying, she stood up abruptly, haughtily kicked the tatami mats twice or thrice, and concealed her figure within a single room. Coming up in hurried substitution for her was Umori. Umori performed the customary two claps, and while bowing his head to the floor,

Umori: 'Awe-struck though I am, as the Director of Umori, I address myself to Crown Prince Taishi. Since the state of utter chaos in the castle-town since last night will have been reported to you by the Director of Samori, I shall not report it over again. Even upon Your Highness, I gaze upon your robust and healthy countenance, and for Umori's own part, I feel a supreme degree of joyful gratitude. In connection with this, His Great King's condition has suddenly taken a fresh turn, and from the breath of the underworld, he says, "Call the Prince." I humbly beg that you will make your august approach to the Great King's living room as quickly as even a single moment allows.'

Alina, evading one point and then another,

Alina: 'Ah, how trying it is to be a false crown Prince, too. By the side of Great King Denka there will no doubt be many nursing attendants, and senior vassals as well. If I were to go to such a place, my secret would instantly be exposed, and I might very well be bound hand and foot.'

Thus feeling an extraordinary shock in his heart, but being a brazen fellow, he purposely put on an unconcerned face and said,

Alina: 'What do you say—that my father the King is in critical condition? If that is so, I must hasten to visit him without delay; since I am henceforth going to change my garments, offer worship to the Gods, and pray for the recovery of my father the King, and immediately set out to visit him, convey that circumstance to my father.'

Umori: 'Though they are the words of Your Highness, the changing of your brocade garments is well and good, and prayers to the Gods are also well and good, but since it is already the time of his passing, please come at once. I shall accompany you. Make haste to have your parent-and-child reunion. For even if you utter lamentations to whatever degree afterwards, it will be a matter that cannot be undone.'

Alina: 'I shall go directly. Now, make haste and go to my father's side without concerning yourself with me. I cannot be at ease unless I pray to the Gods, come what may. Will you not quickly depart from this place and go to father the King's side?'

Thus he shouted with gathered force in his voice. Umori, silenced by that single command like a crane's cry, has no choice but to stand up and return. Afterwards, Alina said,

Alina: 'Ah, a troublesome situation has come to pass. It would be better to remain Alina, the son of Samori after all. Ah, how shall I push my way through this difficult pass?'

『アヽ偽太子もつらいものだ。大王殿下の傍には沢山の看病人も居るだらう、重臣共も居るだらう。そんな所へ往かうものなら忽ち秘密が露見して、フン縛られて了ふかも知れない』

と心に非常な驚きを感じたが、横着者の事とてわざと素知らぬ顔をして、

『何と申す、父王殿下が御危篤と云ふのか。それでは早速参上致さねばなるまい、余は是より衣服を着替へ神様に拝礼致し父王殿下の平癒を祈り直ちに参上いたすによつて其由を父王に伝へて呉れ』

右守『殿下のお言葉で厶いますが、錦衣のお着替へも結構、神様へのお祈りも結構で厶いますが、最早御臨終で厶いますから、直ちにお越し下さいませ。私がお供を致します。早く親子の御対面を遊ばしませ。後で如何程お悔やみ遊ばしても返らぬ事で厶いますから』

アリナ『余は直に参る。サ早く其方は余に構はず父のお側に行つて呉れ。余はどうしても神に祈らねば気が済まぬ。早くこの場を立ちのき父王の傍に行かぬか』

と声に力を籠めて呶鳴りつけたり。右守は鶴の一声に止むなく立つて帰り行く。後にアリナは、

『アヽ困つた事が出来たものだ。やつぱり左守の悴のアリナで居る方がよい。アヽどうしてこの難関を切り抜けようか』

§ 4

Thus she sits with drooping head. Thereupon Shinobu, the head maid, comes running up,

Alina-sama, the Denka has returned. Come, come, quickly change your garments.

What, the Denka has returned? That is indeed ketsukō. Ah, it is as though a rescue boat has returned. Where is he?

Shinobu: 'He is standing at the back door still wearing his raudōfuku.'

Alina hurries out to the back door,

Ah, Denka, you have returned safely. Just as my true nature is about to be revealed, Chichiōdenka is now at the point of gorinjiu. Come quickly and meet him. Then I will cast off these kini and return to my original self as Alina. This is the very shiyōnenba of kikiitsupatsu; come quickly and change into the kini. We never know when the jiushin might come.'

Hearing of his father's gorinjiu, the Taishi forgot to change his clothes, and Alina, in her raubai, also forgot to have the Taishi put on the kini. The Taishi rushed in as he was and sat down before the hibachi.

Taishi: 'Ah, this is taihen. I am still in my raudōfuku. I must somehow quickly change into the kini. Oi, Alina, bring those kini here quickly!'

Though he calls and shouts, Alina, in her raubai, has thrown the kini away into the maid's room, taken out whatever nemaki were at hand from a trunk to change into, and is hiding inside the benjiyo, trembling. Meanwhile, the Taishi is anxious, wondering what to do. Just then, the Uganotsukasa arrives in a flurry and, squeezing out weeping voices from beyond the bamboo blind,

Denka, please come out quickly. It is the gorinjiu.'

Hearing of his father's gorinjiu at this voice, the Taishi forgets everything else and, in his soiled raudōfuku, follows behind Uganotsukasa and rushes to the Daiō's sickbed. As Uganotsukasa is nearsighted and in such a great flurry, he fails to notice the Taishi's raudōfuku.

太子は父の臨終と聞き着物を着替へる事を忘れ、又アリナも狼狽の余り、太子に錦衣を着せる事を忘れて了つた。太子はそのまま駆けつけ火鉢の前に坐つて見た。

太『ヤ、これや大変だ。労働服の儘だ。何とかして早く錦衣と着かへねばなるまい。オイ、アリナその錦衣を早く持つて来い』

と呼べど叫べどアリナは狼狽の余り錦衣を女中部屋に投げ捨て、トランクの中より有合せの寝衣を取り出して着替へ、便所の中に潜んで慄つて居た。一方太子は如何はせむと焦慮して居る。其所へ慌ただしく右守の司がやつて来て、簾の外より泣声を絞り乍ら、

『殿下早くお出下さいませ。御臨終で厶います』

この声に太子は父の臨終と聞いて何も彼も打ち忘れ、汚い労働服の儘、右守の後に跟いて大王の病床に駆けつけたり。右守は近眼の事なり余り慌てて居るので、太子の労働服が目につかざりけり。
