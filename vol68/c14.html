Chapter 14: 会者 Clean 離 [1738]

会者浄離〔1738〕

§ 1

To young men and women burning with the blood of youth, renai is truly the gensen of seimei. When renainetsu reaches a kauchō, it casts off the bonds of rinri and dautoku, discards richi, and casts aside fūki and meiyo. Advancing still further, it forgets parents and kiyōdai, and comes to forget hōiu and chiki. Nevertheless, viewed from renai itself, it cannot enter within the scope of richi and dautoku. It everywhere bears kuwakudaisei and is equipped with riutsūsei. If it is renai flavored with richi, the seimei of renai itself has already perished. Verily, Prince Sudarman of the Tarahan country, though born of a sonki house and in a station that should ascend to the kiugo throne, pours his whole heart and blood into an otome brought up deep in the mountains who has welcomed her jifugo spring, and attempting to taste love to the fullesttwelfth, forgets the crisis of the state, abandons the fact that his father is falling into a moribund state, slips away to a ruined temple that has stood from of old in the valley of Mount Ōhara, and secretly tastes love.arina, the son of Samori, as a supreme criminal who disordered the conduct of the prince, was a person in the shade upon whom an arrest warrant had been issued, and indeed unable to dwell even in the bustling capital avenues, lowered his body into the guise of a bhikkhu, lying low in the mountain forests by day, and threading his way along field paths step by step by night, wandering about with not a single resting place for his own body in the wide world, yet having stopped by to spend a night's lodging in the aged, ruined temple in the valley of Mount Ōhara, when he looked and listened, behind the shumidan in the main hall came the whispering voices of a young man and woman; Arina, with his wounded foot, paused for a moment, held his breath, and considered the situation within. Incidentally, when that Arina escaped from the inner palace, he whispered to dearest Shinobu and said,

Asama shall from here for a short time lurk within the shrine of thebanko shinnō of Mount Ōmiya, and waiting for the world to somewhat settle, I shall return, so do thou anywhere depart from this inner palace.

Thus he had told her. Shinobu therefore believed that Arina still remained within the shrine of Mount Ōmiya. That which he whispered into the ears of Samori was also precisely this.

Subaru: 'Prince-sama, you will surely never abandon me and will love me, won't you?'

Taishi: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha. Do not worry about such a thing. Is it not in order to accomplish renai with you that I have discarded even the rank of prince and hidden myself in such a place? My father-sama is gravely ill, and when he might ascend to the heavens no one knows; even in this case, it cannot be exchanged for love, and people who do not know the refinement of the world must surely be laughing at me as "a hallowed thing of unfilial, a fool, a spineless man." No matter how much I am laughed at, it cannot be exchanged for your love.'

Subaru: 'If the Prince has that intent, then I shall dedicate my chastity to you anywhere. Even at the far end of the fields or in the depths of deep mountains, the dwelling place of fierce beasts and poisonous snakes, if I endure hardship together with the Prince, I shall not be reluctant in the least. Since I am an otome accustomed to life deep in the mountains, I shall gather nuts and dig up potatoes, and I will surely support the Prince. Please be completely at ease.'

The Prince, touched by the gentle words of Subaru, unconsciously shed tears.

Subaru: 'Oh, is the Prince weeping? Did the words I spoke offend your ears? If they offended you, please forgive me somehow.'

Taishi: 'No, no, far from offending my ears, your heart was so joyful that tears of gratitude burst forth before I knew it.'

Subaru: 'Oh, do not utter such unworthy things. If it is for the sake of the Prince, I would be content even to dedicate my life. Speaking of that, I wonder how my father-sama, whom I left behind in the valley of Mount Asakura, is doing. Hearing the turmoil of the capital, he must surely be worrying what has become of his own child, wondering. Somehow I would like once to meet my father again and show him that the two of us are safe.'

Taishi: 'It is only natural that you should think so. I am exactly the same. Especially to leave a sick father behind in that chaotic and dangerous state, and to hide here with you—how agonizing my heart is. Subaru, please surmise my heart as well.'

§ 2

Suwaru: 'Parents are precious, and love too is yet more precious; this world is indeed a place that does not go as one desires.'

Taishi: 'Though mountains should cleave and seas run dry, yet shall my heart that yearns for your body never be scattered.'

Suwaru: 'How grateful is the proclamation of Prince Taishi, as though it pierced my very breast.'

Taishi: 'Though the person of my lord and father is not one unthought of, yet do I live drawn by the bonds of love.'

Suwaru: 'Love's garment, though there be an age when it is torn, how could I ever forget your sincere heart?'

Taishi: 'Be it so, be it so; though my body rot upon the wild moor, yet will I shine forth upon the dark path of love.'

Suwaru: 'Moistened by the dew of my lord's affection, the early plum blossoms have begun to unfold.'

Taishi: 'Plucking the blossoms of the white plum that bloom in the fields, today I place them in a vase deep in the mountains to gaze upon them.'

Suwaru: 'The flowers plucked and placed in a vase—when is the age that they will not fade? To have such an example is a sorrow.'

Taishi: 'Uprooting the fragrant plum of the deep mountains, I would plant them along the capital's great avenue to see.'

Suwaru: 'Along the capital's road where dust rises, the plum blossoms too must have an example where their fragrance is put to shame. I would wish to meet a spring where they are ever planted deep in these mountains and bear fruit.'

Taishi: 'Now for the first time do I know the bitterness of what is called love, separated between joy and sorrow.'

Suwaru: 'Lamenting yet rejoicing, rejoicing yet sorrowing, floating and sinking in the rapids of love.'

Taishi: 'If there were no tears in what is called love, how lonely it would be, like a withered tree.'

Suwaru: 'The heart after having met and gazed is yet more than before, an attire of love indeed.'

Taishi: 'The people of the world, whatever they may say, let them say; the path-grass of love is wondrous beyond measure.'

Suwaru: 'The end of the bodies of the two who walk through the darkness of love must be a dwelling in the heavenly August Land.'

Taishi: 'I am one who advances upon the broad path of love, sporting manifestly in the heavenly August Land.'

Alina, coming from without:

Alina: 'The Prince whom I yearn for is surely in this temple, I have come seeking him. You are a Prince who dwells deep in the mountains, leaving the hundred tumults of the capital's roads as a thing apart. Do you dwell together with the Princess of Suwaru? A gentle voice enters my ears.'

Taishi: 'Your voice, which had been lingering as in a dream or trance, entering my ears is indeed a joy. My heart within my breast is joyously filled, and there is no way to stop how my breast beats high.'

Taishi, together with Suwaru, emerges from behind the sacred altar and stands upright. Seeing Alina in the guise of a bikku, he forgets himself, rushes up to him, grasps his hand firmly, and letting tears stream down his cheeks,

Taishi: 'Ah, Alina, you have done well to seek me out and come. I longed to meet you, I longed to see you.'

Alina: 'Ah, Your Highness, how well you have remained safe. Seeing your secure and peaceful appearance now, I have no longer any lingering attachments left in this world. As you see, I have become an ascetic practitioner, cutting myself off from everything in the world, lying down on the wild mountains and sleeping on the moors, with the thought of spending my whole life in the profound contemplation of the Nembutsu. Being a wandering monk with a single seden hat, a single straw raincoat, and a single staff, whose roadside tree manure I shall become, or whether I shall be able to see Your Highness's robust and healthy face once more—since this is a tasteless floating world, I resign myself to everything being a matter of karmic ties, and I beg you to grant me my leave. And I earnestly desire that Your Highness may taste of love enduringly together with Princess Suwaru.'

Taishi, letting out a sigh of relief, with a pale face and in a listless manner, shaking Alina's hand,

Taishi: 'Hey, Alina, can you not think it over once more and return to the capital? I am already resolved to cast aside the throne and lead a beggar's life together with my wife, but you are a promising young man who understands the times; from now on to become an ascetic and rot away is a regrettable thing for the sake of the country. Will you not please help your father and become once more the pillar and foundation of the Talahan Castle, and inherit my will?'

Alina, letting his voice grow moist, with tears dropping from both eyes,

Alina: 'I truly appreciate Your Highness's gracious intentions. However, today I am already a person bearing the status of a criminal. Even though I should receive a grand amnesty and safely return to the soil of the capital once more, since I am the son of Lord Sasumori who has lost the hearts of the people, how could the citizenry ever acknowledge my sincere heart? I have already cast away political ambition. And I have severed my carnal desires as well. Thinking to spend my whole life making the wandering clouds and waters my companions and inspecting the sentiments of the people throughout the realm, I earnestly beg you not to speak of this matter alone.'

§ 3

Yotsugi sighed a deep sigh, and

'Ah, it cannot be helped. Has the nation of Tarahan already perished, I wonder?'

Subaru stepped out before Alina, and drowning in tears of grief,

'Alina-sama, how wretched indeed is the appearance you have assumed! It is so pitiable.'

Alina: 'Nay, this is—this is it, Hime-sama. Do not worry yourselves by any means. It is from my own heart that I have thus fallen. My present situation is ultimately a happiness for my person. A single bamboo basket of food, a single gourd of drink, to wander the realm making the mountains and waters one's friends—this too is a rare diversion. Pray, do not concern yourselves regarding my person.'

Subaru: 'Meeting again with a dear friend I awaited in vain, Will you sow the seeds of sorrow once more? How shall I deal with the impulsive man's eager mind? I am one whose power to stop him is lacking.'

Alina: 'In a world where even all things are guarded by Kami, Could the thoughts of men ever prevail? Gazing out at the sky of passing clouds from time to time, I have come to muse upon the end of my own path.'

Yotsugi: 'Though left and right we part our sleeves from each other, Our souls surely remain united together.'

Alina: 'Rather than bowing in gratitude and reverence, There is no other art for me today, alas. The precious heart of my lord, as always, Soaks deep into my body, and tears overflow.'

Yotsugi: 'With you and Subaru Hime left behind in the world, There is none other I can rely upon as strength. Feeling as though one of my arms has been torn away, The pain in my chest as I now part and go.'

Subaru: 'How am I to turn aside The heart of Alina no kimi in this ruthless world?'

Alina: 'Himegimi, compose your mind and take peace, If for you there is the protection of Kami. Both you and my lord and my own person are children of Kami; Could Kami ever bring themselves to cast us aside? Come now, away, to the prince and himegimi Let us part with our reluctant sleeves and go. May you remain peaceful and robust for all time, While drenched in the dew of Kami's grace.'

Leaving such parting songs behind, Alina once more blew her conch shell, purifying the evil qi of the mountains and fields, and without any certain destination, advanced into the depths of the mountains. Seeing her off from behind, Yotsugi and Subaru tumbled prostrate upon the earth,

'Oi, oi, Alina, Alina, show me your face once more!'

though they called and shouted, blocked by the echo of the conch's sound, as if her ears could not catch the voices of the two, without even looking back, she plunged her figure swiftly into the shade of the dense trees. Alina sang along the way,

'Clouds and mists choke the four directions, Into a perpetual dark Where neither black nor white can be told, The world of beasts has truly turned. Though the morning sun rises in the sky, There is no way it can illuminate the nether world; Though the moon hides deep within the earth, There is no art to bring day to the world. In the skies of Kumoi, day by day, Strange stars appear and vanish; Spreading the air of demons and fiends Upon the above of the earth, It blows down rain of fire, Rain of swords. The echo of the wind somehow Sounds like the voice of ruin. Advancing through the fields Where tigers, wolves, lions, and bears howl fiercely, My person is protected by Kami, Seeking ever the bedchamber of eternity As a wandering shugenja. If Kami dwells in this world, Once and for all, He should let it shine Upon the world wrapped in utter gloom. However great one's authority may be, Being but common folk and not Kami, How could this world ever be governed? Though men proudly boast That they are children of Kami, in reality They are but dew-drops before the morning sun Lodging on summer grass blades. How could they ever truly serve In the eternal and infinite work of Kami? Ah, Kaminagara, Kami, Kami, Reign in spiritual bliss and abundance! Protect the land of Tarahan Created by Kami in the places I go after, Safely and peacefully indeed, and aiding The myriad affairs of state of the Great King, Forgive the deep sins Of my father whose heart is foul!
