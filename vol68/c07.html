Chapter 7: 茶 Fire 酌 [1731]

茶火酌〔1731〕

§ 1

Tarchin, a master of the tea ceremony living on the edge of the forest of Mukahi, was in high spirits at the unexpected Gorairin of a god of fortune, turning his Chiyashitsu into a place of free pleasure for Taishi and Subaru, and while he claimed to be teaching the tea ceremony to Hime Subaru, that was merely an Omotemuki; in reality, he had been asked by Arina to facilitate the love between the two, receiving a Takusan amount of Kokorozuke, and was singing of the Taihei of the Tenka in high spirits. While sitting cross-legged in a room of an old Omoya separated by a Nakaniha, he was drinking sake with his wife Fukuro, who looked like an Ōhoteizen, and clicking his tongue in satisfaction.

Tarchin: 'Oi, Fukuro, is it not a Fushigi thing, the fortune of Ningen? We have inherited our family name as masters of the tea ceremony since the days of our parents, becoming favorites of various Taike Kanatakonata, but as the world fell into recession, the gifts from the Taike at the Bon and New Year festivals gradually decreased, my head became a bald mountain, frost settled on my beard, my pockets grew cold, the wind of poverty blew through my wallet, and I could not even afford to buy charcoal for the hearth; yet, ever since that Benzaiten appeared from the deep mountains, the divine miracles have been manifest, and it is such great good fortune that even the honorable Taishi Sudarman has come to visit in secret. It seems the god of destiny has not yet abandoned us. Tell me, Fukuro, you have always scolded and mocked me, calling me a good-for-nothing, and have nagged me to let you go, but how about it, shall I grant you your leave?'

Fukuro: 'Hey, hey, what are you saying? Just because money happened to come in, you should not get so carried away. You act all important, calling yourself a master of the tea ceremony, but from the eyes of your wife, you are not such a respectable Ningen-sama. You are just a dull-witted monk and a wandering star who treats the floating world with mockery, dividing it into thirds and quarters, so do not say such clever things. Even Taishi is here in secret; who knows when his retainers might appear and call him back to the castle? And if those high-ranking retainers try to prevent Taishi from going out, will you not be unable to suck the sweet nectar again?'

Tarchin: 'Eh, do not worry. Even if Taishi were to be hindered by his retainers and could not come out, Arina-sama, the son of Samori-dono, is waiting. Since Arina-sama is free to move about, he will arrange any convenience for us.'

Fukuro: 'You cannot be so optimistic. If Arina-sama were also forbidden from going out, you would have nowhere to turn, would you not? Furthermore, if you were charged with a heavy crime for keeping a beauty from the deep mountains and letting her have secret meetings with Taishi, causing them to fall into depravity, would that not cost you your head? You are not made to be clever, so my worries as a wife are not trivial. Please be careful; when money comes in, if you spend it so recklessly, what will you do in an emergency? You are a withered pumpkin, so it does not matter when you change your country, but what do you intend to do with a young wife like me? Please limit yourself to two bottles of sake today. When you get drunk, you go on a drinking spree, jumping out of the house and rambling into the waiting rooms in the back streets, wasting money that does not even exist; I cannot manage the household accounts that way.'

Tarchin: 'Eh, the sake does not taste good when you talk of logic. Let me drink in a good mood today. I am a tea master, yet I am somewhat annoyed by being told such hopeless household stories. Do not worry as if you are rooting yourself into a stone. My house, thanks to the blessings of my ancestors, is now heading toward a mood of Ichiyōraifuku. Think about it carefully. That Hime Subaru whom we captured from the deep mountains is Benzaiten-sama. And Taishi is Bishamonten-sama. You, needless to say, are Hotei-osho, and since I have a long head, I am Fukurokuju. And here, the gold notes with Ebisu and Daikoku are tucked into my pocket just like this; the six gods of fortune are perfectly assembled. Since one more will make it the seven gods of fortune, do not worry. Because this is a land where the Kototama brings happiness, at times like this, it is best to celebrate by saying auspicious things. With the six gods of fortune assembled, and your name being Fukuro, it is perfectly complete as the seven gods of fortune. It is auspicious, it is auspicious; one must not fail to eat and drink. One must not fail to eat rice, etsu-he-he-he!'

Fukuro: 'How on earth can you talk so easily at your age? Has it not already been three years since I became your second wife? You have never bought me even a single change of clothes, nor have you ever bought me a single pair of tabi socks, yet you always wear a master's face and act so grand—what is the meaning of this? Hand that money over here; I will keep it for you. It is dangerous to let you hold money. You are the kind of hopeless wretch who instantly turns all mushy the moment you see a woman with a peeled-chestnut skin, forgetting both your house and your wife. Truly, you are an unbearable kettle-old-man.'

Tarutin: 'Hey, hey, what are you saying? Even if I am poor, I am a grand tea-ceremony master serving in the Taruhan Castle. If you intended to become my wife, you should have at least mastered various arts such as the tea ceremony, flower arrangement, poetry, and haikai, and you ought to have kept your diction refined as well! With you acting like a giant, letting out a voice like a frog, and going honk-honk like a cracked temple bell, it is a disgrace to a noble house, ahee! Since there is a saying like "such a husband, such a wife," I cannot have you around unless you behave somewhat like a wife and restrain your conduct! Always, despite being a woman, you sit cross-legged by the hearth, puffing away endlessly at tobacco, and when you cook rice you let it burn; and if by chance it does not burn, you feed me half-cooked, raw-hearted rice—have you ever fed me a proper meal even once? Ah, I took on a wife like this with no other choice, but now that my pocket has grown this warm, more...'

Fukuro grabbed him fiercely by the collar,

Fukuro: 'What, kettle-old-man, are you about to let me hear the rest and drive me out? Rather than being driven out by you, I shall drive myself out! I thought of bolting down time and time again up to now because there was no prospect for us, but what comes first is money. This kettle-fellow, even he is bound to make his pocket bulge out sometime. At that very time, my plan was to snatch away all the money from his pocket and vanish in a puff of smoke. To stay with someone who performs such a dangerous stunt of leaping onto dark clouds—my life as Fukuro is in danger!'

So saying, she yanked away the bundle of bills from his breast, and using all her brute strength to kick the old man's rear end two or three times while thrusting up her chin,

Fukuro: 'Thanks to your grace, I have come by a thousand ryo of money. Thank you for your care for so long. Taruchin-sama, one thousand yen for three years is quite a bargain, is it not? Do your best and make a profit. When you have saved up again, I will come out to receive it once more. Farewell.'

She hiked up her cow-like backside with a jerk,

Fukuro: 'Kettle-old-man, eat my kick for all I care!'

So shouting, she fled at full tilt.

Taruchin gnashed his teeth in bitter vexation and tried to chase after her, but because he had been kicked with all her might on his rear end by the great woman's immense strength, he felt a sharp pain in his thigh bone, and wincing, he watched regretfully as his fleeing wife went away.

"Ah, that wretch Fukuro is making a fool of me! She managed to cleanly pocket the thousand ryo she had neatly scammed, and on top of that, she went off spewing harsh venom at me. Ah, I am back to square one again. I am a penniless beggar. It truly seems I am a man with no affinity for money. However, I too must ponder upon something. If by any one-in-a-million chance word should reach the ears of the high lords that I am harboring the Crown Prince and letting them have secret trysts, not only will I be banned from entering the castle, but my very hat-platform might go flying off, just as Fukuro said. Moreover, even if I were lucky enough to save my life, if it came to such a pass that the Crown Prince stopped visiting and even Alina-sama ceased to come, what on earth is this tea-priest to do? I have grown terribly worried. If this recent precious guest, whom I treasure more than any heirloom treasure passed down in my family—this indestructible wish-granting jewel—were to be discovered by the senior retainers and taken back from my residence, that would truly be the ruin of me. A tea ladle breaking right in the middle, swinging back and forth between hell and paradise. The water temperature up until today must now suddenly be plunged into the chaos of the bottom dropping out of the foot-cauldron. Ah, is there no good plan whatsoever? No matter how much I wring out of my dried-up brain, no good wisdom comes forth, and how on earth shall I make preparations for the worst?"

Folding his arms, sitting in the agura posture, and leaving the kandokuri sake flask rolling before him, he was lost in shian.

After a short while, Tarutin smiled broadly,

Tarutin: 'Well, as expected of a master of chiyanoyu. A fine chie has come to my mind. Even if by manmanichi misfortune, Taishi-sama should fail to frequent the palace, it matters not. Surely there is no way he would boldly drag that Princess Subaru back into the inner palace. If that happens, this very Tarutin will undoubtedly have to hide her somewhere. Taishi is bound to wish me to do just that. No matter how much I think, there are no hauhafu or shiyudan other than that. Since both Taishi-sama and Alina-sama are carrying this on secretly behind the scenes, they have a yowami. Capitalizing smoothly on that weakness and disposing of that meigiyoku is the ultimate shiyoseijiyō trump card. Even as a suchōri, that jewel has a kachi of twenty or thirty thousand. Rather than living in constant anxiety on a paltry pocket-money stipend of a thousand or two thousand sen, if it comes to this, I will sell it off for twenty or thirty thousand yen, flee to the nation of Tolman, acquire a splendid niyōbau, and live out this life in glorious luxury and ease—that is by far the best course. What marvellous good fortune has come my way! Hehehehe!'

He was lost in his own laughter. Just then, the sound of an alarm bell tolling wildly could be heard; as Tarutin dragged his feet, opened the window, and looked outside, a fire had broken out in the direction of the Tarahan castle town, and tongues of flame were licking high into the broad sky.

Tarutin: 'My word, this is serious! If regular patrons were to encounter a fire, it would deal a heavy blow to our pockets. And as a matter of daily deiri courtesy, I must go to offer condolences for the fire. At any rate, judging by that force, the fire does not look as though it will subside easily. I am sorry for Taishi-sama, but I think I shall ask them to look after the place while I go off to pay my fire-condolences.'

So saying, leaning on a thick cane and making the foot that had been kicked by the giant woman's bag twinge and ache, he entered the tea room of the detached quarters,

Tarutin: 'I say, you two honoured guests, a terrible fire has broken out in the Tarahan castle town. Since this place is a good distance from the town, flying sparks will rarely reach us, so you may rest easy, but I shall just step out for a moment to visit a regular patron to offer my condolences; therefore, please do keep watch over the place while gazing at the fire.'

Taishi: 'Indeed, it looks like a terrible, massive fire. At this rate, there is a fear that danger may approach the castle grounds as well. However, since I shall stay here quietly with Subaru and keep watch, you may go along without minding me.'

Tarutin: 'Very well, I humbly thank you. In that case, I shall hasten off at once to offer my condolences.'

So speaking while dangling a shōsan lantern, grasping a cane in one hand, and wearing geta with broken thongs, he set off, making his aching feet twinge through the mud-filled wicked paths.

Taishi: 'Look here, Subaru, is this not quite a magnificent sight? I have never yet seen such a huge fire since I was born. A fire is truly a spirited thing.'

Subaru: 'As you say, indeed fires are a fine spectacle. Even ants crawling upon the ground can be clearly seen just like this. However, are not the people who meet with a fire to be pitied? I truly hope there will be nothing involving the loss of human life.'

Taishi: 'Hn, that is so. I hope it will quiet down safely. Look, look, the fire is spreading and blazing wider and wider. That straight, high, white-glowing wall is the corner turret within the castle. Is the fire not beginning to lick at the corner turret? This is a serious matter. And a tremendous war cry can be heard. If I had returned into the castle, and if something like fire flying into the castle had occurred, my whereabouts would surely be hunted about by the senior vassals without fail. I only hope Alina handles things smoothly; ah, that is the sole thing weighing on my mind.'

Subaru: 'Ah, if the fire reaches the inner palace, Alina-san will be in great distress. Still, Alina-san is clever, so she will surely manage things somehow, I imagine.'

Taishi: 'That may be so, but one can never tell with a woman. Oh, look, the flames are rising even higher. The sparks are flying all the way here. This is truly breathtaking.'

Subaru: 'It is truly a rare spectacle. For us to view a fire from such a detached place is almost like watching a theatrical play.'

Taishi: 'Ha-ha-ha, that is a witty way to put it. It certainly does resemble a theatrical play. Still, those people fleeing in the distance look truly pitiable. Ah, the roof of that large building has collapsed with a crash. Fire is indeed a terrifying thing.'

Subaru: 'Indeed, human power is utterly helpless before a fire. It makes one deeply realize the majesty of the deities.'

Taishi: 'You speak wisely, Subaru. Even the mightiest castle can be reduced to ashes in an instant by a single flame. We too must be ever mindful.'

Subaru: 'Yes, indeed.'

As the two of them thus gazed fixedly upon the blazing fire, the dark night was dyed entirely in crimson, and the whole area glowed as bright as day.

暫らくしてタルチンはニツコと笑ひ、

『イヤ、流石は茶湯の宗匠だ。いい智慧が浮かんで来たぞ。万々一不幸にして太子さまがお出入遊ばさぬやうになつても構はぬ。よもやノメノメとあのスバール姫を殿中へ、連れて帰られる筈はない。さうすればキツト此タルチンが、どつかへお隠し申さねばなるまい。太子もキツト、さうして呉れと仰有るに定まつてる。何程考へても、それより外に方法手段はないもの。太子さまだつて、アリナさまだつて、実の所は内所でやつて厶る事だから弱味がある。そこを甘くつけ入つて、あの名玉を処分するのは処世上の奥の手だ。捨売にしても二万や三万の価値はある玉だ。僅に千円や二千円のつまみ金を貰つてヒヤヒヤとして暮してゐるよりも、さうなりや二三万円にでも売り飛ばしトルマン国にでも逃げ出し、立派な女房でも貰つて此世を栄耀栄華に気楽に暮すが一等だ。俺には何とした幸運が見舞うて来たのだらう、エツヘヽヽヽ』

と一人笑壺に入つて居る。折から聞ゆる、警鐘乱打の声、タルチンは足をひきずり乍ら窓の戸をあけて外を眺むれば、タラハン城下に当つて、火災を起し炎の舌は高く大空を舐て居る。

タル『ヤア、此奴ア大変だ。御得意先が火事にでも会ふやうな事があれば、俺等の懐に大影響を来す所だ。そして日頃お出入の情誼として火事見舞に行かねばなるまい。どうやらあの勢では容易に火事もおさまりさうにはないわい。太子様には済まないが一つ留守を頼んで火事見舞に出かけようかな』

と太い杖をつき大女の袋に蹴られて痛んだ足をチガチガさせ乍ら、離室の茶室に入り来り、

『もしもしお二人様、タラハン城下は大変な火災が起つて居ります。ここは町を余程離れてゐますから、メツタに飛火もしませぬから、安心で厶いますが、私は一寸お出入先へ見舞に行つて参りますから、どうぞ火事を見物し乍ら留守をしてゐて下さいませ』

太子『成程、大変な大火事と見えるな。此調子では、どうやら城内も危険が迫る恐れがある。然し乍ら余はここに神妙にスバールと留守をして居るから、余に構はず行つて来るがいいわ』

タル『ハイ、宜しうお願ひ申します。そんなら之から急ぎ見舞に行つて参ります』

と云ひ乍ら漿酸提灯をぶらつかせ、片手に杖をつき、チガチガと泥濘に満ちた悪道を尻きれになつた下駄を穿ち出て行く。

太子『これ、スバール、随分壮観ぢやないか。余はまだあんな大きな火事を生れてから見た事はない。火事と云ふものは本当に勇ましいものだな』

ス『仰せの如く実に火事は人気のいいものです。此通り地上に蟻の這うてゐるのさへもハツキリ見えます。然し乍ら火災にあうた人は可愛さうぢやありませぬか。どうか人命に関するやうな事がなければよう厶いますがな』

太子『ウン、さうだな。どうか無事にをさまればいいが。あれあれだんだん火が燃え拡がつて来た。あのスツと高く白く光つてゐる壁は城内の隅櫓だ。火は隅櫓を舐出したぢやないか、こいつア大変だ。さうして大変な鬨の声が聞えて来る。余が城内に帰つて居つたならば、又何とか工夫したらうに城内へ飛火がしたりするやうな事あれば、忽ち俺の所在を老臣共が尋ねまはるに違ひない。アリナが甘くやつてくれればいいが、アヽそれ許りが気にかかる』

§ 4

So saying, he hangs his head.

Subāru: 'Taishisama, are you not weak of heart? What did you say last night: "If I am by your side, even should the heavens fall, the earth shatter, and Tarahan Castle burn down, I care not the least. As long as the love between you and me is perfectly maintained, it is the greatest happiness of all. I have abandoned my royal throne, my wealth, and my castle as well."- Did you not say that? Calm yourself just a little bit. Is it not unseemly? "

Thus she speaks with bold audacity.

Though his heart is chilled by Subāru's words, the Taishi feigns an unconcerned air and says,

Taishi: 'Ahahaha! Indeed, perfectly true, ten million times over; a mere fire is not worth caring about. Come, taking advantage of the nighttime, shall we hold hands, the two of us, and walk out to the suburbs for a stroll to go sightsee at the fire?'

The sounds of alarm bells wildly beaten could be heard from all four quarters and eight directions, and the shouting war cries of the masses came echoing like roaring waves.

と、うなだれる。

ス『太子様貴方はお心が弱いぢやありませぬか。夜前何と仰有いました、「お前の側に居るならば、仮令天は落ち、地はくだけタラハン城は焼けおちても敢て意に介せない。お前と俺との恋愛さへ、完全に維持されたら何よりの幸だ。余は王位も富も城も捨てた」と仰有つたぢやありませぬか。チツとお落ち付きなさい。見つともないぢやありませぬか』

と大胆不敵の事を云ふ。太子はスバールの言葉に肝を冷し乍ら、さあらぬ体にて、

『アツハヽヽヽ如何にも尤も千万、火災なんか意に介するに足らないよ。サア夜分を幸ひ、お前と二人手をひいて郊外の散歩に出かけ火事の見物をしようではないか』

警鐘乱打の声は四方八方より聞え、民衆の叫ぶ鬨の声は鯨波の如く聞え来りぬ。
