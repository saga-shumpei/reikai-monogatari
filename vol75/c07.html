Chapter 7: 瑞の Kotodama [1901]

瑞の言霊〔1901〕

§ 1

Hereupon Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami knelt before the altar, and since the divine works of creating the lands and producing the divine children had been accomplished, for the sake of all deities and men, He offered up the great liturgy of the living Kototama and prayed for the eternal peace of Tenkai.

原文（近代文語）

爰に太元顕津男の神は大前に跪座して、国土生み御子産みの神業成りたれば、万神人の為に生言霊の太祝詞を奏上し、天界永遠の無事を祈らせ給ひぬ。

§ 2

Reverently, awe-stricken, and bowing low, we humbly address Thee: Beginning with Amanominehio no Kami, Takahoko no Kami, and Kamuhoko no Kami, who dwell in the purple-robed palace of Takaamahara, we speak before the august presences of the eight million deities of Amatsukami and Kunitsukami. Since the deities born of this Musubi are, by virtue of the supreme Musubi of the Great Ōmisumaru—the fundamental source of their physical forms—exhausting the utmost limits of Musubi, exhausting the boundaries of Musubi, and existing as bodies born of Musubi in a manner wondrous and miraculous, Ametsuchi (heaven and earth) are perfectly and roundly equipped therein. The harvested ears of rice in Akita do not compete even for a fleeting moment, and the countless clusters of bamboo do not lean even slightly to one side. The creepers of the deep mountains never catch or halt; without a moment's stagnation in the flowing currents, the mitama (spirits) remain limpid like white jewels, shining and radiating brilliantly like red jewels. This singular body fills and overflows within the Great Ōmisumaru, and since there is no place where the utmost bounds of the Great Ōmisumaru do not divide, there is no sinking in particular, no place within the limits of Takaamahara where our bodies do not exist, and furthermore, no place where they do not exist within this body. Furthermore, there is no thought of limiting this body solely to one's own. For this reason, the distant heavens flow limpidly within our physical bodies, and the solid earth manifests its firm and identical form within our physical bodies, moving not and shaking not. The sun shining in the heavens also pierces and illuminates through the six directions from within the heart, illuminating and brightening the world without leaving a single dark corner, and the tidal waters of Ōwada deepen their bottoms within our bodies, swirling the tides through the six directions, raising white waves of wind to surge and circle the tips of the islands. Therefore, if the feather-beater awakens its heart, that heart immediately manifests as Kami; even if one moves the physical body in the slightest, it instantly reveals the wondrous forms of all creation in the universe. There is not a single one among the eight million, tens of millions, and myriad deities who does not appear within the heart. For this reason, what is thought in a single heart directly becomes the hearts of billions upon boundless numbers of Kami, becoming for the boundless and infinite children [of Kami], and the boundless and infinite hearts of the Kami likewise turn back to become our own. Where a single body moves, it becomes the actions of billions of Kami to attain their blessings, and the actions of billions of people return to our bodies to receive those very blessings. Not a particle of dust shifts its heart toward self-interest, nor does it perform deeds for the sake of the body even for a moment. For this reason, all of the Amatsukami, Kunitsukami, and eight million, tens of millions, and myriad deities—great and small alike, every single pillar down to a single acorn—are deities from whom not a drop leaks out of a bowl's water. Not even half of a flying speck of dust leaves behind a shadow of the heavenly waters; they manifest their physical forms, blessing the Aramitama and Nigimitama. Constantly coming like wisteria vines entwining a tree, guarding the eyes like eyelids, serving as the protection of the dazzling day and the shelter of the dark-as-crow-feathers night, they guard us unchangingly and bless us unfailingly. According to the august names of those Kami, according to the ways of those Kami, soaring through the distant skies, entering the solid earth, diving into and emerging from the depths of the ocean, loving us, pitying us, helping us, blessing us, favoring us, and bestowing grace upon us—what is thought in the true heart is instantly accomplished, and what is performed in the righteous body is immediately fulfilled. Therefore, not a single one of the hundreds of thousands of wishes and the billions of prayers fails to be realized, and not a single one of the billions of endeavors fails to be achieved to the slightest particle of dust. Those that manifest form in nothingness include the bodies of Kami, human beings, beasts, birds, fish and shellfish, insects, creeping bugs of trees and grasses, and even the single-leaved daylilies. Every single thing that possesses form having been born into this world through Musubi, not a single one is left behind, not even half of a flying speck of dust is lacking. When the service of the night and the service of the day come and are served, and when the active use of the morning and the active use of the evening come and are put into use, there is nothing lacking in the body even for a moment, nor is there any affliction remaining in the body. Therefore, for this reason, they themselves call that name 'Kami-hito' (divine human). Troubles of every kind from time to time, such as sorrows of illness and suffering, are things that scarcely find a place within the path of beasts, birds, insects, and fish; yet in the precious, wondrous, and divine path of the Kami-hito, even the shadow thereof should utterly and completely be nonexistent. The fact that the Kami-hito of the purple-robed heavenly realm fall ill, writhe, suffer, and anguish arises entirely because they walk a path equivalent to that of beasts, birds, insects, and fish, thereby losing the path of the Kami-hito, causing various disasters to well up like swarms of flies. Now, when water rots away, insects swarm; when tree leaves flourish, birds naturally gather and come. If one thus walks that defiled path, one will eventually end up bearing the physical form of beasts, birds, insects, and fish, and permanently become beasts, birds, insects, and fish. How awe-inspiring! While attaining and receiving through Musubi the incomparable, precious, and wondrous body of a Kami-hito, to thoroughly know, clearly realize, and fully master the divine law whereby stagnant water flows backward to become a lowly mitama—deeply reflecting upon this, fearing and trembling at it with shuddering bodies, fearing and trembling in excess, and erasing the hearts of beasts and birds and the actions of insects and fish that have been aroused up to this very day, just as the morning sun dissolves dew and frost, or just as the wind of Shinato blows away and clears up the deep morning and evening mists, purifying them utterly and sweeping them away to the utmost—if one advances straight ahead into the path of the Kami-hito where the sun shines brightly, without raising a single trace of selfish thought, even to the half of a flying speck of dust, without acting for the sake of one's own body, purifying one's actions according to the august name of the Kami-hito, holding neither the hearts nor actions of beasts, birds, insects, and fish, without straying and without coveting, and thus enters the path of the Kami-hito, then the two deities of the Su Great Deity sitting in Kaminomiya will also bless us in accordance with their august names and the path of the Kami-hito. Just as the true-word-performing path of the Kami-hito itself thinks and wishes, the naturally occurring Great True Path originally endowed in the heavenly realm shall be transmitted eternally. The heavenly sunshades shall shine universally, the heavenly clouds shall moisten universally, and the great august seat of the Su Great Deity who knows the eight corners shall, together with heaven, earth, sun, and moon, continually and enduringly be transmitted eightyfold in succession, never to be lost even for a moment. All the host of deities, each and every one, shall multiply eightyfold as children of birth, and ever-multiplying and succeeding one another, shall serve and attend like hemp pillars for all eternity, according to their respective ranks. The Kami-hito, each guarding and serving their respective allotted divine labors, serving and revering the Su Great Deity while rejoicing without turning away from the path of the Kami-hito—even the small deities who gather salt and fish among the grasses of the sea in the depths of the deep mountains shall not suffer from starvation, nor shall they know the distress of heat and cold. We pray that the Kami-hito of upper, middle, and lower ranks alike may together receive a single great joy and become the true august land of Su. We pray that the three deities of the palace dwelling in the purple-robed realm of Takaamahara, together with the hundreds of thousands of deities, will hearken to these matters, and we worship and address You with awe and reverence.

原文（近代文語）

『謹み畏み敬ひも白さく、高天原の紫微宮に大坐します天之峯火夫の神、高鉾の神、神鉾の神を始め奉り、天津神国津神八百万の神等の御前に白さく。此産霊に成れる神々は、其身体の大本源なる大御須麻留の上無き産霊に依りて産霊の極み極み尽し、産霊の限り限り尽して、此も霊妙く此も霊端に産霊成れる身にしあれば、そが地水火風空円く備はり、秋田の刈穂仮初にも競争ふ事なく、些少群竹聊も一方に片寄る事の無ければ、深山の葛懸り止る事無く、おどみにおどみ滞ることなくして、身魂永久に白玉なす伊澄み渡り、赤玉なす赫らひ照らひて、此一の身大御須麻留が中に充塞り塞り満て、大御須麻留の極み別らざる所しなければ、殊に底る事なく、高天原の限り我身の在らざる所なく、更に此身に有らざる処なし。更に此身を吾身と限る思ひ無し。此故に久方の天は我身体の中に伊澄み渡り、荒金の地は我身体の中に堅身同身を顕はして動く事なく揺ぐ事なく、天に照る日も心の内より六合に伊照り貫通り、世の中を照らし明めて落つる隈なく、大和田の潮水も我身の内に底を深めて潮を六合に廻らし、風の共白浪を立て起して嶋の崎々伊せき廻る。故れ羽叩きも心を起せば、その心即て神と顕はれ、僅少にても身活用けば、立所に森羅万象の妙体を現し、八百万千万諸の神、一つも心の内に現はれずと言ふ事なし。此故に一つの心に思ふ所、直に億兆無量の神の心となりて、無量無辺の御子の為となり、無量無辺の神の心は亦立返りて我為となる。一つの身動く所、億兆の神の行となりてその幸ひを得、億兆の人の行ひ我身に帰りて亦その幸ひを受く、微塵程も吾為に心を移す事なく、且くも身の為に行ひ為す事なし。此故に天津神国津神八百万千万の神、大き小さき神てふ神の悉々、樫の実の唯一柱も碗の水の漏るる神なし。空飛ぶ塵の半分も天津水影遺る神なく、現身を顕はし荒魂和魂を幸へ、常に来りて藤葛の木を纒ふが如く、目蓋の目を守るが如く、茜刺す昼の守り烏羽玉の夜の護りと、弥守りに護り弥幸ひに福ひ、其が神の御名のまにまに、そが神の道の任々、久方の空に天翺翔り、荒金の地に入り、海中に潜き入り潜き出て、愛しみ玉ひ、憐み玉ひ、扶け玉ひ、幸ひ玉ひ、恵み玉ひ、福ひ玉へば、真心に思ふ所、立所に成り、正身に行へば直ちに成る。是故に百千万の願ひ、億兆の祈り事一つも成らずと言ふ事なく、億兆の業微塵程も遂げずと言ふ事なし、無に形体を顕はせるものは、神を始め人の身、獣類、禽鳥、魚介、昆虫、木草の螻虫、萱草の片葉に至る迄、其現身の世に産霊て、形体なせる物てふ物は一つだも遺る物なく、飛ぶ塵の塵の半分も欠くる物なく、夜の仕へ昼の仕へに来たり仕へ、朝の活用夕の活用に来たり活用けば、且くも身に乏しき事なく、身に残るも煩ふ事なし。故れ此故に其が名を自ら神人となむ言ふなる。折々の諸の煩ひ病苦の悲しみの如き災は、禽獣虫魚等が道の内に備はれる事方にして、貴き霊き神人の道には更に更にその影だにも有る事なきものを、紫微天界の神人の病み悶へ苦しみ悩む事あるは、禽獣虫魚に均しき道を行きて神人の道を失へるより、諸の災難五月蠅如す皆湧き起るになむある。抑も爰に水腐り果つれば昆虫湧き、木の葉茂れば自ら鳥集り来たる。如此て其が穢き道を歩みぬれば、遂に其が獣鳥虫魚の身としも成果てて、永く獣鳥虫魚と成らむ。畏きかもよ、比類なき貴き霊しき神人の身を産霊得ながら、おどみの水のおどみ帰りて、卑しき身魂と成らむ神理を、真玉如す深く知り明に悟り極めぬれば、是をしも恐み畏み深く思ひて、身震ひ恐懼く迄に畏み恐み過ちて、今日まで起しつる獣鳥の心、虫魚の行ひは朝津日の露霜を消し尽す如く、朝の深霧夕の深霧を志那戸の風の吹攘ひ清むるが如く、清め尽し攘ひ極めて、照り渡ります陽の一進みに神人の道に進み入り、空飛ぶ塵の塵の半分も私の思ひを起す事なく、吾身の為に行ふ事なく、神人の名のまにまに行ひ澄まし、獣鳥虫魚の心を持たず、行ひを為さず、迷ふ事なく欲りする事なくして神人の道に入りぬれば、紫微宮に坐すスの大神二柱の神も、そが神名のまにまに神の道のまにまに、夜の守り日の守りに幸ひ玉ひて、真言為す神人の道自ら思ひ願ふがまにまに、天界の本より備はれる自らなる大真道永久に伝りて、天津日蔭普く照らし、天雲の普く潤ひて八隅知しスの大神の惟神の大御座は、天地日月と共に常永に八十連に伊継ぎ給ひて、且くも失はせ給はず、諸の神達おのもおのも生みの子の八十継きいや継ぎ伊継ぎて、己が位＠のまにまにいや遠永に麻柱ひ仕へまつり、神人等が各自々々仕さしの神業を守り仕へて、スの大神に仕へ奉り楽しみつ、神人の道に背く事なく、奥山の深山の奥、海の草、塩焼き漁る小さき神も飢餓に苦む事なく、暑さ寒さの悩みを知らず、上中下の各位の神人は、共に一つの歓楽を受け、真の大スの御国と成し玉へと願ぎ奉る事の由を、高天原の紫微の宮居の三柱の神、百千万の神等共に聞召し玉へと、畏み畏みも拝み白す。

§ 3

Tamanōka swells and expands, and the land of Manazuru has firmly been established.

The waterfalls of Mount Tamamoyama, reaching up into the distant sky, are flags of white silk.

Through the blessing of the living Kototama of the Kamigami, the water of Tamanokosui has dried up.

The water of Tamanōmi rises by degrees, and upon the sloping surface of Mount Tamamoyama, the weeds grow.

Singing of the dawn in the kaka-kuke-ki of the living Kototama, the kakedori birds are born forth.

With the voice of the kakedori singing the morning clearly, the land of Manazuru has dawned brightly.

While the land was yet immature, hazy and misty, amidst the universe of yomoyamo,

Mist rises over Mount Tamamoyama, and the neighing of the white horse is high in the garden of morning calm.

Though the land is yet immature, by the divine majesty of the Lord God, it has thus far solidified,

Solidifying on to the very ends of all yorozuyo, I think upon the prosperity of this Kunihara.

Through the blessing of the living Kototama of the Lord God, Shinrabanshiyō flourish day by day.

Both heaven and earth dividing into youthfulness, the joy of standing and solidifying the Mikuni.

Gradually the land of Manazuru has solidified.

Presently, the Kunitama no Kami who shall be born—'

Having thus chanted the sacred poem, he respectfully withdrew from the grand presence, and returned toward his own hall. The two deities, Tamanohikohime no Kami and Ikutahikohime no Kami, following in the wake of Akitsuo no Kami, withdrew from the great presence and concealed their divine forms in a chamber.

Tōmiwo no Kami chants a sacred poem:

Standing upon the summit of Mount Tamamoyama to view the land, as far as the eye can reach, mists are rising.

The magnificence of the youthful Kunihara of the south, unto which the auspicious Mitama was anchored, has now become visible.

Maruyahiko no Kami, settle from hence in the sacred place of Mikasayama and guard the land.

As for the youthful Kunihara of Manazuru, may Kunakahiko no Kami eternally be anchored here.

At the very moment when he thus chanted, Kunakahiko no Kami, rejoicing that Mount Tamamoyama had newly been born, and desiring to celebrate and praise the merits of the Kamigami, leaped upon the heavenly spotted horse, and before his sacred poem was even finished, he swiftly arrived at this sacred place and chanted his sacred poem:

Creating the land according to the divine words of the Lord God, and causing the Kunitama to be born—ah, the auspicious Mitama!

Obeying the divine words of Tōmiwo no Kami, I shall govern the Kunihara of Manazuru.

The bottom of Tamanōmi swells to become the eastern sloping surface of Mount Tamamoyama.

The rising steam, hazy and misty, and the hollows of the land of Manazuru have begun to dry.

Guarding and supporting the Kunitama no Kami who shall be born, would that we might open up the land through chiyo and yorozuyo.

By the precious merits of the hundred deities, the form of the land of Manazuru has been determined.

Upon the vast Kunihara, whose limits are unknown, Shinrabanshiyō have also begun to sprout forth.

Even birds, beasts, and insects have welled forth into being through the Su Kototama of the auspicious Mitama.

If all the things that have welled forth are the spirits of heaven and earth, we must not treat them lightly.

Gods and men are gods and men, beasts are beasts, birds are birds, and for both insects and fish, there is a Way.

It is by walking the Way of beasts that beasts and birds shall cause this heavenly realm to prosper eternally.

If even gods and men were to walk the Way of beasts, this heavenly realm would instantly fall into disorder.

The Way of beasts is easy; to tread the Way of the Gods is no easy matter, but a thing of awe.

Maruyahiko no Kami chants a sacred poem:

I think upon the divine work of Kunakahiko no Kami, who rules over the land of Manazuru, as being no ordinary matter.

Obeying the divine words of Tōmiwo no Kami, I shall eternally guard the land of the dawn.

The merit of Tōmiwo no Kami, who governs all of the southern land, is to be revered.

Come now, let us proclaim the living Kototama and pray for the blessing of the august child who shall be born.

Purifying ourselves in the pure waters of the Tamasen Waterfall, let us pray for the august child in the Great Shrine of Tamano.

Thinking upon this as the auspicious divine work of the almighty Kami, today's purification is a joy.

Born as a god, born as a beast, and becoming a bird that flies through the empty sky, I shall guard the age of the gods.

Even though my body should descend into the flocks of beasts, for the sake of the Mikuni it must not be grudged.

玉野丘膨れ拡ごり真鶴の 国土は固らに定まりにけり 久方の空にのび立つ玉藻山の 千条の滝は白絹の旗よ 神々の生言霊の幸ひて 玉野湖水はほしあがりたり 玉野湖の湖水次第に高まりて 玉藻の山の傾斜面に生ふる藻草 カコクケキ生言霊にあかときを うたふ家鶏鳥生れ出でにけり 家鶏鳥の晨を歌ふ声さやに 真鶴の国はあけ渡りけり 未だ国土稚くありせばもやもやと 霧立ちのぼる六合のうち 玉藻山霧立ちのぼり白駒の 嘶き高し朝しづの庭に 未だ国土は稚くあれども主の神の 御稜威にここまで固まりしはや 万代の末の末まで固めゆく この国原のさかえを思ふ

遠見男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『玉藻山頂上に立ちて国見すれば 目路の限りは靄立ちのぼれり 瑞御霊依さし給ひし南の 稚国原の秀は見えにけり 圓屋比古神はこれより三笠山の 聖所に鎮まり国守りませ 真鶴の稚国原は国中比古の 神とこしへに依さしたまはれ』
