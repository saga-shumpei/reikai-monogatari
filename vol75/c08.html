Chapter 8: 結の Kotodama [1902]

結の言霊〔1902〕

§ 1

Regarding the divine work wherein the Kami Ōmotoakitsuo gives birth to Kunitamagami, He is about to set forth the great essentials of the application of Kototama. Namely,

Aouei is the heavenly position,

Waōuewi is the earthly position, and,

Yayouyei is the human position.

Therefore, if the distinctions of the Kototama of A, Wa, and Ya are not made correctly, it is most inconvenient to explain clearly the truth of heaven, earth, and man. It may be said that in the modern age, the application of the Kototama is disturbed, and without distinction in the sounds of the three rows of awa-ya, confusion has reached its extreme.

And

Akasatanahamayarawa is the heavenly position, residing in Heaven, and is the position of Kimi.

Okosotonohomoyorowo is the earthly seat, and is the position of Tami.

Ukusutsunufumuyuruu is the seat of binding, and is the position of Kami.

Ekesetenehemerewe is the water seat, and is the position of Omi.

Ikishitinihimiiriwi is the fire seat, and is the position of Ōmi.

Hence it should be known that Kimi means the sovereign, Tami means the people, Kami means the deity, Omi means the lesser ministers, and Ōmi means the great ministers.

Akasatanahamayarawa is called the A-row, and the rest should be known by analogy. Therefore,

The A-row dwells in the heavenly position of all things in the universe,

The O-row dwells in the earthly position of all things in the universe,

The U-row dwells in the binding position of all things in the universe,

The E-row dwells in the water position of all things in the universe,

The I-row dwells in the fire position of all things in the universe.

By this, one should comprehend the source from which the names and meanings of all things arise.

Now, as the time drew near for the Kami Ikuchihime to give birth to Kunitamagami, the Kami Akitsuo bowed before the Great Shrine and respectfully presented the sacred congratulatory words, chanting the Kototama clearly with the voice, 'Ukusutsunufumuyuruu.' When He thus chanted the Kototama, the other Kami followed behind Him, repeating and chanting 'Ukusutsunufumuyuruu' with one voice and in unison, which was awe-inspiring indeed.

Let the child be born who shall brightly illuminate In detail the world of nukubatama! Fū-fū-fū-fū F. With the swinging of the spirit and pure misogi, The light of the residing child is most august! Ū-ū-ū-ū U. The divine spirit of the beautiful kami-mizura, The divine work of child-bearing has drawn near.'

The Kami Ikuchihime recites a sacred poem:

'Ū-ū-ū-ū U. Beautiful is my Kimi, and beautifully Have I been blessed to harbor the divine child! Kū-kū-kū-kū K. Pregnant with Kunitamagami, Have I indeed come to this sacred place? Sū-sū-sū-sū S. Fū-fū-fū-fū F. Let a responsive child be born, In the True Crane Land to solidify a thousand reigns! Mū-mū-mū-mū M. Steamed and being steamed, my body is heavy Wherein the child of mizura has taken abode. Yū-yū-yū-yū Y. Oh, what a child, white and pure beyond snow and frost, Being the child of the divine spirit of mizura! Ū-ū-ū-ū U. Now am I at the very limit of joy, As the days are fulfilled to give birth to Kunitamagami.'

The Kami Maruyahiko recites a sacred poem:

'At the summit of the beautiful mountain of tamamo, Have we welcomed today's auspicious day? This very day when Kunitamagami is born, I feel as though heaven and earth open at once. In this sacred place of Tamamoyama, Which seems the very height of purity, the born mizuko— Do the moon and sun celebrate today's auspicious day, Clearing away the clouds and shining brightly strong? The darkness of nukubatama shall be cleared away more and more, From this very day when the Kimi of Mizuko is born; With the crisp sound of the wind blowing past, Heard distinctly, the ever-green pines are swaying. To a joy of which there is no known precedent from ancient times, Have I encountered today as Kunitamagami is born; May the child be born and give life To the young land of dreams, reality, and illusions! Even the land where floating clouds drift, from this very day, Shall flourish smoothly and harmoniously.'

原文（近代文語）

太元顕津男の神が国魂神を生み給ふ神業に就き、言霊応用の大要を示さむとす。即ち、

アオウエイは天位にして、

ワヲウヱヰは地の位＠なり、又、

ヤヨユエイは人位なり。

故にア、ワ、ヤの言霊の区別を正しくせざれば、天地人の真理を説き明かすこと最も不便なり。現代は言霊の応用乱れてアワヤ三行の音声の区別なく複雑極まれりと言ふべし。

又

アカサタナハマヤラワは、天位にして、天に座し、貴身の位置なり。

オコソトノホモヨロヲは、地の座にして、田身の位置なり。

ウクスツヌフムユルウは、結びの座にして、隠身の位置なり。

エケセテネヘメエレヱは、水の座にして、小身の位置なり。

イキシチニヒミイリヰは、火の座にして、大身の位置なり。

故に貴身は君、田身は民、隠身は神、小身は小臣、大身は大臣の意と知るべし。

アカサタナハマヤラワをア列といふ、其他は準じて知るべし。故に、

ア列は森羅万象の天位に居り、

オ列は森羅万象の地位に居り、

ウ列は森羅万象の結びに居り、

エ列は森羅万象の水位に居り、

イ列は森羅万象の火位に居るなり。

是を以て一切万有の名義の出づる根源を悟るべきなり。

今、生代比女の神が、国魂神を生ます時の迫りければ、顕津男の神は大前に額きて神嘉言を奏上し給ひ、『ウクスツヌフムユルウ』と声音朗かに言霊を奏上し給へば、他の神々は御後に従ひて、異口同音に『ウクスツヌフムユルウ』を繰返し繰返し宣らせ給ひしぞ畏けれ。

§ 2

Tohomio no Kami chants a sacred poem.

"Ah, this beautiful, joyous day of the divine birth itself, Is the day of joy when the auspicious child is born; As the Kunitamagami is born ever more and more, Today of the mountain of jewels is indeed wondrous, From the living kototama and sacred fire and water of the Lord God, All things in the universe have verily been born; Both moon and sun push aside the cloud and mist of the august sky, And cast their radiance upon the earth below, Though she is a woman like the creeping pue-grass, O Ikuyohime, Do thou bring forth a valiant august child! Before the great august presence of the jewelled palace of Tamano, The heart of this day's divine birth is pure indeed; Binding and binding, fire and water meet together, and of the auspicious child, Bright and clear is the moon and day of today when it is born, When I think of the things of the future to come, how joyful it is, The land of the true crane that shall flourish eternally, Surely this is the height of joy, today when the auspicious child Is born as the Kunitamagami."

Ureshiho no Kami chants a sacred poem.

"In the true fine land of the youthful land like floating oil, Today, settled wondrously and intimately, In the joy that the Kunitamagami is born, I know not where to place my body! Revering the divine words of the Lord God, my spouse, has brought forth the soul of the true crane land; Receiving the dew of the august spirit of Tsukiyomi no Kami, Ikuyo no Hime shines brightly; Becoming the weft and becoming the warp, the Lord God, How noble to cause the birth of lands and the birth of gods! Thinking that even the falling rain is the achievement of the auspicious august spirit, How awe-inspiring, in the land of the true crane, The tangles of the bound threads have also been unravelled! Since there are no clouds or mists to trouble the heart, Going and going, the great moor whose going has no end, The achievement of my spouse who opens it up is awe-inspiring, The heart of Ureshiho no Kami is filled with courage, Joyous and delightful is this auspicious day!"

Mibashihiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

"Even when I think of building the divine bridge that opens up the age of this beautiful divine land, The land is youthful, the trees and grasses are youthful, the gods are youthful, The Himegami is youthful in the land of the true crane; The lofty achievement of the Lord God is seen right before my eyes, On the ridge of the mountain of jewels, methinks; Cranes dance, domestic fowls sing, plum blossoms scatter their fragrance, Oh, the august child born in this sacred place! To brighten the darkness of the black-dyed-nut-hued night one after another, Bring forth an august child superior to moon and sun! In the true fine land of the peerless land of the true crane, The life of the child born is eternal; Even if I were to stand in the midst of the burning fire, I would not disdain it, Thinking of the hardships of Ikuyo no Hime, Purifying the august spirit in the sacred garden, to wait for this day Is also the joyful birth of the august child; Even while the plain of the land is like drifting floating clouds, The god of firming is about to be born, methinks."

Samutama no Kami chants a sacred poem.

"Appearing as Samutama no Kami and coming to the land of the true crane, to protect the august child as well, Although the land is still youthful, the auspicious august spirit Born in fire and water, the august child is noble; Amidst the clear and limpid heaven and earth, The auspicious child born is surely the treasure of the world! Drawing upon the moon for the spirit of Tsukiyomi no Kami, It brings forth the soul of the land of the true crane; Washing the thread of the soul in the tepid jewel spring, My body has become pure; Even the blowing wind is warm, yet today when the auspicious child is born is pure and cool; Rising up thickly like smoke, The mist has completely enveloped the sacred mountain; While steam rises and protects the born august child, This sacred place has built an eightfold fence; Enveloping both above and below in dense mist, so that the birth of the auspicious child May be easy, an eightfold fence is built."

Tamakihare no Kami chants a sacred poem.

"Growing up more and more eternally, the born child Shall protect this plain of the land; I shall eternally protect the life Of the auspicious child born as the Kunitamagami; Tamakihare granted by the Lord God, I shall protect its life morning and evening; Both moon and night are hidden in mist and there is no wind, Only the voice of the true crane echoes; To give warmth and cause the august child to be born, Awe-inspiring is the eightfold fence of the Lord Great God; So that even the blowing wind may not touch him, raising up the eightfold mist The Lord God awe-insuringly protects him; Is it the fire and water of the august child born in the sultry steam? The four quarters of heaven and earth are a curtain of mist; Steam rises and the four quarters of heaven and earth are soft And abundant, today when the august child is born; Protecting the newborn august child and wrapping around heaven and earth, How deep indeed is the mist that has enveloped them!"

§ 3

'At the voice of u is born the Miko, whose ubugoe We have awaited and awaited, and today has arrived; The merit of Kimi, who brings forth the kuni and generates Kunitamagami, Is indeed the pillar of Miyo. Aweing the words of yosashi of the Lord God, The Miko of the auspicious Mitama is born; Shining brightly like a sword, tachi, and mirror, Be born, O Miko of the misumaru jewels! Walking with bare feet upon the aragane earth, I pray that a good Miko may be born. Solemn is the misogi of Furutama; Ikuyohimegami's Miko is now about to be born more and more; Steaming and steaming, condensing water and fire with water and fire, The months of the Hime of Ikuyo have been fulfilled. How beautiful is the Mitama washed In the takitsuse of the spring of jewels, purified and cleansed! Floating clouds, one after another, have gathered And enshrouded this sugado.'

Umashimoto no Kami chants the miuta.

'I pray that the delightful Miko may soon be born, In the heart of me, Umashimoto no Kami, Through the wondrous hataraki of water and fire and water and fire, Kunitamagami is about to be born. Concealing the moon and sun of the clear, limpid misora, The heart of this fukagiri is noble; Concealing the moon and sun in the fog, for the birth of the Miko, It is the yahe busuma of bashful modesty; O Great Lord God, protect the birth of the Miko Of the young Ikuyohime, tender as nue grass; Even the blowing wind has ceased today, and the sacred mountain Has been enveloped in the busuma of yahe-giri. I, Umashimoto, will protect forever The happiness of the Miko born from our union; Slowly, very quietly, and peacefully, Be born, O Kunitamagami! Long have we awaited the Miko to be born As the Kunitama of the beautiful Manazuru no Kuni.'
