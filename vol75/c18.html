Chapter 18: Kami の Way 行 [1912]

神の道行〔1912〕

§ 1

Ureshiho no Kami, mounted grandly upon a steed, stood before Akitsuo no Kami and proceeded forward while chanting a sacred poem.

Within the Takaamahara, Solidified purely and elaborately, O the preciousness of Manazurukuni! Akitsuo no Kami has emerged, And upon the land where the earth is yet young, By the blessing of the living Kototama, He has created and solidified it; Until the life of Chiyotsuruhime, Who was established as Kunitamagami, Giving birth and bringing forth step by step, Leaving behind Tamamo no Yama, To give birth to the realm of the western direction In eternal darkness blocked by black clouds, This present departure on a journey As I send you off, how joyous I am! In heaven and on earth, black clouds gather, In the western realm whose destination is unknown, Wicked Magakami and Sabahe swarm. As we hear that myriad calamities arise By day and by month, the Lord God, Sending forth the wondrous Mitama, Opening up the eternal and immutable Amatsukuni, How reverent it is! With the winding corners of the road free from harm, Straddling a steed of ten thousand leagues, Bathed in the wind blowing the dust of Tartary, Across the hazy wilderness extending for a thousand leagues, The sound of insects crying on the right and on the left Echoes clearly down to the roots of the grass, Making the heart within face the challenge boldly. With a brave heart and a brave steed, Today as we set forth, how joyous it is! Ah, how truly divine! Where is Minamihikogami now? The meritorious service of going ahead To guard the imperial progress of Kimi Is now visible before our eyes. The road-markers for Kimi's progress Grow ever firmer, and the hoofs of the swift steed Advance steadily over the iron-hard ground, Making the journey peaceful. Ah, how truly divine! By the blessing of divine grace, There are not a cloud in today's sky, And the blowing breeze gently Caresses our faces as we go. Truly, thinking of this departure as a journey to heaven, How joyful I am now, At the riverbank of Hinode no Kawa, Sending off Kimi as I proceed. Sending off Kimi as I proceed.

Akitsuo no Kami halted his steed for a while, and looking back upon the path he had come, gazed up at the distantly hazy Tamamo no Yama, and chanted a sacred poem.

In the sky to which I turn back and gaze, Tamamo no Yama Is hazy, wearing robes of purple clouds. Drawing a curtain of purple clouds around, Will Tamano no Hime serve in the palace? Though Manazuru's mountain is distant and cannot be seen, Ikuyo no Hime must be peacefully abiding. The winding paths of my journey too have been entirely free of harm; By the protecting grace of the Lord Ōkami, When I look up, the destination ahead in the western Realm's sky, where black clouds also rise, Day after day serving in the divine work of kami-creation, Am I not a restless Mitama? Escaping the ugly calamities of the ugly kami, How I have advanced up to this day! Now that it has come to this, serving in the Takahi no Miya, The eight pillar kami have been fondly remembered. Gazing at the skies of the southeast, the eight pillar Himegami must be yearning for me. Without a single day to rest the Mitama at ease, How painful it was to be one setting out on a journey! Setting forth to give birth to lands and give birth to kami, I have thoroughly realized the flavors of the world.

Ureshiho no Kami chanted a sacred poem.

Realizing now more than ever The merit of the wondrous Mitama kami, O Kimi, who go forth illuminating the realm Where clouds rise in all eight directions and both moon and sun are hidden! The voices of a hundred birds sing of a thousand ages, While insects cry across the roots of eight thousand grasses. As we advance through the great plain where young grasses sprout, I pray for blessings upon the path of Kimi's progress. The journey of Kimi, who goes forth illuminating The kunihara where fog rises billowing thick and dense, When I look up, so blinding to the eyes is the noble light Emitted by Akitsuo no Kami; All the great birds and small birds traveling through the sky Follow faithfully in the wake of Kimi's progress, And many thousands of cranes, aligning their wings, Dance along in send-off of Kimi's imperial progress. The great sky has grown bright, with the numerous wings Of Manazuru shining brightly in white.

Tamakiharino no Kami chanted a sacred poem.

Stopping my steed and gazing upon the configuration of the lands in all directions, As far as the eye can reach is a world of clouds. Feeling as though I am drifting amidst the world of clouds, As I follow in the wake of Kimi's progress, With waves of clouds roaring up, how distant indeed Is the western realm to which Kimi goes! Manazuru sings of a thousand ages while swift steeds Neigh for ten thousand generations on today's journey. Unto the very end of Kimi's life of hundreds of millions of years, May you preserve the land so that it may be guarded; The life-giving kami shall guard it, rejuvenating The life of the wondrous Mitama for eternity; Rejuvenating and rejuvenating, through hundreds of millions of ages, May Kimi become a pillar of the divine land. Ahead of us, the great serpent kami with eight heads and eight tails Goes forth and awaits Kimi's arrival to welcome him. This great serpent straddles many mountain ranges, Releasing strange water and fire in all directions; To exorcise the water and fire of the great serpent kami Who dims the western realm is the blessing of Kototama.

§ 2

What strength of mine could avail

If I should stand against the monstrous water and fire of the hideous Orochigami?

Through the sacred water and fire of my Kimi's living Kototama,

Even the hideous Orochi shall surely submit and serve.

With Hinatagaha spread before it, the hideous Orochi

Shall transform into the shape of a mountain and await.

Transforming into mountains and rivers alike, the ugly deity

Seeks to afflict the progress of Kimi.

Whatever events may come, through the boundary-crossing limit of time,

The life of Kimi shall never be cast down.

Since Kimi serves in the endless divine work,

Even the hideous Orochi is surely of no account.'

Musubiahase no Kami chants a divine poem.

'The distant heavenly sky is high, the earth is vast,

Kimi who travels through this Nakakuni,

When looking up, the mountain of Tamamo is above the clouds,

Shining upon its very summit.

On the slope of Tamamo Mountain, a single black shadow

Running across must be the reflection of the clouds.

From between the tattered clouds and the unbroken clouds,

The surface of Tamamo Mountain is yet visible.

The noble form of Tamamo Mountain is crumbling away,

As we have come far across the great plain of Ōno,

Illuminating the plain of drifting mist and clouds,

The majestic divine power of Kimi who sets forth is high.

You who have started forth from Tamamo Mountain, my Kimi,

Your shining light has grown ever more intense.

Not knowing you to be such an awe-inspiring Kimi,

I must have been enveloped in your radiance.

Now that the time has come, my eyes have awakened,

To gaze upon the mighty light of the august spirit.

Hearing that the Yamata no Orochi dwells in the path ahead,

My soul-threads rise up in courage.

By the light of the water and fire of my Kimi's Kototama,

The hideous Orochi shall submit and serve,

Turning into mountains, rivers, and clouds,

The hideous Orochi that would obstruct the way.

Go forth with caution, my Kimi, for both mountains and rivers

Are the temporal incarnations of the hideous Orochi.

The hideous Orochi upon the path ahead in various ways

Will change its form and seek to obstruct you.

Rivers, swamps, trees, and grasses will all speak words

Seeking to throw the world into disorder, and truly dreadful it is.

To sweep away the arrogant bluster of the swarming Magakami

There is only the Kototama of Kimi.

In the brilliant light of the solemn living Kototama,

The Magatsuhi upon this earth shall perish.

The heavenly realm is the land of Kototama, the land of will,

The land of thought, an endless domain.

When will and thought are clouded, the hideous Magakami

Are born forth, and terrifying indeed it is heard to be.

Would that we had as our strength the water and fire light

Of the supreme great ancestral gods, whose names are awe-inspiring to utter.

According to the guidance of the Lord God you set forth on your journey,

May there be great achievements in the path of Kimi.

Leaving the eight goddesses behind in the palace,

O hero Kimi, who set forth to engender divine children.'

Umasu no Kami chants a divine poem with rich equestrian grace.

Stopping our steeds to rest with Kimi on the great plain,

The wind blowing across is also the water and fire of Kototama.

The refreshing breeze of Shinato's wind blowing upon my face

Is truly the divine water and fire.

The voices of the insects singing hidden in the shade of myriad grasses,

Without exception, are the water and fire of Kototama.

Yearning for the achievements of Kimi, the cranes of Tamamo Mountain

Align their wings and come to see us off.

From nowhere the fragrance of white plums drifts softly,

And music echoes for the journey of Kimi.

In the west-blowing wind of Tamamo Mountain, plum and cherry blossoms,

Pure petals, come dancing through the air.

Do plums bloom even high above in the cloud-bound sky?

Petals are scattering over the head of Kimi.

Pleasantly clear and serene is the vast open sky,

Beneath which I journey onward in joy.

The light of the midday moon floats whitely in the august sky,

Displaying its round form.

Even in broad daylight, far and wide across the great sky,

Large stars have begun to shine.

Halt your steed and take your leisure to rest,

For ahead upon this path await the Magatsuhi.

We shall solidify and prepare the water and fire of Kototama

That turns and pacifies the crooked deities.

The plain of the country where eightfold clouds rise, where the earth is still young,

Is the dwelling place of the hideous evil deities.

Ruled over by the deity who illuminates both moon and sun,

The realm of the west is still an infant earth.

Here and there across the infant earth of the western realm,

The rising clouds conceal the evil deities.

If you cross Hinatagaha, it is already the western land

Where the Magatsuhi dwell, the land of eternal darkness.

Now come, let us set our stirrups side by side and advance,

Enveloped ever within the light of Kimi.

Thus finishing the chanting, the hoofbeats of the steeds sounding crisp and clear, their neighing high and valiant, and the sound of the bells ringing softly, they caused [them] to advance and press forward before the verdant plain of Wakagusa.

The four pillars of Kami, with awe and treed, followed and proceeded in the august rear.

The august song of Kami Ureshiho:

'Unwittingly and unknowingly my heart grew arrogant, / and I repent of the mind that stood before my Kimi. / Is this also to be found in foolish me? / Not knowing even the radiance of my Kimi, I stood before [Him]. / Than the Kototama of the hundred Kami, / how brilliant is the living Kototama of my Kimi! / Because the water and fire of Kototama were not complete, / now I repent of my foolishness in serving before the august presence. / If I would serve by purifying the water and fire / of the vast land of Manazuru! / Not perceiving at all / the precious radiance of my Kimi, foolish am I! / Mountains, fields, and trees and grasses, I have begun to realize, / are all born from the water and fire of Kototama. / When I think that Chiyotsuruhime-no-Mikoto is also born / from the pure water and fire of Kimi, it is awe-inspiring. / When I think that [He] defiles the land of Manazuru / brought forth by my Kimi, it is fearsome. / Since my august spirit is turbid, / the water and fire of Kototama are also clouded, how shameful it is! / From today onward, while striving in the ritual service of purification, / I shall polish my heart and make the Kototama live.'

The august song of Kami Tamakiharu:

'Did I think that my Kimi of the wondrous Mitama / is indeed the Kami of the radiance of water and fire? / Ever since I heard the valiant words of my Kimi, / my soul-string has trembled. / How shameful is the heart that boasted, / "I am serving the august service of the Kami of Heaven and Earth!" / Even while being employed in the august service of the Kami of Heaven and Earth, / unwittingly and unknowingly my heart grew arrogant. / By the grace of the Lord God, morning and evening, / I was indeed one who serves the Kami. / While clasping the seeds of endless life, / I could not realize the Kimi of Kototama. / Maintaining eternal life, the wondrous Mitama / shall bring forth the country-spirit until myriad generations. / How shameful I have become, unwittingly and unknowingly / my heart grew arrogant and forgot the radiance.'

The august song of Kami Musubiai:

'Crossing upon the back of a steed, going to serve as an august attendant, / happy indeed is my body today! / Across the country-plain where both Heaven and Earth are clear and bright, / sent off by cranes and welcomed by insects. / Sent off by the many millions of wings of Manazuru, / the joy of serving in the attendants for the birth of the land! / Not thinking of myself, but for the august sake of the country, / to devote oneself I consider to be good. / The heart that thinks of oneself and cedes the country / is truly and rightly evil. / Establishing the distinction between good and evil, from today onward / I shall respectfully serve as the august attendant of the Kami. / Even if I fall at the end of the wilderness, / for the sake of the divine country it must not be begrudged. / Since to serve as the august attendant of my Kimi, / who has brought forth the land and brought forth august children, should be a pure Mitama, / if my soul is utterly clouded, / the water and fire of Kototama will also be turbid, how shameful it is! / Since there is no divine decree from my Kimi, from today onward / I shall not utter the Kototama facing the Magatsuhi.'

The august song of Kami Mumasu:

'Embraced within Heaven and Earth, advancing onward, / the august attendants of Kimi were awe-inspiring. / The land of the western direction is vast, and the eightfold rising / clouds of the cloudy sky are also turbid. / I reflect upon the greatness of the merit of Kimi, / who illuminates the clouded land of the western direction. / My Kimi has become the Kototama-Kami, / and as He shines upon the place to which He goes forth, / uniting the water and fire of Heaven and Earth and advancing onward, / there is no Kami who obstructs the journey of Kimi.'

The Kami, each chanting their songs of self-reflection, follow the august rear of Akitsuhiko-no-Mikoto across the boundless Ōnogahara with eager hearts and advance onward, which is truly awe-inspiring.

斯く歌ひ終り、駒の蹄の音カツカツと嘶き高く勇ましく、鈴の音もさやさやに、若草もゆる大野原を前に立たせ進ませ給ふ。四柱の神は恐る恐る御後方に従ひながら続かせ給ふ。

宇礼志穂の神の御歌。

『知らず識らず心傲りてわが岐美の 前に立ちたる心を悔ゆるも 愚なる吾にもあるか吾が岐美の 光も知らず前に立ちける 百神の言霊よりもわが岐美の 生言霊は輝くものを 言霊の水火完からずして吾は今 御前に仕へし愚さを悔ゆ 真鶴の国は広けし言霊の 水火を清めて仕へ奉らばや わが岐美の尊き光みながらに 気付かざりしよ愚なる吾は 山も野も木草もすべて言霊の 水火に生ると始めて覚りぬ 千代鶴姫命も岐美の清らけき 水火に生れますを思へば畏し わが岐美の生ませ給ひし真鶴の 国土を汚すと思へば恐ろし わが御魂濁らひあれば言霊の 水火も曇りてはづかしきかも

今日よりは禊の神事を励みつつ 心みがきて言霊生かさむ』

魂機張の神の御歌。

『思ひきや瑞の御霊のわが岐美は 水火の光の神にますとは わが岐美の雄健び言葉聞きしより わが魂線はをののきにけり 天地の神の御用に仕へしと 誇り居たりし心の恥づかし 天地の神の御用に使はれ居ながらも 知らず識らずに心傲りぬ 主の神の恵によりて朝夕を 神に仕ふるわが身なりしよ 果しなき生命の種を抱へつつ 言霊の岐美を覚らざりける 永久の生命保ちて瑞御霊 国魂生まさむ万世までも 恥づかしく吾なりにけり知らず識らず 心傲りて光を忘れし』

結比合の神の御歌。

『駒の背に跨り御供に仕へ行く 今日のわが身は楽しかりけり 天も地も澄みきらひたる国原を 鶴に送られ虫に迎へられつ 真鶴の幾千万の翼に送られて 国土生みの供に仕ふる嬉しさ わが為を思はず国の御為に 尽すは善とわれは思へり わが為を思ひて国を次にする 心は正しく悪なりにけり 善悪の差別を立てて今日よりは 神の御供につかへ奉らむ よしやよし荒野の果に倒るとも 神国の為には厭はざるべし 国土を生み御子生みませるわが岐美の 御供に仕ふるは清き御魂なるべきを わが魂はひたに曇れば言霊の 水火も濁りて恥づかしきかも わが岐美の神宣なければ今日よりは 曲津に向ひて言霊宣らじ』

美味素の神の御歌。

『天地の中に抱かれ進み行く 岐美の御供は畏かりけり 西方の国土ははろけし八雲立つ 雲井の空も濁らひて居り 曇りたる西方の国土を照らします 岐美の功の大さを思ふ わが岐美は言霊神にましましぬ 出で行く先に輝き給へば 天地の水火を合せて進み行く 岐美の旅路にさやる神なし』

神々は各自述懐歌をうたひながら、果しも知らぬ大野原を、顕津男の神の御後方に従ひ心いそいそ進ませ給ふぞ畏けれ。
