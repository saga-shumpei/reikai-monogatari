Chapter 9: 千代の Crane [1903]

千代の鶴〔1903〕

§ 1

Beginning with Akitsuo no Kami, Tamanohime no Kami, Ikuyohime no Kami, and the other Kamigami, when they offered the prayer of Ikukototama before the great presence of Tamanomiya, Ikuyohime no Kami now at last, as the months were fulfilled and the days had passed, peacefully gave birth to a divine child of jewel.

That which assisted in this divine work of the birth of the child was indeed the Kami of Ubudama.

The divine name of the born child is revered as Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto.

Akitsuo no Kami, rejoicing in the auspicious sign of the birth of the child, pronounces words of gratitude and divine celebration before the great presence.

Awe-inspiring and wondrous beyond measure, with pillars of the shrine solidly erected down to the bedrock of Mount Tamamoyama, and the cross-timbers reaching high into Takaamahara, before the great presence of the Ōkami of Tamanomiya, who dwells in peace, with reverence, awe, and trembling, we humbly speak. Though eighty days and nights have passed, on this auspicious day and at this auspicious hour, prospering endlessly for a thousand ages and ten thousand generations, in the true Mahoraba of the Land of True Cranes upon the summit of Mount Tamamoyama, before the great presence of the Lord, the Great Deity, the Deity of Amenominefuo, who descends and dwells in purity, we offer words of gratitude. Now, this Land of True Cranes is still spiritually young, and the morning and evening mists linger unceasingly, while even the light of the moon and sun has at times grown cloudy; yet Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto, whom you designated as the deity of the land's soul through the divine majesty of the Ikukototama, has here triumphantly raised her first divine birth cry. Therefore, placing various delicacies of sea, river, mountain, and field upon a tray with eight legs before your great presence, we pray with awe and trembling, bending down to the heaven and crouching upon the earth, that you may guard the growth of this child, and hearing and accepting this with peace and tranquility, throughout the duration of heaven and earth, may she live and flourish eternally in precious life, and serve in the divine work of the great divine mandate, thus we humbly and yearningly entreat with awe and trembling.

Ah, the feeling as if heaven and earth open in a single moment! The deity of the land's soul has now been born. Clear is the hearing of the first cry, at the dwelling of the Tamanomiya Upon Mount Tamamoyama, where sun and moon shine brightly. The deep mists that wrapped around as an eightfold fence Have cleared away entirely along with the birth cry. The thought of heaven and earth opening, like the voice of a domestic cock, Following right after the voice of the weeping child. Praying at the sacred place of Mount Tamamoyama That fortune may attend the future of Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto, The divine work bestowed by the Lord of Kami Has at last been completed, and the child is born. When I gaze upon the face of the born child, It is adorned like a true jewel or white jewel, And the two eyes, which might be compared to sun and moon, Are pure and clear through the achievements of Kami. Well-proportioned and dignified is the peak of the nose, With its two nostrils perfectly formed, And at the mouth of the pure, bright spring of jewels, Rouge is applied, and it truly yields a fragrance. Upon the dew of the red plum blossoms, the mouth of the child unfolds, In its gentleness I find myself gazing in fascination. Be that as it may, the pillar of the Land of True Cranes, Born today, how joyful it is! Celebrating the prosperity of the Land of True Cranes, Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto has been born. Born as the leader of the Land of True Cranes, How plump and flourishing Chiyotsuruhime is!

Tamanohime no Kami recites a divine poem.

Mount Tamamoyama swells upward, soaring high into the sky, At this sacred place the child has been born. Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto, who has been born, Is indeed the pillar of the Land of True Cranes. Had the achievements of Ikuyohime no Kami not existed, The deity of the land's soul would never have been born; Yet today both heaven and earth are exceptionally clear and pure, Celebrating the child who is now born. The light of sun and moon crossing the heavens is radiant, Now that the pillar of the land has been born. I, who lagged behind in the divine work of the Lord of Kami, Feel today's joy almost too great to bear. From this day forth, bowing low before the shrine of Tamanomiya, I shall serve with all truth. Praying day and night that there may be many blessings In the future days of Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto. The white plum blossoms are sending forth a wondrous fragrance, As the princess is born in this sacred place, Does the Land of True Cranes celebrate today's joy, Gathering and singing among the ever-green pines? The domestic fowl sings unceasingly, as if heard to celebrate The growth of the princess. The neighing of the white steed is heard loud and clear In the morning courtyard where the child is born. The deep mists that wrapped around the four quarters of the Land of True Cranes Have cleared away, and the sun and moon shine down. The light of the midday moon, white and clear, in the distant Sky shines down in its purity.

この御子産みの神業を助け奉りたるは、産玉の神にぞありける。

生れませる御子の御名を、千代鶴姫の命と称へ奉る。

顕津男の神は、御子の生れませる瑞祥を喜び給ひて、大御前に感謝の神嘉言を宣らせ給ふ。

『掛巻も綾に畏き、玉藻山の下津岩根に宮柱太敷立て、高天原に千木高知りて鎮まりいます玉野宮の大神の大前に、慎み敬ひ畏み畏みも白さく。八十日日はあれ共、今日の吉き日の吉き時に、千代万代と栄え果てなき、真鶴の国の貴の真秀良場玉藻の山の頂上に、清しくも天降り鎮りいます主の大御神天之峯火夫の神の大前に感謝言奉る。抑々此の真鶴の国は未だ地稚く、朝夕の御霧は時じくに立ち籠め、月日の光さへ折々に曇らひぬるを、生言霊の御稜威によりて国魂神と神定めてし千代鶴姫の命は、ここに目出度大御産声を挙げさせ給ひぬ。故此を以て大御前に海河山野の種々の美味物を八足の机代に置き足はして、御子の生ひたちを守らせ給へと、天に跼り地に蹐して恐み畏み願ぎ奉るさまを、平けく安らけく聞召し相諾ひ給ひて、天と地とのあらむ限り、たまきはる生命永久に生ひ栄えて大御依さしの神業に仕へしめ給へと、恐み畏みもこひのみ奉らくと白す。

玉野比女の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『玉藻山ふくれ上りて空高き これの聖所に御子生れましぬ 生れませる千代鶴姫の命はも 真鶴国の御柱なりける 生代比女神の功績なかりせば 国魂神は生れまさざるを 天も地も今日は一入澄みきりて 生れます御子を寿ぎにけり 天渡る月日の光もさやかなり 国津柱の生れましぬれば 主の神の神業に後れし吾にして 今日の喜びたへがてに思ふ 今日よりは玉野宮居に額づきて まことの限り仕へ奉らむ 千代鶴姫神の命の生ひ先きに 幸多かれと日夜を祈らむ 白梅の花はいみじく香るなり これの聖所に姫生れまして 真鶴は今日の喜びことほぐか 常磐の松に群れつうたへり

家鶏鳥は時じく歌ひ姫命の その生ひ立ちを寿ぐがに聞ゆ 白駒の嘶き高く聞ゆなり 御子生れませる朝の庭に 真鶴の国の四方八方包みたる 深霧晴れて月日は照らふ 昼月の光白々と久方の 空にすみきりのぞかせ給ふ』

§ 2

Ikuyohime no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Upon the sky of floating clouds, soaring high at Tamamoyama, In this holy sanctuary, the august Child has indeed been born. The divine word of Akitsuo no Kami—its water and fire congealing— As the Kunitamagami and Mikoto, He has been born into the world. The first cry of the august Child of Kunitama, as He weeps, Resounds across the heavenly expanse of Amatsumisora. The Kototama resounding across the six directions of Yomoyamo Was none other than the birth-cry of Kunitamagami. From this day forth, may the land of Manazuru also remain tranquil, And flourish in utmost peace, now and evermore. By the protection of Ubudama no Kami, the life of Chiyotsuruhime Has been safely brought forth into this world. It goes without saying that the Kamigami rejoice, yet may there be fortune even for the people of future ages through the august severity of Ubudama. O Ubudama no Kami, from this day until the ends of all generations, Protect this birth-chamber down to the furthest posterity. The Child born upon the ridge of Takayama, Whose peerless like has never been known since ancient times, is majestic indeed. When I gaze upward and contemplate the august countenance of the Child, He bears a striking resemblance to Akitsuo no Kami. How lovely is the Child born as the water and fire of Kototama embrace one another! At the very moment I first heard His birth-cry, My soul-line (tamashihi) smiled and flourished with joy. Holding boundless desires within my breast, I yearn to flourish Throughout this boundless plain of Kunihara. Morning and evening, I pray to guard the life of Chiyotsuruhime Kami And thereby govern the land. My love has piled and accumulated until it has been born forth As Chiyotsuruhime's Kunitamagami. How delightful and wondrous! At the august voice of Kunitamagami, All clouds and mists on every side have drifted away. Even the heavenly sunlight traversing the skies shines brilliantly. In this holy sanctuary fragrant with white plums, I pray that the august majesty of Kunitamagami may shine brightly Down to the ends of all future generations. Praying morning and evening to the Lord Ōkami, The reward therefor is this very joy of today. Returning to the mountain of Manazuru, I shall eternally cherish, rear, and protect Kunitamagami.

Tomiwakahiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Serving upon Mizunomitama no Kami, now here in this place, We have surely met with this wondrous joy. Governing the lands of Shinonome and Manazuru, I am filled with joy, for the Mikoto has been born. The neighing of the white steed is fierce, and the crowing of the domestic fowl Is clear and bright on this day when the august Child is born. Gathering upon the treetops of the ever-verdant pines, The voices of the cranes praise the Age of the Gods. The sound of the thousand-stringed waterfall of Tamamoyama is crisp and clear, And the blowing wind is fresh in the pure courtyard of the palace. Enduring various sorrows and sufferings in silence, Akitsuo no Kami has brought forth the august Child. When I think upon the august heart of Tamanohime, Who was made to wait eighty years, I am both joyful and sorrowful. Ikuyohime, who served as the august substitute for Tamanohime, Has indeed accomplished a wondrous deed. The sound of the waterfall reaches our ears so clear and soft, And the courtyard of the imperial dwelling is exceedingly pure.

Maruyahiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

Though I am but one who guards the Mikoto of Tamatehime, I have been blessed to witness the birth of Chiyotsuruhime. Maruyahiko Kami has manifested His august spirit And followed hither in the train of Mizunomitama. From this day, having fulfilled the task of birthing the Child, I shall hasten back to the mountain of Mikasa. While the myriad deities of Mikasa Mountain await my return With longing hearts, I gaze up at the sky. Ah, come what may, I, being a manifestation, must return. O Mizunomitama, remain in peace and health! I who declare these things before the presence of the myriad deities Am none other than the august spirit of Maruyahiko Kami. The august spirit of Maruyahiko Kami has divided into right and left, And has watched over and guarded this place. Ah, come what may, I must return to the mountain of Mikasa. O myriad deities, remain safe and sound! Morning and evening, I shall pray in the mountain of Mikasa For the future path of Chiyotsuruhime Mikoto.

Thus having finished chanting his verse of retrospection, Maruyahiko no Kami straddles the back of the white steed, descends the mountain of Tamamo, and dashes forth like lightning amidst clouds and mist, majestic indeed.

Kuninakahiko no Kami chants a sacred poem.

The distant heavens have cleared, and the rugged earth Has grown pure and clear, shining radiantly. With the feeling that even heaven and earth are opening on this day, The birth of Chiyotsuruhime is a most felicitous event. Born as the pillar of the land of Manazuru, The august Child of Mizunomitama is truly lovely. Being beautiful, majestic, and gentle, O precious Child born as Kunitamagami, Even the plains of Kunihara surrounding Tamamoyama Have cleared of mist on this auspicious day. In the cries of the cranes gathering upon the ever-verdant pines, There echoes the resonance of a thousand ages. Pure indeed are the voices of the domestic fowl crowing In the sky of Shinonome on this day the Child is born. Particularly magnificent is the neighing of the spirited steed, Blending voices for a thousand and ten thousand generations, Praying that the life of the precious Child may endure long, Named with the august name of Chiyotsuruhime Mikoto, The ruler of the land of Manazuru has been born. With awe and reverence, morning and evening, we shall serve Him.

斯く述懐歌を歌ひ終り、圓屋比古の神は白駒の背に跨りて玉藻の山を下り、一目散に雲を霞と駆け出で給ふぞ雄々しけれ。

Fogs of morning and evening rising in all directions, let the dimly darkening land of plain clear up from today. Fogs rising like steam in all directions, how endless is the plain of our land! Spreading and spreading boundlessly without end, O Miko who rulest the young land of plain, from today onward, serve before the august front of Hime no Mikoto and proceed to develop the land.'

Ureshiho no Kami chants an august poem.

'We have listened purely to the birth cry, our hearts simply dance and we find it hard to endure. Could this be the height of joy, as the Kunitamagami of Manazuru has been born? What could compare to this happiness, there is nothing at all, we only feel it to be awe-inspiring. The horses of the sun and moon traversing heaven also stop their steps, to gaze upon the divine Miko who is born. Do we celebrate the birth of Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto? Today the scent of plum blossoms is especially fragrant. The voice of the Karabinga singing at the tips of the white plum blossoms is also clear. To the heavens-piercing high peaks, the posture of the Miko who raises her birth cry is sublime. O Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto, where the white jewels thread across and the red jewels shine brilliantly! Though the cords of the red jewels shine, the adornment of the white Miko was truly majestic. Preserving life, which spans but a temporary jewel-like existence, eternally, rule over the land of Manazuru.'

Minashihiko no Kami chants an august poem.

'The heavenly sun also descends to this sacred place, illuminating this auspicious day today. The boat of Tsukiyomi traversing heaven has its white jewel light shining pure and clear beyond the white clouds. The curtain of white clouds is torn and the skin of the blue clouds shines deeply. Could it be the pure and clear august spirit of the Lord God? The expression of this noble Miko is pure. The jewel-seaweed mountain of Adahiki is high, but it does not reach the height of the Miko's achievements. We see the majesty of the awe-inspiring Akitsuwo no Kami in the face of the born Miko. It is indeed the noble Miko born when the flame of Ikudayohime's love flared up and solidified. Since the heavenly realm is a world of love and goodness, how could we fear the thoughts of love? Though there are many loves called love in the heavenly realm, such an example is the very first.'

Tamadama no Kami chants an august poem.

'Hearing in detail the birth cry of Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto, my heart suddenly has grown calm. Peacefully and exceedingly serenely she gave birth, I think upon the blessings of Ikudayo no Hime. The pivot of the land of Manazuru, Ikudayohime's true love has caused the Miko to be born. The Miko born by the binding together of fire and water with fire and water was none other than Kunitamagami.'

Tamahatanohiko no Kami chants an august poem.

'Protect eternally the life that spans but a temporary jewel-like existence, the Miko who is born as Kunitamagami. Extending life like a jewel cord in the heavenly realm, rejuvenating, we shall serve the Way. Upon the summit of the eight-cloud-rising jewel-seaweed mountain, we hear the divine voice opening heaven and earth. The steed neighs as if to sing of the blessing that the Miko is born. The voice of Manazuru is heard sounding valiantly, upon the very tips of the ever-verdant pine branches. The Karabinga singing untimely and domestic fowls crying, this sacred mountain is the august dwelling of the Kami. Standing in the pure courtyard of Tamanomiya, today is the day we celebrate the coming forth of Kunitamagami. May the adornment of white plum blossoms eternally be blessed, O Chiyotsuruhime no Mikoto! O Chiyotsuruhime, dwell in blessedness with the moon and sun lodged in the evergreen pine branches.'

Yuihiai no Kami chants an august poem.

'Pure is the figure of Kunitamagami born by binding together fire and water with fire and water. Binding water and water to the august spirit of Ikudayohime, the Miko born by binding them together is indeed one who served as the Kami of binding together, revering the divine word of the Lord God. The difficult love called love will also be easily accomplished by Yuihiai no Kami. Binding together mountain and sea, rising in flames, trees, grasses, and steam moisten the age of the Kami. Kunitamagami has been born; one can imagine the joy of Ikudayohime no Kami. Ikudayohime, who once manifested majestic fury, has grown calm and given birth to the Miko. Even when I recall the Hime who made the waves of Tamanoko dance and caused wild winds to blow in sport, now I find it wondrous that she gave birth to the white-jewel Miko as a sport of love. If you yearn for the Kami, like Ikudayohime, carry it through unswervingly until the Miko is born.'

Umesu no Kami chants an august poem.

'To the august spirit of the born Kunitamagami, we shall present beautiful tastes. In the tastes granted by Umesu no Kami, the Kami of the four quarters surely submit. If an august spirit lacks taste, how could this land of plain ever be governed? All things in the heavenly realm without exception are those that have taste and flourish.'

The Kami each chanted their personal reflections, and as evening approached, each entered their sleeping quarters and rested.

四方八方に朝夕の霧立ちて 小暗き国原今日より晴れなむ 四方八方に霧なす湯気の立昇る わが国原の果もなきかな 果しなく拡ごり拡ごり限りなき 稚国原を知食す御子よ 今日よりは姫の命の御尾前に 仕へて国土を拓きゆくべし』

宇礼志穂の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『産声を清しく聞きぬ吾はただ 心をどりてたへがたく思ふ 喜びの極みなるかも真鶴の 国魂神は生れましける 嬉しさは何にたとへむ物もなし かたじけなしと思ふのみなる 天渡る月日の駒も歩み止めて みそなはすらむ生れし貴御子を 千代鶴姫命の生れまし寿ぐか 今日は梅ケ香殊に芳し 白梅の花の梢に鳴きたつる 迦陵頻伽の声も冴えたり 天そそるこれの高根に産声を 挙げたる御子の姿たふとし 白玉のいすみ渡らひ赤玉の 輝き給ふ千代鶴姫の命よ 赤玉は緒さへ光れど白玉の 御子のよそほひ尊かりけり たまきはる生命を永久に保ちまして 真鶴の国を知食しめしませ』

美波志比古の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『天津日もこれの聖所に降りまして 今日の吉き日を照らしましつつ 天渡る月読の舟は白玉の 光澄みきらへり白雲のあなたに 白雲のとばり破れて青雲の 肌ふかぶかと輝きにけり 主の神の澄みきらひたる御霊かも この貴御子の面ざし清しも 足引の玉藻の山は高けれど 御子の功の高きに及ばず 顕津男の神の御稜威の尊さを 生れます御子の面に見るかな 生代比女恋の炎の燃え立ちて 固まり生れし貴の御子かも 天界は愛と善との世にしあれば いかで恐れむ恋の思ひを 天界に恋てふ恋は多けれど かかるためしは始めなりける』

産玉の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『千代鶴姫命の産声まつぶさに 聞きしゆ心とみに和めり 平けくいと安らけく産みませし 生代の比女の幸を思へり 真鶴の国の要よ生代比女の 誠の恋は御子を生ませり 水火と水火結び合せに生れませし 御子は国魂神にましける』

魂機張の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『たまきはる生命を永久に守るべし 国魂神と生れます御子を 玉の緒の生命ながらへ天界に 若返りつつ道に仕へむ 八雲立つ玉藻の山の頂上に 天地開く神声聞くかな 駿馬は御子の生れます幸ひを うたふが如く嘶きて居り 真鶴の声勇ましく聞ゆなり 常磐の松のこずゑこずゑに 迦陵頻伽時じく歌ひ家鶏の鳴く この神山は神の御舎よ 玉野宮の清庭に立ちて国魂の 神の出でまし寿ぐ今日かな 白梅の花の装ひ永久に 幸くあれませ千代鶴姫の命よ 千代鶴姫堅磐常磐の松ケ枝に 月日宿して幸くいませよ』

結比合の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『水火と水火結び合せて生れませる 国魂神のすがた清しも 生代比女瑞の御霊に水火合せ 結び合せて生れます御子はも 主の神の神言畏み吾はしも 結び合せの神と仕へし むつかしき恋てふ恋もやすやすと 遂げさせ給はむ結比合の神は 山と海結び合せて燃え上る 木草も湯気も神世をうるほす 国魂の神生れましぬ生代比女の 神のよろこび思はるるかも 一度は雄猛び給ひし生代比女も なごみて御子を生ませ給ひぬ 玉野湖の波ををどらせ荒風を 吹かせすさびし比女思ひ出すも 今となりて白玉の御子を生みましし 恋のすさびをあやしと思ふ 神を恋ふるならば生代の比女のごと つらぬき通せ御子を生むまで』

美味素の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『生れませる国魂神の御魂に 美し味はひおくり奉らむ 美味素の神のさづくる味はひに 四方の神々従ろひこそすれ 味はひのなき御魂ならば如何にして この国原の治まるべきやは 天界のよろづのものはことごとく 味はひありて栄ゆくものなる』

神々は各自述懐を歌ひ給ひて、黄昏になりければ、各自寝所に入りて休ませ給ひぬ。
