Chapter 11: Soul 反し [1905]

魂反し〔1905〕

§ 1

The Lord Ōmotoakitsuo, wishing to invite the mitama of the Goddess Yukuehime, chanted the eight kinds of sacred miuta and performed the Chinkonsai, and these were His sacred miuta.

Thus, as He chanted the sacred miuta of Shōkon, the shinrei of the Goddess Yukuehime responded and arrived instantly; a spring breeze blew, a divine fragrance permeated, the evergreens swayed back and forth and left and right, and even to the eyes of other deities, Her misugata could be clearly beheld. Thereupon the Lord Akitsuo caused His sacred miuta to be chanted.

The Goddess Yukuehime, who had departed into kamusa, has manifested her precious misugata before the great presence.

Compared to those days when She was with me, her misugata is now all the more noble.

Celebrating this very day when we have given birth to four children, I have invited the Hime's mitama.

Even though You dwell well in the Reikai, I pray You protect the Takaamahara which I create.

Ever since the Goddess Yukuehime departed into kamusa, I have re-established my heart.

Did Your mitama protect my surroundings, that I have served in the divine work free from trouble until this day?

Morning and evening my spirit, which yearned for You, was too busy with the divine work to look back.

As the land of Manazuru was gradually born, I thought upon Your achievements and thus invited You.

Recalling the things of days gone by, I have invited the Hime's soul into my sincere heart.

The shinrei of the Goddess Yukuehime chanted a sacred miuta with gentle grace.

Everything is according to the august will of the Lord Ōkami.

Even now that I serve as a mitama, compelled by the sincere heart with which the Mizu-no-mitama invited me, I have descended to this place.

Parting the overlapping clouds of Izumo where the eight clouds rise, I have descended to the mountain of Tamamo.

Even though this physical body should be swallowed and lost by the great serpent, the thread of my soul's life is eternal.

My soul's thread was washed in the middle waterfall, leaving my body this day free of sin and impurity.

Living and living on in the Yūkai, I shall protect the great divine work as a Mizu-no-mitama.

Morning and evening through the future days of Princess Chizuru, I shall protect her, thinking of her as the mitama of Kimi.

Escaping from this hopeless Akitsuyo world, I flourish in the heavenly realm of eternal life.

Protecting the mitama of the Eighty Hime deities, I shall assist in the glorious divine work of the Lord Kami.

Now then, I must part the cloud-paths and return to the heavenly Takamiya where the Lord Kami dwells.

Though I have no heart that yearns to linger, as I part and go forth, how precious this fleeting moment is to me.

The sacred miuta of the Lord Akitsuo.

Within the endless Takaamahara, You are the noble One who united fire and water with fire and water.

Having descended a distant heavenly road, now instantly I feel a pang of sadness to see You returning home.

Now that I have accomplished the glorious divine work of the Lord Kami, I too wish to ascend to the heavenly realm.

I have become one who deeply yearns for the august presence of the Lord Kami in the distant heavenly Takamiya.

The land of Manazuru has gradually been born.

What shall I do about the troubles that lie ahead in the future?

O mitama of Yukuehime, help me, who creates the land amidst encountering various troubles.

Revealing her light misugata, the Goddess Yukuehime parted the clouds of the august sky, mounted a divine steed, and leisurely departed toward the other side of the Takahina Palace in heaven.

如衣比女の神は軽き御姿を現しながら、御空の雲を押し別け神馬に跨り、いういうとして、天津高日の宮のあなたをさして帰らせ給ひぬ。

§ 2

Tamanohime no Kami, worshipping Her gentle and divine Misugata, chanted this Miuta.

Awe-inspiring is the Mitama of Yukue no Hime no Kami, Who fully proclaims the state of Shibikiu! Even to look up at It is dazzling, And the form of Yukue no Hime is noble. Realizing the Kamikuni where even the distinction of Ikishini does not exist, I enjoy the Miyo of Kami. Even the Kami who had died have manifested Their forms, And awe-inspiring is the Miyo where Kototama are proclaimed. Purifying oneself in the jewel spring morning and evening, The Misugata reflected in the cleansed eyes— Realizing the reason why the Tamashihi lives and works Until the end of Ikuyorozuyo! Though I am aged this year, when I think of the youth of the Inochi Of the Tamashihi, I am filled with joy. Ever through Ikikaha and Shinikaha, serving in the Miyo of Kami, I shall protect the Kuni eternally. Yukue no Hime, who was buried in the belly of the Orochi at Nakatsutaki, Was actually living! When I think of the sorrowful Mikokoro of Akitsuo no Kami, Tears fall unbidden. Thinking it to be a parting as long as a chicken's tail, Kimi has met Yukue no Hime once again.

Ikuyohime no Kami chanted this Miuta.

Beholding the Misugata of Yukue no Hime, who had undergone Kamusa, Right before my eyes now, I am filled with wonder. Though I have heard from of old that such precedents exist, To witness it now for the first time, I am indeed startled. Serving in the distant Takama-ga-hara of Heaven, Pure is the Misugata of Yukue Hime no Kami. Surmising the Mikokoro of Akitsuo no Kami, I find myself overcome with tears. Living and living, serving in the heavenly palace, To think upon the blessings of Yukue no Hime— Because it is the Kamikuni of Aizen, surely She has been protected By the profound heart of Su no Kami. Blessed indeed is the body that preserves the Inochi Of the thread of jewels in the Kamikuni where there is no Ikishini.

The Miuta of Toomio no Kami.

To the pure courtyard of the upper bedrock of Tamamoyama, O Hime who hast descended and proclaimed the divine words! The Mizumitama of the Kami who had dwelt in Kakuriyo Has been revived by the living Kototama. O Hime no Kami who preservest the Tamakiwaru Inochi eternally in Heaven And servest in the divine work! Filled with the light and virtue of Aizen, How gentle are the forms of the deities and mortals of the Kamikuni! Having passed through the divine work of giving birth to the Miko, and having ascended To the Kamikuni, the forms of the mortals are truly alive. O the loving heart of Yukue Hime no Kami Who hast descended to this pure courtyard of Tamamoyama! O the valiant heart of the Kami of the Mizumitama, Sweeping away all attachments!

The Miuta of Ureshiho no Kami.

Purifying one's body upon the ridge of the foot-dragging mountain, The wonder of meeting the mortal who had died! Thinking Him to be a dead mortal, yet beholding right before my eyes His living Misugata, how I revere it! Seeing the endlessness of the living Inochi of the jewel thread, I rejoice at having been born in Kamikuni. In the felicitous birth of the land of Manazuru, Yukue Hime no Kami who must have descended— Ikuyohime no Kami must surely be overjoyed. Giving birth safely to the child of the Kuninotokotachi no Kami, O the joy of the birth of the jewel divine child at the summit of Tamamoyama! The Kami who had passed away have also descended to the mountain And are rejoicing over the birth of the Miko.

The Miuta of Mimashihiko no Kami.

Has a divine bridge been spanned even across the distant sky, Since Yukue Hime no Kami has journeyed to and fro? Crossing the floating bridge of distant heaven, The Goddess returning to Takama-ga-hara— The land of Manazuru is gradually solidifying, And even the dead Kami come to celebrate. This limitlessness of felicity—the Kuninotokotachi no Kami Keeps the birth cry clear and bright! At the proclamation of the Mizumitama Tamafaeshi, Yukue Hime no Kami has come soaring across the heavens. Now once more I have realized The power of the majesty of Kototama through the Norito of Tamafaeshi; How marvelous are the living Kototama Of the Kami administrators whose hearts like the multilayered liver are upright!

The Miuta of Tamutama no Kami.

The Kami of Kakuriyo have also come to Tamamoyama And are celebrating the day the Miko was born. How wondrous it is that even the Kami who had already passed away Have descended and are praying for the Kuni. When I reflect upon the painful Mikokoro of Akitsuo no Kami, My heart can scarcely bear it, Yet the youthful Kuni is solidifying one after another, And even the Kami of Kakuriyo have descended to dwell here.

The Miuta of Tamahibari no Kami.

Oh, the sublimity of the divine words of the deities and mortals who stretch the soul! Flourishing in the unity of Ikishini without end, The body of the mortal who appeared as the Lord of the universe Shall never perish unto eternity. Blessed is the mortal who preserves the eternal Inochi and serves In the divine work of Kamikuni! Proclaiming the eight kinds of divine words for Chinkon, Thou didst summon the dead mortals; Since it is a Kuni where Kototama shine brightly from heaven and aid us, Such a precedent was bound to be. In this mountain where Chiyozuruhime no Mikoto is born, How clear is the voice of the crane singing! Until thousands and tens of thousands of years, O Hime no Mikoto, Remain in tranquility and protect the Kuni.
