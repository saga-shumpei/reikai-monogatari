Chapter 16: The Episode of Crane の訣別 (Part V) [1910]

鶴の訣別（五）〔1910〕

§ 1

At this time, Akitsuo no Kami, taking the opportunity of the completion of His divine work, was peacefully descended from Mount Tamamoyama, accompanied by all the Kami. Thereupon, Tamanohime no Kami, unable to bear the loneliness, squatted down before the great threshold of Tamanomiya, finished chanting the Kamigoto, and quietly intoned a Miuta.

Akitsuo no Kami, having finished the divine work of Kuniu and Mikō, Departed to establish the age of the Kami; How nostalgic is the retreating figure Of the Mizumitama that went forth! The light of the shining Tsukihi, too, somehow Was lonely, for lack of Kimi; The Mizumitama that descended from the mountain of Takachiho, His Misugata was so majestic. Kimi went forth to solidify the southern Kuni, Now Kimi is gone, and the Shiraume are scattering; The spring sun quietly deepens, and the loneliness Of the Tamamoyama where Natsugusa sprouts. Maruyahiko no Kami has returned to the root Of Mount Mikasa, increasing the loneliness yet more; Kami are born as He tours the Yashimaguni completely, Oh Kimi, how tender and yet how cold you are! Even the words naming you as my Kimi faltered on my lips, Not even a single word of farewell did you utter; The Misugata of the awe-inspiring Kami floated before my eyes, Making me long for you all the more and grow ever more lonely.

Since Kimi's Misugata appeared upon Tamamoyama, A hundred days already seem to have passed; How nostalgic is Kimi, bearing A adornment resembling the flowers of the Shiraume! Though the oath of the Lord God is weighty, Growing old through the years while waiting endlessly; Touring the west, east, south, and north, Serving in the work of creating Kami— Counting the days and passing the months, as my Kimi Tours the lands of the four directions? Though there is neither right nor left pledge, thinking of Kimi's Misugata made me feel such longing; I grieve for my own body, which has no way to bring together Fire and water, fire and water, to give birth to a child. Though flowers bloom in various ways, if the bed of fragrance Of the Shiraume harbors the seed of the Lord, The plums fall, the cherry blossoms scatter, and the summer day Grows ever deeper, and the grass ever thicker; Entangled by the cord of wondrous ties, Though you called me your spouse, water and fire did not meet.

The Lord God is the guiding Kami who will save me, How joyful it is to hear that He sends Him down from heaven! Living all alone, only all alone in the pure courtyard, Though I pray for the age of the Kami, how utterly lonely it is; In a world where Tsukihi are set side by side as two, I live all alone in my solitude. The darkness of the nukubadama presses upon my heart; In that fleeting moment when I parted from Kimi, Thinking upon the coldness of this day of parting, When my heart felt it had no way to meet you ever again. Though the Kami of binding ties exists, the years grow old, And it has all been to no avail for me. Watching the rich adornment of Kimi depart, My tears welled up anew. As I watch the floating clouds drift, I ponder On this fleeting world of the path I walk; Tamanooka swells with the Kototama of the Mizumitama, And the loneliness only intensifies. Even the scenic view of Tamamoyama Makes me feel resentful today.

Where is Kimi, my spouse, now? In the great plain, He must be parting the ugly weeds and plying his whip. Yearning for Kimi, who is alone in heaven and earth, I live to see this long parting. The sound of insects, too, sounds all the more sorrowful, Without the tears in my eyes ever drying; Realizing the heart of Kimi, who made no distinctions, I secretly harbored a lingering grievance. Restraining the soul-threads of my foolish self, The painfulness of the moment when I greeted you with a smile. Ikudayohime no Kami is the child of Kuninotokano Kami, How easily she gave birth! If Ikudayohime had not been young, the true crane Kuninotokano Kami could not have been born. Rejoicing in the merits of Ikudayohime no Kami, Why does this resentment well up in my heart? Ah, how terrifying things are, this love, this heart, Since there is no distinction between good and bad; Could my heart have grown so confused, envying The precious merits of Ikudayohime?

The blessing of the command of Chiyozuruhime, Who was born by uniting fire and water with Kimi, my spouse, Though she is not a child conceived in my own womb, Oh, she has become my own child, Kuninotokano Kami! Until the morning when the child of Kuninotokano grows up, Ikudayohime will surely nurture her. The blessing of the manner in which Ikudayohime Will serve as the foster mother of Kuninotokano Kami! Though I hold the heart of the pine tree of the Tokiwagi, I find myself fading in color little by little; Ashamed am I, thinking that even the tranquil heart of spring Clouds over because of love. Affection and longing grew stronger, and the words Trembling upon the lips of Kimi, my spouse, were distressing. We parted without having the leisure To talk of the heart shattering into a hundred and thousand pieces. With no words to answer the Lord God, I forgave the enemy-Kami of love; Awakening, seeing, lying down, and pondering, I grieve Over myself, who have no child, on Tamamoyama.

§ 2

Even as she thus chanted her poem, swaying the ever-verdant pine treetops of Tamamoyama back and forth and to and fro, parting the cloud-paths into the pure precincts of the palace of Tamanomiyai, two deities majestically descended from heaven, and standing close beside Tamanohime-no-kami, they chanted a divine poem.

I indeed am the deity of soul-binding, who have descended here by the divine command of the Lord Ōkami. The deity of the middle pillar has descended, wishing to save you, in awe of the divine command of the Lord God.

Tamanohime-no-kami, at once rejoicing and wondering, turned reverently toward the two deities and chanted a poem.

Has the prayer of my morning and evening been fulfilled? How wondrous that the revered deities have appeared! Hearing that the deity of the middle pillar has descended, my soul-string is reviving. If the deity of soul-binding descends to this land, my divine work will surely become easy. In the loneliness of my spouse Kimi setting out on a journey, I uttered repetitious lamentations before the divine presence. If the divine command of the two deities were to reach my ears, what a feeling that I would vanish! Being a goddess, as expected, perhaps I could not help repeating such things over and over again. Now for the first time, I have come to feel ashamed of my own body, though I am one who serves the deities.

Here the deity of soul-binding chants a poem.

Now that the land of Manazuru has gradually settled, I have descended to save you. Tamanohime, dwell in peace of heart; your sincere heart has reached heaven. The Lord God has perceived your sincere heart and sent me down to aid your divine work. How could the deity of Toomio alone govern the land of Manazuru, which is so vast? From this day forth, within the pure precincts of the palace of Tamanomiyai, serving you, I will assist you. It has become a blessed age of the gods, as the Lord God from time to time descends upon the divine mountain of Tamamo.

The deity of Nakatsuhashira chants a poem.

As the middle pillar of the vast plain of Manazuru, manifesting as a deity, I have descended. Awe-struck by the stern Kototama of the Lord God, I descended with the intent to govern. The sincere prayers of the deity of Akitsuwo have been granted by the Lord Ōkami. Through the correctness of the sacred fire and water of the deity of Akitsuwo, I have descended from the Shibi Palace. How tender are the trees and grasses of the land of Manazuru, newly created and solidified! How wise is Tamanohime, who, keeping the divine command of the Lord God, has waited patiently for so long! The deity of the country's soul has been born; it is not the child of Ikumusubi-hime, but your own child. Recognizing it as the child born from your womb, show your grace to the child of the country's soul. From this day forth, traveling around the land of Manazuru, I will assist your divine work. The deity of Toomio is the ruler of all; you are the ruler of the palace of Tamanomiyai. Serving eternally in the palace of Tamanomiyai, protect the deity of the country's soul. Traveling around Mikasayama and Manazuruyama, we shall open up and protect every corner of the land. When the plains of the land of Manazuru are thoroughly solidified, I shall return to the august Takamiyahime of heaven. Acting for the deity of Akitsuwo, I have now descended to solidify the land. The deity of Tatakumi traveled here and there, and when did he conceal his form? Through the achievements of the deity of Tatakumi, the land of Manazuru is being opened up to every nook and corner.

A-ka-sa-ta-na Ha-ma-ya-ra-wa Ga-za-da-ba-pa With the blessing of the living Kototama, the land of Manazuru has been born. The deity of the country's soul has been born healthily, for a thousand and eight thousand reigns. The prosperous age of the gods, together with the thousand-year age of Manazuru, and along with the sun and moon, shall remain unshakeable. The pure precincts of the country's palace, soaring ever higher above the cloud-wells, illuminate the light of the Lord God in all directions. I, by the divine command of the Lord God, have manifested as the middle pillar. Together with the deity of soul-binding, we shall forever and ever protect this sacred place and elaborately unfold the glory of the Lord God.

Through countless hundreds of millions of years, at last separating us, how venerable is today's divine work to solidify the imperial land, the great land of Yamato! How awe-inspiring is today's divine work! Ah, how truly divine they are! How venerable is the majestic authority of the Kototama!
