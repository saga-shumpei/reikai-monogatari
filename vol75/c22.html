Chapter 22: Pure Clean 潔白 [1916]

清浄潔白〔1916〕

§ 1

Akitsuwo no Kami, whose body and spirit were in perfect enman, was rich in reigi, rich in jiai, and rich in the way of fūga; thus, wherever he went, encountering things and moved by circumstances, he chanted miuta. Akitsuwo no Kami, now crossing the Hinatagaha and intending to build and solidify the western kuni where akuma infested, troubled his mikokoro, recalled the circumstances of the Yahashira no Himegami and Yasohimegami residing in the palace of Takachiho, and while sinking for a while into tears of hitan, chanted miuta.

Hereupon Akitsuwo no Kami descended and stood in the flow of the Hinatagaha, and when he performed the sacred ritual of misogi, the yahashira no kamigami also leaped into the swift rapids, thinking not to be left behind, and floating and sinking while chanting the Tianjin Norito, performed the sacred ritual of misogi.

Akitsuwo no Kami and the yahashira kami gradually climbed up the bank, declared, 'Our feelings have become pure and clear,' and with quiet hearts chanted miuta.

§ 2

As the Kunitsukami joyfully flourish, illuminated by the divine light of the crystal,

Without bathing in the divine radiance of the sun and moon for grass and trees,

How could the young lands of the earth possibly thrive?

Starting today, we have performed purification in the Hinatagaha falling from the peaks of Takateru,

Though I am born of the U sound of the Lord God,

I had failed to grasp the preciousness of purification.

Serving day and night in the sacred service of purification,

I think to bring life to the lands of Nishikata.'

The Kami Ōmichishirio chants a sacred poem.

'Though I am the Kami Ōmichishirio,

Neglecting the sacred service of purification,

The great path opened by the Kamunagara Kami

Was indeed the essential service of purification.

By purifying, my body and my soul have grown clear,

The ugly Magatsumi shall no longer assault us.

Emerging to give birth to lands and bring forth Miko,

The radiant Mizu no Mitama was indeed light itself!

Even to gaze upon it has become dazzling.

The Kami of the light of Mizu no Mitama,

Upon the manifestation of such shining, glowing Ikigami,

What is there left to lament?

The lands of Nishikata, which had continued to lament day and night,

Shall now be revived.

I too, morning and evening, while teaching the great path,

Had still not known purification.

There is nothing in the Tenkai that compares to the sacred service of purification,

Surpassing all other sacred works.

The clouds that enshroud the skies of the lands of Nishikata

Shall also scatter in the sacred service of purification.

Such preciousness of the sacred service of purification,

I had never realized until this day.

Both my spirit and my soul have grown pure,

Ever since I performed purification in the rapids of the Hayakaha.

The Kami Ushihagi chants a sacred poem.

'Though I had been lingering about this region,

Being dimmed on account of Magatsumi,

Without knowing the Kamunagara service of purification,

How foolish I was to try and govern!

For one who purifies and proclaims the living Kototama,

There is no means for the ugly Magatsumi to trespass.

From this day forth, teaching and passing on the Kamunagara

Sacred service of purification to the Kunitsukamitachi,

The near mountains have already turned green.

By the virtue of the light of Kimi's purification,

Magatsumi becomes green mountains, becomes marshes,

Becomes boulders, and lurks in hiding.

The Kami of light who descended from the distant Takama-ga-hara of heaven

Is truly present right here.

To stand before the light of Mizu no Mitama while holding a clouded heart is painful.

Though countless flowers bloom in the mountains and fields,

Their colors fade amidst the revelry of distortion.

The voices of insects also grew faint by degrees,

Tormented by the water and fire of Magatsumi.

From this day forth, the singing voices of birds, the voices of insects,

And the echo of the wind shall ring crystal clear.

Though the Kalavinka sings out of season, in the lands of Nishikata

It is but the sound of destruction.

From this day forth, the singing voice of the Kalavinka

Shall grow ever purer and be revived.

The lands of Nishikata, where the shadows of the sun and moon traversing the sky cannot be distinguished,

Smitten by the chilling mountain wind blowing drearily,

Have half of all their trees and grasses withered.

From this day forth, down to every single leaf of grass,

All shall be revived by the light of Kimi.

Even the dewdrops of Tsukiyomi's grace from this day forth

Shall descend abundantly upon the grasses and trees.

The Kami Usuzukunoo chants a sacred poem.

'As I performed purification and plunged to the riverbed, burdened by the heaviness of my body,

How I suffered, nearly drowning!

One after another, the powers of purification manifested,

And my body became light and thoroughly crystal clear.

I was astounded at the depth of the grime of the spirit

That had made the river water muddy as it flowed.

If it be thus, then the dwelling places of fleas and lice

Vanish without a trace, O crystal spirit!

Like crystal, down to my soul and my body,

Shone brightly once purification was completed.'

The Kami Notori chants a sacred poem.

'In the rushing, flowing waters, the divine soul of the Kami Notori

Has become pure.

Born in the lands of Nishikata, which were dimmed by the water and fire of Magatsumi,

I too was clouded.

The flow of the Hinatagaha, where even the gravel at the riverbed shines,

Is pure—is it not Mizu no Mitama?

The face of the gazing Mizu no Mitama

Shines even brighter than the face of the moon.

Since it is my Kimi who appeared as the spirit of the Kami Tsukiyomi,

It is only natural that He shines brightly.

The opposite shore of the Hinatagaha is the radiant land of light

Brought forth by Kimi, home of the white cranes.

From this day forth, let each and every one purify themselves

And illuminate the lands of Nishikata, so I think.

The thousand-fold boulders standing here and there in the great moor north of this place

Must surely be Magatsumi.

The thousand-fold boulders that obstructed the path I came by

Were indeed the incarnations of the eighty Magatsumi.

Because my soul was clouded, I came hither

Without knowing the boulders were incarnations of Magatsumi.'

§ 3

Looking back, the huge boulders of Chibiki block only the path where I am to go. According to the teachings of my Kimi, in the sacred ritual of purification (misogi), I shall make my Tamashihi shine as I go. When I climb to the bank after performing the misogi, both my Karatama and Mitama feel lightness and strength. Having been born into this Aizen Tenkai, if I do not perform the misogi, I would instantly grow cloudy. Although I am a Kami, yet acting in accordance with the divine will (kamunagara), how negligent I was of the sacred ritual of misogi! The pure stream of Hinatagaha teaches the Kunitsukami to perform the misogi. What a foolish one I was, even while facing this Sunagare morning and evening! What a feeling as if both Ame and Kuni open up all at once! At that very moment when the misogi is finished, the land in the nishikata, where ugly clouds (shikogumo) rise up on all sides, shall from now on be illuminated by Tsukihi."

The Kami Hatsumusubi chants a sacred poem (miuta):

"My heart has revived, my Karatama has also become light; in the sacred ritual of misogi, I have for the first time realized the ritual of performing misogi. It is a sacred ritual that washes away a hundred sins and transgressions; washing and purifying sins and impurities, I now for the first time know the divine work (kamuwaza) of Musubi. When I dive into the minasoko while performing misogi, large and small fish peck and circle around my Karatama. Large and small fish surround my person, pecking at the dirt of my Karatama. Enduring and bearing the suffering at the minasoko, I caused the fish to take away the sins of my Mitama. My skin has become pure white, and the dirt has become food for the fish tribes and disappeared. Even the riverbed has grown bright, as the Mizu-no-Mitama shone and performed the misogi. Thus even the Kami of light, in accordance with the divine will, serves in the sacred ritual of misogi! My body, which has grown cloudy, I wish to purify with eternal misogi, and at least wash away the dirt of my Mitama."

"Though my body is devoid of light, through morning and evening misogi, my Mitama shall become clear and bright."

The Kami Aimiwo chants a sacred poem:

"Diving into the depths of the Hinatagaha water, you have accomplished the sacred ritual of misogi! How noble is the sacred ritual of misogi, which washes away all impurities of Tenkai! Born into the living Kototama of the sound 'U', when did I too become clouded? Realizing that the Mitama grows cloudy instantly if not polished, I have understood by serving in the misogi. My eyes have become pure; mountains and rivers now proudly have clear colors. My ears have become sharp; even the sounds of insects are heard clearly after finishing the misogi. My nose has also grown transparent; the fragrances of a hundred flowers have surely become pure. The water and fire of Kototama have also become pure, through the precious efficacy of the sacred ritual of misogi. Both Ame and Kuni have become pure, since the dirt of Karatama and Mitama was washed away. Come now, following the Kimi of light, let us advance toward the dwelling place of Magatsumi."

"If we pacify the Kami with words and build a Kuni filled and overflowing with brilliant light, even the eightfold black clouds that envelop the vast sky will scatter and disappear in the light of Kimi. The land of the nishikata will shine from this time forth, and the spirits of Magatsumi must also submit."

Thus the Kami each finished their misogi, and praising its efficacy, following behind the Kami Akitsuo, with Kashiwagi Forest as their landmark, they set forth proudly riding side by side in high spirits to conquer the Magatsumi of Suuyatogoru.

かへりみれば千引の巌ケ根わが行かむ 道の行手をのみふさぎたる わが岐美の教へ給ひし禊の神事に わが魂線を輝かしゆかむ 禊して岸にのぼれば気魂も 神魂も軽さ強さを覚ゆる 愛善のこの天界に生れ来て 禊せざれば忽ち曇らむ 神にある吾なりながら惟神 禊の神事をなほざりにせしよ 日南河の清き流れは国津神に 禊をせよと教ふるものを 愚なる吾なりにけり朝夕に この清流に居向ひながらも 天も地も一度にひらく心地かな 禊をはりしそのたまゆらは 醜雲の四方に立ちたつ西方の 国土はこれより月日照るらむ』

初産霊の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『わが心よみがへりたり気魂も 軽くなりたり禊の神事に 禊する神事を初めて覚りけり 百の罪とが洗ふ神事と 罪けがれ洗ひ清めて吾は今 はじめて産霊の神業を知る 禊すと水底くぐれば大魚小魚 わが気魂をつつきめぐりぬ 気魂の垢をつつくと大魚小魚 わが身辺を取り巻きにけり 苦しさをこらへ忍びて水底に 神魂の罪を魚にとらせり わが肌は真白くなりぬ魚族の 垢は餌食となりて失せぬる 河底も明るきまでに瑞御霊 光り給ひて禊ましける かくのごと光の神も惟神 禊の神事に仕へますはや 曇りたるわが身は非時禊して せめて神魂の垢洗はばや

光なきわが身なれども朝夕の 禊に神魂冴ゆるなるらむ』

愛見男の神は御歌詠ませ給ふ。

『日南河水の底ひをくぐりつつ 禊の神事ををさめけるかな 天界の総ての穢れを洗ひ去る 禊の神事ぞ尊かりける ウの声の生言霊に生れし吾も いつの間かは曇らひにける 磨かずば忽ち曇る神魂よと 吾は覚りぬ禊に仕へて わが眼清しくなりぬ山も河も 今は雄々しく色冴えにけり わが耳はさとくなりけり虫の音も 禊終りて清しく聞ゆる わが鼻も透き徹りけむ百花の 薫り清しくなりにけるかも 言霊の水火も清けくなりにけり 禊の神事の貴の功に 天も地も清しくなりぬ気魂と 神魂の垢の洗はれしより いざさらば光の岐美に従ひて 曲津見のすみかをさして進まむ

神々を言向け和し光明に 満ち足らひたる国土造らばや 大空を包みし八重の黒雲も 散りて失せなむ岐美の光に 西方の国土はこれより輝きて 曲津見の魂もまつろひぬべし』

斯く神々は各自禊終り、其の功を讃美し乍ら、顕津男の神の御後に従ひ、柏木の森を目当に、スウヤトゴルの曲津見を征服すべく、意気揚々と轡を並べて立ち出で給ふ。
