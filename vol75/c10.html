Chapter 10: Prayer 言 [1904]

祈り言〔1904〕

§ 1

Tamanohime no Kami and Ikuyohime no Kami, gradually realizing that the Kunitamagami had been born in peace and tranquility and rejoicing that the foundations of the land of Manazuru had become firm, stepped forth into the pure courtyard of the Tamanōomiya, and while fluttering the long sleeves of their white robes in the mountain wind, holding a white fan in their left hand and a five-hundred-sounding bell in their right hand, they danced and whirled, celebrating the completion of the birth of the land and the birth of the Kami.

That is, the two goddess Kami:

'Tātātarāri tararīrā tarari agari rarārītō chiriya tarari rararītō'

Thus, throwing themselves prostrate in the Ukefu posture and stamping and thundering, they proclaimed:

'Ametsuchi hiraku tokoyonaru Kami wa kinikeri isago isago Isasago isago yahare yahare Ahare ahare wahare wahare Ihire ihire ihire ihire Wihire wihire tarana tarana Chirini chirini tsurunu tsurunu Terene terene torono torono Kogoko kukuzu kegede kigiji Tarana torono tsurunu terene Chirini hasaza hosozo fusuzu Hehese hishijitari kamunagarahogisu Iroha niohedo chirinuru o Waga yo tare so tsunenaramu Uwi no okuyama kefu koete Asaki yumemishi ehi mosesu Aa Kamunagaramitsureba Mitama sachihi ohashimase'

To such a place, Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, wearing light white robes, appeared in the Saniha of the Ōmiya, and waving the Ōnusa again and again, with a voice that was perfectly enmanseirau, without the slightest hesitation, proclaimed the divine words. We shall now set forth the full text of that Norito here.

原文（近代文語）

玉野比女の神、生代比女の神は、漸くに国魂神の安らに平に生れ給ひて真鶴の国の基礎の固まりしを喜び給ひて、玉野大宮の清庭に立ち出で、白衣の長袖を山風にひるがへし乍ら、左手に白扇を持ち、右手に五百鳴の鈴を持ちて踊りつ舞ひつ、国土生み神生みの完成を祝し玉ふ。即ち二女神は、

斯かる所へ太元顕津男の神は薄き白衣を纒ひ給ひて大宮の沙庭に現れ、大麻を打振り打振り御声も円満清朗に、少しの淀みもなく神言を宣り給ひぬ。其の祝詞の全文を此処に示し奉らむとす。

祈り

§ 2

'In the awe-inspiring Takaamahara of the purple-twixt heavenly realm, wherein the great Su no Ōkami universally rules the eight corners, since it is within the ultimate dwelling grand Su of Amanominehio no Kami, within the vast hall of Su no Kami, countless myriad Amatsu innumerable things are conceived and provided, the divine essence fully and completely fulfilling itself, forming one great ōmitama; because the nest of Su is thus established, it divides the ranks of heaven, earth, fire, and water, so that that heaven preserves the negayashi no mitama, that earth preserves the tarumitama, that fire preserves the sachimitama, that water preserves the toyomitama, and that musubi preserves the makarukahe no mitama. Therefore, the earth settles and stops in the nakago of Takaamahara, water becomes the guardian of the earth, fire rubs and flares up, and moreover, clashes and blazes forth. Heaven is changelessly established and builds the ikisashi; musubi goes back and forth and preserves sincerity; binding, governing, and gathering these together in the interior and exterior, the front and the back, it is called the great spirit-source globe (tairéigenkyū). The spirit-source globe acts dynamically and becomes the great august heart. The subtle body of the great spirit-source globe becomes the great august body. Therefore, the imperial glands (kōsen) of the world are none other than the great august eyes, the great august nose, the great august ears, the great august mouth, the great august hands, the great august feet, the great august chest, the great august abdomen, the august lungs, the august heart, the august spleen, the august liver, the august kidneys, the august muscles and bones, the august intestines, the august glands, and the august nails and hair, which are completely provided within the great supreme body of Su no Ōkami. The grand elements, which form one single supreme body and yet divide various august names, are because they are the grand imperial jewel of Misumaru which Su no Kami hangs around His neck; since they are the one-stranding veins of its structural molecules, and are those which, through the machine-approaching flourishing essence generating musubi, have emerged as kami-hito, they can by no means stand as independent solitary deities; all together, musubi-active, completely clustered, without any ego, without any separation or scattering, the body rises and is the imperial gland of Su no Kami. Hereupon, Su no Ōkami, presiding over the great august heart, is grandly seated upon the heavenly august throne of the purple-twixt; the great minister deities, presiding over wisdom, are positioned upon the fire-seat; the minor minister deities, presiding over instruction, are positioned upon the water-seat; the body deities of the fields (tami no kami), presiding over manual and pedal labor, are positioned upon the earth-seat; unitedly musubi-active, they serve in the heavenly great affairs of state. From ages remote, the great deity rules the world backward and forward, eternally, ever-lastingly, more and more distantly linked, guarding its thread-veins, making its lineage the lineage, its office the office, its house the house, generation after generation being born, so tenderly, tenderly, fondly, fondly, and affectionately imagined. Transmitted into its mechanism and rope, penetrating through, from the heavenly grand primeval ancestor passing through countless myriads of ages, now reaching this body, and from this body becoming children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, great-great-grandchildren, and down through the successive descendants of remote generations, seed descendants, benevolent descendants, primeval descendants, continuing and linking together without bound, turning and revolving for ever and ever. As the colors of those respective times, they thread the warp and weft of the structural warp and economic thread-veins of the great spirit-source globe of Su, passing through the warp of threads, the daily, monthly, and yearly courses, to construct the age of the great deity. Linked to the divine weaving of the divine garments, they weave and offer up the heavenly true words of the shuttle, making the brocade flowers bloom, and musubi-generating the truth of the heavenly Su glands. Therefore, since the great supreme body of Su no Ōkami sits as the great spirit-source globe ruling over the Su glands of the world, if there be those who, as kami-hito being the Su glands of the great spirit-source globe, stand in solitary egoism, self-assertive and self-willed, arousing the foul and defiled hearts of beasts and birds of prey characterized by body-conquest, victory, and the elimination of the weak by the strong, and performing the deeds of fish, insects, trees, and grasses, thereby acting in rebellion against the path of kami-hito, they forthwith disorder the threads of heavenly truth, break the structure of the great spirit-source globe, and destroy the parent's heart; that is to say, they thereby disorder, injure, afflict, and desecrate the grand, dazzlingly sacred, and wondrously numinous supreme body of Su no Ōkami—whose very name is fearful even to mention and wondrous to speak of—and thus they become objects of abhorrence to all kami-hito, subjects of divine reprimand, and targets of ancestral curses, inevitably sinking, drifting, encountering manifold disasters, and suffering utter destruction; how awesome, how unsightly, thus paining the heart of Su no Ōkami, defiling the world, afflicting the self, and bringing ruin upon the self! Deeming such bewilderment and sinking to be a grave affliction, since this is manifestly the calamitous deed of Magatsuhi no Kami, they reverently and swiftly take up the great purification wand (ōnusa), see anew and hear anew through Ōnaobi and Oonaobi, reform those transgressions, purify those defilements, set aright those disorders, mend those ruptures, cleanse and purify them, and vowing never to commit the offenses again, all deities—great and small—and all kami-hito, with deep awe and reverence, seeing and hearing with utmost caution, become completely purified and limpidly clear. Thus, the supreme body of Su no Ōkami Himself becomes ever more complete, ever more revered, ever more noble, ever more wondrously numinous, ever more brilliantly and transparently clear; His grand majestic authority pierces and permeates through, with none failing to prostrate themselves in awe, the light of His great wisdom shining brightly without a single dark place, rejoicing in His great harmony, softening in His great warmth, and musubi-virtuing. Therefore, now in this grand land of Toyoakitsushima in the purple-twixt heavenly realm, the kami-hito who have emerged through the mutual musubi of kami and man—those very kami-hito who presently comprehend Su no Ōkami ruling over the five-layered heavenly realms, and who are the imperial veins of the great august body of the enthroned great ōkami—strictly following the grand heart of Su no Kami with awe and trembling, exerting and striving diligently for the sake of the world and for the sake of kami-hito; when facing affairs, not shrinking even from entering fire or water; if they know it to be the truth of the grand deity, they shrink not even from entering a forest of drawn swords according to the Way, nor do they shrink from the midst of fiercely flying arrows and slingstones; advancing and ever advancing in divine progress, they repel calamities and purify the land, the sharpness of their hemp-stalk pillars being fiercer than lightning, and the precision of their rectitude being clearer than the path along which the sun travels. Since all things are thus, Su no Ōkami sits majestically upon the heavenly august throne, eternally rules the heavenly land, crosses His arms upon the august cushion, melts and permeates the divine crest of the august cushion to make it universal across the sixteen faces, arranges in groups of five the countless well-regulated great heavenly economic principles, clearly rules and knows every affair of the world in accordance with the structure of the great spirit-source globe, and guides them clearly in accordance with the law, in accordance with the path, by means of the supreme words of heavenly divine doctrine (amatsuhagoto); that all the great deities, minor deities, great minister deities, minor minister deities, field body deities, and all others in Takaamahara, down to the infant deities—without letting fall even a single pillar, and leaving not even half a particle of all living things—may help them, favor them, govern and rule them through ages of myriad myriads of generations yet to come, make them worship and revere the heavenly truth of the heavenly Su no Kami, make them hold the heavenly truth of the heavenly Su in their palms and grasp it in their hearts, perfectly pacify and order the effortless age of the great deity, tranquilize and flatten it out in utmost peace, and that He may listen to the heavenly truth upon the heavenly august throne, in exact accordance with the divine laws of the heavenly age, safely and peacefully guarding and blessing this imperial lineage—thus do we reverently, reverently declare and offer up.''

§ 3

The Sacred Song of the God Akitsuo.

Aha, aha! The land of Manazuru has solidified; The Kunitamagami has come into birth. That one among the entrusted works of the God of Su, How joyous is this day of its wondrous accomplishment! Through the sincere hearts of Tamanohime and Ikuyohime, The land of Manazuru has begun to shine. Since I, the male deity, left the Palace of Takahi, The deities of the four pillars have been born forth. While the Yasohimegami wait for me, the years will grow old; Alas for this regret, as I am a lonely god. Is it because the sincerity of the goddess Tamanohime makes the colors of the ever-green pines Of Mount Tamamoyama deepen so? The voices of the qilin, the phoenix, and the kalaviṅka ring clear, And the domestic fowl crow loud in the land of Manazuru.

The Sacred Song of the Goddess Tamanohime.

How awe-inspiring! The God Ōmotoakitsuo Has given birth to the Kunitama in the living kototama. Were it not for the divine words of the goddess Ikuyohime, Those noble divine works would never have been accomplished. Though the right and left, water and fire, were not joined, if thus An august child is born, what have I to lament? Even the moon crossing the heavens sees the shadow of transience; Should I alone hope for perfection?

The Sacred Song of the Goddess Ikuyohime.

Now that it has come to this, how ashamed I feel when thinking of those days When I grew wild, longing for the male deity! Whenever I listen to the august words of the goddess Tamanohime, My heart feels ashamed of its own inadequacy, Thinking that even if I were to die of longing, I must restrain myself, And my face burns with shame. Today, when the noble august child has been born, it is both joyous And yet also shameful, alas! When I reflect, even the faces of all the deities, When I gaze upon them, fill me with shame. The flames of passion that had dried up to the very depths of Lake Tamano Have at last vanished, leaving no trace. From today, I will reform my strange heart And not obstruct the divine work of the august spirit. Chiyozuruhime-mikoto has been born, and in the land of Manazuru There is not a single trouble. My work, with the birth of Chiyozuruhime, Has truly grown heavier still. Though I know we are a pair who must part, when I think of the youth Of the newborn child, I wish to remain as a pair.

I regret that the fleeting moments of a heart that went mad with longing for its partner Might remain even unto a myriad generations. Having become an ogre, having become a great serpent, I went mad, And there is no way to erase this love of mine. Though it was a love carried to its end, that madness Of those fleeting moments is not dissolved even now. Oh, a terrifying thing is love, making both body and soul Forget and feel no shame before the eyes of others. Purifying my body in the waterfall of a thousand streams on Mount Tamamoyama, I shall erase this love for all eternity. Even for me, who troubled the heart of the God Akitsuo, the Lord God has granted forgiveness. From now on, altering the work I have done, I shall solely and devotedly nurture the august child. With the august spirit of the mountain of Manazuru, eternally, I shall guard the land of the divine good grace. Since the God Akitsuo has the office of giving birth to deities, He will soon depart from the land of Manazuru. Though parting for myriad generations is grievous to me, Because it is divine work, I must resign myself to it, so I think.

The Sacred Song of the God Akitsuo.

In the purity of the heart of the goddess Ikuyohime, Shall I set forth on my journey in peace? I too, enduring the pain of the same sorrow, Will part, for the sake of giving birth to deities. Though you are not my eternal wife, how sad it is! With the divine revelation of the Lord God weighing heavy, Even if I am separated to the distant shores of a myriad leagues, Your sincerity I shall never forget. The binding together of water and fire and water and fire once more Is a deep red cord until the child is born. When I think of the heart of the goddess Tamanohime, I am only dimmed with tears. What words shall I offer to the hime Who has waited for me long in the forest of Tamano? Setting out on a journey of a myriad leagues through the wilderness, What I ponder must be today's parting. Without even knowing the growth of the newborn child, It is painful for me to set out on a journey of a myriad leagues. Meeting and parting one after another with the goddesses of the eighty pillars, It is a painful plight for me.

Since I am not a body of wood or stone, I too Understand the pathos of things. The pain of setting out, leaving behind the newborn child And the land of Manazuru that has just begun to solidify! Do you also flourish, pines of the ever-green trees, and white plums, And shed your fragrance out of season even though I am no more! Phoenix, cranes, domestic fowl, and all birds, Sing forever and ever in this sacred mountain! Even the clear voice of the kalaviṅka, from today onwards, Is to be lamented, thinking there is no way to hear it. When I think of parting from the Tamano Shrine, built high with rafters pure, My chest feels lonely and desolate. The land of the Western region to which I go is a young land; How shall I fare? My heart is troubled. To govern the hearts of the divine people of the land of Manazuru, I have prayed divine words before the august presence. The sincerity of the Great Ancestor of the Heavenly Deities, the God of Su, Is indeed the path that the divine people should learn and advance upon.

§ 4

Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami

When explaining the Shibitenkai in Honshiyo, regarding the divine works of the birth of the land and the birth of the divine children in the same heaven, I felt the necessity to attempt a brief explanation from the standpoint of Kototamagakujiyō concerning the divine name of Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, who has the deepest kuwankei, and therefore I have demonstrated their hataraki based upon each and every ichigonichigon.

Although the kototamakai of the four sounds of Ōmoto was briefly described in the 'Ōmoto' issue of the 'Shinreikai' magazine in September of the eighth year of Taishiyō, there are many kashiyo that should be teiseigy corrected in various ways, and moreover, as it is baubaku, there are tata points that are difficult to hosoku; therefore, on the occasion of now attempting to interpret the divine name of Ōmotoakitsuo no Kami, I desire to once again add explanations from the standpoint of Kototamagakujiyō, and thereby clearly indicate the divine office of Mizurei no Kami.

To briefly explain the kototama of Ōmoto, namely ō-mo-to:

The sound O is situated in the second step of the a-row, and thus it is emergence, it is itsuku, it is izu. All sounds of the a-row belong to the heavenly position; they are father sounds, they are mother sounds. The five sounds a-o-u-e-i all resound as horizontal sounds. This is indicated in the Amatsunoritori as the great door (sound) of Tachibana no Hinata in Tsukushi.

The Kototama of the Sound O

It has kototama hataraki such as arising, nobility, loftiness, being at, arousing and assisting, great atmosphere, great accomplishment, holding the molecules of hundreds of millions while knowing the beginning and end of the molecules, the meaning of receiving and accepting at the barrier of the heart, the attractive force of truth and love, intense rights and privileges, primordial spirit, encompassing the great earth, coming gradually and solidifying, extending outward, and so forth.

The Kototama of the Sound Ho

There are various hataraki such as being the beginning of heaven, earth, and all things, mother, spear, hidden gate, navel, dot, bag, spirit of the sun, kototama appearing above, heart of heaven, meaning of shining into, fire residing in water, ditch, sail, father, name and status of the sun, reflecting in the heart, longing, stopping to look, and so forth.

The Kototama of the Sound Mo

There are various hataraki such as towing together, clumping together, also, person, piling up, yielding, presiding over perfection, working below, sprouting of the world, hand of heaven, numbers gathering and numbers forming, having expansion and contraction, finally solidifying and becoming a thing, fundamental origin, upper surface of the soil, seat of water, essence of molecules, and so forth.

The Kototama of the Sound To

It is man, roaring, solving, foundation, person, ascent, presiding over the seeds of all things to make numbers from one to a hundred, a thousand, and ten thousand. The ta-row all have the significance of a forerunner, ten, ability to give birth, binding and penetrating fully, being completely governed, presiding over binding, origin of form, running in eight directions, position in the world, and so forth.

The Kototama of the Sound A

Heaven, earth, actuality, being out of nothing, manifest emergence, center of the world, Ōmononushi, daytime, Minakanushi, the earth, entry of the circular image into the eye, manifesting the power of ray beams, lingering in the eye, form of manifestation, seeing closely, primary head of Ōmoto, soul of the name, great mother public, universal and benevolent, essence of su, soul of the heart, seeing that direction, residing lowly, complete fulfillment in the present, form of the hidden, reaching far, being endowed with solar heat, gathering and rounding, all returning thereto, meaning of hiding and entering, night, encompassing everything.

The Kototama of the Sound Ki

A kototama with nothing above it, lord, exhausting and reaching the absolute in one, piercing and continuing, complete clarity, seeing and determining the world at a glance, precision mechanism of the soul sphere, divine mechanism of the soul manifestation, striking back, returning to stand, ruling and perfecting the world, having nothing that is not possessed, truth of the ultimate ancestor and ultimate origin of the world, currently ruling and presiding over the world, holding the world as a child, binding the origin and binding to perfection, reaching the limit and returning, returning to all human hearts, ultimate origin office of the divine soul, ultimate mother, crawling across below, returning to all things in the world, grasping all flora and fauna, smashing, building and solidifying, the absolute upper limit of the i sound, meaning of assisting below, mechanism, tree, castle, life, essence, spirit.

太元顕津男の神

本書紫微天界を説くに当り、同天界に於ける国土生み御子生みの神業に関して、最も関係の深き太元顕津男の神の神名に就いて、言霊学上より略解を試むる必要を感じたれば、その一言々々に依りて活用を示し置くなり。

オホモト四言の言霊解は、大正八年九月の「神霊界」雑誌「おほもと」号に略述しおきたれども、種々訂正すべき箇所多く、且茫漠として、捕捉するに難き点多々あれば、今回太元顕津男の神の神名を解釈せむとする機会に際し、今改めて其真相を言霊学の上より説明を加へ、以て瑞霊神の御職掌を明示せむと欲するものなり。

太元即ちオホモトの言霊を略解すれば、

オ声は、ア行の第二段に位＠して即ち出なり、厳也、稜威なり。総てア行は天位にして、父音なり、母音なり。アオウエイの五音は何れも横音に響くなり。是を天津祝詞には筑紫之日向之橘の大戸（音）と示されたり。

オ声の言霊

起る也、貴也、高也、於なり、興し助くる也、大気也、大成也、億兆之分子を保有し且つ分子の始終を知る也。心の関門受納の義也、真と愛の引力也、権利強烈なり、先天之気也、大地を包蔵し居る也、漸次に来りて凝固する也、外及也等の言霊活用を有せり。

The Kototama of the Sound Tsu

It is strength, it is continuity, it is the great source of Sashi, it is the extremity of velocity, it is the ultimate power of Daizaukuwa, it is the musubi of Teu, it is that which pierces through, it is that which governs all things, it is Daikongauriki, it is that which never returns, it is Ubosuregiaōei; this is called tsu. The true aspect and wondrous subtle nature are called tsu. It is without Kandan, it is the treasury of jewels, it is the blazing of the numina and deities, it is the extremity of Nare, it is the aya of Su; this is called tsu. To appropriate this tsu to oneself is called sin, it is Terinomiyako, it is daringly without Seishi, it is the great source of Kikuwan, it is Daigenmiyōō, it is Ketsudanriyoku, it is that which condenses and shrinks, it is that which preserves the entire phenomenon of U, it is the great source of Musubi, it is that which cuts and separates, it is Tororogi, it is Tsuraragi, it is Tereregi, it is Sururuki.

The Kototama of the Sound Wo

It is that which unites to become one, it is food, it is flesh and matter, it is Tamashihi, it is the musubi of uo, it is the cord of spirit, it is that which causes form to act as a servant, it is that which causes to act as a servant freely, it is the tail, it is a slender form, it is a cord, it is length, it is to govern, it is Oshihe, it is to leap, it is the guardian of divine decrees, it is Matsuoretsuku, it is to abide, it is the self, it is a calling voice, it is a shouting voice. It is the meaning of binding opposing things with a cord, pulling, and controlling them, it is the male phallus. It is ah, agreement, it is the upper command and the lower assent, it is a single streak of atmosphere, it is blue, it is to fall short and descend, it is the form of a separate faction, it is to untie, divide, and rule, it is the place that reaches afar, it is breath.

By means of the above interpretations of the Kototama, one may discern the meaning of the sacred name and the outline of the divine activity of Taigen Akitsunoo no Kami.
