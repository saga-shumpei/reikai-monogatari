Chapter 4: 千条の滝 [1898]

千条の滝〔1898〕

§ 1

By the living kototama of Mizu-no-Mitama Akitsuwo-no-Kami and Makotoizu-no-Kami, the wide plain of Manazuru saw heaven and earth tremble, shaking back and forth and left and right, with torrential storms incessantly arriving, until at last the entire region centered upon Mount Tamano swelled and expanded more and more, revealing a heaven-shaking and earth-quaking spectacle; thus the Kamigami could not help but marvel at the power of the kototama, and each chanted miuta of the living kototama, praising the divine work of nation-building.

Tamanohime-no-Kami chanted her miuta:

Awe-inspiring is the divine word of the Lord God, / coming here to witness this divine work for the very first time; / born forth from the ever-fresh fruits of the kagu tree, / am I not a divided spirit of the Lord God? / Descending from the high plain of heaven, two divine pillars, / will you help in this divine work? / The distant heaven thunders, / the rugged earth trembles, and the land is solidified. / The flashing lightning in the distant sky, / how swift must it be as the divine work of Kami! / The roar of thunder is strong, / and the crooked kami must have trembled in fear and vanished away. / To wash away sins, impurities, and errors, the jade spring / flows as a waterfall, bringing life to the world. / Being one behind in the work of kami-birth, / how joyful it is to stand at the place of nation-birth! / How noble indeed is the achievement of Akitsuwo-no-Kami, / who appeared as the mitama of Tsukuyomi! / The jade spring overflows purely, / plunging and roaring down high from the ridge of Mount Tamamo.

In the roaring sound of the plunging cascade, / the water and fire of the kototama are contained to bring life to the world. / Serving month after month at the Tamano Palace, / to behold such auspicious divine work, / before my very eyes the prosperity of the land of Manazuru / I behold upon the mountain of Tamamo, do I not? / The living form of Mount Tamamo, / stretching high into the distant heaven, was truly a Kami. / All things of heaven and earth / are surely the solidified pure water and fire of the Lord God! / To solidify the foundation for myriad ages, / do You appear, Mizu-no-Mitama? / Proclaiming the true words that the strict mitama has ordained, / I will proclaim them universally to the Kami and bring them to life. / Living and living, unto the endless life of the heavenly realm, / how joyous is the body to live and prosper! / In the precious kototama of Makotoizu-no-Kami, / even the lake of Tamano has dried up.

Billowing and rising in clouds of steam, the lake of Tamano / sees even the water at its bottom dry up next. / Full of valiant majesty is the form of Akitsuwo-no-Kami, / who has become the pillar of the land of Manazuru.

Ikuyohime-no-Kami praised and chanted a miuta:

Born as the mitama of the mountain of Manazuru, / I revere our divine work of creating kami. / Will I bear a child upon Mount Tamamo, / which soars aloft amidst the clear skies of heaven and earth? / Will the water and fire of Izuru-mitama and Mizu-no-Mitama congeal, / and the divine child lodge within my womb? / The kototama of Makotoizu-no-Kami brings bliss, / and the plains of the four directions have expanded ever more. / Beholding the power of the water and fire of the kototama before my eyes, / I gaze upon it and think upon the precious age of the kami. / Possessing a body of light vapor in the heavenly realm, / I ponder the difficulty of the divine work of bearing a child. / Yet, beholding the achievements of the kototama of the Great Lord of All, / my breast is at ease. / Waiting peacefully for the day when the divine child shall be born, / I resolve to devote myself to this divine land. / As far as the eye can reach, steam billows and rises, / which must be the burning breath of water and fire. / Looking out, steam has risen over the plains of the four directions, / making the divine work of nation-birth vivid and clear.

In the rising steam, the moon and sun of the august sky / are slightly dimmed; the land of Manazuru / and the sacred precinct of the Tamano Forest have swelled and expanded, / soaring high into the august sky. / Upon this peak, which bore the name of Mount Tamamo, / with reverence I will bear a jade divine child. / Rejoicing in the auspicious day when the jade divine child shall be born, / morning and evening I will proclaim the living kototama. / Morning and evening performing misogi in the waterfall of Tamamo, / I will nurture and tend the Kunitama-no-Kami. / As time passed, my belly swelled and expanded, / resembling the birth of the land of Manazuru. / Captivated by the pure heart of Tamanohime, / without cowardice I stand in the sacred precinct.

Mibashihiko-no-Kami praised and chanted a miuta:

Ever-fresh, the wind of Shinato sweeps away the dust / of heaven and earth—what a pure plain of the land! / All the hundred impurities upon the earth / are completely washed away by Mikahaya-no-Kami. / Surpassing even the God of Shinatobe and Mikahaya-no-Kami, / the land of Manazuru is free of sin and impurity. / The thunder god roars in the heavens, / banishing the curves of heaven and earth to form a new plain of the land. / To illuminate eternal darkness and clouds, / how pitifully the lightning flashes and runs! / Mizu-Mitama, having completed the divine work of nation-birth and kami-birth, / remain tranquil within this divine land. / Forever tranquil within this divine land, / open it up widely and intimately. / Mount Tamamo, which has appeared as the sacred precinct of the Lord God, / shall remain unshaken for thousands of generations.

§ 2

Ubudamagami deigned to rejoice and chant a poem,

'In the crystal spring the august spirit performed purification,

And deigned to stir today's divine work.

It feels as though I behold the moon's shadow reflected upon the surface of the spring, O the countenance of Kimi!

Majestic and tender is the august spirit,

And the living Kototama makes me weep.

O Kimi, who proclaims the Kototama of sweet savor,

Thy achievement has at last given birth to the land.

The august spirit of the god of true and strict word is the august substitute-vessel

Of Amenomichitatsukami of Hisakata,

I have realized it to be the august substitute-vessel of the god of Michitatsu.

In the light of the Kototama thou hast deigned to proclaim,

Upon Tamamoyama, which the Lord God bestowed,

Spirits of strictness and of auspiciousness were born.

Ashamed, I think of how I dwelt

Without knowing myself to be the august substitute-vessel of the god of Michitatsu.

Peacefully shall I give birth to the august child in the august belly of Ikuyohime,

I, Ubudama,

O Ikuyohimegami, dwell in peace,

Ubudamagami shall protect the august child.

Am I not one who serves in the great divine work?

Is not the god born to protect the august child?

The figure of the god Ikuyohime standing on the shore of the crystal spring was light.

Since she is a god born from the mountain of Manazuru,

I pray that the life of existence may be eternal.

The life of the august child who shall be born, unto eternity,

Shall I protect, infusing the true word.

Coming hither, how joyful it was

To serve a portion of my divine work.'

Tamakibarinokami deigned to rejoice and chant a poem,

'How shall I praise this auspiciousness

Wherein heaven grows clear and earth grows bright?

The birth of the land of Manazuru is accomplished, and the god Ikuyohime

Conceives the Kunitamagami.

The land of Manazuru having successfully given birth through magnificent holiness,

How noble is the birth of Kunitamagami.

Unto eternity the life of the august child,

To protect it, I have appeared.

The ever-verdant pines of Tamamoyama, which upon the very sight grow noble,

Growing ever more brilliant in hue,

And the springs that gushed forth this way and that in Tamano-mori

Have without exception turned into waterfalls.

Beyond the Tamamo waterfall falling from a height of ten thousand fathoms,

A thousand strands of waterfalls have appeared.

Morning and evening let the gods reverently

Perform purification at the thousand-strand waterfall,

For it is an age of the gods where the blessings of Kototama aid us.

How could we neglect purification?

Morning and evening purifying the heart to cleanse ourselves,

I shall protect the life of the ever-verdant gods.

By the bestowal of the Lord God, I, Tamakibari,

Have emerged as the god of life.

In the heaven of Hisakata, upon the earth, and among the gods,

And in trees and grasses, may there be life in all.

Distributing my soul-threads to all things,

I think to protect eternal life.

Though there are eighty days, this very day of life

Is truly the founding of the land of Manazuru.

Standing upon the ridge of Tamamoyama and looking upon the shapes of the land

Here and there, vapors rise up,

The provincial plains where vapors rise up in clouds

Shall I sweep clear and solidify with the life of water and fire.'

Ureshinohinokami deigned to rejoice and chant a poem,

'Boundless indeed is the joy, as Tamamoyama

Soars and stands aloft in the august heavens of heaven!

Drawn by the soaring mountain of Tamamo,

The lake waters of Tamano have turned into a mountain.

Beholding an auspiciousness hitherto unknown in heaven and earth,

I am blinded by tears of joy,

In a divine realm filled and overflowing with joy,

What is there to lament, I am Ureshinohinokami?

Looking at the sky, joyful and hopeful; looking at the earth,

My soul-threads are reviving.

In this divine realm of Aizen, distributing the soul-threads

Of joy, I shall make all things live.

Thinking upon the fortune of the god born

In the divine realm where both heaven and earth are joyful, joyful,

Should a heart of lamentation arise, by the living Kototama

Of the Lord God, let it be swept away.

The divine realm, wherein there is nothing whatsoever to lament,

Is where the flowers of joy, joyful and joyful, are blooming.

Yearning for the fragrance of white plum blossoms,

The Kalavinka sings of the age of the gods,

And the phoenix too descends to the mountain of Tamamo,

And beats its wings to celebrate the world.

Gathering together upon this sacred mountain of Manazuru,

How clear is the voice singing of the age of the gods!

Since the age of the gods there have been such auspicious precedents,

And I have awaited it with joy.

It is the height of joy, conceiving an august child

As the prosperity of the land of Manazuru.

In all things rejoice and be courageous, if it be a divine realm

Filled and overflowing with joy,

Nurtured by the water and fire of the true word of heaven and earth,

I think upon the joy of life.

O land of Manazuru, living in the moisture

Of the precious august spirit of Akitsuonohinokami!

The dew-drops of Tsukuyomi dripping down,

The jewel spring must have gushed forth.

The crystal spring overflowing and overflowing becomes a waterfall,

And shall purify this provincial plain.

Heaven and earth alike, a divine realm filled with joy,

I pray that it may dwell in the eternal world.'

§ 3

Umashimoto no Kami sang in celebration and caused poems to be chanted:

'As the water and fire of Kototama bless, the land of Manazuru is wondrously born; if the land of Kunihara were without taste, what would be done, and there would be no way for the hundred kinds of trees and grasses to spring forth and grow. If there were no taste in the hearts of Kamigami, how could the precious divine work ever be accomplished? Appearing as Umashimoto no Kami, I will protect the taste of all things in Ametsuchi. Even the bubbling waters of Tamaizumi, if devoid of taste, would leave no way to protect all Inochi. According to the gracious command of the Lord God, Umashimoto no Kami will eternally protect the flavor. If the water and fire of Kototama become turbid, it cannot be helped even if the taste of Ametsuchi vanishes. With the taste of Kototama present, all things in Ametsuchi live and flourish.'

Musubiahase no Kami sang in celebration and caused poems to be chanted:

'Binding together the living Kototama of Izutsu and Mizu, the mountain of Tamamo has solidified. With the water and fire of Izutsu and Mizu bound together, the land of Manazuru shall smile and flourish; binding together mountains and rivers, I will cause the pure water of Mashimizu to gush forth and live everlastingly. If there were not the taste of the dew of Mashimizu, the hundred kinds of tree fruits would surely not ripen. I now descend to this Kamikuni, thinking to assist the divine work of Kimi. As the heart of Tamanohime, the hime of Ikuyo, blesses with taste, the kuni flourishes. Through the Kototama where everything becomes and reaches without end, this Ametsuchi revolves and flourishes. The thunder that roared across the Ōzora has also softened its sound, and the Misora has cleared. When I look out, as far as the eye can see, the divine land where the wings of Manazuru shimmer is indeed a marvel. White plums are blooming in full abundance right up to the ridge of the mountain of Tamamo, and their fragrance fills the surroundings. Enveloped in the pure fragrance of white plums, the joy of beholding the shape of the land today is supreme.'

Machiatsuhiko no Kami sang in celebration and caused poems to be chanted:

'Waiting and meeting upon the auspicious day when the divine spirit of Mizu shall arrive, to meet on this felicitous and good day is also a wonder. The sanctuary of the hill of Tamamo has soared up to the sky of Kumoi in the Kototama of Izutsu. Phoenixes align their wings, and Manazuru cries out in celebration of the birth of this kuni. Being one who has waited for how many years, to see the purely manifested shape of the kuni is also a joy. Both moon and sun shine high in the Misora, illuminating the mountain of Tamamo. From today, purifying oneself in the waterfall of Tamamo, I will protect the divine work of the divine spirit of Mizu.'

Thus the Kamigami rejoiced and celebrated as the land of Manazuru expanded and spread ever wider and ever more distantly, and having finished singing their poems, they proclaimed words of gratitude to the deity at the summit's Tamano-ōmiya, which was truly wondrous.

『厳と瑞の生言霊を結び合せ 玉藻の山は固まりにけり 厳と瑞と結び合せの水火をもて 笑み栄えゆかむ真鶴の国は 山と河を結び合せて真清水を いや永久にわかせ生かさむ 真清水の露の味はひなかりせば 百の木の実もみのらざるべし 吾は今この神国に天降りして 岐美の神業をたすけむと思ふ 玉野比女生代の比女の御心の 味幸ひて国土は栄ゆも なりなりてなりの果てなき言霊に この天地はめぐり栄ゆく 大空に轟き渡りし雷も 音やはらぎて御空はれたり 見渡せば目路の限りは真鶴の つばさかがよふ神の国はも 白梅は玉藻の山の尾の上まで 咲き満ちにつつ辺りに匂へり

待合比古の神は寿ぎ歌詠ませ給ふ。

『瑞御霊来まさむよき日待合せ 今日の目出度きよき日にあふも 玉野丘の聖所は厳の言霊に 雲井の空まで飛び上りつる 鳳凰は翼を並べ真鶴は この国土生みを寿ぎてなくも 幾年を待合せたる吾にして 清く生り出し国形見るも 月も日も御空に高く輝きて 玉藻の山をてらし給へり 今日よりは玉藻の滝に禊して 瑞の御霊の神業を守らむ』
