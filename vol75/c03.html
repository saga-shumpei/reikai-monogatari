Chapter 3: Jewel 藻 Mountain [1897]

玉藻山〔1897〕

§ 1

Akitsuo no Kami stood at the shore of the spring of jewels, and wishing to thoroughly, intimately, and exquisitely build and solidify the land of Manazuru, proclaimed the seventy-five sounds of Kototama; thereupon Tamanōka swelled up limitlessly, higher and higher, expanding to the right, to the left, to the south, to the north, and in all eight directions, reaching such a height that even the summit of Manazuruyama looked as if it were right beneath one's feet, and during this time He spent almost seven days and seven nights. Although the myriad deities were present, because they were not as clear and limpid as the Mitama of purity, they had no means to chant the Kototama with one accord; first Akitsuo no Kami proclaimed the living Kototama, and next Makotoizu no Kami chanted the pure Kototama, thereby opening up, swelling, and spreading the land of Manazuru to infinity—how awe-inspiring it was.

When deities whose water and fire of Kototama are not completely clear and limpid intermix their water and fire, it causes confusion in the universe, and since the divine work of Shuri-kosei is difficult to accomplish, He arranged matters thus.

Once, when I climbed Yotsuoyama, chanted the Amatsu Norito together with numerous believers, proclaimed the divine words, and chanted to the utmost limit of the seventy-five sounds of Kototama, the only thing that could be clearly heard at the distant Ōmoto office across the mountain base was my own Kototama, while the voices of all the other people reached the height of confusion and could only be heard as a vague clamor—so the administrative officials of Ōmoto and others have related to me. You should understand that chanting such turbid Kototama with one accord all at once conversely disturbs the water and fire of heaven and earth. Even with the divine words offered before the altars morning and evening, because they are constantly disturbed by the turbid voices of believers and the clear and limpid Kototama cannot be chanted, except for Ōmoto grand festivals, chanting them together with believers is withheld out of reverence for the Kami.

Even at the time of grand festivals, having my Kototama disturbed for the sake of the multitude is extremely unpleasant, and I am keenly aware of how awe-inspiring it is before the divine powers. Therefore, you should understand the reason why the Kototama for creating the land in the distant divine age of the Purple Microcosmic Heavenly Realm (Shibi Tenkai) were not proclaimed with one accord.

Akitsuo no Kami selected Makotoizu no Kami, who was the master of thoroughly limpid Kototama, and they alternately chanted the living Kototama, while the other deities each proclaimed the Kototama one by one to assist in the divine work. Thus, the purity and turbidity, beauty and ugliness of Kototama have a great bearing upon the water and fire of heaven and earth, and since there is a vast divergence regarding the administration of the divine realm, what must be observed with strict care is the application of Kototama.

Therefore, those who are about to peruse this book must purify their hearts and bodies, train themselves in Kototama day by day, and become masters of flawless clarity and serenity; otherwise, they will not be able to impart inspiration to their listeners, soothe the breath of the deities, and assist in the divine work.

May the single thread of life Of the children of Kami, Unchanging through ten thousand kalpas, Continue on and on Endlessly! Using this spiritual line as a Mitamashiro, Having completely solidified the greatest celestial sphere, To solidify the prosperity of the Imperial Land of the Gods As firm as the everlasting rocks May the pure and righteous Kototama of the Lord Kami Flourish most eternally! Ah, Kannagara, gods, gods! May the Imperial Land of the Gods, Settling firmly as the everlasting rocks In the sanctuary of the Gods And the realm of the descendants of the Gods, The Emperor, Rule over all the world, As we proclaim the pure Kototama on high!

大祭の時と雖も、我言霊を衆人の為めに乱さるるは甚だ不愉快にして、神明に対し恐れ多きを自覚しつつあり。然るが故に遠き神代の紫微天界の国土造りの言霊も、異口同音に宣り給はざりし理由を知るべきなり。

顕津男の神は澄みきらひたる言霊の持主なる真言厳の神を選みて、交る交るに生言霊を奏上し給ひ、其他の神々は各自一柱づつ言霊を宣りて神業を助け給ひたるなりき。斯の如く言霊の清濁美醜は天地の水火に大関係を有し、神界の経綸に就いても大なる逕庭あれば、謹むべきは言霊の応用なり。

故に本書を拝読せむとする人は、心を清め身を清め、平素に言霊を練り、円満清朗の持主とならねば、聴者に感動を与へ、神明の気を和らげ且つ神業を補佐する事を得ざるなり。

§ 2

As He thus chanted, the plain beyond the reach of the eye, centered upon Tamanōka, swelled and expanded as steam rose progressively, and its height grew higher and higher in succession, and its width broadened and broadened in succession, leading to the direct beholding of the auspicious omen of the land of Manazuru. Here the god Makotoizu chanted a miuta of the Kototama.

Through the merit of the power of tatotsutchitata, This plain continues to expand; By the blessing of the water and fire of the aouei Kami, This plain is being revived; Until the moon and the sun draw near my meji, Tamanōka, which has grown ever higher, When I look out, the waters of Tamanokosui progressively Swell up and become a tidal flat; The water of Tamanōmi gradually dries up, Leaving behind only the blue tamamo; Even to the bottom of the yachihiro lake, by the Kototama, It swells up and turns into a mountain; How valiant, ah, how joyful, in the Kuniu Divine work, the glowing strict Kototama! The blending of water and fire of the strict and auspicious Ikukototama Caused the mountain of Tamamo to surge forth; Even down to the water's bottom of Tamanokosui, the slopes Of Tamamoyama became the joyousness of this day; A wind arises in the auspicious mitama Ikukototama, And rain falls, beating upon the great earth.

The earth shakes, thunder roars in the skies, Oh, the water and fire of the Kototama making lightning flash! Both heaven and earth shake, a wind arises, Could it be that this plain is being made to live? The shaking of the earth and the destruction of a hundred impurities and Magakami Are truly joyful indeed; Blow, O wind, to sweep away the impurities of heaven and earth, Fall and fall, O rain, let thunder roar! By the merit of the aouei Ikukototama, The mountain of Tamamo has grown and stood erect; By the power of tatotsutchitata, the oil-like Plain solidifies and proceeds; Kakokukeki, the moon shines brightly in the august sky, Illuminating the divine country to the limit of its light; Could it be the mitama of the Lord Kami? Of the sun of Takama, As there is not a single shadow of the noble light, The shadow of the sun of Takama containing The heavenly road-establishing path of eternal heaven, The moon, where the mitama of the god Hinotsuo shines brightly, Could it be the mirror of the august sky?

Tamaisen wells up by my Kototama, And perhaps becomes a falling cataract on the mountain's slope; Tamaisen overflows and finally becomes a waterfall, To moisten and save this plain; From today, altering the mountain of Tamano, I shall praise and revere it as Tamamoyama; The falling rapids gushing forth from Tamaisen Shall from today be called the Waterfall of Tamamo; The sound of the rapids falling from the myriad-fathom sky Resounds and echoes on all sides.

The god Tōmiwano Kami chanted a miuta.

How noble are the water and fire merits of the Kototama Passed down from the age of the gods when the world was shaken; By the auspicious mitama Ikukototama, Tamanōka Has risen and grown tall up to the heavenly sky; Since it is the kimi who births the land and births the gods, How fitting is the clarity of the Ikukototama! How awe-inspiring is the august majesty of the Kototama, the auspicious mitama, How joyful to witness today's divine work! I simply worship in awe and from today Shall serve in the divine work of the auspicious mitama; The plain progressively and successively expands, And Tamamoyama has surged forth; Tamanokosui, which held myriads of koku of water, Has dried up through the Ikukototama; Now I know the merit of the god Makotoizu, A god whom I cannot even approach; Though I am named the god Tōmiwano Kami, now having become such, I have become the god Chikamiwano Kami; Without the power of the Ikukototama to see afar, I am a mitama that sees close at hand.

From today, based on the august name bestowed by Kami, I shall serve as Chikamiwano Kami; Being one who cannot even see things at his feet, I think the name Tōmiwano is too grand for me; Two pillar gods were born from Shibi-gū, And perhaps they will assist in the divine work of nation-building? The merit of the auspicious mitama descended to this land Shall be the foundation of a thousand generations.

The god Maruyahiko no Kami chanted a miuta.

The nobility of the water and fire of the Ikukototama Of the auspicious mitama who bears the divine work of god-birth; The merit of the kimi who wields the water and fire Of the Kototama that moves heaven and earth at once! Now I know the merit of the god Makotoizu, The Ikukototama is overflowing; The youthful plain of Manazuru from today Shall solidify and flourish as Tokiwa and Kakiwa; The mountain of Tamamo where I stand, one after another, Roars and roars as it grows higher; Ringing and ringing, roaring and resounding, to the endlessness, It has birthed the land through the Ikukototama; Now I have come to understand in detail The divine work of Tamano-hime, the hime of life-birth.
