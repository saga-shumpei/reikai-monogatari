Chapter 5: 愁 Cloud 退散 [1687]

愁雲退散〔1687〕

§ 1

Two men who had entered the house of Jank as envoys from King Toruka of the Castle of Borgan in the country of Torman were named Aol and Kōs.

Aol sat properly and solemnly, conveying the king's command to Jank of Rishiyō.

Aol: 'Our country of Torman, since its foundation, has been united in upper and lower, and the relationship between the king and the people is like that of parents and children, like brothers, like husband and wife. The heavenly blessings are abundant, the soil is fertile, and it is called the treasure house and paradise of the whole of India; the citizens are joyful and harmonious, and have woven dreams of great peace for three thousand years. Yet, unexpectedly, the military forces of the Ōkuronushi Mikoto who reigns in the capital of Haruna have dispatched soldiers from afar for the purpose of subduing the Ural religion. However, Ōashiwake, who serves as the general of the entire army, is violent and cruel like a tiger or wolf, tormenting the people within our country, violating women, plundering wealth and goods, and worse still, burning private houses without a single act of violence left undone, and riding on the momentum, he attempts to capture the Castle of Borgan, the capital of the country of Torman. You loyal and good people, display the red heart of loyalty, bravery, righteousness, and fierce dedication, and in order to save the unprecedented great national crisis, all males from eighteen years of age to sixty years of age must carry their respective weapons and head toward the rescue of the royal castle. Protecting the territory opened by your ancestors must be at this very time. You, Rishiyō, convey the intent of my thoughts to the villagers, and follow the army without losing a moment. I am Aol and Kōs, messengers of King Toruka of the Castle of Borgan in the country of Torman, conveying the holy will of His Majesty the Lord.'

As he finished reading aloud, Jank of Rishiyō respectfully stepped down from his seat,

Jank: 'Although we are powerless, we shall obey the imperial decree, promptly summon a volunteer army, and dedicate our lives to the perils of the royal castle and the nation.'

The two envoys, Aol and Kōs, let out a laugh saying 'Satisfaction, satisfaction,' and while exchanging greetings, without even drinking the tea offered by Jank, they hurriedly stepped out to the porch, mounted their horses nimbly together with the dozen or so soldiers they had left waiting, with rich red reins, and the sound of hooves going clack, clack, clack, they proceeded toward the neighboring village.

Jank returned to the living room where Terukuni's entourage was staying, and with a slightly tense complexion,

Jank: 'Everyone, I have been terribly rude.'

Terukuni: 'No, not at all, how was the purport of the envoy? Actually, I was feeling worried.'

Jank: 'Yes, thank you very much. For me too, the time has come when I must abandon my family and my single life for the sake of the country.'

Sinking into melancholy, unlike an old man whose spirit was utterly dampened, a color of determination appeared from nowhere.

Terukuni: 'What on earth do you mean by saying you must abandon your person and your family?'

Jank: 'Just now, according to the imperial envoy from King Toruka of the Castle of Borgan: "The army of Ōkuronushi-sama that should protect the country of India, led by General Ōashiwake, threatens the people at the foot of the castle in the country of Torman, burns private houses, plunders women and children, extinguishes the race, and not yet satisfied, attempts to capture the royal castle and banish the king." Since it is a strict command that at this juncture the citizens, males from eighteen to sixty years of age, must sacrifice themselves for the national crisis without a single one remaining, I am also fifty-eight years old this year, and though I may be called dotard, I still fall within the proper age. I have already abandoned thoughts of my daughter. I am resolved to follow the army splendidly and expose my corpse to the mountain fields for the sake of the country.'

Terukuni: 'Indeed, the more I hear, the more pitiable it sounds. If it is the command of the king, as a citizen, it must be your duty to rise at this juncture. Since I have also come from afar to the Country of the Moon receiving divine command as a propagandist to remove the evils of the world, I would very much like to participate.'

Jank: 'Yes, thank you very much. I ask for your kind favor.'

Umekō: 'Ah, teacher, please think carefully. Is it not the Misokochi-kyō of the non-resistance principle that leads and harmonizes all myriad people by means of the Kototama of good words and beautiful phrases? Participating in a warlike army and dashing through smoke and a rain of bullets is by no means the true duty of a propagandist. Misokochi-kyō is by no means militarism, you know.'

Terukuni: 'Ha-ha-ha-ha, as you say, we never hate the enemy, nor do we wish to wage a satsubatsu, jiniteki sensō. Our desire to join the giyūgun is to save the shiyōbiyōshiya, to preach and expound the true way without kubetsu between tekimikata, to resolve things in heiwa, and not only in this Toruman country, but to bathe the kokumins of the seventy-thousand-plus countries of Indo in the divine jion, as our daiitsupo. It is for this reason that we seek jiyūgun.'

Umekō: 'Ya, I understand then. I have no particular monku, but what do you plan to do about my family's daughter, Miss Sugako, or Thunder-sama? We cannot simply misu katagata either.'

Terukuni: 'Ah, that is also a concern, but how about asking Oyabun Inca?'

Umekō: 'That is so indeed. If Junk-sensei says so, then whether you accompany her to jiyūgun or not, we must save Lord Sanyo's imōtomusume Hanaka-sama, and we cannot let our own family's Sugako-sama and Thunder-sama be misugoroshi either, so please leave this search to me, zetcher?'

Junk: 'Your kindness is appreciated, but today, this is no longer the time to be talking about things like my daughter. We must devote all our strength for the sake of the King and the country. Please make your actions one with Terukuni-wake's propagation envoy as well. If it were said that I relied upon the propagation envoy for the sake of my own daughter, it would be a lifetime's shame. Therefore, Oyabun Inca, you too are a part of the kokumin, become a general of the giyūgun, and set out to the front together with me. Matters of my daughter and Thunder are secondary to the next, zetcher.'

Inca: 'Indeed, a truly admirable determination; without it, you could not be called Satoshō-sama. Even I am a kyōkaku-totose who aids the weak and crushes the strong; upon hearing the King's proclamation, could I remain at ease? In accordance with your words, I shall engage in jiyūgun.'

Takuson: 'If so, Junk-sama, nay, goshujin-sama, shall I also accompany you?'

Junk: 'Nay, you have already promised, as I hear, to become the companion of the Izumo-shin's propagation envoy. A man's word in Toruman is as good as iron and steel. Participate as a disciple of Terukuni-wake-sama.'

Elson: 'If Satoshō-sama, I too have become a disciple of Terukuni-wake, but as a part of the kokumin, shall I follow the army together with Satoshō-sama?'

Junk: 'Nay, nay, you too are already a subordinate of the propagation envoy of the Izumo-shin. It is best that you make your actions one with Takuson.'

Elson: 'Hai, thank you. Then, I shall accompany the precious propagation envoy, and without holding a sword, holding only the Quran, I shall exert my utmost efforts for the sake of heaven and all people.'

Junk: 'Ah, good, good, with that I too am at ease. If Terukuni-wake-sama, please treat the circumstances of both of them well.'

Terukuni: 'Ya, deeply moved by everyone's determination, I have understood all details.'

He ordered the gatekeepers Banko and the receptionist Sale to convey the King's command to everyone in the village, and instructed them to set out for the front looking forward to the dawn. The village suddenly became turbulent, and an atmosphere of satsubatsu filled the air like a fire scene. Junk organized the men of the village into a military force, and decided to march out to the front as a general himself.

Inca: 'Surely the same command must have been issued to my village as well. Excuse me ahead.'

So saying, he greeted everyone, tucked up his skirts, made the great earth resound with heavy steps, and returned as if flying.

Here, everyone offered a great prayer to the Great Deity of Izumo and the Great Deity of Brahmā in order to obtain victory in their journey ahead, and held a naorai banquet as a celebration for their departure until daybreak. Courage was suddenly added to each of them, possessing the momentum to swallow mountains and rivers. The grand festival concluded, and finally shifting to the naorai banquet, scooping and exchanging sake, the room was filled with harmonious energy, drifting with an atmosphere just like spring.

Takuson: 'Before the august majesty of Terukuniwake no Kami no Mikoto, I offer my absolute reverence. How joyous it is to pray for blessings before the peerlessly majestic and august Ōkami!'

Elson: 'Those who reform the age of Tokoyami and open it into the Age of the Gods are truly human among humans. How shall we quell the seething tumult of everyone within? There seems to be no way to do so.'

Junk: 'Today's divine army is to sweep away the gathering clouds that would hide the heavenly sun. Though I have grown old, my soul shall revive in all its youthfulness and sally forth to battle. There are warriors who dye their white hair and face the fray; I too shall follow their example. Seeing my white hair gleam as I die a martyr to the national crisis, they will surely be astonished.'

Terukuniwake: 'How fascinating! The time has come to advance a step in the divine work entrusted by Kami. No matter how fiercely the AURA mountain winds may blow, they shall be blown away by the divine breath of Kami. It feels as though we are in a military encampment where warriors gather and converse late into the night. When dawn breaks, leading a hundred soldiers, I shall advance our steeds toward Barugan Castle.'

Umekō: 'How the figures of the brave warriors, aligning their steeds as they advance across Ōnogahara, leap before my eyes!'

Terukō: 'It feels as though we have already driven away and vanquished the enemy forces at Barugan Castle.'

Takuson: 'How fascinating! Following my master and lord, I shall achieve meritorious deeds for the divine army.'

Elson: 'The clouds of love too shall soon clear away, and I am resolved to devote myself for the sake of the country and the people.'

At length, the crowing of roosters and the cries of fowls were heard from all around. Junk was the first to rise to his feet,

Junk: 'It is already the hour of the cock. Everyone, please make your preparations. The time for our sortie draws near at last.'

With a courageous and spirited roar, he took out the armor he had stored away in preparation for an emergency and set about arming himself.

Just as this was happening, a strangely white-haired old man appeared and came shouting 'Tamanou! Tamanou!' from the entranceway. Who on earth could this old man be?
