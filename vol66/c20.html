§ 1

Sandā and Sugako, the two of them, were having a hard time with Genshinbau's tyrannical love requests, when seeing that yet another man and woman had been thrown in, Sandā unconsciously cried out, 'Ah!' The woman also looked at the faces of Sandā and Sugako, and saying only, 'My, oh my,' she closed her mouth.

Umekō, acting with carefree nonchalance and laughing unconcernedly,

Umekō: 'At last the proverb that even monkeys fall from trees; the great tengu of Mount Aura also made a botched job of his theatrical performance, plummeted from the stage, was bound up tightly by nameless fellows, and has fallen into such an Island of Niyōgo. My, what a mysterious thing, if such things exist. They say well that human blessings and misfortunes are like the horse of Saiō—or rather, horse manure. All of you together are peerless beauties, save for my wife as an exception... O two Himegoze, for what reason do you sit here in reverent chinza?'

Sandā: 'Alas, I am one called Sandā from the village of Koma. This person is Princess Sugako, the daughter of Junk-sama from the village of Tarai. We have been abducted by bad men and are made to experience painful things day by day. How is it that you have come to such a place?'

Umekō, having listened to the story from Sanyo of the village of Tarai, and having formed the determination to rescue Hanaka, and hearing that he had stayed at Junk's house, that Princess Sugako had gone missing, and that Sandā had disappeared, from the sincere heart that despite following the volunteer army he somehow wanted to rescue these three persons, parting from his master, while secretly whipping his horse and galloping toward Mount Aura, saved Hanaka from her peril, accompanied her to ascend the mountain, climbed the giant cedar, blew out the lamp which was the secret art of their magic, used the voice-imitation of a tengu, and when his disguise appeared and he was caught and seized, he told and explained every single detail.

Sandā and Sugako, both hearing this story, while wringing their sleeves with tears of gratitude,

Sandā: 'You, whom we do not know at all, have thought to help us to that extent and have taken such trouble; we have no words at all to express our gratitude. Actually, as you may have heard, although I am dressed as a woman, I am a man. I am betrothed by our parents to become the husband of this Sugako here, but in order to search for Sugako's whereabouts I came on pilgrimage to this mountain, and until today I have been shut away by Genshinbau. Please kindly understand this.'

he says feebly.

Umekō: 'Ya, now everything is understood. Now, there is no need to worry. A cave like this, smashing it open, is before breakfast. Hello, you called Sugako-sama, do not worry by any means. I will surely rescue you.'

Sugako: 'Oh, thank you very much. Since I am of an unfortunate lot, unless I receive your help, there is utterly no way to escape.'

Hanaka: 'Hello, my lady, I am Hanaka, the daughter of Sanyo. Saying that you had been snatched away by someone or other and your whereabouts were unknown, there was a great uproar throughout the village, but after a short while, a Brahman soldier came, snatched me away, inflicted a wound upon my mother, led me into the forest of Hora ga Oka, dragged me all around that neighborhood, and tried to break my chastity; yet thanks to the gods, I gradually escaped that hardship, and when it came to the end and I was now about to be disgraced, this person came out and helped me. What are called karmic connections are strange things. I had one sister, but on a certain night, hand in hand with a shugenja, she escaped from home, and it is already three years now, but how she is doing, her whereabouts are completely unknown. Moreover, the strange thing is, that man called Genshinbau, I cannot help thinking I have seen him somewhere before. Although I cannot tell clearly in the dark, judging by the shape of his body and his voice, I think he might just be that fellow.'

§ 4

He is muttering thus. Chīgō thrusts his face out abruptly from the next room,

Chīgō: 'Aha-ha-ha, hey Genshin, what are you in such a panic about? Now that it has come to this, there is no other way than to employ the principle of fighting poison with poison. We must cleverly win over the Sendenshi to make them our allies, revere them as the master strategists of Niyoteisama, step down one notch below us, and strive to accomplish our long-cherished ambitions. If we let Niyoteisama run her mouth too much, her authority will drop, so I am well aware of that point. However, whenever the crucial time arrives, you panic, and your inner helmet is immediately seen through. Since Chīgō will take charge of the brunt of this negotiation, you had better rather maintain your silence; that would be much more tasteful and proper. And as for the woman named Thunder whom you were in love with, she is the son of the village headman of Koma; one of them is your betrothed, Princess Sugako. Since it is utterly impossible to break off a flower that has an owner, you had better give up on her cleanly right now. If you give me strange looks, it will reflect on our faces. Above all, it concerns the authority of Niyoteisama. Hey, do you understand?'

Genshinbō's dream of love is completely awakened, and he sits sulking with a face like a raccoon dog that has fallen into a urine pot. At this time, the dark clouds enveloping the entire sky are cleared by a mountain blast blowing just then, and the great expanse of heaven begins to shine brilliantly with starlight against a pear-skin-colored backdrop. Ah, Kamugara Tamashii Sachisaku Masase.
