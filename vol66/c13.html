Chapter 13: 恋の懸嘴 [1695]

恋の懸嘴〔1695〕

§ 1

Sandā was placed in a handcart by the gang of thieves, including the sub-chief Shōru and Kori, and after being sent along a sloping road of about one ri, she arrived in front of the mansion of Genshinbau at the foot of a giant cedar. When Genshinbau exchanged glances with Kori and Shōru, he put on a deliberately astonished expression and fled down the slope, tumbling and stumbling as if gravel had been blasted down from a roof. Gazing in fascination at the beautiful visage of Sandā standing there, Genshinbau feigned complete ignorance and spoke thus,

Genshin: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha! Those petty thieves, terrified of my spiritual power and struck with astonishment at my glare, fled like scattering spiderlings, aha-ha-ha-ha! Good grief, what troublesome fellows they are. Well now, young woman standing there, it seems you were abducted by them and carried all the way to this place, but fortunate indeed it is that, thanks to the might and spiritual power of Genshinbau, the petty thieves fled without inflicting any violence upon you; this is entirely the result of my spiritual power, come, come, enter the inner chambers.'

Sandā: 'I am seeing you for the first time. I am a maiden of Satoshō in this village named Sandā, but having heard that a living god called Lord Genshinbau had descended to this mountain to save the hardships of the people everywhere, hiding it from my parents and for the sake of my faith, I set out along the night road walking unsteadily; no matter what, with a body afflicted by illness, the movement of my legs also would not obey my wishes, and day broke at the foot of this mountain. With the footsteps of a fragile woman unaccustomed to treading, completely exhausted, with retreat cut off and advance impossible, I rested my breath upon a stone by the roadside, and just as I was dozing off in a light slumber, a group of fifteen or sixteen men who appeared, saying they were a band of thieves, spoke to me thus: "Is not this woman the empress of our boss, Princess Yoriko?" Since they said this, though I understood nothing, I inadvertently nodded, whereupon a great crowd of people hoisted me up and brought me all the way to this place; will you not please allow me to see Genshinbau-sama even for a single glance?'

Genshin: 'The Genshinbau whom you are seeking is this humble monk. To think that you have managed to make a solitary pilgrimage here despite a woman's fragile body; you must be thoroughly exhausted. Please make yourself completely comfortable in my living room.'

Sandā: 'Ah, were you indeed the living god renowned in rumors, that is to say, Genshinbau-sama? I have committed a grave rudeness.'

Genshin: 'Aha-ha-ha-ha. Before long, a multitude of men and women will come to worship, so I shall be busy from now on, but until then I shall hear the purpose of your business. First, please come out to my living room.'

Sandā: 'Yes, thank you very much.'

With that, Sandā plunged her figure into the magnificent cavern. Within the room, a sutra desk was properly installed, and two or three volumes of scriptures edged with gold were tastefully displayed. In one corner, Buddhist ritual implements such as a hossu, a tokko, and a golden bowl were decorated. Genshinbau was a species of erotomaniac. Abandoned by Princess Yoriko, with whom he had lived for three years, he had barely become her second disciple due to that attachment, spending his days in a somewhat disgruntled state. Therefore, he had sweetly deceived the peerless beauty Sugako, confined her in a single room, and in order to achieve his desires, pleaded with her every spare moment; but far from budging an inch, he always received a barrage of elbow-blows and rear-kicks, leaving him utterly depressed and disheartened. At that very juncture, Sandā had visited him—possessing an innate beauty manifold greater than that of either Princess Yoriko or Sugako. Deeming this a fortunate stroke of luck and a gift from heaven, he leaped for joy, clenched his teeth in breathless anticipation, determined this time to employ every secret art in battle and win the glorious laurel wreath of victory, and adopting a deliberately gentle voice, he spoke thus:

Genshin: 'You said your name was Sandā, did you not? Regarding your coming on a pilgrimage to this mountain as a lone woman along a perilous night road, there must be some deep circumstance. Since I am the savior who saves all things in the three thousand worlds, speak of your wishes without any reserve. Whatever the matter may be, I will grant your desire.'

Sandā: 'Ah, I am deeply grateful for your kind words steeped in jinji. As I have a younger sister and she has become yukuhefumei, I have tried various ways and had my shimobe and murabito sōsaku for her, but I cannot find her arika by any means. I have heard that you have descended from ten to save banmin, and thinking that if I inquired about the arika of my sister you would oshihe it to me, I have come to make inquiries even though it is shitsurei.'

Thus, he was completely playing the part of a woman. And... if he could not kanpa that this ikigami was actually a man, he would be no better than a yamakobauzu or a maisu. He must pretend to be a woman thoroughly to the end... such were his shian. Genshinbau looked into Sandā's face with narrow, sleepy eyes, giving off a thoroughly unpleasant kibun. However, a sudden suspicion flashed through Sandā's mind: could it be that the Sugako hime whom he loved had been plotted against by these bauzu and hidden away somewhere? Therefore, he resolved to carry on as a woman until akumade. Genshinbau, for his part, was completely convinced that Sandā was a woman by tensei, and never even in a dream kizu that he was a man.

Sandā: 'Oh, shiyugenjiya-sama, I have come to beg you because I want to meet my sister, but what do you think, would it be possible to let me meet her? My sister's name is Sugako and she is eighteen years old; if you will only let me meet this sister, I will not refuse any request whatsoever.'

Hearing that Sugako was this woman's younger sister, Genshinbau felt his chest pound and was slightly startled, but as an old rogue at heart, he firmly pressed down his thumping chest and put on an innocent face.

Genshin: 'Aha, so your younger sister is named Sugako. Hmm, I will let you meet her. However, first, regarding letting you meet your sister, I also have a proposal for you in exchange. If you will only listen to that, I will use my spiritual power to summon Sugako hime right here and let the sisters meet.'

Realizing that this fellow was indeed a maisu, Sandā acted even more foolishly and naively.

Sandā: 'My master-sama, what kind of business might it be? If it is something within my power, please command me to do anything.'

Genshin: 'Yoshiyoshi, then I shall bring forward a proposal from my side. It is none other: I am one who has received the command of ten and descended to this sacred ground for the salvation of the realm. Regarding this, an imperial divine decree was issued this morning from the Moon Great Deity of the Deepest First Spirit Realm, stating that after half an hour from now, a natural beauty shall be given to you. That beauty is none other than the incarnation of Tamanohime-sama, the himegami of the Deepest First Heaven. The divine will was that you and she should become husband and wife without a moment's delay, and participate in divine work. As you also know, since I am in the position of a shiyugenjiya, to take a wife seems indeed to break ancient customs, but in this present world of daily progress and a hundred restorations, the rules of the divine realm also seem to have changed, and it has been commanded that I, starting from myself, should set an example for the world and become husband and wife united in soul. The reason you suddenly wished to come here was also because the spirit of Tamanohime-sama, who guards you, guided you here. You, too, being young, must be quite surprised to become husband and wife with me, whose age is different. However, the command of ten must not be refused. Sandā-sama, do you understand?'

Sandā firmly endured what felt almost like an urge to burst out laughing at the sheer absurdity, and with a feigned innocent expression and a hint of a smile,

Sandā: 'I wondered what kind of business it might be, only to hear an unexpected explanation of divine destiny. How could a tainted body like mine ever become the wife of one of such noble person. Please stop such a joke; by no means can I believe it.'

Genshinbau, seizing this exact moment as his utmost endeavor, poured forth the whole of his physical valor, splashed his hot blood, leaned his body forward, grasped Thunder's hand tightly, and shook it two or three times,

Genshin: 'Now, now, my lady, though your wonder is natural, there is never any falsehood in the Kami. Since you can meet your younger sister and also encounter your true husband in Shinkai, there can be no happiness greater than this. If you follow the decree of Ten and become my wife, the Kamisama will surely let you meet your younger sister; yet if you turn your back upon the will of the Kamisama and refuse to become the wife of Setsusō, the Kamisama will not let you meet your younger sister either. Come, here is the place for reflection; you had better give a good reply.'

Thunder: 'To a foolish, frail, and inexperienced woman like myself, though I know not whether it be the Kamisama or whatever, that you should bestow such a gracious will upon me is far beyond my merits, and I am deeply grateful. Nevertheless, unless you let me meet my sister first, whatever you may say, I cannot obey your command. Being people of shallow faith, we cannot yet believe you to be the incarnation of the Kamisama either. Therefore, absolute obedience is also impossible for me. However, since I am only a person whom you met and laid eyes upon just now, and you have not even shown me any wondrous divine virtue, I cannot help feeling suspicious, but please overlook that matter.'

Genshin: 'Since you are a woman who until now did not even know the character for Kami, it is not unreasonable that you cannot trust me immediately. However, since so many people have received divine virtues here and are rejoicing in adoration, well then, you, a wise woman, ought to have deduced whether it is a Kami or not.'

Thunder: 'Will you truly not let me meet Sugakohime no matter what?'

Genshin: 'It is absolutely impossible to let you meet a woman who will not listen to the decree of Ten. If you wish to meet your sister, will you obey the command of Kami and become husband and wife with Setsusō? Even for Setsusō, it is troublesome to take a wife after growing old, but it is hard to go against the decree of Ten, and for the sake of the nation and all people, I am offering this pure and undefiled body, which has never been defiled until now, as a sacrifice. Did not the future Christ and the like also carry the cross and save all people? Do you not also have the sincere heart to become a sacrifice for the sake of the world? If not, you cannot be called the spirit of Tamanohime of the innermost first heaven.'

Thunder: 'Whether I am the spirit of Tamanohime or the spirit of Tanukihime, I do not care about the matters of the spirit realm in the slightest. If only you will let me meet my sister through your divine virtue, I will be satisfied with that. If you will not let me meet her no matter what, there is no help for it; I shall follow your instructions and become your wife. However, since it is only six months since I parted from my precious birth mother, and I am in a state of mourning, please postpone the wedding for a year. If you permit that, I will offer this body to Kamisama. Or rather, I leave it to your freedom.'

Genshin: 'Ah, such a prompt consent, satisfying indeed. However, Princess Thunder, no, my wife, you don't need to be so stiff and wait a whole year. Since it is the permission of the Kami of Ten, in the world they call it an incantation and as long as the form is fulfilled, it is fine. Since six more months have already passed, you don't need to make such a fuss. It will be well if you leave it to this husband. Right, Princess Thunder?'

changing even the tone of his voice, how disgusting it was as he stroked her back.

Thunder: 'I say, Genshinbau-sama, since I have promised to become your wife, sooner or later we must have the wedding. Will you not let me have a glimpse of my sister even a moment sooner? You are surely not a husband who would refuse to grant the wish of his most beloved wife, are you?'

Genshin: 'Um, I would love to let you meet her, but the truth of the matter is that Sugakohime has a slight connection with me. Therefore, since I would be somewhat troubled if my first wife and second wife were to glare at each other and grab each other by the collar, I am saying I will not let you meet her. Even if I let you meet her, surely you will not be jealous, will you? If there is no jealousy, I shall let you meet her at any time.'
