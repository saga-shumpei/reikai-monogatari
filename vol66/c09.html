Chapter 9: Valley 底の宴 [1691]

谷底の宴〔1691〕

§ 1

In the valley of Mount Aura, bluish water flows quietly, forming a fairly wide stream.

If one climbs seven or eight chō upwards from here, the water current resembles an extremely steep waterfall, but by now this spot has a gentle water flow and a deep bottom, with a mood like a deep pond.

In this river, countless boats, both old and new, were floated and provided for transportation purposes.

It was the work of the kobun to drop the goods, which were brought from the four directions day by day, down into the valley from under the floor of the shrine in the Forest of Tenō by the action of iron wires and pulleys, fill the boats with them, and send them to the hideout of the hall.

A lot of the kobun were fatigued by running about on missions, producing a slight sense of tedium, gradually grumbling about the poor quality of food, and about two hundred of them were plotting to secede from the alliance.

Seven-tenths of the three thousand subordinates were scattered in various places on various bad official duties.

Thereupon, the boss strain of Seegō, together with Pank, hung down a lot of delicacies and rich liquor.

Smiling broadly, Seegō speaks toward everyone.

Seegō: 'All you kobun, you have put yourselves to a great deal of trouble. Truly, Her Majesty the Empress and all of us executives are grateful. Since there were happily a lot of offerings today, I want you to eat the delicious delicacies to your heart's content, drink some liquor, build up your spirits, and carry out a great activity. Her Majesty the Empress also said to convey her best regards to everyone.'

At this voice, wherever their previous dissatisfaction might have gone, upon hearing of the liquor and food, everyone became like Ebisu, and the sound of applause made the echoes of the valley resound, having the force of falling thunder.

Pank: 'Hey, all you fellows, you were muttering and leaking complaints, but both Her Majesty the Empress and the Boss know very well about your hardships. Today, by the will of Her Majesty, you are receiving feasts the likes of which you have never seen before, so you should be grateful.'

First Man: 'Hey, Pank-sama, it is thanks to your efforts that we have come across a splendid meal and drink for the first time in a long while. Even if we fill our mouths and bellies with millet and foxtail millet day after day, we were about to lose our bones and could not perform activities as we wished, but if you oil us so splendidly, the engine will probably operate smoothly. Please say regards to Her Majesty the Empress. I express my thanks on behalf of everyone.'

Pank: 'Yoshiyoshi, I will surely convey it. Her Majesty the Empress will surely be satisfied if she looks at your good-humored faces. Seegō, the boss Genshimbō-sama is also satisfied, and we too are satisfied. You fellows must also be greatly satisfied, aha-ha-ha!'

Laughing as he goes, he points to the living quarters of the Empress and climbs up the steep slope.

The feast of excessive drinking and eating was opened unceremoniously.

If there is no liquor, they drop their spirits, make pale faces, and mutter complaints; if they drink their fill of liquor and their bellies are filled, they get angry again, cry, scream, hit each other, and since only junk that cannot be managed by any means is gathered, control is not easily achieved.

Becoming a boss of thieves is indeed a difficult thing once more.

As the intoxication gradually begins to circulate, muddy kototama warfare was started here and there.

First Man: 'Hey all you fellows, will you not eat to a proper degree? What is this, how disgusting, clicking your tongues with tjon-tjon, and on top of that licking plates and licking chopsticks, isn't it entirely the behavior of beggars? Oh, how vulgar it is. It is not like the piles of the Aura River, but gluttonous and long-eating is a matter of you fellows. Because it is an article that says once it starts eating it does not let go, just like a floating loincloth in a river. To share actions with fellows who look like ghouls is truly pathetic. It just becomes annoying.'

Second Man: 'Na-na-na, what is it, what is vulgar? At any rate, we are the subordinates of Her Majesty Yoriko who are entrenched in the stronghold of Mount Aura. If you utter things like sweeping away dust with a broom, we too have our own thoughts.'
