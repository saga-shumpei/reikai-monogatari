Chapter 7: 女白浪 [1689]

女白浪〔1689〕

§ 1

In the highest part of the Kaugenchiu plateau, and moreover in the extreme northwest of the nation of Tolman, whose soil is the most fertile, there stands a great towering mountain piercing the clouds. It is said that trees grow densely there, making it dark even in broad daylight, and that ferocious beasts and venomous snakes inhabit it in the greatest numbers. This is Mount Ōra. To call it Mount Ōra means that many mountains are lined up in the same shape, extending for several hundred ri, and this district is called the Mount Ōra mountain range zone. The waters flowing out from the valleys all pour into the Ōra River, which is a famous large river cutting straight through India and emptying into the Indian Ocean.

Seigōbo, who was formerly an ascetic of Brahmanism, taking with him Genshinbō whom he relied upon as his right arm, established his stronghold in the central part of this mountain range, namely Mount Ōra. Waiting for the time to arrive, first of all to occupy Bargan Castle, gather his forces, rush upon the capital of Haruna, conquer Daikuroshu, and seize hegemony over the whole of India, leading three thousand subordinates, he built a mountain stronghold, utilized the Ōra River, and had set about all preparations for raising an army. First and foremost, what was necessary was military funds. He racked his brains to devise methods and means everywhere to collect money, goods, grains, as well as weapons. On the mid-slope of Mount Ōra, there was a somewhat wide flat land, and from a far distance separated by several ri, the dense, bushy branches of giant cedars, which one could not tell how many arm-embraces wide they were, stood out conspicuously. The two men, Seigō and Genshinbō, put their heads together and held a secret meeting.

Seigō: 'Hey Genshin, our plans are gradually getting started, and three thousand subordinates have gathered, but still, whether you speak of the shortage of food or the lack of weapons, if things proceed just like this in the end, there is no way for our painstaking plans to end up as a painted cake either. Also, if we are to unify subordinates who are like jackals and wolves, unless the provisions are abundant, they will stop listening to our commands as well. Might you have some good scheme, I wonder?'

Genshin: 'That is so indeed. I too have been pondering various things, but no matter what one says, this country is solidified by Brahman belief; how would it be to manifest a single auspicious omen, win over the hearts of the people, and by the power of faith, have them offer money, articles, and other weapons? I suppose there is no good method other than this.'

Seigō: 'Indeed, you have hit upon a good point. Though I too have been acting as a Brahman ascetic for a long time, I had not thought of the best method and means to win over popular sentiment through religious faith and accomplish a grand ambition. However, situated as we are in such deep mountains, gathering people will not be an easy matter.'

Genshin: 'Well, regarding that matter, I too am racking my brains. If we go out near the villages, people gather well, but there is a fear of secrets leaking out. If we stay deep in these mountains, it is safe, but gathering people will hardly be an easy thing. Unless we perform some quite extraordinary feat to show them, it will likely be difficult to lure foolish men and women into these deep mountains.'

Seigō: 'I too have thought about various miscellaneous things, but this grand ambition cannot be achieved by anything like announcing a starvation of provisions. Even if we keep letting our three thousand subordinates just steal, in the end unity will become impossible, and finally we will buy the disgust of the people and become a focus of resentment, which would likely bring about an obstruction to our great enterprise. Can we not somehow concoct a good method and means?'

Both men tilt their heads and are lost in thought. Entering there from the next grotto by opening the door of the grotto was Genshinbō's wife, Yorikohime.

Yoriko: 'Ah, well, well, Master, Husband, it seems some good talk is taking place, is it not. Please let me share in the company as well.'

Seigō: 'Zoom, nay, it is nothing at all. The scenery of the great Ohara plain is so fine that I was just enjoying the view to my heart's content with Lord Genshin.'

Yoriko: 'Heh-heh-heh-heh, what a strange thing you say. Keeping the door of the grotto tightly closed while gazing out over the great Ohara plain, and saying the view is fine—words like deceiving a child; no matter how much you try to hide it, I see right through to the depths of your hearts, you know.'

Genshin: 'Naani, I was truly looking just now. I had just closed the door a little while before you came.'

Yoriko: 'Hohohoso, what a pack of lies, to be able to speak with such a brazen face. You people despise me as a woman and are always whispering and plotting improper things behind my back, but rather than doing petty evils, why don't you commit a grand evil like a real man? Isn't there a saying... that great evil resembles great good?'

Genshin: 'Unlike you, who are as gentle as a goddess, today of all days you are saying such bold things. Human beings really do change when they change, don't they.'

Yoriko: 'That is certainly so; as the saying goes, mix with vermilion and you turn red, mugwort grows straight when aligned with hemp, the novice at the temple gate chants the sutras without having learned them, and the sparrows of the Kakuangakuin chant the Mōgiu; since I am receiving daily practical training in the philosophy of villains, when it comes to evil I am a woman who might even be said to have surpassed her master. No longer am I the maiden Yoriko of the Tarai Village era. I am the wife of Genshinbau, a relatively grand villain, hohohoso, I must have developed quite a bit.'

Seigō: 'How admirable, my word. Hey Genshin, you can rest easy now too. Since you have acquired such a powerful supporter, it is like giving a demon an iron club. In this regard, it is as if I too have obtained a million reinforcements. Ah, how delightful, delightful, otsuhohohso.'

He shakes his shoulders and laughs.

Genshin: 'Indeed, as the master has said, she has become quite thoroughly corrupted. Hey, Yoriko, the truth of the matter was that it was right on the tip of my tongue to open up to you about the grand ambitions of the master and myself, and to ask for your support; but no matter how one looks at it, you are like someone stepped out of a master's painting, a graceful and slender beauty. Viewed from the relationship of the unity of spirit and body, your heart is graceful, noble, and honest. If I carelessly let you hear such villainous plots, you would be disgusted, your three years of love would vanish at once, and it would be a disaster if you shook me off, ran back home, or even committed suicide. Up until today I kept this secret locked in my heart, but if you have that much villainous mettle, I can finally be completely at ease. The world will soon become as I desire. It is a truly delightful thing, atsuhahoho.'

Yoriko: 'Am I not a wife attached to her husband? When you stayed at our home in Tarai Village, I intuitively sensed that you were a dashing hero who, behind some eccentricity in your gaze, was planning a great enterprise—whether good or evil I knew not. Therefore, I abandoned a mother of great grace, parted from a beloved younger sister, and pledged my heart to you. Now that I have opened my heart to you this far, please have no worries whatsoever, and let me share in consulting on all matters.'

Genshin: 'My, what a creature a woman is. Or rather, what a terrifying creature; she is an absolute monster. To think I managed to sleep holding such an extreme beauty of a villain without having my throat cut while sleeping, atsuhahoho.'

Seigō: 'Utsuhohohoho, things are getting more and more interesting. A demonic husband and a serpentine wife... how well said that is, etsuhehe.'

Yoriko: 'Now, Master Seigō, why look so surprised? Has it not been said since ancient times that women are demons? While I have been living with Genshinbau-sama these past three years, every time he spoke with a faint heart or lamented the world... I thought to myself: "Ah, what a spineless coward. Shall I just cut his throat while he sleeps? No, wait, wait. As for men of this modern age, every last one of them is nothing but a coward. There is neither a dashing hero who carries good through to the utmost, nor one who thoroughly executes evil. Compared to the men of this world, this Genshinbau-sama seemed to have a slight edge in the arts of hypocrisy and malice, so although he falls short of my desires, I thought there might be some use for him in the future, and that is why I have endured until now, otsuhohohso. Genshin-sama, I am truly sorry, but please never think ill of it. I am pouring my love into you from time to time, am I not? No matter what, according to the natural laws of the human body, once or twice a month, a period of estrus is bound to come, you see. When one is starving, no food tastes bad; when one is thirsty, they say even the water from a muddy paddy tastes like the nectar of immortality. Truly, you were my sole comfort, my minor savior, hozuhoho.'

Genshin: 'Chie tsu, you are making a fool of me. Then you were afraid of my power and obeyed me against your will, weren't you? And you must have been using me as a pump to extinguish the flames of your passions, I suppose.'

Yoriko: 'Your pump was superior to ordinary ones and like a horse's, you know. However, thanks to that, the mouth of my hole became enlarged, and a great Dairiumondō cavern measuring one hundred degrees East longitude and two hundred degrees North latitude was formed, hotsu ho ho ho ho.'

Genshin: 'Eh, are you mocking a man?'

Yoriko: 'Ho ho ho ho, that is right, indeed; does not the saying of the sages also tell us to revere the elders? So-called, mocking you was after all merely expressing respect to you, wa. Besides, whenever I heard you utter occasionally such cowardly, sickly, and petty whimpers like a decayed woman, my chest felt nauseous and sick, you know. They say that a priest's farts smell like an elder's, after all. Since you would make vague plans that could not be grasped at all like farting in the water and then fail, were you not utterly untrustworthy?'

Genshin: 'Ah, ah, what a formidable empress you have taken for a wife, alas.'

Yoriko: 'How interesting, you called me an empress, didn't you. They say that to revere too much is to lose courtesy, and you probably regard me as a target of contempt and mockery. However, whether meant as ridicule or contempt, that Kototama of being called an empress is, for me, fitting to my spirit and body, and I truly thank you, hotsu ho ho ho ho.'

Shīgō: 'Now, now, Empress-sama, is that not a tremendous horsepower?'

Yoriko: 'Yes, rather than horsepower, it is elephant power. Does it not say that even a great elephant is towed by a single strand of a woman's black hair? A man has horsepower, which explained in detail is called fool-power, and a woman has so-called elephant power. The elephant is connected to hatred and harbors deep resentment against men to the end without knowing a woman's spirit, yet harboring the immense fool-power of thinking "she must have fallen in love with me," putting on the airs of a master and settling back complacently—this is the disease of men in general in this world, wa. Truly, men are a pitiful lot, ho ho ho ho.'

Shīgō: 'This is outrageous, to take two peerless warriors as your playthings, indulge in abuse and mockery, and inflict absolute and infinite insults upon them is an intolerable thing. I will surely take revenge for them, so you should be prepared for that.'

Yoriko: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, if I have offended you, please forgive me. This empress has merely vented ten thousand zhang of spirit against ordinary junk-men of the world. It is an exception for a man among men and a splendid, valiant hero like you. The truth is, Shīgō-sama, I truly like you, you know—Shīgō-sama, while wearing pure white snow on your head, that glossy face of yours, might this be called what the world terms a white-haired youthful face? You look entirely like an aged baboon that has passed through kalpas, hi hi hi.'

Shīgō: 'This is strange; does this outrageous empress's words mock and slander an eight-foot man even more?'

Yoriko: 'Far from it, is not Genshin-bō-sama sitting right here?'

Shīgō: 'Ehe ho ho ho ho, praising and disparaging, lifting up and casting down, I haven't the slightest idea what is what. Ambiguous and vague like a dragon or like mist, with our horsepower we cannot possibly capture its outcome at all, etsu he ho ho ho ho.'

Yoriko: 'I like that white-haired youthful face of yours very much, you know. For you are endowed with the grand qualification to lead a great political party and flourish as a commoner minister. However, you must be careful. You never know when Nakaoka Gōichi might appear in a station or a crowd, otsu ho ho ho ho.'

Shīgō: 'To be the prime minister of a self-overturning island in the future is auspicious. However, meeting an assassin is the one thing I absolutely detest.'
