Chapter 8: Kami 乎 Demon 乎 [1690]

神乎魔乎〔1690〕

§ 1

Even a natural beauty adored as the incarnation of beauty, the embodiment of love, the absolute pinnacle of goodness, the outward expression of true feeling, and the goddess of peace, once she treads upon frost and snow, drifts amid fierce waves and raging billows, and is swallowed in the vortex of all manner of dangers and sins, when a sudden derangement comes over her inner spirit, she instantly becomes an ogress, a demon, a serpent or dragon that overthrows countries and ruins castles, melts the very heart and soul of every man, and stretches out her demonic hands even to the point where a man's stern and rigid backbone is stripped of its flesh. Indeed, woman becomes the very height of heresy, the head chieftain of beasts and demons, the gathering place of devils, and the root cause of pitch-black ignorance, throwing the world into confusion and spreading every manner of noxious poison. That Yoshikohime was a seductive and alluring beauty with lips like plum blossoms, eyebrows like willows, eyes that seemed to have bells embedded in them, a well-proportioned and gently swelling nose right in the center of a white face, teeth the color of white coral, a stature neither too tall nor too short, Jizō's shoulders, plump and well-shaped breasts, smooth skin like a gem, hair possessing a black luster like lacquer or lapis lazuli casting its radiance about her cheeks, wrists and fingertips like ivory carving, nails the color of agate, her walking posture swaying like spring flowers in a gentle breeze, and from whichever angle she was viewed, vertically or horizontally, she was a peerless beauty without a single flaw. From the time she was in her own home in the village of Tarai, her beauty brought misfortune upon her, and arrows of love were concentrated upon her by every man, yet she could find no lover endowed with the true essence of a wise and valiant man matching Yoshikohime's own will. Furthermore, she was surrounded and attacked by malignant men possessing lizard faces, frog faces, goblin faces, pumpkin faces, gourd faces, oval faces, and black faces like eggplants, and day and night she came to know the weaknesses proper to men, spending her days from her sixteenth spring to three fleeting years feeling disgusted and sickened, finding life unpalatable and sorrowful. The men of this world, so-called men, not a single one of them was any good; even when she encountered men praised by the world as paragons among men without a single fault—who at a glance looked like men of talent and courage, or deep-feeling men of virtue—and when she tore open the outer skin called the flesh that wrapped their souls and peered into the very bottom of their spirit, she felt that every one of them swarmed with foul odors just like a garbage dump, piled high with feces and urine, and she learned that love is something that can never truly and completely be tasted. Yoshikohime was richer in intellect compared to other women. Everything about her was masculine. Therefore, seeing high-collar men who disguised themselves with the scent of seductive scented oils or face powder, she loathed them as if they were venomous snakes and scorpions. And she realized the truth regarding the cowardice, the feebleness, and the utter helplessness of men before women. She never let go of the thought that she somehow wanted to marry a sturdy, manly man who surpassed herself.

As luck would have it, a man known as Genshimbō, a mountain ascetic of the Brahman religion, stood at the doorway on a certain evening as fierce winds blew and howled, and begged for a night's lodging. When Yoshikohime caught a glimpse of the ascetic through the knot-hole of the door, because he wore his straw hat pulled down deeply she could not clearly discern his facial features, but from somewhere she intuitively sensed that he was a sturdy, manly man. Thereupon, she willingly opened the door and invited the ascetic inside, talking away through the whole night about the various states of the world; and as her heart was drawn to Genshimbō—who, unlike ordinary people, possessed an untamed, mountain-like air from somewhere—she thought to herself: 'Although he is somewhat unsatisfactory... compared to ordinary men, he has at least a bit of manly character. As the ways of the world dictate that what waxes must wane, in a world where light and shadow endlessly intermingle, one surely cannot hope for perfection, completeness, or flawlessness. Ah, whatever shall be! Even though I feel sorry for my mother and younger sister, I will join hands with this man, slip away from my home, and try to place a great gamble that will astonish the entire world. Sink or swim, the success or failure of the undertaking is not what I question. Once dead, one does not die a second time. They say that even fierce gods avoid whatever is resolutely carried out with the resolve of death. Ah, resolution, resolution...' Nodding thus to herself, she talked Genshimbō into it of her own accord, and around the dead of night on the following day, hand in hand, leaving behind her hometown where she had lived for eighteen years, while wandering hither and thither, at the end of her eighteenth year she finally shut herself up in this Mount Ōra, about to take the first step in executing a grand conspiracy.

§ 2

Kanojiyo feigned gentleness and modesty on the surface until the jiki arrived, keeping the heart of a sairau deeply hidden within her breast; and as the tsukihi passed by, to Genshinbau—who had begun to sense an intolerable hanamochi—she displayed every manner of coquetry on the surface, waiting for the time to come while becoming fūfu with him.

No longer was it a chiuchiyo for hesitation; praying to Kami day and night, he desired to make the Tolman country his own by utilizing the three thousand akutaubara gathering under the buka of Seego and Genshinbau, to become the dainiyotei of seven thousand and more yokoku in the Moon Land with the Barugan castle as his konkiyo, and to make his geumei shine brilliantly throughout tenka.

Therefore, he stripped off the kinpaku he had hitherto painted, revealed his true kiji, turned toward the two kiyotō, Seego and Genshin, and made bold gendō.

Indeed, the two kiyotō were robbed of their shintan by his daitanfuteki words and his dokiyō; just as frost exposed to the morning sun melts away, so did they easily lower their flags before him, hail him as the dainiyotei and mastermind, and finally consent to become his subordinates.

Seego: 'Dainiyotei-sama, what kind of methods and means should we take in order to accomplish our daily desires? We wish to receive your guidance.'

Yoriko: 'Will you two not go against my words? First of all, let us settle that.'

Seego: 'At this point, why should we ever go against your words?'

Yoriko: 'Very well, how about Genshinbau?'

Genshin: 'I am of the same opinion as Seego.'

Yoriko: 'In that case, I shall teach you my unprecedented grand plan and the ultimate secret of divine strategy and cunning tactics. First, designate the shrine of Tennō, which has been worshipped on this mountain since ancient times, as the base of operations; Genshinbau shall become the savior descended from heaven on the surface, Seego shall command the three thousand buka, and I shall become the divine incarnation of Tanabatahime descended from heaven to delude the myriad people of tenka, and first of all, we must set about requisitioning money, goods, provisions, and military arms for the preparation of raising an army.'

Seego: 'Indeed, what comes first are money, provisions, and weapons. How shall we proceed to safely collect them?'

Yoriko: 'First, Genshinbau shall loudly proclaim himself as the savior sent from heaven; circulate the word far and near that day and night, stars from heaven descend upon the famous large cedar tree on this mountain to listen to the savior's teachings, pour oil into a gyaman and set fire to it, light fifteen or sixteen fires from midday on the treetop of the cedar to astonish the people far and near, and establish the shrine of Tennō as the center of faith. On the other hand, Seego shall employ numerous buka, sneak into the houses of all the wealthy, steal away beauties, and confine them in the natural caverns of this mountain; and thereafter, Seego shall call himself the senior disciple of the savior Genshinbau, appear as a shugenja at the houses whose daughters were stolen, and engage in frantic propaganda. To those who fortunately come to believe this, in order to invite the eight million gods of heaven and earth and pray for divine assistance, according to the amount of the believer's property, fifty ryo to five hundred ryo of gold per household, and in addition one hyo to one hundred hyo of grain shall be offered to Kami; during the night, pass iron wires through the valley of the Ora River, transport them to the bottom of the valley using pulleys, numerous buka shall build many transport ships, rice and wheat shall be transported by ship down eight ri to the valley of Horo to be allocated as provisions at the time of the attack on Barugan castle, and I shall conceal my body in the shrine of Tennō, transmit divine oracles to superstitious foolish men and women, and make preparations for the departure of troops. I think there can be no cleverer means than this. You two must be in awe of my divine strategy.'

Seego: 'Indeed, a plan without a single leak; no, I am already filled with admiration. Hey Genshinbau, the heavenly heroine who is the fountainhead of knowledge, is utterly beyond our reach. Well, you too must have been filled with admiration, haven't you?'

Genshin: 'Oh, I am already deeply impressed. Now then, let us embark on a grand theatrical performance.'

From this time, Seigō felled the large trees in the ravine, built numerous boats, and employing a multitude of subordinates for the purpose of transporting provisions, he set to work day and night on the construction of the boats, while Genshinbau painstakingly hung rope ladders on the great cedar tree and made a glass lamp shine high up in the treetops.

The villagers in the vicinity saw a brilliant light lingering and shining night after night on the great cedar tree of Mount Aura, and all of them knitted their brows in suspicion; waiting for the dusk, they made a din like villagers watching fireworks, making all sorts of criticisms. Seigō selected about one hundred of the strongest and sharpest-witted men from among his three thousand subordinates, dispatched them to the nearby and distant villages, and set about seizing money and valuables directly if luck permitted, or else kidnapping the daughters of the wealthy, and secretly dragging them back to Mount Aura to cram them into numerous natural caves. As the rumor spread from place to place that bandits were running rampant and that daughters and wives were being snatched away, the villagers far and near bolted their doors when evening came, and eventually there was no one left to sightsee the light in the treetops.

Seigō disguised himself as a shugenja, and standing at the prominent gates of the villages while shaking his shakujō, he went on a pilgrimage through the distant and nearby villages.

Seigō: 'All is impermanence, this is the law of birth and extinction; when birth and extinction are extinguished, the quiet extinction is bliss. Originally there is no east or west, how can there be south or north? Because of delusion there is the castle of the three realms, because of enlightenment there is the void of the ten directions. The living must surely die, those who meet must certainly part. Namu Paramita Śakra Svāgatîśvara, I take refuge, bow my head, and respectfully offer this again and again.'

This propaganda was immensely effective, and those who were filled with terror and sunk in the abyss of sorrow all came to desire to rely upon the savior of Mount Aura and escape all calamities. Seigō dressed up his subordinates as shugenja and had them tour far and near, proclaiming with clever words:

Seigō: 'A great divine savior has appeared in Mount Aura; needless to say about mountains, rivers, grasses, trees, birds, beasts, insects, and fish, he bestows the teaching of universal love and protection. In the end, even the deities of heaven and earth are moved by his virtue, and night after night they descend from heaven to the treetops of the great cedar standing beside the sacred sanctuary where the savior dwells, emitting a brilliant light to listen to the teachings of the savior. This is an unprecedented auspicious sign.'

Because of this clever propaganda, those who had lost daughters, those who had lost wives, and those suffering from illness vied with one another, each rushing ahead, to have an audience with the savior of Mount Aura, and came on pilgrimage like ants gathering to sweetness.

Mount Aura, where even human footprints had hitherto been cut off, at the rumor that a savior had appeared, regardless of young or old, man or woman—to say nothing of money, goods, and grain—even weapons such as spears and naginatas, which had been preserved as family treasures, came to be dedicated under the name of sacred treasures, and in the twinkling of an eye a mountain of grain and a forest of spears were built. Genshinbau faced the people gathering to the point of overflowing, taking the great cedar tree as a shield, and in a haughty tone said:

玄真『汝等一切の衆生、善男善女、吾教ふる言を聞け。吾は父なく母なく天を以て父となし地を以て母となす宇宙唯一の天帝の再来なるぞ。先づ吾を信ずるものは躄は立ち、盲は明りを見、聾は聞き、癩病は清まり、身体壮健にして無限の長寿を保ち、富貴繁昌し、死しては天国に上り、百花爛漫芳香馥郁たる天国の楽園に無限無極に歓喜の生涯を送り、求めずして百味の飲食を給せられ、不老不死なるべし。汝等かかる美はしき天国に至らむ事を望まば吾救世主の言を聞け。汝等が花婿をとり、花嫁をとるにも、相当の結納が要るだらう、金銭なり、物品なり、箪笥、長持、旗、指物、武器等は嫁入りに要する肝腎要の結納なるべし。汝等天国の楽園に至り天人と結婚をなし、平和の生涯を永遠に送らむとせば、先づ神の御前に結納金を献上すべし。金銀珠玉米穀その他あらゆる武器を天帝の化神たる吾前に奉り、永遠無窮の幸福を得よ。汝等の知る如く、吾を天帝の化身として久方の天津空より天の星下らせ玉ひ、吾徳を慕ひ、吾説法を聴聞し玉ふ。その証拠には毎夜この神木に星光燦爛たるを見るならむ。決して疑ふ事勿れ。怪しむ事勿れ。疑は信仰の門を破り、疑は地獄を造り、暗黒を作り、滅亡を招くものぞ。愛善なる神に対するには愛と善を以てせよ。信真なる神に対するには信と真とを以てせよ。神は相応の理に住し玉ひ、内面外面共に汝等の行為を調査し玉ふ。神の愛するは即ち吾身を愛し吾家を愛し、国土を愛するの謂ひなり。疑ふ事勿れ。善男善女よ、一切の衆生よ、帰命頂礼神道加持、謹上再拝々々々々』

§ 4

To the foolish men and women who gathered day after day, he repeated the aforementioned words, made them sniffle and shed tears of joy, and poured his mental strength into the collection of war funds.

And for those who had lost precious daughters or wives, he called it a special prayer, made them offer numerous valuable articles and money, accompanied them to the shrine of Tenō where Yoriko's fake Tanabatahime was hiding, and caused them to receive divine oracles.

Since it was deep in the mountains, some people came and gathered from around daybreak in the morning, but due to Genshinbau's oracle, once the seventh hour struck, not a single person was left behind and all were made to descend this mountain.

The reason was:

At and after the seventh hour, the Heavenly Deities, Earthly Deities, and the Eight Hundred Myriad Kami descend every night, and cause Genshinbau to preach in order to save the world.

Therefore, ordinary people whose wisdom and enlightenment are inferior must refrain.

Should anyone stubbornly insist on staying, divine punishment will instantly befall them.

Thus he threatened them, and after the believers went home, they ate all kinds of delicious food, drank wine, with Yoriko in the middle, Shīgō and Genshinbau forming a triad, while the other leaders waited beside them and smacked their lips in gluttony and drunkenness.

Yoriko: 'Shīgō-dono, it seems you have exerted yourself considerably, and tremendous results have been achieved.

That the foolish men and women of the world have come gathering like ants and have come to send numerous treasures and goods without regarding this steep mountain is entirely your effort.

If this pace continues for six months, there will no longer be any need to worry about military provisions.

Even with one hundred thousand subordinates, you should easily be able to support them.

Your splendid work is truly the Order of the Rising Sun, First Class, with Grand Cordon.'

Shīgō, with a smug face and looking thoroughly delighted,

Shīgō: 'Receiving the praise of Her Majesty the Empress for our clumsy and feeble efforts is an honor beyond my worth.

Henceforth, I shall shatter my bones and crush my body, and I will not spare the labor of dogs and horses.

I humbly beseech you to look upon me favorably once our great ambition is accomplished.'

Yoriko: 'Such things are understood without being said.

You are my right arm; without an arm, no work can be done, no matter what hero or great warrior one might be.

A superior because of followers, followers because of a superior.'

Shīgō: 'Hehe, thank you very much.

It will not do to be put in such a miserable plight as "the deer and boars being exhausted, the hunting dogs are boiled"...'

Yoriko: 'Ho, ho, ho, ho, ho! Since it is an alliance between villains and villains, even that cannot be guaranteed to the end, ho, ho!'

Genshinbau: 'Empress, how do you find my work?'

Yoriko: 'Your work is exceptional once again.

No matter what is said, you are the incarnation of the Heavenly Emperor.'

Genshinbau: 'Which of our achievements is greater?

Unless you let me hear that, I won't feel any zeal at all, and no spirit of competition will arise whatsoever.

For all things progress and develop through competition.'

Yoriko: 'You are the left hand.

Just as the right hand is necessary, the left hand is also necessary.

Both of you are in a relationship where it is hard to say who is the elder brother and who is the younger.

There is by no means any difference in merit between your achievements.

My achievement is the achievement of you two—no, the achievement of all subordinates; the achievements of you and the general subordinates are my achievement.

A firm relationship of absolute unity and inseparable oneness between high and low is forged.

I ask both of you to work together with one mind and concerted effort for the unification of India.

Today, it would be well to give celebratory wine to the subordinates in general as well.'

Shīgō: 'Yes, I understand.

The subordinates will surely rejoice as well.'

To such a place, a sub-leader named Panku appeared timidly, thrust both hands to the ground before the three of them,

Panku: 'I report to you, Chief.

A serious thing has happened.

Unless you have someone like Shīgō come and suppress it, Panku's arms alone will never be able to resolve it.'

Yoriko: 'Panku, what kind of thing has happened?'

Panku: 'I hesitate to report it, but the subordinates complained about the food, saying that they want their leave now to return to their country and pursue honest livelihoods, and have organized an allied army of about two hundred men.

As much as possible, I did not wish to let the Chief hear about such internal strife, but it is no longer any use.

According to their assertions, the Chief and the leaders smack their lips at feasts of a hundred flavors, while we must make do with tasteless things like kaoliang and parched rice, and are fed only greens so that flesh has separated from bone, making us utterly unable to move, and therefore they keep saying they will go home, go home.'

Yoriko: 'Indeed, that is probably only natural.

Hey, Shīgō-sama, as quickly as possible, proclaim the wine to the general public just as I said a moment ago, and let them smack their lips at the feasts gathered in such abundance.

Even lions, wolves, tigers, and wild dogs do not bite people if only their bellies are full, ho, ho, ho, ho!'

Shīgō, together with Panku, aimed at the group of subordinates at the bottom of the valley, and rode a rope ladder down to convey Yoriko's command.
