Chapter 12: The Wish-Fulfilling Jewel [674]

如意宝珠〔674〕

§ 1

The colors of the heart, too, at the foot of Terayama, the Takadono built there is known to the world as the Nagatoko of the Palace of Brocade, echoing like the autumn wind. With the brocade of Momiji scattered about, even when the cold seeps into the body, the Sendenshi of Ananai-kyō, wearing frost upon their heads—Kurohime, Takahime, Aohiko, and Murasakihime—spend the entire night without sleep, whispering and indulging in secret talk.

Takahime: 'Everyone, I cannot say this in a loud voice, but do you all know that while Tamateruhiko-sama and Tamateruhime-sama are both important, the treasure of the authentic Shinsei achievement, which is even more essential and fundamental, has been lost?'

Aohiko let out a startled cry of 'Eh!', and in his surprise, he nearly fell backward, only just managing to steady himself.

Takahime: 'Now, now, Aohiko-sama... your name is Wakahiko, but... I said that out of habit, so please forgive me. Since the color of young leaves is green, Wakahiko or Aohiko, it all passes well enough... However, please calm yourself and listen. If others were to hear such talk, Takaamahara would be in a great Ōsaudō, and we must devise some Kufū. I have not told this to anyone yet, but a truly worrisome matter has arisen.'

Kurohime: 'What is this worrisome matter that has occurred? If it is within my power, I shall gladly serve you, even at the cost of my life.'

Takahime: 'The truth is, Otama-no-kata has been snatched away by the evil gods of Brahmanism, and as her whereabouts are currently unknown, I have even reported this to Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto-sama, and we are deeply worried.'

Hearing this, Kurohime, Murasakihime, and Wakahiko turned deathly pale, and after saying 'Hee,' they were left stunned, merely looking at one another, completely at a loss.

Takahime: 'You all, if you are this shocked just because Otama-no-kata has been snatched away, that will never do; you must steady your resolve. As it is written in the Ofudesaki, "If your spirit is not polished, you will tremble in times of crisis. If you keep your spirit polished, no matter what worry arises, you can remain steady and endure it with ease," but there is still one more master of surprises.'

Murasakihime: 'Takahime-sama, what is this master of surprises? Please, I beg you to tell us. I, too, will do everything in my power to serve.'

Takahime: 'When I say the master, I mean that the jewel has been stolen.'

Murasakihime: 'You mean that Otama-no-kata?'

§ 2

Takahime: 'It is a jewel, yes, an orb, but this jewel is different from such common jewels. It is a great disturbance that would turn heaven and earth upside down. I would like to tell you all, but you are not sufficiently composed, so I cannot do anything. Ah, I wish there were people who could become more reliable instruments of the Kamisama.'

Kurohime: 'What is this jewel, if I may ask?'

Takahime: 'It is a golden orb, and it has been stolen.'

Kurohime: 'Is it Kotoyoriwake-sama, or Takayamahiko-sama, who... in such a place... and who, and how... could have... taken it?'

Takahime: 'Oh, you are slow to understand; it is not a kintama. It is the golden orb of the Mototsumitama, the Niyoihotsushiyu treasure, which brings about the Shinsei, brought from Seiunzan and hidden by Haniyasuhikonokami-sama at Okefuseyama... because everyone was careless, it has finally been stolen. This must surely have been snatched away by the Brahman Kyō; it will be a great disaster, will it not?'

Kurohime: 'This is terrible; what should we do? Shall we inquire of Kotoyoriwakenomikoto-sama?'

Wakahiko: 'This has become a troublesome matter; shall I go and inquire quietly?'

Takahime: 'I have been going to consult the leader of the Kotoyoriwake sect about such matters quietly many, many times since the other day, but he says this and that, telling me, "Just stay silent, the Kamisama will surely do something about it," and he maintains an attitude as if he would not move even if the great mountains were to collapse, so I am so anxious and agitated that I cannot stand or sit still; that is why I have gathered you all here today, thinking that we must do something, and why I am consulting with you.'

Kurohime: 'This is yet another grave failure; was there no one patrolling at night?'

Takahime: 'As for that night watch, they let someone in who looked like a believer of the Sangokyō, and that fellow acted as a guide to steal the golden orb and fled somewhere. That is why the Kamisama says that each of you must be vigilant. Because they made the young ones, who are in their sleepy prime, stand night watch, and the old ones, who have trouble sleeping, tried to sleep by force and were lazy, the Kamisama inflicted punishment for the sake of their repentance. From now on, the old ones shall not sleep at night. Instead, you may sleep as much as you like during the day, but you must be vigilant at night, for I do not know what kind of things will happen from here on. If you let the young ones play during the day and stand night watch, nothing good will ever come of it. It would be better to let them sleep from the early evening and work during the day, but because the way the main executives do things does not accord with the divine will, such worries arise. Kurohime-sama, please be a little more careful.'

§ 3

Kurohime: 'Yes, yes, I shall be careful. No matter what one says, it is the Innen and Shiyōrai of the Mitama, so there is no Shikata. Since the Kamisama distinguishes and shows us which Mitama are to serve the Goyō of evil and which are to serve the Goyō of good, Takahime-sama said just before that "the punishment of the Kamisama has struck," but is that not a slight misunderstanding? For the Kamisama to have the essential treasure that He Himself Shigumi stolen by the enemy, and to punish us on top of that... is that not Oka? It is not that we had it stolen; in Tsumari, the Kamisama had the treasure of the Kamuwaza stolen. In other words, the punishment has struck the Kamisama. That is why, although Susanōnomikoto-sama has good points, because He is a Henjiyōniyoshi, He commits an Ōshikujiri in the midst of things. When they say the weft shuttle falls or the thread breaks, it must be about this. If only the warp were kept standing in a Hitosuji, such things would not happen. Ah, but since even the Kamisama cannot prevail against the Tokiyojisetsu, once we have undergone Kaishin, there is no other way for us but to follow the Tenka of the time. If the Taishiyō does not remain firm, even those below will suffer Nangi. They say that if one horse goes mad, a thousand horses go mad, so I wish for a good Taishiyō Kamisama. No matter what, when the Mitama of a Henjiyōniyoshi insists on its own way, such disciplinary measures emerge. Even the Kamisama, after all, has failures, you see.'

Wakahiko: 'Hey, Kurohime-sama, is your way of thinking not a bit off? If I may say so, it is because your supervision is poor that things have come to this. You set aside your own responsibility and, secondly, you shift the responsibility onto the Mizu no Mitama-san, do you not? Even if you say He is a Kamisama who bears a thousand seats of Okido... that is too much; is your logic not full of derailments?'

Kurohime: 'I might derail a little; since a great commotion of natural disasters is occurring... it seems likely that there would be one or two derailments of trains and electric cars.'

Takahime: 'No matter how long we keep saying such things, there is no sign that the golden jewel will return, and there is no reason to expect that Otama-no-kata will come back. Here, we must act as one in a thousand, stake our lives to take back the golden jewel, and search for and bring back Otama-no-kata; otherwise, first of all, the responsibility of us and you will not be settled.'

At this moment, two men opened the front door with a clatter and entered; Wakahiko saw them quickly and said,

Wakahiko: 'Hey, aren't you Teruji and Korombo? Are you keeping a tight watch?'

Teruji: 'I am keeping the watch faithfully, but ever since that scoundrel Toku, who was here before, conspired with the spy of the Brahmin Mukadehime and stole the golden jewel on the sly, I do not know why I am even keeping watch; it has become a name without substance, and it is so ridiculous that I am starting to feel like giving up on the watch.'

Takahime: 'What, so that is why Toku has not been seen around here lately; he was the one pulling the strings? What a wicked fellow he is; that is why the Kamisama says not to trust people easily. Everyone, please listen well; because you took too lightly the Fudesaki which says not to deal with anyone without consulting the Kami, even if they come to you with sweet talk, this is how it has ended up.'

Kurohime: 'Excuse me, Takahime-sama, you always used to say that Toku-san was great and a man of sincerity, and that if only people like him became believers, the Ananai-kyō could turn the palm of the world in an instant, and you told me to learn from Toku-sama and take him as a model. If I had listened to what you said and taken him as a model, I do not know what kind of commotion would have started by now. We would have ended up in a miserable state, like having one's anus sucked by a soft-shelled turtle.'

Takahime: 'Kurohime-sama, what are you saying? Who said such a thing? Go and wash your hands for a moment.'

Kotoyoriwake-no-Mikoto, feeling restless and unable to sleep, bathed in the moonlight and came strolling in front of this high hall, leaning on his staff. He caught the sound of the dispute inside, pulled open the rain shutter himself, and entered.

Kotoyoriwake: 'Hey, everyone, you are working very hard until late; are you having some kind of splendid discussion?'

Takahime: 'Are you not the general of Takamagahara? How can you speak so lightheartedly? The essential, fundamental treasure has been lost, and the fleshly palace of Otama-no-kata has gone missing; we are unable to sleep properly at night and are worried with red eyes like this, yet you are not bothered at all? Because you are acting so unconcerned, all the members of the executive staff are saying things like "Kaminagara" or "It is convenient," and without doing what they ought to do, they are standing with their hands in their pockets, just staring blankly; please pull yourself together a little.'
