Chapter 2: Horse 上 Song [1127]

馬上歌〔1127〕

§ 1

Leaving behind the rocky grotto of the high-shining Takateruyama, where the Miizu of Kami dwells, the Kshatriya of the capital of Iruna, the retinue of King Seran, bestrode their chestnut horses.

Treading upon the roots of trees and rocks along the mountain path where wolves howl, and crossing the field path where the winter wind rages, they arrived at the foot of the pass of Teruyama, famous for its sound, with the hoofbeats of their horses ringing out valiantly.

While riding, they sang in abundance, advancing toward the capital.

King Seran sang quietly upon his horse:

'Following in the footsteps of my father, I, whose Mitama was darkened, became the King of the land of Iruna. Lacking in Tokubau, I erred in the Tōsotsu of my Buka, and at last, I allowed the Umori-no-tsukasa to fall into the hands of the Jiyashin. Though I was in the noble position of a Brahmin, inheriting the Oshihe of Kami, and ought to have shown a true Mohan to the Kunitami morning and evening, I made light of the grace of Kami. Without knowing it, the clouds of Manshin rose up, and my Murakimo heart wandered in darkness. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. May the Mitama be blessed, for when the heart is fragrant with a thousand flowers and a hundred blossoms, the Northern Light—'

'Helped by the grace of Kami, and by the Oshihe that leaves no water leaking through, the darkness of my heart has finally cleared. The sun and moon shine in the sky, and the sky of my heart has cleared up. The achievement of the Hime-Mikoto, who adorns the autumn fields, shone as Kiyoteruhime. Now that I have realized the noble Miizu-no-oshihe, where Kami appears in the world to distinguish between the good Kami and the Jiyashin, I, Seran, shall purify my office of Kami. Repenting of my past wrongs, and while striving for the Zen-gen Bi-kō of the San-go, which is the one Sincerity in heaven and earth, I shall manifest the heavenly duty of the office of Kami perfectly and in detail, and become a mirror for this world. Yasudarahime, Ryūun, Kami is with you.'

'The body that entrusts itself to the Great Way of Kami, no matter how the Ōkuninushi-no-Kami may appear to invade the Iruna Castle with mysterious divine power, why should I fear? As long as I possess the Yamato-gokoro of Shikishima, Kami will surely save us into peace. Knowing that man is the child of Kami and the palace of Kami, and that we are those who serve the Keirin that opens heaven and earth, let us grind our bones and crush our bodies. For the sake of Kami and for the sake of the world, let us unite our hearts and solidify our strength as one, and together repay the great mercy and compassion of Kami. Even if the slope of the Teruyama pass is steep, once the footsteps of the horse of the heart are aligned, we shall surely advance to the capital of glory, just as we shall accomplish all things.'

'Advance, advance, now advance, treading the path of Sincerity. Advance quickly to the capital of victory, advance, advance, now advance. Making the Oshihe of Kami your strength and the path of Sincerity your staff, the horse of the heart shall valiantly ascend and descend. My Murakimo heart is already like the autumn sky. The Onrai is before my eyes, and as it begins to shine, I am filled with the joyful feeling of a thousand flowers and a hundred blossoms blooming in the spring fields. For one who advances on the path of Sincerity, any twists and turns are like the dew drops on summer grass; as they vanish in the morning sun, their destruction is before my eyes. Take courage, take courage, together, O promising Kami-tsukasa, for the future of your life is reliable. Ah, Kamunagara, Kamunagara. May the Mitama be blessed.'

Yasudarahime, following behind King Seran, sang as she rode, her voice quiet:

'I, who was born into the lineage of King Seran, the Kshatriya of the land of Iruna, was the betrothed of my parents. I lived, wishing morning and evening to Kami that I might become the consort of King Seran and protect the land of Iruna forever. Yet, unexpectedly, as if I had disobeyed the awe-inspiring heart of Kami, I was driven away to the land of Teruman. I fell to becoming the wife of Shiyaru, of the lowly Vaisya house, and spent my days sighing and lamenting over my unhappy life. Just then, a thunderbolt from the clear sky shook my heart, and a tragic situation pressed upon me. Thinking, "Do the Bonten, Taishakuten, and Jizaiten not exist in this world?"'

§ 2

The moon and sun of Shinniyo shone brightly in the Shinten. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. In this world thick with Akuma, that you are so rich in Jinji, I wonder if the true Kami exists. Tears of Kanshiya became a river, and my sunken heart rose up. As I pushed the oar into the boat of salvation and wandered in the Rakuen of Tengoku Jiyōdo, how joyful it was that the unexpected Setsuteiri, the party of King Seran, came seeking me. Before the two pillars, who had been separated for so long, could exchange words of reunion, the Kamuyogoto taught solemnly at the office of the Kitateru Kami were divided between the two upon their necks.

Wiping away the tears of Kanshiya, they mounted their horses and looked back at the Ihayado, reluctant to leave. As they rode, the storm blew across the Bajiyō. When they finally crossed the Noji and arrived at the Teruyamatauge, the Momijiba on the tips of the Kigi had scattered before they knew it, looking lonely. The autumn wind blowing over the peaks made even the lonely Yamaji feel somehow different. As I, who follow you, climb, my heart feels like spring, and my Murakimo heart is filled with a Harugokochi. Guided by the teachings of Kami, I am happy to advance. When we arrive at the capital of Inna, Samarihime-sama, who is the child of the official of the Right Guard, will surely gaze upon my figure and be troubled in her heart.

Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. Following the will of the Kami of heaven and earth in all things, the attachment of love is like the leaves of the autumn fields scattering in the wind. Cleansing it away cleanly and affectionately, holding hands as sisters, we shall uphold the one sincerity. Manifesting the divine power of the Bankyō, we shall be sung of as the flowers of the capital of Inna-kuni and pass down our honor for a thousand generations. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. King Seran, who bears the name of the dragon of the earth, listen as I mount the pure white horse and manifest my sincerity. The clouds of love that enveloped me are swept away by the wind of Shinato, blown by the divine spirit of Mizu.

They have become free of even a speck of dust. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. The Imperial Kami dwells purely and coolly in our souls, and has illuminated our thoroughly defiled bodies valiantly. Advance, advance, come, advance. Devotedly on the path of sincerity, advance with all your heart. The capital of victory has drawn near. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. May your spirits be blessed. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, sincerity alone saves the world. Treading the path of sincerity, rebuilding the pillar of heaven and earth, and being sung of as the flowers of heaven and earth, we shall bear the fruits of abundant sincerity.

While tying them heavily upon the branches, the Kushimitama, which unravels the heart that has been tangled until now, and the blessings of the Kami of Sofuto make me, who advances, valiant. Ah, Kamunagara Kamunagara. May your spirits be blessed.

The road became increasingly steep, and they encountered a difficult path where the utmost caution was required in the saddle. However, all were masters of horsemanship; it was a state where there was no person in the saddle and no horse under the saddle, and they climbed in a single file, filled with high spirits while having their faces blown by the winter wind. Ryūun began to sing, rich in the saddle.

Hi-hi-hi-hi, you horse. It is a steep slope at Teruyamatauge. Please be careful, Kurige-sama. It would be unbearable if you were to tumble. Hi-hi, do-do, hi-hi, do-do. Since I have heard that the journey of human life is like climbing a steep slope, I think of the state of the world of impermanence and go hi-hi-hi. A longing fills my heart. Ryūun, who dropped his umbilical cord in the house of the Shuda in the land of the moon, is driven by unexpected desires like a heavenly horse galloping through the sky. The island of Shiro floating between the waves. The divine official of the capital of the divine land. Hi-hi, tighten the reins again. It is dangerous if you do not hold on tight. Banishing King Sagaren, and taking advantage of the wind and clouds that arise at that moment,

I dance up to the heavens, and my heart and mind suddenly go mad, hi-hi-hi. The horror of having become the source of cruelty and lawlessness. The oni and great serpents lurking in the heart, the power of the ugly Kami, are just like the horse crossing the steep slope with its mane bristling. Without end, the miso soup flies up to the steppes, and in high spirits above the clouds, hi-hi-hi-hi, do-do-do, I look down upon the world beneath heaven. Just as I was laughing, thinking if there is anyone in the three thousand worlds as lucky as I am, my fate ran out, and my wild spirit was crushed at the office of the Kitateru Kami, and I was suddenly sent to the bottom of the valley.

Ryūun: '...' (Having finished singing, he leaped lightly off the horse, sat down on a rock nearby, and rested his breath.)

King Seran and the others followed the words of Ryūun, jumped off their horses, and both men and horses rested for a while.
