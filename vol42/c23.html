Chapter 23: Dragon Mountain 別 [1148]

竜山別〔1148〕

§ 1

Riūn sings with quiet words.

Riūn: 'The Kami have appeared, distinguishing between good and evil, right and wrong. The Kamunahohi who created this world, and the great Ōnahohi of vast heart. Using the Mitama of Nahohi as the warp, and the Mitama of Mizu as the weft, a brocade has been woven. The Toeu crest shines upon the sacred banner. Helping the good and bringing the Magatsumi to submit to the path of truth, this is the pure and righteous path of the Kami. I am the Kamutsukasa Riūn. Following the path of Ural, I crossed over to the island of Ceylon. While I wandered here and there, the time arrived, and by the grace of being discovered by Hime Kerisu at the Kaujijiyō, the gate of Tōriu has finally opened, and I have ascended with the speed of a climbing eel.'

Riūn: 'My heart grew arrogant, and in the end, I, a Shikotsukasa who had exhausted every limit of Akugiyakubudau, have been revived by the admonition of Kitaterugami, and oh, what joy it is to have repented of my past wrongs and returned to Magokoro. The dark clouds that hung upon my heart have cleared away, and I have become one whose body shines with the sun and moon in my breast. This is entirely the precious grace of the San-Go no Kami. My gratitude cannot be expressed in words. Even the Akugiyakubudau Riūn has been looked upon and heard by the precious heart of the infinite and merciful Ōkami, and I, a practitioner who had not expected such a thing in my heart, am filled with joy to be inspecting and touring the moon countries of over seven thousand nations, following the teachings of Kitaterugami.'

Riūn: 'What are wind and rain to me? Compared to the sins of the past, this is but one ten-thousandth of the repayment of grace; it is truly precious beyond measure. Blessed by the grace of the Kami, my heart shines clear. Accompanied by Kiyoteruhime-hime and the golden Hime-mikoto, I appeared in the capital of Inna, and oh, the joy of serving and devoting my heart and body for the sake of King Seran. Now that it has come to this, Riūn has no desire left in the capital of Inna. Ah, how brave, how brave! I am a messenger of the truth, clear and joyful. Renaming myself Tatsuyamawake, I shall cause the Magatsumi that scatter in the four directions to bow down, just as grass leaves bend before the wind.'

Riūn: 'Without leaving a single one, I shall turn their words and raise the cry of victory for the land of the Kami. What a promising departure this is. Now, now, farewell, now, now, farewell. Kitaterugami, King Seran, Hime Samari, Kāruchin, and the Left-guarding Kamutsukasa Kūrinsu, and all other hundred officials, I bid you farewell here. Teēmusu, Rēbu, and the Kamutsukasa Karu, I shall now take charge and tour the moon kingdoms, and raise and nurture them as pillars of the precious and pure Ōkami. I shall return to the presence of Hinodewake at the Iso mansion on Mount Ubusuna, and with great courage, I shall report my return, living in accordance with the will of the Kami, and praying before the Kami.'

Riūn: 'How joyful it is to be one who illuminates far and wide the words of the Kami and the sovereign, who shakes the heavens. My soul, which had been disturbed by the evil Kami, has been blown away by the wind of Shinato. Now, farewell, I shall rename myself Tatsuyamawake and transmit the path for the sake of the people of the world. How joyful it is to advance through the wilderness together with the Kamutsukasa Rēbu, Karu, and Teēmusu. Under the protection of the precious Kami, I shall advance peacefully through the wilderness. I long for Haruna, where the divine pillar of the life of the golden Hime-kami advances. From here, I shall tour the moon countries and soon advance to the capital of Haruna. If you go, sovereign, transmit well to the pillar of Onikumawake that I am Tatsuyamawake. If you hear that Riūn is on the path of the evil Kami, even Onikumawake will surely be struck speechless. Nevertheless, having cast aside the evil and having had the flowers of Magokoro bloom, I am a true human.'

Kogane: 'I shall transmit the words of the Kamutsukasa Tatsuyamawake well to my lord. If it reaches the ears of the Kamutsukasa Ōkuninushi-kami, he will surely change his heart. Who would believe that Riūn, the Kamutsukasa of the Kami, is truly calling himself Tatsuyamawake?'

Riūn: 'After the clouds of the tornado have cleared, there is the moonlight that shines across the sky. The pine wind clears the sky, which was clouded as both sun and moon were wrapped in the tornado. The wind of Shinato blowing through the branches of the pine is the sacred water and fire of the Kami.'

Kiyoteru: 'How interesting. I cannot help but smile as I see off the appearance of Tatsuyamawake. It is interesting to see you depart while wearing a headband on your shining head.'

Riūn: 'The headband that shines on my bald head is to make the evil ones roll their tongues. To make them roll their tongues is not to crush the weak, but only to make them submit to the path of truth. The mysterious and miraculous evil Kami have suddenly become good, and now I am a precious Kamutsukasa.'

§ 2

Yasudara: 'The brave figure of Tatsuyamawake, how well the monk-style headband suits him.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'Well then, behold the martial bearing of Tatsuyamawake in this mansion. Fields, mountains, grasses, trees, and rivers, I shall illuminate them all at once with this bald head. My body, walking the great path of the Kami, shines because the Kami dwells within. My bald head shines without shadow, appearing as the Mikoto of Kiyoteru Hime. Though I have aged, my complexion has shifted into the color and fragrance of Ōgonhime.'

Ōgon: 'How ridiculous. Though my Karatama has aged, my heart is like the wild grass of a young spring. As I advance into the Kamikuni, there is no distinction between old and young, so I am always spirited. Though I have crossed the slope of Musoji, my heart is perhaps the elegant figure of a Nihachi.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'This is a blunder; my Kototama slipped and fell in an unintended direction. Ōgonhime-no-Mikoto, I pray you forgive the error of calling you old. I forgot the riddle that says a sparrow does not forget the Ondori even at one hundred. Whatever may happen, with a broad heart, Kamunahohi, please look upon me with Minaho, Ōgonhimetsukasa.'

Ōgon: 'Placing my body in the Furaufushi Mikuni of the Kami, I rejoice and enjoy the spring of Tokoyo. Even if I were to abandon this world and ascend to the Mikuni, I would not forget your figure.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'The bald head of Tatsuyamawake-no-Kami-no-Mikoto, I would not forget it even if I tried. The moon and the sun shine upon this head, and it permeates my heart, making my chest bright.'

Seran: 'How interesting. The words of one who has submitted to the path of truth are free of sin and blame.'
