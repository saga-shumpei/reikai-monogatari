Chapter 14: 吃 Wonder [1139]

吃驚〔1139〕

§ 1

Umorinokami no Karuchin, in high spirits, entered the inner room where Kiyoteruhime and Serisu-hime were talking.

Karuchin: 'Ah, Yasudara-hime-dono, Serisu-hime-dono, it has been a long time since I last saw you. I had intended to see you yesterday, but a slight complication arose, and I was forced to be rude and miss our meeting.'

Serisu-hime: 'That sounds like you have been very busy. I hear you have had quite an active time, bumping heads on the road, kindly visiting graves, being deceived by a tanuki, and jumping into a river. My sister was impressed, saying that you are truly Umori-sama. Do not stand there like that; come, sit here. I would like you to tell us the whole story of yesterday from beginning to end.'

Karuchin scratched his head and replied,

Karuchin: 'Heh, it is not as if I have been particularly active. It was unavoidable due to the momentum of the times, and I was moved in a Kamunagarateki way. Who on earth came to report such things to you?'

Serisu-hime: 'I was watching you with my Tengantsū from here for a moment. First of all, it is a relief that you were not injured. By the way, how is Yufutesu-sama doing? I have been waiting since yesterday, but as he has not shown his face, I am very worried.'

Karuchin: 'Eh, what are you saying? Did Yufutesu not come yesterday? He had his cheeks pinched quite hard and his face was swollen. I heard you bullied him terribly, but you should not play such pranks. It is more graceful for a woman to act like a woman.'

Serisu-hime: 'I have not seen Yufutesu-sama’s face for some time now. That must be some misunderstanding on your part. You were likely toyed with by an old fox of Adashino, thinking it was me. What a troublesome person you are.'

Karuchin: 'In these times, demons are running rampant, and it seems old tanuki and foxes are appearing here and there to torment men. Wahahaha.'

Serisu-hime: 'Excuse me, sister, why are you looking down so shyly? You have been so noisy and anxious, so why are you so timid? Come now, why don't you greet him?'

Kiyoteruhime, in a thin voice and looking shy,

Kiyoteruhime: 'Hai.'

She said only that and looked down even further.

Karuchin: 'Ahahaha, it seems you have become quite shy. As expected of a young lady. No, without that, a woman has no value. It is troublesome that women these days do not think a man is worth even three mon. No, I am deeply pleased. A crabapple blossom, damp with rain and bowing its head ever so slightly, has an indescribable charm. Ehehehe.'

Serisu-hime: 'Excuse me, Umori-sama, is there not a long string hanging from your mouth? Please pull it in quickly. It does not look very good if my sister sees it. Hohohoho, look at those narrow eyes. It really seems that you are sweet-hearted toward my sister, Umori-sama. Congratulations, congratulations. Now, sister, won't you lift your face? Why are you acting like a twelve or thirteen-year-old girl? How will your love succeed with such timidity? Even watching from the side, I find it truly frustrating.'

Karuchin: 'It seems she is quite shy. No, Serisu-hime-sama, it appears that with you, her sister, here, the princess feels bashful and cannot say what is on her mind. Would it be possible for you to step out for a moment?'

Serisu-hime: 'Hohohoho, that is an easy task. In that case, this nuisance will hide herself for a while, so please enjoy yourselves intimately.'

原文（近代文語）

右守司のカールチンは意気揚々として清照姫、セーリス姫の話してゐる奥の間へ入り来り、

『あゝヤスダラ姫殿、セーリス姫殿、えらい御無沙汰を致しました。昨日はお目にかかる積りで居りましたが、少しく差支が出来まして到頭失礼を致しました』

『それはお忙しいことで厶りましたな。道中で頭の鉢合せをしたり、親切にお墓参りをなされたり、狸に騙されたり、河へ飛び込んだり、大変な御活動で厶りましたさうですな。流石は右守さまだと云つて、姉さまも感心して居やはりました。さう立ちはだかつて居らずに、マアここにお坐りなさいませ。昨日の一伍一什を一つ聞かして頂きたいもので厶ります』

カールチンは頭を掻きながら、

『ヘー、別に活動したと……云ふ訳でもありませぬ。時の勢やむを得ず、惟神的にさされたのですよ。誰が又そんな事を御報告に参りましたかな』

『私の天眼通で一寸此処から透視して居りましたよ。まづまづお怪我がなくて結構でしたな。時にユーフテスさまは如何してゐられますかな。昨日から待つてゐますが、お顔を見せなさらぬので大変に気を揉んで居ります』

『エ、何と仰有います。ユーフテスは昨日来たぢやありませぬか。大変に頬辺を抓られて顔を腫らして居ましたよ。大変苛めなさつたと云ふ事ですが、さう悪戯をするものぢやありませぬぞ。女はヤツパリ女らしう為さる方が床しいですな』

『この間からユーフテス様のお顔を拝んだ事はありませぬ。そりや何かのお考へ違ひでせう。大方私だと思つて仇志野の古狐にでも弄ばれて居らつしやつたのでせう。何とまア困つた人だなア』

『何分此頃は悪魔横行しまして、彼方にも此方にも古狸や狐が出現し、男を悩ますと見えますわい。ワハヽヽヽ』

『もしもし姉さま、何恥かしさうに俯向いて居られますの。あれほど八釜しく焦れて居ながら気の弱い、何です、早く御挨拶をなさいませぬか』

清照姫、細い声で恥かしさうに、

『ハイ』

と云つたきり益々俯向く。

『アハヽヽヽ、余程恥かしくなつて来たと見えるな。流石はお嬢さまだ。いやさうなくては女の価値がない。今時の女は男を三文とも思つてゐないから困るのだ。いやズンと気に入つた。海棠の花でも雨に湿つてチツとばかり俯向いて居る所に、得もいはれぬ風情のあるものだ。エヘヽヽヽ』

『もし右守さま、口から何だか長い紐が下がつて居るぢやありませぬか。早うお手繰り遊ばせ。姉さまが御覧になつたら、あまり見つともよくありませぬよ。ホヽヽヽヽ、あのまア細い目わいのう。本当に右守さまも、姉さまにスウヰートハートして居られると見えますな。お目出度いお目出度い。これ姉さま、お顔を上げなさらぬか。何ですか十二か十三の娘の様に、そんな気の弱い事で如何して恋が成功しますか。私、側に見てゐても本当にジレツたいわ』

『どうやら恥かしいと見えるわい。いやセーリス姫さま、姉妹の貴女がここに居らつしやると、姫も気がひけて思ふ事も云へないと見えます。何卒少し席を外して貰ふ事は出来ますまいかな』

『ホヽヽヽヽ、それはお易い御用で厶います。それなら邪魔者は暫く姿を隠しますから、何卒シツポリと御両人様お楽しみ』

と態とにプリンとして見せ、畳を二つ三つボンボンと蹶つて早々に自分の居間へ走つて行く。

§ 2

Karchin: 'Ohohoho, what an interesting thing this is. Yet, there is an indescribable meumi in it. It seems Princess Seris is jealous of our romance. No, Princess Yasudara-sama, Seris-chama has returned. Come, there is no need for any enriyo with anyone anymore. Lift your face. And why do we not settle our shiyōrai gosaudan in peace?'

Yasudara: 'Ohohoho, what a detestable man you are. You are the ritsupa Umorisama of Iluna Castle, and you have a wife, Princess Tena-sama, with a face like a ritsupa, ritsupa botamochi, do you not? If you say such things to a demodo woman like me, does it not concern your meiyo? Please stop this at a proper kagen.'

Karchin: 'This is unexpected, such okotoba from the princess, contrary to my expectations. I did not think it would be so. What a strange change this is.'

Yasudara: 'I have not changed in the slightest. It is your heart that has changed.'

Karchin: 'No, wareware have not changed in the slightest. Is it not the princess's heart that has changed completely?'

Yasudara: 'Is that so? I loved you so much that I could not help it, but today, for some reason, I have come to dislike you so much that it makes my hair stand on end. Since the you I love has changed into the you I dislike, the honnin must still be you. It is because the honnin has changed that you appear changed to the eyes of the ahitegata, myself.'

Karchin: 'That sort of thing does not matter. Come, let us finally conclude our jiyōyaku today. Since I am now the shiyujin and setsuteiri of this taujiyō, you shall be my seihi; surely you have no complaints.'

Yasudara: 'I dislike you because I see no future in a man as cold-hearted as you. After all the hardship and suffering we have endured to build our position as husband and wife, now that you are about to advance to become a setsuteiri, you indulge in arrogance, and after heartlessly sending your wife to be killed, you think to make a woman like me, who has no experience and is worthless, your wife—I absolutely dislike such a man. If you find another beautiful woman, I might end up like the second Princess Tena-sama and have my life taken, so I shall refrain from being a partner to such a dangerous person.'

Karchin: 'It is troublesome for you to say such things now. When Lord Ōkami-nushi was going to send five hundred troops to support us in achieving our goal, I declined at your request, and furthermore, I gathered all our allies and sent them on an expedition to the land of Haruna. Now that our defenses are thin, how can this Umorishi carry on if you turn your back on me? Could you show a little consideration? Eeen!'

Yasudara: 'Hohohoho, you are such a fool, which is why I dislike you. You have no eyes or nose when it comes to women; you really are a hopeless blockhead. Ufufu.'

Karchin: 'Hey, Princess Yasudara-sama, you are black-hearted. Please stop teasing me at a proper kagen. It is a man's honor, you know.'

From the next room, a man's cough was heard: 'Ahem, ahem.' This was Princess Ōgon, who had been listening with amusement to the exchange between the two and had deliberately mimicked the voice of King Seran to cough. Princess Kiyoteru lowered her voice and said,

Kiyoteru: 'As you can hear, the King is waiting in the next room with only a sliding door between us, so it is troublesome for you to say everything so bluntly as you do. Please be a little more careful.'

Karchin nodded his head two or three times and said in a low voice,

Karchin: 'Hmm, alright, alright, ah! So that is it. I thought you were saying something strange, but you said those things because the King was in the next room. Alright, I understand. I have grasped it now, so please do not worry.'

Kiyoteru: 'Hohoho, what is it that you have grasped? Do not deal with a woman like me, and please negotiate with someone more ritsupa.'

Just as she said this, Miru came running in a hurry and said,

Miru: 'Excuse me, Umorisama, Princess Yasudara-sama, a person who looks exactly like you, Princess Yasudara-sama, has just returned with the King.'

Karchin said,

Karchin: 'What, Princess Yasudara has returned? The King has returned? Well, what should I do?'

He folded his arms, sat cross-legged, and sank into thought for a while.

『何、ヤスダラ姫が帰つた。王様がお帰り、ハテ、如何したものかな』

と腕を組み胡坐をかいて、暫し思案に沈みつつあつた。
