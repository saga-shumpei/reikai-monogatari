Chapter 26: 宣直 Song [1151]

宣直歌〔1151〕

§ 1

Tatsuyamawake: 'The world, clouded over in Tokoyami, is illuminated by the noble head of Tatsuyamawake. As a sign that both body and soul shine, even the head of Tatsuyamawake shines purely.'

Thames: 'How grateful I am for the Kamutsukasa whose head shines; the Kami who gave this to me is truly awesome. Rebu, Karu, you too rejoice, for we serve the Kami whose head shines, and we are his Mitomo.'

Kiyoteru: 'How wonderful is the Kamutsukasa of Tatsuyamawake; he shall illuminate even the land of Tokoyo.'

Ōgon: 'The shining head of Tatsuyamawake must be the Daujiyō for Kidō Enshifu. The head that illuminates both the moon and the sun shall surely make the Ametsuchi my own.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'Upon my head, which shines with the moon, the sun, and the stars, the Kami of Ametsuchi still resides. It is said that the Kami dwells in a Shiyōjiki head; that Kamubashira is truly Medeta.'

Karu: 'The head of Tatsuyamawake, which harbors the moon, the sun, and even the stars, is truly a manifestation of the Kami.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'Haha! Even though my face shines at the words of these Hitobito who are utterly astonished.'

Summary: 'Though his head shines and he appears as an Oito, one knows he is young by his blushing face.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'I am Tatsuyamawake, who has been reborn into this manifest world, growing young again and again. I am the Miko of Kototamawake, the reincarnation of Tatsuyamawake. Having entered and exited Keniu many times, I have again brought shame upon myself in this world. My spirit is a twisted one that followed Shikoharawake in the Jerusalem of the Kamiyo. I am the descendant of the spirit of Tatsuyamawake, who disobeyed the command of the Hime of Kuniteru. Nevertheless, the Kami must have forgiven me, for today I have become a Kamutsukasa once more. I am happy that I have cast away so many sins and impurities into the waves of the rough shore.'

Thames: 'Come then, shall we set out on our journey with the Kamutsukasa of Tatsuyamawake before the presence of our Lord?'

Tatsuyamawake: 'Rebu, Karu, and Thames, you three shall open the path of three-five with me.'

Kiyoteru: 'Come then, Karuchin of the Right-Guardian Kamutsukasa, our bond shall not end; let us meet again. Please, I beg you, forgive my sins; do not hold resentment, do not harbor hatred.'

Karuchin: 'How foolish I was to think that the Kamutsukasa of the Hime of Kiyoteru was the Kamutsukasa of the Hime of Yasudara. How foolish I was to fall into the trap of laughter, believing that I was truly loved and cherished from the heart. Come then, Hime of Kiyoteru, proceed righteously upon the path of the Great Kami.'

Seris: 'I am ashamed of myself for having disguised myself, wearing a robe of love that was not in my heart. I shall revere the command of the Great Lord and look up to Yuufutesu as my husband. Do not think of me as a shameless woman; it was a falsehood born of truth. Even the lies have now become the truth, and I am now with the one I manipulated.'

Yuufutesu: 'If everything is as the Kami leaves it, then be at peace, Hime of Seris. Having layered robes of love, I have now become one who cannot easily cast them off.'

Samoa: 'I, too, have dared to commit the ugly deeds of the ugly, going against the Great Path of the Kami. Perhaps it is the heart of the Kami that binds me in a marriage contract with the person I once loathed. Now, I have come to love him; I shall pledge a thousand generations to Mammosu, my husband. Mammosu, please forgive my false and impure actions.'

Mammosu: 'Everything has vanished like the morning dew, and today the morning sun shines in my heart. The shadow of the morning sun gradually reaches the castle of Iruna in the west; the evening pledge is truly reliable. Shall clouds not frequently hang over the path of love where we embrace in the night of Tokoyami?'

Samoa: 'The words of my reliable husband feel like meeting in the spring when flowers bloom. Let us be intimate and close for a thousand and eight thousand generations, and open the path for the sake of the Hitobito of the world.'

Hokkou: 'The day is noble, for the clouds and mist that wrap the Ametsuchi have cleared away without a trace.'

Seeran: 'The darkness of the eternal night has cleared, and I am illuminated by the light of the three-five moon.'

Ōgon: 'The moon and the sun are above the castle of Iruna in the west, and the flock of crows celebrates a thousand generations.'

Kiyoteru: 'The light of the Great Kami shines everywhere, and there is no shadow of evil in the castle of Iruna.'

Tatsuyamawake: 'Come then, I shall take my leave of the Great Lord and go forth to illuminate the mountain path of the moon.'

Haruman: 'Whipping the horse of Komahiko's heart, he gallops toward the capital of Haruna. I am happy to be the one who advances with the spirit of the spring horse to convey the path of three-five.'

Yasudara: 'Parting with sad sleeves from the Great Lord, I am now embarking on a happy journey.'

Karuchin: 'Come then, hundred Kamutsukasa, proceed safely upon the Great Path of the Kami.'

Kuurinsu: 'The castle of Iruna is clear of the night of Tokoyami, and the Left-Guardian Kamutsukasa is also courageous. Hime of Yasudara, take care and proceed through the fields where tigers lie. Follow the teachings of the Kami, the Kannagara, and travel safely to the capital of Haruna.'

Yasudara: 'Come then, Father, live long and serve the Lord faithfully.'

Thus, they exchanged poems, bade farewell with regret, and set out on their journey of propagation. The winter sun shone brilliantly, casting its light obliquely upon the heads of the propagandists. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haese.
