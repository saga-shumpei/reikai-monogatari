Chapter 21: 応酬 Song [1146]

応酬歌〔1146〕

§ 1

When Kami appears on the surface, distinguishing between Zenshin and Jiyashin, the reason the Lord God sent humans down to this world was with the Oboshime to open the prosperity of the Tengoku Jiyōdo. Selecting pure souls, they appear in Takaamahara, and while opening the path of Fūfu, they begin Reiteki Kuwatsudō just as Genkaijin do. As emotion binds to emotion, Tennin men and women, while performing Mitonomaguhahi together, give birth to pure and righteous Reishi into the Chijiyō Sekai, sowing them into the bodies of Fūfu who are active in the Ningenkai. The Reishi sent down from Ame, through the Miiki of father and mother, suddenly enters the mother's body.

Based on the three bodies of the eight powers and Gaujiuriu of Dōseikaigiyōinchibungafu, it hides in the mother's body for ten months to complete its physical form, and using the palace of the Reishi as an engine, it appears in this world. People who are born as children of humans, being the children of Kami, while receiving education on Chijiyō to the fullest, develop both spirit and flesh, and at the dawn of their adulthood, they abandon this world and return to the garden of Tengoku. Indeed, the human physical body is a seedbed where the spiritual children of the Tennin who dwell in the Amatsu Mikuni complete their growth.

When the seeds are sown and the seedlings are transplanted into the fields of Tengoku, people finally leave the Genkai, resurrect in Ame, and participate in the divine work of the Tengoku Jiyōdo. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. The kingdom of Kami is constructed right before our eyes upon this earth, and as a reflection of the Tengoku Jiyōdo, we enjoy this short life, polishing and nurturing the Mototsu Mitama, pushing aside the clouds and mist to return to the land of Kami. Ah, how grateful, how grateful.

The Kshatriya of the capital of Iruna, King Seran, until now, because he did not know the news of the Amatsu Mikuni, had his heart driven by various desires on Chijiyō, but upon hearing the teachings of the Sangokyō, he gradually realized the noble mission of life. The wickedness and lawlessness of the right-hand guardian who plotted to achieve his desires in this short life was corrected and purified by the Tadahi, and he was saved with such courage. I am the Kami of Hokkōhiko, the Ame no Hitotsume no Kami-tsukasa, who has departed from Takateruyama.

Coming here in secret to protect and save your life, when I look, it is truly a magnificent sky today. The light of the sun and moon is refreshing, and the castle of Iruna has become a world where flowers bloom and scent forever. Yoshikohime, Kiyoteruhime, the noble life, Yasudarahime, starting with Ryūun-tsukasa, Thames, Rebu, Karu, Rīdā, and the other hundred officials, seeing the flowers of their pure hearts, my heart is filled with joy and courage.

If the teachings of the Sangokyō spread widely across Chijiyō, there will be no enemies and no allies. Good and evil, right and wrong, all alike will be moistened by the noble grace of Kami, and the appearance of Tengoku will be manifested on Chijiyō, just as we see it before our eyes now. Truly, the noble ancestor of the country, Kuninotokotachi no Ōkami, the Ōkami of the Mizu no Mitama, the Kami of Konohanahime who resides on Tenkyōzan, and the Kanayamahiko no Ōkami who guards the earth as well.

The Hinodewake, whose divine authority shines, and the divine virtue of the Hinode no Kami, may the Ame no Iwato of the eternal darkness that opens suddenly never be closed again; Kannagara. Before the presence of Kami, I, the Ame no Hitotsume no Kami-tsukasa of Hokkō, with the heart of Kami of supreme benevolence and supreme love, pray on behalf of all things in heaven and earth. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. May your Mitama be blessed.

While singing this, the Kami of Hokkō appeared from the inner room with a dignified air, such was the mystery. King Seran and all those present were filled with boundless wonder at the sudden descent of the Hitotsume no Kami, treated him with the utmost respect, and invited the Hokkō-kami to take the seat of honor.

Hokkō-kami looked around at everyone with a smile: 'The opening of the teachings of the Chihayaburu Kami, the Takemikazuchi-no-Kami of sincerity. Where is the life of Takemikazuchi-no-Kami? In the heart of the person who guards sincerity. Where is the kingdom of Takaamahara? In the heart of the person who is truly strong. Where is the divine residence of the life of Kuninotokotachi? In the Seika Tanden of your physical body. The ugly wind that rustles the leaves of the Iruna mountain has calmed, and what a peaceful sky it is today. Having departed from the mountain of Takateru, the light of the Kami of Hokkō must be shining.'

Seran: 'Illuminated by the light of the Kami who brings spiritual happiness, the darkness of Iruna has cleared away. I have completely forgotten the Karatama of Yasudarahime-sama, whom I loved and yearned for, in accordance with the will of the Kami. Yasudarahime-sama, Mikoto, take heed; it is not that I harbor a heart of hatred toward thee. Nevertheless, illuminated by the path of truth, the black clouds of love have vanished. My heart, which thinks of you beautifully and dearly, remains unchanged from now as it was in the past. I rein in the horse of my heart, which runs wild for the sake of the path and for the sake of the Yobito. Rather than uttering Yogoto that are not in my heart, I shall reveal my true feelings in accordance with the will of the Kami.'

Yasadara: 'Arigata is the Mikokoro of my Ōgimi; even if many Ikuyo pass, I shall never forget it. Ōgimi, protect the Mikuni together with Samarihime-sama in Tokotoha, in accordance with the will of the Kami. From now on, as a Kamutsukasa of the Ananai-kyō, I shall perform the Kashimada for the sake of the Yobito. I speak to the Himemikoto of Samari; dedicate your sincerity and serve the King.'

Samari: 'Please be Urayasu, Himemikoto; I shall serve you purely morning and evening. If you depart, we who remain behind will likely spend our days in loneliness. Nevertheless, do not be anxious about my person, who is held in the Onte of the Kami. While dedicating my Makotogokoro to the Ōgimi, I shall peacefully protect our Kunitami.'

Ōgon: 'The distress of my Murakimo heart has vanished, seeing the harmony between the two Hime.'

Kiyoteru: 'The Amatsu sun shines high in the Misora, and the heart of the Hime of Kiyoteru shines brightly. I speak to the Kamutsukasa of the Right Guard, Karlchin; please forgive my mischief. To re-examine all things is the path of the Kami, for it was never a wicked heart from the beginning.'

Karlchin: 'Arigata are the words of Kiyoteru-hime; they are the life of my lonely self. From today, I shall correct my base heart and devote my sincerity to the Kami and the King.'

Seris: 'While knowing that this will happen if I do this, it is a situation where I am drawn in and cannot pull back. Ōhōkami of Heaven and Earth, forgive me for the sin of deception committed with knowledge.'

Hokko: 'Everything is entirely Kannagara, Kannagara. May spiritual happiness be bestowed upon the disciples. To say good or to say evil is but a temporary separation of the world of men, as the Kami hears and corrects. I am the Kamutsukasa who opens the Amatsu Makoto teachings to the countries of the four directions. Nevertheless, in this world, there are clouds on the moon, wind on the flowers, and the morning sun on the snow. To leave everything to the breast of the Kami is the bridge that ascends to Takamagahara.'

Seran: 'King Seran sings with a cool voice. The song is as follows: "The Kami has appeared in the manifest world, and dwells in the castle of Iruna. The preciousness of driving away the Magatsu of the ugly and commanding the truth! I am the Kshatriya of Iruna, a Kamutsukasa of the Brahman teachings. Having reverently served the teachings of Onikumawake morning and evening, it was worth it. Supported by the Himemikoto of Ōgon, who is with my wife and children, and the noble Kamutsukasa of Kiyoteru, and again by the Kamutsukasa Hokko and other loyal people, my body is protected. I have become a happy person who reigns again as King of Iruna Castle and protects the world eternally. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the great earth sinks, the teachings of Ananai-kyō shall never be forgotten in the firm and eternal rocks. Though the names of the teachings vary, such as Ananai-kyō, Ural-kyō, and Brahman-kyō, the true Kami who created heaven and earth is one. It is that which returns to the one of Kuninotokotachi-no-Ōkami. The protection of the Mikoto of Banten, Taishakuten, Jizaiten, Banko-Ōkami, and Shio-naga-hiko becomes ever higher and deeper. I reverently thank and serve the vast and boundless divine virtue led by the Ananai teachings, the only one in the universe. Kurinsu, the Kamutsukasa of the Left Guard, and Karlchin, the Kamutsukasa of the Right Guard, purifying our hearts and cleansing our bodies, from now on we shall assist the sacred office of King Seran in Iruna Castle. We shall save the Kunitami from the sufferings of fire and water, guide and instruct them in the true essence of the Heavenly Pure Land, and construct the Heavenly Kingdom on earth in detail. My heart is to serve, harmonizing and being intimate with those above and below, in the heaven-sent vocation of being born as a human. May the hemp pillars be firm, Kannagara. Swearing to the Kami, I proclaim and transmit this before all the Kamutsukasa. Ah, Kannagara, Kannagara. May the spirits be happy."'.
