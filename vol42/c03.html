Chapter 3: Mountain Storm [1128]

山嵐〔1128〕

§ 1

The party of King Seran, having mounted their horses and finally reached the summit of Teruyamatauge, felt the late autumn wind blowing against their faces as they crossed the Onohe. Gazing down at the Genya below, they were struck by a sense of immeasurable emotion, with joys and sorrows intermingling in their hearts, and they heaved deep sighs. The horses, free of care, happily raised their heads and neighed, or grazed on the discolored golden Shibakusa; both man and horse were relaxing their hearts for a while, nurturing a spirit of Kōzen.

Just then, five knights, acting as a search party for Yasudarahime, rode up on horseback. The troop of knights, taken aback to find Yasudarahime resting there calmly with the King and four or five of his subordinates, widened their eyes and, feeling somewhat intimidated, began to retreat.

Korton: 'Ah, is that not Yasudarahime who dwells there? I am a knight named Korton, sent by Shiaru-dono of the Teruman country. It is a fortunate meeting. Come, we shall now accompany you, and let us return to the Teruman country.'

Yasudarahime: 'Ah, you are the knight requested by Shiaru-sama. Thank you for your trouble in coming from such a distance. However, no matter what is said, I shall not return to the mansion of Shiaru, so please return quickly and report that fact. I cannot bear to be thrown into an iron prison again on false charges. Ho ho ho ho.'

Korton: 'This is troublesome, Hime-sama. If you speak in such a manner, we shall lose face. Shiaru-shujin has repented, saying he will never commit such cruel acts again, so it is safe for you to return. Furthermore, as long as I am with you, I shall never allow such things to happen. Please be at ease and I pray you return to your country with us.'

Yasudarahime: 'Ho ho ho ho. Birds of a feather; if it were night, I might be deceived, but even if we are in the mountains, such deception will not work. My body trembles even at the mention of Shiaru's name. Do not speak such drivel ever again, not even a single word. Ho ho ho ho.'

Korton: 'Here now, Hime-sama, or rather, Okusama, I am asking you with all my heart, fulfilling my mission, so this is no laughing matter. I pray you, respect the way of women, and it would be proper for you to follow your husband's command and return.'

Yasudarahime: 'I have upheld the way of women to the very end. I do not recall ever having lacked in the way of women by even a speck until now. Nevertheless, because it is dangerous for my self-protection to return to the house of a husband who throws his innocent wife into an iron prison and abuses her, please convey the message that he should give up on our bond. Shiaru-sama is not one who upholds the way of a husband. He does not consult me, his wife and mistress of the house, on household affairs; he ignores his wife and keeps numerous lowly women by his side, heaping infinite insults upon me. Even if I were to die, I would never return to such a place. That it has come to this is something that has welled up from the rust in Shiaru-sama's heart, so there is no longer any hope for recovery. Spilled water does not return to the tray; no matter how skillfully you try to entice me with fine words, that will not work. Yasudarahime has some spirit, so I cannot endure infinite insults forever. In these days when the issue of women's rights has been raised, even if I am but a mere woman, how could I do such a foolish thing from the standpoint of protecting women's rights? I could not face the women of the world if I did. ...It would not do to be told that Yasudarahime has smeared mud on the faces of many women. I can no longer think as a single woman. It is my intention to represent the women of the world and protect women's rights to the utmost. No matter what you say, I shall never return to my country, not even if I fall into the depths of hell. Please tell Shiaru-sama not to look upon such a defiant woman, and to live happily and merrily with the woman of his choice, as Yasudarahime has said. Right, Korton-sama, is that not so?'

騎士の一『ヤア、そこに居らるるはヤスダラ姫にましまさずや。吾こそはテルマン国のシヤール殿より遣はされたるコルトンと云ふ騎士で厶る。いい所でお目にかかりました。さアこれから吾々がお供を致し、テルマン国へ帰りませう』

と馬上より声を震はせて云ふ。

『ヤア其方はシヤールさまから頼まれて来た騎士だな。遠方のところ、御苦労で厶りました。併しながら、妾は何と云はれてもシヤールの館へは帰りませぬから、早く帰つて其由を復命して下さい。又無実の難題で鉄牢へ放り込まれては、堪りませぬからな。ホヽヽヽヽ』

とヤスダラ姫は神力無双の神司を伴うて居るため、心強くなり、平気の平左で、顔色も変へず笑ひながら答へてゐる。その大胆さに騎士は益々気を呑まれ、目を丸くし、

コルトン『これはしたり姫様、左様な事を仰せられては、吾々の顔が立ちませぬ。決して今後は左様な残酷な事はせないと、シヤールの主人も悔悟して居ましたから、お帰り下さつても大丈夫です。又私がついて居ります以上は、決して左様な事はさせませぬ。御安心の上何卒吾々と共に御帰国を願ひます』

『ホヽヽヽヽ同穴の貉、夜分なれば騙されるかも知れませぬが、なんぼ山の中だと云つて、その騙しは利きますまい。シヤールの名を聞いても身慄ひが致します。もう左様な繰言はこれきり一言も仰シヤールなや。ホヽヽヽヽ』

『これ、お姫さま、いや奥様、一生懸命に使命を以てお願ひ申して居るのに、滑稽所ぢや厶りますまい。何卒貴女様も婦道を重んじ、夫の命に従つてお帰り遊ばすが正当で厶りませう』

『妾は飽迄も婦道を守つて来ました。今迄微塵も婦道に欠けた事を致した覚えは厶りませぬ。それにも拘はらず、罪なき妾を鉄窓のもとに投げ込み、虐待をなさるやうな夫の家へは、護身の関係上剣呑で帰る事は出来ませぬから、之までの縁と諦めて下さいと伝言して下さい。シヤールさまは夫道を守る方ぢやありませぬ。一家の主婦たる妾に対し、家政上について一回の御相談を遊ばすぢやないし、妻を無視して数多の卑しき女を侍らせ、無限の侮辱を加へたお方、仮令死んでも左様な処へは滅多に帰りませぬ。かうなつたのもシヤールさまの心の錆から湧き出たのですから、最早回復の見込みはありませぬ。覆水盆にかへらず、何程巧妙な辞令を以て籠絡しようとなさつても、そりや駄目ですよ。ヤスダラ姫だつて少しは精神もありますから、何時迄も無限の侮辱に甘んずる事は出来ませぬ。女権拡張問題の持上つた今日此頃、たとへ女の端くれでも女の権利を保護する点から見ても、どうして左様な馬鹿げた事が出来ませうか。天下の婦人に対しても妾の責任がすみませぬ。………ヤスダラ姫は多数婦人の面上に泥を塗つたと云はれては済みませぬ。最早一個の婦人として考ふる事は出来ませぬ。天下の婦人を代表して、女の権利を極力保護する妾の考へで厶ります。何時迄何と仰有つても金輪奈落帰国は致しませぬ。何卒シヤールさまにもこんな不貞腐れ女を目にかけずに、貴方のお気に入つた御婦人と面白う可笑しくお暮し遊ばせと、ヤスダラ姫が云つたと仰有つて下さいませ。なあコルトンさま、さうでせう』

§ 2

Colton: 'If you put it that way, perhaps it is so, but is that not too Reitan? If I do not receive a Gohenji filled with some Onjiyō, how can I make my Fukumei to the Dannasama?'

Yasudarahime: 'Ho ho ho ho! Onjiyō makes me laugh. The Shihonka of today use the term Onjiyōshiyugi toward the Raudōshiya, using Heugo that are convenient for themselves, but Yasudarahime hates such Yūgenfujitsukau methods. Shāru-sama is also a great Daifugau and Daishihonka of the Teruman nation, so he used to spout Onjiyōshiyugi like a Kuchiguse. Ho ho ho ho!'

Colton, while scratching his head, said: 'Okusama, you are not like your usual self lately; have you not had a Shinkiitsuten?'

Yasudarahime: 'Eh, how Shinki! Do you think I would have a Shinkiitsuten? Like the autumn sky that clouds over in an Itsuten and clears up in an instant, I have already been abandoned by Shāru-sama. I can no longer endure continuing a life of Kiyogi, being threatened and subjected to Giyakutai like that. More than that, I must hurry to the Miyakoi of Iruna, so please leave me alone and return.'

Colton: 'If you will not listen even after I have said this much, I have no choice given my duties. Forgive my rudeness, but I will tie you up and take you back, so be prepared for that.'

Yasudarahime: 'Ho ho ho ho! Do as you please. If you dare to touch me with even a single finger, just try it.'

Colton signaled to his subordinates, intending to capture Yasudarahime. As the four men approached the Hime, tightening their ropes and attempting to seize her, Rēbu suddenly rose to his feet and shouted in a loud voice: 'You insolent, violent men!' As he said this, he grabbed the collar of one of the men who was trying to seize him and threw him headlong onto the mountain path.

Colton: 'What, you impudent fool!' As Colton spat on his hands and moved to seize him, Rēbu kicked him squarely in the shin. Colton let out a cry of 'Ah!' and collapsed on the spot, grinding his teeth in frustration while rubbing his shin with a grimace.

Rēbu: 'Aha ha ha! Colton-sama, with a kick to the shin, has tumbled down, has he not? / The five men coming from the land of Teruman / Are foaming at the mouth on Teruyama Pass. / Ihi hi! You who do not value your life / Try opposing Yasudarahime. / Ufu fu! If there is anyone who carelessly lays a hand on her / I shall separate your head from your torso. / Ehe he! Look at the attitude of the one who, after spouting nonsense / About this and that with such airs. / Oho ho! Coming here with such a terrifying, grand manner / Only to end up in such a pathetic state of weeping.'

Colton: 'Aita ta! Against the strong will / Of the Okusama who has lost all patience, no shield can be held. / I thought to deceive her and take her back / But now I have no way to use my hands or feet. / Wandering about, following the Hime's path / I have met with such a painful predicament. / Chosen to be the leader of the search party / Today's disaster is a matter of life and death. / I fear no demons, great serpents, tigers, or wolves / But I am astonished by the Hime's stubbornness.'

Sēran: 'To leave all things to the divine will of God / Is the true path that man should follow. / Whether in the west, east, south, or north, the heavens and earth / Are a world thick with the protection of God. / The person who follows the dark path of the soul / Stumbles, falls, climbs, and descends. / Without listening to the words of sincere exhortation / How pitiful the flower that scatters without mercy. / The fields and mountains have already cast off their feathered robes / And Colton's heart trembles and shakes.'

Colton: 'Frustrated, knocked down upon the pass / There is no way to relieve the heartburn in my chest. / Despite searching day and night to no avail / The sadness of meeting such a miserable fate. / In the wintry wind of Teruyama Pass / I am blown about and foaming at the mouth. / Astride a farting, reed-colored horse / Here again, I am speechless and bow my head in defeat. / Thinking to be praised and perform a feat / My bones are crushed and I am filled with pain.'

『さう聞けばさうでもありませうが、そりやあんまり冷淡ぢやありませぬか。少しは温情の籠つた御返事を承はらなくては、如何して旦那様に復命が出来ませうか』

『ホヽヽヽヽ温情が聞いて呆れますわ。今の資本家は労働者に対して温情主義だとか云つて、うまく自分に都合のよい標語を用ひますが、そんな有言不実行のやり方はヤスダラ姫は大嫌ひで厶ります。シヤールさまもテルマン国の大富豪、大資本家だから、口癖の様に温情主義をまくし立てて居られましたな。ホヽヽヽヽ』

コルトンは頭を掻きながら、

『奥様、貴方は俄に此頃の空ぢやないが、心機一転したのぢや厶りませぬか』

『エー、辛気臭い、心機一転もしませうかいな。一天俄にかき曇ると思へば忽ち晴れる秋の空、妾は已に既にシヤールさまからあきられてゐました。妾もあの様な脅迫されたり、虐待されて虚偽の生活をつづける事は最早忍びませぬ。それよりも早くイルナの都入りをせなくてはなりませぬから、何卒妾に構はずお帰り下さいませ』

『これだけ申し上げてもお聞き下さらねば、私の職務上止むを得ませぬ。失礼ながらフン縛つてでも連れて帰りますから、其覚悟をなさいませ』

『ホヽヽヽヽ御勝手に成されませえな。妾に指一本でも触へるなら触へて御覧』

コルトンは部下に目配せし、ヤスダラ姫を捕縛せしめむとした。四人は姫に向つて捕縄をしごきながら武者振りつかむとするを、レーブは此時突然身を起し、大音声を張り上げて、

『無礼者、狼藉者』

と云ひながら、武者振りつかむとする一人の襟首をとつてスツテンドウと谷道へ投げつけた。

『何、猪口才な』

とコルトンは手に唾し、武者振りつくを、レーブは向脛をポンと蹴つた。コルトンはアツと一声其場に倒れ、無念の歯ぎしりをしながら、向脛を顔を顰めてさすつて居る。

レーブ『アハヽヽヽコルトンさまがコルトンと 脛をけられて転げけるかな。 テルマンの国より来る五人づれ 照山峠で泡を吹くなり。 イヒヽヽヽ命の惜くない奴は ヤスダラ姫に手向うてみよ。 ウフヽヽヽうつかりと手出しを致す者あらば 首と胴とを分けてやるぞよ。 エヘヽヽヽえら相に何ぢやかんぢやと世迷言 吐いたあとの其態を見よ。 オホヽヽヽ恐ろしい大権幕でやつて来て 吠面かわく浅ましの態』

コルトン『アイタヽヽ呆れはてたる奥様の 強い腰には楯もつかれず。 詐つて連れ帰らうと思ひしに 今は手足も使ふ術なし。 ウロウロと姫の御後を慕ひつつ 苦しき破目に遇ひにけるかな。 選まれて捜索隊の長となり 九死一生の今日の災難。 鬼大蛇虎狼は恐れねど 姫の剛情に吾は驚く』

セーラン『何事も神の御旨に任すこそ 人のゆくべき真道なるらむ。 西東南も北も天地の 神の守りのしげき世なるよ。 奴羽玉の暗路を辿る人の身は 転けつ輾びつ上りつ下りつ。 懇に諭す言の葉聞かずして 情なく散りし仇花あはれ。 野も山もはや羽衣を脱ぎすてて 慄ひ戦くコルトンの胸』

コルトン『腹立たし峠の上に倒されて さがる由なし胸の溜飲。 昼夜に探ねまはりし甲斐もなく こんな憂目に遇うた悲しさ。 冬近き照山峠の木枯に 吹かれながらに泡を吹くなり。 屁放りの葦毛の馬に跨つて ここで又もや閉口頓首す。 ほめられて手柄をしようと思ひしに 骨挫かれて痛み入るなり』

Riūn: 'Used by the vile Osaki of the Magakami, / You foolish Colton, ignorant of your own state. / Seeking to capture Yasudahime in exchange for your own life, / You are kicked by the horse of lies in Tsukushi. / The way of Kami, unchanging from ancient times to now, / How foolish to attack the Mabito who walks it. / How strange, upon the summit of Teruyamatauge, / To witness a shibai unheard of even in the Kamiyo. / With various schemes held within your breast, / Pitiable is the lot of the minister of the vile.'

Karu: 'The body that advances upon the Ōji of the Kamunagara Kami, / Its heart-steed also stands valiant. / Chasing after the Hime who has abandoned / Her displeasing husband and returns home, you foolish one. / Enduring the pain while rubbing your shins, / You still do not learn and stir up trouble. / Used by the vile ones of Shiaru, / Pitiable is the man who leads out his steed. / Here and now, set your heart-steed aright, / And even the heavy Omoni of your sins shall be saved by Karu.'

Tēmusu: 'Climbing the Sakamichi, I met Colton / Coming in search of the Hime. / With the reins gathered loosely, upon the back of the steed, / The Torite of the performance come riding. / Thinking "now is the time," they took up the Torinaha, / But before they could press before the Hime, their legs were broken. / Saying that one cannot trade one's back for one's belly, Colton / Had the strength drained from his waist in the Kohadanpan. / Seeing before my eyes the warning of the Kami, / Susanō, who descended to the village of Soshimori.'

Colton, his leg pain having somewhat recovered, quickly mounted his horse, and while signaling to the four knights, he pulled the reins of his horse tightly and fled for his life, heading straight for Teruyamatauge. Watching him go, Yasudahime sang again.

'Pitiable is the man who has come from afar, / Seeking my trail, only to return in dejection. / I am no oni, so I never thought / To cause suffering to the people of the world. / The pain of having to cause suffering / To the Torite who unexpectedly pursued me. / Yet in this situation, from which there is no escape, / Look down upon me, O Kami of Heaven and Earth. / As I watch the back of him fleeing, / My heart grows sad and troubled.'

Sēran: 'The Kami of Heaven and Earth will surely / Look down and forgive this as an unavoidable event.'

Riūn: 'Pitiable is the man who has become a Torite / In a world where there is so much that must be done.'

Tēmusu: 'Even he was not born a vile one; / Soon he shall awaken to the path of Makoto.'

Rēbu: 'Standing on the pass of Teruyama, / It is wretched to see the figure of the man fleeing.'

Karu: 'With the protection of the Kami of Heaven and Earth being thick, / You have escaped the tiger's mouth, my lord. / Come then, let us mount our steeds and, slowly, / Set our course for the capital and advance.'

Sēran: 'Since the knights sent by Shiaru have already come this far in search of the Hime's whereabouts, we must never be off our guard. Once we descend this pass, it will be an increasingly dangerous area. Furthermore, riding on a slope is actually extremely perilous; let us take the horses by the bit and descend slowly, shall we? I have begun to feel an inexplicable sense of unease. Come, everyone, let us go.'

So saying, he took the lead and descended the steep slope.

The party followed behind the king, leading their horses, and descended while calling out, 'Hai, hai, hai.'

と先に立ち、急坂を下り行く。

一行は王の後に従ひ、駒を曳き連れ、ハイハイハイと声をかけながら下り行く。
