Chapter 16: 失恋会議 [1141]

失恋会議〔1141〕

§ 1

In a room deep within the mansion of Umorinokami, Samarihime and Samoahime were secretly deliberating on a strategy, their hearts pained by the restless and unsettled behavior of Umorinokami of late.

Samoahime-dono, do you not find anything strange about Father's behavior recently? I am so worried that I do not know what to do. Ever since Mother departed for the Haruna country, he has been constantly out of the house, leaving all domestic affairs to you. Furthermore, what I cannot comprehend is how he has suddenly taken to trimming his hair, fussing over his beard, applying white powder to his face like a man who has lost his senses, grooming his eyebrows, and changing his clothes every single day—is he not doing things that only a young man would do? I cannot help but worry that he has been sweet-talked by Yasudarahime-sama and is being driven to such madness.

In my estimation, it seems that Danna-sama has become infatuated with Yasudarahime-sama and has lost his senses. Considering the talk of Mammoth, it appears that a terrible demon is targeting Danna-sama. This is something that must be addressed by a remonstrance from you, Hime-sama; it is impossible for someone like me to advise him effectively.

This has become a troublesome matter. Father has no intention of listening to what we have to say, so I wonder if it would be effective to have Yufutesu, who holds Danna-sama's deep trust, speak to him.

That would be completely useless. It is Yufutesu who has caused Danna-sama's spirit to become confused.

That Yufutesu? Did he really encourage Father to do such things? What a wicked man he is. I shall summon Yufutesu now and interrogate him strictly. Hey, Mammoth, are you there? I have a small errand for you. Come here quickly.

It was Mammoth who entered, pushing open the sliding door at the sound of this call. He had been immorally eavesdropping on their secret conversation from the next room, holding his breath.

Hime-sama, was it me you called? Please, command me with whatever you require.

Samarihime: 'Ah, Mammoth, you are remarkably quick. I suppose you have heard the entirety of our conversation from before.'

Indeed, as you have guessed, I have heard every detail of the circumstances. This has truly become a troublesome matter. This is entirely the work of Yufutesu, and it never originated from Danna-sama's own heart. That is why I have always told Danna-sama, Okusama, and you, Hime-sama, that Yufutesu is truly the explosive of the Umori household, and that you should banish him as soon as possible and employ this Mammoth in his place. I am not a man of such petty heart as to be driven by self-interest to bring others down for my own advancement. It is only because I think of the importance of this house that I have risked my life to offer you my counsel.

In any case, I am sorry to trouble you, but please find Father's whereabouts as soon as possible and bring him home. We cannot afford to be sluggish.

I understand. However, today is the day of the great event of Mammoth's life, where I am to be married to Samoahime, as you previously permitted, so please entrust this errand to Haruman. Since this is the day of my life's celebration, it is not as if Danna-sama's situation will change in a day or two.

Hey, Mammoth, I do not recall making such a promise to you. I only said that if you served with true loyalty, I might make a beauty like Samoahime your wife, depending on the circumstances. I am sorry to say this, but after you snuck into the Samori mansion with ninjutsu the other day, failed, were captured, and—in a manner unbecoming of a man—revealed all the secrets entrusted to you by your master to the enemy, and then returned home fearing for your life like a coward, how could Samoahime not lose all interest in you? You had better give up such ambitions. In the first place, your social standing is entirely different.

§ 2

Samaryhime-sama: 'Yes, that is fine. Even a small insect has a soul of five bu, and it is not only the moonlit night that exists. Since there are also dark nights, please be very careful. Samaryhime-sama, Samoa-dono, farewell.'

Leaving behind such harsh words, she walked out pointing toward the front, her footsteps sounding rough.

Watching her go, the two remained stunned for a while.

Samoa-dono: 'Ho ho ho, how unsightly it is to be dazed by a man's love. Samoa-dono, if you were to look at that style, you would surely be satisfied, would you not?'

Samoa-dono: 'Ho ho ho, I am sick of it from the bottom of my heart. Ehe he he.'

Changing the subject, Mammoth, having been showered with painful Kototama of iron elbows by the two women, and unable to bear his regret, ran toward the mansion of Eufetes—with whom he had been on terms as bad as dog and monkey—to seek support from a fellow victim of unrequited love, running with a rhythmic thumping sound.

Upon the arrival of Mammoth, Eufetes, thinking that it could not be a trivial matter for Mammoth, who had never once crossed the threshold of his house, to come flying over—though he would usually sprinkle salt and stand up a broom—felt somewhat lonely as a result of his own heartbreak, and feeling as if he had gained a strong ally today against his usual enemy, he went out to the gate to welcome him.

Eufetes: 'Hey, Mammoth-dono, what is the reason for such a flustered manner? It is surely not a derailment or overturning of the Samoa Railway, is it?'

Mammoth: 'It has gone beyond derailment or overturning; it has been completely destroyed. What has become of Serishime-sama?'

Eufetes: 'Whether it has become this or that, I have no idea at all. That woman was a monster of an old hag.'

Mammoth: 'Huh, that Serishime-sama... what kind of monster do you mean? Did she, like Samohime, deceive you until now, and then in the final five minutes, an ambush appeared and she started shelling you with clip-cannons?'

Eufetes: 'Well, if it were that, it would still be clever, but the one I thought was Serishime-sama was a terrible old fox. It seems that the fox must have gobbled up the real Serishime-sama before I knew it and disguised itself well; no, I am truly fed up with women. Even now, when I think of it, I shudder.'

Mammoth: 'What, how could such a thing be? Even this morning, Serishime-sama was truly worried and said, "I have not seen Eufetes-sama's face lately." However, she is also a follower of Samoa. Since it is different from what you thought—that you were loved—you should just give up.'

Eufetes: 'It is indeed a matter of extreme regret. However, I wonder what happened to Reko of the God of Right Protection; since she had a sore tongue for some time, she had been staying inside without going out, so I do not know her situation at all, but surely the curtain must have fallen on the play, must it not?'

Mammoth: 'Well, regarding that matter, Samaryhime-sama and Samoa are very worried. When I think of that, I feel very sorry for them and cannot bear it... that is not true. No, I actually feel a sense of satisfaction.'

As they were talking, from the gate:

Carlchin: 'Eufetes, Eufetes.'

Calling out as he entered was Carlchin, the official of Right Protection. Seeing his figure, Eufetes pursed his lips.

Eufetes: 'Hey, Master, with your face changed, what is the matter?'

Carlchin: 'Hey, it is terrible, it is terrible. Since there are two kings and two Yasudahime-samas, it is too frightening to stay there. Because of the matter of Sute... the castle interior, where I thought my life would be saved, is full of nothing but monsters. They spit out sweet and gentle things to make us feel at ease, with a plan to take us away quietly, so I, being a strong man, made a face as if I had repented, recited a poem to deceive the situation, and saying I was going to the toilet, I escaped quietly through the toilet hole and returned. In any case, one is the real one and one is a monster. Now that it has been exposed, Eufetes, you will not be saved either, and I must do something, so I came here to consult with you. Hey, Mammoth, you were here too? Since three people together have the wisdom of Monju, let us have a consultation here, shall we?'

They proceed into the inner detached room. Here, the three were putting their heads together in a conspiracy to murder Ōgonhime and the other key leaders, setting the time for twelve o'clock tonight. Chiru, a maidservant serving in the house of Yūfutesu, overheard every bit of their plot, and with an indifferent face, she slipped out the back door and reported the entire sequence of events to Samarihime, the daughter of Migimori. How will Samarihime act from here on?
