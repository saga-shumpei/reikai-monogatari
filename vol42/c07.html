Chapter 7: 女武者 [1132]

女武者〔1132〕

§ 1

Autumn deepens at last, and the morning sun shines clear. Pushing open the eastern window, today my heart is also light. Together with Tenahime, how auspicious, how auspicious, how truly auspicious it is.

With raw fish such as sea bream and bonito, the couple sits facing each other here. Showing their intimate bond, which allows not even water to leak through, on their faces, the Umoritsukasa pours sake for her. With a tipsy, good-humored, loud laugh, they stack their cups, and with a strange, swaying motion around their waists, the light in their eyes, lost in love, glitters here and there. Asahiko, who shines into the cup, is a dazzling Yasudara woman; having become a wife, she is like the starry sky, with the Ama-no-Kahara in between.

Once a year, the Tanabata meeting is a clear river of love. When he offers a cup to his wife with an indifferent face, Tenahime receives it looking very happy. Not knowing that this is a parting cup, the Kamigokoro, the Umoritsukasa, with a Hoteigaho, folds a secret in his heart. Gradually, the Umori raises his face and says, 'Tenahime, my wife, the time for our long-cherished desire to be fulfilled is finally drawing near. I am a Kshatriya of Iruna, and you are a noble consort. By harmonizing the breath of Yin and Yang, it is a joy to protect the land of Iruna forever in the reign of the pine. It is worth waiting for, this joy of husband and wife, a bond for a thousand generations.'

'Dance and sing with courage and joy. Bonten, Taishiyaku, Jizaiten. The grace of Ōkunihiko-no-Ōkami is not merely of the heavenly skies; it is as vast as the great ocean, and He lets the dew of grace fall upon the Aohitogusa, both far and near. The Aohito are the children of God. Having been entrusted with the precious palace of the noble God, the child, and the palace, I reign over the land of Iruna, and I am happy to protect the world in eternal firmness. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may your spirits be blessed. Whether the morning sun shines or clouds over, whether the moon waxes or wanes, even if the earth should sink, you and I shall be a harmonious couple forever.'

'Enjoying the spring when the camellias and Udumbara flowers of eight thousand generations bloom, we shall serve the Great Way of God. Before the presence of God, I swear and pray with all my heart to Bonten, Taishiyaku, Jizaiten.'

With a tipsy air, the Umoritsukasa is singing while drinking sake with his wife, Tenahime. Tenahime, with her spirits bright and her face full of smiles, begins to sing in an interesting rhythm while offering a cup to her husband.

'Great Bonten-ō Jizaiten, the grace of Ōkunihiko-no-Ōkami is manifest before my eyes. My husband is a Kshatriya of the land of Iruna, and the time has come for you to be revered as the parent of the four classes and to flourish. I, too, as your wife, am like the mugwort that grows alongside the hemp. When you ascend to a high position, I shall also attain a high rank, and side by side, like the stars in the heavenly sky, we shall rule over the gathered Aohito peacefully and calmly, following the will of God, and as living gods of the land of Iruna, we shall make our names resound for ten thousand generations. It is a joy to be a noble pillar of God as a married couple.'

'Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may your spirits be blessed. I pray that you will establish this purpose in detail, even if only for a moment, as soon as possible. When I think of it, in the previous world, we, as a couple, must have devoted our bodies and sincerity before the God of Heaven and Earth. The god of fortune who suddenly enters is Daikokunushi, to say nothing of the Ebisu-face of the Umoritsukasa, the Hotei-belly that does not worry about small things, the Fukurokuju-head with a long life, and through the blessings of Benzaiten, we pile up precious treasures and demonstrate the martial prowess of Bishamonten endlessly in the countries of the four directions, building the auspicious state of the Heavenly Kingdom upon the earth before our very eyes. Ah, how reliable, how reliable. God protects us, and we revere God. May you grant us the great virtues of name, rank, longevity, and wealth for a thousand generations and ten thousand generations forever, Jizaiten. I pray with awe before the presence of Mototsu-mioya-no-Ōkami. Ah, Kannagara-kamu-nagara, may your spirits be blessed.'

Tenahime, it seems the opportunity for our long-held ambition has finally arrived. The sake tastes especially good today, doesn't it? You should drink plenty today as a pre-celebration. En.

§ 2

Wife: 'Sayō de gozaimasu na, this is all due to the Goriyaku of Daijizaiten-sama whom we serve day by day. Nanitozo Dannasama, please ensure that even if you achieve Goshiyutsuse, you will not abandon me. It is the way of women to be anxious the more you achieve Shiyutsuse. I beg you again and again not to Misu me.'

Husband: 'Ahahaha, you are a woman who always does Toriko-Kurau. Tatohe I were to possess the entire Sanzensekai, if I were to live apart from you, there would be no joy in it, would there? We have been together for over Nijifunen, and yet you still do not understand my heart. En.'

Wife: 'If that is your heart, Dannasama, I am happy. In return, no matter what you say, I shall never disobey you. For if I were to disobey such a kind Dannasama, I fear the Gomeibatsu of Daijizaiten-sama.'

Husband: 'Iya, you have spoken well. That is why you are my Niyōbau. However, I have one Saudan for you, and I am sure you will listen, will you not?'

Wife: 'Ohohoho, why do you ask if I will listen? In what Sekai would a husband ask a favor of his Niyōbau? What is this Nanigoto you desire? Nanitozo, tell me quickly; I wish to hear your request as soon as Itsukoku passes.'

Husband: 'Ahahaha, the truth is, I want you to depart for the capital of Haruna. You surely would not disobey your husband's Kotoba, would you? En.'

Wife: 'Hate, I do not Kokoroe those words; is it not the case that Ōkuronushi-sama is supposed to be sending five hundred soldiers? And yet, you speak of leading an army from here instead; what is your thinking behind this?'

Husband: 'King Seran has already made his decision, so there is no need for an army at all. Ōkuronushi-sama has dispatched many soldiers due to disturbances in neighboring lands, and the defense of Haruna Castle has become quite weak. On one side, Oniharawake has been sent to conquer the San-u-kyō, and on the other, Ōashiwake has departed to conquer the Ural-kyō. One might say it is a time of many national affairs. Is it not truly inexcusable to beg for reinforcements when there is no war? Furthermore, since Karlchin lacks divine power, if it were said that we borrowed an army from Ōkuronushi-sama to achieve our ambitions, it would be a shame for generations to come. Rather than that, if we were to send an army from here to assist Ōkuronushi-sama, our military prowess would resound throughout the world, Ōkuronushi-sama would look upon us favorably, and the state we protect would be like a jewel of wish-fulfillment, indestructible as diamond. En, Tena, do you understand?'

Wife: 'It is not that I would disobey your Kotoba, Dannasama, but I cannot be so optimistic. In this world where good is followed by evil, one never knows when or what might suddenly occur. I would like you to think this over carefully.'

Husband: 'Can a woman understand the heart of a hero? Oi, Tena, do you intend to resist your husband's command? Yoshi, yoshi, if that is the case, I have my own intentions. From today on, you are no longer my wife, and I am no longer your husband. Saa, leave this mansion as quickly as you can. En.'

He shouted, sharpening his voice and intensifying his tone.

Wife: 'That is too cruel a word; is it not too cold and distant to say such a thing?'

Husband: 'I am by no means being cold and distant; you are the one who is extremely indifferent by disobeying your husband's Kotoba. I do not wish to do such an unreasonable thing as casting away a Niyōbau with whom I have lived for so long. It is because you are so stubborn that I have become angry. En.'

Wife: 'You truly do not love me, do you? Your behavior lately seems a bit suspicious, does it not?'

Kāruchin: 'I hate it, I hate it, for ten years I have lived, and there is even a rumor in the world that we have lived together through thick and thin; it is not as if one only has a wife one likes. However, I have never once felt even a hint of dislike toward you. In truth, I am grateful, wondering where there could be such a good wife as you. Because you are so dear to me, I just happened to say such things. Now, Tenahime, please forgive me, as I am doing this for you.'

He puts his hands together and bows to show her. Tenahime suddenly improves her mood, and while shaking her shoulders happily,

Tenahime: 'Ho ho ho, what a manly face you have; the memories of our new marriage bed come back to me as if it were only yesterday, and tears of joy fall. If that is the case, my Tsumasama, I shall follow your command and go to the capital of Haruna.'

Kāruchin is secretly overjoyed that he has succeeded, and in a deliberately solemn tone,

Kāruchin: 'Ya, splendid, splendid. Now, from here you shall head toward the land of Haruna, send the five hundred cavalry reinforcement army back to their origin, and set out to provide reinforcements for Lord Ōkuronushi. I shall remain in the capital, counting the days on my fingers, waiting for the day of your triumphant return. Now, as quickly as possible.'

He urges her on. Tenahime gathers all of her subordinate soldiers, and with over three hundred cavalry in tow, she leaves the capital of Iruna behind and sets out on an expedition toward the capital of Haruna.

Afterward, Kāruchin, feeling as if a boil on his thigh had festered and burst, laughs to himself in secret,

Kāruchin: 'Ehe he he, as expected of Migimorisama. Even a wife as suspicious as mine has finally been convinced by our divine schemes and strange tactics and has gone off. Ehe he he, with my three-inch tongue I have swallowed up five hundred reinforcements, moved my wife, and sent another three hundred soldiers to Lord Ōkuronushi; even the Lord Daijizaiten would surely be amazed at my power. Once I have made Tenahime stand at the head of the battle line, her death in battle is a certainty. Ufu he he, oh my, it has become truly and thoroughly interesting. Now, shall I go and see the face of Yasudahime, who is like Benzaiten?'

He lets out a loud voice without thinking. Samoahime, who was in the next room, has heard all of Kāruchin's monologue, and she is left wide-eyed, stunned, and dumbfounded. However, putting on an innocent face, she approaches Kāruchin's side with a graceful gait,

Samoahime: 'Danna-sama, you must be very lonely now that Okusama has departed for the front. I am a woman myself, but I cannot possibly serve as a substitute for Okusama, so I offer my sympathy for your feelings. However, Danna-sama, you are truly a great person. Since Yasudahime-sama must be waiting for you, why do you not go and let her see your face?'

Kāruchin: 'Now, now, Samoa, do not say such reckless things. I have no business with the likes of Yasudahime.'

Samoahime: 'Ho ho ho ho, that is certainly true, there is no reason for you to have any business with her, is there?'

Kāruchin: 'Now, Samoahime, today is my day to report for duty, so I am going to the castle; I leave the house in your care. And please supervise the actions of the servants.'

Samoahime: 'Yes, I understand. Please be careful not to trip and fall again. Ho ho ho ho.'

Kāruchin: 'Eh, do not say unnecessary things.'

He scolds her slowly, and without even taking an attendant, he proceeds alone from the back gate toward the inside of the castle.
