Chapter 13: 揶揄 [1138]

揶揄〔1138〕

§ 1

In a room deep within Iruna Castle, Ōgonhime, Kiyoteruhime, and Serisu-hime sit in a triangle, laughing and enjoying themselves over something.

Ōgonhime: 'Serisu-hime-sama, you are quite a daring tomboy, are you not? Did you not finally make Euphtes change his mind and send him packing? If you do such things now, will it not be a problem if your essential purpose ends up as a mere pipe dream?'

Serisu-hime: 'Yes, please do not worry so much. Euphtes will surely come again. They say even a great elephant is led by a single strand of a woman's hair. My subtle Kototama is deeply, deeply implanted in his mind, so no matter what, he will not be able to forget me. Even if I just use a side-glance, a grown man loses his liver and bones until he becomes soft and limp like a boiled octopus, ohohohoho.'

Ōgonhime: 'You are a wasteful one. If you were born in the twentieth century, three hundred thousand years in the future, you would surely become a fine geisha. You are such a fearsome woman who can toy with a rough man like a ball, that even I, Ōgonhime, have had my courage taken away. Ohohoho.'

Serisu-hime: 'Hohohoho, what things you say, Ōgonhime-sama. There is a teacher here who is even more so than I.'

She points to Kiyoteruhime.

Ōgonhime: 'Kiyoteruhime is still in the testing phase. You are already an honor graduate and are ready for a silver watch. However, does Kiyoteruhime look like such a tomboy to your eyes? There is nothing as foolish as a parent judging a child, so I do not fully understand my daughter. If you recognize her as such from your perspective, perhaps it is as you say. What a troublesome daughter I have. But you, you disguised yourself as a fox and frightened people, did you not? It has become quite a reputation throughout the castle. Because you exert your skills too much and display your mischievous nature, I am a little worried that it might hinder Kiyoteruhime's future activities.'

Serisu-hime: 'Why, for that, I will deploy an artillery unit to cover the close-quarters combat, so it will be fine. Right, Kiyoteruhime-sama? You have it all planned in your mind, do you not?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Ohohoho, it has become even more interesting. I wish Karuchin-sama would come quickly. What is that lover of mine dilly-dallying for? I am worried that, like the sky these days, his mood might suddenly change, and he might meet someone nicer than me on the way, and that the curtain of his desire might be drawn down. Truly, having a lover is a worrying thing. What shall I do if Karuchin-sama has a change of heart? Mother, please try to guess your daughter's heart a little. Fuhohohoho.'

Ōgonhime looks puzzled, blows her nose with her fingers, wipes the foam from her lips, rounds her eyes for a moment, purses her wrinkled mouth, and peers intently into Kiyoteruhime's face.

Ōgonhime: 'Now, now, Kiyo-sama, has your demeanor not changed a little? If you truly fall in love, will we not be unable to play this act?'

Kiyoteruhime: 'Hohohoho, the one you cannot let your guard down with is your daughter. Even I, at first, thought that Karuchin fellow was unlikable, and I planned to deceive him with my three-inch tongue, and when the time came, throw him with a back-drop, give him a good thrashing, and make him go "afun," thinking it would be funny to see his face—a heartless thing to think. But when I thought about it secretly in the dead of night, I am a woman, too, so there is no reason why I should dislike the opposite sex. No matter how much of a fool Karuchin-sama may be, compared to Tomohiko, he might be many times more handsome. Mother, does it not seem so from your eyes? Now that it has come to this, it is no longer a play; I have come to think of Karuchin-sama seriously. I intend to pull this through as Yasudahime to the very end, so please, with parental mercy, let my love be fulfilled. I beg of you.'

Speaker: 'This is outrageous. Women are creatures against whom one must always be on guard. Ah, what should I do? Seris-hime-sama, you are truly magnificent; it is admirable that you do not indulge in men. This Kiyoteruhime has had a bad habit since she was small, and it is troublesome because as soon as she is placed near a man, she becomes all mushy like this. Please, I beg you to speak to Kiyosama and make her understand thoroughly. Now that things have come to this, I cannot help but feel anxious hearing such things.'

Speaker: 'Ho ho ho ho ho, Kiyoteruhime-sama is still as modern as ever. That must be the truth. An antique like me is already behind the times. Oh, I am truly impressed.'

Speaker: 'Mother-sama, try thinking about it carefully. Is it not said: a cat to a mouse, a woman to a man? If you place dried bonito before a cat, it will eventually bite into it. Once it has learned the savory taste, no matter what anyone says, it will never let go. Ho ho ho ho, no matter what anyone says, is there anyone else as sharp-witted as Karuchin-sama? I wish he would come quickly. Even if Mother-sama says otherwise, it is only right that I decide my own husband. It is not as if he is Mother-sama's husband; how could I ever do something as lacking in judgment as letting someone else choose my husband for me? Tell me, Seris-hime-sama, is that not so?'

Speaker: 'Oho ho ho ho, once again, Kiyosama, you are teasing me, are you not? Where is there a daughter who says such things in front of her parent? You have become such a worldly, utterly incorrigible tomboy.'

Speaker: 'How is my skill? Even Mother-sama would admit it is the real thing, would you not? Unless one can pull off something like this, one can never catch a carp with mere barley rice. Who would ever fall in love with a villain like that detestable Karuchin? Oho ho ho.'

Speaker: 'With that, this old woman is also relieved for a moment. Please, do it well.'

Speaker: 'However, Mother-sama, I will warn you in advance: human hearts are living things, so who knows when a change of heart might turn a lie into truth? When that time comes, I hope you will resign yourself to it, thinking it is fate. Miuranosuke Yoshimura surely says to Tokihime: "Will you follow your parent or your husband? There is but one path to peace. Pressed with questions of how to answer and how to think, and torn between the weight of gratitude and the burden of love, with words and tears together... I shall reveal it with resolve. Father-sama, please forgive me." That is what he would say. Therefore, when the critical moment comes, it is a true benefit to follow a husband who will be a real support for the rest of your life, rather than a mother who is short-lived and unreliable, with one foot already in the coffin.'

Speaker: 'Eh, what a hopeless woman you are. Do as you please. I no longer have the leisure to interfere even in your love affairs.'

Speaker: 'Oho ho ho ho, what an interesting drama I have just witnessed. If one were to take this to the Orion Theater in France and perform it, it would surely gain quite a following.'

Speaker: 'No matter what, she is the star actor of the San-iku-kyo, so she is quite skilled. Even before she could properly wear her shoulder-tucks at the age of fifteen or so, she was eloping with men, giving them the cold shoulder, leaving her parents behind to dash off to Ryugu-jima, and calmly calling herself Koryuhime, acting all composed as a queen; she is such a daring person that even I, her parent, cannot get close to her. Oho ho ho.'

While they were indulging in such idle chatter, Miru, the receptionist, came rushing in, prostrated herself before the three of them, and said:

Speaker: 'Right now, Migimori-sama has arrived.'

Seris: 'That is hard work; tell him to come right through here.'

Miru answered, 'Yes,' and ran out to the front. Koganehime left behind a sly grin and quickly hid herself in the King's private chamber.
