Chapter 20: 入那立 [1145]

入那立〔1145〕

§ 1

Samarīhime, feeling pained that her father Kāruchin had not returned as the night deepened, followed by Samoahime, Haruman, and two or three other servants, slipped through the front gate of Iruna Castle and came as far as the wide garden.

The sky of Shinonome was gradually turning red, and Kāruchin and more than ten of his household servants were sprawled out, collapsed upon the lawn and the earth of the garden where the Shimobashira stood.

Samarīhime, knitting her brows, said:

Samarīhime: 'How pathetic. Why on earth are Father, and Yūfutesu and Manmosu as well, lying collapsed in a place like this? Haruman, could you shake them awake?'

Haruman: 'Yes, I shall do so at once.'

No sooner had he said this than he tightened his Nejihachimaki, adjusted his sash, and first of all lifted Kāruchin up.

Kāruchin, rubbing his eyes and looking around restlessly, said:

Kāruchin: 'Hey, you are Samarīhime and Samoa. Where on earth is this? You thought I was drunk and threw me out into the open, didn't you? This is an outrageous thing to do. Very well, I have my own thoughts on this matter.'

He attempted to stand up. Whether it was the prank of some person or other, he was tied with a rope to a huge Suteishi in the garden, and he could not move.

Samarīhime: 'Kāruchin-sama, please pull yourself together. This is the great Hironiha inside Iruna Castle. You must have been plotting something wicked last night and trespassed into the castle. You must have been struck by divine punishment on the spot and left lying in the open like a beggar in such a wretched state. Hey, Yūfutesu, you too, pull yourself together. What kind of black face is that? Is your whole face not smeared with ink?'

Yūfutesu: 'Well, in any case, I accompanied my master, and we successfully annihilated the enemy, and upon celebrating his becoming a Kshatriya, we were treated to sake; it seems that after getting too drunk, the vice-guardian deity brought his physical body along and went out on a trip to sober up. Oh, what a ridiculous thing to have happened. Hahaha!'

Samarīhime: 'What? You and Kāruchin assassinated King Sēran, did you not? Now that it has come to this, you had better be prepared, as you are the enemy of the King.'

She drew her dagger smoothly and held it in a reverse grip, and as she was about to strike at Yūfutesu, Haruman grabbed both of the princess's arms from behind and said:

Haruman: 'First, please wait. There must be some reason for this. You must not act rashly and make a mistake.'

Manmosu: 'Hey, this was the castle's riding ground. It seems we were completely bewitched by a fox. Excuse me, Kāruchin-sama, it will be terrible if the conspiracy is exposed. Come, let us flee quickly. Hey, Yūfutesu, you too, flee quickly, flee!'

Kāruchin: 'Hey, hey, you two, there is no need to make such a fuss. First, untie my rope, and then flee after I have fled. Is there any such thing as abandoning your master and taking freedom of action only for yourselves? Waaah!'

Samarīhime: 'Oh ho ho ho, what a collection of dazed fools this is.'

Samohime: 'Looking at them, the master, Yūfutesu, and Manmosu—the three of them, all leaders of the Unrequited Love Party, are all here together... What an interesting dream you must have had.'

Kāruchin, glaring with anger, said:

Kāruchin: 'Hey, hey, Samoa, what is this Unrequited Love Party? Say it once more. I will not forgive you.'

Samohime: 'I have said something truly rude. It was just so funny that I slipped up. Oh ho ho.'

At that moment, Ryūun, Rēbu, Karu, and Tēmusu, the four of them, threw open the entrance of the mansion and appeared.

Ryūun: 'Hey, Kāruchin-dono, Samarīhime-sama, please come inside first. The King is waiting for you.'

Samarīhime, looking happy, said:

Samarīhime: 'Yes, you are Ryūun-sama, are you not? Please admonish this villain Kāruchin well and send him back. I shall ask for an audience with the King as soon as possible. Hey, Haruman, show me the way.'

Yufutesu: 'No, that is preposterous. I have done nothing bad enough to be thanked by the King. Please let me off the hook for this time.'

Temusu: 'Do not be so worried; birth is easier than worrying. Come, just go as far as you can; who knows whether it will be good or bad, fortunate or unfortunate. Even if you feel it is a bad omen, just think that at most, you will only have your head taken. What is one head, anyway? Ahhh.'

Yufutesu, feeling around his neck with his hands, said:

Yufutesu: 'Ah, as I thought, my head is still firmly attached. Please, let me off with a warning today. You are the savior of my life.'

Temusu: 'No matter what you say, it is an imperial command. A royal word is like sweat; once it has left the mouth, it cannot be retracted. Come, let us go.'

He grabbed him firmly by the scruff of his neck. Yufutesu, like a Yanosuke doll, lost his footing in surprise, and with his hands and feet dangling, he was dragged by Temusu and carried to the inner palace. Riun picked up Karuchin and likewise advanced into the inner chambers. Rebu and Karu carried Manmosu between the two of them, and likewise transported him into the inner palace.

The three members of the Shitsuren-tō were brought before the King, their faces pale, their lips stained purple, and their teeth chattering.

King: 'Umoritsukasa-dono, you must have had a very pleasant time last night.'

Karuchin: 'Yes, I had a dream within a dream, and, well, I have nothing to say.'

He answered fearfully, saying things that made no sense.

King: 'Ahaha, to conquer the world must be a very pleasant thing. How about it? Would you like to succeed the Kshatriya from now on?'

Karuchin: 'No, that is preposterous. I leave everything to the King's will. If it is the King's word, I shall not disobey even a single syllable.'

King: 'You said you would not disobey even a single syllable if it were my word. Are you sure about that?'

Karuchin: 'Yes, a warrior does not go back on his word.'

King: 'Then, you, Umoritsukasa, I order you to commit seppuku for the crime of attempted rebellion.'

Karuchin was terrified by these words, flipped over, and screamed, 'Ah!'

King: 'Ordering you to commit seppuku is merely for show; as of today, I order you to remain in confinement until Tenahime returns in triumph. Be grateful.'

Karuchin stroked his chest in relief and said:

Karuchin: 'Yes, compared to seppuku, confinement is nothing at all. Am I to be alone?'

King: 'No, Yufutesu and Manmosu are the same. If you are confined separately one by one, you might harbor strange thoughts and fall into pessimism, which would be counterproductive, so I order you three to live together in confinement at the Umori mansion. You may drink as much as you like and hold a heartbreak meeting if you wish.'

『然らば汝の望みに任す』

§ 4

Karchin: 'Arigata, thank you. For us whose hearts were clouded, that you would forgive our akugiyakubudau without punishment, and permit us to remain with only heimon, we have no words to express our onrei. From now on, we intend to devote our sincerity everywhere and repay the gōn of the Ōsama, so nanitozonanitozo, do not omisu us, and if you would use us as the lowest of your shimobe after hiyakunichigo, we would be grateful.'

Samarīhime sings the misohitomoji with a clear voice.

Samarīhime: 'Moistened by the dew of the Ōgimi's grace, even the shikogusa of the fields has revived. You, who correct and purify even the jiyūjiyū sins and defilements, are truly noble. Karchin, Mikoto of my father, from now on, serve the Lord without futagokoro.'

Karchin: 'Arigata is the mikotonori of King Seran, which I shall not forget for chiyo or yachiyo. The Kami of Ananahi, who looks upon me, a sinner, and minaho, kikinaho, and norinaho. The kohigumo has now cleared away completely, since I saw the light of the moon of Ananahi.'

Yūfutesu: 'Manipulated by the magic hands of love, I have plotted things that were not good. The demon and great serpent that dwelt in my murakimo have now completely vanished.'

Sērisu: 'Listen, O official of the Kami Yūfutesu, the two paths of love and desire cannot stand together. How pitiful are those who are manipulated by being lost in desire and lost in love. Even I, knowing it was not good, am ashamed of the heart that led the Lord astray.'

Samoa: 'How wretched I am, to have toyed with Manmosu in various ways with the tip of my tongue.'

Manmosu: 'How shameful, to have ended up a prisoner of love and to have exposed my shame unexpectedly. The dew of the Ōgimi's grace is deep, and my heavy sins have been forgiven.'

Ryūun: 'The dark clouds of the castle of Inuna, as well as the moon and the sun, have cleared, and it has become the reign of Hinodewake.'

Kogane: 'The light of the Kami of Ananahi has appeared, and the morning sun shines upon the castle of Inuna.'

Sēran: 'Protected by the divine authority of the Kami, the moon shines upon the castle of Inuna.'

Yasudara: 'I have fled far from the land of Teruman and have been able to meet the noble Lord. Yet, illuminated by the light of the Kami, my kohigumo has vanished. Samarīhime-sama, Mikoto, from now on, serve King Seran. Yasudara-hime-sama, Mikoto, shall follow the teachings of Kogane-hime and walk the path of the Kami.'

Tēmusu: 'The clouds and mist that deeply enveloped the four directions have cleared, and what a joyful sky it is today.'

Karu: 'The mountains and fields have also cleared cleanly, in the divine breath of the castle of Inuna.'

Rēbu: 'The reign of the Lord, which is governed without incident, feels just like the age of the Kami. I, too, follow Kogane-hime, and how joyful it is to head toward the sky of Haruna.'

At last, Yasudara-hime was illuminated by the teachings of the Kami, turned her heart from pining for King Seran, vowed to propagate the true path to the four directions for the sake of all people under heaven, and decided to proceed to the capital of Haruna together with the party of Kogane-hime. King Seran came to love Samarīhime deeply, whom he had previously detested; the two stood together to spread the teachings of Ananahi in the castle of Inuna, and they solidified the foundation of the nation for a hundred years. As a result of his repentance, Karchin was appointed as the Right Guardian Official as before, and after waiting for the triumphant return of Tēna-hime, he served the King in marital harmony. Sērisu-hime became the wife of Yūfutesu through the matchmaking of the King, and Samoa-hime also became the wife of Manmosu through the matchmaking of the King; they served in the castle of Inuna and their descendants prospered. Kogane-hime decided to proceed toward Haruna Castle together with Kiyoteru-hime, Yasudara-hime, and Haruman; Rēbu, Karu, Tēmusu, and Ryūun organized a separate unit and proceeded toward the capital of Haruna while touring various places singing the propagation songs of the Ananahi-kyō. Rīdā became a loyal subject of the King and served him closely. Ah, Kannagara Tamachi Haemaese.
