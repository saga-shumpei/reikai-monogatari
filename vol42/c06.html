Chapter 6: Field 人の Dream [1131]

野人の夢〔1131〕

§ 1

Kāruchin is all alone in the inner chamber of his hall, smiling as he abandons himself to a meditation of love. Coming unto that place were the two, Yūfutesu and Sērisuhime.

Sērisuhime: 'Umori-sama, we are truly grateful that you were pleased to visit our castle earlier and meet with my elder sister. She said that after you departed she felt exceedingly better, and she is in remarkably high spirits. Furthermore, as for what my sister says, she feels uneasy, wondering how a man's heart might change like the autumn sky. She cannot rest assured until she meets with you, Umori-sama, once more. She said she fears that your Okusama might become aware of it, resulting in various disputes and a change of your heart, so she told me, like a complete madman, to go and inquire about your situation once. No matter how many times I told her to rest assured because you are not such a distant person, she would not listen. Therefore, despite it being late at night, I have come to intrude upon you. Thinking that I could not pass through the gates late at night, I had Yūfutesu-sama accompany me.'

Kāruchin whispered in a low voice:

Kāruchin: 'Ah, was that so? Thank you for your trouble. Please tell Hime that my heart shall surely never change. If you do so, she will probably rest assured.'

Thus he already treated her as his wife. Sērisuhime, suppressing her amusement, intentionally assumed a tearful voice:

Sērisuhime: 'Ah, those grateful words! I am as happy as if you had become my husband. Ho-ho-ho-ho-ho!'

Kāruchin: 'Now, now, Sērisuhime-dono, you must not speak of such fickle things. Is not your beloved Yūfutesu right over there? He is terribly anxious, and even the color of his face has changed. A-ha-ha-ha-ha!'

Sērisuhime: 'Ho-ho-ho-ho! A man like this with swollen lips, who can hardly speak properly—I have suddenly come to dislike him.... Or so I say outwardly; right, Yūfutesu-sama? You know the inside of my heart very well, don't you? One cannot speak so fondly of love in front of others, after all.'

Yūfutesu, looking pleased:

Yūfutesu: 'U-fu-fu-fu-fu!'

Sērisuhime: 'Oh my, what an unpleasant way to laugh! I truly come to dislike him. Why did I ever fall for such an unpleasant man? I cannot judge my own heart.'

Kāruchin: 'Now, now, Sērisuhime-sama, is that not too much? Let us have you treat us to something. Having been made to listen to so much love-talk.'

Sērisuhime: 'The love-talk is mutual. As for treating you, let us be frugal and beg for a money order instead. By the way, Kāruchin-sama, what did you do about Okusama? Unless we hear that, my sister will remain anxious after all; isn't that right, Yūfutesu-sama?'

Yūfutesu: 'Th... That is our crucial business right now. Master, what has become of it? Surely you have taken some measure immediately.'

Kāruchin: 'To tell the truth, today Tēna was invited by Manmosu and Samoahime, and she has not yet returned home. However, if I speak of such a thing all of a sudden, a complication will arise at this juncture, so I wish to ask for a stay of execution for a while. In due course, taking an opportunity, I intend to handle it gently, so please convey to Yasudarahime that Kāruchin's heart is as solid as a great rock, so she should rest assured. Rather, we on our side are anxious lest Hime's heart might change. A-ha-ha-ha-ha!'

Sērisuhime: 'It is a matter of anxiety to be born a handsome man, isn't it? Being a homely woman like me might rather be a blessing. Even so, the world is wide, for Yūfutesu-sama, who looks like eyes and a nose attached to dried gourd strips, casts sweet glances at me; so even a homely woman need not be so pessimistic after all.'

Karuchin: 'Serisu-hime-dono, let us meet again.'

He said this, turned away, and quickly hid his figure, heading toward the inner room. The two had no choice but to slip into the darkness, leave the mansion, cross the field road where the winter wind was blowing fiercely, and return toward the castle.

Karuchin parted from the two, sat in the large inner hall with several cushions piled up, composed himself like a monster of vanity, tilted his head repeatedly, leaked laughter he could not seem to suppress, picked his nose, removed food from his teeth with the back of his hand, adjusted the shape of his eyebrows, stroked the back of his neck, and suddenly feeling rejuvenated, he settled back in high spirits. It was Tena-hime who returned there, escorted by Manmosu.

Speaker: 'Husband, I am truly honored that you allowed me to visit such a filthy place for the sake of your wife today. Samu-hime also said she was very satisfied and asked me to convey her gratitude to you, Husband. Seeing you in such an unusually good mood today, I, Manmosu, am also filled with extreme joy.'

Speaker: 'Manmosu, thank you for escorting her back. It would have been fine if you had been a little later.'

Tena-hime wore a displeased expression, and taking advantage of her drunken state, she stepped forward in front of Karuchin and suddenly grabbed him by the collar.

Speaker: 'Hey, Husband, you old kettle, what is this you are saying, that it would have been fine if I had been longer? You must not like me returning home. I thought you had been acting restless for the past two or three days, but you have finally spoken your true feelings, have you not? Did you not complain noisily even if I were away for just a moment until two or three days ago? You must have found another flower to grow. Or perhaps you have plucked a flower that can speak. Now, I will not let this go until I have investigated everything. You always look down on your wife, thinking she is a criminal under your thumb, but I will not tolerate it today. Now, confess, like a man.'

Thus turning desperate, she put forth the horns of Rinki and vented her rage upon the sliding doors, the tatami mats, and the utensils around that place. Instantly, the chest of drawers was overturned, the sliding doors collapsed, the teapot cracked its belly and dribbled its urine, and the brazier danced into the mid-air. A thick fog of ash and smoke engulfed the entire room. It became impossible to set either hand or foot therein.

Mammoth: 'Man-man-man-mos-mos-mos, please wait a little! Oku-sama, if you lose your temper like that, it will harm your health. Karlchin glared sharply at Mammoth, and with a trembling voice:

Karlchin: 'Here, Ma-ma-ma-man, do you mean to utter such outrageous things and disrupt the manor of the Right Protector? Thou foolish fellow of disloyalty and unrighteousness, I grant thee leave of absence from this very day! Go forth at once!'

Tena: 'Look here, Karlchin-sama, with Mammoth present it must be extremely inconvenient for you. As a consequence, with me also remaining here, your convenience must be terribly ill-served. Thus kicking up the tatami mats with her feet and disheveling her hair, caring nothing for shame or public repute, she hid her form away from the manor to parts unknown, weeping aloud like a howling wolf.

Looking after her as she departed, Karlchin shrugged his shoulders with immense delight, jerked his chin, and uttered a soliloquy.

Karlchin: 'A-ha-ha-ha-ha! When things are convenient, how convenient they can truly be! While I was solely deliberating how to drive her out, her offering to jump out on her own accord—what an extraordinarily convenient thing this is! Having committed such violence before departing, Tena could hardly harbor any thought of returning, and for me to meekly keep a woman in my house who has trampled upon me to such an extent would utterly destroy the prestige of the Right Protector. There is no longer any need to listen even if anyone steps in to mediate. E-he-he-he-he! The entry of the Deity of Good Fortune, the retreat of the Deity of Poverty! Truly, the Kishibojin of Fearful Iriya! Thereupon, the Benzaiten of Yasudarahime comes descending, and while circumambulating the Heavenly Pillar and the Earthly Pillar for a short while, she instantly transforms into Hotei-sama, building a harmonious household such that even Daikokuten-sama would envy! Even if the wife's original nature were Bishamonten, so long as she is a beauty, my instinct is satisfied therewith. Even beyond that, Yasudarahime is of the Kshatriya lineage—how on earth could such a splendid thing have erupted? I bow in reverent worship and offer my gratitude to Namu Daijizaiten Ōkunihiko-ōkami! Ah, ah! Could there be anything as delightful as having the lump above my eye removed today? E-he-he-he-he! I need no longer feel constraint toward anyone; we are a married couple before all the world!'
