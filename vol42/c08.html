§ 1

The autumn sky is wild with the gale, / The voice of the deer calling for its mate / Grows thin, and already the winter sky is here. / The cold wind blows through the window, / The fields and mountains adorned in brocade / Have cast off their robes of leaves, / And have at last become bare. / At such a time, O Yūfutesu, / The love-robe that thinks of my own body / Grows ever thicker and heavier, / A feverish state of a hundred times or more, / Mistaking winter for summer, / How shameful it is to rage and rave. / While walking the path of sincerity, / Compared to Umoritsukasa, / My heart deceives and manipulates / A man who is somewhat honest. / Truly, though I feel ashamed, / For the sake of King Sēran and the nation, / Even if I should fall into Jigoku,

In this situation where I must deceive, / May the Kami of Tenchi also accept / The heart of Sērisu, / And Kamunagara, / By Nahohi, Minaho, and Kikinaho, / May the great Taihen of the land of Iruna / Be prevented before it occurs. / Yūfutesu, who has been completely deceived, / Must surely be spirited now, / Biting his tongue and striking his jaw, / Forgetting even his painful body, / With his heart set solely on my affairs, / Leaving his Iheji and walking the Daidau, / Hurrying and advancing. / May Bonten, Taishiyaku, and Jizaiten / Protect him well / So that he may not fall on the Daidau again / And suffer a great Kega.

Yūfu is also, after all, / A child of Kami born of the Miiki / Of the Kami of Tenchi, a palace of Kami. / I do not think of him with hatred, / But as a sacrifice of loyalty / For the sake of King Sēran, / I perform this deception, which is not in my heart, / Shamelessly in broad daylight. / O Kami of Tenchi, O hundred Kami, / Do not blame the sins of Princess Sērisu, / But quickly forgive them, Kamunagara. / I pray before the presence of the Kami.

It is Princess Sērisu who is singing this elegantly, accompanying herself on the two-stringed koto, as the words of the song reveal.

It is Yūfutesu who has crept in, hiding his footsteps in the corridor and peering around. With a style like a thin dog that has sniffed out smelly beggar's food, he sticks out his injured tongue about five minutes, rests it carefully on his lower lip, swings his waist and neck alternately, stoops slightly, spreads his left and right hands out in a strange posture with palms facing upward, and with a comical gesture like a beggar asking for something,

Sērisu: 'Moshi... Moshi... Hime-sama.'

He began to speak as if it were difficult to do so. Sērisu thought... Ah, that unpleasant man has come again; I must suppress my disgust for a while and perform one act. No matter how much I dislike him, I cannot show a look of dislike. There is a saying, 'Smiling at an unpleasant guest, and secretly weeping on one's lap.' She quickly decided in her heart that it was the art of war of Sun and Wu to shower him with charm without reserve, and filling her face with a dozen parts of smiles,

Sērisu: 'Yūfutesu-sama, how is your injury? Please take care of yourself. I was so worried that I could hardly sleep last night. If you were not alive in this world, I would no longer have any hope of living in society. Say, you, aren't you precious? Oh, how embarrassing...'

Hearing this, Yūfutesu scratched his head until it made a grating sound, and while drool poured endlessly from the side of his tongue that he could not control, he hurriedly wiped it with his sleeve,

Yūfutesu: '(Speaking as if it were difficult) O Hime-sama... thank you... thanks to you... it is nothing serious... so, well, please be at ease... I am perfectly... healthy. It hurt terribly yesterday, but thanks to you, the great throbbing has stopped today, and my mood has improved quite a bit.'

Sērisu: 'I am truly happy to hear that. That must be it, because I was praying to Jizaiten-sama all day and all night. If it is for your sake, even if my life were to end, I would not regret it in the slightest.'

Yūfutesu: 'That is... grateful... that your... spirit... was that... passionate... I never even dreamed of it. At first, I suspected that you might be trying to distract me... but after all, suspecting you... was because my own heart was impure... Please, Hime-sama, even a worthless man like me has understood you this far, so please cherish me for a long time to come... In return, if it is for your sake, even to the den of demons, or to the rocky cave of lions and wolves, if you tell me to jump, I will jump. Not to mention the habitat of wild beasts, even in the midst of raging fire or at the bottom of the water, if it is your command... no, if it is your request... I will faithfully carry out your service.'

Ohoho, Anata, if you speak so formally to me, I feel as though we are becoming strangers, and it makes me feel quite uneasy. Please, from now on, dispense with such false formalities and call me by the name of a wife. If you would do that for me, you cannot imagine how happy I would be. Ohoho.

Now, Hime-sama, or rather Serisu-hime, rejoice, for a great thing has come to pass. It is a major upheaval where heaven becomes earth and earth becomes heaven. Since this is the result of what was squeezed out from the minds of the peerlessly cunning Yūfutesu and Serisu-hime, it is a formidable thing indeed, otsuhoho. Aita, if I laugh too much, my tongue hurts again. Aahn.

What do you mean by a major upheaval? Please tell me quickly. I am so anxious that I cannot help myself. Whether it is good or bad, virtuous or evil, come, let me hear it quickly.

So saying, she narrowed her eyes, tilted her head, thrust her chin forward, stuck her tongue out toward the corner of her right lip, and cast a flirtatious glance. Yūfutesu became increasingly proud and was eager to boast of his achievements with great exaggeration, but because his tongue would not obey him as he wished, he grew frustrated and, blinking his eyes repeatedly, said:

The changing of heaven and earth means... well, it means that the five hundred cavalrymen whom you were worried about, dispatched by the Lord Ōkuninushi-sama, have been turned back.

Eh, so it has finally been carried out, has it? Kiyoteru-hime-sama must be very pleased as well; Kiyoteru-hime-sama is truly great, is she not?

That is certainly true, for she has the skill to become... wait, your fake sister, so if one calls her great, she is indeed great, but the credit for eighty or ninety percent of it still belongs to Yūfutesu and Serisu-hime. No matter how much wisdom or beauty one possesses, one cannot perform a play alone, so it would be no exaggeration to call us a couple of thousand-ryō actors. Aahn.

Ohoho, you should not call us a married couple before we have even had a formal wedding. If such a thing were to reach the ears of the sharp-tongued children of the capital, and they were to say that Yūfutesu and his wife had a free marriage, or that Serisu-hime is a specimen of a tomboy or a new woman, it would be a nuisance for both of us.

Then what should I call it? Being called a married couple should not be such an unpleasant matter, should it? Aahn.

That is certainly true; I am dying to live together, calling each other husband and wife as soon as possible. I was just so happy that I acted a little coy. Ohoho.

Eh, what a difficult woman you are. You should not tease your husband like that.

Whether you are my husband, a man, an ottosei, or a nattosei, does it not matter? In this vast world, you are the only one who will be my true ottosei. They say that among a thousand, there is only one true husband; even among the fur seals that inhabit the area around Etorofu Island in large numbers, it is said that in truth, there is only one real ottosei among a thousand. It is said that the other nine hundred and ninety-nine are all nattosei. Ahotuhoho.

Nattosei... that is the first time I have heard of such a thing, but I wonder where the distinction lies between an ottosei and a nattosei.

There is certainly a distinction. A nattosei, if we were to speak in terms of humans, is a yakuza man. After choosing a husband, one finds that no matter who one looks at, they are too short for a belt or too long for a sash, and one cannot find the husband of one's heart. While doing so, the horse of days and months gallops like an arrow, and the cherry blossoms that have begun to bloom seem as if they are about to leave the branches if one hesitates. So, one hurriedly decides on a man to be one's back. At that time, no matter who one looks at, there is no distinction between A, B, C, or D, but one grabs onto one point one likes—such as this man having a high nose, a firm mouth, or cool eyes—and decides, 'I will make this one my husband.' That is what is called a nattosei. Otsuhoho.

『俺はお前の事なら、何でも皆秘密を明かしてやる覚悟だ。時に何だよ………五百騎を差止めたばかりでなく、テーナ姫さまが三百余騎の強者を皆引率して、ハルナの国まで行つて了つたのだから、カールチンの部下は最早一人も残つてゐないのだ。もう斯うなつちや、何程謀叛を企まうたつて、駄目だからなア。後に残つてる奴ア、目ツかちや、跛や聾、間しやくに合はぬ奴ばかりウヨウヨしてるのだ。屈強盛りの豪傑連は、皆テーナ姫に従軍したのだから、之を一時も早く、清照さまに………報告して喜ばしたいものだ。アーン』

『ホヽヽ、そんな事ですかい。それなら夜前私の許へチヤンと無言霊話がかかりましたワ。清照姫様も既に既に御存じですよ。そんな遅い報告は駄目です。モチツト早く報告して貰はぬと、女房のあてえが清照姫様へ申上げて手柄にする訳には行かぬぢやありませぬか』

『何分舌を怪我したものだから、舌が遅れたのだよ。それは惜しい事をしたものだ。ウツフヽヽヽ、此奴ア一つガツカリした』

『オツホヽヽヽ時に右守は如何して居られますか、随分御機嫌が良いでせうなア』

ユーフテスは最前から余り舌を無暗に使つたので、チツとばかり腫れて来たと見え、

『アヽヽ』

と言ひながら、手を拡げて不恰好な仕方をして見せて居る。

『オホヽヽまるで蟷螂が踊つとる様だワ。モウ一つ違うたら米搗バツタの手踊みたやうですワ。あたえ、そんなスタイル見るの、嫌になつたワ。オツホヽヽヽ』

『アーンアーンアーン、ウーウーウー、シシ舌が、オオ思ふよに、きけなくなつた』

セーリス姫は両手を組み、鎮魂の姿勢を取り、心静かにユーフテスの舌に向つて、

『一二三四五六七八九十百千万』

と天の数歌を三四回繰返し祈願をこらした。不思議やユーフテスの舌は其場で腫が引き、痛みもとまり、又もや水車の如く運転し始めた。

『ヤア有難う、不思議の御神徳で輪転機の破損が全部修繕したと見え、運転が自由自在になつて来ました。サア是から三寸の舌鋒を縦横無尽にふりまはし、懸河の弁舌滔々と神算秘策を陳述する事としよう。女房喜べ、天の瓊矛は恢復したぞや、アハヽヽヽ』

『オホヽヽヽあの元気な事、わたしも之で一安心しました。イヒヽヽ』

かく云ふ所へやさしい女の声で、襖の外から、

『モシモシ、ユーフテス様、あてえはセーリスで厶います、どうぞ開けて下さいな』

『ハテ合点が行かぬ様になつて来たワイ。俺が今セーリス姫と話をしてゐるに、なアんだ。又チツトも違はぬ声を出しよつて………ユーフテスさま、開けて下さい………と吐しよる、ウーン、此奴はチツト変だぞ』

と首をかたげ、眉毛に唾をぬりつけ始めた。

セーリス『オホヽヽ』

襖の外から、同じ声色で、

『オホヽヽ』
