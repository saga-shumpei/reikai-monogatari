Chapter 13: 蚊燻 [1620]

蚊燻〔1620〕

§ 1

When a person (Seirei) is in an inner Jiyōtai, they contemplate their own Jiiu Ishi as it is, and therefore their Saunen originates from their original Jiyōdō, which is love itself. At that time, Saunen and Ishi are in Itsuchi. Due to this Itsuchi, the inner Seirei of a person does not perceive itself as thinking, but merely as willing. There is something similar to this when one engages in Gensetsu, but the only point of Saui is that such Gensetsu contains a feeling of hesitation to expose the Saunen that belongs to that Ishi in a Sekirarateki manner. The reason for this is that the Shifukuwan of pursuing worldly things and conducting one's life while the person (Seirei) was in the Genkai has become attached to that Ishi.

When a Seirei is in the inner Jiyōtai, it clearly reveals what kind of Jinkaku that Seirei (person) possessed. This is because the Seirei at this time acts solely based on Jiga. A Seirei that resided in goodness internally while in the Genkai can, at this point, recognize that its actions are increasingly in accordance with Risei and Shiyōkaku. This is because it has now severed its Kuwanren with the Nikutai, cleared away the Kumokiri that clouded its spirit, and cast off all material things to which it was attached. Conversely, a Seirei whose inner self resided in evil has now escaped the outer Jiyōtai, and its actions are like those of a fool or a madman, exposing a state of madness even more intense than when it was in the present world, revealing an ugly countenance. This is because the Seirei whose inner self was evil has now gained freedom and is released from the shackles of the outer Jiyōtai that decorated its surface. Those who, while in the present world, were anxious to pose as rational individuals by feigning outer goodness, beauty, and soundness have had their outer facade completely removed, and thus their mad nature has been exposed without reservation. A person who pretended to be a good person on the outside and posed as a scholar or an intellectual is like a box of brocade-wrapped horse manure; when viewed from the outside, it emits a truly beautiful luster that makes others unable to suppress feelings of envy, but when one removes the cover and looks at the inner side, one is surprised to see the filth lying within. Even those who are considered distinguished people, such as spiritual scholars, philosophers, or religious leaders, when their outer cover is removed, truly have the filth of dementia and madness accumulated within, exposing the current state of the hellish realm.

Those who, while in the present world, recognized the divinity of God, loved the truth of God, and acted according to their inner conscience, are, upon entering the spiritual realm, immediately led into the inner Jiyōtai, as if awakening from a long sleep or as if one who has advanced from darkness into light. Because their Shisaku is also based on the light of Takamagahara and originates from their inner Shiyōkaku, all their actions arise from goodness and overflow from their inner Jiyōdō. Thus, Takamagahara flows into their Saunen and Jiyōdō, filling their inner self with joy and happiness, and they taste a happiness they have never known before. Once it has reached this state, communication with the heavenly beings of Takamagahara is opened, and they worship the Lord God, serve with all their heart, exert their independent spirit, leave behind outer religious practices, and enter into inner religious practices. Such is the Jiyōtai that those who have lived a life of inner goodness and truth according to the teachings of Ananai-kyō are about to enjoy. However, even those who have joined religious groups other than Ananai-kyō, if their Seirei loved the truth of God, resided in inner goodness, and recognized the divinity of God to serve Him, the same applies to them.

Conversely, the inner Jiyōtai in the spiritual realm of those who, while in the present world, lived in hypocrisy, abandoned God and lived in evil, extinguished their conscience, denied the divinity of God, or were ashamed to call upon the name of God and hid behind various names to immerse themselves in spiritual research, invited evil spirits to probe the spiritual realm and deceive people in the present world, or those who once believed in the teachings of Ananai but changed their minds and cast them aside like worn-out sandals, or slandered them and drove the hearts of the people of the world into madness, is entirely the opposite.

人（精霊）の内面的情態に居る時は自有の意志その儘を思索するが故にその想念は元来の情動即ち愛そのものより来るものです。そして其時に於て想念と意志とは一致する。この一致によつて人の内面的なる精霊は自ら思惟するを覚えずただ意志するとのみ思ふものです。又言説する時も之に似たるものがありますが只相違せる点は、その言説はその意志に属する想念そのままを赤裸々的に露出することを憚るの情が籠つてゐるものです。その故は人（精霊）が現界に在つた時に俗を逐ふて其生を営みたる習慣がその意志に附属するに至るからであります。

精霊が内面の情態に居る時は、その精霊（人）が如何なる人格を所有して居たかと云ふことを明かに現はすものです。この時の精霊は自我に由つてのみ行動するからであります。現界に在つた時に内面的に善に居つた精霊は茲に於て其行動の理性と証覚とにかなふこと益々深きものあるを認め得られるものです。今や肉体との関連を断ち、雲霧の如く心霊を昏迷せしめ、且つ執着せる物質的事物を全部脱却したからであります。之に反し精霊の内面が悪に居つたものは、今や外面的情態を脱れて了ひ、その行動は痴呆の如く狂人の如く、現世に在つた時よりも層一層の癲狂状態を暴露し、醜悪なる面貌を表はすものであります。彼精霊の内面悪なりしものは今や自由を得て表面を飾る外面情態の繋縛を離れたからです。現世にあつて外面上善美と健全の相を装ひ理性的人物に擬せむとして焦慮して居たものが、全く外面の皮相を取り除かれたので、その狂質は遺憾なく暴露するに至つたのであります。外面上善人を装ひ学者識者を以て擬して居た人間は馬糞を包んだ錦絵の重の内の様なもので、外面より見れば実に美麗なる光沢を放ち人をして羨望の念に堪へざらしむるものですが、その蔽葢を取り除けて内面を見る時は始めて汚物の伏在せるを見て驚くやうなものです。心霊学者だとか、哲学者だとか、宗教家だとか、種々の立派な人間も外面の蔽葢を取り去つて見れば、実に痴呆癲狂の汚物が内面に堆積され、地獄界の現状が暴露されるものであります。

現世に在つた時神格を認め神真を愛し内面の良心に従つて行動を為せしものは、霊界に入り来る時は直に内面の情態に導き入れられて永き眠りより醒めたる如く、又暗黒より光明に進み入りしものの如きものであります。その思索もまた高天原の光明に基き内面的証覚より発し来るが故に、凡ての行動は善より起り、内面的情動より溢れ出づるものです。かくて高天原は想念と情動との中に流れ入り歓喜と幸福とを以て其内面を充満せしめ、未だかつて知らざる幸福を味はふものです。最早かくなりし上は高天原の天人との交通が開けて居るので、主の神を礼拝し真心を尽して奉仕し、自主の心を発揮し、外的聖行を離れて、内面的聖行に入るものです。かくの如きは三五教の教示に由りて、内面的善真の生涯を送りしものの将に享くる所の情態であります。併し三五教以外の教団に入信したものと雖も、神真を愛し内面的善に住し神格を認めて奉仕したるものの精霊も亦同様であります。

之に反し現世にあつて偽善に住し神を捨て悪に住し、良心を滅し神格を否定し、或は神の名を称ふる事を恥ぢて、種々の名目にかくれ霊的の研究に没頭し、凶霊を招致して霊界を探り現世の人間を欺瞞し、又は一旦三五の教を信じ乍ら心機一転して弊履の如く之を捨て去り、或は誹謗し、世間の人心を狂惑したるものの精霊界に於ける内面的情態は全然之と正反対であります。

§ 2

Furthermore, those who do not acknowledge the Shinkaku within their Naishin, or who treat it with Keishi, attempting to ascertain the Zaihi of Kami by basing everything upon Kuwagaku, and who pride themselves on their own Gashiki, are all of an evil Reisei. Even if they do not deny Kami in their Guwaimenteki Saunen, and though they may acknowledge Him and exhibit a somewhat Keishinteki Taido, those whose Naimenteki Seishin is not truly so are Yatsupari evil. This is because acknowledging the Shinkaku and dwelling in evil are Ahii and cannot coexist. Furthermore, they proclaim, 'Since we merely believe in Kami and study Shiukyō, we shall never abandon our Chii as scholars nor cast aside our Yakume to engage in Nifushin. We have merely believed in certain Monku of the Shinyu. If, by any remote chance, that Shinyu were to be delayed in appearing in the world even by a year, or if there were any error, I myself would be the first to smash the teaching platform to pieces,' and in the end, they destroy the sacred group of Kami both physically and spiritually as they intended, burdening others with heavy debts, casting the pillar of Kami into the depths of the valley, pouring boiling tea over His head, and hoisting their sails to flee, leaving Jerusalem behind to begin various new schemes; such as these guardian spirits are truly evil spirits of a malicious nature within. However, such spirits wear a mask of goodness on the surface and play with discourse of goodness and truth like heavenly beings, and therefore, they do not easily expose their inner ugliness in the present world. When such spirits come to the spiritual world and enter into their inner state, and one hears what they say and observes their actions, they appear as if they do not know the difference between before and after, looking like madmen.

The malicious greed of these spirits explodes here, manifesting every aspect of hatred, insulting others without limit, and showing the true form of evil in every place, daring to commit evil deeds to the point that one doubts if they are the actions of human beings at all. When they were in the present world, they were suppressed and hindered by external things, but now they have cast off those shackles and are able to behave with unrestrained freedom, leaving themselves to the Saunen born of their own will. The reasoning power they possessed while alive all dwelt in the exterior and did not dwell in the interior, which is why they come to manifest such an evil appearance. Yet, they were confident that they had more inner discernment than others. The spirits of those who are called scholars and intellectuals today generally have only their Guwaimenteki state opened, and it seems that the majority have their interior become a dwelling place for evil spirits.
