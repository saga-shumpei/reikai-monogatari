Chapter 15: Waves の上 [1622]

波の上〔1622〕

§ 1

The two ships in which Tamakuniwake and Hatsuwakahime were riding carried Itahiko and four others divided between them, setting out across the surface of Lake Sūraya toward the southwest, and with the favorable wind of the moment, they raised their full sails and proceeded toward the port of Eru.

Brawāda, Asumagaruda, Kākusu, and Bēsu were aboard the ship of Hatsuwakahime. Itahiko was riding alone on the ship of Tamakuniwake.

To pass the time while crossing the vast and boundless surface of the lake, they began to share various stories of success and failure.

Masumihiko turned to Itahiko and said,

Masumihiko: 'Itahiko-sama, you have achieved quite a feat. Well, now that the Yakuō jewel is in your hands, you should have no cause for dissatisfaction. Since you are the hero who managed to Kotomukeyaha the Riuō Takushaka, we cannot even dare to approach your side.'

Itahiko: 'No, no, to be told such things is more than I deserve. In truth, the Riuō Ubananda said that he would not hand it over to me because I lacked divine power, but that he would give it to Hatsuwakahime-sama, and after complaining at length, he finally handed it over. This time, it was a complete failure. Ha ha ha!'

Masumihiko: 'However, Itahiko-sama, I hear you have acquired a wife named Brawāda.'

Itahiko turned bright red and said,

Itahiko: 'Oh, you embarrass me. No matter how much I refused, the other party would not accept it, and due to the earnest requests of her parents and siblings, I have made a reservation of sorts. However, it has not yet reached the point of being a formal marriage. In any case, I must obtain the permission of the teacher. The Riuō Ubananda said that the official Itahiko had become weak in divine power because his heart was captured by a woman. It is strange, as my heart is not captured by any woman.'

Tamakuniwake and Mitsuhiko suppressed their amusement, looking down and laughing quietly.

Masumihiko: 'Itahiko-sama, it is impossible that you of all people would have your heart captured by a woman, but perhaps that Riuō fellow was jealous and teased you by saying such things. If it were true, did he not hand over to you the jewel that should have been given to Hatsuwakahime-sama? He gave it to you thinking that Brawāda-sama was by your side. If I say that, it might offend your heart, but there, for the sake of your wife, the Riuō-sama was being considerate. Ha ha ha!'

Itahiko: 'No, no, absolutely not, that is not the case at all. When I finally crossed the line of death on that mountain and entered the cavern, because my divine power was insufficient, all five of us in the party were struck by evil spirits, fell into a state of suspended animation, and set out on a journey to the world of the dead. We had a confrontation with Takahime of the Uranai sect, threw around some trivial sophistry, and as we proceeded through the dark, dark wilderness, we bumped into a mountain like a needle; we had a truly terrible time. Then Smart-sama appeared, and subsequently Hatsuwakahime-sama appeared, drove away the guardian deity of Takahime, and returned us to the manifest world. Oh, even now, just remembering it makes me shudder. They say, "Success is hard to achieve and easy to lose, and time is hard to obtain and easy to waste," and the world does not go well at all. Without knowing it, I became arrogant before I knew it, and my failure was in acting like a married couple; that Takahime creature took a great deal of my vitality even in the world of the dead.'

Masumihiko: 'Why did you hand over to Hatsuwakahime the wife with whom you shared life and death after crossing the line of death? Is that not a bit too distant?'

Itahiko: 'No, she took care of me until the port of Eru. Besides, I would be in trouble if I were teased by you all on the ship.'

Masumihiko: 'No, Itahiko-sama, you teased Mitsuhiko-sama and his wife, so as a man as quick to see the situation as you, Itahiko-sama, you must have been setting up a defensive line.'

Both moon and sun float between the waves of Lake Suraya; today, a celestial maiden crosses over.

The divine official joining the ranks of the heavenly beings, how his heart must be emboldened. The port of El has finally come into view, and the mountain of El is faintly visible. The moon of the fifteenth night is in the sky, and I row the boat from early in the morning. Beyond the port of El lies Jerusalem; I wish to visit the presence of God once. Drifting on the waves morning and evening, I always look at the water mirror of the moon and sun.'

流石の高姫驚いて 裏口あけて裏山の 枯木林に身をかくし 雲を霞と消えにける 吾等五人は勇み立ち 凱歌あげし心地して 枯野ケ原をさまよひつ 神の試練に会ひ乍ら 三五教の信仰を 生命にかへて守りたる その報いにや神使 スマートさまが現はれて 高姫司の守護神 銀毛八尾の悪狐をば 追ひやり玉へば忽ちに 四辺の光景一変し いと苦しみし吾身体 俄に快くなりて 勇気日頃に百倍し 天地の神に打向ひ

感謝祈願の太祝詞 唱ふる折しも三五の 神の司の初稚姫が 此場に現れましまして 吾等が迷ふ霊身を 明きに救ひ玉ひけり 気をとり直し四辺をば よくよく見ればこは如何に 歓喜竜王の岩窟と 判りし時の嬉しさよ ここに竜王は初稚姫の 生言霊に歓喜して 多年の苦悶を免れしと 喜び勇み幾度か 感謝の言葉奉り 夜光の玉を伊太彦に 手づから渡し玉ひつつ 別離の歌を宣りおへて 大空高く昇りけり あゝ惟神々々

神の恵の有難さ 初稚姫に従ひて 海に通ずる岩窟の 光を見当てに隧道を 探り出づれば有難や 吾師の君は玉の舟 波打際に横たへて 吾等を救ひ玉ふべく 待たせ玉ふぞ尊けれ 朝日は照るとも曇るとも 月は盈つとも虧くるとも 神の恵みと師の恵み 幾千代迄も忘れまじ 思へば思へば有難や 大空清く海清く 月亦清き玉の舟 清き真帆をば掲げつつ 清けき風に送られて 清き教の司等と 清き話を取交はし

珍の都へ指して行く 吾身の上こそ嬉しけれ あゝ惟神々々 御霊幸はひましませよ』

斯く互に歌を謡ひ或は雑談に耽り乍ら、翌日の東雲頃玉国別の舟はエルの港に着いた。早くも初稚姫は一行と共に上陸し玉ひてスマートを引連れ波止場に立つて一行の来るを待ち受け玉ひつつあつた。スマートは喜んで「ウワッウワッ」と鳴き立てて居る。
