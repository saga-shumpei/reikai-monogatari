Chapter 10: 鷺と鴉 [1617]

鷺と鴉〔1617〕

§ 1

After the separation of spirit and body, there is a process that every Ningen must tread before falling into the Rakudo of Takaamahara or the Ankokukai of Jigoku, and this state has three kinds of distinctions. These three states are broadly divided into the state of the Guwaimen, the state of Jiyunbi, and the state of the Naimen. However, as the Dokushiya is already Goshiyōchi from the Monogatari up to Konnichi, there are Seirei who ascend directly to Takaamahara after death and Seirei who fall into Jigoku. Those who go directly to Tenkai or Jigoku without passing through the Jiyunbi of the Chiūkai, also known as the Seireikai, are those whose Jiyunbi was already complete during their Seizen, and who obtain a place corresponding to their own spirit according to their emotional movements of Zenaku and their In-en. For such Seirei whose Jiyunbi is already complete, once they wash away the natural and worldly evil habits along with their physical bodies, they are immediately led to a Rakudo comparable to the love of the master of the corresponding place in Tenkai through the protection and guidance of the Tenjin. Conversely, for those Seirei who fall directly into Jigoku, they are those who, while in the Genkai, only touted love and Zen on the surface and performed only hypocritical actions, while harboring evil deep within their hearts; they are those who used Zen and love to deceive others by whitewashing their own wickedness. Among them, those who are most full of deceit and fraud are thrown in as if they are being cast down with their feet toward the sky and their heads toward the ground. Besides this, there are those who fall into Jigoku in various other states, and some are thrown deep into rock caves immediately after death; such states are all by the providence of the Lord God in order to separate them from the Seirei in the Seireikai. At times they are taken out of the rock caves, and at other times they are drawn in; such Seirei are those who, during their Seizen, only pretended to be kind with their lips to catch people off guard, and took advantage of that void to calculate their own profit and cause damage to the people of the world, but such cases are relatively few. The majority are kept in the Seireikai, where they are taught the Shinkyō, and according to the degree of the Seirei's own Zenaku, they are made to follow the order of the Lord God and prepare to enter the third lower heaven or Jigoku. Each Ningen's Seirei has two aspects: the Guwaimen and the Naimen. The Guwaimen of the Seirei is the means by which a Ningen, when interacting with other people in the Genkai, makes their body conform to them, which is the external state such as facial expression, language, and movement; the Naimen of the Seirei is the state belonging to a person's will and the thoughts arising from that will, which does not easily appear on the Guwaimen. All Ningen learn from childhood to display weapons such as the feelings of friendship, benevolence, righteousness, sincerity, and morality on the Guwaimen, but because they deeply store all thoughts arising from their will in the inner depths, it is truly impossible for the eyes of other Ningen to see through them. It is common for modern Ningen to lead a pseudo-moral and pseudo-civilized life for the sake of convenience in surface life, no matter how full of evil and lawlessness their hearts may be. As a result of long years of habit in the Genkai, Ningen have become spiritually paralyzed and can no longer even know their own Naimen. It is rare for them to even worry about the Zenaku of their own inner life. How much more so is it in perceiving the inner life of others besides themselves? The state of a Ningen's Seirei in the Seireikai immediately after death is such that their appearance, language, and temperament are still very similar to when their body was in the Genkai, and there is not the slightest difference in their life state regarding morality and civilization. Therefore, if a Ningen's Seirei after death does not pay attention to the things they encounter in the Seireikai, and even when the Tenjin revives that Seirei, if they do not recall that they are already a Seirei, that Seirei has no choice but to have the sensation that they are still living in the Genkai like Yoshikohime. Therefore, the death of a Ningen is merely a passage between these states.

人間が霊肉脱離の後、高天原の楽土又は地獄の暗黒界へ陥るに先んじて何人も踏まねばならぬ経過がありまして、この状態は三種の区別があります。そしてこの三状態を大別して、外面の状態、準備の状態、内面の状態と致します。併し乍ら死後直に高天原へ上る精霊と地獄へ陥る精霊とのあることは、今日迄の物語に於て読者は既に已に御承知の事と思ひます。中有界一名精霊界の準備を経過せずして直に天界又は地獄に行くものは生前既に其準備が出来て居て善悪の情動並に因縁によつて各自霊魂相応の所を得るものです。右の如く準備既に完了せる精霊にあつては只その肉体と共に自然的世界的なる悪習慣等を洗滌すれば直に天人の保護指導に依つて天界の、それ相応の所主の愛に匹敵した楽土に導かるるものであります。之に反して直に地獄に陥る精霊にあつては、現界に於て表面にのみ愛と善とを標榜し且つ偽善的動作のみ行ひ、内心深く悪を蔵し居りしもの、所謂自己の凶悪を糊塗して人を欺くために善と愛とを利用したものであります。中にも最も詐偽や欺騙に富んで居るものは足を上空にし頭を地に倒にして投げ込まれるやうにして落ち行くものです。この外にも種々様々の状態にて地獄へ陥ち行くものもあり、或は死後直に岩窟の中深く投げ入れられるものもありますが、斯の如き状態になるのは凡て神様の御摂理で精霊界にある精霊と分離せむがためであります。或時は岩窟内より取り出され、又ある時は引き入れられる場合もありますが、斯の如き精霊は生前に於て口の先計りで親切らしく見せかけて、世人を油断させ其虚に乗じて自己の利益を計り、且つ世の人に損害を与へたものですが、斯様な事は比較的少数であつて、その大部分は精霊界に留められて神教を授かり精霊自己の善悪の程度によつて神の順序に従ひ、第三下層天国、又は地獄へ入るの準備を為さしめらるるものであります。人間各自の精霊には外面的、内面的の二方面を有しております。精霊の外面とは人間が現世に於て他の人々と交はるに際し其身体をして之に適順せしむる所の手段を用ひる事で特に面色、語辞、動作等の外的状態であり、精霊の内面とは人の意思及びその意志よりする想念に属する状態であつて、容易に外面には現はれないものであります。凡ての人間は幼少の頃より朋友の情だとか、仁義誠実、道徳等の武器を外面に模表する事を習つて居りますが、其意志よりする所の凡ての想念は之を深く内底に包蔵するが故に、人間同士の眼よりは之を観破することは実に不可能であります。現代の人間はその内心は如何に邪悪無道に充ちて居つても、表面生活上の便宜のために似非道徳的、似非文明的生涯を営むのは常であります。現界永年の習慣の結果、人間は精神痳痺し切つて了つて、自己の内面さへ知る事が出来なくなつて居ります。また自己の内面的生涯の善悪などに就いて煩慮することさへも稀であります。況んや自己以外の他人の内面的生涯の如何を察知するに於ておやであります。死後直ちに精霊界に於ける人間精霊の状態はその肉体が現世にありし時の如く依然として容貌、言語、性情等は相酷似し、道徳上、民文上の生活の状態と少しの相違もない。故に人間死後の精霊にして精霊界に於て相遇ふ事物に注意を払はず、又天人が彼精霊を甦生せし時に於ても、自己は最早一箇の精霊だといふことを想ひ起さなかつたなれば、その精霊は依然高姫の如く現界に在つて生活を送つて居るといふ感覚をなすの外は無いのです。故に人間の死といふものは唯此間の通路に過ぎないものであります。

§ 2

The human spirit that has left the Gensei and has not yet passed many days, as well as the spirit that has entered the Reikai due to the temporary Henchō of a Genkaijin, first exists in such a state as described above, and they meet and recognize their friends and acquaintances from their previous life. This is because spirits perceive one another through their Menshiyoku, Gengo, and the like, and when they approach each other, they perceive one another through the Ensau of their life force. In the Reikai, when person A thinks of person B, they immediately recall their Menbau, and at the same time, they think of all the Jibutsu that occurred during their Shiyōgai. And when person A does this, person B immediately appears before person A, just as if one had sent a Wazabito to summon them. If one asks why such Jiiu exists in the Reikai, it is because the Reikai is a world of thought, where there is a natural traffic of thought and everything is governed by spiritual phenomena, and there is no such thing as time or space as in the Genkai. Therefore, those who have entered the Reikai cannot fail to recognize their friends, relatives, and acquaintances through the emotions of their thoughts, and they converse and associate with one another according to the affections they held while in the Gensei, with almost no difference from when they were in the Gensei. Among these, the reunion of spouses is considered common, but when spouses reunite, they congratulate one another, and they spend their lives together for a period that may be long or short, compared to the degree to which they tasted and enjoyed the joy of living together as husband and wife in the Gensei. And when there is no true marital love between those spouses, that is, a harmony of hearts based on the love of the Shinkai, those spouses part ways after a short time.

Furthermore, if there was no mutual understanding between spouses in the Gensei, and there was jealousy, discord, strife, or other inner feelings of aversion, this hostile thought immediately bursts to the surface, and they fight and separate. The good spirits in the Reikai, namely the Tennin, use various methods to examine the good and evil, the righteousness and wickedness, of the spirits who have newly arrived from the Genkai. The character of a spirit is not easily distinguished in its outward state after death. No matter how wicked and lawless a spirit may be, they can speak well of outward truths and perform good deeds, and one cannot find the slightest difference from a good spirit of perfect sincerity and perfect goodness. Outwardly, they all lead a life that appears to be virtuous and loving. In the Genkai, they lived under a certain governing system, obeying the laws, and thereby gained a reputation as righteous and sincere persons, or received special favors, rose to noble positions, and accumulated wealth; these are recognized as good and virtuous persons for a short time after death. However, when the Tennin distinguish the good and evil of these spirits, they generally follow the method below. Since everyone is swayed by the love of their master, evil spirits are extremely fond of discussing only outward Jibutsu and do not pay the slightest attention to inner Jibutsu. They do not discuss the teachings of God, the divinity of the Savior of the Holy Land, or the truth and goodness concerning Takamagahara, and they dislike hearing them taught, pay no further attention to them, and do not enjoy hearing the teachings of God, but rather feel unpleasant and even show it on their Menbau. The majority of Genkaijin call the teachings of the gods and Buddhas superstition, and the vast majority consider it a great disgrace to even mention the gods and Buddhas; I have often experimented and found that no matter how kindly one tries to make them hear the teachings of Omoto and how anxiously one tries to save and help them into the Tenkai, it is ultimately useless for humans who have registered themselves on the path to hell. Nevertheless, we must embody the heart of the great God of great mercy and compassion, and lead even one person to advance to the Tenkai and head toward an eternal and infinite life, thereby serving a part of the divine administration of the Shinkai. It is the same in the Reikai as it is in the Genkai, but in order to distinguish between an evil spirit entering hell and a good spirit heading to the Tenkai, one can see that evil spirits often try to advance in a certain direction. When an evil spirit is left to its own devices, it travels along the roads that lead to it, and by the direction they travel and the roads they turn onto, one can ascertain where the love of their master lies.

§ 3

There are no spirits that newly enter the Reikai after leaving the Genkai that do not belong to some Dantai such as Takaamahara or Jigokukai, but since this is a matter of the internal, the spirit does not reveal its Naijitsu while it remains in a Guwaimenteki Jiyōtai. This is because Guwaimenteki Jibutsu completely cover up all the Naimen, and those whose Naimen are Bauaku are especially clever at covering them up. However, after a certain Kikan has passed, when their spirits shift to an internal state, all of their Naibun is exposed. This is because at this time, the Guwaimen has already fallen asleep and Shōshitsu, and only the Naimen is opened. The first Guwaimenteki Jiyōtai after the Shigo of a Ningen may span one day, several days, several months, or one year. However, it is extremely rare for it to exceed one year. The reason why there is a difference in the length of the Guwaimen state for each spirit is due to the consistency or inconsistency of both the internal and external aspects. This is because in the Seirei-kai, no one is permitted to separate their thoughts and will from their speech or actions, and thus the internal and external aspects of each spirit must become one and correspond to each other.

Since there is nothing in the Seirei-kai that is not an image of love, which is its own emotion, it is impossible for it not to reveal everything that is in its Naimen to its Guwaimen. For this reason, the celestial beings who are good spirits first cause the Guwaimen of the spirit to be exposed, and by placing this within the order, they make it a plane that corresponds to its Naimen. And the place where such an order is taken is the checkpoint of the Ame no Yachimata, which is the center point of the Chūyū-kai, the Seirei-kai, and it is carried out in Bulgaria, which is under the jurisdiction of Lord Ibukido-nushi.

The internal state refers to the circumstances of the spirit belonging to the heart, that is, the will and thoughts, after the Shigo of a Ningen, having passed a certain Kikan in the Chūyū-kai. When observing the life, speech, and actions of a Ningen, it can be known that everyone possesses two aspects, Naimen and Guwaimen. There is a distinction between the internal and external aspects in both thoughts and will. All those living in a society where civilization has developed, when thinking about others, use what they have seen or heard through public reputation or conversation regarding that person as the basis for observing the performance of a Ningen. However, a Ningen does not speak their true heart when talking with others. Even if one knows the other party is a wicked person, or even if it is a person who does not suit one's own tastes, one tries to be as polite as possible in social interactions and to avoid hurting the other party's feelings; one truly dares to perform hypocritical acts, and it is a world where contradictions arise such that one would be ostracized from society if one did not do this. And although all people are very happy when they are praised well, even if it is a transparent lie, conversely, when the truth is stated clearly and nakedly before that person, they feel extremely unpleasant, eventually come to view one as an enemy, and may even inflict harm. Therefore, what modern Ningen say and do is the exact opposite of what they think and wish. Those in the circumstances of a hypocrite, if left to speak of the Keirin of Takaamahara, the world after death, the salvation of the soul, the truth of sacred places, the welfare of the nation, or one's neighbor, may appear as if they are based entirely on love, goodness, and true faith like celestial beings, but in their Naijitsu, they not only do not believe in the Keirin of Takaamahara, the salvation of the soul, or the world after death, but the only thing they love is their own profit. Such hypocrites and false believers were quite numerous even among the believers of ancient times, but among the modern San-iku-kyo, if one were to count them on one's fingers, there would be nothing left but those in a Guwaimenteki Jiyōtai, and it seems there are few spirits that can ascend directly to heaven.

現界を去つて霊界に新たに入り来る精霊は何れも高天原とか地獄界とかの或る団体に属して居ないものは有りませぬが、併し之は内的の事ですから、その精霊が尚ほ依然として外面的状態にある間は其内実を現はさない。外面的事物が凡ての内面を蔽ひかくして了ひ、内面の暴悪なるものは、殊に之を蔽ひかくすこと巧妙を極めて居るからです。併し或る一定の期間を経たる後に彼等の精霊が内面的状態に移る時に於て、その内分の一切が暴露するものです。この時は最早外面は眠り且つ消失し内面のみ開かるるからであります。人間の死後に於ける第一の外面的情態は或は一日、或は数日、或は数ケ月、或は一年に渉ることがあります。されど一年を越ゆるものは極めて稀有の事であります。斯の如く各自の精霊が外面状態に長短の差ある所以は、内外両面の一致不一致によるものです。何故なれば、精霊界にあつては何人と雖も思想及び意志と言説又は行動を別にする事を許されないから、各精霊の内外両面が一となつて相応せざればならぬからであります。

精霊界にあるものは、自有の情動たる愛の影像ならぬものはありませぬから、その内面にある所の一切をその外面に露はさない訳にはゆきませぬ。此故善霊なる天人は先づ精霊の外面を暴露せしめ、是を順序中に入らしめて以て其内面に相応する平面たらしめらるるのであります。そして斯の如き順序を取る所は精霊界即ち中有界の中心点たる天の八衢の関所であつて、伊吹戸主神の主管し給ふ、ブルガリオに於て行はるるものであります。

内面的情態は人間の死後或る一定の期間を中有界にて経過し、心即ち意志と想念に属する精霊の境遇を云ふのです。人間の生涯、言説、行為等を観察する時は何人にも内面外面の二方面を有することが知り得られます。その想念にも意志にも内外両面の区別があるものです。凡て民文の発達した社会に生存するものは他人の事を思惟するに当り、その人に対する世間の風評又は談話等に由つて見たり聞いたりした所のものを以て人間の性能を観察する基礎となすものです。されど人間は他人と物語る時に際して自分の心の儘を語るものではありませぬ。たとへ対者が悪人と知つても、又自分の気に合はない人であつても、その交際応接などの点は成る可く礼に合ふべく、又相手方の感情を害せざる様と努むるもので、実に偽善的の行為を敢てするもので、又此でなければ社会より排斥されて了ふやうな矛盾が出来する世の中であります。そして凡ての人は見えすいたやうな嘘でも善く言はれると大変に歓ぶものですが、之に反し真実を其人の前に赤裸々に言明する時は非常に不快の念を起し、遂には敵視する様になり、害を加ふるやうな事が出来るものです。故に現代の人間の言ふ所、行ふ所は、その思ふ所願ふ所と全く正反対のものです。偽善者の境遇にあるものは、高天原の経綸や死後の世界や、霊魂の救ひや聖場の真理や国家の利福や隣人の事を語らしておけば恰も天人の如く愛善と信真に一切基づける様なれども、其内実には高天原の経綸も霊魂の救ひも死後の世界も信じないのみか、只愛する所のものは自己の利益あるのみであります。斯の如き偽善者、偽信者は随分太古の教徒の中にも可なり沢山あつたものですが、現代の三五教の中には十指を折り数へたら最早残るは外面的状態にあるもの計りで、天国に直ちに上り得る精霊は少いやうであります。

§ 4

All human Saunen have a distinction between Naimen and Guwaimen, and such humans speak based on Guwaimenteki Saunen, while harboring strange Kanjiyō within their Naimen. They strive to distinguish between the inner and outer aspects, endeavoring to ensure they do not become one. If one truly wishes to support the Keirin of Takaamahara, pray for the prosperity of the Seidan, and attain an Anjiushiyo after Shigo, one must endure any Jijiyō for the sake of the Way. This is because they forget that the Yoyū granted to them to serve the Goyō of the Kamisama is entirely thanks to the Kamisama, and they mistakenly believe it to be their own Jiiu. Herein arises the Shiyōtotsu between the Guwaimen and the Naimen. However, from the perspective of Gendai Rikutsu, it is considered Shitau to distinguish between the inner and outer aspects. Against such views, they counter with various evil names and disparage them as the words of the devil. Furthermore, such people strive to prevent their Naimen Saunen from flowing out to the surface and being exposed, and they attempt to resolve everything according to Gendai-teki dōri, thereby obliterating the Naimen-teki shinzen.

Nevertheless, when a human is created, there is a reason why their Naimen Saunen must be made to correspond and coincide with their Guwaimen Saunen. This coincidence is seen in the true good person, for what they think and what they say are only good. Such a coincidence of inner and outer Saunen can never be seen in a hellish evil person. The reason is that they speak of good with their mouths while thinking of evil in their hearts, existing in a state completely opposite to that of a good person. They show good on the Guwaimen while harboring evil. Thus, the good is controlled by the evil and ends up being used by it. In order to achieve the purpose belonging to the love of their master, the evil person decorates the surface with good, making it their sole expedient. Therefore, the good deeds that appear in their Gensetsu and actions harbor an evil purpose within them, so it is clear that the good is never truly good and becomes stained by evil. Those who see this from the Guwaimen and deem it a good deed are those whose words do not know their Naimen in the slightest.

Those who dwell in true good do not disturb the order; their good all flows from their Naimen, emerges to the Guwaimen, and becomes Gensetsu and action, because humans were created under such an order. The Naimen of a human is entirely in the Kamikai of Takaamahara, enveloped in the light of the Kamikai. That light is the divine truth originating from the Ōkami, which is the so-called Lord of Takaamahara. As mentioned above, humans have Saunen of both inner and outer aspects, and these Saunen are separated from each other. When I said Saunen, I included the will within it and spoke of them together. Indeed, Saunen comes from the will, and without the will, no one, whoever they may be, can have Saunen. Also, when we speak of will and Saunen, this will also includes Kanjiyō, love, and the joy and pleasure arising from these. This is because all of the above are related to the will. The reason is that a person loves what they desire, and through this, they produce feelings of joy and pleasure. Also, Saunen refers to everything by which a person confirms their Kanjiyō, that is, their love. This is because Saunen is nothing more than a form of the will. That is to say, it is nothing more than that which the will desires to manifest itself through. This form appears through various rational dissections, and its source originates in the Reikai and belongs to the human spirit.

One must know that the reason why a human is a human lies entirely in their Naimen, and not in the Guwaimen that has left the Naimen. The Naimen belongs to the human spirit, and a person's life is nothing but the life of this Naimen spirit (Seirei). It is because of this Seirei that there is life in the human body. By this principle, a person lives as their Naimen is, remains unchanged throughout eternity, and maintains the eternal life of immortality. However, because the Guwaimen belongs to the physical body, it inevitably disperses and vanishes after death, and the part that belonged to the spirit sleeps, merely serving as a plane for the Naimen. Thus, the distinction between what belongs to a human's Jiiu and what does not becomes clear. In the case of an evil person, one may know that what belongs to the outer Saunen that causes their Gensetsu and the outer will that causes their actions cannot be considered their Jiiu. Only that which belongs to their Naimen-teki Saunen and will can be their Jiiu. Therefore, when entering eternal life, the only things that will become one's Jiiu are the flowers and fruits of efforts made for the glory of the Kingdom of God; all other things are stripped away in the Chūukai. Kannagara Tamachi Haemase.

原文（近代文語）

凡て人間の想念には内面、外面の区別がありまして、斯の如き人間は、外面的想念によりて言説をなし、内面には却て異様の感情を包蔵して居るものです。そして内外両面を区別する事に努めて、一とならない様にと努むるものです。真に高天原の経綸を扶け聖壇の隆盛を祈り死後の安住所を得むことを思はば如何なる事情をも道のためには忍ぶべきものであります。神様の御用にたて得らるるだけの余裕を与へられたのも皆神様のお蔭である事を忘れ、自有と心得て居るからです。爰に外面内面の衝突を来すことになつて来るのです。併し現代の理窟から言へば、内外両面を区別して考ふる事が至当となつて居ります。そして右様の説に対しては種々の悪名を以て対抗し、且つ悪魔の言と貶すものであります。又斯の如き人は内面的想念の外表に流れ出でて爰に暴露することなからむを勉め、現界的道理によつて、凡てを解決せむとし内面的神善を抹殺するものであります。

さり乍ら人間の創造さるるや、その内面的想念をして相応に由りて外面的想念と相一致せしめなくては成らない理由があるのです。この一致は真の善人に於て見る所であつて、その思ふ所も言ふ所も、唯々善のみだからであります。かくの如き内外両面の想念の一致する事は到底地獄的悪人に於ては見る事が出来ない。何故なれば、心に悪を思ひながら善を口に語り全く善人と正反対の情態にあるものです。外面に善を示して悪を抱いて居る。かくて善は悪のために制せられ、之に使役さるるに至るのであります。悪人はその所主の愛に属する目的を達成せむがために、表に善を飾つて唯一の方便となすものです。故にその言説と行動とに現れる所の善事なるものは、その中に悪き目的を包蔵して居るので、善も決して善でなく、悪の汚す所となるは明白なものです。外面的に之を見て善事となすものは、その内面を少しも知悉せざるものの言葉であります。

真の善に居るものは、順序を乱すこと無く、その善は皆内面的想念より流れて外面に出てそれが言説となり行動となるのは、人間は斯の如き順序のもとに創造せられたものであるからであります。人間の内面は凡て高天原の神界にあり、神界の光明中に包まれて居る。その光明とは、大神より起来する所の神真で所謂高天原の主なるものです。人間は内外両面の想念があり、その想念が内外互に相隔たり居ることは前述の通りであります。想念と言つたのは其中に意志をも包含して併せて言つたのです。盖し想念なるものは意志より来り、意志なければ何人と雖も想念なるものは有りませぬ。又意志及び想念と云ふ時は、この意志の裡にも又情動、愛、及び、是等より起来する歓喜や悦楽をも含んで居ります。以上のものは何れも意志と関連して居るからです。何故なれば人はその欲する所を愛し、之によつて歓喜悦楽の情を生ずるものだからです。又想念といふことは人が由りて以て其情動即ち愛を確かむる所の一切を言ふのです。何んとなれば想念は意志の形式に過ぎないものです。即ち意志が由りて以て自ら顕照せむと欲する所のものに過ぎないからであります。この形式は種々の理性的解剖によつて現れるもので、その源泉を霊界に発し人の精霊に属するものであります。

凡て人間の人間たる所以は全くその内面にあつて内面を放れた所の外面にあらざることを知らねばならない。内面は人の霊に属し、人の生涯なるものは、此の内面なる霊（精霊）の生涯に外ならないからです。人の身体に生命のあるのは此精霊に由るものです。是の理によつて人はその内面の如くに生存し永遠に渉りて変らず、不老不死の永生を保つものです。されど外面は又肉体に属するが故に、死後は必ず離散し消滅し、其霊に属して居た部分は眠り、唯内面のために、是が平面となるに過ぎないのです。かくて人間の自有に属するものと属せざるものとの区別が明かになるのであります。悪人にあつては其言説を起さしむる所の外的想念と、其行動を起さしむる所の外的意志とに属するものは、一も以て彼等の自有と為すべからざるものと知り得るでありませう。只その内面的なる想念と意志とに属するもの而己が自有を為し得るのであります。故に永遠の生命に入りたる時自有となるべきものは、神の国の栄えのために努力した花実ばかりで、其他の一切のものは、中有界に於て剥脱されるものであります。アヽ惟神霊幸倍坐世。
